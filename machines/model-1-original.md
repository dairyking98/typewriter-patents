## Notes from Dingwerth's *Geschichte der deutschen Schreibmaschinen-Fabriken*

- 1903-04: typebar, visible. 30 keys in 3 rows, double shift; 275 M; ~1,300 made; remainder sold as Cito
- 1904-05: typebar. solid side panels replacing lattice, wider carriage, automatic line feed (Zeilenschaltung), black keys from 1905; 295 M; ~1,000; sold as Lloyd
