## Notes from Dingwerth's *Geschichte der deutschen Schreibmaschinen-Fabriken*

- 1928: typebar portable. 44 keys, carriage shift, automatic ribbon reverse, typebar linkage (Typenhebelgetriebe); 20 per day by mid-1929. Same as Adler 32.
