## Notes from Dingwerth's *Geschichte der deutschen Schreibmaschinen-Fabriken*

- Nov 1922-1924: typebar, push-rod ("Stoßstangen") system. Copy of the Adler 7 with rectangular cover instead of Adler's rounded one; wheel-type carriage escapement (Radschaltung); separate ribbon advance independent of carriage travel; gauge-made interchangeable parts; about 2,000 made; made in Berlin-Borsigwalde
