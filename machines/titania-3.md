## Notes from Dingwerth's *Geschichte der deutschen Schreibmaschinen-Fabriken*

- 1913-1918: typebar. Minor changes; 24 cm carriage, with 39 and 49 cm wide carriages from spring 1914
- 1918-1925: typebar. Small numbers, made mostly by women
