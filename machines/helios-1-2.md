## Notes from Dingwerth's *Geschichte der deutschen Schreibmaschinen-Fabriken*

- 1909-12: keyboard + type wheel. 98 M (foreign-language 110 M) at launch; see Helios section.
- 1909-10: keyboard, type wheel. 98 M.
- 1910-12: same. 125, then 135 M.
