## Notes from Dingwerth's *Geschichte der deutschen Schreibmaschinen-Fabriken*

- 1911-1913: typebar portable, three-row, double shift. 30 keys, 90 characters; first non-folding three-row portable with a universal keyboard; no segment slots but a type guide; carriage on balls and rollers; easily re-adjustable levers; knife-type line feed (Messerschaltung); billing and decimal tabulator; about 5.5 kg; 200 marks in wooden case, 205 in travel case
- 1913-?: same. Knife feed replaced by an exchangeable wheel feed (Radschaltung); back-space lever replaced by a key in the keyboard; simplified ribbon shift (knob); new frame; moveable margin stops; letter-sheet stop with scale; 5.0 kg; 225 marks (235 with case per ad); bichrome ribbon with triple-position ribbon control
