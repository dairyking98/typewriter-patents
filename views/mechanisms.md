# Mechanisms by subsystem

Every mechanism feature seen on at least 3 reading cards, grouped by subsystem. *Index-type* = read patents that are likely index machines (by class and text) or are linked to an index machine. The bar strip shows the cards per decade, 1820s to 1950s. Each feature page has the comparison: how it developed, its variants and the best examples.

*Counts are over the patents that were read (reading cards), not every patent ever granted; "first seen" is the oldest card in the library, not necessarily the invention.*
