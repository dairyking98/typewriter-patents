# Timeline browser

Every dated machine in the library (1025, from 22 countries), the patents granted each year, and when the main typewriter features appear, on one timeline: index machines, type-element machines and typebar machines together. Show lanes by country or by kind of machine, or everything in one lane; filter by kind; and click a year to see what was made and patented then.

<div class="tl-browser" markdown="0"></div>

The browser needs JavaScript. Without it, see [Machines](../machines/README.md), the [Timeline](../views/timeline.md) and [Typewriter features](../views/typewriter-features.md).
