Types on a sphere or hemisphere

## Patents, oldest first

| Patent | Year | Inventor | Solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| [US393259](../patents/US393259.md) | 1888 | Perry, Charles H. | A hand-held printing plunger with a spherical face carrying concentric circles of type is tilted by a universal joint to be located by notches in a guide plate, then pressed on a sliding platen. |
| [US409073](../patents/US409073.md) | 1889 | Stover, Daniel C. | A spherical type segment hung on a universal joint at its centre is tilted by three oscillating cross-bars, selected by notched 'wards' on each key lever, so the chosen type comes under the centre and the whole block is then pushed straight down onto the paper. |
| [US435654](../patents/US435654.md) | 1890 | Perry, Charles H. | A single gimbal-mounted printing plunger with a convex type-plate is tilted to the chosen character by a key-operated push-pin, then pressed down onto a short platen while the whole print head travels along the line. |
| [US607270](../patents/US607270.md) | 1898 | Pastre - Lambert, Eugène - Frank | A single nutating disc key with all characters marked on it carries a rigid spherical type-ring beneath it; pressing the edge at a character tilts the ring until a stop pin seats in a recess, and continued pressure drives the ring straight down to print and release a spring carriage for one space. |
| [US640208](../patents/US640208.md) | 1900 | Lambert, Frank | A single tilting disc key ('nutating key') carries a spherical type block underneath; tilting selects a type, and pressing the key straight down prints it and releases one escapement step. |
| [US892891](../patents/US892891.md) | 1908 | Richards, Francis H. | Mount the types in three tiers on a spherical turret that a continuously friction-driven shaft rotates and a rock-frame tilts, with key-raised stop pins arresting the rotation and the same stop impact tripping a spring-loaded hammer. |
| [US1079812](../patents/US1079812.md) | 1913 | van Loy, Alois | A spherical segmental type carrier hangs from a long single-armed printing lever, with the pointing key at the lever end, so a light press prints and the pointer key and carrier move together by a jointed parallelogram. |
