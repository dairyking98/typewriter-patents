# Basket shift

*Typebars feature · code `BAR:basket-shift`*

Case shift by moving the type basket or segment

**10 patents**, 1908–1947. Often together with:

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md) (4)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md) (10), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md) (10), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md) (4)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md) (5)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md) (5)
- **Frame and body:** [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md) (5), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md) (4)

**Machines:** [Zalsho](../machines/zalsho.md) (1), [Smith Premier 10](../machines/smith-premier-10.md) (1), [Smith Premier Simplex](../machines/smith-premier-simplex.md) (1), [Smith Premier Ratiotyp](../machines/smith-premier-ratiotyp.md) (1)
