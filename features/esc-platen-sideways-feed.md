# Platen sideways feed

*Spacing and escapement feature · code `ESC:platen-sideways-feed`*

The platen (not a carriage) is slid sideways along its axis, driven by a rack and pinion, to space the line *(proposed by a reader)*

**3 patents**, 1943–1943. Often together with:

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md) (1)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md) (1)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md) (3), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md) (2), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md) (1), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md) (1)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md) (1)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md) (2)
