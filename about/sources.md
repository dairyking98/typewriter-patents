# Sources and credits

## Patents

- **US patents (1829–1951):** the typewriter patent list behind this library, with texts, drawings and classes from [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com) (public domain documents). Drawings are linked from Google, not copied.
- **German patents (1877–1950):** lists from [DEPATISnet](https://depatisnet.dpma.de) (the German patent office, DPMA) and Google Patents; texts and the English translation of old German patents from Google Patents; every German patent page links its original scan at DEPATISnet. Dates of old German patents without a listed date are estimated from the patent number.
- **Japanese patents and utility models (1892–1949):** the list and the scans from [J-PlatPat](https://www.j-platpat.inpit.go.jp) (the Japan Patent Office's database, INPIT); the transcriptions and English translations were made by machine from the page images and checked against OCR. Every Japanese patent page links its original at J-PlatPat. More are added as they are translated.
- **Summaries** (the Summary and Reading on patent pages, feature tags, patent families) were made by machine from the patent text and OCR. Check the patent and its drawings before relying on a detail.

## Machines

- **Leonhard Dingwerth, *Kleines Lexikon historischer Schreibmaschinen* (1997)**: the machine facts (year, maker, country, carrier, strike, inking, shift, characters, sales, price, rarity); each machine page cites its page in the scan on the Internet Archive, uploaded by [Otto Koponen](https://archive.org/details/@otto_koponen). The Lexikon's text is not reproduced. Machine names the original OCR missed were read off the scans' name boxes and checked by eye.
- **Leonhard Dingwerth, *Die Geschichte der deutschen Schreibmaschinen-Fabriken***: short factual notes (units built, prices, features) on many machine pages, summarised in our own words.
- **Ernst Martin, *Die Schreibmaschine und ihre Entwicklungsgeschichte* (1934)**: machines it describes.

## Timelines and landmarks

- [The American typewriter, landmark by landmark](../views/american-timeline.md) cites its sources entry by entry: Google Patents, museum and collector references, and period books: Remington's *The Story of the Typewriter 1873–1923*, *Typewriter Topics* (October 1923), Charles Oden's *Evolution of the Typewriter* (1917) and George Carl Mares's *The History of the Typewriter* (1909).
- [Typewriter features through time](../views/typewriter-features.md): counts from this library's patents (classes and text) and the Lexikon's machine data.
