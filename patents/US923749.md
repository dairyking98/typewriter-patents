## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to simplify and improve the construction and operation of the latch or trip mechanism, which causes the dog or pawl to skip the teeth of the notched wheel to effect the spacing between the sucessive bills on the carbon sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A simplified latch or trip that holds the pawl out of the notched platen wheel, letting the crank skip teeth and space each bill backward and forward.

**Solution:** A simplified latch or trip that holds the pawl out of the notched platen wheel, letting the crank skip teeth and space each bill backward and forward.
