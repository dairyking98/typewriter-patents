## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A power-driven multi-type sliding type bar needs a simple, positive way to drive the bar to the print position and also swing the bar carrier to select the other type group.

**Solution:** A pivoted carrier holds the sliding type bar; a ratchet device on the constantly turning power shaft moves it, and a pin-and-slot locking lever swings the carrier when the second key is pressed.

**How it works:** Each type bar has four types and slides in a guide member pivoted on a fixed part. A toggle link pair moves the bar forward, and its support position is adjustable by a screw. Pressing a key engages a pawl with a tooth into a ratchet wheel on a continuously rotating shaft, which pulls the link system and drives the bar forward. When the second key of the pair is pressed, a locking lever with a slot catches a pin on the driving lever so the carrier also tilts, bringing the lower type group to print. The pin-and-slot link lets the drive continue moving after the carrier reaches its limit. The platen is switched to a second position to choose between the other pair of types.
