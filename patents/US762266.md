## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writers, and particularly to the ribbon movement thereof.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon spool shafts are pivoted and adjustable with blocks and set-nuts; the feed shaft is driven directly from the universal bar; reversal is automatic.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon winding shafts need adjustment to the platen, and ribbon tension and reversal need a better arrangement.

**Solution:** Ribbon spool shafts are pivoted and adjustable with blocks and set-nuts; the feed shaft is driven directly from the universal bar; reversal is automatic.
