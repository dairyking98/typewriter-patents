## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A ribbon mechanism is needed that moves the ribbon laterally across the line as well as feeding it.

**Solution:** The ribbon spool frames on a rock shaft are drawn sideways by a spiral cam race on the spring-motor wheel, so the ribbon shifts its width over one line of writing, while a separate pawl mechanism feeds it.

**How it works:** Two spool frames hang on arms of a rock shaft, with a top roller over which the ribbon runs. An arm with a lug on a spool frame engages a spiral cam race cut in a shell on the spring motor wheel; as the wheel turns with the carriage the frames shift across about the ribbon width over a full line. Spool shafts have splined boxes so the spools slide along them. Separate reversing and feeding gear, with sliding pieces in grooved plates, pawls on notched wheels on the spool shafts and spring-returned arms, feeds the ribbon longitudinally at each carriage return to the right.
