## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shorthand pad sheets must be turned by hand on a document holder, and a line pointer should turn the page automatically without two sheets catching together.

**Solution:** Successive sheets carry recesses alternately at the margins and in the middle, so that the line-indicator hooks grab exactly one sheet at a time and the leaping pointer flips it backward as it rises.
