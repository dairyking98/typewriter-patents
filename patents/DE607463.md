## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type lever drive for small typewriters with segment switching, circular Typhebeh uhelager and circular segment, in which each key lever through each two handlebars are guided almost parallel to themselves and the rear ones Ends of the lower link by enclosing them at the same angle with the horizontal and tension members of the same length are connected to the type levers.

**How it works (reading):** Key levers are mounted on straight axes, and the distances of tension member joints on type levers scale in the same ratio as those on the lower links.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Segment-switching type lever drives give uneven key depth, and curved key lever bearings are costly to make.

**Solution:** Key levers are mounted on straight axes, and the distances of tension member joints on type levers scale in the same ratio as those on the lower links.
