## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by denominational arrangements

**How it works (reading):** Steel stop plates are pushed onto bearing rods and locked in place by their tooth gaps; one rod is slid by springs for the width.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Tabulator stop plates are loose or insecure and can shift out of place when removed and reinstalled.

**Solution:** Steel stop plates are pushed onto bearing rods and locked in place by their tooth gaps; one rod is slid by springs for the width.
