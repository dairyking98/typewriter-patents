## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (shorthand and text writing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement and further development of the machine according to patent 590 367.

**How it works (reading):** Three switches, operated alone or together, change between shorthand and plain text and between cases, and a toggle turns the paper roller to print above or below the baseline.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter that prints both plain text and shorthand needs switching and height adjustments that the main patent does not provide.

**Solution:** Three switches, operated alone or together, change between shorthand and plain text and between cases, and a toggle turns the paper roller to print above or below the baseline.
