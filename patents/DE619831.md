## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (castable matrix strip production) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** Punchings for folding and stretching are made in the same stroke as embossing, on the type wheel carrying the dies.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-stretching punchings in the word spaces are weakened when punched in a separate operation.

**Solution:** Punchings for folding and stretching are made in the same stroke as embossing, on the type wheel carrying the dies.
