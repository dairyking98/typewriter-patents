## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A two-button typewriter had to be usable by blind people and by people with one arm.

**Solution:** A setting pin with a socket leaves the fingertip free to slide over a Braille character board; the print and carriage keys can be linked to pedals.
