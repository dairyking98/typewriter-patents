## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A flat U-shaped margin stop body carries a locking spring whose legs lie on the flat sides of the body, one leg hooking over the end with a nose that locks it, and the other anchored in a hole, so the leg yields when the stop is moved.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A margin stop must lock reliably in the toothed strip while keeping a flat body, without a spring that protrudes or needs extra parts.

**Solution:** A flat U-shaped margin stop body carries a locking spring whose legs lie on the flat sides of the body, one leg hooking over the end with a nose that locks it, and the other anchored in a hole, so the leg yields when the stop is moved.
