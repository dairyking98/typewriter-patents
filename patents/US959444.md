## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the ribbon mechanisms of writing machines, especially those in which the ribbon is vibrated to cover and uncover the printing point at each type stroke.

**How it works (reading):** Make the ribbon actuator on the universal bar frame lockable or free by a keyboard finger piece, disconnect ribbon winding from the carriage spring when the carriage is released, and reverse the ribbon by a follower riding on the ribbon coils.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator could not easily be silenced for stencil writing, and carriage-spring-driven ribbon winding ran the ribbon rapidly and could break it or foul the reverse when the carriage was released.

**Solution:** Make the ribbon actuator on the universal bar frame lockable or free by a keyboard finger piece, disconnect ribbon winding from the carriage spring when the carriage is released, and reverse the ribbon by a follower riding on the ribbon coils.

**How it works:** A ribbon actuator is pivoted on the universal bar frame. A lock holds it so it reciprocates and vibrates the ribbon at each stroke; releasing the lock lets it swing idly so the ribbon stays still for stencils, and the same finger piece silences ribbon winding. The ribbon is wound from the carriage driving spring, but the carriage-release device also releases the spools from it so the ribbon does not run during tabulating. A follower on the winding shaft bears on the edges of the ribbon coils; when the spool is empty it enters the spool, releases a latch and lets the driving shaft shift to reverse the feed.
