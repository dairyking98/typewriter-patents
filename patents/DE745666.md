## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink ribbons characterised by the material from which they are woven

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a method for marking ribbons.

**How it works (reading):** The marking is embossed into the finished coloured ribbon with a dry stamp; the pressed areas show the mark without any pigment or drying.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbons are hard to mark for theft tracking; threads and pre-marking are costly.

**Solution:** The marking is embossed into the finished coloured ribbon with a dry stamp; the pressed areas show the mark without any pigment or drying.
