## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed in calculating and recording machines must reverse when the ribbon runs out on one spool, which needs a dependable automatic reversal.

**Solution:** Ribbon carrying members with ratchet wheels and pawls that automatically move one pawl into or out of engagement, reversing the feed when the ribbon is nearly unwound.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
