## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a device whereby the paper when being written upon in a type-writing machine will be controlled and prevented from being blown and shaken by the wind or other causes.

**How it works (reading):** A rod frame pivoted to the carriage holds the paper controlling bars in vertical or inclined positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper blows and shakes on the carriage in the wind, spoiling the writing.

**Solution:** A rod frame pivoted to the carriage holds the paper controlling bars in vertical or inclined positions.
