**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to type sets for machine writing or the like, in which part of the letters are optically corrected is.

**How it works (reading):** Optically corrected characters are referenced to the baseline or head/foot lines of unshifted characters, so one or two main stops align the whole type set without marks.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Aligning type bodies on the stop by hand is slow, and the usual alignment marks on the body are costly or distorted by hardening.

**Solution:** Optically corrected characters are referenced to the baseline or head/foot lines of unshifted characters, so one or two main stops align the whole type set without marks.

**How it works:** Foot lines of corrected characters are placed on the baseline, with ascender and descender lines at equal distance, so the edges serve as guides.
