## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator must take hands off the keys to turn the platen, return the carriage and shift.

**Solution:** Knee levers connected by rods, rock shafts and cords perform line spacing, carriage return and shifts.

**How it works:** A rock shaft at one side of the Blickensderfer is connected by a rod to a short rock shaft at the other side and by another rod to a U-shaped operating lever. A cord from the lever passes round a sheave on the frame and one on a sliding arm, to act on the carriage. Knee levers also work the caps and figures shifts. A buffer is shown at the rear.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md)

**Parts:** rock shaft; connecting rod; operating lever; cord; sheave; sliding arm; spring catch; knee lever; buffer
