## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must be changed and the striking point on the ribbon shifted so the whole ribbon wears evenly.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools slip onto non-circular extensions of ratchet wheels on journal pins, held by spring catches, and a set-screw slides the whole frame sideways to change the striking point.
