## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Companion to US902326: the mechanism must adjust type dies selectively through different distances and then hold them precisely.

**Solution:** A step-advanced rotary head carries slides each with four type dies; selectors choose which die of the slide at the working position is shifted to the exact working point.

**How it works:** A power-driven cam shaft and supplemental shaft drive the machine. A rotary head on a vertical shaft carries five slides of four dies each, twenty dies in all, in slideways transverse to the plane of rotation. The head is advanced step by step through worm gearing and a star wheel. Lugs on the slides ride in a raceway that holds them idle; at the working position a transverse channel lets a selected slide be shifted. Selectors, manual or otherwise, pick which die of the slide at position goes to the working point.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** rotary head; die slide; type die; raceway; lug; cam shaft; worm wheel; star wheel; selector

**Numbers:** 20 type dies; 5 slides of 4 dies
