## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key levers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to keyboards of a foldable front strike typewriting machine such as disclosed in the patent to F.

**How it works (reading):** Key-levers are stamped and bent from single pieces, suspended from a support by bearing parts, and arranged so the key bank folds with the frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboard key-levers needed substantial bearings and simple, cheap construction for a foldable front-strike machine.

**Solution:** Key-levers are stamped and bent from single pieces, suspended from a support by bearing parts, and arranged so the key bank folds with the frame.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
