## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Line spacing needs a simple, intermittent platen drive that can be disengaged for free rotation.

**Solution:** A pneumatic cylinder on the carriage pushes a toothed rack that rotates the line-space ratchet and platen, with an adjustable stop and a disengaging swivel.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
