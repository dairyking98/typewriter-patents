## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to tabulating mechanism for typewrit-ing machines, and a feature of the invention is an improved form of stop-carrying rack bar having a series of rack teeth on'each side, and means for'independently supporting adjustable stops on each sideof the rackbar.

**How it works (reading):** A stop-carrying rack bar with rack teeth on each side, each side supporting its own stops, and a locking rod that holds stops in place and locks the rack against rotation when released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stops on a tabulator rack interfere with one another and cannot all be adjusted independently.

**Solution:** A stop-carrying rack bar with rack teeth on each side, each side supporting its own stops, and a locking rod that holds stops in place and locks the rack against rotation when released.
