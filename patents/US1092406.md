## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My main objects are to provide a simple and cheap though eficctive construction and one in which only a single row of balls is employed and in which the bearing surfaces may be increased in size without increasing the width of parts at the joint or bearing or using more space for the hanger and type bar.

**How it works (reading):** A type bar eye carries a circular V-groove with one row of anti-friction balls, so the bearing surface can grow without widening the joint, and the hanger has beveled surfaces to take the balls.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars swing in hangers with friction bearings that are bulky or costly to make.

**Solution:** A type bar eye carries a circular V-groove with one row of anti-friction balls, so the bearing surface can grow without widening the joint, and the hanger has beveled surfaces to take the balls.
