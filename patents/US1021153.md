## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for adjusting the tension of the returning springs for the keys or key levers of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** The return springs hang from a tension bar that moves as a whole relative to the key-lever ridge, with regulating screws that still allow individual adjustment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Each key-lever return spring must be tensioned separately, which is slow and gives uneven pressure.

**Solution:** The return springs hang from a tension bar that moves as a whole relative to the key-lever ridge, with regulating screws that still allow individual adjustment.

**Features:** [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
