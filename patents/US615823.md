## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ordinary ball bearings with a fixed centre cone give friction at the typebar pivot.

**Solution:** Replace the centre cone by a centre ball that sits in hemispherical seats in the hanger yoke and turns freely.

**How it works:** A U-shaped yoke or hanger has oblique side arms, with a cross screw whose removal lets the arms spread to admit the ball. The typebar has a casing box on each flat side holding a ring of balls. A single centre ball sits in hemispherical recesses on the inside of the yoke arms and acts as the pivot. As the centre ball rolls, it presents fresh surface to the surrounding ring of balls.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** yoke; hanger; typebar; ball casing; centre ball; ring of balls; spreading screw

**Improves on:**

- cone-centred ball bearing: centre ball replaces the fixed cone so contact surface keeps changing
