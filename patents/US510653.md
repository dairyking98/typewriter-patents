## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor wants to simplify and cheapen a typewriter and make it easier to use by dispensing with manual operation of certain parts such as letter spacing and carriage return.

**Solution:** A typebar machine whose carriage is driven by a screw shaft turned a quarter turn by every key stroke and returned by a strap-and-gear key lever.

**How it works:** The carriage rides on a support with a screw shaft that carries a disk with four pins. A hooked bar, pulled by a spring-returned spacing key lever, engages a pin; on releasing the key the spring turns the shaft one quarter turn, advancing the carriage one letter. A bar from the key levers works the same spacing lever, so every printed character spaces automatically. To return the carriage, a separate key lever pulls a strap wound on a barrel of a gear that multiplies rotation into a pinion on the screw shaft; a variant is shown at the other end of the machine. The text also mentions a ribbon feed and a cleaning brush.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** base plate; top plate; carriage support; screw shaft; pinned disk; hooked bar; spacing key lever; spring; strap; barrel; gear; pinion; return key lever; ribbon spool; cleaning brush

**Numbers:** 4 pins on disk; quarter turn per letter; 5 sheets of drawings
