## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cast or injection-moulded type cylinders need expensive moulds, give much waste and warp on cooling; cylinders made from type strips wrapped on a round body print a blurred, curved face.

**Solution:** A type strip with recesses cut between the characters is rolled over a mandrel so it kinks at the recesses and forms a polygonal cylinder with flat type faces.

**How it works:** Recesses, round, pointed or flat, are made in the strip material between the types, on either the top or the underside. The strip is rolled over a mandrel, and the weakened points fold into small creases. The type faces therefore stay flat between creases and form a polygon, so each character at the print point prints fully and sharply against the platen.

**Features:** [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md), [ELEM:metal-type](../features/elem-metal-type.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** type strip; recess; mandrel; type cylinder; type face

**Improves on:**

- cast and injection-moulded type cylinders: no costly moulds, no cooling distortion or waste
- type strips applied to a round base cylinder: flat faces from kinks rather than a curved surface
