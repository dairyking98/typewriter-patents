## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Moving the platen left or right needs a simple drive that gives exact spacing from one spring.

**Solution:** A single spring pulls a cord that tilts a rocking plate left or right, and its inclined slot switches one of two meshing pinions into the platen frame rack, with a one-way ratchet metering each step.

**How it works:** A spring drum pulls a cord, led over a roller and between two guide rollers, whose tip is hooked to the left or right end of the platen. The cord tilts a rocking plate, pivoted on the platen frame rack, to the left or right. An inclined slot in the lower part of the plate engages a projection on a mounting plate that carries two meshing pinions, and tilting the plate rocks the mounting plate so that either one pinion or the other meshes with the rack. The pinions are linked to a one-way ratchet wheel and pawl, so the rack, and with it the platen, moves intermittently left or right by the ratchet spacing under the spring force.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Left/right feed change-over device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a left/right feed change-over device for a typewriter built as follows. A cord is pulled by the force of a spring, and a rocking plate is made to tilt left or right. An inclined long slot is made near the lower edge of the rocking plate. Two pinions, which mesh with each other and are linked with a ratchet wheel, are switched with respect to the rack of the platen frame by the engagement of a projection on their mounting plate with the slot, so that the platen is moved left or right by a ratchet wheel that turns in one direction only. The object is to move the platen left or right as required by the force of a single spring, and to fix the printing spacing accurately and automatically by the pinions.
