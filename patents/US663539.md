## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet materials for use therein by transferring ink from the master sheet using solvent-soluble dyestuffs on the master sheets, e.g. alcohol-soluble

**How it works (reading):** A copying compound applied to a typewriter ribbon, sheet or pad transfers only the struck characters onto ordinary paper, which can then be used to transfer the writing to a stone or zinc plate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Transfers made from typewritten originals need special transfer paper, which is sensitive and easily damages or blots the original writing.

**Solution:** A copying compound applied to a typewriter ribbon, sheet or pad transfers only the struck characters onto ordinary paper, which can then be used to transfer the writing to a stone or zinc plate.
