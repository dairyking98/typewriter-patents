## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Connection between the escapement and the carriage feed rack needs to be quiet and low friction, even when the platen carriage shifts.

**Solution:** A new connection between the escapement shaft and feed pinion with the rack that stays engaged while the carriage rails ride up and down.

**How it works:** The platen carriage runs on ball runways on rails that ride on a shift rail raised by a shift shaft; a tie rod with a threaded screw adjusts the platen distance to the type. A fixed dog and movable dog form the escapement, connected through the escapement shaft to a feed pinion meshing with the carriage feed rack. The pinion arrangement allows for the vertical shift.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** escapement shaft; feed rack; feed pinion; fixed dog; movable dog; shift rail; tie rod; ball runway
