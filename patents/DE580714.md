## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (reversible ribbon switching for address printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends; Inking devices using ink ribbons, ink sheets, or carbon tape or paper; Mechanisms for conveying copy material through addressographs or like series-printing machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an automatically reversible ink ribbon switching device for printing machines, in particular address printing machines, in which the ribbon reels and all control parts of the switching device are arranged in a removable frame are.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon spools and reversing controls sit on a frame that folds down on a pin, and the drive reconnects automatically when folded back.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon switching on address printers had to be disconnected and realigned every time the ribbon was replaced.

**Solution:** The ribbon spools and reversing controls sit on a frame that folds down on a pin, and the drive reconnects automatically when folded back.
