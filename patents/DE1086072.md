## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Text stored as signals for later automatic writing needs a way to insert corrections at marked places.

**Solution:** Mark the signal sequence to be corrected with identification signals that trigger scanning of a separate memory location holding the correction sequence, which is then output in its place.

**How it works:** Characters from the keyboard are stored as signals on magnetic drums, discs or tapes before being written. Identification signals at the start or end of a sequence indicate where the correction sequence is stored. Memory is divided into areas per line and fields per character, selectable by selection means. Contents can be shown on a cathode-ray tube. The writing unit is a single-character head on a slide on a guide rail.

**Parts:** memory; magnetic tape; sensing device; writing unit; keyboard; guide rail; drive motor; display screen
