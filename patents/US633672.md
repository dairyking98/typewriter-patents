**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods on side surfaces thereof, e.g. fixed thereto the types or dies being movable relative to the bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to various improvements in type-writers or type-writing machines, and said invention relates more particularly to a type-writing machine such as that illustrated in our Patent No.

**How it works (reading):** A set of improvements to the Wagner typebar machine: a platen shift whose lever is locked in three stages by a cam-slotted locking lever, a spring-loaded releasable feed roller set, a ribbon spool drive with adjustable feed, a detachable envelope guide and a hand release for the typebar lock.

## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines (Underwood front-strike type) needed a platen shift that holds alignment without vibration, paper feed rollers that can be released, an envelope guide, and a manual release for the typebar lock.

**Solution:** A set of improvements to the Wagner typebar machine: a platen shift whose lever is locked in three stages by a cam-slotted locking lever, a spring-loaded releasable feed roller set, a ribbon spool drive with adjustable feed, a detachable envelope guide and a hand release for the typebar lock.

**How it works:** A shift key depresses a bell-crank on each side of the frame, joined by a rock-shaft, which raises a rail carrying the platen casing on links to bring upper-case type to the platen. A pawl holds the shift key down and moves a spring-pressed locking lever whose recesses grip a lug on the bell-crank so the platen cannot vibrate; a second key releases the pawl, and a momentary key shifts for one capital only. The ribbon spools are stepped by ratchet wheels and reciprocating pawls driven from the universal bar on every type stroke or space, with a centrally pivoted bar selecting the feed direction and an adjustable cam setting the number of teeth stepped. Feed rollers on spring-loaded arms bear on the platen and are lifted away by a hand lever through sliding arms and cams. A detachable envelope guide and a manual release of the automatic typebar lock complete the machine.
