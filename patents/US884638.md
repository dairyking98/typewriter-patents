## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting ma'- chines and especially to a type bar and hanger construction for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Two type bars pivot on one hanger, one on each side, with a ball-bearing type-bar pivot; hanger attaches to the segment with a headed screw.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars need a better pivot and hanger arrangement, with the room in the segment used more economically.

**Solution:** Two type bars pivot on one hanger, one on each side, with a ball-bearing type-bar pivot; hanger attaches to the segment with a headed screw.
