## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting the carriage stop to a chosen denominational point (units, tens, hundreds) in tabulating was uncertain and inconvenient for the operator.

**Solution:** A carrier that moves with the carriage in parallel motion in front of the keyboard and bears graduated push rods selecting a stepped stop that meets a column stop on the frame.

**How it works:** A bar carrier at the front of the keyboard runs on two linked levers, so it follows the carriage in a straight line. On it is a stepped stop member with steps at letter-space intervals and graduated push rods projecting through a release plate. Pressing a finger piece positions the matching step to meet a pendent tooth of an adjustable column stop on a rack on the frame, and in the last part of the stroke moves the plate to release the carriage. Shown on a Sun machine.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** carrier bar; lever; link; stepped stop member; push rod; release plate; column stop; rack; carriage; escapement wheel
