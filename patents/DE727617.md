**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding machine switching mechanism)

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adding machine linkage coupled to typewriters is bulky and costly when totals and subtotals are carried.

**Solution:** Each counter's shift linkage uses a double fork lever driven by a common link and reversing gear, giving a simple compact arrangement.

Such switching and drive mechanisms are already known. But they had the disadvantage that with provision of totals and subtotals from the counters the shift linkage of a very complex nature and therefore bulky and were expensive to manufacture.
