## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing machine for printing plates) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the invention is now to use the known embossing machines for printing plates to improve with male and female stamps so that the embossing in the Printing plates turn out to be perfectly even and sharp-edged without warping of the pressure plate sheet can occur.

**How it works (reading):** A hold-down fixed to the machine frame has a fork-shaped piece that grips the male punch on at least three sides and presses the plate at the embossing point from above or below, so it cannot warp. The hold-down swings out so plates with a strong front edge can be inserted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In embossing machines for thin sheet metal address plates, the plate warps during embossing, so the text comes out skewed and uneven.

**Solution:** A hold-down fixed to the machine frame has a fork-shaped piece that grips the male punch on at least three sides and presses the plate at the embossing point from above or below, so it cannot warp. The hold-down swings out so plates with a strong front edge can be inserted.
