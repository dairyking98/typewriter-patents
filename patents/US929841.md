## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen or track of a flat-platen typewriter can be disturbed out of its normal position while the tilting or travelling mechanism is adjusted, such as when a page of a book is turned.

**Solution:** A locking member holds the tiltable platen in its normal position, is released by a hand-operated device, and is automatically engaged when the traveling mechanism moves to the rear, so the platen is held while tilted.
