## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage advance and return by hand is laborious, and the energy spent on the carriage return must be restored.

**Solution:** A spring motor advances the carriage in printing and is coupled back to the carriage for return; a second electric motor restores the spring energy during the return and cuts out automatically when done.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
