## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewriting machines, the improvement pertamingparticularly to the spacer bar mechanism and adjustments of a typewriter machine.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable sheet-metal spacer bar slides longitudinally and is contacted by the key levers, with means to lock and retain its position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The spacer bar must be adjustable in manufacture, and key-lever stops need adjusting after assembly.

**Solution:** An adjustable sheet-metal spacer bar slides longitudinally and is contacted by the key levers, with means to lock and retain its position.
