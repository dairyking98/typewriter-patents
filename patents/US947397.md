**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing machine for addressing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines for embossing plates or material, more particularly for nse in addressing or mailing machines for printing addresses or names upon the articles or magazines to be sent ont to subscribers.

**How it works (reading):** Two axially arranged die carriers, one above and one below the plate, with an escapement that moves the plate carriage between embossing positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Embossing machines for addressing need the two series of dies arranged so they can be operated from one point with a plate carriage.

**Solution:** Two axially arranged die carriers, one above and one below the plate, with an escapement that moves the plate carriage between embossing positions.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
