## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists of the features of construction, arrangements of parts and combinations of devices set forth in the following specification and partioularly pointed out.

**How it works (reading):** A graduated stop is fixed on a scale at the column field; keys throw a single plunger into the path of the stop to a graduated distance, so the carriage jumps to the column position; the mechanism works with the carriage release devices.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typist must move the carriage by hand to the first position in a column of figures or words, which is slow to do by hand.

**Solution:** A graduated stop is fixed on a scale at the column field; keys throw a single plunger into the path of the stop to a graduated distance, so the carriage jumps to the column position; the mechanism works with the carriage release devices.
