## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** Each type-bar has an improved type-bar and type block or head construction, with a slotted sublever and actuating link, that makes the type alignment easier to set.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Alignment of the types is hard to achieve with the usual type-bar and type-block construction.

**Solution:** Each type-bar has an improved type-bar and type block or head construction, with a slotted sublever and actuating link, that makes the type alignment easier to set.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar and type-head construction](../features/bar-typebar-construction.md)
