## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**How it works (reading):** One three-position shift lever turns a fork-shaped double-pawl cam that sets or clears a single column stop, returned to centre by leaf springs and a detent.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier column (tab) stop setting devices were laborious, required special levers, or disturbed neighbouring stops when deleting.

**Solution:** One three-position shift lever turns a fork-shaped double-pawl cam that sets or clears a single column stop, returned to centre by leaf springs and a detent.

**How it works:** Column stops slide in a U-rail and each has a nose. The lever (rest, set, delete) acts through links on an axle bearing the control cam, whose two pawl arms push the nose one way or the other. A wedge cam on the axle with two leaf springs and a spring detent with roller returns and holds the centre position.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** column stop; U-rail; control cam; shift lever; angle lever; leaf spring; detent pawl
