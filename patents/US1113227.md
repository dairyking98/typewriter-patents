## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing machines need a reliable tally-strip feed that follows the platen both ways, quick roll changing and a paper gage that does not block the operator's view.

**Solution:** Gear the tally-strip winding spool to the platen through a clutch, use retractable gudgeons for easy roll change, and a shiftable paper-arresting gage tied to the pressure-roll release key.

**How it works:** Platen rotation winds the tally strip forward and unwinds it backward through gears and an interposed clutch that lets platen and spool turn independently. The roll frame has retractable gudgeons, one rotatable, for inserting rolls. A paper gage with leading and side edge stops lies behind the tablet and swings out when the pressure roll release key operates, and a lever shifts it toward or away from the printing line for ruled sheets. Contractile jaws clamp the gage on a guide rod to slide it along the platen.
