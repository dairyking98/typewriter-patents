## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix machine feed adjustment) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the improvement is to provide I means for readily changingthe measurements of feed movementby an adjustment of parts connected With an escapement.

**How it works (reading):** An escape-wheel on a sleeve carries interchangeable pinions that a rack on the carriage engages, with an adjustable rack and a spring for each size.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed measurement needs changing by easy adjustment of the escapement.

**Solution:** An escape-wheel on a sleeve carries interchangeable pinions that a rack on the carriage engages, with an adjustable rack and a spring for each size.
