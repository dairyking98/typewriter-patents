## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to paper-feeding devices for typewriting machines, and especially to means for feeding paper, cards, I etc, at the front side of the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A rod carrying the front paper guides is moved to the delivery side of the platen, clear of the work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front paper fingers and their rod interfere with the operator holding sheets down for erasures.

**Solution:** A rod carrying the front paper guides is moved to the delivery side of the platen, clear of the work.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
