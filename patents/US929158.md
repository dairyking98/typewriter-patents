## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier sheet-register and directrix arrangement is more complex than needed for adjusting and controlling the paper before writing.

**Solution:** A register tablet on a bar above the platen front is adjusted up and down and along the platen, with a directrix on a rock shaft tied to the pressure-roll release key so the stop and directrix engage when the rolls are released.
