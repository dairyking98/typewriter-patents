## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention are to lock the type bars from displacement to prevent them :trom becoming jammed together, to permit ready removal and replacement of the type bar supports, tovcause positive feeding of the paper carriage, and generally to simplify the various parts and reduce the number thereof.
