## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is an auxiliary line switch for typewriters, Calculating machines u.

**How it works (reading):** A roller or ball of a clamping lock sits in a recess formed by a concentric cut-out in the gear and a driver disk on the hand lever; the lock drives the gear in one turning direction and runs free in the other, so the lever stops precisely without clicking parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Auxiliary line-advance devices with pawls are noisy, and the hand lever must be held exactly at the end of the desired platen rotation.

**Solution:** A roller or ball of a clamping lock sits in a recess formed by a concentric cut-out in the gear and a driver disk on the hand lever; the lock drives the gear in one turning direction and runs free in the other, so the lever stops precisely without clicking parts.
