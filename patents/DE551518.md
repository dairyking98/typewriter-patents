## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-copy form tapes distort on cylinder typewriters because the platen moves with the type stop.

**Solution:** The platen is held stationary and separately from the stop mechanism, which moves along it in prismatic guides with rollers; the form tapes run from fixed roll holders over the platen.
