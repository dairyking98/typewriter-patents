## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, cheap device is wanted that lets anyone produce printed letters without a complicated typewriter; existing toy versions are crude.

**Solution:** A self-coiling spring steel type tape is pulled through a spaced carriage on a guide frame and struck with a thimble hammer worn on a finger.

**How it works:** A guide frame clamped to the writing pad carries a sprung slide. The type tape, worn by a ring on one hand and coiled in a spring housing, passes through the slide; pulling it to the wanted character selects it. An ink ribbon on two spools lies under the tape. A thimble-shaped hammer on the index finger strikes the character through the slide's opening, depressing the slide. On release a spring raises the slide and a second spring moves it right so a bolt enters the next notch, spacing the letter. The tape can be shifted sideways for upper or lower case, and the slide has a line scale.
