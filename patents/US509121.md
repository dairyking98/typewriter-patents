## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Not stated; the text is a fragment about a spacing attachment on the end of a Smith Premier feed cylinder.

**Solution:** A thumb-operated spacing arm mounted concentrically on the cylinder end, inside the spring space dog, that lets the cylinder be turned a different amount.

**How it works:** The attachment sits between the spring space dog and the cylinder end, concentric with the cylinder, and has an arm pressed by the operator's thumb. In its rest position it has no function and the dog engages a space tooth normally. Most of the specification, including the operating position, is missing from the OCR.
