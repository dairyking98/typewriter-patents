## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Standard typebar actions use intermediate sub-levers and long key levers, and the key travel is large.

**Solution:** Connect each typebar straight to its operating lever with cone-bearing hangers so little key movement gives a strong stroke and bars can be removed in pairs.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot on cone bearings, a cone on the hanger and a cone on a screw head, with hardened steel bushings in the bar, lapped for no play. A lead pellet under each screw locks it against loosening. Bars are mounted in pairs on separate hangers fixed to a support, and each connects to its operating lever by a straight connection with no sub-lever. A pair can be disconnected and removed without disturbing the others, and the operating levers can be shorter.
