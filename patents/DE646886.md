## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (electromagnet for switch positions) · **Subject (patent classes):** Armatures entering the winding

**How it works (reading):** Stacked magnets whose cores each stop at a precise position, so the stroke of earlier magnets is not added, giving fixed positions independent of voltage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-position electromagnets are tall, and their intermediate positions depend on voltage and load.

**Solution:** Stacked magnets whose cores each stop at a precise position, so the stroke of earlier magnets is not added, giving fixed positions independent of voltage.
