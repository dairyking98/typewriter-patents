## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbons must be put on and removed without the operator touching the inked ribbon with the hands.

**Solution:** Two stamped metal handle members with bent fingers grip the ribbon so it can be put on and taken off the machine without handling it directly.
