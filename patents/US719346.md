## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in a ribbonplate such as is ordinarily used on the Remington and all of that class of machines, in connection with means carried thereby for keeping the ribbons apart and preventing their twisting and means for quickly shifting and locking the ribbon-plate to and in either of its extreme positions.

**How it works (reading):** Two half-width ribbons side by side in one ribbon plate, shifted and locked to either extreme position.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A single wide ribbon cannot give two colours or record and copy inks quickly.

**Solution:** Two half-width ribbons side by side in one ribbon plate, shifted and locked to either extreme position.

**How it works:** A Remington-style ribbon plate keeps two narrow ribbons apart and prevents twisting. A quick shift moves the plate so either ribbon lies at the printing point and locks it there. Each ribbon is about half as wide, may be half again as long, and uses less material.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** ribbon plate; ribbon; ribbon guide; locking device

**Numbers:** ribbon width about half normal

**Improves on:**

- single wide Remington ribbon: two narrow ribbons side by side
