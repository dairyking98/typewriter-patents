## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing spaced words normally needs an extra space-key strike after each letter.

**Solution:** A controlling star-wheel on the escapement wheel and a detent roller let the stepping pawl skip every second tooth, switched on and off by one key.

**How it works:** A star-wheel fixed to the escapement wheel works against a detent roller on a three-armed lever. When set for spaced copy, the detent holds the oscillating, slidable stepping pawl so it engages only the second tooth after each stroke. A cam slide, moved by a single reversing key, puts the mechanism in or out of action. The star-wheel is adjustable relative to the escapement wheel so spaced copy starts correctly at once.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; star-wheel; detent roller; three-armed lever; stepping pawl; pawl carrier; cam slide; reversing key; spring

**Improves on:**

- earlier Wanderer stepping-pawl patent: star-wheel adjustable so spaced copy starts immediately
