## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing inachines, and has for its object to provide an improved ribbon operating and reversing mechanism which will be operated in conjunction with the mechanism which shifts the platen for upper or lower case printing.

## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal on the Underwood machine needs spring power to throw the reversing clutch, and a way to keep that spring set without an extra operator action.

**Solution:** The spring that throws the ribbon-reversing clutch is re-cocked by every platen shift-key stroke, and a latch holds it until the ribbon's end pin trips it.

**How it works:** A rotating shaft, driven by gearing from the carriage spring drum and always meshed through a grooved gear, can slide lengthwise. It carries two clutch sections, each of which engages a clutch section on one of two ribbon-spool shafts, so sliding it left or right drives one spool or the other. Each side has a spring-loaded rod that tends to throw the shaft; a gravity latch holds the rod compressed. When the ribbon end is near, a pin on the ribbon strikes a forked vibratory frame that lifts the latch on the side that is driving, releasing the spring and sliding the shaft to the other clutch. Each shift-key stroke raises a slide with a pivoted pawl that catches an angled lever on whichever side is relaxed and recompresses its spring until the latch drops, then the pawl swings out of the way. The shift linkage also locks the platen against vibration at both positions, and spools lock on their shafts with a spring latch that doubles as a crank.
