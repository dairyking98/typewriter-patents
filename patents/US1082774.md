## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**How it works (reading):** Motion of the platen or type bar is transmitted to one of two ribbon spools, and the drive switches spools at each travel limit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon must shift between each impression so inking stays even, and the reversing drive must switch at ribbon ends.

**Solution:** Motion of the platen or type bar is transmitted to one of two ribbon spools, and the drive switches spools at each travel limit.

**How it works:** Platen or type-bar motion drives the ribbon spool; a shift switches drive at the limits of ribbon travel.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
