## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typebar machines move a heavy full type-bar segment for shifting, risk capitals printing below the line, and let the index fingers of the two hands interfere.

**Solution:** Split the type-bar segment into two vertically sliding quadrants so the shifted weight is about half, and split the keyboard into separated left and right halves.

**How it works:** Two vertically slidable quadrants carry the typebars and replace the single semicircular segment, riding on a central guide post with stop bars and nuts limiting up and down travel. Links, rock arms and a rock shaft connect the quadrants to the shift mechanism. Auxiliary space bars run parallel to the main space bar outside the frame, a shift lock and a universal quadrant release bar are provided.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** typebar quadrant; central guide post; stop bar; rock arm; rock shaft; auxiliary space bar; main space bar; shift lock; release key

**Numbers:** 6 sheets of drawings; shifted weight about half of ordinary machine
