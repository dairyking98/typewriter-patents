## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Narrow paper or envelopes are passed around the platen unevenly, and the platen has to be lifted to straighten them.

**Solution:** A guide on the platen carrier that moves into position around the platen and feeds the paper straight without lifting the platen.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
