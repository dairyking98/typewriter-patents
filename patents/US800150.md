## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A Hammond for the blind should write the whole Braille alphabet from a small shuttle and few keys.

**Solution:** A shuttle with two duplicate series of points, swung left or right by keys on each side of the keyboard, prints each Braille letter as two halves with paper moving one space between.

**How it works:** The hammer face has six pits in two rows of three. The shuttle has on each side of centre a series of points or point groups. Striking a key on one side swings the shuttle toward the opposite side, bringing the left half of the letter to the first pit row, and the hammer impresses it. Letter-spacing moves the paper one block, moving it left to right (reverse of ordinary), then a key on the other side prints the second half at the second pit row. The paper is read from the pitted side.
