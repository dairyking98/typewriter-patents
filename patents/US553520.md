## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Drive control means for carriage movement

**In the patent’s own words:** The object being to provide an indicator whereby the position of any point in a line of writing may be accurately read or indicated when the platen is shifted or raised from the type to its inoperative position to bring the line of writing into position for reading the same in the manner common.

**How it works (reading):** An indicator arm shifts with the platen and paper scale but stays fixed along the line, so it reads the printing position in both printing and reading positions.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** With a shifting platen raised to read the writing, the operator cannot accurately read which point of the line the printing point is at.

**Solution:** An indicator arm shifts with the platen and paper scale but stays fixed along the line, so it reads the printing position in both printing and reading positions.

**How it works:** The platen is carried on a pivoted carrier that raises it to a reading position. A paper scale moves with the platen carrier. An indicator held against longitudinal movement relative to the machine frame has an arm that moves transversely with the scale, sliding relative to it along the line, so it always points to the printing point.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** frame; carriage; platen; platen carrier; paper scale; indicator arm; support rod; stop device; track rod
