## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines having in addition to the ordinary uniform feed or spacing mechanism for the paper-carriage a supplemental variable spacing mechanism which enables the carriage to skip a predetermined number of single spaces for tabulating'purposes or for printing words or phrases as well as single-space characters.

**How it works (reading):** A simple governor or retarder for the carriage placed where it does not interfere with other parts.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** When a tabulating carriage skipping many spaces is stopped by a rigid stop, the impact jars the machine.

**Solution:** A simple governor or retarder for the carriage placed where it does not interfere with other parts.

**How it works:** The carriage is driven by a feed spring and escapement through a shaft with an escapement wheel and a pinion engaging a rack on the carriage. Auxiliary push-keys act through bell-crank levers and a universal frame to lift the rack out of mesh, a lock holds it up during travel, and stop and trip devices release it. The governor checks the carriage speed under the spring while it is free of the escapement; its internals are cut off in the opening.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; pinion; gear-rack; auxiliary push-key; bell-crank lever; universal frame; lock; governor; spring drum
