## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen and the carriage track did not keep the correct relative plane after regrinding or with thick work sheets.

**Solution:** Means acting on the platen or the tracks adjust their relative height and horizontal position so the writing surface and printing mechanism sit in the proper planes.

**Features:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
