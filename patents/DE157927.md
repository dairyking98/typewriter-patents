## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a line adjusting device for the paper roll of typewriters of the kind in which the switching of the roller once according to the line spacing and also can be fully released so that they can be set at any point can be.

**How it works (reading):** A switch-off device acts on the spring so that it temporarily removes the spring pressure on the locking roller or stumbling block, letting the paper roller turn freely while the brake stays engaged.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** In known paper-roller arrangements the locking roller and drag shoe share one spring, so disengaging one also releases the other and the roller cannot be freed independently.

**Solution:** A switch-off device acts on the spring so that it temporarily removes the spring pressure on the locking roller or stumbling block, letting the paper roller turn freely while the brake stays engaged.
