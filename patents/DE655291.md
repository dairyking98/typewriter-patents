## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The holding piece rests on the platen with spring coupling means, so it stays on the roller and is brought into or out of use position by turning the roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bracket-type auxiliary ribbon holders must be inserted and removed by hand between the sheets, which wrinkles or soils the paper and damages the holder when it falls.

**Solution:** The holding piece rests on the platen with spring coupling means, so it stays on the roller and is brought into or out of use position by turning the roller.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
