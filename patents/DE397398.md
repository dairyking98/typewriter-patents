## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Radially arranged keys in cash registers curve the key-top surface, so the lower rows are hard to see and the finger angle causes fatigue.

**Solution:** Key stems increase in length and angle down the rows so the key tops lie on a flat S-shaped surface, which is more visible to the cashier and easier to press.
