## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter or calculating machine needs a compact travelling case that can be carried by hand and then serve as a rest in use.

**Solution:** A case with a roll top on a spring roller and hinged end plates; closed it is carried, opened the folded parts form a desk-like base for the machine.
