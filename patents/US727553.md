## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple escapement is needed that can space one tooth or more of the feed rack, and adjustable for different feeds.

**Solution:** A rocking frame with two feed-pawls on pivot screws, with an adjustable stop on the pawl-carrier, so the feed member advances one or more teeth per stroke.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** rocking frame; feed-pawls; rack 7; adjustable stop
