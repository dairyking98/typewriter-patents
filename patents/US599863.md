## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements on Oliver's earlier machine (US562337) with downward-striking typebars over a platen.

**Solution:** Revised carriage with platen shifting, adjustable line feed, feed rollers, ribbon throw and letter spacing.

**How it works:** Typebars swing on horizontal axes above the platen and strike downward. The platen is carried in an endwise-moving carriage and is shifted by a mechanism with a holding pawl, line feed length is adjustable, and paper-feed rollers on arms support the paper. A universal bar drives the letter spacing and ribbon throw.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** platen; carriage; typebar; universal bar; ribbon throw; paper roller arm; platen-holding pawl; base plate

**Improves on:**

- [US562337](../patents/US562337.md): improved carriage and shift
