## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The patent aims at an improved type action and key action for a front-strike typebar machine.

**Solution:** Pivot the typebars on segment hangers with conical-point screws, in groups, with link-driven bars and a specially arranged key lever system.

**How it works:** A segment holds 42 typebars, each with a type arm, pivot axle with conical end depressions, and crank arm. Cone-ended pivot screws seat in sheet-metal hangers fixed to the segment, groups of four bars sharing a pair of hangers. A link from each crank arm is driven by the key lever to throw the bar to the front of the platen. Bars on each half are mirrored so crank arms lie toward the middle.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** typebar; segment; hanger; pivot screw; crank arm; link; key lever; platen; escapement wheel

**Numbers:** 42 type bars; 4 groups of 4 bars plus 5 at end
