## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for attaching the types to the type levers of Typewriters in which the type levers are stored in a segment and individually in a position can be swiveled in, in which the types are attached.

**How it works (reading):** A fixture holds the type lever segment on a base; the lever is pivoted in and pressed against its bearing, and the type is held on a die with an alignment notch by electromagnet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Types fixed off the machine may not sit in the same position as in use.

**Solution:** A fixture holds the type lever segment on a base; the lever is pivoted in and pressed against its bearing, and the type is held on a die with an alignment notch by electromagnet.
