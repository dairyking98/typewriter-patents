## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making photographic line negatives or positives of equal length for printing sets is complicated and unreliable because of the line exclusion problem.

**Solution:** A film strip is exposed through a replaceable stencil wheel of characters, developed and fixed, then notched between words and at line ends so the strip can be folded and stretched to a prescribed line length.
