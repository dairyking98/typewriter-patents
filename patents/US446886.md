## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Remington the operator must shift the ribbon-drive shaft by hand to reverse the ribbon; if forgotten the ribbon stops and the type strikes one spot, giving indistinct printing.

**Solution:** The spring-driven carriage shaft is reciprocated automatically by a worm, toothed cam wheel and springs so its gears engage the two ribbon spools alternately.

**How it works:** The sliding shaft is turned one way, step by step, by the carriage's spring-actuated let-off wheel. A worm on it drives a toothed cam wheel on a bracket; an arm pivoted on the shaft carries a pin riding that cam. As the cam turns it winds springs against collars on the shaft, and at predetermined points the springs snap the shaft longitudinally so a gear meets the other spool arbor's gear. One spool is then positively driven while the other is turned by the stress of the ribbon, and the ribbon reverses automatically.
