**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on a switching device for typewriters with power drive.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On power shift the case change was slow so both upper and lower case parts printed.

**Solution:** A spring moves the changeover frame to the shift position while the power drive returns it, so it snaps back to the basic position instantly.
