## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon reel built from two symmetrical rotatable sheets with differently shaped central and eccentric holes and arc recesses, locked into the spool carrier in positions that suit about half of the common 13 mm ribbon machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbon spools differ by make, and the DIN standard reel fits only a small group of machines.

**Solution:** A ribbon reel built from two symmetrical rotatable sheets with differently shaped central and eccentric holes and arc recesses, locked into the spool carrier in positions that suit about half of the common 13 mm ribbon machines.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [ELEM:hub-keying](../features/elem-hub-keying.md)

**Numbers:** 13 mm ribbon; about 165 machine types; about half of these machines fitted
