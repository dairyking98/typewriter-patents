## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**How it works (reading):** The type cushion is an elastic rubber strip with a trapezoidal recess that clamps onto its bracket, with projections and longitudinal grooves that create an air gap for damping and catch dust for easy removal.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Felt type cushions sag, need special fasteners, and let the type levers bounce and clatter; dust in the groove is hard to clear.

**Solution:** The type cushion is an elastic rubber strip with a trapezoidal recess that clamps onto its bracket, with projections and longitudinal grooves that create an air gap for damping and catch dust for easy removal.
