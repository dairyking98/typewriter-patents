## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen scale on a shifting-platen machine sits far from the print line because the platen moves, so paper adjustment is slower and less accurate.

**Solution:** Carry the scale plate on the carriage truck, pivoted and spring-pressed against the platen, independent of the platen frame.

**How it works:** A bent sheet-metal scale plate with indices at letter-space intervals has a pocket at its bottom holding two soldered spindles. The spindles pivot on the ends of adjusting screws held in slotted bearings in the carriage truck, locked by nuts. A coiled spring presses the upper edge against the platen, so the scale keeps contact in both cases. The screws adjust the scale plate lengthwise and up and down, and shifting the platen frame up gives clearance for lower-case descenders when upper case prints.

**Parts:** scale plate; index; spindle; adjusting screw; slotted bearing; nut; coiled spring; carriage truck; platen frame; platen
