## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a single-ratchet feed with several selectable character spacings, the feed pawl's engagement with the wheel can be upset by uneven typing, stop-pawl reaction or outside shock, so fast typing gives inaccurate spacing.

**Solution:** A pivoted feed-pawl lever with toggle links on its far end: the links buckle and straighten with the typing stroke, keeping the pawl clear on the forward stroke and locking it into the wheel (while releasing the stop pawl) at the limit of travel.

**How it works:** A feed ratchet wheel sits on the platen shaft, and a rocking plate linked to the print mechanism carries a pawl lever pivoted at its middle, with two toggle links on the end opposite the pawl. At rest the links, bent by resting on a change arm, hold the feed pawl clear while a spring-loaded stop pawl holds the wheel. On the forward typing stroke the links strike an end stop and straighten, which engages the feed pawl and presses down the stop member to release the stop pawl; the straightened toggle locks the feed pawl in so no shock can separate it. On the return stroke a spring draws the rocking plate back and the pawl turns the wheel; the links then strike the change arm tip, buckle again, lift the feed pawl and let the stop pawl re-engage. Turning a changer moves the change arm to alter the feed interval.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter feed device. Nature and object of the invention. This invention is an addition to Patent No. 104642 (Patent Application No. 7830 of 1932; Examined Publication No. 3321 of 1933). In feeding the character spacing in several sizes with a single ratchet wheel: the feed pawl that engages the wheel is always kept clear of the wheel during the forward motion of the typing stroke; at the limit of that motion, that is when the forward stroke reaches its end, the pawl is engaged with the feed wheel and at the same time the stop pawl is released from the wheel; after that, as the typing action turns to its return, the character-spacing feed begins. The middle of the lever carrying the feed pawl is supported on a pivot, two toggle links are joined to it on the side opposite the pawl, and the bending and straightening of these links, following the typing action, engage and release the feed pawl and the feed wheel.
