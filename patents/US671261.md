## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A fixed holding dog stays engaged too long after impact and slows the feed; bevel landings cause skipping and heavy touch.

**Solution:** A pivoted spring holding dog with step-like rack-tooth stops and a spring feed dog on one reciprocating carrier, set to let the rack drop slightly between them.

**How it works:** A reciprocating arm carries a spring-pressed holding dog with stepped stops and a feed dog, both moving along the carrier path. The dogs coact with rack teeth; the working faces allow a slight forward drop when the rack passes from feed dog to holding dog. A stop sets their spacing.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)

**Parts:** rack; reciprocating arm; dog carrier; holding dog; feed dog; stop

**Improves on:**

- fixed-dog and spacing-dog escapement: stepped dog removes skipping and heavy touch
