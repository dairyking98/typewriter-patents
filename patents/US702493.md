## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Forms and papers get mixed and removed when they are carried to a typewriter on a desk.

**Solution:** The typewriter frame is moved on a desk over fixed platens sunk flush into the desk at permanent stations, so each form is typed at its own location and stays there.
