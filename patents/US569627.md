## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriting in bound books or on loose pages needs a machine that travels over the page.

**Solution:** A machine runs on tracks resting on the page above a rubber-faced printing plate placed under the leaf.

**How it works:** Tracks lie on the sheet or page, one fixed to the plate with clips and one loose for width adjustment. A frame with front and rear bars has seats for different track intervals and carries a type-bar-bearing ring, with a gage consisting of a rock-shaft and spring-held gage plate. Typeheads on a shifting arm print upper or lower case. Ribbon guides mount on the ring.

**Features:** [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)

**Parts:** track; printing plate; clip; frame; front bar; rear bar; rock-shaft; gage plate; spring; type-bar ring; type-head; shifting arm; ribbon guide

**Improves on:**

- Fisher's earlier applications: adds an adjustable track and gage to the travelling machine
