## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon feed and typebar brackets of a visible-writing typebar machine need to be cheaper to manufacture.

**Solution:** Die-stamped sheet-metal ribbon standards oscillated by the universal bail lift the ribbon over the printing point, with a pawl-fed two-spool ribbon and an improved typebar bracket.

**How it works:** A universal bail under the key levers carries a ribbon standard on each side; each key depression oscillates the table end of each standard, throwing the ribbon between type and platen just before impact and withdrawing it so the impression is visible. Standards are cut and folded from sheet metal, punched with holes, slots and a ribbon groove, and carry a resilient ribbon guard that clasps the ribbon and protects the paper from smearing. Two ribbon spools with ratchets are fed step by step by sheet-metal pawls, one winding while the other pays out, and the roles change automatically at the ribbon end.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** key lever; universal bail; ribbon standard; ribbon guard; ribbon spool; ratchet; pawl; typebar bracket; platen

**Improves on:**

- Spiro Serial No. 319,736: cheaper die-made standards and bracket
