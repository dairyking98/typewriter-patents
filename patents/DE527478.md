## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and manually actuated

**How it works (reading):** The carriage lock button is moved to the keyboard area, where it is always reachable, and it locks the carriage in the central position while releasing the rack drive.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage lock button sits on the carriage, so it moves away and is hard to reach when the carriage is shifted to one side.

**Solution:** The carriage lock button is moved to the keyboard area, where it is always reachable, and it locks the carriage in the central position while releasing the rack drive.
