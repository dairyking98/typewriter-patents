## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Autographic registers or like manifolding apparatus using movable strips or webs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a carbon-sheet holder and lifter or separator,and particularly to devices located at the front end -of the track-frame and edge of the platen for clamping and holding the carbon-sheet and for automatically raising and separating it from the work on the platen in a flat-platen typewriting machine.

**How it works (reading):** A clamp at the front edge of the track-frame holds the carbon sheet, and linked connecting devices automatically lift and separate it from the work on the platen when the operator wants to set up the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator had to handle the carbon sheet by hand when inserting or removing work on a flat-platen machine, soiling the fingers and disturbing the carbon.

**Solution:** A clamp at the front edge of the track-frame holds the carbon sheet, and linked connecting devices automatically lift and separate it from the work on the platen when the operator wants to set up the paper.
