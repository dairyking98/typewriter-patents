## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adding and subtracting on a typewriter-linked machine has too many parts and needs fractional values.

**Solution:** A totalizing carriage moves pawls under the control of tripper set by figure keys; the paper carriage position selects the denomination, and keys lock until the total is taken.
