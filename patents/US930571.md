## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage travel needs retarding on a Monarch type of machine, with the device usable on under-stroke and visible-writing machines.

**Solution:** A hollow device on the top plate holds a cooperating rotary device; the carriage-retarding apparatus is fixed near the rear edge of the top plate and damps the spring-driven carriage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
