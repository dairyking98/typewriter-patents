## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writers, and has for its object to simplify the construction and produce a machine practically noiseless in use and one in which the writing-surface I5 is visible to the operator.

**How it works (reading):** The type-bars are checked a short distance from the paper by a resilient check-action, and a hammer driven by the momentum of the key lever then strikes the checked type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are noisy and the writing surface is hidden, because the type-bars strike the paper directly at full speed.

**Solution:** The type-bars are checked a short distance from the paper by a resilient check-action, and a hammer driven by the momentum of the key lever then strikes the checked type.
