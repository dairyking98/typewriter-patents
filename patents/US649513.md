## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to attachments for type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary ink-ribbon attachment fitted to the paper-feed carriage, with its own ribbon and feed roll, so that a second ribbon strikes the paper alongside the regular ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two or more original copies cannot easily be made at once on a standard typewriter without a separate ribbon arrangement.

**Solution:** An auxiliary ink-ribbon attachment fitted to the paper-feed carriage, with its own ribbon and feed roll, so that a second ribbon strikes the paper alongside the regular ribbon.
