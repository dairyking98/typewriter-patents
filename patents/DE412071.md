## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculators converted to a new currency still print and add unwanted decimal places because the unused type series cannot be turned off.

**Solution:** Finger-shaped or tongue-shaped blockers, fitted to the hood or ribbon guide and slid over selected type carriers, hold those carriers down so that the matching keys do nothing in printing or addition.
