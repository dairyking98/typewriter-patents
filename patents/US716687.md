## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper must be held firmly against the cylindrical rotary platen during printing.

**Solution:** A paper-holding mechanism on the carriage, with a scale blade, a supporting bar and rollers that press the paper against the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** platen; scale blade; rollers; carriage end pieces
