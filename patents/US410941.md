## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A very simple and cheap toy typewriter for the amusement and instruction of children.

**Solution:** A stamped sheet-metal wheel carrying both type rim and indicator rim is stepped by a key through a duplex ratchet and held by an opposing pawl while a hammer key prints and feeds the paper strip.

**How it works:** A type wheel and an indicator (letter) wheel are stamped from one piece of sheet metal with a hub and a duplex ratchet in the disk, and turn loosely on a stationary wooden shaft. Pressing the front of a key lever drives a spring pawl on the ratchet to step the wheel, while a spring holding pawl on the other side of the ratchet locks the wheel in position. The indicator letters show through a sight opening in the case, in an order that registers with the hammer when the matching type is over the hammer. A separate impression key swings a hammer that presses the paper strip against the type, which is inked by a roller (or carbon paper). The same key stroke pushes a pawl on a ratchet feed roll to advance the paper strip just before the impression, and the second roll has blotting paper to take surplus ink.
