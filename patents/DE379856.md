## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rail-car counter driven directly by a lever at the rail gives violent jolts to the ratchet and must sit close to the lever.

**Solution:** The lever pulls a cable that moves a sledge carrying a pawl, cushioned by a spring, so the counter can be placed at any distance and the ratchet moves softly.
