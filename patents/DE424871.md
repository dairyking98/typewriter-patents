## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**How it works (reading):** A pivoted link with two teeth of finer pitch than the camshaft always engages at the same relative position, giving a fixed run-up of about 1 mm.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Power-driven typebars strike unevenly because the coupling engages the cam at varying positions.

**Solution:** A pivoted link with two teeth of finer pitch than the camshaft always engages at the same relative position, giving a fixed run-up of about 1 mm.

**How it works:** The key lever lowers a pull lever through a slotted driver lever until a tooth of the link meets the continuously rotating camshaft. A stop rail and a pin make the link and pull lever a rigid whole, with about 1 mm play before the second tooth is gripped. The shaft then pulls the intermediate lever and throws the typebar; further variants are shown.
