## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Table-writing devices needed easily made and arranged stop parts for the paper carriage.

**Solution:** Adjustable tabs on the paper carriage engage tooth gaps in a rack and stop against two-armed stop levers that move in and out of their path, with sectors and inclined surfaces setting the stops across the type width.
