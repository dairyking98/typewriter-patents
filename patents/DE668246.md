## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a low-noise platen for typewriters, Typing calculators, adding machines and the like, in which the upper layer of the roller forming rubber on a core up to the thickness of the normal platen diameter is vulcanized.

**How it works (reading):** The platen core is a small-diameter metal tube widened at its ends, and the rubber layer is vulcanised over the thinner middle, which gives a thicker rubber layer on the writing area and room for the clutch inside the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A thick rubber layer on a platen to cut impact noise leaves no room inside the platen for the line-ratchet clutch, so the clutch sits outside and widens the machine.

**Solution:** The platen core is a small-diameter metal tube widened at its ends, and the rubber layer is vulcanised over the thinner middle, which gives a thicker rubber layer on the writing area and room for the clutch inside the platen.
