## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms for ensuring maximum life of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A Monarch-type typewriter in which the ribbon vibrator is actuated by key-lever linkages and novel controlling devices. Type bars link to the key sub-levers and are carried on a shiftable segment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator needs improved controlling devices (the OCR is too poor to state the exact problem).

**Solution:** A Monarch-type typewriter in which the ribbon vibrator is actuated by key-lever linkages and novel controlling devices. Type bars link to the key sub-levers and are carried on a shiftable segment.

**How it works:** Each key lever moves a sub-lever that links to a type bar on a shiftable segment; the ribbon vibrator is driven by an actuating means with the new controlling devices.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** key levers; sub-levers; type bars; shiftable segment; ribbon vibrator; fixed abutment
