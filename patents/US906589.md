**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the pa er-feeding rolls of typewriting machines, ant its object is to rovide simplified and improved means for re easing from the platen the usual springpressed rolls which run thereon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The spring-pressed paper-feed rolls must be cast off the platen so the operator can adjust paper, and the usual release is awkward.

**Solution:** Releasing levers on a rod of the platen frame, moved by a key-operated cam shaft, carry all the roll shafts away from the platen together.
