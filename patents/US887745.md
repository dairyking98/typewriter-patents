## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Key and typebar linkage should give a uniform, light, elastic and quick touch.

**Solution:** An intermediate lever between key lever and typebar engages the bar first low on a shoulder (high leverage), then nearer the pivot (speed), then through a link pulling a tail stud, with a return shoulder that speeds the bar home.

**How it works:** The key lever is pushed up by a spring finger and its headed stud works in a slot of a pivoted intermediate lever. Early key travel presses the intermediate lever on a shoulder well below the typebar pivot, giving large leverage. Further travel shifts engagement to a point nearer the pivot so the bar accelerates, then a link takes up a stud on the bar's tail for a positive pull. Near printing the bar trips the universal bar which operates carriage feed and ribbon shift, and on release a shoulder on the intermediate lever strikes a tip on the tail to speed the return.
