## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Foldable typewriters

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a collapsible Typewriter with support bars on the sides of the paper slide support so that the slide can be raised and lowered.

**How it works (reading):** The slide support rods are hinged so that their connection points on the slide carrier lie farther apart than those on the frame; the slide and its arms then fold behind the frame, and a rod locks the keys when the arms are swung down.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper slide of a typewriter sticks out behind the frame, so the machine takes up a lot of space when collapsed.

**Solution:** The slide support rods are hinged so that their connection points on the slide carrier lie farther apart than those on the frame; the slide and its arms then fold behind the frame, and a rod locks the keys when the arms are swung down.
