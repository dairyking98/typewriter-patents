## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device to inhibit the carriage on typewriters. e Typewriters are known in which the letter switch mechanism is removable. If the device is established in a known manner that the carriage drive spring is also removed from the machine with the switching mechanism, the carriage loses its driving force when it is removed and stops. But if, as is also known, only the rear derailleur is removed while the car remains under the influence of its drive (tension) spring, it is released from its inhibition by removing the rear derailleur and runs unbraked to its end position. With this type of mini machine, special care should be exercised and the car either held or moved into the end position beforehand when the rear derailleur is to be removed. If this caution is neglected, then, when the car is running rapidly, damage due to the hard hitting against the mechanism limiting the extreme end position of the car must be expected.
