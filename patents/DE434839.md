## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**How it works (reading):** A mechanical link is set up between removing the switching mechanism and a locking member, so the carriage is inhibited by a pawl in the rack or by friction on the spring drum whenever the mechanism is removed; the inhibitor is released only when the mechanism is properly engaged.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the rear derailleur is removed while the carriage is still under spring tension, the carriage runs unbraked to its end stop and is damaged.

**Solution:** A mechanical link is set up between removing the switching mechanism and a locking member, so the carriage is inhibited by a pawl in the rack or by friction on the spring drum whenever the mechanism is removed; the inhibitor is released only when the mechanism is properly engaged.

If the device is established in a known manner that the carriage drive spring is also removed from the machine with the switching mechanism, the carriage loses its driving force when it is removed and stops. With this type of mini machine, special care should be exercised and the car either held or moved into the end position beforehand when the rear derailleur is to be removed.
