## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** In Underwood's two-ribbon machine the idle ribbon vibrator could move when disconnected.

**Solution:** A single finger on the shiftable connector yoke locks whichever ribbon vibrating lever is disconnected from the universal bar.

**How it works:** Two ribbons of different colour or quality, each with its own spools and vibrator, sit in front of the platen, the vibrators on levers carried on a bracket on the platen shift rail. A key-shifted yoke with slotted ears couples one lever's wrist to the universal bar frame. The new stop finger moves with the yoke and locks the other lever, so only one ribbon is active.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; lever; bracket; yoke; stop finger; universal bar frame; ribbon spool
