> Description Translated from German Power operated typewriter switching device. The invention relates to power-operated switching devices for the paper carriage of typewriters and writing office machines with a single-speed coupled lift disc and a lifting linkage that works together and is locked when the upper level is reached, and it sets itself the task of creating a device that the paper carriage lifts gently with little effort and also a dampened, but immediate relapse of the paper sledge released from its elevated position guaranteed in the initial position. According to the invention this is achieved by using known locking parts for the Lifting rods the lifting disc as a one-sided rising, one full turn for lifting of the paper carriage until the locking parts engage or the free falling back of the paper carriage into the starting position enabling cam disk is formed and for the lifting rod a lifting, but especially the falling back of the paper slide into a slightly inclined one Slideway rolling motion converting intermediate member is provided.
