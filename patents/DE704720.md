**How it works (reading):** A one-sided rising cam disc with locking parts lifts the carriage, and an intermediate member converts the lift into a rolling motion down an inclined slideway on return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Power carriage lift should be gentle, yet the carriage should drop back immediately and damped.

**Solution:** A one-sided rising cam disc with locking parts lifts the carriage, and an intermediate member converts the lift into a rolling motion down an inclined slideway on return.
