## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (music note transcribing device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**How it works (reading):** Notation symbols are placed on a common field on a type cylinder, and a hand pointer stick selects a symbol so the carriage positions the copy point for each note.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand-copied music notation is slow, irregular and hard to read, and free carriage movement on a typewriter gives no positional control.

**Solution:** Notation symbols are placed on a common field on a type cylinder, and a hand pointer stick selects a symbol so the carriage positions the copy point for each note.
