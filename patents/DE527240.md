**How it works (reading):** Each writing plate has its own cutter bar and front and rear clamps, operated by a foot lever system under the plate, with shared bearing shafts and a separate carbon paper holder groove per plate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In known multi-plate book typewriters the writing plates are not independent of one another.

**Solution:** Each writing plate has its own cutter bar and front and rear clamps, operated by a foot lever system under the plate, with shared bearing shafts and a separate carbon paper holder groove per plate.
