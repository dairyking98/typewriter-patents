## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stereotype matrices in paper) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes; Plural tools selectively engageable with single drive

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to certain novel improvements inthe art of producing matrices in paper or.

**How it works (reading):** An oscillating segment carries die letters on an arc, and a pointer on it is moved over a fixed letter arc on the frame; when the pointer registers with a letter, the die beneath a vertical plunger is struck into the paper on an adjustable bed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stereotype matrices are made by setting type, locking it in a form, and impressing it into paper by hand, and then distributing the type is laborious.

**Solution:** An oscillating segment carries die letters on an arc, and a pointer on it is moved over a fixed letter arc on the frame; when the pointer registers with a letter, the die beneath a vertical plunger is struck into the paper on an adjustable bed.
