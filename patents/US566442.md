## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists are slower than the machine; earlier ways to print two letters at once (word characters, double-letter types, paired type forms, two bar sets) greatly complicate the machine.

**Solution:** Let the operator make two letters simultaneously by a new principle that adds little machinery (details lie beyond the opening text).

**How it works:** The opening text only states the object: printing two letters at the same time on a typewriter, type-setting machine or similar instrument, on a different principle from earlier schemes. It notes that good machines can run at 150 to 200 words a minute and that the operator is the limit. The actual mechanism is in the truncated portion (16 sheets of drawings), so it cannot be described here.

**Numbers:** 150-200 words a minute machine capability

**Improves on:**

- [US457333](../patents/US457333.md): avoids word characters on the type form
- [US531904](../patents/US531904.md): avoids double-letter characters
- [US466490](../patents/US466490.md): avoids cooperating type forms at adjacent print points
- [US297086](../patents/US297086.md): avoids two sets of typebars with adjacent print points
