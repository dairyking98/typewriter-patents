## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-space wheel needs a binding and locking device that is convenient to use and keeps the finger wheel from accidental backward movement.

**Solution:** A drum fixed to the line-space wheel encloses a cap-shaped finger wheel; a lever pivoted on a collar on the axle presses a split ring to bite the drum, binding the line-space wheel and locking the finger wheel.
