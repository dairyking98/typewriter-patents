## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon winding from one spool to the other driven by the carriage, with automatic reversal of the ribbon feed and means to choose between ribbon colours.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon feeds unevenly and reversal at exhaustion needs reliable automatic operation; multi-colour ribbons need selection.

**Solution:** Ribbon winding from one spool to the other driven by the carriage, with automatic reversal of the ribbon feed and means to choose between ribbon colours.

- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
