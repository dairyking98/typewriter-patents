## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating mechanisms need to be simple, compact and inexpensive, and attachable to machines already built or in manufacture.

**Solution:** Denomination keys on arms from the carriage bar depress denomination stops into engagement with column stops on the frame, and a release bar along the keys releases the carriage, so one selected key stops it at its denomination.
