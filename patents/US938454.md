## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Blank spaces between carbon entries of successive bills on a record sheet need a single simple action, improving on earlier multi-motion crank mechanisms.

**Solution:** A crank loose on the platen axle carries a pawl that is spring-held out of a notched wheel on the platen; a single swing throws the pawl into engagement so the platen turns back a short distance and then forward a longer one, leaving a space between bills.
