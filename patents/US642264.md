## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Justifying a line of type by hand requires the operator to count units and add extra space between words until the line is full, which is slow and error-prone.

**Solution:** Copy is typed on a unit-system typewriter that spaces differentially, with a unit count marked at the line end; the operator then adds the indicated extra units evenly across the word spaces when setting the line.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
