## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention has reference to that class of type-writers in which a downwardly-acting writing mechanism is mounted to move laterally and longitudinally over a flat horizontal platen employed to sustain paper on which the writing is to be effected.
