## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Creation of print templates. Addendum to patent 404949. Patented in the German Empire on January 20, 1922. Longest duration: December 12, 1939. The invention relates to the production of templates for the photomechanical production of printing forms according to patent 404949 and consists in particular in the fact that the types of typewriter with the aid of which the printing templates according to the main patent are written with rubbed fat colors be colored. According to the main patent, in particular a precisely excluded typesetting should be generated in an expedient manner. According to the present invention, it should be achieved with this excluded sentence structure that the outlines of the individual letters are completely sharp. This is extremely important for the use of print templates, since the typeface is in any case exposed to the risk of losing its sharpness during photomechanical reproduction. According to the invention, a typewriter is advantageously used, the types of which are colored by paint rollers which can be detached from a fixed inking unit and pivoted or guided over the types.
