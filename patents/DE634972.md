## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an automatic switching device for moving the ribbon lengthways on typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A drive cross shaft pivots at the middle so one end engages a spool; a pawl on the ribbon-carrying screw moves a toothed ratchet on the shaft to swing it out and reverse, with depressions on a spring plate holding the shaft in each position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing devices for portable typewriters are heavy and have too many parts for the little space available.

**Solution:** A drive cross shaft pivots at the middle so one end engages a spool; a pawl on the ribbon-carrying screw moves a toothed ratchet on the shaft to swing it out and reverse, with depressions on a spring plate holding the shaft in each position.

**Features:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** drive cross shaft; ratchet wheels; pawl; spring-loaded plate with depressions
