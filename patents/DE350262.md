## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Preventing rebound or clash of levers or type members

**How it works (reading):** A narrow arched rail buffer stop is fixed at both ends over thin sound-absorbing spacers on the bearing segment, so it does not touch the bearing directly; it is easily replaced and adjusted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bump stops on type lever bearings are noisy, and rubber stops soften, so type bounce and strike through the paper.

**Solution:** A narrow arched rail buffer stop is fixed at both ends over thin sound-absorbing spacers on the bearing segment, so it does not touch the bearing directly; it is easily replaced and adjusted.
