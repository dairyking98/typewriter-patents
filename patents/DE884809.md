## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter covers and the type lever layout leave no room between keyboard and platen for a shorthand notebook, and a notebook there is unsteady and blocks the view.

**Solution:** The machine casing behind and above the top row of keys is shaped as an inclined support surface, clear of the writing point and steady against type impact, to hold the notebook.
