## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A toggle rod links each intermediate lever to its type lever; the toggle rods swing in planes parallel to the median plane, so motion transfers under more favourable angles, and the type lever segment can be raised or lowered for case switching.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Direct linkage from intermediate levers to type levers that swing in different planes creates clamping and friction resistance.

**Solution:** A toggle rod links each intermediate lever to its type lever; the toggle rods swing in planes parallel to the median plane, so motion transfers under more favourable angles, and the type lever segment can be raised or lowered for case switching.
