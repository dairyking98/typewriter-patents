## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a bar-lock typebar machine the case shift must move the carriage beneath the type while the escapement and ribbon still work accurately in either shift position.

**Solution:** Mount the paper carriage on an oscillating cradle pivoted on the base, with the feed rack in the vertical plane of the cradle pivot so the escapement is unaffected by the shift, and hang the ribbon arms on the cradle.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on ball races in a cradle pivoted at its lower ends. A shift key fixed to the cradle rocks it forward so the platen sits under the capital characters; a spring on the opposite end restores it when the key is released. A cam lever with an eccentric projection can hold the shift key down as a shift lock. The feed rack and the escapement wheel and dog lie beneath, in the plane of the cradle pivot, so rocking barely moves the rack. Ribbon arms pivoted on the cradle are linked to the universal bail so each key or space bar throws the ribbon over the platen and also oscillates the escapement dog.
