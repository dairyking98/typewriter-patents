## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal objects of this invention are to reduce the dimensions and weight of its actuating devices so as to lessen the inertia thereof, thereby enabling greater speed of the machine with a lighter and easier touch;

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Universal bars in front-strike machines are heavy, so inertia limits speed and touch.

**Solution:** A short sectional universal bar rock shaft with separate light actuators, each controlled by only some typebar actions.

**How it works:** A horizontal rock shaft acts as universal bar behind the typebar segment, operating the carriage escapement and ribbon vibrator. Separate independent actuators, each driven by part of the typebar actions, rock it, reducing moving weight. Bearing brackets of bent sheet-metal stampings hang from the top plate. Auxiliary lever, link, pinion and rack bar form a modification.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
