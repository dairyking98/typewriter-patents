## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** With a shift carriage raised by a shift key, quick writing makes the carriage fly up past its limit, spoiling alignment.

**Solution:** A second shift-key lever, which also carries the first lever down, locks the vertical frame with a lever against a nose at its upper limit.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on ball-supported rails fixed to a vertical frame guided by pins in vertical slots and linked to a rocking frame on a shaft, which is made in one piece with the first shift-key lever. Pressing the first lever raises frame, carriage and platen. The second shift-key lever is beside it, with an arm under the first so it pulls the first down too, and also turns a lever on a shaft whose locking lever strikes a nose on the vertical frame and stops it. This prevents the vibration and overshoot.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; vertical frame; rails; balls; rocking frame; shift-key lever; second shift-key lever; locking lever; nose; shaft
