## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a flat-platen typewriter, manifolding with several long paper strips or a folded manifold strip is awkward to guide and to separate.

**Solution:** Guides the paper over the stationary platen, holds transfer sheets between the plies, and feeds the paper independently of the transfer means so the printed portion is displaced from the platen and can be severed.

**How it works:** Paper is guided over the flat platen; transfer elements sit between the sheets; a separate feed moves the printed section off the platen, where a severing means cuts it.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)
