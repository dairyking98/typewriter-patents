## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars and hangers take much lateral space, and dust gets into their bearings.

**Solution:** Each type-bar vibrates on ball-bearings in a hanger that takes little space sideways, so many bars can be mounted close together on a segment with a cover or bearing plate guarding the bearings.
