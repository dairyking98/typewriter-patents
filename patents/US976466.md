## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter platen carriages are heavy, wear loose and lose alignment of the writing; platen bearings make platen removal slow.

**Solution:** A light rigid skeleton carriage rides on adjustable ball-bearing race bars with detachable platen journal bearings and guard rails.

**How it works:** The carriage is a skeleton casting with front and rear bars, end plates and a front scale bar. It runs on a shift frame through ground steel race bars fitted with bearing balls held in a retainer. Adjusting screws on the race bars set alignment and take up wear. The platen shaft sits in detachable sleeve bearings held by arms so the platen lifts out quickly. Guard rails cover the end mechanisms. The shift frame is pivoted so the carriage rises and falls for case shift.
