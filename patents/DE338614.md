## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On machines where the ribbon spools are tied to the carriage shift, the ribbon must keep its position relative to the platen when the platen is raised or lowered for case shift.

**Solution:** Mount all ribbon parts, including the spools, on the vertically sliding shift frame so the whole ribbon device rises and falls with the paper carriage.

**How it works:** The shift frame slides vertically on pins in the machine frame guided by slots. A continuous ribbon spool shaft runs in the bent parts of the frame, in sections with a releasable coupling, with a ratchet wheel in the middle driven by a pawl, and a disc with driving pin at each end taking the spool. Nuts on threaded shaft ends hold the spools, which may be vertical or horizontal. The ribbon runs from one spool over guide rollers, guide pieces and guide forks to the other. The shift key on a lever pivoted on a bolt moves the shift frame, and everything attached rises and lowers with it without intermediate parts.
