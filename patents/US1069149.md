## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to devices for feeding a web or plurality of webs from a roll or rolls around the revoluble platen ot a type writing: machine, and also to machines in which in addition to the usual ribbon a the webs, so that the webs will be written in duplicate.

**How it works (reading):** A lifter bar that lifts the web reach on each carriage movement, loosening it and turning the spool slightly.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Webs fed from fixed rolls to a travelling platen strain and shift as the carriage moves.

**Solution:** A lifter bar that lifts the web reach on each carriage movement, loosening it and turning the spool slightly.

**How it works:** Rolls sit on a fixture behind the machine and webs pass under and around the platen. The lifter bar engages the web between the carriage and spool, lifting it to pay out web and relieving all webs at once. A second ribbon may sit between the webs.

**Features:** [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** web roll; platen; lifter bar; ink ribbon; carriage
