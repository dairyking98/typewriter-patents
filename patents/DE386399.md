**How it works (reading):** Each button lever turns a toothed segment that engages a rack on the rear of the stamped type bar; cover plates riding over each other keep the segment in line with the rack and relieve tooth loading.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Driving the type bars through a rack and toothed segment loads the teeth too heavily.

**Solution:** Each button lever turns a toothed segment that engages a rack on the rear of the stamped type bar; cover plates riding over each other keep the segment in line with the rack and relieve tooth loading.
