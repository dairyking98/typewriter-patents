## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets of a multi-sheet job did not line up at the left margin when placed on the platen.

**Solution:** An adjustable marginal guide with a lip slides along the paper-table on the carriage to set the same left margin on each sheet.
