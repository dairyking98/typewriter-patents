## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin width is hard to set when several stops must be moved together.

**Solution:** Hinged blocks on the carriage rod, with locking pins, swing singly or together into or out of the path of the carriage stop-lug to set the margin.
