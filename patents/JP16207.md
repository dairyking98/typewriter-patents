## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A new, comparatively simple typebar printing machine that works well, using a different paper arrangement from the usual carriage and roller platen.

**Solution:** Hold the paper by its top edge on a fan-shaped horizontal holder that swings about a vertical axis for letter spacing and rises for line spacing, while typebars with several types each strike a small disc platen that is shifted vertically to choose the type.

**How it works:** The top edge of the paper is gripped on the curved edge of a fan-shaped holder on a rod inside a hollow shaft; a curved guide keeps the paper cylindrical as it hangs before the small disc platen. A spiral spring in a barrel swings the holder left, checked step by step by a segment-gear escapement with ratchet and pawl, while the holder rises step by step for line spacing and an adjusting screw trims its height to the ruled lines. Key levers throw typebars carrying several types each; shifting moves the platen and holder vertically so the platen meets the chosen type. An ink ribbon runs between paper and types. A key lever with pointer on a scale plate positions the paper at any point, a tripped hammer rings a line-end bell adjustable along a curved plate, stop plates give a tabulator, and a knee-operated device returns the holder. Only the first 40,000 characters of the 100,896 were shown, so later parts (shift, ribbon, return gear details) are not read.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Improvements in printing machines. Purpose. This invention relates to a new printing machine. Its main points are: a support against which the paper is held during printing, namely a printing platen (印字板); a fan-shaped paper holder (扇形紙持器), to whose curved edge the paper is fastened, which is kept horizontal, swings about a vertical axis so as to carry the paper across the printing point of the platen for letter spacing, and can move vertically so as to move the paper vertically for line spacing; and type bars carrying the types that print, swung by key levers. It also has the further points described below. Its object is a new printing machine of comparatively simple construction that works well. Drawings.
