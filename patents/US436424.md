## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in a brush, pad, or equivalent cleaning device, provided with means for attaching or applying it to a typewriting machine in position to be struck by the printing-type forthe purpose of cleaning the latter.

**How it works (reading):** A brush or pad with a concave backing and clasping arms is fitted to the platen or frame so the type strike into it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type need cleaning in place, and a removable cleaning device must sit where the type strike.

**Solution:** A brush or pad with a concave backing and clasping arms is fitted to the platen or frame so the type strike into it.

**How it works:** Brush or pad placed where the type strike it, with clasping arms that hold it on the machine.
