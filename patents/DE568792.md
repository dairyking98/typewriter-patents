**How it works (reading):** A solid of revolution carrying adjustable symbols is driven from the machine's constantly moving drive member through a single-turn clutch, so a normal keystroke or a control key prints the recurring data on a standard power typewriter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic writing of recurring data such as dates needs a special machine, which is only worth buying for continuous use.

**Solution:** A solid of revolution carrying adjustable symbols is driven from the machine's constantly moving drive member through a single-turn clutch, so a normal keystroke or a control key prints the recurring data on a standard power typewriter.
