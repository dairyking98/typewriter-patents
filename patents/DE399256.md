> Description Translated from German Cash register with a device for skewering receipts. The invention relates to a cash register with a device for impaling of the receipts cut from the issued bills onto a needle. That Impaling of sections on a needle is known per se in the case of being checked. The needle is fixed in these known devices and they are pretty cumbersome facilities are required to transfer the cut sheet of paper onto the Bring needle a temporary failure of the device can hardly be avoided. In contrast, the needle according to the invention is movable and is moved against a guide plate connected to the movable knife, so that the needle and guide plate move against each other at the same time and the Section is safely impaled.
