## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register receipt impaling)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register with a device for impaling of the receipts cut from the issued bills onto a needle.

**How it works (reading):** The needle is movable and is pressed against a guide plate tied to the knife, so the needle and plate move together and impale the cut section; a table top is raised by a thumb disk to meet the needle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed needles for impaling cut receipt sections are cumbersome and the transfer often fails.

**Solution:** The needle is movable and is pressed against a guide plate tied to the knife, so the needle and plate move together and impale the cut section; a table top is raised by a thumb disk to meet the needle.
