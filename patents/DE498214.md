## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (receiver for type printing telegraph) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for recording received coded signals after translation, e.g. as type-characters

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to receiving devices for electrical telegraph apparatus and especially refers to the one! Class where means for transferring resp.

**How it works (reading):** The receiving station moves the selecting parts in oscillating, rotary and axial motions, with signal-controlled guides, channels and pins, so that opposite characters are distinguished and translated into typed characters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Morse characters that are opposites of each other have the same time value, so a receiving station cannot tell them apart and selects the wrong letter.

**Solution:** The receiving station moves the selecting parts in oscillating, rotary and axial motions, with signal-controlled guides, channels and pins, so that opposite characters are distinguished and translated into typed characters.
