## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (braking device for calculator shafts)

**How it works (reading):** Brake lamellae slide axially from outside on the shaft, so more or fewer touch the conical part of the capsule wall to set the braking effect.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the braking effect of a carriage drive brake requires opening the capsule and swapping discs.

**Solution:** Brake lamellae slide axially from outside on the shaft, so more or fewer touch the conical part of the capsule wall to set the braking effect.
