## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjusting the platen of a machine with a switchable platen frame is hard, because the parallel guide arms are carried on a single sliding part that cannot be set in two directions independently.

**Solution:** The support part is two pairs of plates, one plate on the machine frame and one on the other, so each pair adjusts in one direction independently, giving the line level and stroke force settings.
