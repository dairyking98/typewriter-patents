## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guards, shields or dust excluders

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and it has for its principal object to provide a dust guard for the type bar bearings of a front-strike typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** A dust guard covers the type bar bearings and also carries a guide and pointer for the ribbon vibrator.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bar bearings collect dust and the ribbon vibrator lacks support.

**Solution:** A dust guard covers the type bar bearings and also carries a guide and pointer for the ribbon vibrator.
