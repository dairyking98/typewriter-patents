## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pads for circular type-bar arrangements must be ring-shaped and keep a supply of ink.

**Solution:** Annular wick-fed ink pad with a ring valve.

**How it works:** An annular casing has an ink basin, channels, and an annular chamber under the cushion separated by a perforated partition. Wicks lead through the opening and the cushion absorbs ink. An annular screw valve presses on the wicks for transport and seals on a rubber washer; removing it refills the basin.

**Features:** [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md)

**Parts:** annular casing; ink basin; wick; chamber; perforated partition; cushion; annular valve; rubber washer
