## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must reverse automatically when it is fully unwound from one spool, and a twist can also cause tension that the reversal must handle.

**Solution:** A constantly driven master wheel carries a double pawl that engages ratchets on shafts geared by miter pinions to the two ribbon spools, so tension on the ribbon moves the pawl to drive the other spool.
