## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Writing, carbon and copy sheets must be straightened before insertion, which is slow by hand.

**Solution:** A bracket clamped to the table top carries a sliding angle bar with a cutting edge; the stack is pushed into the corner, pressed against the bar, straightened and held with a clip.
