## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for writing, calculating and similar machines for straightening the writing, carbon and copy sheets to be introduced into the machine. The aim of the invention is to create a simple and therefore cheap device which enables the writing, carbon and carbon paper to be inserted into the typewriter to be quickly sorted. This is achieved according to the invention in that 'one leg of a bracket to be placed on the desk top is equipped with a displaceable angular bar, one leg of which ends in: a cutting edge, the device shown for example in the figures consists of a simple wooden bracket a, b and a side part e that can be slid on the leg b (Fig. i). The wooden angle reinforced at a by a thin sheet of iron. has a small pin embedded in the wood on its underside at the point marked i, which protrudes only a little and prevents the device from moving on the table top. The leg a receives a light metal clamp d at the lower end to attach the device to the table top (see Fig. Q.).
