**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**How it works (reading):** The switching frame is a thin sheet metal U-bracket; a roller on an adjustable and fixed axle supports it laterally, so it stays light and resilient while the carriage-train forces are absorbed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A switching frame strong enough to take the lateral forces of the carriage train is heavy and slow, but a cheap frame made of thin sheet metal cannot take those forces.

**Solution:** The switching frame is a thin sheet metal U-bracket; a roller on an adjustable and fixed axle supports it laterally, so it stays light and resilient while the carriage-train forces are absorbed.
