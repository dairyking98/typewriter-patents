## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a new embodiment of such a paper feed device, namely, the advanced is a coupling device in which the Angular rotation of the crank regardless of the respective height of the billing head is the same, namely approximately 360 °.

**How it works (reading):** A coupling engages the crank with the paper roller for a fixed turn of about 360 degrees and uncouples once the set head height angle is reached.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A crank turning the paper roller to a set invoice-head height hits its stop violently and breaks when the head height is reset.

**Solution:** A coupling engages the crank with the paper roller for a fixed turn of about 360 degrees and uncouples once the set head height angle is reached.
