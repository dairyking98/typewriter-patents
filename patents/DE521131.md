## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Spoken sounds need to be written down automatically on an electrically driven typewriter.

**Solution:** Match the light-vibration trace of a spoken letter against stored negatives of the user's own pronunciation on a rotating disc, and use a photocell to trigger the corresponding type lever.

**How it works:** A sound-film style recording apparatus throws light vibrations through a lens onto a ground glass. A rotating disc carries negatives of each letter's vibration pattern made in the user's own voice. When the live pattern coincides with a negative, the photocell is shielded and its circuit breaks, releasing an armature that closes a second circuit. A synchronous disc with sliding contacts distributes the current impulse to the type lever of the matching letter on an electric typewriter.
