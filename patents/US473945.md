## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods on side surfaces thereof, e.g. fixed thereto

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writers or type-writing machines in which the typebar is separated from and adapted to be held in the hand and applied to the machine, with which it co-operates at the proper point to permit the desired letter or character to print.

**How it works (reading):** A detachable hand-held type-bar with curved type faces and guides is set on a spring-loaded frame on the carriage; pressing it prints and the spring spaces the carriage one letter along fixed rack-bars.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Provide a cheap typewriter in which a separate hand-held type-bar is applied to the machine at the point where the letter is wanted.

**Solution:** A detachable hand-held type-bar with curved type faces and guides is set on a spring-loaded frame on the carriage; pressing it prints and the spring spaces the carriage one letter along fixed rack-bars.

**How it works:** A base carries two toothed rack-bars and a central paper support. A carriage slides on the racks and carries a curved paper-holder with a window, a type-bar frame, and a pawl-carrying frame. The operator places the type-bar on the frame, its guide co-acting with a vertical guide on the holder so the letter falls on the window opening. Depressing the frame prints through an ink roller and presses the holder harder on the paper to stop slipping. A spring compresses on depression and, on release, drives the pawl to move the carriage one letter-width along the rack.
