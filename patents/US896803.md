## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the present invention are to rovide mechanism for effecting the latera movement of the ribbon, said mechan ism bbing operated by the dog operating mechanism which controls the step by step movement of the pa er carriage;

**How it works (reading):** Lateral ribbon movement is driven by the dog mechanism that steps the carriage, longitudinal feed is driven by the carriage motor, and a cam collar on the spool gearing reverses the ribbon when it has wound off one spool.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must be moved sideways away from the printing point and fed longitudinally between spools, and the feed direction must reverse automatically when one spool is empty.

**Solution:** Lateral ribbon movement is driven by the dog mechanism that steps the carriage, longitudinal feed is driven by the carriage motor, and a cam collar on the spool gearing reverses the ribbon when it has wound off one spool.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spools; cam shoe; cam-grooved collar; ribbon support; dog operating mechanism; carriage motor
