## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Narrow-band projection signs print letter by letter along the tape and advance it by one letter spacing, which is slow and narrow.

**Solution:** A typewriter prints across the tape transversely, the tape advances one line spacing per line, the type basket moves along the paper carrier, and the tape is held taut on the projection window, with the paper lifted for large letters.

**How it works:** The type basket moves longitudinally while the tape stays stationary during writing, then the tape advances by one line and is kept taut for projection.
