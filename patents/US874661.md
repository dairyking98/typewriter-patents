## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are complicated and weak; the inventor wanted a strong, simple visible-writing machine with typebars carrying several characters.

**Solution:** Use typebars each carrying a vertical stack of characters, the carriage rocking on a pivoted frame by rock-shaft keys to present the right character, with an inking pad.

**How it works:** Each typebar has a horizontal arm, an upright type-bearing arm with several characters one above another, and a twisted depending arm that passes through aligned round and rectangular openings in a guiding frame. The forked end of a key lever embraces the neck of the bar. Rock-shafts at the machine centre, with finger discs, move a rod frame on which the U-shaped carriage oscillates and slides, so the carriage is brought to the proper position for the chosen character. The carriage has a platen and feed roll with a knurled wheel for line spacing, and a pawl on a bell crank engages ratchet teeth on the carriage for letter spacing. An inking pad is returned by a bell-crank frame.
