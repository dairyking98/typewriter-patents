## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink containers held too much liquid, which made the type smear and made the container heavy and a drag on the type movement.

**Solution:** A small ink container feeds a felt inking pad drop by drop through a drip channel and hole above the highest liquid level, so only a little ink is needed.
