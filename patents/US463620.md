## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper feeding mechanism) · **Subject (patent classes):** Retainers of the roller type, e.g. rollers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of 1nachines or mechanism adapted for automatically feeding through the medium of suitable feeding-rolls sheets of paper or similar material arranged in quantities upon a suitable support to a machine for either pressing, ruling, printing, or otherwise operating upon the same;

**How it works (reading):** The paper table rises automatically as sheets are fed, and a regulator limits the sheets fed forward to one at a time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets fed from a stack must be kept at the feed rolls and fed one at a time without doubling.

**Solution:** The paper table rises automatically as sheets are fed, and a regulator limits the sheets fed forward to one at a time.
