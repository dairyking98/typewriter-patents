## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing for the blind in the point system needs a simpler carriage feed, spacing and embossing device.

**Solution:** A hand embossing implement with a spring-fed carriage on a cylindrical rack and depressed levers that press points into paper over a plate of projections.

**How it works:** A base plate with toothed flanges carries a tilting frame held at an incline by a swinging rack. The frame bears a rod with an adjustable plate having two rows of upward projections as the die, and a feed roll with a shield that clamps the paper. A carriage rides on a cylindrical rack and is pulled by a spring, held by a lever and auxiliary lever with a spring catch that steps it one space. Operating levers are pressed down to the platen to form points. Details of the embossing action are mostly beyond the opening.

**Features:** [USE:blind](../features/use-blind.md), [SEL:dummy-keyboard](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** base plate; tilting frame; swinging rack; die plate with projections; feed roll; shield; carriage; cylindrical rack; spring; spacing lever; operating lever
