## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (writing calculating typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with switching of the type of calculation and the font made from the car.

**How it works (reading):** Adjustable plates on the vertical counters act through a roller and angle lever linked to the shift key, switching the reversing gear and font with the ordinary counter design.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching the calculating function and the font needs special control combs on each counter.

**Solution:** Adjustable plates on the vertical counters act through a roller and angle lever linked to the shift key, switching the reversing gear and font with the ordinary counter design.
