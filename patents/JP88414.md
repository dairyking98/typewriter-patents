## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A light, simple printing machine is wanted in which pressing a type plunger prints accurately and also feeds the sliding plate by an exact, constant step.

**Solution:** A hand-turned type wheel of spring-linked plungers sits on a tipping support plate; pressing a plunger locks the wheel, prints, and on return a pawl on a side arm advances the carriage along a rack.

**How it works:** A round type wheel (7) with many type plungers (10) round its rim turns freely on a support plate (6) hinged on a post of a sliding plate (3); the operator turns the wheel until the wanted plunger is at the pointer (31) at the front. Pressing that plunger drives its type past a spring-held ink roller (24), which it pushes aside, while its bent link (12) enters a guide groove (21) in the support plate and locks the wheel and works a stop (19) that engages a rack (18) on the base so the carriage cannot move. The link then bears on the groove bottom and tips the support plate and wheel forward until the plunger is vertical and prints through the base slot onto paper. Tipping lifts a rear side arm (16) whose spring pawl (17) leaves the rack. On release springs return everything, the stop lifts, and the side arm's pawl drops and pushes the sliding plate forward one tooth; since the tilt is always the same, the feed is constant. A front handle (15) lifts the pawl to free the plate for hand movement.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. A round type wheel is mounted on a support plate that can tip up and down on a sliding plate. Round its edge are many type plungers, each engaged with the tip of a link that is sprung upward. Choosing the wanted plunger and pressing it at the front drives it down past the ink roller, pushing the roller aside, to print on the paper. At the same time a link that enters the guide groove of the support plate works a stop that engages a rack on the side of the base, so that the plunger comes down accurately; and a spring pawl at the rear of a side arm, which rises and falls with the support plate, engages the same rack, so that as the support plate returns to level the sliding plate is moved forward. The object: a light, efficient printing machine that, just by pressing a plunger, puts the link into the groove and works the stop, so printing easily, quickly and accurately, while the feed pawl on the side arm, rising and falling with the fixed tilt of the support plate, gives the sliding plate an exact feed at each impression.
