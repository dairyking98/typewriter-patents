**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an automatic switching device for the ribbon drive of writing, calculating or similar machines in which in a known manner against the ribbon reels push button, change their position brings about the switchover.

**How it works (reading):** A feeler, set by the ribbon roll diameter, moves a wedge stop into contact with a nose on the drive shaft, so about half a turn of the shaft shifts it fully and reverses the ribbon quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon-reversing switchover on the drive shaft is slow and unreliable because the old screw-thread shifting needs several shaft turns, so the ribbon stalls and gets damaged.

**Solution:** A feeler, set by the ribbon roll diameter, moves a wedge stop into contact with a nose on the drive shaft, so about half a turn of the shaft shifts it fully and reverses the ribbon quickly.
