## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventors say prior air-cushion key caps collapse, lose resiliency, and are cut through by their attachment to the key.

**Solution:** A cushion key whose yielding finger-impact crown is supported against excessive yielding and has a stout peripheral wall, so it keeps its normal shape.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
