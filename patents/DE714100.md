## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**How it works (reading):** Rests for the type levers and key levers are made of sound-damping material about half a type lever long, with a slot to absorb rebound, and the linkage is aligned so it cannot reverse at its joints.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type lever and key lever rebound on return to rest caused clattering noise in worn machines.

**Solution:** Rests for the type levers and key levers are made of sound-damping material about half a type lever long, with a slot to absorb rebound, and the linkage is aligned so it cannot reverse at its joints.
