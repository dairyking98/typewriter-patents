## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to line-spacing mechanism for type-writing machines, and has for its object the regulation of the distance between the lines, so that the lines may be close together or farther apart, as desired.
