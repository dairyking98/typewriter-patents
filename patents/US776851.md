## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to line-spacing mechanism for type-writing machines, and has for its object the regulation of the distance between the lines, so that the lines may be close together or farther apart, as desired.

**How it works (reading):** A line-space latch block on a pull-rod engages notches to set the distance between lines, with a notched ratchet pawl and a buffer roller that damps the platen ratchet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing is fixed, and the user needs to change the distance between lines and have it change automatically.

**Solution:** A line-space latch block on a pull-rod engages notches to set the distance between lines, with a notched ratchet pawl and a buffer roller that damps the platen ratchet.
