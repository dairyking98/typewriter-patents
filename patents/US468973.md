## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A matrix-making machine for printing plates needed fast, accurate operation with one key per die.

**Solution:** Dies on levers hung from a wire ring, each moved to the centre by its key and struck by a constantly reciprocating plunger.

**How it works:** A drum with radial slots guides the die levers, which hang loose on a round wire ring. A flywheel shaft with eccentric and connecting rod drives a piston and plunger continuously. A key lever carries its die to the centre, where the plunger presses it into the matrix. The matrix carriage moves on a rack under the printing point by an escapement and spacing keys.

**Features:** [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** bed plate; drum; die lever; wire ring; plunger; piston; eccentric; flywheel; matrix carriage; rack; escapement; spacing key

**Numbers:** 7 sheets of drawings
