## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return to the right shocks the feed rack, and the alarm and line lock need an actuating stop.

**Solution:** A carriage stop mechanism absorbs the return shock and triggers the bell alarm and line lock.

**Features:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
