## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The shift key must be held down to type capitals; a way to retain it automatically in the shifted position is wanted, cheaply made.

**Solution:** A rock-lever with a shift key at one end and a lock key at the other carries a pivoted latch that hooks a fixed plate to hold the shift bar down; touching the shift key releases it.

**How it works:** A stamped sheet-metal shift bar is attached to the cradle that carries the platen carriage and is returned by a spring. A rock-lever pivoted on the bar has a shift key and a lock key. Pressing the shift key swings the latch out of the way, so the spring restores the parts when released. Pressing the lock key throws the latch into the path of a fixed plate; the latch tooth passes the plate and engages its underside, holding the shifted parts against the spring. Touching the shift key frees it.
