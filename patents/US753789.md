## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriters need a work-holder and support that lets the book be cleared for turning pages without a long desk.

**Solution:** A shiftable machine-supporting frame and a self-leveling work-holder let either page of a book be brought beneath the printer in front of the operator.

**How it works:** Adjustable base rods carry a machine-supporting frame on longitudinal guides, with the frame and the work-holder both shiftable. Moving them brings either page under the printing mechanism, or both clear of the printing zone to turn pages or replace the book. Rest tables on leveling levers support the book and automatically keep the sheets in the printing plane. A raising and lowering device on the base rods sets the height.

**Features:** [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** work-holder; rest table; leveling lever; base rod; machine-supporting frame; longitudinal guide

**Improves on:**
