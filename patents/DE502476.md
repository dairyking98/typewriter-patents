## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (switch lock for calculator paper carriage) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switch lock for the column circuit of the paper cart of calculating or adding machines, which consists of a swinging rocker with two alternating ratchet teeth come into effect.

**How it works (reading):** A rocker with main and secondary ratchet teeth is lifted by a lever to release the main tooth, and a rider holds the secondary tooth until the rocker resets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage locking in calculators must be released mechanically for each entry and then held for the next.

**Solution:** A rocker with main and secondary ratchet teeth is lifted by a lever to release the main tooth, and a rider holds the secondary tooth until the rocker resets.
