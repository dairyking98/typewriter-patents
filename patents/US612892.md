## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines for cutting stencils and analogous purposes;

**How it works (reading):** A die-carrier disk on a main shaft holds a circular row of female dies, and a plunger lever operates whichever punch lies beneath it, with a circular feed device stepping the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil cutting by hand is slow and inaccurate, and the feed of the stencil sheet must be controlled.

**Solution:** A die-carrier disk on a main shaft holds a circular row of female dies, and a plunger lever operates whichever punch lies beneath it, with a circular feed device stepping the paper.
