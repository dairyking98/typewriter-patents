## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix indenting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention is directed to the production elevation showing the machine in still anof indented, engraved, or iigured surfaces by other form.

**How it works (reading):** A universally movable carrier holds the blank beneath a die or tool with a common point of action, while a pointer follows the pattern so the tool reproduces its form.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Solution:** A universally movable carrier holds the blank beneath a die or tool with a common point of action, while a pointer follows the pattern so the tool reproduces its form.
