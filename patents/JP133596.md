## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A Japanese-script typewriter should steady the type drum, bring the type to the platen and strike it at the centre of its back so the print is clear and quiet.

**Solution:** One handle's successive movements steady the type drum, raise the held type frame to the platen, then drive a circular-path striker against the back of the type.

**How it works:** The operator turns or shifts a type drum by its grip until a pointer is over the wanted type, then presses a handle carrying two rollers and a cam. The lower roller tilts an actuator whose pawl stops the drum on a ratchet and whose pusher drops a toothed latch to stop sideways shift. The cam next raises the type frame, held in receivers, up a guide slot past an ink pad to the front of the platen. The upper roller then pulls an arm and link so a striker swings on a circular path behind the pointer and strikes the centre of the type's back. On release, springs return everything and the drum steps a set distance sideways.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a Japanese-script typewriter characterised by the combination of: a drum-steadying device, in which a handle mounted loosely on the type-drum shaft carries working rollers above and below and a cam between them, and the action of the lower roller stops the turning and the sideways movement of the type drum; a type-frame moving device, which by the action of the cam raises the type frame, already held, to the front of the platen; and a striking device, in which a striker, moving on a circular path through an arm pressed by the upper roller and a linkage connected to it, is fitted so that it can come out and withdraw behind the pointer. Its object is to make the print clear by striking the type at the centre of its back with a striker moving on a circular path. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show an embodiment.
