## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Letter spacing devices are complicated and prone to derangement.

**Solution:** A letter-space regulating attachment with few parts.

**How it works:** An attachment for regulating letter-spacing that reduces cooperating parts, lightening the typewriter and cutting cost.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** attachment
