## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stores and agents need large-letter announcements, and the inventor wants a universal machine that prints such letters quickly and cheaply.

**Solution:** A universal letter-type forms the bottom part of the printing mechanism; printing type-segments are moved to positions by a carriage to print large letters.

**Features:** [Type sector or segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md), [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)
