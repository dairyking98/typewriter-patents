## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locks for subsidiary keys, e.g. for shift keys

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a trip mechanism for controlling the use of the upper and lower cases in type-writing machines provided with type-bars carrying a plurality of type-faces, and in which the change from one case to the other is accomplished by the depression of a case key to alter the position of the platen carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Separate keys lock the carriage permanently in upper or lower case, with a trip that releases a temporary upper-case lock on the next key or space.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On Remington-type machines the case key must be held for capitals, so the operator wants the carriage locked in either case without holding the key.

**Solution:** Separate keys lock the carriage permanently in upper or lower case, with a trip that releases a temporary upper-case lock on the next key or space.

**How it works:** The case key lever links through a bell crank and rock-shaft to the vibrating carriage guide-bar. A slide-bar riding in a vertical guide slot is lifted until a notch engages an adjustable detent, held by a leaf spring, locking the carriage in upper case. A coil spring returns it. The slide-bar lies in the path of the feed bar beneath the key levers, so any key or space bar depression trips the temporary lock, while a second key releases the permanent lock.
