## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typebars striking the platen while the keys are used for adding cause impression damage.

**Solution:** A padded stop is interposed to arrest the typebars before the printing point, so the type cannot strike.

**How it works:** Padded stop linked to the key lever halts typebars short of the platen during computing.
