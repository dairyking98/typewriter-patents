## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The guide head that steadies typebars at impact covers the writing.

**Solution:** Each typebar head has a spur-like projection that engages under a bar on the frame just before the stop, so no guide head is needed.

**How it works:** Typebars move on a guide plate; the lower part of each head has a rounded projection. Just before impact the head passes between jaws joined by a bar, and the projection engages under the bar so the bar is guided between plate and bar. Alternatives are a foot-like, conical or angular projection, or conical head sides fitting jaws.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** typebar; head; spur projection; jaws; bar; guide plate

- typebar guide head: eliminated; writing is unobscured
