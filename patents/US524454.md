## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check perforator) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in the class of'perforators employed for punctu ring in bank-checks, and the like, the amounts marked on their faces.

**How it works (reading):** A perforator that presses the check against a stationary punching character on a rotatable head, with the check fed across the head by an automatic reciprocating holder.
