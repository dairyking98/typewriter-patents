## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Flat page-size platens or smaller flat platens having a greater size than line-size platens

**How it works (reading):** A friction-driven auxiliary feed pulls a single web off the supply roll and passes the others loose to a main feed that aligns them with pins.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Several carbon-paper webs stacked tightly are hard to align without tearing, because pulling them all at once puts strain on each.

**Solution:** A friction-driven auxiliary feed pulls a single web off the supply roll and passes the others loose to a main feed that aligns them with pins.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
