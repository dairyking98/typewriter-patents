## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins

**How it works (reading):** A display linked mechanically to the margin-stop position so the setting is shown in front of the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops mounted behind the platen are easy to forget and are not visible from the writing position.

**Solution:** A display linked mechanically to the margin-stop position so the setting is shown in front of the paper.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
