## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Electromagnetic typebar actuation made the type lever move with the armature through its whole stroke, needing a long armature travel.

**Solution:** A lost-motion link: a pin on the key lever's link rides in a curved slot in the type-lever head, so the lever gets a short impulse then flies free and falls back by itself.

**How it works:** A key lever has an armature end over an electromagnet. Its other end carries a link whose pin passes through a curved slot in the head of the pivoted type lever, centred when at rest. Pulling the armature raises the link and the pin hits one end of the slot, throwing the type lever like a projectile to print; the type lever then returns by its own weight. A mechanical key can drive the same lever. Posts and a wire stop arrange levers concentrically.
