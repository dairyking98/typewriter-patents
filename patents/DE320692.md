## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register voucher transport)

**How it works (reading):** The voucher is transported during the return stroke of the gear parts to the zero position, so it is ready when registration begins at the start of the lever descent.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Voucher transport in hand-lever cash registers happens during the forward stroke, which needs a fast, strong drive and risks the safety of the function.

**Solution:** The voucher is transported during the return stroke of the gear parts to the zero position, so it is ready when registration begins at the start of the lever descent.
