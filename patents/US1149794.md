## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets for a multi-copy job must be collated before writing, but collating boards hinder use of the machine.

**Solution:** A collating board is angled so it swings forward on the platen axle, letting the operator arrange sheets with sheet-carrying devices.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
