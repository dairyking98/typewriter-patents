## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A keyboard Japanese-script machine that brings a type to the printing point by key press needs a simpler, surer mechanism than the earlier JP106107 container-and-stop arrangement.

**Solution:** Pressing a key advances the whole type magazine forward by the type's row position, then slides that type's column-box sideways by its column position, both by motor-driven crank discs engaged by key-selected pins.

**How it works:** Pressing a key engages a first clutch from a constantly running motor disc, which in turn releases second and third clutches in sequence. The second drives a horizontal disc carrying a row of retractable pins; the key pushes up one pin into a hole of a link joined to the magazine, so the disc acts as a crank of key-set throw and moves the magazine forward a definite distance. A third clutch and second pin disc similarly slide the selected column-box sideways through a stud sliding in a grooved lug under the box. The type is then at the printing point and the printing lever prints it.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. This extends Patent No. 106107. It is a keyboard Japanese-script printing machine in which a type magazine made up of type column-boxes that can slide sideways is itself mounted to slide forward and back. Linked to the pressing of any character key on the keyboard, the magazine is moved forward lengthwise by an amount set by the type's place in its row, and then the column-box holding the type is moved sideways by an amount set by the type's place in its column.
