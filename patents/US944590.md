## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On single-shift-key machines, a capital at word start needs the space bar, the shift key, then the letter, wasting time and causing misalignment if the letter is struck before the shift completes.

**Solution:** Combine the spacing bar with the shift key so depressing shift also spaces, and latch the shift until the next letter key releases it.

**How it works:** A bar joins the spacing bar to one shift key so shift carries the spacing bar down while the spacing bar can still be pressed alone. The shift lever is held down by a pawl. Any letter key hits a bridge piece whose projection trips the pawl, releasing the shift, and a spring returns shift key and bar. The second shift key still gives continuous capitals.

**Parts:** spacing bar; shift key; shift lever; connecting bar; pawl; bridge piece; spring
