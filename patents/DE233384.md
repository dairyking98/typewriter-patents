## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Edge stops on a support rod must be set and fixed in position for different line widths.

**Solution:** Edge stops with a locking sleeve and spring that engages a ratchet rack, which doubles as the guide rail for the paper carriage.
