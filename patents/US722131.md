## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cleaning type with the fingers soils the operator's hands and requires holding the type.

**Solution:** A cleaning brush on a spring-operated drum or cylinder, so the type can be cleaned without holding it by hand.
