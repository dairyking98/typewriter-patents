This invention relates to a brush for cleaning the type of type-writing machines; and the primary object of the same is to provide a simple and effective device of this class which can be conveniently used for cleaning type-writing-machine type without soiling the fingers of the operator by avoiding the necessity of the latter to hold the type while being cleaned.
