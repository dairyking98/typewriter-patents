## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (clasp bending feeder) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rotating movement in the plane of the rotating part

**How it works (reading):** A rotating drum with a back-and-forth rotated base disc and a fixed ring feeds the parts into a discharge channel, where a vertical part and an inclined part guide them upright.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Workpieces in a feed drum jam or fall in the wrong position, and clamping damages them.

**Solution:** A rotating drum with a back-and-forth rotated base disc and a fixed ring feeds the parts into a discharge channel, where a vertical part and an inclined part guide them upright.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** inclined rotating drum; fixed ring; base disc; discharge channel; vertical channel part
