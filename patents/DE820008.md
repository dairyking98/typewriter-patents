## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers; Walls made by casting, pouring, or tamping in situ

**How it works (reading):** A detachable inking device with a felt or microporous rubber roller on a pivoting lever, released by a control lever coupled to the swing-back of the struck type lever and returned by a tension spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inking types on a typewriter with rocker-arm type carriers fouls the type head and ribbon, and the sticking inking roller spoils print clarity.

**Solution:** A detachable inking device with a felt or microporous rubber roller on a pivoting lever, released by a control lever coupled to the swing-back of the struck type lever and returned by a tension spring.
