## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (print hammer for registering machines)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a printing device for registering machines, especially cash registers tuid booking machines -whose the print hammer under the influence of released spring forces its pressure movement performs.

**How it works (reading):** Mainspring tensioning is switched off by the machine gear, so the hammer can rest against the types without winding the spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Print hammers in registers are held tensioned, and the mainspring is wound even when the hammer does not print.

**Solution:** Mainspring tensioning is switched off by the machine gear, so the hammer can rest against the types without winding the spring.
