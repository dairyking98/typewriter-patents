## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a carriage switching device for typewriters and especially those in which the movement of the swingable pawl carrier is carried out by the universal rod against which initially move the type lever independently in the first part of the keystroke.

**How it works (reading):** A movable pair of stops between the universal rod and the pawl carrier moves the pawl slowly with a short lever arm first and then faster with a long lever arm, and a separate linkage from the space key moves the pawl carrier.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage switch resists the universal bar from the start of motion, which makes the key stroke harder and the linkage heavy.

**Solution:** A movable pair of stops between the universal rod and the pawl carrier moves the pawl slowly with a short lever arm first and then faster with a long lever arm, and a separate linkage from the space key moves the pawl carrier.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** universal rod; pawl carrier; forked handlebar; curved arm; space key linkage
