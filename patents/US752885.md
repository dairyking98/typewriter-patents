## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing in the margin (e.g. testimony numbers) needs the carriage to travel past its normal stop.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded margin stop slide with a locking tooth on the stop rod that yields, letting the carriage go beyond the stop and return without delay.

**How it works:** A margin stop slide on the stop-rod has a pivoted spring-pressed locking lever whose tooth engages between rack teeth on the rod. A second stop slide is held apart from the first by a coiled expansion spring on a spindle, and is itself locked to the rod teeth. The carriage margin stop normally meets these stops, but force or release of the locking tooth lets the carriage move past. Applied to a Remington No. 6.
