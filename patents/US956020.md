## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In standard machines the carriage is raised for upper case, and lost motion in the carriage-raising parts can put the platen out of alignment.

**Solution:** Raise the typebar segment and the key levers together for upper case instead of shifting the carriage.

**How it works:** The typebar support segment slides vertically in the frame and carries the pivoted typebars; key levers, each with a lower- and upper-case letter, are raised with it. A horizontally movable rod on a vertically movable bar engages the rear of the key levers so the connection holds in either position. Two springs restore all bars and a bar under the key levers returns them.

**Features:**

**Parts:** typebar; type-bar segment; key lever; platen; comb plate; return bar; spring
