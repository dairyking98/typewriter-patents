## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing machine for address plates) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an embossing machine for thin sheet metal of any type existing address or similar printing plates with an adjusting handwheel and a Knee lever embossing linkage through which the two type wheels are arranged during its stretching Embossing stamps are moved towards one another.

**How it works (reading):** Swinging the adjusting handwheel engages the clutch and the pawl into the gear of the type wheel shaft, the clutch disengages automatically after one turn, and a V-belt connects the handwheel shaft to the type wheel shaft.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On embossing machines for address plates the clutch needs a foot kick to engage after the handwheel has set the type wheels, which forces the operator to adjust seat height.

**Solution:** Swinging the adjusting handwheel engages the clutch and the pawl into the gear of the type wheel shaft, the clutch disengages automatically after one turn, and a V-belt connects the handwheel shaft to the type wheel shaft.
