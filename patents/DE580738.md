## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (zero print device for calculating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such zero printing devices, in which for the purpose Setting of the Null types to be printed in the print line on the type carrier obtained by a special drive generated additional movement, and the purpose of such To train devices so that each part without the use of a force fit the device is held and guided in a form-fitting manner at all times.

**How it works (reading):** An additional movement is passed to the type carriers through rotary members with moving pins, so zero types print without a force fit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Zero printing on calculator type carriers needed an extra movement that the device did not hold in a form-fitting way.

**Solution:** An additional movement is passed to the type carriers through rotary members with moving pins, so zero types print without a force fit.
