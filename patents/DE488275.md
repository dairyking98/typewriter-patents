## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Curved key rows do not give a sure hand position for blind touch typing, since the keys are not curved enough and not close enough to guide the fingers.

**Solution:** Key rows are offset in length by finger, arc-shaped and stepped from front to back, with straight parallel side edges so the keys form a closed surface and each finger finds a stop in three directions.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [USE:blind](../features/use-blind.md)

**Parts:** key groups; thumb key; index, middle, ring and little finger keys; space key
