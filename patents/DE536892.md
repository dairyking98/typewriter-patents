## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Retracting an address plate holder on an embossing machine gives a hard push, and removing the holder is awkward.

**Solution:** A downward bent leaf spring on the holder's front edge, limited by a rigid part, cushions retraction against a ring on the conveying axle that can be shifted sideways to release the holder.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Shock absorbers for address plate holders of embossing machines The object of the invention forms a shock absorber for address plate holders of embossing machines. The address plate holder of address plate embossing machines generally consist of one with a recess for inserting the address or other type of printing plate provided frame, which on its lower side with two racks to slide back and forth of the plate holder is provided by means of a conveyor shaft carrying gears, which in turn in the slide which is displaceable in the longitudinal direction of the address plate the embossing machine is stored. The essence of the shock absorber to be provided in an address plate holder according to the invention resides in arranging a downwardly bent leaf spring on the front edge of the full part of the holder, the extreme bending of which is due to a bent down rigid part is limited and against the retraction of the holder encounters a ring sitting on its conveying axis, which after overcoming a certain resistance can be shifted sideways and then pulling it out of the plate holder.
