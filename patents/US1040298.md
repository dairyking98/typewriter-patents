## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mecha: nisms for typewriting mach'ines and its principal object is to provide improved mechanism of the character specified.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon stop, separate from the throw-varying device, set or adjusted automatically by the space key or printing keys to limit ribbon throw.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon stop must be set separately from the ribbon throw-varying mechanism and reset by key strikes.

**Solution:** A ribbon stop, separate from the throw-varying device, set or adjusted automatically by the space key or printing keys to limit ribbon throw.
