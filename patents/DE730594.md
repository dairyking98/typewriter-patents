## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special adaptations of handles

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a handle, preferably for a typewriter case, consisting of one or several elastic bands with ears, slots or the like.

**How it works (reading):** A carrying handle of one or more polyamide plastic bands, curved in the middle where the hand grips, with ears or slots at the ends for sliding attachment to the case; no reinforcing insert is needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Leather typewriter-case handles are weak and wear quickly, while rigid plastic handles do not suit band-shaped handles.

**Solution:** A carrying handle of one or more polyamide plastic bands, curved in the middle where the hand grips, with ears or slots at the ends for sliding attachment to the case; no reinforcing insert is needed.
