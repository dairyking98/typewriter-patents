## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon cartridges for endless ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an ink device with an ink ribbon on lever type typewriters and the like with full keyboard.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon ends run over outer rollers, with a driver roller pressed by a spring-loaded pressure roller and driven by ratchet and bevel gear when a button is pressed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A loose self-contained ribbon has no drive and must be moved by hand.

**Solution:** Ribbon ends run over outer rollers, with a driver roller pressed by a spring-loaded pressure roller and driven by ratchet and bevel gear when a button is pressed.
