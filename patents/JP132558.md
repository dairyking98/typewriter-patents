## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Japanese typewriters are heavy and complex with separate actions for selecting, positioning, printing and feeding.

**Solution:** One handle hub picks the type bar and one sub-handle pressed by the same hand locks the magazine, raises the bar, strikes and feeds the roller.

**How it works:** A rotary magazine of two slotted disks holds type bars, each carrying a row of types, and slides left-right on the base frame. The hand turns the hub handle to bring the wanted bar to an upright slot at the platen and slides the magazine to the place. Pressing the sub-handle rod slides a sloped collar: a pin enters a hole in the disks to lock them, a bent lever lifts the bar to the print position, and a rising tooth drops into a rack to hold the magazine. A second lever pulls a cord to turn a hammer bar that strikes the type from behind. On release a spring returns all parts and a rocking bar with pawl feeds the paper roller one tooth.

> Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. The object is a durable machine, simple in mechanism, light and easy to carry, easy to work and quick to print with. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show an embodiment.
