## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a Λ kzent writing device for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** When the shift frame moves, a toggle system fills the recess of the accent key so it becomes a dead key, while the other keys on that carrier operate normally; accents can then share keys with ordinary characters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accent keys needed their own key row because a non-moving key was required when the paper carriage shifted, so accents could not share keys with numbers or letters.

**Solution:** When the shift frame moves, a toggle system fills the recess of the accent key so it becomes a dead key, while the other keys on that carrier operate normally; accents can then share keys with ordinary characters.
