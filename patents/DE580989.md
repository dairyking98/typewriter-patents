While in the known typewriters of this type, the type body either in individual slots of two rotatable discs or individually in compartments or lamellas on the edge of a rotating body formed from several parts sit, according to the invention, the arrangement is made such that without special Guide pieces lying next to each other and due to their trapezoidal cross-section in their entirety a closed circular ring forming type body by turning this ring on a fixed, with a width approximately the width of a type body corresponding cutout provided along the circular rail so far, until the type to be printed comes into the cutout of the rail, in which it is of a pressure arm engaging therein is detected and imprinted. In this way you get a much simpler, smaller one and also easier to use pocket typewriter.
