## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing ring-type pocket typewriters need many parts, slots or compartments for each type body.

**Solution:** Trapezoidal type sticks lie side by side forming a closed ring that is turned along a fixed rail with a cutout where one stick is pressed down.

**How it works:** Type sticks with trapezoidal section hang by a notch on the inward-bent edge of a circular rail and form a closed ring. A wheel with pins turned by a knob rotates the ring until the chosen stick sits in the cutout of the rail. A pressure arm permanently in the cutout engages the stick's notch; pressing it moves the stick axially onto the paper through a ribbon carried on a plate turning with the wheel. A flat spring returns the arm and stick. A window in the arm shows the selected character.
