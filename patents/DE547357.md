## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The front guardrail of a paper guide has to be lifted out of the way when inserting sheets and must not block the view.

**Solution:** A guardrail on swing arms is pivoted on forward-hinged support arms carrying small rollers against the platen, and folds away out of sight.

**How it works:** A hand lever turns the axis and swing arms, bringing the guardrail into place between the ribbon fork and platen.
