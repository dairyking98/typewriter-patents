## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a card or sheet holder1 for use in connection With that class of type-writing machines which print upon a sheet or page in a at condition.

**How it works (reading):** A card or sheet holder attaching to the machine that holds the card flat in relation to the platen so writing reaches anywhere within the margins.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Index cards must be kept perfectly flat so they can be filed, and printing must reach any part of the card within its margins.

**Solution:** A card or sheet holder attaching to the machine that holds the card flat in relation to the platen so writing reaches anywhere within the margins.

**Features:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
