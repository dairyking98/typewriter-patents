## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Exchanging a platen roller body is cumbersome, needing triggers and careful alignment.

**Solution:** The platen shaft part is displaceable and is held by a detachable bolt; parallel-walled coupling halves prevent rotation between roller body and shaft.

**How it works:** The sliding shaft part is locked by a bolt, and the coupling halves fit with lugs in grooves so the body rotates with the shaft.
