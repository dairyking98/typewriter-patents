## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accounts need an itemised record with running totals shown at once.

**Solution:** Keys operate typebars and number wheels together so each stroke prints and adds or subtracts.

**How it works:** A series of keys operate typebars and numbering wheels simultaneously. Parallel shafts carrying indicating elements can be run together or independently, with locking for the computing elements and selecting means to show totals. Manifolding is provided.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)

**Parts:** key; typebar; number wheel; shaft; locking mechanism
