## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a practical and effective stop for the bundle of carbon paper, thereby limiting its displacement without the need for any auxiliary means except those connected Carbon sheet itself.

**How it works (reading):** The carbon sheets are joined by an edge strip around both sides of the paper path, their trailing edges overlap, the front ends are narrower, and a flexible carbon strip folded over one sheet acts as the stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carbon pack for typewriters can shift and bunch when moved, and needs a stop that does not depend on extra parts.

**Solution:** The carbon sheets are joined by an edge strip around both sides of the paper path, their trailing edges overlap, the front ends are narrower, and a flexible carbon strip folded over one sheet acts as the stop.
