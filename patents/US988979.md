## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The linkage that operates typebars binds and twists the typebar and its operating parts, especially on a combined typewriter and adding machine.

**Solution:** Guide each vertically moving key bar between pairs of grooved rollers at top and bottom, and equalise the key travel by varying the lug lengths while keeping connecting rods the same length.

**How it works:** Typebars hang in hangers on a lower arc plate, each linked by a U-section connecting rod to a lug on a vertically sliding rectangular key bar. The key bar runs between pairs of grooved guide rollers held in brackets with open-topped recesses and overhanging lips, giving a running fit. Because the hangers lie on arcs of three circles while the key bars lie on a different arc, the radial distance from guide bar to the connection point varies, and lugs of different lengths compensate so that outer and inner keys depress the same distance. A cap plate secures the hangers.
