## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to adjustable paper guides for holding the paper firmly against the platen during the typewriting operation;

**How it works (reading):** Individually adjustable paper guides on a rod, with curved fingers pressing the platen and room left behind the rod for an eraser.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guides cannot each be set individually and space behind the guide rod is blocked for erasing.

**Solution:** Individually adjustable paper guides on a rod, with curved fingers pressing the platen and room left behind the rod for an eraser.
