## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing numbering disks) · **Subject (patent classes):** Machines or apparatus for embossing decorations or marks, e.g. embossing coins by pressing; Devices for holding or supporting work

**How it works (reading):** The disk is thickened in the middle and pressed by a flat-bottomed punch that stretches the material outward so the figures are formed in one operation; pins with mats are held by a ring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Numbering disks for numbering and paginating machines need the embossed figures pressed in without distortion of the disk.

**Solution:** The disk is thickened in the middle and pressed by a flat-bottomed punch that stretches the material outward so the figures are formed in one operation; pins with mats are held by a ring.
