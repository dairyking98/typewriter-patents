## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A column stop rod with slots cut through two walls is weak, and the stops can tilt in use.

**Solution:** A square tube column stop rod with slots that do not cut through the wall webs, and stops fitted with an axis of their own.

**How it works:** The stops pivot in the slots within the tube, held by slots that keep the walls strong.
