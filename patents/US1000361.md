## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fitting many typebars into a front-strike basket with a light, quick, accelerating action and fast return.

**Solution:** Two interleaved typebar segments of different radius, each bar driven by a toggle of a radial link and sub-lever pulled by a key through a bell crank and link.

**How it works:** Forty-two short bars on a smaller segment alternate with forty-two long bars on a larger one, both ball-bearing mounted below the platen, so the long bars play between the spread type of the short bars. Each bar heel is pinned to a radial link, which joins a sub-lever pivoted on a third larger segment; a second link from mid-lever runs forward to a bell-crank key lever. Pressing the key pulls the sub-lever forward, breaking the toggle and giving a gentle start and continuous acceleration, while a return spring keeps good leverage on the sub-lever at the end of the stroke for a quick return. Sub-levers have open slots with a notch to prevent unseating, and short bars carry lower case with keys near the front.

**Numbers:** 84 typebars, 42 per segment; 7 transverse key rows; long bars swing about 90 degrees
