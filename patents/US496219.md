## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of my invention is to set and hold the platen in position to receive the impression of any character out of its usual position in line with the impression of other characters, and particularly to utilize the ordinary hyphen for an underscore in machines which are not provided with a special character for that purpose.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoted pawl on the holdfast dog catches the back of a spacing-wheel tooth to hold the platen at a fractional interval of a turn.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A machine without an underscore character cannot underline; the platen must be held at a fractional line position so a hyphen prints low.

**Solution:** A pivoted pawl on the holdfast dog catches the back of a spacing-wheel tooth to hold the platen at a fractional interval of a turn.

**How it works:** The platen spacing wheel normally engages a holdfast dog on a spring-loaded arm. The attachment adds a lever pivoted to the dog arm, with a pawl riding on one edge of the dog and held by a flanged plate. The operator turns the platen by hand a fraction of a space, then moves the lever so the pawl slips into the gap between the dog and the advancing tooth. The platen thus stays half-way between lines while the hyphen prints, and the spring returns everything to normal afterwards.
