## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper; Hammer-impression mechanisms; Special processing for irreversibly adding or changing the sheet copy material characteristics or its appearance, e.g. stamping, annotation printing, punching

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention has reference to that class of type-writing machines in which a carbon sheet or web and an underlying web of paper are extended over a flat platen beneath a downwardly-acting writing mechanism for the purpose of making a copy of the writing efiected on an upper sheet of paper.

**How it works (reading):** The forward clamp is raised so that the end of the paper is lifted and separated from the carbon web, letting the copy sheet be grasped and pulled forward without touching the carbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says that when the clamp is released to grasp the copy sheet, the operator's fingers touch and get soiled by the carbon sheet.

**Solution:** The forward clamp is raised so that the end of the paper is lifted and separated from the carbon web, letting the copy sheet be grasped and pulled forward without touching the carbon.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
