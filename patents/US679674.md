## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a type-bar-actuatin g mechanism or movement and associated parts of the machine relating to a shifting type-bar support for upper or lower case printing, a universal-bar organization, and a movable-type-bar guide, all as hereinafter set forth in detail.
