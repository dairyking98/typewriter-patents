## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Front-stroke typebar actuation needs a light touch with slow start and quick finish, plus a shift and universal bar arrangement.

**Solution:** A flexing toggle connection (two links hinged to a third link on the key stem) throws each type-bar to the printing point by a pull, with a shifting type-bar support for upper and lower case.

**How it works:** Each type-bar pivots in a segment and lies nearly horizontal facing the front of the platen. From its heel a two-link connection runs forward to a fixed point, hinged at the middle to a transverse link on the key stem. Depressing the key flexes the toggle: early resistance is higher and the bar moves slowly, then speeds up as the link comes to line with the key axis, so the touch is light at the end. The type-bar support and guide are shifted relative to the platen for case. A universal bar organization is also described but cut off.
