## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal on address printers needs grooved spools because a feeler lever falls into a groove.

**Solution:** A weight-loaded rocker with sensing rollers follows the ribbon roll diameter and tips over its axis to switch the reversing pawl.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** ribbon reel; switching wheel; switching pawl; rocker; sensing roller; weight
