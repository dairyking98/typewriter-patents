## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A circular-basket typebar machine (Yost type) needs a light, cheap rigid frame, key-to-typebar linkage that does not pull the bars out of alignment at oblique angles, and a convenient carriage handle that also line-spaces.

**Solution:** A cast hollow base frame with an integral key-lever pivot rib, swivelled and forked connecting rods to the typebars, a combined carriage-return and line-space handle, and a transverse-shifting platen worked by two extra keys.

**How it works:** Key levers pivot on a rib cast into the hollow base frame and connect through screwed swivels and bifurcated rods that clasp a journal on each typebar, which is a first-order lever with a two-type body (capital and small). Two extra side key levers rock a bar and reciprocating plates that shift the platen carrier forward or back to bring the capital or small type to the same line. The carriage is drawn back by a handle hinged on the axle rod; pulling it lifts the letter-space ratchet bar clear of its pawls and, at the same time, a driving pawl turns the platen one line space. A trip-ratchet with an elongated finger lets the ratchet bar drop back into engagement when the carriage is moved. A scroll mainspring in a wheel, protected by a plate and shield, draws the carriage.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
