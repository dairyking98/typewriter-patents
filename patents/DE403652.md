## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** Sector-shaped brake bodies loosely carried on the driver pin are thrown outward against the stationary capsule only when carriage speed exceeds a set value.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Liquid paper-carriage brakes are unreliable, and shot-filled or lamella brakes drag or brake too early.

**Solution:** Sector-shaped brake bodies loosely carried on the driver pin are thrown outward against the stationary capsule only when carriage speed exceeds a set value.
