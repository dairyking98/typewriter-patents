## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The space-key escapement can skip the carriage by more than one space if the key is not released promptly, and feeding during the key's upstroke requires releasing the space-key before striking another type-key.

**Solution:** Mechanism that operates the carriage escapement from the space-key so that the escapement wheel cannot skip a tooth during operation of the space-key.

**How it works:** A rocker carries a loose feed dog and a rigid detent dog engaging the escapement wheel. The rocker moves when the space-key is depressed, and the carriage feeds one space on release. The invention changes the cycle so feeding is controlled and the escapement wheel cannot skip a tooth, allowing faster typing.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; feed dog; detent dog; rocker; space-key; type-key

**Improves on:**

- space-key feed that skips when the key is held: changed cycle prevents tooth skipping
