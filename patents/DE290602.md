## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**How it works (reading):** The feed device is set so that a hole symbol punched in the tape makes the feed respond twice to a space, and an improved scale shows the missing space.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Justified lines need a way to double word spaces and show whether the missing space can be made up.

**Solution:** The feed device is set so that a hole symbol punched in the tape makes the feed respond twice to a space, and an improved scale shows the missing space.
