## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (linotype matrix escapement) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for assembling matrices and space bands

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention has reference to mechanism for actuating the matrix-releasing escapements of linotype machines, such as represented in Letters Patent of the United States #436,532, and for kindred purposes.

**How it works (reading):** Short key movement calls power-driven cams and a constantly rotating roll to lift the reeds that actuate the escapements, with springs drawing the reeds back down.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Linotype finger keys with very slight resistance and short travel must still drive escapements positively, even if the key is released early.

**Solution:** Short key movement calls power-driven cams and a constantly rotating roll to lift the reeds that actuate the escapements, with springs drawing the reeds back down.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Impression:** [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)
