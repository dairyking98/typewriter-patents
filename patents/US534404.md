## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates especially to devices employed for marking checks, etc.

**How it works (reading):** A check-protector with a shell and operating mechanism that cuts or punches the amount figures into the check body so alteration is impossible.
