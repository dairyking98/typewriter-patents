## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper feed rollers must grip sheets, cards and envelopes of various kinds firmly.

**Solution:** A roller of soft rubber fingers on an annular ring slides on a keyed shaft; the fingers bend to contact the paper.

**How it works:** Elastic fingers on a sliding annular ring grip the paper on a keyed shaft.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
