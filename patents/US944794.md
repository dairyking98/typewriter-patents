## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**In the patent’s own words:** My object is to produce an escapement mechanisrnwith asfew parts as practicable, thereby eliminating some of the elements with the result that certain defects, due to the presence of those elements, are done ThlS escapement mechanism is mounted, in its action away with and the mechanism is made lighter in weight, more positive and rapid and enables the carriage to be moved backward and forward.

**How it works (reading):** Escapement with fewer parts, star-wheel and pinion on adjustable self-oiling bearings, meshing with a small rack on the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The escapement has too many parts and the star-wheel bearings are not free or durable.

**Solution:** Escapement with fewer parts, star-wheel and pinion on adjustable self-oiling bearings, meshing with a small rack on the carriage.
