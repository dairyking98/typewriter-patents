## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Make a typebar machine faster in operation without sacrificing simplicity, durability or ease of operation.

**Solution:** An improved escapement with a main dog, a secondary dog swinging both vertically and horizontally, and stepped abutment faces, plus a carriage impelled by a spring within the paper roll acting through a quick-thread screw.

**How it works:** A ratcheted bar on the carriage cooperates with a dog pivoted to a fixed bracket, a secondary dog pivoted to its rear end that can also swing horizontally, and a lever linked to the dog arm. A horizontally movable abutment pivoted to the frame has several faces that support the secondary dog at different positions, so key and space-bar actions give single or multiple spaces. The carriage is impelled step by step by a spring placed inside the paper-supporting roll which turns a quick-thread screw feeding the carriage sideways. Modified key and space-bar layouts are shown.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** main dog; secondary dog; ratchet bar; abutment; link; lever; impelling spring; paper roll; quick-thread screw; key lever; type lever; space bar

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph: faster escapement and spring drive in the roll
