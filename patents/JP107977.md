## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** When a fast motor shaft is clutched to the printing mechanism by a hand-operated handle, the shaft can turn again before the handle returns and strike the same character twice.

**Solution:** Put a lug on the intermittent shaft and add two stops on a sliding shelf-like body, so that one is always in the lug's path and one clutch engagement gives exactly one strike.

**How it works:** Pressing the handle slides a two-level stop body while the clutch engages the second shaft. The lower (first) stop plate leaves the lug and the shaft turns, its strikers setting off the type push-up lever and printing lever. After the strike the upper (second) stop plate has come into the lug's path and halts the shaft. When the handle is released the stop body returns, the upper plate leaves the lug, and friction from the clutch spring carries the lug onto the lower plate, which again holds it. The shaft cannot turn again until the handle fully returns.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Single-strike device for an electric printing machine. Summary of the nature and purpose of the invention. This invention relates to a single-strike device to prevent repeated striking, in a Japanese-script electric printing machine of the kind in which the motion of a turning shaft is passed at will to the starting part of the printing mechanism to print. A lug is provided on the shaft that transmits this intermittent motion, and first and second stops engaging it are added: the first stop normally prevents the lug from turning, and the second is made to come into the path of the lug after printing is finished, so as to stop its further turning for the time being. Its purpose is that, although the turning shaft turns fast, there shall be only one strike for the first release of the first stop, so preventing the same character from being struck twice, and in consequence that power turning fast, such as an electric motor, can easily be used as the printing machine's power.
