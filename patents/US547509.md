## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spaces between words cost an extra keystroke and time.

**Solution:** Make the word space with the last letter by a double-notch escapement action.

**How it works:** A universal space bar under the keys moves a fixed detent and a pivoted shifting detent in the carriage rack as each key is released. A space key retracts the shifting detent a double distance, so the carriage moves two notches at once after the last letter. An auxiliary spacing lever is fulcrumed on the frame.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** rack bar; escapement lever; fixed detent; shifting detent; space key; universal space bar; auxiliary spacing lever

**Numbers:** detent travel about 3/16 in.
