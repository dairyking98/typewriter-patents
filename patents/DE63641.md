## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter is wanted that is simple, light and cheap.

**Solution:** A ring-shaped type band (or disc) is turned by hand until the wanted letter is over a slot in a plate, and a spring-lifted pressure rod with spacing grooves is pushed down into notches on the ring to print it and index it to the next position.

**How it works:** The paper is slid between a base plate and a thin slotted plate and fed by corrugated rollers. The ring-shaped type carrier has letters on its outer edge, matching labels on the inside to find them, and grooves on its inner edge. With the right hand the handle turns the ring until the letter is over the slot; with the left hand a cranked rod, held above the slot by a spring, is pressed down so its spaced grooves engage the ring's grooves and press the type to the paper through ink pads either side of the slot. On release the rod lifts and moves the ring one notch to the right. In a variant the carrier is a disc with a handle and no push rod or rollers, with one or two guide strips lying in the gaps between types to keep the line straight.
