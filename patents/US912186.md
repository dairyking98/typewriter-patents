## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**How it works (reading):** Feed-roll hangers pivoted on ears of the traverse bar, with springs pressing the rollers toward the platen, and a transverse shaft carrying independently movable feed rollers, which can be released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The excerpt does not state a specific defect; it concerns the feed rollers of a Monarch carriage, which must be released from the platen for paper adjustment.

**Solution:** Feed-roll hangers pivoted on ears of the traverse bar, with springs pressing the rollers toward the platen, and a transverse shaft carrying independently movable feed rollers, which can be released.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
