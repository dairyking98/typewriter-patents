## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for setting and resetting the column stops on typewriters with a column stop bar fixed to the paper trolley, in which both the setting and the resetting of the column stops takes place by a push rod connected to a double-armed lever.

**How it works (reading):** A single push rod, moved from a central position in two directions by two independent feeler levers acting on a double-armed lever, sets or resets the column stops on the carriage bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Setting and resetting column stops on a paper carriage needs many components or separate lever mechanisms, which add cost and weight to the carriage.

**Solution:** A single push rod, moved from a central position in two directions by two independent feeler levers acting on a double-armed lever, sets or resets the column stops on the carriage bar.

**How it works:** Pressing one key moves the push rod to set the stops in the working position; pressing the other key resets them; a spring holds the stops so they can be set without hindrance.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
