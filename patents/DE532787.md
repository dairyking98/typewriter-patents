## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for setting and resetting the column stops on typewriters The invention relates to a device for setting and resetting the column stops on typewriters with a column stop bar fixed to the paper trolley, in which both the setting and the resetting of the column stops takes place by a push rod connected to a double-armed lever. Devices have become known in which the setting and resetting the column stops are carried out by lever mechanisms that work separately from one another. These have the disadvantage that many components are required through which the Manufacturing becomes much more expensive. A device is also known in which one of the column stops supporting rod is mounted on levers, which from a central position upwards as well is pivoted down by adjusting a lever, whereby by a fixed Fork that grips over the column stops and against which the rod is pivoted push the column stops, the setting and resetting of the column stops takes place.
