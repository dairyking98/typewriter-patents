## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters with a moving paper carriage cannot write on several sheets or books at once, in different sizes or fonts.

**Solution:** Keep the writing surface fixed and move a slide carrying a swivelling type drum over it, with interchangeable slides and drums for different sizes and fonts.

**How it works:** Sheets, notebooks or books lie on a fixed base plate under a removable frame whose front part is a key and control panel. A sliding carriage moves parallel to the plate, pulled by cables from a shaft turned by one button; a type-drum slide moves perpendicular to it by cables from a second key. The type drum, with sunk letters in one or more rows around its perimeter, swivels about a horizontal axis. A setting slide moved along the panel turns it through shafts to the chosen character. A spring-held hammer lever on the slide, actuated by the type key through shafts, strikes the drum. Several slides and drums can write several sheets at once, in proportionally enlarged or reduced size.
