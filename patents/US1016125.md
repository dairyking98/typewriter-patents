## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The metal sheet in a typographic stamping machine must be moved precisely for character spacing and line length, with feed that varies with character width.

**Solution:** A feed device moves the metal sheet for letter spacing and line spacing; a cam moved by character width sets the feed, and the feed can be varied for characters of different widths.

**How it works:** Character width groups set the position of a cam on a shaft; the cam drives the feed of the sheet, and a separate line-spacing mechanism steps the sheet between lines.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** cam; shaft; feed mechanism; line-spacing mechanism
