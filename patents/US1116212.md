## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriters, having special reference to a machine with a novel frame and the details thereof.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper must be easy to insert at the front, stay in full view for writing and notation, and line spacing should be automatic.

**Solution:** A machine with a novel frame in which the paper carriage keeps the paper in one line of direction through the machine, so writing stays visible, and line spacing is applied automatically with each reciprocation of the paper carriage, with the spacing set by the operator.

**Features:** [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** frame; paper carriage; line-spacing mechanism
