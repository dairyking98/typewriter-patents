## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Original and duplicate on a continuous strip need different line spacing from one platen.

**Solution:** The platen is split into a central printing section and end sections that turn at different peripheral speeds to feed each strip.

**How it works:** End sections rotate independently of the centre section, feeding the duplicate and original strips at different rates.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md)
