## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Control for hammer-impression mechanisms for deciding or adjusting hammer-drive energy

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in certain parts of type-writing machines provided with a type-sector and key-levers, the principal objects aimed at in the construction being lightness of touch, simplicity, and certainty of operation.

**How it works (reading):** Keys rest loose on spring-loaded plungers and depress a frame that drives a bolt against a spring, with a platen-hammer held by a latch on a frame projection until the release. The OCR cuts off before the release action is fully described.
