## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A stepped cam serves as the stop body, swung about an axis parallel to the pawl's pivot by a linkage from the intermediate lever to the required width position; a common tension spring holds the movable pawl against it so every stroke reaches its end position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper carriage steps for characters of different widths were fixed by stepped stops that wore and were inaccurate, and extra step sizes were hard to add.

**Solution:** A stepped cam serves as the stop body, swung about an axis parallel to the pawl's pivot by a linkage from the intermediate lever to the required width position; a common tension spring holds the movable pawl against it so every stroke reaches its end position.

**Features:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)
