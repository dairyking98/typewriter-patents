## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Rebounding type-bars returning to rest can collide with other bars in front-stroke machines.

**Solution:** Each type-bar is actuated by a flexing two-member connection from a fixed axis whose geometry makes rebound flex it the wrong way, so a stop locks the bar at rest independently of the key-lever.

**How it works:** Two members joined by a hinge run from the type-bar heel to a fixed axis. Depressing the finger-piece flexes the joint one way, drawing the bar to the printing point. Rebound from the back-stop would flex the joint in the opposite direction, and a stop resisting that flexure holds the bar in place at rest. The locking is independent of the key lever.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type-bar; flexing connection; link; fixed axis; back-stop; finger-piece; key-lever; stop

- [US686487](../patents/US686487.md): same, separate stop-based lock
