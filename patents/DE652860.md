## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Brushes do not remove eraser dust from typewriter parts well, and the dust falls into the inner machine.

**Solution:** Interchangeable rubber bodies, a dabbing swab and a roller-shaped body, sit in one handle so dirt is dabbed off or rolled off the surface.
