> Description Translated from German Desk with raisable and lowerable as well as in the position of use against lateral movement of secured slat, z. B. for a typewriter. The invention relates to desks that can be raised and lowered as well as in the position of use towards the side Movements secured support plate, z. B. for typewriters. It consists in that in order to secure against lateral movements in the support strip of the desk Springs are embedded in their V-shaped central part on the underside of the support plate engage arranged pin. The new protection scheme has opposite the known the advantage that it is sunk and has no protruding parts on which the clothes of the person using the typewriter can get caught and torn.
