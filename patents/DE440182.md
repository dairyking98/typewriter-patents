## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A raised desk support plate can slide sideways and the usual lateral lock sticks out where clothing can catch.

**Solution:** V-shaped leaf springs set into the support strip are engaged by pegs on the underside of the plate, sunk out of sight, to hold it against sideways movement.
