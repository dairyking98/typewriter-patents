## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** Two cylindrical rollers per guide body run on the rail with their running surfaces in the rail centre plane, and a bracket touches the rail from below only in places.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cone-shaped carriage rollers tip on the rail, and single-roller guides do not absorb type-stop impacts well and cause friction.

**Solution:** Two cylindrical rollers per guide body run on the rail with their running surfaces in the rail centre plane, and a bracket touches the rail from below only in places.
