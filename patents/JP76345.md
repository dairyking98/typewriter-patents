## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing Japanese typewriters of the type-magazine kind put the whole printing load on the typist, causing fatigue and slowing printing.

**Solution:** Divide the work: one hand-held handle moves the printing cylinder left-right (by turning) and fore-aft (by pushing and pulling), while a foot pedal does the printing action.

**How it works:** A handle on a turning shaft is also fixed to an operating lever under the frame. Moving the handle forward and back swings the lever, whose rocking rod moves the fore-and-aft frame carrying the cylinder. Turning the handle turns a grooved pulley that pulls a flexible shaft (or belt and pulley train) left or right to move the cylinder carriage along its guide rods; both motions can be combined. The print action is a foot pedal and lever that pulls a flexible shaft to the type-holding lever's working point, returned by a spring. A mirror above the cylinder helps the operator see.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a typewriter in which the left-right travel of the printing cylinder is made by passing the turning of a handle to any suitable medium; the fore-and-aft travel of the cylinder is made through a lever worked by moving the handle forward and back; and the printing action is made by a foot-operated device. Its object is to divide up the force needed for printing so that the load on the body is as even as possible, and to lighten that force, improving the existing typewriter, reducing the typist's fatigue and raising the printing rate. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show one example of the typewriter of this invention.
