## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** One-way pawls on the control linkage of a calculating machine cannot return to their working position after the counter nose has passed.

**Solution:** Modifies the control linkage of the main patent so the one-way pawls can reset past the counter noses.
