## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriting machines in which mechanism inter mediate the platen and line-spacing ratchet wheel, o erates to differentiate the rotation of the p aten to the rotation of the ratchet wheel by the use of the line-spacing lever and pawl in ordinary line spacing.

**How it works (reading):** A mechanism between the platen and line-spacing ratchet makes the platen rotate differently from the ratchet, so impression lines overlap and the surface wears evenly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The rubber platen wears in pits from repeated type blows at fixed line positions and then glazes, so line spacing slips.

**Solution:** A mechanism between the platen and line-spacing ratchet makes the platen rotate differently from the ratchet, so impression lines overlap and the surface wears evenly.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
