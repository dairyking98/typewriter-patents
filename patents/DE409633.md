**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to portable typewriters which can be folded up for transport into a special transport position, and which have a switchable paper trolley which can be held in the switchover position by a locking lever.

**How it works (reading):** A release lever for the locking lever is actuated by the folding of a foldable part, so the carriage unlocks during transport preparation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriter with a switchable paper trolley could stay locked in the switched position when the cover closed.

**Solution:** A release lever for the locking lever is actuated by the folding of a foldable part, so the carriage unlocks during transport preparation.

> Description Translated from German For the transport to be scaled down typewriter with switchable paper trolley. According to the Union Treaty of z. June 1 9 1t claims priority on the basis of the application in the United States of America from January 29, 1923 . The invention relates to portable typewriters which can be folded up for transport into a special transport position, and which have a switchable paper trolley which can be held in the switchover position by a locking lever. The invention provides a guarantee that when the machine is being prepared for transport, the car does not inadvertently remain locked in the switchover position and that as a result damage can occur when the cover is closed. According to the invention, a release lever is provided for the locking lever, which when downsizing the typewriter for the transport position by the movement of a foldable part is influenced and the locking lever out of his Locked position triggers.
