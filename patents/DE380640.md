## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing line-space width by shifting the platen axially with its ratchet wheels makes lines start in different places and loosens the platen.

**Solution:** The pawl that engages the ratchet wheel is laterally adjustable and a special pawl is used for each ratchet wheel. A lever switches the working pawl, so the line pitch changes without moving the platen.
