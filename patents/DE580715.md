## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**How it works (reading):** A tensile-stress means acting on the free end of the paper keeps the sheet under constant tension at the impression point against the feed mechanism, removing play in the paper feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction or pin-roller paper feed slips or cannot hold tension, so line spacing errors add up and letterpress output is not accurate.

**Solution:** A tensile-stress means acting on the free end of the paper keeps the sheet under constant tension at the impression point against the feed mechanism, removing play in the paper feed.
