## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** The recess widens conically inward, with a felt or foam layer under the rubber washer for sound absorption.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Foot rubbers with cylindrical recesses do not fit different foot diameters and wear loose.

**Solution:** The recess widens conically inward, with a felt or foam layer under the rubber washer for sound absorption.

**Features:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
