## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide a holder of the character specified, adapted for use with or without a typewriter and provided with detachable means for connecting it with the typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** A roller on pivoted bearing brackets, a guard rod and a rubber-roller paper support hold the copy and can be attached to or removed from the typewriter.
