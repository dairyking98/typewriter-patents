## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic carriage travel in both directions with line feed and return needs a simpler device that can be fitted to existing machines and can move the carriage to any point.

**Solution:** A roller parallel to the carriage path carries a zigzag guide slot and is turned by weight or spring; a pin on the carriage follows the slot, so the carriage moves forward or back, feeds the line at the end, and returns automatically.
