## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key levers

**How it works (reading):** The intermediate lever bears on balls seated in a bore of the lever and in a slot of its bearing; a comb-shaped retaining plate with inclined seat surfaces holds the balls, so the lever runs free without play and can be removed individually. The bearing and plate are injection moulded.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bearing an intermediate lever of a type lever drive needs a separate bearing block with a rivet, which is costly and makes replacement hard, and a ball bearing with a cover plate rubs and causes friction loss.

**Solution:** The intermediate lever bears on balls seated in a bore of the lever and in a slot of its bearing; a comb-shaped retaining plate with inclined seat surfaces holds the balls, so the lever runs free without play and can be removed individually. The bearing and plate are injection moulded.
