## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing on a front-strike machine with a vertically shifting platen frame must work in both case positions.

**Solution:** Holding detent and actuating dog ride on the shifting platen frame while the operating lever stays on the fixed carriage frame.

**How it works:** The line-space wheel, detent and dog are mounted on the platen-shifting frame so they move with the platen. The operating lever sits on the main carriage frame and does not shift, linked to the dog by a bell crank and links. The pivoted detent carries a movable line-space stop; the stop's abutment force pushes the detent into the wheel, preventing overthrow. The lever hangs close to the top plate with weight low so gravity returns it.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** line space wheel; holding detent; actuating dog; line space stop; operating lever; bell crank; link; platen shifting frame
