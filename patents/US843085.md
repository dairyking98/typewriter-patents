## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention is to provide compact, simple,- eflicient, and - easilyunderstood and operated means for connecting the stop-wheel at will to the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A finger-wheel on a central rod, with a clutch to the line-space wheel and a clutch to the stop-wheel, releases the platen from the line-space wheel and connects it to the stop-wheel in one endwise movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be easily connected to a stop-wheel for limiting rotation, and the platen cannot be released from the line-space wheel for fine adjustment.

**Solution:** A finger-wheel on a central rod, with a clutch to the line-space wheel and a clutch to the stop-wheel, releases the platen from the line-space wheel and connects it to the stop-wheel in one endwise movement.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; line-space wheel; stop-wheel; clutch bars; finger-wheel; detent
