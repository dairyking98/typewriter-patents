## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters of the Remington pattern feed the carriage one space per key; the user may want two or more spaces.

**Solution:** An adjustable bracketed stop on the fixed escapement pawl limits the stroke of the movable pawl so it skips alternate rack teeth.

**How it works:** A bent sheet-metal bracket is fixed to the fixed pawl by a screw through a slot. Its edge stops the movable pawl at a limit of its stroke so it passes one tooth or skips alternate teeth of the feed rack. A sliding stop in a dovetail groove, held by a spring nose in notches, and a thumb piece or operating rod let the stop be shifted by hand.
