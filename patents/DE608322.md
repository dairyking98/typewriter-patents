## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a line ratchet clutch on writing, arithmetic and similar machines with a multi-plate clutch that can be compressed by a spring.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoting pressure piece with two clamping levers compresses the clutch plates while a weak spring is used, and the levers are forced back on release.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-plate line ratchet clutch needs a strong spring that is hard to release, so the spring is weakened and the clutch slips.

**Solution:** A pivoting pressure piece with two clamping levers compresses the clutch plates while a weak spring is used, and the levers are forced back on release.
