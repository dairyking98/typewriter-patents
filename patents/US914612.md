## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type blows are noisy and the platen is worn; the platen also needs fine rotation and line offset for raised or lowered characters.

**Solution:** A muffled platen has a sound-deadening bed under the tympan cover, rubber rings, and thumb wheels at each end for fine rotation and backward or forward offset.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
