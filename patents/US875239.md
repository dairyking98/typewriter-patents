## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Operating parts need less movement to return for a new line, control without lifting the hands, and a simple case shift.

**Solution:** A universal frame and operating levers return the parts to position and drive a simple case shift; the OCR is too poor for detail.
