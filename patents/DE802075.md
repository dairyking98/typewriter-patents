## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Small typewriters must be carried with separate writing materials in a separate bag or suitcase.

**Solution:** The typewriter is mounted on the lid of a flat box holding paper and supplies, and the protective cap rests on the box as a stand.
