## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Cards and small forms must be printed close to the top, bottom and side edges, but they cannot be held reliably against the platen.

**Solution:** A paper holder on arms carried on a transverse bar, with fingers that run around the front of the platen and pressure rolls, grips the card so it can be printed close to its edges; a scale travels with the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
