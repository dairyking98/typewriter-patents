## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and has special reference to certain improvements in machines of this character whereby there may be secured a low keyboard, visible writing, and a permanent alinement of the type,with a minimum number of operating parts and low cost of construction.

**How it works (reading):** Type-bars are arranged so no hanger ring is needed, with a direct operating connection from each type-bar to its key-stem, giving a low keyboard and visible writing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Conventional type-bar hanger rings and bearing supports add parts and cost and make the keyboard high.

**Solution:** Type-bars are arranged so no hanger ring is needed, with a direct operating connection from each type-bar to its key-stem, giving a low keyboard and visible writing.

**How it works:** Type-bars carry their bearings without a separate hanger ring, and link directly to the key-stem; the bars are grouped to leave a view of the writing from the front.

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** type-bars; key-stems; type-bar bearings
