## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Extra paper-bow groups could not be indexed independently of the normal line feed, and earlier devices were cumbersome.

**Solution:** A device lets several superposed paper-guide arch groups move independently of the main line feed rollers while the normal paper guides still work.
