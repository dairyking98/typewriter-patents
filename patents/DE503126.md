## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mounting or connecting key buttons on or to key levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to key levers with removable, resilient fixed key, especially for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The key lever has one flat tongue that snaps into a slot on the underside of the key cap, with a side-bent stop to locate it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Known removable key caps need two springy legs that are hard to reach for fitting and removal.

**Solution:** The key lever has one flat tongue that snaps into a slot on the underside of the key cap, with a side-bent stop to locate it.

**Features:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
