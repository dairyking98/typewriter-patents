## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typebar pivoting in its hanger wears, and the bearing needs taking up from time to time.

**Solution:** The typebar hub has a tapered trunnion on one side and, on the other, a movable stud with a tapered boss pushed outward by a tapered-end screw, so the boss spacing in tapered sockets in the hanger can be adjusted.

**How it works:** The hanger has two leaves with outwardly tapering sockets. The typebar hub has a tapered boss at one side and, at the other, a stud sliding in a socket in the hub carrying the opposite boss. A transverse screw with a tapered tip bears on the inner end of the stud. Turning the screw in forces the stud outward, changing the distance between the bosses, taking up wear in the bearing.

**Parts:** type bar; hub; hanger; tapered socket; tapered boss; movable stud; adjusting screw
