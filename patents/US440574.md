## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact; Manually variable

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in type bars or levers of type-writing machines and hangers therefor, the invention particularly relating to means for insuring the hanging of the type-bar from the hanger and for taking up any wear in the connection and bearing;
