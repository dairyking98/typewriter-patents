## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Fixed-length spacers stack up length errors, so feed rollers jam or play too much.

**Solution:** Feed rollers and spacers may vary in length within limits, and the shaft fits are arranged to avoid stack-up of length errors.
