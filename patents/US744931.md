## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The link between key levers and type-bars must be removable so repairs to key levers and type-bars can be done without dismantling the machine.

**Solution:** A connecting rod made of continuous wire has a type-bar-engaging hook at one end and coincident eyes at the other that take a spring-actuated pin, which engages a hook on the key-bar and secures it detachably.
