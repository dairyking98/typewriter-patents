## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to stop mechanism for type-writingmachines;

**How it works (reading):** A stop on the carriage is released so the carriage can travel beyond its normal arrest point, and the stop mechanism then locks automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage needs to move beyond the point where it is normally arrested, and existing stop mechanisms do not allow this simply.

**Solution:** A stop on the carriage is released so the carriage can travel beyond its normal arrest point, and the stop mechanism then locks automatically.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
