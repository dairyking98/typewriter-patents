## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to line spacing or platen rotating mechanisms for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A line-space lever at each carriage end turns the platen, while a platen brake holds it friction-wise and releases the detent; carriage release is combined with the same mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line spacing and platen rotation mechanism needs to be compact, with guards and a brake for the platen.

**Solution:** A line-space lever at each carriage end turns the platen, while a platen brake holds it friction-wise and releases the detent; carriage release is combined with the same mechanism.
