## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an attachment for typewriters to be used in winding a sheet, such, for example, as a bill ng sheet, together with a carbon paper or ribbon upon the platen of the machine in order to make manifold copies.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment hooks the end of the carbon or ribbon to the platen periphery and keeps the carbon in tension while the carbon, billing sheet and platen wind together, with a hinged bar in the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manifold copies on a multiform billing sheet need the carbon paper or ribbon wound with the sheet onto the platen, with the carbon kept in tension.

**Solution:** An attachment hooks the end of the carbon or ribbon to the platen periphery and keeps the carbon in tension while the carbon, billing sheet and platen wind together, with a hinged bar in the platen.
