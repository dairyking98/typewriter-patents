## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Platens need to be swapped between hard (manifold) and soft (plain writing), and the ribbon, bell and margin need improvement.

**Solution:** Spring-pin bearings that let a roller platen be removed and replaced, with spool-shifting ribbon reversal and a bell and margin stop device.

**How it works:** The roller platen has end plates with sockets. Spring-loaded bearing pins through the carriage end bars enter the sockets and are withdrawn by milled heads, so the platen lifts out. The ribbon spools slide on grooved shafts and a spiral groove on the spring motor, engaged by a roller on a spool shifter, shifts them to reverse the feed. A bell ringer and an adjustable right-hand margin stop that also locks the keys are included.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** carriage frame; roller platen; end plate; bearing pin; spring; spool; spool shifter; spring motor; bell; margin stop
