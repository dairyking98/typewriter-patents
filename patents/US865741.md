## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to tabulating mechanism for typewriting machines and has for its main object to provide improved devices of the class specified.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulating stops on brackets fixed to the carriage, with a carriage releasing mechanism that stops the carriage at set points across the top plate as the escapement wheel is released.
