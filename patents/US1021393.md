## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Key-controlled tabulating mechanism locks its parts in the set position and a later operation releases the locking means automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating mechanism cannot arrest the carriage at any chosen columnar field or denominational position in one run.

**Solution:** Key-controlled tabulating mechanism locks its parts in the set position and a later operation releases the locking means automatically.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
