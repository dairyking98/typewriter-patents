## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (conductor's tongs with printing device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction; Inking devices using ink ribbons, ink sheets, or carbon tape or paper; Inking devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose is a double-armed locking lever which can be locked in two end positions is and each blocked one or the other leg of the angle slots.

**How it works (reading):** An angle lever for each ribbon roll is spring-pressed against its roll; when a roll runs empty, a guide pin jumps across an angled slot and shifts the control slide that reverses the ribbon direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal in the conductor's tongs was tied to the used roll rather than the empty roll, and the brake levers needed for the ribbon rolls added bulk and inconsistent pressure.

**Solution:** An angle lever for each ribbon roll is spring-pressed against its roll; when a roll runs empty, a guide pin jumps across an angled slot and shifts the control slide that reverses the ribbon direction.
