## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A way is needed to step the carriage backward one space at a time to bring a given spot to the printing point for corrections or interlineations.

**Solution:** A back-space key lever carries a spring-loaded push-pawl that acts directly on the carriage feed ratchet to turn it backward one tooth per key stroke.

**How it works:** A key at the side of the keyboard is fast to a rock-shaft with a forward arm carrying a vertical pivoted push-pawl. A spring normally holds the arm down so a rear arm on the pawl bears on an adjustable stop and keeps the pawl out of line with the ratchet teeth. Depressing the key raises the arm, the pawl spring swings the pawl behind a tooth, and further travel turns the feed ratchet backward one step, which moves the carriage rack through the pinion. A slotted plate guides the pawl. No change to the rest of the machine is needed.
