## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines and the object thereof is to provide therefor improved carriage stop mechanism, such stop mechanism being particularly adapted'for use.

**How it works (reading):** Carriage stop mechanism with margin stops is used with a Burroughs adding machine, and can also be used on a typewriter alone.
