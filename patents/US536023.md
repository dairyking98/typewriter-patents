## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbons should be fed in a straight line through their whole length, reversed quickly, and shifted sidewise in parallel lines as they are reversed.

**Solution:** Two reel feeds with pawls and ratchets, plus switch slides and arms, reverse the longitudinal feed and shift the ribbon transversely in step.

**How it works:** Two ribbon reels are splined on shafts at each side of the frame under the top plate. Each shaft has a ratchet wheel, set to rotate oppositely, driven by a pawl on a reciprocating feed lever, which connects by a rod to a side lever of the universal bar under the key levers. A single control throws one feed device into engagement and the other out, reversing the longitudinal feed, while switch slides and switch arms move the ribbon carrier toward the front or rear. The switch mechanism automatically changes the direction of the transverse movement at each reversal so the ribbon is used in parallel lines.
