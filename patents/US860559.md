## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**In the patent’s own words:** This purpose is effected by suitably covering a cylinder of suitably light material, wood for example, with a suitably flexible body of sufficient strength.

**How it works (reading):** The cylinder has a wooden core bored along its length, covered with soft rubber treated with varnish or rosin so the outer surface is hard while the body stays soft.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hard-rubber cylinders loosen on their shaft, make noise, and develop holes that spoil the impression.

**Solution:** The cylinder has a wooden core bored along its length, covered with soft rubber treated with varnish or rosin so the outer surface is hard while the body stays soft.

**How it works:** The soft body yields under the type blow, and the hardened surface gives a uniform printing face.
