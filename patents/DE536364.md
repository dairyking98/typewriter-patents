## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Aprons or guides for the printing section concentric with roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on a paper guide for typewriters and calculating machines, in which in well-known Way, by turning the paper guide release shaft, both paper pressure rollers together or the rear pressure roller is lifted from the platen by itself can be.

**How it works (reading):** The release axis carries flats; in the normal turned position both bearing arms are lifted, and when the axis is shifted axially a further flat lifts only the rear roller's bearing arm.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A paper guide release should lift both pressure rollers, or only the rear one, from the platen.

**Solution:** The release axis carries flats; in the normal turned position both bearing arms are lifted, and when the axis is shifted axially a further flat lifts only the rear roller's bearing arm.
