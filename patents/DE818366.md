## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates look to new and beneficial improvements in moving ribbon forks for Typewriters and similar office machines and relates in particular to a ribbon fork design, in which the ribbon is easily inserted into the slots of the fork and quickly removed the same can be removed.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon fork with movable arms set between the fixed arms opens quickly with one hand to insert or remove the ribbon and closes again automatically when any type key is pressed; the fork fits conventional typewriters without changing them.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting or removing a typewriter ribbon in the fork slots is awkward and dirties the fingers.

**Solution:** A ribbon fork with movable arms set between the fixed arms opens quickly with one hand to insert or remove the ribbon and closes again automatically when any type key is pressed; the fork fits conventional typewriters without changing them.
