## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements to attach the ribbon to the spool

**How it works (reading):** Each ribbon end is coated with a shellac solution before inking, so the ends stay free of ink and can be handled cleanly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink-saturated ribbon ends stain the fingers when the ribbon is installed by hand.

**Solution:** Each ribbon end is coated with a shellac solution before inking, so the ends stay free of ink and can be handled cleanly.
