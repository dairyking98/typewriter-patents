## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement efficiency must be increased; plane-rack and escape-wheel forms both need improvement.

**Solution:** An escapement with improved racks or escape-wheel teeth that move relative to the carriage at a different rate, aiming for more efficient feed.
