## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the class of escapements which are embodied in a type-writing machine in which there is a power-driven carriage, the movements of which are controlled by keys through the medium of an escapement 5 which is operated by the movement of the keys or the universal bar of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** A dog is thrown into engagement with the escape-wheel on a rack-bar driven escapement, so the operator controls carriage movement at any speed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapements for power carriages must work at fast or slow speeds and keep the carriage under operator control, while staying simple and cheap.

**Solution:** A dog is thrown into engagement with the escape-wheel on a rack-bar driven escapement, so the operator controls carriage movement at any speed.
