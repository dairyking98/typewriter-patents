## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventor wants a small, cheap typewriter of simple construction, with a simple way to operate the type carrier and the printing hammer from the keys.

**Solution:** Pressing a key lever swings a geared segment that turns a central post carrying a curved type segment until the lever itself stops the post, and further travel of the same stroke releases the pinion from the post and fires a hammer that presses the paper against the type.

**How it works:** A vertical post turns through part of a revolution and carries a curved type segment concentric to it. Key levers hook a pivoted frame; its stroke swings a gear segment that drives a pinion loosely mounted on the post, and a sliding key locks the pinion to the post. A stop arm on the post meets the depressed key lever, so each key sets a different angle of the segment. When the stop arm meets the lever, a latch holds the segment in place and a rock shaft lifts the sliding key, releasing the pinion. The remaining key travel turns the pinion alone and the frame's arm strikes a hammer that drives the paper and flexible platen against the type. The same frame stroke first spaces the carriage, then positions the type, then fires the hammer. An ink roll rolls over the type as the segment swings into position.
