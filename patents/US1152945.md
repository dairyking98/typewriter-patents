## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be adjusted relative to the line-space wheel without a second operator action to release the lock.

**Solution:** The line-space wheel is frictionally connected to the platen through a clutch with springs, and a lock holds the line-space wheel stationary during adjustment, released automatically.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
