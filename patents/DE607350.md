## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control strip switching for cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for tensioning or winding the web

**How it works (reading):** After each line advance a downshift wheel fixed to the unwinding roll tensions the control strip again.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The control strip in cash-register printing goes slack when the unwinding roll is rotated too far during fast printing, so line spacing becomes irregular.

**Solution:** After each line advance a downshift wheel fixed to the unwinding roll tensions the control strip again.
