## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention has reference to cushion-keys forltype-writing machines, constructed for the purpose of relievingthe fingers of the operator of soreness caused by striking the hard surface of ordinary keys;

**How it works (reading):** A cup-shaped metal shell closed by a rubber diaphragm to form a sealed air chamber, attached to the key-bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier cushions formed their air gap only when the auxiliary key was applied, so they were not self-contained or permanent.

**Solution:** A cup-shaped metal shell closed by a rubber diaphragm to form a sealed air chamber, attached to the key-bar.
