## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**How it works (reading):** Each ribbon spool sits on a shaft with a fixed and a loose conical disc; a displacer moved by the ribbon slides the drive shaft axially between the discs so the spool drive direction reverses.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known ribbon-reversal devices should be simple, reliable, cause little ribbon stress and reverse quickly, while staying independent of how the reversal is triggered.

**Solution:** Each ribbon spool sits on a shaft with a fixed and a loose conical disc; a displacer moved by the ribbon slides the drive shaft axially between the discs so the spool drive direction reverses.
