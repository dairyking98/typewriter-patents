## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hectograph duplicating sheets need a fresh ribbon field for each impression on a fixed writing line.

**Solution:** The ribbon runs along the writing line, moved incrementally with the carriage so an unused ribbon field is available after each type impression.
