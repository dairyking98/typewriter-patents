## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage must be releasable whether the shifted platen is in the working position or swung back to inspect the writing.

**Solution:** A finger-key release lever pivoted on the platen frame acts on an elbow lever that drops the feed rack from the pinion.

**How it works:** The truck carries curved arms with a feed rack meshing with a feed pinion connected to the escapement wheel. A pin on the rack arm sits in an elbow lever with an antifriction roller. A release-key lever pivoted on the platen frame passes under that roller, held clear by a leaf spring during normal case shifting. Pressing its finger key raises the roller and lifts the rack out of the pinion, in either platen position.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** truck; feed rack; feed pinion; escapement wheel; elbow lever; release-key lever; leaf spring; platen frame
