## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Remington escapement for a front-strike typebar machine needing a simple, cheap construction.

**Solution:** Escapement dogs on a rocker pawl the carriage rack, with a spring drum driving the carriage and the carriage supported on the shift rail.

**How it works:** Typebars with pull links are driven by key stems. The carriage rides on balls on a shift rail that moves in case shift on parallel links. A spring drum and band propel the carriage right to left under the control of feed dogs on a plate-like dog rocker: a holding dog and a loose dog engage a rack. A space key acts on the escapement.
