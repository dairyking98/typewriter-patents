## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter with an adjustable stamp device. The invention relates to a typewriter with an adjustable stamp device for stamping a group of cells, for example to print the day, month and year, with a single stop. Such a combination of certain signs persists for a while the same for all documents, e.g. B. in the example drawn for a day. Then the set of characters has to be changed in whole or in part. In known devices of this type, the stamp is through a special inking unit colored or against a lateral deflection of the ribbon pressed, in both cases laterally outside the ribbon spool. It's awkward. It also bothers the scribe when he draws the bow so far apart Must adjust stop points. These disadvantages are eliminated according to the invention in that the stamp in the manner known from Liniier- and Ornamentiervorrichtungen an axis 6 located to the side of the stop point in one to the stop point vertical plane is mounted so four-pivot that it is next to the stop point the individual types can be hit or pressed against the ribbon.
