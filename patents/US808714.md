## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-head pivoted to or rotating on lever

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates solely to what is known as the typermovement or typeaction, and for this reason I have shown in the accompanying drawings nothing more than is necessary to illustrate the principle of construction and mode of operation of the type movement or action embodying my improvements.

**How it works (reading):** A type-carrier with two types is moved into printing position over a flat surface, and a position-locating mechanism at the rest end of the stroke seats both faces on a shaped inking pad.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two characters on one type-carrier must each print cleanly on a flat surface without the other character marking the paper, and the carrier must return to a repeatable inking position.

**Solution:** A type-carrier with two types is moved into printing position over a flat surface, and a position-locating mechanism at the rest end of the stroke seats both faces on a shaped inking pad.
