## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The projecting line-space lever risks bending in transport or shipping and needs a larger box; known folding solutions are complicated.

**Solution:** The usual rear limit stop is made spring-loaded so the lever can be pushed over it and swung back about 180 degrees.

**How it works:** The line-space lever pivots on a vertical axis and acts on a spring-loaded slide. The stop limiting its backward swing sits on a vertically resilient tongue. Pressing the lever against a domed contact surface depresses the stop, so the lever can swing past it into a space-saving position.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** line-space lever; slide; spring; stop; spring tongue

**Numbers:** about 180 degree swing back

**Improves on:**

- hinged thumb plate or second-axis folding line-space levers: only the usual stop is made resilient
