## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card accounting machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to punch card evaluation machines (tabulating machines and the like) and relates in particular to a machine of this type for accounting work performed automatically.

**How it works (reading):** The web is advanced until the hole pattern of the card matches the entry field, controlled by conductive contacts, then stopped at the print line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punch-card accounting needs the paper web to stop with the correct account entry field at the print position.

**Solution:** The web is advanced until the hole pattern of the card matches the entry field, controlled by conductive contacts, then stopped at the print line.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** punch card; transport rollers 2; electrically conductive surface 3; paper web
