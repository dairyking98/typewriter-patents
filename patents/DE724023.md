## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for tensioning or winding the web

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for the noiseless movement of the Paper strips on high-speed typewriters with a desk that is on the frame the machine is articulated and a take-up roller for the paper strip carries whose rotary movement from the universal rail with the help of a on the desk articulated double lever is generated.

**How it works (reading):** A coil spring between the lower end of the double lever and the rear of the universal rail is stiff enough to drive the take-up roller but yields to excess force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rigid link between the double lever and the universal rail jams or bends when the desk is not exactly on its pivot axis.

**Solution:** A coil spring between the lower end of the double lever and the rear of the universal rail is stiff enough to drive the take-up roller but yields to excess force.
