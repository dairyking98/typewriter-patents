## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing macated at Sin dotted lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ruling and ornamental work cannot be done easily or efficiently on a typewriter with ordinary mechanism.

**Solution:** An arrangement of parts between the type-bar guide and the platen, with a ribbon moved into the type path during the printing stroke by a lever connected to the carriage, operated from a finger key.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
