## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen case shift in typebar machines is noisy and vibrates.

**Solution:** A shift mechanism that moves smoothly with reduced noise, shown for both platen-shift and type-shift forms.

**How it works:** Fig 1-2 shows a platen carried on a carriage truck on ball tracks and lifted for case change; Fig 3-4 applies the same idea to shifting the types. Typebars carry upper and lower case types and are driven by key levers. Detail of the cushioning is beyond the opening text.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** key lever; typebar; platen; carriage truck; ball track; hanger
