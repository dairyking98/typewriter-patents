## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A platen that moves at the moment of printing blurs the impression, and a Japanese printing machine needs vertical feed, back-spacing, run-on, blank feed and fine adjustment from one mechanism.

**Solution:** A swing frame worked by the print key alternately meshes a fixed gear (holding the platen still during printing) and a rotary gear (letting the platen spring-feed afterwards) with a large gear on the platen shaft.

**How it works:** A knife-shaped swing frame carries a shaft with a fixed gear and a free rotary gear of equal size. Pressing the print key swings the frame so the fixed gear meshes with the platen shaft's large gear, locking the platen still during printing. On release the frame swings back, the rotary gear meshes, and the platen's wound spring turns it forward until an arm on a turning ring hits a pin; the play set by an eccentric adjuster plate sets the line step. A back-feed pawl on the back-space key turns the rotary gear the other way by the same step, while a blank-feed key lifts a rod that holds the ring with a detent and frees the stop pawl so the platen runs on freely. Moving the swing frame's pivot gives fine adjustment of the print position.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Vertical feed device in a printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a vertical feed device in a printing machine, made as follows. On a swinging frame that works with the printing key, a shaft is provided, carrying side by side a fixed gear and a rotary gear of equal diameter and equal number of teeth. As the frame swings, one or the other of them meshes in turn with a large gear fixed to the end of the platen shaft. A stop pawl and a back-feed pawl act on the rotary gear in opposite senses, and an adjustable feed device is linked with a turning ring. The object is to obtain a new vertical feed device that holds the platen still during printing and feeds the platen just after printing, the so-called feed-after (後送リ) type, so that printing is accurate, and that can also do all the feeding operations the use of a printing machine needs: vertical feed, back-spacing, run-on (飛送), blank feed (空送) and fine adjustment. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show the part of a printing machine carrying the vertical feed device of the invention.
