## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with number and arithmetic keys)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter, accounting machine or similar machine with separate number keys and arithmetic keys as well as a power drive.

**How it works (reading):** The same digit type lever can be coupled to either the writing drive or the arithmetic drive, independent of each other.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Digit printing and arithmetic drives can fall out of coupling and miss printing a value during addition.

**Solution:** The same digit type lever can be coupled to either the writing drive or the arithmetic drive, independent of each other.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
