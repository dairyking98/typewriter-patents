## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (film cassette) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holders for containing light sensitive material and adapted to be inserted within the camera specially adapted for motion picture film, e.g. cassettes

**How it works (reading):** A bevel on the lid edge rests on a matching bevel on the magazine so the gap is removed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Film can jam in the gap between cassette lid and magazine edge.

**Solution:** A bevel on the lid edge rests on a matching bevel on the magazine so the gap is removed.
