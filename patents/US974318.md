## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting ma= chines and more particularly to tabulating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulator mechanism with means to move stops into and out of operative position quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops must be quickly selected, set, and cleared when the character of work changes.

**Solution:** Tabulator mechanism with means to move stops into and out of operative position quickly.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
