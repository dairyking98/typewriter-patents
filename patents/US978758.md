## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier means of varying the throw of the escapement dog so the carriage moves two letter spaces per impression were complicated and expensive.

**Solution:** A hand crank turns a shaft carrying a stop arm that is swung into or out of the path of the escapement rocking arm, with a leaf spring detent holding each position.

**How it works:** The escapement wheel turns on a shaft with a pinion meshing the carriage rack. A spring-retracted rocking arm engaged with the wheel normally strikes a frame stop. A parallel rocking shaft carries an arm that lengthens the stop to limit the arm's travel for ordinary writing; a hand crank turns the shaft part of a revolution to withdraw it, giving the full turn and double-spaced writing. A leaf spring bearing on one of two faces of the crank hub holds either position.
