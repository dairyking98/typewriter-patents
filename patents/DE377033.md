## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switching wheel clutch for typewriters, in which the paper roller can be connected or loosened with the line switching wheel in the simplest possible way by tightening a screw, in that the screw attached to the hand roller outside of the axis of rotation of the paper roller moves a sleeve that acts as a washer on both sides is trained.

**How it works (reading):** A screw set outside the platen axis clamps a sleeve acting as a two-sided washer, which presses a friction disc on the platen. The clutch engages through the screw's leverage and does not shift the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The line-spacing clutch that frees the platen for lined sheets or between-lines writing needs force, and sliding or spring parts wear.

**Solution:** A screw set outside the platen axis clamps a sleeve acting as a two-sided washer, which presses a friction disc on the platen. The clutch engages through the screw's leverage and does not shift the platen.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
