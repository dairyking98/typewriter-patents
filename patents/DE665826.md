## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Underscore characters leave gaps or saw-tooth ends, so continuous underlines are poor.

**Solution:** Underscore type with wedge-tapered ends, made longer than the carriage step so the strokes overlap evenly.
