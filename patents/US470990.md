## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key levers and typebars connected through intermediate parts are costly and add friction.

**Solution:** Key levers joined directly to the inner ends of the type-bars, separated by division plates, with the type-bar frame raised for case shift while the platen stays still, and a spring-dog carriage.

**How it works:** Division plates keep the type-bars and key levers apart, and the key levers pivot on a curved rod. Each bar carries capital and small letters, and the whole frame holding bars and keys is lifted at its inner end to shift case while the platen stays stationary. A pivoted guiding frame also holds the ribbon and lifts at its front as a bar prints, then drops so the writing is visible. Two pivoted spring dogs advance the carriage one space per stroke and let it slide back freely.
