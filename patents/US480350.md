## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (justifying attachment for matrix machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to matrix-making machines of the single-die class, wherein the impression is madefn the matrix material by a series of dies brought in succession 'tothe printing-point.

**How it works (reading):** Copy is pre-measured on a differential-registering typewriter so the extra spaces per line are known for the matrix operator.
