## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the present invention is to avoid the necessity of connecting the braking mechanism with the carriage-driving mechanism when released from its letter-,feeding devices, and for this purpose the braking mechanism is permanently connected with the driving gear of the carriage, but is operable only'when the carriage is advanced in letter-space direction.

**How it works (reading):** A centrifugal brake permanently geared to the carriage drive barrel through a pinion, with a ratchet so the brake works only in the letter-space direction and rides idle on the return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage brake that must connect to the carriage drive only when the tabulator key releases the carriage adds clutch complexity; the carriage should not exceed a set speed.

**Solution:** A centrifugal brake permanently geared to the carriage drive barrel through a pinion, with a ratchet so the brake works only in the letter-space direction and rides idle on the return.
