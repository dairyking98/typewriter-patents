## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A portable, cheap typewriter with typebars striking a central point needed a light carriage, a durable platen for varying manifolding, and a quickly detachable folding stand.

**Solution:** A ring-frame typebar machine with a light sheet-metal channel carriage, a rotatable four-faced bar platen and removable foot-pieces on a folding stand.

**How it works:** The type mechanism is a bed ring and upper ring joined by posts, with typebars striking downward at the ring centre. A carriage made of sheet-metal channel bars joined by tie-bars runs on pivoted rolls. A rectangular bar platen of oblong section is journaled across the ring centre; two faces are coated with hard and soft india-rubber and two are bare metal, and a spring seat holds any one face upward. Paper is advanced by a feed roll with a six-tooth ratchet and hand lever, one third turn per coarse line step. The bed ring has studs that couple with foot-pieces on a stand of jointed legs whose tops stop end movement.
