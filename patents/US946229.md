## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Special characters such as accents must print without letter-spacing in some shift positions but with spacing in others.

**Solution:** A dead-key lever acts on the ribbon throw only, or also on the escapement lever, depending on the shift-frame position.

**How it works:** On the Oliver typewriter a dead-key lever carries a special type-bar and operates a horizontal lever that lifts a connecting rod to throw the ribbon. The lever's upturned U-shaped end can engage the escapement lever, so that in one shift position the escapement lever is bypassed (no feed) and in another the key also gives letter-space movement. The space bar's rock-shaft and escapement lever drive the pinion and escape wheel on the telescope-wheel shaft.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** dead-key lever; type-bar; escapement lever; escape wheel; space bar; rock-shaft; connecting rod; shift frame; ribbon throw lever
