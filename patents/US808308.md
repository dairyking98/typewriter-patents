## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in stencil-cutting machines;

**How it works (reading):** A stencil cutter with a visual indicator showing when the dies are correctly placed, a guided operating means, and a male die-carrying member the operator can turn by hand safely.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stencil-cutting machine gave no visual sign that the dies were in place to cut, and the rotating male die member could injure the operator.

**Solution:** A stencil cutter with a visual indicator showing when the dies are correctly placed, a guided operating means, and a male die-carrying member the operator can turn by hand safely.

**Features:** [SEL:dial](../features/sel-dial.md), [ELEM:disc-face](../features/elem-disc-face.md)
