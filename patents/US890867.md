## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On bill-writing with a long record sheet, the operator must remember to advance the record sheet after each bill so that lines do not print on top of each other.

**Solution:** A separate line-spacing lever on the platen frame, which also casts off the pressure rolls, turns a ratchet on the platen by a pawl so the platen advances automatically when a bill is removed.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
