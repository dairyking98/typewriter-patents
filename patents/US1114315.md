## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ruled forms whose line intervals are not multiples of the normal line space force awkward hand turning of the platen knob.

**Solution:** A clutching dog and line-space wheel with an adjustable stop on a regulating lever and rack give any line interval, with a permanent stop relieving stress.

**How it works:** The line-space lever drives a slide against a return spring; through a link it rocks a pivoted clutching dog whose jaws grip a clutch flange on the platen-axle wheel and rotate the platen. An adjustable front stop, set by a regulating lever located in a detent rack with notches in aliquant relation, limits the throw. A permanent stop arrests the clutch at stroke end so the lever hits its usual abutment and the hand's pressure does not strain the clutch, and the dog bites harder to prevent overthrow. Shown on an Underwood front-strike machine.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** line-space lever; slide; return spring; link; clutching dog; clutch flange; line-space wheel; carrier; adjustable stop; regulating lever; detent rack; permanent stop
