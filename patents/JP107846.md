## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier typebar drives only accelerate the typebar; keys in the different rows travel different depths and strike with different force.

**Solution:** Pivot a rocking lever to each key lever so its effective arm length changes along a rolling curve on a rail foot, with lever lengths graded by row so every key goes down the same depth and every typebar strikes with the same force.

**How it works:** Each key lever carries a rocking lever resting by a hump on a rail foot. First the rocking lever swings about the hump with a long arm, lifting the typebar with great force but little speed, through a pull-rod, an intermediate lever and a link. Then the lever rolls on its curved surface so its arm ratio changes and the typebar accelerates toward the platen. Rocking levers of short key levers are longer and have different rolling-curve radii, so all rows give equal key travel; pull-rods of equal length drive intermediate levers through equal angles, and typebar arms lengthen toward the segment sides. A spring returns the typebar, and the intermediate levers can alternatively take a power drive with the keys acting only as release.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typebar drive for typewriters, typing calculating machines and the like. Nature and object of the invention. The invention relates to a typebar drive for typewriters, typing calculating machines and the like, in which the movement of all the key levers, swinging about a common shaft, is transmitted through rocking levers and pull-rods to the typebars or to intermediate levers; characterised in that the rocking lever of each key-lever row is pivoted to the key lever by its end facing the keys, in such a way that the pivot point of the rocking lever on a short key lever lies farther from the key levers' swinging shaft than on a long key lever, and that the lever-arm length of the rocking lever is changed by the rolling curve of the rocking lever working on a rail foot. Its object is a device that not only drives the typebars at increasing speed, but makes all keys, in whatever row, go down the same depth and strike with the same force.
