## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Re-seating rings for embossing letters inside them is slow and hard to set accurately.

**Solution:** A swivel ruler sets the base height after each ring change, and the types are pressed into the ring by a rotating shaft.
