## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typebar machine needs adjustable tension of the spacing lever and key levers, a removable carriage-bar frame that keeps the bars exactly aligned, and a better carriage construction.

**Solution:** A worm-and-gear adjustment of the spacing lever spring tension, a removable end-piece frame for the carriage bars, and a one-piece cast carriage with a scale and pointer.

**How it works:** Finger-pieces drive bell-crank levers and rods to pawl stems that act on the type-bar heels. A spacing lever carries spacing pawls that engage a ratchet bar on the carriage sliding bar. A spring on the lever's cross-bar is anchored in a worm gear which a vertical worm turns, raising or lowering the torsion force. The carriage bars sit in a frame of end pieces and parallel bars that slides into ways in the end posts and is screwed in place, so upper and lower bars stay aligned and can be taken out. The carriage is a single cast rectangular frame carrying the platen, a character scale on the front bar and a roller running on a bar with a pointer showing carriage position. A ball bearing runs between the top plate flange and the type-bar ring.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
