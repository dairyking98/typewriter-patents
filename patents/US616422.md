## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter type needs cleaning easily and cheaply.

**Solution:** A bar carrying a short-pile brush clipped under the ribbon to the shift-rail so that working the shift key rubs the brush on a held-up type.

**How it works:** A plate with a brush on its underside is fitted fore and aft under the lifted ribbon of a Remington No. 6. Prongs at one end embrace the letter-spacing pinion and a bifurcated spring hook at the other grips the shift-rail and straddles its indicator. A type key is held down to bring the type against the brush, then the shift key is worked rapidly, so the shift-rail reciprocates the cleaner and the brush scrubs the type. The brush is slotted on friction-tight headed pins so it can be shifted lengthwise to use fresh pile.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** bar; brush; headed pin; slot; prong; spring hook; shift-rail; spacing pinion

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 6: adds a clip-on cleaner using shift-rail motion
