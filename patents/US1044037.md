## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting inachines and it has for its principal object to provide an improved back spacing device for the carriages of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** A back spacing key depresses a pawl that engages the carriage feed rack to move the carriage back one space.
