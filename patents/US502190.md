## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar pivots must allow only rotation in one plane yet be tightenable to resist continuous wear.

**Solution:** A bracket-and-washer joint with segmental collars and a conical seat, tightened by a screw that cannot over-jam.

**How it works:** A bracket with two jaws receives the lower end of the type lever. Two washers on either side carry segmental rims that embrace segmental collars on the lever, so the collar works between jaw surface and washer rim. One washer has a central projection with a cylindrical, conical and cylindrical section that passes through the bracket and the conical hole of the lever, and is threaded for the screw. The other washer is loose so the screw head turns in it, and the washer ends are brought into contact so the screw cannot jam the joint. Recesses can hold lubricant.
