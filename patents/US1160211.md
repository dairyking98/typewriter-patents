## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A copy must be followed line by line and word by word when copying on a typewriter.

**Solution:** A copy-holder clamps to the platen knob of the carriage and a rectangular frame on a standard holds the copy; as the carriage moves, an intermittent motion from the cylinder moves the holder, with stops and a ratchet indicating each line.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** copy holder; knob clamp; ratchet and stops
