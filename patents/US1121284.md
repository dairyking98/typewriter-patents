## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are complex and costly and hard for beginners to learn.

**Solution:** A simple index machine with a flexible type band moved lengthwise by an index over a keyboard-layout plate and tilted by a key lever to print.

**How it works:** A paper carriage with a letter-space rack slides on guideways. A flexible strip with two rows of raised characters (upper and lower case) is mounted on a rigid slide that an index moves across the machine, the index running over a plate in three or more rows laid out like a standard keyboard. A key lever works a printing arm whose rounded front edge enters a notch in a strip behind the band for exact alignment, tilting the band to the paper. The index does not take the tilting motion.
