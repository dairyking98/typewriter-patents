## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen case-shifting on a front-strike typewriter needed a light, evenly acting shift that keeps the platen level and is adjustable.

**Solution:** A rigid shift rail lifted at several points by key-driven links on a rock shaft, with counterbalancing springs, raises the whole platen frame for case shift.

**How it works:** The platen sits in a frame hung from the carriage truck on parallel guide links. A ball-bearing roller on the platen frame rides on a horizontal shift rail. The rail is rigidly pinned to two vertical, turnbuckle-adjustable links guided by grooved rollers. The links hang from crank arms on a rock shaft that a shift key turns. Contractile springs on a second set of arms counterbalance the platen weight so a light key press raises the platen. Adjustable screw stops limit the platen travel.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen frame; shift rail; shift link; turnbuckle; guide roller; rock shaft; crank arm; counterbalance spring; shift key; stop screw; ball-bearing roller

**Improves on:**

- earlier platen-shift mechanisms: lifts the shift rail at multiple points, level and adjustable, with counterbalance springs
