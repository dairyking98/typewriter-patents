## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method for embossing types, especially for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The type is pre-stamped with a steeper cone (about 64-66 degrees) and then finish-embossed with a less steep die (about 78-80 degrees), so the material can flow without filing between the two stamps.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Embossing a type with a steep die cone leaves the tips low, so the printing face had to be filed flat and then re-embossed, which is slow manual work that also makes the product uneven.

**Solution:** The type is pre-stamped with a steeper cone (about 64-66 degrees) and then finish-embossed with a less steep die (about 78-80 degrees), so the material can flow without filing between the two stamps.

**Features:** [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md), [ELEM:metal-type](../features/elem-metal-type.md)

**Numbers:** pre-stamp cone 64-66 deg; finish die cone 78-80 deg
