## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Method of embossing types. The invention relates to a method for embossing types, especially for typewriters. When stamping the types, the following procedure has been used up to now: With an embossing stamp in which the type shape to be embossed was milled, namely in such a way that the edges of the milling had a relatively steep incline, the types were initially embossed. At first there were especially those on the side protruding tips of the types lower than the rest of the parts of the type. To also To emboss these tips well, after this first embossing process, had to be done on the tips the material is filed away and the pressure surface is filed down. After filing the type was then re-embossed with the above-mentioned die. According to the invention, the types with a steep cone are pre-stamped and embossed with a less steep cone, d. H. So, for the first embossing process a die is used, into which the types are milled using a milling cutter which has a significantly steeper milling cone.
