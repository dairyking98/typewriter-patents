**How it works (reading):** Auxiliary channels are formed as part of the character design, such as underlines or borders that join the injection channels, so the mould fills fully and the channels show as a decorative part of the key top.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Moulded key tops with inset characters are hard to fill cleanly, so they need extra feed channels, pressing or reworking.

**Solution:** Auxiliary channels are formed as part of the character design, such as underlines or borders that join the injection channels, so the mould fills fully and the channels show as a decorative part of the key top.
