## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide device for typewriters and in particular to an improved arrangement of the paper fingers and the latter supporting them Pole.

**How it works (reading):** A paper-finger frame that swings back from the paper roller so the fingers release the paper above the writing line, letting sheets be knocked back to expose the line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Making corrections on sheets in the machine requires the paper fingers to be moved along the roller and readjusted, which costs time.

**Solution:** A paper-finger frame that swings back from the paper roller so the fingers release the paper above the writing line, letting sheets be knocked back to expose the line.
