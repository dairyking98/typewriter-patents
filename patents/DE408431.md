## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**How it works (reading):** Two independently adjustable guide jaw carriers are inclined by conical screws in opposite slots, to set both width and sideways position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever guide jaw adjustment was too coarse and could not steer the type to the platen point.

**Solution:** Two independently adjustable guide jaw carriers are inclined by conical screws in opposite slots, to set both width and sideways position.
