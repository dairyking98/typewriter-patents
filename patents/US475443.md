## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The universal bar that trips the escapement is costly to arrange below the key levers.

**Solution:** Each key lever has a hook at its upper edge that engages a universal bar mounted above, so any key pushes the bar down to operate the spacing mechanism.

**How it works:** Thin corrugated sheet-metal key levers pivot on a rear rod and carry a hook member on the upper part, either a separate pinched strip or integral. The universal bar, carried on arms pivoted at the rear, sits in the hook slots with free space beneath the hooks. Pressing any key forces the bar down away from the other keys' hooks, and a rod from the bar actuates the escapement.
