In order to insert the writing sheets into the To enable writing position without removing the paper holding rail from the platen Having to take off has already been suggested, essentially below the platen arranged paper guide means in the advancing direction of the writing sheets beyond the writing line upwards through numerous across the width of the machine to extend distributed tongues and these tongues to just below the paper holding rail respectively. This arrangement has the disadvantage that the insertion of the sheets only in the middle position of the platen carriage is possible because the parts where the Tongues are attached, namely the so-called line height pointers; usually on the machine frame are stored while the paper holding rail moves with the platen carriage.
