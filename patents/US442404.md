## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A driving gear must be able to turn either way and drive a driven wheel while, at rest, acting as a detent that locks the driven wheel against reverse torque.

**Solution:** A stud wheel meshes with a notched wheel whose notches have a circular locking portion concentric with the driver and a toothed driving portion, so the pair both drives and locks.

**How it works:** The driving wheel carries studs at equal radius; the driven wheel is a notched plate that overlaps it. Each notch has a circular locking part and a gear part. While a stud sits in the circular part, the driven wheel cannot turn and cannot back-drive the driver. Further rotation makes the stud act as a cog in the gear part and turn the driven wheel by one step. Fig. 4 applies it to winding the carriage-driving spring drum of a typewriter.
