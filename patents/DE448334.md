## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Exchangeable type lever baskets are slow and error-prone to swap: levers are hard to remove from their bearing wire, fall out or get mixed up, and reinstalling them in key order takes much time and skill.

**Solution:** A locking device for the basket simultaneously uncouples the key-lever connecting rods from the type levers by pivoting them, so the basket with its levers can be removed and replaced as a unit and reconnected safely.
