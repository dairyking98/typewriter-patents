## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter loses time when the carriage has to return at line end; the inventor wants continuous writing with no carriage return.

**Solution:** Three type cylinders on an endless belt run past a fixed vertical platen, each stepped one character along the line, so a new cylinder takes over as the previous one leaves the line.

**How it works:** A fixed paper roller sits on two columns, and an endless perforated belt around it carries three type cylinders spaced at the line length. Each cylinder has ten rings of types; keys in five groups of ten select a ring and a character. Pressing a key energises a magnet that pushes a pin through a driver rod; an electric motor disc draws the rod across, the pin moving a toothed rack that rotates the cylinder (further for earlier pins) while a catch pin sets how far the cylinder is lifted for the ring. A stop bar then stops the cylinder against the paper roller and prints. On return of the driver the rack resets, an eccentric steps the whole cylinder system one letter along the line, and the line spacing is advanced when a cylinder reaches the end. A space key does the same step, and a contact lets the operator skip the cylinder ahead when the rest of a word will not fit.
