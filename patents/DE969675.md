## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line setting and switching, cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line setting and line switching device 1 on cash registers, booking or similar machines, the receipts of which are inserted into the machine one or more times for the purpose of carrying out one or more booking processes and which trigger and control their automatic adjustment to the line to be printed.

**How it works (reading):** Sensors on the card edge set the line and block the print hammer until the card is set, so one sensing set serves setting and print control.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Documents must be set to the right line before printing, and the same sensing had to serve both setting and printing control.

**Solution:** Sensors on the card edge set the line and block the print hammer until the card is set, so one sensing set serves setting and print control.
