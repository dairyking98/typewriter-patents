## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letters get equal space regardless of width, and the carriage cannot be accurately adjusted by hand.

**Solution:** A spacing escapement that varies carriage advance with the key pressed, with double- and treble-distance bars and a disengaging carriage.

**How it works:** A spring barrel pulls the carriage; a rack meshes with a pinion on a shaft carrying a notched escapement wheel. A rocking escapement lever with a hinged supplementary pawl is lifted by a rod and a cross-bar common to all key levers. Different cross-bars give double or treble distances for wide letters. The carriage can be thrown out of gear and moved by hand to an accurate point. It is built on the Remington or Caligraph.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** spring barrel; rack; pinion; escapement wheel; escapement lever; supplementary pawl; rod; cross-bar; key lever
