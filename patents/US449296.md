## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A separate space key costs time and hand movement.

**Solution:** Pressing the last letter key a second time without release makes the space, via a clutch or cam between draw-bar and type-arm.

**How it works:** Instead of a fixed connection, the draw-bar drives the type-arm through a clutch, cam or similar mechanism. The first depression prints the letter and, on release, spaces the carriage. Holding the finger down and pressing the same key again produces a word space without printing, so no space key is needed.
