## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to tabulating-stops for typewriters, and has' for its object to provide a stop which may be readily thrown into its operative or into an inoperative position and which will be securely held in either position.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulating stops swing on a grooved carrier rod; a top bar with a scale and rack, and a tabulating key linked to a lever, position and hold each stop.
