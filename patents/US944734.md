## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feeding, reversing and vibrating on front-strike machines are complex; two-colour ribbons and stencil cutting need extra provisions.

**Solution:** Compact direct driving of spool shafts from feed levers, cam-wheel reversing, and a vibrator guiding two ribbons moved different distances.

**How it works:** Feed levers worked from the carriage drive pawl-lever mechanisms that wind the spools on upright shafts. A cam wheel reverses the feed positively. The ribbon vibrator guides two ribbons edge to edge and rises to either of two printing heights from one normal position, giving a two-colour choice, and can be disconnected to rest idle for stencils. The vibration is the same in either position of the shifting platen frame.
