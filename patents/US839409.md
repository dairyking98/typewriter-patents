## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operator needs to choose upper or lower colour of a two-colour ribbon and silence the vibrator for stencils.

**Solution:** Stepped piece between universal bar and ribbon-vibrating lever, selected by finger-piece, gives two lifts or none.

**How it works:** Steps bring either colour band to the printing point; a third position silences the ribbon vibration for stencil cutting.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** universal bar; vibrating lever; step; finger piece; ribbon

**Improves on:**

- Underwood front-strike machine: adds colour-band and stencil selection
