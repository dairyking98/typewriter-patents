## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the Yost shift machine, pressing one shift key made the other rise, which operators disliked.

**Solution:** Give the two shift keys separate levers so each can swing the shift ring without moving the other.

**How it works:** Each key lever lifts a sub-lever controlling three connecting rods hanging from three type carriers (lower case in the middle, figures on one side, capitals on the other). The rods pass through holes in an oscillating shift ring. Two shift keys act through transverse levers whose rollers ride up inclined legs on the ring front and swing it left or right. A spring-pressed arm with a roller in a V-notch at the rear returns the ring to centre when the key is released.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** shift ring; connecting rod; type carrier; sub-lever; shift key; inclined fork leg; V-notch; spring-pressed centring arm

**Numbers:** three type carriers per key
