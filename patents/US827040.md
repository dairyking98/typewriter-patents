## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen line-spacing rotation and its brake and stop need to be controlled accurately, and the line adjustment must stay precise.

**Solution:** A sliding spring pawl with a rack driven by a segment gear engages the teeth of a ratchet on the platen piston to step the platen; an adjustable stop-pin on a finger lever sets one of three line-spacing positions.
