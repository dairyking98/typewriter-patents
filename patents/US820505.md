## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabulating devices for type-writing and other machines.

**How it works (reading):** A single row of column-stops is cleared by a revolving bar with sets of projections; one finger-piece rotation brings a chosen set into use and moves the other stops to idle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the tabulator for a different class of work means readjusting every column-stop along the bar.

**Solution:** A single row of column-stops is cleared by a revolving bar with sets of projections; one finger-piece rotation brings a chosen set into use and moves the other stops to idle.
