## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** For invoices on a long sheet, the paper must advance by set distances that match invoice heights, which the line index cannot give.

**Solution:** A loose disc on the paper roll axis has a row of holes matching the index-wheel teeth, and a stop link with an adjusting pin sits on its circumference; a fixed stop on the frame limits free rotation to the distance set between the stops.

**How it works:** The distance between two stops sets the free rotation of the roll, so the sheet advances the set amount.
