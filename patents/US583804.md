## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type striking an ordinary platen makes the machine noisy.

**Solution:** A platen is made of an imperforate rubber printing surface over a solid cork core on a spindle, with metal heads at the ends, to deaden impact noise.

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
