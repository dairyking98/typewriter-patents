## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an attachment for type-writers and similar machines and belongs to the class of attachments adapted to produce a plurality of copies and now usually designated as multigraphs.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage attachment holds a supplemental inking ribbon that can be thrown in or out of the way to make one copy or several copies.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon paper is expensive, perishable and slow to place and remove, and earlier multigraph attachments could not easily switch between single and multiple copies.

**Solution:** A carriage attachment holds a supplemental inking ribbon that can be thrown in or out of the way to make one copy or several copies.

**How it works:** The attachment fits on the swinging carriage of a Remington typewriter. A holder carries a supplemental inking ribbon next to the platen; for one copy it is thrown out of the way by ribbon-lifting mechanism, and in its extreme up position it is ready for the paper to be fed to it. Wire holders and guides keep it positioned, and reels with a mechanism secure it to its shaft. The usual ribbon still inks the top sheet.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** supplemental ribbon; ribbon holder; wire holder; reel; platen; carriage; ribbon-moving mechanism

**Numbers:** 4 sheets of drawings

**Improves on:**

- carbon paper: a supplemental ribbon replaces loose carbon sheets
- earlier multigraph attachments: easy change between single and multiple copies
