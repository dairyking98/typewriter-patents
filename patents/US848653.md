## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a machine adapted to be used in conjunction with a typewriter of any of the well-known constructions, and adapted to actuate such typewriter by operating the keys of the same.

**How it works (reading):** A separate keyboard machine, coupled to any typewriter, depresses the typewriter's keys and punches a record that can later drive the typewriter to make repeat copies.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Repeated copies of a typed document must be made by retyping each one by hand.

**Solution:** A separate keyboard machine, coupled to any typewriter, depresses the typewriter's keys and punches a record that can later drive the typewriter to make repeat copies.

**How it works:** Keys on the attachment press corresponding keys on the typewriter and punch a hole per key into a record sheet; running the record through the attachment replays the writing.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
