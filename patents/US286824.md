## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter of simple construction with a flat fixed platen and a shifting paper carriage.

**Solution:** Typebars fixed to an arc-shaped bed strike against a fixed flat platen, while a paper carriage on rails is advanced by a rack, pinion and pallet ratchet.

**How it works:** Typebars are attached to an arc-shaped bed, and extra beds for capitals can strike between those of the inner bed. An ink ribbon winds between two spools, and a fixed flat platen takes the impression. Rollers hold the paper and advance it a line when a lever pulls a pawl on a ratchet. The paper carriage runs on four grooved wheels on rails, and a rack and pinion with a ratchet and pawl and pallets in a ratchet wheel step it. Key levers connect to the typebars by rods, with a spacing bar on similar levers.
