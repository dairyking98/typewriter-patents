## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to provide a typewrit-ing machine which will permit of the operator writing continuously from one line to thevnext without having to impart to the carriage any motion other than that accompanying the ordinary printing with the type, thereby reducing the cost of manufacture, increasing the speed attainable on the machine, and materially reducing the noise.

**How it works (reading):** A cylindrical paper carrier turned a little by every key stroke and driven obliquely so it also creeps along its axis, writing in a helix with no carriage return.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return costs money, noise and speed; the writer should be able to type continuously from line to line.

**Solution:** A cylindrical paper carrier turned a little by every key stroke and driven obliquely so it also creeps along its axis, writing in a helix with no carriage return.

**How it works:** A paper cylinder rests on front and rear wheels. Typebars on a circular support all strike one point on the top of the cylinder. A pawl, depressed by a universal bar on every key stroke, steps a gear and wheel to rotate the cylinder a small arc. The rear wheels are mounted obliquely, so the cylinder also moves lengthwise by an amount that sets the line spacing. The paper is wound on the cylinder in a helix whose pitch equals the line spacing, held by rubber bands. A circular ribbon disc is turned stepwise by a band, pulleys and worm from the same shaft.
