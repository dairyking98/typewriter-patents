**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a mechanical type lever drive for writing, Calculators and similar machines, in which by pressing down a button part lever the drive linkage of the associated type lever is coupled to the mechanical drive and.

**How it works (reading):** A motor-driven gear sits between the drive member and power source; pressing a key couples the type lever's drive linkage to the mechanical drive and it uncouples automatically after the imprint, even with the key still held.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pendulum-return and constantly running drive shafts make type-lever drives noisy and prone to faulty switching; per-lever lifting disks are costly.

**Solution:** A motor-driven gear sits between the drive member and power source; pressing a key couples the type lever's drive linkage to the mechanical drive and it uncouples automatically after the imprint, even with the key still held.
