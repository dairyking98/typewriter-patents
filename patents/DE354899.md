## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine keyboard design)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to Calculating machines with a keyboard and consists of such a design of the keyboard, that each key, when it is struck, the key of the next lower numerical value takes with him.

**How it works (reading):** Each key carries a spur that engages the key of next lower value, so striking one key carries the adjacent lower keys down with it and the row can be made much more compact, with the pressed keys held down until the operation is complete.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculator keys must be spaced widely so that a finger striking one key does not also hit its neighbours.

**Solution:** Each key carries a spur that engages the key of next lower value, so striking one key carries the adjacent lower keys down with it and the row can be made much more compact, with the pressed keys held down until the operation is complete.
