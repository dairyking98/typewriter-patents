## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating stops cannot place numerals in decimal columns because the arrested platen position is fixed relative to the stop.

**Solution:** Selective means alter the arrested platen position relative to the tabulating stop, and the stop is positioned before the carriage is released.
