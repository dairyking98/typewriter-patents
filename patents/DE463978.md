## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (zero print gear adding machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a zero pressure gear for the printing unit of adding machines, Cash registers and the like, especially for machines that can be used as cash registers with item and sum printing as well as for pure arithmetic work as an adding machine can be used with two different calculators.

**How it works (reading):** Pairs of stop levers (non-pressure and zero levers) in front of each adjustment and type support member hold the type carrier in rest or zero position; key levers swing drive sectors that release them.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing types in an adding machine or cash register must set to zero automatically without a zero key.

**Solution:** Pairs of stop levers (non-pressure and zero levers) in front of each adjustment and type support member hold the type carrier in rest or zero position; key levers swing drive sectors that release them.

**Features:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
