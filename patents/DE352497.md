## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters write lines in the direction of platen rotation, so the text is hard to read from the operator's seat.

**Solution:** Type images are arranged to print upright on horizontally rotating platen lines, so the writer can read the text from the front, for Chinese script.
