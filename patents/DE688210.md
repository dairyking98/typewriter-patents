## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Price stops on ticket printing plates can be changed or tampered with and are hard to replace quickly.

**Solution:** A printing plate with a fixed type block and a releasable holder for interchangeable price stops, locked against fraud.

> Description Translated from German Printing plate for counter ticket printing machines is the subject of the invention is the special design of a printing plate for counter ticket printing machines, with the one carrying the printing block for the unchangeable text of the ticket Type plate a holder for exchangeable stops for controlling a printing unit or adder is releasably and lockably connected. In the event of a tariff change, this applies until a certain point in time the old prices and immediately afterwards the new ones. The exchange of the price notices must therefore be carried out as quickly as possible, especially since there is another one on the shelf Pressure control of the newly inserted price notices is necessary. For counter printers where the printing plate is used to print tickets lying inside the printer, guided and enclosed on all sides, has already been proposed a sheet metal containing the price notices as, step-shaped cutouts to be placed loosely on the back of the pressure plate, in order to avoid a tariff change to be able to replace quickly.
