## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to copy holders for typewriters and it consists in the combinations, constructions and arrangements herein described and claimed.

**How it works (reading):** A copy holder with an adjustable base and feed mechanism that moves copy when the typewriter is operated or manually.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The copy must be fed forward manually, and the copy holder is hard to adjust to the typewriter.

**Solution:** A copy holder with an adjustable base and feed mechanism that moves copy when the typewriter is operated or manually.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** adjustable base; feed mechanism; copy holder
