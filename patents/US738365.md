## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide improved means for controlling, gaging, and determining the step-by-step movement of the carriage across the frame of a type-writing machine.

**How it works (reading):** A bracket-mounted escapement shaft carries a pinion that engages the carriage feed-rack, with a holding detent and feed dog arranged to reduce friction between the detent, wheel and dog.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventor says friction in the carriage escapement, between the holding detent and the escapement wheel and between that wheel and the feed dog, makes step-by-step movement less controlled.

**Solution:** A bracket-mounted escapement shaft carries a pinion that engages the carriage feed-rack, with a holding detent and feed dog arranged to reduce friction between the detent, wheel and dog.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
