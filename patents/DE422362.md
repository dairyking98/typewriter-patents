## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Linkage from keys to typebars under a horizontal sheet has many pivots, play and wear.

**Solution:** A push-only linkage with two pivots: rear-arm push rod lifts an intermediate lever that swings the typebar.

**How it works:** Paper lies on a carriage moving only toward the keyboard, mounted on a second carriage adjustable along the writing direction. Each double-armed key lever carries at its rear end a push rod, spring-cushioned to the frame, that pushes an intermediate lever linked by a connecting rod to a typebar pivoting up onto the horizontal sheet. The pivots stay loaded in one direction so play cannot develop.

**Parts:** key lever; push rod; coil spring; intermediate lever; connecting rod; type lever; paper carriage
