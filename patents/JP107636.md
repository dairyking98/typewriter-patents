## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a typewriter that prints vertical columns by turning the paper cylinder, moving to the start of the next column at a different spacing and at a chosen level is slow and imprecise.

**Solution:** Two facing knobs pinched together first turn the cylinder back to an adjustable stop (the level of the next column's first character), then pulling one knob further releases the carriage to slide sideways by one spacer-block interval under an alternating-knife escapement.

**How it works:** The carriage frame, which carries the cylinder shaft, is constantly pulled one way by a spring-loaded band. Pushing the first knob turns a sector gear and so the cylinder backward until a stop lug on a ring gear at the cylinder end meets a raisable stop bar; the lug's position is set beforehand by releasing a pawl with a small knob. With the cylinder stopped, pulling the second, facing knob rocks a lever whose wedge pushes a conical-faced body along the shaft, which through short levers, a pin and a cam plate lifts a key-lever and its cross-bar. The cross-bar moves two stacked escapement plates whose knife edges enter and leave alternately between spacer blocks set in a rack bar on the carriage, so the carriage moves past one block to the next. The spacer blocks can be placed at equal or unequal distances and removed freely.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Line-spacing (sideways feed) device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a sideways-feed device for a typewriter that has a device for printing vertical columns by turning the paper cylinder. It combines: a push-back device by which the cylinder can be turned backward with the knob used for turning the cylinder; a reverse-stop device that adjusts and limits how far the cylinder is turned back; and a sideways-feed device by which, by pulling another knob facing the first, the cylinder carriage, once the cylinder's reverse turn has stopped, goes on to slide horizontally to the position of the next column at any spacing. Its object is that, after printing any column, to print at any position of the next column at any spacing, a simple movement of the facing knobs always first turns the cylinder back, along the column printed, to the same level as the set position in the next column, and then makes the horizontal sideways feed; so that columns at equal or unequal spacing and at any position can be printed at will, simply and accurately.
