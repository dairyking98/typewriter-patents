## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shifting case and holding the shift ties up a hand or finger, when both hands should be on the character keys.

**Solution:** A knee or foot lever on the typewriter stand, chained to the ordinary shift key, that holds the shift down as long as wanted.

**How it works:** A hinged standard slides out of a socket in the front edge of the stand, then folds down vertically. A lever pivoted on its front end has a chain from its short left end running through a slot in the stand to the shift key. The long right end is weighted so the lever tilts up and to the left at rest. Raising the right foot or knee pulls the chain and holds the shift key down; releasing returns it. The standard and lever push back into the socket and recess when not in use.

**Features:** [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** stand; socket; hinged standard; weighted lever; chain; shift-key

**Improves on:**

- Hand-operated shift key: foot or knee shift frees both hands
