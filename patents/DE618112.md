> Description Translated from German Device for the production of labeled with holding frames Wax matrices The invention relates to a device for producing with holding frames provided and labeled wax matrices. Making such wax matrices made due to imperfection of the existing facilities relatively great difficulties, since the known facilities a lettering of the wax plate only after the union could be done with the frame, so that in the one used to label the die Embossing device had to be provided with special guides and labeling The operator required special skills in order to buckle the frame prevent. The aim of the invention is to obtain a device in which initially the wax matrices are labeled and then connected to the frame. To this The purpose of the invention is according to an embossing device, e.g. B. a typewriter or the like, through which a wax strip for writing as a continuous band is, one of the wax strips after labeling, from the embossing device, preferably from their keyboard, downstream controllable device in which the wax strip is combined with the holding frame and optionally by a cutting device is broken down into individual matrices.
