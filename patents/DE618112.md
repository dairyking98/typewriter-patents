The aim of the invention is to obtain a device in which initially the wax matrices are labeled and then connected to the frame. To this The purpose of the invention is according to an embossing device, e.g. B. a typewriter or the like, through which a wax strip for writing as a continuous band is, one of the wax strips after labeling, from the embossing device, preferably from their keyboard, downstream controllable device in which the wax strip is combined with the holding frame and optionally by a cutting device is broken down into individual matrices.
