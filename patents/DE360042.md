> Description Translated from German Universal rail, the invention relates to perfection on universal rails and universal rail frames of typewriters and intended simplified training of these parts as well as the facilitation of their work. The essence of the invention is that the levers are used to move of the universal rail on curved stop tongues formed from one piece with this act that, from one edge of the shape having a curved cross-section and thereby stiffened rail starting, protrude over the top of the rail and are adjustable by bending, so that the lever pressure without damaging torque in the direction of the actual rail is transferred to this. Is beneficial a narrow insertion gap between the edge of each tongue and the free edge of the rail for a flat tool to bend open, and the continuous rail part is designed tubular in such a way that, together with the tongues, it has almost a Hollow cylinder without. Forms kink or change in curvature at the transition point.
