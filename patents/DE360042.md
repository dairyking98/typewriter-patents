## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to perfection on universal rails and universal rail frames of typewriters and intended simplified training of these parts as well as the facilitation of their work.

**How it works (reading):** Adjustable curved stop tongues are formed in one piece with a tubular universal rail, bent to adjust, and the rail, side arms and rear rod are stamped and bent from a single sheet-metal blank into one frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Universal rail stop pieces made as threaded pins or separate protective fingers do not transmit key-lever pressure cleanly, and the frame is costly to build from separate parts.

**Solution:** Adjustable curved stop tongues are formed in one piece with a tubular universal rail, bent to adjust, and the rail, side arms and rear rod are stamped and bent from a single sheet-metal blank into one frame.
