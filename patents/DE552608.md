## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed for simultaneous multi-type strikes is complicated and heavy.

**Solution:** A paper gripper rides on the platen axle with play and is driven by a handlebar from an angle lever, so one tension spring both advances the paper and drops the keys.
