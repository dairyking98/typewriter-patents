## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen of rubber on a wooden core changes length after manufacture, so it does not fit between the platen frame ends, and fitting by grinding the hubs is slow and costly.

**Solution:** One platen head carries an externally threaded adjustable hub that screws into the head to take up play, and a set screw on the outer end fastens the hub to the axle after adjustment.
