## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Aprons or guides for the printing section movable for insertion or release of sheets

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to paper advancement and the associated devices of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded sheet-metal rail hangs from the carriage and carries a holder for two feed rollers at each end, so pressure on all four rollers is evenly spread; a secondary front roller set is attached through a narrow flexure with bent arms that also ease roller release.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Feed-roller pressure on the paper is uneven and the paper advance unreliable, and the design must stay light and cheap for portable machines.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded sheet-metal rail hangs from the carriage and carries a holder for two feed rollers at each end, so pressure on all four rollers is evenly spread; a secondary front roller set is attached through a narrow flexure with bent arms that also ease roller release.
