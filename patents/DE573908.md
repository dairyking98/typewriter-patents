## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The pawl of a power drive for type bar machines needed a reliable way to stay engaged with the ratchet wheel during the stroke and release afterwards.

**Solution:** An arm on the pawl carrier is joined to the pawl by a spring whose pull line crosses the axis, so it holds the pawl in engagement until the arm hits a stop that flips the spring back.

**How it works:** A continuously turning power shaft carries a ratchet wheel. A pawl carrier sits loosely on the shaft and drives the type bar linkage. Depressing a key lever moves a release lever that brings the pawl into the ratchet. An over-center spring then holds it engaged. When the arm meets its stop surface the spring is moved back and the pawl disengages. Two key levers per bar choose between plain advance and lift plus advance.
