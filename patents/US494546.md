## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes; Plural tools selectively engageable with single drive

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to stencil machines, and has for its object to produce a machine whereby proper characters may be cut or punched in suitable plates or blanks, for the purpose of making stencils, shipping brands and like articles.
