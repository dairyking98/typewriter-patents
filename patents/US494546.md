## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes; Plural tools selectively engageable with single drive

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to stencil machines, and has for its object to produce a machine whereby proper characters may be cut or punched in suitable plates or blanks, for the purpose of making stencils, shipping brands and like articles.

**How it works (reading):** A stencil machine with spring-loaded male punches and matching dies in a fixed frame, with a movable carriage that carries the plunger and a ratchet feed that positions the blank.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil and shipping-brand characters must be cut by hand, and a machine is needed to punch them in plates.

**Solution:** A stencil machine with spring-loaded male punches and matching dies in a fixed frame, with a movable carriage that carries the plunger and a ratchet feed that positions the blank.
