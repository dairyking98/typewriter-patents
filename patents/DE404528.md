## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register totals printing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a Cash register with adding and printing mechanism, for which, apart from the individual amounts, too Totals can be printed and the same organs (drive segments), which at Registering Individual Amounts the Adding set the wheels, germinating a final sum these up from the adding wheels (read Transfer printing unit.

**How it works (reading):** An axially shifting swing frame carries one wheel set, switched between the register gearing and the total printing gearing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing a total on a cash register needs the wheels moved away from the individual amount drive.

**Solution:** An axially shifting swing frame carries one wheel set, switched between the register gearing and the total printing gearing.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
