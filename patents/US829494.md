## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A standard set of about forty type-bars cannot hold the extra characters needed for Russian and other languages, and the long double types at the ends of the segment leave no room to add bars.

**Solution:** Auxiliary type-bars are added at the ends of the system, each carrying two types for upper and lower case, arranged so little extra space is used and the main system is unchanged.
