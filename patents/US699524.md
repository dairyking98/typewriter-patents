## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Drive control means for carriage movement

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved index-plate or graduated scale, being particu larly adapted for use as a letter-space-indieating scale for type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A letter-space index plate has an open-sided frame holding an opaque coated back piece with graduations, covered by a transparent facing, so the marks are protected and stay clear without glare.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Polished index scales glare and their graduations get worn or obscured.

**Solution:** A letter-space index plate has an open-sided frame holding an opaque coated back piece with graduations, covered by a transparent facing, so the marks are protected and stay clear without glare.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
