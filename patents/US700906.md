## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Teaching typing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improved typewriting clavier, the object of the invention being to provide a machine of this character whose touch will be as near as possible identical with the complete machine and which will when a key is struck give forth a sound similar to that of type striking the platen on an ordinary type-writing machine.

**How it works (reading):** A typewriting clavier with keys that feel like the real machine, a sound resembling type striking the platen, a word counter, and a copy holder, with the working parts on one removable frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Practice on a typewriter needs a silent-keyed stand-in that has the same touch as a machine and makes a sound like type striking the platen.

**Solution:** A typewriting clavier with keys that feel like the real machine, a sound resembling type striking the platen, a word counter, and a copy holder, with the working parts on one removable frame.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
