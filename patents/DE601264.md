## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicating typewriters where each writer has a full typewriter with its own selector and remote electrical control are very complicated and expensive.

**Solution:** One continuously rotating contact arm sends current surges to several writers, each with a type wheel turning synchronously with the arm; a keyboard sets contact stops, and the printing surface is struck at the instant the correct character passes.

**How it works:** Because all type wheels rotate in step with the contact arm, no angular setting is needed per writer; electrical pulses timed to the rotation strike the selected character.
