## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line locks stop the keys too early or need a finger key to complete.

**Solution:** The carriage and a space key together move a locking obstruction into the path of the printing keys at line end.

**How it works:** The carriage near line end throws a part into position, and then a depression of the dividing (space) key completes the movement of the line lock so the keys are locked. This lets the operator finish a word or syllable without premature locking. Made of few simple parts.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** carriage; line lock; space key; locking obstruction; printing key
