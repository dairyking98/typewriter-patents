## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon vibrator lifts the ribbon by a fixed amount, so only one widthwise portion or colour field of the ribbon can be used.

**Solution:** A controllable ribbon vibrator whose stroke can be varied by a finger piece so different widthwise portions or colour fields of the ribbon are used at will, or the ribbon is dispensed with.

**How it works:** On a Remington platen-shift typebar machine, a ribbon runs between two spools, back and forth, through guides in a vibrator made of two parallel wire loops on a body. The vibrator slides up and down on a screw-and-slot guide and is lifted during printing. A bell crank and bracket, set from a finger piece with indicating devices on the front, vary the vibrator's movement so a different part of the ribbon width sits at the print point. The platen shifts on a rail carried by a shifting frame raised by shift keys.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon spool; ribbon vibrator; wire guide loop; bell crank; finger piece; shift rail; platen; typebar; universal bar
