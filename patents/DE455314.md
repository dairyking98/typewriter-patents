## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen-shift key that lifts the roller with fixed stops is hard to press, while a purely spring-loaded one may not reach the end position reliably.

**Solution:** The shift key levers first act on the lifting links through a spring, then through fixed adjustable stops, giving an elastic keystroke with a positive end position.

**How it works:** Two shift key levers joined by a rod pivot loosely on a shaft that carries links connected to the lifting rail for the paper roller frame. An extension on one key lever first presses a spring-held angle lever that moves the links elastically. Further travel brings a bore edge against a stop screw, while the other lever's extension meets an adjustable eccentric stop, so the links are forced up and the rail lifts the roller to the shift position. Eccentric pins set the end position exactly and the spring tension is changed by choosing a notch.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** shift key lever; connecting rod; link; lifting rail; paper roller frame; angle lever; spring; eccentric stop; stop screw

**Improves on:**
