## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator has to take a hand from the keyboard to throw the carriage back and turn the roll for a new line.

**Solution:** A lever on the keyboard side bar pulls cords over pulleys and discs to shift the carriage lever, returning the carriage and turning the roll without moving the hands.

**How it works:** A lever pivoted on the keyboard side bar pushes a link to a second lever. The lower end of the second lever pulls a flexible member over a small pulley onto a small disc joined to a larger disc on a bracket. A second flexible member from the larger disc runs over two pulleys to a shifting arm, which crosses the back of the frame and moves in guides. A link from its forward end connects to the ordinary carriage shifting handle, so one push on the lever returns the carriage and turns the roll one space.
