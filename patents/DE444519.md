> Description Translated from German Paper guides for typewriters. The invention is a Paper guide for typewriters, calculating machines and the like, in which in an in a known manner, one or more paper pressure tongues resilient against the platen are provided. The purpose of the invention is to design a device of this type in such a way that that the paper is easily inserted under the tongues and when the paper holder is folded up can be continued unhindered, and that furthermore at the same time more favorable Contact pressure the possibility is preserved on carbon copies to be able to easily erase. To achieve these advantages, the paper guide is characterized according to the invention in that the pressure tongue, which is expediently designed in the form of a frame , on a paper holding rail that can be folded up and moved lengthways Sleeve or ferrule is attached and, of. this one reaching backwards, the paper vertically above the roller axis or even further backwards against the platen presses.
