## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates particularly to type bar hangers and has for its main object the provision of means for adjusting the hanger arms to the type bar pivot ends in the first place when the type bars and hangers are assembled and subsequently when wear takes place between the pivot ends and the bearings in the hanger.

**How it works (reading):** Adjustable hanger arms on the type bar segment take up wear at the pivot ends when assembled and later.
