## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Power-driven ribbon feeding with reversal, end-of-run warning and ribbon positions for a two-case platen-shift machine.

**Solution:** A ribbon mechanism with a single reversing key, automatic stop at end of run with bell, and ribbon guide keeping the same ribbon portion at the print point in both platen positions.

**How it works:** A power-driven feed winds the ribbon between spools and stops automatically at the end to prevent injury, giving audible notice. A reversing part always moved the same way reverses the feed whichever direction it runs. The ribbon is vibrated to and from the print point in both platen positions, can be held stationary in print position, moved to expose the printing point or held out, with the feed thrown out when the ribbon is held away. Shown with type bars with two sets of type and a shifting platen.
