## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon holders are built for one machine width and paper-rule distance and do not fit other typewriters.

**Solution:** A bow-shaped ribbon holder whose supports slide along the legs and whose back part can be split, so the holder adjusts to any machine.
