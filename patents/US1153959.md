## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier ribbon-reversing mechanism (US1105081) was costly and complicated.

**Solution:** A simplified ribbon feed with a clutch and ratchet driven by the universal bar, reversed by a suspended yoke that is shifted automatically when a spool empties or by hand.

**How it works:** Ribbon reels on vertical shafts are driven through bevel pinions and short shafts carrying clutch members. A sleeve with clutch teeth and a ratchet wheel is stepped by a dog on the universal bar. A yoke hung from pivoted links embraces the ratchet and shifts the sleeve between the two clutches, with a handle projecting for manual reversal. Each reel hub has a spring hinged section held closed by the ribbon; when the ribbon runs out it opens and a spring-loaded control lever drops a roller into the recess, pushing the yoke over to reverse the feed.
