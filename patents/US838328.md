## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guides in the printing zone, e.g. guides for preventing contact of conveyed sheets with printhead

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to overcome this difliculty and to rovide devices which may be readily ap lied to existing machines, especially those 0 the Underwood frontstrike type, which will hold the entire top edge and the entire bottom edge of the paper against the platen, so as to permit writing very close to the edges, this bein especially desirable at the bottom edge of the pa er.

**How it works (reading):** Thin springy clips slip onto the front roll rod and press paper-guiding fingers against the platen near the printing line, without altering the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** It is hard to write close to the top and bottom edges of paper because it bulges away from the platen.

**Solution:** Thin springy clips slip onto the front roll rod and press paper-guiding fingers against the platen near the printing line, without altering the machine.
