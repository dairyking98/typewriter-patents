## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Crossing out or erasing matter already written, and inserting between lines, are needed when dictated text changes; the printing time must be controlled.

**Solution:** Index-bearing cam leaves on a carrier engage a connecting mechanism to control the time of the printing operation; the OCR is partial, so the exact linkage is not fully recoverable.
