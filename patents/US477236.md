## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon adjusted transversely

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to produce ribbon-feeding devices which -will feed the ribbon lengthwise, and also lcause it to travel widthwise over the printingpoint,so as to present practically its entire surface to the printing-point while it passes from one spool to the other and to accomplish these movements with the simplest possible mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Spools are carried in cranked supports that revolve bodily with parallel axes so the ribbon at the printing point moves in a circle across its width, plus a lengthwise feed per revolution.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbons are used only in a narrow strip, wasting most of their width.

**Solution:** Spools are carried in cranked supports that revolve bodily with parallel axes so the ribbon at the printing point moves in a circle across its width, plus a lengthwise feed per revolution.

**How it works:** Two spools sit in U-shaped supports joined by a frame bar so their axes stay parallel. Idle cranks and a driving crank of equal throw rotate the frame and spools, making the ribbon at the print point trace a circle about half the ribbon width in radius. An elliptical ratchet wheel driven by a pawl on a vibrating arm, rocked by the universal bar under the key levers, slows the drive mid-ribbon and speeds it near the margins so the ribbon wears evenly. The ribbon also shifts lengthwise after each revolution and the drive can reverse.
