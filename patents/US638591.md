## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in actuating mechanism for keyboard-operated machines of the class used for the dissemination ofintelligence, einbracinglinotype, typesettingmachines, type-writing machines, and the like.

**How it works (reading):** An actuating mechanism that works keys of key-operated machines automatically from received matter while leaving hand operation possible.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboard machines such as linotypes and typewriters need skilled operators.

**Solution:** An actuating mechanism that works keys of key-operated machines automatically from received matter while leaving hand operation possible.

**How it works:** Sixteen sheets of drawings; the excerpt gives only objects: a device that actuates the keyboard automatically (telegraphic feed to newspapers). Mechanism not in the excerpt.

**Features:**

- **Uses:** [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** actuating device; keyboard machine
