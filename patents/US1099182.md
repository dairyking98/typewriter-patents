## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (manifolding apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer; Hand crank

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to manifolding apparatus for delivering invariable constant lengths of paper within a measured stroke or rotation of an operating crank handle.

**How it works (reading):** A member inside the roll moves concentrically to vary the circumference so the roll matches the paper length.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A fixed master feed roll circumference must match each blank length exactly.

**Solution:** A member inside the roll moves concentrically to vary the circumference so the roll matches the paper length.

**How it works:** Internal concentric member changes the feed roll circumference to match a prescribed paper length.
