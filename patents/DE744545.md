**How it works (reading):** The special key is locked after it is pressed, so the feedback signal is sent once only after the connection is set up.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Name-giver key on a teleprinter may be pressed by accident during a message.

**Solution:** The special key is locked after it is pressed, so the feedback signal is sent once only after the connection is set up.
