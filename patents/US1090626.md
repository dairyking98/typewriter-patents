## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Golf scores need to be recorded and shown on a score card in a legible printed form.

**Solution:** A score-recording device with a platen drum that holds a paper score card and a row of number printing wheels on a fixed pinion shaft, each wheel printing a score digit; the platen is ratcheted to advance the printed score.
