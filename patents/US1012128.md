## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike type actions should start easily, accelerate the typebar near the platen, keep leverage and key dip uniform, and stop typebars rebounding from the type rest into neighbours.

**Solution:** A sub-lever type action with added means to prevent typebar rebound from the type rest.

**How it works:** Key levers act through sub-levers and links on segmentally arranged typebars that strike the platen front, giving an accelerated movement. Several modifications are shown, including one with rebound-preventing means at the type rest. The opening text does not describe the details.
