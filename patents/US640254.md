## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improve the Krauss and Anderson typebar machine with an obliterating key, visible writing, carriage locking and many other refinements.

**Solution:** A typebar machine with obliterating key, hinged paper carrier that exposes the printing, carriage shift for different type styles, and end-of-line carriage and key lock with bell.

**How it works:** The opening lists the features: an obliterating key; a hinged paper carrier raised by a key to expose characters; forward or rearward carriage shift from centre to present paper to type of different styles; synchronous locking of carriage and keys at a set point, with unlocking for extra steps and relocking; anti-bounce for the carriage; double or single line spacing; type-lever stops; a detachable platen with cushion; and a bell before the first lock. The detailed mechanism is not in the text.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** obliterating key; hinged paper carrier; carriage-moving wheel; type-lever; detachable platen; bell
