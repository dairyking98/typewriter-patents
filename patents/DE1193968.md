**How it works (reading):** Electromagnetically driven two-armed writing levers with impression surfaces pivot on a common shaft and strike the paper, with magnets holding each arm to select the pivot point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Character writing from dots or strokes on moving paper is hard for large sheets and needs a moving writing spiral.

**Solution:** Electromagnetically driven two-armed writing levers with impression surfaces pivot on a common shaft and strike the paper, with magnets holding each arm to select the pivot point.

There are writing devices known in which characters from points or Strokes are composed by moving a strip of paper while moving in front of this a writing spiral turns and an imprint of a drawing element through the stop of a blade-shaped anchor against the back of the Paper strip takes place. When it comes to labeling sheets of paper, it is now a matter of course proved to be disadvantageous that both the writing spiral and on the back of the paper the cutting edge is moved. Especially when using larger paper formats further difficulties arise: The paper must float freely at the writing point be performed, what when clamping the sheet, especially if at the same time carbon copies should be made, brings difficulties with it.
