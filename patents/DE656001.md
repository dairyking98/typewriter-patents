**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper trolley switching device, preferably for typewriters with a low-noise impression, where the types in the imprint point of the action of the buttons are withdrawn and in which the step switching of the paper trolley caused by the type carrier with the mediation of intermediate links will.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type carriers bounce back against drive parts after printing and cause repeated carriage stepping.

**Solution:** Intermediate links are arranged so the carriage release moves only through less than half the type travel.

**How it works:** The switching elements release the carriage during a short part of the type travel only.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
