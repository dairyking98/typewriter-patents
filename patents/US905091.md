## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen can turn slightly by accident during writing, especially with stiff cards, and the operator does not notice until characters are out of place.

**Solution:** An auxiliary lever on the platen axle, alongside the detent release lever, brakes the platen by acting on the detent pin so the platen is held more firmly against rotation when needed.
