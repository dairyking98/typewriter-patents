## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On Remington machines the carriage can only be moved or the cylinder reversed by pressing the thumb-piece or turning the cylinder by hand.

**Solution:** Compound thumb levers on the carriage lever release the line-space pawl or engage a reverse pawl on a toothed cylinder head.

**How it works:** Two curved compound levers pivot on the carriage lever, with a slotted shoulder and springs. The thumb on lever H presses the line-spacing pawl off the cog head so the carriage lever moves the carriage without turning the cylinder. Pressing levers H and I together brings a reverse spacing pawl onto the cogs of a replacement right cylinder head, turning the cylinder backward. A spring lifts the reverse pawl off when idle.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** carriage lever; compound lever; line-spacing pawl; reverse spacing pawl; cogged cylinder head; spring; slotted shoulder; thumb-piece

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter: adds reverse line space and pawl release without hand on cylinder
