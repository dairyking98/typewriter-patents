## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for returning the carriage of a typewriting machine, to begin a new line of writing.

**How it works (reading):** A pneumatic cylinder on the carriage, operated by a key-controlled valve, returns the carriage while air is applied and turns the platen through a link.
