## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keys on a typewriter must be released before the next stroke, which slows typing and causes jamming.

**Solution:** A key whose stem releases the key-lever at a certain point so a spring returns the lever quickly, independent of the finger.

**How it works:** The key stem passes loosely through a collar on the lever end, with a double spring in a slot of the stem. Pressing the key drives the lever to a point where it is let go from the key, and the spring or a weight on the typebar returns lever and typebar to rest before the finger rises. The next key can then be struck without releasing the first, helping the paper carrier and ribbon return quickly.
