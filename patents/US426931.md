## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a machine having a series of type-bars printing at a common point, which may be operated and printing performed, the type-bar and typelever being enabled to resume their initial position independently of the key, whereby a succeeding key maybe operated without releasing or relieving from pressure any key SPECIFICATION forming part of Letters Patent No.
