## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A keyboard type-wheel machine needs the wheel to be stopped at exactly the right character and held steady while it strikes the platen, without rebound blurring the print.

**Solution:** Pressing a key raises a stop pin and rotates the type wheel through a driving lever and gear until a geared stop arm hits that pin, after which continued key travel tilts the wheel frame to the platen while locks hold the wheel and arm against rebound.

**How it works:** Each key lifts its own stop pin in a straight row and, through a link and bail, works a driving lever that strikes a shoulder on a disk and oscillates a segment, rotating the type wheel through a gear. The wheel is geared by a pinion to a second segment with a stop arm; the arm swings until it meets the raised pin, which stops the wheel at the chosen character. A locking bail holds the wheel frame from tilting until the key's inner end releases it; further key travel then pulls the frame down so the wheel dips onto the platen. A toothed disk on the wheel engages a fixed arm, and a spring pawl drops into the disk at the start of the dip, so the wheel is held against oscillation. A bevelled stop arm with eyes catches over the pin so the arm cannot rebound; a lever shifts the wheel shaft for case.
