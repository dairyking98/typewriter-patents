## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special processing for irreversibly adding or changing the sheet copy material characteristics or its appearance, e.g. stamping, annotation printing, punching

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to machines of that class which employ a flat platen over which the printing mechanism is movable laterally and longitudinally for letter and line spacing.

**How it works (reading):** A platen attachment with hinged transfer elements that are inserted between manifold folds and moved apart to free the sheet, with a clamping and cutting device at the front edge of the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Extensive manifold records such as railroad accounts are hard to produce on a flat-platen machine because the plies must be fed and held as the sheet advances.

**Solution:** A platen attachment with hinged transfer elements that are inserted between manifold folds and moved apart to free the sheet, with a clamping and cutting device at the front edge of the platen.

**Features:** [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
