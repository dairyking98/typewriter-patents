## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rotary type-wheel machine with a feed roll on a vibrating frame needs a better ribbon arrangement, paper feeding, bell and a way to make the type.

**Solution:** An annular ribbon encloses the type wheel, the paper feeder is guided against a single roll, a stationary bell is struck by a tappet on the vibrating carriage, and the type is an electrotype strip fitted in a rim groove.

**How it works:** The type wheel turns on a spindle; its flanged rim forms a cylindrical groove into which an electrotype strip of types is fitted, trimmed to size with a punch and die. The platen feed roll sits on a carriage sliding on a frame that vibrates toward and away from the wheel, carrying the paper to the type. An annular ribbon on two hoops of different size surrounds the wheel at a small distance from the types, supported by a circular carrier pivoted loosely on the spindle top, so that different parts of the ribbon present themselves as the wheel turns. A hammer on a lever arm rings a stationary bell when a tappet on the carriage reaches it.
