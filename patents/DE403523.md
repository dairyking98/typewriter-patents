## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shift and control keys feel heavy because the carriage resists more than a typebar; twin toggle keys outside the frame tilt and widen the machine.

**Solution:** Make the grips of the shift and control keys spoon-shaped so the finger bears on a larger area and the extra resistance is less noticeable.

**How it works:** The shift keys keep their usual place behind one another beside the keyboard inside the frame. Their grip plates are dished to fit the finger, giving about 250 sq mm contact versus about 50 for a letter key. Resistance per unit area then matches a letter key, and the finger can roll from the short-lever key to the longer-lever one.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** shift key; control key; spoon-shaped grip plate; shift lever

**Numbers:** contact area 50 sq mm for letter key; about 250 sq mm for spoon key; 50 g letter key force; about 250 g platen shift resistance
