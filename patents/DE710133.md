## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to line break devices of the paper strip in typewriters and calculating machines, namely in such? machines, which have a platen hitting the types when printing, the latter driven by a pawl mounted eccentrically to its pivot axis will.

**How it works (reading):** The platen pivot sits inside the pawl bearing so the axis distance equals one line spacing, with no separate stop arm, and a spring keeps the pawl on the platen teeth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The eccentric line-switching pawl takes much space and has dead play.

**Solution:** The platen pivot sits inside the pawl bearing so the axis distance equals one line spacing, with no separate stop arm, and a spring keeps the pawl on the platen teeth.
