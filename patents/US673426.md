## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention has reference to improvements in type-writing machines, and particularly to what is commonly known as the escapement--that is, the means whereby the step-by-step motion of the carriage is accomplished upon the depression and subsequent release of any of the finger-keys or of the space-bar.
