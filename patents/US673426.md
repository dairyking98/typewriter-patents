## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention has reference to improvements in type-writing machines, and particularly to what is commonly known as the escapement--that is, the means whereby the step-by-step motion of the carriage is accomplished upon the depression and subsequent release of any of the finger-keys or of the space-bar.

**How it works (reading):** Each tooth of the space-rack has a face at right angles to the rack axis and an oblique face; a pivoted stepping-dog engages the straight faces and a rigid space-cam engages the oblique faces, which together return the spacing rocker to its normal position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The escapement is heavy, slow, and complicated, and the machine does not act lightly and quickly.

**Solution:** Each tooth of the space-rack has a face at right angles to the rack axis and an oblique face; a pivoted stepping-dog engages the straight faces and a rigid space-cam engages the oblique faces, which together return the spacing rocker to its normal position.
