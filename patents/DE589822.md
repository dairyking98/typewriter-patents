**How it works (reading):** Two ribbon spools are offset axially inside the cylinder and the ribbon is led over skewed guide rods, so it crosses the printing segment helically; ratchet pawls with drive levers reverse the spools.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A straight ribbon path over a forme cylinder wastes ribbon when the printed text runs around the cylinder.

**Solution:** Two ribbon spools are offset axially inside the cylinder and the ribbon is led over skewed guide rods, so it crosses the printing segment helically; ratchet pawls with drive levers reverse the spools.
