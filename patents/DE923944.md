## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper tape circuit, punched card printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering

**How it works (reading):** Extra paper feed is set per card hole that controls a stop limiting the paper switching element when groups change.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic paper advance between card groups must be set by hand on the machine rather than by card holes.

**Solution:** Extra paper feed is set per card hole that controls a stop limiting the paper switching element when groups change.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
