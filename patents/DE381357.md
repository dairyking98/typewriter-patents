## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type lever drive with an intermediate lever was complex and tall, and removing a type lever needed the comb removed.

**Solution:** A button lever drives an intermediate lever, which lifts the type lever and then a handlebar knocks it down; a comb holds the levers and a slot lets a lever be removed.
