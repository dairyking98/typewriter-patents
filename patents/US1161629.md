## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check writer selector attachment) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cheque stamping machines

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to new and useful improvements in selector attachments for check writing machines and consists in the provision of a simple and efiicient attachment to a checkwriter to facilitate the setting of the type to print certain words, numbers or characters.

**How it works (reading):** A key is depressed and the pointer thrown to actuate a stop lug, and a spring returns a pawl solidly against the stop so the selected character is locked in position before printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check writers need the operator to place a pointer exactly on the scale line for each character, and any deviation causes a printing error.

**Solution:** A key is depressed and the pointer thrown to actuate a stop lug, and a spring returns a pawl solidly against the stop so the selected character is locked in position before printing.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Pointer over a chart](../features/sel-pointer-chart.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
