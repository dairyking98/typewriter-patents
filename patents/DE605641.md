## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switching device for writing, writing arithmetic and similar machines with easy switching, especially those where the type lever segment is raised and lowered opposite the paper trolley.

**How it works (reading):** The segment is moved by a U-shaped swing arm and two knee joints that are limited by one common stop, so acceleration is smooth at both ends.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hard switching stops wear and cause noise and vibration, and elastic inserts change the switching stroke.

**Solution:** The segment is moved by a U-shaped swing arm and two knee joints that are limited by one common stop, so acceleration is smooth at both ends.
