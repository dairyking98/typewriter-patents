## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register two-wheel printing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Printing device for cash registers with two type wheels for the control and the check slip or the receipt by a common one of the adjustment members set of driven wheels and for printing the control and des Check stripes and the receipt slip jerkily and against fixed ones Pressure bars are struck.

**How it works (reading):** Two type wheel sets are driven by a common drive so that the first set swings out once, twice or three times for no check, a single check or a double check, while the second set prints the receipt twice.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cash register must print a control strip, a check slip and a receipt, and the number of checks issued varies with the key setting.

**Solution:** Two type wheel sets are driven by a common drive so that the first set swings out once, twice or three times for no check, a single check or a double check, while the second set prints the receipt twice.
