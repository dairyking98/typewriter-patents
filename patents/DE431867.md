## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to power-driven typewriters with reciprocating, by spring force for The type lever is driven faster and retracted again by the engine Universal rail.

**How it works (reading):** A continuously running motor shaft carries several friction clutches (for free advance, free retraction, line feed and a spare), engaged through change gears set by buttons; the type lever rail is driven by a spring and returned by the motor, with a lock that holds the type lever drive during carriage shift.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Free carriage advance, free retraction, carriage return and line feed in a power-driven typewriter need several mechanical sequences that are hard to make without interference.

**Solution:** A continuously running motor shaft carries several friction clutches (for free advance, free retraction, line feed and a spare), engaged through change gears set by buttons; the type lever rail is driven by a spring and returned by the motor, with a lock that holds the type lever drive during carriage shift.
