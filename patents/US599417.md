## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improving the Remington No. 6 ribbon motion, carriage stop collars and escapement.

**Solution:** Ribbon moving mechanism, adjustable stop collars on a toothed bar, and a revised carriage escapement.

**How it works:** A rear bar with teeth matching the carriage rack carries slidable stop collars. A cam shifts the ribbon crosswise, and left spool movement is made by a frame. Escapement mechanism is shown in section.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)

**Parts:** bar; stop collar; carriage; feed rack; ribbon spool; cam; escapement

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 6: improved ribbon, stop and escapement
