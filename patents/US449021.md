## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage needs to be moved across for spacing and returned, and the platen turned for line feed, by an improved arrangement.

**Solution:** A rod with a spring-supported rack bar parallel to the carriage guide, engaging a toothed wheel, moves the carriage and turns the platen, with double cams.

**How it works:** The usual helical spring draws the carriage by a strap to the line start. A rod parallel to the guide rod, with journal lugs, carries a horizontal rack bar held up by springs and slotted so it moves only vertically. The rack engages a toothed wheel to move the carriage for spacing, and lets it return automatically or by hand; double cams and a platen-turning apparatus perform line feed.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; guide rod; rack rod; rack bar; journal lug; toothed wheel; helical spring; strap; double cam; platen-turning apparatus; spacing bar
