## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control strip movement regulation)

**How it works (reading):** The swing arm carries an inclined guide slot in which a guide roller engages, so the swing-out and return of the arm drives the step-by-step take-up roller through a friction clutch, and the strip is wound at the same time as it advances.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The control strip needs two separate drives, one for the swing arm that relaxes the strip and one for the take-up roller.

**Solution:** The swing arm carries an inclined guide slot in which a guide roller engages, so the swing-out and return of the arm drives the step-by-step take-up roller through a friction clutch, and the strip is wound at the same time as it advances.
