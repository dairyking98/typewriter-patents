## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Manifold copies must be made with one operation and removed from the platen automatically.

**Solution:** A carbon ribbon with a tension roll rewinding it is wound about the platen with the manifold sheet, and copies are automatically removed.
