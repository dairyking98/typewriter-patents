## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines, but more particularly to tabnlating mechanism therefor.

**How it works (reading):** Two independent catch devices, a variable denominational catch and a tabulator unit-catch, each connected to the carriage-release so the carriage is freed when either is moved into operative position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops must catch the carriage both at variable denominational points and at a fixed point, and each catch must release the carriage without interfering with the other.

**Solution:** Two independent catch devices, a variable denominational catch and a tabulator unit-catch, each connected to the carriage-release so the carriage is freed when either is moved into operative position.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** variable denominational catch; tabulator unit-catch
