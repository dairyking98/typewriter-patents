## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be rotated by a chosen number of spaces, which the existing line-spacing arrangement does not allow in a simple way.

**Solution:** Pivoted plates, pins and a stop limit the rotation of the platen ratchet to any chosen number of teeth from one upward.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
