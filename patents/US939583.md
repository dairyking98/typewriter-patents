## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type bars deflect sideways in printing and can lose alignment at their bearings.

**Solution:** A simple type-bar bearing with bearing disks and hangers that guide each type bar against lateral deflection, with an alternative segment-and-hanger construction.
