## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, sure platen sideways feed is needed for a typewriter whose type drum turns and rises and falls.

**Solution:** One lever links the printing shaft to an L-lever with a catch pawl on the platen rack, so each print stroke steps the platen one tooth.

**How it works:** A lever pivoted on a fixed pivot bears at its front end on the lower end of the printing shaft that passes through the middle of the type drum. Its rear end presses on the horizontal arm of an L-shaped lever under the platen, which carries a pawl engaging a feed rack across the platen underside. Pressing the printing shaft makes the pawl engage the next tooth, and when the shaft returns a spring on the L-lever pulls the platen sideways one tooth. The drum is raised and lowered by a gear meshing with teeth on its outer shaft, and carries three-tier columns of types in holders struck by the same lever swing.
