## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing must work with wide paper that projects past the platen ends, on a shorter, lighter carriage.

**Solution:** Compact line-spacing parts between the platen and carriage allow wide paper to pass them, with adjustable single, double or triple spacing and a friction brake that holds the platen.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
