## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a Line feed device for typewriters and similar machines, in which Interline spacing with only one line index wheel and two ratchets of different lengths are done in such a way that these ratchets as well as the two associated locking rollers alternating with the ratchet teeth of the line indexing wheel come into engagement.

**How it works (reading):** A single double-width ratchet wheel is used with two pawls of different lengths, one shifting by a half division so the spacing can change between lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-ratchet line switches are jerky and wear fast at small tooth pitches.

**Solution:** A single double-width ratchet wheel is used with two pawls of different lengths, one shifting by a half division so the spacing can change between lines.
