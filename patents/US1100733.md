## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen shift, shift lock and release devices on a front-strike typebar machine need accurate platen alignment to the printing point and easy shift action.

**Solution:** A carriage raised on parallel links, counterbalanced by springs, with eccentric bolts to adjust platen alignment and a latch that releases on pressing either of two shift keys.

**How it works:** The carriage base rail rides on ball bearings on a shift rail suspended by parallel links from the frame; the rear link ends pivot on eccentric bolts that fine-tune platen alignment. A spring counterbalances the carriage and adjustable stops fix the lower position. Two shift key levers on a rock shaft lift the carriage by pushing rollers on arms from the shift rail. A bell-crank latch locks the carriage in lower case and is withdrawn by a spring in the lost motion before the key engages. A shift-lock key holds the upper-case position.
