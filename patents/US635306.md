## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Connecting rods between key levers and typebars used too much metal, were often several jointed pieces that could loosen, and needed a way to stay in the typebar eye.

**Solution:** A one-piece reduced-diameter rod with a recurved hook at the top and a clamped sheet-steel spring strip that closes the hook.

**How it works:** The rod has a threaded lower end thicker than the rest, which is reduced above the thread to save weight. Near the top it is bent back and forward into a hook, curved downward at the tip, whose center is directly above the straight part of the rod for a direct pull. A thin sheet-steel strip with up-turned edges is sprung over the rod and soldered or indented, its flat tapering end bearing on the hook tip. To fit, the spring is pressed back and the hook inserted in the typebar eye; the spring then retains it.
