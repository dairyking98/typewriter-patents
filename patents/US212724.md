## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenographic phonetic printing apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to 'construct an apparatus that may be used to print steno graphic or phonetic signs, thereby facilitating records of human language, and to arrange the said apparatus so that syllables maybe instantaneously recorded, as well as single stenographic or phonetic sounds.

**How it works (reading):** A two-hand keyboard, piano-style, where each key drives a push-rod and lever to punch a phonetic mark; keys pressed together record a syllable at once and the key movement feeds the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Syllable and phonetic-sign recording by hand was slow, and single signs could not be recorded together.

**Solution:** A two-hand keyboard, piano-style, where each key drives a push-rod and lever to punch a phonetic mark; keys pressed together record a syllable at once and the key movement feeds the paper.

**Features:** [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
