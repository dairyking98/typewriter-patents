## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Erasing or correcting tables

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide a simple key operated device by which erasures may be conveniently made without moving the paper on the platen and without using a hand operated eraser.

**How it works (reading):** A key-operated eraser on a bracket on the type bar; a serrated eccentric steel eraser wipes the paper when the key is depressed.
