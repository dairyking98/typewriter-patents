## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to devices for producing line spacing for paper trolleys of adding or typewriters and is intended to provide means, which allow the paper roller to quickly move a certain adjustable distance to rotate, whereby a setting can be achieved by which a much larger Feed is made possible than that, which is with the usual automatic devices to produce the 7eil distance can be achieved.

**How it works (reading):** A lever-driven paper roller drive with a settable stop gives a large adjustable line advance and a quick return feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Adding-machine paper carriages need a quick, large, adjustable line feed for printed forms, beyond the usual automatic spacing.

**Solution:** A lever-driven paper roller drive with a settable stop gives a large adjustable line advance and a quick return feed.
