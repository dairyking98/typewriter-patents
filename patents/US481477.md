## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Simplify and cheapen the construction of the inventor's earlier shifting type-bar machines (US457258, US470990).

**Solution:** A simplified shifting-type-bar machine with a spring-drum carriage drive, a universal feed lever coupled to the spacing lever through a pin in a slot, and an extensible carriage track for long carriages.

**How it works:** A base with a large central opening holds the spacing lever, two parallel bars with a finger bar, and the upper-case shifting lever. A universal feed-lever frame, pushed down by every key-lever, is tied to the spacing lever by a pin sliding in a vertical slot. Pressing a key lowers the feed frame without moving the spacing lever, while pressing the spacing lever drags the feed frame down too. The feed frame moves feed dogs that release the carriage a space at a time under a spring drum. Yokes under the carriage run on the carriage track, which can be lengthened to take a long interchangeable carriage, and a bell is tripped near the end.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
