## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to mechanism for ad- IO justing the leverage of the keys upon the types of writing-machines whereby at the will of the operator the keys may be given less or more leverage with a shallower or deeper keystroke, as fully set forth in my application, Serial No.

**How it works (reading):** Leverage of the key on the type decreases automatically during the stroke, and the dip and leverage are set by a separate adjusting mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key leverage on the type is fixed, so the key stroke cannot be shortened or lengthened to suit the operator.

**Solution:** Leverage of the key on the type decreases automatically during the stroke, and the dip and leverage are set by a separate adjusting mechanism.
