## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a type-writer with a convenient device for holding the type firmly in position for cleaning and without the necessity for soiling the fingers in the operation.

**How it works (reading):** A notched spring plate fixed to the frame catches under the typehead to hold the type-bar still while it is brushed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cleaning type with a brush is awkward because the type-bar must be held by hand, soiling the fingers.

**Solution:** A notched spring plate fixed to the frame catches under the typehead to hold the type-bar still while it is brushed.
