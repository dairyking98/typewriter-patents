## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide a marginal clamp for holdmg workon a flat platen, such as is used in that class of type-writers known as book or commercial type-writers.

**How it works (reading):** A marginal clamp on the platen track, supported independently of the platen and raisable off it, fixed by a tubular bolt so the track can shift without moving the clamps.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work on flat-bed book typewriters cannot be held firmly, and the clamp is disturbed when the platen tilts or the track shifts.

**Solution:** A marginal clamp on the platen track, supported independently of the platen and raisable off it, fixed by a tubular bolt so the track can shift without moving the clamps.
