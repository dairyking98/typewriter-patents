## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Adjacent type levers are pivoted so one hangs with its head down and the next stands upright, letting them swing from opposite directions to a closer, shorter bearing arrangement that strikes the platen squarely.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers all hanging from one axis need too much space and strike the platen at an acute angle, which smears the imprint.

**Solution:** Adjacent type levers are pivoted so one hangs with its head down and the next stands upright, letting them swing from opposite directions to a closer, shorter bearing arrangement that strikes the platen squarely.
