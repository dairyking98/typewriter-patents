## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In electromagnetic type-cylinder machines the impression magnet was fired early using a delay winding, which times the strike imprecisely and wastes time at small rotation angles.

**Solution:** Lock the printing swing with a pawl and release it mechanically only just before the cylinder's rotation ends, so the strike always follows the end of rotation by the same interval at every angle.

**How it works:** Pressing a key energises one of two rotary electromagnets that turn a square shaft; a toothed sector on it turns the type cylinder gear until a stop arm hits the stop lever the key advanced. The impression electromagnet is energised at the same moment but the cylinder carrier is held by a pawl on the shaft sleeve. Angle levers on the stop arm hit the advanced stop just before the end of rotation and lift the pawl, so the strike starts at full force and begins even while rotation is finishing. Just before impact a lever disengages the key pawl, breaking the circuits so a spring withdraws the cylinder and returns it to the start position.
