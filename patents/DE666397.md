In order to prevent the stop movement from taking place too early, the impact solenoid is used provided with a delay winding in this known machine. This kind the regulation of the time of the attack is imprecise. In particular, it must be @appropriately the largest angle of rotation of the type cylinder can be set so that the stop is unnecessarily delayed at small angles of rotation. In that according to the new arrangement the stop device is initially locked and only released towards the end of the rotary movement the time of the stop can be set precisely, and the stop takes place at all angles of rotation of the type cylinder at the same time interval from the termination of the rotary movement.
