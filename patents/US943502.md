## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to'improvements in justifying typewriters, and the objectof it is to produce a high speed, power driven, automatic machine adapted by being known in the art as a mechanical controller,- to produce columns or pages of -ty ed matter the lines -of which are properly ustified at both ends.

**How it works (reading):** A power-driven, mechanically controlled justifying typewriter with a type carrier, type selecting slides, cam shaft and justification mechanism.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Producing justified columns of print at both margins automatically at high speed.

**Solution:** A power-driven, mechanically controlled justifying typewriter with a type carrier, type selecting slides, cam shaft and justification mechanism.

**How it works:** A controller (as in a patent of Amos Johnson) feeds the machine under power. A cam shaft and driving clutch run the cycle. Wheel pins and slides, a type selecting mechanism and a type impressing mechanism choose and strike a character from a type carrier, and a justification mechanism with link-and-lever devices spaces the lines. Only the opening is available.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [USE:telegraph-printer](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** type carrier; controller; cam shaft; driving clutch; slide; wheel pin; justification mechanism; type impressing mechanism
