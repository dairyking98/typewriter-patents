## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriters print fixed type; the inventor wants to write manuscripts mechanically in handwriting form.

**Solution:** Each character is split into two coordinate curves cut as cams; a key couples a cam pair to a rod that guides a pen over the paper.

**How it works:** A character's handwritten outline is resolved into its x and y movement curves, with allowance for paper advance, and made into cams on rotating shafts. A keyboard couples the chosen cam to the coordinate rod carrying the pen, which draws the letter on the passing sheet. A shaft shift selects capital or small letters, and the pen lifts between characters. Further embodiments write several copies at once.
