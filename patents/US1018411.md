## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to new and useful improvements in attachments for typewrit ers and more particularly to an attachment for a well known form of typewriter wherein the spacing mechanism is operated simultaneously with the return of the carriage to its initial position, and my object is to provide a device of this character which will lessen the exert-ion necessary for the completion of this operation.
