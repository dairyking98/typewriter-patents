## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal objects of my invention are to overcome these objections and to produce a card holder which will lie closer to the platen than prior devices of this sort and which, moreover, will enable a line to be written near the top edge of the card.

**How it works (reading):** A card clamp attached to the platen lies closer to the surface, with teeth gripping the card edge, and it can be retracted so a line can be written near the top edge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card holders stand too far out from the platen, get struck by types and disturb the feed rollers, and force the first line far from the card edge.

**Solution:** A card clamp attached to the platen lies closer to the surface, with teeth gripping the card edge, and it can be retracted so a line can be written near the top edge.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
