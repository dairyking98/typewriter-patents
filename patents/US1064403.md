## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Keyset tabulators need a stop magazine that can be moved lengthwise without moving the stop-receiving bar or the carriage, and stops must be positively limited and retracted reliably.

**Solution:** A tabulator stop magazine movable lengthwise relative to the stop-receiving element, with stop ejecting and retracting devices and positive limits on relative movement, so the carriage can move while the magazine is held.

**How it works:** A keyset tabulator stop magazine can slide lengthwise against the stop-receiving element or bar, so stops are ejected into the receiving slot without moving the carriage. Means fix or hold the magazine while the carriage travels. Mechanical limits set its relative position, so the stop being retracted is always positioned. Shown on a No. 16 front-strike Premier machine.
