## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a roller guide for the carriage of typewriters, which under Interposition of ball bearings on rollers with screwed-in journals is running.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage runs on rollers with ball bearings on machined pins, with the pins set at right angles to a precisely machined frame surface; the front guide shaft has two diameters and runs on a guide rail.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typewriter carriage needed a guide that is both low in friction and accurate.

**Solution:** The carriage runs on rollers with ball bearings on machined pins, with the pins set at right angles to a precisely machined frame surface; the front guide shaft has two diameters and runs on a guide rail.
