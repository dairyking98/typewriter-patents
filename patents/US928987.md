## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to provide simple, inexpensive and easily operated means which can readily be applied to existing machines for either bringin either ribbon into use and silencing the otier one or silencing both simultaneously, at will.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A machine with two ribbons of different colors needs a simple, cheap way, fittable to existing machines, to bring either ribbon into use, or neither.

**Solution:** Two ribbon carriers share the one ribbon vibrating lever through a loose yoke so one rises while the other drops, or both can be held below the print point.

**How it works:** The usual ribbon runs through an upright carrier loosely connected to a pivoted ribbon vibrating lever, operated by the universal bar frame at each type stroke. A second carrier for an endless red supplemental ribbon stands beside it, and the two are joined at their lower ends by a yoke pivoted on offsets and carrying a ball that sits in an eye on the lever as a universal joint. Depressing a finger piece on the front carrier tilts the yoke, raising the other carrier higher so that ribbon covers the print point and the first is silenced, and both can be set too low to cover the print point. The carriers ride on the shift rail so they rise with the case shift.
