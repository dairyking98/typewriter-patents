## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriters and, more particularly, to a typebar and its actuating mecha'nism.

**How it works (reading):** The typebar head carries two, three or more characters and is shifted to bring the chosen character to the printing point, then locked against movement in each position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typebar carrying one character needs a way to print several characters without changing bars.

**Solution:** The typebar head carries two, three or more characters and is shifted to bring the chosen character to the printing point, then locked against movement in each position.
