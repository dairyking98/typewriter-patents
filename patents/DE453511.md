## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink supply arrangements therefor

**How it works (reading):** A single small wing, only as high as one character, swings out on the snout of a liquid storage container against a spring; soaked with ink, it is struck by the returning type and inks only the character that hits it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Impact typewriters with horizontal type bars have no room in front of the type guide head for a paint container, so the economical liquid-ink reservoir cannot be used on them.

**Solution:** A single small wing, only as high as one character, swings out on the snout of a liquid storage container against a spring; soaked with ink, it is struck by the returning type and inks only the character that hits it.
