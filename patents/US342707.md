## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in copy-holders for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A copy holder clamped to the carriage flanges moves with the carriage, with a ratchet and feed rollers to move the copy up or down one or more lines from the front.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The copy is hard to read from the side, and the operator must turn the head to copy from a sheet or book.

**Solution:** A copy holder clamped to the carriage flanges moves with the carriage, with a ratchet and feed rollers to move the copy up or down one or more lines from the front.
