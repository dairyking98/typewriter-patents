## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal should not impose a strong pull on a narrow, weak ribbon.

**Solution:** A follower on the full spool swings a carrier that engages an idle pinion with the power-shaft gear, whose reaction shifts the shaft sideways to change the drive from one spool gear to the other.

**How it works:** Two vertical spool shafts have winding pinions at the bottom, and a horizontal drive shaft carries two gears, only one meshed at a time. A spring-loaded follower rides on the ribbon coil of a spool. As coils build up it is crowded away and rotates a shaft and carrier arm. This moves a cam with an idle pinion into mesh with the drive gear, and the cam reacts against a fixed abutment, pushing the drive shaft endwise so the other drive gear meshes with the opposite spool gear. A spring detent on a beveled collar holds the shaft in position.
