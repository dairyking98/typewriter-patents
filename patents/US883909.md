## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to cylindrical platens for type writing machines, andhas for its object to provide a simple and efficient parts forming an improved platen by means of which the noise produced by the impact of the type against such platen is eliminated to a very material extent, all as will herein after more fully appear.
