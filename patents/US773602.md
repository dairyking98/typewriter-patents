## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbons and their operating mechanism are complicated and give poorer definition.

**Solution:** A single spring-loaded inking pad on a vertical spindle at the common striking point is lifted into the type path and inks each type as it passes.

**How it works:** An arm inside the typebar basket carries a socket aligned vertically with the common striking point, holding a spindle topped with an inking pad. A rocking bar under the key levers moves a spring pawl under the spindle, lifting it into the path of the types; a spring returns it down fast. The pad is a truncated cone, preferably a perforated hollow rubber cone packed with absorbent material and covered with ink-absorbing cloth, which collapses partly under type blows and spreads ink. A tilting top spindle section or a slit hinged-band pad yields to the direction of the blow.

**Features:** [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** spindle; socket; arm; inking pad; spring pawl; rocking bar; spring; cap

**Improves on:**

- inking ribbon: dispensed with; pad at the strike point instead
