## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** Two pressed half-disc plates with type lever slots are held apart by spacer and intermediate plates, and an intermediate plate with a circumferential groove holds the axle, which is clamped by nuts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cast type lever segments are costly and sheet-metal ones lack rigidity and cannot hold the type lever axle firmly.

**Solution:** Two pressed half-disc plates with type lever slots are held apart by spacer and intermediate plates, and an intermediate plate with a circumferential groove holds the axle, which is clamped by nuts.
