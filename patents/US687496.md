## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen could not be freely turned by fractional amounts of a line-space, and existing devices for this were objectionable.

**Solution:** Engaging pawls connect the platen to its line-spacing mechanism and can disconnect it, so the platen can be rotated freely in either direction for fractional or other feed distances.

**How it works:** Pawls engage a co-operating surface on the platen and are moved to a disconnected position to allow free rotation.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen; engaging pawls; line-spacing mechanism
