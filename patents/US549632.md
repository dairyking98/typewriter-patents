## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type wheel should be simple and cheap and let worn printing characters be renewed easily.

**Solution:** A two-plate horizontal type wheel with radial slots holding loose, individually removable type plates, aligned at impression by a finger entering beveled teeth from beneath.

**How it works:** The wheel is a lower plate with beveled teeth, one gap per character, and an upper plate with matching radial vertical slots, joined by a metal band slipped into grooves at their outer edges. Each type is a thin plate with shoulders that rests in the slots and drops in or out freely, carrying the printing character on its upper end. The wheel is turned to roughly the right position, then a hammer swings and a rock-shaft finger rises between the lower plate's teeth, centring the character exactly. The hammer strikes a movable anvil loosely held in a frame slot, which jumps up and pushes the type plate upward against the paper and platen.
