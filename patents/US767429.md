## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my present invention is to overcome these difficulties and to provide means whereby a carriage may be arrested at any of a number of predetermined columnar positions without being first checked or arrested by other column-stops.

**How it works (reading):** Column stops can be arrested at any predetermined column without being checked by the earlier stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating stops arrest the carriage at every column in turn, so it cannot skip to a column directly.

**Solution:** Column stops can be arrested at any predetermined column without being checked by the earlier stops.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
