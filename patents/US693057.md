## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On the Oliver machine the leading edge of the sheet, after passing the printing line, could be caught by the incoming part of the paper moving up from below and jam.

**Solution:** Two adjustable guide plates behind the platen deflect the advancing paper edge rearward, away from the platen and clear of the incoming sheet.

**How it works:** The paper enters over a lower guide plate, is pressed around the platen by presser rollers, and passes beneath oblique guides with guide fingers that engage its side margins. Two metal guide plates, one at each end of the carriage, have upturned inner edges close to the rear of the platen. They are attached to the sockets of the oblique guides, which slide on a square longitudinal bar, so they move with the guide fingers and need no separate adjustment for sheet width. When the leading edge passes the oblique guides it meets the plates and is deflected rearward, horizontally over the incoming paper resting on the lower guide plate.
