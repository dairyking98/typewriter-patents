## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing typewriters are complex and hard for an unpracticed person to use; a simple machine of few parts for private use is wanted.

**Solution:** A stationary character dial is read by a hand lever that rotates a hub with radial flexible type fingers; the lever's handle pivots down to press the type finger lying over the chosen character onto the paper.

**How it works:** The operator grips the printing lever above the dial and swings it round to the desired letter; this turns a tube and the slit annular plate with flexible type fingers beneath so that the matching type lies over the rubber platen roll. Pressing the lever lifts a spring-loaded inner shaft and flanged sleeve, tilting a curved lever that pushes the one type finger down onto the paper. The same stroke tilts a pair of elbow levers that retract and then advance a spring pawl on the carriage rack, spacing one notch per letter. Ink rollers pivoted inside the drum ink the types. Case is changed by a thumb lever that shifts the dial one finger-pitch against a spring, since upper and lower case types are on alternate fingers.
