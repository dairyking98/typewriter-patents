## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter must be operated only for a paid time, and a coin-operated lock must prevent use afterward.

**Solution:** A coin-operated timer that unlocks the machine for a set period, such as half an hour, then relocks it until another coin is deposited.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
