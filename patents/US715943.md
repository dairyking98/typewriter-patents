## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen scale of an under-strike machine needs to be visible at the line of print when the platen is tilted up and out of the way when writing.

**Solution:** Mount the platen scale so it moves automatically to the line of print as the platen frame is raised and swung back, with a lock holding the frame turned up.

**How it works:** The scale bar sits under the platen on slotted arms guided by screws on a rod along the platen frame. Raising and swinging the platen frame about the rocker shaft moves the scale to the printing line and back. A locking device engages at the limit of upward and rearward swing to hold the platen carriage. Shown on a Remington No. 6.

**Parts:** main carriage; guide rail; platen frame; rocker arm; platen; scale bar; slotted arm; locking device
