## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** A spring-drum tensioned rotating transfer member engages the paper web over the platen, so the arch support returns to its position without spring force on the copy sheets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon paper arches move with the paper and are hard to set against the platen.

**Solution:** A spring-drum tensioned rotating transfer member engages the paper web over the platen, so the arch support returns to its position without spring force on the copy sheets.
