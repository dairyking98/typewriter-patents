## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements of selective printing mechanisms, e.g. ink-jet printers or thermal printers, specially adapted for supporting or handling copy material in continuous form, e.g. webs

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is distinguished from this known device characterized in that the envelopes without the use of special connecting links are directly connected to each other.

**How it works (reading):** Envelopes are joined directly to each other in a chain, with gumming set back to a perforation, so they can be typed one after another and separated afterward.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Addressing envelopes one at a time wastes time because each must be clamped in the machine.

**Solution:** Envelopes are joined directly to each other in a chain, with gumming set back to a perforation, so they can be typed one after another and separated afterward.
