## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A fold-up paper roll frame must be held firmly in both its basic and raised positions.

**Solution:** A catch bar on the frame hooks behind the carriage track in the raised position, and a lever-operated rail and rod unlock the carriage and the paper guide.
