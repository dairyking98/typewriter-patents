## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card tabulator zero suppression) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for producing a permanent visual presentation of the output data, e.g. computer output printers using printers by rack-type printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device that is used in printing punch card tabulating machines and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A row of thumb-shaped locking members, each with a locking pawl, sits between the hammers and the type support rods, and blocks the hammer from the null type while letting digits print.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Leading zeros printed in front of numbers on punch-card tabulators make lists unclear.

**Solution:** A row of thumb-shaped locking members, each with a locking pawl, sits between the hammers and the type support rods, and blocks the hammer from the null type while letting digits print.

**Features:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md)

**Parts:** locking members p; locking pawls q; hammers h; type support rods a
