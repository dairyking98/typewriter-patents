## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machine needing a convenient way to release the platen carriage and advance the paper by a variable interline amount.

**Solution:** A carriage-release slide lifts the feed rack out of the dogs, and a combined lever both releases the carriage and rotates the platen by an adjustable amount set by a shield.

**How it works:** The platen frame rides on rollers along a track and is stepped by two feed dogs oscillated by a rockshaft engaging a rack. A spring-urged slide with inclined slots lifts the rack clear of the dogs so the carriage can be set anywhere. A lever can do this alone, or together with a pawl that turns the platen ratchet for a line space; a pointer-positioned shield limits the pawl stroke so the interline space is larger or smaller. The opening also shows a ribbon-feeding device and upper and lower typebars.
