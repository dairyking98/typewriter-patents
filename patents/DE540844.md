## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Lateral ribbon guides near the spools are awkward when changing ribbons and with spools of different size.

**Solution:** The paper-holding plate is extended to the ribbon spools with slots at its ends that guide the ribbon near the spools, while the middle is shaped as a postcard holder.
