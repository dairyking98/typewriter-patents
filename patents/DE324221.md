## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers; Buffers with metal springs with helical springs arranged to damp each other by mutual friction

**How it works (reading):** Removable side plates fit the frame to enclose the whole key-lever and typebar mechanism, with the type exposed only at the print point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dust, paper and eraser waste collected in the open lever mechanism and jammed it.

**Solution:** Removable side plates fit the frame to enclose the whole key-lever and typebar mechanism, with the type exposed only at the print point.

**Features:** [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)
