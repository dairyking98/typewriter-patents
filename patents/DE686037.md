## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** Carbon-sheet holder whose front edge is bevelled obliquely to the platen axis so the edge engages the roller gradually across its width.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon-sheet holders with a straight front edge buckle and distort paper webs when fed through a typewriter.

**Solution:** Carbon-sheet holder whose front edge is bevelled obliquely to the platen axis so the edge engages the roller gradually across its width.
