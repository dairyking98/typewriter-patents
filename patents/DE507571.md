## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter connected to card punch)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an electrical connection of a card punching machine with a Recording machine (typewriter, typewriter or the like), their keys Operate electrical contacts from an attachment designed as an accessory of the registration machine.

**How it works (reading):** A bar with levers, one per key, is fixed in front of the keyboard frame on side arms, so the keys keep their habitual height while driving the punch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Mounting the card punch contact base under the keyboard raises the typewriter keys, which users dislike.

**Solution:** A bar with levers, one per key, is fixed in front of the keyboard frame on side arms, so the keys keep their habitual height while driving the punch.
