## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Show cards, bulletins and signs need a simple, quickly operated printing machine.

**Solution:** A rotating type-wheel whose rim carries sliding wedge-shaped type-bars; striking a bar end pushes its type out to print, then it resets.

**How it works:** The wheel turns on a shaft in a frame arm above a cylindrical platen. The operator strikes the keys or bar ends for the letters wanted, and the wheel carries each struck bar round to the printing point; a cam resets it afterwards. A wedge-shaped tongue on each bar bears on grooves of the rim face. The bar presses the tympan onto the platen, which moves the paper carriage. A shifting frame moves the carriage back so spacing varies with each letter. An ink device and a bar-shifting cam sit on a bracket.
