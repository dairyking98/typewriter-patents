## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to type-bars and hangers for writing-machines, and has for its main objects the provisionof means for guiding and steadying the type-bar at the printing moment and the construction of the type-bar and the hanger and their mounting.

**How it works (reading):** The type-bar is pivoted in a hanger fixed to a segment, with improved construction of the type-bar, the hanger and their mounting so the bar is guided and steadied at the printing moment.
