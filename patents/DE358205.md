## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper carriage ratchet clutch cannot be disengaged for free carriage movement in both directions.

**Solution:** A drum with conical surfaces disengages pawls from the ratchet wheel when the carriage is pushed back, and a key operates the drum.
