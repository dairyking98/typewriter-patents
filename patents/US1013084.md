## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A platen must move to either side of a central position so one typebar can carry three types, while staying firmly located and silent, and not being pushed down during erasing.

**Solution:** Two shift levers act on a floating lever whose other end drives a cam-track roller, so each key pushes the platen one way from a dwell-held central position.

**How it works:** The platen frame rides on a rail carried by arms on a rock shaft, so the rail can be raised or lowered. A lever with a roll on its end runs in a fixed cam track, and its endwise motion rocks the shaft. The track has dwells at the middle and at both ends, on arcs of different radius, which hold the platen centred or fully shifted. Two shift levers join one floating lever at different points, and a link carries its motion to the roll lever. A ball hung on a swinging rod between the shift levers locks the undepressed lever, which then acts as a fixed pivot for the floating lever. Hooks on the shift keys can latch a depressed lever as a shift lock, and springs return everything to the centre.
