## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rotary printing machines need a simple way to present fresh ribbon to the type surface.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools inside a recess in the type drum are wound automatically by a pawl on a stationary eccentric strap acting while the gap in the drum passes the impression platen.

**How it works:** A drum carries longitudinal rails forming channels for individual type, with a quarter-circumference recess holding a pair of ribbon spools; the ribbon is wrapped around the drum between them. A stationary eccentric on the frame carries a strap with a pawl. As the drum turns, the pawl engages a ratchet wheel on a spool when the open recess passes the platen and draws the ribbon on, with the mechanism shifting itself to wind the other spool.
