## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front lays, stops, or gauges

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more especially to the indicating and paper feeding and conl trolling mechanisms of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** The form holder turns with the platen on line spacing and is uncoupled and reset when the paper-feed mechanism is released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An indicating device or form holder should release with the platen and restore to initial position; the paper feed roller and stops must be lockable for loading.

**Solution:** The form holder turns with the platen on line spacing and is uncoupled and reset when the paper-feed mechanism is released.
