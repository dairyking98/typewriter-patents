## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon lift was driven from the universal rail shortly before impression, which overloaded the gearing and risked disruption.

**Solution:** The ribbon lifter is driven from a separate universal link, and an adjusting device lets the lever pivot to set different ribbon lift heights.
