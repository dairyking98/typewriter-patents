## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper side gage on the Underwood platen clamps the shelf so tightly that it is hard to move along the platen and scratches or mars the paper shelf.

**Solution:** A rod-mounted carrier holds the side gage and a hinged front paper-guiding finger; a spring-loaded feather in a longitudinal groove locks the carrier, and a finger-piece relieves the spring so the carrier can be adjusted freely.
