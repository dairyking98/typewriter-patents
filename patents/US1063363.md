## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Producing characters (e.g. Braille-like or dot patterns) from many wires.

**Solution:** A bank of 100 character-forming wires in 10 rows of 10, driven by levers and toothed bars whose teeth can be redistributed to pick different groups.

**How it works:** Each wire is attached to the top of an intermediately pivoted lever. Sets of keyed main bars with shiftable supplemental bars present teeth to the lower ends of the levers; shifting a supplemental bar changes which group of wires a key operates, so each key forms a different character.

**Features:** [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** wire; operating lever; main bar; supplemental bar; key lever; goose neck; link

**Numbers:** 100 wires in 10 rows of 10
