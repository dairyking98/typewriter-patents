## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboard layouts make rapid writing hard, with keys not easy to locate by touch.

**Solution:** Keys arranged in rows and columns, decreasing in height from rear to front, with projections on the keys as finger guides, and each column of keys assigned to one finger.

**How it works:** Keys in each column share one finger or thumb; projections on one or two sides of selected keys guide the finger, and the front row is worked by the phalanges.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** keys; finger guide projections

**Numbers:** 4 rows; 40 keys
