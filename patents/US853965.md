## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices actuated when platen reaches the end of a line

**How it works (reading):** A bifurcated actuating lever on the connecting lever engages over a rocking locking bar that the key levers are fastened to, lifting it into an elevated position to lock the key levers when the carriage nears the end of its travel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier line-lock arrangements connected the carriage to the key levers indirectly, requiring a special linking lock-bar and not acting with a very slight touch.

**Solution:** A bifurcated actuating lever on the connecting lever engages over a rocking locking bar that the key levers are fastened to, lifting it into an elevated position to lock the key levers when the carriage nears the end of its travel.
