## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanism of a front-strike machine needs better vibrator action, spool drive, automatic reverse, field selection and a way to disable the vibrator for stencil work.

**Solution:** A package of improvements to the ribbon vibrator and its operation, spool drive, ribbon-controlled automatic reversal, ribbon field selection and vibrator cut-out.

**How it works:** The machine is a front-strike with an inclined ball-bearing carriage rail, a feed rack engaged by gears and a spring-drum escapement. The ribbon vibrator is operated from the universal bar through a shaft. The spools are driven mechanically and the ribbon itself operates devices that reverse the feed when it runs out. Different longitudinal ribbon fields can be brought to the print point, and the vibrator can be thrown out of action for mimeograph stencil cutting.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon vibrator; universal bar; ribbon spool; reversing device; carriage rail; feed rack; spring drum; escapement; platen
