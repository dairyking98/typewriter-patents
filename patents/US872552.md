## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A carriage feed mechanism that letter-spaces on the key return stroke may need to be converted to space on the downstroke instead, or to give an ordinary or reverse feed at will, and needs a back stop for the escapement wheel.

**Solution:** A shiftable dog carrier and rocker convert the escapement between feeding the carriage on key depression or on key return, with a back stop on the escapement dogs.

**How it works:** On a Monarch typebar machine, the carriage rack meshes with a feed pinion on a short shaft behind which a circular escapement wheel is loosely mounted. Spring pawls in a box housing on the shaft ride a ratchet wheel so the escapement wheel turns with the shaft when feeding but not when the carriage is returned. A dog carrier or rocker with escapement dogs engages the wheel teeth, and a shift member changes the relation so the letter space occurs on depression or on return of the key. A combined dog and escapement wheel stop acts as a back stop, as in Gabrielson US717837.
