## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** With manifolding, thick stacks of sheets lower the outer sheet on an underside-striking platen, so types strike at an angle instead of full face.

**Solution:** An eccentric on the carriage front wheel, with a scale indicator, raises the platen in proportion to the number of sheets.

**How it works:** The paper carriage front runs on a wheel along the machine's top rail. The wheel turns on an eccentric slug on a shaft; an indicator on the shaft end moves over a curved scale on a plate fixed above the wheel, with a rack and spring thumb-piece to hold the setting. Turning the eccentric changes the wheel's effective radius under the carriage, raising the platen so the striking point stays fixed for 1 to 5 or more sheets. The pointer also doubles as a line indicator.
