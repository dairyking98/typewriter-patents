## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators need to find tab or column positions on a typewriter scale.

**Solution:** A removable paper index strip held in a frame beside the carriage scale.

**How it works:** A frame screwed beside the main scale holds a ruled paper strip under a spring clip. The strip is marked for columns or blanks and read against a pointer fixed to the machine.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [SEL:chart-interchangeable](../features/sel-chart-interchangeable.md)

**Parts:** scale; frame; index strip; spring clip; pointer
