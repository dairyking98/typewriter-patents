## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**How it works (reading):** Two separate full-width ribbons, each with its own mechanism, one released and actuated while the other is disengaged and locked.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single ribbon with side-by-side colour or copying strips has strips too narrow to adjust reliably, wears unevenly, and allows no lateral traverse.

**Solution:** Two separate full-width ribbons, each with its own mechanism, one released and actuated while the other is disengaged and locked.

**How it works:** Two standard-width ribbons are carried with separate operating mechanisms. A control lets the operator choose which mechanism is released to receive the impression, and the other is locked against operation. Only the opening is available, so the linkage details are unknown.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon mechanism; control; spool

**Improves on:**

- single ribbon with lengthwise colour portions: two full-width ribbons selected one at a time
