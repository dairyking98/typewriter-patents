## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the present invention is a device for the permanent locking of the character key levers on typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** When the carriage stop reaches the line-length stop, a rod turns and pulls a rod that swings a locking bar under the noses of the character key levers; an adjustable nut and counter-nut on a lever lets the operator swing the bar back out.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators need a way to lock the character key levers so unauthorized persons cannot type, while still allowing characters to be added at the end of a line.

**Solution:** When the carriage stop reaches the line-length stop, a rod turns and pulls a rod that swings a locking bar under the noses of the character key levers; an adjustable nut and counter-nut on a lever lets the operator swing the bar back out.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md)

**Parts:** locking bar; pull rod; rotatable rod; spring; adjustable stop; signal bell; key levers; nut and counter-nut
