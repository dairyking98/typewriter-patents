## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Brown ribbon feed and reversing mechanism on the Smith Premier needed improvement.

**Solution:** A rocking dog on the spool shaft engages ratchet teeth on the flanges of one spool or the other, and cams with a detent spring trip it to reverse.

**How it works:** A transverse shaft is turned through spiral gears and pawls on the escapement gear only when the carriage moves left, with a hand crank to turn it independently. Ribbon spools sit loosely on the shaft collar and a spring puppet; ratchet teeth on adjacent spool flanges are engaged by a first-order dog pivoted in a slot of the shaft. Rocking the dog switches which spool is driven, which reverses feed. A pin and grooved roller on the dog end are tripped by cams and a detent spring.
