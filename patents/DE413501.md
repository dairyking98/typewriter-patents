## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**How it works (reading):** Adjusting disks are geared to the slides and interchangeable dividing rings matched to the staff line spacing give each disk a repeating division, so a full turn moves one unit and staff positions are read directly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Note typewriters need precise setting of the cross slide by screw spindles, so small errors ruin staff transcription.

**Solution:** Adjusting disks are geared to the slides and interchangeable dividing rings matched to the staff line spacing give each disk a repeating division, so a full turn moves one unit and staff positions are read directly.
