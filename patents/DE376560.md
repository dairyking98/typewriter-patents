## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a typewriter, which the well-known advantages of a typewriter, regarding perfect usability, Performance, durability, visible writing and the like, wherein it is built in such a compact and compact manner and is so light in weight possesses that it can be carried extremely comfortably when traveling.

**How it works (reading):** The keyboard, key levers and type levers sit in a flat box housing, with the paper carriage running in a slotted tube that slides over the box, so both fold together for transport. Type levers are mounted on a comb-like arc segment and each carries upper and lower case types.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter should be compact and light enough to carry when travelling, while keeping the usual performance and visible writing.

**Solution:** The keyboard, key levers and type levers sit in a flat box housing, with the paper carriage running in a slotted tube that slides over the box, so both fold together for transport. Type levers are mounted on a comb-like arc segment and each carries upper and lower case types.
