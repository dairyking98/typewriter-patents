## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Throwing off the paper feed devices against their spring tension strains the parts, which forces them to be made heavy and costly, especially with a traveling carriage.

**Solution:** The sustaining means of the feed rolls is positioned so that, when released, the rolls fall away from the platen by gravity, and the sustaining springs carry no tension during release.

**How it works:** Feed rolls are carried on a frame whose support point can move, so releasing them lets gravity drop them away from the platen without flexing the springs.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** front and rear feed rolls; feed roll frame; support springs; platen; paper guide
