## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Coatings without pigments; Structurally defined web or sheet [e.g., overall dimension, etc.] including variation in thickness with component conforming to contour of nonplanar surface and conforming component on an opposite nonplanar surface; Coating layer not in excess of 5 mils thick or equivalent

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper for receiving typewriter writing.

**How it works (reading):** The paper has a thin slow-permeable coating that holds the fresh ink in an easily erasable state, with the ink passing into the paper over time, so a soft rubber eraser removes it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fresh typewriter impressions are hard to erase cleanly without ink-eraser scrapings, which can damage the paper, and older impressions resist erasing.

**Solution:** The paper has a thin slow-permeable coating that holds the fresh ink in an easily erasable state, with the ink passing into the paper over time, so a soft rubber eraser removes it.
