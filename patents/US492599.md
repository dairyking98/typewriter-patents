## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Remington No. 2 the platen is shifted back with one key and forward with another, and holding it locked in the upper case is slow and confusing.

**Solution:** One key lever shifts the platen back or forth depending on which position it is locked in, always returning by the same spring.

**How it works:** Two shift-rod levers on a rock shaft carry the shift rod that yokes the platen carrier. A connecting rod from the key lever hooks onto a stud on a pivoted lever, and a spring connects to another lever. A hand lever swings the spring across the pivot of the shift rod so it holds the platen forward or back. With the platen locked in either position, pressing the same key moves it to the other and the spring brings it back on release.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md)

**Parts:** platen carrier; shift rod; rock shaft; key lever; connecting rod; hand lever; spring; yoke

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 2 shift: one key shifts either way from the locked position
