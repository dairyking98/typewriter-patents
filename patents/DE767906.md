**How it works (reading):** The funnel has two side parts and an interchangeable middle piece, with slots and pins for lateral adjustment; resilient clamps mount it on the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Insertion funnels for cards are narrow at the edges, so wide cards or thin sheets buckle.

**Solution:** The funnel has two side parts and an interchangeable middle piece, with slots and pins for lateral adjustment; resilient clamps mount it on the carriage.

**How it works:** Adjustable two-part funnel with interchangeable centre piece and spring clamps on the carriage.
