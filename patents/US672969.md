## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards and envelopes narrower than the platen cannot be written to their extreme edges.

**Solution:** A paper guide permitting writing right to the edge of small cards.

**How it works:** A guide attached to the platen carriage holds small cards or envelopes so printing reaches the edges.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** paper guide; platen carriage
