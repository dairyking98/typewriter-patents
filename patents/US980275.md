## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper take-up reel can wind more paper than the feed advances, since the roll diameter grows during writing.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded friction disk on a stub shaft at the reel end limits the rewinding so that the amount wound never exceeds the amount fed forward at each key stroke.
