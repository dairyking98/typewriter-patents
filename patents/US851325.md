## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Erasing on the top sheet presses the eraser onto the carbon, which smudges the copy below.

**Solution:** A removable shield placed between the top sheet and the carbon sheet takes the eraser pressure so the carbon copy is not smudged.

**How it works:** A body with a rearward triangular tab, perforated to hang on a nail, is inserted between the original sheet and the carbon and removed when done.
