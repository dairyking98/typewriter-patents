## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The fixed reserve type frame (rarely used characters) of the Shimada-type machine limits how far the moving frames can travel and makes the machine bulky.

**Solution:** Make the sides of the reserve type frame into rails with rack teeth, driven by a hand-turned gear so the whole frame draws out to the front and serves as the rails of the moving frame.

**How it works:** A base carries fixed side rails on which the reserve type frame, holding rarely used characters, slides. Rack teeth are cut on the reserve frame's own rails and a gear turned by an outside handle draws it forward or back, with balls reducing friction. A rail frame moves forward and back over the reserve frame, and the common-character type frame moves left and right on the rail frame, moving forward and back with it. Drawing the reserve frame out enlarges the reachable range so the machine body can be smaller. A character index board may be fitted over the reserve frame.
