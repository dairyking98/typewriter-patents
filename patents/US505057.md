## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon wears at a single line because it moves in a fixed course.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools slide along their shafts in yokes, so different parts of the ribbon are presented at the impact point.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
