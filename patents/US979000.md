## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to paper-feeding rolls of typewriting machines, and its object is to improve the means for putting the desired tension upon the rolls.

**How it works (reading):** A single spring structure at each end of the platen bears on both front and rear roll axles and is hung on eccentric-journalled shafts, so tension can be adjusted quickly at either end without dismantling the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjusting the tension of the paper feed rolls requires removing the platen frame to reach the adjusting screws.

**Solution:** A single spring structure at each end of the platen bears on both front and rear roll axles and is hung on eccentric-journalled shafts, so tension can be adjusted quickly at either end without dismantling the machine.
