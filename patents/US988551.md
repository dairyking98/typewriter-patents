## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A back-space key needs to move the carriage back exactly one space and then lock it against further movement.

**Solution:** A bell-crank key lever drives an angular pawl, which engages the escapement rack and moves the carriage back one space; a spring-loaded cam slot on the pawl returns it and holds the carriage at that space.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
