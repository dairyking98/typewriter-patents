## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actuators for print wires using piezoelectric elements

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to means whereby the hammer is normally free from the tension of its spring, the said spring being normally relaxed, and being placed under tension sufficient to impel the hammer forward only when a key has been operated.

**How it works (reading):** The hammer spring is normally relaxed and is put under tension only when a key is operated. The spring drum connects directly to the escapement instead of through the paper carriage, so the carriage can be light and removable without changing the spring adjustment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the spring-pressed hammer through the carriage spring needs a very heavy carriage and heavy spring tension, and the blows vary.

**Solution:** The hammer spring is normally relaxed and is put under tension only when a key is operated. The spring drum connects directly to the escapement instead of through the paper carriage, so the carriage can be light and removable without changing the spring adjustment.

**How it works:** A key operation tensions the hammer spring, releasing it to strike; the carriage is no longer the return path, and the spring drum drives the escapement directly.
