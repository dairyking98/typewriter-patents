## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructional details or processes of manufacture of the input device

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to attachments for typewrit-ing machines to facilitate the mastery of touch typewriting and comprises a screen removably attached to the machine in position between the keyboard and the eyes of the operator, in such position as not to'interfere with the manipulation of the keys.
