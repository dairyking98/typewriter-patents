## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventor wants a simple, moderately priced finger-key machine using weighted type-levers.

**Solution:** Counterweighted type-levers and finger-keys sit in radial slots of two semicircular guide plates, tapped to strike the print point, with each key stroke also spacing the carriage.

**How it works:** Semicircular plates with radial slots and an intersecting groove hold type-levers on a wire bearing; their enlarged lower ends keep the type ends vertical. Tapping a finger-key throws its lever to the print point and depresses a circular lever that works a spacing lever with a beveled tooth over two racks, while a spiral spring draws the carriage. A bevel-headed screw takes up wear in the slots. Hinged ribbon spools are ratchet-fed from the key lever, and a metal cover with a cushion encloses the type-levers.
