## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the matters hereinafter set forth, and more particularly pointed out in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** A dead key connects to the escapement and right-hand stop mechanism; a spring raises the universal bar and keys, and an auxiliary spring supports the lower paper guide.
