## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (automatic blank control cross counter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**How it works (reading):** A pawl and lever lock the clear-character key and amount keys until the cross counter is actually written empty; if it is not, the amount keys are briefly unlocked for correction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Clearing the jumping cross counter at line end is not enforced; amount keys can be used on an unbalanced total.

**Solution:** A pawl and lever lock the clear-character key and amount keys until the cross counter is actually written empty; if it is not, the amount keys are briefly unlocked for correction.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
