## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**How it works (reading):** An interponent on the dog-carrier is withdrawn by the accent key so the universal bar moves without feeding the carriage, while the ribbon still vibrates over the printing point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Striking an accent key before a letter moves the carriage, so the accent cannot be placed over the letter.

**Solution:** An interponent on the dog-carrier is withdrawn by the accent key so the universal bar moves without feeding the carriage, while the ribbon still vibrates over the printing point.
