## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in'-type- Writers, linotype'machines and the like, andmore particularly to means for operating the carriages of such machines by a fluid under pressure or by suction.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage, normally moved by a coil spring, is returned by a fluid-pressure or suction cylinder pair controlled from the keyboard, with a valve that can stop the carriage at any point in its return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage causes shock and jar, and the return cannot be stopped at a chosen point.

**Solution:** The carriage, normally moved by a coil spring, is returned by a fluid-pressure or suction cylinder pair controlled from the keyboard, with a valve that can stop the carriage at any point in its return.
