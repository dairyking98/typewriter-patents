## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable folding typewriters need a frame that is both light and strong, which cast frames fail to provide cheaply.

**Solution:** The back and two sides are stamped from one sheet of metal bent into a U, with openings and cross braces at the front and corner plates for feet.
