## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a bearing for levers, in particular for the type, key and intermediate levers of typewriters and calculating machines, in which the levers are in slots a bearing body are performed.

**How it works (reading):** Sheet-metal disks, spaced by intermediate links and held by riveting or similar, form the slot walls; they are overmolded with injection-molded material so the hard disk faces guide the type levers and keep the slot widths precise.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-lever bearing bodies milled from solid material have slot inaccuracies, and injection-molded ones wear quickly at the guide faces.

**Solution:** Sheet-metal disks, spaced by intermediate links and held by riveting or similar, form the slot walls; they are overmolded with injection-molded material so the hard disk faces guide the type levers and keep the slot widths precise.
