## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines in which, in addition to the usual linespacing mechanism for the revoluble platen, there is provided an auxiliary mechanism, usually at one end of the platen,for feeding a paper ribbon around the platen indepencl tionary.

**How it works (reading):** The paper ribbon runs between two spools, the delivery spool behind the paper shelf and the receiving spool close to the shell, with the ribbon carried over a smaller-diameter auxiliary platen so the spool flanges clear the main platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tally strip used for recording bill totals gets in the way of wide sheets and of the operator handling them.

**Solution:** The paper ribbon runs between two spools, the delivery spool behind the paper shelf and the receiving spool close to the shell, with the ribbon carried over a smaller-diameter auxiliary platen so the spool flanges clear the main platen.

**How it works:** The ribbon passes over the auxiliary platen between the two spools, which are placed out of the operator's way.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** delivery spool; receiving spool; auxiliary platen; paper shelf; main platen; tally strip
