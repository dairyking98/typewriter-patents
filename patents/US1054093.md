## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the tabulator stops and consists of a rod 5 carried by the tabulator bar 6 and the stops? each having its eye 8 sufficiently larger in diameter than the rod to allow freedom of movement to enable the stops to be readily set in place, removed, and shifted along the rod to different positions with relation to the bar.
