## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash-register printing units could not be switched on or off according to whether dependent items are entered.

**Solution:** A set adjusting member decides whether dependent items print, switches the unit off when needed, and returns automatically to normal after independent items or totals.
