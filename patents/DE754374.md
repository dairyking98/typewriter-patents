## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to switch frame bearings for typewriters, where the switching bracket of handlebar pairs, which is arranged concentrically to the type lever axis to be led. In known designs of this type, the front pair of links cannot be adjusted mounted on the segment carrier, so that an adjustment to the oscillating shaft is impossible. This means that the switch frame must be adjustable in itself, but this has the consequence has that the switching frame becomes heavier and a large part of its adjustment ability loses in the machine. In order to avoid this disadvantage, the pivot points are according to the invention the handlebar is designed to be adjustable in such a way that the pivot points of a pair of handlebars are arranged on adjustable by pivoting about a pin bearing arms and the pivot points of the other pair of links are on a bearing block that is adjustable parallel to the shift rail or type lever axis level.
