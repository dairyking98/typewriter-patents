## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing plate manufacture) · **Subject (patent classes):** Type

**How it works (reading):** Type metal is deposited finely divided (vapour or galvanic) into the mould and then compacted by rolling or pressing, for a sharp and durable type wheel or drum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type wheels and drums wore quickly or lacked sharp impressions when made by existing methods.

**Solution:** Type metal is deposited finely divided (vapour or galvanic) into the mould and then compacted by rolling or pressing, for a sharp and durable type wheel or drum.
