## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in the features of construction, arrangements of parts and combinations of devices to be described in the following specification and set forth in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** Billing devices on the platen and carriage (rotary stop on the platen frame, finger wheel) position the paper mechanically for condensed record work.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing or condensed-record work on a typewriter forces the operator to do mental calculations and watch indicators to place entries in columns.

**Solution:** Billing devices on the platen and carriage (rotary stop on the platen frame, finger wheel) position the paper mechanically for condensed record work.

**How it works:** From the opening only: billing devices are fitted to a front-strike machine and act on the platen, with a finger wheel and a rotary stop carried by the platen frame, so the line position is set mechanically rather than by eye. Only the object and the figure list are in the excerpt; the working is not described.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** platen; finger wheel; rotary stop; platen frame; billing device
