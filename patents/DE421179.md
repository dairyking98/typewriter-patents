## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter and a calculating machine must be linked so that typing digits also presses the matching calculator keys, without structural change to either machine.

**Solution:** Each of the ten digit keys of the typewriter drives a lever system (levers 4, 5, 6 on a horizontal axis) that presses the matching key of the calculator; the system is carried on a base plate with supports.
