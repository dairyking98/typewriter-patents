## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates particularly to tabulating mechanism for typewriting machines and its general object is to provide improved devices of this class.

**How it works (reading):** A combined denominational tabulator and column selecting mechanism, in which column-selecting devices normally inoperative are brought into operative position by a key bar. Full details are not read.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Denominational tabulating needs improved mechanism, and column selection must reach a large number of columns (only the first 3000 characters were read).

**Solution:** A combined denominational tabulator and column selecting mechanism, in which column-selecting devices normally inoperative are brought into operative position by a key bar. Full details are not read.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** column selecting devices; key bar; denominational tabulator
