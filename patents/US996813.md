## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more especially to a ball bearing type bar and hanger construction for front strike or visible typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Each type bar sits on a separately removable hanger with a wide pivotal bearing; carriage runs on ball-bearing race.

**Problem:** Many type bars on front-strike visible machines need separately removable hangers and a wide pivotal bearing.

**Solution:** Each type bar sits on a separately removable hanger with a wide pivotal bearing; carriage runs on ball-bearing race.
