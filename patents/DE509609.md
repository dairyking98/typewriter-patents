## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carbon paper stuck to ledger sheets cannot be used fully, and gluing or bending it over the edge is cumbersome and weak.

**Solution:** The ledger sheet has separable strips above and below; the carbon paper is slipped onto punched tongues on the lower strip and its upper edge is bent over the upper strip.
