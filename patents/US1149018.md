## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper-feed controlling apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for effecting positive rotation of web roll in which power is applied to web roll; Web or thread actuated

**How it works (reading):** A rail with perforations on the paper supply roll senses damage and stops the press, and the paper is adjusted to keep register with the printing devices.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper fed to printing presses may be damaged or run irregularly, and the press is not stopped in time.

**Solution:** A rail with perforations on the paper supply roll senses damage and stops the press, and the paper is adjusted to keep register with the printing devices.
