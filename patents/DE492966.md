## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** The lever is guided by a pin passing through a transverse slot at the fork end and is loosely connected at its pivot, so it cannot bend and clamp; a stop limits its deflection.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon fork drive lever bends sideways under lateral pressure and clamps the ribbon fork on its guide, so the ribbon is not presented to the print point.

**Solution:** The lever is guided by a pin passing through a transverse slot at the fork end and is loosely connected at its pivot, so it cannot bend and clamp; a stop limits its deflection.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** ribbon fork; drive lever; pivot in slot; universal bar; knee lever; stop; guide pin
