## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper feeding devices of typewriting machines, and especially those of the front strike variety, in which it is desired to make carbon copies record sheet (to be subsequently bound into a book) of checks.

**How it works (reading):** The top pressure rolls are carried on a rod that can be moved forward and back so they bear on the platen at the exact point needed for the width and stiffness of the sheet; the pendent parts can be inclined to compensate, and the rolls can be cast off to withdraw a check.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Small record sheets, checks and bills are hard to hold against the platen in front-strike machines, and the usual paper fingers and pressure rolls do not keep them in place or let the sheet be withdrawn.

**Solution:** The top pressure rolls are carried on a rod that can be moved forward and back so they bear on the platen at the exact point needed for the width and stiffness of the sheet; the pendent parts can be inclined to compensate, and the rolls can be cast off to withdraw a check.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
