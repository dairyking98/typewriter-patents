## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens with balancing means

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the invention is now to provide a simple and effective adjustment device for the roller to create the roller with reference to the line of the type imprint can be adjusted so that the two ends of the roller have the same positions In relation to the stop surface.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustment device on the roller frame sets the platen so both ends are at the same distance from the type-lever stop surface.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** If the platen is not parallel to the carriage line, type levers strike the stop surface before the type reaches the paper, so impressions differ at the two ends of a line.

**Solution:** An adjustment device on the roller frame sets the platen so both ends are at the same distance from the type-lever stop surface.
