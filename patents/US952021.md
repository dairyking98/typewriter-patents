## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Combine the advantages of a full keyboard and a shift machine, with large types and many characters.

**Solution:** Front-strike typebar system and keyboard arranged for a large number of characters.

**How it works:** Front-strike machine with base, posts and top plate carrying a rectangular carriage on grooved rails with anti-friction balls. A developed diagram of the type bar system and keyboard is given. The rest is not in the opening.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** type bar; keyboard; carriage; grooved rails; balls; top plate
