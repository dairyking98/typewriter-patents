## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates primarily to a front strike typewriting machine.

**How it works (reading):** Two accelerating breaking toggle links act in series, the first driven by the finger piece and the second connected to the type bar, so the type bar accelerates at the sum of the two rates.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single breaking toggle link accelerates the type bar too little for a fast front-strike stroke.

**Solution:** Two accelerating breaking toggle links act in series, the first driven by the finger piece and the second connected to the type bar, so the type bar accelerates at the sum of the two rates.
