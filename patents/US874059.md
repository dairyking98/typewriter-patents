## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier tabulators are said to be inadequate for listing numbers in vertical columns.

**Solution:** A tabulator attachment with a rack bar driven by a pinion on a vertical spindle positions the carriage; the OCR is too poor for detail.
