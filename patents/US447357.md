## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor's earlier Hall-type machine made it hard to insert paper and had a complex lever train feeding the carriage.

**Solution:** A Hall-type machine with a stationary printing frame whose pivot arms swing forward to expose the carriage, and a simple lever and pawl that steps the paper carriage along a rack.

**How it works:** The printing frame is two plates joined by links: a lower plate with an inking pad and an upper plate carrying the printing form, as in the Hall patent 238,387. The frame hangs on arms pivoted at front lugs, so the whole frame swings forward to expose the carriage for loading paper. The paper carriage rides on bearing rollers with a T-foot in a base slot and carries a toothed rack bar. A pivoted lever with a pawl steps the rack one space per movement of an operating slide, and removing a pin lets one full depression feed two spaces. A stop limits travel and feed-rollers carry the paper.

**Features:** [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [IMP:platen-to-element](../features/imp-platen-to-element.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [ELEM:plate-flat](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)

**Parts:** printing frame; inking pad plate; printing-form plate; link; pivot arm; paper carriage; rack bar; feed lever; pawl; operating slide; stop; feed-roll

**Numbers:** one depression feeds two spaces when the pin is removed

**Improves on:**

- [US414588](../patents/US414588.md): front-pivoted arms for easy paper insertion and fewer levers in the carriage feed
- Hall typewriter US238387: keeps its printing mechanism but moves the carriage rather than the printing head
