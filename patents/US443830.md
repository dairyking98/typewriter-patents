## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type faces collect ink and dust and need brushing without picking them one by one.

**Solution:** A disk of stiff bristles on a lever-bar rises under hand pressure and returns by its own weight to rest out of the way of the type.

**How it works:** Bristle disk on a pivoted lever-bar, raised by hand and falling back by weight.
