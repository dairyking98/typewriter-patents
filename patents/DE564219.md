## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**How it works (reading):** Mount the key set on a slider, with keys whose heads span two type levers, so shifting the slider makes the keys strike either one type set or both at once.

## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Booking typewriters with two type sets joined by long rods take much space, develop play, and need laborious rod swapping to work the sets independently.

**Solution:** Mount the key set on a slider, with keys whose heads span two type levers, so shifting the slider makes the keys strike either one type set or both at once.

**How it works:** Two carriages, one behind the other, carry three paper rollers. Two sets of type levers lie parallel and close together, with no individual keys. A single key set sits on a slider moved horizontally across the levers by a three-position lever. Each key rod, spring-returned, has a widened head wider than the spacing of the two levers. In the middle position the head covers both levers and strikes both type sets together; in the outer positions it covers only one. Projections on the head ends stop the levers slipping.
