## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-cleaning devices for type-writing machines, its object being to provide a simple, cheap, and efficient device of the character specified and one which can be readily attached to or detached from a type-writing machine and also secured to the frame of the machine when not in use.

**How it works (reading):** A casing holds a brush driven by actuating means; the device is extensible and can be stowed in a frame standard or held in the leg by an elastic cord, applied to a Remington.
