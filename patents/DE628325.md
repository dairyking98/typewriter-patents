## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Keyboards

**How it works (reading):** The key button turns on a round pivot pin with locking pins that sit farther from the axis, engaging holes in the key-lever shoulder, and the button is pulled forward to rotate to another face.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-face key buttons on the main patent can wobble because the locking surfaces sit close to the pivot axis.

**Solution:** The key button turns on a round pivot pin with locking pins that sit farther from the axis, engaging holes in the key-lever shoulder, and the button is pulled forward to rotate to another face.
