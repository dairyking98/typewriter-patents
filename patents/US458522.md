## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a horizontal shiftkey bar for controlling the side movement of the carriage of a type-writing machine in writing upper-case letters, the said key-bar reaching, as it does, from side to side of the key-board, and the saddle of its pendent operating-rod mounted on the key-lever that controls the upper-case movement.

**How it works (reading):** A horizontal shift key-bar across the keyboard, pivoted on the front rod, pushes down the upper-case key lever through a pendent rod.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The shift key sat at one side of the keyboard, forcing the hand across to capitalise.

**Solution:** A horizontal shift key-bar across the keyboard, pivoted on the front rod, pushes down the upper-case key lever through a pendent rod.

**How it works:** The elongated bar has arms with clips pivoted on a transverse rod and a screw-pivoted rod at one corner hanging to a saddle clipped on the upper-case key lever. Tapping the bar with either hand depresses that lever and shifts the carriage. Reversed end for end, it works on a machine whose normal state is upper case.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** shift key-bar; clip; transverse rod; pendent rod; saddle; key lever
