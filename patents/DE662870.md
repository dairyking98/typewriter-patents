> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a device on typewriters for setting slip sheets in front of a main sheet already clamped on the paper roller with a device in front of the platen for holding, guiding and taking the slip sheet with you in the respective writing position. There are known devices which are releasably attached to the paper trolley and in which a slide carrying a forward sheet clamping device is displaceably and adjustably mounted on spars. In these known devices, a slip sheet inserted into the clamping device was passed from the front under the platen and held in the writing position.
