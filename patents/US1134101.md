## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapement and shift support need a light, smooth arrangement.

**Solution:** Shiftable carriage on ball tracks in a shift frame on parallel links, with a rack and two feed dogs (one stepping, one fixed).

**How it works:** The carriage rides on balls in a shift bar, which is carried by two sets of parallel links to the side plates. A spring drum pulls the carriage by a band. A rack bar has teeth engaged by a stepping dog and a fixed dog on a dog rocker, linked by a spring so the carriage steps one tooth.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** carriage; shift bar; parallel link; spring drum; feed rack; stepping dog; fixed dog; dog rocker; spring
