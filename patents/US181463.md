## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing several styles and sizes of type with proper letter spacing, without independent adjustable type carriers.

**Solution:** A type-wheel with types inserted radially in parallel circles, one circle per style, is shifted axially to bring the wanted style opposite a fixed print point where a cam lever drives the type onto the paper.

**How it works:** The wheel is loosely mounted on a vertical shaft over a grooved cylinder keyed to the shaft, and has several series of radial slots in parallel lines. Each type has a stem with a head and a spiral spring, so it can be pushed outward in its slot. The same letter in different styles lies on a line parallel to the wheel axis. A sleeve with a circumferential groove on the wheel hub lets the wheel be adjusted along the shaft to choose the style. Finger-keys act through bars, vertical rods and cam bars on sliding cam-rods in the cylinder grooves, and pivoted levers with cam projections strike the type of the selected row as it passes the fixed point, driven by power on the main shafts through bevel gears.
