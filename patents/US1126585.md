## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tally feed roll and tally clamp sat on one rocking frame, leaving an intermediate position where the tally strip was neither clamped nor fed, so it advanced irregularly under spool tension and spaced lines unevenly.

**Solution:** A tally clamp controlled separately from the tally feed roll holds the strip at all times, removing the unclamped intermediate position.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** tally feed roll; tally clamp; rocking frame; tally spool
