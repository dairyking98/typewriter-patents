## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Copy held flat or upright makes copying awkward and line-skipping likely; this is not a typewriter patent but a copy-reading aid.

**Solution:** A frame holds two mirrors so the copy text is reflected to a comfortable viewing angle, and it can double as a line indicator.
