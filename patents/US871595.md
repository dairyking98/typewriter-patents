## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing bills requires a two-fold manipulation of a release finger-piece and a crank to rotate the platen back.

**Solution:** A yielding detent holds the crank in normal position; a single swinging movement of the crank releases it, connects it to the platen, and rotates the platen.

**How it works:** One swing of the crank both disengages the detent and connects it to the platen so the platen turns back for a new bill.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** yielding crank detent; single-swing platen rotation
