## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The cast rear connecting web of the paper carriage frame is hard to make, brittle and breaks often.

**Solution:** The web is punched from sheet metal as a plate with bearing tabs and openings for the carriage rollers, then bent in a bending die into the curved bar, where the flanges stiffen it.
