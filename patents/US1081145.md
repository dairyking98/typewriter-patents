## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar pivots needed a large low-friction bearing that could be ground and that resisted rotation in the wrong direction.

**Solution:** The type-bar head has a conical recess that seats a semi-spherical pivot body on the hanger strap, held by a central screw; ribs on hanger and bar form a lubricant chamber and a substantial bearing surface.
