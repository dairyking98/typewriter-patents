## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switching device for the ribbon drive of typewriters, in which by a pin arranged in a vertical hollow spindle of the coil, which falls down after the ribbon has been unwound, the switchover is initiated will.

**How it works (reading):** A pin in a hollow spindle drops in when the ribbon is unwound; it engages a slot in an expandable lever that is rocked by a drive lever, changing the deflection so the switching levers of the two spools engage and disengage through a transfer rod, with few parts in parallel planes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spool switching that needs crankings and many parts is less reliable and harder to manufacture.

**Solution:** A pin in a hollow spindle drops in when the ribbon is unwound; it engages a slot in an expandable lever that is rocked by a drive lever, changing the deflection so the switching levers of the two spools engage and disengage through a transfer rod, with few parts in parallel planes.
