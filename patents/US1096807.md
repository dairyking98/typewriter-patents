## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Prior platen-crank stops could not arrest the platen at all points and had limited capacity for condensed billing.

**Solution:** An improved stop on a rack lets the crank be arrested at any point within the device's range, increasing its capacity.

**How it works:** The crank is rotated through more than one revolution, and an adjustable stop on a rack arrests it on the second revolution.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
