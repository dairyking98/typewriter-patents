## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-roller frame whose support rail flexes, so the stroke of the paper roller is uneven and the print is irregular.

**Solution:** Gives the support rail a U-shaped cross-section for high rigidity at low weight, and mounts the paper guide rollers on bearing plates and levers that hook onto the rail.
