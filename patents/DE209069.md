## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper roll frame with paper guiding device for typewriters, where the change between large and small letters is achieved by lifting the platen with the paper will.

**How it works (reading):** Gives the support rail a U-shaped cross-section for high rigidity at low weight, and mounts the paper guide rollers on bearing plates and levers that hook onto the rail.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-roller frame whose support rail flexes, so the stroke of the paper roller is uneven and the print is irregular.

**Solution:** Gives the support rail a U-shaped cross-section for high rigidity at low weight, and mounts the paper guide rollers on bearing plates and levers that hook onto the rail.
