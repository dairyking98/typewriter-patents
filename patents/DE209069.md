The stroke of the paper roller must always follow very evenly; any deflection of the support rail of the frame causes an irregularity in scripture. According to the invention, this source of error is eliminated as far as possible by that the support rail of the frame is given a U-shaped cross-section, as such is known Greatest rigidity combined with the lowest dead weight and at the same time the paper guiding device allowed to be attached in a simple manner.
