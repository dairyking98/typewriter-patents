## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** On cash register line setting, the one-line shift of the printing table is derived as a drive movement from the machine gear, which requires a drive connection.

**Solution:** A keypad of twenty-six keys, one per account-book line, sets the printing table by hand, while a tensioned spring performs the shift; the machine gear gives only a control movement.

**How it works:** The pressed key sets the table to the chosen line, and the spring moves it; the machine transmission only controls the setting.
