## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper wrinkles around the platen as the paper is fed in.

**Solution:** Conical edge guide rollers are set so that, during the stepwise platen rotation, the paper is pulled outward from the middle to the edges, which keeps the web taut.
