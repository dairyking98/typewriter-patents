## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**In the patent’s own words:** This purpose is to mount it in a cross-bar, G, hinged at c to the case andheld at its opposite end by a latch, d, which, when disengaged from the bar, will permit the'latter to swing outwardly on its hinge, thus removing it and the center F from the end of the shell and leaving the latter open for the insertion or removal of the shaft or reel or roll of paper.

**How it works (reading):** A supply roll and a take-up mandrel reel, driven from a moving part of the machine, feed a continuous web through the feed rolls and then sever it into lengths.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters fed with separate sheets, and the inventor wants printing on a continuous paper web instead.

**Solution:** A supply roll and a take-up mandrel reel, driven from a moving part of the machine, feed a continuous web through the feed rolls and then sever it into lengths.
