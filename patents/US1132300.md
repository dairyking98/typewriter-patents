## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A carriage bearing needs to absorb unevenness and grit.

**Solution:** A resilient plate presses anti-friction balls so the carriage rail bearing self-compensates.

**How it works:** A C-shaped sheet metal carriage supporting member runs on balls in grooves in a rail and a resilient plate on the shift frame. The plate pushes rearward through balls on the carriage, compensating for irregularity and grit. End plates, tie rod and platen shaft complete the carriage.
