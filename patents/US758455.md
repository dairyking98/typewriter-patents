## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to so construct the ribbon-carrier that the ribbonspools can be quickly and easily placed therein or removed therefrom.
