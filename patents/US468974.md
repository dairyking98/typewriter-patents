## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to matrix-machines adapted to form stereotype-matrices by the to successive impression of type-dies;

**How it works (reading):** Ratchet-wheel feed mechanism and space-registering levers move the matrix carriage by a per-character amount, driven by a rack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Matrix feed and word spacing need to vary for different characters so justification is easier.

**Solution:** Ratchet-wheel feed mechanism and space-registering levers move the matrix carriage by a per-character amount, driven by a rack.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
