## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improves the mechanisms that rotate a common circular type holder and the stylus/plunger of an indicator-plate typewriter of the Axial type.

**Solution:** A rotating holder carrying a circle of independent type-bars is turned to the chosen character via an indicator plate and a stylus/plunger makes the imprint, with new rotating and plunger mechanisms.

**How it works:** A ring of type-bars, each with one character in a circle about the axis, hangs from a common holder on an arbor within a sleeve. A hand-moved indicator over a stationary perforated plate rotates the holder so the chosen type comes to the print point, then a stylus or plunger strikes that bar to take the imprint on paper on a roller carriage. The ink ribbon winds between two rollers above; the upper frame hinges up from the lower frame which carries the carriage, feed, line-space and bell. The details of the rotating and plunger mechanisms are beyond the opening text.
