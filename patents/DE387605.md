**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for Placing the printing unit on a calculating machine with adjusting disks of variable Number of teeth that can only be obtained by moving the rubber roller that causes the printing to the side can be turned off.

**How it works (reading):** The rubber platen roller that presses paper against the type carrier is pulled sideways on a guide rail, which disengages the drive and parks the printing unit while bringing the printed result into a readable position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A calculating machine with variable-tooth setting disks must still print at the drive position, so the printing has to be switched off when the machine is not in use.

**Solution:** The rubber platen roller that presses paper against the type carrier is pulled sideways on a guide rail, which disengages the drive and parks the printing unit while bringing the printed result into a readable position.
