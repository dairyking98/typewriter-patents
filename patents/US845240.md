## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the paper is released for adjustment the platen scale is not at the writing line, and the scale can be struck by the types when the rolls are restored.

**Solution:** Connects the platen scale to the pressure-roll release key so the scale moves to the writing line when the paper is freed and drops clear of the types when the rolls return.

**How it works:** Pressing the release finger rocks a shaft with cams that free the pressure rolls and linkage raises the scale to the writing line; restoring the rolls lowers it.
