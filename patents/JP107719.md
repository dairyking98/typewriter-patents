## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Turning the platen back by hand to type in a dropped character is imprecise and makes the printing uneven.

**Solution:** A back-feed key drives a cam that frees the forward pawls, turns the drive gear back with its own pawl, and re-engages a reverse holding pawl before the cam finishes, so the platen steps back an exact amount.

**How it works:** Normal line feed: a rocking frame worked by the print or vertical-feed key moves a feed pawl on a loose arm over the drive gear teeth. A swinging lever exchanges a stop pawl and a forward holding pawl, so the gear steps forward one set amount, ended by a limit piece. For back-feed, the back-feed key presses a rocking frame which turns a two-lobed cam. The long lobe lifts the feed pawl off the gear, the short lobe momentarily lifts the reverse holding pawl, and a spring-lifted back-feed pawl on the cam meshes and turns the gear backward. While the cam is still acting the reverse holding pawl drops onto the next tooth and stops the back turn. The drive gear meshes with a gear on the platen end.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Paper-cylinder back-feed device for a Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a paper-cylinder back-feed device for a Japanese-script printing machine, in which, by pressing down a back-feed key-lever, a cam linked to a rocking frame that the lever's tip presses down frees from the driving gear the feed pawl at the end of the rocking arm (worked by the printing key-lever or the vertical-feed key-lever) and the reverse holding pawl; at the same time a back-feed pawl, connected to the cam and normally kept out of mesh with the driving gear, turns the driving gear backward; and while the cam is still acting, the reverse holding pawl is made to mesh with the driving gear again. Its object is a simple construction by which pressing the back-feed lever turns the paper cylinder back correctly, so that the back-feeding often needed while typing is done accurately and the unevenness of printing caused by back-feeding is removed.
