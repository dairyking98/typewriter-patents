## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (photocell control for booking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character recognition characterised by the type of writing of printed characters having additional code marks or containing code marks

**How it works (reading):** Control cards carry simple stamps; each character shape gives a different light-cell excitation strength, and cells with different sensitivities are arranged in sequence to address different control devices.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Light-cell control of statistical machines needs many parts or large space, and mechanical card sensing is costly to make.

**Solution:** Control cards carry simple stamps; each character shape gives a different light-cell excitation strength, and cells with different sensitivities are arranged in sequence to address different control devices.
