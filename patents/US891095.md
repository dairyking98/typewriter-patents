## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Right and left margin gages on the carriage need to act at any point of the carriage travel.

**Solution:** A rocking frame at the front of the machine, driven by an interponent on the margin gage block, operates the bell and the key lock at any carriage position.
