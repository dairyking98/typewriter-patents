## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of counting devices

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide a site sides of the machine and extend over the word-counter that can be readily attached to space-bar 5.

**How it works (reading):** A tallying register on one spacing lever counts single spaces, while the second spacing lever does not operate the counter; the spacing mechanism can work with or without counting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The space bar counts every space, but double spaces in orderly layout should not be counted.

**Solution:** A tallying register on one spacing lever counts single spaces, while the second spacing lever does not operate the counter; the spacing mechanism can work with or without counting.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
