## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to carriage return devices for business writing machines in which the pawl is arranged on a lever pivotable through a fixed angle.

**How it works (reading):** The pawl is guided by a pin in a curved path on a coaxial lever, and an adjusting lever sets the curve so that the pawl engages the platen ratchet from behind with a compact layout.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pivot-arm line switching needs a large overhang in front of the platen, while the sliding-pawl type of switching needs large space, and the travel of the line switch cannot be changed without big parts.

**Solution:** The pawl is guided by a pin in a curved path on a coaxial lever, and an adjusting lever sets the curve so that the pawl engages the platen ratchet from behind with a compact layout.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
