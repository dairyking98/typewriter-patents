## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Choosing the colour band of a two-colour ribbon needs adjustable ribbon lift.

**Solution:** Two axially sliding wrists coupled to move oppositely so one engages the ribbon-vibrating lever's slotted actuator while the other is silenced.

**How it works:** Sliding either wrist into the vertical slot of the actuator gives the ribbon vibrator a short or long stroke.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** wrist; actuator; ribbon vibrating lever; ribbon

**Improves on:**

- Underwood front-strike machine: coupled wrists select ribbon lift
