## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon carrier with a constant throw that either vibrates to cover the printing point or is latched and held in a fixed position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Two-colour ribbon fields need to be brought to the printing point by hand or by a vibrating carrier.

**Solution:** A ribbon carrier with a constant throw that either vibrates to cover the printing point or is latched and held in a fixed position.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
