The invention relates to a paper guide device for typewriters, typewriters o. The like. With an insertion plate arranged in front of the platen for pre-plugging Forms that have moved from a rest position into alignment with the lower paper guide plate can be brought. Such facilities are used to make it easier to advance To enable arches and have already become known in numerous embodiments. However, they had the disadvantage that they interfered with the use of the machine by they either obstructed the view of the platen or a manipulation of the Ribbon fork made difficult, etc., so that their arrangement was previously felt to be disadvantageous became. According to the invention, these disadvantages are now eliminated by the Movement of the lead-in sheet from its rest position to the active position and vice versa is controlled by a bow thrust crank gear.
