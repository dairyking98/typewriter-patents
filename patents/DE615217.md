## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lead-in plates for forms in front of the platen block the view or the ribbon fork when in use.

**Solution:** A crank-and-bow-thrust lever linkage swings the lead-in plate towards the platen and lowers it, and returns it to a rest position out of view.
