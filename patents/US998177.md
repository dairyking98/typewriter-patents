## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to protectors intended for use in connection with checks, drafts, bonds, or other commercial paper for mark- 1n,D amount drawn.

**How it works (reading):** A small compact protector cuts characters through the check from both sides and inks the cut surfaces, using duplicate-value characters of different kinds.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check amounts can be raised, and cut characters need to be marked on both sides.

**Solution:** A small compact protector cuts characters through the check from both sides and inks the cut surfaces, using duplicate-value characters of different kinds.
