## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements on the inventor's earlier common-printing-point machine (US357670) in the way the radial typebars are carried and driven.

**Solution:** Typebars arranged radially in a circle slide endwise on fulcra carried by rocking arms driven from the key-levers through toothed segments and pinions.

**How it works:** Typebars lie radially in a ring on a supporting disk or annulus with a post at the center. Their outer ends are pivoted to rocking arms with pinions that mesh with toothed segments on levers. Pressing a key throws a rod up, which lifts a lever, rocking the arm and thereby vibrating the bar on its fulcrum and sliding it endwise to carry the type to the common printing-point. A spring retracts the lever on release. A C-spring is also described. The paper carriage steps across the printing point.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
