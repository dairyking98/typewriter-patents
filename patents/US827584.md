## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a writing-machine;

**How it works (reading):** A machine with two sets of keys arranged for the fingers and thumbs of both hands, pressed or pulled to produce characters, with a device for reading the characters produced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary writing machines are too slow to match an expert stenographer.

**Solution:** A machine with two sets of keys arranged for the fingers and thumbs of both hands, pressed or pulled to produce characters, with a device for reading the characters produced.

**Features:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md)
