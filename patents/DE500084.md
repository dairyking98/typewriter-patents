## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting paper and cards is hard because the platen-side parts and type segment block the platen gap.

**Solution:** A single lever lowers the type lever segment and folds the type guide, ribbon guide, line scale and paper holder away from the platen, held by a locking spring.
