## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to certain improvements in type-bar bearings for typewriting and similar machines employing a system of levers having their types or striking points located some distance from their axes of movement, and adapted to strike at the same point in the same straight line.

**How it works (reading):** Bearing arrangement for type-bars meant to keep alinement longer while letting each bar swing freely under gravity.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar bearings wear over their whole arc, so lost motion builds up and printing alinement drifts.

**Solution:** Bearing arrangement for type-bars meant to keep alinement longer while letting each bar swing freely under gravity.
