## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to adapt typewriting machines having feed-rolls and banks of keys for the reception of stifi cards now largely used in libraries and business-houses for indexing work, whereby it will not be necessary to bend the cards at all.
