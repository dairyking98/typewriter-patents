## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to adapt typewriting machines having feed-rolls and banks of keys for the reception of stifi cards now largely used in libraries and business-houses for indexing work, whereby it will not be necessary to bend the cards at all.

**How it works (reading):** A trough-shaped card holder mounts on the feed-rolls, engages the bottom edge of the card and holds it in the printing line without bending; it is adjustable for card depth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Stiff index cards cannot be fed by feed-rolls without bending, so they do not sit on the writing line.

**Solution:** A trough-shaped card holder mounts on the feed-rolls, engages the bottom edge of the card and holds it in the printing line without bending; it is adjustable for card depth.
