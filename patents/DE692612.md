## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holding, feeding, or guiding means therefor

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon holder runs through a longitudinal slot in the platen, so the coloured strip stays connected to the writing surface.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carbonless writing with coloured sheets smears when the colour ribbon sits loose on the writing surface.

**Solution:** A ribbon holder runs through a longitudinal slot in the platen, so the coloured strip stays connected to the writing surface.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Ribbon carrier for carbonless devices In order to avoid the smearing of the sheets to be written on, which is inevitable when using colored sheets with carbonless devices, colored ribbons or colored stripes are used that are stretched out over the writing surface and usually only have a line width and can be set to one line. The frame, which is used to stretch and guide the color strip, however, requires a relatively cumbersome guidance on the writing device, mostly by means of toothed wheels and racks. - If only U-shaped slides encompassing the longitudinal edges of the writing surface are used to stretch the ribbon, they will not find a hold at the beginning of the writing surface and can slide off. According to the invention a simple and permanent connection between the sliders for supporting and relaxing of the ribbon with the platen is achieved by the fact * that a holder of the ink ribbon extends through a longitudinal slot in the platen. Accordingly, this remains connected to the writing surface without interfering with the easy replacement of the ink ribbon.
