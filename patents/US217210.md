## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Many separate types are needed for a full alphabet.

**Solution:** A nest of elementary-mark types pushed in combinations by a key to form each letter as a monogram.

**How it works:** A rectangular nest of types, each bearing an elementary line, curve or dot, is protruded by key levers so only those types for the chosen letter print. Thirteen elements in the preferred form give letters and digits.

**Features:** [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [USE:special-script](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** nest; type; key lever; barrel

**Numbers:** 13 elements
