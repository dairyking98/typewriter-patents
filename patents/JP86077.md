## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Make a typewriter as simple and quick as possible, with type found fast without moving the hand around a keyboard.

**Solution:** A rotatable polygonal type bar slides sideways in a hinged cover frame by a key-pinion on a rack; a jab on the key drops the frame to print, and the rise of the frame inks the type and ratchets the paper frame one space.

**How it works:** The multi-sided type bar carries types on half its faces and matching index characters on the other half; a wheel turns it to the wanted face. Turning the key rotates a pinion meshing a rack on the type-bar support frame, sliding the bar left or right until the character shows in a sighting hole. A sharp jab on the key makes its foot strike a stop, and the hinged cover frame drops by inertia so the type prints on paper held on a convex paper frame. A spring-loaded lifting lever returns the frame; as it rises a curved arm swings an ink roller up against the types and a pawl turns a ratchet gear that drives a rack to advance the paper frame one character pitch. A plate on the gear sets the pitch and a detent lever holds the rack still.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. This invention concerns a printing machine in which a rack on the frame that carries the type bar meshes with a pinion working with the key, so that the key moves the type bar; a ratchet pawl pivoted on the lifting lever that presses the cover frame upward engages a rack on the paper frame, so that the paper frame is moved as that lever rises; and ink is applied by a curved lever with a spring that throws it back. Its object is to make the machine as simple as possible and printing quick. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show an embodiment.
