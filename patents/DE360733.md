## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriters cannot produce justified lines of equal width as in letterpress.

**Solution:** Individual type sticks of varying widths are stored in magazines, brought out to form the line, and spaces are inserted between words to fill the line length.

**How it works:** Keys push rods that set levers on sliding pieces moving through the machine, stepping along rows of levers per letter. The levers lift the appropriate magazine and push the corresponding type stick out to the writing position. The sticks are assembled side by side in a line, the line length is set and spaces inserted between words, then printed against the roller. Supplement to main patent 357960.
