## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be adjusted by a fraction of a line relative to the line-space wheel, which needs a connection that is firm in use.

**Solution:** A clutch with a loose line-space wheel carrying an internal gear, a pinion on an arm, and toggles with a clutch shoe, with a spring-forced wedge that keeps the shoe engaged and the gear meshed.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
