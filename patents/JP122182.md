## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a Japanese typewriter the paper cylinder feed must be switchable between vertical and horizontal writing at any time.

**Solution:** A slide rod driven by the press lever is raised or lowered by a hand lever through a push-pull rod so it engages either the vertical-feed or horizontal-feed member.

**How it works:** Depressing the press lever moves a link, rocking levers and pawl lever that slide a rod back and forth. The rod has one part for the pin of the vertical-feed lever and a pawl for the projection of the horizontal-feed rocker. A hand lever on the outside pushes or pulls a rod whose pin follows a slanted slot in a tube, turning a shaft and lever that raise or lower an operating rod joined to the slide rod. Upright, only vertical feed is worked; laid flat, only horizontal feed. A separate press bar gives a blank feed.
