## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanism driven by cams engaging rotating roller

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to a new and useful improvement in automatic type-writing machines, and has for its object to provide a simple and effective mechanism for automatically operating the type-bars of such a machine under the control of the keys in such manner as to require but light action of said keys.
