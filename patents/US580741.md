## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need heavy key pressure; the operator should only trigger the strike.

**Solution:** A constantly revolving power roll drives segmental wheels that are brought into contact by a light key touch and swing the typebars through pitmen.

**How it works:** A motor-driven roll turns continuously. Depressing a key lets a lever move inward under spring, pressing a segmental wheel against the roll so it revolves. A pitman from the wheel pulls a lever down, throwing the typebar against the platen; a pin on the wheel hits a stop to halt it, and releasing the key returns everything by spring.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** revolving roll; segmental wheel; key lever; bell-crank; pitman; typebar; stop pin; return spring
