> Description Translated from German Switching device on typewriters or the like. The invention relates to a switching device on typewriters, in particular for with Cumulative pulling device equipped machines or the like. In the previously known devices of this type, the coupling could to operate the paper carriage switchover, e.g. B. when writing out a negative sum by adding sums, only by pressing the general reverse key by hand, which required increased attention on the part of the operator and meant a loss of time. The general reverse key itself could only be operated manually. These disadvantages are now eliminated in that the control the switchover takes place automatically by the manual control element for the sum train, that with the switching device through a between the manual control element and the movement path the linkage arranged for the counter or counters can be coupled. For example, in the drawings is an embodiment of the invention shown.
