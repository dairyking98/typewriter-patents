## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to that class em loyed for writing in books.

**How it works (reading):** The platen is set perpendicular to the line of writing and the printing mechanism travels relative to the keyboard in the line-spacing direction, with several type-carriers thrown to the printing line in turn.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing in books needs the platen set across the line of writing, but a standard machine prints only along the platen line.

**Solution:** The platen is set perpendicular to the line of writing and the printing mechanism travels relative to the keyboard in the line-spacing direction, with several type-carriers thrown to the printing line in turn.

- **Type element:** [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
