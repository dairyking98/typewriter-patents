## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Throwing the escapement out so the carriage can run freely needs a better shift device.

**Solution:** A light bar pivoted on the carriage that swings the escapement pawls rearwards out of the ratcheted bar.

**How it works:** On a Densmore-type machine, a carrying arm with a hook pawl and a spring-pressed sliding pawl rocks with every key and engages the next tooth of the ratcheted bar under the platen carriage. The pawl bracket pivots on a vertical pivot with a torsion spring holding it in. A light strip pivoted on the carriage swings against the pawl to force the pawl devices back and release the carriage for free endwise running.
