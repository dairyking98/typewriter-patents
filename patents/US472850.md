## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators of Remington and Caligraph machines needed to stop the carriage temporarily at any chosen point to align figures vertically in columns.

**Solution:** Numbered stop-keys on the left of the machine move a lever of graded throw that meets a stepped catch plate on the carriage.

**How it works:** Stop-keys of different lengths press the outer end of a lever pivoted at the front of the frame, raising its inner end toward the carriage by an amount set by the key's number. A stepped catch plate with graded notches is screwed adjustably along the carriage. The operator holds down the key for the digit count, advances the carriage with the spacer bar until the plate meets the lever, then releases and types.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** stop-key; pivoted lever; overhanging bracket; stepped catch plate; set-screw; slot; guide rod collar

**Improves on:**

- Remington and Caligraph fixed margin collar: temporary stop at any point in the traverse for columns
