## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding odd sheets, cards and labels (such as prescription blanks) through a machine whose type band is set by a pointer.

**Solution:** A swinging bail with a feed roller pressing a second roller on the carriage clamps and feeds a sheet, with a clip-and-slide label carrier and sheet holders.

**How it works:** The paper carriage has a lug bearing a feed roller and a swinging bail frame carrying a second roller; the frame is locked down by a spring lock and lifted to insert a sheet. A toothed wheel with spring gives step-wise hand rotation of the roller via a button, and a cut in the platen lets a sheet pass out. Carrier compartments on the platen hold blanks, an indicator plate positions the sheet, and a clip on a shaft that slides along bail arms takes a label to the roller.
