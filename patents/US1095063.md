## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making several carbon copies required the operator to stop and change carbon and paper sheets.

**Solution:** A removable attachment for standard typewriters feeds original and carbon sheets continuously, making the desired number of copies without stopping.

**How it works:** A frame carries rollers that move the original and carbon sheets through together, so copies are made in one pass.
