> Description Translated from German i Book typewriter, especially for account sheets to be inserted With the line-by-line posting of account sheets in the typewriter, the whole Particular care should be taken by the scribe that they do not have the. last line is written out because this causes incorrect bookings and extremely annoying Errors arise. An account sheet cannot be completely written on because a transmission line must remain free, in which consequently it is not written on the line may be. The recorder is made aware of the fact by means of visual indications when the last lines come. These visual notices apply but only to the eye and therefore only to the attention of the writer; but this is not enough for the covering of a more extensive lettering. It is used to guide the sheets of paper around the typewriter roller also the one booking sheet is extended by a strip that shows when the the last line of the sheet in question is still far enough around the paper roller reaching around behind.
