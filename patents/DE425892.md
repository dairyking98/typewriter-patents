## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Clutches between the platen and its line-index wheel, used to set the paper to preprinted lines, are complicated.

**Solution:** Pairs of balls in a cage on the line-index wheel are pressed by an axial cone against a brake jacket in the platen core, and a push button releases them.

**How it works:** The line-index wheel runs loose on a platen hub and carries a sleeve acting as ball cage with paired balls on a flattened cone. A spring normally holds the cone so the balls press against a capsule pressed into the platen core, locking platen and index wheel together. Pressing a button on the rod through the hollow axes moves the cone by a pin so the balls lie loose. The platen can then be turned by either handle to any angle independent of the index wheel division. Releasing the button restores the coupling.
