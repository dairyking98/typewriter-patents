**How it works (reading):** A stop body of thin spring steel with an enclosed air space is set in a dovetail groove of the type lever bearing, with the travel limited to a fraction of a millimetre, so the type lever stops at a precise position without much noise.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Soft or resilient stops let the type lever overshoot its path, while hard steel stops are loud, and layered stops are not much quieter.

**Solution:** A stop body of thin spring steel with an enclosed air space is set in a dovetail groove of the type lever bearing, with the travel limited to a fraction of a millimetre, so the type lever stops at a precise position without much noise.
