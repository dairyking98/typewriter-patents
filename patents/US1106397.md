## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic typing from a perforated master sheet has relied on pneumatic actuation, which the inventor wants to avoid.

**Solution:** A perforated master sheet is read by mechanism that depresses the typewriter key levers one by one, transmitting power to the keys directly and positively.

**Features:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
