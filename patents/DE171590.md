To this Purposes, a screw is arranged on the platen axis in a known manner, which when rotating the roller rotates a dental arch lever, which with its free end hits against an adjustable stop and thus prevents the rotation of the roller, as soon as the lower writing limit of the sheet of paper is reached. Here is the dental arch lever in front of the platen axis on a plate that is pivotably mounted behind the same rotatably arranged and is under spring action, so that it disengages from the worm wheel when the plate is lifted comes and is returned to its initial position by the spring action.
