## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for cap positioning

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to pro- Vide for the carriage frame and its medium of rear support, certain adjustable and stop devices for bringing the under face of the platen in exactly the desired plane to receive the printing impact of the type-bars, and for preventing any unnecessary degree of oscillatory or tilting movement of the carriage as it is grasped by the hand to run it from end to end of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** A rear supplemental bar, pivoted at its middle to the carriage frame, rides on the rear rail at two points so the carriage is positively levelled.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage supported at the front and one point at the rear can rock with warp or twist, so the platen does not take the type impact squarely.

**Solution:** A rear supplemental bar, pivoted at its middle to the carriage frame, rides on the rear rail at two points so the carriage is positively levelled.

**How it works:** The carriage is a rectangular frame with rolls at the front on a front rail and a rear member sliding on a rear rail. A supplemental bar is pivoted at its centre to the rear member through a vertically slotted aperture and screw, with its ends supported on the rear rail through hollow hubs. This gives two support points whatever the warp. Adjustable and stop devices set the platen's underside in the exact printing plane and limit tilting when the carriage is run by hand. The propelling spring acts on the part that engages the rail hubs.
