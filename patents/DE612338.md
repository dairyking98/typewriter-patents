## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key lever spring tension is hard to adjust, and a screw on a pivoting plate jams or the rail tilts when adjusted unevenly.

**Solution:** A rotatable axle carries a rail on cams with an adjusting screw lever, so all key lever springs are tensioned together, with per-spring screws optional.
