## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to type bodies with alignment marks for supporting the type body when attaching to the type lever, especially for typewriters, and aims to a reliable support of the type body on a surface during the fastening to be reached on the type lever.

**How it works (reading):** Alignment marks on lateral approaches on both long sides of the type body support it flat during attachment, and the approaches are ground off afterwards.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Narrow long type bodies tip sideways when attached to the type lever, giving a poor and uneven connection.

**Solution:** Alignment marks on lateral approaches on both long sides of the type body support it flat during attachment, and the approaches are ground off afterwards.

**Features:** [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md)

**Parts:** lateral approaches with alignment marks
