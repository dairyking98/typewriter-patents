## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A document holder behind the typewriter needs a folding frame that takes up space on small tables.

**Solution:** A lower document desk part hooks onto the handle of the case lid and rests on its inclined surface, so the lid itself becomes the desk surface and the holder stores in the case.
