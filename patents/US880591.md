## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon fields cannot change automatically at points in a line.

**Solution:** Adjustable shifting members change the throw of the vibratory carrier and so the ribbon field.

**How it works:** Members on the carriage and on a fixed part co-act at chosen carriage points. The change affects the throw on the next key stroke, not the ribbon directly. Applied to a Monarch No. 3.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; vibratory ribbon carrier; shifting member
