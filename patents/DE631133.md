## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a tabulator with a paper roller, the conveying path of the sheet being fed in cannot be easily changed or measured.

**Solution:** The paper roller always turns the same amount on ejecting and inserting; an auxiliary feed, engaged at the platen rotation and idle after feeding, moves the sheet further, and a stop arm or handle set by the tabulator selects off, cumulative-only, or every-cycle feeding.

**How it works:** The sheet insertion depth into the auxiliary feed sets how far the roller carries the sheet; alternate auxiliary feed webs shorten the conveying path.
