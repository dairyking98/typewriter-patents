## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register print stipple)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to printing units at cash registers, in which the print stipple during one and the same amount adjustment executes a multiple pressure movement and those for the generation of a single one Printing image also a repeated pressure movement of the pressure stamp is used.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable stop on the key bolt switches the slip conveying movement between the two plunger strokes on or off, giving single or double impression.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The number of print impressions per amount setting cannot be regulated at will.

**Solution:** An adjustable stop on the key bolt switches the slip conveying movement between the two plunger strokes on or off, giving single or double impression.
