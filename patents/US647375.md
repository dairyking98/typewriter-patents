## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriters in which only a small nu mber of keysfor instance, five-are employed, which are operated either singly or in various combinations to enable a comparatively large number of characters or signs to be written-for instance, thirty-one or thirty-two with five keys, or a multiple of thirty-two if two or more series of type are provided.

**How it works (reading):** A simpler and lighter five-key machine that selects among type-bands and travels bodily over stationary paper.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An earlier five-key combination typewriter by the same inventor was heavy and complicated.

**Solution:** A simpler and lighter five-key machine that selects among type-bands and travels bodily over stationary paper.

**How it works:** Five keys pressed singly or in combination give 31 or 32 characters, or multiples if several series are provided. A base with a line spacer carries a guide bar; a carriage travels on it and feeds letter by letter. A keyboard frame moves vertically toward the paper and works the carriage feed. An impression frame on it can also move transversely and carries a type-box holding type-bands that the keys position. A bell slider marks where writing starts and ends.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Type element:** [Flexible type band](../features/elem-band.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** base plate; guide bar; carriage; keyboard frame; impression frame; type-box; type-band; key; line spacer; bell slider

**Numbers:** five keys; 31 or 32 characters per series

**Improves on:**

- Carmona application Serial No. 669,151: simpler, lighter construction
