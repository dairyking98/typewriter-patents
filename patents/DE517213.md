## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator with reversible drum)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a calculating machine with a reversible drive drum, on whose) Yelle in a known manner adjustable selectors sit and when pressing a button Adjusting member, such as a sector, is adjusted differently, its stepped edge cooperates with an adjusting lever, which in turn controls the setting which induces selectors.

**How it works (reading):** A control disk on the drive drum presses resilient parts against the stepped adjusting member, so that the depressed key value is carried through a toothed arch into the drum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Entering values in a billing machine needs the selectors and adjusting levers to pick up the key value at the start of the drive drum turn.

**Solution:** A control disk on the drive drum presses resilient parts against the stepped adjusting member, so that the depressed key value is carried through a toothed arch into the drum.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** drive drum 2; selector links 6; toothed drive members 5; full keyboard
