## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates vto the line spacing mechanism of typewriting machines and has forits object to provide mechanism for that purposewwliich is practically noiseless in operation, a'nd, further, to provide improved means for causing the platen roll to register with the lines and for lfreeing it to permit universal spacing.

**How it works (reading):** Quiet line-spacing mechanism with a pawl and ratchet and a release for the platen roll, plus an alining scale with adjustable support so the platen registers with the lines and frees for universal spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing is noisy and the platen does not register reliably with the printed lines.

**Solution:** Quiet line-spacing mechanism with a pawl and ratchet and a release for the platen roll, plus an alining scale with adjustable support so the platen registers with the lines and frees for universal spacing.
