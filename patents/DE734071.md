## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Prior carriage stop pawls are cumbersome and give imprecise, unsafe limiting of carriage travel.

**Solution:** A locking pawl on the ratchet is switched on and off by the carriage jump buttons, and raised rails on the frame stop the carriage at the column tab.
