**How it works (reading):** Table top parts are raised and lowered independently by gears that can be slid sideways so they engage racks on one side, the other side, or both.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter table top part cannot be raised or lowered independently of its neighbour when the parts are coupled by a fixed rack-and-gear linkage.

**Solution:** Table top parts are raised and lowered independently by gears that can be slid sideways so they engage racks on one side, the other side, or both.
