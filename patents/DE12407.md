## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (die set and type printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** A table-mounted press in which a pedal-lowered lever drives a punch into a wood, paper pulp or lead plate; fonts (punch blocks) are set in a ring and selected by rotation and racks.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Matrices (moulds) for printing were made by hand rather than by a machine.

**Solution:** A table-mounted press in which a pedal-lowered lever drives a punch into a wood, paper pulp or lead plate; fonts (punch blocks) are set in a ring and selected by rotation and racks.
