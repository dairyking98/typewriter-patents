## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for bodily moving print heads or carriages parallel to the paper surface for serial printing movements superimposed to character- or line-spacing movements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in the mechanism for giving travel to the platencarriage of type-writing machines, its principal object being to provide a device which shall act with equal force on the platen-carriage whatever may be the position of the latter in the machine-frame.
