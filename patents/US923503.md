## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to retarding contrivances for typewriting machines, and has for its main object to provide improved.

**How it works (reading):** A retarding contrivance acting on the escapement wheel and pinion, carried on a bracket, slows the carriage drive.
