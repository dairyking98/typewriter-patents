## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines of the platen-shifting class, wherein each type-bar is provided with a plurality of types;

**How it works (reading):** A transversely shifting rack-bearing truck carries a hinged floating platen frame, so the carriage rocks for shift while the letter-spacing rack stays engaged with the escapement.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen-shift typewriters with double-character typebars need a simpler shift where the whole carriage can move freely while the escapement still engages.

**Solution:** A transversely shifting rack-bearing truck carries a hinged floating platen frame, so the carriage rocks for shift while the letter-spacing rack stays engaged with the escapement.

**How it works:** The carriage is a U-bar truck holding the letter-spacing rack, rolling on flanged wheels along a cylindrical shift rail. A rectangular platen frame is hinged to the truck and can swing up to show the writing. Pressing the shift key rocks the shift-rail frame, moving the carriage rearward so upper-case types strike. A spring on the truck propels the whole carriage letter by letter through the escapement, and a switching wedge locks the shift key down.
