## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paragraph indentation and carriage arrest at points other than the margin need to be automatic, not dependent on the operator.

**Solution:** A member slidable on a notched rod arrests the carriage return at a chosen point; the period key actuates the arrest, and other keys retract it.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
