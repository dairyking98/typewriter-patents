## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writers, and has for its especial object to provide improved tibulat-ing mechanism therefor.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulating mechanism with digit-keys is added to a typewriter so the carriage moves to set columns for lists and bills.
