## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (letter counter drive for typewriters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Word counters

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for driving a letter counter on typewriters and similar machines.

**How it works (reading):** A thin steel wire stretched across the path of the type carriers is pulled by the carriers during the stop movement and drives the counter through a resilient member made from the wire itself.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keystroke counter drives were bulky, hard to fit to existing machines and added mass to the key stroke.

**Solution:** A thin steel wire stretched across the path of the type carriers is pulled by the carriers during the stop movement and drives the counter through a resilient member made from the wire itself.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** steel wire c; shift lever e; tension spring; counter
