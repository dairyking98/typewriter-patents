## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter plate on an arm that swings horizontally on a bolt and slides in a guide, with rotatable clamps so the plate can be set in use or hung below the keyboard console.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters placed on a keyboard instrument are awkward to set up and clear away, and they spoil the instrument's look and tone.

**Solution:** A typewriter plate on an arm that swings horizontally on a bolt and slides in a guide, with rotatable clamps so the plate can be set in use or hung below the keyboard console.
