## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Syllable, line, or like type selection

**How it works (reading):** A detachable coupling in the drive for the font carrier is switched in with a dog clutch; a foot lever moves the shaft to release the coupling between gear and crank, so the movements can be stopped without a line feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In line typewriters the automatic line switching is tied into the drive, so the carrier keeps moving and a line break happens even when the operator wants printing without a line break.

**Solution:** A detachable coupling in the drive for the font carrier is switched in with a dog clutch; a foot lever moves the shaft to release the coupling between gear and crank, so the movements can be stopped without a line feed.
