## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The plunger for each die needs to be thrust without the screw continuing to reciprocate until a catch releases it.

**Solution:** A nut on a continuously rotating screw carries the plunger; a spring-loaded stop engages pins on the nut to halt its rotation until a hammer frees the catch and the stop is thrust forward.
