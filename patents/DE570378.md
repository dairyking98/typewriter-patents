## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** After each tab stop an auxiliary switching rack, lowered by a corner cam, engages the normal switching pinion so the carriage can be reset step by step from the tab position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After an automatic tabulator move, a partly toothed carriage rack cannot be stepped back by the downshift key because the teeth are missing.

**Solution:** After each tab stop an auxiliary switching rack, lowered by a corner cam, engages the normal switching pinion so the carriage can be reset step by step from the tab position.
