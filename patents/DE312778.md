## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** A bracket pivots in clamp bearings on the rear struts of the typewriter, carrying a two-part foldable base plate that can be set at any height or tilt and folded away.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing document holders do not let the copy sit at any distance, angle or lighting, and do not fold away.

**Solution:** A bracket pivots in clamp bearings on the rear struts of the typewriter, carrying a two-part foldable base plate that can be set at any height or tilt and folded away.
