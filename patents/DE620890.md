## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (power touch probe for die setting machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for assembling matrices and space bands

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Power-driven touch probe for die setting and line casting machines in one Tastiverkteile receiving housing, with eccentrics mounted in yokes by the Keystroke with revolving rollers in connection and of these in rotation be moved.

**How it works (reading):** Key board is hinged on a front-wall axis so it swings down to expose the whole drive for cleaning and part replacement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboard units of matrix-setting machines are hard to maintain because the drive is inaccessible under the keys.

**Solution:** Key board is hinged on a front-wall axis so it swings down to expose the whole drive for cleaning and part replacement.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
