## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage feed must give a fixed throw to the feed dogs whatever the key dip, with a friction link between key action and feed element.

**Solution:** A friction connection between a link on the key-driven crank and the dog carrier, limited by an adjustable stop, so the dog carrier gets a set travel and the key can overtravel.

**How it works:** The universal bar moves a crank via a sleeve on the rock-shaft. A link pivoted on the crank has a pin bearing on a friction segment on the movable feed element (dog carrier), which carries feed dogs working on the feed rack or wheel. Both are held by springs. The dog carrier moves with the link until an adjustable stop hits the frame; further key travel makes the pin slide forward on the friction surface against its spring. Dip of short and long keys therefore gives the same feed throw.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** universal bar; crank; link; friction surface; dog carrier; feed dog; feed rack; set-screw; spring

**Improves on:**

- [US480306](../patents/US480306.md): simpler friction connection between feed mechanism and type mechanism
