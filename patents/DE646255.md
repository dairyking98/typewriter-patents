## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Electric calculating machine The invention relates to an electric calculator Calculating machine for the multiplication of multi-digit factors, in which the single products of the digits r to 9 embodied by contact groups arranged on shift drums are sent by the current surges to drive a counter. Known machines of this type are with a single shift drum provided, which carries contact groups, each of which in contact rows the sub-products the multiplication of one of the digits z to 9 by all digits r to 9. When multiplying by a multi-digit multiplier, it is necessary to one after the other a different row of contacts of the contact groups of the shift drum to be selected for each digit of the multiplier. This is done in a known device by a relative lateral displacement between the shift drum and the associated Contact brushes accomplished. This lateral movement requires a certain Time wasted doing the math. Furthermore, through the constant lateral displacement caused a great deal of wear and tear, and some small inaccuracy in the shift gives incorrect results.
