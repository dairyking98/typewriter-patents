## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (electric multiplying calculator) · **Subject (patent classes):** dividing; dividing by partial product forming (with electric multiplication table)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an electric calculator Calculating machine for the multiplication of multi-digit factors, in which the single products of the digits r to 9 embodied by contact groups arranged on shift drums are sent by the current surges to drive a counter.

**How it works (reading):** Nine shift drums each carry rows of contacts for one digit; a switching mechanism closes the circuits selected by one factor so that the other factor's digits set which contact row feeds the counter, with no lateral movement of the brushes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multiplying multi-digit factors on known machines needs brushes shifted laterally or a complete drum and brush set per multiplier digit, which wastes time, wears parts and is large and complicated.

**Solution:** Nine shift drums each carry rows of contacts for one digit; a switching mechanism closes the circuits selected by one factor so that the other factor's digits set which contact row feeds the counter, with no lateral movement of the brushes.
