## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing machine for address plates) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to this known to a special one Type of embossing machines, namely those for embossing multi-line address printing plates, which can be displaced back and forth with one in the line direction of the printing plates Carriage for a plate holder is provided, which in turn is in the carriage is displaceable transversely to the line direction.

**How it works (reading):** A holder for the embossing template is mounted on the sliding carriage beside a guide cheek, and a pointer on the fixed frame shows which plate position is at the embossing point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** On embossing machines for multi-line address plates, two scales make it hard to tell which part of the plate is at the embossing position.

**Solution:** A holder for the embossing template is mounted on the sliding carriage beside a guide cheek, and a pointer on the fixed frame shows which plate position is at the embossing point.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Pointer over a chart](../features/sel-pointer-chart.md), [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md)
