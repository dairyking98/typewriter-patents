## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**How it works (reading):** Type-bars each carrying more than one character are pivoted in front of the platen and incline away from it; horizontal key-levers at the rear reach under the platen to drive them, with escapement and ribbon-feeding mechanism arranged beneath the segment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a visible-writing machine with inclined multi-character type-bars, the escapement and ribbon-feed arrangement needs to be constructed for accurate operation.

**Solution:** Type-bars each carrying more than one character are pivoted in front of the platen and incline away from it; horizontal key-levers at the rear reach under the platen to drive them, with escapement and ribbon-feeding mechanism arranged beneath the segment.
