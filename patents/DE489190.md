## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** A sheet holder plate with a longitudinal slot in which a sheet-holding bracket slides, so single sheets can be held at any height and the bracket can be pushed down out of the way when a shorthand pad is clamped.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier template holder plates needed separate side holders for single sheets, and the sheet clip obstructs a shorthand pad clamped on the same plate.

**Solution:** A sheet holder plate with a longitudinal slot in which a sheet-holding bracket slides, so single sheets can be held at any height and the bracket can be pushed down out of the way when a shorthand pad is clamped.

**How it works:** The bracket slides in the slot; pushing it down clears the top clamp for a pad, so the pad and sheets do not interfere.
