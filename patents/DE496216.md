## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for the noiseless return of the Papienv agens from typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A friction member swings the pawl out of the escape wheel directly by friction, so the sliding pawl contact that causes noise is avoided.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage-return pawls slide over ratchet teeth during the working movement, which makes noise.

**Solution:** A friction member swings the pawl out of the escape wheel directly by friction, so the sliding pawl contact that causes noise is avoided.
