## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (writing calculating machine stop rail) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**How it works (reading):** A switching movement from the counter passing zero makes the stop rail jump the carriage to the credit or debit column automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Totals in writing calculating machines had to be sorted into credit or debit columns by hand.

**Solution:** A switching movement from the counter passing zero makes the stop rail jump the carriage to the credit or debit column automatically.
