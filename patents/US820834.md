## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Smith ribbon feed on the Elliott-Fisher needs a hand reversal when the ribbon runs out in one direction.

**Solution:** A locked shift-bar is automatically unlocked and shifted by the next key stroke once the ribbon has fully wound one way, reversing the feed.

**How it works:** Ribbon-controlled means act when the feed finishes in one direction. The shift-bar is locked by a dog. The following key depression, through connections to the key mechanism, unlocks and shifts the bar, reversing the feed, and it locks again in its new position. Two alternating groups of mechanism handle the two directions. Fitted to a flat-platen machine whose carriage carries the typebars and an escapement wheel.

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** shift bar; locking dog; unlocking arm; escapement wheel; escapement dog; rock shaft; ribbon spool
