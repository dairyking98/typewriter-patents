## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon use is confined to a narrow strip; the companion patent US477236 advances the ribbon lengthwise only once per revolution.

**Solution:** Orbiting spool supports plus gearing that turns a spool on every orbit so the print point traces a continuous overlapping spiral over the whole ribbon.

**How it works:** Sheet-metal spool supports with integral ribbon guides are fixed to a curved connecting rod carried by cranks of equal throw, so spools orbit bodily parallel to the ribbon plane. Bevel gears on the spool shafts mesh with pinions on the crank pins, rotating the spool and moving the ribbon lengthwise on every orbit. An elliptical ratchet wheel driven by a pawl on a rock-shaft linked to the key levers speeds the drive near the ribbon margins to even out wear. Sliding the spool shafts endwise (held by grooves and springs) engages one spool or the other, reversing the ribbon direction.
