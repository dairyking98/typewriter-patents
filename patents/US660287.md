## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of our present invention is to provide a construction of type whereby changes in the type may be made without loss of alinement and without necessitating the employment of especial skill and tools for such purpose.

**How it works (reading):** The type is made in two parts: a base permanently fixed to the type-bar and a removable face piece carrying the character, so faces can be replaced without losing alignment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Single-piece type on a type-bar must be set and aligned by skilled hands or special tools, and a worn or battered character means re-alignment of the whole type system.

**Solution:** The type is made in two parts: a base permanently fixed to the type-bar and a removable face piece carrying the character, so faces can be replaced without losing alignment.
