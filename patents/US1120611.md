## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Billing needs separate sheets; the daily total is hard to obtain from individual bills.

**Solution:** A third tally strip with a writing face and a carbon face is fed through with the bills, so charges are entered and summed in one pass.

**How it works:** Bill and tally strips run superposed through the feed; carbon transfers the entries onto the original and tally sheets.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
