## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**How it works (reading):** The buffer ring sits on a vulcanised rubber liner held in a mounting ring fixed to the type lever, so it has no metal contact with the segment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever stops are noisy, and metal buffer rings transmit the impact sound into the segment.

**Solution:** The buffer ring sits on a vulcanised rubber liner held in a mounting ring fixed to the type lever, so it has no metal contact with the segment.
