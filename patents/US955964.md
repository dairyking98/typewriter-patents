## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A gage that normally stays out of effective position is moved into position by the operator's grip on the adjustable stop so it contacts the center stop during setting, then returns to normal when the grip is released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting a margin stop against the center stop is awkward, and the stop surface can be mis-set when the stop is adjusted.

**Solution:** A gage that normally stays out of effective position is moved into position by the operator's grip on the adjustable stop so it contacts the center stop during setting, then returns to normal when the grip is released.
