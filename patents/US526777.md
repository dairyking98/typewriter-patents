## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforating device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**How it works (reading):** A check perforator where spring plunger dies in a circular holder are turned by gears through a lever, and that lever also drives the feed and spacing mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Perforating devices are complicated and the feed and spacing need to be automatic.

**Solution:** A check perforator where spring plunger dies in a circular holder are turned by gears through a lever, and that lever also drives the feed and spacing mechanism.
