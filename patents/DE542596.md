## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper advance for each record group on tabulating machines has to be fixed to the length of the form section, whatever the number of lines printed.

**Solution:** An electromagnetically controlled clutch between the paper transport and the continuously running adder shaft is engaged and released by contact organs so that the paper advances one form length per group.

**How it works:** A paper-feed contact switch energises the magnet to engage the clutch; the paper transport contact de-energises it again after one form length.
