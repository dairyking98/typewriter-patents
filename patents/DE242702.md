## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (speaking machine combined with typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Speech recognition

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter key drives a shaft and disk with lugs that start and stop the speaking machine motor or roller shaft, so the dictation pauses while the typist writes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A dictation (speaking) machine runs continuously, so dictation in paragraphs cannot be paced by the typist at the typewriter.

**Solution:** A typewriter key drives a shaft and disk with lugs that start and stop the speaking machine motor or roller shaft, so the dictation pauses while the typist writes.

**How it works:** Pressing a key turns the index shaft; bevel gears turn a lugged disk that engages the coupling or contacts the motor circuit; a cam-groove and lever variant does the same.

**Features:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** lugged disk; bevel gears; clutch; cam-groove lever
