## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter noise and shock pass into the desk and the frame is hard to assemble around the transmission.

**Solution:** The frame is split into an upper part carrying the transmission and a base, separated by elastic sound-absorbing pads, so the base can be light and the upper part rests on its full perimeter.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter frame The invention relates to a typewriter frame and consists in the fact that the frame consists of one for receiving the machine transmission serving upper part consists, with the interposition of elastic, sound-absorbing Means detachable from a base forming the lower part of the machine frame will be carried. The base, in turn, is made of elastic, sound-absorbing means isolated from the substrate. This arrangement offers the possibility of during the Composition of the machine base and upper part to separate from each other so that only the upper part runs through the company. The base is therefore capable of installation the transmission parts in no way to hinder, and it can also be made from lighter Material, for example, made of wood, because it is separate from the upper part is produced and therefore not damaged in the assembly of the transmission can be. The elastic means that on the one hand between the base and the Upper part and on the other hand are arranged between the base and the base, offer the possibility of completely absorbing the shocks occurring during the operation of the machine and keep the noise to a minimum.…
