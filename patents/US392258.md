## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stenographic key machine is limited in speed and in the number of characters it can write.

**Solution:** Two parallel sets of marker keys, extra keys, and keys pressable in pairs, threes or fours with one finger.

**How it works:** A Bartholomew-type stenograph with two thumb keys and marking keys on each side, each carrying a marker that writes on a paper ribbon, in two parallel rows of impressions. Extra keys without markers operate the marking keys from the opposite side. Long keys on the right advance the ribbon one space and those on the left two. Finger-plates let a single finger depress one to four plates together. Only the opening is available.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Uses:** [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md)

**Parts:** thumb key; marking key; marker; finger-plate; paper ribbon; extra key

**Improves on:**
