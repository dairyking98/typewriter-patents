## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates on Typhebdl for typewriters with slot bearing of the type lever and aims ,, the type levers from the weakest possible material: to manufacture, whereby the stop is made easier and the writing speed is increased.

**How it works (reading):** The type lever is made thinner than the slot width, with grooves or spiral work bars formed at the bearing and type-block ends to give a fit in the slot and on the type block, leaving room for solder.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers need heavy material to hold the type block, which costs weight and writing speed, and the transition at the bearing end bends when the lever is straightened.

**Solution:** The type lever is made thinner than the slot width, with grooves or spiral work bars formed at the bearing and type-block ends to give a fit in the slot and on the type block, leaving room for solder.
