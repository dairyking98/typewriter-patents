**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with means for effecting line or character spacing in either direction

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inspecting, cleaning and repairing the lower mechanism of a visible front-strike machine is difficult.

**Solution:** The paper carriage and frame hinge upward, carrying the ribbon spools and drive barrel, to expose the platen and mechanism below.

**Features:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
