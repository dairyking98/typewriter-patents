## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage runs unsteadily at speed, giving wavy lines in the print.

**Solution:** Two parallel guide-rods and a sliding yoke with forked lugs bearing on the lower rod steady the carriage.

**How it works:** Two parallel rods, one above the other, span the machine on standards. A yoke slides on them and carries lugs with holes for the carriage hinge rod, grooves bearing on the lower rod, and screw holes for the escapement rack and a spring for the inner rack.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** guide rod; yoke; lug; standard; rack; carriage hinge rod
