## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**How it works (reading):** A multiple rack bar carrying several sets of adjustable stops on different sides, rotated by a turning head so the chosen set comes into operation with counter stops on the frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulator needs many work forms but changing stops for each form takes readjustment.

**Solution:** A multiple rack bar carrying several sets of adjustable stops on different sides, rotated by a turning head so the chosen set comes into operation with counter stops on the frame.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
