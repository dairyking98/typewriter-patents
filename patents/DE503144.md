## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** A stepped control body on the platen axis has wedge-shaped sliding surfaces so the one lever stroke moves the pawl axially and tangentially at once, giving smooth line feed with 1, 2 or 3 line steps selected by a hand wheel.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-feed devices move the pawl along the platen axis and tangentially in separate jerks, so the platen slips on the paper once the pressure springs weaken and the sheet is not taken along.

**Solution:** A stepped control body on the platen axis has wedge-shaped sliding surfaces so the one lever stroke moves the pawl axially and tangentially at once, giving smooth line feed with 1, 2 or 3 line steps selected by a hand wheel.

**How it works:** A shift lever with a switching tooth swings about two perpendicular pins, one across and one parallel to the platen axis. It slides on inclined faces of a multi-step body that sits on the platen axis, limiting the stroke to one, two or three teeth of the line ratchet. A turning sleeve with three openings and a spring pin locks the chosen step without loosening screws. A clutch lever pushed by a plunger button couples or frees the ratchet wheel and the platen for free turning; a return spring brings the lever back.
