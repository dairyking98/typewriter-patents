> Description Translated from German Accounting machine The invention relates to an accounting machine, especially the equipping of the same with an improved and effective device for inserting sheets to be labeled from the front of the machine here and their interaction with devices for introducing the leaves from the Back. The invention further relates to the formation of the device for Introduction of the business papers from the front using a non-removable platen in such a way that the introduction of the business papers all over again quickly and can be done conveniently. The device corresponding to the invention allows the advantageous Handling and making the entries satisfying numerous different Business types and systems. The invention also provides an effective power drive for the Introduction of one or more business sheets serving device from the front, before. According to the invention, the power drive is carried out by a on the frame arranged power source in any position of the carriage carrying the paper and under the local-er control of this car in a way that the economic Ensures changeability of use and the possibility of general application.
