## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The crank is hinged at the end of the roller shaft and held by a spring in the axial position when unused, then swung through about 90 degrees into use to turn the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen crank that is attached at the roller end is in the way of the carriage during normal typing, and existing spin devices cannot turn the platen freely and repeatedly.

**Solution:** The crank is hinged at the end of the roller shaft and held by a spring in the axial position when unused, then swung through about 90 degrees into use to turn the platen.
