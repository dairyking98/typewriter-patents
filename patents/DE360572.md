## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-space devices interfere with removal of a removable platen roller.

**Solution:** A line-space shift lever mounted below the roller axis, driven by a shaft with a stop pin, leaves the roller removable without disturbing the mechanism.

**How it works:** A handle turns a shaft whose pin swings a shift lever carrying a pawl that engages the ratchet wheel of the roller. An adjusting lever with a bent end limits the stroke of the pin and hence the line spacing. At rest the pawl is clear of the ratchet, so the roller, held by slotted bearings, can be lifted out. A leaf spring with a roller locks the platen against unwanted rotation, released by a lever.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** end plate; platen roller; shaft; handle; stop pin; shift lever; pawl; ratchet wheel; adjusting lever; leaf spring detent roller

**Improves on:**

- line-space devices on removable rollers: pawl is out of engagement at rest so roller removal needs no disassembly
