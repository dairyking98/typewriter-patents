## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**How it works (reading):** The platen rotation is transmitted through a gearbox with a clutch to the feed rollers, so both sheets advance together; a quick hand-turn gear with a ratchet-type coupling on the platen axis allows a single line shift.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On an accounting typewriter with a plug-in auxiliary sheet, the base sheet and the pre-inserted sheet must be advanced together, and the platen alone cannot match the next free lines of both.

**Solution:** The platen rotation is transmitted through a gearbox with a clutch to the feed rollers, so both sheets advance together; a quick hand-turn gear with a ratchet-type coupling on the platen axis allows a single line shift.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
