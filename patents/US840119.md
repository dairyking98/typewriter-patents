## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to guiding and cutting devices for single sheets or for the con-- tinuous sheet or ribbon of paper which may be taken from a roll and guided over the platen in a flat-platen type-writing machine.

**How it works (reading):** Adjustable guides attached to the platen or table, plus paper clamps and cutting or detaching devices, position the roll stock.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Continuous paper from a roll must be guided accurately across the platen at variable widths and positions.

**Solution:** Adjustable guides attached to the platen or table, plus paper clamps and cutting or detaching devices, position the roll stock.
