## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Spring housing for the paper trolley switching device. .The present The purpose of the invention is the spring housing of the carriage drive spring of typewriters to be designed in such a way that the drive spring is also used for the line interconnection Can be used to bring about the lift of the car. The invention achieves this in that the spring housing with two Finite gears equipped peripheral surfaces is equipped, which alternately brought into engagement with internal teeth rier drive pulley for the conveyor belt can be, either directly for driving the Vorschalthewegung or indirectly via a change gears arranged on the drive side for the manufacture of the wagon elevator. In both cases the movement of the drive pulley transferred by an endless belt to a second disc, which is connected to a toothed wheel is connected, which engages in the carriage rack and thereby the movement of the Wagens either in one or the other direction. By using only one drive pulley and only one belt compared to the known similar devices, a significantly simplified, Significantly more modest design achieved without thereby reducing reliability the device is impaired.…
