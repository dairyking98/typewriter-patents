## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known carriage advance and return devices use several drive pulleys and belts, which is complicated.

**Solution:** A spring housing with two toothed peripheries alternately engages an internal-toothed drive pulley, directly for advance or through change gears for carriage return, and one belt drives a disc that moves the carriage rack.

**How it works:** The spring housing is tensioned by the escapement pawl, and the single pulley and belt move the carriage in one direction or the other.

**Features:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
