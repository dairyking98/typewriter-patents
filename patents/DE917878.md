## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Large ticket printers with many card types have long, heavy transport runs to the print point.

**Solution:** Card selection and transport are combined, so a selected slot's card is pushed directly to a conveyor that brings it to the printer.
