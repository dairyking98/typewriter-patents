## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A paper web or roll mounted at the rear of the machine is led to the carriage line feed with the web wandering or displaced from its path.

**Solution:** A roll mounted with its axis at right angles to the carriage travel, led up over a guide bar and along the carriage to a diagonal guide, so the web runs in a controlled path to the line-feeding devices on the carriage.

**Features:**
