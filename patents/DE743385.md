## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (teletypewriter with special signals) · **Subject (patent classes):** Answer-back mechanisms or circuits

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a teletypewriter with devices for transmitting special signals.

**How it works (reading):** The special signal can only be sent if a further protective key is also operated, so one unintended press does not trigger it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accidental pressing of the special-signal key during a telex message breaks up the message.

**Solution:** The special signal can only be sent if a further protective key is also operated, so one unintended press does not trigger it.
