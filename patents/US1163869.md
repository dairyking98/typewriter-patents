## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**How it works (reading):** Each end of the platen carries a gear meshing with a pinion that drives a card-guiding roll, so both guide rolls are positively driven and clamp the card between them; rolls are on splined shafts for adjustment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil address cards are not positively driven in the line-space direction.

**Solution:** Each end of the platen carries a gear meshing with a pinion that drives a card-guiding roll, so both guide rolls are positively driven and clamp the card between them; rolls are on splined shafts for adjustment.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** positively driven rolls; splined shafts
