## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rigid machine mount hardens the touch and turns the typebar blow into a cutting blow on the ribbon, and magnifies noise.

**Solution:** A resilient spring base with a locking device and adjustable hooks mounts the typewriter so it can give under the blow.
