## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Alarms, indicators, or feed disabling devices responsive to ink ribbon breakage or exhaustion

**How it works (reading):** An attachment driven by carriage movement punches a physical mark (a hole) in the transcript sheet at each spacing, so the impression machine can register on it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction feed gives varying spacing, so a transcript sheet cannot be duplicated exactly on an impression machine.

**Solution:** An attachment driven by carriage movement punches a physical mark (a hole) in the transcript sheet at each spacing, so the impression machine can register on it.

**Features:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
