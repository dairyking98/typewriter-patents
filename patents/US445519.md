## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink and dirt fill the types so the letters blur, and hand cleaning is slow.

**Solution:** A brush or cleaner for the types is applied to the machine in one of two forms.

**How it works:** Brush cleaner mounted on the machine, in two forms.
