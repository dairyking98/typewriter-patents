**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (filler character printing, cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices for printing the point

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for cash registers, calculating and accounting machines for switching filler characters that are printed simultaneously with the amount on receipts come.

**How it works (reading):** Higher-digit type carriers carry special type forms that are brought into the print position by setting means to mark certain bookings, so the marks are hard to fake.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Filler characters printed before amounts can be forged by altering a printed 0 into a 6, 8 or 9.

**Solution:** Higher-digit type carriers carry special type forms that are brought into the print position by setting means to mark certain bookings, so the marks are hard to fake.
