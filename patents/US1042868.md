## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in tabulating mechanism 4for, typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Key-locking means acts on the type keys during a tabulating move, and releasing mechanism frees the keys from the lock at the end of each tabulating movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type keys must not operate during tabulating carriage movement.

**Solution:** Key-locking means acts on the type keys during a tabulating move, and releasing mechanism frees the keys from the lock at the end of each tabulating movement.
