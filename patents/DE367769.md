**How it works (reading):** The guide tabs on each type head are made of parts parallel to the lever shafts so the play is limited, with less play for the outermost type levers than for the middle one, giving more even spacing and straighter lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Play between guide tabs and guide slots lets the outer type levers swing more than the middle one, causing uneven letter spacing and poor line straightness.

**Solution:** The guide tabs on each type head are made of parts parallel to the lever shafts so the play is limited, with less play for the outermost type levers than for the middle one, giving more even spacing and straighter lines.
