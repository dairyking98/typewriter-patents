## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage slide guidance on circular-typebar machines is imprecise, and line feed needs separate operations.

**Solution:** Concave roller sets wrap a round guide rod inside a housing, and a single push button on the carriage end performs line feed and pawl release together.

**How it works:** The platen frame is hinged on a rear cylindrical rod and carries a letter-spacing rack on arms; a housing around a fixed round guide rod holds pairs of concave rollers on three or more sides so the slide rolls along the rod. A lever on the platen frame carries two pawls that mesh with the platen ratchet wheel, and a push button slides a pusher whose slot length gives a full or half (single or double) line feed via a folding stop. A locking pawl with wedge teeth lets the platen be turned by hand one way, and a release lifts the pawls to turn it freely. A paper guide bracket with scale folds up with the platen.

> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE CLASS 15: Printing. (New York State, V. St. Α.). The innovation relates to typewriters and in particular to the Slide guidance on type lever machines with type levers arranged in a circle. The purpose of the innovation is to improve the mechanism for guiding the slide on its tracks, which are installed above the top plate of the machine, using of concave rolls that wrap around a round guide rod, are arranged in pairs and on three or more sides to support the bar. These roles are located in a housing that surrounds the rod and is provided with bearings in which the frame of the printing roller is suspended. the The invention also relates to an improvement in the line feed mechanism, which by a Push button on the front of one end of the slide is actuated in such a way that it closes at the same time a switching pawl and a pawl for releasing and switching the print roller, as well as to determine the latter come into effect- The accompanying drawings illustrate the invention.
