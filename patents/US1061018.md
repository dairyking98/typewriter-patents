## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar heads are hard to align after assembly, and the type can be displaced during assembly and adjustment.

**Solution:** Divide the type-bar head by a deep slot into a guide piece and a separate end carrying the type, so the type end can be bent or twisted slightly to bring all type into alignment without disturbing the hanger.

**How it works:** The type-bar head is slotted into two parts: a guide piece that engages the guide, and an end on which the type are secured. After assembly, the type-carrying end is offset by a slight bend or twist to align the type, correcting small manufacturing errors. The bar is hung in a hanger on the segment and driven by a link and key lever.
