## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ruled paper, date-lines and prepared forms must be squared up by hand, because the platen is locked to the line-spacing ratchet and cannot be turned independently.

**Solution:** The platen is detached from its ratchet so it can turn independently in either direction, using a hollow core, a hub and a stud or pivot that lets the platen be set quickly.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
