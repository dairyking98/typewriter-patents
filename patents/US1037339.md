## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for controlling character selection

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to, and is applicable to any of the several forms of type-bar writing machines, but is especially adapted to that class of writing machines usually designated as front strike machines or machin es where the type-bars are vibrated from a horizontal to a vertical position to strike-- 'rearwardly upon the front side of a platen.

**How it works (reading):** An antifriction fulcrum for the key levers and a cushioned connecting link, with adjustable link length and spring tension.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bar impact is transmitted to the operator's finger and the key action is harsh.

**Solution:** An antifriction fulcrum for the key levers and a cushioned connecting link, with adjustable link length and spring tension.
