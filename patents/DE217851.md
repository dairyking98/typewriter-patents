## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The clerk must skip columns at will in tabular writing while seeing the written columns.

**Solution:** Each column stop has its own key and upright push rod that brings the chosen stop into the carriage path; loose connections keep the other keys from moving the stops.
