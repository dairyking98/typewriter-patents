The subject of the invention is a table writing device for typewriters, and preferably for those in which the types from the front against the paper roller hit, so the writing is always visible. According to the invention is the table writing device arranged so that the clerk can select a particular one of the various column stops at will ίο and bring to effect and at the same time with it can trigger the paper trolley from its switching device and bring it to rest again. Since the written columns are always visible on the paper, the writer can constantly adhere to it and easily determine whether one or more of the column stops should be skipped. According to the invention, a number of column stops are attached to the rear of the typewriter, the rod carrying them is expediently arranged on the machine frame. The formation of the column stops themselves does not matter. A corresponding stop is also attached to the paper trolley; instead, there could be a series of digits.
