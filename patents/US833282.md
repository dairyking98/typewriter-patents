## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key and typebar mechanisms of visible-writing typebar machines were hard to assemble, had a poor touch, and gave a weak blow for manifolding.

**Solution:** An intermediate balanced lever with roller bearings links the key-bar to a typebar whose curved tail lengthens its lever arm as it rises, giving an easy touch and a sharp, accelerating blow.

**How it works:** Key-bars, intermediate levers and typebars each pivot in slotted supporting plates, and a locking plate with fingers secures all pivots at once. Pressing a key-bar pulls down one end of the intermediate lever through a pair of rollers, against an adjustable spiral spring. The other end of the lever carries rollers that ride down the curved, downward-and-backward tail of the typebar, so the effective lever arm of the typebar grows as it ascends and the type block gathers momentum. The block strikes the platen and is aligned at the printing position by ball-bearing alining posts that are adjustable. A shoulder behind the typebar pivot trips a universal bar for ribbon and carriage movement.
