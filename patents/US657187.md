## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A card-holder is hard to attach and detach from the platen clamps, and existing versions leave projecting parts that get in the way when writing on cards or paper.

**Solution:** A card-holder that attaches to the platen end through a spring catch and a cam lever, with no projecting members left in the way.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
