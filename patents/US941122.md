## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to tabulating mechanism for typewriting machines and has for its main object to provide improved column selecting devices, whereby the traveling element or carriage of the typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating mechanism with column-selecting devices by which the traveling element is set to chosen columns.
