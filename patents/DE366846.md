## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing words and syllables letter by letter is slow.

**Solution:** Each key prints either a single letter or a three-letter syllable from the same key, and the carriage-feed gear is coupled alternately to a gear that steps one letter or three letters.

**How it works:** Radial typebars on a circular guide rod are snapped to the platen by toggle linkages. The carriage rack is driven by a gear that can be coupled by spring-loaded clutch discs to either a single-tooth step gear for single characters, kept engaged by a spring, or a gear stepping three letter widths for composite characters. The roller is also shifted in height to select the series of types.
