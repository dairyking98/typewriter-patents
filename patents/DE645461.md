## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (derailleur for cash registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to simplify a rear derailleur of this type to use it in to be able to accommodate as small a room as possible.

**How it works (reading):** A coupling member lifts out of zero by the set amount and is brought into effect by a lifting element on the same shaft as the adjusting member.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching mechanisms for registers need a large room for the coupling pinion frame.

**Solution:** A coupling member lifts out of zero by the set amount and is brought into effect by a lifting element on the same shaft as the adjusting member.
