## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the class of machines which are adapted to make matrices by impressing the several characters one by one into suitable impression-receiving material.

**How it works (reading):** A matrix-making machine with type-bars carrying longitudinally movable dies that are driven into clamped wood blocks, with a differential feed to the carriage and mechanism to justify the lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wood matrix blocks for printing had to be made one character at a time by hand, with justification difficult to manage.

**Solution:** A matrix-making machine with type-bars carrying longitudinally movable dies that are driven into clamped wood blocks, with a differential feed to the carriage and mechanism to justify the lines.
