**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that the end extended beyond the fastening screw of the spring is U-shaped is formed and in two bores of a bent tab of the ratchet carrier is guided, the end leg of the spring serving as a stop for the pawl.

**How it works (reading):** A wire loop spring with a U-shaped end guided in bores on the ratchet carrier acts as a resilient stop, which also serves as backup if the spring breaks.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Loose pawl on key switch gave impact noise and the spring stop could bend.

**Solution:** A wire loop spring with a U-shaped end guided in bores on the ratchet carrier acts as a resilient stop, which also serves as backup if the spring breaks.
