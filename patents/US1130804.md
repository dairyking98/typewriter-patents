## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a margin adjusting attachment for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment controlled by a key next to the keyboard that brings the margin stops or slides to any desired position relative to the carriage ends.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops or slides are hard to reach behind the carriage or obstruct the view when placed in front, making margin adjustment difficult.

**Solution:** An attachment controlled by a key next to the keyboard that brings the margin stops or slides to any desired position relative to the carriage ends.
