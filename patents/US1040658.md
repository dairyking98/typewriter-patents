## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar pivots wear and need accurate adjustment without clearance.

**Solution:** A loose ball, double cone or prism in the bracket recess, wedged by a screw so it seats in the conical pivot hole of the type bar, giving a zero-clearance adjustable pivot.
