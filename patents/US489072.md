## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type-writer of simple construction that works rapidly and accurately and can be made cheaply.

**Solution:** A geared annular type wheel on a tilting post is turned by a toothed segment on a printing lever swung over a lettered notched flange, raised by hand levers to pick one of three rows, and tilted against the roller to print.

**How it works:** The type wheel with three horizontal rows of letters and numerals sits on a post with a gear wheel, on a lever pivoted to the bed plate. The printing lever carries a toothed segment that meshes with the gear; it is swung over the bed plate, which has the alphabet and numerals marked, until a notch in a corrugated flange holds it over the wanted character, putting that letter opposite the roller. Hand levers raise a fork under the wheel so the lower rows come opposite a perforated guard plate. Depressing the printing lever then tilts the post back so the type strikes the paper on the roller, and a pawl driven by this motion feeds the carriage one rack tooth. A bell crank, hammer and bell sound at line end.
