## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shift keys are awkward to hold down while the fingers type, and the lock is inconvenient.

**Solution:** Two thumb-operated shift keys with concave faces below the space bar, plus a swinging lock that catches collars on the key plungers.

**How it works:** Two forward-pushed plungers with cupped faces sit under and in front of the space bar. Each plunger turns a crank on a transverse shaft, which links to a shift lever of a Munson machine. The cup lets the thumb pivot while the fingers press printing keys. A swinging lock plate with projections engages adjustable collars on the plungers to hold shift.

**Features:** [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** shift key; plunger; crank arm; transverse shaft; link; shift lever; plunger collar; swinging lock plate; set-screw

**Improves on:**

- US474350 Munson No. 2 shift levers: moves shift keys to thumb position and adds a lock
