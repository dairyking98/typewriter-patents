## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in card or card guides or holders for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A card guide that holds strips of cardboard or paper of different widths against the platen so the type prints on them.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Narrow card strips of varying widths cannot be guided on the machine.

**Solution:** A card guide that holds strips of cardboard or paper of different widths against the platen so the type prints on them.
