## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Two buttons linked by a linkage so one rises as the other is pressed, with rigid projections against firm stops, set the angle lever for three ribbon lift heights.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon lift needs a height setting that the earlier single-button version handled less well (addition to 347699).

**Solution:** Two buttons linked by a linkage so one rises as the other is pressed, with rigid projections against firm stops, set the angle lever for three ribbon lift heights.

**Features:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)

**Numbers:** three heights
