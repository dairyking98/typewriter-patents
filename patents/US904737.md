## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating mechanisms need independently depressible denomination keys that work with the stops, but the existing layout fans the key arms out and they are offset from their stops.

**Solution:** Denomination stops on arms are nested as bails, with key-bearing arms that diverge or fan out at each side of the stop sets to make room for the row of keys; crossbars tie some stop arms to companion arms, and the offset of the outer keys is compensated.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
