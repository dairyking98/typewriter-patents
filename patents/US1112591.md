## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Parts, details or accessories of beds; Stands, brackets or the like for supporting infusion accessories; Resilient type clasp

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to eraser holders, and the principal object of theinvention is the provision of a device of this character which can be attached to a type-writer for the purpose of holding an eraser where it will be conveniently within reach of the typist, the eraser being removed when it is to be'used and then replaced in the holder so that there is no danger of the eraser.

**How it works (reading):** A small spring holder attached beside the keyboard keeps the eraser within reach and returns it when not in use.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasers got lost or misplaced when not in use.

**Solution:** A small spring holder attached beside the keyboard keeps the eraser within reach and returns it when not in use.

**How it works:** A holder clamps to the machine next to the keyboard and grips the eraser.
