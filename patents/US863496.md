## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-part annular scales need special devices to adjust between their parts, which adds cost to existing machines.

**Solution:** A two-part annular scale is adjusted through the fractional line-spacing or platen-releasing mechanism, so the platen can rotate independently of line spacing; this allows billing attachments on existing machines.

**How it works:** Releasing the platen from its line-spacing mechanism lets the scale parts be set against each other by the same movement.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** annular scale; platen release from line spacing
