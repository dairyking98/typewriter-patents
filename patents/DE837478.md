## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type carrier, cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies arranged in stationary or sliding cases or frames or upon flexible strips, plates, bars or rods

**How it works (reading):** The guide means for the types are arranged in two or more zigzag rows, so types can sit closer together, and transport means act near the print point to manage the carrier.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type carriers for cash registers need a long stroke and wide spacing to hold letters and figures, which costs effort and speed.

**Solution:** The guide means for the types are arranged in two or more zigzag rows, so types can sit closer together, and transport means act near the print point to manage the carrier.
