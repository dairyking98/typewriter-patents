## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to combined paper depth gage and paper support, and is an improvement on my application No.

**How it works (reading):** The gage standard is bent from the same wire as the paper support, hinged to the platen frame so it swings out for use or lies on top when stowed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A separate paper depth gage and paper support duplicate parts and cost more to make.

**Solution:** The gage standard is bent from the same wire as the paper support, hinged to the platen frame so it swings out for use or lies on top when stowed.
