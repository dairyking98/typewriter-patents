## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Common letter pairs (th, er, in, e plus space) need two keystrokes each, slowing typing.

**Solution:** Add combination keys and typebars that print two characters at one touch, with a second escapement-control bar that feeds the carriage two spaces for them.

**How it works:** Single-letter keys press a cross-bar linked by a rock shaft and elbow lever to the carriage escapement for one space. Combination keys press a second parallel cross-bar whose linkage moves the escapement two or more spaces. The two bars have interleaved lugs and slots so a key of one kind passes into the slots of the other bar and does not operate it. The arrangement is shown on a Remington-type escapement lever with fixed and movable dogs.
