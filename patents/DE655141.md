## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**How it works (reading):** A gear train on the paper roll engages a fixed rack on the frame during writing carriage movement, driving the roll positively; a roller on the carriage with a rail engages the rack, and it is disengaged on the return stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic paper-roll advance either needs the user to engage a gear by hand, or runs a constant clutch that wears and makes noise, and the roll cannot be turned freely for alignment.

**Solution:** A gear train on the paper roll engages a fixed rack on the frame during writing carriage movement, driving the roll positively; a roller on the carriage with a rail engages the rack, and it is disengaged on the return stroke.
