## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing machines with two-colour or stencil ribbons need the vibrator's throw to be selectable by the operator.

**Solution:** The universal bar is given operator-selectable ranges of movement so the ribbon vibrator reaches the top zone, bottom zone, or stays clear for stencils.

**How it works:** Finger-piece depression rocks a transverse universal bar that drives both the escapement and the ribbon vibrator through levers. A cam plate or adjustment on the bar gives a small or large range of movement, bringing the near or far ribbon zone to the printing point, or too little for stencil work while still tripping the escapement. The patent also covers ribbon guides to the bobbins and ways to attach ribbon ends to bobbin tapes.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** universal bar; ribbon vibrator; cam plate; escapement lever; ribbon bobbin; bobbin tape; idler wheel; ribbon guide
