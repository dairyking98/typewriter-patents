## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In very rapid or erratic typing the escapement rack can move backward when relieved of carriage spring pressure, and earlier fixes either checked the dog rocker or needed a closely slotted escapement wheel.

**Solution:** Improved means to stop backward motion of the escapement rack when the carriage spring pressure is relieved, avoiding both the stop-strike fault and the close-fit slotted wheel.

**How it works:** On a Monarch machine, a carriage with feed rack meshes a feed pinion on a shaft; an escapement wheel with ratchet and pawl in a drum sits on that shaft. A dog rocker driven by a link and return spring carries front and rear dogs pivoted on a horizontal pin, the front normally engaging the wheel. The new arrangement prevents backward turning without the pawl entering wheel slots.
