## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter keys click and clatter, which makes them unusable for stenotype work in courts and churches.

**Solution:** Lengthen the key stems and pass them through stacked perforated layers of cloth or rubber with tight rubber washers between, so key sounds are muffled.

**How it works:** The key of a Sholes and Glidden or similar machine slides in the key-board perforation. Its stem is lengthened to pass through alternating layers of cloth, rubber and similar fabrics stretched over the key-board, perforated for the key's motion. Tightly fitting rubber washers on the stems sit between the layers and let the key move but block the escape of sound. The casing is also covered by sound-deadening layers.
