## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A transmitting keyboard for telegraphic or wireless printing must send one character per key and make a record of messages sent.

**Solution:** Each key-lever closes an electrical circuit and is held locked until a succeeding key releases it, in a portable case, with contacts at the lever ends.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Uses:** [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)
