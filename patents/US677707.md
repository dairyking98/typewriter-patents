## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the invention is to lock the type-bars when they return to normal position after actuation, so as to prevent the rebound thereof from the basket,-and thus avoid collision of the types;

**How it works (reading):** Typebars are locked when they return to normal position so they cannot rebound, and the connections between typebars and keys are improved in a front-strike machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars rebounded off the basket and collided, and the typebar-to-key connections were to be improved.

**Solution:** Typebars are locked when they return to normal position so they cannot rebound, and the connections between typebars and keys are improved in a front-strike machine.
