## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paint roller inking device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inking apparatus with ink applied by pads or rotary discs

**How it works (reading):** A narrow disc set obliquely on an axis presses against the rotating paint roller, and its lower part dips into the ink, so the contact line sweeps along the roller and controls the amount of ink transferred.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Felt paint rollers are inked unevenly by brushes, and soaked pads soil and are hard to clean, so the amount of ink transferred is not controlled.

**Solution:** A narrow disc set obliquely on an axis presses against the rotating paint roller, and its lower part dips into the ink, so the contact line sweeps along the roller and controls the amount of ink transferred.
