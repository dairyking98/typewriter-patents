## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cross counter calculation type control)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a control device for Setting of the calculation type of the cross counters of typewriters, in which the control plate is clamped against a box plate of the steep counter.

**How it works (reading):** A whorl on a bolt fixed to the control plate clamps the plate against the box plate and driver plate, and releases it for one-hand adjustment through notches and a spring-loaded head.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The control plate setting a transverse counter's calculation type needs two hands to adjust, or with one-hand devices the setting is unreliable.

**Solution:** A whorl on a bolt fixed to the control plate clamps the plate against the box plate and driver plate, and releases it for one-hand adjustment through notches and a spring-loaded head.

Devices of this type have already become known, but required use both hands to adjust and lock the control plate. Are known, furthermore Devices in which only one hand to adjust and lock the control plate is required; However, these devices have the disadvantage that the detection the adjusted control plate is unreliable. According to the invention, all these disadvantages are now avoided by the device clamping the control plate against the box plate on a bolt is arranged, which is firmly connected to the control plate.
