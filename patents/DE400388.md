## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (solenoid remote control gear) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection; Control of position or direction without using feedback; combined with other driving or adjusting arrangements, e.g. of gain control

**How it works (reading):** The armature is unpolarized and overlaps several coils, and a group selector pre-selects the next group of coils so that the armature jumps quickly and exactly into the selected position when the current is switched in.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electrically remote-controlled positioning is slow and imprecise because the armature is pulled between coils that are not well arranged.

**Solution:** The armature is unpolarized and overlaps several coils, and a group selector pre-selects the next group of coils so that the armature jumps quickly and exactly into the selected position when the current is switched in.

**Numbers:** seven coils; ten coils
