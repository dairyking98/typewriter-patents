## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attachments operated by the leg, e.g. the foot, the knee

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in pro viding suitable mechanism whereby the typewriter carriage may be automatically returned after completing its travel, and at the same time the roll caused to be revolved the distance of one line by means of the operators foot or knee, thereby obviating the delay and annoyance of having after completion of each line to raise the carriage-lift and draw the carriage by hand.
