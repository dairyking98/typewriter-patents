## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attachments operated by the leg, e.g. the foot, the knee

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in pro viding suitable mechanism whereby the typewriter carriage may be automatically returned after completing its travel, and at the same time the roll caused to be revolved the distance of one line by means of the operators foot or knee, thereby obviating the delay and annoyance of having after completion of each line to raise the carriage-lift and draw the carriage by hand.

**How it works (reading):** A foot- or knee-operated lever pulls a cord that lifts the carriage release and throws the carriage back, advancing the roll one line.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After each line the operator must raise the carriage lift and draw the carriage back by hand, causing delay.

**Solution:** A foot- or knee-operated lever pulls a cord that lifts the carriage release and throws the carriage back, advancing the roll one line.

**How it works:** A downwardly curved foot lever with teeth meshes with a second lever; pressing the foot piece turns the first lever partially and throws the second outward. That pulls a chain or cord running under a pulley on the carriage frame to a spring bar fixed to the hand lift, raising it and returning the carriage to the starting side while the roll turns one line. A spring returns the levers when released. A knee-piece variant uses a weighted arm to restore the levers.
