Such an additional adjustment of the type carrier is intended according to the invention for type sets that do not print information to be added at the same time, avoided will. The invention consists in that the star of the Zero is ten units away and an asterisk is printed when the adjuster of the associated switching mechanism is stopped in the zero position, but a zero for printing arrives when the adjusting member has carried out its greatest possible stroke.
