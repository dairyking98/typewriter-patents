## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (zero print device for registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices for printing the point

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that the star of the Zero is ten units away and an asterisk is printed when the adjuster of the associated switching mechanism is stopped in the zero position, but a zero for printing arrives when the adjusting member has carried out its greatest possible stroke.

**How it works (reading):** Star and zero are placed ten units apart on the wheel, and the zero stop pawl selects the printed character by the adjusting member's stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Zero-print and star-print need an extra adjustment of the type carrier when the entry is cleared.

**Solution:** Star and zero are placed ten units apart on the wheel, and the zero stop pawl selects the printed character by the adjusting member's stroke.
