## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Heavy springs and rigid parts are needed to press front pressure rollers in portable typewriters.

**Solution:** Forked lever arms engage double levers in the carriage side wall, and springs on the far side of the pivots hold the rollers swung out.
