## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (timestamp with type wheels)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a timestamp with type wheels, which are generated by the advancement of stop rails against stepped disks are set by a clockwork.

**How it works (reading):** The clockwork and its stepped disks are separated from the stamp; the stamp is pushed onto the movement only for each setting, leaving a light hand stamp that works like an ordinary hand stamp.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Time stamps with a clockwork in the same housing are hard to hold and use on large documents.

**Solution:** The clockwork and its stepped disks are separated from the stamp; the stamp is pushed onto the movement only for each setting, leaving a light hand stamp that works like an ordinary hand stamp.
