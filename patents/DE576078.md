## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on a type body for type lever typewriters with arranged on the type side, in vertical section wedge-shaped projections in connection with a recess in a teaching for the mechanical straightening of the type bodies when they are placed on the type lever are used, and consists in that the projections at the ends of the type body are arranged.

**How it works (reading):** Wedge-shaped alignment projections are placed at the ends of the type body, lower than the types, and the body is set on the lever against a gauge with matching recesses so every body sits at the same distance from the baseline.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Raised straightening marks lying between the types thicken the printed standard and cannot be ground off cleanly, while deep marks lose accuracy.

**Solution:** Wedge-shaped alignment projections are placed at the ends of the type body, lower than the types, and the body is set on the lever against a gauge with matching recesses so every body sits at the same distance from the baseline.
