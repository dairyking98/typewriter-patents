## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ambler wants a simple, durable, inexpensive and fast type writer.

**Solution:** A thin flexible rotating metal disk, electrotyped with the characters, is stopped by keys under a plunger that pushes the chosen character down onto the ribbon and paper.

**How it works:** A thin annular flexible disk printing plate carries the characters, formed by electrotyping, on a vertical shaft. A constantly driven ratchet sleeve turns the shaft through a pawl-like elbow lever on an arm. Keys stop the plate with the chosen character under the impression plunger. The impression lever drives the plunger, which has a rubber pad, to bend the plate down so the character strikes the ribbon, held between perforated rubber and metal guards. The same lever moves a wedge and a spring-held ratchet bar to space the carriage. Power comes from a treadle through belt and friction pawls.
