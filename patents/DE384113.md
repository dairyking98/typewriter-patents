> Description Translated from German Ribbon for typewriters and printing machines. The ones at typewriters Ribbons used in duplicating machines and similar devices from their packaging, which usually bears the company label, and clamped in the machine. However, once the ribbon has been removed from its packaging exempt, you can no longer tell which company it came from. The consequence of these are often unpleasant complaints, which often even go to the right supplier not meet. According to the invention, this disadvantage is Young by that it helped that in the raw tape characters or symbols of a different color than the basic color are arranged, which after soaking the ribbon in a complementary color to appear. For example, the characters are embroidered or woven with a thread of yellow color into the raw ribbon if the ribbon is colored blue or purple should be so that the characters appear green after the tape is soaked. In the drawing, a is the color ribbon carrying the blue primary color substance. The characters b are embroidered along the ribbon in yellow or woven in.
