## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A segment replaces the gear and pawl pendulum and is positioned by three discs so that a single type-key stroke advances the carriage by one, two or three teeth depending on the letter position in the word and the font type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Conventional line-spacing escapement advances the carriage a fixed step per letter, so spaces between words and barred or block-letter spacing must be made by extra key or space-bar action.

**Solution:** A segment replaces the gear and pawl pendulum and is positioned by three discs so that a single type-key stroke advances the carriage by one, two or three teeth depending on the letter position in the word and the font type.

**Features:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
