## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides stationary

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to ribbon guides, or supports for the inking or transfer ribbons used in typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon guide with cross bars spans the basket and is carried on the carriage rails so the ribbon is held at the print point and spread.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon wears at one line because it sits in a fixed track; it must also be held near the print point.

**Solution:** A ribbon guide with cross bars spans the basket and is carried on the carriage rails so the ribbon is held at the print point and spread.
