## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inking apparatus

**How it works (reading):** The paper is pre-treated with a chemical that ignites or browns from the heat of the type impact, so the writing appears without carbon or ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter impact on paper needs carbon paper and ribbon, which are awkward to use.

**Solution:** The paper is pre-treated with a chemical that ignites or browns from the heat of the type impact, so the writing appears without carbon or ribbon.
