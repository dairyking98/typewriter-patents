## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies arranged in stationary or sliding cases or frames or upon flexible strips, plates, bars or rods; arranged in a typeface box that slides on a plane

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a kanji printing machine in which types, large and small as desired, are set on both faces of slugs that are hooked to one another in chains at fixed intervals in a frame;

**How it works (reading):** Type slugs with a large type on one face and a small type on the other are hooked into spring-tensioned chains in a pivoted frame, so any one slug can be unhooked and swapped and the frame flipped for the other size.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Kanji machines need thousands of types, so it must be easy to gather and exchange any single type among them.

**Solution:** Type slugs with a large type on one face and a small type on the other are hooked into spring-tensioned chains in a pivoted frame, so any one slug can be unhooked and swapped and the frame flipped for the other size.

**How it works:** Each slug has a notch on one side and a bent hook on the other, so slugs hook into chains held in a frame, with spring-loaded anchors (T-bar in a channel frame with a spring) that let the chain stretch and shorten. The operator moves the frame until the wanted character is directly under a plunger, then presses a push button. A link turns a bell crank that lowers the plunger, which catches the type in a hollow on its underside, rubs it across an ink roller and prints it on paper on the cylinder. On release springs return the plunger and chain, and the cylinder frame, pulled by a spiral-spring pulley and checked by a ratchet bar and pawl, advances one character. A second button spaces only. Unhooking a spring hook lets the inner frame be flipped half over to bring the other-size faces up.
