## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, and ' more particularly to mechanism facilitating the production of manifold typewritten records of extensive statements or accounts, such for instance, as the shipping and'expense accounts of railroads.

**How it works (reading):** Means are provided to support and guide a long folded manifold strip, hold carbons between its plies, and feed the strip independently of the carbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Long manifold statements (for example railroad accounts) need a way to be typed on several plies with carbon, which a standard platen machine does not support.

**Solution:** Means are provided to support and guide a long folded manifold strip, hold carbons between its plies, and feed the strip independently of the carbon.

**How it works:** The strip is fed over the platen with carbon sheets carried between its layers by separate holders, so each ply receives an impression.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** manifold strip guide; carbon holders; independent strip feed
