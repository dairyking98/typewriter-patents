## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (metal strip drive belt) · **Subject (patent classes):** V-belts, i.e. belts of tapered cross-section consisting of several parts in the form of links

**How it works (reading):** A single steel strip of tabs carries trapezoidal friction bodies on hinge pins that protrude on both sides, letting each body rotate a limited amount and follow sideways pulley shift.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Metal V-belts with paired bands cannot tilt the friction bodies, so sideways pulley misalignment causes trouble.

**Solution:** A single steel strip of tabs carries trapezoidal friction bodies on hinge pins that protrude on both sides, letting each body rotate a limited amount and follow sideways pulley shift.
