## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in a device to be used for cleaning type on that class of type-writing machines which have the type arranged in a circular or what is known to the trade as basket form;

**How it works (reading):** A brush is mounted on the ribbon shield so the typebars strike it off-centre, and the bristles pick out gum and dirt from the type faces. It can be placed or removed without moving the ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type cleaning on basket-form machines needs a simple, inexpensive device that does not disturb the ribbon.

**Solution:** A brush is mounted on the ribbon shield so the typebars strike it off-centre, and the bristles pick out gum and dirt from the type faces. It can be placed or removed without moving the ribbon.
