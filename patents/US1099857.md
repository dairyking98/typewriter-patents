## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists need a place to hold carbon paper and finished work within reach, which can be put away so the typewriter cover fits.

**Solution:** Wire rack frames hinge on the typewriter base board in notches, and can be swung up or lifted off for the cover.

**How it works:** Each rack is a wire frame whose upper and lower side bars bend into inward rectangular loops so paper does not fall through. The end bars extend downward and are bent into stop lugs and pintles that drop into vertical notches in the base board edge, then into recesses with inclined walls and bearing grooves. The racks swing up to let the cover be placed, or are disengaged. Clips and variants of the attaching members are shown.
