## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The tab key levers all pivot on one common axis, giving short levers and unfavourable transmission.

**Solution:** Two groups of four tab keys are spread toward the frame edges on separate pivot points, with longer levers and better leverage.

**How it works:** Each group pivots on its own bearing point, so the key levers are longer and give smoother stop.
