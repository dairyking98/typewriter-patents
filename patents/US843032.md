## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shift keys sit at the keyboard corners and need a finger removed from the home keys.

**Solution:** A clip-on bar across the front of the machine that presses both shift keys and is operated by the wrist.

**How it works:** An actuating bar lies along the front frame-bar. Arms at its ends hook under the frame-bar and clip onto the shift-key bars with upturned hooks. Pressing the bar down depresses the shift keys and changes case. It detaches by swinging up.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** actuating bar; arm; hook; shift key; front frame-bar; holding screw
