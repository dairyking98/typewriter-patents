## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Condensed-record billing work needed a simple billing mechanism that could be fitted to existing typewriters.

**Solution:** Two toothed indicator disks at the platen end, one fixed to the carriage with numbered indices and one turning with the platen, register at each line space to set line positions for billing.

**How it works:** On a Monarch machine, the left platen head has 33 crown teeth forming the line-space ratchet with pawl and roller detent. A fixed annular carrier around the right platen shaft has 33 interdental spaces and numerals 0 to 33. A second toothed disk turns with the platen; its teeth face the fixed one and their spaces register at each line-space position. The remainder of the description is cut off.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** platen; line-space ratchet; line-space pawl; roller detent; fixed indicator carrier; rotating indicator carrier; finger wheel

**Numbers:** 33 teeth; index numerals 0 to 33
