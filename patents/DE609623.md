## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A type cylinder rotated left or right from a middle position should be driven by one and the same drive member with a constant stroke that keeps moving until the print is made, which earlier spring-triggered or armature-and-stop drives did not do positively.

**Solution:** Drive the cylinder rack through a toggle (knee) joint whose two free ends are stopped by key-set stops, so the joint shifts sideways and the rotation ends at the dead centre when the joint straightens.

**How it works:** Two links joined by a hinge pin form a knee joint, whose free ends are carried by guide members on rails. A spring holds the joint bent; the drive member, acting on the pin through a roller in a U-rail, straightens it every time by the same amount. Key-set stops block one guide member on its path, so the joint is displaced toward the free side. A rod on the pin moves the rack that turns the cylinder gear, so the cylinder turns left or right to the chosen position. Rotation ceases when the joint reaches dead centre, and the remaining drive travel, where the sideways motion is near zero, is used for the print stroke.
