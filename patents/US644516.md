## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to that class of type-writing machines in which the platen has -a movement at right angles to its axis, so that it may occupy two, three, or more positions to receive the impact of either two, three, or more characters upon the typebar;

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon mechanism that advances the ribbon over the printing point on key depression in any platen position and withdraws it afterwards.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a typebar machine whose platen is shifted obliquely to bring one of two or three characters per typebar to the print point, the ribbon must still be brought over the print point for each position and then cleared so the writing is visible.

**Solution:** A ribbon mechanism that advances the ribbon over the printing point on key depression in any platen position and withdraws it afterwards.

**How it works:** The carriage sits at about 45 degrees and carries a platen frame hung on four parallel links. Two shift keys with different stroke stops rock a shaft and cross-bar that push the platen frame up and back along its oblique path to one of three positions, each facing a different character on the typebar. Depressing a key lever swings the typebar and operates the ribbon mechanism, which draws the ribbon from one bobbin to the other by pawls and brings it to the print point, then pulls it away to expose the printing. The text is cut off before the ribbon linkage is fully described.
