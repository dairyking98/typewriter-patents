## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper-guides for type-writing machines, andparticularly to those guides which are employed to press the marginal portion of the paper against the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A margin guide with an extension slidable lengthwise on a carrying section fixed to the carriage bracket, adjusting to paper width along the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin paper guides must adjust to paper width and not block the view of printed matter.

**Solution:** A margin guide with an extension slidable lengthwise on a carrying section fixed to the carriage bracket, adjusting to paper width along the platen.
