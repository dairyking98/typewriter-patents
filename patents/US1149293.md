## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar pivots need a simple, adjustable construction where the pivot wire can be removed from the rear or slid out either way without removing all typebars.

**Solution:** The pivot wire sits in an undercut groove in the typebar segment and is clamped by an abutment screw at one end and a wedging screw at the other.

**How it works:** A typebar segment has radial slots and a curved undercut groove through them in its rear face. A pivot wire with a blunt end and a beveled end lies in the groove. An abutment screw meets the blunt end and a conical wedge screw meets the beveled end, forcing the wire into the undercut. Backing out the screws lets the wire slide sideways to free only a few bars, or be lifted out rearwardly to free all.

**Parts:** typebar segment; undercut groove; pivot wire; abutment screw; wedging screw; typebar
