## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means to release the platen from the line-space wheel in typewriting machines, whereby the platen may -be turned independently of the line-space mount that is fast on the axle, and cause them to exert a powerful pressure against the loose clutch member, to cause it to grip the fast clutch member.

**How it works (reading):** A conical friction clutch between the line-space wheel and platen, with levers exerting pressure on the loose clutch member and a dwell on the cam sleeve locking the clutch in operative position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be released from the line-space wheel so the platen can be turned independently.

**Solution:** A conical friction clutch between the line-space wheel and platen, with levers exerting pressure on the loose clutch member and a dwell on the cam sleeve locking the clutch in operative position.
