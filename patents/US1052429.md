## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means to release the platen from the line-space wheel in typewriting machines, whereby the platen may -be turned independently of the line-space mount that is fast on the axle, and cause them to exert a powerful pressure against the loose clutch member, to cause it to grip the fast clutch member.
