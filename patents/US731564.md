## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and has special reference to an illurnnating attachment designed to illuminate the work-sheet for the purpose of facilitating i the inspection of the work.

**How it works (reading):** A small electric lamp on the frame, with circuit closers that light the work continuously or intermittently.

**Solution:** A small electric lamp on the frame, with circuit closers that light the work continuously or intermittently.
