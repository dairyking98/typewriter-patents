## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Envelopes, cards and stiff paper need guiding against the platen and holding in close contact so the work can be written close to the edges.

**Solution:** A combined card guide and holder with adjustable pressure, plus a printing point indicator and line guide on the scale bar, guides and holds the work against the platen.

**How it works:** The guide finger bears on the sheet with a regulated pressure and works with the scale and point indicator.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** card guide holder; adjustable pressure; printing point indicator
