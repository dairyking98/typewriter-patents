## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates particularly to typewriters in which the type-bars each bear two types and are mounted upon a horizontal reciprocable support, by the reciprocation of which said type-bars are so shifted as to alternately bring one and then the other of said types to the printing-point, when the typebar is swung to the center of the circle in which the type-bars are arranged.

**How it works (reading):** Support the typebar disc on rocking links for parallel motion and lock it automatically in its normal position, releasing it by hand before shifting.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The disc carrying the two-type typebars must be shifted for case but may drift from its normal position during typing.

**Solution:** Support the typebar disc on rocking links for parallel motion and lock it automatically in its normal position, releasing it by hand before shifting.

**How it works:** The typebar disc sits on rockers whose rounded lower ends rest in channels on brackets under the top plate, so the disc reciprocates front to rear in a horizontal parallel motion with little friction. An automatic lock holds the disc in its normal position until manually unlocked for the shift. A rock-shaft and key-lever shift the disc; the specification also touches on platen-carriage ways and track rollers.
