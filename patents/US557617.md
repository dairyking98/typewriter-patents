## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The disc carrying the two-type typebars must be shifted for case but may drift from its normal position during typing.

**Solution:** Support the typebar disc on rocking links for parallel motion and lock it automatically in its normal position, releasing it by hand before shifting.

**How it works:** The typebar disc sits on rockers whose rounded lower ends rest in channels on brackets under the top plate, so the disc reciprocates front to rear in a horizontal parallel motion with little friction. An automatic lock holds the disc in its normal position until manually unlocked for the shift. A rock-shaft and key-lever shift the disc; the specification also touches on platen-carriage ways and track rollers.

**Features:** [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** typebar disc; rocker; bracket; top plate; rock-shaft; key-lever; carriage track roller

**Improves on:**

- Sholes application of 17 Oct 1892 (Serial 449,099): adds rocker support and automatic locking of the typebar disc
