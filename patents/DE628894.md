## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Dust protection plates were fixed by machine feet, which must be unscrewed for cleaning, or by special threaded holes in the frame, which some machines lack.

**Solution:** The dust plate has open-slot lugs that hook onto retaining rails fixed under the machine's own feet, with padding plates interposed, so it can be lifted off without unscrewing anything.

**How it works:** Slotted lugs on the plate engage the rails, and the machine rests on its padded feet with the plate between.
