## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates generally to the ribbon mechanisms of type writing machines, particularly those of the front-strike class;

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon pull rotates a loosely mounted spool cup slightly, which engages a switching lever to the carriage spring-drum prime mover so the feed reverses with only light ribbon tension.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon winding and reversing on front-strike Underwood machines needs heavy ribbon tension, is noisy, and wears holes before reversing.

**Solution:** Ribbon pull rotates a loosely mounted spool cup slightly, which engages a switching lever to the carriage spring-drum prime mover so the feed reverses with only light ribbon tension.

**How it works:** Spools sit in loose cups in front of the platen. When a spool empties, ribbon tension turns its cup, letting a prime mover linked to the carriage spring-barrel throw the switching lever. A transverse shaft driven from the spring barrel turns the upright spool shafts through roller clutches, which are silent. A crank on the shaft winds the ribbon by hand, and spools lift out for replacement.
