## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Details of, or accessories for, typewriters or selective printing mechanisms not otherwise provided for

**In the patent’s own words:** An object of this invention is to provide simple and practical means to enable the operator to economize time and labor by printinga date or the like at one stroke.

**How it works (reading):** A type carrier swung down on a bracket above the platen, with an impression hammer that a latch holds until enough pressure builds, then releases to strike the type carrier sharply.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing a date or several carbon copies at one stroke needs a clear impression, but the hard rubber platen gives a weak one.

**Solution:** A type carrier swung down on a bracket above the platen, with an impression hammer that a latch holds until enough pressure builds, then releases to strike the type carrier sharply.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
