## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Details of, or accessories for, typewriters or selective printing mechanisms not otherwise provided for

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in attachments facilitating the placing of new ribbons on type-writers, being designed simply and only for that purpose and fitted to the machine without the use of screws.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-steel-band-mounted rod at right angles to a bar, attached to the frame without screws, on which the new ribbon is placed and which can be moved back out of the way when not in use.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Loading a new ribbon means holding its spool or unwound ribbon in place with a pencil or other improvised tool.

**Solution:** A spring-steel-band-mounted rod at right angles to a bar, attached to the frame without screws, on which the new ribbon is placed and which can be moved back out of the way when not in use.
