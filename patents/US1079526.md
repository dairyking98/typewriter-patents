## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need heavy finger force, and unselected typebars may move.

**Solution:** Electromagnets finish the typebar stroke and hold the unselected bars at rest.

**How it works:** Pressing a key turns a rod and cranks that raise the selected typebar part way, and shift a plate that closes a circuit to a holding magnet for the other bars. A ratchet wheel advances by one tooth and closes a second circuit through a circular magnet that draws the selected typebar the rest of the way to the platen as a pressure stroke. Gravity and springs return the bar.
