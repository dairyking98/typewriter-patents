## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabulating meshanisms oi typewriting and other machines, in which the carriage may be arrested at any desired denomination point with reference to 011s or more column stops.

**How it works (reading):** A row of denomination keys on lever arms releases the carriage, and a slide stop on the carriage with graduated positions limits how far it runs past the selected denomination stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing tabulators do not let the carriage stop at any denomination position in a decimal column, and the fixed stops are not practical on front-strike machines.

**Solution:** A row of denomination keys on lever arms releases the carriage, and a slide stop on the carriage with graduated positions limits how far it runs past the selected denomination stop.
