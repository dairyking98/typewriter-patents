## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to columnstop and carriage-moving mechanism for typewriting machines in general, and more.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-driven carriage advances quickly from any position and is arrested at one of a set of distances from fixed points; the stop points can be adjusted, and a denomination indicator keeps each denomination in its own vertical line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Numbers in a column are not aligned by denomination, and the carriage cannot be moved quickly from any position to a set stop.

**Solution:** A spring-driven carriage advances quickly from any position and is arrested at one of a set of distances from fixed points; the stop points can be adjusted, and a denomination indicator keeps each denomination in its own vertical line.
