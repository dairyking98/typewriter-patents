## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to the type bar actuating mechanism of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Each key lever carries a sub-lever pivoted to it, and the sub-lever is connected by a link to the type-bar, with a slot and fulcrum rod giving the motion; the joint between these parts is the improved construction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The joints between the key levers, sub-levers and type-bar links of the type-bar actuating mechanism are the object of improvement; the OCR does not state the defect clearly.

**Solution:** Each key lever carries a sub-lever pivoted to it, and the sub-lever is connected by a link to the type-bar, with a slot and fulcrum rod giving the motion; the joint between these parts is the improved construction.
