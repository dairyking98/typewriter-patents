## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Calculating-machine punched-card transfer device lacked versatile value transfer between counter, card and other machines.

**Solution:** A transmission mechanism transfers values from punched cards and counters to other machines and back, including digit shifting.

**How it works:** Pivoting transfer levers connect counter drive elements to a transfer carriage and a parking carriage, letting values move between counter, card and other machines.
