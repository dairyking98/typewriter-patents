## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (draft punching machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in machines for punching drafts and checks;

**How it works (reading):** Jointed operating levers move endwise-sliding punches that are drawn back by springs, and a bar depresses the punches while a mechanism advances the check to the next punch position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Drafts and checks needed to be punched quickly and securely.

**Solution:** Jointed operating levers move endwise-sliding punches that are drawn back by springs, and a bar depresses the punches while a mechanism advances the check to the next punch position.
