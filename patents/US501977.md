## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to means for facilitating tabulated or column work, and has for its principal object to maintain control of the paper-carriage, when released for rapid advancement to a column stop, so as to avoid the shock and jar heretofore occasioned by the sudden arresting of the paper-carriage by contact with the column-stop.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage-release lever with a dog that engages preset pins in a perforated stop rod, giving controlled column stops while the carriage is released.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a released carriage rushes to a column stop it is stopped suddenly, causing shock and jar.

**Solution:** A carriage-release lever with a dog that engages preset pins in a perforated stop rod, giving controlled column stops while the carriage is released.

**How it works:** On a Remington paper carriage, depressing a spring-raised lever turns a rock-shaft whose arm lowers the hinged feed-rack frame, freeing the rack from the letter-spacing dogs. The driving spring then pulls the carriage left until the lever's dog meets a removable pin set in a rod beneath the carriage. The rod has holes spaced to match the machine scale (one per five degrees), numbered, and mounted on detachable blocks. Releasing the lever lets the rack re-engage and normal writing resumes. A modification is shown in the figures.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** carriage; hinged feed-rack frame; feed rack; spacing dog; rock-shaft; release lever; dog; stop rod; stop pin; scale; pointer; driving spring

**Numbers:** one stop hole per five scale divisions
