**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens

**How it works (reading):** A subframe bolted to a normal typewriter base carries an extra carriage track and rods so a longer carriage can be used.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wide carriage typewriters are costly, and normal typewriters cannot write on wide sheets.

**Solution:** A subframe bolted to a normal typewriter base carries an extra carriage track and rods so a longer carriage can be used.
