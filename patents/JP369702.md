## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Make a typewriter small, easy to carry and dust-proof.

**Solution:** A sloping-topped case with a U-shaped trough for the platen and a U-shaped slot in which a type magazine swings.

**How it works:** The case top slopes down to the front. A U-shaped trough at the back holds the platen, and a U-shaped slot in the middle of the top opens into the trough. The type magazine, whose circumference carries rows of types and whose top shows a character index, swings in the slot about a pivot by a knob. A tab raises and lowers the magazine, and another knob turns the platen. The mechanism is enclosed in the case.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Typewriter. Brief description of the drawings. Fig. 1 is a side view of the typewriter of this device, and Fig. 2 a plan of the same. Nature, action and effect of the device. The gist of this device is the shape of a typewriter in which the top of a case 1, nearly rectangular in plan, is made a surface 2 sloping down to the front; at the back of the case is cut a U-shaped trough 3 in which the platen 5 is set, and in the middle of the top a U-shaped slot 4 opening into the trough 3; and the type magazine 6 can swing within the U-shaped slot 4 about its pivot point 7. In the drawings, 8 is the knob for swinging the type magazine, 9 the knob for turning the platen, 10 the index for the characters shown on top of the type magazine 6, 11 the tab for raising and lowering the type magazine, 12 the centre shaft of the magazine, and 13 the magazine's circumference, round which the required types are set in rows. Because the typewriter of this device has this shape, its overall volume is made very small and it is very handy to store and to carry; moreover, as the mechanism is housed inside the case, there is no fear of dust getting in, and the look of the machine is always kept neat.
