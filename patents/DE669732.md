## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**How it works (reading):** A lock acts on the switch body only just before switching and is released immediately after.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switch body on the escape wheel vibrates, so a tooth is skipped on carriage switching.

**Solution:** A lock acts on the switch body only just before switching and is released immediately after.

**Features:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
