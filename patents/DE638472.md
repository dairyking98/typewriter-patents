## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line setting for receipts)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line setting device for receipts in cash registers or booking machines, with which the first free line of the document to be printed, usually one Account card, is discontinued.

**How it works (reading):** For certain machine operations the printing table is connected to the adder actuating means so the document automatically sets to the line of the selected adder, while other operations can switch any adder on.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a cash register, the document table is permanently tied to the adder selector, so every amount enters the line of its adder even when account cards must be filled.

**Solution:** For certain machine operations the printing table is connected to the adder actuating means so the document automatically sets to the line of the selected adder, while other operations can switch any adder on.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
