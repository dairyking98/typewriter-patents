## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a regulating device for the tension of the key lever springs of typewriters, in which the rail serving as a spring bearing is displaced parallel to the spring action is and consists in the fact that this per se known parallel shift by a toggle lever mechanism with two knee joints operated by means of a handwheel and spindle, on which the rail carrying the springs is hinged, takes place.

**How it works (reading):** A handwheel and spindle drive a toggle lever with two knee joints that moves the spring rail parallel to the spring force, so the force rises smoothly with the toggle ratio.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Adjusting key lever spring tension by pivoting levers changes the spring force badly, and the springs cannot be tensioned directly along their line of force, so heavy spring pressure is hard to set.

**Solution:** A handwheel and spindle drive a toggle lever with two knee joints that moves the spring rail parallel to the spring force, so the force rises smoothly with the toggle ratio.
