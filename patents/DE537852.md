## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (multiplying device for typewriters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on typewriter duplicating devices that serve several Make originals or copies at the same time without using carbon paper.

**How it works (reading):** Thin, flexible, polished protective strips hang on a metal bracket and swing up and down to screen the paper from the auxiliary ribbons, which move only with the main ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Protecting the paper back from auxiliary duplicating ribbons: long ribbon bands are costly and bulky, and thick rails are awkward to insert.

**Solution:** Thin, flexible, polished protective strips hang on a metal bracket and swing up and down to screen the paper from the auxiliary ribbons, which move only with the main ribbon.

With these devices, writing on the back of the sheets of paper, that lie between the auxiliary ribbons, .that is prevented by protective strips that lie between the back of the paper and the adjacent ribbon.
