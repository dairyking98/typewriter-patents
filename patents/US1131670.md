## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines, and more particularly to line s'pacing mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Crown ratchet teeth on the platen head serve as the line-spacing wheel, and a roller detent on a carrier that slides along the tie-rod holds the platen against accidental displacement; a line spacing pawl engages the teeth.
