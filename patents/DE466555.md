## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (keyboard for calculating machines)

**How it works (reading):** Gut strings threaded through the key slots are struck by a bridge on the key bar on return, which damps the noise far more than the metal wire stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Metal stop rods in calculator keyboards rattle loudly when keys return.

**Solution:** Gut strings threaded through the key slots are struck by a bridge on the key bar on return, which damps the noise far more than the metal wire stop.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
