## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attachments operated by the leg, e.g. the foot, the knee

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the mechanism associated with the carriage of a type-writing machine, the objects being, first, to provide novel mechanism whereby the return of the carriage to the starting-point causes the upward movement of the paper thereon into position to receive a new line of impressions;

**How it works (reading):** The carriage return raises the paper to the next line, and a mechanism lets the operator return the carriage without the hands. The impression hammer is worked by each forward feed movement of the carriage that goes with printing a character.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage must raise the paper to a new line, and the operator should not need to use the hands to return it.

**Solution:** The carriage return raises the paper to the next line, and a mechanism lets the operator return the carriage without the hands. The impression hammer is worked by each forward feed movement of the carriage that goes with printing a character.
