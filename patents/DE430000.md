## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check dispensing device)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a check dispensing device operating in this way, which can be influenced by two independently adjustable organs that depending on the choice, the check printing and issuing device with the Getritbe or is uncoupled.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded coupling member with two locking arms, controlled by the control knob and the cash-sale button, engages or disengages the check printing and issuing gear and prevents the member from taking its clutch position when no check is to be issued.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The cashier may forget to switch the check dispenser back on, so a check is missed when the business-type key is pressed.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded coupling member with two locking arms, controlled by the control knob and the cash-sale button, engages or disengages the check printing and issuing gear and prevents the member from taking its clutch position when no check is to be issued.
