## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in adjustable tally roll attachment for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable bracket on the carriage rack bar holds a paper roll on a rod with spring-pawl collars; the roll is fed to the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tally roll must be made out simultaneously with a ledger sheet, which needs a means to feed the roll to the platen.

**Solution:** An adjustable bracket on the carriage rack bar holds a paper roll on a rod with spring-pawl collars; the roll is fed to the platen.
