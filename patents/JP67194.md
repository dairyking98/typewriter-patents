## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Bookkeeping needs the same figure entered at once in several columns of a ledger page, which an ordinary typewriter cannot do.

**Solution:** Several groups of typebar linkages, one per column, share one set of keys, and a hand-set selector puts chosen groups in working state so one key stroke prints the same figure in all selected columns.

**How it works:** Each of several groups of ten-figure typebar linkages prints at its own point over a ledger page held on a movable table. Pressing a vertical bar member in a group's guides tips a lever so the group's hooks engage the toothed rods driven by the shared key levers; other groups stay idle. One key stroke then works the typebars of all selected groups, and the table steps by a rack and pawl. At line end a sloping pawl releases all selection levers. A second version uses electromagnets on a carriage: a key drops a stop rod that halts the carriage at the step matching the figure, then the key's second contact energises magnets that pull the armatures of the selected groups' typebars.
