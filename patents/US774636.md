## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to mechanism for feeding the ribbon from one spool to the other and is shown applied to a machine of the char-* acter illustrated in my Patent No.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon mechanism driven from the key-lever and carriage feed moves the ribbon toward the platen and away to expose the print, with a clutch that reverses the feed, ribbon guides and a spool connection.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must move toward the platen at each key depression and away at each carriage feed, with reversing, insertion and spool connection that are easy to use.

**Solution:** A ribbon mechanism driven from the key-lever and carriage feed moves the ribbon toward the platen and away to expose the print, with a clutch that reverses the feed, ribbon guides and a spool connection.
