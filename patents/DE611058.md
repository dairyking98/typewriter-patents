## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a tool for producing plate-shaped or trough-shaped Characteristics at the foot of typewriter type levers.

**How it works (reading):** A tool with a U-shaped support held in a vice carries a spring-loaded pressure bolt that presses the typebar foot, and a wedge locks the block so the typebar foot can be widened by tightening the vice.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Side play of a typebar in its segment slot leaves the type off centre in the type-lifter guide, so it fails to print fully.

**Solution:** A tool with a U-shaped support held in a vice carries a spring-loaded pressure bolt that presses the typebar foot, and a wedge locks the block so the typebar foot can be widened by tightening the vice.
