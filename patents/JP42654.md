## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**Class note:** J-PlatPat files it as a Western-type typewriter (B41J1/00@P); it is a Japanese-script (kanji or kana) machine built on Western lines.

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** The object is a typewriter able to hold a very great number of characters and print any of them freely.

**How it works (reading):** Engrave 45 characters in a long row on each of 52 type bars and bring the paper cylinder to the wanted spot on the row by a two-layer carriage support: a lower layer shifted by four shift keys (5 zones) and an upper layer shifted by the character keys themselves (9 places).

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** An Oriental-script typewriter needs thousands of characters, but a single-layer carriage shift over 45 positions would need strokes and forces too long for fingers.

**Solution:** Engrave 45 characters in a long row on each of 52 type bars and bring the paper cylinder to the wanted spot on the row by a two-layer carriage support: a lower layer shifted by four shift keys (5 zones) and an upper layer shifted by the character keys themselves (9 places).

**How it works:** Pressing any key pulls a long lever and link that swings its type bar about 90 degrees onto the cylinder. Keys in columns a to h also push a crank lever whose link, through a bent guide slot and long slot escapement, pulls one of eight rods under the carriage; hooks on the rods move the upper layer by 1 to 4 character spaces forward or back and a hook post stops it exactly. The slotted escapement lets the key keep travelling after the cylinder has stopped, so the bar strikes only when the cylinder is still. Four shift keys swing levers that slide the lower layer to zones (a to d), with no shift giving the fifth zone. Each key is marked with five characters, the large one for unshifted and four small ones for shifted.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention is a typewriter for Oriental script. A great many characters are engraved in a long row on each of many needles (type bars), which move towards the paper cylinder when a key is pressed with the finger. The support of the paper cylinder's carriage is made in two movable layers: the upper layer rests on the lower, and the lower rests on fixed rails. Both layers can move, each through various distances, in the same direction as the rows of characters on the type bars. The movement of the lower layer brings the paper cylinder, carried on the upper layer, to a rough position, and the movement of the upper layer then brings it to the exact position. The movement of the upper layer is controlled by the same finger keys that work the type bars; the movement of the lower layer is controlled by separate shift keys. So the paper cylinder can be set freely against any of the characters in the long rows on the type bars. The object is a typewriter able to hold a very great number of characters and print any of them freely. Brief description of the drawings.
