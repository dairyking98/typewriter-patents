## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the type actions of IO writing-machines;

**How it works (reading):** A device prevents excessive rebound of the typebars as they return from the platen, so they stop cleanly at the basket.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars rebounded off the basket after striking the platen, causing clashes and limiting speed.

**Solution:** A device prevents excessive rebound of the typebars as they return from the platen, so they stop cleanly at the basket.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Rebound and clash prevention](../features/bar-rebound.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** typebar; basket; levers; hangers
