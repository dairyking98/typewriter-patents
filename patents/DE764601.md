## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electromagnetic typewriters need either large magnets per type carrier or magnets that ride on a reciprocating driver with flexible leads; neither allows many characters in little space.

**Solution:** Hold each axially sliding type rod locked by a small solenoid that, when energised, releases the lock and couples the rod to a common reciprocating driver, with a hand-held multi-button switch box generating the selection pulses.

**How it works:** Many type rods are bundled in parallel with types at their ends, and a funnel in front guides the released rod to the print point. A common driver reciprocates; a small solenoid per rod normally holds the rod locked and its coupling disengaged, and on release the rod couples to the driver and is thrust against the paper, which is pressed on by a rolling abutment. A box held in one hand has four groups of three push buttons for the four fingers and a thumb toggle with about eight positions, giving 96 distinct pulses instead of 24.
