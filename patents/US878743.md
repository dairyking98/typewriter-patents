## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When making carbon copies on a single strip, the operator cannot judge how far to turn the platen back to insert new sheets at uniform spacing.

**Solution:** A scale strip and indicator on the platen shaft show how far the platen has been turned, giving predetermined spaces between copies.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
