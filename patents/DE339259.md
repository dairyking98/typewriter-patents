## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** The type lever bearing axles have an angular cross-section instead of round, and the bearing is a pair of threaded cones with a ring body holding balls, so larger balls fit and the flat rods are removed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Many closely spaced type lever bearings have to use small balls, and flat supporting rods take up space.

**Solution:** The type lever bearing axles have an angular cross-section instead of round, and the bearing is a pair of threaded cones with a ring body holding balls, so larger balls fit and the flat rods are removed.
