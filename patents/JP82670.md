## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a Japanese-script typewriter the paper cylinder advances by a fixed amount per character, so the character spacing cannot be varied when needed.

**Solution:** Limit the travel of the feed pawl with an adjustable gap between a roller on a movable adjusting plate and a limit plate on a tiltable frame lever.

**How it works:** The paper-cylinder shaft sits in a frame; a frame lever is fitted on the frame boss and can be tilted by a screw. An adjusting plate slides left-right on the frame lever by a screw, spring-loaded, and carries a roller. A rotating plate turning with the printing action carries the feed pawl that feeds a gear fixed to the cylinder. The feed pawl's travel is stopped between the roller and a limit plate projecting from the frame lever; moving either changes the gap. A brake pawl holds the gear and can be lifted free by a free lever. The gear can thus be fed one, two, three teeth or a half or third of a tooth per character.
