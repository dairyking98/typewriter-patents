## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Single sheets halt writing for paper changes, as in court reporting.

**Solution:** A roll of paper on the carriage is fed on the platen and a knife in a slot of the platen severs each sheet automatically, with a bell.

**How it works:** A continuous paper roll is mounted on the carriage and fed in the customary way. A knife, mounted at a slight angle to its shearing edge, sits on a sliding frame whose side pieces ride inclined ways in the carriage. The shearing strip is in a slot in the platen periphery. Spring tongues with lugs ride grooves in the platen end plates and act as cams that draw the knife back after the cut. A bell sounds as it cuts. The feed lever has a dog to set a stroke of one unit or a multiple, arranged so the multiple feed always ends on a step boundary.
