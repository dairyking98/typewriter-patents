## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programmable keyboards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in a type wheel or cylinder constructed so as to be adjusted with respect to its carrying-stem and locked irrespective of the keyboard, and also includes a key or guide by which the proper adjustment of the type-cylinder is determined in setting the machine for transposing cipher.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment with a type cylinder that can be adjusted on its carrying stem and locked independent of the keyboard, plus an adjustable key or guide that fixes the cylinder setting for transposing cipher.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing and transposing cipher messages on a type-writer is awkward without a way to set the cipher alignment.

**Solution:** An attachment with a type cylinder that can be adjusted on its carrying stem and locked independent of the keyboard, plus an adjustable key or guide that fixes the cylinder setting for transposing cipher.
