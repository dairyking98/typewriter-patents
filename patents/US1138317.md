## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike key action is slow to accelerate the type bar and needs manual adjustment to align type impressions.

**Solution:** Type bar with heel and notches driven by a pivoted member with prong and hook, linked to the key lever for compound leverage, plus a bracket centered by pins.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md)
