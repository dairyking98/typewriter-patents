## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (thorium dioxide sol) · **Subject (patent classes):** Physical forms of mixtures of two different X-ray contrast-enhancing agents, containing at least one X-ray contrast-enhancing agent which is not a halogenated organic compound; Compounds of thorium

**How it works (reading):** Adding starch breakdown products as protective colloids desensitizes the sol so it can be mixed with proteins.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Thorium dioxide sol is unusable for bladder and kidney imaging because it causes stones and constipation.

**Solution:** Adding starch breakdown products as protective colloids desensitizes the sol so it can be mixed with proteins.
