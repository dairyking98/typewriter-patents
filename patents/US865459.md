## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriters and more particularly to the keys thereof, and has for its objects to provide an arrangement oi keys which will enable the operator to readily find the keys and to operate them quickly and with a very slight movemcht of the fingers.
