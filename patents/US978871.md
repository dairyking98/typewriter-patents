## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**How it works (reading):** An adjusting lever shifts the position of the escapement dogs relative to the wheel and a release lever throws the dogs out of engagement so the carriage can be moved freely; a stop mechanism controls the throw of the escapement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The escapement dogs cannot be readily shifted relative to the escapement wheel, and the dogs cannot be easily released and thrown out of engagement to allow free carriage movement.

**Solution:** An adjusting lever shifts the position of the escapement dogs relative to the wheel and a release lever throws the dogs out of engagement so the carriage can be moved freely; a stop mechanism controls the throw of the escapement.
