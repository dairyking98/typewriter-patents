## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier Ellis ribbon reversing device (US1018176) used constantly driven spindles and clutches, which were complex, heavy and prone to failure.

**Solution:** Replace the spindles and clutches with a loosely mounted pawl and ratchet wheel inside each ribbon cup, reversed by the swinging ribbon clamp when the spool runs out.

**How it works:** Each ribbon spool sits in a cup holding a pawl-and-ratchet wheel driven by whatever ribbon motion the machine supplies. When a spool is fully unwound, tension pulls out a swinging ribbon clamp, which disengages the pawl of the active spool and engages the pawl of the idle spool, reversing feed. A spring-pressed detent ring with a pin keeps the reversing lever in place and prevents dead centres, so reversal adds almost no resistance to key pressing.
