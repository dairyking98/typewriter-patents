## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a power-driven typewriter with type bars.

**How it works (reading):** Eccentric drives that are coupled in dead-centre positions move the type bars gradually, so the striking force is higher and quiet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bar machines are hard to press when copying, while power drive gives a noisy jolt.

**Solution:** Eccentric drives that are coupled in dead-centre positions move the type bars gradually, so the striking force is higher and quiet.
