## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper roll support swings out only after a paper carriage movement, so for repeated single-amount entries on one line it has to be swung out by a separate key.

**Solution:** Control stops on the paper carriage hold a counter stop in the effective position for the chosen carriage setting, so the swing-out is triggered by the machine gear without an extra key.
