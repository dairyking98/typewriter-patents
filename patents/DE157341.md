**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a front paper guide device of Typewriters with front stop and vertically displaceable paper roller, which is guided in a paper carriage, which is arranged by in front of and behind the paper roller Rollers is supported.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper guide weight lifts the whole device when the platen is switched.

**Solution:** Paper guides are attached to a paper carriage so they follow the vertical movement of the paper roller while the carriage does not join that movement.
