## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a device for typewriters for controlling the movement of the paper carriage, during which one is stuck on the paper carriage Rack is in engagement with a gear wheel that sits on a spindle which is further arranged a ratchet wheel, which by a ratchet clutch of the spindle is taken along.

**How it works (reading):** A U-shaped pawl pivoted on the spindle, with teeth on its connecting web engaging the crown ratchet, and drive arms for disengagement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A sliding crown-ratchet clutch on the paper carriage had high friction, wear and looseness on its spring wedge.

**Solution:** A U-shaped pawl pivoted on the spindle, with teeth on its connecting web engaging the crown ratchet, and drive arms for disengagement.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
