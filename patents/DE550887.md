## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card statistical machine printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on endless bands or the like

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to statistical machines controlled by punch cards with printing device and has a special design of the printing device as its subject, which makes it possible the printed specification of the corresponding to the card holes of the individual cards Sizes for different card groups assigned by imprints of the card groups to supplement special printing plates, stencils or the like.

**How it works (reading):** Each printing plate carries a mechanical identifier such as an identification perforation that is compared with the identification punching of the cards, so the machine itself checks that the right plate is printed for each card group.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printed plates or stencils on a punch-card tabulator are assigned to card groups only by careful pre-sorting, so one sorting error misassigns every later card group.

**Solution:** Each printing plate carries a mechanical identifier such as an identification perforation that is compared with the identification punching of the cards, so the machine itself checks that the right plate is printed for each card group.
