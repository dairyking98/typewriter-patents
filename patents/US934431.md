## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the Underwood ribbon reverser both trip wheels could engage their trips at once and lock the mechanism.

**Solution:** Spring-returned trips that hold the idle trip clear of its trip wheel.

**How it works:** A horizontal driving shaft with trip wheels shifts endwise to wind ribbon onto one spool or the other. Each trip is held off its wheel by a ribbon-controlled device until the spool empties, then drops in; the turning wheel swings the trip against its return spring and shifts the shaft, reversing the winding. The spring holds the other trip far from its wheel so both cannot engage.
