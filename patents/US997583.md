## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (rotary feet for stencil machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to rovide a feed adjustment for stencil machines by means of which circular stencils such as are used for marking barrel heads and the like ma be readily and accurately cut.

**How it works (reading):** A frame slides on notched rails and is held by rollers in notches, with a cross bar and rollers that set the feed distance from the stencil cutter.
