## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The known driving member with two coupling lugs has to swing back idle after each stroke, which limits writing speed.

**Solution:** Use a freely turning star wheel with a row of fingers on the power lever so each power-shaft tooth engages two fingers in toggle fashion, with no idle return motion.

**How it works:** A key press frees a spring-loaded power lever. The star wheel at its rear end meets a rib on the continuously turning power shaft; the first finger hit turns the wheel so the next finger meets the following tooth. The two fingers then stand almost straight as a toggle and swing the power lever strongly, moving the type lever through a pull rod and intermediate lever. An angle lever and a protective plate keep the wheel disengaged at rest.
