## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a three-bank visible typewriter the keys differ in lever length and the typebars differ in weight and swing, so key feel and impression force vary across the keyboard.

**Solution:** Proportion the key levers, link connections, cranks, springs, stops and universal bar so the key action and blow are equalised across all keys and banks.

**How it works:** Three banks of key levers (short, intermediate, long) pivot on one rod and act through links with turnbuckles on cranks on vertical rock-shaft typebars that swing in a horizontal plane and nest in two groups. The cranks lie at progressively different angles so heavy outer bars get a strong initial pull and light inner bars a gradual one. Spring prongs of graded length, stepped stop-bar surfaces and a hinged universal bar with prongs of three lengths equalise the travel and resistance. A pin on each type head enters a V-shaped guide at the print point to hold the bar rigid. The whole typebar frame is lifted or lowered on vertical guideways by shift levers and springs to bring one of three type rows (capitals, lower case, punctuation) to the printing line.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** key lever; universal bar; typebar; crank; link; turnbuckle; spring plate; stop bar; typebar guide; typebar frame; shift lever; type block

**Numbers:** three banks of keys; crank angle about 45 degrees behind transverse plane at the centre (text reads 515, likely OCR); half-inch key depression example

**Improves on:**

- earlier nested typebars with horizontal intermediate sections and stepped heads: diagonal flat intermediate sections make stiffer, lighter bars of more even weight
