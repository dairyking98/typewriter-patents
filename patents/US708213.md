## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-head pivoted to or rotating on lever

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing maing and locking device for each shiftable chines, and has special reference to printing type-head.

**How it works (reading):** A type-head with characters at an angle to each other is pivoted to the type-bar and turned between its two positions. A trip and buffer on the type-bar set the shift and a trigger locks the head rigid in each position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A shiftable type-head carrying two case characters needs a positive shift and a true, locked printing position for both cases.

**Solution:** A type-head with characters at an angle to each other is pivoted to the type-bar and turned between its two positions. A trip and buffer on the type-bar set the shift and a trigger locks the head rigid in each position.
