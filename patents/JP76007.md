## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A plain ratchet-and-pawl feed of the paper cylinder sometimes turns it too far or not far enough, and the character spacing is hard to change.

**Solution:** A geared paper-cylinder feed whose per-stroke rotation is limited by two alternating pawl levers and an adjustable limit piece, so spacing is exact and settable.

**How it works:** A drive gear meshing with a gear on the paper cylinder carries a ratchet; a swinging lever with a pawl is rocked by the key or feed lever and a spring, advancing over a tooth on the forward stroke and pulling the ratchet round on the return. A rising-and-falling pawl lever and a swinging pawl lever pivoted on it alternately enter tooth spaces of the drive gear, the sloping end of the swinging lever shifting them. The gear can turn only as far as the swinging pawl lever travels from the adjustable limit piece to a fixed stop pin, so feed per stroke is fixed exactly, and the limit piece has two faces for two spacings. A grip tilts the whole support plate to disengage the drive gear so the cylinder turns freely.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Vertical-feed device for a "typewriter". Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a vertical-feed device for a typewriter, constructed as follows. A drive gear meshes with a gear fixed to the paper cylinder, and a ratchet wheel is fixed to the drive gear. On their shaft a swinging lever is loosely fitted, carrying a pawl that engages the teeth of the ratchet. The lever is given a back-and-forth turning motion through a certain angle by the action of the key lever or the feed lever and of a spring; on the forward stroke the pawl catches a tooth further on, and on the return stroke the pawl pulls the ratchet round. Further, a spacing control mechanism is provided, consisting of a rising-and-falling pawl lever with a pawl acting on the drive gear; a swinging pawl lever, pivoted on it and swung out sideways by a spring, with a pawl acting on the same gear; and an adjustable limit piece that limits how far the swinging pawl lever swings. When the key lever or feed lever is pressed, the drive gear, in a turning motion limited by this spacing control mechanism, turns the paper cylinder through the required angle.
