## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to devices for cleaning the types or printing characters of typewriting machines;

**How it works (reading):** A bracket clamped to the machine side bar carries an adjustable sleeve and block that holds a cleaning brush at an adjustable height and position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cleaning devices fixed to the platen, carriage or frame are unsatisfactory for cleaning types.

**Solution:** A bracket clamped to the machine side bar carries an adjustable sleeve and block that holds a cleaning brush at an adjustable height and position.
