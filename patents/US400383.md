## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Simplify and cheapen the universal bar and the rocker that drives the spacing dog.

**Solution:** Universal bar made as a wheel on a vertical stem, linked by a lever and rod to the rocker bar.

**How it works:** Connecting rods from key levers have inward bends lying on the universal-bar wheel, so any key pushes it down; its stem is joined to a lever whose rod moves an arm on the rocker bar carrying the retaining dog in the feed racks. A spring on the lever returns it, with a screw to set tension. A spring-driven arm and pitman drive the carriage.
