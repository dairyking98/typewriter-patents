## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The elasticity and thickness of the platen roller cannot be varied to suit the paper, and rollers harden with age, which cracks typehammers and makes paper hard to move.

**Solution:** The roller is a heavy tube with a rubber cushioning surface, sealed at the ends by plugs and heads, with a hollow rod through the tube so the roller is rigid, and the cushioning or air contained in the tube can be varied.
