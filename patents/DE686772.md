## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** The carriage stops a set distance short of its end position so a line feed can be repeated by pressing the lift key again; a loose escapement tooth is held out of action to prevent ratcheting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage-return lift needed a separate line-space step; earlier automatic cut-off devices were intricate.

**Solution:** The carriage stops a set distance short of its end position so a line feed can be repeated by pressing the lift key again; a loose escapement tooth is held out of action to prevent ratcheting.
