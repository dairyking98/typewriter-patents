## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter small enough to fold flat and carry in the pocket, yet usable on any sheet of paper laid on a table.

**Solution:** A folding set of nested trays in which a flexible type strap is slid by hand to a hole-indexed character position and the whole upper plate is pressed down to print and step the device along a rack.

**How it works:** The machine lies on the paper; the base plate is held still by rubber strips while the left hand rests on it. The right hand grips a handle on a sliding plate carrying a flexible strap of type and moves it until a spring detent drops into the hole marked with the wanted character. Pressing the upper plate down tilts a lever whose far end is held by a toothed rack, so its head pushes the chosen type through an aperture onto the paper. The same downward stroke trips a dog and pallet on a spring-loaded rod, which lifts out of the rack and re-engages one tooth further, advancing the plates by one letter space. Unselected types are pressed onto inking pads on each stroke, and a space is made by printing with the detent at a blank position. For carrying, the plates nest and a hinged cover plate folds over them.
