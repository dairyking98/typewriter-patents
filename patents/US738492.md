## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column spacing needs stops that are settable anywhere along the line, and any column should be reachable in one operation, skipping intermediate fields, without the carriage running early.

**Solution:** Adjustable column stops on a part that shifts with the carriage, engaged by a key-operated stop dog that is latched in place, with carriage release only after the key is let go.

**How it works:** Shown on a Remington. The spring-drum-driven carriage has a rack/pinion escapement whose rack can swing up to release. Separately adjustable stops define column positions on a shiftable or rotatable part mounted on the frame, connected to the carriage to move with it. A finger key brings a stop dog into engagement with a chosen stop and a detent holds it there independent of the finger. The carriage release then operates only after the key is released.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** carriage; spring drum; drive strap; escapement rack; pinion; column stop; stop dog; finger key; carriage release; margin stop bar
