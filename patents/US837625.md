## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single machine cannot easily write both right-to-left languages such as Hebrew and left-to-right languages such as English.

**Solution:** A supplementary platen-spacing device with a pinion and ratchet and release bars lets the carriage be moved in either direction, so the platen can be spaced both ways.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** platen rack; pinion; ratchet wheel; release bars; spring
