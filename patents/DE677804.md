## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type carrier setting device, scissor action) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for producing a permanent visual presentation of the output data, e.g. computer output printers using printers by rack-type printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for adjusting an organ, in particular one Type carrier, from one setting directly into a new setting position by means of two moved against each other and on the organ to be adjusted like scissors in opposite directions Direction of acting adjusting elements.

**How it works (reading):** Two scissor-like arms set the type carrier: one arm is driven by a cam, the other is held by a locking member, and the locking teeth on the second arm engage to fix the position, driven by card-hole sensing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-carrier setting by differential gears is complex, and a simpler adjustment is needed for punched-card control.

**Solution:** Two scissor-like arms set the type carrier: one arm is driven by a cam, the other is held by a locking member, and the locking teeth on the second arm engage to fix the position, driven by card-hole sensing.

**Features:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
