## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The detachable coupling between paper roller and line-feed wheel must hold firmly against unintended release, yet be light to operate, adjustable and cheap to make.

**Solution:** Friction clamping jaws are pressed against a coupling sleeve by lever pairs with long actuating arms, driven by wedge-shaped displacer rods pushed between the lever ends.

**How it works:** A disc on the paper roller axis carries bolts with cross arms that act as pivots for pairs of levers. Wedge-tapered displacer rods, sliding axially in the handwheel, spread the free (long) lever ends apart, and the short lever ends press adjustable studs in two slotted clamping jaws against a coupling sleeve fixed to the line-feed wheel. The large lever ratio gives strong clamping from a light push on a button. A spring-loaded pin in the handwheel latches the displacers in the engaged position. Studs are adjusted through holes in the sleeve and locked by clamping the slotted jaw.
