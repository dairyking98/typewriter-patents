## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Japanese typewriters with a moving printing part are slow and unreliable; the aim is a Japanese machine that prints characters in succession like a European typewriter.

**Solution:** A round rotating type magazine is turned by hand under several fixed hammer-and-push-button printing sets, with turning indicators showing the characters right way round, so adjacent characters in a row can be struck one after another without turning the magazine.

**How it works:** A ring-shaped magazine of sector compartments, each holding loose types in several rows, rides on rollers and balls on a round base and is turned by rim handles. One fixed hammer per row stands over the magazine, and all hammers strike the same point on the paper cylinder. Pressing a push button turns a lever that drives a type pusher to lift the type into the hammer's holder, then locks the swinging arm to the hammer foot, grips the type with a support that enters a groove round it, and lifts the hammer so the type strikes the cylinder; releasing the button returns everything by spring and weight and the type drops back. Each stroke also turns the cylinder one division through a hook rotator and lifts a coloured paper (carbon-type strip) against the cylinder; a separate button moves the cylinder one line. Two cylinder-shaped indicators turn with the magazine and carry the same characters in black on white with pointers matching the hammer positions, and common compound words are laid in a single row so they can be struck without turning the magazine.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Round Japanese-language typewriter. Summary of the nature and purpose of the invention. In this invention several sets of printing mechanisms, worked mechanically by push buttons, are fitted to a round, rotating type magazine. The printing machinery does not move at all: the magazine alone is turned to bring the wanted type to a given position, and the push button works the corresponding printing mechanism, which picks up that type and strikes it against the paper cylinder. Characters are thus struck in succession, just like a European typewriter. An indicator is also provided, to make choosing the types easy. The purpose is to create a Japanese typewriter that prints reliably and fast. Brief explanation of the drawings.
