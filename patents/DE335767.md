## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on such devices for noiseless return of the car or for noiseless turning back of the ratchet wheel, in which a stationary pawl interacts with the ratchet wheel.

**How it works (reading):** A stationary pawl carries an auxiliary pawl that is frictionally driven by an auxiliary locking wheel, and it lifts the main pawl out of the ratchet when the ratchet turns backwards.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage makes a rasping noise because a stationary pawl drags on the ratchet wheel when it turns backwards.

**Solution:** A stationary pawl carries an auxiliary pawl that is frictionally driven by an auxiliary locking wheel, and it lifts the main pawl out of the ratchet when the ratchet turns backwards.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** ratchet wheel; stationary pawl; auxiliary pawl; auxiliary locking wheel
