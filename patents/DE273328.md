The innovation consists mainly in the special design of the support device for the paper roll, which according to the invention is supported by an axis fixed in a vertical position, namely from a hollow spindle placed on this, which is on the free end of the axis rests and at its lower end a plate-shaped arched plate for support who owns roll of paper is carried. ! 5 This arrangement ensures that the Center of gravity of the paper roll and that of the support device under its suspension point so that the paper roll always remains in balance. Through this kind of Suspension of the paper roll can cause the same to escape from the usual working position do not take place. Furthermore, the new paper roll holder allows any guidance the paper web unwound from the roll in different directions, whereby the paper web is not damaged.
