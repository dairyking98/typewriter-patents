## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper rolls on typewriters can slip out of working position and the web can be damaged when guided in different directions.

**Solution:** A paper roll sits on a hollow spindle on a fixed vertical axis, with a curved plate at the bottom that holds the roll; the centre of gravity lies below the suspension point so the roll stays balanced.
