## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot see the work on a typebar machine and alignment depends on type guides.

**Solution:** Use a vibrating ribbon-guide tube that drops to reveal the writing, with a pointer, and adjustable typebar hangers on an arc bar for alignment.

**How it works:** A flat ribbon guide in front of the platen is lifted by a rock-shaft turned by key levers, stretching the ribbon to the platen as a typebar strikes, then drops to uncover the printed line. The guide has a pointer indicating the next letter position. Typebars sit on slotted hangers clamped to a pendent arc bar, so each can be set to align the print without a type-guide. Ribbon spools shift laterally by nuts to expose fresh ribbon.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
