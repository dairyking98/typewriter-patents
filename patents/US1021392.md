## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating mechanism cannot select any column or any denominational position within a column in a single carriage run.

**Solution:** Key-set tabulating mechanism arrests the carriage at a chosen columnar field and denominational position, with parts locked until a later run releases them.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
