## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The table stop release must stop the carriage at set columns, and the known stop arrangement gives a hard impact.

**Solution:** Two levers pivoting on one pin move together with the table key. One of them, with a retaining notch, catches the table stop on the carriage and slows the carriage at the end of its travel. A line-length stop is also adjustable.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
