## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Elliott-Fisher flat-bed typewriters needed a way to produce manifold records on several paper webs with transfer ribbons.

**Solution:** A web feeding and roll-supporting mechanism with separate retention of paper and transfer ribbons so each can move independently.

**How it works:** Paper webs from spools are fed over a flat platen on a desk, one above another with interposed transfer ribbon. The paper webs and the transfer ribbons are held separately so one moves without the other. Feed mechanism advances webs, a guard guides them from spools, a knife severs printed portions, and a release lets webs be advanced by hand. The transfer ribbon is moved by the machine's ribbon motion.

**Features:** [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [INK:carbon](../features/ink-carbon.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** flat platen; paper web; web roll; transfer ribbon; feed shaft; guard; severing knife; spool shaft retainer; desk
