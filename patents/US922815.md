## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil-cutting machines should be simple and cheap and have the mechanism housed so the operator cannot catch their fingers when adjusting.

**Solution:** A stencil-cutting machine with a rotary carrier of female dies and punch rods in a housed mechanism.

**How it works:** A lower die carrier turns loosely on a central pivot post and holds female dies at cast apertures, secured by binding plates and screws that can be shifted in end pockets to adjust each die. A punch carrier above has a toothed rim and loosely holds punch rods with male dies at their lower ends, kept raised by springs. Lowering a punch rod drives its male die into the female die and cuts the character in the stencil sheet, which a feed mechanism advances.

**Features:** [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [ELEM:interchangeable](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md)

**Parts:** pivot post; lower die carrier; female die; binding plate; punch carrier; punch rod; male die; return spring; table; sheet feeding member
