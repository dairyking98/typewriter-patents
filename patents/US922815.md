## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that character of machines used for producing stencils and it has for its object the production of a machine of this character which is of simple and inexpensive construction and in which the main mechanism is so housed as to prevent the operator from becoming injured by having the fingers of his hands caught in the moving parts of the machine when it is being adjusted.

**How it works (reading):** A stencil-cutting machine with a rotary carrier of female dies and punch rods in a housed mechanism.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil-cutting machines should be simple and cheap and have the mechanism housed so the operator cannot catch their fingers when adjusting.

**Solution:** A stencil-cutting machine with a rotary carrier of female dies and punch rods in a housed mechanism.

**How it works:** A lower die carrier turns loosely on a central pivot post and holds female dies at cast apertures, secured by binding plates and screws that can be shifted in end pockets to adjust each die. A punch carrier above has a toothed rim and loosely holds punch rods with male dies at their lower ends, kept raised by springs. Lowering a punch rod drives its male die into the female die and cuts the character in the stencil sheet, which a feed mechanism advances.
