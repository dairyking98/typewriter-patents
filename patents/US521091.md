## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and particularly to instrumentalities for automatically operating the ribbon shifting mechanism to reverse the ribbon when the same has reached the end of any desired point in its travel.

**How it works (reading):** Stops on the ribbon trip a lever that raises a trip arm into the path of the carriage, which then throws the ribbon shifting bar.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must watch for the end of the ribbon to reverse it, and keys get struck without leaving an impression when the ribbon runs out.

**Solution:** Stops on the ribbon trip a lever that raises a trip arm into the path of the carriage, which then throws the ribbon shifting bar.

**How it works:** On a Caligraph, a stop fixed on the ribbon strikes trip arms on a counterbalanced shaft when the ribbon end approaches. This raises an elevating arm that lifts a trip arm, joined to the shifting bar, into the path of a projection on the carriage. When the carriage next moves to begin a new line it strikes the trip and moves the shifting bar to reverse the ribbon. A spring holds the shifting bar at its limit, and a stop released by the ribbon stop lets the spring reverse it.
