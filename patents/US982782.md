## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check marking apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to apparatus for marking checks and is an lmprovement upon 'v the apparatus shown and described in U.

**How it works (reading):** The type wheel is carried on a pivoted carrier set close to the base and driven by a segmental gear, so the type strikes the check with more power.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A check-marking machine needs more printing force, a more compact form and better sensitivity.

**Solution:** The type wheel is carried on a pivoted carrier set close to the base and driven by a segmental gear, so the type strikes the check with more power.
