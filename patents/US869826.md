## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon with several colours or characteristics needs a simple way to bring any chosen section to the type and to feed the ribbon so every portion is used.

**Solution:** A shifting frame positions the chosen ribbon section, held by a locking spring, with an automatic lateral feed driven from the carriage ratchet.

**How it works:** On a No. 6 Remington, the carriage drum's pawl turns a ratchet wheel only when the carriage travels forward, not on return, so the ribbon feeds uniformly. A bevel gear and spider on the wheel drive a cross shaft whose sliding direction alternates the spool winding (Webb patent 599,428). A shifting frame brings a chosen colour section to the type and a locking spring holds it, while the spider works a lateral feed within that section. Details of the final mechanism are beyond the excerpt. This is a division of the application behind US863697.
