## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving mechanisms, e.g. springs stressed during carriage return

**How it works (reading):** A longer-stroke spring band wound so coils do not touch, with the outer end on a radially displaceable point, works without lubrication and is about half to two-thirds shorter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage spring coils rub and need oil, which turns resinous, and the outer end is stressed by the tight coil.

**Solution:** A longer-stroke spring band wound so coils do not touch, with the outer end on a radially displaceable point, works without lubrication and is about half to two-thirds shorter.

**Features:** [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** spring housing; radially displaceable end point
