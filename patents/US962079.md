## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to type bar hangers, and the main object of the invention is to provide simple and eflicient means whereby the bearings for the type bars may be readily adjusted while the type bars and hangers are in place in the machine.

**How it works (reading):** Type bar hangers have conical bearing projections and an adjusting screw with lock nut that a tool turns in place to adjust the arms.
