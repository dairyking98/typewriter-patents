## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen overthrows or underthrows when the line-space lever is violently operated, and the platen needs a reliable connection to the line-space wheel that can still be disconnected easily.

**Solution:** An internal gear on the line-space wheel meshes with pinions on arms on the platen axle and on a loose arm; ratchets on the pinions are held in mesh by a spring to lock them, and a finger piece separates them so the platen can be turned independently.
