**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen carriage needs a reliable actuating mechanism that can be released by hand so the carriage moves freely in either direction.

**Solution:** A pivoted rack on the platen carrier is released from its pinion by a finger lever, letting the carriage move freely; a pawl frame driven by the spacing lever and key levers steps the carriage one space at a time under spring power.
