## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets are smudged when inserted again under the carbon, because the carbon rests on the platen where paper must slide.

**Solution:** The carbon sheet or web is held fixed over the flat platen, out of contact with it, leaving a gap that lets paper sheets be slid in edgewise.
