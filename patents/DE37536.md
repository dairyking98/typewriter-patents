## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix setting and typewriter stamp carrier) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** The stamp carrier is made as a frame, and punches lie in a circle on a wheel rim or segment; transport can be reversed and stopped automatically at the end of the line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The stamp carrier of the prior patent was too long for the required arrangements.

**Solution:** The stamp carrier is made as a frame, and punches lie in a circle on a wheel rim or segment; transport can be reversed and stopped automatically at the end of the line.
