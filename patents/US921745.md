## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper finger on a Monarch typewriter, the guide and feed roll that hold paper to the platen, needs an improved design.

**Solution:** A paper-finger carrier clamps on a grooved bar by a spring, with a spring-pressed paper guide and a separate pivoted feed roll with its own spring.

**How it works:** A bar above the platen, slightly behind its axis, has a longitudinal groove. A carrier hub embraces the bar and holds a U-shaped bar spring that enters the groove, preventing sliding and rotation while still allowing adjustment. A forward arm carries a paper guide pressed against the platen by a coiled spring hidden between ears. The arm's front end pivots the feed-roll body on a pin; a spindle holds a sleeve with flanges and a rubber sheath forming the feed roll, pressed by a coil spring and plunger. The feed roll can be swung to inoperative position.
