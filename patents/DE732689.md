## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A space bar hung on the outside of the frame loosens its joints when the machine is lifted by its side arms.

**Solution:** The housing carries forward-drawn side arms that cover the space bar arms, and the space bar is linked by a rearward extension to a transverse lever near the key switch.
