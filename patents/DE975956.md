> Description Translated from German There are switching devices for the car of typewriters known, the different sizes, in particular bring about the corresponding carriage shifting steps for the different type widths. Opposite to the known switching devices for constant carriage step have such as proportional Car step switching devices that have become known have the advantage of a better, printed font-like Typeface in which types of small width only take up a small amount of space, while the types of greater width are assigned a correspondingly larger space. In one of the known devices of this type, two racks are on the carriage of the typewriter different division provided, which by means of a linkage alternately with the common Drive gear can be brought into engagement. This facility only enables two different car stride lengths and is also because of the relatively large power requirement required switching only suitable for optional block letters.
