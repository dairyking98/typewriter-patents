## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved device for releasing the carriage of a typewrit-k ing machine from its letter spacing` mecha\.

**How it works (reading):** A release bar forces the spacing pinion out of mesh with the rack bar on the carriage, so the spring-driven carriage can move freely in either direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage is locked to its letter-spacing mechanism, so it cannot be moved freely to insert characters in a printed line.

**Solution:** A release bar forces the spacing pinion out of mesh with the rack bar on the carriage, so the spring-driven carriage can move freely in either direction.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** spacing pinion; rack bar; release bar; spring-driven carriage
