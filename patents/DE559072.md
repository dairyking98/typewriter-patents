## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The auxiliary ribbons are driven by a spring or electric motor triggered by the platen roller, moved faster and past the line stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon-replacement ribbons stuck to the paper when moved by the platen.

**Solution:** The auxiliary ribbons are driven by a spring or electric motor triggered by the platen roller, moved faster and past the line stop.
