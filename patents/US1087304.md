## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copy on a book or billing typewriter is hard to hold in place, and a holder fixed to the machine interferes with the moving writing mechanism.

**Solution:** A spring sheet-metal frame with hooked ends sits over the fixed side bars of the machine and carries rollers on spring brackets that hold the copy paper, leaving the moving mechanism free.
