## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feeler arms riding on the ribbon spools in patent 368,6xx take up space and are awkward to mount.

**Solution:** Reverse the ribbon by buttons near each ribbon end that trip a fork lever and slide to shift the actuator.

**How it works:** A fork lever at each spool embraces the ribbon. A button on the ribbon near its end hits the fork as the ribbon runs out and deflects it. A slider on the fork's lower end pushes an actuator, which makes an angle lever swing out when the universal rail frame next moves. That angle lever shifts the shaft driving the spools to the other spool.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; fork lever; button on ribbon; slider; actuator; angle lever; universal rail frame
