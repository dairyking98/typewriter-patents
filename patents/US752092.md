## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines, such as shelf C and the platen B and is kept against the 0 type-writers, in which a sheet of paper is fed platen by the rollers D.

**How it works (reading):** A shelf beneath the platen is pressed by the operator, and linkage moves the rollers into or out of contact with the platen, with the shelf's own weight keeping them in place.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-pressing rollers had to be thrown into and out of contact with the platen by hand, which was clumsy when releasing the paper.

**Solution:** A shelf beneath the platen is pressed by the operator, and linkage moves the rollers into or out of contact with the platen, with the shelf's own weight keeping them in place.
