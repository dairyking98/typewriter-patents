## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing machine for address plates) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an embossing machine for consisting of thin sheet metal address printing plates or the like.

**How it works (reading):** A key lever above the adjusting wheel is pressed to advance the plate holder automatically one line spacing, and alternative primary gears on each side of the drive shaft give different line spacings.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving the plate holder to the next line of an address plate needs the drive shaft turned by hand one line spacing, which is slow.

**Solution:** A key lever above the adjusting wheel is pressed to advance the plate holder automatically one line spacing, and alternative primary gears on each side of the drive shaft give different line spacings.
