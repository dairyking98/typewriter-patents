## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon actuating mechanism in a typebar machine was assembled piece by piece in the frame.

**Solution:** The entire ribbon feed, reverse and carrier mechanism is mounted self-contained on one sheet-metal plate that is removably attached to the frame.

**How it works:** Ribbon spools on a shaft carry a gear meshing a pinion, then a mitre gear. A sliding cross shaft carries gears that mesh alternately to reverse ribbon direction. Gearing runs to an escapement wheel at the front, whose dogs on a reciprocating rod are moved by the universal bar through a link. A ribbon carrier slides under a guide plate and is reciprocated by an oscillating rod with arms engaging pins on the carrier.
