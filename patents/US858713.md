## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage rebounds when its travel is arrested by the engagement of tabulator stops, and no simple automatic check was provided.

**Solution:** A denominational tabulator stop carrier with an automatically operated device that prevents the carriage from rebounding when the tabulator stops engage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
