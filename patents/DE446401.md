## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator hammer tensioning) · **Subject (patent classes):** Springs

**How it works (reading):** Each hammer spring hangs from a hammer arm and attaches to a transverse axle carried by swinging legs; a stepped disc and levers hold that axle at different distances to set the spring tension.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The total tension of the print hammer springs must be adjustable to the rest position, and existing tensioning devices do not allow this in a simple way.

**Solution:** Each hammer spring hangs from a hammer arm and attaches to a transverse axle carried by swinging legs; a stepped disc and levers hold that axle at different distances to set the spring tension.

**Features:** [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)
