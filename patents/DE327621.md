## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to bearings for in Type, key and intermediate levers guided by slots on typewriters and similar machines, in particular on the bearings of the type levers of typewriters with visible writing.

**How it works (reading):** The bearing body is moulded slightly undersize and then electroplated with a hard metal layer on all sides of the bearing webs, which also forms the stop for the buffer ring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hard guide surfaces for type-lever bearings had to be inserted as stamped sheets in the mould, which cost time and money.

**Solution:** The bearing body is moulded slightly undersize and then electroplated with a hard metal layer on all sides of the bearing webs, which also forms the stop for the buffer ring.
