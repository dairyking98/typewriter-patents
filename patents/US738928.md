## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to devices which require adjustment to diiferent positions, and is particularly applicable to the adjustable stops that are used on type-writers to arrest the carriage at predetermined points, as well as to other attachments it may be desired to adjust lengthwise of the type-writer carriage or its path.

**How it works (reading):** A slide that is a rectangular box fitted over a toothed rack, with notches in its top wall; a spring forces the rack teeth into the notches to hold the slide, and the slide is lifted from the teeth to move it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says adjustable carriage stops and other attachments need a simple way to be set to any position along a rack.

**Solution:** A slide that is a rectangular box fitted over a toothed rack, with notches in its top wall; a spring forces the rack teeth into the notches to hold the slide, and the slide is lifted from the teeth to move it.
