## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to devices which require adjustment to diiferent positions, and is particularly applicable to the adjustable stops that are used on type-writers to arrest the carriage at predetermined points, as well as to other attachments it may be desired to adjust lengthwise of the type-writer carriage or its path.
