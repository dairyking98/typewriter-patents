## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inventor wants a simple, inexpensive, efficient typewriter.

**Solution:** Two stacked type disks on a tilting vertical shaft are rotated by rack frames slid across the machine with finger levers that seat in a notched rack on a tilting letter board; pressing the lever tilts the board and so the shaft, throwing the chosen type onto the paper cylinder and spacing the carriage.

**How it works:** Each of two type disks, each carrying small and capital letters side by side, has a sleeve and gear that meshes with a rack frame on a transversely sliding frame with a finger lever. The operator slides a lever to the letter on the letter board and drops it into a notch of the fixed rack under that letter. The slide thereby rotates the disk to bring that type to the front. Pressing the lever down tilts the pivoted letter board, which through levers tilts the vertical shaft so the disk's type strikes the paper cylinder. Depressing the lever of the second frame lifts the lower disk into the upper disk's place so its types print. A finger board shifts the rack and letter board half a space so capitals come to the print position. Tilting the shaft also swings a dog whose offset teeth step the carriage one notch of its rack, and a spring bell hammer rings near the line end. An ink pad on the disk and a roller ink each type just as it tilts, and a separate bar spaces without printing, limited by a stop so the type does not hit the paper.
