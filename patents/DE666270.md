**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards characterised by means for holding or fastening typewriters or computer equipment

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for releasable Attachment of a typewriter or the like to a typewriter pad, in particular on a swivel base, as is the case with the usual collapsible or lockable typewriter desks are available.

**How it works (reading):** An elastic metal bracket fixed under the front of the machine hooks over one or more upright hook holders on the base, which hold the machine against longitudinal and transverse movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fastening a typewriter to a desk pad needs separate holders on each side, precise placement and changes to the machine or pad, and is hard to remove.

**Solution:** An elastic metal bracket fixed under the front of the machine hooks over one or more upright hook holders on the base, which hold the machine against longitudinal and transverse movement.
