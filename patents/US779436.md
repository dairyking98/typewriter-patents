## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writers and like machines, and has for its object to so mount or support the type-bars that they will move very easily and will be held against lateral movement;

**How it works (reading):** Type-bar hangers or bearings are mounted on a segment in a way that holds each bar against side movement while letting the bars be placed close together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars must move easily, be held against lateral movement, and be set very close together while keeping a strong, rigid bearing.

**Solution:** Type-bar hangers or bearings are mounted on a segment in a way that holds each bar against side movement while letting the bars be placed close together.
