## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon spool must be easily fitted near the receiving reel and be cheap to make.

**Solution:** A bent metal blank with a hook clamp and spring arms whose ends form bearings that enter the spool bore holds the spool beside the reel.
