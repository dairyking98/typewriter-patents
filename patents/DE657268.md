## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card printing business machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for producing a permanent visual presentation of the output data, e.g. computer output printers using printers by matrix printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a printing type controlled by punch cards Business machine for the purpose of training the printing unit for letter printing S to simplify.

**How it works (reading):** Partial letter images are placed as separate part types in sets on the type carrier; card holes select which parts print, and their combination forms each letter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing letters in a card-controlled printing unit required complex letter-forming type sets; the printing unit is to be simplified.

**Solution:** Partial letter images are placed as separate part types in sets on the type carrier; card holes select which parts print, and their combination forms each letter.
