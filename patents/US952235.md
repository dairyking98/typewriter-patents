## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for holding paper, cards and the like upon the cylindrical platens of writing machines, and particularly to that class of devices which may be directly attached to the platen to rotate therewith.

**How it works (reading):** A sprung metal strip shaped to the platen with forked arms and U-shaped spring tongues that catch thin paper or thick cards at the leading edge.
