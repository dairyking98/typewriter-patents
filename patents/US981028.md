## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter key touch must be adjustable across a wide range to suit different operators.

**Solution:** Yielding links between key levers and bell cranks can be swung forward or back together by a handle to vary key resistance and extra dip.

**How it works:** In a front-strike machine, key levers of the second order pull down pendent springy links hooked under studs; each link turns a bell crank connected by a link to a typebar. Swinging the links forward makes the hook catch late so the link bends and the key yields on a sharp blow, giving a lighter or harder touch. Idle links in slots of a bar guided by a rock shaft let one handle with rack adjust all links at once without disturbing the carriage feed.
