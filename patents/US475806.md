## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in the matrix-making machine set forth in my application for Letters Patent filed July 18, 1890, and serially numbered 359,208.

**How it works (reading):** A matrix-carrier rotating with the type-wheel drives an eccentric shaft; the eccentric pushes a plunger onto the dies and draws them back by grippers.
