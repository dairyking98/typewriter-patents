## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on typewriters, machines that move back and forth.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage slides on a guide rail with a play-free sliding fit, and a relief spring along the full path counteracts the carriage weight; a fine adjustment calibrates the compensation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage weight causes excessive friction on long carriages, while roller guides allow shaking and rebound.

**Solution:** The carriage slides on a guide rail with a play-free sliding fit, and a relief spring along the full path counteracts the carriage weight; a fine adjustment calibrates the compensation.
