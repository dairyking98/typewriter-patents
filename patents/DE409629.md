## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With a carriage frame that moves up along an inclined path for the second type set, the overhanging carriage can tilt or sink forward.

**Solution:** An arm on the carriage-shift frame rests on an adjustable stop on the machine base so the position holds.

**How it works:** The carriage frame slides on inclined rails via a lever device. The frame holding the carriage escapement has a thin diagonal arm resting on an adjusting screw, plus screws on the sides contacting the rail at the ends of up and down stroke. Inclined stops limit the stroke exactly.
