**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists of a sound-muflling case or envelope formed of fabric or glass, or in part of each, or of other sound-deadening material.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The clicking noise of a typewriter in operation is annoying, and the operator needs to see and reach the work.

**Solution:** A sound-muffling case of fabric or glass encloses the whole noisy mechanism, with a transparent front and a door or cover giving access to the work, and the soft parts are backed with wood or pasteboard jackets.
