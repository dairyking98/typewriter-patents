## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the switching frame of the paper carriage drops to the lower position, its stops take the braking impact through the joints and bearings, which loosens them and makes lines in the lower writing position no longer straight.

**Solution:** Adjustable support brackets are fixed to the machine frame just outside the outer ends of the switching frame, catching it and the carriage rails in the lower position; they are made with shock-absorbing parts such as springs or fibre flakes to soften the impact.
