## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing several form sheets with carbon copies needs the forms fed and wound automatically, instead of by hand.

**Solution:** Form sheets are fed from a stack into the acute angle between a carbon sheet and guide bands, and a switching device gives the platen several full turns so the forms wind on with carbon copies.
