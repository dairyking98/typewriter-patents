## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Superior characters (exponents) need the paper turned back a definite amount, and the operator needs to do this without losing time or interrupting the typing.

**Solution:** A normally engaged dog or pawl, with a finger-piece, turns the platen backward a fixed distance; a spring restores the platen and paper when the finger-key is released.

**How it works:** A pawl on an arm swinging about the platen axis is actuated by a finger-key through a rod, so pressing it turns the platen back by a set amount and the spring returns it.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen; pawl; arm; rod; finger-key; spring; ratchet wheel
