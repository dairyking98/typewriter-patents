## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies; Curing or drying the ink on the copy materials, e.g. by heating or irradiating; Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my present invention is to supplement the automatic feeding of the supply of paper to typewriting machines by affording means whereby the automatic delivery may be effected;

**How it works (reading):** Supplemental guides added rigidly to the feed guides form a return path that returns the sheet from the delivery guides to the feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic paper feed needs a means to deliver the sheet automatically after printing.

**Solution:** Supplemental guides added rigidly to the feed guides form a return path that returns the sheet from the delivery guides to the feed.
