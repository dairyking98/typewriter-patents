## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved type-bar hanger for typewn'ting machines and the method and means of constructing the same.

**How it works (reading):** The hanger is formed from a blank in a series of press and die operations that form a seat on it, giving it the required fit at the pivot of the type-bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type-bar hanger must fit closely when the type is thrown to the printing point, yet move freely when the type is at rest in the basket.

**Solution:** The hanger is formed from a blank in a series of press and die operations that form a seat on it, giving it the required fit at the pivot of the type-bar.
