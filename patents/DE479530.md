## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter for non-decimal value offsetting)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter for offsetting values that are not based on the decadic number system, in which the introduction of a multi-digit value through addition or subtraction single-digit value parts takes place .

**How it works (reading):** Each place has a drive wheel and its type is arranged so that characters reach the printing position in two different ways, so the carriage steps differently on entering a two-digit value.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Non-decimal values such as pounds, shillings and pence need carries at 12 and 20, not 10, and earlier devices were complicated.

**Solution:** Each place has a drive wheel and its type is arranged so that characters reach the printing position in two different ways, so the carriage steps differently on entering a two-digit value.
