## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (eddy current gear) · **Subject (patent classes):** Dynamo-electric brakes of the asynchronous induction type of the eddy-current hysteresis type; Dynamo-electric brakes of the asynchronous induction type of the eddy-current hysteresis type with a radial airgap; Dynamo-electric brakes of the asynchronous induction type of the eddy-current hysteresis type with an axial airgap

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates in particular to Eddy current gearbox suitable for small machines and aims to regulate the Speed of rotation of a driven device in relation to a drive device to allow within wide limits, as well as the transmission of a constant torque from the drive device to the driven device even in the event of fluctuations to allow the speed of rotation of the drive device.

**How it works (reading):** An eddy-current clutch between drive and driven shafts plus a controllable eddy-current brake on the driven shaft gives fine speed regulation at constant torque.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Regulating small drives to a set speed with steady torque under input speed fluctuations was difficult.

**Solution:** An eddy-current clutch between drive and driven shafts plus a controllable eddy-current brake on the driven shaft gives fine speed regulation at constant torque.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** eddy current clutch; eddy current brake; permanent magnets; copper disk
