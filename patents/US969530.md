## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The escapement load and ribbon lift both fall at the start of the key stroke, making the key heavy.

**Solution:** Operate the escapement from the draw bar so the ribbon is fully lifted first and the escapement load comes at the end of the stroke when the typebar has momentum.

**How it works:** Each key lever prongs a hinged universal bar, whose arm pulls a draw bar forward. The draw bar's pin works a slotted dog arm that turns a ratchet wheel on the ribbon feed shaft, which drives the ribbon through bevel gearing. A cam at the draw bar's rear end rides under a cross rod on a pivoted lift plate and raises the ribbon holder early in the stroke. The escapement dogs are then actuated by a member carried on the draw bar, moved only at the end of its travel, so a slight movement at the end of the stroke releases the carriage.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** key lever; universal bar; draw bar; dog arm; ratchet wheel; feed shaft; ribbon feed wheel; lift plate; ribbon holder; cam; escapement dog; typebar

**Improves on:**

- escapement actuated at start of key stroke: load moved to the end of the stroke
