## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Narrow cards must be held firmly against the platen in various lengths and be easy to fit to standard typewriters.

**Solution:** An adjustable card holder that clamps cards of varying length against the platen and attaches to typewriters such as the Underwood.
