## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a further embodiment of the duplicating device forming the subject of the main patent for typewriters, in which the pivot bearings for the pivot axes of the additional Ribbons stretched carrying arms merge into sliding guides in which the pivot axes can be pushed in so that the additional ribbons are in the working position reach.

**How it works (reading):** Concentric ring groove and detachable ring rim lock the pivot axes of the additional ribbon arms so they cannot slide into the guides; notched handle knobs snap-latch the upward rest and downward working positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Additional ribbon arms on the duplicating device slide out of their bearings unless secured.

**Solution:** Concentric ring groove and detachable ring rim lock the pivot axes of the additional ribbon arms so they cannot slide into the guides; notched handle knobs snap-latch the upward rest and downward working positions.
