## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punched-tape drives for typewriters used intermediate rails and linkages.

**Solution:** Rails controlled by the punched tape act directly on the key levers, via adjustable screws, with the pawls lifted free of the tape after use.

**How it works:** A pawl on each swinging rail rests on a perforated strip passing over a constantly turning roller with depressions. When a hole passes, the pawl drops into a depression and the roller swings the rail sideways under a continuously reciprocating common pressure plate, which pushes the rail down so its adjusting screw presses the key lever. Springs return it and a protective plate lifts the pawl out of the hole.
