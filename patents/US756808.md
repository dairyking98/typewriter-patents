## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanisms for visible-writing machines were complex, bulky, prone to twist the ribbon, hard to dismantle for ribbon changes, and could reverse accidentally.

**Solution:** A simplified automatically reversing ribbon drive with crown ratchets on the spool shafts driven by pawls from the universal bar, with a lifting guide that raises the ribbon at each stroke.

**How it works:** Two ribbon spools sit on vertical shafts in protecting shells, with square shaft tops fitting the spool hubs. Crown ratchets with upward teeth at the lower shaft ends drive the ribbon in one direction or the other. Pawls worked by the universal bar turn one ratchet or the other with a regular, even pull. Lifting guides and levers raise and lower the ribbon to the print position at the moment of each impression, so the writing is visible.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; spool shaft; crown ratchet; pawl; universal bar; ribbon-lifting guide; protecting shell; return spring
