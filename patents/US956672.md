## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to line lock and alarm and margin stop mechanism and the main object of my invention is to provide improved mechanism of the character specified.
