## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Frames and carriages of typewriters should be strong, light and attractive, and easy to manufacture, with the type bars supported on the frame.

**Solution:** A frame stamped from a single piece of sheet metal in which only the transverse or bracing bars are made separately and attached to the side walls; the frame also guides the platen-shift mechanism and supports the escapement.

**How it works:** Approximately the entire frame is one stamped sheet metal piece. Transverse bracing bars are separate parts attached to the sides of the machine frame. The frame is shaped to carry the type bars, guide part of the platen shift, and support part of the escapement mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Frame and body:** [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** frame; side wall; transverse bars; carriage; platen shift guide; escapement support

**Improves on:**

- cast or separately assembled frames: one stamped sheet metal piece with separate bracing bars
