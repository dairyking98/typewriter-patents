## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable rotative block with stop surfaces of different radius arrests the ribbon vibrator, and the shift rail and link system drives the vibrator from the type-bar operating links.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator needs to be moved by different amounts, and the existing arrangement does not give a simple adjustable stop for each extent of movement.

**Solution:** An adjustable rotative block with stop surfaces of different radius arrests the ribbon vibrator, and the shift rail and link system drives the vibrator from the type-bar operating links.

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
