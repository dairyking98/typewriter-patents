## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage and its accessories (platen, spring drum, bell, switching parts) must be removed and reinserted as a unit for changing, setting or repair, which the earlier layout does not allow easily.

**Solution:** The carriage switching gear sits on a movable rack in the machine frame and on a detachable frame that carries the carriage with its switching devices; the two frames are joined by a simple screw connection so the whole assembly lifts out and back without re-adjusting the parts.
