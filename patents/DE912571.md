## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**How it works (reading):** A resiliently mounted stop with a metal piece covered by a continuous rubber layer, with adjustment in the direction of lever travel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber stops for type levers either make impact noise or fail to stop the lever safely.

**Solution:** A resiliently mounted stop with a metal piece covered by a continuous rubber layer, with adjustment in the direction of lever travel.
