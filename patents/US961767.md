## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewriting machines, and particularly to an improved platen brake for fractional line spacing.

**How it works (reading):** A brake spring on the platen shaft bears on a free disk and the platen head, so friction holds the platen in any position and a holding pawl is disengaged for turning.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen cannot be held in a set position for fractional line spacing without slipping.

**Solution:** A brake spring on the platen shaft bears on a free disk and the platen head, so friction holds the platen in any position and a holding pawl is disengaged for turning.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
