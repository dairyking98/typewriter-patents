## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The operator could not return the carriage a chosen distance within a line while also rotating the platen, because no key-controlled means existed for it.

**Solution:** Keys act through contact tips, a notched cross rod and a locking rod to stop the carriage at a selected position, and a separate key rotates the platen without returning the carriage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** contact tips; key bar; locking rod; notched cross rod; stop selecting lever
