## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The rigid metal stop for type levers is too noisy when they strike.

**Solution:** The anvil is a hollow tube of two springy wound wires embedded in the segment groove, which damps impact noise without loss of print sharpness.
