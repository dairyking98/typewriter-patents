## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriting machines known as bar machines, and has for its objects, rst, to provide a cheap,durable, and efficient machine;

**How it works (reading):** The number of type-bars is reduced; each bar's headpiece carries about nine characters in three groups, and the bars are loosely joined to swinging levers in a jointed parallelogram so they move freely toward and from the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bar machines need many type-bars, and the outer bars do not strike the platen squarely.

**Solution:** The number of type-bars is reduced; each bar's headpiece carries about nine characters in three groups, and the bars are loosely joined to swinging levers in a jointed parallelogram so they move freely toward and from the platen.
