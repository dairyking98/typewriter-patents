## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (envelope feeder for addressing machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding articles to machines by belts or chains, e.g. between belts or chains by belts between a pair of belts forming a transport nip

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention has reference to an apparatus for feeding envelops to envelop-addressing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A chute holds the envelopes, and a traveling band or apron with transverse slots and gussets engages the envelope flaps and carries each envelope to the platen; the band is joined into an endless loop and guided by a deflecting guide.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Envelopes must be fed to an envelope-addressing machine automatically and conveyed from its chute to the platen.

**Solution:** A chute holds the envelopes, and a traveling band or apron with transverse slots and gussets engages the envelope flaps and carries each envelope to the platen; the band is joined into an endless loop and guided by a deflecting guide.
