## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Hall typewriter the carriage escapes one tooth per letter, so wider letters or extra spacing needs extra strokes.

**Solution:** A supplemental spacing key shifts the stops that limit the spring plate's throw so the escapement drops one or two rack pitches.

**How it works:** A spring-drum pulls the carriage along a rack bar. Plates X, Y, Z move up and down with the keyplate; spring plate Z leaves the rack notches on depression and springs sideways until stopped by a shoulder on the supplemental spacing lever, then re-enters a notch. With the supplemental lever pressed, the shoulders are in different positions so the plate lands one tooth further, giving extra space for one finger press.

**Features:** [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** rack bar; spring plate; escapement plate; supplemental spacing key; stop shoulder; spring drum; finger board

**Numbers:** spring plate throw at least two rack pitches

**Improves on:**

- Hall typewriter escapement: adds a supplemental key that widens the escapement step
