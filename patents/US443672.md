## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type need cleaning, and the brush must be moved out of the way without removing the attachment.

**Solution:** An attachment connected to the frame carries a brush over the basket centre and can be swung clear of the type.

**How it works:** Attachment with a brush over the basket centre, swung out of operating position.
