**How it works (reading):** A second transfer link is hinged to the swivel arm with a longer lever arm than the drive link, so a constant drive movement gives an accelerated working stroke; the driven part is released from the driving toothed roller automatically during the stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In power-driven flat-plate typewriters the type-carrier drive runs at nearly constant speed during the working stroke, which overstresses the joints.

**Solution:** A second transfer link is hinged to the swivel arm with a longer lever arm than the drive link, so a constant drive movement gives an accelerated working stroke; the driven part is released from the driving toothed roller automatically during the stroke.
