## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source

**How it works (reading):** A small electric motor with a crank pin pulls the key lever through a slack cord, engaged only when a push button or a contact on the letter pointer fires an electromagnet.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand-struck keys give uneven impressions: some strokes too weak, others too strong, wearing and dirtying the type.

**Solution:** A small electric motor with a crank pin pulls the key lever through a slack cord, engaged only when a push button or a contact on the letter pointer fires an electromagnet.

**How it works:** A motor under the machine plate turns a shaft disc with a crank pin that hits a bell-crank lever every revolution. The bell crank is tied to the writing key lever by a cord with a shock-absorbing spring, but the cord is slack. A tension roller on a second lever, worked by a push button or an electromagnet, takes up the slack so the crank stroke pulls the key. On a type-cylinder machine set by a letter pointer, the space key is removed and a blank position on the cylinder is used instead, so striking it prints nothing but spaces. A contact on the pointer's finger grip can energise the magnet so selecting and printing are one hand motion.
