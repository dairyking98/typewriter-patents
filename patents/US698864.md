## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Turning the platen for line spacing and returning the carriage needs a separate hand motion instead of the operator's knee.

**Solution:** A knee-operated arm pulls a metal ribbon belt that drives a bell-crank lever, which moves a pawl on a ratchet on the platen to space lines and return the carriage.

**How it works:** The operator's right knee presses an arm on a rock-shaft; a belt passes over a sheave to a bell-crank lever whose spring-actuated pawl turns the platen ratchet, with an adjustable stop setting single or double spacing.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet; pawl; bell-crank lever; metal ribbon belt; sheave; rock-shaft; adjustable stop
