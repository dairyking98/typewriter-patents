> Description Translated from German Paper carriage locks for typewriters Paper carriage locks for typewriters are known in multiple designs, in particular those with a mounted on the machine frame, swiveling or in the position of use liftable locking body and a co-operating with it, firmly located on the slide body, which hold the paper slide in the middle of the machine as well as the letter switchgear remove the influence of shocks and the locked position in both directions of movement should back up. The invention now wants to create an easy-to-use device all damage and vibrations by shock or impact, in particular from the finely adjusted keystrokes for the font change, the decisive for the accuracy of the writing, should keep away.
