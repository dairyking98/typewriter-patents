## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and manually actuated

**How it works (reading):** A raised locking body with a receiving slot holds the paper carriage at about half lift so it is locked in the raised position and does not get shocks.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper carriage lock is disturbed by shocks and fine key strokes for font shifts.

**Solution:** A raised locking body with a receiving slot holds the paper carriage at about half lift so it is locked in the raised position and does not get shocks.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
