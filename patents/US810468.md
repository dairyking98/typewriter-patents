## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A marking machine for addressing and price tickets should be simple, efficient, durable and usable without special training.

**Solution:** A type-wheel printed on a carriage travelling on a track; each operation of the type-wheel advances the carriage one step through a feed-dog engaging a feed-rack on the base.
