**How it works (reading):** The movable switching tooth is carried on a slide that is held by a pawl, so it stays in position after the downshift without any secondary movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A movable switching tooth must not move again after the carriage steps, but the usual arrangement lets it run on.

**Solution:** The movable switching tooth is carried on a slide that is held by a pawl, so it stays in position after the downshift without any secondary movement.

**How it works:** The tooth is held in engagement by the slide, which a pawl locks against a stop, and a spring returns the slide.
