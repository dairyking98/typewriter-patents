The invention relates on a key lock for typewriters and calculators with vertically displaceable movable ratchet. It consists in the fact that the movable ratchet tooth on a Slider part is stored, the locking lug of which: cooperates with a locking pawl, that the movable switching tooth is held in its switched position. Through this device it is achieved that the movable switching tooth, when the paper trolley is moved one tooth pitch to the right by the downshift device is moved, remains at the relevant point without any other secondary movement to run, since the fixed movable switching tooth is taking place on site Downward movement is granted.
