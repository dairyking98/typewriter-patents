## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink supply arrangements therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with sarcone pads and type levers that can be rotated around their axis.

**How it works (reading):** The type lever is formed as a tube with a collar at its end and is guided on pins by fork-like caps, with an unequal-leg fork in front of the platen to stop each lever at the ink pad or platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bevel gears on type-lever pins are held only by set screws, so they lose centering and the lever cannot be held against longitudinal slip.

**Solution:** The type lever is formed as a tube with a collar at its end and is guided on pins by fork-like caps, with an unequal-leg fork in front of the platen to stop each lever at the ink pad or platen.
