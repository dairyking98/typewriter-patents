## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A silent typewriter with several stored fonts has type that does not hold steadily at the moment of impact, which weakens the impression.

**Solution:** Fixed type letters are held in place by supports at impact, and levers drop flaps onto the rods so the selected character is positioned for printing.
