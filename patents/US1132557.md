## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (linotype attachment gauge) · **Subject (patent classes):** Measuring instruments characterised by the use of mechanical techniques

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide a gage which is exceedingly simple and cheap in construction and which is composed of a single piece of metal bent to conform to the curvature of the assembling elevator shaft and which is provided with spring members for clamping the same in position thereon.

**How it works (reading):** A simple one-piece bent metal line-measuring gauge is clipped to the assembling elevator shaft in front of the keyboard so the proof can be folded over it to read the required measure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators on a linotype must measure the em length of a proof correction by eye, which is slow and inexact.

**Solution:** A simple one-piece bent metal line-measuring gauge is clipped to the assembling elevator shaft in front of the keyboard so the proof can be folded over it to read the required measure.
