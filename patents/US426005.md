## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Moving the carriage back and forth on a Remington also turns the platen a line each time, an unwanted extra motion for tabular work.

**Solution:** A thumb button beside the carriage-return lever disengages the line-feed pawl so the carriage can be moved freely without line spacing.

**How it works:** The usual feed lever on the carriage lifts the vibrating rack out of the spacing pawls and also drives the feed pawl on the roller ratchet. An auxiliary button on a lever pivoted to the feed lever (or to the carriage) is pressed by the thumb of the gripping hand and, through a link to the tail of the feed pawl, holds it out of the ratchet. The carriage then moves either way without feeding the paper. Intended to cut hand motions.
