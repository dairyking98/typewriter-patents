## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to mechanism for typewriting machines and its general object is to provide improved mechanism of the class stated.

**How it works (reading):** Two distinct feed and holding mechanisms on a platen carriage feed or clamp each sheet independently, or feed sheets together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Several sheets side by side on one platen cannot be fed separately while another is held.

**Solution:** Two distinct feed and holding mechanisms on a platen carriage feed or clamp each sheet independently, or feed sheets together.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
