## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key lever cash register)

**How it works (reading):** A locking device on the total-printing drive makes the drive ineffective until the adder is reset to zero, and the reset is blocked while the cover is open.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After the total is printed, the zero reset of the adder can be skipped, so the register can be used again without a proper zeroing.

**Solution:** A locking device on the total-printing drive makes the drive ineffective until the adder is reset to zero, and the reset is blocked while the cover is open.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)
