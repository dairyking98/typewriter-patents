## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator should stay in correct relation to the platen through shifting, while letting the carriage and shift frame be removed without disturbing the ribbon.

**Solution:** The ribbon vibrator is mounted on a support independent of the shift frame yet moves with the shift via an auxiliary frame.

**How it works:** This is a division of US1038906 and describes the same platen shift frame with an auxiliary spring-sustained frame and bearing roller under the carriage. The ribbon guide or vibrator is carried by the auxiliary arrangement, so it shifts with the carriage and keeps its normal relation to the platen. The vibrator is driven from the universal bar by a lever. Tension springs act between the auxiliary frame and main frame to avoid sliding friction.
