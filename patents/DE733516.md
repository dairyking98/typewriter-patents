## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**How it works (reading):** The fixed switching tooth is flexible enough that the carriage advance drives it briefly, then the switch body swings with force and applies back pressure to the type lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching devices with fixed and loose ratchet teeth take much space and do not use the carriage train to press back on the type lever.

**Solution:** The fixed switching tooth is flexible enough that the carriage advance drives it briefly, then the switch body swings with force and applies back pressure to the type lever.
