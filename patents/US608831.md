## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a typebar machine the carriage must be lifted to inspect the print, the ribbon is not always moved enough to give a fresh surface, there is no way to finish a word after the line lock, and double spacing after a word needs an extra stroke.

**Solution:** A set of carriage improvements: a platen carrier that tips up on a non-lifting carriage to show the print, a ribbon moved sideways by cams on the spring drum and lengthwise by the space key, a line lock with a release key, and a second space key for double spacing.

**How it works:** The paper carriage rides on front and rear guide rods and never lifts; on it sits a shift frame and a platen carrier pivoted on the platen axis, held down by a hook with a handle and tipped up to expose the line, with a spring tooth on the platen ratchet and the feed roller on the carrier keeping the paper in register. A ring of cams on the carriage spring drum pushes a sliding pin that moves both ribbon spools sideways by the width of the ribbon, with a spring returning them, while two pawls on a lever worked by the space key wind the ribbon lengthwise one tooth per space. A hooked lever on the space bar drops with each stroke and is stopped by a pin on the spring drum near the end of the line, locking the keys, and a key lever lifts the hook out of the way to let more letters be struck. A second space key over the normal one, pressed with a letter, sets a stepped stop so the feeding dog escapes two notches, giving double spacing without an extra stroke.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** carriage; guide rod; shift frame; platen carrier; hook; platen; line-space lever; ratchet wheel; feed roller; spring drum; cam ring; sliding pin; ribbon spool; elongated ratchet wheel; driving pawl; space key; feeding dog; holding dog; stepped stop; line-lock hook; release key

**Numbers:** ribbon lasts about ten times as long as when fed per letter

**Improves on:**

- typewriters with a lifting carriage for inspection: the platen carrier tips up without lifting the guided carriage
- ribbon fed per letter or per carriage return: ribbon moves across by spring-drum cams and along once per word, so fresh ribbon is always under the type
