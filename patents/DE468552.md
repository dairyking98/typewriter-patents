## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** A sound-absorbing case, open at the bottom and resting on a felt pad, has guide pieces that catch the machine on lifting and flexible lip-like hoses along lever slots.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A lined sound housing acts as a resonance body and the slots for lever passage leak sound.

**Solution:** A sound-absorbing case, open at the bottom and resting on a felt pad, has guide pieces that catch the machine on lifting and flexible lip-like hoses along lever slots.
