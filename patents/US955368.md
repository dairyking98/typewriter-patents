## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper supply and feeding mechanisms for typewriting machines as embodied in what is commonly known as a tally strip device.

**How it works (reading):** A tally strip feed mechanism is operated by a device mounted on the fixed upper carriage rail.
