## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing work needs the platen positioned without mental calculation, and existing machines lack a way to do this.

**Solution:** An attachment with rails and carriage on the machine frame, finger wheels on the platen shaft, and a line-spacing wheel with pawl that limits platen travel for each new sheet.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
