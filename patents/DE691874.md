## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The universal (all-key) rail of a typewriter strikes the resting key-lever drive parts and makes a knocking noise, and the jolt reaches the keys.

**Solution:** A leaf-spring tongue between the universal rail and each drive member cushions the impact.

**How it works:** The universal rail spans the machine width and is carried on levers pivoting on a shaft. Each key lever drives an intermediate lever whose extension lifts the rail. A leaf spring clamped on the rail on one side meets the rising neighbouring lever, absorbs the shock and slides over a curved rail surface. This damps noise and the jolt felt in the keys during fast typing.
