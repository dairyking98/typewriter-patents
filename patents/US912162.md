## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon for calculating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to improvements in ribbon mechanism for type printing machines and 'is particularly designed for use with the recording devices of adding machines.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon spools are carried on detachable shafts and the ribbon runs straight across the printing type path, with automatic reversal of its direction when fully unwound from either spool.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon for adding-machine recording must be fed across the type path in a straight line, and it must reverse by itself when one spool empties.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools are carried on detachable shafts and the ribbon runs straight across the printing type path, with automatic reversal of its direction when fully unwound from either spool.
