## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The pressure-roll mounting on the Underwood platen frame is complicated and costly to make.

**Solution:** Front and rear pressure rolls hang on rock arms pressed against the platen by leaf springs, and a key-operated rock shaft with cams releases both springs so the rolls move away from the platen.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
