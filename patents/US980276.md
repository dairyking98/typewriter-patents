## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Stenographic typewriter ribbons need simple feeding and automatic reversal.

**Solution:** A universal bar under the key levers drives pawls on two ratchet reels, with a manual shift rod to choose which reel is driven.

**How it works:** Any key depression moves a universal bar, which through rods and a shaft oscillates an arm carrying two pawls. Each pawl engages a ratchet on one reel shaft via bevel gears, but only one pawl is engaged at a time while the opposite holding dog is lifted. Two sliding rods with cams, moved by knobs on the frame sides, shift the pawls and dogs to reverse direction. A spring roller in recesses holds the rods in position. Reels can also be wound by hand cranks.
