## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars need to be inked as they swing up to print, without a cup or ink-well.

**Solution:** Stacked flexible felt pads with radial slits and ink-absorbing washers on an adjustable stem, mounted where each type brushes the underside of the pad.

**How it works:** A stem fixed to the frame by a bearer carries a circular plate on a collar, absorbent washers, and one or two slit felt pads clamped by a thumb-nut. The pads are placed so the slits of one do not line up with the other, so a type passing through a slit in one meets solid felt in the other. Each rising typebar presses the flexible pad aside and picks up ink; the nut sets pressure and ink flow toward the pad edge.
