## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** The subject of the present invention is a form of journal-bearing for the lifting-roller by which it is possible that two shift-keys 20 can be arranged one on each side of the machine, so that the roller can be lifted'by each shift-key independently of the other and at the same time an exact printing of the upper letters of the type is afforded.

**How it works (reading):** A lifting roller carried in open-ended slotted forks of two overlapping levers lets either of two shift keys, one each side, raise the platen frame evenly.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Visible-writing typewriters raise the platen frame for case shift, but a single shift key side and uneven lift give inexact upper-case printing.

**Solution:** A lifting roller carried in open-ended slotted forks of two overlapping levers lets either of two shift keys, one each side, raise the platen frame evenly.

**How it works:** The platen sits in a frame of sliding bearings joined by a connecting bar. A pressure roller beneath the bar rests in the overlapping forked ends of two pivoted levers, each moved by a rod from a shift key on its own side. Pressing either key raises its fork and so the roller and platen frame, while a spindle with arms grasping pins on the bearings keeps the platen horizontal. Springs on the levers balance the platen weight so little force is needed.
