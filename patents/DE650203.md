## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holding, feeding, or guiding means therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The main purpose of the present invention is that which is already in use Copy devices of the usual type with a base plate and two clamping rails for Journal and account sheets can also be used for ribbon transcripts without further ado can, but also to simplify the handling, to facilitate the booking and the accounting and organizational design of the forms to give greater leeway than is the case with known facilities.

**How it works (reading):** A thin intermediate plate has a ribbon bridged over a corner cut-out, with a support arm on the left edge, so the existing copy device with clamping rails can use a ribbon without modification.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon copying on ledger devices needs a ribbon moved by hand across the sheet, or a slider on the right that is uncomfortable and cannot take wide forms.

**Solution:** A thin intermediate plate has a ribbon bridged over a corner cut-out, with a support arm on the left edge, so the existing copy device with clamping rails can use a ribbon without modification.
