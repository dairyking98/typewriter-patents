## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**How it works (reading):** Typebar machine with key levers of different projection lengths to give three letter spaces, and a change key giving two types per key.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letters need different space widths; typewriter construction should be simplified.

**Solution:** Typebar machine with key levers of different projection lengths to give three letter spaces, and a change key giving two types per key.

**How it works:** Key levers stamped from sheet metal act on intermediate levers mortised on a rod; a change key moves the intermediate levers so each key works upper or lower group types. Projections of different lengths on the key levers give three spacings. Spring-return keys strike a felt strip.
