## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing unit, booking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Springs

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing unit for booking and similar bookkeeping machines.

**How it works (reading):** A rubber ring replaces the steel spring in the resilient coupling between the hammer and drive shaft, so the hammer strikes sharply without rebound.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Steel springs on print hammers cause rebound, blurring the impression.

**Solution:** A rubber ring replaces the steel spring in the resilient coupling between the hammer and drive shaft, so the hammer strikes sharply without rebound.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md)
