**How it works (reading):** A toggle lever moves the pressure links that press the ratchet wheel's conical bore against the platen's cone; the links are slightly resilient and a stop prevents the toggle from buckling past dead centre, so the coupling holds in engaged or released positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-ratchet coupling of a platen is operated by a hand bumper and must be held by hand in either position.

**Solution:** A toggle lever moves the pressure links that press the ratchet wheel's conical bore against the platen's cone; the links are slightly resilient and a stop prevents the toggle from buckling past dead centre, so the coupling holds in engaged or released positions.
