## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator is distracted by the keyboard, and the operator must sit in the proper position relative to the machine.

**Solution:** A flexible dark cloth web attached over the keyboard, with a wire yoke that detaches when the operator leans too far away.
