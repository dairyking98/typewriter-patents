## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Same ink pad aim: durable pad that supplies ink gradually.

**Solution:** Annular ink basin with a screw piston that squeezes ink to the cushion.

**How it works:** The hollow-cylinder basin has a threaded annular piston; pressing it down forces ink through channels to the top of the cushion, which diffuses it. An annular funnel catches overflow. The cushion is spongy matter covered with fine fabric where it contacts the type.
