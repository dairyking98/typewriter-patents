## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Japanese tray or drum typewriters need a type-gripping action that is simple and sure and does not let the type slip or shift when printing.

**Solution:** Fixed type cases form a rotary drum covered by index plates; the key tips the plate of the wanted case forward to uncover its types, a gripping piece clamps the type, and a skewer on a separate pivot threads through the type during the swing so it prints without slipping.

**How it works:** Type cases with rows of holes, each covered by a lettered index plate, are held between side plates to form a drum turned by a handle shaft. The printing key is slid sideways until a pointer shows the wanted index character, then pressed. Through a cam and levers it lifts that index plate and a guide piece tips it forward, uncovering the type row, while the gripping piece's enclosure comes down over the wanted type and a cover piece closes to clamp it. The gripping piece then swings up toward the paper cylinder; a roller on it carries a pivoted skewer plate along so that, because the two pivots differ, the skewer enters a hole in the type and holds it through the swing to the print.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a Japanese-script typewriter characterised as follows. Many index plates are set side by side round a rotary type drum, covering the rows of types set in the drum's surface. When a plate is moved outward from the drum by pressing the printing key, a guide piece fixed at the side of the drum tips it forward out of the way and uncovers the row of types beneath it. A type-gripping piece is mounted so that, on pressing the key, it swings down, grips the wanted type, and then swings towards the paper cylinder. A skewer rod is mounted on a pivot above and behind the gripping piece's pivot so that its front end crosses the gripping piece as both swing towards the cylinder; so, midway through the gripping piece's swing, the skewer passes through the type together with the gripping piece, and the type is printed. The object is a simple machine, easy and sure to work, that prints clearly without the type slipping out or shifting at the moment of printing. Brief description of the drawings.
