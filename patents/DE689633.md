**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on racks for business writing machines that consist of several rack parts are composed.

**How it works (reading):** The frame is made of side parts supporting the engine plus a keyboard-surrounding frame part, butted together and joined on the inside by connecting links.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Frames built in one piece or as stacked parts are bulky to paint and assemble, and earlier two-part frames narrowed the keyboard or spoiled the side appearance.

**Solution:** The frame is made of side parts supporting the engine plus a keyboard-surrounding frame part, butted together and joined on the inside by connecting links.
