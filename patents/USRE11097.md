## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inking ribbons cannot be shipped separately and swapped quickly, so one set of ribbon spools cannot serve many colours.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools with peripheral teeth driven by the machine; a separate metal bobbin carrying the ribbon end is fitted loosely onto a slotted hub and clamped to the ribbon.

**How it works:** Ribbon is carried on a removable bobbin that slides onto the spool hub, so ribbon packets are exchanged while the spool stays on the machine.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
