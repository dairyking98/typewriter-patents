## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple typewriter free from complications, mainly in how the type is operated.

**Solution:** Each key has a pin that drops into a hole in a heart-shaped cam on the type-wheel shaft, turning the shaft and wheel until the pin seats, which stops the wheel with that letter at the print point; further key travel presses the paper roller against the type.

**How it works:** A type wheel with letters and figures projecting from its face sits on a shaft carrying one heart-shaped cam per character. Each cam's neck is at a different angle and has a hole in it. Above each cam hangs the pin of a key, which has the matching letter. Pressing a key brings its pin down on the heart cam, which rotates the shaft and wheel until the pin enters the hole in the neck, stopping rotation with that letter in line. Continued key travel rocks a bar beneath the keys, whose arm moves the impression roller frame up so the paper is pressed against the type, through an ink ribbon between them. A coil spring returns the key and bar. The wheel can be removable for other alphabets, or several alphabets sit side by side on one wheel and are shifted into place by a lever and rack.
