## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (automatic tabulating control for booking machine carriages) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for automatic control of the tabulating movements of the paper trolleys of booking machines.

**How it works (reading):** A tab control device that automatically stops the carriage at the required column positions during the machine's work cycles, so tabulation of variable length is governed by the cycle rather than by key strokes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Booking-machine paper carriage needs column tabulation to stop at variable positions, but existing column-skipping or cam tab devices only handle even letter spacing.

**Solution:** A tab control device that automatically stops the carriage at the required column positions during the machine's work cycles, so tabulation of variable length is governed by the cycle rather than by key strokes.
