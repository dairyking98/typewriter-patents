## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a tensioning device for the key lever springs of typewriters, in which the springs are suspended in a common, adjustable half, and consists in that a rail with prongs is arranged on the holder, ih.

**How it works (reading):** The springs hang from a rail with prongs at different heights on a holder, so one up-and-down adjustment by screws sets the tension of all the springs together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Key lever springs of different lengths each need a separate tension adjustment.

**Solution:** The springs hang from a rail with prongs at different heights on a holder, so one up-and-down adjustment by screws sets the tension of all the springs together.
