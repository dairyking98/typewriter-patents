## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet output section

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines for feeding envelops and other writing-surfaces to a type-writer;

**How it works (reading):** An attachment projects a single sheet or envelope at a time from a supply across the alignment into writing position, and directs written sheets into a basket; it is detachable from standard machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Envelopes and sheets must be fed to a typewriter by hand one at a time and removed by hand after writing.

**Solution:** An attachment projects a single sheet or envelope at a time from a supply across the alignment into writing position, and directs written sheets into a basket; it is detachable from standard machines.
