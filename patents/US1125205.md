## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to'means for releasing the platen from the line-space wheel of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A gear on the line-space wheel meshes with pinions on a fixed arm and a loose arm on the platen axle; friction disks on the pinions, held together by a spring, lock the gear against rotation on the platen axle, and a finger piece separates them for independent platen rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen overthrow at the line-spacing operation, so the platen needs an effective control and a secure connection to the line-space wheel.

**Solution:** A gear on the line-space wheel meshes with pinions on a fixed arm and a loose arm on the platen axle; friction disks on the pinions, held together by a spring, lock the gear against rotation on the platen axle, and a finger piece separates them for independent platen rotation.
