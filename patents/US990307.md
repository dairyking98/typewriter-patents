## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**How it works (reading):** A billing mechanism driven by carriage movement, with a stop-carrying gear and adjustable stop, that counts or indicates billing positions for condensed records.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing requires remembering indices or watching an indicator, which tires the operator.

**Solution:** A billing mechanism driven by carriage movement, with a stop-carrying gear and adjustable stop, that counts or indicates billing positions for condensed records.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** stop-carrying gear; adjustable stop; carrier
