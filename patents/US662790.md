## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon only wears along one line, or a diagonal, so much of its width goes unused.

**Solution:** Shift the ribbon spools axially a small step each time the ribbon feed reverses, so successive passes use parallel stripes across the ribbon width.

**How it works:** Each spool sits on a splined shaft driven by a ratchet and pawl from the typewriter feed; a sliding frame swaps which pawl is active to reverse the ribbon. Transverse sliding bars with arms bear on the spool ends and are tied to a rockshaft. A cam on each spool shifts the spools slightly at every reversal, so the type strikes a new straight line along the ribbon. Shown on an Elliott & Hatch book typewriter with downward-striking typebars.
