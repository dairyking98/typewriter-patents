## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Process for the production of desensitized thorium dioxide in gel form The main patent 588 563 describes a process for the desensitization of thorium dioxide sol, which is characterized by the addition of colloidal or semicloidal carbohydrates. With further work in the field, it has now been found that the desensitized colloidal toroidal dioxide solutions can be prepared in a simple manner in the gel state. These gels are reversible, ie they are able to return to the sol state in the presence of water. Such solutions have the same advantages as those described in the main patent, in particular the insensitivity to electrolytes. For the representation of such solid preparations one can use the usual drying processes, operate with the aid of a vacuum, if necessary. The addition of desensitizers can take place before, during or after the conversion of the sol into the gel state. The second possible embodiment is illustrated by the following examples.
