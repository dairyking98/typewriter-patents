## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shifting the platen on a two-size-type machine takes a hand on the key; the capital shift should be operable by the knee.

**Solution:** A folding angle lever, with a frictional leather-washer joint, clamped on the frame and pulled by knee to hold the platen-shifting key down.

**How it works:** A block clamped to the bottom edge of the frame side piece holds two levers on a pivot with a leather or rubber washer between them. A spring or rod links the free end of one lever to the platen-shifting key. Swinging the second lever out and pressing it by knee pulls the key down and holds the platen shifted during capitals. When not needed the lever folds up against the first and a spring holds it. The friction joint slips under excess force so nothing breaks.
