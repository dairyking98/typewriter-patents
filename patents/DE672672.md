## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erased or faulty lines on transparent reproduction originals are cut out or wiped and pasted over, which leaves edges that show in copies.

**Solution:** A frame hung in place of the original roll, with adjustable side guides, tension springs and a lockable ruler set to the line spacing, lets the correct lines be printed on the original in the right position.
