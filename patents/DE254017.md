## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method for the production of typewriter rollers, furniture rollers o.

**How it works (reading):** Unsized felt discs with a recess on one side and a projection on the other (or cylindrical or conical central recesses) are lined up on a central tube between end plates and compressed, so the outer part of the roller is tighter; the same method applies to furniture casters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text states no clear problem in the OCR; it describes making rollers from felt discs that are stronger toward the axis than at the circumference.

**Solution:** Unsized felt discs with a recess on one side and a projection on the other (or cylindrical or conical central recesses) are lined up on a central tube between end plates and compressed, so the outer part of the roller is tighter; the same method applies to furniture casters.
