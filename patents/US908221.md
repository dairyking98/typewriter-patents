## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved tabulating mechanism for typewriting ma-' chines, and has in view a simple'and practical construction permittin the work of tabu-lating to be easily an effected, with a minimum num nominational keys or selectors.

**How it works (reading):** A denominational amplifier widens the spacing range of each tabulating selector so that fewer keys are needed to locate the carriage at variable distances from a column point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulating mechanism needs one denominational key or selector for each factor in a column group, so many keys are required.

**Solution:** A denominational amplifier widens the spacing range of each tabulating selector so that fewer keys are needed to locate the carriage at variable distances from a column point.
