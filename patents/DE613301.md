## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Aprons or guides for the printing section

**How it works (reading):** Cover plates hang rotatably and fold on the paper trolley rod, and a slot in one plate shows only the line being written.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copying from an accounting sheet into a card risks reading the wrong column, and existing cover plates are cumbersome to move.

**Solution:** Cover plates hang rotatably and fold on the paper trolley rod, and a slot in one plate shows only the line being written.
