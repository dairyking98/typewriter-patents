## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to the carriage feed mechanism of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** The check pawl is adjustable circumferentially on the escapement wheel so it cooperates with any tooth count.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check pawl must be set for escapement wheels with different tooth counts, which differ by type style.

**Solution:** The check pawl is adjustable circumferentially on the escapement wheel so it cooperates with any tooth count.
