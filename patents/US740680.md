## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple and efficient tabulating device is wanted so the carriage can jump to a variable preset point without the impact of the carriage.

**Solution:** Each of ten tabulating keys moves a vertical stop-actuating member that positions a tabulating stop on the frame, locks it rigid to take the carriage impact, and releases the carriage from its escapement.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** tabulating keys; levers; stop-actuating members; tabulating stop; frame; feed mechanism
