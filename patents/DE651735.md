## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lines must be balanced to equal length by hand selection of a tooth row for each line, which is slow.

**Solution:** A movable member set at the line end adjusts the character spacing switch so the line fits the balanced length.

**How it works:** Moving the member to the line end sets the spacing switch to a fixed amount, controlled by the document position.
