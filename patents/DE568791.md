## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Every forced upward carriage movement advances the line, but some work needs the carriage returned to its start without a line feed.

**Solution:** A rack driven by a power coupling moves a tie rod and lever that turn the line-shift lever; the line-shift movement can be made switchable so the carriage lift does not always feed the line.
