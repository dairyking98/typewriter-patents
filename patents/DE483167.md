## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator drive with friction clutch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving mechanisms for functional elements of tabulation devices, e.g. of carriage skip

**How it works (reading):** A reversible electric motor drives the calculating mechanism in one rotation direction and the carriage return in the other, each through a freewheel, with an adjustable friction clutch on the calculating side.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text does not clearly state the defect; it implies that a single constantly driven motor lacks independent control of calculating and carriage-return drives with adjustable braking.

**Solution:** A reversible electric motor drives the calculating mechanism in one rotation direction and the carriage return in the other, each through a freewheel, with an adjustable friction clutch on the calculating side.

**How it works:** Reversing the motor selects which mechanism it drives; freewheels engage only the wanted drive, and the clutch tension sets how much the motor is braked when the calculating mechanism is not released.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** reversing motor; freewheels; worm gear; friction clutch; carriage return shaft; calculating mechanism shaft
