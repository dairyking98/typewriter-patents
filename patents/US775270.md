## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in the scale and pointer mechanism for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A scale and pointer mechanism is supported on the platen cradle with a spring, so paper can be adjusted easily against the scale when the platen is rocked back.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjusting paper against the platen scale is hard.

**Solution:** A scale and pointer mechanism is supported on the platen cradle with a spring, so paper can be adjusted easily against the scale when the platen is rocked back.
