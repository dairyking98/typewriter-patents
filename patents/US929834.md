## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper on forms must be advanced to the next blank line without the operator aligning each line by eye.

**Solution:** Stops connected to the platen are engaged by a detent so the platen arrests its rotation at a series of predetermined intervals, which can be set for different forms.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)
