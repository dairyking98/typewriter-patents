## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing musical notes by hand is slow and imprecise; the note type must be placed on the staff at the right height and length.

**Solution:** A hand-held device with a type wheel whose faces carry note marks, pressed by a button onto an ink pad; a geared rack sets four heights and a linear scale moves the device along the staff lines.
