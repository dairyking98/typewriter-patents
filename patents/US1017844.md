## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon was used only along one strip; it should be usable across its full width, and chosen fields (e.g. two colours) selectable at will, including on a shifting-platen machine.

**Solution:** A vibrator with a stepped slot is engaged by an operating lever that can be swung widthwise by hand to line up with a chosen step, with vibrating devices shifted automatically by the platen shift frame.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 10, the ribbon vibrator has a slot with a stepped upper edge so the operating lever can pick it up at different heights. The lever pivots to vibrate toward and away from the print point and also swings widthwise under hand control to sit opposite one step. The vibrating devices move automatically when the platen is shifted to change case. Ribbon spools are driven alternately through bevel gears from a shaft shifted endwise.

**Features:**

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; stepped slot; operating lever; ribbon spool; bevel gear; driving shaft; shift frame

**Improves on:**
