## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars with a shiftable two-case type head need positive turning to either case and rigid holding for true alignment.

**Solution:** A trigger carried on each typebar turns the pivoted type head to upper or lower case position and locks it there.

**How it works:** Each swinging typebar ends in a bifurcation holding a type head pivoted on a pin, carrying two types at an angle to each other for lower and upper case. A trigger on the bar acts as shifting and locking device. A trip and buffer arrangement associated with the bars adjusts the trigger so the head turns about its axis for capitals and is held rigid for printing. This is the Elliott-Fisher book-type machine with traveling carriage.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:rotary](../features/shift-rotary.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** typebar; type head; pivot pin; trigger; trip; buffer; carriage guide; supporting ring

**Improves on:**

- Fisher patent 569626: positive shifting and locking of the type head
- Fisher patent 708213: reissue of the original
