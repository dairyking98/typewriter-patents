## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**How it works (reading):** Only the two keys for the little fingers, the a and o keys, get a raised marking such as hemispherical glass discs or a grooved edge, so the home position can be found by feel with no other change to the keyboard.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ten-finger touch typists lose the home-row position when they look away to take shorthand or a new line setting, and existing tactile key designs are for blind learners.

**Solution:** Only the two keys for the little fingers, the a and o keys, get a raised marking such as hemispherical glass discs or a grooved edge, so the home position can be found by feel with no other change to the keyboard.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key plates for a and o keys; raised glass discs; key ring
