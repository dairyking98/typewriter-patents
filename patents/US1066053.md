## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Column stops for tabulation must be set into and out of operative position simply and reliably.

**Solution:** Column stop plates are held inoperative by springs and turned into operation by a dog actuated from a key; a rotatable bar with a keyway resets all stops to inoperative positions.
