## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type-making by lead impression) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** Impressions are made into a lead mould plate with steel types on rotating wheels that are moved lengthwise and locked by a snap-in latch; the lead is oxidised and used as a mould to cast a printing plate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Printing plates had to be made from type by hand and the process was slow and uneven.

**Solution:** Impressions are made into a lead mould plate with steel types on rotating wheels that are moved lengthwise and locked by a snap-in latch; the lead is oxidised and used as a mould to cast a printing plate.

> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENTAM In the present invention the impressions are made in lead and very flat. Then on the surface of the lead, by exposing it to the influence of the air, or otherwise formed a thin layer of oxide and used it as a mold to make therein an actual printing plate made of ordinary or more easily fusible type metal to pour. Before the impressions are made in the lead, it is put on some suitable one Soldered back piece or a pad. This form is placed in a holder that can shift sideways, and there are now a number of types of steel in one rotating wheels are attached and can be moved lengthways, the impressions, one by one, made into the shape. All occurring Movements produced by pressing one or more keys on a keyboard. . It should be emphasized that the type wheel can be stopped without the whole machine to stop; that it is secured in the correct position by a snap-in latch; that every dead walk ceases to exist; that no impression is made before the wheel is stopped, and that the keys are arranged in several rows, or whatever else seems most convenient. Depressing the keys and then ascending them again keeps the type wheel at the correct point…
