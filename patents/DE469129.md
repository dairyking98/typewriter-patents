## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is a card guide device for typewriters with finite paper guide fingers and a scale division which coincides with the writing line.

**How it works (reading):** A foldable guide finger forms a continuation of the scale rail, and the scale rail has slotted legs for vertical and sideways adjustment to the writing line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The writing-line scale on the paper guide is too short and fiddly to adjust, which slows machine assembly.

**Solution:** A foldable guide finger forms a continuation of the scale rail, and the scale rail has slotted legs for vertical and sideways adjustment to the writing line.
