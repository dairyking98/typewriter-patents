## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing machine, impressing designs) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers

**How it works (reading):** A machine with a series of printing designs or characters, operated individually and independently, prints onto a prepared plate or stone with a dampened surface that rejects ink.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters do not give sharp enough work for preparing designs for surface printing, such as lithographic plates.

**Solution:** A machine with a series of printing designs or characters, operated individually and independently, prints onto a prepared plate or stone with a dampened surface that rejects ink.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md)
