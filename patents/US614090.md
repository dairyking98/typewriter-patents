## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters do not give sharp enough work for preparing designs for surface printing, such as lithographic plates.

**Solution:** A machine with a series of printing designs or characters, operated individually and independently, prints onto a prepared plate or stone with a dampened surface that rejects ink.

**Features:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md)
