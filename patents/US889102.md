## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to ink ribbonmechanism for use in printing devices and more especially in typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon mechanism that drives the ribbon transversely and longitudinally from a spring drum, with a reversing device for the striped ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An ink ribbon with coloured stripes needs controlled feed both across and along its length, and a reversing device.

**Solution:** A ribbon mechanism that drives the ribbon transversely and longitudinally from a spring drum, with a reversing device for the striped ribbon.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
