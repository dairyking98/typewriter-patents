## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventors want to remedy defects of current machines with a compact design of three characters per key.

**Solution:** Each key lever carries three types (capital, small, numeral) selected by advancing or retiring the paper carriage lengthwise, with type pressed from an ink pad onto the paper.

**How it works:** About 32 keys, spring-supported, connect by links to type-rods set radially in a segment of a circle; the rods tip up from the ink pad then advance to strike the paper held on a curved plate under a rubber-coated roller between two metal strips. Three shift levers rock a bell-crank that moves the carriage back and forth to select case or figures. The carriage travels along the line by a spring drum controlled by the spacing escapement and is returned by hand lever with pawl and ratchet. Three nested frames under the keys space letters and words automatically.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** key; type-rod; ink pad; paper carriage; curved plate; rubber roller; shift lever; bell-crank; spring drum; escapement; spacing frame; metal strips

**Numbers:** 32 keys; 3 characters per type-rod; 3 shift levers; 3 spacing frames
