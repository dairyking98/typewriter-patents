## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check amounts written by hand can be altered.

**Solution:** A check punch perforates letters and numerals in the check using sets of dies on a sliding carriage.

**How it works:** Two sets of dies (letters and numerals) slide in partitions on a carriage to the desired place. Each male die pin sits on a spring-pressed stem and is pushed through the check into a female die by a sliding anvil that is shifted transversely to select the die.
