## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Writing-tables with substantially horizontally extensible or adjustable parts other than drawers, e.g. leaves with sliding or unfolding parts other than leaves or drawers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Verswkvorri.

**How it works (reading):** The machine support plate runs on a horizontal chassis that pulls out forwards on guide strips in the knee space, supported on legs and rollers, with enlarging pull-out plates.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Desk-height lowering devices that swing the typewriter table in and out bounce and do not stand steadily.

**Solution:** The machine support plate runs on a horizontal chassis that pulls out forwards on guide strips in the knee space, supported on legs and rollers, with enlarging pull-out plates.
