## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Protective sheets over the platen slip and wear, so the paper stack can move.

**Solution:** A plastic film with a smooth front and a roughened back grips the platen, with a transparent stop flap.

**How it works:** A single film (or film laminated to a rough paper base) is placed over the platen with the sheets. The rough back gives friction with the platen regardless of platen wear. The smooth front lets paper slide and the soft film cushions the type. A transparent flap shows the paper stop, and a tongue helps pull the pack in. Printing may be put between layers.
