## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a table writing device for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The ordinary tab key's transfer lever is linked to the last decimal tab key's lever, so the ordinary key brings the decimal stop lever into the same column stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A separate ordinary tab and decimal tab cannot share stop positions, so the writer has to reach for a key or hunt among stop levers.

**Solution:** The ordinary tab key's transfer lever is linked to the last decimal tab key's lever, so the ordinary key brings the decimal stop lever into the same column stop.
