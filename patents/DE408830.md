## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register print plate)

**How it works (reading):** A pointer arm on the form frame slides over a fixed row of numbers as the plate is inserted, so the line at the print position is always readable.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cash register operator cannot tell which line of a form lies under the type wheels.

**Solution:** A pointer arm on the form frame slides over a fixed row of numbers as the plate is inserted, so the line at the print position is always readable.
