## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**How it works (reading):** A transparent wedge-shaped rail with a pointed front edge rests on the paper, magnifying the scale under it while guiding the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** An opaque paper-holding rail hides the scale and is hard to read through.

**Solution:** A transparent wedge-shaped rail with a pointed front edge rests on the paper, magnifying the scale under it while guiding the paper.
