**How it works (reading):** Platen sits in a roller frame that can be lifted and reinserted, with a spring-loaded feed roller pressing paper against it and advancing it by line spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen cannot take paper of any width or be swapped quickly for multiple-copy work.

**Solution:** Platen sits in a roller frame that can be lifted and reinserted, with a spring-loaded feed roller pressing paper against it and advancing it by line spacing.
