## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Fixed letter spacing makes writing look uneven because narrow and wide letters take the same space.

**Solution:** Keys are grouped in three sets whose cranked rocking bars drive different pawl strokes on pin wheels, so carriage feed is proportional to character width.

**How it works:** Key levers press on one of three cranked cross-bars by width class. Each bar links to pawl levers that slip over pins on lantern wheels on a lead-screw shaft and, on key release, rotate it by different amounts, moving the paper roller lengthwise. A separate space key and line-space key with ratchet and pawl turn the roller. A conical type guide holds the type aligned on the ribbon.
