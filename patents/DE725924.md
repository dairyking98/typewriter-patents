## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Zero-print switching in cash registers and accounting machines is spread across parallel planes, so the type wheels are hard to reach.

**Solution:** Control disks on a hollow shaft system drive the switching gears that turn the type wheels from blank to zero, in one plane parallel to the wheels.

**How it works:** Control disks on a shaft system switch the wheels from blank to zero in one plane.
