## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates more especially to the adjustment and operation of the platen and its supporting-bed, to the longitudinal adjustment and operation of the type-wheel, to the locking of the typewheel in its exact posit-ion for printing, and to various details of construction.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Improves Myers' earlier wheel typewriters in the platen and its hinged bed, the sideways shifting of the type-wheel, and locking the wheel exactly in position so it cannot overswing or stop short when typed fast.

**Solution:** The depression of a key-lever sets the type-wheel by how far it travels, a notched locking wheel pinned by a bar locks it before printing, and a single pawl on a toothed rack both spaces the platen and, riding up the tooth back, lifts the hinged bed to print.

**How it works:** Key-levers of equal travel press on a hinged operating frame carrying adjustable screw striking-points at different depths below the keys, so each lever turns the frame by a different amount; gearing (rack, pinion, quadrant) turns the type-wheel shaft to the matching angle. The wheel carries three parallel groups of type and slides along its shaft by a flexible, spring-pin lever that clicks into one of three notches in the frame. A spacing frame depressed by every key pulls a spring catch that swings an upright pawl against the face of a rack tooth on the sliding platen frame, moving it one space; the catch is then thrust off by an abutment and a spring snaps the pawl back so it rides over the inclined tooth back, lifting the platen frame and hinged bed against the type. A locking bar, moved by a forked arm on the spacing frame, enters a notch in a locking wheel on the wheel shaft just as the wheel arrives. A check pawl on a rack on the bed prevents the platen drifting back on the return stroke.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md), [Platen pressed to the element](../features/imp-platen-to-element.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type-wheel; wheel shaft; key-lever; operating frame; adjustable screw striking-point; pinion; toothed quadrant; shift lever; locking wheel; locking bar; spacing frame; platen; sliding platen frame; hinged bed; bed spring; spacing rack; spacing pawl; spring catch; abutment; check pawl; check rack

**Numbers:** three parallel type groups on the wheel; three lateral wheel positions; blank gap in type group on the lower side of wheel

**Improves on:**

- Myers applications Serial Nos. 317,689 and 326,277: pinion fixed on the shaft for constant rotation per frame movement, locking wheel added, and one pawl does both spacing and printing
