## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Conventional escapements jar and are noisy.

**Solution:** Escapement with two feed-dogs fixed to each other that move only transversely to the rack travel, so the rack steps smoothly.

**How it works:** The dogs are on a rock-shaft, separated longitudinally and transversely. Rocking moves them across the rack, releasing then catching the next tooth. Fig. 5 shows the dogs when the rack is pulled back suddenly.
