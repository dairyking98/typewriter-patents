## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type wheels disengaged from their drive during printing have to be stopped and re-coupled in position afterward.

**Solution:** Keep the type wheel turning in drive while its carrier moves so the type rolls on the paper with zero relative speed.

**How it works:** A type wheel is geared through two gears to a ring gear on the continuously turning main shaft, all with equal tooth counts so it turns in step with the shaft. A magnet trips a locking lever that engages the wheel's gear shortly before the chosen type passes the print position, and the constant drive then moves the carrier on two control arms toward and along the paper. The wheel thus rolls over the paper, so the printing type is momentarily stationary relative to it and the print does not smear. Toggle action of the joints holds the carrier raised until a return rail releases it, and the wheel is inked by a roller.
