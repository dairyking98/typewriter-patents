## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register two-group printing device)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Printing device for cash registers with two type character groups, of which the one group a check with the line items and their total and the other one Control strips only printed with the sums.

**How it works (reading):** Two pressure rollers side by side guide the check and control strip, and type bars print both in pairs from one drive axle so both are readable at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a cash register the customer's check is only readable after ejection, and the control strip is readable only later.

**Solution:** Two pressure rollers side by side guide the check and control strip, and type bars print both in pairs from one drive axle so both are readable at once.

**Features:** [Straight type bar or rule](../features/elem-linear-bar.md), [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md)
