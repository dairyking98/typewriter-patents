## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Portable typewriters used without a base plate soil the clothes on the knees, and the frame is not rigid enough; the usual plates are heavy.

**Solution:** A light base plate with an insulating felt underside and bolts or rubber inserts holds the machine frame, and a cutout lets it stand vertically to carry.
