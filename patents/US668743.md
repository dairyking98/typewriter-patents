## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The shift key resists the finger because the shifting mass is accelerated at key speed.

**Solution:** Linkage so a quick blow on the shift key lets the shifting mass move promptly without a hard resisting start.

**How it works:** On a Felbel front-strike machine the shift key connects to the shifting typebars or platen through an arrangement that does not couple key speed to mass speed, so a sharp key blow starts the mass moving easily.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** shift key; shifting mass; typebar; linkage

**Improves on:**

- direct shift key to platen connection: decouples key speed from mass motion
