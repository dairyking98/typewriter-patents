## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention pertains to certain new and useful improvements in type-writer attachments, having for its object the provision of simple and efficient means for effecting the raising or disengaging of the rack-bar from the engaging dogs, whereby the carriage can be moved from side to side without operating the paper-cylinder.

**How it works (reading):** A cord or ribbon fixed to the pivoted rack-bar and held by a plate on the carriage, so pulling it lifts the rack-bar from the dogs.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Moving a typewriter carriage sideways without turning the platen needs the rack-bar lifted off its dogs, which is awkward.

**Solution:** A cord or ribbon fixed to the pivoted rack-bar and held by a plate on the carriage, so pulling it lifts the rack-bar from the dogs.

**How it works:** One end of a ribbon is attached to the pivoted rack-bar; the other end is threaded through a plate with two bent arms on the carriage guard-plate. Pulling or pressing on the ribbon raises the rack-bar and disengages it from the escapement dogs. A slotted plate takes up slack, and a hook plate or curved extension are variants.

**Parts:** rack-bar; dog; cord or ribbon; retaining plate; guard-plate; hook plate
