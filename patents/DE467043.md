## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**How it works (reading):** The ridge raised by embossing is kept and bent down to form the lateral fastening tabs, so the type body is shaped from a smaller ingot without the separate burr removal and slotting steps.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stamped type bodies lost their embossed ridge or needed slotting and extra milling to form the fastening tabs, and used more material.

**Solution:** The ridge raised by embossing is kept and bent down to form the lateral fastening tabs, so the type body is shaped from a smaller ingot without the separate burr removal and slotting steps.
