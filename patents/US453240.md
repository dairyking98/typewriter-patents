## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters were costly; the inventor wants a simpler, more economical key machine for producing printed surfaces.

**Solution:** Finger-keys and weighted typebars are strung on two true circular rings so every bar strikes the same point, with circular lever-arms driving a spring-returned rack escapement.

**How it works:** A circular plate on three posts carries arms holding two true-circle rings, a lower one pivoting the typebars and an upper one pivoting the finger-keys. Each key sits immediately behind its bar and tips it toward a common print point; the bar's weighted end returns it. Circular lever-arms, pushed by the keys and returned by a spring, operate a driving lever with a hinged tooth that advances the rack on the paper carriage. Hooked wires adjust tension, and rubber pieces deaden noise.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
