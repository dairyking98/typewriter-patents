## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Conveyor belts or like feeding devices

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in paper feeding devices for type writing machines, and more particularly to improvements in means for holding the paper tightly against the under central part of the platen, and for carrying and adjusting the endless elastic bands which` are in general use for supporting and holding the ends of the paper to be printed tightly against the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Auxiliary roll presses paper to the centre of the platen, with an adjustable carrier for the elastic paper bands.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper does not hold tightly against the central platen; elastic bands are hard to carry and adjust.

**Solution:** Auxiliary roll presses paper to the centre of the platen, with an adjustable carrier for the elastic paper bands.
