## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column stops for tabulating must be set and cleared easily and the carriage must be released when clearing.

**Solution:** A rotating carrier driven by carriage travel carries column stops that spring into and out of position; a carriage-scale pointer shows position.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
