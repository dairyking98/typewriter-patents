## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The margin-gage rack is at the extreme front or inaccessible at the rear, and the ribbon mechanism is awkward.

**Solution:** The margin rack, ribbon spools and carriage spring tension adjuster are placed beneath the carriage and made accessible by one movement.
