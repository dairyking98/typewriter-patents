## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a decimal tab for Typewriters;

**How it works (reading):** The tab key levers overlap and pivot alternately on two parallel axes, and a guide body with locking stops in front of the space key is released by a linkage to the tab riders, so each tab key sets the carriage stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A decimal tabulator on a moving writing carriage needs its tab key levers and stops to work without crowding the keyboard.

**Solution:** The tab key levers overlap and pivot alternately on two parallel axes, and a guide body with locking stops in front of the space key is released by a linkage to the tab riders, so each tab key sets the carriage stop.
