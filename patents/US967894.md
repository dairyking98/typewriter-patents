## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and it has for its principal object to provide an improved escapenient mechanism for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** A dog rocker carrying fixed and loose dogs engages the carriage rack so each key stroke advances the carriage one step; several forms are shown, including one with a straight-toothed rack.
