## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a typebar machine with an ink pad, if the bar rises while its type face is in contact with the pad, the type fills with ink and prints blurred.

**Solution:** A cam-slotted typebar guided by a fixed pivot and a link makes the bar swing back off the ink pad before it rises to the platen.

**How it works:** The key pulls a connecting rod attached to the typebar. The bar has a cam slot near its butt that rides on a fixed pivot and a link jointed to the frame, so the first motion swings the bar away from the ink pad, then it rises and strikes the platen along the printing line. A forked guide receives the bar on the way up to align the type. On release the bar returns to rest against the pad, a stop on the link keeping it from sinking out of contact. The pad sits on the outside of the ink fountain where the bar can reach it.
