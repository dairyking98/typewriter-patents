## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A traveling jacquard (record-carrying) device on a typewriter is hard to assemble and remove.

**Solution:** Mount the traveling jacquard so the assembled apparatus slides into and out of the machine framework, with guides and resetting pawls.

**How it works:** The jacquard is a traveler recording indications at one point which later control the typearms through tumblers. Actuating means advance its upper and lower runs differentially. Resetting pawls restore the tumblers after they have actuated the type-arms, and guides support the traveler.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** jacquard; tumbler; type-arm; resetting pawl; guide; key-lever; key
