## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being perpendicular to the axis of rotation

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to Jacquard mechanism, and especially to a Jacquard mechanism adapted for use in connection with a keyoperated apparatussuch,for instance, as a type writing machineand by means of which mechanism indications may be selectively recorded on a jacquard and the latter operated to actuate a driven apparatus controlled by the indications so recorded.

**How it works (reading):** Mount the traveling jacquard so the assembled apparatus slides into and out of the machine framework, with guides and resetting pawls.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A traveling jacquard (record-carrying) device on a typewriter is hard to assemble and remove.

**Solution:** Mount the traveling jacquard so the assembled apparatus slides into and out of the machine framework, with guides and resetting pawls.

**How it works:** The jacquard is a traveler recording indications at one point which later control the typearms through tumblers. Actuating means advance its upper and lower runs differentially. Resetting pawls restore the tumblers after they have actuated the type-arms, and guides support the traveler.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** jacquard; tumbler; type-arm; resetting pawl; guide; key-lever; key
