## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage runs freely during a carriage release without any control of its speed, so the carriage moves too fast at the end of its run.

**Solution:** Retarding devices mounted on the upper part of the machine, in a casing with operating parts, act on the carriage during a free run to slow it down.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
