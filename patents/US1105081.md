## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanism of a stenotype machine must reverse accurately and without strain or derangement.

**Solution:** A ratchet-driven clutch sleeve feeds either reel, shifted by hand or automatically by spring-loaded rods when a hinged hub section opens as the ribbon runs out.

**How it works:** A dog on the universal bar turns a ratchet wheel fixed to a sliding sleeve with clutch teeth at both ends, which couple to short shafts and bevel pinions driving the two vertical reel shafts. A yoke holds the ratchet and its operating arm, centred between two spring-loaded rods with rollers riding on the reel hubs. When the ribbon is exhausted, a spring-hinged section of the hub opens, a roller enters the recess, and the unbalanced spring shifts the arm, yoke and sleeve to the other clutch. A spring dog in notches holds the arm otherwise.
