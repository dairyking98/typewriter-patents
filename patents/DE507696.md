**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (combined typewriter and calculator)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Typewriter, which, in addition to the addition, also performs the multiplication and has visible pressure.

**How it works (reading):** Typebar levers strike at a fixed stop on the platen while rake-type rods are guided in a bearing, with multiplier setting wheels and cam disks driven from the arithmetic keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing and calculating are combined in one machine that also multiplies and prints visibly.

**Solution:** Typebar levers strike at a fixed stop on the platen while rake-type rods are guided in a bearing, with multiplier setting wheels and cam disks driven from the arithmetic keys.
