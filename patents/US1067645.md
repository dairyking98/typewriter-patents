## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of this invention is to provide improved "and simplified mechanism for indicating that the lower edge ofthe work sheet has nearly reached'the printing line.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return after the sheet has been advanced past the bottom might overwrite.

**Solution:** A paper-controlled trip locks the carriage against return once the bottom edge passes it.

**How it works:** A latch operated by the paper edge locks the carriage when the bottom of the sheet passes the trip; the letter-spacing rack lever releases it. It works in both shift positions.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** carriage; latch; paper trip; shift rail; rack; escapement wheel; dog
