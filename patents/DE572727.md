**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a motor-driven trolley elevator for typewriters, where there is a coupling between the power source and the carriage is provided, which is influenced by a spring in the sense of the dome, but usually is held in the uncoupled position by a latch, from which it is pressed down a return button can only be brought into effect when the car has a has covered a certain distance of its switching path.

**How it works (reading):** Two pivoting control levers, one parallel to the carriage and one perpendicular, carry a pawl and a roller on the carriage stop; the levers move the clutch control part and the pawl holds the clutch open until the return button is pressed and the carriage has travelled far enough.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The motor clutch for the carriage elevator is held out by a latch that the return button must release, and the release only works once the carriage has covered part of its travel.

**Solution:** Two pivoting control levers, one parallel to the carriage and one perpendicular, carry a pawl and a roller on the carriage stop; the levers move the clutch control part and the pawl holds the clutch open until the return button is pressed and the carriage has travelled far enough.
