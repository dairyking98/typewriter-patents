## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon switching in time recorder)

**How it works (reading):** A lever carries a control pin, and a pawl moved by each downstroke of the pressure lever turns the lever by the pin position, so the drive switches to the other ribbon roll when the first roll fills.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon on work-time clocks must alternate between two rolls for even wear, instead of wearing one roll out.

**Solution:** A lever carries a control pin, and a pawl moved by each downstroke of the pressure lever turns the lever by the pin position, so the drive switches to the other ribbon roll when the first roll fills.
