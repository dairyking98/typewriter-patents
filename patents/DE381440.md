## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Dummy practice keyboard apparatus

**How it works (reading):** A spring-supported key frame with unlabelled keys drives pointers that show the letter behind each key on a character field, so learners see characters without looking at keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Learners looked at the keys and then at a character display, which slowed their typing and was confusing.

**Solution:** A spring-supported key frame with unlabelled keys drives pointers that show the letter behind each key on a character field, so learners see characters without looking at keys.
