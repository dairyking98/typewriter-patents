## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers on bank books or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to associate with the track-carrying frame and the tracku'ails for the type-carriage a positive and reliable form of line-spacing mechanism controlled by an actuating or operating device ou the type-carriage, whereby the linespacing mechanism is placed under the immediate control of the operator.

**How it works (reading):** A book typewriter with a vertically and horizontally adjustable frame carrying track rails, line spacing and typebar action with shift reversal.

## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Book typewriters need height and page adjustment, accurate line and letter spacing and visible writing.

**Solution:** A book typewriter with a vertically and horizontally adjustable frame carrying track rails, line spacing and typebar action with shift reversal.

**How it works:** A support frame adjusts vertically to book thickness and horizontally between pages. Track rails for the type carriage slide longitudinally for line spacing, and the carriage travels transversely for letter spacing, via star wheel and dogs. Typebars adjust vertically for case, with a reversing device for normal case.

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
