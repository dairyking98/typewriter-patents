## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the switching device for the paper trolley of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The ratchet pawl sits on a slide that lifts it out of the two racks on the rightward move and re-engages on the return; the rack rail is shifted by one letter space, and the pawl slide can drag to signal the lower edge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The pawl dragged on the carriage racks while the carriage moved right, causing creaking, wear, and no clear signal at the paper's lower edge.

**Solution:** The ratchet pawl sits on a slide that lifts it out of the two racks on the rightward move and re-engages on the return; the rack rail is shifted by one letter space, and the pawl slide can drag to signal the lower edge.
