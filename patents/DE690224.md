**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stacks of sheets attached to a carrier strip or web

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets in form sets shift and cannot be strung into endless chains.

**Solution:** Carbon sheets and writing sheets are glued or perforated at their edges so the sets hold and separate cleanly.
