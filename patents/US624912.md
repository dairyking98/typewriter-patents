## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to devices which are removably affixed to type-writers to keep the copy in view of the operator while engaged in writing.

**How it works (reading):** A clamped bed-plate on adjustable cross-bars and a standard grips the rear of the machine frame so the copy sits in view and can be swapped.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copy must stay in view of the operator during writing and be easy to change, and the holder must be cheap and easily fitted.

**Solution:** A clamped bed-plate on adjustable cross-bars and a standard grips the rear of the machine frame so the copy sits in view and can be swapped.
