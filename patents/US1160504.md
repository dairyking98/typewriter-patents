## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil cards for addressing machines are typed with the ordinary ribbon disabled, so the impressions are not inked and cannot be read or corrected.

**Solution:** A card holder rocks with the platen shaft for line spacing while a broad ribbon runs around a free cylindrical platen behind the card, advanced by a one-way ratchet and gravity pawl.

**How it works:** A cylindrical platen turns freely on the platen shaft, with a ratchet wheel on its end. A gravity pawl on a collar fixed to the shaft engages that ratchet only on the forward turn. Arms on the shaft carry a U-shaped card holder with spring clips up and down in fixed guides, so forward turns raise it a line. The forward turn also moves the platen and the broad ribbon around it, bringing fresh ribbon to the print point. Backward turns slip the pawl while paper feed rolls hold the platen still. Stops with the usual holding pawl locate the top line and limit travel.
