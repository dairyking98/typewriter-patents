## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Laundry marking with a typewriter needs a holder that does not disturb the margin stops and avoids over-writing.

**Solution:** A holder that clips onto the carriage margin-stop rail, with replacement stops and holders that set the printing area for labelling collars.
