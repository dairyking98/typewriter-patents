## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** The angle arm of the ribbon lever carries notches matching each deflection position, and the drive member from the switching bridge seats directly in the chosen notch, so no slot guide is needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjusting the ribbon fork stroke needs special notch settings and slot-guided drive parts that wear, bind and are imprecise.

**Solution:** The angle arm of the ribbon lever carries notches matching each deflection position, and the drive member from the switching bridge seats directly in the chosen notch, so no slot guide is needed.
