## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Preventing rebound or clash of levers or type members

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to what is known as line-locking mechanism, or means for preventing the types from printing when the end of a predetermined line or amount of carriage travel has been reached, and has for its main object to provide an efficient and desirable mechanism of this description.

**How it works (reading):** Operating pins on the carriage trip a cam that locks the typebar action at a chosen point, with a release key.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Types should not print once the carriage reaches the end of a predetermined line or amount of travel.

**Solution:** Operating pins on the carriage trip a cam that locks the typebar action at a chosen point, with a release key.

**How it works:** Adjustable pins mounted on the carriage engage a cam on a rod as the carriage travels. The cam moves a locking device that stops the type bars from striking, and a release key frees it. Several variants of the actuating cams and release key are shown, applied to a Remington No. 2 (spring-drum carriage, spacing dogs on a feed rack, circular series of typebars) and a Caligraph.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md), [Rebound and clash prevention](../features/bar-rebound.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** carriage; feed rack; spring drum; operating pin; cam; release key; type bar; type basket

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 2 / Caligraph: adds line-lock mechanism
