## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper is not positively held to the platen and a divided platen cannot be line-spaced in sections.

**Solution:** Paper feed means that lock the work sheet to the platen, a divided platen with a line-space device that spaces some or all sections, and an automatic copy holder.
