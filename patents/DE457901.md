## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** A stiffening disc of sheet steel or rubber is inserted between the upper elastic disc and the lower soft cushion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Felt or sponge foot pads wear and sag under the machine base.

**Solution:** A stiffening disc of sheet steel or rubber is inserted between the upper elastic disc and the lower soft cushion.
