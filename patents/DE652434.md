**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switching device at the triggering or visual or acoustic perception of characters, Signals, sounds or the like in typewriters, typesetting machines, teleprinters, signal systems or other machines, devices or systems by optionally combining two or more simultaneously actuated buttons, switch bodies or the like is effected, whereby by pressing each button, each switch o.

**How it works (reading):** Two or more keys pressed together select a magnet group; each switch is wired to only one or two contact groups, so a small number of keys can trigger many characters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known key circuits needed many magnet coils per finger switch and key movements that are too complex for fast writing, while a single contact per key limits the characters that can be triggered.

**Solution:** Two or more keys pressed together select a magnet group; each switch is wired to only one or two contact groups, so a small number of keys can trigger many characters.
