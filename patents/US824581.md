## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** The chief objects of my invention are to obviate these objections, and I do this by providing a mechanism which will cause the ribbon carrier or vibrator to be automatically actuated to cover the printingoint and to be retained automatically in t at position during the ordinary operation of the printing devices, the c arrier or vibrator automatically returning to normal position whenever the operator pauses in the course of the work.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon vibrator that moves every stroke annoys the operator and adds to the key force needed.

**Solution:** The vibrator is moved automatically to cover the print point and held there during continuous typing, returning to normal when the operator pauses.

**How it works:** Key levers act through sublevers and links on segmentally arranged typebars. The ribbon vibrator is driven to cover the print point on the first stroke and retained there while printing goes on, then returns on its own when typing pauses. This avoids lifting the vibrator and ribbon on every stroke. Opening only.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; ribbon spool; key lever; sublever; type-bar; platen

**Improves on:**

- conventional ribbon vibrators: no lifting on each stroke
