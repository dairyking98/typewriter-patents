## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a Japanese typewriter whose type tray moves, the spring that drives the platen sideways gradually unwinds as keys are pressed and its driving force falls.

**Solution:** A ratchet and pawl rewind the platen-drive spring using the irregular back-and-forth motion of the type tray, stopping automatically when the spring reaches a set tension.

**How it works:** A rocking lever on the spring arbor carries a drive pawl that engages a ratchet fixed to the spring's inner end, while a fixed pawl stops reverse turning. A spring on the lever turns it so the pawl winds the ratchet; the other lever end is joined by a link to a bell-crank with a stud that the back of the type tray strikes as it moves back, rocking the lever the other way against its spring. When the tray goes forward the spring pulls the lever and winds the ratchet and the stud follows the tray. When the platen spring is fully wound and balances the lever spring, the pawl holds still and the stud stays back, so winding stops. A nut and screw adjust the lever spring tension and so the set winding.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. An automatic winding device for the platen-drive spring of a Japanese typewriter, characterised in that a drive pawl engages a ratchet wheel, which winds the platen-drive spring whose one end is anchored to it; the back-and-forth motion of the pawl turns the ratchet in one set direction only; a rocking lever, on which the pawl is pivoted and which moves it back and forth, has a spring connected to one end that urges the pawl in the direction that turns the ratchet that way; and to the other end of the rocking lever is connected an arm that the type tray strikes in its back-and-forth motion, returning the pawl, against its spring, in the opposite direction.
