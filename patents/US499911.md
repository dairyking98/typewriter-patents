## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type wheels for printing telegraphs and typewriters were made wholly of hard rubber, of soft rubber type on a hard wheel, or of soft rubber type on a ribbon wound on a hub; all are costly or hard to replace.

**Solution:** A seamless soft-rubber ring with the type moulded integral on its outer edge and shallow radial holes on its inside, sprung over a light hub with radial pins so it can be replaced without a skilled mechanic.

**How it works:** The ring is cast from soft white rubber gum of stamp-type quality in a hollow-cylinder mould with type matrices, and an inner die with short radial pins forms the holes. It is vulcanised, about half an hour at 320 F (about 90 lb steam gauge). The hub is a spun aluminium or brass cup or a turned brass band with a skeleton web, carrying short radial pins at equidistant points matching the holes. The ring is stretched over the hub, where its elastic tension and the pins hold it, and it can be pulled off and replaced readily. A new type ring is all that is needed to renew the wheel.

**Features:** [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [ELEM:ring](../features/elem-ring.md), [ELEM:rubber-type](../features/elem-rubber-type.md), [ELEM:interchangeable](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [ELEM:hub-keying](../features/elem-hub-keying.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md), [USE:telegraph-printer](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** rubber type ring; type; radial hole; hub; web; radial pin; peripheral band; mould; die

**Numbers:** ring about 1/8 in. thick; ring 3/16 to 3/8 in. wide; vulcanise 320 F for 30 min at about 90 lb steam pressure

**Improves on:**

- all-hard-rubber type wheels: cheaper and replaceable by changing only the ring
- soft rubber type vulcanised on a hard rubber wheel: ring is seamless and removable
- rubber type ribbon wound on a hub: a seamless ring has no joint
