## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a moving device for the ribbon fork on accent typewriters, such as one that became known from patent specification 127935 is.

**How it works (reading):** A resilient articulated connection so that the accent dead-key always moves the ribbon fork in the same way regardless of carriage height.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon fork for accent typewriters moves differently depending on whether the paper carriage is in the upper or lower position.

**Solution:** A resilient articulated connection so that the accent dead-key always moves the ribbon fork in the same way regardless of carriage height.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** accent dead-key; fork arm; oscillating shaft; ribbon guide
