## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Gearing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to mechanism for starting and stopping the revolution about its axis of a shaft or wheel having a continuous orbital movement, the object being to furnish an apparatus for so operating the type-wheels of a certain class of type-writing machines, and for imparting to such a wheel or shaft two revolutions each time started.

**How it works (reading):** A turret carries the shaft; a segmental gear on it rolls on internal teeth, a latch starts it, a repeating cam and stop arm make two revolutions.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type-wheel shaft on a revolving frame must be started and stopped to turn exactly twice each time.

**Solution:** A turret carries the shaft; a segmental gear on it rolls on internal teeth, a latch starts it, a repeating cam and stop arm make two revolutions.

**How it works:** The shaft-carrying frame revolves about a fixed stud, driven by a gear. The shaft carries a segmental gear meshing with internal teeth in a wall, a starting arm that engages a sliding spring latch, a pinion driving a repeating cam, and a stop arm that brings it to rest after two revolutions. A tripping rod unhooks the latch, and the latch is reset after. The type-wheel sits on this shaft. Only the opening is available.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)

**Parts:** turret frame; shaft; segmental gear; internal teeth; starting arm; latch; spring; tripping rod; repeating cam; pinion; stop arm; type-wheel

**Numbers:** two revolutions per start
