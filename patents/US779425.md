## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to springs used to return type-actions of type-writing machines to normal position after actuation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Draw springs attached directly to typebars cut through their wire from pivotal action at the bearings and wear out quickly.

**Solution:** A metal sleeve or wound helix is fitted on the end of the return spring subject to pivotal action, spreading the wear and stopping the cutting action.
