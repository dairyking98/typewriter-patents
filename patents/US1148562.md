## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Large work sheets exceed the typewriter capacity when collated on a sheet board attached to the carriage.

**Solution:** The lower end of the sheet collating board is fixed to the carriage while its upper end is carried on an independent slide running on a track parallel to the carriage rails.
