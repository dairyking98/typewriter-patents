## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (shorthand typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a shorthand typewriter, which should be used to write shorthand or shorthand and ordinary writing.

**How it works (reading):** Characters are re-arranged on the type levers so each lever's three signs share a consonant and the e-group signs sit in the base position, so a and o signs need only one switch; the keyboard keeps its ordinary key count.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shorthand written in machine type needs raised and lowered characters, which take more space and read poorly.

**Solution:** Characters are re-arranged on the type levers so each lever's three signs share a consonant and the e-group signs sit in the base position, so a and o signs need only one switch; the keyboard keeps its ordinary key count.
