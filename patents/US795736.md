## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide improved means for carrying marginal and lock stops on the front bar of the carriage of a type-writing machine and at the same time utilize said front bar for the placing of removable stops, as for tabulating, and for the exhibition of a scale whereby the location of Said carriage relative to the center of the type-basket of the machine may be determined.
