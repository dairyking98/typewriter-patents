## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to that class of type- Writing machines which print upon the work in a spread-out or fiat condition and which are associated with a dat platen, upon which the sheet or work is supported during the printing operation.

**How it works (reading):** A work-holder interposed in the plane of the platen so that the sheet or card is held flat while printing.
