## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cross counter on typewriter arithmetic unit) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on typewriters or the like Office machines, in which a cross-counter is provided by the steep counters arranged on the paper trolley is taken within the arithmetic range of a steep counter and an arithmetic unit lock that occurs at the end of the arithmetic process by pressing the Clear character key can be disengaged again.

**How it works (reading):** When the cross counter reads zero, the arithmetic lock releases automatically, so bookings continue without printing a clear sign.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Booking errors on typewriter accounting work must be checked by hand, and the arithmetic lock must be released by a clear-character key.

**Solution:** When the cross counter reads zero, the arithmetic lock releases automatically, so bookings continue without printing a clear sign.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
