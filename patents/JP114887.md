## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hand-operated Japanese typewriters make the typist hunt reversed types in a tray and carry heavy parts by hand to an unstopped point, tiring eyes and hand and limiting speed.

**Solution:** A motor-driven attachment with a keyboard whose keys serve as stops moves the typewriter's carriage and print lever to the type through differentials, then works the print lever.

**How it works:** A motor turns coupled shafts linked by differentials. Pressing a key (one key serves four characters; the keyboard slides one space to choose) lowers a floating plate that brakes the coupling wheel. One intermediate wheel winds a cord to draw a sliding frame forward until it hits the pressed key's projection. The next shaft likewise draws the cylinder and print-lever mechanism sideways to a stop plate. The third shaft then turns a sector gear and push bar to work the print lever. On release, spring drums return the parts.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. A power control device for a Japanese-script printing machine made up of coupled shafts linked by two sets of differential gears, with the mechanisms belonging to them, and a keyboard in which many keys are suitably set, with its mechanisms. It is combined with a Japanese typewriter, each mechanism being linked to the corresponding one of the typewriter; the first of the coupled shafts is connected to an electric motor by a suitable coupling, and while the motor runs, pressing a key of the keyboard moves the typewriter's printing mechanism to the position for picking out the wanted type, after which the printing lever at once works and prints.
