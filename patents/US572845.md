## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A Yost paper carriage needed to run lightly and easily, and the platen frame had to come off quickly for cleaning the types and for repairs.

**Solution:** Run the carriage bar on three antifriction balls between two grooved guide bars and hang the platen carrier from open hooks on the carriage so it lifts out.

**How it works:** A movable bar with V-grooves slides between two fixed guide bars with matching grooves. Two balls on one side are coupled by a perforated link to keep them a fixed distance apart, and a single ball on the other side sits between them, giving three bearing points; screw stops keep the balls in their grooves. The feed rack is fixed under the bar and works with the feed dogs. Forward arms on the bar carry open hooks holding a hinge rod, so the platen carrier swings up for inspection and lifts out with the drive cord still attached to the hinge rod. The rear rod of the carrier lies under the hooks to prevent accidental detachment. A front roller on a track supports the carrier's front, and stops on the fixed bar limit travel in both directions.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** guide bar; movable carriage bar; antifriction ball; ball coupling link; ball stop; feed rack; feed dog; hooked arm; hinge rod; platen carrier; front roller; track; travel stop

**Numbers:** three ball bearing points: two coupled on one side, one on the other

**Improves on:**

- earlier Yost carriages: lighter running and a quickly detachable platen carrier
