## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (zero device for adding and calculating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Transfer mechanisms, e.g. transfer of a figure from a ten-key keyboard into the pin carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a zero device for adding, calculating and calculating Typewriters for keying in one or more zeros with a single keystroke.

**How it works (reading):** A single key sets the stops for all trailing zeros at once, with locking elements that limit the counter drive path.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known zero keys handle at most three zeros per stroke, and a long run of trailing zeros needs several keystrokes.

**Solution:** A single key sets the stops for all trailing zeros at once, with locking elements that limit the counter drive path.
