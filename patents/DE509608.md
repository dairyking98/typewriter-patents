## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide for typewriters with a front stop and with a Swing-out paper guide table, which carries front pressure rollers.

**How it works (reading):** A shaft behind the table carries a crank-like roller that, turned by a hand lever, lifts the table and pressure rollers to a dead-center lock, with no extra lock.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting the paper guide table on portable machines needs heavy springs and locks that take space and add weight.

**Solution:** A shaft behind the table carries a crank-like roller that, turned by a hand lever, lifts the table and pressure rollers to a dead-center lock, with no extra lock.
