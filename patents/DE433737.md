## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Power shifting is violent and noisy when linked rigidly to the motor camshaft.

**Solution:** A motor-driven friction clutch drives a segment that raises the platen for momentary or locked permanent shift.

**How it works:** A clutch half keyed to the motor shaft and a sliding half carrying a gear drive a segment, which swings a shift lever and lifts the paper roller's parallelogram. A key engages the clutch and also frees a pawl that locks the shift. A second key releases the lock, letting the roller drop by its own weight. A spring-finger keeps the clutch from re-engaging during release.
