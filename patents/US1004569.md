## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The paper feed rolls must be released from the platen for paper insertion, and the spring tension on them is awkward to release (OCR is poor, so the stated problem is only partly legible).

**Solution:** Paper feed frame with front and rear feed rolls, carried on coiled springs at each end of the platen. A release lever and bell-crank on a rock shaft move the spring-supported arms so the feed rolls can be thrown off the platen.

**How it works:** Lever arms carrying the springs rock about an axis; a bell-crank with pins in inclined slots moves them when the release lever is operated.
