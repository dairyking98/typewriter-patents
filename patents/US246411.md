## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators need a way to signal many different characters from a few finger keys for type-setting, typewriting or telegraph machinery.

**Solution:** Ten finger keys in chords move superposed punched cards so that exactly one hole lines up through all cards, releasing one pin of a bank for each key combination.

**How it works:** Each finger key moves a punched card or plate through a lever. A bank of spring-urged thrust pins, like a jacquard, is pressed against the stack of cards. For any combination of keys exactly one hole is open through every card, so one pin stays forward and works the mechanism, here by magnetic connections. Ten keys give 1,023 combinations, enough for upper and lower case, italics, figures and marks.

**Features:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** finger key; push pin; lever; movable punched card; fixed casing; thrust pin bank; spring

**Numbers:** 10 keys; 1,023 combinations; 10 cards

**Improves on:**

- jacquard loom pin and card mechanism: cards are superposed and moved by the fingers instead of being a fixed punched sequence
