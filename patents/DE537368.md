## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting a printing plate into an address plate holder first requires drawing a hand-retractable bolt, which slows insertion.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded locking piece below the bolt is pressed down by the new plate, letting the bolt snap over the plate's edge and fix it; a plate being removed catches the locking piece and keeps the bolt withdrawn.
