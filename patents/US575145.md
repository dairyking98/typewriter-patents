## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink supply arrangements therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to a new and improved substitute for inking-ribbons in type writer machines, and has for its object to pro duce a cheap substitute for such inking-ribbons, and one which can be readily attached to the type-writer with celerity without soiling the fingers to too great an extent.

**How it works (reading):** A cheap perforated textile disk with a stiffened rim serves as an inking sheet placed between type and platen.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Inking ribbons are costly and soil the fingers when fitted.

**Solution:** A cheap perforated textile disk with a stiffened rim serves as an inking sheet placed between type and platen.

**How it works:** A flimsy fabric disk is perforated, with washers stiffening the holes and a peripheral stiffener holding it taut. The perforations engage propelling means that rotate or displace the disk in the machine.

**Features:** [INK:disc](../features/ink-disc.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** textile disk; perforation; washer; peripheral stiffener

**Improves on:**

- inking ribbons: cheap disk substitute, easier to fit
