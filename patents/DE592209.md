## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** Ink sheet holders move in longitudinal grooves in the platen itself, clamping the paper webs and ink sheets against the roller, so the machine is no larger than an ordinary typewriter and existing machines can be converted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Duplicating typewriters for long overlapping webs need long carriage extensions, which make them bulky.

**Solution:** Ink sheet holders move in longitudinal grooves in the platen itself, clamping the paper webs and ink sheets against the roller, so the machine is no larger than an ordinary typewriter and existing machines can be converted.
