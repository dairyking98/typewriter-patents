## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (rotation point printing for blind) · **Subject (patent classes):** Braille printing

**How it works (reading):** Small rollers in sealed plates, with a bearing capsule and pipe connection, close the cavity airtight and are pressed against the cylinder.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Air-tight sealing of the compression container and the roller bearings in the Braille point-printing apparatus is not good enough; excessive pressure jams the paper.

**Solution:** Small rollers in sealed plates, with a bearing capsule and pipe connection, close the cavity airtight and are pressed against the cylinder.

**How it works:** Sealing plates with rollers pressed against the cylinder seal the air cavity, and the bearing slot is closed by a capsule.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
