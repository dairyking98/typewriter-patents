## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards do not lie flat on the platen, and the card cannot start printing at an arbitrary point.

**Solution:** A card-holder presses the card to the platen in the printing plane and is adjustable relative to the line-space ratchet so printing can begin anywhere.
