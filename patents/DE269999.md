## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-to-type-lever linkage causes lateral disturbances and wear that cost money in a simple typewriter.

**Solution:** A pull rod from the key lever drives a lever mounted on an arm in the frame, which transfers motion to the type lever with minimal lateral movement.
