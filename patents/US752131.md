## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be guided and held against the platen yet be fully released when desired.

**Solution:** One sliding bar with cam portions swings the front and rear feed rollers away from the platen together, and a separate lever swings the front paper guides away alone.

**How it works:** Curved paper guides with rollers hang from a front bar on adjustable sleeves and are spring-pressed to the platen. Rear feed-roller carriers are loosely pinned so each roller can tilt to suit uneven surfaces and are spring-urged to the platen. A longitudinally sliding bar under the platen has cam cut-outs engaging projections on the front and rear roller carriers, so shifting it left releases the paper. A notch on the bar also works a lever linkage that swings the front bar and its guides away. A knob lever lets the guides alone be swung away without disturbing the other rollers.

**Parts:** platen; front bar; curved paper guide; guide roller; rear feed roller; roller carrier; sliding release bar; release knob lever
