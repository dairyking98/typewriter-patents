## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on typewriters for the automatic return of the paper carriage with stops of the same one and disengageable drive mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Two paper-carriage stops pivot a two-armed intermediate lever that locks or releases the shift lock and, in the same motion, engages or disengages the drive clutch for the automatic carriage return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known locks that block the type levers need separate transmission links to switch the power carriage-return drive on and off.

**Solution:** Two paper-carriage stops pivot a two-armed intermediate lever that locks or releases the shift lock and, in the same motion, engages or disengages the drive clutch for the automatic carriage return.
