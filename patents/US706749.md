## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Several parts of a typebar machine (feed-roll control, paper guide, ribbon guide, platen support, carriage retention, escapement control, scales) need improvement.

**Solution:** A group of improvements to a keyboard typewriter carriage: ball-race ways with spring-loaded hold-down levers, tipping/shiftable platen, paper-guide on a rod, and an escapement control.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on ball races on the top plate ways; levers with rollers, spring-loaded, press on the carriage back rail to remove looseness. The platen spindle can be shifted for case and tipped forward for inspection. A feed-roll is controlled by a lever, the paper guide rides on a rod, and the ribbon guide also guides paper. Details beyond the figure list are mostly in the unseen portion.
