## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention has reference to typewriting machines and relates to certain paper feeding motions in connection with the platen roller, namely, a motion whereby the platen at the first onset: an arrangement of finger pieces for keeping, thev paper closely against the platen;

**How it works (reading):** Finger pieces hold the paper against the platen, a frictional connection lets the platen turn independently of its line-spacing ratchet, and a date-line feed motion and supplementary paper holder feed headed or loose-leaf paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feeding motions needed improvement: paper should go squarely onto the platen, stay against it, and headed paper should be fed into position for dating.

**Solution:** Finger pieces hold the paper against the platen, a frictional connection lets the platen turn independently of its line-spacing ratchet, and a date-line feed motion and supplementary paper holder feed headed or loose-leaf paper.
