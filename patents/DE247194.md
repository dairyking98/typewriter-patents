## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide device for typewriters, in which several sets of Paper guide rollers are used.

**How it works (reading):** Paired paper guide roller sets alternately clamp and advance two sheets so the collecting sheet and the single-line entries can be written separately.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing several waybills with copies needs separate guides for each sheet, which otherwise tangle.

**Solution:** Paired paper guide roller sets alternately clamp and advance two sheets so the collecting sheet and the single-line entries can be written separately.
