## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen carriage, its ways and the ribbon guides block access to the parts of the machine beneath them.

**Solution:** Mount the carriage rails on dovetail bars sliding in frame grooves and split the ribbon guides so carriage, rails and guides lift off as a unit.

**How it works:** Two dovetail bars fit in parallel dovetail grooves in the frame top and are clamped by binding screws. Parallel rails join the bars and carry the platen carriage on tongue-and-groove ways. Ribbon guides run between the rails under the carriage, each made of two rods with a loose sleeve covering the gap. To remove the assembly the sleeves are slid aside, the ribbon is dropped down through the gaps, and the rails and guides lift away.

**Features:** [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** frame; dovetail groove; dovetail bar; binding screw; rail; platen carriage; ribbon guide rod; sleeve
