## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Two scales and a pointer on the earlier Barron machine are needed to follow carriage progress and to locate a character in a printed line for correction.

**Solution:** A single hinged scale plate that travels with the carriage under a fixed pointer, and swings next to the line when the carriage is turned up to read off character positions.

**How it works:** Arms pivoted near the ends of the feed-roller axle carry a graduated bar. With the carriage down, the scale moves with it past a fixed pointer at the printing point, showing the carriage position. To find a mistake, the carriage is turned up and the scale swung through about a half circle about the roller axis to lie beside the printed line, giving each character's number. Lowering the carriage and moving it until that number lines up with the pointer brings the position to the impression point. Shown on a type-ring machine with a paper box carriage using a pile of telegraph blanks as the platen.
