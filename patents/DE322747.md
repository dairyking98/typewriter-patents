## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen height-shift (case shift) devices needed to switch reliably in both directions from a middle position.

**Solution:** One shift key lever holds the platen in the middle; pressing it moves the platen one way, and sliding the lever lengthways moves it the other way.

**How it works:** The shift key lever pivots on an adjustable eccentric bolt and is connected by a link to an upright arm of a shift shaft whose horizontal arms push rods that raise or lower the platen frame. The platen's own weight presses the lever against the bolt, fixing the middle position, which is set by turning the eccentric. Pressing the lever down swings the shaft so the platen is shifted down until the arms hit stops. A second key lever, bent against a pin of the first, shoves the first forward when pressed, which pulls the short arm of the shaft the other way and lifts the platen. Releasing returns everything by springs and the platen's weight, and stop screws limit the stroke.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** shift key lever; second shift key lever; eccentric bolt; link; shift shaft; push rod; platen frame; guide rail; coil spring; stop screw

**Numbers:** three type rows (three types per carrier)
