## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides; Width

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, and particularly to means for gaging and alining the side edge of the sheet-of paper as it is being introduced into the machine and passed forwardly around the platen.

**How it works (reading):** The side guide is mounted on the same rod as the front guiding fingers and coupled to them, so one adjustment moves both to the correct position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Side guides and front fingers must be adjusted to each sheet width separately.

**Solution:** The side guide is mounted on the same rod as the front guiding fingers and coupled to them, so one adjustment moves both to the correct position.
