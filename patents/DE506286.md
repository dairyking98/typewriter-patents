## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (moving long paper web in office machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles characterised by being applied to printers having stationary carriages

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to writing office machines, Zvie typewriters, Calculating machines and tabulating machines, in which the texts, numbers, etc.

**How it works (reading):** A motor-driven fast drive is added beside the hand crank for the two spools, engaged through a magnetic coupling on a switch key for long moves while short moves stay manual.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving a long paper web on a two-roll account strip by hand to a distant account takes a great deal of time.

**Solution:** A motor-driven fast drive is added beside the hand crank for the two spools, engaged through a magnetic coupling on a switch key for long moves while short moves stay manual.
