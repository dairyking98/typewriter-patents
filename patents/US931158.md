## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Embossed letters on metal labels need characters brought into position, spaced by their widths, and struck with enough force and precision for clean impressions.

**Solution:** Circular type carriers on parallel shafts carry characters in ways around their rims; an escapement auto-spaces each character by its width and the selected character is impressed with force.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
