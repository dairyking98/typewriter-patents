## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to ribbon mechanism for type-writing machines, and more especially for that class of type-writing machines known as visible-writing machines, being machines in which the work is visible to the operator as it progresses, as distinguished from those machines in which the work can only be observed by lifting the paper-carriage or otherwise moving it to abnormal position for that purpose.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon vibrator that moves ribbon to and from the printing point and is at rest at impact.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon vibrator must expose the line of print and sit still at impact without slackening the ribbon.

**Solution:** A ribbon vibrator that moves ribbon to and from the printing point and is at rest at impact.

**How it works:** On a visible-writing typebar machine, the ribbon lifts away from the printing point to expose the line and is brought up by a vibrator operated from the key action. It is arranged so the ribbon never goes slack in any vibrator position. Spool construction is detailed.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; ribbon spool; type-bar; key-lever; carriage
