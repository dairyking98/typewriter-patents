## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The rack bar of a typewriter wears out and the machine clicks noisily.

**Solution:** A rotatable cylindrical rack bar with several series of teeth, each series brought into action by turning the bar, and covered in rubber to deaden noise.

**How it works:** The cylindrical rack bar carries radial teeth in several series around its circumference, journaled by spindles in arms. Indentations in the spindles take set-screws which hold one series of teeth in the active position. When a series wears, the screws are backed off, the bar turned to a fresh series and the screws reset. A rubber casing with hollow projections covers the teeth against the escapement pawl. Rubber also covers the platen, carriage wheel tire, buffer ring and key rubbers.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** rack bar; teeth; rubber casing; arm; spindle; set-screw; escapement pawl; platen cover; buffer ring

**Numbers:** eight or more sets of teeth
