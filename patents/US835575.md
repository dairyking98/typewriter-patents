## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing niachines, and more especially to the type-action of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** The type-bars are connected to the wider key-lever system so every part moves by direct pulls or pushes in the planes where its pivoted parts swing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says a wide key-lever system is hard to join to a narrower type-bar system, so the parts twist under torsional strain.

**Solution:** The type-bars are connected to the wider key-lever system so every part moves by direct pulls or pushes in the planes where its pivoted parts swing.
