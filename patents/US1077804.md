## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen shift and space bar are awkward to reach without leaving printing position.

**Solution:** Thumb-operated shift and space bars, with a platen that also shifts forward/back or up/down to place a character in subnormal positions to change its sound value for shorthand.

**How it works:** A shift bar at the left of the keyboard moves rod arms, a rock shaft and a bell crank whose cam part shifts a rod on the platen frame, rocking it and raising a supplementary front platen that receives the type. The outer platen face is shaped so type strikes squarely at any height. Each type face bears one character; a character printed higher or lower means different sounds, shown with a shorthand keyboard.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [USE:stenotype](../features/use-stenotype.md)

**Parts:** shift bar; rock shaft; bell crank; platen frame; supplementary platen; paper guide; feed roller

**Improves on:**

- Remington two-character typebar: one character per type face
