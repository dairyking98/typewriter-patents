## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed with two or more sets of type or printing elements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the type-actions of writing-machines.

**How it works (reading):** Many type-bars are mounted on wide bearings in a compact front-strike arrangement, with improved links to the keys and rebound prevention on return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Too few type-bars fit in a front-strike machine; type-bars rebound after printing and link poorly to the keys.

**Solution:** Many type-bars are mounted on wide bearings in a compact front-strike arrangement, with improved links to the keys and rebound prevention on return.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Rebound and clash prevention](../features/bar-rebound.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
