## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key for calculating machines)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an operating element for calculating machines equipped with a key body and similar machines.

**How it works (reading):** The key body is pressed onto a U-shaped stamped shaft with a hole for the moulding compound, so the symbol is injected into a cavity.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculator keys with strong multi-leg shafts are costly; the key symbol body cannot be moulded directly.

**Solution:** The key body is pressed onto a U-shaped stamped shaft with a hole for the moulding compound, so the symbol is injected into a cavity.

- **Frame and body:** [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
