## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Type lever drive for typewriters The invention relates to a Type lever drive for typewriters, especially for small typewriters, in which, in order to reduce the dimensions of the machine, the type levers in one mounted vertically or approximately vertically in front of the platen sector and are swung out by intermediate levers that are concentric to the type lever sector are arranged, and in which, in order to simplify the drive, the intermediate lever are connected by tie rods with angle levers, on which the key bars are directly attack. The main purpose of the invention is to measure the dimensions of machines Above type to reduce even further and the drive of the same even further to simplify. This purpose is to be achieved according to the invention in that the lowest point of the intermediate lever bearing exactly or approximately at the same height is arranged like the common bearing axis of the angle lever, while the connection points the key bars with the angle levers are higher than their bearing axis and in turn the connection points of the tie rods with the angle levers are higher than theirs Connection points with the pushbutton rods.…
