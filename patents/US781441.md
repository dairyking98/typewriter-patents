## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With uniform spacing, narrow characters (i, l, t) leave gaps and wide ones (m, w) crowd.

**Solution:** Space the carriage for a two-unit character and shift the platen sideways one unit with a screw spindle and ratchet before printing narrow or wide characters.

**How it works:** The type face is divided into thirds: narrow letters take one unit, most take two, wide ones three. The carriage escapement steps two units. The platen shaft slides lengthwise on a slot and pin, moved by a screw spindle engaging a ratchet wheel on the carriage. A lever with a spring-pressed pawl steps the ratchet one tooth, shifting the platen one unit; a crank and gears reverse the spindle. For a narrow letter the platen shifts one unit, and for a wide letter one unit the other way, giving net one or three unit advance; a stirrup carries the platen and pressure roll.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen shaft; screw spindle; ratchet wheel; lever; pawl; spring; crank; toothed wheel; pressure roll; stirrup; carriage

**Numbers:** character width in 1, 2 or 3 units; carriage steps 2 units
