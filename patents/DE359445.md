## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Type lever guide for typewriters. For this registration is according to: the union treaty of June 2nd cgtt the priority on the basis of the registration, in Austria from t6. Claimed March 1942. The invention relates to an accurate readjustable Guide the type lever near its axis of rotation. This will take the lead of the end of the door lever carrying the types in front of the ribbon superfluous, so that also the noises are eliminated, which by hitting the lever on this Leadership arise. The subject of the invention is shown in the drawing, for example Embodiment shown in Fig. I in section and in Fig_ 2 in 'front view. The type lever 3 rotatable about the axis 2 in the type lever agment i is provided with one end which runs in a groove in the guide piece 4. The guide piece is fixed by means of a screw 5 on the type lever system i and can be in the height direction can be adjusted. The guide piece is against lateral displacement 4 secured by webs attached to type lever segment i.
