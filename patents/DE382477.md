## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter carrier sliding into a lower table stand jammed in its guide rails.

**Solution:** The rear of the carrier runs in guide rails and its front is held horizontal by a double-armed pull rod; a nose on a lever engages the carrier while lifting and releases it at the working position.
