## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the class of devices used for punching numerals and other characters in checks, receipts, and the like, designating the amount of money for which the check or receipt is drawn, dac.

**How it works (reading):** A check punch with numeral dies set on a rotating die-carrying plate on a stem, held by a return spring, so that rotating the plate selects the figure to punch.
