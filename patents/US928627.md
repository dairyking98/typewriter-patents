## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The back-spacing action should move the carriage back one letter space at a time through a usable mechanism.

**Solution:** A rack on the carriage meshes with two loose gear wheels; the carriage is driven in letter-space direction by one wheel, connected to a driving spring barrel, and a back-space key reverses the drive by one letter space.

**Features:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
