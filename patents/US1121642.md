## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be released from the control of the usual line-space wheel, so it cannot be turned freely.

**Solution:** An arm inside a drum on the line-space wheel, pivoted to the platen, with a spring that presses it into wedging contact with the drum; it binds in one direction and so locks the platen to the line-space wheel, and a release frees them.
