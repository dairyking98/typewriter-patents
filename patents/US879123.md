## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar connections from finger lever to bar use many parts and need heavy finger pressure to get a proper printing stroke.

**Solution:** Connect each finger lever to its typebar by a single slotted angle lever with a nosed, clawed top that flicks the bar then pulls it to the platen.

**How it works:** A finger lever with a side pin enters a slot in the short arm of an angle lever pivoted on a cross rod. In rest, a pin on the typebar butt sits in a recess at the front of the angle lever's nose. Depressing the finger lever swings the upper arm forward, the pin rides along the nose giving the bar a slight upward impetus, and a rear claw catches a second pin on the bar and pulls it into printing position. A spring returns the parts; pins may carry small rollers.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** finger lever; angle lever; nose; claw; typebar; pin; cross rod; return spring
