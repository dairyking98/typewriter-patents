## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in tabulating devices for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** Column stops are not adjustable longitudinally but move laterally in and out of the tabulating key device, so they can be set for a column instantly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Column stops need careful adjustment and take time to remove.

**Solution:** Column stops are not adjustable longitudinally but move laterally in and out of the tabulating key device, so they can be set for a column instantly.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
