## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes; Brakes using the internal friction of fluids or fluid-like media, e.g. powders with blades or like members braked by the fluid

**How it works (reading):** An impeller in a closed liquid-filled housing turns past counter-vanes so the liquid circulating between them brakes the rotation; braking force is set by the vane shape, number and size, the speed and the liquid viscosity, and there are no rubbing solid parts to wear.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A friction brake that regulates the running of an office-machine carriage or similar mechanism wears and changes with dirt on its surfaces.

**Solution:** An impeller in a closed liquid-filled housing turns past counter-vanes so the liquid circulating between them brakes the rotation; braking force is set by the vane shape, number and size, the speed and the liquid viscosity, and there are no rubbing solid parts to wear.
