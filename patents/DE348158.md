## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The machine takes too much space when not in use.

**Solution:** Type levers mounted in a fixed frame are held in rest by a liftable abutment moved by a hand crank; lowering the type levers out of use folds the machine into a small space with simple means.
