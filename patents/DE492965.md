## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Long sheets or ribbon groups need interrupted work on several groups, and changing between carbon sheet sets is slow.

**Solution:** An upper carbon sheet carrier is stacked on the lower one and hooks onto posts through openings in its base plate; it can be swung up and detached for a quick change.
