## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Inking pads dry out and need frequent renewal.

**Solution:** Ink pad cushion fed by wicks from a connected ink well, with screw valves to stop flow in transport.

**How it works:** An ink basin sits below the cushion and connects through openings to a chamber separated from the cushion by a perforated partition. Wicks carry ink up to the chamber and the cushion absorbs it. Screw valves press the wicks into the openings to stop flow when shipping.

**Features:** [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md)

**Parts:** ink basin; wick; chamber; perforated partition; cushion; screw valve
