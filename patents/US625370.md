## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Students need a keyboard practice device with a standard key feel that is cheaper than a typewriter.

**Solution:** A dummy keyboard frame has standard key arrangement and a vertical space bar held by springs, so students learn touch on it.
