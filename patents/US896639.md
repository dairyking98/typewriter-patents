## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (protectograph check printer) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**How it works (reading):** A rotating printing base carries raised numerals and guard lines that are inked by rollers and printed onto the check, which is advanced by rollers after each numeral so that the figures are evenly spaced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check amounts can be raised by adding numerals, and plain printed figures can be altered with a pen.

**Solution:** A rotating printing base carries raised numerals and guard lines that are inked by rollers and printed onto the check, which is advanced by rollers after each numeral so that the figures are evenly spaced.

**Features:** [USE:check-writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
