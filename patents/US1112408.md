## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must judge by hand how far the printing point is from the bottom of the sheet.

**Solution:** An automatic sheet-position indicator: a reciprocating paper-feeler, actuated by a cam and cam-follower, drives indices showing the remaining distance, with a bell signal and machine-lock at a set sheet position.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)

**Parts:** reciprocating paper-feeler; cam and cam-follower; indices; bell signal; machine-lock
