## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A removable type cylinder should be simple to make, and must always seat in the correct rotational position against the platen without special alignment.

**Solution:** Make the type cylinder a thin smooth-bored sleeve that drops over a permanent cup fixed on the spindle, keyed by a pin in an angular slot and retained by a spring-latch.

**How it works:** A cup-shaped cylinder is fixed once and for all on the spindle top with a screw, and holds a coil spring that pushes the type sleeve upward. The type sleeve carries a pin that must enter an angular cutout in the cup, which turns it to the right position and blocks it if put on 180 degrees out, and an arrow disc on top points at the platen as a visual check. A U-shaped two-part spring pawl with lugs snaps over the sleeve top to hold it against the spring. To remove, the operator squeezes the pawl lugs together (travel limited by a pin); the fingers also grip a handle bracket on the sleeve so the spring cannot throw it off. Several cylinders with different type can be kept ready.

**Improves on:**
