## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in IQ type-Writing machines,and has special reference to the equipment of a flat platen with an improved follower-plate designed to hold the work-sheet in a smoothly-spreadout condition by hearing thereon contiguous to the I5 printing-point.

**How it works (reading):** A follower plate bears on a leaf or loose sheet on the flat platen near the printing point and is moved over the platen in the line-spacing direction as the work progresses.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A loose follower plate on a flat platen does not positively engage the platen or machine, so it does not hold a book leaf smoothly at the print point.

**Solution:** A follower plate bears on a leaf or loose sheet on the flat platen near the printing point and is moved over the platen in the line-spacing direction as the work progresses.
