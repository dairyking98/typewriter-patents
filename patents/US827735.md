## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**How it works (reading):** A trip on the universal bar is swung to an ineffective position by the accent key, so the bar cannot move the carriage-feeding mechanism while the ribbon still vibrates.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Striking an accent key before a letter moves the carriage, so the accent cannot be placed over the letter.

**Solution:** A trip on the universal bar is swung to an ineffective position by the accent key, so the bar cannot move the carriage-feeding mechanism while the ribbon still vibrates.
