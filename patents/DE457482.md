## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**How it works (reading):** A one-piece guide with a slot extending nearly to its lower edge, joined by a narrow elastic web, lets the two legs be spread apart for smooth machining of each guide surface, then returned and set to the exact slot width with adjusting screws.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Two-part type lever guides need matched parts and an exact slot width that is hard to hold, while one-piece guides have slots too narrow to machine the guiding surfaces smoothly, so they wear quickly.

**Solution:** A one-piece guide with a slot extending nearly to its lower edge, joined by a narrow elastic web, lets the two legs be spread apart for smooth machining of each guide surface, then returned and set to the exact slot width with adjusting screws.
