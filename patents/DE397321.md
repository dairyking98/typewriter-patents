## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letter spacing of a typebar machine is fixed at one step, so larger type cannot be used.

**Solution:** A rotatable universal rail with lugs of different lengths changes how far the key levers swing it, hence how many steps the carriage advances.

**How it works:** Key levers all act on a universal rail on a swinging frame linked to the escapement. The rail has a rectangular section with two lugs of different projection set at an angle. With the short lug under the levers they run idle and the carriage steps once; turning the rail 90 degrees puts the long lug under the levers so the rail swings further and the escapement makes two steps. The rail is turned by a pivot rod and angle lever operated from a button or tied to the shift keys, and a spring returns it to single spacing.
