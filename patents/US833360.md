## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to feeding mechanism Jfor type-writers.

**How it works (reading):** A rack-bar frame that moves the book across the platen strip, with clamps on the leaf edge and tension rollers on cords and springs that keep the leaf stretched.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The inventor says a book leaf must be drawn taut over the platen while the book is fed, which the usual method does not do.

**Solution:** A rack-bar frame that moves the book across the platen strip, with clamps on the leaf edge and tension rollers on cords and springs that keep the leaf stretched.
