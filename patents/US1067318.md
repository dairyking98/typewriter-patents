## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Leaving two to five spaces at a sentence end requires pressing the space key repeatedly.

**Solution:** A multiple-space key lifts the feed rack off the escapement pinion and lets the carriage spring advance it until a pinion running on the rack meets an adjustable stop.

**How it works:** The multiple-space key, via a link and bell-cranks, swings a forked arm carrying a small pinion into mesh with the carriage feed rack, lifting the rack clear of the escapement pinion. The carriage spring then advances the carriage, turning the small pinion until a projection on it hits a stop screw placed in one of several holes in the arm. The key's release lets the rack re-engage the escapement pinion and a spring returns the small pinion. The hole positions correspond to letter spaces, so the stop sets 2, 3 or more spaces and the rack re-meshes properly.
