## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type-arranging device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Toy typewriters

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a device for arranging or setting types, matrices, punches and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A device that holds groups of sectors so that any one can be moved to the operating point with its type rim correctly aligned, replacing the earlier rigid-plus-elastic arrangement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sectors of type for syllable printing and casting must be brought to the operating point, but rigid and elastic sectors leave the type faces at an angle to the printing surface.

**Solution:** A device that holds groups of sectors so that any one can be moved to the operating point with its type rim correctly aligned, replacing the earlier rigid-plus-elastic arrangement.

**Features:** [Type sector or segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md), [How the element is made](../features/elem-element-construction.md)
