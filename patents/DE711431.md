## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal triggered by a feeler lever acts only at the last moment, when the ribbon suddenly tightens and can tear.

**Solution:** An energy store (tension spring) is partly loaded while the ribbon runs and partly by the tightening ribbon, and the feeler lever trips the pawl so the reversal proceeds smoothly.

**How it works:** A feeler lever rides on the taut ribbon and swings as a spool unwinds. A lever on its axis carries a locking pawl and a shift pawl. A spring between frame and shift pawl is stressed progressively; when the ribbon tightens, the feeler releases the locking pawl and the stored force completes the switchover. Stops on the pawls or levers, bendable, adjust the action. The whole device mounts in screw-in brackets as a unit, so it can be retrofitted.
