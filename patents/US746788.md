## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing typewriters gave no way to guide the sheet's side edges to the platen, and paper had to be lined up by hand.

**Solution:** Side guides on the paper table are set by a rack and connecting arms, adjustable to different paper widths, and the device is fitted to existing machines without changing their structure.
