## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rotating type wheels must be moved as a whole or the paper must be pressed onto them, which makes the imprint unclear, and a rotating wheel needs to be light for high speed.

**Solution:** The type levers sit in a basket sleeve that rotates and slides axially on a fixed tube, so an axial move selects a ring of levers and rotation picks one; a single central actuator presses the selected lever radially through a slot.

**How it works:** Axial movement of the sleeve chooses the row, rotation chooses the lever, and an intermediate member pivoted by the actuator's axial motion pushes that lever out.
