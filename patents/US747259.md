## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special purpose keyboards

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of my invention is to avoid zo the necessity of unduly crowding the key-levers together or of using inclined connections at the sides of the machine, which have a more or less cramping effect'upon the keylevers and produce uneven wear on the typebar pivots.

**How it works (reading):** Key-lever hangers and posts and their type-bar connections are arranged so the key-lever system fits within the width of the type-bar system without inclined connecting rods.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The key-lever system is wider than the type-bar system, forcing levers to be crowded together or connected by inclined rods that cramp the levers and wear the type-bar pivots unevenly.

**Solution:** Key-lever hangers and posts and their type-bar connections are arranged so the key-lever system fits within the width of the type-bar system without inclined connecting rods.
