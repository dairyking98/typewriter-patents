## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen shift stops hit hard, causing jolts and noise.

**Solution:** A shift-key-driven crank mechanism raises the platen and stops at dead centre against a damped elastic piece.

**How it works:** The shift lever's toothed arc turns a crankshaft whose crank head raises lifting rods and rollers carrying the platen carriage. At each dead centre the crank head rests broadly on a sound-absorbing but non-yielding intermediate piece on the frame, so the stroke stays exact. A tension spring on a short arm of the shift lever returns it, tensioned only after half the crank travel.
