## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to the aper-feeding devices of a type-Writing machine;

**How it works (reading):** A paper reel and carbon-ribbon spools are carried by the carriage, so paper feeds from a roll that moves with the carrier; the manifold carbon is actuated automatically at the end of each carriage reciprocation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding paper from a continuous roll and manifolding (carbon copies) need separate handling of paper, registration and carbon ribbon for each copy.

**Solution:** A paper reel and carbon-ribbon spools are carried by the carriage, so paper feeds from a roll that moves with the carrier; the manifold carbon is actuated automatically at the end of each carriage reciprocation.

**Features:** [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [INK:carbon](../features/ink-carbon.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** paper reel; carbon-ribbon spools; reel-supporting frame; conjunctive devices; carrier
