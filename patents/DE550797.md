## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The meter reading of type-wheel counters must be read directly; the inspector needs a printed record.

**Solution:** A carriage running in guide channels along the counter type wheels carries the supply roll, pressure roller and inking roller to print the reading on the strip.

**How it works:** The carriage is slid along the guide troughs; the pressure roller presses the strip against the wheels while the inking roller inks them.
