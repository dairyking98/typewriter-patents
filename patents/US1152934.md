## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Envelopes, cards and sheets cannot be fed one after another from a magazine by turning the platen.

**Solution:** A separate feed attachment holds a magazine of envelopes or sheets and uses an operating connection from the platen roll to drive feed rollers, with adjustable guides that clear the emerging sheet from the magazine.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** magazine holder; platen-driven feed rollers; adjustable guides
