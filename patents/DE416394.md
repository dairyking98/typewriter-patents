## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriters need the carriage held in the centre position for transport, but the line-switch lever gets in the way of closing the case.

**Solution:** A line-switch handle on the roller is extendable outward for use, and when pushed in it locks the carriage in its central position and disengages the rear derailleur, and is released again when pulled out.

**How it works:** The handle, when pushed in, acts on a locking device that holds the carriage centre, and pulling it out makes the lock ineffective.
