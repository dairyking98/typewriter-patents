## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Cards fed around the platen need positive feeding, but the existing feed does not grip them firmly.

**Solution:** A card feed attachment mounted on the platen rod with a main feed roll and two driving rolls that press against the platen; the rolls are rubber and the front one is grooved to crimp and grip the cards.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
