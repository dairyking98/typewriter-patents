## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-Writing machines in general, but particularly to what is known as front-strike machines, which can be both electrically and non-electrically operated.
