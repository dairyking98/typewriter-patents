## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-Writing machines in general, but particularly to what is known as front-strike machines, which can be both electrically and non-electrically operated.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An electric typewriter stops when current fails, and electric machines needed each key released before the next is struck.

**Solution:** A front-strike machine convertible by the operator between electric and ordinary key-lever action by changing the fulcrum pin position.

**How it works:** Each key lever has two fulcrum apertures: one for electric operation, one for non-electric. A pin or rod through the key levers and vertical levers couples them electrically-operated; a sliding block with a spring tongue on the key lever's end acts via a stump-joint opening only downward. Magnets or a solenoid move a hooked channel-plate, driving a plunger bar and crosshead to actuate the typebar levers. In electric mode keys can be held while others are struck.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** key lever; vertical lever; fulcrum pin; sliding block; tongue; electromagnet; hooked channel-plate; plunger bar; crosshead; typebar lever
