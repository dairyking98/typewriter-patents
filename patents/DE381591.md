**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on a manuscript holder for typewriters with one to be written Line indicating ruler, which is attached to the side of the document holder, can be set by means of a button actuated switching device.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A manuscript reader must keep the line being copied in view as the typist moves along it.

**Solution:** A ruler on the manuscript holder steps along the line through a ratchet wheel driven by a Bowden cable from a dictation key, and drops one line at line end.
