## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mecha-' nism for typewriting machines andits object, generally stated, is to provide improved mechanism of the class specified.

**How it works (reading):** A two-piece ribbon guide, one piece pivoted on the other, with a vibrating ribbon carrier lets the types strike the stencil directly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon must be dispensed with for stencil or mimeograph work without a separate operation.

**Solution:** A two-piece ribbon guide, one piece pivoted on the other, with a vibrating ribbon carrier lets the types strike the stencil directly.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
