## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** A bent-wire resilient clip clamps the sheet and slides on a connecting piece over the upright rod, so the sheet can be adjusted in height, rotation and sideways. The rod stands in a type case compartment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known manuscript holders on an upright stick have limited adjustment, and better adjustment should not cost simplicity.

**Solution:** A bent-wire resilient clip clamps the sheet and slides on a connecting piece over the upright rod, so the sheet can be adjusted in height, rotation and sideways. The rod stands in a type case compartment.
