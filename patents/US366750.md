## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improves the author's earlier machine (US 341,503) in which batteries of type are carried in a moving frame.

**Solution:** A frame carrying a multi-row type drum slides along a rod to bring a letter to the print centre, with a notched centering bar and a paper cylinder that is spaced by a ratchet and pawl plate.

**How it works:** A rectangular frame holds rods on which the type-carrying frame slides; its front has knobs for moving it by hand. A type drum with rows of type turns in the frame and is locked by a sliding bolt in notches at one end to choose the row. A centering bar with notches marked like the type line locates the letter at the printing point. Brackets on the frame rear push a pawl plate that turns a ratchet disk on the paper cylinder, spacing between letters. The cylinder rides on a left-hand screw rod, moving rearward as it turns, and a cut-away part of the ratchet makes the pawl strike a bell at line end.
