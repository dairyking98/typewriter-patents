## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hard to see the line being printed and to correct a letter on a typewriter with a platen under the print.

**Solution:** A carriage running on balls in grooves carries a platen cradle that rocks off the print point to bring the line to the front in view.

**How it works:** The carriage frame has grooved bars that ride on metal balls in grooves cut across the top of the machine, with stops to keep the balls in. A shifting frame (cradle) with segmental curved racks on its ends rolls on a rack on the carriage and holds the printing roll, paper apron and pressure rod with spring fingers and rollers. Rocking the cradle moves the roll backward off the print point and presents the line in front without rotating the roll. The rock can be inclined so gravity returns it.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** carriage frame; ball; groove; printing roll; shifting frame; paper apron; pressure rod; spring finger; segmental rack
