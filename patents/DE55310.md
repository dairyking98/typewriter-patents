## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter with a sliding roller frame needs a way to set the paper's margin width and to carry the frame smoothly.

**Solution:** The sliding frame carrying the pressure roller runs on steel balls in grooved rails, is stepped by two alternating pawls on a rack, and has an adjustable rail stop that sets the margin.

**How it works:** The frame carrying the pressure roller rests on steel balls in grooves of two guide rails, one adjustable, and is pulled by a barrel with chain or tape. A rack on its rear is held by a locking hook on a shaft turned by the key mechanism through a pull rod, and a second pawl that is lifted out by a thumb on the same shaft, so the two pawls alternately hold each tooth and the frame steps one tooth per key. A swinging rail with a lever and a nose that meets a stop on a toothed rail sets the margin width; lifting the rail lets the frame travel into the margin. The stop rail can be shifted along its teeth.
