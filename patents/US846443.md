## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing musical notation with ordinary means is slow and hard, and a machine is needed that places notes, signs and characters on a music staff quickly and correctly.

**Solution:** A music type-writer in which a type-disk carries the notation characters, moved by keys and a stop-lever, with a paper-carriage shifted to place each symbol on the staff.

**How it works:** Keys drive a stop-lever that positions the type-disk; a spring returns the disk, and the paper-carriage is shifted for each placement.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Type disc (types on the face)](../features/elem-disc-face.md)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** type-disk; stop-lever; keys; paper-carriage; spring; staff tracer
