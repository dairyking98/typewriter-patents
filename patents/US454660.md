## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** An ink pad that inks the types has to be removed from the machine to be re-inked once exhausted.

**Solution:** A slotted casing and a grooved pad allow ink to be added in place and spread evenly, also in only the exhausted spots.

**How it works:** The pad sits directly under the top of its casing, which is slotted along the top; the pad has a V-shaped groove in its upper face. Ink is poured through the slot with the cover removed, the groove holds it and spreads it uniformly through the porous pad. Slots and grooves run all around the ring except at the joint of a two-half casing, or can be sectional. A variation uses a trough or curb round the top.

- casing with permanent pad fed by wicks from a well: this pad is re-inked directly through slots instead
