## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention has reference to machines in which a flat bed or platen is used to sustain the paper beneath a downwardly-acting writing mechanism mounted to shift laterally and longitudinally over the platen for the purpose of letter and line spacing.

**How it works (reading):** A movable scale or indicator is connected to the sliding frame under the writing mechanism; it is retracted out of the type path at rest and moves forward to the printing line when the writing mechanism is lifted, so the paper can be aligned to it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says it is hard to adjust a flat-bed machine quickly to print a line at an irregular spacing on a form.

**Solution:** A movable scale or indicator is connected to the sliding frame under the writing mechanism; it is retracted out of the type path at rest and moves forward to the printing line when the writing mechanism is lifted, so the paper can be aligned to it.
