## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the parent design (DE519544) the push rod is intercepted by a bent tab, so its elastic flap makes the push rod oscillate and swing the type lever.

**Solution:** The handlebar joins the key lever to the push rod through a downward slotted guide, resiliently held by a spring and moved by a tab of the button lever, so the push rod moves without oscillation.
