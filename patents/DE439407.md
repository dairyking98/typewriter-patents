## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to line switching devices for the paper roll of writing and calculating machines, in which the one holding the paper roller in the writing position Lock consisting of two swinging symmetrical levers under the action of a spring exists, which engage with rollers in the gear teeth of the roller ratchet wheel and can be triggered before the paper roller is switched to the next line position.

**How it works (reading):** Two symmetrical spring-loaded locking levers, linked by toggles to the drive rod, spread before carriage return so the roller advances.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-roller line-switch locking is tripped by a sideways force.

**Solution:** Two symmetrical spring-loaded locking levers, linked by toggles to the drive rod, spread before carriage return so the roller advances.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Line switching device for the paper roll of typewriters and calculating machines. The invention relates to line switching devices for the paper roll of writing and calculating machines, in which the one holding the paper roller in the writing position Lock consisting of two swinging symmetrical levers under the action of a spring exists, which engage with rollers in the gear teeth of the roller ratchet wheel and can be triggered before the paper roller is switched to the next line position. The invention consists in that the two levers by means of toggle links are connected to the drive rod causing the line switch that their Spreading takes place before the entry of the carriage return. The advantage of this training is the simplicity of the constructive Means and, above all, that on the drive rod to trigger the locking mechanism not a one-sided, directed transversely to its longitudinal axis, but always an axial one, pressure falling in the drive direction is exerted.
