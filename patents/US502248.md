## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Levers mounted on fixed pivots and connected to transmission members, e.g. toothed gearing

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to make a type writer in which the type bars have longer bearings than in common type-bar machines, and to this end the type bars are made and hung in a manner to secure such result.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars on common machines have short bearings, so they wobble and wear.

**Solution:** Rock shafts with long bearings are mounted tangentially and inclined on a conical ring so typebars swing to a common centre.

**How it works:** A ring shaped as the frustum of a hollow cone holds uniform hangers on its underside, with the outer and inner hangers at different heights. Each rock shaft runs between two hangers, tangent to the inner face of the ring and inclined from horizontal, so more of them fit in a given ring and the bearings are far apart. The typebar, shaft and crank lever are one piece, and the bar hangs inclined but swings to roughly horizontal and radial at the printing point. A guard stops the bars when depressed, and the crank can be arranged for push rather than pull.
