## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants a cheap, portable, noiseless writing machine that needs no inking ribbon, usable by anyone who can spell.

**Solution:** A motor-driven pointer machine with a rotary, interchangeable type-carrying plate in the carriage, pad inking in two or more colours, and a threaded power shaft that also positions the type.

**How it works:** The paper runs over a roller on a backboard serving as platen, line-spaced by a detented knob with staggered recesses for full or close spacing. A motor in a chamber of the base turns a combined power and type-operating shaft whose threads engage segmental threads on a sleeve tied to the rotary carriage plate. The operator brings the wanted character under a pointer. A stop halts the power shaft at line end or at any chosen point. Adjustable ink pads on a bedplate, with a wiping section, ink the type instead of a ribbon. The remaining mechanism is cut from the opening.

**Features:** [SEL:pointer-chart](../features/sel-pointer-chart.md), [ELEM:disc-face](../features/elem-disc-face.md), [ELEM:interchangeable](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
