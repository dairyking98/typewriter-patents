## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A compact, serviceable machine is wanted that writes upper and lower case and a full set of punctuation, with keys and typebars above and the platen reciprocating below.

**Solution:** A circle of keys and typebars on a tilting key-base above a reciprocating roller platen, with a collar-actuated typebar ring and pawl escapement.

**How it works:** The key-base is pivoted on arms and held inclined; keys are vertical rods in an annular keyboard, each linked to a typebar between its pivot and type. A collar ring supported on a rock shaft contacts the typebar heels. Pressing a key tilts the typebar to print on the platen below. The roller platen rides in a carriage in the base, with escapement, ratchet and swinging-frame feed pawls, a shift lever, ribbon reel with ratchet, ribbon guide, and a carriage stop with bell.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [IMP:platen-to-element](../features/imp-platen-to-element.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** base; key-base; key rod; typebar; collar; rock shaft; roller platen; platen carriage; escapement; ribbon reel; ratchet; shifting lever; bell
