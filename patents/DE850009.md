**How it works (reading):** The ratchet wheel shares an axis with a cable drum; two opposing cables pull the carriage, so the step depends only on the drum diameter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Gear-and-rack carriage stepping gives uneven spacing and noise and fine racks are hard to make.

**Solution:** The ratchet wheel shares an axis with a cable drum; two opposing cables pull the carriage, so the step depends only on the drum diameter.
