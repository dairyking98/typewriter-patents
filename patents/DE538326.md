## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (card sorting machine auto stop) · **Subject (patent classes):** Checking presence, absence, correct position, or moving status of cards

**How it works (reading):** A brake (oil or eddy-current) on the card lever damps its movement so the motor-relay contact opens only after a delay.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a card sorter the motor cuts off at once when the last card passes, so cards still in the line are left behind.

**Solution:** A brake (oil or eddy-current) on the card lever damps its movement so the motor-relay contact opens only after a delay.
