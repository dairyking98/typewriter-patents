## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a novel means for liberating the paper-carriage of a type-writing machine from the control of the feedingr 5 pawl in order to be able to readily place the paper-carriage in any desired position, and appertains more particularly to an improvement upon that type-writing machine known in the market as the Caligraph.
