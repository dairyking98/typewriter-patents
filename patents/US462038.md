## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a novel means for liberating the paper-carriage of a type-writing machine from the control of the feedingr 5 pawl in order to be able to readily place the paper-carriage in any desired position, and appertains more particularly to an improvement upon that type-writing machine known in the market as the Caligraph.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage-release lever on the right-hand side, independent of the line-feed handle, withdraws the feed pawl so one hand can position the carriage.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On the Caligraph, the lever that disengages the feed pawl is on the left while the carriage handle and line-feed are on the right, needing two hands.

**Solution:** A carriage-release lever on the right-hand side, independent of the line-feed handle, withdraws the feed pawl so one hand can position the carriage.

**How it works:** A lever pivoted at the right end of the frame, spring-returned, connects through a rod to a slotted finger beside the carriage-feeding pawl. Moving the carriage slightly right and pulling the handle with the forefinger lifts the pawl off the ratchet bar, freeing the carriage to be set anywhere.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** paper roller; carriage; ratchet bar; feed pawl; release lever; spring; connecting rod; slotted finger

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph: moves the carriage release to the right side, same hand as carriage and line feed
