## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punches used in banks and businesses are cumbersome and unwieldy.

**Solution:** An automatic check punch with pivoted jaws, rollers with spiral grooves, and a slide carrying an index that is set against a scale to position the punch.

**How it works:** Spiral-grooved rollers and a slide with a nose and roller move the upper and lower jaws; corrugated feed rollers draw the check across the punch.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md)
