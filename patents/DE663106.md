## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** A wafer-thin fog of solvent is sprayed evenly onto the broad side of the ribbon, which softens the dried dye so the ribbon can be used at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Dried typewriter ribbons lose colour, and earlier re-dyeing by spraying or dye rollers left blotchy results.

**Solution:** A wafer-thin fog of solvent is sprayed evenly onto the broad side of the ribbon, which softens the dried dye so the ribbon can be used at once.
