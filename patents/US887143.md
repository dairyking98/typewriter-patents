## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object is to provide means whereby multiple copies of typewritten matter may be produced upon along web of paper through the intermediary of an endless carbon band or ribbon, so that copies of many papers may be made without danger of losing the same bethe present invention copies of a great many papers may be made seriatim upon 'a single web whiclrmay be preserved for future reference.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment with an endless carbon band travelling around the platen and over a spool, so copies of many papers are made on one long web of paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making several copies one at a time needs separate carbon sheets and second sheets, which is slow.

**Solution:** An attachment with an endless carbon band travelling around the platen and over a spool, so copies of many papers are made on one long web of paper.

**Features:** [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [INK:carbon](../features/ink-carbon.md)

**Parts:** endless carbon band; long paper web; carrying reel; spool; extensible supports
