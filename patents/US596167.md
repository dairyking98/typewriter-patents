## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines cannot easily write on bound book leaves, and the last-printed character is not visible.

**Solution:** A typebar machine with converging typebars and a hammer striking at one impressing point, usable on a plain sheet or a bound book.

**How it works:** Finger keys on four rows operate levers of four lengths hinged on one axle, each carrying an upward typebar guided by pins and plates in four layers. All bars converge on a single impressing point. Each type is threaded to its bar and adjusted by nut to print in line. A hammer mechanism presses the type against the paper; a book-supporting stick and spring clamp hold bound leaves.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** key frame; key; lever; typebar; guide pin; semi-annular plate; type; hammer; book-supporting stick; spring clamp

**Numbers:** four key rows; four lever lengths
