## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guides in the printing zone, e.g. guides for preventing contact of conveyed sheets with printhead

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to devise a paper-guide which will not interfere with the free passage of the paper no matter what the width of such may be, which will not be liable to be struck by the type, and thus deleteriously afiect such type, and which it will not be necessary to adjust;

**How it works (reading):** Two curved plates with upturned lips are fixed to the bottom of the type-bar frames and extend inward and forward over the scale-bar, so the paper is guided without being struck by the type and needs no adjustment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing paper guides using U-shaped bars of graduated height can be struck by the type and must be adjusted for paper width.

**Solution:** Two curved plates with upturned lips are fixed to the bottom of the type-bar frames and extend inward and forward over the scale-bar, so the paper is guided without being struck by the type and needs no adjustment.
