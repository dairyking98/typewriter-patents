## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded pivoting catch in the lid has a beveled nose that snaps into a slot in a bracket on the base plate, so the lid engages on a straight downward push.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lids of portable typewriter cases must be lifted, held and turned to hook their rear locks, which is inconvenient.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded pivoting catch in the lid has a beveled nose that snaps into a slot in a bracket on the base plate, so the lid engages on a straight downward push.
