## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In ordinary machines the type bars are fastened to the body, so changing the alphabet (for example Russian to Latin letters) is impracticable.

**Solution:** The lower part of the semicircular type-bar frame, which holds the type bars, separates from the body by removing a screw, leaving the upper frame and keyboard in place, so one set of type bars can be replaced by another.

**How it works:** The type-bar frame is bolted to the body; the lower part with the type bars slides on guide members and comes off as a unit, while pressure levers and the junction levers stay with the body.
