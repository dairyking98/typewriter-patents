## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices actuated when platen reaches the end of a line

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to combined margin-regulating and signal-operating mechanisms for type-Writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** The left margin gage slides along the full length of a splined rock shaft that runs across the machine, and it operates the bell and key-locking devices through cams engaged by a tappet on the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The left margin gage on a wide-carriage front-strike machine can only be set over a limited range, so both gages cannot reach either extreme of carriage travel.

**Solution:** The left margin gage slides along the full length of a splined rock shaft that runs across the machine, and it operates the bell and key-locking devices through cams engaged by a tappet on the carriage.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)

**Parts:** left margin gage; rock shaft; spline bar; teeth; dog; tappet; bell and key-locking devices
