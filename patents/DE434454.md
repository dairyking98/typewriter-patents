## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand-dipping cloth printing blankets into ink is messy, slow, and warps the cloth unevenly.

**Solution:** A machine with an inking roller running in an ink container, a doctor roller metering a uniform layer, and pressure and tensioning rollers in a hinged two-part stand that feeds the wide cloth web through and dries it.
