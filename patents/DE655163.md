## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On security ticket printers the booking strip could be removed or handled without advancing, leaving a gap or leaving the last printed line accessible.

**Solution:** A ratchet outside the booking box, operated by opening its covering flap, advances the strip whenever the box is removed, so an unmistakable gap appears and the last printed line is moved inside the box.
