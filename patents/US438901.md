## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention being to provide mechanism which shall automatically reverse the direction of the movement of the inking-ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** A lever lying across the ribbon path is pushed by pins fixed near the ribbon ends and shifts a swinging plate that moves a double pawl from one spool ratchet to the other, reversing the feed automatically.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed on typewriters had to be reversed by hand when the ribbon end reached the printing point, stopping work. The carriage guide rod was also awkward to remove for access.

**Solution:** A lever lying across the ribbon path is pushed by pins fixed near the ribbon ends and shifts a swinging plate that moves a double pawl from one spool ratchet to the other, reversing the feed automatically.

**How it works:** Two ribbon spools with ratchets sit on a standard; the ribbon runs from one spool over a guide bar to the other, passing through the forked arms of an oscillating lever. Each keystroke rocks a double pawl through the key-lever bail, rock arm and link, advancing the winding spool step by step. A pin or button on the ribbon near each end catches the fork as it arrives, and the ribbon tension swings the lever. This moves a pawl-shifting lever with a resilient arm that stores force and then snaps a swinging plate over, lifting one pawl end off its ratchet and engaging the other spool, so the delivering spool becomes the winding spool. Separately, the carriage rod ends are flattened and held to the frame by thumbscrews so the whole carriage lifts off.
