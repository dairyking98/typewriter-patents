## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles characterised by being applied to printers having transversely- moving carriages and mounted on the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an accessory machine device for the production of sticker addresses non-stop, whereby (read inscribed The end of the tape is automatically wound onto a reel under a certain tension will.

**How it works (reading):** A light narrow-web attachment clamps to the paper carriage and winds the printed adhesive-label tape onto a reel under tension, so the tape does not hang free.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Roll holders for typewriter paper webs are heavy and load the carriage, or hang loose; full-width holders are cumbersome.

**Solution:** A light narrow-web attachment clamps to the paper carriage and winds the printed adhesive-label tape onto a reel under tension, so the tape does not hang free.
