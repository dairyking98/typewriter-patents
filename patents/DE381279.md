## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**How it works (reading):** Make type wheels from fine-grained ceramic mass pressed or moulded in a steel negative from one master, fired high enough for strength, with bases slightly wider to ease demoulding.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hand-engraved steel type wheels are expensive and cannot be made in quantity by a mechanical process.

**Solution:** Make type wheels from fine-grained ceramic mass pressed or moulded in a steel negative from one master, fired high enough for strength, with bases slightly wider to ease demoulding.
