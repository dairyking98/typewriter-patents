> Description Translated from German Type wheel. The manufacture of type wheels according to a mechanical Procedure is an extremely important economic issue, which is particularly important for type wheels, that have to endure heavy use (such as that of high-speed typewriters or telegraph), has not yet been resolved. Rather, these type wheels are so far Hand-cut and engraved in steel at great expense as it has been until now after no process has succeeded in converting them into usable material from another To make shape. According to the invention are great for making such character wheels fine-grain ceramic masses are used, which in a steel negative produced once pressed in or by a method known per se in ceramic technology be shaped according to a suitable steel model. This enables you, after a one-off of Hand made engraving to carry out a very large number of character wheels. The use of ceramic bodies used to be avoided, because it was feared that ceramic masses were too fragile, and also because it was Was concerned that the letters would not come out sharp enough, and thirdly, because porcelain does not take on any color.
