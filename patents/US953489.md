## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inventor wants a portable, economical, durable typewriter occupying minimal space, with many characters available from a single flexible type disk.

**Solution:** A rubber type disk carried on a small hand-moved carriage is turned by a pointer arm over a serrated-edge dial, and pressing the carriage down onto a rolling platen prints the selected type while an alternating tooth pair advances the carriage one space.

**How it works:** The operator swings a selecting arm whose finder drops into a saw-toothed flange around a stationary character plate; the arm is fixed to a flexible rubber type disk with two concentric rows of types, so the chosen type pad is presented over an opening in a felt inking pad (which inks it as it passes). The platform carriage is then pressed down by hand, a presser foot forcing the aligned type through the inking-pad opening onto the paper on a roller platen, the inking pad shielding all other types. The carriage is pivoted on a rod with a rack bar and slides on a guide rod, and is pulled along by a spring-wound wheel. A fixed tooth and a spring-yielding tooth engage a toothed spacing bar alternately as the carriage is pressed down and let up, so each print steps the carriage one space; a blank space on the disk gives a space without printing. A foot lever shifts the platen roller sideways against a spring to receive the outer (capital) row instead of the inner row, and the roller has a ratchet line-space pawl.
