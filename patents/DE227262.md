## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Writing letter by letter is slow; a syllable could be struck at once if the keyboard were split into groups by position relative to the basic vowel.

**Solution:** A keyboard of eight key groups (three before the vowel, the vowel group, four after) printing all letters of a syllable simultaneously, with the carriage advanced by two independent spring-drum feeds, one on key press and one on release.

**How it works:** Groups A3 A2 A1 B P1 P2 P3 P4 hold letters by their usual position in a syllable; one key in each group is pressed, and the types strike eight adjacent points together. Space and five shift keys serve the little fingers, with thumbs on groups B and P1. The carriage moves by a number of steps equal to the highest-numbered group used, part on press and part on release, through two ratchet feeds on a shaft each driven by its own wheel of one spring drum.

**Parts:** key group; space key; shift key; shaft; double gear wheel; spring drum; drive wheel; paper carriage

**Numbers:** 8 key groups; syllables up to 8 letters; about a dozen letters in groups A2 and P3; about twenty letters in groups A1, B, P1; 5 shift keys
