## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**How it works (reading):** The key is clamped rigidly to the key lever by an angle lever that presses a leaf spring into a cutout in the key lever's tab, a firm non-releasing clamp that does not stress the lever when the key is fitted or removed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Interchangeable keys that rely on resilient clips do not hold securely on the key lever, so they come loose.

**Solution:** The key is clamped rigidly to the key lever by an angle lever that presses a leaf spring into a cutout in the key lever's tab, a firm non-releasing clamp that does not stress the lever when the key is fitted or removed.
