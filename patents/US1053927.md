## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators cannot see the sheet end, and legal and thesis work needs set bottom margins.

**Solution:** A sheet-controlled system signals the sheet end and measures line by line the remaining unused paper beginning well up the sheet.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
