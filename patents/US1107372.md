## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens; Pin traction elements other than wheels, e.g. pins on endless bands

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to sheet-supporting and feeding devices of typewriting machines, especially those having revoluble platens, and particularly to means for facilitating the assembling or collating of work-sheets, one upon another, in a variety of relationships;

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work sheets were hard to assemble and convey without disarrangement to the printing position.

**Solution:** Endless bands extend from the revolving platen to support and feed sheets, bringing the first blank line to the printing point.

**How it works:** Bands carry the sheets from a collating area to the platen, where they are fed to printing position.
