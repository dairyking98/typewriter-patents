## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Correcting errors needs the carriage moved back, but the carriage can only be returned by grasping and hand-moving it, which takes care and time.

**Solution:** A lever with a draft-rod and dog or pawl engages the ratchet wheel on the letter-spacing shaft so that each rocking stroke backspaces the carriage one space.

**How it works:** A rocking lever pivoted on the frame carries a draft-rod to a pawl; the pawl tooth engages the ratchet on the shaft that the rack pinion drives, turning it back one step per stroke.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** rocking lever; draft-rod; pawl; ratchet wheel; pinion; rack
