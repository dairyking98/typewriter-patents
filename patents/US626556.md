## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen should make a record copy of everything typed without extra paper handling.

**Solution:** The platen carries spring-pressed paper rollers at its ends that take a paper roll and draw it around the platen so the typed matter is recorded.
