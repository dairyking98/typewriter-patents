## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**How it works (reading):** A layered cushion of adhesive fabric tape, saturated with viscous material, stops the type bar with little rebound.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars rebound and make noise when they strike the stop.

**Solution:** A layered cushion of adhesive fabric tape, saturated with viscous material, stops the type bar with little rebound.
