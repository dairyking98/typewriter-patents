## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**How it works (reading):** Anti-friction roller spacers between carriage truck members, with spring-pressed locking connections so long or short platens and frames can be used interchangeably.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platens of differing length must be interchangeable on the same carriage truck, and the spacing must be smooth.

**Solution:** Anti-friction roller spacers between carriage truck members, with spring-pressed locking connections so long or short platens and frames can be used interchangeably.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
