## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Turning the platen back by a set amount needed a simple, reliable device operable by one key press.

**Solution:** A press key swings a cam that lifts a spring-lowered sector gear into the platen's toothed bar, with an adjustable disc limiting how far it turns back.

**How it works:** A sector gear slides in an upright slot and is drawn down by a spring out of mesh with the toothed bar on the paper cylinder. Pressing the reverse key moves a link that turns a cam piece straddling the gear; the cam face rides a fixed roller and lifts the gear into engagement, and the gear then turns with the cam piece, reversing the cylinder, until a pin on the gear meets an adjustable travel-limiting disc. The gear then drops out and returns. A separate feed-adjusting key sets the disc through links and a detented sector, so the reverse amount is chosen in advance.
