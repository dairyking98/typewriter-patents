## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Appliances specially adapted for applying liquid, e.g. using roller or ball using a roller, a disc or a ball; Arrangements for producing a permanent visual presentation of the output data, e.g. computer output printers using printers

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon-feed mechanism that moves the ribbon both laterally and longitudinally, with ribbon-protecting plates that lift and a ribbon guide.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The inventors say the ribbon needs hand-operated feed, automatic lateral and longitudinal feed so the whole ribbon is used, and better guiding and protection.

**Solution:** Ribbon-feed mechanism that moves the ribbon both laterally and longitudinally, with ribbon-protecting plates that lift and a ribbon guide.
