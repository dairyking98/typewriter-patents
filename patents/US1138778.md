## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cheque stamping machines

**How it works (reading):** A type-carrying member with words on a series of rows is mounted to move rotatively and axially, driven by one manually operable member, so the selection and platen feed are coordinated.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check-writing machines need a single manual member that both selects the amount-word character and advances the work.

**Solution:** A type-carrying member with words on a series of rows is mounted to move rotatively and axially, driven by one manually operable member, so the selection and platen feed are coordinated.
