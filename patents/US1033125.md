## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be turned freely by hand to any position, and then reconnected to normal line spacing, which needs a convenient throw-off.

**Solution:** A throw-off connects or disconnects the platen hub from the regular line space mechanism through a centering pin and a loose sleeve on a bearing sleeve attached to the carriage frame.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)
