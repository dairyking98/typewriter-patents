## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (complement counter for accounting machines)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a complement counter with a volatile one-bringing device for Accounting machines, in particular typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A locking device for the transmission element is engaged while the complement is introduced, so the gear train cannot overrun.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Overthrow of the lowest computing gear when the complement is entered by a sudden hand-slide action gives wrong digits.

**Solution:** A locking device for the transmission element is engaged while the complement is introduced, so the gear train cannot overrun.
