## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools in small portable typewriters fall out or shift, especially in transport or when caught by the ribbon.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded arm on a bracket on the housing floor engages over the lower flange of the spool to hold it, and releases automatically when the spool is lifted out, with no change to the spool.
