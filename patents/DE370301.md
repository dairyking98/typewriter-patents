## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type wheels need to be swapped quickly, one-handed, when mixing fonts in a document, and existing spring latches only hold the body without helping removal.

**Solution:** A push-button latch on the shaft that, when pressed, releases the wheel and lets a spring lift it off the shaft by itself.

**How it works:** A sleeve on the shaft carries a U-shaped spring whose two legs end in tapered locking lugs poking out through slots. A push button above them has a hole into which the lug tips fit, and its inclined faces push the lugs outward. The wheel is pushed down over the sleeve, compressing a helical spring, and the lugs snap out above it, holding it against the spring's pressure so it sits fixed without play. Pressing the button pulls the lugs back into the sleeve, and the helical spring then throws the wheel up so it can be taken in one hand while the next goes on. The same can be formed in the shaft itself and used for knobs, discs and other bodies.
