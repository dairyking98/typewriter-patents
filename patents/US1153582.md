## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A keyboard machine with a key-released mechanical action has a second locking device that stops repeated strokes of the same key.

**Solution:** A device disables the additional locking device when the key is held down, so a repeatedly struck key can keep the mechanism working without interruption.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Power-driven type action](../features/bar-power-action.md)
