## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special supports, platforms or trolleys for supporting machines on tables

**How it works (reading):** The typewriter stands on its own feet directly on the floor through holes or a section cut in the table, or on resilient spring supports that do not transfer vibration to the floor.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Felt or rubber pads between typewriter and table do not stop noise because the table top acts as a soundboard that amplifies the typing.

**Solution:** The typewriter stands on its own feet directly on the floor through holes or a section cut in the table, or on resilient spring supports that do not transfer vibration to the floor.
