## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter that can write on the pages of books as well as on ordinary paper.

**Solution:** A hinged carriage frame slides on longitudinal bars over a main frame that holds the book, with lifters to raise the book to the strike level.

**How it works:** A rectangular frame on posts carries two longitudinal lifter bars hung on straps; a handle with ratchet pawl lifts them under each side of the book spine to bring the page to the type. Adjustable angular cross-bars hold the book edges. A second frame carrying the typewriter carriage slides on longitudinal bars, hinged so it can be raised. Rack-bars, levers, and a pinion shaft control letter and word spacing, and a device on the frame bars sets line spacing.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** main frame; lifter bar; strap; ratchet handle; cross-bar; carriage frame; rack-bar; pinion; disk
