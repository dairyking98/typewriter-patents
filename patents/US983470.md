## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The main objects of my present invention is to overcome these difficulties and to provide simple and efficient means for preventing the types from strikingthe paper fingers or the paper feeding devices irrespective of the positions to which said devices may be adjusted alongthe platen.

**How it works (reading):** The paper fingers and feeding devices are kept out of the type-bar paths at all adjusted positions along the platen, and the paper is still fed correctly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars strike the marginal paper fingers and paper feed devices, which damages the type faces.

**Solution:** The paper fingers and feeding devices are kept out of the type-bar paths at all adjusted positions along the platen, and the paper is still fed correctly.
