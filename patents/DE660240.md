**Problem:** Auxiliary ribbon spools need a single common locking and braking device.

**Solution:** A lever presses the spools against a compression spring on a shaft parallel to the spool shaft, giving common locking and braking.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a ribbon switching and locking device, in particular for Typewriters in which the copies are made with the help of auxiliary ink ribbons whose coils are arranged on the carriage, according to patent 658 822. In the main patent, a device was described in which the detection and Braking of the auxiliary ribbon spools was done by one and the same device. As a special Embodiment, an arrangement has been described which consists of one or more friction or braking parts that are attached to attack the coil or coils or on a part connected to it, these Brake parts sit on a shaft which is arranged parallel to the spool shaft. According to the present invention, there is now the common locking and braking device for the auxiliary ribbon spools from a lever through which the spool or Coils can be pressed against a compression spring arranged on the shaft.
