## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The support for the type bars and their coacting mechanism needs to be simple and efficient.

**Solution:** A hanger-support crosswise of the frame carries type bar hangers on both sides, with lever supports and levers joined to the key bars by links so each key drives its type bar.
