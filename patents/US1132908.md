**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of the invention being to produce a satisfactory platen-clutch of, an especially simple and compact construction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A fractional line-spacing platen clutch that is simple and compact is needed.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded block in the platen shaft normally connects the platen to the line-spacing wheel through a slotted cylindrical clutch body; a push rod inside the hub lets the platen be rotated separately in the carriage.
