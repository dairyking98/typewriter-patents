## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operating shift with the little finger slows typing and forces the fingering to change.

**Solution:** Split the space bar, using the left part to operate shift and the right part for spacing.

**How it works:** The single keyboard machine shifts the platen or type basket sideways for capitals. The space bar is divided near its centre; the left half is connected through a bent rod to the shift mechanism and the right half still spaces. It is made as an attachment for machines already in use.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** split space bar; bent rod; shift mechanism; platen; type basket

**Improves on:**

- little-finger shift key: shift moved to a thumb bar in front of the keyboard
