## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key machine with power drive) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source

**How it works (reading):** Each keystroke trips an intermediate slide or rocker link that briefly engages the clutch with the power drive, and a blocking device stops repeated engagement until the key returns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In power-driven key machines the key must not do the work, yet the clutch must be engaged each time a key is pressed.

**Solution:** Each keystroke trips an intermediate slide or rocker link that briefly engages the clutch with the power drive, and a blocking device stops repeated engagement until the key returns.

> Description Translated from German Key machine with power drive. With typewriters, printing machines, arithmetic machines and card punching machines and the like, it is desirable to prevent the keyboard operator from operating to relieve the working organs of the machine at the keystroke and you only to leave the triggering of a power drive for the working organs. With such Machines with a power drive and keyboard therefore require a clutch to be engaged between the force organ and the working organ (type lever, calculating wheel, punch and the like) of the machine, which must be effected by the key. In the key machine subject of the invention, there is a particularly expedient design of the mechanism actuated by the respective key for engaging the clutch between the force organ and the working organ thereby. achieved that an intermediate member is provided between the coupling member and the button which is triggered each time a key is pressed and then in frictional connection with the coupling member occurs and through this during whose operation is returned to the rest position.
