## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to stencil machines.

**How it works (reading):** A stencil machine with a hand lever for cutting, an adjustable supporting stud and screw for positioning the stencil blank, and a feed wheel with an idler, usable standing or sitting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil cutting machines are complicated, unsafe for the operator, and hard to adjust and center.

**Solution:** A stencil machine with a hand lever for cutting, an adjustable supporting stud and screw for positioning the stencil blank, and a feed wheel with an idler, usable standing or sitting.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
