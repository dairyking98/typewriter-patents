## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stowable tables with detachable top leaves

**How it works (reading):** Scissor-lever pairs at each foot let the feet shorten and adjust to any width, locked by a tension lever at each pivot.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Folding typewriter-table feet cannot adapt to different machine footprints.

**Solution:** Scissor-lever pairs at each foot let the feet shorten and adjust to any width, locked by a tension lever at each pivot.
