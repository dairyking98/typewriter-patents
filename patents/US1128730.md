## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor's earlier roll-paper attachment (US1096202) needed special side members and did not fit typewriters of varying construction or follow the carriage.

**Solution:** A frame on an adjustable post carries paper rolls in a traveling inner frame that shifts sideways automatically with the typewriter carriage.

**How it works:** An outer rectangular frame with legs rests in a hollow post at any height, with grooved tracks on its top and bottom bars. An inner traveling frame with anti-friction wheels runs in the tracks, carrying one or more paper roll shafts set in equally spaced holes in the end members. Adjustable disks on each shaft hold a roll of any width, and a pressure roller of rough rubber on a curved flat steel spring bears on the roll. A forked adjustable device links the roll frame to the paper carriage so it follows its movement.
