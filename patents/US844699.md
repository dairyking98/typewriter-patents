## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On under-strike machines the writing is hidden, so after an interruption the operator cannot tell whether a space or a letter was last struck, causing missed or double spaces.

**Solution:** A target indicator driven by the escapement-operating plate that shows the word Letter or Space.

**How it works:** Applied to a Smith-Premier. Key rock-shafts strike an escapement-operating plate that moves right and back for each letter or space. A flat plate on it carries a slot engaging the target's lower end. Linkage of a shoulder-plate, a space-plate on the spacer rod and a spring-connected shifter swings the pivoted target. Either LETTER or SPACE shows from behind the cover plate.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
