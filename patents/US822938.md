## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-levers are spaced wider than the typebars, so the linkage between them tends to rock and bind; typebar joints also wear.

**Solution:** Fan-shaped horizontal sublevers connect the key-levers to more closely spaced upright sublevers, with bearings in line with their connections so they cannot rock, plus a hardened-steel ring in the typebar joint.

**How it works:** Each key-lever connects to a horizontal sublever, arranged in a fan, whose other end connects to an upright sublever. The upright sublevers sit closer together than the keys and drive the front-strike typebars. The operative points of each fan sublever lie in a straight line with its intermediate bearing, which prevents rocking. Springs on the sublever support bar throw the type back from the platen to quicken return and prevent double impression. The typebar joint is a U-section bar around a hanger with a hardened-steel ring riveted to it.
