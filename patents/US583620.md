## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stenographic printer must make lines without carriage return.

**Solution:** Printing bars and moving anvils print dots in transverse lines while paper steps, giving automatic line-spacing.

**How it works:** A few keys actuate printing bars; revolving anvils pass along the bars in turn, forcing the ribbon to the paper, while the paper feeds step by step.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
- **Uses:** [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md)

**Parts:** key; printing bar; anvil shaft; ribbon shaft; paper roller; universal bar
