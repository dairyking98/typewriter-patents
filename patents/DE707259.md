**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing unit for high-speed calculating machines with one-piece type bars at the height of the pressure zone with moved by spring force and suddenly for the pressure Approved pressure links coupled positively as well as type rods and pressure links at the height of the pressure zone with little play in Kärnrnen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars and pressure links of a high-speed calculating machine's printing unit shake after printing, degrading print quality.

**Solution:** Slide-type pressure members and the type bars coupled to them are pulled away from the platen with a jerk right after printing, by cam links on the main shaft.

**How it works:** Printing unit on a high-speed calculator; type bars are lifted into the print position by links, and spring-moved pressure members strike; a cam-controlled jerk withdraws them immediately after impact.
