## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a mounting for typewriter carriages, such as serves to carry the platen, paper guide, and other mechanism usually mounted on a carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Idle rollers mounted independently of the trackway, on a channelled bar, so the trackway can be short and self-clearing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage rear rollers run on trackways that collect dirt and make carriage travel jerky.

**Solution:** Idle rollers mounted independently of the trackway, on a channelled bar, so the trackway can be short and self-clearing.
