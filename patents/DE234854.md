## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-space pawls either engage constantly, making noise when the platen is turned freely, or need extra parts to be lifted off.

**Solution:** Pivot the feed pawl on the line-space lever and let friction against a plate on the lever's arm swing it into the ratchet on the pull stroke and out of it on the return.

**How it works:** The line-space lever, pivoted on the carriage frame, carries a pawl on a pin at the end of a branch arm. A lateral lug on the pawl rubs a friction plate on the arm, so when the lever is pulled the pawl lags and rotates into the ratchet teeth, turning the platen. A fixed curved stop limits the upward stroke, after which the lever pulls the carriage back. On release a spring returns the lever and the friction swings the pawl clear, so the platen turns silently. A rotatable stepped stop sets how many teeth the pawl advances.
