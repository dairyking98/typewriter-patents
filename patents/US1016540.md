## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to retarding mechanism for typewriting machines and has for its main object to provide improved means for controlling and retarding the motion of the carriage when said carriage is released from its step-by-step feed devices as for eXample during tabulating operations.

**How it works (reading):** A retarding mechanism that controls and slows carriage motion after release from its step feed.
