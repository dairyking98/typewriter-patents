## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar pivots lose adjustment, giving lash and lost motion that spoil alignment, and the fault is hard to cure.

**Solution:** A two-part hanger holds the conical pivot studs of the typebar at three points in V-notches, with a screw-adjusted spring strip that takes up wear.

**How it works:** The typebar has tapered pivot studs on both sides. A rigid trough-shaped hanger member fixed to the machine ring has cheek-pieces with V-notches, with steeper sides than the studs' taper so contact is at points. A spring metal strip riveted to the hanger back carries ear-pieces with beveled lower edges that bear on the tops of the studs. A vertical screw through the strip presses the ears down and takes up wear. Each stud is held at three points near its end, and the quarter-circle swing never brings worn parts to the contacts.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type bar; conoidal pivot stud; main hanger member; cheek-piece; V-notch; spring strip; ear-piece; adjusting screw; supporting ring

**Improves on:**

- earlier type-bar hangers: three-point conical bearing with screw take-up replaces bearings that wear loose
