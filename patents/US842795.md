## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On front-strike machines with a vertically shifting platen, the front paper-guide rides on the platen so its whole weight is borne by the platen when raised.

**Solution:** Mount the front paper-guide rollers on spring-pressed pivoted arms on sliding shoes on the carriage so they follow the platen's vertical shift independently.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on antifriction rollers along guide bars. A dovetail guide on the front side bar carries sliding shoes, each with a pivoted arm ending in a forked paper-guide roller. A pin in a slot limits the arm's swing and a coil spring presses the roller onto the paper on the platen. The arms follow platen lift without moving the carriage, and the shoes can be slid across the whole platen width.
