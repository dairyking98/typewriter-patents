## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventors want a simpler, more durable center-strike typewriter.

**Solution:** A ring of pendent typebar arms on hanger blocks with guides and stops, each driven by its own key-lever and draw-bar through a crank-rocker, all printing at a common central point.

**How it works:** Typebar arms hang from hanger blocks in a type-bar ring and swing up to strike at the center. Each is joined to its key-lever by a draw-bar supported by a crank-rocker and a rear hanger. The draw-bars are made in different lengths in groups of six. Guides and stops position each arm at the printing point. A carriage runs over the top plate.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** type-bar ring; type-bar arm; hanger block; guide; stop; key-lever; draw-bar; crank-rocker; rear hanger; basket; carriage

**Numbers:** draw-bars in groups of six lengths; 7 sheets of drawings
