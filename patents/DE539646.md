## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A rocker-type pawl can swing out of the escape wheel so the carriage steps twice, and the carriage cannot be lifted without a special means.

**Solution:** The pawl is a rocker pulled into a dead-centre position by a tension spring and stopped in both directions; a support on the ratchet axle is dragged by friction and holds the pawl in the ratchet teeth during a return or carriage lift.

**How it works:** The rocker rests at a dead centre without swinging out; friction from the escape wheel moves a loose support until it hits a fixed stop, which holds the pawl in the teeth when the carriage is lifted.
