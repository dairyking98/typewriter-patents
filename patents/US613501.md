## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Erasing or correcting tables

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a type-writing machine with an attachment whereby errors in type-writing, more particularly' of single characters, may be quickly corrected by erasure while the paper remains in position on the machine.

**How it works (reading):** Erasing disc mounted below the platen, with the platen moved to it by a shift lever.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasing a wrong character requires raising the carriage.

**Solution:** Erasing disc mounted below the platen, with the platen moved to it by a shift lever.

**How it works:** A rotary rubber disc on fixed bearings is driven independently; the platen is brought up to it (using the shift key linkage) while the ribbon is moved out of the way.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** erasing disc; shaft; platen; guide rail; hinged lever; shift key
