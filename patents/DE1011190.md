## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (form setup controlled by payment cards) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the present invention is to meet the above-mentioned needs in form printing by that the machine except with the usual switching devices for line feed and for Form switching is still equipped with an additional switching device, which is the printing document each time a fixed switching path is granted.

**How it works (reading):** An extra switching device advances the form a fixed path each time, under card-controlled engagement, so form fields print without monitoring the print line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Forms printed from counting cards need preprinted headers and fields at fixed intervals, which the normal line feed cannot supply.

**Solution:** An extra switching device advances the form a fixed path each time, under card-controlled engagement, so form fields print without monitoring the print line.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
