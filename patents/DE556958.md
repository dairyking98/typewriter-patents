## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**How it works (reading):** A transparent, sealed rubber air cushion is placed between the key top and a transparent elastic keycap, so the air compresses and absorbs the shock of each key press.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hard key tops hurt the fingertips and dull the touch, while earlier elastic caps and springs in caps were unreliable or dangerous.

**Solution:** A transparent, sealed rubber air cushion is placed between the key top and a transparent elastic keycap, so the air compresses and absorbs the shock of each key press.
