## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**How it works (reading):** The column stop is made of two parts adjustable against each other within one type width, one engaging the tooth rod and the other carrying the actual stop, so the stop can be fine-tuned between teeth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A column stop on a toothed rod can only sit at the tooth pitch, so column rules and printed numbers often do not line up with the form's headings.

**Solution:** The column stop is made of two parts adjustable against each other within one type width, one engaging the tooth rod and the other carrying the actual stop, so the stop can be fine-tuned between teeth.
