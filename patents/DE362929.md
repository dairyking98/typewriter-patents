The interlocking of these Column pole corresponds exactly to the division of the calculating machine printing unit, i.e. the distance from one type to another, or the width of the types of typewriters. It is but now it is not possible to always clamp the forms in such a way that the rubrics separating lines coincide exactly with the tooth pitch of the column rod, rather the numbers often appear on or above the line of the heading when writing, which means the overall impression of a written form is significantly impaired.
