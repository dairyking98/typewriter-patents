## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guards, shields or dust excluders

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded cover can be opened only after the lock is released, and the lock blocks opening while carriage parts are in the way.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A hinged typewriter cover is hard to open when the carriage is in the way, and the lock must be released in several steps.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded cover can be opened only after the lock is released, and the lock blocks opening while carriage parts are in the way.
