## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typebar writer's paper carriage needs an easy way to space lines, expose the printed line for inspection, warn of line end, and re-position the paper to insert an omitted letter.

**Solution:** A light open paper-carriage frame, hinged on a spring-drawn sliding frame, with an adjustable line-space lever, a reverse-rotation lever to show the printed line, a scale and fixed type-centre pointer, and a line-end warning disc.

**How it works:** The platen sits in a rectangular rod frame hinged to a frame that slides on a guide rail and is drawn left by a constant-tension spring, advancing one letter width after each type strikes. A hand lever carries a dog that turns a platen ratchet; a pivoted space regulator with two faces limits the sweep to one or two teeth. A second lever on the platen axle enters the ratchet and rotates the platen backward by one sweep to bring the printed line to the top and then returns it. A traveler on a bar with a graduated scale shows carriage position, and an adjustable arm on the carriage rod flips a light disc out from behind a dark casing near line end as a warning. A stationary pointer marks the type centre so that omitted letters can be inserted by aligning the line with the pointer.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** paper-carriage frame; platen; guide rail; sliding frame; line-space lever; dog; ratchet wheel; space regulator; reverse lever; scale; traveler; pointer; type-centre indicator; line-end disc; adjustable arm; spring paper-bar; guide wire
