## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line lock device for typewriters with switchable type lever segment and from a switchover rail and the space key from powered Zeilenschaltvo @ rrichtung, at.

**How it works (reading):** A two-part locking hook engages edge stops on the paper carriage in both segment shift positions, so one hook serves both positions without changing the ratchet assembly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Locking the space key and writing keys together on machines with switchable type-lever segments needed complicated ratchet and drive parts.

**Solution:** A two-part locking hook engages edge stops on the paper carriage in both segment shift positions, so one hook serves both positions without changing the ratchet assembly.
