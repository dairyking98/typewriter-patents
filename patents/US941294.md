## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens with balancing means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in a platen for a typewriter to which the paper is applied for the purpose-of writing.

**How it works (reading):** A worm and gear mounted on a bracket slot move the platen longitudinally, and a pointer indicator on the paper finger shows horizontal and vertical alignment, letting the platen be set accurately by turning the wheels.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A removed sheet must be returned and re-aligned precisely so one letter or figure can be replaced, which is hard to do by hand.

**Solution:** A worm and gear mounted on a bracket slot move the platen longitudinally, and a pointer indicator on the paper finger shows horizontal and vertical alignment, letting the platen be set accurately by turning the wheels.
