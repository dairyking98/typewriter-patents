## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The space between lines must be variable, and the platen must be locked against accidental rotation.

**Solution:** An eccentric and bell-crank line-space pawl vary the platen rotation, and a lock holds the ratchet in position.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
