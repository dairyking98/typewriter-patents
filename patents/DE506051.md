## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a tabulator device for typewriters, in which the Pressing the tab key frees the carriage from the rear derailleur and a tab stop moved in the way of a stop on the carriage, whereupon the tab stop is held by a lock that is only triggered when the carriage stop hits the tab stop.

**How it works (reading):** A tab stop rides on a spring slider in an elongated hole, and the holding pawl is one unit with the handle part.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tab latch levers need separate parts with their own springs and release pieces.

**Solution:** A tab stop rides on a spring slider in an elongated hole, and the holding pawl is one unit with the handle part.
