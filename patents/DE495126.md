> Description Translated from German Device for cutting conveying moving, warm and plastic State of the glass ribbons The invention relates to one for cutting of moving glass ribbons in a warm and plastic state certain device in which the glass ribbon is cut by a knife, which is rotatable about an axis lying transversely to the conveying device of the glass. In a known device of this type, in which a cutting of the Manufacture of rolled-out glass ribbons that are used for window glass, i.e. thin ones narrow strips of glass, the cutting edge of the knife is parallel to the axis attached to the knife body and engages in a corresponding groove when cutting of a counter body. With this device, the cut is made all at once the entire width of the ribbon of glass, which is particularly important when it is wide thick glass ribbons require a not inconsiderable amount of force and stress of the knife conditionally. In addition, the known device has the disadvantage that cutting edge and counter-groove sit on the forming rollers, so that only plates from one and of the same length, of a length corresponding to the circumference of these rollers, can be cut.
