## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (glass ribbon cutting device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Severing glass sheets, tubes or rods while still plastic by cutting

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to one for cutting of moving glass ribbons in a warm and plastic state certain device in which the glass ribbon is cut by a knife, which is rotatable about an axis lying transversely to the conveying device of the glass.

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable knife with a helical edge rolls along a counter-rail, cutting the ribbon gradually from edge to edge; the cut is done automatically by the moving conveyor.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A single full-width cut of thick warm glass ribbon needs much force and stresses the knife.

**Solution:** A rotatable knife with a helical edge rolls along a counter-rail, cutting the ribbon gradually from edge to edge; the cut is done automatically by the moving conveyor.
