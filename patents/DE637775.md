## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine red printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** Sliding the paper carriage over a counter stop switches the ribbon to the other colour regardless of final carriage position, and the switch resets automatically on the next operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing a chosen column in red needed a separate stop and a second keystroke, so the ribbon colour change was awkward.

**Solution:** Sliding the paper carriage over a counter stop switches the ribbon to the other colour regardless of final carriage position, and the switch resets automatically on the next operation.

It may be desirable, regardless of the final one Adjustment of the paper cart to print certain amounts in red. Such a case lies z. With the previously known machines one had to perform this task on both the debt balance and a stop at the point of the paper carriage corresponding to the credit balance column for the ribbon reversal.
