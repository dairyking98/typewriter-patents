## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter small enough for the vest pocket, attached to a tablet of paper.

**Solution:** A cup-shaped case holds a type disk with radial arms; a knob turns it to a character on a dial and pressing it prints through an ink pad.

**How it works:** The disk of slitted radial arms with cupped ends carrying type sits under a dial of characters. Turning the knob with its pointer brings a type over an opening; depressing the knob lowers the arms to the ink pad and a screw pushes that arm to the paper. The screw adjusts impression force; a spring returns the disk and a dog spaces.
