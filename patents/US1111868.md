**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** A releasing sector and ratchet disk let the platen be disconnected from the line spacing mechanism and reconnected.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen needed to be connected to and disconnected from the line spacing mechanism.

**Solution:** A releasing sector and ratchet disk let the platen be disconnected from the line spacing mechanism and reconnected.

**How it works:** A pawl sector engages a ratchet disk on the platen, and a releasing sector disengages it.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
