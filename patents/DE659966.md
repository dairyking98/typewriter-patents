**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a support for a device that pulls typewriters of lines and the underlining of what is written.

**How it works (reading):** A carrier fixed to the type guide holds the ink-cushion line wheel so the underline falls at the usual distance without platen adjustment and without fouling the ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter lining and underlining devices required platen adjustment and interfered with the ribbon.

**Solution:** A carrier fixed to the type guide holds the ink-cushion line wheel so the underline falls at the usual distance without platen adjustment and without fouling the ribbon.
