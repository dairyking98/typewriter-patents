## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage feed should move in proportion to the number of keys depressed.

**Solution:** A spring drum drives the carriage, with a spacer cam and suspension bars whose lateral motion sets the carriage step in proportion to the keys pressed.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
