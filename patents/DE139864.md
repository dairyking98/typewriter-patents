## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE. The present invention relates to a paper feed device on typewriters and is characterized in that an endless belt with a width equal to the length of the Paper roller is used to guide the paper, which is not rubbed by the Paper roll or the paper located on the latter, but inevitably independent of it is driven with the paper roller at the same time. This will have major benefits achieved. Not only can papers with the full width equal to the length of the paper roller, but also those of smaller width, such as cards, envelopes, with equal ease around the paper roller. The inevitable movement of the wide endless belt creates a much greater security of the paper feed than was previously achieved by the movement of parts by means of friction was possible alone.
