## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Conveyor belts or like feeding devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a paper feed device on typewriters and is characterized in that an endless belt with a width equal to the length of the Paper roller is used to guide the paper, which is not rubbed by the Paper roll or the paper located on the latter, but inevitably independent of it is driven with the paper roller at the same time.

**How it works (reading):** A wide endless belt, the width of the paper roller, rests on the roller and is driven together with it so the paper is fed positively, including narrow cards.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guided over the paper roller by friction alone slips, and narrow cards and envelopes are hard to feed.

**Solution:** A wide endless belt, the width of the paper roller, rests on the roller and is driven together with it so the paper is fed positively, including narrow cards.

This will have major benefits achieved. Not only can papers with the full width equal to the length of the paper roller, but also those of smaller width, such as cards, envelopes, with equal ease around the paper roller. The inevitable movement of the wide endless belt creates a much greater security of the paper feed than was previously achieved by the movement of parts by means of friction was possible alone.
