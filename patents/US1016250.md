## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Condensed billing needs paper controlled and guided in ways the standard feed does not provide.

**Solution:** Paper fingers, feed rollers and guides on a Monarch carriage control and guide the billing sheet, with released and held positions.
