## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Endwise sliding typebars need low-friction guiding and well aligned actuating parts.

**Solution:** Guide the sliding type bars between arcs of guide pins on the bed and under a plate, with actuating parts kept in alignment.

**How it works:** Endwise sliding type bars rest on a two-part bed with a plate above bearing on their upper edges to prevent vertical movement. Two arcuate series of guide pins stand up from the lower plate and another series hangs from the upper one, engaging the bars' sides so they slide toward the platen. The actuating mechanism is arranged to cut friction.
