## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of our invention is to provide simple, inexpensive, and durable mechanism to cause each finger-key to alternately and successively actuate two type.
