## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Each typebar needs to print two characters with simple, inexpensive and durable mechanism.

**Solution:** A compound typebar: a rock-shaft in the bar carries a type-block with two printing faces at intersecting planes, turned between positions by a rotatable slotted ring over the type basket.

**How it works:** A typebar pivoted in a bifurcated bearing is raised by a link from its key lever. A rock-shaft journaled in the bar carries a crank and a type-block with two faces in intersecting planes. A slotted ring or cap above the basket of type engages every crank; shifting it through a link and cord or belt from a shift key rotates all type-blocks so one or the other face is presented. A toggle joint throws the type against the paper.
