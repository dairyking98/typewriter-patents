## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On front-strike machines with a shiftable platen the ribbon vibrator cannot use the whole ribbon width, and the automatic cross feed cannot be turned off.

**Solution:** A ribbon vibrator with a single rest position is thrown different distances to use different widthwise parts of the ribbon, with the throw increased when the platen is shifted.

**How it works:** The ribbon vibrator or carrier sits in one normal position and is thrown by varying amounts to bring different ribbon fields to the print point. A shiftable frame controlled by an eccentric sets the extent of throw, and hand-operated devices set it. When the platen is shifted to upper case the throw is increased to follow the changed printing point. Automatic cross feeding can be dispensed with in favour of selecting a predetermined ribbon field. Longitudinal feed is by bevel pinions from the ribbon spool shafts. Shown on a No. 10 Remington typebar machine with spring-drum carriage.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; shiftable frame; eccentric; ribbon spool; bevel pinion; platen carrier; platen; shift lever; universal bar; spring drum

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 10 ribbon vibrator: uses full ribbon width and selectable ribbon fields with a shifted platen
