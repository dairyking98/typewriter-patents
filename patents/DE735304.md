## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (vehicle preselector gearbox) · **Subject (patent classes):** Control devices for speed-changing geared mechanisms, e.g. specially adapted couplings for synchronising devices, devices to simplify control, control of auxiliary gearboxes with a preselection system, mainly semi-automatic, e.g. with automatic preselection, but controlled at the intended moment, with force amplification; Control units where shifting is directly initiated by the driver, e.g. semi-automatic transmissions

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The object has been achieved in such a way that that to actuate the preselector in a known white one of the output shaft of the transmission powered energy source is used and @ that the selected Gear by the driver by setting the engine control lever, preferably to idle, can be switched on.

**How it works (reading):** An oil pump on the output shaft powers the preselector. The driver confirms the preselected gear by setting the engine control lever to idle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A fully automatic gearbox removes driver control of gear selection, and the existing preselector control is inconvenient.

**Solution:** An oil pump on the output shaft powers the preselector. The driver confirms the preselected gear by setting the engine control lever to idle.
