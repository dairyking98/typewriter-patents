## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the paper-feed rollers of a typewriting machine and consists in a new method for constructing the same and new means and arrangement of ,parts whereby they may be released.

**How it works (reading):** Feed rollers are carried on arms on a shaft under the platen; a rotatable cam bar with a handle lifts the arms so the rollers clear the platen, and an adjustable spring sets the roller pressure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-feed rollers could not be easily released from the platen to let paper move freely.

**Solution:** Feed rollers are carried on arms on a shaft under the platen; a rotatable cam bar with a handle lifts the arms so the rollers clear the platen, and an adjustable spring sets the roller pressure.
