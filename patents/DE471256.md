## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**How it works (reading):** A harder buffer ring is rolled or pressed into a dovetail groove cut across the bearing body, perpendicular to the lever axis, so it is held on all sides with no fasteners.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A type lever buffer ring fixed by screws or rivets loosened in use and gave a clinking noise, and a ring pressed axially into a groove still clinked.

**Solution:** A harder buffer ring is rolled or pressed into a dovetail groove cut across the bearing body, perpendicular to the lever axis, so it is held on all sides with no fasteners.
