## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Tabulating needed a combined denominational and column-selecting arrangement that moves the carriage to a chosen column.

**Solution:** Column key levers and denominational stop levers are linked so that depressing a key sets a stop and moves the carriage to the selected column, with a lock holding the chosen position.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** column key levers; denominational stops; stop operating train; tabulator guide plate
