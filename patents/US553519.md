## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon wears along one line and needs reversing at the spool ends.

**Solution:** Ribbon feed that reverses by tension when a spool unwinds and shifts laterally at each carriage return.

**How it works:** Spools splined on carrier rods are stepped from the carriage feed. When a spool is fully unwound, the abnormal ribbon tension trips reversing devices at the spool and swaps the winding direction. A separate ribbon-shifting mechanism, driven by carriage return, moves the ribbon sideways a step at each return so the ribbon width is used progressively.

**Parts:** ribbon; spool; spool carrier; friction roll; reversing device; ribbon-shifting mechanism; carriage rack; universal bar
