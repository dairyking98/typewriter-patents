## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Hammond typewriter needs a tabulator that sets the carriage precisely for decimal columns.

**Solution:** Tabulating keys of different denominations set a swinging stop arm whose shoulders limit the hammer lever's backward throw, so the escapement wheel advances one or more letter spaces.

**How it works:** The carriage rack meshes with the spring drum gear and the escapement wheel gear, controlled by detents on levers. A bank of six numbered keys (hundred-thousandths to units) acts through a rockshaft and link on a swinging arm with stop shoulders. The shoulder selected arrests the hammer lever's retracting movement after a varying amount, so the escapement wheel moves a corresponding number of tooth spaces. The keys also print via the Hammond hammer.
