## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons; Stabilising means therefor using a scissor mechanism as stabiliser

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in' caps or heads for type-writer keys, and has for its object to obviate the confusion which arises in having more than one letter or character printed on the same face of a key-cap.

**How it works (reading):** A key-cap has a horizontal top face with one character and an inclined ledge with a second character in a different colour; two shift keys select which face prints.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Two characters on one key-cap face cause confusion when choosing the character.

**Solution:** A key-cap has a horizontal top face with one character and an inclined ledge with a second character in a different colour; two shift keys select which face prints.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
