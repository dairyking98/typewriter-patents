## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punching machine for hole combinations) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus specially adapted for preparation of record carriers for controlling composing machines

**How it works (reading):** A punch machine that can make single-position holes and multi-position holes, with keys controlling fewer punches than the theoretical minimum, so each hole symbol takes fewer positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hole-combination punch codes need at least four or more hole positions per character, and multi-position punching in older systems used even more punch positions, wasting card.

**Solution:** A punch machine that can make single-position holes and multi-position holes, with keys controlling fewer punches than the theoretical minimum, so each hole symbol takes fewer positions.
