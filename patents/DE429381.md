## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** In power-driven typebar machines the key jams if the coupling hits a cam tip.

**Solution:** The coupling pawl is spring-mounted in a longitudinal slot so the key can still reach its end position.

**How it works:** The pawl is attached to the key lever by a pin in a slot, with a tension spring and stop holding its rest position. Pressing the key pulls the pull rod down so its nose enters the cams on the drive shaft. If the nose lands on a cam, the pawl yields along the slot and the key still travels fully down, avoiding rebound.
