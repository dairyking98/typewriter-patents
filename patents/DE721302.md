## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing plastic lines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** Only the embossing face is heated and the excess material flows into recesses along the edges, so the blocks stay cold and hold exact size.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Plastic line blocks lose dimensional accuracy when hot-embossed, and the warm line comes out soft.

**Solution:** Only the embossing face is heated and the excess material flows into recesses along the edges, so the blocks stay cold and hold exact size.

**Features:** [Straight type bar or rule](../features/elem-linear-bar.md), [How the element is made](../features/elem-element-construction.md)
