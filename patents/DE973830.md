## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stylus character drawing method) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actuators for print wires of spring charge type, i.e. with mechanical power under electro-magnetic control; Mechanisms for bodily moving print heads or carriages parallel to the paper surface for oscillating, e.g. page-width print heads provided with counter-balancing means or shock absorbers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a method and an apparatus for production of a visible sign on a writing medium by combining them in succession individual drawing elements by means of a stylus, which, as is known, assumed is that a major dimension of the character is determined by the linear velocity at which the Scripture is moved forward is determined.

**How it works (reading):** The stylus makes a fast oscillation parallel to the writing surface plus pulse-driven strokes perpendicular to it, with many pens in a multi-row head.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Recording characters with one stylus at high writing speed is limited by the character size set by the moving medium.

**Solution:** The stylus makes a fast oscillation parallel to the writing surface plus pulse-driven strokes perpendicular to it, with many pens in a multi-row head.
