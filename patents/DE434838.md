## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen-release locking must be hand-filed to match the switching arms, which is costly skilled work.

**Solution:** Locking hooks are made adjustable along slotted rails on the switching arms, so timing is set by adjustment instead of filing.
