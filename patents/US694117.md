## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locks for subsidiary keys, e.g. for shift keys

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the present invention is to 0 provide such connections between the shiftkey and the platen or the type-bar system, as the case may be, that said shift-key may be depressed as quickly as any type-key and without shock to the finger of the operator.

**How it works (reading):** Adjustable fulcrum and connections so the shift key moves as quickly as a type key with alterable leverage.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen shift key is harder and slower to press than type keys.

**Solution:** Adjustable fulcrum and connections so the shift key moves as quickly as a type key with alterable leverage.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 6, the shift-key lever is fulcrumed on an adjustable bracket with a rocker so leverage can give deep dip with light resistance or short dip with more.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** shift key; fulcrum bracket; rocker; shifter bar
