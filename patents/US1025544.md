## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanisms for t-ypewriting machines and lts'general object is to improve the same.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon stop operated by a lever with the stop mechanism, together with a ribbon vibrator and universal bars, controls ribbon movement.
