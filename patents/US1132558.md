## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a mechanism for tabulating and feeding the typewriter carriage, so as to bring any desired point of a written line opposite to the printing point of the typewriter, and is a division of my application No.

**How it works (reading):** Two series of tabulating keys, one to space the carriage right and one to space it left, control the connection of mutilated gear or similar mechanism to set the carriage a definite distance; the rest of the OCR is cut off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A desired point of a written line must be brought to the print point, and the carriage needs definite spacing both right and left.

**Solution:** Two series of tabulating keys, one to space the carriage right and one to space it left, control the connection of mutilated gear or similar mechanism to set the carriage a definite distance; the rest of the OCR is cut off.
