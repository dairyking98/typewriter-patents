## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing telegraph type wheel) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using mechanical translation and type-head printing, e.g. type-wheel, type-cylinder

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is such a printing telegraph through which one in connection with a dialing device telegraphic messages in the form of perforations in a register strip in normal block letters can, but the device just as well by direct electrical impulses can be controlled.

**How it works (reading):** The type wheel is driven by friction from a continuously rotating auxiliary shaft running about 30 to 35 percent faster than the main shaft; a selector lever engages and holds the wheel in print position until the character is imprinted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier printing telegraphs drove the type wheel by friction slip from the main shaft and were slow, so faster and more accurate operation was needed.

**Solution:** The type wheel is driven by friction from a continuously rotating auxiliary shaft running about 30 to 35 percent faster than the main shaft; a selector lever engages and holds the wheel in print position until the character is imprinted.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Uses:** [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** type wheel; auxiliary shaft; main shaft; selector levers

**Numbers:** auxiliary shaft about 30 to 35 percent faster than main shaft; about one fifth of a main shaft revolution per character
