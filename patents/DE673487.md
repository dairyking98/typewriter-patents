**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper slide bearing for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** An inclined cross strut pivoted in the frame carries the fixed slide part, the tension spring and the ribbon fork, is balanced so the slide is easy to lift, and is moved via pull rods from the shift key.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper slide must be lifted for capitals and needs a stable, self-balancing support.

**Solution:** An inclined cross strut pivoted in the frame carries the fixed slide part, the tension spring and the ribbon fork, is balanced so the slide is easy to lift, and is moved via pull rods from the shift key.
