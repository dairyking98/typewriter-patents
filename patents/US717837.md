## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An escapement wheel moves backward when the carriage is returned to the right to start a new line.

**Solution:** A combined feed-dog and escapement-wheel stop blocks the wheel from reverse movement when the carriage is returned.
