## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE. The subject of the invention is a checkout with special works (special printing, Special adders, special display units, etc.), their setting, as from the patent specification 205433 became known, straight forward. Instead of the one bearing the special works Sled acting tooth segments according to patent 205433 is in the present innovation there is an approach provided with inclined surfaces on which the behind the money containers lying and raised by their movement rods act. In the drawing, an embodiment of the innovation is shown as an example. On the slide carrying the special works α sits an attachment m provided with sloping surfaces. The boxes carry attachments I 1 with surfaces sloping at the front, at the foot of which are vertical rods e 2 reaching as far as attachment m . If one of the boxes is shifted inwards, the corresponding rod e 2 is pushed up by the inclined surface of the relevant extension I 1. The upper end acts on the inclined surface of the extension m and thereby causes the carriage to be displaced until the rod has reached the highest point of the bevel.
