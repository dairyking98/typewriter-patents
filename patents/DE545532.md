## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cross counter control on calculating typewriter)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a control device for setting the type of calculation of several cross counters, at which presettable control plates attached to the steep counters each serve to set several types of calculation according to their location.

**How it works (reading):** Each presettable control plate has its own adjusting member and can be set and locked independently of the others.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a multi-counter calculating setup, adjusting one control plate also moves the others, causing wrong calculation settings.

**Solution:** Each presettable control plate has its own adjusting member and can be set and locked independently of the others.

**Features:**

- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** control plates; adjusting members; vertical counter; plate with bolts and elongated holes
