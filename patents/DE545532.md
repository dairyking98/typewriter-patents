> Description Translated from German Device for controlling the type of calculation of several cross counters by means of Presettable Control Elements on Typewriters The invention relates to a control device for setting the type of calculation of several cross counters, at which presettable control plates attached to the steep counters each serve to set several types of calculation according to their location. According to the invention, the control plates are by at them j e for arranged adjusting members can be adjusted and locked independently of one another. Through this it is avoided that when one control plate is adjusted, the other is also adjusted so that an incorrect setting of the calculation method of the cross counters does not occur. As a result, the adjustment of the individual control plates is much easier and requires considerably less attention and skill than the known ones Facilities of this type, so that a substantial time saving is achieved.
