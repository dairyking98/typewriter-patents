## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Three-case type bars need a smooth, accurate vertical shift of the type-bar segment.

**Solution:** Mount the type-bar segment on ball-race guides and shift it to three positions with a stop-controlled shift mechanism.

**How it works:** The type-bar segment, carrying bars with three types each, slides vertically in grooved guide bars with anti-friction balls held apart by a spacer plate. Studs with rollers on the segment sides are acted on by the shift mechanism. Upper and lower stops limit travel and further stops set the normal middle position. Guides are laterally adjustable by screws and locked by set screws.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type bar; type-bar segment; guide bar; anti-friction ball; spacer plate; stud with roller; stop; frame

**Numbers:** three types per bar; two balls per race
