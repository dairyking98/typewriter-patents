## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Raised printing surfaces for address plates must be produced by a machine that the inventors can set from a key bank, since the text gives no fuller statement of the defect.

**Solution:** Key-operated die-carrying wheels are arrested in positions set by the keys, then the drive is transferred so the die wheels are brought together to make raised impressions on blanks fed between them.
