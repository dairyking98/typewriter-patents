## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Same as US843247: shift a two-colour ribbon so print falls on either edge cheaply.

**Solution:** A horizontally sliding cam plate on the reciprocating stem lifts the ribbon-carrier shank relative to the stem.

**How it works:** The ribbon carrier sits in a socket on the stem, which reciprocates with the vibrating lever. A plate with a cam edge slides across the stem and carries a cam on the carrier's shank. A finger-piece rod slides the plate; the cam lifts the shank to bring the lower colour band to the printing point. The carrier returns by weight and ribbon reaction.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier; shank; stem; cam plate; finger-piece; rod; vibrating lever

**Improves on:**

- Underwood single-piece ribbon carrier: cam slide adjusts ribbon height
