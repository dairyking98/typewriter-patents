## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**How it works (reading):** An adjustment member returns the ribbon switching axis from the ineffective position to its original position so all parts resume their prior relation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After the ribbon switching device is disabled, it does not return to its exact prior position, so the wrong spool gets driven.

**Solution:** An adjustment member returns the ribbon switching axis from the ineffective position to its original position so all parts resume their prior relation.

**Features:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
