## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide a type-writer with simple and efficient means operated by a key in the keyboard for causing the carriage to skip from any point to a certain desired predetermined position and then to resumeits normal operation.

**How it works (reading):** A key-operated jumper lever lifts the Remington rack out of the escapement pinion so the spring strap runs the carriage to an adjustable stop.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Billing and tabular work needs the carriage to jump from the end of the text to a fixed column position quickly.

**Solution:** A key-operated jumper lever lifts the Remington rack out of the escapement pinion so the spring strap runs the carriage to an adjustable stop.

**How it works:** Depressing the jumper key rocks a lever whose roller lifts a wire guide on the pivoted rack bar, freeing it from the escapement pinion without locking the carriage. The strap pulls the carriage left until an arm on the rack bar drops into the path of an adjustable stop block and halts it. Releasing the key lets the rack drop back into the pinion and the escapement resumes control. The arm passes idly over the stop block in normal use.
