## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (clearing-house check machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Supports or magazines for piles from which articles are to be separated with means for advancing the articles to present the articles to the separating device controlled by height of pile

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an apparatus which is designed for the rapid-disposal of checks and the like in the transactions of clearing-houses.

**How it works (reading):** A vacuum shoe draws one check at a time from a weighted, rising stack in a receptacle, under pressure rollers, and feeds it to the marking and disposal stage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Large numbers of checks handled in clearing-houses must be marked and disposed of quickly and correctly.

**Solution:** A vacuum shoe draws one check at a time from a weighted, rising stack in a receptacle, under pressure rollers, and feeds it to the marking and disposal stage.
