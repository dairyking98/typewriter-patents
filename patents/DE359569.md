**How it works (reading):** Two or more rubber-covered main rollers are driven by handwheel gears and pressed against swinging counter-rollers with springs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriage paper feed relies on a fixed pressure abutment, which lets the paper slip.

**Solution:** Two or more rubber-covered main rollers are driven by handwheel gears and pressed against swinging counter-rollers with springs.
