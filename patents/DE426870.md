## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** Four identical sheet-metal strips are bent into angle plates with ball troughs and paired opposite each other, with a plate joining the inner troughs for the rack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Grooved sheet-metal ball tracks for the carriage need different tools, cannot be adjusted per side, and long troughs are hard to keep parallel.

**Solution:** Four identical sheet-metal strips are bent into angle plates with ball troughs and paired opposite each other, with a plate joining the inner troughs for the rack.
