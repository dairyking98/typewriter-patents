## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for controlling the revolution of the platen of a typewriting machine adapted to fill-in, successively, forms.

**How it works (reading):** The platen rotation is split into two stages: the first is the usual step-by-step line spacing, and the second is one continuous stage driven by a pawl on a platen ratchet wheel that is arrested by a stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Forms fed from a continuous strip need the platen to advance by a variable amount after the form is filled, which step-by-step line spacing does not allow.

**Solution:** The platen rotation is split into two stages: the first is the usual step-by-step line spacing, and the second is one continuous stage driven by a pawl on a platen ratchet wheel that is arrested by a stop.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet wheel on platen spindle; pawl; stop; line-spacing mechanism
