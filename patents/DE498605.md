## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage is complicated and costly to manufacture.

**Solution:** The platen housing slides on cross bars of a U-section support rail that runs on machine rods, and loose brass sleeves with rubber rollers on the guide rods also feed paper.
