## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (optical scanning, booking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards; Arrangements for producing a permanent visual presentation of the output data, e.g. computer output printers using printers by rack-type printers

**How it works (reading):** A second scanning photocell, slid mechanically into the third station as the card advances, lets three stations read one writing zone and print three lines one under another with two scanners.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card-controlled booking machines need a card's data printed on three lines, and a single writing zone normally forces extra guidance cards or a costly tabulator.

**Solution:** A second scanning photocell, slid mechanically into the third station as the card advances, lets three stations read one writing zone and print three lines one under another with two scanners.
