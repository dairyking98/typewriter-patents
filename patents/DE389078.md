## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**How it works (reading):** The rear plate gets a small curved plate that swings out in all directions, and the front fingers sit on a cross brace lifted by support arms; two springs of different strength let the front guide swing out independently of the locking arms.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A paper guide holding the sheet on the roller front and back could not be swung out for one part without disturbing the others.

**Solution:** The rear plate gets a small curved plate that swings out in all directions, and the front fingers sit on a cross brace lifted by support arms; two springs of different strength let the front guide swing out independently of the locking arms.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** rear paper guide plate; curved swing plate; front fingers; cross brace; support arms; locking arms; springs
