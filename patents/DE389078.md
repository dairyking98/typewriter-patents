The invention relates. to a paper guide @ for typewriters. -the the paper roller in front and behind is enclosed by fingers, and consists in that the back fingers in the behind the paper roller located in a known manner paper guide plate to both Sides of an additional plate that can be swung out slightly on all sides are, while the front fingers are carried by a cross brace will, which is supported by arms swingable below the axis of the paper roll. By the design according to the invention it is achieved that the paper better held and due to the additional curved, movable guide plate is guided smoother.
