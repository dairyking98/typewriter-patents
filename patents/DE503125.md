## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing paint-roller inkers do not fit type-wheel machines where the type moves only a short distance from the paper.

**Solution:** A paint roller sits just in front of the print point and is rolled sideways over the moving type by the type's own impression movement; the roller and paint container form one replaceable unit.
