## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (oil-resistant printing rollers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Top layers; Location or type of the layers in shells for rollers of printing machines characterised by macromolecular organic compounds; Trimming

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to oil-resistant Druckwaken longer life than they own previous printing rollers.

**How it works (reading):** The roller surface is a flexible alkyd resin made from polyhydric alcohol and polybasic acid, moulded and baked on the roller, which stays resilient and does not absorb oil.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber-covered printing rollers absorb oil from the ink, warp, soften in heat and wear quickly, so print comes out unclean.

**Solution:** The roller surface is a flexible alkyd resin made from polyhydric alcohol and polybasic acid, moulded and baked on the roller, which stays resilient and does not absorb oil.
