## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**How it works (reading):** The solvent is sealed in a breakable ampoule inside the handle, and the wick-type plug that protrudes from the handle is passed back and forth along the type series, so the cleaner is used once and thrown away.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cleaning typewriter types with a solvent-fed handle leaks, is hard to dose, and the brush leaves loose fibres in the type.

**Solution:** The solvent is sealed in a breakable ampoule inside the handle, and the wick-type plug that protrudes from the handle is passed back and forth along the type series, so the cleaner is used once and thrown away.
