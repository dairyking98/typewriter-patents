## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for cleaning the types of typewriters There are devices for Cleaning the types of typewriters known in which the handle becomes a Container for a paint solvent is formed and by means of a Valve lockable line in connection with a brush on the handle stands. If the valve is opened and the device along the type series of the typewriter guided back and forth, so the solvent flows from the handle to the bristles of the Brush so that at the same time as mechanical processing of the types a loosening the color crust takes place. However, these known devices are not practical in this respect set up when the valve leaks slightly and the handling a lot of attention and requires care. The dosage of the amount of solvent is also considerable Trouble. In the device according to the invention, the color solvent is also used housed in the handle. 'But this differs from the known device Color solvent enclosed in an ampoule that is made of a tough cloth low absorbency, e.g. B. paraffin paper, manufactured container is located, closed by an absorbent plug, expediently by a wick is that an end piece of the plug protrudes from the container serving as a handle…
