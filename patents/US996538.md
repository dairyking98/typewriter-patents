**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed with two or more sets of type or printing elements

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to typewriting machines/of the variety known as visible or front -strkek machines, termediate connections.

**How it works (reading):** Two independent segmental bars each carry alternate type bars, with sub-levers in a sub-lever segment and tension devices on the key levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible front-stroke machines need simple, light-touch type bar linkage.

**Solution:** Two independent segmental bars each carry alternate type bars, with sub-levers in a sub-lever segment and tension devices on the key levers.

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type sector or segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md)
