## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making copies on several paper webs with carbon sheets needs a clamp that grips the webs and moves them with the carriage.

**Solution:** A paper guide frame on carriage wheels has a clamping slide that is pulled along chains or bands between stops, and releases the webs at the far end.
