## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention has reference to improvements in that class of writing-machines which are provided with a movable paper.

**How it works (reading):** A simple mechanism with operating means spaces the paper carriage to a chosen point on the scale and automatically rotates the platen roll to feed the next row.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage must be spaced quickly to a point on the machine scale, and the platen must be advanced to the next line automatically, for check and column writing.

**Solution:** A simple mechanism with operating means spaces the paper carriage to a chosen point on the scale and automatically rotates the platen roll to feed the next row.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
