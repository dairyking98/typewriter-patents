## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing planetary type carriers must be set spinning for each impression, so they are noisy and need finger effort.

**Solution:** Type carriers are planet wheels that rotate continuously on a revolving disc and print by rolling tangentially on the platen; a light key press only lowers the chosen carrier into working position.

**How it works:** Planet gears carrying one type (or several) are mounted eccentrically on a drive shaft so the type follows a cycloid, coming to standstill relative to the platen at the contact point. Shafts can slide longitudinally and are mounted in a disc revolving in a horizontal plane. A key lever selects the required planet wheel with a light touch, through a selection mechanism with a rotating comb, bringing it into working position. The type rolls over the platen for a nearly silent impression. Alternatives use a cycloidal groove and a star-shaped carrier.
