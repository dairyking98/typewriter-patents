## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage drive, trigger, braking and reset devices are housed separately and take up space.

**Solution:** All of them are put on one common shaft and housed in one unit, with a torsion-split drive wheel and a spring drum that the handwheel tensions.
