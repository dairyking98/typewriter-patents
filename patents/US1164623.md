## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Alining scale, printing-point indicator and card holder were separate parts on the Noiseless typewriter.

**Solution:** One spring-loaded scale with a cut-away at the print point that presses paper or card against a flat platen and moves with the carriage and shift.

**How it works:** A shift rail carries a support with an escapement shaft and rack drive. A rock shaft on adjustable trunnions carries two arms with wing scales and a central notch for the print point. A coiled spring presses the scale against the flat platen, holding paper or a card even at the sheet edges. Locknuts allow lateral and vertical adjustment. Ribbon guides sit on the same support.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [IMP:anvil-backing](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)

**Parts:** alining scale; rock shaft; spring; flat platen; shift rail; ribbon guide; locknut

**Improves on:**

- separate alining scale and card holder: combines scale, point indicator and card holder in one part
