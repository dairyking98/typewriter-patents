## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and has for its main object to provide simple and efiicient means which can be manufactured at small cost for connecting a ribbon spool to its driving means and for readily detaching the spool therefrom when desired.

**How it works (reading):** A locking device connects the ribbon spool to a vertical driving shaft and releases it for detaching.

**Solution:** A locking device connects the ribbon spool to a vertical driving shaft and releases it for detaching.
