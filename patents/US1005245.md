## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** On Underwood machines the ribbon carrier is disconnected for stencil work and may stay in the shifted position.

**Solution:** An extra actuating pin on the vibrating lever keeps the ribbon carrier under key control, normally above the print line, and withdraws the ribbon when keys are struck.

**How it works:** The Underwood carrier shifts with the platen so the ribbon throw is the same in both cases. The vibrating lever, as in Cook patent 926,050, gets a second pin on the other side of the fulcrum. Connected to the vibrating universal bar frame, the carrier rests above the printing line and moves away when the keys are operated, fully withdrawing the ribbon before the type arrives, which allows stencil cutting.
