## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Folding or tilting line-shift levers on low-profile typewriters need several hand movements and bulky bearings.

**Solution:** The line-shift lever swings on its own horizontal shaft into a spring-latched rest position and springs back to working position when the carriage lifts for shift.

**How it works:** The lever turns on a horizontal shaft in two bearings on the carriage side wall. Swung left and down it is caught by a spring-loaded catch, which also locks the carriage for transport. A torsion spring on the shaft returns it, and pressing a shift key lifts the carriage and frees it from the catch. A driver pawl on a loose bushing on the shaft is taken by a screw pin in the bushing's recess when the lever moves right, pressing the pawl down to advance the platen.
