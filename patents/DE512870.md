## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (transverse summing units with reversing gear)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with several transverse summing units, the rotational movement of which is controlled by a single reversing gear according to patent 502891.

**How it works (reading):** Coupling sleeve teeth are lengthened so that the drive wheel stays engaged with the central member and with one link at each end, letting all transverse units be coupled together while keeping the simple construction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A transverse summing unit could previously add or subtract in only one of several units at a time, and the wish was to run them all at once.

**Solution:** Coupling sleeve teeth are lengthened so that the drive wheel stays engaged with the central member and with one link at each end, letting all transverse units be coupled together while keeping the simple construction.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** coupling sleeve; drive wheels 6 and 7; transverse summing units
