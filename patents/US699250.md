## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Opening only: a typebar writing machine whose carriage advances step by step through an escapement as the key levers are operated; the inventor claims various unspecified novel features.

**Solution:** A Remington-type upstrike typewriter with a platen-shifting frame for upper case, a tilting platen, ball-bearing carriage, front presser rolls and line-spacing gear.

**How it works:** The paper carriage steps along under an escapement driven by the key levers. The platen sits on a shifting frame supported by links and pillar-blocks, locked and released by a shift key lever, to print upper case. The carriage runs on antifriction balls in grooves, with a U-frame beneath it. Front presser rolls and a line-spacing mechanism act on the platen. Only the figure list was readable in the opening; the claims and detailed operation are not shown.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; platen-shifting frame; shift key lever; antifriction ball; U-frame; presser roll; line-spacing mechanism
