## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a device which can be used both for resetting the Paper slide of a typewriter by means of an electric motor as well as constant tension the pull cord of this slide is intended.

**How it works (reading):** Two coupling members held in contact by resilient members slip relative to each other when the carriage hits its stop, cutting the motor circuit, and a device keeps the pull cord taut after rewinding.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction clutches for carriage return and pull-cord tension cannot reliably stop the carriage at its end and cannot also tension the cord during manual return.

**Solution:** Two coupling members held in contact by resilient members slip relative to each other when the carriage hits its stop, cutting the motor circuit, and a device keeps the pull cord taut after rewinding.
