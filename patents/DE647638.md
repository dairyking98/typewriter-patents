**How it works (reading):** The paper-holding rollers or the paper table are moved into the receiving position by a force independent of the carriage travel, released by a control element, so the carriage needs only a small movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On calculating machines the carriage must also move the platen or pinch rollers into the paper receiving position, which wears the carriage and its tabulating mechanism.

**Solution:** The paper-holding rollers or the paper table are moved into the receiving position by a force independent of the carriage travel, released by a control element, so the carriage needs only a small movement.
