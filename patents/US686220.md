## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage-position numbers should be larger, easier to read, and more compact, so they do not take room needed by other parts.

**Solution:** A column of numbers wound spirally around a drum or wheel is seen through a fixed sight-hole; the drum rotates and slides on its bearing so the number for the carriage position comes into view.

**How it works:** The drum both rotates and slides, bringing the spiral numbers one at a time opposite the immovable sight-hole in the top plate.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** drum with spiral numbers; sight-hole in top plate

**Numbers:** US 686,220; 1901-11-05; application filed 1900-12-29
