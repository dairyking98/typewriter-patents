## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spool-to-spool ribbon feeds speed up as the wound ribbon bulk grows, so the feed must reverse too often and wears the machine.

**Solution:** Feed the ribbon at constant speed with clamping feed rollers and drive the spools through compensating drive so they only wind what is fed.

**How it works:** Constant-rate feed devices with clamping feed rolls (on a vibratory frame separated by a pitman) pull the ribbon past the printing point. The spools are driven through a compensating means that takes up the growth in winding capacity as bulk increases. An automatic reversing mechanism, with a spring-pressed reversing lever and ribbon buckle on each spool, shifts the main operating shaft on a key stroke when the ribbon end arrives. The ribbon vibrating mechanism is operated by the ribbon feeding mechanism. Opening text only; much detail not shown.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; feed roller; compensating spool drive; main operating shaft; reversing lever; ribbon buckle; ribbon vibrator; pitman

**Improves on:**

- earlier spool ribbon feeds: ribbon speed no longer rises as spool bulk increases
