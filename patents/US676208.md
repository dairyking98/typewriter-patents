## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of my invention is to reduce the liability of the types to collide when operated at high: speed, and to this end I effect a rearrangement of the order in which .

**How it works (reading):** Frequently used typebars are staggered at opposite sides of the typebasket circle, with key levers connected to types at alternating positions to reduce collisions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Under-strike typebar machines collided at high speed because closely packed typebars struck the same points.

**Solution:** Frequently used typebars are staggered at opposite sides of the typebasket circle, with key levers connected to types at alternating positions to reduce collisions.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** typebar; typebasket; key lever
