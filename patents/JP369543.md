## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A very small, portable typewriter that can print at any place on paper of any size or thickness.

**Solution:** A hand-held palm-operated device with a thumb-turned type belt, a pressing lever and polygonal wheels that step it along the paper.

**How it works:** A knob turns a shaft gear that drives the type belt by its toothed back. Capitals and small letters are placed at opposite positions on the belt, so the wanted letter at the bottom end shows its opposite at the top for checking. Wool or felt inking rollers near the bottom touch the type faces as the belt travels. Pressing the lever with the palm lowers the spring-mounted belt case so the bottom type meets the paper. The device is then moved to the right on a polygonal direction wheel whose angle fixes the spacing.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Palm-operated Western-letter typewriter. Brief description of the drawings. Fig. 1 is a side view showing the internal construction, Fig. 2 a side view, and Fig. 3 a front view, partly cut away. Nature, action and effect of the device. In this device, turning the knob 1 turns the knob's shaft gear 13, as in Fig. 3, and the back 16 of the type belt, which meshes with it, makes the type belt 8 travel round. The Western letters are placed so that, as with 17 and 18 and with 18の2 and 17の2, the capitals [the claim: capitals and small letters] lie at exactly opposite positions; so when the letter wanted for printing is brought out at the bottom end 3 of the belt, its opposite letter appears at the top end 2. Further, as the type belt travels, an automatic inking device 11, made of turning rollers of woollen cloth, felt or the like soaked with ink and fitted near the bottom of the belt, front and back, so as to touch the type faces lightly, rubs ink on the type faces. So the moment the pressing lever 4 is pressed down, the type-belt case 5, carried on springs 6, comes down as the springs give, the type 3 at the bottom of the belt meets the paper, and the wanted letter is printed at once…
