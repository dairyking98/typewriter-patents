## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates tonew and useful improvements in automatically operated typewriters, and the object of the invention is to produce a simple and efficient means, whereby a strip may be perforated to form a stencil roll to be used for allowing balls to be fed at predetermined moments to positions where they cooperate with mechanism for causing the type levers of themachine to be operated.

**How it works (reading):** A perforated stencil roll feeds balls at set moments to positions where they cooperate with the mechanism that operates the type levers, so any number of copies can be made automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Hand-operated typewriters need a separate operator for each line; the inventor wants automatic operation with copying.

**Solution:** A perforated stencil roll feeds balls at set moments to positions where they cooperate with the mechanism that operates the type levers, so any number of copies can be made automatically.
