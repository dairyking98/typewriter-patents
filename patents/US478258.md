## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to matrix-making machines of the class described in several of my patents issued of date November 5, 1889;

**How it works (reading):** Die bodies are measured in points and faces in self-spacing pica-em units, so each character and line is spaced automatically.

**Problem:** Matrix spacing must match hand-set type of any font in both characters and lines.

**Solution:** Die bodies are measured in points and faces in self-spacing pica-em units, so each character and line is spaced automatically.
