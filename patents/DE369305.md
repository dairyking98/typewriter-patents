## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On power-driven typewriters the shift key must carry heavy carriage mass, and half-line-height prints can occur.

**Solution:** The shift key only releases a power-driven linkage that lifts the carriage and latches it until the next type impression.

**How it works:** Pressing the shift key lowers a lever whose nose enters the area of a rotating camshaft, which moves a lever linkage and raises a changeover plunger under the carriage. A pawl latches the plunger in the shifted position. Striking a type lever pushes the universal bar back, tripping the pawl so the carriage drops to normal. Both movements are very quick, so impressions at half line height are impossible.
