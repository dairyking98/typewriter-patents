## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** At line end the typebars are locked out and punctuation cannot be typed without releasing the lock by hand.

**Solution:** Only the key levers for letters have hooks that the line-end locking bar catches, so punctuation keys remain free after the lock engages.

**How it works:** A line stop on a rocking rod is struck by a carriage stop near line end, rocking the rod and a depending arm. This turns a rock-shaft that lets a spring-loaded locking bar move under hooks on the letter key levers, blocking them. Punctuation key levers lack the hooks and stay operable. A button allows hand release, and the line stop is adjustable along a rack with a scale and pointer.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key lever; hook; locking bar; rock-shaft; line stop; rack; carriage stop; scale; pointer; release button
