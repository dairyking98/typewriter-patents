**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates particularly to visible writing machines but it will be obvious that certain of the principles involved may be applied to any type of writing machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage escapement must control step-by-step movement and allow free release.

**Solution:** A toothed carriage bar with oppositely faced teeth, engaged by spring-pressed dogs that are oscillated by the universal bar, with a finger lever to release the carriage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
