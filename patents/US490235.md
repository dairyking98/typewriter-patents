## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Direct-acting typebar keys needed more rigid typebars and guided key arms so the type strikes accurately.

**Solution:** A direct operating key with two vertical arms joined by a cross rod, a truss-shaped typebar with lateral ribs, and serrated plates that guide the key arm.

**How it works:** Each direct key has an outer arm carrying the finger key and an inner arm carrying the typebar, joined by a connecting rod with a loose pivot so the inner arm has slight play and can be detached. The inner arms pass up through slots or serrations at the inner ends of the upper and lower plates, which limit side play and allow removal of any arm without taking the machine apart. The typebar has lateral projections forming a truss that vanishes toward its lower end, adding stiffness with little bulk.
