## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Label printers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists of an attachment for type-writing or other machines for feeding or holding paper, the same being adjustable so as to be adapted to be fitted to machines of different sizes.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable roller on a length- and width-adjustable bar support with pins that enter sockets on the machine frame, clamped by a screw.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper for feeding must be held on machines of differing sizes.

**Solution:** An adjustable roller on a length- and width-adjustable bar support with pins that enter sockets on the machine frame, clamped by a screw.
