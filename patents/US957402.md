## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary paper feed roller carried on an attachment frame grips and guides small sheets without a special machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Small label and work sheets are hard to handle in a standard machine.

**Solution:** An auxiliary paper feed roller carried on an attachment frame grips and guides small sheets without a special machine.
