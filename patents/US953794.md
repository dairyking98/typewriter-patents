## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide a suitable attachment for employing the aforesaid braking means in connection with the simple tabulating'mechanism heretofore generally employed upon Underwood typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A yielding spring connection between the brake frame and the carriage-release key lets the depression be completed, and the spring forces the pinion into mesh with the spring-barrel gear when the carriage is released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a tooth tip of the release pinion catches on the gear tooth tip, the carriage-release key cannot complete its depression and the carriage braking fails to engage.

**Solution:** A yielding spring connection between the brake frame and the carriage-release key lets the depression be completed, and the spring forces the pinion into mesh with the spring-barrel gear when the carriage is released.

**Features:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
