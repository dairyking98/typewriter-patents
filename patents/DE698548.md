## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The auxiliary ribbon piece has detachable buckles, so the elastic end pieces stay on the wire holders and only the ribbon is replaced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Auxiliary ribbon pieces with elastic end pieces permanently joined to the ribbon waste the elastic ends when the ribbon wears out, and make the ribbon costly.

**Solution:** The auxiliary ribbon piece has detachable buckles, so the elastic end pieces stay on the wire holders and only the ribbon is replaced.
