## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cylindrical hubs on cylindrical shafts are hard to make uniform enough for interchangeable type wheels, wear loose, and gum up with dirt or oil.

**Solution:** Mount the type wheel hub between two conical centres that fit conical seats in the hub, so the wheel runs true, wear is taken up by closing the centres, and wheels swap easily.

**How it works:** The wheel hub has conical recesses at both ends. An upper cone and a lower cone, carried on a bracket, seat into the recesses and centre the wheel. Bringing the cones together takes up wear (one version uses a threaded stem with a nut and jam nut, another a ball race). The bracket is carried on an arm and riser raised by a shift lever, so the wheel rises for case shift while a driving arm that rotates it stays engaged with the spokes at the highest level. The wheel can be lifted out without disturbing the shaft or its bearings.
