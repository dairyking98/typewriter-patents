## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing on both faces of the printing material

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to that class of paper-guiding mechanism for typewriting machines in which paper-guiding fingers or arms upon the carriage frame are employed for guiding the paper to be operatedupon.

**How it works (reading):** Paper-guiding fingers or arms on the carriage frame guide the sheet, with a toothed rack bar joining the end frame members to hold them.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guiding fingers on the carriage must be set accurately and carried with it.

**Solution:** Paper-guiding fingers or arms on the carriage frame guide the sheet, with a toothed rack bar joining the end frame members to hold them.
