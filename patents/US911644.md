## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A full-keyboard front-strike visible writer with eighty or more typebars needs them packed in less than half a circle in a simple, cheap machine.

**Solution:** Typebars are arranged in nests of grouped supports in a small space, with adjustable typebar pivots and novel connections to the keys.

**How it works:** A large number of typebars are mounted in nests on supports, occupying less than a half circle around the platen. The typebars have adjustable pivots and some have integral operating arms. A system of key levers connects each key to its bar. The extract does not describe the rest.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** typebar; typebar pivot; typebar nest; key lever; platen; key

**Numbers:** 80 or more typebars; less than half a circle
