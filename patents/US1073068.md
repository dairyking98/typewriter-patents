## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage positioning by decimal tabulating keys needs both letter spacing and column finding.

**Solution:** Decimal tabulating keys with counter-stops move a rack out of mesh with the carriage gear so the carriage runs to a chosen column or letter space, with four keys doubling as column-finding keys.
