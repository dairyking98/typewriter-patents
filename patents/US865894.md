## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** A spring raises the ribbon vibrator to the printing point; a part moved by the universal bar holds it down until a finger piece is depressed, and adjustable stops set how far the vibrator rises.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator must be held down against its spring and released to elevate the ribbon to the printing position, and the height of elevation must be controlled.

**Solution:** A spring raises the ribbon vibrator to the printing point; a part moved by the universal bar holds it down until a finger piece is depressed, and adjustable stops set how far the vibrator rises.
