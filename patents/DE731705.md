## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (complementary vertical counter for totalizer)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a complementary vertical counter, in particular for typewriters or the like equipped with a sum train, with one the volatile one One switching device actuated by the movement of the paper carriage.

**How it works (reading):** A carry switching device, driven by the carriage step and dependent on the digit-roller positions, that automatically adds the carry in the additive sense to the positive number set when a value is present in positive form.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Complementary vertical counters did not reliably carry the volatile one (carry) when used with automatic totalizing typewriters.

**Solution:** A carry switching device, driven by the carriage step and dependent on the digit-roller positions, that automatically adds the carry in the additive sense to the positive number set when a value is present in positive form.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
