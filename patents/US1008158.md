## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops on typebar machines needed to stop the carriage exactly and lock the keys so characters are not printed over each other at line end.

**Solution:** A lock lever and release key stop the carriage at the margin stop and lock the universal bar, ribbon vibrator and typebars, working the same in normal and shifted platen positions.

**How it works:** A spring-held lock lever is pivoted in the shift frame extension in front of the margin abutment. When the carriage reaches the stop, the lever swings over a shoulder on the ribbon-vibrator operating lever, locking it and the universal bar. A lock-releasing key lets the operator free the parts to print extra characters. The arrangement is designed to act identically with the platen in shifted position.
