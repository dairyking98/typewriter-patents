## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon spool clips must be attached and removed with one hand, and the pliers legs are easily lost.

**Solution:** The two pliers legs are joined by a flat spring that holds the second leg ready and keeps it in the hooked position so the clip can be fitted with one hand.
