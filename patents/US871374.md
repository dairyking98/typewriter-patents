**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Radial type bars pivoted on a curved rod strike rearward against the platen, with their hubs in radial slots of a segment that can be shifted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike type action is complicated and costly.

**Solution:** Radial type bars pivoted on a curved rod strike rearward against the platen, with their hubs in radial slots of a segment that can be shifted.
