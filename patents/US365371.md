## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Too many key blows are needed per sentence, and the machine did not count words.

**Solution:** An escapement with two alternating dogs on an escapement wheel and a yielding stop lets the space key move the paper roll a letter or a word-plus-space in one blow, and a pawl bar drives a word register.

**How it works:** The lower carriage has a rack meshing with a gear on a shaft carrying a grooved escapement wheel, pulled by a spring drum. A horizontal bar, moved by a lever on each key, carries one dog directly and a second dog on a stand opposite so the dogs alternately enter the wheel's radiating grooves. A plate with a yielding spring stop limits the downward movement of one dog when only a single letter space is wanted, while a full stroke lets more teeth pass for a word space in one blow. A pawl-carrying bar operates the registering mechanism which counts words written. Type keys act through radiating levers on a central spindle that lifts to move the bar.
