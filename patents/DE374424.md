## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** A resilient arm, cut free from the type-lever shaft by a slot, takes up the excess force of the stroke at the bounce stop, so the lever stops more softly while the spring-back is still driven by the stored energy.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers strike the platen or roller with full force, causing loud noise, wear on the types and rebound.

**Solution:** A resilient arm, cut free from the type-lever shaft by a slot, takes up the excess force of the stroke at the bounce stop, so the lever stops more softly while the spring-back is still driven by the stored energy.
