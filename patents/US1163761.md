## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to power apparatus for operating the line-space mechanism and returning the carriage at the completion of each typewritten line.

**How it works (reading):** An electric motor drives a pinion that meshes with a rack on the carriage; a clutch and switch are released by a finger on the carriage as it nears the end of return, and the cut-off point moves with the margin stop setting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage and line-spacing at the end of each line is done by hand, and the return must stop at the margin the operator sets.

**Solution:** An electric motor drives a pinion that meshes with a rack on the carriage; a clutch and switch are released by a finger on the carriage as it nears the end of return, and the cut-off point moves with the margin stop setting.
