## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machine actions were costly to build and hard to adjust and assemble.

**Solution:** Mount the key levers, transmitting levers and escapement linkage on one slidable frame (slitted tube fulcrum plus comb) that can be pulled forward for adjustment.

**How it works:** Flat key levers hook over a slitted tubular bar that spaces them and acts as the fulcrum, held down by a leaf spring. A pin on each key lever rides in a slot in a thin transmitting lever held in a comb, which through a link throws the typebar to the platen. Depressing any key pushes a rod that turns a bar and tilts the escapement lever, rocking a dog and spring against the escape wheel to release the carriage a step. The carriage rack drives a pinion that is clutched to the escape wheel; a shifting bar disengages the clutch so the carriage can be slid freely. All of this sits on one frame that slides forward on guides after loosening screws.
