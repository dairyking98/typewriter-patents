## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line adjuster on flat panel typewriters in which a carriage that supports the writing device, which can be adjusted along the line to be written and optionally carries the arithmetic unit containing carriage, by means of a pure electric motor is moved back and forth across the paper to be written on.

**How it works (reading):** A shift lever on the writing carriage resets the stop to the line width at each line start, so the carriage moves to the correct line and the carriage is locked during writing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On flat-bed electric typewriters a stop only halts the carriage at a set line, and automatic line shifting cannot be combined with setting a line start.

**Solution:** A shift lever on the writing carriage resets the stop to the line width at each line start, so the carriage moves to the correct line and the carriage is locked during writing.
