## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon carrier on a platen-shift machine must present any of several color fields while staying in step with the shifted platen.

**Solution:** A ribbon-actuating slide carried on the platen shift rail, with slotted bell crank taking either of two pins at different distances to give two strokes.

**How it works:** The ribbon carrier is pivoted to a bell crank on a slide that moves up and down with the platen-shift rail but can slide along it. Two slots in the bell-crank arm, at different distances from the pivot, take pins on a forked actuator. The actuator is on a rock shaft returned by a spring and struck directly by the key levers through a universal bar under them. The slide position picks the slot, and the stroke length picks the color field.
