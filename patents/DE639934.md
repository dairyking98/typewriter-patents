## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (planographic printing plates from typewriter setting) · **Subject (patent classes):** by mechanical pressure, e.g. from a typewriter by electrical recording ribbon therefor

**How it works (reading):** Individual text and image parts are transferred onto rubber partial rollers, which then print the composed form on a partial-roller press.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewritten text must be combined with images and transferred to printing forms, and photomechanical methods are cumbersome.

**Solution:** Individual text and image parts are transferred onto rubber partial rollers, which then print the composed form on a partial-roller press.
