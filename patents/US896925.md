## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage shift on a Royal machine should be holdable in the shifted position.

**Solution:** The shift key lever carries a pivoted latch key that locks the carriage shifted until released.

**How it works:** The carriage on parallel arms and a rock shaft is counterbalanced by a coil spring with adjustable notches. A shift key lever pushes the carriage through a projection. Pressing the latch key finger piece locks it shifted, released by slight vertical or lateral pressure.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** shift key lever; latch key; rock shaft; counterbalance spring; spring adjusting plate; carriage
