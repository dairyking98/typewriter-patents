## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever bearing segments were crowded; levers on the outside and inside of the segment needed guide slots.

**Solution:** A segment has slots on both outside and inside, so half of each type lever runs on the outside and the other half on the inside, making it more compact.
