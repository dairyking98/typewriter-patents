## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor's earlier writing ball (US125952) used a paper drum; a flat paper table is wanted instead.

**Solution:** A stationary writing-ball of converging sprung type pistons prints onto paper on a flat table moved in two directions, with a spring-driven escapement released by an electromagnet.

**How it works:** The ball carries circularly arranged pistons, each with a type on its lower end and a letter on its knob, axes converging on the ball's centre, each returned by a spiral spring. Pressing a piston closes an electric circuit through a flexible contact between an insulated outer shell and the inner section. The table runs on wheels on rails carried by a frame that slides on crossing rails, so it moves longitudinally and transversely. A rack under the table meshes with a toothed wheel on a shaft carrying an escape wheel and a pinion driven by a fusee-box mainspring wound with a key. Two electromagnets pull an armature that rocks a verge with pallets in and out of a crown wheel, releasing one tooth per stroke to space the paper.
