## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**How it works (reading):** Column stop pivot and setting bar allow quick setting and resetting of column stops to the inoperative position, with a bell-ring mechanism described.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Tabulator column stops are hard to reset and to set for the tab positions.

**Solution:** Column stop pivot and setting bar allow quick setting and resetting of column stops to the inoperative position, with a bell-ring mechanism described.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
