## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar guide rails are open to dirt, hard to mount and adjust, and the side guide members on the bars are expensive and add friction.

**Solution:** The common guide plate ends at the rest position and individual fixed radial guide rails continue below the bars, with a small rider pressed into each bar's slot sliding on the rail.

**How it works:** Each typebar has a rider pressed into a slot that slides upright on its own radial guide rail. The rails are held by the front guide comb, which also guides the bars, and at the far end in cheeks adjustable along an arc about the print point. Bars are about 0.05 mm thicker than the rails for a correct sliding fit. The drive lever reaches over the comb.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** typebar; guide plate; guide comb; radial guide rail; rider; adjustable cheek; drive lever

**Numbers:** bar 0.05 mm thicker than rail
