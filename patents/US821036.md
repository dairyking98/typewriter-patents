## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Wear on the escapement teeth, which force the back-check out of the way, and faulty operation of the back-check.

**Solution:** A back-check moves with the feed dogs but is driven by the finger keys, not by the escapement teeth, and is pivoted so it yields if it sits on the wrong tooth.
