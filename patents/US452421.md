## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wanted a simple, cheap shift machine with many characters per key.

**Solution:** Type carriers on a circle driven by connecting rods that pass through a shift ring; swinging the ring left or right brings the first or third rod of each group over its lifting sub-lever.

**How it works:** Each of 26 key levers lifts a radiating sub-lever that stands under the middle rod of a group of three. The rods' lower ends pass through perforations in an oscillating shift wheel/ring on a central hollow stand. Left and right shift keys act through links, bell-cranks and rods on an arm of the shift ring, which is centred by a spring lever with a roller in a V-notch. Shifting puts the first or third rod over the sub-lever, so the same key prints one of three characters.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md)

**Parts:** key lever; sub-lever; fulcrum ring; connecting rod; type carrier; shift wheel; shift key; V-notch roller lever; central stand

**Numbers:** 78 type carriers; 78 connecting rods; 26 sub-levers; 26 key levers; groups of 3

**Improves on:**

- typebar machines with one bar per character: three characters per key by shifting the rod ring
