## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for rotating the platens oi typewriting machines through relatively long arcs first backwardly then forwardly, 'the first move ment to bring the platen into position for insertion of the bill and the return motion to bring the first line of writing on the bill to the printing point.

**How it works (reading):** A crank handle which, by one stroke, engages gears that rotate the platen backward to insert a new bill, and a return stroke that turns it forward to bring the first line to the printing point. Ordinary line spacing is unchanged.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing requires inserting a new bill and bringing its first line to the print point without wasting space on a long record sheet that stays in the machine.

**Solution:** A crank handle which, by one stroke, engages gears that rotate the platen backward to insert a new bill, and a return stroke that turns it forward to bring the first line to the printing point. Ordinary line spacing is unchanged.
