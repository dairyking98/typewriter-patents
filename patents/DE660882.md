## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to sound-absorbing platen for typewriters with a transverse wall roller cavity divided into closed air spaces.

**How it works (reading):** Hollow light-metal support cylinder with transverse walls forming closed air cells, with a regular corrugated outer surface under the usual rubber covering.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wood-core platen transmits impact noise along the roller and into the frame, and soft liners lose print sharpness.

**Solution:** Hollow light-metal support cylinder with transverse walls forming closed air cells, with a regular corrugated outer surface under the usual rubber covering.
