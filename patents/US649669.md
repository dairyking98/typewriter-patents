## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type become clogged with dust and ink from the ribbon, and hand brushing them while hanging in the type basket is tedious.

**Solution:** A brush attachment kept on the machine that works inside the type basket to clean the types while they are in use.
