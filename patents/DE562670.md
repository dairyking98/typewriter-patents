## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On tabulating machine printing drums, the strength of the type lever blow depends on drum speed, limiting the working speed range.

**Solution:** Type levers on a rotating drum are driven by separate cam bodies, shaped for the desired blow strength at any drum speed, instead of by hook stops catching the levers themselves.

**How it works:** A ring-shaped carrier drum turns once per card passing the sensing brushes. Inside, type levers and cam bodies sit in sets of plates, each set axially offset. Four shafts turn once per drum revolution through gears driven by a fixed gear, carrying ratchet wheels and return cams, and magnets select the levers to fire. A selected cam swings its type lever in the direction of drum travel with a cam-surface-controlled motion to strike the pressure abutment. The actuating mechanism slides axially out of the drum for assembly.
