## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card holders in front of the platen hide the line of print.

**Solution:** Mount the card holders on hinges so they swing down out of the way.

**How it works:** Standards on a plate screwed to the shift rail carry hinged roller or curved-plate card holders with hinge axes parallel to the platen. A spring-metal scale plate retains them in the raised position. They curve to guide card edges and can be removable.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** standard; card holder roller; hinge; scale plate; shift rail

**Improves on:**

- US950326 card holders: holders swing away to expose the print line
