## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** Rubber cover plate and inflatable air cushion are united into one pad that needs no adjustment and can be pumped up through a valve.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter pads of rubber ball are two-part and costly.

**Solution:** Rubber cover plate and inflatable air cushion are united into one pad that needs no adjustment and can be pumped up through a valve.
