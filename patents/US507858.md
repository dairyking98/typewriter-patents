## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cheap machines need simple automatic spacing and a design usable with both hands.

**Solution:** A very cheap machine in which segmental type plates on hinged broad levers are swung by thimble-like finger pieces to place a type under a hole, and the lever press prints and spaces.

**How it works:** Two broad hinged levers pivoted at the centre carry a keyboard at the front and a segmental type plate pivoted on each rear end. A belt from each plate leads to a finger piece worn like a thimble on a finger. Pulling the finger piece outward swings the plate so a chosen type lies under a hole in a table beneath the platen. Pressing the lever raises the plate level and the type projects through the hole and prints on the paper. A shifting plate and carriage pawl on the rack bar give automatic letter spacing from the lever motion.
