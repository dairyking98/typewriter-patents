## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing machines use a narrow ribbon with no provision for choosing between two parallel colored or functional lines (for example copying and record) across its width.

**Solution:** A ribbon guide attached to the ribbon carrier slides sideways between two locked positions so either half of a duplex ribbon sits over the line of writing.

**How it works:** On the Oliver machine, the ribbon carrier swings toward and from the platen on linked arms driven from the universal bar. The attachment is a plate with a U-shaped portion whose bent ends form ribbon eyes, with a shank extending from its closed end. Retaining flanges hold the U portion against vertical movement against the carrier plate while allowing it to slide sideways. It is locked at each adjusted position so it does not shift as the carrier moves, and the shifting mechanism is carried entirely on the ribbon carrier itself.
