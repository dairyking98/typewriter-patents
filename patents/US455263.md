## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements to attach the ribbon to the spool

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention has reference to the manufacture of inking-ribbons for use on type-writing machines, and its general object is to enable users to handle these articles with greater convenience and cleanliness.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon end is left uninked, varnished and covered with metal foil so it can be handled and embossed with identifying marks.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon ends smear transfer ink on the fingers and garments when the ribbon is handled.

**Solution:** The ribbon end is left uninked, varnished and covered with metal foil so it can be handled and embossed with identifying marks.
