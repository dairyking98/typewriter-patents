## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to to devices for applying paint to a tape, in particular typewriter ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** A smooth roller replaces the grooved one and the helical groove on the paint-lifting roller works against a smooth opposite wall, so ink is lifted and transferred evenly in either ribbon direction, in amounts matching consumption.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A helically grooved inking roller only feeds ink when the ribbon moves in one direction, and a toothed inking wheel gives uneven, excessive ink.

**Solution:** A smooth roller replaces the grooved one and the helical groove on the paint-lifting roller works against a smooth opposite wall, so ink is lifted and transferred evenly in either ribbon direction, in amounts matching consumption.

**How it works:** The paint-lifting roller is immersed in the ink container, and its surface lifts paint to the reversible ribbon, which runs against the smooth wall.
