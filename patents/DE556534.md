> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a device for reversing the longitudinal movement of the Ribbon of stenography machines with driven by a tiltable worm gear Ribbon spools. The main thing in the invention is that reversing the worm gear is effected by a spring that is tensioned by hand by pressing a key button when the end positions of the Ribbon triggered by means of a lever controlled by the ribbon and then the reversal is effected automatically, the tensioning of the spring by the writer at any given moment between the change in direction and the reaching of the end positions of the ink ribbon can be made. Devices of this type are known in which the tilting can be pivoted by a bearing frame is effected, which carries a screw on each side, both of which Worms alternately engage with worm gears, which are located on the »5 axes of the ribbon spools are located. It has also been proposed to cause the worms and worm gears to engage with a spring which can yield when the tips of the teeth collide.
