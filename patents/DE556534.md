## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenography machine ribbon reversing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for reversing the longitudinal movement of the Ribbon of stenography machines with driven by a tiltable worm gear Ribbon spools.

**How it works (reading):** The writer tensions a reversing spring at any chosen moment with a key, and a lever driven by the ribbon end releases the spring so the worm gear reverses the ribbon automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On stenotype machines the ribbon reversal is triggered by a key press, so the writer often learns too late that the ribbon has run out and must stop mid-sentence.

**Solution:** The writer tensions a reversing spring at any chosen moment with a key, and a lever driven by the ribbon end releases the spring so the worm gear reverses the ribbon automatically.
