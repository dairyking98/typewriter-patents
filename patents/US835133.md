## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**How it works (reading):** A note scale on a segmental base, moved over by a pinion and wheel on the platen shaft, with a ruling pen on a rail for staff lines, so the machine prints notes at set positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says ordinary typewriters cannot print musical notes on staves.

**Solution:** A note scale on a segmental base, moved over by a pinion and wheel on the platen shaft, with a ruling pen on a rail for staff lines, so the machine prints notes at set positions.
