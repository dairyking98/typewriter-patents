## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements to attach the ribbon to the spool

**How it works (reading):** A steel-wire clamp pivots on the reel washers and snaps by a hook into the spool drive groove, releasing with a pencil push.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon clamps hooked into teeth or pressed over the hub, dirtied hands and popped off.

**Solution:** A steel-wire clamp pivots on the reel washers and snaps by a hook into the spool drive groove, releasing with a pencil push.
