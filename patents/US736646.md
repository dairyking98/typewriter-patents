## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper; Hammer-impression mechanisms; Special processing for irreversibly adding or changing the sheet copy material characteristics or its appearance, e.g. stamping, annotation printing, punching

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention has reference to that class of machines wherein the sheet to be written upon is supported on a fiat bed or platen beneath a writing mechanism arranged to travel thereover for letter and line spacing.

**How it works (reading):** A normally stationary carbon sheet lies over the platen with one edge exposed; the platen has a longitudinal depression under the projecting carbon edge so the second sheet can be thrust in beneath it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says duplicate copies on bills and statements need the carbon sheet to be adjusted each time, and folded sheets are awkward to insert.

**Solution:** A normally stationary carbon sheet lies over the platen with one edge exposed; the platen has a longitudinal depression under the projecting carbon edge so the second sheet can be thrust in beneath it.
