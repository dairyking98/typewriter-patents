## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The inventor says earlier escapement types, with a rear or forward dog releasing the carriage, gave an uneven touch and poor spacing at high operating speed.

**Solution:** An escapement in which a mainspring feeds the carriage through a rack, with a detaining member and a spacing member that engage it in turn; the detailed sequence is beyond the first 3000 characters.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
