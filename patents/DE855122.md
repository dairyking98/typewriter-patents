## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**How it works (reading):** The typewriter is attached by parallelogram scissors to the lid of a box that folds into a compact unit, so the box is out of the way in use and the machine can stow in a desk drawer.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A loose cover for a portable typewriter is annoying in use and easily lost, and the machine is not stored neatly.

**Solution:** The typewriter is attached by parallelogram scissors to the lid of a box that folds into a compact unit, so the box is out of the way in use and the machine can stow in a desk drawer.
