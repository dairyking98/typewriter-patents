**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to automatic typewriters or similar machines of the pneumatically operated' type 'and' has particular reference to the line spacing mechanism thereof, but is not restricted to such use as it may be used in any connection for which it may beapplicable.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing on a pneumatically operated automatic typewriter needs a simple, durable mechanism that can be attached to most makes.

**Solution:** A line spacing mechanism attached to the carriage side plates, with a platen-engaging arm and pivoted parts, advances the platen between lines.
