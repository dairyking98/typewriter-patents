## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Fixed-length pawls cannot be adjusted to change platen line-space accurately.

**Solution:** Both the detent pawl and platen-actuating pawl have nose pieces that thumbwheel screws lengthen or shorten for adjustment.

**How it works:** The platen has ratchet teeth on its end plate. The detent pawl has a main body and a nose carrying a pawl wheel, joined by a screw with oppositely threaded ends and a central thumbwheel. A spring metal retainer on the main portion prevents the nose turning and also locks the thumbwheel. The platen-actuating pawl has a screw-threaded nose in the main portion. Turning the wheel changes the pawl length and shifts the platen circumferentially to adjust line position.
