## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On high-speed typewriters the operator does not notice when the ribbon has run out to one end.

**Solution:** A double lever under the hood is dragged out at one end by the ribbon-end buckle, showing a coloured tip outside the hood.

**How it works:** Ribbon ends are fixed to the spool hubs by buckles with horns that protrude inside the tape. A double lever lies loosely on a plate above the typebasket, held by a pin in a slot and a tension spring against two upstanding pins. When a spool runs out, the buckle catches an arm of the lever, which swings about the pin on the opposite side so one coloured end is pulled out from under the hood and into view. Pivoting about the outer pins gives a large visible movement and a small one inside the hood.
