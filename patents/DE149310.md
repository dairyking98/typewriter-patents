## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable cylinder with rows of holes on its jacket, moved by the table key; a stop pin on the carriage engages the hole in the cylinder position for each column, so changing the cylinder changes the form.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Changing forms with many columns requires re-adjusting every stop; this is time-consuming.

**Solution:** A rotatable cylinder with rows of holes on its jacket, moved by the table key; a stop pin on the carriage engages the hole in the cylinder position for each column, so changing the cylinder changes the form.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** scale cylinder; rows of holes; stop pin; table key
