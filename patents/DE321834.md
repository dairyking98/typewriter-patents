**How it works (reading):** A power drive starts from zero speed and increases gradually after each keystroke, with a coupling and drive members that give the keys an even light stroke and switch automatically between addition and subtraction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Motor-driven typewriter calculators jerk the arithmetic units when they start, which can damage sensitive parts, and the key stroke is uneven.

**Solution:** A power drive starts from zero speed and increases gradually after each keystroke, with a coupling and drive members that give the keys an even light stroke and switch automatically between addition and subtraction.
