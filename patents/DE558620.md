## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**How it works (reading):** A steel tube with an inward bent springy tongue on the coil bar presses against the spool shaft through an opening in the hub, holding the spool from sliding axially, or the tongue is punched from the spool hub itself.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Loosely fitted ribbon spools slide along the spool carrier pin while writing, so the ribbon unrolls too far and wrinkles.

**Solution:** A steel tube with an inward bent springy tongue on the coil bar presses against the spool shaft through an opening in the hub, holding the spool from sliding axially, or the tongue is punched from the spool hub itself.
