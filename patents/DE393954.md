## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (zero-setting shaft overrun prevention)

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded locking cone rides on the zero disk and drops into a gap at zero; a small backward move of the cone lifts an obstacle that blocks further rotation until the handle is released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Zeroing shafts on calculating machines can overthrow past the zero stop when the hand turns them.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded locking cone rides on the zero disk and drops into a gap at zero; a small backward move of the cone lifts an obstacle that blocks further rotation until the handle is released.
