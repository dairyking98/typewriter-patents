## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Sheet-metal housings for calculating and office machines transmit and amplify machine vibration as noise.

**Solution:** Parts of the housing walls are made of fine-mesh fabric or braid, which damps vibration and radiates less sound than a solid sheet.
