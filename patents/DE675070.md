## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Worn or dried-in typewriter ribbons need a means to refresh the ink without replacement.

**Solution:** A container holds absorbent material soaked in a solvent agent whose vapour dissolves the ribbon colour for even redistribution.

The invention relates to a device for carrying out the method according to the main patent. This offers a large evaporation surface. Through a. This body is useful in front of a grate, a sieve or similar equipment direct contact with the tapes protected.
