## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to tabulating mechan isms for type-writing machines, and has for its main object to provide a novel and efficient tabulating mechanism and one wherein the actuation of a key will effect the release of the carriage and will interpose a tabulating-stop in its path of movement,so as to arrest it at the desired point for column or, tabulating work.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating key releases the carriage and interposes a stop from a rock-shaft stop-bar that arrests a carriage-carried stop at the chosen column.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column work needs a tabulating mechanism where one key both releases the carriage and sets a stop in its path.

**Solution:** A tabulating key releases the carriage and interposes a stop from a rock-shaft stop-bar that arrests a carriage-carried stop at the chosen column.

**How it works:** Typebars on a curved fulcrum wire are driven by bell-cranks and links from key levers; a universal bar moves a rock-shaft with feed dogs working a feed wheel, pinion and rack as in a Remington No. 6. The tabulating key rocks a stop-bar carrying spring-fitted tabulating stops so they stand in the path of a stop on the carriage, while a connected carriage-release device frees the feed rack so the carriage runs until stopped. The rack is mounted on short rock-shafts with springs that hold it engaged with the pinion.
