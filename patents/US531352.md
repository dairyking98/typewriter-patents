## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Mechanical linkages for operating type are complex; the inventor proposes pneumatic actuation.

**Solution:** Type on the ends of rods sliding in converging tubular holders are driven upward to the paper by air pushed from finger-pressed collapsible air chambers.

**How it works:** Tubular type holders converge on one point on the printing roller, each with a rod carrying a type at its end. Each rod is connected by a tube to a collapsible air chamber serving as a key. Pressing a chamber compresses the air and drives its rod up so the type strikes the paper; on release the chamber re-expands and gravity returns the rod. Chambers sit on a rocking board whose depression draws a toothed bar by cord to advance a pawl-rack letter space, and a crank and ratchet on the roller gives line feed. Rod order can be arbitrary for cipher writing, with a movable lettered band scale.
