## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops and paper fingers must be set separately, risking wrong paper-finger placement and broken types.

**Solution:** Margin stops and paper fingers share one axis, free to move independently in rotation but coupled axially, so moving the carriage to set the margin positions the fingers too.

**How it works:** The margin stop lever pivots in the carriage side part; a spring links it to an intermediate lever on a rotatable margin stop axis. The stops slide freely along the rod but turn with it. Paper fingers on the same axis pivot independently but follow the stops axially, so each stop shift moves its finger. An elastic coupling joins the setting lever and axis, and the lever can be sunk into the side wall.
