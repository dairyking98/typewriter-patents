## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improve the paper-carriage and platen: how paper is held, how the carriage runs on its ways, and how the platen turns for line spacing.

**Solution:** A carriage hung on rods with a friction roller and a platen line-space action, based on the inventor's earlier patent.

**How it works:** The carriage frame is a rear rod on a sliding bar and a front rod with a friction roller on a rail; end yokes pivot on the rear rod so the carriage swings up to show the line. The platen is journaled in the yokes. A pulling cord to a spring drum moves the carriage, and dogs on a rack controlled by a universal lever stop it letter by letter. A device rotates the platen for line spacing. The opening ends before the platen action is described.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** carriage frame; sliding bar; front rod; friction roller; rail; end yoke; platen; letter scale; pointer; paper guide; pulling cord; spring drum; rack; dog; universal lever
