## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Fixing a card-holder to the platen with screws would mar the rubber cover.

**Solution:** A C-shaped tapered locking ring on the platen head wedges the card-holder's end hook against the rubber cover, with no screws.

**How it works:** A ring with one end narrower and tapered, plus an inward chamfer, sits on the smooth periphery of the left platen head. A curved card-holder bar has a hook at each end, one catching the bevelled right edge of the rubber cover. Turning the ring drives its tapering end between the left hook and the cover end, wedging the bar outward and clamping it. The grip is friction only.
