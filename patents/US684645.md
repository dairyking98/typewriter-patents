## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars in visible-writing machines could bind or clamp between the type-bar hub and its hanger, and could vibrate sideways, when the bar is pivoted on a short pivot.

**Solution:** The type-bar hub is guided sideways by a series of balls placed between the side of the hub and the side of the hanger, so the bar moves accurately without clamping.

**How it works:** Balls between the hub and the hanger side act as anti-friction guides that prevent sidewise vibration of the pivoted type-bar.
