## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar return springs connected to the frame or rod are inefficient; typebars rebound or lack momentum and the stroke strains the bar.

**Solution:** Pivot the typebar, connecting rod and return spring so the rod's leverage is greatest at stroke start and falls to a dead point, while the spring's leverage is smallest at start and small again at rest.

**How it works:** The typebar hub pivots on a pin; the connecting rod from the key lever attaches at one point on the hub and a spiral spring runs from another hub point to a lug on the rod. At rest the rod lever arm is at right angles to its motion, giving maximum leverage and momentum, and near a dead-point at the end of the stroke. The spring passes through its own dead-point mid-stroke and has high leverage at the start of the return then low at the end, withdrawing the bar quickly and slowing it to avoid rebound.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Rebound and clash prevention](../features/bar-rebound.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** key lever; connecting rod; typebar; hub; spiral spring; stop; serrated bar

**Improves on:**

- earlier typebar return springs: spring fixed between typebar and frame or rod gave poor leverage profile
