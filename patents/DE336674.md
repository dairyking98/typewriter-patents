## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spool can drift off its hub when the ribbon is wound.

**Solution:** A hub clamping lever with teeth grips the ribbon end, and a second lever arm presses against the outside of the spool to hold it on the axis.
