## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon guide needed a strong spring for the spool housing and a weak one for the arm, so two springs were used.

**Solution:** A single tension spring serves both the arm and the spool housing, attached near the arm's pivot.
