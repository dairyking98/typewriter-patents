## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Cash register with printing device: The invention relates to a Particularly suitable for the offices of electricity, gas, waterworks and the like Cash register with printing device, in which a Receipt slip (e.g. monthly statement) is printed twice, of which then a part remaining in the till is cut off. The essence of the invention is that the remaining parts of the receipts in the cash register on a The table is automatically stacked one after the other and held on the same by grippers will. This arrangement has the advantage that in the control comparison the receipts with the control strip or from the sellers led cash books eliminates the time-consuming organization of the receipts comes because the receipts are taken from the cash register in the order in which they are piled up can be. Since the receipts are printed with a consecutive number, can be determined immediately by stacking them one after the other whether a receipt is missing. The drawings show the cash register in one embodiment.
