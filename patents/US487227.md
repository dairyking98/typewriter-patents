## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A message typed at one station needs to appear on a typewriter at a distant station or stations.

**Solution:** A transmitting typewriter has a normally open circuit-controller under each key, wired to electromagnets that work the keys of a Remington-style receiving typewriter.

**How it works:** At the sending station each key strikes a spring-returned cap that presses a head onto spring arms, closing a circuit independent of the key lever. The circuits run to receiving machines where one electromagnet per type lever, arranged in circular rows in a case, pulls an armature linked by rods to its type lever. An indicating device tells the sender whether the line circuits are in working order. The receiver carries paper on a roll around an impression roller on a movable frame, and mechanisms rotate the roller and feed the carriage by electric means.
