## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Board of a stereotype matrix for expression by typewriter. The invention relates to a matrix board for producing stereotypes after imprinting with the typewriter. The die board according to the invention is characterized in that an indentable-oak mass, e.g. B. from -2% Io @ dellierton, plaster, white lead, with a binding agent such as marshmallow root slime and water on a net-like support grid is laid on with letter-sized meshes. When letters are embossed, parts of the driven-in matrix layer flow down into the mesh of the support grid. The counterpressure of the support grid distributed according to the inventor in the soft:% - Iatrize mass so evenly, that even on those parts of its surface below which parts of the Lattice-shaped, igen supporting organ are located, shows no disruptive resistance. The matrix mass stored in this way allows with perfect sharpness Individually embossed next to each other, millimeter-deep impressions at fractions of a millimeter of a millimeter, can be done with little effort and high speed edit and can be bent as required for rotational molding before hardening.
