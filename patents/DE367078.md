## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (time clock ribbon switching with card printing)

**How it works (reading):** A spring lock with a nose between pins on the switching disc holds the ribbon carrier at its end positions and completes the switch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon change on a time clock was creeping and could happen during printing, giving double-colour prints; the carrier could also stall between positions.

**Solution:** A spring lock with a nose between pins on the switching disc holds the ribbon carrier at its end positions and completes the switch.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
