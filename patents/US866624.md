## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Shape of flexible containers rectangular and flat, i.e. without structural provision for thickness of contents; Embossing; Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in a paper-carrying roll forming both the platen of a typewriting machine and a type cylinder of a printing press;
