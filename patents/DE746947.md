## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (locking summation control members on typewriters)

**How it works (reading):** Controllable blocking members sit between the summation control members and the reverse key and respond to the paper carriage motion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On typewriters with both vertical and cross totalizers, the reverse key may accidentally release the wrong summation control member.

**Solution:** Controllable blocking members sit between the summation control members and the reverse key and respond to the paper carriage motion.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** summation control members; blocking members; reverse key; vertical counters; cross counters
