## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved carriage for typewriting-machines which is made noiseless in its motion by means of antifriction-rollers arranged at the points of support, and to which, by reducing the friction between the carriage and its supports a free and easy motion is imparted;

**How it works (reading):** A grooved guide rail on the carriage bottom runs on anti-friction balls between it and a supporting rail, with a retaining roller engaging the rack bar and anti-friction rollers at the upper frame bearing against an upper guide rail.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage friction makes motion noisy and stiff, so a free and easy carriage motion is wanted.

**Solution:** A grooved guide rail on the carriage bottom runs on anti-friction balls between it and a supporting rail, with a retaining roller engaging the rack bar and anti-friction rollers at the upper frame bearing against an upper guide rail.
