## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage hits the tabulator stop bar after a long run and the shock stresses the machine.

**Solution:** Shock absorbing springs in the stop bar mechanism of the tabulator that absorb the impact of arrest and distribute the stress.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
