## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** The purpose of my invention is to provide improved means for supporting sheets of paper or cards of various widths beneath a writ ing-machine adapted to print upon fiat surfaces;

**How it works (reading):** A platen carriage runs on fixed guideways under the printing machine and takes interchangeable platens of different sizes; each platen has edge guides, a locking means aligns it on the carriage, and the carriage moves for line spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards and sheets of various widths need support beneath the machine, and a single platen cannot suit every size.

**Solution:** A platen carriage runs on fixed guideways under the printing machine and takes interchangeable platens of different sizes; each platen has edge guides, a locking means aligns it on the carriage, and the carriage moves for line spacing.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
