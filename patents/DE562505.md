## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Arabic and other oriental scripts need many letters taking double space, more than the available type levers, so both shift positions must carry double-space letters yet some letters need single spacing.

**Solution:** An auxiliary stop on the escapement frame is moved into the path of the movable pawl by certain key levers, but only in the lower carriage position, limiting the carriage to one step instead of two.

**How it works:** The switching frame carries a ratchet wheel with a fixed pawl and a movable pawl on a swingable carrier. Normally the movable pawl travels to a stop that gives double spacing. A pivoted lever with a bent end can be swung into the pawl's path, limiting it to a single step. A forked lower end of the lever engages a pin of an arm on the machine frame, which is moved through an adjustable link and an oscillating shaft by a shift rail below the key levers. The rail has cutouts and projections so only certain key levers act on it. The device acts only in the lower writing position of the paper carriage.
