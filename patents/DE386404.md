## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A separate pull-off arm used to hold the paper webs drifts, so the copies at the end of a form do not line up with the original.

**Solution:** The pull-off clamp is attached directly to the carbon sheet carriage and spans the full width, holding the overlapping web ends together so they cannot creep.
