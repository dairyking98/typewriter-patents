## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On typebar machines (shown on an Underwood) the main, marginal and central feed rollers had to be moved off the platen separately to straighten the paper, which took time.

**Solution:** One hand lever lifts all feed rollers off the platen at once and, in the same movement, raises an index scale to the printing line.

**How it works:** A hand lever pivoted on the platen frame slides a longitudinal rod. Cam fingers on the rod push spring-arm supports so the marginal rollers leave the platen, and a cam on the rod moves the spring-pressed central roller away. Cams on the main roller shaft are driven by arms on the same rod to lift the three main rollers, whose arm tension is set by ratchet-toothed sleeves and coil springs. The rod's studs also run in cam grooves of an indicator bar linked to the platen frame, raising its equally spaced indices to the printing point, whether the platen is in upper or lower case. Moving the lever back lets all rollers bear on the paper and drops the scale out of the way.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; main feed roller; marginal feed roller; central feed roller; hand lever; sliding rod; cam finger; spring arm; ratchet sleeve; coil spring; indicator bar; paper shield; platen frame

**Numbers:** three main feed rollers

**Improves on:**

- earlier machines with separately operated marginal, central and main feed rollers: one lever moves all rollers and the scale together
