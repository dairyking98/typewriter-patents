## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Toy typewriters

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal objects of the present invention is to provide means which will to a very great extent eliminate this objectionable action and the disastrous results caused by careless or inexperienced operators.

**How it works (reading):** A mechanism between the key and the typebar damps the impact force so that all keys strike with about the same force and the platen and small types are protected.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Careless or heavy key strikes make small characters puncture the paper, indent it and flatten the type.

**Solution:** A mechanism between the key and the typebar damps the impact force so that all keys strike with about the same force and the platen and small types are protected.
