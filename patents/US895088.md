## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters with a rotatable type carrier needed a carrier for the type bars that is simple to build and light, to cut manufacturing cost.

**Solution:** The type carrier is two spaced disks on a shaft with concentric notches guiding sliding type bars, turned by a lever-and-segment index and struck by a hammer, with vertical carrier shift for the second row.

**How it works:** Two disks on a horizontal shaft in front of the platen guide radial type bars, each held back by a spring, arranged in two concentric circles. The operator swings a lever over a lettered segment; a toothed rack on the lever turns a pinion and spur gear to rotate the carrier to the chosen type. Pressing the lever down moves a hammer, which hits the type bar in front of the platen and throws it against the platen. A shift key rocks a shaft that raises the carrier on two vertically sliding brackets to bring the second row into line. An inking roller between carrier and platen is lifted by the lever to ink the type and moves out of the type path.
