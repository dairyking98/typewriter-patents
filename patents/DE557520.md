**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**How it works (reading):** Place a fixed key board above the swinging drive boards, and split the swinging board into halves so a rack turns the type wheel left or right of centre.

## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the main patent DE469128 the keys were attached to the movable letter board, which made operation less convenient and slower.

**Solution:** Place a fixed key board above the swinging drive boards, and split the swinging board into halves so a rack turns the type wheel left or right of centre.

**How it works:** Two swingable boards a and a1 correspond to the letter board of the main patent. A fixed board b above them carries the keys m. A struck key depresses one half; its lever d moves a push rod h, which shifts right or left depending on the half. The rack meshing with the type wheel axis gear is moved with it, turning the wheel to either side of its rest position, with the setting arm resting centrally between the halves. A helical spring under gear wheel p returns the parts.
