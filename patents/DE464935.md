## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-feed devices either gave several switch sizes or allowed precise setting of the line-space unit, but not both, and the platen must still turn freely by hand.

**Solution:** Combine a fine adjustment of the line-space unit on the line lever's short arm with an adjustable multiplier (1, 1.5, 2, 2.5 units), and put a normally open, self-engaging clutch between the lever's toothed segment and a worm drive on the platen.

**How it works:** The line lever, which also returns the carriage, acts through a rod and short arm (adjustable in effective length) on a toothed segment and drive. The drive turns one half of a clutch whose other half is fixed to a worm shaft meshing with a worm wheel on the platen. The clutch is open at rest so the platen can be turned by hand; the first movement of the lever shifts the loose half axially up inclined surfaces by driving pins and then carries it round, so teeth are fully engaged first. A stop limits the stroke and an adjustable line-switch controller holds the lever back to give multiple line spaces. The worm, clutch and drive sit on a sheet-metal bracket fixed to the carriage.
