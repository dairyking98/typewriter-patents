## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guides in the printing zone, e.g. guides for preventing contact of conveyed sheets with printhead

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to avoid the necessity for extending the tallysheet the full length of the work-sheet by mounting the tally-sheet on the frame of the type-writer and by providing for the automatic line-spacing cf the tally-sheet past the printing-point ofthe machine as the machine is line-spaced relative to the work-sheet on the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Mounts a tally sheet on the machine frame so it prints the same data as the bill and is line-spaced automatically past the printing point as the machine line-spaces.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a flat-platen billing machine, a tally of printed data must be recorded separately and a tally sheet cannot easily span the whole work sheet.

**Solution:** Mounts a tally sheet on the machine frame so it prints the same data as the bill and is line-spaced automatically past the printing point as the machine line-spaces.

**How it works:** The tally strip is fed on the machine frame and advanced in step with the line-spacing of the frame over the platen.
