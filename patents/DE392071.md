## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** A three-legged support holds the type levers in writing position and can be folded down by a sliding member, lowering the height of the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bow-shaped type levers make the machine tall and hard to pack for travel.

**Solution:** A three-legged support holds the type levers in writing position and can be folded down by a sliding member, lowering the height of the machine.
