## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The step-by-step escapement needs improvement to be more positive in action so that failures to act are less likely.

**Solution:** An escapement with dogs carried on a dog rocker actuated from a universal bar suspended beneath the key levers, stepping the escapement wheel carrying the carriage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
