## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A very simple, cheap writing device is wanted, in which the machine travels over the paper instead of the paper moving.

**Solution:** A small frame rides on the paper on toothed rollers, and a hand-held type wheel is rolled along a rack on the frame so that the wanted letter lands over a writing opening and prints through onto the paper.

**How it works:** The frame plate rests on two toothed rollers whose teeth are spaced at the letter pitch; a finger on the roller shaft through a cutout advances the frame one tooth per letter, and the same rollers give line spacing. On the frame top lie a dye pad and a rack carrying the characters in reverse order of those on the type wheel. The type wheel is carried in a fork on a handle, and a mark on the wheel is aligned with the wanted letter on the rack. Rolling the wheel along the rack carries that letter to the writing opening, where it prints through onto the paper. A coil spring returns the wheel to its base position, a curved stop collar guides it to the opening, and the type is inked either on the dye pad or by a small spring roller on the handle.
