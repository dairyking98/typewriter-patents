## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ribbon support in a Densmore machine's typering must be moved aside for cleaning, aligning or repairs without soiling the hands.

**Solution:** A hinged ribbon support and guide on a plate fixed under the type-bar hanger screws swings up out of the way.

**How it works:** A sheet-metal plate lies on two typebar hangers and is held by their screws. It projects inwards with two parallel projections, each with an ear and pivot pin carrying a hinged arm that underlies the ribbon. Integral overlying arms keep the ribbon from rising and slipping off, and are joined by a curved member that eases ribbon insertion. The ribbon and its support swing up to vertical, and the ribbon is removed or restored by grasping the support, not the ribbon.
