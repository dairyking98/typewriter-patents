## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-driven carriage return via a steep screw jams and jerks the carriage.

**Solution:** An endless chain driven continuously by a motor is coupled to the carriage for the return stroke, giving faster and smoother return.
