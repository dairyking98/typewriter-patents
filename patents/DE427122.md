## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Column addition on a typewriter needs a counter on the carriage that is set by the keyed numbers.

**Solution:** Keyed numerals rotate a shaft through cam organs, and a clutch from a power source transmits the value to the counter adjusting wheels through a relay roller and dial.
