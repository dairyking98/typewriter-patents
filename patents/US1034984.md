## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper presser rolls do not feed the paper uniformly and are hard to adjust when displaced.

**Solution:** Presser roll held against the platen in slotted bearings on an adjustable shaft, with springs and ratchets for uniform paper feed.
