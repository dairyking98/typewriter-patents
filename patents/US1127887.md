## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cards and envelopes are hard to print on a visible writing machine.

**Solution:** A plate with scale and resilient curved fingers holds a card close to the platen at the line of print.

**How it works:** A card plate is mounted adjustably on the shift rail, stationary relative to the platen's transverse motion, with a scale edge. Central resilient arms with trowel-shaped ends curve rearwards to press the card to the platen. The deflected ends guide the card in smoothly, and it does not hide the print.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)
