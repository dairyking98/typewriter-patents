## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates generally to tabulating mechanism for typewriting and other machines, and particularly to means for enabling the carriage to jump to any selected column or field, such devices known in the art as column skippers.

**How it works (reading):** A pinion beside the column-stop rack that rotates as stops pass, carrying helical stops; thrust rods operated by keys place one into a revolving stop path to arrest the carriage at the chosen column.
