## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A movable member such as a type wheel must be located at will in any one of several fixed positions by a continuously running power drive that is normally disconnected from it.

**Solution:** A reciprocating carrier holds one settable pin per position; a key sets one pin, and as the carrier rises the pin rides a helical cam face on the wheel's shaft, turning the wheel by the shorter angle to the pin's position and locking it there.

**How it works:** The wheel (which may carry types) is fixed to a shaft with a cylinder bearing two oppositely inclined helical flanges that merge into a straight channel as wide as a pin. Eight radial pins sit around a carrier that reciprocates along the shaft axis, each held by a detent either retracted or projected. Pressing a key lever sets its pin inward and at the same time trips an escapement lever that frees a friction-driven power shaft for a half turn; a crank drives the carrier upward. The projected pin meets one helical face and turns the wheel through the smaller of the two possible angles until it enters the channel, which locks the wheel; a lever detent in a notched locking wheel also holds it. Near the top a cam face pushes the pin back to idle, and releasing the key lets the shaft finish its rotation and draw the carrier down for the next selection.
