## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guides for printing material; Width

**In the patent’s own words:** The main objects of my invention are to provide for a finer, quicker and more convenient adjustment of the guide.

**How it works (reading):** The side-edge gauge is carried on a threaded, flattened shank fed by a nut, with a positioning block, giving a finer and quicker adjustment of the gauge along the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Side-edge paper gauges are not adjustable finely or quickly enough.

**Solution:** The side-edge gauge is carried on a threaded, flattened shank fed by a nut, with a positioning block, giving a finer and quicker adjustment of the gauge along the platen.
