## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon edge strip only is used; operator should use upper or lower edge.

**Solution:** An adjustable actuator on the universal-bar frame, set by a side rock-shaft, selects upper or lower ribbon edge.

**How it works:** The slotted arm that actuates the ribbon vibrator is adjustable on the reciprocating frame, moved by a rock-shaft with finger-piece at the side, and works in both shift positions.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** universal bar frame; actuator; rock-shaft; finger piece; ribbon vibrator

**Improves on:**

- Underwood front-strike machine: adds adjustable ribbon height
