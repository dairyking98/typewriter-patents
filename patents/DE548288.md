**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guiding and cutting device on typewriters, where the writing paper or the forms to be written on are in tape form Rolls wound or folded are fed to the platen.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary roller pair lifts the sheet off the platen above the writing line, and two paper guide rods, one of them grooved to guide the knife, tension the sheet against the knife edge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-layer ribbon forms create friction between sheets and ribbons; the sheet must be tensioned to be cut.

**Solution:** An auxiliary roller pair lifts the sheet off the platen above the writing line, and two paper guide rods, one of them grooved to guide the knife, tension the sheet against the knife edge.

**How it works:** Gears drive the roller pair upward; turning the cutter handle moves the rear rod to tension the sheet for cutting.
