## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon on a polygraph duplicator must unwind from one reel to the other positively, at an adjustable speed, and in either direction.

**Solution:** An adjustable pawl and ratchet feed the ribbon from one reel to the other in either direction, with the feed speed set by an adjusting device on the pawl.

**How it works:** A pawl engages a ratchet on the ribbon reel and is adjusted for stroke, so each cycle unwinds a set amount of ribbon.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** feeding pawl; ratchet; adjusting device; ribbon reels
