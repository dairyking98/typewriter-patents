## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column tabulators on the Underwood needed a simpler way to choose the stop for a tab, and an extra set of tabulator keys was awkward to fit.

**Solution:** Column stops on the carriage turn a pinion that carries a volute (spiral) series of stops, and a key-lifted single stop picks which volute stop, hence which column, arrests the carriage; the figure keys 1-0 double as tab keys when a key-bar is pressed with them.

**How it works:** Column stops set on a rack bar on the carriage engage a pinion intermittently as the carriage runs, turning a plate with ten volute stops at increasing radii. A key-operated stop is raised to a height that puts it in the path of one volute stop. A key-bar behind the figure-key row, pressed with a figure key in one finger stroke, shifts a rock shaft with graduated arms under the figure key levers. The figure key then lifts the stop by an amount set by its arm length, a universal bar releases the power-driven carriage, and the carriage runs until the chosen column stop turns the pinion enough for the volute stop to hit the raised stop. A stop under the universal bar prevents the types from printing during a tab.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** column stop; rack bar; stop pinion; volute stop disk; key-operated stop; rock shaft; graduated arm; key-bar; universal bar; pawl; carriage release bar

**Numbers:** ten column stops; ten volute stops; ten figure keys 1 to 0

**Improves on:**

- Underwood tabulators with a separate set of tabulating keys: reuses the figure keys for tabulating so no extra key bank is needed
