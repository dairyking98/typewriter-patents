## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A folding portable front-strike typewriter needs a backspace mechanism that works on the carriage rack and still folds away with the carriage and bed.

**Solution:** A spring-returned slotted lever with a tooth, mounted on the foldable carriage bed, is pushed by a pivoted finger lever on the folding support so the tooth engages the carriage rack and pushes the carriage back one space.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on a bed carried on a foldable support, pulled by a spring drum and cord, and escaped by a pinion, clutch and ratchet wheel with dogs worked by the key-lever bar. A bar-like lever lies above the bed, slotted so it can slide lengthwise around its pivot screw, with a tooth at one end and a spring pulling it one way. A finger lever pivoted on the folding support has an upturned end through a slot in the bed that presses the head of the bar lever. The bar lever's cam incline brings its tooth into the rack and pushes the carriage back against the spring drum by one space per stroke. Because the finger lever is carried on the pivoted support and the connection is loose, the whole backspace device folds with the carriage over the keyboard.
