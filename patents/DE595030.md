## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an auxiliary ribbon device on typewriters, in which the Auxiliary ribbons serving for copies between the ends of two concentric arms curved to the paper roll are clamped.

**How it works (reading):** A loose U-shaped frame of thin flexible sheet metal carries the auxiliary ribbons between the writing sheets on the paper roller and is held only by the upper paper pressure device.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Auxiliary carbon ribbon holders on arms or shafts make inserting sheets awkward, get in the way of the paper roller, and soil the main ribbon.

**Solution:** A loose U-shaped frame of thin flexible sheet metal carries the auxiliary ribbons between the writing sheets on the paper roller and is held only by the upper paper pressure device.
