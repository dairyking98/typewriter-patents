## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (carbonless accounting strip) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens or like sheet supports for manifolding using pressure-sensitive layers or intermediaries, e.g. for book-keeping purposes with non-mechanical means for holding the elements in registered position, e.g. magnetic means

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for carbonless accounting from a strip with a fold made of paper or similar.

**How it works (reading):** A folding strip with recesses in its fold edge, and colour sheets with tabs that engage those recesses, so the copy sheets stay in register like the leaves of a book.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon-copy accounting sheets shift when several booking sheets are aligned, because the colour sheets are not held securely in the fold margin.

**Solution:** A folding strip with recesses in its fold edge, and colour sheets with tabs that engage those recesses, so the copy sheets stay in register like the leaves of a book.
