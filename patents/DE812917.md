## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**How it works (reading):** Fluorescent tubes behind a metal screen are fixed to the machine frame to light the keyboard and platen without glare.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Arm lamps and pendant lights cause glare and heat, and are hard to install and move.

**Solution:** Fluorescent tubes behind a metal screen are fixed to the machine frame to light the keyboard and platen without glare.
