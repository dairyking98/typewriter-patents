## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On the Elliott & Hatch flat-bed book typewriter, manifold copies are identical, but some copies must omit prices or marginal figures.

**Solution:** An insulating sheet fixed to the paper-confining frame is slid under the carbon sheet to stop it transferring print onto selected areas of the lower copy.

**How it works:** Paper sheets are laid on the flat platen with a carbon between them under the paper-confining frame. A cardboard, oiled-board or rubber insulating sheet is placed beneath the carbon, covering the part of the lower sheet that should stay blank. A thin metal bar embracing the sheet edge is screwed (slotted holes) to the frame at chosen hole positions, so the sheet rises and falls with the frame.
