## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**How it works (reading):** An indicator attached to the end of the platen roll shows distance-to-bottom characters one at a time through a window fixed to the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot see how far the paper's bottom edge is from the writing point without lifting the carriage.

**Solution:** An indicator attached to the end of the platen roll shows distance-to-bottom characters one at a time through a window fixed to the carriage.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
