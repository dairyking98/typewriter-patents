## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typing one character at a time is slow; the inventor wants two characters printed at once with the carriage spaced to suit.

**Solution:** Two concentric rotating type carriers (a disk and a drum), each turned by its own hand lever, project one spring-loaded type each side by side in one stroke while the carriage moves two spaces.

**How it works:** A disk with horizontal spring-pressed type rods sits on a hollow shaft; a drum below it with slanting type rods sits on an inner shaft. Each shaft is turned by a lever held in one hand over a semicircular character layout on the casing top. Depressing a lever raises its shaft, and a bell-crank pushes out the type rod facing the paper roll. The two types print side by side on the platen. Collars on the shafts lift a slide; as the shafts return the slide drives a pawl, bar and segment that move the carriage two spaces.

**Numbers:** two characters per stroke; carriage moves two spaces per double impression
