## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A narrower ribbon with two imprint lines and a carriage driver that lowers it for figures, so capitals and figures share one line, and the ribbon is turned more often.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** With three-row typebars and shift, the ribbon's lowercase imprint line wears much more than the capital and figure lines.

**Solution:** A narrower ribbon with two imprint lines and a carriage driver that lowers it for figures, so capitals and figures share one line, and the ribbon is turned more often.

**How it works:** Ribbon has reduced width, giving one line for lowercase types at the bar top and one for capitals in the middle. A driver rod on the paper carriage strikes a finger on a ribbon bracket when the figure shift is pressed, pushing the ribbon down against a spring so figures strike the capital line. On release the carriage rises and the spring restores the ribbon. The ribbon is flipped more often to wear both lines evenly.
