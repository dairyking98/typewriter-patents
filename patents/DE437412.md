> Description Translated from German Switching device for the paper trolley of typewriters. The invention refers to a switching device for the paper trolley of typewriters, in which the loose pawl in a known manner by a curve guide is performed in order to achieve the most silent operation of the switching device. It consists in the fact that the curve guide, in which a role on the loose pawl runs, is located on a stop piece that rotates on the fixed pawl is mounted and by a spring on the roller of the movable pawl is pressed. This design ensures a safe effect and permanent operability, since the essential parts are hardly accessible to impurities. An embodiment of the invention is shown in the drawing. The fixed pawl d is rotatably mounted in the bearing housing a between the tips o. On it sit around the axis / rotatable the loose pawl e, which carries a roller g at its extended end, and a stop piece h with curve guide rotatable around axis i. In the curve, the role g of the loose pawl e runs. The curved guide of the stop piece h is always pulled against the roller g by a spring k.
