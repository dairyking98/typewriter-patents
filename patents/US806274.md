## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars are hard to connect and disconnect, dust and grit falling from the paper enter the type-bar pivots, and link axes do not align with the bar pivots.

**Solution:** Type-bars connect to their sublevers by links that can turn relative to each other so their pivotal axes can lie at angles, and shields keep dust out of the type-bar pivotal bearings.
