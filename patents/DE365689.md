## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A linkage with a detented three-position setting lever and an operator key changes the lift stroke of the ribbon carrier.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon carrier lift needs to be adjustable between ribbon zones (red, blue) and a stencil (wax matrix) position.

**Solution:** A linkage with a detented three-position setting lever and an operator key changes the lift stroke of the ribbon carrier.

**How it works:** The ribbon carrier is guided in a sleeve on the lifting frame, which is hinged to the key lever, and connects to a two-armed lever. A link and bracket arrangement driven by a lever oscillated by the universal rail on each key stroke lifts the carrier, with the stroke set by where a pin engages the bracket's curved slot. A lever with a roller snaps into one of three notches of a leaf spring; a pull rod links it to a bracket moved by an upright key. A pointer on a three-field display shows red, blue or matrix writing.
