## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Make a machine that impresses characters one by one into wood-fibre matrices for stereotype casting, with the die wheel turning continuously.

**Solution:** A continuously rotating cylindrical die wheel whose radial dies are struck outward by an intermittently clutched eccentric, with a swinging matrix carrier locked to the wheel during the impression.

**How it works:** Type-dies slide in radial openings in a cylindrical wheel, one or more fonts as rows around it, and the hub is keyed to a hollow continuously driven shaft. A lever and collar slide the hub along the shaft to change font. An eccentric inside the wheel, on its own shaft driven faster through a friction clutch, forces a slide and plunger onto the head of the die at the proper position. Striking a key-lever closes the clutch for one revolution of the eccentric, then releases it. The swinging matrix carrier is coupled to the die wheel during the impression, then released and returned.

**Features:** [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [ELEM:hub-keying](../features/elem-hub-keying.md), [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** die wheel; die; hub; hollow shaft; key; shift lever; collar; eccentric; slide; plunger; friction clutch; key-lever; matrix carrier; matrix-supporting frame

**Improves on:**
