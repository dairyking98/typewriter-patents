## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasures must be made quickly on paper already in the machine, and existing machines have no convenient erasing device.

**Solution:** A bearer bar under the carriage carries a pivoted eraser lever with a socket for a rubber eraser, swung by a handle to erase at the print point.
