## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike type actions put side strain on the parts and give uneven leverage and dip across the keyboard.

**Solution:** A type action is designed so the typebars swing to the platen with uniform leverage and dip, avoiding lateral strain.
