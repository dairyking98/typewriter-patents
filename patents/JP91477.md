## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A machine is wanted that, driven by a motor or treadle, finds and prints any character at a key press at fixed spacing, and can fill several identical lines at once (e.g. tax notices).

**Solution:** A continuously spinning type disc is stopped by a key-operated stop dropping into a hole of a selector cylinder on the same shaft, then a clutch-started one-turn shaft lowers the disc shaft to print and advances the paper box.

**How it works:** A motor or treadle turns a driving shaft that spins the disc shaft (type disc plus selector cylinder) through friction wheels. Pressing a key drops its stop into the hole in the selector cylinder that lies 180 degrees from the matching type, halting the disc with that type facing the paper. The same key motion lifts a pin from a disc and meshes a part-toothed gear, giving the intermittent shaft one revolution. Cranks and links lower and raise the sliding disc-shaft bearings once, separating the friction wheels and pressing the stopped type onto the paper; a guide pin enters a small hole to keep the descent true. Cams on the same shaft then press a pinion against a saw-tooth wheel and step it with a feed pawl, moving the paper box one fixed pitch. Ink rollers from a pot ink the rim, and a knob engages a friction drive to return the box by power.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Power-driven rotary printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a printing machine. A driven shaft is turned, through a friction drive and other suitable drives, from a driving shaft turned by a suitable prime mover such as an electric motor or a treadle; it is carried in bearings that can move up and down, and on it is mounted a disc holding many type characters round its edge. Keys, each with an indicator button corresponding to one of the characters, are pivoted side by side separately, and a character-selecting device connects the disc shaft with the keys. When any indicator button is pressed, the rotation of the disc shaft is stopped at the moment the corresponding character faces the printing paper. At the same time, an intermittent drive, started from each key, turns an intermittent driven shaft from the driving shaft; this shaft stops by itself after every revolution, and is connected to the bearings of the disc shaft by a suitable mechanism that turns its circular motion into straight reciprocation, so as to move them up and down. A paper-box feed, worked by a cam fixed on the intermittent shaft, is combined with all this…
