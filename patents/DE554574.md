## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink ribbons having arrangements to facilitate threading through a machine

**How it works (reading):** A fork-shaped holder with prongs that reach either side of the ribbon fork, with spring-pressure jaws to grip the ribbon and an optional pressure lever to give it the curved setting, lets one hand feed and remove the ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting or removing a typewriter ribbon in the ribbon fork needs both hands, gets the fingers inked, and tweezers cannot hold the ribbon at the right tension.

**Solution:** A fork-shaped holder with prongs that reach either side of the ribbon fork, with spring-pressure jaws to grip the ribbon and an optional pressure lever to give it the curved setting, lets one hand feed and remove the ribbon.
