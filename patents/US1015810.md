## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**How it works (reading):** A mirror mounted under the platen, on an adjustable arm attached to the typewriter, reflects the paper's lower corner to the operator, with a second mirror to light the underside.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator cannot see where the paper ends near the platen bottom, so the last lines can spring out or are not reproduced on manifold copies.

**Solution:** A mirror mounted under the platen, on an adjustable arm attached to the typewriter, reflects the paper's lower corner to the operator, with a second mirror to light the underside.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
