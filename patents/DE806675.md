## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an automatic switching device for the longitudinal movement of the ribbon on typewriters and calculating machines.

**How it works (reading):** Pawls on a shaft with the ribbon reels: when one reel's torque rises as its ribbon runs out, a pawl pivots and the ribbon direction reverses with little force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeler arms on ribbon windings load the linkage unevenly and are bulky.

**Solution:** Pawls on a shaft with the ribbon reels: when one reel's torque rises as its ribbon runs out, a pawl pivots and the ribbon direction reverses with little force.

**Features:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
