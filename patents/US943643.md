## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar pivots wear and need a low-friction, adjustable bearing.

**Solution:** Give each typebar a ball-bearing pivot on a grooved curved rod, with the outer race formed by two threaded conical rings in the typebar eye whose spacing adjusts the bearing.

**How it works:** The type-bar segment is a curved rod with peripheral grooves that serve as ball races. Each typebar ends in an annular strap eye, internally threaded, split at one point with two lugs and a tightening screw. Two externally threaded rings with bevelled inner faces are screwed into the eye, facing each other to form a race around the balls. Turning the rings with a pin tool through the gap between bars adjusts the play or shifts the bar sideways; tightening the strap screw locks the rings.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type-bar segment; ball race groove; anti-friction ball; type-bar eye; annular strap; threaded conical ring; tightening screw; adjusting tool
