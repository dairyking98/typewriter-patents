## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter ribbon on two spools must reverse its feed direction when one spool is full and the other empty, without the operator doing it.

**Solution:** A roller riding on the winding spool's outer coil slides a plate that moves a spring-loaded wedge past a pin, snapping the plate over and shifting a bar that swaps which spool's feed pawl is engaged.

**How it works:** Two ribbon spools with ratchet wheels sit side by side with a roller between them touching the outer coil of the spool being wound. As the coil grows, the roller and its sliding plate move slowly toward the unwinding spool, while a spring pin riding on a double wedge under the plate steadies the motion. When the wedge apex passes the pin, the pin snaps the plate across so the roller meets the other spool. An arm on the plate strikes a shoulder on a sliding bar, shifting it so that the finger of one pawl drops into the bar's depression and engages its spool, and the other pawl is lifted clear. Both pawls are on a rocker arm that is rocked by the key levers through a bail, so each key press winds the ribbon one or more teeth.

**Parts:** ribbon spool; ratchet wheel; tension spring; thumb nut; roller; sliding plate; double wedge; spring-pressed pin; reversing bar; feed pawl; pawl finger; rocker arm; cross-shaft; bail; key lever
