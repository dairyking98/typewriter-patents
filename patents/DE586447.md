**How it works (reading):** Two adjusting members set the advance and gap switching, so the missing space is divided evenly over spaces with a calculated surcharge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-length balancing on ratchet-fed machines could only spread missing space approximately, since spaces are whole feed units.

**Solution:** Two adjusting members set the advance and gap switching, so the missing space is divided evenly over spaces with a calculated surcharge.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
