## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**How it works (reading):** Move the contact handle away from the setting pins, linking it indirectly via a jointed linkage so contacts can be placed for each user.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electric type stop of the earlier patent was hard to use for war-disabled writers who have limited limb movement.

**Solution:** Move the contact handle away from the setting pins, linking it indirectly via a jointed linkage so contacts can be placed for each user.

The present invention is an embodiment of the invention protected by the additional patent 322607 , according to which an adjustment and contact handle with contacts for the type stop, the inter-word circuit, the backspace key and the line feed is connected to the adjustment pins movable over the character plate .
