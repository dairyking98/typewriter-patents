## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case shift on noiseless typebar machines (Remington Noiseless No. 10 type) moves the heavy carriage, platen or whole type segment.

**Solution:** Shift by moving only the swing axes of one set of the links guiding every typebar, so the bars are swung slightly in their plane to hit one of two paths to the print point.

**How it works:** Typebars are guided on two links and brought to the print point by a toggle that straightens. The pivot of one link set is carried on angle levers mounted on an actuator connected to the shift key, so shifting adjusts all together. Each key lever pair drives one typebar with four types, and shifting selects the upper or lower pair. The shift operates with minimal, constant force independent of the keystroke, and the typebar guide is switched in step with the bars.
