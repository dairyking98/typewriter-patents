## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (computing typewriter display device)

**How it works (reading):** A display segment shows the operation type through an opening and a second segment shows whether the device is off, with simplified control parts and carriage-driven counters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculating and typewriter carriages need complicated linkage to coordinate horizontal and vertical counters.

**Solution:** A display segment shows the operation type through an opening and a second segment shows whether the device is off, with simplified control parts and carriage-driven counters.

- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
