## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in the constructions, combinations, and arrangements herein described and claimed.

**How it works (reading):** Each typebar carries a multiple type-head, and when an ascending head is struck by a descending one it shifts sideways enough to avoid interlocking, while a guide-yoke spring keeps the bar from shifting in normal operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a descending and an ascending type-head interfere, they interlock and jam the typebar mechanism.

**Solution:** Each typebar carries a multiple type-head, and when an ascending head is struck by a descending one it shifts sideways enough to avoid interlocking, while a guide-yoke spring keeps the bar from shifting in normal operation.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md)

**Parts:** typebar; multiple type-head; key mechanism; guide-yoke; spring; platen
