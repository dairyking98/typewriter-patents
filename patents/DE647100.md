## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** An ink roller transfers ink onto an endless belt, and the belt, spread by rollers, transfers it evenly to the ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ink applied directly to the ribbon colours it unevenly and smears the type.

**Solution:** An ink roller transfers ink onto an endless belt, and the belt, spread by rollers, transfers it evenly to the ribbon.
