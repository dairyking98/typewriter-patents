## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** An adjustable carriage stop clamped with a set-screw could not be set accurately to a letter-space without a scale and unscrewing a nut.

**Solution:** Ratchet-toothed bar with a spring-held stop whose lugs drop into the tooth spaces, so it indexes in exact letter-space steps.

**How it works:** A graded stop has steps corresponding to a row of pivoted catch keys; pressing a key makes its catch hit the matching step and halt the carriage. The stop slides on a frame bar that has ratchet teeth, one per letter-space, and lugs on the stop fit the notches. A pin through a slot carries a nut or cap with a spring under it, holding the lugs in the notches while allowing the operator to pull the stop one way and click it into the next notch without unscrewing anything. The ratchet-tooth points face the carriage catch so impact cannot move the stop.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** graded stop; catch key; key fulcrum; return spring; toothed bar; lug; slot; pin; nut; retaining spring

**Improves on:**

- Blickensderfer application Serial No. 410,232: stop set by detent steps rather than a set-screw and scale
