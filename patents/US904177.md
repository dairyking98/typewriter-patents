## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Denominational tabulators need improvement.

**Solution:** An improved denominational tabulator with a frame and column stop bar on the carriage.

**How it works:** The carriage on a Remington-type machine carries a column stop bar with adjustable stops. A tabulator frame, controlled by denomination keys, works with carriage release devices.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** column stop bar; column stop; tabulator frame; carriage; feed rack

**Improves on:**

- Woodward application 297026: improvement
