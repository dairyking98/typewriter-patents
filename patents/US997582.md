## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil machine feeding mechanism) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to improve the general construction of stencil machines and particularly to improve the feeding mechanism by which the cardboard or other material from which the stencil is formed is controlled.

**How it works (reading):** A rotary member on a central spindle carries the punch stems and dies on a ring, and the feed controls the stencil sheet.
