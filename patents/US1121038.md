## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand-tightened thumb nuts to pick the driven ribbon spool were easy to mishandle, jamming or damaging the ribbon.

**Solution:** Automatic reversal: a transverse bar throws a set of pawls out of one spool and into the other so only one spool is driven.

**How it works:** Front-strike machine with a platen carriage on a folding support. A transverse bar near the platen, pushed from either side, swings the pawl set between the spools, so one spool is driven step by step from the escapement action at each key depression, and the other turns free. The reversing parts fold with the platen.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; pawl; shift bar; lever; rod; key lever; type bar; platen carriage

**Improves on:**

- Petermann's earlier thumb-nut ribbon drive: pawl shift replaces thumb nuts
