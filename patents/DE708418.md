In such cases, only the account card is printed and the process in the account book later, when it is on the spot, added. It is well known that when such a booking is made “without a book”, the machine is used only set to the printing of the account card and the one for printing of the account book to suppress certain printing. The opposite is true for the Subsequent entry of the process in the account book, the machine only on printing of the account book is set and the printing process for the account card is suppressed. The previous procedure is not recognizable from the account card, whether a process contained in it, which as an entry "without a book" by some Character is specially marked, already entered in the associated account book is or not.
