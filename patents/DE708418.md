## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing device, booking machines)

**How it works (reading):** A supplementary entry prints the amount on the card with a special mark in the same line to show the entry was added later.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Subsequent entries to an account card made without an account book were indistinguishable from entries already in the book.

**Solution:** A supplementary entry prints the amount on the card with a special mark in the same line to show the entry was added later.

**How it works:** Dual printing points for account book and account card; a supplementary-entry marker is printed on the card so later additions are recognisable.
