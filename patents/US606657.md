## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars made of loose type need to be justified and held together as a solid bar; an earlier machine made swage-locked bars.

**Solution:** A machine that justifies a line of short-bodied type on a reusable hard slug and swages the metal so the line locks into a solid type-bar.

**How it works:** A line of short-bodied type is arranged on a hard body slug (preferably aluminium) in a receiving channel with a gage or composing stick. Justifying levers carry rods and wedges that are thrust between the types to justify the line, steered by steady-arms on rocking shafts. A swaging bar or hammer, worked by a cam-driven lever, then swages the softer type metal onto the slug. The wedges are withdrawn by cams and the finished bar is released, while the slug can be reused by removing the used type.

**Features:** [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [ELEM:metal-type](../features/elem-metal-type.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md)

**Parts:** body slug; short-bodied type; justifying lever; justifying wedge; swaging hammer; steady-arm; cam; receiver channel

**Improves on:**

- Gally earlier swage-locked type-bar machine: builds the bar from a reusable hard slug plus short type, with independent justifying wedges
