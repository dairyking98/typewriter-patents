## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Multiple web-feeding apparatus for webs superimposed during printing; Manifolding or like arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the further development of a duplicating device for typewriters, which are intended in particular for writing on form tapes of unlimited length, according to patent 574 082.

**How it works (reading):** The writing abutment is a separate part moved independently of the switching and guiding device, so the platen is withdrawn before each line feed to loosen the sheet pack, then the sheets are advanced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On duplicating typewriters writing sheets slip against each other when sheets are switched, and auxiliary ribbons get dragged out of the writing zone by the tension of the sheets.

**Solution:** The writing abutment is a separate part moved independently of the switching and guiding device, so the platen is withdrawn before each line feed to loosen the sheet pack, then the sheets are advanced.

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
