## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools should let the ribbon be connected and disconnected easily, and the loose spool needs a brake to take up slack.

**Solution:** An improved ribbon spool that the ribbon connects to readily, with spring finger brakes bearing on the spool shafts to take up slack automatically.

**How it works:** Applied to the Fay front-strike typewriter, which strikes the front of the platen below centre and holds the ribbon inclined so the print stays visible without a ribbon shifter. Two vertical spools sit on horizontal shafts below the top plate, driven by bevel gears from a longitudinally sliding main shaft, taken from the carriage drum. Sliding the shaft engages one pinion or the other and so reverses the ribbon direction, with a handle for hand winding. The ribbon passes over guide rollers and U-shaped clips twist it into a plane parallel to the tangent at the print point. Collars on studs carry spring metal fingers that rest on the spool shafts as brakes.
