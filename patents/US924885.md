## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A book typewriter needs to write on a bound record that lies flat on a fixed platen.

**Solution:** A stationary flat platen carries a transversely moving carriage on a line-space frame that moves toward and from the operator, driven by rack bars and pinions.

**Features:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)
