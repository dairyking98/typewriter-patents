## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Fisher book-typewriter the crescent type-bar ring vibrated transverse to the line of writing at speed, spoiling alignment, and the book table needed a good way to hold the tracks.

**Solution:** A guide bar in front of the ring horns slides with the type-bar supporting ring to stop forward-and-rearward vibration, plus an adjustable book-supporting table.

**How it works:** The machine rides on parallel tracks resting on the page; pinions on a spindle engage racks on the tracks to line-space the frame by a ratchet and lever. A carriage moves along the line of writing on guides and carries a crescent type-bar ring with bearing clips for type-bars worked by keys and draw rods. A guide bar parallel to the line of writing, in front of the ring horns, has a sliding connection with the ring, so the straps supporting the ring cannot yield or twist. A table with adjustable supporting strips and a hinged platen holds the book.
