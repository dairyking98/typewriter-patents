## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operating space bar and shift by hand slows the typist.

**Solution:** Foot-operated space bar and shift key attachment, quick to attach.

**How it works:** Foot pedals connect through cords or levers to the space bar and shift key, attachable without holes in the table.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** foot pedal; space bar; shift key
