## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition

**How it works (reading):** Place the ten most frequent characters on keys in the two lowest rows in a thumb-inclusive basic hand position, with the space bar replaced by a vertical bracket.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ten-finger touch typing is hard to learn and fingers still travel needlessly; thumbs have not been used effectively.

**Solution:** Place the ten most frequent characters on keys in the two lowest rows in a thumb-inclusive basic hand position, with the space bar replaced by a vertical bracket.

**How it works:** The ten most frequent characters, which account for 73.8 percent of German keystrokes, are assigned to two keys in the bottom row next to the center line for the thumbs and four keys on each side in the row above for the other fingers. A vertical bracket between the hands acts as space key, and the shift lever is moved to the extreme edge of the palm and doubles as a rest. Other characters need finger bending or index sideways reach.

**Numbers:** ten most frequent characters; 73.8 percent of strokes; 59 million letters counted
