## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Disks, cogs and serrated edges make the machine costly; straight work and fewer parts are wanted.

**Solution:** A matrix machine with a die-carrier that moves longitudinally and has channels, each holding a row of separate dies; the key lever shifts the carrier transversely to the impression point.

**How it works:** Dies sit in rows in channels of a straight carrier; a key lever shifts the row across and the carriage feeds the matrix for each impression.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md)
- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
