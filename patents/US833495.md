## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A two-colour ribbon needs a front lever to select the colour and an indication of which is in use.

**Solution:** A lever-operated ribbon shifter moves the ribbon sideways to bring a colour section to the type, with an indicator.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 6 the ribbon spools and guide sit in a shifting frame moved by a front shift lever in a sleeve bearing, and a locking spring holds the frame. Lateral feed within one colour section happens automatically; a modification is also shown.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spool; carrier; shift lever; shifting frame; locking spring

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 6: adds front-lever colour change
