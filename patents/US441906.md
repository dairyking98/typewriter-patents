## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to devise an automatic type-cleaning brush suitable for any form of type-writing machine having a type well or basket, which may be cheaply manufactured, and which will, owing to its simplicity, be impossible to get out of order;

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type need automatic cleaning on a machine without a complicated mechanism.

**Solution:** A cross-bar clamped to the base carries a threaded standard with a cleaning brush centred in the basket, above the rest position of the type.

**How it works:** Height-adjustable central brush over the type basket, clamped to the base.
