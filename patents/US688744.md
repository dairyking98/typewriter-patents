## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Many type-bars must be pivoted in a very small compass, and they must be rigidly supported against lateral vibration while swinging freely.

**Solution:** A type-bar with a slit, adjustable split hub carrying a V-groove for anti-friction balls, pivoted on an arc-shaped segment with V-grooves, so the bar is rigid laterally and swings freely.

**How it works:** The hub is divided edgewise and drawn together by transverse screws to adjust the bearing on the balls; the segment is cut with V-grooves to take the bars.
