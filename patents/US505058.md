## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Clock or volute spring carriage drives pull strongly at the start of the line and weakly at the end, and resist the return strongly.

**Solution:** A torsion spring on a shaft parallel to the carriage, wound by an adjustable ratchet and gear, pulls the carriage through a chain, drum and strap so the force stays nearly even.

**How it works:** A spiral spring surrounds a horizontal shaft, one end fixed to the shaft and the other to a loose sleeve that can be locked to set the preload. A pinion on the sleeve, a gear and a ratchet with a two-armed pawl allow winding to any degree or reduced force. An arm on the shaft pulls a chain wound on a drum that is one with a larger disk, and a strap from the disk is fixed to the carriage part. The carriage therefore advances in the steps allowed by the escapement and is returned against the spring. A device for the crosswise movement of the ribbon is also described.
