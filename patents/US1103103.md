## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The standard line spacing offers only single, double and triple spaces, and the sheet cannot be realigned precisely.

**Solution:** An oscillatory collar and gripping roller attached to the platen grip it at the required instant, giving any line spacing using the machine's own spacing device.
