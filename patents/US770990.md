## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cable or belt constructions for driving print, type or paper-carriages, e.g. attachment, tensioning means

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to that group of parts known in the art as carriagepropelling mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage-propelling mechanism for a flat-platen book typewriter in which the machine frame, carrying the line-spacing and propulsion parts, moves longitudinally under the carriage; the detailed construction is not legible.
