## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters with keyboards have many delicate connections that break or go out of order and are costly to build.

**Solution:** A machine built without the usual keyboard: a carriage on toothed tracks is moved by a rope or chain wound on a windlass, operated by a lever with a pawl and ratchet, so that it has few delicate parts.
