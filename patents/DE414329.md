## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon feed on typewriters and calculating machines, which is driven by the universal rail moved by the type lever.

**How it works (reading):** The universal rail has unbuttoned legs that drive angle levers carrying switching pawls, which turn the ribbon reels, and separate fixed pawls on the frame lock the reels against reverse rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed linkages for typewriters and calculators use many limbs.

**Solution:** The universal rail has unbuttoned legs that drive angle levers carrying switching pawls, which turn the ribbon reels, and separate fixed pawls on the frame lock the reels against reverse rotation.

**How it works:** Each type lever's nose pushes the universal rail, the angle levers swing and their pawls advance the reel teeth, and a spring links the pawls.
