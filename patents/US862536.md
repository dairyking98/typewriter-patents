## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Line spacing on a flat platen machine needs a feed linkage that works with a printing carriage moving over a supplemental line-space frame.

**Solution:** A line-space ratchet and feed pinion with pawl, on a frame that tilts the printing mechanism up to expose the platen surface.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
