## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing devices with stop pins on a rotatable adjustment axis give only whole multiples of the ratchet division; half or other intermediate line spaces cannot be had.

**Solution:** The adjustment axis is locked against axial movement in normal position and, once released, can be shifted by one ratchet division so each stop pin lengthens or shortens the line feed by that distance.

**How it works:** A switching slide moves the line pawl against the line ratchet wheel on the platen; stop pins on a rotatable adjustment axis limit the pawl travel to one, two or three line spacings, with one pin in the pawl path at a time. A spring-loaded ball in notches secures the set spacing. A spring lever drops into a ring groove on the axis to hold it retracted for normal spacings; lifting it lets the axis be moved one division so every feed gains one extra tooth. A locking roller on a spring-loaded lever holds the ratchet wheel.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; line ratchet wheel; switching slide; line pawl; adjustment axis; stop pin; locking lever; detent ball; locking roller

**Numbers:** single line spacing = 2 ratchet divisions; with extra shift = 3 divisions

**Improves on:**

- line switches with rotatable stop-pin adjustment axis: adds half-step intermediate feeds by axial shift
