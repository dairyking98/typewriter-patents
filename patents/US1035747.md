## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Hammond typewriter the old shuttle-shield frame was hard to disengage, and unequal movement of its two sides bent and damaged the shuttle-shield when it was replaced.

**Solution:** A shuttle-shield frame pivoted in a cut-away bracket that can slide sideways, then swing up and rest on a shoulder to stay raised, with a sliding locking lug to hold it down while typing.

**How it works:** The frame embraces the anvil and holds the shield between the hammer and the Hammond shuttle, and is pivoted in a bracket on the front top of the frame. To open it, a locking lug in a slot is slid aside on its link, the frame is shifted sideways clear of the ribbon-lift lugs, and lifted up until its straight part rests on a shoulder cut in the bearing sleeve. In this raised position ribbons can be changed, shields cleaned or broken shields unhooked from the frame's bent ends. Moving the frame back sideways lets the locking lug move it into operative position and hold it there.
