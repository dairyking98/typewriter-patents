## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Using two ribbon colours at will normally means changing ribbons.

**Solution:** A second, special-colour ribbon strip is stretched on the carriage in front of the platen and has its own vibrator, so either ribbon can be raised to cover the printing point.

**How it works:** On an Underwood front-strike machine the regular ribbon runs on spools with vertical axes either side of the print point. A red strip is hooked at its ends to the carriage, lying on edge below the printing line and yieldingly supported so it can vibrate up. Each ribbon has its own carrier or vibrator moved by levers. The actuators can be held so neither ribbon rises, useful for cutting stencils.
