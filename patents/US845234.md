## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide means for producing a vertical line or lines and also horizontal lines which can be applied to the office machines now in extensive use and rule the paper as well as print upon it, and thus make the machine useful for a large and extensive class of work from which it is now debarred.

**How it works (reading):** Attaches a ruling disk to an arm that makes vertical lines and a telescopic arm with a disk that makes horizontal lines, so the machine rules paper as it prints.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Office typewriters cannot rule vertical and horizontal lines, so ruled work is excluded from them.

**Solution:** Attaches a ruling disk to an arm that makes vertical lines and a telescopic arm with a disk that makes horizontal lines, so the machine rules paper as it prints.

**How it works:** A disk on an arm draws vertical lines past the paper; a telescopic arm carries a second disk for horizontal rules; a cylindrical bar guides the paper.
