## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing machine with carriage advance) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** Tab key levers carry rollers that run on the guide rail fixed to the counter pawl, and carry the stops that halt the carriage at the wanted position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known tabulator stops on embossing machines are either too complex or too weak to withstand the impact of the embossing.

**Solution:** Tab key levers carry rollers that run on the guide rail fixed to the counter pawl, and carry the stops that halt the carriage at the wanted position.

**How it works:** The tab key lever moves the guide rail with its rollers; the carrier stops against the stops on the lever.
