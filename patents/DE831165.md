## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to means for inking of both monochrome and multicolor ribbons during their use.

**How it works (reading):** A removable dye container sits under the inking roller, and a porous wick carries the dye from the container to the roller by capillary action; the roller inks the ribbon only while the ribbon is being fed, and a pressure roller holds the ribbon against the inker.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter pad inkers are often too dry or too damp because the dye flow is poorly controlled.

**Solution:** A removable dye container sits under the inking roller, and a porous wick carries the dye from the container to the roller by capillary action; the roller inks the ribbon only while the ribbon is being fed, and a pressure roller holds the ribbon against the inker.
