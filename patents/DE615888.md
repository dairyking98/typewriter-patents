## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mounting or connecting key buttons on or to key levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a pair of pliers for old pulling and putting on the Typewriter key rings or d-1.

**How it works (reading):** Both plungers sit in one pair of pliers with separate controls, both driven from the handle, so one tool does both jobs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Separate pliers were needed for pulling and fitting typewriter key rings because one control could only drive one stamp.

**Solution:** Both plungers sit in one pair of pliers with separate controls, both driven from the handle, so one tool does both jobs.
