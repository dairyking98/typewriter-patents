## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Center-strike typebar rings must be large to hold enough bars, and bar alignment is hard to adjust.

**Solution:** Mount type-bars in banks of two or three on separate diverging rock-shafts in light hangers whose bearing points can be bent to adjust alignment.

**How it works:** A hanger of two angular sections with slotways forming points carries rock-shafts, each with a type-bar and rearward crank arm. Slotways are narrower on one side so upper and lower shafts diverge, making their bars strike the common center from slightly different directions and clear each other. Bending or twisting a point adjusts the shaft level and tension.
