## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Mechanical type actuation levers are replaced by a pneumatic system.

**Solution:** Each type sits on a light rod in a tubular holder, driven up against the paper by air from a squeezable key chamber through a tube, with a movable character band over the keys for cipher use.

**How it works:** Tubular type holders, all directed at one point on the platen roller, hold light rods with a type at the outer end, resting on stop pins. Each holder's lower end connects by flexible tube to a compressible air chamber, arranged like keys. Pressing a chamber drives the rod up and prints, and it returns by gravity and the chamber's elasticity. The chambers sit on a vibrating plate that pulls a toothed rod by cord to step the paper carrier through a pawl. A flexible lettered strip wound between two rollers can be shifted so keys print other letters, making a cipher writer, and an oscillating ink pad inks the types.
