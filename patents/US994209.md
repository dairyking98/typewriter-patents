## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulator mechanism for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulator mechanism combined with a selector chooses any one of several columns defined by the adjustment of the column stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Denominational tabulator keys must select among columns set by column stops.

**Solution:** Tabulator mechanism combined with a selector chooses any one of several columns defined by the adjustment of the column stops.
