## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Parallel guide levers for the platen are hard to adjust precisely.

**Solution:** Each parallel lever is two stamped parts joined by a claw; a connecting screw with a flat in wedge-shaped recesses changes the effective length, then a nut locks it.
