## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for attaching machines to baseboards

**How it works (reading):** Two cups under the feet are joined by rails, and sliding brackets clamp the front edge of the table, so the typewriter can be set up on another desk without damage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter cannot be held to a desk top without screwing wells into the wood, which fits only one desk.

**Solution:** Two cups under the feet are joined by rails, and sliding brackets clamp the front edge of the table, so the typewriter can be set up on another desk without damage.

**Features:** [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)
