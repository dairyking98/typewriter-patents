## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A line lock that blocks the keys at a fixed place stops the operator mid-word and gives no warning that the line end is near.

**Solution:** A margin stop on the carriage arms a lock bar that only drops onto the keys when the space key is next pressed, so the line locks at the end of a word, and a spring stiffens the space key as a warning.

**How it works:** When the carriage margin stop reaches a crank arm, a linked bar and bell crank lower a vertical rod that had been holding the lock bar up. The lock bar falls toward the key levers but is held off by a notched lug on the space-key lever, so letter keys still print until the word ends. Pressing the space key moves the notch out of the way and the lock bar drops onto the lugs of all key levers, locking them. After the stop is reached, a spring also engages the space-key linkage so the key feels sluggish as a warning, and it could ring a bell. A release key lifts the rod to free the keys, and a catch can hold it down for several extra words.
