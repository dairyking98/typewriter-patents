## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** The pawl sits in the path of one arm of a two-armed lever whose other arm carries a gear on the carriage rack; a brake spring raises the lever's bearing friction so it swings out at the first slight return movement and lifts the pawl.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage return was noisy because the ratchet pawl stayed engaged until the carriage had already travelled a long way.

**Solution:** The pawl sits in the path of one arm of a two-armed lever whose other arm carries a gear on the carriage rack; a brake spring raises the lever's bearing friction so it swings out at the first slight return movement and lifts the pawl.
