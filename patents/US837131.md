## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the ribbon-feeding mechanism oftype-writing machines, more especially of that class of machines wherein the ribbon has connections with the means for driving or impelling the carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Disconnect or control ribbon feed during carriage release and tabulator runs.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Where the ribbon is driven by the carriage spring drum, carriage jumps feed unused ribbon and may disturb reversal.

**Solution:** Disconnect or control ribbon feed during carriage release and tabulator runs.

**How it works:** Only the opening: when the carriage is released or tabbed, the spring drum pulls it and feeds the ribbon with no printing, causing patchy print and possible tearing at reverse.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spring drum; carriage; release key
