> Description Translated from German Device for moving the type levers or type bars of typewriters The invention relates to a device for moving the type levers or type bars of typewriters, in which the _ type carrier braked before the stop and be pressed against the paper roller by a flywheel. In the known devices of this type, the impression is by means of caused by a toggle lever, which is brought into the extended position by the flywheel and thereby causes the imprint. This arrangement requires one in the knee joint larger number of points of articulation, which are subject to severe wear, so that the extended position of the knee lever is no longer sufficient after a certain operating time, to ensure a perfect, even impression. According to the invention, this disadvantage is thereby eliminated. avoided the Type levers or rods are resiliently connected to their drive linkage and after stopping the drive linkage by a push rod that can be moved in a straight line with spear-like effect trained flywheel pressed against the paper roller will.
