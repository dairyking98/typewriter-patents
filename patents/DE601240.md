## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Knee-lever linkage to the type levers wears at its joints and loses the even impression.

**Solution:** Type levers are resiliently linked to the drive and a push rod drives a flywheel-like mass straight against the paper roller after the drive stops, so inertia gives the impression.

In the known devices of this type, the impression is by means of caused by a toggle lever, which is brought into the extended position by the flywheel and thereby causes the imprint. This arrangement requires one in the knee joint larger number of points of articulation, which are subject to severe wear, so that the extended position of the knee lever is no longer sufficient after a certain operating time, to ensure a perfect, even impression. According to the invention, this disadvantage is thereby eliminated. avoided the Type levers or rods are resiliently connected to their drive linkage and after stopping the drive linkage by a push rod that can be moved in a straight line with spear-like effect trained flywheel pressed against the paper roller will.
