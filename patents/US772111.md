## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holders for erasers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to furnish a device to place an eraser (such as are employed in connection with type-writing) in a position for instant use all the time.

**How it works (reading):** A metal rod with a loop and clamp holds an elastic-corded eraser in reach on a table or typewriter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Erasers get lost on a desk, so they need a fixed holder.

**Solution:** A metal rod with a loop and clamp holds an elastic-corded eraser in reach on a table or typewriter.
