## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting bed-strip) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes; Anvil

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is the production of a bed-strip fulfilling the requirements above noted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Punch cuts in a paper bed strip crush fibres and leave ragged stencil edges.

**Solution:** The bed strip is paper coated on both sides and filled with fine hard particles that support the fibres, so the punch cuts cleanly.
