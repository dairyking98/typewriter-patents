## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine keypad transfer)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a further embodiment of the calculating machine according to patent 378167, the special feature of which is that between the (possibly laterally movable) keypad and the rest of the setting mechanism of the machine (which can be of any design) special Devices are stored, which transmit the numbers set in the keypad to the rest of the setting mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** One bar runs under each key row; a rod with a roller rests on the bar and its travel is limited by the depressed key, so the drive stroke transfers the setting by rods and eccentrics.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The rail and inclined-slot transfer from keypad to setting mechanism is complicated, so a simpler way to move keyed numbers to the setting gear is needed.

**Solution:** One bar runs under each key row; a rod with a roller rests on the bar and its travel is limited by the depressed key, so the drive stroke transfers the setting by rods and eccentrics.
