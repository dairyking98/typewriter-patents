## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A hand perforating stamp needs independently movable punches to be selected, and the matrix must be turned to the right face and pressed with enough leverage by one hand.

**Solution:** A revoluble matrix on a pivoted lever lets one hand turn the matrix to the required face and apply leverage to perforate the check.
