## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching between two positions of a type segment needs a simple device that also keeps the platen registered in each position.

**Solution:** Two reversible shift levers, one for numbers and one for letters, release a pawl that holds the type segment in its normal position, and a movable stop limits the segment travel in one of the two positions.

**How it works:** The number lever pushes the locking pawl aside and an arm takes hold of the segment attachment, while a spring pulls the segment against a stop.
