## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier devices moved the ribbon sideways in irregular zigzag lines, wasting ribbon surface.

**Solution:** A spring and cam move the ribbon carrier across the ribbon in regular back-and-forth lines, with a one-step lengthwise wind at the end of each sweep.

**How it works:** A rocker arm with two opposed pawls steps a ratchet and cam shaft as keys are struck. A slide on the ribbon carrier rides the cam, and a spring pulls it toward the front, so the ribbon moves across forward and back. At the end of the rear stroke a pinion on the spool meshes with a pinion on the cam shaft, and the next stroke winds the ribbon one lengthwise step. A switch bar with forks selects the pinion at either end so the ribbon can run in either direction. A hand crank is also provided, and the scheme fits Caligraph and Remington carriages.
