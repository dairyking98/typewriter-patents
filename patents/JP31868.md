## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter parts and assembly cost too much, and parts are hard to adjust and repair.

**Solution:** Build the machine as two sub-assemblies, a base carrying the actuating parts and a top plate carrying the working parts, joined by simple connecting links.

**How it works:** The frame is a base and top plate fixed on corner posts; with the top plate upside down all parts are easy to fit before it is turned over and connected. Key levers on the base link by pin-and-slot to levers and typebars pivoting on a wire seated in a groove in the typebar segment. The carriage runs on rails on the top plate, pulled by a spring motor, and a sub-carriage lifted by a shift rail shifts the platen, with the line indicator and ribbon vibrator moving with it. An escapement with a rack steps the carriage at each typebar or space bar, and the machine also has a tabulator, line lock, backspace, automatic ribbon reverse and line feed.
