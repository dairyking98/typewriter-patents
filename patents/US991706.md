## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the pressure rolls, which run upon the cylindrical platens of typewriting machines, for feeding the paper around the platen.

**How it works (reading):** The pressure rolls are geared together so the rear roll is driven by the forward roll and both feed the card.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Thick cards or packs of sheets separate the pressure roll from the platen and are not carried.

**Solution:** The pressure rolls are geared together so the rear roll is driven by the forward roll and both feed the card.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
