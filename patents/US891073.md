## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cutting sheets from a paper web on a typewriter needed an inexpensive attachment that fits existing machines.

**Solution:** A cutting blade on two slotted brackets fixed to the platen frame ends, each end adjustable toward or away from the platen.

**How it works:** A bracket on each platen frame end carries a rigid arm reaching down in front of the platen, and a hardened blade is riveted across the two arms, inclined to the platen. Slots and screws let each bracket slide back and forth independently so the gap suits thin or thick paper. Paper from a roll passes round the platen and up between platen and blade, and the blade is set above the type strike line.
