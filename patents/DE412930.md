## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**How it works (reading):** The guide slots for the accent levers are not radial to the segment centre but cut nearer the segment circumference, and the accent levers are bent outside their guide to clear the other levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Accent type levers, which sit offset by one letter width, are hard to fit into the type segment.

**Solution:** The guide slots for the accent levers are not radial to the segment centre but cut nearer the segment circumference, and the accent levers are bent outside their guide to clear the other levers.

**How it works:** The non-radial slots at the segment ends let the accent levers pass each other and swing in their own plane.
