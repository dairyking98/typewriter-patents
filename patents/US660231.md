## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Accent marks must be written without the carriage advancing so the accent lands over the letter.

**Solution:** A bolt-on blank key attachment whose spring-loaded locking dog stops the escapement feed when the key is pressed, so the accent prints without spacing.

**How it works:** On an Underwood-type machine, each type-bar nose pushes a universal bar that moves a feed table with dogs against the carriage rack. A carrier clamped to the frame beside a chosen key lever pivots a locking dog held up by a spring, with a rod from the key lever through it and an adjustable spring. Pressing this blank key engages the locking dog with the feed mechanism, so the bar can strike but the carriage is not fed. The remaining action is not in the opening text.
