## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inventor wants a simple, inexpensive, easily handled typewriter that does not get deranged in long use.

**Solution:** A rotating type drum with spring-loaded radially movable types, turned by a handle knob over an indexed dial, printing onto a platen carriage that is moved to the drum.

**How it works:** A type drum on a horizontal shaft has a rim with an upper-case set around one side and a lower-case set around the other, with capital and lower-case of a letter paired along a line parallel to the axis. Each type is a head on a spindle with a foot, held inward by a spring so it can be pushed radially. A vertical shaft turns the drum through bevel gears; a knob on top, splined to the shaft, is turned until an index pointer sits at the wanted character on a dial that mirrors the drum order. Pressing the knob down forces the dial and drives the print action. The platen is a small carriage sliding on ways at right angles to the bed carriage, so it provides letter and line spacing.
