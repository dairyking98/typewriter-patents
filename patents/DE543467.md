## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stretching a printing pad to make letterpress-like lines distorts the letters when stretched far.

**Solution:** A non-stretchable strip is cut and folded in the word spaces so it stretches only there; the stretched strip is clamped between moulds and cast with liquid metal into a finished line.

**How it works:** Bridges between cuts are folded; clamping moulds hold the folded shape, which is poured with molten printing metal to make a line.
