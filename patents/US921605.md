## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing on carbon sheets needs blank spaces between entries, and the earlier crank-driven platen turn was not simple enough.

**Solution:** The crank carries a dog that skips the first teeth of a notched wheel on the platen on the forward stroke and then engages it on the return, so the platen turns back a short distance and forward a longer one.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
