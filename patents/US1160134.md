## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to type bars for typewriting machines and its principal object is to provide an improved type bar of an exceptionally cheap and simple construction.

**How it works (reading):** A type bar with a sheet-metal shank and a head made of a separate U-shaped piece welded on, giving a solid three-ply head that holds a printing type block.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars are expensive to make because the head must be built up from several pieces.

**Solution:** A type bar with a sheet-metal shank and a head made of a separate U-shaped piece welded on, giving a solid three-ply head that holds a printing type block.
