## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed rolls and retaining guide on a cylinder-platen carriage are hard to handle and adjust.

**Solution:** A paper retaining guide can be rocked away from the platen to load paper and returns automatically on carriage traverse; feed-roll tension is adjustable.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
