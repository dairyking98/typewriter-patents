## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage moved vertically for case shift must still letter-space in every position, and the machine must fold light.

**Solution:** A feed mechanism with part moving vertically with the carriage and linked to the key levers, so the spring-drum escapement works at every shift position.

**How it works:** Aluminium or sheet-metal frame; typebars with three characters each. The carriage runs on a rear rail in a slot and a front rail on rolling buttons. A spring in a drum under the carriage bed pulls it through a cord fixed to an adjustable pendant arm. The vertical feed part keeps connection with the key-lever operated escapement.

**Features:** [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** frame; key lever; type bar; carriage; carriage bed; spring drum; cord; pendant arm; felt strip

**Numbers:** three characters per bar
