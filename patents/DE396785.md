## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for automatic tilting and uncoupling of paper trolleys and pull cords for typewriters, which grippers are provided on the paper trolley and on the carriage guide.

**How it works (reading):** A catch pan in a cylindrical ring groove on the cord end engages catch hooks on the carriage guide and paper carriage, so the coupling works regardless of cord twist.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper carriage pull cords can be turned and fail to couple reliably.

**Solution:** A catch pan in a cylindrical ring groove on the cord end engages catch hooks on the carriage guide and paper carriage, so the coupling works regardless of cord twist.
