## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to` matrix-machines;

**How it works (reading):** Fluid-pressure valves operated by keys work the hammer and type-holder, and steady the type-holder while the matrix cylinder steps along the line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type-holder must be steadied during the hammer stroke and matrix alignment must be exact.

**Solution:** Fluid-pressure valves operated by keys work the hammer and type-holder, and steady the type-holder while the matrix cylinder steps along the line.

**Features:** [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
