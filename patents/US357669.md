## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Radial type levers need a convenient inking surface whose ink delivery can be regulated.

**Solution:** An annular felt pad held in a two-part screw-together case, with the cap adjusting how much ink reaches the inking surface.

**How it works:** A felt ring sits in a short cylinder with an inward flange so its inner edge projects past the flange. Types are pressed against the underside of this projecting part and pick up ink. Ink is spread on with a brush or by laying the pad on an ink-covered board. Screwing the threaded cap down or up squeezes the pad and so regulates ink delivery; surplus pressed into the gap around the pad is reabsorbed.

**Features:** [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** felt annulus; case cylinder; inward flange; threaded cap; type lever; crank
