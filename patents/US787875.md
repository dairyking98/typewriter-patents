## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier tape translators needed a special equal-length code and could not read Wheatstone-Morse tape with letters of unequal length.

**Solution:** A tape feeder whose step length varies, set by selecting mechanism reading the perforations, advances the tape letter by letter for printing telegraph messages.

**How it works:** Perforated tape is guided and fed by a feed device driven from a driving mechanism. A selecting mechanism, dependent on the perforations and spaces of each letter, controls how far the feed advances, so each letter plus its separating space is passed in one step. Reciprocating notched combs then control the typewriter or type-setting device. The opening describes only the tape feed; the printing side is cut off.

**Features:** [USE:telegraph-printer](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md), [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)

**Parts:** perforated tape; tape guide; tape feeder; selecting mechanism; comb; feed wheel; driving mechanism

**Improves on:**

- fixed-length-code tape translating machines: handles Wheatstone-Morse letters of variable length
