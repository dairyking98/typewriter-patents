## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tally strip feed on Underwood machines is complicated and needs key release, and it cannot be fitted as an attachment easily.

**Solution:** A simplified tally strip feeding mechanism that line-spaces the strip with the platen and can be released by the usual key, sold as an attachment.
