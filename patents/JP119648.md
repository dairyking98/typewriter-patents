## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms; Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an electric typewriter characterised in that a delay device is connected to the type-tray locating device.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded, catch-held delay cam keeps the tray-locating device engaged until the type has had time to fall back.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a motor-driven machine the tray is unlocked the moment printing ends, before the type has dropped back, so a hasty operator can move the tray and misplace or break the type.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded, catch-held delay cam keeps the tray-locating device engaged until the type has had time to fall back.

**How it works:** Pressing the operating handle slides a toothed piece that engages locating racks on the tray and frame and also starts the motor-driven printing mechanism. Normally the locating device returns when the printing mechanism returns. Here the printing arm's bent end pushes a cam disc against its spring and a hook catches the disc, and the disc holds back the shaft that returns the locator. When the printing arm finishes returning it releases the hook, and the spring slowly turns the cam back, then the locator releases. Cam face length and spring force set the delay.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Electric typewriter Summary of the nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to an electric typewriter characterised in that a delay device is connected to the type-tray locating device. The delay device is swung against the force of a spring by the printing action of the printing mechanism and then held there by a catch; when the printing mechanism's return action ends, the catch is released and the device swings back by the spring. In this way the locating device is kept in action during the time, after the printing mechanism has finished, in which the type drops back of itself into the tray. Its object is an electric typewriter that makes sure the type drops back to its original place and, by making it impossible to move the tray before the type has dropped back, makes operation safe.
