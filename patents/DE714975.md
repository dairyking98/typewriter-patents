## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The aim of the invention is to eliminate the disadvantages of the known devices and to create a vehicle jumping device that works reliably and is suitable for high loads.

**How it works (reading):** Adjusting stops on the stop bar and counter stops are guided in slots with broad faces, spring-latched in both positions, so each impact is spread across the stop surfaces.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage jump stops had small contact faces and were damaged or shifted by impacts at the end of long carriage jumps.

**Solution:** Adjusting stops on the stop bar and counter stops are guided in slots with broad faces, spring-latched in both positions, so each impact is spread across the stop surfaces.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** rail; adjusting stops; counter stops; stop bar; spring latch
