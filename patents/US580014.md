## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple, durable, quiet and cheap typewriter that can write fast, change type styles, print in two colours or cipher, and avoid the noise and complexity of typebars.

**Solution:** A type wheel lying lengthwise on a triangular shaft travels across the machine in front of a fixed, rocking platen; keys turn the shaft to bring the chosen character to the platen, which then tilts into the wheel to print.

**How it works:** The type wheel is a hub (carrier) with a detachable type rim, riding six antifriction rollers on an equilateral triangular shaft that spans the machine, one edge of which is a rack. Each rim has its characters in three segments (lower case, capitals, figures), and each shaft face presents one segment to the platen. A key raises an alignment lever by an amount specific to that key, turning the shaft by up to a third of a turn to bring the character opposite the platen; a roller then drops onto a key plate to lock the wheel there. Further key travel tilts a platen on rockers less than a quarter inch into the wheel, lifting the ribbon between them, and the same stroke moves a ratchet bar to step the wheel one space along the shaft. Cap and Fig keys turn the shaft one third each way by an oblique groove; the line key frees the pawls so a coil spring inside the wheel runs it back to the left in one move. The rims can be swapped, and rims with whole words and affixes print several letters per stroke and space by one more than the word length.

**Parts:** type wheel; carrier hub; detachable type rim; triangular shaft; rack; antifriction roller; coil spring; alignment lever; key plate; L-shaped key lever; platen; rocker; feed roller; ratchet bar; feed arm; wedge; shift key; oblique groove; ribbon reel; ribbon lever; ink receptacle; distributing roller; paper roll; line key; cam

**Numbers:** three segments per type rim; three lugs and three notches per rim; six antifriction rollers; platen travel under 1/4 in.; ribbon 1/4 in. wide; shaft turns up to one third of a revolution; feed steps of 1 to 6 letter-spaces; 5-space key; word printing speeds up writing by more than 25 percent; feed roll 3 in. circumference

**Improves on:**

- typebar typewriters: platen simply vibrates against a wheel, which is quieter and permits roll paper
- shift on typebar machines: the shift key can present any segment, so key and printed character need not match (cipher)
