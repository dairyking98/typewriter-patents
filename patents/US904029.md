## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Denomination tabulator bails nest poorly and bend in use.

**Solution:** Nested bails of graduated size with keys directly on the stop arms, diverging key extensions and stops under the keys.

**How it works:** On an Underwood, denomination keys are on arms carrying stops that engage carriage column stops at letter-space intervals. Two sets of bails are nested, key extensions fan out, stop arms pivot on a common axis, and single arms between the sets save room. Pressing a key sets the stop and releases the carriage.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** denomination key; stop arm; bail; column stop; carriage

**Improves on:**

- [US853280](../patents/US853280.md): simpler nesting of bails
