**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** Spring-loaded upper and lower rollers with a ratchet on the shaft are driven through the machine operation to move the copy along, and can also be turned by hand.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The copy must be moved along as fast as it is typed, and irregular lines must be handled without the typewriter being stopped.

**Solution:** Spring-loaded upper and lower rollers with a ratchet on the shaft are driven through the machine operation to move the copy along, and can also be turned by hand.
