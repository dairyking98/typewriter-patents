## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Prior proportional-spacing stops (several toothed rings on a rotating stop) feed the carriage in jerky multiple steps, slowing wide letters and making noise.

**Solution:** A stop drum with helical strips is turned by a variable angle per key, so the carriage is released over one uninterrupted path of a length matching the letter width.

**How it works:** A comb strip fixed to the carriage has teeth that lie between helical stop strips on a drum at the frame. Each key lever, when struck, hits a cross bar with cut-outs of varying depth per letter, which swings a lever and, via a pawl and ratchet, rotates the drum by an angle set by the letter width. Rotation moves the helical strips so the comb teeth are free to advance a distance proportional to that angle. A locking device holds the carriage until the type stroke ends.

**Features:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** stop drum; helical stop strip; comb strip; key lever; cross bar; switching lever; ratchet wheel; switching pawl; locking device; carriage

**Improves on:**

- earlier rotatable multi-ring stop: one continuous release path instead of three staged jumps, quieter and smoother
