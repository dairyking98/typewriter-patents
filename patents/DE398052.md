> Description Translated from German Control strip conveyor device for printing units of cash registers. The invention relates to such conveyor devices for the control strip from Rügistrierkassen, through which the last print line of the control strip before printing moved back from a viewing opening to the line spacing at the printing point and after printing, the newly printed line is brought under the viewing opening. According to According to the invention, the control strip is from one located on the supply reel Grab bar past the pressure point and the inspection opening to one with the grab bar inevitably connected and coupled with the take-up roll guide bar out and is in each work cycle of the printing unit as a result of the gripping bar and the pendulum motion imparted to the guide bar by the cash register engine thereby by different Moved great distances back and forth that with the first movement of the control strip the coupling between the guide bar and winding roller before the end of the path of the Guide bar is released while on the second movement of the control strip the guide bar for the entire duration of its back oscillation with the take-up roll is coupled.
