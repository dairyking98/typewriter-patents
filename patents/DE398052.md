## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control strip conveyor cash register) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such conveyor devices for the control strip from Rügistrierkassen, through which the last print line of the control strip before printing moved back from a viewing opening to the line spacing at the printing point and after printing, the newly printed line is brought under the viewing opening.

**How it works (reading):** The strip is gripped between two bars and moved past the print point by a pendulum guide bar; the take-up roller is released on the first stroke and coupled on the second so the strip moves in the correct direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The control strip must move back to the print line before printing and forward afterward, and a fixed drive does not give the needed back-and-forth travel.

**Solution:** The strip is gripped between two bars and moved past the print point by a pendulum guide bar; the take-up roller is released on the first stroke and coupled on the second so the strip moves in the correct direction.
