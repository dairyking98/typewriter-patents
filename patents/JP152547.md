## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The vertical (line) feed of the paper cylinder in a Japanese typewriter needs to work from a printing key, a vertical-feed key or a hand lever, reverse, and free the cylinder, all in a durable mechanism.

**Solution:** A spring-driven paper cylinder is stepped by a rocking frame whose pawl and pinion release a set angle of gear rotation per stroke, with a sliding lever for hand feed and reverse and an operating plate that frees the cylinder.

**How it works:** The cylinder is urged by a spring and geared through gears (4), (11) and (12). A key stroke swings a rocking frame (14) so its pawl (17) locks gear (12) while pinion (26) on a spring-loaded rocking plate (25) disengages and the plate swings to a stop rod (34). When the frame returns, pinion (26) re-meshes and the cylinder spring turns the gears until the plate meets the frame, giving a feed of a set distance; a check pawl (27) holds the pinion. A polygon adjusting piece (33) with a knob changes the stop rod position and so the feed distance. A sliding lever (47) can swing the frame for a hand feed or be pushed lengthwise so a tooth (53) turns gear (12) backwards. Pulling a sleeve (6) on the cylinder shaft moves an operating plate (37) that lifts the check pawl and the roller out of the key lever path, freeing the cylinder both ways.

In this invention a gear is fitted to a paper cylinder that is always urged by a spring to turn one way, and another gear is fitted beside it on a gear shaft that meshes with it. On the extension of the gear shaft a pinion that meshes with the said gear is mounted on a rocking lever pivoted on the rocking frame, and the pinion is given a check pawl that allows it to turn in one direction only.
