## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case shift of a segment-shift typebar machine needs adjustable travel limits and a lighter key action.

**Solution:** Independently adjustable clamp stops and a spring counterbalance on the type-bar segment with a lost-motion finger lever and roller lifting linkage.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot on a segment that rises and falls on upright columns with clamping sleeves limiting upper and lower travel. A rod with an adjustable spiral spring and two levers counterbalances the segment weight. A shift key lever carries a roller that lifts a pivoted lever linked to the segment, and a loose strap connection lets the segment's position be adjusted without moving the finger key. A spring wire bracket at the guide throws typebars back after impression.
