## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen must stay in correct relation to the printing plane when extra sheets of paper (carbons) are inserted.

**Solution:** A dial and clamped pointer on the platen tie-rod shaft set the platen offset by the number of sheets inserted.

**How it works:** The platen carriage is held by a spring against an abutment. A shaft from the movable tie-rod carries a toothed collar meshing with a pointer hub, so the pointer can be reset to zero for one sheet and clamped. A front dial calibrated 0 to 20 sheets with stops shows the platen position. The operator moves the platen to match the number of sheets.
