## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools were hard to replace and the ribbon feed and reversal were clumsy.

**Solution:** Easily removable spools, optionally pre-wound with ribbon, with a feed-reversing lever and a ribbon-path shifting lever, driven by pawls on a shiftable plate.

**How it works:** Spools sit by friction on slotted, compressed tubular spindles carried on studs and lift off for replacement. Spool-actuating pawls on a shiftable plate wind the ribbon intermittently from a feed lever. A long and a short lever can each shift the ribbon to a different path, with or without reversing the feed. Opening only.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; tubular spindle; feed lever; reversing lever; pawl; shiftable plate; spool bracket

**Improves on:**

- hand-wound ribbon spools: spools come pre-wound and drop in
