**How it works (reading):** A flexible brake band runs between a fixed and a movable brake shoe, with an adjustable spring between a tab-key part and the movable shoe, which presses the band firmly regardless of slack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The tabulator carriage brake rod must be precisely shaped and fitted, and key pressure changes the braking, so the carriage can stop abruptly.

**Solution:** A flexible brake band runs between a fixed and a movable brake shoe, with an adjustable spring between a tab-key part and the movable shoe, which presses the band firmly regardless of slack.
