## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Changing the line-feed increment of an address plate holder on an embossing machine needed two separate handles for the two locking disks, which was clumsy and error-prone.

**Solution:** One rotating rod with two offset flats lifts one locking lever while the other engages, so a single handle selects the line spacing.

**How it works:** A carriage carries a plate holder driven by racks from a hand-turned shift drum with two gears and two differently divided locking disks. Spring-loaded levers with rollers engage the disks and set the step. A rod with ends ground semicircular and offset 90 degrees lifts one lever out as it turns, forcing the other in. The drum is turned by hand wheel.

**Parts:** shift drum; gear; rack; locking disk; locking lever with roller; selector rod; plate holder; hand wheel

**Numbers:** rod ends offset 90 degrees; two different line increments
