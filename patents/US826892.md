## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting the platen for case needs a light, even touch against the weight of platen and carriage.

**Solution:** Shift levers lift the platen through sublevers and a shifting frame with variable leverage, a band spring counterbalancing the weight and antifriction rollers easing travel.

**How it works:** Pressing a shift lever draws down forked links across sublevers, raising the rear ends and pushing a shifting frame up. This raises a swinging frame, links and the platen until stops halt it, so the platen shaft sits above or below the rear bar. The links bear at two points on the sublevers for greater leverage first. A band spring offsets the platen weight and an antifriction roller runs on a track flange.
