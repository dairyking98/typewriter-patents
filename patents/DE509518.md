## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing material for printing negatives) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** A soft metal such as lead is faced with a thin layer of a higher-melting metal such as brass or copper before embossing, so the matrix stays intact for line casting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lead matrices with a paper or plastic face cannot be cast in lines without damage from hot metal spatter.

**Solution:** A soft metal such as lead is faced with a thin layer of a higher-melting metal such as brass or copper before embossing, so the matrix stays intact for line casting.
