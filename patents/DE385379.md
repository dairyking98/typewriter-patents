## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**How it works (reading):** A segment behind the type levers, moved by the universal rail, pivots an angle lever and sleeve that a detent plate sets; the position is shown in a front window and also controls ribbon switching and a secret lock.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon movement at different heights, switching the ribbon off while writing, releasing the universal rail, and blocking unauthorized use all need separate devices.

**Solution:** A segment behind the type levers, moved by the universal rail, pivots an angle lever and sleeve that a detent plate sets; the position is shown in a front window and also controls ribbon switching and a secret lock.
