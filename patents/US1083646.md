## Summary

**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements intypewriters, and particularly to an attachment for the platen to indicate the number of lines to one revolution of the platen, from which is determined the number of revolutions of the platen to' furnish a predetermined number of lines to be written on the page.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typists writing on a page need to know when the end of the page nears.

**Solution:** A platen-end attachment with a numbered bell that counts line spaces and rings when the chosen line is reached.

**How it works:** A shaft replaces the platen knob and a sleeve carries a graduated bell numbered 1 to 33 which can be turned against spring friction to set the start. A disk with a pointer carries a block with a spring clapper. A finger on the carriage strikes the block and rings the bell at the set line.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** shaft; graduated bell; sleeve; clapper; pointer; finger; coil spring

**Numbers:** 33 lines per platen revolution; 25 lines double space; 48 lines single space
