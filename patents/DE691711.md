## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening devices embodied in machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates a stop device for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Superposed steel sheets with sound-absorbing layers between them increase in rigidity with distance from the lever, so contact resistance grows gradually with contact area.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A rigid stop makes the type lever bounce and the impact is loud because the whole surface hits at once.

**Solution:** Superposed steel sheets with sound-absorbing layers between them increase in rigidity with distance from the lever, so contact resistance grows gradually with contact area.
