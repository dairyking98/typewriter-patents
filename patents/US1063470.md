## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing music by type or by hand is slow and inaccurate, and music copies must be made by lithography to get clean notes.

**Solution:** A typewriter in which a carriage holds a music keyboard of all notes and signs; fixed spacing and a staff-ruling device place each note at a set size and position against the staff lines.

**How it works:** A frame holds a carriage that slides and carries a music keyboard with all note and sign characters. Fixed spacing ensures each note is struck at a precise distance from the last, so notes of the same value and pitch come out at the same size and are aligned with the staff. A staff ruling device marks the staff lines. The paper is supported by a platen and the ribbon is reversible. Ordinary writing keys are also present.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** frame; carriage; music keyboard; platen; staff ruling device; ribbon

**Improves on:**

- music copy by hand or lithography: uniform typed notes aligned to staff lines
