## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Buffer telescoping tubes need extra machining or breaks to set the stroke limit and seat the spring.

**Solution:** The tubes have only one constriction at one point, which acts as the stroke limiter, and the spring is retained by constrictions on the inner tube end.
