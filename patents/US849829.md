## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be line-spaced by hand, which costs the operator attention during the carriage return.

**Solution:** Line-spacing mechanism on the carriage moves the platen automatically during the carriage return, with an adjustable regulating device that sets the extent of the line-space movement.

**How it works:** The carriage return drives linkage and an escapement-shaft pinion that rotates the platen through a set extent, adjusted by a regulator on the carriage end plate.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** platen; line-spacing mechanism; escapement shaft; pinion; links; regulating device; carriage end plates
