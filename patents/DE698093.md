## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on duplicators on typewriters for writing on Paper webs in which the carbon sheets are attached to holders that can slide in guides are attached, that they are by means of the holder after writing on a sheet in the initial position can be withdrawn.

**How it works (reading):** Both ends of the carbon sheets are held by holders that are driven in common with the paper web unwinding roller, so the sheets are guided evenly; the holders are supported on the platen and linked to their carriers by sliding joints.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets on a duplicator are pulled back by friction from the platen, so they can be withdrawn accidentally, and they warp when one side is clamped harder.

**Solution:** Both ends of the carbon sheets are held by holders that are driven in common with the paper web unwinding roller, so the sheets are guided evenly; the holders are supported on the platen and linked to their carriers by sliding joints.

**Features:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
