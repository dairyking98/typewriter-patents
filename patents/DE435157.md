The invention relates to a Power-driven typewriter in which the type lever is continuously revolving Shaft are moved by a spring-loaded drive rail that triggered by the button levers and after coupling with the power drive by this is withdrawn into its tense resting position. The purpose of the invention is, on the one hand, without prejudice to the operation to increase the writing speed and on the other hand the special stop, which peculiar to hand-operated machines. This purpose is achieved according to the invention in that the drive rail during their working stroke by the lever mechanism that retracts the rail is suddenly stopped before the type touches the platen roller on the forward stroke. The type lever is therefore in no way connected during the last part of its stroke with the drive rail and is under the effect of its own mass balance with the type hit the paper roller. In the meantime, the drive rail is back has been brought into a position by the motor to drive the next type lever.
