## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (dust brush for printing paper web) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices for treating the surfaces of sheets, webs, or other articles in connection with printing cleaning devices for sheets or webs

**How it works (reading):** The brushes are mounted at the mouth of the suction line so loosened dust is sucked away at once; brush groups on one or both sides of the web act under suction, and their adjustment is set in plan and end view.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dust and fibre brushed from a moving paper web in a closed housing is whirled around and settles back on the web.

**Solution:** The brushes are mounted at the mouth of the suction line so loosened dust is sucked away at once; brush groups on one or both sides of the web act under suction, and their adjustment is set in plan and end view.

**How it works:** Brushes sit in suction bodies connected to a suction line, so suction air pulls loosened particles off the web as they are released.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for brushing off and vacuuming the dust from both sides a paper web to be printed In printing machines and the like, which have a paper web is continuously fed, it is important to keep the entrained dust. loose fibers etc. to be removed before the web enters the printing unit. It is known to itself to use a brush and the loosened dust through a suction device suck off. The known devices of this type consist of one to one Suction line connected housing, which has slots for the passage of the paper web is provided. The detachment of the dust particles from the paper web takes place inside the housing arranged brushes. These arrangements have the disadvantage that the Dust is not sucked off immediately by the brushes when it is released, but is first whirled around in the housing and thereby again on the paper web starts. The device according to the invention avoids this disadvantage by the brushes are attached to the mouthpiece of the suction line and thus those of Fiber and dust particles detached from the brushes immediately upon their detachment be sucked off.
