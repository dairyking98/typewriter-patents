## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Escapement needs to lighten the finger load, wear less and let the carriage off quickly.

**Solution:** One escapement surface is a roller against whose periphery the other surface bears during release.

**How it works:** On a No. 6 Remington, key levers push the universal bar, rock shaft and links to rock the dog rocker. The dog rocker restoring spring is a C-spring whose pressure is adjusted by a set-screw device. A loose or stepping dog pivots between lugs on the rocker with a tailpiece stop; the roller dog's roller rolls rather than slides on the escapement tooth surface in release. Plan diagrams show the steps (Figs. 5-7).

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** dog rocker; loose dog; roller dog; universal bar; rock shaft; C-spring; escapement wheel

**Improves on:**

- No. 6 Remington escapement: roller replaces sliding contact, lowering key load and wear
