## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** The driving tooth sector is built from two partly toothed discs whose overlap sets the number of teeth to the bill head height; the lever always travels the same distance and the sector frees the paper roller at both end positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A billing device needed a clutch the writer had to engage and disengage, and a fixed tooth sector driving the paper roller hit an adjustable stop with wear.

**Solution:** The driving tooth sector is built from two partly toothed discs whose overlap sets the number of teeth to the bill head height; the lever always travels the same distance and the sector frees the paper roller at both end positions.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** two partly toothed discs; tooth sector; gear on paper roller; lever
