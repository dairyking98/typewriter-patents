## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier air-powered writing machines let air escape to the atmosphere and set the type carrier by a fixed route, wasting power and not always taking the shortest path.

**Solution:** A closed pneumatic or hydraulic pipe system branched by keyboard-operated converters drives turbines that rotate and slide a type cylinder to the shortest path, with locking drop pieces to stop each exactly.

**How it works:** A row-and-column bar keyboard opens valves that send compressed air through converters to two turbines: one turns the type cylinder and one slides it along its axis, each with opposed vane sets and a dead chamber so it moves the nearest way. A spring-loaded drop piece locks each turbine wheel and is released by a plunger when medium arrives, then falls back early to brake and stop it exactly. When both turbines have stopped, plungers open a pipe to a hammer plunger that prints. The hammer stroke also admits air that spaces the carriage by turbine. Further keys give repeat printing, accent keys without spacing, line feed, diagonal printing, and number-key positioning of the carriage with a rough and fine converter, with adding and subtracting of carriage moves.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Writing machine worked automatically by air or water power. Object. This invention relates to a writing machine in which, by the action of the keys, a pressure medium (water or air) is led to the various setting or working devices, so as to carry out the setting of the types, the movement of the paper carriage and the machine's other duties. Its object is to build a writing machine that saves motive power and performs reliable setting, the limitation of the range of movement and its other duties. The prior art and the difference. As one kind of air-power writing machine, machines are already known in which air from a pressure source escapes to the atmosphere through a pipe system that is open, and a certain striking action is performed by momentarily blocking that escaping air. This machine differs from the known machines in that it draws no more pressure medium from its source than is needed. The medium supplied is distributed to the various devices through a pipe system or channels branched into several groups, and a directing device at each branch point is worked from the keyboard.
