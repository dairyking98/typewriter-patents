## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-bars must strike a common center from a flat disk, with room for two ranks.

**Solution:** Annular disk with straight chord-parallel journal grooves on each side of alternate slots, plus two rings of different-length type-bars.

**How it works:** The disk has radial slots and pairs of straight grooves in its upper face; trunnion type-bars sit in them. Long and short bars alternate, pivoted in outer and inner groove series (possibly at different heights), so more bars fit around the same disk. Keys, levers and wires connect them to the bars; platen on an axle driven by a weight and cord.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [How the element is made](../features/elem-element-construction.md)

**Parts:** annular disk; radial slot; journal groove; type-bar; trunnion; key lever; wire; platen; ribbon
