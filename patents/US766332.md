## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar and type impact on the platen make too much noise.

**Solution:** A light platen with a rubber covering on an apertured internal support, with partitions that damp sound and perforations that reduce the vibration.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
