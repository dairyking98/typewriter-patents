## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Clips that hold paper or envelopes on the platen need adjusting for each width, take time, are hard to reach, and the type can strike them if they are left inward on wide paper.

**Solution:** A paper-holding bar across the front of the platen, needing no width adjustment, is thrown into or out of clamping position by thumb levers and also serves as a straight edge for ruling.

**How it works:** The platen is carried in a rigid carriage frame of end plates joined by bars. Spring-loaded feed rollers under the platen are swung away by a cam with a finger piece acting on a stud on the paper guide. A rod across the front of the platen is carried on two fulcrumed levers with thumb pieces, and rollers on the lower lever ends ride over spring fingers that snap behind them to hold the bar against the paper. An apron is held by springs against the lower platen.
