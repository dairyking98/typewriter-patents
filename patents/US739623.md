## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars must pivot smoothly and be packed closely in a limited space, so the bearing joint has to be thin yet accurate.

**Solution:** Each type-bar hub is formed from two offset plates pressed inward to make opposed cones that cooperate as a peripheral V-groove ball bearing, with a short rivet as the pivot, so many thin bars can be guided to the printing center.
