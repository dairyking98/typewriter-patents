## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A ribbon in a typewriter wears along a single narrow track, so only a small part of its width is used before it must be replaced, and the ribbon must also clear the print point so the writing stays visible.

**Solution:** Move the ribbon sideways across its width as the carriage travels, using a zigzag cam on the carriage, and wind it lengthwise only when the space key is pressed.

**How it works:** The ribbon runs between two spools and through a fork pivoted on a rocking lever. A bar above the key levers lifts at every keystroke and, through a link, throws the ribbon fork toward the platen at each impression, then lets it fall away so the print point stays visible. A plate with a zigzag slot is fixed to the carriage and works a bell-crank lever on the fork, so that as the carriage moves along the line the ribbon is shifted back and forth across its width, giving each letter in a word a fresh transverse strip. A pawl and ratchet on each spool, driven from the space lever through a rock shaft, turns the spool one tooth at each space-key stroke, so the ribbon advances lengthwise between words but not at the character keys.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spool; ratchet wheel; pawl; ribbon fork; rocking lever; bell-crank lever; zigzag cam plate; ribbon operating bar; space lever; rock shaft

**Improves on:**

- single-track ribbon feed: uses the whole width of the ribbon by moving it across the print point
