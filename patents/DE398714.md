## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keys cannot be locked at line end while the carriage moves and the shift frame moves for capitals.

**Solution:** A margin stop carrier moved by the carriage operates a locking lever that swings under the key bar, with a slide passing through the shift frame to transmit the motion at all shift positions.
