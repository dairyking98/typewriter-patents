## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hammond typewriter spaces only one letter width.

**Solution:** Adjustable stop controls how far the escapement pawl retracts, giving spacing of one to six letter widths.

**How it works:** Carriage rack meshes with a spring drum gear and the escapement wheel gear. The pawl is on a hammer lever pivoted on the escapement shaft; a forked arm of the escapement lever drives it through a curved slot. An adjustable stop disc set by a lever and slide with scale limits tail movement of the pawl lever.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** carriage rack; spring drum; escapement wheel; pawl; hammer lever; escapement lever; stop disc; scale

**Numbers:** 1 to 6 letter spaces

**Improves on:**

- Hammond escapement: adds multiple letter-space steps
