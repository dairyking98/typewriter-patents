## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Producing spaced (letter-spaced) lettering by use of the space key is tedious, and a single set of escapement teeth wears under repeated strong impacts.

**Solution:** Give the escapement frame two pawl sets, one for normal spacing and one for double spacing, and select between them by swinging the frame towards or away from the ratchet wheel.

**How it works:** The carriage mainspring drives a rack and gear on a shaft carrying a ratchet wheel. A rocking escapement frame, tilted whenever any key is struck, lets the wheel advance one tooth per stroke through a fixed tooth and a loose spring-loaded shift tooth. A second pair of teeth on the opposite side of the frame is spaced two teeth apart, so when brought into engagement by a slide the carriage advances two spaces per letter. The frame hangs on arms that swing slightly to choose the set.
