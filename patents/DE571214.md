## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (booking machine false entry prevention) · **Subject (patent classes):** Computing machines characterised by the structural interrelation of their functional units, e.g. invoicing machines

**How it works (reading):** Control bookings are marked by a punched mark or perforation made by a marker pen carried on the hollow shaft of the control type wheel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reading subtotals from a card can be misused to make a false booking that looks normal.

**Solution:** Control bookings are marked by a punched mark or perforation made by a marker pen carried on the hollow shaft of the control type wheel.
