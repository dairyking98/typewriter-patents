## Reading (full text, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A large number of typebars must be mounted in a small space with clearance for the type ends, with easy attachment, adjustment and removal of each bar.

**Solution:** Typebars hung by ring-shaped hubs on staggered V-grooved circular bearings formed on one curved support bar, using three bearing balls and a hinged, screw-tightened hub half that takes up wear.

**How it works:** A curved segment bar carries side-by-side annular V-grooves that converge on the print centre, alternate ones offset fore and aft so adjacent bars lie staggered and have room for their type sockets. Each typebar has a hub ring of two segments: one integral with the shank, one hinged to it. Three balls run between the grooves of the segment and the hub, held apart by pins and abutments. A thin springy hasp and screw draw the hinged half closed, tightening the joint to remove shake but leave the bar free, and the spring tension takes up wear automatically. Any bar can be detached on its own by opening the flap.
