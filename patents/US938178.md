## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon cross feed or bicolour field selection must still work on a platen-shifting vibrator machine.

**Solution:** A ribbon carrier mounted on a guide that shifts with the platen, driven by an operating lever on two fulcrums, one fixed and one movable.

**How it works:** The ribbon carrier rides on a guide that shifts with the platen shifting devices so the shift does not alter ribbon and platen relation. The vibrator is moved by a train including an operating lever with a slot over two fulcrum pins, one fixed and one movable. The system can cross-feed the ribbon or write in set paths along different fields of a two-colour ribbon. Shown on a No. 10 Remington style machine.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier; vibrator; operating lever; fulcrum; guide; platen

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 10: adds ribbon cross feed or field selection with platen shift
