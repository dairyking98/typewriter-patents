## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selected by means of keys or keyboards of the typewriter type

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the key-actions of typewriting machines;

**How it works (reading):** Compressed air admitted through key-operated valves into cylinders at the key-lever ends drives the key lever and type bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The key effort is too heavy and the type must be actuated with a very light touch.

**Solution:** Compressed air admitted through key-operated valves into cylinders at the key-lever ends drives the key lever and type bar.
