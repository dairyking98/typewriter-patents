## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Remington No. 6 needed better line locking, bell, margin stops and ribbon motion.

**Solution:** A series of improvements: line stop and lock with release past the line, bell, margin stops that can be passed, ribbon cross and reverse motion.

**How it works:** The carriage is arrested and escapement and printing locked at a predetermined line end, with a release that lets printing continue beyond and a final extreme stop. A bell alarms, a margin stop stops carriage return but can be passed. The ribbon moves crosswise and reverses itself when the supply spool empties, and back rotation of the escapement wheel is stopped on return.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)

**Parts:** margin stop; line stop; rod; bell; ribbon spool; escapement wheel; carriage

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 6: many improvements, also in British patent 6,912
