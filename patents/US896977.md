## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**How it works (reading):** A detachable card-holder strip with teeth for the card edge, whose end forks hook over the platen hub; one end can be released and the holder withdrawn from the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card-holder claws that hook over the platen heads grip so strongly that they are awkward to release.

**Solution:** A detachable card-holder strip with teeth for the card edge, whose end forks hook over the platen hub; one end can be released and the holder withdrawn from the platen.
