**How it works (reading):** A ribbon lift device with an extra adjustable lifting movement, via a swing arm and fork and adjustable stops, to bring the ribbon up to the printing line of tall characters or of a differently coloured ribbon band.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Particularly tall characters, and characters of two-tone ribbon, are not coloured because the normal ribbon stroke is too small.

**Solution:** A ribbon lift device with an extra adjustable lifting movement, via a swing arm and fork and adjustable stops, to bring the ribbon up to the printing line of tall characters or of a differently coloured ribbon band.
