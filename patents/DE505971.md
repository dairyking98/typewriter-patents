> Description Translated from German When projecting a by a mechanical device, e.g. B. from a typewriter or a teleprinter, moving writing surface or the text written on the same, the disadvantage occurs that the writing appearing on the projection surface migrates laterally in the same way as the writing surface moved by the mechanical device. The projection surface must be kept unnecessarily wide in relation to the writing surface. In addition, the wandering of the written text on the projection surface is perceived as annoying. This drawback is eliminated by the invention described below in that one z. B. in a typewriter or a teleprinter intermittently by a letter width with space moving writing surface with the characters located on this in each case by means of appropriately moved optical compensation means (mirror, prism) is projected onto the screen so that the already written text comes to a standstill and is supplemented by the additional characters. The optical means are expediently arranged between the writing surface written on by the mechanical device and the projection screen.
