## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon vibrator moving on every stroke annoys the operator and adds to the force on the keys.

**Solution:** The vibrator covers the print point automatically and is held there until the spacing devices act or as long as desired, with a release for two-colour ribbons.

**How it works:** Operating the type mechanism sets the ribbon vibrator to cover the print point, and a retaining device holds it there during further printing until a spacing device is actuated. A mechanism can disable the retainer so ordinary vibration returns, which a two-colour ribbon needs. A modification is shown. Opening only.
