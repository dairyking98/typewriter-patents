## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Ribbon switching device for stenography machines. The invention refers to a color manual control for stenography machines in which a on the frame of the machine swingably mounted carriages in two different Positions can be held between the ribbon rolls. The invention consists in that on the sides of the slide through a spring connected to each other and fastened against one on the slide Stop abutting plates are swingably mounted, on which pawl carrier with in the ratchet wheels of the ribbon rolls engaging pawls are rotatably mounted by swingably mounted, by a cross to the button levers of the machine itself extending rod interconnected fingers are moved. The main advantage of the new switching device is that that all parts for guiding the ribbon at the front of the machine are in front of the eyes of the worker in the very limited space of stenography machines are. In addition, this arrangement allows an overview of the switching means, without which operation would not be possible.
