## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Stroke control device for the ribbon fork. The subject of present invention is a stroke control device for the ribbon fork of FIG Typewriters to adjust the ribbon to two different heights to enable and to bring about the decoupling of its drives. this will achieved in that a changeover piece of the ribbon fork linkage in the central position is decoupled from the drives, in each of its two inclined positions to the central position executes a limited downward movement, the stroke of the switching piece in the one incline differs from that in the other. The essence of this device consists in the particular simplicity and safe effect, it requires in their Execution no special fitting work and is therefore cheap to manufacture, so that a Advantage in economic relation as well as in the use is achieved, the one patent-establishing technical progress means. In the drawing is the stroke control device according to the invention shown in a longitudinal section of a typewriter.
