## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After a sum is printed, the tape must advance to the start of the next form, but the advance varies with the number of items.

**Solution:** A special ratchet wheel driven through gearing advances the paper by one form length; a pawl controlled by the sum key moves it, and the leftover distance is taken up by the ratchet.
