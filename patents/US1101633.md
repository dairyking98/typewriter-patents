## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the ribbon is fully wound or sticks, the ribbon drive also stalls the spring-driven carriage.

**Solution:** A pair of ribbon driving gears is forced out of mesh when the ribbon stops so the carriage advances freely.

**How it works:** The spring drum drives the carriage by cord and also the ribbon spools via pinion, pawl, toothed wheel and worm gear. When the ribbon feed stops, the gears disengage, disconnecting the spools.

**Features:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** spring drum; cord; pinion; pawl; toothed wheel; worm; worm wheel; ribbon spool; gears
