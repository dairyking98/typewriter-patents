## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**How it works (reading):** A device on the escapement increases carriage feed speed and reduces force; it works with a straight rack and a wheel escapement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage feed needed to be faster and require less force on the escapement.

**Solution:** A device on the escapement increases carriage feed speed and reduces force; it works with a straight rack and a wheel escapement.
