## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A small typewriter used on the knee or in the open lid of its cover sits too low to write comfortably.

**Solution:** The protective cover's lid has two brackets with rubber bodies inside it, so the typewriter rests on the open lid at writing height.
