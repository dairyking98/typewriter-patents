## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (rounding device for booking machine)

**How it works (reading):** An automatic rounding device that rounds the last kept digit of a product in the last preceding automatic feed direction, with rounding stations at several counter places, one of which is made effective by a relay switch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rounding in a typewriter-plus-multiplier booking machine needed either a fixed counter position or a preset step before each calculation, which slows work when decimal places vary.

**Solution:** An automatic rounding device that rounds the last kept digit of a product in the last preceding automatic feed direction, with rounding stations at several counter places, one of which is made effective by a relay switch.
