**How it works (reading):** A semi-elliptical frame carries a cocoon-shaped spiral-bristle brush on a crank shaft; a lever swings it between a rest position on the key levers and a working position among the types.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type cleaning needs a brush always ready that does not soil the hands.

**Solution:** A semi-elliptical frame carries a cocoon-shaped spiral-bristle brush on a crank shaft; a lever swings it between a rest position on the key levers and a working position among the types.
