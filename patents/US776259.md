## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabulating mechanisms for writing-machines, and more particularly to that class of tabulators in which at the operation of a denominational key a denominational stop corresponding thereto is moved into position to coact with another cooperating stop and the carriage is automat-1 tached to existing forms of type-writing machines without altering any of the parts of the latter.

**How it works (reading):** A denominational key moves a denominational stop into position to coact with a cooperating stop, arresting the carriage at set distances, with carriage-releasing means and an escapement wheel with clutch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing tabulator setups do not provide a denominational stop mechanism that can be attached to standard machines without altering their parts.

**Solution:** A denominational key moves a denominational stop into position to coact with a cooperating stop, arresting the carriage at set distances, with carriage-releasing means and an escapement wheel with clutch.
