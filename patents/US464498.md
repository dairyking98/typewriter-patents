## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in that class of devices that are used for perforating checks or other papers representing value, so as to prevent alteration of the same.

**How it works (reading):** A segmental row of punches on a rotating sleeve is indexed by a notched curved track bar, with dies on the opposite bracket.
