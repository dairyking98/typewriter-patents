## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fisher's earlier track-mounted machine needs a better typebar ring, ribbon-spool feeding at the carriage ends, a way to expose the line of writing for correction, a position indicator and a removable letter platen.

**Solution:** Improvements to a typebar machine that rides on track rails: a ring with parallel guide ears, slidable ribbon spools that can be locked back, and a pointer showing the next print position.

**How it works:** The whole machine travels on track-rail racks driven by pinions. Keys act through pivoted levers and draw-wires on typebars whose trunnions sit in ears on a supporting ring. Ribbon spools slide on spindles against return springs and are held back by gravity catches so the writing line is visible. Swinging arms feed the ribbon spools at the ends of carriage travel. A pointer shows where the next letter prints, an adjustable trip works a bell hammer, and a removable letter-writing platen has an adjustable stop.
