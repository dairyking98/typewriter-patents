## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**How it works (reading):** The pivot levers, their axis, the pressure rollers and the lower paper guide plate form one closed component that is removed as a unit and sits in several bearing eyes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-guide parts are hard to assemble and clean because the pivot levers must be inserted into the carriage housing one by one.

**Solution:** The pivot levers, their axis, the pressure rollers and the lower paper guide plate form one closed component that is removed as a unit and sits in several bearing eyes.
