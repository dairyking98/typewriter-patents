## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing wheels with fixed tooth intervals cannot give the fine, occasional variations in line spacing the operator needs.

**Solution:** A clutch is interposed between the line-spacing lever and the revolving platen so the platen can be given fine variations in line spacing while the lever is used as usual for the carriage return.
