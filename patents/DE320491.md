According to the invention, one and the same alternating current phase serves both for switching of the transmitter strip, as well as, through the intermediary of a wire, for printing a drawing element. Be there the drawing elements partly by the positive, partly by the negative current impulses brought to the stop. In the case of two-wire operation, it is advisable to use a transmitter strip with two rows of holes, which come into effect in such a way that the holes of one row for the transmission of positive or negative current pulses two drawing elements and 'the holes of the other row for conveying positive or negative current pulses to the other two character elements.
