## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Colour-ribbon selection on Underwood machines, with ink offsetting between superposed ribbons to be avoided.

**Solution:** Two ribbons wound simultaneously on the usual spools, each with its own vibrator and shiftable actuators, with ribbons inked only on the platen-facing side.

**How it works:** A red ribbon is stretched behind the usual ribbon and wound up with it on the same spools. Individual vibrators for each ribbon, one before the other, are moved by side-by-side levers. Actuators on the universal-bar frame are shifted to operate either lever or neither (stencils). Each ribbon is inked only on the side facing the platen, and an ink-proof strip may sit between ribbons to prevent offset.
