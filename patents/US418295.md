## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix machine variable feed) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the mechanism in matrix-making machines for producing movements of a matrix-carriage to feed the matrixbody forward proportionately to the varying sizes of the dies to be impressed.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-actuated matrix carriage is fed by a screw; an escapement controls a rotary barrel with spiral pins that stops limit to set the feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Impressions of different sizes must be spaced automatically and quickly adjusted.

**Solution:** A spring-actuated matrix carriage is fed by a screw; an escapement controls a rotary barrel with spiral pins that stops limit to set the feed.
