## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feeds expend ink locally, check ribbon movement, and take work from the operator's fingers.

**Solution:** A carriage-driven rock shaft with spiral wing advances the ribbon lengthwise after each impression and reverses automatically at the end, with spools sliding sideways to follow the ribbon.

**How it works:** A rock shaft with a spiral wing is pressed against a carriage arm, so it oscillates as the carriage moves back and forth. This oscillation turns ratchet wheels on two spool shafts through pawls, so the ribbon advances lengthwise independent of the keys. A tilt bar balanced on a pivot joins two matched sets of mechanism at the ends, with one set in action while the other is out. A tripping pawl inside each spool, tripped by the wound ribbon, throws the tilt bar so the feed reverses automatically. The shafts slide endwise by about the ribbon width so tension devices and guides move transversely with the ribbon, which is run back and forth along its whole length.
