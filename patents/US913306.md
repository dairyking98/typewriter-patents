## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the present invention is to furnish mechanism whereby these results may be accomplished.

**How it works (reading):** Key mechanism with rock-levers in which one key selectively operates lower-case or upper-case key-levers depending on shift.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single key or lever must be able to operate one of two characters (lower or upper case) to the exclusion of all others.

**Solution:** Key mechanism with rock-levers in which one key selectively operates lower-case or upper-case key-levers depending on shift.

**How it works:** Described for a typewriter with keyboard, rock-levers fulcrumed on a common axis, and feed mechanism. The shift setting determines which of two connected levers a key actuates, so the same key gives lower or upper case. The text is cut off before details.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** key; key lever; rock lever; feed mechanism; typebar; platen
