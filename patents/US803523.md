## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Making several original (non-carbon) copies normally needs repeated typing or carbons.

**Solution:** A typebar carries duplicate characters spaced so that one stroke prints the same character on two platens at once.

**How it works:** Each typebar carries two plates (or directly cut types) of duplicate upper and lower case characters, one set at the free end and the second set spaced so both land on the centres of two platens. When the bar is depressed both characters print simultaneously, and a platen shift brings both capitals into line. The duplicate may differ in style (Roman and italic). Variants are shown for Franklin, Remington and Oliver machines; the basket must be enlarged and bars shaped so they do not interlock. A ribbon or other ink medium is used.
