## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing unit for adding machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** A second resilient switching tooth is placed opposite the first on a ratchet wheel, so the roller advances twice on one stroke, and the tooth can be switched off for single spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing on adding-machine printing units is only a single step, and a double line spacing is wanted.

**Solution:** A second resilient switching tooth is placed opposite the first on a ratchet wheel, so the roller advances twice on one stroke, and the tooth can be switched off for single spacing.
