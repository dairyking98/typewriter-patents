## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The shift key needs an economical attachment so a treadle can work it.

**Solution:** A table-clamped lever with a clawed finger on the shift key, driven by a treadle through levers and a rod.

**How it works:** A clamp on the table carries an adjustable angle piece and a pivoted arm with a finger whose wire claws hook under the shift key. A leaf spring keeps the arm raised. A rod links it to a second lever on a depending arm, joined by a rod to a spring-held treadle. Pressing the treadle depresses the shift key; threaded nuts adjust the rod length.
