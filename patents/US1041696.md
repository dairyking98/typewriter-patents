## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Touch typists and beginners cannot tell which key they are striking without looking.

**Solution:** A transparent key cap with raised characters, either letters or point-blind characters, that fits over an ordinary key.

**Features:**

- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [For the blind](../features/use-blind.md)
