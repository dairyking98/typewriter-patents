## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper must be fed in square and gaged against the platen when inserted.

**Solution:** A paper table with flanged gages at its rear and front guides the paper between the feed rollers and the platen.

**How it works:** The paper enters below the table, passes up the front of the platen and back over the table, guided by flanges.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
