## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple, rapid and efficient carriage escapement is wanted.

**Solution:** An anchor escapement giving carriage let-off at the upstroke of the printing key.

**How it works:** In a Remington No. 10 style machine, the carriage feed rack drives a pinion on a shaft whose rear end connects through a pawl-and-ratchet drum to an escapement wheel, driven by the usual spring drum. The anchor escapement member is controlled by the key action so the carriage is let off on the upstroke. A modified form is also shown. The opening text does not describe the anchor in detail.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** feed rack; feed pinion; shaft; escapement wheel; anchor; spring drum; key lever; typebar
