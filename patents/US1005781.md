## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage motor, a spring drum, drives the carriage too fast on tabulation and column work, which can injure the carriage.

**Solution:** A centrifugal governor with weights on an arm on the motor drum shaft, inside a cylindrical casing, restrains the drum speed so the carriage moves at a steady rate.

**How it works:** Weights pivoted on an arm fixed to the drum shaft act within the casing to limit speed as the drum turns.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** carriage motor drum; tape; governor weights; arm on shaft; cylindrical casing; ratchet and pawl
