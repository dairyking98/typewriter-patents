## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Moving paper forward on the roller for line spacing and backward at will.

**Solution:** A bent lever with a spring pawl steps the platen ratchet one tooth per stroke, and a second lever lifts pawl and detent together for free hand turning.

**How it works:** A bent lever pivoted under the carriage has a tubular short arm holding a spring pawl that engages ratchet teeth on the roller end; an adjustable stop limits its stroke to one tooth. A tube with a spring detent holds the roller otherwise. A curved arm joins the pawl and detent tube so that pressing a second lever releases both, letting the roller be turned freely in either direction. Two strokes give double space.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** bent lever; spring pawl; ratchet; detent; tube; curved release lever; adjustable stop; roller
