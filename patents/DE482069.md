## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards characterised by means for holding or fastening typewriters or computer equipment

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a wooden base board for typewriter cases.

**How it works (reading):** A thin wood base and support plate are riveted to a light sheet-metal frame filled with another material, making a rigid, light board that also dampens noise.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A wooden base board for a typewriter case warps and is not light or strong enough.

**Solution:** A thin wood base and support plate are riveted to a light sheet-metal frame filled with another material, making a rigid, light board that also dampens noise.
