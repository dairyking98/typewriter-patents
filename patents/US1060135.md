## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The universal bar and escapement operating mechanism had lost motion and undesirable resiliency, and was not as positive in action as needed.

**Solution:** A redesigned universal bar and escapement operating linkage, arranged so that the parts are stronger and act without lost motion or resiliency; the platen is locked on its arms by a sliding latch.
