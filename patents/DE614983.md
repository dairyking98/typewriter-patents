## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register receipt ejector)

**How it works (reading):** After the first hammer stroke a full-width slide, notched and coupled to the hammer shaft, pushes the printed document back out of the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A narrow receipt inserted into a two-receipt machine bypasses the ejector discs and is printed twice without a control-strip print.

**Solution:** After the first hammer stroke a full-width slide, notched and coupled to the hammer shaft, pushes the printed document back out of the machine.
