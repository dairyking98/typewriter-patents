## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen needs improved rotating devices: free turning either way by a finger wheel yet automatic locking when released, and line spacing by a finger lever.

**Solution:** A spring latch riding in a cam-lever recess on the platen shaft locks the platen when released but is cammed out when the finger wheel is turned, with a separate ratchet and adjustable pawl stop for line spacing.

**How it works:** Inside the platen cylinder a vibratory latch pivoted at one end is spring-pressed toward the platen shaft and engages a toothed wheel. A cam lever fixed on the shaft has a recess with curved edges; turning the shaft either way drives a curved edge against the latch and swings it out of the teeth, and when released the spring returns it, locking the platen. A finger wheel on the shaft end allows turning. A cup-shaped ratchet case holds a ratchet and toothed wheel that rotate on a sleeve; a pawl arm driven by a horizontal finger lever turns the ratchet. A pawl stop with a handle is set in different positions to limit the pawl arm's swing and so the amount of line space.
