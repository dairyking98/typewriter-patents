**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switching device for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A toggle lever buckles down for lowering and is straightened to lift; one auxiliary link driven by the two shift keys moves both the angle lever and the double lever, so one switching rod serves both directions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Double-shift typewriters with three characters per carrier needed a platen that stays centred for lower case and lifts or lowers equally for the other two cases, using a single shared link.

**Solution:** A toggle lever buckles down for lowering and is straightened to lift; one auxiliary link driven by the two shift keys moves both the angle lever and the double lever, so one switching rod serves both directions.
