## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjacent type-bars conflict and collide near the type-bar basket because of rebound during rapid operation.

**Solution:** A type-bar basket pad of felt or leather, in several forms including a pad with pins, absorbs the type-bar rebound.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md), [Rebound and clash prevention](../features/bar-rebound.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type-bar basket; pad; pins; key-levers; restoring springs
