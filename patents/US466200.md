## Summary

**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods on side surfaces thereof, e.g. fixed thereto

**In the patent’s own words:** My present improvements relate more particularly to that class of type-writing machines in which the type are carried on a bar which is moved longitudinally by the hand of the operator in connection with an index-plate and adjusting-bar to bring the proper type character to position and is moved vertically downward to produce an impression of the chosen character upon the paper below;

**How it works (reading):** A bar with two rows of type slid longitudinally by a carrier, rolled to choose a row, and pressed down onto a flat paper carriage.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wanted to enlarge the capacity of a sliding type-bar index machine and share work between the hands.

**Solution:** A bar with two rows of type slid longitudinally by a carrier, rolled to choose a row, and pressed down onto a flat paper carriage.

**How it works:** Type lie in rows on a strip fixed to a rib on a cylindrical bar that slides and oscillates in a tubular guide. A carrier on guide rods moves the bar along an index plate with an adjusting bar. Rolling the bar presents row two. The guide, with its shield, moves down for the impression, so one hand positions and the other prints. The carriage holds paper flat under the overhanging arm on a hollow pillar, with a fixed platen. Inking and spacing devices are mentioned.

**Features:** [ELEM:linear-bar](../features/elem-linear-bar.md), [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [SEL:linear-slider](../features/sel-linear-slider.md), [SEL:pointer-chart](../features/sel-pointer-chart.md), [SHIFT:rotary](../features/shift-rotary.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** base; overhanging arm; pillar; type bar; rib; type strip; guide; carrier; guide rod; index plate; adjusting bar; paper carriage; fixed platen

**Numbers:** two rows of type
