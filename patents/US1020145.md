## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shift keys at the side of the keyboard make the little finger lift the whole carriage and disturb touch typing.

**Solution:** A supplemental shift key placed centrally near the space bar so either thumb can shift.

**How it works:** A key is attached to the keyboard in the middle, directly above, in front of or behind the space bar, and engages the existing shift key so the thumb can depress it. No new mechanical linkage to the carriage is claimed in the opening.
