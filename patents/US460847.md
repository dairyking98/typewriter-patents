## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my present invention is to improve the prior apparatus or machine by providing novel means for rotating the cylinder to move the copy;

**How it works (reading):** A key-operated arm moves a friction roller against a flanged rim on a paper-moving cylinder, rotating it to advance the copy one line.
