## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter key levers need to be operated automatically from a stored program rather than by hand.

**Solution:** A rotatable cylinder carries a perforated controller strip, and slides drawn down by the holes operate the key levers.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Pointer into a hole or notch](../features/sel-pointer-hole.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
