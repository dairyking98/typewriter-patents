## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (receipt line setting cash register)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line setting device for receipts at cash registers, in the place of a sliding frame that holds the receipt a simple externally adjustable stop is used to control the insertion movement of the receipt slip.

**How it works (reading):** A single stamped slider with a stop, a numbered guide bar, a reading edge and a ball detent sets the stop for each line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line setting on cash register receipts needed a simpler device than a sliding frame.

**Solution:** A single stamped slider with a stop, a numbered guide bar, a reading edge and a ball detent sets the stop for each line.
