## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen shift needs less effort lifting, while keeping the platen's lower position firmly located so letter alignment holds.

**Solution:** A spring lifts the rocking frame that carries the platen rail, with a detent holding it down in lower case and a latch to hold the shift key depressed.

**How it works:** The platen frame rides on balls on a rail fixed to a rocking frame pivoted at the rear. A spiral spring under the frame presses it upward. A detent hooks over a lever end of the rocking frame to hold it in the lower position for alignment. Pressing the shift key turns the detent clear via a rod and, through a bell-lever, lifts the frame. A second key moves a detent that latches the shift key down and releases it.
