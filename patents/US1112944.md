## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriters need a separate table or desk to sit on when in use.

**Solution:** A foldable support attached to the typewriter folds against the base as feet when not wanted, and extends to serve as a self-contained table.

**How it works:** The support hinges from the typewriter base; extended it forms a table top, folded it becomes a sub-base with feet for the machine.
