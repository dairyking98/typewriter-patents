## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles; Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a duplicating device for typewriters, in particular for writing on of form tapes of unlimited length with a platen over which the writing sheet can be carried away and on which she braids while writing and fit tightly, and a line switch device for the writing sheet.

**How it works (reading):** The writing bow and the writing abutment are shifted relative to each other before line feed, releasing the grip so the form can move without dragging the copies; platen, form and carbon movements are driven by one common lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When switching a continuous form sheet with carbon copies under tension, the forms slide on each other and the auxiliary carbon ribbons get pulled out of the writing zone.

**Solution:** The writing bow and the writing abutment are shifted relative to each other before line feed, releasing the grip so the form can move without dragging the copies; platen, form and carbon movements are driven by one common lever.
