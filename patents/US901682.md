## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing circulars or letters that differ only in small details, such as the address, needs a typewriter whose printing bed can print without first setting the carriage to a particular point.

**Solution:** A printing bed slides in the same carriage as the platen, at right angles to carriage travel, and prints on paper on the platen; one ribbon feeds automatically between a reel on the carriage and a reel on the printing-bed carrier.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** platen; carriage; printing bed; bed carrier; ribbon reels; typewriter type
