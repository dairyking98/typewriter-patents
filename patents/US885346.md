## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in typewriting machines, in which a segmental carrier carrying a series of type arms fulcrumed thereto is suitably mounted in the machine, and is adapted to carry said type in consecutive order to writing position to and at right angles to the impression roller, or platen, by the operation, or the depression of a key.
