## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typebar machines are noisy and strike the paper unevenly; the inventor wants a silent machine with equalized type pressure.

**Solution:** A segmental carrier holding fulcrumed type arms is moved bodily toward the platen on parallel links so the selected type is pressed flat against the paper.

**How it works:** Keys are connected by rods and cranks to type arms hung on a segmental fulcrum wire in a carrier. Depressing a key brings its type into a recess in a shift block at the centre of the carrier. A toggle lever and link, driven from the key, then carry the whole carrier on parallel arms toward the platen at right angles, pressing the type on the paper in one quiet motion. A needle enters holes behind the type to position it and a shift block adjusts upper or lower case. The platen carriage slides on horizontal rails.
