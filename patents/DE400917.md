## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculating and typewriting both demand heavy key force, making the keys hard to press lightly.

**Solution:** Motor-driven constantly rotating elements are coupled to the transmission on key press; the write key can be driven directly or through the arithmetic keys after the transfer is complete, with interlocks against pressing both.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
