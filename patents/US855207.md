## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to printing point indicating mechanism for t ewriting machines and one object of the invention is to provide simple and efficient mechanism of the character specified which is automatically actuated at each printing operation to move the indicator from the normal or indicating position to the norind c-ating position.

**How it works (reading):** An indicator is moved automatically from its normal position to the printing position at each printing operation, and the mechanism can be applied to existing typewriters without changing their structure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The machine has no printing-point indicator, and existing machines cannot easily be fitted with one without structural change.

**Solution:** An indicator is moved automatically from its normal position to the printing position at each printing operation, and the mechanism can be applied to existing typewriters without changing their structure.
