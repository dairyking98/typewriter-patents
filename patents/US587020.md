## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using mechanical translation and type-head printing, e.g. type-wheel, type-cylinder

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A small visible-writing typewriter with interchangeable type-wheel type that can be removed.
