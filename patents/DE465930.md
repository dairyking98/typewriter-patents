## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that devices are provided which make it possible to bring the pressure roller bracket into two different working positions, in one of which the pressure rollers are on the paper roller significantly closer to the writing line hang up - than in the other, to also when writing on small cards or pieces of paper ensure good leadership.

**How it works (reading):** A stop lever with two stop surfaces and a locking lever set two working positions of the pressure roller bracket, and the bracket returns to the chosen position after rest.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The pressure roller bracket must sit near the writing line for small cards, but a single rest/working position does not suit all paper sizes.

**Solution:** A stop lever with two stop surfaces and a locking lever set two working positions of the pressure roller bracket, and the bracket returns to the chosen position after rest.
