## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash desk stamp wheel control) · **Subject (patent classes):** Transfer mechanisms, e.g. transfer of a figure from a ten-key keyboard into the pin carriage

**How it works (reading):** An addendum to patent 98810: a catch lever falls into a detent on the disk, a cover plate is moved over the number discs, and the drawer's release pulls the catch back so the set amount becomes visible again. A second embodiment uses a hand-folded sign on a rail.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a cash register, the amount displayed must be hidden until the seller has triggered the display, and the viewing opening must be closed and released in the correct order with the stamp wheels.

**Solution:** An addendum to patent 98810: a catch lever falls into a detent on the disk, a cover plate is moved over the number discs, and the drawer's release pulls the catch back so the set amount becomes visible again. A second embodiment uses a hand-folded sign on a rail.
