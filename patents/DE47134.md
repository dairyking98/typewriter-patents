## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing telegraph that can also be an ordinary typewriter, with spacing done electrically.

**Solution:** Electromagnets throw weighted type levers against an inked belt and paper roller, and a local circuit steps the paper roller.

**How it works:** Pointer devices at two stations send circuits through segments; at the printer each electromagnet pulls an armature and rotates a weighted lever with a type, pressing it through a soft inked belt onto a paper roller. A special magnet and key close a local circuit after each print, driving mechanisms that advance the roller for letter spacing, word spaces and return. Also a plug switch connects or removes the printer from the line. Only the opening is available.
