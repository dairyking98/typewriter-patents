## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** A flexible rubber membrane sits between key heads and the cover plate, sealing key holes while letting keys depress.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dust enters through the key holes of covered card-printing machines.

**Solution:** A flexible rubber membrane sits between key heads and the cover plate, sealing key holes while letting keys depress.
