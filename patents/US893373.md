## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to carriage stop mechanism for typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulator stop devices.

**How it works (reading):** Stops mounted on a stop bar resist the shock and stay in place, with a cooperating stop and adjustable mounting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops are weak and slip under carriage shock.

**Solution:** Stops mounted on a stop bar resist the shock and stay in place, with a cooperating stop and adjustable mounting.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
