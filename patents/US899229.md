## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Listing machines need a lever pulled after the keys are set, which tires the operator and slows the work.

**Solution:** Pressing the keys alone sets the type digits, carries the total and returns the keys; printing is independent of calculation and can be used or not.
