## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (duplicate printing of movable type) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of printing-machines in which movable type are carried to and from a platen and a strip of paper is fed between the platen and the type intermittently;

**How it works (reading):** Two independent ribbons, one fed across and one fed either across or vertically, are combined with feed rollers that carry a continuous original strip and a superposed transparent strip so the type prints both at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicate copies with carbon sheets are impossible on continuous strips or sheets, and loose carbon sheets cannot be used there.

**Solution:** Two independent ribbons, one fed across and one fed either across or vertically, are combined with feed rollers that carry a continuous original strip and a superposed transparent strip so the type prints both at once.
