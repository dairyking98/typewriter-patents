> Description Translated from German Drive for the ribbon fork of typewriters. The invention refers to a drive for the ribbon fork of typewriters, the characterized by the fact that it can be produced cheaply and quickly in a simple manner, in the machine is easy to install and just as easy to remove from it is. The invention consists in that the lower arm engages the ribbon fork Angle lever by a handlebar with a vertical two-armed lever is connected, the lower lever arm with the help of a rod in different Can attack height on the lever arm, is driven by the universal rail. The drawing shows a side view of the drive. An angle lever b , which is articulated to the usual ribbon fork c, can be rotated about a bolt a. The downwardly projecting arm of the angle lever is connected by a link d to the upper end of a vertical two-armed lever e rotatable about the bolt f, the lower end of which is driven by the universal rail. A rod is used for this purpose, one end of which is connected to the universal rail and the other which engages the lever e.
