## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to securing means for thetype-bar hangers or bearings of type-writing machines 1n which the hangers or bearings are separately secured to and adjustable on or removable from their support or segment.

**How it works (reading):** A securing means holds each hanger on its segment rigidly and lets a single hanger be adjusted or removed without moving the others.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typebar hangers must sit rigidly in correct position yet be adjustable or removable one at a time without disturbing neighbours.

**Solution:** A securing means holds each hanger on its segment rigidly and lets a single hanger be adjusted or removed without moving the others.
