## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon shields or backings

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to prevent the ink ribbon or tape from flapping against and smudging the paper and rubber guides or friction-bands which hold the paper against the printing-cylinder, thus preventing an accumulation of the ink upon the said frictionbands, and its consequent transference to the paper.
