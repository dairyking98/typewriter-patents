## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon shields or backings

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to prevent the ink ribbon or tape from flapping against and smudging the paper and rubber guides or friction-bands which hold the paper against the printing-cylinder, thus preventing an accumulation of the ink upon the said frictionbands, and its consequent transference to the paper.

**How it works (reading):** A wire reticulated frame hung on elastic bands between the printing cylinder and the ribbon keeps the ribbon off the paper while letting it play.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ink ribbon flaps against and smudges the paper and the rubber guide bands, so ink accumulates on the bands and transfers to the paper; first impressions are too heavy and later ones faint.

**Solution:** A wire reticulated frame hung on elastic bands between the printing cylinder and the ribbon keeps the ribbon off the paper while letting it play.

**How it works:** A rectangular metal frame with eyes at the corners is joined across by spaced wires that can be adjusted to alter the interior open square. Elastic bands with hooks attach it into the corners of the ribbon slots of the machine, interposing it between cylinder and ribbon. The type passes through the open square, striking the ribbon with less force, so a fresh ribbon does not spread too much ink while a worn one still prints, equalizing first and last impressions.
