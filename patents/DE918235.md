## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type wheel adjustment, cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an adjusting device for one of a A series of type wheels mounted on a rotating roller for check registers and similar calculating and accounting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Each type wheel pivots on the roller and an electromagnetic blocking relay with a pin locks it into a gap at a set angle while the roller rotates.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type wheels on a roller for check registers must be set to a chosen letter sequence, and mechanical pulse triggering of printing limits this.

**Solution:** Each type wheel pivots on the roller and an electromagnetic blocking relay with a pin locks it into a gap at a set angle while the roller rotates.
