## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier columnating attachments used a series of stops or catches one letter-space apart, which left no room for a decimal point or comma between figures and were complex.

**Solution:** A single variable catch on the carriage that can take graduated positions, grouped in threes, against a toothed adjustable stop on the frame.

**How it works:** A single catch, preferably on the carriage and automatically returning to its normal position, replaces the series of catches. A stop-bar on the frame has teeth pointing to the catch, and an adjustable stop with a lip that engages the teeth and a stop-lug that meets the catch pin. The catch is placed in different graduated positions, grouped periodically so spaces are left for commas or decimal points, to stop the carriage at the right place for the amount. The stop can be moved to set the column position on the page, and several stops can be used for several columns.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; frame; stop-bar; adjustable stop; stop-lug; lip; catch pin; variable catch

**Improves on:**

- Blickensderfer applications 399,117 and 410,893: one variable catch instead of a series of side-by-side catches
