## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements to attach the ribbon to the spool

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to means for connecting the ends of an inking-ribbon to the spools or bobbins of type-writing and other machines, and has for its main object to provide a construction whereby the ribbon may be attached anddetached with facility and expedition and without soiling the hands of the operator.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon with an enlarged, clamped end that engages a spool with a spring catch or cover, which is drawn back to insert the ribbon end, so the ribbon attaches and detaches quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon ends are hard to attach to and detach from spools, and the operator's hands get soiled doing so.

**Solution:** A ribbon with an enlarged, clamped end that engages a spool with a spring catch or cover, which is drawn back to insert the ribbon end, so the ribbon attaches and detaches quickly.
