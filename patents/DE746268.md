## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Summation counters and register counters each needed their own zero-limit point and fine adjustment, and spring-loaded zero limits wore out.

**Solution:** The zero-position setting members use a front gear set with zero-setting lugs, so both counter types share one zero limit point and the setting is simplified.
