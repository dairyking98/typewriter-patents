## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pin feeds on or within the platen-rollers

**How it works (reading):** Transport pins that rotate during forward movement, with eccentric or elliptical cross-sections and hook-shaped ends, are used to align the documents in both directions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Sheets on platen rollers were pulled by friction and shifted when stacked, so precise alignment of writing pads was hard to keep.

**Solution:** Transport pins that rotate during forward movement, with eccentric or elliptical cross-sections and hook-shaped ends, are used to align the documents in both directions.
