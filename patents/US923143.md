## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocks actuated mechanically by coins, e.g. by a single coin

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the invention are to provide a slmple arrangement which may be applied to typewriters of ordinary construction, when so desired, and in which the resetting of the look is dependent upon the actuation of the printing mechanism of the typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** Coin-controlled lock that releases the typewriter for a length of composition set by the coin value, and resets the lock as the printing mechanism operates.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters placed for public use need coin control so the machine is locked until the proper coin is deposited.

**Solution:** Coin-controlled lock that releases the typewriter for a length of composition set by the coin value, and resets the lock as the printing mechanism operates.
