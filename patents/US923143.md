## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocks actuated mechanically by coins, e.g. by a single coin

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the invention are to provide a slmple arrangement which may be applied to typewriters of ordinary construction, when so desired, and in which the resetting of the look is dependent upon the actuation of the printing mechanism of the typewriter.
