**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Quiet typebar drives with toggle and swing weight are slow to clear the space in front of the platen on the return stroke, limiting speed.

**Solution:** Make the type rod in two jointed sections so the front, type-bearing part drops out of the printing plane on return and frees the way for the next type.

**How it works:** The key lever draws a toggle that pushes the type rod quickly then slowly against the roller and the swing weight hits a rubber pad silently. On return the front section A' falls away and its upper edge cut-out sits below the path of the other rods. It is raised again later by the lower guide plate. A repeated letter works because the weight's upward motion first lifts the front section. Stroke is about 10 cm.
