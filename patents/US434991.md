## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shifting the Remington paper roll for upper case needs a hand-operated device that can also be held down.

**Solution:** A push-rod and lever shifts the roll carrier; a hook on the rod latches against a rib to hold the upper-case position.

**How it works:** A lever in the base is pushed down by a thumb button on a vertical push-rod. A connecting rod turns a rocker shaft whose arm engages posts on the paper-roll frame, moving the roll rearward to receive the upper-case type. A spring retracts it when released. A hook on the rod catches a rib in a slot to hold it shifted (shift lock); a light push releases it.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md)

**Parts:** lever; push-rod; thumb button; connecting rod; rocker shaft; arm; paper-roll frame; retracting spring; hook; rib

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter: adds latching shift mechanism
