## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters need a compact, durable layout that leaves open space inside for other mechanism, with uniform key touch and column tabulation.

**Solution:** Typebars each carrying three characters in a row, in two groups on pivoted side frames that shift to bring the chosen character to the platen.

**How it works:** Two groups of typebars sit in pivotally mounted frames at each side, leaving a wide central space. Each typebar carries three characters and the middle one strikes unless the frame is moved. Moving the frames selects which of the three prints. An escapement and tabulating device move the platen sideways, with a restraint to keep it from stopping too hard. A separate independent key bank sits under the platen.
