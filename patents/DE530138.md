## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** Each carriage roller on the two longitudinal edges of the paper carriage is mounted on an adjustable holder that can be set perpendicular to the edge, so the rollers can be set independently; rollers alternately bear on the inside of the upper and lower V legs and touch the guide at one point only.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper carriage roller adjustment in V-groove slide guides is impossible or very cumbersome, since the guide rails or the slide must be reset to adjust the rollers.

**Solution:** Each carriage roller on the two longitudinal edges of the paper carriage is mounted on an adjustable holder that can be set perpendicular to the edge, so the rollers can be set independently; rollers alternately bear on the inside of the upper and lower V legs and touch the guide at one point only.

**How it works:** Holders with slots and screws let each castor roller be shifted perpendicular to the carriage; alternating rollers engage the upper and lower legs of the fixed V-shaped guide strips.
