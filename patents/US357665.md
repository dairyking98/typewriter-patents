## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The journals of the radial type-lever shafts need a bearing that is cheap, steady and adjustable.

**Solution:** V-grooves in the pillow blocks hold the shaft journals, and a broad-headed screw between two journals caps both and tightens or loosens the bearing.

**How it works:** Pillow blocks are formed in a circle on a circular face plate. Each type-lever shaft lies between two blocks with its journals in V-shaped grooves cut in the blocks. Between the ends of the two neighbouring journals a screw is tapped into the block, its broad head covering both journals. The groove sides and the screw head touch each journal at three points, holding it steady without side play, and turning the screw adjusts the bearing. Belts and a spring on a crank rock each shaft to move the lever.
