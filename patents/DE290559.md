## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** A feeler-lever-controlled tooth on the drive shaft and a control lever with a reversing screw switch the drive when the ribbon runs out.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reel drive switchover makes the ink ribbon load or jam during switching.

**Solution:** A feeler-lever-controlled tooth on the drive shaft and a control lever with a reversing screw switch the drive when the ribbon runs out.

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
