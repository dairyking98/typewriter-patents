## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (remote selection telegraph) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pulse position modulation; Baseband coding techniques specific to data transmission systems by transition coding, i.e. the time-position or direction of a transition being encoded before transmission

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device in order from a transmitting station to apparatus to operate a receiving station with the help of electrical current surges, which by one or at most two lines and earth return from the former station to the can be 'sent to the latter'.

**How it works (reading):** Positive-polarized and negative-polarized relays at the receiver respond to the sequence and number of current surges sent over one line with earth return, switching local circuits such as lamps and magnets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing selective telegraph systems need many current-surge combinations and depend on line current strength and resistance between the stations.

**Solution:** Positive-polarized and negative-polarized relays at the receiver respond to the sequence and number of current surges sent over one line with earth return, switching local circuits such as lamps and magnets.
