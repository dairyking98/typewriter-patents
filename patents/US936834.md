## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed, reverse, vibrator and two-colour shift mechanisms on a visible-writing typebar machine were complicated, with many parts, and ran the ribbon over direction-changing rollers that added friction.

**Solution:** Drive the ribbon spools straight through a sliding bevel-pinion feed shaft ratcheted by the universal bar's link bar, with the same link bar cam-lifting a hinged ribbon holder and a shift lever to hold it up for the second colour.

**How it works:** Each key stroke swings the universal bar, which draws a link bar forward. A pin on the link bar turns a slotted dog arm that steps a ratchet wheel on a cross feed shaft. Two bevel pinions on the shaft face opposite ways, and sliding the shaft sideways meshes either one with a pinion under one of two spool shafts, so the ribbon winds in either direction without reversing rollers. The spools sit on spring prongs that slip when the ribbon runs out. The link bar's cam head also lifts a hinged lift plate carrying the ribbon holder on each stroke, so the ribbon rises to the type and falls to show the line. A shift lever with a locking button lifts the plate further for a second colour, and a notch in the link bar lets it clear.
