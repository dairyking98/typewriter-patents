## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar and typelever machines need many different shaped parts with individually angled levers, which are costly, delicate and hard to align.

**Solution:** Arrange the type levers and their pivots side by side in a slide that moves along the platen, so each lever can be identical and the slide is moved to the chosen type before printing.

**How it works:** Type levers and pivot devices sit side by side with the keyboard offset one behind the other in a slide that travels along the paper roller. To print, the slide moves right or left until the lever with the desired type is in front of the point of impact. The text then breaks off. The result is few simple parts, light weight and cheapness, with somewhat lower speed.
