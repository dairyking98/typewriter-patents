## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for driving the type lever of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The drive arm of each type lever pivots as one part of a toggle linkage whose other joint is carried by the segment; the knee extends at impact to stop rebound and gives a long lever arm at the start of the stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever drives need a light keystroke, smooth acceleration, and no rebound from the platen in a low-built machine.

**Solution:** The drive arm of each type lever pivots as one part of a toggle linkage whose other joint is carried by the segment; the knee extends at impact to stop rebound and gives a long lever arm at the start of the stroke.
