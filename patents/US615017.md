## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention is directed more particularly to the adaptation of machines of this general character to large record-books;

**How it works (reading):** An open base frame rests on the book and carries the writing mechanism, which moves longitudinally and laterally and prints downward through the frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Solution:** An open base frame rests on the book and carries the writing mechanism, which moves longitudinally and laterally and prints downward through the frame.
