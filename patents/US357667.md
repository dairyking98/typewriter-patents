## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improving the step-by-step feed of the paper carrier in the inventor's radial-typelever machine (application of 13 March 1884).

**Solution:** A universal lever worked by any key swings a spring-driven arm with a dog that steps a toothed bar on the carrier one tooth per keystroke.

**How it works:** The carrier plate has a toothed bar on its front edge; a swinging arm carries a dog engaging the teeth and a spring pulls the arm and carrier leftward. A bell-crank universal lever, moved by a thimble pushed down by a ring of feed levers (one per type lever, resting on the thimble flange and pressed by a spider spring), releases and re-engages the arm so the carrier advances one step per key.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
