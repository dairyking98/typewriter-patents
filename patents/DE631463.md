## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Conductor's tongs (ticket punch-style printers) lack automatic ribbon advance because there is little space and the ribbon spools sit asymmetrically to the pressure yoke.

**Solution:** Pawls on a free leg of a pivoted angle lever advance the ribbon; the other leg hits a stop on the pressure yoke return stroke, and the ribbon switches automatically as the reel thickens, with reels on a plate on the yoke.

**How it works:** The yoke return stroke rotates the angle lever, advancing the ribbon with the pawls; the ribbon moves with the yoke so no extra tension arises.
