## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention are first, to give the operator of the typewriting machine a signal whereby he may know that the bottom of the paper sheet is near;

**How it works (reading):** An orbital paper feeler and rotary indicator move with the sheet to show the bottom position, giving a signal as the sheet ends.
