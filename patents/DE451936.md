## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (slide circuit for calculating and typesetting)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a slide circuit for arithmetic, writing and typesetting measures.

**How it works (reading):** A single spring lever is arranged so the angle to the spring force increases as the carriage moves, keeping the driving force nearly uniform; a latch on a notched toothed strip, linked to the space key, steps the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A spring-driven carriage gives an uneven drive force over its travel, and a stepping device with a toothed strip is hard to operate.

**Solution:** A single spring lever is arranged so the angle to the spring force increases as the carriage moves, keeping the driving force nearly uniform; a latch on a notched toothed strip, linked to the space key, steps the carriage.
