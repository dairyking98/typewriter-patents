## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A two-colour ribbon needs the ribbon vibrator to lift by different amounts, and dirt could leave a prior device in the wrong position.

**Solution:** Two pins on the universal frame enter different holes in the vibrator, giving two throws, selected by keys and shifting with the platen shift rail.

**How it works:** The vibrator is pivoted on the platen shift rail and is moved by a pin frame carried in a housing on the universal bar. A key moves a link and rock arm to slide the pin frame so one of two tapered pins enters a hole nearer to or farther from the pivot. The longer throw raises the lower ribbon colour to the print point, the shorter the upper. The pins move with the shift rail so the ribbon stays correct in both cases.
