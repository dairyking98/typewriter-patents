## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers strike the paper roller with noise and impact.

**Solution:** A fixed stop or anvil stops the type lever just before it touches the platen, and a movable retarder opposite it slows the lever before it reaches the anvil, timed for a quiet stop.

**How it works:** The retarder and fixed anvil are adjusted against each other so the lever is braked before the stop.
