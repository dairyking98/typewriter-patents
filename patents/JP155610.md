## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Printing-cylinder feed on earlier typewriters came in few, coarse steps (1, 2 or 3 mm), unsuitable for types of all sizes, and reversing was awkward.

**Solution:** A pot gear with 73 ball-detented hollows sets the stop for a swinging sector gear, so a key stroke feeds the cylinder one of many very fine distances (around 0.1 mm), with a reverse gear for back-feed.

**How it works:** A switch gear with a ratchet normally holds the main gear on the power shaft; a key swings its pillar so a switch sector gear meshes instead, and the power shaft turns the sector until its tip hits the pillar, feeding the cylinder by that many teeth. The sector's rest position is set by a stop lever on a pot gear (7), held by a spring-loaded ball in one of 73 hollows in three rows and turned by a dial (38) scaled 0 to 73 through a dial gear. On release the sector returns to the stop lever and the switch gear re-meshes. Pressing a reverse key lifts a push rod, tilting a second pillar so reverse gear (16) meshes its feed teeth with the ratchet and turns it back, with a claw lifting the ratchet pawl so it turns easily.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Feed device for the printing cylinder of a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a feed device for the printing cylinder of a typewriter, built as follows. A switch gear and a switch sector gear are mounted on a pillar so that either one can be brought into mesh with a main gear driven together with the power shaft that governs the feed of the typewriter's printing cylinder. A ratchet is fitted on the shaft of the switch gear. The lower end of the switch sector gear, which swings, is linked to a stop lever on a "pot gear" pivoted on a base plate. The pot gear has some tens of pots (hollows), and one of these is engaged by a ball mounted in the base plate to hold the pot gear fixed. A dial showing some tens of different feed distances is linked to the pot gear. A reverse gear is mounted on a pillar so that feed teeth on it can be brought into mesh with the ratchet, and the tip of the switch sector gear lies in the path in which the reverse gear turns.
