## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cutting printed wrapping strips into equal sections at a set point is unreliable when the strip is moved intermittently by grippers.

**Solution:** Feeler fingers enter punched hole marks in the strip, which is backed off until the marks seat against them; scissors then cut at the stationary strip.
