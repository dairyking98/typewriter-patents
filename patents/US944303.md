## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapement should adjust to different operators and speeds and convert between normal and reverse feed.

**Solution:** An adjustable escapement whose timing is regulated, convertible between ordinary and reverse feed, with a lock to hold the escape wheel when the carriage is released.

**How it works:** The escapement wheel acts under a regulated mechanism that the operator can set for speed. A conversion changes ordinary to reverse feed so the carriage runs the other way. When the carriage release is used, a locking device holds the escapement wheel.

**Features:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; escapement dog; adjuster; locking device; carriage
