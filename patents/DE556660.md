## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Address printing plates must be changed by hand: the old embossed plate is removed from its frame and the newly embossed one is inserted.

**Solution:** A plate carriage ejects the embossed plate from the frame, a conveyor moves the new plate from the storage container, and an insertion device places it in the frame, with the index card removed at the same time.
