## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Character-by-character adjustment

**How it works (reading):** Two adjusting means are used: one shifts the cutting-edge carrying rod to set where the cam engages the drive roller, and the other moves a stop finger to set the impact point and end of the drive; resilient stops add cushioning.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjusting the stroke of type levers in power machines with a cam drive was limited to one adjusting screw, so cushioning and impact were hard to set.

**Solution:** Two adjusting means are used: one shifts the cutting-edge carrying rod to set where the cam engages the drive roller, and the other moves a stop finger to set the impact point and end of the drive; resilient stops add cushioning.
