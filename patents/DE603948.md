## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Stamp device with ribbon The invention relates to an improvement of the stamp device described in the main patent 594087, in which locking wheels of the ribbon rolls are operated by a reversible, double-sided barb, which in turn is under the influence of a spring, which comes into effect independently of the downward movement of the stamp. The device described there is characterized in that a multicolored ribbon is guided on both sides of the device at an angle to the vertical in pivotable guide rails. Depending on the swivel position of the rails, the red or black part of the ribbon moves under the stamp roller. The present improvement relates to that in the reference patent to make manually operated pivoting of the ribbon automatically to so to create the possibility, for example continuously stamping alternately black or red. The invention achieves this in that on the pivot axis a device is provided which, when the ram is actuated during the upward movement is taken and so the ribbon swings out or back to its rest position lets go back.
