## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbons and their mechanism are to be eliminated.

**Solution:** Present an individual inking pad to each type by pivoted pad carriers around a spindle that the typebar knocks over and then restores on its return.

**How it works:** A circular plate with hooked peripheral arms sits over a second plate with resilient radial arms, both on a spindle fixed to the frame near the types. Bell-crank inking-pad carriers pivot on a wire in the hooks, each with a pad on one end and a rounded end that the returning typebar cams. A heel on each carrier rides a raised projection on the resilient arm, holding the carrier in either position and pressing the type against the pad to transfer ink. The struck carrier is knocked over and the return stroke sets it upright again. A modified form uses dome plate, springs and rocking carriers.

**Parts:** circular plate; resilient arm; pad carrier; inking pad; spindle; wire pivot; heel; spring
