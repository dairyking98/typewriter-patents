> Description Translated from German Sewing machine furniture with closure hood The invention relates to sewing machine furniture with closing cap for the non-retractable upper part, in which the closing cap used during operation of the sewing machine to enlarge the table area will. The well-known sewing machine furniture of this type consisted of a table of considerably larger dimensions than the base plate of the upper part and one small cap that just covered the top. The locking hood could be attached by hooks on the transverse edge of the table surface and served here as an enlargement of the table surface. The achievable enlargement of the table surface has only been slight. In addition, the hook attachment of the hood to the The transverse edge of the table was relatively awkward.
