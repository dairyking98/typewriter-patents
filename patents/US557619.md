## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen-shift machine's spring-held platen frame can yield under typing strain and move off its normal position.

**Solution:** Add an automatic lock that holds the platen frame at its normal position until released manually when shifting.

**How it works:** The platen frame slides on a carriage at right angles to the platen axis. A shift key turns a rock-shaft whose arms move a rod rearward against two springs, and lugs on the frame arm engage that rod to shift the platen. Previously only a spring held the rod forward and it yielded to strain, so a lock engages the platen frame in its normal position until the key releases it.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen frame; platen; rock-shaft; shift key; link; rod; return spring; lock

**Improves on:**

- spring-only platen shift holding: adds a positive lock so strain cannot move the platen frame
