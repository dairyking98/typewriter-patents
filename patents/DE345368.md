**How it works (reading):** The paper roller can be uncoupled from the switching drive at either end by counter-gear levers or rollers, with simple lathe-made parts and a punched frame for cheap production.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A paper roller that can only be released from one end is awkward to reposition with one hand while the other hand is still typing.

**Solution:** The paper roller can be uncoupled from the switching drive at either end by counter-gear levers or rollers, with simple lathe-made parts and a punched frame for cheap production.
