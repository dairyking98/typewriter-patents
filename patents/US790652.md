## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and has for its main object to provide a support for the paper on which erasures may conveniently be made.

**How it works (reading):** A paper support is fitted to the Monarch paper guiding means so the sheet is held for erasing; it is simple and works with the existing paper guides.

**Solution:** A paper support is fitted to the Monarch paper guiding means so the sheet is held for erasing; it is simple and works with the existing paper guides.
