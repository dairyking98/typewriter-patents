## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in type alining and leveling devices, the main object of the invention being to facilitate the alinement and level adjustment of the type of a type-writer, the invention being particularly applicable to bar type writers.
