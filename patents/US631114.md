## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Aligning and leveling the type faces of a typewriter so each face coincides with the platen face is laborious, and tipped type prints badly.

**Solution:** A transparent gage plate with a cylindro-segmental face and an obscuring film shows by contact whether each type face conforms to the platen surface.

**How it works:** The typewriter is held on an adjustable support with a gage whose face is curved to match the platen at the print point, with several such segments for multiple-shift machines. A transparent plate carries a displaceable or translucent obscuring film on the side toward the type; the type shows through only where its face touches the film in true contact. The operator brings the typebar up, sees the contact pattern, and adjusts the type until the whole face is in evidence. It also detects pits and raised spots, and is said to be usable for type wheels of index-wheel machines and for grinding type blocks.
