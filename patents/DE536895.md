> Description Translated from German Reverse gear clutch for typewriters The invention relates on a line-change gear clutch for typewriters, in which those with a Head provided paper roller is freely rotatably mounted on the roller axis on the the row ratchet wheel is seated, and one of its coupling parts is connected to the ratchet wheel while the other is movably supported by journals seated on the roller head and is guided into the indented position by spring action. In a known line shift wheel clutch of the specified type a plurality of bolts slidably mounted in the roller head, and the inner_ Ends of these bolts carrying the clutch plate are in engagement with springs, which sit in recesses in the roller core. Besides the unfavorable weakening of the Roll head through the recesses provided, the bolts mentioned must be so the clutch plate can move freely, very precisely attached and adjusted will. In contrast, according to the invention, the device is made so that the movable coupling part is carried by pins firmly seated on the roller head and is under the action of a single spring surrounding the roller axis, which lies in a recess in the roller head.
