## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on a line-change gear clutch for typewriters, in which those with a Head provided paper roller is freely rotatably mounted on the roller axis on the the row ratchet wheel is seated, and one of its coupling parts is connected to the ratchet wheel while the other is movably supported by journals seated on the roller head and is guided into the indented position by spring action.

**How it works (reading):** The movable coupling part is carried on pins fixed to the roller head and is pressed by one spring around the roller axis, so the platen roller head is not weakened and the clutch is simpler to make.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A line-spacing clutch that uses sliding bolts in the roller head weakens the roller and needs precise fitting and adjustment of the bolts.

**Solution:** The movable coupling part is carried on pins fixed to the roller head and is pressed by one spring around the roller axis, so the platen roller head is not weakened and the clutch is simpler to make.

**How it works:** A cup-shaped coupling member with fine internal teeth carries the line index ratchet wheel; a triangular plate with outer fine teeth slides on pins and engages it, held by one spring.
