## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing requires the carriage to stop at the decimal-point column of a figure, and the operator cannot easily do this by hand.

**Solution:** A special key releases the carriage, which then stops automatically at tabulator stops set at the decimal-point column or at any chosen point in front of it.

**How it works:** A spring-barrel drives the carriage; a tabulator stop on the rear carriage meets the stop and holds it, with a scale indicating the position.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** spring-barrel; tabulator stops; denomination stop; guide-block; tabulator-scale indicator
