## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writer actionsthat is, to the mechanism for transmitting motion from a key to the corresponding type-and has for its object to provide a novel support for the links which form part of the action, which support operates to hold the said links in place even when their ordinary support or pivot is removed for efiecting the repair or renewal of parts.

**How it works (reading):** A support for the elbow-lever links holds them in place when their ordinary pivot or fulcrum rod is taken out.
