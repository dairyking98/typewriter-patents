## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier typebar actions could hardly swing the typebar through about 180 degrees, or did so at uniform speed, and gave the fingertip an unpleasant, uneven key resistance.

**Solution:** Join the typebar force point to the key lever's rear actuating point by one short link at set angles, so the typebar swings through about two right angles with an angular-speed ratio that starts near zero and rises steeply at the end.

**How it works:** Typebar (1) pivots on wire (2) in a segment slot, with force arm (2)-(3) at about 130 degrees to the line from its pivot to the key-lever pivot (7). Key lever (K) has an actuating arm (7)-(4) several times longer than the force arm. A single link (5) joins force point (3) to actuating point (4), standing at about a right angle to the actuating arm at rest. As the key swings (0 to 22 degrees), the link rotates to about 105 degrees to the actuating arm and about a right angle to the force arm, turning the typebar through about 180 degrees with accelerating speed. Trigger, ribbon device and carriage-stepping parts are worked from the key's rear (hook 19 lifting trigger bar 20) when the speed ratio dips mid-stroke, evening out touch. A return spring (15) on an adjustable holder bar (16, screw 17) sets key feel.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Printing device. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a printing device in which the force point of the typebar and the actuating point at the rear end of the key lever are connected by a single, comparatively short link. At rest the link stands at about a right angle to the actuating arm of the key lever, and the force arm of the typebar makes an angle of about 130 degrees with the straight line joining the typebar pivot and the key-lever pivot. In printing, as the key lever swings, the link comes to an angle of about 105 degrees with the actuating arm, and brings the force arm of the typebar to about a right angle with the link; this turns the force arm, and so the typebar, through about two right angles. The object is to let the typebar turn through about two right angles with a very simple construction, so that the machine can be made small [printed 少] and light, while the key feels soft to the fingertip at the start of the stroke and gives a very good touch from beginning to end.
