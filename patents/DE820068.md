## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed wooden formwork for monolithic masonry is costly and mostly not reusable.

**Solution:** Upright stands are set along the wall at intervals, formwork is fixed to them, and a concrete closing ring bridges the stands to carry the ceiling.

> Description Translated from German (WiGBL p. 175) ISSUED NOVEMBER 8, 1951 B 1054 V 1 37 a is used The invention relates to a method for the production of monolithic masonry between removable formwork and formwork supports for carrying out this process. The invention is primarily concerned with bulk construction, in which, as is known, a masonry material is used that combines sufficient thermal insulation with sufficient strength so that the building does not require any special, frame-like or skeletal supporting structure. As is well known, the economy of bulk construction is highly dependent on the cost of the formwork. With a conventional, fixed wooden formwork, however, these costs are very considerable because of the high expenditure of work, time and material, and there is also the fact that the individual parts of the formwork, including its extensive support structure, are only partially reusable after removal of the formwork. This last-mentioned disadvantage is avoided in the also known wall formwork made of ao steel, but the latter have the disadvantage of a more or less complicated construction and therefore very considerable manufacturing costs.
