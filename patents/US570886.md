## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator wants a double letter space after a word without a separate extra stroke.

**Solution:** Pressing the space key together with the last letter key makes the Smith Premier carriage escape two notches on release.

**How it works:** On a No. 2 Smith Premier, a universal sliding space-bar operates an oscillating spindle with detents allowing the escape wheel one tooth per key. Striking the space-key simultaneously with a letter key adds a second detent action so the rack advances two notches after both keys are released, so the double space is made automatically. The carriage is driven by its spring through gearing to the escape wheel.
