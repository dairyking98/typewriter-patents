## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register individual amounts printing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register printing device, by means of which both individual amounts as well as totals on checks, receipts, etc.

**How it works (reading):** The check is advanced by a ratchet and pawl linked to the amount-key button connector, so it moves only per registered amount, and a numbered plate at the check exit shows the count of items printed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The cashier often forgets an individual amount and cannot easily see how many items are already on the check.

**Solution:** The check is advanced by a ratchet and pawl linked to the amount-key button connector, so it moves only per registered amount, and a numbered plate at the check exit shows the count of items printed.
