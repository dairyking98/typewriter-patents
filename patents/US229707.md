## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in a reel of novel construction, to be used in connection with typewriters, whereby the inkedribbon used with these machines can be more readily removed and replaced without soiling the fingers, and whereby, also, the reel can be made to serve temporarily as a spool, when it is desired to change for a brief period the color of the ink used, all as hereinafter more fully set forth.

**How it works (reading):** A reel of new construction (crank shaft with cross-arm and studs) used with the bed-plate to remove and replace the ribbon, and that can stand in temporarily for one of the ribbon spools.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inked ribbon is hard to remove and replace without soiling the fingers, and a reel cannot easily serve as a spare spool for a colour change.

**Solution:** A reel of new construction (crank shaft with cross-arm and studs) used with the bed-plate to remove and replace the ribbon, and that can stand in temporarily for one of the ribbon spools.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)
