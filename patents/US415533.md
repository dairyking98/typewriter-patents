## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ihe ribbon-holder for type-writers;

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon-holder frame slides horizontally on a dovetail and is moved by a knobbed screw-rod. Ratchet wheels with movable and fixed pawls hold the ribbon spools.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon-holder must be shifted sideways so the whole ribbon surface is used, and the ratchets must not slip off their journals.

**Solution:** A ribbon-holder frame slides horizontally on a dovetail and is moved by a knobbed screw-rod. Ratchet wheels with movable and fixed pawls hold the ribbon spools.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
