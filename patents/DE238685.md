## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor; Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** The star-shaped regulating disc is not rigidly connected to the gear wheel but has a limited independent movement on the ratchet, so the lock takes effect at once when the switch is engaged.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the locking switch of addendum patent 229107, the lock only takes effect after a space key press, because the regulating element is rigidly tied to the gear wheel and may stop on a tooth.

**Solution:** The star-shaped regulating disc is not rigidly connected to the gear wheel but has a limited independent movement on the ratchet, so the lock takes effect at once when the switch is engaged.

**How it works:** A star disc with tips and recesses matching the ratchet teeth is free to turn slightly, so it can settle into a recess and let the locking switch engage immediately.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** star-shaped regulating disc; feeler member; ratchet wheel; gear wheel; paper carriage switching gear
