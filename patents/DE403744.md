**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (tabulating adding machine auxiliary switches) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to one with tabulation equipped writing adding machine, especially intended for bookkeeping, whose Auxiliary facilities such as print prevention, addition prevention, repetition and Subtraction, automatically controlled by the paper trolley, can be switched on and off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching a bookkeeping adding machine between work types needs many manual settings.

**Solution:** Auxiliary functions on the paper carriage are controlled so the same control elements on the carriage are assigned different functions by a switch.
