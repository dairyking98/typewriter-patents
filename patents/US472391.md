## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforating stamp) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to printing, and more especially to the perforating-stamps under that class which are useful for punching checks;

**How it works (reading):** Fifteen spring-raised punches in segments rotate around a die plate, and ratchet operating gear selects each punch.
