## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Amounts printed on the main sheet should also be recorded in a close arrangement on a control strip for checking.

**Solution:** An additional laterally movable paper holder is placed between the main paper carriage and the types so that footer amounts are duplicated on the control strip by carbon copy while the sheets are printed.
