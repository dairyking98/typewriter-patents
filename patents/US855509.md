## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be held with increased pressure against the platen on a front-strike machine, and the paper guide needs a better arrangement.

**Solution:** A thin elastic steel paper guide is pivoted between ears on an adjustable arm, with a slot at the printing point (about two spaces wide) and outturned ends that let the platen and paper fingers pass.

**How it works:** The slotted guide sits just below the printing point; the slot clears the impression while the guide bears on the paper ahead of the platen.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** adjustable arm; pivoted paper guide; slot at printing point
