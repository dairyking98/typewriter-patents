## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The shifter that disengages the carriage rack-bar from the advancing pawls at line end may push unevenly and make the rack-bar hang.

**Solution:** A pair of equal levers and links acting on the rack-bar, driven by a shifting lever that meets a fixed roller, move the bar squarely out of engagement so the retracting spring returns the carriage.

**How it works:** The carriage's toothed rack-bar slides in side guides and is held to the pawls by a central retractile spring. Two levers of equal length are linked to the bar at equal distances from its ends and joined to a shifting lever. When the carriage reaches the fixed obstruction roller, the lever's cam end is diverted, pulling both levers and moving the whole bar sideways clear of the pawls. A head on a pawl-carrying lever then drops behind and holds the shifting lever until released. The machine is an electric typewriter whose motor advances the carriage step by step.
