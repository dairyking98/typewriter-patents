## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines of the day had carriages and feed dogs that could not be readily swapped, and spring-drum mechanism needed improving.

**Solution:** Quickly detachable carriage and feed dogs so interchangeable carriages and dog sets can be substituted, plus a spring-drum feed.

**How it works:** Each typebar works with a toothed slide, an angular lever and a key lever, each removably connected, with a latch that frees all the levers at once. Key-lever spring tension is adjustable by set-screw so side and centre keys feel equal. The carriage and feed dog can be attached or detached, and the carriage is pulled by a spring drum. Opening only; the rest is not shown.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** type-bar; toothed slide; angular lever; key lever; latch; feed dog; spring drum; carriage
