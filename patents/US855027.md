## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the invention is to provide, sim le, inexpensive and reliable means for enabling a release key to throw off the rolls, especially when two roller shafts are employed at different points around the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A release key drives a lever on the rear roller shaft that swings both roller shafts away from the platen, and a pivoted latch on the key holds it depressed until an upward movement of its arm releases it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed rolls pressing paper against the platen must be released so the operator can handle the paper, and the release key should stay down without the operator holding it.

**Solution:** A release key drives a lever on the rear roller shaft that swings both roller shafts away from the platen, and a pivoted latch on the key holds it depressed until an upward movement of its arm releases it.
