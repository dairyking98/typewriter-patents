## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On shuttle machines (Hammond type) the user must remove and replace the shuttle to get another font or language, so only one shuttle's characters are available at a time.

**Solution:** Mount several shuttles end to end around a one-piece circular anvil so they form a ring that oscillates as one, and rotate the group to bring any chosen shuttle into play.

**How it works:** A vertical ring-shaped anvil with a circumferential slot carries two or three curved shuttles in tandem; the anvil arms have clearance openings so the shuttles can travel the full circuit. Each shuttle has a driving fin with a bushing, a selecting fin with a coupling notch, and a retaining fin with a beveled edge; the beveled ends interlock and a clip resists centrifugal thrust. An oscillating driving arm's finger enters the bushing of the shuttle in play and swings the whole group in front of a fixed impression hammer, so the other shuttles ride along without printing. A shuttle-selecting key lever lifts the anvil to uncouple the driving finger, a selecting hook enters the coupling notch of the next shuttle and swings the group 120 degrees, then the anvil settles to recouple. A double-decked anvil with two slots, raised or lowered, holds two groups for many characters, and a three-faced rotating card index shows which shuttle is in play.
