## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line locks and tabulating devices need to be provided in a form that works from a single actuator.

**Solution:** The line lock and tabulating devices are both operated by one actuator; a dog mechanism works with a rack plate having graduated teeth, and an adjustable spring-loaded stop locks the line.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** rack plate; stop; spring; rails; carriage rollers; platen; dogs; universal bar
