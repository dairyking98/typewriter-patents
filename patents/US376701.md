## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar pivots could not be adjusted for wear after the hanger was attached, and connecting rods were hard to detach.

**Solution:** Sheet metal hanger and typebar punched with conical journals, joined by a screw or rivet that sets friction, with a hooked short arm for easy rod removal.

**How it works:** The hanger is a flexible sheet metal strip bent into jaws with punched cone-shaped bearings that meet conical parts of the type-bar. A screw with a conical head and a cross hole for a fine rod is tightened to regulate friction without removing the hanger, or a tubular rivet can be re-swaged. The type-bar short arm ends in a hook and the connecting rod has spring fingers with a pin so it can be removed readily.
