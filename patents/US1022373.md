## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to paper feeding devices for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Two individually adjustable tension springs on the shaft that carries the roll-supporting arms set the pressure of the feed rolls against the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The pressure of the forward and rear paper feed rolls against the platen is hard to regulate.

**Solution:** Two individually adjustable tension springs on the shaft that carries the roll-supporting arms set the pressure of the feed rolls against the platen.
