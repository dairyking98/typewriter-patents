## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to typewriting machines, and more particularly to a series of stops which may be supported on the typewriter carriage and are adapted to be selectively set into position to cooperate with counter-stops.

**How it works (reading):** Stops are set in vertically movable rack bars with internal teeth; spring-pressed balls seat in side notches to hold each stop in any position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage stops must be set easily and must hold firmly against the lateral strain of engaging counter-stops.

**Solution:** Stops are set in vertically movable rack bars with internal teeth; spring-pressed balls seat in side notches to hold each stop in any position.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
