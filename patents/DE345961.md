## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a Myriograph-type duplicator with a reciprocating pressure roller, the ribbon must be advanced after each pass so it is not always struck in the same place; existing pawl-and-ratchet feeds are crude.

**Solution:** Cam-operated double levers, tripped by the roller axle on each pass, drive pawls on ratchet wheels that wind a main ribbon across the type set and auxiliary ribbon strips along it.

**How it works:** A hand crank turns gears on the roller axle, which roll on racks and carry the pressure roller over the ribbon-covered type plate. The axle hits cam projections on a pivoted double lever on each guide, rocking it. Its arms carry adjustable pawls that step ratchet wheels on two spool axles, winding the ribbon onto one spool and off the other. Only the pawls on one side are active at a time; they are swapped when a spool is full. A further pawl on each lever steps a ratchet carrying guide rollers for the auxiliary ribbon strips, so these advance once per full back-and-forth stroke.
