## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements of selective printing mechanisms, e.g. ink-jet printers or thermal printers, specially adapted for supporting or handling copy material in short lengths, e.g. sheets

**How it works (reading):** Two sets of feed rollers and endless belts advance the outer sheets at a different speed from the platen so the inner sheets slip behind, with equal sheet widths.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Invoice copies under the top sheet needed to be seen and spaced differently from the original.

**Solution:** Two sets of feed rollers and endless belts advance the outer sheets at a different speed from the platen so the inner sheets slip behind, with equal sheet widths.
