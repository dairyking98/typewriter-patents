## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing needs to be easier, faster, simpler and cheaper.

**Solution:** A permutational letter selector controlled by few keys, with two sets of typebars to print two letters simultaneously.

**How it works:** Two arcs of typebars each strike a common printing point, adjacent on the line. Two key sets are pressed together, each driving its own permutational selector through push-ups equidistant from each other, so single keys and combinations choose among many letters. A releasing mechanism lets the bars return though keys remain held. The selector is also shown operating linotype matrix escapements.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Keys that only set the element](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** typebar set; key set; push-up; permutational selector; release mechanism; fulcrum; linotype escapement

**Improves on:**

- [US600120](../patents/US600120.md): simpler, cheaper mechanism
