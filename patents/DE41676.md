## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter with few moving parts that places any of 49 characters over one print point using a small keyboard.

**Solution:** A rail carrying all characters is swung horizontally by a spring-driven axis whose travel is limited by a row of plates whose total length is set by combinations of six keys.

**How it works:** The type rail B with letters, numerals and signs on its underside hangs on an arm on axis C. Six keys each raise a wedge between two of seven small square steel plates in a row; the combination changes the row length and thus how far a pin on axis C can turn under its spiral spring. The rail then stops with the chosen sign exactly over fixed point A. At that moment a pin at A rises (driven by a rail Q on a frame moved by the keys) and presses the paper and carbon strip against the character. A paper roller frame on rails is stepped by a ratchet and fork each stroke.
