The invention is based on a drive device of the type in which the two arms of a lever arm belonging to the drive parts, its one leg is formed by the support arms of the universal rail, in different large angles to each other are adjustable, creating different sized ribbon strokes develop. The essence of the invention is that the adjustment of the arms of the Angle lever is done by an eccentric.
