## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing is tiring.

**Solution:** A keyboard device placed over a typewriter's keys with electromagnets pressing each key.

**How it works:** A casing with keys moves magnet-driven rods to press the typewriter's keys.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** casing; key; electromagnet; armature; connecting rod
