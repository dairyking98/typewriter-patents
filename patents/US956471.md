## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage return spring fitted to the carriage weakens the feed spring and gives uneven work over the stroke.

**Solution:** A resilient cord or coil runs the length of the frame, is guided round a segment, and is pulled down by a key-lever arm, so the return force is applied from the front lever with near-uniform work.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** feeding spring; resilient cord; grooved segment; arm and shaft; key lever
