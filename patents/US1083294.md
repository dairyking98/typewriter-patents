## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (recording machine paper feed) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for tensioning or winding the web

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to paper feeding mechanism for recording machines, and has for an object to provide an improved paper feeding mechanism whereby the paper will be automatically fed to the recording mechanism when the machine is operated, and then re-wound onto a suitable roller whereby the paper will be supported and prevented from becoming torn or defaced.

**How it works (reading):** A roller automatically rewinds the paper as it leaves the recording mechanism, so it can be fed again.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Recording paper should not be torn or defaced after printing, and it cannot be reused without rewinding.

**Solution:** A roller automatically rewinds the paper as it leaves the recording mechanism, so it can be fed again.

**How it works:** Paper passes the recording point and is wound on a secondary roll that can be reversed for reuse.
