## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (coin-operated time-limit device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus which can be set and started to measure-off predetermined or adjustably-fixed time intervals with driving mechanisms, e.g. dosimeters with clockwork with mechanical driving mechanisms using electrical contacts, e.g. for actuating electro-acoustic device; Clock train

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to coin-operated, time-controlled devices for limiting the use of typewriting and other machines or appa- -ratus, and is in the nature of an improvementupon the invention disclosed in United States application of Rice and Broughton, filed May 22, 1909.

**How it works (reading):** A latch independent of the clockwork holds a locking arm that keeps the key-locking bar in place; a coin releases it, and a clock trips the latch to relock the machine after a set period.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A coin-timed typewriter lock can trip by accident from jarring, and the latch needs to be easy to trip.

**Solution:** A latch independent of the clockwork holds a locking arm that keeps the key-locking bar in place; a coin releases it, and a clock trips the latch to relock the machine after a set period.
