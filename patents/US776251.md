## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A machine on a desk or cabinet is loud, and a rubber mat compresses under the foot and stops muffling.

**Solution:** A rigid iron disk with a felt pad is clamped under each foot of the machine, spreading the load so the felt stays thick and muffles noise.
