## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In pneumatic typewriters with a rotating type wheel, a double-alphabet wheel with two escapements is hard to align and can print out of key order; a single alphabet with two escapements needs high speed, uses much air and the stop is loud.

**Solution:** A single-alphabet type wheel geared 1:2 to the air distributor's ratchet wheel, with key tubes arranged over only a semicircle, so each half turn of the distributor gives a full wheel turn and prints in key order.

**How it works:** Tubes to the keys end in two staggered rows over half the circle of an air distributor; a cylinder rotating in it routes air through diametral bores to two membranes that operate escapements. The cylinder shaft carries a ratchet wheel with an internal ring gear driving the type wheel via a gear at 1:2, so the type wheel turns once per half turn and every type passes the print point. Since only openings on one side meet the compressed-air tubes in turn, types print in the order played. Ratios of 1:3 or 1:4 if the tubes cover a third or fourth of the circle. A fixed-type wheel driven by crown gears and pressed to a roller by a pressure device is also shown (Fig. 4), which lessens impact noise.
