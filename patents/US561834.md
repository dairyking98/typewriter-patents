## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type machines need many characters from few keys.

**Solution:** Mount the ring of typebars, each carrying three types, so it moves vertically to bring the chosen type to the print point; one-third of keys do not shift it.

**How it works:** A circular frame carrying the typebars rides vertically in the main frame standards. Each bar has three types, so fourteen bars give forty-two characters. Fourteen finger keys act directly, fourteen shift the frame to the middle type and fourteen to the outermost, via divided rods with turnbuckles. A spring-roll carriage with clutch and an inclined bar for line spacing complete the machine.

**Features:** [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** circular frame; typebar; finger key; key lever; divided rod; turnbuckle; spring roll; clutch; inclined line-space bar

**Numbers:** 14 typebars; 3 types per bar; 42 characters
