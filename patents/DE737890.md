## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening devices embodied in machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method for reducing noise in writing, writing calculators and similar machines by reducing the frequency or amplitude of the natural vibrations of the type lever.

**How it works (reading):** A firmly bonded lead coating is applied to type levers, graded in thickness for each lever, to lower the natural frequency and amplitude of the lever vibration.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Clinking noise from the natural vibration of type levers is a long-known problem, and earlier attempts to change their stiffness or mass did not solve it.

**Solution:** A firmly bonded lead coating is applied to type levers, graded in thickness for each lever, to lower the natural frequency and amplitude of the lever vibration.
