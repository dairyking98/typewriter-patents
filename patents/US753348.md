## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The printing position of each type cannot be adjusted to suit construction variations or repair, and existing adjustment adds friction.

**Solution:** A type-bar plate carries an adjusting screw and mechanism that alters the horizontal and vertical printing position of each type with little added friction.
