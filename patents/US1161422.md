## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The depth of the bottom margin is hard to judge from the paper edge.

**Solution:** A collapsible indicator bracket fixed to the carriage holds rods graduated in scale; the paper edge is read against the rods to find the depth of the bottom margin.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** graduated rods; carriage bracket
