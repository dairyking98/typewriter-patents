## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**How it works (reading):** The corrugated hollow cylinder sector is clamped or spring-loaded to the platen, covering more than half its circumference, and can be fitted and removed easily.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A corrugated pad for non-erasable writing rests only by its weight on the platen and shifts sideways.

**Solution:** The corrugated hollow cylinder sector is clamped or spring-loaded to the platen, covering more than half its circumference, and can be fitted and removed easily.

**How it works:** Spring-loaded or articulated sectors nest around the roller so the pad cannot move.
