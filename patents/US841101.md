## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**How it works (reading):** A music type-writer with a type-disk, a stop-lever, a paper-carriage that shifts for staff spacing and a device that traces the staff lines, so notes and signs can be placed in many positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriters print in one line, so music notation with notes and staff lines cannot be written easily.

**Solution:** A music type-writer with a type-disk, a stop-lever, a paper-carriage that shifts for staff spacing and a device that traces the staff lines, so notes and signs can be placed in many positions.

**Features:** [USE:special-script](../features/use-special-script.md), [ELEM:disc-face](../features/elem-disc-face.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** type-disk; stop-lever; paper-carriage; staff-tracing device; return spring
