## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A copy sheet must not touch the inked ribbon except at the moment of impression.

**Solution:** A platen roller carries raised marginal rings, and a wide carbon ribbon fills the space between them; the copy sheet rides on the rings and only meets the carbon at the striking point.
