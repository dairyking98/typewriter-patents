## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (word counter on typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Word counters

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for counting the words written by a typewriter, its counter activated by pressing the space key on the typewriter .

**How it works (reading):** A pin-wheel counter driven by a lever on the space key advances one pin per word; a stop lever between two pins prevents overrotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Counting the words written on a typewriter page is done by hand, which is tedious and error-prone.

**Solution:** A pin-wheel counter driven by a lever on the space key advances one pin per word; a stop lever between two pins prevents overrotation.
