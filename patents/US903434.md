## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin and line-lock stops on front-strike machines need a simple, adjustable way to be set and locked at chosen points on the carriage.

**Solution:** A dovetail stop bar runs under the carriage and carries a scale; a margin stop and a line-lock stop slide on the bar, each with a dovetail groove and a looking member with spring, so they can be set to the scale and locked in place.
