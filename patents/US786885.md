## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The presser roll and spring that hold paper on the platen cannot be relieved when the operator wants to shift the paper.

**Solution:** A release lever swings the presser-spring frame away against the coil spring, withdrawing the pressure from platen and paper.

**How it works:** Presser rolls ride in swinging frames on the platen frame's front rod, drawn toward the platen by a coil spring whose tension is set by an adjusting screw. A release lever pivoted on the roll frame has an offset behind a lug on the spring frame; turning it forward pushes the frame away so roll and spring leave the platen. A heel limits travel and a spring washer holds the lever in position. Returning the lever lets the coil spring reapply the pressure.
