## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (bottle prick guard) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for discouraging or indicating unauthorised opening or removal of closure

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to that class of devices which are provided with pricking-points and applied to bottles containing poisons or hurtf ul materials, so that when the bottles are thoughtlessly grasped, or when grasped in the dark, the points will prick the fingers, thus calling attention to the character of its contents.
