## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The operator must lift the hand from the keyboard to return the carriage, which slows typing.

**Solution:** A lever on an oscillating shaft at the keyboard side turns a segmental gear that, with a pinion, drum and flexible tape, draws the carriage back in a single lateral hand movement. The device attaches to an existing typewriter.
