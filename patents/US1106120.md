## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making an invoice with connected copies required inserting several sheets and separating copies.

**Solution:** An attachment feeds a paper roll and several ribbons or carbon layers through a movable frame so original and copies form a connected strip.

**How it works:** A paper roll on supports travels with the carriage with a drag bar held by springs. Ink ribbon spools with ratchets and cranks at each end of the platen pass several ribbons in front of the print point. A vertically movable L-shaped frame carries idler rolls around which the paper doubles back between the ribbons and typewriter ribbon, and discharge rolls with a crank eject the finished sheets. The frame is lifted each line and dropped by latches on carriage return.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** paper roll; drag bar; ribbon spool; ratchet wheel; pawl; ribbon guide; movable frame; idler roll; discharge roll; crank; latch

**Numbers:** two ribbons shown on a single spool; guide fold about 45 degrees

**Improves on:**

- separate sheet invoice insertion: connected roll and ribbons produce copies in one pass
