## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A Japanese-script typewriter should be easy to operate and print accurately with a simple, strong mechanism.

**Solution:** Types are strung on rods inside cases arranged in a cylindrical type drum; pressing the print lever pushes a case out and lowers it, and a fixed raising piece swings the wanted type up onto the paper.

**How it works:** Many cases (2), each holding swinging types (1) strung on a rod (14), form a type drum (3) journalled in side plates above the printing plate (5); an index strip (19) on the front plate shows the types. The operator turns or shifts the drum sideways to bring the wanted type over a type-raising piece (7) in the middle of the support plate. Pressing handle (54) turns the print lever (9) and operating lever (27): the first arm (28) turns a cam plate (22) whose link and rocking lever (30) drive the push-out piece (10), pushing the case into the transverse groove (11) of the transport plates, where its end catches (18) seat in U-shaped supports (42) on a push-down cross bar (41). The second arm (38) then turns a bent lever (23), whose arc lever (39) lowers the cross bar along the vertical grooves (12), so the case descends and the raising piece swings the wanted type up to print on the paper by pressure. A tension spring (36) and the retreat of the lever return the parts, and a link on the lever's rear end feeds the printing plate.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a Japanese-script typewriter in which many "cases", each holding types strung side by side so that they can swing up and down, are arranged in a cylinder to form a type drum. The drum is journalled in two side plates and mounted on a support plate set above the printing plate. A type-raising piece stands at the centre of the front of the support plate. Transport grooves for the cases are made in the side plates, and a case push-out piece linked to the printing lever is fitted. Pressing the printing lever down pushes a type case out and lowers it along the transport grooves of the side plates, and meanwhile a type in the case is swung up and prints on the printing plate. The object is a Japanese-script typewriter that is easy to operate and prints accurately. Brief description of the drawings.
