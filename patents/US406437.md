## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Seeks to simplify and cheapen the typewriter while enhancing its efficiency.

**Solution:** A type head with radial grooved portions carrying series of type is slid along a rack bar carriage, indexed by a notched plate, and depressed onto a pivoted platen bed.

**How it works:** A base of two plates with a slot between holds a pivoted platen or bed which carries a rack-bar with a thumb disk and pinion to move the paper clips. Four standards support a toothed longitudinal bar on which a type carriage rides in blocks. A transverse shaft in the blocks carries the type head, whose radial portions are grooved to hold a series of type, with inclined arms to support it when depressed. An index plate with a notched or graduated front edge and a scale of characters matching the head's series lies on the carriage and is held by spring ears. A dog on the shaft engages the plate notches, the rest of the mechanism being cut off in the text.
