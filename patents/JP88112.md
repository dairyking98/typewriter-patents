## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Japanese typewriters with many loose types risk damaged or soiled type faces, types falling or shifting out of their cases, and unclear print.

**Solution:** A hand-turned type drum holds type cases whose faces are covered by swinging index plates; the key lifts the wanted case, flips its index plate away, and a surround and pin through the type grip it and swing it against the paper cylinder.

**How it works:** Many long type cases, each with supports at both ends, lie in radial slots in two side discs of a hand-turned type drum; the supports ride in grooves of fixed plates so cases cannot fall out. The operator turns the drum and slides an L-shaped frame carrying a pointer sideways until the wanted index character is under the pointer. Pressing the key first lifts the chosen case by a clamp lever, then swings its index plate forward to uncover the type faces, then moves an ink roller across the face. A U-shaped surround swings over the wanted type and a pin slides through the surround and a hole in the type, holding it by its centre; the surround, pin and type then swing up together and print on the paper cylinder.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a Japanese typewriter characterised as follows. Index plates bearing index characters are fitted so that they can swing; type cases holding many types, with supports formed at both ends, are arranged in a cylinder on the side plates of a type drum; beside the side plates, stationary plates are fixed with guide grooves that carry the case supports; in part of each guide groove a clamp, formed on a lever pivoted on the stationary plate and linked to the printing key, is set so that it can move; above the clamp a rocker is pivoted so that it can swing forward. The clamp lifts the wanted case and at the same time the rocker swings the index plate forward off the type faces; a surround mounted behind the drum is fitted over the wanted type in the case, and a pin is pushed through the surround and the type, and the type is printed. Its object is a machine of simple construction in which characters are easily chosen, the type faces are not at risk of damage, and the types do not come out or shift in use, so that the print is clear.
