## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The main objects of my present invention is to provide a construction whereby a cylindrical type-writer platen may be used either alone when printing is to be done upon paper or in conjunction with an attachable card-holder when printing is to be done upon cards;
