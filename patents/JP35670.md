## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Printing the same character on many sheets takes repeated selection, and the aim is to select once and print it quickly on a large number of sheets.

**Solution:** A hand-pushed row of types is selected on a fan-shaped character board and struck upward electrically, while a wheel of paper cylinders turns step by step so the same character prints on each sheet in turn.

**How it works:** Types sit in flexible spring-sided type boxes laid out radially as a fan under a character board; a handle pushes a row forward until the wanted type reaches the printing point, where parallel walls straighten the bent box. A push button on a hand grip energises an electromagnet that swings a lever and hammer to strike the type up against the paper through the ribbon. A second button turns the paper wheel by a step to the next sheet, so printing and turning alternate; after a full turn a bell sounds and a further button moves the wheel lengthwise for the letter space. At line end another bell sounds and a handle turns all the take-up shafts together by one line space.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)
