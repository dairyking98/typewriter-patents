## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letter-spacing on typebar machines suits neither fast nor slow operators.

**Solution:** A spacing rocker carrying two pivoted dogs that engage the feed rack with a tripping lever so spacing happens on release as well as on depress, for rapid as well as slow typing.

**How it works:** A spacing rocker is pivoted at its ends and driven from the key levers through a universal bar and connecting rods. Its dog holder carries a front and rear dog on springs; their overlapping projections allow the rear dog to carry the front dog, and a stop limits their travel under the carriage-drive spring. Tripping lever positions change which dog engages the feed rack so the feed occurs in two stages. Description truncated.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** spacing rocker; dog holder; front dog; rear dog; stop; universal bar; feed rack; tripping lever; connecting rod
