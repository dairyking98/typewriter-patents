## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanisms do not reverse easily and the ribbon hides the character being printed.

**Solution:** A reversible ribbon feed on two spools, with a depressing device that moves the central ribbon from in front of the character so the operator can see it.

**How it works:** Each spool shaft carries a pawl mechanism and a ribbon clamp so the ribbon winds in either direction; a depressing device pushes the ribbon centre aside.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spools; pawl mechanism; ribbon clamp; depressing device
