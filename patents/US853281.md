## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Independently operable denomination keys in a tabulating mechanism must stand in regular denominational order even though the bails that carry their stops are of graduated sizes and cannot all sit in one row.

**Solution:** Denomination stops are carried on bails nested in two sets, with the key-bearing arms fanned out on each side so the keys stand in a row ordered by denomination, the keys of the highest denomination at the left.
