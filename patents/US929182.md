## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper table needs a simpler way to be mounted, detached, and spring-returned to its normal position after being swung forward.

**Solution:** The paper table is pivoted on brackets that clamp by headed screws to lugs on the supporting rod, with a frame on the table giving spring pressure that returns it to normal when released.
