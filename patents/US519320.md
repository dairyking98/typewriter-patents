## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Uniform letter spacing distorts characters and wastes space for narrow letters.

**Solution:** A graduated stop wheel, turned by the key lever to a position set by the character, limits the slip rack's travel on the fixed rack to give variable spacing.

**How it works:** Racks have double teeth; a dog engages the slip rack and the fixed rack alternately as a key is pressed and released. A graduated bar under the key levers has projections matching character widths and turns a rock shaft, crown-gear segment and pinion, turning a stop wheel so that a different part of its periphery lies opposite a projection on the fixed rack. This limits how far the slip rack moves and so how far the carriage advances. Logotypes for short words are also contemplated.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** fixed rack; slip rack; letter-space dog; graduated stop wheel; rock shaft; crown gear segment; graduated bar; key lever

**Numbers:** racks with twice as many teeth as usual

**Improves on:**

- uniform spacing on Caligraph: adds variable stop on slip rack
