## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter, typewriter or similar machine, in which the platen with its paper guiding devices is in the switch position by a switch key and by a special device with the paper guiding devices, which are made up of front and rear levers arranged on common Paper guide rollers and a paper guide plate consist, from which the base can be raised into an insertion position, according to patent 593927.

**How it works (reading):** Resiliently pivoted rear rollers stay on the platen, and lugs swing the paper guide plate so the card runs tangentially onto the rollers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rear paper guide rollers lose contact with the platen when the guide is lifted, and the card can hit and damage them.

**Solution:** Resiliently pivoted rear rollers stay on the platen, and lugs swing the paper guide plate so the card runs tangentially onto the rollers.
