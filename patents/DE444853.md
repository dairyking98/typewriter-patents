## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register check drive)

**How it works (reading):** Two drive levers are optionally engaged by adjusting elements and the pressure foot lever, so one check gets a single print and the conveyor roller is switched off before its second edge acts, while two checks get a double pressure with both edges working.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A cash register must issue either one check or two, and the single-check case should not run the second conveyor edge.

**Solution:** Two drive levers are optionally engaged by adjusting elements and the pressure foot lever, so one check gets a single print and the conveyor roller is switched off before its second edge acts, while two checks get a double pressure with both edges working.

**Features:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md)
