## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates toA attachments for typewrit-ing machines and has particular reference to an attachment constituting indicating means whereby the operator may determine at a glance the position of the paper.

**How it works (reading):** A gage plate at the platen with a movable gage member shows the paper position and margin while writing, and lets a removed sheet be replaced accurately.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operator cannot tell at a glance where the paper sits or how much bottom margin remains, and sheets are hard to reinsert straight after removal.

**Solution:** A gage plate at the platen with a movable gage member shows the paper position and margin while writing, and lets a removed sheet be replaced accurately.
