## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Address plates need a hole beside each digit so that auxiliary calculating machines can be controlled.

**Solution:** Several rows of rotating discs carry embossing punches offset radially, so each digit gets its own hole at a distance equal to its value.
