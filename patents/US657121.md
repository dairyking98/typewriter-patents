## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing platens for book and flat-sheet typewriters were a heavy flat metal plate with a rubber face, which was expensive, noisy under key strokes and lacked rigidity.

**Solution:** A lightweight but strong platen body, built with a core and a rubber-covered sheath, that is cheaper to make and deadens the sound of the keys.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
