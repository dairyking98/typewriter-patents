## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing mechanism of that class in which the paper carriage has an intermittent, rectilinear move* ment, for the letter-space, while the papercylinder mounted on said carriage receives a rotary feed, after the completion of each line, to form the line-space.
