## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing mechanism of that class in which the paper carriage has an intermittent, rectilinear move* ment, for the letter-space, while the papercylinder mounted on said carriage receives a rotary feed, after the completion of each line, to form the line-space.

**How it works (reading):** Replace the spacing rack with a different letter-space mechanism that works with an automatic carriage release and return triggered by the last letter-space or space-key stroke of the line.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The usual spacing rack on Caligraph and Remington style carriages is not suited to automatic release and return of the carriage at the end of a line.

**Solution:** Replace the spacing rack with a different letter-space mechanism that works with an automatic carriage release and return triggered by the last letter-space or space-key stroke of the line.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on a cylinder-shift rail and a hinged rod, with small rolls in slots reducing friction. Instead of a rack, a novel feed gives the intermittent letter-space. The last letter-space movement after the final impression, or a space-key stroke at that point, trips devices that first disengage the spacing mechanism, then let the carriage return automatically, and finally re-engage the spacing so the next line can be typed. No separate release key is needed. The rest of the machine (line-space lever, bell, pointer) is as in the Caligraph.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen cylinder; cylinder-shift rail; hinged rod; eye-bar; support roll; space key; spacing devices; bell

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph: drops the spacing rack and adds automatic line-end release and return
- Remington: same
