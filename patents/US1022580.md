## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage jumps or rebounds past the margin stop after impact, leaving an uneven left margin.

**Solution:** A latch on a lever hooks under a frame catch at the moment of carriage impact, locking the escapement members together so the carriage cannot skip forward.
