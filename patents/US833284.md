## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen shifting from lower to upper case must be simple, easy and durable, and the upper case should be held without effort.

**Solution:** A key-operated platen shift that automatically locks the platen in upper case and releases it by another key.

**How it works:** The platen is carried by side plates on a carriage that rides on rails; a platen-shifting roller on a side frame works against an auxiliary rail to raise it. One key-bar raises and locks the platen in upper case, and another releases it.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md)

**Parts:** platen; side plate; side frame; track bar; shifting roller; auxiliary rail; key-bar
