## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators release the denomination key before the carriage finishes its run over a long column gap, so the carriage re-engages early and the number is written in the wrong place.

**Solution:** A single stroke of the selected denomination key projects a stop and releases the carriage, which runs to the correct column and is arrested there without further attention from the operator.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
