## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Sending telegraph messages by hand is laborious and error-prone, and leaves no record.

**Solution:** An attachment that sends Morse signals as the typewriter key levers are struck, so each letter is printed and transmitted at once.

**How it works:** Hard-rubber insulating brackets on each side carry a transverse shaft with one collar per key lever. Each collar's periphery is shaped to the Morse symbol for its letter. When a key lever moves, a motor or mechanical link rotates the shaft, and the shaped collar opens and closes a circuit in series with a telegraph instrument. The attachment can be disconnected so either machine works independently.

**Features:** [USE:telegraph-printer](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** bracket; shaft; coded collar; key lever; typebar; universal bail; telegraph instrument
