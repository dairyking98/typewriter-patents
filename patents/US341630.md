## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbons run dry and have to be taken off the reels to be re-inked.

**Solution:** A detachable frame clamped to the machine carries a felt-covered perforated ink roll and a guide roll so the ribbon is inked in place by winding it back and forth.

**How it works:** A metal roller frame with a clamp screw attaches to the typewriter frame beside the ribbon. The ink roll is a perforated tubular ink reservoir enclosed in a porous felt cover. A loop of ribbon is passed over the ink roll and under a guide roll, and turning the reel draws the ribbon across the roll. When not inking, the roll gets a tube cover and the rolls can serve as ribbon guides.
