## Summary

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a drive device for power-driven Small machines, such as typewriters, calculating machines, etc.

**How it works (reading):** Each key lever moves into the coupling member path of its drive disc, and a lever swings the disc back near the end of one rotation to release it; a key-lock stop prevents further keys during the cycle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Single-turn clutches on drive shafts can be left engaged or coupled in several places when the machine is idle.

**Solution:** Each key lever moves into the coupling member path of its drive disc, and a lever swings the disc back near the end of one rotation to release it; a key-lock stop prevents further keys during the cycle.
