## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon is exposed to vapours of a hydrocarbon that dissolves the ink, so the solvent soaks in and capillary action redistributes ink to the worn areas.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Worn ink ribbons are discarded although the fabric is still sound, and earlier refreshing methods gave uneven or dirty results.

**Solution:** The ribbon is exposed to vapours of a hydrocarbon that dissolves the ink, so the solvent soaks in and capillary action redistributes ink to the worn areas.
