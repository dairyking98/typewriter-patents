## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The alining scale bar must be moved into the print line when the feed rolls disengage, so it is not left a space below the line of print.

**Solution:** Feed rolls are mounted on shafts in elbow levers; a rock shaft with notches holds them engaged or disengaged, and disengaging the feed rolls automatically moves the alining scale bar to the printing line.

**How it works:** A hand lever rocks the shaft via a circular rack; the notches on the shaft let the levers ride clear, and the scale bar follows the feed rolls.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** scale bar moved to print line; rock shaft with notches
