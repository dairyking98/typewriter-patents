## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon fastener between support arms by means of clamping pieces, especially for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The ends of a short ribbon are glued into a cheap paper clamp piece that is folded into an eyelet, which hooks onto the support arms of the paper carriage so the ribbon sits between the sheets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Short duplicating ribbons need metal end clamps, which cost money and soil the operator's hands.

**Solution:** The ends of a short ribbon are glued into a cheap paper clamp piece that is folded into an eyelet, which hooks onto the support arms of the paper carriage so the ribbon sits between the sheets.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; paper clamp piece; eyelet; support arms on paper carriage
