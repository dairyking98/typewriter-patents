## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**How it works (reading):** A card-holding strip fastens at its ends to any of several fasteners on the platen heads, so the wear is spread around the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card holders wear the platen at one spot when used repeatedly.

**Solution:** A card-holding strip fastens at its ends to any of several fasteners on the platen heads, so the wear is spread around the platen.
