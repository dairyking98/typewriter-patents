## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**How it works (reading):** A roller mounted in the end of the rigid dog bears on the escape-wheel teeth, so the release contact is rolling rather than sliding.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement release needs more speed and less load on the finger-pieces, and the escapement surfaces wear.

**Solution:** A roller mounted in the end of the rigid dog bears on the escape-wheel teeth, so the release contact is rolling rather than sliding.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
