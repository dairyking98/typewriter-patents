## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Writing machines need an intermittent motion that can be thrown out of engagement and adjusted in extent.

**Solution:** A feed mechanism with a rocker-block and dog-block on a trunnion plate gives intermittent movement, which can be released for general release and whose extent can be adjusted.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** rocker-block; dog-block; trunnion-plate; bearing-yoke; escapement parts
