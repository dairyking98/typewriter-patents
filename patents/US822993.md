## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing machines lacked a simple automatic ribbon reverse that also lets the spools be removed for renewal.

**Solution:** A lever on each spool spindle, attached to the ribbon end, is pulled by the ribbon when fully unwound and shifts the drive shaft into gear with the other spool.

**How it works:** A driving shaft carries two gears and moves endwise so that one or the other meshes with the gear on a spool spindle. Each spindle holds a pivoted lever fixed to the ribbon end. When the ribbon unwinds fully its pull swings the lever so its end hits a collar on the shaft and shifts the shaft to engage the other spool, rewinding the ribbon. The lever also serves as the ribbon attachment, so the spool is easily removed. A hand crank can also turn the shaft.
