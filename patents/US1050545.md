## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting'machines of the' type' bar class, and has for its main object to provide improved constructions of type bars and type bar hangers for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Type bar hangers giving exceptionally wide bearings, the bars spaced equally in an arc of 120 degrees or less on a segmental support, and bars removable individually without disturbing adjoining bars.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars and hangers on a visible writer have narrow bearings, and individual bars cannot be removed without disturbing their neighbours.

**Solution:** Type bar hangers giving exceptionally wide bearings, the bars spaced equally in an arc of 120 degrees or less on a segmental support, and bars removable individually without disturbing adjoining bars.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)
