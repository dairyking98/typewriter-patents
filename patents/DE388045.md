## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When several brake actuation forces act at once, their sum can exceed what the structure can take.

**Solution:** A differential gear (lever, roller, planetary, screw or hydraulic) sums two or more input forces at one common point so the resultant stays no larger than a single force.
