## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Teleprinter type wheels must be stopped and the letter printed by a complicated manual device.

**Solution:** When the type wheel stops, a falling weight in a spoke tube drops to the letter's angular position and pivots its type lever, and an electromagnet resets it.

> Description Translated from German Eri electrical teletypewriters are already known, with those on the transmitting station both as well as on the receiving station a shaft with type wheel when switching on of an electric current is set in uniform rotation. With these devices the rotation of the shafts can be interrupted by pressing a button on the transmitting station, whereupon a device for Printing a letter is pressed. In these devices there is a disadvantage that the device for printing the Letters are very complicated and awkward to use. These deficiencies are remedied by the subject matter of the present invention by a device is created by means of which a type lever when the type wheel is at a standstill is moved in such a way that the letter to be marked is automatically printed.
