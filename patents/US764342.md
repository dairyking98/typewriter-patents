## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon storage in the receptacle is uneven, so the ribbon cannot be withdrawn uniformly.

**Solution:** Endless ribbon is fed into a storage receptacle by two rollers placed so the ribbon lays in even layers.
