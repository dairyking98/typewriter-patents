## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon wound between two rollers, the rear roller spring-driven through a gear train to take up ribbon, driven with the platen lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon must be moved back and forth by hand alongside the platen lever.

**Solution:** Ribbon wound between two rollers, the rear roller spring-driven through a gear train to take up ribbon, driven with the platen lever.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
