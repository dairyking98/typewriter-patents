## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator ribbon color printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** A four-pivot double lever formed from the outside of an arm is added to the segment lever and angle lever so the operator can lift the ribbon hanger into the second colour at any time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a calculator the second (red) ribbon colour is available only for totals and the item after them, not at will.

**Solution:** A four-pivot double lever formed from the outside of an arm is added to the segment lever and angle lever so the operator can lift the ribbon hanger into the second colour at any time.

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
