## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Originals on separate sheets must be fed while duplicates are made on a continuous sheet, which the platen feed alone does not handle.

**Solution:** A separate auxiliary set of rubber feed rollers, carried on a shaft by brackets behind the platen, feeds the strip independently of the platen rollers, with guide fingers on converging paper shelves.

**How it works:** The auxiliary rollers are independent of the main feed and guide the paper strip around the platen.

**Parts:** auxiliary feed rollers; guide fingers on paper shelves
