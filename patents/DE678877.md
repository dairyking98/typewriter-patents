## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculating machines with column-moved paper carriages need stops or cams that trigger operations at specific column positions.

**Solution:** Control devices are set automatically at successive positions of the movable paper holder, so the machine performs the programmed operations at those positions.
