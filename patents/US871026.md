## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Two or more complete typewriters coupled for simultaneous operation

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewritinginachines, and has for its principal object to provide a duplex or twin typewriter, by which two distinct, original impressions may be made at each stroke, of a key, the impressions being exact duplicates of each other, and being made on either a single sheet or on two separate sheets of paper.

**How it works (reading):** A twin typewriter in which one key operates two typebars, one in each bank, printing duplicate originals at different places on a long platen.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making an original and an exact duplicate (for example a bill and its stub) normally takes two operations or carbon paper.

**Solution:** A twin typewriter in which one key operates two typebars, one in each bank, printing duplicate originals at different places on a long platen.

**How it works:** Built on the lines of the Oliver No. 3, with U-shaped typebars in two banks at each side and a shared keyboard, platen, spacing and inking. The roller platen is lengthened so the two banks print at points well apart, one on the body of the bill and one on the stub. Only fourteen typebars per bank are used, worked by fourteen keys each carrying three characters, the common one in the centre. Each key drives a left bar and a right bar carrying duplicate type, through auxiliary U-shaped bars pivoted at the rear and joined by a cross bar, with springs and links to the typebars. Less-used characters print with the platen shifted forward or back.

**Parts:** typebar; key lever; auxiliary bar; cross bar; spring; link; roller platen; carriage

**Numbers:** 14 typebars per bank; 14 keys; 3 characters per key

**Improves on:**
