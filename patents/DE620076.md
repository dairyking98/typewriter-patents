## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Auxiliary ribbon spools for duplicating are dragged by the platen when the platen is turned backward.

**Solution:** Interchangeable clutch drives a group of ribbon spools in one fixed direction from the platen axis regardless of platen rotation.
