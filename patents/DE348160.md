## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (telegraph type printing receiver) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using electric or electronic translation

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to receivers for type print telegraphs to which the character to be printed is transmitted through a combination of several consecutive power surges will.

**How it works (reading):** A capacitor and a relay are provided for each character element; line-current surges charge the capacitors, and their discharge, switched in sequence, sets the polarized relays and the print magnet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Single-element telegraph receivers need an extra relay group and a complex switching arrangement to decode multi-pulse characters.

**Solution:** A capacitor and a relay are provided for each character element; line-current surges charge the capacitors, and their discharge, switched in sequence, sets the polarized relays and the print magnet.
