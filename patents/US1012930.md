## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic tab contact devices should work for carriage travel in one direction only and not hinder the return.

**Solution:** Disengaging devices on the feed rack are made effective on the forward travel and ineffective on the return travel automatically.

**How it works:** Improves on Steele's applications 616,318 and 616,319 and Smith's 631,505. The perforated feed rack has recesses above each perforation; individually adjustable disengaging devices with upper and lower arms seat in a recess and perforation at any letter-space position. They cooperate with a contact member to lift the rack from the pinion and skip the carriage. Automatic means render the contact devices effective for one direction of carriage travel and ineffective for the other. Modified form in Figs. 4-6.
