## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hand and wrist posture in typing lessons is hard to teach because the usual wrist rests prop the joints up and do not train a correct hand position.

**Solution:** A straight, edged or narrow rod is fixed at the top in front of the key rows of a typewriter or attachable keyboard, so the student must hold the wrist high and any lowering of the wrist meets the rod and feels uncomfortable.
