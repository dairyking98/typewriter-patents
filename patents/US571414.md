## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies arranged in stationary or sliding cases or frames or upon flexible strips, plates, bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a typewriting machine having on its type-plate letters, combinations of letters, syllables, words, and other characters which by a singlepressure on a key are impressed upon the paper, a space being moved by the paper-carriage to correspond with the length of the type or character printed.

**How it works (reading):** A pressing lever moved over a perforated sign board lets a stud push the selected rubber type through the hole onto the paper, with row choosing 1, 2 or 3 letters.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are complex, costly and cannot print syllables or words quickly.

**Solution:** A pressing lever moved over a perforated sign board lets a stud push the selected rubber type through the hole onto the paper, with row choosing 1, 2 or 3 letters.

**How it works:** A sign-indicating board with holes shows letters, syllables, words and signs in rows of one, two or three letters. A bar slides on a guide and the guide slides at right angles, so the button of a pressing lever can be put over any sign. Bars hang from the pressing bar and carry a frame with a rubber type-plate beneath the board, whose type is pressed through the hole by a stud when the lever is pressed. The same press moves a plate that trips the carriage feed pawl, with three recesses for letter, syllable or word spacing.

**Features:** [SEL:pointer-chart](../features/sel-pointer-chart.md), [SEL:pointer-press-prints](../features/sel-pointer-press-prints.md), [ELEM:plate-flat](../features/elem-plate-flat.md), [ELEM:rubber-type](../features/elem-rubber-type.md), [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [SEL:chart-interchangeable](../features/sel-chart-interchangeable.md)

**Parts:** sign-indicating board; pressing lever; bar; guide; rubber type-plate; stud; carriage; feed pawl; spacing plate; spring; column

**Numbers:** rows of 1, 2 or 3 letters
