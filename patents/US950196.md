## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriage mechanism needed a more compact, better arranged construction for mounting the platen, paper guides and line-spacing devices.

**Solution:** A rectangular carriage frame with end plates carrying the platen shaft, a spring-loaded paper guide plate set with rollers that drop away for paper insertion, and a geared line-spacing lever.

**How it works:** A carriage frame with a toothed rack bar slides on a guide rail and a rod, advanced by an escapement dog and stop dog driven from a universal bar and feed lever. End plates carry the platen shaft with milled adjusting wheels. A bowed spring presses curved paper guide plates carrying rubber rollers against the platen; a rock shaft with a lever lowers the plates so paper can be inserted. A gear on the platen shaft meshes with a spur gear operated by a line-spacing lever with several adjustment positions.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** carriage frame; rack bar; platen shaft; platen; bowed spring; paper guide plate; guide roller; rock shaft; line-spacing lever; platen gear; escapement dog
