## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a tabulator device on typewriters, in particular portable typewriters, with a switching rack which can be moved into and out of engagement with the carriage ratchet wheel and a tab stop rod which carries the usual tab stops and which is carried by a swivel frame. It has already become known to move a frame carrying the tabulator rod into a position when a tabulator key lever is depressed, in which the carriage ratchet wheel 1 is disengaged from the rack and is held disengaged for the duration of the key press, while the resulting free carriage movement upon impact a tab stop is limited to a counter stop. In the known device, however, the carriage rack is supported by a swivel frame carried, which is independent of the pivot frame supporting the tab rack so that the case may arise that, if the tabulator device is improperly operated or in the event of a shock exerted on the machine, the swivel frame carrying the gear shift rack is lifted before the tab rack is in its working position.
