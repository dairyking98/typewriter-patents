## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a tabulator device on typewriters, in particular portable typewriters, with a switching rack which can be moved into and out of engagement with the carriage ratchet wheel and a tab stop rod which carries the usual tab stops and which is carried by a swivel frame.

**How it works (reading):** One swivel frame carries both the tab stop rod and the carriage shift rack; pressing the tab key pivots a lever that frees the carriage only once it sits on a tab stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** In the known tab device the carriage shift rack and the tab stop rod sit on separate frames, so they can get out of step and the rack can lift before the tab rod is in working position.

**Solution:** One swivel frame carries both the tab stop rod and the carriage shift rack; pressing the tab key pivots a lever that frees the carriage only once it sits on a tab stop.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
