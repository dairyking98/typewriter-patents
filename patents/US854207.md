## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-colour ribbon machines needed convenient operator control to switch colour or ribbon quality while typing.

**Solution:** A mechanism to adjust the ribbon sideways on its spools so different widthwise portions print, controlled from the keyboard, with a notched collar clutch.

**How it works:** Ribbon spools ride on sleeves on shafts, driven by an arm and pin so the spool can slide along the shaft. The lateral ribbon-shifting mechanism from the inventor's earlier application moves the ribbon back and forth while it feeds. This patent adds a notched collar connection that can be thrown in or out so the ribbon sits at one colour band or another, and uses the full width of a single-colour ribbon.
