## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide an apparatus embodying in combination a columnating mechanism whereby the paper-carriage or movable member may be arrested at a predetermined one only of a series of column-fields ranging across entire line of print and tabulator or denominationalstop mechanism whereby the orderly printing of rows of figures or the like may be effected.

**How it works (reading):** Column-skipping mechanism that releases the carriage and arrests it at one chosen column field without being checked by earlier stops, combined with a denominational tabulator.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier column stops halted the free-running carriage at every stop it passed on the way to the desired column.

**Solution:** Column-skipping mechanism that releases the carriage and arrests it at one chosen column field without being checked by earlier stops, combined with a denominational tabulator.

**How it works:** A stationary frame carries a series of column-field stops and a shifting frame selects which one the carriage dog will meet. Once the carriage is released from its escapement it runs past the intermediate stops and is caught at the selected one. A second tabulator part gives denominational stops at letter-space distances for aligning figures. Locking devices hold the shifting frame in position.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** carriage; escapement; column-field stop; stationary frame; shifting frame; denominational stop; locking device

**Improves on:**

- earlier column-stop tabulators: carriage no longer stops at every intermediate column stop
