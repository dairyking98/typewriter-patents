Typhehelantrieb for typewriters, in which the movement in a known manner the button lever on: the type lever is transferred by means of a two-arm intermediate lever, by changing the ratio of their two lever arms to each other Increasing key movement resistance bring about an accelerated type lever movement. The purpose of the invention is to provide a type lever drive of the above type; to be designed so that with the simplest construction and the least initial Driving force for the key lift. the greatest possible acceleration of the type lever movement is achieved. In order to achieve this, the type lever drive according to the present Invention made the device so that. the intermediate levers on one after Roll off the curved surface in such a way that. the lever arms connected to the type lever increase in the same amount as the arms connected to the key lever decrease.
