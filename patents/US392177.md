## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars made by soldering, forging or cutting sheet bend, wear unevenly and are costly.

**Solution:** Stamp a blank from steel plate, bend the tail, swage in dies, deburr, flute and drill.

**How it works:** A steel sheet about one eighth inch thick is stamped into blanks with a main arm, lateral journal arms, a tail and a head. The tail is bent up in a die, then the blank is swaged to compress the arm and shape head and arms, and the fin is removed. The arm is fluted along its length. Holes are drilled for the type, the key link and the pivot pin through the lateral arms. A modification omits the lateral journals and leaves a raised rim near the pivot to reduce side friction.
