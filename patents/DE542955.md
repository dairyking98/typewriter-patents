## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A flat-plate machine needs a tabulator that stops the moving writing plate at set points in the line-spacing direction, and the tabulation must be quick to set and simple.

**Solution:** Tab stops in two groups work on different levels: one group is rigidly adjusted on a rod below the plate frame, and the counter-stops on the plate underside are pivoted into engagement when the plate-front key is struck.

**How it works:** Once set for a row arrangement, a line tab key brings each plate position in turn; a counter-stop swings into the engagement plane of its fixed stop.
