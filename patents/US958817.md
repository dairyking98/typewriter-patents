## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabulating mechanisms for typewriting and other machines, and particularly to column stops.

**How it works (reading):** Thin column stops sit in notches of the rack and slide forward or back into or out of effective position, each held by a latch, and a keeper bar releases them so they can be set without displacement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the machine for different tabulating work requires readjusting the column stops along the carriage rack.

**Solution:** Thin column stops sit in notches of the rack and slide forward or back into or out of effective position, each held by a latch, and a keeper bar releases them so they can be set without displacement.
