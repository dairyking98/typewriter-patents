## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and manually actuated

**How it works (reading):** Pins on the carriage below the paper roller are engaged by short locking levers hung on the frame, held in place by springs, with the lever length adjustable so the pins line up after assembly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage locks on paper-roller levers can shake loose during writing and damage the machine, and locks above the roller widen the machine.

**Solution:** Pins on the carriage below the paper roller are engaged by short locking levers hung on the frame, held in place by springs, with the lever length adjustable so the pins line up after assembly.

**Features:**

- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** carriage pins; carriage shaft nuts; locking levers; retaining spring; adjustable lever length
