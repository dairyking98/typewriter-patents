## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to construct a stencil-cutting machine-that is, one intended to cut stencil characters, addresses, dre.

**How it works (reading):** A rotating die plate driven by a toothed arc and pinion, held by superposed toothed plates, and advanced by a main feed disk with a ratchet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Stencil cutting needs a rotating die plate held in place and fed reliably.

**Solution:** A rotating die plate driven by a toothed arc and pinion, held by superposed toothed plates, and advanced by a main feed disk with a ratchet.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
