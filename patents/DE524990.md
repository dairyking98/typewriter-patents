## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a new device for cleaning the types of Typewriters, stamps, etc.

**How it works (reading):** The cleaning mass is a replaceable rotating body on a freely turning handle, with a roller that pushes the used mass back to the centre so fresh mass always contacts the type when it is rolled over the type face.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cleaning type by dabbing a doughy cleaning mass is tiresome and dirties the hands, especially for larger areas.

**Solution:** The cleaning mass is a replaceable rotating body on a freely turning handle, with a roller that pushes the used mass back to the centre so fresh mass always contacts the type when it is rolled over the type face.
