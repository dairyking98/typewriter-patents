## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a clutch adapted to control the movements of the sprocket wheel which engages the rack of a carriage to feed the carriage longitudinally of itself.

**How it works (reading):** A clutch member that slides straight in and out of engagement with a fixed clutch member, with tension to return it and guides plus an adjustable stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The clutch that drives the carriage feed sprocket must free the carriage on return and step it during writing.

**Solution:** A clutch member that slides straight in and out of engagement with a fixed clutch member, with tension to return it and guides plus an adjustable stop.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
