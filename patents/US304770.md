## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon-spool ratchet pawls in typebar machines needed better guiding and control of the pawl rods.

**Solution:** A slidable bar carrying two retaining pawls engages one ribbon-spool ratchet at a time while a spring-loaded pawl rod driven by the letter-space frame winds the other.

**How it works:** The ribbon passes between two spools, each with a ratchet wheel. A flat bar on the upper platform is slotted and can be shifted lengthwise, held by a flat spring in notches, so one retaining pawl engages its ratchet. Each feed pawl sits on a rod whose lower end is linked to the letter-space frame, which is depressed by every key stroke. Shifting the bar releases one pawl and brings the other into engagement, so the ribbon winds in the chosen direction.

**Parts:** ribbon spool; spool ratchet wheel; feed pawl; pawl rod; retaining pawl; slotted bar; flat spring; letter-space frame

**Improves on:**

- earlier pawl-rod ribbon feeds: pawl rod guided and controlled by the slotted bar
