## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (weft bobbin thread holder) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cutting, holding, manipulating, or disposing of, weft ends

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a holding device for the thread beginnings of the replacement bobbins a weft bobbin replacement device, in which the thread beginnings of the replacement bobbins held taut by two clamping surfaces that are automatically moved relative to one another will.

**How it works (reading):** Two plate clamps with one-directional hair pile grip the thread ends on the pulling stroke only, so the thread is held and the cut remnants are released on the return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Thread ends from weft bobbins tangle and tear when held between rubber rollers or an endless band.

**Solution:** Two plate clamps with one-directional hair pile grip the thread ends on the pulling stroke only, so the thread is held and the cut remnants are released on the return.
