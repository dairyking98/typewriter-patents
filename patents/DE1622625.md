## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Same reversing device as DE807626: left ribbon guide arm releases a crankshaft and double pawl that shift the ribbon feed shaft to the other spool.

## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier automatic ribbon reversing needs control parts at both spools.

**Solution:** Same reversing device as DE807626: left ribbon guide arm releases a crankshaft and double pawl that shift the ribbon feed shaft to the other spool.

**How it works:** The pull of the emptied ribbon moves the left guide arm, which frees the crankshaft. A spring slides it under the shift rod. Next keystroke pushes it, and the double pawl turns the armature, moving the clutch disc on the transport shaft to the other spool. A sprung cam holds the armature.

**Features:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** ribbon guide arm; crankshaft; double pawl; armature; clutch disc; ribbon transport shaft; spring cam; shift rod
