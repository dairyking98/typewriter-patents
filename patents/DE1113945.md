## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (message sequence display device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Control, indicating, or safety devices or systems for composing machines of various kinds or types; Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying; Cooperation and interconnection of the input arrangement with other functional units of a computer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for creating and writing down continuous, step-by-step displayable message sequences, their Content is to be put together with partial messages from a large number of pre-stored partial messages are selectable.

**How it works (reading):** Address symbol combinations call pre-stored partial messages from a magnetic selection memory, matched by relay comparison groups, into a writing or printing machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known message-writing machines have an open loop, poor typeface consistency, and few abbreviations.

**Solution:** Address symbol combinations call pre-stored partial messages from a magnetic selection memory, matched by relay comparison groups, into a writing or printing machine.
