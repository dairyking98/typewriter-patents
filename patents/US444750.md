## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple typewriter is wanted in which the operator picks the character without a full keyboard.

**Solution:** A style on a cord inserted into labelled sockets rotates a horizontal type disk to the character, and a pressed swinging keyboard drives the paper against that type.

**How it works:** A vertical shaft carries a horizontal type disk with rubber types around its edge in two sets, mostly letters in one and punctuation, figures and rare letters in the other. A cord wound on a pulley on the shaft passes round a roller on a shifting arm, with a spring winding it back, and ends in a style. The style is inserted into a socket in the keyboard; each socket is at a different distance from the pulley so the cord turns the disk to the matching type. The keyboard on swinging arms is then depressed, bringing a striking bar and the paper against the type shown through a notch in a fixed pressure arm. Springs return the keyboard and a knob spaces the lines.
