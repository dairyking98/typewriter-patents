## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The locking movement and the locked movement run approximately in straight opposite directions so they cannot miss each other; a margin lever acts on the universal rail.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Key locks often failed because the locking and locked movements did not meet in time.

**Solution:** The locking movement and the locked movement run approximately in straight opposite directions so they cannot miss each other; a margin lever acts on the universal rail.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
