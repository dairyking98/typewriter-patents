## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Space and shift keys placed at the keyboard ends or in the front row make hands lose their basic position, and double shift was awkward.

**Solution:** Hollow, thumb-shaped space and shift keys converging backwards, with the shift key also pivoting sideways for a second shift.

**How it works:** The space key and shift key below the normal rows are shaped as grooves into which the thumbs fit, so the thumbs return to the correct basic position. The left key moves up and down for the first shift and pivots sideways when the thumb presses the groove wall for a second shift, without moving the hand. Locking can use the normal device. A double shift allows three characters per type lever, adding 12 to 14 new characters.
