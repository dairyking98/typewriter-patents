> Description Translated from German Typewriter Keyboard The invention relates to keyboards for typewriters to use the ten-finger system. There are already known keyboards in which the foremost row of letter keys is interrupted, and at this point next to each other a flat space and a simple toggle button are arranged, which can be moved up and down. Known is also to leave the four-row letter keys unchanged and in front of them to attach a space key in the form of a bar, while on the left or on the left and right outer end of the keyboard a single or double toggle key is provided is. The essence of the innovation now consists in the fact that those below the normal Type key rows arranged space and toggle keys for inserting the Thumb fillets are formed, that also the fillets, the natural Position the right and left thumb accordingly, converge backwards and that finally at least one of these keys, which can be moved up and down in the usual way by means of the pressure to be exerted from the inside on the outer wall of the groove is also mounted laterally pivotable to in this way a second switch to be able to make.
