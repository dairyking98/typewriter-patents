## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the ribbon-controlling devices of type-writing machines, and particularly to the mechanism in frontstrike machines which vibrates the ribbon to cover and uncover the printingepoint at each type-stroke.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Underwood needed one simple means to switch between regular, bichrome upper or lower ribbon band, and stencil (no ribbon) writing.

**Solution:** An idle wrist riding in aligned slots of the ribbon actuator and vibrating lever, raised or lowered to change the lever throw or to disengage.

**How it works:** A loose wrist passes through slots in the reciprocating actuator and the ribbon-vibrating lever. It hangs on two links pivoted to arms of a rock-shaft. Placed nearest the lever pivot it gives the greatest throw and brings the lower (red) band up. Rocking the shaft lifts the wrist away from the pivot for a shorter throw and the upper band. A further position puts the wrist where the actuator cannot move the lever, so the ribbon rests idle for mimeograph stencils.
