## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holding, feeding, or guiding means therefor

**How it works (reading):** Sink the ink-carrying stripe by the thickness of its ink layer (or more), so the ribbon has the same thickness across its whole width and the colour does not protrude.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon colour stripes stand proud, so when they are wound up or pressed the ink can offset and soil.

**Solution:** Sink the ink-carrying stripe by the thickness of its ink layer (or more), so the ribbon has the same thickness across its whole width and the colour does not protrude.
