## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (addressing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing mechanisms using line-contact members, e.g. rollers, cylinders; Tool motion initiates work feed and vice versa

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines for automatically printing a series of addresses, nu mbers, or like marks upon a series of envelops, cards, or other objects.

**How it works (reading):** An addressing machine with independent stencils and an oscillating bell crank driving a printing and feed mechanism.
