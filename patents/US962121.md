## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the line-spacing devices of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A stop on the line-spacing rack arrests the wheel and presses the detent harder at the moment of seating, and a release lets the platen turn freely.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Line-space detent overthrows the platen and the detent cannot be released for free platen movement.

**Solution:** A stop on the line-spacing rack arrests the wheel and presses the detent harder at the moment of seating, and a release lets the platen turn freely.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
