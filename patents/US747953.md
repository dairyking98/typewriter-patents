## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a book typewriter with flat platen, the edge of a card or sheet is exposed to the traveling print frame and may slip.

**Solution:** A work-guard plate that clamps or protects the top edge of the work and can be moved in the line-space direction to any point over the platen.

**How it works:** A web or plate-like work-engaging member spans between the two guide rails of the flat platen and engages the top edge of the sheet or card. It is adjustable along the line-spacing direction so it protects and helps hold work anywhere in the printing area, with optional auxiliary springs for a positive grip. Useful for billing or index work with duplicates transferred to an underlying sheet.

**Features:** [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)
