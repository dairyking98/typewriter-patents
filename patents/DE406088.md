## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar motor drives used a tooth dropped into a rotating shaft, giving uneven blows.

**Solution:** A friction bar on the typebar linkage is pressed by a roller against a continuously rotating friction shaft, then released just before impact so inertia completes the stroke.

**How it works:** Pressing the key swings a driver lever that locks under a rail and rocks a two-armed lever, whose roller presses a friction bar against a rotating, finely toothed friction shaft. The bar moves, swinging the intermediate lever to throw the typebar. Shortly before impact the bar hits an adjusting screw on the driver, releasing it and the friction; momentum finishes the stroke. The screw sets stroke strength, and the rail height sets friction for all types.
