## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal that senses the ribbon winding diameter with feeler levers engages the reversing screw only gradually, so switching is unreliable.

**Solution:** Two cooperating levers on the feeler shaft, one braked and notched, make a spring-loaded switching lever snap abruptly into a cam path that slides the drive shaft to the other spool.

**How it works:** A shaft carrying bevel gears slides sideways to engage one spool's gear or the other. A feeler lever pressed on the winding spool rotates its shaft as the spool diameter grows. This lifts a pivoting member that releases a spring-loaded switching lever from a detent on a braked loose lever. The lever's roller then leaves or enters the path of a cam disc on the drive shaft, and the cam pushes the shaft over to the other bevel gear in one step.
