## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter with platen-sideways spacing could only space at the type's normal pitch and gave no easy way to leave a one-character gap between letters.

**Solution:** A handle moves an adjusting pawl so it catches the feed gear one tooth or two teeth per stroke.

**How it works:** Each typebar stroke strikes a semicircular sliding plate whose projection presses a contact piece on a pivoted guide, rocking it so the fixed dog and adjusting pawl alternately engage the teeth of a feed gear, which advances one tooth per stroke. A second gear on the same shaft meshes with the platen rack so the platen shifts. A handle in a slot moves an L-lever, an adjustable-length link and a lever that tilts the adjusting pawl left or right. In one position it takes the next tooth (normal spacing); in the other it takes the second tooth, so the platen moves two teeth per stroke, leaving a one-character gap.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Letter-spacing adjusting device. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a letter-spacing adjusting device made as follows. A guide carries a sliding contact piece in its middle and a fixed dog for engaging a gear at its left end. An adjusting pawl is hung loosely beside the dog. A spring and the upper end of a lever are hooked to part of the adjusting pawl. A link adjustable in length is joined to the middle of the lever, and a spring plate bears on the link. The other end of the link is hooked to the tip of an L-shaped lever hung loosely on the typewriter frame. The bend of the L-shaped lever carries a handle, held in a hole in part of the frame. The object is to move the gear-engaging adjusting pawl to the left or right by moving the handle left or right. The pawl then engages the gear one tooth at a time or two teeth at a time, so that on the face of the platen driven by the gear the printing is set either at the type's normal spacing or at a spacing that leaves one character between letters.
