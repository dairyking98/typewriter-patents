## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**How it works (reading):** A shaped armature carries a loose tooth pressed outward into the carriage rack by two springs; a release rod on the carriage pulls the tooth back to free the carriage, and the key-lever axis and universal rail move the armature to engage it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage transport mechanisms wear quickly and then move the carriage unevenly, so letter spacing becomes irregular.

**Solution:** A shaped armature carries a loose tooth pressed outward into the carriage rack by two springs; a release rod on the carriage pulls the tooth back to free the carriage, and the key-lever axis and universal rail move the armature to engage it.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
