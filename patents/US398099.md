## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Adjusting the key-touch spring on Remington machines meant the collar spun free and the spring unwound.

**Solution:** An adjusting screw engages a threaded groove in the shaft and turns the spring collar positively.

**How it works:** The tension spring is fixed at one end to the hanger and at the other to a collar on the rocker shaft. A screw turns freely in the collar and engages a threaded groove running around the shaft tangentially, so turning it rotates the collar relative to the shaft and winds or unwinds the spring. The screw also locks the collar against sliding lengthwise.

**Features:**

- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** tension spring; collar; rocker shaft; hanger; adjusting screw; threaded groove; key bar

**Improves on:**

- Remington Standard set-screw collar: set-screw let the collar unwind; the tangential worm screw keeps the position
