## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type faces collect ink and dirt, and cleaning them by hand is awkward.

**Solution:** A cleaner sheet of absorbent textile fabric on a reinforcing backing is inserted in the carriage like paper; the keys are operated so the types press into the cloth and are cleaned.

**How it works:** The types are struck against the absorbent face with the ribbon moved aside, so they are wiped without moving the alignment.
