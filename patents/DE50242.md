## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Only the opening survives; the aim is a typewriter with types on one or more wheels turned a set amount per key.

**Solution:** Keys deflect a comb frame in proportion to their position, turning through a gear segment a vertical type wheel with several parallel type circles that also slides axially to choose a circle.

**How it works:** Key levers in three rows pivot on a shaft and when pressed strike a stepped comb plate on a frame C4 and turn it by an amount set by the key. A toothed segment connects the comb frame to a gear on the type wheel shaft so the wheel rotates until the wanted type is directly over the paper roller. The wheel, above the platen, rotates in a plane parallel to the roller axis, with typically six parallel circles (capitals, small letters, figures, in pairs) on one wheel or on separate discs; sliding the axle lengthwise brings one circle over the roller, giving the same keys for capitals, lower case and figures. The whole frame rocks on an axis to bring the type onto the paper. The rest of the machine is cut off.
