## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Levers having moving or variable fulcra to alter the mechanical advantage during the stroke

**How it works (reading):** The auxiliary toggle points are aligned in a straight line at rest so the keystroke starts slowly, the swing weight's centre of gravity supports the initial motion and the return, and the return movement of the swing weight is not stopped by any limit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Toggle-joint type drives give a heavy keystroke, and the return of the type lever is resisted, with noise.

**Solution:** The auxiliary toggle points are aligned in a straight line at rest so the keystroke starts slowly, the swing weight's centre of gravity supports the initial motion and the return, and the return movement of the swing weight is not stopped by any limit.
