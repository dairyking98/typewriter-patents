## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement on the device protected by patent 335767 for the noiseless return of the car from typewriters or to noiseless turning back of the ratchet wheel against a cooperating with this stationary pawl.

**How it works (reading):** The auxiliary pawl's pressure on the auxiliary ratchet wheel is directed radially in the working position, so little friction is needed to move and hold it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The auxiliary pawl of DE335767 creates friction and resistance when the carriage is pulled back, because its auxiliary ratchet is pressed too hard.

**Solution:** The auxiliary pawl's pressure on the auxiliary ratchet wheel is directed radially in the working position, so little friction is needed to move and hold it.

**Parts:** ratchet wheel; pawl; auxiliary ratchet wheel; auxiliary pawl; stop
