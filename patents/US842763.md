## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Encoding and decoding text by hand is slow, and a typewriter needs a way to shift one cipher alphabet against another.

**Solution:** Two type-cylinders are connected to the key mechanism, and one is moved a variable angle relative to the other by a motion mechanism, so the typed letter is enciphered or deciphered in either direction.

**Features:** [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)

**Parts:** two type-cylinders; movable cylinder; fixed cylinder; gearing; key mechanism
