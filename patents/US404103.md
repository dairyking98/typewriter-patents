## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a paper-holding attachment for type-writing machines, particularly of the Remington class;

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper on a Remington carriage must be held where the operator can see the print, and written sheets must be carried away from the roller.

**Solution:** A base-plate clamped to the carriage carries posts and a cap-plate holding the paper in front of the roller, with an automatic feed-away of the written paper.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
