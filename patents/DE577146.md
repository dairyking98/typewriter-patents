## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbons associated, but not moving, with typewriter platens, e.g. extending transversely to the length of the platen and extending parallel to the length of the platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a color device of the type mentioned one during the hitting of the types on the paper to be written on the types and the paper lying color carrier against which the types in their Create rest position for the purpose of coloring.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon-like ink carrier on a pivoting carrier is advanced by a ratchet linked to each key, so the carrier presents fresh ink to the type at each stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing through a fabric ribbon gives a rough, textured print, and types inked directly from fixed pads are unevenly coloured.

**Solution:** A ribbon-like ink carrier on a pivoting carrier is advanced by a ratchet linked to each key, so the carrier presents fresh ink to the type at each stroke.
