## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a split platen for typewriters with independently rotatable, Roller parts arranged on a common tube, which are driven by the ratchet wheels independent means can be coupled.

**How it works (reading):** A common tube is pinned to one roller part and pressed onto resilient segments by a cone, so coupling is made around the full circumference.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Coupling split platen roller parts at a chosen relative position is awkward, and coupling at one point on the circumference is weak.

**Solution:** A common tube is pinned to one roller part and pressed onto resilient segments by a cone, so coupling is made around the full circumference.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
