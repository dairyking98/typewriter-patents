## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (telegraph receiver sheet printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using mechanical translation and type-bar printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to selection and printing devices for telegraph apparatus, in particular for pressure telegraph apparatus with sheet printing.

**How it works (reading):** A stationary platen with a light circular type-lever basket on a rotating spindle, with the selector mechanism moved inside the basket.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-lever baskets for telegraph printing are heavy, and character wheels print poorly.

**Solution:** A stationary platen with a light circular type-lever basket on a rotating spindle, with the selector mechanism moved inside the basket.
