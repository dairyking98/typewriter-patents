## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object is to provide improved devices of the class specified.

**How it works (reading):** A vibratory ribbon carrier moves the ribbon to the print point on a front-strike machine, with ribbon mechanism in side elevation.
