## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating mechanism with denominational stops needed compact placement beside the keyboard.

**Solution:** Denominational stops above the carriage column-stop path are carried on rods pivoted to forward-extending key levers, and pressing a key also lifts the feed rack off the escapement pinion.

**How it works:** Pressing a denominational key pulls its stop down into the path of the carriage column stops and, via the universal bar and a lever with a shoe, lifts the feed rack from the escapement pinion so the carriage runs left until a column stop hits the stop. Releasing restores everything by spring. The stops sit a letter-space apart, with converging rods.
