## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The types of a circular-basket typewriter (Remington, Caligraph) collect dust and surplus ink and are cleaned one at a time.

**Solution:** A circular brush on a screw shaft below the centre of the type circle that is cranked up and rotated to clean all the characters at once.

**How it works:** A bracket fastened to the frame carries a threaded socket under the centre of the circle formed by the depressed typebars. An annular brush on a spider is fixed to a screw shaft that runs in the socket. Cranking the shaft with a detachable key raises and rotates the brush so it passes in a spiral over the faces of every type. When finished the brush is run down below the levers or removed.

**Features:** [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** bracket; threaded socket; annular brush; spider; screw shaft; key post; detachable crank key

**Improves on:**

- hand cleaning of individual types: all types cleaned simultaneously by one brush
