## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** My improvements consist in the various features of construction and combinations of devices hereinafter more fully described, and particularly pointed out in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** At the end of longitudinal ribbon travel, the keyboard and printing mechanism are automatically locked and a signal is given, prompting the operator to reverse the ribbon shifting mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must be notified when the inking ribbon reaches the end of its longitudinal travel and must reverse it.

**Solution:** At the end of longitudinal ribbon travel, the keyboard and printing mechanism are automatically locked and a signal is given, prompting the operator to reverse the ribbon shifting mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** ribbon shifting mechanism; signaling device; keyboard lock
