## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools with clamping springs punched from the hub sheet are hard to push onto the axle and turn into position so the driver pin finds its hole, work on one side only, so the spool ends up crooked or misfitted, and the sheet springs wear.

**Solution:** Loose spring-steel-wire rings set in grooves of the spool's axle bore, with a slightly smaller relaxed inner diameter than the axle, grip the axle yet let the spool rotate on the rings while being plugged on.

**How it works:** Two side discs are joined by two tubular hubs; the inner hub slides on the axle and the ribbon winds on the outer hub. A driver pin on the machine engages a hole in the spool. Wire rings lie loosely in grooves at both ends of the inner hub, their relaxed diameter slightly under the axle diameter. As the spool is pushed on, they spread and grip, preventing accidental axial displacement, while the loose fit lets the spool turn on the rings until the driver pin drops into its hole. The rings can alternatively encircle the hub outside and reach in through window openings.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; side disc; inner hub; outer hub; spring steel wire ring; axle; driver pin

**Improves on:**
