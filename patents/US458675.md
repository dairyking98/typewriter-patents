## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The rigid universal bar of the Caligraph makes the key touch hard and the machine noisy.

**Solution:** A universal bar with a soft rubber strip so key levers first compress a cushion then move the rocking frame.

**How it works:** Key levers press on a universal bar carried on arms of a pivoted rocking frame that holds the letter-space dog engaging the carriage rack. The bar is a rigid cross piece with a vulcanized-rubber strip clamped on top. A key first compresses the strip, then the bar moves bodily and the rocking frame steps the carriage. This gradually overcomes the inertia and quiets the contact.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** key lever; universal bar; rubber strip; clamping plate; rocking frame; dog; letter-spacing rack; return spring

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph universal bar: adds a rubber cushion to lighten touch and reduce noise
