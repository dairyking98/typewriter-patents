## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by applications of scales or indicators

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writers in general, and more particularly to the scales thereof;

**How it works (reading):** A scale with a dovetail slot in which slidable strips carry numerals with comma groups and a dollar mark and decimal period, so one strip can be positioned to put the decimal point at a chosen column.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ordinary tabulating scale makes it hard for an operator to set the proper spacing for a column of figures.

**Solution:** A scale with a dovetail slot in which slidable strips carry numerals with comma groups and a dollar mark and decimal period, so one strip can be positioned to put the decimal point at a chosen column.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
