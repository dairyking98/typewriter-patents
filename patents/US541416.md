## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenographic punching machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**How it works (reading):** Six puncturing dies on longitudinally sliding bars are combined in permutations to form letters, punching a strip of paper.
