## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The brush cannot reach types held in the typebasket, so types must be moved to it one at a time.

**Solution:** A detachable holder retains one or two types in position for a reduced brush on a slotted pivoted bar, while the rest stay out of reach.
