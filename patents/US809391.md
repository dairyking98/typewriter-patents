## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage feed on a typewriter is uneven and noisy, needs fine spring adjustment, and the finger-key load varies during printing.

**Solution:** The detaining and spacing members sit on a rocker arm, and the spacing member is driven positively by the carriage-controlling mechanism so the feed starts when the rocker reverses and the carriage moves steadily without spring tension adjustment.
