## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (teleprinter line break) · **Subject (patent classes):** tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a line break "for the by the paper carriage Sheets of paper worn by teleprinters with two synchronously operating electric typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** When the paper carriage reaches its extreme right position a lifting rod engages a lever with the motor-driven ratchet wheel, and linkage then turns the paper feed roller by one line width, after which the lever is pushed back.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Teleprinters with two synchronous typewriters need the paper sheet to change line automatically when the paper carriage reaches its end position.

**Solution:** When the paper carriage reaches its extreme right position a lifting rod engages a lever with the motor-driven ratchet wheel, and linkage then turns the paper feed roller by one line width, after which the lever is pushed back.
