## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Forming arithmetic series needs repeated manual switching of several sliders and levers.

**Solution:** A detachable lever mechanism attached to the printing-unit switch slide alternately sets and triggers that switch, easing series entry on keyboard machines.

> Description Translated from German Removable auxiliary device for the keyboard of typing, calculating or Recording machines For typewriters, calculating machines or recording machines, in particular in adding machines with typewriters and keyboards, it is considered a major flaw felt for the operation of the machine that in the formation of arithmetic series repeatedly by hand in several sliders, buttons or levers in succession the switch-on or switch-off position must be brought. The invention provides a remedy here by means of a removable auxiliary device for the keyboard of typewriters, calculating machines or recording machines, in connection with with the switching device for switching the printing unit of the machine on and off a lever mechanism is detachably attached, which to facilitate the formation of arithmetic series depending on the position of the switching device, which is preferably designed as a slide the interposition and the triggering of the latter alternately influenced.
