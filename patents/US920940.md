## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Figures added on an adding machine may not match the items typed on an invoice on a separate typewriter.

**Solution:** Electric couplers and a cable link the keys of the adding machine and the keys of the typewriter so that the items typed and the items added correspond.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
