## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key calculator printing unit)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing unit on key calculators and consists of a new facility for printing Summands and sums at which the stroke of the rods controlling the type carriers is limited by stepped bodies.

**How it works (reading):** Stepped bodies (stairs) on the rods, driven by the keys and the main eccentric, limit the stroke of the type-sector rod; a nose on the rod rests against the steps so that the sector is positioned for the keyed value or the counter value.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The stroke of the rods that drive the type carriers must differ for a summand print and a sum print on a key calculator.

**Solution:** Stepped bodies (stairs) on the rods, driven by the keys and the main eccentric, limit the stroke of the type-sector rod; a nose on the rod rests against the steps so that the sector is positioned for the keyed value or the counter value.
