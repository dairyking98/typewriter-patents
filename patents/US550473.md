## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On shift-key machines the operator must hold the upper-case or figure key down for the whole run of capitals.

**Solution:** A spring catch that holds the depressed shift key down and releases with a natural wiping motion of the finger.

**How it works:** A spring with a base flange has a notched head with a shoulder and an inclined face placed beneath the key finger-piece. Pushing the key down swings the head outward, then it springs back over the finger-piece and locks it. Raising the finger with a slight wiping motion presses the protruding tip and frees the catch so the key springs up. Variants use a pivoted catch-bar, or a spring-loaded latch on the finger-piece engaging a fixed head.

**Parts:** shift key; finger-piece; staple; spring catch; notched head; base flange; catch-bar; pivoted latch
