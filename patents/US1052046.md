## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary receptacles for articles, e.g. erasers, pencils

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates broadly to typewriters and specifically to an attachment which comprises a shelf designed to rest on the typewriter frame at or near the visual angle to the printing point.

**How it works (reading):** A light detachable shelf rests on the front bar of a visible-writer typewriter, carries a memorandum pad in line with the visual angle to the printing point, and slides along the bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pencil notes for adding figures need the writer to shift attention from the typed sheet to a desk pad.

**Solution:** A light detachable shelf rests on the front bar of a visible-writer typewriter, carries a memorandum pad in line with the visual angle to the printing point, and slides along the bar.
