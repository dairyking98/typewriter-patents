## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage mountings and the Densmore carriage parts needed general improvement for easier running and release.

**Solution:** A platen truck on roller bearings with a platen frame joined by adjustable connections, and a feed rack that can be swung out of mesh with the pinion.

**How it works:** The platen axle is journaled in side bars of a platen frame; its front wheel runs in a grooved shift rail, which a pair of pivoted arms move fore and aft to shift case. The rear ends of the side bars join a hollow rod through threaded bushings with polygonal heads. The carriage travels on a truck with roller bearings, and the feed rack can disengage from the feed pinion to release the carriage.
