## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Switching devices for the paper carriage of compact typewriters need separate bearings for the pawl carrier and ratchet shaft, adding weight and parts, and loosening causes jerky stock.

**Solution:** The pawl carrier and the ratchet shaft are mounted in eyes punched and bent from the carriage plate, so the parts can be simple stamped sheet metal with no separate bearings.

**Parts:** ratchet wheels; pawl carrier; ratchet shaft; release rail; punched eyes in carriage plate

Such devices must despite the limited Space to be designed very simply so that all parts of such machines as Stamped parts can be made from sheet metal. Care must be taken that the necessary stability is ensured with the lowest weight. This object is achieved according to the invention in that the knife carrier and the ratchet shaft are mounted in eyes, which as a lobe from the carriage plate are punched out and bent. This device has the advantage that by eliminating special Bearings for the pawl carrier and the ratchet shaft and their fasteners a not insignificant weight reduction is achieved. In addition, however also avoided the disadvantages that were stressed by loosening the heavily jerky Stock can occur in the known designs. The drawing shows an embodiment of the arrangement.
