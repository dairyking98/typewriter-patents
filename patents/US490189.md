## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in the construction of an automatic ribbon-governing device a mirror and a reflector-holder and the arrangement and combination thereof with the platen, ribbon and types of a writing or printing machine, as hereinafter set forth pointed out in my claims and illustrated in the accompanying drawings in which;

**How it works (reading):** A ring-shaped ribbon governor, hung on elastic bearers under the platen, is lifted by the type-arms so the ribbon meets the paper at impact; a mirror and reflector let the operator see the writing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot see the writing line, and the ribbon needs governing so it lies clear of the paper until a type strikes.

**Solution:** A ring-shaped ribbon governor, hung on elastic bearers under the platen, is lifted by the type-arms so the ribbon meets the paper at impact; a mirror and reflector let the operator see the writing.
