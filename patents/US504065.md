## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hard key rims hurt the operator's fingers.

**Solution:** A metal sleeve fits over the key rim and holds an elastic rubber cap with the character; a trapped air space softens the strike.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
