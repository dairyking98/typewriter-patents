## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**How it works (reading):** Either ribbon bobbin can be driven from the geared spring-box cover through a shiftable shaft with a worm and gear, or both can be disengaged to allow manual winding.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon bobbins are driven only from the spring box, so the ribbon cannot be wound by hand unless the drive is disconnected.

**Solution:** Either ribbon bobbin can be driven from the geared spring-box cover through a shiftable shaft with a worm and gear, or both can be disengaged to allow manual winding.
