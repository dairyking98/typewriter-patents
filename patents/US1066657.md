## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriters equipped with tabulating devices.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulator keys raise plungers that catch a stop on the carriage rack; each plunger has a latch that holds it up, and the pinion is disconnected from the carriage during tabulation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator keys must be held down until the carriage stops, which is tiring and slow.

**Solution:** Tabulator keys raise plungers that catch a stop on the carriage rack; each plunger has a latch that holds it up, and the pinion is disconnected from the carriage during tabulation.
