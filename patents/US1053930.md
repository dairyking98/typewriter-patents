## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** Electrically controlled indicators, magnetically induced friction drive from the platen, and a sheet feeler show the remaining distance, give visual and audible signals, and lock printing after a set line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot judge the decreasing distance to the sheet end without manual care.

**Solution:** Electrically controlled indicators, magnetically induced friction drive from the platen, and a sheet feeler show the remaining distance, give visual and audible signals, and lock printing after a set line.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
