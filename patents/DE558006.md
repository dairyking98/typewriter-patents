## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In ribbon reversing devices the direction change can happen during the feed pawl's advancing stroke and disturb the feed.

**Solution:** The reversing member is operated only when the ribbon feed pawl is in its retracted position, via an oscillating piece with two switch hooks.

**How it works:** Two ribbon spools sit on shaft parts, and a ratchet wheel advanced by a feed pawl on a loose disc turns whichever spool is coupled to it. Coupling sleeves with bolts, moved by a slide rail with fork arms, engage teeth on one spool shaft or the other, so only one spool is driven at a time. The slide rail is moved by a switching arm on a two-armed oscillating piece with a switch hook at each end. An oscillating shaft driven from the printing mechanism carries a fork piece; one of the hooks is lowered into reach of the fork arms by angle levers, so the reversal happens only while the shaft returns and the pawl is retracted. Angle levers are tripped when the ribbon end pulls a pin at the end of a spool slot.
