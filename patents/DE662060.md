**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (tabulator value transfer to register)

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Transferring a tabulated value to a register took so long that the value-embodying organs stayed stopped too long.

**Solution:** Sensing happens during the brief standstill, and the register adjustment follows later after the organs are released.
