## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to obtain a machine which may be secured or attached to a type-writer and so adjusted that in tabulated work the carriage of the type-writer shall, upon the proper manipulation of the key of my machine, be released from any given position and at once stopped in position for the first figure or letter of the next column.
