## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to obtain a machine which may be secured or attached to a type-writer and so adjusted that in tabulated work the carriage of the type-writer shall, upon the proper manipulation of the key of my machine, be released from any given position and at once stopped in position for the first figure or letter of the next column.

**How it works (reading):** A dial-plate attachment with a pivoting key-lever whose position selects a column stop, releasing the carriage and stopping it at a preset sleeve.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating work on a typewriter needs the carriage released and stopped precisely at the first position of the next column.

**Solution:** A dial-plate attachment with a pivoting key-lever whose position selects a column stop, releasing the carriage and stopping it at a preset sleeve.

**How it works:** A key-lever on a vertical spindle is swung over a dial plate with holes and detented by a spring until a pin on its underside can drop into a chosen hole. Depressing the key raises a rod carried on arms fixed to the carriage, which presses an arm on the typewriter's release key and frees the carriage. The carriage runs left under its spring until an adjustable sleeve on the rod (set to a column mark) meets a lug on the key-lever. The hole chosen sets the digit position (units, tens, hundreds) within the column.
