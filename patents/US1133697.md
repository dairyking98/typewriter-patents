## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A roller platen must be inexpensive yet good, and it must be detachably mounted in the machine.

**Solution:** A platen with a wooden core built from pieces with half-round grooves fastened around the axle, detachably mounted on the carriage.
