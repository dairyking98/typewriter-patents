## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-end warning bell is small and gives a harsh high-pitched tone that users find unpleasant.

**Solution:** A long clamped sound bar spanning the frame is struck by a clapper released by the margin stop near the line end, with an elastic stop and opening that limit its swing.
