> Description Translated from German Trolley brakes, in particular for typewriters. For this registration is according to the union agreement of June 2nd igii the priority based on the registration claimed in Austria from July 13, 192i. The subject of the invention is a car brake, which on typewriters, calculating machines, but also others that work in a similar way Machines can be arranged, and in which the braking members on a support member are arranged, which can be actuated and adjusted with the tab lever linkage and brings the braking elements into the working position, where they are transmitted by means of transmission organs from carriage drive parts be set in rotating motion, whereby they act as a brake. Such car brakes are known in various forms. The purpose of the present invention is a safe acting, easily adjustable Brake of simple design, and this is achieved by the fact that the close to the fixed Brake disc receives a thread and by turning more or less against the movable brake disc is pressed, whereby the Brenlsscheihe influencing Spring is more or less compressed.
