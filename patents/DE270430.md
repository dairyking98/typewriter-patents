## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The clutch between platen and line-index wheel (patent 238851) can only be disengaged while the button is held pressed.

**Solution:** Add a sliding sleeve with locking balls that holds the uncoupling bolt in its disengaged position until released.

**How it works:** A carrier on the platen shaft holds spring-loaded tubular pinch rollers in cages that press against the inside of the line-index wheel hub, coupling them. Radial expanding pins with wedge tips sit between roller pairs. Pressing the button drives a conical hollow bolt that spreads the pins and pushes the rollers back, uncoupling the platen for free turning. Sliding a sleeve over the handwheel pin lets steel balls drop into a cross hole, holding the button shaft depressed until the sleeve is slid back.
