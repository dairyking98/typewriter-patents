## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter should print compound and single characters and space the carriage by the width of the compound character.

**Solution:** Typebars carry both compound (wide) and single characters and the carriage is fed a distance corresponding to the width of the one printed.

**How it works:** The main frame carries an annular section holding the pivoted typebars, with horizontal loops that hold ribbon spools and ratchet wheels advanced by a pawl. The type-levers act on a vibrating frame that is depressed to a greater or lesser extent according to which character is struck, giving a different carriage feed. A mechanism exposes the paper cylinder for feeding, reverses the carriage movement for line-spacing and feeds the ribbon. A spring holds the key-lever frame elevated.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
