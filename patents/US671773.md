## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Preventing rebound or clash of levers or type members

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of this invention are to enable a short type-bar to be employed, whereby an alinement of type can be maintained for a longer period and greater lightness of the machine be secured;

**How it works (reading):** A short type-bar is linked to the finger-key through a segment with a raised curved rib carrying radiating saw-cuts, giving a lighter, freer key touch and steadier alinement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Long type-bars lose alinement over time and the keys are heavy, so a short type-bar and a lighter key connection are needed.

**Solution:** A short type-bar is linked to the finger-key through a segment with a raised curved rib carrying radiating saw-cuts, giving a lighter, freer key touch and steadier alinement.
