## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a sound and shock absorbing base for office machines, especially typewriters, in which two cone-shaped and nested one against the other elastically supported, cap-like parts are pushed apart by springs.

**How it works (reading):** Two nested cap-shaped parts are pushed apart by a spring and sit on a rubber ring, so the foot is simple to fit and absorbs impacts in all directions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A foot for office machines has to be simple to assemble and absorb shock and sound.

**Solution:** Two nested cap-shaped parts are pushed apart by a spring and sit on a rubber ring, so the foot is simple to fit and absorbs impacts in all directions.
