## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the two-part connecting links or rods of type-writing machines wherein the separate parts are united by screw-thread connections.

**How it works (reading):** A means of uniting the two threaded members of each link so that they cannot be accidentally displaced or broken, without jam-nuts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Jam-nuts on two-part threaded connecting links do not hold, and tightening them starts thread fractures.

**Solution:** A means of uniting the two threaded members of each link so that they cannot be accidentally displaced or broken, without jam-nuts.
