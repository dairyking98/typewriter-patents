## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register control strip switching)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switching device for the control strip of cash registers, through which the part located between the supply and take-up reels of the control stripa, can be brought into different layers without the unwinding and spin the take-up spool.

**How it works (reading):** The strip runs in a loop over two movable rollers on a lever, so the part between the reels can be moved back and forth to bring the last print behind the viewing window.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The control strip of a cash register cannot be moved so that an earlier imprint is visible behind a window without also winding the supply and take-up reels.

**Solution:** The strip runs in a loop over two movable rollers on a lever, so the part between the reels can be moved back and forth to bring the last print behind the viewing window.
