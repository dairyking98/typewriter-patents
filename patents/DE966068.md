**How it works (reading):** Two related cards are scanned in one machine pass, the data from both is transferred to the printing means, and the combined information is printed on the card carrying the balance holes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing registration cards requires one card pass to print data from several scans; earlier machines could not combine a holes-coded balance card with a name and address card.

**Solution:** Two related cards are scanned in one machine pass, the data from both is transferred to the printing means, and the combined information is printed on the card carrying the balance holes.
