## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Device on power-driven type lever typewriters that repeated one Stop of a type lever is prevented by the power drive, if the associated Key lever is held in the depressed position.

**How it works (reading):** A resilient L-shaped intermediate member placed in a recess of the key lever lets the power-lever extension enter and be locked, so a held key does not repeat the strike.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Repeated strikes of a type lever while a key is held down are not reliably prevented by the spring-pawl device.

**Solution:** A resilient L-shaped intermediate member placed in a recess of the key lever lets the power-lever extension enter and be locked, so a held key does not repeat the strike.
