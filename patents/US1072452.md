**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Label printers

**How it works (reading):** A tally roll attachment on a combined typewriter and computing machine records each successive amount on a roll, without carbon, so a single unitary record is made alongside the individual accounts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing and combined typewriting and adding machines need a unitary list of all successive amounts, and carbon or transfer paper is needed to make one.

**Solution:** A tally roll attachment on a combined typewriter and computing machine records each successive amount on a roll, without carbon, so a single unitary record is made alongside the individual accounts.
