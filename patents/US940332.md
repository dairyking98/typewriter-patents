## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating mechanisms are usually operated by separate keys; the inventor wants one that the ordinary character keys can control.

**Solution:** A simple and efficient tabulating mechanism driven by the ordinary character keys of the machine.
