## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mounting or connecting key buttons on or to key levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key button, in particular for typewriters or the like, made of synthetic material or the like.

**How it works (reading):** One pressed or cast body with several viewing openings fits all key lever types, and writing paper can be clamped on its top by a ring pressed onto a slight cone.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pressed or cast key buttons fit only one key-lever shape, so a separate mould is needed for each lever size and shape.

**Solution:** One pressed or cast body with several viewing openings fits all key lever types, and writing paper can be clamped on its top by a ring pressed onto a slight cone.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
