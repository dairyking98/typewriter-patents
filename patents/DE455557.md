## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing ribbon colour on subtraction had to be reset by hand after switching back to addition.

**Solution:** A flexible coupling between the arithmetic switch and the ribbon colour mechanism lets the colour be changed by hand but restores black automatically on the next addition switch movement.
