## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements thereof of single hammers, e.g. travelling along printing line

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to hand-operated printing-machines of the class which includes typewriting machines and stenographic machines.

**How it works (reading):** A type-cylinder carries three characters in succession along each longitudinal row and is slid axially along its axle, through three positions, to bring each character into action, giving three times the range without adding rows.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier continuous-printing machines gave only one set of characters per row on the type-cylinder, so the character range was limited.

**Solution:** A type-cylinder carries three characters in succession along each longitudinal row and is slid axially along its axle, through three positions, to bring each character into action, giving three times the range without adding rows.
