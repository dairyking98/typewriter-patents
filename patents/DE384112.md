## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings therefor

**How it works (reading):** Roller support arms carry lateral attachments on a rod parallel to the platen, with adjustable springs held by a lockable set screw and a spring-loaded lift lever that stops rattling.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Existing paper pressure rollers on arms against the platen rattle, do not hold reliably, and their pressure cannot be adjusted.

**Solution:** Roller support arms carry lateral attachments on a rod parallel to the platen, with adjustable springs held by a lockable set screw and a spring-loaded lift lever that stops rattling.
