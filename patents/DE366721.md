## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** An intermediate link that acts only in the capital-letter writing position gives an extra carriage advance for the wide keys only; a jamming device with an additional lock wheel and pawls controls the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Wide capital letters need extra carriage advance, but the existing device for this is complicated and advances the carriage for every key regardless of writing position.

**Solution:** An intermediate link that acts only in the capital-letter writing position gives an extra carriage advance for the wide keys only; a jamming device with an additional lock wheel and pawls controls the carriage.
