## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to adapt the spool or roll holder to be quickly and easily placed in and removed from its bearings or supporting frame.

**How it works (reading):** Spool holder frame on the carriage with removable spool journals, tension device and a cutter for severing the sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper roll is hard to hold, remove and replace, and sheets must be cut at set points.

**Solution:** Spool holder frame on the carriage with removable spool journals, tension device and a cutter for severing the sheet.
