> Description Translated from German Printing device for cash registers with one between a writing and a Control strips pulled back and forth at a pressure point There are already printing devices known for cash registers with visible and writable control strips where the control strip located between the take-up and unwinding rolls pulled back and forth by guide rollers and in this way the impression of the pressure point is moved to the writing point, so that at this point the printed amounts etc. and at the same time handwritten entries made next to them can be. The invention consists in the fact that with a stationary unwinding roll the print hammer and the guide rollers associated with it are half the size Make movement like the take-up spool. With this arrangement, the printed Control strips moved from the printing point to the writing point with each machine run, without the need for special parts for this movement.
