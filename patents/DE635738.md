## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stereotype material from typed lines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Forme preparation by embossing, e.g. with a typewriter; Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** Metal foil tape is embossed, folded at the word spaces and stretched to the prescribed line length before casting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-length exclusion by embossed cardboard matrix tape leaves paper fibres and is too thick for accurate word-space folding.

**Solution:** Metal foil tape is embossed, folded at the word spaces and stretched to the prescribed line length before casting.
