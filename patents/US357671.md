## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper must be inserted into the carrier of the radial-lever machine conveniently, although the printing tablet sits over the centre of the type circle.

**Solution:** The feed-roller frame is hinged to the sliding carrier frame so it can be swung up toward the operator to load paper, then laid back down.

**How it works:** A frame of side bars hinges on posts above the machine face; a sliding plate runs in grooves in it, stepped left to right by a toothed bar. On the plate a second frame carrying two feed rollers and the printing tablet is hinged at its front edge. Swung over toward the front, it exposes the rollers so paper is slipped between them and drawn in with a finger piece on one roller. Swung back, a leaf spring with a countersink catches a conical stud and holds it with the tablet over the type circle.
