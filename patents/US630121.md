## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the escapement mechanism for type-writing machines whereby the paper-carriage is fed forward the distance of a letter-space at each depression and elevation of a key.
