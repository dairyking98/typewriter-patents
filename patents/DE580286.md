## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Means checking correctness of setting

**How it works (reading):** The adjusting projection is a separate shoulder piece on the type body, used for alignment during soldering and then removed, leaving a body of the usual length.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Adjusting projections left on the type body lengthen it and can cause inconsistencies, so the body may not fit some machines.

**Solution:** The adjusting projection is a separate shoulder piece on the type body, used for alignment during soldering and then removed, leaving a body of the usual length.
