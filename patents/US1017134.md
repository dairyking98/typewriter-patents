## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to apparatus for feeding envelops to typewriters and it refers to apparatus of the class in whlch the envelops are placed in a magazine supported independently of the machine and from which they are removed by an endless band provided with means for engaging the flaps of the envelops and which band carries them around the platen of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** A magazine of envelopes feeds an endless band with blades that draws each envelope down guides to the platen; the band is driven by the platen spindle and can be switched off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Envelopes must be fed to the platen by hand, and the feeder must fit any carriage without interfering with normal typing.

**Solution:** A magazine of envelopes feeds an endless band with blades that draws each envelope down guides to the platen; the band is driven by the platen spindle and can be switched off.
