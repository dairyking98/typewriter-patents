**How it works (reading):** A helical spring whose coils press on cam-like locking flanks holds each rider on a rod in its working or resting position, and a rider is repositioned by pushing aside only the coil that sits on it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Tab riders must be held in set and unset positions, but feather combs need a separate tool for each division, and other spring holds need extra levers.

**Solution:** A helical spring whose coils press on cam-like locking flanks holds each rider on a rod in its working or resting position, and a rider is repositioned by pushing aside only the coil that sits on it.
