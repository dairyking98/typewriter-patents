## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holding, feeding, or guiding means therefor

**How it works (reading):** Several opaque ribbons hinge at one end on a carrier and are carried on a swivel arm with elastic ribbon loops, and one lever spreads them so sheets can be inserted and removed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Copying on loose sheets with one ribbon only gives a single copy and the ribbon cannot be held clear of the sheets.

**Solution:** Several opaque ribbons hinge at one end on a carrier and are carried on a swivel arm with elastic ribbon loops, and one lever spreads them so sheets can be inserted and removed.
