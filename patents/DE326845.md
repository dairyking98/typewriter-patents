## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards characterised by means for holding or fastening typewriters or computer equipment

**How it works (reading):** A ball-bearing swivel base with four radially adjustable screw columns holds the machine feet above the bench, giving access from all sides.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machines are hard to reach from below for adjustment without tipping over.

**Solution:** A ball-bearing swivel base with four radially adjustable screw columns holds the machine feet above the bench, giving access from all sides.
