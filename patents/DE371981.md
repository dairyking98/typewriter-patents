## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A second, releasable stop is placed in front of the margin adjuster; releasing it lets the carriage move further right past the normal stop, and a sloped lug on the carriage slides under the stop as it returns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Margin adjusters only allow the carriage to be stopped at the line start, so writing before the line start (marginal notes) is not possible.

**Solution:** A second, releasable stop is placed in front of the margin adjuster; releasing it lets the carriage move further right past the normal stop, and a sloped lug on the carriage slides under the stop as it returns.
