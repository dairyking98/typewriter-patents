## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Linotype magazines hold only about ninety characters, so bookwork needs extra matrices that are hard to assemble and distribute.

**Solution:** The machine gets several independent magazines with matrix channels, plus mechanism that automatically assembles and redistributes the extra characters.

**Features:** [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [ELEM:metal-type](../features/elem-metal-type.md)
