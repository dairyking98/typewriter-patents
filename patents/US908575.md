## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Recording speech by typing each letter needs too many key depressions to be fast.

**Solution:** Ten characters in two parallel sets of five dashes and five dots are struck singly or together so that combinations stand for syllables, words or sounds, reducing the number of depressions.
