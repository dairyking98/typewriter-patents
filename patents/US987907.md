## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Mounting the carriage shift, shift lock and back-feed key levers on the Royal Grand was awkward, and the shift mechanism did not itself hold the carriage in lower case.

**Solution:** Use the shift key levers themselves as the stop that locks the vertically shifting carriage in lower case, and mount the paired levers on ball bearings that assemble as a unit.

**How it works:** The carriage shift rail hangs on parallel elbow-lever links with adjustable stop screws on their lower arms. At rest the rear end of each shift key lever touches the stop screw and locks the carriage down. Depressing either shift key moves its locking end clear, and its edge seats in a grooved roller on an arm under the shift rail, so the carriage rises. A rock shaft with slotted arms ties the two shift keys together so power reaches both ends and operating one frees the other. The shift lock key and back-feed key sit on the same axes, each pair on ball bearings between circular races spaced by toothed plates, so a pair and its bracket can be assembled and dropped in.
