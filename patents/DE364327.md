## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switching device for the paper trolley of typewriters in which in a known manner designed as a lever, arranged on the carriage guide Switching lock is used.

**How it works (reading):** A swing-out lock lever with an angled connector engages stops on the carriage ends from either side, so the carriage is held in use over its full writing length; a release lever on the carriage swings the lock stop clear so the carriage can be lifted out.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage switch lock lets the carriage be removed from its guide only with difficulty, and it must not block full use of the writing length.

**Solution:** A swing-out lock lever with an angled connector engages stops on the carriage ends from either side, so the carriage is held in use over its full writing length; a release lever on the carriage swings the lock stop clear so the carriage can be lifted out.
