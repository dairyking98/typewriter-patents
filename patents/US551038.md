## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a type-cleaning brush which will be free from the disadvantages just mentioned, which will enable the cleaning of the keys of the Reniington type-writer or of othertype-writers Whose type-bars are arranged out of a true circle to be accomplished quickly and thoroughly, and which can be easily attached to and detached from the machine on which it is to be used.

**How it works (reading):** A type-cleaning brush shaped for elliptical or non-circular type-bar arrangements (Remington style) that cleans several keys at once without disturbing alignment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing type-cleaning brushes clean only two or three types at a time and throw types out of alignment, and fail on machines whose type bars are not on a true circle.

**Solution:** A type-cleaning brush shaped for elliptical or non-circular type-bar arrangements (Remington style) that cleans several keys at once without disturbing alignment.
