## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing both addition and subtraction on calculating type wheels is inconvenient, and switching sign should not cause a full impression.

**Solution:** Each type wheel carries two numeral series, one for addition and one for subtraction, and the sign is switched by moving the pressure member tangentially to the wheel.
