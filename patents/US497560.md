## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a typebar machine, the type well is hidden under the carriage, making it hard to inspect the type and alignment; the machine should also be stronger, faster and less error-prone.

**Solution:** Mount the carriage support on track rails on columns so it slides back (or comes off) to expose the type well, and use a universal bar with typebars pivoted on disks on a rotating tube that shifts the bars for upper and lower case.

**How it works:** Track bars on the base plate carry a sliding carriage support, held at the front by pins and spring latches, which can slide back to uncover the type well. A tubular standard forms the centre of the well, with a spring-loaded rod inside, and a tube around it carries standards, rings and rods with felt-lined cushioning. The typebars are arranged in a circle around a sliding universal bar, with their lower ends mounted pivotally on disks of a rotatable tube. Rotating or lifting this tube shifts the typebars so lower or upper case types print. An inking roller is also shown.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [SHIFT:rotary](../features/shift-rotary.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** base plate; track bar; spring latch; carriage support; tubular standard; spring; rod; rotatable tube; ring; typebar; universal bar; inking roller
