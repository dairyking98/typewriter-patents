## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen-shift typewriters need a simple shift that can be adjusted for different type fonts and for wear, with the type basket adjusted to match.

**Solution:** The whole platen carriage hangs from a rock-shaft with equal-length arms and is raised by a shift key through a guide bar, with screw stops to set the shift distance and an adjustable basket ring to match.

**How it works:** The carriage's side bars pivot at the rear on arms of a rock-shaft held in hooked hangers with catches, so the carriage can be lifted off. A guide bar parallel to the front bar swings on equal-length arms and is moved by a crank, connecting rod and the shift key; a V-shaped guide on the carriage straddles this bar so the carriage can still travel along it. Pressing the shift key raises the front of the carriage and lowers the rear by equal amounts. Screw stops on either side of the guide-bar arm set the shift for each font and take up wear, and a pointer on the V-guide reads a scale on the bar. The typebar ring on slotted legs is moved to centre the impact for the new shift.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Interchangeable element](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; rock-shaft; hanger; catch; guide bar; V-shaped guide; shift key; connecting rod; adjustable stop screw; scale; typebar ring

**Numbers:** arms 6 and 12 of equal length
