## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage retarding on tabulation needs a separate drive and separate connections.

**Solution:** Tabulator-controlled clutch that releases the escapement wheel shaft from the wheel and connects the shaft to carriage retarding mechanism, avoiding separate gearing.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
