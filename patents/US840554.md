## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A writing-in-sight machine designed for speed.

**Solution:** Dividing the travel between a traveling type-carriage and a paper-carriage.

**How it works:** Only the opening: a front-strike machine with a type-carriage carrying printing parts and a paper carriage carrying the platen and ribbon, both controlled from the keyboard.

**Features:** [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** type carriage; paper carriage; platen; ribbon mechanism; keyboard
