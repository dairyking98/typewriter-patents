## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a moving device for the ribbon fork of typewriters, whose stroke can be changed in such a way that, as desired, different zones of the Ribbon can be brought in front of the printing point.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon-fork handlebar is suspended from the paper carriage support and its stops are linked to the adjusting linkage, so capital shift moves the handlebar vertically without disturbing the fork.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon zones of different colours need accurate ribbon-fork movement, and the stroke is not adjustable for capitals.

**Solution:** The ribbon-fork handlebar is suspended from the paper carriage support and its stops are linked to the adjusting linkage, so capital shift moves the handlebar vertically without disturbing the fork.
