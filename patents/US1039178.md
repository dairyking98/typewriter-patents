## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting the carriage and turning the platen takes a hand off the keys.

**Solution:** A palm-operated key bar that first turns the platen one line and then draws the carriage across by a flexible band on a spiral eccentric.

**How it works:** A pivoted operating bar with a palm key pushes up a rack bar that meshes with a ratchet segment. The segment turns a gear and an eccentric with a spiral face which winds a flexible band, pulling a draw bar on the carriage. The first part of the pull works a bell crank, link and dog that rotates the platen ratchet; further pull moves the carriage a distance set by key travel. Releasing the key returns the bar and the rack slips over the segment teeth. The carriage then feeds normally.
