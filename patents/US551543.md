**How it works (reading):** A soft rubber cushion over the space-bar with flanges that leave an air space, so the surface yields when touched.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The hard space-bar is uncomfortable for the operator's fingers.

**Solution:** A soft rubber cushion over the space-bar with flanges that leave an air space, so the surface yields when touched.
