## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** An arm on the other side of the carriage carries a roller that grips the rail from under and rolls on it, so the carriage cannot lift off; pushing the arm back frees the roller for removal.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A one-sided tongue stops the carriage lifting off its rail, but cannot prevent lift-off on both sides, and a two-sided lock would prevent removal.

**Solution:** An arm on the other side of the carriage carries a roller that grips the rail from under and rolls on it, so the carriage cannot lift off; pushing the arm back frees the roller for removal.
