**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded one-armed lever under the plate pushes the written card out of the writing frame into a collecting container when the plate is depressed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing booking slips in a flat-plate machine is slow and unreliable when the slip is released from the stack.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded one-armed lever under the plate pushes the written card out of the writing frame into a collecting container when the plate is depressed.
