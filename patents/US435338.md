## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Justifying by hand with spacekeys in matrix machines is tedious and error-prone.

**Solution:** A feed mechanism adds the extra space needed to justify automatically; the operator only touches a key or sets an index for the number of extra units, and variable feed stops set the carriage feed.

**How it works:** A right-and-left screw and an elongated rack with pinion move the matrix carriage, which is impelled by a spring; an escapement operated by a magnet feeds the carriage for normal spacing, and variable stops limit the extra feed.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
