## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-writer operation is noisy and the hammering wears the machine, which is a problem in telegraph offices where messages are read by sound.

**Solution:** An improved platen plus buffers at the shift-frame and carriage-receiving dog, which absorb the blow and reduce noise and wear on a Franklin type-writer.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; shift-frame end buffers; sliding carriage-receiving dog; carriage rack; type-bar guide

**Numbers:** Patent 653,282; Serial No. 682,420
