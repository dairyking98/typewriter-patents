## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing by rolling pressure roller, cash registers)

**How it works (reading):** Pressure rollers that roll over the type wheels are mounted eccentrically so they engage only in one direction of travel, without a separate spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hammer printing jolts the type carriers and shafts, and the separate spring that lifts printing rollers off the type is unreliable.

**Solution:** Pressure rollers that roll over the type wheels are mounted eccentrically so they engage only in one direction of travel, without a separate spring.
