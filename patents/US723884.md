## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Book typewriters need the ribbon guided, guarded and shifted.

**Solution:** Ribbon guiding, guarding and shifting mechanism for a Fisher book typewriter.

**How it works:** Only the title and drawings survive in the opening. The patent concerns ribbon guides, guards and a shifting mechanism on a machine travelling over a book.
