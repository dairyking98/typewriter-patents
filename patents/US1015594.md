## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon guides in the shuttle shield are pressed in during striking and the ribbon is not tensioned properly on the Hammond machine.

**Solution:** Means for tensioning the ribbon in the shuttle shield, with controlling devices that press against the ribbon and are disengaged for control movements.
