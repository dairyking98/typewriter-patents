## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cast parallel guide frames are heavy and brittle, and built-up bridge frames are hard and costly to make.

**Solution:** The guide frame is punched from one flat sheet-metal blank and bent into a rigid channel shape, with lugs bent to carry the journals, so it is light, stiff and cheap.

**How it works:** A flat blank is punched in one operation, the middle web is bent into a channel, and the bearing lugs are bent at right angles to the channel edges.
