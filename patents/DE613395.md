## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register forgery prevention)

**How it works (reading):** The punch device is coupled so that when it engages the stamping position, the print device is rendered ineffective.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash registers and booking machines can be defrauded by hand-adding a total print mark where a punch mark should be.

**Solution:** The punch device is coupled so that when it engages the stamping position, the print device is rendered ineffective.

- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
