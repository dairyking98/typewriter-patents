## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to devices on business printing machines for pulling back lying between writing paper webs and during the writing a section of the paper webs with this line-by-line preceding carbon paper sheets, their ends on common upstanding pegs one in the longitudinal direction of the tracks movable carriage are attached.

**How it works (reading):** Carbon-sheet ends are hooked onto holding tongues at an angle to the slide, with the tongues plugged onto common pins on the carbon carriage, so the sheets change easily and their ends are offset.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon-paper sheets in continuous forms must be pulled back by hand after each run, and each sheet attached separately on the carriage is slow to insert.

**Solution:** Carbon-sheet ends are hooked onto holding tongues at an angle to the slide, with the tongues plugged onto common pins on the carbon carriage, so the sheets change easily and their ends are offset.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
