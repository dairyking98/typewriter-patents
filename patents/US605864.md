## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage frames are heavy, vibrate and make it hard to see or correct the writing.

**Solution:** A two-part carriage frame whose forward platen portion is hinged and springs up on a lever release to expose the printing.

**How it works:** The rear carriage part runs on a guide rod (grooved roll) and a track (two rolls), with flange and extension preventing tilt or lift. The forward part with platen is hinged to it and held down by hooks on a shaft catching lugs. A lever releases the hooks and springs raise the platen part. The driving cable attaches between the bearing points, keeping the line of friction in the line of draft. The rack-bar lifts out of the escapement by a lever for free travel.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** carriage frame; guide rod; grooved roll; track; flange; hinge; hook; release lever; spring; cable; rack-bar; platen
