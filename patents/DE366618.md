## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typists need a signal when the last line of a page is reached, ideally by an attachment needing no changes to the machine.

**Solution:** A carriage-mounted ratchet wheel with a presettable pointer counts line feeds and rings a bell at the chosen line.

**How it works:** A frame screwed to the carriage carries a notched wheel with a return spring and stop. A pointer is turned by a knob to the notch for the number of lines wanted, and a pawl is engaged by a button. Each time the line-space lever hits a sliding rod it advances the wheel one notch. When the wheel has turned the preset count the pointer strikes a lever that releases a spring hammer against a bell. Withdrawing the pawl lets the wheel spring back.
