## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter connected to dictation machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Speech recognition

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a connection between typewriter and speaking machine (dictation machine), and that is supposed to switch the speaking machine on and off with the help of the typewriter or their speed of rotation can be regulated.

**How it works (reading):** A shaft of the typewriter drives a centrifugal governor whose disk sets a contact that changes the dictation machine's speed to match the typing pace.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typist must control a dictation machine by hand, usually with a foot lever, and the two machines are not linked.

**Solution:** A shaft of the typewriter drives a centrifugal governor whose disk sets a contact that changes the dictation machine's speed to match the typing pace.
