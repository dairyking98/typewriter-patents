## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The spring that loads the escapement or feed-dog rocker must have an adjustment for its tension that is simple, cheap and not prone to derange or break.

**Solution:** The rock-shaft of the feed-dog rocker is carried in screws tapped into a bracket, and an adjusting plug with a spring sets the tension on the rocker, which carries the feed and detent dogs that cooperate with the escapement wheel.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** bracket; bearing screws; rock-shaft; arm; feed dog; detent dog; escapement wheel; adjusting plug; lock-nut; spring
