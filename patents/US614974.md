## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen detent holds line spacing to fixed ratchet steps, so irregular spacing on ruled paper or blank forms is awkward and the free platen would not stay put.

**Solution:** A pivoted arm lifts the detent roll out of the ratchet and at the same moment drops a rubber-faced brake shoe onto the platen.

**How it works:** A lever pivoted on the platen-frame end bar carries the detent roll on one side of its pivot and a curved brake shoe with soft-rubber facing on the other. A wedge-shaped catch on a leaf spring holds the lever in either of two positions through inclined faces. Normally the roll is spring-pressed into the line-space ratchet teeth and the shoe is held off the platen. Pushing the spring finger piece swaps the positions, so the platen can be turned any distance by hand wheels while the shoe holds it firm.
