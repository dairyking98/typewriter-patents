## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon should feed lengthwise at each stroke, reverse automatically when a spool empties, and shift sideways to present fresh ribbon to successive types.

**Solution:** A gear train driven by carriage spacing feeds the ribbon, a ribbon-held trip releases at spool end to slide a bevel-gear shaft and reverse, and a cross-feed moves the ribbon transversely.

**How it works:** Applied to the Elliott book typewriter. Ribbon spools on the carriage have bevel gears that alternately mesh with bevel gears on a shaft driven during letter or word spacing (carriage forward), a pawl-and-ratchet blocking it on return. A trip strip held by the wound ribbon on each spool is released when the ribbon unwinds and trips an idle part of the mechanism, which shifts the shaft to engage the other spool's gear. A separate worm-gear cross-feed moves the ribbon transversely for a different contact point.
