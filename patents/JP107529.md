## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Toggle-lever shift devices that raise or lower the typebar segment accelerate only one way, so one end position is reached with a hard blow that shakes and makes noise.

**Solution:** Move the segment through a U-shaped wing driven by two toggles at right angles, one carrying the other's middle joint, so speed rises gradually then falls to zero at both ends, with both end positions fixed by one soft common stop.

**How it works:** A U-shaped wing pivoted in the frame is linked by guide links to the typebar segment, which is guided vertically by rollers between rails. Pressing the shift key swings a lever that straightens the driving toggle, which in turn straightens the toggle joined to the wing until it meets a common stop covered with elastic material. The toggle geometry retards the motion strongly near each end, so the shift is soft and silent in both directions. Play-free adjustable point-bearing joints, an arc-slot slider that sets the stroke, and a shift lock using a quarter-round recess on an intermediate lever complete the device.
