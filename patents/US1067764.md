## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide an attachment in the form of a supplemental key for operating the tabulating har and to arrange the supplemental keys at one end of the keyboard in convenient position to be Voperated withV one hand, whereby lthe operator 'will be enabled to move the carriage quickly in either direction the desired distance and instantly lock the carriage at such point.

**How it works (reading):** A supplemental key at one end of the keyboard operates the carriage return and tabulator bar, so the carriage moves and locks without moving the hand.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return and tabulation need the hand to leave the keyboard, which slows writing.

**Solution:** A supplemental key at one end of the keyboard operates the carriage return and tabulator bar, so the carriage moves and locks without moving the hand.
