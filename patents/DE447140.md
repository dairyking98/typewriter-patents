## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A handle with two conical fork claws matching the type shape is hinged to a cleaning brush and can be stowed in a recess when not in use.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Dirt in type faces is hard to remove with a needle, which scratches or cannot reach the edges.

**Solution:** A handle with two conical fork claws matching the type shape is hinged to a cleaning brush and can be stowed in a recess when not in use.
