## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keys in one row are spaced too closely and keys tilt forward when depressed on pivoted levers, causing finger slip.

**Solution:** Shaped key striking surfaces are spaced more widely per row, with a counter-acting feature offsetting forward tilt of the key top when depressed, and a superimposed spacebar.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
