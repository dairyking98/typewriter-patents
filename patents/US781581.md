## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A strip of chemically prepared felt is laid on the platen under the paper fingers like a sheet of paper; the keys are struck onto it to clean the type faces, and the carriage is fed so fresh surface meets the type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Felt or fabric type-cleaning strips were objectionable because their construction and attachment to the platen injured the type and threw them out of alignment.

**Solution:** A strip of chemically prepared felt is laid on the platen under the paper fingers like a sheet of paper; the keys are struck onto it to clean the type faces, and the carriage is fed so fresh surface meets the type.
