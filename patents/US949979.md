## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typewriting and computing functions are wanted in one machine, which the earlier separate machines do not provide.

**Solution:** A combined typewriter and computing machine with escapement and frame arrangements that carry out both functions; the OCR is too poor for the detailed mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
