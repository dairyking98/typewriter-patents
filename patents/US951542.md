## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the carriage escapement mechanism of typewriting machines, and particularly those of the Underwood type.

**How it works (reading):** A dog stop is shifted by a key-operated lever so the loose dog passes either one or two escapement teeth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must move two letter spaces rather than one for headings and emphasis, without a new machine.

**Solution:** A dog stop is shifted by a key-operated lever so the loose dog passes either one or two escapement teeth.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
