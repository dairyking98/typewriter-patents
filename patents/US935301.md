## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my present invention is to provide certain improvements in typewriters of one of the forms of so-c'alled visible writers, wherein provision is made for the mounting of a suiiicient number of type bars in the machine withoutcrowding or placing them in such close juxtaposition as to inter fere with their ready placing, adjustment and removal;

**How it works (reading):** A segmental type-bar hanger beam with improved type-bar bearings, where the form of the bearing compensates for wear and each bar delivers to the printing point accurately.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars must be mounted so they can be removed and aligned without crowding, return to the same place, and wear is compensated.

**Solution:** A segmental type-bar hanger beam with improved type-bar bearings, where the form of the bearing compensates for wear and each bar delivers to the printing point accurately.
