## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In electric type-wheel machines the print magnet must be energised late enough that the wheel has turned to the right letter but be released before the wheel returns, which earlier contact arrangements delayed or smeared.

**Solution:** A special contact arrangement switches the rotary and print magnets on in sequence, closing the print circuit sooner or later according to the wheel's angle of rotation, and switches both off in time.

**How it works:** A type wheel with three superimposed rows sits on a shaft that is lifted against a coil spring to bring the middle or lower row to the paper roller. Pressing a key closes contacts: a rotary magnet turns the wheel to the character, then the print magnet drives the impression. Intermediate parts between key and contacts are decoupled so contacts open regardless of how long the key is held.
