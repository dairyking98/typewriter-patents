## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods

**How it works (reading):** Put a roller on the guide arm so that it runs onto the guide plate surface just before the type reaches the platen, so almost no sliding wear occurs.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The small guiding surfaces on type bar guide arms wear fastest for the common letters like e, spoiling the straightness of the line.

**Solution:** Put a roller on the guide arm so that it runs onto the guide plate surface just before the type reaches the platen, so almost no sliding wear occurs.

**How it works:** The type bar runs on the upper surface of a guide plate and a guide arm is pushed under the plate when a key is struck. At the front end of the guide arm is a roller, which shortly before impact runs onto a surface of the plate and, with the guide surface of the type bar, steers the character into line. Since the roller turns only a fraction of a rotation, rubbing is avoided and alignment holds.
