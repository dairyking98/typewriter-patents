> Description Translated from German Device for automatic feeding of envelopes for typewriters. Devices for the automatic feeding of envelopes are already known for typewriters, in which the envelopes through a feed device fed between the paper roller and the pressure rollers that work together with it "-earth. The device for the automatic feeding of envelopes for Typewriters according to the invention is thereby new and peculiar, claß a switch lever when moving in one direction the platen in its Starting position returns and at the same time the feed device in motion sets and when moving in the opposite direction of the paper roller a partially Rotation granted, with this roller the envelope fed by the feeding device captured and puts him in the position required for writing, while at the same time the previous envelope is ejected. The movements of the paper roller initiated by the switching lever and the feed device are carried out in a particularly simple manner, wherein the last envelope delivered in the position required for writing brought and at the same time the previous envelope with the address is ejected.
