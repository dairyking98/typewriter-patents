> Description Translated from German Copy sheet The invention relates to copy sheets for typewriter and handwriting, which on the front, i.e. the writing surface, with an opaque layer of paint suitable for accepting writing and on the The back is provided with a layer of copying ink. The carbonless sheets previously used for this purpose existed made of paper. In contrast, the essence of the invention is that transparent Cellulose films, e.g. B. Viscose films (the so-called commercial transparencies), can be used as the material for the carbonless sheets. Such transparencies are known per se. They have been due to their transparency and because of other advantages over paper but essentially only for packaging purposes used. By the proposal according to the invention, however, they become a different one and put to a new purpose, for which they are opposed to paper mainly as a result of their particularly suitable for greater resistance. As a result of the greater resilience of the material used, the carbon copy transparencies are almost completely torn excluded, especially for multiple use and longer storage the carbon copies are of great value as files in trade and with authorities.…
