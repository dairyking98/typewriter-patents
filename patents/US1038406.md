## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section; Rollers or like rotary separators separating from the bottom of pile

**How it works (reading):** An inclined paper frame with bands and idler rolls transmits motion to flexibly mounted feed mechanism that separates and angles the envelopes or cards.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier envelope feeders drove their mechanisms poorly and did not separate inclined envelopes or cards reliably.

**Solution:** An inclined paper frame with bands and idler rolls transmits motion to flexibly mounted feed mechanism that separates and angles the envelopes or cards.
