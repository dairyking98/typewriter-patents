## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key levers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewritin machines and more especially to Improved eatures in the fulcrum-pivot by which the rear endsof the key-levers are connected with the frame of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** The rear ends of the key-levers are pivotally attached to the base frame by a transverse rod in two laterally separated groups on each side of the centre line, housed in a hollow base frame with downturned flanges; the OCR leaves the attachment details unclear.
