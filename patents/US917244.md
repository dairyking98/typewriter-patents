## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a type bar and hanger, and particularly to a structure by which the type bar may be adjusted relative to its support and segment and securely held at such adjustment.

**How it works (reading):** A headed bolt draws the hanger against the support when a bearing screw presses on its seat; the hanger pivot runs on ball bearings in the bifurcated type bar end.
