## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A sheet whose leading-edge cut-out is too short cannot be registered by the existing gage on the sheet-adjusting plate.

**Solution:** An extended registering plate with a gage member so that sheets with short or uneven cut-outs can be brought into register on the plate.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
