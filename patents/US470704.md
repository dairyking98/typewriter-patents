## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriting machines in which the type-wheel is moved by a means or power independent of that of the finger-key levers.

**How it works (reading):** A Hammond-assigned keyboard machine whose spring-driven type-wheel is stopped by key-lever stop pins, with twin levers rewinding both springs, a shiftable escapement for variable spacing, and a type-wheel that slides out of the hammer's range to expose the last letter.

## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-driven spring machines needed a single action to rewind the type-wheel and carriage springs, a way to vary letter spacing, and a way to see the last printed letter.

**Solution:** A Hammond-assigned keyboard machine whose spring-driven type-wheel is stopped by key-lever stop pins, with twin levers rewinding both springs, a shiftable escapement for variable spacing, and a type-wheel that slides out of the hammer's range to expose the last letter.

**How it works:** A coiled spring drives the type-wheel through gearing that turns two shafts studded with stop pins; each key lever has an adjustable projection that meets a pin and stops the wheel with the chosen type opposite the impression hammer. A detent lever across all key levers unlocks the gear train a little before the key is fully down, and a spring-cocked hammer then strikes. A rocker key slides the wheel on its square shaft to bring the upper or lower row of the three rows into line. A second spring pulls the carriage through a cord and pulley, released by an oscillating escapement on a rack bar. Two twin levers on one shaft are pulled together to rewind both springs, retract the carriage and line-space. Variable spacing comes from shifting the escapement on a screw cylinder and tilting the wheel standard sideways by half the amount.
