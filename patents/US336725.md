## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Two keys of a keyboard or telegraph transmitter can be struck together and jam or garble the letter.

**Solution:** A spring-returned hinged flap lies across slots in all keys; the slot of a depressed key tilts the flap out of line so no other key can go down until the first returns.

**How it works:** Each key has an inclined slot over a hinged upright flap. Depressing a key makes its slot cam the flap over, which moves it out of line with the slots of all other keys and blocks them. When the key returns, the spring (tension adjustable by a headed pin) restores the flap. A variant uses a rock shaft with two wings.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** key; slot; hinged flap; return spring; adjusting pin; rock shaft
