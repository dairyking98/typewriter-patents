## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** A front lever on the switching shaft presses the pawl into engagement by spring force while it moves, keeping the engagement certain.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Quick line switching could let the pawl miss the ratchet tooth.

**Solution:** A front lever on the switching shaft presses the pawl into engagement by spring force while it moves, keeping the engagement certain.
