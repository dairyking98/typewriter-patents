## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This improvement consists essentially in that the type lever and the buffer ring of the type lever bearing serving as a stop for the same are designed so that the dangerous cross-section is not in the attack Type lever suspension eye, but in a type lever part with a larger cross-section is, and at the same time a type lever pressing down against its bearing axis Force component occurs.

**How it works (reading):** The buffer ring is set at an acute angle to the plane of the type lever bearing, and the type lever has matching inclined noses, so the stop force moves the cross-section away from the eye and presses the lever down against its bearing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A buffer ring stops the type lever at the platen, but the lever's narrow hanging eye is the dangerous cross-section and breaks, and centrifugal force lifts the lever so the characters miss the print line.

**Solution:** The buffer ring is set at an acute angle to the plane of the type lever bearing, and the type lever has matching inclined noses, so the stop force moves the cross-section away from the eye and presses the lever down against its bearing.
