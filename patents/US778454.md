## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On Elliott-Fisher flat-platen machines, cards, bills and sales-sheets must be located and held on the platen without displacement, and reversing a sheet forces tab-stop readjustment.

**Solution:** A set of platen equipment: adjustable card-guiding flexible strips, individually adjustable gauge pins for a reversed sales-sheet, and a machine-operated work clamp shiftable in the line-space direction.

**How it works:** Thin flexible strips run lengthwise over the flat platen, with entrance guides at their front ends, and can be moved transversely to suit card widths while guarding card edges. Billing strips with gauge pins retain a sales-sheet by its file holes; extra pins within the platen limits can be adjusted lengthwise and crosswise so the reversed sheet sits properly without resetting the tabulator. A work clamp or guard shifts over the platen with line spacing and is moved to and from the surface automatically by the machine.

**Features:** [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** flat platen; flexible strip; card guide; billing strip; gauge pin; sales-sheet; work clamp; tabulating stop

**Improves on:**

- Ziegler application Serial 117,717: several adjustable strips instead of one
- Smith application Serial 67,346: adjustable pins within the platen limits
- Eckert and Fisher applications: clamp both shiftable and machine-operated
- US562625 and US513868 (Fisher): platen equipment for the flat-platen machine
