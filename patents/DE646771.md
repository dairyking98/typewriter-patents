## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device on typewriters, calculating machines and similar machines for braking the paper carriage 'The invention relates to a device Typewriters, calculating machines and similar machines for braking the paper cart by braking members that rest against each other when the paper trolley is released to be brought. In known devices of this type occurred as a result of decreasing Force of the carriage tension spring differences in speed in the carriage movement. This was particularly noticeable when writing with long paper carts. The aim of the invention is, in a simple manner, to be as uniform as possible To achieve movement and reliable braking of the triggered paper carriage. According to the invention, this is essentially achieved in that for regulating the speed of the paper cart with regard to the decreasing force of the carriage tension spring on the at least one of the braking members, preferably. braking force exerted by a spring is automatically reduced while the carriage is running.
