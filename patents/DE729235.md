**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (booking engine with journal sheet) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**How it works (reading):** The alignment rail lies below half the roller and pivots up into contact, driven by a lever that also releases the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An alignment rail that is up and blocks the view causes parallax and inserting account sheets is hard.

**Solution:** The alignment rail lies below half the roller and pivots up into contact, driven by a lever that also releases the paper.
