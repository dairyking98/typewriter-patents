## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book type-writers use type-bars; a cylindrical type-wheel cannot print a flat page because its characters are on a curved surface.

**Solution:** A carriage slides along a base over the page and carries a type-wheel printing mechanism that moves across the carriage and advances itself to space letters.

**How it works:** The type-wheel, rotated and vibrated by its mechanism, moves the printing mechanism to form letter and word spaces; a line-spacer key-lever advances the whole carriage toward the front of the base.
