## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Hammond, pressing a key on one side of the keyboard before releasing a key on the other side makes the second driving arm jam against the hub of the shuttle arm, locking the shuttle until the first key is let go.

**Solution:** Fit the shuttle arm with two laterally extending pivoted arms that rest against fixed posts, so a second driver always pushes the shuttle back the other way as soon as the first is released.

**How it works:** The Hammond shuttle (type carrier) is moved by a shuttle arm on a post, turned by two driving arms with pins that engage notches on the arm's hub. Each lateral arm is pivoted to the hub and its free end rests on a fixed post. When one driver swings the shuttle, the opposite driver's pin lands on the lateral arm between its pivot and its post, not on the hub rim. The pressure is therefore live and starts the shuttle moving the other way once the first key is released. The arms and shuttle arm form one body that replaces the existing arm on a standard Hammond with no other change.
