## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**How it works (reading):** All mechanisms are mounted on one universal plate that forms a rigid whole, is adjusted outside the machine, and is then fitted into the frame, with the plate able to be turned on all sides.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machine frames enclose the mechanisms, so adjustment and access are difficult and screw holes drift when the frame is assembled from parts.

**Solution:** All mechanisms are mounted on one universal plate that forms a rigid whole, is adjusted outside the machine, and is then fitted into the frame, with the plate able to be turned on all sides.

**How it works:** Bearing pieces and combs are bolted to the universal plate, which is then placed in the machine frame as one unit.
