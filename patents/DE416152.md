## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter. In the typewriters known so far, the Movement mechanisms in their majority attached to the frame. This surrounds the mechanism the typewriter on all sides. Occasionally there were those that support the mechanisms The frames are still surrounded by a special closed housing. With these well-known Machines, the adjustment process was difficult to carry out because of the mechanism surrounding frame made this mechanism difficult to access. According to the invention, this disadvantage is eliminated in that a Universal plate carries all mechanisms exposed on all sides, preferably by means of studs. The mechanism can then be finished on this universal plate can be attached and adjusted, then as a whole together with the universal plate to be used in the machine frame.
