## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Writing several copies at once on a large flat-platen typewriter that can also type into bound books and ledgers.

**Solution:** A flat tilting platen with a self-levelling sliding book rest, a gantry carriage running on a tiltable track, and typebars whose heads carry three types and are rocked to the case by a half-ring behind the bars.

**How it works:** The flat platen hangs on a spring hinge and rises automatically, with hand-wheel rack-and-pinion height and lever front/back adjustment; a book rest with an evener linkage holds the open book level as leaves transfer. A carriage hangs from rails on a cross support whose side supports run on hinged tracks beside the platen, line-spaced by a clock-spring-driven ratchet-and-rack. Each U-shaped typebar is thrown by a key lever through a toggle joint that gives quiet contact, quick return and locking, and its head, carrying three types, is rocked by a tail riding a half-ring raised by caps and figures keys. Any key works a universal bar that steps letter-spacing (two-pawl rack, one or two spaces), a multi-colour ribbon feed and a ribbon guide that drops to the print point and rises again for visible writing. Tabulator stops, margin stops, a line finder and paper and carbon rolls complete it.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Patent No. 7850. Specification. Flat-platen typewriter (ruby: "flat platen typewriter"). Filed 23 June Meiji 36 (1903); granted 13 October Meiji 37 (1904). Object. This invention relates to a flat-platen typewriter used generally in business and for writing in books. Its object is a combination of the parts needed to write several copies at one time. Special features. The invention comprises: a flat platen that moves horizontally and is hinged so that it can be tilted up; a device for moving that platen; a sliding book rest under the platen with self-adjusting plates; a device for working and fixing the sliding book rest; a hinged, tiltable track for a carriage, set beside the platen; a carriage frame running on the track; a printing mechanism traversing that carriage frame; a device for tilting the track automatically; a device for adjusting the track forward and back; a device on the carriage frame giving regular, automatic line spacing; a letter-spacing device; movable margin stops and a tabulating device; a line finder; typebars with type heads carrying several types, with a device that sets one of those types so that it comes to the printing face at the start of the typebar'…
