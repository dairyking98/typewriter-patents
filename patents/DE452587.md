## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Clutches used to return the writing carriage give a hard start and a hard stop at the end of the return.

**Solution:** A motor drives a cam disc that makes one revolution and switches the motor off again; the cam swings a toothed-segment lever that turns cord pulleys, so the carriage moves slowly at the start, speeds up, and slows at the end without a clutch.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for the automatic retraction of the writing and calculation slides - on typewriters. The invention relates to a device for the automatic Retrieval of the writing slide and at the same time the calculation slide at Typewriters with independently working writing slides and Computing slide according to patent 437283. Devices are already known on typewriters, to return the writing carriage to its starting position by means of an electric motor, for starting and stopping the drive, claw clutches, friction or electromagnetic clutches have been used. These types of drives usually result Difficulty getting around the hard start and the hard bump at the end of the way back to avoid. The invention is used for the retraction of the writing or computing carriage no clutch, but drives a cam disk by an electric motor, which always switch the motor off again after one revolution. This cam disc takes on a part of its rotation with a lever arm, which in connection with a Toothed segment two cord pulleys set in rotation, from which a cord pull or tape transmits the movement to the carriages in a known manner.
