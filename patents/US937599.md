## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A portable typewriter where all the writing stays visible to the operator.

**Solution:** Fixed open-ended frame with a sliding work carriage under the overhang, cranked type bars on slides that swing type from inking pads onto the paper, carriage moves rearward as writing proceeds.

**How it works:** The work carriage holds a loose sheet or open book. It travels longitudinally for line spacing in guides that themselves travel transversely for letter spacing. A spring drum pulls a bar endwise and an escapement anchor released by a transverse bar under all key levers meters one letter space per key. Type bars slide in fixed tubular guides with cam slots, so depressing a key moves the bar forward and partly rotates it to bring its type from an inking pad to the paper.

**Features:** [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** open-ended frame; work carriage; cross-travelling guides; cranked type bar; tubular guide with cam slot; inking pad; spring drum; escapement anchor; transverse release bar
