**How it works (reading):** Resilient tongue is cut with two perpendicular slots so the cap can be pushed on partly, with clearance for adjacent keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Replaceable key caps on key levers are hard to fit when neighboring keys are in the way.

**Solution:** Resilient tongue is cut with two perpendicular slots so the cap can be pushed on partly, with clearance for adjacent keys.
