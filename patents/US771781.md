## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the invention is to provide a type-writing machine with an improved carriage feed or escapement which produces the usual step-by-step movements of the carriage for ordinary Writing and enables the carriage to be moved irregular distances by the operation of a key to produce special work, such as tabulated or column work.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage escapement driven by a spring drum with a key-operated irregular-travel feature using tabulator disks, plus a single-space set-back device.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-strike visible machine needs a carriage feed that steps normally, can move irregular distances for column work, and can back up one space for corrections.

**Solution:** A carriage escapement driven by a spring drum with a key-operated irregular-travel feature using tabulator disks, plus a single-space set-back device.

**How it works:** The platen carriage slides on a ball-bearing track with a sheet-metal sliding bearing-bar and runs on round steel rods; coil springs hold it up. A driving drum pulls the carriage and an escapement releases it step by step with each key stroke. Operating a special key lets the carriage move irregular distances for tabulating, using tabulator disks. A set-back device moves the carriage back one space at a time for reprinting or insertion.

**Features:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; bearing bar; ball race; driving drum; escapement; tabulator disk; key lever; typebar; ribbon
