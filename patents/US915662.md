## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapements jump and jerk between impressions, causing noise and uneven spacing.

**Solution:** Place the escapement under positive speed control of the impression mechanism by a two-dog escape wheel, so the carriage feeds part before and part after each impression.

**How it works:** An escape wheel with cycloidal teeth sits on a shaft geared by ratchet gearing to the carriage rack. A forked lever from the oscillating gate carries two independently sprung dogs, one normally in, one normally out, that play between the teeth. As a key is pressed the dogs move with the key action, releasing and re-locking the wheel in steps so the movement is split before and after the impression and the carriage is held rigid at the stroke limits. Shown on a Smith Premier.
