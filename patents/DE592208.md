## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Levers having moving or variable fulcra to alter the mechanical advantage during the stroke

**How it works (reading):** Type levers are arranged in radial planes with their drive rods and rockers, the key levers are aligned along a straight support rod, and each rocker sits on its own bearing block hooked into radial slots of the frame plate, giving equal transmission for all keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Horizontal type-lever segments with a fixed key layout produce lateral tilting forces and need different key lever lengths.

**Solution:** Type levers are arranged in radial planes with their drive rods and rockers, the key levers are aligned along a straight support rod, and each rocker sits on its own bearing block hooked into radial slots of the frame plate, giving equal transmission for all keys.
