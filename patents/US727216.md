## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case shifting mechanisms for a single set of type-bars.

**Solution:** Case-shifting arrangement with key-locking device for Remington No. 6.

**How it works:** Drawings show the case shift in progress with a key-locking device. Only the opening is available.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md)

**Parts:** shift key; locking device; type-bar; carriage
