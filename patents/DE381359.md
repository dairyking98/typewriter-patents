## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen-to-line-wheel clutches could be released only from one side and jam when dirty.

**Solution:** A rod sliding in the hollow platen axle carries a driver that engages the line wheel bore, held by springs and released by pushing a button at either end.

**How it works:** The platen has a hollow axle holding a rod with a driver in a slot. The driver's toothed ends engage the finely toothed bore of the line index wheel, coupling it to the platen. Compression springs on both sides hold the rod in engagement. Pressing the button at either end pushes the driver out of the bore, freeing the platen.
