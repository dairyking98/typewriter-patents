## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars of differing lengths strike the platen with different arcs and impact force.

**Solution:** Pivot equal-length typebars on a circular arc concentric with the printing point so every type head swings an equal arc and strikes with equal force.

**How it works:** Typebars sit in two groups on vertical pivot rods in segmental frames at both sides of the printing point, with equal radius to it. Their inner ends are at different heights so the bars pass clear of each other, and all heads swing in one horizontal plane. Springs hold the heads against a curved rest; key levers throw them inward. The platen is a horizontal wheel or disc turning on a vertical axis with a narrow marginal face one letter wide.

**Parts:** typebar; pivot rod; segmental frame; type head; type-bar rest; spring; key lever; disc platen
