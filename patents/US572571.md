## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (music-writing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide a machine of simple, strong, and durable con struction that may be easily and rapidly operated to print musical characters upon a printed stafi.

**How it works (reading):** A sliding frame carrying the paper, moved on a rack by a pinion and hand-wheel below a fixed indicator, positions the type against the staff lines.
