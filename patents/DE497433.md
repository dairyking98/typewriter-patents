**How it works (reading):** The two digit rows of the month-date keys are reused as register rows for ordinary accounting; the twelve-key month row drives a step rail that prints the month name, and the printing unit is split behind the second digit row.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculating machines with printing need three key columns for monthly dates, which leaves fewer rows of items for arithmetic.

**Solution:** The two digit rows of the month-date keys are reused as register rows for ordinary accounting; the twelve-key month row drives a step rail that prints the month name, and the printing unit is split behind the second digit row.
