## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known pneumatic typewriters keep air leaking through open pipes and holding parts by closing outlets, wasting pressure and giving uncertain setting.

**Solution:** Branch compressed air from a central reservoir through key-controlled valves to drive and setting devices that stop their own motion, so air is only spent doing work.

**How it works:** An air tank feeds a pressure-reducing valve and a branching pipe network with control valves moved by the keys, whose movable bridges are taken along by the key but return independently. Some branches go straight to drives that interrupt their own motion; others go through setting devices to drives that move other parts. The cylindrical type carrier is shifted axially by one drive and turned by a second, either pendulum-like or continuously in both directions, so the new character is reached by the shortest path even from a different type group. Spring-loaded valves are latched and released for sudden movement, and braking and locking devices hold parts at the desired position. The paper carrier is moved by its own air drive on a common shaft.
