## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers or sets of front or rear rollers each mounted on a separate carrier

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the following: If there is a change in the paper thickness at one point on the paper, this way, one of the pinch rollers becomes farther than the others from the paper guide roller removed, and the two rolls to the right and left full of this druchiolle are exposed to the pressure exerted on the first roll and lie down more forcefully against the paper.

**How it works (reading):** Front and rear pinch roller pairs are each sprung independently on supported pivots so they can take an inclined position over varying paper thickness.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known paper guides use rigid forks that cannot tilt for varying paper thickness and are weakly supported.

**Solution:** Front and rear pinch roller pairs are each sprung independently on supported pivots so they can take an inclined position over varying paper thickness.
