## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improve the letter-spacing of the inventor's earlier electrical typewriter (US367650).

**Solution:** An electromagnetic letter-spacing circuit advances the carriage one space after each type strike.

**How it works:** Type levers are fulcrumed in adjustable supports on a ring above a circular base, each linked by a chain to the armature of its own electromagnet. A ring of metallic segments and circuit controllers close a spacing circuit on each impact so a magnet releases an escapement and the carriage moves by one letter space. Parts are on an insulating base and ring.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** circular base; platform; non-conducting ring; metal segment; type lever; adjustable support; chain; armature; electromagnet; circuit controller
