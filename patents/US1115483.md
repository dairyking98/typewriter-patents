## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The shiftable type-bar frame of a three-case machine was heavy and hard to move to three positions.

**Solution:** Shift with two toggles joined by a balanced lever; each toggle moves the frame to one position while the other acts as a fulcrum.

**How it works:** The shiftable segment frame is spring-counterbalanced by a contractile spring with screw tension adjustment. A lever of equal arms joins two toggles; one toggle breaks backward by a shift key to move the frame to the second position, the other toggle by a second key to the third. The unused toggle serves as the fulcrum of the lever.
