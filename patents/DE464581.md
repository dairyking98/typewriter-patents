## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (setting device for cash register printing)

**How it works (reading):** Intermediate gears with internal and external teeth, with spacer disks on their own axles, drive the loose type wheels so any number can be used on one shaft set.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Telescoping hollow-shaft drives limit how many type wheels fit in one row.

**Solution:** Intermediate gears with internal and external teeth, with spacer disks on their own axles, drive the loose type wheels so any number can be used on one shaft set.
