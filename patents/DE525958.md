## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing unit with type wheels for tabulators)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing unit for punched card tabulating machines and the like.

**How it works (reading):** Each type wheel is disengaged from the drive when it stops and is then driven back to its start position by gear segments, so the impression is made on a stationary wheel and sharp.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjustable type wheels that keep turning while the impression is made print blurred characters, and the gear dead play affects accuracy.

**Solution:** Each type wheel is disengaged from the drive when it stops and is then driven back to its start position by gear segments, so the impression is made on a stationary wheel and sharp.
