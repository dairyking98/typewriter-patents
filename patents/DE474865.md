## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** A holder plate on T-shaped rails with snap-ball detents and a double bearing locks in any position by pressure without screws.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Template holders need screws loosened to reposition, which is slow.

**Solution:** A holder plate on T-shaped rails with snap-ball detents and a double bearing locks in any position by pressure without screws.
