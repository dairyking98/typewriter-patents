**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists of a device which causes the spacing-key, when depressed, to move the inking-ribbon for a little distance, thus lpresenting a fresh ink-surface of ribbon to the type.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The printed line is hidden by the ribbon, so errors are discovered late.

**Solution:** Arrange the ribbon so the line is visible, and add ribbon motion by the space key, one-space paper feed per key via a cord, and automatic line feed on carriage return.

**How it works:** The ribbon is held by supports so the operator can see the page. A cord runs over the rounded teeth of a cross-plate beneath the keys; any depressed key lever deflects it, pulling the paper carrier one space. The space key also moves the ribbon a short distance to present fresh ink. Returning the carriage left to right advances the paper one line.

**Features:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon support; key lever; toothed cross-plate; cord; paper carrier; tube; spacing key
