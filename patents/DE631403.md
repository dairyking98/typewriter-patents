## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for changing the switching range of the paper slide of typewriters with directly engaging the rack Ratchet.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoting rack body whose teeth sit at different radial distances selects the range, and a double-armed ratchet with a long feed pawl and a limiting arm sets the travel for each tooth profile.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the paper-carriage switching range with a rotating rack needs many special pawls, and the tooth-depth approach gives unfavourable engagement and wear.

**Solution:** A pivoting rack body whose teeth sit at different radial distances selects the range, and a double-armed ratchet with a long feed pawl and a limiting arm sets the travel for each tooth profile.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** pivoting rack body with two tooth profiles; double-armed switching ratchet; feed pawl; limiting pawl arm
