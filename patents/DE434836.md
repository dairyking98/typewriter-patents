## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to power-driven Typewriters in which the type lever is inevitably used by a camshaft Stop to be brought to the paper roller, and the purpose of this machine is a To create a device that prevents drive links from breaking in the event of overloads avoids.

**How it works (reading):** An overload clutch on the camshaft consists of a socket with ratchet teeth on one end face, pushed against a second ratchet socket by a spring; the sockets slip when the type lever stops early, so the drive links do not break.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** When a type lever is stopped before the end of its travel, a tooth on the drive can break, because the drive force keeps acting on it.

**Solution:** An overload clutch on the camshaft consists of a socket with ratchet teeth on one end face, pushed against a second ratchet socket by a spring; the sockets slip when the type lever stops early, so the drive links do not break.
