## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**How it works (reading):** The stop rod is built from several stacked, punched sheet-metal plates with aligned slots, riveted into one rigid bar that resists sagging without stiffeners.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Column stop rods were costly to slot by milling, and single rods sagged or bent sideways when several were used together.

**Solution:** The stop rod is built from several stacked, punched sheet-metal plates with aligned slots, riveted into one rigid bar that resists sagging without stiffeners.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Column stop bar. The table writers of the Typewriters usual column stop rods, which for inserting the tabs or column stops, get their slots into which the tabs are inserted usually by milling or similar operations that have the disadvantage have that the machining of the stop rod is costly and time consuming. aside from that these rods are usually kept quite weak, so that there is a risk of that they can be bent sideways or downwards. These evils occur then particularly noticeable when in a known table writing device Way a plurality of column rods is used next to each other. The invention eliminates these disadvantages of the previous column stop rods in that the rods from a plurality of relatively thin sheet metal plates or sheet metal strips are assembled in which the slots for the column stops or riders are made by rods. The sheet metal strips or sheet metal plates are placed on top of each other in such a way that the punched slots fit exactly together and then become a rigid whole through rivets or screws that are pulled through united.
