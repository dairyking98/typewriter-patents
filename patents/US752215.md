## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a feed mechanism for platens of type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A platen feed mechanism rotates the platen independently of the usual line-spacing mechanism, so any point of the paper can be brought to the printing line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Any point on the paper cannot be brought to the printing line without adjusting the paper on the platen, and the line-spacing mechanism is too coarse for this.

**Solution:** A platen feed mechanism rotates the platen independently of the usual line-spacing mechanism, so any point of the paper can be brought to the printing line.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
