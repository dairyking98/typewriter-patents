## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The endwise-shifting shaft that drives the ribbon spools and reverses ribbon direction needs a simple, efficient detent to hold it in either position.

**Solution:** Two spring-mounted detent rollers engage notch pairs cut in a collar on the shaft to hold it yieldingly at either end position.

**How it works:** Ribbon spools on horizontal shafts carry bevel gears that mesh alternately with bevel pinions on a transverse driving shaft. Sliding the shaft endwise brings one pinion or the other into mesh, reversing ribbon feed. A spring drum with ratchet and pawl turns a bevel gear only when the carriage moves left, driving the shaft through a sleeve and a pin that lets the shaft slide. Two notch pairs on an elongated collar are engaged by two rollers on the arms of one sheet-metal spring joined by a yoke, which straddles the shaft and is screwed to the sleeve block.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; spool shaft; bevel gear; bevel pinion; driving shaft; spring drum; ratchet wheel; pawl; sleeve; collar; detent roller; sheet-metal spring

**Numbers:** two notch pairs; two detent rollers

**Improves on:**

- earlier detents for the Remington visible shifting shaft: one sheet-metal spring carries both rollers
