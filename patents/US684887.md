## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to carriage-escapement mechanism for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** The escapement throw can be changed by an adjustment so the carriage is released either just after the impression (natural feed) or just before it (speed feed).

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapement needs to release the carriage either just before or just after the impression, and the existing arrangements did not allow simple change between the two.

**Solution:** The escapement throw can be changed by an adjustment so the carriage is released either just after the impression (natural feed) or just before it (speed feed).

**How it works:** An adjustable throw of an escapement part sets whether the carriage escapes before or after the type strikes.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** escapement part with adjustable throw

**Numbers:** US 684,887; 1901-10-22
