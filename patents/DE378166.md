## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The ribbon reversal drive of calculating machines did not reliably reverse the ribbon at the end of a spool.

**Solution:** A feeler lever gradually stiffens a lever linkage so the switching lever engages the ribbon spool drive in the right order.
