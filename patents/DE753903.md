## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with automatic multiplier) · **Subject (patent classes):** Calculating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with an automatic multiplier device, both factors using the same keyboard (ten key pad) and by from this adjustable setting and transmission elements in from the tabulating process of the paper carriage selectable gear trains to be brought into engagement with the transmission organs the multiplier.

**How it works (reading):** A locking device, switched on and off by the tabulation movement of the paper carriage, stops the product plant from moving prematurely into the transfer organs while the multiplicand is keyed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter multiplier must be compact, but the multiplier and product gear trains clash when the multiplicand is keyed in.

**Solution:** A locking device, switched on and off by the tabulation movement of the paper carriage, stops the product plant from moving prematurely into the transfer organs while the multiplicand is keyed.
