## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in type-writers, and has to do more particularly with back-spacingv mechanism adapted for use when it is desired to movel the carriage back a space, or as many spaces as desired for that matter, "the mechanism being a1"- ranged at one side of the keyboard, so that the operator can back-space the carriage by ressing his thumb.

**How it works (reading):** A rocking lever at the side of the keyboard has a thumbpiece and a dog that engages teeth on the carriage rack, so each rock backspaces the carriage one space.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving the carriage back requires the operator to reach out and grasp it by hand.

**Solution:** A rocking lever at the side of the keyboard has a thumbpiece and a dog that engages teeth on the carriage rack, so each rock backspaces the carriage one space.

**How it works:** The thumbpiece rocks the lever, and the dog's tooth engages between two rack teeth and moves the carriage back one space per stroke.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** rocking lever; thumbpiece; dog; carriage rack; pivot bearings
