## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Simple multiplication should be shown in figures by a keyboard, without complex arithmetic.

**Solution:** A keyboard of 81 keys in nine rows and nine columns, each key carrying a product type, so two-figure addition gives results.

**How it works:** Keys carry digits and product types so that one key pair shows the product.
