## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil cutting needs the ribbon kept away from the printing point, but the ribbon vibrator moves it up at every stroke.

**Solution:** A rocker arm driving the ribbon lever can be uncoupled from the constantly vibrating arm by sliding a wrist out of its slot with a finger-piece marked S and R.

**How it works:** On the Underwood front-strike machine the universal bar rocks an inclined slotted arm at each stroke. A wrist in a carrier engages that slot and rocks an arm whose slotted extension moves a bellcrank ribbon lever. Sliding the carrier along its shaft by a shifter arm and rock shaft withdraws the wrist so the ribbon stays below the printing point. A detent spring holds the finger-piece in either position.

**Features:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; rocker arm; wrist; carrier; shifter arm; finger-piece; detent spring; universal bar

**Improves on:**

- Underwood ribbon vibrator: adds a stencil switch to stop ribbon lift
