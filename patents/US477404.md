## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typebar machine whose type mechanism travels on the carriage should be usable as a book/flat-bed printer with removable stand and paper feed.

**Solution:** Legs or feet hold either carriage or type mechanism stationary, and a removable stand with a strip platen and feed rolls attaches to the carriage.

**How it works:** An annular plate carries the pivots of ring-arranged typebars with key rods above; the plate rides on carriage grooves via roller guides and adjustable roll-gibs that remove lost motion for good alignment. A spring-drum with a wrenched, automatically pawl-locked spindle shifts the type mechanism by cord. A stand with foot pieces, a T-bar, a flat strip platen and a feed roll with spring-pressed rolls locates onto the carriage by steady-pins. Ribbon spools, a spacing lever with rack, and a bell are conventional.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
