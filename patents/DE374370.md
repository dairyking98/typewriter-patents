## Summary

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Parallelogram-guided shift rails for segment-shift typewriters take different positions relative to the typebars in raised and lowered positions, giving uneven shift travel.

**Solution:** Split the shift rail into a base body moving horizontally and a rail that rises and falls on it with the bearing segment, keeping a constant relationship to the typebars.

**How it works:** The base body is guided by levers in a parallelogram and moves only horizontally. The actual shift rail, carrying the type lever bearing segment, slides vertically in slots on fixed bolts of the base body and is pushed by noses on the typebars. Pins keep its position unchanged relative to the typebars and the bearing segment.

**Features:** [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** base body; shift rail; type lever bearing segment; parallelogram lever; slot; bolt; pin

**Improves on:**

- parallelogram-guided shift rail: avoids uneven shift travel from imprecise pivots
