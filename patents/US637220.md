## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (hand printing device for cards) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes hand-held

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved hand printing instrument for the purpose of print 'ing window-cards, show or display cards, notices, &c.

**How it works (reading):** Type-levers guided in a circular ring and a rotary disk print through a frame that rolls along a serrated guide bar, with a spring-pawl spacer that locks the position on the bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Window cards and display notices need quick hand printing, and the printing frame must move smoothly along a guide.

**Solution:** Type-levers guided in a circular ring and a rotary disk print through a frame that rolls along a serrated guide bar, with a spring-pawl spacer that locks the position on the bar.
