## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar key-levers were costly to make and hard to attach to or detach from their links.

**Solution:** A key-lever stamped from one flat metal blank in a single die blow, with a snap-on link connection and a guide extension.

**How it works:** The lever is die-struck as a flat blank with a bearing for the straight pivot shaft, an upturned finger end and a vertical guide extension that resists twisting. The rear end is slit into three tongues that form a quick detachable pivot joint with the link to the type arm. The finger-piece is a round shell holding a glass plate and a character card, fixed by bending lugs onto two quadrant plates. The same construction can be fitted to existing levers.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** key lever; bearing; guide extension; link; finger-piece shell; glass protector plate; character card
