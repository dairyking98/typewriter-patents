## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Secret telegraphy needs complicated special apparatus to convert plain text to cipher and back.

**Solution:** Common remote printers with typewriter-like keyboards and type wheels are used, and the key designations or type sequence are changed by agreement so the transmitted text appears as cipher to unauthorised stations.
