## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The usual line-spacing mechanism cannot turn the platen forward a large, variable arc, so the machine cannot address envelopes or start writing at a chosen point; condensed billing also needs a backward turn.

**Solution:** A crank that is normally disconnected from the platen can be connected to it, through reversible gearing, so the initial turn of the crank moves the platen forward or backward a mechanically limited arc of variable extent.

**How it works:** A clutch connects the crank to the platen through reversible gears; a stop limits the arc, and the same mechanism serves condensed charging.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** crank; platen; reversible gearing; clutch; stop
