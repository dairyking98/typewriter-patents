## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (electromagnetic control punch card machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Card magazines, e.g. pocket, hopper

**How it works (reading):** Brushes have no direct connection to the magnet; a thermionic valve under brush control lets a capacitor discharge excite the electromagnet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Brush contacts on card-sensing machines suffer sparking and need direct-current feeding, and they feed back current.

**Solution:** Brushes have no direct connection to the magnet; a thermionic valve under brush control lets a capacitor discharge excite the electromagnet.
