## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators spend much effort on the space bar between words; yielding key connections are unwanted because they weaken typebar action and manifolding blows.

**Solution:** An attachment lets any type-key, held down, advance the platen a double space so no separate space stroke is needed.

**How it works:** Fitted to a Remington typebar machine, the attachment acts on the escapement so that a maintained depression of a type-key gives an extra step to the toothed carriage bar after the letter prints. The key keeps its normal positive stroke and the typebar connection is unchanged. Detail of the mechanism is beyond the opening.
