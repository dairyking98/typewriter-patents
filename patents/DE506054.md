## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machines with removable platens needed a special line-feed gear, so the platen could not be taken out with the simple, direct top-acting pawl gear.

**Solution:** Mount the line pawl, line lever and linkages on one support piece that folds up on a pin behind the ratchet, so the platen can be lifted out without altering the gear.

**How it works:** The platen has a hollow spindle in an open-topped bearing slot at one end and a stepped sleeve with a finger-wheel spindle at the other. A locking plate engages a groove in the platen pin to hold it. The line ratchet, line lever, return spring, line-space setting and limits all sit on a support piece that pivots on a short shaft at its rear. A spring pawl on the end plate secures the support piece in working position; pressing it lets the piece fold up, which frees the left end of the platen for lifting out.
