## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** A control member linked to the clutch button, operated by a palm key, allows engaging and disengaging the carriage lift by hand so it can stop in any position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Car elevator clutch engages from a single stop, so the carriage cannot stop at any column without a tab key.

**Solution:** A control member linked to the clutch button, operated by a palm key, allows engaging and disengaging the carriage lift by hand so it can stop in any position.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
