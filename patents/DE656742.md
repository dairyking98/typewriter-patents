## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Structure of the surface

**How it works (reading):** A protector of two fabric sheets glued together, with a stiffening impregnation or hard elastic strips along the face and corners, gives the rigidity needed to slide over the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Celluloid platen protectors are too stiff and oilcloth or cardboard too soft, so paper wraps around the roller and stops sliding high enough.

**Solution:** A protector of two fabric sheets glued together, with a stiffening impregnation or hard elastic strips along the face and corners, gives the rigidity needed to slide over the platen.
