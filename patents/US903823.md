## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to ribbon'actuating mechanism for typewriting machines intended more particularly for application to machines of the kind known as the Oliver typewriter, such as is shown and described in the prior patent granted to Thomas Oliver, No.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon actuating mechanism with a ribbon lift and feed, shown in several figures of sections; the claims cover the ribbon-feed mechanism as applied to the Oliver machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon actuating mechanism of the Oliver typewriter needs improvements, and some of these improvements are also wanted for machines of different construction.

**Solution:** A ribbon actuating mechanism with a ribbon lift and feed, shown in several figures of sections; the claims cover the ribbon-feed mechanism as applied to the Oliver machine.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
