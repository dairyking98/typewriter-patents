## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-break levers on the carriage must lift the pressure rollers together with the line feed for invoice work.

**Solution:** The line switch operating lever shares a bearing with the release lever and carries a pin; an eccentric tooth lever lifts the pressure rollers as the line feed runs.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** operating lever; eccentric tooth lever
