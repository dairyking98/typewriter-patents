## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The springs that return the key-levers after they operate the type-bars cannot have their tension adjusted easily.

**Solution:** Coiled retracting springs hang from a cast spring bridge bar, and their tension can be adjusted there so each key-lever returns to its normal position.
