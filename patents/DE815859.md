## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Ribbon switching device for cash registers, arithmetic, accounting or similar machines. The invention relates to a ribbon switching device for Cash registers, calculators, accounting or similar machines. For reasons of price, the automatic one in simple machines The switching of the ribbon has been replaced by a manual switch. Such Device is very cheap to manufacture, but has the disadvantage that it is easy to forget to switch the ribbon by hand. If then, a large number of bookings are made without switching the ribbon, this leads to indistinct prints on the receipts. In order to still be able to use manual switching, according to the invention, in order to achieve at least one advance during the day, the ribbon advance mechanism is coupled with any setting mechanism that is set at least once a day when the system is put into operation, for example with the date setting device, the switching device for the consecutive number or the device for locking or unlocking the machine.
