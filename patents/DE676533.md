## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a table with more than two movable top parts, the middle fixed part must be able to lower or raise the outer parts so they form a multi-level top.

**Solution:** Double-armed pivot levers with slots on the fixed middle part engage bolts on the front and rear top parts, so that the outer parts move in opposite directions and their height is set by slot length and lever pivot points.
