> Description Translated from German Table, in particular typewriter table, with interdependent movable table plate portions addition to Patent 661979 The invention relates to a further embodiment of the table according to Patent 661 979, in which the connection of two successively arranged. Table top parts for generating a counter-rotating movement of the parts is preferably carried out by gears. According to the invention, when using more than two table top parts one behind the other, the side supports of the central, fixed table top part are each provided with a double-armed pivot lever, the arm ends of which are provided with slots include bolts attached to the front and rear side supports so that the front and rear table top parts face one another can lower or raise the stationary middle table top part in order to form a multi-level table top. The difference in height and distance between the table top parts can be determined by the length of the slots in the helmets and also by the depression or lifting of the pivot points of the levers on the fixed central table top part determine.
