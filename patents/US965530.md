## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates particularly to the manner in which the stop-bars .

**How it works (reading):** Several stop bars each carry its own stop members and are mounted on the frame so any one can be brought into active position, releasing the carriage to stop at the columns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Tabulating stops of different column widths must be changed one at a time on the frame.

**Solution:** Several stop bars each carry its own stop members and are mounted on the frame so any one can be brought into active position, releasing the carriage to stop at the columns.
