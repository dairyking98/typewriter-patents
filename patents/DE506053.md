## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for noiseless return of the paper car from typewriters, in the known manner when the car is withdrawn a friction member influenced by the jam wheel removes the loose pawl from the area the ratchet teeth swing out and up to the reversal of the hemnirad rotation in the swing out Holds position.

**How it works (reading):** An escape wheel carries a concentric disc with an inclined edge that drives a leaf-spring friction member, which keeps the loose pawl out of the ratchet teeth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pawl disengagement by the friction member on carriage return is not reliably driven.

**Solution:** An escape wheel carries a concentric disc with an inclined edge that drives a leaf-spring friction member, which keeps the loose pawl out of the ratchet teeth.
