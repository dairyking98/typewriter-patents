## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar key-levers on front-strike machines are heavy, frictional, and bulky for a foldable portable machine; the typebar should accelerate toward the platen.

**Solution:** Edgewise-rocking sheet-steel key-levers bear on a flat plate and link to the typebars near their pivots, giving an accelerating typebar motion with individual spring tension.

**How it works:** Thin sheet-steel key-levers stand on edge and rock on a transverse bearing plate, with a projection in a plate aperture as the fulcrum. The inner end of each lever is linked to its typebar close to the typebar pivot, so a small key travel throws the type head to the platen with rising speed. Each lever has its own spring lever and spring, adjustable by hole position, and a dust plate covers the levers. Levers and typebars lie close together so the carriage and platen can fold over the keyboard for transport.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** key-lever; bearing plate; link; typebar; spring lever; spring; dust plate; folding platen carriage

**Numbers:** three banks of key-levers
