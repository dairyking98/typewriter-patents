## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to visible or front strike typewriting machines in which the platen and carriage may be folded or shifted into compact relation to another part of the machine.
