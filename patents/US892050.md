## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The main objects of my invention are to provide simplified and improved tabulating mechanism for typewritmg machines;

**How it works (reading):** A single key shifts the carriage to a chosen column, with stops at units, tens and hundreds set by a clip stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage column positioning needs many key moves and separate stops for each column.

**Solution:** A single key shifts the carriage to a chosen column, with stops at units, tens and hundreds set by a clip stop.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
