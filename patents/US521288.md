## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in i the mechanism for moving the ink-ribbon in type-writing machines ofthat class in which the ribbon is mounted upon two reels and is wound alternately from one reel on to the other.

**How it works (reading):** A pin in the ribbon lifts a lever into the carriage path, so the carriage movement shifts the reel drive automatically.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On two-reel ribbon machines the operator must engage the other reel by hand at the end of the ribbon.

**Solution:** A pin in the ribbon lifts a lever into the carriage path, so the carriage movement shifts the reel drive automatically.

**How it works:** Two vertical rods driven from the keys carry pawls that turn ratchet wheels on the reel shafts, and a shifting bar engages one pawl and frees the other. A pin through the ribbon near its end lifts a hinged plate on a light frame at the reel, which raises a horizontal lever into the path of an abutment plate on the platen carriage. The carriage then pushes the lever, moving the shifting bar and reversing the ribbon without attention.
