## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage return lever and line-space action of the Remington needed to be more positive and less liable to derangement.

**Solution:** A carriage lever with a gravity pawl above the platen axis turns the platen ratchet one line, and its end lifts the letter-space ratchet bar to free the carriage in the same stroke.

**How it works:** Pulling the front arm of the carriage lever K pushes the rear arm down, so the pawl P above the platen axis turns the platen ratchet wheel one definite line space. The lever end then presses a ratchet-bar lever so the letter-spacing ratchets are lifted and the carriage can be returned by hand. A spring pawl and a pin-stop between ratchet teeth stop the platen turning during the return. The lever's travel is limited by a stop T and the ratchet-bar lever J, and the machine also has an adjustable guide rail, a margin stop and a bell trip.
