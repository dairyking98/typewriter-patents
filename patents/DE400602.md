## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The key levers roll on a bearing surface instead of pivoting, and the intermediate-lever fulcrum is placed so the drive arm change is compensated, giving even acceleration to the stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers driven by a sliding pin accelerate unevenly, so the hit force is limited.

**Solution:** The key levers roll on a bearing surface instead of pivoting, and the intermediate-lever fulcrum is placed so the drive arm change is compensated, giving even acceleration to the stop.
