## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines are complex, costly and large.

**Solution:** Radial sliding bars in one plane, keys attached directly, two or three characters per bar and the paper carriage shifted vertically for case.

**How it works:** Sheet-metal type-bars slide in radial grooves and deliver impressions at a common point by end thrust. Key levers in three banks engage slots in the bars. Each bar carries three characters, and the paper carriage shifts vertically to select one. Movable wings guide the type at the print point, and an incline under each bar rocks a shaft to trip the escapement for any key.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** sliding type-bar; key lever; frame; movable wing; rocker shaft; escapement pawl; paper carriage

**Numbers:** three characters per bar
