## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a Blickensderfer the operator must use hands off the keyboard to turn the platen and return the carriage.

**Solution:** Keys in the keyboard turn the platen and return the carriage, and the platen also turns automatically at the end of the carriage return.

**How it works:** A bail-lever around the platen carries a pawl on a pivoted pawl lever that engages a ratchet wheel on the platen, so a key lever lifting the bail turns the platen. A bell-crank on the carriage meets an abutment at the end of the return and lifts the bail to line-feed automatically. For return, a shaft carries a gear clutched by a ratchet that meshes with the carriage rack, plus a pinion driven by a segmental gear on a bell-crank worked by a key lever through a link. A slide rod from the same key releases the carriage detent plate.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; bail-lever; pawl; ratchet wheel; key lever; bell-crank; segmental gear; pinion; one-way clutch; rack; detent plate; slide rod
