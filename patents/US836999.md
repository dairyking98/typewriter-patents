## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Each multi-type typebar needs a way to shift for different characters through the key manipulation, not by a separate shift.

**Solution:** Fulcrum each multi-type typebar on a shifting lever that the key tilts, so pushing the key stem back raises the pivot and brings the lower type to the print point.

**How it works:** A typebar with two types is pivoted at a shifting lever, itself pivoted to the frame, and driven by a key lever through a link and an intermediate lever. The key stem runs in guides with openings corresponding to the types. Depressed straight down, the upper type prints. Pushed back first, the stem tilts and via a link and rod raises the shifting lever and the typebar pivot, so the lower type strikes the same point on the platen. A spring returns the key stem to the vertical.
