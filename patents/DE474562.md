## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Aprons or guides for the printing section

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that the upper part of the paper support plate including its swivel arms is pressed from a piece of sheet metal, and that each Swivel arm has an open slot at its free end, which over the Trunnion for the mudguard pushed and then by bending the two together Leg of the slot is closed.

**How it works (reading):** The upper part is pressed from sheet metal, its swivel arms slot onto the trunnion and are closed by bending, a front-leg stop engages the margin stop rod, and a spring holds the extreme positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper support plate needs a cheaper upper part and must hold the paper platen without separate fittings.

**Solution:** The upper part is pressed from sheet metal, its swivel arms slot onto the trunnion and are closed by bending, a front-leg stop engages the margin stop rod, and a spring holds the extreme positions.
