> Description Translated from German Paper support plate with one to expose the margin stops to the rear foldable upper part The invention is a paper support plate with a Upper part that can be folded back to expose the margin stops. The invention consists in that the upper part of the paper support plate including its swivel arms is pressed from a piece of sheet metal, and that each Swivel arm has an open slot at its free end, which over the Trunnion for the mudguard pushed and then by bending the two together Leg of the slot is closed. The invention reduces manufacturing costs significantly reduced for the upper part, and also the possibility is created to hold the paper platen in place without the addition of other means. The front leg of each slot is according to the invention with a Provided stop which cooperates with the edge stop rod. Through this a very compact design is obtained, and the stop can at the same time be formed with the manufacture of the paper support plate without it being a further work is required.
