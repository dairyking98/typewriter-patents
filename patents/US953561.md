## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tahulators such as used in typewriting and other machines, and its object is to condense, simplify and v ilnorove the mecl'ianism, particularly the denomination stops and the means for adjusting a column stop to cooperate therewith.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage stop mechanism that uses denomination stops on a keyrack and an adjustable column stop cooperating with them; the carriage is driven by a spring drum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator denomination stops and the column stop are cumbersome, so the tabulator needs to be condensed and simplified.

**Solution:** A carriage stop mechanism that uses denomination stops on a keyrack and an adjustable column stop cooperating with them; the carriage is driven by a spring drum.
