## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the tabulating mechanism of typewriting machines, and more especially 'to that class of tabulating mechanisms which employ a denomination sto member or members in cooperation wit a plurality of column stops.

**How it works (reading):** A single key-actuated means moves a denomination stop member and a selected column stop into cooperation, so that the chosen denomination in the chosen column reaches the printing position when the carriage is released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Prior tabulators needed two or more operations, or several key-actuated means, to bring a chosen denomination position in a chosen column to the printing point.

**Solution:** A single key-actuated means moves a denomination stop member and a selected column stop into cooperation, so that the chosen denomination in the chosen column reaches the printing position when the carriage is released.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** denomination stop member; column stops; tabulating key; carriage
