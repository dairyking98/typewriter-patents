## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on a paper guide rail for typewriters, where the paper guide roller and the pressure roller is lifted off the paper roller with the aid of the paper insertion plate and the aim is not only to press the pressure roller at the same time with the same pressure, but also the upper paper guide rail with its pressure rollers from the paper roller take off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Guide rail pressure rollers block straightening of paper.

**Solution:** Slide on the paper insertion plate grips a pin in the bearing and lifts the guide rail with its rollers off the platen together with the pressure roller.

Such a design is not necessary for rails without pressure rollers, since the guide rail does not rest on the roller, so after lifting the lower one Guide roller can comfortably straighten the paper.
