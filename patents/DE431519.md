## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper guide rails for typewriters. The invention relates on a paper guide rail for typewriters, where the paper guide roller and the pressure roller is lifted off the paper roller with the aid of the paper insertion plate and the aim is not only to press the pressure roller at the same time with the same pressure, but also the upper paper guide rail with its pressure rollers from the paper roller take off. This is achieved according to the invention in that a paper insertion at the ungsblech attached arm with a slide under a pin on the side part of the The paper guide grips so that the paper guide plate with the pressure roller and the paper guide rail with its pressure rollers at the same time from the paper roller be lifted off. Such a design is not necessary for rails without pressure rollers, since the guide rail does not rest on the roller, so after lifting the lower one Guide roller can comfortably straighten the paper. By attaching pressure rollers However, this is no longer possible on the paper guide rail, or one would have to First put the guide rail with the rollers backwards and then push it off align the paper with the pressure roller…
