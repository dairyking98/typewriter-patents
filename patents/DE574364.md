## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter frames built from many parts are heavy and make the drive parts hard to reach and remove.

**Solution:** A light, rigid frame of cast side segments with a comb plate that carries the type levers and toggle bearings, arranged so the drive parts can be removed easily.
