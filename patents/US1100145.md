## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stereotype matrices need uniform impression depth and micrometric control of the chase for hyphenation.

**Solution:** A modified typewriter without ribbon, with a stroke-limiting device and a micrometric set screw on the flong chase.

**How it works:** Typewriter impresses wet flong; stroke limiter sets depth and a micrometric screw moves the chase.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)
