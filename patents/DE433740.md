## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper trolley for typewriters of any type, the two for guiding and driving the Has paper serving roller pairs, between which a flat impact surface having pressure abutment is arranged.

**How it works (reading):** The pressure abutment is mounted in the paper carriage between two roller pairs and is moved away from the paper by a fixed inclined surface, set by an eccentric or bolt or by a spring, so it contacts the paper only at the type stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A paper support that stays fixed rubs the paper when the paper is driven by the feed rollers, and the paper must still press against the platen at the type stop.

**Solution:** The pressure abutment is mounted in the paper carriage between two roller pairs and is moved away from the paper by a fixed inclined surface, set by an eccentric or bolt or by a spring, so it contacts the paper only at the type stop.
