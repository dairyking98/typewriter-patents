## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for coloring the ribbons on typewriters and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A toothed transfer wheel dipping in ink delivers drops into a notch on a paint roller that inks the ribbon; the gear sits in a bearing chamber linked to the reservoir so ink cannot leak when tilted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing typewriter ribbon inkers brake the ribbon too hard and put too much ink on it.

**Solution:** A toothed transfer wheel dipping in ink delivers drops into a notch on a paint roller that inks the ribbon; the gear sits in a bearing chamber linked to the reservoir so ink cannot leak when tilted.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** ink transfer wheel; paint roller with notch; bearing chamber; main and secondary ink tanks

These devices have via a paint container and a member taking out the paint from the same, the transfers the ink to a roller driven by the ink ribbon. All of these devices have the disadvantage that they exert too great a braking force on the ribbon, and that too much paint is applied to the ribbon. These disadvantages are avoided according to the invention in that the member that transfers the paint to the paint roller has teeth on its circumference that engage in a notch provided on the circumference of the paint roller.
