> Description Translated from German Apparatus for dyeing the ribbons on typewriters The invention relates to a device for coloring the ribbons on typewriters and the like. There are devices of this type by which the ribbon during the operation of the 31 aschine o. The like. Is colored. These devices have via a paint container and a member taking out the paint from the same, the transfers the ink to a roller driven by the ink ribbon. There are also already devices in which a wick or a pillow removes the paint from the paint container absorbs and is in contact with the ink ribbon, so that thereby from the pillow the color is immediately transferred to the ribbon. All of these devices have the disadvantage that they exert too great a braking force on the ribbon, and that too much paint is applied to the ribbon. These disadvantages are avoided according to the invention in that the member that transfers the paint to the paint roller has teeth on its circumference that engage in a notch provided on the circumference of the paint roller.
