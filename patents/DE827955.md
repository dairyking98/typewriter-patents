## Summary

**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** A heavy line pointer on a steep non-self-locking spindle sinks by its own weight, stepped one line at a time by a ratchet released from a Bowden cable.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line pointers on sheet holders bend, lie off the sheet, are hard to remote-control, and do not step exactly one line.

**Solution:** A heavy line pointer on a steep non-self-locking spindle sinks by its own weight, stepped one line at a time by a ratchet released from a Bowden cable.

**How it works:** A board behind the typewriter holds the sheet to copy. A line pointer nut runs on a spindle with steep thread and is weighted. The spindle carries a ratchet wheel held by a leaf spring. Pressing a button beside the keyboard pulls a Bowden cable that lifts the spring with a release pin, letting the pointer fall one tooth. Thread direction presses the pointer against the sheet even on a vertical board.
