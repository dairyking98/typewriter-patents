## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage edge stops were complex and had many parts.

**Solution:** A two-part edge stop of three parts: a box-section body holds a slider with a finger piece, a ratchet leg and a stop bracket, pressed against the rack by a coil spring between the flat pieces.
