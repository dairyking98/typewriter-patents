## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (receipt printing unit for cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**How it works (reading):** Two constantly contacting, stationary rollers prevent a receipt from being inserted by hand, but they turn with a fed receipt.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Receipts can be pushed by hand into the printing point of a cash register, evading the conveyor and blocking the control-strip print.

**Solution:** Two constantly contacting, stationary rollers prevent a receipt from being inserted by hand, but they turn with a fed receipt.

**How it works:** Print hammers strike type wheels through a ribbon onto a control strip; a pair of rollers at the printing point locks against hand insertion while still feeding receipts.
