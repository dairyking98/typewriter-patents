## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Mounting typebars in the segment needs compact bearings; the inventor's earlier patent held only two typebars per supporting block.

**Solution:** Make segment blocks with radial slots of different lengths and cross-drilled pivot holes at two elevations so one block carries three or four typebars.

**How it works:** The typebar segment is divided into blocks screwed to the main segment. Each block has short and long radial slots, with pivot holes drilled from each side at right angles to the slots. The longer slots reach lower, giving a second row of pivot perforations so upper and lower typebars sit at different elevations. Pivot pins pass through the holes and the typebar ends so each bar swings accurately.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** typebar; segment; supporting block; short slot; long slot; pivot pin; key lever; connecting rod

**Numbers:** 3 or 4 typebars per block; 2 typebars per block in prior US415538

**Improves on:**

- [US415538](../patents/US415538.md): blocks held only two typebars; now three or four with slots at different depths
