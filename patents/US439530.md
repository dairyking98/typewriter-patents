## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the means of mounting two type-bars in a single supporting block or bracket, so slotted and perforated as to form the proper bearings for the bars.
