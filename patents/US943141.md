## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and especially to means for controlling the paper in suchzmachines.

**How it works (reading):** A paper-controlling device on the carriage that works with a catch for the feed-roll release lever, so the sheets are controlled during condensed record work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Controlling the sheets of paper is hard in condensed record work, where many sheets are handled.

**Solution:** A paper-controlling device on the carriage that works with a catch for the feed-roll release lever, so the sheets are controlled during condensed record work.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
