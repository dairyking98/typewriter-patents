## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-feed pressure rollers must be moved out of contact with the platen for insertion, and the inventor wants them locked in that release position.

**Solution:** A finger lever engages a cam edge on a second lever connected by a link to a rock-shaft carrying roll-releasing cams, so depressing the finger key lifts the spring-pressed rollers off the platen and holds them released.
