## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (sheet music printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**How it works (reading):** The cross slide carrying the workpiece is adjusted by a fine setting mechanism, so the faulty point can be brought exactly under the type of the rotating type disc without reading pointer positions against a scale.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a sheet music printing machine the cross slide is set by pointers on graduated scales, which are hard to read and too imprecise to bring a faulty point under the type.

**Solution:** The cross slide carrying the workpiece is adjusted by a fine setting mechanism, so the faulty point can be brought exactly under the type of the rotating type disc without reading pointer positions against a scale.
