## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a drive for the switching device for the step-by-step movement of the paper carriage of typewriters, in which the movements of the type key levers, which the switching of the paper carriage effect, are limited by braking noiselessly.

**How it works (reading):** A common brake on the support rod of the type key levers and space key lever absorbs the movement before the limit, so one brake serves both.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage switching is noisy because each type key and the space key strikes the deflection limit.

**Solution:** A common brake on the support rod of the type key levers and space key lever absorbs the movement before the limit, so one brake serves both.
