## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar actions are complicated and have uneven touch.

**Solution:** A floating type-bar pivoted on two moving members so the type end moves sideways then strikes endwise at the paper.

**How it works:** The heel is pivoted on a straight-moving rod (key rod). A link at right angles pivots the bar at an intermediate point so the type travels laterally and then straight onto the platen. A plate with a guide aperture aligns the type at impact and the type end has limited angular play.
