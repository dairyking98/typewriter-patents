## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants a typewriter compact enough to fit a lady's portable writing case, with the writing visible and the ribbon easy to renew.

**Solution:** Typebars hinged on keys around a semicircular ring, striking forward and down to a point at the front, with a carriage-less frame that moves instead of the paper.

**How it works:** Keys and typebars sit in notches around a semicircular ring and an overlying annular bar. Pressing a key swings its typebar forward and downward to the printing point; the bar is pressed down on its bell-cranks, which also drive a pawl on a ratchet to advance the whole frame one space. A glass-plate ring in front acts as a space key and locks all other keys. The paper roller lies in front of the printing point and is fed by a ratchet when the frame is returned; a ribbon frame slides over the print point with the bar.
