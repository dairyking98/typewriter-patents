## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The doll-typing toy needed printing that moves in step with the doll's arms, so letters could be printed on a strip of paper.

**Solution:** The doll's arms are driven by hand or a spring motor or clock, and simultaneously drive a type roller, paper feed roller and roller inking, so printed text appears on paper fed through the toy.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
- **Uses:** [Toy or teaching machine](../features/use-toy.md)

**Parts:** doll figure; type roller; paper supporting and feeding roller; inking means; spring motor
