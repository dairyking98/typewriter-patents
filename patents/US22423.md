## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (mechanical typographer type actuation) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies arranged in stationary or sliding cases or frames or upon flexible strips, plates, bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention consists in an improved arrangement of means for actuating type, feed ing thereto the article or substance to receive the impressions, and also for keeping the parts properly adjusted relatively with each other, so that the desired work-to wit, printing direct from the type-may beperformed with great facility.

**How it works (reading):** A bed-piece pivoted on the frame carries a sliding frame of type plates, which are actuated to print directly on paper or to make impressions in wax for electrotype moulds.
