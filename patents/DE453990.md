## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** The swinging link is mounted between side cheeks separated from it by sound-absorbing intermediate layers, so vibration is not transmitted to the lock body.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The swinging ratchet-tooth link in the paper carriage switch lock strikes its stop with force and makes a loud noise, since its bearing approaches lie directly on the lock body.

**Solution:** The swinging link is mounted between side cheeks separated from it by sound-absorbing intermediate layers, so vibration is not transmitted to the lock body.
