## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (music writing apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved apparatus for writing music upon suitable material.

**How it works (reading):** An apparatus for writing music with many sheets of drawings; the description is not clear enough in the first 3000 characters to state how the staff and notes are produced.
