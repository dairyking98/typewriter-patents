## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed by friction rollers and positive pin feed both need switching, and the pins must be driven by a cam.

**Solution:** A delivery cylinder with pins on a metal tube, driven by a cam disc; a common switch brings either the pin feed or the friction pressure rollers into operation.
