## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (metal strip feed embossing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a device for advancing of the metal band with metal sign embossing machines, with which by pressing down a hand lever at the same time the switching device for the feed rollers in action is set.

**How it works (reading):** The counter roller sits on a swinging lever carrying the rolled-up strip, so its weight presses it against the indexed driving roller; meshing toothed rings keep both rollers turning together and a pawl advances them on each stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Metal strip feed on embossing machines needs a simpler, more reliable way to advance the strip uniformly between feed rollers.

**Solution:** The counter roller sits on a swinging lever carrying the rolled-up strip, so its weight presses it against the indexed driving roller; meshing toothed rings keep both rollers turning together and a pawl advances them on each stroke.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
