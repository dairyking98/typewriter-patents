## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Make bearing and joint points elastic via cut-out resilient arms and add sound-absorbing plastic layers so impact vibration is damped before reaching the frame.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stamped sheet-steel levers and joints in type lever drives make much of the typewriter noise, with high tones, even when the platen is removed.

**Solution:** Make bearing and joint points elastic via cut-out resilient arms and add sound-absorbing plastic layers so impact vibration is damped before reaching the frame.

**How it works:** In a free-flight toggle drive, the key lever, intermediate lever and a knee joint throw the type lever to the platen, where the extended knee joint stops it with a jerk. Resilient tabs punched out of the type lever and bearing plates (a closed bracket around the bearing) absorb the shock. The type lever axis and linking rod are sleeved in vinyl-resin tube, and plastic layers sit between bearing plates and between plates and frame rail. The layers must be stiff enough to still locate the drive in space.
