## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar bearings should be cheap and compact, individually adjustable and disconnectable, and kept free of dust and dirt.

**Solution:** Antifriction bearings are fitted in hangers on a curved type-bar segment that is shaped to protect the bearings from dust; the bearings can be adjusted singly or in groups and removed individually from the segment.
