**How it works (reading):** An adjustable bolt on the pawl carrier is set by a button lever (or by finger pressure) so the movable pawl either strikes a stop for normal writing or runs into a recess for barred writing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A pawl-escapement paper carriage can be set to bar script: a bolt limits the movable pawl's deflection so the carriage advances by a fixed amount.

**Solution:** An adjustable bolt on the pawl carrier is set by a button lever (or by finger pressure) so the movable pawl either strikes a stop for normal writing or runs into a recess for barred writing.

> Description Translated from German Device for locking writing. The object of the invention is a device for writing barren scripts on typewriters, in which the step-by-step movement of the paper slide by one with a fixed and a loose pawl equipped swingable carrier is regulated. The essence of the invention consists in that the deflection of the movable pawl is arranged by a pawl carrier adjustable bolt is limited, which is recessed so that depending on the setting of the bolt, the movable pawl is either one for ordinary writing or for Barring the required advance of the paper carriage. The setting of the bolt is advantageously carried out by means of a button lever with the aid of a linkage such that the own gravity of the linkage determines the position of the bolt for the permanent ordinary: writing and the persistent finger pressure on the key lever the position of the bolt for the temporary locking script causes. The new device draws compared to known devices of the same type due to their particular simplicity and takes up little space.
