## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line adjuster for booking machines)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to line adjusters for to be inserted into a printing unit Receipts in which the table receiving the receipt depends on a particular Interlocking is moved so that the line of the document to be printed is in the print line is set.

**How it works (reading):** Interlock is split into decimal units (units, tens, hundreds) and the table is brought to the stop by a clamping switch mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line setting for accounting machines needs many keys and force-locked stops that slip when the document swells.

**Solution:** Interlock is split into decimal units (units, tens, hundreds) and the table is brought to the stop by a clamping switch mechanism.
