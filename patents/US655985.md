## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ink ribbon mechanism for printing press) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to mechanism whereby an ink-ribbon may be detachably secured and automatically shifted in cooperative relation with a printing-press of ordinary construction.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon-shifting mechanism carries a detachable ink ribbon across the press, driven from the cam and bed, and a feed roller with an automatic signalling device gives notice when the ribbon must reverse.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ink ribbon of a printing press must be shifted back and forth, and the operator needs a signal when the direction of traverse must reverse.

**Solution:** Ribbon-shifting mechanism carries a detachable ink ribbon across the press, driven from the cam and bed, and a feed roller with an automatic signalling device gives notice when the ribbon must reverse.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
