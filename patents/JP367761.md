## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple typewriter printing mechanism for a small English-letter machine, with many characters on one carrier.

**Solution:** A cylindrical type magazine turns and slides on an outer shaft while a coaxial inner shaft doubles as the print lever that flips a type holder to strike.

**How it works:** A cylindrical magazine sits on the outer shaft of a double shaft and can turn and move up and down. Many type holders, each carrying types in several tiers, are pivoted round its lower rim. The operator turns the magazine to the wanted column using an index dial on top, and turns a gear meshing with a rack on the outer shaft to raise or lower it to the wanted tier. Striking the inner shaft down pushes a lever, which moves a parallelogram linkage that swings the chosen holder about its pivot against the platen. Springs return the lever, linkage and holder.
