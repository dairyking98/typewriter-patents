**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**How it works (reading):** Type bodies with raised characters are used as embossing models in a powder or plastic mass that hardens, producing matrices whose recesses share the same even spacing and height.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Engraved recesses for aligning type bodies on lever ends had to be cut precisely by hand, which was slow and costly.

**Solution:** Type bodies with raised characters are used as embossing models in a powder or plastic mass that hardens, producing matrices whose recesses share the same even spacing and height.
