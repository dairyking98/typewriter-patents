## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Engraved recesses for aligning type bodies on lever ends had to be cut precisely by hand, which was slow and costly.

**Solution:** Type bodies with raised characters are used as embossing models in a powder or plastic mass that hardens, producing matrices whose recesses share the same even spacing and height.

**Features:** [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
