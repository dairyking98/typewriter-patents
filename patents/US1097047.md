## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines adapted to use inking ribbons of different colors, and which can also be used for preparing wax stencil sheets, for reproducing apparatus;

**How it works (reading):** A set of three keys moves a shaft and bar so the red or blue ribbon strip is raised to the typing level, or the ribbon stroke is disengaged completely.
