## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (aperture counter for typewriters)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an improvement in aperture counters which only prepare the switching on of the volatile one or the aperture or both when the counter passes through zero and which can be carried out by a power circuit, for example by the force of the receding aperture counter.

**How it works (reading):** Two pawls replace the upper transfer teeth so the counter can always be prepared and turned at the zero passage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Aperture counter jams at zero when a worn transfer tooth or wheel is ground down, locking the arithmetic unit.

**Solution:** Two pawls replace the upper transfer teeth so the counter can always be prepared and turned at the zero passage.
