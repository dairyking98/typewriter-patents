## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (zero printing device for accounting machine)

**How it works (reading):** The zero key's electromagnet is energized through relays once a non-zero digit is entered, so the zero print uses no moving contacts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Zero printing with moving contacts in an accounting machine wears and needs many moving parts.

**Solution:** The zero key's electromagnet is energized through relays once a non-zero digit is entered, so the zero print uses no moving contacts.
