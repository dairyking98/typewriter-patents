## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for returning the paper cart of typewriters by means of an electric motor.

**How it works (reading):** The motor drives a helical spring that turns a shaft, and a stop piece on the shaft is carried by adjustable friction, so it cushions the carriage-return coupling and absorbs the shock.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A powerful motor for carriage return throws the carriage back violently, so the parts stop abruptly and the carriage beats, shortening machine life.

**Solution:** The motor drives a helical spring that turns a shaft, and a stop piece on the shaft is carried by adjustable friction, so it cushions the carriage-return coupling and absorbs the shock.
