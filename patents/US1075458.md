## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to attachments for typewriting machines through the medium of which a continuous web of paper may be fed from a rollto the platen to receive the impressions from the printing mechanism of the typewriting machine.

**How it works (reading):** Rolls of paper are carried on a support that moves with the carriage so the web always meets the platen at right angles; a stationary cutter tears off lengths, and fixed-axis manifold ribbon rollers are turned by the carriage at the end of its throws.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Single sheets are slow to feed, and a continuous web needs a way to be torn off in chosen lengths while manifolding ribbon rollers are actuated.

**Solution:** Rolls of paper are carried on a support that moves with the carriage so the web always meets the platen at right angles; a stationary cutter tears off lengths, and fixed-axis manifold ribbon rollers are turned by the carriage at the end of its throws.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
