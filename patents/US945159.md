## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulator mechanism for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Denominational tabulator and column-selecting mechanism brings the carriage in one continuous movement to any selected column position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage must be stopped at every intervening column before reaching a chosen tabulator column.

**Solution:** Denominational tabulator and column-selecting mechanism brings the carriage in one continuous movement to any selected column position.
