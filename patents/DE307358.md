## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The releasable coupling between platen and line-index wheel needs to be light yet hold firmly when engaged and release quickly.

**Solution:** Use two friction shoes spread against a drum by two floating levers, which an axially sliding cone piece pushes apart.

**How it works:** A disc fixed to the platen shaft carries two loose clutch shoes, set against a spacer block with a wedge screw for precise adjustment. The line-index wheel is fixed to a drum around the disc. Two levers on a floating pivot press the shoes outward through rollers in grooves. Sliding a cone-shaped hub piece along the shaft spreads the lever ends and, via a near-straight toggle action, grips the drum with great force; retracting it lets the platen turn freely against the index wheel.
