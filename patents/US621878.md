## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide an improved type-writing machine in which the type are carried by a revoluble wheel operated by the depression of the keys to bring corresponding characters in line for forming an impression.

**How it works (reading):** Each key-lever's depth sets how far a segment rack turns the type wheel, then the same stroke lowers the whole swinging frame carrying the wheel onto the platen, and returning the frame steps the carriage.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventor wants a simple keyed type-wheel typewriter in which key depth selects the character and the same key stroke prints and spaces.

**Solution:** Each key-lever's depth sets how far a segment rack turns the type wheel, then the same stroke lowers the whole swinging frame carrying the wheel onto the platen, and returning the frame steps the carriage.

**How it works:** Key levers pivot on a front cross-rod and press on a bar in a swinging frame that carries the type wheel. An adjusting screw on each lever meets a graduated, stepped edge, so a key farther along the row pushes a pivoted bar further and swings a segment rack further. The rack turns a pinion, shaft and keyed sleeve, rotating the wheel to the matching character. A roller lock holds the frame up until that rotation is done, then releases so further key travel swings the frame down and the wheel strikes the paper. The frame's motion rocks a shaft that cocks a pawl on the carriage rack, and the return spring then pushes the carriage one step. A lever slides the sleeve along the shaft, notch-detented, to choose one of three circumferential rows (lower, capitals, figures). A felt roller fed from an absorbent pad on a spring arm inks the type.
