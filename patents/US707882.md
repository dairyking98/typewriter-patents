## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars with inking cushions need a way to rest against ink and still print directly on paper.

**Solution:** A typebar carrier on the end of a pivoting type lever is flipped 180 degrees by a guided sliding rod moving in a curved slot as the key is struck.

**How it works:** The sheet-metal type lever is pulled by a rod from the key lever to horizontal. A rod linked to a frame standard slides in a curved slot of the typebar carrier, turning the carrier and typebar 180 degrees so the types face down onto the paper over the platen. At rest the types lie against an ink pad. On release a spring returns everything, the rod turning the carrier back. Several types per bar are chosen by platen adjustment.
