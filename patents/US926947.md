## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (combined typewriter and music printer) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**How it works (reading):** The whole key frame is displaced to switch between music signs and letters, and piano-like keys set the paper carriage to the correct staff line before printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A typewriter cannot print musical notes on the correct staff lines because its keys and carriage only handle letters and signs.

**Solution:** The whole key frame is displaced to switch between music signs and letters, and piano-like keys set the paper carriage to the correct staff line before printing.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
