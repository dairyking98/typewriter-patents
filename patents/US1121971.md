## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention pertains to a new and useful improvement in typewriting machines, being designed and applicable as an attachment for the line spacing mechanism of the/ machine.

**How it works (reading):** A spring in the connecting train between the line-space lever and the platen, so the platen follows the lever gradually and never turns faster than the spring can drive it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A sharp line-space lever throw batters the line-space pawl and jerks the platen, which causes faulty line spacing of the work sheet.

**Solution:** A spring in the connecting train between the line-space lever and the platen, so the platen follows the lever gradually and never turns faster than the spring can drive it.
