## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Folding the carriage forward over the keys leaves the machine tall.

**Solution:** Fold the carriage and platen rearward and down to a compact position below the frame top.

**How it works:** A shiftable support with bar-like members slides in posts at the corners of the frame and is raised by a rocking rod and arm for case shift. Sliding members locked with slots and clips make a rigid support in use. Releasing them lets the carriage bed, carriage and platen lower behind the frame.

**Features:** [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** frame; shiftable support; post; slide; rocking rod; arm; carriage bed; carriage; platen

**Improves on:**

- forward-folding Corona machine: folds rearward, lowering height
