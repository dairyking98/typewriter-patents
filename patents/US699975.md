## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Addressing envelopes on a typewriter needs the operator to feed each one by hand.

**Solution:** An attachment holds an envelope supply and automatically feeds envelopes one at a time to the platen when the carriage is returned.

**How it works:** A stack of blank envelopes sits in a supply device beside the machine. Operating the carriage shift for a new address sets the feeding devices going; dogs release one envelope into a carrier between supply and platen. Stop-guides position the envelope, then are released so it can be fed round the platen, while the holding rollers are separated from the platen to let it enter. Only the objects and figure list are visible in the opening.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** envelope supply; carrier; stop-guide; holding roller; platen; feeding dog; locking dog
