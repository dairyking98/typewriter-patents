## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single escapement timing cannot suit both fast operators (who want the feed before the type strikes) and slow operators (who want it after).

**Solution:** An escapement mechanism that can be adapted to either the speed (feed before impact) or the natural (feed after impact) action, with a stop that buffers the depressed key.

**How it works:** A rack and feed-dog arrangement with a rocking element and stop; the stop meets a fixed abutment near the moment of impact and acts as a buffer for the key.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** rack; feed-dogs; rocking element; stop

**Numbers:** US 686,435; 1901-11-12; application filed 1899-06-28
