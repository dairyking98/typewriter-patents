## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (translator for printing telegraph receivers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using aggregate motion translation; Tripping mechanism

**How it works (reading):** Each planet gear is driven directly by a connecting rod from the rotating control member, removing intermediate levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Backlash and dead travel in the translation of telegraph type wheels reduce accuracy.

**Solution:** Each planet gear is driven directly by a connecting rod from the rotating control member, removing intermediate levers.

- **Selection:** [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
