## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-zone ribbons and stencil work need the vibrator throw set, without overthrow of the vibrator or ribbon creeping to the printing point.

**Solution:** An adjustable pin in one of three notches on an arm of the universal bar sets the vibrator's throw, with a stop lever holding it down for stencils.

**How it works:** The universal bar arm has three notches; a link-carried pin engages notch 1, 2 or 3 chosen by an operator rod and an indicator plate marked Top, Bottom, Stencil. A bell crank links both the escapement lever and the vibrator bell crank. Notch 2 gives enough motion to trip the escapement but not to lift the ribbon to the print point. A stop lever limits overthrow and holds the vibrator depressed.
