## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**How it works (reading):** The type strike a flat surface on which the paper lies. A spring-driven escapement, a ratchet wheel with a detent lever, is released by each key depression and advances the carriage one letter space through gearing. An inked ribbon runs across the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Impressions from a curved platen are unclear, and manifolding is weak, because the paper is struck over a cylinder instead of a flat surface.

**Solution:** The type strike a flat surface on which the paper lies. A spring-driven escapement, a ratchet wheel with a detent lever, is released by each key depression and advances the carriage one letter space through gearing. An inked ribbon runs across the carriage.
