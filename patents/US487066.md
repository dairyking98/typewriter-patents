## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With a centre guide, one-piece type levers had to be moved bodily to correct alignment, losing momentum and impression force and stiffening the key touch.

**Solution:** Mount the type in a separate holder pivoted on the lever end so only the light holder is corrected by the centre guide.

**How it works:** Radiating type carriers are pivoted in hangers around the top plate and driven by connecting rods from the key levers. The end of each carrier is bent down, with a round hole and an oblong slot. A type holder straddles the carrier and is fixed by two riveted wire pins, the lower acting as a pivot and the upper moving in the slot. As the type passes up through the beveled centre guide, the holder swings slightly about its pivot until its axis coincides with the guide's. The inexactly pivoted heavy lever therefore needs no bodily correction.
