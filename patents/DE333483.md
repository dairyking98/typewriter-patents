## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Preparing or treating stereotype matrices

**How it works (reading):** The spacing pawls sit above the drive shaft and are pulled by magnets energised through the key contacts, so one pawl pair can advance the carriage by any chosen number of equal-width steps.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Setting any spacing between characters needs one pawl pair per spacing value, so many pawl pairs are required.

**Solution:** The spacing pawls sit above the drive shaft and are pulled by magnets energised through the key contacts, so one pawl pair can advance the carriage by any chosen number of equal-width steps.

In the device according to the main patent 329781, the ratchets necessary for moving the carriage forward are attached to the button levers. They are brought into engagement with the gears located on a shaft by pressing down the buttons, thus causing the carriage to shift. The contact to excite the magnets is brought about by the buttons in a known manner.
