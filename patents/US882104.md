## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to dust shields or covers for the type bar bearings of writing machines, and more particularly to that kind of machines known as front strike or visible typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A dust shield is hung on brackets in front of the typebar bearings and can be swung into operative position.
