## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter that is light, small, cheap, quick and accurate to operate is wanted.

**Solution:** A sliding type bar carrying letters, numerals and whole frequently used words, mounted in a pivoted frame whose downstroke also moves the carriage one space.

**How it works:** A single type bar with side flanges slides in guide grooves in a pivoted frame; it carries the alphabet, numerals, punctuation and common words such as and, the, in, to and a place name. The frame is depressed to print, and its downward motion strikes a lever on a rock shaft so that gravity pawls engaging rack teeth on the bed shift the carriage back one space. A spring keeps the frame slightly raised. The paper roller sits fixed in the bed with a ratchet and spacing lever for line feed, and the carriage rides in tracks over the roller.

**Features:** [SEL:linear-slider](../features/sel-linear-slider.md), [ELEM:linear-bar](../features/elem-linear-bar.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** type bar; pivoted frame; guide groove; carriage; rock shaft; gravity pawl; rack teeth; roller; ratchet wheel; spacing lever

**Numbers:** words up to ten letters or more on the bar
