## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper of different sizes must be located under the machine, and changing the paper size should be quick.

**Solution:** Interchangeable platens of various sizes with fixed edge guides fit on a platen carriage that moves them for line spacing.
