## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for producing a permanent visual presentation of the output data, e.g. computer output printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to new and useful improvements in apparatus for automatically operating typewriters and comprises a simple and efiicient mechanism which may be applied to the ordinary typewriter and afl'ords means whereby the type levers may be actuated as well as the feeding of the sheets and envelops to be used, the spacing and movement of the carriage automatically.

**How it works (reading):** Apparatus operates a typewriter automatically in sequence, with perforated jacquard strips controlling the operation (per description fragments).
