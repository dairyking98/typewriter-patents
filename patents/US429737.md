**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to devices for operatshorter or longer thrust.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The plunger reciprocating devices should give only one intermittent thrust per die presented.

**Solution:** An electromagnet releases a spring-catch so a spring hammer frees a stop, letting the plunger drive give a single thrust for each die presented.
