## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (tabulating machine with printing device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements of selective printing mechanisms, e.g. ink-jet printers or thermal printers, specially adapted for supporting or handling copy material in short lengths, e.g. sheets control of the transport of the copy material

**How it works (reading):** Tabulating machine with adding and printing units prints the result directly on loose cards, which are fed and removed automatically from a stack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Results of tabulation runs were copied by hand from a continuous list onto individual index cards, which is error-prone and slow.

**Solution:** Tabulating machine with adding and printing units prints the result directly on loose cards, which are fed and removed automatically from a stack.

**How it works:** Each time the printing unit takes effect, it draws a new card from the supply stack and brings it into the print position.
