## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Flat bookkeeping copy boards with feed rollers below the writing surface have a hollow, weak, low drive and clips at the edges that get in the way, and need perforated sheets.

**Solution:** All sheet holding, feeding and switching is combined in one unit at the top edge of the board, with the lower feed roller advanced by a ratchet driven by the clamp bar.

**How it works:** A plywood plate with a metal plate carries a ribbon wrapped round it through a slot, giving a ribbon and line indicator at the writing point. At the top, brackets hold a shaft with arms and a rubber-covered clamping bar for the top sheets, spring-loaded, with a handle. A fixed and an eccentric-lifted movable conveyor roller above the writing level advance the base sheet by a line width through a ratchet worked by the clamp bar on one side shield. The writing surface is thus free of holding and switching parts.
