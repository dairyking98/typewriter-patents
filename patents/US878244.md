**In the patent’s own words:** My improvement relates wholly to means for automatically locking the shift lever in depressed position when depressed by a selected key and is not dependent upon or limited to any particular connections between the shift key lever and part to be shifted, such connections need not be' shown or described.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shift key must be held down by the finger when writing capitals or shifting the ribbon.

**Solution:** A pendent latch on the key lever, pushed by a secondary key lever, locks the shift lever down against a keeper.

**How it works:** A pivoted two-cheek latch on the shift key lever has a tooth that engages a fixed keeper. A secondary key lever pivoted over the shift lever forces the latch forward when depressed, locking the shift. A tension spring retracts the latch otherwise. Usable for ribbon shifting too.

**Features:** [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** shift key lever; pendent latch; keeper; secondary key lever; tension spring
