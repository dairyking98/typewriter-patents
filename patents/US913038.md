## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Not stated in the opening; the machine is a complete typewriter with typebars, disc platen, paper holder, spacing, back-spacing and tabulating.

**Solution:** A typebar machine with a horizontal disc platen and a semicircular paper holder, driven by a spring barrel for letter spacing.

**How it works:** A vertically slotted tube turns on a pointed screw in the base and carries a rod that lifts a segment with spring-loaded plates and triggers that clamp the paper edge. The paper hangs in a semicircular holder and guide. The visible text covers only the paper holding; the type action, spacing spring barrel, back-spacing and tabulating levers are listed in the figures but not described in the opening.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** slotted tube; segment; clamping plate; trigger; semicircular paper holder; spring barrel; base plate
