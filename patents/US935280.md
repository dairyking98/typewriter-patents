## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon vibrator travel must vary to show either colour of a two-colour ribbon, and disconnect for stencil work.

**Solution:** A universal bail movable on the rock shaft arms toward or from the pivot, varying the vibrator throw, with a connect and disconnect bar.

**How it works:** The rock shaft with lever arms carries a universal bail kept against the key levers by a tension spring. The bail is pivoted so it swings toward or from the shaft axis, changing how far key motion oscillates the ribbon vibrator and so which colour band lies in the type path. A connecting bar can disconnect the vibrator so the ribbon does not move, for mimeograph stencils.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** rock shaft; universal bail; key lever; ribbon vibrator; connecting bar; tension spring
