## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register total printing)

**How it works (reading):** Unlocking one lock enables the total print, and a second lock keeps the printed total strip hidden so only the owner or agent can read it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On key-lever cash registers the person who prints a subtotal also sees the sums printed, which is unwanted when a business owner wants to keep totals private.

**Solution:** Unlocking one lock enables the total print, and a second lock keeps the printed total strip hidden so only the owner or agent can read it.
