## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a paper guide for typewriters and extends in particular to the arrangement of the locking device for the support arms of the paper holding rail.

**How it works (reading):** Short spigots on the support cross carry the arms, and a tension spring holds them in any position automatically, without a continuous axle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Continuous support axes and long locking rollers made the paper holding rail hard to lift and the springs fatigued.

**Solution:** Short spigots on the support cross carry the arms, and a tension spring holds them in any position automatically, without a continuous axle.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
