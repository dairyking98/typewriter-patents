## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An efficient way to produce a single-copy document by type where a pen would have been used.

**Solution:** Thirty keys pick a type from a closely packed holder struck by one weighted hammer, with two shift keys sliding the holder sideways to select capitals or figures.

**How it works:** Seventy-eight types sit close together in a holder M, arranged in three sections of three rows: capitals, lower case, and stops and numerals. A working key alone prints from the lower-case section. Holding key 6 first pulls a lever and plate with an inclined slot over a pin, sliding the holder to the capitals section; key 5 does the same toward the figures section. A hammer with an adjustable screwed weight strikes the chosen type, heavier when the weight is near the point. Another key raises the bed plate to underscore, and a spacer key engages gear wheels on the rollers to feed the paper between lines by an adjustable incline. The text is truncated.
