## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my improvement is to provide a means -by which said roller may,by the same lever,be moved forward or reversed,and thereby the paper be carried in either direction at the will of -the operator on the said machines,and thereby facilitatethe correcting of errors and provide a speedy method or means of interlineation.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The paper roller turns only one way, so paper cannot be backed up to correct or interline without removing it.

**Solution:** One lever works two pawls on the platen ratchet so the roller can be turned forward or backward, with a regulator setting single or double line spacing.

**How it works:** Lever A moves pawl C when raised to turn the ratchet wheel and roller one way, and pawl B when pressed down to turn it the other way. Pins on the pawls ride shoulders of a pawl guide so only one pawl engages at a time. A space regulator D limits lever travel to one or two ratchet notches. A holding pawl keeps the roller still.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** lever; pawl; ratchet wheel; paper roller; space regulator; pawl guide; pawl pin; spring
