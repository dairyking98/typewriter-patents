**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cooling or ventilating arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a copying attachment for typewriters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon copies are faint and blur, and hard striking strains the typewriter.

**Solution:** A moist copying-paper strip from a roll in a drum passes between the platen and a rubber pressure roller, making a press copy at the same time as typing.

The present invention relates to a copying attachment for typewriters. While hitherto the matter written on typewriters was manifolded by means of carbon sheets and carbon copies, the object of the present invention is to produce a regular press-copy of the writing of the typewriter simultaneously and on the typewriter itself. Such presscopies have hitherto been made only. separately of the typewriter, and the carbon copies made on the typewriter itself, were frequently very indistinct, will easily blur and even become invisible…
