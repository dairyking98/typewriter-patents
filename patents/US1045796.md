Serial No. 703,505. ,To all whom it may concern: Be it known that 1, JOHN KERNER, engi neer, a subject of the Queen of the Nether lands, and resident of Voorburg, Netherlands, whose post-oilice address is West Vlietweg, have invented new and useful Copying Attachments for Type-Writers, of whlch the following is a specification. The present invention relates to a copying attachment for typewriters. While hitherto the matter written on typewriters was manifolded by means of carbon sheets and carbon copies, the object of the present invention is to produce a regular press-copy of the writing of the typewriter simultaneously and on the typewriter itself. Such presscopies have hitherto been made only. separately of the typewriter, and the carbon copies made on the typewriter itself, were frequently very indistinct, will easily blur and even become invisible…
