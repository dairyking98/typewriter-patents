## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Column stops on the Underwood are hard to set to an exact position because the spring ball detent binds on the rod.

**Solution:** A spring-pressed detent lever with a knob is attached to the stop, and squeezing two handles releases it for fine adjustment.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
