## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (metal embossing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Oscillating embossing tools performing a rolling-off motion with respect to the workpiece

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on a device for embossing metal objects by means of tilting or pendulum rolling stamping body, in which this stamping body or one of the same after Act like a toggle lever.

**How it works (reading):** The toggle-like tilting or rolling stamping bodies are driven from a crankshaft through links so the stamping ends at or near the extended middle position, so the final blow hits the metal square to the object and the type form is accurate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Embossed type for writing and calculating machines loses accuracy because the tilting or rolling stamp leaves small errors on the final impression.

**Solution:** The toggle-like tilting or rolling stamping bodies are driven from a crankshaft through links so the stamping ends at or near the extended middle position, so the final blow hits the metal square to the object and the type form is accurate.
