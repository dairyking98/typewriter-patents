**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the invention is to remedy this deficiency, and this happens because the disengagement and engagement of the line index wheel with the platen connecting coupling with the help of an externally rotatable on the platen axis in the longitudinal direction of which the control sleeve is displaceable in both coupling positions is automatically recorded.

**How it works (reading):** A control sleeve on the platen axis is turned by a handle and moves axially by a wedge tooth and abutment, holding the line-index wheel decoupled without a hand on the button, and it is friction-locked.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Piercing-roller clutches must be held released by one hand while the platen is adjusted, so both hands cannot be free.

**Solution:** A control sleeve on the platen axis is turned by a handle and moves axially by a wedge tooth and abutment, holding the line-index wheel decoupled without a hand on the button, and it is friction-locked.
