> Description Translated from German Printing unit for accounting machines The present invention relates to a printing unit for booking machines, which are especially used in larger companies, such as Banks, savings banks and the like should be used. Such machines collect the Introduced amounts in several rows of adding units, calculate automatically the account balances that can be changed by incoming or outgoing payments and print these results on a booking slip and on one or more receipts (account card, Savings book, settlement slip, etc.). In addition to the amounts, the imprints contain these are specified in more detail and are given in certain print columns. There are Machines of this type are known, in which the column-wise impression of the various Amounts are achieved by setting several amount type records in the same way are arranged side by side, their selection by the drive of the desired Column corresponding print hammer takes place. These machines are not just the accounting receipts, but also the control strip printed in columns.
