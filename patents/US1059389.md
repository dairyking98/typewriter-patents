## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A roll-paper holder on an adding and typewriting machine must be swung out of use and must lock the carriage while roll paper is used on the platen.

**Solution:** A self-contained roll holder that locks when swung into position, locks the carriage, and unlocks automatically when the paper holder is raised.
