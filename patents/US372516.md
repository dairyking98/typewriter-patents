## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machine with a flat, flexible-surfaced platen that must be fed laterally in steps for letter spacing and longitudinally for line spacing, with paper held on the platen.

**Solution:** A flat diaphragm platen carried on a laterally moving carriage that is driven by a spring through a cam-equalized cord and stepped by a key-operated two-pawl detent escapement.

**How it works:** The paper lies on a leather diaphragm stretched on a platen frame, supported under the print line by a platen bar. Typebars on pivoted levers swing in a horizontal semicircle from an inking pad to the print point in front of a pointer when a key is pressed. All keys depress a common pad on a detent lever; this releases a spring pawl from a lateral ratchet and engages a fixed pawl, then on release the spring pawl catches the next tooth so the carriage spring pulls the platen one step. A spring, cord, cam and pulley supply the lateral drive, the cam equalizing spring force over the travel. The line-spacing lever with a spring pawl pushes the platen rearward one step via a ratchet on the platen rod. A finger lever lifts the lateral ratchet to free the carriage for return, and a spring clamp holds the paper on the platen.
