## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Removing the platen frame from an Underwood carriage for cleaning or repair required several disconnections and re-adjustment on replacement.

**Solution:** A single cam lever drops the platen-frame guide arms clear of the trunnions so the frame lifts out and drops back without re-adjustment.

**How it works:** The platen frame rides on trunnions in open bearings of guide arms on a rock shaft, and a slotted guide arm on a stud keeps the lift parallel. A finger-piece arm with a cam edge pulls the rock-shaft arms down off the trunnions, and a link also frees the slotted guide arm. The platen frame then tilts up and back out of the carriage. A notch holds the arms down against the counterbalance spring until the cam arm is released, when the spring raises the arms to re-engage the trunnions.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** platen frame; guide arm; rock shaft; trunnion; cam arm; counterbalance spring; limit stop

**Improves on:**

- Underwood platen-shift carriage: single-lever removal of the platen frame with no re-adjustment
