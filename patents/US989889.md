## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (copy of entries on sheets) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to provide means forobtaining a copy on a single sheet or strip of paper of a part of each of the entries made on separate sheets of paper which are successively inserted in and removed from the machine.

**How it works (reading):** A carbon sheet sits between statement and copy, and a third ribbon strip receives the item amounts in a column, so the day's total is summed from the tally.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Daily statement entries must be copied onto a tally sheet, which is slow to do by hand.

**Solution:** A carbon sheet sits between statement and copy, and a third ribbon strip receives the item amounts in a column, so the day's total is summed from the tally.
