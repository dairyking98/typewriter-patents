## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter lift tables are easily forced open, and their lifting mechanism carries the full machine weight, causing wear and needing much force.

**Solution:** A steel cabinet whose machine frame rides in grooves, with lever arms on the flaps running in arc and straight grooves to lift the frame out, and counterweights on rollers balancing the machine so the lift only changes position.
