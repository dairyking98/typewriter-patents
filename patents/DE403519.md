## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Aprons or guides for the printing section

**How it works (reading):** A paper-table guide piece on a rail with curved tongue and guide eyes that grip the rail firmly and set easily.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guide for the typewriter platen frame is hard to set and loose-fitting.

**Solution:** A paper-table guide piece on a rail with curved tongue and guide eyes that grip the rail firmly and set easily.
