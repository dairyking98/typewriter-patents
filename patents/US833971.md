## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says the carriage escapement is complicated, is not sensitive at high speed, and is hard to adjust for different speeds.

**Solution:** A simple escapement that moves the carriage step by step, driven by the key-bar linkage, with a simple adjustment for different operating speeds.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)
