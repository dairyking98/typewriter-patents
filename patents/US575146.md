## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A fabric ink disk must present fresh surface to the single strike point.

**Solution:** Gearing rotates the disk while an eccentric gear train also reciprocates it, so the strike point traces many crossing paths over an annulus.

**How it works:** The carriage spring barrel drives a winding shaft, bevel gears and a vertical shaft carrying an elliptical wheel. This meshes with a wheel carrying the disk on a slide, giving rotation plus slow reciprocation. With 70 and 72 teeth wheels there are 35 crossing paths.

**Features:** [INK:disc](../features/ink-disc.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** ink disk; elliptical wheel; slide; spring barrel; bevel gear; platen

**Numbers:** 70 teeth; 72 teeth; 35 paths

**Improves on:**

- [US575145](../patents/US575145.md): companion application: the shifting mechanism for the disk
