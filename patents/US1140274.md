## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism for typewriting machines, and more particularly to mechanism in which two ribbons, usually of different colors, are adapted to be brought alternately into use, to be vibrated over the printing point when the type-bars are actuated.

**How it works (reading):** Two interchangeable ribbon guides are lifted alternately into use by bell cranks and a shift bar, and a single vibrating lever auto-couples to whichever guide is active.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-colour ribbon machines need ribbon guides that keep the same relation to the platen in both cases and switch cleanly.

**Solution:** Two interchangeable ribbon guides are lifted alternately into use by bell cranks and a shift bar, and a single vibrating lever auto-couples to whichever guide is active.

**How it works:** The vibrating lever rides on the platen shift rail so the active guide keeps its position to the platen in upper and lower case. Each ribbon guide has a lost-motion connection to a bell crank, letting it vibrate over the print point. A shift bar tied to a key-operated rock shaft raises one guide and lowers the other. A slot and pin keep vibration amplitude the same in all positions. The mechanism can be locked with both ribbons inoperative.
