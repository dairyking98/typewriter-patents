## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabulating attachments for type-writers.

**How it works (reading):** A spacing (tabulating) attachment for type-writers with simple construction, operated by a multi-sheet arrangement of levers and racks.
