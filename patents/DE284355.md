## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (telephone answering phonograph) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller; Recording arrangements for recording a message from the calling party

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for telephone stations for automatic phonographic Answering and recording the conversations and relates less to the institutions directly eligible for answering and recording, but in particular on the aids through which the communication with the telephone exchange is regulated and the phonographic facilities are brought into effect.

**How it works (reading):** A telephone-station device that switches the subscriber circuit so the answering and phonographic recording are triggered and the exchange monitoring signals follow the call state.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Telephone stations need automatic answering and recording, and the subscriber station must switch correctly on answering, which the exchange monitoring relies on.

**Solution:** A telephone-station device that switches the subscriber circuit so the answering and phonographic recording are triggered and the exchange monitoring signals follow the call state.
