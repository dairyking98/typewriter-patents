## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The ink reservoir feeds a paint roller through a needle valve and absorbent pad, and a double screw connection vents the reservoir to prevent vacuum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hand ribbon re-inking gives uneven ink and an air vacuum that stops ink flow.

**Solution:** The ink reservoir feeds a paint roller through a needle valve and absorbent pad, and a double screw connection vents the reservoir to prevent vacuum.

**How it works:** Metered ink roller feed with a needle valve and an air vent to stop vacuum.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Hand-held device for re-soaking ink ribbons for typewriters etc. Like. There are handheld devices for re-soaking ribbons with color on typewriters or the like. Became known, which have not proven themselves due to various circumstances. A major drawback with the known handheld devices is the paint supply to the Paint roller etc. and the color distribution. Sometimes felt rolls or felt strips are used from the inside or even by dripping onto the front of the rolls or in some other way Colored on the outside. It is left to chance how the color changes distributed on the rollers and the amount of ink fed to the rollers. Through this it is impossible to impregnate the ribbons evenly, and achieved will only smear and become unusable of the ribbon. This is avoided by the subject matter of the invention, once by the arrangement of the paint roller and the pressure roller and further by the design of the dye container and the nature of the dye guide. The sleeve-like dye container runs on its lower End into a right-angled fork-shaped paint roller holder. This roll holder is provided with ink supply holes, which are also at right angles to the dye container…
