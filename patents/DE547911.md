> Description Translated from German The invention 1 relates to manuscript holders for typewriters, the support of which is mounted in a horizontal joint, in such a way that the desk board can be pivoted backwards from a front position of use in which it is located approximately above the machine cylinder. It consists in the fact that the pivoting support is under the action of a brake that holds the manuscript holder in the two end positions by friction and at the same time prevents the desk from being displaced by the vibrations when writing. The brake is dimensioned in such a way that it secures the support »5 can hold without making it difficult to pivot the holder by hand. Appropriate the brake consists of a spring that is perpendicular to the pivot axis on the pivotable The support exerts a pressure that creates the necessary friction. In contrast to the known designs, in which a spring serves as a stop for the pivotable support or this gives a resilient effect itself the spring only to generate a frictional pressure that brakes the support.
