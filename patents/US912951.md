## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Protective covers for food containers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to receptacles, and more particularly to a case adapted for the reception of a typewriter, and has for an object to provide a case which will be simple in construction and which will be so arranged that the weight of the typewriter will be equally distributed.

**How it works (reading):** A two-section hinged case with a lock and a carrying handle at one end, so the weight is balanced and the case can be carried away from the body.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter is awkward to carry and its weight is not evenly distributed in a case.

**Solution:** A two-section hinged case with a lock and a carrying handle at one end, so the weight is balanced and the case can be carried away from the body.
