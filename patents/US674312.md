## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon wear is uneven, and an empty spool gives no warning.

**Solution:** Improved ribbon vibrator, manual width adjustment of ribbon wear, and an empty-spool lock that stops printing.

**How it works:** On a Felbel front-strike machine, an escapement wheel with stepping and detent dogs moves a rack-and-pinion carriage pulled by a spring drum. Ribbon spools, a vibrator and a manual widthwise adjustment distribute wear; when a spool is empty, the ribbon locks the printing mechanism.
