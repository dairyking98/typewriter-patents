## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a grand total is printed the adding wheels must be stopped at zero and their drive members disengaged.

**Solution:** A swingable bracket moves into the path of the lateral approaches of the adding wheels to stop them at zero, and a second bracket linked by an arm and pawl disengages the drive members.

The invention relates to a cash register with a total printing device in which at Total pressure locking arms of a swingable bracket caused by automatically Pivoting this bracket in the path of the lateral approaches of the adder wheels to stop them in the zero position. The invention consists in that the bracket with another bracket is connected, which by means of an arm and a pawl, the adding wheels when printing a grand total with the drive members out of engagement. The advantage of this innovation consists in both the simplicity and the reliable operation of the device. In the drawings, an embodiment of the invention is shown.
