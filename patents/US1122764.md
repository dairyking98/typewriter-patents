## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar actions have a heavy or noisy touch because the bar is thrown to the platen at poorly shaped speeds.

**Solution:** A toggle-and-cam linkage moves the typebar with positive acceleration then negative acceleration, giving strong pressure with light touch.

**How it works:** Pressing the key swings a rocker arm whose roller runs in a cam groove of an oscillating cam member. That member drives a double toggle that pushes the typebar to the platen. The cam groove shape makes the bar start slowly, accelerate, then decelerate. At the end the pivots line up so the key stops noiselessly. A spring restores everything.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [IMP:impression-adjust](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
