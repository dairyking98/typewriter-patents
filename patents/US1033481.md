## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Escapement parts on the earlier machine are not positive enough, so they may fail to act as quickly as required.

**Solution:** Parts of the escapement are made more positive in their action, with normal-position and depressed-key positions illustrated, so the escapement acts reliably.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
