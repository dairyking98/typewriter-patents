## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a book typewriter the ribbon obstructs the view of the line, and the type-bar ring can vibrate sideways.

**Solution:** Ribbon mechanism that withdraws from the line of sight after each stroke and returns to the type path on each key depression, plus means to hold the type-bar ring steady.

**How it works:** The machine runs on stationary parallel tracks resting on the page, with a supporting frame whose base bars carry racks engaged by line-spacing pinions, and a carriage with bearing rollers on guides. Type-bars hang on a ring and are depressed to print. The ribbon spools are moved so the ribbon is out of view except while a printing key is down, a rocking trip alternately engaging spool feed for either direction. Spool holding pawls and a spring-dog on the pinion hub for locking the pinion spacing are described.
