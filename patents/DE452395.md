## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the attachment of carbon paper in typewriters and accounting machines with one of the bookings to be made on the individual forms, such as invoices, account sheets, etc.

**How it works (reading):** Carbon paper is fixed permanently to a collective sheet by pockets at the top and bottom edges, so it stays in place during the copying run.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon paper must be removed and reinserted for every booking, which costs time.

**Solution:** Carbon paper is fixed permanently to a collective sheet by pockets at the top and bottom edges, so it stays in place during the copying run.
