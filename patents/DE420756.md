> Description Translated from German Typewriter with visual writing. In the development of the typewriter, most of the points have been copied from the American models, including all of their flaws. This applies in particular to the overall structure. In order for the keyboard to be at a convenient height, the standing surface of the machine (table surface) must be around 65 cm above the floor. In the endeavor to obtain a machine that is as small as possible, one has to proceed from this footprint. the machines kept as low as possible so that the point of attachment of the types, ie the writing line, is in the highest case about 25 cm, on average only about ½ cm and in the minimum case only 7.5 cm above the standing surface. This low position of the writing line makes checking what has been written very difficult for both the writer and the dictator, so that in numerous cases curvatures of the spine and visual disturbances can be observed in the writers. Furthermore, the low position of the writing line means that the concept usually placed somewhere to the side of the machine when copying or transferring must be, which in turn is very disadvantageous for posture and eyes.
