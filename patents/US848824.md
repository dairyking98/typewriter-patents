## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing name-plate dies) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in providing dies which are adapted to be held at one end and to emboss at the other end.

**How it works (reading):** Dies are carried on radial tongues on a pair of superimposed disks near their outer edge and are inserted and removed from sockets; a die holds at one end and embosses at the other, and each die can be removed by applying tension.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Name-plates, labels and similar surfaces must be embossed with letters, but dies have to be held and removed easily.

**Solution:** Dies are carried on radial tongues on a pair of superimposed disks near their outer edge and are inserted and removed from sockets; a die holds at one end and embosses at the other, and each die can be removed by applying tension.
