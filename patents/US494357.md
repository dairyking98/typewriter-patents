## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**How it works (reading):** A spool attachment uses a sliding shaft with cog wheels meshing with the spool shafts; pushing the shaft one way or the other winds the spool in the opposite direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must wind and reverse on a Remington, and the operator wants more than one ribbon.

**Solution:** A spool attachment uses a sliding shaft with cog wheels meshing with the spool shafts; pushing the shaft one way or the other winds the spool in the opposite direction.
