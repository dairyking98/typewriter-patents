## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for projecting typed text Characters that support it, for example those based on epiprojection Facilities, allows both the characters and the writing face during to see their emergence.

**How it works (reading):** A translucent rod-shaped glass body behind the paper acts as paper counter and type stop, lit through a condenser, with an optional elastic layer on the stop side and a pivoting auxiliary mirror for viewing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Projection of typed characters onto a viewing screen is limited by the epiprojection optics and lacks strike cushioning.

**Solution:** A translucent rod-shaped glass body behind the paper acts as paper counter and type stop, lit through a condenser, with an optional elastic layer on the stop side and a pivoting auxiliary mirror for viewing.
