## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an escapemo ment mechanism for type-writing and other machines, and has for one object the provision of means whereby the machine is adapted for the use of operators of all.

**How it works (reading):** An escapement in which the spacing mechanism automatically adapts to the varying speed of the operator.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A pawl-and-rack escapement with dogs stays engaged too long at rapid key speeds, delaying carriage spacing and causing overlapping or irregular letters.

**Solution:** An escapement in which the spacing mechanism automatically adapts to the varying speed of the operator.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** rack; dogs
