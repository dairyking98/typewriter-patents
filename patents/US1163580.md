## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbon feeds drive both spool shafts with separate mechanisms, adding parts and weight.

**Solution:** Drive only one spool shaft with two opposed ratchets and a loose-sleeve ratchet linked to the other spool shaft by a belt; the engaged drive pawl sets the feed direction.

**How it works:** The first spool shaft carries two ratchets with oppositely facing teeth: one fast on the shaft and one on a loose sleeve. A separate drive pawl for each ratchet is moved by upstanding rocker arms on a rock shaft that is rocked through links from the universal bar, and the same shaft works the ribbon lift. When the fast ratchet is driven it turns the first spool directly; when the loose one is driven it turns the sleeve and, through a belt or endless connection, the second spool shaft. Feed direction depends on which pawl is engaged, and a shift rod with stirrups disengages one pawl and engages the other.

**Parts:** spool shaft; fixed ratchet; loose sleeve ratchet; drive pawl; rocker arm; rock shaft; belt; universal bar; shift rod; stirrup

**Numbers:** 2 sheets of drawings; 2 ratchets; 2 drive pawls

**Improves on:**

- ribbon feeds with separate drive on each spool shaft: one driven shaft, the other by belt, fewer parts
