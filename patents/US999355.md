## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (tallying device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Methods or arrangements for marking the record carrier in digital fashion otherwise than by punching by printing code marks for colour code marks

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to new and useful improvements in tallying devices and our object is to provide means for directing numerals or other tabulating devices into engagement with a sect-ion of paper to make an imprint on the paper.

**How it works (reading):** Keys drive numeral type into a ribbon and paper, a ribbon can be turned to a second colour, and the carriage moves one space per key while paper rolls are rewound.
