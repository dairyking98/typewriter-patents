## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (business control device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Computing machines characterised by the structural interrelation of their functional units, e.g. invoicing machines

**How it works (reading):** Each staff member has a numbered adjusting lever that sets the control device, which registers each goods voucher amount on that person's control strip instead of printing vouchers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Goods vouchers printed per sale in restaurants use much paper, slow the goods check and still allow misuse, and the staff-number lever makes the device large.

**Solution:** Each staff member has a numbered adjusting lever that sets the control device, which registers each goods voucher amount on that person's control strip instead of printing vouchers.
