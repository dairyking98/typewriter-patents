> Description Translated from German Automatic or hand-operated typewriter. The invention relates to a typewriter which is operated either by hand by pressing on keys or automatically by means of a paper strip provided with holes or groups of holes. From the previously known typewriters for self-employed operation, the invention differs in that there is not one for every letter or key. .Special hole. is. necessary in the paper strip, because the strip is provided with individual holes or groups of holes in this way. that each hole or group of holes corresponds to a certain symbol. In order to be able to operate all characters of an ordinary typewriter in this way, only a number of 5 to 7 holes and consequently only a narrow strip of paper is required in the width of the strip. The arrangement is also made in such a way that the typewriter can be operated with the aid of four or five keys by pressing one, two or more keys at the same time.
