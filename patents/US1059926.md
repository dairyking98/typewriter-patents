## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Centering a heading by mental calculation of letter counts was slow and error-prone.

**Solution:** A centering indicator on the carriage scale shows the starting point for a centered line directly, so the operator does not calculate it.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Pointer over a chart](../features/sel-pointer-chart.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
