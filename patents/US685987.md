## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book type-writers need several characters printed by one type-bar, and the earlier arrangement did not let the type-head be shifted cleanly on the bar.

**Solution:** Type-shifting mechanism on the type-bar: a tripping member and bracket move the type-head so one bar prints any of a plurality of characters.

**How it works:** A tripping member, operated by a bracket, shifts the type-head carried on the type-bar before printing; other embodiments are shown in figures 6 to 9.
