## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen turned up for erasures or inspection needs secure locking, and the platen frame must be removable.

**Solution:** Standards on the carriage hold the platen-frame journals, with a swinging latch-plate hook over one journal and a lock that holds the platen up.

**How it works:** Two metal standards with outward feet are fixed on top of the carriage. One has a hole for a journal stud of the platen-frame, the other an open seat. A pivoted latch-plate with a hook covers the open journal to prevent slipping out; it is turned down by a lever when the platen is to be withdrawn. Upward extensions limit how far the frame turns up, and a lock holds the platen raised to expose the print.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen frame; journal stud; standard; journal seat; latch-plate; hook; lever
