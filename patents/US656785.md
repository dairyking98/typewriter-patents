## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** Each hanger is a sheet-metal plate with a slot doubled back into a yoke with bored sockets for type-bar cones; a screw adjusts socket size, and the bearings sit as far apart as possible while staying nearly in line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-lever hanger bearings are hard to keep in alignment, and the hangers and key tension are costly to make and adjust.

**Solution:** Each hanger is a sheet-metal plate with a slot doubled back into a yoke with bored sockets for type-bar cones; a screw adjusts socket size, and the bearings sit as far apart as possible while staying nearly in line.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** sheet-metal hanger plate; yoke; sockets for type-bar cones; adjusting screw; key-lever tension adjuster; links to type-bars

**Numbers:** Patent 656,785; Serial No. 708,418
