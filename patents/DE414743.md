## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapements need milled ratchet teeth and are hard to clean and oil.

**Solution:** Two pawls guided in a housing are alternately driven by a gear on a drive arm, with one pawl hinged, to advance the ratchet one tooth.

**How it works:** A rod collar presses a drive arm fixed to a small gear, which meshes with teeth on the ends of two pawls in a housing. Turning the gear withdraws the hinged pawl from under a ratchet tooth while pushing the other in. A leaf spring returns the parts; the hinged pawl slides over the next tooth and the carriage advances one tooth.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
