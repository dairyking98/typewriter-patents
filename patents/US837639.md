## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the invention are to devise an arrangement of parts which shall obviate the necessity of crowding the larger characters into spaces suitable for medium-width characters and the spreading of smaller characters to fill spaces suitable for the medium letters and whereby the typing of conjunctions and articlessuch as and, or, an, and tl1e--may be accomplished in one operation and spacin in the finishe copy.

**How it works (reading):** Several sets of key levers act on the universal bar at different travel distances, and a stepping device on the escapement turns that travel into one, two or three letter spaces.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Uniform spacing crowds wide characters into medium spaces and spreads narrow ones, and common words like and, or, the need several strokes.

**Solution:** Several sets of key levers act on the universal bar at different travel distances, and a stepping device on the escapement turns that travel into one, two or three letter spaces.

**How it works:** The carriage is pulled by a constant spring and held by an escapement of fixed and moving dogs on a fork rocked by a rod. Sets of key levers push the universal bar different distances according to character width. A spacing member of the escapement is stepped to match that travel, so a spring-held dog engages the first, second or third step of the stepping device. A capital of the same letter can take a wider step. A rotary escapement variant is shown.
