## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type was made by rolling characters onto blanks with dies, which is slow.

**Solution:** Cold-forge a steel cone blank into a die with the character matrix by crushing it, then trim.

**How it works:** A punch in relief makes the matrix in a steel block die, with a rectangular tapering depression. A steel blank with a cone and shank sits in a holder; strong pressure crushes the cone so metal fills the matrix, with stippled surfaces on die and holder to stop spreading. Trimming dies then finish the type.

**Features:** [ELEM:metal-type](../features/elem-metal-type.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md)

**Parts:** punch; female die; blank holder; guide; type blank; trimming die

**Improves on:**

- rolled type from circular dies: forged in one press from a cone blank
