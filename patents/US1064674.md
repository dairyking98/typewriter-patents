## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars need vertical alignment at the printing point without the fixed guide preventing the types from striking the platen or sticking.

**Solution:** A yielding auxiliary guide with a projection that wedges into a V-notch on the type bar as it approaches the platen, beside fixed forked guides for side alignment.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
