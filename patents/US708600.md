## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A line-space pawl that disengages loses reliability; the platen must still be turned freely by hand either way.

**Solution:** A permanently engaged driving pawl on a slide, plus an auxiliary locking slide that locks the pawl into the ratchet only when the spacing lever is operated.

**How it works:** On an Underwood carriage the platen shaft carries a ratchet. A pawl pivoted on a transverse slide always rests on it. At rest a recess in an auxiliary slide lets the pawl lift, so the platen turns freely in either direction. Moving the spacing lever first pushes the auxiliary slide to block the pawl, then both slides move together and turn the ratchet. Springs restore them, and a pivoted stop limits the throw to vary line spacing.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet wheel; driving pawl; main slide; auxiliary locking slide; spacing lever; adjustable stop; spring
