## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage escapement, its release, the bell support and the margin stop of a cylindrical-platen carriage machine needed improvement.

**Solution:** A carriage escapement with a fixed pawl and sliding pawl on a carrier, a release, a bell mounted on the front rail and a marginal stop at the right.

**How it works:** The platen is mounted on a carriage moved intermittently right to left by the key-levers on rails on the top plate. A fixed pawl on a bracket and a sliding pawl on a sliding carrier work together as escapement dogs, with a release for free carriage movement. A bell is fixed on the front carriage rail with its own support, a marginal stop is at the right-hand side, and a line-spacing mechanism is carried on the carriage end.
