## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars on a holder are hard to replace, and a combined spring-and-bar part cannot use both spring steel and hard metal.

**Solution:** Type bars slide in radial sockets of a holder with separate spring members that retain them, so the bars can be removed or replaced by bending the springs.
