## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon-type writing sheets need manual lifting or wires to clear the writing from the base.

**Solution:** The sheet is pulled over a round edge so the non-adhered areas peel away, which allows continuous recording on a band.
