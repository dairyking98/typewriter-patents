## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The Underwood shift and shift-lock keys are arranged so only one key locks the platen.

**Solution:** Two temporary shift keys, either of which releases a lock hook, plus a third key that raises and locks the platen.

**How it works:** The platen axle rides in a shift frame whose wheel runs on a shift rail carried on a bell crank. A shift key lever pushes the bell crank to lift the platen. A pin on the lever cams a spring hook that snaps over it and holds the key down. Pressing either temporary shift key drives a pin or rock arm that cams the hook free, releasing the lock.
