## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Springs for returning type-levers lose tension over time, so the impact of type-levers on the platen varies and copies are uneven.

**Solution:** A constant weight is chiefly used to return the keys and type-levers to their initial position, with springs only assisting it.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)

**Parts:** return weight; rods; type-levers
