## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my improvement is to provide improved means for holding an envelop-guiding arm in inoperative position.

**How it works (reading):** A clip or keeper on the guide arm's tension spring retains the arm in its inoperative position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The envelope-guiding arm can be knocked out of its inoperative position or rise into the operator's view.

**Solution:** A clip or keeper on the guide arm's tension spring retains the arm in its inoperative position.
