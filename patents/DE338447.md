## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (counter drive wheel displacement)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a typewriter in which the counter relative to its main drive wheel is transversely displaceable, so that according to the respective Keystroke the counter is shifted by such an amount that the main drive wheel with the gear for the next one Priority engaged.

**How it works (reading):** Curved lifting pieces on each number key move a rack that brings the main drive wheel into or out of engagement with the counter, shifting the counter transversely with the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier counter-shift designs for combined typewriter and calculator were complex and less certain in operation.

**Solution:** Curved lifting pieces on each number key move a rack that brings the main drive wheel into or out of engagement with the counter, shifting the counter transversely with the carriage.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
