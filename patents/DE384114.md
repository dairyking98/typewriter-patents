## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a mirror device for typewriters with visible writing that serves the purpose, the written line just above the last or top row of buttons to appear.

**How it works (reading):** Mirrors placed just above the top keyboard row reflect the line written on the platen, so the operator sees the line while looking at the keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The writer must look up to see the line being typed, which slows and tires the operator.

**Solution:** Mirrors placed just above the top keyboard row reflect the line written on the platen, so the operator sees the line while looking at the keys.

**Features:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** mirror a; mirror b; platen; keys
