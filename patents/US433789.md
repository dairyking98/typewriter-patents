## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must find carriage positions for margins and centring by mental arithmetic on the plain scale.

**Solution:** A metal scale strip with turned-over edges fits to the bed-plate; a pointer on the carriage reads a principal scale and a head-line centring and margin scale, with an erasable paper channel.

**How it works:** Multiple graduated scales on the bed-plate read against a carriage pointer.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
