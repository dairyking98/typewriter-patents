## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** When loading paper on the Caligraph the line-spacing pawls hold the platen ratchet, which must be released separately.

**Solution:** One lever with a pin lifts both spacing pawls off the platen ratchet wheel and holds them up while the paper is adjusted.

**How it works:** The line-space lever carries a pawl that steps the ratchet wheel; a pivoted arm carries the holding pawl. A separate hand lever pivoted to a frame lug has a flattened pin on which both pawls rest; raising it to vertical lifts both clear of the ratchet, freeing the platen. A lug on the lever limits its backward travel.

**Parts:** line-space lever; spacing pawl; holding pawl; ratchet wheel; release lever; flattened pin; stop lug; impression roller
