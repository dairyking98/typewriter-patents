## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feeding for coin-controlled typing machines needs few parts, positive action, constant tension and automatic reversal.

**Solution:** A reciprocating bar carries a pivoted disk with two pawl arms that alternately engage the two bobbin ratchets, and the disk flips automatically when a bobbin runs out.

**How it works:** The ribbon winds between two bobbins, each with a ratchet. Two oscillating arms ending in pawls sit on a common pivot on a disk, which is pivoted on a reciprocating bar driven by a pitman from the machine. A detent engaging one of two notches in the disk holds one pawl on its ratchet and the other clear. When the ribbon is fully unwound and anchored to the empty bobbin, the engaged ratchet resists and drags the active arm, rotating the disk to the other notch. That swaps the pawls and reverses the feed direction.
