## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed dog and carriage release on a Yost typebar machine need to be simpler and cheaper to make.

**Solution:** A rock-shaft built of a solid rod, a hollow rod and a slotted housing carries the pivoted feed dog, with the release key passing through the hollow part.

**How it works:** A rock-shaft is actuated through a link from the universal bar and carries a feed dog pivoted in the slot of its housing. On key release the dog swings back and re-engages the outer feed rack, and a spring-driven arm moves the carriage one letter space. The dog can vibrate in its slot so the rack teeth slide over it on carriage return. A release key whose shank runs through the hollow rod disengages the dog so the carriage can run freely.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** rock-shaft; feed dog; feed rack; release key; universal bar link; spiral spring; driving arm
