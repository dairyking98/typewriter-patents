## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Operator must set column-selecting and line-spacing actions during carriage return with one hand.

**Solution:** Column-selecting devices mounted on the carriage, with a pawl and rocking arm on the platen, act during the return stroke to stop the carriage and line-space.
