## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single machine should take interchangeable platen frames of different lengths for narrow or wide carriages.

**Solution:** Platen frames detach from the carriage truck and are swapped for short or long frames, with supports for a long tabulator bar.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
