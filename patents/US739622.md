## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates generally to writingmachine type-actions,and particularly tothose i in which type-bars strike the front side of a platen.

**How it works (reading):** Link motions connect keys to type-bars so that a slight change in the relative positions of the connecting members makes the touch lighter or heavier; the connections also lock the type-bars in normal position and cushion the key stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says key touch cannot be set to suit different operators, and type-bars rebound from the typebasket after rapid operation, causing jar to the finger.

**Solution:** Link motions connect keys to type-bars so that a slight change in the relative positions of the connecting members makes the touch lighter or heavier; the connections also lock the type-bars in normal position and cushion the key stroke.
