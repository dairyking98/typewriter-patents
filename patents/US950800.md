## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single rack escapement lets the carriage skip or jump teeth because the carriage moves too quickly for standard forms.

**Solution:** A dog escapement on a single rack, with a spring drawing the carriage, limits movement so the carriage does not skip.
