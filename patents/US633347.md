## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage and advancing a blank paper space after each line is tiring by hand.

**Solution:** A foot-treadle attachment draws the carriage back and trips the line-space lever to advance the platen for a blank space.

**How it works:** A treadle with return spring acts through a rocking lever and pitman on a lever fixed to the typewriter frame. This moves a shifting lever against a pressure pin on the carriage, pushing the carriage back against its feed. A trip fixed to the line-spacing lever with a cam finger works with a stationary cam to turn the roller platen to a plain paper portion.
