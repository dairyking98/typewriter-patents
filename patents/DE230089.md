## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing for sighted correspondence with the blind needs two machines or special equipment.

**Solution:** One keyboard operates two typebar mechanisms on a common step-by-step carriage drive, one for ordinary print and one for Braille.

**How it works:** Two sets of type levers hang on bearing plates, each cut out separately. The two carriages share an escapement through transmissions for the feed and the rotation of a slide. The second set can carry Gothic type or secret script, or Braille tips which emboss relief dots against a paper roller shaped as a negative. Ribbon mechanism supports visible writing for the ordinary set.
