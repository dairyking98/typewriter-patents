## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier carriage structures had disadvantages in mounting the truck, supporting the platen frame and handling the hand lever.

**Solution:** Carriage structure with a truck mount, a platen frame that swings back, and a hand lever that disengages the feed rack from the pinion.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
