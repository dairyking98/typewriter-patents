## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Transport locks are held only by a lid fit and remain on the machine after transport.

**Solution:** Pressed sheet-metal fittings with cup-shaped bulges grip the platen rollers and screw to the carriage girders, and are removed and reused on other machines.
