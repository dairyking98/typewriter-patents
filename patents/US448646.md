## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ribbon needs both longitudinal feed and a transverse (spool to spool) movement with simple means.

**Solution:** Spacing keys rock a frame whose pawls turn the ribbon-spool worm shafts by ratchets, while a switch lever changes the direction.

**How it works:** A rocker bar at the rear carries a rocking frame with push-pawls for each side. The pawls turn ratchet wheels on worm shafts, which through worm gears rotate the ribbon spool shafts. Lifting rods with slotted tops, worked by cam levers joined by a connecting rod, form a ribbon switch that engages one spool at a time. Pitmen from the spools to horizontal ratchets also move the ribbon transversely.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** rocker bar; rocking frame; push-pawl; ratchet wheel; worm shaft; worm gear; ribbon spool; ribbon switch lever; lifting rod; pitman; guide wire
