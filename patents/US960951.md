## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenographic machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to stemgraphic machines of the kind shown in Letters Patent of the United States, No.

**How it works (reading):** A combined paper feed roller and platen driven by a mechanism with a paper feed tension roller and a spacing device, plus improved key and frame details.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stenographic machines must feed paper and print characters noiselessly and precisely, with improved construction over the earlier design.

**Solution:** A combined paper feed roller and platen driven by a mechanism with a paper feed tension roller and a spacing device, plus improved key and frame details.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md)
