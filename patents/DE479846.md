## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An addressing rotary printer needs its envelope roller to run at a speed matched to the type drum.

**Solution:** A smaller pressure roller is driven through a gear coupled by a cam-controlled clutch, so its speed follows the drum gear ratio while the address plate is printed.
