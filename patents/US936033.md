## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides stationary

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to prevent a curling or folding of, the ribbon, to cause the ribbon ,to be evenly and smoothly wound upon spools, dud to impart a proper tension tot-he ribbon in its passage over the printing point.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon guides formed as slotted fingers on the ribbon vibrator bracket that keep the ribbon flat and smoothly wound, and give proper tension at the printing point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon tends to curl as it runs between spools, so it wraps unevenly and the tension at the printing point is wrong.

**Solution:** Ribbon guides formed as slotted fingers on the ribbon vibrator bracket that keep the ribbon flat and smoothly wound, and give proper tension at the printing point.
