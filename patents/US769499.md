## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (inking device for mail-marking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ducts, containers, supply or metering devices with duct-blades or like metering devices

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates generally to inking devices for printing or marking machines, and particularly to inking mechanisms for mail-marking machines;

**How it works (reading):** A swinging bracket hung inside the ink-well supplies ink evenly to the die or type through a roller or inking wheel, with the device usable on any mail-marking machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink supply to the die or type in mail-marking machines was uneven and needed to be applied consistently.

**Solution:** A swinging bracket hung inside the ink-well supplies ink evenly to the die or type through a roller or inking wheel, with the device usable on any mail-marking machine.

**Features:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
