## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulator mechanism and to means for readily bringing the column stops into use at difierent column determining positions and for clearing ---the stops preparatory to resetting them in a difi'erent arrangement.

**How it works (reading):** Stop setting devices bring column stops into use, and a stop positioning device on the column stops is connected to the setting means, with an automatic letter space after each stop is made operative.
