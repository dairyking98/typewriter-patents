## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The type-bar and key action of front-strike machines needs an improved arrangement of levers and linkage between the keys and the type bars.

**Solution:** A key action with a top plan view of the key linkage drives the type bars to the platen; a modified framing for the key action is shown in a further figure. The OCR does not reveal the linkage in enough detail to describe it further.
