## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (coin-operated time-limit mechanism) · **Subject (patent classes):** Coin-freed facilities or services for printing, stamping, franking, typing or teleprinting apparatus for weighing devices

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to coin-operated, time-controlled mechanism for permitting a typewriting machine to be used for a predetermined period of time upon prepayment of a given sum or charge.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-held locking member is released by a coin, and a clockwork notched disk resets the lock after a set period.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Prior coin-operated typewriter timers need a hand-operated lever to release the lock.

**Solution:** A spring-held locking member is released by a coin, and a clockwork notched disk resets the lock after a set period.

**How it works:** Coin releases a spring-held lock; clockwork and a notched disk return the parts after the timed period.
