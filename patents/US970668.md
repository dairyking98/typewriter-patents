## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Variable line spacing needs uniform intervals at any chosen setting, without platen overthrow.

**Solution:** A line-spacing lever works a clutch dog that bites a flanged wheel on the platen axle and is arrested by an adjustable stop that makes it bite harder at the moment of arrest.

**How it works:** A flanged wheel is fixed on the platen axle. A dog pivoted near its jaws on a loose carrier is moved by the line-space and carriage-return lever with strong purchase, so the jaws first bite the flange then carry the wheel and platen round. A spring biases the dog toward gripping, while another holds the dog released and the carrier against an abutment at rest. A stop engages the dog at the end of the stroke, tightening the grip so the platen cannot overthrow. The stop is finely adjusted around the axle by a screw through a block and link, with a click wheel and index and a scale on the platen frame matching usual line spacings.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen axle; flanged wheel; clutch dog; carrier; line-space lever; spring; adjustable stop; regulating screw; block; link; click; scale

**Improves on:**

- notched line-space wheel: continuously variable line spacing with clutch dog and adjustable stop
