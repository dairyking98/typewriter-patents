## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (carriage traverse counter and alarm) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to inform the attendant of the number of times the carriage to which it is attached has traversed its track and to give an alarm at the proper point at which the machinery is to be stopped, and also to indicatethe near approach of such stopping-point;

**How it works (reading):** A numbered disk on the platen shaft carries a pin that trips an alarm spring and bell as the line count nears the end.
