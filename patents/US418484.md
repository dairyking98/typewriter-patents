## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (telegraph perforating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms producing characters by printing and also producing a record by other means, e.g. printer combined with RFID writer

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates particularly to improvements in perforating-machines used in connection with automatic telegraphy, wherein the transmit-ting strips or fillets are perforated to represent the messages to be transmitted;

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter-style keyboard sets mechanically applied power that punches the telegraph fillet and at the same time prints a Roman-letter copy of the message on a second tape as a record.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Telegraph perforators make the operator work the perforating apparatus directly, which is hard to manipulate delicately and slow to prepare tape for.

**Solution:** A typewriter-style keyboard sets mechanically applied power that punches the telegraph fillet and at the same time prints a Roman-letter copy of the message on a second tape as a record.
