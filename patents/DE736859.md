## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (booking machine typewriter with multiplier)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an accounting machine, in particular a typewriter a multiplier in which the multiplication of two factors by cumulative addition is effected.

**How it works (reading):** Multiplication by repeated addition is done purely mechanically: a plunger driven by power moves a rack-and-pinion system that transfers the multiplicand into the product.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electrical contact multipliers in typewriter-based accounting machines are fault-prone and hard to repair.

**Solution:** Multiplication by repeated addition is done purely mechanically: a plunger driven by power moves a rack-and-pinion system that transfers the multiplicand into the product.
