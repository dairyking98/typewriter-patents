## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**How it works (reading):** A signal device, a bell rung by an oscillating bar, is operated after a predetermined number of line-space operations counted by a rack bar on the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The writer cannot tell where the bottom margin of a work sheet is, so a warning is needed.

**Solution:** A signal device, a bell rung by an oscillating bar, is operated after a predetermined number of line-space operations counted by a rack bar on the carriage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
