## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to that class of typewriters in which the printing mechanism, consisting of type mounted on the ends 50f movable type-bars, is located below the platen, the impressions being made on the under side of the platensuoh, for instance, as described in my application, Serial No.

**How it works (reading):** An understroke machine with type-bars below the platen, a tilting platen to expose the print point, a ribbon holder and feed, automatic carriage return with paper advance, and an electric motor for the key levers.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Underneath-printing typewriters hid the printing point and needed better ribbon and carriage return.

**Solution:** An understroke machine with type-bars below the platen, a tilting platen to expose the print point, a ribbon holder and feed, automatic carriage return with paper advance, and an electric motor for the key levers.

**How it works:** Type on bars in a basket below the platen print upward on its underside. The platen can be thrown up for inspection, and the carriage return feeds the paper forward automatically. A bell hammer, sheet indicator, spring-wire paper guide, key locking device and electromagnet motor for the key levers are shown.
