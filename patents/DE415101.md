## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Removable character buttons are either costly, slow to change, or wear out soft rubber rings.

**Solution:** An annular spring inside the cylindrical extension of the character button presses through slots against the inner wall of the cup on the key lever, so the button is clamped and changed quickly by hand.

**How it works:** The spring snaps the button into the cup, and pulling the button out releases the spring grip without tools.
