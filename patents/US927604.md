## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for fixing the film on the axis of a reel or spindle

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon spools for typewriting and like machines.

**How it works (reading):** Two sheet metal flanges joined by rivets with a driving perforation pattern, and a detachable ribbon end securing means.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools must be simple, cheap and strong, and the ribbon end must attach easily.

**Solution:** Two sheet metal flanges joined by rivets with a driving perforation pattern, and a detachable ribbon end securing means.
