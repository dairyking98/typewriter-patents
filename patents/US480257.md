**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to attachments for type-writing machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type faces clog with ink and dirt and cleaning each one by hand is slow.

**Solution:** A brush wheel carried on a frame attachment fixed to the machine is placed over the printing point, so the type are brushed as they are struck up to it, and is turned by a handle.
