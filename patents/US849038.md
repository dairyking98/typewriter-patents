**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing and embossing for checks) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks and bonds can be altered by erasure, so the amount must be marked in a way that makes any attempt at alteration visible.

**Solution:** Cooperating raised and recessed dies in two sets emboss the characters into the sheet and deposit ink on both sides, so that any erasure makes more colour show through the paper.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Platen pressed to the element](../features/imp-platen-to-element.md)
