> Description Translated from German Device for locking individual keys of cash registers, calculating machines and the like in a depressed position. The invention relates to a device for blocking individual keys of cash registers, calculating machines and the like Position by means of a bracket supported on the key in question. Of known locking devices of this type, in which the locking member each row of keys by a sliding guide or the like. Slidable with the keyboard is connected, the subject matter of the invention differs in that the locking member is designed like a bracket and is completely independent of the cash register, which has the advantage that a special preparation of the cash register for Use of such clips is not required and the locking clip immediately can be moved from one key to another without the intervening ones Buttons somehow affected.
