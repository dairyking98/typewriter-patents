## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (blocking keys of cash registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for blocking individual keys of cash registers, calculating machines and the like Position by means of a bracket supported on the key in question.

**How it works (reading):** A separate spring clip with a side finger holds one key depressed and can be moved from key to key without modifying the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Built-in key locks on cash registers and calculators required special preparation of the machine.

**Solution:** A separate spring clip with a side finger holds one key depressed and can be moved from key to key without modifying the machine.

**Features:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
