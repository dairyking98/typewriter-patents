## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage must skip blank spaces in forms without printing and without the operator counting spaces.

**Solution:** Interchangeable shield pieces with cam blocks are placed along the release bar so they lift the let-off pawl and lock the printing key pawl over a chosen interval.

**How it works:** Shield pieces of different lengths are pinned into perforations on the feed-release bar at positions matching the front scale. A cam block lifts the holding let-off pawl so the carriage runs freely over that interval, while a second cam block acts on the limber pawl to prevent printing keys. When the block passes, the pawl drops into the rack and printing resumes.
