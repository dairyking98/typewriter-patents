## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The front paper guide and card holder must give way when the feed rolls are released or the scale is moved to the print point.

**Solution:** Pivoted feed rolls lift off the platen by a rotary shaft, which also moves a vertical scale bar to the printing point and swings the front guide clear.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** feed rolls; scale bar; shaft; guide
