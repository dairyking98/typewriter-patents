## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known card holders on the paper roller need special guide parts for card insertion and their holding plate can collide with other machine parts.

**Solution:** A card holder plate fastened near one edge to the paper roller, with leaf springs on its inner face that push its other edge off the roller so it clears other parts and also act as the lateral and lower guide when a card is inserted.

**How it works:** Fastening at one edge and springs pressing the free edge away from the roller let the card slide in between the plate and the roller, guided by the springs.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** card holder plate; leaf springs; paper roller; fastening screws; card guide scale; guide fingers
