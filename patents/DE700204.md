> Description Translated from German Paper tape unwinding and winding device for accounting machines or the like. The present invention relates to an unwinding and winding device for accounting machines and in particular table machines that work with punched cards and are used as a printing underlay a continuous paper tape is used that is unwound from a roll, fed to the platen and rewound on a roll after printing will. In such machines, the platen is apart from the device for the usual line feed often with a device for an additional Provide paper feed when writing totals or forms. You also have the take-up device provided with a power drive that prevents the printed part of the paper tape hangs loosely and possibly touches the floor. In contrast, the platen had previously had to remove the paper tape from the supply roll peel off immediately. Given the high working speed of the punch cards controlled tabulating machines consists in pulling the paper tape from the Supply roll through the platen there is a risk that the paper will either tear or between the pinch rollers and the platen slides, so that the size of the paper advance becomes imprecise.
