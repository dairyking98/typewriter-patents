**How it works (reading):** The platen rotates for letter-spacing and moves longitudinally for line-spacing, in a simpler construction with fewer parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen-line typewriter has many parts and is costly to build.

**Solution:** The platen rotates for letter-spacing and moves longitudinally for line-spacing, in a simpler construction with fewer parts.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
