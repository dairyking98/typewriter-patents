## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to devices for sliding the cart of typewriters, especially writing adding machines, and The purpose of this is to position the carriage opposite the point of impact of the writing types to enable in a particularly simple manner.

**How it works (reading):** A row of five keys on a shaft is depressed to set the carriage slide to the chosen decimal place; a shaft-carried strip limits the rotation so that only the pressed key moves, and a lever drives the line-switching pawl.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage of a writing adding machine must be positioned under the print point for each decimal column in a simple way.

**Solution:** A row of five keys on a shaft is depressed to set the carriage slide to the chosen decimal place; a shaft-carried strip limits the rotation so that only the pressed key moves, and a lever drives the line-switching pawl.
