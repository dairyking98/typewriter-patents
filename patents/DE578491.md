## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for recording stenograms and transferring them on the typewriter The invention relates to a device for recording Stenograms and their transfer to the typewriter. She aims one Simplification and cheaper typewriter operation through. use of long, narrow strips of paper running over rolls in the manner of Morse code instead of the previously used shorthand blocks as well as an increase in performance and typist convenience by arranging the shorthand strip in close proximity to the eyes above the keyboard. There are already devices known in which relatively narrow strips of paper can be used. But here was the entire device firmly connected to the typewriter and could not be removed at any time and can be reattached. Then there were always several lines of writing on the visible from a lower strip that can be wound onto a dbere roll, whereby the typist can easily be misled. These known devices are not suitable for labeling the shorthand strip on a special Writing surface and for later clamping in the typewriter.
