## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a holder carrying two type plates (for example Russian and Latin) is turned half a revolution to change alphabets, the plates must not slide out of place.

**Solution:** Spring-loaded hooks grip the eye of each type plate while the holder is raised and turned, and are released by fixed levers when the holder is lowered so the driving pin can move the plate.

**How it works:** A holder with slots carries two type plates, each with an eye that the machine's driving pin engages when the holder is lowered. The holder hub has pivoted hooks with forked ends, held up by a coil spring so that they grip the plate eyes from below. Raising the holder, by hand on a nut or with a key and lever, takes the driving pin out of the eyes and lets the hooks lock the plates. The holder, gripped by spring pincers on its axle, is turned half a revolution while resting on a stand. When it drops back under a spring, rollers on fixed levers enter between lugs on the hooks and release the plates for the driving pin.
