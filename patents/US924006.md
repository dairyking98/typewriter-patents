**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to carriage stops therefor.

**How it works (reading):** A notched cylindrical tabulator rod takes stops formed from sheet metal blanks, spaced one letter apart.

**Problem:** Tabulator stops need a detachable stop that is cheap and strong.

**Solution:** A notched cylindrical tabulator rod takes stops formed from sheet metal blanks, spaced one letter apart.
