## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewriting machines, and the object is to enable an operatorto conceal the typewritten sheet in.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoted opaque screen on the platen frame overhangs the platen and the printing point and swings out of the way for access.
