## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Flat typewriters for book-keeping that move the machine over the sheet are heavy and awkward, and an earlier design with type-set carriers per column was complex, costly and wear-prone.

**Solution:** A flat typewriter in which the paper is moved by lever pressure against the types and many parallel type-set carriers, one per letter position in a line, are each stopped by key-set pins in a stop frame to bring the chosen type into a writing slot.

**How it works:** One type-set carrier (setting rod holding all keyboard types) is arranged per character position along the line, so working width is changed by adding carriers. Each key pivots a lever that raises a staggered stop pin into the path of a stop frame, whose position matches the type spacing on the carrier. A pivoting bracket on a carriage triggers a pawl holding the carrier at rest, so the carrier shifts until its stop meets the pin and the chosen type enters the writing slot in the paper table. The paper is then moved against the types for the impression.
