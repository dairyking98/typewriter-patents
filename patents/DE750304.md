> Description Translated from German Backlash compensation device in the feed drive of milling machines The invention relates to a backlash compensation device in the feed drive of Milling machines with two arranged on a spindle, via gears with different Speeds of drivable nuts, the speed difference of which is reflected in a friction clutch compensates. Such devices are already known in various designs. A backlash compensation device is known, in which on an im. Slide non-rotatable and uriverschiebbaren spindle two nuts act, one of which is driven directly by the drive, while the second either via a Friction clutch is driven faster or optionally rigid with the first nut can be connected. The larger one that is present when the friction clutch is used The speed of the second nut is compensated for by the friction clutch slipping. the both nuts are therefore clamped on their spindle: The spindle is thereby shifted without play. This freedom of play of the slide has to be bought with disadvantages will. The nut driven over the clutch must be used with every down-cut milling work to be driven.
