## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (backlash compensation, milling machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Preventing backlash

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a backlash compensation device in the feed drive of Milling machines with two arranged on a spindle, via gears with different Speeds of drivable nuts, the speed difference of which is reflected in a friction clutch compensates.

**How it works (reading):** A backlash-compensated feed drive with a regulated friction clutch and two nuts; the drag force is set to match the cutting load.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Backlash compensation with a friction clutch between two feed nuts causes spindle and nut wear and is hard to set.

**Solution:** A backlash-compensated feed drive with a regulated friction clutch and two nuts; the drag force is set to match the cutting load.
