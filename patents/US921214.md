## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fractional line spacing needs platen clutches, and prior latch clutches release the platen from the line-space wheel by sliding a part along the platen axle.

**Solution:** Improved means operate the platen clutch so fractional (half) line spacing can be done; the dogs and wedges on the line-space wheel and the operating mechanism are only partly legible.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
