## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Underwood ribbon spool cups sit under the carriage front bar so threading ribbon is awkward, and the ribbon reversing arms of US828548 could engage together.

**Solution:** Swivelling ribbon cups with finger piece and return spring, and a latch spring tensioned by the swinging arm so only one reversing arm engages.

**How it works:** Each spool cup turns about the spool axis to bring its opening into reach for threading, with a finger piece and a spring to return it. The ribbon passes over flanged guide pulleys through the cups and a vibrating guide. In the reverser, a reversing arm swung by its toothed wheel tensions the spring controlling the latch so the spring pulls the arm clear afterwards.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; swivelling cup; flanged pulley; vibrating guide; feed-reversing arm; toothed wheel; latch spring

**Improves on:**

- [US828548](../patents/US828548.md): makes simultaneous engagement of both reversing arms practically impossible
