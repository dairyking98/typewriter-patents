## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a typewriter that feeds auxiliary colour ribbons between carbon copies, the ribbons must be pulled taut at the end of each ribbon switching movement, and having separate devices for braking and locking the spools is complicated.

**Solution:** One and the same device, driven by the ribbon-switching drive lever, both brakes the auxiliary ribbon spools during switching and locks them near the end so the ribbons stretch taut.

**How it works:** The carriage carries a spool frame holding a replaceable basket of auxiliary ribbon spools. The ribbons run to the front of the platen between the sheets, past the main ribbon, to a switching device with feed rollers on the right. Normally each spool is lightly braked during switching. The ribbon-switching drive lever also operates a cam and brake spring that increases the braking steadily until the spools are fixed, shortly before the end of the switching movement, so the ribbons are stretched taut and cannot be pulled upwards by the sheet on the following line feed. The spool frame runs on a rail fixed to the main frame.
