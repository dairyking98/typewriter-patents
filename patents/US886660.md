## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Preventing rebound or clash of levers or type members

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, particularly-those known as visible writers, and its objects are to provide means for regulating the strength of the blows of the types, and to improve and simplify the case-shifting mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Type bars radiate from a curved fulcrum and sit in an adjustable segment, with means to set the segment horizontally, and a simplified case shift.
