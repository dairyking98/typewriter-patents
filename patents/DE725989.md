## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter automatic summation device)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with an automatic Sumummzugv device in which is held in its effective position by a locking member via a common clutch control element with the control element for the single-turn clutch related to patent 721,607.

**How it works (reading):** Each arithmetic key lever is held in its effective position by a common control member, and the locking member is brought into a fixed locking rail independently of the drive coupling.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The arithmetic key levers were held by a movable locking element that needed a precise timing relation to the drive.

**Solution:** Each arithmetic key lever is held in its effective position by a common control member, and the locking member is brought into a fixed locking rail independently of the drive coupling.

**How it works:** Common control member locks each arithmetic key lever against a fixed rail, independent of drive timing.
