## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** The arithmetic key cam drives a slide and lever that unlock the counter and total wheels in sequence before the gear train moves, so acceleration is harmless.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carry-over errors occur in a calculating typewriter because gear-train inertia moves the counter past the intended digit.

**Solution:** The arithmetic key cam drives a slide and lever that unlock the counter and total wheels in sequence before the gear train moves, so acceleration is harmless.
