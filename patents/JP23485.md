## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** Its object is to print a great many characters or types with a simple apparatus.

**How it works (reading):** A quarter-circle type plate carried on a pivoted arm is swung by a single key lever that is moved along a notched character table to choose the character, then pressed straight down to strike it, the same press also advancing the paper by one mainspring-driven space.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventor wants to print a very large number of characters with a simple apparatus, with letter spacing and line spacing that can run either way across or along the paper.

**Solution:** A quarter-circle type plate carried on a pivoted arm is swung by a single key lever that is moved along a notched character table to choose the character, then pressed straight down to strike it, the same press also advancing the paper by one mainspring-driven space.

**How it works:** Several type plates, each with a quarter-circle edge of types drawn about the arm pivot with the printing point as radius, are arranged in an arc converging on the printing point. The operator slides the key lever sideways over a character table (turning it about a lower shaft) until its edge meets the notch for the wanted character; this swings the arm and plate so that character stands at the printing point. Pressing the lever straight down along the notch then strikes the plate toward the printing point, linkage keeping the angular position fixed during the blow. The downstroke also turns a rock shaft that lets a mainspring-driven roll move the paper one tooth through a pawl-and-lug feed, so printing and letter spacing are one action, and a separate spacing lever spaces alone. A second key and a second spring feed the whole paper support by one line, a rack and handle free the support for quick sliding, and by swapping two link connections letter and line feed can be exchanged between the two directions.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention concerns a typewriter in which a type plate, with a quarter-circle edge carrying types or characters, is moved angularly by an arm so as to bring any type or character into its proper position relative to a fixed printing point, holds that position, and strikes, so performing the printing. It is characterised in particular by advancing the letter space vertically or horizontally, either with each impression or independently, and by advancing the line space horizontally or vertically by pressing another key lever. Its object is to print a great many characters or types with a simple apparatus. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show one form of mechanism representing the invention.
