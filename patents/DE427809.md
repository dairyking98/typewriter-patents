## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** The drive gear is stamped from sheet metal and mounted in bearing eyes pressed out of the cage web, so it can tilt sideways while staying in mesh with flexible thin-sheet racks.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage flex under load makes the gear that drives the ball cage disengage from its racks.

**Solution:** The drive gear is stamped from sheet metal and mounted in bearing eyes pressed out of the cage web, so it can tilt sideways while staying in mesh with flexible thin-sheet racks.
