## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to ribbon actuating: mechanisn't for typewriting machines in tended more particularly for application to 5 machines of the kind known as the ()liver typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** Vertical ribbon spool shafts at each side are rotated by a swinging actuator, so the ribbon can be reversed by winding from one spool to the other.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon actuating mechanism of the Oliver typewriter is complicated and should be simplified.

**Solution:** Vertical ribbon spool shafts at each side are rotated by a swinging actuator, so the ribbon can be reversed by winding from one spool to the other.
