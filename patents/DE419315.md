## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator keyboard clear and lock) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocking devices, e.g. between keys

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the invention is now to create an extinguishing device, those for clearing the keyboards both in the rotation of the drive shaft in one as well as in the other direction can be used, at the same time a device to lock the keyboard is provided to prevent the keys of the keyboard can be adjusted when the drive shaft moves out of its zero position which is sure to prevent false results.

**How it works (reading):** A loosely mounted tooth is driven by the shaft through a lever to clear the keyboard in either direction, and a cam on the shaft disables the key-lock bar as the shaft leaves zero, so the keys cannot be set mid-calculation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboard erase devices only work when the drive shaft turns one way, while calculators also turn it the other way, and keys must be locked whenever the shaft leaves zero.

**Solution:** A loosely mounted tooth is driven by the shaft through a lever to clear the keyboard in either direction, and a cam on the shaft disables the key-lock bar as the shaft leaves zero, so the keys cannot be set mid-calculation.
