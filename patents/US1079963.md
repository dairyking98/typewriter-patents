## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pneumatic carriage return and line spacing need flexible control of return speed and independence of the two actions.

**Solution:** A set of key-controlled connections to a pneumatic motor lets the carriage return at variable speed, the line space operate with or without return, and a brake stop the carriage.

**How it works:** Air valves drive the letter feed, the carriage return, and the line spacer, all under key control. Mechanical key connections cut off the air for a slow return, and settings let line spacing occur on every return or independently. A key applies a brake to stop the carriage mid-return. Connections reset to normal letter-feed drive automatically.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** pneumatic motor; slide valve; three-way valve; key connections; brake; carriage; line spacer
