## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The shift key is hard to work and earlier attachments needed skilled fitting.

**Solution:** A clip-on attachment with a shaft and bar over the space bar that operates the shift key without altering the machine.

**How it works:** A U-shaped clip fits over the keyboard front frame, held by bolts through perforated wings. Two journal bearings on its lower edge carry a rod whose bent end lies over the space bar, with a padded finger part parallel to the space bar, to operate the shift key.

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
