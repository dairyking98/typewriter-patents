## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforating punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in a perforating-punch, the same being more particularly designed as a check-punch and for analogous uses.

**How it works (reading):** A V-shaped dial plate on a swivelled post selects a numeral on an arc, and a pressure plate clamps the check while male and female dies punch it.
