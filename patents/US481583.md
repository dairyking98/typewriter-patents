## Summary

**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in a perforating-punch, the same being more particularly designed as a check-punch and for analogous uses.
