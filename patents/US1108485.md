## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type action needs better touch and less effort, and the printing instrument should swing toward the platen without reactive strain on its pivot.

**Solution:** The actuating member follows the type bar and drives it at increasing speed through its entire stroke, arranged so the force is applied near the center of percussion.
