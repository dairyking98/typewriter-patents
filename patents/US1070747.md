## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Underwood front-strike machine needed access to the underside of the platen carriage and a case-shift that carries the spring barrel with the carriage.

**Solution:** A platen-shifting carriage support that can also be swung up out of the way, carrying the spring barrel and letter-feed dogs with it.

**How it works:** Front-strike typebars hit the front of a platen on a carriage whose support shifts up and down for capitals. The carriage-driving spring barrel and feed dogs are mounted on the shifting frame, so the carriage need not be hinged in two parts. The support can be tilted up to expose the underside, and the spring is held from driving the carriage when tilted up. Keys operate the dogs when the frame is lowered.
