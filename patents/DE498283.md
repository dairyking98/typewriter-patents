## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The friction member is attached directly to the loose pawl itself, so the loose pawl stays swung out, held by friction from the ratchet wheel, until the ratchet reverses.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The separate locking pawl needed to hold the ratchet reversal in the carriage-return device lowers reliability.

**Solution:** The friction member is attached directly to the loose pawl itself, so the loose pawl stays swung out, held by friction from the ratchet wheel, until the ratchet reverses.
