> Description Translated from German Sound-absorbing footrest for office machines The subject of the Invention forms -a sound-absorbing foot support for office machines, such as S_ typewriters, calculating machines, etc. There are already foot bases with sliding one into the other Helical spring surrounded guide sleeves have been proposed, in which, however the rigid upper part is in direct contact with the rigid lower part. Here, the spring can not completely suppress the noise generated by Resonance and conduction arise between the two guide parts. That's why it is a special buffer pad is provided on the lower part, which, however, after a short time Use is squeezed and hardened, increasing its sound-absorbing effect is also lost. There have also already been proposed damping pads which two flat springs on a hard surface in a row like this one against the other are attached opposite that their free ends lie one above the other. As soon as however When the machine is placed on it, these springs bend and are supported on each other, whereby the resilient effect is largely lost and that The whole forms a hard base that no longer has a sound-absorbing effect.
