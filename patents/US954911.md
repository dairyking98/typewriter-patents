## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A long record sheet hangs over the back of the machine and gets in the way when smaller sheets are removed and inserted in condensed billing work.

**Solution:** A hinged frame carries adjustable yokes with paper-feeding and guiding fingers and rollers that run along the platen; the rollers can be swung against the platen and locked on a supporting rod, and an auxiliary paper table holds the long sheet.
