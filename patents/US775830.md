## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the invention is to provide comparatively simple mechanism whereby the type-bars may be actuated from the source of power and whereby the control of such source of power and the selection of the type-bars may be readily effected.

**How it works (reading):** Electromagnetic (solenoid) power drives the typebars through key-lever fulcrum holes, with the fulcrum rod moved to another hole for manual operation.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars should be driven by motive power with the operator only selecting, yet the machine should still work manually.

**Solution:** Electromagnetic (solenoid) power drives the typebars through key-lever fulcrum holes, with the fulcrum rod moved to another hole for manual operation.

**How it works:** Key levers fulcrum on a common rod through one of two holes: one for solenoid operation, the other for manual. A secondary lever pivoted on each key lever becomes a rigid arm of it in manual mode and engages a slot in a type lever. The type levers, bent according to position, connect through twisted links to semicircularly fulcrumed typebars. In power mode, key action selects and controls the solenoid that drives the bars.
