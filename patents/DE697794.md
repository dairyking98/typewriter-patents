## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Quiet type lever drives with a swing weight gave only weak, uneven impressions, and earlier designs needed platen adjustment to fit writing force.

**Solution:** The energy store that continues the type lever after the key stops is an eccentric swing weight serving as the articulation in a toggle linkage, giving a strong, even, quiet impression.

**How it works:** The type lever is hinged to two riveted rods, whose other ends carry a swing weight held by a spring against a rubber buffer. The swing weight pivots eccentrically on two levers and is returned by a spring. When the key motion stops, the swing weight keeps moving and straightens the toggle, carrying the type to the paper. Sponge rubber segments and rubber rings damp the rest position and impact.
