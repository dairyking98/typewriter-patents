## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A light, cheap, durable and fast machine is wanted, with many characters on few keys and typebars and visible writing.

**Solution:** A light compact machine with few parts; more characters are printed with a minimum of keys and typebars, and the writing is visible at once.
