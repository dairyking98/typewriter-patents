## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in certain novel features in the construction of the independent linelock-release key, andV in so far as such novel' features are concerned I do not wish to limit myself' to any particular form of line-lock, but wish it to be understood that they are applicable with any of the various forms of line-lock known in the art.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage reaching a predetermined point locks the keys with the line-lock in its normal rest position, and a release lets the escapement print a few letters past the lock point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier line-locks needed a separate movement of the line-lock to lock the keys at line end, which added force and parts.

**Solution:** The carriage reaching a predetermined point locks the keys with the line-lock in its normal rest position, and a release lets the escapement print a few letters past the lock point.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
