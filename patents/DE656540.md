**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (die setting machine actuated by typewriter keys) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for assembling matrices and space bands

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to die setting and line casting machines in which the setting device by pressing down bumpers using buttons one Typewriter or the like with the interposition of rocker arms and connecting them Tendons.

**How it works (reading):** Bumpers are hinged to rocker arms and spring-drawn into the gaps of a guide comb, so they rest on the comb and stay aligned with the keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Setting bumpers in a casting machine driven from a typewriter keyboard drifted out of position opposite the keys.

**Solution:** Bumpers are hinged to rocker arms and spring-drawn into the gaps of a guide comb, so they rest on the comb and stay aligned with the keys.
