> Description Translated from German Keyboard for calculating machines. The subject matter of the present invention forms a split key stick assembly for a detachable key board, in which the push of a button is supported by a suitable spring arrangement. It is already known in calculating machine construction that the keyboard can be removed to design and to make the key stem from two parts. Furthermore, one also has Keys that are pulled up by laterally arranged tension springs. By this lateral arrangement of the tension spring is the straight movement of the key stem adversely affected. In order to remedy this drawback, is according to the invention by a suitably mounted compression spring arrangement; the like pressing the button in the middle the key acts in the vertical direction, a particularly light key press is achieved.
