## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Setting a slip sheet to the base-sheet line needs a separate numbered sheet and a selection device.

**Solution:** A finger-hole endless tape drives the slip-sheet feed rollers by an amount set by hand, aligning the sheet with the chosen line.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
