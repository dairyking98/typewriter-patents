## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single ribbon wears in one narrow strip and its direction is awkward to change; cleaning the type levers needs the ribbon out of the way.

**Solution:** Ribbon driven from the carriage movement, with a guide that shifts a loop of ribbon sideways so each letter hits a fresh part of the ribbon, plus quick reversing and shifting aside.

**How it works:** A cam on the carriage shaft, turned by ratchets and pawls during the forward carriage movement, swings a lever that moves a ribbon guide plate lengthwise. The ribbon is tucked in a loop through slots so a rhomboid section moves transversely and lengthwise, and each type strikes a new point. The pawls on the spool ratchets are controlled by a cam shaft and can be reversed to change the ribbon direction. A rod with a hook lets the operator slide the plate away from the centre to clear the type levers for cleaning.
