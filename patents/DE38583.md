## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cheap simple writing machine is wanted in which letters are selected by hand and printed by one pressing lever that also advances the writing.

**Solution:** A type disc with types on a flange rim is turned by a handle and pointer to the chosen letter, and a pressure lever that swings on a toothed rack prints, steps along the rack and swings an ink pad against the types.

**How it works:** The frame of base plate and two side walls is cast in one piece, with a platen roller and a ratchet line-spacer. A toothed rod between the side walls can turn about its axis; a pressure lever with an eyelet sits on it. The type disc H has a downward flange carrying the types on its lower edge and is screwed to a toothed wheel, so discs with other types can be fitted. A handle with pointer J1 turns the disc to the wanted letter on a letter circle. Pressing the lever prints, and its pawls F and G step along the rack by one tooth; the same stroke swings a spring-returned ink pad against the types, which can be flipped away to re-ink it.
