## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper; Annealing glass products in a continuous way with horizontal displacement of the glass products of glass sheets

**How it works (reading):** The carbon sheets sit on a common carrier that slides on a subframe fixed to the carriage; a hand-operated pressing device clamps the paper webs and carbon sheets together so the machine's normal feed means carry both to the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reusing carbon sheets on paper webs needs unusual machine construction or complicated auxiliaries, and carbon leaves must be pulled back after each section is typed.

**Solution:** The carbon sheets sit on a common carrier that slides on a subframe fixed to the carriage; a hand-operated pressing device clamps the paper webs and carbon sheets together so the machine's normal feed means carry both to the platen.
