## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spoken words and speeches should be recorded on a paper strip as fast as they are pronounced.

**Solution:** The Klavigraph: a keyboard with letters grouped in six sector fields, where several keys pressed together swing hammers onto a clockwork-fed paper strip.

**How it works:** Keys work like piano mechanisms, and each key's pusher throws a gravity-returned type hammer, which passes an ink pad and prints on the strip in one of six fields. Pressing a group of keys together prints a whole word. A rail under all keys acts on the escapement of a clockwork that draws the paper strip from a reel by a roller. The escape wheel has two teeth stopped and released by an oscillating armature. The hammers pivot in notches of an oval frame ring around the ink pad.
