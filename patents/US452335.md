## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The description gives no stated problem in the opening; it concerns the form and arrangement of a whole typebar machine of unusual layout.

**Solution:** A flat-bed typewriter with a laterally moving carriage carrying a paper table that also reciprocates front to back, with a raisable bank of keys.

**How it works:** A horizontal bed on rubber feet carries a carriage on wheels in ways; a clock-spring lever arm and rod pull it right to left. Two racks, the second sliding slightly on the first with a spring and set-screw, are engaged alternately by a weighted dog on a rockshaft for the escapement. The top of the carriage is a sheet-steel paper table that slides endwise at right angles, stepped by a bell-crank pawl on teeth on its edge. A frame above carries the key bank, which can be raised and lowered, and the bracket-pivoted typebars.
