## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Decimal tab stops are set separately from the carriage release, which makes the mechanism more complicated.

**Solution:** The tab stops are pushed into the carriage path by a two-armed thumb on a shaft; setting the stop by a lever moves the thumb under the stop, and pressing the key lever disengages the carriage rack, so one control both sets the stop and releases the carriage.
