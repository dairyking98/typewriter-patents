## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Pulley and strand mechanism

**In the patent’s own words:** The objectof our invention is to provide a type-writing machine that will consist of few parts, so as to enable it to be manufactured and sold at a low cost and the liability of getting out of order diminished, and which atthe same time will be capable of performing firstclass work with all required speed.

**How it works (reading):** A type-wheel is rotated to each character by keys that cam a slotted plate, which drives a rack and pinion on the wheel shaft.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters were costly and prone to fail because of many parts.

**Solution:** A type-wheel is rotated to each character by keys that cam a slotted plate, which drives a rack and pinion on the wheel shaft.

**How it works:** A shaft carries the type-wheel at its front end and a pinion at the rear, and is journaled in a pivoted block and a vertical sliding plate. A horizontal rack engages the pinion and is attached to a plate that slides sideways. Each key passes through a slot in that plate with one side vertical and the other inclined, so depressing the key cams the plate sideways. The slot's incline differs per key, so each key turns the wheel by its own amount. Slots on opposite sides of the wheel incline oppositely so the wheel turns either way and never needs more than a half turn. A plate on arms connected to the key shaft then completes the action.
