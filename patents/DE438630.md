## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (plate embossing machine with character setting) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a plate embossing machine of the type in which initially the character to be embossed is set on a scale by means of a hand lever and then when the hand lever is pressed down, the stamp in question for the purpose of embossing is moved.

**How it works (reading):** Matrices and male molds sit in several rows on roller segments with convex faces toward each other; a hand lever on a scale plate sets the character by rotating the segments in opposite directions, and the segments are pressed together to emboss.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing plate embossing machines use a complex keyboard-based construction to bring the right character to the stamp.

**Solution:** Matrices and male molds sit in several rows on roller segments with convex faces toward each other; a hand lever on a scale plate sets the character by rotating the segments in opposite directions, and the segments are pressed together to emboss.
