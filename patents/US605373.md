## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shifting the platen for capitals needs the hands.

**Solution:** A foot treadle connected to the platen-shift lever lets the operator shift with the toe.

**How it works:** A treadle on an adjustable standard on the frame or desk connects by a rod with turnbuckle to the platen-shift lever so lifting the toe shifts the platen.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** treadle; standard; rod; turnbuckle; platen-shift lever
