## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must be braked when released by the tabulator.

**Solution:** A centrifugal brake pinion always meshed with the carriage gear, joined by a clutch operated by the denomination key.

**How it works:** A brake drum with pivoted weight arms spins on a pinion meshed with a carriage gear. A clutch between pinion and arms is engaged by the denomination key that also releases the carriage from the escapement, so the carriage speed is limited.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** brake drum; weighted arm; pinion; carriage gear; clutch; denomination key; column stop; rack
