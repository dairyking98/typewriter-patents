## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The space between words has to be made as a separate stroke after the last letter.

**Solution:** A slip ratchet bar and a flap with lugs, tripped by the space bar through a rock-shaft, let the carriage move an extra tooth while the last letter prints.

**How it works:** The fixed ratchet bar carries a slip ratchet held by set-screws in slots; both are engaged by the pawl on the vibrating rod worked by the key bars. Normally the slip bar advances one tooth until lug L hits flap lug K. The space bar on lever bars turns a rock-shaft whose finger lifts the flap, releasing the stop so the slip bar moves a second tooth with the last letter.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** fixed ratchet bar; slip ratchet bar; pawl; flap; lug; rock-shaft; space bar; vibrating rod; coiled spring

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph spacing: word space made together with the last letter
