## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with means for effecting line or character spacing in either direction

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type writing machines, and more particularly to improvements in mechanism for driving the carriages of type writing machines, and the object of my invention is to furnish a type writing machine the carriage of which may be driven in either direction.

**How it works (reading):** A reversible carriage drive using two alternately engaged idler gears between a spring-driven main gear and the carriage rack, with a double escapement.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriages only travel right to left, so they cannot write Hebrew and other right-to-left scripts.

**Solution:** A reversible carriage drive using two alternately engaged idler gears between a spring-driven main gear and the carriage rack, with a double escapement.

**How it works:** A coil spring turns a shaft carrying main gear F. Two loose gears H and I can each be shifted into mesh with F; I drives the carriage rack gear directly, H drives it through an extra intermediate gear, so the carriage direction reverses. A lever, links and forks on the gear hubs shift one gear in while the other goes out, worked from a reversing lever through a crank and rock-shaft. A double escapement with two racks lets the carriage step in either direction.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** carriage; rack; main gear; idler gear; intermediate gear; coil spring; shifting lever; fork; double escapement

**Improves on:**

- ordinary left-to-right carriage drive: allows right-to-left writing for Hebrew
