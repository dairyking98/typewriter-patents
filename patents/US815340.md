## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the invention being to render the type action of such machines (and especially t lat of so called front strike machines) responsive to an extremely-light stroke or touch on the finger-keys, and articularly at the beginning of the stroke;

**How it works (reading):** The type-bar actuating mechanism is arranged so the type responds to an extremely light stroke at the start of the key movement and strikes without blur; the details are cut off in the first 3000 characters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Front-strike type-actions respond poorly to a light touch, especially at the start of the stroke, and the type can blur or splotch the impression.

**Solution:** The type-bar actuating mechanism is arranged so the type responds to an extremely light stroke at the start of the key movement and strikes without blur; the details are cut off in the first 3000 characters.
