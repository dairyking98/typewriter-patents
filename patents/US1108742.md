## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Label printers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to overcome and avoid the aforementioned drawbacks met with in the use of type writers in which the type letters, figureso'r other signs or characters by means of which the impressions are made and ,whiehare arranged in rows, one below" the other on a plate.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing scripts with characters stacked of two to four parts, such as Javanese, needs paper repositioning after each part.

**Solution:** A type shuttle with rows of characters whose row-setting keys are tied to the backspace so stacked parts print at the same place.

**How it works:** A Hammond-style type shuttle with rows of characters is moved to the proper level by shift keys. Touching a key prints at, above or below the line, and the row-adjusting keys are connected to the backspace so the paper returns to the previous position automatically.

**Features:** [Type sector or segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md), [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** type shuttle; key; shift key; backspace key; platen
