## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a Japanese-language typewriter the selected type can fall out of its holder during the printing stroke, and the return stroke slams the parts back, so printing is inexact and noisy.

**Solution:** A bell crank with a slanting slot drives a roller that pushes a type support into the selected type to grip it, then swings the holder along an arc guide to the platen, with a sprung rocker and free roller cushioning the return.

**How it works:** The forked type holder hangs on a shaft and is kept level by a spring. Pressing the handle turns a bell crank; its slanting slot pushes a roller that moves a type support through a hole into a recess in the side of the type already brought into the holder's type hole by other linkage, gripping it. The same roller's free roller presses a rocker that swings an ink pad over the type face. Further travel lifts the holder, its guide roller rolling along an arc plate until a stop, where the type prints on the platen. On release the spring returns the holder; the free roller runs against the back of the spring-pulled rocker, easing the shock, and the roller reseats and withdraws the support from the type.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Printing mechanism in a Japanese-language typewriter. Nature and object of the invention.
