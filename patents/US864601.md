## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In power-operated machines, feeding movements of the carriage must be rapid and positive, but the carriage must be free of the feed mechanism except while feeding, and must not quiver, over-feed or rebound.

**Solution:** A feeding mechanism with engaging devices that grip the carriage only during the feeding movement and hold it under control so it can be stopped with precision.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
