## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ribbon feed on a Remington must be reversed by hand when a spool runs out.

**Solution:** Catch-bars on the ribbon spools trigger shifting levers that the carriage ears operate.

**How it works:** Loosely hung swinging frames hang below the spool shafts. U-shaped catch-bars pivoted in the spool heads lie against the spool while ribbon remains, and drop out when it is nearly unwound. Turning with the spool, the bar drives the swinging frame so its arm throws the pivoted point of a shifting lever into position. Ears on the carriage then strike that lever and move the shaft that alternately drives the spools, and a spring detent holds the shaft in either position. As the ribbon winds back it presses the bar flat again.
