## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switching device for typewriters or the like.

**How it works (reading):** A positive connection between the feed pawl and the locking roller lifts the roller at the start of line spacing and drops it back automatically at the end, while a friction brake lets the pawl lift out of the ratchet on the return so it travels back idle and the platen is never unsecured.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing devices make noise from the locking roller snapping into the ratchet teeth and the pawl jumping back over them, need a long lever stroke when the roller is lifted before the pawl engages, and may leave the platen unsecured for a moment.

**Solution:** A positive connection between the feed pawl and the locking roller lifts the roller at the start of line spacing and drops it back automatically at the end, while a friction brake lets the pawl lift out of the ratchet on the return so it travels back idle and the platen is never unsecured.

**How it works:** A pawl carrier lever turns about the platen axis and carries the line-space pawl, driven by a pull rod from the line-space lever. At the start of the stroke the pawl tooth drops into a gap of the line index wheel, and through a link and lever the locking roller is lifted out of the teeth, held by a form-fitting contact between two pawl surfaces. A friction leaf spring on the carrier delays its motion relative to the pawl in both directions. At the end of the feed the two mating surfaces slide apart, so the roller returns on its own to the wheel as the pawl is already holding. On the return the brake disengages the pawl from the wheel and it runs back empty to rest, and a resilient pawl surface gives way to restore the connection.
