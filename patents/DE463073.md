## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The motor-driven drive rail that moves the type lever also pushes a fork and angle lever that lift the ribbon fork into the printing position, with a spring for the return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon fork was lifted only by the writer's strength and dexterity, so the ribbon position depended on the operator.

**Solution:** The motor-driven drive rail that moves the type lever also pushes a fork and angle lever that lift the ribbon fork into the printing position, with a spring for the return.

Furthermore, despite this narrow ribbon, it is possible to use different To bring colors to the print in a safe way. A particularly simple training is achieved in that with the type lever drive rail is connected to a push rail, the fork of which formed free with legs offset from one another in horizontal planes The end acts on an angle lever acting on the ribbon fork.
