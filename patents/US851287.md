## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms; Pivoted

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the invention are to provide simple and efiective means for readily and securely connecting the type-bars to the supporting-bar, and which shall facilitate the travel of the interposed antifriction bearings and shall also afiord the advantages of permitting the type-bar to be removed from the supporting-bar without disturbing the companlon type-bars.

**How it works (reading):** Each type-bar is coupled to a segmental support bar that has V-shaped circumferential grooves on a common printing point, so the bars are located by the grooves and can be lifted out individually.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars must be attached to their support so that one can be removed without disturbing the others, and the connection should allow the pivot to travel easily.

**Solution:** Each type-bar is coupled to a segmental support bar that has V-shaped circumferential grooves on a common printing point, so the bars are located by the grooves and can be lifted out individually.
