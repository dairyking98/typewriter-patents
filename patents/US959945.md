## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbons need a simple, positive automatic reversal of feed direction when one spool is unwound.

**Solution:** When the unwinding spool releases a latch, a weighted lever falls past centre and shifts the driving shaft and engages a detent to swap which gear drives the spools.

**How it works:** Shown on a Remington-class machine. A power shaft is driven from the drum gear and can slide endwise to mesh one of two gear pairs with the ribbon spools. Loose shifters with teeth sit opposite the gears. When a spool runs low, a latch on that spool is released; a lever or weight is pushed past its centre of support and falls, a wedge compresses a spring that pushes a plunger into a tooth of the shifter and halts it, which shifts the shaft and reverses the feed. A variant has an arm in the ribbon's path struck by an obstruction near the ribbon end.
