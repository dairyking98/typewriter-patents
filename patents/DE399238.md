## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal driven only by gear tooth pressure stalls part-way when the meshing bevel gears are disengaging, so the switch is incomplete.

**Solution:** Store energy in a torsion spring between split spool-shaft halves and release it at reversal to snap the drive shaft fully into the other gear engagement.

**How it works:** A ribbon drive shaft swings on a lever to engage bevel gear pairs with one spool or the other. Each spool shaft is split in two halves joined by a torsion spring. When one spool runs empty, the ribbon tension trips a locking block and the full spool's shaft keeps winding, tensioning its spring. Tooth pressure starts the shift, and the stored spring force then throws the drive shaft completely into the other gear position.
