## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locks for subsidiary keys, e.g. for shift keys

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a trip-mechanism for controlling the use of the upper and lower case type in type-writing machines in which two cases are carried by one set of type-heads operated by one set of keys, and in which the change from lower to upper case is accomplished by the depression of a key (known as the upper case key) to throw the roll carriage to the rear.

**How it works (reading):** A trip that latches the carriage in upper-case position after a momentary key press and releases when the next letter key or space bar is struck.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On carriage-shift machines the upper-case key must be held down while the capital is struck.

**Solution:** A trip that latches the carriage in upper-case position after a momentary key press and releases when the next letter key or space bar is struck.

**How it works:** Depressing the case key rocks a shaft that throws a hanger forward and moves a slide-bar so its notch engages a detent on a guide bar, held by a leaf spring; this keeps the carriage shifted to the rear. Striking any letter key or the space bar operates the feed bar and universal bail, which release the detent so a coiled spring draws the slide back and the carriage returns to lower case. A lever allows the trip to be set so the carriage stays locked in upper case as a shift lock.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** upper-case key; rock shaft; bell-crank lever; carriage guide-bar; slide-bar; detent; swinging hanger; leaf spring; coiled spring; universal bail
