## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper drops down over the printing point, covering the line and obstructing the type bars; erasures are hard to make.

**Solution:** Detachable guides beyond the platen form a passage that holds the written paper above the printing line, with one side removable to expose the paper for erasures; an erasing shelf is added.
