## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforated-tape printing apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines

**How it works (reading):** Pneumatic devices, driven by valves reading a perforated tape, actuate the type carriers, which combine with an inking device and paper cylinder in one machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tape-controlled telegraph printers need a separate keyboard typewriter, which adds parts.

**Solution:** Pneumatic devices, driven by valves reading a perforated tape, actuate the type carriers, which combine with an inking device and paper cylinder in one machine.

**Features:** [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [USE:telegraph-printer](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)
