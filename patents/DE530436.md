**How it works (reading):** A grooved support arm on the lid with a pin on the box, with a bent groove and no fork, so the pin reliably seats.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Gravity-only cover supports for gramophone housings can enter the wrong groove at the fork.

**Solution:** A grooved support arm on the lid with a pin on the box, with a bent groove and no fork, so the pin reliably seats.
