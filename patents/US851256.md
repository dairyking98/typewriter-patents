## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Erasing or correcting tables

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide improved means to shield printing produced on paper by typewriting machines when erasure of the printed type is to be made, and to permit one or more letters or characters to be exposed through the shield by a simple adjustment of the latter according to the number of letters or characters desired to be erased.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When typed characters must be erased, the adjacent characters are exposed to the eraser unless they are shielded.

**Solution:** An adjustable shield of slidably joined thin members has registering slots, so the number of exposed characters can be set by moving the members together or apart.

**How it works:** Two members with corresponding slots slide relative to each other; the length of the opening they form sets how many characters show, and stops limit the movement.
