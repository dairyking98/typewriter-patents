## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type lever mechanism for typewriters with front Intermediate levers that are rotatably attached to a shoulder of the key lever and on their front end are swingably and slidably supported.

**How it works (reading):** The intermediate levers slide on a backward-curved bar of the key-lever guide comb, guided by upturned tongues, and springs hold them on the comb and return the key and type levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Intermediate levers in a type lever mechanism needed special bearing parts, which made their mounting and adjustment awkward.

**Solution:** The intermediate levers slide on a backward-curved bar of the key-lever guide comb, guided by upturned tongues, and springs hold them on the comb and return the key and type levers.
