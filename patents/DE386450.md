## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (loom harness lever alignment) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices for holding the shed open; Cam or other direct-acting shedding mechanisms, i.e. operating heald frames without intervening power-supplying devices operated by rotating cams

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such a device, but the alignment of the harness lever is done by a hand lever, which is usually rests in a detent and in this position the lever does not swing freely with special needs.

**How it works (reading):** A front-mounted hand lever with a spring clip latch can be swung out and locked past dead centre, so the operator can straighten the harness levers and bring all warp threads into one plane.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** After a thread break on an endless-ribbon loom, the harness levers must be straightened by hand, and the control lever does not swing freely from its detent.

**Solution:** A front-mounted hand lever with a spring clip latch can be swung out and locked past dead centre, so the operator can straighten the harness levers and bring all warp threads into one plane.
