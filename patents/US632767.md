## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon should move lengthwise and widthwise together so the whole width is used, without extra power sources.

**Solution:** The carriage spring wheel turns a slip-ratcheted double cam whose spiral camways raise and lower a rack that shifts the gear-toothed spool shaft lengthwise, while ratchet disks and pawls step the spools.

**How it works:** The spring-wheel shaft drives a loosely mounted cam cylinder through a slip ratchet, so it turns only while the carriage advances in printing. Two inclined spiral camways raise and lower a rack bar, which turns a pinion driving the gear-toothed spool shaft back and forth lengthwise, moving the ribbon across the print point. A disk with ratchet teeth on both faces on each spool shaft and alternately engaging swinging pawls steps the spool round for the lengthwise feed. One pair of pawls can be thrown out to unwind the spool.
