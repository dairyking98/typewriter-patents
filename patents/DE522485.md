## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device on typewriters for aligning the inserted sheets of paper The purpose of the present invention is to be. a facility to use on typewriters Alignment of the imported sheet of paper, which in a known manner with two line height indicators lying on the same line is equipped to be designed in such a way that an exact alignment of the inserted paper sheets both for one on the ruling as well as a line of text lying above the ruling is possible. For this purpose are in the device according to the invention expediently on the sides facing each other with one of the two line height indicators Graduated with the same height. so that the top of both line height indicators for aligning the sheets of paper for a line of text lying on the ruling and the edge of the gradation for aligning a line of text lying above the ruling can be used.
