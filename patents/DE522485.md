## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Sheets aligned by a line height indicator work only for text sitting on the ruling, not for text written above it.

**Solution:** A second line-height indicator is mounted level with the first and graded at the same height, giving two extra edges so sheets can be aligned for writing either on or above the ruling; the second indicator slides aside when not in use.
