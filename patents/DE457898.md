## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices

**How it works (reading):** The carriage is moved from a motor-driven bevel gear through a two-part cord pull, with a single spring in the cord that keeps it taut, cushions the start and handles the carriage return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Motor-driven carriage movements in typewriters and calculators caused bumps, vibration, jamming and dead gear in the coupling, hurting accuracy.

**Solution:** The carriage is moved from a motor-driven bevel gear through a two-part cord pull, with a single spring in the cord that keeps it taut, cushions the start and handles the carriage return.
