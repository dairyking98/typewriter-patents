## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to mechanism for vibrating the ribbon to cover and uncover the printing point in visible typewriting machines, and particularly to mechanism that can be adjusted to present different portions of the ribbon at the printing point for writing with various colors.

**How it works (reading):** Fulcrum the ribbon-vibrating lever on a support sliding on vertical rods and linked to the shift rail.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ribbon throw for different colours should be the same whether the platen is in lower or upper case.

**Solution:** Fulcrum the ribbon-vibrating lever on a support sliding on vertical rods and linked to the shift rail.

**How it works:** The vibrating lever is mounted on a slidable support linked to the platen shift rail, so it moves with the platen. Straight vertical slots on the actuator engage wrist pins on the lever, giving identical throw in both cases, and a stop prevents over-travel on the lower colour field.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier; vibrating lever; actuator; universal bar frame; shift rail; wrist pin

- US926050 (Cook): keeps ribbon throw constant across shift
