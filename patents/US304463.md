## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheehy wants uniform, distinct impressions that do not depend on the operator's touch force, with each character visible as soon as printed.

**Solution:** A constantly driven shaft revolves an annular set of type levers before the paper and a key stops it at the wanted type, which also releases a toothed wheel that actuates the print.

**How it works:** Individual type sit at the ends of an annular series of vertical levers and are carried round before the paper by a shaft driven by constant force. A bank of key levers arrests the shaft at the position of the chosen type. Arresting the shaft releases a toothed wheel that tends to turn but is normally held, and that wheel causes the arrested type to strike. Releasing the type afterwards advances the carriage. The paper roll sits on a rail-guided carriage with a rack and pinion, and a flexible shaft turns the platen roller for line feed.
