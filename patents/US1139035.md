## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed and ribbon vibration need separate mechanisms or extra driving power.

**Solution:** Use the pull of the carriage mainspring, through the roller-dog escapement, to reciprocate both the ribbon feed pawls and the ribbon vibrator.

**How it works:** In a visible-writing typebar machine with a ring of typebars, two stationary ribbon spools have ratchets and pawls on a rock shaft. The shaft is linked to the dog rocker frame of the escapement, so each key stroke feeds the ribbon step by step, winding it alternately onto each spool. A ribbon guide pivoted on a fixed arm is linked to the dog rocker to lift the ribbon to the print point and drop it away. The repulsive action of the mainspring through the escapement roller moves the ribbon away from the print point.
