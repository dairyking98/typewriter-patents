## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On Remington-style machines with two type characters per lever, the case shift has to be made by moving the paper roller laterally, and the existing arrangement of shift keys is awkward.

**Solution:** An improved key mechanism for laterally shifting the paper roller so its axis sits over the common point of the upper or lower case series, held there by a yielding mechanism.

**How it works:** Pivoted type-levers are arranged in a circle, each with more than one type face, so that all faces of one series converge on one common printing point and the other series on another. The paper roller sits in a carriage that moves step by step, and is mounted so it can also slide sideways by keys on the keyboard. A yielding holder keeps the roller axis over the chosen point; pressing the other shift key moves it momentarily over the other point. The text then describes the existing Remington's left and right shift keys and the improvement on them.
