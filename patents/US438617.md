## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor wants faster, more accurate, cheaper, smaller and more durable typewriting with various type styles.

**Solution:** A letter wheel with flexible radial arms carrying types is turned by geared key levers to bring the chosen type under a hammer that drives the arm onto the paper.

**How it works:** Pressing a letter key first lifts a ratchet out of a slot in a locating wheel, then engages a cogged arc on the key lever with a cogged wheel on a shaft. Gearing between the shaft wheels turns the letter wheel to bring the corresponding type under the hammer. Continued key pressure drives a link, levers and a hammer-lever that strike the back of the flexible arm and impress the type on the paper. A set-screw on a second beam limits the hammer sweep. Springs return the keys and the ratchet drops back to hold the wheel exactly in position; a ratchet also advances the carriage.
