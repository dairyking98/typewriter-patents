## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (addressing machine for wrappers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for directly duplicating from hectographic masters in mirror image, i.e. "wet duplicators" for producing positive copies

**How it works (reading):** An addressing machine that prints addresses onto a fed web of wrapper paper from a long run of addresses, with multiple ink colours for batches.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Newspaper wrappers need addresses printed fast enough to keep pace with the press.

**Solution:** An addressing machine that prints addresses onto a fed web of wrapper paper from a long run of addresses, with multiple ink colours for batches.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md)
