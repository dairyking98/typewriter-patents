## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon feed and reversing are driven by the carriage power, so the ribbon reverses automatically at the end of its travel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed needs automatic reversal at the right time without accidental actuation, and it should be quiet and cheap.

**Solution:** Ribbon feed and reversing are driven by the carriage power, so the ribbon reverses automatically at the end of its travel.

**Features:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
