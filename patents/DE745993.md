## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating or accounting machine carriage stops)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that the adjustment means by the paper carriage stops with a pull-in device driven by an adjusting member constantly moved by the drive motor be coupled.

**How it works (reading):** Adjustment means are pulled into the active position by a pull-in device driven by a constantly running adjusting member, triggered by the paper carriage stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-loaded motor and total keys are fatiguing and noisy when they snap into the active position.

**Solution:** Adjustment means are pulled into the active position by a pull-in device driven by a constantly running adjusting member, triggered by the paper carriage stops.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
