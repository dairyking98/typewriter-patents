## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**How it works (reading):** One handle-operated lever with two lugs lifts all ribbon drive pawls off the ratchet and is held there by the pawl springs.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hand-shifting the ribbon (to type without ribbon) needs several pawls released at once.

**Solution:** One handle-operated lever with two lugs lifts all ribbon drive pawls off the ratchet and is held there by the pawl springs.

**How it works:** Drive pawls pushed by a reciprocating lever turn the ribbon spool ratchet. A release lever on the same pin has two lugs. Turning it pushes one lug against a tab on the drive pawl and the other against the counter pawls, lifting them off the ratchet. The lugs are placed so spring torque holds the lever in the disengaged position.

**Parts:** ratchet; drive pawl; counter pawl; release lever; wire spring
