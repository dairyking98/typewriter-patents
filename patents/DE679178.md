## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms; Levers having moving or variable fulcra to alter the mechanical advantage during the stroke

**How it works (reading):** An eccentric, freely rotating pressure roller engages the type-lever head near the end of its path and presses it against the platen, so the stroke is cushioned and pressure is applied at the impression point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cam-disc pressure members fail to press the type at the exact point of impression, so full pressure is not used.

**Solution:** An eccentric, freely rotating pressure roller engages the type-lever head near the end of its path and presses it against the platen, so the stroke is cushioned and pressure is applied at the impression point.
