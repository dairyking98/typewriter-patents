## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Library guide cards and catalogue cards could not be printed to their very edge on a Caligraph because the scale plate stood back from the print point.

**Solution:** A card holder clamped on the front carriage rail presses the carriage scale against the platen so cards can be written to their edge.

**How it works:** Two curved spring arms joined by a cross plate are clamped by jaws and a screw onto the front rail of the carriage. The plate is shorter than the scale but as wide, and its forward edge lies just ahead of the scale's and close to the impression point. Its pressure forces the scale hard against the platen, trapping the card between them. The carriage is turned up vertically and down again as usual; the platen, feed roller springs and paper guides are those of the Caligraph.
