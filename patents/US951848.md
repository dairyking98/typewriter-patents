## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The finger force on the key is positively transferred to the type, causing too heavy an impression.

**Solution:** A type bar with a yielding outer end and an abutment spindle allows the impression to be limited rather than fully applied from the key.
