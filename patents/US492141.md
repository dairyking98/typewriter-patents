## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improving a typebar machine (Hartford/Pope) in many particulars: a detachable carriage, a better escapement, a ribbon that uses its full breadth, and easy paper and carriage handling.

**Solution:** A simplified typebar machine with a carriage detachable on ways below the platen, a vibrating-rack escapement, and one bar across the key levers that drives both letter spacing and ribbon vibration.

**How it works:** Key levers operate typebars arranged in a near-vertical semicircle at the rear, thrown forward and down to a common printing point above the platen. The carriage runs on ways below the platen and can be lifted off. A vibrating rack on the frame works with a fixed and a movable catch on the carriage to space the letters. A single bar across the key levers triggers both the escapement and the ribbon vibrator, which holds the ribbon above the print point and carries a pointer and scale. A paper pocket travelling with the carriage keeps the paper clear of the levers, and the pressure roll, carriage-to-rack connection and platen ratchet can each be released by hand.
