## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Small-key typewriters need a positive type-locating mechanism that does not deteriorate by wear, with simple parts and little carriage movement.

**Solution:** A five-key typewriter in a folding box whose keys, singly or in combination, shift a sectioned slotted locating bar to position the type before a printing device.

**How it works:** The box has L-shaped sections hinged so that closing it folds the feet up through bevel gears. The top section carries five keys, each with a roller in a channel between blocks. Each roller's pin runs through slots in two overlapping bar sections, one fixed with a vertical slot and one with a slot that has an inclined deviation. Depressing a key drives the pin down and, at the deviation, forces the movable section sideways by an amount equal to the offset, so combinations of keys add up to a locating position. The remaining text (printing device, carriage feed) is cut off.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** folding casing; key; roller; slotted locating bar section; spring; printing device; carriage

**Numbers:** five keys
