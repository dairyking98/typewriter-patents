## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machine needed improved paper holding, escapement, carriage release, line lock, bell, margin stop and ribbon handling.

**Solution:** A Smith Premier-style machine combines a ratchet-and-dog escapement, carriage release, carriage stop that locks the keys at line end, bell trip and an automatic ribbon feed that reverses itself.

**How it works:** The opening lists: paper holding on the platen, a step-by-step escapement using a ratchet wheel with ratchet dogs and a rack pinion that can be disengaged from the feed rack to release the carriage, and a combined carriage stop, line-lock actuator and bell trip adjustable along the line. A universal bar or sliding gate works with the escapement. The ribbon moves lengthwise and widthwise, a reversing hook engaging the feed pawl when the ribbon unwinds from a spool, and a device prevents winding the wrong way. Only the opening is available.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)

**Parts:** escapement ratchet wheel; ratchet dog; rack pinion; feed rack; carriage stop; line-lock actuator; bell trip; ribbon spool; reversing hook; feed pawl; universal bar
