## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Waiters' checks needed prices printed and an identical running record kept for each waiter.

**Solution:** A keyed check printer with a recorder carriage that brings each waiter's strip into position.

**How it works:** Keys and type bars print prices in their places on the check, and connecting means print an identical record on a strip kept for that waiter. A recorder carriage shifts the strip of any waiter into position so that his record accumulates.

**Features:** [USE:check-writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md)

**Parts:** key; type bar; check; recorder carriage; waiter strip
