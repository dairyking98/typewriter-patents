## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inner structure

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object being to form the felt or body of the pad (which supports or holds the ink) in such a manner as to enable more than one side or part of the felt to be used for type inking purposes, means being provided to enable the several different inking surfaces to be brought into co-action.

**How it works (reading):** A felt pad with a square or other multi-sided body is twisted or turned so that a fresh unused face is brought into contact with the type, giving several uses from one pad.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter inking pad gives only one inking surface, so the pad wears out quickly and needs frequent replacement.

**Solution:** A felt pad with a square or other multi-sided body is twisted or turned so that a fresh unused face is brought into contact with the type, giving several uses from one pad.

**How it works:** The pad body turns on its support to present a new face, and a handle indicates the ink colour and kind.

**Parts:** felt pad body; pad support or clip; tray; handle or knob
