## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates particularly to a novel means for locking the guide finger in elevated position and for tripping it for return to depressed condition as will hereinafter appear in detail.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoted guide finger across the print line is positively locked in raised position and tripped back to lowered position by the operator.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards need to be guided and held against the platen on a visible writer without being shaken loose.

**Solution:** A pivoted guide finger across the print line is positively locked in raised position and tripped back to lowered position by the operator.

**How it works:** The finger is raised across the printing line, locked against lateral strain, and released by a trip for return.
