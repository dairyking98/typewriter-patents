## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage backspacing needs a key-operated device that can be added to existing machines and prevents overthrow.

**Solution:** A key moves a back-spacing pawl into the escapement rack to backspace the carriage, and a dog engages a notched wheel on the spring barrel to limit overthrow.
