## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special purpose keyboards

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of my invention is to reduce the distance necessary, as above set forth, in machines of the class stated, between the type-bar circle and the series of key-levers, and thus to lessen the height or equivalent dimension of the machine, and, furthermore, to permit the type-bar circle to be contracted in its circumference or area, thus promoting compactness of structure.

**How it works (reading):** Auxiliary levers fulcrumed outside the type circle, pivoted to certain key-levers and linked to the nearby typebars, shorten the key-to-bar spacing; ribbon spool feed is also claimed.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keylevers laid out wider than the typebar circle force a large distance between circle and keys, making the machine tall and the circle large.

**Solution:** Auxiliary levers fulcrumed outside the type circle, pivoted to certain key-levers and linked to the nearby typebars, shorten the key-to-bar spacing; ribbon spool feed is also claimed.

**How it works:** Typebars sit radially in a circle with a holding ring, striking at the centre. Key-levers fulcrumed at the rear connect to bars by links. For bars near the fulcrums an auxiliary lever crosses and pivots to certain key-levers, and its link meets the typebar nearly at a right angle. Devices also feed the ribbon spools. Only the first 6000 characters were available.
