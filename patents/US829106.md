## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In long carriages the paper guides, guide-rollers and scale-bar flex outward away from the platen when paper is inserted.

**Solution:** Auxiliary supports and holding clips fixed between the ends of the carriage brace the guides, rollers and scale.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
