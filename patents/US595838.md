## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-selecting or type-actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in type-writing Inachines, having for its object, primarily, to materially simplify and cheapen the construction of the various parts thereof, whereby the device as a whole is rendered more perfect and complete in its details, capable of performing a greater amount of and better work with less liability to wear or to get out of order.
