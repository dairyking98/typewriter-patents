## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventor wants to simplify and cheapen a typebar typewriter and improve its ribbon feed, platen mounting, case shift and manifolding adjustment.

**Solution:** A set of improvements to a typebar machine: gravity-seated tilting platen cradle, a front guide rod that shifts the platen for case, a vertical platen adjustment for sheet count, and an automatic ribbon feed and reverse triggered by clasps on the ribbon.

**How it works:** The platen sits in an open cradle by its own weight; a lever raises a bail that tilts the paper table and cradle forward so the writing can be inspected, and gravity returns it. The carriage front guide rod is carried on pivoted uprights moved by a spring-detented lever or key lever (or a cam lever), swinging the platen between lower and upper case. A cam-slotted bar with handle raises or lowers the support of the guide rod to match the number of sheets. The ribbon spools turn one ratchet tooth per key stroke through a pawl on the universal bail; a clasp on the ribbon end lifts a lever into the path of the bail lever, which swaps the drive to the opposite spool and so reverses the feed. A manual lateral bail shift selects ribbon color, and the column bar is slid out and replaced by a spring bolt.
