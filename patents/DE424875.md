## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Aprons or guides for the printing section

**How it works (reading):** Lower paper guide and sheet holder are made of clear celluloid or glass so the writing stays visible up to the last line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Opaque metal paper guide and sheet holder hide the written line and cast shadows.

**Solution:** Lower paper guide and sheet holder are made of clear celluloid or glass so the writing stays visible up to the last line.
