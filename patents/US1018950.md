## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The toggle clutch that releases the platen from the line-space wheel is complex and costly.

**Solution:** An axially sliding head carries pivoted dogs that grip the drum of the line-space wheel and connect it to the platen, replacing the toggle.
