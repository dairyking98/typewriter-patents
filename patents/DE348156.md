## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-switch wheel is locked to the paper roller, so the roller cannot be set independently of the line-switching mechanism.

**Solution:** A ball-type clutch between line-switch wheel and paper roller is disengaged by a separate control organ on the hollow shaft, so the roller can be adjusted independently, and re-engages for line-by-line switching.
