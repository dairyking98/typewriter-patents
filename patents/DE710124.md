## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (double check printing unit)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing unit for cash registers or accounting machines for the production of double checks.

**How it works (reading):** The main-part type-wheel set and the separable-section set are arranged in mirror image on a shared sleeve system, so they sit compactly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Double check printing needed two type-wheel sets, and their arrangement took too much space side by side.

**Solution:** The main-part type-wheel set and the separable-section set are arranged in mirror image on a shared sleeve system, so they sit compactly.

**How it works:** Two type-wheel sets set by the same amount switch on a fixed shaft, one set mirrored to print the detachable section and the other the main part.
