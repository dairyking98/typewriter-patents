## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed with two or more sets of type or printing elements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters and Machines of a similar kind.

**How it works (reading):** Several keyboards sit side by side, each with its own writing position on the platen. A carriage with a slide rail is shifted axially along the platen to bring whichever keyboard is wanted to the writing point, and the carriage (rail, platen and frame) can be removed as one unit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter with only one keyboard cannot offer alternate character sets such as maths, chemical or foreign-alphabet symbols without changing the machine.

**Solution:** Several keyboards sit side by side, each with its own writing position on the platen. A carriage with a slide rail is shifted axially along the platen to bring whichever keyboard is wanted to the writing point, and the carriage (rail, platen and frame) can be removed as one unit.
