**How it works (reading):** The driving gear pairs of the reversing gear are mounted on one core shaft and each further counter's driven pair sits on sockets along that shaft.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reversing gears for several cross counters need separate bearings for their transmission, making the layout complicated.

**Solution:** The driving gear pairs of the reversing gear are mounted on one core shaft and each further counter's driven pair sits on sockets along that shaft.
