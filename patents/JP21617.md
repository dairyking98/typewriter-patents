## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A conventional typebar typewriter is bulky to carry; the inventor wants a light, simple, front-strike machine whose carriage section folds over the keys, with positive line-space stop, ribbon vibrator and type guide.

**Solution:** A typebar machine with three-character type heads (lower, capital, figure) in which the whole carriage bed, platen, escapement and ribbon mechanism are lifted by shift levers to three heights and fold back over the keys for transport.

**How it works:** Key levers throw pivoted typebars, each carrying a head with three characters, to the printing point; a universal bar under the keys works the feed dogs, ribbon ratchets and the ribbon vibrator. A mainspring in a drum pulls the carriage, and the fixed and movable dogs on a rack release it one tooth per stroke. Capital and figure shift levers lift sliders carrying the carriage bed, platen and mechanism to two raised stops, with a lock lever holding either position. The platen line-space lever has a pin and stop that locks the ratchet and sets single or double spacing. Ribbon spools are driven by worms and ratchets from the feed levers, and a slide on the feed bar sets the vibrator throw to choose ribbon colour or step across the ribbon width. A tapered slot guide and an indicator at the printing point align the types; a back-spacer tooth, carriage release, bell and line lock complete it.
