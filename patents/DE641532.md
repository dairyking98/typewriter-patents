## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Swing-out print hammer heads with pressure bars can only print parts of the type wheels for one receipt at a time.

**Solution:** A shared type-wheel set prints two control strips in turn, with each strip's unwind roller held by a friction disk.
