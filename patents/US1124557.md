## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Tabulation to columns and denominational positions needs selectable stops, and existing tabulating mechanisms are complex.

**Solution:** A tabulating and column-selecting mechanism with column stops set by a stop-setting key, a tooth-bar carrier and a release lever, with a selectable arrest at a chosen column position.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
