## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the carriage-stops or tappets which are employed in connection with the tabulating mechanism of type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulator carriage-stops or tappets are redesigned and mounted on their support so they resist twisting or displacement when they impact the dogs or detents of the tabulator mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops need to be simpler to make and rigid enough not to twist when struck by the tabulator dogs.

**Solution:** Tabulator carriage-stops or tappets are redesigned and mounted on their support so they resist twisting or displacement when they impact the dogs or detents of the tabulator mechanism.
