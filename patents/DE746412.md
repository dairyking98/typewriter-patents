## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (film band controlled business machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Checking presence, absence, correct position, or moving status of cards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a business machine for processing.

**How it works (reading):** A rotating body sensed by the film marks stores the identification markings as adjustable control elements, so the control effect occurs at a set time after the marks are read.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Film-tape-recorded business transactions need group identification sensed without stopping the film, which needs a sensing point that does not stall.

**Solution:** A rotating body sensed by the film marks stores the identification markings as adjustable control elements, so the control effect occurs at a set time after the marks are read.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** film tape; rotating body; control elements; sensing point
