## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key touch of front-strike typebar machines was tiring and not adjustable.

**Solution:** An adjustable touch device in the rear base lets the key action vary from stiff to elastic.

**How it works:** Horizontal second-order key levers run beneath the typebars and drive bell cranks that pull the typebars rearward against the platen front. Touch-adjusting devices at the rear lower part of the machine are moved to give a yielding or a resisting touch.
