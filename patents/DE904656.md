## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This purpose is achieved according to the invention in that the tension members are stepped in such a way Have lengths that the shortest tension members are assigned to the longest key levers, and vice versa, and that the articulation points of the tension members on the key levers of the same row of keys from the Key lever axis of rotation have essentially the same distances.

**How it works (reading):** The tension members linking key levers to intermediate levers are stepped in length, shortest for the longest key lever and vice versa, and their articulation points on each row sit at equal distances from the key-lever pivot, so every key has the same travel, acceleration and stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a multi-row keyboard the type lever drives differ between rows, so stop and acceleration conditions are not uniform across all type levers.

**Solution:** The tension members linking key levers to intermediate levers are stepped in length, shortest for the longest key lever and vice versa, and their articulation points on each row sit at equal distances from the key-lever pivot, so every key has the same travel, acceleration and stop.

**Parts:** stepped tension members; intermediate levers; key levers; common axis of rotation
