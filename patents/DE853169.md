## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** A single two-armed triangular lever carries both the locking roller and a braking surface at a set distance; a release lever shifts the force between them, so one position engages the roller and the other the brake. Springs supply the pressing force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen's locking roller (engaging the line index wheel) and its brake were operated alternately by a linkage of several levers and joints.

**Solution:** A single two-armed triangular lever carries both the locking roller and a braking surface at a set distance; a release lever shifts the force between them, so one position engages the roller and the other the brake. Springs supply the pressing force.
