## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On manuscript holders for typewriters, the clamp holding the copy and the line pointer must be moved separately when changing the original, which is cumbersome, so line pointers are not used.

**Solution:** Mount the line pointer on a slider on the clamping strip so it lifts and folds down with the clamp in one motion.

**How it works:** Two sheet-metal clamping strips are hinged along the left and lower edges of the reading desk and pressed to it by leaf springs; pressing the two handles together lifts both. The line pointer, a plain steel wire, hangs on a slider on the vertical strip, joined by a friction hinge that stays put but can be turned by hand to suit the original's thickness. Lifting the strip lifts the pointer with it, so nothing separate has to be moved. The slider is a steel magnet that clings to the iron strip in one version, and a spring clip around the strip in the other.
