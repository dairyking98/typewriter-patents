## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon movement in ordinary typewriters is driven from the carriage power, which is cumbersome and adds friction.

**Solution:** Drive the two ribbon spools by pawls on the letter-space vibratory frame, independent of the carriage power, with regulators to choose direction; and pair a fixed rack with a hinged rack for letter spacing.

**How it works:** Pressing and releasing any type key or space key vibrates a right-angled frame under the key levers. A driving pawl on a post at each side of the frame turns a ratchet wheel on the adjacent ribbon spool, and a holding pawl stops back-turning. Weighted regulators, linked by a connecting rod, drop one driving pawl into gear and lift the other out (via lugs on the holding pawl) so the ribbon winds in the chosen direction, reversed by moving the rod sideways. Letter spacing uses a holding pawl on the frame working in a rigid rack on the carriage and a second rack hinged on hangers that swings against the side of the rigid one, the carriage being drawn by a spring through a pendulum bar.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** vibratory frame; driving ratchet; holding ratchet; ribbon spool; spool ratchet wheel; weighted regulator; connecting rod; rigid rack; hinged rack; hanger; carriage; platen; pendulum bar; carriage spring

**Improves on:**

- ribbon drive from carriage power: ribbon moved by the key-driven vibratory frame instead
- letter-space ratchet with two sliding racks: one rack hinged to swing against a rigid rack
