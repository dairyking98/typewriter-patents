## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a workbench for sheet music typewriters with two slides that can be moved perpendicular to the staff lines, one of which is used for coarse and the other for fine adjustment of the sheet music, and its purpose is to create a workbench of this type which has increased accuracy allows fine adjustment.

**How it works (reading):** The fine adjustment device acts directly on the carriage that holds the sheet music, while coarse adjustment is done by a second slide in the same direction, so fine positioning is independent of the coarse setting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On note typewriters the fine adjustment of sheet music works only at detent positions of spring fingers in the grooved staff rulers, and the fine device on a separate slide loses setting through transmission play.

**Solution:** The fine adjustment device acts directly on the carriage that holds the sheet music, while coarse adjustment is done by a second slide in the same direction, so fine positioning is independent of the coarse setting.

**How it works:** Coarse slide moves by groove; the fine slide carrying the music is moved by its own adjustment device, so no transmission link lies between the fine device and the music.
