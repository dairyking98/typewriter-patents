In the known machines with double adjustability of the sheet music perpendicular to the note lines is the slide with a sheet of music equipped with resilient fingers, which snap into the corresponding grooves in the staff lines can. If you want to move this workpiece slide by one or more grooves, so the resilient finger must be lifted up and disengaged from the grooves whereupon the shift can be done by hand.
