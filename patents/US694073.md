## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple low cost typewriter is wanted.

**Solution:** Typebars with nine type in three groups and shifting fulcrum cams, with a three-position platen shift.

**How it works:** Each typebar carries nine type. A shift key positions the platen carriage for one of three groups, and a vibrating fulcrum cam moved by one of three keys projects the bar for the wanted type in the group.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** typebar; fulcrum cam; slide; platen carriage; shift key

**Numbers:** 9 type per bar; 3 groups of 3
