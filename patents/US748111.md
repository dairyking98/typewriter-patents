## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanisms that give the ribbon a crosswise shift as well as lengthwise feed (as in Diss US599417) were not positive or reliable enough.

**Solution:** A cam driven through worm gearing from the carriage-spring-drum ratchet shifts the ribbon spools sideways in steps so the whole ribbon width is used.

**How it works:** The spring-drum shaft carries a ratchet driven by a pawl only in the forward carriage direction, so ribbon motion stops on carriage return. A bevel gear on the ratchet drives a cross shaft with bevel gears that engage either spool shaft, selected by sliding the shaft with a handle. The cross shaft also drives a worm and worm-wheel train to a vertical shaft carrying a cam, half parabolic and half stepped. The cam moves a bell-crank lever that shifts the frame carrying the ribbon spools crosswise.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; ratchet wheel; bevel gear; worm; worm wheel; stepped cam; bell-crank lever; spool frame; spring drum

**Improves on:**

- US599417 (Diss): more positive and reliable crosswise ribbon shifting
