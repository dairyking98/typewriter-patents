## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** The ratchet wheel carrier and the switch blade rocker are each mounted on the frame through separate resilient connecting pieces, so the shock vibration is isolated from the frame and the stop itself stays hard and exact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hard carriage stops send impact noise through the machine frame, which acts as a sounding board, and soft buffers would hurt the accuracy of the carriage stop.

**Solution:** The ratchet wheel carrier and the switch blade rocker are each mounted on the frame through separate resilient connecting pieces, so the shock vibration is isolated from the frame and the stop itself stays hard and exact.
