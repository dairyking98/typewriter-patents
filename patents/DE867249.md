**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for back spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a downshift device for the car on typewriters where the pivot point of the downshift lever is on the bridge and the pivot point of the downshift pawl are arranged on the downshift lever in such a way;

**How it works (reading):** Pivots of the downshift lever and pawl are placed so the two lines through them form an obtuse angle, which sets how long the roller stays in the ratchet tooth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage downshift pawl must engage the ratchet for a controlled duration.

**Solution:** Pivots of the downshift lever and pawl are placed so the two lines through them form an obtuse angle, which sets how long the roller stays in the ratchet tooth.
