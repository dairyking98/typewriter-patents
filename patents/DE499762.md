## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (card punch combined with registering machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a machine with a card punching machine united typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable cam rail on the typewriter carriage, with steps on both sides, switches the punch selector monitoring circuits on and off by carriage position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter-to-card-punch linkage needs a manual switch to skip punching, which is cumbersome and error-prone.

**Solution:** A rotatable cam rail on the typewriter carriage, with steps on both sides, switches the punch selector monitoring circuits on and off by carriage position.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
