## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (plain-writing test for cross counter)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters in which the in a line written down in different columns and in special vertical or longitudinal counters Items are recorded in a so-called cross counter, which alternates with the various vertical units is coupled and must be written "clearly" at the end of the line.

**How it works (reading):** The last vertical counter has a gap and a special safety edge that intercepts the cross counter, so calculation stays blocked until the cross counter is written blank and the clear-character key is released; an asterisk clear character gives a visible check on the printed sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cross counter that is not cleared blank should block calculation, but the old block could be overridden without a proper clear.

**Solution:** The last vertical counter has a gap and a special safety edge that intercepts the cross counter, so calculation stays blocked until the cross counter is written blank and the clear-character key is released; an asterisk clear character gives a visible check on the printed sheet.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
