## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Special means, e.g. repulsers, for ensuring return of type- levers

**How it works (reading):** A wire recoil bracket behind the type-lever segment has looped, more flexible ends so the outermost levers meet a strongly resilient stop and snap back.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Outer type levers fall back less forcefully than central ones, so they linger on the platen and print unevenly.

**Solution:** A wire recoil bracket behind the type-lever segment has looped, more flexible ends so the outermost levers meet a strongly resilient stop and snap back.

For this purpose, the bracket is between its arched bridge and the branched off from it, for its attachment to the machine serving legs wound into loops. the outermost type lever thus meet a very strongly resilient abutment, so that they snap back with considerable force.
