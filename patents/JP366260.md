## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple typewriter with a swinging type shaft over a horizontal platen needs a sure, simple way to move the platen sideways one space per stroke.

**Solution:** A plate turning on the swinging type shaft is linked to a feed-pawl lever, so every swing of the shaft turns the plate and steps the platen rack one space.

**How it works:** A horizontal plate turns freely on the upright type shaft; one side is joined by two links to a lever swinging about a fixed upright shaft, and that lever carries a feed pawl. The pawl engages a rack fixed across under the platen. The other side of the plate has a guide slot on a fixed upright, so each forward-and-back swing of the type shaft turns the plate and moves the platen one type space in the same direction. An arm on the plate carries an ink roller that inks the type as the plate turns. The type case is round with several tiers and is raised and lowered by a sliding cam plate.
