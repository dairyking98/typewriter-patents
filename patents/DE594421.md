> Description Translated from German Multi-part bearings are used for the storage of cylinders in rotary printing machines known. These are designed in the usual way and are designed for quick replacement the cylinder, as required for the forme cylinder of rotogravure printing presses, is unsuitable or too cumbersome. Furthermore, in a bore of a housing housed multi-part bearings known, in which the bearing parts with easily removable, against the inside and outside "of the housing walls opposing rings are provided. After removing one ring, the upper bearing part can easily can be removed from the housing bore. The shaft carried by the bearing is now exposed and can easily be removed from the Bearings are taken out. To now such multi-part bearings good for the forme cylinder of a rotogravure printing machine to be able to use, in which often a forme cylinder must be quickly exchanged for another, is according to the Invention of the removable ring located on the inside of the bearing as one on the Bearing parts formed screw-on nut that carries a toothing on the outside.
