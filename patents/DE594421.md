## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (gravure forme cylinder bearing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Supports for bearings or supports for forme, offset, or impression cylinders; Auxiliary devices for exchanging forme cylinders

**How it works (reading):** The removable inner ring of the bearing is a screw-on toothed ring nut that a crank key inserted through a hole in the frame wall can loosen and tighten quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-part bearings for a gravure forme cylinder are too cumbersome for quick cylinder exchange.

**Solution:** The removable inner ring of the bearing is a screw-on toothed ring nut that a crank key inserted through a hole in the frame wall can loosen and tighten quickly.

**Features:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** bearing parts; ring nut; frame wall; key; slot
