## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tally strip mechanism on a typewriter platen needed improved feed and control with a divided platen.

**Solution:** A two-part platen has a supplementary tally section fed by clutch and ratchet mechanisms with a pawl shifting mechanism and locking out of the tally feed.

**How it works:** A main rubber-sheathed platen on a wood core is joined coaxially to a supplementary tally section. A ratchet wheel on the shaft is clutched by slotted clutch members released by rollers on a sliding shaft pressed by a spring. A shiftable pawl on the line-space slide chooses the main or tally feed, and the feed devices can be locked out. A tally delivery holder carries the strip. Built on a Remington No. 11 with traveling platen carriage.
