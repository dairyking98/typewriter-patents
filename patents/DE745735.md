## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device, in particular for typewriters, for automatic repetition of the step switching of the paper cart by means of a tab 5 or the like, whose swiveling rider bar by hand or automatically by the carriage release button or the tab key can be switched off, according to patent 661 498.

**How it works (reading):** A rack spanning the full carriage length, with one control piece per carriage step, can be made effective or ineffective from the keyboard, so the carriage can be stopped at any step; the rack is pivoted on the paper carriage and a pawl engages it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A fixed tab setting must be reset for each new form layout, and there is a need to skip any distance along the carriage without preset tabs.

**Solution:** A rack spanning the full carriage length, with one control piece per carriage step, can be made effective or ineffective from the keyboard, so the carriage can be stopped at any step; the rack is pivoted on the paper carriage and a pawl engages it.
