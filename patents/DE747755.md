## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (counter drive, calculating machines)

**How it works (reading):** The counter drive is positive by a coupling member with claws and a spring pawl, with no drive springs in the operating path.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Calculating-machine counters are driven by springs and a complicated button mechanism, which causes wear and reliability problems.

**Solution:** The counter drive is positive by a coupling member with claws and a spring pawl, with no drive springs in the operating path.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** coupling member; claws; spring pawl; counter setting rail
