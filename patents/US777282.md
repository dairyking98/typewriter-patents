## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing should throw the emptied spool out of drive and the other into drive instantaneously.

**Solution:** A dog on each spool drops into a toothed wheel when the ribbon runs out, and the resulting turning shifts a rack rod that moves an axial shaft with clutch wheels to swap the driven spool.

**How it works:** Each spool hub holds a pivoted dog kept raised by the ribbon; when the spool empties the dog's tooth drops into a toothed rotary device on the spool shaft. The rotary device's bevel gear turns a vertical shaft whose pinion drives a rack on a tubular slide rod. The rod pushes through spring arms the axially movable shaft carrying two toothed ratchet wheels with clutch faces. This clutches the wheel near the empty spool in and the full one out, so key-stroke pawls drive the other spool.
