## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (electric signal control) · **Subject (patent classes):** Hand-held casings the switches being fixed to the operator's hand, e.g. integrated in a glove or fixed to a ring; Hand-held casings

**How it works (reading):** Contacts are mounted in a glove or insulated finger cots, one on one fingertip and the mating contact on another fingertip or the palm, so that bending the fingers closes a circuit that operates horns or indicators.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Electrical signal switches are fixed to walls or cords, so the operator must keep a hand free to work them while driving or operating other controls.

**Solution:** Contacts are mounted in a glove or insulated finger cots, one on one fingertip and the mating contact on another fingertip or the palm, so that bending the fingers closes a circuit that operates horns or indicators.
