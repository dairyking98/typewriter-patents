## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets stacked on a table stick together, so edge aligners cannot square them easily.

**Solution:** The stacking table is resiliently mounted and struck or vibrated during alignment, loosening sheets from one another before edge aligners push them to stops.
