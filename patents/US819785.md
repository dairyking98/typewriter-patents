## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper-feeding devices of type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** The platen can be turned by the usual line-space mechanism or knobs, or by a crank whose back and forth limits are fixed mechanically, adjustable for different bill types.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon-copy billing needs the platen turned back and forward by a known amount for each bill, and setting that amount by eye from a scale is error-prone.

**Solution:** The platen can be turned by the usual line-space mechanism or knobs, or by a crank whose back and forth limits are fixed mechanically, adjustable for different bill types.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
