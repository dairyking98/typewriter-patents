## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sensing the print slip stops the engine or print drive, which is a poor and inflexible control.

**Solution:** The sensor changes the direction of movement of the plunger driven by a lifting disc, so the print is switched off or made at a different printing point.

In known devices of this type, this regulation takes place in the manner that by the action of the sensing device scanning the print slip, the engine or signal box of the machine, the drive of the printing unit or the printing stamp is blocked. According to the invention, the effect in question is achieved by that the direction of movement of the driven in the same way for each machine gear Pressure stamp changed by the action of the Füblvorichtung for the print slip will. This arrangement has the advantage over the known that the control process for the pressure is independent of the engine and control mechanism of the machine and the drive of the plunger and there is the possibility of not only switching off the print for yourself, but also - if several are arranged Type sets - to be made at a different printing point.
