In known devices of this type, this regulation takes place in the manner that by the action of the sensing device scanning the print slip, the engine or signal box of the machine, the drive of the printing unit or the printing stamp is blocked. According to the invention, the effect in question is achieved by that the direction of movement of the driven in the same way for each machine gear Pressure stamp changed by the action of the Füblvorichtung for the print slip will.
