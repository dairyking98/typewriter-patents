## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage feed of a typebar machine needs a simpler escapement that is driven directly by the type-levers and can be freed for hand movement.

**Solution:** A universal bar struck by every type-lever (and by the space key) engages a star-wheel through a rigid heel and a spring tooth to step the carriage one space per stroke.

**How it works:** Typelevers pivot on a curved rod concentric with the print point and strike a spring-held universal bar fulcrumed on a lug. Moving the bar toward the paper makes its spring portion drop below a star-wheel tooth and spring aside, while the rigid heel catches the tooth just passed. The bar then returns under its spring, and the carriage spring turns the star-wheel one tooth, so the carriage steps one space. A bell-crank space lever with a slotted connection also vibrates the bar. A bolt releases the space lever so the bar lifts clear of the star-wheel and the carriage can be moved freely by hand. A shifting frame carrying the carriage moves it to one of three positions for the three type series.
