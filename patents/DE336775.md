## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable stop member moved by the type keys controls how far the pawl of the locking part jumps, and the ratchet has a fine pitch so the pawl advances one, two, three or four pitches to match the width of each letter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper carriage advance is the same for every letter, so large and small letters are printed with distorted spacing, and existing uneven-advance controls are tied to particular machines and keys.

**Solution:** An adjustable stop member moved by the type keys controls how far the pawl of the locking part jumps, and the ratchet has a fine pitch so the pawl advances one, two, three or four pitches to match the width of each letter.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** adjustable stop pin; lever device; locking part with loose pawl; ratchet wheel with narrow pitch

**Numbers:** one to four tooth pitches for small, middle, large and wide letters
