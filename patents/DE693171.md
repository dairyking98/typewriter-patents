## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper; Carbon gloves

**How it works (reading):** A carbon paper holder made of paper strips that are longer than the carbon sheet and folded over its narrow sides like flaps, so the carbon is held without a cutting connection.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Loose carbon paper is wasteful and slow to handle, and earlier carbon holders were either not easily detachable or could not take the wear of carbon paper.

**Solution:** A carbon paper holder made of paper strips that are longer than the carbon sheet and folded over its narrow sides like flaps, so the carbon is held without a cutting connection.
