## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple machine is wanted to write on a flat sheet or surface, even along a circle.

**Solution:** A pivoted frame carrying a type wheel turned by a horizontally moved index pointer and pressed down by hand to print.

**How it works:** A type ring made by electrotype sits on a type wheel, with an index of characters on its face and a toothed register ring on the back. The wheel turns with its shaft, moved by an index pointer sliding horizontally. The wheel frame is pivoted to a slide; pressing it down prints and a spring lifts it. That down-and-up movement also works a pawl on a fixed rack that feeds the slide along the line and swings the ink-roller arm. The bed-plate runs on rollers for line changes and has a radius bar to print on circles.
