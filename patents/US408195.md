## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing type for presses and typewriters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Branding or tattooing devices for animals; Stamping-out using a press, e.g. of the ram type

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type for printing characters through the medium of ink,wh ether used in a printing-press, a type-writing machine,a handstamp, or otherwise.
