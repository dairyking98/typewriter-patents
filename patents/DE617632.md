## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper band reading and writing device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Movable-strip writing or reading apparatus

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a reading, writing and drawing device, in which the used paper in the form of a tape from a roller up and down another roller is wiggled up.

**How it works (reading):** Rollers are mounted on pivoting frames that lock at any angle to the base, with a cord drive and folding supports for use on a desk.
