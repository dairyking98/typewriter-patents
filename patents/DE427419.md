## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper roller clutches that rely on clamping wear or are unreliable.

**Solution:** A curved three-legged spring plate in a grooved disc is spread by a push pin in the roller knob to lock the line ratchet wheel to the platen.

**How it works:** A housing disc on the platen shaft has grooves for a three-legged resilient plate; the line index wheel turns on the housing. Turning the hand knob axially moves the push pin and flexes the plate so it spreads and sticks in the ratchet wheel. A stop in a slot of the knob sleeve limits the turn and an adjusting ring prevents crushing the plate. Turning back releases the plate by its own spring.
