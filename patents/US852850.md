## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** A pawl-carrying member, driven by the register resetting shaft, feeds the ribbon from one spool to the other, and the direction of feed changes automatically so the ribbon rewinds onto the first spool.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon must be fed from one spool onto a second spool and then rewound automatically, without manual reversal.

**Solution:** A pawl-carrying member, driven by the register resetting shaft, feeds the ribbon from one spool to the other, and the direction of feed changes automatically so the ribbon rewinds onto the first spool.
