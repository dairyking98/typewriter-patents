## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** A perforated threaded metal cylinder holds dye and the ribbon passes it tangentially, picking up dye by adhesion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon refresher with a paint roller inks the ribbon unevenly and pressure distorts the ribbon.

**Solution:** A perforated threaded metal cylinder holds dye and the ribbon passes it tangentially, picking up dye by adhesion.
