## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Impression devices (type or dies) are carried on shafts extending in both directions; each shaft is shifted lengthwise into its operative axial position before the impression is made, and selective devices let one chosen type or die act to the exclusion of the others.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A machine with a large number of impression devices needs to keep its size down while each impressor is brought into an operative axial position before printing, and a feeding movement of the material is needed. OCR is too damaged to confirm the full statement.

**Solution:** Impression devices (type or dies) are carried on shafts extending in both directions; each shaft is shifted lengthwise into its operative axial position before the impression is made, and selective devices let one chosen type or die act to the exclusion of the others.
