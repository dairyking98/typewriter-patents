## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (cylindrical ampoule) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carpules

**How it works (reading):** One or both ampoule stoppers get an inner cavity for the powder, sealed by an easily removed closure that is pushed out with a cannula; the cannula has side openings behind its cone tip.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Powdered medicament must be kept apart from the solvent in an ampoule and mixed in only when wanted, and earlier containers were awkward to empty.

**Solution:** One or both ampoule stoppers get an inner cavity for the powder, sealed by an easily removed closure that is pushed out with a cannula; the cannula has side openings behind its cone tip.
