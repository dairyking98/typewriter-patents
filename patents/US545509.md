## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Folio numbers cannot be typed in the margin without hand resetting of the carriage stop.

**Solution:** An attachment that automatically lifts the margin stop every set number of lines.

**How it works:** A curved stop blade on a shaft on the carriage limits carriage travel to the normal margin. A ratchet-driven arm on the carriage, advanced by the spacing lever, counts lines. After a preset count, five or ten, it shifts the stop so the carriage runs on into the margin, and a bell rings. The next spacing-lever operation restores the stop.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** stop blade; shaft; ratchet wheel; spacing lever; stop-operating arm; bell

**Numbers:** every 5 or 10 lines
