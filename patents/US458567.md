## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapements require extra key pressure and allow a letter to print in the space between words.

**Solution:** Two independent pawls on a rack segment are released alternately by the key levers so the space key feeds the platen on its downward stroke.

**How it works:** A spring-driven gear segment gears with the carriage rack and its lower arc has teeth. Two pawls on parallel shafts, normally in the same tooth, hold it; shafts have limited longitudinal play against spiral springs with set-screw stops. Key levers rotate a toothed wheel or wheels that release the pawls alternately so the carriage steps forward one tooth. A separate space key is included and a lock stops the key levers at line end.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** gear segment; rack segment; pawl; pawl shaft; spiral spring; toothed wheel; key lever; space key; set screw

**Numbers:** two pawls
