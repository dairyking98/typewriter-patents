## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (rotary printing cylinder elevator) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving, synchronising, or control gear for impression cylinders

**How it works (reading):** The cylinder has two pits with mirror-symmetric clamping rails and expansion shafts so one elastic blanket fits in either direction of rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing cylinders that run in either direction need elastic blanket clamping in each direction, which is slow and unclear to reset.

**Solution:** The cylinder has two pits with mirror-symmetric clamping rails and expansion shafts so one elastic blanket fits in either direction of rotation.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [IMP:anvil-backing](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
