## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to carriages of foldable front strike or visible typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Carriage and frame with a ball-bearing guide are elevated to different heights and fold over the keyboard.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Folding front-strike carriages need a rigid, guided support and need to be raised to different heights.

**Solution:** Carriage and frame with a ball-bearing guide are elevated to different heights and fold over the keyboard.
