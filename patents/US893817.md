## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A felt ink pad on which the typebar types rest gets exhausted unevenly, because some characters are used more than others, and the pad case is hard to remove.

**Solution:** A segmental pad case holds the ink pad and carries a follower plate pushed by several set screws, so different parts of the pad can be squeezed to force ink to the worn spots; the case lifts out loosely.

**How it works:** The typebar ends rest on a felt pad impregnated with ink, contained in a segmental trough-like case with a closed convex side so excess ink cannot drip into the machine. Between the case's front wall and the pad is a segmental follower plate. Five set screws through the wall, spaced along the case, press the follower against sections of the pad, compressing it locally and driving ink from the interior to the exhausted surface. The case rests by end tabs on the top plate and is located centrally by a bracket with a crotch, so lifting the typebars slightly lets it slip out.

**Features:** [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** ink pad; segmental pad case; follower plate; set screw; end tab; guide bracket; top plate; typebar

**Numbers:** five regulating screws
