## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** The pawl is attached to the platen swivel arm and disengaged from the switching gear when the platen is raised by two fixed pins: one in an elongated hole and one in a curved path that stops the pawl in both end positions. The platen pivot also serves as the guide pin.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen pawl set eccentrically takes too much space, and a pawl that stays always engaged prevents backward correction of the platen.

**Solution:** The pawl is attached to the platen swivel arm and disengaged from the switching gear when the platen is raised by two fixed pins: one in an elongated hole and one in a curved path that stops the pawl in both end positions. The platen pivot also serves as the guide pin.
