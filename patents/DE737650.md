## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving mechanisms, e.g. springs stressed during carriage return

**How it works (reading):** A separate unit with a spring motor and a single toothed clutch, with an electromagnetic stepping release, pulls the carriage back by cable and is fitted to existing paper carriages.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Remote-controlled printers need the carriage returned to its start quickly at each line end without losing transmission time, and existing spring-motor couplings use too many parts.

**Solution:** A separate unit with a spring motor and a single toothed clutch, with an electromagnetic stepping release, pulls the carriage back by cable and is fitted to existing paper carriages.
