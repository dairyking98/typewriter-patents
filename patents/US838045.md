## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be cut by hand after printing on machines with paper guides and feed rolls.

**Solution:** An angular cutter bar on the platen is attached to the paper-guide arms, so the guides and the knife move together and the paper is torn against the knife.
