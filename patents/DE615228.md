## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter, typewriter or similar machine, in which the platen with its paper guide devices by a shift key into the switching position and through a special device with the paper guide devices from the basis in a one-Z5 can be attached, according to patent 593 927.

**How it works (reading):** A lifting device with one stop controls additional paper guides so they move with the platen and hold the sheet while the platen is lifted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Collecting sheets lift off the lower guide when the platen is raised, and earlier fixes needed separate handles.

**Solution:** A lifting device with one stop controls additional paper guides so they move with the platen and hold the sheet while the platen is lifted.
