## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of the present invention is to simplify and: impro-ve the construction kand operationof the column-select ing or column-skipping devices, particularly with a view to rendering them more readily adaptable to diierent types of typev'vritingl machines.

**How it works (reading):** Column stops pivot on adjustable mounts along a rack on the carriage; column keys operate cams with graduated projections so that each key lifts a chosen number of stops out of use.
