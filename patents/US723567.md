## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing wheel machines are not described in the available opening; the aim is a mechanism giving results usually obtained by complex means with a type-wheel on a vertical shaft.

**Solution:** A multi-row type-wheel on a lifted, rotated shaft is raised by cord-and-pulley keys to select the row, rotated by a compound pulley train to select the character, and struck by an electromagnet.

**How it works:** The type-wheel (32 faces, three rows: capitals, lower case, figures) sits on a shaft in a frame that rises vertically on guides by a line from two shift keys, putting one row at the print level. A disc at constant height on the shaft turns the wheel via a non-elastic line drawn out through a chain of movable pulleys attached to keys, so combined key travels sum to the rotation, with a spring cord returning it. An electromagnet pulls an armature on a universal-jointed shaft so the wheel strikes the paper through a ribbon, the tailpiece sliding in a slot for accuracy.
