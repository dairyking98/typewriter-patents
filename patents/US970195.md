## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriters needed a clear, unobstructed view of the work, with carbon sheets and a book support, for writing in bound ledgers.

**Solution:** A flat-platen book typewriter with a suspended writing carriage that travels laterally on a longitudinally travelling carriage over a laterally moving book support.

**How it works:** Two spring-driven carriages with escape mechanisms: one travels laterally for letter spacing, carrying the writing mechanism, and rides on a second carriage that travels longitudinally for line spacing. The second carriage hangs from tracks above a flat platen so the whole sheet is visible. An underlying book support moves laterally and can be raised or lowered toward the writing mechanism. Carbon-carrying frames position carbon sheets over the platen and work holders confine the sheet. Each typebar has a pivoted type-head with several characters that can be shifted and held in several positions and returned to normal.
