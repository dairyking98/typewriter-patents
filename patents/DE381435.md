## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the special design of the adjustment device.

**How it works (reading):** A spring bolt in the crank engages a clamping ring that is pressed onto the hand knob, coupling it to the crank for setting the platen rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Form-spacing adjustment by a disc and stop was unsafe; hand knob and crank coupling had to be clutched during billing.

**Solution:** A spring bolt in the crank engages a clamping ring that is pressed onto the hand knob, coupling it to the crank for setting the platen rotation.
