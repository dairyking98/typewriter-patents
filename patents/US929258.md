## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**How it works (reading):** The carriage is released in letter-space direction just after the stepping dog leaves the escapement tooth, and an ordinary escapement can be converted into a reverse-feed escapement without changing its members.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary escapement feed does not release the carriage quickly after the type strikes, and the inventor wants a reverse-feed effect on the same escapement.

**Solution:** The carriage is released in letter-space direction just after the stepping dog leaves the escapement tooth, and an ordinary escapement can be converted into a reverse-feed escapement without changing its members.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
