## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A one-handed typewriter is wanted with an automatically moving carriage that carries the printing devices and keyboard.

**Solution:** A light disk-and-frame carriage carrying the keyboard and printing devices is moved across a stationary paper roller by a spring box with escapement, with a telescopic key-lever.

**How it works:** A frame of end pieces, a side rail and a loosely held rack bar carries a rubber-covered paper roller turned by a milled button with a holding ratchet and pawl, and spring-pressed small rollers on pivoted arms hold the paper and carry a graduated gage plate. The carriage has a disk-like front portion and a light metal frame with arms bearing the keyboard. A spring box with gear teeth on its periphery meshes with the rack bar to drive the carriage laterally, and a grooved traction wheel and adjustable stops limit its travel. Spacing keys at the keyboard edge or sides release the escapement. A hinged, telescopic key-lever (from an earlier patent of the inventor) reaches from the keyboard to the printing parts. Most of the printing mechanism lies beyond the opening text.

**Features:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)

**Parts:** end piece; side rail; rack bar; paper roller; milled button; holding ratchet; paper-holding roller; gage plate; carriage disk; keyboard; spring box; traction wheel; adjustable stop; spacing key; telescopic key-lever
