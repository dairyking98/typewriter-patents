## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-index spindle of a portable typewriter can slide out by accident, and it is hard to remove it for transport.

**Solution:** A locking mechanism holds the line-index spindle against accidental longitudinal movement, and the spindle and its bearing are shaped so it can be pulled out of the platen roller with tools when not in use.
