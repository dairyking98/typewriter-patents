> Description Translated from German Portable typewriter. The invention relates to collapsible or Portable typewriters with one that can be pushed from a position of use into a transport position Line index spindle, which at the same time influences a center lock for the paper carriage. The invention improves on these typewriters in that they have a locking mechanism provides, which secures the line index spindle against accidental longitudinal displacement, and that it forms the spindle and its storage in such a way that the former is without use can be easily pulled out of the paper roller by tools.
