## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing machine for address plates)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on an embossing machine for the address plates made of thin sheet metal from Address printing machines with a toggle lever embossing linkage.

**How it works (reading):** The embossing machine's toggle-lever linkage is reworked so the pawl blocking the type-head handwheel engages a tooth gap reliably.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Blocking pawl on the setting handwheel of an address embossing machine fails when the handwheel is not at rest or the toggle moves it, so the pawl hits a tooth tip and not a gap.

**Solution:** The embossing machine's toggle-lever linkage is reworked so the pawl blocking the type-head handwheel engages a tooth gap reliably.

- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [Element mounting on its hub](../features/elem-hub-keying.md)
