## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A decimal tabulator needs ten magnets and ten tab bars for a ten-digit column, which takes many parts and space.

**Solution:** Only one tab bar is used to stop the carriage at the column start; the tab key also switches electrical contact banks synchronised with the carriage, which step the carriage to the target decimal position and a test relay stops it.
