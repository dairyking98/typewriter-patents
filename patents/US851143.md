## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing in three or more colours or without ribbon, with simple operator control.

**Solution:** Two ribbons with separate vibrators, a winding mechanism connectable to either spool pair, and one finger-piece with an index plate.

**How it works:** Front ribbon and rear ribbon sit on interleaved spool pairs, each with a vibrator. A unipartite universal bar drives them with variable throw so different widthwise portions reach the print point. A winding mechanism with miter gears is shifted to either spool pair or an intermediate neutral position. One finger-piece controls both vibrator selection and winding, read against an index and colour plate.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** front ribbon; rear ribbon; vibrator; spool; universal bar; ratchet; pawl; miter gear; finger-piece; index plate

**Improves on:**

- Underwood two-ribbon machines: single finger-piece sets both vibration and winding
