## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stenographing machine needs to print letters and combinations of letters quickly from a single set of types, with the case changed by a pedal.

**Solution:** Key levers rise against striker rods, and each striker drives a hammer that presses the paper against a letter plate; a pedal rocks the plate about a pivot so one of several rows of letters comes above the hammer, and the paper passes under a fed ink ribbon.
