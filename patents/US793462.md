## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in cushion-keys for type writers and other keyoperated machines, and has for its object to provide a device of this character that will be simple in construction, easy of application, cheap to manufacture, and efficient in operation.

**How it works (reading):** A sleeve or cap open at both ends, with an internal transverse partition, is fitted over an ordinary key to act as a soft cushion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter keys are too soft and flexible, allowing lost motion, and earlier cushion-keys were costly or fitted only to the key stem.

**Solution:** A sleeve or cap open at both ends, with an internal transverse partition, is fitted over an ordinary key to act as a soft cushion.
