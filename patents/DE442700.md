## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (die embossing process and machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Machines or apparatus for embossing decorations or marks, e.g. embossing coins by pressing

**How it works (reading):** The upper and lower dies start at a mutual tilt, which is then gradually reduced while they move symmetrically, so the embossing starts from the sides, migrates to the middle and ends in vertical pressure at low force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Embossing in dies, as used for typewriter and calculator types, leaves the material displaced from the middle and needs high pressure for sharp results.

**Solution:** The upper and lower dies start at a mutual tilt, which is then gradually reduced while they move symmetrically, so the embossing starts from the sides, migrates to the middle and ends in vertical pressure at low force.
