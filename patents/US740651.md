## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing ina-- chines and the like in whicha printing-ribbon is shifted during the operation of the machine, so as to secure a uniform wear of the ribbon, and in which the direction of the ribbon-feed is periodically reversed.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon end-tabs trip movable guides that load a spring against a ball detent, which then snaps a sliding bevel-gear reversing shaft from one spool drive to the other.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal must act certainly and with little power so as not to strain the ribbon.

**Solution:** Ribbon end-tabs trip movable guides that load a spring against a ball detent, which then snaps a sliding bevel-gear reversing shaft from one spool drive to the other.

**How it works:** Each ribbon spool shaft carries a bevel wheel; a sliding, rotating reversing shaft carries two bevel pinions spaced so only one meshes at a time. Movable U-guides near each end are swung by projections near the ribbon ends as the ribbon winds off, pushing a collar that compresses a spring on the shaft. When spring force overcomes a spring-loaded ball seated in one of two grooves on the shaft, the shaft snaps across, reversing drive. A ratchet and crank allow manual setting.
