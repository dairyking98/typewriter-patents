## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper table of typewriting machines and comprises spring tension means holding the table in normal position.

**How it works (reading):** A leaf spring seats in a notch in a cam block on the paper-table rock shaft to hold the table in normal position against jarring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper table vibration causes noise and damage during operation.

**Solution:** A leaf spring seats in a notch in a cam block on the paper-table rock shaft to hold the table in normal position against jarring.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
