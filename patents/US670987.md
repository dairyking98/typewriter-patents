## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to ribbon mechanism for type-writers, its object being to provide a simplified means for driving the spools in order to cause the ribbon to be gradually transferred from one spool to the other while using the machine.

**How it works (reading):** A simplified drive for the spools that moves the ribbon from one spool to the other, automatic reversal of ribbon travel when one spool is empty, and a hinged ribbon shield that is raised by a lever to inspect the character.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must be transferred gradually from one spool to the other, and the operator must reverse it when a spool empties; the character just typed is hidden by the ribbon.

**Solution:** A simplified drive for the spools that moves the ribbon from one spool to the other, automatic reversal of ribbon travel when one spool is empty, and a hinged ribbon shield that is raised by a lever to inspect the character.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
