**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guides in the printing zone, e.g. guides for preventing contact of conveyed sheets with printhead

**How it works (reading):** A tally sheet is strung between carriers at the front and rear of the frame so it is printed by the moving writing mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typed records on a tally sheet need a manifolding attachment for a flat or book typewriter.

**Solution:** A tally sheet is strung between carriers at the front and rear of the frame so it is printed by the moving writing mechanism.
