## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing the pawl being normally in engagement with the ratchet

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in the features of construction, arrangements of parts, and combinations of devices to be hereinafter more fully described, and particularly pointed out in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** A firm but releasable engagement between the line-space ratchet and the platen, so the platen follows the ratchet exactly and can still be turned independently.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction connection between line-spacing mechanism and platen slips under violent actuation, giving unequal line spacing.

**Solution:** A firm but releasable engagement between the line-space ratchet and the platen, so the platen follows the ratchet exactly and can still be turned independently.

**How it works:** A firm but releasable engagement between the line-space ratchet and the platen, so the platen follows the ratchet exactly and can still be turned independently.
