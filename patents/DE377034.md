**How it works (reading):** A switchable segment carrier, moved by shift-key levers, brings the upper or lower case types into the printing position. Its upper part is adjustable toward the type stop to reset the strike point against the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The type lever bearing segment must be light and strong, and the type stop position needs adjusting as the platen wears and is reground.

**Solution:** A switchable segment carrier, moved by shift-key levers, brings the upper or lower case types into the printing position. Its upper part is adjustable toward the type stop to reset the strike point against the platen.
