## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to that class of typewriters in which the type are arranged on the periphery of revoluble type-wheels carried on a revoluble turret, and it is in the nature of an improvement on a typewriter belonging to the same class and described in my prior Letters Patent, No.

**How it works (reading):** A revolving turret carries several free type wheels which finger-key starters spin, to be stopped at the right letter, printing on a vertical cylinder carriage.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvement on Dodson's earlier turret type-wheel typewriter US299751.

**Solution:** A revolving turret carries several free type wheels which finger-key starters spin, to be stopped at the right letter, printing on a vertical cylinder carriage.

**How it works:** A foot-power shaft rotates a turret carrying type-wheels of hard rubber or metal at a distance from its centre. Each wheel has types on its rim, a blank sector, a starting arm and a notch for a detent. Finger-keys operate wheel-starters that begin each wheel revolving on its own axis; devices continue the rotation and stop it. The paper rides on a cylindrical carriage that stands vertically, moved by a feed-nut and plate, held with clips. Text is truncated before the stopping details.

**Features:** [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [ELEM:rubber-type](../features/elem-rubber-type.md), [ELEM:metal-type](../features/elem-metal-type.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** turret; type-wheel; starter arm; finger-key; driving shaft; tripod stand; foot-power apparatus; paper cylinder carriage; clip; feed-nut; feed-plate; detent
