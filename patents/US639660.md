## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Improves Burridge's earlier typebar machines in simplicity and compactness.

**Solution:** A compact bed-plate machine with typebars on ball-and-socket joints, a guided platen carriage, and an inking roller.

**How it works:** A base with skirt carries a track on which a platen carriage rides, held down by a bow spring that also adds friction against overrun. The platen has a ratchet and dog on a lever with a stop arm with two faces giving full or half line spacing. A rack bar with a feeding dog on an elbow lever spaces the carriage. Typebars are supported and actuated through ball-and-socket joints, a type-bar guide, and an inking roller.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** base; track; carriage; bow spring; platen; ratchet wheel; line-space lever; stop arm; rack bar; feeding dog; type-bar; ball-and-socket joint; type-bar guide; inking roller
