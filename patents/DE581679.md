## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter chair backrests were hard to adjust because they needed time-consuming screwing.

**Solution:** A tongue lever pivots and clamps a support bolt, so the backrest angle changes by pressing the tongue.
