**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of my invention is to provide improved' devices whereby the use of the ribbon may be dispensed with at will, whenever it is desired to write a stencil or-mimeograph sheet or for any other purpose.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon cannot be dispensed with at will when a stencil or spirit-master sheet must be typed.

**Solution:** The vibrator has two cross-pins for the operating lever, and a slidable frame moves the lever between them. The upper pin lifts the vibrator only partly, so the types strike the stencil directly, and a fuller lift presents the ribbon.

**How it works:** Connecting the operating lever to the upper cross-pin lifts the vibrator a little, leaving the ribbon clear of the print point, and the full throw brings the ribbon up.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** vibrator with two cross pins; operating lever; spring-pressed slidable frame
