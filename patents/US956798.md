## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms for ensuring maximum life of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to electrically actuated devices for operating typewriters and other similar machines, and more particularly to electrically operated carriage-return mechanism for typewriters and means for automatically line spacing the platen on the return movement of the carriage.

**How it works (reading):** A solenoid with a sliding core, air-cushioned by an adjustable air inlet, returns the carriage on a key touch and also line-spaces the platen automatically.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Time and strain are lost reaching for the carriage-return lever and turning the platen; earlier electric devices were complicated, bulky, noisy and stopped short or rebounded.

**Solution:** A solenoid with a sliding core, air-cushioned by an adjustable air inlet, returns the carriage on a key touch and also line-spaces the platen automatically.

**How it works:** Touching a key closes a circuit that energises a solenoid mounted at the rear of an Underwood typewriter. The core slides in a tube whose end plug has an air inlet screw and lock-nut regulating air admission and ejection, which slows the stroke so the carriage stops without shock. The pull is transmitted to the carriage and a coupled device turns the platen for a new line. It plugs into an ordinary light fixture.
