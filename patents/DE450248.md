## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A combined duplex adding machine and cash register needs the mark printed, the control strip advance and any strip cutting to match the chosen operating mode.

**Solution:** Two switch levers that select the operating mode also control, on one turn of the main shaft, the length of strip transport, strip cutting and the printed mark.

**How it works:** The two levers U and Z set the front (item) and rear (result) calculating units into different modes such as add, subtotal, total or clear. Their combined position selects single-line, three-line or six-line strip transport, whether the strip is cut, and which marking character is printed on the strip. The levers return automatically after totals. The opening text mostly lists the mode table.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** switch lever U; switch lever Z; front calculating unit; rear calculating unit; control strip; cutter; main drive shaft

**Numbers:** 1, 3 or 6 line strip transport
