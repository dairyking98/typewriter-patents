## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cheque stamping machines

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in devices for puncturing the value-marks for banking-checks, an object being to provide a device for this purpose that will be simple in its construction, having no parts liable to get out of order or break, and that may be conveniently carried in a persons pocket.

**How it works (reading):** A hand punch in which spring-yielding members, each carrying pins for one figure, are pressed down onto a depressed lower die so the chosen figure is punched into the check; a cap limits one member at a time.
