## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key setting for calculating machines)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key setting mechanism for calculating machines adjustable number of teeth according to patent 509 451.

**How it works (reading):** Springs in the key stem let the key overstroke slightly, so the locking lug seats below the locking rail and the adjustment is held by the springs, not by the finger.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rigid keys let the finger hit a hard stop, and the adjustment elements can be set imprecisely because there is no compliance in the key.

**Solution:** Springs in the key stem let the key overstroke slightly, so the locking lug seats below the locking rail and the adjustment is held by the springs, not by the finger.
