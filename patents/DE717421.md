## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**How it works (reading):** The rail lift member is placed next to the rear paper guide so the same hand that holds the sheet can operate it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding a sheet on a typewriter requires one hand to lift the paper-holding rail and the other to guide the sheet, so both hands are tied up.

**Solution:** The rail lift member is placed next to the rear paper guide so the same hand that holds the sheet can operate it.
