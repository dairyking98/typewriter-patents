## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Automatic forms-feeding machines must stop and reposition the continuous form at a fixed line count or total.

**Solution:** After a line count is scanned, a movement is switched on that advances the platen only over the unused portion of the form, so a new form starts at the correct position.
