## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** With a multicolour ribbon, the chosen colour section must be brought to the print point, and the narrow strip of ribbon being struck wears out quickly if it is not moved sideways.

**Solution:** A ribbon-carrying frame is shifted by a lever and cam to present a chosen colour section, and a ratchet-driven mechanism automatically feeds the ribbon laterally within that section so all of its width is used.

**How it works:** Applied to a Remington (No. 6) machine, the carriage drum's pawl turns a ratchet wheel as the carriage travels. A bevel gear and spider on that wheel drive a cross shaft, which winds the ribbon spools and, via the Webb automatic reversing, alternates direction. A shifting frame on supporting links, moved by a shifting lever and cam, brings the coloured section to the printing line. An added mechanism driven from the same ratchet moves the ribbon sideways within the chosen colour so impacts fall across every part of its width. The rest of the description is not shown.
