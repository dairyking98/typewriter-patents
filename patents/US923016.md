## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen release on existing machines is complicated and should be simpler and more effective.

**Solution:** A platen release uses a detent roller on a lever pressed by a spring against peripheral teeth of the platen ratchet wheel, with the line-space pawl engaging the crown teeth.
