## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper roll closure manufacture)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for manufacturing a closure for several simultaneously produced in a rotary cutter Paper rolls, in particular check or control strip rolls for cash registers, Booking machines, calculating machines or the like.

**How it works (reading):** Self-adhesive closure strips are applied to the ends of several simultaneously cut paper rolls by pressure rollers, one strip per roll.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper rolls must be closed one at a time by gluing, which is slow and needs a special wetting device.

**Solution:** Self-adhesive closure strips are applied to the ends of several simultaneously cut paper rolls by pressure rollers, one strip per roll.
