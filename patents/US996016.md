## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Remington ribbon drive shaft can lock up through operator carelessness, needing a skilled mechanic to free it.

**Solution:** A release lets the ribbon driving shaft's clutch disengage automatically when it would otherwise jam.

**How it works:** The existing ribbon mechanism has a driving shaft with clutch members and ribbon-reverser pin levers. The new parts automatically release a transmission member (the clutch) under the locking condition so the shaft is not left immovable. Little change to the existing mechanism is needed to fit it.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** driving shaft; clutch member; ribbon reverser pin lever; ribbon spool

**Improves on:**

- Remington ribbon mechanism: adds automatic release so the shaft cannot become jammed
