Of the or the pens are in the radial direction of the disc, above the paper writing surface, gradually moved so that the depressed pens on the paper form the characters give in spirals. For recording the Morse code on the paper writing surface one or more pens that sit in a guide can be used. Telephone lines are often used in position warfare and in other wars as well destroyed by the enemy after the advanced post. It enables the recording of the observations made on a sheet of paper in Morse code or agreed morse-like signs, allows easy putting on and taking off the paper disc and is set up as a pocket carrier.
