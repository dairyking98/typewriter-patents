The subject of the invention is a writing device which is used to produce messages in the shape recorded by Morse or Morse-like characters on a paper disk will. The disk carrying the paper is set in slow rotation. Of the or the pens are in the radial direction of the disc, above the paper writing surface, gradually moved so that the depressed pens on the paper form the characters give in spirals. For recording the Morse code on the paper writing surface one or more pens that sit in a guide can be used. Telephone lines are often used in position warfare and in other wars as well destroyed by the enemy after the advanced post. It is therefore important that the advanced posts can submit their reports to the competent authorities, without any oral transmission. The device according to the invention serves this purpose. It enables the recording of the observations made on a sheet of paper in Morse code or agreed morse-like signs, allows easy putting on and taking off the paper disc and is set up as a pocket carrier.
