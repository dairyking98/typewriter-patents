## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a trolley frame for writing office machines and aims to make it simple, cheap and accurate to manufacture is and has a great rigidity, whereby an exact location of the stop surface of the types on the roller in relation to the guide surfaces of the carriage and thus a good typeface is achieved.

**How it works (reading):** The rails and side walls are cast in one piece with the paper support and rear wall, and platen bearing brackets are adjustable by eccentric pivot screws so the platen sits exactly at the type stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage frames built from separate parts are costly to make and not stiff enough to keep the platen stop surface exactly placed against the type.

**Solution:** The rails and side walls are cast in one piece with the paper support and rear wall, and platen bearing brackets are adjustable by eccentric pivot screws so the platen sits exactly at the type stop.
