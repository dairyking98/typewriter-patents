## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage return should also turn the platen a line, or not, as desired.

**Solution:** A base lever turns the platen via a crank-lever pawl, then pulls the carriage back; a dog selects no platen turn.

**How it works:** An operating lever links to a crank-lever carrying a pawl on the platen ratchet; spring returns it. The platen turns until the crank-lever hits a stop, then further movement runs the carriage back. A sliding plate with finger piece engages a dog in notches of the carriage plate to stop the platen turning. A pivoted block shifts the stroke between one and two notches for line spacing.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** operating lever; link; crank-lever; pawl; ratchet wheel; stop; dog; notched plate; pull-rod; block; spring

**Numbers:** one or two ratchet notches
