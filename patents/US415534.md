## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in typewriters, and relates particularly to a device whereby the roller of the carriage may be rotated as the latter is moved back after a line or part of a line is written, or whereby the carriage may be moved back without rotating the roller.
