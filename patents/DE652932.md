## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing ribbon guide)

**How it works (reading):** A single ribbon is guided double at the printing point and single at a writing point beside it, on a removable rocking carrier; the ribbon is advanced by a toothed disc on the carrier that engages a leaf spring as the carrier rocks with each stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash registers need a handwritten note beside each printed entry on both the receipt strip and the control strip.

**Solution:** A single ribbon is guided double at the printing point and single at a writing point beside it, on a removable rocking carrier; the ribbon is advanced by a toothed disc on the carrier that engages a leaf spring as the carrier rocks with each stroke.
