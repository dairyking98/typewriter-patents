## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating stops are fixed in the carriage line direction, and their points of engagement must be adjustable.

**Solution:** A tabulator where stop members are fixed in position but their engagement points are adjusted, with a rack bar, double pawl on the carriage and a finger lever to set the tabulator.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
