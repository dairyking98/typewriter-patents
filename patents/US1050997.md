## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A ribbon carrier should lift the ribbon into the print zone and clear it after each impression so the writing is visible, and the lift must vary to select black or red on a two-colour ribbon.

**Solution:** A key-driven rocking yoke raises the ribbon guide, and a movable link on the yoke tail piece changes the lift height to bring the upper or lower ribbon colour to the print point.

**How it works:** The ribbon guide slides in vertical slideways and is rocked by a horizontally pivoted yoke whose arms engage both sides of the guide. A curved tail bar on the yoke is engaged by an adjustable link hanging from a transverse rod that every depressed finger key presses down. Each keystroke pulls the tail down, raising the guide, and a spring returns it on release. With the link at the free end of the tail the lift is small and the upper (black) half of the ribbon prints. Shifting the link nearer the fulcrum by a handle gives a bigger lift and the lower (red) half prints.
