## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Bearing pieces pressed or soldered into a metal tube make paper pressure rollers costly to produce.

**Solution:** A longitudinally slotted tube with stepped lugs and tabs holds plastic bearing bushes, with a rubber tube over it, so the parts assemble without extra machining.
