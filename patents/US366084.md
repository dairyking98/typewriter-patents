## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Forged or cast typebars are heavy, tiring to the fingers and expensive; the inventor wants them light, stiff and cheap.

**Solution:** Make the typebar from a tapered sheet-metal blank folded lengthwise into a U-trough, reinforced with brazed socket plates.

**How it works:** A tapering sheet-metal blank is folded into a U-shaped trough. A small socket plate is inserted in the upper groove and held by a pin through both sides and the plate, then brazed; the pin projects to form the pivot journals. A type-receiving plate is brazed into a groove at the lower end and perforated to take the type. Arms on the blank are perforated to take the pin for the connecting rod.
