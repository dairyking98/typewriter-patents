## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons must reverse automatically at spool ends, and spools and ribbon should be swapped without detaching the ribbon.

**Solution:** Automatic ribbon reversing mechanism on the spools, with spools and ribbon detachable together as a unit.

**How it works:** On a front-strike machine with a shifting type-segment, ribbon spools sit on the top plate and wind via a transverse shaft with a double-acting spring. Bell-crank levers on the spool flanges and sliding devices reverse the winding when the ribbon runs out. A ribbon-carrier throws the ribbon variably to cover and uncover the printing point, with an adjustment linked to the universal-bar frame. A buckle holds the ribbon end, and housings cover the spools. Detail beyond the opening is unseen.
