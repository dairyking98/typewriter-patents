## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Oriental-script typewriters that print both horizontally and vertically need the platen carriage to be stepped left for horizontal text and right between vertical lines; earlier machines carried two separate sideways-feed devices, which was complicated.

**Solution:** One limited-distance feed device serves both directions: a hand-swung handle for rightward feed is joined to or released from the feed device by a cam-edged engaging piece that also moves the gear's check pawl in or out of action.

**How it works:** A spring or power store pushes the platen carriage left; its rack meshes with a gear integral with a toothed limiting gear. A spring arm with a pawl tooth and a pressed slide with a catch tooth alternately hold and release the gear, so each key or actuating-lever press lets the carriage advance one set step, the arm's swing limited by a notched spacing plate. For vertical printing the oval cam-edged engaging piece on the swinging handle is turned so its U-slot captures the feed device's pin, locking handle and feed device together, and so its cam leaves the check pawl free to hold the gear against leftward turning. Swinging the handle then drives the gear rightward one step, limited by the spacing plate, and a stronger handle spring returns everything. For horizontal printing the cam holds the check pawl clear and the slot releases the feed device.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Platen sideways-feed device in a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a platen sideways-feed device in a typewriter, characterised as follows. Besides the leftward feed device of the sideways-feed gear, which meshes with the rack of the platen carriage, the carriage being loaded so as to be fed to the left automatically, a swinging handle for rightward feed is provided. The handle and the feed device are engaged and disengaged by an engaging piece on the handle whose edge is formed as a cam. For leftward feed, the engaging piece is turned to release the handle from the feed device, and its cam face puts the pawl that stops the gear from turning to the left into its idle position, so that leftward feed is possible. For rightward feed, the engaging piece locks the feed device and the handle together and puts the stop pawl into its working position, so that swinging the handle turns the gear to the right through the feed device. The object is a sideways-feed device in which one limited-distance feed device can feed in either direction, left or right. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show one embodiment.
