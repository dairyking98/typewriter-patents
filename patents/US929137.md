## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** A ratchet wheel on the platen shaft is engaged by a spring pawl pivoted on an arm, and a rod with a lateral arm connected by a link moves that arm to advance and retract the pawl.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The line spacing needs a mechanism operated by a lever on the platen carriage, and the inventor describes an improved line-spacing device.

**Solution:** A ratchet wheel on the platen shaft is engaged by a spring pawl pivoted on an arm, and a rod with a lateral arm connected by a link moves that arm to advance and retract the pawl.
