## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the invention are to obtain more perfect results as regards the straight guidance of the paper across and in close and firm contact with the platen, irrespective of the width of the paper or card, within the limits of the machine, or whether the same is a single sheet or is in manifold;

**How it works (reading):** Carriage-mounted feed devices give positive feed at both sides of the sheet, with a pawl-carrying lever and guard for line spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper does not track straight across the platen, the sheet next to the pressure in manifold work does not feed at the same speed as the top sheet, and line spacing needs improvement.

**Solution:** Carriage-mounted feed devices give positive feed at both sides of the sheet, with a pawl-carrying lever and guard for line spacing.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
