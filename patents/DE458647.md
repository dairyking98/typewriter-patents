## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The shift-lock and space-shift linkage was complicated and costly to make and to install in the machine.

**Solution:** The levers that lock the shift position and trigger the shift lock are placed on one common axis with the space-shift movement, simplifying the parts.
