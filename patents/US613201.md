## Summary

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter suited to printing on book pages.

**Solution:** A book typewriter with letter-carrying arms on a notched segment, lateral feed control, ribbon lateral feed, line-length stop and book supports.

**How it works:** The machine sits between two book supports with leaf-engaging plates. Letter arms swing on a notched segment and can print from a middle letter. Three special keys and a spacer control the lateral feed on a shaft, a worm shaft and ratchets feed the ribbon, and an adjustable stop sets line length.

**Features:** [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)

**Parts:** letter-carrying arm; notched segment; book support; leaf-engaging plate; lateral feed shaft; ratchet wheel; worm shaft; stop
