## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typewriters are driven by hand force; the inventor wants the machine actuated by electricity from the ordinary keyboard.

**Solution:** An electric typewriter where key contacts send battery current through branch circuits to vibrate a pivoted type-frame, fire a hammer on the selected type-plate stem and move the carriage.

**How it works:** A type-carrier frame with an arc plate of equidistant notches pivots between adjustable cone points on a post, so wear can be taken up. Key levers lift a bar that moves the type-case and plates so different rows (caps, signs or numbers) face the roller. One battery, a main circuit and branch circuits vibrate the type frame, a hammer strikes the stem of the proper type-plate, and the carriage moves a letter space; a spring barrel and ratchet drive the carriage and a ribbon movement is included. Only the first part of a long specification was available and the OCR is poor.
