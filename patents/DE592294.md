## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (reversible ribbon switching for address printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing mechanisms with inking or ink-ribbon devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an automatically reversible ribbon switching device for office printing machines, in particular for address printing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A pawl on a bracket on each spool axis, driven by a toothed disc on the spool, moves a toggle switch through a linkage that snaps over its dead centre with a spring, so ordinary smooth spools work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing sensors needed grooved spools, and the sensor had to press hard on the ribbon.

**Solution:** A pawl on a bracket on each spool axis, driven by a toothed disc on the spool, moves a toggle switch through a linkage that snaps over its dead centre with a spring, so ordinary smooth spools work.
