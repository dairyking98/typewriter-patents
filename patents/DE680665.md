## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a shorthand and Document holder for typewriters with one above the keyboard of the typewriter arranged, rotatably mounted drum with line pointer and a swiveling, pivotable attached resilient terminal strip to hold the concept in place.

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable drum above the keyboard holds the pad, which is turned over into the drum interior through a wall section on the lower edge, with a spring flap and adjustable stand.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The shorthand pad must be removed and reinserted under the terminal strip for every page.

**Solution:** A rotatable drum above the keyboard holds the pad, which is turned over into the drum interior through a wall section on the lower edge, with a spring flap and adjustable stand.
