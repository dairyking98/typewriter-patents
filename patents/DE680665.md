The known versions of this type have the shortcoming that the shorthand pad after one page has been copied out of the holder, turned the page and reinserted under the terminal strip to copy the next page got to. Compared to these known devices, the subject of the invention the advantage that the typist can operate, especially so far exhausting and time-consuming turning of the individual pages of the shorthand pad as well as the import and export of it after the copying has been completed, very important is facilitated in order to maintain and increase its performance. To achieve this, according to the invention, the terminal strip is on the lower edge an axially extending wall section of the drum, through which the Leaves a concept block held by the terminal strip into the inside of the drum can be turned over.
