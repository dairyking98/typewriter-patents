## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to lever type typewriters with visible writing of the genus in which the type levers and the intermediate levers in semicircular, radially slotted concentric to the pressure point of the types Segments are stored, and in which the motion transmission from the keys is conveyed to the type lever by tie rods and intermediate levers.

**How it works (reading):** Intermediate levers are carried on two common axes concentric with the printing point, so the effective lever length changes as the key is pressed and the type lever accelerates at impact and on the return swing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tie-rod transmissions that change lever ratio at several attachment points are entangled and add friction, while the type lever needs acceleration near the end of its throw and on its return.

**Solution:** Intermediate levers are carried on two common axes concentric with the printing point, so the effective lever length changes as the key is pressed and the type lever accelerates at impact and on the return swing.
