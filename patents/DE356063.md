> Description Translated from German The known ones at so-called American desks and typewriter tables used protective hoods made of two optionally foldable or retractable side walls and one connected to these Retractable roller shutters therefore do not offer reliable protection against theft of the typewriter because the Roller shutter from the side walls relatively easily and with little noise is possible. From the roller shutter that has been lifted off at one point then easily cut a number of sticks with a sharp knife so that the typewriter standing under the protective hood is easily accessible. The invention aims to make this impossible and consists in that on the inside the swinging side walls on the one hand and the roller shutter hook-shaped on the other Locking members are attached, which is in the closed position of the protective device so interlock or overlap that a stern apart or the roller shutter is prevented from lifting off the side walls.
