## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**How it works (reading):** Hook-shaped locking members on the swinging side walls and on the roller shutter interlock in the closed position, so the shutter cannot lift off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Roller-shutter typewriter covers can be lifted off by cutting the slats.

**Solution:** Hook-shaped locking members on the swinging side walls and on the roller shutter interlock in the closed position, so the shutter cannot lift off.
