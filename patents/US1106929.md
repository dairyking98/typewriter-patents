## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** When the operator forgets to return pressure feed rolls, the line-space stroke does not feed paper properly.

**Solution:** A line-space lever returns the feed rolls from the platen during its preliminary movement, before a rack-and-pinion drive rotates the platen.
