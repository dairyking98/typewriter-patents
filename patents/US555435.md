## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriters need carbon sets or a repeat to make copies.

**Solution:** A second, automatically fed ribbon runs between additional transcript sheets so each type blow prints two or three sheets at once.

**How it works:** Besides the usual ribbon, a supernumerary ribbon on spools set at a right angle passes through a twist into line with the work between the transcript sheets. The paper rests are guide bands on spring supports with separating bands between the sheets. A gear drive from the printing roller pays out the supernumerary ribbon, with a tension to stop free running. Each tap of the type prints the sheets by transfer from both ribbons.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** supernumerary ribbon; ribbon spool; gear drive; printing roller; drive pressure roller; guide band; separating band; spring support; rocking lever

**Numbers:** two or three sheets

**Improves on:**

- carbon-paper copying: second ribbon prints copies
