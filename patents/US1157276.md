## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper cutting and controlling devices for typewriting machines and its object is to provide new and improved devices of the character specified, the novel devices being especially useful in systematically cutting short strips or sections from a continuous roll or web although of course not restricted to such use.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoted cutting blade on the carriage is moved across the paper web to cut it; the paper is fed by the carriage's platen assembly and the blade is moved to partially cut the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Continuous paper rolls must be cut into short strips at a controlled point.

**Solution:** A pivoted cutting blade on the carriage is moved across the paper web to cut it; the paper is fed by the carriage's platen assembly and the blade is moved to partially cut the paper.
