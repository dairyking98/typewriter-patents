## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** An attachment fixed to the machine frame carries a small rotatable duplicator, backed with carbon material like a ribbon, in front of the platen and behind the ribbon support, so the type strikes radially on one side of it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets used for duplicating are messy and inconvenient to use with a typewriter.

**Solution:** An attachment fixed to the machine frame carries a small rotatable duplicator, backed with carbon material like a ribbon, in front of the platen and behind the ribbon support, so the type strikes radially on one side of it.
