## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an organlza'tlon providing for the ready removal of the platen and its shaft from the carriage frame;

**How it works (reading):** Locking arms pivot on the end plates and grip the enlarged hub of the platen shaft so the platen can be lifted out and replaced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen is hard to remove from the carriage for service.

**Solution:** Locking arms pivot on the end plates and grip the enlarged hub of the platen shaft so the platen can be lifted out and replaced.
