**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is shown in the drawing was shown in one embodiment.

**How it works (reading):** Pressure rollers sit in U-shaped levers with a spring between them, and a rod operated by a crank lifts the pressure rollers first, then the loosely resting paper guide plate, through interlocking lever pins.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The paper guide and pressure roller lifting mechanism is complicated and costly, and the paper guide must lift off the platen.

**Solution:** Pressure rollers sit in U-shaped levers with a spring between them, and a rod operated by a crank lifts the pressure rollers first, then the loosely resting paper guide plate, through interlocking lever pins.
