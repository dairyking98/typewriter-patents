## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key lever cash register)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key lever cash register for printing individual amounts, subtotals and grand totals for which the setting by printing individual amounts or submitting a subtotal or total by means of a manually movable actuator takes place.

**How it works (reading):** A linearly movable push frame stays in its middle position for adding and individual printing; at either outer limit it drives the total printing device so an intermediate or final total is printed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rotatable actuator that handles total printing works on cylindrical keypads but cannot be used on key-lever cash registers.

**Solution:** A linearly movable push frame stays in its middle position for adding and individual printing; at either outer limit it drives the total printing device so an intermediate or final total is printed.
