## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guides in the printing zone, e.g. guides for preventing contact of conveyed sheets with printhead

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in paper-guides for the carriages of type-wri ting machines by which the advance ed e of a sheet of paper when inserted into t e machine is guided around the platen from one feed or presser roller to the other.

**How it works (reading):** A holding device fixed to the carriage supports the central portion of the paper guide so it stays aligned with the presser rollers and platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a long carriage the paper guide cannot be made stiff enough, so its middle sags and shifts out of position against the feed rollers and platen.

**Solution:** A holding device fixed to the carriage supports the central portion of the paper guide so it stays aligned with the presser rollers and platen.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
