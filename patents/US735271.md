## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**How it works (reading):** Differently coloured inking bands are guided on a band-holder; a bent lever with claws, moved by a tension-rod, pushes one band out of the type path and holds it while the other is seized and brought into the striking position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says writing in several colours on one machine normally means unwinding and swapping the inking band on its reel.

**Solution:** Differently coloured inking bands are guided on a band-holder; a bent lever with claws, moved by a tension-rod, pushes one band out of the type path and holds it while the other is seized and brought into the striking position.

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
