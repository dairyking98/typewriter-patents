## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenographic writing machine paper rewinding) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for tensioning or winding the web; Multiple acting

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines and more particularly to rewinding mechanism for stencgraphic writing machines,-one object of the invention being to so construct the mechanism that the increasing size of the roll of paper being rewound shall be compensated for in such manner that the feeding of the paper for line spacing shall be maintained uniform.

**How it works (reading):** Rewinding is driven from the typewriter mechanism with a compensating clutch that adjusts for roll size.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The growing paper roll changes rewind speed, so line-spacing feed becomes uneven.

**Solution:** Rewinding is driven from the typewriter mechanism with a compensating clutch that adjusts for roll size.

**How it works:** Typewriter-driven rewind with a compensating clutch keeps line feed uniform as the roll grows.
