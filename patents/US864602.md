## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to produce an improved mechanism for locking the mechanism of the machine against operation alter the carriage has reached a predetermined point in its printing movement, such mechanism being particularly adapted for use in power machines.
