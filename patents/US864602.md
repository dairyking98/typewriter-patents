## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to produce an improved mechanism for locking the mechanism of the machine against operation alter the carriage has reached a predetermined point in its printing movement, such mechanism being particularly adapted for use in power machines.

**How it works (reading):** A mechanism that stops the carriage from rebounding as it completes its return after printing, and locks the machine against further operation once the carriage reaches a predetermined point in its printing movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the carriage is thrown back by power, it rebounds, so the next line does not start at the proper point and the margin is uneven.

**Solution:** A mechanism that stops the carriage from rebounding as it completes its return after printing, and locks the machine against further operation once the carriage reaches a predetermined point in its printing movement.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
