## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (complementary vertical counter with volatile switch)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a complementary vertical counter, in particular for typewriters or the like equipped with a sum train, with one the volatile one One switching device actuated by the movement of the paper carriage, with the the volatile one switching device in the presence of a value in positive Form in the negative number roll set after the exit of the complement counter the place of work and if available.

**How it works (reading):** A fixed facilitating device on the drive-wheel axle and cam that, once per full revolution, opens the side wall at the highest computing point so the overflow is handled without damaging the gear train.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The complement counter of patent 731705 could be damaged when its capacity was exceeded with a positive value in the negative number set.

**Solution:** A fixed facilitating device on the drive-wheel axle and cam that, once per full revolution, opens the side wall at the highest computing point so the overflow is handled without damaging the gear train.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
