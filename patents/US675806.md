**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines;

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typing a word letter by letter takes many key strokes, and the carriage must be moved one space at a time.

**Solution:** Frequent words are cut as whole blocks, several words can be carried on one block, and a block is moved into line so one key stroke prints the whole word, with an extra spacing device advancing the carriage by the word's width.
