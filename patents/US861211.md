## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Bringing a chosen band of a two-colour (polychrome) ribbon over the type normally needs complicated shifting gear; the inventors want a very simple, cheap attachment that an inexperienced operator can fit.

**Solution:** A hand shift lever pivoted on a post moves the ribbon guide, and a spring-loaded locking lever with a hardened wedge point snaps into a slotted clamp to hold the shifted position.

**How it works:** The ribbon guide sits on the bed plate on two pivoted links so it can slide sideways a limited distance. A bifurcated shift lever is clamped around a post and pushes the guide. A shorter locking lever is pivoted on the shift lever and spring-pressed away from it; its case-hardened wedge point enters a slot in a clamp snapped onto another post, holding the guide shifted. A set screw with a spring and knurled nut limits the lever travel, so the operator can set how much of each colour band is presented to the type.
