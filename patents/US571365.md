## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Documents need perforated marks at set positions to detect forgery.

**Solution:** A press with a rising plunger and eccentric disk drives a perforating device whose concentric rings are rotated to set the relative positions of the punches.

**Features:** [Type ring](../features/elem-ring.md), [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
