## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type selection in printing tabulating machines needs many separate movements for each group of characters.

**Solution:** A limited backward return from the default setting, within the spacing between two types of the same group, selects the letter, while card holes control digits.
