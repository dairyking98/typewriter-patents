## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, and particularly to that class of machines employing straight thrust type bars.

**How it works (reading):** An improved cam-based key actuating mechanism for straight thrust type bars that improves touch, analysed with construction, displacement and velocity diagrams.
