## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On the Hammond, the nut that retains the interchangeable type-wheels on the stem can fall into the machine and loses time when wheels are changed.

**Solution:** A captive inclined spring in an axial bore of the stem carries a head whose base overlaps the wheel boss, and is pushed aside with a finger to release the wheels.

**How it works:** The Hammond's non-rotating stem carries two type-wheels with nested sleeve bearings. A deep bore in the stem top holds a spring fixed at its lower end and set to lean sideways. On the spring is a knob head whose flanged base is no bigger than the stem end, and the lean holds that base over the wheel boss so the wheels cannot rise. Pressing the head to the opposite side clears the boss and the wheels slide off with one hand. The flange also covers the bore mouth against dust and gives an extended bearing on the boss.
