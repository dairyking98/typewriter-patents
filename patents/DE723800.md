## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A desk with a retractable typewriter leaves little room for the user's feet and limits machine size.

**Solution:** A front cover wall hinged to the table front is linked by levers so it folds below the support plate; it forms a roof-shaped enclosure when lowered.

**How it works:** Hinged front cover linked to a parallelogram guide folds below the support plate.
