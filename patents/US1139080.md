## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case shifting on a front-strike visible typewriter.

**Solution:** Shift the platen carriage by swinging its hinged supporting frame upward to select upper case, while keeping the escapement working the same.

**How it works:** A front-strike Underwood has typebars carrying lower and upper case types pivoted on a curved fulcrum rod. The platen carriage runs on ball bearings on a rail that is part of a frame hinged at the back of the machine. Swinging the frame up shifts the platen to upper-case position, and tilting it further exposes the carriage underside. The escapement dog-rocker arm pivots about the same axis as the hinge, so spacing works identically in both positions. A universal bar and ribbon vibrator link to the dog rocker.

**Features:**
