## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (feed and pre-plug device for printing business machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering; Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards; Printing or attaching on mailpieces

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to improve the line-by-line setting for Account sheets and the like, whose already printed line items are marked with line markings are.

**How it works (reading):** A control device linked to the perforated tape sets the feed to a chosen line, ejects the printed sheet, and raises or lowers a deflector plate. A detachable pre-plug unit with test levers adapts it to different sheet sizes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Perforated-tape feed programs cannot handle loose single sheets or envelopes, and line selection for loose sheets is unreliable.

**Solution:** A control device linked to the perforated tape sets the feed to a chosen line, ejects the printed sheet, and raises or lowers a deflector plate. A detachable pre-plug unit with test levers adapts it to different sheet sizes.
