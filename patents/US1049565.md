## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriage frames need a better, simpler construction.

**Solution:** A main carriage with a vertically slotted vibratory carriage holding the platen trunnions, detachably supported on angular end frames.

**How it works:** The carriage supporting frame has angular end pieces joined by upper and lower bars and is detachably secured to the main frame. The vibratory carriage carries the platen roll and slides in vertical slots in the main carriage end frames for case shift. A rearward-facing channel rail carries the shift trundle roll so dropped particles do not lodge. A spring and ratchet bar with lever arms keep the platen carrier up.
