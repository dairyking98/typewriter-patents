## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Music needs characters on different lines, which an ordinary typewriter fixed to one line cannot do.

**Solution:** A hand-held music typewriter with all parts on a handle: a type wheel turned by thumb and finger, an inking roller and a marking wheel for lines.

**How it works:** A type wheel with its sleeve sits on a spindle at the end of a handle. The thumb and forefinger turn the sleeve to bring the character to the lowest point while the other fingers hold the handle. A spring on an operating rod normally lifts the wheel off the surface, and pressing the handle down prints. An inker lever with a pivoting frame holds a roller against the type and is worked by a rod and collar. A separate marking wheel with its own inking roller draws straight and curved lines. A modified form mounts on a base.
