## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to paper- IO clamps for type-writing machines of that class known as book type-writing machines such, for instance, asmade by the Elliott & Hatch Book Typewriter Oompanyin which the paper to be printed upon is retained on a [5 flat platen.

**How it works (reading):** A clamp retains the free end of a loose sheet on the flat platen so it does not move when the carriage line-spaces, and it is adjustable along the platen length.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriters hold the leaf on a flat platen, but a loose sheet slips and must be inserted under the base frame to be held.

**Solution:** A clamp retains the free end of a loose sheet on the flat platen so it does not move when the carriage line-spaces, and it is adjustable along the platen length.
