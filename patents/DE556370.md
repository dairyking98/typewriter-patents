## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**How it works (reading):** The lift linkage stays in constant frictional or positive connection with the power drive, with an eccentric drive disengaged at dead centres, for slow noiseless platen movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Power platen lifting thrown the platen hard, causing noise and wear.

**Solution:** The lift linkage stays in constant frictional or positive connection with the power drive, with an eccentric drive disengaged at dead centres, for slow noiseless platen movement.
