## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters need cheap, light, low construction with minimal assembly cost and no rework.

**Solution:** Type lever, intermediate lever and key lever of each drive all pivot in one radial slot of a single conical-sector bearing body, so assembly errors cancel.

**How it works:** A pressed or cast cone-sector body has three concentric annular grooves holding three axles and radial slots cut across them. Each slot holds a typebar, an intermediate lever and a key lever. The key lever's trunnion slides in a slot of the intermediate lever, which meshes with the typebar via a toothing with enlarged gaps to give a light stroke and prevent rebound. Shift rail, key comb and springs are concentric about the cone axis through the impression point.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** bearing body; typebar; intermediate lever; key lever; trunnion; axle; return spring; shift rail
