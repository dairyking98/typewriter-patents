## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Circular letters need the date, name and address placed at the right spot on each sheet without relying on operator skill.

**Solution:** Means on the typewriter control paper position so each line is written at its proper place on the sheet.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
