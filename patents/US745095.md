## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide cooperating mechanisms for writing-mas chines, the several mechanisms being arranged to operate successively.

**How it works (reading):** Several cooperating mechanisms are arranged to operate in succession, so a light key touch starts the first and the rest follow in a train of movement; the text is cut off before the mechanism is detailed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The machine should need only a light touch of the key while still producing a clear, accurate impression.

**Solution:** Several cooperating mechanisms are arranged to operate in succession, so a light key touch starts the first and the rest follow in a train of movement; the text is cut off before the mechanism is detailed.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md)
- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** key element; successive mechanisms; carriage; escapement; platen
