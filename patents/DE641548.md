## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (multi paper web tabulating machine)

**How it works (reading):** A brake on the through-print web is controlled by the counter reset device through a cam and solenoid so it is released automatically at paper switching.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicate printing on a second paper web needs a manual switch when the form changes.

**Solution:** A brake on the through-print web is controlled by the counter reset device through a cam and solenoid so it is released automatically at paper switching.
