## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**How it works (reading):** Key levers pivot in a notched bearing and have rearward tails seated in its notches to keep them in alignment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Key levers of visible machines need a stable mounting that keeps them aligned.

**Solution:** Key levers pivot in a notched bearing and have rearward tails seated in its notches to keep them in alignment.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
