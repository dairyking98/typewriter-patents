## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing escapements lacked adjustability of dog angle and a total carriage release that also safely disables printing.

**Solution:** A pallet-anchor escapement whose dog angle is adjustable, with a total release key that frees the dogs, locks the printing action and engages a retaining dog when the carriage is removed.

**How it works:** An escapement dog and holding dog act on a circular rack tied to the spring-drum shaft that also pulls the carriage. The angle between the dogs can be varied to alter the escapement action. A total release key swings the holding dog out of the tooth path as the escapement dog lifts, letting the carriage run free, and locks the printing mechanism. If the carriage bed is removed a retaining dog drops onto the drum shaft rack teeth.

**Features:**

**Parts:** escapement dog; holding dog; circular rack; spring drum; total release key; retaining dog; carriage
