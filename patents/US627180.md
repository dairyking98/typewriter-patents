## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists need to find a point on the platen without relying on the scale, and to do tabulating work quickly, on either shifting or fixed carriage machines.

**Solution:** A spring-metal pointer pivoted in a base-plate that stands above the carriage and sights at the print point, and bends back out of the way when the carriage is lifted.

**How it works:** The base-plate with hollow bearing lugs and a retaining spring holds the pointer's lower end, which pivots and is kept vertical by the spring. The pointer rises, bends forward in a sight section that extends over the carriage close to the platen, and curves back at the top. The sight is adjusted to point directly above the type's contact line. It is fastened directly to the frame or via a hinge plate on the shift guide bar so that it stays in the same relation to the shifting platen. Raising the carriage presses on the sight and the flattened part bends the pointer back.

**Parts:** base-plate; bearing lug; retaining spring; pointer; sight; hinge plate; guide bar
