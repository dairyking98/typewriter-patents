## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen line-spacer should turn backward as easily as forward and allow the line spacing to be varied quickly.

**Solution:** A spring-arm holds the platen ratchet; a carriage sliding at right angles to the platen axis carries feed pawls with adjustable movement for forward or backward spacing.
