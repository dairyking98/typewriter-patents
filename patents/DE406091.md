## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switch device for line typewriters and consists especially in that, between the paper support which can be moved towards the set line and an intermediate gear is arranged on the line shift gear, which has a large movement of the ratchet wheel is converted into a small movement of the paper carrier.

**How it works (reading):** A worm reduction between the ratchet wheel and paper roller converts a large ratchet movement into a small paper-carrier movement, with an adjustable stop on the return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line advance must be a small paper move when the ratchet is large.

**Solution:** A worm reduction between the ratchet wheel and paper roller converts a large ratchet movement into a small paper-carrier movement, with an adjustable stop on the return.
