## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On typebar machines the line being written is hidden, typebars are hard to reach for cleaning, and the carriage oscillates, misaligning letters.

**Solution:** A visible-writing typebar machine with the carriage on two guide rails, a segmented rack feed, and ribbon rolls driven by the carriage spring.

**How it works:** Key bars strike a universal bar that rocks a shaft and drives a slide carrying a rigid pawl for a stationary rack bar and a pivoted pawl for a movable, adjustable rack bar, so spacing is fine-adjusted by thumb screws. The carriage runs on grooved rollers on a low and a high rear guide rail, with the impact roller held by guide roller, curved plate and pressure roll. A line-space lever with a pawl on the roller ratchet also trips the feed pawl, temporarily releasing the carriage from the feed. The ribbon rolls are turned by power from the carriage operating spring, with reversing, and the type guide and ribbon guide can be withdrawn to show all printed lines.
