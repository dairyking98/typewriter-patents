## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pivoted typebars work loose in their hangers from bearing wear and insecure hanger fastening, misaligning the writing.

**Solution:** Improved pivotal connection between each typebar and its hanger, and a better way of securing the hangers to the segment.
