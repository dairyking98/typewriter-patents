**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Positive-feed mechanisms automatically operated in response to carriage return

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writingmachines in which a paper-carrying roller is moved from right to left above an inked ribbon, while the types mounted upon levers operated by keys placed in banks produce the desired impressions upon the paper.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After each line the operator must lift the carriage, turn the roller one line and push the carriage back by hand.

**Solution:** A wound drum and ratchet mechanism on the machine side returns the carriage at line end and partially rotates the roller for line space automatically.

**How it works:** On a Caligraph the carriage is pulled by a spring rod and held by ratchet bars and a pawl. A vibrating lever on the spring rod is linked by connecting rods to a lever carrying a stop that works a ratchet wheel, pawl and winding drum at the machine side. At end of travel this gear returns the carriage to the start and a roller-turning device rotates the platen one line.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** carriage; spring rod; ratchet bar; pawl; vibrating lever; connecting rod; ratchet wheel; winding drum; roller-turning device
