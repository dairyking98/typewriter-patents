## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Positive-feed mechanisms automatically operated in response to carriage return

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writingmachines in which a paper-carrying roller is moved from right to left above an inked ribbon, while the types mounted upon levers operated by keys placed in banks produce the desired impressions upon the paper.
