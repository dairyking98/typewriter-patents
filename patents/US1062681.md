## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Where the carriage spring drives the ribbon feed, releasing the carriage still turns the ribbon gears.

**Solution:** A lever carrying one gear of the ribbon train rides on the releasable carriage rack so releasing the rack breaks the gear train.

**How it works:** The carriage spring drum drives the ribbon feeder through a gear train during letter feed. One gear sits on a lever bearing against the carriage rack support. When the release key or tabulator lifts the rack from the escapement pinion, the lever shifts the gear out of mesh, disconnecting the ribbon feed from the spring.

**Features:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** carriage spring drum; ribbon feed gear train; carriage rack; release key; tabulator stop frame; gear lever

**Improves on:**

- Young application 569,510: adds disconnection of ribbon feed on carriage release
