## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a type solder bridge for Oliver typewriters of all models, which allow types to be soldered immediately adjusted;

**How it works (reading):** A bridge clamped between the frames holds an adjustable slide with a recess for the middle character, so the type can be aligned and soldered on the typebar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Oliver type had to be aligned exactly when a type was soldered onto its lever.

**Solution:** A bridge clamped between the frames holds an adjustable slide with a recess for the middle character, so the type can be aligned and soldered on the typebar.
