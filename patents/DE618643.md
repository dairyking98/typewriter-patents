## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A power downshift drive cannot be fitted into a two-part frame that folds open for access, because the downshift cam lever sits in the lower part.

**Solution:** The cam disc and coupling pawl are mounted in the lower frame part and the downshift lever and key in the upper part, so the operating positions meet when the upper part is folded down.
