## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements to the Sholes and Glidden typewriter: ribbon motion, letter spacing, line spacing, bell, paper table and type-bar mounting.

**Solution:** A detailed specification of the early Remington typebar machine's details (spring-wheel drive, ribbon spools, letter-space ratchets, bell, line-space regulator).

**How it works:** A top plate on a stand carries a hinged paper carriage with a cylindrical platen running on a rail. A spring wheel drives the ribbon spools through gears, a vibratory frame and letter-space ratchets advance the carriage per key, a bell and hammer warn at line end, and a ratchet and trip regulate line spacing. Type-bars hang on hangers with guides around a disk basket. Only the opening of the description is available.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** top plate; paper carriage; platen; spring wheel; ribbon spool; vibratory frame; letter-space ratchet; bell; line-space ratchet; type-bar; type-bar hanger; paper table
