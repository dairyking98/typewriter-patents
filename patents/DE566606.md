## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In power typewriters, driving everything including the type lever is wasteful; the type lever needs little force while the carriage shift and ribbon lift offer the main resistance.

**Solution:** Strike the type lever by the typist's finger as usual, and power only the carriage step and ribbon lifter, triggered by a universal rail that the type lever moves at the end of its stroke.

**How it works:** As each type lever reaches the last part of its swing, a link with a bent end engages a universal rail and moves it. Through a link and angle lever the rail releases a stop lever, letting a cam ride with a continuously rotating shaft by friction. The cam turns an angle lever that pivots a drive lever, which first lifts the ribbon and then steps the carriage. The power drive releases at once regardless of how fast the key is let go, so parts return quickly.
