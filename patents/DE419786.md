## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register intermediate and final totals)

**How it works (reading):** A hand-lever setting makes the cash gear run two revolutions for a total: the first turns the adjusting organs to zero, and the second moves the zero-stop pawls out of the way so the adjusting elements take the current adder state.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cash register printing an intermediate or final total must reset its adjustment elements to zero and then set them to the adder's state, which needs two cycles.

**Solution:** A hand-lever setting makes the cash gear run two revolutions for a total: the first turns the adjusting organs to zero, and the second moves the zero-stop pawls out of the way so the adjusting elements take the current adder state.
