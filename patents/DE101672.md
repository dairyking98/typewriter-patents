## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Single-key type cylinder machines write invisibly and cannot make copies, because the cylinder sits too close to the paper roller or must strike with a weak stroke.

**Solution:** Hang the cylinder on a separate swinging frame, driven down by a carrier rail under the button, so the cylinder rests far from the platen yet strikes hard with a short button stroke.

**How it works:** A type cylinder with several rows sits on an axis through a hollow axle in an oscillating frame, with a pin letting it slide lengthwise while turning. A crown-wheel segment turned by the button rotates it, and an index cutting edge set in slots of an arc plate selects the character. A second frame of two tie rods and a cross rail hangs from the swinging frame under the button. Pressing the button drops the rail and swings the cylinder against the roller, while a correction wheel and a knife give the final exact rotary setting. A spring returns the frame and an elastic stop damps noise.
