## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operator cannot easily find the exact line position on the paper without moving the platen or carriage, especially when carbon sheets hide the writing.

**Solution:** An adjustable position scale lets the operator read the line position on the paper without displacing the roller or lifting the carriage, so several invoices can be carbon-copied at different heights on one record sheet.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
