## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**How it works (reading):** A round striking rod engages a semicircular groove as the key goes down, and it holds the drive engaged until it slides off, so the key needs to move only 2 to 3 mm for full force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type lever is only fully coupled to its mechanical drive after the key is pushed about 15 mm, so uneven key strikes give uneven type impressions.

**Solution:** A round striking rod engages a semicircular groove as the key goes down, and it holds the drive engaged until it slides off, so the key needs to move only 2 to 3 mm for full force.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Power-driven type action](../features/bar-power-action.md)
- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)

**Parts:** key lever; two-armed levers; striking rod; beater bar; type lever

**Numbers:** key travel 2 to 3 mm instead of about 15 mm
