## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to Paper guiding devices for typewriters, typewriters and the like, in which the lower paper guide means of a paper guide plate and paper feed rollers are formed.

**How it works (reading):** The connection between the paper guide plate and the feed rollers is releasable so the plate presses the sheet on the platen independently.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper guide plate and feed rollers sag and bulge the sheet in front of the platen during form advance.

**Solution:** The connection between the paper guide plate and the feed rollers is releasable so the plate presses the sheet on the platen independently.
