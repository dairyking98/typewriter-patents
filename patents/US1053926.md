## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in i provemenls in the construction of the operative member amlin the slip-drive connections of the said member, as well as in the means and manner of eon'ihining the said member and sli rdrivc connect-kins with each other.

**How it works (reading):** A slip-drive from the platen moves a rotary operative member; a sheet-controlled detent holds it while the sheet is in the machine and releases it at the bottom edge so an indicator shows the distance.
