## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** A smooth roller in a smooth stationary tube lifts paint from the container in either ribbon direction, and a light-spring wheel transfers it to the ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Prior paint applicators for typewriter ribbon only transfer ink when the ribbon moves in one direction, and spring-loaded transfer wheels press too hard.

**Solution:** A smooth roller in a smooth stationary tube lifts paint from the container in either ribbon direction, and a light-spring wheel transfers it to the ribbon.
