## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators do not notice that the ribbon has run off one spool.

**Solution:** A signal arm marked Reverse ribbon swings into view when the ribbon strains the spool shaft.

**How it works:** Ribbon spool shafts are geared to the carriage drive and reverse by a hidden switch. One shaft's pintle sits in a vertically slotted bearing and engages a notch in a pivoted signal arm. When the ribbon is fully unwound, strain lifts the shaft and arm, bringing a plate reading Reverse ribbon out from under the top plate.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; spool shaft; slotted bearing; signal arm; top plate; drive shaft; bevel gear
