## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (music notation writing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**How it works (reading):** Keys select and stop characters on levers or rotating type rollers while a handle-operated device moves the paper automatically up or along, so one motion prints a note with its spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Note-writing typewriters need one character per symbol or manual paper shifting, which wastes much time.

**Solution:** Keys select and stop characters on levers or rotating type rollers while a handle-operated device moves the paper automatically up or along, so one motion prints a note with its spacing.

**Features:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md)
