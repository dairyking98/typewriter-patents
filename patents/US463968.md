## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing entries into large bound record books such as deed registers.

**Solution:** A typewriter on a separate support, movable away from a table that flattens the book, with swinging type-arms, spring-arbor carriage and rack-and-pawl line feed.

**How it works:** The book rests on a movable table that brings it to a flat position. The typewriter is supported independently so it can be swung away to turn pages or read. Swinging type-arms are pivoted one above another and operated through angle pieces from the keys. The carriage is driven by a spring arbor with ratchet and catch that can be released, and the table and typewriter move to space and align the lines using a rack with several pawls of different length.
