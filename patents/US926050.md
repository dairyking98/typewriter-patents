## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Two colour keys on a cross arm swing the chosen colour field of a bichrome ribbon over the printing point, and a lever disables the ribbon vibrator.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two colours of ribbon must be selectable without changing the ribbon, for existing front-strike machines.

**Solution:** Two colour keys on a cross arm swing the chosen colour field of a bichrome ribbon over the printing point, and a lever disables the ribbon vibrator.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
