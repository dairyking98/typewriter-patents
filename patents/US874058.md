## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to attachments for typcwriting machines, commonly known as tahulators, whose purpose is to 'facilitate the listing of numbers, amounts, or other data, in proper order in vertical columns.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulator attachment with keys beneath the key levers drives a rack bar and pinion on a spindle, with escapement and stop mechanisms that position the carriage at set columns.
