## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be inserted under the guide without raising the carriage, and envelopes must lie against the platen at the strike point.

**Solution:** A spring sheet-metal guide bent around the rod that carries the paper guide, held by a bolt and thumb-nut, is adjusted so it presses stiff sheets to the rubber platen.

**How it works:** Spring guide on a rod, adjustable to press envelopes against the platen.
