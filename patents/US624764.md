## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing plate making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** Two opposed sets of movable dies press a line of type-like impressions into a plate that can be made to any width, producing the printing plate on the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Movable type for addressing machines is costly and needs skilled setting and adjustment.

**Solution:** Two opposed sets of movable dies press a line of type-like impressions into a plate that can be made to any width, producing the printing plate on the machine.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)
