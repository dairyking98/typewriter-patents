## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (dial mechanism for remote printers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using aggregate motion translation

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a dial mechanism for remote printers of the kind in which one type carrier under the influence of successive Signals is set.

**How it works (reading):** Two groups of control discs each drive a lever system: one shifts the type carrier axially and the other rotates it, so the selection needs at most 64 steps split across two shorter paths.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single-row type wheel positioned by a long rack of up to 64 steps relies on many joints with play, which causes unreliable setting.

**Solution:** Two groups of control discs each drive a lever system: one shifts the type carrier axially and the other rotates it, so the selection needs at most 64 steps split across two shorter paths.

**Features:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** type carrier; control discs; lever systems; rack; drive motor

**Numbers:** up to 64 positions; maximum 64 steps split over two paths
