## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A bar typewriter's ribbon needed to feed and reverse automatically with no manual handling, and spools had to be easy to place and remove.

**Solution:** A ribbon carrier rocked by the universal bail, with a gravity pawl on each spool and a device that lifts one pawl out of mesh while the other falls in, to reverse the feed automatically.

**How it works:** Key-levers act under a universal bail on a rock-shaft; an arm from the shaft is adjustably screwed to the ribbon carrier so it lifts different parts of the ribbon to the type. The ribbon runs from one spool over turning-bars on the carrier arms to the other spool. Each carrier arm bears a heavy-ended pawl which advances the spool ratchet step by step; a wire lifts one pawl clear while the other falls by gravity into its ratchet, reversing the feed at the end of the ribbon. A spring on the pawl can make the feed positive. A pivoted tension-bar with an adjusting screw keeps the ribbon taut, and a spring wire in the spindle groove holds the spool on but lets it come off easily.
