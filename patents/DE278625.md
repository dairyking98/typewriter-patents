## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Triggers for print hammers drag and fire under friction or vibration when zeros should not print.

**Solution:** Release rails are relieved of spring load and brought in only when printing; a toggle joint and pendulum rails set the triggers.

**How it works:** Release rails and toggle joint move the print triggers only when the work process requires printing.
