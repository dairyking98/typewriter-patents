## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal had to be done by hand or by crude devices when a spool emptied.

**Solution:** Levers resting on the ribbon in each spool trip a latch so the shaft shifts and engages the other spool's miter gear automatically.

**How it works:** A transverse shaft drives both ribbon spools through miter gears; a grooved collar with a cam face sits at each end. A ribbon-controlled lever rests on the ribbon and falls as the ribbon pays off, releasing a latch and spring-tensioned double-armed lever. The freed lever's roller drops onto the cam, which throws the shaft sideways to mesh the other gear and reverse the winding.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; miter gear; transverse shaft; grooved collar; cam; ribbon-controlled lever; latch; spring
