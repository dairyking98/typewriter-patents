## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**How it works (reading):** The platen is built with a light core filled with a mixture of ground pulp and a heat-softening binder, poured in hot and capped, under a hard rubber sheath.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen roller should run quietly, but a solid gelatin and glycerine core is too heavy.

**Solution:** The platen is built with a light core filled with a mixture of ground pulp and a heat-softening binder, poured in hot and capped, under a hard rubber sheath.
