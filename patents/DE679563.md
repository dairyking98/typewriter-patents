## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Registration strip in cash registers loses tension between the strip supply and the take-up roll when the strip is advanced early or late.

**Solution:** A strip-feed device that advances the strip before registration, with a tensioning arrangement, keeps the strip firm between registrations.
