## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates principally to typewriting machines and more particularly to the ribbon mechanism of such machines, but some of the features of my invention are applicable also to printing machines other than typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon vibrator associated with a type guide, with an adjustment so the ribbon vibrates to different fields of the ink ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ink ribbon mechanism of a front-strike typewriter needs improvement, and the ribbon should be usable across different fields.

**Solution:** A ribbon vibrator associated with a type guide, with an adjustment so the ribbon vibrates to different fields of the ink ribbon.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
