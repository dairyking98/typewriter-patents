## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing in several colours with Underwood machines using separate independent ribbons.

**Solution:** Two ribbons, one in front of the other, each with its own vibrator and lever; shiftable actuators on the universal-bar frame engage one lever or neither.

**How it works:** The usual ribbon is on spools with vertical axes; behind it a short red ribbon (or endless ribbon on four spools) is wound by extra spools. Each ribbon has its own vibrator, one in front of the other, moved by side-by-side levers. Actuators on the reciprocating universal-bar frame are shifted to engage either lever and are detained in each position, or placed so neither works for stencils.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** front ribbon; rear ribbon; spool; vibrator; lever; actuator; universal bar frame; ratchet wheel; pawl

**Improves on:**

- Underwood single-ribbon front-strike machine: adds a second independent ribbon with shiftable actuators
