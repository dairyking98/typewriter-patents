## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter calculator improvement)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement in typewriter protected under patent 437 283.

**How it works (reading):** Pins on a laterally moving slide enter zero-position holes in the number rollers, and a pawl locks the slide return unless every counter is at zero.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A computing slide could return to its start while the cross counter was not zero, carrying a residual amount into the next bill.

**Solution:** Pins on a laterally moving slide enter zero-position holes in the number rollers, and a pawl locks the slide return unless every counter is at zero.
