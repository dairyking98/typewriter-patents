## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machines for visible writing on a flat platen need interchangeable type bars to print in different languages or type styles, and a quick change between upper and lower case.

**Solution:** A visible-writing typewriter with a flat plate platen, a rail-carried printing carrier, and type bars that can be interchanged; the change from upper to lower case is made readily.

**How it works:** A flat platen plate slides on ways in a base plate for line spacing. Printing mechanism, including the keyboard and key levers, is carried on a carrier that runs on rails above the platen. Type bars are interchangeable for different languages or styles, and a case-shift changes between upper and lower case. Ink ribbon mechanism is included.

**Features:** [Interchangeable element](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** base plate; flat platen; carrier; rail; key levers; type bars; ink ribbon mechanism
