**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for production of copies on typewriters and calculating machines using auxiliary ribbons.

**How it works (reading):** An endless auxiliary ribbon runs over rollers on adjustable tensioned levers attached to the paper holding rail, passing through a slot between two sheets so it is not pinched; it replaces the carbon sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets used for copies between original and copy paper are inconvenient, and older ribbon arrangements are complicated and hard to change.

**Solution:** An endless auxiliary ribbon runs over rollers on adjustable tensioned levers attached to the paper holding rail, passing through a slot between two sheets so it is not pinched; it replaces the carbon sheet.

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
