## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be held exactly at the desired line-space position, and friction must hold the platen at any adjustment.

**Solution:** Curved arms with rollers engage the teeth of a ratchet wheel on the platen shaft under spring tension, locking the platen at the exact line adjustment.
