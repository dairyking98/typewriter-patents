## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier on the paper apron concentric with the roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement the paper guide of typewriters for the single and double pre-insertion method, in which the paper can be inserted both in front of and behind the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A circular paper guide plate is split by a hinge into two shell halves; the rear half is fixed to the platen frame and the front half folds down under spring pressure, with lugs passing through slots in the paper support plate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guide plate for typewriters could not be inserted smoothly both in front of and behind the platen.

**Solution:** A circular paper guide plate is split by a hinge into two shell halves; the rear half is fixed to the platen frame and the front half folds down under spring pressure, with lugs passing through slots in the paper support plate.
