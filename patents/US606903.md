## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Standard keyboards are not suited to the all-finger touch method and to the anatomy of the hand, and letters are unevenly shared between hands.

**Solution:** A split keyboard of two 4x4 groups of terraced, arched, obliquely aligned keys with thumb keys for space and capitals, plus a rebalanced letter arrangement.

**How it works:** The keyboard is divided into right and left groups of four rows of four lower-case keys each. Each row is raised above the one in front, the middle keys of each row are slightly higher (arched), and rows are obliquely offset outward. Two thumb keys between the front rows carry space and capitals, and are pressed only by the thumbs. A figure shift, if needed, is a bottom-row key. Some keys are shaded black or half black to help memorise the letter layout.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** left key group; right key group; space thumb key; capitals thumb key

**Numbers:** 16 letter keys plus 2 thumb keys; rows 1/4 in. apart; terrace rise about 3/16 in.
