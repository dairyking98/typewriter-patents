## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Intermediate lever pivots can be removed from the segment, and a parallelogram linkage arrangement lets each type lever be taken out or fitted individually.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type stop mechanisms are hard to remove per type lever, and the linkages differ in length.

**Solution:** Intermediate lever pivots can be removed from the segment, and a parallelogram linkage arrangement lets each type lever be taken out or fitted individually.
