## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to means for indenting checks, drafts, and notes and for inking the iiber thereof in such a manner that the check-marks cannot be changed or altered without so changing the appearance thereof that the same would be noted instantly by a person taking the check or draft in hand.

**How it works (reading):** A check-protector in which a circular band or electrotype carries indenting dies that indent, break and ink the paper fibres around each character.
