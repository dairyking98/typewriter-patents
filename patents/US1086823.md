## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Messages typed on a typewriter need to be enciphered and deciphered, and changing between the two modes must be quick.

**Solution:** A cryptographic attachment clamped to the end bars of a typewriter carries removable code strips and slide pieces that change the typed output between converting and deciphering mechanisms.
