**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a modification of the device for moving the universal rail for typewriters protected by the patent 288436.

**How it works (reading):** The rear end of each double-armed key lever acts directly on an inclined surface of the universal rail, so the rail moves straight from the keys and can lock the keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The universal rail is driven through intermediate links, which add dead motion and make its action imprecise and its locking unreliable.

**Solution:** The rear end of each double-armed key lever acts directly on an inclined surface of the universal rail, so the rail moves straight from the keys and can lock the keys.
