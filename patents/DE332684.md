The present invention relates to a modification of the device for moving the universal rail for typewriters protected by the patent 288436. The modification consists in the fact that the universal rail is not driven, as in the main patent, from the intermediate levers set in motion by the key levers and causing the type lever stop, but directly from the key levers. The arrangement is such that the rear end of the double-armed button lever acts on the universal rail or on the inclined engagement surface of the latter.
