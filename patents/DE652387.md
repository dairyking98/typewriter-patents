## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In printing tabulators the printed item can differ from the item added to the counter if the contacts that couple the counting wheels to the drive are imprecise.

**Solution:** The coupling of each counting wheel to the drive is governed by the displacement of its type carrier as it is set, so the counted value and the printed value are tied together.
