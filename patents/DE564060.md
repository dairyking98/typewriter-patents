> Description Translated from German Device for displaying the end line of a written sheet The invention relates to typewriters, calculating machines, accounting machines or the like. which shows the writer when he has reached the end line of a written sheet Has. Devices of this type are known and that is for this purpose on the shaft of the platen against a fixed mark adjustable a setting disc arranged, which takes part in the rotation of the platen after setting, carries different rows of license plates on the circumference and with a projection for triggering a signal is provided. The signal bell itself is on the one hand Frame of the roller carriage attached, on the other hand already directly on the The roller shaft next to the roller control handle. In this case the signal sounds as soon as the roller is brought into the position corresponding to the last line, that is, while the car is being lifted, even before its movement to the right has begun Has. This one-off signal was given at a very early point in time, and it could therefore easily be forgotten again because of its possibility of repetition was missing during the course of the car or in the next line.
