## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter automatic summation for non-decade currency)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an automatic Total pulling device.

**How it works (reading):** Each counter's sum train works independently through a common linkage that lifts the lock, so the decimal lock is released per counter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Non-decimal sums such as English currency cannot be totalled on typewriters with vertical and cross counters.

**Solution:** Each counter's sum train works independently through a common linkage that lifts the lock, so the decimal lock is released per counter.
