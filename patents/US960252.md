## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriter paper feeds cannot hold small cards or strips flat and register the writing at the same place on each, as addressing-machine stencil cards require.

**Solution:** A spring clamp on the platen grips the card flat and a register with pointer shows the platen turn position for each address line.

**How it works:** A V-shaped spring clamp, one leg fixed to the platen and a stiffer leg free, opens to receive a card. Turning the platen by end buttons makes fixed guide strips on the carriage force the stiff leg tangent to the platen, closing the clamp and holding the card flat. A numbered register on the platen end with a pointer marks line 1, 2 and 3 positions, and stop pins on the platen meet a carriage stop to limit rotation.
