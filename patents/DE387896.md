## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**How it works (reading):** Adjustable stop plates in a plate frame have projections whose travel lengths differ, so each plate can be swung on a thumb lever to a chosen position and comes into action in a different sequence along the line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage setting devices can only stop at fixed points, so they cannot be set to arbitrary, varying tab or line stops.

**Solution:** Adjustable stop plates in a plate frame have projections whose travel lengths differ, so each plate can be swung on a thumb lever to a chosen position and comes into action in a different sequence along the line.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
