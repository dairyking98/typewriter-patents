## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with means for effecting line or character spacing in either direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for reversing the direction of movement the paper cart of typewriters on which languages left to right or read from right to left, can be written.

**How it works (reading):** The pull cord from the spring drum runs over the carriage to the front and back, and a line roller on the opposite side lets the cord be shifted to the other end of the carriage to reverse it, while a reverse gear keeps the escape wheel turning the same way.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reversing the carriage direction for right-to-left writing on a spring-drum machine is cumbersome in known designs.

**Solution:** The pull cord from the spring drum runs over the carriage to the front and back, and a line roller on the opposite side lets the cord be shifted to the other end of the carriage to reverse it, while a reverse gear keeps the escape wheel turning the same way.
