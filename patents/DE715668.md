## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** A knife on the platen is tilted in two electromagnetic phases, each pulling one end against a fixed counter knife, giving a full cut with a small stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Continuous paper webs from typewriters and tabulators must be cut into sheets, and a mechanical tilting knife needs a large travel.

**Solution:** A knife on the platen is tilted in two electromagnetic phases, each pulling one end against a fixed counter knife, giving a full cut with a small stroke.
