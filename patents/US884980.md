## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar hangers are hard to attach and detach because a locking cam blocks access.

**Solution:** Two curved seat-rods on the segment and two hooks on the hanger let it be hung without the cam, and the cam then locks it firmly.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Element mounting on its hub](../features/elem-hub-keying.md), [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md), [Typebar and type-head construction](../features/bar-typebar-construction.md)

**Parts:** two seat-rods; two hooks
