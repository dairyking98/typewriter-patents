## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon is not held forward of the printing line in a stable way when the machine is in normal position.

**Solution:** Two rock-shafts with bent inner ends carry ribbon guide loops that hold the ribbon forward of the printing line.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
