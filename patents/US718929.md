## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Gearing

**In the patent’s own words:** Our object is to reduce to a minimum not only the noise incident to the operation of this class of machines,but 10st motion between the parts and the consequent shock or jar to the parts.

**How it works (reading):** An electromagnetic motor drives the mechanisms set off by key movement to print characters and space the paper, with direct linkages.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Motor-driven machines should be compact, with the fewest parts, the most direct linkages and minimum wear.

**Solution:** An electromagnetic motor drives the mechanisms set off by key movement to print characters and space the paper, with direct linkages.

**Features:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
