## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The key lever runs straight down behind a common straight bearing body, with an angle lever whose longer arm drives the type lever through a handlebar and whose shorter arm links to the button lever, giving a large leverage in a compact layout.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Known linkages with key levers, angle levers and type levers are bulky, and the key lever and bell crank share a bearing body awkwardly.

**Solution:** The key lever runs straight down behind a common straight bearing body, with an angle lever whose longer arm drives the type lever through a handlebar and whose shorter arm links to the button lever, giving a large leverage in a compact layout.
