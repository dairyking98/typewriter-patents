## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a paper guide device for typewriters, calculating and accounting machines, in which the guide plate used to guide the paper sheets is pivotally mounted on the frame of the paper carriage and provided with slots, through which protrude with the roller cooperating pressure rollers, which are arranged in a pivotable bracket and around a greater amount than the baffle can be lifted off the roller. As a result of the different sizes of movement of the guide plate and roller bracket, it happens that when these parts are lifted from the roller, the bracket carries out slingshot movements, whereby the front pressure rollers are easily lifted too far and the rear ones again almost to the roller with the effect that the unhindered introduction of a Sheet of paper affected from both the back and the front of the platen is. In order to prevent this, the deflection of the front pressure rollers is according to the invention limited by stops attached to the guide plate.
