## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide device for typewriters, calculating and accounting machines, in which the guide plate used to guide the paper sheets is pivotally mounted on the frame of the paper carriage and provided with slots, through which protrude with the roller cooperating pressure rollers, which are arranged in a pivotable bracket and around a greater amount than the baffle can be lifted off the roller.

**How it works (reading):** Stops on the guide plate limit how far the front pressure rollers can deflect, so the bracket is returned to position at the end of the movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the guide plate and roller bracket lift off the platen, the front pressure rollers overshoot, blocking paper insertion.

**Solution:** Stops on the guide plate limit how far the front pressure rollers can deflect, so the bracket is returned to position at the end of the movement.
