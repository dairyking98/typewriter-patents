## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type wheel brush re-inking, telegraph) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inking apparatus with ink applied by pads or rotary discs

**How it works (reading):** A dye tube or cylinder with its outlet as a nozzle sits so the rotating brush passes over it, letting dye out slowly so the brush soaks it up during rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand re-inking of a telegraph type wheel brush leaves an ink blob, and wiping off excess is awkward.

**Solution:** A dye tube or cylinder with its outlet as a nozzle sits so the rotating brush passes over it, letting dye out slowly so the brush soaks it up during rotation.
