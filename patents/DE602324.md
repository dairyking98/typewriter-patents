**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a setting tabulator for typewriters, typewriters and the like with tab tabs pivoted on an axis and a die Rails leading to their free ends.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tab tabs must be simple and cheap to make and must not yield when they hit column rods.

**Solution:** Tab tabs are held and guided on a shaft by two joined sheet-metal combs of stamped parts that support the shaft and the tabs.
