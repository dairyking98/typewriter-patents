## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on a paper holding device for typewriters, which is known per se Way with three holding members that can be lifted off independently of the platen is provided for several writing sheets.

**How it works (reading):** Three holding members mounted on rails above the platen fold down at the back, with pressure rollers and support plates that can be swung out of the way so the sheets are clamped independently and exchanged quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting and aligning three writing sheets in a paper holder is cumbersome because the front pressure rollers, which can swing only a small angle, disturb the sheets.

**Solution:** Three holding members mounted on rails above the platen fold down at the back, with pressure rollers and support plates that can be swung out of the way so the sheets are clamped independently and exchanged quickly.
