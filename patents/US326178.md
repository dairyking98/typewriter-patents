## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars carrying one character limit the number of characters per key.

**Solution:** Typebars carrying two or three types each are mounted in a vibratory frame that is swung to bring the chosen type to the print line.

**How it works:** Each typebar carries three types and is worked by a key. The bars and keyboard hang from a vibratory frame of three links, with a handle moved between two stop pins and returned by springs to the middle. With the frame in the middle, the middle type prints; swung one way or the other, the upper or lower type prints. The keyboard stays horizontal through pivots, or the keyboard can stay fixed while the frame moves.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** typebar; key; vibratory frame; handle; stop pin; spring; key board; hanger

**Numbers:** three types per typebar

**Improves on:**

- typebars with two or more characters: shifts the whole bar frame instead of the platen
