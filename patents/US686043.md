## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention are to avoid jarring the finger when putting the key and the type-bar suddenly in motion and also to secure a' sharp blow of the type upon the paper, as well as to increase the speed of operation of the machine.
