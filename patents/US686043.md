## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention are to avoid jarring the finger when putting the key and the type-bar suddenly in motion and also to secure a' sharp blow of the type upon the paper, as well as to increase the speed of operation of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** Improved type-action combinations of key, link and type-bar that avoid the finger jar, give a sharp blow to the paper, and speed the machine; the exact linkage is not legible in the OCR text.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Suddenly putting the key and type-bar in motion jars the finger, and the blow on the paper was weak.

**Solution:** Improved type-action combinations of key, link and type-bar that avoid the finger jar, give a sharp blow to the paper, and speed the machine; the exact linkage is not legible in the OCR text.

**How it works:** Type-action linkage between key and type-carrier, shown in figures 1 to 4 with the key half-way and fully depressed; details not legible.
