## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapement released the carriage at one fixed point, so it was too slow or too fast for different operators.

**Solution:** The escapement is adjustable so the carriage is released either just before or just after the type makes its impression, using feed-rack and spacing-dog arrangements.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
