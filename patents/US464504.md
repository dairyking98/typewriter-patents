## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to produce with increased economy of stock and labor a rapid and durable many-keyed type-writing machine which is quickly assembled and adapted for first-class work.

**How it works (reading):** Key-levers are meshed by toothed segments directly with radial multi-type type-levers in one slotted cast support, with a reciprocating plate driving the carriage escapement and a two-part carriage shifted toward/away from the operator for case.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Many-keyed typebar machines were costly in stock and labor to make and assemble, and key/type-lever alignment and key travel were uneven.

**Solution:** Key-levers are meshed by toothed segments directly with radial multi-type type-levers in one slotted cast support, with a reciprocating plate driving the carriage escapement and a two-part carriage shifted toward/away from the operator for case.

**How it works:** Each key-lever and its type-lever sit in slots of a single milled segmental casting, fulcrum rods in cross-grooves set at an angle so the meshed levers hold each other in place. Toothed segments of varying radius at the inner ends give every key equal travel. Depressing a key swings the type-lever to a common print point and, through a bar, lifts a common reciprocating plate that rocks a rocker-shaft carrying a detent. The detent shifts from a fixed rack to a spring-driven sliding rack, letting the spring-and-cord carriage advance one space. Shift keys move a slide-bar that moves the platen section of a two-part carriage toward or away from the operator to choose which type on a lever prints. The ribbon runs at right angles to the line and is advanced by a ratchet on the rocker-shaft.
