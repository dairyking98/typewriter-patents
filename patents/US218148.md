## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A neat, cheap, portable typewriter whose line being printed stays fully visible, so forms can be filled in.

**Solution:** A spring-driven type carriage travels on a fixed bed while a pressed ring and hinged arms print and step the carriage via alternating pawls, and the endless ribbon slides aside to give a clear view of the impression roller.

**How it works:** The type carriage runs on small wheels along tracks on a bed plate and is driven by a spring through gears to a rack. A ring supported by guide rods rests on three hinged arms with springs, and the type arm passes through it to print. A pin on each arm moves two pawls alternately on the rack, so each complete stroke spaces one letter, and the slot shape gives a rest while the type strikes, preventing smear. A lever disengages the pawls to return the carriage, rewinding the spring. A sliding catch grips a cord on return to advance the endless ribbon a fresh section each line. The impression roller is held by springs and worm wheel, and the ribbon can be shifted aside for a clear view. An ink reservoir packed with felt moistens the ribbon.
