**How it works (reading):** Inking rollers travel back and forth across the type line, so the line is inked again for the second impression on the transparent sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A double impression on transparent film needs the type line re-inked, which means changing the pressure and dwelling for a second pass.

**Solution:** Inking rollers travel back and forth across the type line, so the line is inked again for the second impression on the transparent sheet.
