## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Franklin typewriter needs a ribbon actuating and shift mechanism that handles the carriage shift for two cases.

**Solution:** Ribbon feed, ribbon shift and carriage-shift keys are linked by shift levers and a ribbon frisket so the ribbon moves with the case shift.

**How it works:** Typebars on a front-geared key set strike the platen; the platen carriage rides on a traveling track frame on a shift track-bar. Carriage-shift keys engage horizontal pivoted shift levers joined by a connecting rod. These move a ribbon frisket sliding on an alining plate, shifting the ribbon position between lower- and capital-case. A separate ribbon-feed mechanism advances the ribbon as the typebar prints. Opening text ends before the detailed parts.
