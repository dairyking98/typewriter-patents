## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a flat platen, work-sheets of different sizes need guarding at their edges, and earlier flexible tape holders need tension take-up.

**Solution:** Work-holding and guarding members are individually adjustable on a vertically movable frame, some transverse and some longitudinal, to protect and hold the sheet edges for sheets of various sizes.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
