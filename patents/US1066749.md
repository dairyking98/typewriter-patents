## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The type may strike while the shift is half done and so print misaligned.

**Solution:** An obstacle that intercepts the printing movement unless the shifted part is in a proper printing position.

**How it works:** An element moved by the printing mechanism and another element moved by the shift mechanism interlock. In an intermediate shift position the second blocks or deflects the first, so the type cannot strike or the blow glances and pushes the shift to the right position.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** printing element; shiftable part; stop; bevel surface
