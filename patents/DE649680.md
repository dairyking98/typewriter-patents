## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key levers must travel the full stroke of ordinary typing, so no faster striking is possible.

**Solution:** A hand lever loads a spring through a shaft and cam roller; releasing the spring suddenly drives the key lever and typebar.

**How it works:** Hand lever preloads the spring by turning a cam disc; the spring relaxes past the cam apex and impels the type lever.

**Features:** [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
