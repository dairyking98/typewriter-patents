## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hand operation of carriage and platen slows typing.

**Solution:** Pneumatic cylinder on the carriage worked by a foot pedal air pump through a tube.

**How it works:** A cylinder on the carriage frame has an arm against the spacing lever. A hollow rod and piston connect through tubing to an air pump under the desk, driven by a sliding pedal. Pedalling moves the carriage and platen without the hands.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
