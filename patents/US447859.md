## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On Yost machines the carriage guide and hinge rails are arranged so that a conventional rigidly fixed paper table would block the carriage from being tipped up to vertical for inspection, correction and type cleaning.

**Solution:** Hinge the paper table to the rear bar of the hinged carriage and let a spring and a pin-and-slot connection fold it out of the way when the carriage is raised.

**How it works:** The paper table has two lugs that wrap the carriage's rear bar like a hinge. A coiled spring on the bar presses on the table's back, tending to rotate it to the raised-carriage position. A pin on the bar rides in a slot in one lug; with the carriage down the pin sits at the slot's top end and holds the table in working position. As the carriage is tipped up, the table first moves with it until its upper edge meets a fixed arm, then stays still while the pin travels down the slot and the spring winds tighter. Lowering the carriage reverses this and the pin pulls the table back to its place. The pin also stops the table sliding along the bar.

**Parts:** paper table; hinged paper carriage; rear carriage bar; perforated lug; slotted lug; pin; coiled spring; stop arm; guide rail; yoke; type ring

**Improves on:**
