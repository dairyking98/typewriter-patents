## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the earlier Brady and Warner machine (US202143) the type wheel was moved sideways by nuts on separate screw shafts and the paper holder was vibrated by a yielding platform under a yielding pin; the inventor wants these parts better built and worked.

**Solution:** The type wheel is splined to a spirally ribbed shaft and swivelled to a nut on that same shaft, so turning the shaft rotates the wheel to a letter and turning the nut relative to the shaft slides the wheel sideways to space.

**How it works:** A spirally ribbed shaft has straight longitudinal grooves, and arms from the type wheel run in them so the wheel turns with the shaft while it is free to slide along it. The wheel is held by collars on a nut running on the screw ribs, and a toothed wheel is fixed to the nut. A lever pivoted on a disk with a bevel gear engaging a gear on the shaft is carried round to rotate the wheel to the letter, and pushing it down throws the paper-holder rollers up against the type. When the lever is released and the holder falls, a hinged hook carried with the holder engages the nut's toothed wheel and turns it slightly while the shaft stands still, so the nut and type wheel move one space along the shaft before the hook slips off. A blank character set and the lever gives a word space, and holding the lever down while turning moves the wheel further to set it for margins.
