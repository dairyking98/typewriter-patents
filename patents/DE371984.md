## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type pieces must be set to the exact stop position on the type lever, but aligning them by eye on a hot setup is imprecise and risks the type jumping.

**Solution:** The type is clamped adjustably on a sliding frame and sighted against a glass plate marked with type-width and type-height lines on micrometer hinges, the plate being swung out before the hot attachment; the type is then fixed to the lever.
