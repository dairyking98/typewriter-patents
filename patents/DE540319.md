## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (booking machine printing unit)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to printing units for booking machines with a main adding unit and several control adding units, in which the imprint of the offset amount, among other things, on one on different Lines adjustable receipt can be made.

**How it works (reading):** The hammer drive is switched by the line position of the sliding printing table, so imprints from the main adder are suppressed while control adder readings still print.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A subtotal reading can be fraudulently printed on a customer card, because the print hammer strikes whenever the total is drawn.

**Solution:** The hammer drive is switched by the line position of the sliding printing table, so imprints from the main adder are suppressed while control adder readings still print.
