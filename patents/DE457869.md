## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A slip inserted into the booking-slip guide could intercept the printing intended for the booking strip.

**Solution:** The receipt guide stays closed by a locking device that opens only when a receipt-printing key or lever is pressed.
