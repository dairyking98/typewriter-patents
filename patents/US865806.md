## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Tabulating mechanisms are bulky and have many parts, and the operator needs a single convenient control.

**Solution:** A simple, compact tabulating mechanism on the top plate, where one finger piece at the front controls both the denomination-determining stop and the carriage releasing device.
