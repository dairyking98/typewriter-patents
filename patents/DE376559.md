## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon zone adjusters hung on the universal rail add weight and resistance to the typebars.

**Solution:** A zone plate with hooks at different distances, mounted on the ribbon-fork lever not the universal rail, selects the ribbon stroke or none for stencils.

**How it works:** The universal rail, pushed back by a typebar, moves an arm that oscillates a lever linked by a rod to the ribbon fork. A slotted plate on that lever carries two opposed hooks at different levels that catch the arm; a hand lever moves a slide, rocker and rod to shift the plate so one hook, the other, or neither engages. This gives two ribbon zones or no ribbon movement for stencils or carbons.
