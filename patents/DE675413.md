## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Felt or leather rest cushions are noisy, and spring-core or pin cushions bounce levers or are rigid.

**Solution:** A resilient strip, rubber-based, in a rigid support bracket absorbs the type lever on its rest, reducing noise and sagging.
