> Description Translated from German Type lever rest bearings for typewriters The invention relates to on type lever rest bearings for typewriters made of a cushion of strip-shaped, resilient material, especially rubber, on a rigid support bracket of a typewriter is stored. With the usual structure, where one is firmly on the mounting bracket applied hoist (pad) made of asbestos, felt or the like. Use is a Considerable noise as well as vibration and sagging of the type lever on rebound inevitable on the type lever cushion. The vibrating and sagging of the type lever causes their wear and tear as well as an inhibition for the keystroke and the rest Mechanism whereby the writing speed is impaired. There are type lever cushions known, one with a curved metal rod assembled coil spring contained by a hose as well as a fabric sheath Surrounded with leather strips is Lind which takes dowels at both ends into the fastening screws intervention. Such a pillow works noisily and is particularly rigid, so that the type levers bounce back and can easily get caught.
