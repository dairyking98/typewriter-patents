## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing columns of figures with blank gaps (invoices, accounts) meant tapping the space key many times or unlatching the carriage by hand and checking alignment, which was slow and tiring.

**Solution:** A supplemental skip key that releases the carriage rack from the escapement detent and lets the carriage run until it hits an adjustable column stop lug.

**How it works:** The spring-driven carriage normally advances by a detent engaging a rack bar. A pivoted arm on a frame post holds a longitudinally adjustable stop rod under a cross-arm of the rack bar, with set-screw lugs clamped at the column positions. Pressing the supplemental key lifts its lever, which through a vertical rod raises the stop rod, lifting the rack bar out of the detent so the carriage runs freely. The raised lug is in the path of the rack-bar cross-arm and stops it at the column. Releasing the key lowers the rod, removing the stop and re-engaging the detent, with a spring returning the arm.
