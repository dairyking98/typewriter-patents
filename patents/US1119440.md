## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Waybills in sets with collect or prepaid forms need several different form sets, which a standard carriage cannot handle.

**Solution:** A special carriage with multiple platens swinging about a common axis, each receiving paper from its own set of rolls, with one platen brought to the type bars at a time.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
