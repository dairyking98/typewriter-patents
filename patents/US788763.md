## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike typewriters need type-actions and carriage feed that are efficient, simple and easy to assemble, and whose feed is reliably coordinated with the type-bars.

**Solution:** Pivoted type-bars swing up and back to strike the paper in front of the platen, and the carriage feed is triggered whenever a type-bar returning from printing has travelled a set distance.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
