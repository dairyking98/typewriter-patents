## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In electric cylinder typewriters the magnet that withdraws the type cylinder used two coils (one always on) that never match exactly, giving uneven strikes and demanding an oversized strike magnet.

**Solution:** Use a separate single-coil retraction magnet switched on only after the strike, or a free spring in place of a magnet, so the strike runs unopposed with uniform force.

**How it works:** The type cylinder turns and slides axially in a sleeve (row by sliding, character by rotation) and is rotated by two electromagnets limited by stops that the pressed key advances. The sleeve pivots on pointed screws and its rear arms are linked to the armatures of two magnets; the strike magnet, energised with the rotation magnets but held by a lock released at the end of rotation, throws the cylinder at the roller. Shortly before the type reaches the paper the strike magnet is switched off by the stroke itself and the retraction magnet is switched on to pull the cylinder back. Alternatively a coil spring between arm and frame retracts it, or a spring does the strike while a magnet tensions it; a delay winding on the strike magnet ensures axial shifting finishes before the strike.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md), [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)

**Parts:** type cylinder; sleeve; rotation electromagnet; strike electromagnet; retraction electromagnet; armature; link; coil spring; stop arm; stop bracket; paper roller

**Improves on:**

- earlier machines retracting the cylinder with a two-coil magnet, one coil constantly excited: single coil switched at end of strike so the strike is unopposed
- earlier spring-strike machines where the key had to tension a strong spring: spring acts freely and the strike force is uniform regardless of keystroke
