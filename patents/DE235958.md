## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters with multi-colored ribbon and has the special design of the adjustment device for the ribbon to the subject.

**How it works (reading):** An adjusting lever with setting push buttons that lock the driver position, so the key stroke is fully or partly used to lift the ribbon guide to the required colour.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon guide lift in multi-colour ribbon typewriters is set by an adjustment that uses only part or none of the key stroke.

**Solution:** An adjusting lever with setting push buttons that lock the driver position, so the key stroke is fully or partly used to lift the ribbon guide to the required colour.
