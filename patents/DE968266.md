## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-step feed on print-roller adders can give uneven feed and occupies much space.

**Solution:** A feed hook pivoted on a feed crank engages or is blocked from a return wheel by a cam, so one feed member works for single steps and another adds the two-step or multi-step feed.
