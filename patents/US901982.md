## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In visible typewriters with a ribbon vibrator, a multi-field (e.g. two-colour or copy/record) ribbon could not easily be brought to the printing point in any chosen field, and the crosswise ribbon feed could not be switched off.

**Solution:** A switching slide in the ribbon vibrator mechanism lets the operator set which field of a longitudinally divided ribbon covers the printing point at each stroke, and cut out the crosswise feed.

**How it works:** The Monarch typebar machine has a ribbon divided lengthwise into fields (e.g. black and red) wound between two spools. A ribbon vibrator moves the ribbon to and from the printing point under key control. A switching member or slide with positioning and stop devices sets how far the vibrator rises, so the chosen field is presented. The same slide can disable the crosswise feed used with lengthwise-and-crosswise feeding ribbons.
