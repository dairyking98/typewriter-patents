## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type lever typewriter with an inclined type Type lever segment and directly from the button levers without an intermediate link means Toothed segment driven type levers.

**How it works (reading):** An inclined type-lever segment carries the middle group driven directly by its key levers; the outer groups are driven by intermediate levers with adjustable bearing pins so all keys have equal stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A portable type-lever typewriter is too tall and deep, and outer type-lever groups cannot be driven directly without jamming.

**Solution:** An inclined type-lever segment carries the middle group driven directly by its key levers; the outer groups are driven by intermediate levers with adjustable bearing pins so all keys have equal stroke.
