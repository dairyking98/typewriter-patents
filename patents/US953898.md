## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The pressure rolls are hard to tension and release, and the small rear rolls cut into the paper shelf and jam sheets.

**Solution:** Pressure roll axles carried on arms from the platen rod, bearing springs on collars whose tension arms can be set individually and released.
