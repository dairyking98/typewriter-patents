## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to that class of typewriting machines in which a series of type bars or levers, each bearing two type or printing characters, are arranged in a circular series and caused to strike at one or the other of two points, the paper-sustaining roll or platen being shifted to receive the blow of one or the other of the type, as required.

**How it works (reading):** A detailed refinement of the Remington-type machine with type-bars in a circle striking at two points and a shifting platen.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Jenne's double-type-bar machine needed more exact work, less wear and power, and easier adjustment and repair.

**Solution:** A detailed refinement of the Remington-type machine with type-bars in a circle striking at two points and a shifting platen.

**How it works:** Type-bars with two characters each are arranged in a circle and strike at one of two points. The roller frame shifts forward and back on the main carriage to bring the platen over the right character. The carriage runs on two rods; one is D-shaped and acts as a hinge so the carriage can be raised to view the line. Bushed lugs give wear take-up.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** main frame; carriage; roller frame; platen; D-section hinge rod; guide rod; supporting roller; bushing
