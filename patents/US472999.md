## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon inking devices for typewriters need reversal between two spools and a way to hold the ribbon flat under the type head.

**Solution:** Two ribbon spools with gear wheels side by side are driven by a single sliding pinion that engages either spool, with a pivoted ribbon platform that also locks the type head.

**How it works:** The ribbon winds on two spools mounted on one axis, each with a toothed wheel. A driving pinion, driven by a ratchet and pawl from the machine, slides or is shifted along its arbor to mesh with either spool wheel, so the ribbon is wound in either direction. The ribbon passes over a spring-raised pivoted plate that holds its central part under the type head; when depressed the plate's notch engages the type head or typebar and locks it. The spools are removable and the pinion position is held by a spring catch in grooves.
