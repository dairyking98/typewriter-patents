## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switch for small typewriters with one in a horizontal Level swing-out hand lever and one carried along by this against the action of a spring Slide that is in a slot guide of the cheek of the machine carriage carrying the switch lever is slidably guided and on a horizontal bolt from above into the ratchet wheel incident latch carries.

**How it works (reading):** Only a slide sits outside the cheek; its bent end runs in a guide slot and carries a pawl that engages the ratchet wheel inside, with an adjustable end stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A line switch with a lever and slot guide on the outside of the carriage cheek is complex on small typewriters.

**Solution:** Only a slide sits outside the cheek; its bent end runs in a guide slot and carries a pawl that engages the ratchet wheel inside, with an adjustable end stop.
