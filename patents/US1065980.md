## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper fingers must be placed and held in position along a support rod over the platen.

**Solution:** Paper-finger hubs slide along a rod on a slot, and a key in the slot holds each hub frictionally and stops it turning.
