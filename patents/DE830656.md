## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Writing device for portable typewriters The invention relates to a portable typewriter. A portable typewriter must of course above all else have the smallest possible height, especially in terms of height, through which the Carry away and handling should be made easier. There are a relatively large number of typewriters known as portable in the trade available, which are still too high compared to office machines despite this height difference and are bulky to permit convenient carrying by the hearing smoker. A non-limiting exemplary embodiment is shown below with reference to the drawing of the subject matter of the invention described. Fig. I is a vertical section through the writing device according to the invention; Figs. A, 3 and 4 are partial sections for comparison the arrangement of the type lever with that of conventional typewriters. Approved for reducing the height of known portable typewriters So far, the main parts of all office machines have been arranged more tightly can be found without, however, making the essential changes to these parts, which are indispensable for achieving very low machines.
