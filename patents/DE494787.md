## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control strip feed cash register)

**How it works (reading):** A third device, driven when certain amount keys are pressed, advances the control strip so unprinted gaps mark each amount type in the printed strip; a counter pawl stops the take-up roll rotating backwards.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash register control strip entries cannot be told apart by amount type, so totals must be sorted by hand at the end of the period.

**Solution:** A third device, driven when certain amount keys are pressed, advances the control strip so unprinted gaps mark each amount type in the printed strip; a counter pawl stops the take-up roll rotating backwards.
