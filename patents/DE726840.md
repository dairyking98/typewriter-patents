## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter sum and subtotal pulling device)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter or the like with a sum and subtotal pulling device for automatic sums by decimal places, with which the values introducing counter element a special summation element is used.

**How it works (reading):** The special summation element and the counter are both coupled to the arithmetic unit, and after summation the summation element is decoupled, giving subtotals by decimal places.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Sum and subtotal pulling devices of earlier design were cumbersome and unreliable.

**Solution:** The special summation element and the counter are both coupled to the arithmetic unit, and after summation the summation element is decoupled, giving subtotals by decimal places.

**How it works:** Summation element coupled to the arithmetic unit for subtotals, then decoupled.
