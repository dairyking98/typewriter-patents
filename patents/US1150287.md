## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Collated sheets slip or fail to stay in order when carried around the platen.

**Solution:** Flexible endless bands with sheet-impaling studs run on the platen ends and engage platen studs or sprockets so they travel in unison.
