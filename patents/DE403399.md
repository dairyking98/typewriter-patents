## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter lowering table tilts front or rear when pulled out or pushed in.

**Solution:** Stiffening rail joins the extended suspension rails to form a compensating parallelogram that keeps the table top horizontal.
