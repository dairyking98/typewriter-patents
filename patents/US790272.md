## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to the paperfeeding mechanism;

**How it works (reading):** Improved paper-feed roll on a hanger, with a guide-rail and carriage arrangement, applied to the platen and carriage of a Densmore machine.
