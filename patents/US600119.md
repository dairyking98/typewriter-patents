## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Large keyboards slow operators; keys need force and eye movement.

**Solution:** A few keys, singly and in combinations, select among many electromagnets through a permutational contact device that closes the circuit at the instant of key touch.

**How it works:** A small keyset feeds a permutational selector made of two groups of controlling elements; a combination of one element from each group closes the circuit for one of many electromagnets. The magnets control type-carriers of a typewriter, or key-levers of type-setting and linotype machines. Letter spacing is designed so interword space is made with a letter simultaneously.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Keys that only set the element](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Uses:** [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** key; permutational selector; electromagnet; circuit closer; type carrier; spacing mechanism

**Improves on:**

- [US246411](../patents/US246411.md): no preliminary positioning before circuit closes
- [US352143](../patents/US352143.md): no preliminary positioning before circuit closes
- [US331275](../patents/US331275.md): simpler selecting mechanism
