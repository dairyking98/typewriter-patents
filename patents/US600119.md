## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for controlling character selection

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to increase the amount of work which may be done by an operator upon an instrument of the kind described in a given timein other Words, to increase the ease and the rapidity of opera- My invention is applicable to type-writing machines, type-setting and linotype machines, printing-telegraphs, and probably to other devices.
