## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A folding typewriter's foldable carriage support needs adjusting and a shift lever.

**Solution:** Adjustable-length rod between folding arms and a shift lever set the platen to three positions.

**How it works:** A single-piece sheet metal frame holds a rod with a shift lever. The shift lever connects through a rod to slides carrying the foldable support bars, which pivot with the carriage bed forward over the keyboard. A threaded rod with a sleeve and tool holes can be lengthened or shortened to adapt the spread of the arms.

**Features:** [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** frame; shift lever; slide; arm; carriage bed; carriage; platen; threaded rod; adjusting sleeve
