## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cheap, simple typewriter for writing on flat surfaces such as bound book pages, with visible writing and letter-position scales for corrections.

**Solution:** A carriage frame on a base frame laid on the book runs across and down the page, with typebars arranged about a common centre and a key-operated feed for the carriage.

**How it works:** An open rectangular base frame rests on the book; a supplemental frame slides on it with a tongue-and-groove guide and carries the type carriage down the page, line spacing set by a lever and stop. The carriage frame runs on wheels along a rail and rod. Typebars are arranged about a common centre so the keys lie in straight lines before the operator. A feed device with fingers on a swinging rack, driven by the key bars, steps the carriage per letter and can be thrown out to return it. Writing is seen at all times without lifting the carriage, and scales indicate each letter's position.
