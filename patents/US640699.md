## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A rotatable multi-face type head on a typebar should stay accurately placed for clean printing, be interchangeable between bars, and allow wear or looseness to be taken up.

**Solution:** Mount the type head between two conical centre bearings on the typebar, one of them an adjustable nut, so the head turns freely and wear can be taken up.

**How it works:** The typebar ends in a thin threaded stem with a conical shoulder between stem and body. A conical nut on the stem, locked by a jam nut, forms the second centre. The type head has a hub with conical recesses seating on both centres and rotates between them, showing different faces of type. Lugs on the head let it be turned by hand or automatically, and a spring stops accidental rotation. Tightening the nut takes up wear.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Interchangeable element](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [Element mounting on its hub](../features/elem-hub-keying.md), [Shift by turning the element](../features/shift-rotary.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** typebar; type head; conical shoulder; threaded stem; conical nut; jam nut; lug; detent spring

**Improves on:**

- Tubular hub on cylindrical shaft: Cone centres make bars and heads interchangeable and take up wear, which a cylindrical fit does not
