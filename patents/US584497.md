## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in mechanism for winding the ribbon 011 typewriters, and has for its object to provide a mechanism which will automatically reverse the direction of movement of the ribbon when it has been run its full length in either direction.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-operated trip, initiated by stops on the ribbon, shifts which spool is geared to the drive.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon must reverse automatically at its ends.

**Solution:** A spring-operated trip, initiated by stops on the ribbon, shifts which spool is geared to the drive.

**How it works:** Two spools with drive gears sit side by side; a shifting connection engages one with the driving gear; stops on the ribbon trigger a spring trip.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** spool; drive gear; shifting connection; trip; stop; guide roller

- [US544571](../patents/US544571.md): adds automatic reversal
