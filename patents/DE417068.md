## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (dye manufacture) · **Subject (patent classes):** Foldable typewriters; Preparation from starting materials already containing the dibenzanthrone or isodibenzanthrone nucleus by etherification of hydroxy compounds

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the manufacture of bodies, either as dyes or as intermediates can be used to produce dyes.

**How it works (reading):** Ketones of the perylene series, from oxidation of dibenzanthrone and related compounds, are reduced mildly and then treated with alkylating agents such as dimethyl sulfate to give dyes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dyes of the perylene series need a better route to alkylated dye bodies, since the oxidation product alkylates poorly.

**Solution:** Ketones of the perylene series, from oxidation of dibenzanthrone and related compounds, are reduced mildly and then treated with alkylating agents such as dimethyl sulfate to give dyes.
