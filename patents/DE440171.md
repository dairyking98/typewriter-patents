## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** The trunnion end is itself a cone, and the threaded nut on the flange is guided in slots so it cannot turn but can shift perpendicular to the roller axis.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The exact centring of a platen trunnion depends on forcing the cone into a conical bore without eccentricity.

**Solution:** The trunnion end is itself a cone, and the threaded nut on the flange is guided in slots so it cannot turn but can shift perpendicular to the roller axis.
