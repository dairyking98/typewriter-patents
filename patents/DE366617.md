## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**How it works (reading):** Three or more rockers each carry a locking pawl and jamming wheels on the carriage rack's gear shaft, or act on the rack directly; each pawl engages gaps of different widths in ratchet wheels, so the carriage advance follows the character width in a compact unit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing escapements that advance the carriage by the natural width of each letter need complex differential gearing or racks and take too much space.

**Solution:** Three or more rockers each carry a locking pawl and jamming wheels on the carriage rack's gear shaft, or act on the rack directly; each pawl engages gaps of different widths in ratchet wheels, so the carriage advance follows the character width in a compact unit.
