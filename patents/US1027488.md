## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to a ribbon carrier which is vibrated to cause the ribbon to cover and uncover the printing point when the keys of the typewriter are operated in the usual manner.

**How it works (reading):** A vibrated ribbon carrier with rigid arms and spring-loaded movable arms that form guide eyes; the movable arms swing open so the ribbon can be removed or inserted and close to hold it while the carrier vibrates.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Replacing a narrow front-strike ribbon, especially when switching between two colours or inks, is difficult with the ordinary ribbon carrier.

**Solution:** A vibrated ribbon carrier with rigid arms and spring-loaded movable arms that form guide eyes; the movable arms swing open so the ribbon can be removed or inserted and close to hold it while the carrier vibrates.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
