## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars must be pivoted so they move freely side to side and up and down, but must not rotate or vibrate sideways, otherwise the type prints unevenly.

**Solution:** A fulcrum post with a cross-section matching the type-bar, and a guide-plate, hold each type-bar so it can move laterally and vertically about the fulcrum point but cannot twist on its axis.
