## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reducing complexity of the key-lever, type-bar and letter-space linkages of a typebar writing machine.

**Solution:** Wires connect the type-bars directly to the key-levers, guide pins keep the levers apart, and a vibratory frame links the key-levers to the letter-space ratchets of the carriage.

**How it works:** Pivoted type-bars sit in radial slots of an annular disk so that all strike the same place. Each is tied by a wire or ligament to a point on its key-lever between the pivot and the key. A rack of parallel pins held in cross-bars guides the key-levers. Beneath the levers a pendent cross-bar is hung by wires from a vibratory frame pivoted across above them, so any key movement rocks the frame and operates the letter-space ratchets: a main axle with a cylinder, two ratchet wheels and a cord-and-weight pulley, and a walking beam with ratchets. A lifting spring is also combined with the frame.
