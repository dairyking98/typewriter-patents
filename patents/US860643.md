## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the invention being to provide a machine of this class, which, while greatly simplified and thereby materially cheapcned in the cost of manufacture, shall at the same time be more cilicient in operation and less liable to get out of adjustment and repair than those heretofore in use.

**How it works (reading):** Type bars swing their types down onto the paper on the cylinder and back, connected to their key levers and supported by simplified mounts and linkages.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike type bar machines are costly to make and prone to losing adjustment and needing repair.

**Solution:** Type bars swing their types down onto the paper on the cylinder and back, connected to their key levers and supported by simplified mounts and linkages.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
