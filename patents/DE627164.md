## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register print hammer control)

**How it works (reading):** Three-position setting lever stops the check conveyor manually while a feeler lever sets the hammer's working strokes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash-register print hammer runs continuously and the check conveyor cannot be controlled per slip.

**Solution:** Three-position setting lever stops the check conveyor manually while a feeler lever sets the hammer's working strokes.
