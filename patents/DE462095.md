## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On accounting typewriters the invoice-sheet scale and the pressure rollers can only be lifted separately, which means two operations when changing sheets.

**Solution:** A clutch engagement between the scale rail and the pressure-roller arms lets one lever lift both together, while the scale can still fold back alone.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper holder for typewriters for accounting purposes Certain typewriters are designed in such a way that, on the one hand, they can handle the invoice sheets and on the other hand a current account sheet independently of one another on the platen able to hold on. Accordingly, two retaining devices are provided, One of which consists of pressure rollers and for holding the current account sheet serves, while the other is designed as a scale and holds the invoice sheets. In the known devices of this type, both retaining devices are independent stored from each other in such a way that each can only be lifted for itself. In contrast, the invention consists in the engagement of a clutch between the two retaining devices, in such a way that the pressure rollers move only lift off the platen at the same time as the scale, while vice versa the scale can be folded back independently of the pressure rollers. This is a simplified and more appropriate operation of the paper holding device conditional, because only one lever has to be operated each time.
