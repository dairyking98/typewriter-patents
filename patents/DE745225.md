## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen bearings on round bars are set with hammer blows or shims, and adjusting one direction moves the other, so precise type-imprint alignment is hard.

**Solution:** A wedge between the bearing carrier and a sloped frame surface is moved by screw to set the platen in the type-imprint direction, and an eccentric on a slotted screw sets the height, with the parts locking each other after adjustment.
