## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a type lever design and all-grip cone storage, by which the geiiannten disadvantages are eliminated, so that even with typewriters with exchangeable type lever or lever sets a smooth, noiseless gait is achieved.

**How it works (reading):** The lever drive end has a resilient drive-pin eye that snaps onto the pin and pivots on it, so the connection is a pure pivot and the lever stays interchangeable without noise.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Interchangeable type levers rattled at each keystroke because the drive pin slid in a slot at the lever end.

**Solution:** The lever drive end has a resilient drive-pin eye that snaps onto the pin and pivots on it, so the connection is a pure pivot and the lever stays interchangeable without noise.
