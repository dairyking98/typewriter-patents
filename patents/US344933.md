## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage escapement needs to release exactly one step and also let the carriage be run freely.

**Solution:** A spring-wound gear on the carriage rack carries a scape wheel, and a vertically moving scape plate with an upper fixed dog and a lower pivoted dog releases it one step per stroke.

**How it works:** A coil spring rotates a gear that meshes with the carriage rack, and a scape wheel is fixed to the gear hub. A slotted scape plate between them carries a fixed upper dog and a pivoted lower dog, pointing opposite ways. Raising the plate frees the upper dog and engages the lower dog, which moves with the wheel until it meets a stop, giving one step. Lowering the plate re-engages the upper dog. A thrust bar wedges the lower dog out of the wheel plane so the carriage runs free.
