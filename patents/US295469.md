## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The paper carriage needs a motive power that moves it one letter space per keystroke and whose strength can be set.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded hinged lever, tensioned by a coiled spring on a shaft with an adjustable toothed nut, pulls the carriage leftward while a tilting key-frame pawl and a toothed rack release it one tooth per stroke.

**How it works:** A frame holding the platen slides on a guide rail and is linked to the top of a lever whose lower end is fixed to a shaft in the bed. A coil spring on that shaft pulls the lever, and so the carriage, leftward, and a toothed nut with a spring catch sets and holds its tension. A frame under the keys tilts when any key is pressed, throwing a weighted pawl off one toothed rack and onto the other. A second rack bar, spring-drawn and limited by slots, moves one tooth. When the key is released the pawl re-engages one tooth further on, so the carriage has advanced one space. The carriage is pushed back right against the spring to start a new line, the pawl clicking over the teeth.
