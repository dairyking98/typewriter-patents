## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A power carriage return must start the carriage smoothly and softly, not with a jerk.

**Solution:** A rope from the line shift lever is wound on a drum by a motor; the drum is half of a clutch whose balls are pressed outward by a conical part when the clutch closes, so the force builds up gently and the carriage starts softly.
