**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on a paper guide device for typewriters, in particular booking mashes, with an articulated above the usual paper table on the trolley, second paper table equipped with special pressure rollers for a base sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A second paper table on articulated arms is hinged behind the normal table; it pivots into position with pressure rollers that press onto the platen and can be folded back.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A second paper table for base sheets is needed on bookkeeping typewriters, but must not interfere with normal typing.

**Solution:** A second paper table on articulated arms is hinged behind the normal table; it pivots into position with pressure rollers that press onto the platen and can be folded back.

**How it works:** Leaf springs and articulated arms hold the second table against the roller; folding it back clears the writing point.
