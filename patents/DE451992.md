## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (keyed value transfer to counter)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a device for transferring the scanned numerical value to the counter in typewriters, with a staggered roller as the most important transmission element in a known manner and a dial that can be displaced along the same are used.

**How it works (reading):** The staggered roller and the displacement of the adjusting wheel are both driven by the power drive, and a raised tabulator rod stops the displacement at the keyed value, so the roller turns only after the shift is complete.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting the adjusting wheel through cams, slot guides and gears makes the keystroke heavy and slows the calculations.

**Solution:** The staggered roller and the displacement of the adjusting wheel are both driven by the power drive, and a raised tabulator rod stops the displacement at the keyed value, so the roller turns only after the shift is complete.
