## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** A paper-controlled trip holds a latch that interposes in the line-space mechanism to prevent feed until the sheet's bottom edge passes the trip; a separate release finger can dislodge the latch and hold it idle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The line-space mechanism keeps feeding cards or envelopes past their edge when the sheet should stop the feed.

**Solution:** A paper-controlled trip holds a latch that interposes in the line-space mechanism to prevent feed until the sheet's bottom edge passes the trip; a separate release finger can dislodge the latch and hold it idle.
