## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be rotated freely by hand without moving the ratchet, yet the ratchet must hold position when line-spaced.

**Solution:** The ratchet wheel is friction-held on the platen shaft by a cupped spring washer and retained by a swinging detent, with a line-space gage on the detent cooperating with the feed pawl.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
