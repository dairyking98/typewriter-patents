## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inked ribbon ends are hard to thread onto spool catches without soiling hands, and ink transfers to the threader while it is on the spool.

**Solution:** A combined threader and protector attached to each ribbon end. It guides threading and shields the ribbon-end ink from the threader while the ribbon is wound.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** inked ribbon; combined threader and protector; ribbon spool
