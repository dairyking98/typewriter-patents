## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to focus on keys for office machines and similar machines with a support tray designed for receiving character plates to be fastened by attachment rings Lower part.

**How it works (reading):** The key base has openings that take either inserted character plates (riveted) or plugged-in moulded tops, with the openings also acting as alignment marks for the tops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key tops were either inserted metal plates or plugged-in moulded caps, forcing two different key stick types and stock sets.

**Solution:** The key base has openings that take either inserted character plates (riveted) or plugged-in moulded tops, with the openings also acting as alignment marks for the tops.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** key base; attachment rings; character plates; moulded key tops; alignment openings
