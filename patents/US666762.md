## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and has special reference to an improved tabulating attachment designed to secure speed and accuracy in tabulating work where it is desirable to write columns of words or figures.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating attachment that stops the carriage at any point in a column so the printing point can be located without the scales; the preview cuts off before the stop mechanism is described.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating on a standard typewriter is slow and depends on reading the machine scales, and existing attachments give only unit stops.

**Solution:** A tabulating attachment that stops the carriage at any point in a column so the printing point can be located without the scales; the preview cuts off before the stop mechanism is described.
