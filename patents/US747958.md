## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and has special reference to means for protecting or holding or for assisting in holding a card, sheet, page, or other work in the printing position.

**How it works (reading):** A clamp or guard engages the edge of the card, sheet or page to hold it on the platen while printing, protecting the work and holding it in position for the type-writing mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work on the platen can be displaced during printing, and a loose sheet needs a holder that keeps it in the printing position.

**Solution:** A clamp or guard engages the edge of the card, sheet or page to hold it on the platen while printing, protecting the work and holding it in position for the type-writing mechanism.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
