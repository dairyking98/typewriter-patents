## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line device for the platen of writing business machines, whose drive means at both ends of the platen arranged, interconnected handles can be done.

**How it works (reading):** Two handles at the platen ends drive the line index through four-bar linkages in front of the carriage, transmitting motion via a separate bar, usable from either hand.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-switch linkages running under the platen are awkward and expensive to install and need special paper carriage design.

**Solution:** Two handles at the platen ends drive the line index through four-bar linkages in front of the carriage, transmitting motion via a separate bar, usable from either hand.
