## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon rolls move bodily between the platens at print time, avoiding sliding friction, and their position sets a mechanism that reverses the ribbon feed when one roll is substantially exhausted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed did not work well with rotary platens, where the ribbon abraded, and the feed needed reliable reversal when a roll ran out.

**Solution:** The ribbon rolls move bodily between the platens at print time, avoiding sliding friction, and their position sets a mechanism that reverses the ribbon feed when one roll is substantially exhausted.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
