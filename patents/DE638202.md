## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Sets of carbon and writing sheets for typewriters shift relative to each other, and the forms cannot be separated cleanly after writing.

**Solution:** Wire clips hold the writing and carbon webs together in the perforation line between forms; the clips fall free when the set is cut, and the set folds in zigzag.
