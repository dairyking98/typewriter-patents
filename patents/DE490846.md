## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon and paper circuit for calculators) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means; Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**How it works (reading):** A paper roller and ribbon spool unit mounts on the outside of the machine housing so it can be attached or removed without disturbing the housing; a lever-driven pawl reverses the ribbon automatically at the end of the stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Attachable paper and ribbon units on calculating machines must be removed by taking off the housing, and the ribbon-reversal drive is awkward.

**Solution:** A paper roller and ribbon spool unit mounts on the outside of the machine housing so it can be attached or removed without disturbing the housing; a lever-driven pawl reverses the ribbon automatically at the end of the stroke.
