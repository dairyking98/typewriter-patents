## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Full keyboards are large and shift machines are slow.

**Solution:** Letter keys each control two typebars (small and capital) with no shift while the other keys drive one.

**How it works:** A full set of typebars is used, one per character. The alphabet keys each operate two types through a case selecting action, while the remaining keys operate one bar, combining the advantages of full-keyboard and shift machines. Smith Premier No. 10 is the base.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** key lever; typebar

**Improves on:**

- Smith Premier No. 10: alternative to shift
