## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Wants a simple, cheap and durable typewriter usable as an educational toy or a commercial machine.

**Solution:** Elastic spring plungers carrying a type at the bottom run through bent conduit tubes into a funnel so every type is directed to one printing point.

**How it works:** A three-tier keyboard of finger-pieces sits on stepped planes. Each key is on a plunger made of a close-wound flat piano wire spiral whose lower end carries a type, and each plunger runs through its own curved conduit tube. All tubes converge into a funnel that leads the type into a vertical collar over the paper rollers. A spiral spring raises each plunger after being pressed. The ink ribbon winds on rollers and passes between the collar and paper. Paper is moved sideways by a wheel, cam, ratchet and lever key.
