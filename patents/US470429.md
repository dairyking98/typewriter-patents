## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typists had to feed single sheets, and a paper roll behind the platen unbalanced the hinged carriage.

**Solution:** A roll-supporting frame holds a paper roll behind the platen so paper feeds continuously without unbalancing the carriage or hindering its hinge movement.

**How it works:** On a Caligraph, a roll-supporting frame is mounted at the rear of the machine and holds the roll behind the platen. The paper feeds forward to the platen over the paper table. Its attachment is fast to fit and remove, and variants are shown.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** roll-supporting frame; paper roll; platen; paper table; carriage; driving spring

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph: adds a rear paper roll without unbalancing the tilting carriage
