## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tension member driving a bell-crank typebar acts at poor angles except mid-stroke.

**Solution:** Tension member (wire) from the key lever engaging the typebar so that force acts uniformly favourably through the whole stroke, with a spring return.

**How it works:** A tension member pulled by the key lever swings the typebar; a spring in the opposite direction returns it. In older designs the member engages the lower arm of the bell-crank, so its angle goes from acute to obtuse and power transmission is good only mid-way. This design changes the connection point so that pressure acts evenly from start to end.
