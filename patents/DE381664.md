## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** Intermediate lever fulcrums rest freely on a console so the bearing tolerance can be looser, while keeping the variable lever length of the parent patent.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Intermediate lever bearings required a precise fit of axles and slots.

**Solution:** Intermediate lever fulcrums rest freely on a console so the bearing tolerance can be looser, while keeping the variable lever length of the parent patent.
