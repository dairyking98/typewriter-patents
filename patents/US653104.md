## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A person needs to record conversation discreetly from a pocket without writing.

**Solution:** A pocket recorder with spring-actuated keys, each carrying a type that prints a dot or dash on a paper tape wound between reels, over a cushion-block with an inking ribbon, by a dot-dash code.

**Features:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** spring-actuated keys; type on each key; paper tape; reels; cushion-block; inking ribbon

**Numbers:** Patent 653,104; Serial No. 10,701
