## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a typewriter counter that locks its number wheels outside the calculating zone, the known lock needed a special bracket on the sprung lever.

**Solution:** The existing locking flap that holds the tens-transfer levers also locks them via the stop-rail-controlled lever, so no extra part is needed.

**How it works:** A two-armed lever under a tension spring presses on the locking flap, which holds the tens-transfer levers in the disengaged position and so locks the number wheels outside the calculating zone. When the counter moves into the calculating position, a roller on the other arm runs onto a stop rail, turns the lever and frees the flap and levers. A comb guides the tens-transfer levers in their swing.
