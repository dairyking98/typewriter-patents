## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed rollers and paper-holding fingers on a front-strike typewriter needed even, adjustable pressure, a way to lift the rollers off the platen, and fingers that cannot be struck by the type.

**Solution:** A rocking roller cradle on a spring-loaded rock shaft carries the feed rollers and a curved paper guide, and sliding spring fingers with rollers hold the paper to the platen front and mark the printing line.

**How it works:** Rollers behind and in front of the platen run on shafts in rockers pivoted to adjustable arms on a rock shaft, forming a cradle. A coiled spring on the rock shaft presses the rollers to the platen, and its tension is set by a clamped sleeve. A lever pulled toward the operator depresses an arm on the shaft collar and rocks the cradle to withdraw the rollers for paper adjustment. A curved sheet-metal apron on the rockers is lightly sprung against the platen front with the rear hanging free for paper insertion. Sheet-steel fingers with small rollers, hinged on slides on a bar below the print line, are sprung against the paper and stay where placed by spring tongues. Their projections show the printing line on ruled paper, and stop pieces hit the type guide so the fingers never reach the printing point.
