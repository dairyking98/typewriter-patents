## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means; Tool movable to cooperate selectively with one of a plurality of mating tools

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in the construction and combination of elements hereinafter fully to be set forth and particularly as embodied in the claims which form a part hereof.

**How it works (reading):** A simple, compact perforating machine cuts the amount into the paper at any chosen spot, such as over the signature, so that the figures cannot be raised.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks, drafts and bonds can be fraudulently raised, and existing perforating machines are not simple, compact, quick and easy to operate.

**Solution:** A simple, compact perforating machine cuts the amount into the paper at any chosen spot, such as over the signature, so that the figures cannot be raised.
