## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (print hammer drive cash register)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on such print hammer drives, in which the hammer is driven by a lifting disc The tensioned spring is thrown from a limit position into the pressure position.

**How it works (reading):** The print hammer is held in its limit position by a pawl while its compression spring is tensioned, and the pawl is lifted by a two-part push rod linked to the lifting-disc intermediate piece so the hammer is released only during the print stroke; a lifting disc with three cams prints three times.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Print hammer springs kept tensioned during rest suffer fatigue from constant load.

**Solution:** The print hammer is held in its limit position by a pawl while its compression spring is tensioned, and the pawl is lifted by a two-part push rod linked to the lifting-disc intermediate piece so the hammer is released only during the print stroke; a lifting disc with three cams prints three times.

**How it works:** Lifting disc rotation first compresses the springs and holds the hammer back by the blocking nose and pawl; near the end of the stroke the pin lifts the pawl and the hammer is released.

**Parts:** print hammer; lifting disc; lifting cams; pawl; push rod; compression springs; tension spring; intermediate piece; levers; stop

**Numbers:** three lifting cams
