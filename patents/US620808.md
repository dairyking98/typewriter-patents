## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter should handle marginal notes, tabulating jump feed, margin lock and easier bearing adjustment, all from one key.

**Solution:** A single key controls carriage feed for marginal notes, keyboard lock at a set margin and a change from step feed to jump feed.

**How it works:** Key levers connect by ligaments to typebars, each pivoted on a transverse shaft with coned ends in a bracket, one cone being a screw-threaded slotted bearing adjusted by a tapered edgewise tool. The bracket base has lugs formed by dies and a screw-threaded tie bar prevents the walls spreading. A ribbon-spool arrangement and ribbon shift mechanism are shown. The parts of the carriage-feed control are described only in the missing remainder.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key lever; ligament; typebar; bracket; cone bearing; adjusting tool; bridge; tie bar; ribbon spool; ribbon shift
