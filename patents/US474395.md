## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar actuation should give each key uniform leverage and permit more typebars in the basket.

**Solution:** Each typebar is fixed at right angles to a curved oscillating shaft that is turned by a key through rods and elbow levers.

**How it works:** A curved shaft runs diagonally across the frame on a conical-screw bearing at one end and a socket at the other. The typebar projects inward from it. Pressing the key turns an elbow lever, horizontal rod, second lever and rod to a short arm on the shaft, rocking it so the bar swings up to the ribbon and paper. The arch of the curve contacts the frame to limit swing.
