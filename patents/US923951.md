## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be detached and replaced quickly without losing alignment with the platen axles.

**Solution:** Radially mortised hub on the platen head receives a tenoned axle end, and a locking disk on the head locks the platen in exact alignment for easy removal.
