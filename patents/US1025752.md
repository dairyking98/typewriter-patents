## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar actions use too many parts, are heavy and costly, and need a heavy touch and extra travel.

**Solution:** Each type bar action is compacted into fewer parts that give accelerated motion to the printing point, with a lighter key touch and a rebound lock.

**Features:**
