## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting column stops needs the space bar or carriage release plus careful watching, so exact vertical alignment for tables is slow.

**Solution:** A single laterally movable tabulating key shifts and adjusts a rocking stop rod to the chosen column point and releases the carriage, which then stops on an adjusted stop.
