## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Leader lines of stars, dots and underscoring need to be printed rapidly without repeated key strokes, in bills, statements and indexes.

**Solution:** An attachment prints liner characters concurrently with or by the travel of the carriage, using a liner type bar actuated from the carriage truck; two embodiments are described, one with a fine escapement adjustment and one with a different device.
