## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A torn or expired control strip on a cash register must lock the register.

**Solution:** Tension of the strip's tensioning mechanism is used to block the register when the strip tears or a sensor detects partial tearing.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Control strip conveyor device on cash registers or accounting machines The invention relates to such control strip conveyor devices on cash registers, in the case of the control strip apart from the line transport for the purpose of visualization the last line of print is given a reciprocation which according to the main patent is done in such a way that at one end of the control strip one at each Machine gear back and forth moved member attacks in a known manner and the control strip in cooperation with one attacking at its other end, on the strip a permanent tension exerting tensioning mechanism adjusted back and forth. According to the invention, the tensile effect of the tensioning mechanism is used to a device to block if the control strip is torn or expired to activate the cash register release. Another idea of the invention is that this locking device is also effective if it is detected by a sensor, that the control strip is partially torn.
