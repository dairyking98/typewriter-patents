## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In Webb's reverse-feed carriage spacing, slow or held key strokes let the carriage move while the type presses the paper, blurring the impression.

**Solution:** Rebuild the reverse feed so a slow or held key cannot blur: the dogs are jointed to the spacing rocker with a detaining dog so the carriage is detained while the type is against the paper.

**How it works:** A spacing rocker moved by the universal bar under the key levers carries a pivoted dog holder with a forward detaining dog (with offset and sprung joint for quiet return) and a spacing dog engaging the vertical feed rack. In reverse feed the carriage is released as the type strikes. Added jointed dog holder and detaining dog hold the carriage back while a depressed key keeps the type on the paper, and release it when the key is let go. Fast strokes still work as before since the typebar falls back by gravity.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** spacing rocker; universal bar; dog holder; forward detaining dog; spacing dog; feed rack; key lever

**Improves on:**

- Webb reverse feed application Serial 315,787: prevents blur when keys are operated slowly or held down
