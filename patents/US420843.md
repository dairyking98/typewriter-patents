## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Not clearly stated beyond providing a new type-writer construction; opening cuts off before the type nest.

**Solution:** Machine with paper on an advancing cylinder moved axially by a cam-grooved sleeve, and a type nest.

**How it works:** A shaft turns in a cam-grooved sleeve; a spring dog riding the grooves moves the paper cylinder along its axis stepwise each revolution (300 degrees of true circle, 60 degrees oblique). The cylinder has a rubber covering and a detachable clamp plate for paper. A sleeve with thumb-piece rides the bar and trips a bell near the end of travel. A hand-oscillated wheel with pawl turns a ratchet on the shaft. Type nest is described later and not in the text.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)

**Parts:** base; shaft; radial wing; cam-grooved sleeve; spring dog; paper cylinder; clamp plate; bell; ratchet wheel; pawl; type nest

**Numbers:** 300 degrees circular, 60 degrees oblique groove
