**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (chair armrest) · **Subject (patent classes):** Supports for the arms of detachable type

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an armrest for chairs, benches or the like, which, according to the invention is provided with a clamping bracket in such a way that it can be easily attached to any ordinary chair or the like, by screwing the clamping bracket directly onto the frame or the seat plate.
