## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE. CLASS 15: Printing. · The purpose of the present invention is to enable the blind to do one thing easily to be used apparatus to produce raised letters on paper. The machine made for this purpose is characterized in that two of the Hand made to be moved and firmly connected to one another so that they can rotate about a common axis Slices on the sides facing each other with those for making the raised Font required type matrix resp. Type patrix are provided, one of which (Female part) is firmly seated, while the other (male part) is 'movable' in such a way that the latter, displaced against the former, enter the relevant matrix and the raised letters on a sheet of paper between them can produce. The setting of the discs to the desired letter is made possible by that the disc serving as a die is on the edge of its upper side, the corresponding die opposite, is provided with raised letters; the blind now picks up with his finger the desired letter and using this finger turns the disc until the finger bump into a fixed stop, after which the exact setting of the disc by the now caused raising of the patrix takes place automatic…
