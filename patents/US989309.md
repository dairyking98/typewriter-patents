## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (duplicating copies) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to means for duplicating" copies of typewritten matter and the general object of said invention is to provide improved devices of the class specified.

**How it works (reading):** Three web rolls of paper and two carbon sheets are interleaved and fed together around the traveling platen so all copies print in one pass.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Solution:** Three web rolls of paper and two carbon sheets are interleaved and fed together around the traveling platen so all copies print in one pass.
