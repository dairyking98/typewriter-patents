## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The existing margin stop cannot be set quickly or held simply when the left margin must be regulated.

**Solution:** An auxiliary stop is mounted on the margin-regulating lever ahead of the rack stop, and a simple detent holds that lever in position against the stop.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
