## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**How it works (reading):** Build the frame from two loose side plates and front and rear crossbeams joined by flexible rubber plugs, with a central V-section crossbeam that encloses all the delicate mechanism in a downward-open cavity.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Small flat portable typewriters are shock-sensitive, noisy and their delicate mechanisms are exposed; rigid cast or plastic-shell frames break or warp.

**Solution:** Build the frame from two loose side plates and front and rear crossbeams joined by flexible rubber plugs, with a central V-section crossbeam that encloses all the delicate mechanism in a downward-open cavity.

**How it works:** Side walls are flat parts with rubber plugs near both ends; bolts with thickened heads on the front and rear crossbeams press into the plugs, giving a flexible articulated joint. A central V-section crossbeam is screwed to the side walls and carries the typebar bearing on balls held by springs, plus the shift lock, ribbon mechanism, key lever bearings, segment shift and carriage drive in its cavity. A sheet metal frame carries the key lever comb and bracket for intermediate levers. A roller-shutter cover runs from the rear crossbeam over the machine to the front crossbeam. Parts are meant to be moulded or pressed plastic.
