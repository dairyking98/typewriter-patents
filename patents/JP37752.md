## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies arranged in stationary or sliding cases or frames or upon flexible strips, plates, bars or rods; Japanese-language typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a printing machine in which, merely by pressing down a handle, a type bearing two engraved characters is fitted into a seal cylinder, and at the same time the seal cylinder is turned so that the character is printed, in a vertical column, on a printing roller mounted above and behind it.

**How it works (reading):** Pressing a handle lifts a loose two-character type from a tray into a swinging seal cylinder, which then turns to print it on a roller; a turn of the handle head changes lift height to choose the upper or lower character.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing seal-type printing machines hold few characters or are awkward to operate; a machine is wanted that holds many characters in small space and is simple to select and print.

**Solution:** Pressing a handle lifts a loose two-character type from a tray into a swinging seal cylinder, which then turns to print it on a roller; a turn of the handle head changes lift height to choose the upper or lower character.

**How it works:** Loose types stand in a flat tray, located by stop-strip pins, each with two characters on its side and index characters on its top. A bar frame moves forward and back on tray-edge grooves with a carriage sliding along it, carrying a seal cylinder over the wanted type. Pressing the handle turns a long shaft which, through levers and a fan gear, extends a support into the type's oblique hole and lifts it into the cylinder on arms; the type passes an inking roller, and the cylinder swings on curved grooves to print vertically on a roller above. Turning the handle head shifts an adjusting plate to change lift height and choose the other character. The frame and carriage are locked by a rack stop and notch claw during printing. The paper roller is advanced by a pawl and sawtooth wheel, with a bell and scales for line end.
