## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools were hard to mount, remove and keep centred.

**Solution:** Spools slip onto square-ended ribbon shafts carried by hinged levers with centring pins, with reversing feed.

**How it works:** Ribbon shafts have collars resting on bracket arms and square upper ends that turn the spools yet let them lift off. Pivoted levers carry centring pins, withdrawn by handles to free the spools. Reversing mechanism winds the ribbon first onto one spool then the other.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** ribbon shaft; spool; bracket; lever; centring pin; handle
