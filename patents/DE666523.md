## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an apparatus on with a Main ribbon or the like provided writing business machines for simultaneous Describe independent basal and front leaves.

**How it works (reading):** A feed device above the platen, with feed rollers and guides, pushes the front sheet between the main ribbon and an added ribbon carried below the infeed rollers, and the sheet is held in writing position automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing a front sheet over an uncovered base sheet with a ribbon, and reading the entries, is hard because auxiliary ribbons must be placed by hand between the sheets.

**Solution:** A feed device above the platen, with feed rollers and guides, pushes the front sheet between the main ribbon and an added ribbon carried below the infeed rollers, and the sheet is held in writing position automatically.
