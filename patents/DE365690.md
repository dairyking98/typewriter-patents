## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The upper part (frame with paper-carriage rails) had to be fitted with special bearing surfaces to keep the carriage parallel to the base.

**Solution:** The upper part rests on four corner pillars of the machine frame through openings in its frame, so the precisely machined rails set the carriage parallel; dowel pins in the pillars locate it laterally and it is removable.
