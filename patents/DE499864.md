## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a typewriter with an adding unit, switching between addition and subtraction, ribbon colour and type style by separate actions is error-prone and gives calculation errors.

**Solution:** A single debit key lever simultaneously shifts the carriage to the italic (slanted) figures, lifts the ribbon fork to red, and moves the adder's add/subtract button.

**How it works:** The debit key pivots on a bolt and its front arm carries a nose that presses a roller on the ordinary shift key, so depressing the debit key shifts the platen carriage via the shift lifting rod. The rear arm acts through an angle lever and a sliding link with an oblique control surface to lift the ribbon fork from blue to red. A slide articulated to the key arm has an inclined slot engaging a pin on the adding unit's add/subtract button, so pressing and holding the key moves the button to subtraction. Released, everything returns to addition, blue vertical figures. Only the slanted figures print in red.

**Improves on:**
