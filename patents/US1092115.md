## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A typist who wants carbon copies must handle carbon sheets and copy paper separately, and the copy cannot be cut into lengths easily.

**Solution:** A manifold attachment on the carriage feeds a web of carbon or manifold paper with a roll of copy paper, a cutter severs the copy sheet, and a spring drive is wound by the periodic advances of the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
