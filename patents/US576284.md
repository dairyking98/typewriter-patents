## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A circular-arrangement typebar machine (earlier application of 17 Feb 1893) needs a universal bar and typebar action that work from a ring of typebars.

**Solution:** An annular universal bar under a ring of typebars is depressed by a link on each typebar when it reaches the platen, and tripped key-levers throw the bars.

**How it works:** Typebars are pivoted in a ring-shaped top plate. Key-levers radiate from a cushioned ring and each has a vertical trip connecting to its typebar. A short forked link under each typebar pushes down the annular universal bar when the bar reaches the platen. The ring rocks a shaft and link that work the spacing and detaining dogs on the power-driven carriage rack.
