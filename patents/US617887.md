## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**How it works (reading):** A card-holder with spring legs passes through perforations at the platen ends and locks its bent ends onto interior platen parts, so it stays firm when attached and the platen still works for paper when it is removed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Removable card-holders on the platen were hard to attach and detach and could slip off in use.

**Solution:** A card-holder with spring legs passes through perforations at the platen ends and locks its bent ends onto interior platen parts, so it stays firm when attached and the platen still works for paper when it is removed.
