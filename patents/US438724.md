## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms; Radial cam

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to that class of typewriters (recent-ly invented by me) in which the type-wheels have independent orbital and axial rotary movements, and in which the mode of printing the character is by rolling the type therefor on the material to be printed, while said type-wheel has both of said movements.

**How it works (reading):** Type-wheels with independent orbital and axial rotation print by rolling the type on the paper, inked just before printing, with an automatic carriage in all its movements.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier orbit-and-roll type-wheel typewriters inked poorly and left carriage movements to the operator.

**Solution:** Type-wheels with independent orbital and axial rotation print by rolling the type on the paper, inked just before printing, with an automatic carriage in all its movements.

**How it works:** Type-wheels are given an orbital motion and an independent rotation about their own axis so the selected type rolls over the material. Key levers work latches in latch-frames to start the wheel, with a starting arm and a stop arm on the wheel shaft following simultaneous paths. The type is inked directly before printing. The carriage feeds automatically, returns automatically, and can also be moved by hand. Only the opening of a long specification was available, so detail is thin.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md)

- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** type-wheel; starting arm; stop arm; wheel shaft; latch; latch frame; key lever; carriage; feed mechanism

**Improves on:**

- inventor's earlier orbital type-wheel machine: inks type just before printing and automates the carriage
