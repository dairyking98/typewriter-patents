## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons are in use on typebar machines; the inventor wants a ribbonless inking method that fits any existing typewriter.

**Solution:** A sponge or absorbent pad on an adjustable arm bolted to the frame is placed so the typebar faces barely graze it on the way to the platen.

**How it works:** A standard bolted to the typewriter frame ends in a ball-and-socket joint locked by a thumb screw. An arm carries a screw-in socket holding an ink-soaked sponge. A rod with a thumb nut and a tension spring, fixed through a bracket on a plate, fine-adjusts the sponge position relative to the typebar path. The arm can be folded down out of the way and the sponge removed for re-inking.
