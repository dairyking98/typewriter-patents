## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in consisting of stamped parts Type lever guides with angled and roughly V-shaped slotted guide part at the level of the slot, an upper surface directed towards the platen of the angled guide part extending backwards, preferably heart-shaped To arrange a bead in which the guide slot extends straight back and down will.

**How it works (reading):** The stamped guide has an angled, roughly V-shaped slotted guide part, with a heart-shaped bead pressed into its upper surface so that the slot runs straight back and down, giving long straight guiding edges.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Thin stamped type lever guides with V-slots touch the type lever only at points or lines, so they wear quickly, let the type levers flutter and twist, and giving them thicker walls is costly.

**Solution:** The stamped guide has an angled, roughly V-shaped slotted guide part, with a heart-shaped bead pressed into its upper surface so that the slot runs straight back and down, giving long straight guiding edges.
