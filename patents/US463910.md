## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a type-writing machine in which the type are arranged above the printing roller or platen upon the under surface of a horizontal disk, the said disk being moved intoa given position foreach type thereon, the relative amounts of such movement depending upon the relative distances to which the key-levers are depressed.

**How it works (reading):** Type on the underside of a horizontal disc; each key's depth rotates a shaft a distinct angle to bring the disc to its character.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wanted a typewriter with a few keys that places a type disc by key travel.

**Solution:** Type on the underside of a horizontal disc; each key's depth rotates a shaft a distinct angle to bring the disc to its character.

**How it works:** The main frame carrying nearly all the mechanism is pivoted to a fixed bed and held up by a spring with adjustable stop. Keys are in three rows on levers of different lengths, mounted on a key-lever shaft by a feather in an oversized slot. Slots in a striking rib have different depths, so each key stops at a different depth and rotates the shaft through a unique angle. Wide slots avoid dragging all levers. A spring-loaded feather and return springs restore it. A device for spacing lines is shown.

**Features:** [ELEM:disc-face](../features/elem-disc-face.md), [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** main frame; fixed bed; spring; adjustable stop; key lever; key-lever shaft; feather; guiding and striking rib; key; type disc; line-spacing device

**Numbers:** three rows of keys
