## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon cartridges for endless ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Ribbon device for typewriters, in which the predominant part of a endless ribbon is stored in a container and used in the means of transport intended for removing the tape from the container and returning it to it are.

**How it works (reading):** The endless ribbon is packed in the container as tightly touching windings under pressure, so the whole supply is conveyed through the container and the pull on the ribbon stays low.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Endless fabric ribbons poorly absorb ink, and random folds in the storage container jam and stall the feed.

**Solution:** The endless ribbon is packed in the container as tightly touching windings under pressure, so the whole supply is conveyed through the container and the pull on the ribbon stays low.
