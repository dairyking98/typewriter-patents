## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in ribbon spool devices for typcwriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon spool built from a top and bottom flange joined by a barrel with engaging lugs, fitting a vertical ribbon shaft and suited to ribbon reverse attachments.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools are hard to fit and support, and cost too much to make.

**Solution:** A ribbon spool built from a top and bottom flange joined by a barrel with engaging lugs, fitting a vertical ribbon shaft and suited to ribbon reverse attachments.
