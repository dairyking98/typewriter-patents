## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Patented Dec. 13, 1927. UNITED STATES Des. 74,052 PATENT OFFICE. MAX GARBELL, OF CHICAGO, ILLINOIS. DESIGN FOR A FRAME FOR TYPEWRITING MACHINES. Application filed February 6, 1925. Serial No. 12,283. Term of patent 14 years. To all whom it may concern: design. The characteristic feature of the design comprises a top plate, the rear Vertical wall of which embodies in a continuous sweeping curve the sections adjacent the ribbon spool containers and the intermediate bowed section, said intermediate section merging at its center with the front vertical wall of the top plate. I claim: The ornamental design for a frame for typewriting machines, as shown and described. MAX GARBELL.
