## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper carriage switchgear with multiple switching for typewriters, typewriters and the like the circuit according to the desired step size preparatory switching bridge and a second switching bridge effecting the switching.

**How it works (reading):** The bridge that prepares the step size is driven by intermediate levers only after the type-lever knee joint has passed its dead centre, so the type-lever force moves the bridge; the bridge is a bar pivoted on one longitudinal edge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Selecting the carriage step size needs an extra key pressure, and the switching bridge is heavy and resists the key.

**Solution:** The bridge that prepares the step size is driven by intermediate levers only after the type-lever knee joint has passed its dead centre, so the type-lever force moves the bridge; the bridge is a bar pivoted on one longitudinal edge.

**How it works:** A bar pivoted on one edge forms the switching bridge; the intermediate lever engages it at the knee-joint dead centre so the printing stroke helps move it.
