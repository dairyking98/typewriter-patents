## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dust and grit falling into the type bar pivots make the type action sluggish, and the type bars can lateral-play or clash near the basket.

**Solution:** A type bar and hanger joint protected against dust, with guided type ears that prevent lateral play, easy bearings, and an improved restoring spring arrangement.
