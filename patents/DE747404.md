## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Rubber erasers wear, deform and let the eraser disc fall out, so erasing becomes inaccurate.

**Solution:** A steel knife roller is set in motion by a transmission and sits in a housing that protects it; the roller removes letters by scraping without wearing.
