## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple way to print by moving a type wheel over the paper without a full keyboard.

**Solution:** A type wheel and a matching index wheel are geared together on a lever; an arm swung round the index wheel to the wanted letter and carried to a fixed notched post turns the type wheel to that letter, and pressing the lever prints and then spaces the carriage.

**How it works:** The carriage rides on a perforated guide strip across the paper. A lever pivoted on the carriage carries a type wheel (letters on its edge) and, on top, an index wheel whose rim is divided into lettered spaces; the two are joined by bevel gears so they turn together. The operator drops a jointed arm pivoted at the index wheel's centre into the wanted space and swings it round until it drops into the slot of a fixed post, which turns both wheels to that letter. Pressing the lever brings the type wheel, inked by a roller, onto the paper. When the hand relaxes, a spring lifts the lever and the same motion works a ratchet and pawl that moves the carriage to the next perforation. Feed rollers carry the paper from line to line.
