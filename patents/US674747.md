## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter keyboards slow the operator and force looking at the keys; a layout allowing touch typing and high speed is wanted.

**Solution:** Arrange keys in left and right sections, grouped one group per finger, laid out on arcs around the thumb positions, with tactile stops on certain keys.

**How it works:** Existing key levers are replaced by detachable levers with keys placed so the fingertips of each hand sit as on a piano. Keys for each finger are set on arcs centred on the thumb keys, at different heights, so the hand pivots rather than moves. Some keys have upward projections or stops on various sides so they can be found by touch, even in the dark. The space key is among the thumb keys.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** key lever; key; stop projection; space key; frame

**Numbers:** 27 keys basic, up to 42 keys
