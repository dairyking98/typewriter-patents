## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**How it works (reading):** A matte, translucent glass disc covers the key symbol carrier, so it reduces glare and reflection while keeping the symbol visible.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Celluloid key-symbol covers yellowed, wore, and glared under artificial light.

**Solution:** A matte, translucent glass disc covers the key symbol carrier, so it reduces glare and reflection while keeping the symbol visible.
