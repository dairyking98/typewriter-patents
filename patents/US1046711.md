## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon winding and reversing on a front-stroke visible typewriter needs to be simple, strong and durable.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools driven by friction and cords from the carriage tension drum, with a shiftable shaft that reverses direction.

**How it works:** Spring-loaded hinged arms hold and retard each ribbon spool. A pawl and ratchet on the tension drum turns a friction disk only as the carriage feeds left. A shifting shaft with cord pulleys drives the right or left spool shaft. Pushing the shaft by a knob engages the other pulley and reverses the winding, helped by a snap spring and roller. A ball clutch stops the idle pulley, and a shifting rod moves a ribbon guide.

**Parts:** ribbon spool; hinged spring arm; tension drum; pawl; ratchet; friction disk; cord pulley; shifting shaft; ball clutch; universal bar
