## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**How it works (reading):** The spool has a hub with a detachable flange and a removable core on which the ribbon is wound, so cheap supply rolls can be fitted directly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools with reversing devices are expensive, so ribbons must be rewound by hand onto the working spools.

**Solution:** The spool has a hub with a detachable flange and a removable core on which the ribbon is wound, so cheap supply rolls can be fitted directly.

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
