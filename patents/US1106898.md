## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-spacing mechanisms of the day were complicated and gave only a fixed line-space increment; the inventor wants a simple, cheap hand lever that can give single or double spacing.

**Solution:** A hand-operated bell-crank pawl carrier carries a spring-raised pawl whose rest height is set by a two-step regulator lever, so the pawl idles through more or less of its stroke and advances the platen ratchet one or two teeth.

**How it works:** A bell-crank lever pivots on a vertical screw behind the platen at the right end bar; its long arm runs forward under the platen axle to a finger piece and its short arm sits behind the line-space ratchet wheel. A pawl pivoted on the short arm on a horizontal axis is pushed upward by a wire spring and normally clear of the ratchet. A regulator lever pivoted on the short arm has two stop faces at different heights; swinging it lets the spring lift the pawl tooth to the higher or lower rest height. Pushing the finger piece rightward swings the pawl into the underside of the ratchet; from the lower setting it idles over the longer face of one tooth and moves the platen one tooth, from the higher setting it engages the next tooth sooner and moves it two teeth. An abutment on the short arm hits the ratchet to limit the stroke, and a coil spring returns the lever against a rubber stop; the same swing also returns the carriage. A spring-loaded roller detent holds the platen between teeth.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** platen; line-space ratchet wheel; roller detent; bell-crank pawl carrier; finger piece; line-space pawl; pawl spring; regulator lever; abutment; restoring spring; rubber stop; platen carrier end bar

**Numbers:** one or two ratchet teeth per stroke; three working parts besides springs

**Improves on:**

- earlier Remington line-spacing mechanisms: only three working parts and an adjustable pawl height giving single or double spacing
