**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines;

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key action is slow with lost motion, and the printing point and bearing of each type-bar cannot be adjusted separately or the parts easily taken apart.

**Solution:** An auxiliary lever and connecting link move the type-bar from the key-lever with quick action, and a new journal and an adjustable hanger carry each type-bar, so the printing point and bearing can be set independently.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** key-lever; auxiliary lever; connecting link; type-bar journal; adjustable hanger; type-bar heel
