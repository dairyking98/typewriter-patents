## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates especially to improvements in the construction and arrange.

**How it works (reading):** A system of key levers, with type bars pivoted on a common wire in a segment and actuated through connecting links, does the work of combined levers without torsion on the type bars.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-lever and sub-lever combinations were needed to do the work, and they subjected type bars and actuating parts to serious torsional stress.

**Solution:** A system of key levers, with type bars pivoted on a common wire in a segment and actuated through connecting links, does the work of combined levers without torsion on the type bars.
