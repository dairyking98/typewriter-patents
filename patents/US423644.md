## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter that is simple, inexpensive, easy to operate and gives perfectly aligned writing.

**Solution:** A flat type-plate on a sliding carriage is moved by hand to an index letter-plate and held by a registering lever on a serrated bar, then struck from above by a printing lever.

**How it works:** The type-plate carriage slides on two longitudinal bars with a handle and carries an index that moves along a lettered plate on the frame. Two linked levers make the slides at each end move together, and the type-plate is screwed into brackets on those slides. A printing lever on a rock-shaft reaches over the plate and its stud strikes the top of the plate to print a single letter on paper on the platen. A registering lever with a V edge engages the serrated lower edge of a ratchet-bar to hold position, and a key-lever with a pawl acts on the ratchet-bar to move the paper carriage. The paper carriage has a feed roller with milled wheel, a miter-geared ratchet line feed, and a spring comb pressing the paper to the roller with adjustable clamp arm.
