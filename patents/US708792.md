## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to escapement mechanisms for typewriting and similar machines, and has for one object the avoidance of shock to the fingers of the operator and for another object the avoidance of blurred impressions, and other objects, as will hereinafter more fully appear.
