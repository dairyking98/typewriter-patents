## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Auxiliary copy ribbons must be tautened before a line break, and braking and locking happen inside the spool shaft.

**Solution:** The taut clamping device acts on the auxiliary ribbon spools from a supporting shaft outside the spools.

**How it works:** A tensioning device on the external support shaft tightens the auxiliary ribbons just before the line switch completes.
