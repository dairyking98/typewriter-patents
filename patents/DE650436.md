## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (gelatin ribbon duplicating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for indirectly duplicating from hectographic originals by means of hectographic intermediaries or transfer surfaces, i.e. "dry duplicators" with flat supports over which gelatin-paper is stretched

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a duplicating machine with a gelatin ribbon lying on a table and a parallel to the contact surface of the gelatin: eband, which carries the pressure roller Sleds.

**How it works (reading):** The pressure roller is made of sponge rubber, which conforms to tape and base irregularities without adjustment and is softer than the gelatin.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A hard or soft rubber pressure roller on a gelatin duplicator needs its own adjusting device and does not follow the thickness of the gelatin tape.

**Solution:** The pressure roller is made of sponge rubber, which conforms to tape and base irregularities without adjustment and is softer than the gelatin.
