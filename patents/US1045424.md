## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**How it works (reading):** A scale on a shield concentric with the platen is seen through a slot, and a mirror reflects it so the margin is read while the paper is in place.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The bottom margin left on the sheet is hard to see while typing.

**Solution:** A scale on a shield concentric with the platen is seen through a slot, and a mirror reflects it so the margin is read while the paper is in place.
