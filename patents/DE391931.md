**How it works (reading):** The carriage guide rests on fixed side stops and adjustable front stops and is held by one central rotary locking arm, which lifts off and goes back on quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage slide is awkward to remove for maintenance and cleaning.

**Solution:** The carriage guide rests on fixed side stops and adjustable front stops and is held by one central rotary locking arm, which lifts off and goes back on quickly.
