## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper is hard to position accurately and quickly for the first line, and the paper must be advanced over long distances or fed out when writing is complete.

**Solution:** An adjustable paper-advancing device with a pawl and driving parts that engage the line-space wheel on the platen, so the paper can be advanced to the first line or fed out of the machine.
