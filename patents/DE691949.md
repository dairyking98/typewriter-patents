## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register line advance)

**How it works (reading):** Release buttons and a coupling let the set-line arm be moved out of the way so the automatic indexing pawl can work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash-register document carriers cannot be both set by hand and automatically line-advanced.

**Solution:** Release buttons and a coupling let the set-line arm be moved out of the way so the automatic indexing pawl can work.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
