## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (indentations in printing plates) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** A carriage moved by a gear drive and crank with a circular scale gives a slow uniform shift set to the character spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Mechanical embossing of printing-plate typefaces needed uneven spacing; stop rings for distance were fragile and error-prone.

**Solution:** A carriage moved by a gear drive and crank with a circular scale gives a slow uniform shift set to the character spacing.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)
