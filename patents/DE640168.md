## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (railway car air heating) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard; Means for heating only

**How it works (reading):** A room contact thermometer switches an evaporating-liquid thermal relay that moves a spring body gradually, which adjusts the hot and cold air ducts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A sudden on-off steam or air valve gives drafts, and a sensor in the room cannot slowly control the air heater.

**Solution:** A room contact thermometer switches an evaporating-liquid thermal relay that moves a spring body gradually, which adjusts the hot and cold air ducts.
