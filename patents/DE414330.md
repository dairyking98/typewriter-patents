## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Booking work needs several columns of a sheet written at once, which a single typewriter cannot do.

**Solution:** Several groups of number-printing devices are arranged side by side, each group independently coupled to or uncoupled from a common key device, with a mechanical version and an electrical transmission version, and devices disengage automatically at the line end.

**How it works:** Each group has its own carriage and printing devices, and a common keystroke device can be coupled to each group in turn.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
