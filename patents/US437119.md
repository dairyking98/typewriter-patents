## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A light, compact, inexpensive and efficient typewriter is wanted.

**Solution:** A compact machine with a small two-row piano-style keyboard of 29 keys that partly rotates a type-wheel, with cap and figure keys, spacing key, an inking spool and a carriage at the rear.

**How it works:** The keyboard of 29 keys sits in two rows, the rear row higher; 26 keys carry a letter and a punctuation mark or special character, the front centre key is the spacer and two rear centre keys are CAP and FIG shifts. Each key, when depressed, moves a notched transverse bar endwise, partly rotating the type-wheel to a position set by the key. The whole keyboard frame with type-wheel and inking spool is hinged on pins so it can be thrown forward to inspect the writing on the impression roller of the carriage at the rear. A line-spacing mechanism is at the right end. Only the opening of a long specification was available.

**Features:** [SEL:dummy-keyboard](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** key; keyboard frame; notched bar; type-wheel; cap key; figure key; spacing key; inking spool; ribbon; impression roller; carriage; bed plate; line-spacing mechanism

**Numbers:** 29 keys; 26 character keys
