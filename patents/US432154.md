## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On typebar machines with two or more characters per bar, the case shift usually needs a complicated moving carriage.

**Solution:** Mount the typebar basket and key-levers on a frame that slides on the main frame, shifted by elbow levers on which the upper-case and lower-case key-levers rest.

**How it works:** A sliding frame carries the basket with pivoted typebars and the key-levers, linked to the bars. Two elbow-levers pivoted to this frame and to the base have short arms with pins, one forward and one rearward, on which the lower-case and upper-case key-levers rest. Depressing a lower-case key tips the levers forward, so the frame places the lower-case type over the printing point; an upper-case key throws them back. A spring may hold the frame in either position. The carriage then has only paper feed on it.
