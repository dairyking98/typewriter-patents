## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The universal bar that operates the escapement on every key stroke needs to be smooth, light and quiet.

**Solution:** A tubular fixed fulcrum for the key levers with a coaxial rock shaft inside it on ball bearings; teeth on each key lever pass through slots in the tube and rock the shaft, which operates the escapement.

**How it works:** Key levers pivot on a fixed tube with recesses on their lower edges. Inside, a rock shaft in ball bearings has a longitudinal slot. A tooth on each key lever extends through a slot in the tube into the shaft slot, so any key depressed rocks the shaft, which operates the escapement and other parts moved at every stroke.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** fulcrum tube; rock shaft; ball bearing; key lever; type bar; sub-lever
