## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangement of mixed bristles or tufts of bristles, e.g. wire, fibre, rubber

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is a steel wire brush for cleaning typewriter types.

**How it works (reading):** Steel wire bristles are softened at their tips and bent to a knee so they press perpendicular to the type face while the resilient shank keeps pressure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Steel brushes can damage soft typebar and type surfaces while cleaning dirt out of the type.

**Solution:** Steel wire bristles are softened at their tips and bent to a knee so they press perpendicular to the type face while the resilient shank keeps pressure.
