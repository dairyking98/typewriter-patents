## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Means for displaying the edge markings on Braille strips The present invention relates to a device according to patent 7i5 203 for marking Braille strips for blind typists and locating or displaying aims in particular such edge markings. While so far these marginal markings (excerpts) from the blind Users had to be located by feeling the edges of the strips with their fingers, The invention provides a device that, in addition to the means for convenient attachment and Unwinding the braille strip is the means for quick and easy retrieval which contains edge markings. The features of the invention are set out below in FIG. Order of Operation described. The crankshaft c mounted on the frame has a clamp f to Fasten the strip to be wound up, the overturned end of the strip when it is wound up takes it with it, but releases it again when turned to the left, making it easy to wind up or removal of the strip angle permitted. A simple pushing out or insertion of the crankshaft in their Storage is made possible by the arrangement of grooves g and leaf springs lt.
