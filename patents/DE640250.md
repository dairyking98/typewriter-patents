## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Worn friction linings grab harshly, causing judder, because the impregnant is burned out of the surface.

**Solution:** A worm-driven piston in a container meters regenerant liquid drop by drop onto the friction surfaces, driven by the engagement movement.
