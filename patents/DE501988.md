## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (setting unit for tabulating machines)

**How it works (reading):** Sliding frames carrying intermediate rods are moved by a punched card identification hole so that one of two stop members is lifted, without changing the sectors or stop links.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stop members for twice the number of characters would need the type and tooth sectors of a tabulator to be changed, and the value spacing cannot grow.

**Solution:** Sliding frames carrying intermediate rods are moved by a punched card identification hole so that one of two stop members is lifted, without changing the sectors or stop links.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Type sector or segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** sliding frames 121; stop members 14; actuators 120; intermediate control rod
