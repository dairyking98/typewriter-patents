## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is a device on writing and booking machines for displaying the last line of a sheet of paper to be written on, in which the circuit of an electrical connected to a power source Signal bell closed after writing the last line ".

**How it works (reading):** A battery, bell and slip-ring contact are housed inside a hollow platen, and a contact roller closes the circuit when the sheet's lower edge passes, so the bell rings at the last line; the whole platen swaps between machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing last-line warning devices mount the power source and bell on the frame where they are exposed to damage and must be altered per machine.

**Solution:** A battery, bell and slip-ring contact are housed inside a hollow platen, and a contact roller closes the circuit when the sheet's lower edge passes, so the bell rings at the last line; the whole platen swaps between machines.
