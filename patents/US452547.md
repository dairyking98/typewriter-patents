## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in card-writing attachments to type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A detachable card-holding attachment for Remington, Smith Premier and Standard machines with adjustable guides that hold a card in alignment on the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards cannot be held square and aligned on the paper roll so the whole card can be written.

**Solution:** A detachable card-holding attachment for Remington, Smith Premier and Standard machines with adjustable guides that hold a card in alignment on the platen.
