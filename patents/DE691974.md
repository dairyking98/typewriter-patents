## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on writing office machines for creating the paper roller in the direction of the type imprint, with softer one in the paper feed engaging sensing element to maintain a uniformly strong type imprint different.

**How it works (reading):** An unloaded sensing element is brought into position with a handle and the platen is set on stops, so the paper gap is set without moving the full mass.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting the platen for paper thickness moves the whole carriage mass, which is sluggish and can let the sensing roller pressure carry through carbon.

**Solution:** An unloaded sensing element is brought into position with a handle and the platen is set on stops, so the paper gap is set without moving the full mass.
