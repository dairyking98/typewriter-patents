## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (telegraph with moving type lever basket) · **Subject (patent classes):** tabulating

**How it works (reading):** Rectangular intermediate rails move in the plane of their wide faces on the fixed part and transfer selector settings positively to the selector rails of the moving basket, keeping the selector gear fixed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A teleprinter with a moving type-lever basket needs selector flaps that bend under force or carry heavy evaluation parts on the carriage.

**Solution:** Rectangular intermediate rails move in the plane of their wide faces on the fixed part and transfer selector settings positively to the selector rails of the moving basket, keeping the selector gear fixed.
