## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is an elastic one Typewriter silencers.

**How it works (reading):** A housing with an internal groove holds a multi-tongued expanding spring that carries the foot load and spreads vibration to the housing, with an interchangeable spring for different weights.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machine vibration and noise were carried into the table through the feet.

**Solution:** A housing with an internal groove holds a multi-tongued expanding spring that carries the foot load and spreads vibration to the housing, with an interchangeable spring for different weights.
