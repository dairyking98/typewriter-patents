## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed needs a simple, efficient mechanism that reverses automatically.

**Solution:** Driving gears on swinging hangers on each side are engaged alternately with the spool pinions by a click-held toothed rod, tripped by cam carriers on the spools.

**How it works:** Ribbon spools sit in cups in the top plate corners, each with a spindle and gear pinion. Counter-rotating shafts on each side of the machine, driven from a rear cross shaft, end in driving pinions on swinging hangers. A rod joining the hangers lets only one pinion engage a spool at a time and carries a three-toothed projection held by a spring click. Arms under each spool carry a semicircular cam carrier that, when the ribbon runs out, swings the hanger rod over to shift drive to the other spool.
