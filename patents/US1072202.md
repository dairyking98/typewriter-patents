## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding a continuous paper web from rolls to the platen is awkward and the paper does not run straight, and the manifold ribbon reels need a fixed course.

**Solution:** A suspended rack carries the paper rolls and reciprocates with the carriage so the paper always feeds straight to the platen; a stationary cutting edge tears off written sheets and stationary means rotate the ribbon reels at the end of each throw.
