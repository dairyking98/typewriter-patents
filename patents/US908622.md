## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object is to guide the leading edge of the paper away from the platen at the proper tune, soas to avoid liability of the paper fouling with certain parts ofthe paper- I guiding and other carriage mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A stripper and adjustable paper guide at the front of the platen guide the paper away from the platen at the right moment, with a scale pressed against the platen by springs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The leading edge of the paper can foul parts of the paper carriage after it leaves the printing point.

**Solution:** A stripper and adjustable paper guide at the front of the platen guide the paper away from the platen at the right moment, with a scale pressed against the platen by springs.
