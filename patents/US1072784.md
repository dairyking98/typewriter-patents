## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar action with constant leverage gives a heavy start; also keyboards are hard to level with rigid key lever connections.

**Solution:** A key lever drives a sublever through a rocker stud sliding in a slot, so leverage decreases through the stroke, with the rocker adjustable on the key lever.

**How it works:** The typebar's angle arm is linked by a wire to the sublever. A rocker fixed at an angle on the key lever carries a stud that sits in a slot in the sublever's end. As the key goes down the stud moves inward along the slot toward the sublever pivot, reducing leverage: the typebar accelerates away from rest and then travels steadily under decreasing leverage to print. A slotted fastening and steady pin let the rocker be shifted to regulate acceleration and align key levers.
