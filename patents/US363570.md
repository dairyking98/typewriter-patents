## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simpler typewriter is wanted in which letters are carried on a comb and pressed to the paper, with a uniform step-by-step carriage motion matching the letter spacing.

**Solution:** A pointer-and-key selects a comb tooth while the same key stroke swings a notched rack against a segmental pawl that advances the impression carrier one space.

**How it works:** A sliding, swinging type carrier bears a comb whose teeth have the letters on their under surface. The operator slides the carrier until a pointer is over the letter, then presses the key; the pointer blade engages a notched rod at the tooth under that letter. Further pressure swings the rod onto a weighted moon-shaped pawl on the impression-arm carrier, driving it one space along. At the same time a finger pushes the chosen comb tooth down so an inking roll presses it onto the paper. A counterweight shifted to the other side of the pawl reverses the stepping direction.
