## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper feeding over the platen must hold the sheet so writing stays in view of the operator.

**Solution:** Frusto-conical paper clip rolls are mounted on the platen roll and form a paper feeding mechanism that holds the sheet against the platen while it is fed.
