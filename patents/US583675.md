## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars wobble sideways and strike at inconsistent points.

**Solution:** A fixed arc-shaped guiding arm on each bar's bracket passes between the two plates of the typebar, forcing it to move in one vertical plane.

**How it works:** Each typebar is two parallel sheet-metal plates pivoted at its lower end on a bracket under a ring frame and linked to a key lever. A guiding arm extends from the bracket along an arc centred on the bar pivot and runs between the plates, preventing side motion so the type strikes at the same point.
