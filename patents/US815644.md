**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Supports or magazines for piles from which articles are to be separated adapted to support articles substantially horizontally, e.g. for separation from top of pile

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention has reference to means for adator will be notified when the sheet of paper haspassed a predetermined position.

**How it works (reading):** A graduated arc-shaped indicator on the carriage is paired with a paper-contact and a hand-set guide point; when the sheet passes the contact a signal (a glow-lamp circuit in Fig. 1, or mechanical movements in Figs. 5-6) tells the operator where the line falls.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must watch for the end of the sheet and the line position after the last desired line, which is a mental burden.

**Solution:** A graduated arc-shaped indicator on the carriage is paired with a paper-contact and a hand-set guide point; when the sheet passes the contact a signal (a glow-lamp circuit in Fig. 1, or mechanical movements in Figs. 5-6) tells the operator where the line falls.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** platen-roller A; shaft B; insulated shaft C; arc-shaped indicator; paper-contact; hand-controlled guide point; glow-lamp
