## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to platen releasing mechanism for typewriting machines and has for its main object to provide improved devices for connecting and disconnecting the platen from its line space `wheel whereby said platen is enabled to be turned, at the will of the operator, irregular distances in dependent of said line space wheel.
