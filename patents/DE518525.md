## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by denominational arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a table writing device for typewriters with rotatable on an axis, laterally fixed column stops, which are divided into two in the working position one and the same rail located chimney rows lead and with projections and Detents for adjusting and locking are provided.

**How it works (reading):** The stop axle sits close to the rail of comb rows and spacer plates tie axle and rail into a rigid frame, while a feather comb with resilient prongs detents every stop in both end positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Table column stops lack strength and accuracy, and their rest positions are not reliably held.

**Solution:** The stop axle sits close to the rail of comb rows and spacer plates tie axle and rail into a rigid frame, while a feather comb with resilient prongs detents every stop in both end positions.
