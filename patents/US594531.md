## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to adapt the platen of a type-writer to hold a strip or block in position so that it may receive the impress of the type.

**How it works (reading):** Jaws on the platen, one fixed and one spring-loaded, clamp a block between them.
