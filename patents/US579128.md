## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hard platen faces cause sharp impressions but crack, and soft faces wear and blur, so neither is ideal.

**Solution:** A platen facing with an inner layer of soft-vulcanizing rubber and an outer layer of hard-vulcanizing rubber.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
