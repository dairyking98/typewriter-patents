## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a bearing block, in particular for typewriters with an overstop, in which the type lever and the connection of the same with the key fogs mediating intermediate lever in a bearing block made of superimposed, concentric and radially slotted bearing, bearers rest.

**How it works (reading):** The bearing block is made of two segment-shaped carriers joined by stud bolts, cast in one piece and then slotted and separated, so the slots of associated levers lie on the same radius through the point of impact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bearing slots for type levers and intermediate levers are hard to align exactly radial to the point of impact, causing rapid wear and damage to the lever linkage.

**Solution:** The bearing block is made of two segment-shaped carriers joined by stud bolts, cast in one piece and then slotted and separated, so the slots of associated levers lie on the same radius through the point of impact.
