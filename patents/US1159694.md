## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (justification symbols in register bands) · **Subject (patent classes):** Methods or arrangements for marking the record carrier in digital fashion by punching

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a method of punching perforated symbols in register bands, having error symbols, for operatlng, writing, type-setting and type-casting machines, and apparatus therefor.

**How it works (reading):** Justification symbols are punched as perforations in the register band for a machine that operates from the band, with error symbols recorded in the band.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Justification of lines for type-setting machines is done only after the text has been symbolically perforated in a register band, which makes the process slow.

**Solution:** Justification symbols are punched as perforations in the register band for a machine that operates from the band, with error symbols recorded in the band.

**Features:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
