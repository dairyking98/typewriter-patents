## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A key-bar needs to give a graded series of throws, and the existing bars do not provide this.

**Solution:** Keys of different lengths, or equal lengths, are mounted transversely across a key-bar above a base, so each key that is depressed sets a graduated throw of the bar.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
