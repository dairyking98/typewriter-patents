## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Separate sets of typebars for upper and lower case would need two sets of keys.

**Solution:** One set of keys with a shifting guide bar lets pull rods engage either the upper-case or lower-case typebar pull rods.

**How it works:** Upper and lower case typebars are pivoted side by side, each with its own horizontal pull rod ending in a head. A series of shift pull rods from the key bell-cranks end in heads beside these. Depressing the shift key rocks a shaft and raises the guide bar and the inner ends of the shift pull rods so their heads face the upper-case heads. The weight of the guide bar returns them without a spring.
