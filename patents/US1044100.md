## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide for locking .

**How it works (reading):** A stop bar guides set stops during carriage movement, stop locking holds the carriage during stop ejection, and a single-key tabulator can be swapped for a decimal one.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stop guiding and locking are inadequate, and a single-key tabulator should interchange with a multi-key one.

**Solution:** A stop bar guides set stops during carriage movement, stop locking holds the carriage during stop ejection, and a single-key tabulator can be swapped for a decimal one.
