## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special supports, platforms or trolleys for supporting machines on tables

**How it works (reading):** Disc-shaped coasters pinned through the felt spread the feet load over a larger area, and rubber washers on the underside stop the pad sliding.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber feet of a heavy typewriter dent a felt pad and the pad slides on the table.

**Solution:** Disc-shaped coasters pinned through the felt spread the feet load over a larger area, and rubber washers on the underside stop the pad sliding.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** felt pad; coasters; rubber washers
