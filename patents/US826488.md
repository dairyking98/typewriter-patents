**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to e-writers, and particularly to a' combination work-holder and rimitiearbon carrying and holding device.

**How it works (reading):** A combined work-holder and carbon carrier, linked to one operating mechanism, raises the carbon and the work to different heights and clamps the work to the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets and work sheets must be separated, lifted and clamped by hand each time paper is changed on flat-platen machines.

**Solution:** A combined work-holder and carbon carrier, linked to one operating mechanism, raises the carbon and the work to different heights and clamps the work to the platen.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
