## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a simple attachment for type writing machines for continuously copying matter written on the machine, thereby producing an exact duplicate of the work done.

**How it works (reading):** Folding copy attachment with take-up roller and hook brace, hung on the table or desk.
