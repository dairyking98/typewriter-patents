## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lettering large maps and drawings by hand is slow and costly, and ordinary typewriters cannot handle such large sheets.

**Solution:** A typewriter that writes on a flat surface without cylindrical platens, placed on the map and quickly shifted between positions so it can print parallel to an edge or at an inclination.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)
