## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage must be positioned rapidly at a chosen letter-space in either direction.

**Solution:** A settable stop on the carriage and a counter-stop on a stop-bar arrest the carriage at the same letter-space in either direction, freed from the escapement.
