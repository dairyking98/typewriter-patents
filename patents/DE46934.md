## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter with a small keyboard yet many characters, with visible writing.

**Solution:** Three rows of keys act through pressure pins guided by a rotatable pin wheel to pick among three times as many radially slotted type levers.

**How it works:** Keys on weighted key levers (no springs) press vertical pins; the pin wheel in different rotated positions presents each pin under a different one of three type levers, so a small keyboard yields triple the characters. Metal type levers slide in radial slots in a conical cover plate on a wire ring and print upward through a central hole against a step-fed paper sledge. The text also covers visible writing, paper holding, sledge release, type inking, line spacing and an alarm bell. Only the opening is available.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SHIFT:rotary](../features/shift-rotary.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** base plate; key lever; key button; pressure pin; pin wheel; cover plate; radial slot; type lever; wire ring; paper sledge

**Numbers:** radial slots extend about 20 mm inward; 3 type levers per pin
