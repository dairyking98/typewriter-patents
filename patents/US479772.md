## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix making from copy) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of our invention is to provide an improved mechanism by means of which the matrix may be made from the copy by the successive impression in the matrix material of dies corresponding to the required characters, instead of setting up type and then forming the matrix from the bed or forms of type.

**How it works (reading):** Character-selecting devices bring dies to a common center by group, with a movable carriage and independent feed devices.
