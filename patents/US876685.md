## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter platen carriage runs noisily and is hard to remove for cleaning and repair.

**Solution:** Mount the platen carriage on ball-bearing rollers and rails with a sliding lock so platen or carriage can be detached.

**How it works:** The carriage and platen sit at the back on antifriction ball-bearing rollers running on guide rails. A top guide rail hung on parallel-motion links carries the platen forward and back for capital and figure shifts, with a shift-key spring returning it. A locking slide under the platen frame fastens or releases platen and carriage from the body. A spring-driven step-by-step feed connects to the carriage, and a silencing spring quiets the escapement. Only the first part of the description is available.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** platen; carriage; ball bearing roller; guide rail; parallel motion link; shift key spring; locking slide; margin-stop rail
