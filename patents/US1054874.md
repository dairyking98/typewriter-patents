## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen-shift Yost No. 15 is noisy and the shifted part can vibrate sideways.

**Solution:** Convert it to a shifting typebar basket guided and locked against lateral vibration, with spring return.

**How it works:** The type actions (key lever, link, sub-lever, pull link, driver, typebar, guide link) are carried in a basket that shifts for case instead of the platen. Locking links hold the shifted part against vibration in both normal and shifted positions. The same locking means are usable on platen-shift machines. Each typebar has a lower-case type in line with the stem and an offset upper-case type, and rests on an ink pad.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** shifting basket; typebar; type block; key lever; sub-lever; pull link; driver; guide link; locking link; ink pad; platen

**Improves on:**

- Yost No. 15: converts platen shift to typebar basket shift without redesigning the type actions
