## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (shunt motor coil arrangement) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a shunt motor in which the excitation coil in subdivided several parts connected in series, partly with variable number of turns is and these parts are arranged spatially offset from one another on the field magnet are that several force fields of different magnetic reluctance arise;

**How it works (reading):** Excitation coil split into series-connected parts placed on offset projections of the field magnet, so several flux paths of different reluctance give speed changes without control resistors.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text describes a shunt motor with a subdivided field coil; the stated benefit is fitting the motor into small hand tools without gears.

**Solution:** Excitation coil split into series-connected parts placed on offset projections of the field magnet, so several flux paths of different reluctance give speed changes without control resistors.
