## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with movable counter)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with movable counter, devices to add numbers to the one in the computing zone To transfer the counter, buttons to operate these devices and organs, which prevent the keys from being hit if the counter is set incorrectly.

**How it works (reading):** Locking devices on the digit keys are controlled by a swinging frame that engages when the main counter is in the wrong position, so those keys cannot be struck.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Number keys struck where digits do not belong (decimal places, separators between counters) can enter wrong values into an adder on a typewriter.

**Solution:** Locking devices on the digit keys are controlled by a swinging frame that engages when the main counter is in the wrong position, so those keys cannot be struck.

The establishment is such in such machines that the adding device is only in operation occurs when the typewriter carriage is in the compute zone. In particular such machines are suitable for issuing invoices during their preparation one first the various details, z. B. Date, subject, etc., write down and then the slide is expediently moved through the tabulator into the computation zone, where the individual amounts are written down one below the other. It is clear that precaution must be taken here, that number items are not written in places where they do not belong. This is for example the case at the decimal places, the space between the main and column counter, the separating fields between thousands and hundreds, etc. In these places is it is necessary to lock the buttons.
