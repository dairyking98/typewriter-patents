## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide a stop for limiting the action of the machine while writing on the sheet of paper, which, however, after it stops the machine can be set to write another line or a number and will be automatically reset Without further attention, especially during the return movement of the paper-feed.

**How it works (reading):** A spring on the platen shaft winds as the platen turns; releasing the platen lets the spring return it and eject the sheet, with an adjustable stop limiting rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing must stop at a chosen point on the sheet, paper-feed travel must be limited both ways, and sheets must be discharged mechanically.

**Solution:** A spring on the platen shaft winds as the platen turns; releasing the platen lets the spring return it and eject the sheet, with an adjustable stop limiting rotation.
