## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key lever bearing for stenography machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a key lever mounting for stenography machines, in which the key lever swing around a common axis.

**How it works (reading):** Each key lever runs between two rigid flat discs on a common pivot axis, with cross pieces and screws holding the discs in a solid guide block, so no combs or adjustment are needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Key levers of stenotype keyboards need combs that must be adjusted tooth by tooth to let the levers move freely.

**Solution:** Each key lever runs between two rigid flat discs on a common pivot axis, with cross pieces and screws holding the discs in a solid guide block, so no combs or adjustment are needed.
