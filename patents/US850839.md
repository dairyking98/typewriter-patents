## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to means whereby the operator may select and operate any particularV one ofv a seriesA of column-stops, and thereby cause the release of the carriage and its arrest by the selected stop.

**How it works (reading):** Column stops on a bar at the front of the machine are each turned into position by pressing them; pressing one moves the bar back so the stop meets a carriage stop, releasing the carriage and arresting it at the chosen column.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator needs a way to skip to chosen columns on a tabulated page, with the stops visible in front of the keyboard.

**Solution:** Column stops on a bar at the front of the machine are each turned into position by pressing them; pressing one moves the bar back so the stop meets a carriage stop, releasing the carriage and arresting it at the chosen column.
