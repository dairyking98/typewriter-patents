## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to and its object is to provide means for overcoming the difficulty here pointed out, and more particularly to so construct type for type-writing machines that the shoulder or ledge above referred to shall be dispensed with and so that type will free themselves from adhering lint, gum, and the like.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Raised type with a shoulder collects lint and ink gum, so letters blur and need frequent cleaning.

**Solution:** Type are bevelled from the face margin backwards so there is no ledge, and enclosed spaces are cut right through.

**How it works:** Each type piece, on a lug or pin socketed into the typebar end, has its body chamfered from the margin of the face backward. Enclosed counters in o, a, b, d, e, g, p are cut right through from face to back, widening toward the back. Partly enclosed shapes such as c, u, m, n, s, y are cut away at broken junctions. Straight-line letters are bevelled only. Enough metal is left for strength and connection.

- flat-faced raised type: removes the shoulder that traps lint and gum
