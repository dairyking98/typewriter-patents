## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-stroke visible typebar machines need a simple action with a quick, powerful print stroke and a quick return of the typebar.

**Solution:** An intermediate sub lever between key lever and typebar connects to the typebar at three points that act at the start, middle and end of the stroke.

**How it works:** Each unit has a hinged key lever, a hinged typebar and a hinged sub lever, which has a pin-and-slot connection with the key lever. Depressing the key rocks the sub lever forward and its front edge pushes a pin on the typebar to start it upward. As the typebar rises, a second pin meets a shoulder in the sub lever's slot and forces it to the printing position. On release the sub lever rocks back and a pin on it pushes a shoulder on the typebar to throw it back quickly, clear of neighbouring typebars. A universal bar under the keys works the escapement rocker.
