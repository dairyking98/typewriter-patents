## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Billing work needs accurate platen rotation without calculation, and the platen should be locked until the sheet is in.

**Solution:** Platen rotated by a spring and a hand crank, with locks and a release that let a set amount of rotation be repeated.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
