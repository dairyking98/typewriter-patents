## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Motorcycle frames need stiffness, a housing for the engine and gear, and room for passengers.

**Solution:** The frame is a cast hollow body split into two vertical shells joined by flanges, with ribs, transverse walls and a closed fuel and rear wheel room, so it can also be built as a tricycle.
