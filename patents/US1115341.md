## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inking ribbons by hand is slow, uneven and wasteful, and unskilled office staff cannot do it well.

**Solution:** A standalone ribbon inking machine passes the ribbon between pressure rollers, with pressure set for ribbon width and ink depth, and rewinds it onto a spool.

**How it works:** Rollers press ink into the ribbon as it is drawn through; spool supports are adjusted to suit ribbon width and spool size.
