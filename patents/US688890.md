## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to escape to ment mechanisms for type-writing machines and also to means for locking the escapement against further forward motion at a predetermined point in the forward traverse of the carriage and for preventing the impress of I5 type upon the paper after such locking.

**How it works (reading):** A holding dog with two angled faces locks the escapement at a set point in the carriage travel and prevents impression after locking.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement holding dogs let the carriage move past the point where printing should stop, and type still strikes after locking.

**Solution:** A holding dog with two angled faces locks the escapement at a set point in the carriage travel and prevents impression after locking.

**How it works:** The stiff holding dog and feed dog engage the rack; a locking face stops forward motion at a predetermined point and blocks further printing.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** holding dog; feed dog; rack
