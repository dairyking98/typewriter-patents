## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known momentary switches for carriage-return motors load the carriage with a strong spring that is tensioned by the carriage movement, so the carriage is heavily loaded.

**Solution:** The key lever itself tensions the switch spring during key depression, so the contact arm is pivoted against its spring and snaps into the closed position at the end of the stroke, relieving the carriage.

**How it works:** A movable part of the key linkage slides the contact arm against its spring; at the end of travel the arm slides off and snaps shut, closing the motor circuit; catch springs hold the arm until the carriage reaches its end position.
