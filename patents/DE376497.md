> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a desk with a retractable support plate for typewriters o. The like. In which the support plate is guided on an inclined guide by means of a slide. It consists in that the support plate or the slide with locking devices under spring pressure are provided that enable the support plate to be fixed at different heights. The support plate can therefore be adjusted according to the size of the people working on the plate. According to the invention, a foot protection plate can also be used be provided with approaches such in the path of the slide or the support plate engages that it when pulling up or returning the support plate is brought into the use or rest position. Because of the special connection this foot protection plate with the support plate, it will adjust itself accordingly the position of the support plate.
