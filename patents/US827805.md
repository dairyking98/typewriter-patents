**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**How it works (reading):** A loose escapement-wheel with a hub and an upper back ratchet give a uniform, certain feed at high speed without the jerk at key depression.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A vibratory rocker with fixed and loose dogs holds the carriage stationary too long, and a beveled fixed dog causes rubbing resistance that can stop contact between type and paper on a light stroke.

**Solution:** A loose escapement-wheel with a hub and an upper back ratchet give a uniform, certain feed at high speed without the jerk at key depression.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
