**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter to be attached card holder, through which it is possible to hold cards made of stiff material, without needing to bend them to hit the types on the paper roller to be provided and to be described line by line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card holders with frame side parts or spring strips are hard to use for stiff cards, which must not be bent, and the holding frame must move line by line.

**Solution:** A chain-like hinged holding frame hangs from a rod on the platen; its lowest links form a pocket web that holds the card unbent upright in front of the roller, and the frame moves up and down like a curtain on line switching.

**How it works:** The top web is bolted to the roller ends; when the platen is turned for line spacing the hinged side links move with it, keeping the card lower edge parallel.
