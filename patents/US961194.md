## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to line lock and margin stop mechanism for such Inachines.

**How it works (reading):** A line locking bar is moved by key levers into a locking position, and a bell hammer is released to sound an alarm when the line lock is actuated near the margin.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The machine needs efficient line lock and margin stop mechanism, with an alarm.

**Solution:** A line locking bar is moved by key levers into a locking position, and a bell hammer is released to sound an alarm when the line lock is actuated near the margin.
