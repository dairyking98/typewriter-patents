## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the revoluble platens of type-Writing machines, and particularly to the means whereby the platen is released from the control of the usual line-space detent.

**How it works (reading):** A depressible key on a lever acts through a cam on the detent arm to lift the detent off the line-space wheel, freeing the platen while the key is held.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be released from the line-space detent so it can be rotated freely by hand.

**Solution:** A depressible key on a lever acts through a cam on the detent arm to lift the detent off the line-space wheel, freeing the platen while the key is held.
