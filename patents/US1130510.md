## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be fed about a platen that moves with the carriage.

**Solution:** A large feed roll with two smaller spring-held rolls feeds the sheet, with adjustable spring tension.

**How it works:** The platen and a large feed roll with finger pieces move with the carriage. Two smaller rolls press the large roll; their shafts sit in slots with springs to pivoted levers adjustable by screws. The big roll can serve as the platen itself.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [IMP:anvil-backing](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)

**Parts:** feed roll; small roll; spring; lever; adjusting screw; platen; carriage frame
