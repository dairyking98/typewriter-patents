## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon sideways shifting tied to ribbon reversal or the universal bail gives regular lines and uneven ribbon use.

**Solution:** Move the ribbon spool frame a small step on each space-bar stroke via a ratchet wheel and crank, so the lateral shift occurs at irregular intervals.

**How it works:** The space-bar lever, through a link and rocker, drives a pawl that advances a ratchet wheel a few teeth per stroke. A wrist pin on the wheel works a slotted arm on a rockshaft, which moves the sliding spool frames back and forth. A retaining pawl stops reverse rotation. The space bar is chosen because it fires about equally often per line but irregularly in time.

**Parts:** ribbon spool; sliding frame; rockshaft; ratchet wheel; wrist pin; slotted arm; pawl; retaining pawl; space-bar lever

**Improves on:**
