## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper feeding and controlling mechanism for 'typewriting machines andits general object is to provide improved mechanism of'the character stated.

**How it works (reading):** A divided platen whose sections are driven by line-spacing mechanism, with secondary feed devices that advance one work sheet during part of the line-space cycle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicating entries on overlapping sheets that are line-spaced differently needs a divided platen and independent feed.

**Solution:** A divided platen whose sections are driven by line-spacing mechanism, with secondary feed devices that advance one work sheet during part of the line-space cycle.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
