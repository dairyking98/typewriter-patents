## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A misspelled character must be erased in a way that a special correction mechanism and covering colour cannot do reliably.

**Solution:** A ribbon of paper-coloured dye, alternating with the ordinary ribbon, writes over the error in the paper colour so the mistake is covered.
