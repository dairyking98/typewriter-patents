**How it works (reading):** Keys are arranged in rows like type carriers at normal printing pitch, guided in a removable key basket, with a stylus used to press them; each key stops the drive member and a locking rail holds the depressed key until a flap clears all keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Adding machines are heavy, costly and large, and the key tops are not sized to the printing pitch.

**Solution:** Keys are arranged in rows like type carriers at normal printing pitch, guided in a removable key basket, with a stylus used to press them; each key stops the drive member and a locking rail holds the depressed key until a flap clears all keys.
