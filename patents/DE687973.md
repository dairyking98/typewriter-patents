## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an auxiliary device for bending the type lever and applying the types to the type lever of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** An interchangeable auxiliary device bends the type-lever head and sets the type angle and distance from the lever axis.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Replacing a defective type on a type lever is imprecise, so types do not sit correctly in the typewriter.

**Solution:** An interchangeable auxiliary device bends the type-lever head and sets the type angle and distance from the lever axis.
