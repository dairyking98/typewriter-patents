## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Kaley's opening gives no stated problem beyond details of construction for a typewriter with an interchangeable type disk.

**Solution:** A concave metal disk of spring type-arms, detachably keyed on a cam shaft, is moved by key levers through a cam so the chosen type meets the paper.

**How it works:** The types are on the outer ends of spring arms radiating from a hub, forming a concave disk with lower, capital and figure alphabets in concentric circles. The hub fits an angular seat on a cam shaft and is held by a washer with cam surfaces that binds under pins after a quarter turn, so disks can be swapped. A spiral-faced oval cam on the shaft, with a pivoted lever and two members in vertical slots, is acted on by the type keys, which are levers around a segmental plate, to move the disk. Inking is by reservoirs, and the carriage has an adjustable pawl and a bell.
