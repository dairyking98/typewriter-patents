## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage column skipping needs a simple, inexpensive way to jump to any chosen column.

**Solution:** Column keys raise a single frame-mounted stop, via tappets and an interposer of graduated length, to engage the matching column stop on the carriage rack.
