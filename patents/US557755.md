## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The Remington No. 2 ribbon could be moved widthwise only by hand.

**Solution:** A crank, pitman and rocker linkage driven from the spring-drum shaft moves the ribbon spools sideways automatically as the ribbon advances.

**How it works:** On the Remington No. 2, the spring drum's ratchet and pawl turn a bevel gear and a long cross shaft only on carriage return. The shaft drives both spool shafts through bevel gears. A crank at the shaft end drives a pitman connected to the rocker arm of the existing slides and rock shaft that carry the wire ribbon guides, shifting the ribbon widthwise. Spools slide along their shafts.
