## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon reversing on the Remington No. 6 needs improving (divisional of US559428).

**Solution:** Spring-drum power is carried by a long shaft with bevel pinions that engage either ribbon spool, and weighted spool arms with worms flip the engagement when a spool runs out.

**How it works:** A ratchet and bevel gear on the carriage spring-drum stud is turned by a pawl during carriage forward travel and slips on return. It meshes with a miter gear on a long power shaft. At each end a bevel pinion can engage or disengage a larger bevel gear on the right or left ribbon spool shaft, so either spool can be wound. A weighted arm in a slot of each spool body, linked by a pin in the hollow spool shaft to a worm on the power shaft, signals the end of the ribbon so the pinion pairs can be swapped. The rest of the reversal action is truncated.
