## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper carriages of small typewriters have many parts, are heavy and cramped.

**Solution:** One loosely rotatable axis serves both as the platen shift pivot and as the carriage release shaft, and a stiffening bar on the roller frame does several jobs.

**How it works:** The platen sits in a U-shaped frame whose link pivots on a rotatable axis in the lower carriage. A handle on that axis, turned forward, pulls the carriage rack out of the pawl via pins in rack recesses, releasing the carriage. A stiffening bar closing the U-frame serves as stop for the swinging paper guide plate, takes the shift key motion to lift the platen and guides the frame via extensions in slots.
