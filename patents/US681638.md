## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting typebars needs much force and shakes the machine.

**Solution:** A long rocking frame hinged near the front and lifted at its rear shifts the typebar support smoothly with little effort.

**How it works:** The segmental typebar support and a buffer segment ride on a rocking frame pivoted on a cross-bar near the front. The rear is lifted by a bell-crank and rod from a shift lever and rests on an adjustable cushion bolt. A long radius keeps the typebar-to-printing-point variation unnoticeable. A shifting guide maintains the relation to each bar at impression.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** typebar support; rocking frame; bell-crank lever; buffer; cushion screw; platen
