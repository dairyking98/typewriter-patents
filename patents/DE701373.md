## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on push-fit devices according to the patent 662 87o, through which the stacking sheets are fed to the lower paper guide plate will.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoting bar at the type stop is driven by a driver so it swings out of the type-lever path during feeding and returns when the driver lifts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pre-inserted sheets can strike the type levers when the lower guide plate is pushed into the impact area, and handling a special handle is needed.

**Solution:** A pivoting bar at the type stop is driven by a driver so it swings out of the type-lever path during feeding and returns when the driver lifts.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
