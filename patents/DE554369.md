**How it works (reading):** The card writing plate and its type carrier sit below the keyboard, so cards can be pulled out from under the machine while the journal is written in parallel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accounting machines needed the card written behind the platen, which made card changes awkward.

**Solution:** The card writing plate and its type carrier sit below the keyboard, so cards can be pulled out from under the machine while the journal is written in parallel.
