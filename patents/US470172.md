## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Caligraph paper feeding and ribbon feeding were unreliable.

**Solution:** Improved paper-feeding devices with a pressure roller and ribbon-spool ratchet pawls that feed the ribbon longitudinally.

**How it works:** The paper carriage can be turned up to expose the feed. A pressure or feeding roller engages or disengages the platen. Driving pawls on the ribbon-spool ratchet wheel feed the ribbon on the upward stroke, or the downward in a variant, so the types strike different parts of the ribbon.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** type ring; carriage guide bar; platen; pressure roller; ribbon spool; ratchet wheel; driving pawl; paper guide

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph: new paper and ribbon feeding
