## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Spacing between words needs a separate space-key stroke, said to take perhaps 30 percent of keyboard time.

**Solution:** A rocking frame under the keys makes the carriage feed a double space when a type-key and the space frame are pressed together.

**How it works:** The universal bar and a special rocking frame are both operated by the keys. Pressed alone, a type-key or the space frame moves the carriage one letter-space; pressed together the frame moves bodily down and, through its connections to the escapement, gives a double feed. Auxiliary transverse bars behind each key bank let a finger pushed slightly rearward trigger the double space. A separate two-space key is also added. Shown on a Remington No. 6.

**Features:** [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type-key; universal bar; rocking frame; space-key; auxiliary transverse bar; escapement; two-space key

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 6 single space-key: double space comes from the same finger stroke as the letter
