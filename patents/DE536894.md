> Description Translated from German Device for pressing down and smoothing the paper in book typewriters The invention relates to a device for depressing and smoothing the Paper in book typewriters, preferably in those whose trolleys are for the Letters and for the line separation are arranged one above the other and relatively be moved to a fixed frame. There are already flat-plate typewriters with press-down plates for pressing down and smoothing the paper have become known in which these plates are elastically mounted on the machine frame. In these known devices, however, the plate is pointed Angle and as a result only one edge on the paper. It is therefore required to take special approaches to avoid damaging the paper, especially when sliding the plate back over the surface. In contrast, the invention now has the advantage that the printing plate Due to their special arrangement and design in their working position, they are not at the cutting edge Angle is against the paper surface, but rests parallel to it, so that protection against damage is provided to a more effective extent.
