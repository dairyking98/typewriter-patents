## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Flat page-size platens or smaller flat platens having a greater size than line-size platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for depressing and smoothing the Paper in book typewriters, preferably in those whose trolleys are for the Letters and for the line separation are arranged one above the other and relatively be moved to a fixed frame.

**How it works (reading):** The press-down plate is pivoted on an axis above it so it lies flat over the whole sheet, with hinge arms that stop against a shield and are loaded by a spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A pointed press-down plate touches the paper only at one edge and can damage it when slid back over the sheet.

**Solution:** The press-down plate is pivoted on an axis above it so it lies flat over the whole sheet, with hinge arms that stop against a shield and are loaded by a spring.
