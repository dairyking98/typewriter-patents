## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to key set tabulating mechanism for typewriting machines and its general object isto provide improved mechanism of the character specified.

**How it works (reading):** A stop magazine with devices that eject stops at set positions along the platen to cooperate with other stops, and retract them on demand.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-set tabulator stops need to be ejected and set at points along the platen, and retracted at will.

**Solution:** A stop magazine with devices that eject stops at set positions along the platen to cooperate with other stops, and retract them on demand.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
