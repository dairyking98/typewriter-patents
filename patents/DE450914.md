## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**How it works (reading):** The intermediate links that drive the universal link attach directly to the type levers (through a second hole in the lever), giving more precise triggering, a lower segment position and fewer parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Universal-link drive links run from the tie rods under the segment, taking up space and adding play, so the trigger timing is imprecise and the layout is limited by the segment height.

**Solution:** The intermediate links that drive the universal link attach directly to the type levers (through a second hole in the lever), giving more precise triggering, a lower segment position and fewer parts.
