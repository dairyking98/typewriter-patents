## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike typebar actions with case-shifting bars were hard to make with easy touch, uniform leverage and uniform key dip.

**Solution:** Two concentric staggered sets of typebar hangers on a shiftable segment carried by a hollow rock shaft on adjustable ball bearings, with key-touch adjustments.

**How it works:** The segment has two concentric stepped faces holding hanger stems set at a 45 degree angle arrangement, so the two typebar sets of different lengths reach the same forward distance. The segment is pivoted on arms to a hollow rock shaft that turns on anti-friction balls around a fixed shaft. One bearing cup is adjusted along the shaft and locked by a nut to take up lost motion. Shifting rocks the shaft to raise the segment. Further text on touch adjustment is cut off.
