## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand shift keys strain fingers and slow typing.

**Solution:** A foot treadle operating shift keys.

**How it works:** A base piece with lugs, a rod, a foot piece and an adjustable-length pitman link actuate the shift key lever.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** base piece; rod; foot piece; pitman link; knob
