## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be held positively against the platen so it moves with it during line and letter spacing, yet be quickly releasable for adjusting the sheet.

**Solution:** Front and rear feed rolls on detachable shafts are carried by spring-loaded paired levers on brackets, and one cam rod rotated by hand lifts all rolls off the platen at once.

**How it works:** Independent L-shaped brackets are fixed to the carriage base. Each bracket pivots two oppositely extending levers with hooked ends that cradle the shafts of a front feed roll and a larger rear feed roll; the rolls are separate rubber members spaced by collars. Springs (tension adjustable by a screw) pull the levers so the rolls press on the platen and hold the sheet. A transverse rod with cut-away cam portions, turned a quarter or half turn, pushes all levers against their springs and frees the rolls; turning it back lets the springs re-engage. The open hooks keep the shafts in place only because the platen is there; removing the platen frees the rolls. The carriage also rides on a two-ball bearing held by a tie strap and a rear support disc.
