## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier combined denominational and column tabulators (Smith's application 730,350) were complex and not simple or reliable enough.

**Solution:** A simpler combined tabulator in which the same keys and independently operable stops necessarily act alternately as column selectors and denominational selectors.

**How it works:** A member with limited movement can be coupled to move with the carriage or freed from it, and key-controlled stops arrest it variably, which fixes where the carriage stops. Automatic means controlled by the tabulator keys switch the keys and stops between column and denominational duty in turn, and an automatically actuated indicator follows. Only the early part of the text was read in detail, the rest is on the same tabulator theme.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** tabulator key; independent stop; limited-movement member; carriage coupling; indicator

**Improves on:**

- [US1147821](../patents/US1147821.md): simpler combined denominational and column selecting mechanism (cited as application Serial No. 730,350)
