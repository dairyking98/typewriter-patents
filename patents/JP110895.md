## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection; Cylindrical Japanese-language typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a typewriter in which a small number of type cases are carried at both ends by a swinging frame (the text has 擺動杆, "swinging rod", a misprint for 擺動枠, "swinging frame") that is itself carried by an axle in the machine frame;

**How it works (reading):** Type cases ride on a swinging frame turned by a hand wheel, and a spring-coupled cover plate with openings slides over them to expose a chosen case or to cover all when the frame is swung fully forward.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A small portable kana or alphabet typewriter with few types needs its type cases kept from spilling when carried and from collecting dust when idle.

**Solution:** Type cases ride on a swinging frame turned by a hand wheel, and a spring-coupled cover plate with openings slides over them to expose a chosen case or to cover all when the frame is swung fully forward.

**How it works:** Pressing a front handle raises a push rod that lifts a type from a case, and a type-holding bar presents it to strike the paper on the platen; handle, rod and holder ride on a carriage sliding left and right. Three cases (a, b, c) hung between fan-shaped swinging frames are rocked by a hand wheel to bring one over the push rod, and a ball and spring click in a notched wavy plate holds the position. Side plates on the same axles carry a cover plate with openings, joined to the swinging frame by pins through arc slots and springs, so it moves with the frame and exposes the active case. When swung fully forward the cover meets the frame front first, the spring stretches, and all cases are covered.
