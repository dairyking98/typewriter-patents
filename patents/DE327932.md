## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** Two plates joined by a leather band with spring bearings form a single resilient base for any machine foot pattern.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Felt pads sink and clog, rubber is costly, and separate feet inserts are awkward.

**Solution:** Two plates joined by a leather band with spring bearings form a single resilient base for any machine foot pattern.
