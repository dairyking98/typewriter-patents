## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**How it works (reading):** The card holder hangs from an articulated support on the switching frame and is guided by rollers on the fixed frame in slots; a reversible holder seats in V-cut bearings held by spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stiff card holder fixed to the platen lift frame moves with the platen when it switches, so it cannot hold cards steady.

**Solution:** The card holder hangs from an articulated support on the switching frame and is guided by rollers on the fixed frame in slots; a reversible holder seats in V-cut bearings held by spring.
