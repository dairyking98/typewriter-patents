## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to indicating mechanism therefor.

**How it works (reading):** An indicating device assists uniform line spacing during fractional and step-by-step spacing.
