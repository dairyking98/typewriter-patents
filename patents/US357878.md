## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stylus-operated typewriter needs a way to select a character by a point on a marked plate and print it with one action while feeding the ink ribbon.

**Solution:** A stylus dropped into one of a circle of holes in a stationary perforated indicator pushes a spring-returned frame whose imprint pusher drives the aligned type of a rotating circular type holder against the paper through a ribbon.

**How it works:** Type characters sit in a circle at equal spacing on a common rotating holder; a perforated indicator plate with a matching circle of holes lies in a parallel plane, with a movable frame between them. The stylus is put in a chosen hole and pressed, pushing the frame against its spring; the frame presses an imprint pusher onto the type in line, which presses through an ink ribbon onto the paper. The same frame motion works a pawl on each ribbon-roller ratchet: the stroke slips the pawl back and the return turns the ratchet, feeding the ribbon lengthwise. The paper roller and its carriage step intermittently after each imprint.
