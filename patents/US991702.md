## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Label printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriters and has for its object to provide an instrument by means of which a series of figures, letters or other signs may be successivcily impressed upon a narrow ribbon of paper or tape and especially for preparing drafts by textile designers for use in setting up the designs for weaving.

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter prints figures or signs in series on a narrow paper tape for drafting weaving patterns.
