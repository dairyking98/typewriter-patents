## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods on side surfaces thereof, e.g. fixed thereto the types or dies being movable relative to the bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to so improve the construction of the type-writing machines that the working elements of the same are greatly simplified and rapidly and conveniently manipulated, and also the number of keys and type-levers, and thereby the cost of the type-writers reduced.

**How it works (reading):** Each type bar carries several types and can be swung forward or back for two, while the platen vibrates to give further printing points.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters need too many keys and type bars, making them costly.

**Solution:** Each type bar carries several types and can be swung forward or back for two, while the platen vibrates to give further printing points.

**How it works:** Finger levers and type bars are grouped sidewise around a common printing point. Cams near the pivot of each bar engage fixed standards so that tilting the finger lever forward or back throws the bar a fixed distance and selects the front or rear type. Thumb keys vibrate the platen in the direction of the type bars to offer different printing centres for different groups of types. Paper feed is unaffected. Forty-eight types would need eight bars of six letters and two platen positions.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** finger lever; type bar; cam; fixed standard; platen; thumb key; finger cup; spring

**Numbers:** 48 types; 8 keys; 6 types per bar; 2 platen vibrations
