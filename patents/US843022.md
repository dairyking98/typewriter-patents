## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Billing and book entry on a typewriter require the platen to be swapped or re-registered when a book support is used for billing.

**Solution:** A flat base carries a platen and a book support for joint use; the book support is removable and the platen can be depressed to a position clear of the book for billing, supported by a machine frame.

**How it works:** The vertically movable platen and its book support sit on a flat base; removing the book support lets the platen serve for billing work.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)
