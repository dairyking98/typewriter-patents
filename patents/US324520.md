## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Each letter gets the same carriage space regardless of width.

**Solution:** A graduated stop on a flap-bar limits the slip rack's travel to one, two or three teeth according to letter class.

**How it works:** A caligraph has a fixed rack and a sliding slip rack under spring tension; the rocker-shaft pawl normally holds the slip rack, then releases it to slip left before re-engaging a tooth further right. Rack teeth are spaced for the narrowest letters. A flap-bar carries a rabbeted wing plate whose depths act as the graduated stop, set by the keys, limiting the slip to one, two or three teeth.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** fixed rack; slip rack; pawl; rocker shaft; flap-bar; graduated stop; spring-rod; key lever

**Improves on:**

- [US315701](../patents/US315701.md): graduated stop for width classes instead of word-space
