## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to type-Writers, and particularly to that class in which a plurality of ink-ribbons can be employed, each charged with ink of a different color, or character, the ribbon-supports being capable of such adjustment that the ribbon carrying the required ink may be readily broughtjnto proper position for making type-impressions.

**How it works (reading):** Duplex reels carrying two ribbons side by side slide along their shafts so one ribbon or the other is in position, with slotted guides keeping the ribbons apart.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter using ribbons of different colours or inks needs an attachment that fits Remington and Caligraph machines.

**Solution:** Duplex reels carrying two ribbons side by side slide along their shafts so one ribbon or the other is in position, with slotted guides keeping the ribbons apart.

**How it works:** Drop brackets carry shafts at opposite sides of the frame, with splined duplex reels having three flanges and two winding spaces, each holding a ribbon of a different ink. Shifting the reels along the shafts brings one ribbon to the type area and removes the other. Narrow slotted bars on screws, with a wire, guide and adjust the ribbons so they do not touch and transfer ink. The ordinary ribbon feed drives the reels. Another form fits the Smith Premier.
