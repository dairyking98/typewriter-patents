## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage drive, escapement and paper handling of existing typewriters are said to be complicated and poorly guided.

**Solution:** A paper carriage on inverted-V ways riding rollers, driven by a spring barrel on a sliding bar, with new grip-wire paper holding and a stepped line-feed.

**How it works:** The carriage platform runs on rollers in inverted-V ways with a gib stopping rear displacement. The platen has knobs at both ends, a lower press roll on slotted bearings that can be thrown back by a lever, and a grip-wire held on the paper by a spring. A line-feed ratchet is turned by a lever and pawl, and a stepped, spring-raised space-block sets the line spacing. A spring barrel mounted on a sliding bar drives the carriage via a special escape movement (text cut off).

**Features:** [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
