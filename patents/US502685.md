## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructional details or processes of manufacture of the input device

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to attachments for typewriters, as a new article of manufacture, having particular reference to detachable finger-guides, suitably mounted upon the keyboard, whereby I am enabled to locate easily, :5 readily and without using the eyes, the respective keys thereof.

**How it works (reading):** Adjustable finger guides are fixed on a bar that is placed across the keyboard and can be angled and widened to suit different machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists must look at the copy and find the keys without looking at the keyboard.

**Solution:** Adjustable finger guides are fixed on a bar that is placed across the keyboard and can be angled and widened to suit different machines.
