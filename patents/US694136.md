## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand brushing of type is slow and exposes the operator's hands to dirt, and earlier attached cleaners are slow too.

**Solution:** A detachable cleaner has a bristled head on a handle, with a curved clip that holds it on the machine so the type can be brushed quickly.
