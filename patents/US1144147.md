## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to manifolding attachments for typewriters, of that type in which one or more webs of paper are fed from rolls, supported in the rear of the typewriter, through a series of ribbons supported by special ribbon-carrying devices and interposed between the usual typewriter ribbon and the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon-carrying frame slips onto a fixed part of the carriage, is moved into and out of operative position, is locked, and drives its ribbon feed from the line-spacing mechanism.
