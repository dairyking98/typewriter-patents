## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies; Western type typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a device connecting the typebars and key levers in a printing machine, in which an arm on the typebar has a long slot, the end of a link connected to the key lever hooks into that slot, and the hooked end normally sits at the end of the slot near the typebar pivot, but, when the key lever is pressed, slides away from the pivot while turning the typebar.

**How it works (reading):** The link from the key lever hooks into a long slot in an arm on the typebar and slides outward as the bar turns.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars are slow, crowd the segment and print weakly with a plain key-lever connection.

**Solution:** The link from the key lever hooks into a long slot in an arm on the typebar and slides outward as the bar turns.

**How it works:** An arm on the typebar has a long slot. A link, joined to the key lever through an intermediate lever, hooks into it. At rest the hook sits at the slot end next to the pivot, giving a fast start; as the key goes down the hook slides outward along the slot, so a smaller lever angle turns the bar fully and the blow is stronger. At rest the arms are farther apart near the pivot, so more typebars fit on a segment of the same size.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Device connecting the typebars and key levers in a printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a device connecting the typebars and key levers in a printing machine, in which an arm on the typebar has a long slot, the end of a link connected to the key lever hooks into that slot, and the hooked end normally sits at the end of the slot near the typebar pivot, but, when the key lever is pressed, slides away from the pivot while turning the typebar. Its object is to make the typebar act quickly, to allow more typebars to be fitted, and to print clearly. Brief description of the drawings. The invention concerns the connection between typebar and key lever in a printing machine in which pressing a key lever 1 turns the typebar 2 belonging to it, so that the type 3 at its tip strikes the platen 4 and prints the wanted character.
