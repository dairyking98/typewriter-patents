## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar typewriters are costly and slow to make and assemble; the inventor wants mass production even by unskilled workers.

**Solution:** Arrange all typebar bearings and guides in segment form so that all levers except the key levers are identical and can be punched from one die, in a riveted sheet-metal frame.

**How it works:** The frame is five punched plates (raised rear wall, central wall with stiffening, front wall, two side walls) riveted together through tabs and slots. Slots in the walls hold rivet clamps through which a bearing wire passes to form the type-lever segment. Hooks punched from slots anchor tension springs acting on intermediate levers. Other slots guide the key levers. Nearly all parts are made by punching.
