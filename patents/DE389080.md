## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter. Subject of the invention is a new arrangement of the Construction of typewriters. Compared to the known arrangements, the subject matter of the invention offers such a simplification, cheaper and clarity that a reliable Manufacture of typewriters as a mass-produced item, even when used by inexperienced users Workers is possible, with both the production of the individual parts as well as special the assembly only a fraction of the expenditure in time and material compared to the know machines. This is achieved according to the invention in that all bearings and guides of the type lever linkage are arranged in segment form 'so that all parts, levers, etc., with the exception of the key levers, can be of the same shape and length and only for each group of parts the same die cut is required.
