## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to types with alignment marks for aligning the types on the type levers.

**How it works (reading):** Each type body has alignment notches on its sides at a fixed distance from the baseline, cut at the same time as the burr trimming; two opposite points engage the notches to align the type on the type lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Raised alignment marks on type bodies print into the paper and wear, and recessed alignment marks are hard to make in the stamping die.

**Solution:** Each type body has alignment notches on its sides at a fixed distance from the baseline, cut at the same time as the burr trimming; two opposite points engage the notches to align the type on the type lever.

**How it works:** Lateral notches sit at a set distance above the baseline, so the type can be fixed regardless of the edges; a trimmer cuts the notches together with the mounting points and the burr removal.
