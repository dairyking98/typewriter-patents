## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An improved platen shift is wanted for a typebar typewriter whose platen is raised and lowered for upper and lower case.

**Solution:** The platen frame hangs on parallel links and rides on a rail raised by a rock shaft from the shift key, with a three-armed lever to hold it in upper case.

**How it works:** The platen shaft sits in a platen frame pivoted at its lower part to crank arms on a rock shaft in the carriage and at the top to links, forming parallel links so the platen moves vertically. A roller on the platen frame rests on a shift rail carried by arms of a main-frame rock shaft, which a link and second rock shaft connect to the shift key. Pressing the key lifts the platen; on release it drops by its own weight, partly carried by coiled springs. Adjusting screws limit the up and down travel, and a spring-held three-armed lever can hold the platen in upper case.
