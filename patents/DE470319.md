## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Variable-spacing arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for setting the writing unit of flat printing typewriters and relates in particular to a device of this type in which a stop on the writing unit carriage is stopped by adjustable attachments arranged on the side of the carriage guide .

**How it works (reading):** A block adjustably fixed to the carriage guide carries a row of key-operated stops that spring into the carriage's path; a common locking rail releases any previously set stop when a key is pressed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known stop-setting devices on flat-bed typewriters were expensive to make and broke down easily.

**Solution:** A block adjustably fixed to the carriage guide carries a row of key-operated stops that spring into the carriage's path; a common locking rail releases any previously set stop when a key is pressed.
