## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing on type-cylinder machines is complex.

**Solution:** Two switching levers on the swinging ribbon bridge hit fixed pins, and a sliding rail picks which spool's ratchet pawl is engaged.

**How it works:** A swing bridge carries the ribbon spools and a laterally sliding rail holding pawls for each spool's ratchet. Switching pawls on levers are moved by fixed stops as the bridge swings at each type stroke. Shifting the rail moves one pair of pawls out of engagement and the other in, reversing the winding direction; a leaf spring bead in holes in the rail holds each position.
