## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier sideways-feed devices free the feed gear from the platen rack by swinging the whole device, which strains the mesh and complicates freeing and meshing.

**Solution:** The feed gear sits on a lower plate hung on parallel links from a fixed upper plate, so a key lever slides it straight in and out of mesh with the platen rack against a spring.

**How it works:** A sideways-feed rack lies crosswise along the front of the platen carrier, and a feed gear meshes with it. The gear is carried on a lower plate joined to a fixed upper plate by two pivoted links, so the lower plate moves horizontally. A spring on a link keeps the gear in mesh. Pressing a key lever turns a bent plate whose slot pushes a projection on the lower plate, drawing the whole feed device straight back and freeing the gear from the rack. A spring-urged L-lever with a finger piece can catch a notch on the key lever to lock the gear out of mesh.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Platen sideways-feed device for a Japanese-script printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a platen sideways-feed device for a Japanese-script printing machine, characterised in that an upper plate fixed to the machine frame and a lower plate carrying the sideways feed device, including the sideways-feed gear, are joined at both ends by links pivoted to both plates, so that the lower plate can move horizontally; a spring always urges it toward the platen's rack; and a key lever pivoted on the upper plate moves the lower plate against the spring, drawing the feed gear carried on it straight back from the rack and freeing the mesh. The object is to move the sideways feed device, with its feed gear, horizontally by simple means, and so make its working smooth. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show an embodiment. Fig. 1 is a side view of the main part with the feed gear meshing with the platen's rack; Fig. 2 a similar side view with the feed gear out of mesh; Fig. 3 a perspective view of the locking device of the key lever.
