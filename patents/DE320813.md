## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (fabric strip winding)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The aim of the invention is to make them the same or approximately the same fully wound onto a spool, regardless of the diameter of the coil, which increases with the number of turns.

**How it works (reading):** A ratchet-driven spool and a spring- or weight-loaded disc that the spool drives against and then releases take up slack each cycle regardless of diameter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Strip is fed in equal lengths but the take-up spool diameter grows with turns, so the wound amount varies.

**Solution:** A ratchet-driven spool and a spring- or weight-loaded disc that the spool drives against and then releases take up slack each cycle regardless of diameter.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
