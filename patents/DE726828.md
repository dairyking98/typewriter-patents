## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper trolley switching device for writing, Calculating machines or similar machines in which the frame provided with the carriage guide is mounted pivotably about an axis for switching.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage frame itself carries the switching links, and its centre of gravity is placed so the swing axis runs through the carriage guides, reducing tipping moments.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A separate changeover frame on the carriage gives poor centre-of-gravity conditions and extra oscillating mass.

**Solution:** The carriage frame itself carries the switching links, and its centre of gravity is placed so the swing axis runs through the carriage guides, reducing tipping moments.
