## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to table writing devices for typewriters, at which the paper trolley always advances until it reaches the selected point is brought to a halt by a column stop.

**How it works (reading):** The drive wheel stays fixed in the path of the column stops; the stops sit helically on its axis and column-key rods raised into the matching stop position halt the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column-stop carriage stops required the column key to move a locking wheel into the stop path, which the inventor wants to avoid.

**Solution:** The drive wheel stays fixed in the path of the column stops; the stops sit helically on its axis and column-key rods raised into the matching stop position halt the carriage.
