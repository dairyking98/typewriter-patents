## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Entries on bank forms are slow to set and error-prone when typed line by line and copied across many forms.

**Solution:** Adjacent type bars carrying numerals, the alphabet and symbols sit in a pressure frame that lifts for form insertion and lowers to press the form against the bars and a roller.

> Description Translated from German Device for making entries on forms for banks etc. with adjacently arranged adjustable type bars The invention relates Devices for making entries on forms for banks, etc., at which adjoining adjustable type rods are arranged, which under Use a pressure roller to print the forms. These well-known Devices are improved according to the invention in that the type bars in a raised and lowered pressure frame are housed, which after its lifting by hand lever o. the like. A comfortable insertion of the form to be printed on the housing table allowed, while the form using the lowering of the print frame a system rail is automatically brought into the desired position for the printed text, whereby the line setting for the form can be done beforehand.
