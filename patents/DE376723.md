## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator

**How it works (reading):** A backing sheet impregnated with basic aniline dye is placed directly under the wax paper, with no carbon in between, so the dye penetrates the wax and marks the character edges sharply.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary carbon paper does not stain wax stencil paper sharply enough, so typed characters look faint at their edges.

**Solution:** A backing sheet impregnated with basic aniline dye is placed directly under the wax paper, with no carbon in between, so the dye penetrates the wax and marks the character edges sharply.

**Features:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** wax paper; dye-impregnated backing sheet; protective cover sheet
