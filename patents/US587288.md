## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text is garbled at its opening; the stated aim is a machine whose key-levers are linked by power connections that complete the type-bar action.

**Solution:** Key-levers drive auxiliary power-levers, pivoted at the bell-crank arms, that actuate the type-bar and make the impression, adaptable to standard machines.
