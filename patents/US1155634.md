## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a quiet (Noiseless) typebar machine with a shifting platen carriage and three types per bar, the guiding of the carriage must be noiseless, move both ends equally and keep the platen parallel to the type faces at all three printing positions.

**Solution:** A ball-race shift rail is guided by sliding-trunnion arms coupled to a balancing rock shaft, which varies the swing radius so the rail moves in an almost straight line to three positions.

**How it works:** The platen carriage runs on two balls-in-grooves rails (one fixed, one screw-adjustable to take up wear) on a vertically shiftable rail. Links from a shift rock shaft lift or lower this rail, with a spring holding it at the centre. Arms pivoted to each end of the rail slide in pivoted trunnions on the fixed frame, and their far ends connect to arms on a balancing rock shaft. As the rail moves, the sliding changes the arm radius so the rail path is near a straight vertical line within a few ten-thousandths of an inch. Variants use different trunnion and stub arrangements and a round rail with trucks.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** platen carriage; platen; shift rail; ball rail; ball; adjusting screw; shift link; shift rock shaft; sliding arm; trunnion; balancing rock shaft; centering spring; type bar

**Numbers:** 3 types per bar; 2 balls per groove pair; straight-line deviation a few ten-thousandths of an inch

**Improves on:**

- parallel-link platen shift guides: sliding-trunnion arms give straight, equal motion at both ends and no impacting parts
