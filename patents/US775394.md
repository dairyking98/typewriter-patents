## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides

**How it works (reading):** A front-strike writing machine with pivoted rearward-striking typebars on a curved pad, key-levers linked to bell-cranks, and a ribbon vibrator that lifts the ribbon to the printing point and drops it away at each stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The stated aims are to have a ribbon vibrator cover and uncover the printing point at each strike and to help the typebars rebound from the platen.

**Solution:** A front-strike writing machine with pivoted rearward-striking typebars on a curved pad, key-levers linked to bell-cranks, and a ribbon vibrator that lifts the ribbon to the printing point and drops it away at each stroke.
