## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to 1 0 tabulating stops adapted to be arranged in groups and means for moving any one of the groups into operativerposition where the stops are adapted to coperate with the key actuated, tabulator plungers of any usual and well'known typewriter tabulating device.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulator stops are grouped, each group is moved into operating position as a unit, and only one group can be active while the others are locked out.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops for several different forms could not all be in operating position at once, and rearranging stops for each form was impractical.

**Solution:** Tabulator stops are grouped, each group is moved into operating position as a unit, and only one group can be active while the others are locked out.
