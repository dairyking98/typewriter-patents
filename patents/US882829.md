## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention being to secure a noiseless feeding and reversing mechanism, I have, therefore, found it desirable to provide mechanism of such character as to eliminate concussion or impact of moving parts, insuring thereby a substantiall silent operation.

**How it works (reading):** Reversal is triggered by the tension of the ribbon when its end is reached, so feed direction flips with little strain and little noise.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal must happen quickly and without straining the fragile inking ribbon, or the ribbon burns through at one spot.

**Solution:** Reversal is triggered by the tension of the ribbon when its end is reached, so feed direction flips with little strain and little noise.

**Features:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
