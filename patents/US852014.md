## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes of plates, e.g. metal plates, plastic cards with automatic plate transport systems, e.g. for credit cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting mac ines and more especially to line spacing mechanism wherein the means for returning the carriage to its starting position imparts a position.

**How it works (reading):** The line-space mechanism moves a lever that engages the paper roller, and the carriage return also imparts a line feed, with the lock release tied to the operating lever so the feed stays exact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing can be misfed because the device that unlocks the paper-roller pawl is independent of the line-space lever, so the operator can overlook whether the pawl is disengaged.

**Solution:** The line-space mechanism moves a lever that engages the paper roller, and the carriage return also imparts a line feed, with the lock release tied to the operating lever so the feed stays exact.
