## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The impression surface for wax or plastic matrices must be advanced for each character, and the inventor wants this done automatically.

**Solution:** A type-wheel or type knuckles strike a waxed matrix plate that slides on a feed bar, and the insertion of each type moves the plate on for the next character.
