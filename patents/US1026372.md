## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to the type actions of writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable frame for the restoring springs changes the spring tension and touch on all the type actions at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tension of all the restoring springs of the type actions cannot be adjusted together, and the key touch cannot be changed without affecting acceleration.

**Solution:** An adjustable frame for the restoring springs changes the spring tension and touch on all the type actions at once.
