> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a device for achieving a permanent stop of the zero-type lever on a typewriter with a motor-driven shaft, with the S the eccentric for hitting the type lever be coupled by switching on drivers. With the usual typewriters, when writing numbers with several Zeros every single one of the same are posted. When writing, for example The arithmetic key ο was hit six times for the number 1,000,000. Purpose of The invention is to save the operator the repetition of the zero stop. Although there are devices on electric typewriters for achieving one Permanent stop already known, but the subject matter of the invention differs in that the zeros located at the end of a number automatically change to the required Number to be written. The aim of the invention is achieved by a lever mechanism that the driver for the Type lever after hitting the zero key keeps switched on as briskly as that Koloinnemzählwerk in Rechiensteöuing exerts an influence on the lever mechanism by means of its control rail.
