## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for achieving a permanent stop of the zero-type lever on a typewriter with a motor-driven shaft, with the S the eccentric for hitting the type lever be coupled by switching on drivers.

**How it works (reading):** A lever mechanism keeps the driver for the type lever engaged after a zero key is struck, and the counter's control rail influences that lever so the zeros stop at the number's end.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing repeated zeros on a motor-driven accounting machine requires repeated key strokes.

**Solution:** A lever mechanism keeps the driver for the type lever engaged after a zero key is struck, and the counter's control rail influences that lever so the zeros stop at the number's end.
