## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A slider on the universal rail carries height-adjustable stop screws that act on an intermediate lever for the ribbon guide, so the ribbon stands still before each type-lever strike and the colour is selected by the stop heights.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-colour ribbon adjustment needs the ribbon guide held still before each key strike, and earlier stop arrangements did not do this reliably.

**Solution:** A slider on the universal rail carries height-adjustable stop screws that act on an intermediate lever for the ribbon guide, so the ribbon stands still before each type-lever strike and the colour is selected by the stop heights.
