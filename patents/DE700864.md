## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Fixed carriage braking wears the brake and is too strong as the carriage nears its end.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded braking member is automatically relieved or switched off when the carriage is released near its end position, via stop-member actuators.
