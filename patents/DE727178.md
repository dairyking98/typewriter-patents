## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (number roller identification marks) · **Subject (patent classes):** Shaping removing material; Making multilayered or multicoloured articles; Drums

**How it works (reading):** Number rollers are made by moulding a light body with smooth outer surface and digits formed in the moulding process.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Metal and enamel number rollers are heavy, have rough surfaces, or are hard to make with raised digits.

**Solution:** Number rollers are made by moulding a light body with smooth outer surface and digits formed in the moulding process.
