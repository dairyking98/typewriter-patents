## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Letter-cards or letter-sheets, i.e. cards or sheets each of which is to be folded with the message inside and to serve as its own envelope for mailing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter postcard, at which address and text without taking it out of the typewriter or re-clamping written on the card.

**How it works (reading):** A normal postcard has a crease line so the address part is folded over, written, and then unfolded to lie flat.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Postcards must be written on both sides, and an extended postcard costs more in material and glue.

**Solution:** A normal postcard has a crease line so the address part is folded over, written, and then unfolded to lie flat.
