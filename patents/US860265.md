## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for perforating or stencil cutting using special types or dies

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more especially to the feed mechanism for the ink ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon feed is driven from the carriage spring only while the carriage moves in the letter-spacing direction, so the ribbon does not feed during a release or tabulator move.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon is fed when the carriage moves leftward without writing, such as on releases or tabulating, so parts of the ribbon are used up unevenly.

**Solution:** The ribbon feed is driven from the carriage spring only while the carriage moves in the letter-spacing direction, so the ribbon does not feed during a release or tabulator move.
