## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work sheets on a flat platen are hard to displace, replace and adjust, and carbon sheets need separate handling.

**Solution:** A swinging machine-supporting frame carrying work-holding and carbon-carrying means is raised off the flat platen by a cam when the machine is moved back past a set position, so the sheet can be removed or inserted.

**Features:** [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
