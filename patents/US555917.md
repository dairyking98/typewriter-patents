## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple, efficient, easily-operated type-writing instrument is wanted.

**Solution:** A rotary drum carrying independent type-bars, stopped at any point by movable stops worked from individual character keys.

**How it works:** A revolving cylindrical drum has axial slots holding type-bars, each with several types on its head, retained by two annular bands. Notches in the bars align when retracted. A key for each character moves a stop pin to arrest the drum's rotation at the chosen position, and a type-bar locking device engages the notches. The paper roll moves bodily to take the different types on each bar, and a carriage feed of new design spaces the paper, with an auxiliary spacer for each key.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** rotary type carrier drum; rim; hub; type-bar; shank; head; annular band; stop pin; stop-operating lever; paper roll; carriage feed
