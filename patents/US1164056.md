**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typebar system and its mechanism in a single-shift front-stroke machine need better construction and operation.

**Solution:** A front-stroke typewriter whose typebar action, pusher device and frame of five castings are rearranged so the segment, not the platen, shifts for case change.
