## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (composite gear) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction providing resilience or vibration-damping

**How it works (reading):** A soft elastic insert ring (rubber, leather, or an air-filled hollow ring) between the toothed rim and wheel disc, clamped radially, transmits force by friction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Gears must run quietly, last long, be simple to make and be interchangeable, and rigid rim-and-disc gears are sensitive to tooth errors.

**Solution:** A soft elastic insert ring (rubber, leather, or an air-filled hollow ring) between the toothed rim and wheel disc, clamped radially, transmits force by friction.
