**How it works (reading):** A forked lever moves inclined axes in control plates: a downward stroke clears the setting counter, and a forward movement carries the subtotal into the setting counter while driving the printing mechanism through a pawl.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cash register needs separate ways to reset the setting counter and to carry a subtotal, but these are hard to do with one lever.

**Solution:** A forked lever moves inclined axes in control plates: a downward stroke clears the setting counter, and a forward movement carries the subtotal into the setting counter while driving the printing mechanism through a pawl.
