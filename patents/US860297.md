## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon arrangements mounted on moving carriages

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of my invention is to provide a ribbon mechanism which is so constructed that the ribbon will be held taut at all times without the aid of a take-up device.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon spools are mounted on an oscillating frame and advanced by pawls on a stationary member each time the frame moves, keeping the ribbon taut.

**Problem:** Ribbon must be held taut without a take-up device, and moving the ribbon should need little power.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools are mounted on an oscillating frame and advanced by pawls on a stationary member each time the frame moves, keeping the ribbon taut.
