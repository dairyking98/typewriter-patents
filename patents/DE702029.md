## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punched-card systems with several marking systems in one map column cannot be read by one sensing system without separate passes or devices.

**Solution:** Cards carry two marking systems, and a shift in the sensing timing of one system lets the machine read both through separate evaluation devices in one run.
