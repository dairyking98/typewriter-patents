## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper fingers block the sheets so that individual manifold sheets cannot be turned back for erasure.

**Solution:** The paper fingers and their support rod are swung bodily away from the platen, leaving the paper free to be lifted sheet by sheet.
