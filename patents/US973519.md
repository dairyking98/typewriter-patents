## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Perforated work sheets must be impaled on pins that obstruct feeding, and stripping them off is awkward.

**Solution:** A yielding apertured paper table over retractable alining pins that drop out of the way once the feed rolls catch the sheet.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
