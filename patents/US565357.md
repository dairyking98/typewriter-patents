## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Hammond-type machine with two type segments needs inking without an ink ribbon.

**Solution:** Each of the two type segments carries an inking roller that inks the opposite segment, and the roller is drawn over an ink slab whenever its own segment is moved to print.

**How it works:** Two type segments, each with three rows of letters, swing on one spindle in front of a striker that presses the paper against the chosen type. An arm on each segment carries a spring-loaded roller-arm whose roller inks the type of the other segment. When a segment moves to print, it pulls the opposite-side roller back across an ink slab, which works the ink onto the roller for even distribution. The roller spindle slides vertically in the roller so that the roller stays fixed in height while the segments are shifted up or down for case. Ink reservoirs on each side feed driven distributing rollers and a transferring roller on a spring lever, which is moved by an arm on the segment.
