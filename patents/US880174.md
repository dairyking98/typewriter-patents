## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage cannot be controlled or returned from the keyboard at all times.

**Solution:** A lazy-tong lever arrangement on the side of the carriage lets the keyboard return, release or stop the carriage at any point.

**Features:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
