## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper for dealing with the impression-transfer material after use

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a power-driven device on special writing and accounting machines for withdrawing carbon paper webs lying between paper webs or bow.

**How it works (reading):** A powered carbon-carriage retraction, driven by a spring motor or drum, that pulls the carbon trolley back after the paper has been slackened by the front-sheet system, with the trolley moved away by a linkage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Retracting carbon paper by hand needs a special handle and tears the carbon when the paper is not stretched, while a spring-powered retraction arrangement is clumsy to operate.

**Solution:** A powered carbon-carriage retraction, driven by a spring motor or drum, that pulls the carbon trolley back after the paper has been slackened by the front-sheet system, with the trolley moved away by a linkage.
