## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The usual billing crank covers less than one revolution, so turning the platen backward more than a full turn at one stroke needs movement-multiplying gearing.

**Solution:** The platen-driving crank is itself made to rotate through more than one revolution at a single stroke, so it can be connected directly to the platen, and relatively adjustable stops limit the throw of the crank.
