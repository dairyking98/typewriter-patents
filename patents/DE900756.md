## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing unit, booking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering by marks or formations on the paper being fed

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing unit for booking machines with several printing points, and their beltge sensing, the effectiveness of the feelers controlling the pressure points, especially the implementation from Nachtragsbucheri.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Booking machines allow the operator to set the wrong posting mode, switching off or opening the wrong print points.

**Solution:** A setting means with several positions controls the print points, so that the operator's entry cannot be set to an incorrect position.
