A known device of this kind provides for this purpose an actuating means which can be divided into several positions and which controls the printing positions of the print in such a way that it can be seen from the account card and the account book whether an entry is made without the account book or an entry in the account book has been made. Since it is left to the machine operator to transfer the setting means either into this or that position, a ver -, genuinely incorrect setting of the setting means is not prevented. This is because it can happen that the machine operator moves the setting means into the "without book" position, although he should move it into the "supplementary book" position.
