## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to centering scales for typewriting machines, and the prime object 18 to provide a device of this nature by which may be easily and instantaneouslyascertained the center of any line of writing for which the typewriter margin-stops may be adjusted.

**How it works (reading):** A centering scale attached to the typewriter is read against the margin stops so the centre of any line can be found directly, without reorganising the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Finding the centre of a typed line requires arithmetic when margin stops are set at arbitrary positions.

**Solution:** A centering scale attached to the typewriter is read against the margin stops so the centre of any line can be found directly, without reorganising the machine.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** centering scale; margin stops
