## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to inking mechanism for typewriting machines and more particularly to mechanism for duplicating inked copies of typewritten work by means of a ribbon supiiilementary to the ordinary or main ribbon of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** A supplementary three-ply ribbon on spools on the platen frame, automatically shifted lengthwise and crosswise by cams on the platen each line so the types strike fresh ribbon.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Duplicating inked copies of typewritten work with a supplementary ribbon needs a simpler, better guided and fed carriage-carried ribbon mechanism that spreads wear.

**Solution:** A supplementary three-ply ribbon on spools on the platen frame, automatically shifted lengthwise and crosswise by cams on the platen each line so the types strike fresh ribbon.

**How it works:** A supporting frame on the platen carrier carries two coaxial ribbon spools at the platen ends. The ribbon runs down over turning bars and inward along the printing line, looped over upright fingers to form three plies. Cams on the platen and a spring move the frame lengthwise and transversely as the platen turns, changing where the types strike the ribbon. The spools can also be turned by hand to feed the ribbon back and forth. Shown on a Smith Premier No. 10.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** supporting frame; ribbon spool; turning bar; upright finger; cam; platen frame; platen; spring

**Numbers:** 3 plies of ribbon

**Improves on:**

- earlier carriage-carried supplementary ribbon mechanisms: simpler mounting, guiding, feeding and impact-point variation
