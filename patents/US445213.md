## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cheap, simple and easily worked typewriter is wanted.

**Solution:** A board-mounted machine in which the type wheel is pushed forward against a rubber roller on a reciprocating bearing to print.

**How it works:** A base with side brackets carries three rods at the back; one is an elastic rubber roller with a ratcheted end, beneath which a spring bail presses the paper from below, and a top rod with spring-loaded wheels holds the paper. A weighted spacer with a beak engages the ratchet for line spacing. A smooth front rod and a toothed, numbered rack rod carry a sheet-metal wheel-bearing carriage with a sleeve, pulled against the rack by an elastic cord running over a pulley. The type wheel, with a flange carrying the characters on its periphery, sits in this bearing and is pushed forward (reciprocated) to print, with stops at the front braced against the table to prevent the base moving. Selection of the character is beyond the opening text.
