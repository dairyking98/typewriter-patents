## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing requires the platen to be positively arrested after a set amount of rotation, which existing machines do not do.

**Solution:** A stop-carrying disk and ratchet wheel on the platen, attachable to a Remington No. 6 without structural change, positively arrest the platen after a predetermined rotation.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
