## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is an auxiliary ribbon device of the type mentioned, in which, as is known, two or more auxiliary ribbons with their carriers and spools Can be pivoted upwards and backwards about an axis parallel to the platen axis are, each of which with its two-sided carriers and coils plus one of these connecting axis forms a member.

**How it works (reading):** Two or more auxiliary ribbons with their spools are stacked on a member that pivots up and back about an axis parallel to the platen, seated in a top-open slot on the carriage, so ribbons can be lifted or placed singly or together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier auxiliary ribbon devices for copying either need costly additions or take up platen length so that normal-width paper cannot be used.

**Solution:** Two or more auxiliary ribbons with their spools are stacked on a member that pivots up and back about an axis parallel to the platen, seated in a top-open slot on the carriage, so ribbons can be lifted or placed singly or together.
