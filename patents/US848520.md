## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dust and grit get into the slots of the type-bar segment and clog the type-bars.

**Solution:** A segmental sheet-metal shield covers the slots and type-bar pivots over the segment, and is moved out of the way by the universal-bar frame when the bars swing up to print.

**How it works:** The shield is a curved plate with a flange that overlies the slots and a top flange under the anvil; the universal bar pulls it back as the type-bar strikes.
