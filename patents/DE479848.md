## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** The motor and its intermediate gear housing pivot together on a pull-out bolt; the motor shaft end with a transverse pin loosely engages the slotted worm shaft, so the assembly can be lifted off and removed in a few steps.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The motor that returns the carriage on a motorised typewriter is hard to remove, since the gear housing and motor shaft are tightly connected to the machine.

**Solution:** The motor and its intermediate gear housing pivot together on a pull-out bolt; the motor shaft end with a transverse pin loosely engages the slotted worm shaft, so the assembly can be lifted off and removed in a few steps.

**Features:**

**Parts:** carriage return motor; intermediate gear housing; pull-out bolt; transverse pin on motor shaft; worm shaft with longitudinal slots
