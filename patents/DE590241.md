## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device on typewriters to secure the writing limit on lower edge of the paper sheet The invention relates to a device Typewriters to secure the writing limit at the lower edge of the sheet of paper, in which the display of the last line is independent of the clamped sheet and the typewriter when the last line is reached by triggering a bell signal he follows. According to the invention, the device takes the respective number of revolutions the platen, which does this when the clamped sheet is indexed, immediately by friction, so that to attach the device to the typewriter there is no need to take any precautions. The invention consists in that the device. is driven by a friction wheel resting on the front surface of the platen button, which can be moved from the center of the axis of the platen button to its edge to accelerate or slow down the process of the device, which takes place with any number of lines or sheet length.
