## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the engagement between platen and line-spacing ratchet should need no extra levers or buttons.

**Solution:** A hand wheel and shaft are movable relative to the platen and ratchet, with clutch means: one direction connects platen and hand wheel independently of the ratchet, the other disconnects then reconnects all in unison.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
