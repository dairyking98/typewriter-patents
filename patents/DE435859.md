> Description Translated from German Paper guide device for typewriters. The invention relates to a paper guide device for typewriters with a common linkage successively liftable pressure rollers and consists in the fact that on one lever arm of the paper guide plate, which is designed as a two-part lever, the upper paper pressure roller is stored directly and on the other lever arm as a carrier for the lower pressure roller a three-armed lever is hinged to which both the clamping device as well as the manual release linkage attacks. This training has the advantage; to take up little space with the simplest construction, and is therefore particularly suitable for Use suitable for small machines. The drawing shows an embodiment of the subject matter of the invention shown.
