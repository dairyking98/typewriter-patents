## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple cheap machine with a small number of finger keys controlling many characters.

**Solution:** Nine typebars carry three types each, and switches or gates set by the finger keys decide which bar moves and which character prints.

**How it works:** Twenty-seven finger keys are grouped in a circle on a curved cast base plate. Pressing a key acts through gates that determine the movement of the type-bar and so the character printed (capital, small letter or numeral). A carriage on a bed carries the platen and a toothed rack engaged by a feed dog for letter and word spacing.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** base plate; finger key; gate; type-bar; carriage; platen; rack bar; feed dog; paper guide

**Numbers:** 27 keys; 9 type-bars; 3 types per bar

**Improves on:**

- earlier Burridge application: refined switch/gate selection
