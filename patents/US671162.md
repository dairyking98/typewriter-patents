## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide a ribbon-movement including in its organization a novel construction of ribbonguide associated with operating mechanism providing means whereby the guide will normally hold the inking-ribbon in front of the line of writing, so that all of the writing is absolutely visible and the line being written is only covered by the ribbon when the character is being struck.

**How it works (reading):** Trip latches on the spool ratchet disks move a reciprocating reversing bar, switching pawls; ribbon guide holds ribbon in front of the writing.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed needs reliable automatic reversing and a ribbon kept out of sight of the line being typed.

**Solution:** Trip latches on the spool ratchet disks move a reciprocating reversing bar, switching pawls; ribbon guide holds ribbon in front of the writing.

**How it works:** A rockshaft transmits key action to two feed pawls on the spool ratchets. Pivoted trip latches on the ratchet disks trip a reciprocating reversing bar when a spool empties. The bar changes which pawl works. A ribbon guide keeps the ribbon in front of the line until a character is struck. Shown on a book typewriter.
