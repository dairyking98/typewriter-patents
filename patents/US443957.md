## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing machines for printing on a flat sheet or page are large, awkward and easily broken.

**Solution:** A vest-pocket typewriter whose arched frame carrying keys, typebars and ink pads slides on a threaded horizontal shaft that feeds it across the sheet.

**How it works:** A light frame holds a stationary horizontal threaded feed-shaft. A carriage with a sleeve slides and rocks on that shaft and has two concentric curved plates with perforations in three rows, in which typebars carrying type at their lower ends work. Pressing a key or the end of a pencil drives a type down onto the paper over ink pads. A feeding mechanism on the shaft moves the carriage along as writing progresses and when returning. The machine rests on the sheet being written.
