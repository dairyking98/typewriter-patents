## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In the patent’s own words:** The aim of my invention is to avoid this useless and objectionable sliding movement of the carriage-top, and to avoid the consequent noise and wear by providing a device which shall 3 5 automatically lock the two parts of the carriage together whenever it is turned upward, and automatically release them when again brought to an operative position.

**How it works (reading):** A gravity dog locks the upper and lower parts of the carriage together when the carriage is tilted up and is lifted clear when it is lowered.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On Remington machines the upper carriage part, which shifts the platen for case, slides violently back and forth whenever the carriage is lifted to inspect the print, causing noise and wear.

**Solution:** A gravity dog locks the upper and lower parts of the carriage together when the carriage is tilted up and is lifted clear when it is lowered.

**How it works:** The carriage has a lower part sliding left-right and an upper part carrying the platen that slides front-back for upper and lower case. A dog rides on a thumb-latch of the feed bar while the carriage is down, held out of engagement. When the carriage is lifted, the dog drops and its shoulder engages the carriage rear, locking the upper part against rearward motion. Other forms use a gravitating latch on a shaft engaging a lip on the lower carriage, normally held off by the shift bar.
