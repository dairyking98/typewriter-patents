## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a front-strike typewriter the shiftable typebar support and basket are heavy and need counterbalance, guiding, and quiet adjustable stops.

**Solution:** A typebar support carried on links and rock-shaft arms with an adjustable spiral counterbalance spring and adjustable buffered stops and guides.

**How it works:** The typebar support and its basket hang from a pair of links pivoted to the upper rear frame and a pair of rock shaft arms on a transverse rock shaft at the lower rear. A shift key lever, through a link, turns an arm on that shaft to raise or lower the support. A spiral spring on the shaft is anchored to a hanger at one end and to a worm gear at the other; turning a screw against the worm gear changes the spring tension so the whole weight of the support is balanced. Adjustable stops with buffers limit travel while absorbing vibration and noise.
