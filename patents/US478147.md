## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon should move lengthwise and widthwise to use its whole surface on a Remington-type machine.

**Solution:** A carriage-driven shaft with wiper cams and heart cams vibrates pivoted ribbon spool frames to feed lengthwise by ratchet and shift widthwise.

**How it works:** A long rear shaft is turned in one direction by bevel gears from the carriage spring-drum only while the carriage travels left. Double tappet cams at each end swing a pawl lever that steps a ratchet on the ribbon spool shaft for lengthwise feed. Heart-shaped grooved cams vibrate a pivoted frame carrying the spool shaft, shifting the ribbon widthwise. Spools slip on friction springs, can be hand-turned or removed, and a weighted detent holds the ratchet.
