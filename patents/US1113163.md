## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** An intermittently actuated paper-feeler retracts from the paper path at each space-lever movement, and a sheet-controlled, space-lever-actuated drive advances the central operative member that carries the indices, with visual, audible and machine-lock signals at set positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator needs progressive automatic indication of the distance from the printing point to the bottom of the sheet, without manual care.

**Solution:** An intermittently actuated paper-feeler retracts from the paper path at each space-lever movement, and a sheet-controlled, space-lever-actuated drive advances the central operative member that carries the indices, with visual, audible and machine-lock signals at set positions.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md)

**Parts:** paper-feeler; space lever; drum 6 as central operative member; indices; signal devices
