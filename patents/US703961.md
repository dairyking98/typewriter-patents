## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and has special reference to the linespacing mechanism thereof, the object being to provide a mechanism of this character which shall be positive and reliable in action, while at the same time embodying every necessary adjustment to meet the requirements of type-writing in the spacing of the lines.

**How it works (reading):** A shiftable stop device sets fixed or universal line spacings, and the actuator also acts as a holdback dog when the machine frame is moved freely.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing on a book (flat-bed) typewriter needs to be positive, adjustable to fixed spacings and to variously ruled paper, and leave the traveling frame free to move.

**Solution:** A shiftable stop device sets fixed or universal line spacings, and the actuator also acts as a holdback dog when the machine frame is moved freely.

**How it works:** On a Fisher book typewriter the printing frame travels over a flat platen. An actuator advances the frame line by line; a shiftable stop device in several forms limits the throw to single, double or universal (variable) spacing. In its normal position the actuator acts as a holdback dog, letting the frame be moved back and forth over the platen. Only the objects and figure list are readable in the opening.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** actuator; holdback dog; shiftable stop device; machine frame; flat platen
