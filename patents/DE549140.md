## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an accounting machine, the so-called flat-plate typewriter with one above the flat plate for the purpose of line switching contains movable frame carrying the type carriage, which is advanced by a certain amount for the line feed.

**How it works (reading):** A switch bracket behind the carriage pivots and transmits its movement through a ratchet and ratchet wheel to the frame axle, and the bracket is pressed to use the limit stops to add force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-feed in flat-plate machines needs a powerful spring-based retraction and the spring force is limited.

**Solution:** A switch bracket behind the carriage pivots and transmits its movement through a ratchet and ratchet wheel to the frame axle, and the bracket is pressed to use the limit stops to add force.
