## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic embroidery machines are unsuitable for embroidering several ribbons at once, and the adjustment has to be done by hand.

**Solution:** Embroidery heads on a longitudinally and transversely movable support are steered by a jacquard, which adjusts both directions for each stitch, so several ribbons are embroidered at once in pattern.
