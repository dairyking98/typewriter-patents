## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (combined typewriting and computing machine inking) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character-by-character adjustment; Input mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to combined typefor its object to connect the inking mechanism of the typewriter withthat part of the computing machine which determines the character of the arithmetical operation to be performed, said connection being ofsuch a sort that the character of arithmetical operation performed by the computing mechanism will be indicated by the color of the printing.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon colour selector of the typewriter is linked to the add-or-subtract handle of the computing mechanism, so that choosing blue or red sets addition or subtraction and vice versa.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a combined typewriter and calculator the operator must set the arithmetic operation separately from the typing.

**Solution:** The ribbon colour selector of the typewriter is linked to the add-or-subtract handle of the computing mechanism, so that choosing blue or red sets addition or subtraction and vice versa.

**How it works:** The ribbon vibrating mechanism on the typewriter is connected to the add-and-subtract handle of the computing machine, so one setting moves both.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Key touch and even impression](../features/bar-touch.md)

- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** two-colour ribbon mechanism; add-and-subtract handle; ribbon vibrator
