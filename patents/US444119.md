## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters are wanted that can write directly on a bound book or flat sheet without a base.

**Solution:** A baseless typebar machine whose frame rests on two adjustable-height supports lying on the paper and steps along slotted bars as keys are pressed.

**How it works:** A frame carries a basket of type-levers that swing down onto the book or sheet through a ribbon. Supports on each side bear on the paper and carry toothed bars; pawls on side bars lock the frame at a chosen height above them. Small faceted wheels at the front of the supports, turned by line-levers on ratchet wheels, advance a full or half line. The frame moves step by step along slotted bars on the supports as keys are depressed, and there is an ink reservoir feeding the ribbon.
