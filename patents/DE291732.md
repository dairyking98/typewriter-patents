**How it works (reading):** A cast body with hooks grips the rear posts of the typewriter, and the holder pivots about the same axis as the drive pin of the keyboard-driven handlebar, so it swings down while staying connected to the drive.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The manuscript holder must stay driven by the typewriter, yet it must be swung down into a compact shape for storing or carrying without loosening the drive connection.

**Solution:** A cast body with hooks grips the rear posts of the typewriter, and the holder pivots about the same axis as the drive pin of the keyboard-driven handlebar, so it swings down while staying connected to the drive.
