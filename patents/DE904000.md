## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Many imprints (amounts, date, number) must be applied in a small stamp field of a receipt that is divided into fields.

**Solution:** Printing drums and printing rollers geared 2:3 so that despite several revolutions only one impression falls on the stamp field as the receipt passes.

**How it works:** A receipt is inserted at a feeler, which releases the machine and also positions the receipt on the field to be printed. Conveyor rollers carry it to printing rollers and printing drums A, B, C, each built from type wheels for the amount, the date and a designation number. The amount wheels are set through gears and a gear chain from a known interlock, secured by locking pawls, then disengaged; date and number wheels are set by hand knobs. The rollers turn three times per cycle while drums turn twice, so only one impression occurs, and the three drums print their amounts one after another. Inking rollers on spring arms ink the type carriers and cliche.
