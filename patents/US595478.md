## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typebar normally carries only one or two types, limiting characters per bar.

**Solution:** Pivot a segment-shaped type head with three types on the end of the typebar arm and rotate it by a lever striking an abutment as the arm swings.

**How it works:** A spring holds the head with the first type in print position. A lever on the arm moves with it; as the arm swings toward the platen the lever strikes one of two projections (short or long) on a pivoted abutment plate concentric with the arm pivot. That turns the head via a rod so the second or third type is in print position before the arm reaches the platen. With the abutment withdrawn the first type prints. A notched plate holds the abutment in its positions.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SHIFT:rotary](../features/shift-rotary.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** arm; bracket; type head; rod; lever; spring; stop pin; abutment plate; notched plate; key-lever rod

**Numbers:** three types per head
