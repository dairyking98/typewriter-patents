## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier carriage drives used two motors, or a reversible motor needing pole-changing switches and constant current.

**Solution:** A single low-torque electric motor, normally out of circuit and never reversed, drives the carriage in both directions through shiftable gears controlled by a reverse key; the circuit closes at key actuation.

**Features:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
