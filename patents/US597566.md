## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Gearings for conveying rotary motion with intermittently-driving members, e.g. with freewheel action between one of the shafts and an oscillating or reciprocating intermediate member, not rotating with either of the shafts; Radial cam

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to feed mechanism Ygenerally and especially to variable-feed mechanism, which may be used in connection with type-writing, matrix, and analogous machines lin which an actuated member or feed member normally in inoperative relation With suitable actuating mechanism may be intermittingly brought into engagement with a feed-actuator and moved a determined distance thereby.

**How it works (reading):** A series of feed actuators, each giving a fixed feed value, are engaged selectively, even by a jacquard card.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feed members in typewriters and matrix machines need variable feed distances chosen selectively.

**Solution:** A series of feed actuators, each giving a fixed feed value, are engaged selectively, even by a jacquard card.

**How it works:** A feed member normally disconnected is brought into engagement with one of several actuators, each imparting a different movement. Any combination or order of movements can be made, and the jacquard records the sequence automatically.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** feed member; feed actuator; jacquard; indicator
