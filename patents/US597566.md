## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feed members in typewriters and matrix machines need variable feed distances chosen selectively.

**Solution:** A series of feed actuators, each giving a fixed feed value, are engaged selectively, even by a jacquard card.

**How it works:** A feed member normally disconnected is brought into engagement with one of several actuators, each imparting a different movement. Any combination or order of movements can be made, and the jacquard records the sequence automatically.

**Features:** [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** feed member; feed actuator; jacquard; indicator
