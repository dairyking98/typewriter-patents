## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention has reference to sight assisting attachment for typewriters and consists of certain improvement-s fully set forth in the following specification and shown in the accompanying drawings, which form a part thereof.

**How it works (reading):** Mirror reflectors, optionally magnifying, let the operator view the type by shifting the ribbon lever instead of lifting the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriter operators must lift the heavy carriage to see the printing.

**Solution:** Mirror reflectors, optionally magnifying, let the operator view the type by shifting the ribbon lever instead of lifting the machine.
