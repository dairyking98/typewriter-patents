## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card business machine control)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to, and has in particular the training of the institution through which the implementation the control pulses is effected.

**How it works (reading):** Rotating commutators with contact brushes store impulses during one machine cycle and pass them to the display in the next, so no extra machine cycle is needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Converting card-hole control impulses for a display unit required a complex electrical conductor and switch system and slowed the machine.

**Solution:** Rotating commutators with contact brushes store impulses during one machine cycle and pass them to the display in the next, so no extra machine cycle is needed.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
