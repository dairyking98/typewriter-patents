## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter ο.

**How it works (reading):** A second linkage is added that follows the counter movement and can be separated from the coupling member by the hand-operated member, so the automatic control of a drive wheel can be switched off and more varied bookings made on a Mercedes Addelektra-type calculating typewriter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier counter-controlled drive couplings let the operator switch off only one drive wheel or all auxiliary wheels by hand, which limited the bookings that could be made.

**Solution:** A second linkage is added that follows the counter movement and can be separated from the coupling member by the hand-operated member, so the automatic control of a drive wheel can be switched off and more varied bookings made on a Mercedes Addelektra-type calculating typewriter.
