## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Underwood front-strike ribbons could only use one widthwise band; a cheap attachment was wanted to bring the upper or lower edge or colour band to the print point.

**Solution:** Shift the ribbon-vibrator lever sideways so one of two wrists at different distances from its fulcrum engages a slotted arm on the universal bar, giving a short or long vibrator throw.

**How it works:** The ribbon runs through a vertical vibrator moved by a lever driven from the reciprocating universal-bar frame. A bar carries the lever bodily from side to side. Two wrists on opposite sides of the lever, at unequal distances from the fulcrum, engage slotted arms on the universal-bar frame alternately. The far wrist gives a minimum throw (upper band), the near wrist a maximum throw (lower colour band). A key mechanism at the keyboard controls the shift.
