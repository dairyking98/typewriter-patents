## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines of the class in which a collating board is provided, on which are collated loose leaf ledger sheets, bills, or other work-sheets, the work-sheets usually being provided with regularly spaced marginal perforations to engage studs or pins on endless conveyers' running along the marginal edges of the board, whereby the sheets are conveyed to the platen and.

**How it works (reading):** Endless conveyors with marginal pins engage perforations in the work-sheets and carry them to the platen in register.
