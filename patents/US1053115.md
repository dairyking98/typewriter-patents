## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing on both faces of the printing material

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and.

**How it works (reading):** Paper controlling devices that keep the paper snug against the platen at the print line, with the paper carriage kept from being struck by the printing instrumentalities.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper must be held snug against the platen at the printing line and paper controlling devices must not obstruct printing.

**Solution:** Paper controlling devices that keep the paper snug against the platen at the print line, with the paper carriage kept from being struck by the printing instrumentalities.
