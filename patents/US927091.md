## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The timing of the carriage-feeding dog rocker relative to the universal bar and type action needs adjusting at the start, for wear, or for particular requirements, and fixed connections do not allow it.

**Solution:** An extensible two-bar link between universal-bar frame and dog rocker is lengthened or shortened by a hand-turned eccentric-slotted disk and then clamped by one bolt and nut.

**How it works:** A key press lowers the universal bar frame and an attached two-part slotted link; the upper bar's lip then presses the curved tread of the dog-rocker arm, rocking the feed dogs against the escapement wheel. Each bar carries a pin working in a slot of the other bar, and a sheet-metal disk between them has two eccentric slots engaged by the two pins. With the clamp nut loosened, turning the disk by its handle forces the pins apart or together, lengthening or shortening the link and so changing how far the lip sits from the tread. The nut then clamps both bars and disk. A sliding line-lock lug on the upper bar engages the line-lock hook.
