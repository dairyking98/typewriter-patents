> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a pad for holding punching material in place and small formats, such as postcards, in the typewriter, which compared to the Main patent has a simplified version, which is particularly useful for filling out of small formats of all kinds, such as checks, receipts, bill of exchange forms, pay slips, Delivery notes etc .; suitable. Experience has shown that the small formats in the typewriter are insufficient so that they move easily when you write on them, especially in the lower lines. Since in most cases copies are also made need to be, the work is made by the frequent insertion and removal of the Carbon sheet and carbon sheet very cumbersome and time consuming and also the The risk of the sheets of different sizes slipping out of place is greatly increased. The invention remedies this drawback. simple and practical Reject, in that the pad is provided with slot-shaped cutouts strips or sheets of paper going into the slots are arranged in such a way that that they are on the one hand on the upper edge of the slot-shaped cutouts and on the other hand are attached to the back of the pad.
