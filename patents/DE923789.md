## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Strips for supporting or holding papers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a pad for holding punching material in place and small formats, such as postcards, in the typewriter, which compared to the Main patent has a simplified version, which is particularly useful for filling out of small formats of all kinds, such as checks, receipts, bill of exchange forms, pay slips, Delivery notes etc .

**How it works (reading):** A pad with slot-shaped cutouts holds carbon sheets and the card in the slots so several cards can be written without re-clamping.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Small forms and carbon sets slip in the typewriter when written on, and copy sheets must be removed for each card.

**Solution:** A pad with slot-shaped cutouts holds carbon sheets and the card in the slots so several cards can be written without re-clamping.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
