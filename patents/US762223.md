## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writers, and particularly to the carriage construction thereof.

**How it works (reading):** Platen and platen-carriage frame are secured to the machine body with adjusting means so they can be removed and a wider or narrower platen fitted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen and carriage frame cannot be removed easily to swap in a larger or smaller platen.

**Solution:** Platen and platen-carriage frame are secured to the machine body with adjusting means so they can be removed and a wider or narrower platen fitted.
