## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot turn freely for small or large distances when the paper has ruled lines spaced differently from the line-spacing ratchet teeth.

**Solution:** A releasing mechanism throws the dogs or pawls out of engagement with the line-spacing ratchet wheel so the platen can be rotated in either direction.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
