## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (lamp letter display typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Signs formed by filament-type lamp

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to light typewriters, in which the individual letters are formed from incandescent lamps arranged in rows that are shared with a pole directly with the power source and individually the other pole is connected to a switchboard.

**How it works (reading):** Each lamp has a socket on a control panel and a banana plug, inserted through a hole in a common plate, connects that lamp to a letter; removable guide templates ensure the plug is put in the right place.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the letters of a lamp-type light typewriter from a remote location is inconvenient.

**Solution:** Each lamp has a socket on a control panel and a banana plug, inserted through a hole in a common plate, connects that lamp to a letter; removable guide templates ensure the plug is put in the right place.
