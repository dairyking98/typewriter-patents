## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a front-strike typebar machine with many typebars, the bar hangers are crowded so each bar gets only a narrow pivot bearing.

**Solution:** Mount the typebar hangers on a stepped segment in three staggered, overlapping sets, with bars of three lengths, so each bar can have a wide pivot bearing.

**How it works:** An upright type-bar segment has three concentric stepped faces at rising heights. Three sets of hangers (10, 16 and 16) are screwed to these faces, each hanger with its own screw reached from the front, so it can be adjusted radially and swung around its screw. The typebars of the three sets have different lengths and gooseneck shapes so their free ends rest side by side in one row at the printing point. Key levers act through sub-levers and pull links connected at different heights to the bars. The whole segment shifts on balls for case change, giving 42 bars with two types each, 84 characters.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type-bar segment; hanger; typebar; securing screw; securing plate; key lever; sub-lever; pull link; fulcrum bar; bearing ball

**Numbers:** 84 characters; 42 typebars; three hanger sets of 10, 16 and 16; two types per bar
