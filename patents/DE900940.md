## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (roller freewheel, writing machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a roller freewheel for writing, booking and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A roller freewheel with wedge-surface clamping jaws and a simpler structure couples and uncouples the paper roller from the line-index wheel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Roller freewheels between the paper roller and line-index wheel are complex, unreliable, and need much space in the paper roll.

**Solution:** A roller freewheel with wedge-surface clamping jaws and a simpler structure couples and uncouples the paper roller from the line-index wheel.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
