## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper strip transport, accounting machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pin traction elements other than wheels, e.g. pins on endless bands; with web storage arrangements by means of rollers, wheels, or chains, e.g. with pins transversely

**How it works (reading):** Pins mounted on holders that extend the chain links and stay within the joint-axis surface, so their tips are essentially on that surface and enter and leave the holes nearly at right angles, allowing holes almost the size of the pins.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pins on chain-driven strip feeds enlarge or tear the strip holes, because pin cross-sections are elliptical when they leave the strip plane.

**Solution:** Pins mounted on holders that extend the chain links and stay within the joint-axis surface, so their tips are essentially on that surface and enter and leave the holes nearly at right angles, allowing holes almost the size of the pins.
