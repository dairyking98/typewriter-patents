## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improves the author's single-key machine (US 306,295) in several particulars.

**Solution:** A movable type-bar slides on a rod to bring a type under a hammer, with a sliding index-plate carriage stepped by an escapement and a spring-drum and cord.

**How it works:** A carriage slides on parallel rods and carries an index plate, an inking device and an escapement. A notched bar vibrating on pivots carries a curved prong forming the hammer, and a lever with a plunger locks the carriage to notches in the rod, holding it still during printing. The carriage is pulled to the right by a cord on a scored drum with a coiled spring. A spacing lever for word spaces, a bell and hammer, a bent lever that disconnects the carriage from the rack bar, and an inking roll are also shown. The text breaks off before the type selection is fully explained.
