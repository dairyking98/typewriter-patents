## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** A base made of bent metal wire with loops for elastic material absorbs keystroke and carriage shocks and reduces noise.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber feet do not absorb the lateral shocks of the carriage or quiet the typing noise.

**Solution:** A base made of bent metal wire with loops for elastic material absorbs keystroke and carriage shocks and reduces noise.
