## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Releasing the platen from the line ratchet wheel needs much effort or movement.

**Solution:** A tooth clutch of spring-loaded swing arms is engaged by a conical hub and released by a plunger through the hollow platen axle, needing a short movement.

**How it works:** The line wheel carries a disc with two swing arms toothed at their ends, held inward by springs on a conical hub. The plunger pin in the platen's hollow axle pushes the disc, which first loosens the arms from the cone and then withdraws them from the inner ring of teeth in the platen's coupling sleeve, so the platen turns free. On release a spring pushes the disc back and the cone presses the arms into the teeth.
