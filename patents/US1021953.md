## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates especially to inking mechanism for typewriting machines and its general object is to provide improved devices of the character specified.

**How it works (reading):** Inking devices are made to work with a carbon strip as well as an ink ribbon and can be added cheaply to existing machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inking devices built for the ordinary ribbon cannot easily take a strip of carbon paper.

**Solution:** Inking devices are made to work with a carbon strip as well as an ink ribbon and can be added cheaply to existing machines.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
