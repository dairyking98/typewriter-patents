## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that character of machine utilized for the production of stenoils and in the production of stencils which are usually made from heavy paper.

**How it works (reading):** A stationary dial with a pointer on a rotatable die carrier, plus male dies operated on their strokes and a stencil-sheet feed that is driven by the die operating mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The dial of a stencil-cutting machine should stay fixed while the die carrier moves, so the operator can see which die position is in use.

**Solution:** A stationary dial with a pointer on a rotatable die carrier, plus male dies operated on their strokes and a stencil-sheet feed that is driven by the die operating mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Dial or knob](../features/sel-dial.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
