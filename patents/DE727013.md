## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a drive device for the ribbon fork of typewriters, which not only makes it possible to alternate the ribbon in two different positions to bring, such that once, the lower and then the upper half of the ribbon is put into use, but through which it can also be achieved to put the ribbon completely out of action while writing, for example when writing on wax sheets for duplication purposes.

**How it works (reading):** Two offset bearing eyes on the operating lever and a vertical slot let the ribbon fork be fully disengaged in the middle bearing position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon fork lever engages on wax stencil writing even when the ribbon should be out of action.

**Solution:** Two offset bearing eyes on the operating lever and a vertical slot let the ribbon fork be fully disengaged in the middle bearing position.
