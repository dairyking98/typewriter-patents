## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hammond anvil and shuttle were limited in size, limiting the number of characters on the shuttle.

**Solution:** Rearrange the ribbon spools to turn on horizontal axes transverse to the machine, so a larger anvil and shuttle fit with more characters.

**How it works:** The shuttle carries the type and a hammer prints through a ribbon passing along a shield across the shuttle face, ends on two spools at each side of the center frame. The spools turn in vertical front-to-rear planes, driven through toothed wheels. A pawl on an arm fixed to a rock-shaft is operated by a rod from the universal space-frame, stepping the ribbon spool with each key. A detent engages the wheel and a cam-collar shaft can lift the pawl out to choose which spool feeds. Ends of the arms also lift a wire loop of the ribbon shield.
