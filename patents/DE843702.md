## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier on the paper apron concentric with the roller platen

**How it works (reading):** A paper guide plate pivots on slides in the carriage side walls with pressure and guide rollers, and lugs on the paper holder lift it against a spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper guide plates on lever typewriters must be lifted off the platen when the paper holding bracket is raised.

**Solution:** A paper guide plate pivots on slides in the carriage side walls with pressure and guide rollers, and lugs on the paper holder lift it against a spring.
