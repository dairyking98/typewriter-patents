## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typebar case-shift should be simple, and the leverage of the type bar actuating means should stay the same in upper and lower case.

**Solution:** Shift the whole type basket via parallel links and use sub-levers with a sliding fulcrum rod so leverage is substantially uniform in both cases.

**How it works:** Second-order key levers carry angular sub-levers pivoted at varying distances, whose horizontal arms are slotted over a fulcrum rod hung on swinging links. Upright sub-levers of the third order, on a pivot wire in a kerfed support on the typebar segment, drive the typebars through pins in inclined slots in their heels. The segment hangs on two sets of parallel links with screw stops limiting the shift.
