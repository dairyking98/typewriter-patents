**Problem:** Check protectors of the period were not controlled electrically; the text is a poor OCR read of the stated object.

**Solution:** An electrically controlled check protector: an electric circuit operated with the platen, with the protector printing under electrical control.

**How it works:** Electric circuit closed by the platen movement controls the check-protecting print.
