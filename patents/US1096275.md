## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter key and type wheel action is slow and heavy on the keys.

**Solution:** Flexible spirals in rigid tubes carry key thrust to type pawls, and locking bars spring up to stop a rotating member that sets the type wheel.
