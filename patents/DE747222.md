## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (interrupting automatic zero print on calculators) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for Interrupting the automatic zero printing in the case of arithmetic or accounting systems, in which the driver pawls that trigger the automatic zero pressure by means of the Paper trolleys arranged stops or the like can be controlled automatically.

**How it works (reading):** Actuators on the paper carriage move driver pawls through levers on a shaft running in the carriage direction, so the pawls are held back and released as needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic zero printing must be interrupted at any column, so many actuators are needed and the older design is bulky.

**Solution:** Actuators on the paper carriage move driver pawls through levers on a shaft running in the carriage direction, so the pawls are held back and released as needed.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** driver pawls; actuators; shaft; levers; paper carriage stops

**Numbers:** eleven actuators for a twelve-digit printing unit
