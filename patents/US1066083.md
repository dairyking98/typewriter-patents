## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an overthrowcheck for line-spacing mechanism, and is herein illustrated as applied to an Underwood typewriting machine, and comprising a hand lever driving the usual plunger on which is pivoted a'pawl, which engages a ratchet Wheel on the axle of the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A detent roller in the ratchet wheel holds the platen, and a cam on a bell-crank arm of the detent forces the line-spacing pawl into the ratchet teeth, so overthrow is prevented.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing by the space lever can overthrow the platen, so it does not return to the same alignment each time.

**Solution:** A detent roller in the ratchet wheel holds the platen, and a cam on a bell-crank arm of the detent forces the line-spacing pawl into the ratchet teeth, so overthrow is prevented.
