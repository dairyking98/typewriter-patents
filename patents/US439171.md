## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improve a key-driven type-wheel machine (Myers' English patent 14,651 of 1888 and pending application 317,689) in wheel arrangement, platen adjustment, locking of the wheel at the print position, word spacing and construction.

**Solution:** Keys rotate a light multi-row type wheel by an amount set by lever travel, the whole main frame carrying the wheel is pivoted near its centre of gravity so it is depressed to print, and the wheel is locked exactly before the strike.

**How it works:** Finger-key levers on a shaft each depress a transversely lying wheel-operating frame by a distance depending on the key; gearing from this frame turns the type-wheel pinion and so rotates the type-wheel to a definite angle. The wheel sits above the platen on an overhanging standard, on a shaft that both oscillates and slides lengthwise so any of six parallel circles of type (capitals, small letters, figures and rarer signs) can come to the print point; this keeps wheel diameter and weight small. The main frame carrying nearly all the mechanism is pivoted to a fixed bed on an axis through its centre of gravity, held up by springs and a release catch, and is depressed to print, taking less force than earlier machines. The wheel is locked in its exact position before printing, and word spacing and platen adjustment are separately provided (text truncated).
