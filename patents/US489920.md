## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to a papercutting and guiding attachment to and measuring device for type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment holds a supply roll, has a cutter/perforator and anvil at the impression roller to sever the written part, a length-measuring device, and a take-up roll driven by contact with the supply roll.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Separate sheets are slow to load and length is hard to measure; the inventors want to use a roll of paper.

**Solution:** An attachment holds a supply roll, has a cutter/perforator and anvil at the impression roller to sever the written part, a length-measuring device, and a take-up roll driven by contact with the supply roll.
