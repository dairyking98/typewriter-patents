## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating device attached to a typewriter, or used with it, uses a differential device with a notched bar and a stop carried by the carriage, so one key operation positions the carriage for the units of the number.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A typewriter has no tabulating device, and a single operation should release the carriage and bring it to a stop in position to print the first figure of a number.

**Solution:** A tabulating device attached to a typewriter, or used with it, uses a differential device with a notched bar and a stop carried by the carriage, so one key operation positions the carriage for the units of the number.
