## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink evaporated from the pad when it was at rest, so ink was wasted and the pad dried out.

**Solution:** The ink well and pad sit on arms that swing and slide on a rod to bring the pad against the face of the type wheel only while inking, and a protecting pad covers the inking pad at rest.
