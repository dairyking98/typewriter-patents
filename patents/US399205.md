## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants a simple, strong, cheap typewriter, easily handled and understood.

**Solution:** A sliding carriage with ratchet bar and thumb-wheel platen under a stationary frame carrying a letter plate and a type-bar frame.

**How it works:** A base plate has grooved strips in which a rectangular carriage slides. A ratchet bar on one side of the carriage spaces it, and a platen rod with a thumb wheel marked with space marks is journaled in the carriage; a movable index pin gives equal line feed. A stop and stud stop the carriage from leaving the base. A standard on the base supports pivoted arms with a type-bar-supporting frame and stationary arms with a letter plate, with a central guide over the platen in line with the strike. The remainder of the mechanism is cut off in the text.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** base plate; grooved strip; carriage; ratchet bar; platen; thumb wheel; movable index; spring retaining rod; paper guide; stop; standard; type-bar frame; letter plate; central guide
