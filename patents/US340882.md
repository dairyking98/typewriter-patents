## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On Caligraph-type machines extra letter spacing for legibility needs repeated strokes of the space key.

**Solution:** The slots joining the two feed-rack halves are lengthened and an adjustable stop covers more or less of each slot, so each stroke feeds one, two or three teeth.

**How it works:** The feed rack is split longitudinally and the halves are joined by pins in slots. The slots are made long enough for about three teeth, and a movable stop (a sliding notched stop with thumb piece, a pivoted stop, or an eccentric circular stop with spring dog) covers part of a slot to limit the relative stroke. The carriage thus advances a chosen number of teeth per key stroke.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** feed rack; sliding rack; slot; pin; adjustable stop; thumb piece; spring dog; ratchet

**Numbers:** slots long enough for about three rack teeth

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph: adds an adjustable stop so the feed per stroke can be set to one, two or more teeth
