## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** A two-position switch holds indexing pawls out of engagement with the ribbon reels, moved by an energy store charged during spool rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal needed force at the switching moment, which strained the ribbon.

**Solution:** A two-position switch holds indexing pawls out of engagement with the ribbon reels, moved by an energy store charged during spool rotation.
