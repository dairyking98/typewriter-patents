## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Front-stroke type-bar actuation should start with minimum resistance and rising speed while keeping bars in order.

**Solution:** A flexing connection from the type-bar projection to a fixed point, with the finger-key forming part of it, throws the bar by a pull, with a shifting type-bar support.

**How it works:** Type-bars pivot on a curved rod in a segmental support and lie horizontally forward, resting on a buffered segment. Each has a projection extending down and back, joined by a universal joint to a flexing connection whose front end joins a fixed frame piece. Links join the projection, the key stem and the fixed point, so depressing the key deflects the connection and pulls the bar to the print point; resistance and bar velocity rise through the stroke. Return is by rebound, gravity and a spring on the projection. The type-bar guide and support are shifted for case. The printing point sits slightly above the platen axis to cut foreshortening.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** type-bar; segmental support; pivot rod; flexing connection; link; finger-key; spring; back cushion-stop; type-bar guide; platen
