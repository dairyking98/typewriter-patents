## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter in which a number of overlapping paper webs or a web laid in longitudinal folds is described and individual parts of the same be separated after passing around the platen and after writing, and in which the interposed sheets of coal are withdrawn up to one new place to be written on the paper webs.

**How it works (reading):** The platen frame is made to swing to two positions, which lets both lower and upper case be written and carbon sheets be withdrawn.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen-swinging machines do not allow case shift; the platen must hold one position for capitals.

**Solution:** The platen frame is made to swing to two positions, which lets both lower and upper case be written and carbon sheets be withdrawn.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
