## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in tabulators for type-writing machines'.

**How it works (reading):** An improved tabulator with better connections to the tabulator stop, a lock that prevents carriage release except when the tabulator is operated, and a means to release the escapement while tabulating.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The tabulator is inaccurate if the carriage stop sits over the notched tabulator plate, and beginners can run into this.

**Solution:** An improved tabulator with better connections to the tabulator stop, a lock that prevents carriage release except when the tabulator is operated, and a means to release the escapement while tabulating.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** tabulator stop; notched tabulator plate; carriage-stop; escapement release; connections to tabulator stop
