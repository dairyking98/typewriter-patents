## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must otherwise keep typing past the end of the line, so an automatic stop at the line end is wanted.

**Solution:** A tappet on a rack-bar on the carriage depresses a lever that moves a bail-frame to engage a stud on the universal-bar frame, locking the key-levers at line end, with a release key to override.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
