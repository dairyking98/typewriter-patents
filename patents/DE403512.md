## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**How it works (reading):** A key only releases a spring-loaded drop shoe that a cam on a constantly turning shaft then catches and carries to throw the typebar.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Motor driven typewriters need many joints between key and drive and are unreliable.

**Solution:** A key only releases a spring-loaded drop shoe that a cam on a constantly turning shaft then catches and carries to throw the typebar.

**How it works:** The key lever turns a pawl that releases a drop shoe, which a compression spring pushes onto a constantly rotating motor-driven shaft. A cam ridge along the shaft engages a cutout in the shoe and carries it, pulling the type lever linkage to strike. The same movement lifts the shoe back to the pawl. Keys need only about 1 mm travel.

**Features:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** key lever; pawl; drop shoe; compression spring; motor-driven camshaft; type lever

**Numbers:** key travel about 1 mm
