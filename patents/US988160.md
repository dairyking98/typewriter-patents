## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing syllables fast on a typewriter without a larger machine.

**Solution:** Syllable typebars carrying multi-letter types are placed at the sides of the typebar circle, with their own key rows and traction rods, and advance the carriage by the syllable's letter count.

**How it works:** Normal single-type bars and keys stay as usual. Extra typebars at the machine sides carry syllables (types with two or three characters). Extra key rows in front connect to them by horizontal traction rods and sub-levers. Extensions of the syllable bars act on concentric universal rails that move the escapement, so the carriage advances two or three spaces. Examples: to-get-her, ma-ch-in-e.
