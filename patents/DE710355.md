## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method for attaching type bodies to type levers or type bars.

**How it works (reading):** The type body is spot-welded to the type lever while clamped in heat-conducting jaws, with a narrowed heat path and cooling, so the printing face stays hard.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Soldering or riveting types to type levers is unreliable, and heating during spot welding softens hardened types.

**Solution:** The type body is spot-welded to the type lever while clamped in heat-conducting jaws, with a narrowed heat path and cooling, so the printing face stays hard.
