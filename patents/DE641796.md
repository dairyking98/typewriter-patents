## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (photoelectric teleprinter character transmission) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or local circuits for mosaic printer telegraph systems at the transmitting end

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relate to the particular training the device for selecting the one in the path of the projecting light beam bringing letter banners as well as the control of the perforated disc.

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter-like keyboard moves a transparency with the character set into the light beam, and a perforated disc scans it to make current pulses.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electrical telex must send letter images as light pulses, which need a fast scan of each character.

**Solution:** A typewriter-like keyboard moves a transparency with the character set into the light beam, and a perforated disc scans it to make current pulses.
