## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (electric switch drive) · **Subject (patent classes):** Switches having rectilinearly-movable operating part or parts adapted for pushing or pulling in one direction only, e.g. push-button switch having a single operating member the contact returning to its original state upon the next application of operating force with contact-driving member rotated step-wise in one direction

**How it works (reading):** A T-shaped prism on the push button rides in a leaf-spring slideway and locks in notches to alternate the end positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electric switch positions need a simpler and cheaper alternating-stop mechanism that works from one push direction.

**Solution:** A T-shaped prism on the push button rides in a leaf-spring slideway and locks in notches to alternate the end positions.
