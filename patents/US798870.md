## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and has for its object to provide for supporting a roll of paper upon the carriage of the machine in a novel and simple manner, whereby a continuous feed of paper may be effected, and thereby avoid the interruption commonly occasioned by the removal of a written sheet and the insertion of a new sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A paper-roll holder attached to the carriage supports a roll of paper so the feed is continuous.
