## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (zero lock for totalizing typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices for printing the point

**How it works (reading):** A carriage-dependent locking member moves into the path of the type lever carrying the zero, blocking that type when needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic totals printed a leading zero in the tens position for single-digit values, making figures unclear.

**Solution:** A carriage-dependent locking member moves into the path of the type lever carrying the zero, blocking that type when needed.
