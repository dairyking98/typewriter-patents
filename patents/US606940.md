## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Tabulation of columns needs the carriage to skip from the end of a word to a fixed starting point on each line, without shocking the escapement.

**Solution:** A detachable stop-plate on the spring barrel, with a movable stop key, arrests the carriage actuator at preset points while the feed escapement is released.

**How it works:** The carriage is pulled by a spring barrel through a winding ratchet and normally controlled by the rack and feed dogs. A stop-plate with projections at the wanted starting points is attached to the barrel cover. Pressing the stop key disengages the escapement so the spring pulls the carriage and puts a stop in the path of the nearest projection, which halts the barrel. Releasing the key restores the escapement and normal step feed. A brake prevents undue acceleration during the jump.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** carriage; spring barrel; spring; winding ratchet; rack; feed dog; stop-plate; stop projection; stop key; brake

**Improves on:**

- jump feed controlled through the feed escapement: stop acts on the actuator so the escapement takes no shock
