## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Printing several closely spaced lines (for example three tiers) at one place needs the printing position to be changed quickly among them.

**Solution:** Three interlocked button levers on the spring-urged platen cylinder, caught by a sawtooth spring claw, set the cylinder to the upper, middle or lower tier when the matching button is pressed.

**How it works:** The printing cylinder is spring-urged to turn one way. The middle lever is fixed to the cylinder shaft; the upper lever is loose on its root and rests on it, and the lower lever is loose on the shaft with its projection under a projection of the middle lever. The projections fit between two sawtooth claws of a spring standing on the frame. Pressing a button chains the levers so they are caught together or freed one at a time, and the cylinder spring turns the cylinder by the thickness of each projection, so the tier moves by that amount.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Device for a typewriter for printing in any one of a fixed number of lines, changing between them at will. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a device for a typewriter for printing in any one of a fixed number of lines, changing between them at will, made as follows. On a printing cylinder that is always urged to turn in one direction, a pressing lever corresponding to one tier of lines (段行) is fixed; separate pressing levers corresponding to the other tiers are provided; and the levers are linked to one another so that pressing the required lever turns the corresponding tier of the cylinder to the printing position. Its object is that, when any line of a fixed number of lines is to be printed, the printing position can be changed at will simply by pressing a button or the like corresponding to that line.
