## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing clutches between the platen roller and the line-spacing ratchet wheel with radial pins give too weak a clamping action.

**Solution:** Radial pins pushed out by a cone bear on the middle of balls arranged around them, so the balls clamp the line-space wheel to the platen axle much more firmly.

**How it works:** A cone-ended actuator slides lengthwise in the hollow platen pin, driven by a lever whose eccentric end bears on the platen knob extension. In the coupled position the cone pushes three radial pins outward. Each pin has a conical foot that acts centrally between four balls sitting in recesses on the platen pin outer surface, and an outer end pin guided in a ring groove in the line index wheel. The balls clamp the wheel to the platen pin. Turning the lever about 90 degrees lets a spring pull the cone back, the pins retract and the clamp is released so the platen turns freely.
