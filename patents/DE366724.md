## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A single release key lifts the line-end lock and also disables the line-start stop, with the rail that moves the margin-stop rail on to the line-lock lever also carrying the key motion to the rail when the start stop is disabled.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Line-end and line-start margin stops need two separate release devices, so releasing the line lock at line end and disabling the line-start stop each need their own action.

**Solution:** A single release key lifts the line-end lock and also disables the line-start stop, with the rail that moves the margin-stop rail on to the line-lock lever also carrying the key motion to the rail when the start stop is disabled.
