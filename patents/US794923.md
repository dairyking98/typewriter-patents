## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism for type-writing machines, and more particularly to mechanism for automatically reversing the direction of feed of the ribbon when an end thereof is approached;

**How it works (reading):** A mechanism reverses the direction of ribbon feed automatically as an end of the ribbon is reached, with simpler parts than earlier designs.
