## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles characterised by being applied to printers having transversely- moving carriages and mounted on the carriage

**How it works (reading):** A roughened area on the front guide table, placed opposite the alignment bar, grips the inserted sheet and stops it from sliding longitudinally; a groove in the guide table also takes an endless paper tape from a roll below.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On front paper guide tables, sheets inserted in turn push earlier sheets out of their alignment.

**Solution:** A roughened area on the front guide table, placed opposite the alignment bar, grips the inserted sheet and stops it from sliding longitudinally; a groove in the guide table also takes an endless paper tape from a roll below.
