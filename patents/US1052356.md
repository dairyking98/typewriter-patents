## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A continuously rotating multi-disk type wheel in a telegraph-style printing machine must be shifted axially to bring one of its type disks to the hammer, and stepping directly between positions is imprecise.

**Solution:** The wheel is spring-pushed to one end position and held by a stop at the chosen position; to change position the stop is fully released so the wheel first returns to the end, then a cam-driven mechanism lifts it to the new position.

**How it works:** The type wheel, with three disks of types on its rim, is keyed and splined on a shaft that turns continuously and can slide axially. A spring presses the shaft down onto a stop lever, which a locking nose holds at position I, II or III. A magnet couples a cam on the timing shaft for one revolution, which releases the nose so the shaft drops to its lowest position. A cam groove then drives a slide down, and a bell crank on it meets or misses an adjustable stop, set by a cam fixed on the type-wheel shaft according to the rotary position. This lifts the shaft to the highest, middle or lowest position and the nose relocks it. The hammer then strikes the paper strip against the chosen type.
