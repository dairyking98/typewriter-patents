## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine housing)

**How it works (reading):** A rigid metal frame holds walls of non-resonant molded panels such as cork pulp, paper pulp or molded insulating material, which damp vibration and deaden noise.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Metal-framed calculating machine housings ring and resonate, and glass housings are fragile and costly, while cloth or wicker walls are unstable.

**Solution:** A rigid metal frame holds walls of non-resonant molded panels such as cork pulp, paper pulp or molded insulating material, which damp vibration and deaden noise.
