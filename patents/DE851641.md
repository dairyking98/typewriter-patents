## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon-style copies need a printing fluid that makes its own colour when it meets a sensitized sheet, without separate carbon paper.

**Solution:** A printing fluid of ink plus colour former (crystal violet lactone in a chlorinated diphenyl solvent with a dye) is carried on a substrate; it reacts with an attapulgite-coated sheet to give a second colour.

**Features:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
