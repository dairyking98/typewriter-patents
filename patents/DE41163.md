## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text does not state a problem outright; it aims at a typewriter with type levers arranged radially in a circle, self-inking types, and even letter spacing with paper moved crosswise and line-fed.

**Solution:** Type levers arranged radially in a circle each carry a type to a common centre point at the paper, passing a ring-shaped ink pad on the way, while a pressed key also moves a paper slide by a number of ratchet teeth matched to the letter width.

**How it works:** Pressing a character button turns a lever whose rack drives a toothed segment on a swinging arm, giving it about half a turn; the arm swings the type lever so its type passes the ring-shaped felt ink pad at the start of the stroke and then strikes the paper against a rubber-covered roller at the centre. A spring returns the button, lever and type to the ink pad. The same key also depresses a central column by a distance set by the length of the lever's short arm, which rocks an angle lever and swings a spring pawl across a toothed rack on the paper slide, advancing the slide by one, two or three teeth according to letter width (narrow I, medium N H E, wide M W); a separate space plate moves the pawl further or disengages it for free hand movement. The paper runs between two feed rollers on the slide, the roller frame folds forward to insert paper, and a lever with pawl and ratchet advances the paper two lines per full swing, with a handwheel for free feed.
