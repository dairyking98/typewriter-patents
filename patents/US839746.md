## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in stops for the carriages of type-writing inachines, and is more particularly adapted for use as a stop in connection with tabulating devices for type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A detachable, adjustable stop made of sheet metal, with slots that engage the notches of the stop-bar, so it can be placed and held securely.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops must be easy to attach, detach and adjust, and must sit compactly on the stop-bar.

**Solution:** A detachable, adjustable stop made of sheet metal, with slots that engage the notches of the stop-bar, so it can be placed and held securely.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** stop-bar with teeth and notches; stop body with slots
