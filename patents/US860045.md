## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bar bearings clog with sand, rubber and paper dropped at the front of the platen, and the parts are hard to assemble.

**Solution:** A type bar and hanger bearing with hollow sleeves and a bifurcated heel that is protected against dust and is easy to assemble.
