## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Telegram-style fixed-form printing with a copy needs quick ribbon changes, fixed line positions with a cut-off at set length, and paper rolls that move with the carriage without shock.

**Solution:** An attachment for a European-script typewriter: a swing-away copying ribbon fed by the line-feed lever, ruled paper-guide plates with a cutting knife, and a spring-coupled swinging frame for original and copy paper rolls.

**How it works:** A copying ribbon runs between two reels on a plate swung on a rod, held parallel to the platen at the print face by two guide rollers; the line-feed lever pushes a feed bar that turns a ratchet and pawl so the ribbon advances once per line, and a bent lever lifts the pawl so the reels turn free for changing. Swinging the ribbon support down lets the copy paper be wrapped on the platen. Sloping guide plates above the platen, hinged on swinging arms, carry ruled lines to set line positions, and a full-width knife on the arms cuts the original and copy when pulled forward. The two paper rolls sit in a frame hung on swinging posts, coupled to the carriage through coil springs that absorb the shock of each letter-space.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Copying device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a copying device for a typewriter, made up of a copying-ribbon support and feed mechanism, a paper-guide plate device and a paper-roll support mechanism. Its features are: a paper-guide plate is provided above the platen, able to move with it; the frame of the guide plate can be raised and lowered and carries a knife at its front end; the guide plate bears ruled lines that fix the lines to be printed; and the ribbon-support mechanism is pivoted on the machine frame so that the ribbon can be brought close to the platen or moved away from it. The object is to improve existing devices of this kind: to make putting in and taking out the copying ribbon simple and quick; to print fixed-form matter such as telegrams accurately and quickly and always cut it off at a set length; and further to remove as far as possible the shocks that come with the movement of the frame carrying the two paper rolls.
