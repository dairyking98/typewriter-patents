## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sawing the type-bar slots in a cast-iron segment springs the segment, so the slot widths vary and each type-bar must be fitted by hand to its slot at high cost.

**Solution:** The segment is thickened not only at the slotted portion but also up to and past it, which minimizes springing so that the slots stay even and type-bars can be finished alike and fit without individual fitting.

**How it works:** Extra material is cast around the slotted periphery and the anvil rib, stiffening the segment during slot cutting so the bars fit the slots without individual filing.
