## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon-feed mechanism for type-writing machines;

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon direction must be reversed by hand or by costly parts, and only a strip of ribbon width is used.

**Solution:** Automatically reverse the ribbon feed direction when a given length has passed from one spool to the other, and feed it crosswise so the whole width is used.

**How it works:** Only the opening is available. Shown on an under-strike typebar machine with a spring-drum carriage driven by key levers, a universal bar and feed dogs on a rocker. The ribbon is fed spool to spool and, when an amount has been wound, reversing mechanism changes direction. A transverse feed also steps the ribbon sideways. Details are cut off.
