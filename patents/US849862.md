## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machines used in dim rooms make the printed matter hard to read because the platen is poorly lit.

**Solution:** A mirror on an adjustable bracket, clipped to the machine, reflects light from a chosen direction onto the platen so the printing is visible.

**How it works:** A mirror in a frame is held on brackets clamped to the machine and angled to bounce light onto the platen roll.
