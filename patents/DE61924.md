## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines' keys and levers were hard to repair or adjust.

**Solution:** Easily removable keys, bent hook connections, adjustable hangers and V-rail rollers for the carriage.

**How it works:** Keys sit on pins with star heads clamped by hard rubber buttons so a pin can be removed by turning. Key rods are guided by links and coupled via rods with a nut thread for length. Type levers are solid rods on hanging tongues, screwed to a ring plate and adjustable radially and in angle. The carriage with V ribs runs on grooved rollers on rails.
