## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a typebar machine with three characters per bar, the platen must be shifted up and down to reach the upper, middle and lower type while still being rigidly backed against the typebar blow, and its distance from the type must be adjustable for the number of sheets.

**Solution:** The platen carriage rides on a vertically shiftable rail that is spring counterbalanced on a rock shaft, while a rigid tie rod anchored in the front wall forms an unyielding abutment behind the platen.

**How it works:** A tie rod anchored in the front wall runs rearward, drops and rises into a vertical horn whose slot receives a roller block, forming an abutment that stops the platen yielding when the typebars strike. The platen carriage rides on a rail for letter spacing, and the rail is carried on legs sliding in vertical ways on the tie rod. A rock shaft with arms engaging slots in the legs lifts or lowers the rail, driven by two shift levers acting through a link and a cam-track lever with dwells at the centre and either end. A coil spring around the rock shaft, with an adjustable ratchet collar, counterbalances the carriage and rail so the shift takes little force. Turning a handle on the front screw thread slides the rear tie rod portion fore and aft to set the platen distance from the type plane for more or fewer sheets, with a pointer and scale.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [IMP:anvil-backing](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md), [IMP:impression-adjust](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen carriage; rail; tie rod; horn; rock shaft; counterbalance coil spring; ratchet collar; shift lever; cam track; adjusting handle; pointer and scale

**Improves on:**

- earlier shiftable-platen typewriters: adds a rigid abutment behind the platen and a counterbalance so shifting is easy and the platen does not recede under the blow
