## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keystrokes are noisy and the printing feel is hard.

**Solution:** A platen roll is an air-inflated rubber casing on heads and a spindle, with supporting disks and stiffening, giving a sound-deadening, slightly elastic print surface.
