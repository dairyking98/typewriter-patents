## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Poor alignment and uneven blow of typebars, and slow return of downward-striking bars.

**Solution:** A forked spring plate under each typebar hinge, with its V-slot in the bar's path just short of the type, equalizes the blow and aids return.

**How it works:** A steel spring-plate is screwed beneath the heel of each typebar and extends radially to a fork in the path of the bar slightly short of the type head. The forks lie a little above the paper. As the bar descends it passes through the fork, which resists the blow evenly and returns the bar quickly. Plate thickness and width set the resistance.
