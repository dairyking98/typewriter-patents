## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting niachines and more particularly to bearings for type bars and the object of said invention generally stated is to provide a simple and ellicient anti-friction roller construction to effectively support and guide the type bars in their movements.

**How it works (reading):** Type bars are arranged segmentally on a type bar segment, each on a bifurcated hanger, with anti-friction rollers and spacing rings.

**Problem:** Type bars need a simple, low-friction support and guide in their movement.

**Solution:** Type bars are arranged segmentally on a type bar segment, each on a bifurcated hanger, with anti-friction rollers and spacing rings.
