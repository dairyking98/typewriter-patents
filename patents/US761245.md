## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to that class of typewriting machines in which the writing is produced on top of the platen, so as to be exposed freely to the inspection of the operator.

**How it works (reading):** Type-levers carried on curved steel bars with lazy-tongs mechanism, one per type-lever, an ink pad under the type, and a thumb-lever line-spacing ratchet on the platen.

**Solution:** Type-levers carried on curved steel bars with lazy-tongs mechanism, one per type-lever, an ink pad under the type, and a thumb-lever line-spacing ratchet on the platen.
