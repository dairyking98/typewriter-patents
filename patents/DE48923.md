## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Soennecken wants a compact easy typewriter whose types are quickly replaceable by other fonts.

**Solution:** One sledge sliding both sideways and forwards/back carries two rows of types in a dovetail-mounted plate and is set by a handle into depressions in an alphabet board, then a key drives a hammer from below to print.

**How it works:** Sledge s runs on two round rods and can lie on either; this brings the upper or lower type row over the hammer, so pulling or pushing the handle g selects capitals or lower case. The sprung handle is pressed into depressions in the alphabet board b to locate the letter. The left hand then presses key m on a two-armed lever, raising hammer h through a hole in a diaphragm so the paper is pushed up against the type; the type plate slides along an ink ribbon so it is always inked. The same key stroke tensions a spring and a resilient pawl which engages the rack of the paper carriage and, on release, advances it. The rack is rotatable by a lever through 60 degrees so the carriage can be returned freely. Paper passes between pinch rollers held by a rubber band, with a line-feed knob and pawl, and the type plate slides in from the side by a dovetail.
