## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon refreshing with hydrocarbon vapours was slow because mixed-boiling liquids left heavy fractions behind.

**Solution:** Using vapours of hydrocarbons of uniform boiling point saturates ribbons faster and reduces storage time.

**How it works:** Ribbons are refreshed with vapours of a single-boiling hydrocarbon, so they saturate quickly and evaporate uniformly.
