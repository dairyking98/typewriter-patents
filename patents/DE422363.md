> Description Translated from German Typewriter. The invention relates to a device on typewriters, the advance of the writing carriage by letter width when you hit the space bar should cause without lifting the ribbon at the same time. With typewriters of the usual type are facilities that fulfill this purpose, known in which a special linkage bypassing the drive for the ribbon lift on the Acting pawl carrier. The present invention aims to provide such a device for creating such typewriters where the escapement is on the moving one Switching frame is arranged, while the drive linkage in the fixed machine frame is stored. This is achieved according to the invention in that the space bar driven drive lever for the escapement engages directly on the pawl carrier, without being in rigid connection with it, the drive lever being a part of the pawl carrier detected, which is parallel to the direction of movement of the switching frame extends.
