## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon must be advanced to present a fresh surface to the types, ideally without extra operator action.

**Solution:** Ribbon spool shafts are ratcheted forward by a segment rack driven by the forward movement of the paper carriage.

**How it works:** A spring barrel on a stud shaft feeds the carriage by a strap, its tension set by a ratchet and nut. A pin gear on the barrel meshes with a cogged segment on a pivoted arm, carrying a curved rack segment that engages a ratchet wheel on a ribbon spool shaft. Arms at both ends are linked by a bar so they move together, and spring catches hold the rack segments out of engagement when not wanted. Ribbon spools slide on slotted shafts and ribbon shifters at each end move the ribbon sideways.
