**How it works (reading):** Each selectable tooth series has normal-pitch zones at both ends of the rack, with the deviating pitch confined between them so every line switches at the same place.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-balancing racks switching between tooth series at different points in different lines produce uneven type spacing.

**Solution:** Each selectable tooth series has normal-pitch zones at both ends of the rack, with the deviating pitch confined between them so every line switches at the same place.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Line balancing device for typewriters The invention relates to on line balancing devices for typewriters, in which a rack is provided with several series of teeth of different pitches, each series of which can optionally be brought into action for itself. With the known such Devices extend the individual series with the same division over the entire length of the rack. Here there is the possibility that in different Lines switch from a tooth series of a division to such a other division can be made at different points and thus an uneven one Typeface emerges. According to the invention, this disadvantage is now eliminated in that for each tooth series that can be selected, the pitch deviating from the normal one between tooth zones with normal pitch arranged at both ends of the rack lies. This training enables a line adjustment in certain columns and thus the reliable achievement of a uniform typeface. In addition to the zones at the ends with normal pitch, you can one or more intermediate zones of normal pitch and the tooth zones with a division that differs from the normal between these zones of normal division be arranged.
