## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters are slow to assemble, mechanisms cannot be tested or adjusted before mounting, and repair needs the working parts to be reached inside the frame.

**Solution:** Build the lower action (key levers, bell cranks, shift and ribbon-vibrator parts) and the upper action (type bar segment, type bars, guides, ribbon vibrator, universal yoke) each as a self-contained unit that is tested, then fixed into the frame with a few screws and withdrawable as a whole.

**How it works:** The lower unit frame has a bar, two end plates and forward and rear guide combs carrying the key levers, bell cranks, space bar rock shaft, shift levers, ribbon-vibrator universal bar and ribbon-spool reversing worm gear, and is screwed in from below. The upper unit is a bracket carrying an arcuate type bar segment that slides vertically on ball races between three positions for three-row typebars, a cushion, a center guide and a universal yoke that works the escapement. Key levers are folded and resilient and drive type bars through bell cranks with arm lengths varied so each link works at right angles. Shift levers raise or lower the segment through slotted cam arms with interlocks. A cam-slot ribbon vibrator equalises the travel of the three key-lever lengths and segment positions.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** lower action unit frame; key lever; bell crank; guide comb; type bar; type bar segment; ball race; slotted shift cam; shift lever; universal bar; ribbon vibrator cam; ribbon spool; reversing worm shaft; universal yoke; escapement lever; space bar

**Numbers:** three banks of key levers; three characters per type bar; key arm to bell-crank short arm length increase ratio 3 to 2 (example); 9 sheets of drawings

**Improves on:**

- conventional typewriters assembled piece by piece in the frame: pre-assembled, testable and removable unit actions
