## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating normally needs the operator to press tabulating keys; the inventor wants tabulation to happen automatically as writing proceeds, with no care or judgment from the operator.

**Solution:** Adjustable disengaging devices set on the perforated feed rack cooperate with a second member so the rack is lifted off the feed pinion and the carriage skips freely to a preset column.

**How it works:** The carriage feed rack, pivoted on rear arms, meshes with a feed pinion tied to the escapement wheel. Removable disengaging devices with arms fit in recesses and perforations of the rack and can be set at any letter-space position. A cooperating cam-faced member (with a dwell) near the pinion bearing meets the device as the carriage travels and disengages rack from pinion, so the carriage skips until the device passes. A hand-controlled member can hold one of the two disengaging parts in or out of cooperation, making the automatic tabulating operative or inoperative. A modified form is shown in Figs. 6-8.
