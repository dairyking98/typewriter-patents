## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards or small sheets of paper must be held in close contact with the platen as they pass the printing point on an Oliver typewriter.

**Solution:** Resilient paper guides attached to a standard hold the card against the platen at the printing point.

**How it works:** Applied to the Oliver machine (US599863) with U-shaped typebars in two sets either side of the striking point. A lower guide plate directs the sheet between the platen and a lower rear presser roller, and an upper guide plate receives its advancing edge. Added guides on a standard, with some resiliency, press short sheets or cards against the platen near the print point.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** platen; paper guide; guide plate; presser roller; feed roller; guide finger; scale strip; standard; carriage frame; typebar

**Numbers:** three types per typebar
