## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods on side surfaces thereof, e.g. fixed thereto the types or dies being movable relative to the bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention consists in the platen itself being adapted to be adjusted, relatively to the type, both from group to group and also within each group.

**How it works (reading):** The platen frame is made adjustable relative to the type, in steps from one character group to the next as well as within each group, with hook-and-hole stops fixing each position.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machines carrying two character systems (e.g. German and Russian) need a way to switch between systems as well as between case and figures without jamming.

**Solution:** The platen frame is made adjustable relative to the type, in steps from one character group to the next as well as within each group, with hook-and-hole stops fixing each position.

**How it works:** The platen roller sits in a roller frame that is held in a shift frame by spring-loaded hooks engaging holes. Pressing a handle swings the hooks out so the platen can be pushed down to a second set of holes, which brings the second group of three characters (capital, small, figure) on the typebars to the print point. Within each group the ordinary shift roller and lever move the frame for case and figures. Racks and pinions form a parallel guide so the roller frame cannot skew or jam. Three groups can be used instead of two.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [USE:special-script](../features/use-special-script.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen roller; roller frame; shift frame; locking hook; hook spring; handle; rack; pinion; shift roller; shift lever

**Numbers:** two groups of three characters per typebar

**Improves on:**

- Typebars with grouped type and ordinary shift: the platen itself is also stepped from group to group so two writing systems share one keyboard
