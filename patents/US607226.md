## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Remington-type machines need a smoother, quieter carriage escapement, better ribbon movement, and paper-pressing devices, all covered in one long specification.

**Solution:** An improved carriage escapement of an escape wheel with a fixed holding dog and a loose feeding dog (with an anti-rattle device), plus a cam that moves the ribbon crosswise and paper-pressing bands.

**How it works:** The carriage is fed by an escapement wheel acting on a rigid dog and a feeding (loose) dog, the latter carrying an anti-rattling device; the same dogs can work on a straight rack instead of a circular one. The escapement wheel can be lifted to release the carriage from the dogs. A long ribbon-driving shaft with a cam rotated by a latch lever moves the ribbon guide plate and the ribbon crosswise. Thin paper-pressing bands, a paper finger and a small paper-feed roll mount on an adjustable bracket on the platen carrier's front bar. A gravity detent is also described. Only the opening of the specification was available, so details of the later parts are not read.
