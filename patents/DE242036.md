## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column stops must be moved by hand and selecting them for tabulation takes time and care.

**Solution:** A selector follows the carriage so that any number of column stops on a toothed rod are placed in working position by carriage rest positions and returned when needed.
