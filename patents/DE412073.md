## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check strip feed on-off)

**How it works (reading):** An eccentric adjusting disc on the feed roller shaft is turned by hand to disengage the check feed, and the same disc moves a locking lever so that the print hammer is out of action and the cash box gear is held.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The check-slip feed on a cash register must be switched on and off at will, and simpler devices of this kind are mechanically complicated.

**Solution:** An eccentric adjusting disc on the feed roller shaft is turned by hand to disengage the check feed, and the same disc moves a locking lever so that the print hammer is out of action and the cash box gear is held.
