## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of my invention is to provide in a type-writing machine an improved platen which may be quickly and easily adjusted so that the platen-roll may be freely adjusted independent of the line-spacing mechanism or so that the same may be adjusted by the line-spacing mechanism, as desired.

**How it works (reading):** A platen spindle made of sections, threaded and carried by the platen cradle, with a friction spring; the platen can be set free or locked to the line-spacing mechanism.
