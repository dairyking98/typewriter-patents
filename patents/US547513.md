## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printed or written matter on the sheet is visible to onlookers as it passes through a type-writing machine.

**Solution:** A concealing plate fixed in front of the cylinder, with guides behind it that hide the sheet, plus a front copy-holder for a manuscript.
