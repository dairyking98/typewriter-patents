To aZZ whom it may concern.- Be it known that I, THOMAS -HENRY BUR CHELL, a citizen of the United States, and a resident of New York, in the county of New York and State of New York, have invented certain new and useful Attachments for Type: Writing Machines, of which the following is a specification. In all type-writing machines, as far as lam informed, the written or printed sheet is exposed to View as it passes through and out of the machine, and persons standing near the machine are able to-read what is being written or printed. This is objectionable, and I have sought to remedy the defect by an attachment designed, primarily, to conceal from View the written or printed matter as it passes through, out of, or into the machine, and, further, to provide a copy-holder conveniently arranged infront of the operator…
