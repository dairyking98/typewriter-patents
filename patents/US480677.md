## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stereotype matrix line justification) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide means for facilitating the justification of lines of print produced by the successive impression of type-dies into a yielding mate rial employed in making a matrix from which to cast stereotype-plates.

**How it works (reading):** Feed movements tied to each character's space-units are adjusted so the last character of each line lands at the line end.
