## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon shifts, e.g. for exposing print, for case-shift adjustment, for rendering ink ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to new and useful improvements in type-writers, and more especially to ribbon-carrying attachments;

**How it works (reading):** Detachable guides on the carriage plates hold slides that carry an auxiliary ribbon, kept off the paper by a tensioner and quickly removable.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage has no way to hold a second, differently coloured ribbon clear of the platen.

**Solution:** Detachable guides on the carriage plates hold slides that carry an auxiliary ribbon, kept off the paper by a tensioner and quickly removable.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
