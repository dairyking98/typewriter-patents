**How it works (reading):** A single wheel and member follow the lateral carriage motion and guide, via a rail at fixed points, to counters in a separate base housing, so one link serves several counters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Cash-register or calculator counters linked to the typewriter carriage need a sequence of linkages that grows with the number of counters.

**Solution:** A single wheel and member follow the lateral carriage motion and guide, via a rail at fixed points, to counters in a separate base housing, so one link serves several counters.
