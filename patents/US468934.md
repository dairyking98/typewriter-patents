## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen cannot be turned back for interlineation, keys can type over each other at line end, and paper guides handle only wide sheets.

**Solution:** Several Caligraph improvements: a lever that frees platen pawl and detent together, a key lock at line end, a simpler escape-pawl release, a narrow-sheet paper guide, and a supplementary feed roller.

**How it works:** A pivoted lever on the carriage lifts the platen's actuating pawl and detent from the ratchet at once so the platen turns backward. Key depression is blocked automatically when the carriage reaches the end. A jointed lever lifts the escape pawl from the rack to return the carriage. A guide passes narrow sheets and envelopes, and an extra feed roller feeds paper nearer its bottom edge.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet wheel; actuating pawl; detent; releasing lever; escape pawl; rack bar; feed roller

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph: adds platen release, end-of-line key lock and a supplementary feed roller
