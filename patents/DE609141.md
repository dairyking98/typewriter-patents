## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (ball point writing device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink cartridges; Arrangements for feeding ink to the ball points

**How it works (reading):** Balls sit free in their chambers against an adjustable stop pin, set by a threaded bolt and outer tube, so line weight is fixed by the setting; a steep thread lifts the stop to feed ink.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ball-tip writing instruments give line weight that depends on the writer's pressure, and ink flow is hard to start and stop cleanly.

**Solution:** Balls sit free in their chambers against an adjustable stop pin, set by a threaded bolt and outer tube, so line weight is fixed by the setting; a steep thread lifts the stop to feed ink.
