## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stenographic typing of words and syllables at one stroke needs a mechanism that selects several type in sequence from simultaneously pressed keys.

**Solution:** A key-impelled type wheel makes one cycle through all type, checked by key-controlled locks at each struck key's type, with a spring-motor impression at each stop.

**How it works:** Pressing one or several keys impels a type-wheel through a full revolution through a yielding connection that stores power if the wheel is held. Locking stops controlled by the keys check the wheel with a struck key's type at the print position. Abutments moving with the wheel, one per type, operate detent-displacing mechanism set by the keys. That releases a spring motor to bring the impression bed and wheel together, then the lock retracts so the wheel moves on to the next struck key's type. The paper carriage feeds as the printing couple separates.

**Features:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Platen pressed to the element](../features/imp-platen-to-element.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md)

**Parts:** type wheel; key; yielding connection; locking stop; abutment; detent; spring motor; escapement pallet; impression bed; paper carriage
