## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A portable visible-writing typewriter must collapse small without disconnecting the key and type-lever linkages.

**Solution:** Keys and typebar mechanism are rigidly mounted in one common frame hinged against the carriage so both fold together, with the whole machine enclosed in a box.

**How it works:** The key mechanism and typebar basket are connected by rods in a single frame that swings up about a rod in the box housing, so nothing is disconnected on folding. Paper carriage bed and ribbon spools lie flat against the box wall. The universal rail connects with keys and typebars through cam levers. A hinged cover closes the box, the space key folds inward, and articulated supports hold the frame at the right height when open, giving a bag-sized unit.
