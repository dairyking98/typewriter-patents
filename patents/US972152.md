## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Backward-stepping devices for corrections existed only for spring-and-escapement carriages, not for machines whose carriage is moved by a positively driven feeder.

**Solution:** A mechanism that gives the carriage a limited movement backward on a Blickensderfer-type machine whose feeder engages a rack bar only when it feeds.

**How it works:** The carriage has a rack bar and is under no stress from any feeding device except when engaged. A toothed feeder formed with an arm on a bell crank engages the rack bar to feed and is disengaged otherwise. The new mechanism moves the carriage a limited distance opposite to the feed direction, cooperating with the advancing devices to position it. Key levers rock a shaft whose gear meshes with racks moved by sectors, operating the type wheel as in Blickensderfer's US583840.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)

**Parts:** carriage; rack bar; feeder; bell crank; key lever; rock shaft; gear; rack; sector; type wheel

**Improves on:**

- backspacing devices on escapement machines: adapted to a positively fed carriage
- [US583840](../patents/US583840.md): type mechanism taken from this earlier Blickensderfer patent
