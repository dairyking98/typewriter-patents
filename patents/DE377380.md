**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with totalizer power drive)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Typewriter with several computers and one controlled by them Total calculator or totalizer.

**How it works (reading):** The totalizer is carried on its own carriage with a separate spring, and a source of power returns it to start after the last digit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A totalizer driven by the carriage in typewriters was pushed and then sprung back, which disturbed the carriage's movement.

**Solution:** The totalizer is carried on its own carriage with a separate spring, and a source of power returns it to start after the last digit.
