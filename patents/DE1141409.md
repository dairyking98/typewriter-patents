## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dye affinity of cold-drawn synthetic threads varies along the thread and between batches.

**Solution:** Cold stretching of linear polyamide threads in the solid state is controlled so the thread cross-section, elongation and dye affinity stay uniform.
