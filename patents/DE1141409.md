> Description Translated from German It is known that freshly spun rayon threads in a semi-coagulated state, i.e. before solidification, to stretch to reduce the thread diameter. The shrinking of the threads has also been avoided, by winding them up under greater tension. The one obtained in these procedures However, stretching was within relatively small limits. In contrast, the invention relates to the stretching of threads made of linear polymers in cold, solid state several times its original length, which is known to have a longitudinal orientation the thread molecules enter. Linear polymers can be deformed into threads from the molten state, and the shaped ones Threads must be stretched cold. Cold stretching involves stretching the threads by means of tension in the solid state, ie below the melting point of the polymer, understood, whereby by the molecular Orientation achieved a compensation while improving the mechanical properties of the threads will. The filaments then have crystal orientation, as can be determined by X-ray examination along the thread axis.
