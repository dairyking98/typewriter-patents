## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Counter control pieces were fixed to the counter and needed replacement to change which cross-counters were coupled.

**Solution:** The actuator is a cam disk with several points of action, arranged over the control piece so it can set the control piece without ever moving it automatically.

**How it works:** Rotatable multi-point cam disk actuator presets control pieces so that the counter coupling drives the left, right or both cross-counters.

**Features:** [Dial or knob](../features/sel-dial.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
