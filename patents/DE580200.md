## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**How it works (reading):** The jig uses only a few recesses matched to the type height, with adjustable retaining jaws on either side of the lever's plane of symmetry that hold the body in its correct lateral position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Placing type bodies on type levers needs many recesses for every type shape, and bodies with wide slots lack lateral support, so their lateral position is not fixed.

**Solution:** The jig uses only a few recesses matched to the type height, with adjustable retaining jaws on either side of the lever's plane of symmetry that hold the body in its correct lateral position.
