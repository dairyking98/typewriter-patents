## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Underlines and rules have to be made by repeated hitting of keys, which is slow, ties both hands, and tears carbons; earlier line-type proposals were complicated or damaged the ribbon.

**Solution:** A locking lever on the machine frame puts a line-type lever in working position and couples the ribbon spool to the carriage drive, so pressing the tab or carriage release key draws a continuous line.

**How it works:** The line-type lever (or line wheel) carries a stroke type. A lever on the outside of a side plate locks it in the working position and also operates a clutch, driven by the carriage, that moves the ribbon in step with carriage advance. Reversing the ribbon spool drive at the same time moves the control shaft for ribbon reversal. Pressing the carriage release or tab key then makes the carriage run and draws the line evenly, independent of font, with the ribbon moving in sync so it is not damaged.
