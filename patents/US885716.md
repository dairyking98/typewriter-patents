## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Opening text does not state a clear problem; a machine with a stationary flat platen and moving print carriage with ribbon mechanism.

**Solution:** Carriage carrying the printing mechanism travels over a stationary flat platen, with new ribbon mechanism.

**How it works:** A stationary flat platen on posts has rails; a line-space frame on rollers moves by rack bars and pinions. A carriage on a rod and rail runs on it. Ribbon spools have clamps for the ribbon end. Only the opening is available.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** flat platen; post; rail; line space frame; rack bar; feed pinion; carriage; ribbon spool; clamp
