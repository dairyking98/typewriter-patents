## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars deflected in use, spoiling alignment.

**Solution:** A lightweight bent-double steel type arm whose pivot spacing is adjusted by a screw to remove play.

**How it works:** A flat steel bar bent double carries a type block at the bend. A third bar with a 45-degree extension is riveted between the parallel parts. Steel pivots on the free ends sit in a hanger, and a screw through the three bars sets the pivot spacing to adjust bearing pressure. A rod from the extension to the key lever swings the arm.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Numbers:** pivot angle about 45 degrees
