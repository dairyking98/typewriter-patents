## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies arranged in stationary or sliding cases or frames or upon flexible strips, plates, bars or rods; Japanese-language typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a right-hand-operated Japanese typewriter with a moving type tray, in which the operator, holding in the right hand a handle fitted to the type store, picks out and points to the required type by a character pointer fixed to a type tray that can slide freely forward, back, left and right over the base;

**How it works (reading):** The first part of the handle press drives pointed pieces into a cross rack and a lengthwise rack, locking the tray in both directions before the later part of the press prints.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** With a one-handed Japanese typewriter whose type tray is slid by hand to point at a character, it is hard to hold the pointer exactly on the character while pressing the handle, so printing is slow and misaligned.

**Solution:** The first part of the handle press drives pointed pieces into a cross rack and a lengthwise rack, locking the tray in both directions before the later part of the press prints.

**How it works:** The type tray slides left and right on a frame that slides forward and back on the base; the operator moves both with one handle and a pointer over a character plate. Pressing the handle turns an arm whose pin lifts and lowers a slider; the slider's point drops into a cross rack on the sliding frame, fixing the sideways position. The same arm presses a plate cam along the tray's travel, which pushes a second slider whose point enters a lengthwise rack on the base, fixing the front-to-back position. Both racks have a pitch equal to the type spacing. Further pressing of the same handle works the printing mechanism (not described).
