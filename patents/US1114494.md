## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting the coupling that reverses the ribbon drive must happen quickly and with certainty.

**Solution:** Ribbon-controlled fingers trip a ratchet-driven shifter that throws a longitudinally sliding bevel-gear shaft to the other spool when the ribbon runs out.

**How it works:** The ribbon runs between two spools whose shafts carry bevel gears. A continuously driven shaft with bevel pinions slides longitudinally to engage one spool gear or the other, driven from the carriage drum through pawl, ratchet and gears. A center-pivoted shifter held in mid-position by a leaf spring and linked by a lost-motion disk between collars to the shaft is thrown by teeth meeting a ratchet wheel. Fingers resting on the ribbon drop when a spool empties and release the shifter, and stop collars prevent gear jamming.
