## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the present invention is a typewriter-mounted apparatus for manufacture of copies with at least one auxiliary ink ribbon guided past the platen, which can be pivoted out of the writing position, and with a friction roller drive that takes place from the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoting attachment guides an auxiliary ribbon past the platen, driven by friction from the platen; one manual switching element couples one ribbon roll to the drive and uncouples the other, and a spring returns it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making copies on a typewriter needs an auxiliary ink ribbon, and reversing its travel meant handling each ribbon roll separately.

**Solution:** A pivoting attachment guides an auxiliary ribbon past the platen, driven by friction from the platen; one manual switching element couples one ribbon roll to the drive and uncouples the other, and a spring returns it.
