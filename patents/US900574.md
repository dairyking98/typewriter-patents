## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen frame of the Underwood quivers up and down on the carriage in fast typing because it is spring counterbalanced for easy shift.

**Solution:** Latches lock the platen frame to the carriage and are released by the shift key before the platen shifts.

**How it works:** Latches on a rock shaft at the carriage ends catch over a rod on the platen frame. An auxiliary rail on the shift rail engages a swiveled roller on the latch shaft to release them first, then the main shift rail lifts the platen. On return the latches snap back.
