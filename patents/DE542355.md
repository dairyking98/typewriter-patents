## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** The edge stop rail with its margin stops and setting scale is attached to a paper table hinged to the carriage, while the rail moves on the machine frame; tilting the paper table forward releases the margin stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Margin stops on a typewriter need a separate release means, and it is hard for the writer to find and release the stop.

**Solution:** The edge stop rail with its margin stops and setting scale is attached to a paper table hinged to the carriage, while the rail moves on the machine frame; tilting the paper table forward releases the margin stop.

**How it works:** Arms hinged behind the platen carry the paper table; its bent lower edge rests against the paper guide; the rack and margin stops sit under the table, and forward pivoting of the table moves a stop out of the way of the carriage stop.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
