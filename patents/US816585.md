## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A small sheet cannot be written near its bottom edge or copied onto a larger record sheet at a chosen spot, because the paper cannot be pushed back around the platen.

**Solution:** The paper is positioned by moving it around the platen so the bottom portion reaches the printing point, letting a small sheet and a larger carbon-copy sheet be aligned at a chosen spot; the paper-deflector is arranged to permit both pushing and pulling.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)

**Parts:** platen; paper-deflector beneath the platen; carbon-copy sheet; record sheet
