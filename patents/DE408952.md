## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a collapsible and self-erecting Sheet holder, especially for typewriters, and consists in that the intermediate part of the holder is assembled in a parallelogram shape, and that on both sides of the intermediate part connect swingable angle levers attached to the machine, act on the tension springs.
