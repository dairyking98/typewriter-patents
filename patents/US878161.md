## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Envelope addressing needs a margin setting that is not fixed on the stationary frame.

**Solution:** A margin regulating device is mounted on the moving carriage and cooperates with a stationary stop to set varying line start positions.
