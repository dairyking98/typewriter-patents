## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need improved feed, inking and a way to get two characters per bar.

**Solution:** Each typebar carries a pivoted shifting type-holder that is flipped from an inked face-down position to the printing position as the bar swings.

**How it works:** On a Remington-type frame with key levers and rods to typebars arranged in a circle, each bar has a type-holder pivoted at its free end. At rest the holder presents the types face down to an ink pad; an extension on the holder strikes a bar or plate as the bar swings, turning the type to face the paper on the platen. Further described are a line-feed mechanism for the roll, a release rod, carriage bearings, and attachments letting the feed mechanism operate a ribbon feed.
