**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Coin-freed facilities or services for means for safe-keeping of property, left temporarily, e.g. by fastening the property

**How it works (reading):** A coin-operated release unlocks the printing mechanism for one document, and the lock resets after printing through a common driver that also feeds the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are to be used only after a coin is paid, which needs a lock on the printing mechanism.

**Solution:** A coin-operated release unlocks the printing mechanism for one document, and the lock resets after printing through a common driver that also feeds the paper.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
