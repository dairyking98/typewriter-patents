## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers on a universal-rail machine are hard to replace, because the release bridge must be pushed out by hand from the front and held down during removal.

**Solution:** A pull rod on the front of the key housing releases the universal-rail lever so a type lever can be removed without holding the release.
