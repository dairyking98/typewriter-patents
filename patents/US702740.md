## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor wants to ink the type directly, just before the impression is made on the paper.

**Solution:** An inking device mounted to swing in and out of the path of the type-bars, so it touches each type just before printing. The type of inking device (roller or pad) and its drive are not clear from the OCR.
