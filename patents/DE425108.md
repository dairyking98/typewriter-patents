## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforated web drum) · **Subject (patent classes):** Label feeding from strips, e.g. from rolls the labels being cut from a strip

**How it works (reading):** The drum circumference is slightly larger than the multiple of the perforation pitch, and loosely resilient pins sit in the radial holes so each pin can move freely into a perforation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A fixed-pitch drum with rigid pins stops feeding a perforated paper web when the perforation spacing varies by fractions of a millimetre.

**Solution:** The drum circumference is slightly larger than the multiple of the perforation pitch, and loosely resilient pins sit in the radial holes so each pin can move freely into a perforation.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** supply roll a; switching drum d; guide roller c; pins e

**Numbers:** hole spacing about 0.8 mm larger than perforation pitch
