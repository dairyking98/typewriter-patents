## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters either print out of sight with full type rings, or print in sight with only partial arcs of type-bar pivots.

**Solution:** Pivot the typebars in a whole ring in four 90-degree arcs around the platen so printing is visible and a full ring is retained.

**How it works:** Bars pivoted in the top, left, right and bottom quadrants strike the platen from different directions. Bell-crank rock shafts with transverse axes link the upper bars to key levers off to the sides, keeping the line of sight clear. The paper carriage shifts to several printing levels and the platen can be lifted to view the work. A vibrating inking ribbon is brought over the printing point at impact then retracted out of sight.
