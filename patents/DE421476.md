## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (tactile board for matrix casting machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for assembling matrices and space bands

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a tactile board for 1, tatrizenetz- and Zeiiengießinaschiner, in which eccentrics mounted in levers or yokes through Revolving rollers arranged underneath are offset in rotation when the key is pressed will.

**How it works (reading):** The drive rollers have triangular ribs or grooves on their periphery that engage matching triangular-section eccentrics, so the key stroke turns the eccentric positively.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber-coated drive rollers in matrix-setting touch boards wear out, and oil makes them slick, so eccentrics slip and matrix release is delayed or fails.

**Solution:** The drive rollers have triangular ribs or grooves on their periphery that engage matching triangular-section eccentrics, so the key stroke turns the eccentric positively.
