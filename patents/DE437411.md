## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A key-driven stop must act very fast yet never repeat, even when the key is held down.

**Solution:** A key-moved pawl releases an eccentric disc, which a motor roller turns, and a pawl then holds it after one revolution.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
