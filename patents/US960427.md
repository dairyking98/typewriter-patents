## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to paper feeding mechanism for typewriting machines and its general object -is to provide improved devices of the class specified.

**How it works (reading):** Paper feed devices are made so the release key or hand lever can be operated without freeing the paper, and the leading edge stops are positioned for the next sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single release key frees all paper from the feed mechanism, so a record sheet cannot be kept in place while bills are changed.

**Solution:** Paper feed devices are made so the release key or hand lever can be operated without freeing the paper, and the leading edge stops are positioned for the next sheet.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** paper feed devices; release key; leading edge stop devices; platen
