## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A disappearing ribbon guide on a visible-writing typewriter could not present different colour stripes of a polychrome ribbon.

**Solution:** Shift the fulcrum of the ribbon-guide lever so the guide stops at a different height, presenting the chosen colour stripe at the print line.

**How it works:** A lever raises the ribbon guide into the type path at each stroke and withdraws it afterward, oscillated by a slot in the universal spacing frame. Its fulcrum sits on a bar pivoted to the lift rail and linked to an arm on a shaft. Turning the shaft with a knob, held by a ratchet and spring pawl, moves the fulcrum and so raises or lowers the guide. The elongated slot allows the extra vertical motion. Shown on an Underwood.
