## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbon must advance and reverse automatically without extra operator effort, using little energy.

**Solution:** The universal shaft rocked by every typebar drives a ratchet and shifting shaft with worm wheels that wind either ribbon spool, and shifting the shaft reverses direction automatically.

**How it works:** A typebar strikes the universal bar, which rocks the universal shaft; that raises the ribbon frame and carries a pawl back over a ratchet on a shifting shaft. On release a spring returns the shaft and the pawl turns the ratchet one tooth. Worm wheels on the shifting shaft engage gears on the two vertical spool shafts, winding the ribbon onto one spool. When the ribbon is fully wound, the shifting shaft slides longitudinally so the other worm engages, reversing the feed (reversal detail is past the opening text).

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** key-bar; typebar; universal bar; universal shaft; ribbon frame; ribbon spool; ratchet wheel; pawl; holding pawl; shifting shaft; worm wheel; spring
