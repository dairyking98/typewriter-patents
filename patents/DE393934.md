## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing couplings between paper roller and line-index ratchet wheel either could not hold the roller in every position (toothed ratchet with hooks) or needed very precise parts to avoid bending and to let the release cam return to neutral.

**Solution:** A pair of spring-loaded pinch rollers on pawls couples roller and ratchet wheel steplessly, and a single lever, swung by a twist of the central knob rod, retracts all the rollers at once to release the platen.

**How it works:** The ratchet wheel sits in a housing around a disc fixed to the paper roller's hollow shaft, and springs push paired pawls apart so their rollers wedge between disc and housing ring, transmitting line-space rotation to the roller. Turning the knob rod through a small angle swings its bent end in a recess of the shaft, which rotates a lever whose shoulders push all the pawls inward together, freeing the rollers so the roller turns independently. Further rotation brings the rod end against the shaft and carries the platen round by hand. A spring ring returns the knob and rod to the neutral central position when released. Variants link the pawls through plates with notches, or use a curved disc and a split lever plate that frees diagonally opposed rollers first.
