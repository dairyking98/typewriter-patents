The invention relates to a device for typewriters for coupling the paper roller to the line index wheel using diametrically opposed pinch rollers which are held in the clamping position by springs. Devices in which the rollers are pushed out of the clamping position by the insertion of bolts or the like are known, the bolts being moved by axially displacing a rod. In other devices, the decoupling of the paper roller and the ratchet wheel is achieved by rotating an axially arranged rod, during the first part of the rotation the decoupling takes place, whereupon the paper roller is taken along in the further part of the rotation. The locking hooks are brought out of engagement with a toothed ratchet wheel, and the paper roller is taken along by the clamping position of the cam between the locking hooks.
