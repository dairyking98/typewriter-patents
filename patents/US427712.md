**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an attachment for an ordinary type writing machine which spaces equally for all the characters printed, to be used in the production of copy to be re produced 011 matrix-machines which have a variable spacing corresponding to the width of the characters printed.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Copy for matrix (justified, variable-spacing) machines must be prepared on an ordinary equal-spacing typewriter so lines will justify when rewritten.

**Solution:** A spring-driven pointer and dial attached to a Caligraph steps by the matrix width of each character typed, so the operator knows when a matrix line is full.

**How it works:** A pointer on an arbor travels over a graduated dial, with a ratchet wheel held by a dog and urged by a coiled spring. Characters are divided into five or six groups of equal matrix width and their key-levers act on an escapement. A toothed sector arm engages the ratchet wheel, and the arm's vibration, limited according to the key group, lets the wheel advance by a different number of units for each group. A cord and finger-lever rewind the pointer before each line, and an alarm is mentioned. When the units reach a line's total, the operator starts a new line.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** pointer; dial plate; arbor; coiled spring; ratchet wheel; dog; finger-lever; cord; toothed sector arm; escapement plate; key-lever

**Numbers:** five or six groups of characters of equal width
