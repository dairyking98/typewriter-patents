## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons are hard to change without soiling the hands, and changing colour or ribbon is slow.

**Solution:** A reel has a removable flange and a slot split in the hub for the ribbon, and a guide at the reel cup leads the ribbon out.

**How it works:** The ribbon is threaded through the slot and the removable flange lets the ribbon reel come off cleanly.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** removable reel flange; split hub slot
