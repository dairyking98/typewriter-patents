## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention has reference to the paperto carriages of type-writing machines, and more especially to the pressure-roller and paperguides thereof, and has for its main object to provide a simple and efficient means for supporting these devices in position and for regulating the force of the pressure-roller.
