## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen pressure rollers and paper guides on the Yost typebar machine needed simple support and an easy way to regulate how hard the roller presses the paper.

**Solution:** Hang the pressure roller from pivoted arms at the rear of the carriage, pulled to the platen by coil springs whose tension is adjusted with turnable pegs, and carry curved paper guides on the roller shaft.

**How it works:** Two arms pivot on the rear bar of the carriage frame and hold the pressure roller at their lower ends. Coiled springs run from the arms to the carriage side bars and pull the rubber roller against the platen. Turning a slotted peg that has a cross-pin in the spring coils winds coils onto the peg or releases them, raising or lowering spring tension and roller pressure. Semi-cylindrical paper guides are sleeved on the roller shaft at the back and slide on pins in the front rail, so they follow the roller as it moves and turn the paper around the front of the platen. A line-space lever and push-pawl on a ratchet at the platen end complete the feed.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** platen; pressure roller; pivoted arm; coil spring; adjusting peg with cross-pin; paper guide; carriage frame; line-space lever; push-pawl; ratchet rack; retaining dog
