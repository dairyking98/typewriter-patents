## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line feed for cash register documents) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register or accounting machine with a line feed device for accounting documents.

**How it works (reading):** The line feed is made dependent on which special key (old balance, disposals, new balance, check II) is pressed; the conveyor rollers are driven only for certain keys and separated from each other for certain others to release the document.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On an accounting machine the slip feed must advance the document a line only for certain booking keys, and the document must be releasable at the right moment.

**Solution:** The line feed is made dependent on which special key (old balance, disposals, new balance, check II) is pressed; the conveyor rollers are driven only for certain keys and separated from each other for certain others to release the document.
