## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with one right and one left edge stop and another edge stop to indent a line.

**How it works (reading):** A two-armed rocker on a leaf spring sits mid-frame, and a key slides a counter-stop into the path of the left and right edge stops to indent a line; a spring returns it, and the leaf spring softens the impact in either direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage impacts on the edge stops were hard, and the indent stop needed a separate counter-stop path.

**Solution:** A two-armed rocker on a leaf spring sits mid-frame, and a key slides a counter-stop into the path of the left and right edge stops to indent a line; a spring returns it, and the leaf spring softens the impact in either direction.
