## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (temporary deactivation of clear character printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an apparatus for temporarily deactivating the plain character printing device on typewriters and the like There are devices of this type are already known in which the clear character printing device with the deactivation of the carriage step lock Pressing a tab key or the carriage release key simultaneously and automatically is rendered ineffective.

**How it works (reading):** When the cross counter or its driver linkage is disengaged, a linkage at the same time disables the clear-character printing device, using the tab or carriage release action.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The clear-character printing device still printed when the cross counters were at zero and the counters were not in use.

**Solution:** When the cross counter or its driver linkage is disengaged, a linkage at the same time disables the clear-character printing device, using the tab or carriage release action.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** cross counters 10 and 11; step lock; two-armed lever 13 14 15; spring 13U
