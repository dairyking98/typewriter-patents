## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**How it works (reading):** The engaging part of the pawl swings on the rest of the pawl against its own spring, limited by a pin in an enlarged bore, so the return spring is independent of the pawl engagement part.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A one-piece pull or push pawl must be strong where it engages the drive shaft yet light where the spring attaches, so making it is costly and the spring tensions must be matched.

**Solution:** The engaging part of the pawl swings on the rest of the pawl against its own spring, limited by a pin in an enlarged bore, so the return spring is independent of the pawl engagement part.
