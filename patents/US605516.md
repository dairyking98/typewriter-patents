## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Emphasising words in a different colour needs ribbon changes.

**Solution:** Two coloured ribbons are coiled in separate compartments of divided rollers shifted sideways by a key.

**How it works:** Partitioned rollers are splined on rotating shafts and slid endwise by a frame on a sliding bar worked by a lever from a key, bringing the black or the contrasting ribbon to the print point.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** divided roller; shaft; frame; sliding bar; lever; key; ribbon

**Numbers:** two ribbons
