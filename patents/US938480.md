## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The escapement must advance the escape wheel one tooth in two steps, a small then a large or the reverse.

**Solution:** A pair of dogs, one a first-order and one a second-order lever, in a U-shaped head of a rocking lever so one moves positively as the other moves.

**How it works:** A rocking lever operated by a push-pull rod from the universal bar has a U-shaped head. Dogs 13 and 14 pivot in it, one as a first-order lever, the other as a second-order lever, linked by a pin in a recess so moving one moves the other the opposite way. They engage the teeth of the escape wheel, which advances the distance between two teeth in a smaller and a larger step.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** rocking lever; dog; escape wheel; universal bar; push-pull rod; pin
