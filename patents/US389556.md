## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars falling back into the basket of a typebar machine make noise, and the old tape-wrapped oval type-rest wears out quickly under the impact.

**Solution:** A type-rest whose soft felt cushion is clamped edgewise in a C-section metal rim so the typebars land on the felt's edge.

**How it works:** A flat metal strip is bent to a C section. A felt or cloth fillet is folded lengthwise and its folded edge is inserted in the C, and the edges of the C are pressed to bite the felt. The strip is then bent into an oval and its ends soldered. Hooked arms riveted or soldered to the rim fasten it to the machine frame. Falling typebars strike the projecting felt edge, which cushions them silently.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** type-rest; metal rim; felt cushion; supporting arm; hook; rivet

**Improves on:**

- spirally tape-wrapped oval type-rest: felt edge presented to the falling bars is more durable and quieter
