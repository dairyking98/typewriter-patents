## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A line-switch stop for several line-spacing values must be adjustable, exact, reliable and take little space.

**Solution:** A single flat rotatable body on the carriage side wall has stop surfaces of different sizes and distances from the pivot, one for each line-spacing setting; a rod spring notches the body into each stop position.
