## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates 'to a device for use in typewriting machines for the purpose of indicating to the eye-the rinting point on the paper, or the point at w ich the type will strike the paper when the paper-carriage is released from its letter-spacing mechanism and moved by the hand of the o erator to a point at which it is desired to egin' a line or word, or to insert one 0;

**How it works (reading):** A movable indicating member shows on the paper the point where the type will strike when the carriage is released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The printing point is hidden when the carriage is moved by hand to start a line.

**Solution:** A movable indicating member shows on the paper the point where the type will strike when the carriage is released.
