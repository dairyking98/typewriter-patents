## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keys are slow to assemble because the character sheet, glass cover and cap must be fitted separately on each key.

**Solution:** The character sheet and glass plate are combined in a cardboard cap made in stock, and this assembly is fixed to the key-lever plate with retaining tongues.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Interchangeable chart](../features/sel-chart-interchangeable.md)
