## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** In tabulating work the carriage must be stopped at a chosen column and then allowed to continue, which had no simple, convenient attachment for existing machines.

**Solution:** A stop device attached to the machine engages the escapement wheel and bar so the carriage can be halted at any desired point and then released to continue on its course.
