## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A pin on the type lever runs along a thrust cam on the swing weight, so the weight is accelerated smoothly, brakes the lever, and returns its stored energy when the type reaches the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A flywheel that meets the type lever at high speed on its linkage causes a shock, rebound and noise.

**Solution:** A pin on the type lever runs along a thrust cam on the swing weight, so the weight is accelerated smoothly, brakes the lever, and returns its stored energy when the type reaches the platen.
