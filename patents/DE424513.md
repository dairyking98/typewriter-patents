## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register for mail order)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The Invention relates to a cash register for such mail order houses, which the incoming or orders to be executed separately according to different states, provinces, Cities, etc.

**How it works (reading):** The printing unit prints an order slip and an upper control strip at the same time through double type sets and two hammers, while a lower registration strip carries only the totals.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Mail-order houses need separate totals per state, province or city, which a single register strip cannot give.

**Solution:** The printing unit prints an order slip and an upper control strip at the same time through double type sets and two hammers, while a lower registration strip carries only the totals.
