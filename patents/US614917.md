## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods on side surfaces thereof, e.g. fixed thereto the types or dies being movable relative to the bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of machines in which the paper-carriage is adapted to be moved forward and backward from a normally central position, so as to receive impressions from any one of the three types mounted upon the type-bars;

**How it works (reading):** Two spring-raised sliding cam-keys with oppositely inclined slots shift the carriage, lock it at centre and in each shifted position, and double as the centering springs; separate coil springs control the scale-bar.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a three-character-per-typebar machine whose carriage shifts forward and back from a central position, the centering springs had to be strong and the scale-bar used the paper-guides as its springs.

**Solution:** Two spring-raised sliding cam-keys with oppositely inclined slots shift the carriage, lock it at centre and in each shifted position, and double as the centering springs; separate coil springs control the scale-bar.

**How it works:** A shifting bar fixed to the girder track-frame carries two lugs with rollers. Each of two vertical sliding keys has a nose, an inclined cam slot and a shoulder, and is held up by a spiral spring. At rest both noses engage the rollers and lock the carriage centrally. Depressing one key cams the bar to a shifted position; depressing the other slightly lets its shoulder lock the carriage there by friction. Releasing the key lets its spring and cam return the carriage to centre, where the noses stop and lock it. The scale-bar is pivoted and held to or lifted from the platen by coil springs tied to a rigid bar, freeing the curved paper-guides from spring duty.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** shifting key; cam slot; shifting bar; antifriction roller; girder track-frame; scale-bar; coil spring; curved paper-guide; platen

**Improves on:**

- earlier carriage-shift machines with strong centering springs: the key springs act through steep cams as the centering springs, so no large separate springs are needed
- paper-guides used as scale-bar springs: coil springs now hold the scale-bar and the guides are fixed to a rigid bar
