## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment

**How it works (reading):** Eccentric pins in clamped lugs at each end of the carriage let the platen be moved forward or back and brought parallel to the supporting rod; two independent adjusters take up end play.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen is not held exactly parallel to the carriage travel and there is end play between the platen supports.

**Solution:** Eccentric pins in clamped lugs at each end of the carriage let the platen be moved forward or back and brought parallel to the supporting rod; two independent adjusters take up end play.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
