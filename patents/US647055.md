**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**How it works (reading):** A Yost-type machine with improved line-spacing and regulating mechanism, paper-carriage release, platen locking for paper adjustment, paper feed, dog pick-up at any stroke point, and a space-key mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Several escapement and paper-handling problems: undue dog movement in rapid writing, line-spacing and carriage release, platen locking to adjust paper, and paper feed.

**Solution:** A Yost-type machine with improved line-spacing and regulating mechanism, paper-carriage release, platen locking for paper adjustment, paper feed, dog pick-up at any stroke point, and a space-key mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** dogs and rack; line-space mechanism; paper-feeding mechanism; space-key guide; platen lock; type-bars and keys

**Numbers:** Patent 647,055; Serial No. 552,893; 5 sheets of drawings
