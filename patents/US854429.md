## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platens were expensive to make, and a worn platen surface had to be discarded or the whole roller replaced.

**Solution:** A platen is made of felt stitched into a seamless tube, covered with a silk fabric saturated in rubber solution, so that the silk surface can be removed and a new one applied when the surface is impressed by use.
