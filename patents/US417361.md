## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (automatic ribbon reel for stenographs) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for tensioning or winding the web

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved attachment for that class of machines which are known as stenographs;

**How it works (reading):** A spring-driven shaft turns a gear train that winds the ribbon onto a drum, with a friction brake lever keeping tension.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The printing ribbon should coil evenly from a stenograph.

**Solution:** A spring-driven shaft turns a gear train that winds the ribbon onto a drum, with a friction brake lever keeping tension.

- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
