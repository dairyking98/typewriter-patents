## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-spacing ratchet moves the platen only in fixed steps, unsuited to ruled lines or blanks that do not match.

**Solution:** A cam lever lifts the detent roller from the ratchet and at the same time applies a friction brake shoe to the platen, so the platen can be turned freely and held anywhere.

**How it works:** A detent roller on a leaf spring normally engages the platen ratchet. A sheet-metal frame pivoted on the detent pin carries a brake shoe over the platen. A cam or eccentric lever on the frame bears on a pin of a bracket on the platen frame; swinging it lifts the detent from the ratchet and presses the shoe on the platen. A notch on the cam holds it locked in the released state, so the platen is held firmly at any chosen position.
