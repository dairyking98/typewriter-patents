**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the present invention is to piovide a proper depthof depression oftheiger pieee and key lever, while preserving a proper delicacy or softness of touch;

**How it works (reading):** Flexing key connections with an elastic cushion for the finger-piece that takes effect after impact, plus a rearranged universal bar actuated by the heel of the type-bar and a new type-bar spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The inventors say the key depression needs a proper depth while keeping a soft touch, and the finger-piece needs a yielding cushion after the type-bar strikes the platen.

**Solution:** Flexing key connections with an elastic cushion for the finger-piece that takes effect after impact, plus a rearranged universal bar actuated by the heel of the type-bar and a new type-bar spring.
