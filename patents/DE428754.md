The one-sided wear and tear of such feet, especially the elastic ones and that made of sound-absorbing material is particularly noticeable disadvantageously for machines with a mechanical device in front of it. Drive, especially if still elastic pads are used that compress. The key depression is lower in this case and thus also the energy with which the type lever hit against the platen. In order to eliminate these disadvantages, feet are provided according to the invention, which are expediently adjusted in height by adjusting a nut can.
