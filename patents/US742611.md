## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide an improved marginal stop for type-writers having a locking device that will automatically lock the carriage and other parts, if desired, against further operation and which canbe released and thereby permit additional characters to be printed.

**How it works (reading):** A stop carrier clamped to a track rail with a rack and engaging lugs is adjusted along the rail; it locks the carriage automatically and can be released and re-locked after a preset count.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A marginal stop should lock the carriage automatically at the line end, be releasable to print more characters, and lock again after a set number of characters.

**Solution:** A stop carrier clamped to a track rail with a rack and engaging lugs is adjusted along the rail; it locks the carriage automatically and can be released and re-locked after a preset count.
