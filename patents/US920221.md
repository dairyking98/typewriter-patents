## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars need better guidance and must rebound quickly from the platen after each imprint.

**Solution:** Each type bar has resilient side plates riveted to a solid type-block shank, pivoted on a hanger, with a fixed guide on the hanger and repulsing means to throw the bar back fast.
