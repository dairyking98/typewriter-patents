## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type bars and universal bar do the work of moving the ribbon, making the touch heavier.

**Solution:** Drive ribbon feed and lateral ribbon movement by mechanism independent of the typebars and universal bar, with a vibrating index showing the printing point.

**How it works:** Only the opening is available. The ribbon runs between spools, fed lengthwise from one spool to the other and moved crosswise in front of the printing point by independent means. A ribbon guide or vibrator can be set to expose the printing point after every impression or only when the space key acts. A vibrating index doubles as a scale and can be turned out of the way. The machine is a front-strike typebar type with a power-driven platen drum and escapement.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; ribbon guide; vibrator; vibrating index; universal bar; type bar; platen drum

**Improves on:**

- typebar-driven ribbon vibration: separate ribbon drive lightens the touch
