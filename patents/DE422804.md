## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type alignment for calculators) · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The alignment rail is pushed into the comb cut-outs of the type carrier and withdrawn again by the same machine parts, driven through a curve piece and guide piece on the machine shaft that holds the rail at print time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Small height differences between set types in a calculating machine must be compensated, and the alignment rail that does this needs safer, timed movement.

**Solution:** The alignment rail is pushed into the comb cut-outs of the type carrier and withdrawn again by the same machine parts, driven through a curve piece and guide piece on the machine shaft that holds the rail at print time.
