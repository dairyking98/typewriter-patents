## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electrical typewriter carriage needed an automatic return at line end.

**Solution:** Electromagnet-driven letter spacing with a gear and ratchet stop and an automatic reverse mechanism.

**How it works:** An electromagnet actuates an armature pawl-lever that turns a ratchet wheel one tooth, limited by a stop-tooth on a gear of equal teeth. The carriage is pulled forward by it and at line end a sliding bar and cam stud lift the rack-bar so a spring returns the carriage; a line-spacing pawl rides an inclined plane.

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
