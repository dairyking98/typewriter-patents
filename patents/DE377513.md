## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A removable upper part must be cheap, and the switching and carrying gear should not all sit on the part that is lifted off.

**Solution:** The upper part (paper carriage) lifts off four corner pillars, guided by rods in grooves and held by spring locking levers. Only the wagon brake and the rest of the movement gearing stay on the machine frame and lower part.
