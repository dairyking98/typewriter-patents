## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with separate computing keys) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary functional arrangements, e.g. interlocks

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with separate numeric computing keys and automatically locked in the arithmetic position of the machine - numeric writing keys and types common to both key series;

**How it works (reading):** A locking member normally outside the key trajectory is moved into the key trajectory to lock the number keys, which simplifies keystroke action.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Numeric writing keys are locked in arithmetic position, but the locking part moves with the keypad, which makes the number keys harder to press.

**Solution:** A locking member normally outside the key trajectory is moved into the key trajectory to lock the number keys, which simplifies keystroke action.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md)

**Parts:** two-armed lever; flap; lever; rod; locking member
