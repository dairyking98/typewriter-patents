## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving mechanisms, e.g. springs stressed during carriage return adapted for silent return

**How it works (reading):** A single spring-ring friction member replaces the pawl spring: it holds the switch lever in the release position and brings the pawl back to its engagement position, which reduces parts, friction and pawl noise.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A carriage switch pawl with a friction member needs an extra pawl spring and its friction slows the carriage and makes noise.

**Solution:** A single spring-ring friction member replaces the pawl spring: it holds the switch lever in the release position and brings the pawl back to its engagement position, which reduces parts, friction and pawl noise.
