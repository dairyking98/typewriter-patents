## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Underwood needed separate main frames for ordinary and extra-wide carriages and could not convert a machine.

**Solution:** Interchangeable short and long front, rear and shift rails on detachable blocks let one main frame take either carriage.

**How it works:** Front rail standards share similar feet that seat on the main frame. Rear rails are mounted on detachable blocks that also carry the column-stop rocking frame. The shift rail is made in telescoping tubular sections that slip over the short one. Wheels under the long carriage's rear rail stop tipping.

**Features:** [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** main frame; front rail; rear rail; shift rail; detachable block; carriage; ball track; column-stop bar

**Improves on:**

- Underwood separate wide and ordinary main frames: one main frame fits both
