## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Interchangeable types on a typebar either needed costly resilient type carriers, tongues that let the type jump off, or several stop surfaces and thin pins that gave no secure seat.

**Solution:** The type block slides onto a slideway at the typebar head from below and is held by a pawl-like retaining spring snapping into a locking groove, with a fixed stop above.

**How it works:** The typebar head ends in a slideway with a fixed upper stop. The type block has a matching milled slot and a recess. It is pushed on from below until it meets the stop, and a pawl-like spring on the typebar drops into the recess and blocks downward movement. Lifting the spring releases the type so it can be pulled off downward. The stop also takes centrifugal force during typing.
