## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** Our main object is to provide a small, simple, and low-priced type-writer, but one capable of doing first-class work and operable with both hands in a manner similar to the larger high-priced standard machines.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are complicated and costly because keys, typebars and linkages are separate.

**Solution:** Make each type carrier a key-lever: a single lever with finger key and types, fulcrumed in slotted posts and guided by a transverse guide-plate.

**How it works:** Each carrier has a sheet-metal key portion riveted to a round wire bar bearing three types at its end, pivoted in split fulcrum posts. A transverse guide-plate with vertical slots guides each lever and allows slight lateral play so the three types can be selected sideways. The lever strikes upwards against the platen, or downward in a second form. An inking roller and center guide are shown.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md)

**Parts:** bed-plate; fulcrum post; type-bearing key-lever; finger key; guide-plate; inking roller; platen

**Numbers:** 3 types per bar
