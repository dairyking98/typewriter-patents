## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Keys could still be pressed at the line end, so one character was struck over another in the same spot.

**Solution:** A bar pivoted on the frame is raised by a bell crank struck by the carriage at line end, arresting the depression of the key levers; slots let period, comma and hyphen still be used.
