## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** A safety lever holds the delete key lever locked and must be swung out together with it before the stops can be cleared.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The delete key is struck accidentally and pushes the set column stops back.

**Solution:** A safety lever holds the delete key lever locked and must be swung out together with it before the stops can be cleared.
