## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a common carriage escapement the fixed dog is held in the rack too long, so the carriage waits after the type strikes and the machine is slow.

**Solution:** An improved carriage escapement, driven by a motor-spring, is claimed so the carriage is released and moves promptly; the complete method is cut off in the excerpt.
