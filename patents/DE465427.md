## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** A locking part moved by the tab keys cuts the motor drive or its circuit, so the tabulator is ineffective during the power carriage return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic carriage return with a motor could collide with a tab key already pressed, risking damage to the machine.

**Solution:** A locking part moved by the tab keys cuts the motor drive or its circuit, so the tabulator is ineffective during the power carriage return.
