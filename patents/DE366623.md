## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pocket typewriters were not able to write normal-width lines when ready to use.

**Solution:** Keys on a lazy-tongs (Nuremberg scissors) linkage drive types through springy wires in flexible tubes, and the platen and carriage fold in half on hinges.

**How it works:** A Nuremberg scissors on a base plate carries supports for flexible, spring-loaded wires in tubes. Each wire has a key at one end and a type at the other, snapping it to the roller. The roller and carriage are split in the middle with hinges (or a tension spring for the roller) so one half folds down when not in use, and the scissors collapse flat.

**Features:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
