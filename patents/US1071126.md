## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reverse tabulating needs manual reversal of the tabulating mechanism, so it cannot change automatically during a carriage run.

**Solution:** Breaking the link between tabulating and carriage release lets the tabulating direction change automatically at set points during the carriage run, or be set by hand.
