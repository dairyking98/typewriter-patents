## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cover plate can be removed by anyone, and fitting it is not secured.

**Solution:** Dowel pins on the cover enter bores in the frame and a rotating locking rod with notches locks them; a snap holds the rod in each position.
