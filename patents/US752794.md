## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improves Moya's earlier machine where characters are brought to the print point by combined type-carrier and paper-roll movement; wants a cylindrical carrier that can reach every character with few keys and stops.

**Solution:** A type-cylinder is swung to a key-chosen angle (stopped by the depressed key's inner end) and then slid endwise against a common stop, so each key picks one lengthwise row and a double row of characters is split by two endwise stops.

**How it works:** Depressing a character key raises its inner end into the path of an arm on the type-carrier and rocks a lever whose cam turns the carrier axle until the arm hits that key end, selecting a lengthwise row of type. The key also lifts the carrier endwise on its axle, freeing it from the driving pin, and a stud on the carrier enters a slot in a fixed ring to lock the angle. The endwise lift ends against one of two stops (one per key set), which selects which of a pair of characters is at the print height. A hammer is struck via a cross-bar, the paper-roll carriage is shifted sidewise by a shift key to change row, and a spacing lever feeds the carriage pawl. Types are on a rubber sheet cemented to a grating on the cylinder and are inked by absorbent rolls as the cylinder swings past.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [ELEM:rubber-type](../features/elem-rubber-type.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** type cylinder; carrier axle; character key; stop lever; locking ring; hammer; paper roll; rubber type sheet; ink roll; spacing lever

**Numbers:** six circumferential rows in three pairs; fourteen lengthwise rows; fourteen pairs of keys

**Improves on:**

- Moya application Serial No. 89,168 (1902): uses a cylindrical carrier with sidewise-moving paper roll and double-row characters with two endwise stops
