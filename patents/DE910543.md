## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment

**How it works (reading):** An adjusting screw couples the carriage to the rack with limited play so the paper carriage shifts a fraction of a tooth.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Fine horizontal adjustment for letter corrections was not possible without releasing the paper pressure rollers.

**Solution:** An adjusting screw couples the carriage to the rack with limited play so the paper carriage shifts a fraction of a tooth.

**How it works:** The carriage rack sits on the carriage in long slots with pins, and an adjusting screw on the carriage release lever engages a collar in the rack. Turning the screw shifts rack and carriage relative to each other within the slot length (up to one division). The carriage stop moves with it.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** carriage rack; adjusting screw; slot; pin; connecting rail; release lever; carriage stop

**Numbers:** adjustment range limited to one tooth division
