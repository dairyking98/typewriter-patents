## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type levers swinging about 180 degrees should accelerate steadily under a steady key stroke instead of at uneven rates.

**Solution:** The cam slot in each type lever is shaped so the arm acted on by the intermediate lever shortens during the stroke, giving a slight lift and constant acceleration.
