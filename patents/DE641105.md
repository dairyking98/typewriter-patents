## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (booking machine with hole control) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving of record carriers

**How it works (reading):** Hole combinations on a punched tape are sensed and selected by setting members so a single pass can route transactions to adders.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accounting machines with many accounts need several passes through punched tape, which is slow.

**Solution:** Hole combinations on a punched tape are sensed and selected by setting members so a single pass can route transactions to adders.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [SEL:pointer-hole](../features/sel-pointer-hole.md)
