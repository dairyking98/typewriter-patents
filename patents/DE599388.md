## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (automatic ribbon switching for address printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing mechanisms with inking or ink-ribbon devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an automatically reversible Ribbon switching device, in particular for address printing machines, in which the Ratchet wheels of the two ribbon spools by a swivel, under the influence a resilient toggle switch standing pawl are driven by on the periphery of the ribbon rolls sensors and one of these pivotable Start lever is thrown.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded toggle drives the start lever, which throws the pawl with a sharp jerk past dead centre into the other ratchet at the first attempt.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A weak mercury rocker start lever can leave the ribbon pawl short of its dead centre, so it fails to switch ratchets.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded toggle drives the start lever, which throws the pawl with a sharp jerk past dead centre into the other ratchet at the first attempt.
