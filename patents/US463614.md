## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing on a pile of sheets in succession requires a carriage that can hold a bundle without the usual platen.

**Solution:** A platenless paper-carriage with an open bottom holds a pile of paper pressed up by levers and springs, feeding each sheet in line-space direction as printing progresses.

**How it works:** A yoke slides on a hinged guide rail with a feed rack and feed dog. The carriage, with a bottom plate and two end plates, has a large opening along its length where the type strikes. Pile-compressing levers and springs press the bundle against the opening, and a line-spacing lever advances the sheets; extra-long sheets can also be printed.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** frame; guide rail; yoke; feed rack; feed dog; carriage bottom; end plate; pile-compressing lever; spring; line-spacing lever
