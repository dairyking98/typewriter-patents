## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (jerk mechanism adjustment, cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an adjusting device for jerk mechanisms on cash registers, calculating machines.

**How it works (reading):** Gears or type wheels are riveted to short tubes that are carried by a guide plate in a guide comb, which is screwed to a support part, so no long hollow shaft is needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type wheels on long hollow shafts need full machining along their length and produce lag and friction when set directly.

**Solution:** Gears or type wheels are riveted to short tubes that are carried by a guide plate in a guide comb, which is screwed to a support part, so no long hollow shaft is needed.
