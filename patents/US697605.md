## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar type must be inked without a ribbon.

**Solution:** A felt ink roller in the path of all typebars swings aside on impact against a reservoir roller.

**How it works:** A porous roller on a spring-returned pivoted bracket sits at the center of a flaring guide. Each type strikes it, inking the face, and pushes it round against a supply roller that recharges it. A coiled spring flexing sideways gives gentle return tension.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md)

**Parts:** ink roller; bracket; pintle; coiled spring; supply roller; flaring guide
