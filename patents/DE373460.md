**How it works (reading):** A parallelogram linkage with bent extensions that lock into the furniture foot when the plate is pushed in, with balancing springs on rods whose ends slide in guides.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A sliding typewriter plate on a pivoted linkage is not rigid in working position, and the lever fatigue is high because the support points are close together.

**Solution:** A parallelogram linkage with bent extensions that lock into the furniture foot when the plate is pushed in, with balancing springs on rods whose ends slide in guides.
