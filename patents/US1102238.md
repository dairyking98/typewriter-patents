## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage stops must be easy to move and must not lock the machine if the carriage passes them.

**Solution:** A rack bar and rock bar carry slidable stops that interlock in any position, and a cam-faced stop lets the carriage latch ride over it.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
