## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters were tall and complicated, so not portable.

**Solution:** Horizontal type-arms swing about half a revolution to print and are driven by the finger-key levers without being connected to them.

**How it works:** Finger-key levers are metal blades fulcrumed on posts, with vertical pins at the inner ends. Type-arms are fitted into radial slots of a segmental support near the paper cylinder and pivot on a wire, lying flat. A pin on a depressed lever strikes its arm, which flies about half a turn to print. The text also covers a spacing bar under the base, a ribbon guided between two spools with a drive for one spool, a line-spacing device on the carriage end plate, and a movable under frame.
