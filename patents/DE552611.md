## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Keyboard rows for ten-finger touch typing cause arm and hand fatigue.

**Solution:** Each hand's keypad is concave with tightly fitted key tops, mirrored around a blank field with guide-strip side walls covering the thumb space.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)
