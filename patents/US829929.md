## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The upper and lower platen positions of a case-shift machine need accurate, easy adjustment for even printing and alignment.

**Solution:** Platen bearings slide in vertical slots closed at both ends, with lock-nut set screws at top and bottom limiting the travel.

**How it works:** The platen spindle runs in bearings on the platen frame, sliding in slots in the paper carriage. Set screws with lock-nuts at the slot ends stop the bearings in the lower and raised positions, so each is set easily at any time. A bearing can be unscrewed from the frame so the platen frame is inserted into the carriage.

**Parts:** platen roller; spindle; bearing; platen frame; paper carriage; slot; set screw; lock-nut
