According to the invention, the drive frame is on one on the machine frame fixed and equipped with vertical guide rods for the key levers Frame rotatably arranged and provided with resilient hooks, one of which detects the somewhat depressed key lever And after a power source for driving the frame is switched on in a known manner is, brings about the full decline of the key lever.
