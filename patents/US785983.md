## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a tabulating attachment for type-writers, and particularly to a structure embodying a series of carriagestops such, for instance, as shown in the patent to Charles Spiro, No.

**How it works (reading):** A row of carriage-stop keys tied to a release key, with stop levers nested for independent keyboard operation; a joint key action removes the dogs from the star-wheel to release normal feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stop keys must be set and released together, and the stop and release mechanisms should nest in minimal space.

**Solution:** A row of carriage-stop keys tied to a release key, with stop levers nested for independent keyboard operation; a joint key action removes the dogs from the star-wheel to release normal feed.
