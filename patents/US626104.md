## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvement in efficiency of a typebar machine carriage spacing, line spacer, bell and bumpers.

**Solution:** A rack bar on a ball-bearing carriage rail is fed by a key-driven lever carrying a dog and a lower spring pawl, with a release lever for free carriage movement.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on a traverse rail in ball races. A lever with a dog and a lower spring pawl engages a letter-space rack bar; pressing a key lowers the arm so the pawl clears one tooth and the dog takes the next, and release feeds the rack one tooth. A ratchet segment adjusts the tension of the return spring. A lever at the left end of the rail tips the rack bar out of the pawl to move the carriage freely, and bumpers, bell hammer slide and line spacer complete the carriage.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)
