## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper is hard to insert square with the printing line, and operators must judge squareness by eye.

**Solution:** Feed-roller carrying frames on the carriage clamp the paper so its leading edge starts from a fixed point and is squared in the feed.

**How it works:** Pairs of feed-roller frames on the carriage are opened and closed to clamp the sheet; the end plates of the platen frame carry the assembly.
