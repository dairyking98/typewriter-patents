## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Seeks a typewriter combining simplicity, efficiency and convenience of operation.

**Solution:** A hand-held printing plunger with a spherical face carrying concentric circles of type is tilted by a universal joint to be located by notches in a guide plate, then pressed on a sliding platen.

**How it works:** A platen slides on a base between guide cleats and is pinned in one of a series of holes for each line. A sleeve slides on a guide bar and carries an arm that oscillates toward the platen; at its end a printing plunger is pivoted by a universal joint of two perpendicular pivots. The plunger's lower head has a convex spherical face with fields of type in concentric circles of different diameters. A guide arm on the plunger passes through a central aperture of a guide plate with radial notches, one per character in a circle, and sleeves of different diameters on the guide arm engage different notch depths to select a circle. The operator tilts the plunger into the notch for a letter and presses it down to print, and a spacing lever riding on the plunger gives different spacings.
