## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons; Stabilising means therefor using a scissor mechanism as stabiliser

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-bar mechanism for type-writers, and has for its object to provide a simple and efiective type-key for performing several functions and also to provide a shield for the said type-bar mechanism to shield the eyes of the operator from observing the constant motion of the type-levers.

**How it works (reading):** A double-function key construction with adjustable spring tension per key, plus a hinged shield over the typebar mechanism.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need a simple key that performs several functions, and the constantly moving type levers distract the operator.

**Solution:** A double-function key construction with adjustable spring tension per key, plus a hinged shield over the typebar mechanism.

**How it works:** Each key lever has a front key section latched to the lever body so it can be swapped between double-function and single-function form. A spring and adjustable screw regulate each key independently. Typebars connect through elastic links and elbow levers to the key levers; the links snap on and off for removal. A hinged shield, latched at the far end, hides the moving levers from the operator.
