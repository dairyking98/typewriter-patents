## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type faces need thorough cleaning, but a fixed brush would interfere with normal typing.

**Solution:** A crank-driven brush on a bracket rotates through the type basket by gears. The brush can be tipped out of the way on a hinge when not in use.
