## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage support had to be fitted into several recesses in the machine frame, which made it hard to insert and attach.

**Solution:** A removable plate with beveled edges carries the carriage support and engages one recess; the plate is held by rotary holders and a hook on the spring drum band.
