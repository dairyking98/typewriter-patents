## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** A flat paint vessel with hinged, foldable axes holds spools; a hand crank drives the take-up spool, and rollers squeeze out the dyed ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Worn ribbons need reinking, and the existing dyeing devices are awkward to load.

**Solution:** A flat paint vessel with hinged, foldable axes holds spools; a hand crank drives the take-up spool, and rollers squeeze out the dyed ribbon.
