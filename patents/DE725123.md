> Description Translated from German Automatic switching device for the longitudinal movement of the ribbon on writing, calculating and similar machines The invention relates to a ribbon transport and switching device, especially on cash registers, writing, calculating, adding and similar machines. Such devices are in multiple designs known. The device according to the invention is characterized by compact Design, a small number of parts, insensitivity and operational reliability, furthermore by easy, almost frictionless gait. Switching the longitudinal movement the ribbon is also not done as in most known devices in that several car switching steps or type stops are required and under spring action lever or the like moved with the help of the ribbon that is heavily stressed here, but by sudden in and Disengagement during a single printing process or type lever stop without stress of the ribbon.
