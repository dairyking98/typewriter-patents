## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the external periphery of the wound ribbon

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon transport and switching device, especially on cash registers, writing, calculating, adding and similar machines.

**How it works (reading):** A coupling piece between the ribbon reel shafts with conical ring grooves and a feeler lever switches the ribbon drive in one print action when the ribbon runs out.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal in writing and calculating machines needs several carriage switching steps or type stops that stress the ribbon.

**Solution:** A coupling piece between the ribbon reel shafts with conical ring grooves and a feeler lever switches the ribbon drive in one print action when the ribbon runs out.

> Description Translated from German Automatic switching device for the longitudinal movement of the ribbon on writing, calculating and similar machines The invention relates to a ribbon transport and switching device, especially on cash registers, writing, calculating, adding and similar machines. Such devices are in multiple designs known. The device according to the invention is characterized by compact Design, a small number of parts, insensitivity and operational reliability, furthermore by easy, almost frictionless gait.
