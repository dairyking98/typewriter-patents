**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ball or roller bearings; Means to synchronise movements; with grooves in the bearing surfaces

**How it works (reading):** Ball bearing where balls in a linear race are each encircled by a ring gear meshing with racks, which keeps the balls positioned.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage sliding on a traversing support has friction.

**Solution:** Ball bearing where balls in a linear race are each encircled by a ring gear meshing with racks, which keeps the balls positioned.
