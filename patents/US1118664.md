## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Oriental-language Hammond shuttles carry five rows, more than the shift lifts for.

**Solution:** Give each shift key two lifting motions with adjustable stops, for four shifted positions.

**How it works:** The shuttle on the anvil shaft has five rows of type. Each shift key lever has a slot letting the key slide backward on the lever against a spring, to reach a second adjustable stop screw for a further lift. A stop lever locks the key in the first lowered position. Slots in the key guiding bar allow extra play, with recesses holding the key down.

**Parts:** shift key; key lever; shuttle; anvil; anvil shaft; stop screw; stop lever; spring

**Numbers:** five rows of type; four shifted positions
