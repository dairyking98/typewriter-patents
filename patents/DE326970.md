## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a document holder with a device for covering the written part and, if necessary, for enclosing the concept.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-wound roller blind in a housing clips to the typewriter, and a key-operated ratchet lowers it by one or more lines so the written part is covered as typing proceeds.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Concept holders were clumsy and did not cover the written line during fast typing.

**Solution:** A spring-wound roller blind in a housing clips to the typewriter, and a key-operated ratchet lowers it by one or more lines so the written part is covered as typing proceeds.
