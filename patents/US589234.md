## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two keys pressed at once print badly, and mechanical typebar drives limit speed.

**Solution:** A power-driven type carrier whose dogs engage a continuously rotating cam driver at the operator's key, with locking that prevents simultaneous key depression and electromagnet-operated impulse mechanism.

**How it works:** A toothed rotary driver turns constantly. The segmental type carrier (a single row of type on its edge) is on a spring-returned shaft with arms at different angles. Each key trips a pawl releasing a spring dog that engages the driver and rotates the carrier to that character; a hammer and carriage are also driven from the driver. Interlock devices let keys be held if pressed successively but block simultaneous ones.

**Features:** [ELEM:sector-segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md), [SEL:dummy-keyboard](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** rotary driver; segmental type carrier; shaft; arm; dog carrier; spring dog; spring pawl; key lever; hammer; electromagnet; carriage

**Improves on:**

- Tyberg application Ser. 385,411 (1891): adds key interlock, new hammer and carriage drive, electromagnetic impulse
