## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (carriage setting stop for calculating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is shown in the drawing in an exemplary embodiment shown, namely Fig.

**How it works (reading):** The holding rod with dividing grooves is fixed to the carriage while a stationary catch device with many pawls, arranged like a vernier scale, snaps into a groove at each carriage position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stop members on the paper carriage add complexity, and vibration can shift the carriage from its set position.

**Solution:** The holding rod with dividing grooves is fixed to the carriage while a stationary catch device with many pawls, arranged like a vernier scale, snaps into a groove at each carriage position.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage 1; holding rod 8; catch pawls; locking member 12

**Numbers:** vernier-spaced catch pawls
