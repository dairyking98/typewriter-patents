## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line selection on account machines is slow, and the printing table must be realigned after each choice.

**Solution:** A movable display plate with notches guides the operator's finger against a stop that sets the printing table to the chosen line, then returns it to centre.
