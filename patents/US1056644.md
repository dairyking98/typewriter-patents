## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters on a round platen cannot write easily on bound books or large sheets.

**Solution:** A flat-platen typewriter where the writing mechanism runs on tracks over a hinged flat platen, with a book support underneath.

**How it works:** The platen is hinged leaves above a desk, held down by lock tapes, with a vertical-adjustable book support beneath. The writing mechanism, typebars, ribbon and keys ride on a carriage over adjustable tracks that can tilt away. Multi-color ribbon, tabulating and letter-spacing stops, and web-fed paper with carbon for manifolding are also shown, with clamps and work-holders.

**Features:** [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** desk; flat platen; book support; track; carriage; typebar; ribbon mechanism; lock tape; clamp; hand wheel; rack
