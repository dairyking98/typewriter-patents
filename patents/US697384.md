## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writers, and pertains to a machine of the visible-writing type and wherein the typebars are arranged in a substantially horizontal basket.

**How it works (reading):** Vertically shifting typebar basket combined with key levers on a comb, visible writing.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar action should have an even easy touch and a quick shift.

**Solution:** Vertically shifting typebar basket combined with key levers on a comb, visible writing.

**How it works:** The basket carries typebars between division plates and shifts vertically; L-shaped key-levers rest in a comb. A universal frame feeds the carriage.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md), [Key touch and even impression](../features/bar-touch.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** typebar basket; typebar; key-lever; comb; universal frame
