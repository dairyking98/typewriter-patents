## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to Visible writing niachines', particularly to front strike machines, wherein the type bars are actuated bykey-lever-operated links movable toward the front of the machine to pull the'type bars to the printing point.
