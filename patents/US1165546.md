## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal needed manual action when a bobbin emptied.

**Solution:** An automatic ribbon reverse where an empty bobbin's window lets a feeler move to shift the driving shaft to the other bobbin.

**How it works:** Two bobbins are driven by bevel gears from a transverse shaft that is moved by the carriage wheel. The shaft slides to mesh one of two pinions, locked by gravity catches. Each bobbin has a window; a weighted feeler roller presses on the ribbon and drops through the window when empty. Its arm engages a worm on the shaft to slide it and swap the driven bobbin. A handle allows manual turning.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** bobbin; ribbon; driving shaft; bevel gear; worm; feeler arm; window; catch

**Improves on:**

- manual ribbon reverse: automatic via feeler through the empty bobbin window
