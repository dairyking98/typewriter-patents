## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the invention is to have the writing in plain view of the operator when the latter is in the position assumed for operating the keys of the machine without other effort than that required to turn the eyes in the proper direction.

**How it works (reading):** Type-bars are set on a firm base and swing in straight lines to and from the printing point, linked to straight parallel key-levers by straight stringing, with an accelerating swing of up to ninety degrees or more.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing machines hide the type-bars poorly, the type-bars stall on a dead centre, and side strain builds up near the printing point.

**Solution:** Type-bars are set on a firm base and swing in straight lines to and from the printing point, linked to straight parallel key-levers by straight stringing, with an accelerating swing of up to ninety degrees or more.
