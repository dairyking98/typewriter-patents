## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the invention is to have the writing in plain view of the operator when the latter is in the position assumed for operating the keys of the machine without other effort than that required to turn the eyes in the proper direction.
