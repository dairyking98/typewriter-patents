## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools in a typewriter need to be supported and driven so the ribbon cannot escape from them.

**Solution:** Improved ribbon-spool supports and drive with a hand lever controlling the ribbon feed direction, on an Underwood-type machine with a shifting platen.

**How it works:** The preamble describes a standard Underwood-style machine: type-bars pivoted in a segment, linked to key levers, bearing on a segmental universal bar that moves a reciprocating table with feed pawls on the carriage rack. The platen, in its own casing carried on levers joined by a rock-shaft, is held up by an adjustable-tension spring and lowered by a shift key. A toe on the table's rock-shaft with a slotted crank-pin connection drives the ribbon spools from the typing action. A hand lever sets the ribbon feed direction. The ribbon-spool details are cut off in the opening text.
