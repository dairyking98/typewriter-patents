## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An inexpensive typebar machine with a wide range of work.

**Solution:** Type-levers on swinging arms with key plates and a shifting carriage bed.

**How it works:** A flat base carries a carriage bed on rails; the carriage slides in a dovetail; type-levers pivot on swinging standards and are actuated by key plates with switches.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** base plate; carriage bed; rail; dovetail groove; type-lever; key plate
