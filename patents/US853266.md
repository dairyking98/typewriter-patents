## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike typebars of uniform length have short pivot bearings and stagger the types, and the key touch is hard.

**Solution:** Use short and long typebars on two nested pivot arcs so types sit in an even row, with individual folded hangers, single-character shank types and key sub-levers.

**How it works:** Short typebars pivot in an arc just inside the arc of the long bars so both sets of types meet the platen in one even row. Each bar sits in an individual folded-strip hanger adjustable forward and backward with play take-up at the pivot. Separate shank types fit sockets in the bar, which is bent between sockets so all types print at the same point. Sub-levers between key lever and typebar give an easy key start and a faster final stroke.
