## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (pressure telegraph receiver) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or local circuits for mosaic printer telegraph systems

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a pressure telegraph receiver, namely a selchen in which the individual characters through defined impulse series from several writing elements be put together.

**How it works (reading):** The writing elements are arranged on a type wheel that turns synchronously with the transmitter; the wheel brings each needed element into the print field in turn, and a cam wheel times a print hammer to imprint them one after another.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing telegraph receivers that build each character from writing elements need one receiving magnet per element, which makes them costly to produce.

**Solution:** The writing elements are arranged on a type wheel that turns synchronously with the transmitter; the wheel brings each needed element into the print field in turn, and a cam wheel times a print hammer to imprint them one after another.
