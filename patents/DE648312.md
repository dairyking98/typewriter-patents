The normal teletypewriter works according to the start-stop system with the five-stream step alphabet and prints according to a pulse combination off a letter. Such devices are extraordinary in their production expensive, but also reach a telegraph speed of 7 to 8 characters per second. So-called facsimile telegraphs are also known in which the Message is reproduced by applying electrical impulses to a strip of paper be put together point by point. Such devices are particularly useful for wireless Traffic that is particularly susceptible to disruptions.
