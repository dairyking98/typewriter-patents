## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (continuous paper web cutter for printing machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**How it works (reading):** A cutting wheel on a runner sliding along a rod rolls on a crossbar drive wheel as it traverses, so the transverse motion turns it and cuts the web that the tensioning device has stretched.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing paper cutters on type printers need the cutting wheel swung or repositioned by hand and the web tensioned by pivoting the device, which hinders automatic operation.

**Solution:** A cutting wheel on a runner sliding along a rod rolls on a crossbar drive wheel as it traverses, so the transverse motion turns it and cuts the web that the tensioning device has stretched.
