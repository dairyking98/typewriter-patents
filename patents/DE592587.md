## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Device for shortening or lengthening the distance of the head of typewriter type levers from its fulcrum by bending between three pressure points, two of which are on the one hand and the third between these two on the other hand against put on the type lever.

**How it works (reading):** A carrier held in a vice has two fixed pressure inserts and one movable insert; a spindle through the carrier drives the movable insert, so the deflection and the lengthening or shortening of the type lever can be set exactly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Bending type levers with straightening pliers takes much force and gives an uncertain amount of bend.

**Solution:** A carrier held in a vice has two fixed pressure inserts and one movable insert; a spindle through the carrier drives the movable insert, so the deflection and the lengthening or shortening of the type lever can be set exactly.
