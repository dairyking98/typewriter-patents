## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to a novel antifrictional type-arm bearing therefor.

**How it works (reading):** The arm is carried on an antifriction ball race, with the bearing disk secured by an expansible threaded sleeve that is locked by a wedge-headed screw.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type-arm bearing must be narrow, resist vibration from impact, and still allow adjustment for wear.

**Solution:** The arm is carried on an antifriction ball race, with the bearing disk secured by an expansible threaded sleeve that is locked by a wedge-headed screw.
