## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A small, portable, low-priced typewriter that is cheap to make, durable and capable of first-class work.

**Solution:** A segmental rubber type band on a pivoted plate is swung by a finger pointer over a stationary index, then a separate print lever presses the chosen type to the paper, locks the segment with a nose in a notch under that type, and releases a spring pawl that spaces the carriage on release.

**How it works:** The operator puts a finger in the cup of a pointer fixed to the hub of the type segment and swings it to the radial line of the desired character on a fixed index plate, bringing that rubber type to the print point. Pressing the print lever tips a pivoted plate whose type presser forces the type through a shield opening against the paper on the platen, while its nose enters the notch directly under that type and locks the segment. The same plate's other arm pushes a spring-loaded sliding rod that withdraws the driving pawl and locking dog from the carriage rack. On releasing the lever the spring returns everything and the pawl drives the carriage one tooth while the dog stops overthrow. Inking rollers on posts ink the types, and a separate space key retracts and releases the pawl and dog to space or to return the carriage.
