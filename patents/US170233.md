## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple type-writing machine worked entirely by a single lever that selects, prints and spaces.

**Solution:** One lever turns a type-wheel through bevel gears to a letter shown by a finger on an indicator chart, and pressing it down prints and steps the paper table one letter.

**How it works:** A type-wheel with letters on its periphery sits on a horizontal shaft geared by two bevel wheels to an upright shaft. A key or lever on top of the upright shaft carries a finger that is turned over a fixed indicator carrying the same characters; turning the finger turns the wheel until the same letter is at its bottom. A pivoted hook on the lever catches under a smooth ring to hold the wheel at the chosen position. Pressing the lever forces the shaft down and the wheel onto the paper on the table. On each descent a curved lever arm and dog advance a rack on the table sideways one notch, a second dog holds back, and a release shaft with a lever lifts the dogs so the table can return for the next line.
