## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (print hammer lifting thumbs for registers)

**How it works (reading):** The striking arm is locked after its spring is tensioned by the first lift thumb of the drive member, and the lock is cancelled for the last thumb, so unneeded print strokes do not tension the spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing one amount takes several strokes, so the mainspring is tensioned and released on strokes where no print is wanted, and special coupling devices for this are cumbersome.

**Solution:** The striking arm is locked after its spring is tensioned by the first lift thumb of the drive member, and the lock is cancelled for the last thumb, so unneeded print strokes do not tension the spring.
