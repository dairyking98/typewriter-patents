## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter Dust Protector The present invention relates to a dust protection device for typewriters with side panels, a Front panel and a U-shaped key protection strip extending to the front, which is characterized in that the plates, the protective strips and the spool cover have one or more continuous, horizontal, recessed shelves that consist of detachable strips with an ornament-like surface. In the case of the known dust protection boxes, they have a decorative effect exercising Borden a whole with the protective cover of the machine and are from this not seperable. This has the disadvantage that in the manufacture of the machine, if the shelves are chrome-plated, for example, to achieve a decorative effect should be, the whole protective cover must first be chrome-plated and then the the colored parts of the protective cover must be varnished. This creates Significantly higher costs, since the expensive chrome plating under the later painted ones Make the protective cover completely superfluous, and because still special protective measures Must be taken to bake the strips that are not painted should be protected from the paint running over.…
