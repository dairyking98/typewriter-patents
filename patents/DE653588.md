## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a dust protection device for typewriters with side panels, a Front panel and a U-shaped key protection strip extending to the front, which is characterized in that the plates, the protective strips and the spool cover have one or more continuous, horizontal, recessed shelves that consist of detachable strips with an ornament-like surface.

**How it works (reading):** The ornamental strips are detachable and fitted into continuous recessed shelves on the side, front and spool-cover panels, so they are plated separately and also stiffen the box.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Chrome-plated ornamental shelves on a dust cover are fixed to the cover, so they must be plated before painting, which costs more and discolours the plating in the bake.

**Solution:** The ornamental strips are detachable and fitted into continuous recessed shelves on the side, front and spool-cover panels, so they are plated separately and also stiffen the box.
