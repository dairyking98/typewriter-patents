## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operator cannot see at a glance the exact printing point and the carriage position relative to it.

**Solution:** A scale on the carriage below the printing line with a fixed double pointer, one finger indicating the print point and one the scale.

**How it works:** A scale bar graduated in letter spaces is carried by the carriage end plates and lies beneath the printing line in the operator's view. A fixed cross bar on the type-bar segment carries two indexes in opposite directions, one up to the printing point and one down to the scale. The bar edge lies parallel and just below the printing line to help square the paper. In a modification a continuous line forms both indexes.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** scale bar; carriage; double pointer; cross bar; type-bar segment; platen
