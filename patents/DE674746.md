## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (receipt printing guide, cash register) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**How it works (reading):** The feed channel between the print point and the insertion point is given a curved extension outside the hand area, so the slip is held there during the automatic shift and the stiffness does not reach the print point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A double slip with a carbon sheet bends at the print point when it is pushed in and then released, so the protruding end prints badly.

**Solution:** The feed channel between the print point and the insertion point is given a curved extension outside the hand area, so the slip is held there during the automatic shift and the stiffness does not reach the print point.
