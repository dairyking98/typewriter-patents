## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools need feeding and reversing that works on a machine with a depressed carriage track.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools with feed and reversing devices mounted on the top plates feed the ribbon each stroke.

**How it works:** The main frame has corner posts, side bars with U-shaped openings and top plates. The carriage runs on ball rails below the top plates, pulled by a spring drum with cord. Front-strike typebars pivot in a shiftable segment driven by key levers. A universal bar and ribbon spools with feeding and reversing mechanism sit on the top plates.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
