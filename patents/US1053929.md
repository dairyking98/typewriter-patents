## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** Sheet-controlled elements drive a drum to indicate the decreasing distance to the bottom edge, with visual, audible and machine-lock signals.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot know the decreasing distance to the sheet end during printing.

**Solution:** Sheet-controlled elements drive a drum to indicate the decreasing distance to the bottom edge, with visual, audible and machine-lock signals.
