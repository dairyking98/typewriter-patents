## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Loose-leaf sheets need to be registered on the platen, but the feed rolls and aligning mechanism must be cast off independently for correspondence work.

**Solution:** Register pins project through a tablet along the top of the platen to hold perforated sheets at the first printing line; the feed rolls and the aligning pins are released by separate finger pieces, and pressure rolls engage the platen above the printing line.

**How it works:** A finger piece releases the main feed rolls from the platen, separately from the finger piece that protrudes the aligning pins. The pins engage the perforations of loose-leaf sheets and set the first line at the print point. Pressure rolls above the printing line are automatically cast off when the pins are protruded, and restoring the aligning mechanism returns the released feed rolls to normal.

**Parts:** register pins; tablet; main feed rolls; pressure rolls; platen; finger piece; aligning mechanism

**Improves on:**

- feed rolls and aligning mechanism coupled together: independent release so the feed rolls can be cast off for correspondence
