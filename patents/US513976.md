## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists further, in providing means for predetermining the position of said sheet when it shall operate to stop the feeding mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A ratchet wheel on the platen is stopped by a pawl that the sheet edge holds; the pawl disengages when the sheet reaches a set position.

**Problem:** Paper feed does not stop automatically at a chosen position on the sheet.

**Solution:** A ratchet wheel on the platen is stopped by a pawl that the sheet edge holds; the pawl disengages when the sheet reaches a set position.
