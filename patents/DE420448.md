## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** A sliding tube inside the roller shell tensions clamping levers so they lock the index ratchet to the roller; pulling it back disengages the roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-index wheel coupling on the paper roller should be simple, reliable and use few parts.

**Solution:** A sliding tube inside the roller shell tensions clamping levers so they lock the index ratchet to the roller; pulling it back disengages the roller.
