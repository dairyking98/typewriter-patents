## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Aprons or guides for the printing section movable for insertion or release of sheets

**How it works (reading):** The paper guide plate is split into two longitudinal parts that slide against each other by tongues and slots, so the gap stays closed when the rollers are lifted and a wide entry remains at the front.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting the paper pressure rollers widens the gap between rollers and the paper guide plate, where sheets catch and tear.

**Solution:** The paper guide plate is split into two longitudinal parts that slide against each other by tongues and slots, so the gap stays closed when the rollers are lifted and a wide entry remains at the front.
