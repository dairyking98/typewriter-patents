## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Toy typewriters

**In the patent’s own words:** The general object of the invention is to refine in point of simplicity, cheapness of construction and of parts, the mechanism for effecting connection between the type bars and key levers of a type-writer.

**How it works (reading):** Each type bar is joined to its key lever by a single connecting element placed at the pivoted end of the bar, so the type bar is pulled at an angle from the first movement of the key and reaches the platen within the normal key travel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The linkage between key levers and type bars is made of several parts and needs more than the normal key depression to bring the type to the work.

**Solution:** Each type bar is joined to its key lever by a single connecting element placed at the pivoted end of the bar, so the type bar is pulled at an angle from the first movement of the key and reaches the platen within the normal key travel.
