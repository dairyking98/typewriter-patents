## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guides in the printing zone, e.g. guides for preventing contact of conveyed sheets with printhead

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide an attachment that may be readily applied to the frame of a typewriter for the purpose of holding and guiding the sheet of paper in such manner as to allow the sheet to be written upon to the extreme bottom.

**How it works (reading):** Clamped guide rods on the paper rack, held over the feed rolls by a key, carry the sheet past the rolls so it can be written to the bottom.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a Hammond writer the paper runs out of the feed rolls too early to write to the bottom of the sheet.

**Solution:** Clamped guide rods on the paper rack, held over the feed rolls by a key, carry the sheet past the rolls so it can be written to the bottom.
