## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon reversal for calculating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on ribbon switching devices for calculating machines where the reverse of the Switching direction due to the voltage of the occurring at the end of the processing Ribbon is initiated.

**How it works (reading):** A single unitary lever, which pivots around its drive attachment point and has a ratchet at each end, is held by a spring against one of two ribbon ratchet wheels; when the ribbon tension changes, the lever engages the other wheel and reverses the ribbon feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A ribbon must reverse its feed direction automatically when the ribbon is used up.

**Solution:** A single unitary lever, which pivots around its drive attachment point and has a ratchet at each end, is held by a spring against one of two ribbon ratchet wheels; when the ribbon tension changes, the lever engages the other wheel and reverses the ribbon feed.
