## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the present invention is to put the gearinginto compact form and also to house it, thereby enabling it to keep clean, and also'conducing to the neat appearance of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** The platen is geared to the line-space wheel and rotates differently so that impression lines overlap; the gearing is housed and also gives fine platen rotation and automatic reciprocation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen surface wears in lines from type blows, and the gearing needs compact housing for cleanliness.

**Solution:** The platen is geared to the line-space wheel and rotates differently so that impression lines overlap; the gearing is housed and also gives fine platen rotation and automatic reciprocation.
