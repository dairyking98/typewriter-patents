## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine visibility) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a new and useful improvement in stencil cutting machines, the object being to construct a machine of the character described so that the work thereof will be visible.

**How it works (reading):** A stencil-cutting machine redesigned so the work is visible during cutting, with a ratchet-and-pawl feed and male dies in a die carrier on a post.
