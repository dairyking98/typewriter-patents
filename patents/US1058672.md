## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**How it works (reading):** Pressure rolls on forward and rear shafts are carried on arms and released together by a key-controlled rock shaft with cams; springs are adjustable and accessible, and the paper deflector is supported by the front roll shaft.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The pressure-roll mechanism had inaccessible spring adjustment and a bulky paper deflector, and was costly to make.

**Solution:** Pressure rolls on forward and rear shafts are carried on arms and released together by a key-controlled rock shaft with cams; springs are adjustable and accessible, and the paper deflector is supported by the front roll shaft.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
