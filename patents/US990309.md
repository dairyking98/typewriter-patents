## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** A revolving hollow head with a sliding bail-shaped line-space pawl turns a platen ratchet wheel for fine spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Line-spacing and platen fine adjustment is awkward and the parts are costly.

**Solution:** A revolving hollow head with a sliding bail-shaped line-space pawl turns a platen ratchet wheel for fine spacing.
