## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improved typewriter ribbon throw device or ribbon vibrator, such as is usedin typewrit-ing machines, of the kind known as visible or sight writing machines, to carry the ribbon over the printing point for making the impressions and away from the printing point to expose to view the line of writing.

**How it works (reading):** A sliding ribbon-guide plate on the vibrating plate is stepped sideways by a ratchet and pawl at every vibration so the whole ribbon width gets used.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon vibrator on a visible machine (Oliver) wears the ribbon along one strip.

**Solution:** A sliding ribbon-guide plate on the vibrating plate is stepped sideways by a ratchet and pawl at every vibration so the whole ribbon width gets used.

**How it works:** As in the Oliver, an arm carries a base plate and an endwise vibrating guide plate on three oscillating links driven by a rock shaft, crank arm and pitman from a key-operated part. A second plate slides endwise on the guide plate, with arms ending in two loops that guide the ribbon either side of the print point. A ratchet wheel and pawl move this plate to shift the ribbon laterally as it passes through the guides.
