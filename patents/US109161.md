## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A keyboard writing machine whose type-levers all print at one point, with automatic paper feed and inking.

**Solution:** A series of type-levers of differing lengths, pivoted at varied distances from the print point, are keyed by finger-keys and struck by a common striker against a padded roller.

**How it works:** Each finger-key is linked to a bell-crank lever whose rod moves the short arm of a type-lever, so the long end swings to the one print point; longer levers are pivoted farther away and curved to keep their ends in line. Pressing a key also pushes down a balanced vibrating frame beneath the keys, which drives a pivoted striker that forces the selected type against the paper on a cushioned roller. A stop prevents the momentum carrying the lever past the striker point. The frame's return stroke operates the paper feed, and an inking mechanism supplies ink to the types as they move toward the print point.
