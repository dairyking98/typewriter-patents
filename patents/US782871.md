## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter keys striking the platen are noisy, and the platen surface is marked by key impact.

**Solution:** A platen or roller of felt or compressed wool with a firm circular body and a yielding elastic interior, so the surface is quiet, elastic and resists impression marks.

**How it works:** A platen or roller of felt or compressed wool with a firm circular body and a yielding elastic interior, so the surface is quiet, elastic and resists impression marks.
