## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters for ciphering or deciphering cryptographic text wherein the operative connections between the keys and the type-bars are automatically and continuously permuted, during operation, by a coding or key member the connections being electrical

**How it works (reading):** A roller carries rows of alphabets with contacts in any order; a slider with two contact arms and a viewing hole selects the letter, and closing the contact energises an electromagnet that prints the cipher letter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A substitution cipher that a typewriter uses is too easy to decipher, so the number of encryption possibilities must be increased.

**Solution:** A roller carries rows of alphabets with contacts in any order; a slider with two contact arms and a viewing hole selects the letter, and closing the contact energises an electromagnet that prints the cipher letter.

**How it works:** The operator moves a slider over the rotating roller until the plain letter shows in a viewing hole; pressing closes a circuit through the contact rail and the matching letter contact to energise a magnet that prints.
