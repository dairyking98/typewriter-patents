## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**How it works (reading):** A coupling between platen and line-setting device disengages automatically when the set line count is exceeded, so the platen cannot be driven past the limit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A calculating machine with a paper carriage can jam if the line-setting safety bell is ignored.

**Solution:** A coupling between platen and line-setting device disengages automatically when the set line count is exceeded, so the platen cannot be driven past the limit.
