## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter design where the type head should print in a regular line with letters side by side.

**Solution:** Type arms arranged in a circle in a housing that slides on rails, with 180-degree-turning type heads and a rack-toothed rail for spacing.

**How it works:** Buttons in concentric circles under a glass cover push probe rods; springs transmit motion to type arms pivoting toward the center. During the stroke the type head turns 180 degrees via toothed wheel on a tongue, from ink pad to the paper on a rubber covered desk. The housing slides on running bars whose teeth, of letter width, are caught by a spring anchor; a metal ring with pins lifted by the rods releases the anchor so the housing steps right one letter.
