## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Back-feeding can let the escapement rack swing out of engagement with the dogs by accident, releasing the carriage suddenly.

**Solution:** A back-feeding dog and its actuating lever hold the swingable escapement rack against swinging during back-feeding, while letting the dog move freely on the reverse stroke.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md)
