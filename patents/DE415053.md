**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an electrically powered tabulating machine and relates in particular to circuit monitoring in such machines the purpose of avoiding the formation of sparks at the brush contacts as reliably as possible through simple, safe-acting (monitoring devices.

**How it works (reading):** A current-monitoring electromagnet in the circuit interrupts or weakens the card circuit while the counter magnet is excited, and the counter magnet circuit stays closed so no spark forms; ball contacts can replace the brushes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Sparks form at the brush contacts of electrically driven tabulating machines when the card circuit breaks under load.

**Solution:** A current-monitoring electromagnet in the circuit interrupts or weakens the card circuit while the counter magnet is excited, and the counter magnet circuit stays closed so no spark forms; ball contacts can replace the brushes.
