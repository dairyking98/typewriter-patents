## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The type guide (directrix) at the print point had to sit at one fixed height, so it fouled the paper when several sheets were on the platen, could not suit platens of different size, and was hard to set or replace.

**Solution:** Hang the guide on four thin rods carried by spring-loaded posts with adjusting screws so it can be raised or lowered bodily.

**How it works:** The guide is a plate with a central tapering opening that centers each typebar at the print point and secures alignment. Its ends are slit to take the converging inner ends of four thin springy rods, riveted in place. The outer rod ends are held on the frame posts under screw heads, resting on followers over spiral springs in the post bores. Turning a screw down compresses its spring and lowers the rod end; turning up raises it. The guide is set close to the platen for one sheet and lowered for manifolding. A variant drops the springs and seats the rod fork in a screw groove.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type guide; directrix opening; sustaining rod; post; adjusting screw; follower; spiral spring; top plate; typebar; platen

**Numbers:** four sustaining rods
