**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by applications of scales or indicators

**In the patent’s own words:** The main objects of my invention is to provide a scale device that is variously marked or graduated for different kinds or classes of work and that may be manipulated so as to bring a particular scale desired for use conveniently into operative position.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriage scales serve only one kind of work, so waybills and tabular forms need other scales.

**Solution:** A rotatable multi-face scale bar, three faces each marked differently, mounted on a Remington No. 6 carriage.

**How it works:** A scale bar with three plane faces is carried on end brackets attached to the carriage frame. Rotating it brings one scale face, for example the machine scale or a way-bill scale, into place opposite the pointer.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** scale bar; end bracket; carriage frame; pointer

**Numbers:** three scale faces

**Improves on:**

- fixed carriage scale: interchangeable by rotation
