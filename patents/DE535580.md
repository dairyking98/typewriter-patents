## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card controlled printing business machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** The platen and its counter roller are made movable so a loose sheet is pushed in, gripped, and then pinched between the rollers so it arrives at the print point together with the registration tape.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punch-card printing machines cannot make a copy on loose sheets at the same time as the recording tape is printed.

**Solution:** The platen and its counter roller are made movable so a loose sheet is pushed in, gripped, and then pinched between the rollers so it arrives at the print point together with the registration tape.
