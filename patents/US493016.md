## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters cannot print characters between earlier impressions, such as carets, inserted letters, superscripts, fractions or borders.

**Solution:** A half-space adjustment of the paper or of the type carriage relative to the other lets the next impressions fall between existing ones.

**How it works:** In a machine with a stationary stand and strip platen, the type-carrier frame moves step by step on a carriage by pawls on a lever acting on a feed rack. The paper is moved across the platen by a feed roller. The invention shifts the paper, or the carriage, by half a letter space. In a book-type machine over a platen on adjustable legs a locking plate on the bed frame side bar allows the shifted position. Fig. 14 shows work produced with the interspacing device.
