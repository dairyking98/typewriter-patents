> Description Translated from German Typewriter The subject of the invention is a typewriter, the essence of which is that by pressing a button, cutouts in front of the Writing surface are brought through in a known manner, in particular by blowing, paint is applied to the writing surface. The main setup of the machine is the same as those of the well-known typewriters of the most diverse systems, with the difference that the characters are not due to the impact of the type lever or the type roller, but is done by stenciling with the elimination of the ribbon. It occurs accordingly In these machines, instead of the ribbon guide, a paint application device, which advantageously consists of a controlled nozzle including a supply line for compressed air or the like, The invention results in the following essential advantages: noiselessness and less Power requirement as a result of the omission of the impact effect; the stencil levers can swing from bottom to top, therefore better arrangement for visible Font, uniform intensity of the font, since there is no ribbon wear.
