## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Impact typing is noisy, power-hungry and wears the ribbon, and stencil levers are hard to view.

**Solution:** Stencil levers carry character cut-outs; pressing a key opens a valve so compressed-air paint passes through the cut-out onto the paper, with no ribbon.
