## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** There is no convenient way to show the usable writing space between the top and bottom of the page.

**Solution:** A body plate with arms, thimbles and rear stops with a foot is mounted on the carriage or name plate so the operator can see the margin limits.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)
