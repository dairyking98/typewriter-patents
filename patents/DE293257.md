## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanism driven by cams engaging rotating roller

**How it works (reading):** Each typebar has its own mechanical drive from a continuously rotating roller, with an adjustable spring or a shaped thumb disc setting that type's impact individually.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In machines where the stroke is powered separately from the keystroke, the contact pressure of each type could not be adjusted individually to suit large or small type faces and the leverage of each bar.

**Solution:** Each typebar has its own mechanical drive from a continuously rotating roller, with an adjustable spring or a shaped thumb disc setting that type's impact individually.

**How it works:** The key lever is split into a key part and a drive lever that are latched by lugs. Pressing the key releases the drive lever, and its spring swings it so a thumb disc in a yoke is pressed onto a constantly turning rubber-covered roller. The disc rolls on the roller, rotating the drive lever clockwise to throw the typebar to the paper through a link, then back to its latch. The spring's tension is set by a screw to adjust impact, or the thumb disc's shape controls speed and force, so each type can have its own pressure.
