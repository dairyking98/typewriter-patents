**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to type-writers and is particularly directed to the improvement of the escapement devices'for controlling the feed movement of the roll-carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The escapement devices controlling the roll-carriage feed need improvement in the earlier Peterson design.

**Solution:** A sliding plunger with ratchet teeth on a carriage support bar and a platen-carriage escapement, as a division of an earlier application.

**How it works:** A spring-held plunger slides in guides and engages the ratchet teeth to control the step feed of the platen carriage; the excerpt is partly garbled.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** plunger; ratchet teeth; carriage support bar; platen roller
