## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switch device on typewriters where precautions have been taken to avoid over-turning the ratchet wheel to avoid.

**How it works (reading):** A protective pawl on a solid trunnion, positioned relative to the switching pawl, engages the ratchet at the end of the advance so it cannot over-turn; a leather buffer on the slide cushions the return stroke, and rubber-sleeved eccentric adjusters limit the hand lever travel and set the feed distance.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-advance ratchet can be over-turned, and the slide and handle produce noise, wear and uneven platen feed.

**Solution:** A protective pawl on a solid trunnion, positioned relative to the switching pawl, engages the ratchet at the end of the advance so it cannot over-turn; a leather buffer on the slide cushions the return stroke, and rubber-sleeved eccentric adjusters limit the hand lever travel and set the feed distance.
