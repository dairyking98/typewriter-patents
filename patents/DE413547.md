## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon movement on a rotary type printer happens in jerks, but an even advance is needed so that a fresh spot of ribbon covers the type each time.

**Solution:** A planetary gear on the type drum axis carries a wheel system that, during the non-printing part of the revolution, drives the ribbon reels through a pivoting lever, moving the ribbon smoothly over the cycle.
