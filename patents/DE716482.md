## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for typewriters for adjusting the platen, especially for typewriters that use stenography should be written.

**How it works (reading):** Arrow keys turn the platen with ratchet wheels mounted directly on the platen shaft, driven by pawls on two rocker shafts, eliminating the gears.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shorthand typing needs characters shifted half a line up or down, but roller-frame moves were heavy and gears added play.

**Solution:** Arrow keys turn the platen with ratchet wheels mounted directly on the platen shaft, driven by pawls on two rocker shafts, eliminating the gears.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet wheels; pawls; arrow keys
