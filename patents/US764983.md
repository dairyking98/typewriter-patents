## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen scale must be moved out of the way when the platen is down for normal work and be raised to the print point for inspection.

**Solution:** A platen scale moves automatically to the print point when the platen is turned to the inspection position and recedes when the platen is turned back down.
