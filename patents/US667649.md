## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in a tabulating arrangement preferably employed in conjunction with the foot-power 7o carriage-restoring device, so that'the carriage may be stopped at any of a number of predetermined positions, one space apart, for convenience in setting down a column of figures in which it is desired that the units should be in the same column and the tens, hundreds, thousands, 850.

**How it works (reading):** A foot treadle restores the carriage and stops it at a chosen space, releasing it automatically near the target so it cannot slam; a release key lets the carriage jump forward several spaces at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage restoration by hand at the end of each line is slow, and the carriage can slam to a stop.

**Solution:** A foot treadle restores the carriage and stops it at a chosen space, releasing it automatically near the target so it cannot slam; a release key lets the carriage jump forward several spaces at once.
