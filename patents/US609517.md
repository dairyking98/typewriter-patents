## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With a tabulating key the carriage is propelled by its spring and halted suddenly by the stop, jarring and risking breaking the machine.

**Solution:** A rock-shaft with tabulating stop and a brake that bears on the carriage so the operator controls the carriage speed during tabulation.

**How it works:** Normal escapement is by a universal bar and two feed dogs engaging a rack on the carriage. Pressing the tabulating key disengages the feed dogs so the spring-driven carriage runs. A rock-shaft carries an adjustable tabulating stop (toothed to the shaft) and a brake that contacts a leather strip on the carriage, with the brake having a scale matching the typewriter scale. The operator controls how hard the brake presses, controlling the carriage speed and the stop jar.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** universal bar; feed dog; rack; rock-shaft; tabulating stop; brake; leather strip; scale
