## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type lever drive, in particular for small typewriters, in which the intermediate lever for uniform acceleration of the type lever stop are driven by the button levers or their handlebars under sliding action.

**How it works (reading):** Intermediate lever slideways are set at varying angles from the centre outward, so the drive acceleration increases toward the outer levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Outer type levers on low typewriters with an oval rest needed extra acceleration to overcome larger travel.

**Solution:** Intermediate lever slideways are set at varying angles from the centre outward, so the drive acceleration increases toward the outer levers.
