## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selected by means of keys or keyboards of the typewriter type

**How it works (reading):** A hollow base holds electromagnets that operate the key-levers (the levers can act as armatures) and is fitted to a machine in use.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keys are tiring to operate and power assistance should be removable.

**Solution:** A hollow base holds electromagnets that operate the key-levers (the levers can act as armatures) and is fitted to a machine in use.
