## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding assemblies, e.g. book-like assemblies made up of single sheets or forms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an easily detachable connection between carbon papers with the sheets to be labeled together by means of an adhesive compound.

**How it works (reading):** A pressure-sensitive adhesive is applied to the edges of the carbon sheets themselves, so they can be stacked and pressed onto writing sheets at any time without extra tools.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets must be glued to the writing sheets, which needs prepared stock or a hardening adhesive, and the glue leaves unsightly edges.

**Solution:** A pressure-sensitive adhesive is applied to the edges of the carbon sheets themselves, so they can be stacked and pressed onto writing sheets at any time without extra tools.

**Features:**
