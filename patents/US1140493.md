## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to attachments for supplying paper from a continuous roll or rolls to the platen oftypewriting machines, adding machines,and the like.

**How it works (reading):** Paper is fed from relatively stationary roll bearings, and the rolls are rotated with the platen so strips stay at right angles and in register.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper fed from a continuous roll twists and falls out of register, spoiling manifold copies.

**Solution:** Paper is fed from relatively stationary roll bearings, and the rolls are rotated with the platen so strips stay at right angles and in register.
