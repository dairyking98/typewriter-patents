## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters needed a continuous roll-paper holder with a way to make manifold copies with supplemental ribbons.

**Solution:** A roll-paper attachment holds a continuous paper roll with a tension device and supplemental inking ribbons for manifold copies.

**How it works:** A support and tension device hold the roll and supplemental ribbons are carried on added spools.
