## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the operating mechanism of type-writing machines, and ' the object of the invention is to provideimproved, simple, and efficient means for operating the keys by a very light touch.

**How it works (reading):** A key rocks a shaft that gives a partial stroke to a universal bar; a toothed wheel and shoe close an electric circuit that energizes a magnet to draw the universal bar through its full stroke, which then rocks the type bar and actuates the carriage escapement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operating the keys required heavy force, and a light touch was needed to actuate the type bar.

**Solution:** A key rocks a shaft that gives a partial stroke to a universal bar; a toothed wheel and shoe close an electric circuit that energizes a magnet to draw the universal bar through its full stroke, which then rocks the type bar and actuates the carriage escapement.
