## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor wants a typewriter where the paper wraps the platen and spacing is done by rotating the platen.

**Solution:** A cylindrical platen onto which a sheet is wrapped, written in circumferential lines: the platen rotates for letter and word spacing and slides along its axis for line spacing, over a basket of typebars.

**How it works:** The paper sheet is wrapped around a platen roller whose circumference is about the sheet width, on a square shaft in a platen frame hinged so it can be flipped up to read. A toothed disk and handle on the shaft front carries a paper scale read against a fixed pointer, used to set the left margin. The spacing bar is struck upward, and its arm with a spring pawl turns the toothed disk to space letters and words. After a line the platen is shifted lengthwise on its shaft to space the next line. The typebars sit in a basket under the platen.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** platen; square shaft; platen frame; hinge; toothed disk; paper scale; pointer; spacing bar; spring pawl; type basket
