## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage can overrun the edge stop and cause shocks that disturb the line switch.

**Solution:** The edge stop is made resilient so the carriage can run out past it and be pushed back by the spring force.

**How it works:** The spring-loaded stop lets the carriage run out, and its spring returns the carriage during the outfeed movement.

**Features:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
