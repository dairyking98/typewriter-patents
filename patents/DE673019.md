## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The applicator axle moves independently of the ink container so a weak spring suffices, and a gear-driven wheel narrower than the ribbon, turned by a fixed roller, drives the ribbon through a horseshoe loop of rollers for even inking.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A paint applicator mounted with the ink fountain needs a hard spring, presses hard on the ribbon and wears it, and forces frequent refilling.

**Solution:** The applicator axle moves independently of the ink container so a weak spring suffices, and a gear-driven wheel narrower than the ribbon, turned by a fixed roller, drives the ribbon through a horseshoe loop of rollers for even inking.
