## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvement on the Yaw escapement (US911303) of the Remington, with adjustable ordinary or reverse feed.

**Solution:** Two same-size escapement wheels on one shaft, relatively adjustable through slots and clamped to a disk, set the drop for ordinary or reverse feed.

**How it works:** A feed rack meshes with a pinion on a shaft whose rear end carries two toothed escapement wheels, identical in diameter and teeth. A disk fixed by set screw lies between them, and clamp screws pass through slots in the front wheel into the rear wheel. Loosening the screws turns one wheel against the other, up to nearly a full letter space, to give ordinary or reverse feed (stepping on the key downstroke).

**Features:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; disk; clamp screw; slot; feed rack; pinion; shaft

**Improves on:**

- US911303: adds adjustable relative setting of two escapement wheels
