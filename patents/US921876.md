## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side lays or gauges

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of this invention is to avoid this difiiculty and provide a practicable means of mounting the side gage without liability of marring the shelf.

**How it works (reading):** A stiffening rod fixed along the rolled rim of the paper shelf, with the side gage clip coiled around the rod so it does not touch the shelf.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Side gages clamped to the paper shelf mar its visible rear edge.

**Solution:** A stiffening rod fixed along the rolled rim of the paper shelf, with the side gage clip coiled around the rod so it does not touch the shelf.
