## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Clutches between the platen and its line ratchet wheel can fail if the engaging pawls are displaced unevenly.

**Solution:** A ball on a spring pin, pressed by the central knob against inclined faces of radially sliding pawls, spreads them into the ratchet's internal teeth, and a cone disc withdraws them evenly.

**How it works:** Radial pawls in a sleeve on the platen shaft engage the internal toothing of the line index wheel when a spring pulls the ball against their inclined faces. Pushing the knob in presses the ball against a conical disc, which acts on other inclined faces and retracts the pawls together. The resilient pin lets the ball sit off-centre when engaging without upsetting the clutch.
