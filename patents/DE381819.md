## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The main patent 367699's automatic ribbon reversal needed a three-armed locking lever, which was complicated.

**Solution:** Give the shift rods themselves a nose, notch and wedge surface so that no separate lever is needed.

**How it works:** Each ribbon spool has a bracket with a guide roller and a spring-pulled shift rod. As the ribbon runs out on one spool, the bracket swings and its shift rod pushes the control pin of a toggle lever through dead centre. A spring then snaps the toggle over and the other shift rod is pushed back and unlocked by the pin. A slide rail moves the drive shaft's crown gears into mesh with the other spool's gear, reversing the winding direction.
