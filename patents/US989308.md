## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In the patent’s own words:** An object of my invention is to produce page end stop mechanism for arresting the paper feed when the bottom of a sheet is approached, said mechanism being simple in cmistruction, positive and convenient in operation and in, the nature of an attachment to an ordinary typewriting machine.

**How it works (reading):** A page-end stop attachment arrests the paper feed when the bottom of the sheet is approached; it has a scale and pointer for setting the stop to different paper lengths and can be switched in or out at will.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper feed must stop at the bottom of a sheet so the typist does not run past the page end, and the stop needs adjusting for different paper lengths.

**Solution:** A page-end stop attachment arrests the paper feed when the bottom of the sheet is approached; it has a scale and pointer for setting the stop to different paper lengths and can be switched in or out at will.
