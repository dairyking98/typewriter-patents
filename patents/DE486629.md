## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (neon sign switching device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Control arrangements or circuits, of interest only in connection with visual indicators other than cathode-ray tubes to give the appearance of moving signs

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an electrical switching device for the demonstration of changing neon characters by means of a typewriter, whose Buttons each close a series of lamp circuits, which are connected by means of a with a Group of contact devices provided voter compiled and by means of a distributor can be distributed to the various lamp panels one after the other.

**How it works (reading):** Typewriter keys each close a series of lamp circuits through contact groups and a distributor that routes them to lamp panels in turn.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier neon sign displays driven from a typewriter needed a complicated distributor and grouping of lamp circuits.

**Solution:** Typewriter keys each close a series of lamp circuits through contact groups and a distributor that routes them to lamp panels in turn.
