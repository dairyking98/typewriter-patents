## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the linespacing chines.

**How it works (reading):** A double ratchet line-space wheel with opposed teeth, and a two-toothed pivoted member, turns the platen and locks it at the end of each stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen overthrows on quick line-spacing strokes and drifts back during writing, slanting the lines.

**Solution:** A double ratchet line-space wheel with opposed teeth, and a two-toothed pivoted member, turns the platen and locks it at the end of each stroke.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
