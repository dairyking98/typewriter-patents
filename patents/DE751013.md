## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with automatic total printing)

**How it works (reading):** Contacts stepped through by the carriage close the magnet circuits of the type carriers one after another, matching the digits of the total.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Automatic total printing in typewriters is slow or mechanically complicated.

**Solution:** Contacts stepped through by the carriage close the magnet circuits of the type carriers one after another, matching the digits of the total.
