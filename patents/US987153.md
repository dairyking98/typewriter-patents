## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The step-by-step carriage feed needs a variable feed rate, which the existing escapement does not provide.

**Solution:** A weight, connected by a line over a pulley to the feed rack, drives the carriage, and a bracket beneath the rack co-acts with the escapement to give a variable feed of the carriage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
