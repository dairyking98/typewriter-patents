## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed with two or more sets of type or printing elements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates primarily tothe typeactions of front-strike writing-machines;

**How it works (reading):** A large number of type-bars are assembled compactly below the printing point on wide hangers, with improved links from keys to type-bars, adjustable key-levers, and spring return for key-levers and type-bars.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike type-action arrangements do not pack many type-bars compactly below the printing point with guided bearings, and key-lever connections and rebound are poor.

**Solution:** A large number of type-bars are assembled compactly below the printing point on wide hangers, with improved links from keys to type-bars, adjustable key-levers, and spring return for key-levers and type-bars.

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md), [Rebound and clash prevention](../features/bar-rebound.md)
