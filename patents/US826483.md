## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The line-spacing feed of flat-platen typewriters does not allow progressive step movement that is easily adjusted for different step widths.

**Solution:** A feed mechanism with loose and fixed dog-plates gives progressive step-by-step platen rotation, set automatically or by hand, and easily adjusted for different step widths.
