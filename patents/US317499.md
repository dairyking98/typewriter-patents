## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Burt wants a compact, cheap typewriter; the opening only lists the parts of a machine with a rotating type dial and a spring-driven sliding carriage.

**Solution:** A sliding carriage with an indicator over a character scale carries a rotating type dial that is turned, lowered and driven by a plunger from one printing key, with a spring escapement spacing the carriage.

**How it works:** A rectangular frame has a scale on its rear bar and a rack bar on which a carriage slides. The carriage carries an indicator and a rotating type dial with types matching the indicator. A printing key works a plunger that forces the dial's type onto the paper through a slot in an inking plate with a ribbon beneath it. Mechanism turns the dial to the chosen character. A coiled spring in a drum with cogs on the rack drives the carriage, and a lever escapement with pallets spaces letters; space keys work it separately, and pallets can be withdrawn to throw the carriage back. The paper is on a grooved roller with a weighted holder.
