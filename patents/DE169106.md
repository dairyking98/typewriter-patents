## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an intermediate space switching mechanism for typewriters with front stop and with the one driving the paper carriage switching device Universal rail moving device for raising and lowering the ribbon fork, as well as for longitudinal movement of the ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** The space key is an angle lever that moves the pawl carrier of the carriage switching device directly, and the universal rail that moves the ribbon is decoupled, so the ribbon device does not operate on a space.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the known space-key device the ribbon and its guide are disturbed when the space key is struck, and the linkage to the space key is complicated.

**Solution:** The space key is an angle lever that moves the pawl carrier of the carriage switching device directly, and the universal rail that moves the ribbon is decoupled, so the ribbon device does not operate on a space.
