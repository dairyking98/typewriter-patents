## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewrit-ing inachines and especially to the key action of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Front-strike key and type action with uniform leverage, the escapement connected to the keys for a prompt and uniform let-off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keys have non-uniform touch, and escapement let-off is not prompt and uniform in front-strike machines.

**Solution:** Front-strike key and type action with uniform leverage, the escapement connected to the keys for a prompt and uniform let-off.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md), [Key touch and even impression](../features/bar-touch.md)
