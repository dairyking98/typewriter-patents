## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object is to provide improved means for mounting and controlling the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A divided platen whose sections are coupled by a hub and coupling head on the axle, improving the mounting of the platen.
