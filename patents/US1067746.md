## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to typewriting machines of the class in which the ribbon is vibrated at each stroke of the type keys to cause the ribbon to cover and uncover the printing point.

**How it works (reading):** A cross arm linking two ribbon carriers rocked from the keyboard, working in either shift position.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Several ribbons or a stencil setting must be chosen from the keyboard.

**Solution:** A cross arm linking two ribbon carriers rocked from the keyboard, working in either shift position.

**How it works:** Two ribbons sit one before the other in a vibrator casing; the carriers are joined by a cross arm so one is raised effective while the other drops idle. Connections rock the cross arm and stay valid whatever platen shift position.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier; cross arm; vibrator casing; platen shift frame

**Improves on:**

- US928987 (Kunath): adds keyboard selection and works in both shift positions
