## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tally strip was fed out of step when the operator forgot to write the total or to line space the strip, so safeguards were needed.

**Solution:** Tally feed rolls work with the platen and advance the tally strip only at the intervals after a total is written, with a clamp and release devices that block feed at other line spaces.
