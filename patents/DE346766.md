> Description Translated from German Tension spring assembly, especially for moving the carriage. The invention relates to a tension spring arrangement which is intended to provide a uniform pull to effect any parts or links of machines. For example, with this arrangement a gentle, as always constant tensile effect on the Carriages of typewriters and similar machines during its forward or return transport be exercised. The new tension spring assembly consists essentially in that for Exercise of the pulling action two or more coupled together, one behind the other switched or arranged one below the other, acting simultaneously and in an expedient manner Way clamped as well as guided coil springs are used, on which as a result the working movement by half, third, etc. or proportionally to its length distributed. Due to this tension spring arrangement, for example, arise for Trolley transport device for typewriters or calculating machines quite significant benefits.
