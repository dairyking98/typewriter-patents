## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single long tension spring for carriage transport is bulky, needs a housing and locking mechanism, and can tear like a strap, while the pull is uneven.

**Solution:** Two or more coil springs are coupled in series or stacked and guided together, so the working movement is shared among them, giving a gentle, constant pull in a smaller space.
