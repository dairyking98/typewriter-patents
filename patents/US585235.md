## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage feed must advance in proportion to the width of each character.

**Solution:** A retaining pawl and a movable selecting plate with its own selecting pawl, set by a notched bar under the key levers, let the ratchet advance by a key-dependent number of teeth.

**How it works:** The carriage is spring-impelled through a ratchet (circular on a feed screw, or a straight rack in a Remington-type) held by a retaining pawl. A rocking frame with a notched bar under all key levers moves a selecting plate carrying a selecting pawl; the pawls are coupled by a pin in a slot so one engages as the other releases. The plate's travel, fixed by which key is pressed, determines how many teeth pass, giving differential spacing. A cam stop-block resets the pawls.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** ratchet; feed screw; retaining pawl; selecting plate; selecting pawl; rocking frame; notched bar; spring barrel; cam stop-block
