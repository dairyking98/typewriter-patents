## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** One vibrator gives a cross feed to the ribbon and selects the upper or lower stripe, changed quickly by one lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon vibrator cannot handle two-colour ribbon and cross feed together.

**Solution:** One vibrator gives a cross feed to the ribbon and selects the upper or lower stripe, changed quickly by one lever.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
