## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention consists in the combination a typewriter, with an auxiliary feed-roller of means automatically preventing it from rotation in one direction, whereby the crinkling of the paper above-mentioned is prevented.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoted serrated brake plate bears on the auxiliary feed roller so that rotation in the feed direction pushes it away, while reverse rotation draws it in and locks the roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An auxiliary feed roller lets paper turn backwards with it, which crinkles the paper.

**Solution:** A pivoted serrated brake plate bears on the auxiliary feed roller so that rotation in the feed direction pushes it away, while reverse rotation draws it in and locks the roller.
