## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention consists in the combination a typewriter, with an auxiliary feed-roller of means automatically preventing it from rotation in one direction, whereby the crinkling of the paper above-mentioned is prevented.
