## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improve the Fitch typewriter (US366577) with variable line space, a stop for the spacing ratchet, a paper receiver, variable letter feed, positive restoring of type arms and better inking.

**Solution:** A set of improvements to a typearm machine with type shifting, a pivoted ranks of type carriers and an ink-roller.

**How it works:** The patent lists many detail improvements on a frame with four legs: a variable line-space feed on the impression cylinder, a stop on the spacing ratchet against momentum, a paper rack, a variable letter-space feed for border work, beveled shoulders on hinged carrying blocks that push the type arms back into rank, an ink roller on a shaft, pressure rollers on the cylinder, shift keys and an oscillating rack with escapement lever and feed-limit plate. Only the opening is available so the exact action of most parts is not shown.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** frame; impression cylinder; type arm; type block; hinged carrying plate; ink roller; pressure roller; key bar; escapement lever; feed-limit plate; paper clamp; paper rack

**Improves on:**

- [US366577](../patents/US366577.md): simplifies and improves the Fitch typewriter
