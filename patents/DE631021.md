## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** A pressure piece hinged to the attachment part doubles as a line indicator: it covers the exit slot and presses the sheet in working position, swings open for insertion, and drives conveyor rollers stepped by a multi-pawl gear with a three-cam thumb disk for line spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheet holders that have a pivoting pressure element make it hard to insert paper and keep the sheet aligned during line reading.

**Solution:** A pressure piece hinged to the attachment part doubles as a line indicator: it covers the exit slot and presses the sheet in working position, swings open for insertion, and drives conveyor rollers stepped by a multi-pawl gear with a three-cam thumb disk for line spacing.

**How it works:** The pressure piece stays down during typing and folds up only for loading; the conveyor rollers advance the sheet by the line spacing set on the cam disk.

**Parts:** pressure piece; attachment part; conveyor rollers; multi-pawl gear; thumb disk with three cams; exit slot

**Numbers:** three cams on line-spacing disk
