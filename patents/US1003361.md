## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriters could not convert plain text to cipher and back with changeable keys.

**Solution:** Interchangeable small plates carrying normal or cipher characters are fixed to the key tops, so the key legends can follow the cipher key.

**How it works:** Key plates on the key levers have a rectangular hole; loose plates with a rectangular pin on the underside fit into them. For enciphering, type levers carry cipher characters such as 21, 36, 45 and the loose plates show plain letters. For deciphering the type levers carry normal characters and the loose plates carry cipher characters. A combined machine carries both.
