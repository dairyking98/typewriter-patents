## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Same as DE625264: carriage takes several steps if key released slowly.

**Solution:** The holding shoulders are placed at the end of every wedge-faced tooth, with the ratchet wheel built from two adjacent discs, one with wedge teeth and one with shoulders.

**How it works:** Key stroke presses the pawl's nose onto the wedge face of a tooth while the holding pawl releases the wheel. The carriage spring pushes the tooth against the pawl until the nose rests on the shoulder, holding the wheel. On key release a spring returns the pawl. Quick strokes do the cycle in one motion.
