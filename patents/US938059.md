## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must stop automatically at the end of a line, and it must also be lockable at chosen positions for different paper widths.

**Solution:** A locking means that holds the universal key bar against movement at a set carriage position, so the key levers cannot move once the line end is reached.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
