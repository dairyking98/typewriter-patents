## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**How it works (reading):** The carriage is stored only at its rear, with castors on two support rods arranged one above the other behind it, which leaves the field of view to the writing point clear.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a book typewriter the carriage is cantilevered forward, and it hides the writing point from the operator.

**Solution:** The carriage is stored only at its rear, with castors on two support rods arranged one above the other behind it, which leaves the field of view to the writing point clear.

**How it works:** Carriage frame side plates connect to a front bar; the rear is supported by castor rods one above the other, so nothing blocks the line of sight to the writing point.
