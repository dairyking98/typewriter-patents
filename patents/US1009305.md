## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon strikes always along one line so it wears in one strip.

**Solution:** A ribbon carrier that slides the ribbon edgewise on a slotted support to bring different longitudinal strips of the ribbon under the type.

**How it works:** A plate with turned-over branches forming loops holds the ribbon. It is clamped to a support (the Oliver ribbon-carrier support) by a T-bolt in a slot and a thumbnut, with a squared T-screw guide. Marks on the plate and an index pointer show and lock the position. A modified form uses a spring tongue with a pin that enters one of three holes for centre and side positions.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier plate; loop branch; support; T-bolt; thumbnut; index pointer; spring tongue; locating pin

**Numbers:** three positions in modified form

**Improves on:**

- Oliver typewriter ribbon carrier: ribbon can be shifted edgewise to use fresh strips
