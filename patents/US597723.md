## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators must take a hand off the keyboard to return the carriage and feed the line.

**Solution:** A foot-treadle attachment with a folding bracket, pulley and strap that pulls the line-space lever.

**How it works:** A standard clamps to the machine frame and holds a swivelling arm with a wheel/pulley and an end pulley. A strap from the wheel runs over the end pulley to the line-space lever; a cord from the wheel's hub pulley runs down through the table to a treadle arm and pedal. Pressing the pedal pulls the lever as the hand would. The arm swings against the machine side so the cover fits.

**Features:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** standard; swivel arm; wheel; pulley; strap; cord; treadle arm; pedal; base-board

**Improves on:**

- earlier treadle mechanisms: no alterations to the machine, cover still fits
