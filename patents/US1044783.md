## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A variable-throw ribbon vibrator tends to overthrow, so an impression picks up part of two ribbon zones of different colour.

**Solution:** Stop devices, including an eccentric stop, definitely limit each extent of vibrator movement.

**How it works:** A bell crank on the shifting platen support connects to the ribbon vibrator; a second bell crank is rocked by a link from the universal bar. A pin on that link engages one of two notches in the crank, giving long or short throw for the lower or upper ribbon zone, or none for stencil cutting. A hand lever with a three-recess leaf spring detent sets the pin through a rock shaft, link and slotted plate. An eccentric stop on the plate sits under the jaw of the bell crank arm and limits travel so only the intended zone prints.
