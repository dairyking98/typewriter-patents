## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** Starting a totalling operation returns the print-unit cut-off to its on position and locks the shift lever.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash registers printing checks for single amounts or totals can produce incomplete checks if the print-unit cut-off is changed during totalling.

**Solution:** Starting a totalling operation returns the print-unit cut-off to its on position and locks the shift lever.

**How it works:** A shift lever with notched disc and pawl switches check issue on or off. A locking disc on a shaft blocks the pawl during a machine cycle. A cam slot and linkage position an intermediate member that, when active, lets a pin press a counter-roll against a conveyor roller to print and issue the check. The total key forces the cut-off back on.

**Parts:** shift lever; pawl; locking disc; intermediate member; lifting disc; counter-roll; conveyor roller
