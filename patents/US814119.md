## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-Writers;

**How it works (reading):** The carriage advance is differentiated by the key pressed, so letters, punctuation and spaces each get a movement suited to their width, using the letter-key, symbol-key or other key that actuates the movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Carriage feed is equal for every stroke, so wide letters like m are cramped and narrow letters like l get too much space.

**Solution:** The carriage advance is differentiated by the key pressed, so letters, punctuation and spaces each get a movement suited to their width, using the letter-key, symbol-key or other key that actuates the movement.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** carriage; movable member of the printing couple; letter-key and symbol-key actuators; feeding or spacing mechanism
