## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to automatically actuated printing point indicators in which the indicator is actuated at each printing operation to automatically move away from the normal or indicating position-as the type approaches the printing point.
