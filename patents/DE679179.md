## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms; Levers having moving or variable fulcra to alter the mechanical advantage during the stroke

**How it works (reading):** The raceway of the eccentric drive pulley guides an intermediate lever through blocks or rollers on two opposite sides, so the linkage is positively driven in both directions without springs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Springs and pinned slot linkages in the lifting drive rattle, skid and give disruptions.

**Solution:** The raceway of the eccentric drive pulley guides an intermediate lever through blocks or rollers on two opposite sides, so the linkage is positively driven in both directions without springs.
