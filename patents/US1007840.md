## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With a two-colour or striped ribbon, earlier lifts were not positively controlled and could overthrow, so an impression printed partly on one stripe and partly on the other.

**Solution:** A ribbon-vibrator operating lever is positively stopped at one of several fixed points, and a shiftable link connects the keys to it either closely or through lost motion, so the ribbon always lands on the chosen stripe.

**How it works:** Each key depression swings a universal bar frame, which pulls a forked link joined to it through a yielding spring arm. The fork embraces a ribbon-vibrator operating lever with a stud on each side. For the black stripe, the link engages one stud closely, so the lever moves at once and is arrested by a stop at a fixed point. For the red stripe, the link engages the other stud, whose slot gives lost motion, and the lever moves further to a second stop. A middle position disengages the link from both studs so the ribbon stays down for stencil work. A finger button on the front, through two bell cranks, a link and a slide rod, shifts a combined switching and stop member into the selected position, and a notched front plate holds it there.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon spool; ribbon vibrator; operating lever; forked link; universal bar frame; yielding spring arm; stop member; slide rod; bell crank lever; finger button; notched front plate; fulcrum member; spring drum; escapement wheel

**Numbers:** 4 sheets of drawings; 3 notch positions for black, stencil and red

**Improves on:**

- earlier striped-ribbon mechanisms: the ribbon throw is positively stopped at each setting, so there is no overthrow onto the wrong stripe
