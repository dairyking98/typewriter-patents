**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Continuous assemblies made up of webs folded transversely

**How it works (reading):** Edge holes of carbon and writing sheets are made different sizes so the pins seat in the stack without stressing the thin sheets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicating sheets with equal edge holes tear or misalign when conveyor pins enter a stack.

**Solution:** Edge holes of carbon and writing sheets are made different sizes so the pins seat in the stack without stressing the thin sheets.
