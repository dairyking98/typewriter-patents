## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wheel printers need to carry upper and lower case.

**Solution:** Type fingers on a wheel carry types in pairs with a notch so both of a pair can be struck.

**How it works:** A wheel with radial fingers carrying types turns to locate a type, and the frame swings up from the roll platen. Types are in pairs, upper and lower case, with a depression allowing selection of either.
