## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writers, and more particularly to that class of machines having two or more type shuttles or segments adapted to be interchangeably brought into operative position for use.

**How it works (reading):** A vertically movable shuttle anvil with guide horns on the spindle and turn-table seats a stack of shuttles accurately, with added stroke-regulated shift keys, a graduated paper-roll index for printing above/below the line, and two ribbons with a double-headed hammer for two colors.

## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hammond-type machines with several interchangeable type shuttles need a reliable way to seat the shuttles correctly when changed, and cannot print superior/inferior characters (formulae) or multiple colors.

**Solution:** A vertically movable shuttle anvil with guide horns on the spindle and turn-table seats a stack of shuttles accurately, with added stroke-regulated shift keys, a graduated paper-roll index for printing above/below the line, and two ribbons with a double-headed hammer for two colors.

**How it works:** Several type shuttles slide in segmental grooves of an anvil that rotates on a spindle. In the four-shuttle machine the anvil is lifted on the spindle (limited by stop pins) and turned off a turn-table rim; spindle horns hold all shuttles while raised, and on dropping, upper and lower horns hold three shuttles while the driving arm engages the fourth for writing. Shift keys of the anvil have a sector and lever with regulating screws that set the stroke so characters print higher or lower on the text line. An index wheel with graduated scale and pointer on the paper-roll shaft lets the paper be raised or lowered to exact distances for fractions and sub/superscripts. Two ribbons of different color are stacked and a spring-detented double-headed hammer is swung to strike either one.
