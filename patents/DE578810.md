## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register document sensing)

**How it works (reading):** The receipt sensor is driven from the main shaft and blocks the machine after a small partial rotation when no document is inserted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-lever cash registers could not easily drive a receipt sensor from each key lever, and direct sensing would be pierced by the key pressure.

**Solution:** The receipt sensor is driven from the main shaft and blocks the machine after a small partial rotation when no document is inserted.
