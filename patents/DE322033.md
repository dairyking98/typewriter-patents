## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage rollers needed projecting spacer plates to hold them apart, which were in the way and needed extra securing.

**Solution:** Rollers with reduced-diameter ends that sit in diagonal slots of a spacer, which carries the rollers along with the carriage.
