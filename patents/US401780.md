## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (whole-word reporting-speed printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a machine of that class known as type-writers, and has for its object to provide a simple and efficient machine of this character by which whole words or phrases may be printed at once on the same line across a page and at a reporting speed by depressing keys of a key-board conveniently arranged at the front of the instrument.

**How it works (reading):** Type carried on sectors on shafts, selected by sectional keys with coupling devices; the ribbon is shifted and the carriage is retracted by a spring mechanism.
