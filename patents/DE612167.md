## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** A toothed push rod moved by the line shift lever runs in a straight line until a pawl-carrying slide meets an adjustable slide stop, locking the lever after a preset number of line spaces.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typist cannot see how much sheet is left at the bottom edge, so the last lines go crooked or the paper slips out of the roller, and earlier scales or circular ratchet gauges were hard to set or bulky.

**Solution:** A toothed push rod moved by the line shift lever runs in a straight line until a pawl-carrying slide meets an adjustable slide stop, locking the lever after a preset number of line spaces.

**How it works:** A push rod with a rack is guided on the carriage frame and has holes for a stop. A hinged lever on the line shift lever carries a roller that, when the lever moves, hits the stop in the rod and pushes it against a spring. A slide with a spring pawl rides on the rack, with a pointer over a scale, and an adjustable slide stop clamps anywhere on the guide. When the slide hits the stop, the rod cannot move and the line shift lever is locked. The remaining writing lines can be read off the scale, further stops can be set, and the device is detachable so the machine can be boxed.
