## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A check punch that is simple, durable and convenient to manipulate is wanted.

**Solution:** A check punch with a base carrying legs and a channel, angle arms that carry an indicating plate, and a punching unit that perforates figures for the check face value.

**How it works:** A base with a channel carries arms and an indicating plate that positions the check beneath the punch.
