## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to means for doing what is known as condensed record work and has for its object to provide improved devices for facilitating such work in typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A detachable rotatable shaft carries auxiliary paper fingers that swing into or out of operation without changing the terminal fingers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Condensed record billing needs a duplicate written through carbon, which the standard paper fingers cannot handle alone.

**Solution:** A detachable rotatable shaft carries auxiliary paper fingers that swing into or out of operation without changing the terminal fingers.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
