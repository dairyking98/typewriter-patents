## Summary

**How it works (reading):** Drive the shift plate that raises and lowers the type basket through a buffer drive lever so the basket accelerates, passes dead centre and decelerates into each end position.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting a power-driven typebar machine whose type basket (segment) moves up and down for case change is abrupt, causing shock and noise.

**Solution:** Drive the shift plate that raises and lowers the type basket through a buffer drive lever so the basket accelerates, passes dead centre and decelerates into each end position.

**How it works:** The type basket sits on steel leaf springs so it moves parallel to itself between two positions, limited by nuts and elastic washers on a spindle. Two angle brackets on the basket are linked by levers to a two-step shift plate on a shaft. Pressing a shift key releases a pawl on a multi-arm lever, engaging the continuously turning drive roller to rotate the shift plate. The motion is slow-fast-slow, and the same sequence in reverse returns the basket.
