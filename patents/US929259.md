## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage should be retarded during its spring-driven travel, and the inventor wants a controlled retarding device for the carriage.

**Solution:** A hollow device fixed on the top plate contains a cooperating rotary device; their relative rotation damps the carriage travel as it runs under the spring drive.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
