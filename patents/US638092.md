## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of this invention is to obtain with only five keys all the characters and signs usually employed in writing, the keys being limited to five, so that the Writing can be accomplished by the use of but one hand.

**How it works (reading):** Five-key chord machine giving 31 characters, 93 with two auxiliary keys, with automatic spacing measured per letter and writing in sight.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Same as US638091: full keyboards need many keys and both hands.

**Solution:** Five-key chord machine giving 31 characters, 93 with two auxiliary keys, with automatic spacing measured per letter and writing in sight.

**How it works:** Pressing one or several of five keys at once selects a character; the two auxiliary keys shift among sets. Spacing is automatic for each letter so mixed type styles can be used. Writing is visible, paper need not be doubled, so cards and sheet materials work. Machine is light and portable.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [SEL:dummy-keyboard](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** main key; auxiliary lever key; type carrier; carriage

**Numbers:** 5 keys; 31 combinations; 93 characters

**Improves on:**

- [US638091](../patents/US638091.md): later refinement by same inventor, with writing in sight
