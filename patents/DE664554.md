## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (registering machine printing unit)

**How it works (reading):** Several type sets are carried in the rotatable platen and connected to the setting means through differential gearing, so a pressure roller brings one or another set to the print point without changing the settings.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rotary printing rollers are quieter than hammers, but their type sets must be disconnected from the setting means during printing, and one type set cannot serve several uses.

**Solution:** Several type sets are carried in the rotatable platen and connected to the setting means through differential gearing, so a pressure roller brings one or another set to the print point without changing the settings.
