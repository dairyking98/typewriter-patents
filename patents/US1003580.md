## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to bearings for the carriages of typewriting machines and its object is to provide improved governing or controlling devices for the anti-friction rollers of carriage bearings.

**How it works (reading):** A governing or controlling device for the anti-friction roller carrier keeps it separate from the margin stop and its frame, so the carriage stays fixed on its bearings.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Creeping or disarrangement between the carriage and its bearings occurs from jarring and from the inertia of the roller carrier, which is linked to the margin regulating devices.

**Solution:** A governing or controlling device for the anti-friction roller carrier keeps it separate from the margin stop and its frame, so the carriage stays fixed on its bearings.

**How it works:** The roller carrier is controlled independently of the hand margin stop mechanism so it does not move the carriage relative to its rails.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** roller carrier; carriage rollers; hand margin stop; front and rear rails
