## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to wheel escapements and comprises a novel arrangement wherein two escape wheels are employed cooperating with a single movable dog.

**How it works (reading):** Two escape wheels on a shared shaft with one movable rolling-contact dog, the dog passing between adjacent wheels to escape each tooth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Single-dog escapement is a drop device whose dog must move between wheels during each letter space.

**Solution:** Two escape wheels on a shared shaft with one movable rolling-contact dog, the dog passing between adjacent wheels to escape each tooth.
