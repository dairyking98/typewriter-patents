## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The heavy case (shift) key forces an awkward finger posture and takes a hand off the home position.

**Solution:** A knee-operated lever and cord attachment that actuates the typewriter's case bar so both hands stay on the keys.

**How it works:** A plate fixed under the typewriter table carries an extensible plate with a grooved pulley and a folding support bar held by a flat spring. A bell crank lever on the support bar is tied by a cord over the pulley to the case bar. A knee lever with a curved finger engaging a cam groove in the bell crank pulls the cord when the operator presses it with the knee. The lever is adjustable in length to suit table height.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** supporting plate; extensible plate; pulley; folding bar; flat spring; bell crank lever; cord; knee lever; knee plate; thumb screw

**Improves on:**

- finger-operated case key on Remington-type machines: moves case operation to the knee
