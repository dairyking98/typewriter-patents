The ribbon can now be slid flat from above into the Forks are let in. and the tape edges on both sides are not subject to any Leadership, but pull through freely, so that an inhibition of the tape transport or wear of the belt edges cannot occur. This has the further advantage that ribbons from the smallest to largest common width can be used and that also readily multicolored ribbons, for example in multiple coils one on top of the other running, are usable. The conveniently deployable straps have free play in the guide fork, so that an unavoidable entrainment or distortion is excluded.
