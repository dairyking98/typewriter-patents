## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** A two-armed ribbon fork with an open top slot is hung on the swinging type head guide near its axis, so the ribbon drops in from above with free edges and passes the strike point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon guided at its edges on a fixed guide can bind or fray, and wide and multicolor ribbons are hard to run.

**Solution:** A two-armed ribbon fork with an open top slot is hung on the swinging type head guide near its axis, so the ribbon drops in from above with free edges and passes the strike point.
