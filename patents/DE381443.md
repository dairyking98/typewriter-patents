## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriters either folded over the keyboard or had a paper roller too deep to see easily.

**Solution:** Make a deep-set paper roller machine and swing the rear part up on a pivot so the roller is at a good height for writing.
