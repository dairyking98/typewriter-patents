## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (word-counting device for typed text) · **Subject (patent classes):** Word counters

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for counting with a typewriter written words.

**How it works (reading):** A counter linked to the typewriter space key through levers and a pin-and-slot arrangement; a pawl advances a ratchet wheel, and a toothed arc with a second wheel carries the drive so that the counter can reach a large number without overstressing the spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing word counters used toothed wheels that spring back after one turn, which wears the spring and limits the count.

**Solution:** A counter linked to the typewriter space key through levers and a pin-and-slot arrangement; a pawl advances a ratchet wheel, and a toothed arc with a second wheel carries the drive so that the counter can reach a large number without overstressing the spring.
