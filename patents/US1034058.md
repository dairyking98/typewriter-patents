## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand twirling or a hand lever to feed paper on the platen takes several movements and is clumsy.

**Solution:** A platen actuating mechanism that feeds or discharges paper with a single hand movement, meant to be cheap and compact.
