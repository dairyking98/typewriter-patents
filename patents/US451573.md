## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars must reach the same print point though bars wear or differ in size, and rigid or pivoted-dog guides wear loose.

**Solution:** A guide opening ringed by radial, laterally yielding springs centres every typebar and compensates for wear.

**How it works:** A funnel-shaped cup or a support plate with an opening sits near the printing surface. Typically four radial springs, adjustably fixed by slots and screws, extend over the opening with free ends stopping short of the centre. A typebar passes between them and is pushed to the common centre, so bars of different size align; one spring can be made rigid for positive alignment. The slots allow re-centering as parts wear.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)

**Parts:** funnel cup; support plate; radial spring; slot and screw; typebar

**Numbers:** typically four springs

**Improves on:**

- [US403075](../patents/US403075.md): same inventor's funnel guide, here with spring sections
- radial pivoted dogs around type opening: springs have no pivots to wear loose
