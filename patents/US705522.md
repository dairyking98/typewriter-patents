## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a flat billing platen, the carriage must be stopped at each tally-sheet entry and then released to return, and this is hard to do automatically.

**Solution:** A tally-sheet attachment positions the machine over the tally sheet with stop mechanism, and printing the entry releases the stop so the carriage returns to its initial position.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
