## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Keyboards with one set of keys for each denomination; Casings convertible reductible in size, e.g. for transportation

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the keys oftypewriting and other instruments, and particu larly to the denomination keys of tabulating mechanisms in which it is sometimes desired to have the same key indicate either one" or another denomination.

**How it works (reading):** A character disk with two sets of characters rotates under a fixed shutter with an aperture, and a detent holds the disk in either position.

**Problem:** A denomination key cannot show different characters without changing the key itself.

**Solution:** A character disk with two sets of characters rotates under a fixed shutter with an aperture, and a detent holds the disk in either position.
