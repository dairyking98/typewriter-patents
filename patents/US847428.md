## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bills and cards must be squared to the printing line by the operator, which is slow and wastes time when the platen is turned back for each bill.

**Solution:** A gage stands below the platen and is raised against it by the same key that releases the pressure rolls, giving a fixed reference for the leading edge.

**How it works:** Pressing the release key on the platen frame also throws the gage up against the paper, squaring the sheet to the printing line.
