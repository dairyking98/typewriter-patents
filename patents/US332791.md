## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to inking-ribbon annunciators for type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A bell struck by a hammer that is driven from the letter-space ratchet rocking frame through a linkage, sounding when the ribbon reaches its travel limit, while the ribbon is held flat and taut.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator is not warned when the inking ribbon reaches the end of its travel, and the ribbon under the platen can slacken.

**Solution:** A bell struck by a hammer that is driven from the letter-space ratchet rocking frame through a linkage, sounding when the ribbon reaches its travel limit, while the ribbon is held flat and taut.
