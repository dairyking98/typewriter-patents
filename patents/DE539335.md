## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor adjustable, e.g. for case shift

**How it works (reading):** The drive member is connected directly to the ribbon fork, and the driving handlebar is pivoted at one end on the machine frame and at the other on the switch frame, moved by the adjusting device by a smaller amount than the drive member.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A two-colour ribbon fork drive with an adjusted driving link gives uneven keystrokes, because a dead gear is present between the stop and the drive member.

**Solution:** The drive member is connected directly to the ribbon fork, and the driving handlebar is pivoted at one end on the machine frame and at the other on the switch frame, moved by the adjusting device by a smaller amount than the drive member.

**How it works:** The handlebar follows a different path when the carriage switches, so the drive member moves the same distance in the upper and lower carriage positions, removing dead gear between stop and drive member.

**Features:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
