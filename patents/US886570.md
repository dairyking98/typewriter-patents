## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type rest or pad that the type bars strike against wears and its facing needs a secure, replaceable retainer.

**Solution:** A type rest with a facing or pad held by a retaining member, so the facing can be fitted and renewed on the Monarch machine.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
