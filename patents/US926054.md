## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing two-ribbon winding gear on the Underwood front-strike machine is complicated and hard to fit to existing machines.

**Solution:** A single lever on the ribbon-shift rock shaft, linked to two swiveled hangers, moves the winding gears together or apart to connect either pair of spools.

**How it works:** Two ribbons, front and rear, of different colors or qualities, each wind on a pair of spools in cups at either side of the printing point, and pass through their own vibrators. The universal bar rocks a shaft whose arms carry pawls on ratchet wheels that turn mitre gears at each side. Winding gears on shafts in swiveled hangers mesh with the inner or outer spool gears. Keys at the front turn a rock shaft carrying a lever with an upward and a downward arm, joined by a short link to one hanger and a long link under the typebars to the other, so the gears swing toward or away from each other. A detent holds the mechanism in any of three positions.
