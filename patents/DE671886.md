## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The pawl carrier of a line-switch bends and tilts under vigorous hand-lever movement.

**Solution:** The pawl carrier pivots on an outward hub of the ratchet wheel, bears against the shaft bearing and the line index wheel, and has a fixed part running over the wheel circumference to stop it bending.
