## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing cards)

**How it works (reading):** A pivoting carrier with clamps and guide strips holds the card and swings it under the type wheels and print hammer by hand; the pivot can be moved and adjusted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Special cards or receipts of various sizes cannot be printed in a fixed cash register.

**Solution:** A pivoting carrier with clamps and guide strips holds the card and swings it under the type wheels and print hammer by hand; the pivot can be moved and adjusted.
