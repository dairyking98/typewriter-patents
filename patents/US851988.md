## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal and variable vibrator stroke were done with complicated mechanisms on the Sun typewriter.

**Solution:** Swinging gear shaft shifted by followers riding on the ribbon coils, plus a slotted pivoted arm and link to vary the ribbon vibrator stroke.

**How it works:** A swinging shaft with gears on its ends is swung alternately into mesh with two spool-winding gears. Two shifters on a universal bar normally stay idle, held by followers riding on the ribbon coils. When a spool empties its follower drops and the shifter swings the shaft to reverse winding. A pivoted arm with a radiating slot and a link to the vibrator lets a finger lever shift the link along the slot, varying the stroke to select a ribbon band or none.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; swinging shaft; winding gear; shifter; follower; universal bar; slotted arm; link; finger lever

**Improves on:**

- Sun typewriter ribbon reverse: coil-riding followers trigger reversal when a spool empties
