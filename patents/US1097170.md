## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A typewriter needs a movable scale for reading the carriage position against letter spaces.

**Solution:** A movable scale belt runs over pulleys and lies between two fixed scales, with a detent to center the belt, all read against the main scale.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
