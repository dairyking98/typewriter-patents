## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shifting the paper cylinder between capitals and lower case needs a hand on the shift key, so the hands are not free to type.

**Solution:** A foot pedal piston and air tube drive a collapsible rubber pneumatic cylinder connected to the shifter bar.

**How it works:** A foot pedal works a piston in an air chamber on the floor. A flexible air tight tube joins it to a rubber bodied pneumatic cylinder bolted to the Remington frame. Suction collapses the cylinder, and atmospheric pressure moves a rod to a bell crank on the shifter rock-shaft, shifting the cylinder. The existing coil spring returns the shifter bar when the foot is lifted.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** pneumatic cylinder; rubber body; foot pedal; piston; air tube; bell crank; shifter bar; rock-shaft; return spring

**Improves on:**

- Remington shift key: foot operated shift using air suction rather than a hand key
