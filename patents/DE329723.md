## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards characterised by means for holding or fastening typewriters or computer equipment

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a mounting stand for typewriters with one arranged on a stationary stand for clamping the typewriter serving frame, which rotated when lifting the machine clamped in it and can be folded, when you let go of the machine, on the other hand, in any desired way Location is held.

**How it works (reading):** A rotating frame on a stand clamps the machine by its frame; lifting frees it to turn, and releasing the clamp holds it in place.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter needed to be turned and held in any position for assembly and adjustment.

**Solution:** A rotating frame on a stand clamps the machine by its frame; lifting frees it to turn, and releasing the clamp holds it in place.
