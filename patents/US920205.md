## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dirt builds up on typewriter type and needs removing without awkward manual cleaning.

**Solution:** A clamp-on bar carries a conical rotary brush that is raised to the type and spun back and forth by a sliding rack.

**How it works:** A stock bar clamps to the typewriter frame with a U-bolt and thumb nuts and carries a tubular arm. The inverted-conical brush on a shaft is raised or lowered by a shifter lever; spring extensions on the lever hold it up by a cross bar entering a notch and a shaft groove. When raised, a pinion on the shaft meshes with a rack on a slide, so pushing and pulling the slide's knob rotates the brush alternately both ways across the type. Lowering the brush disengages the pinion.
