## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Setting the embossing depth for finger rings is slow and must be guessed.

**Solution:** A typeless rod on the type shaft sets the depth; a carriage on the ring is lifted until it touches the rod, then the types are attached.
