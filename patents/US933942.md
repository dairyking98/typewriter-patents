## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When copying a series of bills onto one larger sheet, the operator loses space or runs bills together because the start of each bill is not indicated.

**Solution:** A dial wheel with line-space numbers is turned by gears from the platen shaft, and an indicator shows where the next bill should start, giving equal spacing between bills regardless of their length.
