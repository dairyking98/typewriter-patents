## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to reduce the weight and simplify the construction of the platen carriage, while insuring the rigidity and stiffness of the carriage to stand the strain without bending or buckling, in any My new carriage is of light weight, its parts are reinforced and stayed so that it will not become twisted or distorted by cross and diagonal strains, and it is, moreover, economical to manufacture.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage is built from pressed sheet metal: a ribbed bottom member and bent end members carrying the platen and depending rails.
