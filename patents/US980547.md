## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Keyboards with one set of keys for each denomination; Casings convertible reductible in size, e.g. for transportation

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the keys of typewriting and other instruments, and particularly to the denomination keys of a tabulating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A revolving annular shutter on a fixed base, under a transparent disk and key cap, exposes one of several characters at a time so the key can be re-set for a different denomination.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The same key must serve different denominations, but a fixed key cannot show a different character without rebuilding it.

**Solution:** A revolving annular shutter on a fixed base, under a transparent disk and key cap, exposes one of several characters at a time so the key can be re-set for a different denomination.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by turning the element](../features/shift-rotary.md)
