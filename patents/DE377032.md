## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** On small portable machines the lock for shift keys must work with little lost motion and few light parts.

**Solution:** A friction-held locking arm grips an adjustable shoulder on the shift key lever, released by a light press on the shift key.

**How it works:** Depressing the figure shift key raises the shift frame and removes a stop so the frame goes to the figures position. Pressing a button swings a lever whose end rides over a shoulder on the key lever and holds it by friction alone against a return spring. A press on the shift key yields slightly and frees it. The shoulder is on a small pivoted lever with a screw, adjustable from the front. The capital key is locked likewise.
