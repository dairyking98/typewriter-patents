## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing in Braille or with keyboards specially adapted for use by blind or disabled persons

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improvement.

**How it works (reading):** A writing machine with styluses or type-bars, a paper carriage with feed rollers, and a clutch, so that power or mechanical advantage reduces the operator's exertion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Embossed writing for the blind required heavy work and great effort by the operator's hands.

**Solution:** A writing machine with styluses or type-bars, a paper carriage with feed rollers, and a clutch, so that power or mechanical advantage reduces the operator's exertion.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
