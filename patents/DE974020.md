## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A punched-card labelling machine that senses a card and prints on it must also be able to sense a master (mother) card and print its data on the following (daughter) card, without the daughter card disturbing the sensing-pin setting made by the mother card.

**Solution:** A card stop that a control hole in the mother card adjusts so it holds the daughter card where its holes are not over the sensing pins, while the pin setting is blocked from resetting until the print wheels have printed the stored data.

**How it works:** Cards from a magazine are fed by a gripper and rollers to a sensing chamber where sensing pins in a rising box pass through the holes and set adjustment pins that drive a decoding unit with stop rails and rack-driven print wheels. A card stop frame holds a card either at the sensing position or, for the daughter card, at a second position where the pins find no holes. A control hole in the mother card sets a hook and latch linkage that gives the card stop a longer stroke on the next cycle, holds the reset pawl out of action so the pin setting is retained, and keeps the print release pawl so that the mother card itself is not printed. On the daughter card the print wheels are released and print the retained data, with a floating pressure pad clamping the card and card stops choosing the top or bottom printing zone. A hand rod switches the machine to the ordinary mode of sensing and printing the same card.
