## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**How it works (reading):** A sheet-metal carrier holds the typewriter freely floating inside the case, suspended by adjustable longitudinal straps that fold over the case edges, and set with a screwdriver.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A portable typewriter in luggage takes up space and its weight damages other contents when stowed loosely in a suitcase.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal carrier holds the typewriter freely floating inside the case, suspended by adjustable longitudinal straps that fold over the case edges, and set with a screwdriver.
