## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Tens transmission and reset device for the transverse counter from Typewriters. The invention relates to a ten circuit for the transverse counter of typewriters like the Elliott Fisher accounting machine available on the market. Its purpose is to ensure safe operation of the machine through simplified means to effect. The ten circuit is set up so that a single group of Drive springs the transmission device either for subtraction or for sets the addition in action. In this device, a toggle lever is provided, which is arranged so that the transmission device is locked in the rest position and that only a very small force is required to release the toggle lever to effect, whereupon the tens transmission is carried out by spring force. In connection with this transverse counter transmission device is a simple and effective feedback device that requires a minimum of Force required to operate it to move the tens transmission device into the Due to rest position.
