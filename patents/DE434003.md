## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (small grinder switch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for stopping or slowing electric motors, generators, or dynamo-electric converters for stopping or slowing an individual dynamo-electric motor or dynamo-electric converter

**How it works (reading):** A notched disk on the drive train lets a spring-loaded double lever release the push button after a set number of turns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A motor-driven grinder keeps running until someone switches it off, so the grind amount is hard to control.

**Solution:** A notched disk on the drive train lets a spring-loaded double lever release the push button after a set number of turns.
