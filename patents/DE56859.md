## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lines of matrix type must be justified exactly; the leftover space at the end of a line must be spread evenly by counting spaces.

**Solution:** A typewriter with a registration device that shows the number of unit spaces left in the line, so the operator can divide the extra space evenly for accurate line justification.

**How it works:** The typewriter counts units of measure against each character width and shows the remaining line space; at the end of a line the number of spaces left is shown, and the die-punching machine receives a differential feed electrically.

**Features:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
