## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Clamping manuscripts of varying thickness and moving the line ruler by hand are awkward.

**Solution:** Clamping rails hinged to arms swing from sloping to upright positions, and chains on wheels move a line ruler line by line, with a switch lever and an adjustable spacing.
