> Description Translated from German Manuscript holder for typewriters. The invention relates to on a manuscript holder for typewriters with a pair of clamping rails via pulling elements that run on two wheels and are connected to a switchable linear ruler. The invention consists in that each clamping rail by at least two arms with the lower part of the manuscript holder is hinged, of which two arms connected by a rod. This makes it easier way enables the clamping of manuscripts of different strengths. The subject of the invention is shown on the drawing in an exemplary manner Execution shown. It shows: Fig. I the top view, Fig. 2 the front view, Fig.3 a longitudinal section according to A-B of Fig. 1, Fig.4 a side view and Fig.5 a cross section according to C-D of Fig. i. The manuscript holder, which can be placed on the side of a typewriter or placed at an angle, has two documents i and 2 intended for placing a sheet of paper, as well as two paper clips 3 and 4. A line ruler 5 slides on two parallel clamping rails 6 and 7, which by means of the arms 8, 9, io, ii and 12 are hinged to the lower parts i and 2.
