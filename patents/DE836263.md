## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printed tickets eject face-down and reversed, or fly out of the tray, so the seller must turn them.

**Solution:** A catch on the carriage holds the ejected ticket upright on edge in a stop-bar holder, ready to take in the correct orientation.
