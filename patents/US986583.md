## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets fed back over the paper shelf catch under the guide ends and jam or skew.

**Solution:** A ridge on the paper shelf below the guide floors lifts the bottom of the sheet so backward feed directs it up over the guides.
