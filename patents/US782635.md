## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A hand typewriter of light weight and small size, certain in action, that can be carried in a pocket.

**Solution:** A squeezable U-shaped hand-grasp sweeps a pointer over a half-circle index plate while a rack and twin pinions turn a type wheel in unison, and a second grasp strikes a hammer and then advances the carriage on release.

**How it works:** A half-circular index plate with radially arranged characters sits at one end of an 8 in. by 1 in. plate; a pinion with a pointer riding in notches aligned with the characters is turned by a reciprocating rack-bar moved by the right-hand spring grasp. A duplicate pinion at the other end, driven by a second rack on the same bar, turns the type wheel through the same angle so the chosen type lies under the hammer. The type wheel holds sliding types in radial slots, each returned by a spring arm. Squeezing the left-hand grasp rocks a shaft that drives the hammer onto the type, with ribbon between. On release of the left grasp its extension kicks a lever and pawl that advance the ratchet carriage one tooth, an auxiliary pawl holds it, and a finger lifts the pawls for carriage return. The grasps fold flat for the pocket.
