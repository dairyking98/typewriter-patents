## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special supports, platforms or trolleys for supporting machines on tables

**How it works (reading):** An air-filled hollow rubber ring foot with a non-elastic ring cap absorbs the excess blow and side vibration.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter key blows bounce the machine and vibrate it on the table, slowing work.

**Solution:** An air-filled hollow rubber ring foot with a non-elastic ring cap absorbs the excess blow and side vibration.
