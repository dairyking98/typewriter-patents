## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to carriage escapement mechanism for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** A simple, rapid escapement with dogs and a wheel and cushioning means that absorb the shock of the carriage let-off, and it can be arranged for ordinary or reverse feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The escapement gives shock and noise when the carriage lets off or drops, and the feed should be reversible.

**Solution:** A simple, rapid escapement with dogs and a wheel and cushioning means that absorb the shock of the carriage let-off, and it can be arranged for ordinary or reverse feed.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; dogs; cushioning means; carriage
