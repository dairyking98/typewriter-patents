## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Control of machine parts from adder disks is complicated and can be unreliable.

**Solution:** The contact member set against zero by an adder disk closes a circuit with a mating contact that rotates with the summing mechanism, which triggers printing of the total.

**How it works:** A commutator with a fixed brush and a rotating segment disc (or the reverse) closes the circuit at the moment the adder disk value is reached.
