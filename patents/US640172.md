## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typebar machine with upward-reaching bars striking down onto the paper, with improved efficiency.

**Solution:** Typebars pivoted on a wire through a slotted segment, struck down to a common point, with felt buffer, key bars, and ribbon spools moved by a slide.

**How it works:** A frame carries a slotted segment with a wire fulcrum through holes in each type-bar. Bars have an angled upper part carrying two or three type that strike a common point on the platen. Key bars with bevelled notches engage bevelled pins as fulcra. Springs return the bars against a felt-lined curved buffer bar. Ribbon spools are moved by a slide with dogs, and a spacer dog gives carriage stop. Carriage shifts forward for the other type on the bar.
