## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage frames of a typewriter are not easily removed from the machine, and the platen supports must be detached and reattached as a unit.

**Solution:** Platen-carrying side plates attach to a carriage-guide support by a simple locking operation, so the platen frames can be removed independently or fixed back on without disturbing the mechanism.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)
