## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjacent type bars clash when frequently combined letters are struck in quick succession because of their order on the basket.

**Solution:** The types are re-ordered in the type basket so that frequently consecutive letters do not sit next to each other and clash.
