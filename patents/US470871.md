## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Officials needed to exchange cipher writing that only a holder of the key and machine could read.

**Solution:** A pocket cipher typewriter with a hand-turned type disc inside a lettered ring that prints on a narrow paper ribbon.

**How it works:** A lettered ring on pillars sits above the base. A disc with a type ring is turned by a hand stem; pressing the stem down axially engages and prints through an inked ribbon on rollers. An arm with a small roller rides in internal teeth of the disc and is pushed out when the stem is pressed, locking the disc during impression. Paper ribbon is fed and held by a base mechanism. A cipher mechanism changes the printed letter. A variant has a double range of types.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Dial or knob](../features/sel-dial.md), [Press the pointer to print](../features/sel-pointer-press-prints.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Type element:** [Type ring](../features/elem-ring.md), [Type disc (types on the face)](../features/elem-disc-face.md), [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** base; pillar; lettered ring; type disc; type ring; hand stem; locking arm; teeth; inking ribbon roller; paper ribbon; enclosing case

**Numbers:** about 3.5 in diameter; about 1.5 in height
