## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**How it works (reading):** A threaded bolt driven by friction from the platen moves an adjustable stop along its thread until it meets a fixed stop, locking the platen or giving a signal; the whole device swings off the platen to be disabled.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing devices that stop the writing limit at the lower sheet edge need gears or worms on the platen axis, which is special construction and gives limited setting.

**Solution:** A threaded bolt driven by friction from the platen moves an adjustable stop along its thread until it meets a fixed stop, locking the platen or giving a signal; the whole device swings off the platen to be disabled.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** threaded bolt; movable stop with spring return; fixed adjustable nut stop; rubber ring friction drive
