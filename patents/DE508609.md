## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (writing unit for tabulating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for producing a permanent visual presentation of the output data, e.g. computer output printers using printers by rack-type printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to writing • -verke with several simultaneously acting Writing elements, such as those used on tabulating machines, calculating machines and the like find, and concerns their design in such a way that the written letters or numerals divided into columns by a line drawing corresponding to their character ' appear.

**How it works (reading):** Extra hammers placed between type carriers strike dotted lines on the paper between digits, with a rail holding them until release, drawing column rules.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column division by moving numerals apart wastes writing positions, so the figures cannot be separated by spacing alone.

**Solution:** Extra hammers placed between type carriers strike dotted lines on the paper between digits, with a rail holding them until release, drawing column rules.
