## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (telegraph receiver with punching) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a telegraph receiving apparatus for optional or simultaneous Printing and punching, in which the attachment or Intermediate pieces are brought into the path of the punch depending on the combination of characters.

**How it works (reading):** Attachments on the selector rails are brought into the punch path by the character combination and directly actuate a shared punching hammer, with no separate eccentric control.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punching a tape from a teleprinter with eccentric-driven intermediate parts is unsafe and needs frequent maintenance, because punching force disturbs the receiving mechanism.

**Solution:** Attachments on the selector rails are brought into the punch path by the character combination and directly actuate a shared punching hammer, with no separate eccentric control.
