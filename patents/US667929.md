## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A cheap, compact typebar machine is wanted, in which the typebars sit in a nearly horizontal fan and so need different counterbalancing depending on their angle.

**Solution:** Counterbalance and return springs for each nearly horizontal, radially fanned typebar are anchored to a curved rod of larger radius, so the spring effect falls off from the centre bars to the outer ones.

**How it works:** Three banks of bell-crank key levers pull rods to short arms of typebars pivoted on a curved rod around the print point, guided in radial combs. Each typebar has a counterbalance spring whose line of pull changes with its position in the fan. Any typebar strikes a universal bar, which steps the carriage through a spring-and-star-wheel escapement, and the space key works the same bar. A felt ink pad on a flexible arm is flipped by each type. Shifting the carriage frame brings one of three type series to the print point, and a bell and spring drum are carried on that frame.
