## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a machine of this type in which the special switching bridge a counter-stop is provided for each stop, which has the purpose of Pivoting the switching bridge over the intended distance in each case impede.

**How it works (reading):** Each key lever gets several stops and counter-stops on the switching bridge, and the bridge is turned about its axis to bring the set needed for that character into use, so each letter switches the carriage by its own width.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The switching bridge gives every key lever the same carriage travel, so capitals and lowercase of letterpress-like wide type cannot have different spacing.

**Solution:** Each key lever gets several stops and counter-stops on the switching bridge, and the bridge is turned about its axis to bring the set needed for that character into use, so each letter switches the carriage by its own width.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** special switching bridge; stops and counter-stops per key lever; intermediate links; paper carriage switching device
