## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Erasing or correcting tables

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an eraser stencil for typewriters and consists in the fact that a per se known shovel-shaped formed template made of flexible material (celluloid or the like) at two right angles bent arms of a bracket is attached, the legs of which are designed to be resilient are that the stencil pressed against the platen by means of the bracket itself clings to the roundness of the roll.

**How it works (reading):** The shovel-shaped celluloid eraser stencil is clamped taut in the resilient legs of a bent bracket, so the stencil seals against the platen and fits around the roller, while bends catch the dust.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Eraser dust from an eraser stencil gets between the stencil and the platen roller.

**Solution:** The shovel-shaped celluloid eraser stencil is clamped taut in the resilient legs of a bent bracket, so the stencil seals against the platen and fits around the roller, while bends catch the dust.
