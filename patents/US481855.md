## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, cheap type-writer is wanted, with a reliable word-spacing feed and a line-spacing attachment.

**Solution:** A rotatable printing disk is turned by an index wheel with spring fingers bearing the characters, a printing key presses the selected type down, and the same key stroke feeds the carriage via a spacing frame.

**How it works:** A printing disk with characters on its underside and an index wheel with radial spring fingers carrying the same characters are fixed on one sleeve and pivot together in a case on the carriage. The operator turns the wheel with a finger on a projection until the finger with the wanted character aligns with a notch in the case. The printing key, on a wire impression frame beneath the wheel, is then pressed so the type-impressing device strikes the paper. A pivoted spacing frame under the key carries a feed lever whose stud rides a ledge on the carriage; depressing the key moves the lever and feeds the carriage along a rack on the way plate. A separate line-spacing attachment is also described.

**Features:** [SEL:index-wheel](../features/sel-index-wheel.md), [SEL:pointer-hole](../features/sel-pointer-hole.md), [ELEM:disc-face](../features/elem-disc-face.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** table; way plate; rack flange; carriage; case; printing disk; index wheel; spring finger; notch; impression frame; printing key; spacing frame; feed lever; ledge
