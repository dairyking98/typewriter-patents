## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Decimal tabulating counter-stops are limited, and column selection needs separate keys in addition to the decimal keys.

**Solution:** Column-selecting keys added beside decimal keys; the tabulating keys release the carriage from the escapement so it stops at selected column stops.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
