## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**How it works (reading):** After each enlarged word space is made, recompute the quotient of remaining missing space over remaining spaces so the leftover is fully absorbed with near-equal spaces.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With a toothed-lock feed, justifying lines by spreading the missing space evenly over word spaces is only approximate and the last line length errs.

**Solution:** After each enlarged word space is made, recompute the quotient of remaining missing space over remaining spaces so the leftover is fully absorbed with near-equal spaces.

**How it works:** Missing space and number of spaces are set at the start of the line. Each space is enlarged by the nearest multiple of the feed tooth pitch to the current quotient. After each space the setting is recalculated for the remaining deficit and spaces, manually or by automatic means shown in the drawings. The last space takes whatever remains.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** feed lock; toothed pitch; spacing setting device

**Numbers:** example: error 17 feed units over 5 spaces, 3 or 4 units each
