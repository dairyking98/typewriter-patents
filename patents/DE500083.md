## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** A detachable spring-held fixed stop on the paper carriage cooperates with a stop on the adjustment slide, giving a middle setting between the notches.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-width slide snaps only at fixed notches, so the middle setting for one and a half line widths is skipped.

**Solution:** A detachable spring-held fixed stop on the paper carriage cooperates with a stop on the adjustment slide, giving a middle setting between the notches.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
