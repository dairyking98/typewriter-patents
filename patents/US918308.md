## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Symbols such as fractions or chemical formulae must print raised or lowered relative to the line.

**Solution:** A toothed disk and frame arm on the paper roller shift the roller up or down, so characters print above or below the line on a Hammond-type machine.
