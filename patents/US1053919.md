## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot tell how near the bottom of the sheet is, and the bottom can pass out of sight behind the platen.

**Solution:** A sheet-controlled paper feeler indicates line by line the decreasing space left on the sheet, and gives a warning signal near the sheet end.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
