## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch with inking) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide an improved check punch adapted to punch holes in checks indicating the amount for which the check is drawn while at the same time impressing ink upon the check at each punched ligure or symbol as a further protection against unauthorized alteration of the check.

**How it works (reading):** A rotating disk carrying punches and figure symbols is turned by a handle, indexed by a spring-seated pin, and the selected punch perforates the check while ink is impressed at each punched figure.
