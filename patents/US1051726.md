## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding loose sheets needs adjustment for each paper size, weight and thickness.

**Solution:** Sheets stand in a vertical block and are fed from the bottom by a feed roll, so gravity holds the paper in feeding position and it works without adjustment on various sheet lengths (for a rotary duplicating machine).

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
