## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Variable line spacing is limited to the few settings of the ordinary line-space ratchet.

**Solution:** Fix several ratchet wheels with differently disposed teeth on the platen, and slide the platen axially to bring the wanted one under the operating pawl.

**How it works:** One end of the platen has a series of ratchet wheels with teeth of different pitch (or same teeth on wheels of different diameter). The platen shaft slides lengthwise in the carriage, a pivoted clip detent drops into one of several grooves on the shaft to hold it in the chosen position while the platen can still turn. The hand lever's pawl engages whichever ratchet is aligned, giving a different angular line feed. The scheme multiplies the settings of existing variable line spacing.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** platen; carriage frame; ratchet wheel; operating pawl; hand lever; groove; clip detent; holding piece; platen shaft

**Improves on:**

- existing variable line-spacing devices: multiplies settings by number of ratchets
