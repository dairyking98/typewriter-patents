## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil machine dial plate) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of my invention being to provide a fixed dial plate on which is arranged a plurality of arcuate rows of characters, and to arrange a pair of pointers on the rotatable punch carrier in such a manner that one of said pointers is always clear when the opposite pointer is moved to indicate one of the characters of the corresponding arcuate row.

**How it works (reading):** Arcuate rows of characters on a fixed dial are read by pointers on a rotating punch carrier, and a toggle joint between the handle and punch lever gives the punches high pressure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Stencil machines lack a clear dial reading and enough cutting force.

**Solution:** Arcuate rows of characters on a fixed dial are read by pointers on a rotating punch carrier, and a toggle joint between the handle and punch lever gives the punches high pressure.
