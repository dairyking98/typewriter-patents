## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Each type-bar action has a key lever, a separate intermediate lever pivoted at its lower end and linked to the type-bar heel, and an equalizing draw-bar joining the intermediate lever to the key lever; the intermediate levers' upper ends lie on an arc concentric with the typebar arc.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type-bar action should be simple, quick and easy, with a comparatively light key stroke.

**Solution:** Each type-bar action has a key lever, a separate intermediate lever pivoted at its lower end and linked to the type-bar heel, and an equalizing draw-bar joining the intermediate lever to the key lever; the intermediate levers' upper ends lie on an arc concentric with the typebar arc.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** intermediate levers; equalizing draw-bar; key levers
