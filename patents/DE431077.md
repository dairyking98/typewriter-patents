**How it works (reading):** A continuously rotated gear member is coupled by an electromagnetic or toothed clutch with a built-in buffer spring, engaged and disengaged automatically by the left or right margin stop, so the carriage lift and the line feed run smoothly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A spring between driving and driven links for the carriage lift is unsuitable because it has long spring travel and fluctuates, which endangers reliable operation.

**Solution:** A continuously rotated gear member is coupled by an electromagnetic or toothed clutch with a built-in buffer spring, engaged and disengaged automatically by the left or right margin stop, so the carriage lift and the line feed run smoothly.
