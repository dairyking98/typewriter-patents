## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewritin machines and more particularly to tabulating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Clearing mechanism for tabulator column stops, with a hand-actuated release, that moves the operative stops to inoperative position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing work needs different column stops, and the stops already set must be cleared, which is slow.

**Solution:** Clearing mechanism for tabulator column stops, with a hand-actuated release, that moves the operative stops to inoperative position.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
