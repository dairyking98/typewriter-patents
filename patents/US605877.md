## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators must watch the paper to avoid typing off the sheet end.

**Solution:** A carriage attachment counting line spaces on a ratchet wheel and sounding a gong when the preset sheet length is reached.

**How it works:** A dog riding on the line-spacing pawl advances a ratchet wheel (one tooth per line space) mounted on a standard clamped to the carriage end. A detent pawl holds it, with a recoil spring. A pin on the wheel lifts a gong striker, and another pin lifts a toothed trip to release the detent so the wheel recoils and the striker rings the gong. An adjustable stop sets the sheet length. A spring stops the striker acting when the carriage is turned back.

**Features:** [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** line-space pawl; dog; ratchet wheel; standard; clamp; recoil spring; detent pawl; trip; striker; gong; adjustable stop

**Improves on:**
