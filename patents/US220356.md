## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter operation by hand is slow; wants electric power.

**Solution:** Electromagnets drive each letter lever, selected by touching a stylus to lettered insulated plates, with escapement magnets for ribbon and paper.

**How it works:** Letter levers around a center are pulled down by springs and thrown up by rods and armatures of an electromagnet each. The operator touches an insulated stylus to a plate on a tablet, closing the circuit. Further magnets and an escapement feed the ribbon and paper, and separate plates make word spaces.
