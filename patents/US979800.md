## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting mato means for enabling the platen tobe readily attached to and detached from the platen frame or carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Each end bar of the platen frame has spring-pressed catches pivoted in slots to receive the platen shaft in open-mouthed bearings, so the platen can be removed and replaced quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be readily attached to and detached from the platen frame or carriage.

**Solution:** Each end bar of the platen frame has spring-pressed catches pivoted in slots to receive the platen shaft in open-mouthed bearings, so the platen can be removed and replaced quickly.
