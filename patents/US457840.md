## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wanted a simple typewriter with one type bar carrying all the characters.

**Solution:** A single type bar is pivoted in a frame and moved along a letter plate, guided by a comb of recesses, then pressed down to print.

**How it works:** The platen is a rod with a rubber cover on a carriage. A letter plate holds the alphabet, numerals and signs in a row and two lines. A comb with a recess before each letter receives a stylus on the type bar, which positions the bar over the platen. The type-bar frame is pivoted and has a channeled bar S with limited vertical swing; pushing it down strikes the type, and a stud on it kicks a pawl that engages the next tooth of a rack so a retrieving spring spaces the carriage back one step. A bell mechanism, a removable character plate with comb, and a perforating-type bar are variants.
