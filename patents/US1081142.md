## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-levers were costly and wasteful to make, and needed extended bearing surfaces to prevent sideways motion.

**Solution:** Each key-lever is stamped as a single sheet-metal piece with an extended bearing, supported in groups on transverse rods with integral spacers, and yieldingly forced in one direction to connect to the type-bars.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
