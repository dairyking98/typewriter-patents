## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cheque stamping machines

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to printing-machines of the type in which any of a series of characters or words may be impressed selectively upon a check orother article.

**How it works (reading):** Two manually operable members, grasped together, rock to select a character and are squeezed together to print it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check printing machines need a single hand to select characters and then impress them, but the operation is awkward.

**Solution:** Two manually operable members, grasped together, rock to select a character and are squeezed together to print it.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
- **Uses:** [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md)
