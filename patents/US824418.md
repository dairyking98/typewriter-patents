## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a typebar machine with a hinged, shifting platen carriage, the operator lifting the carriage to see the line has no fixed mark showing the printing point and line in both upper and lower case, and an indicator resting on the platen would smear the work.

**Solution:** A pivoted pointer on the frame is raised by a link to the platen carriage and is snapped against the platen only at the end of the lift, by a double-acting over-centre spring.

**How it works:** The pointer is hinged to a bracket on the fixed rear frame and joined by a loose link to a light rod running along the rear carriage bar. As the hinged platen carrier is swung up, the link lifts the pointer. A spring pivoted slightly behind the pointer hinge presses the pointer away from the platen until its pivots line up, then flips over centre and presses it close to the platen at the top of the lift. The pointer's cross-piece carries a notch and edge for lower-case point and line, a mark and line for upper-case, and a pointer for the platen scale. Because the rod rides on the shifting carriage, the pointer ends at different heights for the two cases. Lowering the carrier reverses the action and the spring pushes the pointer clear again.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** pointer; bracket; link; light rod; double-acting spring; hinged platen carrier; platen; platen scale; lift piece; typebar; shift rail

**Improves on:**
