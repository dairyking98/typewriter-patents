## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Type lever drive device for typewriters is the subject of Invention is a handle drive device for typewriters. . It is known in typewriters to cause the type lever to stop by spring force and to move the type lever back into its rest position by means of a suitable power drive. It is also known to hold it in the rest position against the tension of the springs by means of a ratchet lock which is released by pressing down the button lever. The pawl lock has also been provided in the form of two pawls. The invention consists in relation to the known in that the pawls, «-Whose hold the type lever, carried by the button lever« -ground and with Approaches to the type levers work together. This has the advantage of that the type lever can be arranged directly above the key levers, whereby the overall height of the machine can be kept lower than with the known valve lever drives of the same kind. A double arrangement of the pawls, known per se, is provided, to usually get the type lever through one pawl and it through the other hold on until the key lever is in its rest position after a stop has returned.
