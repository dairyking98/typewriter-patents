## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper-feeding devices of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Each pressure roll is loose on its shaft and is frictionally driven by the platen, so it turns with the platen independently of the others and avoids skew.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Positively driven pressure rolls on a platen skew the sheets because the platen and rolls are uneven.

**Solution:** Each pressure roll is loose on its shaft and is frictionally driven by the platen, so it turns with the platen independently of the others and avoids skew.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; pressure rolls; loosely mounted shafts; pinions
