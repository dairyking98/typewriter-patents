## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a universal rail for typewriters, which is known in Way to switch the ribbon in its longitudinal direction and to move it transversely and is used for the step-by-step movement of the paper carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Split the universal rail into two relatively adjustable parts so the ribbon-moving part can be switched out of reach of the key transmission levers.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On typebar machines the universal rail always drives both ribbon feed and carriage stepping, so ribbon movement cannot be turned off, which is needed when typing on waxed stencil paper.

**Solution:** Split the universal rail into two relatively adjustable parts so the ribbon-moving part can be switched out of reach of the key transmission levers.

**How it works:** Key levers swing transmission levers that throw the typebars to the platen. Below them a universal rail is hit by the levers on every stroke; the upper part feeds the ribbon lengthways and moves the ribbon fork, while the lower part, hung from it on swing arms, steps the carriage ratchet. A handle on an axle with an arm and rod pulls the upper part toward the keys so the levers no longer hit it, which stops ribbon feed and lift, and a leaf spring holds the two end positions. A cam on the same arm presses the ribbon key lever, and a gear with two segment levers changes which half of the double ribbon is brought to the writing line.
