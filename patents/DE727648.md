## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (accounting typewriter with totalizer)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an accounting machine, in particular a typewriter, with automatic, decimal place working sum train.

**How it works (reading):** A booking typewriter with an automatic decimal-place sum train, plus an attachable multiplying device whose factor presets are engaged by the normal add/subtract hand controls, with the usual sum-train extinguishing path limited.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multiplication on typewriters was done with contact-and-magnet multipliers that often failed, or with mechanical multipliers needing intricate factor settings.

**Solution:** A booking typewriter with an automatic decimal-place sum train, plus an attachable multiplying device whose factor presets are engaged by the normal add/subtract hand controls, with the usual sum-train extinguishing path limited.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
