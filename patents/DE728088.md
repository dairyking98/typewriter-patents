## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (pipe soldering method) · **Subject (patent classes):** Soldering, e.g. brazing, or unsoldering specially adapted for soldering seams circumferential seams, e.g. of shells

**How it works (reading):** The joint surfaces are plated with a non-tin metal such as cadmium, which alloys with the solder, before soldering.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tinned coatings on cast-iron pipe joints peel off during transport and storage, so soft soldering fails.

**Solution:** The joint surfaces are plated with a non-tin metal such as cadmium, which alloys with the solder, before soldering.
