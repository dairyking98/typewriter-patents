## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to trunnion bearings for rotating hoods, especially those on tables for writing and other office buildings chinen. In the known designs of typewriter tables with rotating hoods, the latter was stored either in gelerics or in pivot pins, which were usually accessible from the outside and thus enabled unauthorized opening of the hood. The gap required for lowering the hood in the table top was kept relatively wide in order to simplify the Z 1 U assembly. But that could easily cause the inside of the hood to get dusty. The invention consists in bearing brackets which are to be fastened with their horizontal legs on the table top and whose vertical legs have open slots into which the hood provided with pivot pins on its side walls can be inserted from above and locked by washers provided with an open slot, which are secured against unintentional lifting out by the side walls of the overlying fixed hood covering the pivot bearing. As a result, the pivot pin of the hood is installed completely covered so that it is neither visible nor accessible from the outside, which prevents the hood from being opened by unauthorized parties.
