## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Worn type bars are hard to renew in an Underwood-style segment.

**Solution:** Radial slots in a segment with enlarged round grooves receive shoes that carry each type bar, so bars slide in and out for replacement.
