## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting the calculating unit of an adding typewriter for totals, negative values and non-addition required separate complicated devices.

**Solution:** The ribbon-change key, which doubles as total key, also sets the calculating unit to the opposite mode via a wedge and slides, while locking the other keys.

**How it works:** Pressing the total key swings a bridge and pulls a rail down, which through levers lifts a wedge-shaped part through the calculator base plate. The wedge moves slides that reverse the drive of the vertical and horizontal counters, giving subtraction-type total writing. The same key's rear arm turns the ribbon-changing cross so the red section of the ribbon is used, and a link latches the mechanism until the cross is moved back. The link also shows the setting outside the housing, blocks the non-add key and locks the second (overdrawn) key. The non-add key separates calculating from writing parts.
