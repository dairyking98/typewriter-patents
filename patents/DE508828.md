> Description Translated from German Elastic notor drive device for the arithmetic unit and the digit type lever a calculating typewriter The present invention is an improvement on elastic Motor drive devices for the arithmetic unit and the numeral type lever of a typewriter according to patent 467,438. The invention aims to increase the operational safety of these Drive device. The spring 8 mentioned in the cited patent, which the lower number key lever and the sector-shaped pressure piece after each actuation has to lift and keep in the elevated position is through the uninterrupted Utilizing early limp, thereby reducing security in the Operation of the drive device. In order to increase operational safety instead of the sector-shaped pressure piece in the present subject matter of the invention a pressure roller is used, the axis of which is completely on the slot bearing of the the lower type lever is supported and at the same time is guided horizontally in the same. The aforementioned spring now only has to lift the lower number key lever and to keep him in an elevated position. For this reason the spring is directly on the numeric keypad lever appropriate.
