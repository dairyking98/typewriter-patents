## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A spring must lift the lower number key lever after each stroke, and the draft of the drawstring is uncontrolled, which lowers operating reliability.

**Solution:** A pressure roller on the lever's pin replaces the sector-shaped pressure piece, and the drawstring has a length and a slot that let it release if caught.
