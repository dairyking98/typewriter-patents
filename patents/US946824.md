## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Presses that stamp raised type on printing plates need puncheon and matrix aligned exactly, which makes them costly.

**Solution:** Press the plate material into an incised matrix with a flat counter-stamp that is the same for all characters, so the two need not register exactly.

**How it works:** A hand-lever lifts a stamp holder carrying a flat stamp; it carries a table and plate support up until the plate meets a matrix bar of incised characters. Further pressure forces the plate material (e.g. vulcan fibre) into the incised type, making raised characters. On release a ratchet finger pushes the plate along a notched matrix bar by one character space, giving an equal distance between all characters.
