## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to paper-controlling devices of typewriting machines, and particularly to those of the front strike variety.

**How it works (reading):** A tablet standing on edge above the platen carries a top stop, a directrix and a left-edge gage, and the stop is tied to the pressure-roll release key.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets cannot readily be squared endwise and sidewise to the printing point before writing while clamped by the platen rolls.

**Solution:** A tablet standing on edge above the platen carries a top stop, a directrix and a left-edge gage, and the stop is tied to the pressure-roll release key.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
