## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**How it works (reading):** An indicating member driven from the platen by the line space lever shows sheet position, with detent means giving the member its starting position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot readily tell the position of the advancing sheet relative to the first printed line.

**Solution:** An indicating member driven from the platen by the line space lever shows sheet position, with detent means giving the member its starting position.
