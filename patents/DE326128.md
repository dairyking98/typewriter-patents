## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (railway buffer) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers; Buffers with metal springs with helical springs arranged to damp each other by mutual friction

**How it works (reading):** Hooks are punched and bent from the outer and inner tubes, so the inner tube is made by itself and the outer tube is pushed on before hooks are bent.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hook-shaped stroke limiters in the buffer housing are hard to make and assemble.

**Solution:** Hooks are punched and bent from the outer and inner tubes, so the inner tube is made by itself and the outer tube is pushed on before hooks are bent.
