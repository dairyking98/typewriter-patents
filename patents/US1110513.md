## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage and line spacing needs manual effort.

**Solution:** A key-controlled valve admits compressed air from a reservoir to a cylinder whose piston returns the carriage and trips the line-space lever.

**How it works:** A piston and rod connect to the carriage in a cylinder coaxial with the platen. A reservoir fed by a hand or foot pump supplies air through a pipe and a spring-returned valve, opened by a key lever on the keyboard. Air pushes the piston, returning the carriage against its spring, and a vent port releases the pressure at the end of travel. An arm in the path of the line-shifting lever line-spaces the platen as the carriage arrives.
