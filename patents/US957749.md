## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be turned freely for fractional or ruled-line adjustments while it is locked to the line-space wheel.

**Solution:** A cam in a second bore locks the line-space wheel to a collar on the platen axle; turning the cam releases the platen so it can be turned independently and then relocked.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen axle; collar; line-space wheel; cam with handle; keeper
