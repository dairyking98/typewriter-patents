## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon lifts on shifting-carriage typewriters are mounted on the carriage and lift the ribbon fork with the shift, which blocks the view when writing on wax stencils.

**Solution:** Mount the ribbon lift on the fixed frame and derive the shift-related lift from the shift element's roller so the ribbon fork stays in its base position in the wax setting.

**How it works:** A link driven by the key lever bridge has a slot in which an adjustable link pin slides, so a larger or smaller swing results depending on the ribbon-zone setting. A transmission member with an angled slot is articulated to the link and, by a second pin, to a crank arm fixed on a longitudinal axis that carries the ribbon fork lifter. Pressing a write key moves the link and lifts the fork through the crank arm. Holding a shift key raises a switching arm by a roller, and a driver pin sliding in the angled slot rotates the transmission member about the link pin, lifting the fork to the shift position without moving the link. Both movements can combine when a key is struck during shift.
