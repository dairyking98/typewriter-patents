## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Commercial typewriters are too large to carry; a pocket machine with the essential features is wanted.

**Solution:** A cup-shaped head with elbow typebars carrying three-faced rotating type-heads, all turned together to the required case by a ring with finger-piece.

**How it works:** The frame is a separable cup-shaped head and an annular body. Elbow-shaped flat-wire typebars pivot from lugs under the cap plate, each with resilient actuator arms that pull it to rest. Keys press the actuators to swing the bar. Each bar carries a triangular type-head with three faces on a shaft with a pinion on top. A ring with ribbed cogs meshes with all the pinions; moving a finger-piece along a notched slot turns the ring and all type-heads to a new face. A pad inks the type.
