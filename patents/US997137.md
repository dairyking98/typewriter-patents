## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to carriage feed mechanisms for typewriting machines, that is to the feed or escapement mechanism employed for producing the stepby-step or letter space movements of the carriage.
