## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage feed should step a full letter space right after the typebar impact, at speed, with almost no key power and without a noisy ratchet for carriage set-back.

**Solution:** A set of small independent actuators between the typebars and the escapement, each hit by typebars, trip the carriage escapement and return by their own springs.

**How it works:** Light actuators sit behind the typebar segment on a single bracket with their springs and universal bar. A typebar striking the platen impacts its actuator, which moves the universal bar and trips the escapement so the carriage feeds a full space only after impact. The actuators spring back independently, helping push the typebars off the platen before the carriage moves, and act as dust guards for the typebar bearings.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)
