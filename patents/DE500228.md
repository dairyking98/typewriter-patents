## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (display and print wheels cash register)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to devices for setting display and print wheels on cash registers, which during the idle state of the machine maintain their position resulting from the previous machine cycle.

**How it works (reading):** The drive resets the wheel to character A at the start of each cycle, and it is changed to B only when the corresponding special key is pressed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash register display and print wheels with two characters must hold their position when idle, and the existing connections are awkward.

**Solution:** The drive resets the wheel to character A at the start of each cycle, and it is changed to B only when the corresponding special key is pressed.

**Features:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
