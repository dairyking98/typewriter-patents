## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** With a front-stroke machine and a lifting platen, two shift keys, one per side, must lift the platen evenly and independently of each other.

**Solution:** The platen frame rests on a roller carried in forked levers, one per shift key, whose fork arms interleave so either key can lift it alone.

**How it works:** The platen sits in slide bearings joined into a frame, guided in carriage slots. A pressure roller bears against the frame's connecting rod and sits in open forks of two levers whose arms intermesh. Pull rods link each lever to a shift key on its side; pressing either lifts the roller and the frame. A rotatable frame with forked arms engaging pins on the bearings keeps the platen level. Tension springs take most of the frame's weight so the key force is small.
