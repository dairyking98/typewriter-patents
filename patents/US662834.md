## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special purpose keyboards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to touch type-writing, the object of the same being to provide certain improvements in type-writing machines whereby the different letters or characters on the keyboard may be distinguished and selected by the touch rather than by the sight of the operator.

**How it works (reading):** Key-tops are blank but split into sets with distinct textures and colours that the fingers can feel, with a letter plate as a learning aid.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Learning touch typing is hard because all the blank key-tops look alike and the operator must rely on sight to find characters.

**Solution:** Key-tops are blank but split into sets with distinct textures and colours that the fingers can feel, with a letter plate as a learning aid.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Interchangeable chart](../features/sel-chart-interchangeable.md)
