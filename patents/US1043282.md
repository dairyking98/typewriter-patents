## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Crumb trays

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in instruments for collecting the debris resulting from an erasure effected in a typewriting machine.

**How it works (reading):** A container with a shield or guard is applied to the platen at the erasure point to contain debris, and a brush moves within the shielded area.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Eraser debris falls into the typewriter and abrades the type-bar pivots and bearings.

**Solution:** A container with a shield or guard is applied to the platen at the erasure point to contain debris, and a brush moves within the shielded area.
