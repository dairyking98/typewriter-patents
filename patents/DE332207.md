## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**How it works (reading):** The contact plate is replaced by a plain adjustment plate without current flow, and the contact closure is moved to another place on the setting pin, which is split into an insulated upper part and a conductive lower part that slides on the letter field.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The electrical type stop needs a contact plate in the circuit, and the contact closure happens at the tip of the adjustment pin.

**Solution:** The contact plate is replaced by a plain adjustment plate without current flow, and the contact closure is moved to another place on the setting pin, which is split into an insulated upper part and a conductive lower part that slides on the letter field.
