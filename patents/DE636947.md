> Description Translated from German Paper guiding and holding device on typewriters o. The like. The invention relates to a device on writing office machines with on the platen support frame pivotably mounted paper holding means, which after from an inoperative, virksamen situation The writing sheets were pivoted in the direction of the platen keep writing. There are devices of this type known in which independently on both sides of the platen there are narrow paper guide plates with pressure rollers were intended to hold the writing sheets individually in the direction of the Platen were pivoted and in the pivoted down position under effect a spring pressed the writing sheets against the platen. The contact pressure could here .be changed by a device for readjusting the spring. This readjustment must be made separately for each paper guide plate had to, but was cumbersome and made a quick adjustment of the holding means difficult because of the different kinds of sheets to be written on.
