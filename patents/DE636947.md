**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on writing office machines with on the platen support frame pivotably mounted paper holding means, which after from an inoperative, virksamen situation The writing sheets were pivoted in the direction of the platen keep writing.

**How it works (reading):** A single paper holding rail spanning the platen is pivoted on the support frame with a stop, and a handle switches it between a hold-off position and a position pressed against the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Narrow paper guide plates with pressure rollers leave imprints and need separate spring adjustment, and rigid cards cannot be written to the lower edge.

**Solution:** A single paper holding rail spanning the platen is pivoted on the support frame with a stop, and a handle switches it between a hold-off position and a position pressed against the platen.
