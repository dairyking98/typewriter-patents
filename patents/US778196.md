## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (detachable keybank for punching machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition; Switches having rectilinearly-movable operating part or parts adapted for pushing or pulling in one direction only, e.g. push-button switch having a plurality of operating members associated with different sets of contacts, e.g. keyboard with contacts carried by or formed from layers in a multilayer structure, e.g. membrane switches

**How it works (reading):** The key-bank is made as a separate structure with symbol buttons that register with the actuators, so a whole bank with a different symbol layout can be swapped in.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Redistributing the symbols on a keyboard means removing and reattaching key buttons, which wears the joints and loosens them.

**Solution:** The key-bank is made as a separate structure with symbol buttons that register with the actuators, so a whole bank with a different symbol layout can be swapped in.

**Features:** [Interchangeable chart](../features/sel-chart-interchangeable.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
