## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable head and sliding collar on the platen line-spacing ratchet let the platen turn through large distances and advance automatically for blank lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen line spacing cannot easily make fractional or long platen turns for condensed billing, and blank lines between carbon entries need manual work.

**Solution:** A rotatable head and sliding collar on the platen line-spacing ratchet let the platen turn through large distances and advance automatically for blank lines.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
