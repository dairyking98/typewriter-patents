## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** When a driven member is brought into engagement with a driving member, its full inertia must be overcome at the moment of contact, causing a shock.

**Solution:** A starter on the movable member and a resistant starter engage gradually, with the coupling and uncoupling made at fixed points in the orbital movement, so the load is taken up in stages.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
