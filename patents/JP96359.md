**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a typewriter of the kind in which many types are arranged in a cylinder, the wanted type is driven out radially by the action of a pusher bar, and a printing arm that grips it and swings strikes it against a paper roller set above.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a Japanese typewriter whose many loose types sit in a cylindrical magazine, the plates that retain the types and open a path for the chosen one were narrow, rubbed against one another and wore the characters printed on their backs.

**Solution:** Each retaining plate rides in its own spiral slot in the magazine flanges, overlapping like armour scales, and a key-driven pin on a turning disc pushes aside only the plate over the wanted type.

**How it works:** The operator turns and slides the cylindrical magazine by a knob until the character on the outside of a retaining plate shows in an inspection window. Pressing the key turns a main shaft; through links this turns discs at both ends of the magazine, whose pins ride a fixed cam and push the one plate over the wanted generating line along its spiral slot, opening a path. Next a pusher bar, cammed from the same linkage, drives the type radially out of the magazine into a trough on a printing arm. A cam plate then makes a picking arm grip the type against the trough and locks both arms, which swing and strike the type against a paper roller above. On release the arms return, the type drops free, and the plate springs closed again after the type has gone back. A rack arm engages during printing to stop the magazine moving.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a typewriter of the kind in which many types are arranged in a cylinder, the wanted type is driven out radially by the action of a pusher bar, and a printing arm that grips it and swings strikes it against a paper roller set above. Flanges project from both sides of the cylindrical type magazine; spiral guide slots are formed in the flanges, overlapping one another in sections; in each slot is loosely fitted a search-and-type-retaining plate somewhat narrower than the slot, so that the plates always enclose the magazine like the scales of armour (鎧袖狀).
