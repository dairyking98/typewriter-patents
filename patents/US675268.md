## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**How it works (reading):** A card-carrier supported on a part of the machine and inserted or withdrawn freely, with adjusting screws at its ends that move the card-holder vertically so the printed line lands at the desired point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A card-holder on earlier designs is hard to fit and remove, and its vertical position is set by threaded rods and nuts.

**Solution:** A card-carrier supported on a part of the machine and inserted or withdrawn freely, with adjusting screws at its ends that move the card-holder vertically so the printed line lands at the desired point.
