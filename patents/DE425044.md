## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for stepwise Moving the paper carriage back of typewriters and relates in particular on typewriters in which a manually operated lever acts on a rack and thereby displaced it in the return direction after engaging it brought with a tooth rigidly attached to the paper slide.

**How it works (reading):** The rack bar is carried by two arms on a rotatable handle bar, and a freely swinging angle lever pushes the rack against a fixed machine stop so that further rotation of the bar moves the rack and returns the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known backward-return devices engage the rack late after wear and need a temporary link to be inserted and removed to hold the carriage in place.

**Solution:** The rack bar is carried by two arms on a rotatable handle bar, and a freely swinging angle lever pushes the rack against a fixed machine stop so that further rotation of the bar moves the rack and returns the carriage.
