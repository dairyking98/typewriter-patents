## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to keyboard machines and instruments, such as type-writing machines, cash-registers, pianos, organs, and other machines and instruments of this class;

**How it works (reading):** A device that depresses a key or keys automatically to the full depth after the operator has started the stroke by hand. The OCR is too noisy to give the mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Hand key-depression is laborious, so the operator's effort should be lessened.

**Solution:** A device that depresses a key or keys automatically to the full depth after the operator has started the stroke by hand. The OCR is too noisy to give the mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
