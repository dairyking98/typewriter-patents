## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** A carriage-mounted casing with a hinged cover holds a roll holder; a chute guides the material to the platen and a blade cuts it.
