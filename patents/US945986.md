## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon paper or material must be guided from a roll to the platen and cut from the roll.

**Solution:** A carriage-mounted casing with a hinged cover holds a roll holder; a chute guides the material to the platen and a blade cuts it.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
