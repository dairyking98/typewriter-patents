## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with several transverse summing units, in which for each transverse summing unit a special drive wheel is provided which has an intermediate link are driven by a reversing gear common to all drive wheels, according to Patent 5oa 8gf.

**How it works (reading):** The intermediate member between the common reversing gear and the cross-counter drive wheels is itself a reversing gear: a reversing coupling wheel in the coupling position reverses one drive wheel, so the counters can all add, all subtract, or mix, by reversing the one main gear.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Each cross counter on a calculating typewriter needed its own reversing gear, which had to be controlled separately, and the joint combinations were costly.

**Solution:** The intermediate member between the common reversing gear and the cross-counter drive wheels is itself a reversing gear: a reversing coupling wheel in the coupling position reverses one drive wheel, so the counters can all add, all subtract, or mix, by reversing the one main gear.
