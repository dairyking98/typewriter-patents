## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**How it works (reading):** One-piece punched and pressed brush housing with a bent cleaning needle always ready in the handle, plus a type lifter formed at the handle end.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type cleaning brushes do not remove dirt from type wells, and liquid solvents or steel brushes are inconvenient or damage the types.

**Solution:** One-piece punched and pressed brush housing with a bent cleaning needle always ready in the handle, plus a type lifter formed at the handle end.

**Features:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)
