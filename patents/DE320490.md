**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device to the known method in which means telegraphic apparatus with a typewriter type keyboard and a type wheel ο.

**How it works (reading):** Typewriter-style telegraph keyboards with a type wheel or exchange organ switch their key assignment automatically after an agreed number of letters or revolutions, so the cipher changes often without manual work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Secret telegraph ciphers are easy to break because the key changes by hand are too rare and too slow.

**Solution:** Typewriter-style telegraph keyboards with a type wheel or exchange organ switch their key assignment automatically after an agreed number of letters or revolutions, so the cipher changes often without manual work.
