## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cheap, simple typewriter with a type carrier that holds a great number of characters.

**Solution:** A hollow prismatic type bar with faceted sides slides and turns on a type rod, swung down by a hand-operated frame onto the paper.

**How it works:** A swinging frame depressed by hand moves a type rod through a yoke and crank, swinging it at right angles. A hollow prismatic type bar with characters (electrotypes) on its facets slides and turns on the rod. A double key tilts a guide bar engaging radial slots in the bar flange to turn it for upper or lower case, and a second key turns it further to figures. A front handle on an elbow lever with a knife edge engaging a rack sets the longitudinal position and gives alignment. The printing is visible as it is made, with an inking roller and a carriage moved by spacing levers.
