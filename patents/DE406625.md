## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** Cutting blades are clamped on the carbon holder rods and can be moved to different positions across the web.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon-sheet cutters on a folded paper web cannot be adjusted to other widths without resoldering.

**Solution:** Cutting blades are clamped on the carbon holder rods and can be moved to different positions across the web.
