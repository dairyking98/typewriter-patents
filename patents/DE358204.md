## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Key-to-type-lever linkages are heavy and complex, and each lever needs its own linkage.

**Solution:** A flexible sheathed pull cable connects each key to its type lever, with a return spring, so the motion path is independent of the cable direction.
