> Description Translated from German Device for moving the type lever. For this registration is according to the union treaty of June 2nd igii the priority due to the registration in Austria from March 27th. The invention relates to a device for moving the type lever -of typewriters and consists in that the connection between Button and type lever instead of using the usual lever mechanism with the help a pull rope is carried out in a flexible sheath, such as. B. a flexible one Tube, a tightly wound wire spiral, a flexible hollow shaft or the like., Included is. This becomes the movement device much simplified, as a result of the inclusion of the pull rope in a flexible one that closely surrounds it Sheath the transmission of motion is independent of the direction of the rope and thus there can only be an intermediate link between the button and the type lever. The construction the typewriter is thereby simplified, its weight is consequently material savings and the accessibility of the parts is increased.
