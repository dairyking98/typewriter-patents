## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a case for inclosing type-writing, adding, sewing, and other machines, not only to protect them from dust and exposure to the atmosphere, but also to allow their ready transportation.

**How it works (reading):** A case with a flat base and a top that slides on the base, with a handle; fasteners hold the cover in place, and when they are released the cover moves back automatically.

**Solution:** A case with a flat base and a top that slides on the base, with a handle; fasteners hold the cover in place, and when they are released the cover moves back automatically.
