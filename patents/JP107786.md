## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A Japanese-script typewriter with many type frames needs to be small and portable, yet light to operate and sure in printing.

**Solution:** Carry the type frames on a single rotating drum whose frame end-axles run in a groove in the fixed side covers, with one recess in the groove where the chosen frame can be drawn out.

**How it works:** A drum on a shaft between two fixed disc covers carries U-section holders round its rim; each holds the rectangular end piece of a type frame (a row of types). The end-piece axles ride in a circular groove on the covers' inner faces, which has a recess level with the shaft. A hand wheel turns the drum to bring the wanted frame to the recess. Pressing a knob swings a bent support bar whose groove completes the circle, then draws that frame back onto a channel support frame and lifts a push rod that pushes one type into the type holder (as in JP105244). In a variant the holders are replaced by recesses cut in the drum rim, saving work and giving even spacing.
