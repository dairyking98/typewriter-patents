## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key levers on typebar machines sway sideways when pressed, and fitting the key levers in guides to the intermediate levers complicates assembly.

**Solution:** Guide the double-armed key lever in slots in both legs of a U-shaped stamped bearing comb and link its rear arm to the intermediate lever by a freely movable wire link.

**How it works:** The key lever rests on a cutting edge of a U-shaped bent sheet-metal guide comb, with slots milled in both legs in one pass so the edges and guides coincide. The front arm moves in a slot in the front leg, so it cannot sway sideways. Because the lever is rigidly guided, it cannot be rigidly joined to the angle lever, so a loose wire link follows the deviating motions between key lever and angle lever.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** key lever; U-shaped guide comb; intermediate lever; wire link; angle lever

**Improves on:**

- earlier key lever guiding: no sway of the key button and no fitting of guides to the intermediate lever
