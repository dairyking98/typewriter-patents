## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to create a reliably working Device for releasing the margin stops that are not effectively actuated;

**How it works (reading):** The margin stop is set from the keyboard or a control near it, so the operator does not need to put hands on the stops; the carriage is moved and the stop is locked on its counter-stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops behind the paper support are hard to reach, and earlier remote adjusters need delicate hand moves of the carriage to align the stop with its counter-stop.

**Solution:** The margin stop is set from the keyboard or a control near it, so the operator does not need to put hands on the stops; the carriage is moved and the stop is locked on its counter-stop.
