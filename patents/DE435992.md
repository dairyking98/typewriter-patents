## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**How it works (reading):** The switching mechanism for the space key or space rail acts upstream, so it moves the carriage as soon as the space key goes down, while the write-key switch keeps its downstream action.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A word gap is lost if the space key does not start the carriage switch before the next letter prints, because the switch only works downstream of the write key.

**Solution:** The switching mechanism for the space key or space rail acts upstream, so it moves the carriage as soon as the space key goes down, while the write-key switch keeps its downstream action.
