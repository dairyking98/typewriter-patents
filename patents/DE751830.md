## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (complementary vertical counter for totalizer)

**How it works (reading):** The carry switching device is driven in the positive direction by an energy store whose release is controlled by the carriage movement, while the negative direction tensions the store again.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The complement counter of the main patent could fail to carry the volatile one when the switching device stuck in the gear chain, because it was driven from a spring tensioned by the cross counter.

**Solution:** The carry switching device is driven in the positive direction by an energy store whose release is controlled by the carriage movement, while the negative direction tensions the store again.
