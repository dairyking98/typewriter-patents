## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator forgets the punch key after each line, causing operational disruptions.

**Solution:** A cam step at line end presses a switch that automatically runs the prepared punch, carriage return and card change; alternatively a carriage lock must be released by a key.
