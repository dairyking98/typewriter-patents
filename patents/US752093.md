## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shifting the carriage between cases needs a simpler mechanism that is smoothly guided.

**Solution:** The carriage swings about a rear row of balls as a fulcrum while front balls ride a flaring track as the front is lifted.

**How it works:** The carriage has a ball track at the rear running on a frame track, and rollers at the front running on a vertically movable rail moved by a shift-key frame. Raising the rail swings the carriage about the rear balls. A second row of balls near the centre runs on a frame track whose upright surface flares forward, so the lifted carriage corner raises the balls up the incline and keeps them in relation to the carriage.
