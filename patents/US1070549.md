## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting either the platen or the typebar basket to change case moves a lot of mass.

**Solution:** Each key drives two typebars (upper and lower case) through a slotted link whose fulcrum is moved by a light shift lever, so only a bar and a few arms move on shift.

**How it works:** Each key lever connects to the middle of a slotted link whose two ends connect through rods and bell cranks to two adjacent typebars. A fulcrum rod runs through all the links on swinging levers fixed to a rock shaft. Shift key position puts the fulcrum at one end of the slot or the other, so the key pulls down only one end of the link and operates the matching typebar. A fixed bar in a segmental slot holds the link from moving during the shift.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** typebar; key lever; slotted link; bell crank; fulcrum rod; shift lever; rock shaft

**Numbers:** typebar arc about 90 degrees

**Improves on:**

- platen-shift and basket-shift machines: no heavy part shifts; only the link fulcrum moves
