## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for removing the old sheet and feeding a new sheet to a type-writer when the writing on the old sheet has been finished.

**How it works (reading):** A key ejects the finished sheet, draws a new sheet from a magazine onto the platen, and punches a feed-perforation into the record to repeat the feed on playback.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An operator must attend to each sheet change when copies are made, so a continuous run is impossible without someone renewing the sheets.

**Solution:** A key ejects the finished sheet, draws a new sheet from a magazine onto the platen, and punches a feed-perforation into the record to repeat the feed on playback.

**How it works:** Pressing the paper-feed key removes the old sheet and positions a new one from the magazine; a punched hole in the record later triggers the same feed automatically.
