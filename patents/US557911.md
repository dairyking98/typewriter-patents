## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Feed dogs of a spring-driven carriage wear out and the feed action is too slow.

**Solution:** A letter-spacing escapement of a simple dog and a toggle dog that alternately engage and release the carriage rack.

**How it works:** A spring drum pulls the carriage by a cord; a toothed rack on the carriage is engaged by two dogs on a bracket: a simple dog (first-order lever) and a toggle dog with two members joined by a toggle joint, whose tooth normally engages the rack under a spring that keeps the toggle aligned. A spring holds the simple dog tooth out of the rack. When the key acts, the dogs swap engagement, letting the carriage advance one tooth step. The toggle reduces wear on tooth faces.
