With the present Invention is an improvement "on electric typewriters with cylindrical type carrier, in which the rotation and pressure movement of the type cylinder is effected by electromagnets. The aim of the innovation: greater writing speed, Reduction of the noise and the wear and tear of the machine. A better one should also be Imprint of the individual types can be achieved to the extent that it is to be prevented, that when printing a type occasionally a part or a shadow of the on the Cylinder next to the type is included in the impression. In addition, the production better, clearer copies are made possible in greater numbers. These benefits are achieved by significantly shortening the length of time Rotation of the type cylinder stop arm and arrangement of the types the type cylinder in six rows instead of three, as before, so that the diameter of the cylinder can be reduced to about half.
