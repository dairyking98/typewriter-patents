## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Justified typing on electric typewriters needs the operator to calculate line remainders, key the line twice or punch extra tape, and mechanical-geometric justifiers are inaccurate.

**Solution:** Record keystrokes in two alternating recorders and count line length, word spaces and remainder in units with relays; divide the remainder by the word spaces and then automatically type out the line with widened word spaces.

**How it works:** The operator keys a line on the keyboard without the type levers being driven directly. Key presses set code relays that update a line-length counter in switching-step units, a word-space counter and a remainder counter. A division device divides the remainder units over the number of word spaces, giving which spaces are widened by a unit. On carriage return, the stored line is read out through decoding relays and written automatically, with a second carriage step device advancing the carriage by the correct widths. Two recording arrangements are used alternately so a new line can be keyed while the previous one is written.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** recording arrangement; line length counter; word space counter; remainder counter; coding relay; decoding relay; division device; carriage step device; paper carriage

**Improves on:**

- punched-tape justifying typewriters: no extra calculated punch marks or double keying
- mechanical-geometric justifying devices: exact electrical arithmetic instead of adjustable stops
