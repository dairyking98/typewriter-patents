## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal at the end of the spool must act promptly without straining the ribbon.

**Solution:** A drive shaft slides endwise between two spool shafts, cammed over by a ratchet that a pawl arrests when the ribbon end trips a finger.

**How it works:** The ribbon end carries an obstruction that, when the spool empties, turns a rock-shaft and drops a pawl onto a loose ratchet wheel. The stopped ratchet carries a cone that cams the rotating drive shaft endwise until its pinion meshes with the empty spool's pinion. A spring-loaded V-grooved detent completes the shift. The shaft is turned intermittently by a crank on a rock-shaft rocked at each type-stroke.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spool; drive shaft; bevel pinion; ratchet wheel; pawl; cam cone; spring detent; crank

**Improves on:**

- Underwood front-strike machine: adds a cam-shifted, prompt automatic ribbon reverse
