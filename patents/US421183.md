## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor wants a small, cheap, durable and efficient typewriter.

**Solution:** A box-like carrier of individual type slid along a guide to the printing opening, with the paper carriage fed by a universal bar and a type lifter or ejector.

**How it works:** A cast main frame holds a trough in which a long box-like type-carrier slides endwise. It contains individual type, each with a transverse groove along its rear; a fixed flange in the guide enters the continuous groove to keep the type at the right level for inking and stop them falling out. At the centre an overhanging plate with an opening shaped like a type body guides the chosen type as it moves. Inking rollers sit at the type guide, and a type lifter ejects a type to print. A universal bar in front of the frame, pivoted at the rear, feeds the paper carriage on each print.
