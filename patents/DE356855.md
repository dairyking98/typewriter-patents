## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding machine with printing unit) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**How it works (reading):** A flexible or yielding hand lever moves the carriage laterally against a spring and gears, with adjustable stops selecting the column position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage of an adding machine needs a simple lateral movement to reach several column positions, cheaply and with a new line break.

**Solution:** A flexible or yielding hand lever moves the carriage laterally against a spring and gears, with adjustable stops selecting the column position.

The simplicity and reliability of the new lateral movement device for the car, on the other hand, enables installation and perfect interaction with a new line break that is as simple as it is versatile.
