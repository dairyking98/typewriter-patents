## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen rotation, carriage return and spacing need separate, better controlled actions.

**Solution:** Lever rotates the platen with the carriage at rest, a key returns the carriage, and a lever returns it synchronously while advancing the platen; a catch limits the return.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
