## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Types in the Remington circle of hinged type levers need cleaning without a cumbersome device.

**Solution:** A cleaner is detachably secured to and supported by the ribbon guide, so the type pendent in the circle can be cleaned.
