## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** When typing columns of figures the operator has to look at headings at the top of the sheet or remember where each column goes.

**Solution:** A strip bearing column headings in a holder behind the printing point, parallel to the platen, hinged so it swings up out of the way.

**How it works:** A grooved sheet-metal holder, as long as the platen, is soldered to the auxiliary paper guide rod and its arm on the Oliver carriage, behind the print point. The arm hinges on a rod and a spring holds it down against a stop bar. The column indicator strip slides endwise into the holder's folded-over channels so its marks show through the gap between the flanges. Swinging the arm up releases access to insert or remove strips, and an adjustable sliding stop positions the strip endwise relative to the letter scale.
