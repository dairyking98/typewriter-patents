## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line setting for cash registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a line setting and conveying device for Cash registers and accounting machines in which a receipt is inserted into an insertion opening inserted into the machine and set to a specific line by a document carrier can be.

**How it works (reading):** A document carrier is set by hand to any line and is also advanced automatically by one line after a set number of lines, or returned to the first item position after a total is drawn, through a rack and toothed segment drive.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Receipt line setting in cash registers must be possible by hand and also automatically, which the known devices do not combine well.

**Solution:** A document carrier is set by hand to any line and is also advanced automatically by one line after a set number of lines, or returned to the first item position after a total is drawn, through a rack and toothed segment drive.
