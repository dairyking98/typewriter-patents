## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple, easily operated rack-and-pinion spacing escapement is needed, with a reliable way to disengage it so the carriage can be shifted or returned.

**Solution:** A pair of escapement wheels joined by a spring, one fixed and one loose, step a spacer shaft one letter space per key stroke, and a lever lifts the rack to free the pinion clutch.

**How it works:** The carriage rack meshes with a pinion on a vertical spacer shaft, and the carriage spring drives that pinion. On the shaft sit a fixed escapement wheel and a loose wheel joined by a coil spring, with screws on the loose wheel passing through slightly oversize slots in the fixed wheel. A pawl on a rock-shaft operated by the keys engages both wheels. On key depression the loose wheel moves a little less than the fixed wheel, and on release the fixed wheel advances one letter space. A release lever pushes the rack and a plate down so the pinion's ratchet face leaves its fixed ratchet, letting the carriage move freely either way.
