## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the subject of typewriting machines, and has special reference to an improved mechanism for automatically returning the carriageof the machine and simultaneously efiecting a line spacing operation through the rotation of the platen of the carriage.

**How it works (reading):** An automatic carriage return that can be started at any carriage point and at once rotates the platen to space the line, cooperating with the tabulator and margin stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage and spacing the line were separate operations, and the return had to be quick from any scale point.

**Solution:** An automatic carriage return that can be started at any carriage point and at once rotates the platen to space the line, cooperating with the tabulator and margin stops.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
