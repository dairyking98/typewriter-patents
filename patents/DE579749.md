## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** Make the type rods star-shaped so they touch the guide tube only along narrow edges, driven by low-pressure air.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pneumatic typebar machines needed play-free, well lubricated pistons and tight tolerances for 90-95 bars, and were noisy.

**Solution:** Make the type rods star-shaped so they touch the guide tube only along narrow edges, driven by low-pressure air.

**How it works:** Each of 90-95 type rods runs in its own tube aimed at the print point, with a square-section rod having concave sides so only the edges contact the tube. Air pressure or suction at the rear end drives the rod out and back. The tubes form a truncated-cone bundle about 6 cm from the print point. One diaphragm pump driven by an electromagnet supplies the air, and simple valves in the key system switch it.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Key touch and even impression](../features/bar-touch.md), [Power-driven type action](../features/bar-power-action.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Numbers:** 90 to 95 type rods; about 6 cm from rest to stop point

**Improves on:**

- known pneumatic typewriters: rods no longer fill the tube so no lubrication or tight fit is needed
