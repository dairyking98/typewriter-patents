## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in a type-writer platen having a face composed of resilient hard-surfaced wear-resisting sections, prcferably of thin tempered sheet metal, such as steel, and a yleldingnoise-muffiing backing for said sections supported by the body of the platen,- the said sections being independent ofeach other to such an extent that the vibrations of each section will be absorbed by the yielding backing.

**How it works (reading):** A platen face of separate resilient steel strips, each section independent, over a yielding noise-muffling backing on a rigid body, so each strip's vibration is absorbed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says the impact of type characters on a rigid platen face causes vibration and noise.

**Solution:** A platen face of separate resilient steel strips, each section independent, over a yielding noise-muffling backing on a rigid body, so each strip's vibration is absorbed.
