## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**How it works (reading):** Pivots the paper table and the rear pressure roll so they can be swung together away from the platen, with paper guide fingers on a rod beneath the platen that hug the platen to guide the sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The paper table and front pressure roll on a front-strike machine are hard to keep out of the way of the paper; the OCR is too garbled to state the inventor's problem with confidence.

**Solution:** Pivots the paper table and the rear pressure roll so they can be swung together away from the platen, with paper guide fingers on a rod beneath the platen that hug the platen to guide the sheet.
