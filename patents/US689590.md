## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (inflatable roller for printers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings supporting a hollow roll mantle rotating with respect to a yoke or axle adjustable for positioning, e.g. radial movable bearings for controlling the deflection along the length of the roll mantle by fluid pressure with a plurality of supports along the length of the roll mantle, e.g. hydraulic jacks

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to improvements in inflatable rollers for use by printers.

**How it works (reading):** An internal inflatable tube inside a flexible covering cylinder gives uniform pressure across the bearing points of the roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inflated printing rollers lose their shape by bulging or flattening, so ink and moisture are not uniformly distributed.

**Solution:** An internal inflatable tube inside a flexible covering cylinder gives uniform pressure across the bearing points of the roller.
