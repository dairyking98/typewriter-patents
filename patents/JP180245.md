## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter's platen back-feed (return) could not easily be changed between two amounts when needed.

**Solution:** A knob rod sets a limit plate at one of two heights so a push-back plate driven by a striking key travels either a short or a long fixed distance.

**How it works:** Pushing or pulling a knob rod rocks a cam plate whose slanting slot raises or lowers a lifting plate and the limit plate on it. Striking the key rocks a lever and link that turn a cam; its face on a fixed pin advances the cam, and a spring presses a push-back plate against the arm so its tip engages the platen's rack. The plate advances until its upper or lower bent face meets the top of the limit plate, giving a short or long fixed push. Springs return the lever, link, cam and plate, the plate leaving the rack.

**Numbers:** two back-feed amounts, long and short

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. A typewriter back-feed device in which pushing or pulling a knob rod rocks a cam plate, so that a limit plate fixed to a lifting plate linked to it is set at either of two heights; and in which, with the rise and fall of a link hinged to the other end of a rocking lever that carries a striking key at one end, a turning cam is moved forward and back, and with it a push-back plate having bent faces at two levels, upper and lower, is moved toward and away from the top of the limit plate.
