## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**How it works (reading):** Two swiveling linear mirrors placed at an angle on either side of the finger give uninterrupted reflection with no shadow, and clamp onto the machine without drilling.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single linear writing-light mirror casts a finger shadow on the reflected light, and cutting an opening in the mirror breaks the reflection.

**Solution:** Two swiveling linear mirrors placed at an angle on either side of the finger give uninterrupted reflection with no shadow, and clamp onto the machine without drilling.
