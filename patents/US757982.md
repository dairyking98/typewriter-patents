## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage, spacing, line-lock, line-space, margin-stop, tabulating and release mechanisms for type-wheel machines had too many parts and needed adjustment.

**Solution:** Redesign these carriage mechanisms with very few simple, cheap parts, with the carriage frame built from a stamped blank, for a type-wheel machine.

**How it works:** The patent covers the carriage and its feed mechanism, a line-locking device, line spacing, a margin stop with lock, tabulating stops and a general carriage release, grouped under headings. The type wheel is mounted on a carrier and the paper carriage moves transversely, with key levers beneath. A dog lever carries escapement dogs, and the spacer bar acts on it from beneath. The carriage frame is formed from a flat blank shown in Fig. 5. Only the opening is available, so details of each mechanism are not read here.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** type wheel; carriage; top plate; key lever; dog lever; spacer bar; margin stop; tabulating stop; carriage frame blank
