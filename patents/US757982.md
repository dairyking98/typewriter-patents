## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention is directed to certain improvements in the carriage and its spacing mechanism, the line-locking mechanism, linespacing mechanism, marginal stop, tabulatingstops, and carriage-release,which are particularly applicable to machines of the type-wheel class, although in most part applicable generally to those of the type-lever class.

**How it works (reading):** Redesign these carriage mechanisms with very few simple, cheap parts, with the carriage frame built from a stamped blank, for a type-wheel machine.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage, spacing, line-lock, line-space, margin-stop, tabulating and release mechanisms for type-wheel machines had too many parts and needed adjustment.

**Solution:** Redesign these carriage mechanisms with very few simple, cheap parts, with the carriage frame built from a stamped blank, for a type-wheel machine.

**How it works:** The patent covers the carriage and its feed mechanism, a line-locking device, line spacing, a margin stop with lock, tabulating stops and a general carriage release, grouped under headings. The type wheel is mounted on a carrier and the paper carriage moves transversely, with key levers beneath. A dog lever carries escapement dogs, and the spacer bar acts on it from beneath. The carriage frame is formed from a flat blank shown in Fig. 5. Only the opening is available, so details of each mechanism are not read here.

**Features:** [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** type wheel; carriage; top plate; key lever; dog lever; spacer bar; margin stop; tabulating stop; carriage frame blank
