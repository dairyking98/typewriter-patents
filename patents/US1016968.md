## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dead (accent) keys must not trigger the escapement yet must vibrate the ribbon to the print point; earlier arrangements were elaborate.

**Solution:** A yielding spring connection between ribbon vibrator and its operating lever, with lost motion between the accent key and universal bar, so the accent key prints and lifts the ribbon without a letter space.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 10, the accent key lever acts on the universal bar only after a long initial depression, adjusted so the feed dog is not moved out of the escapement wheel and the carriage stays put. The ribbon vibrator pin is engaged by a two-part operating lever whose parts are jointed and held by a lever tied to the platen shift frame. A special spring connection lets the vibrator still rise to cover the print point for the accent type.
