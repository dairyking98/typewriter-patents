## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reset pawls for the carriage rack need precise spring tension and often fail to advance the rack by one tooth.

**Solution:** Two pawls: a sliding pawl driven by the downshift key pushes a rotating pawl, held by a spring, into the rack tooth gap so the rack advances one tooth.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Downshift device for the trolley of typewriters. The known Reset pawls for typewriter trolleys are first turned so that its tooth is inserted into the rack of the carriage, and then for the purpose of displacement the rack longitudinally shifted by one tooth pitch. This mechanism requires a fine adjustment of its individual links, in particular such a precise regulation the tension spring that it often fails despite the most careful assembly. This disadvantage is eliminated according to the invention in that two Pawls are provided, one of which is a pawl under the action of the downshift key is only subjected to a displacement, but not a rotation, and by a rising inclined surface with the second pawl is in engagement, on the one special spring acts and which is mounted both displaceably and rotatably. The subject of the invention is shown in one embodiment in the drawing shown. Fig. I and 2 show the two pawls in side view and top view. Figure 3 is a side view of the two pawls on another mutual position.
