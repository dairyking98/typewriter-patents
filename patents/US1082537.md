## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates broadly to typearticularly to the escapement mechanism 0 a machine of a type adapted to effect printing noiselessly.

**How it works (reading):** The escapement wheel is driven by a movable feed dog carried on a rocker that is swung into engagement, with a fixed dog and the carriage escapement arranged so the mechanism operates substantially noiselessly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement clicking produced a large part of the noise of typewriters.

**Solution:** The escapement wheel is driven by a movable feed dog carried on a rocker that is swung into engagement, with a fixed dog and the carriage escapement arranged so the mechanism operates substantially noiselessly.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
