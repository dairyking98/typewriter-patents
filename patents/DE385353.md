## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash-register control slips can be altered after the fact, so the operator needs a record that cannot be changed before each control mark is applied.

**Solution:** A writing strip moves behind a housing opening and is coupled to a punch or stamp device for control slips, so each mark is made together with a forced advance of the record strip.
