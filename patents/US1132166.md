## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen adjustment relative to the line-space wheel needs to be finer, and the clutch must be accessible for cleaning.

**Solution:** Clutch dogs with very fine teeth, loose on the platen axle, mesh with a fine ratchet on the line-space wheel; a collar slid along the axle swings them out, and two dogs offset in phase double the adjustment range.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
