## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in a mechanism for reversing the ribbon on an ordinarytypewriter machine.

**How it works (reading):** Reel operating means driven intermittently by the key levers shift automatically to the opposite reel when one reel empties.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Reversing the ribbon on an ordinary typewriter normally needs hand action.

**Solution:** Reel operating means driven intermittently by the key levers shift automatically to the opposite reel when one reel empties.

**How it works:** Two slidable rods carry ribbon guard bars and guide pins; a two-colour ribbon (black and red) is positioned by moving a rod sideways. A cross shaft with loose bevel gears drives bevel gears on the two reel spindles. Any key stroke steps the reel operating means, and when the ribbon runs off one reel the drive switches to the other.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** ribbon reel; spindle; bevel gear; cross shaft; slidable rod; ribbon guard; guide pin

**Improves on:**

- ordinary typewriter ribbon reverse: reversal is automatic, driven by the key action
