## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Filing cards had to be fed by hand, which took more time than the typing.

**Solution:** A magazine holds a packet of cards and feed rollers, driven from the platen roll, feed the cards in succession into the typewriter.

**How it works:** The platen roll drives the feed rollers, which take cards from the magazine one at a time.
