## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a mecha nism adapted to move the carriage of a typewriter in a backward direction, that is, in a direction the reverse from that to which itis moved during the ordinary writing 'operation, and is commonly termed a back feed mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A dog on a rack is first moved into positive engagement with the teeth by an initial movement, then driven to feed the carriage back a fixed distance set by a limiting adjustment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Back feed of the carriage slips and gives unequal throws when the dog meets the rack.

**Solution:** A dog on a rack is first moved into positive engagement with the teeth by an initial movement, then driven to feed the carriage back a fixed distance set by a limiting adjustment.
