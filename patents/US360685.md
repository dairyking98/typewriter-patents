## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators typing tabulated columns on a Remington had to find the starting point on each line, and re-set every pointer when a new sheet sat slightly differently.

**Solution:** A cheap clamp-on indicator bar carrying slidable pointers that show where the carriage pointer should stop to begin each column.

**How it works:** Two clamp plates with edge slots fit over part of the machine frame and are fixed by binding screws. An indicator bar with tenoned ends fits in openings of the plates and carries pointers that slide along it and are secured by screws. The operator sets each pointer opposite the scale-plate number where a column starts and begins writing when the carriage pointer reaches it. If a new sheet is offset, the clamps are loosened and the whole bar is shifted lengthwise.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** clamp plate; binding screw; indicator bar; indicator pointer; scale plate; carriage pointer

**Improves on:**

- earlier pointer indicators for typewriters: simpler, cheaper, clamps to the frame and the whole bar is adjustable
