## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting and resetting tabulator column stops is awkward, and the carriage must be arrested at set points in its travel.

**Solution:** A stop-setting device in which a key shifts a cooperating device into the path of one of the stops while releasing the carriage from letter spacing, so the carriage moves under its spring to the stop position.

**How it works:** Pressing the key moves the device into the path of a longitudinally adjustable stop and disconnects the letter-spacing control, letting the carriage run to the stop.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; shifting carriage; tabulator stops; stop bar; key; letter spacing mechanism
