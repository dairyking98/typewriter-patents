## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon spools are mounted on swinging carriers joined by a rod, so the ribbon pull itself moves them to reverse drive, without locking elements.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal by tooth pressure often fails when the ribbon is taut, leaving weak letters.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools are mounted on swinging carriers joined by a rod, so the ribbon pull itself moves them to reverse drive, without locking elements.
