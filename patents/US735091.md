## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more especially to the key-action of machines of the type-writer class, and has for an object to provide an improved organization designed to minimize the fatiguing effect incident to the continued operation of the machinethat is to say, to imr prove the touch of the keys.

**How it works (reading):** A yielding resilient starter between each key-lever and its type-bar applies a gradually increasing force to start the bar, and the connection lets the bar accelerate and then complete its stroke by momentum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says the key action fatigues the operator, because the key force strikes the type-bar abruptly and the touch is poor.

**Solution:** A yielding resilient starter between each key-lever and its type-bar applies a gradually increasing force to start the bar, and the connection lets the bar accelerate and then complete its stroke by momentum.
