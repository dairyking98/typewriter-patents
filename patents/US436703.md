## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier matrix machines moved the feed in advance of the die stroke, which required timing constraints and limited speed and matrix quality.

**Solution:** An improved matrix-making machine whose feed and die-depressing are electrically controlled from the character-selecting device, with dies grouped by space width.

**How it works:** A die carrier of swinging and sliding lever with a hand-key works over an index plate with countersunk holes and contacts; the escapement and variable stops set carriage feed length for each die group.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [SEL:pointer-hole](../features/sel-pointer-hole.md)
