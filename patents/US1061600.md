## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The guide for a card or paper at the platen must be secured in place and yet be easily attached and detached.

**Solution:** A bifurcated wire guide whose shanks seat in sockets on the type segment bracket, with a tongue bearing lightly on the platen to guide the paper.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
