## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (exchangeable printing plate, cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that the printing plate is located on a carrier made of thin sheet metal, the latter of which is bent into grooves Ends by means of a holding rod in engagement with a longitudinal groove of a pressure roller can be held.

**How it works (reading):** The printing plate is glued or vulcanised to a thin sheet-metal carrier whose bent ends hook into a longitudinal groove of the roller, so the plate can be fitted and removed without tools.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing plates on cash register rollers are curved and fixed with small screws, so changing the advertising text needs tools that the checkout staff may not have.

**Solution:** The printing plate is glued or vulcanised to a thin sheet-metal carrier whose bent ends hook into a longitudinal groove of the roller, so the plate can be fitted and removed without tools.

These cliché plates have hitherto usually been produced in a curved shape and attached to the pressure roller by means of small screws. This type of attachment requires the use of tools for the replacement Checkout-attending salespeople are not always available. Through the invention a type of fastening has been created in which the replacement without assistance can be done by tools. The invention consists in that the printing plate is located on a carrier made of thin sheet metal, the latter of which is bent into grooves Ends by means of a holding rod in engagement with a longitudinal groove of a pressure roller can be held.
