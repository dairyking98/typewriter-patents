## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Printing unit with exchangeable printing plate for cash registers or Accounting machines It is common to use the printing rollers in printing .works of cash registers .To be provided with interchangeable pressure plates in order to e.g. B. on that of the machine to print other advertising texts from time to time. These cliché plates have hitherto usually been produced in a curved shape and attached to the pressure roller by means of small screws. This type of attachment requires the use of tools for the replacement Checkout-attending salespeople are not always available. Through the invention a type of fastening has been created in which the replacement without assistance can be done by tools. The invention consists in that the printing plate is located on a carrier made of thin sheet metal, the latter of which is bent into grooves Ends by means of a holding rod in engagement with a longitudinal groove of a pressure roller can be held. An embodiment of the invention is shown in the drawings.
