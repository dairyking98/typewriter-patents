## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage escapement and backspacing need a precise, simple drive.

**Solution:** The escapement uses an epicyclic gear train with dogs on an escape wheel, and a backspacing mechanism steps the carriage back one letter space.
