## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Narrow work on a flat platen curls and moves into movable parts, and it must be held flat along the platen for any width.

**Solution:** A flexible tape held taut across the platen at one end by a tension device, and at the other by a vertically sliding catch, which can be moved sideways and lifted clear of the work so both hands are free.
