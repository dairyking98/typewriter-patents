## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** No explicit problem stated; provides a segment-type machine with a simple key positioning and hammer action.

**Solution:** Keys depress positioning levers that swing a type-segment until an arm hits the pressed key, then the same key's printing lever carries on to drive the hammer.

**How it works:** Keys sit across two sets of printing levers, one for each half of the keyboard, whose rear arms lift a pin on a hammer. Each printing lever is fulcrumed on a positioning lever. Pressing a key swings the positioning lever, which turns a shoulder piece, shaft, type-segment and arm until the arm hits the depressed key and stops, which places the right type at the print point. The printing lever then continues swinging and carries the hammer up to print the paper through a perforated plate against the ribbon and segment. Another bail on the keys works the carriage dog through rock-shaft arms to space the carriage. On release everything returns to rest.
