## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper finger construction is complex and needs to be simpler and more efficient for front-strike typewriters.

**Solution:** Paper fingers of sheet metal are carried on a rock shaft in a simple construction that holds the paper against the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
