## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**How it works (reading):** The inclined engagement surface is twisted along its length so each intermediate lever, despite different stroke, moves the universal rail by the same amount.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The universal rail moves unevenly when intermediate levers of unequal length strike a sloped surface.

**Solution:** The inclined engagement surface is twisted along its length so each intermediate lever, despite different stroke, moves the universal rail by the same amount.
