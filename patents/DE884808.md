## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** One-piece type levers of many different lengths each need their own punching tools and jigs.

**Solution:** A lever is built from a standard profile middle piece cut to length, with a type head and bearing piece joined at its ends.

For example, the type levers on most typewriters are below different length. The same goes for the key levers on most typewriters. So far, these levers are made from one piece, so that one for each type of lever A number of different punching tools and drilling jigs are necessary. The invention aims to obviate this disadvantage. According to the invention the lever consists of a major part of its length occupying in its Longitudinally uniform profile piece and one firmly connected to it at one end End piece. Usually there are special end pieces with the middle one on both ends Profile piece be connected. A series of such levers belonging to a machine consists according to a further feature of the invention in their middle pieces of the same Profiles of various lengths.
