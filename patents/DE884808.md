## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** One-piece type levers of many different lengths each need their own punching tools and jigs.

**Solution:** A lever is built from a standard profile middle piece cut to length, with a type head and bearing piece joined at its ends.

According to the invention the lever consists of a major part of its length occupying in its Longitudinally uniform profile piece and one firmly connected to it at one end End piece.
