> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a lever for office machines, in particular for typewriters. Levers of this type are used for various purposes, e.g. B. as button levers, type levers, control levers, etc. Such machines often have levers of the same type, but different Length forward. For example, the type levers on most typewriters are below different length. The same goes for the key levers on most typewriters. So far, these levers are made from one piece, so that one for each type of lever A number of different punching tools and drilling jigs are necessary. The invention aims to obviate this disadvantage. According to the invention the lever consists of a major part of its length occupying in its Longitudinally uniform profile piece and one firmly connected to it at one end End piece. Usually there are special end pieces with the middle one on both ends Profile piece be connected. A series of such levers belonging to a machine consists according to a further feature of the invention in their middle pieces of the same Profiles of various lengths.
