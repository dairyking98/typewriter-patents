## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper fingers around the platen were held by screws that had to be loosened or removed to suit different paper widths.

**Solution:** Each paper finger rides on a slotted square bar in a spring-loaded two-part holder whose dog snaps into a slot and is released by pressing with one finger.

**How it works:** A square slotted bar runs along the rear of the carriage between its end pieces. Two holders slide on it, each made of a guide member with a U-shaped head and a spring clamping member whose head has a dog. A spring pushes the members apart so the dog engages a slot in the bar and locks the holder. Pressing the roughened, concave rear of the clamping member with a fingertip withdraws the dog so the holder and its curved paper finger slide to a new width. One holder has a downward stem to which the carriage tension cord attaches, and both holders also bear on a fixed bracket to limit carriage travel.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; square slotted bar; guide member; clamping member; dog; paper finger; spring; tension cord; fixed bracket

**Improves on:**

- screw-clamped paper fingers: repositioned by one finger press with no screws to loosen
