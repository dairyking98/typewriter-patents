## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Method of making turned-down edges Embossing strips in embossing machines. Embossing machines are known in which the to embossed stripes first, then cut off and finally at the edges be bent downwards. However, these machines have several disadvantages because namely, the strip shifts slightly during the embossing and because it is still the finished embossed strip can be provided with bent edges after embossing got to. With this bending, the part not to be bent, i. H. the part on which the embossing is held down with the application of considerable pressure so that it does not bulge upwards. In order to overcome these disadvantages, the method according to FIG Invention changed the order of operations. The embossing strip is first provided with bent margins, then embossed and finally cut off. At the same time, a special type of cutting is carried out in which neither bending of the strips nor annoying burr formation occurs. By a special device consisting of two tools working together, a piece is punched out of the strip.
