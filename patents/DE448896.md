## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing strips with bent edges) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** The strip is first given bent-down edges, then embossed, then cut off by a punch that leaves no bend or burr, so the order of operations is changed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Embossing strips that are cut, embossed and then bent at their edges shift during embossing, and bending after embossing can bulge the embossed part.

**Solution:** The strip is first given bent-down edges, then embossed, then cut off by a punch that leaves no bend or burr, so the order of operations is changed.
