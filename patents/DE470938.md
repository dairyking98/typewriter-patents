## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The long line-shift lever raises the overall height and makes it hard to fit the typewriter in a flat case.

**Solution:** Mount the lever on a bearing bracket that can be folded down below the top edge of the frame, where it also locks the carriage.

**How it works:** A two-armed bracket is friction-mounted on a bolt on the carriage side. One arm carries a vertical pin on which the line lever swings; a leaf spring with a nub snaps into one of two recesses in the carriage side plate. In use the lever is horizontal with the nub in recess 12. Pushing the lever down disengages the nub, folds the bracket down until it snaps into recess 11, and the other arm's claw grips behind the head of a bolt in the housing wall, locking the carriage and the lever.
