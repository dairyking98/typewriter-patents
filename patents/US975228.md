## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Pressure on the feed rolls could only be changed by dismantling parts of the machine, and uniform pressure across rolls was hard to keep.

**Solution:** Each roll shaft's springs are individually adjustable from outside by a rotating tool engaging the spring shaft end, so tension is set without dismantling and remains uniform.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** pressure rolls; roll shafts; adjustable springs; tension shaft; platen frame
