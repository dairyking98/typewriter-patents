## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** One machine cannot write two scripts or sizes (such as English and Japanese) of different letter pitch on the same sheet.

**Solution:** Join two Underwood typebar machines side by side with one long carriage that runs from one printing zone to the other, each machine having its own escapement of different pitch.

**How it works:** Two ordinary frames with their own keyboards and typebars stand on a common base, with long guide rails and a single carriage and platen frame spanning both. Each frame has a spring drum, pinion and escapement wheel; the carriage has two racks, each meshing only with its own pinion, the rack length equalling the distance between pinions so control hands over at the point of change. A pinion size ratio of about 8 to 10 gives different letter spacing in each zone. A release key lifts the rack so the carriage can be moved by hand to the other zone. Four shift keys raise a long shift rail carrying the platen at any carriage position, with a lock, and each zone has its own margin stops, line lock, bell and scale.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Type printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a typewriter consisting of a reciprocating paper carriage and several key-operated letter-feed mechanisms that give the carriage its letter-feed motion. Each step made by one of these mechanisms is of a different extent from the steps made by the other, and the carriage can be moved in the feed direction from a position where it works with one mechanism to a position where it works with the other. Its object is to combine several type systems on one base, so as to obtain a machine that can print, on the same sheet, characters written differently and of larger and smaller size.
