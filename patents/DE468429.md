## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (lining device for tabulating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Writing or like instruments in holders or guides

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable guide roller for the paper strip, placed near the inked ruling wheels, separates paper and ruler as they lift so there is no smudging.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ruling wheels used on tabulators are hard to lift cleanly and ink, and dividing lines between imprints are needed.

**Solution:** An adjustable guide roller for the paper strip, placed near the inked ruling wheels, separates paper and ruler as they lift so there is no smudging.
