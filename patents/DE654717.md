## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that the clampable roller carrier part Has widening clamping slots and on a square bar with through Settling formed.

**How it works (reading):** A roller carrier slides laterally on a square bar, with slotted clamp arms gripping the paper guide; the weight of the carrier pulls the clamp closed and a stepped front edge sets its height.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Screw-clamped manuscript holders mark and damage the machine and loosen from vibration, and fit only machines of certain sizes.

**Solution:** A roller carrier slides laterally on a square bar, with slotted clamp arms gripping the paper guide; the weight of the carrier pulls the clamp closed and a stepped front edge sets its height.
