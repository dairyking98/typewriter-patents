## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to keyactions for type writing and other key-operated machines, particularly for typewriting machines of the front-stroke types ucb, for instance, as the machine commonly known as the Underwood type-writer.

**How it works (reading):** A light spring disconnects the key-lever from the typebar after the printing movement and after the key completes its movement, avoiding wear on the parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A detachable connection between key-lever and typebar wears from being held under tension against a fixed stop.

**Solution:** A light spring disconnects the key-lever from the typebar after the printing movement and after the key completes its movement, avoiding wear on the parts.
