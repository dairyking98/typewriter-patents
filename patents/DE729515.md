**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (accounting typewriter with multiplier and totalizer)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an accounting machine, in particular one equipped with a sum train Typewriter with a multiplier device, in which the transmission of motion from the machine drive to the writing device of the sum value via a from the sum pull process controllable clutch is regulated.

**How it works (reading):** A clutch driven by the carriage movement switches the drive off to the writing device for unwanted decimal places.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Unwanted decimal places of a product are written on the forms in multiplying typewriters.

**Solution:** A clutch driven by the carriage movement switches the drive off to the writing device for unwanted decimal places.
