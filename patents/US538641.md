**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines and particularly to the mechanisms therein, for adjusting the spring-wheel to vary the length of the line of printing, for stopping the carriage at the end of the line, for printing upon.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriage drive needed adjustable line length, a stop at line end that can be overridden, a clutch between spring case and escapement rack, adjustable feed roll gap and a new bell.

**Solution:** A spring wheel whose case and a lower escapement-rack section are clutched together one way, with adjustable line stop, override, eccentric feed-roll shaft and a cam-driven bell hammer.

**How it works:** The carriage rear rail is a tubular rack bar and the platen runs in a carriage. A spring wheel with a mainspring in its case sits on a ball-bearing arbor; its lower section forms the escapement rack, joined to the upper case by a clutch so both turn together in one direction while only the case turns in the other. A T-groove in the case carries an adjustable block that sets the line length and stops the carriage, with a way to print beyond the stop. A feed roll on an eccentric shaft is set by a thumb-nut for the number of sheets. Near line end an inclined lip on the case lifts a bell hammer against a spring, which snaps off the cam and strikes the bell.

**Features:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** spring wheel; mainspring; escapement rack section; clutch; T-bolt stop block; carriage rack bar; platen; eccentric shaft; feed roll; thumb-nut; bell; bell hammer
