## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin-stop devices on rider-style tab rails need a separate locking device, and stopping the carriage by margin stops must not fail when the carriage is pushed further.

**Solution:** A slidable tilting rail carries the margin stop and, on striking a margin, locks the key switch via a double-armed rocker so all keys including the space bar are blocked; a release lever tilts the rail inward to let the carriage pass.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** tilting rail; margin stops with locking pieces; double-armed rocker; spring; release lever
