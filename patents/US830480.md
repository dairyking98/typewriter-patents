## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On front-strike machines with a shiftable platen, paper holders and line indicators must keep their position on the print line in both cases and allow corrections.

**Solution:** Line-indicator plates move with the platen, press the paper at the print line, and individual paper holders on a rock-shaft fold forward for erasures.

**How it works:** Indicator plates ride the vertically shifting platen and keep a fixed relation to the print line, pressing the paper lightly on each side of the print point. They also help line up ruled forms and locate a letter position. Paper holders, adjustable lengthwise, hang on a rock-shaft below the print line and can be thrown forward singly or together so manifold copies can be corrected.
