## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A pawl that releases the carriage switching ratchet is noisy and shocks on stopping, and the pawl rubs on the teeth.

**Solution:** The pawl is moved concentrically about the ratchet shaft with guideways at its end positions, so it stops without shock and with almost no friction.
