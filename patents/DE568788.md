## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper carrier adjustment for perforating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment

**How it works (reading):** A releasable stop limits the adjusting crank in normal use; a special handle disengages it so the adjustment can go past the normal limit, and it snaps back to the locked position afterwards.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carrier adjustment is limited to a normal range, so heavy-impression work such as punching stencils cannot be done.

**Solution:** A releasable stop limits the adjusting crank in normal use; a special handle disengages it so the adjustment can go past the normal limit, and it snaps back to the locked position afterwards.

**How it works:** A spring-loaded stop sits against a contact surface on the crank and is held ineffective while the special handle is used; once the crank returns, the stop snaps back and restores the normal range.
