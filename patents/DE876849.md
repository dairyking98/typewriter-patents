## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to ribbon spools, as particularly so used for typewriters, but also for other types of machines will.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon spool design spaces the type impact points along an arithmetic series so the ribbon wears more evenly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon life is limited unevenly: winding a constant amount per stroke spreads the impact points unevenly across the ribbon.

**Solution:** A ribbon spool design spaces the type impact points along an arithmetic series so the ribbon wears more evenly.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; cotton or silk ribbon; winding bobbin
