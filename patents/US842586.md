## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to key-actions for type-writing' and other key-operated machines, and more particularly to type-writing machines of thefront-stroke type in which the key-lever is disconnected from the typebar after the type-bar has;

**How it works (reading):** The key-lever and typebar are disconnected only after the key-lever has completed its movement, and a means is provided to keep them connected for heavy duplicating work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In front-stroke machines the key-lever stays connected to the typebar until the printing momentum is given, so heavy work cannot be done reliably.

**Solution:** The key-lever and typebar are disconnected only after the key-lever has completed its movement, and a means is provided to keep them connected for heavy duplicating work.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key-lever; type-bar; segment; fixed rest; paper-carriage
