## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control device for card-controlled wire printer) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the printing or marking process for which they are designed characterised by selective application of impact or pressure on a printing material or impression-transfer material using print wires

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a control device on a card-controlled wire printing machine with a Apparatus for driving the printing wires against a platen, wherein a sensing device the Punch cards as they pass through the machine in a known manner and that for printing a selects printing wires required for a specific character.

**How it works (reading):** Each wire has a pivoting ratchet lever held in working position by its own electromagnet; cams on rotating shafts drive the held levers and their wires to the print position, then they return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Wire printer control with springs or a common reciprocating rail is complex and does not reliably select the printing wires.

**Solution:** Each wire has a pivoting ratchet lever held in working position by its own electromagnet; cams on rotating shafts drive the held levers and their wires to the print position, then they return.
