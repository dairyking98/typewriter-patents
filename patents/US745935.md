## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making dotted leader lines on a type-bar machine needed the same key pressed again and again, which is slow in tabulated statements.

**Solution:** A needle-pointed liner wheel mounted on a type bar is held against the ribbon and paper by a key; as the carriage releases and moves, the wheel is turned by the needle points and prints a row of dots through the ribbon. It can also serve as a period type.
