## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Center-strike visible machines needed more practical escapement, release, key leverage, universal bar, margin stop and paper holding mechanisms.

**Solution:** A set of improved mechanisms for the Barney key-lever center-strike machine in which platen and type-bar support move relatively for case shift.

**How it works:** Key levers drive typebars and also the escapement through differential leverage connections. The platen and typebar support shift toward or away from each other for upper and lower case, with a locking member holding the shifted position. Separate universal bars, an escapement release, adjustable margin stops and paper holding are described. Only the opening is available.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** key lever; typebar; universal bar; escapement; platen; margin stop; paper holder

**Improves on:**

- US594978 Barney: further mechanisms for the same class
