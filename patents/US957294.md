## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in speed governors for the power driven carriages of typewriting and tabulating machines.

**How it works (reading):** A bushing is threaded into the governor spindle so the brake adjustment can be made from outside the casing, and the bushing is locked in place.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjusting the brake members of a carriage speed governor means taking the governor apart.

**Solution:** A bushing is threaded into the governor spindle so the brake adjustment can be made from outside the casing, and the bushing is locked in place.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** governor casing; spindle; weights; friction washer; adjustable bushing; lock nut
