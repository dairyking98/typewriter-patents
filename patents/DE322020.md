## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (loom shedding device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Shedding mechanisms not otherwise provided for; Frame-operating devices for ribbon looms

**How it works (reading):** A bell-crank rocker inserted between crank and kick slows closing near the dead point, so the shed stays open long enough for the shuttle to pass.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In crank-driven loom shedding the shed closes as fast as it opens, so the shuttle is caught before it passes through.

**Solution:** A bell-crank rocker inserted between crank and kick slows closing near the dead point, so the shed stays open long enough for the shuttle to pass.
