## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Three systems of raised characters exist for the blind (New York point, Braille, Moon), and a machine should write any of them with correction of errors.

**Solution:** An embossing writing machine whose spacing mechanism and dies are adjusted to write New York point, regular or uniformly spaced Braille, or Moon script.

**How it works:** A paper carriage on rollers travels in a frame as in typewriters; shafts with knurled heads and splined parts adjust the machine for the chosen system. Controller bars operate space blocks and spacing dogs, which can be moved to give uniform spacing between letters or spacing by letter width. A paper spacing ratchet and lever advance the paper. Dies emboss points in groups not exceeding four horizontally in two rows for New York point, or up to three points vertically for Braille.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [For the blind](../features/use-blind.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; frame; die; controller bar; space block; spacing dog; paper spacing ratchet; knurled shaft

**Numbers:** groups not exceeding four points horizontally; up to three points vertically
