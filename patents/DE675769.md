## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check printing unit, registering machines)

**How it works (reading):** A single conveyor moves the check strip end in opposite directions between the cutting point and one or more printing points, shifting it after the cut knife has worked and before the first print.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check printing needs the strip cut to length and moved to the print point, and known devices use two conveyors or cannot move the strip back and forth.

**Solution:** A single conveyor moves the check strip end in opposite directions between the cutting point and one or more printing points, shifting it after the cut knife has worked and before the first print.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Check printing unit for registering machines, e.g. B. Cash registers or accounting machines A check printing unit for cash registers is known in which the distance between the printing point and the cutting point is greater than the length of the finished checks issued. In this known device, the end of the check strip is conveyed out and cut off by the length of the check by means of a normal check conveyor device. Then, before the check printing unit has worked, the severed blank check is gripped by a gripping device and fed to the printing point. The known device therefore requires two different conveying devices, each of which must be specially driven and controlled, in order to carry out the required movements. Another cash register printer has a single conveyor present, which moves an inserted note in opposite directions, whereby a section is separated from the inserted slip of paper, the length of which is smaller is than the distance between the print point and the cut point.
