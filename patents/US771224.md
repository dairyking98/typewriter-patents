## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to improvements in type-Writer platen-rollers in which the outer body of the platen or the portion of said body within the type range consists of a material adapted to yield transferable pigment under the force of a percussive blow from the type;

**How it works (reading):** The platen body within the type range is made of a material that yields transferable pigment under the blow of the type, with a sheath that covers it when copying is not wanted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ordinary hard-rubber platen only resists impression and offers no copying; the inventor wants copying properties with easy covering.

**Solution:** The platen body within the type range is made of a material that yields transferable pigment under the blow of the type, with a sheath that covers it when copying is not wanted.
