## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (telegraph control mechanism) · **Subject (patent classes):** Contact operating means

**How it works (reading):** A pair of levers acts as the intermediate link: a cam pushes the two legs apart at right angles to the control movement so that one leg engages or disengages the controlled member.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known telegraph control members that pivot in an intermediate link are complicated and need several parts to be strung together.

**Solution:** A pair of levers acts as the intermediate link: a cam pushes the two legs apart at right angles to the control movement so that one leg engages or disengages the controlled member.
