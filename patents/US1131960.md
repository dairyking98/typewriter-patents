## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Loose paper drums on the platen, makes noise, and gives blurred type impressions.

**Solution:** Auxiliary pressure rolls ahead of the usual rolls, carried on arms of the guide carriers, are spring-pressed to the platen to hug the sheet at the print line.

**How it works:** Arms pivot on the carriers and spring the auxiliary rolls against the platen, adjustable along the platen.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
