## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon on a multigraph drum needs simple automatic reversal of feed direction.

**Solution:** A mechanism inside each ribbon spool is operated by the pull on the ribbon when the spool empties, shifting the feed from one spool to the other.

**How it works:** A rotary type drum carries two ribbon spools in a recess, with the ribbon passing around the drum surface. An eccentric strap moves a bar in and out as the drum rotates, so a double-faced pawl advances one of two spool ratchets. When a spool empties, a clamp mechanism within the spool is pulled by the ribbon end and operates levers at the frame end that shift the bar to feed the other spool. Levers handle both directions.
