## Summary

**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection; Cylindrical Japanese-language typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the Sakai typewriter, in which a type cylinder free to rotate and slide and a paper cylinder free to rotate, slide and swing are mounted at right angles to each other, and printing is done by pressing down a key lever so that the paper cylinder is brought against the type cylinder.

**How it works (reading):** A multi-row type cylinder that is turned and slid by hand on its shaft, with a matching index drum and pointer, and a paper cylinder that is swung against the type cylinder by a key lever, with a weight-driven rise of the paper cylinder for each character space.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A kanji typewriter must print thousands of characters on a body as small as possible.

**Solution:** A multi-row type cylinder that is turned and slid by hand on its shaft, with a matching index drum and pointer, and a paper cylinder that is swung against the type cylinder by a key lever, with a weight-driven rise of the paper cylinder for each character space.

**How it works:** A large type cylinder carries mirror-image characters in relief in rows along its length and around it, and turns and slides on a shaft by a hand wheel and handle. An index drum fixed to the shaft carries the same characters in true form, and a pointer plate on an indicator rod that slides with the cylinder shows through a cut-out the one character at the print position. The paper cylinder in a rocking frame stands at right angles to the type cylinder; pressing a key lever turns a rocking shaft whose coil spring swings a plate, and the frame rocks the paper cylinder against the type cylinder to print through a ribbon. A guide plate and a double-ended detent engage a toothed bar on the frame so that, at the instant neither end holds it, a cord and weight lift the frame one character space. A rack on the rocking shaft meshes with a gear on the type shaft during the stroke to correct small errors of position, and the swinging plate steps the ribbon spool through a pawl and ratchet.

> Header. Patent No. 22193. Class 30. Filed 12 December 1911, granted 27 May 1912 (published in the patent gazette of 14 June 1912). Patentee (inventor): Sakai Yasujirō, residing in East Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, United States of America. Title. Sakai typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to the Sakai typewriter, in which a type cylinder free to rotate and slide and a paper cylinder free to rotate, slide and swing are mounted at right angles to each other, and printing is done by pressing down a key lever so that the paper cylinder is brought against the type cylinder. Its object is to make it suitable as a kanji typewriter by building it so that it can print as many kinds of characters as possible with as small a body as possible.
