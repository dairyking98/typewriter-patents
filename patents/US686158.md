## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement, keyboard locking and bell ringing were not simply or compactly arranged in existing machines.

**Solution:** A type-writer with a compactly arranged escapement housing with its dogs, a bell-hammer and bell-trip, a stop-pawl, and a keyboard-locking mechanism tied to the paper-table.

**How it works:** The escapement dogs sit in a housing; a bell-hammer is tripped near the line end; a stop-pawl and a paper-table lock link to the keyboard lock.

**Features:** [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)

**Parts:** escapement dogs and housing; bell-hammer; bell-trip; stop-pawl; paper-table lock

**Numbers:** US 686,158; 1901-11-05; application filed 1900-07-27
