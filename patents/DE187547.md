## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**How it works (reading):** The spring drum gets low rounded symmetrical teeth, and the ribbon-reversing arm carries a roller that rides over the teeth on return travel and engages to transmit motion only on the forward carriage movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing arms driven from the carriage spring drum move intermittently in one direction and make a lot of noise.

**Solution:** The spring drum gets low rounded symmetrical teeth, and the ribbon-reversing arm carries a roller that rides over the teeth on return travel and engages to transmit motion only on the forward carriage movement.
