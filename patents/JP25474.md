## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The space between impressions on a printing machine that advances the paper cylinder a fixed step could not be changed at will, which matters especially for kanji typewriters; earlier multi-press schemes only suit one machine construction.

**Solution:** Several gears of identical tooth shape but different pitch slide on one shaft, and the press-lever pawl is engaged with whichever gear gives the wanted step, passing its turning to a rack that moves the paper-cylinder frame.

**How it works:** A type cylinder turns and slides on a cross shaft, and a paper cylinder on a right-angle shaft is carried by arms on a sliding frame joined to a press lever, so the paper cylinder turns, slides and rocks to meet the type at one fixed point. A rack on the sliding frame meshes with a gear on a cross shaft; the same shaft carries several sliding gears of different pitch. A pawl rod joined to the press lever rocks a forked pawl; one prong turns the engaged gear at each stroke and the other holds it still otherwise, like a clock escapement. A coil spring returns the rod. Changing which gear the pawl meets changes the step between impressions.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Sakai paper-cylinder feed device. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to the Sakai paper-cylinder feed device for a printing machine that moves the paper cylinder on by a fixed distance with each impression. Many gears, whose teeth are of the same shape but of different pitch, are mounted side by side on one shaft so that they can slide along it; any one of them can be connected with a pawl, and its turning is passed to a rack, which moves the paper cylinder on. The object is a suitable construction by which the space between one impression and the next can be changed at will, especially suited to kanji typewriters. Brief description of the drawings.
