> Description Translated from German Paper guiding device for typewriters. The invention relates to a paper guide device for typewriters, especially those with a Roller frame, in whose upwardly directed end plates the paper roller can be removed is stored. According to the invention it is a matter of a simple lock of the rollers, both ends of the roller shaft against a vertical one Movement and one end secured against longitudinal displacement, to for what purpose arms are hinged to the end plates of a paper setting scale, of which one arm engages in a groove in the shaft of the roller, while the the other arm rests on the shaft. Another characteristic is that on one of the end plates an arm is articulated with a hook via a pin on one arm of the End plate engages and holds this in engagement with the roller shaft.
