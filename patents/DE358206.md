## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier on the paper apron concentric with the roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide device for typewriters, especially those with a Roller frame, in whose upwardly directed end plates the paper roller can be removed is stored.

**How it works (reading):** The roller axle is notched at both ends and seated in bearings on slotted end plates, locked by hinged arms on a paper-setting scale, one arm engaging a groove in the axle and the other resting on it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter paper roller must be removable yet held securely against vertical movement and sideways displacement.

**Solution:** The roller axle is notched at both ends and seated in bearings on slotted end plates, locked by hinged arms on a paper-setting scale, one arm engaging a groove in the axle and the other resting on it.
