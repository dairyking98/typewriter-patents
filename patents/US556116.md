## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to automaticallyoperated brushes or type-cleaners for typewriting machines;

**How it works (reading):** A brush swept around the type basket by ratchet and pawl, and struck against the type faces, both driven by the space-bar or another moving part.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A brush for type cleaning must strike the types one at a time or in small groups, not all together.

**Solution:** A brush swept around the type basket by ratchet and pawl, and struck against the type faces, both driven by the space-bar or another moving part.
