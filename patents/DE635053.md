## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the bearing piece for receiving the switching device for the paper trolley of type lever typewriters, where a ratchet body swings across the paper rack.

**How it works (reading):** A stamped part joins the intermediate-lever bearing casting to the key-lever bearing rail, holds the switch-lock pivots and the rocker bearing points, and acts as a stiffening bridge that fixes the distance between bearing points.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cast parts replacing sheet metal need a stiff, precisely placed bearing for the paper-carriage switching device and the key levers, and the switch lock must be easy to reach.

**Solution:** A stamped part joins the intermediate-lever bearing casting to the key-lever bearing rail, holds the switch-lock pivots and the rocker bearing points, and acts as a stiffening bridge that fixes the distance between bearing points.
