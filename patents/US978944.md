## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bar pivots must be tight yet free-moving, wear in them must be compensated, and the type bars tend to be bent or thrown out of true.

**Solution:** A type bar pivot and bearing construction held in proper working tension, with automatic wear compensation and a pivot that can be oiled.
