## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**How it works (reading):** The card slot leads into a cavity inside the roller, with adjustable depth stops and resilient holders, so card tongues stay inside the roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Index card tongues catch on the paper feed and guides and get torn.

**Solution:** The card slot leads into a cavity inside the roller, with adjustable depth stops and resilient holders, so card tongues stay inside the roller.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** roller sleeve; roller heads; depth stop; resilient holders; card slot
