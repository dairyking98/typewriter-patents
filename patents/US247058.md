## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Banks and stores need a small-footprint machine to register times, amounts and other information on money received and paid.

**Solution:** A dial-and-pointer type wheel turned by a single stem knob, which is pushed in to print and pulled out to space the paper cylinder.

**How it works:** A ring-shaped case, hinged on a post so it can swing away from the platen, carries a lettered dial, a pointer on a vertical shaft and a type wheel on the shaft's lower end. A radial stem with a finger knob turns the shaft through bevel pinions, so the pointer shows on the dial which type is under the impression plate. Pushing the stem inward drives a cone against a spring-held plate that forces the selected type down onto the paper; the types ride in slots in the wheel and an elastic ring lifts them back. Drawing the stem out lets the plate rise and, through a lever with an incline, steps a pawl on a ratchet that turns a pinion and racks the paper-cylinder slide one space along. For a new line the cylinder is turned by its knob and the slide returned. An ink roller on a friction-held arm sits under the wheel.
