## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A visible-writing machine with vertical-circle typebars needs a light key touch with true vertical key motion, and a shifting typebar basket must not misalign under heavy touch.

**Solution:** Intermediate levers pivoted midway link vertical-moving key levers to the radial typebars, and an automatic lock holds the shifting basket while a key is depressed.

**How it works:** A vertically shifting basket holds typebars in an arc of a vertical circle between division plates, pivoted on a rod, with typebar blocks carrying caps and lower-case types. Levers pivoted at their middle have inner ends socketed to the typebar pivot ends, with outer ends joined to the key levers by wire links staggered to clear each other. The key levers move truly vertically. The pivots lie on a cylinder surface whose axis passes through the printing point. A lock on the universal feed bar holds the basket against shift while a key is down.
