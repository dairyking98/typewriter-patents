## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A carriage-return lever attached to the carriage runs down and forward under the frame to a grip within reach of the thumb at the keyboard, optionally combined with a line-feed lever system.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage and advancing the line requires the typist to reach up to the carriage-mounted line-space lever or platen knob, slowing writing, especially blind typing, and line feed is normally possible only at the line start.

**Solution:** A carriage-return lever attached to the carriage runs down and forward under the frame to a grip within reach of the thumb at the keyboard, optionally combined with a line-feed lever system.

**How it works:** An arm fixed to the rear of the carriage is led down between the machine feet below the frame and runs forward as a flat bar or rail. It is guided on a roller in the U-profile of the front frame and ends in an upward grip near the space bar. At line end the carriage sits mid-machine so the grip is reachable; the right thumb pushes it back to the line start while the fingers stay on the keys. A lever system carried along on this mechanism can also advance the line at any point in the line. A two-part lever variant is shown.
