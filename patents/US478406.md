## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cleaning brushes must attach easily, work on round or oval type sets, and keep the fingers clean.

**Solution:** A circular brush on a jointed shank with a locking sleeve, tilted up into the type-bars where the machine requires it.
