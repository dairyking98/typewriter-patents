## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column stops cannot be kept set when the work changes to a new column arrangement, and unused stops cannot be easily cleared or made inoperative.

**Solution:** A column stop bar and stop-set and clearing devices that keep the set column stops in place and let unused stops be readily cleared so they are rendered inoperative.

**How it works:** Column stops are carried on a column stop bar in the carriage travel path. Stop-setting devices hold the chosen stops, and separate clearing devices render unneeded stops inoperative so the column arrangement can be changed without disturbing the rest. Described as applicable to several typewriter forms.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** column stop bar; column stops; stop set device; clearing device; carriage

**Improves on:**

- column stops that cannot be retained or cleared easily: stop-set and clearing devices keep and disable column stops
