## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Known tab bar riders are too tall in construction and do not suspend well on the bar.

**Solution:** A forked spring rider with a grip hole has its slot opening into the grip hole, giving a short height and springy grip across the bottom of the tab bar.
