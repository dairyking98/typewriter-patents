## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanisms for front-strike shifting-platen machines needed improvement, in particular using the upper or lower edge portion of the ribbon at the printing point.

**Solution:** A hand-operated actuating link controls the ribbon vibrator so that either the upper or lower edge portion of the ribbon is presented at the printing point.

**How it works:** A front-strike machine has typebars carrying upper and lower case types on a stationary segment, and a platen shifting in a carrier on the carriage. The ribbon is fed lengthwise from the letter-feed mechanism by a link. A ribbon operating lever and vibrator controlling means, set by hand through an actuating link, determine which edge portion of the ribbon meets the platen when the type strikes.
