## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to pro vide means for back spacing said carriage when necessary for corrections.

**How it works (reading):** A removable carriage base is locked by a screw in a cross bar, with back-spacing and locking mechanisms, a pivoted line finder that can be thrown against the platen, and an adjustable paper support with a margin gauge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must be removable yet locked during transport, must back-space for corrections, and needs a usable line finder and margin gauge.

**Solution:** A removable carriage base is locked by a screw in a cross bar, with back-spacing and locking mechanisms, a pivoted line finder that can be thrown against the platen, and an adjustable paper support with a margin gauge.
