## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** Two nested sleeves with ring flanges, a coil spring and cork insert form the foot; a single sheet-metal body lets the plate tilt and absorb side loads.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter noise and shock pass into the table through rigid feet.

**Solution:** Two nested sleeves with ring flanges, a coil spring and cork insert form the foot; a single sheet-metal body lets the plate tilt and absorb side loads.
