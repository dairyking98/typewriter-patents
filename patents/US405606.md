## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Remington typewriters of the day used a long feed roll with rubber bands, and a stationary scale board, to hold and feed paper.

**Solution:** A pivoted gravity paper-holding device replaces the feed roll, holds single or manifold sheets on the platen, and carries a scale board.

**How it works:** On the Remington, a gravity paper holder is pivoted in a carrier on the carriage and rests on the platen with anti-friction rollers in adjustable stops, so sheets and carbons pass under it at even pressure. The scale board on its lower side lets the operator align the carriage to insert or strike out a letter. Adjustable stops take paper of varying width and limit line length, and an automatic alarm and a raisable carriage for inspecting the work are also described (text cut off).
