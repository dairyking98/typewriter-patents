## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to produce in a type-writing machinean arrangement whereby I can write Words consisting of two or more letters, as well as single letters, at one uniform stroke of the key, said machine automatically spacing one, two, three, or more spaces, according to the number of spaces required for the word written.

**How it works (reading):** Word keys on type-bars with types for whole words, with a stop-guide of steps and grooves for a limber pawl and a dog-rocker with arms of different lengths giving differential leverage for 1, 2, 3 or 4 spaces.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters write single letters only; the inventor wants words of two or more letters printed in one stroke with automatic corresponding spacing.

**Solution:** Word keys on type-bars with types for whole words, with a stop-guide of steps and grooves for a limber pawl and a dog-rocker with arms of different lengths giving differential leverage for 1, 2, 3 or 4 spaces.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 2, key-levers are supported by hinged frames, one set each for 1-, 2-, 3- and 4-letter keys, and connected by rods to a dog-rocker with arms of different lengths. A longer arm gives a longer leverage for single letters; shorter arms yield smaller leverage for longer words, so the feed travel differs. A limber pawl moves sideways against a stepped stop-guide with returning grooves, which controls how far it advances and returns to the ratchet bar. The carriage then spaces by the number of letters in the word.

**Parts:** key-lever; type-bar; supporting-frame; rod; dog-rocker; limber pawl; stop-guide; ratchet bar; spring-lever
