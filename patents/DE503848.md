## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing tabulator for punched cards)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to and aims at a printing tabulator an improvement to the printing mechanism of such machines, especially when controlled by scoring cards should be that have a very large number of hole gaps.

**How it works (reading):** Columns are sensed one after another, but several sensing points work at once, feeding several groups of typewriter-style printing mechanisms.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card-controlled printing tabulators sense columns in sequence, which limits operating speed.

**Solution:** Columns are sensed one after another, but several sensing points work at once, feeding several groups of typewriter-style printing mechanisms.
