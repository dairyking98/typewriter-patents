## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rod-shaped type levers bend under load.

**Solution:** A V-shaped type lever framed as a triangle, with pivot pins at the leg ends, is stiff against bending; conical collars allow easy insertion into the bearings.
