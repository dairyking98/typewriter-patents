## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operating the tabulator required the hand to leave the keyboard.

**Solution:** An auxiliary tabulating key at the front of the keyboard, beside the space bar, is pivoted and linked to the tabulator key lever so the wrist or hand can operate it without moving off the keyboard.
