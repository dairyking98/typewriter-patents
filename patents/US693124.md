## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A copy-holder on a typewriter must be removed to fit the machine cover.

**Solution:** A copy-tablet hinged on a bail is held above the machine in front of the operator by a catch lever, and it folds down without detaching so the cover can be fitted.
