## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A continuous paper roll on the reciprocating carriage adds its weight to the carriage and makes the machine harder to operate.

**Solution:** The roll is held apart from the carriage, and guiding arms secured at the rear of the carriage feed the paper to the platen so the carriage does not carry the roll's weight.
