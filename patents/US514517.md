## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Particles of ink or other matter from the ribbon fall or are thrown onto the paper; the opening begins mid-sentence, so the exact problem statement is lost.

**Solution:** A detachable shield of rubber, silk, cloth, paper or membrane in a carrier is held over the ribbon beneath the platen and periodically moved by a pawl and ratchet so the type always strikes a fresh spot.

**How it works:** On a Caligraph, a rectangular sheet-metal carrier with a large central opening holds a shield slightly larger than the opening, secured by two pivoted spring arms whose perforations press the shield over small pins on the frame, with no puncturing. The carrier's tongue rests on a leaf spring at the front, and its rear shank is on a crank or eccentric on a vertical shaft with a ratchet wheel. A pawl on the carriage driving arm turns the ratchet at each carriage return, shifting the shield slightly to present a fresh spot and prevent wear and holes.
