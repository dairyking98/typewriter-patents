## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A flat-platen machine needs a quick temporary shift off the line for superscripts or interlineation.

**Solution:** A device that momentarily moves the platen, the track frame or the writing mechanism out of its aligned line and back.

**How it works:** Several forms shift the track frame, the racks that carry the writing mechanism, or the platen relative to the writing mechanism. A lever with block and locking latch gives the temporary offset between regular line spaces. It can be combined with a fine-line adjusting device so a single mechanism gives both.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** track frame; platen; rack; gear; operating lever; block; locking latch; tooth
