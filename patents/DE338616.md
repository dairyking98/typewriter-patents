## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Driving the universal rail suddenly on key press causes a shock and noise, while the earlier rolling-surface fix was noisy and wore quickly.

**Solution:** The universal rail stays coupled to its drive links and is linked to the paper carriage switch and ribbon drive so the pivots lie in a straight line, letting the rail move empty at first and then pick up speed gradually.
