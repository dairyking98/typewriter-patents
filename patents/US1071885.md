## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Methods or arrangements for marking the record carrier in digital fashion otherwise than by punching by printing code marks

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter with two sets of numeral keys and type bars for tens and units, no letter spacing, and a line feed key that winds the printed strip on a spool.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Weaving designers need a typewriter that prints numbers in a column on a narrow telltale strip.

**Solution:** A typewriter with two sets of numeral keys and type bars for tens and units, no letter spacing, and a line feed key that winds the printed strip on a spool.
