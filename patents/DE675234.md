## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for aligning and fixing the type bodies on the type levers of typewriters and the like, with a support surface corresponding to the type curvature for the types, a straightening element for aligning the baseline and a pivoting straightening element to adjust the vertical position of the types.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoting directional piece engages the slot of the type body and the type guide head to set lateral alignment; the type body is then clamped in place and the alignment members removed before soldering.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type bodies are hard to align and fix exactly to the type levers, and soldering or staining ruins the alignment links.

**Solution:** A pivoting directional piece engages the slot of the type body and the type guide head to set lateral alignment; the type body is then clamped in place and the alignment members removed before soldering.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
