## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Silencers and shock absorbers for typewriters. The invention relates based on a sound and shock absorber for typewriters, which consists of a housing consists of a ribbon spring on which the typewriter base rests. The invention consists in that the annular band spring on one on the bottom of the housing stationary disc is attached and carries a support plate above. This achieves that a paralyzing of the pen is almost impossible and the vibrations as well as the hard strokes of the machine, which occur while writing, directly on the Base plate are transferred downwards so that lateral moments do not occur. The invention is illustrated in the drawing, namely: FIG. 1 shows a vertical section through the device and FIG. 2 shows a top view. The round ribbon spring b, which is provided at the top with a support surface c for the typewriter base, is attached to a disk d resting on the bottom e of the wooden housing a. The vibrations when writing are thus transmitted directly down to the base plate.
