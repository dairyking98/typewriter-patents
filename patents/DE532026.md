## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The friction arm is mounted on the machine frame and swings out by friction on the rotating spring barrel of the carriage return, holding the escapement tooth out of the way for the duration of the return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The switch-lock tooth on a typewriter rattles over the escapement teeth during carriage return, and the friction arm on the switch lock adds load to a light machine.

**Solution:** The friction arm is mounted on the machine frame and swings out by friction on the rotating spring barrel of the carriage return, holding the escapement tooth out of the way for the duration of the return.

**How it works:** As the carriage returns, the escapement wheel and spring barrel turn; a friction rod on the barrel swings an arm that engages the switch tooth and holds it withdrawn until the return ends.
