## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Foldable typewriters

**How it works (reading):** The side walls of the front key frame carry flanges that serve as feet resting on the rear frame's upper edge when folded.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A folding typewriter's keypad must rest safely clear of the carriage when folded; swinging support frames on the paper table are noisy, costly and weak.

**Solution:** The side walls of the front key frame carry flanges that serve as feet resting on the rear frame's upper edge when folded.

**How it works:** The machine frame has a main member carrying the working parts and a hinged key member. The key member's side walls are joined by a cross strut and have flanges on the upper part. When the key member is folded over the paper roller the flanges rest as feet on the upper edge of the main member's side walls.

**Features:** [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** main frame member; hinged key member; side wall; flange; cross strut

**Improves on:**

- earlier collapsible Corona machines: no swinging support frame on the paper table
