## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (show-card and ticket printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Toy typewriters

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a machine designed to print show-cards, priectickets, 870.

**How it works (reading):** A printing wheel on a yoke carries two sets of characters on opposite faces, and turning the yoke presents either face to print.
