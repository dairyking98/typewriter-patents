**How it works (reading):** The upper angled drive member works through a straight slot in a radius from the fulcrum, and a swing weight lever links it to the typebar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar drives with stationary plain bearings need a more even stop and simpler circuit arrangement.

**Solution:** The upper angled drive member works through a straight slot in a radius from the fulcrum, and a swing weight lever links it to the typebar.

**How it works:** The slot lets the drive pin move so the pushing members follow the swing weight lever through the free lift.
