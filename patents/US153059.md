## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check amounts printed by punching can be changed by altering the face of the check.

**Solution:** The numerals are cut bodily out of the check by male dies on a numerically indexed slide, moved by rack and pinion and driven through female dies by a vertical plunger, and the paper is advanced by a ratchet and pawl.
