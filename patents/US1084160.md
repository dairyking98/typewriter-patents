## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A quiet, accessible escapement for a typewriter whose carriage is raised by the shift is wanted, driven by both print keys and the space bar.

**Solution:** A cam-lever-driven escapement wheel that steps the platen carriage once per key or space bar, working at any carriage elevation.

**How it works:** Thin flat typebars rest on a felt buffer and are linked to a rocking bar centred by a spring on a rod, one end strut filled with non-resonant material. Key depression extends a spring and moves the bar to throw the typebar. An escapement wheel under the carriage is stepped by a cam lever, with separate actuation from the printing keys and the spacing key.
