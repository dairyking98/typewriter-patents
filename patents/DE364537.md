**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line delimiter for typewriters in which the paper trolley as a whole has a switching movement executes transversely to its longitudinal direction, to the difference from those where only the platen has such a switching movement.

**How it works (reading):** The line-start and line-end stops, which move with the carriage, act on a sheet-metal angle lever that is pivoted on a fixed part and drives a locking rod, so the two stops work correctly at any carriage switch position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the whole paper carriage shifts across the machine for line switching, the line-start and line-end stops on the carriage and on the frame lose their relationship, and adding fixtures would make the machine heavier.

**Solution:** The line-start and line-end stops, which move with the carriage, act on a sheet-metal angle lever that is pivoted on a fixed part and drives a locking rod, so the two stops work correctly at any carriage switch position.
