## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case shifting by moving the carriage and platen strains the key bars and needs much mechanism; each typebar must carry two characters.

**Solution:** Shift by moving the lower ends of the typebar levers sideways so each typebar carries just one character.

**How it works:** Transverse perforated bars slide on flanges in slots, linked by rods to a plate with arms and rocking levers. Moving the bars shifts the lower ends of the typebar levers slightly so a different set of characters is selected, instead of moving the platen carriage. This lets each typebar carry a single character. The opening is cut off before the full linkage.

- platen-shift typewriters: shifts the key bar ends rather than the carriage
