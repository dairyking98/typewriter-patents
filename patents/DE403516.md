## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a device on typewriters in which the vehicle locking device by the space bar regardless of the drive caused by the type keys in Activity is set, so that when you hit the space bar, the ribbon mechanism remains inactive.

**How it works (reading):** The space bar rear end forms a horizontally guided slide with a head that swings the pawl carrier of the ribbon-lift lock; an adjustable screw sleeve sets the stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage locking device must be engaged by the space bar with reliable, low-friction linkage.

**Solution:** The space bar rear end forms a horizontally guided slide with a head that swings the pawl carrier of the ribbon-lift lock; an adjustable screw sleeve sets the stroke.
