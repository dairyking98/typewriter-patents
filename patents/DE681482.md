## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements to attach the ribbon to the spool

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to fastening devices of the tape end: on tape reels, in particular the inner ribbon end on ink ribbon reels of typewriters, where on the spools two fitting sleeve-shaped Body are arranged, between the wall parts of the tape end for the purpose of his Attachment is clamped and where longitudinal slots are provided through the the tape exits from the attachment point to the outside.

**How it works (reading):** Two sleeves sit on opposite spool flanges with play; twisting the flanges clamps the ribbon end, so moulded thermoplastic parts can be used.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon end fastening on spools needs spring steel and precision work because both sleeves sit on one side.

**Solution:** Two sleeves sit on opposite spool flanges with play; twisting the flanges clamps the ribbon end, so moulded thermoplastic parts can be used.
