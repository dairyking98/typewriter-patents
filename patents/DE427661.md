**How it works (reading):** One turn of a knob swings a double-armed lever into an annular groove on a body bolt to block transverse movement, while another arm presses the escapement release lever to block longitudinal movement and release the escapement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Transport locking held only the longitudinal carriage movement, so transverse switching needed extra hand movements.

**Solution:** One turn of a knob swings a double-armed lever into an annular groove on a body bolt to block transverse movement, while another arm presses the escapement release lever to block longitudinal movement and release the escapement.
