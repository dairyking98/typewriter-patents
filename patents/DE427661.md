> Description Translated from German Device for locking paper trolleys, in particular portable typewriters. The invention relates to a device for locking the paper carriage, in particular of portable typewriters to protect them from damage during transport. In the previously known devices, which the determination of the Purpose of the paper wagon to secure against damage, the wagon was mostly only secured in its longitudinal movement. Where the transverse movement (switching) is also determined special hand movements are necessary for this. According to the invention, the paper trolley both in terms of its longitudinal movement and in terms of its transverse movement detected with a single movement and at the same time triggered the escapement.
