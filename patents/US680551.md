## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage had to stop at a set point, and the sheet was damaged while the carriage was locked.

**Solution:** An adjustable surface on the feed drum stops the carriage at a chosen point of travel and protects the sheet.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** feed drum; adjustable surface; carriage
