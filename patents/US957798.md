## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The copy being typed from should come into view line by line in front of the operator.

**Solution:** A copy-holder roller on an upright frame is advanced by a pawl and ratchet, with an adjustable shield setting the advance to one, two or three lines and independent of the platen.
