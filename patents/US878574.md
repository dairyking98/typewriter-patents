## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon field change should revert automatically when carriage position passes.

**Solution:** Shifting members change the ribbon field while engaged and restore the first field when they disengage.

**How it works:** One or more members on the carriage co-act with a fixed one at adjustable carriage positions. While they engage the second ribbon field is used; on disengaging, it returns. Fields can shift several times in one pass but not on carriage return. Monarch front-strike.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; vibratory ribbon carrier; carriage shifting member; fixed shifting member

**Improves on:**

- US878573 / application 332,949: automatic re-shift back to first field
