## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon vibrator must be settable to any zone of a multi-zone ribbon or removed from printing for stencils.

**Solution:** A suspended link with three engagement surfaces at different distances is raised or lowered by push-pin buttons to choose the vibrator's throw.

**How it works:** The universal bar drives a horizontal link suspended by parallel links from the carriage. One side of an opening in the link has three surfaces at different distances from the ends, and the vertical position of the link's support chooses which engages a lateral projection on the ribbon-vibrator bell crank. Surface 19 is placed so the bell crank does not move at all (stencil). A spring holds the guide down against an adjustable stop screw, and push-pin buttons marked Top, Bottom, Stencil set it with a detent holding the position.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; bell crank; suspended link; stop screw; spring; universal bar; push-pin button; detent
