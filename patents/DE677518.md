## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings therefor

**How it works (reading):** Soft springy rollers of larger diameter provide friction, while smaller hard rollers coaxial with them carry the spring pressure, so the soft rollers are not over-pressed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber pressure rollers on a platen are permanently loaded by springs, so soft rubber flattens and hard rubber gives too little grip.

**Solution:** Soft springy rollers of larger diameter provide friction, while smaller hard rollers coaxial with them carry the spring pressure, so the soft rollers are not over-pressed.
