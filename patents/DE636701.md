## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (direct summation printing on registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This purpose is a Note sensor provided, which in various known machines only during of the machine aisle is moved into the document guide.

**How it works (reading):** A locking member in the pressure frame, moved by the feeler lever, engages a slot unless a slip is in the document guide, so the printing frame cannot work without a slip.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In direct total printing, an operator who forgets to insert a slip loses the sum, since the adder is reset after the print.

**Solution:** A locking member in the pressure frame, moved by the feeler lever, engages a slot unless a slip is in the document guide, so the printing frame cannot work without a slip.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
