**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to secure a simple, efficient, and easily-manipulated machine, together with other advantages hereinafter set forth.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wanted a simple, easily manipulated typewriter in which pressing a key automatically selects, prints and spaces by electromagnetic power.

**Solution:** A type-wheel that is rotated to the chosen character by a reciprocating rack frame whose stroke is limited by key-selected stops, then locked and printed, driven electrically.

**How it works:** Letters and capitals are interspaced round the rim of a type-wheel that turns and slides on a shaft carried in a frame. Pressing a key closes the circuit of an electromagnetic actuator that draws a rack frame forward; gears on the wheel shaft mesh with the racks. The frame's stop plate is oblique with notches, and a key throws a stop across its path, so the stroke length depends on the key and rotates the wheel to the matching angle. The stop plate then shoves a yielding bar that works a swinging stop locking the wheel while printing. After printing the wheel is released, and an intermittent screw shaft feeds the wheel frame sideways to the next letter position.
