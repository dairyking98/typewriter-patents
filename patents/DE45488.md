## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter that is easy to operate with little force and lets the writer read the finished line at all times.

**Solution:** Typebars hinged in a circular bearing strike downward onto a ribbon over a paper-bearing membrane on a spring-driven cart.

**How it works:** Key levers, sprung, pull typebars (pivoted in slots of a circular bearing) down so the type strikes through a ribbon against the paper. The paper rests on a leather membrane stretched in a frame carried on a rolling cart; a toothed rod on the frame is advanced by a pawl lever for line spacing, with an adjustable stop screw and a lever for a double line space. Lateral cart motion is driven by a spring through a cord and a non-circular disk (fusee-like) and checked by a rack and shift-lever locking device, released for the new line. Only the opening is available.
