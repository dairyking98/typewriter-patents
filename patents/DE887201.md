That is Of great importance not only for transportable machines, but also for machines of all kinds, as the weight reduction means wear and tear on the bearing points is reduced, the handling of the machine is facilitated and the construction is simplified can be. Another purpose of the invention is to enable the manufacture of the Cheaper levers. Another very important purpose is through appropriate Shape of the lever profile a better construction of office machines, especially typewriters, to enable. According to the invention, the lever consists of a substantial part of it Length from a hollow profile, preferably from a profile that has the shape of a long-legged U has closed at the top.
