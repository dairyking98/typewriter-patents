## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Solid levers are heavy, which wears bearings and makes machines heavy and costly.

**Solution:** The lever is a light hollow profile of thin sheet steel, shaped like a long-legged U with upturned flanges and embossed ribs, with openings in the profile.
