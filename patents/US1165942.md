## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tall characters (e.g. Sanskrit signs) in the upper case are not printed by the normal ribbon lift.

**Solution:** An extra ribbon lift triggered only for type levers with high signs, also usable to select the second colour of a bichrome ribbon.

**How it works:** Normal ribbon lift follows the universal bar through a bell crank and bar to the ribbon guide. Type levers for high signs carry a tension bar whose front end rises when the platen is shifted, so a striking pin releases a stop and the bar gets extra travel, raising the ribbon further.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [USE:special-script](../features/use-special-script.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** universal bar; bell crank; ribbon guide; tension bar; striking pin; stop; locking pin

**Improves on:**

- fixed-stroke ribbon lift: variable lift per character
