## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing unit)

**How it works (reading):** The sum pull lever switches the conveyor to long feed even while the offset or addition key remains depressed, so the offset key can stay held through the total.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a cash register the conveyor stays on short feed while an offsetting key is held, so when the sum is pulled with that key still down the conveyor does not switch to long feed.

**Solution:** The sum pull lever switches the conveyor to long feed even while the offset or addition key remains depressed, so the offset key can stay held through the total.

**How it works:** During item entry the conveyor is held on short feed by the depressed key; the sum-pull lever overrides that and switches to long feed.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** offsetting key; sum pull lever; conveyor; business type keys; clerk keys
