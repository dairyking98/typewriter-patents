## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of this invention is to provide a device which shall be affixed to applied to an Underwood front strike writing machine, the paper-holding fingers being in operative position.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed card holders hide part of the writing line; removable ones get lost.

**Solution:** Paper-holding fingers collapse telescopically on guides fixed to the machine, normally below the line and pulled up over the printing line when needed.

**How it works:** Guides rise from the ribbon-carrier guard plate in front of the platen. Fingers with convex faces slide on the guides; finger pieces pull them up to above the highest case position so they press stiff cards against the platen. Stops limit travel. Pushing them down leaves the writing line clear.
