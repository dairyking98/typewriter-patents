## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage feeding mechanisms of typewriters needed improvement.

**Solution:** A hinged rack-bar meshing with a pinion on a shaft whose ratchet wheel is stepped by two escapement dogs.

**How it works:** The carriage rides on a hinge and guide rail. A vertical rack-bar hinged to the carriage engages a pinion on a shaft set at right angles; at the other end of the shaft is a larger ratchet wheel engaged alternately by two dogs on a rocking dog-holder. A cross-bar and wires connect the holder to the universal bar under the key-levers, so each keystroke steps the carriage one tooth.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** carriage; rack-bar; pinion; shaft; ratchet wheel; escapement dog; dog-holder; universal bar; guide rail; key-lever
