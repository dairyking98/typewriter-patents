## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters for ciphering or deciphering cryptographic text wherein the operative connections between the keys and the type-bars are automatically and continuously permuted, during operation, by a coding or key member the connections being mechanical

**How it works (reading):** Two type cylinders on one axis print plain text and cipher text at the same time, the second cylinder being turned irregularly relative to the first by a pin disc and tooth sectors.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing cipher typewriters offer few adjustment combinations, always shift the wheel in the same sense so a cipher text cannot be decoded with the same setup, and give no check on the written cipher.

**Solution:** Two type cylinders on one axis print plain text and cipher text at the same time, the second cylinder being turned irregularly relative to the first by a pin disc and tooth sectors.

**How it works:** Two type cylinders sit on a common shaft that a key mechanism slides lengthwise to select the character. Cylinder 1 is fixed to the shaft; cylinder 2 turns freely between two discs and a spring pawl couples it to the shaft, releasing it when the shaft shifts. A spring-returned lever carries a sector disc with two toothed arcs that alternately engage a gear on cylinder 2, driven through a chain and sector from a disc whose adjustable pins are in several concentric circles. The cylinder is advanced by a variable amount at each stroke, every second stroke or at chosen places, giving copy in plain and cipher together. Decoding needs only a change of the racks. The cut text ends in mid-mechanism.
