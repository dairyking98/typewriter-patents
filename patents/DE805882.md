## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (rail anchor clamp) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers; Rail anchors; Fastening or restraining methods by plastic or elastic deformation of fastener

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to some improved rail anchors from a leg bent at one end into a hook, on which the hook the upper and lower clamping jaws, which are pushed across a rail in this way be that they grasp the top and bottom of the rail foot with a clamping effect.

**How it works (reading):** Material is redistributed so the tension side is larger than the compression side, with a flat contact surface against the sleeper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rail anchors tear on the tension side of the hook when the hook is bent open.

**Solution:** Material is redistributed so the tension side is larger than the compression side, with a flat contact surface against the sleeper.
