## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Hammond machine needed quick change between two languages or type styles without removing shuttles.

**Solution:** Two shuttles sit on the anvil, one active and one parked, and a lifting mechanism swaps which is engaged.

**How it works:** The anvil has two guide slits, front and rear, each with a shuttle whose bushing is driven either by the shuttle arm pin or held by a stationary shuttle-lock bracket. Radial arms on the anvil shaft lift the shuttles to exchange them, and a locating pin locks the second position. The active shuttle is positioned by the keys as normal.
