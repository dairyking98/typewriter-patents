## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier electric key drives needed a contact pen held in the hand or separate contact buttons, which slowed or complicated writing.

**Solution:** Fit the key buttons with contact plates bridged by metal thimbles on the writer's fingers so the lightest touch energises an electromagnet.

**How it works:** Each key button carries two contact plates, one connected to the key lever and one to a line leading to an electromagnet. A metal thimble on the finger bridges the plates when the button is touched, closing the circuit from battery through the lever and magnet. The magnet attracts an armature on the key lever and pulls it fully down to print the type in the usual way.
