> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE. The invention relates to a key drive for typewriters and similar machines and aims easier and faster writing. This is achieved with the help of Electricity. Devices for driving keys for typewriters are already known, in which the electric current is used as an aid. With these well-known ίο devices, the person uses one Contact pen with which the key is touched or the person writing moves individual contact buttons. The first method is the writing speed considerably reduced compared to the machines with usual key drives. The second method requires more work than the first, since the contact buttons must make a movement to make contact. All these shortcomings are avoided in the subject matter of the invention in that the Key buttons wear contact pieces, which through on the fingers of the person writing inserted metal caps are bridged. The slightest touch with the is enough Button to effect the power supply. The tedious task of holding a contact pin is a thing of the past away here. The invention is illustrated in the drawing.
