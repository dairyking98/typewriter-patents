## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A machine is wanted to press steel letters into a mass (embossing) or serve as a powerful typewriter and copier, with quick change of script.

**Solution:** Interchangeable letter discs carrying spring-mounted steel letters on their rims hang from a sliding rail, and a key-selected disc rotation brings the chosen letter under a cam-driven embossing punch.

**How it works:** Keys marked with letters, plus a letter-shift key and a blank key for spaces, operate lever mechanisms. Each key lever has a lug that falls into a step of a staircase-profile roller (36 steps for 36 characters), so the drop depth, and hence the disc drive, differs per character. Seven letter discs with different alphabets hang from a segment-shaped sliding rail and are moved under the embossing mechanism and fixed with a small spring lever in a notch. The steel letters on the disc edge move down elastically but protrude upward, and a spring-loaded punch with a pin, tripped by a notched cam disc, hits the set letter to the same depth every time. Key levers also turn a toothed cylinder through change gears and bevel gears to advance the setting plate by the letter width, while a crank and screw spindle set line spacing. It can be foot operated with a heavy flywheel or motor driven.
