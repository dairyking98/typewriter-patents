## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**How it works (reading):** A felt-faced type-basket or type-bar support with a pad reduces type-bar rebound to a minimum so that adjacent bars do not conflict.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars in segmental arrangements clash with the bars next to them near the basket because of rebound.

**Solution:** A felt-faced type-basket or type-bar support with a pad reduces type-bar rebound to a minimum so that adjacent bars do not conflict.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md), [Rebound and clash prevention](../features/bar-rebound.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type-basket; felt pad; type-bars in segment; draw-link; key-levers; sublevers
