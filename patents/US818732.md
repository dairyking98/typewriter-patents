## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Horizontal or vertical ruling lines are wanted from a type-writer, preferably in several colours.

**Solution:** A ruling device with a stem is seated in the socket of a type-bar head in place of a type, and an inking device on the ruling head can supply colour.
