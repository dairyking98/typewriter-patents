## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printed characters on a sheet are seen only by those close by; the inventor wants them shown on a distant screen as they are produced.

**Solution:** A machine prints characters on transparent sheet and projects them by lantern onto a screen; letter and line spacing devices are shown in the drawings.
