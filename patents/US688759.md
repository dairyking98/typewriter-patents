**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writers, and particularly to the keys thereof, and has for its object to provide an arrangement of keys which will enable the operator to readily find the keys and to operate them with a very slight movement of the fingers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators must find keys and strike them with much finger movement, so the keyboard is slow to use.

**Solution:** A 28-key layout of twelve vertically movable keys plus forward- and rearward-movable keys with raised projections, so a finger flex selects a shifted key without leaving its home key.

**How it works:** Vertical keys sit under the thumbs and fingers; forward or backward movers are reached by flexing a finger, and they drive the impression mechanism through link connections.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)

**Numbers:** 28 keys
