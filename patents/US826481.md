## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools need to wind and reverse with slack taken up and the ribbon yielding under the type impact.

**Solution:** Spools lock to their cores with a rolling ball wedged in a crescent chamber while a spring lets the ribbon give, and a pawl-and-ratchet drive winds each spool.

**How it works:** Each spool shaft carries a ratchet wheel inside a head, engaged by a pawl that a broken annulus can disengage. A ball in a crescent-shaped opening wedges to lock the spool to its core when winding, and rolls free to allow manual slack take-up. A coiled spring lets the ribbon yield at each impact to save wear. Opening only, details truncated.
