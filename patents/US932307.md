## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Margin clamps must be carried away from the platen to clear the printing point, which needs a special rocking support.

**Solution:** A rocking slotted cylinder below the print line carries the margin clamps away from the platen, driven by the feed-roll release lever through a slotted link.
