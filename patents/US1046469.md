## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the Oliver platen-shift frame, the depending arm and the suspension link can flex toward each other near their upper ends and rub, which a simple spacing washer does not prevent.

**Solution:** A spacing plate with bent lugs, rotatably held on the pivot screw, sits between the arm and link along their whole length and keeps them apart.

**How it works:** The shift-frame carrying the paper carriage hangs from links at each end, so it can swing forward or back from a central position when a shift lever is depressed. A plate is mounted on the lower pivot screw and extends upward between the depending arm and the suspension link. Two pairs of outward-bent lugs on its edges engage the lateral edges of the arm and link, so the plate cannot swing round the pivot. The lugs also hold the link upright while the shift-frame is assembled.
