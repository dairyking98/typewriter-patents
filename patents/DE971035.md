## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an additional device for typewriters for writing on paper webs, the one made up of a row of sheets lying on top of one another with sheets of carbon in between exist.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded guide and a carriage with clamps, driven by an eccentric linkage on the guide, open and close the clamps automatically as the platen moves.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding several paper webs with carbon sheets through a typewriter needs hand tightening or loosening of the clamps, which is slow.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded guide and a carriage with clamps, driven by an eccentric linkage on the guide, open and close the clamps automatically as the platen moves.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
