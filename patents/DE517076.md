## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink ribbons having arrangements to prevent undesired contact between the impression-transfer material and machine parts or other articles

**How it works (reading):** A thin rubber strip moves loosely over the ink ribbon, running the same length and width, so each stroke hits a different spot and the ribbon fabric keeps its ink receptivity.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cleaning type with gasoline and wire brushes is slow, and a fixed protective strip wears through quickly.

**Solution:** A thin rubber strip moves loosely over the ink ribbon, running the same length and width, so each stroke hits a different spot and the ribbon fabric keeps its ink receptivity.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)
