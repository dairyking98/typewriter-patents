## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** The calculator's forward drive moves the platen closer to the type carrier, so the typebar has only a short movement during printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculating and typewriting share one platen, so the type carriers must travel far and printing is slow.

**Solution:** The calculator's forward drive moves the platen closer to the type carrier, so the typebar has only a short movement during printing.
