## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for back spacing

**How it works (reading):** The backspace slide pushes the carriage shift lever so the fixed switching tooth engages and locks the ratchet wheel instead of a separate stop.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Backspace slides stopped by a separate adjustable stop can jam against the ratchet and need a strong return spring.

**Solution:** The backspace slide pushes the carriage shift lever so the fixed switching tooth engages and locks the ratchet wheel instead of a separate stop.

**How it works:** A slide pulled by the backspace key through a bell crank engages the ratchet ring gear and turns it back one tooth. As it moves, its angled end hits a tab on the carriage shift lever and swings the lever until the fixed tooth engages the ratchet, which blocks overthrow. No separate adjustable stop is required. A spring returns the slide, and a guide pin keeps it engaged with the tooth.

**Parts:** carriage ratchet wheel; shift lever; fixed switching tooth; loose switching tooth; backspace slide; bell crank; stop tab; return spring

**Improves on:**
