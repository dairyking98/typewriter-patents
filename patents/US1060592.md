## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops in a stop holder or magazine can shift out of their set positions when the stop is moved or handled.

**Solution:** A simple stop construction with its own self-retaining means on the holder, so each tabulator stop stays in its chosen position on the holder rail.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** tabulator stop; stop holder; ejector; push rod; crank arm
