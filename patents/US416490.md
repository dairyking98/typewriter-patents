## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbons dry out and buying new ones is costly.

**Solution:** A box of ink with a pair of rollers through which the ribbon is drawn by hand to reink it.

**How it works:** A metal box holds ink with a rubber roller partly submerged; a smaller rubber roller in the curved cover rests on it. A handle rotates the upper roller, driving the lower. The ribbon passes in through a slot, between the rollers, and out another slot, inked by the lower roller. Bracket plates attach it to Remington or Caligraph machines.
