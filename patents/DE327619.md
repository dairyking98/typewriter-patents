## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key buttons were glued or fixed in a way that made replacement slow or damaging, which hindered changing to Greek or Russian characters.

**Solution:** A dovetail clamp fixed to the key lever holds a wooden or plastic key piece that snaps in with a spring-loaded bead, so keys can be swapped quickly.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
