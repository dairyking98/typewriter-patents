> Description Translated from German Paper roll for typewriters. The invention relates to a paper roll for typewriters, in the known manner over a middle metal frame an outer shell supported by a soft layer is arranged. The invention differs from the known in that the framework is axially extending and rods connected by end pieces and a central connecting piece, and that between the outer shell and the soft layer an inside with soft rubber or felt-lined metal sleeve is provided. Through this execution will the outer shell on which the letters MJ & i MAARAt open, something yield. learned and at the same time the very frequent, unpleasant noise, that occurs when the types hit rigidly mounted paper rollers, is reduced. The inner frame of the paper roll consists of a number of rods i, which are arranged around a common axis and by end pieces a and a middle connector 3 are connected.
