## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rigid platen amplifies the noise and hammer impact of the types.

**Solution:** Platen has a lightweight rod framework with cork, rubber and metal sleeves under a soft outer shell, which yields and muffles impacts.

> Description Translated from German Paper roll for typewriters. The invention relates to a paper roll for typewriters, in the known manner over a middle metal frame an outer shell supported by a soft layer is arranged. The invention differs from the known in that the framework is axially extending and rods connected by end pieces and a central connecting piece, and that between the outer shell and the soft layer an inside with soft rubber or felt-lined metal sleeve is provided. Through this execution will the outer shell on which the letters MJ & i MAARAt open, something yield. learned and at the same time the very frequent, unpleasant noise, that occurs when the types hit rigidly mounted paper rollers, is reduced.
