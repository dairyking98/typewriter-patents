## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Speech is reported too slowly by any existing instrument, and a better short-hand printing system is needed.

**Solution:** A scheme of selected types connected to two keyboards, one for each hand, prints a syllable, word or short phrase at once in a stenophonotype alphabet of primary signs and cognates.
