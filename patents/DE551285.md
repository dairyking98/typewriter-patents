## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card evaluation machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with separate or detached types or dies

**How it works (reading):** A device on the tabulating machine checks the hole-value sequence of the card row for completeness and indicates deviations from the order without stopping the work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checking a run of punched counting cards for missing numbers in sequence is done by hand, and earlier mechanical monitors stopped the machine or inserted memo cards, which hinders statistical work.

**Solution:** A device on the tabulating machine checks the hole-value sequence of the card row for completeness and indicates deviations from the order without stopping the work.
