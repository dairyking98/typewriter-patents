## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to line spacing mechanism for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A pawl on a vertical-axis operating lever engages the platen's line-space ratchet when the handle moves, and an adjustable stop on the carriage side plate limits the pawl's travel to set the line advance.
