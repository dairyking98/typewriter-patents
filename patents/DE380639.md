## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for the automatic operation of typewriters, typesetting machines and the like.

**How it works (reading):** Only seven holes are used in a paper strip and mouthpiece, with a distributor of sliding carriages connecting air passages to the key working cylinders. Suction selects the key.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Known air-channel selectors for key machines need a hole for every key in the paper strip and a wide mouthpiece.

**Solution:** Only seven holes are used in a paper strip and mouthpiece, with a distributor of sliding carriages connecting air passages to the key working cylinders. Suction selects the key.
