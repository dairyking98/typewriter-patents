## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor wants a typewriter that is simple in construction, easily operated and effective.

**Solution:** A horizontal type wheel with elastic type-carrying arms is turned by a knob to a letter, then the knob is pressed down to drive a centering pin and hammer that print it while the carriage advances.

**How it works:** A metal disc has elastic arms around its periphery carrying type (metal or rubber, upper and lower case). A ring above has a rim with perforations over each arm and matching letters; a spring marker arm with a pin rests in a perforation to show which letter is under the type-centering pin. The operator turns the wheel with a knob on a spring-supported disc, then depresses the knob. This pushes an impression lever down, carrying the centering pin through the perforation to locate the arm, and a type hammer to strike. The lever is linked to a pawl engaging a rack bar, so the frame carrying the wheel is stepped along the paper each stroke. The wheel frame is hinged on sleeves sliding on the rack bar and rests on a graduated space bar.
