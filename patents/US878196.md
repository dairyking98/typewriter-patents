## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon normally held away from the print point, with fields of different colours or characters, could not be steered by the keys to any desired field.

**Solution:** Alter the point where the ribbon vibrator connects to its operating lever so the vibrator keeps the same throw but rests at a different height, presenting a different ribbon stripe.

**How it works:** On a Monarch typewriter the key levers and sub-levers drive typebars, while the universal bar frame operates a lever that moves a vibratory ribbon carrier. The pivotal connection between vibrator and operating lever can be shifted without changing the throw. Moving the connection changes the vibrator's normal position, so a different widthwise stripe of a polychrome ribbon lands at the print point. Several forms are shown, including ones with ribbon of uniform colour used in parallel lines across its width.
