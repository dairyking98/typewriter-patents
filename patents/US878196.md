## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to ribbon mechanism for typewriting machines and has for one of its-objects toprovide an imroved-constructioii wherein a ribbon norma ly removed from the printing point and of fields ofdiflerent characteristics or colors may be controlled so that under the influence of the printing keys, said ribbon may be moved to cover the printing point with-any desired field.
