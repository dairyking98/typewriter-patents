## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A folding typewriter needs ribbon spools and feed that fold with the carriage and shift with it.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools ride on the foldable side bars and are driven by a ratchet that shifts with the carriage.

**How it works:** Side bars pivot on the frame and carry the carriage bed and ribbon spools, so they fold down together. Key lever motion actuates a ratchet that turns the spools a set distance per stroke. The ratchet moves with the case shift. One spring holds both the holding dog and the moving dog against the ratchet wheel.
