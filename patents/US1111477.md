## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter noise disturbed others, and the operator needed to see and reach the machine.

**Solution:** A felt-lined sound-proof case encloses the machine, with a glass shutter and external carriage return.

**How it works:** The machine is enclosed in a sound-deadening case with a shutter and levers that return the carriage from outside.
