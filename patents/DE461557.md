**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This object is achieved by the fact that the heavy Type mechanics on rails attached to the machine housing lets them rest .

**How it works (reading):** The whole type mechanism rests on curved rails attached to the housing, so it is guided and supported while it is removed or replaced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The heavy type mechanism of a typewriter was hard to pull out and replace because the bulky ribbon carrier and other parts snagged on the machine.

**Solution:** The whole type mechanism rests on curved rails attached to the housing, so it is guided and supported while it is removed or replaced.
