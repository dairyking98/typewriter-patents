## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters need re-adjusting the paper for every line; the inventor wants a whole page written on one adjustment, quick paper changing and simple ribbon movement.

**Solution:** Sheets of paper wrapped around a cylinder turned by clockwork on a threaded stationary rod, so it advances one line width per revolution beneath the ribbon, with ribbon fed in step.

**How it works:** A bed plate carries a stud and a screw-rod; a main wheel and tube turn a cylinder on the threaded rod by a clockwork. Each revolution the screw moves the cylinder axially by the line breadth plus line space, under the inking ribbon. When the sheet is full the operator takes it off with special forceps and slides the cylinder back. Paper edges are specially prepared, the main wheel is indexed, and a detachable connection is released automatically by a raised part on the threaded bar. Only the opening was available.
