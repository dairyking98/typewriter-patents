## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (card printing at cash registers)

**How it works (reading):** One transmission setting depends on whether a sheet is in the printer guide, so inserting a sheet disengages card issue while printing continues.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing a control strip and a card on a cash register needed separate switching, and a sheet inserted for printing still let the card conveyor run.

**Solution:** One transmission setting depends on whether a sheet is in the printer guide, so inserting a sheet disengages card issue while printing continues.

> Description Translated from German Device for printing and issuing cards to cash registers or accounting machines The invention relates to devices at cash registers, Booking and similar machines for printing and issuing cards and a Control strips and, if necessary, a special sheet to be printed: They are devices known in which a printing unit and a conveyor device for the card strip under the action of a button that switches the transmission on and off and one second key, which allows the printing unit to work even when it is switched off Conveying device allows such that the printing unit for printing one of the card strips independent sheet can be used. It is also known, the printing unit and the conveyor for the Card strips over two separate, under the influence of a single adjustment means to put stationary transmission gear in or out of operation. According to the invention is the setting on such a device of the transmission gear assigned to the conveyor device into the effective position depends on whether a sheet is in the printer guide or not.
