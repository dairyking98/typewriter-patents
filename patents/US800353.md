## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector puncture inking) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** The object being to provide a machine to puncture the paper on the proper line to form the character desired and at the same time ink the punctures or perforations so that it will be impossible without detection to change any figure or character that has been made upon the paper.

**How it works (reading):** A check-protector that inks the dies before or after perforating the paper, with an improved means for depressing the die-plate and an improved feed mechanism, plus an operating arm for the die.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks and similar instruments could be altered or raised, and earlier check-protectors needed simplifying.

**Solution:** A check-protector that inks the dies before or after perforating the paper, with an improved means for depressing the die-plate and an improved feed mechanism, plus an operating arm for the die.
