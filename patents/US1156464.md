## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improvement in typewriters, and more especially to mechanism for controlling the movement of the carriage and also for controlling the linespace movements of the platen, and is a division of my application, No.

**How it works (reading):** Selector mechanism so one set of controlling elements chooses either platen line spacing or carriage traverse to a predetermined or undetermined amount, varying the carriage relation to the printing point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** One control group cannot select between platen line-space movement and carriage traverse, nor choose the degree of movement.

**Solution:** Selector mechanism so one set of controlling elements chooses either platen line spacing or carriage traverse to a predetermined or undetermined amount, varying the carriage relation to the printing point.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
