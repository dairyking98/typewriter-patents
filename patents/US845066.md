## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The escapement detents, as they were built, are not simple or efficient in construction and operation.

**Solution:** The detents are carried on an oscillating member and pivoted to coact with the escapement ratchet wheel on a shaft, which holds the carriage-feed pinion that engages the carriage rack.

**How it works:** The shaft carries a pinion that engages the carriage rack and an escapement ratchet wheel; detent members on the oscillating support step the ratchet to let the carriage advance.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** detents; oscillating member; escapement ratchet wheel; pinion; shaft; carriage rack; spring
