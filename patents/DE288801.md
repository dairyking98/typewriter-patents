## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Edge-punched sheets must stay in the machine while originals are removed and new bills inserted.

**Solution:** Pinned conveyor belts on the paper roller engage holes at the sheet edges, with an insertion table and guide plate between the belts to feed the sheets to the writing position.
