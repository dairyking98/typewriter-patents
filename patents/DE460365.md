## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Line switching device for typewriters, calculating machines and the like. In the usual line switching devices for typewriters, calculating machines and the like, in which, as is well known, the switching lever acts directly on the slide switch and the movement of the latter that of the switching pawl is derived from, the disadvantage has been shown, that due to the fineness of the toothing of the ratchet wheel caused by the small pitch, the ratchet cannot intervene with sufficient reliability when switching quickly. It was also found that in these known devices, due to the resilience of the only weakly spring-influenced pawl itself and the parts carrying it, the engagement of the pawl is not effective enough to inhibit the rotational movement of the mass of the platen immediately after the line feed has been completed. It therefore happens that the roller, as a result of the living force imparted to it during switching, continues its rotation, the switching pawl sliding over the next tooth and engaging in the subsequent gap.
