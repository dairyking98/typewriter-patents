## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Swing-weight type bar drives either used slotted cam levers that clatter and are costly, or fixed link chains that cannot be adjusted per bar.

**Solution:** Keep all drive links permanently hinged, with a knee joint between the swing-weight drive lever and the double lever from the key lever, so both link groups can be adjusted independently.

**How it works:** The key lever pulls a double lever through a detachable hanging. Its lower leg connects to a knee-joint (toggle) mechanism of pressed lever arms with a regulating screw. Down to about 20 mm from the platen the key drives everything; after that the swing weight's stored momentum extends the toggle and strikes the type. Adjusting the knee screw tunes force per bar without changing key depth, and each drive unit can be removed as a whole.
