## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding machine gearbox) · **Subject (patent classes):** Keyboards with one set of keys for all denominations, e.g. ten-key board

**How it works (reading):** The adjusting disks carry mirror-written printed types between narrow radial incisions in their cylindrical surface, and the summing disks mesh with them through narrow tooth strips; the broad areas between ridges read the set digits.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Gearing between adjusting disks and summing disks in adding machines needs a tooth form that also lets the printed digits be read.

**Solution:** The adjusting disks carry mirror-written printed types between narrow radial incisions in their cylindrical surface, and the summing disks mesh with them through narrow tooth strips; the broad areas between ridges read the set digits.
