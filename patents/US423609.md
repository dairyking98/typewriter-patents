## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Banks need a punch that cuts the amount figures and protective stars into a check so the amount cannot be raised.

**Solution:** A series of levers carry dies at their ends and are operated by keys to punch holes in the check, with feeding mechanism that advances the paper.
