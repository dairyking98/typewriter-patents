## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**How it works (reading):** The line ruler is hinged to a slide on a rail behind the paper table, so it folds down behind the paper label when not used; the same rail serves as the sheet edge stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A loose line ruler for visible-writing machines is easily lost, and when clipped to the paper roller frame it slips off during heavy writing.

**Solution:** The line ruler is hinged to a slide on a rail behind the paper table, so it folds down behind the paper label when not used; the same rail serves as the sheet edge stop.
