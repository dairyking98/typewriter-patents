## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the present invention is to make provision for that at the beginning of the arithmetic work game that of the previous arithmetic work game triggered carriage movement is ended under all circumstances, so that at the beginning the pawl engaged during the work cycle only after the end of the Switching movement.

**How it works (reading):** Arithmetic keys trigger the carriage switch bridge earlier in the stroke than letter keys do.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage release timing differs between letter keys and arithmetic keys, so the pawl can engage before the carriage stops.

**Solution:** Arithmetic keys trigger the carriage switch bridge earlier in the stroke than letter keys do.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
