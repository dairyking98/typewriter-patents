## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Replacing the steel clips that fasten a ribbon to its spool is slow, awkward and leaves the hands inked.

**Solution:** Two identical independent pliers legs with inward hooks engage the clip ends; squeezing the long legs bends the clip onto the spool shaft.
