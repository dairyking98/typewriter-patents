## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Record-book work (court, bank, tax records) needs a typewriter that sits on the bound book's page without moving the book.

**Solution:** A typewriter on adjustable tracks that rest on the page margins, held in position by the machine's own weight, with a platen only for general letter work.

**How it works:** A rectangular frame rides on laterally adjustable tracks resting on the book page, and the machine's weight holds the leaves to receive the impression. A carriage runs on front and rear guides and carries stepped keys, levers and draw-wires to typebars pivoted on a pendent ring. The partitions carrying the levers lift out for repair. A line-spacing lever, adjustable line stop with key lock, and ribbon mechanism are on the carriage. For letters a separate platen is placed under the machine.
