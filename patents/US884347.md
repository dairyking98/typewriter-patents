## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Visible front-strike machines need a traveling carriage, escapement and type-bar hangers; the text is largely unreadable OCR.

**Solution:** A visible writer with type bars on hangers, a cylindrical platen on a traveling carriage, and a key-lever bank with adjustable stroke.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** Fig. 16 type bar and hanger
