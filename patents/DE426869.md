## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A pointer-field type-cylinder typewriter advanced the carriage directly by the writing key but had no backspace.

**Solution:** A backspace key lever depresses the writing-key switch frame to disengage the forward pawl and, through a parallelogram linkage, engages a reset pawl that pulls the carriage back one tooth.

**How it works:** The backspace lever presses an intermediate lever onto the switching frame, so the writing transport pawl lifts from the carriage rack. Through an angle lever and a connecting rod under tension spring, a reset pawl is shifted away from a guide pin, snaps into the rack by its spring and moves the carriage back one pitch. On release, the spring returns the linkage and the guide pin throws the reset pawl out, and the transport pawl re-engages.
