## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists fundamentally in interchangeable combinations of three separate, independent, but mutually-controlling dogs designed to produce various forms of motion in an associated ratchet-escapement.

**How it works (reading):** A mechanism limits and controls the escapement dogs so one or two units of motion occur in the ratchet escapement, giving a letter space or a word space; it attaches to a standard Remington machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A standard ratchet escapement could only feed the carriage one unit per action; a single hand motion needed to produce one or two units, for letter or word spacing.

**Solution:** A mechanism limits and controls the escapement dogs so one or two units of motion occur in the ratchet escapement, giving a letter space or a word space; it attaches to a standard Remington machine.

**How it works:** Combined dogs are moved one or two units by the mechanism, which the type and spacing bars can select through a second special mechanism.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** escapement dogs; ratchet escapement; limiting mechanism
