## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**How it works (reading):** The punch head moves in a straight line toward the operator, with the check inserted under the head so both the check and the indicating numerals stay upright.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check-punches have too many parts and cost too much, and punches that move in an arc are objectionable.

**Solution:** The punch head moves in a straight line toward the operator, with the check inserted under the head so both the check and the indicating numerals stay upright.

**Features:** [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
