## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing ribbon shifts for changing the ribbon vibration path are complicated, need an extra key, and load the vibrator.

**Solution:** A shifting device on the vibrator's bracket moves the vibrator base along a guide, adding no weight to the vibrating parts, with simple locking and removable as an attachment.

**How it works:** On an Oliver typewriter, the ribbon vibrator is a parallel-link mechanism on a base with links, a rock shaft and a fork. The attachment slides the base forward or back on the bracket and locks it in either limit, so the ribbon vibrates through a different path.
