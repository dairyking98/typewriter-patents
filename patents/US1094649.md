## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to ribbon mechanism for typewriting machines, and more;

**How it works (reading):** Superposed ribbon sections sit at the printing area, and separating them from the platen when the work sheets are replaced feeds new ribbon to the print point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A flat platen needs transfer ribbon sections that present fresh ribbon at each sheet.

**Solution:** Superposed ribbon sections sit at the printing area, and separating them from the platen when the work sheets are replaced feeds new ribbon to the print point.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
