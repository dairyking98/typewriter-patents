## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Font holder for typewriters The invention relates to Another design of font holders for typewriters according to Patent 489 562, in which the holder plate with the extension arms and this with the support feet are connected in such a way that the holder plate can be pivoted to the side and which are also provided in a known manner with a device that to allow adjustment of the holder plate in the direction of the writer. The holder plate of such font holder was previously, even if they were provided with a device of the latter type, always in parallel moved to the typewriter roller. The new thing is that the holder plate on the swing-out Cantilever arms by means of sleeves that can be moved lengthways independently of one another on these is stored, so that by moving only one sleeve, the holder plate accordingly the most favorable light incidence can be set at an angle. The inclination is also possible if the extension arms are partially or completely swiveled out to the side are.
