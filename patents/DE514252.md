## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to Another design of font holders for typewriters according to Patent 489 562, in which the holder plate with the extension arms and this with the support feet are connected in such a way that the holder plate can be pivoted to the side and which are also provided in a known manner with a device that to allow adjustment of the holder plate in the direction of the writer.

**How it works (reading):** Each support tube sits on a sleeve that slides independently along the swung-out arm, so one side can be moved forward to tilt the document holder to a favourable light angle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A copy holder pivoted to the side could only move parallel to the roller, so the light angle on the document could not be set.

**Solution:** Each support tube sits on a sleeve that slides independently along the swung-out arm, so one side can be moved forward to tilt the document holder to a favourable light angle.
