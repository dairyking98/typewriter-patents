## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines, and refers more specifically toian improved cushioning device against which the type-bars rest when not in use and constructed to cushion the impact of the type-bars as they return to their positions of 'rest and to deaden the noise of the impact.

**How it works (reading):** A cushioning device against which the type-bars rest when not in use, which absorbs the impact of their return and deadens the noise.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-bars strike noisily and with impact when returning to their rest positions.

**Solution:** A cushioning device against which the type-bars rest when not in use, which absorbs the impact of their return and deadens the noise.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
