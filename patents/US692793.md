## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The excerpt does not state a single defect; the inventor lists many separate objects for a visible-writing typewriter.

**Solution:** A visible-writing typewriter with improved carriage driving, escapement, paper holding and feeding, bell ringing, key-lock release, carriage shifting, margin regulators and disengaging escapement members.

**How it works:** Carriage-driving and escapement mechanisms move the carriage step by step; a key-lock releases locked keys; margin regulators and a bell complete the carriage functions. Details are beyond the first 3000 characters.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** carriage; escapement; bell; margin regulators; key-lock mechanism
