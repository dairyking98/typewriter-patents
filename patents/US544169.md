## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil punch machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention being to produce a stencil-character punch-machine which will be compact, simple, durable, and light, be easily manipulated, and present certain and other advantages resulting from its inherent construction.

**How it works (reading):** A rotating annular disk carries character plates; a pinion turns the disk and a feed pawl on a cam-controlled guide advances it between punches.
