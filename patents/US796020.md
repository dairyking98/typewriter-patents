## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide an improved spacing attachment for tabulating or arranging in parallel columns typewritten matter on a sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating attachment releases the carriage to travel to an adjustable carriage-stop at a predetermined point, then releases it again to the next point, with the stops adjustable along the carriage travel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** No simple way exists to arrange lines in parallel columns on a sheet by moving the carriage to several fixed points.

**Solution:** A tabulating attachment releases the carriage to travel to an adjustable carriage-stop at a predetermined point, then releases it again to the next point, with the stops adjustable along the carriage travel.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
