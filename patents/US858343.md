## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Centrifugal action and lost motion from wear let the type head rise above its proper position at the moment of printing, so the impression is out of line.

**Solution:** A guide stop on the bent part of each type bar enters the channel of the typebar guide and bears against the underside of a guide jaw, fixing the vertical position of the type head at the print point; the guide face is bevelled.
