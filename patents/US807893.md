## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to a tabulating attachment for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating attachment on a scale moves the carriage from any point to any other point on the scale, a full division per key action, or the full length when needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulating move cannot go from any fraction of one scale division to a fraction of another, and it cannot move a full division or the full length in one key action.

**Solution:** A tabulating attachment on a scale moves the carriage from any point to any other point on the scale, a full division per key action, or the full length when needed.
