## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The vertical character feed (the line-wise character-by-character advance, as in vertical Japanese writing) should be simple and also allow quick, accurate reverse movement.

**Solution:** Two pawls, a basic pawl and a spring-stepped feed pawl, engage the feed gear alternately under a strong gear-shaft spring, with a stop pawl that holds the feed pawl and lets the gear be turned back tooth by tooth.

**How it works:** The gear on the paper-cylinder shaft is pre-loaded by a strong spring. Pressing the print key slides the basic pawl piece along its shaft so the basic pawl bites the gear and the feed pawl leaves it, releasing the reverse pawl from the stop pawl; a weak spring then steps the feed pawl forward by the preset spacing. On the key's return the feed pawl re-engages and the basic pawl lets go, so the gear spring drives the gear and pushes the feed pawl back to its start, one character of feed. At the end of the stroke the reverse pawl passes the stop pawl, which locks the feed pawl against going back. Turning the cylinder shaft backwards then makes the feed pawl, held by the stop pawl, vibrate tooth by tooth so the gear can be turned back easily without slipping forward beyond the set feed.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Vertical feed device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a vertical feed device for a typewriter characterised as follows. On a base shaft that turns through a set range under a spring, a basic pawl piece is mounted so that it can slide horizontally along the shaft. On that piece a feed pawl is mounted, with a weak spring that makes it step through the vertical-feed spacing. These two pawls engage the vertical-feed gear in turn. The feed gear has a turning force given to its shaft beforehand by a strong spring, which makes the feed, and each time one action is finished, a stop pawl that prevents the feed pawl going back catches, by gravity or a spring, on a reverse pawl on the feed pawl piece (printed 毎爪片, read 送爪片). The object is to make the vertical feed with a simple device and at the same time to let the reverse movement be done directly, quickly and accurately. Brief description of the drawings.
