You can do this through this achieve that a special intermediate lever is switched on between the key and type lever will. This, however, reduces the parts of the typewriter to a significant extent increased so that the manufacturing cost is considerably increased. It is too difficult to arrange these intermediate levers in such a way that they do not have a disruptive effect and Claim space that you can for housing other parts the typewriter needed. After all, means engaging everyone such an intermediate link an unfavorable increase in the frictional resistance. These difficulties and disadvantages are especially felt when there is are type lever typewriters, in which the key and type lever are mounted in radial incisions of a common carrier.
