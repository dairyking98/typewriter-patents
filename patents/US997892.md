## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to cause both the type-operating keys and the spacing -key to be locked during 4the jump of the carriage, so as to prevent accidental operation of thecarriage feeding dogs by any of said.

**How it works (reading):** One key stroke projects the column stop and releases the carriage to run to the next column, with the other keys locked during the jump.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operator must hold the denomination key until the carriage finishes its run, otherwise numbers write out of column.

**Solution:** One key stroke projects the column stop and releases the carriage to run to the next column, with the other keys locked during the jump.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
