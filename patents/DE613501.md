## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (paint container for decorating ceramics and vehicles) · **Subject (patent classes):** Instruments or apparatus for painting lines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to painting devices, how to decorate ceramic products with patterns and ribbons, for painting motor vehicles or for other purposes, and the consist of a paint container, from which you can press a rubber ball or the like.

**How it works (reading):** A rubber air reservoir beside the paint tube is squeezed to pressurise the paint, which then flows to a wick nozzle; a stop controls the spread.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A paint bottle that is squeezed by hand gives uneven flow, and the hand must keep pressing the rubber ball.

**Solution:** A rubber air reservoir beside the paint tube is squeezed to pressurise the paint, which then flows to a wick nozzle; a stop controls the spread.

**Features:** [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md)
