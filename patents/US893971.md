## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating keys release too early, so the carriage can stop short of the column.

**Solution:** A denomination key and carriage release lock in working position until the carriage has arrested at the preset stop.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
