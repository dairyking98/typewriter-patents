## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manual typing is slow; an automatic operator is needed to press keys and make carriage return and line spacing from a master sheet.

**Solution:** A perforated master sheet passes over a tracker board, with pneumatic connections actuating key levers, carriage return and line spacing mechanisms.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)
