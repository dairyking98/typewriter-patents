## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper carriage switching by a ratchet and pawls driven from the space bar needs a better arrangement so the carriage is held and moves without jamming.

**Solution:** An intermediate link is inserted between the space bar linkage and the pawl carrier; it swings the pawl carrier and holds the ratchet wheel while a loose pawl is out of the ratchet, with a bevelled fixed pawl giving accelerated shifting.

**How it works:** The type lever nose presses on a universal rail which pushes the pawl carrier back; while held back, the loose pawl drops in behind the ratchet tooth, and the fixed bevelled pawl holds the wheel until the carrier swings forward again.
