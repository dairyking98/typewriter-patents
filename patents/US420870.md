## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon only used along its length, wasting its width.

**Solution:** Ribbon feed that moves the ribbon lengthwise and also shifts it transversely so the whole surface is used.

**How it works:** On a Caligraph, two spool shafts each carry bevel gears and cranks; sliding bevel pinions engaged by a switch bar choose which spool drives. A rock shaft moved by every keystroke oscillates arms driving ratchets and eccentrics, which reciprocate guide bars carrying ribbon guides that move the ribbon sideways.
