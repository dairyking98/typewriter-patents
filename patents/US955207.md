## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typewriter needs an improved page-end indicator for paper that feeds from one line to another.

**Solution:** A page-end indicator on the platen frame of a Smith Premier No. 10 shows the end of the sheet, with a retaining friction plate on the platen.
