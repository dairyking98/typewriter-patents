Numerous attempts have been made for many years to design a typewriter so that notes can be typed with the machine. In some of these machines, intricate rods or similar devices were built in to move the paper relative to the stop point of the type lever, or vice versa, and to adjust the paper to a point so that a musical notation, e.g. B. an ote, in the desired vertical position on 1 of the five dead lines could be written. In addition, these typewriters had a carriage step control that automatically moved the carriage a certain distance to the left as soon as a key or the space key was pressed. Typewriters of this type for writing musical notes required an unusual amount of manual labor and time to finish writing a piece of the dead or a piece of music.
