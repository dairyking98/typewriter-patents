## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a particular one Embodiment of the inventive concept underlying the protected device according to the main patent.

**How it works (reading):** An incidence tooth elastically linked to the feeler lever is fed into the worm of the reel shaft, so only a short nudge is needed, and ribbon travel before reversal is about 1 cm.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The main patent's worm-and-tooth ribbon reversal needs too large a movement of the feeler lever to engage the incidence tooth.

**Solution:** An incidence tooth elastically linked to the feeler lever is fed into the worm of the reel shaft, so only a short nudge is needed, and ribbon travel before reversal is about 1 cm.
