## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen turning and carriage return at line end are done by hand.

**Solution:** A key-closed solenoid with a long sliding core joined to the carriage, plus an air cylinder pair, returns the carriage and turns the platen automatically.

**How it works:** At line end the operator presses a key that closes the circuit of a solenoid mounted slightly inclined, whose sliding core travels as far as the carriage and is joined to it through an air-compression cylinder and piston. A supplemental cylinder in communication has its piston linked to the platen-turning lever, so the compressed air turns the platen as the carriage is pulled back. The core is mostly outside the coil at line end, so pull is gradual, and the core passes neutral just before the carriage finishes its return, so the reverse pull brakes it. The inclination lets gravity overcome friction during stepping, and no gearing keeps hand movement free.
