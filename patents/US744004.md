## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters and arithmometers were separate machines, and the inventor wanted one machine to do both typing and calculating.

**Solution:** A machine about the size of a typewriter that performs typewriting and recording-calculator functions together; the mechanism is not described in the first 3000 characters.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
