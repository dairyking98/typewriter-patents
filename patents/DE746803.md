## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (automatic repetition of paper step for calculating typewriters)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device in particular for calculating typewriters for automatic repetition of the step switching of the paper trolley.

**How it works (reading):** The counter's existing decimal-point stop lugs engage a ratchet and lever system that repeats the paper carriage step after another rotation of the driving wheel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic double step on calculating machines needs extra adjustable stops, and counters cannot be swapped without changing parts.

**Solution:** The counter's existing decimal-point stop lugs engage a ratchet and lever system that repeats the paper carriage step after another rotation of the driving wheel.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** counter; stop lugs; ratchet; lever system; stop
