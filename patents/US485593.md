## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With manifold writing the movable feed roll receded for each added sheet, lowering the printing point and spoiling alignment; the carriage frame also deformed.

**Solution:** Make the platen cylinder the yielding member and the feed roll rigid so the print point stays fixed however many sheets are inserted.

**How it works:** The cylinder shaft ends rest in bearings and are held down by pivoted steel springs that bear on stepped indentations, easing the pressure at the end not covered by narrow paper. Pushing the springs aside lets the cylinder be lifted out and replaced. Adjusting screws through the feed-roll bearings press on small blocks to set the cylinder position precisely. The paper scale is curved over the feed roll and a duplicate scale sits on the paper guide. A bell is tripped by a screw on the rack bar.
