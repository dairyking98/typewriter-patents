## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Turning the platen to bring a ruled line or blank space to the print point normally requires changing the line-spacing mechanism, which is inconvenient for non-standard spacing.

**Solution:** A platen-rotating mechanism driven by a finger wheel that turns the platen through minute arcs or large distances without disturbing the line-spacing mechanism or releasing any clutch.

**How it works:** A worm and work wheel on the platen give fine rotation by finger wheel, always in engagement with the line-spacing pawl.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen; finger wheel; work wheel or sleeve; worm; line-spacing pawl
