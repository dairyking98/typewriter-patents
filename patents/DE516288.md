## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a very simple, cheap and space-saving device, around carriage rails, racks and similar parts of writing, calculating and the like Adjust machines precisely and secure them in their set position.

**How it works (reading):** Fastening screws with eccentric extensions press the rail against conical bores in the base, adding a sideways displacement to set and fix it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage rails bend under load, and fixed rails cannot be readjusted once pinned.

**Solution:** Fastening screws with eccentric extensions press the rail against conical bores in the base, adding a sideways displacement to set and fix it.
