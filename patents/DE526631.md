**How it works (reading):** Each key-lever setting piece is riveted on and has a two-legged end; a pin pushed into the legs turns the piece to adjust the key lever against the universal rail.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Universal-rail tongues on stenography key levers wear out quickly and are hard to position exactly.

**Solution:** Each key-lever setting piece is riveted on and has a two-legged end; a pin pushed into the legs turns the piece to adjust the key lever against the universal rail.
