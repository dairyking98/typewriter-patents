## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding long strips of forms from spools must keep pace with the carriage so writing stays aligned.

**Solution:** A spool/roll support mounted with the carriage moves in step with it so strip feed matches carriage movement.

**How it works:** Strip rolls ride on the carriage so their feed tracks the carriage travel, keeping alignment across many forms.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
