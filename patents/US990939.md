## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting ma 1 chines and more especially to tabulating mechanism for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Keys each correspond to a column stop; pressing one lets the carriage skip intervening stops and stop at that column.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulator must step column by column; there is no direct jump to a chosen column.

**Solution:** Keys each correspond to a column stop; pressing one lets the carriage skip intervening stops and stop at that column.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
