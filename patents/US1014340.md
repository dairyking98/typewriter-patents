## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** At the end of a line the carriage stops but the keys still print, piling characters on top of each other.

**Solution:** Contact devices on the carriage render the key levers inoperative to the printing parts when the carriage reaches the line end.

**How it works:** Key levers have slotted projections with an offset notch; each actuates a link with a pin that engages the lever slot and works the typebar. A universal bar under the key levers rocks the dog rocker for the escapement (double escapement wheel as in No. 10 Remington, single feed dog). When the carriage reaches the end of the line, a contact device on the carriage acts so the key movements no longer drive the printing instrumentalities, the pins moving into the offset notches.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** key lever; actuating link; universal bar; dog rocker; double escapement wheel; feed rack; feed pinion; contact device
