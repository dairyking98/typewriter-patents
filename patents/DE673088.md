## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed wrist-rest bars do not suit students of different sizes, so the teaching device must fit each learner.

**Solution:** The wrist bar and table clamps are height-adjustable, and the bar is spring-cushioned so the wrists are supported gently rather than rigidly.
