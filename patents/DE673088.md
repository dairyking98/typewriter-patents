> Description Translated from German Teaching device for typewriter instruction. Present invention aims to perfect the teaching device protected in patent 650 779 for typewriting lessons, through which the typewriter student the correct Wrist and arm posture should be taught. The vocational and technical school students, for which the teaching device is primarily intended for are mostly in the years of greatest physical development, in which the differences in size of the body, hands, Joints and arms are extraordinarily large. The average dimensions assumed in the main patent for this reason offer no guarantee that the teaching device will work in each individual case the correct wrist and arm posture is actually achieved. Rather, it has proved to be expedient to train the teaching device so that it is individual for each Student can be adjusted and existing physical disparities perfectly compensates.
