## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary machines cannot print on upright work such as book pages while keeping the writing visible.

**Solution:** A front-strike typebar machine whose print point lies in a vertical plane, with a compact typebar action of uniform leverage, for flat or roller platens.

**How it works:** Work such as bound book pages is held upright so that the print point is crossed by a vertical plane. Typebars strike rearwards against the front of the platen, flat or roller, through a connection of operating links giving uniform leverage across the keyboard. The writing is completely visible. Opening only.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** type-bar; key lever; platen; operating link
