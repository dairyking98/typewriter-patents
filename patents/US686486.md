## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type-bar rebounds from its rest and collides with the next type-bar, especially in front-strike machines where bars nearly touch.

**Solution:** A cushioning construction at the key that cushions the key-touch at both the beginning and end of the stroke, stops the rebound from the basket, and still gives a powerful blow and quick recession of the type.

**How it works:** Cushioning in the key-to-type-bar connection absorbs the stroke ends; the type recedes quickly from the printing point.
