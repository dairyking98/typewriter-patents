## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** A special key bypasses the common movement member so only the ribbon drive and the accent typebar act, with no letter-spacing step.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing accents above or below a letter requires printing and ribbon lift without advancing the platen.

**Solution:** A special key bypasses the common movement member so only the ribbon drive and the accent typebar act, with no letter-spacing step.

**How it works:** In the main patent 366621 the ribbon drive is part on the frame, part on the movable platen frame. The special key lever has no shoulder to move the universal bar, so the escapement ratchet does not advance. A small angled lever on it connects to the ribbon lifter lever with free play, lifting the ribbon. The platen frame shift position selects one of three accent characters.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ESC:backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md)
