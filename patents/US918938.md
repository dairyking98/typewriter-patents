**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of said invention is to provide efiicient mechanism of the character s ecificd.

**How it works (reading):** A line-spacing pawl carrier and an adjusting plate limit its travel; a rock shaft carries an angular lever that actuates the line-spacing pawl, with a finger wheel on the platen side.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The existing line spacing mechanism is not efficient enough, and the text states this only in general terms.

**Solution:** A line-spacing pawl carrier and an adjusting plate limit its travel; a rock shaft carries an angular lever that actuates the line-spacing pawl, with a finger wheel on the platen side.
