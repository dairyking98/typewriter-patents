## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Writing fractions on a typewriter needs several strokes and separate types for numerator, slash and denominator.

**Solution:** Single types and keys carry a small numerator figure together with an inclined stroke, followed by a line of small denominator figures.

**How it works:** The keyboard has a line of ordinary figures, a line of keys each bearing a small figure plus slanted stroke, and a line of small figures for denominators. The operator strikes a numerator-plus-stroke key then small figures for the denominator, of any size. Types are connected to keys by bars as usual (Caligraph shown).
