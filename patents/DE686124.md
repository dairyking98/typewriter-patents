> Description Translated from German Pulse generator for temperature regulators, in particular for those for use in Hot water tanks often have a contact device with permanent magnets, their moving part is carried by a spring lamella together with the magnet armature and is under the influence of the magnet. As is well known, this causes a sudden Jump around the movable contact member in that with increasing voltage the Spring lamella an instant tearing or tearing of the movable contact part from fixed contact part takes place. In these known pulse generators for temperature controllers the magnet fixedly arranged so that the movable part of the contact device covers the entire must cover the necessary distance to close or open the circuit assigned to the contact. The invention is based on a pulse generator for temperature controllers with magnetic action assisted contact, in which a permanent magnet with one on a spring lamella arranged and interacts with the armature carrying a contact pole.
