## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type basket for teleprinters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers; Printing mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Type levers are cranked in a semicircular bearing and selected by selection rails and tension rods, then pulled to the impression point; the text is cut off before the full remedy is given.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving type baskets on chart recorders must carry selections across to moving rails, and the basket return at line end causes smeared first characters.

**Solution:** Type levers are cranked in a semicircular bearing and selected by selection rails and tension rods, then pulled to the impression point; the text is cut off before the full remedy is given.
