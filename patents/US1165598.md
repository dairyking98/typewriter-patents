## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means, applicable to visible writing machines, that are adapted to hold and guide a card so that printing may readily be done closely adjacent the side and upper and lower edges, and so that the card or sheet is prevented from making contact with the ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** Sheet-metal holding fingers are curved to match the platen and across the printing line; a locking device holds each finger in elevated operative position against lateral strain from the card edge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards must be held and guided close to the sides and edges without touching the ribbon.

**Solution:** Sheet-metal holding fingers are curved to match the platen and across the printing line; a locking device holds each finger in elevated operative position against lateral strain from the card edge.
