## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-head pivoted to or rotating on lever

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to that class of typearms in which the type characters are carried by an oscillating block at the end of the arm, so that it may be turned to bring the character into position for printing, and upon the return stroke of the arm, so as to bring it into contact with an inking-pad.

**How it works (reading):** Type block pivoted on the typebar flips over onto an ink pad at the bottom of the basket on the downstroke and returns upright for printing.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type face sits uninked on typebar.

**Solution:** Type block pivoted on the typebar flips over onto an ink pad at the bottom of the basket on the downstroke and returns upright for printing.

**How it works:** A two-member arm with a type block pivoted at its end; a link from a short lever arm turns the block when lugs hit a yielding rubber stop. Raised, the type faces the platen, lowered, it presses squarely on the inking pad.
