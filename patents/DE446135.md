**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (linking calculator and typewriter operation)

**How it works (reading):** A common table frame holds both machines at an angle, and a key housing with angle levers transmits each key press to the typewriter keys and the adder keys at the same time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter and an adding machine placed side by side cannot easily be operated together from one keyboard position.

**Solution:** A common table frame holds both machines at an angle, and a key housing with angle levers transmits each key press to the typewriter keys and the adder keys at the same time.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for connecting a calculating machine to a typewriter. For typewriters, their individual machines (typewriter and adding machine) can be operated independently of each other and at the same time when coupled, makes the connection of the two machines difficult for their simultaneous operation. Therefore, according to the invention, special keys are used for this purpose, which have an indirect effect on the keys of the individual machines. In the drawings, an embodiment of the invention is shown, namely shows: Fig. i a view of the apparatus from above with both machines in working position; Fig.2 the same view, but without machines; Fig.3 a partially Side view of the calculating machine; Fig. 4 is a left side view of the apparatus; Fig. 5 and 6 details. The apparatus consists of a tubular steel frame or table, the upper frame i of which is supported by feet 2 and 3, which are laterally connected to one another by struts 4 (Fig. I and ? -). A vertical support 5 is arranged between this and the horizontal strut 4 to stiffen the upper frame i. All parts are screwed together and the whole thing can therefore be easily dismantled.
