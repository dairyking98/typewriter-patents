**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing unit, line selection)

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** On account cards with pre-printed line numbers, the user cannot easily see the next free line to select.

**Solution:** A special printing member overprints the pre-printed line number on every filled line, so the next free line is visible.
