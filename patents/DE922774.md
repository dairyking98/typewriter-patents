## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Chinese characters are too many to carry on a practical typewriter or typesetting type set, and there is no simple classification or quick selection method.

**Solution:** A method of classifying Chinese characters by their components (a classifying component on the left and a phonetic component on the right, with many parts usable alone) to select characters for typewriters, typesetters and teleprinters. The mechanical selection device is not described clearly in the OCR.
