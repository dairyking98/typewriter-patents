## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon drive, bobbin mounting and vibrator on a front-stroke visible machine need to be simple, with easy bobbin change and polychrome and stencil settings.

**Solution:** Ribbon bobbins sit on hexagonal driven shafts fed from the spring drum through an endwise-shifting bevel gear, with a variable-leverage vibrator for colour and stencil positions.

**How it works:** A shaft driven from the spring-drum gear through a ratchet clutch turns a short shaft whose bevel pinion meshes with a bevel gear on an endwise-movable shaft. Shifting that shaft left or right engages the bevel gears of the left or right bobbin shaft. Bobbin hubs slip onto hexagonal or ribbed shafts and are held by a ball spring-latch. The vibrator has variable leverage giving three adjustments: two colours and stencil.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon bobbin; hexagonal shaft; bevel gear; endwise shaft; spring drum; ball latch; ribbon vibrator; side guide-roller
