## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (computing typewriter counter drive wheel)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a computational Typewriter with a fixed counter and a sliding one that Drive wheel of the cross counter driving support.

**How it works (reading):** The axis of the lower drive wheel is axially displaceable and its support carries a trigger tooth and locking bar, so the drive is switched between addition and subtraction and the cross counter is fixed and accessible.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On calculating typewriters the drive parts under the cross counter are hard to reach for assembly and adjustment.

**Solution:** The axis of the lower drive wheel is axially displaceable and its support carries a trigger tooth and locking bar, so the drive is switched between addition and subtraction and the cross counter is fixed and accessible.
