## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide a device of the character specified that shall be capable of attachment to typewriters which have passed from the factory into private hands, and of a construction such that it can be readily attached without requiring any alteration or disturbing any portion of the typewriter as already constructed.

**How it works (reading):** A pointer attachment that fits existing machines without altering them and shows on the sheet the point in the printing line where the type will strike when the carriage is returned.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the carriage is raised for inspection the operator cannot see where on the sheet the type will strike, and existing machines have no such indication.

**Solution:** A pointer attachment that fits existing machines without altering them and shows on the sheet the point in the printing line where the type will strike when the carriage is returned.
