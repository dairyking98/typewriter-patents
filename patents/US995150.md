## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting the whole carriage vertically for case is heavy; a lighter shift is wanted.

**Solution:** Only the platen and its slide frame move vertically, via a rock shaft and links, with notched catch levers locking the position and shift keys at the ends of the bank.

**How it works:** Platen slides run in vertical slots of the carriage end supports and rest on a transverse shaft carried on links. The links connect to levers on a rock shaft above the key levers. Two keys at one end raise or lower the roller; two at the other end do so and lock it. Catch levers with three notches engage pins on the links to lock the position and are released by cam slides as the key goes down. A spring and plunger return the roller to normal.
