## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple, cheap and efficient carriage escapement is wanted, and one that lets the carriage be pushed right without using the carriage release.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal dog rocker carries a feed dog and a spring-loaded loose dog with bevelled teeth, so the carriage rack escapes one tooth per key stroke and can be pushed back against the dogs by camming them down.

**How it works:** A stamped dog rocker pivots on a bracket under the carriage rail and is rocked by an arm on the universal bar when a key nears the bottom of its stroke. Two dogs, a fixed-pivot feed dog and a second dog pivoted on it, are guided in a slot formed by a bent-back arm of the rocker. On the key down stroke one dog leaves the rack and the other enters the next tooth, releasing the rack one step; on the up stroke they swap back, as a reverse escapement. The rack teeth and dog teeth are inclined on their reverse faces so pushing the carriage right cams the dogs down and out of the way without lifting the rack. Adjustable stops set the rocker's travel, and the carriage is driven left by a cord to a spring drum.
