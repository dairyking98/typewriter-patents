## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the combination, with the paper-feeding roll, of two series of limiting-stops, and a spring-acting arm having a projection adapted to co-operate with either series of stops, as desired, said stops having two similar limiting edges or faces to thereby enable rotation of the paper-feeding roll backward and forward.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On Becker-type index machines the line spacing of the paper roll cannot be varied between close and wide spacing.

**Solution:** The paper roll hub carries two grooves with different numbers of detent sockets, and a spring arm drops into either groove to give single or wider line spacing.

**How it works:** A milled nut on the paper-feeding roll has a hub with two circumferential grooves; one has six equally spaced sockets and the other four. A spring arm on the frame bears in one groove and its projection enters the sockets, stopping the roll at each, in either rotation direction. Moving the arm between grooves selects line pitch. The base machine (Becker US350717) has a movable index plate that rocks on a guide rod, a pivoted type segment and a printing lever, with a comb-toothed spring guard on the paper roll.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md), [Type sector or segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)

**Parts:** paper-feeding roll; milled nut; hub; groove; socket; spring arm; guard plate; index plate; type segment; printing lever; feed dog

**Numbers:** 6 sockets in one groove; 4 sockets in the other

**Improves on:**
