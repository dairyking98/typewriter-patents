## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the combination, with the paper-feeding roll, of two series of limiting-stops, and a spring-acting arm having a projection adapted to co-operate with either series of stops, as desired, said stops having two similar limiting edges or faces to thereby enable rotation of the paper-feeding roll backward and forward.
