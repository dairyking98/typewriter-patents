## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to devices for releasably connecting the platen to the line space wheel of a type-writing machine, to permit the platen to be rotated independently of the line space wheel.

**How it works (reading):** A finger wheel on the platen axle carries a helical cam that, when turned, drives the line-space wheel against a flange on the axle boss, clamping it; an adjustable device sets the clamping pressure, and releasing the cam frees the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be rotated independently of the line-space wheel, and the existing machines have no simple, cheap way to do this.

**Solution:** A finger wheel on the platen axle carries a helical cam that, when turned, drives the line-space wheel against a flange on the axle boss, clamping it; an adjustable device sets the clamping pressure, and releasing the cam frees the platen.
