## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing automatic ribbon reversing devices are intricate and unreliable.

**Solution:** Toggle levers at each spool are coupled to a spring-loaded locking mechanism that unlocks only when the ribbon tension of an empty spool overcomes the spring, triggering the reversal.

**How it works:** The ribbon moves between two spools on studs, each with a gear driven from a shaft pair coupled by an axially sliding horizontal drive shaft powered by a spring housing at each stroke. A toggle lever at each spool carries the ribbon guide pin. When a spool runs out, ribbon tension on the first following strokes swings the toggle lever, which moves a U-shaped lever and wire to a control lever. The control lever has a wedge end held by a spring roller on a locking lever. Overcoming the spring, it slides the drive shaft so the other spool's gear engages.
