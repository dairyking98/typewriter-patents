**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to support plates for, in particular, heavier calculating machines, typewriters and other heavier machines that are arranged and operated on table tops or the like such as calculating machines with electric motors.

**How it works (reading):** Articulated arms and a swivel lever system move the machine support plate and a protective cover together, with a lever joint that can be set in a straight line to hold the plate in its working position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Heavy machines on a table are hard to raise into working position and cover again by hand.

**Solution:** Articulated arms and a swivel lever system move the machine support plate and a protective cover together, with a lever joint that can be set in a straight line to hold the plate in its working position.
