## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key touch cannot be adjusted over a wide range to suit different operators, so the machine cannot be set for a stiff or a very soft touch.

**Solution:** Curved spring fulcrums on the key levers and bar hubs are set on an adjustable frame, so the key touch can be varied from stiff to very soft.
