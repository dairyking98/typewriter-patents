## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Return of the paper carriage makes noise as the loose pawl of the escapement slaps against the ratchet wheel teeth, and earlier friction members were unreliable.

**Solution:** A resilient stop body with a friction member on the rocker lifts the loose pawl clear of the ratchet teeth during carriage return.

**How it works:** The carriage spring turns the ratchet wheel, with a fixed tooth and a loose tooth on a pivoting shift rocker. When a key is pressed the rocker swings back and the loose tooth exits and hits the resilient stop. The friction finger lifts off the wheel so stepping runs freely. On carriage return the wheel turns backward and takes the stop body along against its spring, which moves the loose pawl out of the tooth circle so it follows the stop under its own spring and does not rattle. Only in the end position does friction act.
