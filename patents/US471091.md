## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The Hammond typewriter needed a ribbon for inking the type segments.

**Solution:** Spring-held inking pads on the type-wheel guard are pressed on the type-segment faces by levers worked by a swinging bar linked to the driving arms, replacing the ribbon.

**How it works:** Absorbent pads sit above the sides of the type-wheel guard where the segments rest. Bow springs pull them away from the type. Pad levers run to the front, where a loosely jointed swinging bar linked to both driving-lever arms moves them, with set-screws to adjust pad pressure. A retracting spring on the bar returns it and also restores the driving levers and segments, replacing their springs.
