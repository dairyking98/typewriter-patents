## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The same check-raising problem; the earlier design needed better detail construction.

**Solution:** Improved check punch: punches in a bearing block select characters on an indicator plate and a lever drives the chosen punch through the check.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
