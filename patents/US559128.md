## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines of that class in which the types are carried on pivoted type-bars, and which are commonly known as typebar or basket machines.

**How it works (reading):** A full-keyboard basket typewriter combining variable-spacing carriage feed with new ribbon shifting, platen turning, carriage stop and bell devices.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typebar (basket) machine with a key per character needs paper feed matched to the width of each type, plus improvements elsewhere in the machine.

**Solution:** A full-keyboard basket typewriter combining variable-spacing carriage feed with new ribbon shifting, platen turning, carriage stop and bell devices.

**How it works:** Keys in banks act through stems and levers on typebars in a basket, mounted on a cast base with a top plate on standards. The carriage spring drum carries a cam, and a detent-bar and spring pawl with a releasing dog form the spacing mechanism, with movable stops on a yoke so feed corresponds with type width. Ribbon spools are moved by slides, the platen has turning devices, and the carriage front bar carries a carriage stop and an alarm bell. The opening describes the figures only, not the details.
