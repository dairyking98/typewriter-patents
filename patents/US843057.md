## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Cards and sheets of different sizes slip on a flat platen and are not held in the printing position.

**Solution:** Work-holding and guarding strips are placed transversely and longitudinally on the flat platen and are adjustable, so they hold the top and bottom edges of a sheet.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** flat platen; holding strips; guard strips; frame
