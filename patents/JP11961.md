## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A compact machine to print letters or signs of any language in sequence onto a paper strip, with strip winding, advance and ribbon motion all done by the key stroke.

**Solution:** About twenty keys each swing a type arm carrying two types, two shift keys slide the type-arm carriage so one or the other type is at the print point, and a common bar under all key levers steps the strip drum, strip feed and ribbon by ratchets.

**How it works:** Each key lever touches a pivoted type arm on a carriage, so pressing a key throws that arm; each arm carries two (sometimes three) types. Two special keys on rocking arms, interlocked by a pivoted piece so only one is down, move the carriage through a rod and fork to bring the first or second type of every arm into position. Any key also lifts a cross bar whose finger rocks a lever, and that lever drives a ratchet that winds the printed strip onto a drum, another pawl that feeds the strip, and a third that moves the ink ribbon forward or backward depending on a shifted gear pair. A single adjustable spring returns the lever; a crank roller is used afterward to rewind the cut strip in reading order.
