## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**How it works (reading):** An endless band passes around an extra roller and the platen, holding the account sheet by itself, with a second alignment device on the roller support.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bookkeeping forms cannot be held on an ordinary typewriter because the paper rollers do not hold the account sheet.

**Solution:** An endless band passes around an extra roller and the platen, holding the account sheet by itself, with a second alignment device on the roller support.

**How it works:** A composite guide roller carries the band; the second device adjusts sideways and vertically on the roller support.
