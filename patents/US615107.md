## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check printing apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing or attaching on mailpieces

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improved apparatus for printing checks, drafts, or other similar papers, which improvements are fully described and illustrated in the following specification and the accompanying drawings, the novel features thereof being specified in the claims annexed to the said specification.

**How it works (reading):** A wheel with raised numbers and words on its outer edge is turned by a handle, and a movable platen roller is pushed inward against it to print the chosen number on the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check amounts were printed with separate type that was difficult to set and align for each check.

**Solution:** A wheel with raised numbers and words on its outer edge is turned by a handle, and a movable platen roller is pushed inward against it to print the chosen number on the paper.
