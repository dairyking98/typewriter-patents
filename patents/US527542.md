## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stabilising means therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates particularly to keyboards having a number of parallel, horizontal, pivoted lkeys having their free ends (the ends which are to be engaged by the fingers of the operator) normally elevated by the action of springs and designed to be depressed manually for operation.
