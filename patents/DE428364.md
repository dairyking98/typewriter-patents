**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A disk under braking, stored with protrusions, lets the pawls engage the ratchet wheel immediately after the return without a forward movement first. The text is cut off before the full mechanism is described.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After a carriage return the pawls do not engage the ratchet at once, causing creaking and irregular spacing at the start of the line.

**Solution:** A disk under braking, stored with protrusions, lets the pawls engage the ratchet wheel immediately after the return without a forward movement first. The text is cut off before the full mechanism is described.
