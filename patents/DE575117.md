## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Toy typewriters

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a writing device for children with a slide against the writing surface, under the action of a Feedback spring standing type wheel, which is in a on a longitudinal bridge in the writing direction sliding sheet is guided.

**How it works (reading):** The type wheel sits on a vertical pin in a sliding carriage so the printed character is visible; a return spring acting in a horizontal plane also advances the wheel one letter space through a transport lever engaging a toothed bridge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type wheels on horizontal axles hide the printed character, and ratchet-plus-weight advancing forces the user to lift the wheel back by hand.

**Solution:** The type wheel sits on a vertical pin in a sliding carriage so the printed character is visible; a return spring acting in a horizontal plane also advances the wheel one letter space through a transport lever engaging a toothed bridge.
