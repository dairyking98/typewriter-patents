## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper trolley elevator device Drive by an electric motor, in particular such a device in which the electric motor only runs during the elevator or return movement of the car.

**How it works (reading):** A centrifugal friction clutch between motor and carriage transmits torque that falls with speed, so the carriage arrives at the stop with nearly the same energy on any stroke length.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On long carriage returns the motor accelerates the carriage too much, so the impact at the stop varies with stroke length.

**Solution:** A centrifugal friction clutch between motor and carriage transmits torque that falls with speed, so the carriage arrives at the stop with nearly the same energy on any stroke length.
