## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check perforator) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in devices adapted to prevent checks, drafts, &c.

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable cap carries downward prongs shaped as numerals; it is pressed down onto the check, which is fed through a slot over an inking pad in the base.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks and drafts can be altered or raised after writing, so a simple, cheap device is needed to print the amount.

**Solution:** A rotatable cap carries downward prongs shaped as numerals; it is pressed down onto the check, which is fed through a slot over an inking pad in the base.

**Features:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md)
