## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The cash register print hammer must print only at the right point in the checkout sequence, after the drawer is in its final position.

**Solution:** The print hammer is held by pawls and released by a push rod on the checkout gear only at the print point, then returns to rest automatically after printing; the drawer release and print release are interlocked with the angle levers.
