## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ticket printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**How it works (reading):** A ticket printer prints part of each imprint on the continuous strip, cuts the ticket, then prints the rest, with a detachable print head for serial and date numbers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tickets need to be printed partly while still on the continuous paper web, and the other part after cutting.

**Solution:** A ticket printer prints part of each imprint on the continuous strip, cuts the ticket, then prints the rest, with a detachable print head for serial and date numbers.

**Features:** [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
