## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side lays or gauges

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to means for controlling and properly positioning and squaring the paper as it is introduced into the machine.

**How it works (reading):** A side edge gauge is carried behind the plane of the paper table and apron on a connecting member that passes through the gap between table and apron, so it can be slid along the platen and bears on the paper edge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be squared to the side of the platen by hand, and an edge gauge on the paper table cannot be adjusted without interfering with the table and apron.

**Solution:** A side edge gauge is carried behind the plane of the paper table and apron on a connecting member that passes through the gap between table and apron, so it can be slid along the platen and bears on the paper edge.
