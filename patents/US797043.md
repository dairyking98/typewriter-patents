**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary character such as an underscore is brought into the regular printing action at the operator's will, so it prints in place with the letter it belongs to.

**Solution:** An auxiliary character such as an underscore is brought into the regular printing action at the operator's will, so it prints in place with the letter it belongs to.
