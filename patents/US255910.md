## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier stenograph keys needed a paired layout and a marker that perforated paper; the marker had to be simpler.

**Solution:** Bent-bar finger keys with a single thumb key press fixed points on the keys against an inked ribbon to mark the paper, a spacing key drives the feed plate, and a ribbon reverses automatically.

**Features:** [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
