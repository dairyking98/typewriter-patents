## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter or ticket-printing machine needs to reverse its ribbon travel automatically at the end of the ribbon.

**Solution:** A pitman-driven ratchet turns the ribbon shaft, and a worm-geared cam with a spring-loaded lever snaps the shaft sideways to swap which ribbon drum is engaged.

**How it works:** Two ribbon drums on shafts carry miter wheels, held by springs. A cross shaft with a miter wheel at each end can slide so only one end meshes with a drum wheel at a time. An eccentric on the main drive shaft reciprocates a pitman whose blade turns a ratchet wheel on the cross shaft a step per stroke, winding the ribbon. A worm on a sleeve turns a spur wheel carrying a cam; as it turns, a lug tensions a forked spring, and at the cam jump-off a follower lever snaps over and shifts the cross shaft to the other drum. This reverses winding. The text ends before the final action is described.
