## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to furnish a ribbon for type-writing machines which may be readily removed or replaced, and also to furnish a carrying device for said ribbon which will allow the same to present a new surface to every type as it is struck by the key.

**How it works (reading):** A removable ribbon carried on a sliding plate, advanced by a ratchet wheel and thumb-screw so each strike meets new ribbon.
