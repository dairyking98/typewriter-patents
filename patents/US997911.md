## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen needed bearings in the carriage that let it be removed and replaced easily yet stay fixed when in use.

**Solution:** Fit flanged bearing sleeves on the platen shaft ends, seated in depressions in the carriage arms and clamped by sliding bolts that can be withdrawn to lift the platen out.

**How it works:** Each end of the platen shaft carries a sleeve in which the shaft turns freely, confined between the knob hub and a shaft shoulder. The sleeve seats in a depression in the carriage arm. A bolt on each arm, moved by a knob and guided by a slot and screw, has a tongue that enters between the sleeve's flanges and locks it against rotation and longitudinal movement. Sliding the bolts back frees the platen to be lifted out.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen shaft; bearing sleeve; carriage arm seat; sliding locking bolt; bolt knob; line-space ratchet wheel; platen knob
