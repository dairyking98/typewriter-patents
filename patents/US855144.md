## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Connecting the keys to the type bars gives the key lever too much leverage over the type bar during the printing stroke, which harshens the key stroke and spoils the impression.

**Solution:** Type bars pivot on levers of the first order whose arms roll on a common fulcrum bar through curved treads, so the leverage is greatest at the start of the key stroke and eases during the rest of it.
