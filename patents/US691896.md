## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Aligning decimal columns of figures when typing tables is hard with the plain fixed carriage scale and pointers.

**Solution:** A one-piece detachable auxiliary scale clipped to the machine frame, carrying series of ciphers, is used with a clip-on pointer on the carriage's fixed scale to position the carriage so the decimals line up.

**How it works:** A plate-metal scale with a 45-degree flange is clasped onto the angle-bar at the front of the machine. The flange carries a notch and groups of ciphers (000, 000, 000) marking units, hundreds, thousands and millions. A bent sheet-metal pointer straddles the carriage's fixed scale at the column where the decimal is to fall. The operator moves the carriage until the cipher matching the first digit of the amount lines up with the pointer, so every number's decimal lands in the same column.
