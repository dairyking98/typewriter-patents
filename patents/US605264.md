## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column or tabular work on a typewriter needs ready, accurate stopping of the carriage at column positions.

**Solution:** Column stops on a bar behind the feed rack, engaged by a tabulating key added to the spacing bar, with a spring-cushioned stop to absorb shock.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 2, the feed-rack carries two dogs on a rocker linked to the keys and spacing bar. A bar parallel to the rack has teeth (column stops), fixed or adjustable on a supporting rod. A tabulating key on the spacing bar lets the carriage jump until a stop is caught; a sprung stem and nuts relieve shocks at stoppage. Fixed stops suit telegraph blanks with five columns.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** feed rack; dog; rocker; spacing bar; tabulating key; column stop; stop bar; spring; stem; lock-nut

**Numbers:** five columns for telegraph blanks; four teeth on the stop bar

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 2: adds column-stop tabulation
