## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Center-strike typewriter carriages need a smooth, tight mounting on their ways and a convenient line-spacing action.

**Solution:** The carriage rides on pairs of concave rollers that wrap a fixed rod enclosed in a casing, and a front push-button operates the push and check pawls together for line spacing.

**How it works:** A rod above the top plate is surrounded on three sides by a casing forming trackways. Sets of three auxiliary roller carriages, each with two concave rollers, bear on the rod and carry the casing, whose lugs hinge the platen frame so it can be lifted to inspect writing. A single wheel under the front bar runs on the top plate. A push-button at the carriage end releases the check pawl and drives the push pawl to turn the platen one or two line spaces, then locks it when released.
