## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a holder for supporting a note-book or copy in position for convenient reading of the notes or copy by a typewriter or type-setter and for indicating the successive lines of copy to facilitate the correct reproduction of it in print or type;

**How it works (reading):** A bed with a flange supports the book, with a screw-driven ratchet and rack-bar indicator arm that moves the line-indicator slide across the copy.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A note-book or copy must be held for convenient reading and its lines indicated while typing or setting type.

**Solution:** A bed with a flange supports the book, with a screw-driven ratchet and rack-bar indicator arm that moves the line-indicator slide across the copy.
