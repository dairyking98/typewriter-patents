## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punching machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to those machines which are employed to punch a check with ligu res indicating the amount for which it is made, so as to prevent any subsequent alteration or raising of the check.

**How it works (reading):** Vertically arranged punches with dies are bearing on characters on a fixed indicator plate and are driven by levers to punch the amount into the check.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check amounts can be raised after writing, so the amount must be made tamper-proof.

**Solution:** Vertically arranged punches with dies are bearing on characters on a fixed indicator plate and are driven by levers to punch the amount into the check.

**Features:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
