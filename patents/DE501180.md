**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on a device for he.

**How it works (reading):** A counter-pressure roller on its own independent support backs the wheel opposite the die wheel, and a second die wheel offset by 180 degrees can roll types on both sides at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Rolling types on a thin pin in hard metal needs high embossing pressure, which deflects the pin and makes the types imprecise.

**Solution:** A counter-pressure roller on its own independent support backs the wheel opposite the die wheel, and a second die wheel offset by 180 degrees can roll types on both sides at once.
