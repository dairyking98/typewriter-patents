## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for attaching machines to baseboards

**How it works (reading):** A folding clamp on an articulated arm slides in a slot on a plate; a wing nut on a screw bolt tightens it, so any typewriter can be fixed to the table.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter clamp screw-fastened to a table top could only fit one machine size or needed slotted plates.

**Solution:** A folding clamp on an articulated arm slides in a slot on a plate; a wing nut on a screw bolt tightens it, so any typewriter can be fixed to the table.
