## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A ribbon mechanism should be simple and durable and reverse the ribbon feed automatically when the ribbon reaches its limit in either direction.

**Solution:** A pawl arm with a shiftable pivot and springs is thrown over centre when the ribbon reaches its limit, engaging the opposite spool ratchet to reverse feed.

**How it works:** The ribbon winds between two spools at opposite sides of the machine, each fixed to a ratchet wheel. A pawl arm pivoted on a crank arm of a rocking shaft engages one ratchet at one end and the other ratchet at the other. A pivot pin in a slot of the pawl arm moves between two end bearings, and springs make the pull unbalanced at either limit so that the arm flips and engages the opposite ratchet. The shaft is reciprocated as the machine is operated. Sheet metal clips on the pawl keep engagement with the ratchet teeth. It is applied to a vertical-platen carriage machine of the inventor's earlier application 201,284.
