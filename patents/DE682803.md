## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Justifying a line on a typewriter requires typing the line twice to spread the remaining space over the word spaces.

**Solution:** Double-wedge filler pieces stored in a magazine are set according to the line remainder and, when the space bar is hit, widen the spaces so the fair copy is exactly line length.
