## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cipher writing device, OCR garbled) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cryptography or similar special procedures in a franking system; Typewriters for ciphering or deciphering cryptographic text wherein the operative connections between the keys and the type-bars are automatically and continuously permuted, during operation, by a coding or key member the connections being mechanical

**How it works (reading):** Key levers on a Bickensderfer-type machine rotate a printing cylinder to the chosen type and then lock it and press it onto the paper; a cipher strip is carried on a swinging frame.

**Solution:** Key levers on a Bickensderfer-type machine rotate a printing cylinder to the chosen type and then lock it and press it onto the paper; a cipher strip is carried on a swinging frame.
