## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to matrix-making machines, and is in the nature of an improvement on the machines set forth in my patents, Nos.

**How it works (reading):** An escapement lever and eight ratchet-wheels with pins and slots give the feed for each character, with electric contacts on the key seats.
