## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** Its general object is to provide improved mechanlsm of the character specified.

**How it works (reading):** A vibratory ribbon carrier that can be moved longitudinally so the ribbon's printing portion sits in or out of the striking position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon must be moved out of the way of the types when the machine is used for stencil cutting.

**Solution:** A vibratory ribbon carrier that can be moved longitudinally so the ribbon's printing portion sits in or out of the striking position.
