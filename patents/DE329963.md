## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ratchet teeth striking locking pawls on a rigid frame make a loud impact noise.

**Solution:** The switch lock carrier is isolated from the machine frame by elastic rings, rubber or cork strips, or buffer springs under a floating bearing bush, damping the impact.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** switch lock carrier; ratchet axle; bearing bush; buffer springs; elastic rings
