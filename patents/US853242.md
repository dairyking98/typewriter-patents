## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Underwood, the framework margin stop can only adjust over about half the carriage travel, so the left-hand margin cannot be widened much.

**Solution:** An auxiliary stop on the carriage, which can be brought into use or held out of use, engages the framework margin stop further ahead of the usual carriage stop, shortening the carriage travel and widening the margin; both stops are released by one key.
