## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters could not emphasise words in bold; overstrike shadow writing looked blotchy and unclear for m and w.

**Solution:** After typing a word, shift the platen sideways by a tiny, precisely limited amount and overstrike it so the letters thicken.

**How it works:** The platen or paper carrier is shifted a small amount by a handwheel with graduated scale or by a stepped plate, so the second impression partly overlaps the first. The user returns the carriage, shifts, retypes the word, and shifts back. A variant limits backspace lever travel with a conical pin and notched disc to move the carriage a tiny distance.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** handwheel; scale; rack carrier; pin; stepped plate; backspace lever; conical pin; notched disc

**Improves on:**

- shadow writing: smaller offset so letters thicken instead of doubling
