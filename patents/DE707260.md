## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punched card tabulating machine printing)

**How it works (reading):** The type-wheel holder is tensioned by a mainspring driven by a reciprocating member, and released by two locks, one released each cycle and one only on a card hole; the holder itself acts as the hammer.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A date printer on a card-controlled tabulator used a separate hammer, and the known spring-loaded version needed manual resetting.

**Solution:** The type-wheel holder is tensioned by a mainspring driven by a reciprocating member, and released by two locks, one released each cycle and one only on a card hole; the holder itself acts as the hammer.

**How it works:** Mainspring cocked by the machine's back-and-forth member during rest, held by two locks; the card-sensed lock releases it and the holder is flung onto the paper and returned to start.
