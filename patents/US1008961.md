**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Separating articles from piles using friction forces between articles and separator; Transporting of cards between stations with continuous movement

**How it works (reading):** A light envelope-feeding device on the platen frame moves envelopes from a stack to the platen, with a delivery device that drops each printed envelope into a receptacle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Envelopes must be fed to the platen of a typewriter without adding much bulk or weight.

**Solution:** A light envelope-feeding device on the platen frame moves envelopes from a stack to the platen, with a delivery device that drops each printed envelope into a receptacle.
