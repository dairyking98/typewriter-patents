## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Means to give uniformity of impression

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an adjusting device for changing the strength of the Type lever springs on hand-operated typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A pointer linked to the spring holder moves inside the frame and shows the spring tension on a scale visible through a window, while a separate adjuster changes the tension.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type lever spring tension can only be judged by trial typing, so the operator cannot tell how strong the springs are set.

**Solution:** A pointer linked to the spring holder moves inside the frame and shows the spring tension on a scale visible through a window, while a separate adjuster changes the tension.
