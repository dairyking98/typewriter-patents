## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punching machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to construct an improved machine for punching checks.

**How it works (reading):** A check-punching machine in which a rock-shaft operates the punches or dies and a feed plate advances the check, with adjustable holders for the check.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text states the object only: an improved machine for punching checks, with the feed and punch operation rebuilt.

**Solution:** A check-punching machine in which a rock-shaft operates the punches or dies and a feed plate advances the check, with adjustable holders for the check.

**How it works:** A feed plate moves the check in steps; a rock-shaft drives the male punches against female dies; a presser plate with spring holds the check, and adjusting devices fix its position.
