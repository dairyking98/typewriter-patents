## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On portable typewriters the raised finger plate of the line-shift lever raises the machine's height and prevents storing it in a flat case.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded finger-plate lever hinged on the line lever is pressed flush by the case lid when the case is put on.

**How it works:** A two-armed lever turns on a horizontal pin in the web of the line lever, whose rear arm is spring-loaded against a bearing eye. Its front arm lies under the line lever and a vertical finger plate passes through a slot in it. In use the plate stands up and moving it moves the line lever. When the case is placed over the machine the lid pushes the plate down flush against the spring; lifting the case lets the spring raise it.
