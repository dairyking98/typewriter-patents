## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards must be fed to the platen in alignment, and a clean gage is needed on the paper table.

**Solution:** A laterally adjustable gage plate with a spring clip and latch attaches to the paper table and has a flange to guide the card edge.
