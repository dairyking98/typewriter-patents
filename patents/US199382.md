## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-bars must be held in constant relation so all strike at the same place without warping.

**Solution:** Mount the type-bar trunnions in concentric grooves of a flat annular metal disk with radial slots and stops.

**How it works:** A metal annular disk set in the top plate has radial slots through its inner edge, forming arms; each arm has a concentric journal groove on its upper face with a partition across it. Each type-bar has a trunnion on each side lying in the grooves, so all bars are the same distance from the center and strike the same point. Levers and wires connect keys to bars; a platen on a long axle is driven by a weight and cord.
