## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mounting or connecting key buttons on or to key levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a further development of the subject of the patent 809 toi.

**How it works (reading):** The key base hooks through a closed slot into an incision in the type lever, and a helical compression spring between the key button and key lever bears on a cup-shaped hollow abutment, slit to let the key foot pass, with a nose on the lever engaging a cross slot to prevent rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The key lever and key base of a previously patented interlocking key were more complex than needed for a simple, easily replaced construction.

**Solution:** The key base hooks through a closed slot into an incision in the type lever, and a helical compression spring between the key button and key lever bears on a cup-shaped hollow abutment, slit to let the key foot pass, with a nose on the lever engaging a cross slot to prevent rotation.
