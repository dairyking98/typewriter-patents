> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a further development of the subject of the patent 809 toi. This relates to an operating button for office machines, in particular Typewriters, on which the key lever and the key base standing across from it interlock in such a way that they are under the action of an interposed spring can execute a limited relative movement. With one shown in the main patent Embodiment are the key base and the key lever arm as oppositely directed formed transversely standing forks, in the middle free space a helical compression spring is located. There is also a special safety link intended to limit the relative movement in the form of a detonation bar. On the other hand, in order to arrive at the simplest embodiment, in the arrangement according to the present invention, the key base with a closed Slot hooked into an incision in the type lever and the one surrounding the key base Helical compression spring arranged between key button and key lever.
