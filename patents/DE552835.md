## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Two rows of spring contacts, overlapping like roof tiles, form a circuit that closes only when a single key is pressed; two keys open the circuit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electric typewriter drives can fire on multiple simultaneous keys and jam the type lever.

**Solution:** Two rows of spring contacts, overlapping like roof tiles, form a circuit that closes only when a single key is pressed; two keys open the circuit.
