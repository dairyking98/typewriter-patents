## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The escapement-lever stops and the die-depressing device must be timed so a die is depressed only after the matrix has advanced the right distance.

**Solution:** Electromagnetic devices control the escapement stops and the die-depressing device through circuits closed by contacts on the character-board, holding the stops until the impression is made.
