## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-stroke machines need a simple way to feed, lift, reverse the ribbon and vary the lift with the platen shift.

**Solution:** Everything runs from the universal bar: spring pawls turn the ribbon spools, a lever lifts the guide, and the lift pivot rides on the platen shift bar so the lift grows when shifted.

**How it works:** Two spools sit on shafts with ratchets stepped by spring pawls from the universal bar, held by friction frames and retaining pawls. A lever hinged to the ribbon guide yoke is tilted by a link from the universal bar when a typebar strikes. Its fulcrum hangs in a yoke on the shift bar, so shifting moves the pivot and raises the guide. A laterally sliding bar with cams retracts one retaining pawl to reverse the feed.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; ratchet; feed pawl; retaining pawl; ribbon guide; lift lever; universal bar; shift bar; reversing bar
