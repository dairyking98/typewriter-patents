## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a variable feed for the paper-carriage of a type writing machine, so that on striking a key-1e ver actuating a narrow type like that of the letter i, the paper-carriage will be fed a less distance than when a wider letter or character is to be written, thus accomplishing what is known among printers as spacing the type, and thereby making the written page appear like ordinary print.

**How it works (reading):** A ratchet train with three-to-one tooth ratios lets a key pawl feed the carriage one, one-and-a-half or two spaces depending on the character.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed one-tooth carriage feed makes typewriting look unlike print because narrow and wide letters take the same space.

**Solution:** A ratchet train with three-to-one tooth ratios lets a key pawl feed the carriage one, one-and-a-half or two spaces depending on the character.

**How it works:** The carriage rack meshes with a pinion on a shaft carrying two ratchet wheels, one with three times the pinion's teeth and a larger one with the same number of teeth as the pinion. A sliding dog under thumbnut control and pawls stop the pinion after one, one-and-a-half or two units, taking the narrow i as the unit and giving a and M more. Key levers and a bell-crank lever operating through the space bar select the feed after each depression and release of the key, while a spring wheel and strap drive the carriage.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; rack; pinion; ratchet wheel; pawl; dog; thumbnut; bell-crank lever; key lever; space bar; spring wheel and strap

**Numbers:** three spacings: 1, 1.5 and 2 units
