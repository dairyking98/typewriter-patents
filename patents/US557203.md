## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of improvements in attachments for type-writing machines in which the object is to enable the operator to see the characters as they are writ ten, and my particular improvement relates to the device which supports the reflecting medium employed.

**How it works (reading):** A thin steel bracket on the carriage that holds a rhomboidal reflecting prism in correct position, with overlapping parts that adjust for different frames.

**Solution:** A thin steel bracket on the carriage that holds a rhomboidal reflecting prism in correct position, with overlapping parts that adjust for different frames.
