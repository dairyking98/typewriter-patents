## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter should fold into small compass without separating carriage, feed and ribbon.

**Solution:** Carriage bed, feed and ribbon mechanism hang on pivoted side bars that fold over, with a latch locking them to the shift linkage.

**How it works:** Side bars pivot on the frame and carry the carriage bed and ribbon spools. In normal position projections of the frame guide them, and a latch ties them to the shift links so case shifting moves everything together. Unlatching lets the whole assembly fold forward to occupy less vertical space. Key lever pivot bars and a shift shaft with counterbalance springs drive the shift.

**Features:** [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** side bar; carriage bed; ribbon spool; latch; shift link; bell crank; counterbalance spring; pivot stud
