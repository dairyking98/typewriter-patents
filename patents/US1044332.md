## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a new and improved column-skipper, whereby the operator can select the particular column at which the carriage is to stop in the operation of tabulating.

**How it works (reading):** Column-selecting keys raise a selecting bar by an amount set by the key, and tappets of graduated lengths with contact points stop the carriage at the chosen column.
