## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type must be cleaned of ink and dirt.

**Solution:** A frame-mounted raisable bristle belt run by a crank brushes the typefaces.

**How it works:** A frame on the typewriter has guideways with racks. Blocks slide in the guideways carrying two shafts; the lower shaft has spur gears meshing the racks, and a crank raises the blocks. Bevel gears drive a crown belt-wheel from the shaft. A bristled belt runs over this wheel and a second loosely mounted, adjustable wheel on a pivoting bracket, brushing the type. A screw adjusts belt tension.

**Parts:** frame; guideway; rack; block; shaft; spur gear; bevel gear; belt-wheel; bristle belt; crank; adjusting screw
