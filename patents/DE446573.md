## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A spring-loaded print hammer overshoots its stop and rebounds in repeated oscillations, which can disturb the cash register sequence and cause wrong entries.

**Solution:** A locking device engages with every impression and holds the print hammer back in its rest position, with several cam and lever arrangements shown.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Cash register printing device. The invention relates to a cash register printing device with spring-loaded pressure hammer, which is force-locked by means of suitable connecting links cooperates with a cam disk. The invention is intended to prevent the print hammer from being too far after its stop, i.e. H. beyond its rest position, it snaps back and as a result executes repeated oscillations, which under certain circumstances disrupt the orderly flow of the checkout process and can lead to incorrect registrations. This purpose is achieved in that with every pressure a locking device comes into action which automatically locks the print hammer back in its rest position.
