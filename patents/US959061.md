## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved tabulating or column stop mechanism for typewriting machines designed to effect the release of the carriage from the control of the usual letter-spacing devices and permit the carriage to move, under the action of its actuating spring.

**How it works (reading):** Column stops adjustable along the carriage meet a key-operated stop member moved into their path, with the carriage released from the letter-spacing escapement to run under its spring.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing items in vertical columns needs the carriage to run to preset points without repeated spacing.

**Solution:** Column stops adjustable along the carriage meet a key-operated stop member moved into their path, with the carriage released from the letter-spacing escapement to run under its spring.

**How it works:** Adjustable stops are set along a longitudinal member of the paper carriage. A key lever moves a stop lever on the machine frame into the path of those stops. When the key is pressed, the connecting bar operates the carriage-release devices so the carriage leaves the escapement and travels under its actuating spring until a stop strikes the stop member. Shown on an Oliver machine with shift frame, typebars and universal bar.
