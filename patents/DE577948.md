## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type wheel machines selected by a pointer on a drawing board were unlike keyboard machines to operate and the mechanism covered the character plate.

**Solution:** A pivoting character plate with notched fields lies under a ball-jointed stylus; sliding the stylus sets the type wheel through a rack, and pressing the plate down swings the type wheel to the platen.

**How it works:** A type wheel and ink roller sit on a drive lever pivoting on the base plate and held away from the roller by a tension spring. A rack that engages a gear on the wheel axis sets the wheel. The rack end joins a connecting rod under the plate, which holds a bracket and an oblique setting arm with a ball tip. Moving the ball into a recessed notch sets the character and the plate stays clear. Pressing the plate down with the arm turns a crank bend on its hinge axis, which swings the drive lever against its spring and prints. A second lever on the drive lever engages the carriage rack to space the carriage, and a universal key can space without printing.
