## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A spur-gear clutch where the drive wheel slides axially off pawls on the shift shaft flange.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage-release clutches either wear the rack by lifting it, or rasp loudly as pawls ride over teeth, or use weak side claw couplings.

**Solution:** A spur-gear clutch where the drive wheel slides axially off pawls on the shift shaft flange.

**How it works:** The drive wheel sits loosely on the shift shaft and a spring presses it against a flanged socket fixed to the shaft with the ratchet. Two spring pawls on the flange engage teeth of the drive wheel that have slanted end faces. The carriage release lever pulls the wheel away, so the pawls disengage and slide on the hub and the carriage runs back freely. On release the spring presses the wheel back and the pawls re-engage silently.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** drive wheel; shift shaft; socket with flange; ratchet; pawl; spring; release lever

**Improves on:**

- lifting-rack and ratchet-pawl derailleurs: quiet, strong, easy to make
