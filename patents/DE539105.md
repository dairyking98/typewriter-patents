## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (raised point printing for blind) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing in Braille or with keyboards specially adapted for use by blind or disabled persons; Braille printing

**How it works (reading):** Dots are formed in a rotary press in one pass and covered with a resistant but elastic mass on both sides, giving two or four pages per revolution.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Braille dots pressed into paper wear out quickly under repeated reading, and stencil-made dots are costly.

**Solution:** Dots are formed in a rotary press in one pass and covered with a resistant but elastic mass on both sides, giving two or four pages per revolution.

**Features:** [For the blind](../features/use-blind.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
