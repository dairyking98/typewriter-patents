## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to escapement mechanisms, and is applicable to typewriters, adding machines, etc.

**How it works (reading):** Carriage-propelling spring or carriage weight is used to accelerate the printing and spacing parts once the impression is made, giving rapid step-by-step carriage travel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement movement is slow and parts controlled by the carriage do not accelerate after printing, causing interference.

**Solution:** Carriage-propelling spring or carriage weight is used to accelerate the printing and spacing parts once the impression is made, giving rapid step-by-step carriage travel.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
