## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter with a flat pressure surface, few parts relative to the characters, simple key-to-lever connection and escapement spacing.

**Solution:** Typebars with button levers print directly onto paper over a flat rubber band on a crescent-section rod instead of a cylinder, with a spring-drum carriage escapement.

**How it works:** Button levers on the base link to type arms hung on a curved rail with type-rod holders, each type directly printing and inking from concave colour pads. A rod of crescent cross section across the carriage carries a flat rubber band, pinned at its perforated ends, giving the paper a flat bed, while a normal pressure roller with rubber bands feeds the paper. The carriage is pulled by a resilient spring drum whose cover comes off for cleaning and whose tension is set by a screw-threaded shaft and a pawl. An escapement of two pawls, driven by a swinging bracket, releases the carriage step by step.
