## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Box lid opening springs and ball locks must work in all climates.

**Solution:** A screw bolt through a clamping ring sets the spring pressure, and a screw cap sets the spring that presses the locking ball, so both can be tuned.
