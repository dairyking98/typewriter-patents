> Description Translated from German The invention aims to provide the device for automatic opening and locking of box lids according to patent 401 597 so that for all occurring conditions with regard to the air temperature and humidity, the respective Position of the box etc. such an adjustment of the opening and inhibiting or Locking means is made possible that an always safe effect is guaranteed. For this purpose, according to the invention, the opening and Inhibiting parts designed so that the pressure effect of one of the springs lifting the lid and the braking effect of the clamping ball lock can be regulated as required.
