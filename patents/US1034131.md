## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Envelopes and letters must be typed automatically from a perforated sheet, without tearing the sheet when it is fed and stopped.

**Solution:** A typewriter's key bars are driven by solenoids energized from a perforated sheet that passes over a contact roll; the contact rolls also drive the sheet so it is separated before a perforation edge can be reached.

**How it works:** The perforated sheet passes over a common contact roll; each key bar has a solenoid and core connected to it, energized through the sheet, and rotary contact wheels drive the sheets.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** contact rolls; solenoids; cores; springs; perforated sheets
