## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide a new and improved type action for type-writing machines arranged to positively and accurately actuate the type-bars to insure proper printing visible to the operator.

**How it works (reading):** Each typebar has a toothed fulcrum end engaged by a sliding toothed rack driven by its key lever.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar action needs to move bars positively and accurately for front-visible printing.

**Solution:** Each typebar has a toothed fulcrum end engaged by a sliding toothed rack driven by its key lever.

**How it works:** Typebars rest horizontally in radial guideways of a segmental basket and strike rearward and upward to a common point. Each key lever ends in a fork whose arm shifts a rack forward; rack teeth mesh with teeth on the hooked fulcrum end of the typebar and swing it to vertical. A notch in the rack stops the bar at the top so it cannot overswing. Each bar has an inking device at its outer end.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)

**Parts:** key lever; fork; rack; typebar; segment basket; bearing ring; inking device; platen
