## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar drive needed to also advance the carriage and shift the ribbon gradually without extra linkage.

**Solution:** Upright rods linking key levers to the rear slotted ends of the typebars carry arms riding on a roller, so pulling them down also moves them forward, swinging the bar and driving carriage and ribbon.

**How it works:** Each key lever pulls down an upright rod whose pin sits in an open slot in the rear end of the sheet-metal typebar. A short rear arm with a curved underside rides on a fixed rod or roller, which pushes the rod forward as it descends. The pin moves along the slot and swings the typebar to the platen. The same rod movement progressively moves the paper carriage and switches the ribbon. Typebars sit in forked arms detachably fixed on a curved carrier and height-adjustable.
