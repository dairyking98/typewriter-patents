> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE. The subject of the invention is a type lever movement device for typewriters, in which upright rods attack at the rear ends of the type lever, which hinged to the button levers and slidably connected to the type levers so that that when a key is depressed, the corresponding type lever by means of the upright Rod swung around its fulcrum to will. The novelty is that the attacking at the rear ends of the type lever upright bars above their connection with the button levers are provided with arms are that rest on a rod or roller and are designed so that the upright Rods, when pulled down by the downward movement of their associated key lever, forward against the Key board are moved out and thereby the drive of the associated type lever that Gradually move the paper carriage and switch the ribbon.
