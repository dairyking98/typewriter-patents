## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-stroke typebar action needs a light, yielding touch that is still fast and can deliver a powerful blow, even for manifolding.

**Solution:** Connect the key lever, an intermediate lever and the typebar through helical springs so the typebar is thrown by stored energy and momentum rather than driven rigidly.

**How it works:** The key lever's rear end is under an adjustable compression spring. An intermediate lever is joined to the key lever by one helical spring and to a rear-downward extension of the typebar by another. At the start of the stroke the intermediate lever's upper end engages an inclined shoulder on the typebar and starts it; then the springs stretch, give a progressively stiffer touch, and the typebar is thrown to the platen, continuing by momentum even after the key is released. On release the springs pull everything back quickly.
