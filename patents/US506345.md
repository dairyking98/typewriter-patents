## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators get no warning that the writing is nearing the bottom of the sheet.

**Solution:** A tympan sheet with folded brass indicators that carry the paper and trip a bell lever as they rise to set levels.

**How it works:** A sheet of good paper (the tympan) is cut to the paper size, with two folded brass indicators clinched on it, forming a pocket that holds the edge of the typing sheet. As the paper and tympan feed upward, the indicator reaches a removable arm on the envelope-holder, aiding the eye, and its fold lifts a spring lever on a carriage-mounted frame. The lever is released and its hammer strikes a bell. The first indicator warns that only the closing lines of a letter remain, the second that there is no room for another line. A black line on the tympan marks each level.
