## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a tray-type Japanese typewriter, the horizontal-writing feed is always locked, so back-spacing it needs a way that unlocks, steps back and relocks in one action.

**Solution:** One press of a back key drives a reverse pawl that engages the feed gear, lifts the feed pawls out, turns the gear back a set amount, then lets the feed pawls drop back in and lock it.

**How it works:** Pressing the lever-shaped back key straightens a toggle of two levers and slides a bar carrying a reverse pawl. The pawl enters a tooth space of the sideways feed gear, and its tip pushes up a movable piece holding the feed pawl and fixed pawl, freeing them from the gear. As the bar continues, the reverse pawl turns the gear backward. At the set point the pawl tip passes beyond the supporting underside, the feed pawls drop into the teeth and lock the gear. Releasing the key lets springs return the levers and pawl.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Sideways-feed back-spacing device in a Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a back-spacing device for the sideways feed of a Japanese-script typewriter in which one unit, made up of the paper cylinder and the printing mechanism, is moved to any point over a flat type tray to print. To back-space the character feed on the gear or rack of its horizontal-writing feed: first, a reverse pawl is engaged in a tooth space; second, the reverse pawl then pushes up and supports the feed pawl part, taking the feed pawls out of the feed teeth; third, the motion of the reverse pawl is turned into a backward turning of the feed teeth; when the backward turning reaches a set point, that is its limit, the reverse pawl gives up its support of the feed pawls [the "fourth" step, not numbered here]; fifth, the feed pawls drop at once into the feed teeth and lock them completely, and the back-spacing of the horizontal character feed is finished. Its object is to make a horizontal-writing feed that is always fully locked and never slips perform the intended back-space directly, by one press of a "back key", for the set spacing.
