## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for guiding very stiff cards and the like around the cylindrical platens of typewriting machines, and particularly to those devices inwhich a rod extends over the platen from one end 'to the other of the platen frame, and carries a series of pendent fingers to hold the card against the platen, the pressure being produced by a spring;

**How it works (reading):** A detachable rod carrying pendent card fingers on a spring-pressed rock shaft, mounted on bearings that come off with the rod.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressure fingers holding stiff cards against the platen are hard to detach and give uneven pressure.

**Solution:** A detachable rod carrying pendent card fingers on a spring-pressed rock shaft, mounted on bearings that come off with the rod.
