## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates broadly to typewriting machines and more particularly to the escapement mechanism of a machine of a type designed to effect printing without producing noise.
