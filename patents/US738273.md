## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The coil-spring carriage drive gives uneven motion and the operator must lift and return the carriage by hand.

**Solution:** A piston and cylinder driven by elastic fluid pressure, with a cock operated by a keyboard key, both advances and returns the carriage.

**How it works:** A cylinder fixed to the frame parallel to the carriage slide holds a packed piston whose rod connects to the carriage. A reservoir feeds a cock with ports leading to both cylinder ends and an exhaust. Normally pressure acts on the operating side so the carriage advances under constant pressure, checked by the ordinary spacing escapement. Depressing a special key turns the cock plug, driving the carriage back. Near the end of its return the carriage trips a lever that reverses the cock, cushioning the carriage and setting it to advance again.
