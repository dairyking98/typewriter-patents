## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (shuttle drive for weaving looms) · **Subject (patent classes):** Smallware looms, i.e. looms for weaving ribbons or other narrow fabrics

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a shuttle drive for ribbon looms in which the reciprocating motion of the shuttle drive rack by one swinging on a swing arm in a circular arc, into a backdrop of the shooter's drive rack engaging driver role is conveyed.

**How it works (reading):** Side walls of the drive link are curved outward so the carrier roller does not act on the backdrop at the ends of its oscillation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A shuttle drive rack on a ribbon loom gets an excessive swing arm movement to obtain a longer shuttle standstill.

**Solution:** Side walls of the drive link are curved outward so the carrier roller does not act on the backdrop at the ends of its oscillation.
