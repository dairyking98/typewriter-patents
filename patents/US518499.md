## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bank-checks are hard to make out and can be raised after writing.

**Solution:** A check-writing machine with a circular track and spacing and printing mechanism that prints the characters in a fixed scheme across the check.

**Features:** [USE:check-writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
