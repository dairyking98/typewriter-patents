## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Known postcard holders are complicated, wear quickly, and their upper part cannot be locked in intermediate positions.

**Solution:** The pivoting upper part is pressed by a torsion spring against a horizontally rotatable flatted disc, so turning the disc sets the holder to the working or rest position.

**How it works:** The upper part of the holder, which also serves as line-height pointer, pivots about an axis parallel to the platen, mounted on a part fixed to the type lever segment. A torsion spring around the axis always presses it against a disc turning about a vertical axis. The disc has a flat; in one rotation position the upper part tips forward away from the platen, turning the disc swings it into contact with the platen. This allows cards of any thickness to be inserted and written on.
