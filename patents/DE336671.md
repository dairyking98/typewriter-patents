## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage switching device does not stop the carriage exactly, so the next line starts a letter space too far right and the left edge becomes uneven.

**Solution:** A loose latch on the ratchet is gradually brought into holding action so the carriage stops at the right place, and a part is kept out of action while writing.

There are other cases where the cart is improperly adjusted when the switching device comes back into engagement.
