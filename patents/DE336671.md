> Description Translated from German Trolley switchgear. The invention relates to Switching device for the paper trolley of typewriters. With typewriters, this becomes a finite paper trolley when you write usually switched to the left and dragged to the right at the beginning of a new line. With individual machines, however, the movement is reversed. When the car is pulled to the right, it is usually stopped by a stop, for example the margin stop, whose job it is to put the next line on a begin vertically under one of the letters on the previous line allow. For various reasons, however, it happens that the carriage switching device the carriage position does not regulate exactly with the result that the next line around begins one letter spacing too far to the right, so that an unequal Edge on the left side is created. There are other cases where the cart is improperly adjusted when the switching device comes back into engagement. The main purpose of the invention is to provide that such incorrect car positions will be reduced.
