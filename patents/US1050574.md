## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to supporting elements or carriages for the platens of typewriting machines and its general object is to provide improved devices of the character specified.

**How it works (reading):** A two-part platen carrier where the secondary part moves lengthwise relative to the primary part at a different speed, so a short stationary guide rail can serve a long carriage up to a 16 inch platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A long carriage needs a long guide rail, and the usual short rail of the Monarch machine cannot carry platens of different length.

**Solution:** A two-part platen carrier where the secondary part moves lengthwise relative to the primary part at a different speed, so a short stationary guide rail can serve a long carriage up to a 16 inch platen.
