**How it works (reading):** The wheel rim is a U-shaped channel holding an elastic insert ring whose outer layer is harder; the metal gear rim is shrunk onto it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A soft insert ring cannot take a shrunk-on metal rim without being pushed out or heat-damaged.

**Solution:** The wheel rim is a U-shaped channel holding an elastic insert ring whose outer layer is harder; the metal gear rim is shrunk onto it.
