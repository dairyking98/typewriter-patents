## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (combined typewriter and adding machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates chiefly to mechanism for automatically operatingv a key-board strument, said instrument in the present instance being a combined typewriting and adding machine.

**How it works (reading):** A motor-driven machine is mounted on and connected to the typewriter to operate its keyboard automatically, illustrating how it works.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A combined typewriting and adding machine needs an operator for display and demonstration, and the operator must be able to drive the keyboard automatically.

**Solution:** A motor-driven machine is mounted on and connected to the typewriter to operate its keyboard automatically, illustrating how it works.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
