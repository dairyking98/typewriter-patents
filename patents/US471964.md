## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a Remington the case change is made with a shift key-bar pressed by a finger, which occupies fingers needed for the character keys.

**Solution:** A detachable thumb-operated rock-shaft linked by a pitman to the existing shift key-bar so the left thumb shifts case.

**How it works:** A rock-shaft hangs on hooks from the lower front cross-bar of the frame. An inward branch of the shaft carries a pitman loop detachably embracing the upper-case key-bar. A thumb disk or lever rising from the shaft is pressed by the left thumb, rocking the shaft, depressing the key-bar and shifting the carriage to upper case. The right thumb stays on the space bar, leaving all fingers free for character keys.
