## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Folding the strip at word spaces needs a gentler and more accurate control than existing folding devices.

**Solution:** Two pairs of pliers clamp the strip at the hole ends, a cam brings them together to form the fold, and a sensing feeler locates the holes.
