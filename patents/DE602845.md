## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon microphones lose high-frequency response and vary unit to unit because the felt-stuffed space behind the ribbon resonates unpredictably.

**Solution:** One or more closed pressure spaces (resonators) are placed behind the ribbon so that a defined transverse resonance restores the high frequencies.
