## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Make a small portable type-wheel machine whose movements follow the user's natural hand and finger motions.

**Solution:** One thumb-operated horizontal main lever, swinging about an upright pin, carries the type wheel, presses it to the paper roller, inks it, and feeds the carriage rack.

**How it works:** The user sets an arm with a ball tip over a character plate, dropping the ball into a hemispherical recess; this moves a toothed bar that turns the type wheel to the chosen character. A key raises or lowers the wheel on its shaft to bring one of three type rows to the print point. The thumb swings the main lever: a double-armed lever on it first engages the carriage rack and feeds one tooth, hits a stop, and then its slot lets the main lever continue and press the wheel onto the paper roller. During the swing a hooked extension swings an arm carrying an ink roller around the wheel's rim, taking ink from a store roller on the main lever. A tooth on the base plate meshes with the wheel's gear at the end to lock its position exactly. A depressible stop limits the swing to feed the carriage without printing.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Type-wheel printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a printing machine with a type wheel that swings against the paper roller, characterised in that the type wheel is mounted to turn on a shaft on a horizontal main lever; the main lever can swing about an upright pin on the base plate; a double-armed lever can swing about a pivot on the main lever, and one arm of this lever engages the teeth of the rack of the carriage that carries the paper roller; so that the single main lever both carries and moves the type wheel and feeds the paper roller. Its object is to let every movement of the machine follow the natural position and finger movements of the user, and to make the construction simple and small so that it is easy to carry.
