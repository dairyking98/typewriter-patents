## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Cards must be fed and removed by hand, and the carriage must be lifted to handle them on a flat platen.

**Solution:** An endless carrier belt across the platen and around drums that feeds cards automatically to one side and carries them away to a receiver that stacks them in order.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** endless carrier belt; drums; receiver
