## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**How it works (reading):** The typewriter plate sits on a frame guided under the desk top and is articulated by crank arms on pins, with lever arms offset 90 degrees and pairs of connecting rods, so the plate swings between a lowered position inside the desk and a raised working position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known retractable typewriter sink tables are intricate in construction and need many fittings.

**Solution:** The typewriter plate sits on a frame guided under the desk top and is articulated by crank arms on pins, with lever arms offset 90 degrees and pairs of connecting rods, so the plate swings between a lowered position inside the desk and a raised working position.
