## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line locks that stop the typebars printing at the end of the line should be simple and still let the typist add a few more characters after the lock has acted.

**Solution:** A rotary catch on the carriage spring drum trips a rocker-rod that swings pins under the universal bar, blocking the keys at an adjustable carriage position.

**How it works:** The spring-drum shaft carries an adjustable catch-plate with a pin. As the carriage nears the line end the pin hooks the top of a vertical rod, which turns a rock-shaft under the key-levers. Pins on the rock-shaft swing beneath the universal bar so it cannot be pulled down and the typebars cannot print. A handle on the catch-plate arm adjusts the lock point, and a push-rod latch allows extra characters to be added. Shown on a Densmore machine.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** key-lever; universal bar; rock-shaft; rocker-rod; catch-plate; spring drum; paper carriage; driving strap

**Improves on:**

- Densmore line lock: applied to the Densmore; adds simple adjustable lock plus a way to add characters after locking
