## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sentence spacing and paragraph indentation need different carriage jumps, which the single space bar cannot give.

**Solution:** Separate superspacing keys, each with its own adjustable jump-controlling device, that move the carriage a set number of letter spaces, also usable for rapid carriage runs.

**Features:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
