## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When tabulating columns of figures, the operator needs to see at a glance how far the carriage must jump so the first numeral of each group lands in the right place, and fixed stops cannot give this.

**Solution:** A single variable stop, shifted relative to the machine frame, co-acts with one or more adjustable stops on the carriage, so the carriage check point can be varied by the operator to suit the number of characters in the group.
