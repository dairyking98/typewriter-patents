## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actuators for print wires using piezoelectric elements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in the type-writers formerly known as the Munson and more recently as the Chicago type-writers, and particularly to the hammersupport and operating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** The hammer is on an arm on a rock-shaft, and a parallel rock-shaft is linked to it by cranks and a link, with an impulse arm driven by the lifter. The rock-shafts are carried inside a U-shaped frame instead of outside it.

**Problem:** The earlier design put the rock-shafts outside the U-shaped frame, which the inventor wants to change.

**Solution:** The hammer is on an arm on a rock-shaft, and a parallel rock-shaft is linked to it by cranks and a link, with an impulse arm driven by the lifter. The rock-shafts are carried inside a U-shaped frame instead of outside it.
