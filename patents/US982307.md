## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wear in the joints between type bars and hangers leaves lost motion and looseness.

**Solution:** Bearing joints are made so that lost motion from wear can be taken up, using a headed screw in the segment whose head bears on the hanger foot to set the adjusted position.
