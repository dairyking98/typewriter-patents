## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ribbon fork on a typewriter lets the ribbon lie slack, so the type strikes with uneven force on it.

**Solution:** Press part of the ribbon fork resiliently against the platen by a spring, without hindering the ribbon's sideways movement.

**How it works:** The ribbon guide part with slots slides in fingers of the line-pointing device. A spring from the typebar bearing segment presses that device against the platen, while a cross rail limits its upward movement. This keeps the ribbon evenly tensioned near the platen and also presses paper against it at the print point. Platen and fork rise together on case shift.
