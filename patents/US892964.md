## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spacing device parts are scattered and hard to fit or remove from the paper roller frame.

**Solution:** All spacing parts are mounted on a plate that is fixed to the slide bearing of the paper roller frame, with a spring-held locking roller and eccentric.
