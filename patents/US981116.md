## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon copies need the original sheet, duplicate and carbon strip fed together, and an existing typewriter has no convenient way to carry them.

**Solution:** A drum attachment on brackets fixed to the carriage carries three rolls (original paper, duplicate paper and carbon strip) that feed together to the platen.
