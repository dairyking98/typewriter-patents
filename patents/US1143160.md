## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The Stenotype permutation keyboard requires the fingers to reach unusual positions, making chord keying hard.

**Solution:** A keyboard is contoured with keys of a given form so the fingers can reach the chord keys more easily, with a central vowel bank and two consonant banks.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
