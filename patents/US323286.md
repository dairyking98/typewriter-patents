## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenographic printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a type writing or printing machine of novel construction for recording speech and performing light work requiring rapidity of execution.

**How it works (reading):** A roller with a ratchet and pawl feeds paper, and thirteen keys act at once so their characters print in succession along a straight line.
