## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**How it works (reading):** A latch stored beside the rack is swung forward, moved sideways to engage the rack and push the carriage back, and held by a stop, so the return is limited; it releases when the button is let go and returns to rest.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A violently pressed back-switch button lets the paper carriage jump back further than intended.

**Solution:** A latch stored beside the rack is swung forward, moved sideways to engage the rack and push the carriage back, and held by a stop, so the return is limited; it releases when the button is let go and returns to rest.
