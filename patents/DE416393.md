## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-feed switching ends too late, so the platen is pushed sideways for part of the carriage return and the line straightness suffers.

**Solution:** A movable intermediate member on the carriage is disengaged by a control member on the margin stop before the carriage carriage return ends, so the line feed stops early and the margin area can be operated by hand.

**How it works:** A pawl on the margin stop works in one direction only, and an inclined surface on the latch lets the carriage return past it without engaging.
