## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Condensed record work needs paper positioned for more than one column or line at a time, which the standard platen feed does not allow.

**Solution:** Movable paper feed devices and side edge guides on the platen frame can be thrown and locked out of action so the paper can be positioned as the work requires.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
