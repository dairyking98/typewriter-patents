## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator is not warned when the ribbon is nearly fully unwound from its spool.

**Solution:** A ribbon-end pin lifts a slotted frame as the ribbon unwinds, tripping a lever that rings a gong on the next key stroke.

**How it works:** The ribbon passes through a slot in a cross-bar of a U-shaped frame guided vertically over the spool shaft; the pin fixing the ribbon end cannot pass the slot. As the ribbon unwinds the pin lifts the frame, which raises a pivoted lever carrying a bell striker on a spring. The key-stroke rocker arm then strikes the lever's arm so the striker hits the gong.
