## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator is thrown at high speed to the printing point and tends to overthrow, so the type strikes off the ribbon or partly on the wrong stripe of a two-colour ribbon.

**Solution:** Put a dwell in the cam slot that drives the vibrator, so the ribbon is locked at the printing point as soon as it arrives while the key continues to bring the type up.

**How it works:** The vibrator is driven by a rotary cam member with a cam slot in which the vibrator engages, connected to the type key levers. The ribbon reaches the printing point somewhat ahead of the type; at that moment the vibrator meets a dwell in the slot and holds still while the cam keeps moving with the type key. For a polychromatic ribbon two differently shaped cams, shiftable so one or the other engages, throw the carrier different distances for each colour field, both with the dwell. The ribbon vibrating mechanism shifts with the platen between upper and lower case.
