## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my present invention is to improve the construction shown in my said pending application, by increasing the number of the parallel paths of the type impressions on the ribbon, so that a uniform anthconiplete exhaustion of the inking surface of a wider ribbon may be had.

**How it works (reading):** The vibrator throw is varied so impressions follow several parallel paths along the ribbon, including separate fields of different character.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A visible-writing ribbon vibrator with one throw wears a single track on the ribbon, so a wide ribbon is not used up evenly.

**Solution:** The vibrator throw is varied so impressions follow several parallel paths along the ribbon, including separate fields of different character.

**How it works:** This builds on the inventor's earlier application where the ribbon vibrator moves to give several parallel impression paths. It increases the number of paths so a wider ribbon is evenly exhausted, supports a ribbon with differently characterised fields, keeps the ribbon quiet and clear of the print field during printing, and includes ribbon reversing. It is shown on a Monarch typebar machine with key levers, segment of typebars, ball-bearing carriage and escapement wheel.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon vibrator; typebar; platen; carriage; escapement wheel; dog rocker; universal bar

**Improves on:**

- inventor's application Serial No. 247,474: more parallel paths and multi-field ribbons
