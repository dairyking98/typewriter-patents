## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key lever cash register)

**How it works (reading):** Total display and total print devices are reset to zero at the start of each total by a common gear, then moved together by one drive so the total is shown and printed on both strips.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash register totals should show on the display and print on check and control slips at once.

**Solution:** Total display and total print devices are reset to zero at the start of each total by a common gear, then moved together by one drive so the total is shown and printed on both strips.
