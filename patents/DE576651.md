## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons; Recording elements for printing with ink or for printing by deformation or perforation of the recording surface, e.g. embossing

**How it works (reading):** A flat box holds ink pads in the ribbon colours with raised partitions; the ribbon is passed over the pad and rolled until it is re-soaked.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Used record-writing ribbons of many colours are discarded when they fade.

**Solution:** A flat box holds ink pads in the ribbon colours with raised partitions; the ribbon is passed over the pad and rolled until it is re-soaked.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md)

**Parts:** ink pads; partitions; box lid; small roll
