## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switching device for typewriter trolleys with switchable type lever bearing segment and with force-fit action of the switch bracket on the switch lock.

**How it works (reading):** The switch bracket is shaped with a stop surface longer than the carrier stroke, so it stays in contact with the switch lock in every position; a toggle key lifts the type-lever bearing segment so the same bracket works in the shift position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type lever struck in the shift position must reliably trigger carriage switching, and the switch bracket must still work when the bearing segment is raised or lowered.

**Solution:** The switch bracket is shaped with a stop surface longer than the carrier stroke, so it stays in contact with the switch lock in every position; a toggle key lifts the type-lever bearing segment so the same bracket works in the shift position.
