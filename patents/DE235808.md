## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter for writing notes.

**How it works (reading):** A gear on the paper roller axle meshes with a toothed cylinder along the roller length, which a hand lever rotates to a pointer-scale setting for each note height.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Writing musical notes needs the paper roller to turn by a larger angle than the usual line feed allows, and earlier keyboard rotation was limited to a small angle.

**Solution:** A gear on the paper roller axle meshes with a toothed cylinder along the roller length, which a hand lever rotates to a pointer-scale setting for each note height.
