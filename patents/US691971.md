## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stereotype matrix musical impressing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Musical notes and other characters must be impressed into stereotype matrices of asbestos cardboard, and the impression must be of uniform depth.

**Solution:** A machine that impresses the type into a sheet of asbestos cardboard with uniform depth and positions the sheet with slide rests, one above the other, to bring it under the type at the impression point.

**Features:** [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
