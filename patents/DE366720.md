## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**How it works (reading):** The segment is carried on vertical guide rails between two pairs of springs that hold it centred; a curved guide piece on a shaft, driven by a lever, lifts it to the other end position and it is held there, so shifting is smooth and fixed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Switching the type-lever bearing segment between small and large letters is jerky or drops suddenly, which is undesirable.

**Solution:** The segment is carried on vertical guide rails between two pairs of springs that hold it centred; a curved guide piece on a shaft, driven by a lever, lifts it to the other end position and it is held there, so shifting is smooth and fixed.
