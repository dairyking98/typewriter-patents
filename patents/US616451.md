## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a variable feed for the paper-carriage of a typewriting machine, so that on striking a key-lever actuating a narrow type, like that of the letter i, the paper-carriage will be fed a less distance than when a wider character or letter is written, thus according to each letter its proper width, as in printers movable type, and thereby making the written page appear like ordinary print.

**How it works (reading):** A variable feed where dogs on the key levers set how many escapement teeth a double ratchet lets pass, giving seven different spaces per letter.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed carriage feed gives i and w the same width, so typed pages do not look like printed type.

**Solution:** A variable feed where dogs on the key levers set how many escapement teeth a double ratchet lets pass, giving seven different spaces per letter.

**How it works:** A cog train links the carriage rack bar to an escapement wheel, one tooth of which equals a single space. A double ratchet or pawl (a stiff and a limber knife-edge section) on the same shaft is worked by a space bar, push-bar and levers. Dogs connected to the key levers regulate how many teeth the escapement is allowed to take after each stroke, so narrow letters feed less than wide ones. Spring-wheel and strap drive the carriage. The remainder of the mechanism is not in the excerpt.
