> Description Translated from German Device for retracting the paper carriage. By a button lever actuated devices for retracting the paper trolley from typewriters and those devices in which a spring capsule and one with which it can be coupled Cord pulley are provided on a common shaft are known. Compared to the known devices is the subject of the present Invention forming device significantly simplified, by the Key lever is designed as an angle lever and has a direct effect on the spring capsule and exercising the pulley by the fact that it moves the spring capsule as it descends releases and the cord disc couples with the spring capsule during its return movement but loosens the cord disc from the spring capsule and fixes the latter.
