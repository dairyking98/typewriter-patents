## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar linkages for low, flat typewriters need a simple layout in which a falling typebar is held in rest position and cannot rebound.

**Solution:** Mount the intermediate levers on two parallel lines (middle ones below, outer ones above) so that in rest each intermediate lever and its tension member sit just past dead centre, locking the typebar against rebound.

**How it works:** A typebar pivots in an inclined, deep segment and is joined by a connecting wire to the long leg of an intermediate lever; a short leg is joined by a wire to the key lever running beneath. At rest the wire joint lies slightly past the straight line between the two pivots, so the typebar cannot bounce back, and the key lever's own rebound is too small to push the joint over the other side. A single moulded bearing rail with slots carries all intermediate lever axles.
