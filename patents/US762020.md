## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Teaching typing

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the improved practicing device for students studying the art of type-writing, and particularly acquiring the speed desirable in practical work of typewriting and in the arrangements and combinations of parts of the same, all substantially as will be hereinafter set forth, and finally embraced in the clauses of the claim.

**How it works (reading):** A cheap practice keyboard with a key touch similar to real machine keys lets many students practise fingering without printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Students need to practise fingering without wearing out real machines, and a large class cannot have many machines.

**Solution:** A cheap practice keyboard with a key touch similar to real machine keys lets many students practise fingering without printing.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
