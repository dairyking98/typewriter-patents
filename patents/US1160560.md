## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A character card on a key lever can come loose, and it cannot be turned to square with the other letter keys after assembly.

**Solution:** A key cup shaped to fit the key lever with recesses, letting the character card be rotated or straightened relative to the other letter keys after assembly.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
