## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide an attachment of the character described which consists of a pulley to be operatively connected with the carriage of v the typewriter and to operate the same and clutch means .

**How it works (reading):** A pulley attached to the carriage, driven by a flexible pull and a clutch that disengages automatically, returns the carriage and then lets the pulley turn freely.
