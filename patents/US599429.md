## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Sliding or ball bearings for typewriter paper carriages are imperfect for supporting the carriage in all directions.

**Solution:** Roller bearings of detached small disks set crosswise at right angles between V-shaped tracks on the rail and carriage.

**How it works:** Rails front and rear have a central groove with two inclined faces at right angles; the carriage edge has matching faces. Small disks, one pair on pivots at right angles to the other, ride between parallel faces in a slotted holder (truck). The crossed disks support the carriage against falling, lifting and sideways motion. Stops at ends keep trucks on the tracks. Several variants are shown.

**Features:**

- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** carriage bar; rail; inclined face; roll; roll holder; stop; platen carriage; supporting wheel

**Improves on:**

- ball-bearing carriages: crossed disks give support in all directions
