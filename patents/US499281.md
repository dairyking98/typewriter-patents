## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (numbering and perforating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**How it works (reading):** A numbering and perforating machine with a single handle that selects and depresses punches on a drum with a centering spring, and that also clamps the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand levers for each punch are slow, and the feed clamp needs its own lever.

**Solution:** A numbering and perforating machine with a single handle that selects and depresses punches on a drum with a centering spring, and that also clamps the paper.
