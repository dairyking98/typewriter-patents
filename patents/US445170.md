## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pivot bearings of swinging typebars wear and the arm needs speedy, accurate take-up without changing the printing-point.

**Solution:** Each arm's pivoted end is split and contracted by a screw or nut onto a fixed pivot pin with a V-groove, so wear is taken up along conical surfaces.

**How it works:** A rigid pivot pin is fixed at both ends in a bracket and has a V-shaped circumferential groove forming tapered wear surfaces. The type arm's pivot hole has matching conical form and the end is slit parallel to the pin axis. A transverse screw (or a nut on a threaded tapered arm end) contracts the split end around the pin. Contraction brings the conical surfaces together without moving the arm sideways or changing its swing path.
