## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Releasing the upper paper-clamping feed-rollers takes separate action on each roller, and the rollers can be displaced automatically.

**Solution:** Spring-held feed-roll brackets on a rod behind the platen and a pivoted apron/guide-plate at the front are operated by a single ever or release device, which moves all the feed-rollers into or out of clamping position against the platen.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** platen 1; platen-frame 2; feed-roll brackets 5; feed rolls 9; apron 13; guide-plate; scale-bar and front feed rollers 16; springs 10
