## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper-feeding devices of type-writing machines, and particularly to means for making carbon records of type-written bills or other memoranda, several of which are to be carbonized or manifolded upon asingle recordsheet.
