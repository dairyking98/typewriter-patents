## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Students learning touch typewriting look at the keys, which defeats the purpose of touch typing.

**Solution:** A shield that covers the keyboard, carried on an adjustable L-shaped bracket that clamps beneath the machine so the student cannot see the keys.

**How it works:** A shield on an arm slides in a slot of an upright; a cross-arm with perforations and rubber-foot holes fixes the bracket under the typewriter.
