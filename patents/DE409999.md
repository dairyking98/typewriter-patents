## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Light-metal typewriter frame needed to stay stiff and keep line straightness.

**Solution:** Cross piece punched and bent from sheet with upper and lower flaps carrying the type lever and intermediate lever segments, stiffened by struts.
