## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator cannot see which line is being printed or judge top and bottom margins without opening the machine.

**Solution:** An inclined numbered scale mounted above the platen shows the printed line position and sets the margins, while also supporting the paper.

**How it works:** A standard fixed to a stationary part carries an arm extending over the platen, ending in an adjustable knuckle and a grooved metal holder. A cardboard or metal scale numbered 1 upward in inverse order, from the bottom, slides loosely into the holder against a stop. Number 1 is set at a distance from the print line equal to the desired top margin. The sheet's upper edge is lined up with 1, and as it feeds up it runs over the scale, so the numbers indicate the line printed; the bottom margin is found by feeding until the edge reaches the number matching the line count.
