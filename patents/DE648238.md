## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type rod drive links sit behind the guide rods, making the machine long; small machines need compact drive.

**Solution:** All push and movement members sit above and below the rear trajectory of the type carrier guide rod, so the drive is short and fits in the space the guide rod used.
