## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements on the Yost-type machine of US487047 (type carriers that swing to a common centre point), with self-inking of the types.

**Solution:** Type carriers pivoted on driving levers roll up a toothed central support so each type turns from a central inking pad to a guide opening over the platen.

**How it works:** Key levers on a base plate pull connecting rods that raise driving levers set in radial slots of a type ring. Each driving lever carries a pivoted type carrier with a type arm and a partial pinion, whose teeth mesh with a rack of concentric rings on a central support. When the lever lifts, the carrier creeps up the rack and turns, bringing the type face away from the central felt inking pad, through a guide opening at the centre, and to the paper on the platen, reversing the type on the way. At rest the types sit against the pad and ink themselves. The pad is fed from a chamber above it by capillary action.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** key lever; connecting rod; driving lever; type ring; type carrier; partial pinion; central rack support; inking pad; ink chamber; guide opening; platen; paper carriage
