## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Leading-edge paper stops must be held away from the platen in one position and moved to it only by a separate key.

**Solution:** Leading-edge stops constantly spring-pressed toward the platen but held away by a stronger spring; they move to the platen only after a separate key is operated.
