## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** The main objects of my invention is to provide stop setting means of the character specified in which the number of column stops provided need not be in excess of the number which are who actually used at one time.

**How it works (reading):** Column stops are adjusted to set positions with the travel of the carriage aiding the adjustment, and the stops are grouped and cleared for resetting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column stops must be readily set and cleared using the carriage travel itself.

**Solution:** Column stops are adjusted to set positions with the travel of the carriage aiding the adjustment, and the stops are grouped and cleared for resetting.
