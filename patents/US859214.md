## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (die carrier for printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** Dies sit in cylindrically arranged sockets in a holder; each die has a shoulder and ledge that lock it against endwise movement, so it can be inserted and removed from the socket.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dies in a die holder used for embossing must be replaceable, and the die must lock against endwise movement except under considerable force.

**Solution:** Dies sit in cylindrically arranged sockets in a holder; each die has a shoulder and ledge that lock it against endwise movement, so it can be inserted and removed from the socket.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Interchangeable element](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
