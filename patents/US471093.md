## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage-position scale on a Hammond typewriter (with a pad-inking device replacing ribbons) was hard to see, so margins and spaces traversed were hard to read.

**Solution:** A scale marked on a flattened top of the carriage guide rod, read against an index line on an arm fixed to the type-wheel bracket.

**How it works:** The paper-carriage guide rod has a flat top on which a scale is marked, numbered both ways from the centre. An extension arm attached to the bracket that steadies the ribbon-attachment shaft carries a fixed index line. As the carriage moves, the rod slides past the index and the scale shows spaces traversed, or the margin left on either side of the paper. The scale on whichever side is hidden behind the type-wheel case can be ignored because the other side reads.
