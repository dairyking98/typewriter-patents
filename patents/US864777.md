## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools must be easy to attach and detach, and the ribbon shifting mechanism must be tripped easily.

**Solution:** A spool with a central hub and heads lets the ribbon be attached and detached readily; the spool works with a trip on a rock shaft that actuates the ribbon shifting.

**How it works:** The spool is mounted on a shaft with a gear, and the trip on the rock shaft moves the ribbon shifting when the spool is tripped.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; rock shaft trip
