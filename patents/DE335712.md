## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**How it works (reading):** The drive wheel has a smooth roughened surface instead of teeth, and an electromagnet presses the stop-button tooth against the friction wheel when the contact closes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The engagement between the key stroke and the electric drive wheel needs exact tooth spacing, which is hard to achieve.

**Solution:** The drive wheel has a smooth roughened surface instead of teeth, and an electromagnet presses the stop-button tooth against the friction wheel when the contact closes.

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** friction wheel; electric motor; electromagnets; stop button tooth
