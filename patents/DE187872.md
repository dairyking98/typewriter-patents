## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Justified lines must be set by first producing trial lines, measuring the shortfall, and then adjusting the widths of the characters or spaces by hand.

**Solution:** The compensation values from a trial line are used to adjust the setting of the mechanical devices that determine the width of the spaces between words, so the final line comes out at the normal length.
