## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon adjusted transversely

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of type writing machines employing an inking ribbon, and has for its main objects to provide simple and efficient means for automatically moving the ribbon widthwise, and also for antomatically reversing the longitudinal movement of the ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon carrier is moved sideways by a crank or eccentric, working with an automatic lengthwise reversing mechanism, on a Caligraph.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbons wore along a single line and needed manual reversing and shifting; the inventor wants the ribbon moved widthwise automatically so the whole width is used.

**Solution:** A ribbon carrier is moved sideways by a crank or eccentric, working with an automatic lengthwise reversing mechanism, on a Caligraph.

**How it works:** The ribbon runs between two spools across the machine under the platen. Bevel gears on a counter-shaft engage one spool shaft at a time through a sliding shifter, and the shaft is turned step by step by a pawl and ratchet driven by a rocker linked to the universal bar. Moving the shifter swaps which spool winds and which pays out. A separate crank or eccentric moves the ribbon carrier widthwise so the types strike different lines on the ribbon. Reversal is triggered by levers acted on by the carriage driving arm.
