## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon should stay under constant tension and reverse itself when a spool runs out.

**Solution:** Friction-braked spools with ratchet and pawl reversing devices whose pawls are thrown over when the ribbon goes taut and the working ratchet stops turning.

**How it works:** Two spools at different heights are friction-restrained by spring-loaded washers so the ribbon stays taut across guides above the platen. Each spool has a ratchet wheel with a pawl on an oscillating lever, driven from the main rock-shaft by a link; the two ratchets face opposite ways and only one pawl engages at a time. When one spool is empty the ribbon pulls tight, the ratchet stops, and the pawl pivot slides along its slot, tripping the pawl out and the other pawl in, so the feed reverses.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; friction washer; spring; ratchet wheel; pawl; reversing lever; link; rock-shaft; guide roll
