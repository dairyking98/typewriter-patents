## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter with a single compact type carrier instead of many typebars, selected directly by the key plungers.

**Solution:** A spherical-shell universal type carrier is tilted by the depressed key plunger to bring that character under the printing opening, then pressed down onto the paper.

**How it works:** A spherical type segment, set in a hemispherical shell with a printing opening at its base, carries types in three planes 13 degrees apart (lower case, capitals, figures) on its outer surface. A top plate (rosette) on the carrier has radial grooves; each key plunger, one per character in nine sets of three, enters its groove and tilts the carrier until the plunger drops into a hole in the shell, bringing its type to the center. A shoulder on the plunger then pushes a plunger that forces the type to the paper through the opening. Case shift turns a tube and four leaf springs that rotate the carrier 13 degrees about its vertical axis. The shell's downward motion advances the ratchet-driven ribbon and the type carriage one space, a space bar pressing the shell directly, and line spacing is by hand with a detent along a rail.
