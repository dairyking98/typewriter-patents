## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a machine operated by hand for neatly and expeditiously printing newspaper bulletins,windowsigns, business announcements, and lettering on paper in general.

**How it works (reading):** A hand-set type-wheel with a locking bolt, ink rollers and a sliding paper table with variable spacing prints large letters.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Large hand lettering (signs, posters) was slow and uneven by hand.

**Solution:** A hand-set type-wheel with a locking bolt, ink rollers and a sliding paper table with variable spacing prints large letters.

**How it works:** A wheel with forty rubber or metal type on its rim sits on a horizontal shaft in a cast bracket. The operator lifts a handle to withdraw a bolt from a notch inside the rim, turns the wheel to the wanted character, and lets the bolt drop to lock it. Two felt ink rollers either side, adjustable by a nut, ink the type. The paper table slides under the wheel, spaced by an automatic variable-spacing clutch and spacing wheel, and a type-guard is provided.
