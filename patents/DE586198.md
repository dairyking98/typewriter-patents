## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing unit for adding machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements thereof of more than one hammer, e.g. one for each character position

**How it works (reading):** The hammer carries a guide bracket that rides on the type carrier's side flanges through the whole printing stroke, with tension springs tensioned during operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hammer and type carrier pairs in calculating machines were separate parts, causing noise, wear and uneven spring loading.

**Solution:** The hammer carries a guide bracket that rides on the type carrier's side flanges through the whole printing stroke, with tension springs tensioned during operation.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Printing unit for addition and other calculating machines The invention concerns a printing unit for addition and other calculating machines with rigid type carriers, which have a guide slot for the attack of a hammer by means of connecting bolts. There are known designs in which a built-in between the type carriers Control actuates these and their guide slots participate in the printing process. In order to eliminate the disadvantages inherent in this known design in the printing unit according to the invention, the hammer in the type printing zone has one Guide bracket, which during the printing process with its two legs on the two Side flanges of the type carrier is effective, and it lies the hammer in a guide slot the reset bracket, which triggers the hammer hook, and by means of a tension spring connected to the hammer. This connection of hammer and type carrier, which throughout Printing process is retained, results in a safe operation of the hammer and Type carrier while reducing lateral friction and securing against the fact that this Parts get stuck together.
