> Description Translated from German Drive device for typewriters with power and manual operation The subject of the invention is an improvement and further development of the drive devices for typewriters with power and manual operation, in which the transmission of motion takes place from the key lever to the type lever through a two-armed intermediate lever. at the known facilities of this type. either a vertical intermediate lever used with a drop shoe, which increases the overall height of the machine, or a rigid lever that turns the power source on or off with each keystroke, which slows down the writing speed. The invention differs from this in that a pawl is arranged on the intermediate lever, which with a stationary locking lug and a stationary stop cooperates and in their movement is limited opposite the intermediate lever by a nose and a leaf spring, of which the latter either - is attached to the pawl and with a wart through a longitudinal slot of the pawl into corresponding notches of the intermediate lever engages or is attached to this and engages with detents of the pawl stands.
