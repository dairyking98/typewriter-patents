## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls; Levers mounted on fixed pivots

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is an improvement and further development of the drive devices for typewriters with power and manual operation, in which the transmission of motion takes place from the key lever to the type lever through a two-armed intermediate lever.

**How it works (reading):** A pawl on the intermediate lever cooperates with a stationary lug and stop and a leaf spring, so the drive parts vibrate rather than jerk, and the drive is disengaged in manual operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known intermediate-lever drives either raise the machine height with a drop shoe or slow writing by switching power on every stroke.

**Solution:** A pawl on the intermediate lever cooperates with a stationary lug and stop and a leaf spring, so the drive parts vibrate rather than jerk, and the drive is disengaged in manual operation.
