## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage needs to stop at a set line end, the escape wheel and rocker arm must lock there, and the carriage must be able to write within a set margin and be released after arrest.

**Solution:** Stop and lock devices arrest the carriage and lock the escape wheel and rocker arm at the end of a predetermined line; a release lets extra characters be printed, and a final stop, bell and margin stops are added, with carriage-rack and pinion adjustment.
