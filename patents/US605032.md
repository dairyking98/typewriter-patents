## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon spool with steps lets the ribbon be shifted laterally in set positions.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Only part of the ribbon width wears.

**Solution:** Ribbon spool with steps lets the ribbon be shifted laterally in set positions.

**How it works:** The spool slides and turns on its shaft against a spring until a pin on the ratchet wheel rests against one of the steps on the spool, locating the ribbon across the type impact.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; shaft; ratchet wheel; pin; spring; step
