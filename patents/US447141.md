## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide a means for cleaning all the types of the typebasket of a type-writing machine by a single rotary motion of the cleaner in a vertical plane, or a plane crossing the plane of said type considered collectively, and to brush the sides as well as the faces of said type.

**How it works (reading):** A brush matching the outline of the type opening, larger than the opening, is rotated across the plane of the types by one rotary motion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** All the types in the basket must be cleaned, including their sides, quickly.

**Solution:** A brush matching the outline of the type opening, larger than the opening, is rotated across the plane of the types by one rotary motion.

**How it works:** Opening-shaped brush rotated across the type plane to clean faces and sides.
