## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-forming machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists of mechanism for presenting dies in proper order to the action of a punch, whereby their impressions may be made in wood or other suitable material, and thus produce a matrix.

**How it works (reading):** A die-carrier presents dies in order to a punch that is tripped to strike the matrix bed; indices guide the hand-lever and a justifier sets the spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Matrices for printing plates had to be made by hand and spacing was not automatic.

**Solution:** A die-carrier presents dies in order to a punch that is tripped to strike the matrix bed; indices guide the hand-lever and a justifier sets the spacing.

**Features:** [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
