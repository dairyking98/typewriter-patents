## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a tilting, longitudinally shiftable typewriter carriage, the platen line-spacing, the escapement rack release and the tilting should work together without disturbing each other.

**Solution:** A combined feed-rack actuator and platen feed lever that rotates the platen for line spacing while simultaneously throwing the escapement rack out of engagement.

**How it works:** The platen sits in a carriage that slides on a front way and a pivoted rear guide bar, and the carriage can be tilted up and back to show the writing. A swinging feed rack with forward arms is fulcrumed on the guide bar. A feed lever carrying a feed pawl turns the platen ratchet wheel and, in the same motion, pushes the rack arms so the rack leaves the escapement. A detent spring prevents accidental backward platen movement, and the pawl can be thrown clear so the carriage can tilt without altering platen position.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; platen ratchet wheel; feed pawl; feed lever; feed rack; guide bar; detent spring

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph carriage: adds a combined line-feed and rack-release lever on a tilting shiftable carriage
