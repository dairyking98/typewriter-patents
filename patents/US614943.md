## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** General improvement of a basket-type typebar machine in manufacture and operation.

**Solution:** A typebar machine with ball-supported carriage, platen mounting that lifts or tilts forward for viewing, new escapement and key-to-typebar links, and writing scale.

**How it works:** The frame has parallel top tracks with V ways holding balls, on which the rectangular carriage rolls, held down by a clip and a roller. The platen shaft is carried on arms and links so the platen can be raised for inspection or thrown forward of the front bar to expose the type. Key levers connect to type bars arranged in a basket via links on guide bars, with an upper-case locking plate, semaphore, margin stop, bell and ribbon feed also described. Only the opening of a long text is available.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md)
