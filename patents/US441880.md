## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to produce a type-writer whose frame is movable with respect to a permanent base, whereby it is adapted to Write in a bound book or the like where it is impossible to secure the pages to a traveling platen.

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter whose frame slides along a fixed ratchet spacing bar over the book and shifts its centre so different types on a multi-type bar strike flat.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriters cannot write in a bound book whose pages cannot be fed on a traveling platen, and changing type case needs a separate mechanism.

**Solution:** A typewriter whose frame slides along a fixed ratchet spacing bar over the book and shifts its centre so different types on a multi-type bar strike flat.

**How it works:** The machine frame rides on a toothed spacing bar on legs, with a spring-box wheel engaging the teeth and a ratchet pawl for adjustment. Two rows of vertically sliding keys connect through pitmen and levers to typebars on a curved pivot piece, with springs lifting them. Each typebar carries several types arranged for different centres, so shifting the frame's centre makes another type strike flat on the page. A hinged frame piece, ribbon mechanism and a transverse spacing bar handle line positioning on the book page.
