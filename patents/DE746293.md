## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (locking device for complementary counters)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a locking device for complementary digital counters, in particular for typewriters and the like equipped with a totalizer.

**How it works (reading):** A locking member normally blocks the digit-display switch and releases it only when the counter is in the working position and when it leaves that position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a complementary counter, operating the positive or negative digit display before the carriage finishes its return could break drive teeth.

**Solution:** A locking member normally blocks the digit-display switch and releases it only when the counter is in the working position and when it leaves that position.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** complement counter; digit wheel display device; locking member; main drive wheel
