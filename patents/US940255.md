## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stenographic machines were noisy and imprecise in paper feed and character printing.

**Solution:** Improved ratchet and detent paper feed with a pawl, plus key levers on rocking bearing bars that engage the feed plate, for quieter and more precise operation.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
