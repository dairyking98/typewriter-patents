## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An earlier flat-bed typebar mechanism that strikes down through an open frame onto a stationary flat platen could not be used with a travelling carriage and line-feed rollers.

**Solution:** Hold the downward-striking typebar frame fixed on a hinged bar and let a paper carriage with line-feed rollers and a polygonal platen travel beneath it on track rails.

**How it works:** An open base-frame carrying typebars with multi-face heads is hinged to a rear bar on the bed so it can be turned up. Beneath it a paper carriage runs on track rails and carries a revolvable polygonal platen with feed rolls and a rack bar for letter spacing. The key levers act through universal bars on the feed escapement so the carriage moves a letter space as the type strikes down onto the paper. The front of the type frame rests on a roller on the carriage top rail, which also carries tabulator stops, and the carriage is locked when the type frame is raised.
