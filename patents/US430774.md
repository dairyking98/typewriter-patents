## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Finger-keys with index letters are costly to make and the key button can turn or work loose on the key-lever stem.

**Solution:** A die-formed button with an integral stem, recess holding a letter disk under a transparent cover, joined to the lever by a slotted socket and radial key that detachably prevents turning.

**How it works:** A metal rod is die-formed with a disk head and an annular flange, making a recess. A paper letter disk and a celluloid cover disk are seated in the recess, the flange being crimped over. The key stem is a cylinder seated in a slotted tubular socket on the lever stem (or vice versa); a radial projection on the seated stem enters the slot so the key cannot turn.
