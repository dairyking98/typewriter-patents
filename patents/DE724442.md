> Description Translated from German Typewriter with a vertical platen The invention relates to a typewriter with a vertical platen and a fixed screw spindle arranged in the axis of the platen is provided for guiding the platen roller screwing up on it, so that a uninterrupted lettering in the form of a helix is created. In the case of a platen of this type, according to the invention, are on the inner wall two-armed levers are hinged to the hollow platen, one of which has arms at its ends are provided with threaded jaws forming the nut for screwing up the roller, so that by pressing together the other lever arms designed as handles with hand disengages the threaded jaws of the nut from the threads the fixed screw spindle by pivoting the lever can and the platen in any position to reach sections in the text is adjustable by one or more threads. In this way, the Possibility to form sections of text created, and the decoupling that serves this purpose from platen and screw spindle can be quickly and. reliable as well as in run at any height of the platen.
