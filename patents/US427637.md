## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple hand-indexed typewriter in which the printing stroke also advances the carriage.

**Solution:** A lever with a plunger is placed over the desired letter on an index plate, and pressing it forces the matching rubber type down while a ratchet-wheel and paired pawls advance the carriage one space.

**How it works:** A type-carrying segment with a rubber piece bearing the characters on its underside is mounted with a hinged two-part lever on a post. The lever is set above the letter on the index plate on the carriage, and its front end pressed down. A frame carries a tube, spring and plunger that pushes the type down through a slot in the ink pad onto the paper. The same motion raises the back of the frame, releasing one pawl from a spring-driven ratchet wheel on a ratchet-bar and letting the second pawl, one cog behind, take the wheel, so the carriage steps one space. A line-feed roller has a grooved thumb piece with spring dog that clicks at each line spacing, and index bars locate the work.
