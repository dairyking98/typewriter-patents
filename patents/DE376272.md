## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A portable typewriter must be light yet rigid, and visible writing is hard to build because the type lever segment sits in front of the platen.

**Solution:** The type lever segment is a triangular or three-legged frame of thin punched sheet metal tied into the main frame, carrying the key lever bearing rail and the connecting part bearings, which keeps the structure stiff and light.
