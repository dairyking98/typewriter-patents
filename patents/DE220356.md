## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a particular embodiment 'of the paper feed device for typewriters according to U.

**How it works (reading):** The flap is replaced by a disc, rotatable about its axis and set in a circular groove around the ratchet wheel, so the pawl engages without premature collapse. It is an addendum to patent 213944.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper-roller reverse-rotation pawl can collapse prematurely, because a spring-loaded flap blocks its engagement with the ratchet wheel.

**Solution:** The flap is replaced by a disc, rotatable about its axis and set in a circular groove around the ratchet wheel, so the pawl engages without premature collapse. It is an addendum to patent 213944.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** ratchet wheel on roller axis; adjusting lever; pawl; spring-loaded flap (replaced); rotatable disc in circular groove
