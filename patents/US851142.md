## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide simple, inexpensive and reliable means for enabling either ribbon to vibrate, so that either its upper edge or lower edge may overlie the printing point.

**How it works (reading):** Two universal bars on one adjustable frame, one per ribbon vibrator, with a single five-position finger-piece.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-ribbon Underwood machines needed a simple way to select either ribbon and its band, or none for stencils.

**Solution:** Two universal bars on one adjustable frame, one per ribbon vibrator, with a single five-position finger-piece.

**How it works:** Front and rear ribbons each run on a spool pair and through their own vibrator. A bipartite universal bar, two bars on one frame, is moved by type keys. The frame shifts to put one bar in action and silence the other, with a ratchet winding rock-shaft per ribbon so a silenced ribbon neither vibrates nor winds. The finger-piece has five positions: both off, or either ribbon at either of two strokes.

**Parts:** front ribbon; rear ribbon; vibrator; universal bar; spool; ratchet wheel; pawl; finger-piece
