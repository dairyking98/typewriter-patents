## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A card clamp mounted on the axle makes the platen hard to remove and replace.

**Solution:** The card clamp and its operating mechanism mount in the platen heads independently of the axle, and the clamp retracts flush with the platen surface.
