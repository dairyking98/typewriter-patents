According to said patent, the card is held by a clamp which can be projected at will from the periphery of the platen, to pinch the leading edge of the card against the surface of the platen. When using the machine for correspondence and other ordinary purposes, the clamp is retired within the platen, its outer edge remaining flush with the printing surface of the platen, and forming a part of said surface.
