## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The adding mechanism could be actuated before it was in position, causing errors in a combined typewriter and adding machine.

**Solution:** A lock on the actuating device that holds it against movement unless the laterally moving accumulating mechanism is in position to be actuated.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
