**How it works (reading):** After each embossing the stop holding the stamp carrier stays locked by a locking lever linked to the key reset, and the carrier moves again only when a new key is pressed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In address embossing machines the power-driven stamp carrier keeps circulating between keystrokes, which wastes motion.

**Solution:** After each embossing the stop holding the stamp carrier stays locked by a locking lever linked to the key reset, and the carrier moves again only when a new key is pressed.
