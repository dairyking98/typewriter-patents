## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** An electric Japanese-script typewriter needs both sideways and vertical paper-cylinder feed from one electromagnet, plus a way to move the cylinder backwards and to release it, while keeping the support frame from drifting when touched.

**Solution:** A gear and small gear on a swing plate, locked by stop pawls, meter the spring-pulled cylinder frame past a rack; one electromagnet and a switchable transmission plate send its stroke either to the sideways or the vertical feed.

**How it works:** The cylinder support frame is always pulled to one side and its rack (3) meshes with a small gear (6) on a shaft with gear (7). Swinging plate (8) forward unmeshes small gear (13) from gear (7) and a pawl holds gear (7); on release the gear turns until an auxiliary plate (12) enters a catch notch, advancing the frame one set step, whose size is set by an adjusting lever (17) and a polygonal knob piece (22). A switch lever under the characters for vertical, off or horizontal slides the transmission plate so the armature of electromagnet (26) either pushes the swing plate or lifts a cross bar to the vertical feed. A double stop pawl (48) and stop pawl (14) normally lock the gears both ways; a second electromagnet (66) lifts both off for free sliding. A back-space lever turns the double pawl so its upper claw turns gear (7) back, by a distance set with the same knob.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Sideways-feed device for a Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a sideways-feed device for a Japanese-script typewriter, characterized as follows. A small gear meshes with a rack fitted to the paper-cylinder support frame, which is always pulled one way. One end of a double stop pawl engages a gear placed beside this small gear. A small gear mounted on a swing plate meshes with the gear, and a stop pawl engages that small gear, so that the gear cannot turn in either direction. A transmission plate is linked to an armature facing a single electromagnet. By moving this transmission plate, the attracting stroke of the armature is passed either to the swing plate, which separates the gear and small gear and so gives a sideways feed (printed シサ, read シテ), or to a cross bar linked to the paper-cylinder vertical feed mechanism. Turning the double stop pawl, and the turning plate it is mounted on, brings the pawl's other end into mesh with the gear and turns the gear backwards, moving the paper-cylinder support frame the opposite way.
