## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by denominational arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A stop mechanism on the tabulating system arrests the carriage at several points; a type segment is moved into position and a printing hammer drives the paper and ribbon into the characters from behind.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating needs the paper carriage arrested at several different points in its travel.

**Solution:** A stop mechanism on the tabulating system arrests the carriage at several points; a type segment is moved into position and a printing hammer drives the paper and ribbon into the characters from behind.
