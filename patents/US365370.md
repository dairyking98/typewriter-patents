## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements in the construction of a typebar typewriter, only the figure list of which survives in the opening.

**Solution:** A pivoted inclined head with typebars whose type hammers are rotated by a ring gear to change case, a duplex carriage driven by a spring drum and cord, a ribbon feed and a bell.

**How it works:** A bed carries a lower carriage on anti-friction trucks and an upper carriage with the paper roll, pulled by a cord from a spring-driven feeding drum. Key-operated levers on a ring bearing in the inclined head drive the typebars and a main carriage-feed lever which turns an escapement wheel. Bevel gears rotate the type hammers, driven by a ring gear, to bring the other case to the print point. The ribbon rolls are fed by a ratchet or cam wheel with a hook pawl, and a cam block operates the bell hammer. Only the figure list is in the opening; the rest is not available.
