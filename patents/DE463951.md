## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements to attach the ribbon to the spool

**How it works (reading):** A gripper with tips impales the ribbon end and is pushed through the spool core opening, where a leaf spring with tips holds the ribbon; pulling the gripper off leaves the ribbon fixed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Fingers get soiled by ribbon ink when the ribbon end is threaded onto the spool, and disposable grip tabs would raise ribbon cost.

**Solution:** A gripper with tips impales the ribbon end and is pushed through the spool core opening, where a leaf spring with tips holds the ribbon; pulling the gripper off leaves the ribbon fixed.
