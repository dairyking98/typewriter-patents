To this In order to counteract problems, you have an elastic handle to touch at each end of the tape provided, which is after the attachment of the ribbon to the spool core with the Tape winds around the bobbin. The main disadvantage of this facility is that this must be inseparably connected with the ribbon and delivered, that is also increases the price of the ribbon. This disadvantage is according to the Invention eliminates the fact that for detecting the ribbon with a tip and a recess provided gripper is used, with the help of which the tape through a Opening of the coil core and inserted on resiliently yielding or fixed mandrels or spikes are impaled.
