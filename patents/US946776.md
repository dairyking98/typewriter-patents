**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to escapement devices of a typewritin machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dogs engaging the escape wheel load the type-bar finger pieces and resist carriage travel.

**Solution:** Rollers mounted at a corner of the fast and loose dogs ease their engagement with and disengagement from the escape wheel.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
