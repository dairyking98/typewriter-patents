## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Releasing the carriage escapement needed a hand-reach away from the keyboard.

**Solution:** A release key beside the keyboard lifts the escapement dogs from the rack at any carriage position; the carriage rides on ball bearings.

**How it works:** The carriage carries a fixed dog and a spring-held movable dog; a toothed rack is rocked by a universal bail from keys and space bar, shifting engagement between the two dogs so the carriage advances one tooth per stroke. A release bar under an arm on the dog is pivoted by a key near the keyboard and lifts the dogs off the rack so the carriage can slide left. The carriage truck rolls on balls seated in countersunk sockets against an angular support bar.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** fixed dog; movable dog; toothed rack; universal bail; release bar; release key; carriage truck; ball; angular support bar
