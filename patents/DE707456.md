## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (lathe bar feed) · **Subject (patent classes):** Form of the jaws; Jaws in the form of cylindrical elements; Arrangements for handling work specially combined with or arranged in, or specially adapted for use in connection with, machine tools, e.g. for conveying, loading, positioning, discharging, sorting by means of roller-ways some of the rollers being driven

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that the relative movement is achieved solely by rotating the rods.

**How it works (reading):** Rollers at an angle to the feed axis are driven only by the rod's own rotation in the open collet, so the stock feeds without its own drive.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Round-bar lathes need a separate drive to feed the stock.

**Solution:** Rollers at an angle to the feed axis are driven only by the rod's own rotation in the open collet, so the stock feeds without its own drive.
