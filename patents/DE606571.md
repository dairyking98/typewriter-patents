## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key lock for calculating typewriters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocking devices, e.g. between keys

**How it works (reading):** A permanent asymmetric-head spreading part rotated by a counter cam spreads the key-lock levers only in one end position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Locking levers in narrow gaps collide with metal edges and jam.

**Solution:** A permanent asymmetric-head spreading part rotated by a counter cam spreads the key-lock levers only in one end position.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
