## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (decimal point printing on calculating typewriter)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an automatic decimal point printing device on calculating typewriters, have to do the multiplication work and with a sum: n printing device according to patent 6o5 740 are provided.

**How it works (reading):** An extra sensing element on the sensing carriage interacts with an adjustable organ in the machine frame that sets the decimal point position, so the decimal point is printed at the correct place when the values are transferred to the movable typewriter carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On calculating typewriters the decimal point in the product counter is fixed, so multiplication results cannot place the decimal point variably or keep successive numbers aligned comma under comma.

**Solution:** An extra sensing element on the sensing carriage interacts with an adjustable organ in the machine frame that sets the decimal point position, so the decimal point is printed at the correct place when the values are transferred to the movable typewriter carriage.
