## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabulating mechanism for typewriting machines, and more especially to column-finding mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded counter-stop held by a latch is released by a trip that is set to different positions by column-finding keys and engaged by column stops with shoulders of graduated length.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column-finding stops for tabulation need simple, compact parts and a reliable trip action.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded counter-stop held by a latch is released by a trip that is set to different positions by column-finding keys and engaged by column stops with shoulders of graduated length.
