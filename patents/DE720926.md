## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller; Manually-operated feeding devices; Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts in which one paper or set is fed towards printing position from the front of the apparatus

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to paper guiding devices on typewriters and similar machines for the separate insertion and advancement of base and stacking sheets with a paper guide provided with pressure rollers underneath the roller for guiding and holding the base sheet and one below this paper guide lying special paper guide for receiving the on the front of the platen introduced forwarding sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A paper guide with pressure rollers under the platen guides the base sheet, and a lower special guide takes the slip sheet from the front.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier devices let sheets be inserted only from the rear, or needed pivoting the platen or manual baffles.

**Solution:** A paper guide with pressure rollers under the platen guides the base sheet, and a lower special guide takes the slip sheet from the front.
