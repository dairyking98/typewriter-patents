## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters on a platen could not print from a continuous roll of paper.

**Solution:** A roll-supporting core mounted on the Caligraph-style carriage feeds a continuous web of paper over the platen, with spring-held brackets on the carriage rear bar.

**How it works:** A frame of end bars and front and rear bars carries the rotating platen; the rear bar is a pintle on which the carriage tips up. The paper roll sits on a supporting core carried on brackets with curved springs on the rear bar, and the web passes through a paper guide partly encircling the platen. Letter-spacing and carriage drive are left to any known mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** frame; platen; end bar; front bar; rear bar; track; anti-friction roller; paper guide; yoke; guide rod; bracket; spring; roll core

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph: adds a roll-paper supply to its carriage
