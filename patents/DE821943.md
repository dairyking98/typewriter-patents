## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to Lever drive on office machines, especially that type of type lever drive, in which two levers connected to each other by means of a slot and one guided in it.

**How it works (reading):** Instead of a slot and roller, two rollers on the driving key lever grip the two working flanks of the guide member (for example an intermediate lever); the flanks are shaped to a common acceleration curve for all type levers, with the gap narrower in the rest position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A roller running in a slot of a link has flank play, so it is flung between flanks and reverses direction, causing braking, shock and wear.

**Solution:** Instead of a slot and roller, two rollers on the driving key lever grip the two working flanks of the guide member (for example an intermediate lever); the flanks are shaped to a common acceleration curve for all type levers, with the gap narrower in the rest position.
