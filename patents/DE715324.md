## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers

**How it works (reading):** Two pairs of rear feet, longer than the rear fittings, let the machine tilt up onto its back wall without contact with those parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter in its case or on a desk needs more space when it is pushed forward, and rear fittings get damaged when tilted.

**Solution:** Two pairs of rear feet, longer than the rear fittings, let the machine tilt up onto its back wall without contact with those parts.

**How it works:** Rear feet longer than protruding fittings let the typewriter tilt up onto its back, and the case base is attached to the back wall for portable use.
