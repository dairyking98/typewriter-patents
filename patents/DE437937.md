## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Switching a cash register between one and two receipts means swapping the lifting disc with one or two edge cutouts.

**Solution:** Two identical lifting discs, each with two edge cutouts offset by 90 degrees, are fixed to the drive gear at a chosen offset, so the spring-loaded print hammer strikes once or twice without changing parts.
