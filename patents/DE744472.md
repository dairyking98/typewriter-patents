## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting tabulator and carriage-lift stops for several writing rollers is slow and needs repeated key presses.

**Solution:** The selection device also limits paper carriage travel in both directions, so zones can be set and skipped in one operation.
