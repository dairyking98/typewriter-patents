## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a foldable front-strike typebar machine the platen must be lifted evenly and stopped positively at both sides for upper case and figures, using shift levers on one side only, with simpler parts.

**Solution:** Two shift levers on one side lift vertical posts joined by a cross rod; rocking arms on both sides of the machine meet stop screws of two different lengths, and the figures lever rocks the arms to change which stop they meet.

**How it works:** Vertical posts slide in the frame and carry the carriage support, joined by a cross rod. The upper-case lever lifts the posts; as it does, a projection on the figures lever acts on a cam on the rocking arms (which pivot on the cross rod), swinging their top ends under the longer stop screw, so lift ends there. Pressing the figures lever instead carries the upper-case lever with it via a bracket and screw, moves the projection off the cam so the spring restores the arms, and the arms then line up with the shorter stop screws, giving the larger throw. Arms and stops sit on both sides, so the platen is stopped evenly at each end. A locking lever with two projections engages recesses in either shift lever.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Numbers:** 2 stop screws of different lengths per side; 2 sheets of drawings
