## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Machines with type carriers sliding in straight radial grooves to one strike point hide the last letters written because the horizontal carriers cover the writing line.

**Solution:** Incline the type carriers and their guide plate downward so they rest below the writing line, leaving it visible and shortening the machine.

**How it works:** Type carriers slide in grooves of a guide plate tilted against the horizontal and converge on a common strike point at the platen, guided at the end by a central guide common to all. Articulated levers moved by key buttons push each carrier up to the platen, and return springs bring them back, helped by gravity because of the slope.
