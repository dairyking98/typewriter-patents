## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon ends are soiled by handling, and the usual ribbon ends need the clean tabs to be attached quickly during inking.

**Solution:** A ribbon has a sewn clean tab on the forward end, lapped and sewn to the inked strip, and a glued tab at the rear end, applied after cutting, so the ends can be handled without soiling.
