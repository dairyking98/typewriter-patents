## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-cleaners for type-writing machines, and has for its object the production of a simple and effective device for performing the desired function quickly and thoroughly, and capable of being shifted or moved into and out of operative position with great ease and without affecting the operation of the type;
