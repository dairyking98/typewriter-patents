## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to paper-feeding devices for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Equalizing bars spread spring pressure to forward and rear feed rolls; a winding tensioner regulates pressure without dismantling, and a deflector hinged to the forward rolls guides leading edges.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feed roll spring pressure is uneven and hard to adjust, and sheets strike the rolls when introduced.

**Solution:** Equalizing bars spread spring pressure to forward and rear feed rolls; a winding tensioner regulates pressure without dismantling, and a deflector hinged to the forward rolls guides leading edges.
