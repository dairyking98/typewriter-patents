## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenography machine paper take-up) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for tensioning or winding the web

**How it works (reading):** The paper roll is driven by a ratchet and pawl on the roll that is turned only by a toothed sector swung by the keys, so the roll winds on the upward stroke and can unwind freely; the arm can also fold to the lower part of the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The stenotype paper roll must be wound by the keys without the roll being dragged when unwinding, and the winder arm is awkward for transport.

**Solution:** The paper roll is driven by a ratchet and pawl on the roll that is turned only by a toothed sector swung by the keys, so the roll winds on the upward stroke and can unwind freely; the arm can also fold to the lower part of the machine.
