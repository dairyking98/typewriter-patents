## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known platen uncoupling holds the decoupled roller with a separate double-armed lever.

**Solution:** A locking disc with a hole sits between the line-index wheel and the knob foot; a driver passes through it, so the platen stays freely rotatable until the knob is turned back.
