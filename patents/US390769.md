## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Same aim as US390768: operate any one of many typewriter levers with fewer operating levers.

**Solution:** Keys move notched slides; tumblers fall into the aligned notches, so each key combination releases only one tumbler.

**How it works:** Each of about six keys, through a bell-crank, moves its own slide endwise at right angles to the key. Slides are notched on the edge facing the tumblers. A tumbler can fall only when one notch of every slide stands under it; notches are laid out so that for the letter A the five slides of the other keys have notches waiting under the A tumbler and the A key's slide brings its notch into line when depressed. The fall of the tumbler moves the corresponding typewriter lever (shown as an attachment to a Remington). A diagram (Fig. 11) shows the notch layout. Only the opening is available.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [SEL:dummy-keyboard](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** key; bell-crank; slide; notch; tumbler; lever operated; fixed bar

**Numbers:** six keys and slides

**Improves on:**

- [US390768](../patents/US390768.md): later version of the same idea with simpler tumblers on the slides rather than tumbler-rods with a rock frame
