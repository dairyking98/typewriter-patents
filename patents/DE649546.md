## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in eliminating this deficiency by that the free ends of the rocker arm, which are not hinged to the key levers are coupled to the key levers by an inevitable connection so that the oscillating roller lever in every position of the key lever with the fixed roller surface be kept in touch.

**How it works (reading):** Free ends of the rocker arm are rigidly connected to the key levers, so the rocker stays in contact with the rolling surface in every position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Rocker arms lift off their rolling surface on fast or double key strokes and clatter.

**Solution:** Free ends of the rocker arm are rigidly connected to the key levers, so the rocker stays in contact with the rolling surface in every position.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
