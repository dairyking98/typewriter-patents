## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Exponent numbers or letters need the platen turned by hand.

**Solution:** A key on the platen shift lever produces a partial platen shift.

**How it works:** A hinged key member on the shift lever extension, limited above its normal position, contacts a stop and partially shifts the platen; the full shift is still possible. It can be built in or fitted as an attachment.

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** shift lever; extension; hinged key member; stop
