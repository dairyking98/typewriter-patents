## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements wanted to a Crandall-type machine: a combined adjustable carriage stop, bell and paper band; a carriage release; a better ribbon vibrator and take-up; easier-acting key levers at the ends of the keyboard; and an adjustable line-space ratchet.

**Solution:** A bundle of detail mechanisms for the Crandall oscillating-and-rotating type-sleeve machine, notably a straight lift bail that eases the end key levers and a pivoted ratchet plate with adjustable stop pawl for line spacing.

**How it works:** The type sleeve is lifted by a bail raised by the key levers; a roller on the bail arm rides a cam to raise the sleeve, which oscillates and strikes the platen. Because the key levers are fulcrumed on a curved rod, a straight bail along a chord shortens the lever arm for the outer keys. A paper band on the carriage carries an adjustable dog that rocks a bevel pawl to strike a bell near line end and then stops against a post; the dog can be tilted to pass the post or to lift the carriage off its track. Ribbon guide arms are springs that throw the ribbon to the platen as a key is struck and back as it is released. Line spacing is done by raising a plate pivoted on the carriage that carries a spring pawl on the platen ratchet, with a stop pawl entering one of several holes to set the line space.
