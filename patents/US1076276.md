## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars that stick in writing position after simultaneous keystrokes must be restored without removing the hands from the keyboard.

**Solution:** A releasing mechanism that restores type bars stuck in writing position against the restoring spring, applied to typewriters generally.
