## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen release clutches are bulky and mounted inside the platen, and the old designs are not simple.

**Solution:** A compact interlocking clutch on the end of the platen shaft, locking platen and line-space wheel together at any position, released by hand with a button and wedge members.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
