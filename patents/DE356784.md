## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator printing unit) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**How it works (reading):** A fixed cam piece drives a locking pawl into an incision of each type wheel as the link pivots, so locking is positive rather than spring-driven; a spring drives the link in a hammer-like impact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type wheels that are locked by springs can twist during printing if the springs break or friction is too high, giving a wrong result.

**Solution:** A fixed cam piece drives a locking pawl into an incision of each type wheel as the link pivots, so locking is positive rather than spring-driven; a spring drives the link in a hammer-like impact.
