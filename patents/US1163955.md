## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates broadly to type writing machines, and more particularly to amachine of a type which isdesigned to effect writing without producing noise.

**How it works (reading):** Improved means for damping the vibratory movements of the escapement dog carrier, quickening carriage feed, and adjusting the escapement parts, so the escapement runs quietly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement clicking makes typewriters noisy; the dog carrier also needs better arresting and adjustment.

**Solution:** Improved means for damping the vibratory movements of the escapement dog carrier, quickening carriage feed, and adjusting the escapement parts, so the escapement runs quietly.

**Features:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
