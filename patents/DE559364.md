## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (transfer of calculator result to typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Combinations of computing machines with other machines, e.g. with typewriter, with money-changing apparatus; Tabulators; Digital output to typewriters

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a special device for driving the Selector switch in connection with the control of the write keys in known Institutions of the last-mentioned type.

**How it works (reading):** A clockwork-driven position selector switch, stepped by a solenoid and controlled by contacts on the number discs, drives the type levers electrically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manually stepped carriage contacts are unreliable for sending adding-machine numbers to a typewriter.

**Solution:** A clockwork-driven position selector switch, stepped by a solenoid and controlled by contacts on the number discs, drives the type levers electrically.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** number disc; contact bridge; position selector switch; solenoid; type lever; key lever
