## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Organ and sound keyboards use lever-action keys whose layout and finger positions are fixed to the piano arrangement.

**Solution:** A honeycomb key field where each key moves only perpendicular to the playing plane, so the field can extend in any direction and be divided at will, with keys also usable for registration.
