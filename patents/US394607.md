## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A separate spacing lever between words slows typing.

**Solution:** A further depression of the last key struck makes the carriage rack advance two spaces instead of one.

**How it works:** Each key lever is connected to its typebar by an extensible spring connection, so the key can move farther after the type has struck. A connecting rod from the key operates a rocker with a rigid dog and a hinged loose dog on a rack. In normal travel a stop on the loose dog meets a fixed stop and the rack advances one space; with extra depression the stop passes around, giving two spaces. A stop-bar with a thumbscrew limits key travel so either single spacing or word spacing is available.
