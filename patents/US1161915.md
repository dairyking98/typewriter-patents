## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to work sheet feeding means.

**How it works (reading):** Auxiliary margin feed devices on the carriage feed a card or stencil in a substantially flat condition, applied to or detached from the machine without changing its structure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil cards and work sheets are bent and mutilated when fed around the platen.

**Solution:** Auxiliary margin feed devices on the carriage feed a card or stencil in a substantially flat condition, applied to or detached from the machine without changing its structure.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** margin feed devices; flat card feed
