## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the hangers of typewriting machines in which the type bars or carriers are journaled orfulcrumed.

**How it works (reading):** A split hanger whose rear jaws are spread by a screw-carried wedge, closing the front jaws on the type-bar trunnions.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-bar hangers need radial adjustment to align the type, and lateral adjustment to take up journal wear without removing the hanger.

**Solution:** A split hanger whose rear jaws are spread by a screw-carried wedge, closing the front jaws on the type-bar trunnions.

**How it works:** Each type-bar has conical trunnions seated in conical bearings of a two-armed hanger tied by a rivet, leaving an opening at the rear. A vertical screw in the top ring carries a spreader (wedge or flat strip) in that opening; turning the screw down spreads the rear jaws so the front jaws clamp the journals. A variant uses a horizontal screw with wedge tip. Radial adjustment is by loosening the hanger's retaining screw.
