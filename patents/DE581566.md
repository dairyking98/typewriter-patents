## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (disabling arithmetic switch on typewriters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**How it works (reading):** Turning the carriage return lever moves the control members so they do not act on the switching device, and the margin stops step aside.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return pushed the change-gear axis back and forth, straining the arithmetic switching linkage on typewriters.

**Solution:** Turning the carriage return lever moves the control members so they do not act on the switching device, and the margin stops step aside.
