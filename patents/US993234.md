## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to paper feeding and paper supply devices for typewriting machines and hasfor itsma-in object to provide improved mechanism for continuousribbon-like work sheets, such as those commonly termed tally strips.

**How it works (reading):** Rotary strip holders on the carriage and platen frame carry a tally strip under the platen, with adjustable holders and an inner transfer coating of two colors.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Continuous tally strips must be held and fed in register with the bill columns, and a separate carbon is needed.

**Solution:** Rotary strip holders on the carriage and platen frame carry a tally strip under the platen, with adjustable holders and an inner transfer coating of two colors.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** tally strip; holder; carrier; platen frame
