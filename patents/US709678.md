## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to mechanism 'for carrying rolls of paper which will be written on by the machine and on which a copy of the written matter may be produced.

**How it works (reading):** Adjustable holders for a paper roll with a knife and guide that display the printed line and let the original and duplicate be cut off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper rolls must be held in position, and the printed matter must be seen and cut from the roll at any point.

**Solution:** Adjustable holders for a paper roll with a knife and guide that display the printed line and let the original and duplicate be cut off.
