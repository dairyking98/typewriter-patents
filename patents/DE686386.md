## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (motor typewriter connected to card punch)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a with connected to a card punching machine, with typing keys and typing arithmetic keys equipped typing calculator with motor drive.

**How it works (reading):** Counter slides driven by the typewriter motor transmit the key value so the card punch is only controlled when the counter is set.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Card-punch control by key-driven typewriter can punch values not actually offset, causing wrong postings.

**Solution:** Counter slides driven by the typewriter motor transmit the key value so the card punch is only controlled when the counter is set.
