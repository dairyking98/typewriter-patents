## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (form feed and cutting for accounting machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**How it works (reading):** A frame clamps onto the typewriter carriage; one lever drives feed and cut, and the cut form is deposited by the same lever movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Form feeding, cutting and filing on accounting typewriters needed separate hand levers for feed, cut and deposit.

**Solution:** A frame clamps onto the typewriter carriage; one lever drives feed and cut, and the cut form is deposited by the same lever movement.
