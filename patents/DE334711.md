## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing in Braille or with keyboards specially adapted for use by blind or disabled persons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the method - the 7 line-by-line transport of the sheet on the paper carriage of the typewriter by means of a writing frame in which the sheet is unchanged for writing on both sides stored [remains and for which a guide device on the typewriter carriage is appropriate.

**How it works (reading):** The writing frame's line-spacing marks on opposite legs are offset by half a line, and the frame guide on the carriage is placed so that the offset is produced automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Double-sided interline braille writing needs the second page's dot positions to be offset from the first page's, which must be set by hand for each line.

**Solution:** The writing frame's line-spacing marks on opposite legs are offset by half a line, and the frame guide on the carriage is placed so that the offset is produced automatically.

**Features:** [USE:blind](../features/use-blind.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** writing frame; frame guide on carriage
