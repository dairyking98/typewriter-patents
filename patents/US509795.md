## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A ribbon feed is needed that moves the ribbon longitudinally and also laterally so that the printing line lies across the ribbon's width and along its length.

**Solution:** A rocking frame carrying the ribbon spools is swung by a cam on the carriage, and a fixed cam acting on a spool ratchet winds the ribbon as the frame swings.

**How it works:** Two ribbon spools, each with a ratchet, sit in arms of a rock shaft. A strip with an inclined face on the carriage works a lever that swings the spool frame as the carriage travels, and a spring returns it as the carriage moves back. A stationary spring cam arm engages a ratchet tooth as the frame swings, turning the spool one tooth and winding the ribbon. A thumbscrew on each spool shaft presses the ratchet against the spool by friction, setting tension and selecting which spool winds; loosening one and tightening the other reverses the ribbon.
