## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check strip insertion for cash registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device to facilitate the Inserting a new check roll and inserting the check strip into the printing, To facilitate the conveying and ejecting device.

**How it works (reading):** Two lifting disks on one axle, joined by a rail and operated by a single lever, lift the printing roller pressure roller and the ejector pressure roller together with one grip.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding a new check strip required both hands to lift the conveyor and ejector pressure rollers one by one.

**Solution:** Two lifting disks on one axle, joined by a rail and operated by a single lever, lift the printing roller pressure roller and the ejector pressure roller together with one grip.

**Features:**
