## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Correcting a wrong stitch on a web-cartridge typewriter needs the ribbon switched twice by hand.

**Solution:** The backspace key switches the two-colour ribbon automatically through a lever linked to the reset lever, so a rewrite with the write key restores the correct colour.
