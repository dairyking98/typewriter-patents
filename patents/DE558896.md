## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tax-strip evaluation by photocells is unreliable because a sideways shift of the strip makes a hole affect a neighbouring photocell.

**Solution:** An optical system images the strip area enlarged onto the photocells so the scanned area is larger than the cell and misalignment is tolerated.

**Features:**

- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** photocell; optical enlarging system; strip
