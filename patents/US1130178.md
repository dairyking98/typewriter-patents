## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an improved means in a typewriting machine for shifting the platen away from the paper guide to permit of easy insertion of considerable thicknesses of paper, or of paper with clasps and the like attached to it.

**How it works (reading):** C-shaped jaws at each platen end latch the shaft and are cammed open to shift the platen.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be removable and shiftable relative to its shaft quickly.

**Solution:** C-shaped jaws at each platen end latch the shaft and are cammed open to shift the platen.

**How it works:** Platen heads have oblong slots over the shaft with slide blocks on the shaft. Shutter-like jaws pivoted on the heads clasp bosses on the shaft, with curved slots and pins limiting swing, and springs closing them. A manual cam member spreads the jaws apart then shifts the platen transversely. A rock shaft connects both ends.
