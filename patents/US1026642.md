## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement should feed the carriage quickly but steadily and allow adjustment so keys work with light finger pressure.

**Solution:** A two-dog escapement on a rocker frame, one dog with a roller, a spring and adjustable stop, improving on the inventor's earlier patents.

**How it works:** On a Remington-type machine the universal bar rocks a frame via wires. The rocker frame, pivoted on the base, carries a normally engaged dog and a normally disengaged roller dog, both vibrating along the line of feed, which alternate to hold and release the rack on the carriage under the mainspring. The roller dog is spring held against an adjustable stop on the arm. Adjusting the parts sets the cycle so key levers need only light pressure.
