## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Back-spacing one letter space is interfered with by the carriage dogging and decimal spacing mechanisms of the Elliott-Fisher machine.

**Solution:** A back-spacing mechanism for the carriage, arranged so that dogging and decimal spacing mechanisms do not interfere with it.

**Features:** [ESC:backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
