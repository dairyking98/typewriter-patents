## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side lays or gauges

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper guides for the side edges of the paper, the guide be1ng slidably attached to the paper shelf.

**How it works (reading):** The paper side guide has a bearing portion that slides inside the rolled rim of the shelf, with its body over the shelf and its top curved over the rim, so the slide stays out of contact with the visible shelf surface.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Clips that grip the side-edge guide on the paper shelf scratch the shelf when adjusted.

**Solution:** The paper side guide has a bearing portion that slides inside the rolled rim of the shelf, with its body over the shelf and its top curved over the rim, so the slide stays out of contact with the visible shelf surface.
