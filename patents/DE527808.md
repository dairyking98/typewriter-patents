## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing unit for checks)

**How it works (reading):** Interchangeable name cliches and facsimile signatures are printed together with the amount and date, locked so unauthorised people cannot insert or remove them.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks need authorising signatures, which is slow when printed separately from the amount.

**Solution:** Interchangeable name cliches and facsimile signatures are printed together with the amount and date, locked so unauthorised people cannot insert or remove them.

**Features:** [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [Interchangeable element](../features/elem-interchangeable.md)
