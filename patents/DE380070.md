## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line adjustment cash register)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such an adjusting device and consists primarily in the fact that with the cutting punch an alignment pin intended to engage in a hole in the sheet connected is.

**How it works (reading):** A cutting punch cuts a rectangular piece from the card edge and a locating pin enters a hole to align the card for each printed line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Individual loose sheets in cash registers needed exact line positioning.

**Solution:** A cutting punch cuts a rectangular piece from the card edge and a locating pin enters a hole to align the card for each printed line.
