## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** A downward-bent stiffening edge on the carriage base limits the outward movement of the divided swingable guide rail, which is pressed by springs and attached with T-hooks in slots.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dirt and damage affect the carriage guide rails and rollers, and the swinging rail must be stopped from moving outward.

**Solution:** A downward-bent stiffening edge on the carriage base limits the outward movement of the divided swingable guide rail, which is pressed by springs and attached with T-hooks in slots.
