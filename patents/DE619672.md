## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (meat cutting device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mincing machines with perforated discs and feeding worms with horizontal axis

**How it works (reading):** Jacket and axis cutters have bevelled ribs at the cutting edges so the meat enters the cutting plane without pre-shredding, with a larger open passage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Meat grinders need a separate pre-cutting knife because the flat-ground cutter faces block the feed and reduce throughput.

**Solution:** Jacket and axis cutters have bevelled ribs at the cutting edges so the meat enters the cutting plane without pre-shredding, with a larger open passage.

**Features:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
