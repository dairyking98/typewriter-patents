## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Multiple web-feeding apparatus for webs superimposed during printing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to What are known as fiat platen typewriters, and more particularly to the equipment of the platen with means facilitating the manipulation and use of certain forms and combinations of work sheets or strips and transfer elements.

**How it works (reading):** Platen equipment with transfer elements and strip guides to handle multi-billing from a manifold strip or separate sheets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Flat-platen billing with manifold strips and separate sheets is cumbersome for transportation billing.

**Solution:** Platen equipment with transfer elements and strip guides to handle multi-billing from a manifold strip or separate sheets.

**How it works:** Equipment attaches to the platen to guide and register work sheets and transfer strips for multiple copies.

**Features:** [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
