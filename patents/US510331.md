## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On Remington and Caligraph machines the paper can only be set forward with the spacing lever or by hand on the platen, which soils the paper and is slow and uncertain.

**Solution:** A hand-wheel attachment meshing with a spur wheel on the platen end lets the platen be turned either way without touching the paper.

**How it works:** A disk with a spur wheel is fitted over the platen spindle and held by the platen end-plate screws. A bracket clamps to the carriage side-bar and carries a spindle with a pinion meshing with the spur wheel and a keyed milled hand-wheel on its outer end. Turning the hand-wheel rotates the platen forward or back to set the paper. It fits existing machines without alteration and can go on either end.
