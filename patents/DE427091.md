> Description Translated from German Switching bearing for typewriters. The invention relates to to a switchover bearing for typewriters with bearings in ball bearings and low movable type lever segment and consists in that the segment carrier is mounted in the machine frame in four ball bearings, two of which are between the rear wall of the machine frame and the rear of the segment carrier vertically one above the other in the middle of the segment carrier, while two more ball bearings between the front of the segment carrier and special, adjustable by bending Abutments close to the vertical edges of the segment carrier in a horizontal position Are arranged level between the other two ball bearings. As a result of this arrangement the segment carrier around these upper and lower bearings - by adjusting the Lateral bearings can be swiveled to the exact transverse adjustment of the segment carrier to enable. In addition, the segment carrier has bearing points in all four always closed ball bearing, so that the one aimed at by this ball bearing Smooth leadership is guaranteed under all circumstances, even if at the Manufacturing the cooperating parts larger tolerances are allowed.
