## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a simple and practical as well as reliably working device for automatic Reversal of the direction of rotation of ribbon spools.

**How it works (reading):** Three coaxial gears: the middle drive wheel always turns one way; a spring-loaded ball-driver couples whichever side gear drives the empty spool, and releases the full one.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-loaded feed hooks in ribbon reversal often fail to start transport after the direction changes.

**Solution:** Three coaxial gears: the middle drive wheel always turns one way; a spring-loaded ball-driver couples whichever side gear drives the empty spool, and releases the full one.
