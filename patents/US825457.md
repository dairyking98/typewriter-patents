## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making manifold records needs loose carbon or transfer sheets, which are troublesome to handle.

**Solution:** One or more ribbons are arranged on a visible-writing machine so that several copies are produced at once without loose carbon sheets.

**Features:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
