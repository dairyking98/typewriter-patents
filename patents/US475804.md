## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators of a machine that impresses matrices on wood strips could not see their errors; and a type-die wheel stopped at speed recoils and strikes out of true alignment.

**Solution:** A radial type-die wheel that is braked while a die strikes, with a tapered guide mouth centring the die and rubber mountings letting it yield, plus a visible typewriter writing a proof as each impression is made.

**How it works:** Keys select a die by rotating a wheel that carries dies radially until the chosen die faces the matrix strip. During positioning the die-actuating mechanism is locked, and once the wheel arrives a brake holds it and prevents recoil while the die is driven. A guide with an inward-tapering mouth between wheel and matrix catches the die and directs it to the exact spot, whether it arrives high, low or to one side. The dies sit in rubber-lined sockets so they can yield to the guide. Other keys are locked once one has been struck, and an attached typewriter mechanism prints a visible proof of each impression.
