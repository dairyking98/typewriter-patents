## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines for cutting stencils-and analogous purposes;

**How it works (reading):** A die-carrier disk on a shaft carries a circular row of dies and punches, and one operating lever that moves in a circular path over the punch row cuts whichever character lies beneath it, with an indicator and guide plate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil cutting by hand is slow, and the punch needs an operating lever that can reach any punch.

**Solution:** A die-carrier disk on a shaft carries a circular row of dies and punches, and one operating lever that moves in a circular path over the punch row cuts whichever character lies beneath it, with an indicator and guide plate.
