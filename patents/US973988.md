## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With column stops alone, the carriage must be arrested and released at each intermediate column, and printing denominationally in a column needs a second release of the carriage.

**Solution:** Tabulating mechanism with column stops and a second, independent denominational stop mechanism, so the carriage can skip intermediate columns and print at any predetermined column in one operation.
