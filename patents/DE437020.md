## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A single key-lever cash register cannot print both individual amounts and totals with the same drive, and the amount key levers were one-piece.

**Solution:** Amount key levers are split into front and rear parts with a spring-loaded pin that carries the rear part with it; an adjustable coupling lets the motor drive either the single-amount or the total printing device.
