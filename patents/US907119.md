## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in certain constructions and arrangements of` parts of my' improved typewriter attachment hereinafter specified and claimed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The existing carriage stops do not allow stopping the carriage at varied points near the left margin when it moves backward, for figure columns.

**Solution:** A detachable supplemental stop attached to the machine that limits carriage travel at adjustable points along its path, for placing figures in a column at the left margin.
