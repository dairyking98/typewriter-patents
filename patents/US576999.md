## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in devices designed for marking checks, bonds, or other instruments with figures or characters in such a' manner as to prevent them from being altered or raised;

**How it works (reading):** A check protector with male and female dies in a frame that punctures or breaks the paper, crushes the fibre, and inks the characters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Figures on checks can be raised unless the fibre is crushed and inked.

**Solution:** A check protector with male and female dies in a frame that punctures or breaks the paper, crushes the fibre, and inks the characters.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md)
