The invention relates to a device for typewriters for underlining and line drawing, in which a slider sitting on the paper trolley is an underline type which is pressed against the paper roller by the type lever. The invention consists in that a second Strichtype is arranged on the slide, which for Draw lines perpendicular to the upper edge of the sheet with the help of the usual dead key is brought to the stop. In this way, not only the well-known underlining, but also in a simple way the drawing of lines perpendicular to the upper edge of the sheet achieved.
