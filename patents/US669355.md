## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On flat-platen book typewriters where a hinged frame holds the sheet along its sides, narrow sheets, cards or small objects cannot be confined by the side bars.

**Solution:** An elastic metal lip on the hinged base-frame automatically clamps the end of the sheet when the frame is lowered onto the platen.

**How it works:** A flat platen supports the sheet, and the open rectangular base-frame carrying the writing mechanism is hinged at one end. A sheet-steel clip screwed to the rear cross-bar overlaps the rear edge of the platen, so lowering the frame presses the paper with yielding force and raising it releases the paper. A similar clip may go on the front bar, or short fingers may replace a continuous edge. For thick stacks, the hinge-pin slot is elongated vertically and a spring pulls the frame down, letting it rise slightly.
