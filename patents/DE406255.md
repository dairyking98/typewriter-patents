## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Support frame for the button lever and the type lever driving Intermediate lever. The present invention is a support frame for Key levers and the intermediate levers that drive the type levers on typewriters. What is new about this support frame is a common guide plate for the "intermediate levers and for the front ends of the key levers. This guide plate for the keys and intermediate levers can have a U-shaped bent part between the legs of which lie the pivot rod like the intermediate levers. The guide plate can be made of sheet metal and in its lower Part slots for guiding the button levers, in their upper part slots for have the leadership of the intermediate lever. Through the invention the construction and assembly of the machine facilitated and accelerated, and the use of metal sheets for the individual gesture parts makes them considerably cheaper. The invention is illustrated by way of example in the accompanying drawing. Figure i is a perspective view of the assembled frame. Fig. 2 shows a detail.
