## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In front-strike machines, rubber and paper particles from erasures fall into the type-bar bearings and interfere with free type-bar movement.

**Solution:** The inventor adds features that keep dust and grit out of the type-bar bearings; the text is cut off before the exact guard is described.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
