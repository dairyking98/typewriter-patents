## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guides on a bar above the platen block the view of the writing, and the guides cannot be brought close together for narrow paper.

**Solution:** The paper guides are supported so that the space between them, above and over the line of print, stays clear, and both guides are adjustable for any paper width.
