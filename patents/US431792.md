## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Messages sent by typewriter or telegraph are not secret, so the order of the characters needs rearranging.

**Solution:** The sender types a message on a typewriter-style keyboard in a rearranged character order, and the receiver's apparatus translates it back into readable text.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Uses:** [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)
