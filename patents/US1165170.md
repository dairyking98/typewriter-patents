## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards and envelopes slip or print poorly near the line of print; no scale at the print point.

**Solution:** A one-piece card holder on the shift rail with scale edge and curved fingers, plus a spring-loaded roller paper clamp.

**How it works:** A sheet-metal holder bolted to the shift rail has a notch for the type heads, an engraved scale edge, and concavo-convex fingers that hold paper against the platen with the front feed rolls. A rod with clamping rollers on spring-loaded arms can swing out of use. A ribbon vibrator form is also shown.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** card holder; scale; finger; clamp rod; roller; arm; spring; ribbon vibrator

**Improves on:**

- [US950326](../patents/US950326.md): adds scale and swing-away clamp
