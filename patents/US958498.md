## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Roller bearings for a shifting typewriter carriage need to run with minimum resistance without the balls displacing relative to each other.

**Solution:** Run steel balls in a groove between the carriage bar and the shift rail, each loosely ringed by a horizontal annular gear meshing racks on both rails so the balls are positively spaced.

**How it works:** A transverse carriage bar has a groove forming one half of a ball race; the carriage shift rail has the other half. A pair of steel balls sit in the race, each encircled by an annular gear that meshes a rack under the carriage bar at the rear and a rack on the shift rail at the front. The gears keep the balls apart and in step. The shift rail is raised by links from the keyboard to shift the platen, counterbalanced by a coil spring, on a Royal Standard front-strike typebar machine with escape wheel and fast and loose dogs.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** carriage bar; shift rail; ball race; steel ball; annular gear; rack; clip; counterbalance spring; platen

**Numbers:** 2 bearing balls

**Improves on:**
