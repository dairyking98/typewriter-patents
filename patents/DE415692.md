## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink ribbons characterised by the material from which they are woven

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon ink is loaded with about 20 percent colloidal ground charcoal so the writing is light-fast and copyable.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbon writing cannot be copied and fades with age.

**Solution:** Ribbon ink is loaded with about 20 percent colloidal ground charcoal so the writing is light-fast and copyable.
