## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Tabular work (bills, schedules) on step-by-step spacing machines is slow because the space key must be struck for every blank space.

**Solution:** A feed rack whose teeth are arranged in groups, so the carriage skips freely between columns.

**How it works:** The usual continuous toothed rack on the carriage is replaced by one with tooth groups whose counts and gaps match the form being filled. The spacing dog, worked by the space key, steps through a group of teeth and then the carriage runs free across the toothless gap to the next group. A duplex reversible rack has different groupings on its two edges for two kinds of work, or one edge ordinary. The rack drops into the existing holder on Remington and Caligraph type machines.
