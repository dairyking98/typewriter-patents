## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple one-key typewriter in which the operator picks the character on a scale and a single key does everything else, avoiding a full keyboard.

**Solution:** A key-carrying slide moves along a fixed scale, drags a multi-row type-form with it, and pressing the single key fires a hammer behind the paper and spaces the carriage.

**How it works:** The operator slides a horizontal slide, which carries both the index and the single key, along a stationary alphabet scale; a rearward arm moves a type-form on a guide bar in step. The key bit drops into a notch in the guide plate opposite the chosen character, which locks the form at the print point. Depressing the key rocks a swinging yoke that, through a lever, compresses a spring and releases a spring-driven hammer, which strikes the back of the paper against an inking ribbon and the type-form. The same yoke oscillates a space lever whose pawl advances the paper carriage a step, and clamp jaws on the carriage advance the ribbon. The type-form has three rows (capitals, lower case, figures); pressing the key into other notches lifts the form to a different row, releases it, or trips carriage return, and a screw on the feed roller engages a fork to line-space on carriage return.
