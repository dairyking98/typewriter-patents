## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type cannot be held steady for brushing and dirt has no place to collect during cleaning.

**Solution:** A socket or sleeve struck up in a plate on the ribbon-support plate receives a raised type so it is held against sideways movement while it is brushed, and an adjacent basin holds cleaning liquid and catches dirt.
