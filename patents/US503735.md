## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars hung on short pivots amplify any pivot play into misalignment at the type end, and a ring of a given size cannot carry long pivots for the full set of bars.

**Solution:** Mount each typebar on a long rock-shaft laid obliquely across the type-bar ring so adjacent shafts overlap, with a bent type stem near the inner end and a crank on the outer end.

**How it works:** Rock-shafts lie obliquely across the ring, supported in upturned ears on the inner and outer edges, with a conical head at one end and an adjustable cone-point screw at the other so they swing smoothly. A stem bent off each shaft near its inner end carries a type facing the direction of swing, so its face strikes squarely at the centre of the ring. A crank arm at the outer end of the shaft is connected by a draw rod to the key lever. Because the bearings are far apart, shaft wobble moves the stem little; bearings can alternate above and below the ring (riveted sheet-metal plates) to carry more bars, and a cushion ring below catches resting bars.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** type-bar ring; rock-shaft; type-bearing stem; type; crank arm; draw rod; upturned ear; cone-point adjusting screw; bearing plate; guard ring; cushion

**Improves on:**

- standard typebars on short trunnions: longer oblique shafts with widely spaced bearings reduce type misalignment
