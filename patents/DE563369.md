## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (ribbon loom for fringes) · **Subject (patent classes):** Selvedges

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Device on the ribbon loom for weaving fringes, in which the fringe stalks are formed from weft threads withdrawn from stationary final bobbins.

**How it works (reading):** A rod with a downward holding finger, mounted above the warp level, keeps each weft loop held continuously against the stop point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Fringe loops on a ribbon loom pull out near the fabric edge when the loop is released.

**Solution:** A rod with a downward holding finger, mounted above the warp level, keeps each weft loop held continuously against the stop point.
