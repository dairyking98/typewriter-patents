## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races; Ball or roller bearings; Means to synchronise movements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper slide guide on writing machines and similar machines with a stationary one and a movable rail between which friction-reducing balls are inserted which are moved by drives engaging with racks of the rails, which comprise the balls.

**How it works (reading):** Friction-reducing balls between fixed and movable rails are driven by racks, and a bracket carries a roller so the balls and roller move together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper carriage rails need a simpler design with constant engagement of balls, racks and rollers.

**Solution:** Friction-reducing balls between fixed and movable rails are driven by racks, and a bracket carries a roller so the balls and roller move together.
