## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker; Pawls and ratchets

**How it works (reading):** The end of the pawl's wedge surface runs out into a small shoulder that holds the ratchet tooth until the key is released.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** With wedge-faced escapement pawls using the carriage spring to speed return, a slowly released key lets the carriage take several steps for one stroke.

**Solution:** The end of the pawl's wedge surface runs out into a small shoulder that holds the ratchet tooth until the key is released.

**How it works:** Striking the typebar swings the pawl so its wedge face meets a ratchet tooth while the holding pawl releases the wheel. The carriage spring pushes the tooth along the wedge, driving the pawl aside until the tooth meets the small shoulder, where the wheel is held. On key release the spring returns the pawl, helped by the shoulder sliding on the tooth. In fast typing the tooth wipes the pawl straight back in one motion.
