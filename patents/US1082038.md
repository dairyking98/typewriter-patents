## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines with several characters per bar need the carriage adjusted vertically while keeping the line visible.

**Solution:** A typewriter with the line visible whose carriage is adjusted vertically to meet types at different distances from the pivot, with an open basket and noiseless felt rest.

**How it works:** Thin flat typebars carry upper case, lower case and a third character, pivot in slots of an arc-shaped bed and rest on a felt-faced second bed. Links join each bar to a rocking flat bar and the key stem. The platen carriage moves in a vertical plane to bring the chosen character level with the platen axis. The typebar compartment is open at top and front, giving view and cleaning access.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** typebar; type head; arc-shaped bed; felt buffer; key; link; platen carriage
