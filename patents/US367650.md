## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An electrical typewriter usable on its own or as the receiver of a printing telegraph, with automatic electric letter spacing.

**Solution:** Mechanical keyboard typelevers print directly; on key release a circuit closes and an electromechanical device spaces the paper roll, while in telegraph use a remote transmitter's circuit-closing keys replace the keyboard.

**How it works:** Pressing a key mechanically swings its type-lever to the paper roll. When the lever falls back to rest it closes an electric circuit that drives the spacing mechanism for the paper roll carriage. A separate spacing key is pressed repeatedly for word spaces. As a receiver, the transmitter keys operate the type-levers electromagnetically at local and distant ends, with plug switches to cut receivers out of the line. Line spacing and paper feed are covered in other applications.
