## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (electric printing instrument for speeches) · **Subject (patent classes):** Electromagnetic power drives, e.g. applied to key levers applied to elements other than key levers and acting on type members

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to report by printing in full, or by any suitable short-hand system, speeches or any oral or other com muv nication.

**How it works (reading):** A clockwork drives a spindle carrying disks, one per letter or sign, with wings and electric contacts so that the chosen character is printed by electrical circuits.
