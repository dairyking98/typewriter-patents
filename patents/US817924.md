## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A support board over the platen must be moved out of position when not in use, and carbon and record sheets must not block backward insertion.

**Solution:** The board pivots detachably on the paper carriage, a paper gage slides across it, and the board can be moved clear; carbon and record sheets are held stationary at the back of the platen to leave a passage.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
