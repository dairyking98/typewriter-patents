**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a switch lock with car freewheel for writing, booking and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A loosely mounted pawl under spring action swings out from the ratchet on an intermediate plate, limited by arc slots and a bolt, with a second spring latch; a clutch-linked latch swings out of engagement when the carriage is returned or released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switch locks on writing and booking machines are complex and can let neighbouring type levers cross and jam; carriage switching must be regular for even spacing.

**Solution:** A loosely mounted pawl under spring action swings out from the ratchet on an intermediate plate, limited by arc slots and a bolt, with a second spring latch; a clutch-linked latch swings out of engagement when the carriage is returned or released.
