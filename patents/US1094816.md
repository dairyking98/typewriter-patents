**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stopping typebars and spacing parts at the end of a stroke makes noise.

**Solution:** Universal bar and arresting parts stop the movements of operative parts silently, with the spacing mechanism timed to the start of the downward or upward key strokes.
