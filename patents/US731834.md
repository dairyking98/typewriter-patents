## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The rubber or elastic platen loses its elasticity over time and needs to be kept soft.

**Solution:** A removable plug in the end of the platen lets a softening liquid be injected into the absorbent inner layer, so that the outer elastic layer stays elastic.
