## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A bichrome ribbon must be used for stencil work and cannot be silenced so the type can strike a wax sheet directly.

**Solution:** An actuator in the universal bar moves the ribbon lever through a cam, and a silencing control holds the ribbon inoperative so the type strikes the wax sheet.
