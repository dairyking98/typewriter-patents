## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keys can be pressed while the paper finger is set, so the carriage can jam at the margin.

**Solution:** Moving the paper finger to the margin locks the keys while the carriage reaches the left end.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
