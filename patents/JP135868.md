## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing in Braille or with keyboards specially adapted for use by blind or disabled persons

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** Its object is to obtain a double-sided braille shorthand machine, economical of paper, that by a simple operation leaves a set interval between the lines of the first written side, prevents the braille of the second side, written from the back, from overlapping it, and, by gripping only the side edges of the paper in the rollers, completely prevents the braille from being crushed by the rollers.

**How it works (reading):** Grip only the two side edges of the sheet between paired upper and lower rollers on a travelling carriage, and feed the sheet in fixed steps that leave a blank band between lines for braille written from the back.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Braille writing machines waste paper and, when writing both sides, the rollers crush the dots and the second side overlaps the first.

**Solution:** Grip only the two side edges of the sheet between paired upper and lower rollers on a travelling carriage, and feed the sheet in fixed steps that leave a blank band between lines for braille written from the back.

**How it works:** Seven key levers carry upright styluses that, when pressed, enter a dot die and emboss the braille cell; the same key-lever frame drives a pawl on the carriage's saw teeth so a cord and drum pull the roller carriage one cell left per stroke. The paper is held at its two side edges by lower rollers fixed on one shaft and upper rollers on a spring-loaded shaft in slotted bearings. A detented knob on the lower shaft turns the paper by one eighth of the roller circumference per pitch; the operator turns it two pitches for a new line, leaving a blank band. An elastic paper rest holds the unfed paper clear of the keys and, when pressed, lifts the upper shaft to release the paper. For the second side the sheet is turned over and fed to the right stop.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Double-sided braille shorthand machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a double-sided braille shorthand machine characterised in that two kinds of roller, set one above the other along both edges of a roller carriage and fitted with special adjusting devices at the two ends of the upper shaft and in its bearings, grip only the two side edges of the paper being written on and feed it lengthwise by a set width each time, leaving a fixed blank space between the written lines, so that braille is later written into these blank parts from the back. Its object is to obtain a double-sided braille shorthand machine, economical of paper, that by a simple operation leaves a set interval between the lines of the first written side, prevents the braille of the second side, written from the back, from overlapping it, and, by gripping only the side edges of the paper in the rollers, completely prevents the braille from being crushed by the rollers.
