## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing device for documents with drum) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface with curved stamping surface for stamping by rolling contact using stamping rollers having changeable characters having automatic means for changing type-characters; Numbering devices; Inking devices using ink ribbons, ink sheets, or carbon tape or paper

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable drum carries an adjustable numbering or date cliche on its circumference and has ribbon rolls inside it; a coupling sets the drum rest position so each imprint lands at a set distance from the document's leading edge against a counter-pressure roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Postal and document stamping drums have fixed impression points, so the drum cannot be readjusted per sheet for a different imprint position.

**Solution:** A rotatable drum carries an adjustable numbering or date cliche on its circumference and has ribbon rolls inside it; a coupling sets the drum rest position so each imprint lands at a set distance from the document's leading edge against a counter-pressure roller.
