## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in carriage mechanism for type writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** Carriage ways that secure alinement and elevation, a shiftable platen support, and a line-lock that locks the key-levers at the line end with its own release.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage ways need proper alinement and elevation, and key-levers should lock automatically when the end of the line is reached, with a way to release the line lock.

**Solution:** Carriage ways that secure alinement and elevation, a shiftable platen support, and a line-lock that locks the key-levers at the line end with its own release.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
