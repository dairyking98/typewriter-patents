## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars carry two or three characters stacked at the head; printing them clean needs the platen or the whole carriage lifted, which is slow, wears and is noisy.

**Solution:** The inventor's arrangement lets the superimposed characters print in correct position without lifting the paper roller or the whole carriage; the excerpt states the problem and the full mechanism is not in the text read.
