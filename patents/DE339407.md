## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Two pins on the key lever engage recesses in an intermediate lever; as the key goes down, one pin then the other acts on it, so the lever-arm ratio changes and the type lever gets the intended translation without tie rods.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier type-lever drive used two tie rods to change the transmission ratio on the way down, which made the drive complicated, costly, tall, and a source of inaccuracy.

**Solution:** Two pins on the key lever engage recesses in an intermediate lever; as the key goes down, one pin then the other acts on it, so the lever-arm ratio changes and the type lever gets the intended translation without tie rods.
