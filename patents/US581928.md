## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards, envelopes and heavy paper are hard to guide, hold and feed on the carriage.

**Solution:** Pivoted feed roller arms and a paper-blade scale bar on the carriage guide and hold the sheet or card against the platen; a card-holder lets it be inserted.
