## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Subject of The present invention is a device for switching the ribbon drive on typewriters, where the tension created in the run-off ink ribbon is used for the compulsory switchover.

**How it works (reading):** Tension in the run-off ribbon pulls a lever that shifts a shaft along its axis, so bevel gears alternately couple each reel shaft to the ratchet drive.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon reels needed an automatic change of drive direction without a separate sensor.

**Solution:** Tension in the run-off ribbon pulls a lever that shifts a shaft along its axis, so bevel gears alternately couple each reel shaft to the ratchet drive.
