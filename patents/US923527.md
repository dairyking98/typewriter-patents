## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machines without end-of-line control let the types print past the margin.

**Solution:** A carriage-controlled interponent locks the universal bar at the line end, with a release key that also frees the margin stops.

**How it works:** The carriage carries an adjustable stop on a toothed bar at letter-space intervals. Near the line end a tappet moves interponents between the universal bar's rock arms and fixed abutments, preventing the types printing. A release key frees the interponents so a word may be finished, and also releases the carriage from the left and right margin stops. Paper feed rolls, finger guides, line-space regulator and platen release are also improved. Shown on a Sun typewriter.
