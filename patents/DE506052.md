## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a noiseless device Returning the carriage of typewriters and similar machines where between Your escapement and drive wheel has a built-in clutch which, by changing its position, for example by swiveling a release rail that is movable with the carriage, is disengageable.

**How it works (reading):** A slide on the release bar is dragged by friction during carriage return and pushes the release lever to open the escapement clutch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return is noisy when the release button is not held, because the clutch is not kept open.

**Solution:** A slide on the release bar is dragged by friction during carriage return and pushes the release lever to open the escapement clutch.
