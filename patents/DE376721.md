## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a writing machine;

**How it works (reading):** Key levers are arranged to converge toward the typebar pivots, and each key lever is carried with its type lever on a thin-walled bracket. The brackets hang from a removable segment, and the space bar is hinged back so it clears the packing space.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A portable typewriter needs small outer dimensions while keeping a usable typebar and key arrangement.

**Solution:** Key levers are arranged to converge toward the typebar pivots, and each key lever is carried with its type lever on a thin-walled bracket. The brackets hang from a removable segment, and the space bar is hinged back so it clears the packing space.
