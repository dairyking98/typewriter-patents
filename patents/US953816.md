## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a paper feed device or feed roller is left locked in the released position, the machine keeps working with the wrong feed and the user must notice and undo it by hand.

**Solution:** Means that automatically lock out the typewriter operation when a feed roller is in the released position, and automatically release the locked feed device and the parts locked out with it.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
