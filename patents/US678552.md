## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator needs to see the carriage position.

**Solution:** Index marks on the escapement pinion register with graduations on a letter-space scale on the rack.

**How it works:** The escapement rack carries a letter-space scale; a series of indexes on the meshing pinion rotate with it and each stands in register with a graduation at its highest point. Shown on a Remington No. 6.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** rack; pinion; index; scale
