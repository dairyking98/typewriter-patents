## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register with microfilm)

**How it works (reading):** A photographic recording device exposes each printed document together with its amounts on film strip for later checking.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash register entries can be altered or falsified after they are printed.

**Solution:** A photographic recording device exposes each printed document together with its amounts on film strip for later checking.
