## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper feed for card-controlled business machines)

**How it works (reading):** A paper feed device that switches the form tape by a controllable path, either as a differential feed after a group change or by a set gap length, controlled by the feed device itself.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card-controlled form printing needs paper switching at group changes and a gap switch of set length between sections, which is hard to control when the remaining space is unknown.

**Solution:** A paper feed device that switches the form tape by a controllable path, either as a differential feed after a group change or by a set gap length, controlled by the feed device itself.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
