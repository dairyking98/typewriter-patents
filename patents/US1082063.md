## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return to its correct position was hard, and line spacing had to be done separately.

**Solution:** An attachment fitted to the machine uses electro-magnets, circuit-closing devices and a cord winding drum to return the carriage automatically and accomplish the desired line spacing.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)
