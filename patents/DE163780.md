## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In swing-down type cylinder machines the cylinder bearing can drift sideways during the print stroke because the parallel guide alone does not hold it firmly.

**Solution:** Use two combs, not one, for longitudinal alignment, so lugs on the bearing locate it at two points in addition to the print point.

**How it works:** The cylinder axis turns in a swingable sleeve on a horizontal axis that is guided to move parallel to itself. In the raised position the cylinder is set roughly, then on each downward print stroke a star wheel enters a fixed bar for exact rotation and the bearing's cutter enters a comb for exact longitudinal position. The patent adds a second comb with lugs on the bearing engaging both combs, so the bearing is fixed at two points plus the writing point.
