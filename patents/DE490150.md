## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making negatives of typewriter font or letterpress type for copying needs a photographic or fluoroscopic detour via a transparent film.

**Solution:** A typewriter wax matrix treated with an opaque wax or equivalent layer; typing or printing with inked type destroys the layer where characters strike, so the sheet can be used directly as a negative stencil.

**How it works:** Impressed characters remove the opaque coating, leaving the non-printing parts covered and opaque.
