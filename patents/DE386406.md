## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**How it works (reading):** A one-way ratchet clutch on the drive shaft is switched by the carriage release lever, space and column adjuster, tab key, and wax-matrix writing, so the ribbon longitudinal movement is disengaged when not needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon moves all the time or only on each keystroke with a complicated clutch that often fails, which wastes ribbon and wears parts.

**Solution:** A one-way ratchet clutch on the drive shaft is switched by the carriage release lever, space and column adjuster, tab key, and wax-matrix writing, so the ribbon longitudinal movement is disengaged when not needed.
