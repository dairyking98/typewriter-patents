## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copy on a linotype machine should sit directly in front of the operator, and the holder should fold for carrying in a pocket.

**Solution:** End frames with slotted legs hook over the keyboard tray and fold, carrying a copy feed roller and presser bar that directs the copy between them.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)
