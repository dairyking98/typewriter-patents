## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the type-bar-operio ating mechanism of type-Writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** The type-lever is pivoted so its fulcrum changes during the excursion from key to platen, so the resistance and momentum are absorbed gradually.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed pivots for the type-lever make the key feel a sudden resistance as the type-bar starts, then a sudden stop when the type meets the platen, which fatigues the operator's hand.

**Solution:** The type-lever is pivoted so its fulcrum changes during the excursion from key to platen, so the resistance and momentum are absorbed gradually.
