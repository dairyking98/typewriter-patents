**How it works (reading):** One right margin handle sets both the bell and the first key lock and the second lock position, with a special release for the second lock.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Right margin needed two adjusters to bell, lock and unlock; a single setting could not cover all cases.

**Solution:** One right margin handle sets both the bell and the first key lock and the second lock position, with a special release for the second lock.
