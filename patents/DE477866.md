## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching a calculating typewriter between add and subtract and blocking the type levers for non-printing were complicated and unreliable with separate controls.

**Solution:** One hand lever shifts the adding mechanism to the opposite calculation type through a rack and sliding block, and through push rods and a slotted arc rail it also blocks or releases the type levers.
