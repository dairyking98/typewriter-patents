## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a new and useful improvement in paper feeding mechanisms for typewriting machines, the object being to provide one or more rolls of paper and carbon capable of lateral movement with the carriage and its platen, whereby one or more copies may be produced.

**How it works (reading):** Supply rolls carried on the carriage give paper and carbon with tension springs that take up slack, and pawls release the receiving rolls for backward movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper and carbon supply rolls should move with the carriage and carbon slack must be taken up when the platen runs back.

**Solution:** Supply rolls carried on the carriage give paper and carbon with tension springs that take up slack, and pawls release the receiving rolls for backward movement.

**How it works:** Tension on the supply rolls takes up slack when the platen reverses; release of the feed pawls lets the sheet move back.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** supply rolls with carriage; tension on supply; pawl release
