## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**How it works (reading):** The sheets are held in a fixed mutual position by a fixed guide, so inserting them into the correct position between ribbons and roller needs no separate operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Positioning the ends of two or more sheets between several ribbons on a calculating machine is done by hand with a pencil or fingers, which is slow and impractical.

**Solution:** The sheets are held in a fixed mutual position by a fixed guide, so inserting them into the correct position between ribbons and roller needs no separate operation.
