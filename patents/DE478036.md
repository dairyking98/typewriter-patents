## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Printing by pressing the paper strip into a type wheel needs bulky mechanism, and spring-carried types or tilting wheels with disengaging gears are awkward.

**Solution:** The type wheel, its gear parts and its setting magnet all sit on one shaft and tilt together onto the paper under a pressure magnet.

**How it works:** One magnet turns the type wheel via a linkage and steering wheel to the character; a cam and contact signal the zero position. A second pressure magnet tilts the whole assembly so the wheel axis lies parallel to the print plane, striking the paper strip. An ink wheel on the same axis inks the types.
