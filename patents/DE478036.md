## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Electric type printer with tiltable type wheel For most of the previously known electrical type printing apparatus with type wheel is the printing movement carried out in that the paper strip is pressed against the type wheel. this requires a rather cumbersome movement mechanism, especially with wider paper strips for the printing underlay and the paper strip lying on it. To avoid these disadvantages, it has already been proposed that to arrange individual types of the character wheel on resilient carriers. The individual carriers are brought into the area of a pen, which they counter to their spring action hits the printing surface. However, such an arrangement is rather cumbersome under construction and can also, especially with repeated printing of one and the same Type; lead to inconveniences as a result of the weakening of its spring action. Attempts have also been made to remedy the disadvantages mentioned by that the character wheel is arranged tiltable.
