## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In the patent’s own words:** Our object is to provide an automatic reversing mechanism, so that the iongitudinal movementof the ribbon will be reversed when the predetermined amount of ribbon has been Wound upon either spool.

**How it works (reading):** Oscillating spool-carrier frames driven from the spring drum through an elliptical gear and ratchet move the ribbon sideways and step the spools, with dogs in spool grooves tripping reversal.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon should use its full width automatically and reverse lengthwise travel when a spool is wound.

**Solution:** Oscillating spool-carrier frames driven from the spring drum through an elliptical gear and ratchet move the ribbon sideways and step the spools, with dogs in spool grooves tripping reversal.

**How it works:** A pawl on the spring drum turns a ratchet fixed to an elliptical gear, which drives a second elliptical gear and ratchet whose eccentric pitman oscillates the spool-carrier frames at each end. The swinging of a weighted pawl carried on a separate pivot steps the spool ratchet, feeding the ribbon lengthwise while it moves across the print point during carriage travel. Dogs drop into grooves in the spool cores and rise when the spool reaches a set size, operating bellcrank levers that cut out one end's pawl and engage the other.
