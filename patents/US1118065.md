## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Leader lines of hyphens or other characters are slow and error-prone to write by hand, because the carriage must be moved back by hand.

**Solution:** A means produces a line of characters by a series of intermittent operations of the type carrier, so the leader line is written in one action up to a set point.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** type carrier; intermittent operating means; hyphen key
