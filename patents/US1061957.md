## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar pivots and their bearings in the hanger need improvement, per the inventor's stated object.

**Solution:** A type bar with a pivot pin fixed in its pivot end, journaled in a two-piece sheet-metal hanger; one hanger arm has a tapered opening that takes a correspondingly tapered pivot head, and the opposite pivot end carries a bearing adjustable relative to the bearing.

**How it works:** The pivot pin is tightly secured in the type bar's pivot end so the two act as one part. It is journaled in two arms of a hanger made of two bent metal strips riveted together, bolted to the segment face. One arm has a tapered hole that seats the tapered pin head, and the other end of the pin runs in an adjustable bearing.
