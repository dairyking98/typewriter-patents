## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the ink ribbon mechanism of typewriting machines, particularly those in which the ribbon is vibrated to cover and uncover the printing point at the type strokes.

**How it works (reading):** Two ribbons with two vibrators and one winder selectable between spool pairs.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters cannot change ink colour at will.

**Solution:** Two ribbons with two vibrators and one winder selectable between spool pairs.

**How it works:** Same as US878988: front and rear ribbons of different colours or purposes each have spools and a vibrator, and the winder couples to one pair while its vibrator operates. Underwood.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spool; vibrator; winding mechanism

**Improves on:**

- [US878988](../patents/US878988.md): related application, same mechanism
