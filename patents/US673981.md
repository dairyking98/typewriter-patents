## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A thin type-bar with a multi-character head and a curved tail bears on a skew fulcrum-bar; finger-key links drive it through a guide plate, and shift-key slots move the bars to bring a second set of characters into action.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type-bar must hang, be actuated and aligned reliably, and the paper is punctured by the type, and shift to a second set of characters is hard to arrange.

**Solution:** A thin type-bar with a multi-character head and a curved tail bears on a skew fulcrum-bar; finger-key links drive it through a guide plate, and shift-key slots move the bars to bring a second set of characters into action.
