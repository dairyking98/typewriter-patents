## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typebar typewriter that should be as easy as possible to work, with parts that come off quickly for repair.

**Solution:** A typebar writing machine with a platen-shift upper case, a ribbon lift tied to the type bar and platen rail, a spring-drum carriage on a double-dog escapement, and removable platen, ribbon spools and key-lever units.

**How it works:** Each key lever drives an intermediate lever that throws a hung type bar to the platen, passing between guide rollers. The bar's shoulder pushes a universal bar that rocks a shaft, which raises the narrow ribbon guide to the print point, feeds the ribbon by pawls on two spool ratchets, and works a double-toothed escapement dog on the carriage rack. Two shift keys raise the platen frame (spring-balanced) to upper case; one key latches it up, the other releases it, and the ribbon lever is lifted further by the platen rail. A spring drum pulls the carriage; the rack can be tilted out by a handle for free travel, and a tabulator key lifts it out with adjustable stops. Space bar, bell, line lock and margin shoulder are also fitted.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Writing machine. Summary. In this invention a ratchet wheel carried by the platen is connected with a pawl pressed by a spring mounted on the vertical axis of an operating lever, and works together with an adjustable stop that changes the spacing of the lines. Shafts mounted on the side plates of the platen frame enter slots in the side plates of the carriage frame, and are held in position by locks carried on the carriage side plates.
