## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The universal bar of a typebar machine needs a better construction for operating the escapement from the key levers.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded universal bar whose lift is engaged by variable-length toes on the elbow levers and carries the escapement dog frame.

**How it works:** Key levers move elbow levers that swing the typebars; each elbow lever has a rearward toe that lifts the universal bar, with toes longer toward the sides to equalise the lift. A spring acting through a notched pressure lever holds the bar down, and its leverage grows as the bar rises. The bar's arms carry a dog-carrier with a fixed dog and a loose dog that engage the carriage rack and step the carriage one space.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [IMP:impression-adjust](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)

**Parts:** key lever; elbow lever; typebar; universal bar; pressure lever; spring; dog carrier; fixed dog; loose dog; rack; carriage

**Improves on:**

- Underwood universal bar: graduated spring and toe lengths
