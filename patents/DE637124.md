**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Lower edge delimitation for typewriters and the like, with which a narrow button after The transition of the paper falls into correspondingly narrow depressions in the roller and thereby switches on the limiting device.

**How it works (reading):** One or more ring grooves within the font width of the platen let the lower margin button work on any sheet width.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lower margin devices outside the font width fail to respond to narrow sheets such as cards.

**Solution:** One or more ring grooves within the font width of the platen let the lower margin button work on any sheet width.

**How it works:** The button drops into a ring groove that lies where the types strike, but is narrower than the letter spacing.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter lower edge limitation. The invention relates to a Lower edge delimitation for typewriters and the like, with which a narrow button after The transition of the paper falls into correspondingly narrow depressions in the roller and thereby switches on the limiting device. With the known lower margin Limitations of this kind are always found outside the of the narrow depressions the space of the platen acted upon by the type levers, i.e. outside the font width, attached close to the outer edge of the roller so as not to disturb the typeface. This has the consequence that with sheets or other documents, z. B. Post = cards, which are narrower than the usual font width on the platen; the lower margin does not respond. This is all the more unpleasant as that of addressing the lower margin accustomed scribes are all the less inclined to keep a sufficiently wide bottom Edge and therefore easily writes too deep on narrow arches. According to the invention, this deficiency is remedied in that one or several ring grooves, in which the buttons of the lower edge limit engage, in the part of the platen width acted upon by the type levers are arranged,…
