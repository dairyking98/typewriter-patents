## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tilton wants a machine that is easily and rapidly operated and simple and durable.

**Solution:** Two slides on rods over the paper, one carrying type that slide through it and a pointing lever, the other carrying an index board and impression lever and stepping automatically.

**How it works:** Two independent slides sit on rods over a flat paper bed. One slide carries the type, free to slide through it, and a pointing lever with pressure buttons. The other carries a letter board or index and an impression lever, and a guard over the ribbon with an opening for the types. Pressing the impression lever works a stepping mechanism of two steel dogs that grip the rod alternately, and a coiled spring plus a cord and weight move the slide one step when the lever rises. The slide is then locked until the next step. The ribbon lies over the paper with a slotted protector.

- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Straight type bar or rule](../features/elem-linear-bar.md)
- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)
