## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices actuated when platen reaches the end of a line

**How it works (reading):** A swivel finger tripped by the carriage margin stop at the end position locks the universal rail, so the intermediate levers and key levers are blocked.

## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** At the end of the line the carriage must stop several typebars from clashing at the same point.

**Solution:** A swivel finger tripped by the carriage margin stop at the end position locks the universal rail, so the intermediate levers and key levers are blocked.

**How it works:** A universal rail swings out with every keystroke by arms on the rod carrying the intermediate levers and triggers the step switch. A locking finger pivots on a fixed stop in the carriage path and is taken along by the margin stop at the end position. Via a tension bar it turns a lever with a hook, which catches a lever coupled by wire to the universal rail and so holds the intermediate levers in rest. A spring returns the lever when the carriage moves back.
