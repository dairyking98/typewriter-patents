## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator carriage spring drum)

**How it works (reading):** Several leaf spiral springs lie side by side along the drum axis, separated by loose washers, with inner ends fixed to a sleeve pinned to the axis and outer ends fixed to the drum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A single leaf spiral spring spanning the whole winding drum cannot be fixed exactly on the axis, so the drum tilts while turning and the spring often breaks.

**Solution:** Several leaf spiral springs lie side by side along the drum axis, separated by loose washers, with inner ends fixed to a sleeve pinned to the axis and outer ends fixed to the drum.

**How it works:** The springs are spaced by annular washers, and the pinned sleeve transmits the spring tension without tilting the drum.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** winding drum; leaf spiral springs; sleeve; axis pins; annular washers; tension string

**Numbers:** four springs in the example
