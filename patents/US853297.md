## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriting attachment for adding machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cooperation and interconnection of the input arrangement with other functional units of a computer

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in typewriting attachments for adding ma 1 5 .

**How it works (reading):** The attachment is mounted on an adding machine; a type-wheel is positioned by a lever and moved into contact with the platen, which is shared between the adding and typing machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An adding machine needs a typewriting attachment so that its items can be printed as well as totalled.

**Solution:** The attachment is mounted on an adding machine; a type-wheel is positioned by a lever and moved into contact with the platen, which is shared between the adding and typing machines.

**Features:**

- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
