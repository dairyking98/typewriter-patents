## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen must sometimes be turned independently of the line-spacing ratchet for variable line feeding.

**Solution:** The platen and ratchet ring are normally held together by a C-shaped friction spring that a knob and cam sleeve can release.

**How it works:** A C-shaped spring in a box on the right platen head bears outward on the flange of the ratchet ring, so ratchet and platen turn as one with no slip. A hollow sleeve on the platen shaft has two cam lugs that rock a lever fixed to one end of the spring. Turning the sleeve knob contracts the spring and frees the ratchet from the platen, which can then be turned by the other knob. Releasing the knob restores the friction grip.

**Parts:** platen; ratchet ring; C-shaped spring; spring box; lever; cam sleeve; knob; platen shaft

**Improves on:**
