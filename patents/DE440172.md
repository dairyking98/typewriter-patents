## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Teaching aids and children's typewriters need a simple, cheap way to reverse the ribbon's longitudinal travel.

**Solution:** A control rod with tapered sliders and intermediate pins alternately lifts one or the other crown gear of the ribbon spool carriers out of mesh with a drive pinion.

**How it works:** Two ribbon rolls sit on vertical axes in a U-shaped frame, each with a slidable crown gear at its lower end. One crown gear meshes with a pinion on the drive shaft, which is stepped by a pawl from the universal rail. A reversing rod with two cone pieces, moved by an outside button, drives upright pins that ride the cone surfaces and lift one crown gear out of its pinion while the other drops into mesh. Ring grooves in the cones snap the pins to hold the rod. Springs around the axes press the gears into mesh and brake the unwinding roll when the gear is lifted.
