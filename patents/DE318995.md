## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type strips, boats and wheels are normally hard rubber to keep weight low, but rubber wears quickly and the type loses sharpness.

**Solution:** Make the type body from an electrolytically deposited cobalt-nickel alloy in a thin section, as light as rubber but far harder and more durable.

**How it works:** The type carrier (strip, boat or wheel) is produced electrolytically from a dense, hard cobalt-nickel alloy. Because it is deposited in a very thin wall, its weight matches the rubber body it replaces. The harder metal keeps type sharpness many times longer. No other mechanism is described.
