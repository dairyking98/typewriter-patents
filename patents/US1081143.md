## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar pivots in front-strike visible machines either have small bearing surfaces that wear or allow no wear adjustment without removing the typebar.

**Solution:** A separate hanger for each typebar combines a ball bearing on one side with a plain wide bearing on the other, with a spring-steel clamping member and a screw to take up wear.

**How it works:** Each typebar has its own hanger of two members screwed to the frame through slotted holes for vertical adjustment. The clamping member, of spring steel, is fixed at its top to the supporting member and has an offset flange at the bottom. A ball sits in tapered openings in the clamping member and a boss on the typebar, forming a pivot on one side. On the other side the boss bears directly on an annular opening in the supporting member. Tightening the lower screw springs the clamp inward and takes up wear without removing the typebar, which can easily be lifted out.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** hanger; supporting member; clamping member; ball; typebar boss; adjusting screw; key lever; platen

**Improves on:**

- conventional typebar hangers: large narrow bearing surface plus per-bar wear adjustment without removal
