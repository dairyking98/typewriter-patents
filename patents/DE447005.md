> Description Translated from German Device for releasable attachment of the paper trolley guide to the typewriter frame. The priority of filing in the UK from e.g. June 1925 is claimed. The invention relates to a device for releasably securing the paper trolley guide to the typewriter frame and consists in that a fixed head bolt is provided on one part and an acute-angled sliding wedge that can grip under the head of this head bolt is provided on the other part. This device is characterized by its great simplicity and reliable effect.
