## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for releasably securing the paper trolley guide to the typewriter frame and consists in that a fixed head bolt is provided on one part and an acute-angled sliding wedge that can grip under the head of this head bolt is provided on the other part.

**How it works (reading):** A fixed head bolt in the frame engages a sliding acute-angled wedge with a keyhole slot in the guide, locked by a spring-loaded handle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The paper carriage guide must be fitted and removed without loose parts.

**Solution:** A fixed head bolt in the frame engages a sliding acute-angled wedge with a keyhole slot in the guide, locked by a spring-loaded handle.
