## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hotel and restaurant checks need the check number, amounts, guest count and waiter's collections printed, with a correct record kept on a continuous duplicate slip.

**Solution:** A type-writing checking machine with a roller platen that prints the check details and a continuous duplicate record slip, built for hotels and restaurants.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
