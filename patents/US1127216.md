## Reading (full text, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A detachable platen carriage must seat in exact register on the machine frame and attach rigidly without screws, yet be quick to remove.

**Solution:** Spring-loaded pivoted latches on the carriage holder hook under the heads of studs on the frame, and their beveled surfaces wedge the holder down and sideways tight against the frame.

**How it works:** The carriage holder is a pair of guide runners joined by end plates. Each end plate has a recessed lug that slides over a headed stud on the machine frame as the holder is moved into position. A latch pivoted on the end plate is spring-pressed to swing its hook under the stud head; the beveled or curved underside of the head wedges the holder firmly down onto the frame as the hook advances. Pressing a finger piece on each latch withdraws the hooks, after which the holder is slid along the top plate and lifted away, clear of the ribbon mechanism.
