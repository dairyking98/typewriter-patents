## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**How it works (reading):** Spring-loaded pull rods engage the universal rail only while in use; each spring hangs freely on a guide hook rod so oil does not impede it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tie rods driving the universal rail rub against the rail when idle and the return springs get oil-thickened and sluggish.

**Solution:** Spring-loaded pull rods engage the universal rail only while in use; each spring hangs freely on a guide hook rod so oil does not impede it.
