The coil springs are pushed over the tie rods, which Return the intermediate levers to their initial position. These well-known bodies have the disadvantage that the universal rail is constantly on the hook when moving remaining tie rods rub. In addition, there is dripping oil on the feathers the overlying machine parts settles so that the sliding on the rods Feathers as a result of the thickening of the oil are impaired in their effect, whereby the stop of the machine is also adversely affected. In order to remedy these shortcomings, stand behind according to the invention the universal rail engaging tie rods under the action of springs, which are free next to the tie rods and with your rear.
