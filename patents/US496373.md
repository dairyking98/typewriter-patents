## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The aims of the invention are mainlyt cheapen the construction, to increase the operative speed, to secure more accurate feeding of the sheet, to permit the ready change y from one style of type to another, to permit the ready insertion of characters at any desired points in the previously formed matrix, and to facilitate the grouping of the characters in tabular form.
