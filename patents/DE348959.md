## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A pawl-and-ratchet clutch between the carriage and its spacing ratchet should release itself on carriage return and stay out for a silent return, with a simple construction.

**Solution:** Two opposed double-armed pawls on the spacing ratchet engage a friction-driven locking wheel on the carriage shaft, which turns them out of engagement when the shaft reverses.

**How it works:** The spacing ratchet turns freely on a shaft linked to the paper carriage, stepped by a fixed tooth and an oscillating tooth on a swinging switch knife. Two opposed pawls on the ratchet engage a locking wheel that is fixed to the shaft against rotation but axially displaceable and spring-pressed against segment-shaped extensions of the pawls. During writing the locking wheel turns forward and the pawls drive the carriage letter by letter. When the carriage is pushed back the shaft turns the other way, and friction between wheel and extensions swings the pawls out of engagement, uncoupling the ratchet so the carriage moves freely. The friction keeps them out for the rest of the return, making it silent.
