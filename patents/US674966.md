## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inking is complicated and cannot travel with the paper.

**Solution:** Inking mechanism mounted on the carriage, with ribbon reels on plates riding on the platen journals.

**How it works:** Plates mounted on the platen roller journals carry a ribbon reel at one end and a ribbon guide arm at the other, removably attached to the carriage frame. The ribbon is kept taut and away from the platen by a spring-actuated arm with a roller that presses it against inking rollers.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** platen roller; journal plate; ribbon reel; guide arm; spring arm; inking roller
