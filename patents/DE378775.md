Alarm protection for typewriters, in which one is provided with a drive spring Bell is triggered by lifting the machine from its base. Patented in the German Empire on May 31, 1922. The invention relates to an alarm device for typewriters in the form of a box-like Pedestal, in which a spring mechanism is attached, which immediately in Activity occurs when the typewriter is off the pedestal is lifted. The spring mechanism then actuates several bell clapper, so that the lifting of the typewriter from the pedestal is immediately audible from afar.
