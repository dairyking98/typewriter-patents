## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stretching printing pad to line length)

**How it works (reading):** Marks are placed on the tape at line start and end, and the tape is advanced until the leading mark meets a counter-mark exactly one line length away.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stretching an elastic printing substrate to exact line length by hand or existing means is unreliable and cumbersome.

**Solution:** Marks are placed on the tape at line start and end, and the tape is advanced until the leading mark meets a counter-mark exactly one line length away.

The inventive method achieves this expansion in that mark the current printing material (tape) at the beginning and end of the line and then, through the interaction of a counter mark with the trailing tape mark, at first only recorded at the end of the line, but for the rest of the time still stretched for so long is moved on until the leading tape mark (beginning of line) with a second Counter-mark, which is removed from the first by the exact line length, in interaction occurs. It is useful to have an adhesive mark at the beginning and end of each line and applied an electrically conductive mark to it.
