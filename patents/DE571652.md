## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Method of stretching a stretchable printing substrate to the exact Line length It is known to produce a print by means of suitable types on a stretchable printing pad, this on the required Line length stretches and then the pressure decreases. The funds used so far to stretch the printing substrate to the line length are unreliable and cumbersome. The inventive method achieves this expansion in that mark the current printing material (tape) at the beginning and end of the line and then, through the interaction of a counter mark with the trailing tape mark, at first only recorded at the end of the line, but for the rest of the time still stretched for so long is moved on until the leading tape mark (beginning of line) with a second Counter-mark, which is removed from the first by the exact line length, in interaction occurs. It is useful to have an adhesive mark at the beginning and end of each line and applied an electrically conductive mark to it. Applying the brands is done by the same means (e.g. buttons and mechanical or electrical Transmission) with which the type carriers are moved.
