## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Column stops for tabulation must be set individually for each column, and the carriage needs to be freed from the normal escapement and stopped at set places in each line.

**Solution:** Two stop members, one on the machine frame with individually adjustable stops that a finger lever shifts to denominational positions, and one on the carriage with a dog that engages the stops, so one tabulating lever moves the carriage past them.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
