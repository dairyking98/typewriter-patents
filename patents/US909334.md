## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to carriage retarding devices for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** The retarder is coupled to the carriage only after the carriage has been released from its step-by-step feed and has moved freely a number of letter spaces, so the carriage overcomes its own inertia first.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage retarder is connected to the carriage at once, so the carriage must first overcome the inertia of the retarder and loses time.

**Solution:** The retarder is coupled to the carriage only after the carriage has been released from its step-by-step feed and has moved freely a number of letter spaces, so the carriage overcomes its own inertia first.
