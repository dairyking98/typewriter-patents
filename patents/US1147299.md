## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a typebar machine with three types per bar and a shifting platen, ordinary parallel links make the platen swing in an arc, so the bars travel different angles in each case position and the type faces would have to differ from bar to bar.

**Solution:** Two sets of unequal-length links guide the platen carriage so its axis moves in a straight line, and the platen stops at three positions (lower, capitals, figures) chosen by two shift keys and stops.

**How it works:** The carriage rides on a shift rail hung from a long pair of rear-pivoted links and a short pair beneath, which together give a near-straight-line rise at the printing point. Two shift keys act through separate first-order levers on a pin on the shift frame. The figures key lifts the frame to its upper stop; the capitals key also lifts it but a pivoted arresting hook, moved by that lever, enters the pin's path and halts the platen at the middle position. A pivoted shift lock holds either key down, and a spring on one link balances the weight of the carriage. The escapement dogs ride on the shift rail so they move with the platen.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; carriage; shift rail; long link; short link; shift key; shift lever; arresting hook; screw stop; shift lock; counterbalance spring; type bar; feed rack; feed dog

**Numbers:** 3 types per bar; 3 platen positions; 2 pairs of links

**Improves on:**

- parallel-link platen shift on two-type bars: unequal links give straight-line shift so three-case bars can have type faces in one plane
