## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check dispenser cash register)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a check dispenser for cash registers and consists essentially in: that with one and the same check dispensing device, depending on the setting of the same, either a single or a double check can be issued.

**How it works (reading):** A setting selects single or double printing; a Maltese cross driven by pins from a disc lifts the pressure plate that presses the paper against the type wheels, and one of two pins can be disengaged to give a single stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** One check dispensing device must be able to issue either a single check or a double check.

**Solution:** A setting selects single or double printing; a Maltese cross driven by pins from a disc lifts the pressure plate that presses the paper against the type wheels, and one of two pins can be disengaged to give a single stroke.
