## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon must move both lengthwise and widthwise, and be fed taut so the whole width is used.

**Solution:** Two spool frames ride on ball bearings and are shifted transversely by a rock shaft while spring-fork-driven ratchet pawls wind the ribbon lengthwise.

**How it works:** The ribbon runs from a spool through an opening in one V-grooved frame across the machine to the opposite frame and spool. Each spool shaft has a ratchet wheel stepped by pushing and pulling pawls on a lever worked through a spring fork. A rock shaft with rocker arms moves both frames back and forth transversely on antifriction balls, so the type strikes different ribbon lines. A crank on the shaft allows fast rewinding or colour change. Shown on a Remington machine with a type ring.
