## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** A frame with movable arms clamps the ends of ribbons in one or more layers, and adjustment devices keep even tension across each band.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons for duplicating must be removable and kept evenly taut, including at their ends.

**Solution:** A frame with movable arms clamps the ends of ribbons in one or more layers, and adjustment devices keep even tension across each band.
