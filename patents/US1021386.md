## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen and line-space wheel cannot be locked together reliably, and bolts may fail to enter their seats.

**Solution:** Spring-pressed bolts on a carrier engage seats on the platen clutch member, placed at unequal intervals so at least one bolt registers, and a finger piece withdraws them.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)
