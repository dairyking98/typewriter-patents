## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Bowden cable on a manuscript holder with line pointer is fixed, so it can kink in packing and the whole holder must go back to the factory if it breaks.

**Solution:** Both the cable and its sheath are hung in sprung open eyes so the owner can detach and replace the Bowden cable.

**How it works:** A key operated by the typewriter pulls the Bowden cable, which moves a rod down against a spring, and a locking sleeve with a slide roller advances the line pointer one line. The cable end sits in a holding piece that is clipped into a curved leaf spring at the rod top; a tongue limits the spring opening so it cannot be bent open. A second angle piece carries a similar detachable fitting for the cable sheath.
