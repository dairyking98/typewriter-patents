## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guards, shields or dust excluders

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and it has for its principal object to provide an improved dust guard to protect the bearings of the type bars of a front strike typewriter from the dust, more especially the product of erasures, which is liable to fall from the platen of such machines into the bearings of the type bars.

**How it works (reading):** A combined dust guard or shield that also serves as a platen plate, platen scale and carriage scale, placed under the platen to keep dust out of the type bar bearings.
