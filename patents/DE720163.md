## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Vorsteckeinrichtung. Office machines The invention relates to Hiding devices on office machines. It has already been proposed to use two or more insertion funnels arranged one behind the other to advance several cards. Each individual pre-installed card is inserted into one of the funnels and arrives in front of the base sheet. or in front of a pre-inserted card in the machine. The card that was inserted first is not retained, however, and it can happen that when a second or additional card is inserted, the one that was inserted first is displaced. The arrangement of several hoppers one behind the other also requires a considerable amount of space which is not available on most machines, and such an arrangement is also too expensive. An attempt has already been made to replace an introducer funnel to only use his only paper mounting plate and since-] I asked for the mounting plate swiveling, swiveling levers arranged "which go to pivot towards the contact plate and at their free end a rubber cushion: or the like.
