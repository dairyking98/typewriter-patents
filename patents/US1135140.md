## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the work-handling devices of typewriting and analogous machines, and particularly to means for feeding the work members, for example envelops, around the platens;

**How it works (reading):** An endless band of thin metal strips with hooked lugs picks envelopes one by one from a magazine and carries them around the platen, driven from the platen shaft.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Envelopes must be fed to the platen by hand and tend to slip or misfeed.

**Solution:** An endless band of thin metal strips with hooked lugs picks envelopes one by one from a magazine and carries them around the platen, driven from the platen shaft.
