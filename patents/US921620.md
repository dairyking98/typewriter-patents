## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of this invention is to overcome these objections, anc make it practicable to feed a card accurately in linespace direction, and to write clearly upon all parts of the same, including all edge portions.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary feed roll above the printing line, driven by rolls on the platen, draws the card up line by line so it stays flat and is fed accurately.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stiff cards bulge and blur at their edges because they spring back around the platen and are not held at the print line.

**Solution:** An auxiliary feed roll above the printing line, driven by rolls on the platen, draws the card up line by line so it stays flat and is fed accurately.
