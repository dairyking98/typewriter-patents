## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing ribbon colour switches had only two positions and could not take the ribbon fully out of the print position for stencils.

**Solution:** A one-way rotating crank disc turned a step by each stroke of a single key gives three ribbon positions with a visible indicator.

**How it works:** A key pulls a rod with lugs that engage one of four driver pins on a disc, turning it a quarter turn each press, with a leaf spring detent. The disc moves a rod and parallel lever linkage that sets the ribbon mechanism, and tabs on the disc, coloured to the ribbon colours, show through a window. Limiting the key stroke at the pull rod avoids play. One colour appears twice per revolution, ideally the most used.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** setting key; pull rod; disc with four tabs; driver pin; leaf spring detent; parallel lever linkage; indicator window

**Numbers:** four positions per revolution

**Improves on:**

- two-position ribbon colour key: adds a third position for stencil writing by cycling in one direction
