## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator of an Oliver machine cannot see where the type will strike.

**Solution:** A pointer on a rocking lever indicates the strike point and is moved out of the way when the typebars swing to the paper.

**How it works:** An upright rocking lever pivoted on the ribbon-guide support arm carries a pointer that rests over the striking point. When the typebars move toward the paper the lever is rocked, moving the pointer clear, and restores it when they retract. The motion is taken from existing Oliver parts, the escapement lever and ribbon throw crank. Only the opening is available.
