## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a movable paper carriage carrier for typewriters and aims to arrange a carriage in such a way that it can be easily removed and replaced, and in which the paper roller with its pressure rollers are supported so that they can be detached from the carriage as a unit.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage carrier is a bracket with hooks that hang on cross bars of the machine stand, so the carriage can be lifted off, and the paper-roller frame is carried by arms so it detaches with the rollers as a whole.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The paper carriage must be removed and replaced easily, and the paper roller and its pressure rollers must come off as one unit.

**Solution:** The carriage carrier is a bracket with hooks that hang on cross bars of the machine stand, so the carriage can be lifted off, and the paper-roller frame is carried by arms so it detaches with the rollers as a whole.
