## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The spherical-type machine (Hansen writing ball) needs adapting to narrow strips of paper like Morse telegraph tape.

**Solution:** A hinged, spring-loaded oscillating anvil carrying a paper strip beneath the converging types.

**How it works:** A hinged base carries an oscillating frame with an anvil and guide flanges for the paper strip, positioned just below where the types converge. A coiled spring in a standard, with a tension button, and two set screws limit and adjust the frame's play. A roller feeds the strip.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Type sphere](../features/elem-sphere.md)
- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md), [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
- **Uses:** [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** base plate; oscillating frame; anvil; paper strip; coiled spring; set screw; spherical type shell; roller

**Improves on:**

- Hansen writing-ball machine: adds narrow-strip paper handling
