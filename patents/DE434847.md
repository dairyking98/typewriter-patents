**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**How it works (reading):** The straight guide of the universal rail is made by two pairs of handlebars; one pair is swung out by the universal rail to lock the carriage, and the other pair is moved by dead keys to drive the ribbon in the longitudinal and transverse directions, using a single bent lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accent characters are omitted on portable typewriters because the usual accent arrangements are bulky and costly, and they take the space the machine parts need.

**Solution:** The straight guide of the universal rail is made by two pairs of handlebars; one pair is swung out by the universal rail to lock the carriage, and the other pair is moved by dead keys to drive the ribbon in the longitudinal and transverse directions, using a single bent lever.
