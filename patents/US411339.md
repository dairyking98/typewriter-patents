## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Remington carriage needed improvement in how the carriage swings and spaces.

**Solution:** A carriage frame mounted on pivoted arms with jointed journal boxes and roller-borne front, with spacing levers on a rock shaft.

**How it works:** The carriage frame rides on its front on a shifting rod through a concave vertical roller and a horizontal roller to remove friction, and an indicator with an inclined face guides it onto the rod when lowered. At the rear, arms carry pivoted short arms with journal boxes holding the carriage rod, with a notch and pin to limit motion, letting the carriage tilt up. A rock shaft with spacing levers and spring return connects to the spacing mechanism, and a feed roller, scale and paper table belong to the carriage.
