**How it works (reading):** A bumper stop (adjustable or an eccentric) limits key-lever travel, and a slot-guide freewheel lets the type lever keep its momentum after the key lever stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hard key stops brake key travel, while fixed stops reduce striking power.

**Solution:** A bumper stop (adjustable or an eccentric) limits key-lever travel, and a slot-guide freewheel lets the type lever keep its momentum after the key lever stops.
