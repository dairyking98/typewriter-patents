## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A platen wears along the same lines because each line space returns to the same longitudinal strip.

**Solution:** An eccentric internal gear driven by the space-lever ratchet turns the platen through a pinion so that successive printing lines fall on different, overlapping strips of the platen surface.

**How it works:** The platen and a concentric pinion sit on one shaft. An internally toothed annular gear of about the platen diameter runs eccentrically on three disk rollers on pins in the carriage frame, and has a ratchet rim engaged by the space lever. The gear has more teeth than the pinion and meshes with it, so turning the gear turns the platen faster and the stopping points differ each revolution. Successive longitudinal lines therefore advance and overlap, using the whole platen surface.
