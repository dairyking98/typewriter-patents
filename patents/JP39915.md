## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies; Western type typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** The object is to make the height of such a machine especially small when it is not in use, so that it fits in a fairly flat case, and to provide means by which the machine can be folded into its case, or brought out of it, very quickly and simply.

**How it works (reading):** The machine frame hangs from the case on parallelogram links, one extended to a crossbar that holds the typebars upright, so pushing the crossbar forward folds the machine and lays the typebars flat over the key levers.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A portable typebar machine with upright typebars is too tall to fit a flat carrying case.

**Solution:** The machine frame hangs from the case on parallelogram links, one extended to a crossbar that holds the typebars upright, so pushing the crossbar forward folds the machine and lays the typebars flat over the key levers.

**How it works:** The frame carrying keys, typebars and platen is joined to each side of the case by two links forming a parallelogram. One link on each side extends past its frame pivot to an arm carrying a curved crossbar that the typebar ends touch when upright. Pushing the arms forward and down lowers the frame onto the case bottom and lays the typebars down above the key levers, with the key levers unchanged relative to the frame. Pulling the arms up raises the frame, lifts the typebars upright for typing, and the pivots pass over centre so the machine does not tip forward. A stop limits backward movement, and two hinged lid pieces close the case.
