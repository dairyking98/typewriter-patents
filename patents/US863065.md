## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to book typewriters adapted to write upon thepage'of any ordinary bound book when'spread t out flat, or to write upon sheets of paper, envelops, cards, and the like.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage is balanced so that it can be raised from or lowered to the writing position of a book typewriter without shock.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In book typewriters the carriage is jarred and other parts damaged when the operator turns the writing mechanism up and down, because the carriage is not balanced.

**Solution:** The carriage is balanced so that it can be raised from or lowered to the writing position of a book typewriter without shock.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)
