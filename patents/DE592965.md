## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper cutting and stacking machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with visual indication, e.g. counter drum

**How it works (reading):** The stacking drive is taken from the same continuously running motor that drives the cutting shafts, switched on through a coupling and a stop that keeps narrow sheets from releasing the cross bar too early.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The stacking device on a receipt cutting machine is driven by hand, which tires the operator and slows the work.

**Solution:** The stacking drive is taken from the same continuously running motor that drives the cutting shafts, switched on through a coupling and a stop that keeps narrow sheets from releasing the cross bar too early.
