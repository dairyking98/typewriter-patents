## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** The Object of the invention is to provide an attachment of the class described, which may be easily attached to and detached from a typewriter carriage, and which will support a plurality of superposed strips of paper,.

**How it works (reading):** A removable attachment with spaced frames and rollers feeds the strips, separates the fair copy from the carbon, and rolls the copy up.
