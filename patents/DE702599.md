## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key lever drives reach the cam or transmission element only through extra intermediate links, adding complexity and interference between rows.

**Solution:** The key lever, forming the coupler of a four-bar parallelogram, acts directly on the cam-guided transmission element, so all cam levers sit on one axis.

**How it works:** Each key lever is guided as a parallelogram by two rocker arms and has a coupling arm whose pin works a cam, rolling arm or gear segment on an angle lever on a shared axis. The angle lever connects by a tie rod to the type lever, giving a transmission ratio that accelerates the type. All keys have equal depth and equal angle lever movement; the outer levers get longer arms for greater type travel. Tie rod joints for all rows can sit side by side, easing cleaning and repair.
