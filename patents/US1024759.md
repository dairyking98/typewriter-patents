## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type writing machines and more particularly to the paper fingers and associated paper feed devices of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** The paper finger is made so it can be held in its released position and returned to the paper more conveniently.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper fingers slide off the paper for corrections and then catch on the sheet edge when restored, often needing two hands.

**Solution:** The paper finger is made so it can be held in its released position and returned to the paper more conveniently.
