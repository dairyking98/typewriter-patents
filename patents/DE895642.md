## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards characterised by means for holding or fastening typewriters or computer equipment; Special supports, platforms or trolleys for supporting machines on tables

**How it works (reading):** Rubber pegs set into holes in the table top carry the typewriter feet and are bolted to them, so vertical vibration is absorbed by the pegs instead of passing into the table.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter and table vibrate and transmit noise into the desk top.

**Solution:** Rubber pegs set into holes in the table top carry the typewriter feet and are bolted to them, so vertical vibration is absorbed by the pegs instead of passing into the table.
