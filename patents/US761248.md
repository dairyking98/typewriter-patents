## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in type-writing machines, and more particularly toA ribbon mechanism therefor, the object of the invention being to provide simple and efficient meansfor feeding and shifting the inking-ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** Ratchets and pawls driven by type-bar movement feed the inking ribbon; a ribbon vibrator raises the ribbon at impression and lowers it afterwards; a manual reversing arrangement and easy spool insertion are provided.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed and shifting were awkward, and the ribbon blocked the operator's view of the writing at the print point.

**Solution:** Ratchets and pawls driven by type-bar movement feed the inking ribbon; a ribbon vibrator raises the ribbon at impression and lowers it afterwards; a manual reversing arrangement and easy spool insertion are provided.
