## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Recessed alignment marks wear the die and are hard to precisely align when type bodies warp on hardening.

**Solution:** A die whose recessed base runs outward at an obtuse angle, with flanks at obtuse angles, produces end straightening surfaces on the type body.

**How it works:** The obtuse-angled flanks form the straightening faces at each type end, and their cutting edges trim the burr without quick wear.
