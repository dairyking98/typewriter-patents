## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters with two-stage (double) shift keep the shift keys down with pawls, but the pawls had to be released by hand.

**Solution:** The latching pawls are released automatically by pressing the held shift key down a little further.

**How it works:** A sheet-metal shift frame carrying the carriage rides on link arms and a spring-loaded shaft that tends to raise it. Two shift keys, one for capitals and one for double shift (figures), lift the frame via arms on the shaft; adjustable stop screws on each side set the shift height so the second or third type on each typebar strikes. A push button turns a pawl over a pin on the key lever to hold the key down. Pressing the held key further frees the pawl, which springs back to rest.
