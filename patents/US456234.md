## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Telegraph blanks must be fed to the typewriter platen one sheet at a time by hand.

**Solution:** A blank holder on the carriage with a geared needle picker that pulls one sheet at a time into the platen as the platen turns.

**How it works:** Blanks stand in a box-like holder on the carriage, resting on a spring-loaded paper support with flange and top abutments, with slotted adjustable stops for sheet length. A shaft with sharp needles is geared to the platen, so as the platen rotates the needles rotate through an opening in the holder front, pick the front sheet and carry it down to the platen and feed roll. The device is shown on a Caligraph.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** platen; feed roll; paper table; blank holder; paper support; spring; stop arm; picker needle; picker shaft; gear wheels; ratchet wheel; dog

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph: adds automatic single-sheet feeding
