## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Feed rollers need adjustable yielding pressure without the spring stress bending the light carrier frame.

**Solution:** One roller turns on a spring steel rod running through its hollow body, whose slotted end bearings are pressed toward the other roller by set screws.

**How it works:** A sheet-steel plate with pillow blocks carries a solid roller on journals; the second roller is a hollow cylinder with small apertures in its heads, loose on a spring rod. The rod ends sit in slotted openings in the pillow blocks and are pushed toward the first roller by set screws, pressing the rollers together. All spring force is taken by the pillow blocks, so the rest of the frame can be slender and the carrier moves easily.
