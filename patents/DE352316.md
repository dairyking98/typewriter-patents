## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculator type bars must be preset from the keys for an addend print and from the counter for a total print, with a simple lever structure.

**Solution:** Two presettable stops are linked so that when one is active the other is switched off; the stroke of the type-bar lever is limited by whichever stop is in the working position, so the bar prints the keyed number or the counter value.
