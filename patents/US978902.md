## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The long rod carrying paper pressure rolls springs and flexes on wide carriages, losing roll pressure.

**Solution:** A hinged support arm with an open bearing engages the roll rod midway to stiffen it and folds away when unneeded.

**How it works:** The pressure-roll rod is mounted in grooved journals on the platen frame and spring-turned so rolls press on the platen. An arm hinged to a bracket on the carriage has a slotted bearing that fits over the rod midway along its length. A spring acting on a knuckle holds the arm in working or disused position. The open slot lets the arm be lifted off so rolls can be slid along the rod. The bearing is long enough to stay engaged when the platen shifts.
