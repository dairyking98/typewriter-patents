## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to mechanism for feeding ribbons such as are used in various machines, for example, those where lprinting is etiectedthrough an inked ribbon.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed of a rotary printing machine must reverse itself at the end of the ribbon.

**Solution:** Ratchet-driving pawls on a plate carried by the drum, held by a detent roller, swap from one spool's ratchet to the other automatically.

**How it works:** A rotary machine has a drum with rails forming undercut channels for rows of grooved type, a crank, and a platen geared to the drum. Two ribbon spools sit in a recess in the drum. A stationary eccentric strap reciprocates a bar; pawls on a plate engage either spool ratchet, and a detent roller holds the plate in either extreme position until the ribbon end throws it over.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** drum; type rail; crank; platen; ribbon spool; ratchet wheel; eccentric strap; reciprocating bar; detent roller; pawl plate
