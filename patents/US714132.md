## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to attachments for type-writing machines,and has reference more particularly to certainimprovements in that class of attachments which are designed to provide an auxiliary ink ribbon or strip intended to substitute the use of the ordinary carbon-sheets in making additional or multiple copies of the record produced by the machine.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary inked ribbon strip fitted to the machine in place of carbon sheets to make additional copies.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)
