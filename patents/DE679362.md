## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Segment-shaped grinding tools revolving in a grinding drum (mill) must be adjusted from outside while running to set their inclination to the drum.

**Solution:** Mount each tool at its front end on one hand-set radial holder and at its rear end on two spoke-like holders adjustable independently from outside through axially sliding shafts, ball-ring articulated to the tool.

**How it works:** An outer hollow drum with helical grooves turns around an inner rotating body carrying the segment tools, which have opposite-handed grooves that convey and tumble the material. The front support axis is set by a two-part threaded linkage and then left. The rear axes sit in radially sliding spoke supports engaged by angle levers, whose inner ends lie in annular grooves of adjusting discs on shafts that slide axially inside the hollow shafts. Handwheel union nuts at the outer ends move the shafts, with lock nuts to hold the setting, so each end of the tool is tilted independently during operation.

**Parts:** grinding drum; segment grinding tool; spoke-like holder; angle lever; adjusting disc; hollow shaft; union nut; handwheel; lock nut; ball ring

**Improves on:**

- known adjustable segment grinding tool mountings: rear ends adjustable independently from outside during running
