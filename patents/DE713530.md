> Description Translated from German Paper carriage switching and winding device on typewriters The invention relates to a device with the aid of which the carriage of a typewriter is opened or automatically returned to its starting position. Its purpose is to be able to work more comfortably with the typewriter by pressing a key when the line is over, whereby the carriage then quickly returns to its starting position. This switching and pulling up of the paper slide takes place in that the drive gear of the switching device engages a gear segment attached as a planet gear around the drive gear, which is under tension from a tensioned spring, which is attached on the one hand to the machine frame and on the other hand to the ring of the gear segment. The z5 gear wheel segment can be kept in an unstable state if its center point is on the line of action of force (longitudinal axis) of the edge, and. be moved in one direction or the other when its center is on one or the other point. Side of the line of action of the spring lies, the carriage being automatically returned to its initial position or being advanced by one step.
