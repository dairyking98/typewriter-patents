## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letter spacing is fixed, and changing it normally means changing the carriage feed devices.

**Solution:** Regulatable carriage feed that sets one of several letter spacings without altering the escapement itself.

**Features:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
