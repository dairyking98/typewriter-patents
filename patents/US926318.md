## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars carrying more than two or three types need large shifts, because the types need wide spacing to avoid touching the platen.

**Solution:** A compound bar of two hinged members whose types interleave in each other's gaps, so many types fit with only a small shift.

**How it works:** Member 1 is pivoted to the lever mechanism and shifted lengthwise; member 2 is pivoted to member 1. The types of one member fit in the gaps between the other's, each member half the type width thick, giving a near continuous column. Teeth and a fixed pawl withdraw the unwanted member's types into the gaps so the selected type alone reaches the platen. Shown for adding machines and in a diagram for typewriters.
