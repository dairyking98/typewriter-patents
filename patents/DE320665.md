## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rigid metal platen core needed an elastic rubber cover, so the rubber had to be thick and was wearing fast.

**Solution:** A platen core of plywood, several veneers wound with alternating grain, gives even elasticity in all radial directions so only a thin rubber layer is needed.
