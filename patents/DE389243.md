## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement knife striking the teeth of a split ratchet wheel makes noise.

**Solution:** Beveled facing edges on the two ratchet halves and a beveled knife tip let the knife slide from one half to the other, nearly silently.

**How it works:** A rack drives a gear on a shaft; a split ratchet of two halves sits on the shaft, joined by claws with play and a spring between them. A rocker-mounted switch blade with a beveled tip rides on the teeth. When the type lever moves out, the blade slides onto a tooth of the other half and the spring turns the first half; on return it slides to the next tooth, giving one step per stroke. Carriage advance is by a spring box via the rack.
