## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper carriage switching device for typewriters With the paper carriage switching device device for typewriters according to patent 469 382 was the return of the the paper carriage carrying the platen by hand. However, it is desirable for the paper carriage to return automatically to achieve, for example, when a full line is written, because stretching the hand for returning the slide is often uncomfortable. According to the invention, this is achieved in a simple manner by that the paper carriage is set under the action of a spring or a counterweight which has the tendency to advance to the left when writing Always pulling the car to the right. With this pulling action to the right, however the drive drum is rotated in the opposite direction of rotation as when shifting, which would result in an inaccuracy for later switching. In order to avoid this inconvenience, locking fingers are provided which lie in the grooves of the drive drum in a similar way to the switch claws and rotating the drum in a direction opposite to the switching direction prevent immediately and in the most sensitive way.
