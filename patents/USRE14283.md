## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a web-or tally strip supporting and feeding mechanism adapted for use with typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A tally strip from a supply roll runs over the platen to a winding spindle driven through a slip clutch from the line-space lever, and advances one line when a sheet is replaced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operator had to line-space by hand after each work sheet was changed, and tally strip needed a clean blank line.

**Solution:** A tally strip from a supply roll runs over the platen to a winding spindle driven through a slip clutch from the line-space lever, and advances one line when a sheet is replaced.
