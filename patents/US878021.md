## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to check-punching machines, or what is generally known as check protectors, and its general object is to provide a new and improved checkprotector by means of which the characters indicating the amount of the check may be so impressed upon the check as to prevent any successful attempt to alter or raise the check.

**How it works (reading):** A manually operated lever breaks or punctures the check surface over an area larger than the figures, and the amount is then printed on the broken area so that any rubbing or erasure destroys the surface and shows the attempt.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The amount of a check can be altered or raised by erasure, so the characters must be impressed in a way that prevents a successful alteration.

**Solution:** A manually operated lever breaks or punctures the check surface over an area larger than the figures, and the amount is then printed on the broken area so that any rubbing or erasure destroys the surface and shows the attempt.
