## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rigid type-bar pivot bearing is damaged or deflected by dirt, by colliding type bars, or by rough handling during cleaning, and is hard to assemble.

**Solution:** A type-bar hanger pivot in which spring means press ball bearings together but let them yield when obstructed, so the bearing is self-adjusting and easy to assemble.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
