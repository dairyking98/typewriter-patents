## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card counting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Braking or stopping movement

**How it works (reading):** An identification hole (the X-point overhole) is sensed and sets a device that selects which card-field perforations are transferred to the common adders, with switching inside adder pairs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card-controlled business machines cannot route amounts from different card fields on different cards into a common adding unit, since the card types cannot be told apart.

**Solution:** An identification hole (the X-point overhole) is sensed and sets a device that selects which card-field perforations are transferred to the common adders, with switching inside adder pairs.

**How it works:** A sensing element reads the identification hole, and the setting selects which sensed hole fields feed the same adder.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** punch cards; identification overhole; sensing elements; adding units; selecting device
