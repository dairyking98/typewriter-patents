## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (wax stencil for stencil printers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stencil units

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a wax template for stencil printers of the known type that have a solid base and parting lines along the top and bottom edges.

**How it works (reading):** The parting lines run through the base but not the wax layer, so one end can be separated for moistening; the protruding ends carry openings for hanging the stencil on the duplicator.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A wax stencil fixed at one end cannot be used both on the typewriter, where the sheet must unfold at the other end, and on the duplicator.

**Solution:** The parting lines run through the base but not the wax layer, so one end can be separated for moistening; the protruding ends carry openings for hanging the stencil on the duplicator.

> Description Translated from German Wax stencil for stencil printer with a solid base and parting lines along the top and bottom edge. The invention relates to a wax template for stencil printers of the known type that have a solid base and parting lines along the top and bottom edges. With the previous wax stencils, one of the fastening edges is used before using the stencil on the typewriter. This means that the stencil can only be attached to the printing press on one side Limited to the end while for typewriter work the sheets at the the other, separated end can easily unfold.
