## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Wax stencil for stencil printer with a solid base and parting lines along the top and bottom edge. The invention relates to a wax template for stencil printers of the known type that have a solid base and parting lines along the top and bottom edges. With the previous wax stencils, one of the fastening edges is used before using the stencil on the typewriter. This means that the stencil can only be attached to the printing press on one side Limited to the end while for typewriter work the sheets at the the other, separated end can easily unfold. Around the fastening edges both while working on the typewriter and on the printing press, are according to the invention the parting lines on both sides only through the base, but not passed through the wax template, so that the base between the two parting lines can be separated and the edges for the mutual attachment of the template remain on the printing press.
