## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On book typewriters the copy is hard to hold in view and the place where copying stopped is lost.

**Solution:** A copy holder with a copy roller on the machine frame holds the copy in view and marks the point the copying has reached.

**How it works:** A horizontal frame carries a copy roller with a tension-adjusting device; a guide bar passes the copy and the roller shows progress.
