## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (card controlled business machine printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Methods or arrangements for effecting co-operative working between equipments covered by two or more of main groups G06K1/00 - G06K15/00, e.g. automatic card files incorporating conveying and reading operations

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable stop in the step-by-step paper circuit is set to the desired form length, and the paper returns to a fixed stop at the start of each card group.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Form length on tabulator paper feed can only be changed by substituting transmission gears.

**Solution:** An adjustable stop in the step-by-step paper circuit is set to the desired form length, and the paper returns to a fixed stop at the start of each card group.
