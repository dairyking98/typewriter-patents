## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** Feeler arms resting on the ribbon rolls trip an angle lever through a spring-loaded actuator on the universal rail frame, shifting the drive shaft instantly.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing by worm threads is not instantaneous, so the ribbon is perforated; strong spring reversing via the universal rail distorts the ribbon or switches prematurely.

**Solution:** Feeler arms resting on the ribbon rolls trip an angle lever through a spring-loaded actuator on the universal rail frame, shifting the drive shaft instantly.

**How it works:** Spring-pressed feeler arms rest on the wound ribbon of each spool. When one spool has run down to a set diameter, a stop lever on the arm's axis pushes an actuator on the universal rail frame. Its recess lets an angle lever deflect when the frame moves, and the angle lever's other leg shifts the displaceable drive shaft sideways, engaging the other spool at once.
