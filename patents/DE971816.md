## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier on the paper apron concentric with the roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide on office machines, in particular typewriters, with a paper guide tray and paper guide rollers, through which the writing sheets under the action of at one end on the carriage frame held, influenceable via a release device leaf springs to the platen be pressed.

**How it works (reading):** The paper guide tray carries the guide rollers and is held by leaf springs gripping its one end; it is opened and closed as one unit, with adjustable screws setting each spring's pressure on the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper guide assembly on a typewriter carriage used many separate support arms and pivots, which made manufacture and assembly costly.

**Solution:** The paper guide tray carries the guide rollers and is held by leaf springs gripping its one end; it is opened and closed as one unit, with adjustable screws setting each spring's pressure on the platen.

In a known device, the paper guide plate and The paper guide rollers are taken up by special support arms that are placed around pins in further holding arms are mounted, which in turn can be pivoted about pins on the carriage frame are. The mentioned support arms are fastened to the carriage frame by leaf springs by means of adjustable screws acting on the support arms against the platen pressed. Since this arrangement has a plurality of individual parts, the Manufacturing and assembly costs increased significantly. According to the present invention, these disadvantages are eliminated by that the paper guide tray on which the paper guide rollers in a known manner are stored, solely by the other end under the paper guide tray gripping leaf springs and when opening and closing the paper guide to be led.
