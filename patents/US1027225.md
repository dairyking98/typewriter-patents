## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabulating mechanism for typewriting and other machines, and particularly to column stops thereof.

**How it works (reading):** A series of column stops, each independently adjustable along the rack and movable transverse to its rod, is arrested by denomination stops and held in or out of position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The machine needs a simple and inexpensive way to be set for different kinds of tabulating work.

**Solution:** A series of column stops, each independently adjustable along the rack and movable transverse to its rod, is arrested by denomination stops and held in or out of position.
