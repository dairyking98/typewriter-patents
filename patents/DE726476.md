## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A platen with grooved wooden core or pipe cover makes uneven surface and noise.

**Solution:** The platen has a hollow tubular core with a rubber covering, supported evenly, which reduces noise and is cheap to make.

**How it works:** Hollow tubular core with rubber cover gives even surface support and less noise.
