## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A visible typebar machine needs a typebar layout and actuation that are simple and give short movement to the most-used letters.

**Solution:** Horizontal-pivoted typebars in two banks of increasing length from the centre, with the commonest letters on the shortest bars.

**How it works:** Key levers move vertical actuating elements pivoted on anchor elements fixed to stepped anchor plates. Each element's pin goes through a link that pulls a typebar around a vertical pivot to the print point through about a quarter turn. The bars sit in banks each side of the centre with their blocks in horizontal alignment, and the innermost bars are shortest, swinging through smaller arcs. Frequent letters go on inner bars for lighter, shorter movement. A shift frame lifts the bars for case, and the pins are long enough to stay engaged with the links.
