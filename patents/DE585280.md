## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar and rocker-arm type carrier drives make impact noise at the platen and at the rest stop.

**Solution:** Drive the type carrier with two toggle-lever pairs that reach their straight positions at print and at rest, absorbing the shock, with a guide piece sliding on a straight rod.

**How it works:** The key lever pulls a tie rod and steering lever that bends a first toggle pair, drawing the type carrier toward the platen along its guide. A second toggle pair at the front straightens as the carrier advances, so the type lands without a hard stop. A return spring, ineffective in the rest position because it lines up with the lever, brings the carrier back, where the rear pair straightens and acts as the rest stop. The bearings are adjustable for wear and mounted on noise-damping material.
