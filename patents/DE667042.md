## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Chromium-nickel resistance alloys wear out and are hard to work into wire.

**Solution:** Adds about 0.1 to 0.25 percent calcium plus 0.1 to 2 percent zirconium to a Ni-Cr alloy to lengthen resistor life and ease processing; not a machine.
