## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Wear of the Remington yoke-blocks and shift rail spoils the alignment of upper-case printing.

**Solution:** Pivoted gravity hooks, shaped to the round rail and relieved at the top inner side, ride on the shift rail and drop to follow wear automatically.

**How it works:** Two arms fixed to the platen-carrier cross rod extend forward, each pivoting a bifurcated hook whose inner underside is a circular segment matching the round shift rail. The rail, rocking on a bell crank by a connecting rod from the shift key, pushes the hooks rearward to upper case and pulls them forward to lower case under spring return. Because the hook is pivoted it rises and falls with the rail's small rocking motion; a cut-away groove on its upper inner side lets it drop and keep a snug fit as the sides wear. A loop bar limits the hook's drop, and the arms guide it against lateral movement. A wedge piece on each arm draws the carrier back to lower case after the carriage is lifted for inspection.
