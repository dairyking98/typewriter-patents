The invention relates to such a design of the bolts that the connection it creates is a particularly sustainable one and therefore neither due to the latching and releasing of the hook latches and vibrations when they snap back of the car is at risk. The anchoring of the bolts in the machine frame is like this carried out that any loosening of the same can be eliminated immediately. For this the holes for the bolts in the carriage bed are cylindrical, but the bolts themselves somewhat tapered and provided with tightening screws so that they can be easily readjusted can be.
