## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The releasable connection of the paper carriage bed to the machine frame can loosen through hook-bolt latching and vibration from the carriage returning.

**Solution:** Cylindrical holes in the bed take tapered bolts with tightening screws, so the bolts can be readjusted to remove any play; spring-loaded hook bolts lock them, and lifting them off releases the bed and carriage.
