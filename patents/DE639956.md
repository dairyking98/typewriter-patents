## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**How it works (reading):** Thin rubber insert with corrugations on both sides, offset so the sheet keeps even thickness, placed between platen and paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Non-erasable writing needs a corrugated insert, but one-sided corrugated rubber sheets are stiff and break easily.

**Solution:** Thin rubber insert with corrugations on both sides, offset so the sheet keeps even thickness, placed between platen and paper.
