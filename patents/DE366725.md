## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with a removable paper trolley and aims to simplify the connection between the holder of the paper frame and the machine frame in such Machines in which the wagon keeper is firmly seated on the machine frame.

**How it works (reading):** The holder is hinged at its rear end by a claw-and-tenon joint on an axis parallel to the paper roller and rests at its front on pins, so the front can be swung up and the holder released from the frame without fixings.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The paper carriage holder is firmly fixed to the machine frame, so the carriage cannot be detached easily.

**Solution:** The holder is hinged at its rear end by a claw-and-tenon joint on an axis parallel to the paper roller and rests at its front on pins, so the front can be swung up and the holder released from the frame without fixings.
