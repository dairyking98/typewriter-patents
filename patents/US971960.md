## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Resetting mechanism that moves all column stops to normal inoperative position in one simple, efficient action.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column stops in operative position must be cleared quickly when a different set is needed.

**Solution:** Resetting mechanism that moves all column stops to normal inoperative position in one simple, efficient action.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
