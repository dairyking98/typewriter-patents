## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type strike mid-way across the ribbon every time, so the ribbon edges are left unused and tend to curl or foul.

**Solution:** Ribbon supports automatically move the ribbon unequal distances to and from the printing line after each operation, so the type strike across the whole ribbon width over time.

**How it works:** After each operation the ribbon supports move the ribbon to and from the printing line by varying distances. Over successive strokes the type hit different parts of the ribbon, using its full width. The arrangement also prevents the ribbon edges from curling or fouling, which happens when the type strike only a narrow strip in the middle.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon supports; spools; printing line; ribbon control devices

**Improves on:**

- ribbon struck at one narrow mid-width strip: unequal ribbon shifts spread strikes across full width
