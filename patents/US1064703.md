## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting and recording machines, and more particularly to the mechanism for controlling the ribbon from which the records are produced.
