## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**How it works (reading):** Ball races take the escape wheel shaft, and drive pawls engage the escape wheel from a toothed wheel meshed with the carriage rack, with an adjustable back check.
