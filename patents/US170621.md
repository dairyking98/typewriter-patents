## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Stenographic transcription is needed before typewritten text can be set into type, and typewriters are not usable for shorthand.

**Solution:** Two keyboards with letters grouped on each side, including capital and lower-case letters, are used so that words are written without spacing and the initial letter of each word is distinct.
