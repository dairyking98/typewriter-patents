## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The coin-operated self-collecting lock on typewriters (per patent 575 630) could be misused by bypassing or switching off the lock.

**Solution:** The locking member is spring-loaded on a pivoting axis linked to the drive lever; an arm holds the counter drive blocked while the control button is pressed and the coin has not yet dropped, and a locking slide engages on slight arm movement.

**How it works:** A shaft-mounted set of arms and levers jointly locks the drive member, type lock and counter so that the lock cannot be defeated by partial operation.
