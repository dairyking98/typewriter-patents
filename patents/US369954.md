**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an electrical typewriter, and has for its object, first, to provide a simple, durable, and operative mechanical devicefor automatically reversing or returning the traveling carriage to the beginning of the next succeeding line after the desired space is automatically made between the completed line and the next to be printed;

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple device to return the traveling carriage automatically to the start of the next line after line spacing, and to line-space automatically at end of line, for electrical typewriters.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded reversing roller on the carriage cross-bar meets an adjustable plate at end of line to trigger line spacing and send the carriage back, driven by a weight and pulley, with a pawl and ratchet on the paper roll.

**How it works:** The carriage steps along guide-rests after each imprint under an electromagnetic letter-spacing motor with pivoted armatures. At the end of the line a roller on the front cross-bar meets an adjustable roller plate in a V-shaped recess of the guide-rest, which actuates a pawl and ratchet on the paper roll end to turn it a line, and releases the carriage to be returned by a weight on a pulley. Plates at either end allow travel left to right or right to left.
