## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Front-strike typebars swinging up and backward must overcome their own weight and a return spring, making the touch heavy and slow.

**Solution:** A counterweight on an extension on the far side of the typebar pivot balances the bar's weight so it lifts toward the printing point easily.

**How it works:** The thin sheet-steel typebar pivots in a crown bearing with a short operating arm linked to the key mechanism and a rear extension carrying a counterweight. The weight offsets the bar's gravity so key force only has to work against the return spring. A spring between the bar and platen intercepts the bar near its end; momentum carries the type through it to print, then the spring throws the bar back.
