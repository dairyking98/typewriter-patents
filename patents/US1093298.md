## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Handling copy materials differing in width

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for feeding webs to the feeding-rolls of typewriters and other printing-machines, etc.

**How it works (reading):** Paper rolls are mounted on a swinging frame pivoted near the rolls, so the frame moves with the carriage and the web runs over an inclined guide that takes up slack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Heavy paper rolls add inertia that slows the carriage on each letter feed.

**Solution:** Paper rolls are mounted on a swinging frame pivoted near the rolls, so the frame moves with the carriage and the web runs over an inclined guide that takes up slack.
