**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Letter-cards or letter-sheets, i.e. cards or sheets each of which is to be folded with the message inside and to serve as its own envelope for mailing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter postcard, which consists of a form twice the size of a postcard, its sub-parts are glued together after labeling and when labeling for glass the entire width is used.

**How it works (reading):** A form twice the size of a postcard with an address flap is used, so the text gets the full width of the postcard; the parts are glued and can be separated later.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter writing on a narrow postcard is cumbersome; a letter sheet is wasteful and costs postage.

**Solution:** A form twice the size of a postcard with an address flap is used, so the text gets the full width of the postcard; the parts are glued and can be separated later.

**How it works:** Double-size postcard with full-width text area; parts glue together and separate after writing.
