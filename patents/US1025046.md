## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to carriage feeding mechanism for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** The back spacing mechanism, or a special key with connections, can lock the carriage against movement at will.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage cannot be held fixed while re-printing or substituting a character, so vertical repeat printing is not possible.

**Solution:** The back spacing mechanism, or a special key with connections, can lock the carriage against movement at will.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
