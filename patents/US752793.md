## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines, and has for its object to devise simple, durable, and efiicientmechanism which, while adapted to move the paperroller carriage with a step-by-step movement as the type key-levers are operated, is capable of permitting 'the paper-carriage to be pushed rapidly by hand in both directions.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A carriage feed pawl that steps the paper carriage with each key must also swing clear so the carriage can be pushed quickly by hand in both directions.

**Solution:** A feed pawl that can both rock and slide along its pivot, with a fixed mask plate over the rack teeth whose gap and inclined end cam the pawl nose sideways and hold it clear whenever it is on its backstroke.

**How it works:** The carriage carries a rack bar with ratchet teeth, one straight side and one inclined side, masked by a fixed plate with a gap that has an inclined end. A pawl on a fixed pivot can rock and also slide along the pivot, with a spring pressing it against the mask plate and another spring holding it in rest position. Pressing any type key lowers a foot on a pivoted frame onto the pawl's lower arm, rocking it so the nose rides up a tooth incline and slides sideways to the gap incline. On key release the springs rock the pawl back and slide it into the gap so it catches the next tooth and feeds the carriage one step. With the nose on the plate incline the carriage moves freely by hand either way, and a small spring tooth stops accidental backward slip.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** rack bar; ratchet tooth; mask plate; gap; incline; feed pawl; pawl nose; pivot; pawl spring; return spring; key-lever; pivoted frame; foot; anti-reverse tooth; paper-carriage; paper cylinder

**Improves on:**

- Pawl-and-rack carriage feeds that must be released before pushing the carriage by hand: the pawl clears itself automatically on the backstroke
