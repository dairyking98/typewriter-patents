## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Preventing rebound or clash of levers or type members

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to means for returning the printing instrumentalities to normal position.

**How it works (reading):** Means are provided by which the sticking type bars and their actuating means are readily restored to normal position, rather than by hand.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars stick, through collision with each other, sticking in the guide, or snug bearings, so they fail to return to normal and the operator must free them by hand.

**Solution:** Means are provided by which the sticking type bars and their actuating means are readily restored to normal position, rather than by hand.
