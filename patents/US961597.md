## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type arms of the older type-writing machine are not rigid enough and their pivots need better formation.

**Solution:** Each type arm is a triangle pivoted at its base on conical pins that sit in a saddle, carrying a type block at the apex that prints lower case, capitals or figures chosen by platen shift.
