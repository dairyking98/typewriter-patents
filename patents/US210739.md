## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing type style or renewing worn type, with more accurate, uniform printing and capitals and lower case, and manifolding.

**Solution:** An oscillating type carrier holds two rows of detachable vertical types that are struck by a hammer, positioned by an index and lever, with a belt that spaces the paper carriage unequal distances.

**How it works:** A segment piece pivoted on a stud has two rows of vertical slots in which the types slide freely, held by shoulders. An arm with a lever passes through the stud to a stem which drives the type hammer and carriage; an index and lever turn the segment to choose the type. The hammer strikes the lower end of the chosen type against a ribbon and the roller, which sits over one row. The roller frame slides on two rods, and a hinged part can be turned up to inspect writing. Three rollers carry a belt linked to a ratchet, pawl and clamp lever so the carriage moves by varying distances to match letter widths.
