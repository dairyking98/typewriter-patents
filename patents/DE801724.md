## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mounting or connecting key buttons on or to key levers

**How it works (reading):** Mount the key cap on a fork pivoted on the key lever so it tilts to match the finger, limited by lugs and cushioned by a spring.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Rigidly fixed keys tilt awkwardly under the finger when the key lever swings; parallelogram or sleeve-guided keys are bulky.

**Solution:** Mount the key cap on a fork pivoted on the key lever so it tilts to match the finger, limited by lugs and cushioned by a spring.

**How it works:** The key is carried by a fork that pivots on an axis on the key lever, which itself pivots on its own axis. Two lugs limit the fork's swing. A spring sits in a cutout of the key lever on guide pins, one end on a pin on the lever and the other engaged by two pins on the fork, giving cushioning.
