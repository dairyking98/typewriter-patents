## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In power-driven typewriters with a common control bar, the type levers either strike slowly or unevenly because spring or electromagnet driving force varies with friction, and returning levers bounce.

**Solution:** The control bar is rigidly coupled to the drive shaft so no spring is needed for the print stroke, with strong return springs and an anti-rebound device to catch the type lever.

**How it works:** A power-driven shaft moves a common control bar through a rigid coupling, which carries the selected type lever to the platen with a uniform impression regardless of friction. Strong springs return the bar and lever quickly to rest. A device engages as the lever comes home to prevent it rebounding. Further figures cover the key arrangement, backspace and a downshift device.
