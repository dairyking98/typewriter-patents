## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Forms on a collective sheet can crease when auxiliary feed rollers rub the writing sheet against the platen.

**Solution:** A pressure roller pivots about the auxiliary feed roller axis and is pressed by toggle levers, leaving the lower paper guide open for loading.
