## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punching machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in devices for preventing the raising of drafts, checks, and other similar papers to a larger amount than that for which they are drawn;

**How it works (reading):** Operating levers and vertically moving rods with slotted arms drive crank-levers that move punch and die frames placed in less than a half circle, with springs that carry them past centre.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks could be raised in amount, and the stamp needed to punch them securely.

**Solution:** Operating levers and vertically moving rods with slotted arms drive crank-levers that move punch and die frames placed in less than a half circle, with springs that carry them past centre.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)
- **Uses:** [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md)
