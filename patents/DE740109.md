## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shift key buttons strain the little finger's joints.

**Solution:** Bend the forward extension of the shift key button up at about 45 degrees so the little finger rests in it up to the middle joint.

**How it works:** The key button's front surface is angled upward about 45 degrees from the key plane, reaching to the middle finger joint. The finger is pressed down fully extended using the middle hand bone. The trough edge may be flattened or omitted so the outer keys are not obstructed.

**Parts:** shift key button

**Numbers:** about 45 degrees angle
