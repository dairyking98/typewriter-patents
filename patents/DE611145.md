## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The aim of the invention is to equip normally designed typewriters, in particular accounting machines, with an auxiliary paper trolley of the type described above, without it being necessary to modify the paper trolley of the accounting machine and in particular its sheet holding devices.

**How it works (reading):** The auxiliary paper trolley is mounted above the paper roller on a support frame that folds down from the main trolley, so it fits normal machines without modifying the main paper carriage and sheet holders.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On an accounting typewriter, the auxiliary paper trolley placed below the main trolley needs a special design of the whole machine and its sheet holders.

**Solution:** The auxiliary paper trolley is mounted above the paper roller on a support frame that folds down from the main trolley, so it fits normal machines without modifying the main paper carriage and sheet holders.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
