## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriters cannot produce original text with justified (even-margin) lines for newspaper, book or music work suitable for flat printing.

**Solution:** A type-cylinder machine whose type are offset to one side of the cylinder's axial section, selected by a knob over a character board, with several space keys of adjustable stroke for justifying spacing.

**How it works:** A knob on a bridge over the keyboard board carries a rack that meshes a toothed wheel on the type cylinder shaft. Moving the knob sideways rotates the cylinder to a line of type, and moving it up or down slides the shaft axially, so placing the knob over a character on the board puts that character in the print position. One hand then presses the cylinder against the paper to print. The other hand presses one of four space keys, which turn an angle lever and feed the paper carriage by a clamping ring and feed bar; a ratchet lever limits each key's stroke, so spacing distance is chosen at will. Transfer paper is carried over a printing bed with press rolls and a tension roll.
