## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic writing of stock sentences or number groups on electromagnetic typewriters needed a hand-moved contact pin or a motor-driven contact disc.

**Solution:** A word key rotates a contact roller to the matching contact line, and a contact pin riding on the platen carriage slides along that line so the carriage's own movement steps through the letter circuits.

**How it works:** Each letter circuit has a shunt with a socket on an insulating board and a contact pin. The contact roller carries rows of insulated contact buttons, wired to the sockets in the desired letter and space sequence. Pressing a word key turns the roller so its row meets the pin and starts the pin on the first button. As each letter is typed the platen carriage shifts, dragging the pin along to the next button, so the sentence writes itself. At the end of the row a circuit releases the word key lock and the pin snaps back without touching the buttons.
