## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type-wheel positioning and its movement into contact with the platen should be one operation, and its longitudinal position should be easy to set for tabulating.

**Solution:** The lever that positions the type-wheel also moves it into contact with the platen, and a simple means sets the type-wheel's longitudinal position for tabulating.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
