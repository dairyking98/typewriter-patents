## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper-controlling devices ,of typewriting machines, and especially to mechanisms for feeding webs of paper and severing the webs into slips or sheets as the writing progresses.

**How it works (reading):** Cutting devices are linked to the web-feeding and line-spacing means so that ordinary line-by-line writing severs the web into slips automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Web paper is either removed before cutting into slips or torn into slips, leaving rough edges and needing operator attention.

**Solution:** Cutting devices are linked to the web-feeding and line-spacing means so that ordinary line-by-line writing severs the web into slips automatically.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
