## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Releasing the carriage from the step-by-step feed racks needed a separate manual action, and the carriage could run uncontrolled after release.

**Solution:** The release of the carriage from the spacing racks is connected to the spacing mechanism and operated automatically, with a stop that arrests the carriage after release.

**How it works:** A sliding carriage with platen runs on guide rods and is pulled by a spring through a link and vibrating bar. A pivoted rectangular frame, moved by the key levers, carries a retaining dog that engages feed racks on the carriage, giving step-by-step spacing and also driving the ribbon spools. Side levers pressed by the operator push the frame so the dog and racks disengage, and the carriage carries a stop or abutment that catches it at a set point after release. Several rack modifications are shown.
