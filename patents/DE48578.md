## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The blind need a writing machine that can produce both ordinary type and Braille.

**Solution:** A hand-positioned writing rod with ordinary type on two sides and Braille dots on two others, located by a lever tip dropped into holes in a character plate and pressed down to print.

**How it works:** A carriage with a writing rod is moved along a channel by a slide; a lever p has its tip placed over the hole in a fixed plate (in two rows) belonging to the wanted character, then pressing its knob swings the whole upper part about two pivot screws and prints that type through the paper onto a support bar. The rod is turned a quarter turn by a spring lever to reach the second row (digits) or the other case, and a spring detent holds it. For Braille the rod is turned to its dotted faces and the support bar reversed so its recessed side takes the dots. Paper is carried on rubber rollers with a wire paper holder, advanced by a notched hand wheel for the line space; an inking roller colours the type when the carriage moves.
