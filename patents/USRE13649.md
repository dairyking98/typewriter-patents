## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the invention is to providea construction of type-levers or type-bars and a manner of mounting the same in bearings whereby each type-bar will have an extended insuring, accuracy in printing action and stability in all positions, the bearings being so constructed that in spite jof their extended bearings the type-bars can be arranged at no more than the usual distance apart, all striking at a common center.

**How it works (reading):** Type-bars are mounted in extended bearings on a supporting plate with threaded adjusting apertures, so each bearing can be independently adjusted to take up play and set type alinement while keeping bars close together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar bearings lacked extended bearing surface and could not be adjusted independently to take up lost motion and correct alinement.

**Solution:** Type-bars are mounted in extended bearings on a supporting plate with threaded adjusting apertures, so each bearing can be independently adjusted to take up play and set type alinement while keeping bars close together.
