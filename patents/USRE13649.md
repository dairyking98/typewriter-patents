## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the invention is to providea construction of type-levers or type-bars and a manner of mounting the same in bearings whereby each type-bar will have an extended insuring, accuracy in printing action and stability in all positions, the bearings being so constructed that in spite jof their extended bearings the type-bars can be arranged at no more than the usual distance apart, all striking at a common center.
