## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters with typebars or reciprocating hammers are complicated; the inventor wants a rotating type cylinder with a flat paper platform that is raised against it and travels with it while printing.

**Solution:** A cylindrical type wheel rotates on a fixed axis while key-selected cams raise a flat paper platform against the type at the instant the chosen type is at the print point, the platform being carried along with the wheel by a cog rack during contact.

**How it works:** The cylindrical multi-row type wheel turns on a fixed shaft with a cog wheel that engages a rack on the paper platform whenever the platform is lifted. Each key throws its own lever against a rotating eccentric and cam on a shaft; the cam strikes the lever's toe at the moment the matching type is over the print point, raising a broad common lever that lifts the platform with a narrow raised platen strip under the one type row in use. While the platform is up, the cog wheel drags it with the type surface so the paper does not smear. On the way down, a ball against a friction wheel turns a screw that advances the platform one letter space; a rod lifts the ball to allow the screw to be reversed. A lever with a ratchet and inclined plane feeds the paper forward in rollers when the platform is drawn back. A space is made by a blank on the wheel or an independent screw key.
