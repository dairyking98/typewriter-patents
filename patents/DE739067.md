## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide device with two before Platen independently of one another arranged so that they can be pivoted forward.

**How it works (reading):** One pressure device becomes a straightening rail whose edge is offset by a set number of line spacings, so offset forms align without pins.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Forms with offset line-number scales need pin rails that tear thin forms and are slow to make.

**Solution:** One pressure device becomes a straightening rail whose edge is offset by a set number of line spacings, so offset forms align without pins.
