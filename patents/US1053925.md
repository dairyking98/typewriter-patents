## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** A rotary member driven by friction slip-drive from the platen indicates line by line the distance to the sheet bottom, with a visual signal, an audible signal, and a machine lock at the last line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot judge the distance to the bottom edge, and the sheet end is not visible.

**Solution:** A rotary member driven by friction slip-drive from the platen indicates line by line the distance to the sheet bottom, with a visual signal, an audible signal, and a machine lock at the last line.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
