## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Print-shop binding devices with a cord clamp are expensive, so the cord-binding angle has not gained general use.

**Solution:** A simple bent metal angle with slots along its legs lets a tape be threaded around a set and clamped by an eccentric bolt and pressure plate, which is cheaper to make.
