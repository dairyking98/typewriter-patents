## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulating mechanism therefor', and seeks to provide a combined eolumnating or column selecting and denominational stop mechanism whereby the carriage may be brought to any one of a number of columnar positions along the line of print and at the same time to any one of a number of adjacent letter space positions in the selected column.

**How it works (reading):** Combine columnar and denominational stops so one run of the carriage reaches the chosen column and an adjacent letter space.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Separate column-selecting and denominational stop mechanisms must be operated one after another to place the carriage.

**Solution:** Combine columnar and denominational stops so one run of the carriage reaches the chosen column and an adjacent letter space.

**How it works:** On a machine with a main and a light supplemental carriage joined by a releasable rack and dog, the tabulator key frees the supplemental carriage to run. Combined stops then arrest it at a chosen column and at one of several adjacent letter-space positions. A letter-space escapement of rack and rocker with dogs serves the main carriage.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** main carriage; supplemental carriage; platen; rack; dog; rocker; universal bar; letter space scale; tabulator key

**Improves on:**

- [US878789](../patents/US878789.md): adds combined column and denominational stop to the earlier two-carriage tabulator
