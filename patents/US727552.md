## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In keyboard-driven type-wheel machines the key-to-wheel connections, the wheel stopping and the noise were unsatisfactory, and the hammer and carriage feed needed a simpler drive.

**Solution:** Each key swings the type wheel through a leveraged diagonal bar to an angle that a sound-deadened gage arm fixes against the depressed key's stop pin, and the same key stroke cocks and then trips a spring-driven hammer.

**How it works:** The keys sit in right and left sets, each set resting on a forward-diverging diagonal bar on rocker arms. A key near the bar's pivot moves it little and a far key moves it a lot, so the wheel is rotated by an angle proportional to the key position, to the left or right of a central rest, through forked upright arms engaging pins on the wheel-driving arm. Each key also pushes a spring pin down in its slot; a long gage arm fixed on the wheel shaft, faced with rawhide, swings until it hits that pin and stops the wheel exactly. A spring under the diagonal bar lets the key keep going after the stop. The key stroke moves a universal bar that stretches a spring to a pivoted upright hammer held by a trigger, and late in the stroke the trigger releases it to snap the paper against the type. The universal bar also trips the carriage feed dogs, and the wheel can be lifted by two shift levers with different stops to bring a second or third row of types to the print point.

**Features:** [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [SEL:dummy-keyboard](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [IMP:spring-energy](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [IMP:platen-to-element](../features/imp-platen-to-element.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [ELEM:interchangeable](../features/elem-interchangeable.md)
