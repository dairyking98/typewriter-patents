## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing in an adding-machine printer should be simple and not easily disordered.

**Solution:** A shaft sliding lengthwise to mesh one of two bevel gears with the spool gears, shifted by weighted arms tripped by the ribbon.

**How it works:** A cross shaft carries two bevel gears; sliding it meshes one with the gear of either spool, so the ribbon winds in that direction. Weighted trip arms at the ends and a rocking plate with beveled flanges shift the shaft when the ribbon end reaches a spool.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; bevel gear; sliding shaft; weighted arm; rocking plate; trip finger
