## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In closely packed typebar baskets, bars rebound from the cushion on return and clash with the next bar at speed.

**Solution:** Rest the typebars on a flexible, inelastic lead ring or segment faced with soft tough material that absorbs the blow without rebound.

**How it works:** Radial typebars are hung on a ring and return by springs to rest against an annulus of lead faced or wrapped with belt-lacing-like inelastic material. The lead yields and jars neighbouring bars, absorbing the blow, and the facing prevents cutting and deadens noise. A stiff sheet-metal ring hung on rods supports the lead on inturned fingers, with a small gap allowing slight movement and friction damping.
