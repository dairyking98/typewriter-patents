## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink supply arrangements therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to means for producing duplicate ink copies on the typewriter, and is more particularly designed for usein simultaneously addressing a letter and its inclosing envelop.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary inking ribbon sits between duplicate sheets, with adjustable exposure of the ink surface and a shield to prevent smearing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicate ink copies are hard to make when addressing an envelope with the letter.

**Solution:** An auxiliary inking ribbon sits between duplicate sheets, with adjustable exposure of the ink surface and a shield to prevent smearing.
