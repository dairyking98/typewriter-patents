## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-locking on a Remington No. 2 stops the spring drum and could damage the carriage strap when the carriage is released.

**Solution:** Line lock acts on the spring drum, with automatic release of drum and lock when the carriage-release key is used.

**How it works:** The line-locking device is attached to the carriage mainspring drum, so locking the escapement also arrests the drum. When the release key is operated and the carriage runs swiftly past the locking point, an automatic release frees the drum and lock to prevent the strap bulging from the drum.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** spring drum; carriage; strap; feed dog; dog rocker; universal bar; line-lock bracket; release key

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 2: adds drum-based line lock with auto release
