## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in such carriage feed mechanisms in typewriters and the improvement consists in that the pawl normaly engaging the escapement wheel for stopping the carriage is adapted to be moved out of engagement with the escapement wheel for releasing the carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Keep the pinion always meshed with the rack and release the carriage by withdrawing the pawl from a separate escapement wheel.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage release by disengaging pinion from rack wears and breaks the rack and pinion teeth.

**Solution:** Keep the pinion always meshed with the rack and release the carriage by withdrawing the pawl from a separate escapement wheel.

**How it works:** The carriage rack meshes constantly with a pinion on the escapement wheel shaft. Two pawls normally engage the escapement wheel to stop the carriage. For release the pawl is moved out of the escapement wheel, so shocks do not fall on the rack teeth. The ratio of pinion to escape wheel can be chosen freely without raising machine height.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** carriage rack; pinion; escapement wheel; pawl

**Improves on:**

- direct rack-engaging pawl escapements: pawl works on an escapement wheel, not on the rack
