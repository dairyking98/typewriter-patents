## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is a tabulator device for typewriters and other office machines.

**How it works (reading):** A rack on the carriage is stopped by levers at tab positions that are set by a slide rail divided into parts, each one-pitch wide; releasing the tab key drives the lever to the nearest unblocked tooth, where it stops the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Set-tabulator riders are heavy, need fine tuning, and the extra load slows the carriage during writing.

**Solution:** A rack on the carriage is stopped by levers at tab positions that are set by a slide rail divided into parts, each one-pitch wide; releasing the tab key drives the lever to the nearest unblocked tooth, where it stops the carriage.
