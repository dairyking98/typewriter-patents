## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cleaning typebar type with ribbon in the way soils the operator's fingers, so machines go uncleaned.

**Solution:** A slotted plate hooks onto the shift rail and holds the ribbon back while keeping raised type suspended for cleaning.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 6, the plate rests on the ribbon guide with a forked end that catches the ribbon and holds it behind the guide. The other end hooks on the shift rail, which is thrown forward by the shift key. Raising a typebar to centre passes its type through an orifice in the plate, where the straight edge holds it by the shank under the head for cleaning. Only the shift key need be pressed and released.
