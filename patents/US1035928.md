## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A folding typewriter needs a back space key that keeps working when the carriage folds.

**Solution:** A key-operated backspacing mechanism with connecting links that drive a backspacing dog on the foldable carriage, with the pivot on the fold axis.
