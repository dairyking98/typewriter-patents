## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon with several differently characterised fields must be usable with a vibrator that produces parallel impression paths.

**Solution:** A switching member positions the ribbon vibrator so impressions fall in the chosen field of the ribbon.

**How it works:** On a Monarch visible typebar machine the vibrator moves to give several parallel paths along the ribbon, extending the inventor's earlier application. A switching member in the ribbon mechanism selects the field. Only the opening is available.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon vibrator; switching member; typebar

**Improves on:**

- inventor's application Serial No. 247,474: adds multi-field ribbon operation
