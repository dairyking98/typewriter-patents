## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to collapsible Typewriters with a part that can be locked in the toggle position, z.

**How it works (reading):** Lock release is made dependent on a device that folds the type levers back for transport, so the shift lock releases automatically before the box closes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Collapsible machine with switchable paper trolley could be damaged if the box closed while the trolley was locked.

**Solution:** Lock release is made dependent on a device that folds the type levers back for transport, so the shift lock releases automatically before the box closes.

**Features:** [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md)
