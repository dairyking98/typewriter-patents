## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing mechanisms have too many parts, are costly, and are noisy.

**Solution:** Line-spacing mechanism with a small number of simple parts, an actuator guided on the platen roller axle, and a platen release lever, made quiet and able to space the platen by different numbers of lines.
