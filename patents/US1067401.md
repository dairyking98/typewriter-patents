## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage released for tabulating runs freely under its spring and jumps forward too fast.

**Solution:** A pinion engages the rack and drives an air-check piston in a cylinder that retards the carriage to a controlled speed.
