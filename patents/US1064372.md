## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Folding portable machines get escapement dogs broken in transit and the carriage must be positioned by hand before folding.

**Solution:** Stop and lock for the carriage that also throws the escapement dogs clear of the rack and returns automatically.

**How it works:** When the carriage and platen are folded, a stop locks the carriage against the spring and moves the dogs out of the rack, holding them released. Moving the carriage a short distance restores the normal state.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** escapement dog; rack; carriage stop; locking member; carriage bed; case
