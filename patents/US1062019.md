## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen needs a simple, inexpensive releasable connection to the line-space wheel so it can be adjusted by a fraction of a full line space.

**Solution:** A loose line-space wheel carries a head with spring-loaded wedge-toothed dogs that engage a toothed wheel on the platen; a beveled disk throws the dogs out of contact so the platen can be adjusted relative to the line-space wheel.

**How it works:** Dogs slide in ways in the line-space head and have wedge-shaped teeth that engage a wheel tied to the platen. Pins on the dogs pass through the toothed wheel and springs connect opposite dogs to hold them engaged. A beveled rim on a disk throws the dogs out of engagement so the platen can be shifted by a fraction of a line. A cover plate encloses the dogs and toothed wheel.
