## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such table writing devices for typewriters in which the column stops have stop lugs increasing in length.

**How it works (reading):** The counter-stop that halts the carriage is positioned by a lever system in the frame, so the mainspring always acts on the same lever arm whichever column stop is in effect.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Column stops with lugs of increasing length make the counter-stop act on different lever arms, which is inconsistent.

**Solution:** The counter-stop that halts the carriage is positioned by a lever system in the frame, so the mainspring always acts on the same lever arm whichever column stop is in effect.
