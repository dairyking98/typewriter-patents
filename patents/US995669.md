## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With the ribbon removed or both spools empty, both reversing pins engage their worms and lock the Remington No. 10 ribbon drive.

**Solution:** Prevent both reversing devices acting at once when the ribbon is entirely removed.

**How it works:** A spring drum ratchet and bevel gears drive a transverse ribbon shaft that shifts endwise to mesh with either spool shaft. Each spool has a weighted arm held up by ribbon coils; when the spool empties the arm drops and pushes a pin into a worm on the drive shaft that shifts it to the other spool. With both spools empty both pins engage and the mechanism binds. The invention (the remaining text is not shown) is aimed at avoiding this lock-up.
