## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In typebar machines the bars are short and their pivots close together, so slight pivot wear or misadjustment throws the type out of alignment at the strike point.

**Solution:** Typebars with a long pivot bar set perpendicular or oblique to the stem, hung in individual adjustable clips so pivots can overlap in different planes.

**How it works:** Each typebar has a stem and a cross pivot bar, whose axis is either square to the stem (with the stem twisted to bring the type face upright) or oblique (stem straight). The pivot ends sit in a clip or hanger with a bow through which a wide-headed screw passes into the top plate, so loosening the screw lets the clip be adjusted. The long pivot axis makes the strike point less sensitive to wear and lets the bars be fitted to existing machines.
