## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letter spacing should suit each character's width, without a separate spacing control.

**Solution:** A revolving disc carries keys with knobs and radial spring arms with types; a key pushed into a tapering slot in a fixed ring brings its type to the print point and its depth sets the letter space.

**How it works:** A disc with keys and engraved knobs is fixed to a shaft, with a circular plate carrying radial springs ending in type sockets. The disc is turned and the chosen key is pressed into a tapering slot in the annular rim of the frame, which places that type over the paper. A spring lever with a projection above the type is pressed to force the type down onto the paper. Each key has a width and enters the slot to a depth proportional to its letter, and a bolt and spring-bar transfer this depth to a lever, bent lever and ratchet wheel that feeds the paper. The upper frame is hinged to the bed plate so it lifts for paper insertion; feed rollers move the paper. The text is truncated.
