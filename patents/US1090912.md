## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carbon paper must be shifted and held under tension for each copy, which the ordinary register does not provide.

**Solution:** A removable carbon roller holder with ratchet and pawl shifts the carbon and keeps it under tension.

**How it works:** Carbon roll sits in a casing; ratchet wheel and pawl advance the carbon roller.
