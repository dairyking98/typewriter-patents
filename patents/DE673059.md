## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register print hammer key)

**How it works (reading):** Special keys pressed while introducing amounts switch the print hammers to a single simple working stroke, also for the total drawing that follows, so the key does not have to be used for hammer control during balancing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Postings made without the account book produce a preliminary receipt, and a double hammer strike could let one of the two prints be intercepted and used for fraud.

**Solution:** Special keys pressed while introducing amounts switch the print hammers to a single simple working stroke, also for the total drawing that follows, so the key does not have to be used for hammer control during balancing.

**Features:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)
