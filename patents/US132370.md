## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Blind readers cannot tell letter shapes by touch, though they can feel the position of dots or raised parts, and the seeing must also read the same print.

**Solution:** Type has raised dots or prominent corners placed to be felt by the blind, combined with a printed letter outline so the seeing can read the same impression.
