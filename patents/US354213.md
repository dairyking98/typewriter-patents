## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A compact portable index typewriter is wanted that uses a pointer in a plate of holes to bring one of many types to a printing opening.

**Solution:** Spring-returned type plungers in tubes on a hand-turned drum are brought opposite a window by a handle with a pointer placed in the matching hole of a fixed letter plate, and a crank lifts the drum for the second row.

**How it works:** An outer covered cylinder carries a letter plate on top with a tapered hole for each character. Inside, a smaller drum revolves on a vertical axis and carries the types in tubes with helical or long springs and retaining pins in slots. The handle, jointed to a tube with forked arms engaging pins on the drum, turns the drum to bring a type opposite the opening when the pointer is dropped into the matching hole. Cranks with rollers lift the drum by exactly one row height to bring a lower row opposite the opening. The type is then pushed through the opening to the paper on the roller.
