## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Flat page-size platens or smaller flat platens having a greater size than line-size platens

**How it works (reading):** One lever releases both locks together, and a single spring motor with one pulling rope returns the carrier to its start position in both directions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A document carrier for flat-panel typewriters has separate locks for row and column, which are complicated, expensive and heavy to move.

**Solution:** One lever releases both locks together, and a single spring motor with one pulling rope returns the carrier to its start position in both directions.
