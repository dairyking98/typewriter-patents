## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed rolls on a platen must run truly and feed evenly under all usual conditions, and a thick card or pack of sheets must not throw the rolls off the other end of the platen.

**Solution:** Forward and rear feed rolls are mounted on one frame swiveled on the platen frame and pressed toward the platen by springs, with the frame yielding independently at each corner.

**How it works:** Both forward and rear rolls sit on a single frame swiveled on the platen frame along a line between them. Two springs bear on each end of the swivel frame, with adjustable tension. The frame can yield at any one corner while the other three stay in place, so a thick pack can pass near one end without lifting the rolls at the other end. The assembly is easily removed from the platen frame.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** forward feed roll; rear feed roll; swivel frame; springs; platen; platen frame; tie rod; deflector

**Improves on:**

- feed rolls that can lift off the platen with thick packs: independently yielding corners keep the rolls on the platen
