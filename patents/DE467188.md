## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that when a tab key is pressed a shaft of the back gear of the brake swung out around the axis of the rotating brake part is so that a gear on this shaft is in engagement with the rack of the paper carriage comes and another gear on this shaft sets the braking part in rotation.

**How it works (reading):** The brake is a retrofit unit on a swinging shaft: pressing the tab key swings a gear onto the carriage rack, and centrifugal brake plates in a housing damp the carriage; plates can be added or removed to tune the force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper carriage braking needed major drive changes to retrofit, and its force was hard to adjust.

**Solution:** The brake is a retrofit unit on a swinging shaft: pressing the tab key swings a gear onto the carriage rack, and centrifugal brake plates in a housing damp the carriage; plates can be added or removed to tune the force.
