## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by one-revolution or part-revolution clutches

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a single-turn clutch, such as that used in mechanically powered typewriters can be used.

**How it works (reading):** The coupling pawl carries a pivoting coupling tooth with a control tooth that enters the driver wheel first; a stop surface on the pawl and a spring-loaded push pin, with a ventilated bore, make the flanks mesh fully and prevent the driven shaft from twisting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In one-turn clutches the pawl tooth often contacts the driver wheel teeth only at its outer end, so it wears quickly and meshing is unreliable, especially when tooth tips meet.

**Solution:** The coupling pawl carries a pivoting coupling tooth with a control tooth that enters the driver wheel first; a stop surface on the pawl and a spring-loaded push pin, with a ventilated bore, make the flanks mesh fully and prevent the driven shaft from twisting.
