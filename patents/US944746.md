## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber paper-feed pressure rolls lose grip as sulfur blooms out, become slippery and soft, and are ruined by stencil paraffin.

**Solution:** Paper-feed pressure roll made from a non-rubber material that keeps the grip of new rubber without its defects and is lighter.
