## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Roll paper for telegraphic forms must be fed, cut and kept under tension.

**Solution:** Paper roll on a shaft beneath the platen carriage, guide rollers, a hinged table and a knife bar cutting the sheet to length.
