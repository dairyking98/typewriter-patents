## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Conditional arrangements for controlling subsequent operating functions, e.g. control arrangement triggered by a function key and depending on the condition of the register

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a special one Locking device in typewriters of the Elliott Fisher type, in which the Key lock depending on the position of the main drive wheel for the cross counters is carried out.

**How it works (reading):** A shift rod on the main drive wheel carriage couples at set slide positions to a key lock slide on the cross counter, so one drive wheel locks several counters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In Elliott-Fisher style calculators the key lock is driven from column counters, which are adjustable and vary, making grouped counters hard to lock.

**Solution:** A shift rod on the main drive wheel carriage couples at set slide positions to a key lock slide on the cross counter, so one drive wheel locks several counters.
