## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check strip feed for cash registers)

**How it works (reading):** The counter-pressure roller is held both positively and by friction against the feed roller, and the machine drive is blocked when the positive fit is broken.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A check-issuing cash register can be defrauded by lifting the counter-pressure roller only slightly, which stops the check feed without triggering the machine lock.

**Solution:** The counter-pressure roller is held both positively and by friction against the feed roller, and the machine drive is blocked when the positive fit is broken.
