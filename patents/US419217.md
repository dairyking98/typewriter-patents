## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters of the time needed one sheet at a time, bent paper, noisy typebars and imperfect spacing.

**Solution:** Typebar machine with a sliding feed plate holding many sheets, key-driven carriage spacing, soft-lined type bars, and rubber-footed frame.

**How it works:** Frame has an annular key plate on a hollow arm. A rubber-covered feed plate slides on the carriage under spring clips so a dozen or a tablet of sheets can be held and removed successively. Typebars are on adjustable hangers, filled with deadening material; ribbon spool detents and a spacing lever advance the carriage with each key. Rubber-lined sockets isolate noise.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** base plate; hollow arm; key plate; feed plate; feed roll; leaf spring; typebar; hanger; spacing lever; pawl; ribbon spool detent

**Numbers:** feed plate holds 12 to 50 sheets
