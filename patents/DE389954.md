## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** A type head guide frame moves toward the platen on each key stroke and the type head sits in its frame, so it is guided and damped during impact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type head bounces and vibrates on impact against the platen.

**Solution:** A type head guide frame moves toward the platen on each key stroke and the type head sits in its frame, so it is guided and damped during impact.
