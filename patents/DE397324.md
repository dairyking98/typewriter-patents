## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Switching device for typewriters or machines of a similar type and intended in particular to train the stop for the movable pawl so that, ß the unpleasant snapping noise that was previously created when the moving pawl moved forward and stopped is almost entirely eliminated.

**How it works (reading):** A sound-absorbing stop is fitted to the latch pusher so the movable pawl stops quietly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A movable pawl snaps audibly when it stops against its stop.

**Solution:** A sound-absorbing stop is fitted to the latch pusher so the movable pawl stops quietly.
