## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (zero print device for registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices for printing the point

**How it works (reading):** The amount switching element is coupled to the zero-setting adjusting member so both stop and release together, so the normal adder engagement is kept.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** In zero printing, the adder engages even when the zero-printing stroke is made, which wrongly moves the adder.

**Solution:** The amount switching element is coupled to the zero-setting adjusting member so both stop and release together, so the normal adder engagement is kept.
