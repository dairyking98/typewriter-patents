## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine keys) · **Subject (patent classes):** Keyboards; Switches having rectilinearly-movable operating part or parts adapted for pushing or pulling in one direction only, e.g. push-button switch having a plurality of operating members associated with different sets of contacts, e.g. keyboard

**How it works (reading):** A locking bar swung through a denomination's keys holds the other keys in that denomination from operating while one key is used.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjacent keys get pressed by mistake on calculating-machine keyboards.

**Solution:** A locking bar swung through a denomination's keys holds the other keys in that denomination from operating while one key is used.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
