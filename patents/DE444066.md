## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-index wheel and roller clutch needs a simple, reliable lock for line change.

**Solution:** Double-armed spring-loaded clamping levers on the roller cap grip the slip ring of the line-index wheel in alternate directions, triggered by a cone on the roller axis.
