## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key drive for writing, or similar machines powered by the push of a button.

**How it works (reading):** The key is carried on a cantilevered leaf spring, and a swivel cam on the key converts its vertical movement into horizontal movement, transferred by one rigid rod, thin band, wire or cord directly to the type lever, so that at most four joints are needed. The cam discs can all be identical.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In known key drives the finger's vertical motion passes through many joints and sliding surfaces, adding mass, friction and fatigue.

**Solution:** The key is carried on a cantilevered leaf spring, and a swivel cam on the key converts its vertical movement into horizontal movement, transferred by one rigid rod, thin band, wire or cord directly to the type lever, so that at most four joints are needed. The cam discs can all be identical.
