## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ticket printing or stamping machines need a ribbon that feeds step by step from one spool to the other and then reverses automatically without straining the ribbon.

**Solution:** When a ribbon is fully unwound, its end rod slides out along slots in the spool and strikes a shuttling bar that reverses the ratchet feed.

**How it works:** Two double-compartment spools carry two ribbons, each separated by disks, with ratchet-toothed outer disks pointing in opposite directions. Each ribbon end is fastened to a rod riding in radial slots, which sits in the hub recess while wound and moves to the outer slot ends when unwound. A bar linked on radius links lies along the disks and acts as a double-acting tappet. The rod of the empty spool strikes one end of the bar as it turns and shifts it, and the bar's horns then engage shoulders to switch which ratchet is driven by the plunger rods, reversing feed. The ribbon is advanced a step at each reciprocation of the print plunger.
