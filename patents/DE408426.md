To this end, according to the invention the drive linkage mounted on the typewriter frame for the switch frame attached switching devices connected to these by slot guides that can be solved by simply lifting a part. Other parts can are brought into connection with each other by resilient snap-in devices, so that a solution can be found by simply pressing in or pressing out one or the other part or reassembly: can be done.
