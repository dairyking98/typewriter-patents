> Description Translated from German Typewriter with a typewriter with a platen and Changeover frame carrying running rails. The invention relates to a typewriter, in which the writing carriage along with the platen and the running rails of one Switching frame is carried, and the purpose of the connections between the on the To design the changeover frame and the gear parts arranged on the main frame in such a way that that the Umschaltrahen is easily removable. To this end, according to the invention the drive linkage mounted on the typewriter frame for the switch frame attached switching devices connected to these by slot guides that can be solved by simply lifting a part. Other parts can are brought into connection with each other by resilient snap-in devices, so that a solution can be found by simply pressing in or pressing out one or the other part or reassembly: can be done.
