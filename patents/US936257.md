## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** The subject of the present invention is a device for automatically reversing the travel oftypewriterribbons.

**How it works (reading):** When one reel empties, ribbon tension moves a right-angle lever and a slide that transposes the drive pawls on the reel spindles so the ribbon reverses.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier automatic ribbon reversers moved the drive pawls only through an indirect slide, not directly from the ribbon.

**Solution:** When one reel empties, ribbon tension moves a right-angle lever and a slide that transposes the drive pawls on the reel spindles so the ribbon reverses.
