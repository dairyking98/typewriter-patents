## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars must be cleaned, adjusted, or replaced, but they are hard to remove from their pivot, guide and link.

**Solution:** The type bar is shaped so it can be quickly detached from its pivotal support, radial guide and operating link.
