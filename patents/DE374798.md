## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Writing-tables with substantially horizontally extensible or adjustable parts other than drawers, e.g. leaves with sliding or unfolding parts other than leaves or drawers

**How it works (reading):** A side-opening compartment box is articulated in a telescoping frame with guide tongues, so it can be pulled fully out of the cabinet and tilted sideways.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tilting cabinet drawers either tip all papers over and wrinkle them, or force the whole load to be lifted when the drawer is pulled out.

**Solution:** A side-opening compartment box is articulated in a telescoping frame with guide tongues, so it can be pulled fully out of the cabinet and tilted sideways.
