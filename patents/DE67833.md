## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A way to print musical notation by machine, with staff lines drawn and transposition to another key possible.

**Solution:** A carriage carrying vertically sprung note and sign type rods slides over a baseplate rack and can be offset forward or back against a graduated scale, so the same note keys print at a shifted pitch for transposition.

**How it works:** A sledge moves on guides over the base plate, advanced by rack, gear, ratchet wheel and pawl. A second carriage inside it holds the note and sign types as vertical rods held up by springs, printed by pressing down; feathers keep them off the paper. A handle moves this carriage across the lines to bring each note to the right height, using a graduation on the sledge side marked with staff lines and spaces. A separate carriage carries a staff-line roller, lifted by spiral springs, pressed down and drawn across to rule staves with its own inking roller. The paper is held by a spring plate and advanced by two rollers with a ratchet and pressure screws. For transposition a second adjustable graduation with a pointer lists keys; shifting it moves the notes to the new key.
