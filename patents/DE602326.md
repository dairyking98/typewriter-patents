## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A moving middle roller section disturbs the sheets already set on it when the roller is rotated.

**Solution:** The middle roller section is turned directly by its line-feed device, and a movable coupling ties it to an end section so that all three turn as one without axial shift.
