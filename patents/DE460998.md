## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink supply arrangements therefor

**How it works (reading):** A light, thin-walled ink container is hung on grain tips at its centre of mass so it swings out to the type, reducing its moment of inertia while keeping the ink capacity.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ink containers that swing against type were heavy and their weight and bulk added friction and inertia to the type movement.

**Solution:** A light, thin-walled ink container is hung on grain tips at its centre of mass so it swings out to the type, reducing its moment of inertia while keeping the ink capacity.
