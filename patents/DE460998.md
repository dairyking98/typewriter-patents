There are inking devices for the types of typewriters and similar machines known, in which a paint container stepping in the way of the types around a penetrating it ideal axis swings out against spring action. The difficulty of the container design and store so that its entrainment the type lever or the type bar Contaminated as little as possible are very important because they are in the smallest possible container as much color as possible should be accommodated so that color is not refilled as often must be, and the small size of the container to save weight and thereby the Reduction of friction appears to be necessary. The invention achieves a reduction in the moment of inertia of the Container without reducing the amount of dye and at the same time a very light one Storage in that the swing bearings of the paint container in the main outside are arranged by him. The ink container is advantageously stored here between the grain tips of a support frame.
