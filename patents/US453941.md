## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typebar machine should write capital or small letters from one set of keys, without a separate shift key movement of the whole mechanism.

**Solution:** Each key's wire loop slides between two paired bell-crank wrists, one driving a small-letter bar and one a capital bar, shifted all at once by a slotted ring.

**How it works:** Capital and small-letter typebars are paired side by side on a rod around a circular opening. Each pair has bell-crank levers with facing beveled wrists; a loop on the key wire rests on one wrist or the other. A slotted ring below the opening carries all the key wires, and a shift lever oscillates the ring so every loop moves from the small wrist to the capital wrist at once. A tooth on the letter-space rocking frame drops into a slot in the ring when a key is struck, locking the ring so it can only turn when both bars are down.
