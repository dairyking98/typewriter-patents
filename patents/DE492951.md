## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control strip feed for cash registers)

**How it works (reading):** The roller support arms carry lugs or shoes that run on a curved guide rail centred on the arms' pivot points, so the rollers move in a controlled path while a cam-driven arm makes a loose loop in the strip between read and print points.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The mostly one-sided feed rollers of a cash register control strip feed unevenly and the strip can shift.

**Solution:** The roller support arms carry lugs or shoes that run on a curved guide rail centred on the arms' pivot points, so the rollers move in a controlled path while a cam-driven arm makes a loose loop in the strip between read and print points.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
