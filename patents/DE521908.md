## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon switching for cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**How it works (reading):** The indexing pawls that advance the ribbon are controlled by the angular position of a control disk that turns with the ribbon spool, so the ribbon movement reverses at the correct point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A ribbon in cash registers that runs out in one direction has to reverse automatically after full unwinding, and the existing reversing does not handle this reliably.

**Solution:** The indexing pawls that advance the ribbon are controlled by the angular position of a control disk that turns with the ribbon spool, so the ribbon movement reverses at the correct point.

**Features:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
