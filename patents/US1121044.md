## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon vibrator must work on a folding carriage, present different ribbon zones, and allow stencil cutting.

**Solution:** A vibrator with an adjustable actuating member offering three settings: ribbon idle, one field, or a larger throw to a second field.

**How it works:** Key levers press a universal bar whose arms rock bell cranks and a rocking frame carrying the vibrator. A manually adjusted member changes the vibrator throw: off for stencils, short for one zone, long for another. The ribbon is restored below the print point after printing and is kept from overthrowing under hard strokes. The mechanism assembles separately and registers whether the carriage is folded or in use.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** key lever; universal bar; bell crank; rocking frame; ribbon vibrator; vibrator cam lever; actuating member

**Numbers:** three settings

**Improves on:**

- Petermann ribbon lifts for folding machines: constant registration folded or not, plus selectable throw
