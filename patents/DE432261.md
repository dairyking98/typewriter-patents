> Description Translated from German Brush for cleaning typewriter types. The invention relates focus on a brush for cleaning typewriter types and consists in that a cylindrical or semi-cylindrical brush arranged on a rotatable shaft which is under the action of a spring provided in the brush handle, which by turning the brush when cleaning the types. This design ensures that the impurities of the types due to the spring suddenly relaxing when brushing brought out will; the bristles of the brush straighten up again. It will as a result causes a thorough cleaning of the types. The subject matter of the invention is illustrated in the drawing, and Fig. r shows a general view, Fig. a a brush in section and Fig. 3 shows the brush arrangement seen from above. In the handle u there is an axis e, which is rotatably mounted in metal sleeves g and o and secured against displacement by a washer f and a nut p. One end i of a helical spring h on the axis is guided in the groove v in the handle u , while the other end l of the spring is inserted through a bore k in the axis.
