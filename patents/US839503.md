## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-levers and bell-cranks wear and bind, and a carriage stop cannot be set at different points from the keyboard.

**Solution:** Roller bearings between the key-levers and bell-cranks give low-friction motion, and key-levers that slide longitudinally control a column stop so the carriage stops at different points.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** key-levers; roller bearings; bell-cranks; type-bars; stepped stop; links
