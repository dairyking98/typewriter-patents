## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A barrel-type machine with many characters should be fast to operate, simple, and need few keys.

**Solution:** Type barrel with 100 characters in five circles and twenty generatrices, positioned by three key groups giving axial translation, small rotation and large quarter-turn rotation.

**How it works:** Two barrels on a shared horizontal spindle that rotates and slides in bearings on vertically movable rods. One key group (five keys) causes lengthwise movement of the spindle through a pivoting frame and a lever in a collar groove. A second group (five keys) turns the barrel by one to four generatrices, and a third group (four keys) turns it by quarter, half or three quarters of a revolution to bring a letter-field to the print position. A long-pitch threaded sleeve on the spindle, keyed by a screw in a groove, and a further mechanism cause the barrel to turn and print. Two barrels print identical impressions on two sheets or a continuous strip.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Keys that only set the element](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
- **Type element:** [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** type barrel; spindle; bearing; guide groove; threaded sleeve; key lever; oscillating frame; collar; carriage; printing roller

**Numbers:** 100 characters per barrel; 5 parallel circles; 20 generatrices; 14 keys in three groups
