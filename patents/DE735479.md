## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is in one embodiment on the Drawings shown, namely Fig.

**How it works (reading):** A new type lever drive replaces the slot-and-nipple connection so that the drive keeps working through the strike and the lever does not lose leverage near the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The standard Underwood-type slotted link drive gives a dead-gear phase, slot friction and noise, and wastes key effort, so strike power and speed suffer.

**Solution:** A new type lever drive replaces the slot-and-nipple connection so that the drive keeps working through the strike and the lever does not lose leverage near the platen.
