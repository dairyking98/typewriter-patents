## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to gross line-spacing mechanisms for typewriting machines, to facilitate the' feeding of paper around the platen for a great distance to a predetermined point by ay single operation.

**How it works (reading):** A handle with a dog drives the ratchet, and a circular rack with adjustable stops lets the platen turn more than one revolution per stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bills with wide headings need many platen turns to reach the print point.

**Solution:** A handle with a dog drives the ratchet, and a circular rack with adjustable stops lets the platen turn more than one revolution per stroke.
