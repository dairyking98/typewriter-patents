## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card duplicating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manually-controlled devices

**How it works (reading):** A device under control of a scanning result at the metering point prevents duplication of holes there while still allowing selected columns of the blank card to be punched.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On punch card duplicators, identification holes that must be swapped or relocated cannot be reproduced, because the metering point blocks duplication of those holes.

**Solution:** A device under control of a scanning result at the metering point prevents duplication of holes there while still allowing selected columns of the blank card to be punched.
