## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Changing line spacing previously meant swapping the ratchet wheel for one with a different tooth pitch.

**Solution:** A second, axially sliding ratchet wheel offset by half a tooth pitch lets the same feed tooth give normal or in-between line spacing.

**How it works:** The usual line ratchet has a hub carrying a second ratchet of equal pitch, offset by half a tooth, which can be slid into or out of work and is held in both end positions by a ball detent. A wide feed tooth engages either only the first wheel (normal steps) or both wheels (adding half-steps). A pivoting detent lever with two rollers always has one roller in a tooth gap, so the platen is locked in every position, and it can be swung away to free the platen.

**Numbers:** half tooth pitch offset

- line spacing by exchanging the ratchet wheel: adds half steps with a second sliding wheel instead of a swap
