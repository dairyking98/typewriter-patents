## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanism needed better ribbon drive and a way to hold the ribbon under tension away from the paper at rest so writing stays visible.

**Solution:** A geared ribbon drive and reversing mechanism that keeps the ribbon taut and clear of the paper, shown on a machine with a horizontal disc platen.

**How it works:** Two vertical spool shafts are driven through bevel gears; an adjustable shifting shaft carries a driving gear that meshes with one spool gear or the other, so the spools turn in opposite directions and the ribbon winds from one to the other. Shifting the shaft reverses the drive, manually by a lever or automatically. The host machine has typebars in two groups on either side of the print point, and a platen that is a horizontal disc turning on a vertical axis with a narrow rim face. The platen and curved-path paper rise and fall to bring one of two vertically spaced types on each head to the rim.

**Parts:** platen disc; type bar; ribbon spool; bevel gear; shifting shaft; paper guide segment; key lever; rock shaft
