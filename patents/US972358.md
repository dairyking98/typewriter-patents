## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators must re-align each new sheet by hand when tabulating or billing, and correcting errors means shifting the paper laterally.

**Solution:** A detachable slotted bar on the paper table carries an adjustable gage and pivoted paper holder so each sheet enters at the same starting point.
