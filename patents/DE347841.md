## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding machine type disc printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**How it works (reading):** One eccentric on the main drive shaft swings a double lever that moves a slotted plate, carrying an ink pad under the type discs and then pressing it up against them; a spring-loaded stamp pushes the paper strip against the type discs and a cutter severs the strip.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing unit of an adding machine needs inking, paper-strip pressing and cutting timed to one main-shaft drive; the text states no further defect.

**Solution:** One eccentric on the main drive shaft swings a double lever that moves a slotted plate, carrying an ink pad under the type discs and then pressing it up against them; a spring-loaded stamp pushes the paper strip against the type discs and a cutter severs the strip.

> Description Translated from German Digit printing device for adding units. The drawing shows an embodiment of the subject matter of the invention shown. The eccentric 2 is firmly seated on the main drive shaft i, which is rotated by a crank. This slot plate has two curved slots 9, and guide pins io, which are arranged on the housing wall, engage through them. A paint pad ii is attached to the lower part of the slotted plate 8 and is pressed against the type wheels i2 from below when the double lever is pivoted out.
