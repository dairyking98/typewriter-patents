The drawing shows an embodiment of the subject matter of the invention shown. The eccentric 2 is firmly seated on the main drive shaft i, which is rotated by a crank. This slot plate has two curved slots 9, and guide pins io, which are arranged on the housing wall, engage through them. A paint pad ii is attached to the lower part of the slotted plate 8 and is pressed against the type wheels i2 from below when the double lever is pivoted out.
