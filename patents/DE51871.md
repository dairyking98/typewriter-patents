## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machine improvements for exact striking, even when worn.

**Solution:** Type levers hung on spherical seats so they strike flat even when worn, plus a barrel slide, ribbon displacement and paper release.

**How it works:** Key levers on a bolt pull wire connections to type levers whose hubs sit between spherical surfaces and can move slightly tangentially, keeping the type flat to the paper. A guide plate with teeth guides each type lever to the strike point. A cylinder slide switches letter and space, with barrel drive and latch lock, line feed, paper release and signal bell. Rubber feet and padding reduce noise.
