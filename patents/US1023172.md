## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil plates or cards cannot be quickly introduced, squared and positioned for the first line, and fed flat through the machine.

**Solution:** Grooved guide rails on standards with feed rollers and a side edge gage square the stencil and position it for writing, with a release key for the feed.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
