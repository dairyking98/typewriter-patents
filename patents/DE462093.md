## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a roller guide for the paper trolleys of typewriters, typewriters, especially those with a very long paper cart, and the like.

**How it works (reading):** Extra rollers at both ends of the carriage rail keep the front frame leg supported after the middle roller leaves the rail, so the rail can stay short.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Long paper carriages could pendulate or leave the frame unguided once they ran past the end of the carriage rail, unless the rail was made longer.

**Solution:** Extra rollers at both ends of the carriage rail keep the front frame leg supported after the middle roller leaves the rail, so the rail can stay short.
