## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single swing arm drives both key groups in an adding and listing machine, and the second group must sometimes be left unaffected.

**Solution:** The stroke of one swing arm is made effective or ineffective for the second key group by a rotatable release bracket with notched parts that allow the release pawls to pass the keys.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Adding and list machine. The subject of the invention is a Adding and listing machine of the known type, in which a swing arm mechanism the keys pressed in a specific key group for each work cycle triggers, the keys pressed in another key group, however, optionally at will leave unaffected for a long time for the purpose of repetition or can also trigger. The new one differs from the known similar machines in that both groups of buttons are not replaced by two different rocker arms, but rather be controlled by one and the same swing arm whose stroke compared to the Keys of the second group can be made either effective or ineffective. In the simplest embodiment of the invention, the rod is the Release bracket rotatable about its axis and assigned to the second group of buttons Notched parts which, when the rod is in a certain rotational position, the release pawls Allow free passage for the second group of buttons.
