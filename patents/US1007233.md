## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The bottom margin on a sheet must be regulated with certainty and varied from sheet to sheet, which manual setting does not do well.

**Solution:** An automatic stop attachment on the platen with a rotary plate and a locking pawl and disk. It stops paper-roller rotation at a set point, can be set to vary the margin, and can be disengaged.

**How it works:** A rotary plate on the paper roller is stopped by a locking mechanism at a point set by the user, so the platen cannot turn past the preset bottom margin.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** platen; rotary plate; locking pawl and disk; stop mechanism; connecting means
