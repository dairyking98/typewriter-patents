## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot tell when the bottom of the sheet nears the print line.

**Solution:** A feeler finger rides on the paper under the platen and drops into a platen groove at the sheet end, releasing a hammer to ring a bell.

**How it works:** A bar from the carriage frame carries a clamp and a pivoted curved arm whose outer end is a hammer and whose inner end is an upward finger that bears on the paper opposite a groove in the platen. When the sheet's lower edge passes, the finger enters the groove and the weight (or an optional spring) lets the hammer strike the gong. A nose on the finger lets a new sheet push the finger out of the groove.
