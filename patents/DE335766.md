## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the platen returns from the switched position it rattles and bounces, which spoils the impression and makes the paper shudder.

**Solution:** Locking members on the platen frame carry a pin that sits against the carriage frame in rest position, and they are released only by the changeover roller, so the platen falls back smoothly with little pivoting or friction.
