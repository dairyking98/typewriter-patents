## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ticket printing drums need variable pressure rollers that are fixed after setting and cannot be changed by accident.

**Solution:** A pin on the adjustable pressure roller stays in engagement with a series of circular guides centred on the drum axis, so the variable imprint is set and locked for each drum position.
