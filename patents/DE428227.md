## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage roller bearing cones and balls fall out and get lost when the roller is removed for repair.

**Solution:** The fixing pin has a head on one side and a thread on the other, and a soft-metal ring placed in the cone recess is driven onto the thread to hold cones, balls and pin together as one unit.

The main object of the invention is to provide a device to create that facilitates removal and handling for the purpose of repair. It Previously, it was common practice to use the cones on the mounting pin or spigot to hold together a mother, which, however, gave rise to the inconvenience that it It was very difficult to hold and prevent the cones during removal or application, that the balls fall out and get lost during this manipulation. About these evils To remedy this, one comes with a head on one side and a thread on the other equipped fixing pin in use, that of a ring made of soft metal is surrounded in such a way that it holds the individual parts of the roller device together. The ring is inserted into a cutout of the cone on the thread side and connected to this pin.
