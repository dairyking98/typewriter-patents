## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The main frame of a typebar typewriter (National) should give access to the mechanism.

**Solution:** The main frame is made in two parts pivotally connected so the rear portion can be swung up, with the platen carrier and spring-drum spacing arranged to follow.

**How it works:** Finger keys with spring-returned arms operate typebars loosely pivoted to them and guided in a hanger. The platen carrier rides on rods of the main frame, and a pivoted portion of the frame can be lifted while arms keep contact with the platen-carrier frame along an arc. A spring drum with a ratchet wheel is controlled by two alternating dogs on a spacing frame operated by the space key. A bell trip and adjustable drum stop on the drum shaft give a line-end bell and a stop at chosen positions. Line spacing is done at the end of carriage return by a plunger through the ratchet shaft.
