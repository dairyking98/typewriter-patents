## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Stenographers need a small, light shorthand machine that prints characters in small spaces so little paper holds many words.

**Solution:** A hollow cylindrical platen that moves endwise along a sleeve as it turns, driven by a screw thread against a rack bar, with small typebars arranged for shorthand.

**How it works:** A pillar on the base carries an axle with a sleeve; a ratchet wheel and gear on the sleeve, with a friction collar acting as brake. The hollow platen slides on the sleeve through a slot and feather but turns with it. A worm thread on the platen engages a spring-hinged rack bar at the front so the platen moves endwise while rotating. A rock-bar with clips holds sheet paper, raised by a lever. Typebars and key levers print shorthand marks on the platen.

**Features:** [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** base; pillar; axle; sleeve; ratchet wheel; gear; friction collar; hollow platen; feather; worm thread; rack bar; rock-bar; clip; lever; typebar; key lever

**Improves on:**

- ordinary stenographic machines: smaller type areas and a helically feeding cylindrical platen
