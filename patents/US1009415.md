## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed devices for visible writing machines need better guiding of the feed rolls.

**Solution:** Front and rear feed rolls under the platen are carried in a frame with a paper pan and table, and rocking links guide the feed devices, pressed by springs.
