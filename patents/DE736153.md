## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers

**How it works (reading):** A small-typewriter box whose front wall folds down onto the desktop, extending the box floor flush, with sliding strips on which the machine's feet glide in and out.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter cases stay on the desk and get in the way, and a box with a pull-out floor is expensive and the machine still stands on it.

**Solution:** A small-typewriter box whose front wall folds down onto the desktop, extending the box floor flush, with sliding strips on which the machine's feet glide in and out.
