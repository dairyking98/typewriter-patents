## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by denominational arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a table writing device on writing, booking and similar machines with column and decimal settings.

**How it works (reading):** The same key levers set any column and the decimal places, since they shift axially and tilt about a pivot with a guide slot, and column levers move a locking device on the rider track.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column tabs that skip columns need a second operation to set the decimal point, and earlier skip tabs stop at each column.

**Solution:** The same key levers set any column and the decimal places, since they shift axially and tilt about a pivot with a guide slot, and column levers move a locking device on the rider track.
