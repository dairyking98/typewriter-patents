## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide a marginal clamp for holding Work on a fiat platen, such as is used in that class of typewriters known as book or commercial type-writers.

**How it works (reading):** A clamping blade on a tubular bolt, supported on a rear bracket, holds the work and elevates away so the platen can be tilted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work on a flat platen of book typewriters needs a marginal clamp that can be supported and raised independently of the platen.

**Solution:** A clamping blade on a tubular bolt, supported on a rear bracket, holds the work and elevates away so the platen can be tilted.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
