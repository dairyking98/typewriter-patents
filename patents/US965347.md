## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen must be released from the notched line-space wheel so it can be turned freely, using a cheap, compact device that fits existing machines.

**Solution:** A head fixed on the platen axle carries two pivoted dogs whose jaws bite a ring on the line-space wheel, operated by a wedge lug on a push-pull button stem.

**How it works:** A disk on the platen axle carries two dogs pivoted near their jaws, which fit over a flange on the line-space wheel. Their inner ends give a long lever so a short dog bites hard. A stem running through the hub carries a wedge lug that spreads the dogs' inner ends and swings the jaws onto the ring, held by a spring check. Pulling the button withdraws the lug and a C-shaped spring pulls the dogs together, releasing the wheel.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen axle; line-space wheel; ring flange; dog-carrying head; dog; C-shaped spring; release button; stem; wedge lug; spring check

**Numbers:** two dogs

**Improves on:**

- Uhlig buckling-plate clutch (US965346): uses pivoted dogs with lever purchase instead of a buckling plate
