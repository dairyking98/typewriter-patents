## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulating mechanism needs its own key; the inventor wants ordinary keys to do double duty.

**Solution:** A front key member that rocks a transverse rod and shaft so that the tabulating key is operated.

**How it works:** A key member is pivoted at the front, spring-held forward, and swings a transverse rod on arms of a rock-shaft. A crank-arm pin on the shaft rides an inclined surface of the tabulating key and swings it, releasing the carriage. The release lifts the feed dogs from the rack and applies a brake to the carriage rail. A tab stop is positioned later.
