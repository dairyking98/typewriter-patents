## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper fingers block erasures and manifold sheet handling because they cannot be cleared from the paper path quickly.

**Solution:** The paper finger is pivoted on an axis diagonal to the platen axis so one swing carries it laterally off the paper edges.

**How it works:** A diagonal pivot lets the finger engage the side margins and swing clear of the paper in a single movement.
