## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to another Training of the typewriter with motor.

**How it works (reading):** An arm on the rear of each key lever reaches the intermediate lever and strikes it directly, so the type lever can be driven by hand without motor power.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A motorized typewriter cannot be hit by hand when the motor fails.

**Solution:** An arm on the rear of each key lever reaches the intermediate lever and strikes it directly, so the type lever can be driven by hand without motor power.
