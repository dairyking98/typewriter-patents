## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** The matrix carriage is split into a main part with one to-and-fro movement and a sliding part with its own line feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lines and the matrix carriage need independent line spacing.

**Solution:** The matrix carriage is split into a main part with one to-and-fro movement and a sliding part with its own line feed.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
