## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Preventing rebound or clash of levers or type members

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a new design of such a solving or feedback device for type levers that are jammed with one another, with a few additional links and in particular by a per se on every typewriter existing button lever can be triggered.

**How it works (reading):** A return stop is formed as a resilient spring latch between the shift-lever coupling rod; a switching bridge pushed out of its base position by jammed levers is returned to base by the relative movement when the shift key is pressed, freeing the jammed type levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers can jam together at the end stop, and special release buttons were needed to free them.

**Solution:** A return stop is formed as a resilient spring latch between the shift-lever coupling rod; a switching bridge pushed out of its base position by jammed levers is returned to base by the relative movement when the shift key is pressed, freeing the jammed type levers.
