## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** An adjustment device on the paper roller that limits reverse rotation to a set amount, with forward rotation unlimited, operated by the ordinary platen handwheel from either side.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** For billing work, where invoices are written together with a journal or carbon sheet, the paper must be backed up a set amount to start the next sheet on the same line spacing. Existing crank devices work on one side only and need the carriage pulled far out.

**Solution:** An adjustment device on the paper roller that limits reverse rotation to a set amount, with forward rotation unlimited, operated by the ordinary platen handwheel from either side.

**How it works:** The adjusting device acts only in the reverse direction of the paper roller, so the roller can be turned back a set amount with the ordinary handwheel, while forward rotation is unrestricted. It works from either side and in any platen position, with no separate crank. A coupling device engages it, and a release lever for the paper guide rollers is part of the same arrangement.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** paper roller; handwheel; paper carriage cheek; release lever; pivot pin; adjustment device; coupling device

**Improves on:**

- crank-controlled reverse rotation on one side only: handwheel-operated limited reverse, usable from either side
