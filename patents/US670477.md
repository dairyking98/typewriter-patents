## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typing on lined paper needs line spacing that is not tied to the platen detent and ratchet pitch, and the platen shift mechanism needs a simpler arrangement.

**Solution:** A lifting frame that disengages the platen detent roller and turns into a brake on the platen so it can be set to any position, plus a bell-crank and roller platen-shift with a lock.

**How it works:** The platen frame hangs on front and rear links so it rocks front to rear for case shift. A rock-shaft with an upright rod and roller presses on the rear bar of the platen frame when the shift key is depressed, rocking the platen forward to adjustable stops; a spring returns it. A supplemental piece pivoted on the shift key can be tilted to lock the key under a ledge. For lined paper, a U-shaped frame around the detent roller is raised, lifting the roller off the platen ratchet and pressing a leg against the platen, so the spring arm's tension acts as a brake while the operator turns the platen by hand to any distance.
