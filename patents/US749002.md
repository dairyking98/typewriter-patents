## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewheel that revolves continuously is fast, but the character wanted must be at the printing point long enough to take an impression without stopping the wheel.

**Solution:** A continuously revolving type-wheel in a rocking frame is bodily moved opposite to the rotation of its impression side so the selected character is momentarily held at the printing point, and a hammer then prints it.

**How it works:** A driver cylinder with spiral projections and the type-wheel shaft turn continuously at the same speed through equal gears. The wheel sits in a rocking frame pivoted on the driver shaft. Depressing a finger key throws a star-wheel on that key into the path of the one driver projection that matches its character. The driver turns the star-wheel, whose tooth lifts a transverse plate on a rock-shaft, rocking the frame so the wheel axis moves from X to Y against the rotation. For that instant the chosen character is stationary at the printing point and a hammer linked to the frame strikes it. A spring and stops return everything, and a continuously turning toothed wheel with a latch and catch feeds the carriage one step per stroke. A splined wheel slides axially to bring a second series of characters (Arabic or Roman numerals in the example) to the printing point.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** driver cylinder; type-wheel; rocking frame; finger key; star-wheel; transverse plate; rock-shaft; hammer; carriage-feed wheel; latch; catch; carriage rack; feed pawl; shifting key; return spring

**Numbers:** ten characters in each of two series on the wheel; one driver projection per character; five teeth on each star-wheel; driver and type-wheel same speed, equal-diameter gears

**Improves on:**

- Tyberg application Serial No. 635,681 (1897): new ways of controlling the continuously revolving wheel and taking impressions
- Tyberg application Serial No. 708,423 (1899): the broader generic claims are there; this case claims the particular key and star-wheel organization
