## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Oliver machine the ribbon is thrown over the printing point and withdrawn after each impression, but only one ribbon could be used.

**Solution:** Two ribbon guides, one per colour, are moved by the throw mechanism, with the operator choosing which advances.

**How it works:** The Oliver paper carriage rides on a shift frame carrying an upright shaft with escape wheel and pinion that meshes with the carriage rack. A ribbon-throw mechanism reciprocates the ribbon guides over the strike point and back to show the printed line. Two guide plates with separate ribbons are fitted, and one is advanced while the other stays retracted. Only the opening is available.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** ribbon guide; ribbon; shift frame; escape wheel; carriage rack; platen

**Improves on:**

- Oliver ribbon-throw mechanism: adds a second ribbon guide and selection
