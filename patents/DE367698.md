## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a so-called billing device on the paper roll of typewriters, through which the roller rotates at a certain, adjustable angle Can be turned back and forth so that when writing: invoices, delivery notes and similar forms, the cover copies on the collective (: posting =) sheet immediately following each other and not in the head of the invoice or the delivery note or the like.

**How it works (reading):** A toothed disc with a stop is fitted to the platen axis, and a claw coupling lets the platen be turned back and forth by the letterhead height, adaptable to typewriters of any system.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When writing invoices on a typewriter, carbon copies must land below the letterhead, leaving the head area blank, and existing billing devices are not easy to fit.

**Solution:** A toothed disc with a stop is fitted to the platen axis, and a claw coupling lets the platen be turned back and forth by the letterhead height, adaptable to typewriters of any system.
