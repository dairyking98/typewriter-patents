## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible writing machines need a ribbon that can cover the printing point with different ribbon fields and use two-colour ribbons.

**Solution:** Mechanism lets a multi-field ribbon be fed lengthwise and crosswise and vibrated to the printing point, with a two-colour ribbon selectable at will.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
