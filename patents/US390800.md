## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The copy-holder sits out of the operator's line of sight and gets in the way when the carriage is lifted.

**Solution:** A copy-holder clamped to the front corner posts of the machine is brought in front of the operator and can be swung aside.

To all whom it may concern.- Be it known that I, TRUMAN GARRAT PALMER, a citizen of the United States, residing at Chicago, in the county of Cook, State of Illinois, have invented certain new and useful Improvements in Copy-Holding Devices for Type-Writers, of which I do declare the following to be a full, clear, and exact description, reference being bad to the accompanying drawings, forming part of this specification. My present invention has for its object to provide an improved means whereby the copyholder that sustains the manuscript or other matter to be copied may be brought directly in front of the operator of the machine while being copied, and which may be readily moved to one side when it is desired to lift the carriage of the type-writer or to place the copyholder temporarily out of use.
