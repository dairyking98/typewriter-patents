## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to mechanism for back spacing the carriage of a typewriting machine by means of a key or similar device, so that upon each operation of the key the carriage may be moved back a definite distance, ordinarily a single space, and thereupon be arrested.

**How it works (reading):** One element actuates the back-spacing dog and also operates both the rack stop and the escapement-wheel stop.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Back-spacing mechanisms use many parts for the dog and its separate rack and escapement-wheel stops.

**Solution:** One element actuates the back-spacing dog and also operates both the rack stop and the escapement-wheel stop.

**How it works:** The back-space key lever links to a pivoted lever and slot-and-pin to a second lever on a bracket. A slotted dog on a stud first moves into engagement with the carriage rack and then moves back one space with it. The same element brings stops into position: one engages the rack from above to prevent overthrow by carriage momentum, one holds the escapement wheel. The pinion rides loosely on the escapement stud with a spring-held pawl, so teeth pass idly in back-spacing.
