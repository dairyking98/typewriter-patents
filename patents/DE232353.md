> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE. PATENT LETTERING - M 232353 CLASS \ 5g. GROUP Patented in the German Empire from May 1909. The invention relates to a type lever movement device for typewriters, their type levers standing vertically, on both sides of the plane of symmetry of the typewriter in front of the paper roll and in a plane parallel to its axis have axes of rotation which are upright Arms of button levers designed as angle levers are in connection. Of the known devices of this type, the subject matter of the invention differs in that that the upright arms of the key levers follow from the sides of the machine their plane of symmetry increase in length in such a way that the moments of force of the associated, type levers which decrease in length towards the plane of symmetry are equal to each other. The invention aims that Force of the stop of the individual type levers on the paper roller as evenly as possible to design.
