## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars of different lengths hit the platen with unequal force.

**Solution:** Make the upright arms of the key levers grow longer toward the sides so the moments of the (shorter-toward-centre) typebars are equal.

**How it works:** Vertical typebars on both sides of the symmetry plane in front of the roller connect through rods with turnbuckles to upright arms of angle-lever key levers. Key-lever arms lengthen outward as typebars shorten toward the centre, so impact moments match, and the key lever lengths vary inversely to their angular swing so key stroke is uniform. Groups of three key levers of different lengths act on a universal rail.
