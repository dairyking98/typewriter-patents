## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single type-bar should carry two characters and bring either one to the printing cylinder by a downward key press or a pull on the operating parts.

**Solution:** A type-bar with two projecting types is moved by mechanism so that either the upper or the lower type comes into contact with the printing cylinder.
