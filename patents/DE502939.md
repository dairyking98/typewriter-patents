## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typist's chair should adapt to body shape and posture.

**Solution:** A chair with a flexible adjustable backrest whose seat plate tilts on a front hinge against a rear tension spring when weight shifts back.

**How it works:** A height-adjustable support cross in the chair base carries a sheet-metal plate bent up at the front and fixed to the seat plate. The rear arm of the cross is joined by a tension spring to a bolt under the rear of the seat plate. When the sitter leans back the spring stretches and the seat tilts about its front edge, returning to horizontal when relieved. A backrest on a support arm is height-adjustable and screw-locked.

**Parts:** chair; seat plate; support cross; tension spring; backrest; support arm
