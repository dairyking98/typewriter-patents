## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Letter-cards or letter-sheets, i.e. cards or sheets each of which is to be folded with the message inside and to serve as its own envelope for mailing

**How it works (reading):** A glue stripe applied and pre-dried before the flap is glued stiffens the free edge strip so it curls inward and stays closed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The free edge strip of a typewriter postcard with a folded address flap tends to gape, so mail items get pushed into the gap in the filing system.

**Solution:** A glue stripe applied and pre-dried before the flap is glued stiffens the free edge strip so it curls inward and stays closed.
