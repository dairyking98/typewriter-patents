## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE CLASS 15: Printing. The present innovation in typewriters relates in particular to the movement device for the ink ribbon. The same is set up in such a way that it automatically adjusts the direction of movement of the belt if necessary reverses. For this purpose, pens or other protrusions are placed on the ribbon, one of which is so close to one end of the ribbon that it can be tied to one or the other arm of a lever comes into contact, which actuates a double pawl, and the unwinding reel turns the tape into a take-up reel by turning one end of the double pawl disengages from the ratchet wheel of one spool and engages the other end in the ratchet wheel of the other spool. Furthermore, the Guide for the slide of the unclamping cylinder designed so that this slide easily can be removed from the machine. This, in connection with the peculiar device of movement for the Ribbon still has the advantage that the parts concerned are much more easily accessible will.
