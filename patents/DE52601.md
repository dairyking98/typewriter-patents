## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**How it works (reading):** A lever and switching plate driven by a pin on the ribbon end swing a double pawl to reverse the ribbon spool direction.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants ribbon reversing and removal of the platen slide to be simpler and more accessible.

**Solution:** A lever and switching plate driven by a pin on the ribbon end swing a double pawl to reverse the ribbon spool direction.

**How it works:** The platen slide guide rod has flattened ends held by cap screws in semi-cylindrical seats so the slide can be lifted out. Two ribbon reels carry ratchet switching wheels. When a pin at a ribbon end touches a fork lever, it turns a switch lever and a plate, which turns the double pawl so one reel's ratchet is released while the other engages. Resilient locking arms hold the wheels, and a stop rail vibrated by the keys gives the pawl motion.
