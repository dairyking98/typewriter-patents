## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Beginners need a way to learn touch typing without looking at the keys.

**Solution:** An adjustable blind and keyboard guide on a standard that sits in front of the keys, adjustable by slotted feet; an expanding variant is also shown.
