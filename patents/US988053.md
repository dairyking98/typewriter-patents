## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A check printer must also make a permanent paper record of the amount, which earlier machines did not do.

**Solution:** A check is held and printed, while a ribbon-inked strip of paper is fed a set length each time to make a permanent record of the transaction.
