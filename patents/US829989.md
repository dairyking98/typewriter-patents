## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to avoid the necessity for such adjustment and to enable the bills when put in the machine to be adjusted instantly and Without appreciable labet or attention to exactly the required position with reference to the printing point or line and also with reference to the usual tabulating' mechanism which is commonly employed in writing bills.

**How it works (reading):** Gauges at the delivery side and the side edges of the paper, plus a tablet along the top of the platen, let the bill be placed precisely each time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bills inserted repeatedly for each item must be realigned by eye to the printed line each time.

**Solution:** Gauges at the delivery side and the side edges of the paper, plus a tablet along the top of the platen, let the bill be placed precisely each time.
