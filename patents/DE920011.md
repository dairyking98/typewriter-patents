## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles characterised by being applied to printers having transversely- moving carriages and mounted on the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guider of the carriage typewriters od.

**How it works (reading):** A pinion-driven four-wheel gear chain links transport roller and pressure roller at any spacing, so both sides of the stack move at equal speed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed on carriage typewriters with several stacked sheets and carbons drags the sheets against each other, and earlier drive trains with slip clutches and universal joints are fragile.

**Solution:** A pinion-driven four-wheel gear chain links transport roller and pressure roller at any spacing, so both sides of the stack move at equal speed.

**Features:**
