## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers on bank books or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** The purpose of my invention is to provide a type-writing machine adapted for writing upon fiat surfaces, such as the pages of a book or sheets of paper placed upon a flat platen or printing-bed.

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter riding on fixed guideways above a flat bed that is line-spaced by moving the machine for books or by moving the platen for loose sheets.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing on the pages of heavy books or on flat sheets needs a machine that does not move paper toward or away from the operator.

**Solution:** A typewriter riding on fixed guideways above a flat bed that is line-spaced by moving the machine for books or by moving the platen for loose sheets.

**How it works:** A main supporting frame moves on fixed guideways to line-space, carrying a tilting carriage-frame, on which a type-frame carriage moves for letter spacing. A rocking type-frame on a horizontal axis carries type-bars radial to the printing point, each with two or more characters in different planes, worked by key levers. For light work a flat platen on guideways under the machine is spaced rearward instead. The universal bar rocks with the type-frame and connects to the escapement through flexible connections.

**Features:** [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** main supporting frame; machine guideway; tilting carriage-frame; type-frame carriage; rocking type-frame; type-bar; key lever; flat platen; universal bar

**Numbers:** 9 sheets of drawings
