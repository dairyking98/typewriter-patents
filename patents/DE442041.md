## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for holding one or more control sheets when writing and calculating machines. The invention relates to a device for holding several control sheets for typewriters and calculating machines, especially those Machines equipped with a Bi: l: ng device. It's on typewriters and writing calculating machines known devices through which one can obtain a number of forms one after the other, while the associated Control sheets remain in the machine and the records one after the other of the individual forms. With these facilities you need to take them out of the forms each time by lifting the pressure rollers on the typewriter the introduced sheets of paper are loosened. This creates the risk that when pulling the forms out of the machine, the control sheet or sheets move, which can put the labels in the wrong place. The present invention relates to an apparatus by which this Disadvantage is eliminated, in particular there is the essential feature of the device that with it several control sheets simultaneously or independently of each other can be held.
