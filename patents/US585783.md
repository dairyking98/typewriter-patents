## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen shift on a two-type machine needs steady position and locking.

**Solution:** A spring plunger sets the platen position, with a shift key moving against it and a normally locked platen lock.

**How it works:** A rock-shaft arm with engaging portions each side of its axis is pressed by a plunger; the shift key moves the platen against spring stress; a lock unlocks automatically on shifting.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** rock-shaft; spring plunger; shift key; platen frame; lock; shift rod

**Improves on:**

- Remington: improved shifting and locking
