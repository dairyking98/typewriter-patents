## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On the standard machine, spacing down the page for headings means unhooking the brake from its spring lever, which hurts fingertips and damages the spring.

**Solution:** Fit a friction coupling between the platen and its ratchet wheel that a screw-on release plate frees, so the platen can be turned alone by the hand wheel.

**How it works:** A graduated wheel is screwed to the platen end. Spring-plate-mounted friction pieces with small teeth stand out through holes in this wheel and bear on teeth in the bore of the platen ratchet wheel, locking them together. Screwing the knurled release plate down presses a pressure plate whose three pins push the spring plate inward, sinking the friction pieces into the holes. The graduated wheel and platen then turn freely relative to the ratchet wheel. Unscrewing the plate re-locks them. This also lets the paper be positioned when loading.
