## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction; Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon; for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** The reel carrier is made to plug onto the lifting rods, and ribbon step switching happens before each lift so the two motions do not interfere.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On calculating machines with ribbons that also lift and lower, the reel carrier is tied to the gearbox and cannot be separated or exchanged.

**Solution:** The reel carrier is made to plug onto the lifting rods, and ribbon step switching happens before each lift so the two motions do not interfere.
