## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Engraving type wheels is slow and costly, and embossing with a fixed die slips because the workpiece diameter shrinks.

**Solution:** The die is built from radially moving punches that shift during embossing, so the effective die diameter keeps the circumference ratio constant and the type comes out cleanly.

That is why it has already been proposed to have type wheels to produce by embossing, and this is done by having a roller-shaped and by a drive rotated die on the rod-shaped, also through a drive unrolls a rotated workpiece. In this known device the die and the workpiece are driven at the same angular speed. In order for the types to come out cleanly on the type wheel, the peripheral speed has to be keep the same between the die and the workpiece during the stamping process. Otherwise, there will be slippage at the point of contact between the die and the workpiece which affects the quality of the embossing. The mere fact that the die and let the workpiece rotate at the same angular speed is for this not sufficient, as the diameter of the part of the workpiece to be machined becomes smaller during the embossing.
