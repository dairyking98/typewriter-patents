## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Continuous checks and stubs need a roll of paper supported and tensioned as it feeds.

**Solution:** A rod bolted under the carriage table carries a roll core with a spring-loaded tension, so the roll feeds slowly and can be rewound.
