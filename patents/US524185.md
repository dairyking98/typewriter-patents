## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In the patent’s own words:** My improvement relates particularly to means for supporting reciprocating parts of mechanism, such as saws, planers, and typewriters, the object being to support reciprocating parts of machinery in such manner as to reduce friction to the greatest extent and to allow the least possible deviation of the reciprocating part fromits chosen path.

**How it works (reading):** Adjustable cone-ended wheels at each side of the part run in channels of two rails, adjustable vertically and laterally.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reciprocating parts such as saws, planers or typewriter carriages need low friction and the least possible deviation from their path.

**Solution:** Adjustable cone-ended wheels at each side of the part run in channels of two rails, adjustable vertically and laterally.

**How it works:** Rails along each side of the sliding part have a channel. Wheels with tapered peripheries and cone-ended axles sit in recesses and engage the part. Screw plugs above and below each axle raise or lower each wheel to align it with the others, and all can be moved together. One rail is held by slotted ears and clamping screws and can be moved toward or away from the part by a screw bolt with collars through a post, for lateral adjustment.

**Parts:** rail; channel; wheel; axle; adjusting plug; slotted ear; clamping screw; adjusting screw bolt; post
