## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**How it works (reading):** The whole type guide, or its guide legs, is adjustable in height and along the type body so each lever strikes a fresh guide point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Worn type guide lets type levers wobble, degrading spacing and line straightness.

**Solution:** The whole type guide, or its guide legs, is adjustable in height and along the type body so each lever strikes a fresh guide point.
