## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** Each ribbon reel has an independent core with a releasable coupling; when one reel runs empty, the coupling of the other reel engages, so the device works on any machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal devices need special parts on the typewriter itself.

**Solution:** Each ribbon reel has an independent core with a releasable coupling; when one reel runs empty, the coupling of the other reel engages, so the device works on any machine.
