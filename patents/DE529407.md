## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A mainspring's outer end works loose from its housing wall at low tension, so the spring fails to wind properly.

**Solution:** The outer spring end hooks into a temporary detachable connection held by spring tension, and it re-engages after a set rotation.

**How it works:** A hinged eye on the outer spring end locks against a pin on the housing, releasing when the spring is fully tensioned.
