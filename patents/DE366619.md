## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Margin stop riders and ratchet-switch parts are made from stamped sheet; a U-shaped rider clips onto the toothed margin-stop rail and is retained by a bolt, so riders are fitted on the assembled machine, and one screw bolt doubles as spring guide.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Margin stops built from machined solid parts need precision work and are costly to make and assemble.

**Solution:** Margin stop riders and ratchet-switch parts are made from stamped sheet; a U-shaped rider clips onto the toothed margin-stop rail and is retained by a bolt, so riders are fitted on the assembled machine, and one screw bolt doubles as spring guide.
