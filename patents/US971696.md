## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjustable column stops for tabulating are slow to set and clear when the work changes, and deciding where to set them is troublesome.

**Solution:** Tabulating mechanism that quickly determines the carriage arrest positions and sets the tabulator stops accordingly, with a means to clear all previously set stops at once.
