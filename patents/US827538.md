## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the invention is to improve the touch of the keys and increase the speed of operation, and another is toministill-maintain a quick the printing device.

**How it works (reading):** Typebars with rear-striking type are actuated through sub-levers connected to a slotted swinging link-hanger, with type-bar and link-hanger supporting rings and a retaining plate, so the key depression is controlled.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The touch of the keys is heavy and the printing blow is unsteady, and the aim is to improve the touch and raise speed.

**Solution:** Typebars with rear-striking type are actuated through sub-levers connected to a slotted swinging link-hanger, with type-bar and link-hanger supporting rings and a retaining plate, so the key depression is controlled.
