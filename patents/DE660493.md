## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter tab device The invention relates to a Tabulator device on typewriters with provided on the frame and the carriage interacting strokes and one locking the pressed tab keys Device together with one that comes into effect when the stops work together Device for releasing the lock. In the known devices that had to return the locking device required force when pressing the tab key can be applied, whereby a there is a considerable burden on the operator. According to the invention, this disadvantage is now eliminated by the Unlocking device from one movably arranged on the stop of the frame Member is brought into effect when the stop arranged on the carriage hits on the stop carrying it is moved with respect to the latter and in doing so on springs or the like serving in a manner known per se for damping, which at the same time serve to actuate the unlocking elements of the tab key. Through this training a simple unlocking of the locking device is obtained, which in none Way makes the keystroke difficult.
