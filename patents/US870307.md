## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type-wheel typewriter with a hammer needs a ribbon feed that winds the ribbon between two spools beside the type wheel.

**Solution:** A bifurcated pawl on the key-operated rocking frame drives one of two ratchet wheels on the spool shafts, and a knob shifts the pawl to the other ratchet to reverse winding.

**How it works:** The typewheel, carriage feed and hammer are all operated from the key levers, which bear on a cross bar of a rocking frame. Two vertical spool shafts either side of the typewheel carry counterpart ratchet wheels and springs to resist overwinding. A bifurcated pawl between the ratchets is pivoted to an arm of the rocking frame, so each key stroke rocks it and advances the wound spool. When one spool is nearly empty, a knob on a yoke shifts the pawl out of one ratchet and into the other. Small rollers guide the ribbon between the spools, passing between the type wheel and a cushion strip shielding the hammer.
