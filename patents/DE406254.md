## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Letter spacing became uneven at fast typing because the carriage only advanced after the typebar was about 15 mm back from the platen.

**Solution:** A roller angle lever lets the escapement anchor disconnect from the universal rail at high stroke speed so the carriage steps almost immediately after printing; the same link is lifted by accent keys to stop spacing.

**How it works:** The universal rail pushes the pivoted escapement anchor through a roller angle lever. When a typebar is struck slowly, the anchor swings and the loose tooth follows the typebar back, so the carriage advances once the type is about 15 mm from the platen. At fast strokes, the anchor hits a limit screw so the angle lever is swung up and the anchor separates from the rail and springs back at once, advancing the carriage when the typebar has moved only a fraction of a millimetre back. A lever linked by pull rod to an accent key also lifts the angle lever, so no spacing occurs for an accent.
