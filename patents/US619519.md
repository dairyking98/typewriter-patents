## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Series of check amount numbers need an apparatus that burns characters into fabric; the earlier apparatus used several independently adjustable disks.

**Solution:** A pivoted frame carries one rotatable disk with characters of thin metal strip on its periphery, and the frame shifts laterally after each character is burned.
