## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type need cleaning, and the cleaning brush must be positioned and raised to the type.

**Solution:** A bar on brackets clamped to the machine frame carries a brush yoke; a threaded shaft with winch moves the brush up and down, and a pin-and-slot drive turns the brush hub.

**How it works:** Winch-driven threaded shaft raises and lowers a rotating brush carried by a yoke on the bar.
