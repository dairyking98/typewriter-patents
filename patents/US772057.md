## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Extra-wide typewriter carriages tip and sag at the ends of travel and are hard to support.

**Solution:** Several ball-bearing rollers along the carriage front run on a front track, with two always engaged so one acts as fulcrum and one as a stop, and a stiffened shift rail.

**How it works:** A rail on the carriage front carries lugs with eccentric bushings holding roller bolts with cones and a V-grooved ball-bearing ring. The rollers run on tracks across the frame while the rear rides on a rod. At an extreme position two rollers leave the track and two stay, one as fulcrum, the overhanging carriage throwing another up against an upper track as a stop. Turning the eccentric bushings aligns the rollers, and a stamped stiffening frame stops the long shift rail sagging.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
