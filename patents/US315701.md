## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Space after a word has to be made separately from printing its last letter.

**Solution:** A supplemental pawl with a bell-crank trip, worked by the space bar levers, lets the carriage advance two spaces when the last letter of a word is printed.

**How it works:** The vibrating escapement pawl G is pulled by the key-bar cross bar into the ratchet bar, while a supplemental pawl P behind it is drawn back and then forced back a tooth by a spring Q until stopped by a bell-crank lever T. Normally the carriage advances one letter until the supplemental pawl hits an offset on the main pawl. When the space bar levers U and rod W trip lever T, the supplemental pawl is free to catch a further tooth, adding the word space with the last letter.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** ratchet bar; vibrating pawl; supplemental pawl; bell-crank lever; space bar; lever bar; flat spring; coiled spring

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph spacing: word space combined with last letter stroke
