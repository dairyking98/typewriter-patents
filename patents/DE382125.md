## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Recording piano playing on paper needs a writer that marks the staff and notes automatically when keys are struck.

**Solution:** Each key contact energizes an electromagnet that moves a writing lever with an ink pen fed from an ink tank by tubing, marking notes on a moving paper tape, while a separate roller inks the staff lines.
