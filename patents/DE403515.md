## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**How it works (reading):** The platen roller and its frame run in ball bearings with precisely fitted rings and tension, so the balls prevent longitudinal and vibrational movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper roller play in its bearings causes rattling and loss of writing accuracy at high stroke rates.

**Solution:** The platen roller and its frame run in ball bearings with precisely fitted rings and tension, so the balls prevent longitudinal and vibrational movement.
