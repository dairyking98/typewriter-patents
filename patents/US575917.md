## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards and envelopes cannot be held to the platen so that they conform to its surface for clean printing.

**Solution:** A card holder mounted on the platen and rotating with it, held to the platen surface and adjustable against the line-spacing ratchet.
