## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator needs automatic knowledge of the sheet position, and a signal when the sheet reaches a set position.

**Solution:** Indicating means (indices, visual and audible signals, and a machine-lock) are controlled by the feed-roll release or pawl-engaging mechanism that positions the sheet.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** feed-roll release; pawl-engaging mechanism; indices; visual signal; audible signal; machine-lock
