## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Justifying typeset lines by hand needs a printer to choose space-types of the right size for each interword space, which is skilled and slow.

**Solution:** A type-justifying machine that fills the line with space-types of suitable size and number, so the interword spaces are completed mechanically. The first 3000 chars describe the problem and the object only.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
