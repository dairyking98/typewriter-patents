## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine column stop skipping) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a simple and reliable device on calculating machines to skip one or more of the jump movement of the Paper carriage inhibiting column plate stops (tabs) or one or more Columns of the calculation form.

**How it works (reading):** A button, pressed with the power drive, sets an adjusting member so the switching tooth swings out of its path for the skipped stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column stops on a calculating machine cannot be skipped during a run without changing the form.

**Solution:** A button, pressed with the power drive, sets an adjusting member so the switching tooth swings out of its path for the skipped stop.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Equipment on calculating machines to skip column regulator stops The subject of the invention is a simple and reliable device on calculating machines to skip one or more of the jump movement of the Paper carriage inhibiting column plate stops (tabs) or one or more Columns of the calculation form. The main inventive feature of the new facility is that by means of a special button, expediently at the same time as the power drive is switched on an adjusting member is brought into the operative position, which in the subsequent reverse gear of the machine with the mediation of the usual, each time at the beginning of the usual machine operation of the car jump triggering the stop link for the tabs (switching tooth) (swinging out) ratchet lever pivoted a release lever, which with this pivoting the switching tooth swings out of its path and through appropriately designed organs of the paper carriage is held in the pivoted position until the swung out The switching tooth has passed the tab or tabs to be skipped.
