B. made of felt, Inking roller rotatably seated on a feed tube provided with passage openings is fed. The invention consists of the known devices of this type in that the central axis of the storage container and the axis of rotation of the inking roller serving part of the supply tube parallel and at such a distance from each other run that between the scope of the. rotatable inking roller and the opposite Wall of the dye container a ribbon guide is formed. This design of the coloring device results in a particularly simple one constructive structure of the small apparatus and a safe, easy pulling in and ribbon guide permitting replacement of the ribbon. The drawing shows an embodiment of the invention in three figures shown. It shows Fig. Z the device in a longitudinal section, Fig. 2 and 3 shows the attachment of the device and the ribbon guide on a typewriter in plan view or side view.
