## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Moistening or coloring typewriter ribbons needs a simple, compact attachment that still lets the ribbon be threaded and replaced easily.

**Solution:** A dye container with a felt feed tube carries an inking roller on its axis; the roller and the opposite wall of the container are parallel, leaving a slot through which the ribbon runs and is inked.
