## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A screwed-in platen journal loosens under the forces of line switching, causing uneven line spacing.

**Solution:** A plain cylindrical journal carries a displaceable clamping cone that a threaded nut drives into a conical bore in the platen flange, locking the platen firmly.
