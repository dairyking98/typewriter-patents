## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary keyboards force hand movement and eye watching of the keys, limiting speed.

**Solution:** A chord keyboard in which characters belong to several groups and each character is printed only by pressing two or more keys together, one per group, using only finger movements.

**How it works:** Ten keys give 5x5=25 combinations from one finger per hand, extendable by extra keys or by double or multiple control for upper case. Combination of keys can be via electric contacts in series operating a magnet, pneumatic valves in series feeding a piston, or mechanically by levers, wires over rollers or differential gears adding up the stroke needed. Each character corresponds to a unique combination of groups. Only the keyboard and control change; the printing mechanism is as in existing typewriters. The idea is borrowed from perforated-strip control of line-casting machines.
