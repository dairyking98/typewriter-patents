## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locks for subsidiary keys, e.g. for shift keys

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in that class of type-writing machines having a carriage-support which is shiftable from its normal position either forward or rearward, or both forward and rearward from said normal position, so that the platen or paper thereagainst will be, as desired, in position to receive the impact thereupon of any one of two or three types carried by a single typebar.

**How it works (reading):** Shift the carriage-support forward or back with cam levers, lock it down in either position, and limit travel with stops.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Double-shift (three-type) typebar machines need better shifting, locking, restoring and limiting of the carriage-support.

**Solution:** Shift the carriage-support forward or back with cam levers, lock it down in either position, and limit travel with stops.

**How it works:** The carriage-support is a pair of rails on parallel swing-bars. Two cam levers with forward shift keys press on a roller stud on the support's extension to swing it rearward or forward. A pivoted lever with roller studs under the key levers locks the shift. Return springs and adjustable stops restore the normal position and fix the shifted limits.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** carriage; carriage-support; swing-bar; rail; cam lever; shift key; locking lever; stop
