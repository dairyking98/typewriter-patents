## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cleaning typewriter types with kneadable cleaning putty is messy, and the putty cannot be reshaped without soiling the hands.

**Solution:** A dab ball is held between two curved end disks on sleeves that turn on a handled shaft and are clamped by a nut, so the ball can be rolled on a flat surface to knead it.
