**How it works (reading):** The existing ribbon-reverse lever releases the shift latch.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Machines where the shifted frame stays up until the next character need a simple way to release the shift without printing, without changing the standard keyboard.

**Solution:** The existing ribbon-reverse lever releases the shift latch.

**How it works:** A shift key lifts the frame carrying the carriage. A two-armed lever on the tab-lever axis latches under a plate on the frame so it stays up. After the next character a bracket flicks the lever clear and the frame falls. A screw adjusts the entrainment. The ribbon-reverse lever is arranged to trip the latch voluntarily, for a mistaken shift or to end capitals.
