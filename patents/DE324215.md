## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the present Invention is a switching device for the ribbon drive of typewriters, whose individual parts are arranged and designed so that their directions of force and movement all lie in parallel planes, so that this is inevitable with the known species Corners of the parts when motion is transferred from one plane to a right or oblique plane is omitted.

**How it works (reading):** A switching device keeps all force and motion directions in parallel planes; a pin-and-lever arrangement alternately engages the right or left drive shaft to wind the ribbon one way or the other.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon-drive switching parts had corners where motion passed between planes, adding parts and complexity.

**Solution:** A switching device keeps all force and motion directions in parallel planes; a pin-and-lever arrangement alternately engages the right or left drive shaft to wind the ribbon one way or the other.
