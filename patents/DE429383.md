**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocks between keys, e.g. without detent arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a new one Type of key locks for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Button levers carry spring-loaded locking levers that engage the locking members independently of the key levers, and a pull or push on the type lever linkage swings them out to release the lock at the right moment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier key locks forced keys to be struck at equal intervals, which blocked free finger movement.

**Solution:** Button levers carry spring-loaded locking levers that engage the locking members independently of the key levers, and a pull or push on the type lever linkage swings them out to release the lock at the right moment.
