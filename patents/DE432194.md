## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In calculating-machine type capsules each digit type has its own coil return spring, which takes space, complicates assembly and breaks under hammer blows; an outside spring arrangement makes removal awkward.

**Solution:** Make the capsule a self-contained unit with the ten types, a wire spring and a cross pressure rail so it mounts and removes as one piece.

**How it works:** The capsule's side walls have rectangular recesses housing ten digit types, each with a notch. A single bent-wire spring has one leg hooked around a pin and held by a bent end in a bottom plate, and the other leg pressing a rail against shoulders of all the types. A hammer drives a type to the paper, and the spring and rail return it to rest with its head against the carrier front. The capsule is hung on the type carrier by a punched tongue and held by one screw.
