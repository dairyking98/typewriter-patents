## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Friction clutches with outwardly-movable clutching members co-operating with the inner surface of a drum or the like shaped as linked or separately-pivoted segments

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates generally to clutches operating by the jamming action of balls or rollers, and particularly to clutches of this type for securing and breaking rotative ct ntinuity of the platen roll with the linespacing mechanism in typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Balls or rollers are held in races milled into one bearing part, with springs and sliding pieces, so the clutch engages and disengages the platen roll from line spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen cannot be turned freely of its feed unless the coupling can be broken at all times.

**Solution:** Balls or rollers are held in races milled into one bearing part, with springs and sliding pieces, so the clutch engages and disengages the platen roll from line spacing.
