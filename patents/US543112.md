## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabular printing needed many space-lever strokes, and the means of stopping the carriage at varied points were complex.

**Solution:** A rack-bar with a cam on it and a pivoted pawl lets the carriage stop at adjustable points, with fewer parts than before.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
