## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**How it works (reading):** Locking bars with circular bolts and deeper guide brackets let the paper roller frame move vertically with low friction and be removed as a unit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper-roller frames on carriages are hard to remove, and the axle wears the guide pieces, so vertical motion becomes inaccurate.

**Solution:** Locking bars with circular bolts and deeper guide brackets let the paper roller frame move vertically with low friction and be removed as a unit.
