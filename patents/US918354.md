## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen must be detachable and rotated by hand, with a ratchet that locates it.

**Solution:** The platen shaft is detachably coupled to the platen, and a ratchet wheel on a sleeve engages recesses in the platen end, driven by a knob.
