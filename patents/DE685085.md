## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material; Manifolding or like arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to ribbon devices on calculating machines, typewriters and the like.

**How it works (reading):** Balancing members touch both runs of the tape; the run under more tension moves and relieves itself while enlarging the slack on the other run.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon tension in the two webs of an endless tape becomes unequal as one reel empties.

**Solution:** Balancing members touch both runs of the tape; the run under more tension moves and relieves itself while enlarging the slack on the other run.
