## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, more particularly to that class of machines in which the impression is effected by pressure, as distinguished from a blow or striking action.

**How it works (reading):** A bank of ten pivoted type sectors with eight characters each slides sideways to bring one to the centre, a toggle throws it up to the chosen character, a bolt locks it in a notch, and a spring-driven lever presses it to the paper.

## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cheap, simple pressure-printing typewriter was wanted in which the impression is made under the operator's eye and the type is held in exact alignment.

**Solution:** A bank of ten pivoted type sectors with eight characters each slides sideways to bring one to the centre, a toggle throws it up to the chosen character, a bolt locks it in a notch, and a spring-driven lever presses it to the paper.

**How it works:** Forty keys act through five roller levers and two longitudinal levers on bell-crank impact heads that squeeze the sector frame left or right, bringing one of five sectors to the centre. The same key then turns a rock-shaft whose toggle throws the central sector up a distance set by a slat and stop-rod chosen by the key's cam, so the wanted one of eight characters sits at the print point. As it rises, the sector passes an inking pad (a pivoted block with a ribbon over felt) and a brush that removes surplus ink. A bolt then enters a notch beside the character to lock it, a catch is released and a spring-loaded lever presses the sector to the paper. The second set of five sectors is brought in by a shift bar that slides the whole frame, and a friction clutch on a bar steps the carriage with a space graded by cutting the rear ends of the key bars.
