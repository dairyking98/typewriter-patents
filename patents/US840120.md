## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Electrically operated typewriters need a controlled select-then-print sequence from each key, including type-head shifting, ribbon and a stationary keyboard for a travelling flat-platen writing head.

**Solution:** A key with several contact stages first energizes a selecting magnet, then a power magnet, then cuts both, so a positioned typebar or type-head shifter is driven by the power device.

**How it works:** Early key travel completes the circuit of one of a set of selecting magnets, attracting an armature-carrying pawl or lever. Further travel switches the current to a power magnet whose armature moves a universal power device that engages the selected lever and operates the typebar or shifts the type-head. Final travel breaks the circuit and the parts return as the key rises. A typebar carries a movable head with several types; an actuator beside the head shifts it. The aim is also a flat-platen book machine whose writing head moves over the paper under a fixed keyboard. This is the companion to US826485.
