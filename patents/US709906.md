## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (web printing and severance) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to`permitv :rapid and convenient printing on the end of a roll or web of paper and to secure the speedy severance of the printed portions from the web.

**How it works (reading):** A roll-supporting attachment on an Elliott & Hatch machine extends the web across the platen, confines it with the vertically movable frame that also carries the writing mechanism, and shears the printed portion off with a fixed blade and a cooperating blade on the frame when the frame is raised and lowered.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing on a roll or web of paper needs rapid, convenient feeding across the platen and quick severance of the printed part from the web.

**Solution:** A roll-supporting attachment on an Elliott & Hatch machine extends the web across the platen, confines it with the vertically movable frame that also carries the writing mechanism, and shears the printed portion off with a fixed blade and a cooperating blade on the frame when the frame is raised and lowered.
