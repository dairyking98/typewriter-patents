## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** In segment-mounted type-bar machines a bar returning to rest rebounds, and a rebounding bar can interfere with the next bar operated when keys are struck rapidly.

**Solution:** The type-bar actuating link is arranged so the bar moves slowly at the start of the key stroke and accelerates as it nears the platen, so the bar cannot rebound into the path of adjacent bars.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
