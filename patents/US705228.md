## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber type-blocks are glued to their handles, and the glue is broken down by the corrosive chemicals in the ink, so the type-block drops off during use.

**Solution:** A metallic handle into which the rubber type-block is inserted and held by flanges, so the handle's weight presses the die onto the surface for inking and printing.
