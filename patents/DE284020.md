## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Devices changing the stroke of the ribbon fork for two-colour ribbons use bearing holes (needing exact fit) or open slots (not secured on all sides), so some type can strike beside the ribbon.

**Solution:** Use two slotted bearing arms that alternately engage one of two pins on the ribbon-fork lift lever, so the pin is held on all four sides in either ribbon position.

**How it works:** Key strokes move a two-part universal rail; the upper part drives the ribbon feed and the ribbon-fork lift lever, the lower the carriage escapement. A shift rod rotates a common gear meshing with two gear segments, swinging one forked bearing arm onto its pin while the other swings clear, so the lift lever pivots around one pin or the other to bring the upper or lower half of the two-colour ribbon to the print point. The upper rail part can also be pulled forward by a handle so the transmission lever no longer hits it, for stencil (wax paper) work.

**Improves on:**
