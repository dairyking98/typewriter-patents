## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Stenographic typewriter striking is noisy, and each type bar needs a simple way to be engaged with its key and returned by spring.

**Solution:** The type-carrying ends of the type bars are placed close to the platen so the stroke is short and quiet, each bar is engaged with a pivoted universal bar, and a spring returns any displaced bar to its normal position.
