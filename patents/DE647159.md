## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter stored in a desk side cabinet needs room, but the lever linkage that lowers the support plate takes space above it, so only the smallest machines fit.

**Solution:** The support plate runs on rails carried by angle levers pivoted below it, with vertical supports, so the full cabinet width stays free above the plate in both positions.
