## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper feed rolls on a writing machine are not clearly positively driven or aligned with the cylindrical platen (OCR too damaged to state the defect exactly).

**Solution:** Front and rear feed rolls set in a frame beside the platen, pressed against it by springs, with a means of driving both feed rolls and a pan paper support; text is largely unreadable.
