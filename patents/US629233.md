## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writing machinesin which the types are provided on the surface of a cylinder to be shifted, rotated, and forced against the paper by the depression of keys;

**How it works (reading):** A type-cylinder that is shifted, rotated and pressed against the paper by combinations of two simultaneously depressed keys, one from each hand, with a shift key selecting sections of the cylinder.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboards with a key per type are hard to learn; the inventor wants a minimum of keys, also usable by the blind.

**Solution:** A type-cylinder that is shifted, rotated and pressed against the paper by combinations of two simultaneously depressed keys, one from each hand, with a shift key selecting sections of the cylinder.

**How it works:** Two key sections, one per hand, with 4 or 5 keys per hand are used so the hands rest stationary. Pressing one finger of each hand together selects one of 25 types in a longitudinal section of the cylinder. The cylinder holds, for example, 3 sections, and a shift key brings one section to print so the same keys control a different 25 types. The cylinders are on a shaft shifted and rotated in a rocking lever frame on a shaft with another rocking frame, a toothed segment lever with steps, pawls and springs. A thumb lever rocks the segment and pawl against steps to set the cylinder position, and a thumbpiece releases it. The drawn machine is duplex with two cylinders and two platens (one for blind characters).
