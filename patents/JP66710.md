## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** After each line the operator of a Japanese typewriter must wind the paper cylinder across the top and bottom margin and slide it to the next line by hand.

**Solution:** A spring-driven toothed wheel with a toothless sector steps the cylinder per character through pawls, and at the line end spins it across the margin while a cam on the wheel changes the sideways rack pawls to feed one line.

**How it works:** A spring case on the frame, geared to a rack, always urges the cylinder frame sideways. A second spring through gears turns the cylinder shaft, held by a toothed wheel and two pawls that release it one character pitch per printing stroke. When the last character is printed, the pawl meets the toothless part of the wheel and the cylinder spins across the margin to the first printing point. At that moment a cam on the wheel drops a roller arm, turning a cross shaft so a fixed pawl leaves a rack and a sliding pawl takes it, letting the frame slide one line. The number of teeth sets characters per line and the sliding pawl travel sets line spacing, and a lever lifts the rack to return the cylinder.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Improvement in the continuous printing device for a Japanese-text typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. In this invention, a spring that gives the paper cylinder of a Japanese-text typewriter its rotation for lengthwise feed is kept always wound; a toothed wheel is fixed on the same shaft as the cylinder and its rotation [printed: "its wheel"] is stopped by pawls; the engaging and releasing of the pawls gives the cylinder its lengthwise feed rotation step by step. At a part of the wheel where teeth are missing, the pawls do not engage the wheel, so nothing stops the cylinder from turning; the cylinder is turned by the spring and stops where the pawls meet the teeth again. At the same time the sideways-feed pawl is released and the cylinder slides sideways to the place of the next line. In this way the first printing point of the next line is brought automatically to a fixed position, reducing the attention printing requires and increasing printing efficiency.
