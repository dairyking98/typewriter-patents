## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines of that class in which the papercarriage as it is advanced step by step to bring the paper into position for successive action of the types thereon is moved through varying distances to permit the use of types having varying widths of faces and whether bearing single letters or groups of letters.
