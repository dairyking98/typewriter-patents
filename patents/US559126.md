## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines of that class in which the papercarriage as it is advanced step by step to bring the paper into position for successive action of the types thereon is moved through varying distances to permit the use of types having varying widths of faces and whether bearing single letters or groups of letters.

**How it works (reading):** The shaft with a fixed and a loose spring-coupled ratchet is controlled by cams that also take account of the shifted position of the carriage.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Variable-width carriage feed must also work on shift-carriage machines where one key serves two characters and the shift moves the carriage.

**Solution:** The shaft with a fixed and a loose spring-coupled ratchet is controlled by cams that also take account of the shifted position of the carriage.

**How it works:** As in the companion Ellis patents, the carriage spring turns a horizontal shaft through a rack and pinion. A fixed ratchet and a loose ratchet joined by a coil spring, with stop pins, are stopped by key-lever actuated cams so each key advances the carriage by its type width. A shifting bar linked to the shift key adapts the feed devices to upper and lower case, shown on a Remington type machine.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** feed shaft; fixed ratchet; loose ratchet; coil spring; stop pin; cam; shifting bar; key lever; carriage

**Improves on:**

- Serial 496,570 and 502,791 (Ellis): extends the variable feed to two-character typebars with a carriage shift
