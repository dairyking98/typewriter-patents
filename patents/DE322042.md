## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (secret telegraphic transmission, add-on) · **Subject (patent classes):** Encryption being effected by mechanical apparatus, e.g. rotating cams, switches, keytape punchers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Its purpose is to make the subject of the main patent usable on all sides.

**How it works (reading):** The meaning of the immobile contacts behind the keys is reordered instead of the key names, so the keys keep their names and no secondary keyboard is needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** After the key meanings are changed for secret telegraphy, the letters must be searched for elsewhere on the keyboard, which slows rapid transmission.

**Solution:** The meaning of the immobile contacts behind the keys is reordered instead of the key names, so the keys keep their names and no secondary keyboard is needed.
