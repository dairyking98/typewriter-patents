## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Price cards and the like need to be hand printed neatly and quickly with rubber type that stays in order without operator care.

**Solution:** Two rotating wheels of pivoted rubber type on a slide are turned by hand to bring the wanted type to the bottom, locked by a centering pin in the dial, struck by finger pressure, and stepped along by an oscillating space rod.

**How it works:** Rubber type blocks on legs are pivoted around a wheel, each held by a coil spring and offset so a finger can press behind it. The operator turns the wheel until the wanted type is lowermost, and a spring-loaded push-key drives a conical pin into a matching hole in the dial to lock it. Pressing the type with a finger prints it on the paper beneath, and the spring restores it. An ink roll touches the type as the wheel turns. A second wheel, mounted back to back, gives more characters, and the carrier is turned half a turn to use it. The slide runs on a guide rod and a rocking space rod with alternating studs, worked by two finger levers, advances it one space at a time.
