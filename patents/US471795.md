## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvement on the inventor's earlier panel type-bar writer; type-bars carrying several characters stacked one above another must be guided true to the printing point without wabbling or excess friction.

**Solution:** Wide panel-shaped type-bars sliding endwise radially toward the platen, with bevelled type-blocks deflected by fixed bevelled guides, and the platen raised and lowered to choose among the stacked characters.

**How it works:** Key-levers act through rocker-arms on type-bars that slide endwise in vertical slots in a rib, their ends running between guides, and strike the paper on the platen by end thrust. Most bars carry two or more characters one above the other on a bevelled type-block, and fixed bevelled guide blocks at the print point deflect the block straight onto the printing point. The T-shaped rocker-arm also works the spacing mechanism, with a dog and sliding pawl engaging a rack on the paper carriage, and a spring returning the rocker shaft. Because the characters are stacked, the platen is mounted in a frame that reciprocates vertically. A modification feeds the carriage endwise.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** base; key-lever; rocker-arm; type-bar; type-block; bevelled guide; guide plate; platen; platen frame; paper carriage; dog; sliding pawl; rack; spring; ribbon spool

**Improves on:**

- Kidder application Serial No. 386,133 (1891): simplified construction of panel type-bars and guides
