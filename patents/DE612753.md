## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register double check printing)

**How it works (reading):** The amount hammer makes two working strokes for an independent amount and for the multi-item total, but only one stroke for a dependent line item, selected by the business-type key and its switching mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On double-check cash registers the amount hammer strikes twice on the same line for dependent line items, which makes those items hard to read.

**Solution:** The amount hammer makes two working strokes for an independent amount and for the multi-item total, but only one stroke for a dependent line item, selected by the business-type key and its switching mechanism.
