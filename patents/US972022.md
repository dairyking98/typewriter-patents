## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting and tabulating machines, and particularly to means for controlling the speed of the carriage when it is released from its letterfeeding devices.

**How it works (reading):** Centrifugal governor head with brake shoes pressed by weighted levers against a fixed friction cylinder, acting only in letter-feeding direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage speed after release from the letter-feeding escapement is uncontrolled, and the prior brake is complex.

**Solution:** Centrifugal governor head with brake shoes pressed by weighted levers against a fixed friction cylinder, acting only in letter-feeding direction.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
