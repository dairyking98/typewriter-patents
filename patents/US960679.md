## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Blows of the types on the platen are loud.

**Solution:** The platen rubber shell is lined with cork and the chamber is filled with sawdust, held between fiber plugs, with a central tube through which the platen axle passes.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** rubber platen shell; cork lining; sawdust packing; fiber plugs; central tube; platen axle; platen heads
