## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement on the latter Contraption.

**How it works (reading):** A shift rod lifts the clutch pawl and holds it until the next forward platen rotation sets it against a housing wall, so reverse rotation has no effect on the display.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The existing paper-end display could be knocked out by reverse platen rotation, so a wrong setting could occur.

**Solution:** A shift rod lifts the clutch pawl and holds it until the next forward platen rotation sets it against a housing wall, so reverse rotation has no effect on the display.
