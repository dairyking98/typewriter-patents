## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for attaching machines to baseboards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter support housing with improved means for quickly connecting the removable cover with the base plate of the housing with the help of joints that the base plate and the Hold the lids together when the case is open and positioned so that they can the cover can be completely removed from the base plate if desired.

**How it works (reading):** The hinge pin sits in an annular groove on one joint part, and a spring-loaded rounded catch in the other sleeve holds the cover, so it only separates under a set force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter case covers hinged to the base can detach accidentally, and earlier locking levers are awkward to operate with one hand.

**Solution:** The hinge pin sits in an annular groove on one joint part, and a spring-loaded rounded catch in the other sleeve holds the cover, so it only separates under a set force.
