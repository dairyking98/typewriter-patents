## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-position scales and arch supports either fall too short, have to be in several parts, or interfere with the protective cover when folded down.

**Solution:** A one-piece arch support pivots about an axis on a transverse arm running along a flat guide bar behind the paper table, folds flat in rest position, and a resilient arm locks it in use position.
