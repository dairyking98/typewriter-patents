## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention pertains to inking devices for type-writing machines, designed to permit the ready substitution of a distinctive or difierent color for that normally used in the type-writing machine.

**How it works (reading):** A separate inking ribbon of the same width, fitted with clamps and a spreader, is folded and mounted to substitute a different colour without changing the built-in ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing in a distinctive colour requires either a multi-colour ribbon or a complex ribbon shift.

**Solution:** A separate inking ribbon of the same width, fitted with clamps and a spreader, is folded and mounted to substitute a different colour without changing the built-in ribbon.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
