## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies; Western type typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** The first object of this invention is a simple and effective typewriter in which the platen, carriage, escapement ("controlling mechanism") and ribbon mechanism can all be moved vertically, so that each type head can print several different characters, and in which these parts are so arranged that for carrying and similar purposes they can all be folded over on top of the key levers, making the whole machine smaller than in use;

**How it works (reading):** A typebar machine whose platen, carriage, escapement and ribbon mechanism lift together on a slide to several locked heights (each type head carries figure, capital and small letter) and fold over the key levers for transport.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A conventional typewriter is large to carry and hard to repair; the inventor wants a light, strong, front-strike machine that folds flat over its key levers yet looks full size when set up.

**Solution:** A typebar machine whose platen, carriage, escapement and ribbon mechanism lift together on a slide to several locked heights (each type head carries figure, capital and small letter) and fold over the key levers for transport.

**How it works:** Stamped sheet-metal key levers hang on hook plates in three rows and, by links, throw individually removable typebars to the printing point. Shift levers raise the slide, carriage and ribbon mechanism to set heights where locks hold them, so the three characters on each head can print. The carriage runs on caged balls between a slider and a fixed slide and is drawn by a spiral spring in a drum with a strap, wound through a ratchet. A new escapement mechanism releases the carriage one space per stroke, and the same feed drives the ribbon spools. A ribbon lifter raises the ribbon freely whatever the shift position. Further parts are a line-space device with a stop lever, back-spacer, tabulator stops, line lock with overrun, quick carriage release, and a guide that steadies the type heads at the printing point.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. The first object of this invention is a simple and effective typewriter in which the platen, carriage, escapement ("controlling mechanism") and ribbon mechanism can all be moved vertically, so that each type head can print several different characters, and in which these parts are so arranged that for carrying and similar purposes they can all be folded over on top of the key levers, making the whole machine smaller than in use; a typewriter very simple and strong in construction and very light, which folded takes little room but when set up looks no different from a full-size machine; and a typewriter whose parts are easy to get at for repair.
