## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar mounting that prints exactly at one point through a type guide.

**Solution:** The typebar is pivoted at two points to two links on fulcrum rings so it turns a quarter and then rises nearly vertically to the guide.

**How it works:** Each type carrier has a type arm at one end and a rear arm at the other, pivoted to two links on two wire rings. The key lever pulls one link, which is also the operating lever; the carrier lifts, turns a quarter turn along a curved path, then rises almost vertically through a type guide to the platen. A felt pad above the fulcrum ring inks the type as it rests against it.
