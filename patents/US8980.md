## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A way for a person to put his thoughts or copied manuscript directly on paper in type, with letters spaced correctly even though the type differ in width.

**Solution:** A horizontal type wheel is turned by a lever until an index sits in the notch beside the wanted letter on a fixed circular rack, then the same lever pushes the wheel down to print on a paper cylinder that is stepped along its axis by the same stroke.

**How it works:** Upright type stand on the periphery of a horizontal wheel on a spring-supported shaft; a hand lever turns the wheel and an index swings over a circular rack with a notch and stamped letter for every character. Dropping the index into a notch and pressing the lever depresses the wheel so the type over the paper cylinder prints. The depression also rocks levers that push a plate with a one-way clutch gripping a rod on the cylinder carriage, so the cylinder advances before the type lands; a stationary clutch stops it slipping back. A roller beside the wheel is pushed by thick type and adds extra advance for wide letters. A weighted alarm rings two letters before line end, then the clutches are tripped, a spring returns the carriage, and a ratchet and pawl on the cylinder turn it one line.
