## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter-like lettering by duplicating roller) · **Subject (patent classes):** Duplicating or printing apparatus or machines for office or other commercial purposes, of special types or for particular purposes and not otherwise provided for

**How it works (reading):** Typewriter type is embossed on a metal sheet wrapped on a roller; the roller is inked and presses a paper web, which is cut to a set length by a timed cutter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typewriter-like lettering for duplicating needs a method that is quicker than hand copying.

**Solution:** Typewriter type is embossed on a metal sheet wrapped on a roller; the roller is inked and presses a paper web, which is cut to a set length by a timed cutter.
