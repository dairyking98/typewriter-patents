## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printed items on a calculator must be viewed, and the paper then needs to return to its place.

**Solution:** A lever engages the paper-advance drive through a sector and plate, advancing the tape to show the printout and returning it by the same amount.
