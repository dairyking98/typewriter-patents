## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to tally strip mechanisin for typewriting machines and its general object is to provide improved mechanism of the character specified.

**How it works (reading):** A divided platen with a second tally strip, its own line-space ratchet and pawl, and a counter indicating entries.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Listing work needs a second tally strip and a count, which ordinary typewriters lack.

**Solution:** A divided platen with a second tally strip, its own line-space ratchet and pawl, and a counter indicating entries.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
