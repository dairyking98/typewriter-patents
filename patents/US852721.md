## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods on side surfaces thereof, e.g. fixed thereto the types or dies being movable relative to the bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to writing'machines of the front strike variety, in which the typebars carry multiple types, provision being made for shifting the platen up and down to enable different types to print.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage track frame is carried on a key-operated vibrating frame in the base, guided by forward pivoted links and a vertical ball-and-groove guide so that it moves only up and down.

## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen-shifting carriage track frame of a Sun front-strike typewriter needed to be cheaper, lighter, more compact and to move freely without binding or tilting.

**Solution:** The carriage track frame is carried on a key-operated vibrating frame in the base, guided by forward pivoted links and a vertical ball-and-groove guide so that it moves only up and down.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on ball-bearing tracks mounted on a vertically shiftable track frame. A depending arm of that frame is pivoted to the rear of a horizontal vibrating frame journaled across the base, whose forward arm carries the capital and figure shift keys. Pushing the key rocks the frame and lifts the track frame and platen, with a spring partly balancing the weight. Two forward links pivoted to the machine frame stop fore-and-aft movement, and plain flanges running between vertical ball grooves stop sideways play. Adjustable props with pointed ends carry the track frame level, a fixed stop sets the capital position, and a pivoted stop is swung aside by the figure key to allow a full lift for figures.
