## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen carriages of typewriters have friction and wear and lack convenient line-space, paper-holding, margin and indicator devices.

**Solution:** A set of improved carriage details: low-friction bearing, selectable one/two/free platen rotation, tension roller release, margin stop, spacing-dog tension, shading device, line indicator and folding paper table.

**How it works:** The platen carriage runs on an improved bearing. Thumb wheels set the platen to rotate one or two line spaces, or free. A device separates the tension roller from the platen for paper insertion. Paper fingers press yieldingly and the outer ones adjust. A marginal stop sets the carriage return limit, and spacing-dog tension is adjustable. The dogs can be shifted slightly to shade (reprint) a letter in the same space. A straight-edge line indicator withdraws automatically when the platen lowers, and a detachable paper table folds toward the raised platen.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
