## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copy must be held in view beside a typewriter, and the holder needs to attach to the machine and be adjustable.

**Solution:** A curved base bar with thumb-screw seats clamps to the machine uprights; a standard carries a platform with slotted arms and spring clamps, adjustable by thumb-nuts.

**How it works:** Base bar clamps to the machine, standard and platform are adjustable, and spring clamps hold the copy.
