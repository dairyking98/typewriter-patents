## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating accounting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Conditional arrangements for controlling subsequent operating functions, e.g. control arrangement triggered by a function key and depending on the condition of the register

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Calculating or accounting machine with a calculating or balancing unit in which each the new balances are calculated from the old balance and the inflows or outflows.

**How it works (reading):** The motor key is enabled or disabled depending on the paper carriage position, so the carriage must be returned to normal before amounts can be entered.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the paper carriage is shifted to print a new balance, an entry or exit can still be keyed in, which the operator should not be allowed to do.

**Solution:** The motor key is enabled or disabled depending on the paper carriage position, so the carriage must be returned to normal before amounts can be entered.
