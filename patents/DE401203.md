## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The resin content of the ribbon impregnation is increased so the ribbon is no longer suited to transfer printing and can write directly on a lithographic printing form.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons adapted for transfer printing to a lithographic plate are unsuitable when typing goes directly onto printing forms.

**Solution:** The resin content of the ribbon impregnation is increased so the ribbon is no longer suited to transfer printing and can write directly on a lithographic printing form.

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Lithographic ribbon. The composition of the lithographic Ribbons have so far been adapted to the transfer printing process in the event that typewriter writing should be transferred to a lithographic printing plate. However, certain printing forms, e.g. B. the protected by the patent 350484, allow immediate writing by means of the typewriter with omission of the transfer. This omission makes the need for Adaptation of the ribbon to the otherwise necessary transfer printing process, so that accordingly a change in the composition of the impregnation solutions of the color ribbons is necessary. This change is the subject of the present invention. The specific composition of the components of the impregnation solutions such new ribbons arises on the basis of the announced idea. It is enough z. B. to increase the resin content so much that the ribbon becomes unsuitable for the transfer printing process. Then it has one for immediate labeling suitable composition.
