## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A very light pocket writing device, easy to carry and operable with one hand holding it while the other selects characters.

**Solution:** A hand-turned type disc with a type under the tip of each radial arm sits beneath a rocking plate whose lever presses down the one arm that is under it.

**How it works:** The disc is divided into radial arms, each carrying a type on its underside, and is mounted free to turn on a centre shaft with a handle. It lies under a plate hinged at one end. At the other end a two-armed lever is pivoted on a rivet, with stops limiting one arm and the other arm reaching over one disc arm. The operator turns the disc until the wanted arm is under the lever arm, then presses the plate down. The outer lever end meets a stop, so the lever turns and its inner arm presses the disc arm down, printing the character.
