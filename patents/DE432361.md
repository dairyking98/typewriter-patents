## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Improvement of the known switching devices for the paper trolley of typewriters, in which a rack connected to the paper cart frame into a gear intervenes, the rotation of which is controlled by a key switch that alternates with a fixed and with a movable pawl in one with the said gear wheel connected gear engages.

**How it works (reading):** The switch lock carries a fixed and a movable pawl that engage an escape wheel with a rack; the movable pawl is kept in a spring-held position so that the carriage advances evenly, and the improvement reduces the motion needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage switch pawls swing with every keystroke, and an uneven hand or a hard edge stop makes the escapement space the letters unevenly.

**Solution:** The switch lock carries a fixed and a movable pawl that engage an escape wheel with a rack; the movable pawl is kept in a spring-held position so that the carriage advances evenly, and the improvement reduces the motion needed.
