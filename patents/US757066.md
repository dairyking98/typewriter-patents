## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In the inventor's earlier pneumatic typewriter each air pipe needs its own compressed-air cylinder and wastes air.

**Solution:** A rotating hollow arm, turning with the type wheel, sweeps past a ring of perforations connected by pipes to the keys, so one common cylinder is triggered when the arm lines up with a finger-covered key pipe.

**How it works:** Each key has a perforation connected by a pipe to a radial bore in a ring around a stationary axle with two air ducts. A hollow arm on the axle hub is attached to the type wheel and rotates with it, its outer end passing each ring bore in turn. Air flows from the reservoir duct through the arm and out the pipe that is open; if the operator's finger covers that key, pressure builds as the arm passes it. The pressure goes through grooves and a second duct to a cylinder whose piston performs printing and carriage movement. A vacuum can be used instead of compressed air to allow a smaller cylinder.
