## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes of plates, e.g. metal plates, plastic cards with automatic plate transport systems, e.g. for credit cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide an attachment of simple and cheap construction which is adapted to be applied to any such type-writer without altering any of the parts and without defacing in any respect the machine or interfering with the operation of the same.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment holds the feed-roll ratchet pawl away from the feed-roll ratchet so the roll can be turned in either direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed-roll pawl on a Hammond typewriter prevents the large feed-roll from being turned freely to bring any line into position.

**Solution:** An attachment holds the feed-roll ratchet pawl away from the feed-roll ratchet so the roll can be turned in either direction.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
