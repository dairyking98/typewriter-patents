## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Printing and word spacing are both triggered by a single contact closed by pressing the setting pin through a lever, not by contact with the plate.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Many contacts on the character plate where the setting pin touches risk sticking and are dangerous with heavy current.

**Solution:** Printing and word spacing are both triggered by a single contact closed by pressing the setting pin through a lever, not by contact with the plate.

**How it works:** The type cylinder is set by an adjustment pin moved over a plate bearing the character names; a rack and gear turn the cylinder. Pressing the pin down closes one contact through a two-armed lever, energising an electromagnet that drives the cylinder against the platen. A special plate spot is the space position: there a steel pin on the cylinder axle hits an anvil so the cylinder cannot reach the platen, but the carriage still advances for the word space.
