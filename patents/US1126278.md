## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates broadly to in provenients in typewriting machines and specifically to type rests particularly adapted for machines knovvn as bar typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A type bar rest with a greater radius of curvature than the type bar hanger segment, so centre bars move through a smaller arc; the bridge, backing and rest are formed from one sheet of metal.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars near the centre of the rest travel through a different arc from the rest to the printing point than bars at the ends, because the rest curvature does not match the segment curvature.

**Solution:** A type bar rest with a greater radius of curvature than the type bar hanger segment, so centre bars move through a smaller arc; the bridge, backing and rest are formed from one sheet of metal.
