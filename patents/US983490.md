## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case-shift keys and shift lock on a typebar-segment-shifting machine suffer lost motion and the lock must release when a shift key is pressed.

**Solution:** Shift and shift-lock levers each connect to a rock shaft via adjustable plates; a spring latch on the rock shaft locks the segment up and is released by a pin on the shift lever.

**How it works:** Two shift keys and a shift-lock lever raise the type-bar segment through links and arms of a rock shaft, with pin-and-slot joints so the lock lever does not move the other keys. Adjustable plates take up lost motion. A latch on the rock shaft rests on an adjustable step on the lock lever when the lock lever is depressed. A pin on a shift lever pushes the latch's cam face to release it.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type bar segment; rock shaft; shift key lever; shift lock lever; adjustable plate; link; latch; step; spring

**Improves on:**

- earlier L.C. Smith shift mechanism: adjustable plates remove lost motion and a latch gives automatic shift lock
