## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter for both Japanese (vertical, right-to-left or left-to-right) and Western text needs the platen carriage to be fed in either direction by set steps, normally requiring two springs or reversing spring tension.

**Solution:** One always-same-direction mainspring drives the carriage through a gear train in which one gear is hand-shifted to make an odd or even number of meshing gears, and the same change-over flips the step-limiting device to the matching direction.

**How it works:** A rack under the platen carriage meshes with a gear on a sliding transmission shaft whose rear gear can mesh with either of two gears that turn in opposite directions via a gear train to the spring-winding shaft. Pushing the carriage back winds the spring through this train; the spring's return force then pushes the carriage the other way. A handwheel with an eccentric rocks a lever and crank that slide the shaft to choose which gear meshes, and the same lever slides the limiting device (a known escapement of gears that turn through a set angle against a second rack) so that the matching gear engages. Each press of the print or sideways-feed lever releases the limiter through a set angle, moving the carriage one step. The spring is pretensioned by a gear device and held by a ratchet and pawl, and a buffer on the shaft eases gear meshing.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Platen sideways-feed device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a platen sideways-feed device for a typewriter in which the spring is loaded by moving the platen back, and the platen is fed sideways by the spring's return force. It is characterised in that one gear of the train of gears that connects the platen carriage with the spring-winding shaft can be moved by hand, so that the number of meshing gears can be made either odd or even; and in that the same change-over also changes the limiting direction of the sideways-feed limiting device, which is connected with the platen carriage and limits the step of its feed. With a spring that always acts in the same direction, the platen can thus be fed sideways by set steps in either direction, left or right, as chosen. The object is to obtain, with a simple change-over device and a spring that always acts in the same direction, a device that feeds the platen automatically by set steps either to the left or to the right.
