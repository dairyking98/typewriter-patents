## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating to a chosen column position needs a locking device and index that hold the stops reliably.

**Solution:** A column-stop bar with an index is set to a denomination position, and a lock holds it in working position while a spring-drum carries the carriage to the stop.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
