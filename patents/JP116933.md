## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A Japanese-script printing machine needs its paper cylinder moved sideways a set step at each character, to the left or to the right at will, for horizontal printing from either end or for line spacing in vertical-strike writing.

**Solution:** A spiral spring strip held between rollers on a rack-moved sector, with its end on a traveller geared to a fixed rack and to the cylinder's sliding frame, gives a pull to the left or right depending on which side of the traveller the rollers are placed.

**How it works:** The paper cylinder sits on a sliding frame (3) with a rack (4), moving left and right in a support frame. A spiral spring in a barrel (8) is led between two rollers (13) on a moving rack (11); its free end is fixed to a traveller (14) that carries a gear (15) on a fixed rack (9) and a gear (16) on rack (4). Turning a handle (18) turns a toothed sector (12) that moves rack (11) and the rollers to one side of the traveller or the other, so the spring pulls the traveller, and through gear (16) the cylinder, left or right. At each impression a device driven by the print mechanism lets the traveller advance a set spacing, so the cylinder steps one character width. When the traveller nears the rollers the handle is turned to reset the line; the same movement gives line spacing for vertical writing.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Sideways paper-cylinder moving device for a Japanese-script printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a sideways paper-cylinder moving device for a Japanese-script printing machine, characterised in that several rollers are fitted on a moving rack that meshes with a toothed sector; one end of a spiral spring led between these rollers is fixed to a traveller that engages a fixed rack; the traveller carries a gear meshing with the fixed rack and a gear meshing with the rack of the sliding frame; and, by turning the sector, the rollers and the traveller are moved into one or the other left-right relative position, so that the spring is given a force pulling to the right or to the left and the paper cylinder is moved sideways. Its object is, by the relative movement of the rollers and the traveller, to give the spring a force pulling to the right or the left, and, together with the working of the printing mechanism, to move the paper cylinder a set distance at a time to the right or the left, and so to perform at will either the line spacing of a vertical-striking printing machine or horizontal printing.
