## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In front-stroke visible machines, lifting and feeding the ribbon from the universal bar action draws extra power from the key stroke.

**Solution:** Feed the ribbon spools from the carriage tension drum, with a clutched shaft pair and cam slide to reverse the direction, while the universal bar only lifts the ribbon guide.

**How it works:** A tension drum pulls the carriage; as it turns it drives a ratchet and horizontal shafts that carry bevel pinions meshing with bevel gears on the vertical ribbon spool shafts. Each horizontal shaft is in two parts joined by a notched clutch sleeve, and the two shafts are linked by a belt, cord or chain. A slide with forked cam members, pushed from the front by a rod, throws the clutches in or out to reverse feed. Pressing any key lever depresses the universal bar, which tilts a lever and raises the forked ribbon guide in front of the printing point.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** tension drum; ratchet; bevel gear; bevel pinion; clutch sleeve; cam slide; push rod; ribbon spool; forked ribbon guide; universal bar; key lever; type bar

**Improves on:**

- ribbon feed from the universal bar action: feed taken from the carriage drum to lower key effort
