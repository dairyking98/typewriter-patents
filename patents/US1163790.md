## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for releasing the platens from the line-space wheels of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** An internal gear on the line-space wheel meshes with pinions on a fixed arm keyed to the platen axle and on two loose arms; ratchets on the pinions lock the loose arms to the fixed arm so there is no relative rotation, and the platen can still be released by hand.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A hard or violent line-space lever action can make the platen overthrow or underthrow relative to the line-space wheel.

**Solution:** An internal gear on the line-space wheel meshes with pinions on a fixed arm keyed to the platen axle and on two loose arms; ratchets on the pinions lock the loose arms to the fixed arm so there is no relative rotation, and the platen can still be released by hand.
