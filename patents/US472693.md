## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed ratchets of the Blickensderfer carriage could not be adjusted to remove lost motion as parts wore, and the bell warning at line end is ignored so letters pile up.

**Solution:** Separately adjustable propelling and locking ratchets on the feed shaft, plus a movable stop that halts the carriage before the end of its travel.

**How it works:** Key-levers move a bail that rocks a rock-shaft. A cam with a friction roller on the rock-shaft depresses a pawl against a ratchet wheel, advancing the feed shaft one tooth, whose pinion engages a rack on the carriage. As the cam finishes, a second pawl on the rock-shaft engages an oppositely-pointed locking ratchet on the same shaft, locking it. Both ratchets are adjustable on the shaft relative to each other and to the pawls, so wear and lost motion can be taken up. The carriage rides on sheaves, and a movable stop in its path arrests it before the end of travel, controlling the down movement of the key-levers; moving the stop parts clear lets the carriage go on.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** key-lever; bail; rock-shaft; feed shaft; propelling ratchet wheel; locking ratchet wheel; propelling pawl; locking pawl; cam; friction roller; pinion; carriage rack; carriage bar; sheave; hook plate; adjustable stop

**Numbers:** shaft advanced one tooth per stroke
