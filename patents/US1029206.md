## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Sheets must be fed one at a time from a pack to an automatic typewriter without manual handling.

**Solution:** A pack of sheets is held in an inclined receptacle, and a shaft-mounted set of suction cups picks each sheet and feeds it to the typewriter paper holding mechanism.
