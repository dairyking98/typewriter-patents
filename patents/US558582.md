## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars linked mechanically to keys are complex.

**Solution:** Typebars rest loosely on a perforated plate beneath the platen and are struck and driven to the platen by hammer ends of the key levers, returning by gravity.

**How it works:** Typebars are massed as a truncated pyramid below the platen, each carrying a complete character, heads together at the top and diverging below. A perforated plate with holes smaller than the type heads holds them at rest, and a second plate lower down guides the levers. Pressing a key swings its hammer end against the typebar end, flinging it to the platen, and it falls back by gravity with no connection. A hammer head form with the fulcrum near the finger piece follows the bar up until it strikes. Hammer heads may be rubber-faced.
