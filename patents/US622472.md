## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter should produce several original copies at once rather than carbons.

**Solution:** A carriage carries a column of platens each with its own paper and a matching set of type wheels that all bring the same character to print simultaneously, driven by electromagnets.

**How it works:** The carriage with a rack is stepped one space by an electromagnet-driven pawl and returned by spring when the holding pawl is released. A platen frame carries rows of platens on shafts, moved sideways against the type wheels by magnets, and turned for line spacing by ratchets. Type wheels in circular rows on cylinders are rotated together by a common rack and raised together on a bar to choose row and character. Magnets also control inking rollers.
