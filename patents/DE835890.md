## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Limit-stop tabulators on the platen force the operator to rotate the platen back and forth, so the next form cannot be pre-set during the current labelling.

**Solution:** The platen is coupled to an actuator only during the adjustment movement, and the travel is set by a selected limit stop, so the next sheet can be pre-set while the machine labels the current one.
