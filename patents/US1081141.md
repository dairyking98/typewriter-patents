## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to i means for hanging the type-bars of visible or front-strike typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Each hanger individually holds one type-bar with a removable bracket, adjustable in the direction of wear, with large bearing surfaces, ribs forming a lubricant chamber, and a curved arc arrangement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar hangers wore and could not be adjusted without removing the parts from the machine, and bearings were not dust-proof or lubricated.

**Solution:** Each hanger individually holds one type-bar with a removable bracket, adjustable in the direction of wear, with large bearing surfaces, ribs forming a lubricant chamber, and a curved arc arrangement.
