## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists must look at the keyboard to find keys; beginners tire their arm muscles.

**Solution:** A removable frame of longitudinal and transverse bars and rods laid over the keyboard so the fingers feel their way to each row and key pair.

**How it works:** End plates rest on the keyboard frame and carry an inclined rear bar and a velvet- or rubber-covered front rod that the fingers touch to tell the rows apart. Transverse bars split each row into compartments holding two keys. A recess clears the space bar, and an arm serves as a thumb rest. The operator rests finger sides against the bars to locate rows and drops into the compartment for the key.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** end plate; longitudinal bar; covered rod; transverse bar; thumb rest
