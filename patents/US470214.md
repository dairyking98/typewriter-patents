## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ink pad or ribbon dries and gets dirty when the machine is idle, and the type levers are exposed.

**Solution:** An annular ink-pad holder that is rotated down against the frame to cover the pad, with a cap and a lock for the type levers.

**How it works:** An annular frame on uprights is surrounded by a ring whose inclined slots engage pins, so turning the ring raises or lowers the pad holder. The annular pad sits above the frame in use and lowers flush with it when idle. Turning also presents fresh pad surface. A cap piece protects the types and levers.

**Features:** [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** base; upright; annular frame; pad holder ring; inking pad; knob; cap piece
