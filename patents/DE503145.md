## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for Longitudinal movement of the ribbon of typewriters, with tier the drive by a Ratchet wheel is effected by the button levers by means of a ratchet at each Keyboard strike offset in partial rotation and set by a pawl Location is held.

**How it works (reading):** Each key stroke advances a ratchet wheel on the main drive shaft by a pawl; the shaft, pawl pivot, key levers and safety catch all run in one guide comb.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon drive and guidance need reliable guiding of the key levers without extra parts, important for a light portable.

**Solution:** Each key stroke advances a ratchet wheel on the main drive shaft by a pawl; the shaft, pawl pivot, key levers and safety catch all run in one guide comb.
