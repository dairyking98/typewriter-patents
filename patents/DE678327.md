## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (fluid meter type wheel setting) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of indicators or registers with means for producing or issuing a receipt or record of sale

**How it works (reading):** A second pawl on a spring and stop engages the ratchet wheel that rotates the type wheel, so a stuck pawl is harmless and the wheel locks in stamp position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type wheels in stamp meters can jam when the pawl tooth catches the ratchet tooth, damaging parts or preventing a stamp.

**Solution:** A second pawl on a spring and stop engages the ratchet wheel that rotates the type wheel, so a stuck pawl is harmless and the wheel locks in stamp position.
