## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by applications of scales or indicators

**How it works (reading):** A cheap spring-steel slider clamped to the paper rail carries numbered positions, with optional colour-coded whole and decimal digits, for column alignment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Decimal tabs are costly and the writer cannot see column positions directly.

**Solution:** A cheap spring-steel slider clamped to the paper rail carries numbered positions, with optional colour-coded whole and decimal digits, for column alignment.
