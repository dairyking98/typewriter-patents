## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition

**How it works (reading):** A statistically derived layout giving index, middle and ring fingers one staggered key column each, with letters in a column poorly related to one another.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing keyboard layouts put frequently paired letters under the same finger, forcing one finger to hit two keys in succession.

**Solution:** A statistically derived layout giving index, middle and ring fingers one staggered key column each, with letters in a column poorly related to one another.

**How it works:** Letter frequency and letter-pair statistics for German were used to split the alphabet into a consonant group and a vowel group, one per keyboard half, with T, K and P moved to the vowel side for balance. Each of the three strong fingers of each hand serves one column of letters that rarely follow each other. The second and third keys in each transverse row sit a half step lower than the first and fourth, so middle and ring finger keys are offset a row toward the platen. The hand therefore alternates or steps down rows, which is the most favourable motion.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Numbers:** six central key columns
