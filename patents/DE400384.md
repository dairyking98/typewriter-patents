## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to refers to a device for carrying out the method protected by patent 359986 for fastening the type pieces on the type levers of typewriters and des Additional patent 371984.

**How it works (reading):** An optical microscope mounted on the same slide as the type-piece clamp, with height adjustment, is used to aim the type precisely before soldering the lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Aligning type pieces on the type levers by eye through a plain glass plate causes setting errors and forces the machine to be turned over when soldering.

**Solution:** An optical microscope mounted on the same slide as the type-piece clamp, with height adjustment, is used to aim the type precisely before soldering the lever.
