## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Door closers are costly and their hydraulic damping depends on temperature.

**Solution:** A vibration engine part moves over a dead-centre position, storing energy in a spring and releasing it to swing back. It is also used for typewriter key operation.
