The work of adding and calculating machines, calculating typewriters and the like consist, to a very large extent, of filling out broad sheets in rubrics are divided and include different columns of numbers to serve as documents for to serve accounting, statistical or other purposes. It is right here often required, the resulting long, horizontal series of numbers as a single calculation or to pull out the list and print their numbers vertically one below the other, so that the form or sheet on which the statement is made remains manageable. For this purpose, printing units are known, by means of which the two prints on two sheets, which can be switched in two mutually perpendicular directions at the same time can be executed. The invention consists in the further training of these machines in such a way that that the even indexing of both sheets by one or more distances when skipping empty sections by pressing once or several times a tab key for the paper carriage can be achieved.
