## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing unit for adding machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the further training of these machines in such a way that that the even indexing of both sheets by one or more distances when skipping empty sections by pressing once or several times a tab key for the paper carriage can be achieved.

**How it works (reading):** A narrower second paper guide sits under a raised platen, and a tab key skips the paper carriage by set distances so that both sheets index together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing a column of numbers vertically from a wide sheet needs the two sheets indexed together when empty sections are skipped.

**Solution:** A narrower second paper guide sits under a raised platen, and a tab key skips the paper carriage by set distances so that both sheets index together.
