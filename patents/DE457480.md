## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a platen-shift typewriter, the shift frame, shift key lever and carriage counterbalance spring were adjusted separately, so correcting the platen height for the typebar stop upset their mutual position.

**Solution:** Mount the shift frame, shift key levers and one end of the carriage compensation spring in a common support (pair of plates) that is adjustable as a unit relative to the main frame.

**How it works:** The carriage rides on balls on rails on a shift frame lifted by arms on a rotatable shaft, driven from the shift key through a lever and a hooked link. Parallel links keep the platen movement exact. Pair of plates carries bearings for the shaft and links, stop screws for the lift, a front extension for key levers and shift-lock pin and a rear one for the compensation spring. After loosening clamp screws the plates are shifted in the frame, and fine adjustment is by a screw through a bent flange of the frame. This sets the platen position against the typebar stop in one move.
