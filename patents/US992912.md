## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dead-key accent devices require writing the accent before the letter, opposite to natural order.

**Solution:** An accent typebar and key that do not operate the universal bar or escapement, so the accent can be written after the letter at the same spot.

**How it works:** On a Remington front-strike machine, accent key levers lack the adjusting screws that work the universal bar, so pressing them does not release the escapement or feed the ribbon. The accent typebar strikes above or below the letter just printed. A linkage from the key to the ribbon vibrator, via levers and a link in a slot on the rock shaft crank, lifts the vibrator independently of the universal bar so the ribbon covers the print point.
