## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stop guides are costly and weak when hit by the stops, and the inventor wants a simple strong construction.

**Solution:** A two-part guide with slots is held together by reeds that separate the slots and fit nicks in the guide, held by a cap, so the stops are guided in strong, inexpensive form.
