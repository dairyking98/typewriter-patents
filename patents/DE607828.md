## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with calculating machines)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter, consisting of a keyboard typewriter and one or more calculating machines with printing device or accounting machines arranged next to it, with a carriage common to all of these machines and with a switchover device, through which the carriage advance alternately on typewriter movement or calculating machine or table movement is adjustable.

**How it works (reading):** A standard calculating machine sits beside an unmodified typewriter, and a lever on the calculator carries a finger that drives an interchangeable switching ruler, which switches the typewriter carriage advance between typewriter and calculator movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Combining a typewriter with a calculating or accounting machine on one carriage previously required modifying both machines, because their parts overlapped.

**Solution:** A standard calculating machine sits beside an unmodified typewriter, and a lever on the calculator carries a finger that drives an interchangeable switching ruler, which switches the typewriter carriage advance between typewriter and calculator movement.
