## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (photographic letter-writing light device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**How it works (reading):** A single light source illuminates all fixed type stencils, and electromagnetic deflection or a movable mirror directs the rays of only the wanted character onto the moving photosensitive writing surface while the rays of the others are blocked.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The main patent's photographic typewriter needs one spark gap per character; this addition uses one shared light source and deflection of the rays, to avoid that.

**Solution:** A single light source illuminates all fixed type stencils, and electromagnetic deflection or a movable mirror directs the rays of only the wanted character onto the moving photosensitive writing surface while the rays of the others are blocked.

**Features:** [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
