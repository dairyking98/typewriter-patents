## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers

**How it works (reading):** Rails on the base plate carry pins and rotatable levers that clamp the machine's metal feet firmly against the base in its case.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter clamped to a base plate in a case can still shift during shipping because the pins and levers have play.

**Solution:** Rails on the base plate carry pins and rotatable levers that clamp the machine's metal feet firmly against the base in its case.
