## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Supports or magazines for piles from which articles are to be separated adapted to support articles substantially horizontally, e.g. for separation from top of pile

**How it works (reading):** Thin resilient paper feelers, insulated and wired to a series of indicating circuits, press on the sheet and report its edge position so that a scale shows the distance from the printing point to the edge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The operator must judge by eye how far the sheet edge is from the printing point, and the inventors want this distance shown automatically at any sheet length.

**Solution:** Thin resilient paper feelers, insulated and wired to a series of indicating circuits, press on the sheet and report its edge position so that a scale shows the distance from the printing point to the edge.
