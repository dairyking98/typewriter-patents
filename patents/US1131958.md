## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must turn independently of the line-space ratchet wheel in either direction, and be locked positively with few motions.

**Solution:** The platen is separated from the platen ratchet wheel by a clutch of dogs in a recess or a flange, and a slide pin and lever lock the platen and ratchet wheel together.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
