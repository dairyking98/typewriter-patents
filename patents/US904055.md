## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in the carriage-feed mechanism of typewriting machines, Including visible-writing machines and understrike or .

**How it works (reading):** A release key on the carriage releases the feed wheel from the control of the feed dogs, so the carriage can be moved unobstructed by the feed mechanism when the platen is in the elevated position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A constantly meshed rack and pinion carriage feed prevents an underswing carriage from being moved freely from right to left while the platen is raised.

**Solution:** A release key on the carriage releases the feed wheel from the control of the feed dogs, so the carriage can be moved unobstructed by the feed mechanism when the platen is in the elevated position.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
