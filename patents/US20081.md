## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stereotype plate process) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a new process of preparingtypographicprinting-surfaces, whereby the setting and distributing types, as heretofore in use in printing, is eflectually superseded.

**How it works (reading):** Letter dies on the outer ends of radiating arms are pressed into a softer plate, all to one uniform depth, and the plate is indexed by screw-driven blocks between each letter and line, so that a cast or electrotype can be taken for letterpress.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Solution:** Letter dies on the outer ends of radiating arms are pressed into a softer plate, all to one uniform depth, and the plate is indexed by screw-driven blocks between each letter and line, so that a cast or electrotype can be taken for letterpress.
