## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage position indicators had small numbers; a compact index with large, readable numerals showing the carriage position is wanted.

**Solution:** An index wheel geared to the carriage spring drum, with numerals spiralling several times round its face, seen through an aperture slide.

**How it works:** The carriage spring drum is geared through a shaft and bevel gears to an index wheel or drum, so carriage travel turns it. The numerals run progressively around the wheel face in a spiral, more than one turn. A housing covers the front, and a transversely movable apertured slide registers with the numerals so only one is seen. Shown on a Monarch typebar machine, improving on Felbel US660988 and Gabrielson US686220.
