## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Hammond typewriter the last printed word is hidden behind the ribbon and guard, so the operator has to move the paper with trouble to see what was just printed.

**Solution:** Pivot the annular disk that carries the ribbon guard so a key near the type-keys can drop the guard and ribbon and expose the last line.

**How it works:** The ribbon guard with its paper opening sits on a wire frame carried by an annular disk that also covers the mechanism. The disk is pivoted to the machine frame and has a depending lever joined by a rod to a rocker shaft ending in keys just below the type-keys. Pressing a key depresses the rear of the disk, and with it the central ribbon and guard, revealing the printed line. A spring returns the disk, and an adjusting screw with a jam nut sets the rest position so the guard opening sits at the right height.
