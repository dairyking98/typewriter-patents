## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating machines need a convenient, positive column-selecting mechanism, combined with denominational selection.

**Solution:** A bank of column-selecting stops that each co-act with one of the adjustable column stops on a carriage stop bar, so the selected key decides where the carriage stops.

**How it works:** Column stops 12-15 are adjustable along a stop bar on the carriage and carry depending projections. Fixed selecting stops 20-23, formed on upstanding movable bars, are projected into the path of a given column stop only, so the chosen selector determines where the carriage is arrested. Stops can be repositioned on the bar to change the columns.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** stop bar; column stop; column-selecting stop; sheet metal strip; carriage
