## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**How it works (reading):** The axially moving coupling is articulated on the drive shaft with the pawl carrier fixed to it, and an axially adjustable swing yoke on an oscillating shaft sets the ribbon stroke length, which reverses the ribbon drive.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Coupling the ribbon-reel drive shaft to its ratchet needs separate coupling elements, and a fixed ribbon stroke is inflexible.

**Solution:** The axially moving coupling is articulated on the drive shaft with the pawl carrier fixed to it, and an axially adjustable swing yoke on an oscillating shaft sets the ribbon stroke length, which reverses the ribbon drive.

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
