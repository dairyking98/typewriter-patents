## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriters wherein the type are attached to typebars which print with an upward stroke, and it is designed to simplify the construction of such machines and lessen their weight and size, also to render the writing visible during operation of the machine and to improve the actuating connections between the keys and the type-bars and the general construct-ion of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** Upstroke typebar machine with visible writing, simplified construction and bent connecting links between key and type levers.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters are complicated and heavy and writing hidden.

**Solution:** Upstroke typebar machine with visible writing, simplified construction and bent connecting links between key and type levers.

**How it works:** Key-levers pivot on a shaft; bell-cranks with links drive type-levers on a wire trunnion held in dovetailed support plates in an arc base. Links are twisted to the lever plane. Typebars rest on an arc pad.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** frame; key-lever; bell-crank; link; type-lever; support plate; arc base; pad; carriage
