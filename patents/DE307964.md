## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a Typewriter, in which, besides the usual, from one on the platen itself rolling roll existing feed .

**How it works (reading):** A second feed roller for the original sheet is geared to the main platen feed, so both move together at a chosen ratio, with an intermediate gear that a hand lever disengages for setting the original.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Carbon copies and original forms cannot be fed in step, because the platen feed moves the copy sheets and the original is fed separately, which wastes time at every line change.

**Solution:** A second feed roller for the original sheet is geared to the main platen feed, so both move together at a chosen ratio, with an intermediate gear that a hand lever disengages for setting the original.
