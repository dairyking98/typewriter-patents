## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method for aligning the guide parts of Type levers, type rods or the like.

**How it works (reading):** Before hardening, guide parts are pressed between a punch and a die whose faces carry small grain-like raised points, exceeding the elastic limit so the alignment is permanent; the resulting lines do not harm the sanded guide surfaces.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Straightening type-lever guide parts by hammering is laborious and the flat tools used earlier spring back.

**Solution:** Before hardening, guide parts are pressed between a punch and a die whose faces carry small grain-like raised points, exceeding the elastic limit so the alignment is permanent; the resulting lines do not harm the sanded guide surfaces.
