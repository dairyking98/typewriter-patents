## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriting machines wherein a flat platen is employed to sustain the paper beneath the writing mechanism movable horizontally thereover for the purposes of letter and line spacing, as inthe Elliott & Hatch machine of commerce represented as to its general organization in Letters Patent to Hatch and Hillard No.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-actuated pressure device lies transversely across the platen, is attached to the movable writing-mechanism frame, and moves along the platen with the carriage so pressure stays at the writing point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On flat-platen machines, extra holding-down is needed so several sheets and carbon sheets stay flat under the writing point.

**Solution:** A spring-actuated pressure device lies transversely across the platen, is attached to the movable writing-mechanism frame, and moves along the platen with the carriage so pressure stays at the writing point.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** flat platen; frame; writing mechanism; spring-actuated clamp; carbon sheets
