## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (subtotal printing cash register)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to devices for pulling of subtotals and totals on cash registers, calculating machines and the like with several Adding units, of which the adder corresponding to the setting is used for the purpose of Buzz pulling in readiness for work and for the purpose.

**How it works (reading):** A common zero switching element carries a zero stop for each adder, so one sum key sets all the working adders and the type wheels to the sum at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Subtotal and total printing on multi-adder cash registers needs a separate sum key or zero stop per adder, which is awkward when several adders are used.

**Solution:** A common zero switching element carries a zero stop for each adder, so one sum key sets all the working adders and the type wheels to the sum at once.
