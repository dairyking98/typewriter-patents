## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an ink ribbon reel for typewriters, in which the ink ribbon in a parallel to the spool axis extending slot of the spool hub is clamped.

**How it works (reading):** A toothed ribbon clamp slides in guide slots of both reel plates and presses the ribbon into the toothed spool-hub slot, holding it firmly without tools.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon attachment is messy and time consuming, and fingers become soiled.

**Solution:** A toothed ribbon clamp slides in guide slots of both reel plates and presses the ribbon into the toothed spool-hub slot, holding it firmly without tools.
