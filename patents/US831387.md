## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Single-keyboard machines tie up the left hand working the shift levers.

**Solution:** A knee-operated lever linked by flexible cords through the table to the shift levers.

**How it works:** Hooks on the shift levers attach to flexible connections passing through holes in the table over pulleys and along a curved surface of a knee lever. Moving the knee operates the shift, leaving the left hand free. Shown on an Oliver; the machine is otherwise unmodified.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** shift lever; hook; flexible cord; pulley; knee lever; table

**Improves on:**

- double-keyboard machines: single keyboard gets hands-free shift
