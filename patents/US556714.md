## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**How it works (reading):** A perforating machine with a rotating index plate and a pawl-and-ratchet, where a housing and shell turn to set word-punch positions on a fixed base.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Money values on bank checks need to be written in words to deter raising.

**Solution:** A perforating machine with a rotating index plate and a pawl-and-ratchet, where a housing and shell turn to set word-punch positions on a fixed base.

**Features:** [Type ring](../features/elem-ring.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
