## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements to attach the ribbon to the spool; Printing mechanisms with inking or ink-ribbon devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an arrangement for attaching a tape, in particular a ribbon, to a Kitchen sink.

**How it works (reading):** A grip plate on a head strip swings into a recess in the spool after insertion, so the ribbon can be removed with a pointed tool.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Attaching ribbon to a spool in an inaccessible place is hard, and hook-type heads need a celluloid handle that adds bulk.

**Solution:** A grip plate on a head strip swings into a recess in the spool after insertion, so the ribbon can be removed with a pointed tool.
