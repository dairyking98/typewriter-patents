## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** If the free-running release lever of a mainspring-driven platen feed is worked while neither rack meshes with the pinion, the spring unwinds instantly, spinning the gears and damaging parts.

**Solution:** An L-shaped plate fitted between the two racks blocks the release lever whenever the racks are in the neutral position.

**How it works:** A mainspring turns a ratchet and pinion; a pair of rockable racks, one meshing at a time, moves the platen right or left. At each print stroke a main pawl and auxiliary pawl alternate on the ratchet so it advances one step. A working lever alone lifts the main pawl, leaving both pawls out, so the ratchet runs freely for rapid platen travel. An L-plate with a forked end between the racks swings as they rock; in neutral its catch stands in the path of a catch lever on the working lever's tube shaft, blocking the lift. When either rack meshes, the catch moves aside and the lever works.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Platen sideways-feed device for a Japanese typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a platen sideways-feed device for a Japanese typewriter of the following kind. A pair of racks, mounted on the platen support frame parallel to the platen, face each other on the two sides of a pinion driven by the force of a mainspring. The racks are rocked to bring one or the other into mesh with one side of the pinion, which changes the direction, left or right, of the platen's sideways feed. And by releasing the pawl that engages the ratchet controlling the pinion's feed, the pinion can be made to turn continuously, so that whichever rack meshes with it moves the platen sideways continuously. In this device, one end of an L-shaped pivoted plate is held between the two racks, so that rocking the racks moves its other end. When the racks are in the neutral position, with neither meshed with the pinion, the other end of the L-shaped plate takes up a position where it makes the working lever that releases the pawl of the feed-control ratchet inoperable.
