## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (multiplying device with additional ribbons) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a duplicating device for typewriters with several movable ones Poor arranged additional ribbons, in which the additional ribbons be rotated from the rest position to an intermediate position, which is in one of the working position of the belts corresponding tangent plane to the paper roll and from they are moved in sliding guides down into their working position.

**How it works (reading):** The sliding guides for the ribbon-carrying arms are replaced by pivot bearings that sit on those arms, so the ribbons rotate and move down into working position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sliding guides and a four-arm linkage that swing the extra ribbons are complex, costly, need lubrication that can drip onto the paper.

**Solution:** The sliding guides for the ribbon-carrying arms are replaced by pivot bearings that sit on those arms, so the ribbons rotate and move down into working position.

**How it works:** Additional ribbons are swung from rest into a tangent plane at the paper roll, then lowered into working position by the pivot bearings.
