## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar journals need to be steady, without sideways sag that throws type out of alignment, and adjustable without disassembly.

**Solution:** The typebar hanger is two spring-fulcrumed levers whose rear ends are spread by a tapered screw, which closes the journal sockets on the typebar pivots.

**How it works:** Two short levers are joined by a transverse plate spring. One end of each carries a conical socket for the typebar's conical journals; the other ends have half-threaded grooves forming a vertical threaded hole. A screw with a tapered part, driven down into the grooves, wedges the rear ends apart and so brings the socket ends together, tightening the bearing. Raising the screw lets the spring reopen the sockets. The screw is vertical and reachable, and the spring is detachable for repair.
