## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wooden hollow platens are resonant, so type striking them makes noise, and they cost too much.

**Solution:** A platen of heavy sole-leather wetted, cut to shape, rolled and riveted or stitched, or built from stacked leather disks, which is lighter, cheaper and deadens sound.
