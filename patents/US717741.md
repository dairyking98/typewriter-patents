## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to mechanism for affording fractional line-spacing of the platen;

**How it works (reading):** A detent engages a line-spacing ratchet; a friction disk and sleeve on the platen shaft allow fractional rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fractional line-spacing of the platen should be simple, cheap and fit existing machines with little change.

**Solution:** A detent engages a line-spacing ratchet; a friction disk and sleeve on the platen shaft allow fractional rotation.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
