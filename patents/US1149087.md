## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-feed pawls on a stenographic machine are hard to remove, insert or adjust, and dull on one end.

**Solution:** Separable pawls on the feeding fingers can be inserted, removed, adjusted, and reversed to use the sharpened other end.
