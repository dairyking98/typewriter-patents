**How it works (reading):** A cylinder and piston damper gives gradually rising resistance on the overtravel in the elevator direction and a sudden rising resistance on return, with a jamming device controlling the loose tooth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage stops at the end of the stroke and the rebound cause shocks; simple buffers are not effective.

**Solution:** A cylinder and piston damper gives gradually rising resistance on the overtravel in the elevator direction and a sudden rising resistance on return, with a jamming device controlling the loose tooth.
