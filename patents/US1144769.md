## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Dispensers for paper towels or toilet paper dispensing from a web, e.g. with mechanical dispensing means the web being rolled-up

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for supporting paper Webs or rolls from which the paper is led to the platen of a typewriting machine, and it constitutes an improvement on a portion of the invention disclosed in United States Letters Patent No.

**How it works (reading):** Each paper roll sits in a cradle that is adjustable in length and breadth, with extensible arms, forming a removable unit.
