## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper carriage switch device on typewriters with a symmetrical to his Jamming wheel with crossed teeth in the middle plane, the side tooth flanks of which through one of two parallel inclined surfaces limited passage slot one Walk through the fork-shaped pawl.

**How it works (reading):** One ratchet arm is hook-shaped with rectangular teeth that stop the pawl, and the other arm has an inclined surface that lets the escape wheel accelerate the return of the pawl; the wheel is simply stamped from flat iron.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Known paper carriage ratchets clamp the pawl, so writing speed is limited and a hard keystroke is needed.

**Solution:** One ratchet arm is hook-shaped with rectangular teeth that stop the pawl, and the other arm has an inclined surface that lets the escape wheel accelerate the return of the pawl; the wheel is simply stamped from flat iron.
