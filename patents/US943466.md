## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Common typewriter keyboard layout makes frequent letters hard to reach.

**Solution:** Keyboard layout with the nine most used letters (H O R S E A W T I N group) at the centre, less-used letters on either side, rare ones outermost.

**How it works:** Letters are arranged by numbered frequency. The H O R S E A T I N group sits at the centre, mid-frequency keys beside them, the seldom-used at the ends. Margin release, shift keys and shift lock are conventional.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key; shift key; shift lock; margin release

**Numbers:** nine central letters
