## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods

**How it works (reading):** Fix two bodies to each typebar: one with no characters to run in the guide, and one carrying the characters above it.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type guides that enclose the typebar at the print point shadow the writing; guiding below the base plate or via special sunk type bodies is unreliable or hard to make.

**Solution:** Fix two bodies to each typebar: one with no characters to run in the guide, and one carrying the characters above it.

**How it works:** The typebars run in two comb guides on an arc, a key lever and intermediate lever throwing them forward. The platen can be raised or lowered for upper, middle or lower type. A guide body riveted to the typebar enters openings of a fixed guide with side and lower plates at the print point, while the separate type body strikes above the guide so the print point stays visible. This allows a narrow ordinary ribbon.
