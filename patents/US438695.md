## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Not clearly stated; the device selects one numeral punch from a set of punches at a time.

**Solution:** A check punch in which a swinging stirrup, pivoted to a lever, engages the beveled head of a chosen punch carried in rotatable disks, so the punch is brought under the stirrup and then driven.

**How it works:** Punches for digits, zero, cents and dollar mark sit in openings in disks with springs; the lever's stirrup notch seats on the selected punch head and the lever presses it through the check.
