## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A shifting type-bar basket for front-strike visible machines should be light, simple and effective.

**Solution:** Type bars pivot on a bent round-rod segment with ball races, and the segment is carried by a light single-piece shifting frame pivoted at the rear.

**How it works:** The type bars mount on a circular-arc round-rod segment with peripheral grooves holding anti-friction balls that run in rings threaded into the bar hubs. Each bar is driven by a push link from a first-order sub-lever, driven by a key lever through a short link. The key levers have restoring springs. The segment hangs from a frame of two arms and a cross bar, all one piece, pivoted at the rear, and a case-shift mechanism raises and lowers it. The carriage rides on balls on a track inclined at about 45 degrees.

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type bar; type bar segment; anti-friction ball; sub-lever; push link; key lever; restoring spring; shifting frame; cross bar; carriage; platen; track

**Numbers:** rail inclined about 45 degrees; four rows of keys
