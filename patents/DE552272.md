## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On flat-plate bookkeeping typewriters, a cam belt holds the sheet but is adjusted by hand only; the total posting sheet must be advanced a line for each account sheet entered.

**Solution:** Connect the cam belt to the vertically movable plate by a lever and ratchet so each plate depression advances the posting sheet one line.

**How it works:** An individual account sheet is clamped between the writing plate and a rail on one long side. The total posting sheet is held on the other side by driver cams of an endless belt that engage its edge perforation. The plate is pushed down, for example by a foot lever and chain, to free the account sheet and returns by springs. A lifting lever bearing on the underside of the plate swings a shift lever whose tooth, under spring pressure, advances a ratchet wheel connected by a gear to the cam belt wheel. The belt thus moves the total sheet one line spacing each time. The belt can also be advanced by hand using a knurled head on the front roller.
