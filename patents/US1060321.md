## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**How it works (reading):** A spring-driven carriage strap holds a button that a cleft hook on the cam mechanism normally keeps engaged; a frame extreme releases the button automatically, and a shoe on the carriage operates the signal and line-locking lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage stop had to be released and reset automatically at the ends of travel rather than by hand; the OCR is too damaged to confirm the full problem statement.

**Solution:** A spring-driven carriage strap holds a button that a cleft hook on the cam mechanism normally keeps engaged; a frame extreme releases the button automatically, and a shoe on the carriage operates the signal and line-locking lever.
