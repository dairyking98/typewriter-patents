## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary receptacles for articles, e.g. erasers, pencils

**How it works (reading):** An inclined tray on a bed plate holds a disk eraser that slides back to rest by gravity, plus a bar eraser, pencil and pin receptacle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Small items, such as erasers, pins, and pencils, are scattered and not within reach of the operator.

**Solution:** An inclined tray on a bed plate holds a disk eraser that slides back to rest by gravity, plus a bar eraser, pencil and pin receptacle.
