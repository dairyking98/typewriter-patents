## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a detachable paper trolley for typewriters, with which by means of a locking lever, which engages in a gear on the axle of the barrel, an expiration of the carriage spring is prevented when the car is lifted.

**How it works (reading):** Spring-loaded pawl on the underframe engages the drum gear automatically when the carriage is lifted, so the barrel stays locked.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Removing carriage releases the carriage spring drum uncontrolled.

**Solution:** Spring-loaded pawl on the underframe engages the drum gear automatically when the carriage is lifted, so the barrel stays locked.

**Features:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
