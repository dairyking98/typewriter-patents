## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reverse strained the ribbon, with poor guide, lateral shift and spool support.

**Solution:** Improved automatic reverse, ribbon guide, lateral ribbon movement and spool guides, with a hinged guide to swing the ribbon aside for cleaning type.

**How it works:** A tripping weight and spool guide trigger reversing; a feeding ratchet moves the ribbon laterally. The ribbon guide hinges to swing the ribbon away from the type basket.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spool; guide; ratchet; tripping weight
