## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators must lift each finger from a key before the type-bar, carriage and ribbon can reset, so keys cannot be struck in quick succession without locking or interference.

**Solution:** A trip or releasing device on the key or key-lever lets the key-lever and its type-bar print and return under spring power without the finger being lifted, so successive keys can be pressed progressively.

**How it works:** The key stem passes through a collar on the key-lever and a cylinder with a sloped outer surface that acts as a tripper. A double spring carried on the stem slides on that incline and at a set point releases the lever from the key, so a spring returns the lever, type-bar and carriage movement ahead of the finger. A cushioned adjustable stop touches the stem at the moment of release. A spring and weight on the type-bar helps it return, and a variant is shown applied to a Remington.
