## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On larger line-writing machines the separate movements that bring the type carrier into writing position take a long time, which interrupts continuous writing.

**Solution:** Keystrokes set stop levers in advance, and motor-driven stop carriers, moved along slopes or slots, bring the type carrier to the writing position directly or via other stops; set stops are then returned to rest by an extinguishing plate.
