## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keys that move only a short initial distance cannot complete their designed movement without extra power.

**Solution:** A key's first movement releases a power-driven shaft that completes the motion, performing exactly one cycle per keystroke even if the key is held.

**How it works:** A power-driven shaft is restrained while the keys are at rest. The first part of a key's travel withdraws the restraint, and the shaft then moves an actuatable member the full distance, with devices keeping it moving parallel to itself. Unlike the inventor's earlier movement, it completes only one cycle until the key is released and pressed again.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** key lever; power-driven shaft; restraint; actuatable member

**Improves on:**

- Richards application Ser. 109,903 (1902): one cycle per key depression instead of repeating
