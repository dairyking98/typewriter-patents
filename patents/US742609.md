## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriter platens must be light yet rigid enough to resist flexure, and need a hard writing surface for manifolding and a softer one for ordinary work.

**Solution:** A thin sheet body-plate of aluminium or steel carries hard-rubber writing surfaces on both sides, and the platen is hinged so it can be reversed.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** platen; body-plate; writing surfaces; hinge
