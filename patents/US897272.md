## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator needs to print a character such as a colon without a hand on the keyboard, and needs both hands free for other work.

**Solution:** An attachment for an existing typewriter has a locking plate that holds the space key in its depressed position, so the operator can leave the space bar down and use the hands freely.

**Features:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** space key; space key levers; connecting brackets; locking plate; fulcrum bar
