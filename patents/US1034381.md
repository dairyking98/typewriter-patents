## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**How it works (reading):** Supporting arms on the erasing plate carry auxiliary guide rollers that move the card to the carriage feed rollers, and one guide roller moves for easy insertion and removal.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards are hard to insert and remove for printing titles and entries.

**Solution:** Supporting arms on the erasing plate carry auxiliary guide rollers that move the card to the carriage feed rollers, and one guide roller moves for easy insertion and removal.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
