## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A back check on the escape wheel must be adjusted finely, and without it the escapement overruns after line lock.

**Solution:** Movable carriage stop set one letter space beyond the usual setting to correct the overrun, which removes the need for the back check.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
