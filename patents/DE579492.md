**How it works (reading):** A round ink plate is turned by a one-way gear from the moving carriage, and a rubbing roller applies ink to the plate and works it in before the plate inks the types; the transfer rate is adjustable with a pressure screw.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink is not rubbed in enough on the paint carrier, so the inking roller gives uneven colour.

**Solution:** A round ink plate is turned by a one-way gear from the moving carriage, and a rubbing roller applies ink to the plate and works it in before the plate inks the types; the transfer rate is adjustable with a pressure screw.
