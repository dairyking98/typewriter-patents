## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typolithograph fast printer apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing on both faces of the printing material

**How it works (reading):** The operator turns a dial-plate handle to bring a grooved type drum to the desired letter, and the drum is pressed onto a paper-carrying cylinder that is turned to suit the size of type, with an ink distributor.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A faster printing apparatus is wanted, and the inventor sets out three parts: the carriage, a rocking frame, and the inking mechanism.

**Solution:** The operator turns a dial-plate handle to bring a grooved type drum to the desired letter, and the drum is pressed onto a paper-carrying cylinder that is turned to suit the size of type, with an ink distributor.
