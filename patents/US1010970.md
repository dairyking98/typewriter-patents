## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to paper feeding mechanism for typewriting machines and the general object of said invention is to provide improved devices of the class specified.

**How it works (reading):** Tally strip feeding mechanism on the platen carrier, with a line-spacing ratchet and a tally feed frame.
