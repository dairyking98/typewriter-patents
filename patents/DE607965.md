## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing shorthand machines are complicated and hard to use, because vowels need three height shifts on top of a double shift.

**Solution:** Each type carries three characters on a double shift, so the three characters stand at top, main and bottom lines. Vowels are shown partly by height (two heights) and partly by stroke weight (three weights from bevelled type flanks), and keys have a small and a broad striking surface so the force used changes the impression.
