## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on a key switch for typewriters according to patent 348959-In the embodiment the main potential is the return of the paper cart, possibly with difficulties tied together.

**How it works (reading):** The approach of the pawl lies against a body connected to the locking rod, so the spring pressure cannot tilt the pawls out of their plane of oscillation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pawls on the carriage-return ratchet tilt or rub, making the paper carriage shudder on return.

**Solution:** The approach of the pawl lies against a body connected to the locking rod, so the spring pressure cannot tilt the pawls out of their plane of oscillation.
