It has already been suggested on typewriters, the line finder is firmly attached to the guide rail for the paper roller frame to attach, and since this guide rail is raised with the roller frame when a capital letter should be written, the line finder in this way too to raise with. The subject of the invention differs from these known arrangements in that the line finder is in a movable articulated connection with the switch frame stands by which the lift of the paper roller frame is concerned, so that the mobility of the line finder is twofold and not just because of the position which depends on the guide rail forming part of the switching frame. This line finder is, however, positively connected to the guide rail by a resilient arm, so that the line finder also returns when the rail returns to its original position is lowered, without that on this part consisting of thin sheet metal Shocks are exerted, which are inevitable when raising and lowering the slide are.
