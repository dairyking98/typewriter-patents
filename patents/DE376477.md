## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A line finder fixed to the rail that lifts the paper roller frame gets shocks when the frame is raised and lowered for capitals.

**Solution:** The line finder is hung in a movable articulated link on the switch frame and is joined to the guide rail by a resilient arm, so it returns with the rail without hard impacts.

The subject of the invention differs from these known arrangements in that the line finder is in a movable articulated connection with the switch frame stands by which the lift of the paper roller frame is concerned, so that the mobility of the line finder is twofold and not just because of the position which depends on the guide rail forming part of the switching frame. This line finder is, however, positively connected to the guide rail by a resilient arm, so that the line finder also returns when the rail returns to its original position is lowered, without that on this part consisting of thin sheet metal Shocks are exerted, which are inevitable when raising and lowering the slide are.
