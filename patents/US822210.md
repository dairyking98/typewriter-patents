## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Front-strike typebar machines need a simple way to release the escapement for manual carriage shifting and to line-space, and the ribbon needs feeding.

**Solution:** Line-space levers on the carriage double as carriage-release and also drive the ribbon feed on each line-space actuation.

**How it works:** A carriage of two end pieces and front and rear rods slides on a fixed horizontal rail at the rear. Typebars are pivoted below and in front of the platen on a curved segment. The platen shaft turns for line spacing via the levers, which release the escapement, and a part of the line-space linkage advances the ribbon. The carriage drops for upper-case printing.
