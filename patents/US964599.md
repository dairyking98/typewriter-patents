## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**How it works (reading):** A platen with a resilient rubber cover over a shell and bearing disks reduces impact noise and backs the paper at the print point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type strike noise is loud on a hard platen.

**Solution:** A platen with a resilient rubber cover over a shell and bearing disks reduces impact noise and backs the paper at the print point.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
