## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a novel arrangement of paper feed devices and an alining or scale-bar in a front strike writing machine.

**How it works (reading):** Feed roll shafts sit in bearings in carriage plates with pivoted links; pressure rolls are drawn toward the platen by springs, and an alining scale bar is set on the paper table with rear feed rolls positively driven from the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed and an alining scale bar must be combined so the scale and the feed rolls work together on a front-strike machine.

**Solution:** Feed roll shafts sit in bearings in carriage plates with pivoted links; pressure rolls are drawn toward the platen by springs, and an alining scale bar is set on the paper table with rear feed rolls positively driven from the platen.

**How it works:** Rocking the feed rolls away from the platen and back controls the paper, while the scale and feed are coupled through the link and gear arrangement.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** pressure rolls on links; alining scale bar; positive gear drive from platen
