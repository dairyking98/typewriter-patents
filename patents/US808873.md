## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (linotype key-operated escapement control) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for assembling matrices and space bands

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to mechanism wherein a series of finger-keys control powerdriven devices for actuating the matrix-releasing escapements of linotype-machines, the ejecting devices of type-setting machines, and similar devices.

**How it works (reading):** Each finger-key controls a reciprocating vertical toothed bar, which is driven by a continuously rotating toothed roll and actuates the matrix-releasing escapement; spring-loaded bars are positively advanced and then disengaged.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Finger-key escapement bars for linotype machines must advance positively and then disengage fully so they can return to their normal positions.

**Solution:** Each finger-key controls a reciprocating vertical toothed bar, which is driven by a continuously rotating toothed roll and actuates the matrix-releasing escapement; spring-loaded bars are positively advanced and then disengaged.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
