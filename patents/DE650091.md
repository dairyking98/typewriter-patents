## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A heavy long carriage spring must be strongly preloaded, so its force varies along the travel and disturbs the typebar impact.

**Solution:** A separate power-driven carriage feed gear, independent of the tension spring, engages the carriage rack and is controlled by the operating keys.

**How it works:** A power-driven feed gear steps the carriage for each key, independent of the spring force.

**Features:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
