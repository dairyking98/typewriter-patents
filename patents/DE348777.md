## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** A bracket carries a spring-loaded cone that engages notches for the writing and tilted positions, adjusting screws set the writing position, and a turn of the bracket releases the carrier for removal.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The tilting carriage carrier cannot be held tilted, the tilt angle cannot be changed, and removing it is cumbersome.

**Solution:** A bracket carries a spring-loaded cone that engages notches for the writing and tilted positions, adjusting screws set the writing position, and a turn of the bracket releases the carrier for removal.
