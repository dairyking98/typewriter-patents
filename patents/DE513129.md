## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**How it works (reading):** Pins on the key levers slide along horizontal guide edges of the intermediate levers, shortening the drive lever arm for faster stop acceleration.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stop speed and collisions limit writing speed in travel machines with laterally inclined type-lever planes.

**Solution:** Pins on the key levers slide along horizontal guide edges of the intermediate levers, shortening the drive lever arm for faster stop acceleration.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
