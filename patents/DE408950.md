## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A slide in a fixed guide crosses a slot in the shift lever so the type basket displacement is limited by the slide.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Elastic stop inserts on type basket shift wore out and changed the stroke, causing uneven lines.

**Solution:** A slide in a fixed guide crosses a slot in the shift lever so the type basket displacement is limited by the slide.

**Features:** [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
