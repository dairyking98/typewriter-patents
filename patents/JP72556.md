## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Japanese tray typewriters print where the writer cannot check the text, or the machine is complex and slow.

**Solution:** A hammer-and-pointer unit slides over a type magazine and index plate, printing upward through a ribbon onto a small paper cylinder close above it, which swings the printed line into view at each stroke or can be set not to turn for speed.

**How it works:** The type magazine and index plate move together sideways by a handle, while the hammer unit with pointer and handle slides on cross rails the other way, so the pointer is brought onto the wanted character. Pressing the handle swings the hammer upward, driving the type through an inked ribbon against the paper on the cylinder. The printing lever roller pushes on a bent lever that rocks a cylinder frame and a pawl-fed rotor, turning the cylinder so the printed place is momentarily shown to the typist and returns. A limiter on the bent lever gives three settings: full turn, part turn for speed, or none for horizontal writing. A spring-drawn long rack with fixed and moving pawls feeds the cylinder sideways one tooth at a time for character or line spacing.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention is a typewriter characterised as follows. In a paper-cylinder device that feeds character spacing and line spacing suitably, vertically or horizontally, a cylinder frame matching the cylinder is mounted on the cylinder support. At each printing action of the printing lever the cylinder frame is moved through the printing lever, turning the cylinder automatically, or the frame is turned by hand by working the bent lever alone; the cylinder thus turns forward and back repeatedly over a fixed range and shows the printed face at a place other than the printing position. Its object is a printing mechanism that is simple, printing with the type and paper close together, and that is particularly advanced both in simplicity and in high-speed printing: with important text, the paper face can be turned each time and the characters checked; the characters can be checked when needed; or printing can be done lightly, at top speed and with full power, without checking.
