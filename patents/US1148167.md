## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to means for connecting and disconnecting the platen and line-space wheel in typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A multi-jaw clutch grips the platen to the line-space wheel; a toggle joins the jaws to the operating levers, a single cam expands them, and a latch locks the cam.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen and line-space wheel can slip relative to each other, and prior split-ring clutches are not powerful or positive enough.

**Solution:** A multi-jaw clutch grips the platen to the line-space wheel; a toggle joins the jaws to the operating levers, a single cam expands them, and a latch locks the cam.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
