## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is a device for moving the stop-bar in the tabulatinglmechanism of type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A shaft shifted lengthwise turns the stop-bar by lever action, and a slight rotation shifts it by toothed gear, so one control does both.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tabulator stop-bar needs two lever motions, one to turn it and one to shift it, which takes too much space and time.

**Solution:** A shaft shifted lengthwise turns the stop-bar by lever action, and a slight rotation shifts it by toothed gear, so one control does both.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** stop-bar with rack; shaft; stop; paper-carriage; toothed gear
