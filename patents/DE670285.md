## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type carrier training in cash register printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**How it works (reading):** Printing units use deepened (negative) types so the original prints each character white inside a black bar, while a carbon copy comes out too illegible to pass as genuine; filler pieces close the gaps between items.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary printing in cash registers and accounting machines is too clear, so an original receipt can be copied on carbon paper and the copy passed off as genuine.

**Solution:** Printing units use deepened (negative) types so the original prints each character white inside a black bar, while a carbon copy comes out too illegible to pass as genuine; filler pieces close the gaps between items.
