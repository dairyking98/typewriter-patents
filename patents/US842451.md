## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A multicolour ribbon needs a vibrator settable so a different field is at the printing point.

**Solution:** Ribbon vibrator adjustable on a Monarch machine, or acting as a plain guide.

**How it works:** Adjusting the vibrator's set position brings another field of the ribbon to the print point; set otherwise it acts as a mere guide.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; ribbon; typebar; key-lever; carriage

**Improves on:**

- Monarch typewriter: adds colour-field selection
