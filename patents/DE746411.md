## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (print hammer drive, card-controlled machines)

**How it works (reading):** The toothed drive member gets a coupling tooth per type group, so the engagement time becomes large and the machine runs at higher speed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a card-controlled printer, the coupling of the print hammer drive is delayed by a double card mark, which limits the top machine speed.

**Solution:** The toothed drive member gets a coupling tooth per type group, so the engagement time becomes large and the machine runs at higher speed.

**Features:** [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md)

**Parts:** type drum; toothed drive member; print hammers; coupling member; card reader
