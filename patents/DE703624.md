## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key mechanism for teleprinters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the transmitting end with keyboard co-operating with code-bars

**How it works (reading):** One or two common stop rails at a fixed distance from the key levers limit the rail travel and hold the same end position at every setting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keypad selector rails are set imprecisely, and keys can bend the rails if they strike the upper edge of the rail.

**Solution:** One or two common stop rails at a fixed distance from the key levers limit the rail travel and hold the same end position at every setting.

**Features:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)
