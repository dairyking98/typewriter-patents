> Description Translated from German Keypad for teleprinters Teletypewriter transmitters are mostly controlled by a keypad that is designed similar to that of normal teleprinters. By pressing a key Selector rails adjusted, which control the position of send contacts. With the previously known arrangements, the assembly and that Setting the key mechanism considerable difficulties. The selector rails are mostly Flat and provided with incisions, on the slopes of the key levers attack and move the rails. To ensure a reliable shift of the rails To achieve this, the key lever must be very specific in relation to the rails Take position. This must be chosen so that on the one hand the rail when it is pressed down is detected on the slope of the key lever and on the other hand so that the rail can move by the size of the adjustment path when the key lever is in the upper end position. These conditions result for every keyboard a very specific position of the button lever opposite the selector rails, both in the elevation as well as in the lateral direction.
