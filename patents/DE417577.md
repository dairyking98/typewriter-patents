## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-spacing devices for portables are complicated and the platen may not be driven reliably at the line break.

**Solution:** A one-piece sheet-metal line-feed lever whose drive pawl and anti-overrun pawl are on a spring-loaded, axially sliding axle, with an adjustable stop that sets single or double line feed.

**How it works:** A shift lever pivots on an axle that can also slide axially against a spring. Its pawl sits just above the platen ratchet without touching it, so the platen can be turned freely by hand either way. Pulling the lever drives the pawl into the ratchet teeth and turns the platen one or two lines, depending on which of two stop edges on a pivoting stop lever limits the lever's rest position. A second shorter pawl on the same lever stops overshoot, and a detent roller on a leaf spring holds the platen.
