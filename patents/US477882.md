## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage of Remington, Caligraph or Yost style machines by hand to begin a new line.

**Solution:** Two extra keys with double levers release the rack from the pawls and drive a cog train that winds a coil spring and snail-box to pull the carriage back by belt.

**How it works:** A key lever pulls rod 5 to lift the ratchet-bearing frame off the spacing pawls, then rod 6 turns a cog wheel and spur wheel about twice on a shaft. This tensions a coil spring on a snail-box which hauls a belt attached to the carriage, returning it to the line start or any chosen position. Releasing the key restores the parts. The slotted pin joint makes the release happen before the return pull.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** key; double lever; pitman rod; ratchet frame; spacing pawl; cog wheel; spur wheel; coil spring; snail-box; belt

**Numbers:** spur wheel turns about twice per stroke
