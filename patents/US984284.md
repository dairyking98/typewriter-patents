## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On the Royal Standard, a latch held the shifting platen carriage in lower case, but upper case position needed separate support.

**Solution:** One pivoted latch both locks the carriage in lower case and, with a rearward projection, supports it in raised upper-case position.

**How it works:** The shift links first withdraw the latch hook from a bracket on a downward extension of the shifting carriage, then lift the carriage. As it rises, a rearward projection on the latch passes under the bracket and holds the carriage up in upper case. The latch pivots on an eccentric bolt so its position can be adjusted. Returning to lower case disengages and re-engages the hook.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** latch; latch hook; bracket; shifting platen carriage; shift link; eccentric pivot bolt

**Improves on:**

- [US858179](../patents/US858179.md): Hess latch only locked lower case; this one also supports upper-case position
