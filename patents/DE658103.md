## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control embossing device for registering machines)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a control embossing device in which these additional parts thereby omitted that the control embossing during the displacement of the printed document is effected by means of the conveyor device, wherein by interlocking the raised and deepened engravings of the conveyor elements when driving one element that others will be taken along.

**How it works (reading):** Interlocking raised and recessed engravings on the document conveyor elements let one driven element carry the other along, so the control embossing is made during document feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Control embossing on receipts needed an extra hammer and die in the printing unit.

**Solution:** Interlocking raised and recessed engravings on the document conveyor elements let one driven element carry the other along, so the control embossing is made during document feed.
