## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides stationary

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of the present invention is to construct the guide or vibrator in such a manner as to simplify and facilitate the operation of threading or unthreadingi the ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** Hooks on opposite sides of the printing point catch the ribbon edge, with upward guiding horns beside them, forming closed eyes that hold the ribbon when threaded.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Threading the ribbon through the vibrating guide is difficult and the ribbon is not held securely.

**Solution:** Hooks on opposite sides of the printing point catch the ribbon edge, with upward guiding horns beside them, forming closed eyes that hold the ribbon when threaded.
