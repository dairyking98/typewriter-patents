## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-space devices for machines with a raised-platen shift are spread over several parts and hard to fit and remove.

**Solution:** Mount the whole line-space device on a single plate fixed to or integral with a platen slide bearing.

**How it works:** A plate on the right platen bearing carries the line ratchet wheel's spring-loaded detent roller, an eccentric button, and a slide holding the feed pawl. A shift lever with a roller presses a plate on the slide; moving the slide engages the pawl on the ratchet, and at rest a shoulder rides an inclined face and lifts the pawl so the platen turns freely. The slide's extension also lifts the carriage rack out of its teeth so the carriage can be pushed freely, or lifts the rack when the platen is raised. A rotatable, slidable shaft with a stop, set in notches, limits pawl travel to one or more teeth for line width.
