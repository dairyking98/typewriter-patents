## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines of the lever class had too few characters for the number of keys.

**Solution:** Mount the whole basket of double-type typebars on a support that slides at right angles to the line, so each bar presents a second type.

**How it works:** A top plate has rabbeted slides carrying a support with a circular opening around which the typebars are hung. Shifting keys pull rods and cranks on shafts that slide the support and basket rectilinearly. Normal position prints one type; shifted, the bars present another type at the printing point. Key levers are linked to typebars by wire. Opposed springs on the actuating shafts and spring seats for the key levers are described.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md)

**Parts:** top plate; slides; adjustable support; typebasket; typebar; connecting wire; shifting key; crank arm; shaft; spring seat

**Improves on:**

- single-type typebar machines: doubles the character set without more keys
