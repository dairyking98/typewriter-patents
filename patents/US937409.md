## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The front shield of a front-strike typewriter should be easy to detach and replace, and the tabulator index needs to be readable beside the tabulator keys.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal front shield held on by leaf-spring clips snapping over pins on the type-bar segment, carrying a slotted inclined portion with a tabulator index plate behind a celluloid cover.

**How it works:** The shield is pushed upward until its top meets the top plate, and leaf-spring catches riveted to its back engage threaded pins screwed into the type-bar rest segment. A depending inclined portion has a transverse slot through which an index sheet, covered by transparent celluloid and clamped by a screwed plate, can be read above the tabulator keys. Removing the screws frees the index sheet; the shield pulls off to detach.
