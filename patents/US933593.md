## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Hand-held or table apparatus with means for moistening or coating the articles or webs, or applying adhesive thereto

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the making of new and the renovation of exhausted'typewriter ribbons, and its object is to provide means whereby ribbons which have become dry or faint from use may be so treated as to enable them to again give a good impres- 510m.

**How it works (reading):** A device with a main roller, a liquid trough or inking means, jockey rollers and doctor blades applies ink or solvent regularly to the ribbon as it passes the roller, possibly several colours at once, and can be used standalone or attached to a typewriter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons that have dried out or faded from use cannot print well, and raw uninked ribbon needs inking.

**Solution:** A device with a main roller, a liquid trough or inking means, jockey rollers and doctor blades applies ink or solvent regularly to the ribbon as it passes the roller, possibly several colours at once, and can be used standalone or attached to a typewriter.
