## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The line ratchet had to be released from the paper roller so the roller could turn freely.

**Solution:** A conical friction dome on the roller axis; spring-loaded levers press the ratchet wheel rim against the dome, released by a push button.
