## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key levers

**How it works (reading):** Short identical key levers roll on a flat keyboard with curved undersides, guided in slots and stopped by surfaces above and below.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Long wooden key levers warp and jam, and the rows have unequal lengths.

**Solution:** Short identical key levers roll on a flat keyboard with curved undersides, guided in slots and stopped by surfaces above and below.
