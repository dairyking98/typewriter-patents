## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On Oliver-type machines the operator cannot see the exact point where the type strikes the paper.

**Solution:** An attachment with a ribbon guide and a visible printing-point indicator is fitted to the machine so the operator can see the impression point during printing.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon-guide; platen; indicator device on supporting arm
