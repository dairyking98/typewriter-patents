## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon-guides for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon guide is moved transversely by a lever at each typebar operation, and it is designed so the ribbon can be inserted and withdrawn easily and cannot be accidentally displaced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon guide can be accidentally displaced, and the ribbon is hard to insert and withdraw.

**Solution:** A ribbon guide is moved transversely by a lever at each typebar operation, and it is designed so the ribbon can be inserted and withdrawn easily and cannot be accidentally displaced.
