## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper fed up in front of the platen needs a guide that fits the platen contour and can be adjusted and held firmly.

**Solution:** A pivoted guide and guide roll on an arm, held to the platen by a spring, carried on a one-piece splined bracket with spring-jaw clutch.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** guide; guide roll; spring; bracket; clutch
