## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Turning a platen independently of its feed wheel (for interlineation) needs a reliable coupling that stays put and can be worked one-handed.

**Solution:** Wedge-ended clutch levers inside the hollow platen, moved by an axial shifting rod, spread curved jaws against a sleeve to couple platen and feed wheel.

**How it works:** The feed wheel, turned step by step by a ratchet lever with detent pawl, has a hub carrying curved clutch jaws pivoted on it. Inside the hollow platen, clutch levers with tapering wedges are shifted by a sleeve on an axial rod, pressing the jaws against the bore of a clutch sleeve on the platen head. This couples the platen to the feed wheel; pushing the rod the other way uncouples it so the platen turns freely. The clutch stays in either state without manual aid. Several modified forms are shown.
