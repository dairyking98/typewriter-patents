## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more especially to the ribbon 'mechanism of that class of typewriting -machines wherein a vibrator or carrier is employedto move the ribbon to cover and uncover the printing point during printing operations.

**How it works (reading):** Improved means let the ribbon stay quiescent, clear of the printing field, during printing when the vibrator is not used.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator moves the ribbon at every printing operation and the inventor wants to dispense with that movement.

**Solution:** Improved means let the ribbon stay quiescent, clear of the printing field, during printing when the vibrator is not used.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
