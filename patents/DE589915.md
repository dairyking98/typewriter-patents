## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line shift wheel clutch, in which a spreader ring and within the side walls of the character ratchet a spreader member cooperating with a wedge gear is mounted.

**How it works (reading):** A spreader member pivoted in the platen's expansion ring opening is spring-pressed into the spread position, giving a compact, easily reached expanding clutch that holds with low force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier line-shift clutches were bulky and hard to reach, or spread poorly and needed too much force.

**Solution:** A spreader member pivoted in the platen's expansion ring opening is spring-pressed into the spread position, giving a compact, easily reached expanding clutch that holds with low force.
