> Description Translated from German Line shift wheel clutch The invention relates to a line shift wheel clutch, in which a spreader ring and within the side walls of the character ratchet a spreader member cooperating with a wedge gear is mounted. Line shift wheel clutches are already in different embodiments known. But they had the disadvantage that they were either very bulky Construction and were therefore difficult to access or only poorly spread of the expansion ring and thus an unsafe coupling resulted or a great force for a flawless clutch required when setting the platen quickly could not always be applied. According to the invention, these disadvantages are now eliminated by the Spreader member at its one protruding into the expansion opening of the expansion ring End is pivotally mounted within the area of the expansion ring opening and from a spring acting at its opposite end into the spread position is pivoted. As a result, an expanding coupling is obtained, which with low A secure coupling enables forces and a compact, easily accessible one Type results.
