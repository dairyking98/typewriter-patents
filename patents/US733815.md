## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register with typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with punching mechanism

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to cash-registers, or more particularly to a cash-drawer and operating mechanism therefor, in combination with a type-writing machine or recording mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A cash drawer combined with a type-writing or recording mechanism so that the drawer cannot be opened without first printing a record of the sale; the tally of sales and receipts is printed as a permanent record.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash drawers gave no permanent record of transactions, and money could be taken out without any printed entry.

**Solution:** A cash drawer combined with a type-writing or recording mechanism so that the drawer cannot be opened without first printing a record of the sale; the tally of sales and receipts is printed as a permanent record.
