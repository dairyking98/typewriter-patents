## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line typewriter with photographed type records) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line typewriter which is displaceable in the longitudinal direction next to each other vertically, arranged and equipped with all types of characters Types, etching carriers that are excluded and then printed or photographed.

**How it works (reading):** Type carriers hang from vertical sliders in the housing and are moved by a hand lever and a drop slide, so the carrier is neither pulled nor bent.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Vertical type carriers on flexible bands need several guide rollers and tensioning for vertical movement.

**Solution:** Type carriers hang from vertical sliders in the housing and are moved by a hand lever and a drop slide, so the carrier is neither pulled nor bent.
