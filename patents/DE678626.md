## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon feed reversal for cash register printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a feeding and reversing device for the ribbon of printing units, especially on cash registers and the like Recording machines with a reciprocating motion through the printing unit set link two alternately effective switching pawls for the ratchet wheels of two Carries ribbon drums.

**How it works (reading):** Each pawl is engaged at the end of its switching stroke by a fixed stop and returned to engagement at the end of the idle stroke by a stop on the ribbon, so the pawl is disengaged during the working stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon-feed pawls press on the ratchet wheels all the time and wear the teeth during the idle stroke.

**Solution:** Each pawl is engaged at the end of its switching stroke by a fixed stop and returned to engagement at the end of the idle stroke by a stop on the ribbon, so the pawl is disengaged during the working stroke.

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
