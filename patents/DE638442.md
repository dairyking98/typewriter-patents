## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On low typewriters with a liftable carriage and a horizontal ratchet wheel on it, backspace stepping must work in both carriage positions.

**Solution:** The tooth of the horizontal backspace rail is stepped so it engages the carriage ratchet in both the basic and the shifted carriage position.

**How it works:** A horizontal rail is guided on a pin in a curved slot and hinged to an angle lever, linked to a reset (back-space) lever. Pressing the lever moves the rail against its spring so its tooth engages a ratchet tooth and slides the wheel back one step; releasing returns all parts by spring.
