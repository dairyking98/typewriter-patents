## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a device for moving the stop rod the table writing device on typewriters .

**How it works (reading):** The lever is replaced by a shaft that moves the stop rod along its length through a lever transmission when shifted axially, and rotates it slightly so a gear drive engages the rack section of the stop rod.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The inventor says the known lever for moving the carriage stop rod needs two different movements, takes up a lot of space and is awkward to install.

**Solution:** The lever is replaced by a shaft that moves the stop rod along its length through a lever transmission when shifted axially, and rotates it slightly so a gear drive engages the rack section of the stop rod.
