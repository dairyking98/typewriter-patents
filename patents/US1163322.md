**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is the production of a machine adapted to be used in practice by students desiring to acquire the knowledge and skill necessary for the proper or skilful operation of a typewriter;

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Students cannot learn touch typing without the cost of a standard typewriter.

**Solution:** A practice machine with a base board carrying keys laid out like a standard typewriter, with keys of proper resistance and location and cushioning, but no printing mechanism.
