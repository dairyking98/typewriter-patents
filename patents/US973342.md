## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Column stops on the adjustable carriage of existing Underwood machines must be set directly on the carriage and are not easily applied to existing machines.

**Solution:** A pneumatic column stop mounted on the carriage that is projected into the path of a carriage stop, selected by depressible bulbs or keys at the front of the machine, which also release the carriage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
