## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Existing practice keyboards with key bolts in stacked sleeves are costly and use a lot of material.

**Solution:** Vertically sliding key bolts with rectangular cross-section are guided between two spaced stepped metal sheets with matching rectangular holes, instead of individual sleeves.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Learning and practice keyboard for typewriter writing. Object The invention is a learning and practice keyboard for typewriting. There are already learning and practice keyboards known in which movable Key bolts are guided in individual sleeves that are stacked in steps arranged strips, are embedded. This type of construction is cumbersome to manufacture and takes up a lot of material. In contrast, the keyboard according to the invention, which is shown in the drawing schematically in cross-section and in a view of two superimposed at the distance arranged, step-shaped bent sheets c with rectangular übereinanderli two by two vertical; egenden holes in this set, Key bolts b, which are slidable in the vertical direction, have a corresponding rectangular cross-section and are arranged between the two metal sheets c, enveloping the key bolts and pushing upwards and mode of action.
