## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to automatically operated typewriters, and has for its object the provision of means which is automatically operable at predetermined intervals to impart a rapid turning or sheet-feeding movement to the platen-roll of a typewriter, and is independent of the means customarily employed for turning the platen-roll to impart line spacing movements thereto.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment with an air bellows that gives the platen roll a rapid turning movement, independent of the line-spacing mechanism.
