## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (planographic printing plates from typewriter transfer) · **Subject (patent classes):** by mechanical pressure, e.g. from a typewriter by electrical recording ribbon therefor

**How it works (reading):** The rubber cover gets a non-stretchable base and a clamping device with pins, holes and screws that fix it at the same position on the roller both in the typewriter and on the transfer roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typed lines printed on a rubber-coated partial roller must be transferred to a printing plate in exact register, but the rubber cover shifts between typing and transfer.

**Solution:** The rubber cover gets a non-stretchable base and a clamping device with pins, holes and screws that fix it at the same position on the roller both in the typewriter and on the transfer roller.
