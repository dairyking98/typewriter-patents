## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a manuscript holder for typewriters, especially for heavy manuscripts such as files and books and the like.

**How it works (reading):** The desk board slides and clamps on a frame of identical supports joined by spacer strips and pivots in bearing blocks between stops, so loads are carried in bending and torsion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter copy holder cannot take heavy books and files; its supports bend and vibrate when swung.

**Solution:** The desk board slides and clamps on a frame of identical supports joined by spacer strips and pivots in bearing blocks between stops, so loads are carried in bending and torsion.
