## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists of an attachment to type-writing machines, whereby the entire system of keys may be securely locked when the last letter which can be printed or written upon a line has been struck, so that a further impression cannot be made by the type of said last letter, and blurring or blotting of the same thereby prevented.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage-mounted stop trips a frame lever that locks the rocking feed support so no key can be depressed until the carriage is returned.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** At the end of a line, pressing further keys prints the last letter repeatedly on one spot, blurring it.

**Solution:** A carriage-mounted stop trips a frame lever that locks the rocking feed support so no key can be depressed until the carriage is returned.

**How it works:** A stop or arm on the carriage or its rack bar contacts the top of a pivoted lever on the frame during the last space. The lever's lower toe then blocks the rocking support that carries the feed pawl, which is moved by every key and the space bar. With that support immobilised all keys stay locked. Running the carriage back releases the lever by gravity or a spring.
