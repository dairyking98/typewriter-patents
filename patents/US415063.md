## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary receptacles for articles, e.g. erasers, pencils

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to avoid unnecessary delay in the work of copying upon type-writing machines, to provide means whereby lead-pencils, erasers, and the like may be within ready and convenient reach of the operator, and to provide certain improved "details of construction;

**How it works (reading):** A rack or holder mounted on the machine frame holds pencils, erasers, paper fasteners and similar items within easy reach.
