## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon switching by sensors was unreliable because it depended on ribbon tension.

**Solution:** A feed pawl and pawl-carrying rocker arm are controlled by a sensing element on the outside of the ribbon spool, so the reversal does not depend on tension.
