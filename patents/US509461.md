## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Writing in bound books or on a sheet lying flat needs a firm leaf support that holds the page in a fixed plane wherever it lies in the book, lets either of two facing pages be written on, and keeps the writing in full view.

**Solution:** A flat-bed typebar machine in which a book-holding casing whose top is the platen moves in two directions beneath type arms that strike down from a semicircular ring in front of the operator.

**How it works:** The type levers are pivoted around a vertical skirt at the front of an overhanging hood and strike downward onto the horizontal platen forming the top of the book-holding casing. Each key moves a rock shaft, a secondary rock shaft and connecting rods to its type lever, with a return spring. A carriage sliding longitudinally on the base gives line spacing, and the book casing slides transversely on that carriage for letter and word spacing. The opening of the front is left clear so the writing is visible. A removable bed plate or platen supports the leaf.
