## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide eiiicient mechanisms of the characters pointed out and to render the Imachine as a whole more eflicient.

**How it works (reading):** A whole type-writing machine in which the tabulating, line-spacing, line-lock, pressure-roller release, carriage-return and scale and pointer mechanisms are arranged so that one may actuate or assist another.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor states an object of making the tabulating, automatic line-spacing, line-lock, pressure-roller release, carriage-return and scale mechanisms more efficient, and of having them work together.

**Solution:** A whole type-writing machine in which the tabulating, line-spacing, line-lock, pressure-roller release, carriage-return and scale and pointer mechanisms are arranged so that one may actuate or assist another.
