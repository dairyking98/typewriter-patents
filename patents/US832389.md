## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates toimprovements in sound-deadening cases for type-writers;

**How it works (reading):** The whole writing mechanism is enclosed in a box lined with sound-deadening felt or rubber, exposing only the key-levers, with lidded openings for paper insertion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The clicking noise of type-levers striking is loud.

**Solution:** The whole writing mechanism is enclosed in a box lined with sound-deadening felt or rubber, exposing only the key-levers, with lidded openings for paper insertion.
