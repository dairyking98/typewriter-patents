## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for releasing the platen of a typewriting machine, the control of the usual lineacev wheel, or of the usual spring-detent w ich engages said wheel.

**How it works (reading):** A clutching lever on the platen axle head bites the line-space wheel, and an axially sliding finger wheel releases it and can also rotate the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen release and line spacing need a simple, compact device readily fitted to existing machines.

**Solution:** A clutching lever on the platen axle head bites the line-space wheel, and an axially sliding finger wheel releases it and can also rotate the platen.
