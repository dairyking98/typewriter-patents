## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line switching device for booking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switch device for the platen mounted on a paper carriage of powered accounting or similar calculating machines with one of one Motor-driven separate drive that has a button-controlled single-turn clutch can carry out the line feed independently of a machine gear.

**How it works (reading):** A one-turn clutch on a separate motor drive performs the line feed and can be engaged by control buttons or by carriage stops, independent of the main machine gear.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line feed in powered accounting machines happens abruptly and noisily during the machine cycle and can only be triggered by the machine drive.

**Solution:** A one-turn clutch on a separate motor drive performs the line feed and can be engaged by control buttons or by carriage stops, independent of the main machine gear.
