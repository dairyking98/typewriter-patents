**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to document holder for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A fastening rail with a wedge-shaped strip inserted under the machine base, suction cups for flat backs, and a drawstring around the frame fixes it screwlessly; a pocket-shaped paper guide wall carries the sheet at eye level.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Document holders for typewriters were fixed with screws or holes specific to each make, so a good holder could not be fitted to any machine.

**Solution:** A fastening rail with a wedge-shaped strip inserted under the machine base, suction cups for flat backs, and a drawstring around the frame fixes it screwlessly; a pocket-shaped paper guide wall carries the sheet at eye level.

This is a significant disadvantage which on the one hand prevents the use of a concept holder that is good in itself and on the other hand -, due to the necessary reconstruction or constriction, a change in the working method conditional.
