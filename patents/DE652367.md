> Description Translated from German Document holder The invention relates to document holder for typewriters. It is known that such concept holders are made with the interposition of a fastening strip attached to the typewriter, the fastening means for each type of typewriter must be different. The fasteners must therefore mostly be in use of screws and clamping elements, often only with the appropriate arrangement of screw holes attached to the typewriter frame. This is a significant disadvantage which on the one hand prevents the use of a concept holder that is good in itself and on the other hand -, due to the necessary reconstruction or constriction, a change in the working method conditional. The holder to be inserted into the fastener that holds the sheet at eye level holds, is always designed as a narrow support in the known facilities, on which the writing sheet in the typewriter cart is hanging down abuts and can be damaged, which is why in a known device this Support is already provided so that it can be swung out, without thereby the intended Purpose is achieved.
