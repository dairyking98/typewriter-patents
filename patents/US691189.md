## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates particularly to the class of type-writing machines which employ an oscillating type-carrier which moves in one direction or the other about its axis to bring its characters to the impression-point and an impression-hammer which forces the paper against the characters at the impression-point to make the impression.

**How it works (reading):** A fixed finger enters a groove in the oscillating type segment to stop it moving up or down at the impression point, with a paper-moving shield.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Characters must be aligned and paper kept from being soiled by other characters.

**Solution:** A fixed finger enters a groove in the oscillating type segment to stop it moving up or down at the impression point, with a paper-moving shield.

**How it works:** A semicircular type segment on a vertical oscillating shaft has characters on its outer rim and a horizontal groove on the inner rim. A fixed finger enters the groove when the segment leaves home, preventing vertical movement; a notch lets it clear at home. A hammer forces paper to the character, a shield protects the paper, and rollers on a pivoted arm ink the type over a rotating inking platen.
