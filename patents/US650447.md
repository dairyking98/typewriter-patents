## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a machine which has been especially adapted for printing music.

**How it works (reading):** A music type-writer with ten sheets of drawings, built to be strong, compact and efficient. The mechanism is not described in the first 3000 characters.
