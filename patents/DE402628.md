## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** Several carbon ribbons on nested sleeves with arms are swung into or out of working position in turn by rotating a shaft with unevenly grouped pinion teeth meshing with toothed sectors.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making carbon copies with ordinary carbon sheets between papers is awkward; ribbons that carry the impression to several copies need a way to be brought in and out of position.

**Solution:** Several carbon ribbons on nested sleeves with arms are swung into or out of working position in turn by rotating a shaft with unevenly grouped pinion teeth meshing with toothed sectors.

**How it works:** A rod behind the platen carries three nested sleeves, each with a pair of arms holding a duplicating ribbon. Each arm pair has a toothed sector at its foot. A hollow shaft with a handle turns the rod's crank arms to move ribbons to working position, while an inner shaft with a pinion whose teeth are grouped at different levels swings the sectors, and hence the ribbons, one after another. The ribbons move along a path with two centres so they clear the platen. An eccentric driven from the platen ratchet and a ball detent in a yoke lock the ribbons in rest or working position and shift them slightly sideways so the full width is used.
