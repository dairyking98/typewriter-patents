## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars clash and blur the impression when two keys are struck together, limiting typing speed.

**Solution:** A taut cord or band threaded alternately through staples on the key levers and on fixed supports, with just enough slack for one key lever to go fully down.

**How it works:** Each key lever has a staple, and fixed staples sit on a rail or partitions between them. A band, string or wire passes alternately above and below these crosspieces. Ratchet rollers or threaded tubes set its tension so that one fully depressed lever takes up all the slack. If two keys are pressed together they share the slack and each typebar only rises half-way, so the bars cannot clash.
