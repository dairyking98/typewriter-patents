## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen could not be freely hand-rotated in either direction without separately releasing the platen check.

**Solution:** The line-space driving pawl also engages the check arm and lifts the platen check clear of the ratchet.

**How it works:** A platen check arm with a tooth is spring-held in the platen ratchet wheel. The line-space lever carries a push pawl that normally drives the ratchet step by step. A notch in the pawl end can instead engage a rounded part of the check arm, disengaging and locking it out so the platen turns freely. A pin turned by a finger-head limits lever stroke to one or two teeth.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet wheel; platen check arm; line-space lever; push pawl; leaf spring; stop pin; spiral spring

**Numbers:** line space of one or two ratchet teeth

**Improves on:**

- Yost line-space mechanism: one pawl both drives and releases the platen check
