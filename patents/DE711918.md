## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to machine frames for typewriters of light construction, in which an inner, all gear parts supporting structure is covered on all sides by an outer decorative frame.

**How it works (reading):** The decorative frame is one piece; the assembled machine is slid into it at an incline through a free passage above the keyboard.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Multi-part machine covers are costly to make and must be removed before the frame can be serviced.

**Solution:** The decorative frame is one piece; the assembled machine is slid into it at an incline through a free passage above the keyboard.
