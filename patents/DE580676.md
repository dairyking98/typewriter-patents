## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing device for punch card machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for operating hammers to effect impression arranged to be clutched to snatch roll

**How it works (reading):** A force organ synchronous with the drum engages a coupling tooth at the correct tooth interval, so hammer release always occurs at a fixed drum point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing from a rapidly rotating type drum fails when electromagnet actuation produces phase shifts, so the wrong type prints.

**Solution:** A force organ synchronous with the drum engages a coupling tooth at the correct tooth interval, so hammer release always occurs at a fixed drum point.
