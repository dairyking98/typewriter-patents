## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a one-piece typewriter body from Synthetic resin molding compound, consisting of a base frame supporting the bearings and a this enclosing cladding frame.

**How it works (reading):** A one-piece plastic body with a base frame, cladding frame and bearings is moulded together, with webs strengthening the bearing eyes and linking them to the cladding frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Metal typewriter bodies are heavy and cost much work to machine, while plastic frames are too weak at the bearings.

**Solution:** A one-piece plastic body with a base frame, cladding frame and bearings is moulded together, with webs strengthening the bearing eyes and linking them to the cladding frame.
