## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a duplicating device for typewriters in which a number of ribbons or the like between several Sheets of paper on the paper roll of the typewriter and opposite the stop point the type lever is arranged on the roller in such a way that only one side of the Ribbon makes an imprint on the correct side of the attached sheet of paper.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbons are carried on pairs of swinging arms, each in a slotted plate, so one or several ribbons can be swung into position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicating ribbons had to be brought together in front of the platen and could not be used individually.

**Solution:** Ribbons are carried on pairs of swinging arms, each in a slotted plate, so one or several ribbons can be swung into position.
