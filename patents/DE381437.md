> Description Translated from German Duplicating device. The invention relates to a duplicating device for typewriters in which a number of ribbons or the like between several Sheets of paper on the paper roll of the typewriter and opposite the stop point the type lever is arranged on the roller in such a way that only one side of the Ribbon makes an imprint on the correct side of the attached sheet of paper. They are such Devices known where sä llitliche Ribbons are brought together in front of the paper roller. The characteristic of the invention is that the ribbons individually or several together can be brought into position in front of the paper roller, the pairs of arms which carry the ribbons and are ineffective in the upright standing Stelhing as well as in the effective position in their position by a spring plate, which sweep straight edges of the arms close to their pivot point, be kept secure. The pairs of arms can be swung out together from the effective position into the ineffective. each ribbon is attached to a plate in your slot, which Plates are carried by one pair of arms each. each plate serves at the same time to cover one side of the ribbon in a known manner.
