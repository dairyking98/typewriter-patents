## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for returning the paper carriage. There are already facilities for returning the paper cart from typewriters, calculating machines and the like. known, in which the return movement of the carriage by one of an electric motor Drivable gear takes place with a rack arranged on the paper trolley can be brought into and out of engagement. The known facilities of this type are expensive not completely reliable in terms of adjustment and also in terms of reliability. The subject of the present invention now forms a carriage return device of the specified type, which is characterized by the extraordinary simplicity of the construction and unconditional reliability in the effect. This is achieved according to the invention essentially in that the activation of the electric motor, the drive and the simultaneous longitudinal displacement causes the axis of rotation of a toothed wheel, which thereby with one on the paper trolley arranged rack enters into engagement, through which in a known manner first the line feed and then the return of the paper carriage brought about will.
