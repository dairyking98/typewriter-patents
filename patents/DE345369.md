## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Thickened areas on each tie rod are engaged by a comb-like spring-loaded body that locks the keys; a lever and bolt release the trapped type lever and return it to rest.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers that jam together, and keys that must be locked, need a simple and practical locking and release arrangement.

**Solution:** Thickened areas on each tie rod are engaged by a comb-like spring-loaded body that locks the keys; a lever and bolt release the trapped type lever and return it to rest.
