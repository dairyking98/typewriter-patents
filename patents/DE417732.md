**How it works (reading):** A tapered ink container pivoted on a type-lever arm has outlet openings covered by absorbent fabric; the striking type presses the container and inks the face, and centrifugal force keeps the ink moving toward the outlets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type faces are not reliably supplied with ink from a simple pad as they strike.

**Solution:** A tapered ink container pivoted on a type-lever arm has outlet openings covered by absorbent fabric; the striking type presses the container and inks the face, and centrifugal force keeps the ink moving toward the outlets.
