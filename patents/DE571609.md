## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (tabulator for account statements) · **Subject (patent classes):** Methods or arrangements for marking the record carrier in digital fashion otherwise than by punching by printing code marks

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable regulating device enables the extra paper feed and can switch it off or make it depend on a monitoring device after a set number of cycles or on total printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bank statements need forms of equal length, so extra paper feed must be applied after the line feed, and this must be controllable.

**Solution:** An adjustable regulating device enables the extra paper feed and can switch it off or make it depend on a monitoring device after a set number of cycles or on total printing.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** grippers; drive pawl; hook; locking device; counting device
