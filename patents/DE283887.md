## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide device for typewriters, in which the paper guide rollers are on two parallel to the paper roller mounted axles are arranged.

**How it works (reading):** Segments carrying the guide rollers are lined up on a notched spacer rail and held on an axle, and springs press the axle and rollers against the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Guide rollers on the paper carriage need screws, nuts, pins or adjusting rings to hold them in position.

**Solution:** Segments carrying the guide rollers are lined up on a notched spacer rail and held on an axle, and springs press the axle and rollers against the platen.
