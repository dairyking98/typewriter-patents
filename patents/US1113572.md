## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** The leading end of the sheet controls the indicating means through an intermediate controlling mechanism, combining a friction drive with a positive toothed-gear drive for the central operative member.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator needs progressive indication of the sheet position, and a positive drive for the indicator is unreliable when the sheet controls it.

**Solution:** The leading end of the sheet controls the indicating means through an intermediate controlling mechanism, combining a friction drive with a positive toothed-gear drive for the central operative member.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** friction drive device; toothed gear drive; intermediate controlling mechanism; indices; central operative member
