**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to revoluble platens of typewriting machines, and its object is to provide simple, inexpensive and efficient.
