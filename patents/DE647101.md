## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Copy holders with a line indicator driven by belts or cords that wrap over the top and bottom plate edges make it hard to slide in wide drafts and impossible to place large ones.

**Solution:** Drive the line indicator with semi-rigid flexible steel strips actuated only from the lower edge, so the other three edges of the holder plate stay free.

**How it works:** An axle at the lower edge of the holder plate carries knobs, rollers and a ratchet wheel. Arched steel strips run over the rollers, held by ribbon springs and engaged by small pins through holes in the strip. The front free end of each strip floats over the plate and carries the line indicator, while the rear end slides in a protective channel behind the plate. A shift rod with a spring-loaded cam steps the ratchet one line at a time, with adjustable and fixed stops limiting the stroke, or the knobs turn it freely. The strips' own springiness presses the indicator on the plate but lets it be lifted off.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** holder plate; axle; roller; ratchet wheel; shift rod; switching cam; steel strip; ribbon spring; line indicator; protective channel; clamping shaft; adjustable foot

**Improves on:**

- copy holders with endless-belt or cord line indicators: actuation only from the lower edge leaves the other three edges free for any size of draft
