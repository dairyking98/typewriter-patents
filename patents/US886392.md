## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type action must be easy to assemble, disconnect and remove without dismantling other parts.

**Solution:** Key levers, sub-levers with open-ended slots and a type-bar hanger form a type action whose parts can be detached individually.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** Fig. 2 type bar and hanger
