## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with separate or detached types or dies

**How it works (reading):** Cast a wire gauze into the type body as reinforcement at the time of casting.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar type bodies are hollowed to save material and weight but become weak and often break in printing.

**Solution:** Cast a wire gauze into the type body as reinforcement at the time of casting.

**How it works:** The type body has a notch for the gripping lever of the typebar and is hollowed out. A metal wire gauze is placed in the mould so it is cast inside the body. This strengthens the body so it does not break, and allows more material to be removed to make the type lighter.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Type for a typewriter. Brief description of the drawings. Fig. 1 is a front view of the type of this device, Fig. 2 a side view of it, and Fig. 3 a section on line A—A of Fig. 1. Nature, action and effect of the device. This device relates to an improvement of type for a typewriter. Its gist is that a metal gauze is cast into the type, as reinforcement, at the same time as the type is cast. In the drawings, 1 is the body of the type and 2 the reinforcing wire gauze cast inside it. A typewriter type must have a notch イ into which the gripping lever of the typebar fits, and to save material and make it lighter, the body is commonly hollowed out as at ロ. Because of this the body tends to be too weak, and it often breaks during printing. This device was worked out with that in mind: when the type is cast, a wire gauze 2 is cast into the body at the same time as reinforcement. The body is thus made much stronger and does not break in use, and it even becomes possible to save more material than before and make the type lighter.
