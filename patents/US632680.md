## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A bookwork typewriter that runs over an open book needs a support that holds the book and presents both leaves flat under a platen.

**Solution:** A table with a hinged, split flat platen over an opening and a crank-and-worm-raised book support that lifts the open book up against the platen leaves.

**How it works:** The table top has an opening bordered by strips that carry the machine's track rails. Two platen leaves, hinged at their outer edges, close over the opening with a slot between them. The book rests on a vertically movable support below, lifted by racks driven by segment gears on two spindles, coupled through bevel gears to a cross shaft with a worm gear, turned by a front crank. The worm lock holds the height without separate locking. Upturned leaves pass through the slot and are laid flat on the platen under the weight of the tracks and machine.
