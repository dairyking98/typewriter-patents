For the purpose of screwing this so designed foot must the user first remove the rubber block on the foot, then insert the wood screw insert the opening provided for this purpose and tighten it with a screwdriver. The screwing on is difficult to carry out because the 21, n tightening means of a screwdriver the screwdriver slips easily, and experience has shown that it does difficult to hold the screwdriver in the direction of pressure. This disadvantage becomes with the furniture foot according to the invention eliminates that the fastening screw by means of square and riveted head is held in the bottom of the fixed part, and that both links are channel-shaped Have driving lugs for coupling both links, so that by turning both parts the screwing on or loosening of the Nöbelffuß is effected.
