## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin scales for typewriter platen knobs had to be fitted to each knob size, requiring stocking many sizes.

**Solution:** A stretchable rubber ring with a raised scale slips over the platen knob and is held by its inward edges, so one size fits the knob range.
