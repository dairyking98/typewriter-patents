## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting ma chines, and one of the objects thereof is to provide new and in'lproved means for adjusting the platen with respect to the printing plane.

**How it works (reading):** The platen can be moved relative to the printing plane to take the blow of the type bars through several sheets, is held fixed after adjustment, and a gage measures sheet thickness.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be moved to the right printing position for impression sheets, and the position must hold after adjustment.

**Solution:** The platen can be moved relative to the printing plane to take the blow of the type bars through several sheets, is held fixed after adjustment, and a gage measures sheet thickness.
