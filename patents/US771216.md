## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Closely set typebars rebound from the basket or cushion on returning and clash with the next bar in rapid typing.

**Solution:** A type-bar rest made of two nested helices of wire that is stiff but inelastic, so struck bars stay dead.

**How it works:** Horizontal front-strike typebars rest at their forward ends on a felt or leather pad against a segmental basket hung from the top plate. The basket is an inner helix of soft iron inside a larger brass helix (optionally triangular wire). When struck, the coils slide on each other and friction opposes both bending and recovery, so the basket flexes but does not spring back. The ends extend beyond the type system as a yielding support, fixed by screws to the top plate.
