## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage switching, ribbon lift and ribbon movement need springs that make the key stroke heavier and do not always work reliably.

**Solution:** A swinging tooth moved by the key lever engages a gap in the ratchet wheel and holds it, and a trigger pawl releases the ratchet near the end of the stroke, so the roller carriage's spring returns the tooth and parts after key release.
