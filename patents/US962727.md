## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A clutch letting the platen be turned freely apart from the line-space wheel needed to be simple, positive, cheap and fit existing machines.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded wedging clutch of conjoined cone rollers jamming between a cam face on the platen axle disc and the inside of the line-space wheel drum, released by a button on the platen axle end.

**How it works:** The line-space wheel is loose on the platen axle and forms a drum. Inside, a disc fixed to the axle has a lug with an inclined face; a lever in the drum carries a pair of conjoint cone-shaped rolls which run on the incline and wedge between it and the drum bore. Weak springs keep the rolls jammed so platen and wheel are locked. Pushing a button along the platen axis moves the lever so the rolls ride to a bevelled clearance and release; an extra push locks the jamming members open. Releasing the button lets the spring return it.
