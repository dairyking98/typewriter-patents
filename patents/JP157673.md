## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type cylinder with many type cases needs quick assembly and quick removal or change of any single case.

**Solution:** Case ends sit in notches round ring plates, held by an outer ring whose groove and hollows catch spring-loaded balls, with a notch to release a case.

**How it works:** Ends of type cases fit into evenly spaced notches on the outside of ring plates (optionally with end plates), forming a cylinder. An outer ring fits round the plate; its inner face has a circumferential groove with deeper hollows, and spring-loaded balls in recesses on the plate edge ride in the groove. Turning the plate until the balls drop into the hollows locks the assembly. To remove one case, the outer ring is turned until its notch lines up with that case's ends and the case is pulled out.
