## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letters with enclosed areas (o, e, a and so on) fill with thick ink, blurring the impression.

**Solution:** Perforate the enclosed area of the type and fix a needle behind the type's rest position so it passes through the hole on each return stroke, clearing the ink.

**How it works:** The typebar carries a type with a hole through the middle of its counter, bevelled outward at the bottom. A needle is mounted on a base fixed to the machine frame, with its point facing the hole. As the bar returns to its rest position the needle enters the hole and pushes out any accumulated ink. Small guide standards beside the needle can ensure alignment. One needle is needed behind each affected type.
