**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator mechanisms on the market lack a simple way to select a column or a denominational position within it by one carriage run.

**Solution:** Column stop bar and associated parts that let any column, or a denominational position in it, be chosen by a single run of the carriage to an arrest point, retrofittable to existing tabulators.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
