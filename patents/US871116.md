## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars rebound too much when they fall back to normal position.

**Solution:** A type rest or cushion behind the type bars reduces their rebound when they return.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
