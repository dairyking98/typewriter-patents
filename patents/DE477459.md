## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (computing typewriter covers)

**How it works (reading):** Counter displays are covered with a non-removable window and the space under the counters is closed by an expandable cover rail, so the counters cannot be reached or adjusted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Counters on a calculating typewriter can be tampered with, by turning wheels or removing counters.

**Solution:** Counter displays are covered with a non-removable window and the space under the counters is closed by an expandable cover rail, so the counters cannot be reached or adjusted.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)
