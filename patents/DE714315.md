**How it works (reading):** A redesigned ribbon fork arrangement is meant to keep the ribbon from being drawn taut when it lifts; the OCR does not show the exact construction clearly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon fork at its uppermost position tightens the ribbon, making the two-colour strike weak and adding key resistance.

**Solution:** A redesigned ribbon fork arrangement is meant to keep the ribbon from being drawn taut when it lifts; the OCR does not show the exact construction clearly.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** ribbon fork; ribbon spools; drive linkage; spring
