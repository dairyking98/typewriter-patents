## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control cash register for baggage) · **Subject (patent classes):** Computing machines characterised by the structural interrelation of their functional units, e.g. invoicing machines

**How it works (reading):** Interlocks between the cash register adjusting means force the piece-number adder, amount setting and printing to change correctly for acceptance versus issue.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Registering related processes at a luggage cash register lets operators make wrong setting manipulations.

**Solution:** Interlocks between the cash register adjusting means force the piece-number adder, amount setting and printing to change correctly for acceptance versus issue.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** key buttons; piece number adder; interlock; key locks
