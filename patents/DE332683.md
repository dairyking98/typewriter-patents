**How it works (reading):** Two chucks with cones that enter the roller bore are drawn together by a threaded spindle, with the cones spring-loaded so they centre rollers of any bore size without damage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter rollers without a driver collar cannot be clamped in grinding machines without damaging them, and bores of differing size make centring difficult.

**Solution:** Two chucks with cones that enter the roller bore are drawn together by a threaded spindle, with the cones spring-loaded so they centre rollers of any bore size without damage.
