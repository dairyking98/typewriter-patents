## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters with switching, in which a latch in an effective position the paper roller in a is able to hold a position deviating from the normal situation.

**How it works (reading):** The switching lever and lifting lever are arranged with a weaker stop spring so that the paper roller can only be switched and held when the switching lever is in its effective position, preventing accidental switching.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A paper roller switched to a non-normal position by a latch can be switched accidentally, and the latch then holds it in a position where nothing is written.

**Solution:** The switching lever and lifting lever are arranged with a weaker stop spring so that the paper roller can only be switched and held when the switching lever is in its effective position, preventing accidental switching.
