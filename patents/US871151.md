## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**How it works (reading):** A separate adjustment of key tension lets the finger touch vary from very light to very firm without disturbing the return tension.

**Solution:** A separate adjustment of key tension lets the finger touch vary from very light to very firm without disturbing the return tension.
