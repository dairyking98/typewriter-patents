## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The copy is held at the side of the machine and is hard to compare with the sheet being written.

**Solution:** A copy-holder on a jointed column is supported on the carriage roller so it moves back when the carriage is swung up and returns to position.
