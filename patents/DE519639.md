## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type wheel for telegraph printers must be lighter, run fast and be removable from its drive shaft.

**Solution:** Type bars are carried in slots of two discs and displaced parallel to the wheel axis; a ring stops the bars, and springs from a movable ring return them after printing.
