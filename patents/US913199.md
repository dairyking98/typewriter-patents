## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar bearings work loose and need accurate alignment; the type bar must be kept free of play.

**Solution:** The type bar pivots on an adjustable cone bearing on a grooved wire; a cam washer and bevelled surface tighten the bearing automatically as the bar moves.
