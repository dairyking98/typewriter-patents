## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (justifying matrix pages) · **Subject (patent classes):** Master sheets for transferring a lithographic image to the forme; Printing on other surfaces than ordinary paper on metals

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an apparatus for justifying type written, printed, or matrix pages.

**How it works (reading):** An apparatus that slits a backing sheet between lines and slides the cut words left or right within the slit guides so that each typed line is justified.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Matter typed in a continuous strip must be cut and pasted into justified lines, which is slow and error-prone.

**Solution:** An apparatus that slits a backing sheet between lines and slides the cut words left or right within the slit guides so that each typed line is justified.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
