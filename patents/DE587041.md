## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the carriage hits the right margin stop, inertia can make the ratchet jump, causing deadlocks and overshoot of the line start.

**Solution:** A ratchet locking part engages when the carriage reaches the right margin stop and is released immediately on carriage release, so the carriage can still be moved later; the rest of the solution is cut off in the OCR.
