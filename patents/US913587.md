## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side lays or gauges

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of the invention is to provide simple and effective means for releasing the gage for adjustment and re-seeuring it after adjustment;

**How it works (reading):** The gage hooks over the tubular rim of the paper shelf, and a rotatable sleeve with a finger-hold presses a tongue on the gage against the paper shelf to clamp it, and releases it for adjustment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Releasing the side-edge paper gage on Underwood machines to adjust it, and re-securing it, is clumsy.

**Solution:** The gage hooks over the tubular rim of the paper shelf, and a rotatable sleeve with a finger-hold presses a tongue on the gage against the paper shelf to clamp it, and releases it for adjustment.
