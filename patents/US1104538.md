## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen roller must be released from the carriage for paper removal, and existing release mechanisms are complicated.

**Solution:** A barrel on the platen roller, carried on the platen shaft with a stop collar, is released by a lever that engages a pin on the carriage member.
