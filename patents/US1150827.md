## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column stops on the carriage must be set and returned to inoperative position reliably.

**Solution:** A stop-setting blade depresses a column stop beneath it into operative position, and a spring-returned reset cam forces stops back up during carriage return.
