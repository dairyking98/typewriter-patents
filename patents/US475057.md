## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letter-spacing escapements for typebar machines needed improvement.

**Solution:** A vibrating hooked escapement arm with a sliding spacing-stop hook, rocked by a universal bar, steps a ratchet bar one tooth per keystroke.

**How it works:** A spring-drum cord pulls a table carrying a serrated ratchet bar. An escapement arm pivoted on a spring-rocked post has a hook engaging the ratchet teeth; a sliding spacing stop with a second hook rides on it. Each key depression rocks a universal bar that works the arm through a connecting rod, so the hooks alternately release and catch the teeth and the bar advances one space.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** escapement arm; hook; spacing stop; ratchet bar; post; coil spring; connecting rod; universal bar; spring drum; cord
