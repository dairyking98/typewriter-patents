## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing needs simple, strong and efficient mechanism, and the platen clutch must grip reliably.

**Solution:** A resiliently slotted clutch sleeve on the platen core, with a hand-wheel and clutch sections, lets the clutch bind the platen shaft with greater tenacity for line-spacing.
