## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need a carriage drive controlled from the keyboard, cleaner inking than a ribbon, and spacing suited to different letter widths.

**Solution:** Sliding converging typebars in a tiltable frame print case shift, with a pad inking the type directly, a cord-and-ratchet-wheel carriage drive and variable spacing.

**How it works:** Typebars slide longitudinally in segmental plates toward a common print point, mounted in a frame pivoted between upper and lower banks; a key tilts the frame to bring upper or lower case type to the print line, limited by adjusting screws. The carriage runs freely on ways with no rack and is joined by a cord to a ratchet wheel with a pulley groove. Key depression raises a frame that carries a push-pawl back one tooth under spring action, while an adjustable holding pawl on a segment removes lost motion. A variable-spacing mechanism for letter widths and an inking pad replacing the ribbon are included.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** typebar; segmental plate; tilting frame; adjusting screw; platen cylinder; ratchet wheel; pulley groove; cord; push-pawl; holding pawl; adjustable segment; space key; inking pad
