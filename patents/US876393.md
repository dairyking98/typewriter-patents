## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of my invention is tocure this defect, and I do this by providing means for preventing rebound when the stops coact, said means consisting preferably of an independent catch or stop which is associated with the usual carriage stop.

**How it works (reading):** An independent anti-rebound catch or stop works with the usual carriage stop so the carriage cannot rebound into the escapement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage stop strikes the frame stop and rebounds, so the carriage can re-engage the escapement and stop at a wrong position.

**Solution:** An independent anti-rebound catch or stop works with the usual carriage stop so the carriage cannot rebound into the escapement.
