## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The erasure plate and scale plate must be ready for use when the paper carriage is tipped back to inspect writing or make erasures.

**Solution:** A paper carriage whose uprights swing backward on bushings; the erasure plate is attached to the uprights and the scale plate turns down below the line of writing, so both stay in position.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** erasure plate; scale plate; swinging uprights; main paper-feed roll; pressure roll
