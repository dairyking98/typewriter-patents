## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in marking the correct position the ribbon on writing or printing machines opposite its guide parts, in front of or behind which it is used to carry out its longitudinal movement and its stroke and Lowering movement is guided by identification marks on the guide parts, such that -the Identification marks are either all covered or not covered by the ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** Identification marks on the ribbon guide parts that are either fully covered or uncovered by the ribbon when it sits correctly, so the operator sees a misloaded ribbon at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting a ribbon through its guides is difficult, and a ribbon that shifts out of position in its guides goes unnoticed until the print quality drops.

**Solution:** Identification marks on the ribbon guide parts that are either fully covered or uncovered by the ribbon when it sits correctly, so the operator sees a misloaded ribbon at once.
