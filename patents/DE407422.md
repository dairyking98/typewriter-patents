## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Eraser stencil for typewriters. When upgrading with the Typewritten font is used to help when erasing on the Typewriter roller to protect the neighboring writing, an eraser stencil, this is a leaf in which there is a cutout. But it is inevitable that the abraded paper or rubber particles fall off and enter the mechanism of the Typewriter. To catch the eraser dust, you have the eraser template provided with a trough which is open on the side facing away from the roller. The eraser dust that collects in the cutout of the eraser template can therefore does not get into the trough, but falls from the roller into the machine. In order to avoid this, according to the invention, the trough is against the paper roll open and lies against the paper with a sharp edge. The eraser dust, which sticks to the paper and is stored in the opening of the stencil, so it gets there when it is removed the template and turning the roller securely into the trough.
