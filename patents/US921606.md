## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier crank-and-dog platen turn for bill blank spaces is complicated; this patent aims to simplify and improve the latch.

**Solution:** A crank loosely on the platen axle with a pawl engaging a notched wheel on the platen gives the back-and-forth platen turn for bill spacing, with an improved latch; the latch detail is cut off in the OCR.
