## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A device for reversing the feed of the ink ribbon is needed on the Monarch front-strike machine.

**Solution:** An improved ribbon feed reversing device on the ribbon spools, driven from the carriage escapement.

**How it works:** On the Monarch front-strike machine, a platen carriage on ball-bearing rails is advanced by a rack and pinion on an escape wheel controlled by dogs and the universal bar. Typebars are in a radial segment, linked to sublevers on key levers. The opening describes the machine; the reversing device itself is cut off in the extract.
