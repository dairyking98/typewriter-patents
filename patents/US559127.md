## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A complete typebar typewriter (Ellis) with a separate key per character; the opening gives no single stated problem beyond general improvement.

**Solution:** A cast-metal framed, full-keyboard typebar machine with grouped hangers for the typebars, spacing, ribbon and platen improvements.

**How it works:** Keys in banked transverse rows have stems on bell-crank levers pivoted on rods in a cast frame with side plates and standards. Typebars are pivoted in hangers of four in a basket. The text lists a carriage drum and escapement, ribbon actuating mechanism with sliding ribbon guides, and a platen that can be raised or lowered and held by detents. The opening ends before the mechanism is described in detail.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** main frame; side plate; key; bell-crank lever; typebar; hanger; basket; carriage drum; ribbon roller; platen; detent
