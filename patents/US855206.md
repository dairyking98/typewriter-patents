## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper cannot be fed to match ruled lines on the paper with the normal line spacing, so the paper must be readjusted at each line, and characters out of line are hard to write.

**Solution:** A clutch rotates the platen through a throw whose limit can be varied, giving minute line-feed variations, and a holding device prevents accidental movement of the platen and paper.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
