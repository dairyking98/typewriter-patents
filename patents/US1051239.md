## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Address plates need a simpler, cheaper semi-automatic embossing machine than the complicated automatic ones.

**Solution:** A semi-automatic typograph sets a die wheel by hand and then embosses through power connections that also move the plate carriage for spacing; line spacing is located by a detent.

**How it works:** The die wheel is set by hand; a hand lever then engages a clutch and flywheel so the die strikes the metal plate, while the same connections feed the carriage and a wedge lever and screw sleeve take up wear.

**Features:** [ELEM:metal-type](../features/elem-metal-type.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)

**Parts:** die wheel; flywheel; clutch wheel; screw sleeve; wedge lever; address plate carriage
