## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line positioning on a flat-bed machine whose writing plate/carriage moves in the line-spacing direction needed exact, shock-free tabulation to any line.

**Solution:** Motor-driven line-space carriage with line tab stops on the carriage and frame that interlock when engaged, control the motor coupling, and prevent overrun and rebound.

**How it works:** Line tab stops on the frame and the line-space carriage interact when a line position is selected. Their interaction disconnects the drive motor and locks the carriage in the chosen line, damping overshoot and rolling back. A centering stop and unlocking device release the carriage for the next move. The machine is a flat-plate typewriter whose plate lowers to clamp forms.
