## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Flat page-size platens or smaller flat platens having a greater size than line-size platens

**How it works (reading):** A row of separating rollers above the main pin-belt feed separates the perforated web tracks so they are fed one after another and aligned from a single supply roll.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-web business forms must be fed separately, which is cumbersome and makes the copies unclear.

**Solution:** A row of separating rollers above the main pin-belt feed separates the perforated web tracks so they are fed one after another and aligned from a single supply roll.
