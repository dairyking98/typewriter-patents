## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On Densmore machines the carriage feed rack, dogs and bearings needed better adjustment and a release.

**Solution:** Feed carriage with rocker-mounted feed dogs, an adjustable cone-bearing and ball-bearing feed shaft with take-up plug, and a feed rack held to the pinion by a leaf spring that a finger-piece disengages.

**How it works:** Feed dogs on a rocker are moved by a link whose length is adjustable and returned by a spring. A feed shaft turns in cone bearings with balls, one raceway on a threaded plug that can be turned by screw driver to take up wear. The feed pinion meshes with a rack on arms on a rock shaft pressed by a leaf spring; pressing a finger-piece rocks it and frees the carriage to move either way. The carriage has a platen frame, a paper carriage and a feed carriage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** feed dog; rocker; feed shaft; ball bearing; adjusting plug; feed pinion; feed rack; leaf spring; finger-piece

**Improves on:**

- Densmore typewriter: adds wear take-up and rack release
