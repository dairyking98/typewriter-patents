## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier linotype keyboard movement (US 1,074,832) needs its plate and driven member arrangement refined.

**Solution:** A continuation of the Bright intermittent movement, with a driven member normally held out of mesh with a constantly rotating driving member by a plate controlled from the keyboard fingers.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
