## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The opening gives no stated problem; the aim is a simple machine with a multi-row type form moved by hand.

**Solution:** A rectangular type form with rows of types on its sides is turned a quarter-turn by hand to pick the row and slid lengthwise on a table to bring the character to the printing point.

**How it works:** A rectangular type form with rows of types along its sides is journaled in a frame. A hand piece at one end rotates it, and a spring bearing on the form holds it at each of several quarter positions so it does not rotate while printing. The frame slides on the table over a slot, guided by notches and strips, to bring type at different points along the length over the paper on a bed roller. The roller turns partly after each line.
