## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (magnetic marking of statistical sheets) · **Subject (patent classes):** Record carriers for use with machines and with at least a part designed to carry digital markings characterised by the kind of the digital marking, e.g. shape, nature, code

**How it works (reading):** Magnetised characters of different frequencies are written on the card and read by relays in the evaluation machine, letting several characters share one column.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Punched or dense mark columns on control cards are hard to read reliably when many marks are packed together.

**Solution:** Magnetised characters of different frequencies are written on the card and read by relays in the evaluation machine, letting several characters share one column.
