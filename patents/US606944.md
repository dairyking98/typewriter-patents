## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** More than one key lever may be struck at the same time, jamming the machine.

**Solution:** A tube of loose steel balls across all levers, with a pin on each lever entering between two balls, so only one lever can move at a time.

**How it works:** A transverse tube holds one more steel ball than there are levers, with total free play only slightly more than a pin's diameter, limited by adjustable end plugs. When a lever is pressed its pin passes through a hole in the tube between two balls, pushing them apart into contact with the others. Another lever's pin then strikes a ball that cannot move and is blocked. A pin at the end of its stroke enters a hole in the far side of the tube, so it cannot be bent. No lever can move until the others have returned.

**Features:** [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)

**Parts:** key lever; pin; tube; steel ball; adjustable end plug
