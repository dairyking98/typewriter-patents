## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register double printing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing device that is particularly suitable for such cash registers which a receipt inserted into the machine is printed twice.

**How it works (reading):** One common drive set acts on both hammers at once, with elastic pressure plates pressing the receipt against the adding-wheel type, so one hammer pressure is applied evenly to both.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two pressure hammers for double printing need separate stops and retraction springs, which makes the printing unit cumbersome and unreliable.

**Solution:** One common drive set acts on both hammers at once, with elastic pressure plates pressing the receipt against the adding-wheel type, so one hammer pressure is applied evenly to both.
