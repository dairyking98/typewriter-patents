## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is an edge stop device for typewriters in which the paper guide plate has a scale on which one is arranged on the edge stop Brand points.

**How it works (reading):** The edge stop rail sits behind the upper rear edge of the paper guide plate so that its adjusting button lies above the plate; each margin stop carries a mark that is matched to the scale on the plate, and the marks can be aligned exactly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** On small travel machines, the margin stop mark must match the scale on the paper guide plate, and an adjustment is hard to make precisely.

**Solution:** The edge stop rail sits behind the upper rear edge of the paper guide plate so that its adjusting button lies above the plate; each margin stop carries a mark that is matched to the scale on the plate, and the marks can be aligned exactly.
