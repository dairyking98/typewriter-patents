## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventors want a simple, cheap typewriter that prints accurately and neatly, using a movable type-plate turned above the paper.

**Solution:** A flexible arc-shaped type plate on a pivoted frame is swung by a lever over a printing bar until the chosen character is in position, then that character is thrust down onto the paper by a hammer, with the carriage stepped along by a spring and pawl feed.

**How it works:** A cast frame on a base plate carries a carriage sleeved on a longitudinal bar. Types sit on a flexible plate bent in an arc, carried on a frame pivoted on a central post; a lever turns the frame to bring the desired type over the printing bar. A type-hammer always above the bar then thrusts that character down onto the paper. Each impression moves a pin and lever that works a rigid and a spring pawl over a rack bar so the carriage advances one tooth at a time. A spring drum with a cord at the right end pulls the carriage, and the pawls can be pushed back to return it. A feed roller and comb advance and hold the paper after each line.

**Parts:** base plate; frame; carriage; flexible arc type plate; pivoted frame; post; selecting lever; printing bar; type-hammer; rack bar; feed pawl; spring pawl; spring drum; cord; paper feed roller; paper comb
