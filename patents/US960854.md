## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Tabulating needs a stop for each column, and the carriage must be set to a position chosen by a scale rather than by stops alone.

**Solution:** A hand lever moved over a scale or dial sets the paper carriage to the chosen position, with automatic cushioning of the carriage when it is released and arrested.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** lever; scale; tabulating stop; cushioning spring; carriage
