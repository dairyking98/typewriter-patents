## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Back spacing must engage the carriage rack reliably on every operation, act positively through the backward throw, and return promptly and reliably to its start position.

**Solution:** A pivoted dog, operated from the key through levers, pulls the carriage rack backward; the rack is locked against lateral displacement during the throw and positive stops limit the travel.

**How it works:** A key drives a system of levers that moves a pivoted dog. The dog pulls in substantially the carriage travel direction, so it exerts little upward pressure on the carriage and cannot bind with it. Stops fix its movement, a lock prevents rack lift, and positive means prevent overthrow of the rack.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** back spacing dog; carriage rack; escapement mechanism; levers; stops; rack lock
