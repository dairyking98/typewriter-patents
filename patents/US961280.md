## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabulating mechanism for typewriting and other machines, and particularly to means for enabling the carriage to jump to any selected column, and is in the nature of an improvement upon the devices disclosed in U.

**How it works (reading):** Column stops hang vertically between the teeth of a rack, each with a downward tappet, and the tappets are of different lengths so the selected column is picked by the tappet length, with fewer parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier column skipping used many parts and was unreliable.

**Solution:** Column stops hang vertically between the teeth of a rack, each with a downward tappet, and the tappets are of different lengths so the selected column is picked by the tappet length, with fewer parts.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** rack; column stops; tappets; fork; interponent; denomination keys; framework stop
