## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools mounted in front of the platen on front-strike visible machines leave unsightly ribbon exposed or are hard to reach.

**Solution:** Mount the ribbon spools beneath the carriage and behind the typebars, with the carriage tilting up for access.

**How it works:** A front-strike visible machine has forward-extending typebars pivoted on a curved fulcrum rod in a slotted segment, striking the front of the platen, with lower and upper case types on each bar. The carriage rides on a rail with a carriage roll and a finger, with a feed-in roller, paper shelf, hinged bail with platen scale and pressure rollers. The ribbon spools sit under the carriage, so only a short length of ribbon shows, and the carriage tilts up to reach them.
