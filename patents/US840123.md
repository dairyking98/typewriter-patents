## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Right margin of typewritten lines is ragged.

**Solution:** Auxiliary racks with fewer teeth per inch are shifted in place of the regular rack to widen spacing and justify lines.

**How it works:** One or more toothed rules with fewer teeth per inch replace the regular carriage rack by a lifting mechanism and a rack-turning wheel, lengthening the line slightly per space.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** auxiliary rack; carriage; rack-lifting mechanism; rack-shifting wheel; catch
