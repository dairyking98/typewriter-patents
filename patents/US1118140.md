## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction clutches between the platen and the line-space wheel slip when the line-space lever is operated sharply.

**Solution:** A friction clutch where one member is flexible spring stock that is bent by the other member when gripped, plus a resilient connection to the line-space wheel that absorbs shock.
