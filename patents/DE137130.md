## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter cannot produce type for printing on the same machine, so the writer needs a way to make printing-type from the same keyboard.

**Solution:** Letter bars with the type cast on their lower ends are held in displaceable magazines of wing-shaped sheets, one per letter. An angle transport lever carries the chosen bar into a line slot, and a keypad key drives it. The text is cut off before the rest of the mechanism.
