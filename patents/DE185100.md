**How it works (reading):** Several ratchet/gear wheels with different tooth counts are mounted on a rotatable star body; a hand knob with a locking pin selects which gear engages the carriage rack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters with interchangeable type need different paper-carriage step distances depending on the average width of the type in use.

**Solution:** Several ratchet/gear wheels with different tooth counts are mounted on a rotatable star body; a hand knob with a locking pin selects which gear engages the carriage rack.
