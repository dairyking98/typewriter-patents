## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen needs to be freed from the line-spacing wheel for fractional or irregular positioning, then locked again with no play or disturbance of the adjustment.

**Solution:** A clutch of spring-loaded lock pins that engage holes in the line-spacing wheel casing, locking it to the platen shaft in many positions and releasing on demand.

**How it works:** The line-spacing wheel is threaded onto a cup that rides on a sleeve pinned to the platen shaft. Several lock pins permanently engage a disk fast to the sleeve and shift axially into holes in the inner face of the cup to lock the wheel to the shaft. A longitudinally movable pin-carrying disk shifts the pins, each held by a leaf spring so that pins not aligned with a hole yield. Leaf springs come in units of six formed from one piece. Releasing frees the platen for fine adjustment.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** platen shaft; platen; line-spacing wheel; cup; sleeve; lock pin; pin-carrying disk; leaf spring; pin-hole plate; hand wheel; pawl; detent

**Numbers:** six springs per unit; three plates holding three pairs of springs
