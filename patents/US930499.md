## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card and form leading edges must be held and aligned against a stop, and the inventor wants a card holder that does this.

**Solution:** A device on the platen carrier serves as a stop or aliner for the leading edge of the card, working with a shift rail and platen roller so the sheet is held in position.
