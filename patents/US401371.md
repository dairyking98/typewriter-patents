## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type-wheel carried on a continuously orbiting turret needs a reliable way to be started and stopped once per revolution so that a chosen character prints at the right moment.

**Solution:** A friction-driven wheel on a rotating turret is held by a detent and released by a tripping latch, then reset by a resetting device so it stops after its turn.

**How it works:** A turret frame turns about a fixed stud, driven by gears from a drive shaft. A shaft on the turret is rotated by a spur wheel meshing with internal teeth in a circular wall, and carries a type wheel held by a leather friction washer under adjustable spring pressure. A detent apparatus on the turret holds the wheel until a starting latch is unhooked by a tripping rod; the wheel then turns and is stopped again by the detent, which is reset by a separate mechanism. Variant forms of latch and a positive-drive device for the wheel after starting are described.
