## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Gearing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to mechanism for 0 starting and stopping the revolution about its axis of a wheel having a continuous orbital movement, the object being to furnish an apparatus for so operating the type-wheels of a certain class of type-writing machines.
