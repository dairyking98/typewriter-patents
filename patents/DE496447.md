## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters, and more particularly to one Type lever drive, in which the type lever means in a known manner in a horizontal Plane parallel to the key lever bearing arranged intermediate levers are driven, which are connected to the button levers by tie rods.

**How it works (reading):** Connecting rods are all the same length, but they grip different points on the intermediate and key levers of each row, and the drive units are assembled separately.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tie rods of different lengths for each key row complicate manufacture of the type-lever drive.

**Solution:** Connecting rods are all the same length, but they grip different points on the intermediate and key levers of each row, and the drive units are assembled separately.
