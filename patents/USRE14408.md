## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates particularly to a tabulating attachment to typewriting machines, and one of its objects is the provision of simple and eflicient mechanism of this character whereby the carriage may be released from its feeding mechanism, gradually stopped at the end of its run and returned to an exact predetermined point.

**How it works (reading):** A resilient buffer, acting against the machine's motor spring, slows the carriage at the end of its run, and a locking-out device holds the buffer engaged with the carriage until it returns to the set point, then automatically reconnects the feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulating carriage must stop gradually at the end of its run and return to an exact predetermined point without jarring.

**Solution:** A resilient buffer, acting against the machine's motor spring, slows the carriage at the end of its run, and a locking-out device holds the buffer engaged with the carriage until it returns to the set point, then automatically reconnects the feed.
