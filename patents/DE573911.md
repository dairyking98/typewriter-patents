## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide device that bdi against the paper roller are pressed from below paper feed rollers, and consists in that on the Conveyor rollers by a special, usually reversible auxiliary spring, which is through a special handle can be brought in and out of action, an additional Force can be exerted.

**How it works (reading):** A removable auxiliary spring, brought into action by a cam handle, adds extra pressure to the feed rollers and can be retrofitted to existing machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stiff or heavy sheets are hard to introduce between the feed rollers and the platen.

**Solution:** A removable auxiliary spring, brought into action by a cam handle, adds extra pressure to the feed rollers and can be retrofitted to existing machines.
