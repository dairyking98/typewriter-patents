## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a type-cleaner for type-writing machines for use in connection with machines in which the type-carrying bars or levers are arranged in basket form and accessible through the medium of an upper opening defined by the basket.

**How it works (reading):** A brush whose peripheral contour matches the curved series of type faces is held inside the type basket and moved up and down so it bears evenly on every face, cleaning and polishing them without shifting alignment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dirt and grit collect on type faces in a basket-form arrangement that is hard to reach, and uneven cleaning disturbs the alignment of the type.

**Solution:** A brush whose peripheral contour matches the curved series of type faces is held inside the type basket and moved up and down so it bears evenly on every face, cleaning and polishing them without shifting alignment.
