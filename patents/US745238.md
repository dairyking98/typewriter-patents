## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a type-cleaner for type-writing machines for use in connection with machines in which the type-carrying bars or levers are arranged in basket form and accessible through the medium of an upper opening defined by the basket.
