## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon adjusted transversely

**In the patent’s own words:** The purpose being to admit of the spool that is unwinding its portion of the ink-ribbon being disengaged from its spring-pawl while the spool that is winding up the ribbon is in engagement and receiving the step-by-step motion in the manner heretofore described.

**How it works (reading):** A ratchet sector with two stops and a reversible double-pointed dog converts reciprocating key motion into step-by-step to-and-fro motion, moving the ribbon spools endwise.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A reciprocating key motion must drive a reversing step-by-step movement that shifts the ribbon sideways and winds it.

**Solution:** A ratchet sector with two stops and a reversible double-pointed dog converts reciprocating key motion into step-by-step to-and-fro motion, moving the ribbon spools endwise.

**How it works:** An oscillating arm worked by a bar under the key bars carries a double-pointed dog that steps a ratchet sector. Pins on the sector hit a finger on the dog at the ends of travel, flipping it so the other point steps the sector backward, held by a spring plunger. Crank arms on the sector shaft slide the non-rotary spool shafts sideways. At the end of each lateral stroke an arm pushed aside advances a spring pawl on the spool ratchet to feed the ribbon.
