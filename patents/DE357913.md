## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ratchet gear for the raised typewriter plate is cumbersome.

**Solution:** A lever linkage withdraws a resilient pawl when the handle is moved, so the pawl holds the plate up and releases it for lowering.
