## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbonless account sheets differ in width, so the column lines do not line up with the booking sheet.

**Solution:** The inlet funnel has a fixed left edge and a spring-loaded right edge; the spring presses each sheet to the left stop so the columns always align.

**How it works:** A light leaf spring on the right edge pushes the sheet against the fixed left edge of the funnel.
