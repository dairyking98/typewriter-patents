## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**How it works (reading):** The form-feed hand lever also drives a cutting knife on an adjustable carriage, so each feed cuts the web at the chosen form length.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Forms need separating after writing and the separation is slow and laborious.

**Solution:** The form-feed hand lever also drives a cutting knife on an adjustable carriage, so each feed cuts the web at the chosen form length.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
