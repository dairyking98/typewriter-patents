## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (electric pen stencil perforating) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to perforate paper similar to the product of an electric pen.

**How it works (reading):** A type made of a metal casing holding sharp puncturing points, set in cement, struck into a cork or rubber cylinder through an ordinary typewriter, with no ribbon.
