## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Seeks a simple, easily manipulated typewriter.

**Solution:** A swinging crown holds key-levers with typebars that are first positioned by a key, then struck down by a hammer frame onto the paper.

**How it works:** A crown on standards swings up to expose the work. Key-levers inside the crown carry pivoted typebars passing through slots; pressing a key pushes the type outward and a coil spring holds the bar against a slot wall to guide it. A semicircular frame with a hammer is then forced down against the bar's spring to print, while a spring and the key release return the bar so the type touches an inking pad. A paper carriage with clamp is also shown.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [IMP:spring-energy](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)

**Parts:** base plate; crown; key-lever; typebar; coil spring; semicircular frame; hammer; inking pad; paper carriage; paper clamp
