## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapement is noisy because of impact of moving parts.

**Solution:** A silent escapement with a feed rack and driving spring eliminates concussion between parts.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
