## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The aim of the invention is to make it possible to read manuscripts and the like on typewriters, To facilitate typesetting machines and the like so that the scribe can view the manuscript close to his eyes no time-consuming or strenuous change in posture and no head turning got to.

**How it works (reading):** A manuscript carrier is moved by a Nuremberg-scissors linkage, operated by a foot lever with Bowden cable, to the reading position near the eyes and returns on a spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typist must turn the head to read a manuscript and change posture when reading.

**Solution:** A manuscript carrier is moved by a Nuremberg-scissors linkage, operated by a foot lever with Bowden cable, to the reading position near the eyes and returns on a spring.
