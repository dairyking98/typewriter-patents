## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement speed is limited by lost motion and springing, so the let-off comes late.

**Solution:** A dog-carrying shaft and feed dogs on the escapement rack are set so that the let-off happens sooner, giving a faster and simpler escapement.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
