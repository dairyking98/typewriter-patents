## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to that type of storage of the type lever of typewriters, in which the type lever are stored in slots which, arranged in an arc, in a bearing body, your Type lever bearing;

**How it works (reading):** The bearing body is made of two sheet-metal discs or a hollowed piece spaced apart at their edges, so the slotted edges have reduced friction surfaces and the guide slots hold the type levers accurately.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever bearing slots cut through a full-thickness segment need wide clearance, causing dead motion, and dust or oil makes the levers stick.

**Solution:** The bearing body is made of two sheet-metal discs or a hollowed piece spaced apart at their edges, so the slotted edges have reduced friction surfaces and the guide slots hold the type levers accurately.
