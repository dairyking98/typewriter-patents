## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, particularly to those of the sliding bar type, and consists of an improved type locking device adapted to act in unison with the locking plate and coact with the universal bar in looking the type and space bar when such plate is in abnormal position, and unlocking the same when the plate returns to its normal position.
