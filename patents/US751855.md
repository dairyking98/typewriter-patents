## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods on side surfaces thereof, e.g. fixed thereto the types or dies being movable relative to the bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in that class of type-writing machines which have a single keyboard and a shifting mechanism for changing the relative position of the platen and type in order to allow a capital letter or other selected character to strike the printing-point and which also have a depressible space key or keys located in front of the keyboard.

**How it works (reading):** A thumb shift-bar placed just in front of and below the space-bar so one downward-forward thumb movement spaces and then shifts.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On typebar machines that shift the platen carriage horizontally, the shift keys at the sides are slow to reach, though a capital usually follows a space.

**Solution:** A thumb shift-bar placed just in front of and below the space-bar so one downward-forward thumb movement spaces and then shifts.

**How it works:** The shift-bar is carried on lever-arms fixed to a rock-shaft pivoted in side plates, with a counterweight behind the pivot. A link and rod run from one arm to the usual L-shaped shift lever, which moves the track-bar and slides the platen carriage between its two extremes. A rocking plate on the lever changes the line of pull so the carriage is held at either extreme. The shift-bar top is lower than the space-bar and both slope about fifteen degrees down toward the front, so the two tops form one plane when the space-bar is fully depressed. The space-bar's front edge and shift-bar's rear edge are rounded, or a roller is fitted in front of the space-bar, so the thumb glides across.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** shift-bar; space-bar; lever-arm; rock-shaft; counterweight; link; rod; L-shaped shift lever; track-bar; platen carriage; rocking plate; roller; type key

**Numbers:** slope of bar tops about 15 degrees

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter shift keys: side shift keys replaced by one central thumb bar
- Horizontally moving thumb shift-key: a downward key lets one thumb stroke space then shift
