## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A back spacer can let the carriage move more than one letter space by inertia, or let it return after the dog releases.

**Solution:** A lever raises an arm that moves a dog into the gear teeth one space at a time, and a locking dog and pawl keep the carriage from moving back.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)

**Parts:** lever A; engaging dog D; locking dog C; pawl P; carriage driving gear; spring J
