## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper carriage alarm for adding machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention pertains to paper carriage signals for adding machines and the like, its object being to rovide a device which will, when the paper is about to run off the printing roller, give an alarm.

**How it works (reading):** When the paper nears the end, a trigger-held bell hammer on a dog is released by a lug on the feed lever, striking a bell on a post to give an audible alarm.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper on an adding machine carriage can run off the printing roller without warning the operator.

**Solution:** When the paper nears the end, a trigger-held bell hammer on a dog is released by a lug on the feed lever, striking a bell on a post to give an audible alarm.
