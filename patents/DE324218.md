## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** A feeler lever resting on the wound ribbon trips a small weighted lever carried on a full-width switching lever, so one keystroke reverses the ribbon direction immediately and directly.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal devices acted indirectly through ribbon tension, the mainspring, carriage, shafts or gears, which made them heavy, sluggish and unreliable, causing pale print, skipped strokes or holes in the ribbon.

**Solution:** A feeler lever resting on the wound ribbon trips a small weighted lever carried on a full-width switching lever, so one keystroke reverses the ribbon direction immediately and directly.

**How it works:** A ratchet on each ribbon spool axis is advanced every keystroke by a spring-loaded pawl on the frame that carries the universal rail. A long two-armed switching lever, also usable as a hand handle, slides in a slot guide across the machine and carries a small weighted lever with a hooked arm. A feeler lever rests on the spool's wound ribbon; when the ribbon is nearly run out and the spool pins show, the feeler swings further and its end catches the hook. The next keystroke then throws the switching lever, whose rod lifts the pawl and counter pawl on one side and engages those of the other spool, reversing the winding. A spring-loaded roller on a wedge surface locks the switching lever against accidental movement; no gears, shafts, worms or couplings are needed, only a few stamped parts.
