## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of my invention is to provide a simple and eiiicient escapement for the carriage of a typewriting machine and one that can be readily converted to give either a reverse or an ordinary feed.
