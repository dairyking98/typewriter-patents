## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage escapement for the Monarch typewriter that can easily be converted to give either ordinary (left-to-right) or reverse feed.

**Solution:** Two spring-urged sliding feed dogs mounted in grooves in the head of the dog rocker, whose relative stop positions can be set to give normal or reverse letter feed.

**How it works:** The carriage is drawn left by a spring drum and its feed rack drives a pinion on a shaft carrying a loose escapement wheel with a ratchet connection. A dog rocker pivoted on a cross shaft is rocked by the universal bar through a link and returned by a spring. Its head carries two slides (feed dogs) with upward teeth, guided in cross-machine grooves and held by pins; springs push them against the motion of the wheel's lower part. Depending fingers and arms limit the slides' travel, the rear slot being cut deeper than the front, so the two dogs engage the wheel teeth in sequence and release one letter space per stroke.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** carriage; feed rack; feed pinion; escapement wheel; dog rocker; feed dog slide; universal bar; spring drum; stop screw

**Improves on:**

- Monarch typewriter escapement: dogs are separate sliding pieces that can be set for reverse or ordinary feed
