## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators are not warned when the end of the sheet is nearly reached.

**Solution:** A single contact arm touches a conducting section of the paper roller, which is paired with a conducting pressure roller, so the alarm sounds only when the paper no longer lies between the rollers.

**How it works:** A spring arm on a rocking spindle is struck into contact by a lever each time the line-feed pawl advances the roller. Both the paper roller and the pressure roller are partly covered with conducting material, the latter wired to the frame. While paper separates them the circuit stays open; once the paper edge has left the nip, the contact closes the battery and bell circuit. Because contact occurs only on the pawl stroke, the bell does not ring continuously when no paper is present.
