## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Joining a type-bar connecting rod to a key lever normally needs screws or rivets, which are slow to assemble and to take apart.

**Solution:** A sliding sleeve locks a pin-and-slot joint between rod and key lever without tools.

**How it works:** The key-lever arm has a slot or hook that engages a cross pin on the bifurcated lower end of the connecting rod. The two ends overlap with their outer edges flush. A cup-shaped sleeve carried on the rod is slid down over both ends and holds them against lateral displacement; sliding it up releases the joint.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** key lever; connecting rod; pin; locking sleeve
