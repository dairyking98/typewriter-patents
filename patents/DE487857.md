## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a cash register the adjusting disc for the adding units had to be set by hand with a lever for each subtotal or total.

**Solution:** Pressing a special summing key sets the cam-slotted adjusting disc automatically as part of the checkout, and locking devices stop an amount key and a total key being pressed together or keys being pressed in more than one special-key row.

**How it works:** Summing key depression couples the adding units to the adjusting disc, and interlocks prevent conflicting key combinations.

**Parts:** amount keys; special key rows; summing keys; adjusting disc with cam slots; adding units; display device; locking devices

**Numbers:** three adding units; three special key rows; three subtotal summing keys; three total summing keys
