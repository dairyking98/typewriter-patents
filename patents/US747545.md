## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In front-strike typewriters with the typebar joints below the platen, erasure rubbings and dust fall into the joints and make the typebars stick.

**Solution:** Give the typebar a U-shaped pivot end that forms a housing over the hanger bearing, and connect link and typebar with a spherical-pin universal joint.

**How it works:** Typebar hangers sit in radial slots in a circular segment below the platen, each clamped by a screw through an elongated eye so it can be adjusted radially; the eyes are staggered on alternate hangers for closer spacing. The typebar's pivot end has two walls with parallel bearing surfaces straddling the hanger bearing, joined by a top portion that covers the joint against falling dirt. A spherical pin in an eye on the typebar is gripped by the ears of a U-section spring-metal link, forming a universal joint that prevents cramping.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)

**Parts:** typebar; typebar hanger; segment; U-shaped housing; pivot pin; link; spherical pin; sublever; key lever

**Improves on:**

- front-strike typebar machines with exposed joints: joint is housed against platen rubbings and dust
