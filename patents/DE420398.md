## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon bunches at the print point when the carrier lifts, so two-colour printing shows mixed colours.

**Solution:** A second, height-adjusting ribbon guide sits beside the usual side slots so the full ribbon width reaches the print point.
