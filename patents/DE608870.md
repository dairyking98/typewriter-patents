## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register check printing mechanism)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on a check ', printing mechanism for key lever cash registers.

**How it works (reading):** A manually set switch lever decouples the key-connector movement from the parts that move during check printing and from the paper conveyor, so checks are not issued, while the type-wheel and cutter linkages keep working unchanged.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-lever cash registers drive the check printing movements from the key depression, and switching off check issuing needs many changes to the machine.

**Solution:** A manually set switch lever decouples the key-connector movement from the parts that move during check printing and from the paper conveyor, so checks are not issued, while the type-wheel and cutter linkages keep working unchanged.
