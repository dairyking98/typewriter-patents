## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On Underwood stencil-cutting machines with bichrome ribbon, the ribbon vibrator must move differently per colour and be silenced for stencils.

**Solution:** A slotted actuator joined to the universal bar by a keeper plate that can sit in three positions: red, black or silent.

**How it works:** A keeper plate is screwed to the universal bar with slotted adjustment. A spring-loaded actuator hangs from it on a swivel head. Setting the actuator in one position gives the ribbon the throw for the upper stripe, the opposite position the lower stripe, and the mid position lets the bar advance without vibrating the ribbon.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** universal bar; keeper plate; actuator; swivel head; spring; ribbon vibrator; typebar

**Improves on:**

- Underwood bichrome ribbon vibrator: adds a silent mid position for stencil work
