## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machine whose work stays visible to the operator while typing.

**Solution:** Typebars each on a separate pivot, with a reciprocating paper-roller frame stepped by a toggle-like pawl on a pin-rack.

**How it works:** Key levers act through bent links on typebars hung on individual pivot pins in a curved frame. Each key stroke (and the spacing lever) also raises a ratchet lever through an angle-bar. The ratchet link carries a pawl that pushes the roller-frame forward one pin of a pin-rack, while a spur drops between pins to hold the frame for the impression. The roller frame runs on antifriction wheels along a rod, carries upper and lower paper rollers with a thumbwheel and line-space ratchet, and is read against a 1-70 scale. Only the opening of the description is available.
