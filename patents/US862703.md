## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more especially to that class of typewriting machines which are adapted to write or print upon the leaves of books.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable platen frame carried on posts above a flat table that holds the book, with the platen raised and lowered by a crank and a paper clamp over a slot in the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriters need an improved platen and support for writing on the leaves of a bound book.

**Solution:** An adjustable platen frame carried on posts above a flat table that holds the book, with the platen raised and lowered by a crank and a paper clamp over a slot in the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)
