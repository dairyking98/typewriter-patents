## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter with type levers radiating around a common point.

**Solution:** A typebar machine with semicircular guide combs for the type-levers, a slotted segment comb for key-levers, piston keys that move the paper carriage forward, and a broad spacer-key.

**How it works:** Type-levers pivot in a semicircular guide comb around the platen, driven by key-levers held in a slotted segment. Pistons or keys advance the paper-carriage forward. The carriage and spacing mechanism are shown in many views. The OCR retains the figure list only and little of the mechanism text.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** type-lever; guide comb; key-lever; slotted segment; piston key; paper carriage; spacer-key
