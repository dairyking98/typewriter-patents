## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Swinging the whole upper pressure roller frame back is needed even for small adjustments, such as new sheets.

**Solution:** A rod carrying the pressure rollers is mounted on the front of the swinging frame so the rollers can lift forward independently.
