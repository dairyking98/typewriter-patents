## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper carriage return on calculators) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a return device for the paper cart on calculating machines and similar office machines, which are used to for the purpose of tabulator work, the paper trolley by keystroke or similar.

**How it works (reading):** Power goes from the motor to the carriage-return drive through a flexible shaft, and the drive switch is worked through a flexible rod, so the drive can be mounted at any point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Motor drives for returning the paper carriage on calculators are rigid and tied to the motor housing, so they fit only particular machine sizes and layouts.

**Solution:** Power goes from the motor to the carriage-return drive through a flexible shaft, and the drive switch is worked through a flexible rod, so the drive can be mounted at any point.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
