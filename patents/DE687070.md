## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (triggered character switching for typewriters and teleprinters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**How it works (reading):** Double levers, each under two actuating gears, transmit motion only when two keys are pressed together in any order.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Simple key-driven switching gives few-key coding of many operations but needs many gear parts and works noisily.

**Solution:** Double levers, each under two actuating gears, transmit motion only when two keys are pressed together in any order.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
