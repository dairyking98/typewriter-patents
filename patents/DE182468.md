## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenography machine, type levers on axis) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a stenography machine which is characterized by that the type levers together on a straight axis in a frame within the machine independent of its drives.

**How it works (reading):** The type levers sit on a straight axis in a removable slide in a carriage; each lever carries two or three types, and displacing the carriage with a button brings the desired type group into the printing position. The slide can be swapped without dismantling the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In stenography machines the type levers are tied to the drive, so changing the type arrangement requires taking the machine apart and releasing the type levers.

**Solution:** The type levers sit on a straight axis in a removable slide in a carriage; each lever carries two or three types, and displacing the carriage with a button brings the desired type group into the printing position. The slide can be swapped without dismantling the machine.
