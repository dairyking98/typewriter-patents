## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen carriage switching produced noise and shock, and the platen was not held rigid in both writing positions.

**Solution:** Support rollers on the switchable part rest against stops on the fixed carriage at both end positions, so the platen holds a rigid position against type pressure.
