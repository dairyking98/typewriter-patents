## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**How it works (reading):** The return spring acts on a threaded slider nut on a partly threaded spindle; the spindle is turned from the front and a spring keeps the nut pressed back onto the thread.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The return spring of the universal rail is adjusted by a tension screw in front, and the nut can come off the screw if the spindle is turned too far, so it is hard to repair.

**Solution:** The return spring acts on a threaded slider nut on a partly threaded spindle; the spindle is turned from the front and a spring keeps the nut pressed back onto the thread.

**How it works:** The spring pulls the universal rail against the slider, which is a nut on a partly threaded spindle. The nut cannot be shifted along the spindle, only turned, and if it leaves the thread the return spring presses it back on.
