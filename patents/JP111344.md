## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a Japanese-script machine with very many types, moving a heavy magazine to bring the wanted type to the printing point makes operation slow and heavy.

**Solution:** Arrange row-boxes of types radially round a free circle at the printing point, and advance only the wanted row-box straight in, with types pre-oriented in each box so each arrives upright.

**How it works:** Row-boxes slide radially around a circular free space centred on the printing point. Pressing a character's key raises a sleeve that brings a pulley against a constantly turning disc, driving that row-box inward. Concentric rings, one per position in a row, rise and fall; the key lowers the ring matching the type's place in its row, and the box's stop lug hits it, stopping the box with the wanted type at the printing point. Types in each box are stored at angles such that, after the radial advance, they face the direction of printing on the paper cylinder. The printing lever then grips and prints the type.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to the type magazine of a printing machine in which row-boxes of types, free to slide radially, are arranged round a circular free space left round the printing point. On a printing action for any character on the character board, for example on pressing a character key, the row-box holding that character's type is made to advance straight towards the printing point by as much as corresponds to the position of that type in its row; and the types in each row-box are stored beforehand in such orientations that, when a type reaches the printing position in this way, it always agrees with the direction of printing on the paper cylinder. The object is to make the weight moved in bringing the wanted type to the printing position as small as possible, so that operation is light and quick and printing efficiency is raised.
