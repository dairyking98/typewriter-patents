## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must advance with each key or several keys together on a stenographic machine.

**Solution:** Key levers with arms drive a notched bar of type levers, and fingers and wheels feed the paper forward on each key stroke.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
