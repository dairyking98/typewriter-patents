## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper cart switch gear for typewriters and machines similar Art, the purpose of the invention List, the latch carrier, the so-called Rücker, so to store that it gives way as a whole during the switching process, whereby the shock added as a result of the movement of the parts and significantly reduced the noise will.

**How it works (reading):** The latch carrier (Ruecker) is mounted so it gives way as a whole during switching, absorbing shock and noise from the ratchet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching shock and noise from the paper carriage ratchet is high.

**Solution:** The latch carrier (Ruecker) is mounted so it gives way as a whole during switching, absorbing shock and noise from the ratchet.
