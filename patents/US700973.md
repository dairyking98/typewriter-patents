## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to mechanism which may be employed in the natureof an attachment for carrying rolls of paper which may be written on by the machine and in which a copy of the written matter may be produced.
