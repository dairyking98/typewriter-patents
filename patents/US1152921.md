## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention is to provide novel ribbon mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon mechanism adapted for tally strip, strip roll holders, paper severing devices, and a platen constructed as multiple backings for the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tally strip work needs better ribbon, strip feeding, paper severing and platen support than existing machines.

**Solution:** Ribbon mechanism adapted for tally strip, strip roll holders, paper severing devices, and a platen constructed as multiple backings for the paper.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
