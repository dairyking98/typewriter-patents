## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be released from the line-spacing wheel by an operator-controlled release that does not bind.

**Solution:** Dogs on the platen press against the line-spacing wheel, and a wedge pressed between the inner ends of two releasing levers opens the dogs; a cam on the axle can also release them.
