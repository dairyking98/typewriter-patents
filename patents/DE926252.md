It was known as typewriters with a noiseless pressure of a similar structure which the types and the type switching are operated manually. These constructions have but the disadvantage that a hard or uneven attack still creates an annoying noise can cause and that the paper slide dancing up and down when the type is switched with the sheet of paper, the eyes tired. Previously it was thought impossible with typewriters with 4-type carriers because of the considerable weight of the type carriers and the joint systems to actuate them to switch types, the elevated position of the type basket opposite the paper roller to change.
