## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Noiseless power typewriters with four-type carriers (Underwood) need a way to shift case without a hard or uneven attack, noise, or the paper bouncing up and down.

**Solution:** Shift case by swivelling the whole type basket about an axis parallel to the platen, with carrier kinematics arranged so impression force is the same in either basket position.

**How it works:** Type carriers with two heads of two types each hang parallelogram-wise in an arc-shaped basket in front of the platen and are driven from key levers via drive members that briefly engage a constantly turning shaft and pass their pulse through links. The two key sets send the carrier along two different paths so one or the other head reaches the print point. Swivelling the basket up brings the other types to the same print point. Carrier arcs are centred on the basket's pivot, and linkages coupling basket and frame are laid out mirror-symmetrically so kinematics and force are unchanged by the shift.
