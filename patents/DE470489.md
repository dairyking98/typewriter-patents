The known types of attachment for Typewriter lamps are either bracket-like, or the lighting fixture is a standing lamp with a foot that is placed next to the typewriter will. The former type of attachment has the disadvantage that the attachment of the lighting fixture directly on the machine is not always possible, the latter version may be possible obstructive.
