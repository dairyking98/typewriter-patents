## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for operating hammers to effect impression; Special processing for irreversibly adding or changing the sheet copy material characteristics or its appearance, e.g. stamping, annotation printing, punching

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to operate a type-carrier which is normally stationary by a power-driven device or driver through mechanism normallyout of operative connection with the driver, but connected therewith at the will of the operator to bring the desired character on the type-carrier into printing position and also to operate the paperearriage and impression-hammer in a similar Way.

**How it works (reading):** A rotating driver is clutched at will to a normally stationary type carrier, hammer and carriage, with keys choosing how far the carrier turns.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing needs hand power for every movement; the inventor wants nearly all work done by a power driver with the keys only making connections.

**Solution:** A rotating driver is clutched at will to a normally stationary type carrier, hammer and carriage, with keys choosing how far the carrier turns.

**How it works:** The type carrier has circular rows of types and is turned stepwise by a rotary driver through connecting mechanism engaged by the finger keys. Stop pawls in frames arrest the carrier at the chosen character and a lock holds it during impression, and the carrier is slid lengthwise to bring a row to the hammer. The driver also fires the hammer, advances the paper carriage step by step, and returns the carriage and line-spaces the platen automatically at line end or by a key.
