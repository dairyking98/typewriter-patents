## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register check issuing)

**How it works (reading):** The check strip feed is driven by the machine through a locking device, forcing a uniform advance of the check paper, with a disconnect device for issuing checks.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On key-lever cash registers the check slip must also be pulled forward by hand, so the operator can forget to withdraw it and check lengths vary.

**Solution:** The check strip feed is driven by the machine through a locking device, forcing a uniform advance of the check paper, with a disconnect device for issuing checks.
