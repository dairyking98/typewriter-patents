## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improved roller bearing mechanism for the paper carriage of a typewriting machine.

**How it works (reading):** The paper carriage runs on front and rear roller sets in ways on the frame, with paired rollers at right angles to hold alignment and stop tilting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage alignment and guidance shift when the platen moves or the carriage jumps.

**Solution:** The paper carriage runs on front and rear roller sets in ways on the frame, with paired rollers at right angles to hold alignment and stop tilting.
