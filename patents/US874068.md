## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon vibrator with fixed throw cannot bring different zones of a multi-zone ribbon to the print point.

**Solution:** Place a movable block with surfaces of different height in the path of the universal bar so the ribbon vibrator lifts to a chosen zone, or not at all for stencils.

**How it works:** The universal bar of the machine always depresses by the same amount. Beneath it sits a pivoted block with two surfaces of different elevation, linked by a bell crank and levers to the ribbon vibrator. The operator moves a finger lever on the keyboard with marked positions Top, Bottom and Stencil, rocking the block so one surface, or none, lies under the universal bar. This sets the vibrator throw, bringing the top zone, bottom zone or no ribbon to the type; a spring-loaded lever and stops positively limit its travel.
