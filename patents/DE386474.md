## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** A lever mechanism driven by a key-lever-operated bar moves a slide so that each key press rotates a ratchet, and bevel gears engage the ribbon reel on one side while the other side runs off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon switch relies on a driver near the ribbon end, and the reel drive must be engaged and disengaged correctly on each side.

**Solution:** A lever mechanism driven by a key-lever-operated bar moves a slide so that each key press rotates a ratchet, and bevel gears engage the ribbon reel on one side while the other side runs off.
