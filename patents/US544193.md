## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** A matrix plate rotates on a bolt and holds matrices in a rim; a patrix plate above is driven by a lever through a feed mechanism to cut stencil letters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil letters need to be cut from card and paper with a simpler means.

**Solution:** A matrix plate rotates on a bolt and holds matrices in a rim; a patrix plate above is driven by a lever through a feed mechanism to cut stencil letters.

**Features:** [Type ring](../features/elem-ring.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
