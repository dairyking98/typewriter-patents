## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Faster regular typing than current machines.

**Solution:** A machine where vertical type rods are guided through concentric hollow-sphere plates and printed by pressing the rod heads; an interval plate spaces the carriage.

**How it works:** Type rods slide through two curved perforated plates (sections of hollow spheres) and an intermediate plate. Collars on each rod push the interval plate, whose guide rod and pawls step the paper carriage on a rack. Springs return the rods and the ribbon runs above the table.

**Features:** [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** type rod; curved plate; interval plate; guide rod; collar; spring; key; paper table; rack; escapement pawl; ribbon
