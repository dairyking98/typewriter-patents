## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines of the type bar class and more especially to the type bars and hangers and to the type actuating mechanism of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Type bars are individually mounted and adjustable on longer pivots at the sides; connecting links are pulled in parallel lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type bars and their hangers in visible machines need individual mounting and adjustment, with links that do not interfere.

**Solution:** Type bars are individually mounted and adjustable on longer pivots at the sides; connecting links are pulled in parallel lines.
