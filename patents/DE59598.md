## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Only the opening survives; it describes a keyboard typewriter whose types ride on steel bands fanned out in a housing.

**Solution:** Keyboard levers push, through linkages, steel bands carrying the types down a converging fan to a common print point, passing a rotating ink apparatus on the way, with the chain-driven paper carriage spaced by letter width.

**How it works:** Two-armed levers on hinges carry the letters in keyboard order, and each connects through pull rods and angle levers to a steel band bearing a type. The bands run in a fan-shaped compartment with converging channels, guided at their sides by rods with eyelets. At the fan's lower end a rotatable ink apparatus on each side has a cylindrical housing with a spring-pressed roller and an ink pad that the type depresses to take colour. As the type descends it also moves a roller and bracket which tighten a rod, and on the type's return a spring turns a driver and chain roller to move the carriage by that letter's width. The carriage has a pair of spring pliers to hold paper and a paper board stepped by a rake for line spacing. Everything is inside one housing.
