## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriters of the class in which the sheet being printed upon is stationary and the type is caused to travel across the sheet, and has for its object to provide an improved arrangement of parts having great simplicity and producing a machine of light construction and convenient for use.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage carrying a rotatable type drum is traversed across stationary paper; depressing a key first rotates the drum a key-dependent amount, then lowers the carriage to print.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters where the paper is stationary and the type travels need a light, simple arrangement.

**Solution:** A carriage carrying a rotatable type drum is traversed across stationary paper; depressing a key first rotates the drum a key-dependent amount, then lowers the carriage to print.

**How it works:** Keys depress an inclined bar so the lost motion between key lever and bar varies by key, and a quadrant and gearing rotate the type drum to the selected angle. A second rod then lowers the frame carrying the rails and type carriage so the drum strikes the paper lying flat below. A pawl, freed when the carriage drops, locks the drum during the impression, and a spring-loaded quadrant stops over-run. The drum has three rings of type, and a three-position shift key tilts the drum frame to bring one ring into play. A cord from a spring drum traverses the carriage for letter spacing.
