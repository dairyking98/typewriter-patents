## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Making inked duplicates with a supplementary ribbon carried on the carriage has been bulky, overhanging the carrier frame.

**Solution:** A compact supplementary ribbon mechanism inside the carrier frame, guiding several folds of one ribbon on one pair of spools so multiple duplicates are inked as well as the original.

**How it works:** On a No. 10 Smith Premier carriage, a supplementary ribbon runs on spools mounted mostly within the platen frame and passes back and forth along the platen in front of it. Guides lay several folds or plies of the ribbon between platen and paper fingers, each fold printing on a separate work sheet in the stack. The ribbon feeds with carriage line-space devices modified to cooperate.
