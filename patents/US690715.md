## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars in the parent design still obstruct the operator's view of the writing from the front.

**Solution:** Type-bars are set at right angles to their operating-shaft axes and grouped so a wide view of the writing stays open at the front of the type basket.

**How it works:** Operating-shaft portions are mounted so each bar sits at a right angle to its rotation axis, and the bar groups leave a front window on the printing point.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** type-bars; operating shafts; type basket
