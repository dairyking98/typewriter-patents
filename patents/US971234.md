## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates principally to typewriting machines although it is also applicable to some other sorts of machines.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon spools with a reverser weight in the spool and a bridge over the spool core opening, on a Remington front-strike machine driven by a spring drum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon improvements are needed; the first 3000 characters give the same ribbon-spool setting as the companion patent without stating a sharper defect.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools with a reverser weight in the spool and a bridge over the spool core opening, on a Remington front-strike machine driven by a spring drum.
