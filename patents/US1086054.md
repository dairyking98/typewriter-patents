## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars in a limited segmental space need long bearings and lateral rigidity.

**Solution:** Each type bar has a bifurcated laterally branching heel pivoted at two fulcrum points on either side of its plane, so the heels of adjacent bars overlap while each moves independently.
