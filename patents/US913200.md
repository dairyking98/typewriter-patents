## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**How it works (reading):** Two stamped sheet-steel bearing members screwed together form an annular groove with pivotal surfaces and protecting walls that carry the type bar end.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type-bar bearings need to be cheap, antifriction and tighten automatically when they wear loose.

**Solution:** Two stamped sheet-steel bearing members screwed together form an annular groove with pivotal surfaces and protecting walls that carry the type bar end.
