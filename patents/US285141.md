## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple cheap typewriter is wanted that the operator can see to use without a keyboard.

**Solution:** A circle of character-marked holes in a cylinder top plate; a hammer tapped into a hole pushes down a shaft that swings a type lever onto a paper cylinder below.

**How it works:** Each aperture in the top plate is marked with a character and holds a spring-raised vertical shaft whose guide pins keep it from turning. The operator taps the tapered hammer into a hole, depressing the shaft. A connecting rod swings a type lever pivoted around a circle in the cylinder, whose type block passes through a central aperture and strikes the paper on a cylinder in the box below. A key-operated toothed wheel inside the cylinder drives the paper cylinder through a cord and pulleys to move the paper. One hole is marked space and has no type lever.
