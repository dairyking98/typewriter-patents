**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversing devices were complex and prone to fault.

**Solution:** The driving shaft itself supplies the power to shift, via crown-toothed wheels and a pivoted shifter arm released by an obstruction on the ribbon.

**How it works:** Two spools on vertical shafts are driven by miter gears from a horizontal transverse shaft that slides endwise to engage either spool. A pawl and ratchet turn it on each stroke. A crown-toothed wheel on each shaft end rides up a pivoted shifter arm when engaged, forcing the shaft axially. A latch holds the arm out of use until an obstruction on the travelling ribbon releases it when a spool empties. Fitted to an Underwood front-strike machine.
