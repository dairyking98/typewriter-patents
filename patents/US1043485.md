## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** Its general object is to provide newand improved means for enabling columnstops to be moved from a non-operative place or position and set in an operative positionon a stop receiving member and for enabling the stop receiving member to be readily cleared of the set column stops preparatory to resetting the column stops for a new variety of work.

**How it works (reading):** Column stops are moved from a non-operative position into an operative position on a stop receiving member, and the member is cleared of set stops before resetting.
