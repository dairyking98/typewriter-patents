## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Step-by-step carriage advance is noisy and slow, and its timing is not adjustable.

**Solution:** An escapement with an escapement wheel and rack driven by key and space bar linkage, where the step can be divided between key depression and key rise, with the advance starting when the escapement returns.
