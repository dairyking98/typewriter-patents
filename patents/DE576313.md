## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source

**How it works (reading):** Each friction rod is moved by an adjustable intermediate lever on a spring-loaded eccentric, released by the key lever and returned by the type-lever action, so the rod briefly presses on continuously running rollers for one stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier power drives needed a hard keystroke or complicated thumb discs to press friction rods onto the drive rollers, and some were too delicate for continuous use.

**Solution:** Each friction rod is moved by an adjustable intermediate lever on a spring-loaded eccentric, released by the key lever and returned by the type-lever action, so the rod briefly presses on continuously running rollers for one stop.
