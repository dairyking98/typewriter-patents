## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanisms of key-lever machines need a simple way to feed the ribbon and lift it to the printing point, then clear it so the writing shows.

**Solution:** A pivoted universal-bar frame, moved by any key lever, lifts the ribbon guide and also drives pawl-and-ratchet ribbon feed to both spools.

**How it works:** Any depressed key lever lifts a shoulder on its elbow lever against the universal bar of a pivoted frame. A pin-and-slot on the frame arm rocks a lever that raises the central ribbon guide to the print point, and release lowers it to show the writing. Feed pawls on either end of the universal bar act on ratchet wheels on two shafts, which drive the spool spindles through bevel gears set in opposite senses. One pawl is spring-held in contact, the other lifted, with retaining pawls to stop backlash; the changeover control is past the opening text.
