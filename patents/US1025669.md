## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator throw cannot easily be varied, which is needed to print longitudinal stripes of two inks.

**Solution:** A mechanism varies the extent of throw imparted to the ribbon vibrator, so the ribbon's upper and lower stripes are struck alternately.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
