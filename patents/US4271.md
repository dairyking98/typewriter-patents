## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier apparatus for writing or copying characters was inefficient, the pantograph too unwieldy for a writer's fingers and other means too troublesome.

**Solution:** A pen on a lever is carried by a vertically sliding frame inside a horizontally sliding frame, and sets of cams combine right-angle motions so the pen traces any letter, with copies made at the same time.
