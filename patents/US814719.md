## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Exchanging a platen normally needs a tool, and shaft-mounted platens are heavier; the platen must also rotate exactly and keep the type striking at the right place.

**Solution:** Each end disk of the hollow platen has a hole with a conical and threaded recess, and the carrying-pins with matching cone and thread fit into it, so the platen is changed by hand with hand-wheels.

**Parts:** platen a; axle-disks b; carrying-pins c; hand-wheels d; conical recesses e; screw-thread g
