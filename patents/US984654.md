## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar actions had heavy, uneven touch, non-uniform key dip and leverage, and torsional strain on the outer type actions.

**Solution:** A key-lever to sub-lever to draw-link to typebar train arranged in matching arcs so every typebar gets uniform leverage, with a light start and gradual acceleration.

**How it works:** Key levers on a pivot rod drive upright sub-levers of the first order, pivoted at progressively higher hangers from centre to sides. Draw links connect the sub-lever tops to the typebars on a segment, giving a straight fore-and-aft pull. Toggles are arranged in similar arcs so resistance is low at first and rises as the bar accelerates. The segment slides vertically on balls for case shift.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** key lever; sub-lever; draw link; typebar; typebar segment; toggle; platen
