**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lever linkages between keys and type carrier make machines costly, heavy to operate and prone to derangement.

**Solution:** A spring-driven rotating type carrier stopped at the chosen character by pneumatic devices, with spring type arms struck individually against the paper.

**How it works:** A clock spring drives a large gear which drives a pinion ten times faster; the pinion shaft carries the type carrier, a hub with spring type-bars. A friction block and escapement let the pinion make one turn per operation, and the same drive spaces the carriage. Keys operate plungers, released by pneumatic devices, which stop the carrier at the chosen character. Cams on the gear drive a hammer that moves the type against the paper without the usual ribbon between.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Interchangeable element](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** clock spring; main gear; pinion; type carrier hub; spring type-bar; plunger; escapement plate; friction block; hammer lever; carriage

**Numbers:** gear to pinion ratio 10:1; 3 sheets of drawings
