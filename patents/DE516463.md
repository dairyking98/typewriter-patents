## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting the letters in a line typewriter's switching mechanism takes many separate parts.

**Solution:** Rows of adjustable stops sit on a common movable box; a shift key sets a switching stop on a double-armed lever, and the stops are cleared when the box returns.
