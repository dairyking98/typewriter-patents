## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The springs that press the forward and rear pressure rolls against the platen of Underwood front-strike machines are hard to mount and adjust, and it is not easy to secure a chosen pressure at each roll.

**Solution:** Springs carried on blocks at the platen frame extend along the rod and bear on the hinged arms that carry the pressure rolls; screws draw each spring down toward its block, so the pressure at each point can be set.
