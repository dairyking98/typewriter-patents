## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Select a type easily on a simple machine with few characters.

**Solution:** Concentric inner and outer shafts: turning the inner shaft rotates the magazine through a pin-and-slot, a hand gear on a rack slides the outer shaft for the tier, pressing the inner shaft prints.

**How it works:** An inner shaft fits loosely in an outer shaft, joined by a pin crossing the inner shaft in a slot of the outer. Turning the inner shaft turns the outer shaft and the cylindrical magazine fixed on top, bringing the wanted column of lever-held types to the platen. A hand gear meshing with an axial rack on the outer shaft raises or lowers the magazine for the wanted tier, independently of the inner shaft. A spring piece in notches round the magazine stops free turning. Pressing the inner shaft makes a starting disc drive a striking piece against the selected lever.
