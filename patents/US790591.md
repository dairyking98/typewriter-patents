## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stabilising means therefor using a scissor mechanism as stabiliser

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the construction of the keys of type-writing machines and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A key cup struck from sheet metal with a boss slotted to receive a socket that fits the key-lever stem, with a paper character disk and glass disk held by a cap, reducing parts and avoiding solder lumps.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Solder lumps in the key cup can break the glass character disk, and the key has too many parts and is costly.

**Solution:** A key cup struck from sheet metal with a boss slotted to receive a socket that fits the key-lever stem, with a paper character disk and glass disk held by a cap, reducing parts and avoiding solder lumps.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
