## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be easily adjusted relative to the line-space wheel and then held securely in position.

**Solution:** A clutch drum on the line-space wheel holds a disk on the platen axle with expanding grips; a detent lever is cammed away to brake the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
