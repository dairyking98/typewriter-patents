## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return motors on typewriters pull harder as the spring winds, so the carriage motion is uneven.

**Solution:** A spring drum pivoted eccentrically pays out the cord at increasing radius so the pull stays even, and the carriage is propelled against it by the keys.

**How it works:** A coil spring inside a drum on a spindle pulls the carriage back to its start through a cord. The drum is eccentric so the radius paying out cord grows as the spring tension rises. Printing and spacing keys, through cams on their shafts, drive a sliding bar, elbow lever and rock arm, pushing a primary pawl on the ratchet on the carriage against the spring. A secondary pawl and tripping mechanism release the carriage so it returns automatically.
