## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (counter for typewriters with totalizing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a counter for typewriters with automatic Sum drawing device in which the counting wheels come from the main drive wheel in decimal places be driven until one is attached to the gear wheels of the counter Stop hits on a counter-stop arranged on the machine frame.

**How it works (reading):** A parallel gear set between main drive wheel and digit roller carries zero-setting noses that work with a counter stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Zero-stop of counter digit wheels is hard to make strong and to arrange.

**Solution:** A parallel gear set between main drive wheel and digit roller carries zero-setting noses that work with a counter stop.

**How it works:** A parallel gear set with zero-setting noses on the digit drive works against a counter stop.
