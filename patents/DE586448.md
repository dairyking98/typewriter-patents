## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter, typewriter and the like with an automatic device Car elevator, which can be switched on and off by means of stops arranged on the car and is rendered ineffective by its locking device when the carriage is released.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage release button or tab key lever carries a member that moves into the path of stops on the carriage, controlling the lift coupling and disabling the inhibiting device, with no separate locking parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known automatic carriage lift devices needed complicated locking members and controls to switch them off.

**Solution:** The carriage release button or tab key lever carries a member that moves into the path of stops on the carriage, controlling the lift coupling and disabling the inhibiting device, with no separate locking parts.
