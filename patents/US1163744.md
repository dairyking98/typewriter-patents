## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to stencil-cutting machines, and has for its object to improve the construction of the tool-carrier, whereby it may be cheiply manufactured, and hold a set of tools from lateral displacement which are made square or other than round, so that they cannot turn, yet free to be moved in a vertical direction for the purpose of cutting the stencil;
