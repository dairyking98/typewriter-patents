## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting tool carrier) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to stencil-cutting machines, and has for its object to improve the construction of the tool-carrier, whereby it may be cheiply manufactured, and hold a set of tools from lateral displacement which are made square or other than round, so that they cannot turn, yet free to be moved in a vertical direction for the purpose of cutting the stencil;

**How it works (reading):** The tools are held square in a tool carrier so they cannot turn, a stencil holder is drawn forward and tilted to insert or remove the stencil, and step-by-step actuating mechanism advances the stencil support.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil-cutting tools turn when they should not, and stencils are hard to insert or remove without bending them.

**Solution:** The tools are held square in a tool carrier so they cannot turn, a stencil holder is drawn forward and tilted to insert or remove the stencil, and step-by-step actuating mechanism advances the stencil support.
