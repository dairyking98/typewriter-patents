## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machinesand more particularly to escapement mechanism for a machine of the type designed to effect printing withoutproducing noise.

**How it works (reading):** An escapement with two co-axial escapement wheels, one spring-pressed relative to the other and limited by slot-and-screw stops, engaged in turn by a feed dog so that carriage spacing is quiet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Carriage spacing on typewriters is noisy because the clicking of the escapement parts makes the carriage feed audible.

**Solution:** An escapement with two co-axial escapement wheels, one spring-pressed relative to the other and limited by slot-and-screw stops, engaged in turn by a feed dog so that carriage spacing is quiet.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
