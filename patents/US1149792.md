## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter's paper roll had no adjustable support that moves with the carriage and stops the roll from over-rotating when the platen is turned.

**Solution:** A vertically adjustable main frame carries a paper-roll frame that travels with the carriage, with tension plugs in the roll cores and a paper cutter.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** main frame; paper roll frame; tension plugs; paper guide and cutter
