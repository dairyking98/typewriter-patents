> Description Translated from German Line setting and line switching device for cash registers, Booking or similar machines The invention relates to a line setting or Line switching device for cash registers, booking machines or similar machines. According to the device according to the German patent 968290 , the document placed in the printing table controls its automatic setting on the line to be printed and triggers the setting also before the start of the machine cycle. This is achieved in that a spring mechanism setting the printing table is made effective by the document placed in the printing table, so that the document is transferred directly to the corresponding line, preferably determined by edge cutouts. Such a device therefore requires the arrangement and effectiveness of a spring mechanism for setting the document. According to the invention, the automatic adjustment takes place document moving in different directions onto the one to be printed Line while the machine is in motion by means of driven means that operate on the conveying means act for the document.
