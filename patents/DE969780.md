## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line setting and switching, cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line setting or Line switching device for cash registers, booking machines or similar machines.

**How it works (reading):** Driven conveying means move the document onto the line while the machine runs, with multiple monitoring elements allowing accruing amounts in several columns and rows.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Documents in a printing table must be set to the correct line, and the earlier spring-based setting did not allow multiple column updates.

**Solution:** Driven conveying means move the document onto the line while the machine runs, with multiple monitoring elements allowing accruing amounts in several columns and rows.
