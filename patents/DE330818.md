## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**How it works (reading):** The height is set by one rear joint of the control pentagon, with control plates or a disc-shaped link with bolts that catch in projections on the fishing-rod, so the type locks at each of five writing positions even with joint play.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Height control of each type wheel sector needed highly accurate, costly joints.

**Solution:** The height is set by one rear joint of the control pentagon, with control plates or a disc-shaped link with bolts that catch in projections on the fishing-rod, so the type locks at each of five writing positions even with joint play.
