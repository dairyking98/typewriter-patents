## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Flat page-size platens or smaller flat platens having a greater size than line-size platens

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to mechanism of the character stated, in which the platen cylinder is displaceable with respect to its shaft, or with respect to the hearings in which it normally rotates in the frame, so that'the platen shaft and platen frame may be stationary during the shift of the platen and introduction of the paper.

**How it works (reading):** A manually set stop catches a part rotating with the eccentrics, so only the proper angle is reached before the shift.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The eccentric platen must be at the correct angle before shifting it away from the paper guide.

**Solution:** A manually set stop catches a part rotating with the eccentrics, so only the proper angle is reached before the shift.

**How it works:** The stop is moved into the path of an element turning with platen and eccentrics, arresting rotation at the angle where the shift is effective, and is set by the same means that operates the shift.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** platen; eccentric; platen shaft; stop; detaining device

**Improves on:**

- eccentric platen with a marked cylinder: mechanical stop replaces a visual mark
