## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter impression and carriage spacing take hand effort; wants foot power to print and to space.

**Solution:** A treadle-operated lever carries adjustable impression keys down onto type pins of a hand-rotated type disk, and the same stroke advances the carriage by a pawl.

**How it works:** The type-carrying disk hangs above the paper table and is turned by knobs to bring the wanted type pin under an impression key. A foot treadle pulls the lever down, whose threaded impression keys strike the type pins and print. An adjustable bolt on the lever also presses a pawl lever that steps the carriage along a rack bar by one or more notches, so narrow letters get one notch and wide ones more. A retaining pawl holds the carriage and can be released.
