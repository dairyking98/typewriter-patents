## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen shift key needs a better way to be held in upper-case position.

**Solution:** A lock lever on the shift key engages a pivoted latch in a notch to hold the key down.

**How it works:** The shift key is pivoted near the rear and spring-returned; pressing it raises the platen to upper case. A lock lever on the key has a wedging surface and spring, and a frame-mounted latch with a lipped arm engages a notch on a projection of the key when depressed, holding it. The lock lever releases the latch.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md)

**Parts:** shift key; lock lever; latch; spring; guide; detent; platen
