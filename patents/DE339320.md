## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a typewriter according to the Underwood-Hanson system, as it is protected by the main patent 294721.

**How it works (reading):** The subtraction changeover is triggered by a stop on the moving carriage that acts on a pawl and linkage, which switches the counter mechanisms to nines-complement subtraction, and the 9-pin group is disengaged after setting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On an Underwood-Hanson keyboard adding machine, converting the counter from adding to subtracting needs a reliable automatic changeover and a way to set the 9-pins without interference.

**Solution:** The subtraction changeover is triggered by a stop on the moving carriage that acts on a pawl and linkage, which switches the counter mechanisms to nines-complement subtraction, and the 9-pin group is disengaged after setting.
