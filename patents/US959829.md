## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Underwood, the space key also winds the ribbon, so the ribbon feeds idly between words and is used unevenly.

**Solution:** Make the space bar throw the ribbon-winding pawl out of engagement so only the type keys feed the ribbon.

**How it works:** The ribbon-winding pawl is carried on an arm driven from the universal bar frame and turns a ratchet and gears on the spool shafts. A finger on a rock shaft moved by the space-bar levers is brought into the path of a cam or nose on the pawl (or acts as a fulcrum). The pawl is lifted off the ratchet so it reciprocates without winding the ribbon.

**Parts:** space bar; rock shaft; finger; ribbon-feeding pawl; ratchet; universal bar; escapement dog; escapement wheel; ribbon spool
