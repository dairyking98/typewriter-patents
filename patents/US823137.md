## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A key-stem and key-lever pivot should have no superfluous parts and let a worn or broken key be replaced easily.

**Solution:** The key stem is a sheet-metal tube with resilient arms that snap onto the key lever, and an inserted key slips into the tube end, so the assembly has three pieces.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** key-lever; key-stem; inserted key
