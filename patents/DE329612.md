## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating typewriter counters)

**How it works (reading):** A calculating machine hangs from a rod on the paper carriage, and as the carriage moves each setting member meets a stepped approach on the number type lever that was struck, adjusting the counter by an amount equal to that digit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter calculator needs its counting wheels set by the number type levers as the paper carriage moves, without separate keys for each counter.

**Solution:** A calculating machine hangs from a rod on the paper carriage, and as the carriage moves each setting member meets a stepped approach on the number type lever that was struck, adjusting the counter by an amount equal to that digit.
