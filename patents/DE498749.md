## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** Two or more adjustable guide rollers are placed diagonally on the carriage frame, so a front and a rear roller both engage the rear carriage rail and resist twist.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage can twist and tilt in the horizontal plane when it strikes the front carriage stop.

**Solution:** Two or more adjustable guide rollers are placed diagonally on the carriage frame, so a front and a rear roller both engage the rear carriage rail and resist twist.

> Description Translated from German Carriage on typewriters The present invention aims to to design the paper carriage guide on typewriters so that. when occurring of forces trying to twist the paper cart in a horizontal direction, such as this, for example, when the paper trolley collides with the front trolley stop the case: is, a twisting of the carriage in the horizontal plane and one with it associated tilting or seizing of the same is reliably prevented. According to the invention this is achieved in that two or more in a known manner adjustable guide rollers for the rear carriage bar diagonally are attached opposite to the carriage frame.
