## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line indicator bars either do not hold stiff cards flat, or if pressed constantly against the platen they smudge copies and leave lines.

**Solution:** Both line indicator bars on a common carrier follow the platen shift and can be moved by one handle between a close-fitting position and a slightly raised position, pressing a card against the platen when needed.

**How it works:** Two line indicator bars lie between the platen and the type guide, one each side of the printing point, joined by springy lugs to a common support piece. The support piece is guided by links and springs so it moves up and down with the platen shift. A double-lever actuator, turned by a handle with an eccentric flat piece against a spring, pushes the support piece toward the platen, pressing both bars evenly onto a postcard or thick stack. Released, the springs return the bars to a small distance from the sheet so thin paper is not smudged. Spring guide rollers keep the bars guided and prevent them bending away and jamming the ribbon.
