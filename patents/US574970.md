## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements to the Franklin typewriter as a whole machine.

**Solution:** A full typebar machine with a swinging platen/paper-roll frame, shift and space levers, feed rolls and ribbon attachments.

**How it works:** Described only from the figure list in the opening: the platen roll sits in a frame hung on links from a rod in the carriage frame, which runs on a wedge-shaped guide rod via grooved rolls. Key-levers drive typebars; separate sheets show shift levers, space lever, feed rolls, ink ribbon attachment and an alignment slot bar. Much of the mechanism is not in the text shown.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen frame; carriage frame; guide rod; grooved roll; key-lever; typebar; shift lever; space lever; ink ribbon; alignment bar

**Numbers:** 11 sheets of drawings
