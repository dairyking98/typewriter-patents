## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register receipt printing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on cash registers for printing receipts, which are placed in the cash register for printing.

**How it works (reading):** The receipt is placed on a table; a grooved disk and a cross conveyor move it out of the printing path between the two prints so that it stays reachable after printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Receipts printed twice in a cash register are drawn back into the machine and cannot be grasped by the operator.

**Solution:** The receipt is placed on a table; a grooved disk and a cross conveyor move it out of the printing path between the two prints so that it stays reachable after printing.
