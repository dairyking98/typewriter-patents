## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon records made on the usual record sheets take excessive room because the check sheet's lines are widely spaced.

**Solution:** The record sheet advances one line-space notch while the check sheet is advanced by a hand wheel and a pair of rolls geared to it, to the next ruled blank.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md)

**Parts:** platen; record sheet; check sheet; hand wheel; pair of rolls; carbon
