## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Twelve listed shortcomings of a typebar machine: ribbon feed, spacing, end-of-line key locking, drawing long dashes, bell tripping, apron tension, typebar shaft bearings and cylinder mounting.

**Solution:** A typebar writing machine with a pulling ribbon-feed hook, key lock at end of line, conical typebar bearings, two-piece adjustable typebars and a cylinder frame that can be tilted to view the work.

**How it works:** A spring-driven sprocket wheel draws the carriage by cord, with a ratchet and pawl feeding the ribbon spools alternately via a heart-shaped cam. Keys are locked at line end to prevent overprinting, with a way to add a hyphen or letters to finish a word. Typebars swing on conical-pointed shafts mounted in grouped hangers on the basket ring. The impression cylinder sits in open Y-bearings and its frame can be placed in normal, view-the-work or paper-insert positions.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)
