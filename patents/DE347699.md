## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such adjustable lifts for the carrier of a typewriter ribbon in which the ribbon carrier cooperates with one with the universal rail Gear part is inevitably connected and the change in lift height only by using a more or less large part of the universal rail route is achieved.

**How it works (reading):** A key lever and bell-crank linkage with a roller and a notched leaf spring sets the ribbon carrier to a few fixed lift heights, and the set position is recognisable from the key.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing ribbon lift height by a larger or smaller part of the universal rail travel is unclear and complicated; the setting is not readable.

**Solution:** A key lever and bell-crank linkage with a roller and a notched leaf spring sets the ribbon carrier to a few fixed lift heights, and the set position is recognisable from the key.

There are facilities of this type are known in which between the Ribbon carrier and a shift lever effecting its change in stroke one with several Stop surfaces for the universal rail provided member is arranged. The invention aims to create a device of the type mentioned, which is through particularly simple design distinguishes and at the same time recognizes in a logical way lets what lifting height the device is set to.
