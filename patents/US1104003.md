## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ordinary rocking dog escapement wears quickly and can skip.

**Solution:** A rotary spacing wheel with spring-actuated dogs replaces the reciprocating dogs.

**How it works:** A spacing wheel on the rocker shaft carries radial spring dogs that successively engage and release one tooth of the escapement wheel at each key stroke, positively stopping the carriage after one space.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; spacing wheel; spring dog; rocker shaft

**Improves on:**

- Remington rocking dog escapement: rotary dogs instead of reciprocating
