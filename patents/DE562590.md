> Description Translated from German Device for placing the key plates on the key bases of typewriters and the like the key plate on the key bases of typewriters and the like. The application of the key plates to the key bases of typewriters took place so far-in such a way that the key bases in a jig clamped, the key plates placed on it, aligned and then on the key bases were glued in place. However, this type of application is very time consuming and required constant attention of the operator, which further enhances his work performance is reduced. The invention now relates to a device which has these disadvantages eliminated. According to the invention is the clamping device for the key bases provided with guide surfaces parallel to the clamping jaws, which are connected to the Key plate itself or with a magazine containing the key plate arranged straightening surfaces cooperate. The button plates are advantageously at least at one edge rectilinearly flattened, preferably perpendicular to the longitudinal axis of the on them printed sign.
