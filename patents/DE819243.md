## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (spring winding for embossing machine trolley) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving mechanisms, e.g. springs stressed during carriage return

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a spring winding mechanism, in particular for the plate holder trolley of embossing machines for address printing plates, with a completely surrounding the spring two-part housing.

**How it works (reading):** The spring is enclosed by one housing part with a central retaining pin, and the two parts are made identical so they can be swapped.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The two-part spring housing of the plate-holder trolley is hard to assemble around the spring, and its two halves differ, so many spares must be kept.

**Solution:** The spring is enclosed by one housing part with a central retaining pin, and the two parts are made identical so they can be swapped.
