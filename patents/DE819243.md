> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a spring winding mechanism, in particular for the plate holder trolley of embossing machines for address printing plates, with a completely surrounding the spring two-part housing. The housing consisted essentially of housing and Lid. Since the case enclosed the spring, it was difficult to get the spring to be anchored with the spring start and spring end in the brackets provided. Furthermore, the cover had a shape different from that of the other housing part. You had therefore have several models in stock for repair purposes. Otherwise it was makes it difficult to keep the spare parts in stock. The invention has for its object the structure of the spring winding mechanism, in particular of the housing to simplify. An essential feature of the invention is that that the partial section of the housing in the middle of the housing and transversely to which the housing penetrating retaining pin runs. Although in such a configuration the The spring is enclosed by one housing part, the insertion is made easier, because the cylindrical edge of the housing part is considerably narrower than before.
