## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism for typewriting machines and more particularly to mechanism in which a ribbon is or may be automatically moved into the path of an approaching type at each printing operation.

**How it works (reading):** Adjusting means on the ribbon vibrator actuating link, with an index plate, to select the ribbon field and to disable the movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons with several colour fields need a way to bring a chosen field to the printing point, and ribbon movement must be disabled for mimeograph use.

**Solution:** Adjusting means on the ribbon vibrator actuating link, with an index plate, to select the ribbon field and to disable the movement.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
