## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter is wanted with a type wheel set by a setting bar and moved by keys.

**Solution:** A type-wheel typewriter whose key levers press a setting bar that positions the wheel, with a hammer printing, escapement, ribbon rollers and a printing-line gauge.

**How it works:** Key levers in three banks of different length act on a transverse type-wheel-setting bar on rollers, so the key travel and force are equal for all keys. The setting bar shifts the type wheel on its carrier, a hammer on a bar prints against the carriage, and the carriage moves with an escapement and pinion and rack that can be thrown out. The opening also names levers moving the carriage without printing, ribbon rollers, a gauge to find the printing line and an alarm tripper.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Keys that only set the element](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md)
- **Impression:** [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** type wheel; type-wheel setting bar; key lever; hammer; carriage; escapement; pinion and rack; ribbon roller; alarm tripper; gauge; spiral spring

**Numbers:** three banks of key levers
