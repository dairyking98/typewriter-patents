## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (drive for sprocket calculating machines)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a mechanical drive for calculating machines, in particular for sprocket calculating machines.

**How it works (reading):** Coupling and uncoupling run circularly in one direction so the multiplier keys are neutral and every position gives a new value; the drive stands ready at any point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multiplier-setting mechanisms on sprocket calculators are complicated and each multiplier key has a fixed value.

**Solution:** Coupling and uncoupling run circularly in one direction so the multiplier keys are neutral and every position gives a new value; the drive stands ready at any point.
