## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to a type-bar action for type-writing machines of that kind known as front-strike or visible-writing machines in which the typebars are arranged below the platen and are pivoted to swing upwardly and rearwardly to make the impression at the front side of the platen where the writing can be seen by the operator without moving thecarriage or platen.

**How it works (reading):** Two-character typebars pivoted on a segment with alternating long and short trunnion pivots so type heads nest closely, driven by balanced key levers acting in the plane of bar swing.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-strike typebar action should stand hard service without adjustment, with balanced key levers and direct lines of force, from few simple parts.

**Solution:** Two-character typebars pivoted on a segment with alternating long and short trunnion pivots so type heads nest closely, driven by balanced key levers acting in the plane of bar swing.

**How it works:** Typebars lie side by side in an arc below the platen and swing up and rearward to strike the front of the platen. Each carries a two-character type head; the second character is struck by shifting the platen downward. Bar pivots are trunnions seated between segment plates, in two alternating lengths, with the short-bar pivot half-way between rest position and the printing point, so all heads rest side by side and strike the same centre. Key levers are balanced on their pivots and pull the bars in direct lines. The bars can be raised for cleaning.
