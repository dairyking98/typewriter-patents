## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper-guides for type-Writer carriages and means for retaining the paper'- in proper contact with the platen during the printing operation.

**How it works (reading):** Paper-holding bail and spring-backed rollers press the sheet to the platen, and a column gauge on a slide indicates margin position; the bail can be thrown back to insert paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper slips out of contact with the platen during printing and a fresh sheet is hard to introduce.

**Solution:** Paper-holding bail and spring-backed rollers press the sheet to the platen, and a column gauge on a slide indicates margin position; the bail can be thrown back to insert paper.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
