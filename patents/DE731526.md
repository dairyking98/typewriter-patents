## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the paper holding rail of typewriters, especially for sheet music typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A paper holding rail with an upper and lower edge carrying the same line division, set at an angle, with a triangular section reaching up to the platen, so the staves can be aligned; adjustable carriage stops follow the rail's division.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Notes of several voices on musical typewriters do not line up across staves, so simultaneous parts cannot be read vertically.

**Solution:** A paper holding rail with an upper and lower edge carrying the same line division, set at an angle, with a triangular section reaching up to the platen, so the staves can be aligned; adjustable carriage stops follow the rail's division.
