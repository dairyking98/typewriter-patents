## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon between type and paper obscures the character last printed.

**Solution:** A circular flexible inked disc ribbon between type-wheel and platen, mounted on the swinging type-wheel frame and rotated each stroke.

**How it works:** The type-wheel on a shaft is carried by a swinging frame that tilts to print. A circular ribbon with a metal reinforce is sprung over a split-tube catch on an arm of the frame. A ratchet on the catch is turned by a lever and spring pawl riding a cam rise on the top plate, rotating the disc to a fresh area each print. The ribbon moves with the wheel and is retracted with the frame, leaving the character visible, and is continuously inked.
