## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements to attach the ribbon to the spool

**How it works (reading):** Attaching means join the free ends of the inking ribbon to tabs of uninked strips that are secured to the ribbon spools.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon ends must be joined to the tabs or strips on the ribbon spools.

**Solution:** Attaching means join the free ends of the inking ribbon to tabs of uninked strips that are secured to the ribbon spools.
