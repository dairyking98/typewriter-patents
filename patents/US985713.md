**How it works (reading):** The platen shaft is divided into two parts joined by pins and steel balls seated in slots; a hand-knurled wheel on the outer part releases the platen from the ratchet-driven line spacing so it can be moved by a fraction or multiple of the normal interval.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen must be released from the ordinary line-spacing so that it can be turned for a partial or multiple spacing between lines without changing the normal adjustment.

**Solution:** The platen shaft is divided into two parts joined by pins and steel balls seated in slots; a hand-knurled wheel on the outer part releases the platen from the ratchet-driven line spacing so it can be moved by a fraction or multiple of the normal interval.
