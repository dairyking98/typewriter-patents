## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must be removed quickly for repair or to fit a wider paper, but the rigid mounting also has to stay rigid in use.

**Solution:** The carriage is mounted on a supporting plate so the whole carriage structure can be removed from the machine body as one piece, with the connecting plate bracketed to the main guide rod.

**Features:** [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)
