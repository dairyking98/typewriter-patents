## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**How it works (reading):** Key levers and angle levers are made as uniformly continuous parts, and the angle levers are carried on a continuous wire, so the linkage sits in a concentrated, uniform arrangement without lateral interference.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Angled tie rods linking key levers to type levers run obliquely and load the type levers sideways, causing jamming and wear, and spreading the parts across the width of the machine makes the layout crowded.

**Solution:** Key levers and angle levers are made as uniformly continuous parts, and the angle levers are carried on a continuous wire, so the linkage sits in a concentrated, uniform arrangement without lateral interference.
