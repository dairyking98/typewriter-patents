## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manuscript holders have a line indicator moved by noisy, complicated ratchet mechanisms.

**Solution:** A cardboard holder whose line-indicator strip is carried down by the sliding manuscript plate and held back by tongues on the stand plate, so it steps silently.

**How it works:** A folded stand plate carries a manuscript plate that slides vertically a limited distance. The ruler-like line indicator has tongues at both ends engaging the manuscript plate front and the stand plate back. Pushing the manuscript plate down takes the indicator with it; a spring returns the plate while the stand plate tongues hold the indicator, so it advances one step per cycle. An adjustable stop sets the step to one or several lines.

**Parts:** stand plate; manuscript plate; line indicator strip; tongue; spring; adjustable stop; strut

**Numbers:** travel of two typewriter line heights
