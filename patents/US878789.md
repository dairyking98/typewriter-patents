**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to type-writing machines, particularly to tabulating mechanism therefor.

**How it works (reading):** A light carriage driven for tabulating work skips the letter escapement and is arrested gently without brakes or dash-pots.
