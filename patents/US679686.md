## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon cartridges

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to means for carrying and feeding the ribbons of type-writers;

**How it works (reading):** A frame carrying two ribbon spools, with ratchets and pawls that turn each spool as the frame oscillates, feeds the ribbon past the printing point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons needed a simple feed in place of manual spool handling.

**Solution:** A frame carrying two ribbon spools, with ratchets and pawls that turn each spool as the frame oscillates, feeds the ribbon past the printing point.
