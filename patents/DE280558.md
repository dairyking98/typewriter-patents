## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The cam slot is curved with its radius directed upward, so the reaction force reduces the horizontal resistance and eases the key stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key strike force is high because the cam slot in the key lever pushes unfavourably on the transmission lever.

**Solution:** The cam slot is curved with its radius directed upward, so the reaction force reduces the horizontal resistance and eases the key stroke.
