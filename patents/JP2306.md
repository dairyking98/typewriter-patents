## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In the shorthand device of JP940 the box shakes when the roller is turned by hand and the fingertip rings can fall off.

**Solution:** Add a thumb push-button and ratchet to advance the paper while the left hand holds the box, and give each vowel-stamp ring a screw adjustment plus a spring-loaded second type for nasal sounds.

**How it works:** A push rod with a button passes through the lid and carries a pawl that steps a ratchet on the front paper roller, so the left thumb winds paper on while four fingers steady the box; a check pawl holds the ratchet and a lever lifts it to wind back. Carbon sheet is held over the lid slots by springs. Five finger-ring stamps, each tightened by a toothed-wheel screw rod, stamp vowels into the printed consonant columns of the ruled paper. Each ring has a fixed type and a spring-retracted second type that is pushed out only by extra finger-pad force to mark a nasal sound.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Printing device. This invention is an improvement on the shorthand device of Patent No. 940. It relates to a printing device in which a push button, for turning the paper-winding roller, projects from the top of the box, and a screw is fitted to the back of each vowel stamp. Its object is, besides achieving the object of the original invention, to let the left hand hold the box down with four fingers while the thumb presses the button to turn the paper-winding roller, so that the box does not shake in use; and to make the vowel stamps adjustable in size, so that anyone can put a fingertip in and tighten it to fit, and, by the movement of the finger pad, also show a nasal sound when needed. The attached drawings show a construction achieving this object.
