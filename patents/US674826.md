## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide novel means in a machine of this character which will act automaticallyto reverse the direction of the ribbon-feed without any care or attentionfrom the operator.

**How it works (reading):** Two ratchet-wheels on the ribbon spools are driven by pawls on an oscillating member, and the ribbon feed reverses automatically at the end of its run; the excerpt is for a workman's time recorder, with typewriters noted as a possible use.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must be reversed by hand when it reaches the end of its length, which needs the operator's attention.

**Solution:** Two ratchet-wheels on the ribbon spools are driven by pawls on an oscillating member, and the ribbon feed reverses automatically at the end of its run; the excerpt is for a workman's time recorder, with typewriters noted as a possible use.
