## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon in a visible typewriter that vibrates toward the printing point is only used along part of its width unless cross-fed automatically.

**Solution:** Drop the automatic cross-feed and give the operator a hand-set control that places the ribbon carrier in one of several fixed positions so different widthwise strips of the ribbon are used.

**How it works:** The ribbon runs from two vertical spools, through guides, to slots in a vibrating ribbon carrier at the printing point. Spool shafts are driven by bevel pinions from a driving shaft that takes motion from the carriage spring drum during letter spacing. A hand key and locking plate set the ribbon carrier frame in predetermined positions. Typebars are driven by key levers with restoring springs.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon spool; bevel pinion; ribbon driving shaft; spring drum; ribbon carrier; locking plate; hand control key; platen; key lever

**Improves on:**

- automatic ribbon cross-feeding: replaced by hand-set predetermined ribbon positions
