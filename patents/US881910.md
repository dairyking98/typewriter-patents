## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed and reversal on typebar machines needed a simple, efficient mechanism; earlier ones were complicated.

**Solution:** Drive the ribbon spools from the carriage escapement shaft through a ratchet and pawl lever, and reverse direction automatically by an axially shifting clutch shaft tripped by levers on the spools.

**How it works:** A shaft carries the carriage pinion and an escapement ratchet wheel whose two detents are rocked by the key-actuated universal bar. A second ratchet wheel on this shaft lifts a pawl on a lever each time the escapement steps; the lever's other end carries a pawl that advances a ratchet on the spool drive shaft. A stationary knockoff disengages the first pawl near the end of each step so a spring returns the lever. The spool shaft slides axially, and its two clutch sections engage one spool at a time, so one spool winds while the other supplies. Bail-shaped levers pivoted in each spool, attached to the ribbon ends, lift when the ribbon runs out and cam the shaft across to the other spool, reversing the feed.
