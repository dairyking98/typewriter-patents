## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In type-wheel machines the wheel is brought to the character and printed in one key stroke, which limits speed and uniformity; fixed letter spacing also looks unlike ordinary printing.

**Solution:** A pipelined method: pressing a key selects the next character while that same stroke prints the previously selected one from the wheel at rest, with a spring-driven vertical type-wheel stopped by key-raised dial pins, proportional carriage feed by quads, and an ink-roller system.

**How it works:** Two type-wheels (lower case and upper case) on a vertical shaft are driven by a spring. Under the shaft is a dial with one sliding pin per key. A key raises its dial pin through a lever; a swinging arm on the shaft stops against the raised pin and so positions the wheel. One pin is always up so the wheel cannot run away. On the next key stroke, a presser bar first prints the character already in position, a locking wheel holds the shaft, the old pin is pushed down, and then the shaft is released to turn to the new pin. Upper case is selected by pressing a bar that raises the other wheel to the print point. A set of quads of three widths blocks the paper carriage; pawls retract the right number for the width of the character, so the carriage advances in proportion to letter width. The carriage is pulled by spring barrels wound when it is returned, and ink is spread by a distributing roller from a cylinder to an inking roller on the wheel.

**Features:** [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SEL:pointer-hole](../features/sel-pointer-hole.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [IMP:spring-energy](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)

**Parts:** type-wheel shaft; lower-case type-wheel; upper-case type-wheel; dial; dial pin; key lever; stop arm; locking wheel; presser bar; mainspring barrel; paper carriage; paper cylinder; quad; feed pawl; ink cylinder; distributing roller; inking roller; shift bar; toggle

**Numbers:** quads of three widths: narrowest, next size 2 quads, widest 3 quads; 7 sheets of drawings

**Improves on:**

- earlier dial/pin type-wheel machines: stop pins stay up so the same key can repeat a character, and printing happens with the wheel at rest while the next character is selected
- fusee/gear-train driven wheel: light spring applied directly to the shaft starts faster and avoids gear momentum shocks
