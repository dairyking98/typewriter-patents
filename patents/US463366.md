## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen can overshoot one notch when the line-space lever is worked rapidly, and paper pressure is uneven.

**Solution:** A stop arm on the line-space lever enters a ratchet tooth to arrest the platen after one notch, plus a spring-loaded paper clip.

**How it works:** The lever's pawl extension engages the platen ratchet sidewise; an upward arm with a beveled end enters the next tooth at the end of the stroke so momentum cannot carry the platen further. A spring returns the lever. A clip on a rock-shaft with slotted arms and springs presses the paper evenly on the platen.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet wheel; line-space lever; pawl extension; stop arm; spring; friction spring; paper clip; rock-shaft
