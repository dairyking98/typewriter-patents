## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter with typebars in a ring needed an easy way to see the writing, plus cheap precise typebar and guide construction.

**Solution:** Typebars in a ring with tapered type shanks guided into a centering plate, and an impression cylinder that can be raised and turned to show the writing.

**How it works:** Key bars are stamped from steel with integral springs. They connect to thin steel typebars given a quarter twist, which swing from a conoidal ring on loose-fitting pivots. The type have tapered shanks that enter a perforation in a centering plate so each prints at the same point despite play. The impression cylinder on its carriage can be raised and turned to bring the writing into view, and an inking-ribbon carrier is reversed by its mechanism. Letter spacing is a pitman with ratchet on the cylinder.
