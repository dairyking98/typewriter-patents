## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Closely packed typebars rebound from the basket cushion on return and clash with the next bar at speed.

**Solution:** Rest the typebars on a flexible tough casing filled with sand or similar non-resilient, energy-absorbing material.

**How it works:** Radial typebars on a ring rest at their type ends against a curved casing of tough inelastic material filled with sand or granular matter. The casing yields on impact, jarring neighbouring bars and absorbing the blow without springing back. A stiff sheet-metal ring on rods supports it on inturned fingers with a slight clearance, so friction adds damping. It is a divisional of the lead-ring cushion patent US811182.
