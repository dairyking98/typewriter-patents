## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (electric teleprinter wiring) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or local circuits for mosaic printer telegraph systems

**How it works (reading):** Each sign is produced by pressing one key that energizes a fixed combination of wires, marking a paper strip at the receiver with dots or lines, with no synchronous parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Teleprinters need synchronous disks and rollers that are costly and sensitive; earlier multi-wire designs needed many electromagnets.

**Solution:** Each sign is produced by pressing one key that energizes a fixed combination of wires, marking a paper strip at the receiver with dots or lines, with no synchronous parts.
