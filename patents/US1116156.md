## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator must lift for each case and also be able to stay down or drop for stencil cutting.

**Solution:** A ribbon-vibrator linkage with a slidable stop bar that gives full-height, low-height or no ribbon motion, and moves the vibrator down for stencils.

**How it works:** A rocking universal bar pulls a link on a pair of levers connected to the vibrator stud. A stop slide with notches and a detent roller sets which stop stands in the path of the lever: one gives full ribbon throw, one a lower throw, and none leaves the ribbon idle. For stencils the lever acts as a second-order lever on a fixed pin so the vibrator moves down, leaving room above the ribbon for the type.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; universal bar; lever; link; stop slide; detent roller; rock shaft; stop

**Numbers:** three notches
