## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-spacing mechanism of the platen needs a simpler and improved construction.

**Solution:** An adjustable stop for the line-spacing pawl, which can be set at different positions so the line-space movement of the platen ratchet wheel is varied.
