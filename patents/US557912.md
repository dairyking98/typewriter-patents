## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper is hard to insert and the platen cannot be swapped between jobs without losing the paper position, especially when making manifold copies.

**Solution:** A removable platen with its paper and feed roll, line spacing adjustable, lifting out of notched carriage ends and latched in place.

**How it works:** The cylindrical platen shaft has a socket over a stud at the right end and a knurled button at the left; it lifts out of vertical notches in the carriage ends and is latched. A hanger on the platen shaft carries arms with a feed roll and a paper table that extends under and in front of the platen to the printing point. Cam levers swing the paper table away from the platen. A line-spacing mechanism with adjustment gives wider or narrower line spacing.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** carriage end; platen; platen shaft; knurled button; latch; hanger; arm; feed roll; paper table; cam lever; line-spacing mechanism
