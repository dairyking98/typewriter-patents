## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On flat-platen book typewriters, sheets narrower than the track spacing are held by shifting rails, and there is no good way to hold sheet margins that can lift automatically for inserting work.

**Solution:** A flexible band across the platen, held under spring tension and shiftable sideways along a rod, clamps the sheet and is raised automatically when the carbon sheets are lifted.

**How it works:** A textile tape band extends across the flat platen. One end carries a clip that engages the platen end, and the other end is wound on a drum on a sleeve that slides along a slotted rod, kept from rotating by a pin, with an evolute spring tensioning it. To place a sheet, the clip is drawn off and the band raised, the paper is placed and one edge clamped, and the tension device is slid along the rod to line up with the margin and the clip set, so the spring keeps the band taut. Other tension forms use a collar or helical spring. Automatic lifting means raise the band along with the carbon sheets.
