## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A syllabic keyboard (patent 886,148) of 192 keys needs a typewriter that prints one, two or three letters per key.

**Solution:** A printing cylinder moved axially and rotated by racks and gearing driven from a grid of crossing shafts under the keys, so each key sets the cylinder for one syllable.

**How it works:** The keyboard has 192 keys in twelve rows of sixteen, each key bearing three symbols. Twelve shafts run right-left and sixteen run front-rear beneath, at right angles to each other; a key engages the right pair. A rack attached to the printing cylinder is moved right or left by duplicated gearing under each key group. Separate mechanism controls rotation of the cylinder for printing one, two or three letters. Paper carriage and paper holder have a return, and the carriage is adjusted left. Eleven sheets of drawings.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md), [Shift by turning the element](../features/shift-rotary.md)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** printing cylinder; rack; gearing; crossing shafts; key; carriage; paper holder

**Numbers:** 192 keys; 12 rows of 16 keys; 3 symbols per key
