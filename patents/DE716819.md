**How it works (reading):** A control member on the pound or shilling counter wheel lifts the lock when reached, so the total can be pulled regardless of which key was pressed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a typewriter adding machine with a lock, the total could only be pulled if the matching arithmetic key was pressed, which fails for non-decimal currency.

**Solution:** A control member on the pound or shilling counter wheel lifts the lock when reached, so the total can be pulled regardless of which key was pressed.
