## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (writing calculating machine sheet displacement)

**How it works (reading):** The advancing cam is made in one or several parts so the one-way transmission can produce different strokes and advance the sheet by varying amounts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The automatic sheet advance can only move a form one line at a time, so forms with uneven line spacing need manual intervention.

**Solution:** The advancing cam is made in one or several parts so the one-way transmission can produce different strokes and advance the sheet by varying amounts.

**Features:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
