## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to tahulntingdevices for typewriting machines andfit consists in certain parts and combinations which will he particularly described herein and set forth in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating device for a Smith Premier-type machine, in which type bars are arranged in a circle about the printing point, are driven by key levers through shafts and links, and a carriage feed device moves the paper carriage.
