## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier ribbon reverse depended on the carriage and special ribbons, and needed an attachment that works at the end or at any chosen point.

**Solution:** A hinged wire trip arm that the ribbon itself lifts, via cross strips or taut ribbon, releases a spring-loaded switch bar independent of the carriage.

**How it works:** On a Caligraph the ribbon passes through a wire guide on the end of a hinged arm at each side. Cross strips on the ribbon raise that guide as they pass, or a taut ribbon end does so with a longer guide. The raised arm releases the ribbon switch bar from the hold of a sliding plate, and a coil spring throws the bar so the dog moves from one spool to the other. A longer marginal stop cocks the sliding plate during carriage shift. Strips can be placed between ink colors on a two-color ribbon.

**Features:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; cross strip; wire guide; hinged arm; switch bar; sliding plate; coil spring; ribbon dog; ratchet disk
