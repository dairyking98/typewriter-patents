## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a new carriage for typewriters with visible writing.

**How it works (reading):** The paper roller frame is carried on support arms and can be lifted freely from the switching device at any carriage position, so it can swing up at any moment for edge setting or erasing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A paper roller frame in visible-writing machines cannot be swung up past the switching position without extra locks and linkage, which is complex and wears the machine.

**Solution:** The paper roller frame is carried on support arms and can be lifted freely from the switching device at any carriage position, so it can swing up at any moment for edge setting or erasing.
