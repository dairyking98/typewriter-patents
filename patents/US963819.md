## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feeds for computing and printing machines need a simple way to wind a ribbon spool to spool and reverse its direction.

**Solution:** Two crown ratchet wheels on the spool shafts, one pawl per wheel on a longitudinally shifting rock shaft so only one pawl engages at a time, oscillated by a cut-away driving shaft and a spring.

**How it works:** Each spool is pinned on a shaft carrying a crown ratchet wheel with teeth on the lower side. A longitudinally movable rock shaft carries two pawl arms placed so that when one pawl engages its wheel the other is clear. A rotating shaft with a cut-away portion pushes a pawl arm one way and a spring returns it. Sliding the rock shaft by knobs between two notched positions (held by a wire spring) selects which spool is positively driven, the other being turned through the ribbon. Spool hubs have friction (pin, sleeve, rubber bushing, screw) to keep the ribbon taut; an automatic reversing device with levers is attached (Fig. 8, not described in opening text).
