## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Franklin typewriter keys radiate over a wide area because the typelevers are on a cylinder arc.

**Solution:** Key-lever and type-lever axes are angled to each other so the keys can sit close together in a square Remington-style keyboard.

**How it works:** Typelevers pivot in an arc around the impression point, each with a segmental gear at its lower end meshing with a gear on the rear of its key-lever. The key-lever pivot axis is angled from the type-lever axis, more so towards the keyboard edges, with the gear pitch line at the planes' intersection; a bent key-lever is the alternative. Springs on rods return the keys, and a segmental bar under them feeds the carriage after each impression. A new bearing for the keys gives easier movement.
