In contrast to known machines of the type indicated above, a simplification is achieved according to the invention aimed at the device and achieved in that the transfer printing unit prints on the same paper web on which the not transferable information are transferred, whereby the advantage is achieved that an image of the overall evaluation of the cards is obtained in one operation.
