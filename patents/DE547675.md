## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Tabulating machines that print totals from punched cards cannot also reproduce the handwritten or typed text on the cards, so a combined record needs separate passes.

**Solution:** A transfer-printing roller (or optical projection onto a photosensitive web) copies the card's written text onto the same paper web on which the tabulator's type units print the punched data.

**How it works:** Cards leave a stack one by one and pass under a dampening roller that moistens the text. A rubber transfer roller picks up the text from the card, then swings against the paper web and deposits it while the tabulator's printing units print the punched information on the same web, and wiping rollers clean the roller. In an alternative, a lens and strong light project the card image onto a light-sensitive section of the web, which is then developed and dried. Two further variants press the card directly against the web, by mirror-written carbon transfer or by contact exposure.
