## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such switching devices for the paper trolley of typewriters with a gearshift and a ratchet cooperating with the ratchet wheel, which by means of one in it in the longitudinal direction provided elongated hole on a pin of the swingable in the direction of the ratchet shaft, a carrier carrying ratchet is rotatably and displaceably arranged.

**How it works (reading):** The pawl's tooth effective area runs along an elongated hole in the carrier, its deflection is limited by a resilient stop, and it is supported against tooth pressure, so a weak spring suffices and the pawl lifts out of the ratchet path on return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A known switching device for the paper carriage needed a strong spring and gave noise when the pawl slid on the ratchet teeth during carriage return.

**Solution:** The pawl's tooth effective area runs along an elongated hole in the carrier, its deflection is limited by a resilient stop, and it is supported against tooth pressure, so a weak spring suffices and the pawl lifts out of the ratchet path on return.
