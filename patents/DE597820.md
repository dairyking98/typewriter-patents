## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a universal rail or universal bar arrangement on typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The universal bar frame is swung on rear arms mounted on stands detachable from the main frame, with return springs adjustable at the stands and an eccentric to set the swing frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Universal bar frame for key levers must be accurate, strong and light, and it is hard to move evenly from all type levers.

**Solution:** The universal bar frame is swung on rear arms mounted on stands detachable from the main frame, with return springs adjustable at the stands and an eccentric to set the swing frame.
