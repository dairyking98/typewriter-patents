## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A back-feed device for the platen rack needed fewer parts and easier engagement and release of the return claw.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded swinging lever carries a claw at its tip that is cammed into the rack teeth by the back-feed operating lever.

**How it works:** A lever pivoted at its lower end carries a claw at the tip, and a cam plate on it meets a roller on the end of an operating lever. Striking the back-feed knob together with the feed knob raises the roller, which swings the lever so the claw enters the rack teeth, while a link turns a plate whose engaging piece pushes the lever's top to move the rack in the return direction. On release a spring swings the lever back against a fixed stop, freeing the claw. The existing fixed and swinging feed pawls hand the rack over just as the claw engages.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. A typewriter back-feed device in which a swinging lever, loosely pivoted at its lower end and moving within a stop fixed to the frame, has at its tip a claw that engages between the teeth of the platen's rack; the swinging lever has a cam plate that engages the end of an operating lever; an engaging piece that works on the swinging lever is set above, and a turning plate, pivoted at its lower end to the frame, is joined at its middle to a link from the end of the operating lever; and the swinging lever is always pressed by a spring toward the operating lever and the stop. Its object is a device in which, by working a simple mechanism, the return claw at the tip of the swinging lever is easily engaged with and freed from the rack, making the back-feed operation, and the manufacture, simple and the device strong.
