## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops must be restored automatically after arresting the carriage, including when the carriage is moved back by hand (reverse tabulation).

**Solution:** A denominational tabulator whose column stops on a carriage-borne stop bar are arrested and the tabulator is automatically restored by that same engagement, in either carriage direction.

**How it works:** A column stop bar hangs from arms on the carriage behind the platen. Tabulator stops are projected into the path of the column stops, arrest the carriage, and the engagement itself restores the tabulator. A clutch member, moved rearward against a spring, releases the carriage from the escapement so it can run to the stop. Reverse tabulation arrests a carriage pushed right by hand. Shown on a machine with stationary typebar actions and a carriage carrying the platen.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** column stop bar; tabulator stop; clutch member; escapement wheel; pinion; carriage
