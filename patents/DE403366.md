## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (ribbon weaving device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Woven fabrics characterised by their shape

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a weaving device for ribbons either running yard goods or especially endless belts.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-mounted guide plate or guide roller under (or over) the weaving point keeps the edge threads and the finished tape in one plane, with an adjusting screw setting its pressure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Woven ribbons get uneven edges because edge warp threads are pulled up or down during shedding.

**Solution:** A spring-mounted guide plate or guide roller under (or over) the weaving point keeps the edge threads and the finished tape in one plane, with an adjusting screw setting its pressure.
