> Description Translated from German Weaving device for the production of ribbons, in particular endless Ribbons. The invention relates to a weaving device for ribbons either running yard goods or especially endless belts. The new thing is that below the weaving point a resiliently mounted and resilient guide piece or Guide roller is arranged, which over the weaving point, d. H. over the top of the shed angle and protrudes over the two side edges. By such a thing Guide piece, the advantage is achieved that the on the guide piece or the guide roller overlying warp threads, especially the edge warp threads, all in one plane and the edge chain threads pulled down during shedding the effort is taken to pull the weft thread down. The weft is also guided by the guide rail during the entry, so that by the weaving device according to the present invention tapes with perfectly smooth edges can be made. The guide rail or guide roller lies against the warp or the woven tape from below, if during the formation of the shed the edge warp threads are pulled down and the weft thread from this side is hammered into the compartment.
