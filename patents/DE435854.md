## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching a machine between power and manual drive needed a separate intermediate linkage and rail.

**Solution:** Switch modes by shifting one of the two drive-lever mechanisms (key levers with their bearing block, or the power lever carriage) so the key lever either engages the type lever directly or the power linkage.

**How it works:** In manual mode the key lever pin engages a slot in an intermediate lever that swings the type lever. For power mode the key levers are slid forward, disengaging them, so a key lever trips a pawl that locks a two-armed lever into the path of a constantly turning camshaft, whose cam swings the intermediate lever and type lever. At the end the pawl hits a stop and releases, and a spring returns the lever. A second embodiment shifts the power lever carriage instead.
