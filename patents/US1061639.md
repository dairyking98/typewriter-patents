## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A fixed carriage feed per stroke gives every character the same width, which suits narrow characters poorly.

**Solution:** A variable-feed escapement where an auxiliary universal bar with adjustable contacts under the key levers sets a short or long carriage feed according to the key.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 10 or 11, the carriage on ball bearings is pulled by a spring drum through a feed rack and pinion on a shaft. A hub on the pinion shaft carries an escapement wheel or rack. An auxiliary universal bar under the key levers has adjustable contact devices which control how far the escapement lets the carriage move. Narrow characters thus get a short feed, wide ones a longer feed.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; spring drum; feed rack; feed pinion; escapement wheel; auxiliary universal bar; adjustable contact; key lever; hub
