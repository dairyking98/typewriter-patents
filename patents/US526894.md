## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Worn grooved guide rolls misalign the carriage, ribbons need automatic movement, and type diverge as they strike the platen.

**Solution:** A bundle of Remington improvements: cylindrical carriage guide rolls on vertical and inclined rail faces, a resistance-free lateral platen shift, automatic ribbon movement and reversal, and a quick-disengaging spacing dog frame.

**How it works:** Two pairs of cylindrical rolls run against vertical and inclined sides of the front carriage guide rail, and the carriage weight wedges the inclined rolls down so the vertical rolls press firmly against the rail. An auxiliary platen carriage shifts laterally for case. The ribbon is moved laterally and longitudinally and reversed automatically until exhausted. The platen bottom is placed relative to the typebar pivots to cut type divergence. The spacing dogs are disengaged and re-engaged with the line spacing and carriage return, and small diameter anti-friction rolls guide the paper close to the line of print.
