## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of my invention is to provide means whereby a card-holder may be readily attached to and detached from the platen of a type-writing machine and also to provide such a construction as that the platen may be used for ordinary Work upon paper when the card-holder is removed, thus obviating the necessity of the employment of two dierent platens for the two kinds of work, which has been general heretofore.

**How it works (reading):** A card-holder with pins fits the platen perforations and a catch engages the platen shaft to resist accidental detachment.
