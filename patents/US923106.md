## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more especially to the carriage feeding mechanism of typewriting machines embodying an escapement wheel, and has for one of its objects to provide a back stop (that is, a stop for preventing a reverse or backward movement of the escapement wheel when the carriage is returned from left to right to begin a new line of writing) of the general character of that shown in the U.

**How it works (reading):** A dog carrier or rocker carries the escapement dogs, and a back stop prevents reverse movement of the escapement wheel during carriage return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A reverse-feed escapement needs a back stop so the escapement wheel cannot reverse when the carriage returns.

**Solution:** A dog carrier or rocker carries the escapement dogs, and a back stop prevents reverse movement of the escapement wheel during carriage return.
