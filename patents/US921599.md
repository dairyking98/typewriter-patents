## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A visible typewriter with two ribbons (two pairs of spools) needs a simpler way to switch the winding drive between the ribbons.

**Solution:** A single endwise-sliding switch bar across the machine, with cams at its ends, disengages one or both winding pawls from their ratchets.

**How it works:** Each of the two ribbon pairs is wound by its own ratchet wheel, advanced by a key-operated pawl and held by a detaining pawl, through gearing to the spool shafts; the shaft slides endwise to reverse travel. A switch bar runs horizontally across the machine, moved endwise by a ribbon shift key via a rock shaft and arm. Cam projections at its ends pull the front side of one driving pawl away from its ratchet, letting the other pawl drop in, or hold both away at once while both vibrators are silenced, with a detent to hold the position. The pawls are on arms fixed on a rock shaft vibrated by the universal bar.
