## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists have to feed single sheets often, and copies need carbon paper.

**Solution:** A frame hung on the platen spindle carries several paper rolls and several ink-ribbon strips led round the platen together, so copies are made without carbon and paper never needs reloading.

**How it works:** A pair of side frames pivot on the platen spindle and run on a track at the rear of the machine. They hold paper rolls in inclined notches and a movement frame of ribbon spools geared together to turn the same way, with a hand crank and pawl. The ribbons pass through a metal guide that folds them through 90 degrees and across the platen between the sheets. A sharp edge on the guide bar tears off the paper and short pins hold the written paper up.
