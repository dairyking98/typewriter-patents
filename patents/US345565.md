## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter keyboards need one key per character; the inventor wants one key to operate more than one letter.

**Solution:** Finger-pieces are turned on their bent stems by a movable plate so each presses either a lower-case or upper-case key beneath.

**How it works:** Finger-pieces with bent stems are held by an upper fixed plate and a lower movable plate pivoted by links, spring-held toward the lower-case position. Pressing a horn on the plate turns the stems so each finger piece sits above the upper-case key instead. Releasing it returns them. Modified forms shift rods or the keys themselves. Half as many finger-pieces as keys are needed.
