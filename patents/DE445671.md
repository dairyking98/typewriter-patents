**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to triggering devices for Paper feed rollers on typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** An oscillating shaft with a cam part acts between a fixed abutment and the roller carriers, which are slotted so they shift in parallel and lift together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feed-roller lift release must be simple, light and must lift the rollers evenly on travel machines.

**Solution:** An oscillating shaft with a cam part acts between a fixed abutment and the roller carriers, which are slotted so they shift in parallel and lift together.

The fact that the fixed abutment between the points of action acts on the roller carrier approximately in the middle of their length, it becomes possible both the oscillating shaft as well as the roller carriers without impairing the good effect very easy to carry out, which is particularly important for travel machines is.
