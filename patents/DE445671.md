## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feed-roller lift release must be simple, light and must lift the rollers evenly on travel machines.

**Solution:** An oscillating shaft with a cam part acts between a fixed abutment and the roller carriers, which are slotted so they shift in parallel and lift together.

> Description Translated from German Typewriter. The invention relates to triggering devices for Paper feed rollers on typewriters. Its purpose is the release device as simple as possible, cheap and designed so that it is lightweight and takes up little space without the risk of the roles unevenly be lifted off. : _ According to the invention, the known, for lifting the feed rolls serving oscillating shaft with a cam part, in particular a square, between a fixed abutment on the carriage and the roll holder carriers and arranged on their Ends supported by slot guides so that they rotate simultaneously a parallel shift in the sense of approaching or moving away from the fixed abutment can perform. The fact that the fixed abutment between the points of action acts on the roller carrier approximately in the middle of their length, it becomes possible both the oscillating shaft as well as the roller carriers without impairing the good effect very easy to carry out, which is particularly important for travel machines is.
