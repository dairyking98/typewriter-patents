## Summary

**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Template holder for office machines with a slide that can be switched line by line arranged, in a non-use position foldable line indicator and aims to this to train in such a way that it is horizontal against any template, whether thin or thick Position evenly and lightly.

**How it works (reading):** Line indicator guided on pins under compression springs presses lightly on any template, staying horizontal for thick or thin originals.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A hinged line indicator presses unevenly on templates of differing thickness, which hurts reading and sliding.

**Solution:** Line indicator guided on pins under compression springs presses lightly on any template, staying horizontal for thick or thin originals.
