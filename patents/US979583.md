## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feeds on typebar machines were complicated; the inventor wants a simpler ribbon feed, a better ribbon lift, and automatic return of the spacer bar.

**Solution:** A central universal bar, moved by any key or the space bar, drives both ribbon-spool ratchet pawls and the escapement lever, and a linked lever lifts the ribbon guide frame, with extra lift when the platen shifts.

**How it works:** Pressing any key lever or the space bar pulls a hooked arm that moves a central universal bar. The bar's outward arms rock two curved levers whose spring-held pawls turn horizontal ratchets and the vertical ribbon spools. Finger pieces move a shift frame (a rod that is also the carriage way-rod) that engages one pawl and disengages the other to reverse the ribbon direction; adjustable pins set how far the spools turn per stroke. The bar's rearward arms swing a pivoted lever carrying the escapement teeth that work against the escapement wheel. A second lever on this one pivots a ribbon vibrating lever that raises a guide frame to lift the ribbon to the print point. When the platen shift rod is turned for upper case, a finger raises the lever further so the ribbon sits correctly for the shifted platen.
