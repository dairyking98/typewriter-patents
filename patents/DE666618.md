## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (film gripper indexing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Claws or pins engaging holes in the film

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to gripper indexing devices for films with a perforation pitch that is a whole multiple of the image height.

**How it works (reading):** One gripper tip whose stroke is split into partial strokes by a crank and cam drive, so it engages and advances only during the advance period.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Film gripper indexing needs separate locking grippers and wears out from sliding on the film.

**Solution:** One gripper tip whose stroke is split into partial strokes by a crank and cam drive, so it engages and advances only during the advance period.
