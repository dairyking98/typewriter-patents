## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The type-bar projection needs a secure rigid joint to the link without solder, and tight pivots wear.

**Solution:** Tongues on the link are bent over and clamped to a projection that has two apertures, one for a link and one for a return spring, making a rigid union.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md)

**Parts:** two non-coincident axes 6 and 7
