## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment; Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on typewriters to adjust the platen in the direction of the stroke of the types.

**How it works (reading):** Running bars on the frame guide the platen carriage over its full length, and actuators on the front of the machine move it forward and back with an eccentric guide.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen carriage guide is short and misaligns when set forward and back, letting the platen tilt under heavy use.

**Solution:** Running bars on the frame guide the platen carriage over its full length, and actuators on the front of the machine move it forward and back with an eccentric guide.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
