## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike typebars should move to the printing point with a better motion curve.

**Solution:** Type action in which the typebars are thrown to the printing point with a motion whose first part is certain (positive), applied to front-strike machines.

**How it works:** Typebars alternate in a basket segment with sub-levers pivoted on a wire in slots; links join sub-levers to the bars. Main frame has side plates with cast corner posts, a top plate and a rolling carriage on grooved rails with balls carrying a roller platen. The remaining description is not shown.
