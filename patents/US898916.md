## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing runs too close to the bottom edge of the sheet, and preserving the bottom margin and feeding back for corrections is troublesome.

**Solution:** A backing sheet with rows of printed line-number marks along its side edges, so the operator can see at a glance how many lines remain before the bottom of the page.
