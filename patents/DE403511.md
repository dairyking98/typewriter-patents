**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** A group of stop members is moved by one selectable lever in set sequence, so one hand action sets several carriage stops together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Setting line-start, bell, margin and tab stops needs separate adjustments for each function.

**Solution:** A group of stop members is moved by one selectable lever in set sequence, so one hand action sets several carriage stops together.
