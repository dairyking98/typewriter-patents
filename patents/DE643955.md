**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method by which these deficiencies are eliminated and above all, the production of a key from plastic mass with inlaid Character body in a matrix allows in which the plastic mass is brought into the shape of a button by pressing or injection.

**How it works (reading):** A character body is held in a die and the plastic key mass is pressed or injected around it, so the inlay is fixed in the molded key.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key character inserts are thin foils that peel, or fills that do not hold, and inlaid bodies distort under heat.

**Solution:** A character body is held in a die and the plastic key mass is pressed or injected around it, so the inlay is fixed in the molded key.
