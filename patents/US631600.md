## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in paper-roll attachments for type-writing machines, and has for its objects to provide a means for supplying the paper to the machine in a continuous roll and to thus avoid the time and skill required in adjusting single sheets of paper successively.

**How it works (reading):** A paper roll is supplied continuously to the platen so lines stay parallel with the sheet edge without re-adjusting each sheet.
