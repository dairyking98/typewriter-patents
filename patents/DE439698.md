## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In accounting typewriters the upper pressure rollers and the pressure rail are coupled, so the rollers cannot be lifted alone.

**Solution:** The pressure rail is carried on the paper carriage independently and driven directly by the hand lever, so the upper rollers can be raised and lowered separately.
