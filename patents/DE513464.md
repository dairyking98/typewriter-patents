## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention consists in a particularly simple one Device for stripping the sheets of paper from the pins, namely a vibrating one Wiper rail which, when swinging out, is attached to the suspension pins of the swing frame stripped off the hanging sheets from the pens and at the same time against the platen presses.

**How it works (reading):** A swinging wiper rail on a square rod strips the hanging sheets off the pins and presses them against the platen when it swings out, with a rocker that releases the pressure roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Perforated-edge forms had to be stripped from alignment pins by hand, which was clumsy.

**Solution:** A swinging wiper rail on a square rod strips the hanging sheets off the pins and presses them against the platen when it swings out, with a rocker that releases the pressure roller.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** square rod; alignment pins; wiper rail; pressure rod 16; hook 9
