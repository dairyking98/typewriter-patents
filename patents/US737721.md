## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and has special reference to an improved card or sheet platen designed to facilitate the handling of card or sheet work.

**How it works (reading):** A flat platen fitted with holding guides that adjust automatically to card thickness and size so cards print accurately.
