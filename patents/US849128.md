## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type mechanism built for a flat platen over a book cannot be used with a movable paper carriage that carries line-feed rollers.

**Solution:** The type mechanism is held stationary on a hinged bed bar, the carriage travels under it on track rails, and the carriage's feeding devices shift the paper for line spacing.

**How it works:** The type mechanism is attached to a hinge-bar in the bed so it can be turned up; a roll on the carriage rests on a track rail under the type mechanism while the carriage carries the paper past it.

**Features:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** type mechanism; hinge-bar; track rails; carriage; feed rollers; rolls
