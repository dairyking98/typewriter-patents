## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Machines therefor

**How it works (reading):** The inventor changes the definition of the cutting tool (the saw blade) to keep the slot on course; the OCR is cut off before the exact method is stated.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Slots cut into typewriter type-lever segments with a saw blade wander, so the slot direction and lamella shape come out inaccurate.

**Solution:** The inventor changes the definition of the cutting tool (the saw blade) to keep the slot on course; the OCR is cut off before the exact method is stated.
