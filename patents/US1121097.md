## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a visible front-strike typebar machine the ribbon must lie at the print point yet drop clear immediately so the writing shows, use its whole width and two colours, run in either direction, and still fold away with the carriage.

**Solution:** A key-driven rocking frame vibrates a ribbon arm through a bell crank whose throw is set by an adjustable cam slide, and ratchets two worm-driven spools, all mounted to fold with the carriage and disengage when folded.

**How it works:** Each key lever lifts a universal bar that rocks a frame. The frame bar strikes a bell-crank lever through an adjustable U-shaped slide with a cam face, which lifts a spring-returned vibratory arm carrying the ribbon to the print point and lets it fall below it afterwards. Sliding the cam changes the lift so that different colour stripes, or different parts of the ribbon width, meet the type. On the return stroke, pawls on the frame arms step ratchet wheels that turn worms and worm gears on the ribbon spools. Only the spool whose thumb nut is tightened is driven, so the ribbon runs in whichever direction is chosen. Folding the carriage lifts the ratchets and bell crank away from the frame.
