## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by applications of scales or indicators

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writer scales of this description, and has for its main object to so construct the ordinary machine or carriage scale that it may be readily employed for tabular work, as well as for its ordinary purpose of denoting the progress and position of the carriage during the work of type-writing.

**How it works (reading):** A knurled, roughened scale surface can be marked with pencil at chosen positions to guide tabular work.
