**How it works (reading):** A slip clutch between the carriage tension spring and the ribbon switching device releases automatically when the resistance in the ribbon circuit exceeds the carriage drive force, so the blockage is freed and the pull cord cannot fall off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the automatic ribbon switching is blocked, the carriage stops and the operator pushes it back and forth, and the carriage pull cord then slips off the barrel and catches in the drive, requiring disassembly to repair.

**Solution:** A slip clutch between the carriage tension spring and the ribbon switching device releases automatically when the resistance in the ribbon circuit exceeds the carriage drive force, so the blockage is freed and the pull cord cannot fall off.
