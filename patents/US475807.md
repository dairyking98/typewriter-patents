## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriting attachment for matrix machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to certain improvements in the matrix-making machines shown in my applications, Serial No.

**How it works (reading):** A second type-writer wheel with fonts on a matrix machine's shaft prints onto a paper carrier linked to the matrix.
