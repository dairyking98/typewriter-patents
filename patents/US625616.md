## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters with type wheels need simple paper carrying and feeding and a carriage that lets the last written line be seen.

**Solution:** A two-part carriage whose rear paper-feed part hinges up on a rod, carrying a feed roller, a smaller pressure roller and a supply roller that winds the paper.

**How it works:** Part A slides on the frame with the ratchet bar; part B is hinged to it by a rod through perforated lugs and can be raised to read the last line. Part B holds a rubber feed roller with knob, a smaller spring-loaded roller in elongated slots, and a larger wooden supply roller that winds the paper through sheet-metal guide and spring fingers or rubber bands. A ratchet wheel on the feed roller journal is turned by an L-shaped lever with a spring-loaded dog for line spacing.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** carriage part; hinged part; hinge rod; feed roller; knob; pressure roller; supply roller; guide; spring finger; rubber band; ratchet wheel; lever; dog; spring
