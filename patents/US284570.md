## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Making several fac-simile copies of a manuscript in one pass.

**Solution:** Two or more circles of typebars are connected to one set of keys and one carriage so one stroke prints several sheets.

**How it works:** Frame carries three circles of type-carrying levers laid out as in the Remington. Corresponding levers of the different circles are connected through rods to a single bar, and each bar is connected to a key midway between the outer levers so that the bar ends are pulled evenly. Sleeves around the connecting rods let the key springs hold the bars up. A carriage carries three sets of feed and printing rollers, with long common shafts or continuous rollers. Ribbon rollers at each end of the frame are shifted together by a rod. With an even number of circles the keys sit between the two central circles.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** circle of type levers; bar; rod; sleeve; key; carriage; feed roller; printing roller; ribbon roller; connecting rod

**Numbers:** 3 circles of levers; 2 sets in modified form

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter: one key set prints several copies at once
