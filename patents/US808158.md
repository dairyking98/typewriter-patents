## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A multi-stripe ribbon (e.g. red/black, record/copy inks) needs a simple way to confine the type contact to one stripe while still reciprocating the ribbon transversely to avoid uneven wear.

**Solution:** A bell-crank lever with an adjustable two-part arm, held by a spring-pressed notched dog, lets a front shifting bar change the ribbon holder's transverse offset so the type hits a chosen stripe while the carriage-driven reciprocation continues.

**How it works:** The carriage spring drum drives a ratchet and gear train on a stud, and a crank arm on the gear shaft oscillates a link. The link moves a bell-crank lever whose upper end moves the ribbon-spool holder sideways on its shaft, so the ribbon wanders across the type. One arm of the bell-crank is pivoted on the other and is held in one of several notches by a spring-pressed dog. Pushing or pulling a shifting bar at the front of the machine flips the pivoted arm to another notch, changing the mean position of the holder so the type strike only the right-hand or left-hand stripe. More notches allow more stripes.
