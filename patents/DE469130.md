## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**How it works (reading):** The paper support plate detaches at spring-loaded pins and folds backward in slots, exposing the tabs to the front, while the slide paper plates keep the platen free.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The writer must leave the seat to set tabulator or column stops because the paper support plate blocks access to the tabs.

**Solution:** The paper support plate detaches at spring-loaded pins and folds backward in slots, exposing the tabs to the front, while the slide paper plates keep the platen free.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
