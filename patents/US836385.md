## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key tops must stay at a uniform height and must not rotate on their key-levers.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal cup has a flattened tubular shank that wedges onto a matching flattened end of the key-lever, centring the key and preventing rotation.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
