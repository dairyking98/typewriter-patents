## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists cannot easily choose between two ink colours or a copying ribbon.

**Solution:** Two ribbons, two vibrators and one winder connectable to either pair of spools, with silencing for stencils.

**How it works:** A front ribbon and rear ribbon of different colours each have a pair of spools and a vibrator. A winding mechanism couples to either pair, and the matching vibrator is called into action so the active ribbon covers the print point. Both mechanisms can be silenced for stencil cutting. Shown on an Underwood.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** front ribbon; rear ribbon; spool; vibrator; winding mechanism; cup

**Improves on:**

- Underwood front strike: adds second ribbon and selectable winder
