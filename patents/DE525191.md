## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink ribbons having arrangements to prevent undesired contact between the impression-transfer material and machine parts or other articles

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improvement of the ribbon protected by patent 5 i 7 076, in which the stop side for the types is covered with a protective layer.

**How it works (reading):** The protective layer of rubber, gutta-percha or balata is permanently bonded to the ribbon fabric, so one ribbon of normal length fits the spools and the two wear evenly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A loose protective strip on a typewriter ribbon takes up extra space on the spools, so the ribbon must be cut to half length, and the strip can wear at a different rate from the ribbon.

**Solution:** The protective layer of rubber, gutta-percha or balata is permanently bonded to the ribbon fabric, so one ribbon of normal length fits the spools and the two wear evenly.
