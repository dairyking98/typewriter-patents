## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary keyboards are slow and cannot write standard form letters automatically.

**Solution:** An electromagnet-driven typebar typewriter with finger cups whose direction of movement closes different contacts, plus an auxiliary contact keyboard for form-letter writing.

**How it works:** Each key-bar has an armature over an electromagnet in its own branch circuit. Ten adjustable insulating cups, five per hand, hold the fingers; moving the finger in different directions closes different spring contacts, selecting the letter. A shift bar with contacts allows two characters per bar. A second keyboard with sliding circuit-closing keys and adjustable shifting contacts wires up fixed form-letter passages.

**Numbers:** ten finger cups, five per hand
