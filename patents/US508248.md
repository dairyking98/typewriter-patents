## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** A matrix machine with a revolving cylinder that carries rows of radially movable dies, a matrix carrier moved in fixed relation to the cylinder, and a finger key that selects the die to impress.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier matrix machines needed safeguards and a type-writer attachment that works with the mechanism.

**Solution:** A matrix machine with a revolving cylinder that carries rows of radially movable dies, a matrix carrier moved in fixed relation to the cylinder, and a finger key that selects the die to impress.
