## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting inachines and more particularly to the carriages of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage can be released from the main frame by locking means and removed, and the platen swings back to an inoperative position to expose parts below it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage should detach from the machine and be swapped, and the platen should swing back for access.

**Solution:** The carriage can be released from the main frame by locking means and removed, and the platen swings back to an inoperative position to expose parts below it.

**Features:** [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; locking means; feed pinion; finger wheel
