## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention has reference to a type-writer desk, and embodies the novel details and features of construction hereinafter shown and described, and illustrated in the accompany.

**How it works (reading):** A desk with a rectangular opening in its top holds a hinged swinging frame that carries the machine on a slide; a counterweighted rope raises the frame through the opening, and a catch holds the slide, which can be lifted out to move the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typewriter had to sit on the desk top, taking up space and needing to be lifted out for storage or use elsewhere.

**Solution:** A desk with a rectangular opening in its top holds a hinged swinging frame that carries the machine on a slide; a counterweighted rope raises the frame through the opening, and a catch holds the slide, which can be lifted out to move the machine.
