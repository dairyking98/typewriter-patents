## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates totypewriting machines, and the object thereof is to provide animproved machine of this class having a consists of a main back art and a supplemental front part'adapted to'be foldedbackwardly over .

**How it works (reading):** The machine has a main back frame and a supplemental front part hinged to fold backward over it, with the key-lever carrying parts pivoted in the front member, so the machine folds to a rectangular shape for a small case.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machines were bulky to pack, ship and carry, and key-action parts were not foldable.

**Solution:** The machine has a main back frame and a supplemental front part hinged to fold backward over it, with the key-lever carrying parts pivoted in the front member, so the machine folds to a rectangular shape for a small case.

**Features:** [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
