## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines needed improved key system, escapement, line stop, alarm, paper roll and ribbon shifting.

**Solution:** Keys on rock-shafts with pitmen to hinged type levers, escapement from the rock-shafts, adjustable alarm, upward-turning platen for inspection and ribbon shifter.

**How it works:** Each key connects to a rock-shaft with a torsion spring return; a crank on it and a turnbuckle pitman pull a type lever hooked in a bracket on the type ring. The type lever strikes through the ribbon. The rock-shafts also drive the carriage escapement and a line-end stop that locks the keys. An adjustable alarm indicates line end and the paper roll turns up to show the writing.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** key; rock-shaft; torsion spring; pitman; turnbuckle; type lever; type ring; cushion ring; alarm; ribbon shifter; platen roll

**Numbers:** 6 sheets of drawings
