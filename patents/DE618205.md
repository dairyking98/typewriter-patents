## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Unlocking the clear-character scanning element on the counters needs a separate linkage that is cumbersome when the element sits on the vertical counters.

**Solution:** The scanning element is unlocked by a clear-character key pressure acting through its control device, so no unlocking linkage is needed wherever the element is mounted.
