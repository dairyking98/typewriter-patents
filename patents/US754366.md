## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing typebar machines are complicated, bulky and hard to assemble.

**Solution:** Use a three-armed key lever that drives the typebar, the universal bar and the escapement directly, with the whole action built in a separate frame that drops into the case.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot on a wire in a slotted segmental block set at an incline in a detachable action frame, striking the platen at about 45 degrees to the horizontal. Each key lever pivots on a wire and has three radial arms: the forward arm takes the key, the upper arm has a slot embracing a rivet on the typebar, and the short rear arm lifts the universal bar. The universal bar is the front of a pivoted bail carrying the fixed and movable escapement dogs, which alternately engage the escape wheels as the bar rises and falls. Springs anchored to a wire return the typebars, with the ear angled so tension rises as the type nears the printing point. The frame is adjusted outside and then studded into the case.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** typebar; segmental block; pivot wire; three-arm key lever; universal bar; bail; fixed dog; movable dog; escape wheel; return spring; action frame; case; guide plate

**Numbers:** 45 degrees to horizontal at printing point
