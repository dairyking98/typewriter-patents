## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A front-stroke visible typebar action needs a light touch and rapid operation without shock to the key lever.

**Solution:** A spring-coupled intermediate lever starts the typebar by sliding inclined lugs and then snaps it to the platen through a spring, while a universal bar driven by the intermediate levers runs escapement and ribbon.

**How it works:** The key lever is joined by a spring to a rear arm of an intermediate lever, whose vertical arm is joined by another spring to a lug on the typebar. Depressing the key tilts the intermediate lever so its inclined lug slides against an inclined lug on the typebar and lifts the bar. Continued pressure lets the spring bring the typebar quickly to the platen without shock on the key. Each intermediate lever lifts a universal bar that rocks a shaft, whose pawls advance the escape wheel and pinion on the carriage rack and move the ribbon vibrator. A spring on the rocker arm returns the universal bar and the intermediate levers.
