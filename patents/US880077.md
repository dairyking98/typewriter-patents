## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shift key uses a hand, so the operator cannot keep both hands writing.

**Solution:** Foot pedal attachment operating the shift key.

**How it works:** A pedal lever below the machine and an upper hinged lever, joined by a connecting rod, press the shift key. A bumper pad and hook locate it. Fits existing machines.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** pedal lever; upper lever; connecting rod; shift key; bumper pad
