## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a paper feeding device for typewriters, and the object of the invention is to provide a device of this class which can be readily attached to a typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** A feed device attaches to the carriage and holds a stack of sheets, advancing them against the platen by a ratchet wheel on the platen shaft driven through an arm, with a feed roller holding the paper against the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets of paper must be held and advanced automatically through a typewriter that is not built with a feed for them.

**Solution:** A feed device attaches to the carriage and holds a stack of sheets, advancing them against the platen by a ratchet wheel on the platen shaft driven through an arm, with a feed roller holding the paper against the platen.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
