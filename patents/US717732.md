## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the power-driven Blickensderfer machine (US656085) every key tried to drive its sector the full distance, so the safety spring slipped on most characters; the stop-pawl recoil unlocked the tumblers; the type-wheel stop and locking were doubled up; and a plain punching blow was harsh.

**Solution:** Give each key's tumbler gear teeth of a different pitch diameter so the sector travel matches the wheel rotation each character needs, and add a single lock, a momentum-driven tapping blow, a double-length spacer and power carriage return.

**How it works:** A constantly running motor shaft drives a face clutch; a key press releases the clutch for exactly one revolution of a cam driver, which pushes a slide-bar through a slip spring and rocks an actuating bar. The pressed key throws a bell-crank locking lever onto that bar, so the bar pulls that key's segment lever, which turns a graduated tumbler, a sleeve and one of two sectors that mesh with a pinion on the type-wheel rod. The tumbler's graduated face lifts a bail that drops a stop-pawl into a stopping-ratchet, halting the wheel at the chosen character, then both sectors swing forward and the wheel strikes the platen by momentum, a tapping blow. A positioning wheel hits a stud to centre the type, and one arm-and-projection lock on the action shaft holds the whole type mechanism after return against a cushioned stop. The three-row type wheel (lower case, capitals, figures) is shifted lengthwise on its rod by a bent lever.

**Parts:** motor shaft; face clutch; cam driver; slide-bar; slip spring; actuating bar; locking bell-crank; segment lever; graduated tumbler; sleeve; sector; pinion; stopping-ratchet; stop-pawl; locking bail; positioning wheel; centring stud; action shaft; return spring; cushioned stop; type wheel; bent shift lever; space-plate; feeding pawl; stop pawl; friction-wheel carriage drive; margin stop; clutch lock bar

**Numbers:** three circumferential rows (lower case, capitals, figures); 14 tumblers on each side of the machine; tumbler slide not more than 1/16 in.; spacer plus key stroke gives twice the normal carriage space; carriage return faster than forward drive (different friction-wheel diameters)

**Improves on:**

- US656085 (Blickensderfer power-driven machine): tumblers of different pitch diameter, locking projections against bail recoil, a single lock on the action shaft instead of two, and keys acting directly on the clutch controller
