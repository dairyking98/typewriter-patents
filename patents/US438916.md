## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventors want a durable, reliable and cheap typewriter, improving their earlier 1889 application (Serial 299,425) in which type slide in directing tubes.

**Solution:** Each character is a type-tipped slotted sleeve riding on a fixed curved directing rod and driven up by a key-operated elastic spiral plunger.

**How it works:** A key pivots a lever bar whose far end lifts a connecting rod, which pushes an elastic close-wound piano-wire plunger up along a fixed curved directing rod. The plunger carries a slotted sleeve tipped with a type, which passes through the upper shelf of the frame. A tongue pivoted to the top of the rod sits in the sleeve's slot, preventing the sleeve from turning so the type keeps its angle. The sleeves are angled so all types strike the ink ribbon at one point against paper on a feed roller; a funnel corrects small misdirection. Releasing the key returns everything by the spring.
