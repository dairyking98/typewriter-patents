## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Emphasising words in a different colour needed ribbon changes or extra labour.

**Solution:** A short auxiliary ribbon with a paper protector and clip ends laid across the type basket over the main ribbon.

**How it works:** A length of red ribbon with a paper strip on one side and leather end clips is laid over the main ribbon so the strip lies between. Weight keeps it in place yet allows slight movement.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** auxiliary ribbon; protecting strip; end clip; fastener
