## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typcwriting machines and more particularly to paper feeding mechanism therefor,-the object ofthe invention to provide simple and eiiicicnt means especially adaptable for use with a stenographic writing machine, for accurately moving the paper step-bystep for line feeding during successive manipulations of the key levers.

**How it works (reading):** Key levers move a slotted comb of type bars, and a platen bar on links with feeding devices moves the paper step by step during key strokes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Step-by-step line feed on a stenographic machine needs accurate paper movement.

**Solution:** Key levers move a slotted comb of type bars, and a platen bar on links with feeding devices moves the paper step by step during key strokes.
