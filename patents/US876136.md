## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops must be adjustable, and the carriage must be able to pass beyond a stop when needed.

**Solution:** Adjustable right and left margin stops, with a release key that disengages the carriage mechanism, let the carriage pass the stop; one stop rings a bell and a spring cushions the carriage.
