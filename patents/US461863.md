## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage line-stop and margin-stop devices are complicated.

**Solution:** A dog slides on the carriage guide rail and swings into a notch of a toothed bar on the frame to arrest the carriage at any chosen letter space.

**How it works:** Toothed bars at each end of the machine have teeth spaced like the letter-spacing teeth and marked with the scale numbers. A sleeve slides along the guide rail carrying a dog; rotating the sleeve lifts the dog out of the teeth, it is slid to the desired notch and turned back in. The carriage yoke then strikes the sleeve, stopping the carriage at the left or right margin.
