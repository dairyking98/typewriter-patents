## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Strips, tongues, or the like, for attaching to the trousers; Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper; Crossed belt accommodating

**How it works (reading):** Two carbon holders are stacked over each other on the paper carriage, and either can be used or coupled.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copy-paper carrier cannot write two forms of different width without swapping paper.

**Solution:** Two carbon holders are stacked over each other on the paper carriage, and either can be used or coupled.
