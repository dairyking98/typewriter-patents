## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Same ring-typebar tripping action, with sub-levers between key and typebar.

**Solution:** Key-levers carry spring-held trips that push sub-levers, which pull connecting rods to the typebars and the universal bar, then slip free.

**How it works:** Sub-levers on a fulcrum rod link by inclined connecting rods to the typebars. Above them, key-levers carry pivoted trips with a horizontal contact arm and spring, resting against a pin. Pressing a key pushes the trip down on the sub-lever, which swings the typebar and also depresses the universal bar to rock the spacing shaft. The trip then slides off, letting the typebar and spacing parts return while the key stays down.
