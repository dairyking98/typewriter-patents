## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil sheets are too stiff to wrap a cylindrical platen, so a flat platen is needed.

**Solution:** A flat platen carries the work sheet, and type with needle-point faces cut the stencil through the flat platen.
