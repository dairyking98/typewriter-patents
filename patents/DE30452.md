## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text excerpt states no problem; it describes a pedal-driven machine where a revolving set of toothed type wheels prints on key command.

**Solution:** Small type wheels ride between two continuously rotating discs; each tooth of a wheel carries one type, and a key lever lets the chosen tooth reach a thin elastic wall where it prints on the paper.

**How it works:** A vertical shaft turned by a pedal carries two discs that rotate inside a cylindrical housing. Between the discs sit horizontal wheels, each with one tooth per key and a toothless fifth of its rim, held still by a spring-loaded pin in a hole. Where the housing carries an inked leather or rubber cushion, the toothed part of a freed wheel rubs on it and spins, while the toothless part leaves the wheel at rest. A key lever with a wedge tooth selects the wheel position so the desired tooth emerges through a vertical slot in the cushion and prints on paper held against it. Each print pushes a spring-mounted head that advances the paper slide by one letter space through a rack.
