## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A type-wheel with continuous orbital motion must be started by a key and then stopped at a determinate point in its orbit, which needs a reliable start and stop.

**Solution:** A latch or gear-starter, pushed in and set, is released by a slight key depression, starting the wheel's rotation about its axis and stopping it at the determined point in its orbital path.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md)
