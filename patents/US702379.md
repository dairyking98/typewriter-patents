## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen carrier must shift for case and tilt up for inspection while staying parallel to the carriage travel.

**Solution:** The platen carrier rides on rolling supports on a shaft tied to the carriage by oppositely wound flexible straps so both ends move equally.

**How it works:** Rearward arms of the platen carrier journal a shaft carrying wheels (or segments) that roll on the carriage top. Two steel straps per wheel, fixed at one end to the wheel and at the other to the carriage, wind on and off oppositely so the wheel cannot slip. Because both wheels sit on one shaft, both ends of the platen frame move together and the platen stays parallel. Adjustable stops limit travel; a bracket prevents the shaft rising. Variants put the shaft in the carriage bearings.
