## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage stops automatically at the normal limit of its travel, so a few extra characters cannot be added at the end of a line.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded locking latch on the carriage engages a locking rod connected to a universal bar, holding the carriage near its limit; a thumb-piece lets the operator release it to move the carriage farther.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** locking latch; stop; spring; trip; universal bar; locking-rod; thumb-piece

**Numbers:** Patent 649,921; Serial No. 726,854
