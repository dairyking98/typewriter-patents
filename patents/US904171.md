## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to back spacing mechanism therefor.

**How it works (reading):** Attachments applied to the existing machine's escapement provide a back feed of the carriage one or more letter-space distances per operation, without changing the machine's structure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing typewriters cannot be moved back step by step, one or more letter spaces at a time, in the reverse direction to normal carriage travel.

**Solution:** Attachments applied to the existing machine's escapement provide a back feed of the carriage one or more letter-space distances per operation, without changing the machine's structure.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
