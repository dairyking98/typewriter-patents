## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in: carriage retarding devices for typewriting machines, and consists of the features of construction and combinations and arrangements of parts which are'heremafter described and specified 1n the claims.

**How it works (reading):** Carriage retarding mechanism in a case mounted to the top plate near the escapement pinion, with a spring in one form.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage retarding is needed for the escapement pinion so the carriage is not overthrown.

**Solution:** Carriage retarding mechanism in a case mounted to the top plate near the escapement pinion, with a spring in one form.
