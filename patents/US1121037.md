## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage work table (paper table) with its feed roll should be simple, cheap, and able to move the feed roll away from the platen to free the paper.

**Solution:** A work table stamped from a single piece of sheet metal rocks directly on the carriage base plate, carries the feed roll, is spring-pressed toward the platen, and is pushed away by a cam rod.

**How it works:** The work table is one sheet-metal piece with integral rearward arms resting on the carriage base plate and held so it can rock there. Springs seated in adjustable screws in the base plate push the table's front, with its feed roll, up against the platen. A rod running the length of the carriage, with cut-away cam portions and a finger handle, sits under the rear ends of the arms; rocking the rod lifts the arms, tilting the table and carrying the feed roll away from the platen until the rod is returned. The roll is loosely journaled in depressed bearings in the table and can be lifted out from above. Line spacing is stated to be as in a separate Hamen application.
