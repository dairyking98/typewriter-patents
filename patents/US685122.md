## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of the invention is to provide simple and efficient connections between the type-bars and the keys in machines in which the type-bars are shifted from lowercase to upper-case position.

**How it works (reading):** Simple connections between the keys and the type-bars, with key-bearing bell-levers fanned or diverging, and a case-shift that moves the type-bar system to upper-case position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-touch was jarring to the finger at the start and end of the stroke, type-bars were slow to leave the printing point and so could collide, and the connections between keys and case-shifted type-bars needed improvement.

**Solution:** Simple connections between the keys and the type-bars, with key-bearing bell-levers fanned or diverging, and a case-shift that moves the type-bar system to upper-case position.

**How it works:** Keys act through bell-levers and connecting rods on the type-bar segment; the whole type-bar system is shifted to upper-case position for capitals.
