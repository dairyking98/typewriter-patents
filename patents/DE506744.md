## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** When a pivoting carriage rack is released, its teeth stay engaged with the drive wheel, so on re-engaging the teeth do not mesh reliably and make noise.

**Solution:** When the rack is swung out, the ratchet wheel moves axially with the carriage drive wheel out of reach of the loose pawl, so the assembly spins freely.

**How it works:** The rack sits on a lever with a key lever and stays meshed with the carriage drive wheel while swung aside. In the normal position it bears on a collar of the drive wheel and a bead on the fixed ratchet wheel. Swinging the rack out shifts the drive wheel and ratchet wheel together along their axis, out of the loose pawl's range, so the carriage can move freely. Releasing the key lever lets a tension spring return everything.
