## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash register printing mechanisms are complex and costly.

**Solution:** One cam disc on the register transmission swings two angle levers that drive the print hammer and the feeds of control strip and ribbon.

**How it works:** Type wheels set by the register drive, mounted on shafts and tubes, print on a control strip and a check slip. A cam disc acts on a roller of an angle lever which drives, via a fork lever and rod, the print hammer and a pawl advancing the take-up ratchet by one tooth. A cliche roller inked by an inking roller prints the check, then knives cut it off. A button shifts a shaft so the check issue is skipped. The text is cut off in the later part.
