## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Clockwork for a coin-controlled lock runs while the keys are locked, forcing frequent rewinding.

**Solution:** Clockwork that runs only while the keys are unlocked, started and stopped without shock inside a locked casing.
