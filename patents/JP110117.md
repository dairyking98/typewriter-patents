## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In existing electric Japanese typewriters the key couples the drive directly to the printer by a friction clutch, so key effort is not reduced, releasing the key early cuts the print short, and holding it prints repeatedly.

**Solution:** The key only releases a latch; a spring-loaded sliding clutch then engages the running shaft for exactly one revolution and its cam does the printing or feed by power alone.

**How it works:** A motor-driven shaft carries fixed clutch teeth. Facing them, a loose slide with matching teeth is sprung toward engagement and carries a starting cam and a catch. A control piece, guided by a pocket-cam groove and a slope on the slide, latches the catch and holds the teeth apart. Pressing a key swings the control piece and frees the catch, so the slide engages and turns once, its cam moving a starting lever that works the known printing lever. Near the end of the turn the slope pushes the slide back and the control piece relatches it, however long the key is held. Three such units serve printing, lengthwise feed and crosswise feed on a hand-type tray machine.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Electric typewriter. Summary of the nature and purpose of the invention. This invention relates to an electric typewriter characterised in that sliding clutch teeth carrying starting cams for the printing mechanism and so on are set facing, and sprung toward, the fixed clutch teeth of a driving shaft; the sliding clutch teeth are normally held latched in a set position, apart from the fixed teeth, by a control piece that latches them automatically at every turn; and when the latch of the control piece is released by the pressing of a key, they at once engage the fixed teeth and are turned through one revolution, and by the starting cam they carry perform one operation of printing, feed or the like. Its purpose is an electric typewriter in which only the release of the control piece's latch on the sliding clutch teeth is done by hand, the printing, feeding and other work being done by power alone with no hand force, and in which one complete, correct printing operation is done automatically and lightly whether the key is held down long or briefly.
