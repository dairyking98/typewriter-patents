## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon shifting to use different parts of the ribbon needed a practical adjustable device.

**Solution:** A two-part adjustable feed guide shifts the ribbon so either the central portion or an edge is used for printing.

**How it works:** Slotted plates and arms position the ribbon guide so the ribbon shifts across the print point.
