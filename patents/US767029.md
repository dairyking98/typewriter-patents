## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writers, and particularly to the mechanism for feeding the carriage and platen from one side to the other.

**How it works (reading):** Usual keys and spacer-bar feed the carriage one way; a key-controlled independent mechanism moves it the opposite way step by step.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage cannot easily be moved in the reverse direction by the usual keys.

**Solution:** Usual keys and spacer-bar feed the carriage one way; a key-controlled independent mechanism moves it the opposite way step by step.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
