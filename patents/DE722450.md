## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Item counters for counters are complicated and require special locking.

**Solution:** An unlocking lever on the lowest point extends beyond the counter surface and drives the item counter directly, without extra links.

**How it works:** Extended unlocking lever drives the item counter directly, with no added links.
