## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil cutting needs the types to strike without a ribbon, but the Underwood ribbon vibrator always rose at each stroke.

**Solution:** A sliding wrist that can be pulled out of the actuator slot, silencing the ribbon vibrator at will.

**How it works:** The vibrator lever has a short arm carrying a wrist that normally sits in a vertical slot of an actuator on the universal-bar frame. The wrist is a shaft sliding in a long bearing; its far end has a head in a cross-fork on a slide with a finger-piece at the side of the machine. Pulling the finger-piece out withdraws the wrist from the slot, so the lever and ribbon stay down. The cross-fork lies parallel to the actuator motion, so the head moves idly without hindering the lever.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier; lever; wrist; actuator; slide; finger-piece; universal bar frame

**Improves on:**

- Underwood ribbon vibrator: adds a disconnect so types print directly for stencils
