## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper feed fingers and erasing shelf need to slide along a rod without rotating out of place.

**Solution:** A sleeve with a tongue in a groove on the rod carries paper finger supports and a lug with extension; the sleeve slides but cannot turn.
