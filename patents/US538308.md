## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Control for hammer-impression mechanisms for deciding or adjusting hammer-drive energy

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in that class of typewriters in which type wheels or segments and a hammer coacting therewith are employed for making the impression as distinguished from those machines employing type bars.
