## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** A flexible strip is threaded alternately through the backing, the folded edge and the carbon sheets, so the pack can be exchanged, and the fold serves as a stop for aligning the sheets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets sewn together with the backing lose their usefulness, because the used-up packs cannot be interchanged and are thrown away with the material.

**Solution:** A flexible strip is threaded alternately through the backing, the folded edge and the carbon sheets, so the pack can be exchanged, and the fold serves as a stop for aligning the sheets.
