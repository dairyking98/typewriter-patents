## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to means for effecting an adjustment of the paper;

**How it works (reading):** A platen frame pivoted on links to the truck can be swung back to free the paper, and a fractional line-spacing mechanism sits at the right end of the platen; applied to a Remington carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper on the carriage needs simple and efficient means of adjustment, including fractional line spacing on the platen.

**Solution:** A platen frame pivoted on links to the truck can be swung back to free the paper, and a fractional line-spacing mechanism sits at the right end of the platen; applied to a Remington carriage.
