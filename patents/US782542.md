## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Denomination keys for tabulating on the Underwood are numerous; the inventor wants a simpler finger interface.

**Solution:** Replace the denomination keys with a single marked finger bar whose pressed point selects the denomination spacing after a column stop.

**How it works:** A column-stop bar rocks forward so a selected stop meets a stop on the carriage, and a finger lifts the escapement rack off its pinion, releasing the carriage. The carriage stops at the column, but the rack cannot re-engage until a slide and pawl, retracted by a spring by an amount set by the selected denomination, allow additional spacing. The single finger bar can operate column stop and release without a denomination, or with one chosen by where it is pressed.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** finger bar; column stop; square rock shaft; rack; escapement wheel; pinion; dog carrier; slide; spacing pawl; carriage spring

**Improves on:**

- [US728170](../patents/US728170.md): single finger bar replaces the set of denomination keys
