## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-bars need to be aligned and held rigidly without end play.

**Solution:** A one-piece bifurcated elastic hanger with a journal bearing in each fork, adjustable by turning and sliding on a fastening screw.

**How it works:** Spring-fork hangers are screwed at intervals around an annular disk; each fork has a bearing in its inner end receiving a cone-pointed trunnion on the type-bar, the fork pressing inward. A slot longer than the screw diameter lets the hanger be turned and moved radially to align the type.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md)

**Parts:** bifurcated hanger; fastening screw; annular disk; type-bar; trunnion; journal bearing
