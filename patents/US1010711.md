## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing needs a tally strip printed simultaneously with the daily total on the bill sheet, and its line spacing must be independent.

**Solution:** Rotary carriers hold a narrow tally strip guided around the platen over the daily total column, with a transfer medium so both are printed together; the strip spaces automatically.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
