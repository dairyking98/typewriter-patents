## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object is to increase the speed I5 of possible operation, to better distribute the work of writing by enabling the thumbs of the operator to be used, -and to decrease the labor of writing.

**How it works (reading):** Place a central thumb-piece shift lever near the front of the keyboard, linked to the carriage shift key, usable by either thumb.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The shift key sits at the left where the left hand is already overworked.

**Solution:** Place a central thumb-piece shift lever near the front of the keyboard, linked to the carriage shift key, usable by either thumb.

**How it works:** A shift lever pivots on a block clamped to the front frame bar, with a thumb-piece across the keyboard centre line. A link joins its lower arm to the Remington carriage shift key. Pressing toward the keyboard rocks the lever and shifts the platen carriage.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** shift lever; thumb-piece; clamp block; link; carriage shift key

**Improves on:**

- Remington left-hand shift key: central thumb lever for either hand
