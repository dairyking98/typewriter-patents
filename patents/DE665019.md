It is known per se in typewriters that with a certain Position of the rocker switch, several passes of the ratchet gear teeth are possible, d. H. the car jumps or a so-called permanent switching occurs. From this lack has already been used by pressing the rocker switch with a special button brings in a position so that the passage through resilient pendulum swing of the rocker switch several teeth or the so-called permanent switching of the ratchet wheel is possible. the Attachment of a special key is not in the usual key board. always possible. To remedy this situation, according to the invention, the space key apart from the limited deflection used to achieve a single switching carry out another additional deflection through which the rocker switch for permanent switching, is released.
