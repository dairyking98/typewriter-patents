## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a ruling or marking device for use in type-writing machines and designed to produce on the sheet a continuous ruled line extending transversely across the sheet and from one margin to the other or of any intermediate length desired.

**How it works (reading):** A hinged U-shaped arm carrying a marker swings to the platen and is pressed on the sheet while the carriage, released from letter-spacing, moves to draw a ruled line.

**Solution:** A hinged U-shaped arm carrying a marker swings to the platen and is pressed on the sheet while the carriage, released from letter-spacing, moves to draw a ruled line.
