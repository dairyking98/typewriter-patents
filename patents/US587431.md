## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriters are heavy and cannot also serve for general writing; the line of writing is hidden while typing.

**Solution:** A light typebar machine whose frame runs on tracks over a book or sits on a base with a separate paper carriage, with ribbon devices that keep the line visible except while a key is down.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot in brackets on a crescent supporting ring, with ears that stop lateral play, and are pulled by rods from key levers. Letter spacing, line spacing and an adjustable stop let the frame advance along stationary tracks, or the machine be bolted to a base used with a moving carriage. A ribbon mechanism lifts the ribbon only during a keystroke.
