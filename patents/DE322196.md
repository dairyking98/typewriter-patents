## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers

**How it works (reading):** A hard back plate or roller behind the paper, with a dry-coloured roller inking the impression, and the type swinging from below against the back of the sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type striking through the paper into soft absorbent backing damaged the paper and blurred writing.

**Solution:** A hard back plate or roller behind the paper, with a dry-coloured roller inking the impression, and the type swinging from below against the back of the sheet.
