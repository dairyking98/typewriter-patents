## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator needs warning that the sheet is nearly at its end, and earlier alarms needed a conducting strip on the paper rollers.

**Solution:** A clamp-on alarm: a wire contact stepped toward a fixed metal strip at each line feed, which sounds a bell when the paper edge has passed it.

**How it works:** A column clamped to the typewriter end frame carries a frame with an insulating spindle. A lever on the spindle is struck by the line-space pawl at each line advance, turning the spindle so a bent wire's flat end presses the paper against a metal strip. While paper is between them there is no contact; when the paper edge passes the wire, the circuit of battery and bell closes and the bell sounds. A spring returns the spindle as the pawl retreats, and the wire height sets the bottom margin.
