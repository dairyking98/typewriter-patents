## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** Guide the pawl positively by a slide with an inclined slot so it first moves parallel to the platen axis into the ratchet teeth and then swings across to advance, with a stepped lever choosing 1, 2 or 3 lines.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-spacing pawls on a universal joint were not positively guided, so engagement with the platen ratchet was uncertain.

**Solution:** Guide the pawl positively by a slide with an inclined slot so it first moves parallel to the platen axis into the ratchet teeth and then swings across to advance, with a stepped lever choosing 1, 2 or 3 lines.

**How it works:** The shift lever carrying the pawl hangs on two perpendicular pivots. A grip lever moves a slide with an inclined slot in which a lug of the shift lever runs, so the pawl first slides along the axis into the line ratchet and then moves at right angles to turn it. The lug's end also engages one of three notches of an adjusting lever, giving one, two or three line spaces. On release a spring reverses the sequence, disengaging before returning.
