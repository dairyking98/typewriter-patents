**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making attachment, variable width) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**How it works (reading):** An indicator or counter fitted to an ordinary typewriter advances by units matching each character's width on the matrix machine, showing when a line is full.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewritten copy for matrix machines must justify, but typewriters have uniform feed and special machines are costly to build for the purpose.

**Solution:** An indicator or counter fitted to an ordinary typewriter advances by units matching each character's width on the matrix machine, showing when a line is full.

**Features:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
