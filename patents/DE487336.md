## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manuscript holders change the viewing opening width with complicated crank mechanisms or toothed slides.

**Solution:** The upper edge of the line-viewing opening is a transverse plate on arms that pivot about the feed roller axis; a handle sets the opening width and a notched stop arm with a leaf spring locks it.

**How it works:** Turning the handle pivots the arms around the feed roller, moving the transverse plate relative to the front wall; a spring-loaded projection seats in depressions to hold the setting.
