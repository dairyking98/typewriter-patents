## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Manuscript Holders The invention relates to manuscript holders of those Type consisting of an upright case attached to the typewriter, a feed roller cooperating with pressure rollers is mounted in its upper end is around which the manuscript is drawn so that its lines one after the other become visible through a viewing opening. The viewing openings of these well-known manuscript holders are mostly formed by two metal strips or bars, in which, in order to change the Width of the viewing opening the strips or rods themselves by complicated crank mechanisms be moved more or less apart or a special slide that underneath of the one strip is guided, is adjusted by means of toothed gears. In contrast The invention is that the upper edge of the line viewing opening of a formed parallel above the upper edge of the housing front wall transverse plate is designed to change the width of the opening by means of arms around the axis of the feed roller is pivotable and lockable by a locking device.
