**How it works (reading):** Make the push rods themselves the collecting organs by giving each an angled guide slot, removing the separate catch levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Decimal tab catch levers were spring-loaded, so tab key springs had to overcome them and the keys were hard to press.

**Solution:** Make the push rods themselves the collecting organs by giving each an angled guide slot, removing the separate catch levers.
