## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hard symbol keys jar and sore the operator's fingers, and keys are costly to replace.

**Solution:** A key cap is detachable and removable without disturbing the key sockets or bars, and is made of a softer material to cushion the strike.
