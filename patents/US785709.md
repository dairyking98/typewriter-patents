## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ribbon needs to be fed continuously in one direction, step by step, and lifted to the print point without tightening or slackening.

**Solution:** An endless ribbon is lifted by a movable guide linked to the key action and advanced by a feed roller driven from the carriage spring drum through a one-way pawl.

**How it works:** Depressing a key-lever drops the universal bar, rocking a shaft that feeds the carriage and, through an arm and crank, raises a movable ribbon guide to put the ribbon in the type path. The ribbon travels in an arc matching the guide's, so it stays at constant tension. A guide roller doubles as feed roller, geared to the carriage spring drum through a pawl and ratchet so it turns only as the carriage advances, not on hand return. A spring-pressed roller grips the endless ribbon against it, and the ribbon coils in a removable support.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** movable ribbon guide; endless ribbon; feed roller; pressure roller; spring drum; pawl and ratchet; ribbon support plate; universal bar; rock-shaft
