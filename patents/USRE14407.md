## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates particularlyl to a tabulating attachment for typewriting machines, and one of its objects is the pro-I vision of simple and eiiicient mechanism of p this character whereby the carriage may be released from its feeding mechanism, gradually stopped at the end of its run and arrested in its return movement at a predetermined point.

**How it works (reading):** A resilient buffer opposes the main motor spring and gradually stops the carriage at the end of its run, and a locking-out device keeps the buffer connected to the carriage until the carriage is returned to the set position, then reconnects the feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulating carriage stops too abruptly at the end of its run and cannot be returned to an exact preset point.

**Solution:** A resilient buffer opposes the main motor spring and gradually stops the carriage at the end of its run, and a locking-out device keeps the buffer connected to the carriage until the carriage is returned to the set position, then reconnects the feed.
