## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (electrotherapy on keyed instruments incl typewriters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applying electric currents by contact electrodes alternating or intermittent currents

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the combination with pianofortes, organs, harmoniums, typewriters and like keyed instruments or apparatus of means whereby the act of playing or operating the same will communicate to the performer or operator a current of electricity.

**How it works (reading):** Electric battery circuits are connected to keys so that playing the instrument passes a current through the performer's body.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Players of pianos and other keyed instruments needed a sensation in the hands for hygienic purposes.

**Solution:** Electric battery circuits are connected to keys so that playing the instrument passes a current through the performer's body.
