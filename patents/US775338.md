## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to bearings for carriages of various machines, and particularly type-writers.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage bearing portion has V-grooves that ride on four bearing wheels on studs on the frame, and the carriage is connected to the escapement through pinions on arms that engage a rack, with a rack-bar driving the escapement feed-wheel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage should travel with very little friction.

**Solution:** The carriage bearing portion has V-grooves that ride on four bearing wheels on studs on the frame, and the carriage is connected to the escapement through pinions on arms that engage a rack, with a rack-bar driving the escapement feed-wheel.
