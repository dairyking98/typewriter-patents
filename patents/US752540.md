## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the combinations and arrangement of devices and in the features of construction set forth in the followingdescription, illustrated in the accompanying drawings, and particularly pointed out in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating mechanism with a carriage release and stop positions the carriage directly to the item position in a simple, few-part construction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Positioning items on the paper needs the machine scale or repeated space-bar presses.

**Solution:** A tabulating mechanism with a carriage release and stop positions the carriage directly to the item position in a simple, few-part construction.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
