## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter producing raised characters for the blind needs the line spacing to be regulated and the punches to register exactly in the indentations of the rails.

**Solution:** Key-actuated punches on a carriage that runs along a bridge over two parallel rails with indentations; a transverse bar moved along the rails sets the line spacing, and the punch points are located in the rail recesses.
