## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine paper feed) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on devices on calculating machines that remove the paper after each impression advance, and essentially in that between, n the print roller shaft and the rocking shaft of the platen frame.

**How it works (reading):** A toothed gear between the pressure roller shaft and the rocking shaft, driven by a thumb disk, advances the paper; the pressure roller swings to each type area so one device serves both directions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be removed after each impression, and the existing paper feed does not work the same for both printing directions.

**Solution:** A toothed gear between the pressure roller shaft and the rocking shaft, driven by a thumb disk, advances the paper; the pressure roller swings to each type area so one device serves both directions.
