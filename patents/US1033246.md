## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**How it works (reading):** The universal bar shifts a rearward arm and fulcrumed swinging arm that drive a two-arm lever engaging the ribbon antenna, which advances the ribbon and reverses it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must reverse automatically when it is wholly unwound from one spool, and the feed should be driven from the typewriting action.

**Solution:** The universal bar shifts a rearward arm and fulcrumed swinging arm that drive a two-arm lever engaging the ribbon antenna, which advances the ribbon and reverses it.

**Features:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
