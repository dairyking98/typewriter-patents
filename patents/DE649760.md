## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen frame's stroke is limited only downward, so upward stroke for writing positions is not controlled.

**Solution:** Front and rear adjustable stop supports with inclined and horizontal surfaces on the frame limit stroke both up and down while the base position stays fixed.

**How it works:** Inclined surfaces on the frame ride stop supports to vary the upward stroke; horizontal surfaces set the lower position.
