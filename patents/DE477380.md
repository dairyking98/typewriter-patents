> Description Translated from German Device for winding or unwinding strips of paper in constant Distances, e.g. at cash registers or counter ticket printers Die The invention relates to a device for winding and unwinding paper strips in always constant routes, z. B. at cash registers or ticket printers, at which the deflection of the transport lever causing the advancement of the paper roll is regulated by a stop, its adjustment by a on the circumference the roll adjacent to the paper roll according to the increasing or decreasing paper roll diameter takes place automatically; and it is mainly characterized by the fact that the stop for the transport lever on a sliding, U-shaped bent Stirrup sits, at the other end of which the roll running on the paper roll is arranged that moves the stopper according to the increasing diameter of the paper roll. In the drawing, the device is shown in one embodiment, as it is used, for example, in a counter ticket printer, and although Fig. r shows a vertical section through the center and Fig. 2 is a side view.
