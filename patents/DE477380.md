## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper advance per stroke changes as the paper roll diameter shrinks or grows, unless the stop is readjusted.

**Solution:** A stop on a U-shaped bracket is carried by a roller running on the paper roll, so the stop follows the roll diameter and the advance stays constant.
