## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need permanent alignment without adjustments, interchangeable manufacture, and positive control of the typebar during its stroke.

**Solution:** Angle-form counterbalanced typebars swing in slots between fixed guide walls on fixed pivots, and are driven by a connecting rod from the key lever.

**How it works:** A circular slotted carrier-guide plate around the print point, with a supplemental stationary guide plate, holds each angle-form typebar in a slot between side walls so it is laterally controlled entirely by the walls. The pivot is set well behind the front of the guide walls so the bar is positively guided at the moment of printing. In rest the type arm hangs down and the angle arm projects rearward, so its weight tends to throw the type arm toward the print point, opposed by a return spring on the key lever. A connecting rod from the key lever pulls the typebar through its stroke to a horizontal print position.
