## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must be restrained at a controlled speed, and column stops must be adjustable.

**Solution:** A rotary speed-control device in a drum retards the carriage, and an adjustable column stop bar with reed frame controls the carriage travel.
