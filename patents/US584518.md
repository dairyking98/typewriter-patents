## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in what are known as check-protectors, or devices to be used as a means for preventing the forging or raising of checks, drafts, rbc.

**How it works (reading):** A hand-operated check protector feeds the check past a set of needles on a disk that are driven into the paper by a lever, with an ink pad in the base and ratchet-and-pawl stepping of the disk to space the punched figures.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks and drafts could be raised or altered after being written, so a protective mark was needed that could not be erased.

**Solution:** A hand-operated check protector feeds the check past a set of needles on a disk that are driven into the paper by a lever, with an ink pad in the base and ratchet-and-pawl stepping of the disk to space the punched figures.
