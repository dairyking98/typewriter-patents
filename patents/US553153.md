## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage play and shake spoil alignment, key touch is heavy, and typebars fly ahead of or rebound against the key-levers, causing double impressions and interference.

**Solution:** A typebar machine with a carriage whose print point lies over its lower rail and whose pull is centred between its rails, a third-class-lever key and typebar joint with a rebound-proof prong, and a synchronous two-dog half-space escapement.

**How it works:** Key-levers engage the typebars between pivot and type, first lifting at the point farthest from the pivot and then shifting toward it, so the bar gets a soft start and increasing speed. A third prong on the typebar traps the key-lever end so the bar cannot outrun the key but can still rebound freely after printing, while another prong abuts the lever end to stop rebound at rest. The whole typebar frame tilts to shift case. The carriage rides on a lower rail directly beneath the print point with a rear grooved rail taking side load through guiding lips, and the feed rack is placed midway between the supports. A universal frame swings the feed dogs: one movable dog and one fixed dog in the simple form, or two movable dogs joined by a rocking bar that each advance half a space, one when the key goes down and one when it rises. A spring drum pulls the carriage.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** shifting frame; division plate; typebar; key-lever; universal frame; tension spring; spacing bar; shift bar; tilting ribbon frame; carriage; lower supporting bar; rear supporting rail; supporting wheel; guiding lip; feed rack; quick-release key; feed dog; rocking bar; spring drum; platen; platen ratchet; line-space lever; space regulator; rubber key button; paper guide roller; cushion
