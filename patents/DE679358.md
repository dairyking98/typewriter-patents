## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (complementary counter for typewriters with totalizing)

**How it works (reading):** The ratchet is pivoted in the opposite direction to the direction of rotation during blank writing, controlled by the total tension member, so the negative set zeroes correctly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Complementary counters need zeroing backlash removal on the negative dial set, which the positive-set device could not provide.

**Solution:** The ratchet is pivoted in the opposite direction to the direction of rotation during blank writing, controlled by the total tension member, so the negative set zeroes correctly.
