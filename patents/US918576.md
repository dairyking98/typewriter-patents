## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column work needs the carriage to stop at chosen denominations, and the existing tabulator is not a practical denominational stop system.

**Solution:** A drum carrying denomination stops, with a development of its interior, is set by keys so the carriage stops at the selected denomination of a Monarch front-strike machine.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
