## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A Japanese-script machine where the type magazine slides over a character plate has little room for separate handles for character spacing, line spacing and table spacing in vertical and horizontal writing.

**Solution:** A single handle's stroke, through a slide with three lugs acting at successive depths, selects character space, line space or tab stop by shifting racks and a pawl.

**How it works:** The type magazine slides left-right on a frame that slides front-back over the character plate; the handle passes through the magazine and, via a wing plate and crank frames, pushes down a slide (the paddle) on the paper-cylinder carriage. A light press makes the first lug shift the cylinder drive from a fixed-distance sliding rack onto a fixed rack, so the cylinder advances one set amount on release (vertical-writing character space). A harder press makes the second lug lift a pawl from a sliding rack onto a fixed rack so the carriage advances one set step (line space). A full press lets the third lug drop a stop onto blocks set under the base, halting the carriage at table positions. For horizontal writing the biases are reversed and the first bell crank is removed.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Feed device for a Japanese-script printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. A feed device for a Japanese-script printing machine, in which a slide on the paper-cylinder carriage moves down when the handle is worked. Early in the slide's stroke, its first lug shifts the paper-cylinder drive (which is always biased to turn one way) from a sliding rack that moves a fixed distance onto a fixed rack, and this can be switched off at will. Midway in the stroke, its second lug shifts the pawl on the carriage (which is always biased to travel sideways) from a fixed-distance sliding rack onto a fixed rack. At the end of the stroke, its third lug makes a stop pivoted on the carriage act on blocks that can be put on and taken off the base. The object is to give character spacing, line spacing and tab spacing, in both vertical and horizontal writing, simply and at will from a single handle. Brief description of the drawings.
