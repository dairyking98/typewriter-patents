## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapements need quick let-off and independence from key lever leverage.

**Solution:** An escapement that releases as soon as the typebar starts back, wherever the retaining dog drops.

**How it works:** Improving Barr's application 425687, a rock shaft carries the dog and is driven through a connection from the universal bar or gate with a spring. Plan views show the normal, fully depressed and up-stroke positions with let-off taking place on the rebound.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** escape wheel; dog; rock shaft; universal bar; spring

**Improves on:**

- Barr application 425687 (1908): positive, quick let-off independent of key leverage
