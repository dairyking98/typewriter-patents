## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**How it works (reading):** Holding and switching pawls are set at an acute angle and overlap so that the switching tooth slides along the pawl, returning the switching body quickly with little resistance.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon and ribbon fork return movements add force to each keystroke and make typing heavy.

**Solution:** Holding and switching pawls are set at an acute angle and overlap so that the switching tooth slides along the pawl, returning the switching body quickly with little resistance.
