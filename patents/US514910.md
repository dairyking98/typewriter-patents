## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage and line-spacing the platen are separate operations with no handy key.

**Solution:** A key beside the keyboard, linked by cords and pulleys to the carriage, pulls the carriage back and ratchets the platen one line via a roller riding a ramp track.

**How it works:** A vertical shaft with two grooved wheels is cord-connected to the carriage and to a finger key at the right of the keyboard. Pulling the key draws the carriage back against its spring drum. A spring-loaded arm carrying a dog and roller engages a toothed wheel meshing with a platen gear; as the carriage returns the roller climbs an inclined adjustable track, turning the platen one line space. During the forward writing movement the roller is inoperative. Track height is adjustable to give single or double spacing.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** spring drum; vertical shaft; grooved wheel; cord; return key; dog arm; toothed wheel; roller; inclined track; platen
