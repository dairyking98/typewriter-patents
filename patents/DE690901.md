**How it works (reading):** A non-oxidizing ring is molded over the upper edge of any key cup, so the key top is formed by injection moulding rather than costly pressed or drawn parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Metal key cups tarnish and molded keys need costly tools, while the old key ring used high-grade metal.

**Solution:** A non-oxidizing ring is molded over the upper edge of any key cup, so the key top is formed by injection moulding rather than costly pressed or drawn parts.
