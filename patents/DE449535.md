## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressure-roller brackets swing forward to a position in front of the platen, forcing an extra shift by the clerk.

**Solution:** A locking pawl stops rotation about the front pivot, so the bracket swings about the other pivot and comes straight into the working position.
