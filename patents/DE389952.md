## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** A locking slide on one guide segment has edge-open slots, so push rods can be dropped in radially from outside and held by the closure slide.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever push rods are hard to insert and fix in their guide segments.

**Solution:** A locking slide on one guide segment has edge-open slots, so push rods can be dropped in radially from outside and held by the closure slide.
