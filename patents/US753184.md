## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator's fingers must reach the space bar on a keyboard machine, which takes them off the keys.

**Solution:** A casing mounted above the key holds a plunger over an aperture; a bulb held in the palm forces air into the casing, projecting the plunger to depress the space bar without finger movement.
