## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists need a way to set and repeat the vertical position of the first, last or any line on the sheet without counting line spaces.

**Solution:** A clip-on case geared to the platen carries a pointer that sweeps a graduated dial and is stopped by an adjustable stop, arresting the platen at a preset line.

**How it works:** A semicircular case clamps to the carriage frame and holds a spindle with a fixed pointer and a knob. A gear wheel on the platen shaft drives a diminishing gear train that turns the pointer a partial revolution for two, three or more platen revolutions. An adjustable stop box slides in a slot around the rim and clamps at any position, its spring-loaded rod resting on the dial face to block the pointer and so stop the platen. Pushing the stop's knob lifts it so the pointer passes. Pulling the knob moves the pointer wheel out of gear, and a spring returns the pointer to zero. A sliding plate locks the spindle out of gear to disable the device.

**Parts:** case; spindle; pointer; knob; gear train; platen gear; adjustable stop box; sliding stop rod; return spring; flat spring; clamp; graduated dial

**Numbers:** two, three or more platen revolutions per partial pointer revolution
