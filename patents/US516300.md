## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons need automatic movement lengthwise and widthwise, and lateral shifting so upper and lower case types strike the same spot on the ribbon.

**Solution:** A cam driven set of crown wheels and partial gears winds the ribbon and moves it, with a ribbon shift for case.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 2, the ribbon runs between two removable spool shafts, each with a crown wheel. Partial gear teeth on a driving shaft engage a crown wheel to wind one spool, while spring detents stop backward turning and a collar lifts the detent when the spool must pay out. A toothed wheel on the driving shaft, driven in steps, with a developed actuating cam, moves the ribbon carrier widthwise and shifts the ribbon for case.
