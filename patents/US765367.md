## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a back-spacing attachment for type-writing machines, and particularly to one having special utility on an Oliver type-writer.

**How it works (reading):** A finger-operated attachment fitted to the carriage backs it one space at a time through a pawl on the escapement rack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Backing the carriage for a correction means moving it by hand, which is slow and inconvenient.

**Solution:** A finger-operated attachment fitted to the carriage backs it one space at a time through a pawl on the escapement rack.
