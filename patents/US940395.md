## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in tabulating devices for typewriters, such as are used to stop the carriage at a predetermined point or points to bring the column or columns of figures into perfect alineme'nt automatically, and the objects of my invention are, first, to produce a tabulator which 1s extremely simple in construction, neat in appearance, and comprehensive in its operation;

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating device for typewriters that stops the carriage at one or more predetermined points so columns align automatically.
