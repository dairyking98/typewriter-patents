## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide for typewriters and Similar: machines in which an upper sheet of paper holds the writing paper in a known manner to a lower, movable paper guide plate.

**How it works (reading):** A helical spring joins the two plates at punched holes along the collision line, so the lower guide can pivot or drop without a gap.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Upper and lower paper guide plates collide, crumpling or tearing paper when the lower plate moves.

**Solution:** A helical spring joins the two plates at punched holes along the collision line, so the lower guide can pivot or drop without a gap.
