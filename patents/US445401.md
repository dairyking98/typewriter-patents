## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (generic joint, not typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangement of linkage connections; Pivotal connections; Pivoted

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the formation of parts to be jointed, the objects thereof being to adapt the said parts to be readily connected and disconnected the one from the other, and to insure the most close and perfect bearing between the parts, whereby lash or play is avoided.

**How it works (reading):** The lever end has two opposing arc-shaped arms, and the push-down rod engages between them so the two parts swing as if pivoted, with no separate pin.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pivotal joints between key rods and type-bar levers needed to be easy to connect and disconnect with no lash or play.

**Solution:** The lever end has two opposing arc-shaped arms, and the push-down rod engages between them so the two parts swing as if pivoted, with no separate pin.
