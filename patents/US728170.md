## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tabulator carriage dog must give a preliminary stop and then move the carriage a set distance; the dog should be held out until the carriage has moved.

**Solution:** A supplemental dog that is held out of engagement until the carriage moves a certain distance, after which it engages the rack again; a preliminary stop is provided when the carriage is released.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** carriage-dog; supplemental dog; rack
