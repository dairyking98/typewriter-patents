## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with calculator counter)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a calculating machine According to Pateht 32-1834, a vertically displaceable removable counter on the vertical counter Presetting device for controlling the type of calculation of the horizontal arithmetic unit.

**How it works (reading):** A vertically sliding plate with an opening is engaged by a rotating eccentric on a bolt; a spring holds a nose in grooves so the setting is secured against twisting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Presetting in a calculating counter is held by fixed notches and needs a simpler, safer way to select add or subtract.

**Solution:** A vertically sliding plate with an opening is engaged by a rotating eccentric on a bolt; a spring holds a nose in grooves so the setting is secured against twisting.

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
