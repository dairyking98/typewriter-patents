## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenotype attachment for typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling; Subscript or superscript character

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to such an improvement in the type-writers lately introduced into use that the same may be made available for verbatim reports by a simple additional attachment, which may be used or not, according as the type-writer is employed for common or stenotypic work.

**How it works (reading):** A separate, shorter marking lever prints outside the common printing point, operated by an adjustable swinging button on the space bar that can be turned off the bar when the marker is wanted as an ordinary key.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters need a way to mark word divisions and initial letters for verbatim stenotypic reports, separate from common writing.

**Solution:** A separate, shorter marking lever prints outside the common printing point, operated by an adjustable swinging button on the space bar that can be turned off the bar when the marker is wanted as an ordinary key.
