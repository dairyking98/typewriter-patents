## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters need a way to mark word divisions and initial letters for verbatim stenotypic reports, separate from common writing.

**Solution:** A separate, shorter marking lever prints outside the common printing point, operated by an adjustable swinging button on the space bar that can be turned off the bar when the marker is wanted as an ordinary key.
