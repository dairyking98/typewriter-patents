## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Make a simple, cheap typewriter an unskilled person can use, with a single type bar positioned by an attached stylus.

**Solution:** A single rocking type bar on a sliding frame, positioned by a stylus over a slotted character bar, printing the character chosen.

**How it works:** A transverse character bar with registering slots along its front margin carries impressed characters in two lines. A type bar frame hangs on arms pivoted to a frame arm and is returned by an adjustable spring wire. The type bar, with two lines of types, oscillates between stops, held at either end by a toggle spring so one of two type lines is directly beneath the centre. The operator moves a stylus into the slot for a character, which sets the printing position; the carriage is operated by a lug and a rack plate, with line spacing by thumb wheel and a bell.

- **Selection:** [Pointer into a hole or notch](../features/sel-pointer-hole.md), [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md)
- **Type element:** [Straight type bar or rule](../features/elem-linear-bar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by turning the element](../features/shift-rotary.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)

- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** character bar; slot; stylus; type bar; type bar frame; adjusting spring wire; toggle spring; rack plate; thumb wheel; bell
