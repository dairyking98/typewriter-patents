## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Used ink ribbons come back too dry and greasy from the old wick-covered wooden rollers, which give weak ironing and also lose grease to the ribbon.

**Solution:** Two rotatable, resiliently pressed roller bodies made of perforated cylindrical lidded boxes, each with a tight replaceable textile cover and loaded with impregnant; the ribbon runs between them to be refreshed.
