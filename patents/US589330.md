## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to copy-holders, and partiularly to' an attachment for t ype-writers for automatically feeding the copy with the feed of the paper being written upon.

**How it works (reading):** A copy-holder on the carriage has a feed roller driven by a pawl and ratchet from the paper feed, so copy advances with the paper and can be offset by lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copy must be fed in step with the typed paper by hand.

**Solution:** A copy-holder on the carriage has a feed roller driven by a pawl and ratchet from the paper feed, so copy advances with the paper and can be offset by lines.
