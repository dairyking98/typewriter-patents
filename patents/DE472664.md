## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Single and double check cash register There are Cash registers with a check slip known that when setting the setting device a check on one group of special designations and, if hired, on the other group of special designations, two checks through a single printing unit output. These known devices have the disadvantage that as soon as two Checks issued. these are almost completely identical in color and imprint and only made known by small, not very noticeable distinctive signs which can easily be misused with the checks. The present invention now aims to remedy this drawback by providing two independently attached check strips, which receive different imprints according to their different types of use and can also have different colors, whereby a striking distinction is achieved. Cash registers are known per se which have two or more check strips attached independently of one another, which receive different imprints according to their different types of use and can also have different colors.
