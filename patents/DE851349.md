## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (friction clutch for row indexing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** An expansion ring of bent sheet metal strips acts as an inner band brake against the clutch housing, expanded by a two-armed lever pair with a spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction clutch for line-indexing ratchet is hard to release and needs adjusting.

**Solution:** An expansion ring of bent sheet metal strips acts as an inner band brake against the clutch housing, expanded by a two-armed lever pair with a spring.
