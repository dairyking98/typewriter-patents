## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** Column counter groups are combined into individual sub-groups, and a coupling linkage makes the cross counter slide return by the difference in spacing so the drives stay in step.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Booking machines must coordinate column and cross counters of different spacing, which is hard with synchronized drive wheels.

**Solution:** Column counter groups are combined into individual sub-groups, and a coupling linkage makes the cross counter slide return by the difference in spacing so the drives stay in step.
