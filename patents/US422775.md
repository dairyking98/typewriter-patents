## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar pivots wear loose and the bar prints out of alignment.

**Solution:** A slot through the typebar behind its pivot with a conical screw that spreads the walls and tightens the journals.

**How it works:** The bar has a saw slot near its rear end; a tapped conical hole at the slot takes a conical screw. Turning the screw forces the slot walls apart, pushing conical journals into the hanger and removing lost motion.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type bar; journal; hanger; slot; conical screw
