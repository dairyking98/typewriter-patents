## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper feed device for typewriters, in which the feed and feed rollers are mounted in one-piece transverse arms and in which the frame formed by the rollers and transverse arms and in particular the guide plate by springs acting on its holding arms against the circumference Platen is pressed.

**How it works (reading):** The rod-and-cross-arm frame is joined with stepped pins in oversize bores and nuts, so the roller frame has a small play and adapts to the roll; the cover plate is only loosely placed and held.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rigid paper feed frame presses unevenly on the platen when the roll is not perfectly round or several sheets with carbon are fed, leaving set-off marks.

**Solution:** The rod-and-cross-arm frame is joined with stepped pins in oversize bores and nuts, so the roller frame has a small play and adapts to the roll; the cover plate is only loosely placed and held.
