## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reel cover on plastic housing needs screws and its opening travel must be limited.

**Solution:** A resilient tab slides on the housing up to a stop and is joined to the cover, limiting the opening of the cover; the coil spring hinges it without screws.
