## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention pertains to attachments for type-writing machines, and is designed for ready application to the platen of type-writ ing machinesof any usual style or construction to hold in position on the platen cards, envelops, and other comparatively small papers or objects upon which it is desired to write.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment that fits onto the platen and clamps the top edge of a card or envelope along its full length, with no parts beneath the paper or projecting inward from the clamped edge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Small cards and envelopes are hard to hold square on the platen, and clamps must not project where the type can strike them or sit under the paper.

**Solution:** An attachment that fits onto the platen and clamps the top edge of a card or envelope along its full length, with no parts beneath the paper or projecting inward from the clamped edge.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
