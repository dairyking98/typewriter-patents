## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A divided platen with one section locked cannot be turned as one unit, and the sections cannot run at different rates.

**Solution:** Two platen sections, one automatically unlocked and both coupled by a shiftable gear, with the lock defeatable for unitary platen use.
