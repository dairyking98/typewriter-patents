## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writers, and particularly to-the carriagefeed mechanism by means of which the carriage may be moved from one side to the other;

**How it works (reading):** A pivoted toothed plate on the carriage engages or disengages the escapement dog, with a tensional spring giving the carriage its right-to-left impulse.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must be freed to move either way and also be moved step by step from left to right by a key, which the existing feed does not allow.

**Solution:** A pivoted toothed plate on the carriage engages or disengages the escapement dog, with a tensional spring giving the carriage its right-to-left impulse.
