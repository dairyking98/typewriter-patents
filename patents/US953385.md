**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the-matters hereinafter set forth and more particularly pointed out in the appended claim.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Heavy cards or stiff sheets shift on the platen and spring away from it, so they cannot take clean impressions near the top and bottom margins.

**Solution:** A paper carriage holds the card against the platen with pressure rollers that run against curved guide plates, so the sheet is held close to both edges.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
