## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bar-lock typewriters have time-consuming bell-stop pin adjustment, play in typebar bearings, and ribbon direction needing manual change.

**Solution:** Several improvements: a slotted margin plate with a captive bell pin, an eccentric-head rivet to take up play in typebar bearings, a roller-hook that stops carriage lifting, and an automatic ribbon direction change.

**How it works:** The bell-stop pin slides in a slot and is clamped, so it cannot be lost. The typebars are riveted into dovetail arms with a rivet whose eccentric head, when turned, squeezes the arms against the hub to remove wear play. A hook under the guide rod keeps the carriage from lifting off. A crosspiece with oscillating ribbon carrier arms automatically changes the ribbon direction.
