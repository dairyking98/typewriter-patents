## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen needs to turn with the line-spacing wheel for ordinary line feed, yet also be freely turnable by any amount for irregular spacing, and the line-spacing relation should be changeable so type impact is spread over the platen surface.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded lever, fulcrumed on the walls of an internal groove in the line-spacing ring, grips the ring to the platen, and a hand plunger releases it so the platen turns independently.

**How it works:** A housing keyed into the platen core carries a freely rotating line-spacing ring with an internal groove and peripheral and face teeth. A loosely seated segmental engaging piece with a lever arm sits in the groove and binds on both side walls by leverage. A bent two-arm lever in the housing, pressed by an adjustable expansion spring, bears on the engaging piece's arm and holds the grip, so the platen turns with the ring when the pawl and rock-shaft feed it line by line. Pushing in a spring-returned plunger rod through the right finger wheel moves the bent lever against its spring, frees the engaging piece, and lets the platen be turned either way by hand independently. Unscrewing one rod removes the whole release assembly.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen frame; finger wheel; housing; line-spacing ring; engaging piece; bent lever; expansion spring; adjusting plug; plunger rod; detent roller; line-spacing pawl; rock shaft

**Improves on:**

- Yost line-spacing mechanism: adds a lever-grip clutch so the platen can be released from the line-spacing wheel for differential feed
