## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in the type-bars of a type-writing machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rigid type-bars slam the type onto the paper with enough force to puncture it, cut the ribbon and wear the roller.

**Solution:** The type-bar is made in sections; the character-carrying head is yieldingly supported on a pin and cup so that it absorbs the momentum of the bar.
