## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators must move their hands far to reach the keys, and the platen turn and carriage move need extra motion.

**Solution:** Two curved key banks, one per hand, with columns for each finger, plus foot or knee keys for certain functions, and a lever that turns the platen and moves the carriage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
