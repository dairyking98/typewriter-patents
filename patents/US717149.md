## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be turned through minute or irregular arcs without first releasing the line-space mechanism.

**Solution:** A finger-wheel rotates the platen independently of the line-space wheel, with a brake and collar at the platen end.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
