## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in that form of type-writing machines in which the type-bars are thrown downward in such a manner that the writing is visible to the operator as the work progresses and which is especiallyadapted fonwriting in blank books and official records.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters lack a sturdy adjustable support for writing in bound blank books and official records.

**Solution:** A downward-striking visible typebar machine with a two-carriage system and an adjustable book-rest.

**How it works:** An end-casting frame with an inverted T-rail carries a lower carriage on a central track and an upper carriage with ball-bearing wheels on transverse tracks, so the book can move in both directions. Typebars on semicircular guide plates are thrown downward by key levers so writing is visible. A book-rest with arms attached to the upper carriage can be elevated for books of any size, or tablets and single sheets. Letter and word spacing devices work through a universal bar and rack, line spacing has a pawl that can be disengaged, and a ribbon reel mechanism with shifting device is included. A graduated scale is fixed under the keyboard.
