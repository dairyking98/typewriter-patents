## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**How it works (reading):** The carriage rack and drive wheel use helical teeth, so the force components oppose the print direction and the clutch re-engages smoothly; axial displacement of the gearbox adjusts the carriage position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Straight-toothed carriage drive is noisy and its shocks blur sharp type prints.

**Solution:** The carriage rack and drive wheel use helical teeth, so the force components oppose the print direction and the clutch re-engages smoothly; axial displacement of the gearbox adjusts the carriage position.
