## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing is slow where whole short words and syllables recur.

**Solution:** Add word typeheads to the front of a large typebasket, with word keys and extra escapements that advance the carriage the proper distance for the word plus a space.

**How it works:** Single-letter types stay at the rear of an enlarged basket; word typeheads, two words each (upper selected by the shift key), sit at the front. Word key-levers raise sliding escapement frames carrying escapements of different lengths, so the carriage moves by 2 to 6 letters or word plus space. The word escapements work through a ratchet bar hung from the release lever rod, leaving the regular escapement untouched. Keyboards can be arranged by trade vocabulary.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md), [Typebar and type-head construction](../features/bar-typebar-construction.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** word typehead; word key-lever; escapement frame; ratchet bar; hanger; release lever rod; single-letter escapement

**Numbers:** words or syllables of 2 to 6 letters, with or without space
