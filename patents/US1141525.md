## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Stencil blanks with stiff card frames cannot bend round a platen, and shifted types on a shift machine foul the card frame.

**Solution:** A flat stencil carrier on a vertical-feed attachment is automatically rocked by the machine's shift key so the card margin clears the inoperative types.

**How it works:** The attachment holds a paper stencil blank flat and line-feeds it vertically. When the shift key is depressed, linkage rocks the supporting frame so the thick card margin moves away from the shifted-out type on the typebars. It attaches to and detaches from the typewriter.

**Parts:** stencil blank; carrier frame; shift key; linkage; type bar
