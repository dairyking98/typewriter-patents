**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for actuating the type-bars and is subordinate to the generic claims made in my application filed April 26, 1901, Serial No.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars in a front-stroke segment machine needed a better actuating connection.

**Solution:** Typebars pivot in a segment on a rocking frame; each has a flexing connection that actuates it, with the typebars normally lying away from the platen.

**Features:**
