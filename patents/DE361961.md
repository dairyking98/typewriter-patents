## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In collapsible typewriters with visible writing the key-lever connection to the type transmission breaks when folded, so a special device is needed to re-couple it on unfolding.

**Solution:** The keyboard frame is articulated on the machine frame so it swings with the key levers while they stay positively connected to the transmission, and it folds over the carriage without disconnecting.
