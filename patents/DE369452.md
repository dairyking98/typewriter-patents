## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a calculating machine with printing unit, the printer or the counter could not be switched off independently.

**Solution:** A two-armed coupling lever with a rod and a three-position setting lever disengages the counter drive or the printing hammer depending on the position.
