## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to obviate the above-mentioned disadvantages and to provide inexpensive, durable, and efficient means for rapidly and thoroughly cleaning the type of a writing-machine at the same time.

**How it works (reading):** A hoop carrying bristles or sponge on its periphery is raised into the type basket, slightly rotated, and lowered beneath it by a mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type clog with fuzz, dust and ink, and cleaning them one by one is slow and tedious.

**Solution:** A hoop carrying bristles or sponge on its periphery is raised into the type basket, slightly rotated, and lowered beneath it by a mechanism.

**How it works:** A hoop of brush material is raised, rotated and lowered in the type basket to clean all the types together.
