## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A center lock on portable typewriters can be left engaged, so the sheet is struck before the machine is ready.

**Solution:** The center lock is coupled to the key lock, so it engages at the end of line when the carriage is moved to the middle position.
