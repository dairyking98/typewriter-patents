## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bodily-movable mechanisms for print heads or carriages movable towards or from paper surface with print gap adjustment mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines and one of the objects thereof is to provide new and improved means for changing the relation of the platen with respect to the printing mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** The whole bank of type bars can move toward or away from the platen, with a gauge for paper thickness or the number of sheets and an indicator showing type-bar position relative to the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen-to-type distance must be changeable, the paper thickness is hard to gauge, and the type-bar position is hard to see.

**Solution:** The whole bank of type bars can move toward or away from the platen, with a gauge for paper thickness or the number of sheets and an indicator showing type-bar position relative to the platen.
