## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bodily-movable mechanisms for print heads or carriages movable towards or from paper surface with print gap adjustment mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines and one of the objects thereof is to provide new and improved means for changing the relation of the platen with respect to the printing mechanism.
