## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Prism pointers on typebar machines clink, crystallise at solder joints and are hard to fit.

**Solution:** A self-adjusting wire pointer with springs or rubber bands.

**How it works:** A curved rear wire bar with upturned arms converges to a yoke through links and springs. A forward bar points the eye at where the next letter will print and also supports the ribbon at the print point to stop it curling. It clips on with no tools.
