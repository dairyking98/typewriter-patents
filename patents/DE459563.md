## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (full stroke lock for typewriter calculator) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocking devices, e.g. between keys

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a full stroke lock for a typewriter mount Calculating device, the counters of which are connected to the number keys by drive slides and a control device upstream of this can be actuated.

**How it works (reading):** A pawl controlled by the control device engages the ratchet of the locking bar when a number key is pressed and blocks re-pressing during the return stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Drive slides of number keys on a computing device attached to a typewriter could be pressed again during the return stroke of the full-stroke lock.

**Solution:** A pawl controlled by the control device engages the ratchet of the locking bar when a number key is pressed and blocks re-pressing during the return stroke.
