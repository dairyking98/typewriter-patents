## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper-carriage of a common-printing-point typebar machine needs adjustment of its height relative to the type in the direction of the strike.

**Solution:** The carriage ways are seated on spring-loaded pillars with set-screws that fix their height, so the platen can be adjusted toward or away from the type.

**How it works:** Pillars fixed on the base carry the carriage ways, which can move vertically over the type circle. Springs seated in the pillars push the ways upward and sustain the carriage. Set-screws engaging the ways and working in the pillars control the spring seats, so the carriage and platen are held at a fixed, adjustable height. A screw through the base into the pillar is an alternative. The carriage steps across by known devices.
