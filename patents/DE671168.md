## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Gears slow the type's final approach to the roller, and the roller shifts briefly sideways so the impact happens at low relative speed; the roller's inertia supplies the impression force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are loud and slow because the type strikes the paper roller at full speed and the earlier quiet designs were intricate.

**Solution:** Gears slow the type's final approach to the roller, and the roller shifts briefly sideways so the impact happens at low relative speed; the roller's inertia supplies the impression force.
