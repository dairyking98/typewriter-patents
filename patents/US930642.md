## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists need a scale on the machine for margins and a holder for heading strips or index members that suits various typewriters.

**Solution:** A clamp-on scale and holder for index strips fixed to the platen carrier, adjustable with knurled nuts.

**How it works:** A sheet-metal plate with a strengthening rib is hung from brackets on the front bar of the platen carrier by threaded pins with knurled nuts. The plate carries a graduated scale read against a pointer on the front rail, and holds removable paper heading or index strips. Shown on a Remington-style machine but applicable to others.
