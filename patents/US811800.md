## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter platens are noisy, wear type when the sheath is hard, and the rubber hardens.

**Solution:** A wooden tube roller holds soft-rubber cushions enclosed inside an outer rubber sheath, with an inner cushion insulating the tube from the journal rod, so the working surface stays soft.
