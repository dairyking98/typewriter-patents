## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for controlling character selection

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal objectof my invention is to make a type-writin g machine capable of writing two letters at a time, with a small number of keys controlling all the characters, as simple in construction, durable, rapid, and reliable in operation as possible;

**How it works (reading):** A type-writing machine of thirteen drawing sheets, described in the patent. The mechanism is not shown in the excerpt.
