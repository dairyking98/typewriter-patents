## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case-shifting mechanism for a front-strike typewriter with both platen and type segment shifting needs to be simple and efficient.

**Solution:** Platen frame and typebar segment hang from diverging arms on one rock shaft, moved by a toggle linked to the shift key, so both shift towards or away from each other.

**How it works:** The platen frame is pivoted on links to a carriage truck and rests via grooved rollers on a shift rail. The rail is on arms fixed to a rock shaft; further arms of that shaft carry the typebar segment. One arm forms a stop between two adjustable screw stops limiting travel. A toggle of two links connected to the shift key actuates the arm, locking the parts at each case position.
