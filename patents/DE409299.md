## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculating with a printing unit cannot be done without printing and advancing paper.

**Solution:** A partly milled locking shaft lets the print hammers pass its cut, and a lever on the shaft blocks the paper feed while it is in locked position.
