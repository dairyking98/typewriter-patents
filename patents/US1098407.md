## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicate copies smudged the worksheet, and the paper needed better feeding around the platen.

**Solution:** A second endless ink web runs around the platen with the worksheet, with a metal belt as side guide and feed.

**How it works:** The endless web carries ink to the folded sheet, and the web plus belt grips the inner flap to feed the paper.
