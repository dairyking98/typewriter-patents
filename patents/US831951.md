## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal should shift only when enough force is stored, to avoid straining the ribbon.

**Solution:** A ribbon-end arm tensions a spring against the drive shaft; a latch releases when tension is sufficient and the spring snaps the shaft over.

**How it works:** An obstruction on the ribbon end catches a fork on a vibrating arm, rocking a shaft that tensions a spring and lifts a stop latch. The released spring shifts the drive shaft endwise to the other spool's gears and a companion stop prevents shifting back. A reciprocating part of the machine turns the shaft through a lever, pinion and gear.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spool; drive shaft; ribbon arm; spring; stop latch; detent arm; lever; pinion

**Improves on:**

- Underwood front-strike machine: spring-stored snap-over reversal
