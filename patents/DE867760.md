> Description Translated from German The present invention relates to the printing device of punch card evaluation machines, preferably for printing numbers representing amounts of money in the decimal system. It contains a device for suppressing the zero print to the left of the highest value place and for the zero print in connection with the automatic printout of a decimal point and a comma to determine the place value. For example, the point separates two decimal places (hundredths and tenths) from the ones place, while the comma is placed between the hundreds and thousands places. The device for monitoring the zero pressure in connection with the decimal point pressure always causes a zero to be printed in hundredths or tenths of a digit if one of these digits contains a value ι to 9, even if no higher digit has a value ι to g . Therefore, for example, the number 108010 is printed as an amount of money with two decimal places in the form 1,080.10. In the same way, amounts that are smaller than one monetary unit, e.g. B. printed as .10 or .01.
