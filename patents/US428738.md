## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improves the paper-carriage feeding of typewriters, here on the Merritt machine.

**Solution:** A bell-crank and feed dog carrier driven from the key-lever universal bar steps the carriage rack one tooth per stroke.

**How it works:** A toothed rack bar is fixed to the carriage yokes. A feed-dog carrier pivoted on the bracket is joined through a link to a bell crank, connected by rods to a lever beneath the universal bar. Pressing a key depresses the universal bar, moving the dog into the rack teeth and advancing the carriage, with a finger-piece lever and spring allowing hand release. Slots on the carrier let the dog withdraw.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** base; standard; carriage guide rail; carriage yoke; carriage rod; platen; rack bar; bell crank; link; feed dog; dog carrier; universal bar; finger-piece lever; coiled spring
