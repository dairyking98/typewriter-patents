## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriters need a better carriage-shifting mechanism, line spacing and ribbon feed/reverse.

**Solution:** A book typewriter whose carriage traverses the platen on a line-spacing frame, with an adjustable ratchet line-spacing hand lever.

**How it works:** A carriage frame slides on a line-spacing frame that itself rides on racks along a platen. A spindle with two feed pinions meshes with the racks; a ratchet on the spindle is turned by a hand lever with a spring-pressed pawl. A movable guard between pawl and ratchet limits how many teeth the pawl engages, making line spacing adjustable. Lifting the shifting lever disengages the pinion so the carriage can be repositioned.

**Features:** [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** platen; line-spacing frame; carriage frame; feed pinion; rack; ratchet; hand lever; pawl; guard; shifting lever
