## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage release mechanism of the front-strike machine needs to be improved, with the carriage feed rack and pinion coupled to a shaft in the frame.

**Solution:** A feed rack under the lower carriage rail meshes with two gearwheels on a shaft journaled in frame plates; the mechanism releases the carriage from the feed train. The OCR cuts off before the release detail is complete.
