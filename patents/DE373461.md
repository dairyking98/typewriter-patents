## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a typewriter table, whose supporting the machine Plate is retractable.

**How it works (reading):** A foot pedal over-centre spring lever drives tie rods to long support levers that lower the machine plate on rails into the table, which is then closed by a flap.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting the machine into a retractable typewriter table requires much force, and existing double-lever designs do not fit the space.

**Solution:** A foot pedal over-centre spring lever drives tie rods to long support levers that lower the machine plate on rails into the table, which is then closed by a flap.
