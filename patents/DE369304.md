## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** With platen shift the carriage must reach the shifted position before the typebar reaches the platen even if the shift key is not fully pressed.

**Solution:** A rocker under the key lever extensions completes the shift travel to a stop when any key is struck, through a slide and angle lever on the shift rod.

**How it works:** If the shift key is not pressed fully, striking a key lever carries a rocker rod that pushes a slide, which moves an angle lever and so the shift rod until the carriage raceway reaches its stop. Coupling just behind the key lever pivot gives a high ratio, so the key's pressing force is not affected. The carriage thus always reaches position before the type lever hits the roller.

**Parts:** shift key; shift rod; angle lever; slide; rocker rod; key lever; raceway; stop

**Improves on:**

- incomplete shifts with fast typing: shift is completed automatically by the key stroke
