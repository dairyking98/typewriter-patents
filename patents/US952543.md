## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Painted characters on the key cap wear off when a resilient rubber cap is used over the cap.

**Solution:** The character indicator is a separate piece fixed to the cap support and projecting above the cap, so the cap can be rubber or cork.
