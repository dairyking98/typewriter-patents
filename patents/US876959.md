## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In computing or combined machines, secondary devices must finish before the key motion can be reversed.

**Solution:** A two-way full-stroke device on Underwood key levers lets the key stroke reverse without waiting for the secondary devices to complete.
