## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons with two fields (colours) need a way to place either field at the printing point, and some machines need crosswise ribbon feed turned off.

**Solution:** A ribbon vibrator mechanism that can lift the ribbon to cover the printing point with either of two ribbon fields and can drop the crosswise feed at will.

**How it works:** A typebar Monarch machine has a ribbon split lengthwise into fields of different colour, such as black and red. The ribbon feeds lengthwise between side spools via bevel gears from the carriage escapement. The vibrator lifts the ribbon at each print stroke, and a selector sets which field is raised. The crosswise ribbon feed can be disabled where only a single-field ribbon is used.
