## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must stop at any column and then at any denomination within that column, which needs tabulating stops beyond the column stops.

**Solution:** Column-stop mechanism stops the carriage at a chosen column, and denominational-stop mechanism works with it, or on its own, to stop at a denominational position within the column.
