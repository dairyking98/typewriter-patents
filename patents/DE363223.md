## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Protective covers for sensitive machines are slow to use, block access, or get knocked against the device when lifted.

**Solution:** The protective box slides on the work table so it fully covers the device in one position and leaves it accessible in another.
