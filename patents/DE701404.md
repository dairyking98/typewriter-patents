## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A switchable platen bearing rattles and loses accuracy under the type impact load over time.

**Solution:** Extra support members at platen-axis height press against fixed rails opposite the type pressure, holding the platen rigid in each writing position.
