## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (automatic zero setting for writing calculators) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a novel, expedient, automatic zero setting for writing calculators, in which the setting of the zeros in the writing mechanism for each row through one moved by the arithmetic unit or the result unit, from the left device working to the right takes place.

**How it works (reading):** Each row has a pair of levers whose rear arcs are moved by the arithmetic unit from left to right; a stop lever holds the type carrier at zero, so the zero is set automatically in the writing mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Zero setting in writing calculators is handled by separate devices and only works for writing machines that print zeros.

**Solution:** Each row has a pair of levers whose rear arcs are moved by the arithmetic unit from left to right; a stop lever holds the type carrier at zero, so the zero is set automatically in the writing mechanism.
