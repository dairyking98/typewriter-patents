## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** Its aims being to attain a uniform and certain feed at high speed by controlling the moment of release of the paper-platen, to avoid the blurringr incident to movement of the paper at the instant of print-- ing, whether in feeding or by reason of irregular action of the machine, and to secure an escapement which by its action polishes its contacting surfaces.

**How it works (reading):** An escapement whose spaced member is also movable transversely, so the carriage is released only after the key is released and the carriage spring helps to lift the key and re-engage the dogs.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapements blur print by moving the paper at the instant of impression and do not feed uniformly at high speed.

**Solution:** An escapement whose spaced member is also movable transversely, so the carriage is released only after the key is released and the carriage spring helps to lift the key and re-engage the dogs.

**How it works:** A rack on the carriage meshes with a spacing dog that reciprocates one step and a detaining dog that cannot move along the feed line. Key depression swings a rocker frame so the spacing dog leaves the rack, is moved back one space, and the detaining dog holds the carriage. On release the rack swings back onto the spacing dog and the carriage steps forward. Variants include a double-rack Caligraph form and toothed-wheel racks.
