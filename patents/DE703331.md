## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (character recording, line font) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or local circuits for mosaic printer telegraph systems at the receiving end

**How it works (reading):** Recording writing levers driven by a camshaft rest on the paper in alternation, each writing a line that is widened to form the transmitted picture elements.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Line-by-line writing with a helix and edge loses contact on uneven paper and gives broken characters.

**Solution:** Recording writing levers driven by a camshaft rest on the paper in alternation, each writing a line that is widened to form the transmitted picture elements.

**Features:** [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
