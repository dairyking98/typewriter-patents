## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Holding the shift key needs a finger, slowing typists.

**Solution:** A large palm-operated shift plate on a long lever in front of the keys, so the ball of the hand shifts the carriage while all fingers stay on the keys.

**How it works:** The carriage roller runs on a rod carried by a bell-crank, linked to a long lever pivoted at the rear that projects through an opening in the front. Its front end bends up and carries a plate larger than the type keys, at key level and in front of them. Pressing the plate with the ball of the hand shifts the roller to bring a different letter row into operation.
