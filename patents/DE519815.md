## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Replacing the paper roller is hard because bearing bushes and sliding pieces can work loose, and the parts need careful machining.

**Solution:** Paper roller bearing tabs fit into slots of the bifurcated carriage side part and are released by a bolt, with a filler piece covering the fork; injection molding and simple sawing suffice.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** paper roller; bearing bracket; filler piece; carriage side part
