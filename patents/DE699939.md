## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (writing calculator)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a writing calculator.

**How it works (reading):** Driver linkage for the cross counters is controlled by a piece moved with the paper carriage, so backspacing also brings the cross counter back to the column to be corrected.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After a keying error, the cross counter jumps with the carriage and is not returned to the error column by the backspace key, so corrections are easily forgotten.

**Solution:** Driver linkage for the cross counters is controlled by a piece moved with the paper carriage, so backspacing also brings the cross counter back to the column to be corrected.

**Features:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
