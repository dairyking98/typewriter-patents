## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**How it works (reading):** A trough sunk into the machine cover holds the roll, provides braking by its weight and a sheet-metal liner, and a pin in side grooves secures it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A paper roll for a calculator is held in a cumbersome, costly external bracket and can fall out when the machine tilts.

**Solution:** A trough sunk into the machine cover holds the roll, provides braking by its weight and a sheet-metal liner, and a pin in side grooves secures it.
