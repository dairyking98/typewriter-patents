## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-bars must strike the paper at the proper alignment at the print point.

**Solution:** A guide of tubular members just forward of the impression roller holds the type-bar end between spring-loaded hard-metal balls, with adjustable spring tension.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
