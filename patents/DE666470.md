## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on paper holding rails with pressure rollers for writing office machines.

**How it works (reading):** The pressure rollers sit in recesses in the rail, so its upper edge is unobstructed and lies close on the writing sheet for exact alignment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressure rollers passing through a slot in the paper holding rail hide the alignment edge and keep the rail away from the sheet, so precise alignment is hard.

**Solution:** The pressure rollers sit in recesses in the rail, so its upper edge is unobstructed and lies close on the writing sheet for exact alignment.
