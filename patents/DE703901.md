## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an adjusting device for the keystroke by changing which counteracts the movement of the universal rail by its return spring Power on typewriters with a universal rail pressed down by the button levers.

**How it works (reading):** A separate return-spring link under the universal rail is set by a hand lever through a pivoting pressure frame, giving lighter or heavier keystroke without changing the spring tension.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Adjusting key return springs also raises the force needed for the initial key stroke.

**Solution:** A separate return-spring link under the universal rail is set by a hand lever through a pivoting pressure frame, giving lighter or heavier keystroke without changing the spring tension.
