## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator clear sign printing device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an automatic function when the computing device is reset clear sign printing device for Typewriters and the like according to patent 49o 61o.

**How it works (reading):** A sufficiently strong spring, magnet or pneumatic force member is moved into action by the paper carriage; digit-roller sensing elements hold it off unless the counter reads zero, so the clear sign prints only at zero.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The clear-sign printing that marks a zeroed counter must fire reliably, and the carriage-driven force alone is not always large enough to overcome opposing forces.

**Solution:** A sufficiently strong spring, magnet or pneumatic force member is moved into action by the paper carriage; digit-roller sensing elements hold it off unless the counter reads zero, so the clear sign prints only at zero.
