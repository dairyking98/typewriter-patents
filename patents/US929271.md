## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and has for its principal object to provide improved means.

**How it works (reading):** The feed rack is disengaged from the step-by-step feed pinion by raising its arms, and the tabulating stops and speed control act on the released carriage that is driven by a spring drum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage release and speed control must work with a tabulating mechanism, and the carriage needs to move smoothly once released.

**Solution:** The feed rack is disengaged from the step-by-step feed pinion by raising its arms, and the tabulating stops and speed control act on the released carriage that is driven by a spring drum.
