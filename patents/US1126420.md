## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The ascending type head is shifted sufficiently on impact so the two heads do not interlock, while the descending head is positively blocked from being shifted in the opposite direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Descending and ascending multiple-type heads on a type bar can interfere and jam, since an ascending head can lock with a descending one.

**Solution:** The ascending type head is shifted sufficiently on impact so the two heads do not interlock, while the descending head is positively blocked from being shifted in the opposite direction.
