## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Subject of the present invention is a type bar typewriter whose type bar housing can be folded up at the front are bulged and on top with a raised platform for punctuation keys the space key is provided.

**How it works (reading):** The type-bar housing is a spherical box folded up on a hinge on a sliding frame, with a raised platform carrying punctuation and space keys and a cut-out so the writing is freely visible.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Upright type-bar typewriters are slow to clean and ribbon-change, hide the writing, and tire the writer, and the punctuation keys give no positional reference.

**Solution:** The type-bar housing is a spherical box folded up on a hinge on a sliding frame, with a raised platform carrying punctuation and space keys and a cut-out so the writing is freely visible.
