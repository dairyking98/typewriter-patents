## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special supports, platforms or trolleys for supporting machines on tables

**How it works (reading):** Rotatable rolling elements of non-adhesive material sit at the rear of the base on the axis of the rear edge, and front support feet of rubber give the grip, so the machine can be slid and tilted without damage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Desktop office machines are hard to slide across a table, and a sharp rear base edge can scratch the table when the machine is tilted up.

**Solution:** Rotatable rolling elements of non-adhesive material sit at the rear of the base on the axis of the rear edge, and front support feet of rubber give the grip, so the machine can be slid and tilted without damage.
