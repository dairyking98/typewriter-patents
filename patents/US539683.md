## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Two-spool ribbons must be reversed at each end, needing a reversing mechanism.

**Solution:** An endless ribbon stored in folds in a receptacle on the spool shaft, drawn out and fed back in as the carriage moves.

**How it works:** On a Remington, a ratchet-driven spool shaft is stepped by the carriage spring and dog. A receptacle sits on the shaft; a spring-pressed roller holds the ribbon against the spool. The ribbon passes up from the receptacle, around under the machine in a guide, over the type-bar point, and back into the receptacle, where the surplus lies in folds. Only the length needed to circle the machine stays outside, so the ribbon always moves one way.
