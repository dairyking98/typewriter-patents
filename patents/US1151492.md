## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Record sheets must stay in the machine while bills are removed, and perforated sheets must be located and line-fed.

**Solution:** Perforated sheets are located and fed in line-space direction by feed mechanism that keeps a record sheet in the machine.
