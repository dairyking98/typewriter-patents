## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object is to produce a machine in which writing may be done upon either wide or narrow sheets, as desired, and in which all of the writtenportion ofthe page shallbe constantlyin full view of the operator.

**How it works (reading):** Paper guides are adjusted along a bar mounted over the platen outside the line of sight; the guide arms grip the paper margins and have gage arms for insertion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guides on a rod along the platen block the view of written lines, so the operator cannot see the whole page.

**Solution:** Paper guides are adjusted along a bar mounted over the platen outside the line of sight; the guide arms grip the paper margins and have gage arms for insertion.
