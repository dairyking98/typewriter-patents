## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper roller cannot be turned by hand while the line-switch drive is engaged.

**Solution:** Balls clamp the paper roller's knob to the axle through a conical ring; pressing the stub axle inward releases the balls so the knob and roller turn freely, with cranks providing the release.
