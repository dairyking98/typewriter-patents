## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Visible-writing typebar machines need the whole printing line and the matter above it to be visible without lifting anything, and need simpler typebars, shifting and key action.

**Solution:** Two groups of horizontally swinging typebars, each with three characters in a vertical line, are mounted on vertical rock-shafts in a stepped, sloping arrangement beside the platen; a holder carrying all rock-shafts is shifted up or down to pick the character row.

**How it works:** Each key lever pulls a flat link that rocks a vertical shaft through an elongated crank, swinging a rigid arm and typebar a quarter turn horizontally to the platen. The shafts are in two groups, one each side of the printing point, with arms of graduated length and graduated heights so the arms nest in a sloping stair against a felt-lined shield, leaving the printing line and paper clear. Shorter arms are weighted so every type strikes with equal momentum, and a forked link with lost motion equalises key pressure. All shafts sit in one holder that slides vertically in a frame opening; the Cap key releases it to descend (weight, balanced by springs) so the upper character prints, and the Fig key raises it through a shifting shaft to print the lower character, with the normal position printing the central lowercase. A universal bar driven by cams on all keys and the space bar moves a yoke that trips the carriage feed dogs.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)

**Parts:** frame; key lever; fulcrum rod; connecting link; forked link; rock-shaft; crank; type-bearing arm; typebar with three characters; weight; vertically sliding holder; shield with felt abutment; Cap shifting key; Fig shifting key; shifting shaft; balance spring; universal bar; spacing bar; yoke; feed dog; platen; ribbon carrier

**Numbers:** three characters per typebar; fourteen typebars per group; two groups; quarter-circle swing of each arm

**Improves on:**

- contemporary visible-writing typebar machines: stepped sloping horizontal typebars below the line leave line and paper visible, with a whole-holder shift instead of platen shift
