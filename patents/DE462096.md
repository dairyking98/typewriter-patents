## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A pressure plate that lies over all key levers and raises all typebars at once with one hand, leaving the other hand free to slide in a protective sheet.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cleaning typebar types normally takes two people or great skill: one depresses all key levers while the other slides a protective sheet under the raised typebars.

**Solution:** A pressure plate that lies over all key levers and raises all typebars at once with one hand, leaving the other hand free to slide in a protective sheet.

**How it works:** A stepped keyboard pressure plate is laid on the keys to match the keyboard layout and pressed with one hand, lifting every typebar. With the other hand the user slides a bent sheet of paper or special cleaning pad under the raised typebars and relieves the plate so they rest on it. Another sheet in front of the type segment keeps the delicate parts clean. Keys not tied to typebars (tab, shift, back-space, margin release) are left out. A simpler plate behind the top key row also works. The plate also serves as a lock against unauthorised typing.
