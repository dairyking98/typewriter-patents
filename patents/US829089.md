**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** Tapered blocks on the guide rod or stem of the shift-frame, engaged by a guide roller in a vertically slotted standard, take up lateral play.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage shift-frame must be held laterally steady on its guide rod, and play in the guide causes misalignment of the platen with the type-bars.

**Solution:** Tapered blocks on the guide rod or stem of the shift-frame, engaged by a guide roller in a vertically slotted standard, take up lateral play.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
