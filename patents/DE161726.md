## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Couple the lock release to the line-space lever itself, so a deliberate extra push of the lever frees the platen and the next normal stroke automatically re-engages the lock.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The device that lifts the line-space ratchet lock so the platen can be turned freely is independent of the line-space lever, so it is easy to leave disengaged, causing inaccurate line feed.

**Solution:** Couple the lock release to the line-space lever itself, so a deliberate extra push of the lever frees the platen and the next normal stroke automatically re-engages the lock.

**How it works:** The platen shaft carries a ratchet wheel engaged by a feed pawl and a spring-loaded locking roller. Moving the shift lever past its rest position against a fixed stop makes a nose on the roller arm slip out of the path of a nose on the lever hub, retracting the locking roller. The platen can then be turned either way by the hand wheel. Moving the lever in the normal direction again brings the noses back and the roller drops into a tooth gap. The lever position shows whether the lock is off.
