## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing type cleaners require removing the ribbon or cleaning the type low in the basket, where access is poor.

**Solution:** A cleaning device fitted laterally and centrally to the ribbon plate pushes the ribbon aside, so a brush can reach the raised type and clean it along its length, with no handling of the ribbon.

**Features:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
