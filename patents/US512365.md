## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage mainspring pulls unevenly between wound and unwound positions, making the carriage work unevenly and noisily.

**Solution:** A compensating spiral spring with lever and cord evens the carriage pull, with ribbon movement driven directly from the key levers.

**How it works:** A typebar machine with a bank of finger keys, each lever carrying a two-type bar on an oscillating segment, prints on top of a cylindrical platen that reciprocates. A rack and pinion joins carriage and escapement, and a spiral spring acting through a lever and cord, in place of a scroll spring in a drum, drives the carriage with near constant pull. A ribbon movement bar across the key levers vibrates the ribbon backward more than upward. A variable space bar, secondary stop-regulating bar, shift key, bell trip and line spacing are also described.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** key lever; typebar; cylindrical platen; spiral spring; carriage cord; rack and pinion; ribbon movement bar; variable space bar; bell trip
