## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a particularly advantageous embodiment such type lever.

**How it works (reading):** Type levers with two intersecting, curved arms joined by an articulated steering rod are arranged in two vertical parallel planes, grouped by identical mounting; the key lever lifts each type head off the ink pad and onto the paper roller and returns it on release.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers with crossing arms in one row are hard to arrange and store in front of the paper roller, and the existing typewriters with visible writing need a better layout.

**Solution:** Type levers with two intersecting, curved arms joined by an articulated steering rod are arranged in two vertical parallel planes, grouped by identical mounting; the key lever lifts each type head off the ink pad and onto the paper roller and returns it on release.
