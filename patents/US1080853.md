## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pneumatic typewriters needed the type-wheel to reach the chosen type position reliably, with a fixed speed ratio between the wheel and the air distributor.

**Solution:** A distributor cylinder with orifices receives air from pipes arranged around a section of a circle, and a ratchet wheel geared to the type-wheel gives a speed ratio equal to full circle over that section, so the type-wheel stops at the selected type when the arms meet abutments.
