## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** If the operator misses the end-of-line bell, matter gets crowded at the line end and must be erased.

**Solution:** A locking bar under the key levers is tripped by the carriage at the bell point so that no key can be depressed, with a setting lever to release it when a word or part-word needs adding.

**How it works:** A bar with adjustable metal heads is pivoted by trunnions beneath all key levers and has a crank arm joined to a spring latch rod. The latch rod works in a wire guide and rests on a keeper at the frame top. When the carriage trip dog, which also rings the bell, strikes the rod it swings the bar up under the key levers and blocks them. A setting lever lets the operator see whether more can be added and manipulates the attachment to admit extra matter. It is adjustable to fit different machines, shown on a Caligraph.
