## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The spacing device must work without moving the ribbon, but in known designs the ribbon and guide are lifted and dropped at the same time as the space-key.

**Solution:** A lever on the universal bar with a slotted rod lets the spacing pawl be operated by the space-key without moving the ribbon-lever mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** universal bar; letter keys; spacing pawl; rod with slotted end; bell-crank lever; ribbon mechanism
