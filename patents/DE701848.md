## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method for fastening type bodies with only one fastening tab (type tail) on the bumpers for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Type body is aligned in a jig and tensioned to the correct angle with clearance, then the single tab is soldered to the bumper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Bumper type bodies with one fastening tab are hard to riveted accurately because holes must match and placement is imprecise.

**Solution:** Type body is aligned in a jig and tensioned to the correct angle with clearance, then the single tab is soldered to the bumper.
