## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage movement limits must be adjusted quickly and easily for different line lengths, with a warning near the line end.

**Solution:** Toothed margin stops tilt out of mesh with a fixed rack for sliding, and one stop carries an alarm bell struck by pins on the carriage.

**How it works:** A toothed rack on the frame carries two margin stops G and N whose teeth mesh with the rack. Pressing the stop's finger piece tilts its teeth free so it slides along the rack to a new position, and a lip prevents over-tilting. A striker on the carriage hits the stops to limit travel but the stops can be raised for travel beyond. Pins on the carriage engage a bell clapper on stop N just before line end, and a spring returns it so it rings. Bell spring tension is adjusted by turning the bell about its screw.
