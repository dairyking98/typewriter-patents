## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines, and more particularly to type bar and hanger constructions for use in visible or front-strike machines and to the manner of mounting the hangers in the machine.

**How it works (reading):** The segment and hangers are secured from the front of the machine, with adjusting means reached from the front so single hangers can be removed, and the segment acts as a dust shield for the bearings.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar bearings must be adjusted, and individual hangers removed, without dismounting major parts of a visible machine.

**Solution:** The segment and hangers are secured from the front of the machine, with adjusting means reached from the front so single hangers can be removed, and the segment acts as a dust shield for the bearings.
