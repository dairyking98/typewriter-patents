In particular, come pivotable platen rollers in such machines in use where it it is important to use a large number of writing sheets at the same time, regardless of whether these are connected at their side edges or not. A space appears. This is done in such a way that when swiveling the platen this device comes into action to advance special collection sheet independently of the other sheets.
