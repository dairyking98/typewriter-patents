## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** Auxiliary feed rollers grip the collection sheet only when the platen is raised, and advance it one way between letters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Swiveling platens let carbon sheets separate, but collection sheets are not advanced between letters.

**Solution:** Auxiliary feed rollers grip the collection sheet only when the platen is raised, and advance it one way between letters.

A space appears. This is done in such a way that when swiveling the platen this device comes into action to advance special collection sheet independently of the other sheets.
