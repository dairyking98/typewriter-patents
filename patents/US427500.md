## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The earlier reciprocating ribbon guide of Cash's US372602 moved the ribbon sideways to cover and uncover the print spot with too much friction and complexity.

**Solution:** A ribbon guide on a swinging arm pivoted on a post, linked by a rod and crank to a rock bar under the keys, so any key press carries the ribbon over the print point and release swings it clear.

**How it works:** A rock bar lies under the inner ends of all key levers and is joined to a rock shaft with a crank arm. A rod connects the crank to a ribbon-guide arm that is pivoted on an upright post of the frame and extends along the front of the machine in the direction the ribbon runs. The ribbon is threaded through loops at the arm's outer end, which also carries an index showing where the type will strike. Pressing a key rocks the bar, swings the guide outward and carries the ribbon over the print spot so the typebar strikes through it onto the paper; releasing the key swings the ribbon back, uncovering the printed character so it can be read.
