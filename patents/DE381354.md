## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (office ribbon printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Letterpress printing apparatus specially adapted for office or other commercial purposes with flat printing surfaces, e.g. with flat type-beds, surfaces made of thin sheet material or moulded from plastics or rubber

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon printing machine with a directly driven Set carriage and above the same immovably mounted, but adjustable Printing roller and the purpose of such a design of printing machines of this type that the needs of the office are met in a special way.

**How it works (reading):** The type carriage runs between an upper and lower roller in a roller frame with detachable non-load guides.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Office printers with type carriages needed heavy guides, which raised cost and size.

**Solution:** The type carriage runs between an upper and lower roller in a roller frame with detachable non-load guides.
