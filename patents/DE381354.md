**How it works (reading):** The type carriage runs between an upper and lower roller in a roller frame with detachable non-load guides.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Office printers with type carriages needed heavy guides, which raised cost and size.

**Solution:** The type carriage runs between an upper and lower roller in a roller frame with detachable non-load guides.
