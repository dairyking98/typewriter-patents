If, on the other hand, such facilities are missing on these type cleaners, so the cleaning compound widens during re-smoothing due to the known rolling on smooth surfaces, so that they are subsequently pushed back by a special process must become. These disadvantages are avoided according to the invention in that the Edge disks of the cleaning compound made of flexible material, e.g. B. rubber, consist, such that the cleaning compound when re-smoothing by rolling on a cannot widen the flat surface.
