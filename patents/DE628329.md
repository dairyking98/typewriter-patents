## Summary

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to type cleaner with between two edge discs held doughy-sticky cleaning compound.

**How it works (reading):** The cleaning compound is held between two flexible rubber edge discs that press on the pad and act as a dam, so the compound cannot spread; the discs can be oval with a fixed handle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cleaning compound on existing type cleaners widens when re-smoothed on a pad, and returning it needs kneading rollers that squeeze it out unevenly.

**Solution:** The cleaning compound is held between two flexible rubber edge discs that press on the pad and act as a dam, so the compound cannot spread; the discs can be oval with a fixed handle.

**How it works:** Flexible edge discs contact the smoothing pad firmly, keeping the doughy compound contained while it is rolled back smooth.
