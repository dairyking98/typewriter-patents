## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter table that is pulled out and lowered in a cupboard jams with the usual groove guides and needs counterweights or springs.

**Solution:** A lever linkage with toothed strips and pawls raises and lowers the machine support plate to any height, with no counterweights or springs.
