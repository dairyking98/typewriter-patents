## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Metal typewriter covers are in the way when not on the machine and scar furniture.

**Solution:** A flexible accordion-plaited cover that folds down on the base and, when in use, its two dome sections are drawn together over the typewriter.

**How it works:** A rectangular body folds down on the base; two dome sections, each with light frames and a spring clamp, swing inward to close over the machine.
