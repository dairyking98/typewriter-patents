## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboards need more speed, case and accuracy, with a clear split between letter-keys and figure-keys.

**Solution:** The keyboard uses three banks of letter-keys numbered 1 to 30, a rear row of figure-keys 31 to 38, and four supplementary keys at the ends of the space-bar, with a universal keyboard arrangement.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
