## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulation key on a typewriter keypad requires long holds and fatigue to skip columns.

**Solution:** An additional key in the arithmetic key area is operated with the palm, and a rolling wall control retracts it automatically.

**How it works:** Extra tabulate key placed in the arithmetic key area, operable with the hand; spring-loaded and retracted by the rolling wall cover.
