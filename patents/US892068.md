## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage to the start of a line needs a heavy pull and a hard return stroke.

**Solution:** A cord-coupled piston in an air cylinder, controlled by a solenoid valve, returns the carriage with about the force of a key press.
