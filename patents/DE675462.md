## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (textile pattern decomposition device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Methods or devices concerned with designing or making patterns, not provided for in other groups of this subclass

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the invention is to create a decomposition device, which makes it possible the textile pattern constantly under the microscope to keep and continue working without interruption.

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter-type printing device on a table, actuated by a foot-operated type lever, records the weave crossings so both hands stay free.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Textile pattern decomposition by hand strains the eyes and forces the draftsman to look away from the work.

**Solution:** A typewriter-type printing device on a table, actuated by a foot-operated type lever, records the weave crossings so both hands stay free.
