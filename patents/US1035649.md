## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section; Rollers or like rotary separators separating from the bottom of pile

**How it works (reading):** A frame on the typewriter carries a feed roller and gate that feed a sheet into the machine when the platen rotates and discharge it after writing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets must be fed to the typewriter each time the platen turns and discharged after writing.

**Solution:** A frame on the typewriter carries a feed roller and gate that feed a sheet into the machine when the platen rotates and discharge it after writing.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
