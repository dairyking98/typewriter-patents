## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mounting or connecting key buttons on or to key levers

**How it works (reading):** The rubber key body is vulcanized onto the metal key carrier in one moulding operation, so the key shape and attachment are formed together and the lettering is part of the rubber.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keytops made of rubber are glued or clamped, which costs labor and the attachment can loosen.

**Solution:** The rubber key body is vulcanized onto the metal key carrier in one moulding operation, so the key shape and attachment are formed together and the lettering is part of the rubber.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [How the element is made](../features/elem-element-construction.md)
