## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon lifting device, in particular for power-driven Typewriters in which, in a known manner, the movement of the universal rail on the ribbon fork transferring drive lever consists of two parts, which by a spring are connected to each other.

**How it works (reading):** The drive lever is two one-armed levers on a common axis, drawn together by a helical coil spring hooked in their bores and limited by lobes, so the spring holds them with no adjustment and is easy to replace.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A two-part ribbon lifting drive lever with a torsion spring needs adjustment and readjustment, and a broken spring is hard to replace.

**Solution:** The drive lever is two one-armed levers on a common axis, drawn together by a helical coil spring hooked in their bores and limited by lobes, so the spring holds them with no adjustment and is easy to replace.
