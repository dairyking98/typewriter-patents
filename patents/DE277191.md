## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a device for moving the ribbon fork for typewriters, in which the stroke of the ribbon fork can be changed when writing in two different colors or switching off the ribbon fork stroke for writing wax paper stencils.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon fork hub is driven by the normal type-lever bridge for ordinary characters, and by a transmission from the accent keys for accents.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon fork stroke must change for two colours and for accents written with dead keys.

**Solution:** The ribbon fork hub is driven by the normal type-lever bridge for ordinary characters, and by a transmission from the accent keys for accents.
