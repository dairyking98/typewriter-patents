## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Preventing rebound or clash of levers or type members

**How it works (reading):** A thin wire is set into holes in the type bar guide cover, just proud of its face, so the bars land on the wire and do not strike the cover.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type bars hitting the felt-covered guide cover when they returned made annoying noise, and felt was costly to fit.

**Solution:** A thin wire is set into holes in the type bar guide cover, just proud of its face, so the bars land on the wire and do not strike the cover.
