## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines, and particularly to mechanism for facilitating the adjustment and proper alinement of the type-bar mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Aliners on the type-bar pivot plate are adjusted so the type is held in the writing position, with the adjustment made at the point where the type strikes the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar alinement at the writing point is hard to adjust, so the types do not sit exactly in the print position.

**Solution:** Aliners on the type-bar pivot plate are adjusted so the type is held in the writing position, with the adjustment made at the point where the type strikes the platen.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
