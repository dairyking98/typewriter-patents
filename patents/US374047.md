## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Further additions and improvements to the author's index typewriter in frame, carriage, escapement and paper spacing.

**Solution:** A hinged light carriage on a rack-and-ratchet bar carries an index plate, plunger and key lever, stepped by a box-spring escapement.

**How it works:** A rigid board-and-rail frame holds a paper roller linked by bevel gears to a ratchet shaft for line spacing, and a second roller opens to insert paper. A bar with rack on one side and ratchet on the other carries the carriage, which hinges on it for inspection of the work. A box spring with an annular rack meshes with the bar's rack and, released step by step, moves the carriage. The top plate holds the index plate and a spring-raised plunger worked by the key lever, while the bottom plate holds an inking pad and a circular track.
