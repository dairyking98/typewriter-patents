## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Standard keyboards split the keys into separated circles, forcing far-apart reaches and fatigue.

**Solution:** Keys are split into two hand fields in staggered straight rows, one row per finger, with a raised space block.
