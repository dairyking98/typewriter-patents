## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hammond-type machine with uneven hammer blows and poor inking.

**Solution:** Hammond variant: an inking roller arm swings over the character at the impression point, plus a uniform escapement resetting a spring-actuated hammer for even blows.

**How it works:** Oscillating type segments are positioned by finger keys. A light arm with a felt roller swings up before the hammer blow and inks the type about to print. Two spring barrels each drive a rack: one carriage with a differential escapement, another a uniform escapement that resets the spring hammer.

**Features:** [ELEM:sector-segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [IMP:spring-energy](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** type segment; inking roller arm; impression hammer; spring barrel; rack; carriage; uniform escapement; differential escapement; key-lever

**Improves on:**

- Hammond typewriter: adds roller inking and spring hammer with uniform blow
