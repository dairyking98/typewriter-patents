## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The purposes of this invention is to minimize the necessary number of manipulations of the space bar and simplify and facilitate the production of accurate tabulated work.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulator or jumper key carries the carriage to the right column directly, so fewer space-bar operations are needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulated amounts need many space-bar presses after each tabulator stop, which is tiring and wears the machine.

**Solution:** A tabulator or jumper key carries the carriage to the right column directly, so fewer space-bar operations are needed.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)
