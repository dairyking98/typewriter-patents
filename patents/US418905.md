## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable punch-head carries the characters on its periphery and is turned to select one; a lever depresses it to punch the check, and the feed is driven positively by the punching arm with no clamp.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier check-punches were not simple or easy to use, and the check feed needed a clamp.

**Solution:** A rotatable punch-head carries the characters on its periphery and is turned to select one; a lever depresses it to punch the check, and the feed is driven positively by the punching arm with no clamp.
