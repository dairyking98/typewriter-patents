## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Eraser dust falls into the typewriter, causing untidy appearance and wear on the mechanism, and the rest of the sheet gets rubbed during erasing.

**Solution:** A guard is placed over the platen with a slot at the erasing point, so only the erased spot is exposed, and a catch collects the particles removed by the eraser.
