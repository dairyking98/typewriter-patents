## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Sheets of paper must be fed singly from a pack at regular intervals, for different thicknesses.

**Solution:** A friction roller bearing on a pack of sheets pressed against a platform individualizes and discharges one sheet at a time, with adjustment for thickness.

**How it works:** The pack is pressed by a carriage against a friction roller, which indents the pack so the front sheets bulge, while friction of the platform detains the edges. A jar or push makes the front sheet spring away from the pack, and the corrugated-rubber-covered roller draws it out over the platform edge. A detention-plate adjusted by shafts regulates sheet escape and accommodates different thicknesses, including envelopes. Shown connected to the feed rollers of a ruling machine.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** platform; friction roller; carriage; detention plate; adjusting shaft; corrugated rubber sleeve
