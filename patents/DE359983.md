## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-spacing setting levers sit among other levers, can be knocked or removed, and are hard to read.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded clutch on the shift lever shaft, with a nose snapping into numbered grooves, sets the line spacing using only three parts.

**How it works:** Raising the shift lever moves a pawl lever that turns the ratchet wheel of the platen until the lever's extension hits the clutch handle. A spring on the shaft returns the lever to a stop pin. Pressing the clutch aside against a compression spring pulls its nose out of one groove; turning it slightly lets it snap into another groove numbered 1, 2 or 3, changing the stroke and the line distance.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** shift lever; clutch; compression spring; stop pin; pawl; ratchet wheel; grooved bearing

**Numbers:** three line-space settings

**Improves on:**

- stop on the adjusting lever designs: the stop is on the clutch handle so it cannot be knocked or lost
