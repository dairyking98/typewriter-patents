## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter with simpler type levers, and carriage drive with settable spacing, set-back and ribbon motion.

**Solution:** A typebar machine whose paper cylinder slide is driven by a mainspring barrel and alternating pawls, with adjustable tension and set-back stops.

**How it works:** Key arms strike a common center through quadrant-guided type levers loosely joined to buttons by spring-return key arms. The paper cylinder carriage slides on rods and is pushed by a rack on a frame geared to a spring barrel. Two alternating pawls on the barrel ratchet advance the slide one letter space per stroke; the mainspring tension is changeable and the cylinder can be set back more than one space. An adjustable stop lets the barrel stop at any point, and the ribbon is moved by a rod. Text is truncated.
