## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in devices for perforating or punching checks and other negotiable or valuable papers to insure against the amount or other matter therein being altered without detection;

**How it works (reading):** A hand check punch with a frame that serves as handle and support, and a die-wheel on a journaled fork that carries the punch characters across the check on a carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing check punches were costly, so small merchants lacked an affordable punch with effective characters.

**Solution:** A hand check punch with a frame that serves as handle and support, and a die-wheel on a journaled fork that carries the punch characters across the check on a carriage.
