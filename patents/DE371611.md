## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Existing manuscript and shorthand holders cannot be adjusted for lighting or workplace and do not allow the block to be read line to line.

**Solution:** A clamp with a rotatable support arm and a holder board with a bevelled rear edge, letting the block lie flat so pages turn freely and the text is readable from the first line to the last.
