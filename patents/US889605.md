## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing machines need line spacing and the amounts written on a strip or sheet.

**Solution:** A platen frame carries a line-spacing mechanism with a feed for a paper strip wound on a coil. The strip is fed forward or backward for line spacing on the machine.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** platen frame on carriage; line-space mechanism; strip feed; coil supply
