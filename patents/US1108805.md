## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to chines and is particularly related to v mechanism by which the feed or pressure rolls, which usually bear against the platen in such machines, may be movedto and from said platen.

**How it works (reading):** A rock shaft beneath the platen, turned by a handle, casts the rear and front feed rolls off the platen by tappets that engage the rock arms.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feed or pressure rolls must be moved to and from the platen on an Underwood machine where the type strikes the front of the platen.

**Solution:** A rock shaft beneath the platen, turned by a handle, casts the rear and front feed rolls off the platen by tappets that engage the rock arms.
