## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The universal bar on a stenotype key action has a long, forceful stroke with a pounding effect.

**Solution:** A universal bar operated by key levers near their fulcrums, with an engaging pad mounted so it can be adjusted or renewed.
