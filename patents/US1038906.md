## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Detaching the carriage from the machine disturbs the ribbon and lifting spring, and the carriage guide wears under the carriage weight.

**Solution:** A spring-sustained auxiliary frame with a roller carries part of the carriage weight, and the ribbon vibrator is supported so it shifts with the carriage without being mounted on the shift frame.

**How it works:** The carriage rides on ball-bearing guides on a shift frame hinged at the rear by adjustable pivots; a cam lever shifts it. Under the shift frame a pivoted auxiliary frame, hinged on the same axis, carries a roller that bears against the underside of the carriage. A pair of tension springs, adjustable by a slotted plate and screw, give the roller its lifting force. The ribbon vibrator reciprocates in an upright guide driven from the universal bar, and a central line gage shifts with the frame.
