## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Inventor wants an improved manipulation and action for typewriters whose types are all on one common arm (a single slotted type-bar), including paper holding, line feed and ribbon feed.

**Solution:** A single slotted type-bar with all characters along its lower edge slides on a tablet; an index finger drops into a notched rack bar beside a reversed character chart to choose the character, and pressing the bar prints it against a ridge-shaped printing block and spaces the tablet one notch.

**How it works:** The type-bar is pivoted at a long slot to a tablet that is itself hinged and slides laterally on a rod across the machine. Beside the bar the tablet carries a chart of the characters in reverse order and a notched rack bar; an index finger on the type-bar is set in the notch beside the wanted character, which brings that type over a ridge-shaped printing block. Pressing the bar down prints, compressing a spring; on release the spring lifts the bar and a pawl steps into the next tooth of a rack bar under the tablet, moving the tablet and type-bar one space sideways. A separate lever works the pawl for blank spaces. At line end the tablet is pushed back and a lever with ratchet turns the feed roller one line; a lever raises or lowers pressure rollers on the paper with a spring-set pressure. A ribbon in a cylindrical box runs over a glass plate and under a roller whose ratchet is advanced by a stirrup the type-bar depresses.
