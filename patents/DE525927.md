## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The spring-loaded pawl that disconnects the ribbon drive on carriage return stays partly engaged and grinds the ratchet teeth, making noise and wear.

**Solution:** A wire ring spring fits in an annular groove on the drive gear and presses the pawl out of the teeth against a pin on the spring housing when the carriage returns.
