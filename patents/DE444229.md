## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (soldering process) · **Subject (patent classes):** Preliminary treatment of work or areas to be soldered, e.g. in respect of a galvanic coating; Tubes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the Inside the metal pieces at the soldering point a closed, but arranges melting chamber connected to the solder joint, in which the vapors produced when the solder melts come under tension and thereby pressing the liquid solder into the solder or connection joint.

**How it works (reading):** A closed melting chamber at the joint holds the solder, and its vapour pressure forces the melt into the joint.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Soldered plug-in joints in bicycle frames waste solder and leave excess solder outside.

**Solution:** A closed melting chamber at the joint holds the solder, and its vapour pressure forces the melt into the joint.
