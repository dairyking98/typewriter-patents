## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** A plate with parallel slots carries a guide device that advances the sheet line by line through a slot, with side clips for rods or folders; a guide rail with sliding pieces clamps the plate to the machine carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shorthand or manuscript notes placed behind or beside a typewriter are hard to read, and earlier holders were either single rollers or plates that fit only special carriages.

**Solution:** A plate with parallel slots carries a guide device that advances the sheet line by line through a slot, with side clips for rods or folders; a guide rail with sliding pieces clamps the plate to the machine carriage.
