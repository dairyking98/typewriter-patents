## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A key-lever typewriter that needs little material and work yet gives sharp print, fast action and long life.

**Solution:** Key levers and typebars are coupled by toothed arcs and seated in slots of a single cast segment frame, whose cross grooves at different angles hold the levers in place against each other.

**How it works:** A circular-segment cast frame has slots for key levers and for typebars, with cross grooves for their pivot pins set at an angle so each pair supports the other. Toothed arcs at the meeting ends couple each key to its typebar, with arc radii chosen so all keys move equally. Radial slot extensions guide light typebars sideways as they swing to the print point. The paper carrier, ribbon feed, spacing regulation and a perforated guide plate are described in later figures.
