## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** A setup plate with set-up pins aligns the sheets, and a marking device on it, linked to the staple slide, marks the line being used when the sheets are stapled.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets of different widths for copy work cannot be aligned line by line, and the line being written on cannot be marked, so the same line gets written on more than once.

**Solution:** A setup plate with set-up pins aligns the sheets, and a marking device on it, linked to the staple slide, marks the line being used when the sheets are stapled.
