## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a card holder for typewriters, consisting of a pair of separate ones extending across the writing lines and after at the back of the paper roller to curved paper guide finger and a scale rail.

**How it works (reading):** One paper guide finger sits beside the type-lever passage and the other, set further away, folds down so the written line is clear; both are carried on a vertically adjustable scale rail with curved edges that hold each finger in its end positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A fixed paper guide finger blocks the view of the written line, and paper edges catch on the guides when writing on cards.

**Solution:** One paper guide finger sits beside the type-lever passage and the other, set further away, folds down so the written line is clear; both are carried on a vertically adjustable scale rail with curved edges that hold each finger in its end positions.

**Parts:** scale rail; fixed and foldable paper guide fingers; arms on the ribbon lift frame
