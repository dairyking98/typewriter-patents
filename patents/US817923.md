## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for adjusting, guiding, and feeding the paper around the cylindrical platens of type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** The usual deflector plate under the platen is curved and separated from it, and when the pressure rollers are released a passage is left for the leading edge; carbon and record sheets are held stationary against the back of the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A sheet cannot easily be inserted backward from the front of the platen, especially when other sheets are already in the machine, and carbon sheets move during insertion.

**Solution:** The usual deflector plate under the platen is curved and separated from it, and when the pressure rollers are released a passage is left for the leading edge; carbon and record sheets are held stationary against the back of the platen.
