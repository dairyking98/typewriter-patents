## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Bank and accounting machines needed printing that could be switched to one print, two prints, or none on each inserted document, and the existing controls were complicated and failure-prone.

**Solution:** Document sensing and cam discs set by business-type key banks engage or disengage the print hammers, so each document gets one print, two prints, or none.
