## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are expensive, and ordinary metal or wood type cannot easily be used to print addresses and cards.

**Solution:** A ruler on a bed-plate with a sliding guide-plate and scale; type is placed in the right angle between the ruler and guide flange to print with a hand-type setup.
