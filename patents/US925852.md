## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type bars and "more particularly to type bars used in connection with writing machines and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A type bar with a movable section carries characters that strike two platens together, so each copy is struck with equal force.
