## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**How it works (reading):** The platen core tube has only metal stub axles running in sound-absorbing bearing bushes, so the vibration is damped at the bearings and does not reach the frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen resonance transmits clinking vibrations through metal axles and bearings, adding noise to the hard type blows.

**Solution:** The platen core tube has only metal stub axles running in sound-absorbing bearing bushes, so the vibration is damped at the bearings and does not reach the frame.
