## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbon holders needed a simple way to hold the ribbon at the platen, reverse its winding and regulate spool tension.

**Solution:** A framed two-spool ribbon holder with a hinged, spring-held ribbon arm, pawl-and-ratchet winding with a sliding reversing dog, and an adjustable brake plate for spool tension.

**How it works:** Upper and lower spools sit on shafts in a frame, and the ribbon runs over flanged rollers to a hinged arm that holds it, doubled, against the platen roller. Pressing a horn on the arm throws it up to expose the printing. A key lever or a link to the key-levers moves a pawl on a ratchet disk to wind the ribbon. A sliding dog wedged between pawl and the other ratchet disk switches winding to the other spool and reverses the ribbon. A spring brake plate with a ratchet-bar lever presses on spool hubs to set tension and hold the shafts in notches.
