## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants a typewriter whose actuating parts are simple.

**Solution:** Key spindle with rack drives a pinion and rock-shaft to throw a type-lever that carries a rubber type with an ink pad.

**How it works:** A key spindle with rack teeth turns a pinion; the spindle rests on a spring arm on a rock-shaft. The type lever hangs on two pivoted arms, so it swings through a circular path to the paper cylinder. A spring makes the blow elastic and withdraws the type. The type is cast india-rubber in a wooden head, inked on a porous pad. The cylinder is on a triangular shaft moved by rack and one-toothed pinion.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Rubber type](../features/elem-rubber-type.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** key; key spindle; pinion; type lever; arm; rock-shaft; type head; rubber type; ink pad; cylinder; triangular shaft
