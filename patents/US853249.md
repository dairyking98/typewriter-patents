## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewrit-ing niachines in which, in addition to the usual line-spacing mechanism for the revoluble platen, there is provided an auxiliary mechanism usually at one end of the platen, for feeding a paper ribbon around the" laten E independently of the latter, or.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment with a detachable spool holder for the paper strip feeds it line by line by a pawl whose throw is adjustable, and can be moved into the printing field without line-spacing the strip.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bill amounts must be recorded on a paper strip wound round a stationary platen, and existing machines do not allow the strip spool to be detached, unwound and fed easily.

**Solution:** An attachment with a detachable spool holder for the paper strip feeds it line by line by a pawl whose throw is adjustable, and can be moved into the printing field without line-spacing the strip.
