## Summary

**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My first object is to provide a device suitable for attachment to type-writers most ordinarily provided with indicators, (such as the Remington,) which shall at a glance indicate to the operator, when the platen-frame is swung upwardly on its hinge, precisely the point on the paper where a letter will be impressed by the type-bar at the next pressure of a key.

**How it works (reading):** A combined position indicator and card holder attached over the platen at the print point.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a Remington the operator cannot tell which letter-space will print next, and cards cannot be held flat at the edges.

**Solution:** A combined position indicator and card holder attached over the platen at the print point.

**How it works:** An attachment mounted near the print point shows exactly where the next typebar will strike when the platen frame is lifted, and holds a card or sheet flat against the platen so the top and bottom edges can be printed.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** indicator; paper holder; platen frame; spacing rule

**Improves on:**

- Remington spacing rules and paper guides: single device shows print point and holds card
