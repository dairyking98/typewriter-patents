## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Metal types are costly and glass, wood, hard rubber and celluloid types have been found unsuitable for strength or cost.

**Solution:** Printing types are made wholly or partly of ceramic material such as porcelain, shaped by casting or pressing and fired hard, solid or hollow, with letter profiles etched and faces ground; partly ceramic types may carry a low metal letter layer on ceramic shafts.
