**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to avoid the extra or special movement of the hand and the additional touch of the finger to make the word-space after the word is printed.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must make an extra hand movement and extra finger touch on the space bar to separate words.

**Solution:** A tilting frame carrying touch-bars between split keys, depressed together with the last letter of a word, makes the escapement dog move two teeth instead of one.

**How it works:** A polygonal frame pivots on a horizontal rod as a first-class lever, surrounding the keys, with touch-bars lying in the splits between keys at its front. A key can be pressed alone, or the finger extended onto a touch-bar tilts the frame as well. Tilting the frame, through a connecting piece, turns a movable stop out of the way of the loose spacing dog, whose own stop is adjusted to allow two teeth of advance. Two ratchet teeth then pass the dogs, which gives a word space with the last letter. A clamp-screw slot adjusts the stop holder.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** tilting frame; touch-bar; split key; pivot rod; loose spacing dog; movable stop; stop holder; connecting piece; ratchet bar

**Numbers:** two ratchet teeth for a word space

**Improves on:**

- separate space bar: word space is made with the final letter of the word
