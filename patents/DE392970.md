> Description Translated from German Key lever bearings for typewriters. The invention relates to on the storage of the keys on typewriters, especially those of the type in which the key levers with the intermediate levers of the type lever mechanism through a Pull rod are connected and arranged below the key plate through Axes are stored. In general, the keys and key levers are continuous Longitudinal axes mounted, which are attached to the side panels of the typewriter are. In the case of the key machines, this often leads to the disadvantage that the axes bend and that the keys are not even in height. To this one To eliminate the problem, the key levers according to the invention with their storage attached directly to the key guide plate, as shown in particular in Fig. i can be seen. This results in the great advantage for assembly and repair, that the whole key guide plate with all keys and key levers as one Pieces attached or removed and put together or repaired by themselves can.
