## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on the storage of the keys on typewriters, especially those of the type in which the key levers with the intermediate levers of the type lever mechanism through a Pull rod are connected and arranged below the key plate through Axes are stored.

**How it works (reading):** Each key lever bearing is cast as a block on the underside of the key guide plate, so the whole plate with keys comes off as one unit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key levers on continuous axles bend and leave keys uneven in height.

**Solution:** Each key lever bearing is cast as a block on the underside of the key guide plate, so the whole plate with keys comes off as one unit.
