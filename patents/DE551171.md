## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for typewriters to secure the writing limit at the lower edge of the sheet of paper.

**How it works (reading):** A self-contained closed box holds the drive and an eccentric axle couples to any paper-roller axis; an internal-tooth gear and an adjustable stop limit the advance and lock the line position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The known paper-roller line-lock devices work only with specific machines and must be fixed to the roller axis.

**Solution:** A self-contained closed box holds the drive and an eccentric axle couples to any paper-roller axis; an internal-tooth gear and an adjustable stop limit the advance and lock the line position.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
