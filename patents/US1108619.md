## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must roll evenly at any speed onto a small or large spool.

**Solution:** A reel with inner actuating disk, pawl and spring-loaded balls gives a positive drive that holds even tension as the spool grows.

**How it works:** Inner disk with pawl and ratchet ring drives the spool; balls and spring set the friction.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
