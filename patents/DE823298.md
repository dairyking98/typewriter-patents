## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjustable-step carriage escapements engage the pawls less deeply as the pawl swings farther for larger steps.

**Solution:** Guide the loose pawl and the return pawl in slots so they move toward the ratchet as the swing grows, keeping engagement depth constant.

**How it works:** An oscillating frame carries a fixed tooth and a loose pawl riding on a pin through a rising slot. A spring holds the pawl against an adjustable abutment. Moving the abutment (basic 1.3 mm step, 2.6 mm for wide keys) lets the pawl swing out farther, skipping teeth. The slot makes the spring push the pawl toward the ratchet as it swings. The return pawl is mounted in an elongated hole, so it too stays engaged. Offsetting the pawl's resting edge beyond the vertical centre line lets the slot be less steep.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** ratchet wheel; oscillating frame; fixed tooth; loose pawl; slotted pin guide; adjustable abutment; return pawl; spring

**Numbers:** basic step 1.3 mm; double step 2.6 mm

**Improves on:**

- adjustable abutment escapements with pivoting pawls: slot guides keep engagement depth constant over a large swing
