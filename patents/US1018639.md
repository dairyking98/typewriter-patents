## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Making carbon duplicates requires inserting carbon and sheets by hand for each copy.

**Solution:** A carriage-mounted frame with two delivery rolls and a receiving roll feeds two work sheets and a carbon, with an automatic carbon return so the carbon is reused.

**How it works:** A frame on the carriage carries two delivery rolls and a receiving roll. Two sheets run round the platen with a carbon between them; the outer receives the ribbon impression, the inner a carbon copy. After the printing line, the outer sheet goes to a severing device, the inner winds on the receiving roll under a weight-operated drive. Gravity-operated return feed brings the carbon back when paper-controlling devices are released.

**Features:** [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [INK:carbon](../features/ink-carbon.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** delivery roll; receiving roll; carbon paper; severing device; secondary feed roller; scale plate; apron
