## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to platen releases which are adapted to releasably secure the platen and line-space wheel together to revolve in unison.

**How it works (reading):** Wedge teeth on the line-space wheel engage spring-pressed slotted disks so the platen and line-space wheel turn together at any relative position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction platen releases slip under heavy use, and toothed releases only hold at tooth positions.

**Solution:** Wedge teeth on the line-space wheel engage spring-pressed slotted disks so the platen and line-space wheel turn together at any relative position.
