## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulator stop must be raised into engagement with column stops and then return to a normal lowered position.

**Solution:** A tabulator stop arm pivoted on a bracket on the carriage back bar is lifted by a rock-shaft and link driven by a tabulator key lever, and a returning spring and adjustable heel set its lowered position.
