**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** A ferrule fitted on one platen hub is adjustable axially to lengthen or shorten the hub, its end bearing against the journal in the frame, and it is locked in the adjusted position to take up any play.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen differs in length from its frame, so it shakes endwise, and fitting it accurately meant grinding the platen hubs by hand.

**Solution:** A ferrule fitted on one platen hub is adjustable axially to lengthen or shorten the hub, its end bearing against the journal in the frame, and it is locked in the adjusted position to take up any play.
