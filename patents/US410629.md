## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Metal type carriers were too heavy, giving high momentum and a hard key touch; a light carrier must still be rigid enough when struck and must be held firmly during impression.

**Solution:** A light thin-steel cylindrical type carrier with a rear abutment and a ring of holes opposite the type for a locking pin.

**How it works:** The type carrier is drawn from thin steel sheet like a cartridge case, with a cylindrical body and a head. A curved stereotyped plate carrying all characters in four circumferential rows is soldered on one side; on the opposite side are perforations in matching rows, each diametrically opposite a type. A rear or internal abutment backs the carrier against the hammer blow. The carrier mounts by a plug and washer on a hollow sleeve that slides lengthwise on a post to raise it to the row and turns axially, left or right, to the column. A locking pin enters the perforation opposite the chosen type to hold it still while printing.
