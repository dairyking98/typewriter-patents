## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage feed must move one or more letter spaces per impression quickly and efficiently, and there must be a backspace.

**Solution:** Primary escapement wheel and feed dogs, a dog-rocker and a planetary pinion feed the carriage one or more letter spaces per keystroke, with a gig-back (backspace) mechanism.
