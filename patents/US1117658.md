## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates particularly to the mechanism for releasing the carriage of a typewriting machine from its feed mechanism, permitting it to run freely under the influence of its impelling spring or motor, and to means for stopping it at any desired point for the purpose of producing tabulated matter, such mechanism being usually termed tabulating mechanism.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a tabulator the released spring-driven carriage hits its stop hard and rebounds, and the stopping depends on how hard the operator presses the key.

**Solution:** A carriage-mounted detent, cushioned by a spring and backed by a rebound-preventing locking pawl, is thrown into line with a stop on a fixed tabulator rack while a spring brake of fixed force slows the escapement wheel.

**How it works:** Pressing the tabulator key rocks a vertical shaft, which lifts the feed pawls off the escapement wheel through a bail, so the mainspring runs the carriage. The same shaft moves a bar parallel to the carriage; the bar rocks a bell crank on the carriage that pushes a detent into line with an adjustable stop clipped to the fixed rack bar. A spring-loaded pawl moves with the detent, rides over the stop, and snaps behind it so the carriage cannot rebound. The detent carrier slides against a cushion spring to absorb the impact, ending on a set-screw stop. A cam on the shaft frees a spring-loaded brake lever that presses on a hub of the escapement wheel, giving a braking force independent of the key pressure.
