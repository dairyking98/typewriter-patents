## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates particularly to the mechanism for releasing the carriage of a typewriting machine from its feed mechanism, permitting it to run freely under the influence of its impelling spring or motor, and to means for stopping it at any desired point for the purpose of producing tabulated matter, such mechanism being usually termed tabulating mechanism.
