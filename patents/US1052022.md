## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Label printers

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the invention is to provide a compact and comparatively 'simple tally strip mechanism enabling the tally strip and posed relationship and which may be readily applied `to existing styles of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Tally strip feed devices move automatically into and out of contact with the strip as it enters and leaves the printing field; a line space advances both the work sheet and the tally strip together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A tally strip must be fed alongside the main sheet with a compact, simple mechanism that fits existing typewriters.

**Solution:** Tally strip feed devices move automatically into and out of contact with the strip as it enters and leaves the printing field; a line space advances both the work sheet and the tally strip together.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
