## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Long continuous forms need a paper guide that does not block access to the platen and carriage parts.

**Solution:** Support rod behind the paper guide plate carries two L-shaped support arms that overlap to hold paper across the gap between table parts.
