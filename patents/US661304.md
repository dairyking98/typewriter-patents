## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Inventor wants a typewriter that is simple, of few parts and cheap to make.

**Solution:** A row of spring-finger type heads on a slidable bar is slid by hand along a guideway over a flat paper bed, and each finger is pressed down to print its letter.

**How it works:** A wooden base carries a guideway in which a bar slides rectilinearly. Spring-metal fingers project from the bar over the paper, each ending in a lug with a type plate and topped by a lettered button. The operator slides the bar until the wanted finger overhangs the spot on the paper, then presses that button; the finger prints and springs back. Sliding the bar over ink rollers set in recesses in the base inks the types by brushing. The paper is slipped through a slot in the bed, registered against a guide strip and advanced by hand against graduated line marks. The writing is visible throughout.
