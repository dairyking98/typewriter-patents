## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Drive and control of the arithmetic unit in typewriters is to be made simpler and more compact while being fully reliable.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded pawl on a release axis is held by a latch on a locking shaft; the keystroke partly turns the locking shaft to release the axis, and intermediate gears turn a control shaft through a partial forward rotation, a rest during the calculation and a return.

**How it works:** Keystroke releases the pawl for one revolution; gearing then moves the control shaft forward, holds it still during the arithmetic process, and returns it, controlling counter transfer and carriage blocking.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)

**Parts:** friction clutch; bevel gear; drive shaft; spur gear; relay roller; switch body; pawl; release axis; locking shaft; control shaft

**Numbers:** one rotation (360 degrees) per keystroke release
