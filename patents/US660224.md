## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On upstrike typewriters the writing is hidden, and when the carriage is lifted to inspect it the operator cannot tell exactly where the next letter will print, which makes inserting words difficult.

**Solution:** A bow-shaped pointer hinged to the type basket is lifted by a link from the carriage rod, so it rises against the paper to show the next print position when the carriage is lifted.

**How it works:** A forked bracket clamps under a basket screw and carries a hinged bow-shaped pointer. A light spring holds the pointer down, where it rests above the ribbon and acts as a ribbon guard, leaving the center open for the types. A wire link loosely embraces the carriage rod; when the carriage is lifted the rod pulls the link and swings the pointer up to the paper. The height of the pointer tip shows the line and its lateral position shows the next letter position. When the carriage is lowered the spring drops the pointer clear.
