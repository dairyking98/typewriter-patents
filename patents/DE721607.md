## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with automatic total printing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with a motor drive for the type lever and the arithmetic units and a device for automatic writing of the Arithmetic units displayed values with simultaneous zero setting of the number wheels, in the case of a stop member common to all numeric keys according to the each digit to be written off is set.

**How it works (reading):** A stop member common to all numeric keys couples the drive to the counter when a numeric or sum key is pressed, so the counter is written out with simultaneous zeroing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Automatic total printing needs many transmission parts, which makes the typewriter costly and unreliable.

**Solution:** A stop member common to all numeric keys couples the drive to the counter when a numeric or sum key is pressed, so the counter is written out with simultaneous zeroing.

**How it works:** One common stop member engages the drive to the counter for numeric or sum keys.
