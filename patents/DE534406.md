## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Centre locks either need the carriage exactly centred before locking or lock only in one direction, so portable typewriters are not secured in both directions.

**Solution:** Moving the carriage past the centre position, or moving it vertically, prevents an arm on the switchover frame from folding into the blocked position, so the carriage is held in both horizontal and vertical directions by fingers on that arm.

**How it works:** A pivoting arm with three fingers engages a fixed approach on the frame, the carriage path, and a rack; the machine cover prevents the typewriter moving out of the middle position.
