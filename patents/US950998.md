## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the line-spacing mechanism of typewriting machines, and particularly to means for releasing the revoluble platen from the control of the usual line-space'wheel, so that the platen may be rotated while the line-space wheel remains stationary, for the usual purposes.

**How it works (reading):** The line-space wheel is loosened from the platen axle by a threaded collar and hub, letting the platen turn independently and limited by a headed screw.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot turn while the line-space wheel stays stationary, so the usual line-space ratchet blocks free platen rotation.

**Solution:** The line-space wheel is loosened from the platen axle by a threaded collar and hub, letting the platen turn independently and limited by a headed screw.
