## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Few long-bearing typebars fit on the supporting disk of a typebar machine.

**Solution:** Four typebar brackets are nested two above and two below the disk with pivots in one vertical plane at equal distance from the disk axis.

**How it works:** Brackets for typebars 1 and 3 rest on the upper face of the supporting disk and brackets for 2 and 4 hang lower, so long pivots nest beneath one another. Two further brackets are clamped to the underside, offset so their bars lie between the upper bars. Bars rest on a ring and connect to the keyboard by rods. The disk is cut away so the platen can lower for heavy manifolding.

**Parts:** supporting disk; type-bar; bracket; clamping plate; ring; platen; connecting rod

**Numbers:** 84 typebars in the complete circle; pivot 11/16 inch long
