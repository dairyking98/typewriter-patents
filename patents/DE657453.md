## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Printing device with a system of multiple sets of printing type wheels The present invention relates to printing devices having a system of in sets uniformly adjustable type wheels. These type wheels are perpendicular to theirs Axis aligned so that an imprint of them on a piece of paper or a card as a result of the uniform setting of the type wheels, a repetition of one and the same Indication shows at certain intervals between each other. To achieve print lines in repetition, the individually adjustable sets of type wheels are next to each other arranged that their type wheels lie next to each other in the axial direction. The invention aims at such a design of such printing devices, that in alternating lines the same information, and between the lines different, however, information that also corresponds to one another can be found on one piece of paper at the same time or a card. In a particular embodiment of the device according to the invention If the type wheel sets are doubled or multiplied, the possibility is given in the individual lines the same information twice or more at the same time to press.
