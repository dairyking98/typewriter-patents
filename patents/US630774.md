## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holders for erasers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an eraser attachment, and has for its object to provide a retracting-spring,whereby the eraser after use may be automatically returned to a fixed place, thus avoiding confusion or delay due to concealment or displacement of the eraser when used in connection with a type-writing machine.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-driven drum returns the eraser on a flexible cord to a fixed rest position after use, and the casing is screwed to the machine.
