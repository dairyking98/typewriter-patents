## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a drive device for writing, typesetting, telegraphing and similar machines and has the purpose of allowing the writer to do the work with increased productivity facilitate and also a simpler and therefore cheaper design of the machine to enable.

**How it works (reading):** Two hand-operated actuators move a type cylinder longitudinally and rotate it through linkage and gearing. The actuators close electric switch points for each character group, which fire electromagnets to strike the set type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The keyboard and the usual lever selection are replaced to raise productivity and simplify the machine.

**Solution:** Two hand-operated actuators move a type cylinder longitudinally and rotate it through linkage and gearing. The actuators close electric switch points for each character group, which fire electromagnets to strike the set type.
