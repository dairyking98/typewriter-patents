## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting a typebar frame between upper and lower case needs holding mechanisms.

**Solution:** A shifting and holding mechanism for an oscillating typebar frame with throw-plate and spring-assisted lever.

**How it works:** The circular typebar frame pivots on legs in the base. A shifting lever with spring and a throw-plate connected to the key-lever tip the frame to bring upper or lower case to the print point, with a buffer ring for the bars.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** typebar frame; typebar; buffer ring; throw-plate; shift lever; spring
