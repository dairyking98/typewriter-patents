## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a book typewriter the open book has unequal thickness on its two sides and is hard to level and adjust under the platen and writing machine.

**Solution:** A laterally shiftable book carriage with two independently spring-supported leaves sits under a hinged rigid flat platen and a hinged frame carrying the downward-acting writing machine.

**How it works:** The platen and the machine's base-frame are both swung up out of the way. The open book is laid on two spring-supported leaves, each held level by parallel-motion rockshafts, so both sides come to the same level regardless of where the book is opened. The carriage is slid left or right to put the page to be printed under the platen. The platen is folded down onto the book and the leaf to be printed is turned over onto it. The base-frame carrying the writing mechanism is then folded down, its weight holding the platen down. The writing machine travels over the base-frame for letter spacing, while a secondary frame slides for line spacing.
