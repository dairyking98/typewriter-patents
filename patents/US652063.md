## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the present invention is with a relatively-narrow type-bar bearing to secure the advantages of a relatively-long pivot or axis and so to avoid or minimize the bad effects of a given amount of wear, which is sufficient in the prior constructions practically to destroy the usefulness of the bearing as a means for securing steadiness of the bar as against endwise and sidewise motion thereof during operation.

**How it works (reading):** A relatively narrow type-bar bearing that gets a longer effective pivot axis using ball raceways, so a given amount of wear has less effect on steadiness against endwise and sidewise motion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A narrow ball-bearing type-bar bearing loses steadiness quickly as it wears, because it needs very accurate fit and a short pivot.

**Solution:** A relatively narrow type-bar bearing that gets a longer effective pivot axis using ball raceways, so a given amount of wear has less effect on steadiness against endwise and sidewise motion.
