## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention has reference to type-writing machines, and relates to mechanism for effecting and controlling the to and fro movements of the platen carriage;

**How it works (reading):** A main hand lever drives a toothed sector and pinion that move the platen carriage along its rack, with carriage release and line-spacing levers pivoted on the hand lever, and tabulating mechanism for columns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage travel, marginal stops and tabulated writing needed better controls under one main hand lever.

**Solution:** A main hand lever drives a toothed sector and pinion that move the platen carriage along its rack, with carriage release and line-spacing levers pivoted on the hand lever, and tabulating mechanism for columns.
