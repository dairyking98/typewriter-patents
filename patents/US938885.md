## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper rolls must be held and fed for continuous work, and the paper should be quick to cut.

**Solution:** U-shaped supporting frames pivot on a central shaft in the carriage and carry removable roll shafts with conical sleeves; spring-loaded rod frames press the paper rolls to stop unrolling, with a cutter for the paper.
