## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike ribbon wears in one stripe, colours cannot be chosen, and ribbon blocks the view and stencil work.

**Solution:** Ribbon vibrator shifts laterally automatically or at will, and can deflect down to expose the print point.

**How it works:** The ribbon is shifted sideways during operation to use its full width, or by the operator to select colours or to throw it out for stencils. A narrow ribbon is guided from a horizontal to a vertical plane through forward arms, and can be deflected below the print line to show the point and scale. Improved spools allow easy ribbon attachment.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; vibrator; guide arm; spool
