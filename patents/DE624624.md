## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a tilted typebar bearing segment the outer typebars need longer guide slots to stay steady and avoid flutter, but longer slots everywhere add friction and weaken the thin walls.

**Solution:** The segment's top face is bevelled so its edges are two eccentric circular arcs, making the guide slots deepest at the outside and shallowest in the middle.

**How it works:** The segment body carries a row of typebar guide slots. Its top surface is bounded by two eccentric arcs with the greatest spacing in the middle. The middle slots are therefore short with little friction, while outer slots are long enough to guide the typebar heads safely. The shape also saves material, makes the segment lighter and eases fitting the typebars.
