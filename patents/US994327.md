## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Keyboards with one set of keys for all denominations, e.g. ten-key board; Casings convertible reductible in size, e.g. for transportation

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the keys of typewriting machines, and particularly to the denomination keys of tabulating mechanisms.
