## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine adding while typing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a calculating machine for Adding and subtracting numbers while typing them ,to be written'.

**How it works (reading):** Pressing a typewriter key moves one versatile lever whose positions replace three separate levers, and the gear reducer for subtraction is engaged when the key moves past it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An adding counter combined with a typewriter needs many levers and axles, which makes it complex.

**Solution:** Pressing a typewriter key moves one versatile lever whose positions replace three separate levers, and the gear reducer for subtraction is engaged when the key moves past it.
