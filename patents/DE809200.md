## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Horizontal platen needs a time-consuming and noisy carriage return after each line; earlier upright-platen designs on a screw spindle wrote in a helix.

**Solution:** Typebar machine with an upright platen turned stepwise by key levers for letter spacing and raised one line space by a second stepping drive at line end, giving normal horizontal lines.

**How it works:** Typebars in a basket strike the upright platen, which rotates and slides on a vertical axis d. Key levers drive a ratchet wheel on a sleeve that turns the platen by one step per character through a tongue-and-groove coupling. The platen hangs from a wire running over a roller to a spring drum that raises it, held by a pawl released by linkage. After a full 360 degree turn the platen is raised a line and continues from the same angle. Designed for electric drive, endless paper rolls, and two foot pedals for one-handed operation.
