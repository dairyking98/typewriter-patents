## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key register check dispenser)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key register with an optionally effective check dispensing device, in which the one conveyor roller of the check slip conveyor approximated by appropriate setting of an eccentric of the second conveyor roller or can be removed from it and the adjustment of the eccentric by a means Crank mechanism influenced by special keys takes place.

**How it works (reading):** A special check-issue key acts through a crank mechanism to set an eccentric so the conveyor rollers repeatedly engage for a chosen number of checkouts, then they are released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A check must be issued repeatedly over an adjustable number of checkouts, which the business-type keys alone do not allow.

**Solution:** A special check-issue key acts through a crank mechanism to set an eccentric so the conveyor rollers repeatedly engage for a chosen number of checkouts, then they are released.

**Features:** [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** main drive shaft 20; business-type keys 22; locking rocker 25; push rod 33; lifting disc 37

**Numbers:** adjustable repeat count
