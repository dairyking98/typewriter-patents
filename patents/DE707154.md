## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Automatic symbol marking on forms through a sensing device was tied to the platen and line switching, which limited collective-sheet widths.

**Solution:** A plug-in punching and sensing device, independent of the platen, marks the sheets; line switching and symbol generation run separately.

**How it works:** A detachable front-mounted plug-in unit punches and senses edge marks on writing sheets, so symbol generation and line spacing work independently.
