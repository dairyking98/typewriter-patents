## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention is to reduce the number of balls in order to cheapen the construction and reduce the friction of the ball-bearing as much as possible.

**How it works (reading):** Fewer balls in each conical ball race with loose arc-shaped wire separators and an adjusting screw taking up wear.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar pivots wear and cost too much when completely filled with ball bearings.

**Solution:** Fewer balls in each conical ball race with loose arc-shaped wire separators and an adjusting screw taking up wear.

**How it works:** A U-shaped hanger on the type ring carries the typebar between two bearing surfaces on its inner side arms. The typebar has a tapered boss on each side facing them, with two sets of balls between. A transverse screw through the bar threads into one arm and its conical head seats in the other, adjusting the bearings for wear. Between the balls loose arc-shaped wire fillers hold them apart and prevent jamming.

**Parts:** hanger; typebar; boss; ball; segmental separator; adjusting screw; connecting rod

**Numbers:** four balls per set (three or more possible)
