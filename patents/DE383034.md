## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type lever heads resting on a flat support get bent during cleaning, and no visible warning shows the bend.

**Solution:** The rest abutment is a sector disk with notches that seat the type lever heads between its elevations, so a bent lever no longer fits its bed and is noticed; it may be fixed to the frame or inserted only for cleaning.
