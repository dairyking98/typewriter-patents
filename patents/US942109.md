## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar actions need smoother motion, no rebound interference, adjustable key-to-bar connections and a way to lock bars at rest.

**Solution:** A typebar linkage in which key-lever links with screw couplings loosely loop around hook arms of the type bars, with padded extensions on the bars, giving adjustable lengths and rebound control.

**How it works:** Key levers connect by links to hook arms on pivoted typebars. Links are pivoted below, have screw couplings to lengthen or shorten, and end in a wire loop that loosely surrounds the bar arm. The far ends of the bars have extensions covered with rubber or felt that act on rebound. Universal bar, rock shaft and feed dogs of a Remington layout carry the carriage feed. Applies to understrike and front-strike machines.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** key lever; link; screw coupling; loop; type bar; hook arm; padded extension; universal bar; rock shaft; feed dog

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter: adds adjustable link and rebound-damped bar extensions
