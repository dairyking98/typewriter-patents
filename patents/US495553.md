## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in that class of machines which are used to perforate mercantile paper, as checks and drafts, in the form of figures, to indicate the amount for which the paper is drawn.

**How it works (reading):** A check punch in which punches are carried on the periphery of a revolving wheel and presented one at a time to a fixed punching point, with the wheel driven by a shaft and pinion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check punching is slow and complicated when each punch is set separately.

**Solution:** A check punch in which punches are carried on the periphery of a revolving wheel and presented one at a time to a fixed punching point, with the wheel driven by a shaft and pinion.
