## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar typewriters with the platen carriage above the keyboard are bulky to carry; the inventor wants the carriage and platen to fold down compactly onto the key banks.

**Solution:** A two-part carriage frame whose rear part is pivoted so the platen carriage can be flipped forward and down over the keyboard, with platen-shift keys that raise the frame on sliding stems.

**How it works:** Typebars are raised by key levers and links to strike a roller platen through a ribbon. The platen carriage runs on a base plate carried by pivoted arms attached to a skeleton frame that rides on vertical stems in hollow rear posts. Two shift levers on a rock-shaft lift the stems against coiled springs; the auxiliary lever, limited by a stop, raises the platen a smaller distance for upper case, while the main lever lifts it fully for numerals and punctuation. For transport the pivoted arms are turned forward and downward so the carriage and platen lie over the key banks, optionally on pivoted rests with concave edges. Key lever tension is adjustable by sliding plates and screw rods.
