## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check writing needs indelible printing, even inking of every type member, and fewer parts, and the amount should not be alterable.

**Solution:** A settable member carries serrated type members that are inked by an inking attachment driven by that same member, and shiftable feed rolls let the check be printed on two lines.
