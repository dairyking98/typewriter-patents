## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing Arabic or Turkish and figures needs the line direction reversed, which the escapement cannot do by itself.

**Solution:** A reversible pulley or snatch block, attached either to the frame or to the carriage, sets the carriage direction under the spring drum; two co-axial escapement wheels with stops provide the spacing.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
