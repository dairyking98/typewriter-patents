**How it works (reading):** The paper sheets are held flat in a laterally sliding carriage and fed from the front so the sheets are not bent and print with a clean impression.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary roller counters and paper feed arrangements bend or damage stiff ledger sheets when they are inserted from the back of an adding and recording machine.

**Solution:** The paper sheets are held flat in a laterally sliding carriage and fed from the front so the sheets are not bent and print with a clean impression.
