## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage feed mechanisms of the time were complex, costly and hard to assemble and take apart.

**Solution:** A simple removable escapement of two overlapping arms on one rock-shaft, one fixed and one sliding, engaging a pivoted spring-driven feed rack.

**How it works:** A feed rack pivoted on the guide bar carries a toothed rack bar pulled forward by a spring through a cord. A rock-shaft, held between conical-point bearing screws so it lifts out easily, carries a fixed escapement arm with an inclined detent catch and a second arm that slides on the shaft between the first and a stop flange. The two arms are inclined to one another and rock together to release and catch rack teeth one at a time. A rack actuator with a feed pawl on the carriage engages the rack's forward arm to shift the rack out and also turn the platen.
