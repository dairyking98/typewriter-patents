## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**How it works (reading):** The stop and the type support curved to the platen are adjustable perpendicular to the baseline in the alignment plane, so every type group sits correctly at the print point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Types with ascenders and descenders on different baselines do not align at the print point, and a flat type support needs skill to set.

**Solution:** The stop and the type support curved to the platen are adjustable perpendicular to the baseline in the alignment plane, so every type group sits correctly at the print point.
