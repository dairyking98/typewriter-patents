## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in stop mechanism which is operative to arrest the platen of a type-writing machine, and thus to facilitate the doing of condensed billing and other work of similar character.

**How it works (reading):** Notched and toothed disks with stops and a detent, enclosed in a case, arrest the platen at chosen positions on the platen-carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Condensed billing needs the platen to be arrested at set positions, and the existing stop devices do not do this.

**Solution:** Notched and toothed disks with stops and a detent, enclosed in a case, arrest the platen at chosen positions on the platen-carriage.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen-carriage; notched and toothed disks; stops; detent; case
