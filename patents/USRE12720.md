## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a construction of type-bars in that class of type-writers wherein a series of type-bars are mounted to strike at a common printing point, and are guided near their heads by a guide with a aring slot into which each type-bar strikes as it approaches the printingpoint.

**How it works (reading):** A broad spot on the back of the guide prevents the second type bar entering the space beside the first and wedging.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A second type bar can wedge in a flaring guide slot when the first is held in it.

**Solution:** A broad spot on the back of the guide prevents the second type bar entering the space beside the first and wedging.
