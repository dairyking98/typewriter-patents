## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes of plates, e.g. metal plates, plastic cards with automatic plate transport systems, e.g. for credit cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and has for its principal object to provide improved devices to automatically disconnect said carriage from its letter feeding devices, preliminary to returning or restoring the carriage in the direction opposite to that of letter feeding, and to reconnect said carriage with said letter feeding devices at the end of a return movement.

**How it works (reading):** Lift the feed rack out of the pinion automatically at the start of carriage return and let the margin stops re-engage it at new line.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed rack stays meshed with the pinion during carriage return, turning the escapement wheel backward, adding wear and noise.

**Solution:** Lift the feed rack out of the pinion automatically at the start of carriage return and let the margin stops re-engage it at new line.

**How it works:** Operating the line-space lever lifts the feed rack from the feed pinion at the beginning of the return movement. The escapement wheel and dog stay still while the carriage travels back. When the margin stop devices arrest the carriage at the start of the new line they release the rack to re-mesh. This lessens return force, noise and wear.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** feed rack; feed pinion; escapement wheel; feed dog; line space lever; margin stop

**Improves on:**
