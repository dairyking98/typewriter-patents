## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**How it works (reading):** Springs are fully relaxed when the paper is released, so the feed rollers lift off the platen and the lever mechanism is set to stay unloaded.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed rollers stayed pressed on the platen by springs, causing wear and flattening of the rubber rollers.

**Solution:** Springs are fully relaxed when the paper is released, so the feed rollers lift off the platen and the lever mechanism is set to stay unloaded.
