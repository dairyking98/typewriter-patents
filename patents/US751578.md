## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typist must take a hand off the keys to shift the platen.

**Solution:** A knee-operated lever under the typewriter table connected by a rod to the shift-key lever.

**How it works:** A base plate is fixed under the table with an operating lever pivoted to it. An adjustable rod with a hook at its top connects the lever to the typewriter shift-key lever. A rocking lever on an adjustable saddle on the operating lever is pressed by the knee, rocking the operating lever and depressing the shift key. When the rocking lever is in its operative position a cam locks the saddle on the lever, and the typewriter's shift-key spring returns everything.
