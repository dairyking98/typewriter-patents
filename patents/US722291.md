## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need keys flexibly connected to bars and a vertically shiftable bar carrier under a raised platen.

**Solution:** Vertical bar carrier below a platen on the carriage, shifted by a flexibly connected key.

**How it works:** The base has front standards carrying the vertical type-bar carrier, with keys flexibly connected to the bars. The platen sits above the carrier on the paper carriage. The carrier is raised and lowered relative to the platen for case change. An inking roll inks the types.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md)
