## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriter housings must be easily removable from the base while the base is still fixed to the floor.

**Solution:** The frame hooks into the base at the rear and is fastened by spring-loaded screws at the front, with rubber feet in recesses.
