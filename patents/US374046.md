## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants a simple, durable and cheap typewriter in which a moving type form is brought into place and struck with a lever.

**Solution:** A telescopic hand lever with a swiveled key is placed over any projection of an index, which positions the type form so the depressed key prints it.

**How it works:** A carriage in a hinged shallow box rides on a brass-tube-and-steel-wire rack bar. The type form and its frame sit near the pivot of a telescopic lever whose free end carries a swiveled hand piece placed on one of a series of index projections. Moving the lever to that spot sets the character indicated, ready to be impressed next in line, and depressing the key prints it. An inking pad sits inside the box, a paper roller and platen strip lie in the frame, and graduated scales mark paper position. The carriage steps by a spacing mechanism and escapement described in the later text.
