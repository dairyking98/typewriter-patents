## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-lever bearings have too much friction and need an adjustable, self-locking pivot with few parts.

**Solution:** A ball bearing for type levers where balls run on a V-shaped path in the lever bore, held between conical extensions of a screw and a nut; the nut tightening relieves friction and locks the adjustment.
