## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An electric typewriter needs a step-by-step carriage drive and automatic carriage recoil at line end.

**Solution:** A weight-driven clockwork motor with an electromagnetic double escapement steps the carriage rack bar after each letter, with automatic recoil at the end of travel.

**How it works:** A weight on a cord turns a drum and ratchet that drive a wheel train into a gear meshing with the carriage rack bar. The train also drives a scape wheel held by a double escapement lever whose armature is attracted by an electromagnet. Closing the spacing circuit just after a letter is printed releases the escapement one step, advancing the carriage one space. At the forward limit, the carriage is placed to recoil automatically.
