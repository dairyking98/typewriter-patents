## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rolled paper must be held taut and fed by a platen that lacks a driving aid, so rolls need automatic feeding.

**Solution:** A roll holder on a detachable frame connects by perforated bands and sprockets to the platen, and spring drums turn the platen to feed the roll.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** roll holder; perforated bands; spring drums; platen; sprocket teeth
