## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must remove hands from the keyboard to return the carriage at the end of each line.

**Solution:** A traveling pedal, rail structure and connecting tube and head return the carriage from the keyboard, with a line-space lever connection.
