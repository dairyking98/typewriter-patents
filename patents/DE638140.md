## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inking apparatus

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a duplicating device for typewriters with a rotatable, disc-shaped ink carrier attached to a typewriter frame arranged carrier is provided and in place of the usual carbon paper between the writing sheets guided around the paper roller can be used.

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable disc-shaped ink carrier is arranged so only its edge sits between the sheets at the pressure point, and it turns freely as the paper roller shifts letter by letter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon discs on typewriters need a special drive or extra key force, and a disc held between writing sheets drags them and blocks paper roller movement.

**Solution:** A rotatable disc-shaped ink carrier is arranged so only its edge sits between the sheets at the pressure point, and it turns freely as the paper roller shifts letter by letter.
