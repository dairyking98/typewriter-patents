## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punched carbon tapes for typewriters must be produced and end-capped by hand for multiple copies.

**Solution:** An automatic machine punches and moistens carbon tape stock, cuts it to length and folds end pieces onto the tape.
