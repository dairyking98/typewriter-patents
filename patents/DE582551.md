## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber or pad platens used for tamper-proof writing give blurred, unclean impressions, rattle, and wear the types.

**Solution:** The platen is wrapped with closely wound fine steel wire, ends soldered to the body so the wire rests resiliently, which embosses and cuts the paper for indelible characters.
