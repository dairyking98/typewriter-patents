## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type on typebars gets clogged with ink and needs a cheap, easily attached cleaning device.

**Solution:** A clip-on rotary brush frame that sits over the type-well when the carriage is thrown back.

**How it works:** A bent frame grooves onto the shift rail, located by an aperture that receives a block on the rail. A loosely mounted horizontal shaft carries a vertical rotary brush with end heads pinned to the shaft, and the shaft's limited end play shifts the brush widthwise so fresh bristle meets the type. A spring plate on the frame clamps onto a carriage rod, holding the brush over the type-well, and the shaft can be withdrawn to change the brush.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** frame; shift rail; shaft; rotary brush; brush head; spring plate; carriage rod; type-well

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 6: attachment for its type-well
