## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need heavy key pressure to drive the typebars.

**Solution:** Keys only select a typebar by raising a coupler, and a single electromagnet with a push bar then drives the selected typebar to print.

**How it works:** Depressing a key lever lifts a selector bar into a coupler pivoted to the typebar heel. Further travel closes a contact through a foot piece and frame, energising one magnet. The magnet pulls a semicircular push bar against the raised coupler, swinging the typebar to the platen. The same push bar motion lifts the ribbon guide through a bell-crank, and springs retract everything.
