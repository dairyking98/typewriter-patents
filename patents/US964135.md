## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locks for subsidiary keys, e.g. for shift keys

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to improvements in case shifting mechanism for typewriting machines, that is, the means by which a relative adjustment is effected be tween the platen and printing devices to cause upper case characters to be printed.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary pivoted latch key on one shift key lever that hooks a frame abutment, released by pressing further on that key or by pressing the other shift key, which depresses the first through a cam.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The shift lock for platen case shifting needed to be released by either shift key without added levers.

**Solution:** An auxiliary pivoted latch key on one shift key lever that hooks a frame abutment, released by pressing further on that key or by pressing the other shift key, which depresses the first through a cam.

**How it works:** The platen is carried on arms fixed to a rock shaft in the carriage, rested on a roller on an arm joined to a rock shaft with a bail under two shift keys. Either key rocks the bail and lifts the platen. An auxiliary key on shift key 12 has a toe that hooks under a frame cross-bar when depressed and rocked back, holding the platen up. Further pressure on key 12, or on key 13, whose lever drives a shaft with a roller arm riding a cam on lever 12, depresses lever 12 enough for a spring to snap the latch clear.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** platen; rock shaft; bail; shift key; auxiliary latch key; toe; cross-bar; cam; roller arm; spring
