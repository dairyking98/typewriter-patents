## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (slide movement control for office machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the device to bring about the gradual movement of the carriage or sled from Office machines, e.

**How it works (reading):** A single handle with two working positions: in one it releases the spring drive for one step only, in the other it releases the carriage for several steps, with a speed-reducing device to soften the stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-driven carriage control needs separate release levers for multi-step movement, and the usual gradual switching device cannot also release the carriage for longer travel.

**Solution:** A single handle with two working positions: in one it releases the spring drive for one step only, in the other it releases the carriage for several steps, with a speed-reducing device to soften the stops.
