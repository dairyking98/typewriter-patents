## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide 7 new antifriction means for supporting the carriage in its supporting frame and a carriage support acting in conjunction with the above mentioned means to prevent lostmotion between the type and the platen when they impinge upon the latter.

**How it works (reading):** Anti-friction balls in a cage and Z-section raceways support the carriage frame, so the carriage has no lost motion against the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lost motion between the type and the platen wastes impact and causes misalignment.

**Solution:** Anti-friction balls in a cage and Z-section raceways support the carriage frame, so the carriage has no lost motion against the platen.
