## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The flat tray type magazine of JP67249 is bulky, and a drum magazine needs a way to keep its types from falling out while turning without blocking the lift of the selected type.

**Solution:** A cylindrical magazine with ring grooves of loose types is wrapped by curved L-section support frames that hold the types by a side groove, leaving a gap at the top where the printing lever lifts the chosen type.

**How it works:** A cylindrical magazine on a horizontal shaft has parallel ring grooves, each with holes holding removable types. A curved inverted-L frame surrounds each groove except for a type-wide gap at the top, its ledge ending in a knife edge that rides in a cross groove in the side of each type. The operator holds a knob on a rack bar and moves it over a character index plate; this slides the magazine frame left and right on balls and rails, and the rack turns a gear on the shaft to rotate the drum. The wanted type thus reaches the print point under its printing lever, whose tip always sits in the type's cross groove. Pressing the key lever drives a trigger via link and bell-crank that strikes the stud of that lever, lifting the type along a curved guide to the paper on the platen.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Cylindrical type magazine for a Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention improves patent No. 67249. It is a cylindrical type magazine for a Japanese-script typewriter, characterised by the following construction. Round the outside of a cylindrical type magazine whose surface has many parallel ring-shaped grooves cut in it for setting the types, leaving a suitable gap at the top, curved type-retaining support frames, one for each ring groove, are set at right angles to the magazine axis and run round the rest of the circumference. Each support frame has an inverted-L cross-section, with a ledge projecting to one side that fits into a cross groove cut at a set depth in one side face of each type and holds it.
