## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Impression strength varies with the operator's key force, and changing the typeface is difficult.

**Solution:** Print from an elastic type-plate moved by a lever to the desired letter, struck by a hammer whose force is set by a wedge and screw, not by the key.

**How it works:** The carriage on a spindle is moved by a pawl and double hinge engaging a fixed rack, one tooth per letter. The type-plate frame is cast with the adjusting lever and guide disk, sliding in a straight guide under the carriage. A key presses a bar forward which, through a wedge surface, drives an impression hammer onto the elastic plate; the plate throws the hammer back on its return. A screw sets the distance of the wedge and so the impression force; removing the inking plate allows another type-plate to be swapped in.
