## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriage escapement and carriage support need improving.

**Solution:** A two-dog escapement worked by a bar under the key-levers, with adjustable dogs and a spring-driven carriage.

**How it works:** A bar beneath all key-levers pulls down an adjustable rod connected to a dog G pivoted to a fixed plate. When a key goes down, dog G engages a rack tooth while a second horizontally pivoted dog I disengages; when the key rises dog I engages the next notch and the carriage advances one notch under spring J. A ratchet and pawl tighten the spring. The dog plate is adjustable by a set-screw. The carriage rides on a track with sockets, and collars limit its travel. A paper guard lifts.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** key-lever; universal bar; connecting rod; dog; second dog; rack; spring; ratchet wheel; pawl; dog plate; track; carriage rail; adjustable collar; paper guard
