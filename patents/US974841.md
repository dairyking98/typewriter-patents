## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, especially those of the front-strike class;

**How it works (reading):** Keys act on the type bars through sub-levers and curved treads with a rocker motion, and the type bars strike rearward against the platen; leverage can be varied without making the key touch harsh.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type bars needed a way to be locked in normal position, better key touch, and variable key leverage on the type bars.

**Solution:** Keys act on the type bars through sub-levers and curved treads with a rocker motion, and the type bars strike rearward against the platen; leverage can be varied without making the key touch harsh.
