## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pulling the paper carriage back by the line-feed lever also needs to release the clutch tooth on the spring housing without noise.

**Solution:** Link the line-feed lever by a rod to the release shaft so swinging it also disengages the mainspring housing's coupling tooth.

**How it works:** The line-feed lever pushes a plunger carrying the line index tooth, indexing one to three teeth on the platen's index wheel. A rod from its other arm turns a lever on the release shaft, which moves a release bar and a decoupling ring disc through a spring link. The disc moves the spring housing and a lever lifts the coupling tooth from the ratchet teeth, freeing the housing. The carriage can then be drawn back noiselessly while the lever, held by a roller on the disc, keeps the tooth lifted. The text is cut off.
