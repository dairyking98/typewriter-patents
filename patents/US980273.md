## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewriting machines and more particularly to such as are adapted for stenographic writing,- t-he object of my present invention being to provide simple andefiicient means for feeding the paper sheet or strip progressively at each manipulation of a key or keys.
