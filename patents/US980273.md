## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewriting machines and more particularly to such as are adapted for stenographic writing,- t-he object of my present invention being to provide simple andefiicient means for feeding the paper sheet or strip progressively at each manipulation of a key or keys.

**How it works (reading):** A supplemental frame carries a paper feed mechanism that grips the sheet or strip and feeds it over the platen, and devices raise the inking ribbon so that it clears the paper during feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper for stenographic writing must be fed progressively at each key stroke, and the paper must not conflict with the inking ribbon.

**Solution:** A supplemental frame carries a paper feed mechanism that grips the sheet or strip and feeds it over the platen, and devices raise the inking ribbon so that it clears the paper during feed.
