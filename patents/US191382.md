## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Re-typing at an exact point on a sheet (for example over erased letters) is hard to align.

**Solution:** A supplemental scale on the carriage, matching the fixed scale, sits next to the line being printed so paper position can be read against the carriage indicator.

**How it works:** The gage has the same length and divisions as the machine's fixed gage and hooks onto the carriage frame by two arms, replacing the pulley rod, bearing lightly on the platen just below the printing line. Adjusted to coincide with the fixed scale, it shows at which numbered position a typed letter will fall. Moving the carriage until the indicator reads a chosen number then lets printing start at a chosen point on a chosen line.
