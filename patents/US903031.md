## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructional details or processes of manufacture of the input device

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide means for locating and defining the keyboard by other than the sense of direct sight, whereby said operator instinctively retains the hands in the proper position in re spect.

**How it works (reading):** A marginal frame of contrasting colour, optionally raised above the keys, around the keyboard, subdivided by a partition and fixed to the machine frame with brackets, so the hands can find the keyboard boundary by touch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must look at the keyboard to locate the keys, which takes attention from the reading copy.

**Solution:** A marginal frame of contrasting colour, optionally raised above the keys, around the keyboard, subdivided by a partition and fixed to the machine frame with brackets, so the hands can find the keyboard boundary by touch.
