## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a device for automatic movement reversal of the ribbon of typewriters with drivers provided on the ribbon in the Proximity of the ribbon ends, with the coupling between the universal rail by the driver and a reversing device is brought about.

**How it works (reading):** When the ribbon runs out, a stop engages a guide lever that drives a rocking arm, which lowers a worm wheel onto a drive worm and disengages the other spool.
