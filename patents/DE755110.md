## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts in which one paper or set is fed towards printing position from the front of the apparatus

**How it works (reading):** A second securing roller set is coupled to the first sheet's pressure rollers so both hold on the platen together and release together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A second pre-inserted sheet can form a pad under the platen, so the typed line is not exact.

**Solution:** A second securing roller set is coupled to the first sheet's pressure rollers so both hold on the platen together and release together.

> Description Translated from German Device for inserting several sheets of paper in a typewriter According to the 755 patent, there is one to hold the sheet that was inserted first special retaining device arranged, which is pivotably mounted so that they rolling back the upper part of the sheet that was first fed back behind the feeder not obstructed, but swung down onto the platen after rolling back can be to hold the pre-inserted sheet on the platen. Thereafter then the second pre-stacking sheet is rolled in with the same pre-plugging device. It happens that the sheet is not sufficiently firm on the platen and forms a pad so that the stop of the writing types becomes hollow and the Scripture does not come exactly on the specific line either. According to the present invention -is in a further development of the main patent underlying thought with the swing arms of the pressure rollers for the first Vorsteckblatt a second securing device coupled so that this with the former at the same time comes to rest on the platen below the type stop and is also taken off at the same time.
