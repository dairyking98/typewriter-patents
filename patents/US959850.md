## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ordinary paper fingers cover the paper margins or get struck by type, so typing cannot reach the extreme edges of the sheet.

**Solution:** Paper fingers whose free ends are offset inward so the pressure roll sits on the paper while the finger body stays outside the sheet edge and clear of the print line.

**How it works:** Sheet-metal slides ride with light friction on a bar fixed to the carriage. A spring-loaded finger is hinged to each slide and carries a rubber-faced pressure roll in an offset end portion, pressing the paper to the platen. The offset puts the roll within the paper margin while the finger stands outside it, so type can print to either extreme edge. The lower edges of the offset portions are flared away from the platen to guide the paper in. Handles let the slides be moved along the bar.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** platen; support bar; slide; paper finger; spring; pressure roll; handle

**Improves on:**

- usual paper fingers with rolls above the hinges: fingers no longer cover the margins or the print line
