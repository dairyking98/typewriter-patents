## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to stencil cutting machines and vone of its objects is to construct a machine in which the hlanli will he exposed to View at the cutting point, whereby the work of the machine is visible.

**How it works (reading):** The blank is carried at an angle to the horizontal so each stencil line is visible at the cutting point, with feeding means that position it accurately with the punches and dies.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil-cutting machines hide the blank at the cutting point, so the work cannot be seen.

**Solution:** The blank is carried at an angle to the horizontal so each stencil line is visible at the cutting point, with feeding means that position it accurately with the punches and dies.
