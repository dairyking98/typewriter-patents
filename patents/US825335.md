## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing and returning the carriage require the operator to take a hand off the keys.

**Solution:** A pull-cord from a treadle, running over sheaves, pulls a supplementary lever on the line-space pawl so the foot line-spaces and returns the carriage.

**How it works:** The platen ratchet is engaged by a line-space pawl hung on the hand lever. A supplementary lever on the same fulcrum, stopped against the hand-lever hub, is joined to a cord running over two sheaves to a treadle. Pulling the cord tilts the lever and pawl, line-spacing the platen while the carriage is swung back. One sheave arm folds away when not used.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet wheel; line-space pawl; hand lever; supplementary lever; pull-cord; sheave; treadle
