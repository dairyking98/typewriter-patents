## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Incomplete platen shifting let type levers strike between lines and make writing illegible.

**Solution:** A bracket with an annular groove on a screw, moved by all type levers, blocks the type lever when the platen is not at the correct height.
