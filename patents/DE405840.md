## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding apparatus zero print suppression)

**How it works (reading):** Printing segments spring-pivot until a locking pawl engages a ratchet on the number roll, holding roll and segment together to suppress those zeros.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Adding machines print leading zeros in front of the highest digit of the total.

**Solution:** Printing segments spring-pivot until a locking pawl engages a ratchet on the number roll, holding roll and segment together to suppress those zeros.
