**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to devices adapted to act as cushions and vibration absorbers to be used with typewriters and similar machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter vibration is noisy and the machine can shift on the desk.

**Solution:** A felt or compressible disk fastened through the machine's rubber foot by a screw into a shell with a bottom plate, side piece and cover, which damps vibration and holds the machine to the table.
