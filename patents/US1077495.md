## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper controlling devices for typewriting machines and its object is to provide improved devices of the character specified.

**How it works (reading):** A duplex card clamp on the platen grips both the bottom and top edges of a card, so a card can wrap completely around the platen and beyond.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen-carried card clamps hold only cards that reach part way round the platen, so a long card cannot be held when it wraps fully around.

**Solution:** A duplex card clamp on the platen grips both the bottom and top edges of a card, so a card can wrap completely around the platen and beyond.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
