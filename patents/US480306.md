## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage should be released as early as possible after impression so it has time to move a letter-space and stop before the next strike.

**Solution:** Connect one member of the spacing mechanism to the spacing rocker through friction so release is earlier.

**How it works:** A hinged feed-rack frame engages two dogs on a dog holder pivoted to a spacing rocker; the front dog is jointed with a spring for quiet carriage return and travel is limited by stops and an adjustable plate. A universal bar under the key levers rocks the spacing rocker via rods. A frictional connection between spacing members lets the carriage be freed at the earliest moment after the typebar strikes.
