## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Embossing with punches and dies tends to separate or tear the machine frame, so the impression force should be compressive in the frame.

**Solution:** A typograph whose dies sit in annular series on a die carrier, with compression levers and a cam shaft, so that the embossing force acts compressively within the frame.

**How it works:** A cam shaft operates compression levers and yokes that press the dies against the plate; the die carrier's springs let each die operate independently.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Type ring](../features/elem-ring.md), [Metal type](../features/elem-metal-type.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** die carrier; annular die series; compression levers; cam shaft; yoke; springs
