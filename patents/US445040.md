## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier machine needed a more compact and efficient arrangement for impressing matrices.

**Solution:** An improved matrix machine that moves the matrix bed with gears and a rack, and has cock-operating mechanism for the air supply.

**How it works:** A rack gives movement to the matrix bed through gearing; the gears and their frame can be set in an alternative position, and cock-operating mechanism controls the cocks.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
