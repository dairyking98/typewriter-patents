## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars rebound when they are restored to normal position and clash with adjacent bars near the type-bar support or basket.

**Solution:** A type-bar rest attached to the type-bar segment absorbs the shock of the bars returning to normal, reducing rebound and preventing neighbouring bars from clashing.

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type sector or segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md)
