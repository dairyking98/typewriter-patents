## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to inking devices more particularly applicable to type-writers of the kind wherein a pad is employed for inking the type;

**How it works (reading):** An inking device attached to the main ring of a Yost machine: a spindle moved longitudinally carries a pad, driven from the mainspring drum, to allow printing in various colours.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing is limited to one colour because pad inking gives no colour change.

**Solution:** An inking device attached to the main ring of a Yost machine: a spindle moved longitudinally carries a pad, driven from the mainspring drum, to allow printing in various colours.

**Features:** [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** inking pad; spindle; frame A; bevel-wheel drive
