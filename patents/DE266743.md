**How it works (reading):** A brake drum around the ratchet shaft has arms inside that brake the shaft directly, so the paper carriage decelerates with no extra linkage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A delay device on the paper carriage needs a separate drive and linkage to engage and disengage.

**Solution:** A brake drum around the ratchet shaft has arms inside that brake the shaft directly, so the paper carriage decelerates with no extra linkage.
