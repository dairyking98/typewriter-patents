## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed and reversing on a front-strike machine needs reliable guiding of the ribbon to the spools.

**Solution:** Ribbon guides on the top plate direct the ribbon to spools whose feed pawls and ribbon-controlled reversing frames reverse the feed.

**How it works:** Inclined guide surfaces with ears hold the ribbon laterally and let it be slid in. Each spool has a driving shaft, an actuating member operating a feed pawl and a reversing frame, and a holding pawl. A controller bearing on the ribbon decides when reversal can occur. Spools are removable with the shaft displaced.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** ribbon guide; ribbon spool; driving shaft; feed pawl; reversing frame; holding pawl; ribbon vibrator; top plate
