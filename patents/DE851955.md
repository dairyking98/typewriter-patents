## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Removing the removable key and type lever basket means disconnecting many rods and levers that link it to the frame-mounted devices, and resetting them on reinsertion is awkward.

**Solution:** The rods and levers of the basket meet the frame-mounted elements with sloping, interlocking butt surfaces, so they couple automatically in the same position when the basket is pushed in, while each mechanism stays a single whole that can be released on removal.
