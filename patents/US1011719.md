## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In ordinary upstroke-release escapements the dog travel through the rack depends on key depression, causing lost travel and a slow, irregular let-off.

**Solution:** A key-controlled escapement member gets a complete movement in one direction early in the downstroke and a complete return early in the upstroke, so the carriage is released almost instantly regardless of key dip.

**How it works:** A spring-drum carriage is held by an escapement rack or wheel with bevelled teeth. A dog rocker carries a fixed holding dog and a stepping dog pivoted to turn at right angles to the wheel plane, spring-held. A slotted arm on the dog rocker is moved by the key stroke so the dogs pass through fully at the first part of each stroke direction. The remainder of the description is cut off.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; dog rocker; holding dog; stepping dog; spring drum; key lever; carriage

**Improves on:**

- ordinary upstroke-release escapements: removes lost dog travel and variation with key dip
