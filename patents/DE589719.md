## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line typesetting and printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for mechanical composition using prefabricated type, i.e. without casting equipment

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line typesetting and printing machine with circumferential male molds, which magazines were taken, combined into lines, colored, printed and again be placed in the magazines.

**How it works (reading):** The inked type line is pressed onto a flexible rubber base by a rubber-blanketed drum, then a transparent sheet is pushed in and printed on the other side, so the types are covered with pressure while the film stays clear.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Transparent printing on a line-casting machine over a pressure roller gives poor coverage of the types.

**Solution:** The inked type line is pressed onto a flexible rubber base by a rubber-blanketed drum, then a transparent sheet is pushed in and printed on the other side, so the types are covered with pressure while the film stays clear.
