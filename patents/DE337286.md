## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Sheeter. Addition to patent 336718. According to the main patent 336718, the knife, which can be moved transversely to the paper web by means of a drawstring and which is used to cut the described forms or letterhead from the paper web, is attached to a slide carriage that is rigidly guided transversely to the latter, on which the two or four for holding the paper sheet when cutting serving pressure rollers are attached independently movable. Because of the fairly large unsupported width of the guide rail on the one hand and the support surface for the paper on the other, in whose groove the knife always extends evenly deep for safe cutting, a certain mutual flexibility of the knife guide on the one hand and the support surface or knife groove on the other must be expected, and consequently, with this device, a relatively strong design of the knife guide as well as the support surface is necessary in order to keep the deflections and the safe cutting within non-impairing limits. This results not only in a great weight, which loads the typewriter cart in an undesirable manner, but the rigid guide also inevitably increases the resistance to movement…
