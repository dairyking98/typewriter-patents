## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the carriage stops at line end, further keystrokes print one character on top of another.

**Solution:** An inclined plane at the end of the carriage track lifts the roller-supported carriage so the platen rises out of reach of the types.

**How it works:** A roller on the platen carrier rides the fixed front track. Near line end it climbs an inclined lifter (integral with the track, or adjustable along it, or hinged so it can be swung away) and tilts the carriage about its rear hinge rod, raising the platen above the impression plane. A flat table at the top lets the roller rest. An adjustable lifter lets the operator choose the stop point, and a hinged one lets extra characters be added. Shown on the Yost machine with a center type guide.
