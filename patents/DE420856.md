## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar drives with two combs are long and tall.

**Solution:** The intermediate lever between the key and the typebar is an angle lever that overlaps the front comb in rest, shortening the whole.

**How it works:** Each typebar is guided at its lower edge by two concentric combs. The intermediate lever passes between the combs and, bent in an angle, engages with a pin a slot in the typebar's web at half its height. This reduces overall height and length for portables.
