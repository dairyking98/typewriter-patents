## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** Each attachable column stop has an attachable key that operates control devices, routing the carriage via one of two rails to the selected column.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column stops on power paper carriage controls are limited by how many can be fitted to the rod and keys.

**Solution:** Each attachable column stop has an attachable key that operates control devices, routing the carriage via one of two rails to the selected column.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
