## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** A displaceable member driven by the space bar interrupts the frictional drive from the space bar to the carriage switching elements and holds an angle lever out of action while the line lock is on, so the space bar can be pressed fully.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The usual margin stop locks the space bar at line end, so the bar cannot be pressed to a stop without bending the linkage.

**Solution:** A displaceable member driven by the space bar interrupts the frictional drive from the space bar to the carriage switching elements and holds an angle lever out of action while the line lock is on, so the space bar can be pressed fully.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
