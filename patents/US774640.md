## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the mechanism for pressing the paper to the platen and properly guiding it.

**How it works (reading):** A feed roller on arms on a rock-shaft is pressed to the platen, with spring fingers and a guide-plate that guide the paper, a scale that holds the paper perpendicular to the platen, and a removable shaft assembly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper must be pressed to the platen and guided, the shaft carrying the parts should be removable, and the feed roller must adjust to surface irregularities.

**Solution:** A feed roller on arms on a rock-shaft is pressed to the platen, with spring fingers and a guide-plate that guide the paper, a scale that holds the paper perpendicular to the platen, and a removable shaft assembly.
