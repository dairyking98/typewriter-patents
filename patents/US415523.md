## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Improves the carriage and escapement of a typewriter; details of the improvement are only partly in the opening.

**Solution:** A vertically moving plate carries a fixed dog and a pivoted dog so each key stroke lets the carriage advance exactly one rack tooth.

**How it works:** The carriage rides on grooved rollers in a track frame and has a rack. A vertically movable plate carries a fixed dog and a pivoted dog and is pulled down by a pitman from the key. Pulling down disengages the pivoted dog and engages the fixed dog, which holds the carriage; a spring moves the pivoted dog back one tooth. On release the plate rises, the fixed dog lets go and the carriage is driven by a spring until the pivoted dog meets a lug, one tooth advanced. A push rod forces the pivoted dog out of the rack so the carriage can be run without printing, and a thumb screw in a slot adjusts the stroke.
