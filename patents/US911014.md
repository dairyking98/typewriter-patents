## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the type bar segments of writing machines having detachable type bars.

**How it works (reading):** The fulcrum rod has recesses at the bar positions, and each bar has a slot and an opening sized to pass over the recessed part of the rod. The rod is moved endwise to lock the bars in place, and a screw secures it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Individual type bars cannot be taken out and replaced without taking the segment apart, and a bar can work loose from its place on the segment.

**Solution:** The fulcrum rod has recesses at the bar positions, and each bar has a slot and an opening sized to pass over the recessed part of the rod. The rod is moved endwise to lock the bars in place, and a screw secures it.
