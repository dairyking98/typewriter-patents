## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guards, shields or dust excluders

**How it works (reading):** Leaf springs on the front of the hood hold it slightly open at first so its edge clears the carriage return lever, and the hood opens fully only when the carriage is moved to a clear position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Opening a typewriter hood can strike the carriage return lever or other protruding carriage parts, and removable covers on pins loosen and rattle.

**Solution:** Leaf springs on the front of the hood hold it slightly open at first so its edge clears the carriage return lever, and the hood opens fully only when the carriage is moved to a clear position.
