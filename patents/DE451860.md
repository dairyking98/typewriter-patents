## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The drawstring is looped through a slit at the foot of the type lever and unwinds along its curved edge, giving increasing acceleration; a rocker arm of the same length as the drawstring keeps the drive unchanged when the basket is switched.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type lever pulled by a side-attached drawstring needs wide spacing between levers, and the drive does not give an evenly increasing acceleration; switching baskets also disturbs the drive.

**Solution:** The drawstring is looped through a slit at the foot of the type lever and unwinds along its curved edge, giving increasing acceleration; a rocker arm of the same length as the drawstring keeps the drive unchanged when the basket is switched.
