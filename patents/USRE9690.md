## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reissue of Phipps typewriter; claims key levers pivoted at their outer ends with a shield, and a typebar cone guide so the type registers accurately.

**Solution:** Typebars with a conical stud seating in a guide hole for accurate register, key levers pivoted at the outer ends under a shield, spring-drum carriage and escapement.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot in guides around a table opening; the short arm's conical stud enters a hole in the guide to align the type and stop the bar. Key levers of equal length are pivoted at the outer ends with keys at differing distances, linked to the bars by rods. A carriage on a rod is pulled by a spring drum and held by a rack, bevel gears and an escapement wheel stepped by a rocking-shaft-driven bar with a fixed pin and loose dog. A frame moves the carriage without printing, and a ratchet lever feeds the roller one line.
