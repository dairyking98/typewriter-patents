**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a clear checking device for booking machines, especially for those equipped with several cross counters Typewriters, in which each cross counter is advantageous with a sensing linkage and at least two transverse counters a common one that triggers the sensing linkage Control device is assigned.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On booking machines with several cross counters, a clear (blank) sign printed for one counter while another counter was written out incorrectly.

**Solution:** A locking device, actuated by carriage movement, that is assigned to the common sensing linkage and blocks machine control while a non-working counter is engaged.
