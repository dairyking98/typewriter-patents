## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Levers mounted on fixed pivots and connected to transmission members, e.g. toothed gearing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a mode of assembling the type bars or levers of type-writing machines, and has for its main objects to provide a construction whereby the usual individual hangers are dispensed with, thus economizing greatly in cost, and whereby the typebars may be speedily assembled in position and without liability of accidental displacement during transportation or use of the machine.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Individual hangers for each type-bar are costly and slow to assemble.

**Solution:** Type-bars hang directly on a fulcrum ring seated in a groove of the type-ring, replacing the hangers.

**How it works:** The type-ring has an inner annular groove and radial vertical slots. A fulcrum ring, flattened on two sides, sits in the groove. Each type-bar has a round bearing eye with a narrower side slot; it is turned so the slot lines up with the flat sides, slipped over the ring, then gravity swings it to where the slot is out of line and it is captured.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** type-ring; fulcrum ring; type-bar; key-lever; connecting rod; type rest; paper carriage

**Improves on:**

- individual type-bar hangers: a single shared fulcrum ring replaces hangers and screws
