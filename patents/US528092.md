## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Upstrike typewriters hide the writing from the operator.

**Solution:** A glass rhomboid prism attached below the platen reflects and re-inverts the printed line so it can be read right-way-up, with an etched spacing scale.

**How it works:** Brackets and adjustable arms fixed to the frame hold a pivoted prism of flint, crown or plain glass below the platen, near the print point but clear of the typebars. Light from the printed line enters one face, is inverted on the second face and re-inverted on the third, so it appears correct on the fourth face. A scale etched on the first face shows letter position.
