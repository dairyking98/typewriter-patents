## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding machine paper trolley drive) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an adding machine, in which the paper trolley in a known manner from the machine drive by partial rotations a crank mounted on the machine frame between different column positions of the calculation form is moved back and forth.

**How it works (reading):** The crank makes several partial rotations that step the carriage through the columns, and a half-turn in reverse brings it back to the first column, with a control and drive mechanism switching the increments.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In adding machines, the paper carriage must return to the first column after each of several consecutive calculations, which needs a separate return action.

**Solution:** The crank makes several partial rotations that step the carriage through the columns, and a half-turn in reverse brings it back to the first column, with a control and drive mechanism switching the increments.
