## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen line-spacing needs to stay adjusted while the carriage is moved.

**Solution:** The line-spacing handle sits at the rear of a post so carriage motion does not change the stop adjustment; an adjustable stop sets the spacing.
