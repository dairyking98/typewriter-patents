## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Setting line spacing to match ruled sheets took repeated trial adjustments.

**Solution:** A line-space lever that sets its own limit stop so any spacing interval can be copied by one stroke.

**How it works:** The normal slide and pawl turn the platen ratchet. A clutch dog engaged on an annular clutch flange allows fine adjustment. Permanent and shiftable stops limit the throw; a gage on the shiftable stop is positioned by the lever's own stroke, then locked by a cam or nut. Later strokes are limited to that interval.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet wheel; line-spacing lever; slide; pawl; clutch flange; clutching dog; stop; gage; locking nut

**Improves on:**

- stepped line-space ratchets: continuously variable interval set by one stroke
