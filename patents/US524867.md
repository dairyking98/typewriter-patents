## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a mechanism to be added to any of the ordinary type-writing machines, the object being to so improve the machines in ordinary use that the user may be able to print vertical columns of characters or vertical division lines (as may be desired) by the direct action of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** A second sliding grate on the key shafts, selectable by latching the space key, feeds the paper roll one line per keystroke instead of the carriage one space.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriters cannot print vertical columns or vertical rules directly.

**Solution:** A second sliding grate on the key shafts, selectable by latching the space key, feeds the paper roll one line per keystroke instead of the carriage one space.

**How it works:** Each key's rocking shaft arm acts on a sliding grate that, through bell-crank, rod and ratchet, advances the carriage rack. A second, identical grate is linked to the platen line-space ratchet. A latch locks the space key or an auxiliary key down, holding one grate out of reach of the key arms and leaving the other active. With the carriage grate disabled, each character keystroke turns the paper roll one line, so a column or vertical line is printed.
