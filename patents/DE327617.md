> Description Translated from German Drive device for type levers of typewriters. The object The present invention relates to a drive device for type levers of typewriters and is characterized essentially by a curved surface on which the free The end of each pull rod slides upwards when the associated button stops, whereby a repeated involuntary type attack, even when constantly depressed Button is prevented. When the key is released, a new stop cannot be made. because the pull rod is inevitably connected to the button and the pull rod in this case is raised upwards. Devices serving the same purpose are known. With these Devices are, however, used tension springs, which make up the individual tie rods their working position and prevent contact with the camshaft. A simple curved surface is an essential feature of the arrangement of many springs technical progress, because firstly, springs do not have the same operational reliability to be awarded is like a simple splint and, secondly, the manufacture of springs is less economical than rail.
