## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a drive device for type levers of typewriters and is characterized essentially by a curved surface on which the free The end of each pull rod slides upwards when the associated button stops, whereby a repeated involuntary type attack, even when constantly depressed Button is prevented.

**How it works (reading):** A curved rail under each pull rod lifts the rod after the type strikes, so the cam disengages and a repeated involuntary strike cannot occur.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Springs held type-lever pull rods in their working position and were less reliable and costlier than a simple rail.

**Solution:** A curved rail under each pull rod lifts the rod after the type strikes, so the cam disengages and a repeated involuntary strike cannot occur.
