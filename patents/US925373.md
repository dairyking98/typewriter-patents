## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon wound by the carriage spring would be driven violently, risking damage to the ribbon and its reversing gear, whenever the carriage is released to run rapidly.

**Solution:** Drive the ribbon-winding shaft from the escapement pinion so that lifting the carriage rack for release also disconnects the ribbon drive.

**How it works:** A bevel pinion on the escapement pinion spindle turns, through a vertical shaft, bevel gears and a worm, a worm wheel on a transverse master shaft. The master shaft slides endwise to engage bevel pinions on the vertical spool shafts and reverse the ribbon, with a clutch pawl-and-ratchet allowing hand winding by a crank. Because the drive comes from the escapement pinion, the carriage-release key that lifts the hinged rack from the pinion, or a tabulator key, disconnects the ribbon drive automatically. The carriage spring itself therefore winds the ribbon as letters are typed.
