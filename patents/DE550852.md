**How it works (reading):** A pawl carrier for the bell clapper slides vertically and horizontally on a bridge linked to the bell carrier by parallel joint pieces, and hooks onto a rail on the switchover part so it follows its movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage stop cannot be lifted with the switching part without disturbing the margin bell.

**Solution:** A pawl carrier for the bell clapper slides vertically and horizontally on a bridge linked to the bell carrier by parallel joint pieces, and hooks onto a rail on the switchover part so it follows its movement.
