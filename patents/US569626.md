## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fisher's earlier machines obstruct the view of the line of writing and need better typebar mounting, interchangeable ribbon parts and a carriage feed with release.

**Solution:** A crescent-shaped typebar ring open to the front lets the line of writing be seen by displacing only the inking ribbon.

**How it works:** Same track-mounted typebar machine as Fisher's earlier patents, with a lightweight aluminium carriage and steel caps on the key-lever posts. The semicircular ring hangs from side and rear hangers, leaving the front free. Ribbon spools and shields are interchangeable for different ribbons. The carriage feed uses movable ratchet bars with a release mechanism. Key levers are returned by coiled springs.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** track rail; carriage; post; steel cap; key lever; coiled spring; crescent typebar ring; hanger; typebar; ribbon spool; ribbon shield; ratchet bar

**Improves on:**

- [US569625](../patents/US569625.md): open ring for permanent visibility of the writing line
- [US569491](../patents/US569491.md): further revision of ring and ribbon mounting
