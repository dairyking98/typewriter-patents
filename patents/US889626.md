## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (lettering apparatus for doors and windows) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**How it works (reading):** A type-wheel with perforations holds spring plungers; each plunger carries a type and a push button, and the carriage is adjustable on a support frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Door and window lettering needs an easy, adjustable way to make inscriptions in a range of sizes.

**Solution:** A type-wheel with perforations holds spring plungers; each plunger carries a type and a push button, and the carriage is adjustable on a support frame.
