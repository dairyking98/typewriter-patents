## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage travel limits and tabulating stops need to be set and alarmed at the margins without fiddly adjustment.

**Solution:** Tabulating stops slide and turn on a scaled rod on the carriage and engage movable frame stops; marginal stops rock a shaft that rings an alarm and locks the typing mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
