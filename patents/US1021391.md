## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating mechanism cannot select any column or any denominational position within a column in a single carriage run.

**Solution:** Selection mechanism lets the operator choose a columnar field and a denominational position, and parts are locked in the set position until a later operation releases them.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
