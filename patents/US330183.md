## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers on bank books or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention pertains to improvements in type-writers, more especially in appliances to adapt the latter for use in writing in books, while it is equally applicable for writing upon separate sheets of paper;

**How it works (reading):** A case with a rack bar and end bars carries the machine's carrying rods and graduated plate, with clamps and notches, to position the machine over a book leaf.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing into books needs the typewriter positioned over a bound leaf, which the standard carriage cannot do.

**Solution:** A case with a rack bar and end bars carries the machine's carrying rods and graduated plate, with clamps and notches, to position the machine over a book leaf.
