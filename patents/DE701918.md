## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing devices for feeding tape

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Device for securing the paper roll against unintentional turning on adding machines and other office machines in which one on the notched paper roll support bar Slidable non-rotatable disc by the action of being in an incision the spring supporting the paper roll support rod against the end face of the paper roll is pressed.

**How it works (reading):** A pretensioned spring acts between an actuator engaging incisions in the support rod and a disc that moves in a limited pin-slot, so brake force stays within small limits regardless of width.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-roll brake force varies greatly with the random width of the roll when a leaf spring presses the roll end face.

**Solution:** A pretensioned spring acts between an actuator engaging incisions in the support rod and a disc that moves in a limited pin-slot, so brake force stays within small limits regardless of width.
