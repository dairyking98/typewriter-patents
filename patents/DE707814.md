## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (spring winding for embossing machine carriage) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving mechanisms, e.g. springs stressed during carriage return

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a spring winding mechanism for the plate holder carriage of embossing machines, the car of typewriters and the like.

**How it works (reading):** The cover disk collar is attached to a journal and engages tabs of the spring sleeve in a claw-like way, so the housing opens while the spring stays seated.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-winding housings of plate-holder carriages could not be opened without releasing the spring, and spring ends could not be checked.

**Solution:** The cover disk collar is attached to a journal and engages tabs of the spring sleeve in a claw-like way, so the housing opens while the spring stays seated.

**How it works:** Spring drum with a hooked sleeve and tabs on the hub, a cover disc with recesses, so the cover can be removed for inspection without losing spring tension.
