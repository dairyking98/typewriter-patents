## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing a ribbon is dirty, and a bobbin is wasted every time the ribbon runs out.

**Solution:** A two-part spool screws together over a ribbon tube, and the ribbon is pulled onto the core and clamped between flanges.

> Description Translated from German Ribbon reel The invention relates to a ribbon reel for Typewriters and the like, which are in the axial direction between the two flanges is divided and, on the core of which the ribbon wound on a tube is pushed will. Such spools have heretofore been proposed to wrap the ribbon with the spool to be able to connect accordingly, whereby in particular precautions have been taken that the ribbon in a specific direction of rotation determined by the drive of the machine could be applied. In the known arrangements and designs of ribbon reels there is on the one hand the disadvantage that when changing a ribbon a cumbersome and dirty work must be done. It usually means major difficulties to attach the new ribbon to the two spools or if with the ribbon a new spool is supplied to pull the worn ribbon off the old spool and reattach the new one to this spool. On the other hand, the well-known Arrangements have the disadvantage that there always remains a bobbin that does not is further used, so that a considerable waste of material is the result.
