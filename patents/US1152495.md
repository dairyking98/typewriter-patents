## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A small, light writing device is wanted that can be set directly on a sheet lying flat on a desk, instead of a full desk machine.

**Solution:** A finger-held type-carrier case holds two rows of slidable type-bars, rack-geared in opposed pairs, so pressing the case down over a held stud drives one type down.

**How it works:** The carrier is a frame with a finger clip holding two rows of longitudinally sliding U-section type-bars, each with an interior rack. Each opposed pair is geared together by a pinion on a shaft, held at neutral by a spring and cord, so one bar going down raises its partner. Each bar has an outside stud in a slot of the side plate. Pressing the carrier down while a post or pin supports one stud turns the pinion and drives the partner type-bar down at double the carrier motion onto the paper. Type blocks sit on ball heads with torsion springs to keep alignment; the later figures show a guide machine for flat sheets, an ink pad, and a carriage-less variant.

**Parts:** finger-held carrier frame; finger clip; rack type-bar; pinion; return spring and cord; ball-head type block; torsion spring; stud; ink pad

**Numbers:** 3 to 7 type-bars per row; two rows; type moves at twice the carrier travel
