## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Sliding lowering supports for sewing or typewriter tables can tilt the machine when raised or lowered.

**Solution:** Two support or damping means act inside the support plate on either side of the vertical through the centre of gravity, moving synchronously with equal force.
