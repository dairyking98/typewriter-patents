**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is the construction of a type-writing machine by means of which a plurality of characters can be printed simultaneously upon a paper tape and extending in groups transverse thereto.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A stenographic machine should print several characters at once as a transverse group on a paper tape.

**Solution:** Six rows of keys each shift a curved segment typebar sideways so one chosen character per row aligns across the tape, and hammers print them simultaneously.

**How it works:** Keys are in six parallel rows each holding an alphabet. Depressing a key moves a longitudinal bar with oblique cam slots, which through a bell-crank swings that row's curved typebar segment about its shaft to one side or the other of the hammer; its centre is blank at rest. Hammers equal in number to keys pressed strike the tape against the aligned characters through an inking ribbon. Any key press also feeds the tape one space so words print across the tape in parallel rows, with the tape on a reel in a wooden casket case.
