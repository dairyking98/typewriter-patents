## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Syllable, line, or like type selection

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is the construction of a type-writing machine by means of which a plurality of characters can be printed simultaneously upon a paper tape and extending in groups transverse thereto.
