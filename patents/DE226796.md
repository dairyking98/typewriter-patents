## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accent marks are written only in the upper ribbon zone, so they print in a different colour from the letters.

**Solution:** The switching device of the main patent is linked to the accent key so that accents stop in the same ribbon band in front of the printing point as the letters.

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
