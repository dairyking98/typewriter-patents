## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to improve the connections between the universal bar and the carriage-escapement dogs, so as to render the touch of the finger-keys more agreeable and elastic and the operation of the escapement mechanism more prompt.

**How it works (reading):** The connections between the universal bar and the escapement dogs are rebuilt, with a dog-rocker arm and operating link, so the key touch is more elastic and the escapement acts more promptly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The connection between the universal bar and the carriage-escapement dogs makes the finger-key touch stiff and the escapement sluggish.

**Solution:** The connections between the universal bar and the escapement dogs are rebuilt, with a dog-rocker arm and operating link, so the key touch is more elastic and the escapement acts more promptly.
