## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machines hide the work and need aligned bars; inventor wants a type-bar support with aligning and locking recesses and a key that bears directly on the bar.

**Solution:** A pivoted type-bar support above the carriage, shifted transversely, with direct key levers that operate escapement and ribbon feed as they print.

**How it works:** Type-bars are pivoted on a rod between lugs around a radial type-bar support, each returned by a coiled spring in a shell. Recesses align and lock the bars at printing position. The support is hinged at one side and slid transversely beneath the carriage by a shift key through a universal joint. Depressing a key lever bearing on the bar also works the escapement and ribbon feed.
