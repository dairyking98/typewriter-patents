## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type wheel receiver, pressure telegraph) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a receiving device for pressure telegraph receivers in which the characters are printed by a rotating type wheel and the print hammer is operated by local force.

**How it works (reading):** A tensioned resilient drive member, released by received characters, drives the print hammer so the moved masses are small.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Telegraph printers with rigid levers or armature-driven hammers need heavy masses, high forces and make noise.

**Solution:** A tensioned resilient drive member, released by received characters, drives the print hammer so the moved masses are small.
