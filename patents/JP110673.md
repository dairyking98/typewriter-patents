## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A Japanese tray-type typewriter needs to print either of two different characters (two sizes or two faces) cut one above the other on a single type piece.

**Solution:** A type holder slides along the striking arm, moved by a sector and link that a coupling locks either to the frame or to the rock shaft, so the upper or lower character lands on the print point.

**How it works:** Loose type pieces with two characters stacked on a flat plate are lifted from the magazine and dropped into a slot in a holder on a bent striking arm fixed to a rock shaft. Catches and a spring presser grip the type, and a striker releases them at the start position. The operating rod turns the shaft, the inked type strikes the platen upside down relative to the magazine. A pinion loose on the shaft drives a sector and link that slide the holder along the arm; a knob-operated catch pin locks the pinion either to the frame (holder fixed, lower character prints) or to the shaft (pinion turns with the stroke, shifting the holder pivot, upper character prints). An inking roller on a swinging arm follows a cam plate so only the character part is inked.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. A Japanese-script typewriter characterised as follows. A bent striking arm fixed to a rock shaft that responds to the operating rod carries a type holder, which can slide only at right angles to the shaft, in a plane containing the shaft. The holder is linked by a link to a toothed sector that meshes with a gear loose on the shaft. By a coupling, that gear can be locked at choice either to the fixed frame or to the rock shaft, so that the sector is either kept independent of the shaft's printing stroke or tied to it; in this way the holder's printing position relative to the platen can be shifted between two settings. The object: a Japanese typewriter that uses type pieces with two different characters set one above the other on a flat rectangular plate, and can print either the upper or the lower one at will.
