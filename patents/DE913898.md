## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters, and more particularly to machines that are all continuous billing machines are known, in which carbon paper between continuous-S the webs or the folds of a fan-shaped folded web are arranged, whereby they are slidable or retractable along the path to achieve conformity with the following Track parts.

**How it works (reading):** A power drive retracts the carbon sheet carrier along the paper path so the carbon is removed before the labelled form is separated.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Continuous billing forms need carbon sheets withdrawn from the forms before separation, and the old spring-motor withdrawal gives too little control.

**Solution:** A power drive retracts the carbon sheet carrier along the paper path so the carbon is removed before the labelled form is separated.
