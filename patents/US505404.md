## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check perforating device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improved device or implement for punching or perforating figures or characters on the margin or other portion of a bank-check or other negotiable paper, to indicate the numerical amount or sum of money for which the instrument is drawn.

**How it works (reading):** A small pocket-sized check punch in a round case, with a swivelling die plate and punching arms that press a cutting die through the check.
