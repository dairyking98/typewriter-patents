## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-strike ribbon should present the upper or lower part of its width to the print point.

**Solution:** A ribbon vibrator thrown at each operation to one of several elevations.

**How it works:** The type bars are on two ball-raced segments of different radius, driven through links and rock shafts from the keys. A ribbon vibrator is thrown at each stroke to one of several heights so that the upper or lower part of the ribbon is raised to the print point.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; type bar; segment; rock shaft; link; carriage; platen
