## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed type brushes bend their bristles on the first impact and take energy from the type blow.

**Solution:** A brush carried on a lever is moved by the type lever: it strokes the type on the way up, then swings clear on the return, so the type meets the brush only in motion.

The invention exists in that the cleaning brush is moved by the type lever so that it first strokes over the type, but then moved away from the path of the type lever is to also return to its rest position when the type lever wanders back. The mutual movement of the brush and the type results in a more thorough one Cleaning achieved than with the known fixed brushes, where the When the type is first hit, fold the bristles over and only the surface touch the type. In addition, the movable brush of the type offers a smaller one Resistance and therefore has a less adverse influence on the impact energy the type out than the fixed. To protect the brush, it is also controlled according to the invention in such a way that that the type only comes into contact with the brush in the course of mutual movement comes.
