## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing the product in multiplying machines is hard because the product is known only after both factors are entered.

**Solution:** Printing units for multiplicand and multiplier, driven by the pen slide, act together from one hand lever that also actuates the product unit.
