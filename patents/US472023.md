## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be freed to turn quickly by hand without the line-spacing parts, and rigid feed-roller supports raise roller pressure unduly when several sheets are inserted, smudging copies.

**Solution:** Mount the feed roller on levers that rock on a fulcrum against an adjustable spring, and add means to release the platen.

**How it works:** A paper-carriage frame of front and back rods and end bars encloses the platen. Brackets on the back rod carry a fulcrum ridge, and a lever rests on it; the lower end holds the pressure roller and a scale bar, and the upper end bears on a spring around a headed pin. The spring forces the roller against the platen, and a nut adjusts its tension. When extra sheets push the roller back, the lever rocks and its upper end presses the spring only gently, so pressure barely rises. Several releasing devices let the platen and paper be rotated freely.

**Parts:** carriage frame; platen; bracket; fulcrum ridge; lever; pressure roller; spring; adjusting nut; scale bar; platen release

**Improves on:**
