## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Perforated-band key drivers serve only one text because the holes are fixed.

**Solution:** A roll-up drive band carries removable pin-like projections that push the extended key levers in sequence, so one band blank can be pinned for any text.

**How it works:** Key levers carry a stop extension, spring-pressed against a drive band wound from one reel to another around a guide roller. Pins screwed into the band strike the extensions in turn and so move the typebars. The band is advanced by spring or motor.
