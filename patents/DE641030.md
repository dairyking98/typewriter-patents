**How it works (reading):** The frame's lower part is formed as an outward flange that the carrying-case hood attaches to on all sides, so the case reinforces the frame and the frame can be made lighter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A portable typewriter frame is weak as a base, so the case had to be made heavy and the frame could not carry the machine.

**Solution:** The frame's lower part is formed as an outward flange that the carrying-case hood attaches to on all sides, so the case reinforces the frame and the frame can be made lighter.
