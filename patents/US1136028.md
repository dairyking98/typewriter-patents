## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide I control of the operator and adapted to retract the carriage at any time and from any position to its initial position at the beging of the line.

**How it works (reading):** Electric-motor carriage retraction via a clutch and retracting tape drum, with a control linking carriage return to automatic line spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Operator must return the carriage by hand and separately space lines; the two operations are not linked.

**Solution:** Electric-motor carriage retraction via a clutch and retracting tape drum, with a control linking carriage return to automatic line spacing.

**Features:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
