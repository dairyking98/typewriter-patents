## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing writing-plate lift devices need extra frames and long levers on both sides, adding pivot points and weight.

**Solution:** Spring rods on bearing sleeves in the frame's longitudinal walls carry pairs of hinged levers that engage hooks under the writing plate, and a foot lever bracket linked to one pair raises and lowers the plate, making the device lighter and simpler.

**How it works:** Foot lever pivots one lever pair; the spring rods and hooks on the plate underside transmit the lift so the plate rises and falls on the frame.
