## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter table with a device for lowering the typewriter in an upright position.

**How it works (reading):** Countersunk machine plate slides down behind a fixed support plate, joined by a roller blind so the plate covers the sunk machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter table requires a free writing surface when the machine is lowered.

**Solution:** Countersunk machine plate slides down behind a fixed support plate, joined by a roller blind so the plate covers the sunk machine.
