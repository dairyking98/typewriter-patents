## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriters print along one line, but music needs notes on any of 26 or more staff lines placed with a simple finger press.

**Solution:** A rotatable type disc carried on a sliding fork frame is first turned and then pushed in a straight line along a print line to a stop set by the key, then struck by the finger.

**How it works:** Pressing a note key first turns the type disc part way about its centre so the chosen type lies over a print line perpendicular to the staff, then drives the disc along that line until an adjustable stop member, positioned by the fixed keyboard, halts it at the right staff height. Pressing the type directly then prints it. A flat paper carriage moves sideways on rails and is pulled left by springs stepped by a ratchet and a button; a wide endless belt under the sheet is moved by a knob or buttons for feeding perpendicular to the staff. The disc is held at rest by a spring and loose rings with shoulders bearing on pins. A ruling device draws the staff lines.
