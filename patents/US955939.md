## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar faces clog with dirt and need cleaning, which is awkward without an in-machine device.

**Solution:** A brush carriage on guides fixed to the frame, normally held clear of the type by springs, that the operator slides across the faces of the resting type bars.

**How it works:** Guide members with heads on the top frame carry an arc-shaped carriage plate conforming to the arrangement of the type at rest. A semicircular brush back with bristles is fixed to the plate, with a thumb piece at the front. Springs hooked to the guides hold the carriage forward and out of range of the type. The operator grasps the thumb piece and slides the brush back and forth over the type faces, then releases it to spring clear and be hidden by a guard plate.
