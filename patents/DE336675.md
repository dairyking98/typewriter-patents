## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**How it works (reading):** Multi-layer leaf-spring shock absorbers wrap the platen pivot pins; air gaps between layers damp the transmitted noise.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen-bearing contacts transmit typebar impact noise into the machine frame.

**Solution:** Multi-layer leaf-spring shock absorbers wrap the platen pivot pins; air gaps between layers damp the transmitted noise.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
