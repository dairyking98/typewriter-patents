## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**How it works (reading):** A foldable bar along the paper roller edge, at a slot parallel to it, lets each form be set on any line without moving the others.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On accounting machines, setting a second form on a line displaces the first form already set.

**Solution:** A foldable bar along the paper roller edge, at a slot parallel to it, lets each form be set on any line without moving the others.
