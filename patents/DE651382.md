## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing unit)

**How it works (reading):** Each print hammer carries its own locking member, so the hammer itself controls the blocking of its re-advance after it bounces off the type wheel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Print hammers driven by strongly tensioned springs can bounce back from the type wheels, and locks controlled by the drive arms are unreliable because the drive arms can also spring back.

**Solution:** Each print hammer carries its own locking member, so the hammer itself controls the blocking of its re-advance after it bounces off the type wheel.
