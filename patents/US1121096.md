## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing should be positive and identical for every line, easily changed between single and double spacing, and the platen should still turn freely when wanted.

**Solution:** A line-space lever on the platen shaft carries a spring pawl whose pin is locked between the lever and an adjustable triangular abutment, giving a positively stopped one- or two-line throw.

**How it works:** A lever pivoted on the platen shaft is spring-pressed against a stop; at rest the stop holds the pawl out of the platen ratchet wheel. Moving the lever lets the pawl tooth engage the ratchet and rotate the platen. A pin on the pawl meets a rotatable triangular abutment, which stops the lever and locks the pawl in the ratchet teeth so every line is the same. Turning the abutment by a thumb nut to another side, held by a spring, changes the throw between one and two lines. A separate spring-loaded detent arm holds the ratchet, and a hand lever lifts it so the platen can be turned freely either way.
