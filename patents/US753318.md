## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenographic typewriting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**How it works (reading):** A compact stenotype with type levers on a straight axis; each type lever carries several types, and sliding the carriage moves the required type into the printing position, while keys act on the levers through two-armed levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stenographic writing needed a type-writer that is simple, inexpensive, and readable by the operator, and that blind persons could also use.

**Solution:** A compact stenotype with type levers on a straight axis; each type lever carries several types, and sliding the carriage moves the required type into the printing position, while keys act on the levers through two-armed levers.
