## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter lamp stand stands in the writer's way and its foot protrudes in front of or behind the machine.

**Solution:** A thin plate foot slides under the typewriter feet, and the lamp arm attaches to its side, with an adjustable telescoping and rotating support.
