## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A rigid spacing key operated at one end or in the middle is awkward and needs force at the key lever.

**Solution:** A spacing key hinged at each end to its two supporting levers so it can be pressed anywhere and either lever alone moves the universal lever.

**How it works:** Two spacing levers carry vertical extensions at their front ends. A wood or metal spacing plate is pivoted to the extensions by socket pieces and pivot pins. Pressing the key in the middle depresses both levers; pressing at one end depresses only that lever, the other end acting as the pivot. Either lever vibrates the universal lever, which drives the pawls on the carriage rack with a pulling spring and cord.

**Features:** [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** spacing key; spacing lever; vertical extension; socket piece; pivot pin; universal lever; pawl; rack; pulling spring; cord
