## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**How it works (reading):** An interleaving ink strip, retained in the machine for repeated use, is fed with the sheets over the platen; it is pulled back by a spring when the feed roll is released, and a work table with a pressure roll holds the ledger sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ledger entries must be duplicated onto a record sheet and a statement at once without carbon sheets, and the record sheet must be kept in the machine.

**Solution:** An interleaving ink strip, retained in the machine for repeated use, is fed with the sheets over the platen; it is pulled back by a spring when the feed roll is released, and a work table with a pressure roll holds the ledger sheet.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** ink strip on web; spring return of strip; work table
