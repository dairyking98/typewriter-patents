## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets of varied sizes creep or move during printing on a flat platen.

**Solution:** Two longitudinal clamping bars on the platen, one fixed and one transversely movable, secure the sheet along one or both edges.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** flat platen; fixed clamp bar; movable clamp bar; guide-rails
