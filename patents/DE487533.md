## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon can slip out of its guide slots in an open ribbon housing, while existing retainers make ribbon replacement difficult.

**Solution:** A rotatable arm bridges the slot of the spool housing and is linked by a lever mechanism to the moving housing half, so it swings aside when the housing opens and the slot is released, keeping the ribbon secured during use but easy to change.

**How it works:** Opening the two-part housing pivots the bridging arm aside through the lever linkage; closing it returns the arm over the slot.
