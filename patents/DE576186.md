## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing; Syllable, line, or like type selection

**How it works (reading):** A set of comb-like sliding rods with projections and gaps of different depths stop movable members at different distances, so each rod position gives a different setting.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A way is needed to set several type groups at once, and to varying distances, from a single key movement in a word typewriter.

**Solution:** A set of comb-like sliding rods with projections and gaps of different depths stop movable members at different distances, so each rod position gives a different setting.

**How it works:** Thin steel rods have slots and stepped projections along their length and slide in a slotted housing. Levers with springs shift selected rods left or right. Plate-like members then move in toward the rods and are stopped by the projections or sink into the gaps, so their travel depends on rod position. That travel sets a controlled device, for example one of several type wheels, to the matching character. Only the opening is available.
