## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Release device for driving typewriters. With typewriters and other key machines controlled by perforated strips has already been proposed between the driven shaft around which the paper tape is directed, and the actual drive countershaft to engage a clutch, in order to be able to automatically interrupt the rotation of the paper roller or the like, without the operation having to be switched off completely. The invention relates to such machines, and in it is this clutch usually held indented by a spring. An electric motor, for example, is used in arrangements of this type. by which the device disengaged a belt for driving the paper roller or the like. Apart from that that this arrangement resulted in constant power consumption, it was difficult, and not so safe due to the sensitivity of the electric motors of work as an arrangement in which no relays are used, such as in the present arrangement. Attempts were also made by arranging pairs of snails in mesh with worm gears, and connected with dog clutches, the paper roll to move in and out.
