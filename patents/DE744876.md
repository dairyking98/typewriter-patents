## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (vibration-damping instrument mounting) · **Subject (patent classes):** Springs made of rubber or other material having high internal friction, e.g. thermoplastic elastomers characterised by the mode of stressing the stressing resulting in flexion of the spring of strip- or leg-type springs; Mode of stressing of basic spring or damper elements or devices incorporating such elements the stressing resulting in flexion of the spring of strip- or leg-type springs

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an arrangement for resilient and vibration-damping Storage of instruments or instrument panels on vehicles, in particular Aircraft.

**How it works (reading):** A rubber-rod mount with a flanged head bonded to a metal disc gives a longer usable rod, with shock and vibration damping.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber mounts for instruments on aircraft cannot take hard shocks and low-frequency vibration together.

**Solution:** A rubber-rod mount with a flanged head bonded to a metal disc gives a longer usable rod, with shock and vibration damping.

**Features:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
