## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operating the shift and space levers by hand slows typing.

**Solution:** Foot treadles connected by rods and levers to the upper-case lever and spacing levers.

**How it works:** Treadles below the table, on a rod between the legs, connect through rods, levers and links to the upper-case lever key and the spacing bar, so the foot works them. Springs return the parts, and rubber buffers on screws limit lever throw to prevent strain and noise.
