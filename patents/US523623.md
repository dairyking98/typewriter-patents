## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Standard line spacing cannot position lines at small or irregular distances, as needed for filling in forms.

**Solution:** An auxiliary finely toothed wheel inside the platen engages fine serrations on the back of the ordinary line-space ratchet wheel so the platen can be turned in minute increments.

**How it works:** The ratchet wheel turns loosely on the platen shaft and no longer screws to the platen; its back has a ring of fine teeth. An auxiliary wheel slides on the shaft inside a slitted metal shell in the platen, with projections fitting the slits so turning it turns the platen. Sliding the auxiliary wheel along the shaft engages or disengages it from the fine teeth, allowing platen adjustments of fractional line spaces while ordinary spacing is retained.
