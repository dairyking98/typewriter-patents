## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding and listing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a different mode of notifying the operator when the list is complete as by causing the numbered keys `or other manipulative amount-determining or setting-up devices to become locked.

**How it works (reading):** The paper advance of a Burroughs adding and listing machine is automated with a roller platen driven step by step by ratchet and pawl, and the operator is notified when the maximum item count is reached.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** In adding and listing machines the operator must watch the printing and count items to know when a list reaches its set maximum length.

**Solution:** The paper advance of a Burroughs adding and listing machine is automated with a roller platen driven step by step by ratchet and pawl, and the operator is notified when the maximum item count is reached.

**How it works:** Platen journal is driven by a ratchet and pawl linked to the operating mechanism; the paper advances per operation and a signal stops the list at the set length.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
