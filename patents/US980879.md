## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter's platen cannot be quickly swapped for a flat card or laundry-marking platen.

**Solution:** An interchangeable segmental platen with oscillating and tilting movement and a clamp swaps with the ordinary platen in under a minute.
