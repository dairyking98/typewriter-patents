## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (value document printer type carrier lock)

**How it works (reading):** The pawls are locked by a stepwise rotated locking roller driven by an intermittent gear; the roller's elevations and depressions hold or release the pawl noses, and the standby position of the gear locks the mechanism automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sliding bars with projections that lock the type-wheel pawls in a security note printer can bend the pawls, and the bar needs awkward, costly guides.

**Solution:** The pawls are locked by a stepwise rotated locking roller driven by an intermittent gear; the roller's elevations and depressions hold or release the pawl noses, and the standby position of the gear locks the mechanism automatically.

**Features:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
