## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable front-strike typewriters were too bulky to carry; a visible-writing machine that collapses to small size is wanted.

**Solution:** The frame is split into a stationary lower section holding type bars and keys and a movable section carrying carriage, platen and ribbon that lowers into it, so the machine collapses to about the height of the keyboard.

**How it works:** The stationary base carries type bars, finger keys and case shift. The movable frame carries paper carriage, platen, carriage actuating mechanism and ink ribbon, and slides up and down relative to the base. When lowered, its parts nest in spaces between those on the base. Moving parts are not linked to the keys, so nothing needs disconnecting when collapsing.

**Features:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** stationary base frame; movable frame; type bar; finger key; platen; paper carriage; ink ribbon; line space pawl; carriage escapement

**Numbers:** about one-third of the space of other front strike machines; collapses to about the height of the keyboard
