## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for obtaining a double-space movement of the papercarriage when an operator may depress simultaneously a letter-key and the space-key, as at the end of a written word, so as to thereby avoid the necessity of making separate space movements to separate the finished word from the next word to be written;

**How it works (reading):** Stops on the escapement dog rocker let the carriage take a double space when the letter key and space key are pressed together.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing a word then spacing needs a separate space stroke when a letter key and the space key are pressed together at a word end.

**Solution:** Stops on the escapement dog rocker let the carriage take a double space when the letter key and space key are pressed together.

**How it works:** A universal bar rocks a dog holder carrying a rigid dog and a spring-loaded feed dog on an escapement wheel geared to the carriage rack. A striker with two rolls on the dog rocker meets two spring-linked pivoted stops, and which stops are moved by the key bail or the shorter space-bail decides single or double spacing.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** key lever; typebar; universal bar; dog rocker; rigid dog; feed dog; escapement wheel; pinion; carriage rack; space key; pivoted stop; striker roll
