**How it works (reading):** The worm shaft is slid axially off the worm wheel so the drum shaft can be turned by hand, and the spring tension is set directly without the fine adjustment engaging.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fine adjustment of the carriage spring tension is awkward on a drum drive.

**Solution:** The worm shaft is slid axially off the worm wheel so the drum shaft can be turned by hand, and the spring tension is set directly without the fine adjustment engaging.
