## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return by a motor-driven friction drive is not reliable and does not start or stop softly.

**Solution:** Friction discs on axes are coupled by friction rollers on levers pressed by springs; margin stops operate a wedge that brings the rollers into engagement, and the springs take up the energy at the end of the return.

**How it works:** The right margin stop lifts the wedge into engagement with the roller levers, and the friction discs drive a toothed wheel on the carriage rack.
