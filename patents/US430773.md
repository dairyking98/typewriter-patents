## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewritin g machines having a vibratory typeactuating device bearing two or more printing-types in combination with a platen capable of moving in the machine to enable it to receive an impression from either of the printvice successively at the same printing-point thereon.

**How it works (reading):** The platen is vibrated bodily at right angles to the plane of the typebar fulcrums so that each of two angled types on a bar strikes squarely at the same printing point.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars carrying two types must reach two different case positions on the same printing line without the bar being tilted or the type striking obliquely.

**Solution:** The platen is vibrated bodily at right angles to the plane of the typebar fulcrums so that each of two angled types on a bar strikes squarely at the same printing point.

**How it works:** Radial typebars each carry two types whose faces are set at an angle to each other. The cylindrical platen, in its carriage, is moved by reciprocating bars under the carriage ways, operated by a shift key through key-lever arms. In each platen position one of the two types meets the paper square on, so upper and lower case print on the same line. The carriage still travels across the machine for letter spacing.
