## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Driving carriages of more than ordinary width needs a more effective drive.

**Solution:** A coiled carriage spring acts through a casing and sleeve with a collar carrying a planetary bevel gear that meshes with a fixed bevel gear, with an adjusting shaft to set the spring tension.
