## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Date, address, signature and similar repeated text take many keystrokes.

**Solution:** A supplemental typebar carrying removable interchangeable type blocks (a word chase) and a signature plate, each worked by one key.

**How it works:** A plate with a rectangular opening and curved arms is attached to the end of the machine frame. A pivoted typebar plate with a groove acting as a chase receives removable word or date blocks and is raised by a pitman rod from a key lever with a return spring. A second typebar has an undercut flange and tapered-head screw forming a dovetail way for a plate bearing a raised autograph signature. One key prints the date and another the signature at the right-hand end without moving the carriage.
