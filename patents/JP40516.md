**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a typewriter for printing a great many characters, such as Oriental characters.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars carrying two or three characters cannot serve Oriental scripts, and moving the whole cylinder or bar carrier for every character is slow or clumsy.

**Solution:** Long slidable (and optionally many-sided, turnable) type bars each carry a row of characters; each key throws its bar and also slides the bar and shifts the paper cylinder by zone so every character reaches one common printing point.

**How it works:** Each type bar, a long seven-sided tube with 196 characters, slides on shafts held by two parallel swinging levers above the paper cylinder. A key presses an upper bar that swings the lever and throws the tube at the cylinder. The key also presses a middle bar, which pushes a rod against a flange whose stud in a screw groove turns the tube to the right face, and a lower bar, which pushes a rod into a collar on the tube to slide it to the right position within a zone. Because only the tube that has jutted out of the row engages the rods, only that tube moves. Seven zone shift keys move the cylinder carriage to front or back through toothed sectors and a toothed bar, and springs return everything.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a typewriter for printing a great many characters, such as Oriental characters. Many type bars, each carrying many characters in a row, are set side by side near the paper cylinder. Each bar is mounted so that it can slide on its support, and by pressing a key, through a suitable mechanism, the bar is slid on its support so that the wanted character faces the printing point of the cylinder, and at the same time the character is printed.
