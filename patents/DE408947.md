## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The main patent DE393928 moves the paper roller to choose among three types per carrier; this addendum applies the idea to machines where the platen stays put.

**Solution:** Each type carrier sits on one arm of a pivoting double lever whose fulcrum is on a vertical slide, and a link guide raised or lowered by electromagnets selects which of three types strikes the platen.

**How it works:** A rotary body is turned by a feeler arm (as in the main patent) so the wanted type carrier faces a solenoid striking member. Each carrier is on the upper arm of a double lever pivoted on a slide in a vertical guide; the lower arm's lug sits between the forks of a link guide concentric with the pivot. Energising upper or lower magnets raises or lowers the link guide, shifting the double lever so the upper, middle or lower type faces the platen. The solenoid core then shoots forward, hits the upper arm, and swings the lever so the type strikes the paper roller. A locking lever on the core enters a conical slot in the rotary body for exact setting, and an inclined surface withdraws it on the return stroke so the body can turn again.
