## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing letters directly from type without setting and taking proofs, and forming electrotype moulds.

**Solution:** A horizontal wheel of spring-loaded vertical types is turned by a hand wheel through gearing, locked by a notched stop, and a treadle presses the chosen type down onto a feed table.

**How it works:** Type are fitted vertically near the rim of a horizontal wheel, each with a spiral spring and a retaining pin. A notched annular plate on the wheel is engaged by a spring stop that holds each position. A toothed rim under the wheel meshes with a pinion on a shaft carrying a notched hand wheel with its own stop and handles. An index fixed to the top plate shows which letter is over the slot. A treadle linked through a lever presses the type at the slot down on paper or wax on the feed table. A ratchet with pawl and racks move the table frame for spacing.
