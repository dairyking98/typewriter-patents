**How it works (reading):** The cartridge is locked by hooked bolts engaging eyelets on its underside, so both cartridge and jewellery stay in the window.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Jewellery on a display cartridge can be stolen because the cartridge itself is only pinned to the stand.

**Solution:** The cartridge is locked by hooked bolts engaging eyelets on its underside, so both cartridge and jewellery stay in the window.
