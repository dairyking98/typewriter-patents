## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A machine is wanted to make type indentations in papier-mache for stereotyping.

**Solution:** A revolving cylinder of rocking rods and a geared type ring: a key tips a rod a quarter turn, and a cam then presses it on the paper strip against the matching type.

**How it works:** A pulley-driven cylinder has longitudinal grooves of rocking rods protruding at the end, each reaching a particular key; a type ring on a separate shaft is geared to turn in unison, so each rod covers one type. A depressed key strikes a cam-pin on the rod and turns it a quarter turn. The oblong end then meets a fixed cam which presses it on the strip, which sits in a grooved plate over a forked spring. A second cam lifts the rod, and a spring behind the cam-pin returns it. Milled rod edges grip the back of the strip to feed it.

**Features:** [ELEM:ring](../features/elem-ring.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [IMP:impression-adjust](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)

**Parts:** cylinder; rocking rod; type ring; cam; cam-pin; key; grooved plate; forked spring; band; gear wheel; pulley

**Numbers:** rods protrude about a quarter inch

**Improves on:**

- Dement earlier machine with cylinder and sliding rods: rocking rods with cams
