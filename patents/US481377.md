## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Improvements to the inventor's earlier typewriter (US447438): the platen should be quickly removable so a hard platen can be used for manifolding and a softer one otherwise, and the line feed, spring barrel, ribbon and margin parts needed refining.

**Solution:** A platen whose journals drop into recesses in the carriage risers and are held by spring hooks, with a line-feed pawl, a spring-barrel carriage drive, a key lock and a margin-determining mechanism.

**How it works:** Each carriage riser has a recess for the platen journal and a pivoted spring-loaded hook whose pivot is in line with its point and the journal centre, so lifting at an angle will not release it; a hook is swung to free the platen, and replacing it pushes the inclined hook back. A gear on the journal is turned by a pawl on a bell-crank lever with a link, giving a line feed, with a stepped detent for line-space length. A spring barrel with a locking and winding screw drives the carriage. There are ribbon-spool gears with retaining springs, a key-locking mechanism and tapered margin-determining parts.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** base; carriage rod; carriage; riser; platen; journal; spring hook; line-feed gear; pawl; bell-crank lever; detent; spring barrel; ribbon-spool gear; type-bar locking plate; margin mechanism; pressure roll
