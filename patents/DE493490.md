## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements of selective printing mechanisms, e.g. ink-jet printers or thermal printers, specially adapted for supporting or handling copy material in continuous form, e.g. webs

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper web for continuous writing Typewriters and similar \ machines in which the individual sheets of the superimposed Original, carbon and transparent straps separated from one another by perforations - are in order, after writing on the sheets, these together with the ones below To be able to separate tapes from the continuous tape.

**How it works (reading):** The sheets of the form set are perforated, and tabs punched and bent over just behind the perforation join the layers together so they cannot separate and wind unevenly on the roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** After separating a continuous-form set, the superimposed sheets peel apart at the perforation and jam feeding the next part.

**Solution:** The sheets of the form set are perforated, and tabs punched and bent over just behind the perforation join the layers together so they cannot separate and wind unevenly on the roller.
