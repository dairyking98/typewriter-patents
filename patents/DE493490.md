> Description Translated from German Paper web for continuous writing on writing and similar Machinery . The invention relates to a paper web for continuous writing Typewriters and similar \ machines in which the individual sheets of the superimposed Original, carbon and transparent straps separated from one another by perforations - are in order, after writing on the sheets, these together with the ones below To be able to separate tapes from the continuous tape. With the hitherto known tapes of this type, there was one D'belstand, as after a component has been separated, the individual superimposed Sheets peeled apart at their separation edge in such a way that the introduction of the following part of the tape into the typewriter was associated with difficulties. In order to avoid this, the invention provides a mutual safeguard of the superimposed sheets of tape, which allow the avoids tapes lying one below the other. So that these fuses when winding Do not apply too much of the tape to the roller, the paper tapes will be short behind the perforation through tabs punched out and bent over in a known manner connected with each other.
