## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (voltage drop relay) · **Subject (patent classes):** Protective switches, e.g. circuit-breaking switches, or protective relays operated by abnormal electrical conditions otherwise than solely by excess current operated by voltage falling below a predetermined value, e.g. for no-volt protection

**How it works (reading):** The escapement's travel is made dependent on the voltage drop, so the difference between the retraction force and the restraint sets the release time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Delay on voltage-drop relays can only vary about 3 to 1, far short of the 7 to 1 needed.

**Solution:** The escapement's travel is made dependent on the voltage drop, so the difference between the retraction force and the restraint sets the release time.
