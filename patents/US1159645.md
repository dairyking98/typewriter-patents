## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Thin ring-shaped ball-bearing members for typebar pivots distort when hardened, so the thread's axis no longer matches the axis of the conical ball race.

**Solution:** Make the ring blank from a hardenable inner core and a soft outer shell, harden it, grind the race, then cut the thread on the soft part concentric to the finished race.

**How it works:** A blank is turned from bar stock having a hardenable steel core and an outer wrapper that hardens little. After hardening, the inner portion is hard and the soft shell restrains distortion. The conical ball race is ground on the hard inner portion and the end face trued. The blank is then held on a centring mandrel by the finished race and the external thread is chased on the soft shell, so thread and cone share an axis. Two such rings screw into a split eye at the typebar end to form an adjustable ball race for the Smith Premier typebar pivot, clamped by a screw.
