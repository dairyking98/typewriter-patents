## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer; Pivoted pawls

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a copyholder that while the operator has complete access to the manuscript an observer is prevented from overlooking the same and thereby gaining a knowledge of its import;

**How it works (reading):** A copy holder with a visual aperture on a hood and a hand key presents the copy line by line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator's copy can be overlooked by observers, and lines must be presented one or two at a time.

**Solution:** A copy holder with a visual aperture on a hood and a hand key presents the copy line by line.

**How it works:** Hooded copy holder with an aperture and key that presents one or two lines.
