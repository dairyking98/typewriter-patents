## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pneumatic typewriters need one air push per type, making them complex.

**Solution:** A single pneumatic push or piston is made to serve two or more types, selected by a shift key that slides a nipple plate or shutter.

**How it works:** Each key presses a pneumatic cup whose air tube leads to a nipple on a sliding plate at the rear of a battery of type-holder tubes. The shift key moves the plate so that the tube connects with one of a pair (or more) of type-holders; air then expels that type to the paper. Alternatively the push feeds several types at once and a shutter moved by a shift key lets only one issue. A device returns the carriage.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md)

**Parts:** pneumatic push; cup; air tube; nipple plate; type-holder tube; battery of type-holders; shift key; shutter; ring

**Improves on:**

- earlier pneumatic typewriters: one push per type
