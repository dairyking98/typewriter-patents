## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in pneumatically-operated type-writing machines, and has for its object to simplify the construction and improve the working of such machines.
