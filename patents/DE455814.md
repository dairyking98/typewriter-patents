## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a grinding device for grinding the end faces the type body of typewriter types.

**How it works (reading):** Type bodies are fitted into recesses of a ring die body placed against a bell-shaped grinding disk that passes over all the type ends at once, and a feeler-pin precision gauge beside the wheel checks the ground face on the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The end faces (printing base) of individual type bodies must be ground to the same distance from the character base line, and grinding them one by one is slow and hard to check.

**Solution:** Type bodies are fitted into recesses of a ring die body placed against a bell-shaped grinding disk that passes over all the type ends at once, and a feeler-pin precision gauge beside the wheel checks the ground face on the machine.
