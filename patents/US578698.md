## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relate to that class of machines in which each of the type-bars carries a plurality of types, and in which provision is made for causing either of the types of a type-bar to strike at the common printing-point.

**How it works (reading):** Ball-bearing brackets support the shifted carrier and hanger rods hook onto typebars.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar disk shifting machines need better bearings and hanger connections.

**Solution:** Ball-bearing brackets support the shifted carrier and hanger rods hook onto typebars.

**How it works:** The typebar carrier disk rides on ball-bearing blocks in brackets and is shifted by levers to bring upper or lower type to the printing point; hanger rods have hooks.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** typebar; carrier disk; ball-bearing bracket; hanger rod; hook
