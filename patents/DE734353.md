## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Identification marks on office-machine key buttons wear off when colored, and multi-part legends are costly.

**Solution:** The key top is pressed from a synthetic protein-like mass that is colored so the dye penetrates the marks; the uniform body keeps the marks durable.
