## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon must be removed to write mimeograph stencils.

**Solution:** Ribbon raising device that can be disconnected from the key action at will.

**How it works:** A part is shifted so the ribbon lifting means is either driven by the key levers or left idle, so types do not hit the ribbon and wax stencils can be cut without removing it. Key levers rock on a rod with a segmental pad, return springs and links.

**Features:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** key lever; ribbon; segmental pad; spring
