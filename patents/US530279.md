## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements `in a type-writing or matrix making machine, in which type characters, in relief, upon pivotal type-bars are actuated from a key-board to imprint uniformly in a certain fixed tield across which the material to receive the characters is caused to travel.

**How it works (reading):** A keyboard-driven typebar machine whose matrix bed travels past a fixed print field, spaced by unit-width differential spacing plates matched to type widths.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A machine is wanted to impress type characters uniformly in a fixed field, either as a typewriter or to make matrices in plastic material for stereotype casting.

**Solution:** A keyboard-driven typebar machine whose matrix bed travels past a fixed print field, spaced by unit-width differential spacing plates matched to type widths.

**How it works:** Pivoted relief typebars are operated from a keyboard, one key per type, and strike in one fixed field. The matrix holder or paper travels across that field on a laterally sliding carrier frame. Keys are grouped by the unit width of their type (2 to 7 units of an 8-unit pica em) beside space keys of equal unit width, and differential spacing plates and reciprocating rods advance the bed by the matching amount. An impression-governing mechanism sets the depth of impression, and a dial with movable pointer is also shown.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Impression:** [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** typebar; type holder; keyboard; space key; differential spacing plate; reciprocating rod; matrix bed; sliding carrier frame; impression depth governor; dial and pointer

**Numbers:** 84 type bars and characters; 8 space keys; 40 type keys shown; 8 units to the pica em; ten point long primer; 6 sheets of drawings
