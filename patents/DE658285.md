## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms; Levers having moving or variable fulcra to alter the mechanical advantage during the stroke

**How it works (reading):** Raise the type lever with a continuously coupled eccentric drive and then press its head to the platen with a rotating pressure cam near the print point.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Power typewriters with eccentric type lever lifting give neither the type's momentum nor the pressure for a quiet imprint, and late clutching wastes time.

**Solution:** Raise the type lever with a continuously coupled eccentric drive and then press its head to the platen with a rotating pressure cam near the print point.

**How it works:** A rotating eccentric disc lifts the type lever through two double-legged levers, releasing it near top dead centre to a pressure cam shortly before the platen. The cam, driven by a toothed shaft with one cam turn per tooth, has an entry curve that accelerates the lever and a rounded exit; it presses on bolts or rollers beside the type head. Springs let the lever lift without skidding. Impression strength is adjusted by moving the platen.
