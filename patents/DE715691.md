**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates now "on the electrolytic deposition of measuring sing, copper, zinc, hadmiuni from alkaline Cvanid baths.

**How it works (reading):** The bath gets added polymerisation products of unsaturated ketones or aldehydes such as acrolein, giving mirror-bright deposits.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Cyanide-alkaline electroplated brass, copper, zinc and cadmium deposits are dull and not durable as bright coatings.

**Solution:** The bath gets added polymerisation products of unsaturated ketones or aldehydes such as acrolein, giving mirror-bright deposits.
