## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (sheet insertion gear for booking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for the rapid insertion of writing sheets in office machines, in particular booking machines, in which by means of a Transmission gear acting on the platen insertion lever only when it Forward gear.

**How it works (reading):** An intermediate member holding the line-lock lever up is brought out of action by stops at the end of the forward gear, so the locking roller engages at the right moment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On rapid insertion-lever release, the platen can keep turning unsecured before the locking roller re-engages the line wheel.

**Solution:** An intermediate member holding the line-lock lever up is brought out of action by stops at the end of the forward gear, so the locking roller engages at the right moment.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
