## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper feed of front-strike machines has several defects in roll support, paper table, finger adjustment, scale support and platen ratchet and brake.

**Solution:** Feed rolls mounted on the underside of the platen frame, an improved paper table, adjustable paper fingers and a scale support, plus a ratchet and brake for the platen.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
