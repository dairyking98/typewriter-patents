## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper carriage must return and advance the paper one line automatically and feed a succession of sheets.

**Solution:** Elastic paper-holding and feeding rollers driven by spring-actuated drums advance the paper a line and feed sheets.
