## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An inn cash register needs a main adder and several individual adders whose subtotals and totals can be printed without separate mechanisms.

**Solution:** Turning one single lever to a larger or smaller angle sets the push rods so that the transport device engages either the main adder or the chosen individual adder when the main shaft rotates.
