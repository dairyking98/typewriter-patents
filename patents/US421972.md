## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix impression device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines adapted to present type-dies in succession at a common point to be impressed by an impression device into a matrix-body;

**How it works (reading):** The plunger is driven from an eccentric strap holding two eccentrics; a coil spring turns the larger eccentric relative to the smaller, so the strap moves only when the smaller turns within the larger.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The plunger drive that depresses each type-die as it is presented to the impression point needs improving.

**Solution:** The plunger is driven from an eccentric strap holding two eccentrics; a coil spring turns the larger eccentric relative to the smaller, so the strap moves only when the smaller turns within the larger.
