## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must reverse automatically at the end of its travel.

**Solution:** A screw-threaded shaft with bevel gears is alternately engaged by a gear on a shaft driven from the carriage spring; a tripping latch on the shaft reverses the ribbon spools.

**How it works:** Carriage spring drives a shaft whose bevel gears reverse the spools through a trip latch.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
