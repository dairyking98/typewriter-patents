## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar keystroke is hard and noisy because of friction at the typebar bearings and the hard impact felt in the key.

**Solution:** Typebars rest floating on a steel wire and band guide, and the key or intermediate lever is made spring-flexible so the impact is softened.

**How it works:** Each typebar rides in slotted combs; the front comb has a rolled edge containing a steel wire, and a steel band behind the rear comb lets the type block float a few tenths of a millimetre above the lower guide plate. Once the typebar has moved part-way it settles onto the guide plate for line accuracy near the pressure point. The key lever or intermediate lever is spring steel, delaying and cushioning force transfer so the finger feels less impact.
