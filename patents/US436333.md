## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage runs with friction on sliding guides and needs a better escapement and margin regulation.

**Solution:** Carriage rides on steel balls in inclined grooves of an adjustable track, with a two-pawl rack escapement and a rocker-bar margin regulator.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on balls in grooves of two guide rails, one adjustable by set screws, balls bearing only on inclined planes so contact is minimal and the carriage cannot lift. A spring and chain draw the carriage. A rack bar on the carriage rear is engaged alternately by two pawls: a key-driven rock-shaft arm engages pawl 15 on the next tooth while a cam lever lifts pawl 17 clear, so the carriage moves one tooth per stroke, the relation adjustable by a screw. A rocker bar hinged on the carriage releases the pawls, and a margin gauge catch is also shown.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** carriage; guide rail; steel ball; adjustable track; rack bar; rock-shaft; pawl; cam lever; adjusting screw; rocker bar; margin gauge; platen cradle; spring and chain
