## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing machine with stamp box) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an embossing machine with a stamp box stored raised and recessed stamps, one of which by means of a keyboard Matching pair of dies selected and powered by stamping pistons is pressed against a metal plate to be embossed.

**How it works (reading):** The stamp box stays fixed in the centre and the carriage holding the plate is moved to bring the selected letter into position; the plate carrier advances one letter width after each embossing, triggered by the return of the carriage to rest.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving the stamp box to bring the selected punches into the working position is a complicated arrangement in known embossing machines.

**Solution:** The stamp box stays fixed in the centre and the carriage holding the plate is moved to bring the selected letter into position; the plate carrier advances one letter width after each embossing, triggered by the return of the carriage to rest.
