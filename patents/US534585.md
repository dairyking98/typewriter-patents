## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improved dog (pawl) mechanism is wanted, along with a way to lock key levers, type-bars and carriage for packing and shipping.

**Solution:** Refinements to the portable typebar machine of US447438: improved escapement dogs, locking of key levers and carriage for transport, a new ribbon spool gear, margin guard, paper table, bell hammer and paper guide.

**How it works:** The key-lever dog mechanism acts on a rack-bar to space the carriage, with dogs positioned differently when the key is up or down. Devices lock the key levers, type-bars and carriage against movement in all directions for shipping, and a clamp and lug fasten the machine to its base or packing case. A paper table can be locked upright to ease platen removal, and a spring drum with a yielding abutment pulls the carriage. A bell on the platen journal is struck by a one-piece hammer arm.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** platen; bell; bell hammer; key lever; dog; rack-bar; spring drum; paper table; margin guard; paper guide; ribbon spool gear wheel; packing clamp; lug; base
