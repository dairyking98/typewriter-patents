## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**How it works (reading):** A grid-like peak type is attached to the typebar and perforates the paper at impact, and a thinner lever reduces fluttering for regular stop spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary type on typewriters cannot perforate checks against forgery.

**Solution:** A grid-like peak type is attached to the typebar and perforates the paper at impact, and a thinner lever reduces fluttering for regular stop spacing.
