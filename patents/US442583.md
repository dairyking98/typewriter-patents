## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriages normally have to be returned by hand after each line.

**Solution:** The spring that normally pulls the carriage is reversed: key action winds it as it moves the carriage by positive gearing, and a key releases it to return the carriage.

**How it works:** A spring in a case on the frame is connected to the carriage by a band and held by a dog on a ratchet. Each key or space-bar stroke turns a rock-shaft whose dog steps a ratchet one tooth, turning shafts and bevel gears and a pinion that moves the carriage one space through a yielding rack. Moving the carriage draws the band and winds the spring. A return key lifts the rack out of the pinion and the spring pulls the carriage home while also stepping the paper roller one line.
