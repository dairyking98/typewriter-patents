## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paraffin plug of a double ampoule softens in tropical heat and is attacked by the solution, and a metal-alloy plug forms a ball that is hard to clear.

**Solution:** Wood's metal plugs the capillary, a vacuum is set in the lower chamber, and the plug is melted with a small flame so it is sucked clear without ball formation.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Double ampoule. By the patent 366 385 a storage container for easily decomposable substances in solutions has become known, in which the solvent to be dissolved and the substance are stored in two separate divisions payoffs, which are connected by a capillary. The capillary is secured by a plug made of easily fusible material, e.g. B. from paraffin or a mixture of paraffin with wax and resins to complete. This closure has not performed well in use, particularly in hot climates. Its melting point must not be too high so that it can be removed from the capillary without excessive heating. If, however, a mixture with a sufficiently low melting point is used, it turns out that it already partially softens at the temperatures of the tropics and is then attacked by the solution in one compartment, so that it is not completely impermeable in the long run. It was now successful, especially in easily melting metal alloys in the well-known one consisting of bismuth, cadmium, tin and lead, which melts at 65 ° Wood's metal to find a material that does not have the disadvantages mentioned, It does not soften in the tropics, is not attacked by water in any way…
