## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper controlling means are complicated and cannot be easily fitted to carriages of various lengths.

**Solution:** Paper feed rollers hung on carriage hangers, with a rock shaft and tie rods so the feed rollers can be released or thrown off the platen to introduce paper, usable across carriage lengths.
