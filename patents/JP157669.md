## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a Japanese-language typewriter the platen must be fed by an adjustable amount, from one tooth to several teeth, at each printing stroke, and the existing feed is hard to adjust.

**Solution:** A rocking lever with a slide piece running in an arc slot of a pivoted lever sets the feed step, and a toothed, detented adjusting lever moves the slide piece's resting place.

**How it works:** A ratchet wheel sits on the shaft of a pinion that meshes with a rack on the platen, which is spring-driven one way. A rocking lever on the same shaft carries an engaging pawl and a slide piece that rides in the arc slot of an arc-slot lever; that lever also carries the stop pawl through a pin, long slot and spring. An adjusting lever with a row of teeth, held by a spring-loaded roller (the brake), moves the rocking lever so the slide piece rests at a chosen point in the slot, and the distance from there to the slot end sets the feed. At each print stroke a push lever driven by the printing lever swings the arc-slot lever, the stop pawl leaves the ratchet, and the platen advances until the engaging pawl reaches the slot end. On release a spring returns everything and the stop pawl catches the ratchet.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Feed device in a Japanese-language typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a feed device in a Japanese-language typewriter, built as follows. A ratchet wheel is fixed to the shaft of a pinion that meshes with the rack fixed to the platen. A rocking lever is fitted loosely on the same shaft, and at its tip are fitted a slide piece, which fits in the slot of an arc-slot lever carrying the ratchet stop pawl, and a ratchet engaging pawl. The position of the slide piece in the arc slot can be adjusted by an adjusting lever, provided with a "brake" device, that engages the rear end of the rocking lever. The arc-slot lever and the stop pawl are rocked by a spring and a push lever to carry out the required feed. The object is to make it easy to adjust the feed so that printing is done at the required spacing of one tooth to several teeth. Brief description of the drawings.
