## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a mechanical drive device for typewriters etc.

**How it works (reading):** A smooth shaft carries loose eccentric drive pulleys with armature levers, and a roller ratchet locks each pulley centrally to avoid tilting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The main drive design is complex and loses effectiveness.

**Solution:** A smooth shaft carries loose eccentric drive pulleys with armature levers, and a roller ratchet locks each pulley centrally to avoid tilting.
