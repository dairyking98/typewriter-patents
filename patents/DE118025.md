## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the paper carriage to its start takes time, which is a loss on long documents.

**Solution:** A lever or thumb key, turned by hand, rotates a drive and rack that pulls the carriage back against the tension of a spring drum, so the return is quick and always the same.
