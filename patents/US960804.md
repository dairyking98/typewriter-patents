## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The automatic ribbon-reversing mechanism of the Monarch (Seib patent US908703) sometimes failed to actuate properly.

**Solution:** Add safety devices that ensure the ribbon reversing mechanism is actuated.

**How it works:** The carriage spring drum has a pawl and ratchet that move with each letter space and drive a bevel gear pair onto a transversely sliding shaft. A lever with an anti-friction roller in a spring-loaded controlling member's depressions holds the shaft in either end position, and a hand crank can shift it. Bevel pinions at the shaft ends drive fore-and-aft shafts to the ribbon spools. Safety stops associated with each spool (Fig. 5) act at the end of the ribbon. Opening text only; the safety devices are not described in the shown portion.
