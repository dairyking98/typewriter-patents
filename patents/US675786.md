## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in 10 stencil-cutting machines;

**How it works (reading):** A stencil-cutting machine on a single cast base, with plungers whose heads are struck by operating levers and a compound stripper, plus a pivoted support so a stencil can be cut along a curved line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil-cutting machines are complicated and have too many working parts, and the operation is hard to manipulate and not accurate enough.

**Solution:** A stencil-cutting machine on a single cast base, with plungers whose heads are struck by operating levers and a compound stripper, plus a pivoted support so a stencil can be cut along a curved line.

**Features:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
