## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (manifold writing tablet) · **Subject (patent classes):** with web storage arrangements by means of rollers, wheels, or chains, e.g. with pins transversely

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a tablet upon which manifold copies of letters, bills, receipts, contracts, and various other documents may be made by once writing them;

**How it works (reading):** A tablet frame holds the paper with carbon sheets on rollers and a slide, so one writing produces copies on several sheets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making several copies of a written document needed a practical tablet for manifold writing.

**Solution:** A tablet frame holds the paper with carbon sheets on rollers and a slide, so one writing produces copies on several sheets.
