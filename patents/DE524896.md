## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for guiding and keeping the paper webs taut in record typewriters The invention relates to a device for guiding and keeping the paper webs taut with record typewriters. those for writing to several of the supply tapes at the same time removed paper webs are set up. The paper webs fed full of a roll of the typewriter tend to form air sacs between them, resulting in bumps and wrinkles so that the type is completely printed through to the bottom layer of paper is endangered through it. The invention eliminates these disadvantages essentially in that each of the paper webs between a fixed bar and a sliding `fold is passed, the latter of which is mounted in inclined slots and of Springs so affected wildly that the webs clamped at the front end after pulled backwards and thus stretched taut.
