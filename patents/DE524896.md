## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for tensioning or winding the web; Multiple web-feeding apparatus with means for registering the webs with each other

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for guiding and keeping the paper webs taut with record typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Each web runs between a fixed bar and a sliding fold that springs back and pulls the clamped web taut, with rollers and slide rods that feed all the webs at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper webs fed from a roll form air sacs and wrinkles, which can let the type fail to print through to the bottom copy.

**Solution:** Each web runs between a fixed bar and a sliding fold that springs back and pulls the clamped web taut, with rollers and slide rods that feed all the webs at once.
