## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Key heads can work loose or be knocked off their stems, and the characters on the head wear or rub off in use.

**Solution:** The key stem is made of two spring-metal plates with outward lugs that pass through an angular opening in the key-head base and lock it in place, so the head is attached without fasteners.
