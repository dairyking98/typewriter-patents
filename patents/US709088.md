## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to tabulating devices intended for use in conjunction with type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating attachment that releases the carriage at any point in its forward traverse, then arrests it at a set point or at letter-space distances from that point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage cannot be released from its control at any point in the traverse, and stopped at a set point.

**Solution:** A tabulating attachment that releases the carriage at any point in its forward traverse, then arrests it at a set point or at letter-space distances from that point.
