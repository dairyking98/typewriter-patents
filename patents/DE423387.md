## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink supply arrangements therefor; Frames or holders for unwound short lengths of ink ribbons the holder being movable to inoperative position, e.g. by swinging upwardly

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon assembly for typewriters in which the ribbon is designed in the form of a ring and is inserted into a frame that, when hitting a type around one of the The amount corresponding to the letter width is rotated to always a different position to allow the ribbon to get in front of the printing point.

**How it works (reading):** A ring ribbon with several colour zones is moved on a double-ring frame, with a lever lifting it to bring a chosen zone to the print point and ink pads fed from reservoirs re-inking each zone.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ring-ribbon arrangements that rotate the ribbon at every stroke gave only one colour and uneven imprint.

**Solution:** A ring ribbon with several colour zones is moved on a double-ring frame, with a lever lifting it to bring a chosen zone to the print point and ink pads fed from reservoirs re-inking each zone.

**How it works:** A frame carried on a bolt in the type segment slot holds the ring ribbon. A ratchet wheel advances one tooth each time a typebar rises, turning the ribbon so fresh ribbon reaches the print point. A hand lever raises the frame to bring another colour zone in front of the print point. Felt discs, pressed with springs against the ribbon and fed through pipes from ink tanks, re-ink the ribbon.
