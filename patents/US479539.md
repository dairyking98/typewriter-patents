## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix machine character key) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to matrix-making machines in which there is employed as the character-selecting device a key or lever mounted on a horizontally-movable pivot, or, in general, to machines of the general charactershown in patent to Goodson, No.

**How it works (reading):** A supplemental circuit on the feed magnets keeps the feed and depression going after the index contact breaks.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Circuit opens at the index-plate before the depressing device completes its stroke.

**Solution:** A supplemental circuit on the feed magnets keeps the feed and depression going after the index contact breaks.

**Features:** [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
