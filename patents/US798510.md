## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to improvements in means for supporting paper and envelops in position upon a type-writer;

**How it works (reading):** A paper holder with supports is attached to the machine frame to carry paper at the platen (text is partly garbled).
