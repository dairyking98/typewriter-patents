## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operating the carriage feed from the space-key can skip teeth or spaces if the key is not released promptly, and feeding during the key's upstroke slows typing.

**Solution:** Mechanism that prevents the escapement wheel from skipping a tooth by changing the cycle of operation of the space-key, so feeding does not happen during the key's upward movement.

**How it works:** In the standard escapement, a rocker carries a feed dog and a detent dog that engage the escapement wheel. Pressing the space-key swings the rocker; feeding happens on release, which can cause skipping if the key is held. The invention reorders the cycle so the escapement cannot skip a tooth and the next type key can be struck without releasing the space-key.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; feed dog; detent dog; rocker; space-key; carriage feed

**Improves on:**

- space-key feed that skips when the key is held: changed operating cycle prevents tooth skipping
