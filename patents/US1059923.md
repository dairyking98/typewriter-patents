## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to the type bars and hangers of typewriting machines, and to methods of assembling and fitting or adjusting such type bars and hangers.

**How it works (reading):** Hangers of different widths hold the type bars at set positions on a vertical segment, and the hanger arms are kept parallel and secured by riveting so the bars fit correctly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Assembling and fitting the type bars and their hangers to the segment was difficult and costly.

**Solution:** Hangers of different widths hold the type bars at set positions on a vertical segment, and the hanger arms are kept parallel and secured by riveting so the bars fit correctly.
