## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** The segment is grooved or cut out at the type lever bearing point, leaving right-angled webs carrying the driven axle, which reduces guide surfaces and stops lateral fluttering.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever bearing slots in a segment wore and allowed lateral fluttering and friction.

**Solution:** The segment is grooved or cut out at the type lever bearing point, leaving right-angled webs carrying the driven axle, which reduces guide surfaces and stops lateral fluttering.
