## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known noiseless carriage return switches need complicated lever and disc arrangements on the escape wheel axis.

**Solution:** An angled latch carrier with a swing-out ratchet, a friction clutch on the ratchet wheel, and a lever moved by the carriage release button disengage the pawl from the escape wheel using only spring-influenced parts.
