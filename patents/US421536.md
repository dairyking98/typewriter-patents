## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter simple enough to be made at small initial cost.

**Solution:** A hand-moved type-bed carrying a rubber plate of type and a pointer that drops into a hole of an index plate on a tilting table, which advances one ratchet notch each time it is depressed.

**How it works:** Metal end pieces joined by rods, two of them ratchet-bars, carry a large rubber paper roller turned by a thumb wheel and a detent disk for line spacing. A tilting table over the roller has a single small opening that admits one type at a time, an index plate of lettered holes, and a light spring that tilts it up. A sleeve slides and turns on a rod and carries two fingers on which the type-bed slides; the bed has a handle and an arm with an adjustable conical-ended pointer. The pointer is set in the index hole of the wanted letter, which places the matching type of the rubber plate over the table opening; pressing down prints, an inking roller on spring-loaded trunnions inks the type, and the dog on the table moves it one ratchet notch right as it is depressed.
