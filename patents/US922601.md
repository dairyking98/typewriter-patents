## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, but more particularly to spacing mechanism designed to automatically exaggerate the letter space movement of the carriage at the proper point to provide for the decimal, comma or other space.

**How it works (reading):** The mechanism automatically exaggerates the carriage letter-space at the decimal or comma point, and can be set relative to the platen to suit where that point falls or switched off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must press the space key by hand before a decimal point or comma, which is slow and error-prone.

**Solution:** The mechanism automatically exaggerates the carriage letter-space at the decimal or comma point, and can be set relative to the platen to suit where that point falls or switched off.
