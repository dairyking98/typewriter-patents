## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Parallelogram-guided plate-lowering devices needed more space and the machine dropped hard when lowered.

**Solution:** A base slide runs on rails in the cabinet; a parallelogram guide (foot, head and connecting frame) raises and lowers the plate parallel to its foot, with the hands on the headboard taking the machine weight.
