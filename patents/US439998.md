## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front and rear paper clamps on a cylindrical platen must act together.

**Solution:** Clips on shiftable supports are positively linked so moving one moves the other; the front bar is graduated as a scale.

**How it works:** Linked front and rear paper clamps move simultaneously.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
