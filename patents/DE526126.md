## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line switching for calculating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** Pawls in the rest position are raised, and the switching transmission is disengaged so the line switch runs only on reverse crank movement, with the pawls returning automatically after the switch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line switching on calculating machines must not run during forward cranking, and the switching pawls must be lifted by hand before the platen can turn freely.

**Solution:** Pawls in the rest position are raised, and the switching transmission is disengaged so the line switch runs only on reverse crank movement, with the pawls returning automatically after the switch.
