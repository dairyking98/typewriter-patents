## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms; .Vertical writing

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** The object is that, in feeding the character spacing in several steps with a single feed wheel, even if the feed motion (rotation) is made faster or slower by the gravitational and mechanical imbalance of the rotating part, the platen itself, or other causes, it is not troubled by these bad effects and performs an extremely accurate feed, fine or coarse alike.

**How it works (reading):** Engage and release the feed pawl and stop pawl through a purely mechanical linkage, so the stop pawl is seated in the wheel first and only then is the feed pawl lifted off.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A single ratchet wheel that gives several character-spacing sizes feeds inaccurately because platen imbalance or varying stroke speed changes how far the wheel turns before the stop catches.

**Solution:** Engage and release the feed pawl and stop pawl through a purely mechanical linkage, so the stop pawl is seated in the wheel first and only then is the feed pawl lifted off.

**How it works:** A feed wheel sits on the platen shaft, with a rocking plate linked to the typing key. A T-shaped control piece on the plate carries the feed pawl and is held in either of two states by a spring lever. At the end of the forward stroke the control piece hits an end stop and flips over, so the feed pawl bites the wheel and the stop pawl is pushed out. On the key's return stroke the pawl turns the wheel. A projection on the plate then presses a lever on an adjustable limiter (set by arm and scale), which forces the stop pawl into the wheel and lifts the feed pawl off. The limiter position sets the feed amount.

> Specification. Typewriter feed device. Nature and object of the invention. This invention is an addition to Patent No. 104642 (Patent Application No. 7830 of 1932; Examined Publication No. 3321 of 1933).
