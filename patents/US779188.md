## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to ribbon-feed mechanism therefor.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon feed from the carriage spring drum with a longitudinally shiftable drive shaft and automatically actuated locking means that confine feed to the chosen field and reverse it.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbons with two or more fields of different colour or character need feed limited to the field in use, with automatic reversal.

**Solution:** A ribbon feed from the carriage spring drum with a longitudinally shiftable drive shaft and automatically actuated locking means that confine feed to the chosen field and reverse it.

**How it works:** Each ribbon spool shaft has a bevel pinion meshing with pinions on a long shaft that can slide in its bearings; sliding selects which spool is driven. A third pinion on the shaft, splined to it, is kept meshed with a gear on a ratchet wheel hub on the carriage spring-drum spindle. A pawl on the drum drives the ratchet only as the carriage moves in its feed direction, so the ribbon feeds with carriage travel. Automatic locking parts and a shifting device (past the opening text) hold the feed to the field in use and throw the mechanism in or out at will.
