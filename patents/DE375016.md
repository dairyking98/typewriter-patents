## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Details of, or accessories for, typewriters or selective printing mechanisms not otherwise provided for

**How it works (reading):** The retaining slide has projecting edges that overlap the plate and are held under frame projections, so the plate needs no recesses; the slide is thin and bends in, and in one form it is pulled in and out with a key.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Replaceable nameplates or tickets on type levers must not be bound to a special machined recess in every plate; the slide that retains the plate should also hold a plate that is not specially prepared.

**Solution:** The retaining slide has projecting edges that overlap the plate and are held under frame projections, so the plate needs no recesses; the slide is thin and bends in, and in one form it is pulled in and out with a key.
