## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holders therefor with fluids

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The aim is to use this device to create an incorrectly posted typewriter letter removed, the typist takes the apparatus shown in Fig.

**How it works (reading):** Chemical bleach is applied to the typed character, then a neutralising second solution cancels it so the spot can be retyped at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasing typed characters with an eraser tears paper and crumbs jam the machine.

**Solution:** Chemical bleach is applied to the typed character, then a neutralising second solution cancels it so the spot can be retyped at once.
