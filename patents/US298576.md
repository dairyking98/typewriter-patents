## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The clock-spring in the carriage drum cannot be tension-adjusted without taking the machine apart, and a raised carriage can fall on the rule and paper guide.

**Solution:** Mount the spring drum on a sliding plate that can be disengaged from the rack and locked, and add a hook that holds the carriage when raised.

**How it works:** The toothed drum with its clock-spring rides on the carriage and meshes with a rack bar, stepping the carriage as a catch is released. Here the drum sits on a rabbeted sliding plate with two sliding pins that hold the drum teeth while it is out of mesh and lock the plate in notches when the drum is in mesh. To change tension the operator locks the drum, slides it off the rack, moves the carriage left or right and re-engages at a different point. A pivoted hook on the carriage secures it when tilted up against the bell shaft.
