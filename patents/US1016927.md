## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon always presented the same strip to the type, and the control had to work in both positions of a shifting platen.

**Solution:** A control that varies the vibratory movement of the ribbon vibrator to present the upper or lower width of the ribbon, mounted on the platen shift frame so it works in either case.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 10 style machine, a ribbon vibrator slides in guides and is connected by a stud to a horizontal lever fulcrumed on a bracket on the shift cross bar. A link joins that lever to a bell crank on the same bar, so the mechanism moves with the shifted platen. Setting the control changes the stroke so the upper or lower field of the ribbon meets the types.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; bell crank; lever; link; shift cross bar; platen frame; universal bar

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 10: allows choosing upper or lower ribbon field
