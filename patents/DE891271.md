## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Drawing lines on typewriters with a ruler or pen is not practical.

**Solution:** A holder with a threaded sleeve takes a ballpoint refill and clamps onto the line sheet, so the carriage draws lines as the roller turns.
