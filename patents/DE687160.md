**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with steep and cross counters.

**How it works (reading):** Two calculating gear sets: the steep counter is driven by number keys and the cross counter is set by keys then driven by a stored energy device.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters with many counters are limited because the number key drives the whole counter train.

**Solution:** Two calculating gear sets: the steep counter is driven by number keys and the cross counter is set by keys then driven by a stored energy device.
