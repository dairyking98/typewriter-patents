## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line spacing on typewriters is fixed and does not suit variously ruled paper.

**Solution:** An adjustable stop on the spacing lever and a rocking cam-and-pawl on a platen head give variable line spacing with a pointer scale.

**How it works:** The spacing lever swings up and, through a link, rocks a plate that carries a cam which bites on a friction tread on a platen end head, turning the platen. The upward travel is limited by an adjustable stop screw, setting the distance between lines. A pointer on the plate reads against a scale, a spring returns the parts, and a brake block holds the platen. Pressing extensions together frees the cam so the platen can be turned back.
