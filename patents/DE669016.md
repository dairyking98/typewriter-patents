## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material; Manifolding or like arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon device on calculating machines, typewriters and the like.

**How it works (reading):** Two colour ribbons form one endless strand with replaceable pins, with spring tensioning to take up slack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two ribbon webs on one spool pair stretch unevenly, causing slack and poor print.

**Solution:** Two colour ribbons form one endless strand with replaceable pins, with spring tensioning to take up slack.
