## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** Column stops in each group are united on a rotatable body with spider straps; a button-driven pawl and linkage set a whole group into position or disengage it, with a run-up bar lifting stops on carriage return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On tabulator machines with grouped column stops, the stops had to be removed and reinserted by hand to change groupings.

**Solution:** Column stops in each group are united on a rotatable body with spider straps; a button-driven pawl and linkage set a whole group into position or disengage it, with a run-up bar lifting stops on carriage return.
