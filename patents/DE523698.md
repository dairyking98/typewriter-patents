## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Needle-type machines that form letters from moving needles need a compact way to drive closely packed needles reliably.

**Solution:** Connect the needle ends through flexible Bowden cables to solenoid cores arranged in rows below.

**How it works:** About 108 needles are arranged radially with tips close together on a curved surface matching the platen. Each needle is pushed by a ring solenoid through a Bowden cable, with the solenoids stacked in rows. Pressing a key energises all solenoids needed for a letter so that the advanced needle tips form its dot pattern against the platen through a ribbon. Some needles are shared between letters.
