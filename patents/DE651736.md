## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A lower writing limit needs a precisely cut groove in the platen, which is hard to make.

**Solution:** A stationary half ring fits in a wide platen groove and gives a lower-edge trigger that engages a lock or signal.

**How it works:** The half ring sits in the groove where types strike, and the button falls into it at the sheet edge to trigger the lock.
