## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Guide points on the type lever must be hardened and ground to size, which risks breakage and makes precise thickness hard to hold.

**Solution:** A hardened and ground steel ball is pressed into a hole at the impact point of the type lever, protruding slightly on both sides to run in the type lever guide, with the guide spacing matched to the ball diameter.
