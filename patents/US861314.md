## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typebar machine with two characters per bar needs a platen shift that can be held in either position or flicked to the other position by a key, returning on release.

**Solution:** Mount the platen frame on forward-inclined rods so it gravitates to its lower position, and raise it with a spring-loaded lever linkage that can be latched or temporarily overridden by two shift keys.

**How it works:** The platen frame rides on rollers along two inclined rods on the carriage and rests on stop collars at the front by gravity. A bar between two pivoted levers pushes the frame rearward, sliding it up the incline. A hand arm on a rock shaft, via coil springs and crank levers, pushes the bar and is latched by a lug to hold the raised position. A shift key lever acts on a bent lever to push the bar back for as long as the key is held, and a second key lever depresses the bent lever to let the platen drop temporarily while the latch holds, with springs restoring it on release.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen frame; inclined rod; stop collar; bar; pivoted lever; rock shaft; operating arm; coil spring; shift key lever; bent lever; lug

**Numbers:** two platen positions
