## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Grooved type wheel cuts the fibrous inking roller because the peripheral speeds differ.

**Solution:** Inking roller and type wheel are driven to the same peripheral speed, so the grooves do not cut the roller.

**How it works:** Roller and type wheel rotate together at equal peripheral speed, with ribs on the roller.
