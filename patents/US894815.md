## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making lithographic imitation typewriting by setting type or photographing a typed letter is slow and costly.

**Solution:** A napped satin or nap-faced ribbon is coated with silicate of soda and then a greasy ink, so that typing transfers directly from the ribbon to paper and then to stone, zinc or other plate.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
