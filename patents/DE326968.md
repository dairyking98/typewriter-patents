## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a guide the ribbon - just before winding it onto the ribbon reel on typewriters, Calculating machines and similar machines working with ink ribbon, and are intended to be to enable easy and clean insertion and removal of the ribbon at the same time but also good guidance of the ribbon before winding it onto the ribbon spool to secure.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon guide on the spool plate can swing inward to thread the ribbon and then swing back to form a closed slot.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed ribbon guide forks were hard to thread, dirtied hands and tore thin ribbon.

**Solution:** A ribbon guide on the spool plate can swing inward to thread the ribbon and then swing back to form a closed slot.
