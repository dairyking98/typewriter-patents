## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (laundry marking machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**How it works (reading):** A single numbered segment is turned by a cam-set setting segment; each key's cam sets it to the right position, and striking the key prints the number.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Multi-digit numbers on laundry marking normally need several separately set digit wheels set by hand.

**Solution:** A single numbered segment is turned by a cam-set setting segment; each key's cam sets it to the right position, and striking the key prints the number.
