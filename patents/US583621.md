## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing in lines needs carriage return.

**Solution:** Type-bars across the paper and moving anvils print successive transverse lines automatically.

**How it works:** Printing bars span the paper and moving anvils pass along them as keys are struck, ribbon pressed to paper, paper stepping.

**Features:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** printing bar; anvil; ribbon; paper feed
