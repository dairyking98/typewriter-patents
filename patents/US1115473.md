## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Justified lines are slow to set; a machine is wanted to print justified lines automatically from a controller strip.

**Solution:** A printing apparatus whose type wheels are stopped and character impressions made under control of a perforated controller strip that also governs spaces and justification.

**How it works:** A controller strip made by a companion machine is fed past sensing devices. Their selections arrest rotating type wheels at the chosen character, and a momentum device releases the impression mechanism when a wheel stops. The type-wheel carrier can shift, and fluid valves are involved. The carriage is spaced to form justified lines. Only the opening of a very long description is available.

**Features:** [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [USE:telegraph-printer](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** controller strip; type wheel; stop lever; momentum device; type wheel carrier; valve; carriage

**Improves on:**

- application Serial 73,824 (same assignee): this machine reads the strip that machine produces
