## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier carrying-case mounts needed a separate locking device that was often forgotten, or a base plate that interfered with office use.

**Solution:** Slide rails on the case bottom take the machine's undercut feet, and a hinged front lid with stops on the back wall locks the machine in both directions when the lid is closed.

**How it works:** The machine is pushed into the rails until the lid stops hold it, so closing the lid secures it without a separate latch.

**Parts:** slide rails; stop buffers; hinged lid; carrying case
