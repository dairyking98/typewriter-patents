## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special supports, platforms or trolleys for supporting machines on tables

**How it works (reading):** The pad is a hollow metal, wood or plastic body with resilient material and elastic bandages at the feet that grip the table, a smooth outside, and a central drawer for cleaning tools.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Felt typewriter pads compress under the feet, let fibres into the machine joints, and slide when the carriage is thrown back.

**Solution:** The pad is a hollow metal, wood or plastic body with resilient material and elastic bandages at the feet that grip the table, a smooth outside, and a central drawer for cleaning tools.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter pad The usual documents for typewriters and similar office machines are massive; they are mostly made of felt. The elasticity of these documents is insufficient. The typewriter feet press themselves into the base, so that fibers of the felt plate can easily enter the machine and get stuck in the joints of the machine parts. Another disadvantage is that felt on the tabletop has only a fairly small friction and therefore when the typewriter cart is thrown back, the machine slips slightly, like this that typing errors after the line break can easily be made when typing. These disadvantages are avoided by the subject matter of the invention. To the invention is the base of a hollow body made of sheet metal, wood, plastic o. The like. Formed in the area of the typewriter feet with flexible material filled and closed to the outside by elastic bandages that are made of the Level sticking out of the bottom of the pad and pretty much on the work table have great friction. The typewriter pad according to the invention is immovable firmly on the work table, and it is completely smooth on the outside and at the same time in high Dimensions elastic.…
