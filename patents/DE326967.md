## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is a switching device for the ribbon drive of typewriters, which are an additional invention to that protected by patent 32q215 Invention represents;

**How it works (reading):** Sector-shaped control disks with a flap and an obliquely recessed channel, driven by feeler levers in the ribbon, keep the switching lever moving so the correct reel drive is engaged.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Worm-driven ribbon reel drives had no reliable protection against the switchover jumping back during fast movement.

**Solution:** Sector-shaped control disks with a flap and an obliquely recessed channel, driven by feeler levers in the ribbon, keep the switching lever moving so the correct reel drive is engaged.

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
