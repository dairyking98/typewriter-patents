## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A visible-writing typebar machine needs a carriage feed whose rack and pinion never disengage, a platen shift that keeps line spacing and ribbon position right in both cases, and easier paper loading for manifold work.

**Solution:** Keep the carriage rack permanently meshed with a pinion and escapement wheel, with a detent that can be thrown clear, and carry the platen shift, line-space pawl and ribbon throw so they work in either case position.

**How it works:** Typebars are swung by key levers onto a platen carried on arms on a rock shaft inside a spring-driven carriage. A rack on the carriage meshes permanently with a pinion whose shaft carries an escapement wheel. A two-tooth spring detent lets the wheel advance one notch each time a universal bar under the key and space-bar levers is pressed and released. A push-button slide plate throws the detent clear so the carriage runs freely in either direction. Two shift keys rock bell cranks and a shift rod to swing the platen up and back for capitals, and a weighted latch locks it when the right key is used and releases it when the left key is used. A ribbon-throw plate carried by a toggle linkage lifts the ribbon under the type at each stroke and travels further when the platen is shifted. A line-space lever on the carriage drives a platen ratchet with a pawl, and a pivoted stop selects full or half spaces. A rocking roller frame with two feed rollers and a third guide roller lets stacks of manifold sheets be inserted and aligned.
