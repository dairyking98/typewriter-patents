## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type drum for line or single letter casting) · **Subject (patent classes):** Kinds or types of apparatus for mechanical composition in which the type is cast or moulded from matrices operating with fixed combinations of matrices for casting individual characters or spaces, e.g. type casting machines

**How it works (reading):** A drum with several type or matrix wreaths on a shaft is kept reciprocating by a mechanical assistant, and button-controlled stops set the axial position of the chosen wreath before each impression.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keystroke-driven relative movement cannot move a heavy type drum fast enough for embossing or casting, so drum selection is too slow.

**Solution:** A drum with several type or matrix wreaths on a shaft is kept reciprocating by a mechanical assistant, and button-controlled stops set the axial position of the chosen wreath before each impression.
