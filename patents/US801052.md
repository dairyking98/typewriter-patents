## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**How it works (reading):** An improved escape-wheel gives proper dog clearance, and a rigid ratchet dog with a yieldable dog holds the carriage and lets it step one tooth on key release.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction in the escapement is too high, forcing heavy tension on the paper carriage, and the dogs do not clear the escape-wheel properly.

**Solution:** An improved escape-wheel gives proper dog clearance, and a rigid ratchet dog with a yieldable dog holds the carriage and lets it step one tooth on key release.

**Features:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
