## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** When the platen shifts to upper case and back, the paper's lower edge fouls the spring-pressed paper-restraining wings and is displaced, so the next line prints askew.

**Solution:** A slide shifter with cam faces pushes the restraining wings away from the platen and locks them before the platen returns to lower case.

**How it works:** Hinged wings on the type segment are pressed to the platen by springs and limited by fingers. A slide with inclined cam faces, guided by ears on a bracket, with a finger-piece at each end, wipes under the wing fingers to swing and lock the wings forward away from the platen. Moving the slide back lets the springs return the wings. Shoulders on the slide limit its travel.
