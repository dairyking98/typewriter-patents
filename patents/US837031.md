## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The inventor says ribbon feed needs simple mechanism, and the ribbon direction must reverse automatically when an end is reached.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools with a releasable engaging device, a ribbon vibrator arm and guide pulley, and mechanism that reverses ribbon feed at each end.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
