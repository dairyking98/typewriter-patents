## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Light work sheets were lifted or torn by drafts when manifolding on typewriters.

**Solution:** A lockable guard holds down the work sheets on the paper table while allowing them to feed.

**How it works:** A curved guard pivots over the paper table and locks in position to hold the sheets down.
