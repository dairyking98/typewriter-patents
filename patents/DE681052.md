## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** Interchangeable stop parts are mounted in various positions opposite the actuators, selected by the keys, so each key sets the carriage step size for its character.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Step sizes for characters of very different widths (oriental scripts) cannot be changed without major machine changes.

**Solution:** Interchangeable stop parts are mounted in various positions opposite the actuators, selected by the keys, so each key sets the carriage step size for its character.
