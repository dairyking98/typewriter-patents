## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing front-strike machines need a platen that can shift obliquely for case change and typebars arranged compactly with gentle key feel.

**Solution:** A platen frame shifting obliquely independent of the carriage, with typebars pivoted on an inclined segment and driven by eccentric gears from key levers.

**How it works:** Type-bar hangers sit in radial slots on an obliquely placed segment, two hangers per screw. Each typebar is joined to a lever by eccentric gears, so it starts slowly under the finger and moves fastest near the platen. Second-order key levers on a common fulcrum rod operate the levers. The platen frame shifts obliquely upward on the carriage for upper case via keyboard-connected devices; printing falls between the top and front of the platen.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type-bar; hanger; inclined segment; eccentric gear; lever; key lever; fulcrum rod; platen frame; carriage
