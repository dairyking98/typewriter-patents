According to the present invention, this plate is widened or extension that is designed and extended so far that it comes into contact with flattened areas of set screws that are in better known Way to serve as a storage for the return springs of the key levers. Devices of this type consisted of three parts, a retaining plate for the lever mounting, a locking plate for the adjustment screws and finally one or more springs that hold the latter plate against the adjustment screws to press.
