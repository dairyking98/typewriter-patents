## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**How it works (reading):** The key lever support plate is extended with a U-shaped flexible tongue that rests on flats of the adjusting screws holding the return springs, so one body holds the levers and secures the springs against loosening.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The key lever bearing, its holding plate, and the return-spring adjusting screws are three separate parts, and the lever rod can jump off or the levers can shift.

**Solution:** The key lever support plate is extended with a U-shaped flexible tongue that rests on flats of the adjusting screws holding the return springs, so one body holds the levers and secures the springs against loosening.
