## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen rubber gives a loud noise when type bars strike, and a hard, dense surface is needed without denting.

**Solution:** A platen made of felt disks compressed to a dense surface over a softer backing gives a hard, quiet impression surface.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
