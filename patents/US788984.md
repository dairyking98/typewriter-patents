## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in the type-actions of type-writing machines, especially those visible writing machines which are known as front-strike machines, the general object of the invention being to prevent the type-bars from rebounding when they return to their normal positions.
