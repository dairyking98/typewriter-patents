## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in the type-actions of type-writing machines, especially those visible writing machines which are known as front-strike machines, the general object of the invention being to prevent the type-bars from rebounding when they return to their normal positions.

**How it works (reading):** Type-bars are carried on separately detachable hangers on a segmental support, actuated by a key-lever, sublever and link, with a hanger and link arrangement that stops the rebound.

**Problem:** Type-bars rebound when they return to their normal positions in a front-strike machine.

**Solution:** Type-bars are carried on separately detachable hangers on a segmental support, actuated by a key-lever, sublever and link, with a hanger and link arrangement that stops the rebound.
