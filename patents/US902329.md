## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type impressors on a carrier must move freely to the working point yet be accurately positioned and rigidly held while striking.

**Solution:** A rotary carrier brings the chosen impressor-bearing slide into the vertical plane of the working point, the slide is shifted radially toward it, and it is precisionized and held for the impression.

**How it works:** A vertical shaft carries a rotary carrier with slides in circumferential guideways, one impressor per slide. Rotating the carrier brings a chosen slide into the plane of the working point. The slide is then shifted toward the working point and finally locked precisely. Thrust-receiving faces on the slideway walls, placed well toward the outer part, resist the forces of the impression.
