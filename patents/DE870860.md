## Summary

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a numbering mallet for drawing wood and tree trunks with a rotatable Single disc and numbers that can be placed on a special extension piece, which thanks to its surprisingly simple construction and easy handling when pleasing Exterior.

**How it works (reading):** The single-digit disc slides axially and rotates; pushing and turning the handle locks a bolt into the tens disc, giving any number from 0 to 99, with an extension for larger numbers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Known numbering hammers either advance by a fragile pin or need many lever turns to reach a given number.

**Solution:** The single-digit disc slides axially and rotates; pushing and turning the handle locks a bolt into the tens disc, giving any number from 0 to 99, with an extension for larger numbers.
