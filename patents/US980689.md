## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of my invention is to overcome the difliculty pointed out above and to provide simple and efficient means for automatically releasing the paper feed mechanism from a locked off or inoperative position should the operator forget to restore the paper feeding mechanism to operative position.

**How it works (reading):** The paper feed mechanism is automatically released from its locked-off position so that it returns to operative feed if the operator forgets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An operator who locks the paper feed rollers off to adjust paper often forgets to restore them, so the paper is not fed and the sheet is spoiled.

**Solution:** The paper feed mechanism is automatically released from its locked-off position so that it returns to operative feed if the operator forgets.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
