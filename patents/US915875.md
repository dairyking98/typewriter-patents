## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper guiding devices of typewriting machines, and its object is to provide simple, effective and inexpensive means readily applicable to existing machines, for writing upon labels for medicine and other bottles.

**How it works (reading):** A yielding plate with a comb of flexible teeth is spring-pressed against the platen from the front around its lower side, guiding the label up to a releasable roll over the printing line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Small paper labels are hard to align and to write along the edges on a platen, and existing machines cannot do this well.

**Solution:** A yielding plate with a comb of flexible teeth is spring-pressed against the platen from the front around its lower side, guiding the label up to a releasable roll over the printing line.
