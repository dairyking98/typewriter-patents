## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to means for arresting the leading edge of the paper as it is introduced into the machine, so that the paper may always be started at the same point and properly squared and entered into the machine.

**How it works (reading):** Feed rolls are thrown out of operation to introduce the paper, then clamped on it; paper-feeding and arresting devices and a paper apron stop the leading edge and square the sheet.
