## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** A swinging pawl around the paper roller axis engages a fixed ratchet on the roller. A fixed stop limits forward deflection and an adjustable stop limits reverse, so an auxiliary switch advances the paper at once while the normal line feed still applies the line spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When writing on pre-perforated forms, the paper must advance a set distance from one form to the next, which the ordinary line switching cannot do in one step.

**Solution:** A swinging pawl around the paper roller axis engages a fixed ratchet on the roller. A fixed stop limits forward deflection and an adjustable stop limits reverse, so an auxiliary switch advances the paper at once while the normal line feed still applies the line spacing.

**How it works:** The pawl drives the ratchet forward with the paper, is free in reverse, and is limited by fixed and adjustable stops, giving a separate jump of the paper.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** paper roller; swinging pawl; ratchet wheel; fixed stop; adjustable stop
