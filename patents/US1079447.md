## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pneumatic type-wheel machines are quiet only with a short type stroke, which spoils visibility, and carbon copies need a hard platen and longer stroke.

**Solution:** The whole type wheel carriage is reciprocated by an eccentric driven by a pneumatic motor, with a pneumatically extensible connecting rod so the touch is a push rather than a blow.

**How it works:** A rigid type wheel with slidable types sits on a carriage in guide rails. A pneumatic motor turns a shaft, and an eccentric reciprocates the carriage toward and away from the platen. A flat spring fork stops the motor and is released by a diaphragm each time a type is in position. The connecting rod is a small pneumatic press, with a piston and valve, that lengthens at the top of the stroke to compensate for removing the soft platen covering when carbon copies are made.
