## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In the patent’s own words:** One purpose of the invention is to provide 1o an auxiliary or corrective spacing device for type-writing machines so constructedthat the direction of the carriage of the machine may bereversed at anytime to the extent of one or more points to enable an operator to correct a misspelled word or a letter or character erroneously struck or to make an insertion.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators cannot move the carriage back by single points to correct a misspelled letter or make an insertion.

**Solution:** A corrective auxiliary spacing device, attachable to a standard machine, that reverses the carriage a point or more via a push-rod and dog engaging a rack.

**How it works:** Brackets on the scale bar carry a rack attached to the carriage. A frame on a bed-plate holds a push-rod with a knob, a spring, and a pivoted action-plate carrying a dog. Pushing the rod swings the dog into the rack and moves the carriage backward one or more points. The spring returns the rod, holds parts together and presses the action-plate down. The device does not interfere with normal carriage motion when idle. The rest of the description is cut off.
