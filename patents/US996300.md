## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (justified-line typesetting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets

**In the patent’s own words:** My improvements relate to pattern-controlled machines for printing, type-casting, typesetting or analogous purposes, and par ticularly intended to produce justified lines of types or impressions from type.

**How it works (reading):** A machine for producing justified printed lines; the first 3000 characters give only the title and drawing list, so the mechanism is not shown.
