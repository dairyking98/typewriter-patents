## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks are altered because the amount can be raised or changed after writing.

**Solution:** A check punch in which key-levers, one key per character, drive perforating and printing punches that pierce the figure through the paper and ink the exposed fibres at the hole edges.

**Features:** [USE:check-writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
