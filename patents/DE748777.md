## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register, accounting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Point-of-sale printers with openable casings allowing access to the rolled-up continuous copy material; Receipt-giving machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Cash register or accounting machine, in which, in addition to the cheats, also not to be added Information (quantities, numbers, etc.

**How it works (reading):** Separate control devices, driven from a gate disk and coupled only for one-time total drawing, switch zero-print blocking of the print hammers for the type carriers, so zero printing follows the highest set value.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash register and accounting machines print non-added information (quantities, numbers) and the zero-setting means for it did not cooperate with the adding wheels' zero/empty positions.

**Solution:** Separate control devices, driven from a gate disk and coupled only for one-time total drawing, switch zero-print blocking of the print hammers for the type carriers, so zero printing follows the highest set value.
