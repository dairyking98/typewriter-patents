## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to the bearings for the type bars of typewriting machines, and has for itsmam ob ject to provide an improved construction of type barhanger and one which while occupying a small space laterally considered, yet, at the same time, furnishes a broad and stable bearing forthe type bar.

**How it works (reading):** A type-bar hanger and bearing plate construction giving a broad, stable bearing for the type bar.
