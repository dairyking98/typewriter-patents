## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for automatic Control of the carriage return depending on the carriage movement, power-driven business typing machines.

**How it works (reading):** The power drive is cut by stop members that engage at the carriage's position; a second stop engages for longer returns, so the carriage stops by its own momentum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Automatic carriage return overruns, and the shock is absorbed poorly without added buffers.

**Solution:** The power drive is cut by stop members that engage at the carriage's position; a second stop engages for longer returns, so the carriage stops by its own momentum.
