## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper fingers cannot be cleared from the platen for erasures on manifold sheets, so the sheets creep out of register when the platen is turned back.

**Solution:** The paper fingers are made to slide and move toward and away from the platen so that the sheets can be lifted for correction without feeding them.
