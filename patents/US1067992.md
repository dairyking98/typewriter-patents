## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets with marginal perforations cannot be positioned reliably against the printing line.

**Solution:** A positioning plate on the platen with movable studs that enter the sheet perforations; the studs can sink out of the holes and can be tied to the pressure-roll release.
