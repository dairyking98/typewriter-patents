## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ink ribbon conveying for punch card tabulators) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms driven by motors independently of the machine as a whole; Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for conveying the ink ribbon for printing devices or printing machines, in particular for punch card tabulating machines.

**How it works (reading):** Two electromagnetic clutches, one per take-up reel, drive the ribbon in either direction; the reel not driving brakes and holds tension, with a spring tensioner taking up slack at each strike.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink ribbon feed in tabulating or printing machines needed tension and had to avoid damage when the ribbon stopped under the type strike.

**Solution:** Two electromagnetic clutches, one per take-up reel, drive the ribbon in either direction; the reel not driving brakes and holds tension, with a spring tensioner taking up slack at each strike.
