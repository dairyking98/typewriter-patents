P. 175) ISSUED SEPTEMBER 1, 1952 C3023 IX to 43 α or printing machines The invention relates to a device for conveying the ink ribbon for printing devices or printing machines, in particular for punch card tabulating machines. The device according to the invention consists of two rollers for rolling up and unrolling the Ribbon with a device for reversing the direction of conveyance. The device for conveying the ribbon consists of an electromagnetic clutch for each ribbon take-up reel, these clutches working in such a way that when a clutch for the positive drive of one role, the coupling device of the other role is effective is also in action and is trying to pull the ribbon in the opposite direction promote, but with a weaker driving force, so that the latter has a braking effect, which causes appropriate tension on the ribbon. There is therefore a feature of the invention in using one of the electromagnetic clutches as the driving clutch while doing the others by a difference in the strength of the current in the two coupling devices for tensioning of the ribbon.
