## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shift mechanisms either lock the shift or release it, but a single key cannot do both conveniently.

**Solution:** Use two shift keys: a locking shift key and a temporary one that shifts the carriage and returns it on release.

**How it works:** The carriage hangs on swinging arms and a yoke, and a shift-crank rock-shaft with connecting rods moves it back when either key is depressed. A spring-held notched latch engages an arm on the shift crank to lock the shift for the locking key. A pin on the temporary key's connecting rod presses the latch down so it cannot engage, and the carriage falls back by gravity, or by a spring, when released.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)

**Parts:** carriage; yoke; shift crank; rock-shaft; connecting rod; shift key; locking latch; return spring; type basket; ink pad
