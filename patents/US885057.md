## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Circular-platen carriage machines need one key to feed the line and advance the carriage, and a single key to do both.

**Solution:** A key or bar operates the platen feed lever and the carriage reciprocation through an adjustable incline and a wheel, with one key for both.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
