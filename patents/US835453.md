## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with means for effecting line or character spacing in either direction

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of our invention is to produce a type-writing machine which can be used for rinting from either left to right, as in Engish, for example, or from right to left, as in Hebrew, for example.
