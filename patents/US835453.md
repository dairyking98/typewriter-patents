## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with means for effecting line or character spacing in either direction

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of our invention is to produce a type-writing machine which can be used for rinting from either left to right, as in Engish, for example, or from right to left, as in Hebrew, for example.

**How it works (reading):** A double rack on the paper carriage, shifted by the operator to bring one rack bar or the other into mesh with the spring-driven actuator pinion, so the carriage steps in either direction.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters write only left to right, so Hebrew and similar scripts cannot be written right to left.

**Solution:** A double rack on the paper carriage, shifted by the operator to bring one rack bar or the other into mesh with the spring-driven actuator pinion, so the carriage steps in either direction.

**How it works:** The carriage is stepped by a spring-driven actuator on an upright post: a dog wheel with a pawl and a ratchet on the spring casing, and a pinion on the casing. A tight dog and a loose dog on a rocker shaft step the dog wheel at each stroke. The carriage frame holds two parallel rack bars facing each other with the pinion between; a lengthwise shaft with end arms moves the double rack towards or away from the operator to engage one bar or the other, and clamp-stops hold either position. Engaging one bar moves the carriage left, the other right.
