## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Exponents and superscript characters need a fractional line movement that the normal line spacing does not give.

**Solution:** An auxiliary fractional spacer that turns the platen by a fraction of a line for printing characters above the normal line, operated by the type bar.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
