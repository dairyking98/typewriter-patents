## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriters wherein the successive depression and release of a series of key-levers operates to vibrate and throw a series of type-characters upward and against the surface of the paper placed upon a suitable platen mounted upon a horizontally and laterally moving carriage;

**How it works (reading):** A rack-bar carriage drive that steps either forward or backward, and ribbon spools that reverse themselves automatically when the ribbon runs out.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On typebar machines the carriage can only step one way, and the ribbon has to be reversed by hand.

**Solution:** A rack-bar carriage drive that steps either forward or backward, and ribbon spools that reverse themselves automatically when the ribbon runs out.

**How it works:** The paper carriage rides a guide rail and is moved step by step by a rack-bar worked from the key levers through a pallet wheel on a carriage-actuating wheel; a device sets the carriage backward. A bar operates the ribbon spools so the ribbon moves alternately from side to side below the platen. Self-reversing spool devices shift the drive when the ribbon is fully wound off one spool.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** carriage; guide rail; rack-bar; carriage-actuating wheel; pallet wheel; ribbon spool; spool-operating bar; hanger; key lever
