## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** A link hinged to the key lever with an elongated hole around the intermediate member lets the key lever and the intermediate member stay engaged, with the type lever return spring independent of the key spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A key held down after striking makes the type lever strike repeatedly, and disconnecting the drive on each stroke wears the parts.

**Solution:** A link hinged to the key lever with an elongated hole around the intermediate member lets the key lever and the intermediate member stay engaged, with the type lever return spring independent of the key spring.
