## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for controlling character selection

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention are to make a typewriting machine in which the operator can depress a plurality of keys in succession to make successive letters without having to release the key first depressed befored'epressing the next key.
