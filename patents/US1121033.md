## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A foldable machine needs a letter-spacing escapement whose parts separate when the carriage folds.

**Solution:** Split feed mechanism with a spring-drum cord and releasable escapement parts that let the carriage run free or return.

**How it works:** Key levers with springs operate typebars and the escapement. A propelling drum with a spring pulls the carriage by a flexible cord. Escapement parts can be released from each other so the carriage moves freely in either direction and the carriage and platen shift to a compact position. The carriage runs on balls held spaced by a wire.

**Features:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** escapement; propelling drum; cord; carriage; ball race; key lever; type bar; gib

**Improves on:**

- Corona folding machine (earlier Petermann): separable feed mechanism for folding
