## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage needs to be released from its regular feed and stopped at set positions for paragraphs and columns.

**Solution:** An auxiliary stop bar with interchangeable stop strips arrests a released carriage at desired positions.

**How it works:** Only the opening is available. On a carriage mounted on a pivoted vertical shift-frame, a release lifts the feed dogs from the escapement wheel and rack so the spring-driven carriage runs. An auxiliary stop-bar with interchangeable stop strips (different stop projections) stops it at chosen positions.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** shift-frame; carriage; escapement wheel; auxiliary stop-bar; stop strip; feed dog
