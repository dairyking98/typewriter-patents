## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to auxiliary carriage releasing and stop or feed mechanisms for type-writing machines of that kind in which the latch-carriage is moved horizontally by a living-spring orpower device and is controlled by a regular feed mechanism which operates upon the actuation of the type-bar keys and space-key to effect an intermittent or step-by-step movement of the carriage to produce the letter-spacing.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary stop bar with interchangeable stop strips arrests a released carriage at desired positions.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage needs to be released from its regular feed and stopped at set positions for paragraphs and columns.

**Solution:** An auxiliary stop bar with interchangeable stop strips arrests a released carriage at desired positions.

**How it works:** Only the opening is available. On a carriage mounted on a pivoted vertical shift-frame, a release lifts the feed dogs from the escapement wheel and rack so the spring-driven carriage runs. An auxiliary stop-bar with interchangeable stop strips (different stop projections) stops it at chosen positions.
