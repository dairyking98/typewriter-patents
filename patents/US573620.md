## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Gearing

**In the patent’s own words:** The main objects of my present invention is to provide an improved coupling mechanism by means of which a rotative driven gear may be carried into and out of engagement with a drivinggear, the orbit of which corresponds to the orbit of movement of the driven gear, the coupling and uncoupling of the driven gear being effected at predetermined points in its orbital movement.
