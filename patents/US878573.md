## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism for typewriting machines and generally stated has for its object to provide means for changing or shifting automatically at a predetermined point in a-line of writing from one field to another of a ribbon having 'a plurality of fields of different characteristics.

**How it works (reading):** Two relatively adjustable members, one on the carriage and one fixed, alter the throw of the vibratory ribbon carrier when they meet.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon colour or field cannot change automatically at set points in the line.

**Solution:** Two relatively adjustable members, one on the carriage and one fixed, alter the throw of the vibratory ribbon carrier when they meet.

**How it works:** A shifting member on the carriage and one on a fixed part co-act at adjustable points of carriage travel, changing the ribbon carrier throw so another field of a multi-field ribbon strikes. Separate from tabulating devices. Shown on a Monarch.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; vibratory ribbon carrier; carriage-mounted shifting member; fixed shifting member
