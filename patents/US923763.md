## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** On a front-strike typebar machine whose platen shifts for case, the frame must allow the platen carrier to travel past the machine sides and the type faces must still match the platen in both shift positions.

**Solution:** A dropped recess in the top plate gives the platen frame room to pivot on forward arms, and bevelled two-type blocks keep the type faces matching the platen cylinder in both cases.

**How it works:** Two sets of typebars with two-type blocks (lower and upper), bevelled outward and downward, strike up and back against the front of a cylindrical platen. The platen frame has forward arms pivoted on the carriage, so the platen can move up and down around those pivots. An angled roller on the platen frame bears on a shift rail carried on arms of a rock shaft, which is turned by a link, bell crank and shift lever to raise the frame for upper case. The recessed top plate leaves an unobstructed space under the platen carrier as it travels beyond the machine sides.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
