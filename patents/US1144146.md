**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Handling copy materials differing in width

**How it works (reading):** A stationary paper support is separated from the moving carriage so the paper feed does not load it, and a manual release lets the carriage return independently.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A manifolding ribbon support on the moving paper carriage causes paper rolls to rebound and drag the carriage.

**Solution:** A stationary paper support is separated from the moving carriage so the paper feed does not load it, and a manual release lets the carriage return independently.

**Features:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
