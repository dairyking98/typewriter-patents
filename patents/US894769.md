## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need uniform key tension and a cheaper, lighter-touch return mechanism.

**Solution:** All type-bearing bars are returned by a single tension spring acting through a common segmental bar, with racks moving pinions on the bars.

**How it works:** Each key lever is connected by a rod to a rack lever whose segmental rack meshes with a pinion on its type-bearing bar. Pressing the key drives the bar to the platen through most of its stroke without loading the spring. A segmental bar under the rack lever arms, also pushed by the space bar, is held by one coil spring with a turnbuckle for tension. A V centring guide aligns the type at the platen.
