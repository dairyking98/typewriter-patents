## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Kana typewriters built on Western machines wind the paper sideways and print each character rotated 90 degrees, which is hard to read and error-prone for telegrams.

**Solution:** Add to a Western typebar typewriter an automatic vertical line feed, driven by the printing stroke, so Japanese kana can be typed in upright vertical columns.

**How it works:** Pressing a key swings a typebar to the platen and also pushes back a moving frame behind the typebars. That frame's presser screw moves a swinging lever and link, which through arms, a bent arm and a forked arm swing a cross bar and a rocking shaft in the cylinder frame. An arm on the rocking shaft swings a two-part pawl arm (upper and lower pawl joined flexibly by a spring) so the lower pawl leaves the ratchet on the cylinder shaft and the upper pawl enters a tooth. On key release the upper pawl withdraws, a spring turns the cylinder by one tooth, and the lower pawl catches the next tooth, so the paper advances one step per character.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a typewriter in which, in a Western-type typewriter, a swinging lever is set behind the moving frame; normally held in a set position by a link and a spring, it is moved by the printing action and so swings a forked arm forward and back. A cross bar, carried by a pair of arms fixed on a rocking shaft mounted in the cylinder frame, is coupled to the forked arm. At one end of an arm fixed on the rocking shaft a pawl arm is pivoted, to engage alternately with the teeth of a ratchet wheel fixed on the cylinder shaft; the pawl arm is made of an upper pawl and a lower pawl joined flexibly on the same axis; and the swing of the forked arm feeds the cylinder lengthwise (vertical feed). Its object is to improve the Western typewriter so that it can write Japanese characters vertically, giving a typewriter convenient for printing telegrams and the like.
