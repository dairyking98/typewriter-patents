## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Details of, or accessories for, typewriters or selective printing mechanisms not otherwise provided for

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on typewriters for writing the Date by means of an interchangeable printing type for day, month and year Type lever.

**How it works (reading):** A separate date type lever sits in a bearing on the outside of the type basket, is driven by a power drive under key control, and carries interchangeable day, month and year types.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A date type cannot be placed anywhere on the print line, because a date lever at the far right can't reach it and the date types can't share a stop point.

**Solution:** A separate date type lever sits in a bearing on the outside of the type basket, is driven by a power drive under key control, and carries interchangeable day, month and year types.
