## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guides in the printing zone, e.g. guides for preventing contact of conveyed sheets with printhead; Separating articles from piles using pneumatic force

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates particularly to automatically operated typewriters, but is not restricted to such use as it may be used in any connection for which it is applicable.

**How it works (reading):** A pneumatic sensor on the sheet table of a feed mechanism carried on the carriage stops the drive when the last sheet is removed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An automatic typewriter driving sheet feed must stop when the paper supply runs out.

**Solution:** A pneumatic sensor on the sheet table of a feed mechanism carried on the carriage stops the drive when the last sheet is removed.
