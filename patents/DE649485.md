## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon ends fixed to spools by mandrels, hooks or loops crumple or shift on reversal, waste ribbon length and are hard to attach.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal clamp riveted across the ribbon end forms a sleeve that a pin through the spool cover discs passes through.

**How it works:** The ribbon end is clamped between a sheet-metal strip across its full width, forming a sleeve. The clamp is slid into the spool through radial grooves in the cover discs with tweezers until the sleeve lines up with holes near the core. A pin is inserted through the holes and sleeve, fixing the ribbon flexibly and securely.
