## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Dust and dirt clog the exposed pivot-points of Remington type-bars.

**Solution:** A detachable flanged cover ring over the type-bar pivots.

**How it works:** A flanged ring of metal or papier-mache fits over the connecting bars. Spring-catches with ears hold it. Hooks on it guide the ribbon bands.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** cover ring; flange; spring catch; hook; pivot block

**Improves on:**

- Remington: covers exposed pivots
