## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding paper to the feed roller needs continual attention from the operator while keying.

**Solution:** A thin flat plate attached to the paper table with adjustable lateral angle-flange guides steers the paper into the feed roller, detachable from any machine.
