## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A one-handed typewriter operated with a holder or stylus.

**Solution:** An elastic disc cast with raised letters on its underside is turned by a stylus laid on a circular chart with radiating bars; pressing prints the aligned letter through an aperture in an ink pad, and the same arm feeds the paper.

**How it works:** The letters, figures and points are cast in relief on the lower face of a single elastic disc on a vertical spindle journaled in a vibrating arm. The top of the spindle has a hinged arm with a V-forked point attached to the stylus. A fixed plate above carries the same characters in a circle with a radiating bar for each, and the operator rests the forked point on a bar to turn the disc to that letter. A table beneath has an ink pad with an aperture slightly larger than one letter, so pressing prints the letter at the aperture. The rear end of the vibrating arm has a retractile spring and an adjustable latch that engages a rack bar to feed the paper. A weight and cord return the rack bar. A notched transverse bar sets the line distance and a sliding clasp holds the paper.
