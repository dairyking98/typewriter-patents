## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriting machines which employ keyboards, type-bars connected to the key-levers,and ribbons for the type-bars to strike against, the platen in my device being arranged vertically, as in the machine covered by Letters Patent No.
