## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electrical type-wheel printers need a delay so the hammer releases before the wheel moves to the next type.

**Solution:** A contact in the pressure circuit, opened mechanically by the wheel advance and lever, interrupts the hammer magnet at the right moment.

**Features:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
