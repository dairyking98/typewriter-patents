## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Two separate pull rods attached at different distances from the intermediate lever pivot act in succession: first the long arm for a quick start, then the short arm for speed.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A one-piece link between key lever and intermediate lever gives a torque, friction and wear and is hard to adjust.

**Solution:** Two separate pull rods attached at different distances from the intermediate lever pivot act in succession: first the long arm for a quick start, then the short arm for speed.

**How it works:** The key lever has two slots, each holding a hooked wire rod. The rods attach to the same arm of a two-armed intermediate lever at different distances from the pivot. When the key is pressed the far rod acts first on the long arm; after lost motion the near rod takes up and drives the short arm so the typebar speeds up toward the platen. The wires are corrugated so they can be adjusted by pliers.
