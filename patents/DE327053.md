## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that @ on the typewriter a movable, rod under spring action.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded rod pushes the rear of the type carrier so the dash or period stays pressed on the roller, and the carriage moves to draw the line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Long horizontal or vertical rules needed the dash or period key struck many times.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded rod pushes the rear of the type carrier so the dash or period stays pressed on the roller, and the carriage moves to draw the line.
