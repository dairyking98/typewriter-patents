## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, and more particularly to ribbon mechanism adapted for use in connection with a work element in the form of a manifold strip or web.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon spools are located between the platen spindle bearings close to the platen ends, and some ribbon guides can be removed, so the ribbons sit in the folds of a manifold strip without blocking its feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mounting is not compact enough for manifold strips, and the ribbon guides are hard to reach and to remove.

**Solution:** The ribbon spools are located between the platen spindle bearings close to the platen ends, and some ribbon guides can be removed, so the ribbons sit in the folds of a manifold strip without blocking its feed.
