## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Label printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper feeding and controlling devices of typewriting machines, and particularly to tally strips and means for writing and feeding the same in conjunction with bills or main sheets.

**How it works (reading):** A tally strip with carbon on one side is wound on spools and advanced by an extra pawl on the line-spacing slide, so one line-space moves both platen and strip.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A bill and tally strip must both show the amount, and writing each amount twice wastes time and invites errors.

**Solution:** A tally strip with carbon on one side is wound on spools and advanced by an extra pawl on the line-spacing slide, so one line-space moves both platen and strip.

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)

- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** tally strip; spool; pawl; ratchet wheel; carbon
