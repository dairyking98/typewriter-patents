## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper carriage switching device. The invention relates to a switching device for the paper trolley of typewriters, in which the circuit by means of a double lever hit by the type levers near the pressure point is effected. In the known devices of this type, the circuit is thereby brought about that the double lever influenced by the type lever by means of two Latches engages in a resilient member seated on a shaft and this with the shaft starts rotating, causing the at the lower end of the shaft attached worm (read worm wheel meshing with this and the ratchet wheel sets in rotating motion. Opposite these known devices takes place according to the invention the release of the ratchet wheel instead of the one struck by the type lever double-armed lever acts on a further double-armed lever and this in such a way brings to swing that the pawl carrier moves backwards and the ratchet wheel is triggered. This has the advantage that the transmission path up to The release of the escapement wheel is significantly shortened, making it quick and safe Circuit is achieved.
