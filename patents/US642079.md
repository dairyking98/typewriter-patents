## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier automatic ribbon reversers relied on the ribbon itself to throw the reversing mechanism, which was slow, left a dead period where the ribbon did not move, and made several characters strike one spot or fail to print.

**Solution:** The ribbon only sets a vibrating arm as it nears its end; the actual reversal is then made positively by a part driven from the spacing mechanism on the next keystroke.

**How it works:** A rock-shaft driven by the spacing toe vibrates a feed pawl at each end of the machine, each working a ratchet that winds a ribbon spool through bevel gears. A centrally pivoted bar carries a pawl set at each end so that shifting it engages one end's pawl while releasing the other's, reversing feed; a hand knob with a detent also works it. The free end of the ribbon is attached to a vibrating arm on the spool spindle; as the ribbon unwinds to its end the arm is pulled outward and its yoke pushes a cam, which moves a shifting abutment into the path of a shifting arm moved by the spacing mechanism. The next keystroke or space then lifts the abutment and bar, swapping the active pawls. Because the ribbon only sets the parts before it is exhausted, there is no dead point.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; ratchet wheel; feed pawl; stationary pawl; shifter bar; vibrating arm; yoke; cam; shifting abutment; shifting arm; rock-shaft; hand knob; bevel gear

**Improves on:**

- ribbon-tension-actuated automatic reversers: ribbon only primes the reverser; a stronger part driven by the spacing mechanism performs the reversal, eliminating the dead point
