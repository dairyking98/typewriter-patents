## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar actuation needs a light touch on the finger-piece and should be simple and economical to build.

**Solution:** Two links of unequal length, flexibly joined at the front, drive the typebar with a pull to the printing point, and one link is a spring that returns the typebar.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Key touch and even impression](../features/bar-touch.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** finger-piece; longer link; shorter link; flexible joint; type-bar; spring link
