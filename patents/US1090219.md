## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage jump on tabulation causes shock; a governor slows the carriage uniformly.

**Solution:** A pneumatic cushion or buffer lets the carriage start quickly and gradually retards it at the end of its travel.
