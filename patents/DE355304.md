## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Preparing or treating stereotype matrices

**How it works (reading):** A half-step escapement with two pawl/ratchet pairs, one per direction, advances the carriage half a character width before the impression and half after, in either travel direction.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage advance is equal for every character, so narrow and wide letters get uneven spacing, and the carriage cannot easily be switched in the reverse direction.

**Solution:** A half-step escapement with two pawl/ratchet pairs, one per direction, advances the carriage half a character width before the impression and half after, in either travel direction.

**How it works:** Pressing a key lowers a bracket and lever so that the first pawl (H1 or e1) engages its ratchet and moves the carriage half a character width left or right, then the type prints. On release, the second pawl (H2 or e2) engages and advances the second half. Only one pawl pair is active at a time, selected by a slide that spreads the other pair's pawls or by shifting the ratchet shaft. Different character widths are obtained by changing the stroke of the ratchet carrier.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** pawl; ratchet wheel; bracket; key lever; slide; ratchet carrier

**Improves on:**

- known half-step escapements: they switch in one direction only; this works both directions and allows quick reversal for corrections and oriental scripts
