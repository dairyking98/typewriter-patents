## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper guiding device for typewriters. Subject of the invention is a paper guide device for typewriters with one on the end plates of the paper roller frame hinged guide plate, with guide rollers for pressing of the paper against the paper roller through the slots in the guide plate and the guide rollers about an axis parallel to the paper roller and one perpendicular thereto Axis are rotatably arranged. The novelty of the invention is that the framework in which the guide rollers are mounted on a perpendicular under the roller shaft downwards bent tongue of the 'guide plate rotatably attached', the rollers reach through the slit created by the formation of the tongue. With this arrangement of the rollers, an extremely simple and inexpensive construction is obtained. Furthermore, by hanging the guide plate at a point above the With the paper roller, the guide rollers are pressed diagonally forwards and upwards against the paper roller and not perpendicular to the same as in the known arrangements, whereby the Vibration at the type stop can be reduced.
