## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier on the paper apron concentric with the roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Subject of the invention is a paper guide device for typewriters with one on the end plates of the paper roller frame hinged guide plate, with guide rollers for pressing of the paper against the paper roller through the slots in the guide plate and the guide rollers about an axis parallel to the paper roller and one perpendicular thereto Axis are rotatably arranged.

**How it works (reading):** The guide plate is hung above the paper roller so its guide rollers press forward and upward, on a frame carried by a tongue cut from the guide plate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Guide rollers pressing the paper straight into the platen cause vibration at the type stop.

**Solution:** The guide plate is hung above the paper roller so its guide rollers press forward and upward, on a frame carried by a tongue cut from the guide plate.
