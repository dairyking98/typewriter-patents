## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says the carriage-return drive needed a simpler and cheaper structure, and that a motor for the return spring lacked an automatic starter and adjustable torque.

**Solution:** An electric motor drives the return-spring and carriage-return mechanism, with an automatic electric switch that starts the motor and a torque setting that follows the return-spring tension.
