## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars fall out when the pivot rod is withdrawn, and grit gets into the slots.

**Solution:** A two-section pivot rod is removable; an auxiliary wire holds the bars in place, and the slots wipe grit from the bars.
