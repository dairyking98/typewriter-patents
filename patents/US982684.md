## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing a character just above or below the main line needs the platen to hold a half-space position, which the single notch check does not give.

**Solution:** A double check with two anti-friction rolls at an interval not matching the line-space notches lets the platen be turned about half a space and held there by the idle roll.
