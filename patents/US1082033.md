## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**How it works (reading):** A row of tappet stops spaced one letter apart is mounted on the carriage, with one or more turned or moved into operating position by a single setting lever on a fixed part of the machine, and reset to inoperative by one lever movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops on the carriage needed many separate movements to set and reset, and resetting took manipulation of the carriage.

**Solution:** A row of tappet stops spaced one letter apart is mounted on the carriage, with one or more turned or moved into operating position by a single setting lever on a fixed part of the machine, and reset to inoperative by one lever movement.
