## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (multi-color printing for punch card machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** Electromagnets switch the ribbon into different positions for a second colour, with a spring returning it when the circuit opens, under control of the card perforations.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator-controlled punch card machines could not print items and totals in different colours.

**Solution:** Electromagnets switch the ribbon into different positions for a second colour, with a spring returning it when the circuit opens, under control of the card perforations.
