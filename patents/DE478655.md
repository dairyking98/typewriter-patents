## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** The round carriage guide bar is carried by two or more adjustable support bolts fixed to the machine frame, adjustable horizontally and vertically, so the bar is held straight.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A long round carriage guide bar sags in the middle or bows when supported by many fixed supports, so the carriage runs lower near the ends and the baseline becomes curved.

**Solution:** The round carriage guide bar is carried by two or more adjustable support bolts fixed to the machine frame, adjustable horizontally and vertically, so the bar is held straight.
