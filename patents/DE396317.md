## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control strip switching cash register)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement in the switching device for the control strip on cash registers according to patent 393847 through which the between the supply and take-up reel located part of the control strip can be brought into different positions, without rotating the supply and take-up spools.

**How it works (reading):** The strip between the reels is guided over two opposite-swinging levers, so the part between the reels moves independently of the spools, and the imprint is shifted by cam-driven levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The control strip cannot be moved back and forth without rotating the supply and take-up spools, which causes the wrong part to show behind the window.

**Solution:** The strip between the reels is guided over two opposite-swinging levers, so the part between the reels moves independently of the spools, and the imprint is shifted by cam-driven levers.
