## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The letter and word spacing needs a simple escapement allowing a double space with the space key.

**Solution:** A spring-drum carriage has a feeding dog and rigid dog on a rock-shaft; a movable stop limits the feeding dog to one notch unless letter and space keys pressed together.

**How it works:** The carriage is pulled by a strap from a spring drum and has a straight feed-rack. A pivoted feeding dog and a rigid detaining dog on a rock-shaft are moved by a universal bail resting on the key levers. A spring tilts the feeding dog so its lug hits a movable pin, limiting travel to one tooth. When a letter key and the space key are pressed together the pin is pushed aside and the dog moves two teeth.

**Features:** [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** carriage; guide rail; strap; spring drum; feed-rack; feeding dog; rigid dog; rock-shaft; universal bail; movable stop pin; spring

**Numbers:** 1 notch or 2 notches
