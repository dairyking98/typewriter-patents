## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Scripts with characters of unequal width need the carriage to advance by double steps, and the known double-step linkage is complicated.

**Solution:** The double-step switching bridge is placed under the key levers so the key levers move it directly, which removes the need for a special linkage to transmit its movement.
