## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reverse in Wales-type calculating machines is complex and needs adjustment on assembly.

**Solution:** Simpler reversing levers that positively lift the working pawl off its ratchet and hold it idle.

**How it works:** An oscillating bar with a pawl at each end drives ratchets on the two spool shafts. Reversing levers, tripped by arms riding the ribbon when a spool empties, positively disengage one pawl and let the other drive the opposite spool.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; ratchet; pawl; oscillating bar; reversing lever; trip arm

**Improves on:**

- US796024 (Wales): replaces grasshopper springs with positive pawl disengagement
