## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for reversing the longitudinal direction of movement of the ink ribbon for typewriters, the two spool shafts of which are driven by a common shaft.

**How it works (reading):** A shaft driven from the carriage spring drum turns two vertical spool shafts in opposite directions through bevel gears; each spool has a fixed gear engaged alternately by a coupling, and both can be disengaged for hand winding.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must be reversed in its travel direction, and the existing drive arrangement is awkward.

**Solution:** A shaft driven from the carriage spring drum turns two vertical spool shafts in opposite directions through bevel gears; each spool has a fixed gear engaged alternately by a coupling, and both can be disengaged for hand winding.

**Features:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
