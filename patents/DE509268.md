## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever drives for an inclined segment need wire links between angle levers and type levers.

**Solution:** A single horizontal double-armed intermediate lever with its pivot in the segment extension replaces the angle lever and wire links.
