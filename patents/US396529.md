## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Clerks need to turn plain text into cipher on an ordinary typewriter.

**Solution:** A removable key frame whose auxiliary keys are lettered abnormally, so typing the plain text prints the cipher.

**How it works:** A sheet-metal casing fits over the keyboard of a Remington or Hammond. Auxiliary vertical keys with anti-friction rollers rest on the normal keys and carry different letters on top. The cipher board is arranged by agreement; a decipher board arranged inversely retranslates the cipher. The same frame may serve for both by rearranging its keys. Only the opening is available.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SEL:chart-interchangeable](../features/sel-chart-interchangeable.md)

**Parts:** casing; auxiliary key; anti-friction roller; normal key
