## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Moving the typewriter carriage and platen by hand is laborious.

**Solution:** Solenoid-driven attachment that steps the carriage by pulling a toothed bar and also turns the platen, fitting any sliding-carriage typewriter.

**How it works:** A notched bar under the carriage is engaged by an inclined spring-pressed tooth on a vertical arm. Plunger armatures in solenoids move the arm horizontally, feeding the carriage one tooth pitch per stroke, with return springs and adjustable collars limiting stroke. A lever and link lift the tooth for manual operation. An insulated end tooth breaks the circuit at the carriage limit. A similar electrical device turns the platen.
