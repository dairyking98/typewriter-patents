## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Flat-platen typewriters let carbon sheets and the work slide out of place, so the operator must handle the carbon to change the paper.

**Solution:** A foot treadle, connecting rod and crank levers raise carbon-sheet holders and clamps together, so the work can be swapped without touching the carbon, then lowered to hold the paper on the platen.
