## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a collapsible manuscript holder for typewriters, which is used in the non-use position in the free space between the typewriter and its Lid is housed.

**How it works (reading):** The desk top hangs on pins at the upper edge of the cover, and a pair of scissors links and braces it at an inclined angle, so it stays with the cover when the typewriter is covered and folds flat inside the lid; a lamp can be fixed to the scissors.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manuscript holders fixed to the typewriter are not adjustable, and holders built into the cover weaken the housing and make portable machines heavier to carry.

**Solution:** The desk top hangs on pins at the upper edge of the cover, and a pair of scissors links and braces it at an inclined angle, so it stays with the cover when the typewriter is covered and folds flat inside the lid; a lamp can be fixed to the scissors.
