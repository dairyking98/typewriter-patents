> Description Translated from German Collapsible manuscript holder for typewriters The invention relates to a collapsible manuscript holder for typewriters, which is used in the non-use position in the free space between the typewriter and its Lid is housed. The well-known manuscript holders of this type are directly at the Typewriter attached, rotatably attached to its base plate. It is also known to form the protective housing of the machine as a holder by the top wall consisting of individual parts connected to one another by hinges can be opened as a desk. The former have the disadvantage that in use they have a unchangeable position to the scribe, what freedom of use such holder impaired, while with the latter, the housing is insufficient solid and tight and therefore not suitable for portable typewriters. Both still have the disadvantage that they form part of the typewriter, so that they always have to be taken. These disadvantages are avoided according to the invention in that the desk top of the holder in the position of use is reversed to that in the removed position Layer serving as a base plate by means of intermediate links attached to it supports.
