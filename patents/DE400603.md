A cover cap is placed over the entire device and folded over the sheet metal tabs on it, thereby holding all parts together will. It now often happens that during assembly and later use typewriter oil, kerosene, etc. drips onto the keys. The liquid can between the edge of the glass pane and the cover cap into the interior of the key capsule penetrate and sucked up by the cardboard plate provided with the characters will.
