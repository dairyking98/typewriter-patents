## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Dust and oil seep into key caps, staining the character plate, and the glass pane cracks when the cap is crimped.

**Solution:** A Klingerit sealing ring is placed between the glass pane and the cover cap to keep liquids and dust out and cushion the crimp force.
