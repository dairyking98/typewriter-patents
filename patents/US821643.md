## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike machines need the ribbon moved out of the way so writing can be seen, and ribbon wears along one edge.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools on horizontal axes feed the ribbon flat, and a guide turns it to vertical in front of the type at each stroke, shiftable to use either edge.

**How it works:** The ribbon runs between horizontal-axis spools, lying flat. A ribbon guide on a lever from the shift frame is swung by a link driven from the key bar. The guide rotates the ribbon from horizontal to vertical in front of the type. A manual setting selects which direction it turns so either edge, or colour band, is presented, and this follows the ribbon-feed shift.
