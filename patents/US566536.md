## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Remington-type platen shift only gives two positions; the operator wants a slightly raised or lowered line.

**Solution:** Add a button and spring strip over the shift key that limits its travel, giving three platen positions for superscript and subscript.

**How it works:** A spring-metal strip is clamped above the shift key with a socket and threaded stem carrying a button. Depressing the button swings an arm on the strip's lower end over the shift key and the stem's length sets how far the key can move. The platen then stops slightly above or below the normal line. A leaf spring returns the parts.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** shift key; spring strip; socket; threaded stem; button; leaf spring; platen

**Numbers:** 3 platen positions

**Improves on:**

- Remington platen shift: adds a limited shift stop for half-line printing
