## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An auxiliary carriage stop should be easy to remove and put back, yet stay securely in place on the carriage rear bar, and be compact.

**Solution:** An adjustable stop is held on the rear bar of the carriage by an improved spring means, and can be removed and put in place without tools.
