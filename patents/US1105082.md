## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stenographic paper strips needed reeling after the writing point, with reverse rotation for inspection.

**Solution:** A rewinding reel is driven from the paper feed mechanism and can be turned backward by hand to inspect the writing.

**How it works:** The paper feed drives a reel by ratchet devices, which allow the reel to be turned backward.
