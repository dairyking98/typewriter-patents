## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (multi-type-carrier card printing device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Letterpress printing apparatus specially adapted for office or other commercial purposes with flat printing surfaces, e.g. with flat type-beds, surfaces made of thin sheet material or moulded from plastics or rubber

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to one Printing device with several adjustable type carriers arranged next to one another, which are preferably used for printing cards with several identical imprints in one or several lines in one operation.

**How it works (reading):** Gaps between the adjacent type carriers form a continuous transverse channel when set, so an auxiliary type bar can print a parallel line in the same operation, and the channel also evens out setting errors.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card printing machines can only print one line at a time, so four lines with two alternating entries need repeated setting of each type carrier.

**Solution:** Gaps between the adjacent type carriers form a continuous transverse channel when set, so an auxiliary type bar can print a parallel line in the same operation, and the channel also evens out setting errors.
