**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guards, shields or dust excluders

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dust sheds/guards around the type bars and ribbon path need a better shape and ribbon clearance.

**Solution:** Dust guard sections with an upright flange along the rear edge and a recess that accommodates the ribbon passing to the vibrator.

**Features:** [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
