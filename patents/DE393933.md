## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The coupling of the platen to the line index wheel in patent 387763 relied on compression springs for pressing the coupling members.

**Solution:** Press the toothed tendons outward with a steel ball driven by an adjusting knob screw, against a ring spring, to engage the line index ring gear.

**How it works:** A hub is pinned on the platen axis, with a ring nut and a loose ring gear (line index wheel) around it. Two tendons in a cross groove carry small teeth matching the ring gear bore and are held inward by a curved tension spring. Screwing the adjusting knob presses a steel ball down the bevels of the tendons so they spread out into the ring gear teeth and couple it to the platen. Unscrewing releases them, so the platen can turn freely while the roller locking bracket holds the ring gear.
