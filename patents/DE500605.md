## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter carbon form printing process) · **Subject (patent classes):** Accessories for manifolding purposes; Sheet materials for use therein by using carbon paper or the like

**How it works (reading):** Carbon or colour paper is placed between the paper layers before printing so each layer gets its impression from the colour sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Individually printed carbon form sets need manual interleaving of carbon or colour paper.

**Solution:** Carbon or colour paper is placed between the paper layers before printing so each layer gets its impression from the colour sheet.

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
