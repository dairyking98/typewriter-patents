## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A book or large sheets cannot lie flat on a typewriter carriage, so the downward-striking type cannot print cleanly.

**Solution:** A lower carriage on tracks carries the book on a flat support, moved by a rack and gear with ratchet line spacing; the type strike downward.

**How it works:** Book carriage on tracks, rack and ratchet line feed, flat support for downward striking type.
