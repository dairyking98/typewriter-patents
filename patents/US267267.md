## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (car coupler) · **Subject (patent classes):** Couplings comprising interengaging parts of different shape or form and having links, bars, pins, shackles, or hooks as coupling means with shackles and hooks, e.g. specially adapted for mine cars

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of couplers which are adapted to automatically connect themselves to or with the ordinary link when the cars to which they are attached come in contact with each other;

**How it works (reading):** A draw-bar with a sliding wedge and spring lets a hooked link fall into place when the approaching car's bar pushes the wedge in and releases the link.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Railway car couplers did not connect automatically when cars came together.

**Solution:** A draw-bar with a sliding wedge and spring lets a hooked link fall into place when the approaching car's bar pushes the wedge in and releases the link.
