## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Individual typebars in a circular series are hard to adjust without disturbing neighbours.

**Solution:** Typebar brackets seated between a rigid annulus and a flexible annular plate, held by screws between brackets so one bracket can be adjusted alone; plus a stop projection on the bracket.

**How it works:** Each typebar pivots on a side of a radial bracket. Brackets stand edgewise on a rigid annulus; a thin flexible annular plate lies on their top edges. Screws pass through the plate between adjacent brackets into the annulus, touching the bracket faces. Loosening the two screws beside one bracket (and tightening those beyond) frees it for radial or lateral adjustment. A projection on the bracket limits the bar's return swing.
