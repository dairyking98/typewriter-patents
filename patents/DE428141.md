## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The up-and-down type segment can jam and needs high lifting force, and it must be cheap and compact.

**Solution:** The segment carrier is held by ball bearings and lifted by levers that engage it in the middle, with a common spring holding it up, which avoids jamming and cuts friction.
