## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Leader lines of stars, dots or underscoring are produced by repeatedly striking keys, which costs time and labour in bills, statements and indexes.

**Solution:** An attachment on the machine has an actuating rack bar, with an adjustable release device and a releasing wedge block, so that the printing instrument for the liner character is operated by the carriage travel and the line prints concurrently with carriage movement.
