## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need a simple attachable device that inks the type directly and keeps ink off everything else.

**Solution:** A wick-fed ink pad and a flexible rubber inking cup stand at the centre of the type basket, and each rising typebar tips the cup against the pad to re-ink and then takes ink from the cup.

**How it works:** A cross-bar clipped to the frame carries a tube that dips into an ink bottle; a wick runs up the tube to an ink pad. A flexible rubber inverted-cone cup on a rubber strip sits above the pad in the path of the type. When a typebar strikes the cup it tips over, the type takes ink from the cup's inked strip, and the strip touches the pad and is recharged.
