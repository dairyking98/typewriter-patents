## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** Column stops on the carriage rack and key-controlled releases arrest the carriage at a chosen column, which allows skipping of columns; decimal stops in each column work the same way, with keys marked for each column.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must be skipped quickly to a chosen column or decimal position.

**Solution:** Column stops on the carriage rack and key-controlled releases arrest the carriage at a chosen column, which allows skipping of columns; decimal stops in each column work the same way, with keys marked for each column.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** carriage rack; column stops; decimal stops; key-controlled releases
