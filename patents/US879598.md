## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a construction wherein the parts may be readily set to effect a movement of the ribbon vibrator in the ordinary manner to cover and uncover the printing point at each print-.
