## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for feeding 'sheets for typewriters, which in a known manner consists of a container attached to the paper trolley, from which the sheets are fed individually to the paper roller by a conveying device.

**How it works (reading):** The container is clipped to the paper cart and its conveyor roller is driven from the paper roller axis by a flexible shaft with a detachable coupling, so the feeder can be connected or removed easily.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A sheet feeder container on the paper cart was hard to fit to or remove from the typewriter.

**Solution:** The container is clipped to the paper cart and its conveyor roller is driven from the paper roller axis by a flexible shaft with a detachable coupling, so the feeder can be connected or removed easily.
