## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Key levers of different lengths give unequal depression and effort across the key banks.

**Solution:** Couple a second-order finger lever to a third-order lever by a link so every key has the same travel and force.

**How it works:** The typebar's connecting rod rests on a third-order lever hinged at the front; a second-order finger-key lever is hinged behind the keyboard. A link between them pulls the upper lever down as the key is pressed. Arrangement of lever lengths per bank equalises depression and effort.
