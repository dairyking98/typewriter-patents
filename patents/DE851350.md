## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**How it works (reading):** The roller tube is made of slotted sheet metal segments bent into a ring and joined at inward-bent webs for low natural frequency and strength.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen rollers with noise and natural vibrations get loud when type strikes them.

**Solution:** The roller tube is made of slotted sheet metal segments bent into a ring and joined at inward-bent webs for low natural frequency and strength.
