## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper feeding mechanism for t-ypewriting machines and has for itsp'rincipal object to provide improved releasing mechanism for releasing or throwing off from the platen the various paper feeding andcontrolling devices which cooperate therewith.

**How it works (reading):** A single releasing operation throws off the main and secondary feed rollers, paper deflector and card guide from the platen, without interfering with the paper finger releasing devices.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed rollers, paper deflector and card guide must each be released separately from the platen, which is cumbersome.

**Solution:** A single releasing operation throws off the main and secondary feed rollers, paper deflector and card guide from the platen, without interfering with the paper finger releasing devices.
