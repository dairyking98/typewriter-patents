## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanisms on a front-strike, shifting-platen typebar machine need improved vibratory ribbon carrier operation, with the right part of the ribbon at the print point in both case positions, plus indicating and hand-positioning devices.

**Solution:** A new operating lever for the vibratory ribbon carrier, plus indicator and hand-positioning devices, controls which ribbon portion meets the platen in lower and upper case.

**How it works:** Key levers drive typebars, each carrying lower and upper case types, against the front of a platen mounted in a platen carrier that rides on the spring-drum carriage. A shift key raises the platen carrier by a shiftable frame supporting a guide rail. Ribbon spools sit vertically beneath the top plate, one at each side, and a vibratory ribbon carrier raises the ribbon to the print point via a novel operating lever. Diagrammatic figures show the printing portion of the ribbon relative to the platen in lower case and shifted upper case position.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** key lever; typebar; platen; platen carrier; carriage; spring drum; guide rail; shift frame; ribbon spool; vibratory ribbon carrier; operating lever

**Improves on:**

- US application 416,080 (shiftable frame): ribbon mechanism added to the shifting-platen machine
