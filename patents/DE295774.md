## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ball bearing part for carrying and guiding the paper trolley of typewriters and connection of the same to its support frame.

**How it works (reading):** The ball track part is a resilient plate fixed along one edge to the support frame or carriage, with its free edge carrying a ball groove, so it presses the balls and compensates automatically for play.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Unevenness and wear in the ball bearings of the paper carriage guide need to be compensated.

**Solution:** The ball track part is a resilient plate fixed along one edge to the support frame or carriage, with its free edge carrying a ball groove, so it presses the balls and compensates automatically for play.
