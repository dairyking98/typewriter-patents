## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (intercepting traversing carriage for tabulating)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for intercepting the transverse work carriage Typewriters or the like In the previously known machines, when tabulating the writing carriage over several longitudinal counters away the Ouerwerkwagen through the set longitudinal counters taken and thereby moved back and forth.

**How it works (reading):** A bolt pushed in by the tabulator key catches a hook on the cross counter and holds it until the carriage finishes its movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When tabulating across columns where counters are set, the cross-counter carriage is needlessly moved back and forth.

**Solution:** A bolt pushed in by the tabulator key catches a hook on the cross counter and holds it until the carriage finishes its movement.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** tabulator rod; bolt; catch hook; cross counter; rail
