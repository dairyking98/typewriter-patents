## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter protective frame The invention relates to a Protective frame for typewriters with detachably attached to the support frame of the machine Side plates, which at their front ends by a U-shaped key lever protection plate are connected. According to the invention, each of the named, separately from then Key lever protection plate designed as special parts on the front side plates and rear ends are formed columnar stiffeners by pressing out the metal and the ends of the key lever guard provided with flanges or tabs which engage the front columnar stiffeners of the side panels and are rigidly connected to them. Preferably, the ends of the key lever guard are given the columnar stiffeners of the side plates corresponding to the engage in the pillars of the side plates and are rigidly connected to them. Through this a simpler and cheaper production of the protective frame is made possible, a effective stiffening of the same achieved and the appearance of the machine improved. .
