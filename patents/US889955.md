## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to the ribbon mechanism of that class of typewriting machines employing a ribbon vibrator or carrier, by which term is meant any device for moving the ribbon to cover and uncover theprinting point.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon vibrator that moves the multi-field ribbon to cover the printing point, with crosswise feed that can be dispensed with at will.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon with several colour fields needs to be moved to cover the printing point and fed crosswise and lengthwise.

**Solution:** A ribbon vibrator that moves the multi-field ribbon to cover the printing point, with crosswise feed that can be dispensed with at will.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
