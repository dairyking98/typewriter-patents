## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper stop on a paper support plate needs a releasable fitting that sits in the window-shaped recess of the plate.

**Solution:** A bent sheet-metal stop bar with guide tabs engages the slot of the paper support plate and is fixed at the back by a bolt-and-spring abutment that can be pulled off.
