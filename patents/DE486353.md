**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a table writing apparatus for typewriters, typewriters, and writing adding machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stop devices need different special fittings for different machines, take up space and are complicated or expensive to make.

**Solution:** Tabulator stops are actuated by pivoting members guided by the position selector levers and acting against the carriage tension spring; one intermediate link serves each pair of selector levers, or one per lever, so the device fits many machines.

**How it works:** Selector lever movement drives the intermediate links into the tab-stop path, and the same links lock or unlock the selector lever.
