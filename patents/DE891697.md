**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a storage of levers for typewriters and the like, in particular on the storage of type levers of sälcher machines.

**How it works (reading):** A tubular bearing piece with hollow conical ends sits on conical pins in two cheeks held under spring tension, so wear is taken up elastically and lubricant stays inside.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers guided in precisely ground slots bind, collect oil-dust and wear.

**Solution:** A tubular bearing piece with hollow conical ends sits on conical pins in two cheeks held under spring tension, so wear is taken up elastically and lubricant stays inside.
