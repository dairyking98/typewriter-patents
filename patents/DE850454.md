## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-carriage parts are hard to assemble precisely and securely on a frame.

**Solution:** An injection-moulded frame with bearing blocks, holes and slots takes axles and levers directly; short axles have heads and are fixed between cheek and bracket.
