## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known line-embossing machines use a reciprocating stamp carrier, which must be moved each time a letter is struck.

**Solution:** A stamp box with matched positive and negative type pairs is shifted sideways by one of two solenoids selected by the key; the stamp carrier returns to its rest position after each coinage.
