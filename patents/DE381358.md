## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shift key had to be a springy switch that locks in the upper case position and release is needed.

**Solution:** A resilient key with spring tension matched to the weight of the paper frame, plus a two-arm lock lever that holds the shift key down until a release button is pressed.
