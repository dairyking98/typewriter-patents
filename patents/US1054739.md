## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to carriage feeding mechanism of typewrlting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A simplified escapement with a perforated escapement disk and feeding dogs, plus a one-way clutch that lets the carriage return without operating the escapement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement feeds the carriage erratically and jumps; its clutch to the pinion is complicated and costly.

**Solution:** A simplified escapement with a perforated escapement disk and feeding dogs, plus a one-way clutch that lets the carriage return without operating the escapement.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
