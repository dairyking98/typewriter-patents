## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for tensioning or winding the web

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for winding and tensioning paper webs, which are to be written on using typewriters or calculating machines.

**How it works (reading):** Each supply and take-up roll is fitted with a spring-loaded friction disc, and gears equalize the spring tensions so the web stays taut and only the supply roll's inertia is overcome.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper webs sag and line feed is resisted by the inertia of all rolls, because the existing winding devices have no elastic effect.

**Solution:** Each supply and take-up roll is fitted with a spring-loaded friction disc, and gears equalize the spring tensions so the web stays taut and only the supply roll's inertia is overcome.
