## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stenographic typebar machine needs a simple, efficient ribbon feed and automatic reverse driven from the machine's own action.

**Solution:** Two spool ratchets driven by spring-pressed pawls on levers that ride on the universal bar, with dogs that detect an empty spool and a lever-and-cam arrangement that swaps which pawl drives.

**How it works:** Two spool spindles carry ribbon spools above and ratchet wheels below. Spring-pressed pawls, wider than the ratchets, are mounted on levers pivoted to the frame and rear arms whose recesses engage the swinging universal bar, so each typebar stroke steps the driven ratchet; springs of the same width prevent slippage on idle strokes. A double-armed lever carries spring-pressed dogs resting on the ribbon on each spool; when a spool is fully unwound its dog drops and, through a slotted lever engaging pins on loose cams beside the ratchets, shifts which pawl is engaged so the ribbon reverses. A retaining dog holds the lever in either position.
