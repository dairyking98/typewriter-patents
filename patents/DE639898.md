**How it works (reading):** Drum is machined in one piece with radial bores and a common ring groove; each square-headed matrix slides in its bore, the heads engage the groove, and return springs sit on the protruding shafts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Matrix or type elements with square heads and smaller guide shafts are hard to fit on a large drum, and drum rings were built up in pieces.

**Solution:** Drum is machined in one piece with radial bores and a common ring groove; each square-headed matrix slides in its bore, the heads engage the groove, and return springs sit on the protruding shafts.
