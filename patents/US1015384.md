## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanism was complicated and its spools hard to detach.

**Solution:** A self-contained ribbon unit with a reversing gear drive, improved spools that detach readily, and a trip lever for reversal.

**How it works:** Vertical spool shafts on brackets have bevel gears at their lower ends. A driving gear on an adjustable shifting shaft meshes with one spool gear or the other, so the spools turn in opposite directions. A swinging sheet-metal arm with a spring-loaded notched lock arm holds the shifting shaft yieldingly at either extreme. A shifting pawl trips the reverse automatically when the ribbon unwinds, and a rock-shaft arm allows manual reversal.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** spool; spool shaft; bevel gear; shifting shaft; swinging arm; locking arm; shifting pawl; base bar
