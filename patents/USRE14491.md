## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to improvements in ribbon operating mechanisms for visible Writin or front strike typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A compact ribbon feed moves the ribbon lengthwise, and a ribbon vibrator with an adjustable throw can be raised or lowered to bring different fields of the ribbon to the print point; the mechanism folds with the collapsible machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed and vibration on a collapsible front-strike typewriter need to be compact, and the throw of the vibrator must be easy to change to use different parts of the ribbon.

**Solution:** A compact ribbon feed moves the ribbon lengthwise, and a ribbon vibrator with an adjustable throw can be raised or lowered to bring different fields of the ribbon to the print point; the mechanism folds with the collapsible machine.
