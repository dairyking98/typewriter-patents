## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** The set or delete button's projection swings a special lever that locks the carriage switching mechanism, the tab switch, and the carriage trigger in the rest position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tab-set and tab-delete buttons can bend or break parts of the carriage switching mechanism if they are pressed while the carriage is moving, so a lock is needed.

**Solution:** The set or delete button's projection swings a special lever that locks the carriage switching mechanism, the tab switch, and the carriage trigger in the rest position.

**How it works:** The set slide moves up through a two-armed lever to the individual tabs; a second lever blocks the carriage release approach while the tab is set; the locked position keeps the keys inoperative until released.
