## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** One escapement should feed the carriage on the down stroke, the up stroke, or both, and the carriage should be stepped back a letter space for corrections.

**Solution:** A single escapement can be set for ordinary, reverse or double letter-space feed, and a manual backspacing device steps the carriage back one letter space at a time.
