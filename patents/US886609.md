## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon colour change should work in both upper and lower case.

**Solution:** Vary the throw of the ribbon by shifting the engagement point between actuator and ribbon lever.

**How it works:** An actuator on a part that reciprocates and also shifts for case has slots engaging studs on ribbon mechanism, one per colour. The actuator pivots to put either slot on a stud, selecting the colour independently of case shift. Underwood.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** actuator; slot; stud; ribbon guide; type bar
