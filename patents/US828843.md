**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper cannot be written to the extreme top and bottom edges on visible-writing machines because the sheet bulges away from the platen.

**Solution:** A guiding arrangement on the carriage holds cards and envelopes against the platen at the print points so writing reaches the edges.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
