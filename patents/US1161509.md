## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the Royal ribbon auto-reverse mechanism the shift roller could strike the side of the shaft cam, straining the latch and the ribbon.

**Solution:** Make the cam-following lever yielding: the roller rides on a pivoted arm held by a light spring so it yields if it meets the high part of the cam.

**How it works:** A shaft driven from the carriage spring barrel has a floating end with a worm that engages either the left spool's worm wheel or a wheel on a cross shaft driving the right spool. When a spool empties, a latch acts on a lever cam face and moves a roller under a cam on the shaft; the cam's high part lifts the floating end to change the worm engagement and reverse the feed. The lever now carries the roller on a pivoted arm with a light spring and a stop lug, so if the roller meets the cam's high part it rocks aside and snaps under once the cam passes. A second improvement lets the automatic control be disengaged for manual spool turning.
