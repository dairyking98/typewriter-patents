## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a type cleaner for type-writing machines, and the purpose of the invention is to provide a mechanism which may be readily put into use without disturbing the arrangement of the machine, and which when in use will serve to remove all dirt, &c.
