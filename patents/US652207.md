## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventor's earlier converging-typebar machine (US570433) had too many parts and complicated typebar guiding and support.

**Solution:** Simplify the machine with loosely resting hooked typebars guided by a converging frame on a shiftable cradle, a single-sheet stamped platen carriage, and many sheet-metal parts.

**How it works:** Converging typebars rest loosely on two cross-rods of a cradle frame; their front ends hook into bell-crank levers driven by key levers. A guide frame with converging walls and a shifting tongue directs each bar to the printing point and also lifts away to expose the last letter, carrying the ribbon guides with it. Depressing a side shift key tips the cradle, bringing the upper or lower set of type into line. A spring-drum pulls the platen carriage, which is a single stamped sheet-metal frame, and a two-pawl escapement driven by a wiper steps it one tooth per key. A deeper release key frees both pawls. A lock on the key levers stops printing at the line end and a bell sounds near it.

**Parts:** typebar; bell-crank lever; key lever; cradle frame; cross-rod; guide frame; shifting tongue; platen; platen carriage; spring drum; rack bar; pawl; wiper; ribbon spool; ratchet; line-space regulator; pressure roller; paper clip; locking lever; bell hammer

**Numbers:** two shift positions (upper and lower series of typebars)

**Improves on:**

- [US570433](../patents/US570433.md): Simpler construction with fewer parts; guide frame now carried by the cradle and typebars hook in without pins
