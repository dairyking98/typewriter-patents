## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing right to left, as in Arabic, normally requires changes to the framework of a machine built for left-to-right writing.

**Solution:** The carriage feeding and returning mechanism can be geared up for either direction with only a minimum change in the mechanism and no change in the framework.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)
