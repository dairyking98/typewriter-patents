## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method of manufacture of key plates for typewriters with alignment marks.

**How it works (reading):** Alignment guide marks are glued in sets to the back of the key card and punched out with the key plates, so the underside marks are made in one step.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Alignment marks on the sides of key plates were tiny and underside marks were hard to make on thin cardboard.

**Solution:** Alignment guide marks are glued in sets to the back of the key card and punched out with the key plates, so the underside marks are made in one step.
