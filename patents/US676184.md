## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of the invention being the provision of a comparatively simple, practical, and effective visible-writing machine.

**How it works (reading):** A visible-writing typebar machine with a case-shift mechanism that locks in shifted position and releases on demand, line spacing at several intervals without adjustment, and margin stops that hold firmly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible writing needed a simple case shift that locks and releases instantly, adjustable line spacing and margins.

**Solution:** A visible-writing typebar machine with a case-shift mechanism that locks in shifted position and releases on demand, line spacing at several intervals without adjustment, and margin stops that hold firmly.
