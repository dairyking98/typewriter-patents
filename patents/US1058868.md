## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards for a card-ruling machine must be fed one at a time from the bottom of the pile.

**Solution:** A card feeder in which a weight rests on the pile, held to a dovetailed gage, and descends by gravity as the cards are fed from the bottom.

**How it works:** The weight rests on the card pile and grips the gage on one side, so it descends as cards are used; set screws adjust the guides laterally on the bed.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** bed; uprights; weight; gage; set screws
