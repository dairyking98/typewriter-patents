## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide an attachment for a type writing machine which will indicate to the operator the particular line upon which he is working, and especially point out the 'last line upon the page, so that the necessity of raising the carria-ge in order to know when the last'line is reached will be avoided.

**How it works (reading):** A graduated plate on the feed table with a slotted, set-screw pointer marks the leading edge of sheets when the last line prints.
