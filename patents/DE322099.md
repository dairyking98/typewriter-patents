> Description Translated from German Key and type lever stop mechanism. The invention is a improved and simplified key and type lever stop mechanism for typewriters, in the drive linkage a toggle link is turned on, which when Keystroke the type lever gradually at increasing speed against the The paper roller moves. In the previously known stop mechanisms belonging to the same type the toggle joint is arranged as the end member of the lever mechanism and engages with one lever arm directly to the type lever, while the drive linkage is connected to the other lever arm and outside the pivot point of the two Attacks lever arms. This training results in a relatively hard stop. and, the further use of lever members between the key lever and the on Type lever attacking toggle joint. This makes the stop mechanism complicated, is difficult to manufacture and Installation and not always reliable in the Operation. It also makes repairs more difficult and is the Cleaning the machine is a hindrance.
