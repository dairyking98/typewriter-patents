## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The toggle joint runs nearly parallel to the key lever and acts directly on it, giving gradual speed-up of the type lever and a softer stop from stamped identical parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The old stop mechanisms had complicated extra lever members and a hard stop.

**Solution:** The toggle joint runs nearly parallel to the key lever and acts directly on it, giving gradual speed-up of the type lever and a softer stop from stamped identical parts.

In the previously known stop mechanisms belonging to the same type the toggle joint is arranged as the end member of the lever mechanism and engages with one lever arm directly to the type lever, while the drive linkage is connected to the other lever arm and outside the pivot point of the two Attacks lever arms. This training results in a relatively hard stop. and, the further use of lever members between the key lever and the on Type lever attacking toggle joint. This makes the stop mechanism complicated, is difficult to manufacture and Installation and not always reliable in the Operation.
