## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to attachments for writing and the like Machines and is used to feed endless paper webs with an intermediate layer of Coal sheets for writing and similar machines.

**How it works (reading):** The frame has adjustable side arms that pivot and slide to reach different carriage parts, a hinged frame that swings back, and a hook that locks it upright on a carriage stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carbon-sheet and endless-paper attachment is hard to fit to carriages of different machines, since the usual arm connection does not suit them all.

**Solution:** The frame has adjustable side arms that pivot and slide to reach different carriage parts, a hinged frame that swings back, and a hook that locks it upright on a carriage stop.
