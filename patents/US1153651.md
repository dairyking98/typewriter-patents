## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Type members repeatedly actuated

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is particularly to provide improved means for writing lines consisting entirely or in part of periods, dashes or other marks in rapid succession without.

**How it works (reading):** Independent actuating means for the printing instruments write lines and vertical rules without repeated key strokes, using a carriage release finger key on the front-strike writing-in-sight machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lines of dots, dashes or vertical rules must be written by repeated key depression, which is tedious.

**Solution:** Independent actuating means for the printing instruments write lines and vertical rules without repeated key strokes, using a carriage release finger key on the front-strike writing-in-sight machine.
