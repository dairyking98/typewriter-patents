## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary tape impregnated with dye is wound together with the spent ribbon, so the capillary action of the ribbon fabric draws colour back into it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbons lose their colour and are discarded, while the fabric is still intact.

**Solution:** An auxiliary tape impregnated with dye is wound together with the spent ribbon, so the capillary action of the ribbon fabric draws colour back into it.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Process for re-inking used ribbons The ones in use today There are ribbons for typewriters, recorders, cash registers and the like almost entirely made of cotton fabric, which is impregnated with a mostly oily dye is. In the course of use, such ribbons gradually become so much Deprived of color that they finally no longer give sufficiently strong impressions and for this reason are no longer usable. However, as the fabric of such color-depleted tapes is almost general is still undamaged, has always consisted of economic for the consumer Establish an incentive to dye the tapes, which are exhausted of color, and thereby continue to make usable. The previously proposed methods for recoloring have However, they cannot prevail because they are expensive, cumbersome and dirty too handle or are too unreliable. The present invention now relates to an inexpensive, reliable and so simple a method of coloring exhausted ribbons of all kinds that it too can be easily practiced by any non-specialist. According to the invention, one or more auxiliary belts are used. These Auxiliary tapes are made of cheap raw materials such as paper, cotton fabric and the like,…
