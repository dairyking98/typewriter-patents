## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, compact, rapid typewriter was wanted.

**Solution:** Inclined sliding type rods that converge on one printing point, with a depressed plate advancing the carriage.

**How it works:** Type rods slide in two guide plates, each inclined so its character at the lower end strikes the same spot on the platen. Springs hold them up. Pressing a rod depresses a central plate through radial slots; the plate drives a bell crank and pawl on a ratchet bar on the carriage, spacing it as the plate rises. A spring pulls the carriage back, a retaining pawl holds it, and a bell sounds near line end.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** type rod; guide plate; coil spring; key button; vertical plate; bell crank; pawl; ratchet bar; carriage; bell
