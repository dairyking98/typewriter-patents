## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon adjusted transversely

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates more particularly to that class of inking-ribbon-feeding mechanism in which the inking-ribbon is reciprocated back and forth laterally while at the same time it is advanced longitudinally, so that the lines of impact of the type occur in zigzag manner across the face of the ribbon and thus more effectively utilize its entire surface.

**How it works (reading):** The spool frames swing sideways while the ribbon advances so impacts zigzag across the ribbon.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon wears only along one line.

**Solution:** The spool frames swing sideways while the ribbon advances so impacts zigzag across the ribbon.

**How it works:** A spring-drum driven shaft carries segment gears engaging upper and lower teeth of driving rods that swing the spool-carrying frames, reciprocating the ribbon laterally as it winds.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** spool frame; segment gear; driving rod; swinging arm; spring drum
