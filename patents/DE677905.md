## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hinged paper guide fingers tilt and can tear or crumple the sheet, and they are swung away when the type strikes them.

**Solution:** Guide fingers slide parallel to the platen at constant distance, are displaced by the type stop, and are returned by springs to their rest position.
