## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**How it works (reading):** A stop screw on a rocker arm limits the backward travel of the frame in normal use; when the stop is moved aside, a locking part pulls the frame back and holds it in the rearward position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** To remove type or key levers, the universal rail (carriage) frame must be pulled far back, but nothing limits or holds it there reliably.

**Solution:** A stop screw on a rocker arm limits the backward travel of the frame in normal use; when the stop is moved aside, a locking part pulls the frame back and holds it in the rearward position.
