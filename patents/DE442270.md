**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocks between keys, e.g. without detent arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such protective devices, in which locking pieces are provided, which by the struck key lever or organs connected to them are moved laterally.

**How it works (reading):** Locking pieces move one after another, with unequal impact distances so the effort is spread, and a fixed distance for each row.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-lock impact on the typist is too strong and differs by row.

**Solution:** Locking pieces move one after another, with unequal impact distances so the effort is spread, and a fixed distance for each row.
