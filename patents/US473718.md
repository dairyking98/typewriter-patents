## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Buyers cannot tell genuine ribbons from inferior ones substituted from the wrapper.

**Solution:** The ribbon is packed in a box of distinctive construction, and the machine has a receptacle shaped to take that box.
