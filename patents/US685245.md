## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copying writings by hand at a keyboard instrument is tedious and unreliable.

**Solution:** A drum with pins, mounted on a shaft, moves strikers that press the keys of the keyboard in sequence, so the instrument reproduces the stored pattern.
