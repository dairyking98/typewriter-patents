## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to automatic ribbonreversing mechanism for type-Writing ma- In the present instance I have applied my invention to an Underwood typewriting machine, though obviously the employment of my invention is not restricted to this particular type of machine, since by slight changes in detail of construction the invention may be adapted to various other type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** The preview ends before the specific reversing arrangement is described; the stated object is to avoid the dead-centre and strain defects.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal can strike the same ribbon point several times, giving faint or no impression, because dead-centre positions occur and strain is put on the ribbon or machine.

**Solution:** The preview ends before the specific reversing arrangement is described; the stated object is to avoid the dead-centre and strain defects.
