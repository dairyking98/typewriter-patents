## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relate to that class of type-writing machines in which several types are mounted upon the type-bars and provision is made whereby either type of any bar can be caused to print at the common impression-point.

**How it works (reading):** Typebar hangers clamp with cap plates to a ball-supported disk, which is shifted for case.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines with several types per bar need better hangers and shifting of the typebar carrier.

**Solution:** Typebar hangers clamp with cap plates to a ball-supported disk, which is shifted for case.

**How it works:** Typebars sit in hangers with journal arms in grooves of a disk, held by a cap plate and screw allowing adjustment. The disk rests on balls and is moved by a lever and lock to shift case; upper-case key shifts the key connections.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** typebar; hanger; cap plate; disk; ball; shift lever
