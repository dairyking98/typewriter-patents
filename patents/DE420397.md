## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction margin slides on edge scales vibrate loose and need exact scale widths.

**Solution:** Edge rule slide holds a spring-loaded tongue that presses the slide against the scale from both sides, so different scale widths work without rework.
