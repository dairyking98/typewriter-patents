**Solution:** A platen core with a sound-deadening filling and a rubber cover, so the noise of the type strike is reduced.

The platens of type-writing machines are usually cylindrical in form and composed of a hollow interior core of wood or metal and a surrounding sheath or cover of soft rubber. In use platens of this construction, while mechanically most desirable, are, however, productive of considerable noise when struck by the types, and hence for this reason are objectionable.
