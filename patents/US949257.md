**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide means operable at will to bring one or another `field of the ribbon, and preferably fields of different colors, into use.

**How it works (reading):** A hand-operated control member on the ribbon vibrator mechanism that shifts the ribbon's rest position to choose a ribbon field or disables the vibrator for mimeograph work.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon vibrator mechanisms on typebar machines lacked a simple way to vary the line of impact along the ribbon, to select between ribbon colour fields, and to disable ribbon lift for stencil cutting.

**Solution:** A hand-operated control member on the ribbon vibrator mechanism that shifts the ribbon's rest position to choose a ribbon field or disables the vibrator for mimeograph work.

**How it works:** The patent is shown on a Monarch-style typebar machine: key levers move typebars through sub-levers and links, and a universal bar trips the feed dogs on a rack so a spring drum moves the carriage one space per stroke. The ribbon vibrator is moved to the printing point by the key action. A controlling member, operable at will, changes the position of the vibrator parts (Figs. 4-6 and a modified form in Figs. 8-10) so a different field of the ribbon, possibly a different colour, is presented, or so the vibrator no longer lifts the ribbon for writing on mimeograph sheets. Opening text only; the controlling details are not in the shown portion.
