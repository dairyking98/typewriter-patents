**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writers, especially of the class designed for use in making type-written entries in books, although capable of being used for the purpose of writing upon single sheets of paper;

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded two-part book support on a moving carriage automatically levels the book surface while the book is moved for line and letter spacing under a relocatable typebar writing mechanism.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A book-entry typewriter must handle books of any thickness, open on either page, and also single sheets.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded two-part book support on a moving carriage automatically levels the book surface while the book is moved for line and letter spacing under a relocatable typebar writing mechanism.

**How it works:** A carriage on rails carries a two-part skeleton book-support with rollers, each half mounted on a cross-shaft with tensioned coil springs so the pages lie level for any thickness. The book is fed for line spacing and moved for letter and word spacing, while the writing mechanism is carried on a cross-bar and can be located over either page and locked. Typebars are operated by keys with a single key set serving upper and lower case through a shifting arrangement, and an escapement lever controls spacing. The ribbon has its own support. Description truncated.
