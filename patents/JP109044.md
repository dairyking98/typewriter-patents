## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter that prints Japanese needs both horizontal and vertical writing, which swaps the roles of cylinder line feed and lateral carriage feed, and there was no single control to change over.

**Solution:** One hooked switching lever shifts the pinion bracket, a bent lever and a travel-and-rocking shaft so the printing stroke and the feed handles drive either the cylinder paper feed or the lateral feed.

**How it works:** The hooked switching lever is linked to the bracket of a lengthwise pinion and passes through a bent lever joined to the crosswise arm and a travel-and-rocking shaft. Pushed left, it meshes the pinion with the cylinder's right-feed rack, biases the toothed sector to turn right, and slides the shaft so its downward lug picks up the typing link; the printing stroke then works the shaft's upward fork end, which feeds the paper in the carriage, while the line-feed handle works the pawl lever against the pinion. Pushed right, the pinion meshes the left-feed rack, the sector is biased left, and the typing link is joined through an engaging bar to the fork-end lever so each stroke works the lateral feed. Return strokes use springs and the lateral feed is driven by a spiral spring in a spring case.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Change-over mechanism between vertical feed and lateral feed in a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a change-over mechanism between lateral and vertical feed in a typewriter. It has two mechanisms: the paper-feed mechanism of the cylinder, and the cylinder's lateral-feed mechanism, which is made of a crosswise arm, a lengthwise flat lever carrying a pawl, a crosswise toothed sector, a crosswise pinion and a lengthwise pinion, linked together as one group. To engage each of these separately with the typing mechanism, the following parts are fitted on the top of the base plate that forms the frame. (1) A hooked switching lever and the bracket of the lengthwise pinion are pivoted there and connected to each other. (2) The lower end of the crosswise arm at the left of the base plate engages, through a bent lever, a fork-end lever loosely fitted on the flat pawl lever. (3) The fork-end lever can be connected to the typing link by an engaging bar. (4) The bent lever engages the travel-and-rocking shaft. (5) The hooked switching lever engages the bent lever.
