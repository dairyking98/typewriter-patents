## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Provide a simple and efficient type movement carrying the typebar from rest to the print point.

**Solution:** The type carrier is pivoted to two links, one a drive link pivoted on a ring and one a horizontal link pivoted to a guided rod, so that its type face moves inward and then vertically to the paper.

**How it works:** Each key lever lifts a radiating guide lever whose outer end moves an upright rod. The rod, pivoted at its top to a downward-inclined drive link fulcrumed on a wire ring, also carries the pivot of a horizontal link joined to another part of the Z-shaped type carrier. The combined motion of the guided rod and the two links turns the carrier, moves it inward toward the centre of the machine, and finally moves it nearly vertically to the platen underside. At rest the type face sits against an inked pad on a ring, which may instead be replaced by a ribbon. A centre guide or directrix under the platen is optional. Modifications connect the rod straight to the key lever.
