## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Not stated in the opening; the text describes a single-key index typewriter with a type wheel and the means for paper feed and inking.

**Solution:** A one-key typewriter in which a pointer swung over a circular character chart turns a type wheel through a sector and band drive, and pressing the single button prints and spaces.

**How it works:** An angled lever pivots about a vertical axis; one arm carries a button and a pointer that moves over a circular segment printed with two rows of characters (letters; figures and signs), the other arm is a circular sector. Two metal tapes from the sector wrap a drum loose on the type wheel's pin, so moving the pointer rotates the wheel. Teeth under the button let it be set into a precise position, and pressing the button lifts a lever linkage that raises a rubber-lined plate carrying the paper against the type wheel. On release a spring returns the lever and a pawl advances a rack under the paper carriage by one tooth (two with the stop removed). The wheel has three rows of type (capitals, small letters, figures) selected by levers that lift the wheel axially; a shield plate with a slot exposes only the chosen letter. A rack and gear drives sponge rollers that move an inked tape, and a separate space key lifts the pawl lever to advance the carriage.
