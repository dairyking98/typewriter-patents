## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an interchangeable and trunnion running Platen for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The platen has axle washers with an internal cone and thread at each end, and trunnions with handwheels screw and cone into them, so the platen is changed without tools and stays concentric.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen rollers running on a continuous axle or on support pins are heavy, and changing them needs tools and loses concentricity.

**Solution:** The platen has axle washers with an internal cone and thread at each end, and trunnions with handwheels screw and cone into them, so the platen is changed without tools and stays concentric.
