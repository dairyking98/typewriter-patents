## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cipher typewriters map key characters to ciphers in a fixed or short repeating loop, so the secret is easy to break.

**Solution:** Each time the carriage is pushed back, a change wheel with irregular tooth gaps for each type wheel is engaged, so the relation between keys and cipher letters is re-scrambled irregularly every line.

**How it works:** A row of type wheels on a shaft is positioned by key-driven selector wheel and intermediate gears, each type wheel having its own gear. A movable carriage runs over the wheels with a hammer that strikes ribbon and paper, a cam that works paper feed and ribbon feed, and a register pawl that holds the wheels during the blow. On the return push the carriage's backward motion rotates the toothed change wheels, which engage the type wheels and advance each by an irregular amount. A pivoting subframe brings gear sets in and out of mesh. A fixed carriage at the keyboard end does the same for decipherment.
