## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices actuated when platen reaches the end of a line

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improvement in type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable alarm bell, carriage stop and type-bar lock act at one chosen point near line end.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator does not notice the carriage reaching line end, and the carriage needs a positive stop there.

**Solution:** An adjustable alarm bell, carriage stop and type-bar lock act at one chosen point near line end.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
