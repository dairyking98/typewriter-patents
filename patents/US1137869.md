## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper gage on a typewriter is hard to reset and can shift accidentally.

**Solution:** A rotatable shaft with a finger-piece and pinions meshing with racks on the gage slides it parallel to the platen, and a thumb-nut locks it.
