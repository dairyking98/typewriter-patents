## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-wheel vehicle steering with a switchable rear-wheel steering needs many shafts and gears, making it heavy and bulky.

**Solution:** Two parallel shafts with a pair of sliding gear segments couple and decouple the rear steering from the front, giving opposed lever motions directly.

**How it works:** Front and rear steering levers sit on two parallel shafts. A gear segment on one shaft is slid by a fork lever into mesh with a segment on the other, reversing the motion. A lateral locking plate covers part of the tooth gaps so meshing is possible only in the middle (straight-ahead) position. A housing is flanged to the frame side member.

**Parts:** steering lever; shaft; gear segment; fork lever; locking plate; housing; push rod
