**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenographic machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**How it works (reading):** A mainspring barrel drives a fly-governor train to a paper-feed roller, with a ribbon and keys that print the strokes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The stenograph needed a self-contained drive to feed the paper without an external motor.

**Solution:** A mainspring barrel drives a fly-governor train to a paper-feed roller, with a ribbon and keys that print the strokes.
