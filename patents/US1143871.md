## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** On Underwood machines the lower edge of the paper springs away from the platen when the platen is shifted, and the old wing scale got in the way.

**Solution:** Replace the wing scale plates with graduated wings carrying fingers that hold the paper lower edge against the platen during shift.

**How it works:** Wings extend from the ribbon vibrator and rise and fall with the platen. Graduations on their upper edges serve as the paper position scale. Fingers or projections cover the printing line and keep the paper under tension when the platen is lifted to capitals. Arms with a yoke slip over posts and over the paper compressor springs.
