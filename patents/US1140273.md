## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Making several inked copies needs multiple ribbons that are wound only at line end, causing uneven wear.

**Solution:** Mount multi-ribbon spools on the carriage and wind them from a rack on the frame during carriage return so fresh ribbon is presented each line.

**How it works:** Several inked ribbons run along the print line with sheets threaded between them. A gear on the carriage meshes with a fixed frame rack. A back-check pawl and ratchet let the gear idle during feed, but on carriage return it drives the spools through bevel gears and shafts. A shiftable shaft reverses winding direction. The rack is shielded behind the frame.
