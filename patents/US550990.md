## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type at the printing point is not reliably aligned, and type are hard to assemble accurately.

**Solution:** A guide or directrix at the printing point aligns each type-lever's type to the centre of the circle, with matching type faces.
