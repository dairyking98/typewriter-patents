## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says type-bars give an uneven stroke because of inertia, and they are hard to remove, adjust and renew individually.

**Solution:** A spiral or extensible link joins each type-bar to its key-lever and cushions the action; each bar can be unhooked and slid out of its socket, and grooves in the type-bed guide the bars so they do not interfere.
