> Description Translated from German Ratchet wheel clutch on typewriters and similar machines The invention relates to a line switching wheel clutch for the so-called piercing roller on writing machines and the like Machines, especially small typewriters, with one loose in the line index wheel lying expansion ring, which is opposite to the one spreading it, on the rotational movement of the Platen continuously participating lever is not rotatable. The coupling between the Line index wheel and the shaft takes place in such. Devices through a widening of the expansion ring in two different ways. In the case of one known type, an approximately sleeve-parallel piece with a pivot pin lying radially to the platen is located as an expansion lever in the opening of the expansion ring. The free end of the lever is bent so that it rests on the tip of a cone of the piercing shaft that carries the handwheel, with the help of which the roller can be rotated by hand.
