## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switching wheel clutch for the so-called piercing roller on writing machines and the like Machines, especially small typewriters, with one loose in the line index wheel lying expansion ring, which is opposite to the one spreading it, on the rotational movement of the Platen continuously participating lever is not rotatable.

**How it works (reading):** A spreading lever or wedge slider on the platen shaft spreads the expansion ring to couple the line index wheel, with more secure grip than the earlier designs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-index wheel clutch with an expanding ring is unreliable because it slips when the ring is dirty or gummed.

**Solution:** A spreading lever or wedge slider on the platen shaft spreads the expansion ring to couple the line index wheel, with more secure grip than the earlier designs.
