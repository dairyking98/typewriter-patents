## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage-type machine with a fixed print point needed a better way to retract the carriage.

**Solution:** A Caligraph-type writer with a carriage that traverses rails over a fixed print point, with a carriage-retracting mechanism that returns the carriage.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Platen pressed to the element](../features/imp-platen-to-element.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
