## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to cushion attachments for type-writer and similar keys, and it has for its object to provide an attachment of this character that shall be both simple and inexpensive.

**How it works (reading):** A glass disk held by an elastic band on the key, so the letter on the key remains visible through the cushion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cushion caps for keys are costly to print with a character and hide the key's own letter.

**Solution:** A glass disk held by an elastic band on the key, so the letter on the key remains visible through the cushion.
