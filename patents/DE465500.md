## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper roll unwinding device for typewriters and the like with one seated between two end disks on a continuous axis of rotation Core roller, the latter in a manner known per se through into the ends of their axial bore entering conical parts is held centrically.

**How it works (reading):** One end disc slides axially on the spindle against a coil spring and locks by a bayonet pin; after the grip disc is twisted the disc and spring pull off the axle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Replacing a paper roll on an unwinding core needs an easy removal method for the core and its end disc.

**Solution:** One end disc slides axially on the spindle against a coil spring and locks by a bayonet pin; after the grip disc is twisted the disc and spring pull off the axle.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
