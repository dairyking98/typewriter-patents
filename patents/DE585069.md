## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Registering devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an alignment device for with rows of holes provided writing sheets on typewriters with one over the shift position liftable platen and one in front of the platen in the insertion direction of the sheets arranged, with several perpendicular to the longitudinal direction of the platen standing pin rows equipped feed table.

**How it works (reading):** The pin-row table pivots on spring-loaded joints, releasing the sheets as it lowers and pressing them against the roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pin-row feed tables for perforated sheets made insertion awkward, and sheets slipped out of the pins.

**Solution:** The pin-row table pivots on spring-loaded joints, releasing the sheets as it lowers and pressing them against the roller.
