## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons with several coloured or functional fields need to be positioned under a key's control, and crosswise feed must be able to be turned off.

**Solution:** A ribbon vibrator or carrier that under key control moves the ribbon to cover the printing point with a chosen field.

**How it works:** A Monarch typebar machine with a split ribbon (black and red, or copying and record fields). The ribbon feeds lengthwise between two spools through bevel gears. A vibrator operated by the printing keys raises the ribbon, and a selecting device decides which field is brought to the printing point. The crosswise feed can be cut out for ribbons used lengthwise only.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon spool; vibrator; bevel gear; dog rocker; universal bar; spring drum
