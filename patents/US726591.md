## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing on both faces of the printing material

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines, and refers more speciiically to guiding devices for guiding the advance edge of a sheet of paper upon the platen as it approaches the striking-point of the typeheads and for holding the same properly engaged with the platen at the time it receives the impression of the types.

**How it works (reading):** Paper-guiding devices holding the sheet engaged with the platen at impression.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper edge must be guided onto the platen near the Oliver striking point.

**Solution:** Paper-guiding devices holding the sheet engaged with the platen at impression.

**How it works:** Guides on the Oliver type-bar frames direct the advancing edge of a sheet to the striking point and hold it against the platen while the types strike.
