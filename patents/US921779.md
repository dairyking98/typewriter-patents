## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the line-spacing devices of typewriting machines, and particularly to means for facilitating the writing of exponents, fractions, etc.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary line-space wheel with staggered teeth and its own detent is added; the two wheels alternately hold the platen, giving sixty rotative positions instead of thirty.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen can only stop at the main line-space positions, so exponents and fractions need half-line movements.

**Solution:** An auxiliary line-space wheel with staggered teeth and its own detent is added; the two wheels alternately hold the platen, giving sixty rotative positions instead of thirty.
