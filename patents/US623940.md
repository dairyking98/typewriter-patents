## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typists have no automatic way to leave a fixed bottom margin on a page.

**Solution:** A screw on the platen shaft drives a nut-and-arm that drops off the carriage rail after a set number of platen turns and locks the carriage.

**How it works:** A threaded sleeve on the platen shaft turns with line spacing, forcing a non-rotating nut and arm sideways along it. After a set amount of platen rotation the arm's end falls into a slot or off the rail terminus of the carriage, so the carriage cannot return. A graduated scale and indicator on the platen end set the number of lines per page.
