## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to construct a stencil-cutting machine which shall be accurate and convenient in its operation and which may be used for cutting letters of more than one size.

**How it works (reading):** Disks carry the punches and dies in two circular rows, and the punch-operating means is adjustable so it engages either row. The disks sit on a low-table framework with a feed mechanism that can be thrown out of operation.
