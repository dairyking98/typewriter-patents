## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on soundproofing devices for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A wide felt or cushion pad sits under the straight and cranked parts of each type lever with a rounded edge near the pivot so the lever rests on it on return, and the frame and covers are sound-absorbing and form a closed space around the working parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter noise comes from the type levers hitting their support on the fall-back and from the open frame that lets the noise out.

**Solution:** A wide felt or cushion pad sits under the straight and cranked parts of each type lever with a rounded edge near the pivot so the lever rests on it on return, and the frame and covers are sound-absorbing and form a closed space around the working parts.
