## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (counter calculation reversal on typewriters)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device to cry breaking machines, calculating machines, Addierniaschinen o.

**How it works (reading):** The operative position of the reverse link is cancelled independently of the calculating drive, automatically at the start of carriage movement, through the carriage-controlled members.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic reversal of the calculating type of counters fails when the carriage is not returned to its start or the reversing link cannot be reset when a special link is used.

**Solution:** The operative position of the reverse link is cancelled independently of the calculating drive, automatically at the start of carriage movement, through the carriage-controlled members.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
