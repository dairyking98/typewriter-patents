## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type lever mechanism for typewriters, its type lever when pressing a key lever a swinging movement in an im perform essential horizontal position in front of the roller, whereupon they are controlled by all type levers common and brought into effect when further depressing the key lever Pressure member are pressed against the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Type levers on a stationary pin are swung into the printing position by toggle levers with slotted guides, and the type block pressure element is then brought onto the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-stop type levers can be stopped too strongly or weakly, leaving the type block misaligned, and the keystroke is heavy.

**Solution:** Type levers on a stationary pin are swung into the printing position by toggle levers with slotted guides, and the type block pressure element is then brought onto the platen.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** toggle levers; slotted guide
