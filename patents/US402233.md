## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Self-acting carriages need a simple way to be driven forward and then automatically returned after a fixed travel.

**Solution:** A rack on the carriage is moved in and out of mesh with a one-way driving pinion by a shuttle-and-spring switching device, so the carriage is driven forward then released to return.

**How it works:** A pinion driven only forward, intermittently one tooth per step, meshes with a rack mounted on the carriage via a parallel-ruler linkage so it can move toward and away from the pinion. A fixed shuttle and a guide on the rack with two spring-carried switch arms throw the rack out of mesh at the end of travel, letting the carriage return, and back into mesh at the start. Hooks may limit the return stroke. Variants omit one switch or mount the carriage differently.

**Parts:** bed plate; carriage; driving shaft; pinion; rack; angle lever; connecting rod; shuttle; guide; switch arm; spring; hook
