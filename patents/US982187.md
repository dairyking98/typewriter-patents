## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to devices for re leasably connecting the platen to the linespace wheel of a typewriting machine, to permit the platen to be rotated independently of the line space wheel when required.

**How it works (reading):** A split friction ring inside the line-space wheel drum is expanded by dogs that a sliding hand wheel on the platen axle wedges apart, which binds or releases the wheel from the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must sometimes turn independently of the line-space wheel, and existing machines lack a simple way to release it.

**Solution:** A split friction ring inside the line-space wheel drum is expanded by dogs that a sliding hand wheel on the platen axle wedges apart, which binds or releases the wheel from the platen.
