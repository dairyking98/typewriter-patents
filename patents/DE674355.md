## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (telegraph type printer escapement) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or local circuits for step-by-step systems

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to pressure telegraphs, their type wheel or the like when receiving telegraphic pulses step by step under escapement drive is rotated.

**How it works (reading):** The type carrier is moved by two escapements acting on the same adjusting element in differently sized steps, double-current pulses drive them in turn, and pauses of different lengths switch from setting to the print operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ticker-driven type printers print very slowly, even with a double type wreath, because each setting step takes a full pulse run.

**Solution:** The type carrier is moved by two escapements acting on the same adjusting element in differently sized steps, double-current pulses drive them in turn, and pauses of different lengths switch from setting to the print operation.

**Features:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)
