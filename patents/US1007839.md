## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A brake shoe pressing on a carriage bar gives uneven braking, because manufacturing irregularities and carriage travel vary the contact.

**Solution:** A flexible strip runs between two pivoted brake jaws that a key depression squeezes together, so the jaws bite the strip with a much greater force than the finger applies, giving uniform braking along the carriage run.

**How it works:** The jaws are connected to the tabulator key so they close on the strip together; a pressure regulator limits the grip on the strip.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** carriage strip; brake jaws; key lever; pressure regulator; back rail; carriage
