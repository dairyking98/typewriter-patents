## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type carriers for number signs) · **Subject (patent classes):** Types or dies upon arcuate bars

**How it works (reading):** Each balancing works gets its own control unit, so it drives additively or subtractively according to the marking on the card field or the sign of its balance, and all units can run in one machine pass.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punched-card tabulating and printing adding machines with balancing works could not set each balancing unit's drive direction independently for direct subtraction.

**Solution:** Each balancing works gets its own control unit, so it drives additively or subtractively according to the marking on the card field or the sign of its balance, and all units can run in one machine pass.
