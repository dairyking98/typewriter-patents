## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a tabulator on writing, writing arithmetic, Adding machines or the like, in which the release of the carriage by triggering the linkage serving the inhibiting device by means of a manually controllable power drive brought into action.

**How it works (reading):** A manually controlled power drive cancels the inhibiting device as a function of carriage movement, so the release is independent of how long the key is held and the carriage stops at the right place.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A hand-operated tabulator releases the carriage inhibit only while the key is held, which interrupts the typing rhythm and causes faults when the key is let go early.

**Solution:** A manually controlled power drive cancels the inhibiting device as a function of carriage movement, so the release is independent of how long the key is held and the carriage stops at the right place.
