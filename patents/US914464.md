## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Stencils made with a typewriter need a perforation for every sign, which wastes paper in step-by-step advance.

**Solution:** The stencil paper is moved continuously with the rotating type wheel, and perforation is made at each stroke of the type across a section of the strip, instead of stepping the paper for each character.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
