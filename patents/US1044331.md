## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen throw between cases must be determined and adjusted exactly and independently of the carriage, and the ribbon carrier must stay correctly related to the platen position.

**Solution:** Eccentrics on a shift shaft driven by shift-key levers via a rack and pinion carry the platen frame to dead centre in both cases, and the ribbon carrier is moved from the same shift mechanism.

**How it works:** The platen frame hangs on arms pivoted to a rock shaft on the carriage, with links down to eccentrics on a shift shaft. Shift keys on bell cranks press rollers against levers on a shaft with a rack that meshes with a pinion keyed on the eccentric shaft, which can slide as the carriage feeds; flanges hold the pinion in mesh. A half turn of 180 degrees puts the eccentrics on dead centre in each case, locking the frames. Adjustable stops in slots of the lever arms set the end of travel and take the shock at the pivot. The ribbon carrier is shifted from the same mechanism.
