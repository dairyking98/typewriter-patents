## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** Refresh ribbons in their closed spool carriers using heavy-boiling hydrocarbons (slow evaporation), with an absorbent layer under a perforated shelf and a single slot for the ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Vapour refreshing of ribbons leaks out of the machine and can over-saturate the ribbon, giving unclean type.

**Solution:** Refresh ribbons in their closed spool carriers using heavy-boiling hydrocarbons (slow evaporation), with an absorbent layer under a perforated shelf and a single slot for the ribbon.
