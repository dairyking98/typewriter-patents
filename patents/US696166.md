## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing needs a fine adjustable width, the platen must hold without spring catches that snap, and mechanisms need many parts.

**Solution:** Automatic line-spacing on one compound lever stroke, a friction-gear line spacing with infinitely fine adjustment, and a platen hold that avoids spring catches.

**How it works:** A compound lever pivoted on the carriage frame carries a beveled segment that engages a friction gear, so the line advance is set by the bevel angle and the platen holds without spring catches.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** compound lever; segment; friction gear; platen
