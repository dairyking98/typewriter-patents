## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**How it works (reading):** A paper cup shaped and mounted so that, pressed from the front, it draws the inserted sheet taut from front to back and keeps the sheets aligned.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Clamping several sheets with pressure rollers lets them drift out of line and the type strikes hollow.

**Solution:** A paper cup shaped and mounted so that, pressed from the front, it draws the inserted sheet taut from front to back and keeps the sheets aligned.
