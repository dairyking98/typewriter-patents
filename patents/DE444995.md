## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash registers that print amounts in different columns of a control strip, customer card and account book need a laterally shifting amount type wheel set, which previously required a cumbersome, expensive intermediate drive.

**Solution:** The hollow shafts carrying the amount type wheels are split so the type-wheel parts slide axially against the drive-gear parts while still rotating with them, removing the intermediate drive.

**How it works:** Amount keys set the amount type wheels through gear sets, and special keys select the column. Six amount type wheels are enclosed between a disc with two bolts and an end plate and shift sideways as a group. Each hollow shaft is in two parts coupled by a shoulder engaging a cutout so the wheel part slides axially but turns with the drive. The shift occurs only in addition mode; the three left wheels used for sub- and final totals stay behind. A hammer prints onto the control strip.
