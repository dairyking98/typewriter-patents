## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The auxiliary sub-frame for long carriages needs a quicker and better centred attachment to the machine frame.

**Solution:** Spring-loaded bolts in the axial bores of the sub-frame guide rods snap into holes in the fixed carriage guide rod, so the frame is attached without screws.

**How it works:** The bolts are pulled back by buttons to slip the sub-frame over the rods, and then snap into the holes in the machine guide rod.
