## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars and their actions are hard to remove and replace, and key tension is uneven across the keyboard.

**Solution:** Rack-geared typebars driven by slides and angular levers that can be detached individually, with a sliding bar that releases the typebar pivots.

**How it works:** Each typebar has teeth meshing with a rack on a slide, linked to an angular lever and a key lever. A pivoted latch lets levers be withdrawn. Typebar pivots sit in open recesses held by a segmental bar on oblique pin-and-slot connections; endwise pressure moves it sideways and releases any typebar. Key-lever spring tension is set per key by screws to equalise key weight.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** typebar; segment; slide; angular lever; key lever; latch; segmental bar; spring; set-screw; universal bar
