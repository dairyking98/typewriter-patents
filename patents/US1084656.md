## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to ribbon mechanism of front strike typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Spool-mounted latches drop when the ribbon runs off a spool and move a shift member that a spring-barrel-driven cam uses to throw the drive from one spool to the other.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand reversing the ribbon direction wastes attention and ribbons can run out.

**Solution:** Spool-mounted latches drop when the ribbon runs off a spool and move a shift member that a spring-barrel-driven cam uses to throw the drive from one spool to the other.

**How it works:** The spring drum drives a worm through a spur gear and shaft, which engages a worm gear for the left spool or one on a cross shaft for the right. On each spool an elbow latch lies against the hub while ribbon is wound. When the ribbon unwinds, the latch drops by its spring and engages a light pivoted shift member that the ribbon draft moves. A cam device on the spring barrel then moves the worm to the other gear. A hand setting holds it in the middle so ribbon can be hand wound.
