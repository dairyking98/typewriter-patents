## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an arrangement on typewriters in which the bearing of the type lever on a segment-like, slotted part of the Machine are arranged.

**How it works (reading):** A toothed segment that doubles as a dust plate is displaceable; in one position its slots lock the type-lever hooks on the bearing rod, and in another they allow removal of the lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers on a slotted segment must be secured in their bearings, but also removable for replacement, and the slots need protection from dust.

**Solution:** A toothed segment that doubles as a dust plate is displaceable; in one position its slots lock the type-lever hooks on the bearing rod, and in another they allow removal of the lever.
