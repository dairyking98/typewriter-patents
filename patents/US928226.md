## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The paper-feed roller pressure is hard to set evenly, and the inventor wants it adjustable and easy to release.

**Solution:** Spring-pressed roller arms are pivoted on a rock-shaft and tensioned by threaded screws that draw the spring ends toward each other, forcing the rollers against the platen; a key-driven crank and link rocks the shaft to release them.
