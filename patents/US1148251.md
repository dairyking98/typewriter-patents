## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage-feeding spring barrel needs a simpler assembly and smoother bearing for rotation.

**Solution:** The spring barrel rotates on a multi-part shaft with a sleeve hooked to the spring; ball bearings and a simple assembled shaft are used.
