## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**How it works (reading):** A reflector beneath the platen with an indicator aligned to the impression point shows the last-typed characters for tabulating without raising the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Under-strike typewriters hide the last line written, so columns need the carriage raised to check.

**Solution:** A reflector beneath the platen with an indicator aligned to the impression point shows the last-typed characters for tabulating without raising the carriage.
