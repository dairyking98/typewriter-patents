## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling

**How it works (reading):** A prismatic body with an ink reservoir and writing wheel has two perpendicular wing lugs that seat in the type guide, holding the wheel horizontal or vertical for straight ruled lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Clip-on line drawers for typewriters are either awkward to install and remove (clamping screws) or wobble in the type guide (spring clamp).

**Solution:** A prismatic body with an ink reservoir and writing wheel has two perpendicular wing lugs that seat in the type guide, holding the wheel horizontal or vertical for straight ruled lines.

**How it works:** The wings locate the body rigidly in the type guide, so the ink wheel rolls straight along the carriage path to draw horizontal or vertical lines.

**Features:** [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** prismatic body; ink reservoir with screw plug; writing wheel; wing lugs
