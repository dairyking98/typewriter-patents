## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed must be reversed by hand when a spool runs out.

**Solution:** Spool-end pawls release a spring-loaded shifting bar that reverses the ribbon drive, also operating a transverse ribbon shift.

**How it works:** On a Remington, a drive shaft turns the ribbon spool shafts, and a bar running across the machine connects shifting mechanism at both ends. A lever moves the shaft and bar one way and a detent holds them; when a ribbon end unwinds, pawls on the spool trip a release lever, the detent frees, and a spring throws the bar to the other position, reversing the feed. The same movement operates the transverse ribbon feed.
