## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writer attachments, and has forits object to construct an attachment adapted to be applied to a type-writing machine for holding the type separately in an easily accessible position while being cleaned.

**How it works (reading):** A plate with a type-receiving hole and locking device that holds one type projected up for cleaning, with adjustable end supports to fit the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type cannot be cleaned easily while still on the typebar of the machine.

**Solution:** A plate with a type-receiving hole and locking device that holds one type projected up for cleaning, with adjustable end supports to fit the machine.
