## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to devices for reconnecting the platen to the linespa ce wheel of a typewriting machine, to permit the platen to be rotated independently of the line-space wheel.

**How it works (reading):** Dogs in a friction drum on the line-space wheel are set by a conical finger wheel that slides along the platen axle through helical cams, connecting or releasing the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must be rotated independently of the line-space wheel, with a low-cost device for existing machines.

**Solution:** Dogs in a friction drum on the line-space wheel are set by a conical finger wheel that slides along the platen axle through helical cams, connecting or releasing the platen.
