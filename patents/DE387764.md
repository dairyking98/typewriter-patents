## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage scale is partly hidden below the print point by the typebar segment and guide, and a single pointer runs off the end of the scale at the margins, so the exact print position is hard to read.

**Solution:** Two pointers, one each side of the print point, each with two tips at fixed distances, read against the normal carriage scale.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on rails and rollers or balls; a scale rail with divisions matching letter spacing is fixed to the carriage side parts. Two openwork pointers, left and right, are fixed on the front rail and point to scale divisions just above the rail. Inner tips are exactly ten divisions from the print point's vertical, outer tips twenty. The operator reads the two tips and takes the print point as the middle (e.g. tips at 32 and 52 give 42). If one pointer runs off the scale end, the other still shows the position.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** carriage; scale rail; pointer; pointer tip; typebar bearing segment; type guide

**Numbers:** inner tips 10 divisions from print point; outer tips 20 divisions from print point

**Improves on:**

- single pointer carriage scale: two flanking pointers keep the print position readable at the scale ends and when the scale is hidden
