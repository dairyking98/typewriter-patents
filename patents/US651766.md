## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a book typewriter with a hinged platen, the free end of the platen must be propped at any height, but the support usually obstructs the space under the platen where the book lies.

**Solution:** A vertically adjustable support for the free end of the platen, mounted on a longitudinally adjustable carrier at the platen end so the space beneath stays clear for the book.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
