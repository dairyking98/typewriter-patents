## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates particularly to a machine in which as finger-keys are operated corresponding characters are impressed in proper relation to each other into suitable impressionreceiving material-as, for exemple, a wood block-thereby producing a linematrix adapted for use in casting a type-bar;
