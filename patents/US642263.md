## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix machine finger keys) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates particularly to a machine in which as finger-keys are operated corresponding characters are impressed in proper relation to each other into suitable impressionreceiving material-as, for exemple, a wood block-thereby producing a linematrix adapted for use in casting a type-bar;

**How it works (reading):** A machine in which finger-keys impress corresponding characters, in proper relation, into an impression-receiving material such as a wood block, producing a line-matrix. The text is cut off before the mechanism is described.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The inventor wants to make line-matrices for casting type bars from finger-key operation rather than by hand, with the same features usable on typewriting machines.

**Solution:** A machine in which finger-keys impress corresponding characters, in proper relation, into an impression-receiving material such as a wood block, producing a line-matrix. The text is cut off before the mechanism is described.
