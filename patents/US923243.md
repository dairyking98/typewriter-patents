## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type bars pivoted on arcs of differing radii give uneven touch, and pivots collect dust.

**Solution:** Type bars hang in journal bearings on transverse pivot bars so that their arcs share a near-uniform radius, giving even touch; the platen can be shifted.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
