## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapements with a main pawl and auxiliary pawl give shocks, noise and limit speed.

**Solution:** A ratchet wheel is held by two pawls on a free oscillating frame; the tooth shape makes the pawl slide out when a key-moved stop releases it, so the pawls act as brake and anchor.

**How it works:** The carriage spring turns a gear meshing a rack; a jamming ratchet wheel on the gear axis is held by two pawls on an oscillating frame, with a control member moved by the keys. When the stop frees one pawl, the wheel's own rotation slides it along the tooth and out, while the other pawl falls behind the next tooth. Drive force goes through the frame pivot so the pawls act as a brake, and an eccentric adjusts letter spacing widely.
