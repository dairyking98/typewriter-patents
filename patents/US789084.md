## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon attachments for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A second ribbon of a different colour is attached and brought to the printing point as the operator chooses, improving on an earlier ribbon-spool carriage design.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A second-colour ribbon must be presented at the printing point on demand, and the earlier ribbon-spool attachment did not do this.

**Solution:** A second ribbon of a different colour is attached and brought to the printing point as the operator chooses, improving on an earlier ribbon-spool carriage design.
