## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing work on a typewriter needs the platen turned back and forward by hand to load each invoice sheet, with the position left to operator judgment.

**Solution:** Billing attachment with stop devices that limit how far the platen rotates backward and forward, so each new invoice is positioned repeatably; it bolts onto existing machines.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
