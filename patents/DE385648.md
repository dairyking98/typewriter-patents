## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such individual bearing bodies For intermediate levers as connecting parts of button lever rods and type levers of typewriters with a front stop and consists in that each bearing of one is formed from sheet metal cut and U-shaped bent bearing body.

**How it works (reading):** Each intermediate lever bearing is a U-shaped body cut and bent from sheet metal, so the bearings can be placed at offset axes on a crosspiece of a switchable segment carrier.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Intermediate lever bearings packed close together, with axes that do not sit on one line or arc, need costly individual bearing bodies on a common plate.

**Solution:** Each intermediate lever bearing is a U-shaped body cut and bent from sheet metal, so the bearings can be placed at offset axes on a crosspiece of a switchable segment carrier.
