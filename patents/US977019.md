## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to line-spacing niechanismsfand especially to devices for advancing the'platen through part of a line space to enable the printing of characters, or symbols, fete, below the usual writing line on the work sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A dog is forced into the line-space wheel to advance the platen through more than half a line, and the spring detent is cast off automatically, so the platen is held where adjusted. Smaller types on the bars print exponents above the line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Denominators and subscripts cannot be printed below the writing line because the platen spring detent resists the needed advance of more than half a line.

**Solution:** A dog is forced into the line-space wheel to advance the platen through more than half a line, and the spring detent is cast off automatically, so the platen is held where adjusted. Smaller types on the bars print exponents above the line.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
