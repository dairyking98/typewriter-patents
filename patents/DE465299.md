## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon guide for cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an ink ribbon guide for check registers, calculating machines and the like, and aims at improvements by means of which the replacement of the ink ribbon is facilitated.

**How it works (reading):** Two push rods, one for each run direction, each carry a pivoted tooth that rides over a ratchet disc in one direction and catches in the other, so the active rod is held according to ribbon direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon advance in check and calculating registers needs a reliable feed in whichever direction the ribbon runs.

**Solution:** Two push rods, one for each run direction, each carry a pivoted tooth that rides over a ratchet disc in one direction and catches in the other, so the active rod is held according to ribbon direction.
