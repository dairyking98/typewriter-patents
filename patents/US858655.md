## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A card-holder on a platen should be easy to detach without fumbling with its claws.

**Solution:** The card-holder has a spring strip with a forked arm at each end; the arms are flexible and hooked so that pressing them releases claws from the platen head and a pull on the hooks lifts it away.

**How it works:** Pressure on the flexible arms flexes the forked ends clear of the platen head; a slight upward pull then removes the holder.

**Parts:** forked arms; hooked release
