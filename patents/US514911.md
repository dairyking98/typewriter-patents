## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars carrying several characters need the platen shifted so each prints on the same line.

**Solution:** A shift key raises the pivoted paper carriage and platen vertically through a rock shaft and roller arms.

**How it works:** The carriage is hinged at its rear and its front rests on rollers on a swinging bail. A finger-strip shift key with levers and links rocks a rod carrying roller arms under the bail arms. Depressing the key lifts the bail, carriage and platen, presenting a different type row of each typebar. Adjustable hooks limit the upward travel.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; platen; swinging bail; rock shaft; roller arm; shift key; link with turnbuckle; adjustable hook

**Improves on:**

- horizontal or vertical platen shifts: vertical oscillating carriage lift
