## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**How it works (reading):** A support with guiding members outside and spanning the printing field lets the machine be moved aside by its line-spacing movement from one member to the other.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A book typewriter needs a support that lets the machine move wholly clear of the printing area so the book can be adjusted or its leaves turned.

**Solution:** A support with guiding members outside and spanning the printing field lets the machine be moved aside by its line-spacing movement from one member to the other.

**Features:** [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
