**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates generally to typewriting machines, and more particularly to typewriting machines including variable spacing means, my object being to provide means whereby three dilferent spacings may be accomplished, one of which is equal to the ordinary spacing between letters.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Standard typewriters have only one letter spacing.

**Solution:** Two escapement shafts under one set of keys give full, half, and one-and-a-half-space advances.

**How it works:** One shaft controls the full spacing and another the half spacing, operated independently or together from a single key lever. Setting the shift lock changes capitals to one and a half times the normal spacing. A release key withdraws one escapement pawl to free the carriage. A front indicator and carriage scale show the normal advance.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** escapement shaft; controlling bar; key lever; pawl; carriage scale; indicator; shift lock

**Numbers:** full, half and 1.5 times normal spacing
