## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a key lever guide comb for typewriters, which is also designed as a bearing for intermediate levers.

**How it works (reading):** The lower end of the key lever guide comb is rolled around an axis that carries the intermediate lever, so the rolled part gives a larger guide surface and the intermediate lever is guided precisely by a slot; the feeler lever runs in the same slot.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Intermediate levers are guided insufficiently by a flat support bar or by slots cut in a second row on the key lever guide comb, and such combs are costly to make.

**Solution:** The lower end of the key lever guide comb is rolled around an axis that carries the intermediate lever, so the rolled part gives a larger guide surface and the intermediate lever is guided precisely by a slot; the feeler lever runs in the same slot.
