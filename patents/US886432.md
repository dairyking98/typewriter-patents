## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing is limited to fixed distances, and the platen is locked when at rest.

**Solution:** A free disk on the platen with a clutch, skew-bevel segment and adjustable dial varies the line space within range and lets the platen turn either way when at rest.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** Fig. 3 adjustable dial
