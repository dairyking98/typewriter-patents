## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding assemblies, e.g. book-like assemblies made up of single sheets or forms; Manifolding assemblies, e.g. book-like assemblies with pressure-sensitive layers or coating other than carbon; Letter-cards or letter-sheets, i.e. cards or sheets each of which is to be folded with the message inside and to serve as its own envelope for mailing

**How it works (reading):** The glue layer of a folded postcard is mixed with a dye, so the adhesive itself copies when a sheet is placed on it, and folding covers it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon paper must be inserted on postcards, and dye in the adhesive can soil the next leaf.

**Solution:** The glue layer of a folded postcard is mixed with a dye, so the adhesive itself copies when a sheet is placed on it, and folding covers it.
