## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shorthand characters need many combinations, which a conventional typewriter layout cannot give compactly.

**Solution:** Key levers are pivoted on stepped spindles and each U-shaped bracket drives a type bar that slides in a straight line within a compact block; the type bars have transverse channels for universal rods, and keys can be struck together or one after another to print at different heights.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Shorthand typewriter. According to the Union Treaty of June 2, igii, priority is claimed for this application on the basis of the application in Great Britain of July 21, 1923. The invention relates to a shorthand typewriter. The key levers of the machine corresponding to the characters are mounted next to each other on spindles. The spindles are arranged one below the other in steps and are firmly connected to the side walls of the machine. In this way, the spindles with the side walls form the actual frame. Below the spindles, the type bars are arranged in the middle of the frame and can be shifted in a straight line, in a block shape one above the other and next to one another. Each key lever consists of a U-shaped bracket, the bracket legs of which are rotatably mounted on one of the spindles. Each bracket has a downward protruding shoulder which engages in a recess of the corresponding type bar, so that the movement of a key lever causes a longitudinal displacement of the associated type bar.
