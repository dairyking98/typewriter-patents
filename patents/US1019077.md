## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type strikes the ribbon directly, soiling or gumming it, and the ribbon cannot easily be moved to a fresh area or colour.

**Solution:** The stock ribbon carriers are replaced by an adjustable ribbon throw that grasps the ribbon and a protector, so the striking point can be shifted or the ribbon removed from the printing field.
