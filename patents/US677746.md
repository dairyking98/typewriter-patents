## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible typewriter needed lighter, more uniform touch and simpler construction.

**Solution:** Improved typebar mounting on rock-shafts and a crown bearing segment that carries the typebars, plus keyboard mechanism, for light and even touch.
