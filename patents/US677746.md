**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more especially to visible type-Writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** Improved typebar mounting on rock-shafts and a crown bearing segment that carries the typebars, plus keyboard mechanism, for light and even touch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible typewriter needed lighter, more uniform touch and simpler construction.

**Solution:** Improved typebar mounting on rock-shafts and a crown bearing segment that carries the typebars, plus keyboard mechanism, for light and even touch.
