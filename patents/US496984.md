## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in the features of construction and combinations of devices hereinafter more fully described and particularly pointed out in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** Card-holding means guide a stiff card between the platen and the feed-roller, and the platen is moved so the card is brought to the writing position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stiff cards and heavy paper blur or slip on the cylindrical platen and lose alignment.

**Solution:** Card-holding means guide a stiff card between the platen and the feed-roller, and the platen is moved so the card is brought to the writing position.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
