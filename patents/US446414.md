## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars cut from sheet steel vary in thickness, so they bind or play in the slots of the supporting ring, spoiling alignment.

**Solution:** Stamp a blank between dies so the sides are recessed and the displaced metal fills a raised bearing rim, giving uniform thickness cheaply.

**How it works:** A blank is cut from sheet steel no thicker than the finished bar; the fore end may be upset. It is pressed between dies that thin the front half, round the head boss, recess the sides of the tail and raise a bearing rim and pivot bosses from the displaced metal. Any fin is removed afterwards. The bar is light, stiff and has minimal bearing area in the ring slot.
