## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (account card tabulating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Methods or arrangements for marking the record carrier in digital fashion otherwise than by punching by printing code marks

**How it works (reading):** Light cells sense marker strips on the account card, and the strip is printed longer each pass so the start line shifts correctly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Account card advance in punched-card accounting machines is hard to control by hand, so print lines can gap or overlap on re-processing.

**Solution:** Light cells sense marker strips on the account card, and the strip is printed longer each pass so the start line shifts correctly.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
