## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Every character gets the same carriage space regardless of width, so narrow letters like l leave gaps.

**Solution:** Divide the rack into fine teeth and use a graduated stop with pins of three lengths so the key touched lets the escapement pawl pass one, two or three teeth.

**How it works:** A rocking shaft carries a fixed pawl and a movable pawl that swings sideways across the carriage rack E to release and re-catch it. In place of one stop, an arm N pivoted on a horizontal shaft carries three pins of different lengths that limit how far the movable pawl swings sideways, so it skips one, two or three teeth. The keys representing narrow, medium or wide letters choose the pin. The arm can yield backwards so the longer pins do not block the pawl's return stroke.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** carriage rack; fixed pawl; movable pawl; graduated stop arm; stop pin; rock shaft; spring

**Improves on:**

- single-stop pawl escapements: graduated stop gives one to three tooth spaces by letter width
