## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A new key-lever and type-bar action is wanted for a typebar machine with two banks of keys, with the bars guided to prevent lateral springing.

**Solution:** Rearward-pivoted key levers in slotted rails of varying width are linked to alternately front and rear pivoted auxiliary levers that pull type-bars guided in radial slots of a type-bar ring.

**How it works:** Primary key levers pivot at the rear and pass through vertically slotted rails, with slots of varying width so they can be grouped and leave passages between the two banks. Connecting bars join them to auxiliary levers pivoted alternately front and rear, guided in comb bars and returned by coiled springs. Each auxiliary lever pulls a type-arm heel by a rod, and the type arm lies in a radial slot of a frusto-conical type-bar ring that guides it. A spacer bar under the auxiliary levers is depressed by each to trigger the escapement.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** key lever; slotted rail; connecting bar; auxiliary lever; comb bar; spring; pull rod; type arm; type-bar ring; spacer bar

**Numbers:** 2 banks of keys; 2 sheets of drawings
