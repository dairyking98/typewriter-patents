## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to a platen release mechanism whereby the platen may be readily released from the line space wheel and rotated independently nected so that both shall rotate in unison.

**How it works (reading):** Two spring-loaded disks with wedge teeth engage the line-space wheel and the platen shell; a finger thrust releases the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen must be released from the line-space wheel and turned freely, but the connection must not slip when engaged.

**Solution:** Two spring-loaded disks with wedge teeth engage the line-space wheel and the platen shell; a finger thrust releases the platen.
