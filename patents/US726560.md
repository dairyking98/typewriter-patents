## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Two or more complete typewriters coupled for simultaneous operation

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** Intermediate mechanical connections transmit the movement of the operator's machine to the corresponding parts of other Underwood machines, including the carriage return and line-spacing feed and the tabulating devices.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Several typewriters placed at remote points must each reproduce the motions of one operator machine.

**Solution:** Intermediate mechanical connections transmit the movement of the operator's machine to the corresponding parts of other Underwood machines, including the carriage return and line-spacing feed and the tabulating devices.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
- **Uses:** [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** operator machine; intermediate connections; tabulating devices
