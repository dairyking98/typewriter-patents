## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Addresses on boxes and parcels are marked by hand or stencil; a machine for marking flat stationary surfaces is wanted.

**Solution:** A movable type bed is slid to bring a chosen type under a plunger carriage, which is advanced letter by letter by a pawl on a notched bar and pressed down to stamp.

**How it works:** A flat frame with a central slot carries a sliding bar with the index plate and flexible type bed in a dovetail groove. A triangular plunger carriage with an index finger slides on rods over the type indicator bar, and moves the stamp over the type chosen. Pressing the plunger's button against a spring both stamps the type onto the surface and, through a pin and lever, drives a pawl bar whose tooth engages inclined V-notches on a rod to advance the carriage one space. Ink pads ink the types and the type bed rests on supporting springs.
