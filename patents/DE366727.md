## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The crosshead has oblique guide surfaces running from the centre, so a driver pin on the letter-key lever pushes it to one side; a separate key releases the crosshead from the middle (stencil) position and brings the ribbon's black field into writing position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Known ribbon changeover devices using a crankshaft and crosshead fail to work reliably, and the ribbon-field knob is unsafe, especially for the middle (stencil) position.

**Solution:** The crosshead has oblique guide surfaces running from the centre, so a driver pin on the letter-key lever pushes it to one side; a separate key releases the crosshead from the middle (stencil) position and brings the ribbon's black field into writing position.

The previously known facilities of this type had the disadvantage that they often failed because the parts in question did not worked with sufficient security. According to the present invention, this drawback becomes eliminated by the fact that the crosshead, known per se, is provided with guide surfaces, which run obliquely from the center in opposite directions and at Striking a key can be detected by a driver pin. When moving downwards the pen is forced to hit one or the other guide surface following it transferred to the hook-like end of the crosshead that is to be pressed down, so that it causes the swing in the desired direction.
