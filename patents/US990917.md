## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike machine ribbons need automatic and manual direction reversal, multi-colour shifting and a ribbon guide that also serves as eraser guide and line indicator.

**Solution:** A ribbon mechanism of stamped parts, driven from the spacing motion, with automatic reversal on a predetermined amount of winding, manual reverse, multi-colour shift and an adjustable-throw vibrator.

**How it works:** Ribbon spools are driven from the carriage spacing mechanism through bell cranks and holding dogs. Reverse levers swap the driven spool automatically when a set amount of ribbon has wound, or at the operator's will. A multi-colour shift mechanism moves the ribbon so chosen portions come to the print point. A ribbon vibrator carries an upper portion shaped as an erasing guide and line indicator. A light friction brake keeps the ribbon taut.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; vibrator; reverse lever; holding dog; bell crank; friction brake; multi-colour shift mechanism; rocker frame; detaining spring
