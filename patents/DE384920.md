## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (pawl drive gear) · **Subject (patent classes):** Gearings for conveying rotary motion with intermittently-driving members, e.g. with freewheel action between one of the shafts and an oscillating or reciprocating intermediate member, not rotating with either of the shafts

**How it works (reading):** Two pawls are driven by eccentric cranks on a shaft and engage the teeth of a load wheel; the pawl load changes at a set angle away from dead centre, with a cam control lifting the unloaded pawl.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Large gear ratios in tight space are hard to get from pawl drives, and the load change at the pawls occurs in the dead position.

**Solution:** Two pawls are driven by eccentric cranks on a shaft and engage the teeth of a load wheel; the pawl load changes at a set angle away from dead centre, with a cam control lifting the unloaded pawl.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)

**Parts:** reel wheel; crank shaft; pawls; load wheel; cam taps; pin
