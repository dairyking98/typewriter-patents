## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A typewriter must sit lower than a desk, and typically needs a space that moves away when a desk is used.

**Solution:** The table top is split into two geared parts that move in opposite directions, so the rear part drops to make a typewriter level and rises for desk use.
