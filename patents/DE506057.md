## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**How it works (reading):** The rail's chamfered ends are clamped into U-shaped holders by a screw, so it can be pulled out quickly and set at the same position on both arms.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Replacing the paper guide rail requires removing rivets or screws that are too weak to hold it.

**Solution:** The rail's chamfered ends are clamped into U-shaped holders by a screw, so it can be pulled out quickly and set at the same position on both arms.
