## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ribbon should shift fore-and-aft when the carriage is returned, and move diagonally while a line is typed, using simple mechanism.

**Solution:** A spring pulls the ribbon spool frames forward during typing while a chain from the carriage spring pulley pulls them back each time the carriage is returned.

**How it works:** Ribbon spools sit in sheet-metal frames carried on a rocking shaft with arms. A retractile spring on the shaft arm tends to advance the frames toward the front. Pawls on the front of the frame engage ratchet wheels on the spools to turn them slightly. Together, the slow forward advance and sideways ribbon feed give a diagonal path past the print point. A chain from one frame runs around a guide pulley to the hub of the carriage spring pulley, so when the operator returns the carriage the chain winds up and pulls the frames to the rear, ready for the spring to draw them forward again.
