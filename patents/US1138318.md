## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In the patent’s own words:** The aim of the present invention is to supply the demand for a portable machine.

**How it works (reading):** Removable carriage that disconnects from the escapement and carriage spring on removal and reconnects on insertion, with interchangeable platen widths.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable machines are not compact enough and carriages cannot be interchanged for different paper widths.

**Solution:** Removable carriage that disconnects from the escapement and carriage spring on removal and reconnects on insertion, with interchangeable platen widths.
