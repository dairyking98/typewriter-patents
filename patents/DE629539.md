## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device to identify certain positions of the paper cart of writing and the like Machines using a pointer and a graduated one, for the reception of identifiers serving rail.

**How it works (reading):** Flat resilient disks with narrow slots are clipped into slits in the paper-holding rail, so narrow colour-coded marker groups can be placed at any column and a fixed pointer reads the carriage position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Round peg marks on a wide rail hide the typeface, and markers wider than one step cannot identify single columns.

**Solution:** Flat resilient disks with narrow slots are clipped into slits in the paper-holding rail, so narrow colour-coded marker groups can be placed at any column and a fixed pointer reads the carriage position.
