## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to mechanism for rotating the platen a certain distance to properly space the lines.

**How it works (reading):** A double ratchet wheel on the platen shaft, with crown teeth and curved-depression peripheral teeth, is driven by an adjustable pawl throw for variable line spacing; the same hand lever returns the carriage, and a stop-pawl and brake hold the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing must be adjustable to different distances, the line-space lever should also return the carriage, and the platen needs a stop and brake.

**Solution:** A double ratchet wheel on the platen shaft, with crown teeth and curved-depression peripheral teeth, is driven by an adjustable pawl throw for variable line spacing; the same hand lever returns the carriage, and a stop-pawl and brake hold the platen.
