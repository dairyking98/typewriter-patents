## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide for typewriters with the main arch supported on guide rods Paper pressure rollers.

**How it works (reading):** Guides are placed above all pressure rollers rather than over only some of them, so the sheet is protected in every position; a telescoping split guide plate is used.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon paper inserted to the collecting sheet is caught under the upper pressure rollers when lines are switched, causing wrinkles.

**Solution:** Guides are placed above all pressure rollers rather than over only some of them, so the sheet is protected in every position; a telescoping split guide plate is used.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** paper pressure rollers; guide plate; platen; collecting sheet
