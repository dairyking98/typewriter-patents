## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typograph (flat platen) needs improvements to platen-feed, ribbon-feed and platen lateral play.

**Solution:** A flat-platen typograph with improved platen-feed lever, ribbon-feed rolls with withdrawable pawls, paper clamp, bell and lateral-play limits.

**How it works:** The platen is carried on a platen-carriage on guideways and moved lengthwise by a feed mechanism like the Cash patent. A platen feed lever, paper clamp lever, a bell and hammer, and ribbon-feed rolls with pawls that can be withdrawn are described. Type-bars and key-levers sit on a top plate over posts.

**Features:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** base; platen-carriage; flat platen; feed lever; paper clamp; bell; hammer; ribbon-feed roll; pawl; type-bar; key-lever

**Improves on:**

- [US372516](../patents/US372516.md): modifies platen feed, ribbon feed and lateral play of the Cash typograph
