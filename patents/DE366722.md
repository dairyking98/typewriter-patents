## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the paper cart drive of typewriters and in particular on the coupling of the ratchet wheel with the paper car drive in such devices.

**How it works (reading):** Coupling pawls on a bearing disc engage the ratchet wheel only when the carriage moves forward under tension, and a brake spring between disc and ratchet damps the disc so the pawls cannot jump during writing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage-return noise and wear from the pawl and ratchet, and uneven line starts or line spacing when the pawl jumps out of the teeth during writing.

**Solution:** Coupling pawls on a bearing disc engage the ratchet wheel only when the carriage moves forward under tension, and a brake spring between disc and ratchet damps the disc so the pawls cannot jump during writing.
