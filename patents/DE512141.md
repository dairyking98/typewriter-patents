## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** With the known shift-frame lock, releasing the lock is not enough to remove the paper carriage, because a tab of the platen support frame is still held under the support rail of the shift frame.

**Solution:** The carriage release lever also trips the shift-frame locking lever, so folding up the carriage frees the shift frame and lets the carriage come off.

**How it works:** Pressing the shift key swings the locking lever via a pin sliding on a curved arm, releasing the lock of the shift frame that lifts the platen. In the invention the carriage release lever (the one used to remove the paper carriage) is also joined by a pull rod to a two-armed lever. Its second arm carries a pin that swings the locking lever out when the release lever is pressed. The shift frame is then free and pivots as the carriage is folded up about its rear rail, until the rollers and supports of the carriage disengage and it can be lifted off.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; platen; shift frame; locking lever; shift key; release lever; pull rod; two-armed lever; rear rail

**Improves on:**

- known shift-frame locks with removable carriage: release lever now also unlocks the shift frame so the carriage can be removed
