## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dust and erasure debris fall into the slots and pivot seats of the type-bar segment, and the type-bar impact limit needs to be adjustable.

**Solution:** Each type bar pivots on a wire seated in a slot cut into the segment periphery, with the slot covered by a solid part of the segment and a type-bar abutment; a separate segmental abutment is adjusted by screw bolts and stop bolts to set the type-face penetration into the paper.
