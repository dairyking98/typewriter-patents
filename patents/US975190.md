## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper feeding and guiding devices on a front-strike typebar machine needed to be efficient and easy to assemble, dismantle and adjust.

**Solution:** A platen frame carries spring-loaded main feed rollers, a sprung paper apron, a tilting paper table and adjustable margin feed rollers with paper fingers that double as the forward feed rollers.

**How it works:** The platen frame hangs on parallel links from a ball-bearing truck and is lifted for case shift by a rail. A paper table is hinged on brackets with coil springs so it can be tipped forward for access, and a curved paper apron is held against the platen by pivoted sprung arms with individual tension screws. Main feed rollers on a shaft sit in pivoted hangers loaded by adjustable springs, and one release lever turns a release bar that lifts all hangers away from the platen. Margin feed rollers with paper fingers slide along an angular bar above the platen, each with its own spring and screw; the bar can be turned by pressing locking bolts, relieving or removing the rollers. The margin rollers press at the printing line, so the usual forward feed rollers are dropped.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** platen frame; truck; ball raceway; parallel link; platen; paper table; paper apron; main feed roller; roller hanger; tension spring; adjusting screw; release lever; release bar; margin feed roller; paper finger; supporting bar; locking bolt; center guide

**Improves on:**

- Front-strike machines with a separate forward set of feed rollers: margin feed rollers at the printing line serve as forward rollers, so the forward set is dropped
