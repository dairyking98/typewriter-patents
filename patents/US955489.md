## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to line lock and margin stop mechanism and the main object of said invention is to provide simple and efficient mechanism of the character specified which may be readily actuated to effect.

**How it works (reading):** Line lock and margin stop mechanism with an actuator that releases the line lock or lets the carriage pass the margin stop, plus an alarm mechanism for the line lock.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line lock and margin stop can only be released with difficulty, and the carriage cannot easily be passed beyond the margin stop position on its return.

**Solution:** Line lock and margin stop mechanism with an actuator that releases the line lock or lets the carriage pass the margin stop, plus an alarm mechanism for the line lock.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
