## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the ribbon mechanism of type-Writers.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon spools slip onto conical hubs so they can be removed and replaced quickly; a spring-loaded pulley with a ratchet pawl winds the ribbon, and a slotted guide-arm steers the unwinding ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the ribbon spool to use another ribbon is awkward.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools slip onto conical hubs so they can be removed and replaced quickly; a spring-loaded pulley with a ratchet pawl winds the ribbon, and a slotted guide-arm steers the unwinding ribbon.
