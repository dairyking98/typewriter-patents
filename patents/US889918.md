## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon-paper copying requires sheets to be assembled and the carbon handled by hand, which soils hands and sheets.

**Solution:** A copying cloth impregnated with copying material is applied to the platen, so copies can be made without carbon paper. The source text is truncated so details are unclear.
