## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the present invention is located on the right side of the typewriter carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Auxiliary paper-roller switching: a ratchet pawl on the platen axis is decoupled on its backstroke and returned by a second stop, with stops limiting total platen travel between one and two revolutions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper roller's auxiliary switching pawl does not give a limited total forward movement of the platen, and the pawl must be returned automatically.

**Solution:** Auxiliary paper-roller switching: a ratchet pawl on the platen axis is decoupled on its backstroke and returned by a second stop, with stops limiting total platen travel between one and two revolutions.
