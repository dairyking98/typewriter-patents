## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to improvements 1n ribbon mechanism for typewriters and particularly to a means for automatically reversing the movement of the ribbon when it has been sufficiently unwound from either of the spools.

**How it works (reading):** An arm in each spool falls out when the ribbon is unwound, trips a shift bar, and swaps the feed pawls between the two ratchets.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reversing ribbon travel when a spool empties needs to be automatic.

**Solution:** An arm in each spool falls out when the ribbon is unwound, trips a shift bar, and swaps the feed pawls between the two ratchets.

**How it works:** Each spool has a ratchet and a feed pawl on a lever driven by a universal-bar rock shaft. A slot in each spool holds a pivoted arm kept flat by the ribbon coil. When the ribbon is nearly gone the arm drops out and strikes a lever on a rock shaft, linked through a lever to a shift bar that moves both pawls so the other spool is fed.
