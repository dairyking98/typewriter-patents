## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Usable paper ink ribbons for typewriters had not been achieved; known hemp-paper ribbons do not meet requirements.

**Solution:** An ink ribbon of wood-free paper made from mulberry bast fibre or mixed with other wood-free fibres, about half the thickness of textile ribbons and roughly 80 percent as durable, soaked with ink on both sides or on one side only.

**How it works:** Paper ribbon is inked by soaking so that it carries colour on one or both faces; its thinness gives sharper, more even impressions.
