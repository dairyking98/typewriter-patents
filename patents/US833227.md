## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A machine needs two colours of ribbon selectable at will.

**Solution:** Two ribbons, one in front of the other, each with its own vibrator lever, and a shiftable actuator picks which one is raised at the type-stroke.

**How it works:** The usual ribbon runs on spools at each side; behind it a short endless red ribbon runs round four spools. Each ribbon has its own carrier moved by a lever, and actuators on the reciprocating universal-bar frame are shifted to engage either lever. The idle ribbon stays down.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; second ribbon; spool; carrier; lever; actuator; universal bar frame

**Improves on:**

- Underwood front-strike machine: adds an independent second ribbon
