## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter was needed to print downward onto the leaf of a bound book to enter records, with type-actuating improvements usable alone.

**Solution:** A circular array of downward-printing type-bars on an annular fulcrum-plate, carried by a frame and carriage that move laterally and longitudinally over a book leaf held on a platen.

**How it works:** Type-bars hinge at their lower ends on adjustable slotted hangers on an annular fulcrum-plate, their free ends held up by a spring. Key-rods pivoted to the bar tops pass through an annular guide-plate, and pressing a key drives the bar down onto a common printing seat. Each bar is made from folded sheet metal. A frame and carriage support the typing mechanism over a bed with a book-lifter and platens that clamp the leaf, and move it over the leaf. There are ribbon spools with a ribbon twister, line-spacing, and an alarm.
