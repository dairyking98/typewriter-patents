## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locks for subsidiary keys, e.g. for shift keys

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a more simple and effective means of making a rapid change from the upper to the lower case letters by the shifting platen of typewriting machines, than by those in vogue and previous'to my invention, in so far as I am at present aware.

**How it works (reading):** A shift lever with a locking lever carrying a lock key and a shift key at equal distances from its pivot, so pressing either alternates locking and release.

## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier shift mechanisms either needed a single lever locked in place separately, or two keys whose action was complicated; a simpler and faster way to change between upper and lower case is wanted.

**Solution:** A shift lever with a locking lever carrying a lock key and a shift key at equal distances from its pivot, so pressing either alternates locking and release.

**How it works:** A shift lever of the first order is pivoted on a stud; depressing one end moves the other end, which swings the platen carriage (or a swinging platen) to upper case, in one version through a second bell-crank lever. A locking lever pivoted at the operating end carries two buttons, the lock key and the shift key, set at about equal distances from its pivot so they act alternately. Depressing the lock key engages the lock to hold the platen shifted; the shift key releases it so the lever returns to normal. Adjusting provisions for the locking position are shown for the two machine types.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** shift lever; locking lever; lock key; shift key; bell-crank; carriage; platen; pivot stud

**Improves on:**

- single-lever locked shift and two-key lock/unlock shifts: simpler alternating lock and shift keys on one lever
