## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Erasing or correcting tables

**How it works (reading):** A curved base plate with an eraser slot has slightly bent side walls that give the fingers grip, guard the written line with finger rests, and channel erasing dust into a collector.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Eraser plates do not hold on the platen during erasing, so paper tears and smears and erasing dust falls into the machine.

**Solution:** A curved base plate with an eraser slot has slightly bent side walls that give the fingers grip, guard the written line with finger rests, and channel erasing dust into a collector.
