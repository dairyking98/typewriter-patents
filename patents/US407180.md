## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Wants a simpler, cheaper typewriter with more positive and reliable operation.

**Solution:** A spring-wound cog on a toothed square bar drives the carriage, a curved spring arm acts as the escapement, and pressing the carriage down drives a striking pin through a type support carrying rubber type.

**How it works:** A sliding carriage on a rotatable square bar with teeth on its rear and lower faces. A hollow cog wheel on the carriage meshes the rear teeth and holds a coil spring that pushes the carriage to the right. A curved spring arm engages the lower teeth and holds it back. Pressing the front of the carriage tilts a connecting lever that lowers a striking pin onto a curved type support with rubber type and perforations over each type, and at the same time depresses the spring arm, releasing the carriage. When the finger is raised the arm re-engages the next tooth and the carriage moves one space. A set screw adjusts the lateral swing of the arm.
