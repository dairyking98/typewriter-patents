## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (addressing method carbon copy smearing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for perforating or stencil cutting using special types or dies

**How it works (reading):** The typewriter width is split into a middle part and two side parts; inserts or enlarged rollers at the sides take the roller pressure so the carbon pack in the middle receives little or none, and a wider tissue sheet keeps the pack from slipping.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-copy carbon packs smear when the pressure rollers press hard on them and the hectographic carbon wears out after two or three strikes.

**Solution:** The typewriter width is split into a middle part and two side parts; inserts or enlarged rollers at the sides take the roller pressure so the carbon pack in the middle receives little or none, and a wider tissue sheet keeps the pack from slipping.
