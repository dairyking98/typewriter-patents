## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Main patent's lower-margin lock blocked only the carriage return, still allowed typing, and its stop needed hand-resetting for each line spacing.

**Solution:** A slide moved by the line-shift lever carries an inclined-face body that pushes stops on a bar to actuate a key lock; a cam on the push rod scales the slide travel to the chosen line spacing.

**How it works:** The line-shift lever pushes a toothed push rod through a cam disk whose radius corresponds to one, two or three teeth, set by a line-spacing knob. A pawl carries a slide forward along the rod. When the inclined body meets one of the adjustable stops on a stop bar it moves the bar and thereby locks the writing keys via a key-lock rail. A pointer shows the slide position on a scale.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** line shift lever; push rod; cam disk; slide; pawl; inclined body; stop bar; key lock rail; spring

**Improves on:**

- main patent (bottom-of-sheet writing limit): locks keys instead of carriage return; stop travel adapts to line spacing
