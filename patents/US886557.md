## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making double or multiple spaces needs the space bar pressed after every letter.

**Solution:** A letter key advances a ratchet through an auxiliary flatten-spacing gear, moving the carriage without the space bar, and a lever engages or disengages it.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
