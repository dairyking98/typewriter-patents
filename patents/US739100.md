## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reverse should work automatically at the end of ribbon travel with a simple, easily applied device.

**Solution:** The ribbon-spool holder carries a pin that, when the spool is exhausted, raises a switch into the carriage path, which then throws a reversing lever between two ratchet feeds.

**How it works:** A key-operated rock-shaft has a crank and feed pawl at each end, each engaging a ratchet wheel. A central reversing lever carries a pawl and retaining pawl at each end, so only one ratchet is driven at a time. Operating rods on the lever are lifted by switches pivoted at the sides. A beveled pin on the ribbon-spool holder raises the near switch when the spool reaches its end; the carriage then strikes the switch and swings it, raising the rod and reversing the lever.
