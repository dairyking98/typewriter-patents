## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the pressure-roll mechanism which coiiperates with the platens of typewriting machines to feed the paper, and particularly to means for casting the rolls off from the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Pressure roll arms turn on a rock shaft, with hubs in elliptical bores and cams that lower the hubs and rolls when the shaft is rocked, withdrawing all the rolls from the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A compact, low-cost mechanism is needed to cast the pressure rolls off the platen.

**Solution:** Pressure roll arms turn on a rock shaft, with hubs in elliptical bores and cams that lower the hubs and rolls when the shaft is rocked, withdrawing all the rolls from the platen.
