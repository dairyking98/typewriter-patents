**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing mechanisms combined with apparatus providing a visual indication

**How it works (reading):** Electrically controlled indices and glow-lamps show progressive sheet positions, with a paper-feeler moved into and out of the paper path and a slant-faced stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator needs progressive automatic indication of the distance from the printing point to the sheet end, and a signal at a set position.

**Solution:** Electrically controlled indices and glow-lamps show progressive sheet positions, with a paper-feeler moved into and out of the paper path and a slant-faced stop.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** electrically controlled indices; glow-lamps; paper-feeler; slant-faced stop
