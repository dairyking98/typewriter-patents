## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operating the shift key by hand is tiring; the inventor wants foot operation without added springs.

**Solution:** A foot lever on the floor is wired to a clip on the shift key.

**How it works:** A flat floor base carries a hinged foot lever held up against a stop by a spring. A flexible metal clamp clasps the shift key and a wire runs from it to a screw-threaded pin in a bolt through the foot lever; the pin adjusts wire tension. Pressing the pedal pulls the shift key down and the spring raises the lever again.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** base; foot lever; spring; stop; clamp; wire; threaded pin
