## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ten-key adding machine zero suppression) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices for printing the point

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement of this device, which shortens the length of the drive racks.

**How it works (reading):** Intermediate gears and drive wheels with a tooth gap let the sum wheels be driven only after the type wheels have been brought from the blank position to zero, which also shortens the racks.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ten-key adding machines print unwanted leading zeros in front of added items, and the drive racks are too long.

**Solution:** Intermediate gears and drive wheels with a tooth gap let the sum wheels be driven only after the type wheels have been brought from the blank position to zero, which also shortens the racks.
