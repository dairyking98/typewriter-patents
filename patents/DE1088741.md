## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Single-line wire printers print only one line per card scan, while the dot-matrix wire heads could print several lines at once.

**Solution:** Several electromagnetically driven wire print heads are arranged in several rows one below another and selected by sensing punch cards, printing several lines together.

> Description Translated from German GERMAN The invention relates to a printing unit for printing characters. In the known wire printers, several print heads are arranged in a row next to one another, wherein the wires forming the individual drawing elements are closely combined with their printing ends while the control ends of the wires are further apart. The pressure and control ends of the Wires are connected to each other by Bowden cable connections, so that the simultaneous drive all of the printhead wires selected for printing is possible without difficulty. Also known is a printing device for printing characters made up of dots, in which the points are selected by sensing punch cards.
