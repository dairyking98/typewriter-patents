## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Leading edge stops on the paper apron need to be put out of operation.

**Solution:** A rock shaft and stop members hold the leading edge stops clear of the paper when not needed.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
