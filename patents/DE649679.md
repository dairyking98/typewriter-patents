## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to teachings by means of which type bodies of typewriters are aligned and soldered to the type lever.

**How it works (reading):** A gauge whose carrier is clamped to the platen on a steel band, with a sliding guide part adjusted lengthwise, so that replacement types can be soldered to the typebar with alignment marks matched to a well-preserved type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing type alignment gauges are tied to particular machines and are hard to adjust precisely because the whole gauge must move on the carriage.

**Solution:** A gauge whose carrier is clamped to the platen on a steel band, with a sliding guide part adjusted lengthwise, so that replacement types can be soldered to the typebar with alignment marks matched to a well-preserved type.

**How it works:** The guide part slides along the platen while the carrier stays clamped; a reference type sets the guide, and the new type is pressed against it and soldered.
