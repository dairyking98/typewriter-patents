## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (letterpress printing of typewriter letters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inking units

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing process for producing typewriter letters , on letterpress machines.

**How it works (reading):** Rubber clichés for letterhead and signature are mounted in a letterpress form, a blanket is stretched over the typewriter text, and the whole form is inked and printed in one pass on any letterpress machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Combining letterpress printing with typewriter-style text, or adding a letterhead, by ribbon or blanket methods is awkward and gives a detectable blanket texture.

**Solution:** Rubber clichés for letterhead and signature are mounted in a letterpress form, a blanket is stretched over the typewriter text, and the whole form is inked and printed in one pass on any letterpress machine.

**Features:** [Rubber type](../features/elem-rubber-type.md), [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)
