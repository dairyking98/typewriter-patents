## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting the platen to shift case needs both a temporary shift for single capitals and a locked shift for longer runs, reachable on either side of the keyboard.

**Solution:** Add a short key lever hinged on the locking shift key so it lifts the platen without engaging the lock, alongside a left-hand temporary key that also releases the lock.

**How it works:** The platen frame is raised by a shift frame whose arms are engaged by key levers on each side. The left key lifts the platen only while pressed. The right key lever lifts and is held by a locking hook on a sprung axle that catches a pin; pressing the left key pushes a pin against an arm on that axle and frees the hook. A short lever hinged on the right key lever lifts the platen but keeps the hook inactive, so no locking occurs.
