## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboards placed in front of the printing point, or above the type bars, block the operator's view of the writing, and a low keyboard in a crescent of rearward-striking bars leaves the printing point too far from the keys.

**Solution:** The keyboard is placed as close to the writing plane as the bar operating connections allow, and the type bars form a forwardly opening crescent above the rear ends of the key levers, leaving an open view of the printing line.
