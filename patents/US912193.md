## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The platen frame is pivoted at its lower end to crank arms on a rock shaft in the carriage and at its upper end to links, forming parallel links so the platen frame shifts up and down to change the printing position.
