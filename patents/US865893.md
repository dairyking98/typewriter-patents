## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Springs that act directly on the type bar strain its pivot and cause wear, and the finger pieces need a light, cushioned touch with fast return of the type bars.

**Solution:** Two springs of different strength applied to a link that operates each type bar, so that the heavier spring acts only during the latter part of the type bar's travel to the printing point, relieving the pivot.
