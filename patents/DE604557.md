## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register direct total printing)

**How it works (reading):** A partial movement of the zero-setting element prints a subtotal without zeroing the adding wheels, and the element then locks until printing releases it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash-register zeroing must be forced after total print, which blocks subtotal printing.

**Solution:** A partial movement of the zero-setting element prints a subtotal without zeroing the adding wheels, and the element then locks until printing releases it.
