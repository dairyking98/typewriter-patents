**How it works (reading):** A button lever linked elastically to the switch body repeatedly triggers the switching pawl, so the carriage steps along automatically while the lever is held and can be attached without special tools.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting the paper carriage to a chosen writing position within the line needs a tabulator or a freewheel release that is either complicated or only suits some movements.

**Solution:** A button lever linked elastically to the switch body repeatedly triggers the switching pawl, so the carriage steps along automatically while the lever is held and can be attached without special tools.
