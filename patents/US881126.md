## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand feeding sheets into a typewriter is slow, and the head-line position is hard to set by hand.

**Solution:** A magazine on the platen-side frame feeds a blank sheet to the platen by friction roller and removes the written sheet to the same magazine. An electric circuit closer arrests the feed when the sheet reaches the head-line position.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** paper magazine; friction roller; sheet separator; circuit closer; circuit diagram
