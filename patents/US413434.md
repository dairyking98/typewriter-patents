## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punching machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**How it works (reading):** A single lever selects and actuates a punch, and a feed mechanism with an intermediate slide-bar moves the check.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check-punching needs accurate registration of punch and die, and a light, simple machine.

**Solution:** A single lever selects and actuates a punch, and a feed mechanism with an intermediate slide-bar moves the check.
