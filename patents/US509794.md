## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventor wants an improved letter-spacing, carriage traverse, release and end-of-line stop mechanism for a typebar machine.

**Solution:** A carriage pawl with two unequal teeth engages a double-row rack bar that is rocked by the spacer bar, with a spring drum wound by a rack and gear on the carriage and an adjustable tappet stopping the carriage at line end.

**How it works:** Each typed letter depresses the spacer bar, which pulls crank arms and rocks the rack bar on its end trunnions. The bifurcated spring pawl, fixed under the carriage, has teeth of unequal length so one tooth escapes and the other catches, letting the carriage advance one space. A releasing rod across the carriage lifts both pawl teeth clear of the rack for free traverse. A rack on the carriage drives a gear on the spring post so the mainspring is wound or unwound by carriage travel, and its tension can be varied. An adjustable tappet on the carriage meets a yielding stop on the top plate at the end of the line.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)
