## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Gear connection between spring carriage and escapement gives backlash and lost motion, so spacing is inaccurate.

**Solution:** Carriage-feed mechanism that removes gear interposition, so the escapement drives the carriage directly with accurate spacing and alignment.

**How it works:** Carriage-feed mechanism that removes gear interposition, so the escapement drives the carriage directly with accurate spacing and alignment.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
