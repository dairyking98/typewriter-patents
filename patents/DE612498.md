## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**How it works (reading):** A lever linkage switches off the safety springs during the first part of the erase stroke, so only the stops in the erase rail are released when all are cleared.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasing or setting one column stop unsecures all the other column stops, which can twist out of position.

**Solution:** A lever linkage switches off the safety springs during the first part of the erase stroke, so only the stops in the erase rail are released when all are cleared.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** extinguishing lever; extinguishing rail; safety springs
