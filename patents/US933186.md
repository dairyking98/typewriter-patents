## Reading (opening only, OCR [])

**Problem:** Visible writing machines need ribbons that vibrate to the print point, feed, reverse and change colour.

**Solution:** Two ribbons in vibrating guides adjusted by a ribbon shift key to bring one or the other (or neither) into position.

**How it works:** Ribbons with different inks pass one in front of the other through vibrating guides that move together toward and from the printing point. A ribbon shift key, on successive like motions, shifts to place one then the other in use; either can be held stationary at the print point, or both held out for stencil cutting. A single ribbon with separate coloured fields can be used the same way, with retainers for the pair.
