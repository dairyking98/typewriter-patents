## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing ribbon mechanisms need to give multi-colour writing, easy ribbon reversal and equal ribbon tension.

**Solution:** Two ribbons (or a two-field single ribbon) pass through shiftable vibrating guides, with differential drive and carriage-power reversing.

**How it works:** Two ribbons carrying different inks pass the printing point one in front of the other through vibrating guides that move them toward and from the platen. A ribbon shift key, by successive presses, selects which ribbon is in position; either can be held in print position without vibrating, or both held clear for stencil cutting. Each ribbon has its own spool pair with common drive and reversing mechanism, using differential gearing to equalize tension, and reversing is triggered automatically using carriage driving power.
