## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing forms need several separate manual steps, including reversing the platen, inserting the form and ejecting it.

**Solution:** Carriage return drives a gearbox that performs the reverse and forward platen shift; a rod linked to the control lever also drives paper insertion and ejection in one movement.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** gearbox; control rod; paper insertion device; ejector lever; form table
