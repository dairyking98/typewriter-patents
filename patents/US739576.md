## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to printing-point-indicating mechanism for type writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** An automatically operated printing-point indicator on the platen carriage and frame, shown in normal and exposed positions; the key-levers connect through a universal bar and links, and the mechanism is detailed beyond the first 3000 characters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says the operator needs a simple, automatically operated way to see the printing point when the platen is turned to expose the writing.

**Solution:** An automatically operated printing-point indicator on the platen carriage and frame, shown in normal and exposed positions; the key-levers connect through a universal bar and links, and the mechanism is detailed beyond the first 3000 characters.
