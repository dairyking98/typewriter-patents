## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Describes a feeding device that moves the carriage one space per key press on a Caligraph.

**Solution:** A rocking rod with a spring-returned sleeve carries two pins that alternately engage the rack teeth to give a one-tooth escapement.

**How it works:** A rod parallel to the carriage rack is rocked by the key universal bar via an arm. A pin on the rod catches a tooth as the pin on a spring-loaded sleeve is released. The spring draws the sleeve pin back one tooth where it engages the next tooth when the rod rocks back. A thumb lever works a bail that rocks the sleeve to free both pins so the carriage can be moved freely.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** rocking rod; sleeve; coil spring; holding pin; feed pin; rack bar; tripping bail; thumb lever
