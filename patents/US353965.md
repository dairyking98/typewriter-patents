## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes hand-held

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relate to special parts of a type-writing machine of a class well known and before generally described, the machine, except as to the features of the invention, will be only described so far as may be necessary to make the present invention intelligible.
