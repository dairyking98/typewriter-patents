## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple portable typewriter with index selection and even spacing is wanted.

**Solution:** A character disc with slots is indexed by a lever tongue that drops into the slot for the letter, rotating a rubber type band disc, and pressing the lever prints and spaces.

**How it works:** In a housing a fixed disc F carries the characters and slots in its edge. A second disc K, joined to a lever J, turns inside it and carries a vulcanized-rubber type band. The operator swings lever J until its tongue is over the wanted slot, then presses down; the tongue enters the slot to lock position, and the same press acts via a bolt and angle lever to push a lever P through openings in the ring against the type band, printing at the paper. The paper-roller support is pulled continuously by a spring but held by a rack, and a shift pawl lets it pass one tooth on the upward stroke. Blank slots with no type give word spaces, a scale and pointer give even spacing, and inking rollers blacken the types.
