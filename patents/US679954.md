## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Types needed a better way to travel from normal to printing position with a steady centre guide.

**Solution:** Radial type levers carry the type from the normal to the printing position, guided by a central guide on the type-bar device at its end.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type-bar; radial lever; guide; type socket
