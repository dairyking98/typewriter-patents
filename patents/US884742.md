## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electric typewriters with a type wheel or drum have trouble making the contacts for type adjustment, the hammer, carriage control and cutting the current, all independently and mechanically.

**Solution:** Let a group of key levers act through a common rocking shaft on one contact wheel that closes three contacts in turn for type-carrier positioning, carriage control, and return, locking and current cut-off.

**How it works:** Keys are split into left and right groups sharing a type carrier that slides left and right like a weaving shuttle, a striking hammer and the paper-carriage control. Depressing a key turns a common rocking shaft and a bell-crank that presses back one of a row of spring pins, which retains the carrier with the required type directly in front of the hammer. A contact wheel is displaced on key press and release, first closing a contact for the electric type-carrier drive, then one for the paper carriage, then one returning the carrier to its start position, locking it and cutting off the current. The carrier holds three superposed rows of types.

**Features:** [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [ELEM:linear-bar](../features/elem-linear-bar.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** key lever; rocking shaft; bell-crank; spring pin; type carrier; guide frame; striking hammer; contact wheel; paper carriage; rack

**Numbers:** 3 superposed rows of types; 2 key groups
