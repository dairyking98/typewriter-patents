## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet materials for use therein by transferring ink from the master sheet using solvent-soluble dyestuffs on the master sheets, e.g. alcohol-soluble

**How it works (reading):** Copy paper is impregnated with a solution of a complex acid such as tungstophosphoric acid in denatured alcohol with the basic dye, so originals and copies do not fade.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon and carbon dyes fade in light, and copies are less lightfast than the originals.

**Solution:** Copy paper is impregnated with a solution of a complex acid such as tungstophosphoric acid in denatured alcohol with the basic dye, so originals and copies do not fade.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)

**Parts:** copy paper; complex acid solution; dye
