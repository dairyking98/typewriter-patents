## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Checks can be raised after issue because typed amounts can be altered or erased without trace.

**Solution:** Pin-pointed type perforates the printed characters, and the paper is backed by a yielding, non-elastic material such as wax so that the perforations make alteration practically impossible.
