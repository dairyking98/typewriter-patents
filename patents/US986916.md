## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Variable-spacing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A variable line-spacing mechanism attached to the platen end lets the platen be set so the paper aligns to a required line, and it can be fitted to any typewriter without changing the platen or its frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper cannot be repositioned accurately to a required line after correction or removal, so the first line of a resumed letter does not match the spacing of the previous line.

**Solution:** A variable line-spacing mechanism attached to the platen end lets the platen be set so the paper aligns to a required line, and it can be fitted to any typewriter without changing the platen or its frame.
