## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A movable paper gage that leaves the sheet's path, especially one tied to the pressure-roll release, is complicated and demands operator attention.

**Solution:** The paper table is pivoted so that finger pressure on the sheet lowers it and uncovers the gage, which then sets the sheet's leading edge, while the gage stays fixed.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** paper table; paper gage; pressure rolls
