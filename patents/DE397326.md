## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Interchangeable eraser for typewriters. The subject of the invention shows an eraser for typewriters to remove characters in typewriter work easy to remove. The device is on the movable slide or on the fixed frame of the typewriter, as is desired in practice, Easily displaceable on a shaft provided with a groove and a square arranged. The guide shaft rests on both sides in rotatable arms, so that the entire eraser attached to the shaft is pivoted with the former can be adjusted in order to be able to be adjusted at all points of the platen. The eraser consists of a known spring drive housing together with regulating device, so that by means of an appropriately designed intermediate gear the eraser to be used is set in a rotary motion, by means of a The rotary movement can be interrupted at any time. If necessary, the eraser can be removed from the shaft to be used for office hand use.
