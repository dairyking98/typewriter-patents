## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasing a typed character needs an eraser that can be moved to every point of the platen and driven without hand effort.

**Solution:** A spring-drive eraser on a sleeve slides along a grooved shaft parallel to the platen and is pivoted on arms; a gear train rotates it, and a finger lever engages the teeth to stop the rotation.

The guide shaft rests on both sides in rotatable arms, so that the entire eraser attached to the shaft is pivoted with the former can be adjusted in order to be able to be adjusted at all points of the platen. The eraser consists of a known spring drive housing together with regulating device, so that by means of an appropriately designed intermediate gear the eraser to be used is set in a rotary motion, by means of a The rotary movement can be interrupted at any time. If necessary, the eraser can be removed from the shaft to be used for office hand use.
