## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to the type actions of type-writing machines, the object of the invention being to relieve the fingers of the shock due to the impact of the type against the platen and transmitted to the fingers through the connecting-rod and keylevers.

**How it works (reading):** The type action is modified so the impact shock is absorbed before it reaches the connecting-rod and key-levers. The exact absorbing means is shown in the drawings, not the excerpt.
