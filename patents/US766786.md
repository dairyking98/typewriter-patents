## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to type-writing machines of the class having type-bars arranged in basket form;

**How it works (reading):** A brush on a slide mounted on the machine frame, with a pivoted brush back, can be moved in to clean the type of a circular basket-type machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Basket-type type-bars cannot be cleaned safely and easily by hand.

**Solution:** A brush on a slide mounted on the machine frame, with a pivoted brush back, can be moved in to clean the type of a circular basket-type machine.
