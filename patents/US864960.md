## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of the invention is to produce an improved construction of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Type bars pivoted on hangers around a base, each with a type head whose face carries needle points embedded in a composition, which punch the stencil sheet; the carriage can be raised to expose the stencil.
