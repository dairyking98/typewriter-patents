**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed was not positive, and the ribbon stayed in the print position between strikes.

**Solution:** Type-bar motion drives ratchets and pawls that feed the ribbon side to side; a second pawl set stops backward movement; a manual reversing rod is used; pivoted guides move the ribbon to the platen only at impression.

**Features:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
