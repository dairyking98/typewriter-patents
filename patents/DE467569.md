## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon holders on a continuous axle block the paper drive and fold-out guide table of electric multiple recorders.

**Solution:** Ribbon carriers turn on short stub axles outside the paper guide, leaving the centre free for the paper drive and the fold-out table.
