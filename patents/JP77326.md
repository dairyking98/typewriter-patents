## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a Japanese type-magazine typewriter (the machine of Patent No. 27877) that normally prints in vertical columns by turning the paper cylinder, there is no way to print horizontal lines with exact spacing, in either direction, with back-spacing.

**Solution:** A rack on the paper-cylinder frame is driven by a cross-feed gear that can be shifted to mesh with either a drive gear or, via an intermediate gear, its reverse, with a pair of pawls and an adjustable limit lever on the intermediate gear fixing the feed step.

**How it works:** Pressing the printing key lever presses a cross-feed lever through a sliding bar; on its return a spring-driven pawl lever pulls a ratchet and turns a drive gear one step. The cross-feed gear, normally sprung up into mesh with the drive gear, feeds the cylinder frame left; pressed down by a handle it meshes the intermediate gear and the same one-way drive moves the frame right. On the intermediate gear a rising pawl and a swinging pawl hand off in turn, so the gear is stopped after an angle set by an adjustable limit lever and its two-faced stop piece, making the character space constant and adjustable. Further handles give one-character back-feed, release the whole cross-feed plate from the rack for free sliding, and a sliding bar on the key lever switches the key between cross feed and the ordinary column feed of the cylinder.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Horizontal feed device for a "typewriter". Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a horizontal feed device for a typewriter, in which a rack is formed on the paper-cylinder frame and a cross-feed gear is meshed with it; an intermediate gear is meshed with a drive gear that is turned by the action of the printing key lever or a cross-feed lever; the cross-feed gear is raised or lowered so as to mesh at will with either the drive gear or the intermediate gear, so that turning the drive gear always in one direction can feed the cylinder frame either to the right or to the left; and, on the intermediate gear, a spacing-control mechanism is provided, consisting of a rising-and-falling pawl lever, a swinging pawl lever pivoted on it and pushed by a spring in the direction away from it, and an adjustable limit lever that limits the swing of the swinging pawl lever. Its object is to allow feeding sideways in either direction at will while keeping an exact character spacing, and to make back-spacing easy as well. Brief description of the drawings.
