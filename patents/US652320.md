**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**How it works (reading):** A removable knife with arched end portions that hook over latch pins on the frame, so the knife is brought into position by longitudinal movement, and paper is fed beneath the cutter guide.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The paper-cutting knife on this paper carrier needs an improved way of being mounted to the frame, with the platen and cutter working together.

**Solution:** A removable knife with arched end portions that hook over latch pins on the frame, so the knife is brought into position by longitudinal movement, and paper is fed beneath the cutter guide.
