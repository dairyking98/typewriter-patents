## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Speed tests and typing demonstrations cannot be seen by an audience.

**Solution:** A lamp, condenser lenses and mirrors project the typewriter's work onto a distant screen.

**How it works:** Light source and lens-mirror train project the typed output and type action onto a screen.
