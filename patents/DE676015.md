## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**How it works (reading):** Several typewriters run from one perforated-tape transmission point through relays: address templates are fed to each machine and a common text template is then fed to all of them together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Schema letters with individual addresses are hard to produce automatically on several typewriters at once, since existing tape control handles only one machine.

**Solution:** Several typewriters run from one perforated-tape transmission point through relays: address templates are fed to each machine and a common text template is then fed to all of them together.
