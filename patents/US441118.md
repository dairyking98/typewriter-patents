## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix machine alignment lever) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My improvements relate to the class of typographic machines in which a type-carrying alignment-lever is employed to move the type to the printing-point and to set in motion the feeding and printing mechanisms.

**How it works (reading):** A matrix machine in which movement of the type-carrying alignment lever itself releases the feed and impression devices, without electrical devices.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier matrix machines were electrically operated; the aim here is to obtain the same results mechanically.

**Solution:** A matrix machine in which movement of the type-carrying alignment lever itself releases the feed and impression devices, without electrical devices.

**How it works:** Operating the alignment lever sets the escapement and impression devices in motion by mechanical releases; the die carrier is swung and slid by the lever to bring a die to the print point.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
