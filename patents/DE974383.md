## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The aim of the invention is to create a system by means of an improved design and arrangement of the individual parts which can be operated easily and which operates at a relatively high speed.

**How it works (reading):** A control device for timing is driven by the stepping mechanism of the sensing device, so each sensing step enables the typewriter processes in order and a delay holds the next step until a slow operation finishes. This is an IBM control system for an attached typewriter, not a typewriter mechanism itself.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic document production from recording media needs the typewriter processes timed and sequenced reliably at each switching step of the sensing device.

**Solution:** A control device for timing is driven by the stepping mechanism of the sensing device, so each sensing step enables the typewriter processes in order and a delay holds the next step until a slow operation finishes. This is an IBM control system for an attached typewriter, not a typewriter mechanism itself.
