## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen must be firmly supported and locked in its working position but easily released and swung up so the writing can be read.

**Solution:** A platen carrier on a pivoting shaft is locked by detent levers on the carriage and released and lifted in one movement.

**How it works:** The platen is pivoted by trunnions in two L-shaped carrier arms on a shaft journaled in the carriage side rails, so it can swing upward and backward in an arc to a reading position. Two levers pivoted to the side rails, tied by an operating bar along the front rail, carry detent catches that engage the carrier arms in printing position. Curved bearing faces on the arms act against the locking member. A spring on the carriage holds the lock closed. Pulling the operating bar releases the arms and the platen is shifted to bring the printing into view.
