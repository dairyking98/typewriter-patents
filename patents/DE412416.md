## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter combined with calculating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** The sheet carriage guideway extends rearward above the selector mechanisms, and the platen folds up to a height where the paper web runs parallel to the guideway; the platen gear disengages from the drive wheel when folded.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter working with an adding unit cannot take the sheet carriage used for bookkeeping, because the selector mechanisms sit higher than the platen.

**Solution:** The sheet carriage guideway extends rearward above the selector mechanisms, and the platen folds up to a height where the paper web runs parallel to the guideway; the platen gear disengages from the drive wheel when folded.
