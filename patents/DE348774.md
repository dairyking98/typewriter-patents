## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-space ratchets with a spring pawl sliding over the teeth need considerable force, wear, and click noisily.

**Solution:** Lift the detent roller out of the teeth during line switching and brake the ratchet wheel, then lower the roller back into a gap afterwards.

**How it works:** Moving the line lever turns a two-armed lever whose pawl engages the ratchet and rotates the platen. The same movement lifts the locking roller from the teeth and presses a brake shoe on the ratchet hub to stop overrun. An auxiliary roller on the pawl drops into a tooth gap to stop the wheel at the exact line position. On release a spring returns everything and the locking roller lowers into a gap.
