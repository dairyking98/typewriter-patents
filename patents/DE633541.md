## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a type love drive for writing, writing arithmetic and similar machines, in which the pressing into a ready position Type brought in front of the platen to the roller through a kinematic one Chain between key and type lever switched on energy source takes place in order to achieve a type imprint that is as quiet as possible.

**How it works (reading):** A swing weight drives a fixed-pivot two-part intermediate lever; one part stops at the rest position while the other is pushed by an eccentric or crank gear to press the type lever against the platen perpendicularly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-lever drives on writing machines are noisy because toggle levers and swing weights clatter, and the impression varies between types.

**Solution:** A swing weight drives a fixed-pivot two-part intermediate lever; one part stops at the rest position while the other is pushed by an eccentric or crank gear to press the type lever against the platen perpendicularly.
