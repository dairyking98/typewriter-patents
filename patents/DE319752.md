## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to typewriters with slot guide, so the lever is guided in slots of a cast segment are, and maintaining the important interchangeability of the individual type lever will.

**How it works (reading):** The radius of the type-lever swing axis is set to about two thirds of the radius of the arc on which the type heads strike, which allows a shorter, rigid lever and a good mounting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers that swing through 90 or 180 degrees on a circular arc are either too long and flutter, or too short to mount firmly.

**Solution:** The radius of the type-lever swing axis is set to about two thirds of the radius of the arc on which the type heads strike, which allows a shorter, rigid lever and a good mounting.
