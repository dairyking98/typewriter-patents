So in their rest position they form an angle of go ° or from 180 ° with its stop position on the platen. To be able to go °, the type levers must all have a certain length obtained due to the construction of the machine and the normal width of the types is. As a result, however, the length of the type lever is such that a flutter soon and blows of the type lever occur, which is an unclear and blurred font have as a consequence.
