## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter key heads need to be fastened to the stem without turning and with a simple key-lever connection.

**Solution:** A split tubular stem grips both the button-head shank and a stamped stirrup holding the key lever.

**How it works:** A split tube is sprung open at either end. The top takes the shank of the finger-piece, the bottom takes the semi-cylindrical legs of a stirrup formed from a slotted flat metal blank doubled over. The key-lever passes through the slots in the stirrup. The spring grip keeps the head from turning and allows easy disassembly.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** button head; shank; split tubular stem; stirrup; key-lever; connecting rod; bridge piece; bed-plate
