## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding machine total printing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a simple and reliable circuit for printing totals and partial totals.

**How it works (reading):** A key switches the machine to partial-sum printing so the rollers keep their value for carryover, while a special circuit prints the total and then returns to zero.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Partial totals on adding machines needed a better circuit, since normal printing of a sum reset the number rollers.

**Solution:** A key switches the machine to partial-sum printing so the rollers keep their value for carryover, while a special circuit prints the total and then returns to zero.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
