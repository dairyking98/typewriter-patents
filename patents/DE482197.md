## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-loaded friction clutches between the paper roller and its line ratchet wheel slacken over time and no longer release cleanly.

**Solution:** Rigid coupling members slide axially in the roller and press with a crosspiece against the inside of the ratchet wheel, freely supported on the roller body, and are moved positively by a swinging lever and an axially displaceable coupling rod.
