## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Repeated hammering of the types in the same places indents or corrugates the platen surface, so that impressions become imperfect.

**Solution:** Turn the platen an aliquant part of a revolution at each line space, and move it slightly lengthwise, so the blows fall in new places, using a washer-built platen cover of adjustable density.

**How it works:** The line-space wheel has evenly spaced teeth, but the platen is turned relative to it by a worm on a bracket on the wheel that meshes with a worm wheel on the platen shaft. A scroll turns the worm on its own axis as the line-space wheel rotates, so the platen turns slightly more or less than one tooth. The types therefore do not hit the same circumferential spot at successive revolutions. The platen is also moved slightly lengthwise independently of letter-spacing. The printing surface consists of washers whose density can be regulated.
