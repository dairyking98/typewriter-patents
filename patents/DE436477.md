## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Banks need the cash register's entries also written into a clamped daily account sheet, which the register does not print.

**Solution:** A special printing unit, partly like a typewriter, prints the same amounts into columns of a clamped loose sheet; the operator selects the column with a special key row, and column totals and running balances are printed at day's end.
