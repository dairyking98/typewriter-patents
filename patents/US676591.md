## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing numbers in columns needed the operator to press spacing keys by hand.

**Solution:** A separate tabulating attachment with column stops that automatically space a number into groups of three digits, attachable to existing typewriters with its own keyboard.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** column stops; tabulating attachment; keyboard
