## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machines with upward impression on the platen top need compact bars without lateral play and equal impact for each type.

**Solution:** Typebars with revolving type heads strike the top of the platen, and a tilting frame with cam-slot shift lever moves the platen horizontally for case.

**How it works:** A tilting frame pivoted on the base carries a guide bar and the carriage, which slides in a guide on an upright at the rear. A shifting lever with a compound cam slot engages a pin on the tilting frame so lowering or raising the lever swings the frame back or forward, shifting the platen. A spring returns the lever, and a supplementary lever with an ear restrains or releases it for a lock. Typebars carry revoluble heads so ink pads sit opposite the impact point. The carriage is fed directly by a feed roll rather than a toothed rack.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** tilting frame; carriage; shifting lever; cam slot; supplementary lever; typebar; revoluble type head; ink pad; feed roll; carriage spring
