## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboard machines force a staccato touch because the mechanism acts only while the key is held down and on its release.

**Solution:** A buckling intermediate link between key and key lever returns the key lever to rest while the key is still depressed, so all functions occur on the downstroke and the touch can be legato.

**How it works:** An intermediate member like a piano action jack, in two articulated parts with a spring, transmits the key motion until near the end of the stroke, where an arm hits a stop and buckles it. The key lever then rises while the key stays down, so the type, ribbon and carriage all complete their actions on the downstroke. When the key is released the link springs straight again. The patent also describes a whole machine with a transport ring, carriage rack and pinion letter-width feed, and ribbon gearing.
