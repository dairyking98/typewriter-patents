## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Wide carriages sway, vibrate or skew, causing misalignment of the printing.

**Solution:** Extend the guide-rail past its brackets and add long arms on the rack-bar frame ending in adjustable grooved rollers that bear on the rail for a longer carriage support.

**How it works:** A guide-rail on end standards is extended beyond them; a yoke on the carriage slides on it with arms carrying the back rod, pinned so the carriage cannot rotate about it. A rack-bar frame hinged to the guide-rail has long arms ending in grooved anti-friction rollers that run against the rear of the rail, each mounted on a screw-locked eccentric to set the contact pressure. The wide support base resists twisting at the ends of travel. The platen lifts about the guide-rail.

**Features:** [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** top plate; standard; guide-rail; rod; platen; yoke piece; rack-bar frame; arm; grooved roller; eccentric
