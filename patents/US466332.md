## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage and spacing the platen at line end needed a hand off the keys.

**Solution:** A knee lever pulls a cord over pulleys that return the carriage and turn the line-space lever.

**How it works:** A knee lever under the desk is linked by cord over an oblique pulley to a double pulley with a large and a small groove. The cord from the larger groove to the lever and the smaller to the carriage shifter. For machines like Remington the cord also goes over a third guide pulley at right angles to the carriage travel, so the knee movement first retracts the carriage then spaces the platen.
