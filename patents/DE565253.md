## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The type lever swings then slides straight on a stationary flat axle through two guide slots, with a resilient bumper stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers swing and push diagonally, which blurs the impression.

**Solution:** The type lever swings then slides straight on a stationary flat axle through two guide slots, with a resilient bumper stop.
