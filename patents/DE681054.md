## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon paper spools cannot be arranged in large numbers, and their webs are overstressed when they converge at the feed point.

**Solution:** Spools are set in a row on an inclined plane close to the merge point so the carbon and paper webs run flat and converge with little stress.
