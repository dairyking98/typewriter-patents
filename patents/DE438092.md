## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operating the calculating typewriter needs simpler control of keys that either compute or only write.

**Solution:** Two-part cam pieces are swivelled through small angles, automatically or by hand, so each key either operates the arithmetic unit, locks all keys, or releases them for writing only.
