## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A check mark must be readable by a distant person who has the key, so the marks need a standard numbering code.

**Solution:** A base plate and a circular index plate with numbered sections are hinged together, and a pin punches a guide hole while a key plate correlates the punched positions to the numerals.

**Features:** [SEL:pointer-hole](../features/sel-pointer-hole.md), [SEL:chart-interchangeable](../features/sel-chart-interchangeable.md)
