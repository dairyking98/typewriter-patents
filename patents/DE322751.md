## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Interchangeable key buttons had spring tongues that could overstretch and fail, and were complex.

**Solution:** A one-piece key cap base with two outward-acting spring tongues that snap into a dovetail slot in the key lever, with a designation insert.
