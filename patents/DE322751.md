There are interchangeable key buttons for Typewriters known, some of which have defects, thereby reducing their reliability and expediency is in question. This applies to such key buttons, in which the suspension of the device used to hold on to the staff acts inwards, because when the resilient parts are overstretched, they lose their spring force and become unusable. Also there are these buttons, i. H. the button bottom and feathers, mostly made up of several parts and are therefore complicated. The present invention does not have such a defect, since the The suspension acts outwards and the springs support each other, the spring force so it cannot fail.
