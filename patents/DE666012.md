## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In column printing, each of several identically set type-wheel groups needs its own print hammer and drive, which makes the drive complicated.

**Solution:** The currently selected print hammer is coupled directly to the always-driven hammer or its linkage, so only the chosen type-wheel group prints in the chosen column.
