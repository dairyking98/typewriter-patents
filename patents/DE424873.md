## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** The take-up rollers are paired on one axis; a spiral spring between them is tensioned by key depression and couples the rollers to return the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the paper carriage by a key needs a separate main spring that must be wound up again each time.

**Solution:** The take-up rollers are paired on one axis; a spiral spring between them is tensioned by key depression and couples the rollers to return the carriage.
