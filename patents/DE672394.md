## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check writing calculating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to check-writing calculating machines, accounting machines or the like, by means of which recording sheets or the like and checks can be printed.

**How it works (reading):** Type rods that carry both a special character and a zero are driven by coupling members so that zeros print in the right places and special characters never appear where they are not wanted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Special characters and zeros are printed in the wrong digit positions when a check amount is printed with one printing unit.

**Solution:** Type rods that carry both a special character and a zero are driven by coupling members so that zeros print in the right places and special characters never appear where they are not wanted.
