## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The end of a line is signalled only by a gong struck by separate means.

**Solution:** A ratchet and pawl lever driven by the carriage travel moves a pinion and gear train that releases a hammer to strike a bell at the line end.

**Features:** [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
