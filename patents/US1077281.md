## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the class of typewriting machines and is designed to simpllfy and improve the action, to reduce the number of working parts and the noise of operation, and produce a comparatively simple machine which may be cheaply made.

**How it works (reading):** Key levers with arc-shaped recesses slipped onto a flatted shaft that locks them, plus independent connections for the spacing mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key levers and spacing mechanism need fewer parts and quieter operation for a cheap machine.

**Solution:** Key levers with arc-shaped recesses slipped onto a flatted shaft that locks them, plus independent connections for the spacing mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
