## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type head for embossing machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** The type head is built from a hub with an annular cut and two cast rings that carry the chambers for the embossing stamps, so only minor reworking is needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Embossing type heads are machined from thick disks with chambers milled from outside and flat tyres shrunk on, which uses a lot of material and machining.

**Solution:** The type head is built from a hub with an annular cut and two cast rings that carry the chambers for the embossing stamps, so only minor reworking is needed.
