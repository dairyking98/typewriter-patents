## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage-return rear derailleurs were noisy and unreliable, with loose pinch rollers and locking washers that fell in and out of pockets.

**Solution:** Free pawls in the ratchet wheel act on an external switching disc and rest on a resilient plate, so they engage and disengage by the direction of rotation with a very shallow tooth depth.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** ratchet wheel; rack wheel; pawls; switching disc; resilient spring plate
