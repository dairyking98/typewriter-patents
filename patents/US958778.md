## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to the type actions thereof, and one of the objects of said invention is to provide alight and-efficient type action which is speedy in operation and wherein there is a uniform leverage and a uniform extent of dip or depression of the keys throughout the system.

## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike typebar actions had unequal key leverage and key dip between banks, a hard shock at the end of the stroke, and were awkward to assemble and repair.

**Solution:** A Yost type action with bell cranks that fulcrum first on one pivot then another (changing leverage to accelerate the typebar), jointed typebars that lock rigid at the end of the stroke, compensated key-lever linkage and leaf-spring key levers, all carried on a detachable auxiliary frame.

**How it works:** Key levers of four lengths are pivoted on one rod at their rear through tapering leaf springs. Draw links join each lever, at points chosen by lever length, to a bell crank so that all keys have the same dip and leverage. Each bell crank has a V-shaped double slot over two fixed pivots on an adjustable hanger; it turns on one pivot first and shifts to the second at about half stroke, shortening the lever arm and speeding the typebar. A second link drives a driver pivoted mid-length on the jointed typebar, whose front end hangs on a guide link; abutments on driver and typebar meet near the end so the two move as one stiff piece. The bar strikes the platen from the front, rests on a segmental ink pad, and enters a fixed beveled type guide. The universal bar, set below the levers, is taken up only late in the stroke so the escapement does not retard the type action. All type action parts mount in a casting removable with four screws.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [IMP:impression-adjust](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** key lever; leaf spring; pivot rod; draw link; bell crank; hanger; jointed typebar; driver; guide link; type block; type guide; inking pad; universal bar; dog rocker; feed rack; auxiliary frame

**Numbers:** universal bar clearance about 1/16 inch; four banks of keys; four screws hold the auxiliary frame
