## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets do not start at a uniform distance from the printing point, are not squared on the platen, and their side edges do not give uniform margins.

**Solution:** A mechanical arresting device stops the paper as it is placed on the platen so all leading edges start at a set distance from the printing point, and a side gauge fixes the lateral position of the sheet.

**How it works:** The paper is held by a gauge on the carriage that stops the sheet edge against a set position on the platen, and a side gauge sets the sheet's lateral position.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; carriage; arresting device; side gauge; line-space ratchet-wheel
