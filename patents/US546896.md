## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** characterised by operational sensory functions, e.g. sound feedback

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in keys for type-writing machines, linotype-machines, and like instruments where the keys are struck repeatedly by an expert operator, and it is intended to relieve the wear on the fingers and joints of the operator and to facilitate the operation of the key;

**How it works (reading):** Cushion pad on the key with soft resilient tongues on the striking side that still press the key down.
