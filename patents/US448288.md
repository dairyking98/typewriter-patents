## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters print on single sheets; the inventor wants to print on strips from a roll.

**Solution:** A small paper carriage carrying a roll of paper strips (optionally alternating with transfer paper) mounted on a Remington's spacing-rack support.

**How it works:** A carriage with grooved wheels runs on a track bracket and is linked by pivoted arms to the support that carries the spacing rack, so sliding the support moves the roll endwise past the type. Open slots in the uprights let rolls be dropped in, and a tension device stops the roll unwinding too fast. Rolls are prepared with alternating printing-paper and transfer-paper strips.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; paper roll; track bracket; grooved wheel; arm; rack support; cross bar; tension device; transfer-paper strip

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter: adds a roll-paper carriage in place of sheet platen feeding
