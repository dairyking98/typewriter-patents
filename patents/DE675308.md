> Description Translated from German The invention relates to type lever sheet teleprinter, in which the return of the carriage at the beginning of the line is only done by a special carriage return character and which are provided with a tab device, which when a special Character instead of the normal forwarding of the car by one character spacing causes the carriage to advance by several steps. Such known arrangements have the disadvantage that if the carriage has reached the end of the line and no switchback sign from the far end is given by further actuation of the indexing device and the Printing mechanism can easily damage the apparatus. There are already sheet teleprinters known, with which an automatic return of the carriage takes place as soon as it has reached the end of the sign. Such arrangements but have the disadvantage that the message can easily be corrupted can, if characters are transmitted during the decline movement of the carriage, and that also continues to damage the Apparatus can occur, since the carriage guidance is carried out in these known arrangements with great speed must become.
