## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type lever teleprinter carriage return and tab) · **Subject (patent classes):** Selection of materials for their magnetic properties of inorganic materials characterised by their coercivity of soft-magnetic materials metals or alloys in the form of particles, e.g. powder pressed, sintered, or bound together the particles being insulated; Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using mechanical translation and type-bar printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to type lever sheet teleprinter, in which the return of the carriage at the beginning of the line is only done by a special carriage return character and which are provided with a tab device, which when a special Character instead of the normal forwarding of the car by one character spacing causes the carriage to advance by several steps.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable stop on the tabulator rail defines the line end, and when the carriage reaches it both the indexing device and the printing device are rendered ineffective, so the rail also serves for multi-space tabulation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic carriage return on a teleprinter can corrupt messages sent during the return and can damage the apparatus at the line end.

**Solution:** An adjustable stop on the tabulator rail defines the line end, and when the carriage reaches it both the indexing device and the printing device are rendered ineffective, so the rail also serves for multi-space tabulation.
