## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewriting machines and more particularly to paper feed mechanism adaptable for use with a stenographic writing machine in which the paper sheet or strip is fed past the platen at each manipulation of a key or keys.

**How it works (reading):** Paper feed is timed to occur after the type pressure is fully released and during the return stroke of the type lever, using guide rollers and a moving platen frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper can be moved past the platen while the inking ribbon is pressed against it, causing misfeed during printing.

**Solution:** Paper feed is timed to occur after the type pressure is fully released and during the return stroke of the type lever, using guide rollers and a moving platen frame.
