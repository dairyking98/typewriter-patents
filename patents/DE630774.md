## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**How it works (reading):** A slidable open or closed frame behind the key-lever lugs carries the universal shaft through levers, moving smoothly against a cylindrical surface coaxial with each key lever, so the links are long and individually adjustable from below.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-lever transfer to the universal shaft has inaccessible noses, too little lever arm, and friction, and the shaft cannot sit at the most suitable place.

**Solution:** A slidable open or closed frame behind the key-lever lugs carries the universal shaft through levers, moving smoothly against a cylindrical surface coaxial with each key lever, so the links are long and individually adjustable from below.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** universal shaft; slidable frame; key-lever lugs; transfer levers
