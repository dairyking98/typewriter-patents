## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in certain features of construction and combinations and arrangements of parts, all of which will be fully set forth herein and particularly pointed out in the claims.

**How it works (reading):** Two universal bars, one for each spacing, with their keys and racks drive the escapement pinion so the carriage moves by one of two increments.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The escapement gave only one spacing increment, but writing Turkish and Arabic needs letters spaced by two different amounts.

**Solution:** Two universal bars, one for each spacing, with their keys and racks drive the escapement pinion so the carriage moves by one of two increments.

**Features:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
