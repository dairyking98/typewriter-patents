## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in the features of construction, combinations of devices and arrangements of parts described in the following specification and particularly pointed out in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** Carriage-mounted sleeve carriers on a cross bar above the platen hold a guide arrangement that positions a flat stencil card for the first line and feeds it through the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil plates or cards cannot easily be introduced into a typewriter, positioned for the first line, and fed flat through the machine.

**Solution:** Carriage-mounted sleeve carriers on a cross bar above the platen hold a guide arrangement that positions a flat stencil card for the first line and feeds it through the machine.
