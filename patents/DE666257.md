## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable machines need a ribbon switching device that can be disabled (for packing away) by a control that is easy to operate and does not require extra openings in the frame; earlier combined controls only worked together with carriage locking in the middle position.

**Solution:** A single control member with its button in the keyboard, which already centre-locks the carriage in horizontal and vertical directions, also disables the ribbon switching device, independent of carriage position through a flexible connection.

**How it works:** A slotted lever on the left frame side, spring-loaded and carrying a key button, is pressed and pivots then slides to a second position. Through links, an angle lever, a sliding rod and a spring-held part it acts on the ribbon switching axis lever to make the ribbon switch ineffective. It also acts on the line-switching lever and a locking lever whose lug enters a recess in the carriage run-up rail, locking the carriage. The flexible link to the carriage lock lets the ribbon switching be disabled wherever the carriage stands.
