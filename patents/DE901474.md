## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Method and device for controlling the sale of goods Im Retail nowadays, controls the sale of goods in general either through a cash register with attached pack control or through a register, which is served by the selling person himself. In the former procedure the customer receives a receipt when purchasing goods, to whom he must go, paid. From here he has to take the receipted receipt go to the packing control to get the packed goods against handing over the slip of paper to be handed over. This procedure is cumbersome especially for small articles and, time consuming. It often happens that the customer does not pay for the goods and not picking up and then a cumbersome write-back procedure is required. Fraud by forging the "paid" stamp is also possible. The second The procedure enables faster processing and makes receipts superfluous, since the cash register issues a receipt for the amount paid, However, it still has considerable disadvantages because the person selling tries will carry out deceptions with the help of the cash register. This is possible once, though the cash register drawer is not closed or if the customer receives the receipt…
