## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A backspace is needed on spring-driven carriages to reprint or correct a weak impression without moving the carriage by hand.

**Solution:** A ratchet on the escapement shaft engaged by a pawl pulled through a rock shaft and rods by a back-space key moves the carriage back one space.

**How it works:** The shaft joining the carriage rack pinion to the escapement wheel carries a ratchet wheel. Arms loosely mounted on the shaft carry a pawl normally held out of engagement. A back-spacing key pulls a bar, vertical rod and rock shaft, which draws the pawl over the ratchet and turns the shaft and pinion so the rack moves the carriage back one space. A spring returns the pawl.
