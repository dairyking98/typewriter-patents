## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink supply arrangements therefor

**How it works (reading):** A bent steel-wire spacer at the outer edge of the container snout acts as a sliding guide for the returning type, reducing friction and keeping the type's side faces clear of paint; a shut-off valve can limit paint escape.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type grinds on the paint-soaked cover of the ink container as it returns, wasting ink, smearing the side of the type, and wearing both the cover and the type, which degrades print sharpness.

**Solution:** A bent steel-wire spacer at the outer edge of the container snout acts as a sliding guide for the returning type, reducing friction and keeping the type's side faces clear of paint; a shut-off valve can limit paint escape.
