## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing unit)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register printing unit with force-fit driven pressure lever controlled by a lifting disc and consists in the expedient embodiment of a known per se on the print hammer control arranged adjusting device, through which the stroke of the print hammer as required without Change of its rest position can be regulated.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable link pin in a slot on the angle lever changes the hammer deflection, and the print head can be adjusted on one side to align with the print line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The print hammer stroke must be regulated without changing its rest position, and the head must line up with the print line.

**Solution:** An adjustable link pin in a slot on the angle lever changes the hammer deflection, and the print head can be adjusted on one side to align with the print line.
