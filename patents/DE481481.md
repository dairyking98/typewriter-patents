> Description Translated from German Cash register printing unit The invention relates to a cash register printing unit with force-fit driven pressure lever controlled by a lifting disc and consists in the expedient embodiment of a known per se on the print hammer control arranged adjusting device, through which the stroke of the print hammer as required without Change of its rest position can be regulated. The print head of the print hammer is also adjustable on one side connected to the print hammer head to to be able to set them exactly in the same direction as the print line of the print wheels.
