## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Billing work needs two separate carriages of platen, and with ordinary platens one cannot print a bill and a record at once.

**Solution:** A second auxiliary platen sits beside the normal one and can be actuated independently, or coupled to the usual line-space mechanism so the two act as one platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** main platen; auxiliary platen; coupling for line-space mechanism
