## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German It is known to use auxiliary ribbons in writing, calculating or similar machines instead of sheets of carbon paper, which, like the main ribbons, consist of textural strips, and to wind one or more such ribbons on a supply roll attached to the carriage. Since these auxiliary ribbons are intended for multiple use, you need two roles for them, between which they are moved back and forth. This causes considerable inconvenience when clamping the carbon copy sheets, since they have to be pulled through between the overlapping tapes. . According to the invention, therefore, carbon paper strips are used as auxiliary color ribbons, which are torn off and thrown away after a single use. A single supply roll is therefore sufficient for them. The carbon strips are guided in guide sleeves, which can be individually swiveled away when the writing sheets are changed, thereby enabling the machine to be operated conveniently. The guide sleeves are carried by tension wires that absorb the tension that is used to tighten the carbon paper strip, since this must not be expected of the paper strip as such.
