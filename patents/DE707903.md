## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon-spool lids swung on internal hinges protrude from a smooth housing surface and cannot be made flush.

**Solution:** The ribbon-spool lid is a removable cover that sits flush with the housing as a single piece, not hinged outward.

**How it works:** Removable ribbon-spool covers in a smooth housing outline, fitted flush so the machine outside surface stays clean and free of protrusions.
