## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**How it works (reading):** The slot in the key top is undercut and the foot inserted in it is held by injected plastic filler, so the foot cannot slide out.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Plastic key tops with metal feet loosen over time and cannot be pressed in without cracking.

**Solution:** The slot in the key top is undercut and the foot inserted in it is held by injected plastic filler, so the foot cannot slide out.
