## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriages need improved line spacing, bell, margin regulation, apron, and a pointer for corrections.

**Solution:** Carriage fittings: pawl line spacer with single/double button, bell hammer, rocking feed apron, margin screw on scale bar, and a pointer combined with the paper guide.

**How it works:** A post on the platen shaft carries a pawl on the platen ratchet and is pushed by a lever whose button sets single or double space. The same lever press lifts the escapement pawls off the rack bar. A bell on a top-plate standard is struck by a hammer near line end. A rocking paper-feed apron is mounted on the end rails of a tilting platen frame, and a margin regulating screw sits on the scale bar. A combined paper guide and letter pointer shows the last letter printed and swings clear when the platen frame is tilted.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen frame; feed roller; line-space post; pawl; ratchet; lever; spacing button; escapement lever; pawl bar; bell; bell hammer; rocking apron; scale bar; margin screw; pointer
