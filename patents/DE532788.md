## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage escapement mechanisms need accurate stepping, cheap parts and easy installation in the machine.

**Solution:** A single stamped U-shaped curved pair-pawl swung to and fro engages the escape wheel alternately at opposite points, mounted adjustably on the axis coupled to the universal rail.

**How it works:** An escape wheel with an odd number of teeth sits with play on a sleeve having two pinions. The U-shaped pair-pawl, punched from sheet, rocks on an axis parallel to the crossbar in an adjustable and lockable bearing ring. Each key lever or space bar press turns the axis back and forth through a shift rod, so the two pawl teeth alternately engage opposite sides of the escape wheel, advancing it one tooth. One pinion meshes with the carriage shift rod and the other with a spring-loaded nose on the escape wheel. When the carriage is moved by hand in the other direction, the nose rides past so only the free pinions turn.
