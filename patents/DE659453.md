**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**How it works (reading):** A U-shaped bracket with shoulders on a single rail holds a rubber-coated roller tube, and the bracket lugs serve as abutment for the brake spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guide rollers needed a second guide rail, were costly, awkward to replace, and brackets could fall off.

**Solution:** A U-shaped bracket with shoulders on a single rail holds a rubber-coated roller tube, and the bracket lugs serve as abutment for the brake spring.
