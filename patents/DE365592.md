## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on typewriters for feeding the writing sheets, in the following the invention is discussed on a typewriter, in which a switchable platen frame is used.

**How it works (reading):** Each pressure-roller spring is tensioned by its own worm-gear adjuster, with the worm-gear parts mounted so the same parts can be made in pairs, and the rollers are carried on oscillating arms supported on the platen frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressure rollers must press firmly on the platen and hold thick or folded sheets, yet be easy to lift off, and each spring's tension must be adjustable after assembly.

**Solution:** Each pressure-roller spring is tensioned by its own worm-gear adjuster, with the worm-gear parts mounted so the same parts can be made in pairs, and the rollers are carried on oscillating arms supported on the platen frame.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
