## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object is to provide devices whereby the use of the ribbon may be dispensed with at will whenever it is desired to write a stencil or mimeograph sheet or for some other purose.

**How it works (reading):** A two-part jointed ribbon operating lever with detents sets the ribbon carrier low so that types strike over the ribbon directly on the stencil.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must be dispensed with when writing stencils or mimeograph sheets.

**Solution:** A two-part jointed ribbon operating lever with detents sets the ribbon carrier low so that types strike over the ribbon directly on the stencil.
