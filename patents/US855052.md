## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists of a new and useful attachment for typewriters and consists of means for preventing movement of the carriage, even after the escapement of the dogs has taken place, until the type has printed.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment on the escapement bar with a movable dog holds the carriage until the type has printed, so the carriage moves only after the impression.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage moves as soon as the escapement dogs release the rack, even before the type has printed.

**Solution:** An attachment on the escapement bar with a movable dog holds the carriage until the type has printed, so the carriage moves only after the impression.
