## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Electric typewriter or telex typewriter The present invention solves the problem with the least amount of means on direct and predominantly electrical means Ways, avoiding types and a type basket, a feed carriage and of a ribbon, with a slight impact, using electrosensitive Paper to produce legible characters in a completely noiseless way that when the machine is dismantled into encoder and receiver onto many receivers at the same time can be transferred. The machine enables a much simpler and faster Write than it could be achieved with the previous machines, and brings generated by opening the type on the purely mechanical typewriters Noise in omission. It enables writing on roll paper and thus saves the clamping. The machine is suitable as a writing instrument for connection to the previously telex apparatus in use and has the advantage of that the type basket, the ribbon, the movable carriage can be omitted. the Machine 1 also has a labor-saving meaning in the sense of rationalization.
