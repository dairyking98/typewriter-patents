## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Mechanical typewriters and telex printers are noisy and need a type basket, carriage and ribbon; existing facsimile-like telex systems are complicated.

**Solution:** Write characters line by line on electrosensitive paper from a fixed row of pole bundles (wire ends) energised in patterns, with no moving carriage, types or ribbon.

**How it works:** A row of wire-end pole groups spans the full width of the paper, each group of seven by seven-style poles fed from 28 busbars. Pressing a key at the encoder sends low-voltage direct current to the busbars forming that character, and the touched poles colour the paper. Characters appear in sequence along the line without lateral indexing; the machine splits into encoder and receiver and can drive many receivers. It extends patent 506065.
