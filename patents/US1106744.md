## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Birefringent or phase retarding elements

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to case shifting mechanism for typewriting machines and its general object is to provide new and 'improved mechanism of the character specified.

**How it works (reading):** Case shift devices are operated automatically at predetermined carriage positions or by the tabulating mechanism, changing the relation of platen and printing elements.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case shifting so that different characters on the printing element can be used has been manual, and the inventor wants it automatic at set carriage points or by tabulation.

**Solution:** Case shift devices are operated automatically at predetermined carriage positions or by the tabulating mechanism, changing the relation of platen and printing elements.
