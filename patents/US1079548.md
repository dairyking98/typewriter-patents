## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing a dotted or continuous line or repeated character requires repeated keystrokes.

**Solution:** A special key throws a spring-loaded typebar to the print point, releases the spring carriage, and a star wheel driven by the carriage vibrates the bar repeatedly.

**How it works:** A two-part typebar has a spring-held bar on an arm worked by the key. When thrown up, its tongue meets a star wheel turned by a pinion on a rack that the key engages with the carriage. Each tooth pushes the bar away from the paper against its spring; as the tooth slips off, the spring throws the bar to print again. The carriage runs freely while the star wheel acts as the escapement, and the ribbon is fed while the type is at the print point.
