## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-strike machine needs an improved vibratory ribbon carrier to bring the ribbon in front of the type.

**Solution:** A plate-like vibratory ribbon carrier with a type opening and slots forming turning bars, with a shiftable guide, driven from the key action.

**How it works:** The ribbon is wound between two spools on vertical shafts with bevel gears, driven by pinions on an endwise-shifting shaft turned from the carriage spring drum through a ratchet wheel and universal joint. A vibratory carrier head with a type opening, whose edges are diagonal and parallel, has turning bars between slot and opening so the ribbon is turned to run flat past the type. The carrier moves in a guide on a support, with guide-shifting mechanism, actuated by the universal bar and rock shaft.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon spool; bevel gear; driving pinion; vibratory ribbon carrier; type opening; turning bar; guide support; spring drum; universal bar; rock shaft; type bar

**Numbers:** two types per type bar
