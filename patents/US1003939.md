## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in machines for cutting out or removing portions of a strip or card of suitable material to form characters in said strip or card.

**How it works (reading):** Keys drive a power-operated tool head, dies are positioned in sequence with each key, and the line-spacing movement is limited automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Power stencil cutters need automatic die positioning and line spacing limits.

**Solution:** Keys drive a power-operated tool head, dies are positioned in sequence with each key, and the line-spacing movement is limited automatically.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
