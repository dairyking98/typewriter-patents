## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter-based label addressing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** A frame clamped to the carriage by a screw and a projection under a flange, with serrated side bars carrying adjustable bearings held by set-screws, so the attachment can be positioned on the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An attachment must be fitted to a typewriter carriage and adjusted in position to suit it.

**Solution:** A frame clamped to the carriage by a screw and a projection under a flange, with serrated side bars carrying adjustable bearings held by set-screws, so the attachment can be positioned on the carriage.
