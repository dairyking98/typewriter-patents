## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** With an adjustable feed step, a back-feed (reverse spacing) of the same step is needed at will, and a separate back-feed would not follow the feed setting.

**Solution:** The same turning lever that sets the feed step also rotates a separating plate that limits the back-feed pawl's travel, so back-feed always equals the forward step.

**How it works:** Same feed train as JP157669: ratchet on the pinion shaft, swinging lever with ratchet pawl and slide piece in an arc slot, stop pawl, and a toothed, detented adjusting lever. A turning lever is linked to the adjusting lever and also, through a second link, to the arm of a separating plate pivoted on the ratchet shaft. The plate turns round the ratchet rim to a set stopping position. A loose lever carrying a back-feed pawl is swung at will by a link, and the plate's position makes it step back exactly one feed step. Forward feed occurs at each printing stroke via a push lever from the printing lever and the arc-slot lever.
