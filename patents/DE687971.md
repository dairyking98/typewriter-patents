**How it works (reading):** Feed pins are guided to move radially with respect to the platen, and cams on telescopic tubes select pin groups for different widths.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Forms with edge holes can be displaced against each other by feed pins that enter and leave the holes at an angle.

**Solution:** Feed pins are guided to move radially with respect to the platen, and cams on telescopic tubes select pin groups for different widths.
