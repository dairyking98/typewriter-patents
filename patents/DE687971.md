It is known in typewriters to fasten the feed pins to be brought into engagement with the holes in the sheet of paper on a disk which is mounted eccentrically to the platen. During the rotation of said disk, the penetration of the conveying pins into the holes of the paper sheet or the exit of the conveying pins from said holes takes place with a lateral pivoting movement.
