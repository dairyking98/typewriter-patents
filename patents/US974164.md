## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Positive-feed mechanisms automatically operated in response to carriage return

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object is to pro-- vide a-- means whereby the carriage of the typewriter will be automatically returned to initial position at the completion of' a line and the-platen will be moved axially a suitable-distance for line spacing.

**How it works (reading):** Electric motor that is energized at line end to drive the carriage back against its spring and turn the platen, with cutoff at home.
