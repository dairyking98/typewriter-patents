## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** The holder plate is articulated to a footplate with springs so it can be set at any inclination and folds flat, and a flexible shaft drives a line-switch device that advances the paper by one or more lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing document holders take up much space when shipped, cannot be folded, and do not change their angle relative to the horizontal.

**Solution:** The holder plate is articulated to a footplate with springs so it can be set at any inclination and folds flat, and a flexible shaft drives a line-switch device that advances the paper by one or more lines.
