## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column stop positions cannot be readily selected and set, and stops in use cannot be quickly cleared when the character of the work changes.

**Solution:** A setting mechanism selects the positions where column stops are to be used and sets the stops accordingly, and a clearing mechanism removes stops in use. This is a companion patent to US979215.
