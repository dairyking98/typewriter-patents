## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact; Ball cages for multiple rows of balls; Ball cages comb-shaped

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to and particularly po nted out in v the claims.

**How it works (reading):** Bearing balls run in recesses in the type-bar hub and between the hanger arms, with an adjusting pin to set the balls.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar pivot friction and wear at the hanger are a problem for the type-bar joint.

**Solution:** Bearing balls run in recesses in the type-bar hub and between the hanger arms, with an adjusting pin to set the balls.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
