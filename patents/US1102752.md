## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** Our present invention relates to means for duplicating typewritten work and its general object is to provide'improved means of the character specified and especially means for producing a duplicate copy, preferably an inked copy, in addition to the usual inked or first copy, which means may be readily thrown into and out of use wlthout disturbing the work sheets on which the writing is bemg done.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon mechanism on the traveling carriage supplements the frame ribbon mechanism to make an inked duplicate copy.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicate inked copies needed to be made without disturbing the work sheets, and paper guiding needed to be improved.

**Solution:** A ribbon mechanism on the traveling carriage supplements the frame ribbon mechanism to make an inked duplicate copy.

**How it works:** A second ribbon on the carriage prints duplicates, while guides and severing devices support interleaved ribbon plies.
