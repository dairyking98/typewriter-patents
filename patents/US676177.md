## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar touch and impression were weak, and high speed risked collisions between typebars.

**Solution:** Rearward-striking typebars pivot on a curved fulcrum wire seated in a radially slotted segment, each with a return spring; the typebars strike the platen to print.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md), [Key touch and even impression](../features/bar-touch.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** typebar; segment; fulcrum wire; return spring; platen
