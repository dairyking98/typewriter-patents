**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with automatic total pulling)

**How it works (reading):** A counter with presettable elements returns the total-pulling device to its preset position automatically after the zero position, so any counter can print a constant value.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A date-printing device on an adding machine needed a special keyboard or extra lever.

**Solution:** A counter with presettable elements returns the total-pulling device to its preset position automatically after the zero position, so any counter can print a constant value.

**How it works:** Preset adjusting members on the counter restore the total-pulling device to the preset position after the zero position, repeating the same value on the add machine.
