> Description Translated from German Device for loading typewriters with one spool each Wrapped writing and colored paper It is known to use writing paper for typewriters and the color paper required for making copies in the form of long ones Use paper webs that are wound on spools behind the platen be stored. In one type of known devices, for the original a spool of writing paper and for each copy two more spools that one with writing and the other with colored paper, used all in the writing carriage be stored. This device is cumbersome and too heavy, especially since all the coils must be moved together with the carriage. In order to overcome this disadvantage, other known devices the coils arranged in a stationary manner, so they do not follow the movement of the writing carriage. This arrangement tends to cause inconvenience in paper feeding. With other devices a paper web for the original, a color paper web and a strike-through paper web wound on one and the same spool and passed together over the platen.
