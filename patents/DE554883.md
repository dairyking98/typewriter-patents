## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**How it works (reading):** The paper spools are mounted in the carriage so they slide radially and are pressed against the platen by elastic means, so the web feeds without extra coupling gears or feathers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage-mounted paper spools for original and copies are heavy, and spools fixed in place cause feed trouble, while a common spool for several webs prevents separating the copies.

**Solution:** The paper spools are mounted in the carriage so they slide radially and are pressed against the platen by elastic means, so the web feeds without extra coupling gears or feathers.
