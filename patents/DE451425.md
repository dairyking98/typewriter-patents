## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**How it works (reading):** The scale is supported in its middle by a lever that rotates about the same pin as the scale, with an abutment on the platen frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A long paper finger scale bends through and jumps off its trunnions because it has no middle support.

**Solution:** The scale is supported in its middle by a lever that rotates about the same pin as the scale, with an abutment on the platen frame.
