## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (book making from typed stencil sheets) · **Subject (patent classes):** Silk-screen printing; Carriers therefor

**How it works (reading):** Stencils are made so that two or more book pages sit on one sheet in letterpress page order with a binding margin, then printed on both sides so the folded sheets bind as a normal book.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typed books duplicated from stencils come out as loose sheets that cannot open flat like bound books.

**Solution:** Stencils are made so that two or more book pages sit on one sheet in letterpress page order with a binding margin, then printed on both sides so the folded sheets bind as a normal book.
