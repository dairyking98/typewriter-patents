## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a typewriter, whose paper roller in a known manner when depressing the individual keys as a result Spring action and a locking device is rotated gradually.

**How it works (reading):** The drive spring travels longitudinally with the carriage, so no transmission is needed, and turning the paper roller back winds the spring again; a ratchet wheel and fixed and loose pawls advance the roller on each key press.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper roller advance needed a separate transmission gear from the fixed spring housing to the moving carriage, and the spring had to be rewound by hand at each new line.

**Solution:** The drive spring travels longitudinally with the carriage, so no transmission is needed, and turning the paper roller back winds the spring again; a ratchet wheel and fixed and loose pawls advance the roller on each key press.

> Description Translated from German Typewriter. The subject of the invention is a typewriter, whose paper roller in a known manner when depressing the individual keys as a result Spring action and a locking device is rotated gradually. The novelty of the invention consists essentially in the fact that the driving spring with the carriage in its longitudinal direction is movable. This eliminates the need for the transmission gear that would otherwise be required from the spring housing attached to the frame to the carriage carrying the paper roller.
