## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by applications of scales or indicators

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to tabulator scales or indicators for assisting the operator in performing tabular work on such machines as type-writers, type-setters, printing-machines, , can be easily and quickly applied to existing I and the like which have a shifting mechanism for operating the carriage when characters of the upper or lower case are to be printed.

**How it works (reading):** A digital tabulating scale is attached to the shifting rail and set beside the carriage scale, so the indicator lines up with the columns; it attaches to a stand with grooves or slots.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabular work needs a column scale the operator can read while writing, and the existing carriage scale did not show the shift-by-shift column positions clearly.

**Solution:** A digital tabulating scale is attached to the shifting rail and set beside the carriage scale, so the indicator lines up with the columns; it attaches to a stand with grooves or slots.
