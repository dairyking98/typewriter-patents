## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Opening text gives no stated problem; it describes a typebar machine with a circular ring of type levers and a frame-mounted carriage escapement.

**Solution:** A typewriter whose keys turn individual rods that pull circularly arranged type levers up against a ribbon and platen.

**How it works:** Each key pin turns a rod by a crank, returned by a spiral spring. A second crank on the rod pulls a two-part adjustable tie rod hooked to the short arm of a type lever, which snaps the type up to the ribbon and platen roller. Type levers pivot in radial slots of a ring carrier and are closed in by a top plate. A swinging grate frame is nudged by pins on the rods; its angle lever and rod move a tooth against a rack on the carriage, giving the escapement.
