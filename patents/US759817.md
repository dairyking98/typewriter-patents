## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A writing machine that moves over a flat printing surface needs a line-spacing mechanism that works with a bodily moving carriage.

**Solution:** The carriage hangs between two toothed guide rails on pinions and double pinions with side disks in a channel, so the whole machine moves for line spacing.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
