## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper inserters with pivoting pinions are complicated and need special adjustment.

**Solution:** A rack in a guide rail is held by friction, and an intermediate pinion on a fixed axis drives the platen in one stroke direction only.
