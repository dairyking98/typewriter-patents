## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Punched strips for folding are fragile and tear at the cut-outs during feeding.

**Solution:** Cut-outs are closed by edge bridges, and grooves stiffen the strip along the cut-outs to stop tearing.
