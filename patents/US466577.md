## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cycle tires were attached to the wheel in an unsatisfactory way, using cumbersome retaining devices.

**Solution:** The felly has central and side grooves, and the tire edges seat in them, held by a convex band that fits grooves on the tire flanges.
