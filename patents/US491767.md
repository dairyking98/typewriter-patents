## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** When the carriage stops at the end of a line mid-word, the remaining letters print on top of each other and make a blot.

**Solution:** A soft shield attached near the platen end covers the last letter space so extra type blows strike the shield and not the paper.

**How it works:** A strip of soft india rubber or leather is held in a sheet-metal holder pivoted on the carriage between the end of the scale and the paper guide. An adjustable screw ear bears on the platen end as a stop in the normal position, where the shield sits at the last letter space. Type striking there hit the shield. A handle lets the operator swing the shield aside to add a final character such as a hyphen. A second form for the Remington has bent ears gripping the paper guide edges.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
