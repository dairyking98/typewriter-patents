## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column-stop tabulation on a spring-drawn carriage ends with a shock or jar when the carriage is stopped.

**Solution:** A brake shoe on the spring barrel, applied by the column-stop key lever, cushions the carriage as the stop engages.

**How it works:** The carriage rack engages a pinion on the escape-wheel shaft, and a spring barrel drives the carriage. Pressing the column-stop key lever swings a lever carrying a stop member up into the path of adjustable stops on the carriage flange, releasing the letter-spacing escapement. The same key lever applies a brake to the spring barrel so the carriage arrests without jar. Built on the Oliver machine with a shift-frame platen shift.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** spring barrel; brake shoe; column-stop key lever; stop lever; adjustable stop; rack bar; escape-wheel shaft; pinion; shift frame

**Improves on:**

- earlier column-stop devices: brake on the spring barrel prevents jar when the carriage stops
