## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a line spacing mechanism for typewriting machines and consists in a new arrangement and construction of parts adapted to operate in an im proved manner for the accurate spacing of lines ot typewriting.

**How it works (reading):** A loose cam disk with notches sits beside a ratchet on the platen shaft; a spring-held sliding pawl rides the disk cams and engages the ratchet teeth only at the cut-away portion, so the operator's knob sets the number of line spaces or disengages the spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing needs to be set accurately for one, two or three lines, or switched off entirely, without relying on hand-counting.

**Solution:** A loose cam disk with notches sits beside a ratchet on the platen shaft; a spring-held sliding pawl rides the disk cams and engages the ratchet teeth only at the cut-away portion, so the operator's knob sets the number of line spaces or disengages the spacing.
