## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Make the rail's stop pivot so it yields to the shift rod until that has fully returned, and make the margin stop yield on the backward pass.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the main patent's margin stop and bell, the sliding rail can jam the carriage-escapement shift rod on its return, and the bell rings twice, on the way out and on the way back.

**Solution:** Make the rail's stop pivot so it yields to the shift rod until that has fully returned, and make the margin stop yield on the backward pass.

**How it works:** A rail on the platen frame's shift plate is moved sideways by the right margin stop, which first trips a bell clapper. Further typing runs the carriage on until a second stop drags the rail into the path of the shift rod that drives the carriage escapement, locking the carriage and the type action. The rail's stop is hinged so it gives way if the shift rod has not yet come back, then falls behind its nose. The margin stop is hinged to pass over the rail on the return without ringing again. An auxiliary lever pushes the rail down to allow typing past the margin.
