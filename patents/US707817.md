## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen must be released from the line-space wheel and re-set in smaller increments than a toothed adjustment wheel allows.

**Solution:** A fine-toothed wheel on the platen engaged by three spring detents offset from each other (a vernier) on the line-space wheel.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 6 platen, a finely toothed wheel is fixed to one end. The line-space wheel, face to face, carries three elbow-shaped spring-loaded detents through perforations to engage the teeth. They are placed so only one detent seats at a time, giving three lock positions within one tooth pitch. A lever on the platen frame releases the platen for free rotation and re-locks it.

**Parts:** platen; fine-toothed wheel; line-space wheel; detent; flat spring; three-armed support; release lever; platen frame
