## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter needs a simple, reliable and cheap design in which the type is held on a turning wheel and the platen steps automatically with carriage reciprocation.

**Solution:** Type is set on the periphery of a rotatable wheel with independent rows of small and capital letters, turned by hand to bring a letter into position; reciprocating a hand-actuated carriage prints it and steps the platen.
