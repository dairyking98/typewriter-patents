## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus specially adapted for preparation of record carriers for controlling composing machines

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** The platen carrier and paper table/apron are used as a base for the paper, with a marginal paper-finger and erasing plate mounted on the platen support.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasures and corrections need a convenient base for the paper, and the paper-guiding and feeding means need a support.

**Solution:** The platen carrier and paper table/apron are used as a base for the paper, with a marginal paper-finger and erasing plate mounted on the platen support.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
