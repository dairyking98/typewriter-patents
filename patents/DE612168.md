## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon drive is moved through extra levers, causing friction and heavier keystroke.

**Solution:** A universal bridge pivots coaxially with the intermediate levers and drives the ribbon pawl directly, with pawls on a common spring returning the bridge.
