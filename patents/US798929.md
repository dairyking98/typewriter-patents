## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar bearings, key-lever mounting and the key-to-typebar linkage of the Williams front-strike machine were hard to adjust, assemble and remove.

**Solution:** Bellcrank sublevers with overlapping forked bearings link removable key-levers to sheet-metal typebars in plate bearings, with eccentric bushings for adjusting the linkage.

**How it works:** Key-levers pivot on a back bar, held by notches and a retaining rod, and each is linked to a bellcrank on a transverse rod. The bellcrank is linked by a hooked, removable link to the heel of a bent sheet-metal typebar. The typebars lie in the surface of a cylinder in plate-stack bearings so all type meet at one point. The bellcrank bearings are forked and overlap to give a wide seat. Eccentric bushings at the key-lever links adjust the relative typebar positions. Any key pulls a universal bar that works the escapement.
