## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to automatic reversible-ribbon mechanism for type-writing machines by which the ribbon is automatically reversed and rewound on the empty ribbonspool when it has reached the inner end of the ribbon, as will be more fully described.

**How it works (reading):** A worm or spiral gear driven oscillating yoke reverses the ribbon automatically and a lever lifts the ribbon to the printing point in advance of the type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon must be reversed and rewound onto the empty spool automatically at the end of the ribbon, and lifted to the printing point ahead of the type.

**Solution:** A worm or spiral gear driven oscillating yoke reverses the ribbon automatically and a lever lifts the ribbon to the printing point in advance of the type.
