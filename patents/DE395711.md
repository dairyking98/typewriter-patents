## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal triggered by the full or empty spool diameter leaves a long unused piece of ribbon at each end and reverses at varying points.

**Solution:** Reversal is released by the emptying spool and carried out by a spring tensioned by the filling spool, so it happens only when the ribbon has completely run off.

**How it works:** A switching lever has two locking pins, each held by a latch lever whose pawl rests on a spool's ribbon. When the ribbon leaves a notch in the core (the spool runs fully out), the pawl drops in and frees the pin. Sliding-shoe levers on the full side, which push out as the spool thickens, tension springs coupled to the switching lever, so it snaps over and reverses the drive. Each core has a ribbon clamp pin and a notch placed so it faces the direction of travel. The text is cut off partway, in the second half of the description.
