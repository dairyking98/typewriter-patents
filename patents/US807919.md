## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typebars and their operating mechanism should be simple to assemble, with adjustable key tension, in a case-shifting typebar machine.

**Solution:** Typebars with toothed hubs mesh with racks on L-shaped key-bars, all stacked on a square curved bar with wedge washers, in a frame that drops to shift case.

**How it works:** Wedge-shaped washers on a square curved bar separate the typebars and guide the racks. Each typebar hub has teeth meshing with a rack on the upper end of an L-shaped key-bar. A spring on each key-bar bears on a rotatable cross-bar whose teeth engage a worm, so a milled head adjusts all key tensions together. The whole type-bar frame hangs on four links and drops to shift case, held up by coil springs. A pivoted cam plate with L-shaped slots locks the frame until the shift key is pressed.
