## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen line-spacing mechanisms are bulky, noisy or hard to assemble.

**Solution:** A self-contained frame on the platen carriage holds a pivoting pawl carrier with a range-setting member and a combined line-space and carriage-return lever.

**How it works:** A frame fixed to the carriage holds the platen line ratchet. A pawl carrier on a bolt parallel to the platen axis carries the pawl and an adjuster that sets the stroke to different line spacings. A hand lever pivoting about a roughly vertical axis acts on the carrier and also returns the carriage. A spring returns the carrier and a stop defines rest.
