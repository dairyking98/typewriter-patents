There are known guides for type carriers of typewriters that, vertically movable, at the same time as at the stop put past the ribbon in front of its opening to serve as a guide so that at Lowering the guides in order to expose the writing point and the ribbon is lowered. In the present invention it is essentially a question of both Guide jaws arranged on the sides of the stop point are guided by two pairs of rods are. This will be in several ways Extra benefits achieved. The arrangement of two pairs of rods makes one unintentional Prevents twisting of the jaws and ensures safe storage of the latter, so that the lowest possible guide jaws can be used. As a result of the double guide rods, the guide jaws have such resistance, that there are special management organs for the upper or lower end of the types-rod superfluous.
