## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** A separate locking device holds the switch locking member and releases it only when the ribbon has run out, so ribbon tension does not oppose tooth pressure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon changeover needed less friction when releasing the switch locking member under ribbon tension.

**Solution:** A separate locking device holds the switch locking member and releases it only when the ribbon has run out, so ribbon tension does not oppose tooth pressure.
