## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A punctuation character such as a period on a multi-character type bar strikes with too much force and can puncture the paper, while the other characters need full impression.

**Solution:** An arresting stop engages the type bar or its actuating part before the period prints, and is held out of use for the other characters on the same bar.

**Features:** [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
