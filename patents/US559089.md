## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Yost machine's carriage feed escapement and carriage release need to be simple, durable and effective.

**Solution:** A rocking trunnion carries a rigid detaining dog and a loose, sprung feeding dog that engage a feed rack on the carriage, with the same assembly releasing the carriage.

**How it works:** The paper carriage rides on a round guide rail and a front track, with a toothed rack on a bar attached to the carriage yokes. A trunnion rocks on conical-point screws, driven through a pull-rod from the universal bar of the keys. A rigid dog fixed to the trunnion detains the rack, while a plate-mounted yielding feeding dog slides and oscillates on slotted screws under a wire spring. Rocking the trunnion alternately releases and re-engages the dogs so the carriage advances one tooth per stroke. An adjustable screw stops the return rocking.
