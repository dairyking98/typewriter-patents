## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper feeding and guiding on the platen is not adequately controlled.

**Solution:** A main feed roller bears on the platen with spring tension that can be varied, and paper fingers have adjustable spring tension, to feed and guide the sheet.
