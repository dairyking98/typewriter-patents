## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper feeding pressure rolls that run upon the cylindrical platen or main feed rolls of typewritingmaehiues.

**How it works (reading):** Each small feed roll is spring-connected so any roll can be lifted away alone without changing the others.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A pressure roll cannot be lifted away from the platen without affecting the other rolls on the same shaft.

**Solution:** Each small feed roll is spring-connected so any roll can be lifted away alone without changing the others.
