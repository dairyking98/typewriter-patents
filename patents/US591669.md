## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide certain new and useful improvements in typewriting machines, whereby any convenient number of letters can be written by pressing a single key, and whereby the accurate spacing of the writing is obtained whether one, two, three, or more letters are printed at one stroke of the key.

**How it works (reading):** Supplementary typebars each carry two or three characters, printed by one key, with spacing scaled to the number printed.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Common words and letter pairs take several strokes.

**Solution:** Supplementary typebars each carry two or three characters, printed by one key, with spacing scaled to the number printed.

**How it works:** Three key sets control bars with one, two or three characters. Each set presses a lever acting on an arm of a shaft at a different distance, so the shaft turns one, two or three units, and this drives the carriage spacing to match.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key set; typebar; lever; shaft; arm; spring; platen

**Numbers:** 2 or 3 characters per bar
