## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists of certain novel devices and detailsof construction, arran gem cut, and combination of the various parts whereby the objects contemplated are attained, as hereinafter more fully set forth, pointed out, and illustrated inthe accompanying drawings, which are made a part of this specification, in which- Figure 1 is a front elevation of the device.

**How it works (reading):** A detachable copy holder with line and word indicators that move step by step as each letter is copied, and fold compactly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copyists lose their place and make errors when transcribing printed or typed copy.

**Solution:** A detachable copy holder with line and word indicators that move step by step as each letter is copied, and fold compactly.
