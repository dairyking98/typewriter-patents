## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage drive gear of the parent patent limits writing speed.

**Solution:** The ratchet wheel is placed on a separate axis from the spring housing, with a 1:2 translation to the escape wheel so the escape wheel runs faster and the ratchet wheel has fewer teeth.
