## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (writing and transfer printing with arithmetic unit) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a writing and transfer printing device with arithmetic unit.

**How it works (reading):** The arithmetic unit senses the previous balance from the account card and writes the new balance, including the new item, onto a transfer sheet in the transfer printing device, so the transfer document is produced during booking.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Transfer sheets and secondary cards must be re-keyed from the booking machine or punched separately, which causes errors and wastes time.

**Solution:** The arithmetic unit senses the previous balance from the account card and writes the new balance, including the new item, onto a transfer sheet in the transfer printing device, so the transfer document is produced during booking.
