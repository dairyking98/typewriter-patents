## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, accurate and quick printing machine for a wide range of characters, cheap to make and easy to operate.

**Solution:** A type cylinder that slides and turns on a spring-raised swinging rod, detented by a ball in index hollows, is pressed down onto a paper cylinder that is advanced a step by the return stroke.

**How it works:** The upper half of the type cylinder carries round hollows marked with index characters, and the lower half carries the matching types at the symmetrical positions. A ball on a spring plate drops into a hollow to hold the cylinder, which slides and turns on a support rod in a swinging frame held raised by a spring. Pressing the rod or the cylinder's handle prints the type directly below through a ribbon onto the paper cylinder. On release the spring lifts the rod and a pawl lever on the frame flicks a ratchet that turns a worm, driving a rack on the paper-cylinder frame one character space sideways. A lever lifts the worm out of the rack to return the carriage, and a second ratchet and lever give line feed; for vertical writing the swing is linked to the paper-cylinder ratchet instead.
