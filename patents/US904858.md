## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Writing or like instruments in holders or guides

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to means for readily producing what may be termed liner work, that is' to say, to means for producing leader or followinglines in invoice or indexes or for producing lines of stars or underscoring and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A liner type bar is driven by a type actuating wheel and a connecting pawl, with the actuating rack or bar moved by the carriage so that the leader line prints as the carriage travels.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Leader lines of stars and underscoring are produced by repeated key strokes, and the existing constructions are limited; this is a further construction for the same purpose.

**Solution:** A liner type bar is driven by a type actuating wheel and a connecting pawl, with the actuating rack or bar moved by the carriage so that the leader line prints as the carriage travels.
