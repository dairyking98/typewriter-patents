## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a Japanese-script type-wheel machine, locking the wheel, holding the lock and printing must happen in a reliable sequence whether the handle is worked fast or slowly.

**Solution:** One handle's stroke first lifts a cam-driven locking piece into the wheel's gear teeth, then tips the rocking frame to print, with a roller on a guide piece keeping the lock engaged throughout.

**How it works:** Replaceable types sit round a ring to form a type wheel, carried in a rocking frame with a coaxial gear; several such wheels turn together through gears from an index drum turned by a grip wheel. Pressing the handle at the position of the indicator needle first presses a small piece on a cam, which lifts a lower roller and a small frame so its locking piece enters the teeth of the wheel gear. The upper roller leaves a hollow in an arc-shaped guide piece and then runs along it, holding the lock. Further pressure on the short arm tips the rocking frame against its spring so the wheel prints on the platen paper. On release springs return handle and frame, the roller drops back into the hollow and the lock releases. Moving the handle sideways also moves the platen and an indicator needle.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. The invention relates to a Japanese-script typewriter characterised in that a small frame carrying an upper and a lower roller is mounted so that it can swing; a cam working with the lower roller is mounted on the same shaft as the rocking frame; the small piece of the cam is kept higher than the short arm of the rocking frame; the upper roller rests on a guide piece having a hollow; one side of the small frame is extended to form a locking piece; and the gear that this locking piece engages is mounted on the same axis as the type frame. The object is that locking, keeping the lock and printing can be done in one sequence, so that whether worked fast or slowly every part engages and disengages surely.
