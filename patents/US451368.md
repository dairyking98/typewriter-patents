## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The check is hard to insert far enough under the die plate for the pins to perforate it, especially on bonds with coupons.

**Solution:** A check punch whose base has a horizontal cylindrical chamber open at both ends beneath the perforating pins, so the paper can slide in beyond the pins.

**How it works:** An opening in the top wall of the chamber sits directly under the pins; a bed die closes the chamber mouth, and the paper is pushed through the chamber.
