## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a tabulator for typewriters, typewriters and other booking engines.

**How it works (reading):** Adjustable cams on the paper carriage automatically switch on a power drive that engages a tab stop and releases the carriage step control, with write keys locked during the jump.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulation needs manual triggering or a partly toothed rack that must be replaced to change column layout.

**Solution:** Adjustable cams on the paper carriage automatically switch on a power drive that engages a tab stop and releases the carriage step control, with write keys locked during the jump.
