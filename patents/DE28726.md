> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENTAIV! T ... The purpose of the new holder is to hold the outer ends of tape or cord which are wound on reels and to prevent the unwinding of the tape or cord from this reel. Usually this is achieved by inserting pins into the ends of the tape or cord, which fastening method, however, is inadequate, inconvenient and damaging to the tape or cord material. The new holder consists of the combination of a peculiarly shaped wire clip with a roll of special shape, as will be apparent from the following description.
