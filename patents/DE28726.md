## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holding devices to prevent the wound material from unwinding

**How it works (reading):** A wire clip with cranks and hooks engages grooves in a grooved sleeve reel and presses the tape against the roll with a cross wire.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tape or cord on a reel must be held at its outer end, and pins are inadequate and damage the tape (off-topic: not a typewriter).

**Solution:** A wire clip with cranks and hooks engages grooves in a grooved sleeve reel and presses the tape against the roll with a cross wire.
