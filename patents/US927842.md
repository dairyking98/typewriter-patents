## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine with dial plate) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil-cutting machines need the pointer and tool-holder to be driven together and the tool-holder to be locked, which earlier machines did not provide well.

**Solution:** A stationary dial-plate with a pointer, driven by a hand ring on a geared shaft that turns the tool-holder simultaneously; a locking device holds the tool-holder during the foot-lever cut.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Dial or knob](../features/sel-dial.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
