## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Type levers for typewriters The type levers for typewriters generally have a shape according to Figs. i and 2 of the drawing, d. H. the cross-sections perpendicular to the elongation of the type lever taper from the point of oscillation of the lever in the type lever assembly to the type lever head and the types wearing the other end. The essentially rectangular cross-section shows its smallest size there, -, before the type lever head attaches to the shaft, at a point that does not coincide with the point at which the type lever heads are bent so that the types are in the correct position on the paper or platen hit the paper. The type lever head itself again has larger cross-sectional dimensions on because the type lever body is attached to it and a guide part is also arranged must be, with the help of which the type lever just before the stop against the platen is guided in a guide fork. The bend of the type lever head opposite the continuation of the lever must have the greatest accuracy in terms of position, direction and angle, because of her, the correct position of the types to the rotary roller and the characters on the paper is dependent.…
