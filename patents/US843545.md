## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing maro chines, and more especially to tabulating and carriage-stop mechanism for such machines,- and the object of this invention is to provide improved devices of the sort specified.

**How it works (reading):** Supports for the carriage rails carry a long tabulator bar mounted in the frame, and a margin-stop bar mechanism releases the carriage from the margin-stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A wide-carriage machine needs a long tabulator bar and a way to release the carriage from the margin-stops.

**Solution:** Supports for the carriage rails carry a long tabulator bar mounted in the frame, and a margin-stop bar mechanism releases the carriage from the margin-stops.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
