## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**How it works (reading):** An overboard guide rod adjusted vertically in upright standards, with a slid frame so the machine can rock while writing on a book.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The machine is hard to hold firmly on a book, and adjusting the guide rod and frame is awkward.

**Solution:** An overboard guide rod adjusted vertically in upright standards, with a slid frame so the machine can rock while writing on a book.

**Features:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)
