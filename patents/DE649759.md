## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Type lever drive for typewriters with the type lever in the second part of its Movement is accelerated by an intermediate lever.

**How it works (reading):** Two intermediate levers, one giving normal acceleration and one a second boost via pins in slots of the typebar, change engagement at a set point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar acceleration by a single intermediate lever changes its pivot abruptly, and the typebar cannot be easily removed.

**Solution:** Two intermediate levers, one giving normal acceleration and one a second boost via pins in slots of the typebar, change engagement at a set point.

**How it works:** Pins at the end of the intermediate lever engage slots in the typebar; the engagement switches as the line through both pivots is crossed.
