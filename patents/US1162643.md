## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon mechanism for addressing machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism', and is designed more-especially for use in connection with printing or addressing machines in which the impressions are made 'from individual printing or record plates Y through an inling ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** A tubular shaft turned step by step carries clutch members, and pawls on each spool engage them under control of a weighted member, so each spool is driven and the ribbon moves in the required direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools on addressing or printing machines need to be fed step by step and reversed reliably.

**Solution:** A tubular shaft turned step by step carries clutch members, and pawls on each spool engage them under control of a weighted member, so each spool is driven and the ribbon moves in the required direction.
