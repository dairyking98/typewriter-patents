## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism', and is designed more-especially for use in connection with printing or addressing machines in which the impressions are made 'from individual printing or record plates Y through an inling ribbon.
