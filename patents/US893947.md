## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars and keys are fixed in the frame, so changing the language or type design is hard, and curved segments with bent bars waste space and risk misalignment.

**Solution:** Mount the typebars and key levers on one flat plate that slides vertically as the shift and lifts out as a unit, so a plate with another type set can be substituted.

**How it works:** A flat supporting plate slides in ways in the frame below the platen and carries type bar hangers staggered in rows at successively greater distances and different angles from the printing point, so straight unbent bars nest in parallel when at rest yet each has a clear path to the print point. Key levers pivot on a bar hung from the plate and act through a crank arm and link on the typebar; a restoring spring and a universal bail follow. A shift key links to a counterbalanced balancing bar whose latch arms engage slots in the plate and raise or lower the whole plate to change case. The key lever tail also works a ribbon vibrator, and the plate unlatches to be removed bodily with its bars and keys.
