## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The rear presser roller support must grip the sheet against the platen when the paper is first inserted.

**Solution:** The presser roller is carried on leaf spring arms in the paper carriage so it presses the paper against the platen at the advance edge.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
