## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (writing device for calculating machines)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to On calculating machines that are operated by a handle, crank or the like, and especially on calculating machines with a rotating drive drum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Not stated in the excerpt; the stated object is a printing device for calculating machines.

**Solution:** A printing device for a ten-key calculating machine driven through intermediate gears from the counting drum, with a non-push button to disable printing and a total key to obtain the result.

**How it works:** Intermediate gears on the counting drum (driven by the hand crank) move the printing device; the excerpt does not detail the impression stroke.
