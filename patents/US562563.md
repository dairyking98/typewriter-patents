## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator of a rapid-writing machine should be able to key continuously while composing, printing and distributing happen automatically elsewhere.

**Solution:** A keyboard-controlled pin cylinder aligns a line of type-bars on a semicylinder, which then prints on a self-spacing carriage and is distributed while the next line is composed.

**How it works:** A rotating cylinder carries vertical rows of pins that keyboard-controlled means project inward as they pass a point, remaining projected until just before they pass it again. A semicylinder holding type-bars, each with full alphabets and punctuation, is lowered into the pin cylinder where the projected pins catch and hold the bars. The bars are locked and the holder rises, carrying the aligned line to the print point, and rotating past it impresses the line on paper on a carriage momentarily geared to it. The carriage then flies back and turns for the next line, and the bars are released to be re-set. Much of the machine is electrically controlled.
