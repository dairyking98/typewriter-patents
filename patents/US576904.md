## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter carriage on rails tends to wobble, and friction should be minimal.

**Solution:** Carriage rail rides on balls between a fixed rail and a spring-metal tension-bar, so the balls are under constant spring force.

**How it works:** A stationary grooved rail with convex edges is fixed behind the carriage. A movable rail is pivoted on arms to the carriage and moves with it. Two balls, located by pins, sit between the stationary and movable rails near the ends; a single ball sits between the movable rail and an adjustable spring-metal tension-bar at the middle. The tension-bar, adjusted by set-screws in slots, presses the single central ball with constant force, which takes up play and stops wobble.
