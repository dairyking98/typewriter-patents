## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must stop gradually at the end of its run and then stop exactly at a predetermined tab position.

**Solution:** A tabulating attachment whose resilient buffer relieves carriage impact, with automatic apparatus that positively stops the carriage at a set position and re-connects the feed only there.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
