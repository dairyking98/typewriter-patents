## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Small or stiff labels cannot be held against the platen to write on, especially at the margin.

**Solution:** A resilient metal strip runs from a fixed frame on the carriage and hooks into a radial slot in the platen, holding the work against it.

**How it works:** The coiled strip stays under tension, unrolls as the platen turns, and keeps the work-piece held while writing.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
