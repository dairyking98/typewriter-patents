## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a method of processing silver photographic images On plates, films, etc., which consists in the fact that the silver image after Bleach is toned with a solution of titanium chloride or titanium trichloride. This Method can also be applied to an image colored with a non-yellow dye will. Furthermore, the method can be applied to photographic plates, films or the like different but not yellow colored silver images applied on both sides by toning one or both of the images. For example, in carrying out the invention in practice, the print or positive is placed in a bleach bath containing about 0.5% chromic acid and 0.5% potassium ferricyanide for about 2 minutes. This solution can be prepared by mixing equal amounts of a 1 percent chromic acid solution and a 1 percent potassium ferricyanide solution or other suitable reagents. After the film has been removed from this bath, it is rinsed in water, treated for about 3 minutes in an 1% sodium bisulfite solution and, if possible, washed in running water for about 10 minutes.
