## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Accounting machine The invention relates to the device for moving of the paper cart of an accounting machine. When preparing certain types of invoices, such as Telephone, gas, electricity and the like, it is often necessary to submit a Amount. to be entered several times on different sections of the invoice. Some these sections go to accounting, and one or more other sections the customer receives. These doubles are usually made in the manner that the machine makes automatic entries in series, during which the paper trolley across the machine in a certain direction from a column is transferred intermittently to the next. Before the next series of entries are made the carriage must be returned to its starting position across the machine and to be switched forward by one line spacing, whereupon di? second series of entries can begin. The return of the car across the machine is relatively easy slowly in front of you. The time required for this is wasted.
