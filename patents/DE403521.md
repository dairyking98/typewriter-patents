> Description Translated from German Ribbon switching device for typewriters. The invention relates to a manually switchable ribbon switching device for typewriters, at which the ribbon when moving in one direction with the upper edge, when moving in the other direction, the lower edge comes into the impression position, and consists in that the switching rod in the middle has a crank which a slide rests on, on which the ribbon fork lifter by means of a changeover slide brought into different attack positions against the end of the connecting lever can be. In this way, a safe switchover is achieved with simple means, independent of the other drive mechanisms.
