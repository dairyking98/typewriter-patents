## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**How it works (reading):** An addendum to patent 364327: a switch lock lever under the carriage guide is swivelled away by a wedge-shaped contact piece under the carriage guide when the carriage is pushed in, disengaging the carriage's teeth from its rack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The paper carriage can be lifted out of its guide while in use and the carriage switching teeth must be released correctly when pushing the carriage in.

**Solution:** An addendum to patent 364327: a switch lock lever under the carriage guide is swivelled away by a wedge-shaped contact piece under the carriage guide when the carriage is pushed in, disengaging the carriage's teeth from its rack.
