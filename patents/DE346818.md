## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** A pivoting paper table or guide lifts the webs off part of the platen so they bend through only a small arc (about 40 to 60 degrees), loosening them so carbon sheets can be withdrawn.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With carbon sheets and round platens, the paper webs grip the carbon sheets so they cannot be slid back to unwritten areas.

**Solution:** A pivoting paper table or guide lifts the webs off part of the platen so they bend through only a small arc (about 40 to 60 degrees), loosening them so carbon sheets can be withdrawn.
