## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**How it works (reading):** The typewriter prints mirror characters directly: its type carriers carry mirror-image types (or readable types arranged to print mirror images), a switch reverses the platen rotation for the line feed, and mirror reading devices at the type stop positions show a correct image for checking.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Mirror-image text for direct planographic printing is made by first typing a readable text on a transfer plate and reprinting it, or by typing on thin plates that cannot be reused.

**Solution:** The typewriter prints mirror characters directly: its type carriers carry mirror-image types (or readable types arranged to print mirror images), a switch reverses the platen rotation for the line feed, and mirror reading devices at the type stop positions show a correct image for checking.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen; reversible platen drive switch; mirror-image type carriers; mirror reading devices
