## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Addressing machines need metal plates embossed with names and addresses, with the die carriers stopped at the right position by keys.

**Solution:** Rotary die carriers stopped by key-controlled stops bring a chosen pair of dies to the stamping point, and the stamp fires immediately once the carriers settle.

**Features:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
