## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special purpose keyboards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements on keyboards for type-writing, type-setting, linotype, and matrix-making machines, rbc.

**How it works (reading):** A keyboard divided into sections with several alphabets in parallel rows, frequent letters near the centre, and keys that select two or more letters with one movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single alphabet of keys gives one letter per movement and does not let frequent letters or words be selected quickly.

**Solution:** A keyboard divided into sections with several alphabets in parallel rows, frequent letters near the centre, and keys that select two or more letters with one movement.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
