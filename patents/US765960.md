## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists of means whereby the operation of all or any plurality of the key-levers being first slightly depressed individually by the operator will be completed by a single electromagnct located under the key-levers.
