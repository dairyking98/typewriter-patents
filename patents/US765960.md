## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists of means whereby the operation of all or any plurality of the key-levers being first slightly depressed individually by the operator will be completed by a single electromagnct located under the key-levers.

**How it works (reading):** One electromagnet under all key levers completes each key stroke after the operator makes a slight initial depression.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operating key levers is laborious and impressions are uneven.

**Solution:** One electromagnet under all key levers completes each key stroke after the operator makes a slight initial depression.

**How it works:** The typewriter stands on a chambered sub-base holding an upright electromagnet whose pivoted armature plate spans the key levers. Each key lever carries a pendent spring-loaded hook that, after slight initial depression, engages the armature edge. A rock-shaft bar bearing under the key levers closes a contact at the same moment, energizing the magnet, which pulls the armature and completes the stroke; the contact breaks before the end of travel. The machine can also be used normally.
