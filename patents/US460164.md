## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in check-punches, and has for its object to simplify the construction of said devices and to greatly facilitate the adaptation of the same in practical use.

**How it works (reading):** A spindle-swivelled die plate and pivoted plate carry the punches, operated by a lever, to simplify the construction.
