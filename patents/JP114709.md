## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A motor-driven Japanese-script printing machine must move its parts to position and return positively, and vary strike force to character density.

**Solution:** A differential between a one-way motor shaft, with a brake and belt-tightening roller worked by the keys, drives the shaft forward or back and sets its speed by key pressure.

**How it works:** A differential has a driving bevel gear, a driven gear on the output shaft and a carrier. Belts from a double pulley on the motor shaft drive the gear (tight) and the carrier (slack). With the key up a brake holds the carrier and the output shaft turns one way (return). Pressing a key lifts the brake and tightens the carrier belt, so the output turns the other way and moves the parts to the type and the print lever works. Light key pressure lets the carrier slip, lowering speed and striking force.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. A power control device for a Japanese-script printing machine in which a differential gear is coupled to the rotating shaft; its driving bevel gear and its carrier are each connected by a belt to a double pulley on the driving shaft; and, separately, a brake and a belt-tightening roller worked by the keys are provided, so that each time a key is pressed the brake on the carrier is released and at the same time the carrier's belt, which is purposely mounted slack, is tightened. The object: by pressing and releasing a key, to drive the rotating shaft forward or backward, so that both the movement of the machine's moving parts to the required position and their return to the original position are positive; and further, to regulate the speed of the shaft's forward rotation by the pressure on the key, so that the working speed of the printing lever, and hence the striking force, can be varied deliberately to suit how dense or open the strokes of the character are.
