## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal objects of the invention are to so connect the key-action and ribbon mechanism that the least power is utilized in the operation of the ribbon, thereby insuring a light touch and rapid easy operation of the machine;

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon feed moves the ribbon lengthwise, and a vibrator with no working joints lifts it into and out of the printing point, so the key action does not carry the ribbon work; a printing-point index can be operated to show the print point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon feed and ribbon vibration load the key action, and the ribbon-vibrating linkage has working joints that cost power and make the touch heavy.

**Solution:** A ribbon feed moves the ribbon lengthwise, and a vibrator with no working joints lifts it into and out of the printing point, so the key action does not carry the ribbon work; a printing-point index can be operated to show the print point.

**How it works:** Key action drives a ribbon vibrator placed beneath and beside the platen; the vibrator is free of joints and can be operated after each impression or by a separate key for a heavier ribbon.
