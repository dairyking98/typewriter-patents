## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** In the shorthand typewriter of DE607965, each type body carries three characters one above another (top, main and bottom line), and two of the three need a shift key in addition to the type key, costing time.

**Solution:** The types are mounted individually on the type bodies, each moved by a linkage from its own part of a split key, so the upper, middle and lower signs reach their line without any shift.

**How it works:** Each type body carries three separate types one over another. The three parts of each button are tied by rods to the three types, so the key part pressed slides its type to the upper, main or lower line height before the strike. No shift key is needed, and since each character need exist only once, more shorthand signs fit on the bodies. The flanks of the printing parts of the types, strongly bevelled per DE607965, are now concave on one or both sides, for a clearer impression of the characters added to indicate a spoken sign.
