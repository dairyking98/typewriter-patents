## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Two-section ribbons waste ribbon and need to be switched off for stencil cutting.

**Solution:** A ribbon feed driven from the spring drum whose drive gear slides on a shaft to disconnect the feed.

**How it works:** Spools sit at each side above the top plate with bevel gear drives to a ribbon shaft. A ratchet wheel on the spring drum shaft turns with the drum by a pawl and gives the feed. A pinion on the driving shaft slides along it, kept in drive by a finger in a notched flange, to break the feed. A forked arm moves the gear. A shifting shaft can reverse the ribbon by connecting alternately to the spools. The ribbon is split lengthwise into fields of different colors.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; bevel pinion; ratchet wheel; spring drum; sliding gear; forked arm; ribbon driving shaft; escapement wheel; dog rocker
