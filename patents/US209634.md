## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are bulky, and letters of unequal width are spaced uniformly.

**Solution:** Type-rods swing type-arms up to the cylinder, self-inking on the way, and a lug on each arm sets the stroke of a pawl so the cylinder turns by the letter's width.

**How it works:** Pressing a rod's button raises its type-arm, which passes an inking roller on its way to the cylinder. A pin on each arm fixes how far a pivoted arm with a pawl lifts, and the pawl turns a ratchet and, through a pinion and gears, the cylinder by a letter-specific amount. Two inking rollers of different diameters are geared so the inking roller meets a fresh surface. The paper gripper is adjustable and a revolving sweep cleans type.
