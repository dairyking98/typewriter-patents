## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a book typewriter the flat platen blocks access to the book underneath.

**Solution:** A flat platen is carried on a support that shifts transversely beneath the track frame and sits in different horizontal planes.

**How it works:** The Donning book typewriter has a track-frame carrying the traveling printing mechanism, a book support, and a flat platen above the book. The platen is mounted so it can be slid sideways out from under the track frame, giving access to the book and its pages, and its support allows it to be set in one plane when under the frame and another when moved away.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** track frame; flat platen; book support; platen support; printing mechanism

**Improves on:**

- [US826482](../patents/US826482.md): Donning book typewriter; platen now shifts out from under the track frame
- [US840203](../patents/US840203.md): Donning book typewriter series
