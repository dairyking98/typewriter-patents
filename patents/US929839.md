## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to {iat-platen type- Writers, and more particularly to automatic locking means for turni ig or tilting parts thereof'.

**How it works (reading):** A platen lock secures the tilting platen in its horizontal position against the tension of an elevating spring, and the track rail sections are raised by a similar spring when the traveling machine is removed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A flat platen must be raised and held automatically once released, and the inventor wants a locking method for the tilting parts.

**Solution:** A platen lock secures the tilting platen in its horizontal position against the tension of an elevating spring, and the track rail sections are raised by a similar spring when the traveling machine is removed.
