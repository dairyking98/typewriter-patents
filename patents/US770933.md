## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of this invention is auto matically to lock or arrest the paper-feed at time such as will prevent the bottom edge of the paper from being fed to or beyond the printing-line or impression-point of the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A lock arrests the paper feed automatically before the bottom edge reaches the printing line, and is adjustable to stop the feed after a chosen number of line-space steps.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a roller platen the operator may keep typing after the bottom edge of the paper has passed the printing point, unnoticed, soiling the platen and wasting time.

**Solution:** A lock arrests the paper feed automatically before the bottom edge reaches the printing line, and is adjustable to stop the feed after a chosen number of line-space steps.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
