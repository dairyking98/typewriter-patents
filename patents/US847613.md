## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The escapement and carriage-feed parts are spread through the machine, so repairing or replacing any of them means disassembling other parts.

**Solution:** All escapement and carriage-feed parts are mounted on an independent frame that is detachably fixed in the machine, so the whole assembly can be removed and replaced as one unit.

**How it works:** A frame carries the escapement and feed mechanism and is screwed to the machine frame; removing it takes the mechanism out as a whole.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** independent frame; escapement; carriage-feed mechanism; trip-escapement
