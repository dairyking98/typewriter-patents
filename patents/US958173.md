## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing cannot be set to move the paper one or more line spaces per operation as desired, and the platen can overrun.

**Solution:** A feed pawl and ratchet on the platen shaft are driven by a lever, and an adjustable pawl travel regulates the distance the paper moves each operation; a spring returns the lever.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen shaft; platen; feed ratchet wheel; feed pawl; lever; spring; platen carriage
