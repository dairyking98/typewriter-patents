## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen carriage of a Remington-type machine: paper of any width should be usable, the platen easily swapped for manifolding, and paper held and fed properly.

**Solution:** A platen carrier rocking on the carriage holds a detachable platen on end ribs, with spring-pressed back and front feed rollers.

**How it works:** A rock shaft in the main carriage lifts a platen carrier of back rod, front bar and U-shaped end bars. The front is supported on a roll on a cylindrical shift-rail connected to the shift key, moving it for case shift, and pins between stop screws limit the movement. The platen rests on ribs on the end bars and can be detached and replaced by another. A feed roller on swinging links is pressed against the platen by curved springs hooked to the front bar. Two small front rollers on spring-loaded hinged brackets hold the paper, the spring tension set by a screw.
