## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (registration device for measured quantities) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bodily-movable mechanisms for print heads or carriages movable towards or from paper surface; Exchanging means for recording surfaces

**How it works (reading):** A driven roller and spring-loaded counter-rollers are placed between the strip-feed and the container to guide the strip into a tighter roll.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Recorder strips that run off loosely into a collecting container get bunched up and cannot be pulled tight.

**Solution:** A driven roller and spring-loaded counter-rollers are placed between the strip-feed and the container to guide the strip into a tighter roll.
