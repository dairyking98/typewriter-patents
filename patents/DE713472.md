**How it works (reading):** Drive members of the feed device lift the pressure rollers when actuated, so the strip can advance after labeling.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-line writing sheets could not be advanced by a sheet length, because pressure rollers lifted with every carriage return.

**Solution:** Drive members of the feed device lift the pressure rollers when actuated, so the strip can advance after labeling.

**How it works:** Feed drive and pressure-roller lift are interlocked so the strip can be moved a full sheet length after multi-line writing.
