## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-colour ribbon machines needed extra or detachable parts to run either two narrow ribbons or one wide ribbon, and the colour-change controls were awkward and did not always return to the same place.

**Solution:** One vibrating ribbon guide and one pair of spools carry either two narrow ribbons edge to edge or one wide ribbon, and a half-turn shaft or single key moves the vibrator lever's fulcrum to choose the upper or lower ribbon.

**How it works:** A sheet-metal vibrator has two guide openings each side for narrow ribbons, plus tall slots for one wide ribbon. A universal bar lifts the vibrator on each stroke through a lever and link. The lever pivots on a sliding fulcrum yoke with two pivot holes, and moving the yoke shortens or lengthens the throw so the vibrator presents the upper or the lower ribbon, or ribbon half, to the type. The yoke is moved by a rod linked to a lever, driven by an eccentric pin on a fore-and-aft shaft; each half turn swings it between two dead-centre, locked positions. A ratcheted rack bar on a ribbon-change key turns the shaft half a turn per press, with a spring-loaded detent and a stop wheel, and an upper/lower indicator handle sits on the shaft end. Separator disks on the spools and fixed ribbon guides with movable separators keep two narrow ribbons from overlapping.
