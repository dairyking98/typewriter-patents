## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**How it works (reading):** A one-sided rubber hollow body is pressed into each key capsule, and a metal ring closes a second rubber insert, which traps compressed air for a soft sealed key surface.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hard keys tired the writer's fingertips, and rubber caps glued on the key were too soft, too fragile, or did not hold air.

**Solution:** A one-sided rubber hollow body is pressed into each key capsule, and a metal ring closes a second rubber insert, which traps compressed air for a soft sealed key surface.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
