## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (multiple print cash register forms)

**How it works (reading):** Overlapping documents are fed into a fixed paper guide against a movable stop that is withdrawn at start, a conveyor draws them in so the top one prints first and then moves them back out one by one for printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing several copies of a shipping form on a cash register requires either sliding forms across the machine or bending receipts, and earlier designs were complicated.

**Solution:** Overlapping documents are fed into a fixed paper guide against a movable stop that is withdrawn at start, a conveyor draws them in so the top one prints first and then moves them back out one by one for printing.
