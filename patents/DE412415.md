## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning a platen-shifted carriage to its lower position needs help, but stored energy must not make the shift key heavy.

**Solution:** A recoil spring that stores energy only near the end of the lifting stroke, so shifting stays light and the return is accelerated.

**How it works:** The shift key lifts the carriage on a sliding rail via levers. A counterbalance spring is relaxed during most of the lift and is re-tensioned slightly at the end by a tensioning lever whose pin moves in an inclined slot, which also cushions the key. Additional springs with loops catch on an adjustable eccentric only in the last part of lift, so they are tensioned just before release. A bolt latch holds the carriage in the lower position.
