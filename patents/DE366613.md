## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a tabulator for typewriters, which compared to the well-known tabs through greater simplicity in construction and characterized in effectiveness and good clarity.

**How it works (reading):** Tab stops are slid along a tab rail on the carriage, each with a pawl engaging teeth; a lever releases the carriage brake and the carriage runs left until a stop on the tab meets the lever nose and holds it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known tabulators were complicated; the inventor wants a simpler, clearer tabulator for forms.

**Solution:** Tab stops are slid along a tab rail on the carriage, each with a pawl engaging teeth; a lever releases the carriage brake and the carriage runs left until a stop on the tab meets the lever nose and holds it.
