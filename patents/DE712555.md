## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type setting device with type support attached to a fixed part of the typewriter frame.

**How it works (reading):** A holder for the type is clamped between jaws on the typewriter's type lever segment, swivels about the clamp axis, and adjusts in depth and about a vertical axis.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type attachment devices for setting isolated types were either hard to fit (platen had to come off) or too complex and heavy.

**Solution:** A holder for the type is clamped between jaws on the typewriter's type lever segment, swivels about the clamp axis, and adjusts in depth and about a vertical axis.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type holder; clamp jaws; clamp screw; vertical swivel axis
