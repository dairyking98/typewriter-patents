## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to Paper guiding devices for typewriters, in which feed rollers or Pressure rollers attached to the platen by tension springs connected to a cross bar .

**How it works (reading):** The tension springs hook onto a rotatable crossbar with right- and left-hand threads, so all springs can be set at once or individually.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Adjusting the spring tension of the lower feed rollers means tightening each spring separately, which is slow and needs a fine feel.

**Solution:** The tension springs hook onto a rotatable crossbar with right- and left-hand threads, so all springs can be set at once or individually.
