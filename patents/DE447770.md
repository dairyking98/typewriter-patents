## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (power drive switch and numeric setting)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device that is through extreme simplicity and cheapness of manufacture and absolutely reliable mode of operation excels.

**How it works (reading):** Upward movement of the stop rods of the number keys, which is the decimal tabulator-like stroke, both switches on the power drive and sets the setting wheel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Motor start and setting of the adjusting wheel are done by cams and racks, which are complicated, costly and unreliable.

**Solution:** Upward movement of the stop rods of the number keys, which is the decimal tabulator-like stroke, both switches on the power drive and sets the setting wheel.
