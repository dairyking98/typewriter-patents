## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (address printing plate of sheet metal) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes; Applications of printing surfaces in addressing machines or like series-printing machines; Materials therefor metallic for relief printing or intaglio printing

**How it works (reading):** Flanged edges stiffen sheet-iron plates (0.26 to 0.38 mm) so they can be embossed repeatedly without warping, like zinc.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Zinc address plates need a separate leveling pass after stamping, and plain iron plates warp and cannot be restamped.

**Solution:** Flanged edges stiffen sheet-iron plates (0.26 to 0.38 mm) so they can be embossed repeatedly without warping, like zinc.
