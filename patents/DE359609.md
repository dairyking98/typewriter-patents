## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening devices embodied in machines

**How it works (reading):** The support bracket is hung on arms and springs from the type basket plate, so it gives way when the type lever falls back.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers strike a rigid support bracket and produce noise when they return.

**Solution:** The support bracket is hung on arms and springs from the type basket plate, so it gives way when the type lever falls back.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)

In contrast, the subject matter of the present invention achieves the substantial Advantage that caused by the knocking back of the type lever on the support bracket Noises are completely or almost completely suppressed. The invention itself consists essentially in the fact that the support bracket for the type lever is not more, as before, rigidly connected to the type basket plate, but resilient to the Type basket plate or the type basket is suspended in such a way that it falls back of the type lever previously brought to the stop is able to give way.
