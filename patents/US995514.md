## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Long cards must be written through more than a full platen turn, and fixed clamps on the platen leave a raised surface.

**Solution:** A radial clamp in the platen slot grips the card edge, releases automatically when a set position is reached, and retracts flush with the platen during line feed.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen; clamp; slot; card edge
