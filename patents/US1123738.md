## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typed messages need to be enciphered in the typewriter, and codes must be carried compactly.

**Solution:** An attachment with slide pieces and a removable code strip converts letters to cipher as they are typed, and can decipher as well.

**How it works:** A removable code strip and letter block are mounted over the carriage; the converted characters are typed directly.
