## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Throwing the rack-bar into and out of engagement with the pinion should be simpler and cheaper.

**Solution:** Spring-loaded pins in thimbles on the carriage that let the rack-bar be moved into and out of mesh with the actuating pinion.

**How it works:** The carriage rear bar has thimbles with spring-loaded pins attached to the rack-bar; cone-headed pins with beveled flats let a slide shift the rack away from the feed pinion at line end so a spring or weight returns the carriage.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
