## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A light portable front-strike machine needs a ribbon that can cover and uncover the printing point and a foldable frame.

**Solution:** Foldable platen carriage and ribbon assembly with a ribbon moved sideways and lengthwise on each keystroke, with multi-field ribbons.

**How it works:** Typebars each carry three characters, and the platen, carriage, escapement and ribbon mechanism move vertically to select the character. The ribbon moves lengthwise and laterally with each key to cover and uncover the printing point, and can have zones in different colors. The platen assembly folds over close to the keys for transport. Key levers are stamped sheet metal hung from bars; the frame is light sheet metal.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** key lever; type bar; platen; carriage; ribbon guide; ribbon spool; spring drum; ball race; frame

**Numbers:** three characters per bar; three key lever banks

**Improves on:**

- Corona foldable front-strike machine (Frank S. patent): adds lateral ribbon movement and multi-colour ribbon fields
