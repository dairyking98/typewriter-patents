## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide improved means for automatically looking out or disconnecting the step-bystep mechanism until the carriage reaches a normal position in relation to the selective spacing mechanism, thus making it impossible for the step-by-step mechanism to stop the carriage in an improper position.

**How it works (reading):** Cushioning between the carriage and an element connected to it, where that element is arrested at the stop and held against rebound, so the carriage is cushioned without slowing it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Long carriage travel into an adjustable stop causes severe shock, and slightly elastic stops or brakes defeat the purpose of selective spacing.

**Solution:** Cushioning between the carriage and an element connected to it, where that element is arrested at the stop and held against rebound, so the carriage is cushioned without slowing it.
