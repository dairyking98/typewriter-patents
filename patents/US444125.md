## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix impressing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide a machine for impressing matrices that shall be simple in structure and easyrand efficient in operation;

**How it works (reading):** A matrix machine in which compressed air drives a type-carrier on a piston rod and a hammer, with cocks opened and closed by operating rods.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The machine needs to be simple and efficient for impressing matrices.

**Solution:** A matrix machine in which compressed air drives a type-carrier on a piston rod and a hammer, with cocks opened and closed by operating rods.

**How it works:** Operating rods with knobs open and close the air cocks; a piston in a cylinder moves the type-holder and a piston-hammer impresses it.

- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
