## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fractional line spacing (to print above or below the line) in Forkarth US918308 needed a dog moved by hand in separate steps, and the interval was not adjustable.

**Solution:** A single finger lever, normally centred, swings forward or back to first engage a dog with the line-space ratchet and then turn the platen part of a line space, with adjustable stops.

**How it works:** A finger lever pivoted on the cover plate has a double elbow cam slot. A toothed dog is loosely hung on the platen axle, with its tooth sitting in the slot's central recess out of mesh with the line-space ratchet. Swinging the lever either way first cams the dog radially into the ratchet, then rotates it to turn the platen forward or backward a fraction of a line. On return the dog is disengaged. Adjustable stops set the size of the fraction.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** finger lever; cam slot; dog; tooth; line-space ratchet; platen axle; return spring; adjustable stop; cover plate; detent

**Improves on:**

- [US918308](../patents/US918308.md): one centred lever replaces separate dog manipulation and the interval can be regulated
