## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line spacing should happen automatically when the traveling carriage reaches the end of the line.

**Solution:** An electromagnet closed by a spring circuit-controller at line end pulls an armature that works a pawl on a platen ratchet to turn the platen one line.

**How it works:** The carriage with its platen slides on a guide rod, driven by a motor. Two ratchet wheels with opposed teeth sit on the platen shaft. At line end a spring circuit-controller closes the battery circuit through an electromagnet under an armature on a pivoted arm. The arm moves a rod carrying a pawl on the ratchet, turning the platen the desired distance; a tension spring restores the arm when the magnet releases.
