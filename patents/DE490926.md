**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary functional arrangements, e.g. interlocks

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for preventing a type imprint Typewriters, in which the printing process is carried out.

**How it works (reading):** The plate is lifted into and out of the print point automatically by the carriage movement, using a frame on the paper guide with rubber-faced pressure rails and a V-groove and bolt detent.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manually switched plates that block type imprint in front of the print point are forgotten, so either typing or recording fails.

**Solution:** The plate is lifted into and out of the print point automatically by the carriage movement, using a frame on the paper guide with rubber-faced pressure rails and a V-groove and bolt detent.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** paper guide axis; rail; rubber plate; tube; V-shaped grooves; bolts; knurled screws
