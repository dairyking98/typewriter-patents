## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriter ribbons used only a narrow strip and gave no visible writing line or colour choice.

**Solution:** A ribbon-shift key on the carriage moves the ribbon guide widthwise to several held positions, using the full ribbon width, two colours, or clearing the print point to view.

**How it works:** Two ribbons on spools pass through a sliding ribbon guide connected by wire to a ribbon-shift key lever pivoted on the carriage. A ratchet bar with four teeth holds the key at different heights, selecting which ribbon and which part of its width is at the print point. Fully depressing the key moves the ribbon behind the print point, exposing it, and a pointer shows where the letter will print. Variants add a shift plate controlling feed direction and a separate multiple-copy ribbon mechanism.
