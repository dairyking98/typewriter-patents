## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the spring-driven carriage is stopped by the escapement dogs, the full thrust shocks and can break the small rack teeth and dogs, and the friction stiffens the key action.

**Solution:** A light spirally-grooved wheel meshing with the carriage rack turns as the carriage runs and acts as a governor and cushion between carriage and dogs.

**How it works:** The carriage rack teeth project into the spiral grooves of a wheel on a shaft carrying a ratchet wheel. A key rocks a detent (fixed dog plus movable dog) to release the ratchet, and the spring moves the carriage freely since the light wheel has little inertia. When the dog catches a new ratchet tooth, the groove pitch converts part of the shock into axial thrust on the shaft's cone-screw bearings. Wheels with different groove pitch change the shock and friction on the dogs.

**Features:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** frame; carriage; rack; spirally-grooved wheel; shaft; cone-screw bearing; ratchet wheel; detent; fixed dog; movable dog; key; elbow lever; spring
