## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide simple and'efficient means for effecting a varying lateral movement of the inking-ribbon, as well as a longitudinal movement thereof, so as to use the ribbon both widthwise and lengthwise.

**How it works (reading):** Mechanism that moves the ribbon laterally, with a varying amount, as well as longitudinally, so the ribbon is used across its width and length.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inking ribbon wears in one place and must be used both widthwise and lengthwise.

**Solution:** Mechanism that moves the ribbon laterally, with a varying amount, as well as longitudinally, so the ribbon is used across its width and length.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** inking ribbon; lateral movement mechanism; longitudinal feed
