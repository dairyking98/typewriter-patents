## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists from one the clamping devices for the type levers and the alignment devices for the Type body supporting base plate.

**How it works (reading):** Each lever head is clamped in a fixture and its enlarged projected silhouette is adjusted against platen and baseline marks until correct, making the type-body position independent of the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bodies were aligned only on the machine itself, so type levers could not be swapped between machines of the same model.

**Solution:** Each lever head is clamped in a fixture and its enlarged projected silhouette is adjusted against platen and baseline marks until correct, making the type-body position independent of the machine.
