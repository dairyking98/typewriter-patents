## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type wheel of a time stamp must be held firmly during printing, but its lock makes stepping hard.

**Solution:** A second lock, acting only during the printing stroke and released right after, holds the type wheel firmly; the first lock is a lighter pawl that the switching magnet can overcome.

**Features:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md)

**Parts:** type wheel 1; pawl 4; leaf spring; hammer 8
