## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with substantially horizontally extensible or adjustable parts other than drawers, e.g. leaves

**How it works (reading):** The top plate carrying the typewriter slides back on rollers in grooves over a lower writing plate with wells for pens and ink, so the machine can be pushed away.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter table is too small to leave room for handwriting and other work beside the machine.

**Solution:** The top plate carrying the typewriter slides back on rollers in grooves over a lower writing plate with wells for pens and ink, so the machine can be pushed away.
