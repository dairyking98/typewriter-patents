## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manifolding should be done without handling the carbon paper or copy sheets directly.

**Solution:** A continuous roll of copying paper is carried on an axle in bracket supports that attach to the typewriter frame; a stationary knife is held in slots in the brackets.

**How it works:** The roll feeds in with the copy-producing platen, and the knife on the brackets cuts the copies.
