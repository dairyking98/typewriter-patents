## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed rolls must be released for free paper movement and then held in the released position, which needs simple and efficient means.

**Solution:** Feed-roll release with means that lock the feed rolls in the released position on the carriage, applied to a Monarch machine and adaptable to others.
