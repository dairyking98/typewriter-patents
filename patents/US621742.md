## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in type-writing machines of that class in which the type and other characters are displayed upon a revoluble wheel which is caused to revolve and when necessary to move longitudinally upon its axis through the medium of levers, the free ends of whichconstitute the keyboard.

**How it works (reading):** A heart-shaped ratchet on the type-wheel shaft is gripped at the same moment by a geared front pawl and a spring pawl seated in opposite notches for steady locking, with a feed that can give an extra space when a key and the space bar are struck together, an adjustable bell that also locks the wheel, and a pointer.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Blickensderfer's earlier type-wheel machine (US583840) held the wheel with a ratchet and a rigid rear pawl that could blur the print; it also lacked a double-space on the last letter, an adjustable and locking line-end bell, and several carriage conveniences.

**Solution:** A heart-shaped ratchet on the type-wheel shaft is gripped at the same moment by a geared front pawl and a spring pawl seated in opposite notches for steady locking, with a feed that can give an extra space when a key and the space bar are struck together, an adjustable bell that also locks the wheel, and a pointer.

**How it works:** Key levers swing bails whose sectors rotate the type wheel to the chosen character, then vibrate its shaft to strike the paper. A sector of larger radius drives a pawl down onto a heart-shaped ratchet, arrests it, and drops the pawl into a square seat before the impression; a spring pawl then seats in a seat on the opposite side, so the wheel is locked in both directions. Each key or space bar stores power in a reciprocating feed plate that releases a feed dog on the carriage rack, then the spring advances the carriage one space; a stud on the wheel mechanism lets key and space bar together make a double feed. A bell on a sliding yoke along a square rod rings at an adjustable point, and a second yoke trips a latch that locks the wheel's swing so no further impression can be made. A pointer shows where the next letter will fall and is knocked aside by a pin as the wheel descends.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** type wheel; heart-shaped ratchet; front locking pawl; spring pawl; sector; key lever; bail; feed plate; feed dog; feed rack; space-bar lever; bell; bell yoke; latch bar; pointer; ink roller; stop rod; scale index bar; paper carriage

**Improves on:**

- [US583840](../patents/US583840.md): replaces the ratchet wheel and rigid rear-comb pawl with a geared front pawl plus a spring pawl, avoiding blur; adds double spacing, an adjustable and locking bell, pointer and other details
