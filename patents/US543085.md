## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing two letters at once with both hands needs a keyboard layout where the hands do not cross or collide.

**Solution:** Two separate semicircular series of typebars and key levers, left and right, with alphabets split between them so two letters print simultaneously in line.

**How it works:** A circular top frame carries two semicircular series of typebars on detachable hangers with pointed journals, each series on its own centre so two bars strike the same line. Left-hand keys carry lower-case and upper-case letters and right-hand keys carry matching letters, numerals and punctuation. Pressing a key on each side prints two characters side by side, and a multi-pawl spacing mechanism with a pawl-bearer advances the carriage for the pair. A ribbon holder and mover is also described.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** circular frame; typebar; hanger; key lever; key stem; pawl bearer; pawl; sliding carriage; ribbon holder
