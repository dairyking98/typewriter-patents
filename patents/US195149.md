**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a new and improved type-writing machine by means of which the alphabetical characters of words are printed in ordinary text, with due regard to their dierence in size and the space which they occupythatis, properly spaced, or with proper spaces between the letters of each word and between the words themselvesand with mechanism so arranged .

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing typewriters do not set letters in ordinary text with proper widths and spacing; typewheels need independent type reaching the paper.

**Solution:** A revolving type-wheel carrying type radially in parallel circles, one circle per style, each type pushed out by turning cam rods that ink, print and wipe it.

**How it works:** The wheel carries radial type with each style or size in its own circle parallel to the wheel axis; the wheel is shifted along its axis to bring a style opposite a fixed point. Cam rods journalled in the revolving cylinder turn on their axes, driving each type outward in turn to take ink, strike the paper and meet a wiper. The same cams drive a paper-carrier that stays still until cam contact and then moves while the type is in contact plus a distance to form the space. The spacing can be lengthened or shortened by adjustment.
