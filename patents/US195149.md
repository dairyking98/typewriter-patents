## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using mechanical translation and type-head printing, e.g. type-wheel, type-cylinder

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a new and improved type-writing machine by means of which the alphabetical characters of words are printed in ordinary text, with due regard to their dierence in size and the space which they occupythatis, properly spaced, or with proper spaces between the letters of each word and between the words themselvesand with mechanism so arranged .
