## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing ink colour or ribbon type on a typewriter requires swapping spools.

**Solution:** Two or more ink bands side by side with their spools on a laterally sliding holder, so that a lever shifts the chosen band into the printing line.

**How it works:** Several ink bands run under the platen, each with its own pair of spools, a common arbor and guide passage per end. Holders carrying guides slide in horizontal slots of vertical hangers fixed to the frame ends. Connecting rods and an operating lever slide the holders and spools sideways to bring a chosen band over the type, and the lever is locked in position.
