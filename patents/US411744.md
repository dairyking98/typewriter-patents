## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punching machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in certain novel combinations of mechanical devices and details of construction, as hereinafter more fully set forth, and pointed out in the claims, whereby a simpler, cheaper, and more desirable machine of this character is produced than is now in ordinary use.

**How it works (reading):** A frame with a feed table holds a wheel with flat faces carrying removable die-plates, with a vertically spring-supported shaft carrying the punches.
