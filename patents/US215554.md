## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stenographic writing needs a small set of keys that can be combined, each combination marking a letter.

**Solution:** Two sets of five keys (thumb and four fingers) each connected to a perforating needle or marker; the combination of needles that act indicates the letter, with plates guiding the markers along the ribbon.
