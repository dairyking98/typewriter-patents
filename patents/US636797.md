## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapements on typebar machines double or skip letters during rapid operation.

**Solution:** A positive spacing mechanism with a ratcheted escapement wheel, pinion and screw-wheel gearing, attachable to ordinary type-basket machines.

**How it works:** A pinion on a spindle meshes with the carriage rack; the spindle carries a ratchet wheel and a loosely mounted escapement wheel with a spring-loaded pawl. A second shaft carries a screw-wheel and ratchet wheels engaged by a spring-actuated pawl on a vertical arm on a rock shaft. The rest (key connection) is cut off.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** top plate; carriage; pinion; spindle; escapement wheel; pawl; ratchet wheel; screw-wheel; spring; rock shaft
