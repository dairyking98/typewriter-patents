## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key button for typing, calculating and similar machines or apparatus.

**How it works (reading):** The key top is a plastic mass moulded around a long flat tube that encloses the key lever tightly and is rigidly connected to the key top, giving a simple, replaceable fixing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key buttons were hard to change: labels were fiddly under a cap that broke the cover glass, and pins or cavities gave weak or hard-to-fix attachment.

**Solution:** The key top is a plastic mass moulded around a long flat tube that encloses the key lever tightly and is rigidly connected to the key top, giving a simple, replaceable fixing.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
