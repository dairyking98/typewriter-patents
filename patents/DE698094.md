## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling

**How it works (reading):** One spring returns the actuating button and also presses the rubber lead on the support and readjusts it as it wears; lifting the nut raises the spindle and lead so the erased spot is visible.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known erasers need a separate spring to press the rubber lead against the support, and the erased area and dust are hard to clear without removing the eraser.

**Solution:** One spring returns the actuating button and also presses the rubber lead on the support and readjusts it as it wears; lifting the nut raises the spindle and lead so the erased spot is visible.
