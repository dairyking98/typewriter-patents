## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper transport rollers and their levers made the carriage tall, and the carriage base plate sagged and needed heavy stiffening.

**Solution:** Three shafts for the roller levers and the selector shaft sit close together, parallel to the base plate, and the base plate is bent with side walls and a box stiffener so the group can be assembled as one unit.
