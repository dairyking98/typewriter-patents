## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Hand-operated locks for retractable typewriter tables require one hand to hold the table, and automatic locks were complicated with many parts.

**Solution:** Swinging hooks hold the retractable table in the working position and release it when the table returns; the text is cut off before the detailed mechanism.
