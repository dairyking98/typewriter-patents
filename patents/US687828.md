## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and has for its object to provide a simple and efficient protective plate for the working parts which will, in conjunction with parts of the machine proper, form a complete and practically imperforate cover for the mechanism within the type-dome.

**How it works (reading):** A thin plate covering the type-dome with a small opening at the printing point, closed by the ribbon.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Eraser rubber and paper dust clog the bearings and corrode a typebar machine.

**Solution:** A thin plate covering the type-dome with a small opening at the printing point, closed by the ribbon.

**How it works:** A sheet shaped to the top of the Remington key dome, with a depressed middle zone resting on the ribbon and guide. The aperture is narrower than the ribbon so the ribbon closes it. Recesses fit other parts.

**Features:** [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** protector plate; key dome; ribbon; aperture
