## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ribbon and its shifting mechanism are complicated; a simpler way to ink the type is wanted.

**Solution:** Replace the ribbon with a rotating cloth ink disk lying between type and platen, turned a little by a lug on the paper carriage each time it moves, with an ink-feeding trough on top.

**How it works:** A T-headed stud slides in a T-slot in the top plate parallel to carriage travel. On it sit a thin supporting flange, a cloth disk of ribbon material, and an upper flange with a hub, an annular ink trough with small holes feeding the cloth, and a soft rubber band. A lug under the paper carriage bears on the rubber band so each carriage step rotates the disk, presenting fresh cloth over the printing point. Sliding the stud toward the print point makes the disk overlap more, offering unused annular area. Capillary action spreads ink in the cloth.

**Features:** [INK:disc](../features/ink-disc.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** T-slot; T-headed stud; supporting flange; cloth disk; retaining flange with hub; annular ink trough; feed holes; rubber band; carriage lug

**Improves on:**
