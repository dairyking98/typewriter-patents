## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper-roll attachments for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A paper-roll attachment with an inverted-U frame clamped to the carriage bar, carrying winding shafts with friction disks and ratchet-and-pawl stops so a roll of paper is always ready.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing paper takes time, especially for receiving operators who must clear a heavy workload without swapping sheets.

**Solution:** A paper-roll attachment with an inverted-U frame clamped to the carriage bar, carrying winding shafts with friction disks and ratchet-and-pawl stops so a roll of paper is always ready.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
