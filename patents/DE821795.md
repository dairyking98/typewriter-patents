## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a postcard holder for typewriters. The typewriters of the previous design got caught in the Turning in postcards and envelopes very often the left or right Upper corner of the paper in the postcard holder, which is usually made of wire or thin sheet metal. This disturbance occurs regularly when writing on the upper margin of postcards, envelopes, etc. and usually leads to the uselessness of the clamped paper, but at least to annoying damage and loss of time. This disturbance occurs particularly unpleasantly and persistently with portable typewriters with a small roller diameter. The invention now consists in the postcard holder used hitherto to take this error away by new design. The bracket made of wire or thin sheet metal is used for this purpose of the postcard holder provided with attachable guide plates, which consist of a transparent consist of organic plastic and the outer edges of which have a semicircular profile with a radius dimensioned so that even very stiff card corners do not get caught on the bracket, but by sliding on the curves of the guide plates back to the platen be introduced.
