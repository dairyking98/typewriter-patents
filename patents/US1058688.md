## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type bars needed to be guided accurately to their printing position so that the pressure of the type on the paper could be controlled.

**Solution:** A longitudinal guide plate with notched recesses, held resiliently against fixed bars, guides each type bar to its printing position in front of the platen.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)
