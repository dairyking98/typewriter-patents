**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a thrust abutment for typewriters that consists of one in the range of types arranged rigid bar carried by the paper trolley with a flat stop surface and more hollow-shaped in accordance with the curvature of the rubber roller behind it Back surface.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pressure abutments behind the type cause fatigue of the typist and overload types, ribbon and key linkage.

**Solution:** A rigid stop bar with a flat front and a curved back, mounted on resilient legs or springs, supports the type against the rubber roller while the roller only conveys paper.
