## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing unit for Thomas calculator)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing unit for Thomassche calculating machines with key setting, especially those Thomasscher calculators, in which the staggered rollers are inclined to the machine plate and transfer from takes place on the counter through spur gears.

**How it works (reading):** Stepped sectors on each setting lever limit the stroke of the toothed gears driving the type wheels, and a swinging frame pushes the type wheels against a platen after setting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing unit for key-set calculating machines must print from type wheels that are set by stepped sectors on the setting levers, and zero printing must be handled separately.

**Solution:** Stepped sectors on each setting lever limit the stroke of the toothed gears driving the type wheels, and a swinging frame pushes the type wheels against a platen after setting.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Numbers:** nine steps per stepped sector

Of the The subject of the invention relates to a printing unit for Thomassche calculating machines with key setting, especially those Thomasscher calculators, in which the staggered rollers are inclined to the machine plate and transfer from takes place on the counter through spur gears. With this printing unit is made of the known printing type wheels mounted in a swinging frame Made use, which are struck against a platen after setting. The essence of the invention is that the setting lever 3 for the adjusting wheels on the one hand step or notch sectors ge. according to the numerical values one to nine, which limit the stroke of toothed gears for the printing type wheels, on the other hand cooperate with sliding or rotating pieces 38 of a rail swinging in the machine frame so that when one of the setting levers, 3 arranged on one side of it, is switched Sliding or rotating pieces 38 are brought out of engagement, while the sliding or rotating pieces lying on the other side can be switched.
