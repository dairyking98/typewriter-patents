> Description Translated from German Printing unit for Thomassche calculating machines with key setting. Of the The subject of the invention relates to a printing unit for Thomassche calculating machines with key setting, especially those Thomasscher calculators, in which the staggered rollers are inclined to the machine plate and transfer from takes place on the counter through spur gears. With this printing unit is made of the known printing type wheels mounted in a swinging frame Made use, which are struck against a platen after setting. The essence of the invention is that the setting lever 3 for the adjusting wheels on the one hand step or notch sectors ge. according to the numerical values one to nine, which limit the stroke of toothed gears for the printing type wheels, on the other hand cooperate with sliding or rotating pieces 38 of a rail swinging in the machine frame so that when one of the setting levers, 3 arranged on one side of it, is switched Sliding or rotating pieces 38 are brought out of engagement, while the sliding or rotating pieces lying on the other side can be switched.
