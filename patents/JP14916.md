## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Stamping numerals easily and quickly with a handheld device.

**Solution:** A numeral stamp in which a turnable disc with numerals marked round its edge turns a ring of swinging arms, each with a numeral plate; a central plunger presses the arm at the stamping opening and also feeds the machine along on rollers.

**How it works:** A disc with numerals round its edge is fixed to a hollow shaft, and loosely mounted on that shaft are many swinging arms, each ending in a numeral plate, so they turn with the disc. Turning the disc brings the wanted numeral to an index mark and its plate over an opening in the case bottom, while the plates rub against inking rollers running in ink wells and so are inked. Pressing a button on a plunger inside the shaft pushes a front lever and pusher that swing the arm at the opening down, so its plate stamps through the opening, and a spring rest pushes the arm back. The plunger also presses a second lever whose jointed pusher rod catches a ratchet wheel; on release a spring drives the rod back, turning a roller to move the machine along for the next stamp.

> Okada-type numeral stamping machine.
