## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the paper feeding mechanism of typewriting machines and has for its main object to provide improved devices of the class specified.

**How it works (reading):** Hanger arms carry the paper feed roller, supported on the platen frame, and a platen scale is mounted for line indication. Details are in the figures, which are only partly legible.
