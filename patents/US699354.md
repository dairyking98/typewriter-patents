## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage supports in typewriters have too much friction and wear on the bearing parts.

**Solution:** A carriage runs on two round rack-toothed trackways with detached geared rollers between them, so friction is rolling and shared.

**How it works:** A fixed rod on the frame and a second rod on the carriage, both circular in section with a rack cut in them, sandwich loose bearing rollers that each carry a circular rack meshing both. The rollers keep a fixed relation to the rods, so the carriage moving a distance moves the rollers half that distance. The upper rod pivots on links so the platen can tilt for upper and lower case. A rod and guideways stop lateral drift; abutments on the fixed rod stop the rollers running off. A wide carriage fits without widening the frame.
