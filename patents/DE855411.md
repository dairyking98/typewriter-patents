## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**How it works (reading):** A special rubberised brush rubs a chemical softening paste into the roller so loosened dirt is taken up and the paste softens the rubber to about 2 mm depth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Elastic typewriter rollers harden and lose grip, so typing becomes uneven.

**Solution:** A special rubberised brush rubs a chemical softening paste into the roller so loosened dirt is taken up and the paste softens the rubber to about 2 mm depth.
