## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets must be trimmed at the leading edge so the edges align, which wastes time.

**Solution:** Each carbon has a clear-paper rear extension marked with transverse fold lines matching the spacing of the blade-like arms.

**How it works:** The extension is folded at the line that matches the arm, so the carbon is attached aligned without trimming.
