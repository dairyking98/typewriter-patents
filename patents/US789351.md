## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to producea machine for printing musical characters on a previously-traced staff in a manner to enable the presentation of a musical composition correctly, attractively, and quickly.

**How it works (reading):** A music type-writer with a type-carrier on a stop-carriage, a platen-carriage that moves the music sheet, stop-keys on a laminated block, and an escapement, so the composition prints complete on a pre-traced staff.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing musical notes on a staff needs a full range of music characters and signs without hand lettering or filling in.

**Solution:** A music type-writer with a type-carrier on a stop-carriage, a platen-carriage that moves the music sheet, stop-keys on a laminated block, and an escapement, so the composition prints complete on a pre-traced staff.
