## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters, typewriters, Adding machines or the like: with a carbon paper cart for paper webs, the one with the typewriter cart is releasably connected.

**How it works (reading):** The coupling device and a holding device that grips the stationary frame after uncoupling are linked so that each is controlled by the other, holding the carbon cart in place.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the carbon paper cart is uncoupled from the typewriter cart, writing vibrations can make the free carbon cart move and tip over.

**Solution:** The coupling device and a holding device that grips the stationary frame after uncoupling are linked so that each is controlled by the other, holding the carbon cart in place.
