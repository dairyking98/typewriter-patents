## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case shift on typebar machines used many parts and needed too much force on the shift key.

**Solution:** The whole typebar cradle is raised on friction rollers in runways by a lever from the shift key, with inclined slot bases so the typebars simply rotate slightly.

**How it works:** Typebar heels pivot in slots in a cradle carried on uprights fitted with rollers that run in vertical runways in the frame. Levers pivoted on each side connect to the cradle uprights and, by a link, to the shift key. Depressing the shift key lifts the cradle to bring upper case type to the platen. Inclined slot bases let the typebars rest on a fixed support while the cradle rises, and slotted actuating levers allow the link pins to rise. Releasing the key lets gravity return the cradle.

**Parts:** type bar; type bar cradle; friction roller; runway; shift lever; shift key; link
