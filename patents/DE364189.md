## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Sound-absorbing box for typewriters, calculators or the like. Object The present invention relates to a sound-absorbing case for writing, Calculating machines or the like, and the novelty is seen in the fact that the box is made of consists of two detachably connected parts, one of which is partially can be opened, the type basket and the paper roller and the other covers the keyboard. Dividing the box into two parts means that one and the same model of the the main part covering the type basket and the paper roller for various types of machines can be used. You just need a different stem for the keys be connected to the main box. The arrangement also enables quick control the (main) keyboard and quick repair. By opening the main part it is achieved that disturbances in the type basket and in the paper roller can easily be eliminated.
