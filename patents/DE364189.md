## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter's noise and dust need covering, but the keyboard and mechanism must remain serviceable.

**Solution:** A two-part sound-absorbing box: a hinged main cover over the type basket and paper roller, and a separate normalized keyboard housing fixed by screws, so the same main part fits several machines.
