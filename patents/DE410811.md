## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register electric drive)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register in which directly by pressing down one button the clutch and the current for the one driving the registration device Electric motor can be switched on.

**How it works (reading):** Pressing one amount key switches on the electric drive and engages the main shaft for one full turn; the depressed amount key is then held by a spring-loaded pawl on a pivoting arm until strip printing ends.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The cash register needs its clutch and motor switched on by a single key press, and the amount key must stay down while the control strip is printed.

**Solution:** Pressing one amount key switches on the electric drive and engages the main shaft for one full turn; the depressed amount key is then held by a spring-loaded pawl on a pivoting arm until strip printing ends.
