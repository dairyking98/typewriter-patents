## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**How it works (reading):** Left and right roll parts carry rulings of two different widths, with end rings whose rulings coincide with the other part, so forms of several line widths can be lined up.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A paper roll has a single ruling matched to one index wheel division, so only forms with one line width can be aligned.

**Solution:** Left and right roll parts carry rulings of two different widths, with end rings whose rulings coincide with the other part, so forms of several line widths can be lined up.
