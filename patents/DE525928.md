**How it works (reading):** The counter sits on a fixed main plate, and an adjustable auxiliary plate carries the linking gears between the switching device and the counter, so the drive can be angled for each counter without changing parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing a counter between typewriters with different fonts needs three gear changes, because the drive shaft must be angled to the counter.

**Solution:** The counter sits on a fixed main plate, and an adjustable auxiliary plate carries the linking gears between the switching device and the counter, so the drive can be angled for each counter without changing parts.
