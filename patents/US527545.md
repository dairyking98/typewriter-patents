## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-end bell must ring a fixed interval before the carriage stops even when the usable line length is changed.

**Solution:** Mount the bell, hammer and carriage stop on a plate rotatable about the spring-drum axis so the bell-to-stop spacing stays constant while the whole set shifts.

**How it works:** The carriage strap winds a spring drum, which carries an arm. As the carriage moves left under the spring, this arm first pushes a spring-loaded bell hammer aside, slips past it so the hammer strikes the bell, then meets a fixed stop that halts the drum and carriage. The bell, hammer and stop sit on a toothed plate meshing with a slidable rack. Moving the rack, with a pointer and scale, rotates the plate and shifts the stop point while the bell-to-stop gap remains the same. A ratchet wheel with a dog and handle sets the spring tension, and a second ratchet on the drum can drive ribbon feed.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** spring drum; mainspring; carriage strap; drum arm; bell; bell hammer; stop; toothed plate; adjusting rack; scale and pointer; ratchet wheel and dog
