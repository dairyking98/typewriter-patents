## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The vertically movable platen must sit at exactly the right height so upper and lower case characters print fully and in a straight line.

**Solution:** Close the vertical guide slots of the carriage top and bottom and fit set screws with lock nuts at each end to set the two platen heights.

**How it works:** The platen frame slides in vertical slots in the paper carriage. In the lower position its slide bearings rest on lower adjusting screws; when raised they hit upper adjusting screws. Lock nuts fix the screws, so both shift positions can be set quickly and accurately.
