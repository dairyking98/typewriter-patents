## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relate specially to the construction of the carriage-escapement devices, the tension devices regulating the motor-spring for sliding the carriage, and the arrangement of the stop-pins for limiting the reciprocating movements of the type-cylinder.

**How it works (reading):** A paper carriage with a storage receptacle grooved along its bottom edge to guide the sheet; the escapement, motor-spring tension devices and stop-pins for the rotary sliding type-cylinder are also reworked.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper inserted into the carriage receptacle tends to curl up under the pushing force, and the carriage escapement and motor spring tension need regulation.

**Solution:** A paper carriage with a storage receptacle grooved along its bottom edge to guide the sheet; the escapement, motor-spring tension devices and stop-pins for the rotary sliding type-cylinder are also reworked.
