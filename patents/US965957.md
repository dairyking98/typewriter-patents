## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism for typewriting machines and more particularly to mechanism in which a ribbon having fields of diiferent characteristics may be set to cause either of said fields to be automatically moved into the path of an approaching type in each printing operation.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon mechanism lets the operator set which field of the ribbon is lifted into the type path, or disables the ribbon lift and feed together.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With a multi-colour ribbon it is hard to present a chosen field to the type, and the machine should also be able to cut mimeograph stencils without moving or feeding the ribbon.

**Solution:** Ribbon mechanism lets the operator set which field of the ribbon is lifted into the type path, or disables the ribbon lift and feed together.

**How it works:** On a Monarch machine, ribbon vibrator motion is controlled by a universal bar, with a setting device selecting the field to present. A rotary and longitudinally sliding shaft with bevel pinions engaging pinions on two shafts drives the ribbon feed from the carriage spring drum. A mechanism throws the lift and feed out of action for stencil work, and indicating devices show the setting.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
