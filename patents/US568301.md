## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Opening text gives no explicit problem; it describes a typewriter whose characters are on reciprocating type rods.

**Solution:** Type carried on the ends of inclined reciprocating rods arranged in concentric rings strike through a perforated button onto a platen.

**How it works:** Keys work levers that are wired to inclined type-bars arranged in one or two concentric series forming a cone frustum. Each bar has a vertical extension with one type and slides in a ring frame (or over a cone) that guides it toward a common point. The type moves through a perforated button and a ribbon-supporting plate to the paper on the platen. A rack, worm-wheel and trip on the carriage space the paper, and gearing turns the platen.
