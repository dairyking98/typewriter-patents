**How it works (reading):** Limits the backward and forward roller travel so that a fixed gap or space is left between successive entries on the register sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** When several invoices are posted to one carbon-backed register sheet, the operator must judge the line spacing between bills by hand after turning the roller back.

**Solution:** Limits the backward and forward roller travel so that a fixed gap or space is left between successive entries on the register sheet.

**How it works:** A special crank or button turns the paper roller back to take a new bill and forward again to the writing position; stops fix the travel so a set gap results.
