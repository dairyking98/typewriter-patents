## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools needed a separate pin to hold the ribbon loop and a separate drive, making attachment and loop fixing awkward.

**Solution:** The retaining pin that holds the ribbon loop is extended through the lower spool disc so it also acts as the driving pin engaging the spool carrier; the loop passes through a triangular opening and is slipped behind the pin.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Ribbon spool. There are ribbon reels for typewriters known, that of two circular discs connected by a central axle pin exist, between which the ribbon, the end of which is designed as a loop, with the help of a pen that is held close between the two discs the axis of rotation of the bobbin in the flesh of the connecting bolt forming the bobbin hub is used. The purpose of the invention is to improve this coil by, that the driver pin for the ink ribbon is designed such that it is at the same time can serve as a driver for the ribbon spool that fits into the spool carrier. For this purpose, the driving pin is arranged so that it is always with a End protrudes through the lower spool disc and as a result as a driver engages in the coil carrier. If in execution of this thought the driving pin is arranged on the upper bobbin, then there is still a special one cheap and convenient attachment of the ribbon loop.
