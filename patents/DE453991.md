> Description Translated from German Frame made of sheet metal for teaching aids and children's typewriters. The priority; it of the exhibition on the i. The Leipzig sample fair opened in March 1925 , including the technical fair and construction fair. The invention relates to a frame stamped from sheet metal for teaching aids and children's typewriters. According to the invention, this frame consists of two parts, one of which is used to receive a bearing plate carrying the entire key lever, type lever and switching mechanism and the other to accommodate the entire car structure. The first part consists of a wide sheet metal body bent in a U-shape, the web surface of which has a sector-shaped cutout and the leg surfaces of which have flanges bent inwardly. The keyboard frame plate sits at the front open end of the sheet metal body, and a rectangular back plate with two narrow longitudinal flanges is screwed on at its rear end, which forms the second part of the typewriter frame and carries the guide rails for the carriage.
