## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typist cannot easily close up spaces when a word is inserted, since the carriage only moves in whole letter spaces.

**Solution:** An extra key shifts the escapement rocker on a movable carrier so the carriage is moved a fraction of a letter space and held there while a word is typed.

**How it works:** The rocker that carries the feed dog is pivoted on a slide or carrier moved by a cam-slotted lever linked to an extra key. Pressing the key moves the carrier and turns the escapement wheel partway, which through its pinion shifts the rack and carriage a fraction of a space right against the spring. While the key is held, the word typed is shifted left by that fraction. Fully depressed, the carriage moves half a space, leaving equal space on each side of an inserted word. A speed-adjusting screw and a threaded pivot adjust the parts.
