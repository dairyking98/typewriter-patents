## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bar pivot bearings wear and need anti-friction support.

**Solution:** Crossed circular roller bearings sit in a V-race inside the typebar eye, with a conical bearing on a screw between hanger arms.
