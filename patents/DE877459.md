## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Alarms, indicators, or feed disabling devices responsive to ink ribbon breakage or exhaustion

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to writing tapes, printing blankets, colored cloths, etc.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon edges are pre-dyed in a contrasting colour, so that misplaced type strikes show the contrasting colour at once and the fault is seen early.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon edges are destroyed before a faulty ribbon lifter is noticed.

**Solution:** The ribbon edges are pre-dyed in a contrasting colour, so that misplaced type strikes show the contrasting colour at once and the fault is seen early.
