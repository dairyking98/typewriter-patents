## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing machines are not suited to writing tags and labels, which need a roll of stock and special handling.

**Solution:** A typewriter for writing tags, labels and similar items, with a roll holder or bracket that feeds stock from a roll and a frame that can be supported on a table.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
