## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines; Compression

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to elastic pedestals or cushions to be placed under the frames of writing-machines;

**How it works (reading):** A flexible elastic pedestal or cushion is placed under the machine frame to absorb vibration and yield to each key stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Machine vibration passes into the desk and makes it vibrate with the machine.

**Solution:** A flexible elastic pedestal or cushion is placed under the machine frame to absorb vibration and yield to each key stroke.
