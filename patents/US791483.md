## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-thrown line-space pawl is unreliable and sometimes engages the wrong ratchet tooth.

**Solution:** A positive, hand-actuated crank-finger drives the line-space pawl into the platen ratchet, with adjustable front-elevation parts and a carriage-mounted rail arrangement.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
