## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating typewriter improvement)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an improvement the typewriter protected under patent 37 283.

**How it works (reading):** Calculator number keys act on offset key levers on a drive shaft, linked to the typewriter keys by a lever so striking a typewriter key drags the calculator key but not the reverse.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The transfer between the calculator and typewriter number keys was complicated and costly in the parent design.

**Solution:** Calculator number keys act on offset key levers on a drive shaft, linked to the typewriter keys by a lever so striking a typewriter key drags the calculator key but not the reverse.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
