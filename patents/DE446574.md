## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Key lever cash register with individual amount and total printing device. The invention relates to a key lever cash register with a single amount and total printing device, not only adjust the amount by pressing the button lever, but the cash register is also driven. In the previously known machines of this type, the booking strip is located constantly in tension, which has the disadvantage that to depress the Keys a disproportionately large amount of force is required. According to the invention, this disadvantage is avoided in that the Booking strips are usually kept relaxed and only just before the start of the Pressure is stretched.
