## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Long-platen machines tip sideways when the platen is fully pulled out.

**Solution:** A removable triangular sheet-metal side support is clamped to the frame feet and rests on the table through an adjustable elastic support, also braced against the machine frame.
