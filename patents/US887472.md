**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements to attach the ribbon to the spool

**How it works (reading):** A spool with a longitudinal slot, and a ribbon end with a securing member that crosses the slot and holds the ribbon to the spool, is attached and released simply.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon end must be secured to its spool without rewinding the ribbon.

**Solution:** A spool with a longitudinal slot, and a ribbon end with a securing member that crosses the slot and holds the ribbon to the spool, is attached and released simply.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool with longitudinal slot; securing member on ribbon end; lugs
