## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Scale bars sit low on the carriage for machines whose type-wheel works down on top of the roll, and are hard to fit to different carriages.

**Solution:** An elevated scale bar on slotted standards that clip to the carriage, read against a fixed centre index finger aligned with the print point.

**How it works:** A scale bar is carried above the frame on two standards with threaded ends; slots in the bar let it be shifted so it registers with the platen scale and fits short or long carriages, held by thumb-nuts. The lower ends of the standards have grooves, one engaging a band and rod at the right, the other a rod and carriage standard at the left, one by horizontal and the other by vertical motion so they lock. The bar travels with the carriage while an adjustable index finger on the frame, in the vertical plane of the type-to-paper contact, marks position.
