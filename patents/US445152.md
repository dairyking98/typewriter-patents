## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Moving the carriage by hand for spacing letters or words is slow and hard to control on a Remington.

**Solution:** A thumb-operated rod at the left of the carriage lifts the spacing rack off the dogs through a second rod, freeing the carriage.

**How it works:** A rod hung in bearings on the carriage frame has a finger-piece turned up at one end and the other end turned under so it rests on a second rod attached to the hinged spacing-rack frame. Pressing the finger-piece depresses the second rod's point and lifts the toothed rack off the spacing dogs. The carriage is then free to slide on its rod either way. Releasing the finger-piece lets the rack drop back onto the dogs.
