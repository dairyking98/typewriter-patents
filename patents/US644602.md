## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** Our object is to provide means to facilitate pivoting, adjusting, and maintaining true alinement of type-bars in type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A duplex hanger carries an equalizing lever with journal bearings and two pivots for two type-bars; a screw adjusts the equalizing lever to set the friction of the journals in their bearings.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars are hard to pivot, adjust and keep in true alignment in a type-writer.

**Solution:** A duplex hanger carries an equalizing lever with journal bearings and two pivots for two type-bars; a screw adjusts the equalizing lever to set the friction of the journals in their bearings.
