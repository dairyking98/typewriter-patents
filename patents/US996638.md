## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Margin stops on the carriage often need special release keys and hand resetting.

**Solution:** Line stops that self-release when carriage pressure is relieved and reset when the carriage moves the other way; the right margin is released by a key lever locking bar.

**How it works:** The left stop limits carriage return to the right and retracts when the return pressure is relaxed, so a second push moves the carriage on; moving the carriage the opposite way resets it. The right stop is released through a locking bar mounted on the key levers, so ordinary typing releases it without a special key.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** left line stop; right line stop; locking bar; key lever; carriage shoulder

**Improves on:**

- conventional margin release key: release is automatic with normal key operation
