## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers

**How it works (reading):** The light inking roller that the types strike is made of rubber (or a rubber-covered, sanded wooden or hard-rubber roller), so it stays clean and keeps the type face clean for much longer.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Felt inking rollers that the type levers strike shed fibers and shred over time, leaving debris stuck in the types and blurring the print, so the types need frequent cleaning.

**Solution:** The light inking roller that the types strike is made of rubber (or a rubber-covered, sanded wooden or hard-rubber roller), so it stays clean and keeps the type face clean for much longer.
