## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies on spherical, truncated-spherical, or like surfaces; Others TOP

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** The object is a printing machine that prints the required type accurately and is simple to work.

**How it works (reading):** Replace the cylinder with a ball that turns freely in two directions, with types on its lower half and index hollows on its upper half, so no sliding part is needed.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The cylinder carrier of JP153606 develops slack in its sliding part so the set position wanders, and its front and back types touch and soil the paper.

**Solution:** Replace the cylinder with a ball that turns freely in two directions, with types on its lower half and index hollows on its upper half, so no sliding part is needed.

**How it works:** A ball is pivoted inside a ring that is itself pivoted left and right at the end of a tilting operating frame held raised by a spring. Types stand out from the lower half and matching index hollows lie at the symmetrical positions on the upper half. A small ball on a spring plate drops into a hollow to hold the ball so the chosen type is under its centre. Pressing the operating frame prints that type onto the paper cylinder below. On release the spring lifts the frame and a pawl turns a ratchet and worm that moves a rack on the paper-cylinder frame one character space sideways. Several balls can be kept and exchanged for more characters.
