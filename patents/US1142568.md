## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multicolour ribbons need a vibrator that works the same in both shift positions and that can be silenced, and the line lock must work either way.

**Solution:** A combined ribbon vibrator and line-lock where the vibrator can select either field of a two-colour ribbon or be held out of printing position.

**How it works:** Segmental actuators on the carriage scale bar operate a long-throw lever and vibrator lever that move the ribbon guide to and from the print point. The universal bar actuates the vibrator and escapement. The vibration is kept the same relative to the platen in normal and shifted positions. A line lock blocks the printing mechanism near line end whether the vibrator is active or silenced.
