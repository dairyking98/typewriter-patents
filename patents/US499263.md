## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in attachments for type-writing machines, the purpose of which is to provide means whereby the carriage carrying the printing cylinder may be rapidly moved either way, but particularly from right to left, and through a larger distance at a time than the ordinary spacing mechanism permits, which latter releases the carriage only to the extent of the space which one letter requires.
