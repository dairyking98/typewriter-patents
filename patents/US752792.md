## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Selecting a character by combining rotation of a type carrier with sideways motion of the paper carriage, with only a few key levers.

**Solution:** Types sit on radial arms of a type disc in circular and crossing rows; key levers rotate the disc by a graded angle to bring a radial row under a slot, while the paper carriage's sidewise position picks the character in that row.

**How it works:** Key levers strike diagonal bars of hinged frames at different distances from the fulcrum, so each key moves a fork down a helical rod and turns the rotary axle by a different angle. The axle turns collars carrying the type arms until a recess and pin stop the required arm opposite a radial slot in a cover plate. The key lever then depresses a rocking frame whose disk lifts a collar, releasing all other arms to rest against an ink pad on the underside of the cover plate. A presser foot pushes the selected arm through the slot into print on the paper roll. The paper carriage is moved sideways to pick the circular row.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Keys that only set the element](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
- **Type element:** [Type disc (types on the face)](../features/elem-disc-face.md), [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** key lever; hinged frame; helical rod; rotary axle; type disc; type arm; cover plate with slot; ink pad; rocking frame; presser foot; paper roll carriage

**Numbers:** three circular rows of types
