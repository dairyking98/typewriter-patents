## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Replacing the paper roll or cleaning the printing parts of a cash register requires working inside the machine.

**Solution:** All printing parts, paper rolls, advance rollers and ink roller are mounted on one plate that swings on a pin of the frame and can be lifted out as a unit by a single manipulation.
