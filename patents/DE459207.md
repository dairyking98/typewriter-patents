## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** A pawl moving axially on the roller axis returns the capsule to zero from any rotational position, so the paper release opens quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the capsule to zero for paper release must be done slowly by hand while following its rotation.

**Solution:** A pawl moving axially on the roller axis returns the capsule to zero from any rotational position, so the paper release opens quickly.
