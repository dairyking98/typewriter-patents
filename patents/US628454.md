## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Friction feed rollers give uneven pressure and oblique feed as they wear, while a platen with a clamp winds the paper around the roller, thickening it and hiding the work.

**Solution:** A paper clamp guided parallel to the platen axis and away from the roller draws the sheet forward flat so lines advance uniformly and the writing stays visible.

**How it works:** The carriage cheek pieces carry a series of frames or arms on an axle at the rear, at different heights at rest. The uppermost arm carries a pivoted spring clamp jaw that grips the paper edge. The clamp is guided away from and parallel to the platen so the paper is fed over the platen line by line and spread out in front of the operator. A modified form is also shown. The text is cut off before the drive details.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** platen; paper clamp; clamp jaw; clamp spring; arm frames; axle; carriage cheek; carriage rod; guide rail; caster

**Improves on:**

- Friction feed rollers: positive clamp feed avoids slipping and oblique feed
- Platen with paper clamp winding paper on the roller: paper is not wound so line spacing stays constant and writing stays visible
