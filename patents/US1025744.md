## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ledger, statement and proof sheets must be superimposed so one writing fills all sheets, and their lines must be registered.

**Solution:** A holder with a column of numbered studs on each side engages punched holes in the sheet edges, so line registration between sheets can be changed quickly; it can be used by hand or on a flat-bed typewriter.
