## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar actions give uneven key force and noise, and key return springs differ between bars.

**Solution:** Auxiliary bell-crank levers under the key levers, alternated on two parallel supports and spring-connected in pairs, give every key the same force of stroke.

**How it works:** Finger keys slide in guide bars and join key levers that slide at their rear on a cross bar through adjusting screws. A link connects each key lever to a bell-crank mounted by trunnions in grooved parallel bars, the cranks alternating between two sets. Draw bars from the long arms of the cranks drive the typebars in a ring. A spring joins the short arms of each pair of cranks and pulls both back to stops, so each spring tension works in both directions and does double work. Equal link and fulcrum distances in the two sets are said to give uniform action. Cushioned cross bars stop the key levers.
