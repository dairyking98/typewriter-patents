## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars rebound off the basket and collide with adjacent type-bars near the printing point during rapid operation.

**Solution:** The type-bar basket is fitted with a composite pad of felt and leather strips, joined in segments, that absorbs the rebound.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type-bar basket; composite pad of strips; key-levers; sublevers
