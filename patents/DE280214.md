## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Signing several checks, banknotes or documents needs a portable writing device that applies even pressure with the pen.

**Solution:** A foldable case holding a pen-holder rod on a parallelogram-linkage swing frame, counterbalanced by springs so the pen force stays nearly constant in all positions; the case lid doubles as a writing pad.
