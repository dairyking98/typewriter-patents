The feature of the invention is that of a fixed horizontal Shaft a suspended swing frame is held supported, which at its lower end has a joint link parallelogram supports keeps. The latter carries a pen holder rod with a plurality of writing members. With the swing frame a counterbalance works together. The penholder bar remains when writing with the springs always at almost the same distance from the lower end of the oscillating frame, so that the on the spring holder rod acting lever arms and the forces exerted by the balancing springs always remain almost the same.
