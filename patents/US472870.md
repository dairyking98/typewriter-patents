## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need accurate type registration and many characters from few keys, plus a quick-acting carriage feed.

**Solution:** Each typebar head carries three characters and a tilting triangular guide is raised by two shift levers to bring the middle or lower character to the print point.

**How it works:** Key levers swing typebars forward through a pivoted triangular type-bar guide that sits next to the platen. Two extra levers with studs of different length lift the guide by two different amounts, bringing the central or the lower character on each bar into printing position. Convex projections on the bar end keep alignment while the guide moves. A projection on the typebar strikes a rod that pushes a pawl against a double rack bar on the paper carriage, feeding it on each stroke.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** typebar; key lever; type-bar guide; shift lever; double rack bar; feed pawl; paper-supporting roller

**Numbers:** three characters per typebar
