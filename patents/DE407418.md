> Description Translated from German Engagement arrangement of the tension element on typewriters with power drive. On power-driven typewriters, the tension element, which is activated by the keystroke is coupled to the rotating camshaft in a known manner at the moment inserted, in which the circumferential speed of the shaft the pulling element the greatest Speed granted, d. H. the direction of the peripheral speed acts in this moment parallel to the direction of pull of the pulling element. This arrangement has since the speed of the tension element is the same as the peripheral speed of the shaft at the moment of coupling, the disadvantage that the tightening torque occurs suddenly, whereby on the one hand the mechanisms are exposed to large impacts, on the other hand they are inhibitory act on the driving force. The invention overcomes these disadvantages in that the coupling of the Tensile element takes place during the period during which the direction of the peripheral speed of the camshaft from the position perpendicular to the pulling direction of the pulling element transitions to the layer directed parallel to the direction of pull of the pulling element.
