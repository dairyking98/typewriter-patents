## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (power meter for typewriters and presses) · **Subject (patent classes):** Registering or indicating the production of the machine either with or without registering working or idle time in graphical form

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a power meter, in particular for Typewriters, letterpress machines and textile machines, in which the Machine to be controlled work done by means of electromagnetic controlled rear derailleurs longitudinally displaceable racks is displayed, their respective the shift corresponding to the sum of the work performed by the machine assigned to them can be read from a graduation;

**How it works (reading):** Each machine's work is counted by an electromagnet-driven rack whose displacement is marked by pens on a diagram sheet behind a display panel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Solution:** Each machine's work is counted by an electromagnet-driven rack whose displacement is marked by pens on a diagram sheet behind a display panel.
