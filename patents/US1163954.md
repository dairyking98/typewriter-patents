## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An automatic pneumatic typewriter operator needs a positive line-space and carriage-return mechanism that does not line-space accidentally when the machine restarts.

**Solution:** A half nut on a shiftable carrier engages a constantly rotating screw to drive the typewriter's line-space lever, shifted into position by a pneumatic star wheel with a detent that stops accidental extra feeds.

**How it works:** A player-piano style operator reads perforated master sheets on tracker boards, where a perforation admits air and fires a pneumatic actuator to work some part of the typewriter. For line spacing, a half nut on a carrier slides on rails between a normal position clear of a stationary rotating screw and a position engaged with it; an arm joins the carrier to the typewriter's line-space lever. A pneumatic actuator swings a cam star wheel down against a shift rail, which moves the carrier and nut into the screw so the lever returns the carriage and spaces the platen. A detent holds the star wheel from reversing on its stroke but lets it turn idly past the rail on return, and is moved aside when the pneumatics stop.
