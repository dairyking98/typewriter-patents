## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A long paper carriage that projects beyond the machine base is prone to distortion.

**Solution:** The rear member of the carriage is built as a truss with a bar, upright struts and a long chord, which resists distortion in its vertical plane.
