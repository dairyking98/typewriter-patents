## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector character marking) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to means for marking cheeks, drafts, bonds, or other instruments with figures or characters in such a manner as to prevent them from being altered or raised.

**How it works (reading):** A check-protector whose operating handle drives a rotating reciprocating die-holder with male and female dies that puncture the figure, with the cuts inked and an improved feed mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks and drafts could be altered or raised, so the figures needed to be punched, cut or crushed in and then made impossible to change.

**Solution:** A check-protector whose operating handle drives a rotating reciprocating die-holder with male and female dies that puncture the figure, with the cuts inked and an improved feed mechanism.
