## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stiff or thick sheets lag behind one another when fed, so they do not move together.

**Solution:** A platen holder attachment for an Underwood, with a universal connection, drives the sheets evenly so they stay aligned.
