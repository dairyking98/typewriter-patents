## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanism driven by cams engaging rotating roller; Lever-operated contractible or expansible band, the lever moving in the plane of the band

**How it works (reading):** A second locking device engages when the key is pressed into position and releases the drive after one stroke; a thumb disk can defeat it for repeated keys like underlining.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On keyboard-driven typewriters a held key keeps re-triggering the mechanical drive, so fingers cannot rest on the keys.

**Solution:** A second locking device engages when the key is pressed into position and releases the drive after one stroke; a thumb disk can defeat it for repeated keys like underlining.
