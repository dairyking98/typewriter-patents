## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocks between any two-carriage-moving mechanisms, e.g. character-space, back space, tabulation, carriage return or carriage- release mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Resilient pawls on both sides of the paper carriage frame engage stationary pins of the machine frame in the lower position; toggle levers, linked to the lifting levers, unlock the pawls just before the upward movement and lock them on reaching the lower position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A locking bolt linked by pull rod to the switching lever locks the paper carriage in the lower writing position imprecisely, may lock too early or late, acts on one end only, and does not suit diagonally or arcuately moving carriages.

**Solution:** Resilient pawls on both sides of the paper carriage frame engage stationary pins of the machine frame in the lower position; toggle levers, linked to the lifting levers, unlock the pawls just before the upward movement and lock them on reaching the lower position.
