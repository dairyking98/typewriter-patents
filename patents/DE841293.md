## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for holding the paper trolley of office machines, in particular from typewriters, in the lower writing position it is advisable to hold the paper trolley in the lower writing position when writing, to achieve straight lines. It is known to do this in a typewriter with a paper trolley that can be raised and lowered vertically, one that is rotatably mounted on the machine frame and to provide a locking bolt rigidly connected to the switching lever via a pull rod, which in the lower position comprises a projection arranged on the paper carriage and is pulled away from this by actuating the toggle lever. This arrangement works imprecisely and is therefore not completely sure that the locking bolt before lifting the paper carriage and only after reaching the lower writing position is locked. As a result, unpleasant disturbances can occur. aside from that this known arrangement acts only on one end of the paper cart and can be difficult redesigned so that it can block on both sides.
