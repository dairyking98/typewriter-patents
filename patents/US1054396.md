## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The shift of a typebar machine with a vibratory (platen-carrying) carriage needs two shift keys, a shift lock, a return spring arrangement and shafts that can be assembled and dismantled easily.

**Solution:** Two coaxial shift keys act on one depressible arm at different lever distances, a pivoted stop bar tied to one key allows the second, deeper depression, and a sliding bar locks either key down.

**How it works:** Two shift keys pivot on a rocker shaft whose arm links by a universal joint to a counter rocker shaft carrying a double lever with a trundle roll that engages a channel bar on the vibratory carriage. Depressing one key lowers the carriage half way to the capitals position, where an adjustable screw stop on the double lever limits it. The other key also moves a pivoted bar through a link, which swings a standard out of the way so the carriage drops fully for figures. A weaker spring keeps the first key raised, and a spring returns the bar. A sliding shift-lock bar on the cover plate, moved by a lock key, engages shoulders on a lock bolt to hold a key down until touched again. The tabulator shaft is made removable so it also supports the shift shaft during assembly.
