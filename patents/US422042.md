## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements to the inventor's earlier typebar machines: cheaper base construction, ribbon spool, margin regulator, spring-barrel carriage drive and carriage feed.

**Solution:** A typebar typewriter with a one-piece cast base carrying carriage tracks, a margin regulator, ribbon-spool devices and an adjustable spring-barrel drive.

**How it works:** The base is cast with an integral rib track, side brackets for a rod track and brackets for a universal-bail shaft, so machining is easy. A margin regulator casting holds a peg in a hole to set margin. Typebars pivot in brackets in a plate apertured on curved lines, linked by wires to the keys, and a carriage is driven by a spring barrel with tension adjustment through feed pawls. Ribbon spools are operated by mechanism under the bed.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** base casting; carriage track; margin regulator; peg; type-bar bracket; type-bar plate; universal bail; spring barrel; carriage-feed pawl; ribbon spool
