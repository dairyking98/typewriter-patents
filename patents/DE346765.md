## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing devices are heavy, costly and bulky, which works against a small, cheap portable typewriter.

**Solution:** All parts of the line-spacing device are sheet metal: the lever pivots on an end plate of the platen frame, and a sheet-metal ratchet with a resilient, adjustable control plate locks the set position by its own springiness.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for producing the line spacing. The invention relates on a device for producing line spacing for typewriters. The main purpose of the invention is to provide a simple, compact, lightweight and to create effective device for producing the line spacing, the -with can be manufactured at low cost and for the manufacture of a small, lightweight, compact, usable and yet cheap - portable typewriter contributes. For this purpose, all parts of the device are for manufacturing of the line spacing made of sheet metal and therefore light and of less Space occupancy. The lever causing the line spacing is on an end plate of the platen frame rotatable and shaped so that it has this end plate at two opposite Pages includes and is guided in this way by this plate. The ratchet, which is also made of sheet metal, works with a rotatable, resilient control plate composed of sheet metal, which is adjustable around its pivot, with its own springiness the locking in the set position secures and the plate a slot with enlarged openings through which a screw with a beveled head engages, -whose engagement with the resilient plate is also secured by its own resilience…
