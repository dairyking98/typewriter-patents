## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** characterised by operational sensory functions, e.g. sound feedback

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide a cushion cap for the purpose set forth in a manner as hereinafter set forth and which is so constructed as to grip firmly the head of the key whereby the cap will be securely retained in position without the em ployment of a hold-fast device or an interlocking element to retain the cap in position.

**How it works (reading):** A one-piece rubber cap with a hollow body and crown grips the key head and cushions the blow.

**Problem:** Key impacts jar the operator's fingertips.

**Solution:** A one-piece rubber cap with a hollow body and crown grips the key head and cushions the blow.
