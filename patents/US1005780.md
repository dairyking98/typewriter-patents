## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Springs in typewriters and other machines cannot be tuned to a required expansive or retractile power without changing the spring.

**Solution:** A hollow element encircles the spring and has projections, such as threads, that fit between the coils. Rotating it expands or contracts the spring so its tension can be set uniformly.

**How it works:** One spring end attaches to the regulating element; turning the element adjusts the coil spacing and therefore the spring power.
