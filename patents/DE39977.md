## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**How it works (reading):** Two-part type arms on a common axis swing sideways along converging guides to one print point, each arm carrying three types, with the axis frame tilted to bring the wanted case to the print line.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machine whose letters are visible as typed, carries several type sets on one set of keys and arms and avoids a swinging carriage mass.

**Solution:** Two-part type arms on a common axis swing sideways along converging guides to one print point, each arm carrying three types, with the axis frame tilted to bring the wanted case to the print line.

**How it works:** Key levers pivot on one axis under the roller, in two concentric arcs, and rods link them to the lower parts of the type arms. The upper arm parts, hinged to the lower ones and bent to different degrees, are swung by converging guide rails toward one point on the paper, which lies on a print-and-transport roller in front. Each arm tip carries small letters, capitals and figures; the arm axis sits in a swinging frame that takes one of three positions so the chosen set prints at the line. A swinging ink roller is pushed aside by a type and then returned to present another type track. The roller frame slides sideways by a ratchet and pawl lever worked by a key and the pawl can be released for free movement.
