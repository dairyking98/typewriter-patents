## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Shields or masks

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of our present invention being to provide a simple and eflicient mounting for the ink-ribbon idle guide spools, whereby said spools may be readily shifted vertically to change the ribbon path and held in positions to which they may be shifted.

**How it works (reading):** Mount each ribbon idler spool on a keyed post sliding in a fixed sleeve with a spring-ball detent, so the spool can be raised or lowered and held.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Stenographic machine ribbon needs its path shifted so a fresh part of the ribbon meets the type.

**Solution:** Mount each ribbon idler spool on a keyed post sliding in a fixed sleeve with a spring-ball detent, so the spool can be raised or lowered and held.

**How it works:** A sleeve is fixed through the top frame plate near each end of the platen. A post carrying a freely rotating idler guide spool slides in the sleeve, kept from turning by a screw in a keyway. The post has vertically aligned recesses into which a spring-pressed locking ball in the sleeve wall snaps, holding the chosen height. Changing the spool height shifts the plane of the horizontal ribbon so it strikes at a new place.
