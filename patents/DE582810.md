## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A dictator must interrupt dictation to ask the typist whether they are ready to continue.

**Solution:** A key built into the normal keyboard closes a circuit that lights a lamp or sounds a signal to tell the dictator to continue; it can be fitted to new or existing machines.
