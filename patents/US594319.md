## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector indenting and inking) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to means for indenting checks, drafts, and notes, and for inking the fiber thereof in such a manner that the check-marks cannot be changed or altered without so changing the appearance thereof that the same would be noted instantly by a person seeing the check or draft.

**How it works (reading):** A simple hollow base holds dies on a ring under a spring-loaded disk, and the dies with serrated punch faces indent the check and ink the fibers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier check protectors were too complicated and costly, and the indented marks needed to be easy for any user to make.

**Solution:** A simple hollow base holds dies on a ring under a spring-loaded disk, and the dies with serrated punch faces indent the check and ink the fibers.
