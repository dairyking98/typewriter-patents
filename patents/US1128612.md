## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cutting stencils: the ribbon must be silenced when typing directly on the stencil but active for the header card.

**Solution:** A card holder on the carriage holds the ribbon vibration control active over the card and releases it for the stencil.

**How it works:** A bell crank on the frame operates the ribbon control. A long bar on the carriage, swung by a rack on the card holder meshing with a platen pinion, holds the crank normal along the card's length. When the platen advances past the card, the bar is released and the ribbon returns to silenced position, so types cut the stencil sharply.
