> Description Translated from German Typewriter with a movable frame housing cover that leaves the keypad free The invention relates to a typewriter with a frame housing, the key and Type lever mechanism on the sides and the type lever mechanism on top at least partially covering movable cover is provided that the keypad releases. This cover usually carries the platen and the ribbon spools. The purpose of the invention is to mount the type lever mechanism in the frame housing a particularly convenient and convenient way to view it or to be able to remove them in whole or in part. This is the case with known writing or accounting machines where the front or rear wall of the frame housing by virtue of a hinged wall part, it can only be partially folded down, for lack sufficient space is not achieved, especially from these sides the handling. the type lever mechanism and parts that can be folded down with the platen is difficult. Furthermore, it has already been proposed that the typewriters said type to provide a cover that completely from the main part of the frame housing can be removed to make the type lever mechanism accessible.
