## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with a frame housing, the key and Type lever mechanism on the sides and the type lever mechanism on top at least partially covering movable cover is provided that the keypad releases.

**How it works (reading):** The frame housing cover is hinged to the main frame part and carries cushioned pressure members that hold the type lever mechanism when the cover is closed, keeping the keypad free.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter cover that is removed completely can be damaged or twisted, and its dowel pins are hard to re-align, while a folding wall does not give access to the type lever mechanism.

**Solution:** The frame housing cover is hinged to the main frame part and carries cushioned pressure members that hold the type lever mechanism when the cover is closed, keeping the keypad free.

**How it works:** Hinging keeps the cover attached and aligned on its supporting surfaces, and the pressure members press on the type lever mechanism when closed.
