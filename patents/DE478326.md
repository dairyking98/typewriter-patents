## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing typewriter lamps cannot light the manuscript, the written line and the keyboard evenly and without glare.

**Solution:** A lamp with reflector is mounted on a clamp-fixed arm below a manuscript holder so that the manuscript, the writing point and the keys are lit without dazzling the writer.
