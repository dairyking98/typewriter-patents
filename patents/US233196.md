## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A key for every letter makes typewriters and telegraph instruments hard to learn and forces the operator to look away from the copy.

**Solution:** Reduce the number of keys with groups of levers selected by left-hand keys and then operated by a small set of broad right-hand keys.

**How it works:** Key levers on the left tilt frames that push one group of levers forward under a row of four broad keys. Pressing a right-hand key then depresses one lever of the selected group, which is linked to a typewriter key lever. A rock shaft with uprights supports the broad keys and, when shifted by a left key, lets them be pressed. So a limited number of keys operates a larger number of typewriter keys.
