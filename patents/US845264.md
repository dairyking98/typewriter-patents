**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates particularly to keylevers for typewriter machines of that class in which the type is carried by a type-wheel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Key-lever banks of different lengths need different force and stroke, so the top bank is hard to operate and the machine as a whole is hard to use.

**Solution:** The key-levers are modified so that stroke and force are equalized across the banks and can be adjusted by the maker.

**How it works:** Key-levers pivot on hooks at their ends under a sector-shaft; their linkage is changed so that each bank gives an equal force and stroke.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)

**Parts:** key-levers; sector-shaft; sub-levers; hooks
