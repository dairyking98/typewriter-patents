**How it works (reading):** Pliers hold a steel letter type pushed through a square bar in one jaw and held by a pin; squeezing the jaws stamps the letter, and the ring is turned by one letter width for the next.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Engraving letters inside wedding rings by hand is slow and costly because each ring must go to an engraver.

**Solution:** Pliers hold a steel letter type pushed through a square bar in one jaw and held by a pin; squeezing the jaws stamps the letter, and the ring is turned by one letter width for the next.

**Features:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
