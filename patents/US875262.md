## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the revoluble platens of typewriting machines, such as are usually provided with notched line.

**How it works (reading):** A sheet-metal brake arm on the platen axle bears against the line-space wheel and is held out of use by the detent release lever, so it brakes when the detent is freed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen's line-space detent holds the platen steadily, but a brake is needed when it is released.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal brake arm on the platen axle bears against the line-space wheel and is held out of use by the detent release lever, so it brakes when the detent is freed.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
