## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Zero-setting of the adding machine's printing rollers was done by a cascade of lever transmissions, so depressing a key far to the left took more force and the zeroing became unreliable with wear.

**Solution:** A second, more forward pawl on each key set lifts with a nose that the left neighbour's pull rod runs onto, so the rear pawl sets the rollers to zero without the lever cascade.
