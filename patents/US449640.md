## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools need to be turned a step at each letter space, and the spools need to be easy to remove from the machine.

**Solution:** Drive the ribbon spools from the reciprocating dog-carrying frame of the spring-drum escapement through operating levers with friction shoes or pawls, and mount each spool on a hollow shaft held by a spring tooth.

**How it works:** The letter-spacing frame reciprocates a dog-carrying frame (upper and lower dogs working in the ratchet of the spring-driven carriage drum). Two operating levers rest by friction pulleys in notches of this frame. Each lever carries either a friction shoe working on a friction driving-wheel on the spool shaft, or a pawl working a ratchet. Each reciprocation of the frame rocks the engaged lever and turns one spool a little, the shoe holding the spool against reverse turning. A hand-operated anchor on the drum shaft pushes one lever out of its notch, making that side inactive so the ribbon winds from the other spool. Each spool shaft has a hollow inner end fitting a projection on the main frame, secured by a spring tooth entering a detent, so lifting the tooth frees the spool and shaft.
