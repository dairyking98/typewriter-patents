## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Back-spacing mechanism for existing machines is complicated.

**Solution:** A simple back-space attached around the escapement wheel shaft to step the carriage one space the other way.

**How it works:** An improvement on Barron and Merritt US783669. The carriage runs on rollers on a track, feed rack meshing a pinion on a shaft, with the escapement wheel on the shaft. A back-spacing device acts on the wheel shaft to step the carriage from left to right one tooth at a time.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** feed rack; feed pinion; escapement wheel shaft; carriage truck; platen frame; shift rail

**Improves on:**

- [US783669](../patents/US783669.md): simpler, applicable to existing machines
