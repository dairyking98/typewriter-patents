## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, more particularly to the tabulating and column skipping devices arranged in machines of this character.

**How it works (reading):** A Column master key converts the four middle denomination keys into column-skipping keys, so fewer keys are needed on the keyboard.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating needs many denomination keys, and the keyboard is crowded.

**Solution:** A Column master key converts the four middle denomination keys into column-skipping keys, so fewer keys are needed on the keyboard.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
