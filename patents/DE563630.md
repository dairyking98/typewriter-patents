## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning type levers to rest by a running motor needs costly drive means, and using the carriage spring makes the carriage hard to operate.

**Solution:** A spring-struck type lever is returned to rest by an electromagnet whose armature acts on a universal rod that also controls the magnet's own switch.

**How it works:** Pressing a key releases a small hook so a spring throws the type lever against the platen. The lever's small support presses the universal rod down, which closes a switch. The magnet pulls its core, lifting the universal rod and returning the lever to its hook, and the switch reopens. A held key lets the spring strike again until released. The paper carriage advances one step when the lever strikes, and the space key works the same way via its own lever acting on the rod.
