## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**How it works (reading):** A line guide plate carries several paper guide tongues spread across the platen width, with adjustable stops, so sheets pass under the paper guide rod while it stays in pressure position and stiff cards are still guided.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Feeding postcards, index cards or sheets into a typewriter requires lifting the paper guide rod, inserting the sheet underneath and lowering the rod again.

**Solution:** A line guide plate carries several paper guide tongues spread across the platen width, with adjustable stops, so sheets pass under the paper guide rod while it stays in pressure position and stiff cards are still guided.
