## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A set of impressors arranged around a support must each be brought selectively to one fixed printing point from its normal rest position.

**Solution:** The impressors sit on a rotary support that first turns through a controlled angle to line the chosen one up with the fixed point, and only then is the selected impressor shifted axially to the point.

**How it works:** A support or carrier capable of rotation carries the series of impressors. Under key control, mechanism rotates the support to the angle that brings the chosen impressor into the vertical plane of the fixed point. Only after that angular move is complete does a second mechanism shift the chosen impressor along the support to the fixed point. The two movements are strictly consecutive. The excerpt shows a key-operated version, with the exact gearing and key linkage in the part not shown.
