## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide a combined paper clip and marginal stop for typewriting machines, whereby the paper clip is shifted every time a marginal stop is moved to regulate the extent of the writing on a sheet of paper.
