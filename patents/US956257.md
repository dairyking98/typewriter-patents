## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide a combined paper clip and marginal stop for typewriting machines, whereby the paper clip is shifted every time a marginal stop is moved to regulate the extent of the writing on a sheet of paper.

**How it works (reading):** A paper clip is carried on the same plate as the marginal stop, so it shifts with the stop each time the stop is moved and keeps the sheet pressed to the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a marginal stop is moved the paper is held against the platen only at its ends, so it does not stay firmly in contact with the platen.

**Solution:** A paper clip is carried on the same plate as the marginal stop, so it shifts with the stop each time the stop is moved and keeps the sheet pressed to the platen.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** marginal stop block; bracket; adjustable plate; paper clip; binding screw
