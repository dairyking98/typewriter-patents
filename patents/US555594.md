## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor seeks more efficient mechanism details for a machine printing from individual type brought to a common printing point.

**Solution:** A bed-plate machine with type levers around an annular head plate and an aligning funnel at the printing point, with several detail improvements to keys, feed dogs, ribbon and inking brush.

**How it works:** Key levers on a knife-edged fulcrum bar, easily detachable, operate type levers through connecting rods. Type are guided by guide slips and align in a funnel at the printing point. The carriage on the bed uses feed dogs on a feed ratchet released by a cam lever on return, with a bail and plunger driven by the keys and an adjustable stop varying dog throw. A feed turns an inking brush and moves the ribbon lengthwise; the platen swings, with line feed and paper guide bands.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
