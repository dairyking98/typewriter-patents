## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing and similar machines which produce impressions from individual type or dies brought in succession to a common printing-point, and has for its object to provide certain improvements in the details of the mechanism with the view of producing a more efficient machine.
