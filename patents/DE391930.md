## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A backspace lever with a multi-tooth head must enter and leave the carriage ratchet reliably.

**Solution:** Hinge the rear of the back-key lever to a drive lever and guide it with a pin in a cam slot so the toothed head moves on an arc into and out of the ratchet.

**How it works:** The back-key lever carries a multi-tooth head. Its rear end is bent down and hinged on a drive lever whose axis lies above the hinge pin. A tension spring between the two levers holds the head down at rest and, past centre, pushes it up into the ratchet. A fixed guide pin through a cam slot in the lever forces the arc-shaped path. On release the spring pulls the head out following the upper cam curve.

**Features:** [ESC:backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** back key lever; toothed head; drive lever; tension spring; guide pin; cam slot; ratchet wheel; push rod
