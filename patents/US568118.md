## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed mechanism has a gear-train at the left of the machine, which is complicated and costly.

**Solution:** The gear-train is removed, and a take-up roller on a spring-pressed frame sits beside the platen roller and is driven by it.
