**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (post counter for typewriters)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a counter, in particular for typewriters Postenz;

**How it works (reading):** A stroke counter with zero position is placed beside the number roller set within its width, is driven by the locking lever for the computing station, and locks after the first digit until the carriage lifts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Item counters on accounting machines take up columns width and paper space in the form.

**Solution:** A stroke counter with zero position is placed beside the number roller set within its width, is driven by the locking lever for the computing station, and locks after the first digit until the carriage lifts.
