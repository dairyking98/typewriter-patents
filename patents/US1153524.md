## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (typewriting doll toy) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means in dolls for producing sounds

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of our invention is toproduce typewriting doll toy comprising among its members a part constructed to simulate a typewriting machine, and preferably supported upon a part constructed to give the appearance of.

**How it works (reading):** A doll figure's arms, linked by clips to an actuating shaft, move over a toy typewriter and drive movable type bars that print onto a paper strip.
