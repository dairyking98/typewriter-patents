This purpose is achieved with the known devices of this type either not completely or only structurally very intricate means achieved. The invention solves this known problem in particularly simple in that the space between the surface the support plate and the base of the side edge guide through a sleeve is produced, which is located between two longitudinal slots of the support plate Longitudinal web is placed and against the webs of the side edge guide by a spring be pressed, on the one hand against two through the longitudinal slots of the support plate passing guide tabs resting on the narrow longitudinal surfaces of the metal sleeve the side edge guide and on the other hand against the back of the sleeve.
