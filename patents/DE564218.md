## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Side edge guides on a typewriter paper support plate scratch the lacquered paper surface when moved.

**Solution:** A sleeve spaces the guide base off the plate surface on a longitudinal web and a leaf spring presses the guide against it.
