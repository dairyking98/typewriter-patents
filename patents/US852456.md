## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A movable carriage or support must be arrested at any one of a number of fields or groups of letter-spaces along its travel, not only at fixed tabulating points.

**Solution:** Spacing mechanism, independent of tabulator devices, lets the operator cause the arrest of the movable part at any one of several fields of letter spaces along its line of travel.
