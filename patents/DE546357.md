## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing music printing plates) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** Staff lines, note heads and bar stamps are split so that few reusable stamps build all notes, with a lift device preventing early contact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Music printing plates needed too many separate embossing stamps for note images.

**Solution:** Staff lines, note heads and bar stamps are split so that few reusable stamps build all notes, with a lift device preventing early contact.

**Features:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md), [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
