## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for guiding the carriage of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded guide hook on the carriage swings out of the guide rail groove, letting the carriage lift off or fold down about the rear rail so it can be removed in any position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Removing and replacing the carriage requires awkward handling of the guide parts, so lay users find it hard to set the roller cage correctly.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded guide hook on the carriage swings out of the guide rail groove, letting the carriage lift off or fold down about the rear rail so it can be removed in any position.
