## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Line-spacing operations on a billing machine need to be counted to signal a set number.

**Solution:** A counter registers line-spacing operations of a billing or calculating machine, with a settable alarm bell that signals when a preset count is reached and a reset to zero.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** register counter; alarm bell; reset to zero
