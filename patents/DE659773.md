## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention is directed to clinging to make the bundle of writing paper in the holding device more perfect this now when spinning through the machine or outside of it.

**How it works (reading):** A holding device keeps the sheets together and opens a wide insertion gap with exposed stop parts, so the sheets can be set precisely in place.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Holding a sheet bundle around the platen shifts sheets, so reinsertion does not align the sheets for carbon corrections.

**Solution:** A holding device keeps the sheets together and opens a wide insertion gap with exposed stop parts, so the sheets can be set precisely in place.
