## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to copy holder attachments for typewriters, and particularly to such attachments of this kind as are operated automatically'by movements of various parts of the typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** A copy holder is connected to the typewriter so that the line-space movements are transmitted to a mechanism that marks line spacing on the copy.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lines typed on the machine must be marked on the copy holder, which is laborious to do by hand.

**Solution:** A copy holder is connected to the typewriter so that the line-space movements are transmitted to a mechanism that marks line spacing on the copy.
