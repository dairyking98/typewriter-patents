## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Phonetic writing needs more distinct characters than a practical number of movable types, so a separate type for each sound is impractical.

**Solution:** Fewer movable types are changed in combination by sets of consonant and vowel keys, with wires or rods carrying the keys to the type, and a paper carriage and ink rollers complete the printing.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md)
