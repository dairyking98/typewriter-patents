## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen-shift typewriters with only two rows need extending to three or four character rows without altering the rest of the machine and with the ribbon staying correctly placed at every height.

**Solution:** Make the platen carrier support in two parts that slide against each other, each with its own shift lever, so two keys give a lift of one, two or three row spacings.

**How it works:** Support part 1 is guided in the frame with stops limiting its stroke to one row spacing and can be hooked in its low position. Part 2 slides on part 1 over a stroke of two row spacings and carries the paper roller carrier rail. Pressing the first shift key lifts part 1 by one row (circuit I). With part 1 locked, the second key lifts part 2 by two rows (circuit II). With part 1 unlocked, the second key raises part 2 relative to part 1 and the pair together for three rows (circuit III). The ribbon carrier is arranged so the ribbon stays at the platen at all heights.

**Numbers:** stroke 1 row spacing for part 1; stroke 2 row spacings for part 2; up to 4 character rows

**Improves on:**
