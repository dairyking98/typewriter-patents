**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** Flanges are friction-fit lockable and non-rotatable on the tube, and the spring spider bolt replaces a sleeve, so any roll width and interchangeable cores fit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon paper spools have flanges that cannot be locked to the tube, so the operator cannot set them up easily and roll widths are limited.

**Solution:** Flanges are friction-fit lockable and non-rotatable on the tube, and the spring spider bolt replaces a sleeve, so any roll width and interchangeable cores fit.
