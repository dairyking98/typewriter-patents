> Description Translated from German Adjustment device for the ribbon stroke of typewriters The invention relates to an adjusting device for the ribbon stroke of typewriters between the adjusting lever and the pin influencing the ribbon fork a lever is connected which carries mutually adjustable detent members. at these known adjustment devices are a fixed plate with a central unchangeable detent for the blue position and two opposite adjustable plates Pawls are provided, which with the plate two variable notches for the red position and form for the wax position. The differs from these devices Subject of the invention in that the detent members consist of two segments, which together form three notches, the distance between which is variable, and that the Notches (e.g. by means of an eccentrically mounted locking roller lever) in their entirety are adjustable. This has the advantage that not only, as before .; achieved a change in the stroke size of the ribbon fork, but also a relocation of the total stroke in the height direction is made possible, so that an accurate adjustment the color zones can easily be made even with different widths of color ribbons can.
