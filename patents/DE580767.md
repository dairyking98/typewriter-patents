## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an adjusting device for the ribbon stroke of typewriters between the adjusting lever and the pin influencing the ribbon fork a lever is connected which carries mutually adjustable detent members.

**How it works (reading):** Two segments form three notches of variable spacing, adjustable as a whole with an eccentric locking lever, so both stroke size and the height of the stroke can be changed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon-colour adjustment only changes the lift size, not the position of the total stroke, so widths of ribbon colour zones cannot be set accurately.

**Solution:** Two segments form three notches of variable spacing, adjustable as a whole with an eccentric locking lever, so both stroke size and the height of the stroke can be changed.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
