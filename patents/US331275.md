## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (steno-typograph) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**How it works (reading):** Five indenting needles and fingers keys combine into thirty-one changes, each of which indents the paper fillet to represent a letter, space, or punctuation mark.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Bartholomew stenograph used indenting needles that were limited in their combinations.

**Solution:** Five indenting needles and fingers keys combine into thirty-one changes, each of which indents the paper fillet to represent a letter, space, or punctuation mark.

**Features:** [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
