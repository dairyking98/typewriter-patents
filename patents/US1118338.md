## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage hits the end of its run with impact, and it must be stopped and re-engaged at an exact tabulated position.

**Solution:** A tabulating attachment with a resilient buffer opposing the motor spring, plus a locking-out device that keeps the buffer connected until the carriage returns to a set position.
