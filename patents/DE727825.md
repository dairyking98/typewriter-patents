## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for producing key buttons or the like Display elements made up of several paper disks arranged one above the other assemble, the top disc printed with the key or other characters with a synthetic resin serving as a binding agent for the panes arranged one above the other is covered.

**How it works (reading):** Foils are punched and pressed in one stroke in a heated die, with the top foil printed with the character, forming a solid laminated disc.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key buttons and indicator discs made of laminated resin-soaked paper foils are slow to produce by separate punching and pressing.

**Solution:** Foils are punched and pressed in one stroke in a heated die, with the top foil printed with the character, forming a solid laminated disc.
