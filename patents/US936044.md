## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A multi-field ribbon must have any field brought to the print point, compensating for the platen shift, and allow stencil work and fuller use of the ribbon width.

**Solution:** A ribbon vibrator mechanism with field selection, automatic compensation for platen shift, a disconnect for stencils and a cross feed of the ribbon.

**How it works:** The vibrating ribbon carrier brings a chosen field of a multi-field ribbon to the printing point at will. When the platen is shifted for upper case, the ribbon vibrating mechanism is automatically adjusted for the changed printing point. The vibrator can be made inoperative for mimeograph stencils. It can also feed the ribbon crosswise, across its full width or only one field, to exhaust the ribbon.
