## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return and platen turning are done by hand, which is slow.

**Solution:** An electrical control returns the carriage to start and simultaneously turns the platen by a ratchet rack driven by a spring-pressed dog and cam fingers on the carriage.
