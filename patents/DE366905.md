## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**How it works (reading):** The whole ribbon mechanism, longitudinal and transverse, is mounted on the fixed machine frame with its drive on the frame, so the motion transfer is independent of the writing position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon lift tightens the ribbon more when shifting for capitals, and the proposed fixes need extra levers that wear and fail.

**Solution:** The whole ribbon mechanism, longitudinal and transverse, is mounted on the fixed machine frame with its drive on the frame, so the motion transfer is independent of the writing position.
