## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by denominational arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a tabulating apparatus arranged to be applied to a type-writing machine for expediting the printing in columnar form of a series of numbers of varying value.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating attachment that can be applied to existing machines lets the operator set stops at numbers on the machine's scale.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing numbers in columns needed the carriage to stop at chosen scale positions.

**Solution:** A tabulating attachment that can be applied to existing machines lets the operator set stops at numbers on the machine's scale.
