## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants a simpler, more accurate and cheaper type-wheel (revolving carrier) machine, with a way to return and lock the wheel at normal and key levers arranged so characters read straight to the operator.

**Solution:** Radiating key levers on a segmental frame turn a type-wheel through a flier and gearing, with a locking device at normal and an adjustable hammer.

**How it works:** The tapered frame has a curved web, one-third of a circle concentric with the flier journal, with notched segmental flanges guiding convergent key levers. Key levers pivot in three segmental grooves, making a rectangular bank of keys in three rows with their legends parallel. Pressing a key rotates the flier and type-carrier by a set amount, and a return device brings the carrier back to normal on release and a lock holds the wheel in normal position. A hammer then prints, with adjustable stroke and a stop. Wear in gearing is taken up by compensating provisions, and indicating devices are provided. Much of the detail is truncated.

**Features:** [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [IMP:impression-adjust](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** frame; curved web; segmental flange; key lever; flier; type-wheel; universal bar; hammer; hammer stop; gear; cover; carriage

**Numbers:** key bank in 3 rows; web one-third of a circle
