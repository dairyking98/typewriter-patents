## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hammond-type machines need the type-plate positioned precisely by the keys and held until the impression is made.

**Solution:** Key levers move the type-plate through rock-shaft linkage to the right place and an automatic lock holds it there until printing is done.

**How it works:** Alternating key levers lift one of two suspended frames, rocking a shaft that moves an arm and the type carrier left or right. Other linkages give the vertical movement. Finger stops on the shafts lock the cross-bar and carrier in position and stop vibration on return. The patent also covers rack letter feed, platen line feed, and locking the carriage in normal position so it can be raised or lowered. The text is cut before the printing stroke is described.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
- **Type element:** [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)
- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** key lever; rock-shaft; suspended frame; cross-bar; type-plate carrier; finger stop; ribbon spool; platen
