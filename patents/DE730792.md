## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A horizontal-key small typewriter drive saves space but needs unfamiliar handling.

**Solution:** Keep vertical key rods that drive horizontal typebars through a large and a small gear with racks.

**How it works:** The typebar has a rack at the rear engaging a large gear, rigid with a small gear that meshes with a rack on a vertical key rod. Pressing the key rod turns the gears so the typebar advances horizontally against the platen. A coil spring on the tapered lower end of the key rod tensions during the stroke and restores both on release. The gear ratio makes a small key travel enough.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** typebar; rack; large gear; small gear; key rod; coil spring; housing; platen

**Improves on:**

- horizontal key rod rack-and-gear typebar drive: vertical key rods keep normal keyboard feel
