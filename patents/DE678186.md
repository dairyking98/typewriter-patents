## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A paper carriage with a rack toothed on both sides, one for carriage feed and one for margin stops, needs many separately mounted parts.

**Solution:** Mount the paper guide plate, the margin stops and the double-toothed rack all on one continuous smooth shaft in the carriage side walls, the rack screwed to the release lever.

**How it works:** A continuous smooth shaft is held in the carriage side walls. The paper entry plate turns on it, and so does the rack, toothed on both sides and rigidly joined to the release lever. On one side the escapement engages the rack to feed the carriage. The margin stops also sit loosely and slidably on the same shaft: a raised upper part serves as a handle and bent side parts partly bear on the shaft and partly form individual teeth that clamp into the rack's other toothing, locking without spring force at the chosen paper-edge position.

**Parts:** smooth shaft; double-toothed rack; release lever; margin stop; paper entry plate; carriage side wall; escapement

**Improves on:**

- earlier carriages with the double rack and stops separately mounted: all on one through shaft with fewer parts, cheaper
