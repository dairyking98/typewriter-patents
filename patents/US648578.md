## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Makers want an efficient front-strike typebar machine in which the parts cooperate well.

**Solution:** A front-strike typewriter with removable type-bar carriers held in a seat strip, a case-shift with a lock, and a spring-drum rack escapement.

**How it works:** The platen carriage runs on front and rear tracks and is pulled by a spring drum, held by a rack and a feed-dog with one yielding and one rigid tooth. Key levers are bell-cranks whose catch engages notches on a slide that hooks a universal bar. Pressing a key rocks the universal bar and a cruciform lever, so the dog oscillates and permits one letter space. Type-bar carriers sit in a seat held by a retaining strip, and the case shifts by a key with a lock. A ribbon-feed or spool driver is shifted by a spring lock. The text is cut off early.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
