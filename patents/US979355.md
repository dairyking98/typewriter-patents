## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The patent seeks an improved type action for moving typebars against the platen.

**Solution:** Each key lever bears on a nearly upright sub-lever that drives the typebar through a sheet-metal link.

**How it works:** Front-strike typebars pivot in a segment and rest on a cushion. Each bar connects by a link to the top of a sub-lever pivoted on a rod across the base; a key lever fulcrumed in front has an arm bearing on the vertical edge of the sub-lever, and in one form two contact points bear successively. Coil, leaf or bow springs return the parts, and a universal bar behind the sub-levers controls the carriage feed. The link is made of two folded sheet-metal members.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** type bar; segment; cushion; link; sub-lever; key lever; universal bar; spring
