## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating or accounting machine carriage stop) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**How it works (reading):** A special adjustment means swung out of rest engages paper-carriage stops; the stop lever releases the carriage from its spring drive only after the carriage has come to rest, keeping spring drive for forward movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating the paper carriage backward to a set column by a drive motor is wasteful, and earlier stop-based coupling could wear and lose column accuracy.

**Solution:** A special adjustment means swung out of rest engages paper-carriage stops; the stop lever releases the carriage from its spring drive only after the carriage has come to rest, keeping spring drive for forward movement.
