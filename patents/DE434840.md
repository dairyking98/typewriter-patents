It is known in typewriters the paper cart rack to be arranged swingable to the same by a short swinging movement with the drive wheel the key switch to be able to engage or disengage. Since the rack moves in a circle during this process, grip the teeth of the same when they move into the meshing position not with their full Width at the same time, but initially only with the corners of one tooth side and only then gradually enters the toothing of the drive wheel with the full tooth width. This has the disadvantage that these tooth corners, especially if the paper trolley is in rapid motion when the engagement position is established, are subject to severe wear and tear, and further that by the initial sliding together the tooth tips make an annoying noise. In order to avoid these abuses, the toothing according to the Invention essentially designed the toothing of the drive wheel or the rack so that when the rack is moved into the engagement position, the teeth of the same with their full width at the same time into the teeth of the drive wheel.
