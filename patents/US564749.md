## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Make a machine in which typebars arranged in a circle move a short distance to a common centre to print, with better key connections, escapement, ribbon support and platen mounting.

**Solution:** Radial typebars slide in guide grooves of a central support to a common printing centre, with a spring-driven escape wheel for spacing.

**How it works:** Key depression rocks a lever, lifts a link and rocks a bell-crank operating lever that slides its typebar radially until the type reaches the centre of the machine. The bell-crank's inner arm depresses a table, which pushes down a rod with a stop. The stop is between teeth of an escapement gear wheel on a shaft driven by a coil spring, so lowering it frees the wheel one step and a second set of staggered teeth catches it again. The paper roller on its carriage lifts for inspection, and a ribbon shifter carries two ribbon frames.
