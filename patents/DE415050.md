## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (strip cutoff for tickets) · **Subject (patent classes):** Details of, or auxiliary devices for, ticket-issuing machines for cutting-off or separating tickets; Coin-freed apparatus for dispensing, or the like, discrete articles in which the articles are torn or severed from strips or sheets

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to such a device.

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable knife is turned by a sleeve and spring: a 180-degree turn selects a half-cut that leaves a narrow connecting piece, and further cutting edges allow cutting, embossing or punching for different card types.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Tickets and checks must sometimes be cut off completely and sometimes only pre-cut in a connected strip, which earlier knife or punch designs could not do.

**Solution:** A rotatable knife is turned by a sleeve and spring: a 180-degree turn selects a half-cut that leaves a narrow connecting piece, and further cutting edges allow cutting, embossing or punching for different card types.
