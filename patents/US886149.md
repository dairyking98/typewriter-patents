Improved Syllabic Keyboard for Type- Writers and Type-Setting Machines, of which the following is a specification. The object of the invention is to obtain great speed and thus enable more work to be done in a shorter time than has been heretofore possible on typewriters and analogous machines. To accomplish-this, both letters and syllables are used in a new arrangement or association. The accompanying drawing represents the keyboard in plan view. The key-board consists of twelve rows of keys, sixteen keys to the row, and the several rows being arranged in an incline plane, one rising above another as in the well-known Smith-Premier machine.
