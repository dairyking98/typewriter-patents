## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text read in this excerpt does not state the defect in detail; the carriage needs a retarding device acting on its travel.

**Solution:** Carriage retarding devices, with grooved guides and balls in ball channels between the carriage and the rails, slow the carriage on a Monarch machine, and a lever engages one part of the retarder with the carriage.
