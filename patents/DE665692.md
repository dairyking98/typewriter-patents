## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a note typewriter, in which the paper roller from the set note height by means of a of two pushbuttons controlled by buttons for setting to a new one Note height can optionally be rotated in one or the other direction.

**How it works (reading):** Two independent drivers engage notches on the roller face, each with wedge surfaces so it only drives in one direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Music typewriter roller height change needs slow, noisy drivers and extra buttons.

**Solution:** Two independent drivers engage notches on the roller face, each with wedge surfaces so it only drives in one direction.
