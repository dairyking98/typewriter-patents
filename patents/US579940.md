## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writers, and has for its object improvements in the carriage of a paper-carrier, by means of which the paper is iirst placed in the carriage easily and accurately and after having been placed in position is fed up for the successive lines.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding paper between pinch rollers or tapes slips and skews lines.

**Solution:** A single main roll with ratchet wheels and a spring-loaded eccentric provides the line spacing.

**How it works:** Main roll on a shaft with hand wheel and two ratchet wheels engaged alternately by a pawl on a spring pin; a coiled spring and crank-turned eccentric vary the spacing.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** main roll; ratchet wheel; pawl; coiled spring; eccentric; crank; rack bar

**Improves on:**

- pinch-roller and tape paper feeds: avoids skew
