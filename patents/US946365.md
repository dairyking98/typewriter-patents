## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Piano-wire links on Royal Standard typebar actions had hooks that were weak or hard to fit.

**Solution:** Wire links with a swaged flat latch and curved eyes to hook to the lugs of the fixed plate and push pins.

**How it works:** Links connect the lugs punched from a plate under the keyboard to the push pin and typebar heel. The front end of the wire is bent to form a seat for the lug aperture, its tip inclined, and swaged flat to form a rib-like latch. The rear end is bent in a circle in parallel with the body and flattened. The link is slightly deflected from the plane of its other part.
