## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter mounting plates in desks are hard to build and to install or remove, and their lowering is awkward.

**Solution:** A frame sliding on ledges carries two linked angle levers; the first part of the pull-out moves the plate forward, the second part raises it into the working position, with a counterweight and leaf springs locking it.
