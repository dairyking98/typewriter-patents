## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, the construction of which is improved so that the speed of writing is greatly increased.

**How it works (reading):** A keyboard lets all letters of a syllable print at once, and the carriage advances in one movement at the end of each word to space the words.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing speed was limited because each letter had to be printed individually and the carriage moved for every character.

**Solution:** A keyboard lets all letters of a syllable print at once, and the carriage advances in one movement at the end of each word to space the words.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
