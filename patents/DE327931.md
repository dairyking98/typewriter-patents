The present invention The underlying task is to improve this facility in the way that one secure holding or holding together of the papers results. Be for this purpose the tongues for holding the coal sheets initially each equipped with a counter rail, in such a way that the carbon sheets between the associated tongues and the counter rails be clamped and thus gain a secure hold. The U-shaped thus achieved Clamp can also be provided with a special gripping piece, which is the resilient Holding force of the clamp increased.
