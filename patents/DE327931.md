## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** U-shaped clamps with counter rails hold the folds, a cutter severs each edge on withdrawal, and an adjustable scaled stop sets the position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets in zigzag web paper were not held firmly and the paper tore on quick withdrawal.

**Solution:** U-shaped clamps with counter rails hold the folds, a cutter severs each edge on withdrawal, and an adjustable scaled stop sets the position.

The present invention The underlying task is to improve this facility in the way that one secure holding or holding together of the papers results. Be for this purpose the tongues for holding the coal sheets initially each equipped with a counter rail, in such a way that the carbon sheets between the associated tongues and the counter rails be clamped and thus gain a secure hold. The U-shaped thus achieved Clamp can also be provided with a special gripping piece, which is the resilient Holding force of the clamp increased. Each clamp can also have a Be equipped cutting blade, which when retracting the common carrier for the clamps separates the paper web at the corresponding folding edge.
