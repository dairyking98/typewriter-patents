## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink from the ribbon blurs and soils the paper around the hammer hole in the ribbon shield.

**Solution:** Raised plates or bars fixed to the ribbon side of the shield, on each side of the hammer hole, keep the ribbon off the hole edges so ink does not deposit there.
