## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops must be set for denominational or column work, and the Yost machine needs separate mechanisms for these.

**Solution:** A tabulator convertible by readily shifted parts into either a denominational or a column-selecting tabulator, driven through a universal bar.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
