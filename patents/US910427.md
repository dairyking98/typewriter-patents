## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen shifts for typewriters were complex, costly and hard to repair and clean.

**Solution:** A simplified platen shift with levers operating at the rear and beneath the platen, combining pivots and levers for easier operation, plus a locking device.

**How it works:** Two shift levers with keys on left and right run rearward along the machine sides, pivoted on a rod near the rear wall. Intermediate pivots connect them to slotted vertical arms moving on an upper rod. One arm links to a platen shift rod at the rear, whose slanting arm has a forked end carrying a platen operating rod that raises the platen. A separate locking device holds the shift.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** shift lever; shift key; vertical arm; shift rod; platen operating rod; locking device; carriage; frame
