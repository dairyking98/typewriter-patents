## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch with inking) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide an apparatus for the above named purpose which will puncture the amount of the check or draft therein and at the same time indelibly ink said puncture, rendering the changing of the amount impossible.

**How it works (reading):** A check-punch in which a revolving disk carries metal point characters that puncture the amount, with an ink roll and automatic pad beneath to ink each puncture.
