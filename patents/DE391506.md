## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper feed device for typewriters, in which the brackets carrying the pressure rollers are rotatably mounted independently of one another on a common bearing rod.

**How it works (reading):** Feed and pressure rollers are carried on U-shaped spring-loaded bails with unequal arms on a rod behind the platen, and the insertion plate slides tangentially on pins so the paper is pressed evenly against the roller just below the line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper feed for very long sheets does not press the sheet evenly against the platen, especially for carbon sets.

**Solution:** Feed and pressure rollers are carried on U-shaped spring-loaded bails with unequal arms on a rod behind the platen, and the insertion plate slides tangentially on pins so the paper is pressed evenly against the roller just below the line.
