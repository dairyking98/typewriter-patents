## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is a ribbon switching device for typewriters in which the changeover of the ribbon is influenced by an approach of the ribbon Guide fork is initiated.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon guide fork sits on a slider that moves in line with the ribbon; an angle lever links the slider to a spring-yielding latch, so a short pull of the ribbon is enough to switch it, and the spools are driven by bevel gears and a spiral wheel with a pawl.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the ribbon for a shift needs a complicated arrangement, and moving the ribbon while switching can disturb the other parts.

**Solution:** The ribbon guide fork sits on a slider that moves in line with the ribbon; an angle lever links the slider to a spring-yielding latch, so a short pull of the ribbon is enough to switch it, and the spools are driven by bevel gears and a spiral wheel with a pawl.
