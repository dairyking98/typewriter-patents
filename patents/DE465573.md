## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers in typewriters with the last part of their travel driven by inertia needed an accelerated strike and a compact bearing layout.

**Solution:** A non-equal-armed angle lever engages the dead-centre intermediate link, and the type lever is driven through a pin and slot, which lets the type lever bearing sit in a plane nearly perpendicular to the key lever bearing.
