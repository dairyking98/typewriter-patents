## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for back spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting niachines and more particularly to tabulating mechanism and to means for overcoming the shock ordinarily incident in arresting the carriage by the tabulating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulating mechanism with means to absorb the shock of arresting the carriage without reducing its speed from the time of release.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Arresting the carriage by the tabulating mechanism gives a shock, and a brake to avoid it would slow the carriage from release.

**Solution:** Tabulating mechanism with means to absorb the shock of arresting the carriage without reducing its speed from the time of release.
