## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**How it works (reading):** End flanges of unequal height let the platen be reversed in the carriage so the type strike lands on an unworn circumferential surface.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen grooves from type impacts wear the same circumference, shortening platen life.

**Solution:** End flanges of unequal height let the platen be reversed in the carriage so the type strike lands on an unworn circumferential surface.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Numbers:** rack division 2.6 mm; flange offset about 1.3 mm
