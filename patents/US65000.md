## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Making a mould by hand for each line is slow, and the spacing of the letters must be proportioned to their different widths.

**Solution:** Keys stop a type-wheel and, through a swinging frame and levers, press the type into a soft surface on a bed that a ratchet and friction wheel advances a set distance per key, with notched spacer blocks giving the width of each letter.
