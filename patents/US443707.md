## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Remington two-case machine the single left-hand shift key is hard to find in fast typing, and mis-hits disfigure printing.

**Solution:** A frame of side bars with shift keys at both sides and a bar behind the key rows, linked to the carriage-shifting elbow levers, lets either hand shift.

**How it works:** A frame of two side bars is pivoted at the rear of the main frame and linked to elbow levers on a rock shaft. The shaft raises a rod engaged by lugs on the carriage platen, shifting the roller to the upper-case character. Elongated keys on both sides, a thumb button over the space bar, and a bar behind the last key row can all depress the frame, so the platen shift is reachable with either hand.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** frame; side bar; link; elbow lever; rock shaft; carriage platen; shift key; thumb button; rear bar

**Numbers:** about one hundred words per minute

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter shift key: adds right-hand and thumb access to the platen shift
