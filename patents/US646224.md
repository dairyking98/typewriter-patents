## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to certain improve ments in' the keyboard mechanism for typewriters and analogous machines which is described in my prior patents, Nos.

**How it works (reading):** Improved keyboard linkage with ratchet-bars connected to combination-bars and a stroke-equalizing mechanism, aimed at speed and ease of operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The keyboard mechanism of the inventor's earlier machines is not fast or easy enough to operate.

**Solution:** Improved keyboard linkage with ratchet-bars connected to combination-bars and a stroke-equalizing mechanism, aimed at speed and ease of operation.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** ratchet-bars; combination-bars; rock-shafts; stroke-equalizing mechanism; frame end plate

**Numbers:** Patent 646,224; prior patents 597,775 and 597,776
