## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a vertically adjustable holder for shorthand books and the like with a friction drive for advancing and turning over the sheets, in particular for use on typewriters, and aims at a particularly simple and effective arrangement of the sheet turning device consisting of rotatable rollers.

**How it works (reading):** A swing-out carrier with rubber feed rollers and a page turner, pressed by its own weight, turns each leaf one step at a time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shorthand booklets had to be turned by hand in a holder for typewriters.

**Solution:** A swing-out carrier with rubber feed rollers and a page turner, pressed by its own weight, turns each leaf one step at a time.
