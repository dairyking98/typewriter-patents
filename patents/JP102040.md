**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a paper-cylinder feed device in a Japanese-script typewriter that has the three mechanisms of type magazine, printing-and-inking unit and paper cylinder, and in which the paper cylinder is given a tendency to be pushed one way by a spring.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a Japanese-script typewriter used for forms, the operator must keep setting in the first character of some lines and leaving wide gaps between groups (unit price, quantity, amount), which needs constant attention to the printing position.

**Solution:** Notched cams turning with the paper cylinder automatically pick a stop pin to limit the cylinder's return at each line, and pick a stepped stop to limit a release-lever run for wide gaps.

**How it works:** A spiral spring in a pulley on the cylinder shaft, tied by a cord to the frame, always drives the cylinder one way. Normal spacing is by a pawl pair acting on a sideways-feed rack, released one step at each impression or space lever press. A notched cam on the cylinder shaft is followed by a Y lever that turns a shaft carrying many stop pins at different angles and positions; the pin presented to a frame stop piece determines how far the cylinder runs back, so the first character of each chosen line is set in by a chosen amount. For gaps within a line, a release lever frees both pawls from the rack so the spring runs the cylinder on, until the lever meets a stepped stop on a lifting lever raised by a second notched cam, the cam notch depth choosing the step and hence the gap. Line feed is by a ratchet wheel and operating lever, with the spring turning the cylinder back when the pawl rides up a ledge and a check pawl is lifted.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Paper-cylinder feed device in a Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a paper-cylinder feed device in a Japanese-script typewriter that has the three mechanisms of type magazine, printing-and-inking unit and paper cylinder, and in which the paper cylinder is given a tendency to be pushed one way by a spring. The device combines: a device in which a cam moving with the paper cylinder at each change of horizontal line works a turning shaft, so that a stop fixed on that shaft meets a stop piece on the frame and prevents the cylinder from returning to its normal position; a device in which a stop on a lifting lever, raised and lowered by another cam moving with the paper cylinder, meets the raised end of a release lever that frees from the sideways-feed rack the pawl that holds back the cylinder; and any line-feed (縱送リ) device. Its object is a simple construction that works the paper cylinder surely as needed in every case, such as when the first character of a line is to stand out of the regular place, or when there are to be large gaps of several characters between printed groups in the same line.
