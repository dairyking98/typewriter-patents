## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter lettering is uneven because letter widths differ but spacing is constant; drawing templates need harmonious spacing.

**Solution:** Each key moves an auxiliary lever with one of three different deflections, advancing the carriage ratchet by one, two or three teeth.

**How it works:** Under each key lever is an auxiliary lever, cranked up, straight or cranked down, so a key stroke moves it by large, half or small amounts. The levers lift a common bar that turns a pawl-and-ratchet wheel one, two or three teeth, driving the carriage rack through bevel gears. A space key gives adjustable small spaces repeated as needed.
