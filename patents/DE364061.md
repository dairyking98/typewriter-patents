## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Foldable typewriters

**How it works (reading):** The machine has two frame parts hinged to each other: the type-lever segment stays on the fixed frame while the keyboard and carriage ride on the movable part, so folding both reduces height and length and the keyboard stays connected to the type levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing typewriters are too tall and long to carry, and the paper carriage and keyboard must be dismounted to shrink them.

**Solution:** The machine has two frame parts hinged to each other: the type-lever segment stays on the fixed frame while the keyboard and carriage ride on the movable part, so folding both reduces height and length and the keyboard stays connected to the type levers.
