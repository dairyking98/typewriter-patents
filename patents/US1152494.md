## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are heavy and bulky because of keys, key levers and typebars.

**Solution:** Dispense with keys and typebars: type blocks are carried on the operator's fingers (glove-mounted) and are guided by movable type guides that also actuate the letter-spacing escapement.

**How it works:** Small type blocks are worn on the fingertips of a glove and pressed against the paper on a cylindrical platen. Type guides, vertically movable and shiftable by geared bars or cords, locate each block, and a guide movement actuates the letter-space escapement. The carriage is a slide plate on a base pulled by a spiral spring through a rack and pinion. Ribbon reels are provided, and an alternative inking pad can replace the ribbon. Rotary and spring-loaded block styles are shown.

**Features:** [Press the pointer to print](../features/sel-pointer-press-prints.md), [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Interchangeable element](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** type block; glove; type guide; platen; carriage plate; rack; pinion; spiral spring; ribbon reel; inking pad

**Numbers:** 5 sheets of drawings

**Improves on:**

- keyboard typebar typewriters: removes keys, key levers and typebars to make the machine small and light
