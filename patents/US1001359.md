## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a typewriter provided with means for writing music and in the embodiment of the invention shown and described has particular reference to certain novel mechanism by means of which any typewriter of the usual or standardtype may be converted into a machine for writing music, without imparing its eltieiency for writing words and figures.

**How it works (reading):** Platen actuating mechanism rotates the platen both ways, with a means to print a staff and hold the paper on the platen; it converts a standard typewriter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A standard typewriter cannot write music; the platen must rotate in either direction to place notes on a staff.

**Solution:** Platen actuating mechanism rotates the platen both ways, with a means to print a staff and hold the paper on the platen; it converts a standard typewriter.
