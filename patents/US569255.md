## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-space mechanisms need an adjustable, easily operated way to turn the platen by a chosen amount.

**Solution:** A finger-operated frame carrying the line-space pawl moves in a fixed guide, and a rotatable stepped disk plus a notched pin set the travel.

**How it works:** The pawl is pivoted to a frame that slides at an angle to the platen plane in guides on the carriage ends. Pressing the finger-rest on the frame drives the pawl in the ratchet and turns the platen, and a spring returns the frame. A spring pawl on the carriage holds the platen against backward motion. A disk with a cut-away portion under the frame stops a pin on the frame, so rotating the disk changes the travel. The pin also has notches and a spring tooth to lengthen or shorten it, and a pawl projection lifts the pawl to free the platen.
