## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-bar hangers for type-writers, and particularly to a structure thereof adapted to be formed principally by machine-work,thus reducing to a minimum the hand labor required thereon and the consequent cost of production, while the finished product is rendered more efficient in use.
