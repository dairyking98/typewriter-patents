## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Film projectors for different widths need the gripper stroke changed by reworking the drive, which is slow and imprecise.

**Solution:** Two eccentric thumb disks drive the multiple gripper; stopping or coupling one disk sets the stroke for 16, 9.5 or 8 mm film.
