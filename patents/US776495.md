## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to line-lock mechanism for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** Line-lock mechanism stops the carriage automatically when the line end is reached, via a carriage-mounted line-lock stop and an alarm, and releases it afterwards so writing can continue.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must manually control the line end, and the line-lock should act automatically at the end of a line and release afterwards.

**Solution:** Line-lock mechanism stops the carriage automatically when the line end is reached, via a carriage-mounted line-lock stop and an alarm, and releases it afterwards so writing can continue.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
