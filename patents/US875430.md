## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Preventing rebound or clash of levers or type members

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to line-lock devices of the class in which the type-bars are locked or prevented from reaching the printing point.

**How it works (reading):** A curved intercepter hanging above the type-bar heads is swung into their paths by a link from the margin and tabulator stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-lock devices are needed so type bars are locked at the end of a line and when the carriage is jumped by a tabulator.

**Solution:** A curved intercepter hanging above the type-bar heads is swung into their paths by a link from the margin and tabulator stops.
