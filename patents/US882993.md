## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon-shifting arrangement moves one ribbon into the printing point while the other is removed, with each ribbon wound on its own spools and reversed automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbons need to swap and reverse automatically, and both ribbons must be movable clear of the type for stencil work.

**Solution:** A ribbon-shifting arrangement moves one ribbon into the printing point while the other is removed, with each ribbon wound on its own spools and reversed automatically.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
