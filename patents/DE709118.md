## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Character-by-character adjustment

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a device for changing the voltage of the Typefaces on typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A slide behind the front plate moves transversely to the springs and moves both ends of the retaining plate equally, with a slot in the front panel as indicator.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A single screw adjusts all type lever spring tensions unevenly, and the adjuster is hard to reach.

**Solution:** A slide behind the front plate moves transversely to the springs and moves both ends of the retaining plate equally, with a slot in the front panel as indicator.
