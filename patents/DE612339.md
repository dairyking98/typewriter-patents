## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manuscript holders needed fixing parts not found on ordinary typewriters and were hard to use when manuscript and copy line spacing differ.

**Solution:** A loose additional manuscript roller on bearing blocks seats on the carriage with a hook under the platen shaft, and its line lever rests on the platen's line lever so both can advance together or separately.

**How it works:** Fork-shaped bearing blocks sit on the carriage cheeks and hook under the platen shaft, joined by a sheet that serves as a paper guide with a reading slot. The holder stays by its own weight and the hook stops backward shift when the line lever is used. The manuscript roller's lever rests loosely on the platen's line lever, so pressing it advances both; when line spacings differ each lever is used alone. The manuscript roller sits above the platen so the two sheets do not touch.
