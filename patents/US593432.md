## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper letter-space and line-space feed mechanisms on Remington-type machines are inconvenient and hard to adjust.

**Solution:** Improved details of platen line-feed pawl, lever and variable stop, plus letter-space feed adjustments.

**How it works:** A feed lever on the platen carrier with a handle at the front turns a weighted pawl onto the platen ratchet and then drives the pawl carrier down, rotating the platen. A projection on the pawl stops the feed. A variable stop limits the pawl carrier's return, setting single, double or triple line spacing. A yielding latch holds the platen at ratchet positions, and the paper feed roll can be released by pulling a cross-bar on extended hangers.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet; feed pawl; pawl carrier; feed lever; variable stop; yielding latch; paper feed roll; cross-bar

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter: easier line-space feed and paper release
