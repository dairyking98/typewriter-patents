## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When tabulating columns of figures, the operator needs to vary how far the carriage jumps for groups of differing length, and fixed tabulator stops cannot do this.

**Solution:** A single stop is shifted in position relative to the frame of the machine so that it co-acts with one or more stops carried by the carriage, letting the operator limit the carriage jump.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
