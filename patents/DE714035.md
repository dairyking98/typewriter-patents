## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters, typewriters or the like with automatic, Depending on the movement of the trolley, a trolley elevator that can be switched on and off, one Achieving device powered by the same power source as the car elevator a permanent stop and a tabulator.

**How it works (reading):** Members controlling the carriage lift are switched off automatically by a switch actuated by the permanent-stop member, so no automatic lift can occur during a permanent stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic carriage lift and tabulator shared one power source, so a permanent stop could trigger a carriage lift mid-underline and damage gearing.

**Solution:** Members controlling the carriage lift are switched off automatically by a switch actuated by the permanent-stop member, so no automatic lift can occur during a permanent stop.
