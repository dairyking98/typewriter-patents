## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Removing the platen frame takes time to disconnect and readjust.

**Solution:** Open bearings, a guide pin and flaring runway let the frame be lifted off and replaced without adjustment.

**How it works:** The platen frame trunnions rest in open bearings on arms of a rock shaft; a guide pin in a runway keeps the frame from pivoting about stops, and a release hook lets it come away quickly.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** platen frame; trunnion; rock shaft arm; guide pin; runway; release hook
