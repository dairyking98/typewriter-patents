## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Single-piece tabs were hard to insert into the U-shaped column stop rod.

**Solution:** Front guide slots are made higher than the rear ones so one-piece tabs can be pushed in from the front, and a screwed safety rail partly covers the front slots to keep them in place.
