## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (pressure telegraph receiver, type wheel) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using mechanical translation and type-head printing, e.g. type-wheel, type-cylinder

**How it works (reading):** A rotating device with fixed cams prepares the test members on each revolution, and the receiver armature position, read by that device, sets them with a force that is not drawn from the magnet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The receiver magnet had to supply the force to set the test members, so it was insensitive to weak current surges.

**Solution:** A rotating device with fixed cams prepares the test members on each revolution, and the receiver armature position, read by that device, sets them with a force that is not drawn from the magnet.
