## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (manifold writing books manufacture) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in the manufacture of manifold-writing'books and pads of that class in which thin transparent paper sheets, designed to be written upon, alternate with sheets of thicker paper, destined to receive an impression of the writing from a carbon or manifold leaf.

**How it works (reading):** Print only the thicker sheets, interleave blank transparent sheets between them, and bind the stack so the thin sheet is exactly over the printed lines when a carbon leaf is used.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Separately printed thin and thick sheets of manifold writing books do not stay in register because the papers stretch differently.

**Solution:** Print only the thicker sheets, interleave blank transparent sheets between them, and bind the stack so the thin sheet is exactly over the printed lines when a carbon leaf is used.
