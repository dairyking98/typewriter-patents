## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a Storage frame for the platen from Typewriters and similar machines, in which a trough-shaped, the release shaft for the paper pressure rollers in front of the middle connecting rail is seen.

**How it works (reading):** A trough-shaped middle connecting rail, stiffer than cast, sheet or wire-braced versions, carries the release shaft for the paper pressure rollers and resists bending.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The connecting rail under a long platen bends when the platen switches, so types do not strike exactly on the line.

**Solution:** A trough-shaped middle connecting rail, stiffer than cast, sheet or wire-braced versions, carries the release shaft for the paper pressure rollers and resists bending.
