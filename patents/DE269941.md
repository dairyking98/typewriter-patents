## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a stroke adjustment device for the ribbon fork on typewriters with visible writing, switching of the platen and front stop of the type lever.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon fork is driven through a pendulum lever on a separate angle lever, with its adjusters on the frame, so the ribbon adjustment is independent of the switching frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon fork adjustment is disturbed by the universal rail and platen switching frame.

**Solution:** The ribbon fork is driven through a pendulum lever on a separate angle lever, with its adjusters on the frame, so the ribbon adjustment is independent of the switching frame.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
