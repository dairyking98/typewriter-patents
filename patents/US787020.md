## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In typebar actions the bar rebounds or vibrates at rest and at the print point, and returns slowly, causing double or blurred print and bars colliding in fast typing.

**Solution:** An intermediary lever joined to the typebar by a sliding flexing joint that shifts toward its fulcrum while printing, with a cam lock at rest and a spring-returned sliding universal bar to speed the return.

**How it works:** A horizontal key lever drives a centrally pivoted second lever through a link; the second lever's other arm is joined by a link with a sliding shoulder in a slot to the typebar heel. As the key goes down the second lever rocks to nearly vertical and throws the bar to the platen with accelerated motion. The sliding joint moves toward the fulcrum, so the return stroke has more power. Near the limit the key-side joint passes in line with fulcrum and key link, holding the bar just short of the print point to avoid double impression. A cam holds the sliding shoulder at the slot end when the bar rests, preventing rebound. A yielding universal bar returned by a spring pushes the lever back faster and also oscillates escapement pawls on an escape wheel to step the carriage.
