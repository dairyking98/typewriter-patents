## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods on side surfaces thereof, e.g. fixed thereto the types or dies being movable relative to the bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more especially to devices andmeans for the application of power to an ordinary typewriter, such, for example, as the Remington No.

**How it works (reading):** A power drive: a belt-driven shaft with a cylinder and rings, one per key lever, assists key and carriage return.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operating a typewriter keys and returning the carriage is laborious.

**Solution:** A power drive: a belt-driven shaft with a cylinder and rings, one per key lever, assists key and carriage return.

**How it works:** A transverse shaft with pulley and belt turns a cylinder carrying one ring per key lever, with a flange and pin. Presumably each key engages its ring to apply power. Fitted to Remington No. 2 or No. 6.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** shaft; pulley; belt; cylinder; ring; key lever
