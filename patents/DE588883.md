## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on typewriters for writing on copy sheets by means of a along the platen between the paper sheets continuous auxiliary ribbon that is attached to the fixed machine frame arranged ribbon reels according to the switching movement of the main ribbon is wound up or unwound.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon runs over guides on the paper carriage with loose loops in the middle position, so carriage travel advances the ribbon without widening the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A continuous auxiliary ribbon for copy sheets, fed from fixed reels, needs extension arms that enlarge the machine width.

**Solution:** The ribbon runs over guides on the paper carriage with loose loops in the middle position, so carriage travel advances the ribbon without widening the machine.
