## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter needs to print in several ink colours without changing ribbons by hand.

**Solution:** Several ink ribbons of different colours, each with its own vibrator, are selected and fed with automatic reversing.

**How it works:** A front-strike machine with segment-mounted typebars and an escapement wheel with fixed and loose dogs has multiple ribbons. Separate ribbon vibrators controlled by a selector bring the chosen ribbon to the print point. Ribbon feeding is by a mechanism with automatic reversal. The opening text mostly covers the base typewriter; the colour selection and reversing detail are lost.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon spool; ribbon vibrator; ribbon guide; type bar; escapement wheel; spring drum; universal bar; key lever

**Improves on:**

- Smith Premier key mechanism: base mechanism similar, ribbon system new
