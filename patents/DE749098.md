## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing calculating machine with type rolls)

**How it works (reading):** Two ribbons print at once; a plate serves as platen at the control-strip print point, interrupting the pressure roller there; built for postal money-order service.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A printing calculator must print calculated values and special information on a receipt and a control strip, but the receipt pressure roller on the control strip path needs a plate platen where the strip imprint arrives.

**Solution:** Two ribbons print at once; a plate serves as platen at the control-strip print point, interrupting the pressure roller there; built for postal money-order service.

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
