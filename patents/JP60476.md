## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type trays of a tray typewriter are heavy and hard to swap when a different type size or typeface, or a larger set of characters, is wanted.

**Solution:** Several trays hang on arm frames of a rotating changing shaft with planetary gearing that keeps each tray level, so a crank brings a different pair of trays into the working position.

**How it works:** Trays (1)-(4) are hung by side pivots on arm frames fixed to both ends of a changing shaft. A crank-driven shaft turns the arm frames through one gear pair and, through another gear pair and a loose gear, turns a central gear at a different speed. Equal gears on the tray pivots mesh with that central gear, so as the trays orbit the shaft they counter-rotate relative to the arms and stay level. A spring pin on the crank drops into a hole in the frame to hold the chosen trays at the top. The machine body is first slid back on the base, and in use the handle pushes a type up from the upper tray where a printing lever grips it and strikes it on the paper cylinder.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Type-tray changing device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a type-tray changing device for a typewriter which has two or more type trays. To each end of a changing shaft is fixed an arm frame with as many arms as there are trays; on each arm is mounted a pivot projecting from the middle of each side of one tray, so that the trays are arranged round the changing shaft. Equal gears are fixed to these pivots and mesh with a gear fitted on the changing shaft at their centre; and a gear train is provided to transmit rotation from a driving shaft parallel to the changing shaft to that gear and to the changing shaft. When the driving shaft is turned by a crank, each tray travels round the changing shaft while staying level, and a tray that was out of use changes places with the tray that was in the working position. The object is to let even a heavy tray be changed simply and easily in a typewriter, so that characters can be printed in a different size or typeface. Brief description of the drawings.
