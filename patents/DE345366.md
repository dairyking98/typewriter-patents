## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention consists in that that is the indentation the clutch causing, usually locked held intermediate member by a Electromagnet is triggered.

**How it works (reading):** An intermediate member holding the clutch in engagement is released by an electromagnet, so a weak magnet only trips the latch while the continuously moving drive does the work of paper transport or card printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electromagnetic control of a power-driven machine needs only to trigger a clutch, but the magnet cannot supply the force to drive the working parts itself.

**Solution:** An intermediate member holding the clutch in engagement is released by an electromagnet, so a weak magnet only trips the latch while the continuously moving drive does the work of paper transport or card printing.
