## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cards and envelopes too short for the ordinary paper feed rollers are hard to hold on the platen.

**Solution:** A curved three-plate pocket holder clipped to the platen ends by spring fingers holds the card edge without clamping it against the platen.

**How it works:** An outer plate, an inner plate against the platen and an intermediate plate form a pocket, all curved to the platen. The card edge enters the bevelled pocket mouth and is held between plates, not touching the platen. Forked spring members with pointed fingers snap over reduced platen ends; forked arms engage flats on the trunnions so it cannot turn. The projecting ends serve as handles.
