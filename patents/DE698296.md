**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine with printer) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a calculating machine with printer, in which the paper depends on the sign of the counter contents dare in two adjacent columns with the help of one adjustable on a pole Rider stop is moved with a counter stop of the machine frame works together to urinate the positive and negative balances separately for printing.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoting stop on the adjustable rider carries a control surface that the counter sign acts on, so the stop swings out of the path of the counter stop during carriage movement until the column is skipped.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Skipping a column on a positive or negative balance by swinging the whole stop rail needs a complicated, failure-prone linkage.

**Solution:** A pivoting stop on the adjustable rider carries a control surface that the counter sign acts on, so the stop swings out of the path of the counter stop during carriage movement until the column is skipped.
