## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (booking machine form strip labeling) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles

**How it works (reading):** Two clamps hold the strip, one fixed and one moving, so the strip advances automatically and a foot-pedal knife cuts it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Form strips are hard to label on machines with tangential slides and hand tearing.

**Solution:** Two clamps hold the strip, one fixed and one moving, so the strip advances automatically and a foot-pedal knife cuts it.
