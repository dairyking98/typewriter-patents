## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A standard scale bar cannot also serve as a tabulator without a separate device.

**Solution:** A tabulating scale-bar overlay that clips or screws onto the scale bar of any standard machine, with oval graduations in colored groups of four that serve as both scale and tabulator stops.
