## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting 1na chines and more particularly to means whereby tabulating operations may be automatically effected as the writing proceeds, and without the exercise of any care or judgment on the part of the operator and without the actuation of tabulating keys.

**How it works (reading):** Automatic tabulating by rack-disengaging devices, plus a mechanism that releases the feed rack from the pinion automatically when the carriage is returned for a new line.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic tabulating devices on the feed rack must not interfere with the carriage return movement.

**Solution:** Automatic tabulating by rack-disengaging devices, plus a mechanism that releases the feed rack from the pinion automatically when the carriage is returned for a new line.

**How it works:** The feed rack, carried on rear arms pivoted to the carriage end bars, meshes with the feed pinion on the escapement shaft. Adjustable disengaging devices on the rack cause it to disengage during the letter feed, skipping the carriage to a tab column. A connection from the line-space finger piece, whose swing lies in the direction of carriage return, releases the rack as the carriage is returned so the tabulating means is out of action. The pinion shaft turns back freely through a pawl-and-ratchet drum during return. Modified devices are shown in Figs. 7-8.

**Parts:** feed rack; feed pinion; disengaging device; line-spacing pawl; line-spacing ratchet wheel; escapement wheel; dog rocker; spring drum
