## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A visible typewriter's ribbon needs intermittent travel at each stroke without overthrow of the spools, and its position must follow the shifted platen.

**Solution:** Spring-loaded ratchet pawls on rock arms feed each spool at every stroke, with stop and brake arms against overthrow and a means of shifting the ribbon with the platen.

**How it works:** Ribbon spools sit on shafts on each side, each with a ratchet wheel at its lower end. A pawl with a resilient blade tooth is pushed against the ratchet and driven by rock arms on a rock shaft in the carriage track bar, once per printing stroke. A spring arm tooth stops reverse movement, while a spring-loaded stop arm locks the spool in place and a brake arm rubs on the shaft to prevent free rotation. The spool is keyed by a cross pin and removable with its support plate. The ribbon position is shifted for upper and lower case writing.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spool; spool shaft; ratchet wheel; pawl; resilient blade; rock arm; rock shaft; spring arm; stop arm; brake arm; support plate
