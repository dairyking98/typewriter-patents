## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink ribbons characterised by the material from which they are woven

**How it works (reading):** Stitched seams or stronger threads along the ribbon edges run in grooves of the guide rollers, so the ribbon cannot skid out of its path.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Calculator ribbons skid and narrow in use, so the print area is not fully covered.

**Solution:** Stitched seams or stronger threads along the ribbon edges run in grooves of the guide rollers, so the ribbon cannot skid out of its path.
