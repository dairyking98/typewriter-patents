## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (duplicate printing for adding machines and typewriters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**How it works (reading):** A thin transparent duplicate sheet is fed with the original through common feed rolls on opposite sides of the ink ribbon, so a single type hammer strikes both sheets and the copy reads through the sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making a duplicate copy normally needs carbon or manifolding paper, which wastes material and effort.

**Solution:** A thin transparent duplicate sheet is fed with the original through common feed rolls on opposite sides of the ink ribbon, so a single type hammer strikes both sheets and the copy reads through the sheet.
