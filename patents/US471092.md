## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The Hammond type-segment faces needed inking without a ribbon, and the imprint area needed limiting.

**Solution:** A felt pad with a short pile on a bow spring is pressed laterally against the type faces by a lever worked by the driving lever, fed from an ink fountain, plus a paper shield between paper and type.

**How it works:** The pad passes through an opening in the type-wheel guard wall on a holder carried by a bow spring that normally holds it clear of the segment. A bent lever, moved by the driving lever, presses the pad onto the type through a lock-nutted adjusting screw. An ink fountain with wells above feeds different pad parts, and a pan catches drips. A paper shield between the paper and type limits the imprint.
