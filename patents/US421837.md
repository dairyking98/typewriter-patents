## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic carriage return at line end needed a simpler construction.

**Solution:** A rack-bar frame hinged on a rod and lifted by a spring when a sliding bar notch comes over bevelled lugs.

**How it works:** The carriage frame holds a rack bar frame pivoting on a rod. A spring lifts it. A rear sliding bar with beveled notches holds the frame down until the notches align with lugs on the end pieces, which rise and free the rack so the carriage returns. Flanged rollers on a rear rod and yoke brackets stop the carriage lifting off.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** carriage frame; rack-bar; rack-bar frame; flat spring; rear sliding bar; beveled lug; flanged roller; yoke bracket
