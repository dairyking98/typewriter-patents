## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In the patent’s own words:** Its main object is to provide means whereby the ribbon may run'or be fed true and even, and without creasing, folding or doubling.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon is wound on spools above the top plate and passes through inclined slots in the spool rims and a guide bracket with grooved arms, so it runs true without creasing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon can crease, fold or double instead of running true and even.

**Solution:** The ribbon is wound on spools above the top plate and passes through inclined slots in the spool rims and a guide bracket with grooved arms, so it runs true without creasing.
