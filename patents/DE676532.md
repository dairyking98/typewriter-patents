## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Writing-tables with vertically-adjustable parts; Desks with double worktop of which one at least is separately height adjustable; Table or cupboards with upwardly concealable compartments

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a further embodiment of the table according to patent 661 979, in which two table top parts that can be moved independently of one another are arranged one behind the other.

**How it works (reading):** Three or more table top parts of different sizes are arranged in a row, with the outer strips moving up and down relative to the middle strip, which can be split and switched off as needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two independently moving table top parts are not enough for a varied working surface.

**Solution:** Three or more table top parts of different sizes are arranged in a row, with the outer strips moving up and down relative to the middle strip, which can be split and switched off as needed.
