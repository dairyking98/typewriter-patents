## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (friction change gear) · **Subject (patent classes):** Gearings providing a continuous range of gear ratios

**How it works (reading):** Friction rollers on spring-loaded forks turn together through gears and a rack, so their angle on the drum sets speed; parallel gives rest and perpendicular gives full speed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Friction gears with offset axes need complicated bearing adjustment to change ratio.

**Solution:** Friction rollers on spring-loaded forks turn together through gears and a rack, so their angle on the drum sets speed; parallel gives rest and perpendicular gives full speed.
