## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the paper feeding mechanism of typewriting machines and has for its object to provide improved devices of the class specified.

**How it works (reading):** Paper feed devices fitted to the platen frame with a paper table, apron and scale, under and behind the platen.
