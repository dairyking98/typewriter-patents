## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wide ribbon use needs adjustable ribbon travel that the operator can change from the keyboard.

**Solution:** A shifting arm driven from the universal bail has adjustable travel set by a keyboard lever.

**How it works:** The universal bail carries screws with heads at different distances. A sliding rod with shifting arms engages a movable plate against these stops so the arm receives a larger or smaller travel, controlled by a lever near the keyboard. The ribbon feeds from the spool faces next to the platen to supply slack. Diagram shows type paths on the ribbon at different adjustments.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** universal bail; key lever; shifting arm; sliding rod; ribbon carrier; spool; control lever
