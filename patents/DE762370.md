**How it works (reading):** Pairs of gears reach through the funnel wall and engage holes in the pre-sheet, aligning it on throw-in, and are driven by the carriage lift force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pre-lined sheets are aligned by hand before insertion, and the old conveying rollers have no exact position.

**Solution:** Pairs of gears reach through the funnel wall and engage holes in the pre-sheet, aligning it on throw-in, and are driven by the carriage lift force.
