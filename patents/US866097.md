## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressure rolls must be releasable from the platen simply, and the release must be retrofittable to existing machines.

**Solution:** A rock shaft with flats or cam surfaces on a tie rod swings the pressure roll shafts away from the platen, with springs pressing them back.

**How it works:** Turning the rock shaft acts on the yokes and cams so that the roll shafts rise off the platen, and they can be detached bodily.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** rock shaft with cams; pressure rolls on tie rod
