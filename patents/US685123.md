## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention are to improve the connections between the keys and the type-bars, so as to minimize the shocks to the operators fingers at both the beginning and completion of the key-strokes;

**How it works (reading):** Improved key connections to the type-bars, compound or link-motion type-bars, an inking pad in place of a ribbon, and vertical shifting of the type-bars and pad so either set of types can print.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key strokes jar the operator's fingers at start and end, type-bars are not sped up near the platen, and the bars can collide when receding; the ribbon arrangement is also a drawback.

**Solution:** Improved key connections to the type-bars, compound or link-motion type-bars, an inking pad in place of a ribbon, and vertical shifting of the type-bars and pad so either set of types can print.

**How it works:** Key levers drive compound type-bars that accelerate toward the platen and recede promptly; a vertical shift brings one of two type sets to the print point.
