## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention are to improve the connections between the keys and the type-bars, so as to minimize the shocks to the operators fingers at both the beginning and completion of the key-strokes;
