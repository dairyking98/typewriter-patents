## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A machine was wanted for shorthand writing with a small keyboard where the characters print along a straight line.

**Solution:** A type plate over fourteen key bars, pressed down on the paper by whichever keys are struck together, in a straight line.

**How it works:** Fourteen pivoted key bars are held by a single stem through a block. A rectangular frame plate carrying the removable type plate (characters formed integrally on it, or on the key bars) sits above their inner ends, held by a flat adjustable spring and limited by rubber or felt stops. Striking keys moves the plate or bars to print the characters in a straight line across a paper roll; the key buttons have a tail with a facsimile of the character. An inking ribbon is carried on a supported frame (details cut off).

**Parts:** key bar; button; block; type plate; frame plate; flat spring; stops; ribbon support; paper roll
