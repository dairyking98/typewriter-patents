## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide simple and improved mechanism for feeding the carriage or paper-holder of a 'typewriter step-by-step across the machine, by manipulation of the key-board or space-bar in the usual Way.

**How it works (reading):** A feed mechanism with a pulley and an escapement wheel and holding detent steps the carriage one tooth or allows a larger movement.
