## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers must be moved either electrically or by hand, and the printing stroke needs a common drive for all keys.

**Solution:** An electromagnet built into the machine, closed by a contact common to all key levers, energizes the selected key lever part way down, completes the stroke and prints, then de-energizes before the lever reaches the bottom.
