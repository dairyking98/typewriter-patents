**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to inking mechanism for machines of the type-writer class, and has for an object to provide a simple organization for effecting the gradual shifting of an inking-ribbon past the printing-point and in proper relation to the typebars of the machine to interpose between the Working types on such bars and the platen an inking-ribbon adapted to ink the successively-operating types.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanism for a top-strike machine should shift the ribbon past the print point in both directions with little operator effort.

**Solution:** Two ribbon rolls each act alternately as supply and take-up, driven by ratchets and pawls on oscillating carriers linked together and operated by the key action.

**How it works:** The ribbon is wound on rolls at left and right and guided past the print point through slotted arms and curved fingers on a U-strip carried by an oscillating rock-frame that draws the ribbon away from the printed character. Each roll has a ratchet wheel, a driving pawl on a pivoted carrier and a retaining spring pawl. The two pawl carriers are linked to move together, and only one pawl engages at a time depending on the roll's axial position. Key action oscillates the frame and carriers, winding the ribbon step by step. The carriage is pulled by a spring drum.
