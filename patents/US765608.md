## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen line-spacing needs a simpler mechanism with an adjustable stop for multiple spacing.

**Solution:** A handle at the left of the platen carries a pivoted pawl that spaces the ratchet by any amount set by an adjustable stop.
