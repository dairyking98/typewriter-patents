## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In manifolding machines the carbon must be returned to a fixed zero position after withdrawal, and nothing reliably does this, so the carbon can be severed with the paper.

**Solution:** The carbon roll shaft carries a pinion meshing with a spur wheel that turns as the carbon feeds; an adjustable arm on the wheel sounds an alarm at a set point, and the wheel is then turned back to rewind the carbon until a fixed stop arrests it.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
