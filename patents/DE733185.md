## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (booking machine automatic skip to balance columns) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**How it works (reading):** The horizontal motor key also engages the skip gearing, so the carriage jumps directly to the correct balance column according to the sign of the balance, with stop elements limiting the jump.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On booking machines the operator must press a skip key for each column jump, and a wrong key press posts an entry to the wrong column.

**Solution:** The horizontal motor key also engages the skip gearing, so the carriage jumps directly to the correct balance column according to the sign of the balance, with stop elements limiting the jump.
