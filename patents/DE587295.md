## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for aligning Printed postcards clamped in a typewriter, the gaps in the text by means of the typewriter should be filled in while producing a Double piece of machine lettering.

**How it works (reading):** A line network or edge marking on the front of a back-coated copy sheet lets the operator set the sheet exactly over the invisible postcard at chosen gap positions before clamping it in the typewriter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Filling text gaps on a printed postcard through a carbon-copy sheet gives faint copies, and the clamped stack is hard to set up accurately.

**Solution:** A line network or edge marking on the front of a back-coated copy sheet lets the operator set the sheet exactly over the invisible postcard at chosen gap positions before clamping it in the typewriter.
