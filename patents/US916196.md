## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a holder or attachment adapted tobe applied to the platen of a typewriting machine for writing upon comparatively.

**How it works (reading):** A flexible sheet (Manila paper) wrapped around the platen with a slot whose celluloid retainers hold the name-slip, so the slip turns with the platen like an ordinary sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stiff, narrow name-slips for index holders cannot be held reliably against the platen by the usual clasps or guides.

**Solution:** A flexible sheet (Manila paper) wrapped around the platen with a slot whose celluloid retainers hold the name-slip, so the slip turns with the platen like an ordinary sheet.
