## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding assemblies, e.g. book-like assemblies made up of single sheets or forms; Letter-cards or letter-sheets, i.e. cards or sheets each of which is to be folded with the message inside and to serve as its own envelope for mailing

**How it works (reading):** A perforated card with a folded address strip, a carbon-stop strip that is folded back, and a rubberised back combines all three.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Postcards need folded address strips, a carbon stop and a writable top edge, which other cards only offer separately.

**Solution:** A perforated card with a folded address strip, a carbon-stop strip that is folded back, and a rubberised back combines all three.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
