## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bar hangers let dust into the bearings, and the bar position cannot be adjusted for wear.

**Solution:** A type bar and hanger with a ball bearing that excludes dust and an adjustment to compensate for wear and to position the bar.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
