## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need many keys, imperfect alignment, and many parts.

**Solution:** The whole type-bar basket rotates and slides axially so a single set of key tongues strikes lower case, capital or figure bars.

**How it works:** Type-bars are fulcrumed on a wire ring in a basket mounted on a shaft that can rotate and slide. Each key pulls down a T-shaped tongue that engages a slotted mortise in a type-bar; the mortise is long enough that one tongue can act on three bars. Rotating the basket one bar-pitch presents capitals, shifting it axially presents figures and punctuation. Elbow-levers rotate the shaft for the two non-normal positions. Twenty-six keys thus serve three sets.

**Numbers:** 26 keys; three sets of type-bars
