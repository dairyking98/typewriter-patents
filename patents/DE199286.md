## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** A feeler on the ribbon runs off the spool and actuates a pawl and toothed sensing lever, which shifts a coupling sleeve to reverse the drive to the spools automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools on typewriters must reverse winding direction when the ribbon runs out, but existing reversers are prone to jamming and ribbon slack.

**Solution:** A feeler on the ribbon runs off the spool and actuates a pawl and toothed sensing lever, which shifts a coupling sleeve to reverse the drive to the spools automatically.
