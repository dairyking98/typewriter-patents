## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Upper and lower case letters are wanted without enlarging the machine, and in different ink colours.

**Solution:** Two concentric rings of staggered typebars (capitals and small letters), moved to the same centre by cranks of different throw, each ring inked by its own annular felt pad.

**How it works:** Each type sits on a bar pivoted on a crank arm, which is pivoted on a fixed fulcrum on a common circle. Crank arms alternate in length so every other bar rests about a quarter inch farther from the centre, putting types in two staggered concentric lines; the longer throw brings each type to the exact printing centre. Two annular felt pads with different opening diameters, separated by a washer and held by a threaded ring in a recess of the housing, ink the two ranks, so capitals can take a different colour.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** type; typebar; crank arm; fulcrum; felt ink pad; washer; annular plate; threaded ring; housing

**Numbers:** about 1/4 inch difference in rest radius between rows
