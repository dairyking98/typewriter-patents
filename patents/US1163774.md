## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Billing and form-work on a typewriter using continuous multi-ply webs needs a ribbon that lies across the front of the platen without being dragged up when the sheets are pulled forward, and that can be moved aside and wound in either direction.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools sit beyond the ends of the platen on a rear shaft, with the ribbon carried forward in a swinging guide frame whose bar also spaces and tears the work-sheets, and a clutch frame picks which spool the line-space ratchet winds.

**How it works:** Two ribbon spools are loosely mounted at the platen ends on a shaft behind the platen. The ribbon leads forward from each spool, is twisted to a vertical plane, runs along the printing line close to the platen, and returns to the other spool. A swinging frame carries the ribbon guides and a tie bar that spaces the work-sheets from the ribbon and gives a tearing edge. A sliding clutch frame, moved by finger-pieces, connects one spool to the shaft and disconnects the other, with a spring detent holding either position. A ratchet on the shaft is driven by a dog on a lever linked to the line-space slide, so each line space winds the ribbon a step. Brake shoes with spring tension keep the idle spool from spinning, and the frame swings up and back to clear the printing line.
