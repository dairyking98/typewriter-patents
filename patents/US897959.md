## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to make a simple type-writing machine, which is operated by pneumatic or other fluid power, the fingers of the operator serving only to call that power into action instead of serving to do all the work.

**How it works (reading):** Key presses only control a pneumatic power device (compressed or rarefied air) that actuates the typebars.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter fingers do all the work of printing.

**Solution:** Key presses only control a pneumatic power device (compressed or rarefied air) that actuates the typebars.

**How it works:** Keys operate a motor-controlling frame, which lets air power act on a pneumatic device that moves a motor frame and thereby the typebars mounted in a hanger ring. Air is supplied from a compressed or rarefied source.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md), [Power-driven type action](../features/bar-power-action.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** key; type bar; hanger ring; pneumatic power device; motor frame; motor-controlling frame; fulcrum plate
