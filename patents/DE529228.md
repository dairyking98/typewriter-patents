## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting the type lever segment so the levers strike the platen roller needs a special adjustment device on the machine frame.

**Solution:** The segment sits on a stamped cross-shaped switching frame with a variable-angle adjusting screw in its lower web, the frame is suspended in three points on a rocking plate, and stop pins set the upper and lower positions.

According to the invention, the type lever segment closes with the lower arm of the segment supporting switching frame a variable angle for the fitting of the segment a. The angle adjustment between the segment and the switching frame is done by a Adjusting screw mounted in the lower web of the frame. This arrangement allows the segment can be adjusted so that the levers correctly attach to the roller attacks. There is therefore no special adjustment device on the machine frame required, so that this, as also the switchover by shifting the segment frame takes place, can be kept extremely simple in its structure. Another simplification the manufacture of the machine is also achieved by the fact that the type guide directly is formed by an extension of the switching frame.
