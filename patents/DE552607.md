## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Multiple web-feeding apparatus for webs superimposed during printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for separating and smoothing the paper layers in flat-plate typewriters for writing on continuous paper webs.

**How it works (reading):** Flat partition plates between the paper layers are set with staggered gaps and pivot on a fulcrum below, limited by stops, and a straightening and separating device behind the carbon paper smooths folds before the paper reaches it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring pressure smoothing surfaces and rollers damage the paper and leave wrinkles, and curved separating plates still wrinkle the paper because tension differs between zones.

**Solution:** Flat partition plates between the paper layers are set with staggered gaps and pivot on a fulcrum below, limited by stops, and a straightening and separating device behind the carbon paper smooths folds before the paper reaches it.
