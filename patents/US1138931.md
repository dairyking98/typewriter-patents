## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars and their hanger joints bind and wear, and assembly and fitting of the bearings is difficult.

**Solution:** Type bar head with an eye and V-groove race, with balls between coned disks to form an anti-friction bearing that can be assembled by a stated method.
