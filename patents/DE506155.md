## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ribbon drive needs to stop when the carriage returns and when the tab key advances the carriage, normally using separate special clutches.

**Solution:** Use the one driver pawl as the only coupling element: lifted radially for carriage return, and moved axially onto an untoothed step of the shaft for the tab key.

**How it works:** A drive wheel turns loosely on an axially sliding axle bushing and takes the ribbon-drive axis along by a pawl on its drive plate while the carriage moves forward. When the carriage is returned by hand, the bushing, braked by a spring, lags and its pin lifts the pawl out of the shaft toothing, so there is no ribbon motion or noise. Pressing the tab key shifts the drive wheel, plate and pawl axially so the pawl rides on an untoothed stepped part of the shaft and does not drive the bevel gear.
