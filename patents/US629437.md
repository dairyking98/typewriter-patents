## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage and bed of a platen typewriter needed to run more easily and be removable together to substitute a carriage of different length, and paper holding needed to be accurate and adjustable for paper or cards.

**Solution:** A friction-roll carriage on bed rails removable with the top plate, plus adjustable presser rolls and a tilting platen frame that exposes the print line.

**How it works:** Front and rear bed rails on pedestals on the top plate are tied together and carry friction rolls linked by side bars between channels in the rails and in the carriage sides. Margin stops sit on the front bed rail. Presser rolls hold the paper against the platen with adjustable spring tension, and the platen sits in a tilting frame that can be locked or turned up. Double escapement pawls work on a rack bar, and a variable line-space regulator sets the platen step. The text is cut off before the full detail.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** bed rail; tie bar; friction roll; carriage side; margin stop; presser roll; tilting platen frame; rack bar; double escapement pawl; line-space regulator
