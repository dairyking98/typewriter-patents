## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates particularly to typewriting machines of that class in which the sheet or page to be printed upon is supported fiat ing mechanism is movably supported upon an open frame.

**How it works (reading):** A yielding work-gage mounted along the platen edge is depressed by the machine frame, clamping the sheet against it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work sheets on a flat platen shift and are hard to adjust and reposition.

**Solution:** A yielding work-gage mounted along the platen edge is depressed by the machine frame, clamping the sheet against it.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
