## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return and platen feed need to be done quickly and with little shock, either automatically or by hand, with minimum effort from the operator.

**Solution:** An electromagnet with an armature and a rack-and-pinion drive reverses the carriage and feeds the platen, with the magnet circuit closed by circuit-closers tied to the typewriter.

**How it works:** A magnet in an electric circuit pulls an armature whose segmental rack meshes with a pinion on a shaft to return the carriage and advance the platen.
