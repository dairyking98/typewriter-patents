## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon edges are differently coloured and the operator needs a simple way to choose which edge prints.

**Solution:** A two-part ribbon carrier whose upper part slides on a stem and is raised or lowered by a toggle controlled by a finger-piece, keeping the same throw.

**How it works:** The ribbon-carrier slides on a stem pivoted to the vibrating lever. A toggle links stem and carrier shank. A rod with a finger-piece engages the toggle's eye; pushing opens the toggle and lowers the carrier to its lowest stop, pulling closes it and raises it. The vibrating throw is unchanged.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier; stem; vibrating lever; toggle; finger-piece; sliding rod; bichrome ribbon

**Improves on:**

- Underwood single-piece ribbon carrier: two-part carrier shifts ribbon position with equal throw
