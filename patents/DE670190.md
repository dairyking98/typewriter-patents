**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for selecting statistical or accounting information with the same identifier from a series of documents any mark that is successively brought into the path of a radiant energy which after passing through the features to be scanned on one of the radiant energy can be influenced and the retention of the selected information Device arrives.

**How it works (reading):** A negative search plate with the wanted identifier is placed in the beam path between the documents and a radiation-sensitive device, so the beam is interrupted only on a match; a photographic registration can then be triggered.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Finding documents with a given identifier in a large series by mechanical search is slow and mechanically complicated.

**Solution:** A negative search plate with the wanted identifier is placed in the beam path between the documents and a radiation-sensitive device, so the beam is interrupted only on a match; a photographic registration can then be triggered.
