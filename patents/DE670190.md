## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Finding documents with a given identifier in a large series by mechanical search is slow and mechanically complicated.

**Solution:** A negative search plate with the wanted identifier is placed in the beam path between the documents and a radiation-sensitive device, so the beam is interrupted only on a match; a photographic registration can then be triggered.
