## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Two-armed copy holders are steadier than one-armed ones but obstruct inserting the sheet, and transmit typing vibration to the copy.

**Solution:** Make the holder plate with its cantilever arms swing aside, with cranked arms guiding the paper back, and optionally stand it on a floor plate with a foot-operated line indicator lever.

**How it works:** A height-adjustable holder plate with a line indicator and paper clip hangs on two forward arms sliding and turning in tube stands clamped to the table or standing on a base plate. Pins hold the height; the arms can be turned aside to insert the sheet, or fixed by screws. Cranks in the arms bend the leaving paper backwards. A toothed rail, pawl and Bowden cable move the line indicator from a lever, or a foot lever in the floor-standing version.
