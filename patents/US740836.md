## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Dust and grit from erasures fall onto typebar bearings of front-strike machines and impede their action.

**Solution:** A segmental dust shield above the typebar pivots that automatically moves aside when a key is struck.

**How it works:** Key levers with sublevers drive typebars through actuating links. The universal bar on a rock-shaft is linked through a bell-crank and link to a segmental dust shield between platen face and typebar pivots. The shield rides on guide rods in grooved rollers on the segment, pulled by contractile springs. When any key is pressed the shield moves out of the typebar's path and returns after.
