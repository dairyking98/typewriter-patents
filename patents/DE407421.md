## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mounting or connecting key buttons on or to key levers

**How it works (reading):** Pliers push a piston through a threaded spindle lever; gripping claws in a capsule pull off or fit the key ring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Key rings on typewriter keys are hard to remove and fit by hand.

**Solution:** Pliers push a piston through a threaded spindle lever; gripping claws in a capsule pull off or fit the key ring.
