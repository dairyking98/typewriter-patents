## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check branding apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of my invention is to pass an electric current through the branding or marking figures, letters, or characters, which are thereby raised to a proper degree of heat for the purpose of branding or marking said letters, figures, or characters on, into, or through the check or article to be marked or branded.

**How it works (reading):** Raised figures or characters on a revolving punch-holding head are heated by an electric current so that they brand or burn the marks into the check.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bank punches could be filled in and re-punched to a higher value, so a way to mark check amounts that could not be altered was wanted.

**Solution:** Raised figures or characters on a revolving punch-holding head are heated by an electric current so that they brand or burn the marks into the check.

**Features:** [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [SEL:dial](../features/sel-dial.md)
