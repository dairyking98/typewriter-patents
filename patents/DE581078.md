## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** Put the line feed ratchet arm on a horizontal axis behind the roller knob, with the lever joined to it so it folds down beside the knob for packing.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A portable typewriter carriage should be as short as possible compared with the paper width, but earlier line feed arrangements left the knob far from the roller or needed a complicated body to fold the lever.

**Solution:** Put the line feed ratchet arm on a horizontal axis behind the roller knob, with the lever joined to it so it folds down beside the knob for packing.

**How it works:** A line index wheel smaller than the platen is fixed to the roller and has two tooth rings, one on its circumference and one on its face. A line feed pawl engages the face ring and steps the roller by line, while a locking roller on the circumference teeth, carried on a U-shaped sheet bracket pivoted on the carriage end plate, fixes the roller at each line. The pawl arm turns about a horizontal axis behind the knob, which also serves as the hinge to fold the line lever down. The knob remains in place with the paper edge only about half a roller diameter from it. An adjustment device sets the interline spacing.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
