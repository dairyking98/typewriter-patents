## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Desks with a lowering typewriter plate leave little room beside the machine for forms.

**Solution:** The typewriter plate lifts on a rod and counterweight, and the whole frame with the plate rolls out from a dummy front wall on floor rollers, leaving compartments beside the machine; a form container tips out as the plate rises.
