## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the special design of a one-piece covered rack frame for lever type typewriters, especially so-called portable typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A one-piece deep-drawn sheet-metal frame has a bottom edge all round and a flanged upper edge, so it stiffens the whole machine without extra tabs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Frames for portable typewriters needed separate stiffening tabs or added weight because the sheet-metal frame lacked a full bottom edge.

**Solution:** A one-piece deep-drawn sheet-metal frame has a bottom edge all round and a flanged upper edge, so it stiffens the whole machine without extra tabs.
