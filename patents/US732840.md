## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A second set of narrow character devices can be thrown into or out of action with the feed-dogs and feed-wheel, so the carriage advances a wide or a narrow step between letters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says a flat-bed machine with both wide and narrow capitals needs a carriage feed that can be quickly changed between the two letter spacings.

**Solution:** A second set of narrow character devices can be thrown into or out of action with the feed-dogs and feed-wheel, so the carriage advances a wide or a narrow step between letters.

**Features:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
