## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line delimiting device for typewriters and the like, in which the switching mechanism from the margin stop by means of a counter-stop double-armed lever and locked by a perpendicular to the locking movement Oscillating movement is canceled again.

**How it works (reading):** A single double-armed lever between the margin stop and the rear derailleur swings on a pin to lock the carriage, and is displaced perpendicular to its swing plane to release the lock.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The margin stop lock used two perpendicular motions on separate members, giving a complicated arrangement with large height and several failure points.

**Solution:** A single double-armed lever between the margin stop and the rear derailleur swings on a pin to lock the carriage, and is displaced perpendicular to its swing plane to release the lock.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
