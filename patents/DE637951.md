## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing device in which the types of discs or Wheels sit that are moved against the printing substrate to print a type.

**How it works (reading):** The locking member is mounted resiliently and engages the stationary gear before reaching the locked position, either as a gear driven in the same direction as the ratchet or as a toothed member on a pivoting arm with a slotted connection, so relative speed at engagement is reduced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a printing device with type wheels on a moving carrier, the locking-lever toothing must reliably engage the gear it locks, and the engagement is not secure enough for tamper-evident printing.

**Solution:** The locking member is mounted resiliently and engages the stationary gear before reaching the locked position, either as a gear driven in the same direction as the ratchet or as a toothed member on a pivoting arm with a slotted connection, so relative speed at engagement is reduced.
