## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The patent addresses the printing action of a front-strike typewriter.

**Solution:** Typebars are carried in sets on supports and struck by a hammer lever worked from the key levers through cam levers, stops and a shift bar.

**How it works:** Key levers press a cross-bail on a rock-shaft that moves a hammer lever; cam levers on the key levers and rock-shafts position the type actuating levers against stops so a selected typebar receives the hammer blow. A shift bar moves the typebars for case change, a space bar works the escapement through a bail, and a ribbon carrier is operated by connected levers.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** key lever; cross-bail; rock-shaft; hammer lever; cam lever; type carrier support; typebar; stop; shift bar; space bar; escapement wheel; ribbon carrier
