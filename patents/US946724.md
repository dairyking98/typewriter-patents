## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Supports or magazines for piles from which articles are to be separated adapted to support articles substantially horizontally, e.g. for separation from top of pile

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention has reference to improvements in paper feed attachments for typewriters and is designed to provide a reservoir for sheets of paper to be fed successively and automatically to the platen into position to be operated upon by the type without the necessity of the operator placing the sheets as needed;
