## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing die for underlines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** The underline shape sits in the middle of the die, with the main hold-down elevation just below it, so the underline can be embossed without losing a line and can be added later.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A standard underlining die removes a full line and distorts the type, and the underline cannot be added after the line is embossed without pushing the text.

**Solution:** The underline shape sits in the middle of the die, with the main hold-down elevation just below it, so the underline can be embossed without losing a line and can be added later.
