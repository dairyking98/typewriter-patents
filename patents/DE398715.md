## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon direction must reverse automatically at the end of travel with fewer components.

**Solution:** One changeover pawl sits between two tooth crowns on a shaft, shifted by a rail, so it engages whichever crown matches the ribbon direction and displaces the coupling shaft.
