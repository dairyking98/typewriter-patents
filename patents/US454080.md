## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Details of a typebar machine needed improvement: platen lifting, margin setting, typebar bracket fit, ribbon spools.

**Solution:** A set of small improvements to a typebar typewriter, including an eccentric-head rivet that corrects non-parallel bracket arms and a friction-held margin pin.

**How it works:** The platen is removable with screw-threaded gudgeons and a bell as knob. A hook under a cross rod stops the carriage lifting when paper is pulled. The margin gage is a slotted plate with a bell-trip pin held by friction so the bell blow cannot shift it but a finger can. Typebars pivot in brackets on convex bearings secured by a rivet whose head is eccentric, so turning it adjusts parallelism. Spool standards and a bail are also covered.
