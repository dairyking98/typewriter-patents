## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**How it works (reading):** A releasing lever system on the platen frame lets the paper-feeding mechanism be moved out of cooperation with the platen and held there by hand pressure or locked inoperative.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-feed rolls on the platen frame need to be released from the platen, either held released by the operator or locked out of operation, and the existing arrangement does not give this.

**Solution:** A releasing lever system on the platen frame lets the paper-feeding mechanism be moved out of cooperation with the platen and held there by hand pressure or locked inoperative.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
