## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator number reading device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** Cards on a carriage advanced by the calculator's trigger key pass a gap, and two angled mirrors and a magnifying lens show the number straight ahead, with lamps lighting it through the gap.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators must turn their head and body to read numbers from card sheets while transferring them through a calculator, which slows the work.

**Solution:** Cards on a carriage advanced by the calculator's trigger key pass a gap, and two angled mirrors and a magnifying lens show the number straight ahead, with lamps lighting it through the gap.
