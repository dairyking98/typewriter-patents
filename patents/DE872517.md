## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type wheel coloring, telegraph devices) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inking apparatus with ink applied by pads or rotary discs; Printing mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A brush soaked in ink, with bristles free to move in every direction, paints the type. A slowly rotating disc brush or a longer brush spreads wear, and it is fed ink from a pitted rotating roller or sphere in a sealed container.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Felt inking rollers shed fibres onto telegraph type wheels, and a roller fails when the type wheel also moves axially.

**Solution:** A brush soaked in ink, with bristles free to move in every direction, paints the type. A slowly rotating disc brush or a longer brush spreads wear, and it is fed ink from a pitted rotating roller or sphere in a sealed container.
