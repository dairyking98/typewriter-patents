## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** A roll holding a duplicating strip is attached to the carriage and turns automatically when the sheet is drawn away, rewound by a spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicate bills need carbon sheets without manual handling.

**Solution:** A roll holding a duplicating strip is attached to the carriage and turns automatically when the sheet is drawn away, rewound by a spring.

**Features:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
