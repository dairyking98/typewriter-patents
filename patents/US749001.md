## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Controlling a continuously revolving type wheel so that a chosen character is held momentarily still at the print point and impressed.

**Solution:** A continuously rotating type wheel is given a bodily diametral motion opposite to the selected character's travel so the character stays at the printing point while an impression is taken.

**How it works:** A power-driven shaft rotates a driving cylinder and the vertical type-wheel shaft continuously. The type-wheel shaft is carried in upper and lower rocking plates on a common axis offset from the wheel axis, so the whole wheel can be displaced bodily. A key selects the moment, producing the composite motion in which the character is momentarily stationary at the printing point. The wheel carries several circumferential rows and can be moved between two positions by the operator. Paper is on a paper roll carriage.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md)

**Parts:** type wheel; type-wheel shaft; rocking plate; driving cylinder; key bank; paper roll; hand wheel

**Improves on:**
