**How it works (reading):** Typebars driving intermediate levers serve as a common drive for both hole punching and sensing, with a reversing device switching the direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punch-card punching and sensing need separate linkages, and the card mechanism is a second drive.

**Solution:** Typebars driving intermediate levers serve as a common drive for both hole punching and sensing, with a reversing device switching the direction.
