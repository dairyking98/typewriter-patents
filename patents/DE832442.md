## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (forwarding sheet setting, booking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering by marks or formations on the paper being fed

**How it works (reading):** A notch is punched into the forwarding sheet after booking and engaged by a locating pin, with adjustable side stops along the sheet guide.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Existing forwarding-sheet guides cannot be adjusted when the base sheet's position differs.

**Solution:** A notch is punched into the forwarding sheet after booking and engaged by a locating pin, with adjustable side stops along the sheet guide.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
