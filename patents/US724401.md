## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet output section

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide an attachment for type-writing machines, including an interchangeable line-spacer, so constructed that the machine may be set to print upon lines any desired distance apart.

**How it works (reading):** A card-feed attachment with an interchangeable line-spacer and a supply receptacle; cards are fed to guides, printed on the platen, and carried over and deposited in a receptacle automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Postal cards and index cards must be inserted by hand, and the machine must be raised after loading the card supply.

**Solution:** A card-feed attachment with an interchangeable line-spacer and a supply receptacle; cards are fed to guides, printed on the platen, and carried over and deposited in a receptacle automatically.

**Parts:** supply receptacle; interchangeable line-spacer; card guides
