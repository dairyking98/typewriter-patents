## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage for an adding typewriter needs paper feed, line spacing, tabulating and marginal stops arranged on a main carriage that also carries the platen.

**Solution:** A main carriage carries the platen and adding mechanism, and a relatively movable subcarriage carries the paper-feed devices and line-spacing mechanism; tabulating stops and adjustable marginal stops sit on the main carriage.

**How it works:** A line-space ratchet-wheel and pawl turns the platen; the subcarriage moves relative to the main carriage, and stops engage members on the carriage.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** main carriage; subcarriage; platen; adding mechanism; tabulating stops; marginal stops; line-space ratchet-wheel
