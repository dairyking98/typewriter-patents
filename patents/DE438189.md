## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a jamming device for the stepwise advancement of the paper carriage of Typewriters in which a swinging pawl carrier is a fixed one as well a loose pawl carries, which alternately in the under the action of Engage the paper carriage drive spring with the ratchet wheel.

**How it works (reading):** A toggle joint limits the movable pawl's stroke so it engages the ratchet without impact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stop-limited pawls on the carriage ratchet are noisy and sliding ratchets are prone to dirt.

**Solution:** A toggle joint limits the movable pawl's stroke so it engages the ratchet without impact.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
