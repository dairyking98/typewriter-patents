## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on typewriters and similar machines for picking up the when the paper carriage is returned by the jamming device using a loose gear tooth that is rotatably mounted on the ratchet axle during the carriage return movement out of engagement with the ratchet-holding friction member.

**How it works (reading):** A friction member with arms close to the outer edge of the ratchet wheel, plus a short arm acting near the ratchet axis, swings the loose switching tooth sideways at the start of the carriage return, so only small forces are needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting the loose ratchet tooth during carriage return needed too much force, and friction parts wore quickly.

**Solution:** A friction member with arms close to the outer edge of the ratchet wheel, plus a short arm acting near the ratchet axis, swings the loose switching tooth sideways at the start of the carriage return, so only small forces are needed.
