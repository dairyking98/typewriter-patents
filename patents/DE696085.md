## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a pivot lever bearing jumper for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The switching bridge is pivoted on levers with helically twisted tension springs above and below the pivot axis, pulling in the same rotational direction, and the lever bodies are light metal with a hard striking surface.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Fast typing needs a light, easy-moving switching bridge that returns reliably, but the old pivot-lever bearings lacked this.

**Solution:** The switching bridge is pivoted on levers with helically twisted tension springs above and below the pivot axis, pulling in the same rotational direction, and the lever bodies are light metal with a hard striking surface.
