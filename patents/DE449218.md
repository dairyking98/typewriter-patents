## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A form with more lines than the control sheet needs cannot be returned past the lines already written.

**Solution:** A rack on a sliding carriage is pressed into engagement with a gear on the platen roller and can be disengaged, so the control sheet can be moved back by the full form length.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device on typewriters and calculating machines for importing and exporting of forms. The invention relates to a device on typewriters and calculating machines for importing and exporting forms, copies of which are on a common Control sheets are to be recorded in direct succession. Such devices are known in which one on the paper roll seated gear is rotated by means of a rack. According to the present invention, the rack is on one in one Guide guided slide arranged so that they can be moved at any time the gear can be coupled or uncoupled from it. This makes it possible that, if. the form more lines contains than were written on the control sheet, the control sheet initially returned to the line following the last line described and then only from this position, withdrawn by the entire length of the head of the form can be.
