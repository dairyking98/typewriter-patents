## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (counter shifting in typewriters with calculator)

**How it works (reading):** A rocker arm and angle lever linked by a spring connect the tab keys to the counter's driver handle so the counter moves automatically to the chosen column.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On calculating typewriters the counter must be moved to each column by hand, which causes errors.

**Solution:** A rocker arm and angle lever linked by a spring connect the tab keys to the counter's driver handle so the counter moves automatically to the chosen column.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for moving the counter with typewriters Arithmetic unit There are devices for moving the counter on typewriters known with arithmetic unit, where between the tab keys and the counter a transmission device is switched on, so that when a tab key is pressed the counter is automatically moved to the corresponding column. The invention provides such a device for typewriters, on the front wall of which Approximately at the height of the paper cart, an arithmetic unit is attached. With such Typewriters has so far done the shifting of the counter by means of a handle protruding from the arithmetic unit by hand, which gives rise to errors. To the invention is the known automatic displacement of the counter achieved in a simple and advantageous manner by one with the known driver handle of the arithmetic unit in a force-fit connection rocker arm, which by a handlebar connected to an angle lever under the action of a spring is, on one leg of which attack rods connected to the tab key levers.
