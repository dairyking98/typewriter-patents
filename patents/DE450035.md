## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Learners at the typewriter need to see neither the keyboard nor the writing line, so a hiding shield is wanted.

**Solution:** A sheet metal shield hinged to a holding rail clips to the machine frame and folds between horizontal (hides keyboard) and vertical (hides the line) positions.
