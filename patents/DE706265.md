## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (impulse combination evaluation, type wheel setting) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using aggregate motion translation

**How it works (reading):** The largest travel of the type wheel is assigned to a later adjusting member, and the adjusting members are set by a cam drive in place of magnets.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Path-sum pulse evaluators need intermediate links and magnets, and the largest travel must be assigned to the element nearest the type wheel.

**Solution:** The largest travel of the type wheel is assigned to a later adjusting member, and the adjusting members are set by a cam drive in place of magnets.

**Features:** [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)
