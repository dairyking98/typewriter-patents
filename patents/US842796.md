## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-levers are locked only by the paper-carriage, so keys cannot be used at the end of a line to finish a word.

**Solution:** A nut-and-spring arrangement with a rod and cam releases the key-lever locking so the keys can be used at any carriage position, such as at the margin.

**Features:** [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** paper-carriage stop; rod with crank; link; lever; lock-nut; spring
