## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage needed a normal tension to advance for letter feed, with a way to turn it at will and produce line spacing together with carriage return.

**Solution:** A pneumatic cylinder mounted parallel to the line of carriage travel, connected to the escapement, keeps the carriage under tension; air admitted to the end of the carriage cylinder also admits air to a line-space cylinder so line space and return happen together.
