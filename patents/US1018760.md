## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewriters provided with tabulating or colunin-stop mechanism designed to efl'ect the release of the carriage from the control of the usual letter-spacing mechanism and to permit the carriage.

**How it works (reading):** A brake shoe is operated by the tabulating key and retards the spring barrel that drives the carriage so it slows before it reaches the stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tabulating carriage strikes its stop with jarring shock because the spring barrel drives it at full speed.

**Solution:** A brake shoe is operated by the tabulating key and retards the spring barrel that drives the carriage so it slows before it reaches the stop.
