## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Japanese typewriters need a simple, compact printing mechanism that handles many characters.

**Solution:** Many types are held sideways by a comb spring in thin channel type cases arranged around disks as a cylindrical magazine; a key raises one case and a hammer arm strikes the rear of the chosen type.

**How it works:** Each type case is a bent sheet with a comb spring whose tongues enter notches in the types. The cases form a cylindrical magazine on disks that is turned and slid by a handle until the wanted character shows at a window. Pressing a key works levers that push up the case to the print position in front of the platen. A little later a cord-linked bell crank swings a printing arm with a hammer that strikes the rear end of the type, printing it.
