## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A type-wheel machine needs a way to set the wheel to the chosen character from a keyboard and then print, with a simple lever train.

**Solution:** Each key lever depresses a bail that rotates a spring-returned toothed sector until its arm hits the pressed key lever, which sets the type wheel through gearing; pressing further pushes the whole sliding frame, wheel included, radially onto the platen.

**How it works:** Keys in a left and right group act through key levers on a bail carried by an elbow lever. The bail presses the radial edge of a toothed sector, which turns against its return spring until a pin on its arm strikes the depressed type lever, so the sector stops at an angle that depends on which key was pressed. The sector meshes with a toothed wheel on the type-wheel shaft, which therefore turns to the matching character. With the wheel unable to turn further, more key travel pushes the sliding frame carrying sector, gear and wheel back against the paper roller against a spring, giving the impression. On release springs return the frame, sector and wheel to rest. Each key group has its own elbow lever and drives the sector in the opposite direction.
