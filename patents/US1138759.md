## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Stencil machines cannot fit three or four extra characters into a line, and hand feeding is slow and uneven.

**Solution:** A device gives the stencil a variable feed so that normal letter spacing is reduced on certain lines and more characters are placed, without disturbing the normal feed.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
