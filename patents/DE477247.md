## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (slip insertion for cash registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on such slip insertion devices on cash registers that have a fuse against withdrawing the inserted print slip.

**How it works (reading):** The slip-operated lock is made effective when the machine gear is released and made ineffective again at the end of the run, locking the drive shaft lever or release button.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An inserted slip can be withdrawn before the machine starts, and the existing lock is only effective at the wrong time.

**Solution:** The slip-operated lock is made effective when the machine gear is released and made ineffective again at the end of the run, locking the drive shaft lever or release button.
