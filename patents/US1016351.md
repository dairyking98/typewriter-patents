## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriers stationary for impression, e.g. with the types or dies not moving relative to the carriers with the types or dies moving relative to the carriers or mounted on flexible carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an organization and arrangement of the type bars in typewriting machines having a forward stroke of the bars and single shift of the platen, 2".

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In Underwood-type front-strike typebar machines the typebar bends below the guide slot, so outer bars wobble as the guide wears and the upper of the two types strikes weakly because of torsion. Removing the guide head instead loses alignment.

**Solution:** Move the typebar bend above the guide head and give the bars guide lugs that run in two angled guide slots, so every bar meets its slot at an acute angle.

**How it works:** Forty-five typebars each carry a two-type block and a guide lug deflected at an obtuse angle from the type block and an acute angle from the bar. About half the lugs engage one slot in a fixed guide head and the rest engage a second slot set 40 to 60 degrees from the first. Because the bend is above the guide, the bars converge to a point above it, so they are shorter, lighter and flatter in their segment and slide past each other more easily. A single-shift platen works with the two-type bars as usual.
