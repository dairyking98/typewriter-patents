In the known devices of this type are to engage and disengage the clutch between platen and line index wheel and to fix them Sensitive and tangled intermediate links in the coupled or uncoupled position provided, which are also very stressed and subject to heavy wear are. As far as engaging and disengaging the clutch by sliding the Platen axis takes place, this also requires a relatively large Effort, which complicates the operation of the device and the durability is affected. The invention aims to obviate these disadvantages and a device to create that is easy and cheap to manufacture, takes up little space, So it is particularly suitable for small typewriters, and is also characterized by easy, shock-free gait and great durability.
