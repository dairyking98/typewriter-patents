## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on a line-change gear clutch on typewriters, with which the connection between the platen non-rotatably connected to a continuous platen shaft and the line index wheel by means of longitudinal displacement of the platen axis and disengageable coupling members is effected.

**How it works (reading):** Two pincer-like coupling jaws on a pivot in the line ratchet carrier are opened and closed by longitudinal movement of the platen shaft, so the platen engages the ratchet without heavy effort.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Clutch between platen and line-index wheel needs sensitive intermediate links and axial shifting of the platen.

**Solution:** Two pincer-like coupling jaws on a pivot in the line ratchet carrier are opened and closed by longitudinal movement of the platen shaft, so the platen engages the ratchet without heavy effort.
