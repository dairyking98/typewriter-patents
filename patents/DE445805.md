## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator printing hammer) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**How it works (reading):** The hammer holding levers are hung independently and swing down only when the type carrier stop engages them, so only a small mass moves and zeros after the first printed digit are suppressed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In known calculator printing units, zero printing is switched off with trigger rods that all swing, so the whole trigger stem and its attached parts must move each time.

**Solution:** The hammer holding levers are hung independently and swing down only when the type carrier stop engages them, so only a small mass moves and zeros after the first printed digit are suppressed.

**Features:** [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)
