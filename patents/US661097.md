## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Writing music needs hand-drawing.

**Solution:** Typewriter for music with typebars carrying musical forms, paper moving on rolls on a vertically sliding frame and a staff ruling device.

**How it works:** Paper winds between two rolls over a platen plate on a horizontal carriage in a vertical frame. Typebars on a curved rod strike through a ribbon whose spools lift. Guide plate registers type forms, a mechanism rules a staff and a copy holder holds music.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** base; upright frame; sliding frame; carriage; paper roll; platen plate; type-bar; guide plate; ribbon spool; copy holder
