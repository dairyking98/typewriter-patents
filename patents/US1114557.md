## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** The general object of the invention is to improve the construction of the escapement mechanismof typewriting machines in point of reducing to a minimum thenumber of parts heretofore employed in the construction of this mechanism, And to this end the invention resides in certain Combinations and arrangements of parts hereinafter more fully described and particularly pointed out in the appended claim.

**How it works (reading):** The escapement uses a rack bar on the carriage with a stationary pawl and a spring-actuated movable pawl that cross its path; the movable pawl is rocked out and restored by its spring while the next stationary pawl takes the tooth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The escapement mechanism has too many parts.

**Solution:** The escapement uses a rack bar on the carriage with a stationary pawl and a spring-actuated movable pawl that cross its path; the movable pawl is rocked out and restored by its spring while the next stationary pawl takes the tooth.
