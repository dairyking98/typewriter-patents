## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Shields or masks

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon feeds into a continuous, sealed, lockable strip that runs in one direction and records everything typed, so the strip acts as a reliable original and can be split into parts for the books.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typed copies can be forged and staff can use the typewriter privately; invoice details must be transferred by hand to stock and customer records, which causes errors.

**Solution:** The ribbon feeds into a continuous, sealed, lockable strip that runs in one direction and records everything typed, so the strip acts as a reliable original and can be split into parts for the books.
