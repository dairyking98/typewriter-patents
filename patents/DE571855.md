> Description Translated from German Typewriter The subject of the invention is a typewriter. The invention can be seen in the fact that behind the ribbon one with this at the same time switched on, always moving in one direction and at the ends encapsulated strip is present, which receives the writing. Behind this Then strip the carbon sheet for writing onto the typewriter drum provided paper sheets located, but what can be a further part of the invention forms, this carbon sheet in a known manner by a second ribbon be replaced, which at the progression of the first ribbon and the font taking part. The capsules for the ends of the strip will be appropriately made lockable so that they are inaccessible to unauthorized persons and the Strips can only be changed by an authorized person. The new typewriter leads to various advantages. First of all, the labeled strip forms a reliable original, so that there is a risk that copies of an alleged transcript will be made for forgery be made anew, which was previously easily possible, is avoided.
