## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating typewriter subtraction) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a calculating typewriter, in which the subtraction is carried out by addition of complement.

**How it works (reading):** Each switching operation gets its own visible mark: the zero key and the nine and single-space keys carry special double-head types whose characters print only on the carbon copies or on the record, so the correctness of each operation can be checked at a glance.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On Elliott-Fisher complement-subtraction book machines the printed record does not show which switching operations were used, so errors are hard to find by skimming the record.

**Solution:** Each switching operation gets its own visible mark: the zero key and the nine and single-space keys carry special double-head types whose characters print only on the carbon copies or on the record, so the correctness of each operation can be checked at a glance.
