**How it works (reading):** A coin-started motor lifts the cover lock and holds it until a timer released by a later coin sets the motor reverse to lock the cover again.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Self-service typewriter cover opening and closing force varies with the user's weight, and the cover is not always closed.

**Solution:** A coin-started motor lifts the cover lock and holds it until a timer released by a later coin sets the motor reverse to lock the cover again.
