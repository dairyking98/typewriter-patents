## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (photoelectric sensing, registration cards) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character recognition characterised by the type of writing of printed characters having additional code marks or containing code marks

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a sensing device for machines in which control processes, mainly sorting processes, calculation and printing processes, through registration cards or recording strips monitored in accordance with the characters indicated on the same will.

**How it works (reading):** A single light cell reads each character by moving the card past a sensing point while a beam-guiding device, moved synchronously, scans the index points.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Photoelectric sensing of graphic characters needed four light cells per character, which was costly.

**Solution:** A single light cell reads each character by moving the card past a sensing point while a beam-guiding device, moved synchronously, scans the index points.

**How it works:** Single photocell with a sideways-moved beam guide, cam-driven, scanning the index points of a graphic character as the card passes the sensing point.
