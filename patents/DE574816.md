## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (combined typewriter and calculating machine)

**How it works (reading):** Each calculator key gets an electromagnet, and the magnets of a row are gated by an additional magnet that the typewriter carriage interrupter closes when a number key is struck.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing calculators cannot be driven by a typewriter without conversion, so typewriter-plus-calculator pairing is limited to special machines.

**Solution:** Each calculator key gets an electromagnet, and the magnets of a row are gated by an additional magnet that the typewriter carriage interrupter closes when a number key is struck.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** electromagnets; interrupter; additional electromagnet; typewriter keys
