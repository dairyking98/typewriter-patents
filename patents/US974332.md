## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cable or belt constructions for driving print, type or paper-carriages, e.g. attachment, tensioning means

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide improved means for applying tension to the carriage ofv a machinev to move it from 'one side to the other'and at the same time provide escapement mechanism of such acharacter that the pressure upon the escapement- Wheel 'which normally has the f greatest amount of Wear shall be much less than the tension ony the carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Carriage tension applied through reducing gears so that pressure on the escapement wheel is much less than the carriage tension.
