## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed with two or more sets of type or printing elements

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is mainly to simplify the construction of type-writing machines and reduce the cost of manufacture and also to enable the operator to print upper and lower case type, script and other styles of type, numerals and punctuation marks and other characters with one set of twenty-six keys without loss of time and without making any material change in the operating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Three removable type baskets of 26 characters each on a rotating carrier, one basket brought under a single set of 26 circularly arranged keys by turning a crank.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters were costly and needed many keys or shifts to print upper case, lower case, script and numerals.

**Solution:** Three removable type baskets of 26 characters each on a rotating carrier, one basket brought under a single set of 26 circularly arranged keys by turning a crank.

**How it works:** Three circular basket carriers on a common centre are rotated by a crank, bevel gears and a detented shaft so that one basket sits under the keys. Twenty-six keys in a circle on the hinged lid act on the characters of the chosen basket; each basket can be lifted out and swapped for another style. Two spacing keys act on the carriage ratchet. The ribbon on two spools is fed after every stroke by chain and sprockets tied to the letter-spacing ratchet, with clutches switching feed direction between spools.

**Features:** [Shift by turning the element](../features/shift-rotary.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md), [Interchangeable element](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** type basket; basket carrier; crank; bevel gear; detent pin; key; spacing key; ribbon spool; clutch; sprocket and chain; carriage

**Numbers:** 26 characters per basket; three baskets; 26 keys
