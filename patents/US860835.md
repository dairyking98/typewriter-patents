## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Fractional line spacing needs a precise connection between the line spacing wheel and platen; differential gears backlash, friction needs too much force, and the parts shift accidentally.

**Solution:** A connection and disconnection between the line spacing wheel and platen for fractional line spacing, held without backlash or accidental displacement (same family as US860834).

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
