## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My improvement relates particularly to escapement mechanism for the platen carriage of type-writers, one of the objects of the in-4 vention being to attach as little mechanism to the carriage as may be, and another object being to make a simple and elective connection between the key levers and the escapement mechanism.
