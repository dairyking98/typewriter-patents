## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** A bottom-margin indicator plate on the carriage, pivoted on the platen bar, carries a sector scale and is turned by a worm on the platen spindle so the operator can read when the set margin depth is reached.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators forget that the paper is nearing the bottom edge and so write over the bottom margin or run the last line too low.

**Solution:** A bottom-margin indicator plate on the carriage, pivoted on the platen bar, carries a sector scale and is turned by a worm on the platen spindle so the operator can read when the set margin depth is reached.
