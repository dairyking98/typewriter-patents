## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide a simple and effective means for feeding the ribbon and for enabling the spools to be shifted laterally in order that the impact of the type may occur in parallel lines throughout the length of the ribbon until its surface is exhausted.

**How it works (reading):** Constantly driven spool shafts with annular ratchet-tooth bands and plain bands let spool pawls engage or ride free, so the spools slide laterally to move to the next track.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed on Remington-style machines needs a simple way to wind the ribbon step by step and shift the spools sideways at reversal so type strikes parallel lines along the whole ribbon.

**Solution:** Constantly driven spool shafts with annular ratchet-tooth bands and plain bands let spool pawls engage or ride free, so the spools slide laterally to move to the next track.

**How it works:** A transverse shaft driven from the carriage-return drum shaft by bevel pinions turns two spool shafts constantly. Each shaft has several annular series of ratchet teeth separated by plain spaces. Each ribbon spool slides on its shaft and carries a spring-loaded pawl that engages the teeth or rides on the plain space. Brackets holding the front ends of the shafts can be pivoted and set with a slotted set-screw. Check-pawl mechanism at the shaft end controls the feed step.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** transverse shaft; bevel pinion; spool shaft; ribbon spool; spool pawl; bracket; winding drum

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 2 ribbon feed: simpler feed and lateral shift of spools
