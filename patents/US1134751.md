## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen rotation for fine line adjustment and quick backward turn is tied to the line-space ratchet, which drags and adds noise.

**Solution:** Platen with internal operating mechanism that turns the platen independently of the line-space wheel, which is released and used as a friction brake.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
