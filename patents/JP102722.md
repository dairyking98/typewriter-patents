## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A machine is wanted that makes varied, complicated prints (a chosen figure or character plus a date and serial number that accompany every print) automatically and reliably from a simple key choice.

**Solution:** Printing plates sit on the rim of a motor-turned wheel with one staggered cam per plate; a key-positioned slider selects one cam, which alone triggers the shared inking and pressing rollers as that plate comes round.

**How it works:** Plates are mounted round a wheel on a shaft, with a narrow cam per plate set in a star and staggered along the shaft, plus two long cams for fixed plates (date, number) that always act. Pressing a key swings a slanted plate by an amount that depends on the key, which moves a sprung slider carrying two follower rollers into the plane of the matching cam. Pressing the key also unlatches a control, closes a switch to start the motor, and engages a latch to the wheel; the key is held down by a latch until the end. As the wheel turns, the selected cam moves the follower and a bent lever swings the inking roller onto its plate, then a second follower swings the pressing roller to print on paper placed between, and the long cams do the same for the fixed plates. After one full turn a stop pin trips the control, which switches off the motor, stops the wheel and returns the key.
