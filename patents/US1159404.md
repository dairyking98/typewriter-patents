## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Label printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewriting machines, and more particularly to a paper supporting and feeding mechanism adapted for use in connection with a downwardly acting printing mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** On a downward-printing machine, the paper web is led around the platen and held by supporting and guiding means, and the machine is retained so it can move back for access.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing on a continuous web needs paper support and feed without shifting the machine for line spacing.

**Solution:** On a downward-printing machine, the paper web is led around the platen and held by supporting and guiding means, and the machine is retained so it can move back for access.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** downward printing; continuous web; retaining means
