## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon obscures the printing point except in the instant of printing.

**Solution:** Spring-held ribbon tension members hold the ribbon taut clear of the print point and are released through a lost-motion cam slot as the type action goes forward.

**How it works:** Tension members on a shaft are held forward by a spiral spring, keeping the ribbon taut and the print point visible. A pin on one member rides a cam slot in an arm on the universal rock shaft. After some free key travel the pin hits a shoulder and the members swing to slacken the ribbon so the type carrier carries it to the paper. On return, the arm travels half-way before returning the members. The lost motion also lets the ribbon be pressed forward for easy threading.
