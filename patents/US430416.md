## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants the platen liftable on its own to read the writing, even manifolding, immobile line-space lever and fewer scales.

**Solution:** A group of improvements to a typebar machine's platen frame, scales, typebar springs and key-lever mounting.

**How it works:** The platen sits in a rectangular frame with a rod in open U bearings on the carriage yoke so it can be lifted forward independently, held by a bent spring. Adjustable springs press the platen bearings against a rigid feed roll for even manifolding. A pivoted front scale is pushed onto the platen. Each typebar has an intermediate lever to throw it up and a spring to throw it down, and key levers are held at three points.
