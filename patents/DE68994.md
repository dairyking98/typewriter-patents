## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ordinary machines cannot print headings and text in bound books, and there is no way to set a book page square under the type.

**Solution:** A typewriter frame holds an open book or sheets, adjusts the book to a flat position, shifts it sideways and by line spacing, and uses a changeover device to choose large or small letters.
