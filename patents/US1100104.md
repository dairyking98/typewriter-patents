## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, especially those of the front strike variety, in which type bars are pivoted in an are below a platen and strike rearwardly against the front side of the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Broad hangers on a segment carry the pivoted type bars, and a linkage turns key downward motion into forward and backward type-bar strokes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Transmitting key motion into type-bar motion must be simple, compact and easy to assemble, and the key stroke must be cushioned.

**Solution:** Broad hangers on a segment carry the pivoted type bars, and a linkage turns key downward motion into forward and backward type-bar strokes.
