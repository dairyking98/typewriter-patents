**How it works (reading):** A tooth sector meshing with the paper feed pinion is carried on a removable frame under the table, driven by the pressure arm, with a handlebar for adjusting the stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Address printers without list feed need a separate attachment to move the list forward automatically, and the stroke must be adjustable.

**Solution:** A tooth sector meshing with the paper feed pinion is carried on a removable frame under the table, driven by the pressure arm, with a handlebar for adjusting the stroke.
