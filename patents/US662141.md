## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of my invention is to provide a construction whereby a card-holder may be readily attached to and detached from the platen of a type-writing machine, so that the one platen may be used for ordinary work when writing is to be done upon paper and also for special work when writing is to be done upon cards, thus obviating the necessity, general heretofore, of providing a separate platen for each class of work.
