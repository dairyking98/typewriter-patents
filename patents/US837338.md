## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Positive-feed mechanisms automatically operated in response to carriage return

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide means whereby the platen may be rotated by devices operatively connected to key-levers extending to the keyboard.

**How it works (reading):** Key-lever linkage that rotates the platen for line spacing regardless of carriage position, with a lever that frees the carriage-return device and returns to normal.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says the platen cannot be rotated from the keyboard independently of the carriage position, and a line-spacing lever must be released at the right moment.

**Solution:** Key-lever linkage that rotates the platen for line spacing regardless of carriage position, with a lever that frees the carriage-return device and returns to normal.
