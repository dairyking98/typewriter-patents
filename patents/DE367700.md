## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and manually actuated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the present invention is to use both devices to accomplish with one hand movement.

**How it works (reading):** A common actuator sets the carriage locking rod and the key lock together, with a stop lever and two-armed stop arm linking them, so a single hand movement locks both; the key lock can also work independently.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Holding the carriage in its middle position and locking the keys each needed a separate handle, so both could not be set with one hand movement.

**Solution:** A common actuator sets the carriage locking rod and the key lock together, with a stop lever and two-armed stop arm linking them, so a single hand movement locks both; the key lock can also work independently.

**Features:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
