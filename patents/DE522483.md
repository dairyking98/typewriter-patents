## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** The tab shaft is made axially displaceable as a push rod, so one hand lever pushes it back to engage the control stops and twists it to raise the tab rail, with a torsion spring returning the parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator setting needs separate actuators to raise the tab stop bar and to position the adjustment stops.

**Solution:** The tab shaft is made axially displaceable as a push rod, so one hand lever pushes it back to engage the control stops and twists it to raise the tab rail, with a torsion spring returning the parts.
