## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Single-step letter spacing is noisy and rough.

**Solution:** A double rocking pawl on an escapement wheel feeds the carriage half a letter space on key depression and the other half on release.

**How it works:** The carriage carries a pivoted rack bar meshing with a gear on a vertical shaft; the shaft has an escapement wheel engaged alternately by two pallets of a double rocking pawl. A spacer bar linked to the pawl is struck by a bail when any key bar is depressed, rocking the pawl through one pallet, and returns on release, giving the second half step. A lever tilts the rack bar out of the gear to free the carriage.
