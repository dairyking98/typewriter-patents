## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (generic rotary-to-reciprocating movement) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for assembling matrices and space bands

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to mechanical movements and more particularly to that class thereof wherein a constant rotary motion may be converted into an intermittent reciprocating motion.

**How it works (reading):** A normally disengaged driven gear, held out of mesh by a separate swinging plate, is engaged with a constantly driven pinion to give an intermittent reciprocating motion, as applied to linotype keyboard matrix release.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier cam-based escapement drives rotate a cam fully on each release, and the inventor wants a different way to convert constant rotation to intermittent reciprocating motion.

**Solution:** A normally disengaged driven gear, held out of mesh by a separate swinging plate, is engaged with a constantly driven pinion to give an intermittent reciprocating motion, as applied to linotype keyboard matrix release.
