## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage, pulled by a spring, can move too fast.

**Solution:** A rotary air-vane fly geared to the carriage rack acts as a speed governor, optionally turning in liquid.

**How it works:** A cog rack on the carriage drives a pinion on a vertical shaft which, through a cog-wheel and pinion, turns the fly shaft with wings of curved form. A spring pawl and ratchet teeth drive it only while writing; on the return the ratchet slips. A liquid-filled cup with wings gives greater resistance; speed is regulated by the quantity of liquid.
