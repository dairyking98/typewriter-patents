## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by applications of scales or indicators

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a tabulator, simple, strong, durable and inexpensive in construction, which will be adapted to be fitted to the scale bar of any typewriting machine and to be easily and quickly secured in'position thereon.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulator plate, scored like the scale bar and reversible, that clips to the scale bar with a spring and gives a fixed step.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulation on a scale bar needs faster positioning than the typewriter offers.

**Solution:** A tabulator plate, scored like the scale bar and reversible, that clips to the scale bar with a spring and gives a fixed step.
