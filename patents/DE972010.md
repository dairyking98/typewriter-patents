## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops; Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a car jumping device on office machines, z_.

**How it works (reading):** A transmission rail pivots on the carriage, so the carriage rider stop moves to the set riders when the car-jump button is pressed; the rider pole can also move out of the margin stop area without deleting the margin stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting the whole tab rider rod for every tabulation takes effort and its stops set up vibration in the machine.

**Solution:** A transmission rail pivots on the carriage, so the carriage rider stop moves to the set riders when the car-jump button is pressed; the rider pole can also move out of the margin stop area without deleting the margin stops.
