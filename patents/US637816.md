## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers on bank books or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my present invention is to construct a book type-writer which shall be reversible, so that the same may write from left to right, or vice versa.

**How it works (reading):** A traveling type-frame carrying an oscillating type disk or cylinder with a type-bar in each socket, advanced by a feed screw with each keystroke and reversible.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriters need to write left-to-right or right-to-left, with accurate alignment and few parts.

**Solution:** A traveling type-frame carrying an oscillating type disk or cylinder with a type-bar in each socket, advanced by a feed screw with each keystroke and reversible.

**How it works:** An oscillating keyboard has a traveling type-frame attached. The type-bars lie in sockets around the periphery of a rotary or oscillating type disk, covered by a plate, with springs returning each bar into its socket. Depressing a key brings its letter to the print position and forces it against the ink ribbon. A shifter-key and shifting plate change case. The type-frame is coupled to a feed screw turned intermittently with each key, with a reversing mechanism, and platforms adjusted by sprocket-chain and screws match the book thickness.
