## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A quick key press on the back-setting key gave the carriage enough momentum to move back more than one space.

**Solution:** A back-setting mechanism moves the carriage back exactly one forward step per key actuation, regardless of how carefully or quickly the key is pressed.

**Features:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
