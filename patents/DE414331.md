## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Travel typewriters must be small and light, yet usable and safe in operation.

**Solution:** The guide comb for the key levers also serves as the bearing for the intermediate lever, with its upper edge rolled into a sleeve, and the type-lever rest cushion sits just above that bearing, saving space.

**How it works:** The intermediate lever's ends slide in slots in the comb, and the sleeve on the comb carries the axis of the intermediate lever.
