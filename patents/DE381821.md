## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A ribbon ratchet advanced by the key stroke either skips (if one tooth) or loses up to a tooth on the return (if almost two).

**Solution:** Use two or more holding pawls offset by a fraction of the tooth pitch so backlash is limited to half or less of a tooth.

**How it works:** A universal rail moved by the key levers turns a shaft whose arm carries the drive pawl on the ratchet. Two holding pawls bear on the ratchet, bent so one rests at the tooth bottom while the other is half a pitch offset. The ratchet can then turn back at most half a pitch; three pawls would give a third. An alternative uses two ratchets twisted half a pitch against each other and joined.

**Features:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** universal rail; ratchet; drive pawl; holding pawl; flat spring; wire spring

**Numbers:** two pawls offset half pitch; three pawls offset one third pitch
