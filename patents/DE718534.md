## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register with photograph device)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register or reservation machine with a photograph means .

**How it works (reading):** The amount is printed by a swinging type set, the set is swung out, and the printed amount and note are photographed on film without moving the document.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Photographing a handwritten note with a printed amount normally needs the receipt moved, which costs mechanism and space.

**Solution:** The amount is printed by a swinging type set, the set is swung out, and the printed amount and note are photographed on film without moving the document.
