## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** At high typing speed a key is pressed before the previous type has left the platen, so the carriage cannot step and letters print on top of each other.

**Solution:** A friction clutch between the escapement puller-rod and the escapement crank that gives the escapement a rapid independent action immediately after the type strikes.

**How it works:** In this class of machine the key pulls a spring-actuated puller-rod down while the typebar rises, and the carriage steps when the rod rises again. The attachment inserts a frictionally engaged clutching device, a friction wheel on a pivoted bar working against an opposed surface, between the puller-rod end and the escapement-shaft crank. This lets the escapement trip and the carriage move one space as soon as the type has struck, before the next type can reach the platen. It can be fitted either in the lower part of the puller-rod or on top of the frame.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)

**Parts:** puller-rod; escapement; carriage rack; friction wheel; pivoted bar; bell-crank lever; finger key; typebar; carriage spring

**Improves on:**

- direct puller-rod escapement: carriage release no longer depends on how fast the finger leaves the key
