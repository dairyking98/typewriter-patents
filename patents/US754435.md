## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in the construction, combination, and arrangementof parts hereinafterdescribed and claimed.

**How it works (reading):** A pneumatically actuated paper feed with take-off reels, a line-number and sheet-length indicator with alarm, and a slide scale for measuring top margins.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators need to track line count and sheet length and to set margins by hand, which is slow and error prone.

**Solution:** A pneumatically actuated paper feed with take-off reels, a line-number and sheet-length indicator with alarm, and a slide scale for measuring top margins.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
