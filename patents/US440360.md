## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar hangers screwed to a single cast-iron ring shift with expansion and contraction of the ring, so the typebars no longer converge exactly on the print point and the type is misaligned.

**Solution:** Make the hanger ring in two layers, a cast ring with an overlying thin stamped steel ring whose punched apertures seat the hangers' projections, with screws passing loosely through the upper ring into the lower.

**How it works:** The upper hanger-supporting ring is a main cast-iron ring with a flat steel plate ring laid on top. The steel ring has stamped mortises or recesses with sharp edges; each U-shaped sheet-metal hanger has downward projections that rest on the edges of these apertures, not quite touching the cast ring. A screw through an oversized hole in the hanger passes freely through the steel ring and threads into the cast ring, clamping everything. The hangers are thus fixed relative to each other and to the support, while the lips of the hanger guide the typebar centrally.
