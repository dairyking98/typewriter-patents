## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon creases or folds as it crosses the machine, and spools are hard to detach and wind.

**Solution:** The ribbon is guided across from one spool through a vibrator to the other so it lies flat over the printing point, with easily detached spools and improved winding.

**How it works:** A ribbon vibrator carries the ribbon over the printing point, with case and thimble parts guiding the spools and a clutch member rotating the main ribbon shaft.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; spools; ribbon-spool lock; clutch member; thimble; top plate
