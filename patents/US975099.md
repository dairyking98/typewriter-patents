## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor seeks a simple and efficient construction of a front-strike typewriter, with the platen frame carried on a truck and the line-lock and margin stop mechanism reworked.

**Solution:** A front-strike machine whose platen frame rides on grooved rails and a carriage truck, with a line lock and a final margin stop acting on a stop rod carried by the carriage.
