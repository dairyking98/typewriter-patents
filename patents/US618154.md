## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage must be arrested and the printing locked at the end of a line, at a chosen point.

**Solution:** A pivoted notched bar carries an adjustable stop that, when hit by the carriage, uses the drive spring's pull to shift the bar endways and lock the escapement.

**How it works:** A rectangular bar at the rear pivots on screw ends in two standards and has teeth matching the carriage rack spaces, plus a graduated scale. A box slide with a spring dog can be set at any notch and carries a pointer. An arm on the carriage meets the slide's abutment, and the drum spring's pull moves the bar endways, locking the escapement and the keys. A push-rod with key button returns the bar. The device also trips the bell.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
