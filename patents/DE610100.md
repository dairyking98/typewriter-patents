## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Power typewriters with only fixed left and right margin stops cannot quickly switch off the carriage lift at an arbitrary point, and a late switch-off throws the carriage lift to its end with full force.

**Solution:** Add an extra adjustable margin stop between the usual two that can be switched in or out and removed by lifting off perpendicular to the carriage.

**How it works:** The added margin stop sits on the counter support rail of the carriage and has two holding devices: one fixes it, the other, once released, holds it on its support and lets it be taken off. Releasing the second device releases the first. A rack on the carriage drives gears to a coupling on the type-lever camshaft, with a push button shifting an axle to engage the continuously running motor. The extra stop trips the carriage lift via a flap, angle lever and pull rod.
