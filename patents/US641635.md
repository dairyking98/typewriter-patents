## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-Writer keys or striker-heads for type-writers and similar machines.

**How it works (reading):** A light striker-head built from a slotted plastic disk and a concave cover disk pressed or cemented together, with the staple loop passed through the slots, so the head is elastic, lightweight and easy to wash or replace.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-writer key heads are heavy, cannot be washed, and are hard to recover or replace.

**Solution:** A light striker-head built from a slotted plastic disk and a concave cover disk pressed or cemented together, with the staple loop passed through the slots, so the head is elastic, lightweight and easy to wash or replace.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** metallic loop or staple; celluloid disk with slots; concave cover disk; elastic cover; letter label
