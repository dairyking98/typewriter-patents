## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Fixed guide heads give typebars only 1-2 mm of straight guidance and sit close to the platen, forcing the ribbon behind the head, which hides the writing and prevents two-colour ribbons.

**Solution:** Mount the guide head on a swinging pivot so it rests away from the roller and moves up to it just as the type strikes, letting the ribbon stay in front.

**How it works:** The guide head swings on a rod in eyes on the fixed guide plate, driven by a key-moved lever through a bolt in a slide. In rest the head is 5 to 6 mm from the roller with the ribbon below. The typebar first lifts the ribbon, then type and head move up together to the stop. On return the head swings back first, and then the ribbon drops, so writing is visible as on a front-strike machine.
