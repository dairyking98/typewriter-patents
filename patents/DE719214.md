## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (spring force conversion gear, machine tools) · **Subject (patent classes):** Gearings or mechanisms with other special functional features

**How it works (reading):** A cam curve on a cylinder drum carries the spring pull. The effective lever arm is changed by pivoting the force's fixed point or shifting the drum axis, so the constant force is adjusted without moving the spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A gear is needed that converts a spring force, which rises as the spring extends, into a constant output force, and the output force could only be changed by moving the stationary spring end.

**Solution:** A cam curve on a cylinder drum carries the spring pull. The effective lever arm is changed by pivoting the force's fixed point or shifting the drum axis, so the constant force is adjusted without moving the spring.
