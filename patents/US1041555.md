## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage step-by-step escapement must be strong, durable and fast.

**Solution:** A rocker escapement with a toothed rack and a key lever and type bar, moving the carriage step by step with a spring-tensioned rocker.
