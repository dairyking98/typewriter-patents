## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to effect the line-spacing by manual and automatic means.

**How it works (reading):** A paper carriage with spring-driven gear wheels that returns automatically at the end of the line, can be returned from the keyboard at any point, has a cushioned stop, runs on tracks or runways, and has manual and automatic line-spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says paper-carriage control was poor: the carriage needed to return automatically at line end, return at any point by key, cushion its stop, run without friction, and line-space by hand or automatically.

**Solution:** A paper carriage with spring-driven gear wheels that returns automatically at the end of the line, can be returned from the keyboard at any point, has a cushioned stop, runs on tracks or runways, and has manual and automatic line-spacing.
