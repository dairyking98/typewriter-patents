## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to card-holders for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A platen with a card-receiving groove or slit, so a card is slipped into the platen and held for printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards need a quick, simple way to be held on the platen without the usual clamps.

**Solution:** A platen with a card-receiving groove or slit, so a card is slipped into the platen and held for printing.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** platen with card slit; platen frame
