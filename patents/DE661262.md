## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (teleprinter sending device with storage) · **Subject (patent classes):** Contact operating means; Apparatus or circuits at the transmitting end with keyboard co-operating with code-bars combined with perforating apparatus

**How it works (reading):** A transmitter stores every typed character in one buffer so the shift character is sent automatically, decoupling the key rate from the transmission.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Teleprinters with office-keyboard layout needed multi-stage storage to hold a character while the type-group shift is sent.

**Solution:** A transmitter stores every typed character in one buffer so the shift character is sent automatically, decoupling the key rate from the transmission.
