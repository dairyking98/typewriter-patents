## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide a new and improved copy-holder specially designed for use on type-writing machines, which is simple and durable in construction, permits of readily inserting and moving the copy, andiseasily applied and conveniently arranged to facilitate the reading of the lines.

**How it works (reading):** A casing hooks onto the carriage front rail; two rollers carry the copy between them, and a lever and ratchet turn one roller either way; the slot in the front gives an inclined reading view.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copy must be easy to insert, move and read, and the holder should be simple and durable.

**Solution:** A casing hooks onto the carriage front rail; two rollers carry the copy between them, and a lever and ratchet turn one roller either way; the slot in the front gives an inclined reading view.

**How it works:** Casing with two copy rollers driven by a ratchet lever, set on an inclined front slot.
