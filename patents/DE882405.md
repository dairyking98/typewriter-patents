## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing in Braille or with keyboards specially adapted for use by blind or disabled persons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters for double dot writing, in which the writing is embossed or punched in a ribbon-shaped writing carrier.

**How it works (reading):** A small hand-held machine is operated with one hand: finger keys emboss or punch a row of dots into a ribbon tape, which feeds forward automatically after each impression.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing dot-writing machines for the blind are too large to carry, and operating them needs both hands.

**Solution:** A small hand-held machine is operated with one hand: finger keys emboss or punch a row of dots into a ribbon tape, which feeds forward automatically after each impression.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
