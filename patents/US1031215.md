**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to new and useful improvements in typewriter attachments.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Counting words per line by eye is unreliable when writing telegraph messages.

**Solution:** An attachment on the space bar mechanism and spring drum steps once per space-bar press and sounds an audible bell after a preset word count.
