## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Electrically operated typewriters need key mechanisms that select the type, then power the impression, in a controlled sequence.

**Solution:** A key that on successive depression stages first energizes a selecting magnet, then cuts it out, then energizes a power magnet that drives the positioned typebar to print.

**How it works:** Each key lever rocks a typebar through links and also operates an armature lever. Early key travel closes a circuit through a selecting magnet, whose armature-lever hooks under a universal power-bar. Further travel breaks that circuit and a latch holds the positioned member, then still further travel closes the power magnet circuit, which pulls the universal bar and prints. Release returns the parts without energizing magnets. The same key may also drive the shifting, ribbon feeding or spacing.
