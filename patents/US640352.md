## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriting machines in which a number of typebearing arms are thrown successively against the paper or other substance to be written on by the depression and release of a series of keys, and while the type and key are going back to place the paper or substance is moved a type-space distance.

**How it works (reading):** Automatic typebar machine with automatic carriage return, platen turn, front-visible writing at 45 degrees, marginal stops, paragraph stop and inking roller.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators lose time on carriage return, line spacing, blind writing and margins.

**Solution:** Automatic typebar machine with automatic carriage return, platen turn, front-visible writing at 45 degrees, marginal stops, paragraph stop and inking roller.

**How it works:** Typebars strike at 45 degrees from the platen front. The carriage runs left a space per stroke under tension on balls in a channel, and snaps back at line end. A pawl turns the platen automatically, a paragraph device skips the start-of-line setting, and an inking roller inks the types.
