## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (shorthand machine with voice sound keys) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**How it works (reading):** Each key carries an ordinary letter type; types are arranged in a circle to one pressure point and the shorthand paper strip runs continuously rather than stepping.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing shorthand typewriters need several keys pressed together per sound and read letter by letter, which slows writing and playback.

**Solution:** Each key carries an ordinary letter type; types are arranged in a circle to one pressure point and the shorthand paper strip runs continuously rather than stepping.

**Features:** [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
