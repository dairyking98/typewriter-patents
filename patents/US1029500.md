## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typewriter carriage must be returned to its start position at the operator's will, which the earlier arrangement did not do pneumatically.

**Solution:** A finger key admits air to a cylinder with telescopic pistons, which push the carriage back to its initial position; releasing the key vents the air.

**How it works:** Pressing a finger key moves a sliding plug that opens a valve and admits air; the pistons extend to push a lever on the carriage, and a spring returns the valve and plug on release.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** telescopic pistons; cylinder; valve; finger key; bell crank lever
