## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage must be released from the duplex letter-space rack and pawl for free movement, using a simple release key.

**Solution:** A rod running the length of the rack, carried on the carriage, presses the duplex pawl down and sideways out of the rack path wherever the carriage is.

**How it works:** A Yost-type carriage carries a duplex rack with two rows of teeth facing each other, and a two-part pawl vibrated by the finger-keys alternately enters the rack notches for step-by-step spacing. A release bar the length of the rack, formed with a hinged wire rod and finger key, is kept raised by a coil spring and a stop. Pressing the key lowers the bar onto the pawl, tipping it aside out of the rack path, and the bar slides along the pawl as the carriage is moved by hand.
