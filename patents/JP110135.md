## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A copying typewriter (used for telegraph relaying and receiving) must be switchable between single and duplicate writing simply and surely.

**Solution:** A presser bar swung against or away from a paper guide plate clamps the copy paper so only the original feeds, or releases it so both feed together.

**How it works:** The platen and the paper-roll holder are joined by a connecting plate and move together. A sloped paper guide plate with a convex ribbed curve carries the copy paper to the platen. A pair of support plates, joined by rods one of which is a rubber-wrapped presser bar, pivot so that swinging up presses the bar on the guide plate, clamping the copy paper, and swinging down releases it; a coil spring holds either position. Copying-ribbon spools and fold-back guides are on the support plates and bring the ribbon near the platen when down. For duplicate writing the copy paper and original feed together; for single the copy paper is clamped and only the original advances.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Device for both single and duplicate writing in a copying typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a single/duplicate device in a copying typewriter that has a copying-ribbon support and a paper-roll support. A presser bar can be brought against and away from a paper guide plate: it clamps the copy paper led over that plate, so that only the original is fed to the platen and a single copy is written; or it releases the copy paper, which is then fed to the platen together with the original and a duplicate is written.
