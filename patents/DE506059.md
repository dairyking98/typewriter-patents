## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Notched ribbon-lift adjustment is imprecise and often needs filing corrections.

**Solution:** Segment-shaped spring steel plates with bores on the pivot axis clamp a ball on the setting lever in the blue or red position.
