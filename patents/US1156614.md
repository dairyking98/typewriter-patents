## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type striking the platen causes noise, mainly from hard platen surfaces.

**Solution:** A platen is covered with soft rubber in which small metal pins are embedded flush with the surface, so a striking type hits several pins and the hard, resonant surface is avoided.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** soft rubber layer; embedded metal pins
