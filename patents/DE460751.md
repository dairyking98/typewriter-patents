## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Individually positioned letters in an embossing machine were hard to align for strong embossed strips.

**Solution:** A movable stamp box carries multiple letters per die part, raised or lowered by a keyed lever, with projections so only one letter contacts the strip, which runs in a channel.
