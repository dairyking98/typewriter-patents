## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that the pressure rollers after each Direction yielding, as it were floating, on the paper guide rod by means of a strangely coiled coil spring are stored, the turns of which are two different have large diameters, of which the smaller diameter turns on the Paper guide rod and the coils with larger diameter on the inner wall the bearing bush of the paper pressure rollers are in contact.

**How it works (reading):** Each roller floats on the guide rod by a stepped coil spring, and flat springs keep the rollers from being dragged along the rod.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper pressure rollers on a rigid guide rod cannot absorb bumps, and the known bearings are unsatisfactory for multi-part forms.

**Solution:** Each roller floats on the guide rod by a stepped coil spring, and flat springs keep the rollers from being dragged along the rod.
