## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjustable margin stops limit carriage travel and cannot be cleared when work needs to pass beyond the normal limit.

**Solution:** Margin-stop bar and rack bar are lifted together by a handle so the stops and escapement dogs step out of engagement, while separate release levers clear each stop individually.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
