## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** To print a comparatively large character set with simple apparatus while letting the user choose characters conveniently, with the hand never leaving the character chart.

**Solution:** A multi-row type drum is turned and the paper holder swung along the drum axis together by one handle with a pointer that moves over the character chart, then a hammer behind the paper presses it onto the chosen type.

**How it works:** A drum with types in several rows turns on a shaft through a gear and an internally toothed sector swung by a lever fixed to a cross bar over the chart. The same handle slides on that cross bar and, through a horizontal bar, sliding rod and guide bar, swings the rocking levers that carry the paper holder along the drum face. The pointer on the handle lever is dropped into grooves in the chart to fix its position over a character. The printing key turns bevel gears through a ratchet clutch, driving a crank with a cam slot that moves a rubber-tipped hammer behind the paper forward and then presses it at constant pressure onto the type. The same crank action releases a clawed crank and pawl on an escapement wheel; the spiral-spring-pulled rack lets the paper holder advance one character space. A spacing key moves an arc bar to feed a space alone, and the rack handle swings the rack out of mesh to return the paper to the line start.
