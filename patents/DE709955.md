## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (message printing, telegraph banking) · **Subject (patent classes):** Methods or arrangements for sensing record carriers, e.g. for reading patterns by mechanical means, e.g. by pins operating electric contacts

**How it works (reading):** An endless belt moving with the record carrier senses the marks, and a synchronising pulse keeps the registration movement in phase.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Telegraphic transmission of character data over a column-by-column sensing setup needed exact timing between the sensing and registration movements.

**Solution:** An endless belt moving with the record carrier senses the marks, and a synchronising pulse keeps the registration movement in phase.

**How it works:** An endless sensing belt and a synchronising pulse from the tape drive keep sensing and registration in step so each marking is registered correctly.
