## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Finding the centre of a line requires calculating between the margin stops.

**Solution:** A pointer joined to the two margin stops through racks and a loose pinion always sits midway between them.

**How it works:** Each margin stop carries a rack, and both racks mesh with a loose pinion on the pointer. Moving either stop moves the pointer half as far, so it indicates the midpoint wherever the stops are set. Each stop has a bell-crank, spring-loaded serrated pin that engages a rack in the margin-stop bar groove to lock its position.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** margin stop; margin-stop bar; rack; pinion; pointer; bell crank; spring; serrated pin
