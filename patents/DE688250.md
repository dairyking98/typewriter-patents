## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing device)

**How it works (reading):** A sliding frame moves the paper strips so special entries print with a special block in the normal heading area.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Special entries on a cash register can be made without a clear visible mark on the receipt.

**Solution:** A sliding frame moves the paper strips so special entries print with a special block in the normal heading area.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)
