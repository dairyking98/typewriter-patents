## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Forgery-proof perforation of documents with punched characters was slow, needing individual punches moved after each character.

**Solution:** Typebars made as punch tools, with counter levers striking from below through a slotted plate, punch characters like typing.

**How it works:** Typebars carry sharpened, hollowed punch types, with small webs left to hold inner parts of letters like O and A. A counter lever per typebar, moving oppositely and carrying a hollow die, meets the type from below through a longitudinal slot in a support plate that replaces the platen. The paper is cut between the two; the hollow dies take the paper scraps. Transport rollers move the sheet.
