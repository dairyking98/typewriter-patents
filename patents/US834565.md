## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Base plate and supports for the Oliver-type typebar machine.

**Solution:** A base plate carries the typebar standards and main parts.

**How it works:** Only the opening: the improvements concern the base plate of an Oliver machine, with upright standards supporting two groups of typebars and key-levers linked by upright links.

**Features:**
