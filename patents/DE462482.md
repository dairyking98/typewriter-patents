## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (mercury relay) · **Subject (patent classes):** Switches having at least one liquid contact with level of surface of contact liquid displaced by non-electrical contact-making plunger

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a relay which is used in particular to limit the current with moving electromagnetically acting on mercury as contact liquid Displacer whose movements are intended to delay the action of the relay in the vessel be stopped by the fact that for the change in the immersion depth corresponding Displacement of the mercury only a narrow passage is provided.

**How it works (reading):** The displacer is guided tightly, and a calibrated notch in its flange sets the mercury flow and delay.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Mercury relay delay depends on an uncontrolled annular gap around the displacer.

**Solution:** The displacer is guided tightly, and a calibrated notch in its flange sets the mercury flow and delay.
