## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to type-v Writing machines inWhich the platen ro-ll is adjustable, independently of the 'linespacing Wheel, by means of a hand Wheel or other device suitably connected to the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A hand wheel turns the platen through gearing for slow adjustment while the line-space wheel stays stationary, and the line-space wheel is locked back to the platen automatically after adjustment without disturbing it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjusting the platen through the line-space wheel introduced play between parts that impaired print alignment.

**Solution:** A hand wheel turns the platen through gearing for slow adjustment while the line-space wheel stays stationary, and the line-space wheel is locked back to the platen automatically after adjustment without disturbing it.
