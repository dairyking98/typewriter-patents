## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-type machines need many drives: type rods out of magazines, a line-straightening wheel, inking and platen movement, all coordinated.

**Solution:** A common main ratchet drives the magazine linkage, the line-straightening roller, the inking roller and paper-roller pressing, through belts and bevel gears.
