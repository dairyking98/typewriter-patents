## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Keyboard overlays

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary keyboard above the main keys, whose lightly pressed keys are caught by constantly driven gears or freewheels and thrown down onto the real keys.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter keys must be pressed fully down, costing time and effort.

**Solution:** An auxiliary keyboard above the main keys, whose lightly pressed keys are caught by constantly driven gears or freewheels and thrown down onto the real keys.

**How it works:** Light auxiliary keys, spring-held and with toothed or roughened edges, move slightly until a constantly driven gear or ball freewheel (part-toothed so it releases at the end) grabs them and slams them onto the main key. The power comes from a spring or electric motor; the key snaps back when released.
