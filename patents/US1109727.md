Specification of Letters Patent. 4Patented Sept. 8, 1914. Application ined August 2o, 1913. serial No. 785,656. o all whom it may concern Be it known that I, GROVER C. WILSON, a citizen of the United States, and a resident of Lexington, in the county of Fayette and State of Kentucky, have invented a new and Improved Copy-Holder, of which the following is a full, clear, and exact description. My invention relates to devices for holding a copy on typewriters or other like machmes, and is more particularly intended for use on the Stenotype. It is a design of my invention to provide a copy holder whereby the copy will be held in the most natural position in front of the key board of a machine. It is a further design of the invention to provide a device of the indicated character which may be readily collapsed into a small compass when not required for use and which may be quickly set up in position for use.
