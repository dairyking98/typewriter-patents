## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copy needed holding in a natural position in front of a stenotype keyboard, and collapsing when not used.

**Solution:** A copy holder that folds to a small size mounts on the pins that hold the ribbon spools of a stenotype.

**How it works:** A folding frame holds the copy and attaches to pins on the machine so it can be collapsed.
