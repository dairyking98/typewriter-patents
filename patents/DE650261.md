## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier on the paper apron concentric with the roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide device on typewriters, in which besides the usual lower and upper paper guide rollers on the side of these guide rollers the collecting sheet regardless of the stacking sheets at least on half the circumference of the platen pressing.

**How it works (reading):** The lower and upper pressure members pivot on a common axis under one spring, so they press the collecting sheet at its edges against the platen automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Additional pressure members for the collecting sheet must be pressed against the platen by hand, so they are often forgotten and the sheet bulges.

**Solution:** The lower and upper pressure members pivot on a common axis under one spring, so they press the collecting sheet at its edges against the platen automatically.
