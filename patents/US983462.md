## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The work sheet must be registered against the platen, and one side guide cannot be moved independently of the other.

**Solution:** Two gages on the paper table cooperate with opposite sides of the sheet, each adjustable on its own to positions between the platen ends and movable out of operation.
