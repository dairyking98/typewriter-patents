## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**How it works (reading):** A single electromagnet, or a spring or motor, drives the type stop and all paper-carriage switching movements, with word space set by a blank contact in the contact plate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The earlier electrical type-stop device needed several electromagnets, which could induce mutual magnetism and unreliable operation.

**Solution:** A single electromagnet, or a spring or motor, drives the type stop and all paper-carriage switching movements, with word space set by a blank contact in the contact plate.
