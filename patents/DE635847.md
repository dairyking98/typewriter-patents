## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an additional device that can be easily attached to typewriters or similar machines for automatic operation Return of the paper carriage by means of a spring mechanism housed in a housing, which by pressing a button with the movement of the triggered spring mechanism a tension member is coupled to the drum transferring the paper carriage.

**How it works (reading):** A flat cylindrical housing on the machine side holds a mainspring drum with screw friction bands as clutch and brake, and a button releases the spring so a tension cord pulls the carriage back.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manual carriage return is tiring, and earlier add-on automatic return devices were too complicated, bulky and hard to attach.

**Solution:** A flat cylindrical housing on the machine side holds a mainspring drum with screw friction bands as clutch and brake, and a button releases the spring so a tension cord pulls the carriage back.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** flat cylindrical housing; spring mechanism; screw friction bands; fastening strip; tension member

**Numbers:** gap about 1.5 to 2 times the band thickness
