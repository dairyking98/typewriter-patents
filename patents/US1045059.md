## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar bases that can be swapped to print another language or font are awkward to release and refit.

**Solution:** Two retaining members on the frame, one locked first by a distance strut, let the removable typebar base be lifted out.

**How it works:** The typebar base sits in notches on a rocking bar and a fixed detent bar on each side. The distance strut normally holds the two bars apart. Raising the strut locks the detent bar by a hook on a pin. Depressing and locking the shift key then rocks the rocking bar toward the detent bar, closing the notch gap so the base can be lifted out. A new base is placed with its pins in the notches and the shift is released.

**Features:** [ELEM:interchangeable](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** typebar base; rocking bar; detent bar; distance strut; shift key; shift lock; spring
