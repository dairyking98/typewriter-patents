## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The spring between pull rod and type lever stretched during each strike, so it weakened and the impression faded.

**Solution:** The spring is attached so that its two ends move in the same direction during the strike, which reduces stretch and extends spring life.
