## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such typewriter paper carriage switches.

**How it works (reading):** Each key lever carries an auxiliary lever with a claw; releasing the key rotates a drum with a few identical longitudinal grooves, and graduated claw arms give a uniform step, so the carriage advance is by letter width and delayed until after the stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage moved during the type stroke, making printing unclear, and the step size varied with where the pawl met the ratchet tooth.

**Solution:** Each key lever carries an auxiliary lever with a claw; releasing the key rotates a drum with a few identical longitudinal grooves, and graduated claw arms give a uniform step, so the carriage advance is by letter width and delayed until after the stroke.
