**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to cipher typewriter systems of the type in which several encryption organs are arranged one behind the other, in contrast to those systems with encryption organs arranged next to each other that none are subject to mutual 'influence and therefore each independently only their own Master the encryption area.

**How it works (reading):** Cipher typewriter where encryption organs (commutators) are arranged one behind another and chosen from keyed combinations by rails, so each keystroke steps a changing selection of organs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier cascaded cipher commutator systems give little choice of encipherment and need heavy wiring.

**Solution:** Cipher typewriter where encryption organs (commutators) are arranged one behind another and chosen from keyed combinations by rails, so each keystroke steps a changing selection of organs.
