## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons on machines with both lengthwise and transverse feed must be reversed by hand at the end of each run.

**Solution:** Levers tripped by projections on the ribbon spools throw the existing ribbon shifting bar automatically.

**How it works:** Fitted to a No. 3 Caligraph. Transverse levers pivoted to the front rail carry swinging tongues that the spool projections move as the ribbon runs out. The levers push vertical levers that rock and move the ribbon shifting bar, sliding pinions into and out of the spool shaft gears. The carriage abutment also acts on the lever when it is raised.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; vertical lever; transverse lever; swinging tongue; shifting bar; pinion; carriage abutment

**Improves on:**

- No. 3 Caligraph: replaces hand shifting of the ribbon bar with automatic trip
