## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** Sleeve that shields part of the ratchet ring so the pawl skips teeth during the first part of its stroke, varying the effective feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-space feed throw must vary, but the platen ratchet has a fixed throw.

**Solution:** Sleeve that shields part of the ratchet ring so the pawl skips teeth during the first part of its stroke, varying the effective feed.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
