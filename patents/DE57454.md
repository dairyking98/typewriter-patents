## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The pointer typewriter of the main patent needs to print both small and capital letters.

**Solution:** Lift the type wheel along its axis to bring a second wreath of capitals to the print point, triggered by pulling out an extension of the pointer's finger ring.

**How it works:** The type wheel carries two wreaths, small letters and capitals. The pointer has a pull-out extension carrying the finger ring. Pulled out, the ring sits over the outer part of the fingerboard, and a pin slide on the extension lifts an arm that raises the type wheel (via a two-armed frame lever acting on the wheel axis) so the capital wreath faces the paper. Pushing the extension back lets the wheel sink by its own weight. A small cone-edged disc on the wheel axis and a vertical lever on the swinging paper support complete the lift if the writer pulled the extension out only part way, and lift the wheel faster just before impression so the print is not smeared. A modification uses separate fingerboards and a fixed forked pointer, the outer board lifting the wheel via a lever on the frame to reduce the pull the writer must exert.
