## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are complicated, need skill and are costly; a very cheap device is wanted for people unskilled with the pen.

**Solution:** A stencil plate that rolls across the paper on toothed spacing drums, with a hand-held stylus bearing type and fed by an ink pad.

**How it works:** A stencil plate lies on the paper, with a shaft carrying two matching toothed drums protruding through slots so that turning the shaft traverses the plate along a straight line. The teeth set the letter spacing, felt by the operator's finger on the shaft under a hand-support shield. A pen or stylus whose point carries type is inked on a pad fixed to the plate and used through the stencil openings, with a spring mechanism and automatic inking device described.

**Features:** [USE:toy](../features/use-toy.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** stencil plate; spacing shaft; toothed drum; hand shield; ink pad; stylus; spring mechanism
