## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks cannot be mechanically sorted because their shape and size vary, and punch cards are separated from the documents.

**Solution:** Memo cards or strips are conveyed in step with the checks through the same machine so each card stays matched to its check.
