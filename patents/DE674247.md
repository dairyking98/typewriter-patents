> Description Translated from German The invention relates in particular to business machines controlled by punch cards and, in a narrower sense, relates to the paper circuit in such machines. As is well known, the paper switching devices of these machines are subject to high demands in that the type of paper switching required in each case changes, with three paper switching being considered in principle, namely line switching, compensating circuit and gap switching. Line switching is understood to mean those paper switching in which the printing material is advanced by a normal line spacing for each item printing, specifically for machines controlled by punched cards for each card machine game. Intermediate space switching is a switching path for the transport of the printing material, which is carried out under certain operating conditions of the machine, namely preferably when a card group change, during which a total print usually also takes place.
