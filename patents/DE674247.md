## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card business machine paper circuit)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates in particular to business machines controlled by punch cards and, in a narrower sense, relates to the paper circuit in such machines.

**How it works (reading):** The text proposes a split platen so that two regions of the form tape can receive different switching movements at once, for example invoice forms and lifting lists in one work step.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper switching in tabulating machines cannot run two registration bands at the same time with different switching movements, since the paper switching devices work one after the other.

**Solution:** The text proposes a split platen so that two regions of the form tape can receive different switching movements at once, for example invoice forms and lifting lists in one work step.

Line switching is understood to mean those paper switching in which the printing material is advanced by a normal line spacing for each item printing, specifically for machines controlled by punched cards for each card machine game. Intermediate space switching is a switching path for the transport of the printing material, which is carried out under certain operating conditions of the machine, namely preferably when a card group change, during which a total print usually also takes place.
