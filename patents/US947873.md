## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the line spacing mechanism of typewriting machines and its object is to make it.

**How it works (reading):** Stop lever on the platen axle sets a fixed partial rotation of the platen, less than a line space, forwards or backwards.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accents, fractions and symbols must print slightly above or below the line.

**Solution:** Stop lever on the platen axle sets a fixed partial rotation of the platen, less than a line space, forwards or backwards.
