## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A machine for printing musical notes needs the platen set to specific lines and spaces of the staff, but the gear that rotates it must not turn along with the platen when ordinary characters are printed.

**Solution:** A toothed gear wheel coupled to the platen shaft by a clutch, driven by a lever-operated toothed sector on a slide that can be thrown out of mesh, positions the platen to any staff line or space.

**How it works:** The platen, a steel cylinder lined with rubber, slides lengthwise for letter spacing on a keyed shaft that passes into its hollow. A gear wheel at one end couples to that shaft with a set screw clutch, and is loosened for ordinary typing. A toothed sector on a rock shaft meshes with the gear, and a lever with a hand piece moves along a circular scale marked with the lines and spaces of the staff. A spring-loaded edge engages a toothed sector to lock the lever in the chosen position. The sector's rock shaft sits on a slide plate moved by a rocking lever, so the sector can be thrown in or out of mesh.
