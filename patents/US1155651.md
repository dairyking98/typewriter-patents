## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Existing typewriters cannot write musical notation together with ordinary letters and figures practically.

**Solution:** A typewriter with ordinary type-levers plus special printing wheels and drums for note heads, stems and staff symbols, with a platen that moves in two directions.

**How it works:** The machine has a keyboard, a striking frame and an actuator, with a platen on a carriage that can be shifted vertically and rotated by cranks. Special devices write note heads and musical symbols, other devices draw the staff and the strokes of notes above or below it. Printing drums are mounted on levers and displaced axially, then held in place during the impression. An inking device and a spacing mechanism are included. A second embodiment uses printing drums instead of ordinary type-levers.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** keyboard; striking frame; actuator; platen; platen carriage; printing drum; printing wheel; inking device; universal joint

**Numbers:** 19 sheets of drawings
