## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike type-shift machines are complex and costly, and their characters need better alinement.

**Solution:** A type-bar segment on a vertically guided carrier is shifted by a shift-key lever system with latch, and type-bars are individually adjustable on slides in the segment.

**How it works:** Shift keys act through cam-slotted fingers and a rock-shaft lever to raise or lower the type-bar segment carrier on guide pins, with a notched arm latch and counterbalance spring. Each type-bar pivots on a slide in a radial slot of the segment, fixed by a binding screw for individual alinement. A key lever pulls a link-rod through a cam-slotted rocker, giving direct horizontal pull on the type-bar.
