## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A variable-spacer user has no scale for measuring the arbitrary spacing and line-space positions of a type-writer.

**Solution:** A graduated band that fits around the type-cylinder, with one scale matching the line-space ratchet notches and another for perpendicular spacing, so spacing can be measured and set.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** graduated band; outer scale D; inner scale E; cylinder support B; carriage front scale

**Numbers:** Patent 651,488; Serial No. 727,578
