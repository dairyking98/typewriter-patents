## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (reading board for adding machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** The reading table is moved by a rack and gear driven from the adding machine shaft while the display device stays fixed; both may also be made displaceable.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The reading board must be forced by the adding machine for certain kinds of evidence, which the earlier arrangement did not allow for.

**Solution:** The reading table is moved by a rack and gear driven from the adding machine shaft while the display device stays fixed; both may also be made displaceable.
