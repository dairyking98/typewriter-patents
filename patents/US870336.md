## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a simple and economically constructed device, by means of which the manifolding ribbon willbe automatically fed step by step through the machine in a positive manner at each longitudinal movement of the carriage.
