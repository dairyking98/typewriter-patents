## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a simple and economically constructed device, by means of which the manifolding ribbon willbe automatically fed step by step through the machine in a positive manner at each longitudinal movement of the carriage.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon feed mechanism with an adjustable trip intermittently revolves the ribbon spools at each longitudinal movement of the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manifolding ribbon must be fed step by step through the machine positively at each carriage movement.

**Solution:** A ribbon feed mechanism with an adjustable trip intermittently revolves the ribbon spools at each longitudinal movement of the carriage.

**How it works:** The trip and feed mechanism turn the spools a step at a time as the carriage moves.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)

**Parts:** step-by-step ribbon feed; adjustable trip
