## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Digits and letters in a line must line up when the carriage is set forward or back by a number of spaces, and the carriage must be checked at a fixed point.

**Solution:** A keybar links through a lever to a rack bar carrying an adjustable stop, and an offset on the carriage is checked by the stop, so the carriage moves back a set number of spaces per keystroke.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** keybar; link; lever; rack bar; carriage; offset; adjustable stop; way-rod
