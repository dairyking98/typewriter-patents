## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Threading or attaching end of record carrier on or to single reel

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to ribbon-attaching-devices, particularly adapted for detachably connecting the end of a ribbon with a spool having a slotted barrel.

**How it works (reading):** A semicircular spring-acting sheet metal clip fits over the outside of the spool barrel, with a lapped or folded edge that clamps the ribbon end and a means to stop it turning on the barrel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon end must be attached to and removed from a slotted spool barrel easily, without soiling the hands and without a tool.

**Solution:** A semicircular spring-acting sheet metal clip fits over the outside of the spool barrel, with a lapped or folded edge that clamps the ribbon end and a means to stop it turning on the barrel.
