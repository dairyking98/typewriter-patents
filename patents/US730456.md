## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Silent keys (accent marks) must lift the ribbon to the print point without feeding the carriage, whether the platen is in upper or lower case position.

**Solution:** A crank on a rock-shaft driven by the silent key lifts the ribbon guide through a carrier, with a vertical guideway so it works in either shift position.

**How it works:** The silent key lever has a forked arm straddling a crank on a rock-shaft carried on the shiftable carriage-rail frame. The crank slides along the fork as the platen frame shifts up or down. The rock-shaft crank engages an arm on a curved carrier hooked to the ribbon guide, pulled back by a spring. Ordinary keys lift the same carrier via the universal bar and a collar in a vertical guideway. Silent keys do not touch the universal bar, so the carriage does not feed.
