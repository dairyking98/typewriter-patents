## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stenographic machine must write whole sentences without a spacer, and the two sets of markers need to be depressed simultaneously or alternately.

**Solution:** Two independent sets of five keys each make dash or dot impressions in parallel groups across a paper ribbon; a spacing device pivoted on the frame is drawn down by a spring, and the ratchet teeth set the spacing between groups.

**Features:** [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
