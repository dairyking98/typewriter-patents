## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar bearings need adjustment, must keep dust out, and the front-strike action should be compact and let the operator see the printing.

**Solution:** Type-bars are pivoted in hangers on a fixed segment below the platen and strike the front of the platen; the bearing hangers are adjustable and sealed, and the platen shifts between upper and lower case.
