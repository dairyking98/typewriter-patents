## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter noise escapes through the front opening of the padded box around the key levers.

**Solution:** Encloses the whole transmission in a closed padded box with only a paper slot and sealed passages for key stems and crank axles.
