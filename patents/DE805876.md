## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The window cover in the side walls of writing machines needs a locking device that is complicated and hard to handle.

**Solution:** Spring-loaded push-up latches on each side wall carry cross bolts with angled noses that hold the window cover in its seat and release it when pressed down.
