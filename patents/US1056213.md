## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen line-spacing feed needed an efficient, adjustable mechanism that could move the paper one or several spaces at will.

**Solution:** A shaft beneath the platen carries an arm with an adjustable engaging pawl, so rotating the shaft turns the platen by one or more line spaces as set.
