**How it works (reading):** A wedge-shaped nose on the eraser head engages the notch in the central guide so the eraser stops exactly at the character to be erased.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasing next to the wanted character is hard to position on a typewriter with a flexible shaft.

**Solution:** A wedge-shaped nose on the eraser head engages the notch in the central guide so the eraser stops exactly at the character to be erased.
