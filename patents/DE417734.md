## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-lever rest supports either cause springback of the type lever or make noise, and both get worse with use.

**Solution:** Felt strips on the support sit on a sponge-rubber insert, which absorbs impact and prevents springback and permanent dents.
