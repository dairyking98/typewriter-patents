## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Supply holders for sheets or fan-folded webs, e.g. shelves, tables, scrolls, pile holders

**How it works (reading):** The upper part rolls on rails of the lower part and connects loosely to the paper guide, so it lifts off easily.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage weight must be lifted when the shift key is pressed, and the whole upper part is hard to remove.

**Solution:** The upper part rolls on rails of the lower part and connects loosely to the paper guide, so it lifts off easily.
