## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and has-for its object to improve the carriage mechanism and brake mechanism and also to provide sundry keys which in addition to normally operating the type-levers are so constructed that they may be utilized for operating a carriage-release mechanism and one of the ordinary keys may besides its ordinary function have that of moving the carriage backward.

**How it works (reading):** Double-function keys whose slidable front section lets a depressed key also release the escapement and apply a spring brake to the rack, or drive a backspace dog.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Underwood typebar carriage needed a better carriage brake and release, and ways to use ordinary type keys for carriage release and backspacing without extra keys.

**Solution:** Double-function keys whose slidable front section lets a depressed key also release the escapement and apply a spring brake to the rack, or drive a backspace dog.

**How it works:** A key has a front section that slides lengthwise on its key lever. In the normal position it prints through the usual key lever, elbow lever and link; pushed down independently, its toe engages a release bar and, through a link and lever, pushes a release-and-brake bar down. That bar throws both feed dogs off the rack so the spring-driven carriage runs, while a semi-elliptic spring brake with a cushion presses progressively harder on the rack to retard it. With the front section slid back, its toe instead moves a lever train that brings a toothed dog into the rack along an inclined slot and moves the carriage one space backward. A separate release lever frees the dogs for free carriage movement, and the two-part carriage joins by a detachable hinge pivot.
