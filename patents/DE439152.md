## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source

**How it works (reading):** Each hammer has a drive pulley with a spring-loaded lock, and non-circular discs couple it to a fast shaft when an auxiliary key is pressed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Auxiliary-key drives couple the hammer clutch in a pressure-dependent way.

**Solution:** Each hammer has a drive pulley with a spring-loaded lock, and non-circular discs couple it to a fast shaft when an auxiliary key is pressed.
