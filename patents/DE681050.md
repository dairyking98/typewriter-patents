## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Music matrices take years of skill to make, because the guide sleeve must be positioned by hand for each note.

**Solution:** Stamp positioning uses two slides moved at right angles, with a guide sleeve that sets each stamp on the template's staff lines.
