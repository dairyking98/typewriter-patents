**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Letter-cards or letter-sheets, i.e. cards or sheets each of which is to be folded with the message inside and to serve as its own envelope for mailing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The object is achieved in that the "lungs of the address flap in formed by a paper strip glued to the card, only on one side open pockets.

**How it works (reading):** The flap is held by open-ended pockets formed from a paper strip glued to the card on one side, so no slots are cut through the card.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Foldable address flaps on typewriter postcards need slots or tongues that weaken the card and tear.

**Solution:** The flap is held by open-ended pockets formed from a paper strip glued to the card on one side, so no slots are cut through the card.
