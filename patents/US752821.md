## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tab stop lets the carriage jump past the position, and the carriage cannot be moved back accurately to a chosen space.

**Solution:** The carriage first jumps left against the tabulating stop, then is moved back to the right by the improvement the required number of spaces; a stop or lock works with a loose-dog escapement.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
