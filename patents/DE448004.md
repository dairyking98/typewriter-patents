**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (booking machine printing unit with two receipts)

**How it works (reading):** A second cam on the lifting disc of the printing unit shaft releases the document button and hammer lock again just after printing, so the hammer is blocked for the empty stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When only one document is inserted, the print hammer makes a second empty stroke onto the ink ribbon, which soils the hammer and transfers marks to later documents.

**Solution:** A second cam on the lifting disc of the printing unit shaft releases the document button and hammer lock again just after printing, so the hammer is blocked for the empty stroke.
