## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide an appliance of simple construction for cleaning the type of type-writers, and also one which may be applied with facility to any ordinary machine.

**How it works (reading):** A brush mounted outside the machine on a slide in a way on the top plate, moved to clean each type separately with the face visible.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A brush set between the types collects dust and blocks the operator's view of the type faces.

**Solution:** A brush mounted outside the machine on a slide in a way on the top plate, moved to clean each type separately with the face visible.
