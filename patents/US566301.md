## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars swinging toward a common centre need accurate aim, interchangeable parts and no side play or twist during the printing stroke.

**Solution:** A one-piece type-bar carrier-guide of two concentric rings joined by radial guide-bars with slots between them positively guides, stops and aligns each typebar.

**How it works:** Typebars are pivoted at their outer ends in the slots between radial guide-bars that are angular in section. The guide-bars hold each bar against rotation about its own axis and against movement off the plane of its stroke during the last part of the working stroke. Outer and inner rings sit in different horizontal planes, and the guide limits the working stroke as a stop. The guide is made from a disc blank swaged between dies, then the radial slots are milled by a cutter.
