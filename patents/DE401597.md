## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guards, shields or dust excluders

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for the automatic opening of Box lids, especially those on sound-absorbing boxes for writing, calculating and similar machines.

**How it works (reading):** Two telescoping springs, with the outer enclosing the inner, halve their length when collapsed, and toggle levers brake the lift and hold the lid firmly at the end stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting the lid of a sound-absorbing machine box takes a large force, and a single spring either hits the stop hard or lets the lid rebound.

**Solution:** Two telescoping springs, with the outer enclosing the inner, halve their length when collapsed, and toggle levers brake the lift and hold the lid firmly at the end stop.

**Features:** [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** box lid; telescoping springs; spring sleeves; toggle levers; stroke limiter stop
