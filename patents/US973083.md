## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-strike typebar action with a shiftable type basket needs an easy touch and nearly uniform leverage across all keys.

**Solution:** Key levers act through horizontal first-order sub-levers and rearward pull links on typebars pivoted on a shiftable segment, in two concentric staggered series.

**How it works:** Each key lever connects by an upright link to a horizontal fore-and-aft sub-lever pivoted on a hanger on a fixed segment. A pull link runs rearward from the sub-lever to a typebar, pivoted on hangers on a vertical segment that can be shifted for case. The typebars and their links are arranged in two staggered concentric series, so the bars of different lengths all end in the same transverse plane at a type pad. The segment hangs on shift links from a shift frame, and the whole basket moves for upper case.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** key lever; upright link; sub-lever; hanger; pull link; typebar; type segment; type pad; shift frame; shift link; platen carriage

**Numbers:** two concentric series of typebars

**Improves on:**

- Smith application Serial No. 479,859: simpler and more efficient front-strike action with shiftable segment
