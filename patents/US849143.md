## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon moistened by a fixed roller receives uneven or excessive colouring matter and cannot be regulated.

**Solution:** A damping roller of absorbent sections, fed from a fluid tube with metered holes, is adjustable in permeability and is held at rest so the ribbon takes a regulated amount of ink.

**How it works:** A tube holds the fluid and feeds it through apertures into a damping roller; the roller sections are secured together and the inking roller can be removed or changed.
