## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register with cashier keys) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with visual indication, e.g. counter drum

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to Cash registers with detachable cashier keys that do this when inserted into a the intended opening of the cash register unlock the machine drive and at the same time adjust the print wheels bearing the cashier's characters as necessary so that each Registration also specifies the cashier who has been active in each case.

**How it works (reading):** The cashier key engages the drive racks positively both on insertion and on withdrawal, so setting and return of the print wheels are forced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction-driven setting of print wheels by a removable cashier key was not reliable.

**Solution:** The cashier key engages the drive racks positively both on insertion and on withdrawal, so setting and return of the print wheels are forced.
