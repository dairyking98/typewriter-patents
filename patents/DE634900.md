## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a carbon paper feeder for typewriters or the like, in which the sheets of carbon paper fed in from the side transversely to the direction of advance of the writing sheets via detachable deflection guides located at an angle to the feed path of the writing sheets be diverted into this feed path.

**How it works (reading):** Inclined rod-shaped deflection guides with transverse wire braces are attached to oscillating, removable axes so they can fold up from horizontal, letting carbon sheets be inserted easily.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed deflection guides for carbon paper sheets are immobile in working position, so inserting carbon sheets between writing sheets is slow and tears sheets.

**Solution:** Inclined rod-shaped deflection guides with transverse wire braces are attached to oscillating, removable axes so they can fold up from horizontal, letting carbon sheets be inserted easily.

**Features:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
