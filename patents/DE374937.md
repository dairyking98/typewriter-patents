## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register two-check dispenser)

**How it works (reading):** A gear and lever linkage ties the setting elements so that only one check is printed for some items and two for others.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash registers issued two identical checks even when only one was needed for drinks.

**Solution:** A gear and lever linkage ties the setting elements so that only one check is printed for some items and two for others.
