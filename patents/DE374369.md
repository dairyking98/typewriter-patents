## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a drive for typewriters and similar machines controlled by punched tape.

**How it works (reading):** Pawls on swinging rails follow perforations in the punched tape and drop into recesses of a rotating roller, so each rail shifts sideways; the rails act directly on the key levers through adjustable screws, and a common pressure plate releases all pawls together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punched-tape drives that move rails through spaced approach blocks are complex, and the drive positions cannot be changed easily.

**Solution:** Pawls on swinging rails follow perforations in the punched tape and drop into recesses of a rotating roller, so each rail shifts sideways; the rails act directly on the key levers through adjustable screws, and a common pressure plate releases all pawls together.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** rails 10; pawls 20; perforated tape S; roller 30 with recesses 31; shoulder 12; pressure plate 60; adjusting screws
