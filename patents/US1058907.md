## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Column tabulation and denomination stops needed a better arrangement for figure work on a visible machine; the text is only partly legible.

**Solution:** Column stops on the carriage are set by a forked arm that controls a denomination stop, with a guide rack and scale for the tabulating lever on a main frame with rollers and balls.
