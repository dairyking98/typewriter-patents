## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A portable printing machine that prints text easily.

**Solution:** A rubber-ringed disc cut into spring fingers, each carrying a type underneath and a matching button on top, turned to a notch and pressed by finger to print, while the press also feeds a rack to slide the paper-holding slider along a rail.

**How it works:** A tippable frame on a sprung post carries a disc cut like a chrysanthemum into many spring fingers; a rubber ring on the disc carries a type on each finger's underside and a matching button on top. The user finds the character on the labelled central plate, turns the disc to bring its button to a notch in the frame, and presses it, so the type goes through a hole and prints on paper wrapped on a roller. The frame goes down against its spring, and a spring pawl on the frame feeds a rack on a slider one tooth, so the slider carrying the two paper rollers moves on for the next character. Turning the disc rubs the types over ink dishes carried on a flat spring bar and showing through holes. A finger tab spaces by feeding the rack without printing, and a pawl on the rail and a star wheel with pawl on one roller regulate the slider movement.

> Motoki-type portable printing machine. Nature and object.
