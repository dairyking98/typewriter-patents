## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter carriage needs to trip an attached adding machine at chosen points along its travel.

**Solution:** A clip-on tripping block with a spring latch that grips the teeth of the carriage rack and can be slid to any position.

**How it works:** A body with wings and flanges surrounds the rack bar at the rear of the carriage. A latch pivoted to the body has a catch that engages between the rack teeth, held by a compression spring. As the carriage travels, the latch's inclined face meets a pawl on the adding machine and shifts a member sideways, freeing the accumulator bar to move. To reposition the block, the latch is released and the block slid along the rack.

**Parts:** tripping block; latch; compression spring; carriage rack bar; pawl; accumulator bar
