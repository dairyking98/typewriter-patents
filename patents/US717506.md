## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Postal wheel machine: the type-wheel drive needed to be simpler and positive in both directions, with the wheel turned to the chosen character and then tilted to the platen on a rocking frame.

**Solution:** A keyboard turns a type-wheel through a rocking segment and pinion until a geared rack stop-arm hits a pin raised by the pressed key, then the whole frame tilts the wheel down onto the platen, with a locking bail and toothed disk holding it accurate.

**How it works:** Each key lever raises a pin that pushes a bar on a swinging driving lever; the lever strikes one of two opposite shoulders on a slotted bracket, rotating a rocking sleeve with a gear segment that turns the type-wheel pinion left or right (and positively back again). A rack geared to the wheel carries a stop-arm that slides until it hits the pin raised by the pressed key, halting the wheel at that character. Continued key travel lifts a locking bail out of a hook and tilts the whole frame, so the wheel strikes the platen; a toothed disk engages a horn to lock the wheel at impact. A key with no pin becomes a spacer working the escapement dog. Case shift moves the wheel shaft lengthwise through a lever operated by shift keys.

**Features:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
