## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**How it works (reading):** A toothed piece on the switching frame meshes with a tooth segment carried by push rods, and a sliding piece on the segment removes play, so the platen is locked in its middle position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Toggle-lever platen switching develops play in its joints, so the platen does not stay in the middle position and the baseline drifts.

**Solution:** A toothed piece on the switching frame meshes with a tooth segment carried by push rods, and a sliding piece on the segment removes play, so the platen is locked in its middle position.
