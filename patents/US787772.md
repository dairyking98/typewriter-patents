## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** A mechanism counts and indicates written lines and locks the platen against rotation when a set number is reached, through a sliding block in an indicating casing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Solution:** A mechanism counts and indicates written lines and locks the platen against rotation when a set number is reached, through a sliding block in an indicating casing.
