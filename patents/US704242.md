## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon attachments for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A second ribbon on a spool at one end of the carriage is drawn across the printing point when wanted, held by a catch until released, and retracted by a spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing in two colours needs a second ribbon without removing the normal ribbon or the paper.

**Solution:** A second ribbon on a spool at one end of the carriage is drawn across the printing point when wanted, held by a catch until released, and retracted by a spring.
