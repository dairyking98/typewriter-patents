## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The space bar acting through the universal bar releases the escapement as far as a character key does, so a slow stroke could cam the dog back and let the carriage run several spaces, and it also needlessly lifts the ribbon.

**Solution:** The space bar acts on the escapement directly, independently of the universal bar, with a shorter dog movement, and does not vibrate the ribbon.

**How it works:** Typebars strike the front of a platen on a ball-bearing carriage fed by rack and pinion through an escapement wheel with a stepping dog and a beveled holding dog on a pivoted dog rocker. Character keys act on the rocker through the universal bar. The space bar is arranged to operate the rocker by its own link so the movement is smaller and does not engage the ribbon vibrating mechanism. Only the opening is available.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; stepping dog; holding dog; dog rocker; universal bar; space bar; ribbon; typebar; platen

**Improves on:**

- universal-bar-driven space bar: space bar operates the escapement independently and skips the ribbon lift
