## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a carriage switching device for typewriters, hechenin machines and the like, in which the reciprocating movement of the pawl rocker is effected by the type lever with the intermediary of a natural universal rail.

**How it works (reading):** The type lever moves a universal rail whose curved end engages a pin on the pawl rocker, so one common spring drives the rocker in both directions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage switching pawl rocker needs a simpler, more reliable drive and fewer springs.

**Solution:** The type lever moves a universal rail whose curved end engages a pin on the pawl rocker, so one common spring drives the rocker in both directions.
