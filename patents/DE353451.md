## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The movable paper carriage is carried by a stationary guide part, and the author wants a thinner slide and a simpler way of storing it in the bed.

**Solution:** The carriage is a sheet-metal part with an X-shaped cross-section whose vertical faces run between the flanges of a detachable bed on rollers or other friction devices; the bed is bolted on and locked by a flap.
