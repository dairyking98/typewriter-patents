## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies arranged in stationary or sliding cases or frames or upon flexible strips, plates, bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of my present invention is to provide a machine of the type referred to which is so designed that the type-carrying member will arrive in printing position before the type-actuating hammer is operated.

**How it works (reading):** Key levers do both jobs in one stroke: the first half of travel positions the type carrier vertically and laterally, the second half fires the hammer.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machines that position a type carrier before a hammer strikes need the carrier to arrive before the hammer fires.

**Solution:** Key levers do both jobs in one stroke: the first half of travel positions the type carrier vertically and laterally, the second half fires the hammer.

**How it works:** A type-carrying member holding numerals and the alphabet is moved vertically and transversely by arms to bring one type in line with a hammer. The first half of key-lever descent drives the positioning arms, and the last half operates the hammer that strikes the type against the paper on the platen. A modified hammer actuating form is also shown. Only the opening is available.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
- **Type element:** [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)
- **Impression:** [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** type-carrying member; hammer; key lever; lateral arm; vertical arm; platen
