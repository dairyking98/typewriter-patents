## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, inexpensive typewriter was wanted; this patent targets cheapness with a hand-operated type wheel.

**Solution:** A hand arm swung over a curved dial plate turns a type wheel through a rack and gear until an indicator spring points to the letter, then pressing the arm down drops the indicator into a notch, locks the wheel, prints and advances the paper.

**How it works:** The operator swings an arm sideways; a curved rack on the arm meshes with a gear fixed to the type wheel, so the wheel turns as the indicator on the arm moves along a removable curved dial plate. When the indicator points at a letter, pressing the arm down moves the frame carrying the wheel onto the paper; the indicator drops into a notch of a toothed rack that blocks any sideways motion, and a pin on a bracket locks the gear teeth so the wheel cannot turn after touching the paper. Upper and lower case alternate on the wheel, and a thumbscrew shifts the indicator within a recess on the arm by one letter-pair spacing to select case. On release, a leaf spring raises the frame and the swing of a hanging arm drives a pawl that advances the sliding paper-roller frame one ratchet tooth. A bell hammer falls when the frame passes a crank-shaft near line end, and a lever lifts the pawl for return. A spring-mounted inking roller rides on the wheel.
