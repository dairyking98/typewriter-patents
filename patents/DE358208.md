## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper feeding device for typewriters and the like in which the axle carrying the pressure rollers held in spring-loaded arms and for the purpose Release of the paper to be pressed off by means of the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Pressure-roller arms are mounted on a separate axle in a small frame that installs as a whole, independent of the platen frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressure rollers on a single axle either spin freely or are rigidly bound, so the middle roller slips; assembly also disturbs the platen frame.

**Solution:** Pressure-roller arms are mounted on a separate axle in a small frame that installs as a whole, independent of the platen frame.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper feeding device, in particular for typewriters. The invention relates to a paper feeding device for typewriters and the like in which the axle carrying the pressure rollers held in spring-loaded arms and for the purpose Release of the paper to be pressed off by means of the platen. The previously known institutions of this type show one Series of evils, most of which the difficulty of assembling the facility emerges. So far one has the paper feed in its most essential Parts arranged on connecting axes of the writing roller frame, so that to one Disassembly of the device removed the connecting axis had to become, whereby the stability of the platen frame was impaired and this his Could change shape. These drawbacks are remedied by the present invention in the manner helped that the bearing arms for the pressure rollers with their springs on a special Axis (on each side of the paper roller separately), which is stored in a small frame, which is then inserted as a whole into the machine can be built in and with the help of a screw and some mounting pins can be attached so securely that the pressure rollers are in the desired position maintained…
