## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manual carriage shifting at line end, manual line spacing and separate letter-spacing between words take effort and time.

**Solution:** A compact enclosed machine where the carriage shifting, line spacing and letter spacing all happen automatically from the operation of the printing keys, with adjustable line width.

**How it works:** A carriage of terminal spiders carries a roller platen and moves transverse to the lines of writing on a guide and a track. Key levers link through bell-crank levers to type-arms whose type heads rest on a fixed inking pad and strike the platen. A main ratchet and an auxiliary ratchet with a rocking pawl-carrying lever perform the line spacing, with adjustment to change the line width. A feed pawl and stop pawl, depressed in turn, do the letter spacing, and a platen with means to engage opposite edges of the sheet is shown. The text stops before the full operating description.
