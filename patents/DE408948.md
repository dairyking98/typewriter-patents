## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Column stop locking with a steel wire and toothed bar was costly to make.

**Solution:** Column stops sit on a shaft combined with a cylinder-section locking member with notches, engaging a catch on each stop to lock and delete.
