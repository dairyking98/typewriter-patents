## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The typebar guide plate was a thick hardened, straightened and ground steel plate screwed on all sides: expensive and slow to make.

**Solution:** Use a very thin (about 0.2 mm) watch-spring steel plate screwed only on the platen side, loosely resting on the guide frame on the other side.

**How it works:** The thin plate carries the type slots for the typebars and is screwed to the guide frame together with the type guide head only at the side facing the platen. The side towards the guide combs rests loose, held down by the typebars' weight. The frame is an injection-moulded part; the plate gives a smooth wear-resistant sliding surface and is exchangeable.
