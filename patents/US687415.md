## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Type

**In the patent’s own words:** The object being to provide means whereby any of the machines nowin use for European languages can be readily converted into a machine for writing Arabic or any other language in which the formation of letters is similar to Arabic-such as Turkish, Persian, and the like-and which improvements are also applicable in the construction of new type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** Signs and characters are attached to the type-levers, with matching signs on caps or finger-keys, and the carriage travels left to right instead, so an existing machine can be converted for Arabic.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Existing European-language machines could not write Arabic or similar scripts, whose letter forms differ from Latin letters.

**Solution:** Signs and characters are attached to the type-levers, with matching signs on caps or finger-keys, and the carriage travels left to right instead, so an existing machine can be converted for Arabic.

**How it works:** Combinations of signs on type-levers select the Arabic-form characters; the carriage direction is reversed. Details are in drawings not legible in the OCR.
