## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines with an inclined segment and four-row straight keyboard need intermediate levers between key and typebar, which complicates them.

**Solution:** Connect key levers directly to the typebars by coupling links, with key levers cranked more and more toward the sides so each key end swings in its typebar's slot plane.

**How it works:** Key levers sit in open slot bearings on a base plate that is also the frame, held by springs. Each is joined by an equal-length coupling link to a typebar guided in a slot of an inclined segment, on a curved wire axis. Typebars grow longer from the middle outward. Key levers have increasing crank and inclination toward the sides and all bearing points lie in one plane.
