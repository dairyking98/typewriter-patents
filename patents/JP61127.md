## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies; Western type typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an arrangement of the typebars in a printing machine in which the resting position of the type block of each typebar is set progressively further inward from the middle of the row toward the outer ends on the left and right.

**How it works (reading):** Set the resting position of each type block progressively further inward from the middle toward both ends, by making the bars progressively shorter toward the ends or by setting their pivots progressively further back.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a large typebar basket the bars at the outer ends must bend sharply between bar and type block, so their type blocks need wide spacing, the basket is big, and the bars touch and twist on impact.

**Solution:** Set the resting position of each type block progressively further inward from the middle toward both ends, by making the bars progressively shorter toward the ends or by setting their pivots progressively further back.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot on one continuous steel wire laid in an arc groove of the segment plate about a point B, in slots that converge on a point A beyond B; both lie on the centre line of the type block at the moment of printing. The bend between bar and block falls at A, so the bar length equals the distance from A to the wire: longest in the middle, shorter toward both ends. This closes the gaps between resting type blocks, reducing the occupied area and the bend angle. A variant keeps all bars one length but sets the pivot line further back toward the ends of the plate. A guide slot at the bar neck fixes the strike position.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Improvement in the arrangement of the typebars in a printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to an arrangement of the typebars in a printing machine in which the resting position of the type block of each typebar is set progressively further inward from the middle of the row toward the outer ends on the left and right. Its object is an arrangement of the typebars that makes the area occupied by the type blocks at rest smaller, and so makes the mechanism smaller, while reducing contact between typebars in use and reducing the harm that comes from a large bend between typebar and type block at the moment of printing.
