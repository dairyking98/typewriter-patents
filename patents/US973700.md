## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be moved sideways between two recording machines sharing one platen.

**Solution:** A laterally movable carriage holds the paper at one platen position and feeds it letter and line space, then slides it to the adjacent platen.
