## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A releasable clutch between the line-space wheel and the platen should be simple, cheap, compact and fit existing machines.

**Solution:** A dished three-armed spring plate on the platen axle is buckled by a lever so its arm tips spread and bite a flange on the line-space wheel.

**How it works:** The line-space wheel runs loose on the platen axle with an annular flange forming a drum. Inside the drum a dished spring plate with three radial arms turns with the axle via pins in radial slots. A lever on the axle, moved by a finger button pushed or pulled outside the finger wheel, buckles the plate to a dead centre. The arms lengthen and grip the flange, locking wheel to platen. Pulling the button relaxes the plate so the platen turns freely while a check holds the line-space wheel.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen axle; line-space wheel; flange; dished spring plate; clutch arm; clutch lever; finger button; finger wheel; spring check

**Numbers:** three radial arms

**Improves on:**

- existing Underwood line-space clutches: simpler, cheaper, compact buckling spring plate
