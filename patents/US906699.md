**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**How it works (reading):** A revised carriage-feed arrangement in which a pair of feed dogs on a dog shaft, with a spring, acts on a feed wheel through the rack and pinion to move the swinging carriage.

**Problem:** The excerpt does not clearly state the defect; the carriage-feed mechanism of a No. 6 Remington-type machine is being improved.
