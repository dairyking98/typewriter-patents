## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers or sets of front or rear rollers each mounted on a separate carrier

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to Paper guides for typewriters and similar machines, in particular such with the paper guide plate loosely inserted under the platen.

**How it works (reading):** The guide plate is hung on bolts and swings down while moving forward with the front pressure rollers, so the plate openings need not be enlarged.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting pressure rollers and the paper guide plate pushes the plate forward against the platen, which is awkward for thick stacks.

**Solution:** The guide plate is hung on bolts and swings down while moving forward with the front pressure rollers, so the plate openings need not be enlarged.

In this way it is made possible that from the front as well how thicker piles of paper can be introduced from backwards, as is the case with accounting work etc. is often necessary.
