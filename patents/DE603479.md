## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for limiting the beginning and end of the line on typing, calculating and similar machines which margin stop blocks work together with a fixed stop a common or on a special rail each and are performed by the The ends of the car have movable pawls with teeth or notches work together on the guide rail.

**How it works (reading):** Each margin stop is spring-loaded to move to its fixed stop after its pawl is lifted, and an extra release bar lifts all pawls from the toothed guide rod.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stop reset requires several manual steps to move the paper carriage to a new margin position.

**Solution:** Each margin stop is spring-loaded to move to its fixed stop after its pawl is lifted, and an extra release bar lifts all pawls from the toothed guide rod.
