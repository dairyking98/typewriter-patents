## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reduce the muscular effort of typing and make impressions uniform.

**Solution:** A solenoid-driven device mounted over the keyboard in which pressing a key engages a finger with a cross-bar that the magnet pulls down to operate the typewriter's own key.

**How it works:** A frame holds rock-shafts with bell-cranks, bars and a spring-restored lever. Above the shafts are key levers whose pendent arms push a spring-loaded bolt on a bifurcated finger into the path of a cross-bar; the finger's lower end rests on the typewriter key. A solenoid plunger pulls the cross-bar down, driving the engaged finger and the typewriter's key. A yielding link protects the parts if blocked. Contacts closed by the keys energize the magnet; space bar and shift are included.
