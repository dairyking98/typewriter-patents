## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The Remington platen shifts between only two positions, so a typebar can carry only two characters. Brooks wants three or more types per bar printing on one line.

**Solution:** Two shift key-levers move the platen forward by different amounts to three stopped positions, so each typebar can hold three or more types.

**How it works:** The platen sits on a secondary frame that rolls on the carriage and is pushed at right angles to its axis by a shifting bar on a bell-crank lever. Two broken levers on a common rock-shaft are slotted over two shift key-levers. One slot has lost motion, so both keys can be pressed together while the shaft takes the larger throw. Pressing one key gives the second position and pressing the other gives the third. Stops under each key and on the carriage hold the three positions. A spring returns the platen to its normal position when the keys are released. In a variant the broken levers are pivoted to the key-levers at different distances from the fulcrum to get different throws.
