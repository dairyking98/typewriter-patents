## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles characterised by being applied to printers having transversely- moving carriages and detached from the carriage

**How it works (reading):** An add-on device holds a paper web and its carbon apart from the carriage so that ordinary typewriters can label web forms without the machine itself moving sideways.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Labeling continuous form webs on ordinary typewriters needs expensive special machines or sheets pinned to a belt, because the paper web cannot be guided sideways on the carriage.

**Solution:** An add-on device holds a paper web and its carbon apart from the carriage so that ordinary typewriters can label web forms without the machine itself moving sideways.
