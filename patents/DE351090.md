## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** After roller replacement or thicker paper, the roller diameter changes the type-to-paper setting, so the imprint is wrong.

**Solution:** The roller frame bearings are cylindrical bushings rotating in small housings with eccentric bores, so turning them moves the roller frame to set the correct distance to the type.
