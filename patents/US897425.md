## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more especially to the line lock mechanism of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** The universal bar is arrested at varying points in its return to lighten the touch, and at the end of a line it is moved to its extreme return position and locked there.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The keys must move a long way against the universal bar spring, which makes the touch heavy, and at the end of a line the keys need a lock.

**Solution:** The universal bar is arrested at varying points in its return to lighten the touch, and at the end of a line it is moved to its extreme return position and locked there.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** universal bar; universal bar spring; keys; line lock; shaft
