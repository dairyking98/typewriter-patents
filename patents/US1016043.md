## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating needs the carriage to advance more than one letter space at predetermined points.

**Solution:** A double letter spacing mechanism, set by the tabulating mechanism, makes the escapement advance two spaces on one key stroke.

**How it works:** Monarch front-strike machine: spring drum pulls the carriage; a feed rack on pivoted arms meshes with a feed pinion on a shaft carrying a loose escapement wheel with a pawl-and-ratchet housing. The dog rocker carries dog members; at preset points in the carriage travel, the tabulating mechanism sets the dogs so a single stroke lets the carriage advance a distance greater than one letter space. A modification is shown in Fig. 10.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** feed rack; feed pinion; escapement wheel; dog rocker; feed dog; spring drum
