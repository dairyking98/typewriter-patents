## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Automatic cut-off devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for automatically switching off mechanically driven typewriters, Calculating machines and similar machines in which one of the drive device driven device automatically switches off the drive device.

**How it works (reading):** The drive is disconnected inevitably by a member moved by the machine's own parts (such as keys) when the machine is not in use, reset by a periodic control member; a locking member, held by an electromagnet in series with the motor, blocks use when power is interrupted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-driven automatic switch-off of mechanically driven machines is complicated, expensive, and jams easily so the drive is not cut off.

**Solution:** The drive is disconnected inevitably by a member moved by the machine's own parts (such as keys) when the machine is not in use, reset by a periodic control member; a locking member, held by an electromagnet in series with the motor, blocks use when power is interrupted.
