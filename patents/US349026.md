## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Colour change effected automatically

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in an ink-ribbon for type-writing or printing machines, having a portion at or near its end or ends charged with ink of a different color or shade from that of the body of the ribbon, for the purpose of notifying the Operator of the near approach of the end of the ribbon and the necessity of reversing its feed.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon body is charged with ink of one colour and a portion near the end with a contrasting colour, warning the operator of the approach of the end.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The end of a traveling ink ribbon is reached without warning, and the machine can strike at the same point until the ribbon tightens.

**Solution:** The ribbon body is charged with ink of one colour and a portion near the end with a contrasting colour, warning the operator of the approach of the end.
