## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with jumping cross counter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typing and accounting machine with jumping Totalizer or cross counter.

**How it works (reading):** The locking device keeps the clear signal key locked normally and releases it only when the transverse counter has been cleanly written and is back in its return position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The clear-character key on a typewriter accounting machine can be pressed even when the checksum facility was not properly cleared.

**Solution:** The locking device keeps the clear signal key locked normally and releases it only when the transverse counter has been cleanly written and is back in its return position.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter with jumping cross counter and clear character key. The invention relates to a typing and accounting machine with jumping Totalizer or cross counter. In such machines it is known to have a button to print a special character, e.g. B. a star, immediately after writing the total from the cross counter to indicate that the total is correct written off and the checksum made free or brought to zero became, this key being locked each time the checksum facility is not properly made free. The invention consists in that the locking device is designed such that it keeps the clear signal key locked normally and only is moved into the release position when the transverse counter is clearly written and is in the return position.
