## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the present invention is to provide means for allowing the carriage to move varying distances, according to the letters struck, so as to provide a uniform spacing between the adjacent letters instead of a uniform spacing between the centers of the letters.

**How it works (reading):** Add a set of key-driven arms of different lengths on a rocking shaft that vary how far the escapement lets the carriage move per letter.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters space every letter by the same pitch, so wide and narrow letters look cramped or loose.

**Solution:** Add a set of key-driven arms of different lengths on a rocking shaft that vary how far the escapement lets the carriage move per letter.

**How it works:** Each key lever lies over an arm on an oscillating shaft; arm lengths differ so a wide letter such as m or w rocks the shaft further than i. The shaft raises a lever linked to a rocking member on the escapement shaft, so the carriage advance varies with the letter struck. A sliding shaft with a collar is moved sideways by the shift key to bring a second set of arms under the keys for capitals. A spring returns the shaft and arms.
