## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** Widen the slots at the exit point of the arc axis so a burr cannot foul the type lever, and allow smoothing or softer metals such as brass.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type lever bearing slots were left with a burr where the arc axis passes through, so levers could catch and stiffen.

**Solution:** Widen the slots at the exit point of the arc axis so a burr cannot foul the type lever, and allow smoothing or softer metals such as brass.
