## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Type lever linkage for typewriters, their key levers and intermediate levers a slot and pin connection are connected to one another.

**How it works (reading):** The point where motion is transferred is moved into the same plane of oscillation as the intermediate and key levers by a lateral bend on the key lever or intermediate lever, and the bearing wire seats on a sloped wall so no rattle occurs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The linkage from key lever to intermediate lever to type lever sits in two parallel planes, producing a tilting moment, side tension and a rattling noise.

**Solution:** The point where motion is transferred is moved into the same plane of oscillation as the intermediate and key levers by a lateral bend on the key lever or intermediate lever, and the bearing wire seats on a sloped wall so no rattle occurs.

The invention relates to a Type lever linkage for typewriters, their key levers and intermediate levers a slot and pin connection are connected to one another. With type lever linkages of this type, an annoying message arises when writing Noise when, as is the case with known designs, the key lever or the lever connected to the stop button and the intermediate lever moved by it of the type linkage lie in two mutually parallel planes. This deficiency is avoided according to the invention in that the Point of application of the transmission of motion in the same plane of vibration with the intermediate lever and the Key lever or the one moved by the pressed key Lever is misplaced.
