## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A perforated-tape controller for key-operated machines needs tape tension, exact registration of each letter's holes with the die-plate, and reverse feed.

**Solution:** Friction tension device, spring-pressed contact lever, a rotating shuttle feeding the tape a sub-division at a time, and releasable pawls for reverse feed.

**How it works:** A paper tape passes over a die-plate carried by a cam-reciprocated carriage; holes registering with the plate move slotted combs, aligning slots so a cross-bar advances and throws a hook into the path of a universal bar, which operates the typewriter key through a strap. A channeled friction plate and a spring-lever plate keep tension, and a curved spring lever keeps the tape taut. A shuttle arm on the sprocket axis underruns the tape to advance it one feed-hole spacing per turn, so a letter's group can be registered. Pawls can be thrown out to reverse the feed.
