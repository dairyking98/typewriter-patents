## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Machine for printing and outputting different numbers of driving or tickets with different prices. The invention relates to a machine for printing and issuing a different number of travel or entry tickets with different prices, especially the facilities for advancing and ejecting the cards. The innovation consists primarily in the fact that the feed or Counter-pressure rollers for the paper strips lying next to one another on one another offset eccentrics of the same shaft are arranged, so that by rotation of this Shaft a feed roller in working position and at the same time another out of working position is brought. The wave is controlled by in its various positions Locking devices held. The paper strips can be used independently of the feed rollers by means of a device influenced by the keys in their longitudinal direction or moved back so that only the strip to be put into action enters the Area of the feed rollers and the cutter is brought while the other strips are withdrawn from their area.
