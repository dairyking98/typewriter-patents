## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines havin a tabulating mechanism or attachment.

**How it works (reading):** A scale bar with denomination markings for tabulating with commas and without commas is clipped to the machine at the tabulating keys.
