## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage return ratchet and its pawl arm are hard to align and rebalance when either part is removed and refitted.

**Solution:** The pawl rocker is carried on a main holder, and the ratchet wheel shaft with its feed wheel and reverse gear sits on a detachable holder fixed to it, so the whole switching unit can be removed and set precisely outside the machine.
