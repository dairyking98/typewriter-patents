## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting ma chines and has for its principal purpose to provide an improved tabulator for such inachines.

**How it works (reading):** A column stop bar connected to the paper carriage through an arm, with stops that can be set for columns, and a locking and releasing device that holds or frees the mechanism in normal position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The existing tabulator arrangement needs an improved tabulating mechanism with column stops that can be adjusted to match the columns.

**Solution:** A column stop bar connected to the paper carriage through an arm, with stops that can be set for columns, and a locking and releasing device that holds or frees the mechanism in normal position.
