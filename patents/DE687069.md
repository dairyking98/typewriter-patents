> Description Translated from German Column stop for tab devices on typewriters etc. Like. The invention relates to a column stop for tab devices on typewriters o. the like .. and forms an addition to patent 63 140 'z. In the main patent are between a pair at a distance from each other arranged racks, which are opposite on their facing surfaces Have incisions spaced from one another, a number of column stops provided, their upper arms and lower legs extending from a horizontal web can be pivoted in the transverse direction in the incisions of the racks. The arms and legs of these column stops are dimensioned in such a way that that the stops from a front inoperative position in a rear operating position and vice versa can be pivoted. Resilient means in the form of wire springs are provided at each stop to allow them through in each of their extreme positions To hold on to friction. If a stop of this type breaks, for example as a result of an excessive impact of the stop against 'the stop lever, is it required to first remove an end plate and then the stops until one arrives at the broken stop.
