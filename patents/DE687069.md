## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Broken column stops on a tab rack are hard to replace because they must all be removed first.

**Solution:** Each column stop is removable individually, with a locking plate that limits stop movement so it can be taken out only after the plate is removed.
