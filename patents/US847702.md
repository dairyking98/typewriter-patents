## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar hangers on a vertical arc needed a secure, easily mounted fixing, and the carriage-release/escapement trip needed a universal bar suited to typebars whose end fulcrums sit nearer the carriage track.

**Solution:** Clamped, tongue-and-groove typebar brackets on a slotted bridge, with a curved sliding universal bar that is stepped at its ends, is struck by the typebar heels and trips the carriage escapement.

**How it works:** Each typebar bracket is made of three plates whose center plate forms a tongue fitting a radial groove in the bridge and is clamped by a screw. Brackets toward each end of the arc are stepped or shortened so their fulcrums sit nearer the carriage-track plane. A universal bar curved concentrically with the fulcrum arc slides back and forth in the bridge; the heel of each typebar hits it at the end of the stroke, and its stepped front edge matches the stepped brackets. A tappet on the universal bar trips a rocking plate carrying two escapement dogs that engage a gear on the carriage pinion for step-by-step feed. A spring restores the plate and a dust guard on the bar covers the pivots. The space-key rock-shaft also drives the universal bar.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** typebar; typebar bracket; bridge; universal bar; tappet; rocking plate; escapement dog; carriage rack; pinion; space-key lever; rock-shaft; dust guard; clamp screw

**Numbers:** six brackets at each end of the arc stepped
