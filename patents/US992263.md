## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to tabulating mechanism for typewriting machmes and the main object of said invention is'to provide an improved and simple column selecting mechanism which enables the traveling element or carriage to be released and moved to a predetermined one of a plurality of columnar positions, any intervening columnar positions being skipped or passed.

**How it works (reading):** Column-selecting levers set a variably movable frame stop so the released carriage stops at a predetermined column stop on the stop bar.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column tabulators needed a simple selector that releases the carriage and skips intervening columns to a chosen one.

**Solution:** Column-selecting levers set a variably movable frame stop so the released carriage stops at a predetermined column stop on the stop bar.

**How it works:** On a Yost visible front-strike machine, a spring drum pulls the carriage leftward against a feed rack that meshes with an escapement pinion. Column selecting levers move a part that positions a variable frame stop. The carriage is released and runs until a column stop on the stop bar meets that stop, passing other columns.
