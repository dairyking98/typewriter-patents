## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be guided and held to the platen but also fully released when wanted.

**Solution:** Spring-pressed front and rear rollers on pivoted carriers are all thrown off the platen by a single sliding cam bar.

**How it works:** A front bar on the platen frame carries adjustable sleeves with brackets for curved paper guides, each with a spring-pressed roller. Rear feed rollers sit on loose carriers that can swing at an angle to adapt to platen unevenness. A longitudinal bar beneath the platen has cam portions; sliding it by a crank and handle swings both front and rear roller carriers away from the platen. A knob lever also swings the front bar and its guides clear.
