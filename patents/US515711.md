## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line spacing and carriage return were done by separate keys on machines with an automatic retrograde carriage.

**Solution:** One finger key trips the carriage escapement pawls and simultaneously operates the line-space pawl.

**How it works:** The carriage is propelled by a ratchet and primary pawl moved by the print keys and is drawn back by a spring drum and cord. A finger key turns a rock-shaft whose arm lifts a secondary pawl and throws the primary pawl out, releasing the carriage. A second arm on the same shaft rocks a lever that presses a rocking bar on the carriage, driving the pawl on the platen ratchet to turn the roller one line. Details permit increasing or reducing the spacing movement.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** spring drum; carriage ratchet; primary pawl; secondary pawl; rock-shaft; finger key; platen ratchet; line-space pawl; rocking bar
