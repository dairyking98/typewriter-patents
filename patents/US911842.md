## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** About 35-40% of strokes are a letter followed by a vowel/y or the word-ending space, so typing needs many strokes.

**Solution:** Add extra typebars striking one space right of the normal ones, on extra vowel and space keys struck together with a normal key, with differential spacing advancing two spaces.

**How it works:** An extra set of typebars sits at the right of the basket, hung to strike the platen one space right of the regular bars. Extra keys (a e i o u y and space) at both ends of the keyboard are joined by pivoted rods so either end operates the same bar. Pressing a normal key and an extra key together prints two letters, or a final letter plus space. A differential spacing mechanism with a ratchet bar and a slotted plate with dogs advances the carriage one space for a normal key alone and two with an extra key.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** extra typebar; extra key; connecting rod; ratchet bar; slotted plate; dog; cross bar
