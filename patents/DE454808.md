## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (amount type setting cash register)

**How it works (reading):** A special setting member couples the type wheels to amount setters only while the machine gear is locked; after setting it decouples, releases the gear and locks the setters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Amount type setting in rotating-cylinder cash registers was unreliable and costly when set through the printing cylinder.

**Solution:** A special setting member couples the type wheels to amount setters only while the machine gear is locked; after setting it decouples, releases the gear and locks the setters.

**Features:** [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
