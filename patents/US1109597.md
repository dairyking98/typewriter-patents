## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Repetitive letters are typed by hand; an operator is needed for each copy.

**Solution:** A pneumatic player-piano style actuator driven by perforated master sheets strikes the keys of a standard key-lever typewriter.

**How it works:** Perforated paper master-letters pass over a tracker board and valve air into primary, secondary and power pneumatics. Power bellows drive vertically reciprocating rods that strike the typewriter keys like fingers. Other pneumatics work shift and the space lever. A second master sheet takes over for inserts such as addresses and the carriage is located automatically for line feed and first stroke.

**Parts:** perforated master sheet; tracker board; primary pneumatic; secondary pneumatic; power bellows; hammer rod; spring carriage

**Numbers:** 40 to 44 character keys; 7 sheets of drawings
