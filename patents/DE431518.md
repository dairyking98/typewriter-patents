## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting a platen between three positions on a three-type typebar needs a simple and certain mechanism.

**Solution:** A swinging shift frame held locked in the middle position, freed by either shift key, with a spring returning it to centre.

**How it works:** Each typebar carries three types striking the platen in upper, middle or lower position. The carriage runs on rails of a shift frame hung from the main frame by connecting rods and guided by arms on an oscillating shaft. One shift key acts directly on the drive arm to raise the frame, the other through a spring-loaded intermediate lever that multiplies the movement to lower it. Pressing either key first releases a lock that holds the frame in centre, and stops limit both end positions.
