## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator is not told when the ribbon has unwound from one spool and the feed must be reversed.

**Solution:** A spring lever on each ribbon spool, compressed by the wound ribbon, releases when the ribbon runs off to ring a bell or throw the reversing lever.

**How it works:** Each spool carries a loop spring on its side that presses on a lever lying along the barrel in a recess. The lever is held toward the axis by the ribbon wound on the spool, storing energy in the spring. When the ribbon is nearly drawn off, the lever is released and the spring throws a hammer against a bell on the spool, or moves the ribbon reversing lever instead. A secondary device sounds the alarm if the ribbon releases too gradually.

> Description (No Model.) n. E. FBL T. INK RIBBON MECHANISM. No. 578,919. Patented Mar. 16,1897. UNITED STATES PATENT OFFICE. DORR E. FELT, OF CHICAGO, ILLINOIS. INK-RIBBON MECHANISM. SPEOIFIGATION forming part of Letters Patent No. 578,919, dated March 16, 1897. Application filed April 3, 1896. Serial No. 586,049. (No model.) To aZZ whom it may concern.- Be it known that I, DORR E. FELT, a citizen of the United States, residing in Chicago, in the county of Cook and State of Illinois, have invented a new and useful Improvement in Ink-Ribbon Mechanism, of which the following is a specification. This invention relates to the ink-ribbon mechanism of type writers and other machines employing ink-carrying ribbons. My main object in the invention has been to provide the spools with means whereby when the ribbon has been drawn off from either spool either an alarm will be sounded adapted to give notice to the operator that he should reverse the ribbon-feed or the reversing-lever which controls the ribbon-feed will itself be automatically shifted so as to reverse the feed. To this end I provide upon each of the spools a spring device adapted to be compressed by the ribbon when it is wound up…
