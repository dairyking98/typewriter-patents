## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line-setting device adjuster reset) · **Subject (patent classes):** Syllable, line, or like type selection

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the arranged on parts displaceable in its longitudinal direction To delete adjusters of line-setting devices in a convenient manner, the invention consists first of all in that a device is provided which individually returns the adjuster to their rest position.

**How it works (reading):** A rod moving through the machine resets each adjuster one by one, and holders are locked against movement, with a latch securing the general erase.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjusters on line-setting devices of letterpress typewriters are hard to return individually to rest.

**Solution:** A rod moving through the machine resets each adjuster one by one, and holders are locked against movement, with a latch securing the general erase.
