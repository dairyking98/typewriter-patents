## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The underside of the platen and carriage, and the mechanism below, are hard to reach for inspection, cleaning and repair in a front-strike machine.

**Solution:** The carriage frame is hinged to the main framework and tilts up, carrying with it the carriage spring barrel and the letter-feed escapement dogs.

**How it works:** The paper carriage runs on a frame hinged to the main frame so frame and carriage swing up, exposing the parts below. The carriage-driving spring barrel and the dogs that engage the carriage rack are mounted on the hinged frame and swing up with it. When folded down, the key action still operates the dogs, so letter feeding works. Type bars pivot on a curved fulcrum rod in a slotted segment and strike the front of the platen.

**Features:** [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** hinged carriage frame; spring barrel; escapement dog; carriage rack; type bar; fulcrum rod; segment; platen; key lever; carriage guiding bar

**Improves on:**

- Stickney applications 153,024 and 481,022: this division claims the escapement and tension mounted on the tilting frame
