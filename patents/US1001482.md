## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructional details or processes of manufacture of the input device

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a new and improved attachment for typewriters whereby the operator is aided in operating the typewriter by the touch system, both in learning and subsequently, if desired.

**How it works (reading):** A frame set over the keyboard divides the key rows into aisles with partitions, and sub-divides them into tiers with stops, to show finger positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Touch typists need guidance to keep hands on the right keys, which are arranged in rows.

**Solution:** A frame set over the keyboard divides the key rows into aisles with partitions, and sub-divides them into tiers with stops, to show finger positions.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** frame; partitions; aisles; stops; tiers
