## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The loose pawl of the paper carriage switching gear does not reliably stay in its intended rest position.

**Solution:** A helical spring holds the loose pawl against a stop on a swing-out lever. The lever is swung out only by the gear shift rack on the paper carriage, so the pawl rests in a constant position independent of the return spring tension.

**How it works:** The rack on the paper carriage swings out the lever at each step; the spring keeps the loose pawl against the lever stop in its rest position.
