## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for the noiseless return of the Paper car from typewriters, in which the carriage wheel and the escape wheel of the Paper carriage switching device are connected by a crown gear, whose Teeth are held in engagement with each other by a spring.

**How it works (reading):** A crown-gear coupling is held engaged by a spring; balls in pits in one coupling half run up inclined ramps in the other half when the carriage returns, pushing the halves apart so the return is quiet and the carriage runs free.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing devices that disconnect the carriage wheel from the escape wheel on return are unreliable, slow, collect dirt, and take too much space.

**Solution:** A crown-gear coupling is held engaged by a spring; balls in pits in one coupling half run up inclined ramps in the other half when the carriage returns, pushing the halves apart so the return is quiet and the carriage runs free.
