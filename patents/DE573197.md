## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for the noiseless return of the paper cart from typewriters The invention relates to a device for the noiseless return of the Paper car from typewriters, in which the carriage wheel and the escape wheel of the Paper carriage switching device are connected by a crown gear, whose Teeth are held in engagement with each other by a spring. With the known Devices of this type is the device for automatic disconnection and coupling of the two wheels unreliable and sluggish in their effect, their servants Aids are exposed to soiling and difficult to clean, and the overall design takes up more space than is available on many types of typewriters stands. These disadvantages are eliminated according to the invention in that in one half of the coupling roller bodies are mounted, which when moving back of the Paper carriage run up on inclined surfaces in the other coupling half and thereby cause both coupling halves to separate. This achieves that the device always works quickly and safely and has such a compact design it is possible that the device can also be built into existing typewriters can turn.
