## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** After a line is typed the platen carriage must return and the paper must line-feed automatically, for fixed-form printing and telegraph receivers.

**Solution:** At the end of travel a base slider swings the sideways-feed rack out of mesh, letting the stored spring force return the carriage, and the rack swing turns the platen one line.

**How it works:** The carriage stores return force as it feeds. A rack on a swing arm is spring-pressed onto a feed gear on the base, which is turned a set angle per printing stroke by a link, clutch teeth and spring. At the end of the line a projection on the rack meets a slider that moves once per print stroke and pushes the rack out of mesh. A hook pawl catches a pin on the arm and holds it, so the carriage runs home. The arm's cross shaft turns a sector gear and clutch on the platen shaft to give a line feed. At home a control piece frees the hook pawl and the rack remeshes.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Automatic platen feed device in a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. The invention relates to an automatic platen feed device in a typewriter, characterised as follows. A sideways-feed rack, carried so that it can swing forward and back on the platen carriage frame (in which a return force is stored as the frame feeds sideways), is sprung towards a sideways-feed gear on the machine base so as to mesh with it. On the rack are provided a projecting piece, standing out from the row of teeth at the end of the rack in its feed direction, and a pin answering to a hook pawl that is pivoted under spring on the frame and is swung by a suitable control piece at the frame's starting position. On the base a slider is mounted that makes one to-and-fro movement forward and back for each printing stroke. On the platen shaft a vertical-feed device is fitted that turns the platen a set distance in a set direction as the rack swings.
