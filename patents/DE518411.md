## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The stop for the release button is made adjustable or pushed back, so the trip bar clears the lifting roller and the carriage can be lifted out.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage cannot be lifted out past the release rail because the trip bar of the release slide catches the lifting roller.

**Solution:** The stop for the release button is made adjustable or pushed back, so the trip bar clears the lifting roller and the carriage can be lifted out.
