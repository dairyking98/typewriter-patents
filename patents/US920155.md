## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Billing invoices need their numbers registered as they are typed, and the sheet should be released from paper feed control by the same operation.

**Solution:** A counting or registering device on a typewriter, with a bracket and guide roller, counts the invoice numbers and releases the paper from the feed control in one operation.
