## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets are held with a flexible strip that needs special recessed carbon sheets, and writing sheets cannot be held the same way.

**Solution:** Holes in the pad and cover strip above the stop let office needles clamp both writing and carbon sheets to the base in correct position.
