## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be guided around the platen during insertion, and a fixed guide blocks the platen path.

**Solution:** A perforated block on the carriage rod carries a curved bar finger that slides in a concentric slot, spring-retracted and extended by an arm.

**How it works:** Spring-retracted guide finger slides in a curved slot to guide paper around the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
