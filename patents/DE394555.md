## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (stepladder) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper; Carbon gloves; Ladders capable of standing by themselves with hinged struts which rest on the ground

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to collapsible Step ladders with hinged collapsible ones in the middle Steps that are attached to the bars by struts.

**How it works (reading):** Each side rail is made of two telescoping bars, and every step is held by two pairs of struts with sliding ends, so the ladder folds small and locks rigid when open.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Folding stepladders are wobbly when open and take up too much room when folded.

**Solution:** Each side rail is made of two telescoping bars, and every step is held by two pairs of struts with sliding ends, so the ladder folds small and locks rigid when open.
