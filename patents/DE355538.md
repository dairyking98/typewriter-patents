## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening devices embodied in machines

**How it works (reading):** Rubber plates, tubes or hoses between the auxiliary key and the main key absorb sound vibration.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Auxiliary keys transmit sound through the metal of the key plungers to the outside of the machine.

**Solution:** Rubber plates, tubes or hoses between the auxiliary key and the main key absorb sound vibration.
