## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A typewriter carriage stands at full height when not in use, so the machine is bulky to carry.

**Solution:** A separable support lets the carriage and platen shift rearward into compact relation to the frame when not in use, lowering the machine height.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; separable support; frame
