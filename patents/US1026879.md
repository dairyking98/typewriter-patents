**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewritin machines and more particularly to tabu ating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A key operates the resetting mechanism and releases the carriage at the same time, so the stops reset at full carriage power.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Previously set tabulator stops are hard to clear and reset quickly when new stops are needed.

**Solution:** A key operates the resetting mechanism and releases the carriage at the same time, so the stops reset at full carriage power.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
