## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are complicated and expensive; a simple cheap machine is wanted.

**Solution:** A horizontal type wheel whose spring arms each end in a key with the type beneath and the index letter on top, turned by hand over a notch and pressed down to print and space.

**How it works:** The hub of the wheel has radial resilient arms ending in keys, with metal or rubber type underneath and the matching letter on top, all at equal radius. The operator turns the wheel until the desired key is over a notch in a curved guide rail and presses it down, printing on paper held in a slotted holder on a dovetail slide. Pressing the arm also depresses a lever whose sliding pawl moves the paper holder by one ratchet tooth, so the next type meets clean paper. Releasing returns arm and lever by spring. Words are separated by lifting the pawl by its hook and sliding the holder. Types are inked by an ink pad in the rear of the frame, or optionally a roller. Rigid hinged arms with underneath springs are an alternative.
