## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to carriage es'capement mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A rapid, quiet escapement with an adjustable roller holding dog whose action is timed to the release of the stepping dog.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapement is noisy and needs adjusting to regulate operation.

**Solution:** A rapid, quiet escapement with an adjustable roller holding dog whose action is timed to the release of the stepping dog.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
