## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register serial numbering)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register in which the indexing of the printing device for the consecutive number can be switched on and off by means of a hand lever, but can also be prevented by pressing a special key when the hand lever is set to forward, by the hand lever driving the printing unit .

**How it works (reading):** A hand lever switches the serial printing on and off, and a special key moves a shift lever into lock position, blocking the lifting disc.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Serial-number printing on cash-register strips must be switchable and lockable.

**Solution:** A hand lever switches the serial printing on and off, and a special key moves a shift lever into lock position, blocking the lifting disc.
