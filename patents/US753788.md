## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and has special reference to that class of machines Commonly known as book typewriters, and comprising means for printing upon the pages of books or other surfaces held in a flat or spread-out condition.

**How it works (reading):** A compact book typewriter whose printing mechanism travels lengthwise and transversely over the page on a vertically adjustable, shiftable frame.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriters were bulky and heavy, with many parts and a high keyboard; the printing mechanism had to clear the book to turn pages.

**Solution:** A compact book typewriter whose printing mechanism travels lengthwise and transversely over the page on a vertically adjustable, shiftable frame.

**How it works:** A main supporting frame carries track rails for the traveling printing mechanism. The frame can be adjusted in height for the thickness of the book and lifted to clear the work. It can be shifted over either page of the book or moved wholly outside the printing zone so pages can be turned. Printing advances lengthwise and transversely of the work to give line and letter spacing. The aim is a very low keyboard dip, few operating parts, small size and light weight.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** main supporting frame; track rail; printing mechanism; keyboard

**Numbers:** 17 sheets of drawings

**Improves on:**

- earlier book typewriters: smaller, lighter, fewer parts, lower keyboard
