## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the means located at or forming a part of the upper ends of the arms 1 and acting to hold and rearwardly deflect a card or sheet being printed on.

**How it works (reading):** Arms in front of the platen hold a card or sheet against the platen, and an adjustable scale on the arms keeps its edge parallel with the print line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards and sheets are not controlled at the printing surface, and the scale must stay aligned on shifting carriages.

**Solution:** Arms in front of the platen hold a card or sheet against the platen, and an adjustable scale on the arms keeps its edge parallel with the print line.
