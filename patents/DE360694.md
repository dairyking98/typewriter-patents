## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cheap, simple typewriter with visible writing and accurate line alignment is wanted, without a full keyboard.

**Solution:** A flexible type strip on a swing frame is slid by a hand pointer over a staggered character board to bring the chosen type opposite a pressure arm, which presses it to the paper.

**How it works:** A flexible strip carries two rows of types (upper and lower case) on a holder rod that can slide sideways and swing on a frame. The operator moves a pointer, joined by a bracket to the holder rod, over an adjustment board whose characters are staggered in four rows so that no two share a vertical line. A row of points on the pointer shows which type is in position. A notched strip behind the type strip is engaged by the rounded rear edge of a pressure arm, which locates the chosen type exactly, then presses it against the paper on the platen. The holder is withdrawn after each impression so writing is visible. An ink-pad plate and a spring protective plate sit between strip and paper; the plate is cut out so only the selected type reaches the paper. Pressing the lever also advances the paper carriage by one letter through a sliding pawl on a rack; a word-space lever advances it further.
