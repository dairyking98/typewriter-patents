## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to` those parts of a typewriting machine, involved,in support ing thesheet ef paper to be written upon and for giving line .

**How it works (reading):** Paper supports and actuating devices for the platen give line-space and letter-space movements of the sheet, as part of a larger machine covered by earlier applications.
