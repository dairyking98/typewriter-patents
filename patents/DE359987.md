**How it works (reading):** A channel with obliquely converging side walls, fixed above and behind the paper roller on the paper guide plate, holds stacked sheets so their lower edges align before insertion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Loose sheets fed into a typewriter do not line up their edges, so text is not square to the sheet.

**Solution:** A channel with obliquely converging side walls, fixed above and behind the paper roller on the paper guide plate, holds stacked sheets so their lower edges align before insertion.
