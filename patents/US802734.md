## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pawl-fed carriages need the holding pawl and feed pawl thrown out to return the carriage, which is awkward especially in power-driven machines; the carriage should be free to move either way except while the feed is operating.

**Solution:** A carriage feeding mechanism that leaves the carriage free in both directions except while the advancing mechanism acts, shown on a power-driven Blickensderfer type-wheel machine.

**How it works:** A constantly running power shaft drives a gear on a short shaft; a toothed-plate clutch engages a cam driver for each printing operation. A pivoted controller lever with a cam separates or lets spring-press the clutch plates, and is operated by a spring-ended slide-bar with a hooked end that disengages as soon as the clutch engages. The type mechanism is normally stationary and is clutched in for each print. The carriage feeding gear and the type-wheel return are shown in later figures that are cut from the opening.

**Features:** [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)

**Parts:** power shaft; driving gear; toothed clutch plate; cam driver; controller lever; slide-bar; type wheel; feeding pawl; holding pawl; carriage

**Improves on:**

- pawl feed with holding pawl: carriage stays free in both directions with no need to throw pawls out
