## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dirt enters the pivot of the type bar and its guide slot in the support, causing sticking.

**Solution:** A type-bar support and type bar shaped so the bar acts as a wiper or ejector, clearing foreign matter from the guiding slot and keeping it out of the pivot.
