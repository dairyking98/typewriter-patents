## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machines where the paper roller is pressed against types on an endless rubber band need a simple way to advance the paper carriage without intermediate rods.

**Solution:** The paper sledge's forward and backward stroke itself advances the roller carrier one hole: perforated walls on the roller carrier engage fixed tapered pins of different strength.

**How it works:** A U-shaped paper sledge slides on guide rods and is pushed by the key lever against a rotatable type disk, then returned by a spring. Cross guides in it carry the roller carrier, whose long walls have equally spaced holes. Fixed wedge pins on the frame engage them: pushing the sledge forward shifts the carrier sideways by one hole width, while the weaker rear pin lets it slide back only the web between holes, so the next hole is presented to the pin each stroke.
