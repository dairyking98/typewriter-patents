## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The spaced-member escapement needs key-levers that work with light finger pressure while giving a quick but steady feed.

**Solution:** A swinging-rack escapement where the rack is pivoted on the carriage and dogs control carriage movement, giving a steady feed under light key pressure.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
