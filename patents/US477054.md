## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A single-keyboard typewriter needs a separate shift manipulation to change from small letters to capitals, interrupting typing.

**Solution:** Each key has two independent movements: pressed on top it prints the small letter, pressed on its front face it slides forward and prints the capital.

**How it works:** The key cap rides on a dovetail rib along the key lever. A downward strike moves the lever for the small letter. A forward push moves a rod, via a pivoted lever, to the capital's typebar rod, leaving the small-letter levers untouched, and a stud on the rod trips the spacing bar lever. A spring returns the rod after release.

**Parts:** key cap; dovetail groove; key lever; key rod; pivot lever; typebar actuating rod; spring; spacing bar lever; cam
