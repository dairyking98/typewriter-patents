## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Manuscripts and shorthand copy must be held and advanced line by line on a typewriter or desk, and paper advance is hard to control by hand.

**Solution:** A roller loosely mounted in a slotted sleeve; the manuscript is fed through the slot and advanced by turning the roller with grip wheels, with the front edge of the case setting the line to be copied.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE. The present invention relates to a holder for manuscripts, concepts or shorthands and so on, which is used on typewriters as well as on the desk can be. It consists of a roller a, which is mounted loosely in a slotted sleeve c. The manuscript, etc. is inserted through the slot opening between the roller and the sleeve and moved by turning the roller in the sleeve, as indicated in the drawing by the arrow. The front edge of the case delimits the line of the manuscript to be reproduced. Because the roller α causing the advance of the paper is loosely rotatably mounted in the sleeve, it is possible to easily control the advance of the same when the roller is rotated, both by unequal pressure on the ends of the roller a, which are provided with grip wheels d, as well as If there is enough leeway in path b , it can be regulated by occasional, arbitrary straightening with the hands, even if the paper is wound and unwound on the roller instead of letting it emerge from the slot opening of the sleeve while moving.
