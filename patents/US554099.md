## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Screws securing typebar hangers loosen and need a secure, firm washer.

**Solution:** A washer with raised jaws that lock the neighbouring washer's screw so adjacent screws hold each other from turning.

**How it works:** Each washer has a bearing surface with a screw hole and two jaws projecting from it, bent up at the ends. Jaws of one washer lie over the next washer so its screw sits between them, preventing rotation. The last hanger uses a washer with a raised portion that fits between the preceding jaws. A variant uses opposing projections.
