## Summary

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a device for reversing the movement of the ink ribbon without any outside touching of the ribbon, which gives a clean and precise changeover and starts at the moment when the ribbon has completely run out of the supply reel.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal triggered by feeler on the roll or by ribbon tension fires too early or too late, wasting ribbon or tearing it.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded radial segment in each ribbon spool core springs outward when the ribbon is fully unwound and its tooth then trips the reversing mechanism, with no touching of the ribbon.

**How it works:** The sleeve of each ribbon spool contains a radially movable segment held inward by the wound ribbon. When the ribbon has run out, the spring pushes the segment outward and its axially projecting tooth, on the flange on the drive side, strikes an organ as the spool rotates. That moves a reversing mechanism and pull rod that changes the drive direction of the ribbon. Shown for cash registers and accounting machines.
