## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typing music needs the paper moved vertically to the height of each note, independently of normal letter and line movement, with exact placement.

**Solution:** A hand-moved rack with a pointer on a staff scale turns a toothed cylinder as long as the platen, which turns a gear on the platen axle to set the platen angle.

**How it works:** A gear on the platen axle always meshes with a toothed cylinder as long as the platen, so it engages wherever the platen has slid sideways. A second gear on the cylinder is joined by a detachable clutch to a pinion on a longitudinally sliding rack. The operator pulls a grip on the rack, and a pointer moves over a double staff scale (notes between lines on one half, on lines on the other). The rack turns the cylinder, which turns the platen to the note height. A lever disengages the clutch so ordinary text can be typed and the paper can be fed normally. Intended for machines with an exchangeable type sector or type wheel, though typebars also work.
