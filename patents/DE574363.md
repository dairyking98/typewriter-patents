## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**How it works (reading):** Several thin type wheels on one axis, each carrying a few types, float on elastic rings and a shaped punch pushes only the selected wheel against the print surface.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Striking a multi-ring type wheel against a flat or cylindrical surface also presses the neighbouring types onto it and smears the print; hollow type drums with radially sliding types are costly and heavy.

**Solution:** Several thin type wheels on one axis, each carrying a few types, float on elastic rings and a shaped punch pushes only the selected wheel against the print surface.

**How it works:** Wheels b1, b2, b3 sit on axis a with a bore larger than the axle, the gap filled with elastic rubber rings or steel springs, and are separated by discs so they move only perpendicular to the axis. The axis is turned and shifted so the chosen type of one wheel is at the print position. Punch t on the side opposite the print plate p has a shaped lower edge and side noses that catch only that wheel and hold it from lateral escape. Projections v of the punch enter a recess v1 in the wheel for each type, locking it, and further punch travel compresses the elastic ring so the wheel presses onto plate or film f alone.
