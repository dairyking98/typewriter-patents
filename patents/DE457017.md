## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Holder combining several needles for the production of Braille. The invention relates to a holder with needles, which is used for the production of Braille serves. This has six buttons that are pressed individually or together (see Fig. z). The buttons a are with needles. connected and quick after perforation of the paper back again due to its spring force. The holder has a handplate b and is expedient in a known manner by means of clamps, leather or rubber bands attached to the hand and drawn over the paper lying on a felt plate. The holder can be used with or without an impeller d. The impeller can be round or be angular. If angular, the length of each edge is equal to the spacing of one Letter from the other. It is best to use fluted springs, but also spiral springs used. Fig. Z illustrates a mainspring, which is also designed as a needle e and is connected to the a key. The needles with their guides can be put in this way that when all needles are pressed down together the ones shown in Fig. 3 and 4 Groups of holes arise.
