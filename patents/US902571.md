## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to line spacing mechanisms and one object of my invention is to provide simple and efficient means of the character specified.

**How it works (reading):** A line-spacing hand lever at one end of the platen is linked by a rocking shaft and connecting rod to line-spacing devices at the other end, so one movement of the lever moves the carriage to the right and also line-spaces the platen; the lever can also lift the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing controls were placed inconveniently, with the operator needing to reach past the carriage to advance the platen by hand.

**Solution:** A line-spacing hand lever at one end of the platen is linked by a rocking shaft and connecting rod to line-spacing devices at the other end, so one movement of the lever moves the carriage to the right and also line-spaces the platen; the lever can also lift the carriage.
