## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter with a cylindrical platen cannot print on thick cards with thin type-receiving surfaces without bending them.

**Solution:** An attachment that feeds and ejects cards in one hand movement so an ordinary typewriter can address cards.

**How it works:** Card feed and eject attachment on an ordinary typewriter carriage, using the usual platen.

**Features:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
