## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting and adjusting a thick pad of sheets around the platen is awkward and needs care in positioning the platen.

**Solution:** The platen is hinged eccentrically to its axle, latched to the axle in normal use, and a lever releases the latches and swings the platen up and away to open space for the pad.

**How it works:** The platen is carried on arms fixed to the axle through eccentric hinge pivots, with latch teeth engaging keeper notches remote from the axle. Pressing the pressure-roll release finger piece casts off the rolls and moves an abutment into the path of a stop on the platen. A lifting lever then releases the latches and a toggle dog swings the platen up, locking it past dead centre. Returning the lever lets the platen fall back by gravity and the latch spring snaps it home.

**Features:**

- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen axle; hinge arm; latch; keeper; pressure roll; finger piece; lifting lever; dog; abutment
