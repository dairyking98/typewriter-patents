## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a novel billingplaten for type-writing machines of the class exemplified in Patent No.

**How it works (reading):** A platen with a follower-plate or bill housing that holds the sheet and moves in line-spacing as a gauge to position each bill.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing on a flat platen wastes sales-sheet space and bills need precise positioning.

**Solution:** A platen with a follower-plate or bill housing that holds the sheet and moves in line-spacing as a gauge to position each bill.
