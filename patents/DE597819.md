## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator operations had to be repeated by hand for each strip of embossed material.

**Solution:** One press of a special key repeats a group of operations automatically until stopped, and the tabulator returns to start when the cut strip moves back.
