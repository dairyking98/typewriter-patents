## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a new and useful improvement in automatic type-writing machines, and has for its object to so construct a type-writer and organize its interdependent mechanisms as to cause their operations to be brought about automatically and by a power outside of the operator, it being only required that said operator lightly manipulate the keys to set the mechanisms in operation.
