## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A calculating machine's paper carriage moved by levers and many pawls and springs wears and goes out of order; the hand crank must be turned at the right moment.

**Solution:** A continuously rotating crank drives the paper carriage back and forth through a crank arm, so each carriage move is smooth, with ratchet and pawl drive for column positioning.

**How it works:** A continuously rotating axle with a crank drives a link to the carriage; pawls on ratchet wheels produce intermittent half-revolutions, and a shield can disengage the drive.
