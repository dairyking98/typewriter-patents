## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reverses add load when they are thrown by the ribbon.

**Solution:** A ribbon-tension-sensitive device reverses the ribbon shifter on the next key stroke, so the stroke supplies the reversing force.

**How it works:** Two bell-crank levers fulcrumed on the frame have their long arms across the ribbon path beside the spools. When the ribbon goes taut it moves a lever, which via links moves a lever arm that obstructs a part driven by the key stroke (universal bar rocker shaft). The next key stroke then moves the slide bar and shifts the ribbon feed over to the other spool.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** bell-crank lever; ribbon drum; link; lever arm; slide bar; retaining pawl; universal bar rocker shaft

**Improves on:**

- earlier automatic ribbon reverses: the ribbon only triggers; the key stroke does the work
