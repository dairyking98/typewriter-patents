## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting paper is hard to regulate: the leading edge and side edge of the sheet are not consistently positioned, and the feed roll and paper-stop stay against the platen.

**Solution:** A paper-stop for the leading edge cooperates with the main feed roll, a side gage positions the sheet laterally, and a mechanism separates the feed roll and paper-stop from the platen during insertion.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** platen 1; side bars 3 and 4; main feed roll; paper-stop; side gage
