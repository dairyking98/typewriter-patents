**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens; Tape or page guiding or feeding devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a counter bearing for the label receiving Paper strips from typewriters or telegraph machines.

**How it works (reading):** A swash plate engages a groove in a disc on the roller so that one motion both rotates the roller and shifts it axially.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The counter roller behind the paper strip wears unevenly and the old drives for its rotation and sideways shift are complex and imprecise.

**Solution:** A swash plate engages a groove in a disc on the roller so that one motion both rotates the roller and shifts it axially.
