## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single-key typewriter should be simple, durable and work perfectly.

**Solution:** A hand-turned sleeve carrying a type disk with flexible type fingers on an inclined drum, with a printing lever whose hammer strikes the chosen finger, and a shift lever that moves a dial to choose capital or lower case.

**How it works:** An inclined drum fixed on the base carries a dial with a notched flange that guides the printing lever so it descends accurately at each character. A sleeve in the dial carries at its lower end a type disk with flexible type fingers, with a handle-ended printing lever pivoted on a lugged arm. Depressing the lever drives a shaft and a hammer, guided by a vertical groove in the drum, onto the selected finger. A three-armed shift lever with a locking pin moves the dial right or left so the hammer hits a capital finger or the adjacent lower case finger. The shaft motion also tilts an elbow lever that moves a pawl one notch along the carriage rack, spacing it, and a rubber platen roll with thumb piece and ratchet can be turned back to inspect the writing.
