## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to the platenreleasing mechanism;

**How it works (reading):** A platen spindle cross-head with engaging pawls works with a housing so the platen can be released and turned by a handwheel on the spindle, with the feed ring carrying the line-space lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen-release mechanism needs to be simple and efficient; the inventor states this only as a general object.

**Solution:** A platen spindle cross-head with engaging pawls works with a housing so the platen can be released and turned by a handwheel on the spindle, with the feed ring carrying the line-space lever.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
