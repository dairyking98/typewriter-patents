## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars strike the ribbon or paper with force that cuts or punctures small characters such as the period and comma, and dents the platen.

**Solution:** A stop and stop-plate on the typebar action cushions the impact at the printing point.
