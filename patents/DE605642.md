**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving mechanisms, e.g. springs stressed during carriage return

**How it works (reading):** The outer spring end is held on a radially movable bridge with a pin inside the last coil, keeping the pull tangential and the coils apart so less spring is needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The outer spring end bends and the coils rub, wasting drive force that the carriage spring should deliver.

**Solution:** The outer spring end is held on a radially movable bridge with a pin inside the last coil, keeping the pull tangential and the coils apart so less spring is needed.
