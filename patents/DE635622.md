## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (tabulator print hammer control) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for producing a permanent visual presentation of the output data, e.g. computer output printers using printers by rack-type printers

**How it works (reading):** A trigger tied to the highest-order type carrier releases its pawl and simultaneously sets a second lock that blocks all print hammers before they lift.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single lock cannot stop complementary numbers from printing in a registering machine, because the second lock engages too late for fast operation.

**Solution:** A trigger tied to the highest-order type carrier releases its pawl and simultaneously sets a second lock that blocks all print hammers before they lift.
