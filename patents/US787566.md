> Description No. 787,566. PATENTED APR. 18, 1905. 0.711. DOWLBN. RIBBON GUIDE FOR TYPE WRITERS. APPLICATION FILED JUNE 23, 1904.. UNITED STATES Patented April 18, 1905. PATENT OFFICE. CONNELL H. DOWLEN, OF HILLSBORO, TEXAS. RIBBON-GUIDE FOR TYPE-WRITERS. SPECIFICATION forming part of Letters Patent No. 787,566, dated April 18, 1905. Application filed June 23, 1904. Serial No- 213,809. To all whom it may concern: Be it known that I, CoNNELL H. DoWLEN, a citizen of the United States, residing at Hillsboro, in the county of Hill and State of Texas, have invented new and useful Improvements in Ribbon-Guides for Type-Writers, of which the following is a specification. This invention is amulticolor device intended to be applied to that class of type-writers known as visible writing machines, in which the ribbon is projected to the printingpoint and retracted after the stroke of the key by means of an oscillating arm or support which carries the ribbon-holder.
