## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**How it works (reading):** Two spring-loaded guide arms swing on vertical axles and are pushed by the striking nose of the type lever; they form a toggle-like mechanism that centres every type lever and reduces friction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters need a guide that brings each incoming type lever to the exact centre of the printing point, but the existing guides cause friction and rolling losses.

**Solution:** Two spring-loaded guide arms swing on vertical axles and are pushed by the striking nose of the type lever; they form a toggle-like mechanism that centres every type lever and reduces friction.
