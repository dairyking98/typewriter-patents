## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** The object being to provide such improvements in the construction of said levers and hangers and in means for pivoting one to the other as shall obviate any lateral motion in the end of the lever to which the type is attached, and to provide improved means for adjusting the bearing of the ends of the hanger against the sides of the lever at its pivot-point.

**How it works (reading):** Concave countersinks in the lever boss mate with convex bosses on the hanger arms, and the pivot screw is turned through a hole in the boss with a pin.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-lever ends play sideways at the hanger pivot, and the pivot screw is hard to reach for adjustment without removing the hanger.

**Solution:** Concave countersinks in the lever boss mate with convex bosses on the hanger arms, and the pivot screw is turned through a hole in the boss with a pin.

**How it works:** Remington-style type-lever has its boss countersunk on both sides around the pivot hole. The bifurcated hanger has convex bosses on the inner faces of its arms that seat in these concavities (or cones). The pivot screw has a transverse hole reachable through a wide opening in the boss top, so a pin can turn it in place and tighten the hanger arms to remove side play.

**Parts:** type-lever; boss; hanger; convex boss; pivot screw; concavity; adjusting pin

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter hanger: pivot screw adjustable in place and conical seats remove lateral play
