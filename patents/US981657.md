## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines and particularly to those known as silent typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Mechanism is added to arrest the stroke of the universal bar on which the key levers are supported, reducing impact and concussion of moving parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Impact and noise from the universal bar stroke are too high in silent typewriters.

**Solution:** Mechanism is added to arrest the stroke of the universal bar on which the key levers are supported, reducing impact and concussion of moving parts.
