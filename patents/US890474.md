## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copy on a linotype machine is hard to follow line by line, and operators need a graduated rule to count ems in tabular work.

**Solution:** A copy holder with a rubber feed roller on a shaft, with a graduated rule carrying em marks above and inch marks below, and a card strip for laying off figures so that the copy can be moved one line at a time.
