## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Devices for spacing single characters or tabulating without the space bar make the carriage hit its stop hard and noisily, since it travels farther per step and gains speed.

**Solution:** Give the carriage a second rack and larger pinion, switched in by a handle, to move twice the distance per step.

**How it works:** The carriage rear arms carry a spindle with two racks on cross-arms. Turning the handle lifts the normal rack from the step pinion and lowers the second rack onto a pinion of twice the diameter on the same shaft, so each key stroke advances two letter spaces. A spring pawl on the carriage engages one of two notches in a locking plate to hold either position.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** carriage; spindle; rack; pinion; handle; locking plate; spring pawl

**Numbers:** second pinion double the diameter
