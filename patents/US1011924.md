## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With a key lowered at uniform speed, the typebar should start slowly and speed up as it nears the platen, and levers of different banks differ in throw.

**Solution:** Key levers with convex rocker edges roll along the straight edges of sub-levers so the contact point shifts and speeds the typebar up through the stroke.

**How it works:** Bell-crank key levers in four banks have a depending arm with a convex rear edge. This engages a straight contact face on a forked sub-lever pivoted at its lower end, linked at its top to a typebar pivoted on a segmental rod. As the key is depressed the engagement point shifts along the sub-lever, so the typebar moves at increasing speed; varying the engagement compensates for lever length. Bearing plates with adjusting means let any lever be removed without disturbing others.
