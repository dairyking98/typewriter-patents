## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (transverse totalizer driver on typewriters)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a driver device for the transverse totalizer on typewriters, in which the transverse summing mechanism is off when it exits the work zone from the vertical counters carried by the trolley to return to its normal location is released.

**How it works (reading):** The device that cancels automatic entrainment of the totalizer acts directly on the step lock through a link tied to the mechanisms that actuate it, so the totalizer is released on skips without a separately swung hook.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The driving hook of the transverse totalizer is torn by blows when the carriage skips several counters, causing premature wear.

**Solution:** The device that cancels automatic entrainment of the totalizer acts directly on the step lock through a link tied to the mechanisms that actuate it, so the totalizer is released on skips without a separately swung hook.
