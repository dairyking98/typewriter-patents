## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of the invention is the provision of means for Vsupporting and adjustingtlic type bars whereby is attained with ease in placing and lreplacing the bars and accomplishment of accurate and ready adjustment thereof.

**How it works (reading):** Flat hangers slide into notches in the segment and are fixed by screws, so a hanger with its type bars can be removed and replaced in a single move.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars are hard to place, replace and accurately adjust on a movable segment.

**Solution:** Flat hangers slide into notches in the segment and are fixed by screws, so a hanger with its type bars can be removed and replaced in a single move.
