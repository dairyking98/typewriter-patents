## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My improvements relate more particu larly to the carriage and feed mechanism of the type-writer;

**How it works (reading):** An attachment that holds and feeds a tag strip through feed rolls with a detent ratchet, and severs each printed tag automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The inventor says a Hammond typewriter cannot print railroad tags from a roll, which must be fed, printed, and severed.

**Solution:** An attachment that holds and feeds a tag strip through feed rolls with a detent ratchet, and severs each printed tag automatically.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
