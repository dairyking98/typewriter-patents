## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The remote-printing electric typewriter of application 434,603 needed a better carriage motor and automatic ribbon reversing.

**Solution:** A clock-spring motor drives the carriage through a belt and escapement-controlled gearing, and an automatic mechanism winds and unwinds the ribbon.

**How it works:** A Remington-type bedplate has type levers linked by elbow levers and rods to armatures of electromagnets arranged in semicircular rows in a case below. The carriage is pulled by a belt driven from a mainspring and clock gearing with a crank-driven reversing mechanism, held still until released between letters. A winding drum and spring supply the motor. A separate reversing mechanism automatically changes the direction of ribbon winding.
