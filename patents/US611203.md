## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (machine for composing printed matter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines for composing printed matter.

**How it works (reading):** A matrix-making machine that composes characters automatically with escapement and feed of the work, applicable to type-setting and casting machines as well.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Composing printed matter by hand with movable type needs skill and time, and earlier machines were not general enough.

**Solution:** A matrix-making machine that composes characters automatically with escapement and feed of the work, applicable to type-setting and casting machines as well.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
