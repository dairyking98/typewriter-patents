## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Aprons or guides for the printing section movable for insertion or release of sheets

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the formation of the paper guide for Small typewriters made from a paper guide plate carrying the pressure rollers with a rearward extension forming the introduction funnel, whereby the Paper guide plate with flaps bent from the side edges of the funnel part is mounted on axle journals extending from the side walls of the machine carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Coil springs and a pull tab linked to a corner angle lever open and close the guide plate, with stops limiting it past a dead centre.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper guide carrying pressure rollers and insertion funnel needs a complicated opening adjustment on small typewriters.

**Solution:** Coil springs and a pull tab linked to a corner angle lever open and close the guide plate, with stops limiting it past a dead centre.
