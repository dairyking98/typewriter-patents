## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators cannot quickly place the paper or see line position and number on the page.

**Solution:** A printed scale on a fixed platen end with line numbers, plus a pointer on the carriage arm, that shows the line position.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
