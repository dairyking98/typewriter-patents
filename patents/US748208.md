## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and particularly to those in which the impression is effected through the medium of a ribbon which is fed intermittently, so as to present a new surface to the type, and reversed whenever the end of the ribbon is reached.

**How it works (reading):** A corrugated switch disc on the reversing shaft is shifted lengthwise by friction-coupled feed arms when the ribbon goes taut, throwing the other bevel pinion into mesh.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversing mechanisms are too complicated.

**Solution:** A corrugated switch disc on the reversing shaft is shifted lengthwise by friction-coupled feed arms when the ribbon goes taut, throwing the other bevel pinion into mesh.

**How it works:** Two ribbon spools are driven by bevel pinions, one of two pinions on a sliding reversing shaft engaging at a time. A feed pawl turns a feed wheel, which carries the shaft by friction through wheels bearing on the corrugated switch disc. When the ribbon ends the shaft stalls, but the feed wheel keeps turning and its arms ride the corrugations, sliding the disc and shaft lengthwise. A spring plunger on a notched disc periphery snaps the switch over quickly.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; bevel pinion; reversing shaft; corrugated switch disc; feed wheel; feed pawl; spring plunger

**Improves on:**

- earlier automatic ribbon reversers: simpler friction-slip switch using the stalled shaft
