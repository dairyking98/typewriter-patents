The new arrangement makes better use of the space required for the pivoting movement of the type rods and the links supporting them, so that the machine can be reduced to a smaller size. In addition, it is achieved that the type bars receive very precise guidance during the entire stop movement, which prevents any lateral deflection of the type bars. Finally, the types of attack on the platen is carried out precisely in the direction of its axis. These means ensure a sharp impression and enable the production of a large number of good copies.
