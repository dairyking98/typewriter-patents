**How it works (reading):** Each typebar carries two type bodies; its front and rear links are placed so the middle and rear of the bar swing in an arc while the type face moves nearly straight to the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need a compact pivot space, and the typebars must be guided precisely so they strike the platen accurately.

**Solution:** Each typebar carries two type bodies; its front and rear links are placed so the middle and rear of the bar swing in an arc while the type face moves nearly straight to the platen.

> Description Translated from German Typewriter with type bars each carried by two arms 723 to improve. The new arrangement makes better use of the space required for the pivoting movement of the type rods and the links supporting them, so that the machine can be reduced to a smaller size. In addition, it is achieved that the type bars receive very precise guidance during the entire stop movement, which prevents any lateral deflection of the type bars. Finally, the types of attack on the platen is carried out precisely in the direction of its axis. These means ensure a sharp impression and enable the production of a large number of good copies.
