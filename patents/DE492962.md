## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriters need the platen carriage rails set exactly parallel to the type surface, and the existing setting is hard to make for even printing across the line.

**Solution:** A support rod with a pivot bearing on one side wall of the switching frame and a screw-adjustable rod at the other end sets parallel guidance from one side, with a clamping screw in a slot to lock it.
