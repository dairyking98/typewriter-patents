## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lower paper guide release on a typewriter needed a carriage end-stop, so narrow sheets required carriage travel to the stop.

**Solution:** The end stop controlling the paper guide is adjustable and acts as a stop for the carriage drainage movement, releasing the guide at any set position.

**How it works:** Adjustable stop on the machine frame that trips the lower paper guide when the carriage reaches a chosen point, so narrow sheets can be handled without moving to the end.
