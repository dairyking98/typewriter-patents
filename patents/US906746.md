## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocks between any two-carriage-moving mechanisms, e.g. character-space, back space, tabulation, carriage return or carriage- release mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to means for notifying the operator when the paperfeeding devices or feed rollers are in the released or inoperative osition.

**How it works (reading):** The machine notifies the operator when the feed rollers are locked off and automatically blocks the printing instrumentalities until the rollers are restored to operative position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After the feed rollers are locked in the released position to adjust paper, operators forget to restore them, so paper feeds badly and sheets are spoiled.

**Solution:** The machine notifies the operator when the feed rollers are locked off and automatically blocks the printing instrumentalities until the rollers are restored to operative position.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
