## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriters are only shrunken versions of carriage-and-roller machines; book typewriters with a circular type frame are rigid and bulky.

**Solution:** A detachable, collapsible circular book typewriter: a loose circular writing device with keys in concentric circles sits on a foldable rail frame that carries the paper and does line feed.

**How it works:** The frame consists of a vertical rail on which a slide carries horizontal toothed rails joined by a cross rail; it folds flat to the size of the longitudinal rail. The loose writing device holds keys in concentric circles linked by plungers, rocking levers and toothed gears to type levers in a circle. Depressing any key operates ring-shaped half-circle rocking levers, offset a quarter circle in pairs, which on rising under spring force advance the device one type width along the rail and also feed the ribbon, whose spool switching works automatically from tension. Line feed shifts the horizontal rails along the toothed longitudinal rail by straightening a spring-closed pawl-like knee lever. Shift keys, space bar and two diagonally offset ribbon spools fit in the free corners of the circular frame.
