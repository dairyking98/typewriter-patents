## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stereotype type indentations in strips) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My improvement relates to the art of printing, and more especially to that branch ofthe art wherein type indentations are made in strips, which are afterward stereotyped;

**How it works (reading):** Two parallel sliding shelves hold the type-bar being justified and a correction bar, and a justifying apparatus sets the strips into column or page form.
