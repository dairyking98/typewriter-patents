## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guards, shields or dust excluders

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the present invention is the device to train so that the protective strip not only when folding up the roller frame automatically unfolds to the outside, but that it also when lowering the roller frame automatically pressed against the paper roller again, without it being necessary would be to undertake special handling for this return of the protective rail.

**How it works (reading):** The guard strip on the pressure roller shaft is spring-pushed away and is actuated by a lever arm with an abutment, so it folds out when the roller frame goes up and presses back on lowering.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Eraser dust falls into the type lever mounting, and the guard strip of the main patent is only semi-automatic.

**Solution:** The guard strip on the pressure roller shaft is spring-pushed away and is actuated by a lever arm with an abutment, so it folds out when the roller frame goes up and presses back on lowering.
