## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Booking cards fall out of the collecting box in random order.

**Solution:** A lock holds each card in free fall so the cards are aligned and drop in the order they were removed from the frame, into a collecting box with slides.

> Description Translated from German Card collection box for booking machines The invention aims to improve the device for collecting and organizing booking cards in booking machines according to patent 728 805, in which the cards are clamped or clamped on the writing frame by means of a clamping rail and released by lifting the rail from the frame and fall into the collecting container . These devices have the defect that the cards are randomly mixed up. In order to eliminate this, according to the invention, the cards are removed when they slide by a lock at any point in front of the, collecting box in its free fall held up and aligned with lopsided, so that after lifting the lock the individual cards aligned and in the same order in which they were removed from the frame fall, slide into the collecting container on the slide provided.
