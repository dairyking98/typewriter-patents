## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** A screw and finger wheel adjust a platen frame carried on a truck, with a line spacing wheel on the rotary platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fine line spacing and billing work need a screw-controlled, adjustable platen frame.

**Solution:** A screw and finger wheel adjust a platen frame carried on a truck, with a line spacing wheel on the rotary platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
