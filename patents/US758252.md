## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with means for effecting line or character spacing in either direction

**In the patent’s own words:** The main purpose of my invention is to avoid this difficulty, which I do by providing series of heads on the bars at the sides of the machine which not only overlap each other, but also overreach each other, as I will now describe.
