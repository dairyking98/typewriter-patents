By this storage is achieved at the same time that the overall frame is well stiffened receives. The shaft can be designed as a fixed bolt and ball bearings be surrounded by which respectively the fixed parts of the bearings in the main part of the frame or sit in the bend while the ball bearings are tightened by tightening the screw the desired safe freewheeling due to the tension of the bent frame part obtain.
