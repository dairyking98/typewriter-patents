## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The escapement frame and its shaft need to be removable yet stay rigid and firmly mounted.

**Solution:** The escape wheel shaft is carried at one end in the main frame and at the other in a U-shaped bend of the stamped frame, which stiffens the frame and lets the escapement frame be removed as a unit.
