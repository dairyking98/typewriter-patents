**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the printing or marking process for which they are designed characterised by selective application of impact or pressure on a printing material or impression-transfer material

**How it works (reading):** A release means separates the escapement so the carriage can move rapidly in either direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement devices lock the carriage so it cannot move rapidly either way.

**Solution:** A release means separates the escapement so the carriage can move rapidly in either direction.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
