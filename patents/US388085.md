## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punching device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**How it works (reading):** A hand numbering punch with jaws drives a rotary die-carrier with dies and guard plate, and a feed mechanism moves the check through.
