## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a printing calculator the control strip cannot be pulled freely to the tear-off point, because the strip conveyor holds it, so pulling it fast tears it in the wrong place and jams the guide.

**Solution:** The strip gripping device engages the control strip only while the machine is working, and releases it at other times so the strip can be moved by hand.
