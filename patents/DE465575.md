**How it works (reading):** The cross rail that carries the type lever bearing and the key mechanism bearing connects both frame sides, and stiffening plates carry the shaft hubs and the universal rail, which are braced to the frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Lightweight typewriters with sheet metal or aluminium frames lacked the rigidity needed for straight carriage travel and firm bearings.

**Solution:** The cross rail that carries the type lever bearing and the key mechanism bearing connects both frame sides, and stiffening plates carry the shaft hubs and the universal rail, which are braced to the frame.
