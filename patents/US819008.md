## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A carriage-feed mechanism should be simpler, more compact and more reliable than earlier designs.

**Solution:** The feed spring barrel and the escapement are combined into one unit, and the barrel's pinion teeth mesh with a rack bar on the carriage so the spring drives the carriage in steps.
