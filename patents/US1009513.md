## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type bar must reach the printing point quickly without the usual slow approach and retreat.

**Solution:** A link from the key lever pulls the type bar to the printing point with rapidly increasing velocity; the text tabulates degrees of bar travel per quarter of key stroke.
