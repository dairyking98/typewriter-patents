## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with means for effecting line or character spacing in either direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on a letter switch mechanism for typewriters for right and left movement of the paper cart with reversible pawls and consists in that the pawl carrier has two pivot pins around which it can swing alternately, depending on the one desired Feed direction of the paper carriage.

**How it works (reading):** The pawl carrier has two pivot pins that fit into open slots, one of which is closed by a toggle lever, so the same pawl steps the carriage in either direction.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage letter-spacing mechanisms that move the paper carriage right and left are complicated.

**Solution:** The pawl carrier has two pivot pins that fit into open slots, one of which is closed by a toggle lever, so the same pawl steps the carriage in either direction.

**How it works:** The transport pawl is on a pawl lever that swings about whichever pivot pin is closed by the shift lever. Pressing the writing key or space key withdraws the pawl from the rack. The pawl spring also pulls the pawl to a triangular position so the left or right lug engages the rack. On release a spring pulls the pawl back and pushes the carriage one tooth.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** pawl lever; transport pawl; switching lever; rack; slotted base plate; pawl spring; writing key; space key

**Numbers:** one tooth per stroke
