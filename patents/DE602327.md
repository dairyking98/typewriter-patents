## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** Switching elements are pivoted off-centre on the scanning element for the ribbon reel, so a small pin-and-slot lever gives a large travel and reverses the ribbon with fewer links.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing needs many linkage parts moved on each keystroke and is complex.

**Solution:** Switching elements are pivoted off-centre on the scanning element for the ribbon reel, so a small pin-and-slot lever gives a large travel and reverses the ribbon with fewer links.
