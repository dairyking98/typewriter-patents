## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In typebar machines two adjacent key-levers pressed together jam their typebars.

**Solution:** A row of pivoted lockers between the key-levers is thrown by the depressed lever into engagement with its neighbours, locking them until it returns.

**How it works:** Typebars swing to a common centre and are driven by key-levers through rods. A series of pivoted key-lever lockers (tumblers) hangs from a supporting bar between the levers. When one lever is depressed it tips the lockers on either side of it into positive engagement with the adjacent levers. Those levers cannot move until the first lever returns to rest. Only the first 6,000 characters are available, so the locker geometry is not fully described.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key-lever; typebar; rod; key-lever locker; supporting bar; framework; type-bar carrier guide
