## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Writing-tables with substantially horizontally extensible or adjustable parts other than drawers, e.g. leaves with sliding or unfolding parts other than leaves or drawers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to Writing and typewriter tables with a machine-supporting, at 18o ° swiveling middle plate as well as with side drawers.

**How it works (reading):** Drawer housings are hinged to the side panels so the frame folds into a cabinet and the top halves form a table.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Writing tables with fixed drawers do not fold compactly and take up knee room.

**Solution:** Drawer housings are hinged to the side panels so the frame folds into a cabinet and the top halves form a table.
