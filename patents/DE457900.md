## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A portable typewriter that folds to a transport position can be damaged if it is used without being restored to the use position.

**Solution:** Make the line-space mechanism depend on the transport/use position device so that it becomes ineffective in the transport position and ready again when restored.

**How it works:** On a Remington portable the carriage rides on rails and is stepped by pawls on a pivoted rack plate, which a release lever pushes out. The platen spindle slides axially but not rotationally; its pawl carrier and line-space lever act on the line ratchet wheel. A control member pivoted on the end plate with a finger locks the platen spindle or knob in the use position and, when turned to the transport position, releases the spindle to be pushed in, withdraws the rack from the pawls via a hook on the carriage release lever and locks the carriage in the middle by a finger entering a slot in a leaf spring. Pushing the spindle in makes the line switch inoperative because its end enters the arm's web.
