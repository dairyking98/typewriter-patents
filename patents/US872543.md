## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars and their bell-crank linkage bind and wear, so the action is not as durable or easy as it should be.

**Solution:** Smedberg's type bar is linked by a link and a bell crank to a key lever whose rear end curves to roll along the fulcrum plate, cutting friction at the contacting parts; a spring returns the bar.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)
