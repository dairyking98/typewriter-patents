## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention consists in certain details of construction and novel arrangement of parts, all of which will be first described and then specifically pointed out in the appended claims, reference being had to the accompanying drawings,in which-- Figure 1 is a rear elevation of a portion of the Remington type of writing-machine with myimprovements applied.

**How it works (reading):** Spring-actuated means return the paper carriage to the left, with tripping devices that release it at the end of the line and intermediate devices for control.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage had to be returned manually to the start of each line.

**Solution:** Spring-actuated means return the paper carriage to the left, with tripping devices that release it at the end of the line and intermediate devices for control.
