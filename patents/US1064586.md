## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator can slide sideways in its bearing when a key is struck hard and come off the operating lever; its stroke also needs adjusting.

**Solution:** An open bearing on the long arm of the vibrator lever takes a detachable vibrator, with a locking projection beside the bearing that stops the vibrator from sliding out. The short arm is made easily distortable to change the stroke.

**How it works:** The lever raises the ribbon carrier, or vibrator, to the print point. A projection next to the open bearing blocks the vibrator when it slides laterally, so it cannot disengage accidentally, yet it can still be removed. Bending the short arm changes the throw of the long arm, which adjusts the vibrator stroke.
