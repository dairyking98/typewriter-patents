## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting' machines and moreparticularly to-line spacing mechanism for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable line-spacing stop and arresting stop for the line-spacing pawl, set so the pawl engages the line-spacing wheel at the end of each movement, with a supporting structure for the pawl.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier line-spacing construction (the patent to Arthur W. No. 911,275 cited in the text, OCR-garbled) does not provide the adjustability the inventor wants for the line-spacing pawl and its stop.

**Solution:** An adjustable line-spacing stop and arresting stop for the line-spacing pawl, set so the pawl engages the line-spacing wheel at the end of each movement, with a supporting structure for the pawl.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
