## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed driven from the key levers adds to finger pressure.

**Solution:** Drive the ribbon wheels from carriage travel, using an inclined plate on the carriage that rocks a shaft pulling a chain to turn a ratchet dog.

**How it works:** As the carriage steps, an inclined actuating plate passes under a finger on a rock-shaft, rocking it. A flexible connection (chain) over a grooved segment and pulley turns a sleeve with an arm bearing a dog, which engages ratchet teeth on a flange of either ribbon wheel (movable along its shaft to pick the wheel). On carriage return a spring rotates the sleeve back. Shown on a New Franklin typewriter.

**Parts:** impression-roller carriage; ribbon wheel; ratchet flange; dog; sleeve; arm; grooved pulley; chain; rock-shaft; inclined plate; finger; coiled spring
