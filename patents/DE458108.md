## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A counter mounted on the paper carriage guide rail must be able to slide and still mesh correctly with the drive wheels of the calculating unit when moved into the calculating position.

**Solution:** Notches and hook-shaped claws on the counter flap sit with play in a rear groove of the rail, so a guide comb on the calculating unit pulls the counter forward into correct gear engagement.

**How it works:** The counter has a spring flap whose side walls carry notches that match the prismatic guide rail, so the counter can be hooked on and removed by folding the flap. Behind the notches, hook-shaped claws engage a rear groove in the rail with some play. In the non-calculating position this lets the counter tilt forward a little. When the carriage moves the counter into the calculating position, a guide comb on the calculating unit enters a groove of the counter and pulls it down so the number wheels mesh with the drive wheels.
