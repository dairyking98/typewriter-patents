## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to paper guide devices with typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Guide roller carriers are mounted on an articulated support so they hold parallel to the platen without binding under pressure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Guide rollers misalign and tilt when pressed against the platen.

**Solution:** Guide roller carriers are mounted on an articulated support so they hold parallel to the platen without binding under pressure.
