## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Coin-operated typewriter locks wasted coins inserted while the writing time was still running.

**Solution:** The coin-holding switching element re-engages the release lever when the clock winds, then returns to rest, so coins are accepted at any time.
