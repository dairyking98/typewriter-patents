## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in arranging the carriage-feeding devices with reference to the movement of the type-bars in a manner such that the carriage shall be released for a full letter-space while the typebars are moving to print and shall be in transit at the moment of impact of the type in print-ing.

**How it works (reading):** A reverse feed: release the carriage for a full letter-space during the key downstroke so it is in transit at the moment of impact.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Escapements give the carriage too little time to move between spaces, causing irregular spacing, alignment, wear and vibration.

**Solution:** A reverse feed: release the carriage for a full letter-space during the key downstroke so it is in transit at the moment of impact.

**How it works:** The feeding devices are arranged so the carriage is released for a full space while the typebar is moving toward the platen and is arrested after impact. This allows the carriage to move easily over more time rather than the roughly fiftieth of a second of the old arrangement. The machine is Remington-like with a type ring, key levers and connecting rods coupling to typebars.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** frame; type ring; key lever; connecting rod; typebar; carriage; feed dog; rack

**Numbers:** about 1/50 second available in old escapements

**Improves on:**

- conventional Remington-type escapement: carriage released before printing instead of after
