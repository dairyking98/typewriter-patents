## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash registers with a check printer needed a separate hand crank to issue checks.

**Solution:** A single drive unit attached to the key connector drives the print hammers, paper feed, ribbon switching and cutter from one keystroke.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Key lever cash register There are cash registers known that only operated by depressing a key lever. You also have machines like that already with a printing unit for the issuance of a check and the production of one Provided with a control strip. However, there was always an additional driving force, z. B. a hand crank, with the help of which only the issue of a check was possible. These cash registers are improved according to the invention in that all required for issuing a check and printing a control strip Movements can be applied solely by pressing a keystroke. According to the invention is used to drive the entire printing unit, such as print hammer movement, Paper feed, receipt output, ribbon switching, a single drive unit, which attached to the button connector. This also has the particular advantage of that without further changes to existing machines, a check printer is provided can be. An embodiment of the invention is shown in the drawings.
