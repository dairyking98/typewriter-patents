## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Vertical-writing character feed needs to be simple, allow direct reverse of the cylinder, and run at an even speed whatever the spacing.

**Solution:** A feed pawl on a spring swing lever with a limiter stops one spacing per print, and the return spring is anchored to the limiter so its tension scales with spacing.

**How it works:** On the print stroke a response rod tilts a control plate that engages a stop pawl on the gear and lifts the feed pawl, so the swing lever springs back to the limiter. On the return stroke the plate lifts the stop pawl and seats the feed pawl, letting a strong cylinder spring turn the gear until the lever hits a stop. A stud then locks the lever by its notch. The spacing is the gap between limiter tip and stop, set by an eccentric wavy cam. Because the pawl pivot lies outside the gear, pushing the gear backward just lets the pawl ride over the teeth.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Vertical feed device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. The invention relates to a vertical feed device for a typewriter, characterised in that a feed pawl for the vertical feed is mounted on a swing lever turned by a spring; each time this pawl, engaged with the vertical-feed gear, completes one vertical feed movement, a projection on a control plate hooks onto part of the swing lever and stops its turning; and one end of the spring provided to make the swing lever spring back, in the direction opposite to the feed, by an amount matching the feed spacing is fastened to part of the spacing limiter, so that the speed at which the swing lever springs back is made proportional to the size of the character spacing (printed with ラシメタ doubled). Its object is, with a simple device, to feed the paper cylinder of a typewriter vertically, to allow its reverse movement to be made directly, and to adjust the feed speed automatically to the size of the spacing, so that the character feed is made quietly and accurately.
