## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is shown in an exemplary embodiment in the drawing shown.

**How it works (reading):** A curved rod links the ribbon drive shafts on both sides of the machine instead of a guided slide, and a locking device holds the inactive ribbon spools still.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Unintended movement of the inactive ribbon lets its sagging part fall into the path of the types.

**Solution:** A curved rod links the ribbon drive shafts on both sides of the machine instead of a guided slide, and a locking device holds the inactive ribbon spools still.
