## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The paper carriage should run light and true, with its weight on roller bearings.

**Solution:** Carriage hung on crossed pairs of rolls: vertical rolls take weight, horizontal rolls resist the turning moment.

**How it works:** On a Remington No. 6 style machine, the carriage top and a fixed rail have parallel bearing surfaces with vertical rolls between them that carry the weight. Horizontal rolls between vertical bearings of rail and carriage oppose the tendency to swing forward. A flanged finger in a slot of the rail stops rising and tilting without touching under normal use. A guide wheel steadies it laterally and stop pins keep rolls from escaping. A modification uses balls. The platen frame shifts on links with two stops for case.

**Parts:** carriage; fixed rail; vertical roll; horizontal roll; roll holder; flanged finger; stop pin; platen carrier; shift rail
