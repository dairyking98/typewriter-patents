## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanism of a front-strike shifting-platen typewriter must uncover the printing point after each stroke and use the same or different transverse fields of the ribbon in different platen positions.

**Solution:** A reciprocating ribbon guide covers and uncovers the printing point during the stroke, and the platen and ribbon guide can be shifted together or independently to select ribbon strips.

**How it works:** A front-strike typebar machine with two-type bars has a platen carried in a swinging frame on the carriage. A ribbon vibrator reciprocates a ribbon guide to cover the printing point when a typebar strikes. The platen and the guide are shifted in unison so that the same part of the ribbon is used at both cases, or independently so different longitudinal fields (upper or lower half) are used at each point.
