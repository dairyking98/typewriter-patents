## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to ribbon feed mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon actuating mechanism selects a field and gives a transverse plus longitudinal feed for some fields and longitudinal only for others.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A multi-field ribbon needs to bring any field to the operating position with transverse or only longitudinal feed.

**Solution:** Ribbon actuating mechanism selects a field and gives a transverse plus longitudinal feed for some fields and longitudinal only for others.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
