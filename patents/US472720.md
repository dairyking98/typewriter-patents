## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to furnish a device for cleaning the type of type-writing machines which will be rapid and effective in its operation, which can be used equally well in machines in which the type-bars are set in circles or ellipses, and which can be thrown into or out of position to clean the type by the operator instantly and without danger of soiling the hands.

**How it works (reading):** A bracket on the lower frame with vertical guides carries an arm holding a truncated-cone brush, turned by a crank through bevel gears.
