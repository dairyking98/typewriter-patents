## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shorthand machines that print several characters at once with a key group per hand are heavy and complex, and they are often carried and used on the lap.

**Solution:** Give the write keys and shift keys a common pivot axis and connect each shift key to the type-lever carrier by pin and slot, saving a pivot axis, springs and levers.

**How it works:** Vertical type levers each carry up to three types, and the type lever height is changed by a pivoting carrier (beam) so that the upper, middle or lower type meets the platen. The write keys and the shift keys swing about the same axis. Each shift key has a downward extension with a guide slot (inclined entry, circular middle part) in which a pin on the carrier runs. Pressing one shift key moves the pin up and thus the type lever down, the other key moves it down and the lever up. Within the circular part of the slot, the lever height does not change, which gives precise and quiet shifting regardless of how hard keys are struck.
