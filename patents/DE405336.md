> Description Translated from German Type carrier for calculating machines. Forms the subject of the invention a type carrier for calculating machines and similar machines, in which the types are stored in a removable type capsule. The subject of the invention differs from the known devices essentially in that the type capsule against its carrier in the altitude can be adjusted to enable alignment of the type sets. Furthermore remain the types when removing the capsule due to the advantageous arrangement of two springs with return rail and special guide rod in their bearings, such as that neither types, nor feathers, nor guiding organs can fall out of the capsule.
