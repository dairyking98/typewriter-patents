## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a mounting of the swing axis of the changeover frame of typewriters with reversible paper rollers, -which pursues the purpose, that, especially with one-sided loading, bending is avoided with certainty and thereby always perfect parallel guidance of the platen when switching is achieved.

**How it works (reading):** The shaft ends rest on rollers between top bearings on the carriage frame, and these bearing supports are adjustable by eccentric screws to keep the platen parallel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A long platen shaft bends under one-sided load when the platen switches, so parallel guidance is lost.

**Solution:** The shaft ends rest on rollers between top bearings on the carriage frame, and these bearing supports are adjustable by eccentric screws to keep the platen parallel.
