## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Claw couplings between a removable platen and the line-index ratchet can only be set at coarse steps (about 0.6 mm of platen circumference) because there is no room for more claws.

**Solution:** Use a vernier coupling with many closely spaced pins on star-shaped springs and nested toothed discs of 33 and 32 gaps for about 1.1 mm of arc.

**How it works:** Following the vernier principle, the two coupling halves have tooth counts that differ by one, so the platen can be coupled to the line-index ratchet at a fraction of a tooth pitch. The axially sliding coupling pins sit on one or more star-shaped springs, packed very close together. Two nested discs, serrated inside and outside, are coupled by the pins entering the gaps (33 gaps in one half, 32 gaps and pins in the other). The pins have conical ends so that the turn continues until one pin fully engages. The text is cut off after the first part of the description.

**Parts:** paper roller; line index ratchet; coupling pin; star spring; serrated disc; sleeve; axial rod
