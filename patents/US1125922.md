## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper use in typewriters and adding machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting g, machines and also to other machines in which paper is used after the fashion of typewriting machines, such, for example, some forms of adding machines.

**How it works (reading):** Paper feed mechanism on the carriage of a typewriter is arranged to hold and release the paper in normal and releasing positions, built with simple, low-cost parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed mechanism for typewriter carriages and similar machines is needed that is inexpensive yet still good and efficient.

**Solution:** Paper feed mechanism on the carriage of a typewriter is arranged to hold and release the paper in normal and releasing positions, built with simple, low-cost parts.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
