## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing device for registering machines)

**How it works (reading):** Print hammer is driven by a cocked spring and released from the drive so it returns to rest on its own spring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Print hammer and drive are rigidly linked, so the return to rest is slowed by inertia of the drive masses.

**Solution:** Print hammer is driven by a cocked spring and released from the drive so it returns to rest on its own spring.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
