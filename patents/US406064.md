## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Becker's index typewriter (US350717) lacked an automatic way to turn the paper-feed roll.

**Solution:** An inclined rod on the sliding index-plate moves a pawl arm that rotates the paper roll a set amount each time the index plate returns.

**How it works:** A bent rod with an inclined and a straight part is fixed to the index plate and passes through a hole in an arm. The arm's hook engages a pin on a disk loose on the hub of a ratchet wheel fixed to the paper roll. As the plate moves left to right the arm swings toward the operator and does not turn the roll; on return it swings back, and a spring pawl on the disk turns the ratchet and the roll a fixed distance. Several pins at different radii on the disk change the throw and so the line width, and tipping the plate back lifts the arm free. A modified form fixes the disk to the roll with pins at regular spacing, and reversing the disk selects another spacing.
