## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for noiseless moving back of the Papielivagens on typewriters by means of one built into the car switchgear Freewheel gear with in inclined slots of a cage attached to the ratchet sliding clutch rollers.

**How it works (reading):** Coupling rollers in inclined slots of guide plates give a noiseless freewheel that stays reliable with grease.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return from a ratchet and detent is noisy and unreliable when grease or dirt builds up.

**Solution:** Coupling rollers in inclined slots of guide plates give a noiseless freewheel that stays reliable with grease.
