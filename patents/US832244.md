## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention being to character whic while simple in construction and efficient in operation shall at the same time effect the printin of the successive lines at a greater or lesser distance apart, as may be desired.

**How it works (reading):** Intermittent rotation mechanism for the paper cylinder, plus a separate adjustable mechanism that sets and controls the distance between lines.
