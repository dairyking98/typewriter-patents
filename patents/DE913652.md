## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** A translucent original sheet with a dye-coated roller behind it is used in the typewriter, so the impact passes through the original to copy paper with the ribbon, without carbon paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets are awkward to insert between paper layers, and the auxiliary-ribbon method risks soiling the paper.

**Solution:** A translucent original sheet with a dye-coated roller behind it is used in the typewriter, so the impact passes through the original to copy paper with the ribbon, without carbon paper.
