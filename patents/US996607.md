## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening or shock-absorbing devices or measures therein

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, and more particularly to means fo preventing the rebounding of thetypewritei carriage when the latter is suddenly arrested, as for instance, by a tabulator stop.

**How it works (reading):** A check dog rides the rack and holds the carriage against rebound, released by the operator's carriage return.
