## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage needs constant, uniform tension to advance it, and it should be returnable easily from any point at the operator's will.

**Solution:** A pneumatic cylinder with a piston on a rod attached to the carriage: compressed air advances the carriage, and a special control key reverses the air on the piston to return it.

**How it works:** A rod on the carriage carries a piston that moves in a pneumatic cylinder mounted on the main frame. Compressed air admitted through a valve pushes the carriage forward. A control key moves the valve to reverse the air pressure, returning the carriage. A spring-loaded valve and bell-crank hold the valve in its normal position.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)

**Parts:** carriage; rack; escapement pinion; escapement wheel; piston; pneumatic cylinder; valve; air chest; spring; bell-crank

**Improves on:**

- spring or manual carriage return: constant pneumatic tension and key-controlled return
