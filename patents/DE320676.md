## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (paper strip advancing device)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for switching strips off Paper, tissue or the like.

**How it works (reading):** Before each advance the strip is withdrawn a short distance from the supply reel, so the feed needles do not pull the reel during the switching stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feed needles pulling thin multi-copy receipt strips off the supply reel tear the paper, and reel inertia interrupts operation.

**Solution:** Before each advance the strip is withdrawn a short distance from the supply reel, so the feed needles do not pull the reel during the switching stroke.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)

**Parts:** feed needles; supply reels; paper strips
