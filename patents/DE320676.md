The purpose of this is to advance such strips by one exact and unchangeable amount with each switching. The establishment is certain especially for control devices with several register strips where one or more slips of paper containing the record are issued while one or more identical notes remain under lock and key in the device. Such devices are used in shops as machines for installation of receipts and replace the carbonless books currently in use. One of the pieces of paper is given to the customer as an invoice, a second remains the hate and a third or even more if necessary for later billing stored in the machine.
