## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing device, cash registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a printing device on cash registers, computing or booking machines for printing related documents.

**How it works (reading):** The table opening is locked while the document is conveyed, so superimposed documents move freely past the hammer.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stacked receipts compress at the table opening and jam when several documents are fed past one print point.

**Solution:** The table opening is locked while the document is conveyed, so superimposed documents move freely past the hammer.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
