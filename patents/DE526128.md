## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash-register print hammer gives a double impression only if the blow is fed twice, and a sudden hammer rise overloads the machine drive.

**Solution:** Hammer is partly withdrawn between two blows and returns fully only after the second; the drive has two parts acting one after the other.

**Features:** [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
