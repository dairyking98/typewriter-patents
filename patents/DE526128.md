> Description Translated from German Printing device for cash registers and accounting machines The invention relates to a printing device for cash registers and similar machines and aims a double imprint of types posted by one during a checkout single print hammer on the document advanced between the print hammer blows to effect. According to the invention, a double strike of the print hammer takes place without renewed triggering of the print hammer drive by placing the print hammer between the two blows is partially withdrawn against its rest position. A complete one Return to its rest position takes place only after the second blow. To this end the print hammer drive consists of two parts that come into effect one after the other, The print hammer oscillates back between the times of attack. This type of print hammer release puts the heavy load on the machine drive as a result of the sudden sharp rise of the crest of the Pressure hammer comb disks avoided.
