## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The side-edge gage of a Monarch carriage cannot be adjusted easily along the platen and is not detachable.

**Solution:** A paper gage on the platen shaft and paper table is adjustable along most of the platen length and can be removed from the machine when wanted.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
