## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (transverse counter holding device)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a holding device for the transverse counter on typewriters, which the repeated taking and jumping back of the cross counter with one larger step of the carriage by pressing the tab key or the carriage release key prevented.

**How it works (reading):** A retaining pawl and lug hold the cross counter in place so it cannot repeatedly follow the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pressing tab or carriage release with many vertical counters makes the cross counter jump back and forth fast, causing noise and damage.

**Solution:** A retaining pawl and lug hold the cross counter in place so it cannot repeatedly follow the carriage.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
