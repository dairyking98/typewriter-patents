## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Overhung platen carriages wear and sag, and shifting the platen for case changes needs a simple reliable support.

**Solution:** The carriage rides on ball races on a shift frame, with pivot screws and balls that self-adjust to wear under the overhanging weight.

**How it works:** The carriage rides rearward on two balls in a downward-facing and upward-facing raceway in one vertical plane, and in a lower pair of balls in a horizontal plane at right angles. The shift frame, as wide as the carriage, pivots on two adjustable screws with hardened balls in the sockets to take up end play. The shift key moves the frame between lower and upper case against a lifting spring, with adjustable stop screws setting each position. The rolling balls travel half as far as the carriage.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** carriage; ball; raceway; shift frame; pivot screw; lock nut; lifting spring; stop screw; shift key; platen

**Numbers:** two balls in each upper and lower raceway

- Hillard application Ser. No. 303,845: simpler species of the same self-adjusting ball-mount idea
