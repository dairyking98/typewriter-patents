## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inner structure

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists of a cushion saturated with ink and composed of a combination of fibers which are especially adapted to retain and conduct the ink uniformly to the face of the types, and yet will resist the repeated strokes of the sharp face of the types for a long time, thus rendering this cushion more durable than the cushions employed at the present time.
