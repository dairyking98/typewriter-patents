## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The opening text does not state the problem; the figures show a front-strike shifting-platen typewriter with a vibratory ribbon carrier and devices for positioning it.

**Solution:** A mechanism for positioning the vibratory ribbon carrier on a front-strike typebar machine with shifting platen (details beyond the opening are not available).

**How it works:** The machine is a conventional front-strike typebar machine: key levers drive typebars with upper and lower case type against a platen carried in a shiftable carrier. The carriage is fed by a spring drum, with a feed rack and pinion, escapement wheel and dogs worked by a universal bar. The invention concerns devices that position the vibratory ribbon carrier, but the description of them is cut off.
