## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Stepped disc motor for any type of current with internal stator and external rotor. If machines or gear parts of machines have to be driven at different speeds directly by an electric motor, a stepped pulley is placed on the free shaft end of the motor, especially if it runs at a constant number of revolutions, which drives a corresponding counter step pulley on the machine. Such a step pulley loads the bearing located on the free shaft blunt :: de fX-otors not only by its weight, but mainly by the belt pull, especially when the belt runs on the outermost step facing away from the bearing, so that the bearing wears out in a short time. The motors were therefore designed with a third bearing, a support bearing, on the outside of the stepped pulley in order to relieve the actual motor shield bearing. If the motor with the attached stepped pulley is already quite space-consuming in and of itself, the attached bearing takes up even more space, and these motors are difficult to arrange on a rocker for the purpose of belt tension.
