## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**How it works (reading):** A member driven in step with the platen moves in response to the sheet and releases indicators and signals (visual, audible, lock) at set sheet positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An operator needs warning when the leading edge of the sheet nears printing position.

**Solution:** A member driven in step with the platen moves in response to the sheet and releases indicators and signals (visual, audible, lock) at set sheet positions.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
