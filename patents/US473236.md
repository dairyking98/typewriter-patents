## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage of an ordinary typewriter (Hammond shown) must be returned by hand and the paper advanced a line each time.

**Solution:** An electric motor with a magnetic clutch and cord returns the carriage and line-spaces the paper automatically when the carriage reaches the end of its travel.

**How it works:** A sliding bar with a fixed and an adjustable lug lies parallel to the carriage rack. At the end of the line a pin on the carriage pushes the bar into contact with a spring, closing a circuit through a battery. This energises a clutch magnet that couples a continuously running motor shaft to a pulley and cord, which tilts a bell-crank lever, drives the carriage back and rewinds the carriage drum spring. At the other stop the bar breaks the circuit, the clutch releases and a spring lets the bell crank feed the paper one line.
