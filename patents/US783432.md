## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The universal Hammond (companion to US780102) needs a paper carriage that can print in either direction for oriental scripts and Braille with one escapement, one release and one back-space, plus supporting conveniences.

**Solution:** Drive the carriage by a cord that can be re-clipped to either end and mesh either the escapement gear or a smaller always-meshed change gear with the carriage rack, so one escapement steps the carriage in either direction.

**How it works:** The spring drum pulls the carriage by a cord that passes between two central guide rollers and attaches to a clip that can be latched at either end of the back bar. The escapement ratchet always turns one way; a shifting escapement gear and a smaller change gear, meshed together, are moved by a finger lever so that one or the other engages the carriage rack, giving left-to-right or right-to-left stepping. Release sleeve and a back-space gear acting through the escapement gear work unchanged in both settings. A worm on a front rock-shaft slides the carriage on its rack for variable spacing of wide and narrow letters. Other details are a pivoting card holder, a Braille scale, a pointer and erasing shield on the anvil post, a forward-stroke line-space lever and a hammer spring wound together with the carriage spring.
