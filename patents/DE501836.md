## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements of selective printing mechanisms, e.g. ink-jet printers or thermal printers, for supporting or handling copy material in sheet or web form

**How it works (reading):** A thin hard but elastic metal underlay sheet is fed in behind the forms to give a hard abutment for the type strokes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Many-copy typing with interleaved carbon sheets blurs the lower copies unless hard rollers are fitted, which wears the machine.

**Solution:** A thin hard but elastic metal underlay sheet is fed in behind the forms to give a hard abutment for the type strokes.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
