## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to tabulating mechanism for typewriting and other machines, particularly to means for allowing the carriage to jump to any selected field or column, such devices being known in the art as column skippers, and more especially consists of means for arresting the carriage at any desired decimal pointin any selected column.

**How it works (reading):** Column stops individually movable into and out of position to engage decimal stops, with key-operated displacement of any selected column stop so that the column is skipped.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage tabulation must allow the carriage to skip columns and stop at a chosen decimal position.

**Solution:** Column stops individually movable into and out of position to engage decimal stops, with key-operated displacement of any selected column stop so that the column is skipped.
