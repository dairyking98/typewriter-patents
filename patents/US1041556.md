## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-stroke visible typebar action needs a very light touch yet must still be able to deliver heavy pressure for manifolding, and be quick.

**Solution:** An intermediate lever first pushes a roller on the typebar's rear arm for a fast light start, then a slotted-pin and link finish the stroke in a toggle-like straight line that multiplies pressure on the platen.

**How it works:** The key lever's stud sits in a slot of an intermediate lever, which rocks when the key is pressed. The intermediate lever's upper arm presses a roller on the typebar's rear arm, rolling along a curved surface to raise the type. Late in the stroke a pin on a link, which is hinged to the typebar, is forced to the end of a slot in the intermediate lever's second arm. Pivot, arm, pin, link and pin then nearly line up with the typebar, giving a toggle effect and great pressure. Shoulders on the intermediate levers also work a universal bar for the escapement dog and ribbon guide.
