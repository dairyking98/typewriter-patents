## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon switching on type rotary printers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Continuous, e.g. endless, band apparatus

**How it works (reading):** The fixed stop is replaced by a middle gear that drives the ribbon ratchet wheels through intermediate gears, so the switching is silent and free of impact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ratchet wheels on the ribbon spools strike the frame stop, causing noise and tooth damage when the ribbon is switched on a rotary printer.

**Solution:** The fixed stop is replaced by a middle gear that drives the ribbon ratchet wheels through intermediate gears, so the switching is silent and free of impact.
