## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed with two or more sets of type or printing elements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewrit-ing machines, and particularly to improvements in typewriting machines of the type-bar variety.

**How it works (reading):** A front-strike typewriter with double-row type-bar segments and hangers, adjustable key-lever connections, a top coupler, and an improved type-basket.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Front-strike type-bar machines need better support for a double row of type-bars, counterbalancing, and key-to-bar connections.

**Solution:** A front-strike typewriter with double-row type-bar segments and hangers, adjustable key-lever connections, a top coupler, and an improved type-basket.

**How it works:** Key levers couple through adjustable connections and a top coupler to type-bar actuating levers; segments and hangers carry two rows of bars.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)

**Parts:** type-bar segments; type-bar hangers; top coupler; type-basket; universal escapement bar
