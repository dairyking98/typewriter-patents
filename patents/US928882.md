## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side lays or gauges

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to devices usually provided for engaging the side edges of the sheets as they are placedinto position for writing.

**How it works (reading):** A side gage plate bent up from a base plate overlies the shelf front, its top curves over the beaded edge, and a hinged second plate with claws catches the shelf rear under spring force, with finger-pieces for release.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing side gages on the paper shelf have hooks and claws that must grip so tightly they are hard to detach or slide along the shelf and they scratch and mar the shelf.

**Solution:** A side gage plate bent up from a base plate overlies the shelf front, its top curves over the beaded edge, and a hinged second plate with claws catches the shelf rear under spring force, with finger-pieces for release.
