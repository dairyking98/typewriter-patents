## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Back spacing can let the carriage run back more than one tooth under acquired momentum.

**Solution:** A key-operated hook-shaped rack pawl, guided by a cam-shaped stop, moves the carriage back one tooth and locks it against further backward movement.

**How it works:** A bell-crank key on a fixed support is linked to a hook-shaped pawl with a toothed portion that engages the carriage feed rack. A stop guide at the opening through which the pawl reciprocates acts on the pawl's cam to throw it into and out of mesh. At full depression the pawl embraces the stop guide, setting the rack back exactly one tooth and locking it. A spring holds the cam against the guide and a spring on the key restores it.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** key; support; link; hook-shaped pawl; stop guide; feed rack; feed pinion; spring

**Numbers:** one rack tooth per stroke
