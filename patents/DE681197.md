## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** Carriage and type-lever linkage sit on a subframe that is elastically suspended on the main frame and can swing in all directions; the key levers stay on the main frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter noise from the type striking the platen at the end of a fast stroke, which rubber feet under the whole frame do not damp.

**Solution:** Carriage and type-lever linkage sit on a subframe that is elastically suspended on the main frame and can swing in all directions; the key levers stay on the main frame.
