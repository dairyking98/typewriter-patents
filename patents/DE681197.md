To avoid these noises, you already have rubber feet or the like. Between the frame of the typewriter or arranged on the desk or the pad. In this arrangement, the frame is seated the machine with all parts stored in it on the rubber feet or the like. on, which on the one hand the rubber feet due to the heavy weight of the frame and all other parts of the typewriter are strongly compressed and thereby lose their vibration-damping properties to a large extent and on the other hand sideways swinging of the frame is not possible. Furthermore, it has already been proposed that the entire typewriter factory including the keys, the type carrier and the slide device in one To store auxiliary frame, which in turn is supported by a main frame, wherein the subframe rests on elastic means arranged on the main frame.
