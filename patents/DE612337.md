## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method for producing a platen for typewriters, their roller shell to dampen the effect caused by the impact of the type lever Noise consists of individual ring disks.

**How it works (reading):** A rubber shell is pulled on the core in its unground state, cut into narrow rings at letter spacing, and then sanded, so the discs damp vibration.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen made of separate ring discs is hard to assemble, and the rings twist and do not sit evenly, so impact noise is transmitted.

**Solution:** A rubber shell is pulled on the core in its unground state, cut into narrow rings at letter spacing, and then sanded, so the discs damp vibration.
