**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of counting devices

**How it works (reading):** Links with jaws obstruct the other key levers when one is depressed, and the links can be disconnected by a shifter rod when interlock is not needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** When one key is held down the others must be prevented from operating, which is not always wanted.

**Solution:** Links with jaws obstruct the other key levers when one is depressed, and the links can be disconnected by a shifter rod when interlock is not needed.
