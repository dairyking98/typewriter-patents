## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platens are noisy and the vibration of typing is not damped.

**Solution:** A platen is built from a rubber outer tube, a felt inner tube and a perforated sheet-metal tube between them, to absorb vibration and reduce noise.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
