## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Choosing the ribbon zone to print from by shifting the vibrator pivot changes the load on the keys.

**Solution:** The vibrator link has several rest positions set by a pointer, so it has a different throw per zone without changing leverage, plus a stencil position where the vibrator does not move.

**How it works:** An elbow lever rocking about a point fixed to the platen shift rail moves the vibrator when the universal bar pushes a link. A pointer arm on top of the machine turns a vertical shaft through a link to rock a hub with three flats held by a leaf spring: bottom, top, stencil. This moves the link so printing is from the upper or lower ribbon zone or the vibrator stays at rest. Stop means adjust with it to limit the throw.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; elbow lever; link; pointer arm; rock shaft; hub; leaf spring; stop

**Improves on:**

- vibrators that shift the pivot or leverage: link position changes, so key load stays uniform
