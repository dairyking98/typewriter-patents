## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting ma chines and more particularly topaper feeding and indicating devices therefor.

**How it works (reading):** A printing-point indicator, shown at the printing point in front of the platen, along with card or paper feeding devices and a front paper guiding device, each part of the paper handling of the machine.
