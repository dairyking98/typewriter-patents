## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to means for holding, guiding and feeding paper, envelopes, postal cards, &c.

**How it works (reading):** A lever and cam lock the feed-roller away from the platen and unlock it automatically when the platen-carrier is turned up or down.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed-roller presses against the platen and interferes when the platen-carrier is swung.

**Solution:** A lever and cam lock the feed-roller away from the platen and unlock it automatically when the platen-carrier is turned up or down.
