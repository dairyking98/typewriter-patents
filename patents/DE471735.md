## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Address printing plates on a rotary printer drum are hard to fit and remove.

**Solution:** A retractable arched carrier between the drum ribs has a thickened centre and bent-over edges, so the plate with bent-down flanges slides under the edges and can be removed easily.
