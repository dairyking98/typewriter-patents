## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typebar action should be simple and cheap, made of stampings.

**Solution:** Stamped typebar driven by cam member and links pulled by a wire from the key lever.

**How it works:** A stamped steel typebar slides between type bed plates. L-shaped type member riveted on carries types with protecting lugs. Links stamped from sheet metal join to an oscillating cam member on an adjustable block. A bell crank with a roller follows the cam slot, pulled by a wire from the key lever.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** typebar; type member; link; oscillating cam member; bell crank; roller; pull wire; key lever; adjustable block

**Improves on:**

- Noiseless typewriter: cheaper stamped action
