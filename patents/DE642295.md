## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type cleaning with brushes or dough is inefficient, and sticking pads soil the hands.

**Solution:** Velvet-backed brush whose bristles are coated with a sticky mass reaches into the type recesses and is thrown away after use.
