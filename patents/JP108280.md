## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a flat-paper printing machine for horizontal writing the paper table steps sideways per character, and there was no way to return it automatically so the printed sheet could be removed at the front edge.

**Solution:** After printing, releasing a clutch lets a mainspring return the paper-table frame sideways, then a hook is tripped and a spring pulls the table forward, giving an L-shaped (┐) return path.

**How it works:** The paper table slides back and forth on a frame by its guide rods; the frame runs on rails and carries a crosswise rack meshed through gears to a mainspring gear. Each press of the handle feeds a ratchet wheel, which through a clutch turns the gears, winds the mainspring and steps the frame one fixed length left. Pressing a release lever unclutches the ratchet, so the mainspring returns the frame to its start, where a notch catches a raised catch and stops it. At that moment a hook on the table strikes a hinged obstacle at the rear of the base, leaves its hole in the frame, and a coil spring slides the table forward to the front of the base. A catch that is pressed down by the first character and a spring clip soften and sequence the motion.
