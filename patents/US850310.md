## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting several sheets together between the platen and feed rolls is hard, because the leading edges do not line up parallel with the printing line.

**Solution:** A spring-opened clip runs along the platen, is carried round by the platen, and closes on the sheet edges against a gauge edge, holding them square until the feed rolls take them.
