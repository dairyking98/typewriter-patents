## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers

**How it works (reading):** A paint roller in the type path, turned by a driver wheel on a corrugated rail of the carriage and a ratchet, moves fresh ink onto the types on every stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type inking was uneven and the inking roller ran dry without fresh colour per stroke.

**Solution:** A paint roller in the type path, turned by a driver wheel on a corrugated rail of the carriage and a ratchet, moves fresh ink onto the types on every stroke.
