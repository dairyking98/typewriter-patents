The ribbon consists of a long, narrow, rolled-up strip of ink-impregnated paper, silk, etc., and lies in the capsule b, which is attached to the bottom of the index plate b , in the circumference of which a slot is made for the passage of the strip. The latter is then pulled away over a glass plate α , which is also seated at the bottom of the index plate, namely at its front end, over which a feed roller c is located. So it moves at right angles to the direction of the writing over the arc to be written on; whereby the advantage is achieved that the letter just written is immediately visible when the next one is printed on it, so that one always has the finished script in front of one's eyes while writing. On the axis c 2 of the roller c , the ratchet wheel c l is attached, which is set in rotation by the pawl c l connected to the bracket c s.
