## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in copy-holders for type writing machines,the object of which is to hold the copy before the eye of the operator and to roll it up after passing the point of vision;

**How it works (reading):** A copy spool is rotated through a clutch and gearing from the ribbon-feed shaft, with an escapement lever and rack driven by the carriage.
