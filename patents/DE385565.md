**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter of the type in which the switchable frame arranged in the paper carriage the paper roller can be folded up at a certain point in its trajectory can.

**How it works (reading):** A locking device holds the carriage automatically at any height of its upward movement against the return spring, and a separate catch holds the raised frame at its top position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the paper carriage frame is folded up for repairs, the return spring pulls the carriage to the left, so the operator needs a second hand to hold it.

**Solution:** A locking device holds the carriage automatically at any height of its upward movement against the return spring, and a separate catch holds the raised frame at its top position.
