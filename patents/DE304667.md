**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for the simultaneous production of several carbon copies on typewriters by means of an auxiliary ribbon between the main ribbon and the platen is reciprocated to form multiple layers of tape, and its spool axes of rotation parallel to 'The platen shaft is provided on the paper carriage.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary ribbon carrier on the paper carriage is guided through punched guide plates with triangular cutouts and a slotted plate so the ribbon layers stay the same distance apart across the whole line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing carbon-copy auxiliary ribbons leave uneven copies at line ends because the ribbon layers spread apart there.

**Solution:** An auxiliary ribbon carrier on the paper carriage is guided through punched guide plates with triangular cutouts and a slotted plate so the ribbon layers stay the same distance apart across the whole line.
