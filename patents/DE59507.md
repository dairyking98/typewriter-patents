> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE. The subject of the present invention is an inking device on typewriter machines, which is characterized in that the ink ribbon that is used when printing the types is unwound from one reel and wound onto the other reel, but after unwinding one reel an automatic switchover to takes place in such a way that now the spools rotate in the opposite direction and consequently the ribbon is wound again from the full to the empty spool. : The attached drawing shows: Fig. Ι the part of the typewriter in the Section containing the innovation; Figs. 2 to 6 show construction details and Figs Fig. 7, to 9 two vertical sections and the top view of the inking unit. The ink ribbon J is wound onto two reels SS 1 , FIGS. 7 to 9, which are loosely arranged on the axis α fixed in the stand L , so that when one reel is rotated, the ink ribbon is wound onto the same and unwound from the other reel . The reels are actuated when the type keys are pressed by two interconnected tape guides HH \ Fig. 7 to 9.
