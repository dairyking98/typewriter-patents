## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons must be reversed by hand when one reel runs out.

**Solution:** The ribbon unwinds from one reel to the other and automatically reverses when a reel is emptied, using pawls on an obliquely pinned shaft.

**How it works:** Pressing a type key depresses a crossbar on a lever under the base plate, swinging two sheet-metal ribbon guides up and toward the platen roller. The back-and-forth swing works push pawls on the guides that turn gear rings on the reels. Ratchet pawls hold the wound reel while the paying-out reel is released. An oblique pin through a rotatable vertical shaft selects which pawls engage; when a reel empties, the shaft turns and the roles swap.
