## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a further embodiment of the table writing device according to the patent 290911, in particular the facility for route selection through the present invention is greatly simplified.

**How it works (reading):** Position and route selector levers, linked by springs and bend-shaped stops, raise point stops into the path of column stops or tabs for the chosen column and uncouple the paper carriage; the point-stop housing can pivot to match row spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Choosing the column position on a table writing device needs a simpler route selection than the parent patent offers.

**Solution:** Position and route selector levers, linked by springs and bend-shaped stops, raise point stops into the path of column stops or tabs for the chosen column and uncouple the paper carriage; the point-stop housing can pivot to match row spacing.
