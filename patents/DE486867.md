## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** Holder plate and holder foot head pieces run on T-slots of an I-shaped rail whose upper and lower legs have teeth, and cranked gears in the head pieces engage those teeth for smooth gear-driven adjustment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hand-pushed template holder shifts are not uniform and do not always move smoothly over short distances.

**Solution:** Holder plate and holder foot head pieces run on T-slots of an I-shaped rail whose upper and lower legs have teeth, and cranked gears in the head pieces engage those teeth for smooth gear-driven adjustment.

**How it works:** Turning a crank rolls a gear along the rail's teeth, moving the holder plate precisely along the rail and the rail within the holder foot.
