## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon coil of the parent patent is expensive to make precisely, and the user must choose the right disc setting for his machine.

**Solution:** The coil disks are snap-in cover sheets that engage grooves and openings of the spool tube, shaped so they fit only one way, color-coded, and interchangeable for different machines.
