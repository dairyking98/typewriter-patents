## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing machines need the ribbon guide to lift with a case shift and drop out of sight between strokes.

**Solution:** The ribbon guide is raised by the universal bar via a U-shaped lever, and the shift key lifts the carriage and the ribbon shifter together.

**How it works:** The universal shaft, rocked by a typebar, raises the ribbon guides through a slotted lever and a U-lever. Shift-key bars move a sliding bar that rocks a shaft to lift the carriage wheel frame, carrying the ribbon shifter up for the upper case. A locking arm holds the shaft while the shift key is down.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** universal shaft; universal bar; U-shaped lever; ribbon guide; shift-key bar; rock-shaft; locking arm
