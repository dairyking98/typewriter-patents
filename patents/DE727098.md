## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing device for data cards) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated; Methods or arrangements for marking the record carrier in digital fashion otherwise than by punching by printing code marks

**How it works (reading):** Printed numerals are shaped with line lengths graded evenly by value so that a photocell can tell them apart.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Digits in punched-hole data carriers are hard to read by photo-cell when the characters are printed with uneven ink areas.

**Solution:** Printed numerals are shaped with line lengths graded evenly by value so that a photocell can tell them apart.
