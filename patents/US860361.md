## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and moreparticularly to the ribbon mechanism of such any desired field of the said ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** Means to dispense with the crosswise ribbon feed and to allow a multi-colour ribbon to be used with the ribbon vibrator.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The ribbon always has crosswise feed with the vibrator, and a multi-colour ribbon cannot be used in such machines.

**Solution:** Means to dispense with the crosswise ribbon feed and to allow a multi-colour ribbon to be used with the ribbon vibrator.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
