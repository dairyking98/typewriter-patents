## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The paper holding rail must lift clear of the platen so the advancing sheet passes between platen and rail during line switching.

**Solution:** The holding rail is carried on one leg of a toggle (knee) joint, and a cam roller on the line-switch hand lever lifts it in an arc before the sheet arrives.
