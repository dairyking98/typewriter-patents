## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (variable cam disc, no typewriter content) · **Subject (patent classes):** Adjustable cams

**How it works (reading):** The cam is formed by a flexible band bent against movable abutments, which are pivoted to vary the cam shape.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjustable cams in gearboxes need many separate articulated partial cams to change their shape.

**Solution:** The cam is formed by a flexible band bent against movable abutments, which are pivoted to vary the cam shape.
