## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type bars vibrate after impact and give a double impression.

**Solution:** A rigid abutment arc catches each type bar partway along its length so the bar flexes to print and then cannot rebound against the platen.

**How it works:** A fixed abutment shaped as a circular arc concentric with the type-bar pivots is placed so each bar touches it midway along its length. Normally the bars rest clear of the platen. On operation the bar swings into the abutment with force and the upper part springs forward so the type strikes the paper. The abutment then prevents the type from reaching the platen again, so vibrations make no second impression.
