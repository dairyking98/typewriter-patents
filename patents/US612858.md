## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Preventing rebound or clash of levers or type members

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines of that character known as bar-machines, wherein one or more types are carried upon each of a series of type-bars, which are adapted to be oscillated to cause the types thereon to contact with the paper carried upon a suitable platen or paper-support.
