## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register several checks)

**How it works (reading):** A single circular knife cuts at two different points on either side of a septum, sending each check to a different destination.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Cash register cut-off system issues two checks in one checkout run into the same waste channel, so the second check falls where the first did.

**Solution:** A single circular knife cuts at two different points on either side of a septum, sending each check to a different destination.

**How it works:** A lifting disc and toothed segment swing the circular knife, cutting first on one side and then the other side of the partition.
