## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type printing telegraph receiver) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using mechanical translation and type-head printing, e.g. type-wheel, type-cylinder

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to type printing telegraph apparatus such as those in particular are used in stock exchange operations, in which the imprint takes place in two different rows, so that the letters in one row and the numbers in the row below.

**How it works (reading):** A type wheel with grooves and teeth carries two offset rows of types, and one of two print hammer extensions engages depending on the groove arrangement, so letters and figures print in different rows.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-printing telegraph receivers needed a shift character for each letter/figure change, or a separate hammer per row, which slowed transmission.

**Solution:** A type wheel with grooves and teeth carries two offset rows of types, and one of two print hammer extensions engages depending on the groove arrangement, so letters and figures print in different rows.
