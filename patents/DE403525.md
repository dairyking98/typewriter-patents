## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices used in association with a real typewriter, teleprinter, or the like

**How it works (reading):** Flexible guide strips on spring-mounted articulated rods give way horizontally and vertically, then return to position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Finger on a typewriter key hits a rigid finger guide and is knocked off the key.

**Solution:** Flexible guide strips on spring-mounted articulated rods give way horizontally and vertically, then return to position.
