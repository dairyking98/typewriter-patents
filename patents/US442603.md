## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter platens need to be inexpensive, durable, light and to avoid undue wear of the type.

**Solution:** A hollow hard-rubber platen shell moulded in one piece with its ends and journals, covered by a softer vulcanised rubber layer.

**How it works:** The hard-rubber cylinder with integral ends is blown in a two-part mold in which separate metal shaft journals have been placed; their inner ends are roughened or grooved so the rubber grips them and they turn with the cylinder. An outer layer of softer rubber is vulcanised on, or a separate tube is slipped on and cemented. Variants use a through shaft, rubber journals, metal sleeves, or skeleton ends. The hollow shell keeps the roller light so it can be shifted longitudinally by a light force.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [ELEM:rubber-type](../features/elem-rubber-type.md)

**Parts:** platen; hard-rubber tube; soft-rubber covering; end journal; mold; sleeve
