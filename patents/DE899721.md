## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Negative sums on a light printing calculator need a different ribbon colour, and manual setting is easily forgotten.

**Solution:** Flexible intermediate members link the drive to the ribbon shift member, and an inhibiting pawl with a second holding member selects the colour automatically during subtraction or sum drawing.
