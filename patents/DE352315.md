## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type box for calculator printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies arranged in stationary or sliding cases or frames or upon flexible strips, plates, bars or rods

**How it works (reading):** A single leaf spring, shaped to press evenly on all types, acts directly on lugs of the types, is guided by the types and the box lid, and is held by the box head and a lug; the lid slides upward so the box can be opened without removing the printing unit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stacked types in a calculator type box need separate return springs or intermediate links, which add reciprocating mass and wear.

**Solution:** A single leaf spring, shaped to press evenly on all types, acts directly on lugs of the types, is guided by the types and the box lid, and is held by the box head and a lug; the lid slides upward so the box can be opened without removing the printing unit.
