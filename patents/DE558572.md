## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Multiple web-feeding apparatus for webs superimposed during printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper clamping device for typewriters with flat writing surface, both for holding the both in individual sheets and also from the roll of the writing plate feedable paper on the front of the frame is also used as a cutting knife, which is lifted off the paper by stepping on a foot pedal.

**How it works (reading):** Clamps hold only the corners of the sheet and a cutting-knife clamp is lifted first, while a long-slot clutch lets a rear clamp lift and then lower the sheet in sequence, leaving the carbon path clear.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On flat-plate typewriters the paper clamps block carbon paper sheets and ribbons placed across the paper, and the sheet cannot be smoothed out easily while clamped.

**Solution:** Clamps hold only the corners of the sheet and a cutting-knife clamp is lifted first, while a long-slot clutch lets a rear clamp lift and then lower the sheet in sequence, leaving the carbon path clear.
