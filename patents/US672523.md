## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage escapement cannot easily change between regular and speed spacing, the carriage slips back under spring tension, and the printing does not stop at the end of a line or signal when the ribbon runs out.

**Solution:** An escapement that switches between regular and speed spacing holds the carriage at the graduation it is pushed to, a line-end lock stops the printing mechanism with an adjustable control, and a signal shows when the ribbon is unwound.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
