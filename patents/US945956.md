## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Friction clutches slip under violent line-space operation, and toothed clutches only engage at fixed positions.

**Solution:** Platen clutched positively to the line-space wheel at any adjusted position, released for fractional rotation with the wheel held by its detent.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
