## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The margin stop limits the carriage return fully, so the left-hand margin cannot be written on when needed.

**Solution:** A left-hand margin stop that sets the left margin on the carriage return while still allowing the carriage a further movement to write on the marginal part.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
