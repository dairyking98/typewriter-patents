## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line

**How it works (reading):** The cooperating upper part of the line stop is bent parallel to, and in front of, the front face of the centre stop, so the margin stop can pass without jamming.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the line delimiter is turned off to write a syllable, parts of the margin stop jam between the centre stop and the line stop.

**Solution:** The cooperating upper part of the line stop is bent parallel to, and in front of, the front face of the centre stop, so the margin stop can pass without jamming.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Line delimiting device for typewriters is the subject of the invention is a line delimiter for typewriters with an on the machine frame pivotally attached center stop, on which a stop when the right edge stop hits the universal rail locking line delimitation lever is rotatably attached, which pressed by a spring a limited distance in the direction of the edge stop will overlap the side edge of the central stop facing this edge stop and hit at this point by the edge stop arranged on the paper trolley will. In the case of the line delimitation devices of known design, it has been shown that that parts of the margin stop are between the center stop and the line stop jam and thus prevent further use of the machine if the Turning off the device when writing for any reason, for example to write out a syllable on a line by pressing a special key will. The invention eliminates this disadvantage in that the one with the margin stop provide the cooperating upper part of the line stop with a bend which is parallel to and in front of the front face of the center stop.
