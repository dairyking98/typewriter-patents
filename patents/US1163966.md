## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Balls running between the carriage and its rails need positive control so they stay in position and move together.

**Solution:** A stamped sheet-metal ball retainer with openings for the balls and a pinion-carrying tongue, with flat forked keepers that surround and hold each ball in place.

**How it works:** The carriage has upper and lower guide rails and an intermediate shift rail that supports it and along which it travels. Facing surfaces have V-shaped runways holding steel balls. A longitudinal retainer lies parallel to the runways, with holes through which the balls project; it is stamped from a rectangular blank with enlarged corners, lightened centre and a tongue that carries a pinion for positive movement. A flat perforated keeper punched from sheet metal is inserted through a slit at each hole to surround a ball, and its forked ends are bent apart to lock it. The retainer is self-contained and can be installed or removed easily.
