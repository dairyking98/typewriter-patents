## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper roller could only turn with automatic carriage return, and premature return needed the paper rotated from the keyboard.

**Solution:** A key partially turns the paper roller regardless of carriage position, and the return mechanism can be operated from the keyboard before a line is complete, each with a partial roller turn.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
