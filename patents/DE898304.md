## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (multi-line printing device controlled by keys) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets

**How it works (reading):** Type rails slide and rotate with drawstrings driven differentially by control levers, and individual print hammers print selected types across several sections at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing from fixed type positions is slow, and multi-line printing needs repeated setting.

**Solution:** Type rails slide and rotate with drawstrings driven differentially by control levers, and individual print hammers print selected types across several sections at once.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
