## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A satisfactory electrically operated carriage return for typewriters is needed.

**Solution:** An electric motor with a drum keyed to its shaft and a winding drum that slides to engage a ratchet clutch winds a cord connected to the carriage, and the return key and braking keys control the motor and clutch, with a terminal stand and circuits for AC or DC supply.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** electric motor; ratchet clutch drum; winding drum; cord; carriage return key; braking keys; terminal stand; clutch mechanism
