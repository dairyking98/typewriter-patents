## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a Remington No. 6 style shift machine whose platen-carriage hangs on vibrating arms, the platen frame could swing loose when turned up for inspection.

**Solution:** A locking device that engages the vibratory links on the first lift of the platen carrier, in both lower and upper case positions, and also serves as a stop and rest for the carrier when upturned.

**How it works:** A non-shifting truck rides on a rear guide rod and is pulled by a spring drum and strap under escapement control. The platen carrier is mounted on vibratory arms or links connected to this truck. A locking device drops into engagement when the carrier begins to be lifted, whether in the lower-case or upper-case position, so the links cannot swing; when the carrier is turned down it is released. It also provides the stop and support for the carrier in its raised position.
