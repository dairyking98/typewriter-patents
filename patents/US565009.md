## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar movements need to be rapid, positive, easy and simple.

**Solution:** A head pivoted on the top of one rod is swung around by a second rod pivoted eccentrically to it, both driven by a single finger lever.

**How it works:** A small rod is pivoted to the base and carries at its top a head. A second rod is pivoted to the head eccentrically, and its lower eye loosely surrounds the first rod and slides along it. A lever pivoted on an upright has a finger disk on its outer end and its inner end joined to the second rod. Depressing the lever tilts the first rod up and lifts the second rod, so the head swings around to its working position, limited by a stop shoulder on the first rod. A spring to the base returns the lever. An inking pad on a standard and a platen are drawn in the figure.
