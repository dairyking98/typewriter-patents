## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The slide guides run smoothly, and springs at the ends of the support rod press the slides against the guide, so the rod can be fixed and turned in any position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The support rod for the ribbon-holder arms of a duplicating device is hard to move, it only sets at notches, and a tension spring does not clamp it tightly enough.

**Solution:** The slide guides run smoothly, and springs at the ends of the support rod press the slides against the guide, so the rod can be fixed and turned in any position.
