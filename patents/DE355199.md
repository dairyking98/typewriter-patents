## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine printing unit) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a further embodiment of this calculating machine, and although it relates mainly to the printing mechanism of the same.

**How it works (reading):** Independent levers carry the type wheels, and approaches on sheets shifted by the arithmetic sliders block the wheels that are not wanted; the crank reciprocates so that the impression is made only during the first phase of its movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Each type wheel in the calculator printing unit must print only when its digit is needed, and the print must happen once per cycle of the crank.

**Solution:** Independent levers carry the type wheels, and approaches on sheets shifted by the arithmetic sliders block the wheels that are not wanted; the crank reciprocates so that the impression is made only during the first phase of its movement.
