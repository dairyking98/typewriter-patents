## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** The foot of the machine is placed between the casing and the table with rubber or felt dampers, so the cladding is isolated from the base plate vibrations.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machine working noise passes into the outer cladding and makes it resonate.

**Solution:** The foot of the machine is placed between the casing and the table with rubber or felt dampers, so the cladding is isolated from the base plate vibrations.
