**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a character printing device for computing machines in which for sensing the positive and negative sign position of the sign carrier have the same step stucco is used, and consists in the fact that the swivel arm adjusting the character carrier with one barrel on the same axis, with bearings;

**How it works (reading):** A step piece only pivots to select the positive or negative group of characters, and a pawl on the swivel arm sets the sign position from the counter's subtraction slider.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A calculator needs to print a plus or a minus sign from a counter, and the usual step piece both pivots and slides, which is complicated.

**Solution:** A step piece only pivots to select the positive or negative group of characters, and a pawl on the swivel arm sets the sign position from the counter's subtraction slider.
