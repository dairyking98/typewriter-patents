## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists essentially in that the gear itself which is in direct engagement with the carriage rack is still presented to the circuit engagement of a counter-rotating pawl that can be moved from the downshift key.

**How it works (reading):** A downshift key lever slides a rail whose counter-rotating pawl engages the gear that meshes with the carriage rack and turns it backward by a set amount; the pawl has two noses so a low-power drive can be used.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters that have covert (backspace-type) correction work need a way to move the paper carriage back one step at a time, and no simple add-on backshift key existed for this.

**Solution:** A downshift key lever slides a rail whose counter-rotating pawl engages the gear that meshes with the carriage rack and turns it backward by a set amount; the pawl has two noses so a low-power drive can be used.
