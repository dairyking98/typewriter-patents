## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The two-pawl escapement of the companion application needs a way to hold the pawls in the chosen regular or speed adjustment.

**Solution:** An adjustable stop member on the escapement rocker, set by a screw, limits pawl travel and selects between regular and speed escapement.

**How it works:** A shaft with a carriage pinion and an escapement wheel is carried in ball bearings in a hanger. A rocker carries a stationary pawl and a pivoted pawl that alternately engage the wheel. The movable pawl pivots to either side of the other and is held by a locking pin and leaf spring. A pivoted stop member, positioned by a screw and spring-linked to the rocker, limits rocker swing and can hold the movable pawl out of engagement.

**Parts:** hanger; escapement shaft; pinion; escapement wheel; rocker; stationary pawl; pivoted pawl; locking pin; stop member; adjusting screw; ball bearing
