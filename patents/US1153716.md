## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column tabulation must be selected by keys that stop the carriage at column positions.

**Solution:** Column selecting keys release the carriage, and column selecting stops set on the carriage arrest it at the chosen column in the tabulating mechanism.
