## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two sheets and a copying belt need to be fed through a typewriter with a copy belt that travels with the platen.

**Solution:** A collapsible endless copying belt support with adjustable tension, optional backward paper feed, and a paper holder and guide is an attachment on the carriage.
