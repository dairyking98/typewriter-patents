## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Types become clogged with ink, and manual cleaning is slow.

**Solution:** An automatic brush is driven from the carriage rack through a linkage and a shaft with crank, and the rack can be disengaged.

**How it works:** Brush driven from the carriage rack by a crank shaft, disengageable by lever.
