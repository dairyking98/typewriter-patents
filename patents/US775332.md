## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A cylinder scale next to the platen rubs and blurs the paper when the platen shifts between cases.

**Solution:** The scale is pivoted on the stationary segment and sprung toward the platen, with a toe and stop limiting travel, so it stays put when the platen shifts.

**How it works:** Two graduated scales sit either side of the type-bar guide, each on a pintle carried by lugs on a bracket on the segment. A coiled spring on each pintle presses the scale rearward toward the platen, transverse to the shift movement. A toe on the bracket meets a fixed stop, setting the scale edge close to but off the paper. The scale can yield forward to admit paper. Graduations are letter-spaces.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** segment; type-bar guide; platen; scale; pintle; bracket; spring; toe; stop
