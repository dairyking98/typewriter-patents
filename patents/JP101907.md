## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Giving the paper cylinder two distinct feed rates (for example one tooth or two teeth) without the rates ever mixing was done by complicated mechanisms usable only on particular machines.

**Solution:** An adjustable inclined plate in the path of the spring-loaded feed pawl holds the pawl off the rack during part of its stroke, so that the pawl feeds either one tooth or its full stroke of teeth depending on the plate's set position.

**How it works:** A pawl piece pivoted on a swing lever moves back and forth between fixed stops, sprung toward the rack teeth of the cylinder. Pressing a print or feed key swings the lever for the idle stroke and releasing it lets a spring drive the working stroke. A sprung inclined plate, set by an adjusting piece, lies in the path of a lug on the pawl piece. With the plate in one position the lug is pressed down during the idle stroke, then freed at its end, so the pawl engages and feeds the full two teeth. With the plate in the other position the lug cannot pass the plate's far edge, so the pawl stays disengaged until near the end of the working stroke and feeds only one tooth.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Paper-cylinder feed device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a paper-cylinder feed device for a typewriter, characterised as follows. In the path of a feed pawl, which is moved back and forth through a set distance while sprung towards engagement with the feed teeth of the paper cylinder, is placed an inclined plate that, at the start of the pawl's idle stroke, presses the pawl away from the teeth and guides it. With the plate in a first position, the pawl meets the teeth only at the end of its working stroke, and feeds them by one tooth only; with the plate set in a second position, the pawl slips off the plate at the end of its idle stroke, engages the teeth, carries out its full working stroke and feeds the set number of teeth. The object is a simple, sure feed device in which the two feed rates are never mixed. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show one embodiment of the device: Fig. 1 a plan, Fig. 2 a front view of the main parts only, Fig. 3 a side view. Detailed description.
