## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line counters

**How it works (reading):** A gear on the paper roller shaft turns a toothed number wheel (about 84 teeth, roughly 3:1 to the roller gear) on a spring-loaded lever; a retaining pin inserted through a numbered hole stops against a fixed stop at the chosen line count.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier line-number stops on typewriters move in steps and cannot be set exactly to the number of lines to be written or read the remaining lines near the page end.

**Solution:** A gear on the paper roller shaft turns a toothed number wheel (about 84 teeth, roughly 3:1 to the roller gear) on a spring-loaded lever; a retaining pin inserted through a numbered hole stops against a fixed stop at the chosen line count.

**Features:**
