## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the escapement ratchet to a different tooth pitch for a different type width takes too long because parts are fitted one by one.

**Solution:** The ratchet wheel and its ball bearing are made as one interchangeable unit that is removed and replaced whole.
