## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector) · **Subject (patent classes):** Combinations of writing implements with other articles with hand tools, e.g. erasing knives with cheque protectors

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in check protectors and hasfor its principal object the construction of a small device of this character which may be carried on or about the person of a user.

**How it works (reading):** A small case holds a spring-mounted roller with cutting edges that cuts the check, and a pad of ink that stains if erasing chemicals are used, so tampering shows.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks can be tampered with by erasing the written amount, and a protector must be small and cheap enough to carry.

**Solution:** A small case holds a spring-mounted roller with cutting edges that cuts the check, and a pad of ink that stains if erasing chemicals are used, so tampering shows.
