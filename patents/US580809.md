## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring power for carriage advance must be strong and quick, and carriage and cylinder return is by hand.

**Solution:** Feed the carriage positively and directly by the keys through a toggle, pawl and pinion, and use a reversed, weaker spring to return the carriage and turn the cylinder automatically.

**How it works:** Any depressed key lever acts on a stirrup bar hung by wires from toggle arms, which straighten and drive a sliding bar. A pawl on this bar turns a notched wheel and pinion that move a rack on the carriage one step. The spring sits on the opposite side and only returns the carriage after the line, with toggle-arm gears that can disconnect, and a mechanism turns the cylinder for a new line.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** key lever; stirrup bar; toggle arms; sliding bar; pawl; notched wheel; pinion; rack; return spring; cylinder
