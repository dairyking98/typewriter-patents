## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Carriage travel meets too much resistance, and the carriage is not firmly guided along its path.

**Solution:** A guide rod with inclined bearing faces and a rack in its underside guides the carriage with pinions, so the carriage runs with little resistance and firm guidance.
