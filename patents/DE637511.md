## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements, e.g. hinges, for mounting sewing-machine casings to frames, stands, or tables

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a lowering device for sewing or typewriters, in which the parallel to self-retractable support plate of the machine on the two narrow sides in Guides slides and means to support the lifting work or to dampen the Lowering movement are provided and in particular represents a further development of the device according to patent 6i4 o64.

**How it works (reading):** A spring attached to a hinged end plate pushes it against the retracted support plate, which also helps lift the machine into working position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A retractable sewing or typewriter support plate needs front end plates to hold it down, and the lifting work is hard.

**Solution:** A spring attached to a hinged end plate pushes it against the retracted support plate, which also helps lift the machine into working position.
