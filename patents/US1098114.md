## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal needs to be accurate and sure, with uniform feed, few parts and little to go wrong.

**Solution:** Two independent ratchet-driven reel shafts, each with a pair of pawls from the universal bar, switched by a hand shift bar or by a spring-hinged hub that triggers when the ribbon runs out.

**How it works:** Two vertical aligned shafts carry the ribbon reels, each with bevel pinion and ratchet wheel engaged by a pair of pawls pivoted to the universal bar. L-shaped levers lift one pair of pawls clear while the other pair engages, set by a longitudinal shift bar the operator can move; a spring plunger holds the bar in place. Each reel hub has a recess spanned by a hinged section held closed by the ribbon. When a reel empties, the spring opens the hinged section and an L-shaped arm with a wheel running on the hub moves the shift bar, reversing feed.
