## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**How it works (reading):** A soft rubber intermediate layer with staggered hollow cone or pyramid recesses on both faces forms closed air spaces between the roller shell and core, damping sound without resonance.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type impact noise is loud on the platen roller, and earlier sound-damping layers were ineffective because they resonated or had too much contact area.

**Solution:** A soft rubber intermediate layer with staggered hollow cone or pyramid recesses on both faces forms closed air spaces between the roller shell and core, damping sound without resonance.
