## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register with two printing units) · **Subject (patent classes):** Transfer mechanisms, e.g. transfer of a figure from a ten-key keyboard into the pin carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register in which the respective amount setting is displayed on two sides, by means of two different printing units on a control strip as well as an inserted receipt is printed and finally summed up in an adder, which in an oscillatable The frame is stored and coupled to the adjusting members at the beginning of the checkout process will.

**How it works (reading):** Adjusting members, displays, two printing units and the adder sit in one oscillating frame that is lowered by an eccentric disk so both prints are made in one movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cash registers need a compact, cheap design that prints both the control strip and the receipt, and both amount displays, safely.

**Solution:** Adjusting members, displays, two printing units and the adder sit in one oscillating frame that is lowered by an eccentric disk so both prints are made in one movement.
