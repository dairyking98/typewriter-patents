## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike typebars need a convenient way to shift between upper and lower case and to convey key action to the bars.

**Solution:** The whole typebar frame is pivoted and rocked between two adjustable stops to bring upper or lower case type to the print line, with flexing links from the key levers.

**How it works:** The typebars sit in a frame pivoted near the machine front. A rear extension of the frame moves between two stops with adjusting screws that set the shift distance. Each typebar connects by a link to a rock lever with a cam, the link and lever flexing at a pivot, and a curved rod under the key levers actuates them.

**Features:** [SHIFT:rotary](../features/shift-rotary.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** type-bar; type frame; stop; adjusting screw; link; rock lever; key lever
