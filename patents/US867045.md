## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Impact of the keys on the platen is loud and the platen cylinder needs more strength and durability.

**Solution:** A cylinder with flanges at its ends and spaced longitudinal ribs, with a resilient endless belt over the ribs and an idler roller, so the key strike meets a cushioned surface; the flanges have air vents.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
