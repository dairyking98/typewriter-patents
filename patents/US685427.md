## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The earlier tape-controlled actuator was tied to the typewriter by a cord connection, which the inventor wanted to remove.

**Solution:** An automatic actuating mechanism operates a key-operated machine from a perforated paper tape, with no cord connection between the machine and the controlling mechanism.

**How it works:** A perforated tape, made locally or sent telegraphically, drives the keys of the machine through the automatic controller.

**Features:** [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** perforated tape; automatic controller

**Numbers:** US 685,427; 1901-10-29; prior patent 638,591
