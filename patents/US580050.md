## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage spacing on a spring-driven typewriter needs a variable (differential) step per key, and the paper roll must be fed on carriage return.

**Solution:** A spring-wound ratchet wheel is released by key levers and stopped by a per-key arm spacing, so each key advances the carriage by a different number of teeth; a returning carriage also turns the paper roll through an incline-and-roller linkage.

**How it works:** The carriage rack drives a ratchet wheel that contains a helical spring. Depressing a key raises a stepping lever which engages two pawls and disengages the retaining pawl. On key release the wheel turns until a lever arm is stopped by the key's own stop arm, so the tooth count depends on the spacing between the arms. A separate roller and adjustable incline turn the paper roll only when the carriage is pushed back, and a rotating handle can lift the roller to suppress line feed.
