## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Small forms and carbon sets shift in the typewriter when written on, especially at the lower lines.

**Solution:** A simpler pad has one pocket cut across its width that holds carbon sheets and the card together while the pad is held in the platen.
