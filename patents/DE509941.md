## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a book holder, especially for typewriters, with a table top that is rotatably arranged on pivotable support arms.

**How it works (reading):** Pawls on toothed segments are released together by a two-armed lever linked to release shafts so the table top and arms can be locked in position by one operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Table top and support arms of a book holder need a finer and simpler locking adjustment than a sector disc with retaining pins.

**Solution:** Pawls on toothed segments are released together by a two-armed lever linked to release shafts so the table top and arms can be locked in position by one operation.
