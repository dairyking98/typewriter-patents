## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage stops for column and tabular work are hard to set on a typewriter carriage.

**Solution:** Adjustable vibratory abutment column-stops engage a stop-rod on the carriage so it halts at chosen columns.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
