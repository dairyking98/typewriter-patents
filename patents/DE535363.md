## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Straightening projections on a type body interfere with printing, and grinding them off after soldering is costly. Notches cut into the type body are hard to make in the embossing die.

**Solution:** Directional projections are placed in a recess of the type body, and the embossing die has a matching elevation with milled depressions. The alignment projections sit in the recess and do not reach the printed impression.

**How it works:** The die elevation that forms the recess carries depressions that emboss the alignment projections, so the type body can be seated accurately on the type lever at a constant distance from the bearing axis.
