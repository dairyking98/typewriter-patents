## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Split-order billing needs multiple sheets with carbon held and fed together around the platen.

**Solution:** A clamp holds the rear or bottom edges of the stacked order sheets and yields as they feed around the platen, so each sub-order can be removed in turn.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
