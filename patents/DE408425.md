## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Holder for the paper roll of stenography machines. The invention relates to a holder for the paper roll of stenography machines, consisting of made of fixed and movable arms under the action of closing springs with slots running at an angle to each other for mounting the roller axis. According to of the invention, the movable arms are connected by a rail on which Closing springs work. This simplifies handling, since only one hand is required to open the holder and insert the paper roll. In addition, the closing spring brakes the roller axis so that the paper strip is always kept taut. On an axis i, which rests with pins 2 in bearings 3 in the machine frame 4, there are fixed arms 5, at the free ends of which longitudinal slots 6 are attached.
