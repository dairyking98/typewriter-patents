## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In front-strike machines the type can strike the edge of the ribbon, which causes imperfect impressions.

**Solution:** Ribbon guides feed the ribbon horizontally across the machine, so the ribbon moves longitudinally past the type, and the type no longer meets the ribbon edge.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon guides; frisket
