## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text read in this excerpt does not state the defect in detail; the carriage needs a retarding device acting on its travel.

**Solution:** Carriage retarding devices, with carriage-mounted guides running on balls or rollers in channels in fixed rails, slow the carriage through the mechanism of a Monarch machine, with a lever that engages part of the retarder with the carriage.
