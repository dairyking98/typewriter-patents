## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Special means, e.g. repulsers, for ensuring return of type- levers

**How it works (reading):** Flexible springs wound on a bolt in the tie rod engage a ring-shaped impact rail at the end of travel, so the spring is loaded through the drive link.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever springs that contact the lever hinder penetration power.

**Solution:** Flexible springs wound on a bolt in the tie rod engage a ring-shaped impact rail at the end of travel, so the spring is loaded through the drive link.
