## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Liquid such as ink or paste must be evenly distributed over a roller or periphery before contacting a printing surface.

**Solution:** A pivoted arm and adjustable banking stop on a table that spreads liquid from an ink well onto a roller or moistening member for an even distribution.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [Ink disc](../features/ink-disc.md)
