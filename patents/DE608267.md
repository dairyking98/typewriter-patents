## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing unit)

**How it works (reading):** The type wheels are placed in a rigid frame with their adjusting and drive members, and the print hammer and control-strip paper rolls are in a separate frame; both are inserted vertically into the machine, one above the other.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In cash registers the printing unit sits on the side in a cover-mounted frame, so its attachments are needed and the housing is awkward to build.

**Solution:** The type wheels are placed in a rigid frame with their adjusting and drive members, and the print hammer and control-strip paper rolls are in a separate frame; both are inserted vertically into the machine, one above the other.
