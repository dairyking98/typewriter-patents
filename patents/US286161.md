## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inked ribbon sags and moves when lifted by the type, giving unclean impressions.

**Solution:** A spring and adjusting screw on the ribbon-spool shaft apply friction tension so the ribbon stays horizontal between the spools.

**How it works:** The spool shaft turns in bearings under the top plate and is driven by a bevel pinion at one end. A screw with a plain cylindrical body larger than the shaft replaces the old flat-head screw at the other end. A spiral spring around the body is compressed between the screw head and the shaft bearing, creating friction that retards the shaft. The cylindrical body also stops the shaft end-play, and the screw's depth sets the tension, which must stay low so it does not overload the carriage spring.

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** spool shaft; ribbon spool; bevel pinion; screw; spiral spring; bearing

**Improves on:**

- flat-head screw end bearing on ribbon spool shaft: spring-loaded adjustable tension plus a positive end-play stop
