## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the double-case Remington with a shifting paper roll, characters can only be printed on the line or fully shifted, so fractions and out-of-line characters cannot be typed.

**Solution:** An extra fraction key pivoted to the frame presses the shift lever part way, moving the paper roll less than its full shift.

**How it works:** The shift key lever normally swings an elbow lever that moves the pivoted sliding roll frame to upper case. A supplementary key, pivoted to a bar or clamped to the frame, straddles the shift lever and rests on top of it. Pressing it moves the shift lever only as far as an adjustable set screw or stop allows, so the roll rests between lower and upper case positions and either character of a type prints out of line. A spring returns the roll frame.
