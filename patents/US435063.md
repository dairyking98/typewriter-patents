## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in check-punches, and has for its object to provide a machine of this description which shall be simple in its c011- struetion and operation and inexpensive to manufacture;

**How it works (reading):** A novel punch with operating mechanism, plus feed mechanism that holds the check and automatically advances it one step after each punching for correct spacing of the characters.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check-punches need to be simple in construction and operation and inexpensive to make, and the check must be held and fed to the cutting mechanism.

**Solution:** A novel punch with operating mechanism, plus feed mechanism that holds the check and automatically advances it one step after each punching for correct spacing of the characters.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
