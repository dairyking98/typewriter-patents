## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to condensed record or billing attachments therefor, and theobject of my invention is to provide a simple, cheap and efficient device of the character specified.

**How it works (reading):** A pawl on the line-spacing lever cooperates with a combined friction plate and adjustable stop on the platen frame, with a billing device, attachable to a No. 6 Remington without altering the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Condensed record and billing work needs the platen to be positioned by a friction plate and adjustable stop, which existing line spacing does not provide.

**Solution:** A pawl on the line-spacing lever cooperates with a combined friction plate and adjustable stop on the platen frame, with a billing device, attachable to a No. 6 Remington without altering the machine.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
