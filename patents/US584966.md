## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operator has to move the carriage back for each line.

**Solution:** The cylinder revolves after each character and shifts laterally for each line.

**How it works:** Rod linked to keys controls spacing; cylinder rotates with adjustable lateral movement of two or three spaces; a bell hammer sounds.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** vertical rod; cylinder; paper clamp; rocker bar; bell
