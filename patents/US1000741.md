## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**How it works (reading):** Magnets and rock shafts, controlled by perforated jacquard strips, selectively operate the typewriter levers in sequence.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Form letters that differ only in an address must be produced automatically.

**Solution:** Magnets and rock shafts, controlled by perforated jacquard strips, selectively operate the typewriter levers in sequence.
