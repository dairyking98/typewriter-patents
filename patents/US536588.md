## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Frequently used words and phrases take many strokes to type.

**Solution:** Extra keys and typebars carrying whole words or phrases, with automatic spacing matching the word length.

**How it works:** A bank of additional keys sits above the usual keys on levers pivoted at the rear. Rods connect each lever to a typebar carrying a word or phrase, arranged in the circular basket opening. The key levers rest on a transverse spacing rod tied to the ordinary space lever, so pressing a word key also trips the spacing mechanism. Sliding spacing dogs, chosen by the key, make the carriage advance the number of spaces equal to the word.

**Parts:** additional key; key lever; typebar; connecting rod; spacing rod; release shaft; sliding spacing dog; space bar
