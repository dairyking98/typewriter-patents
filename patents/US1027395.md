## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of cutting devices cutting perpendicular to the direction of paper feed

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide a device wherein the necessity of inserting an additional sheet each time a letter or paper is to be written, can be avoided.

**How it works (reading):** A frame carries a paper roll fed over the apron to the platen, with a gage-controlled shear that severs the sheet at set lengths.
