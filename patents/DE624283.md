## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Auxiliary copy ribbons can be pulled or shifted when the continuous forms are line-advanced, and the operations must be done in the right order.

**Solution:** The line-feed drive lever is linked to the friction abutment and auxiliary ribbon circuit so the form advances only after the platen has moved back and the ribbon switching is done, with a cutting device on the line-index rollers.
