## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reading rulers on manuscript holders obstruct the view and create a visual stop point, and their pressure rollers are hard to load.

**Solution:** A single sharp-edged brass sheet forms a roller shield, a reading ruler with scale, and the bearing bracket for the pressure roller, pressed by leaf springs onto the drive roller.
