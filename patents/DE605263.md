## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control strip switching cash register) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**How it works (reading):** Rotating a fixed disc that carries the paper rolls switches the strip between display and print position with only a short loop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Control-strip switching by rollers wastes paper and tears the strip under strong pulls.

**Solution:** Rotating a fixed disc that carries the paper rolls switches the strip between display and print position with only a short loop.
