## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**How it works (reading):** Angle levers guide the rail ends in parallel, and an oscillating shaft placed below the pivots carries a middle arm, so the rail is supported at three points.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A curved universal rail for switching the type-lever bearing carrier had no fixed support near its middle, where the mechanism had to stay clear.

**Solution:** Angle levers guide the rail ends in parallel, and an oscillating shaft placed below the pivots carries a middle arm, so the rail is supported at three points.
