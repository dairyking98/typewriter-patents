## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforating writing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention are to prod uce an effective protection against the fraudulent alteration of checks, drafts, and other-instruments and to produce a simple and practical perforating writing-machine;

**How it works (reading):** A punch carrier on a rocker shaft carries punches with crossbar heads, guided in a registering slot, and a punch-colouring mechanism inks the holes.
