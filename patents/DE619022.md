## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**How it works (reading):** An angle lever linked to the switch bracket engages the switching element and moves it back to rest under the carriage spring tension, so the pawl does not slide across the teeth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Returning the paper carriage switch parts after each keystroke with a wedge pawl rubbing on the teeth causes friction and jamming.

**Solution:** An angle lever linked to the switch bracket engages the switching element and moves it back to rest under the carriage spring tension, so the pawl does not slide across the teeth.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper carriage switching device on typewriters The invention concerns a paper: sledge switch device on typewriters with through the Type lever moving switch bracket. It has been suggested to bring back the keystroke on every keystroke moving parts of the typewriter, such as switch bracket, switch lock, etc., and a wedge-shaped pawl to return the struck type lever to be used, which is moved perpendicular to the direction of movement of the shift teeth. These Arrangement causes frictional and jamming resistance between the pawl and Gear tooth. The invention overcomes these shortcomings. What is aimed for is achieved The aim is that a lever linkage connected on the one hand to the switch bracket with its opposite end in the path of the switching element (ratchet wheel, rack or some other part influenced by the wagon tension spring b) and brought by the under the influence of the released when moving the switch bracket and constantly under Effect of the wagon tension spring standing switching element moved back into its rest position will. The lever linkage is advantageously designed as an angle lever, its one arm is articulated on the switch bracket…
