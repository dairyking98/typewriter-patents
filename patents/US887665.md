## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** The primary object of the invention is to provide' a compact device for this purpose which may be carried in a persons pocket and which will automatically be checked so that any: number of number perforations may be made in the check.
