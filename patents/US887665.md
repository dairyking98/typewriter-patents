## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (pocket check puncher) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** The primary object of the invention is to provide' a compact device for this purpose which may be carried in a persons pocket and which will automatically be checked so that any: number of number perforations may be made in the check.

**How it works (reading):** A hand-held device has a character wheel in a pocket, set by a finger-operated member; a pivoted handle forces the check against the wheel to punch it, and the check is fed on the handle's return.
