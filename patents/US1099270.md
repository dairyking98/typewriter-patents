## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Heavy type bars jolt the fingers on the keys, and the type bars must lie close together in the basket.

**Solution:** Key downward motion is transmitted to the forward and backward type-bar motion through a linkage that cushions the key stroke and lets the types sit close together.
