## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A visitor to a closed office has no safe, private way to leave a name or message; loose pen and paper is exposed, limited in space and can be read or destroyed.

**Solution:** A door-mounted recorder where a pointer knob over a letter dial turns an internal type wheel and a finger-rest crank prints each letter on a roll inside the door.

**How it works:** On the outside of the door a knob turns a spindle with a pointer over a character dial; the spindle passes through the door to a type wheel, or sector of one, so the chosen character meets a fixed striking spot on an impressing roll. A second spindle carries a crank outside, linked to a rod with a finger rest that is moved up and down, and a crank inside, to carry the print motion through the door. The record stays hidden inside the door.

- writing materials left near a closed door: private, durable, tamper-resistant record
