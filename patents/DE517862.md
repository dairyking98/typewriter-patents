## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register counting device)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a counting and switching device for those cash registers where each of the individual amounts is on the same control card are printed that correspond to the various purchases made by the same customer, and at the same time the sum of these amounts is printed on the same card is recorded.

**How it works (reading):** Toothed wheels under each key row change by the pressed key value; a deep direct engagement with longer lever arms gives a simpler printing table that fits under the existing cash register.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier cash register control-card printers were intricate and fault-prone, and their levers were too short.

**Solution:** Toothed wheels under each key row change by the pressed key value; a deep direct engagement with longer lever arms gives a simpler printing table that fits under the existing cash register.

**Features:**

- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** toothed wheel per key row; thrust washers; printing table
