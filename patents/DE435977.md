## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper pressure rollers on a square rod cannot be set to press evenly, and adjusting the rod is slow.

**Solution:** A prismatic socket with play on the rod, a leaf spring, and a sleeve carrying the rubber roller lets each roller self-level on the platen.
