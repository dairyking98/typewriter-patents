## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculating-machine carriages need a counter for printed items that resets automatically, and it should attach to existing machines.

**Solution:** An indicating mechanism on the carriage counts the items printed, operates regardless of line spacing, and resets automatically.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
