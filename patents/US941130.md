## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements'in ribbon feed mechanism for stamps, type writers and the like, and is applicable to any printing or stamping device in which an inked ribbonis arrangedto travel across or cooperate with the face of the type or other impression means, the ribbon being from one spool or reel upon another.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon feed that advances at each print and reverses when a predetermined amount has wound onto one spool.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stamps and typewriters need a ribbon that moves on at each impression and reverses automatically.

**Solution:** A ribbon feed that advances at each print and reverses when a predetermined amount has wound onto one spool.

**How it works:** The ribbon winds between two reels with knurled shaft ends on either side of the impression wheels. Each printing stroke advances the ribbon, and the direction of travel reverses automatically at a set extent of winding. Shown on an electrically operated time stamp whose head moves toward and away from the platen.
