## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper guides for typewriters. The invention relates to a paper guide for typewriters. With many typewriters, the letterhead is above the print line from a hinged, mounted in swivel arms and resiliently against the paper roller pressed metal rail. The metal rail has the disadvantage that a part of the writing and, in addition, a cast shadow is cast on the writing when there is light becomes what not only reading, but also adding superimposed numbers make it difficult and a continuous lifting of the rail makes necessary. The listed disadvantages prevent brisk work. These disadvantages are eliminated by the present invention in that that a transparent rail is used instead of the metal paper holding rail which are made of glass or other suitable material can. The curvature of the holding rail is adapted to the circumference of the platen, whereby a clear show through of the writing is achieved. The glass rail is connected to the Ends stored in shoes which are provided with a threaded pin; she is attached to swivel arms in the same way as the metal rail.
