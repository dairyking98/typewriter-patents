## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a closed paper trolley switchgear for typewriters, which is connected to a Slide is firmly connected, which is introduced into the machine and through a with lobed spring in holding machine.

**How it works (reading):** The switchgear is mounted on a slide running in grooves in the frame and held in use by a spring; the pawls and derailleur arms sit so they need not be disconnected, letting the whole unit be pulled out and reinserted quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Removing the paper carriage switchgear for cleaning or repair means loosening many screws, and the pawls and arms tied to the drive levers must be disconnected first.

**Solution:** The switchgear is mounted on a slide running in grooves in the frame and held in use by a spring; the pawls and derailleur arms sit so they need not be disconnected, letting the whole unit be pulled out and reinserted quickly.
