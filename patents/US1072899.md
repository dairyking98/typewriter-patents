## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon vibrator control to print from the upper or lower edge of the ribbon was complicated and did not work in both platen-shift positions.

**Solution:** A bell crank and slotted lever pair give a selectable ribbon vibrator throw that works in either platen position and has a stencil position.

**How it works:** The ribbon passes over fixed guides to a vibrator with a stem. A stud on the stem is moved by a slotted bell crank and slotted lever, the latter linked to a crank on the escapement rock shaft, so key action lifts the vibrator. Setting the slots with a pin alters the lift for the upper or lower ribbon edge, or for stencil work no lift. The mechanism is shown on a No. 10 Remington.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; bell crank; slotted lever; link; rock shaft; universal bar; shift rail

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 10: new simplified ribbon control
