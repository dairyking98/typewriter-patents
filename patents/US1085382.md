## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices actuated when platen reaches the end of a line

**How it works (reading):** A center stop moves transversely on a toothed rack behind the platen so it engages the margin stops, with margin stops using pivoted spring latches that snap into the rack teeth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin and tabulation stops on the carriage need a reliable line-lock arrangement.

**Solution:** A center stop moves transversely on a toothed rack behind the platen so it engages the margin stops, with margin stops using pivoted spring latches that snap into the rack teeth.
