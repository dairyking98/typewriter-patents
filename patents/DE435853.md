## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier key drive for a typewriter needed too many parts to transmit the depressed key motion to the type.

**Solution:** A frame that swings on axles under the keyboard is pulled by a spring and driven by one or two electromagnets placed at the back of the machine, with pawls on a comb that engage the key levers, so the key motion is transmitted directly.
