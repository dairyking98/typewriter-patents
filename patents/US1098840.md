## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to rolls such as used for feeding the paper in typcwriting machines, and particularly to those which run upon a rotatable platen around Which the Work sheet is Wrapped or carried.

**How it works (reading):** A pressure roll with a yielding construction grips the work sheet reliably against the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Solid rubber feed rolls did not carry paper accurately around the platen and were hard to insert sheets into.

**Solution:** A pressure roll with a yielding construction grips the work sheet reliably against the platen.

**How it works:** Rolls are made of a more yielding construction than solid rubber so they carry the sheet around the platen.
