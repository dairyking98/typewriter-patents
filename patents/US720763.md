## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The springs pressing the paper-feed roller against the platen need a fine tension adjustment that does not creep and can be set independently at each end for even paper feed.

**Solution:** Each feed-roller spring is anchored to a worm wheel driven by a worm screw, so turning the screw sets the spring tension and self-locks.

**How it works:** A paper-feed roller is carried on pivoted links at the ends of a platen frame. A coiled spring around the frame's rear bar presses each link so the roller bears on the platen. The far end of each spring is fixed to a worm wheel in a housing screwed to the frame. A worm screw with a nicked head, held in a bearing on the housing, turns the worm wheel with a screwdriver and so changes the tension; the worm gearing resists creeping. A finger key on a forward arm lifts the roller and paper blade off the platen for paper insertion.
