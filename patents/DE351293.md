## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a modification of the patent 349016 marked contact arrangement for type cylinder typewriters with electrical Drive.

**How it works (reading):** The setting plate carrying the type designations is spring-pressed by the setting pin and acts as a contactor that closes the drive circuit; contact opens when the pin's action ceases.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The single contact point of the main patent's electric drive sits on the moving pin mechanism, not on the character plate.

**Solution:** The setting plate carrying the type designations is spring-pressed by the setting pin and acts as a contactor that closes the drive circuit; contact opens when the pin's action ceases.
