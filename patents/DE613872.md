## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing adding machine with typewriter)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing adding machine with a typewriter next to it and a paper carriage shared by both machines.

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter and printing adder share one paper carriage, with removable visible counters in front of the platen spaced at the typewriter step and set by a button field.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Combined typewriter and adding machines were complicated and their counters were not visible, so the writing and arithmetic could not be controlled.

**Solution:** A typewriter and printing adder share one paper carriage, with removable visible counters in front of the platen spaced at the typewriter step and set by a button field.
