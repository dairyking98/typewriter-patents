## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**How it works (reading):** Coupling teeth on the platen and the switching member are inclined so they slide into engagement and reach full depth only at the end of the shift, letting the parts self-seat in gaps when slightly misaligned.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A separable line-spacing coupling must be secure and accurate, but tooth-tip clutches wear and misalign.

**Solution:** Coupling teeth on the platen and the switching member are inclined so they slide into engagement and reach full depth only at the end of the shift, letting the parts self-seat in gaps when slightly misaligned.
