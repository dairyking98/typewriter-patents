## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Drawers or movable shelves coupled to doors

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to one at a time Storage cabinet serving as a work table for heavy office or household machines or devices and aims to train the cabinet so that the to be kept in the cabinet Machine in the most convenient and simple way both in its working position and can also be brought into their rest position.

**How it works (reading):** Drive rods attached to the lid run in vertical slots in sliding guides, and a pair of levers in a Nuremberg scissors arrangement gives the leverage to lower and raise a heavy table.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Storage cabinets with lowering work tables cannot lower heavy office machines because their levers act directly from the lid and give too little stroke.

**Solution:** Drive rods attached to the lid run in vertical slots in sliding guides, and a pair of levers in a Nuremberg scissors arrangement gives the leverage to lower and raise a heavy table.
