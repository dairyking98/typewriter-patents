## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Center guides for typebars are heavy and deflect when the shift frame moves.

**Solution:** A light, inexpensive center guide fixed to the main frame, with parts on the shift frame to prevent deflection, lets type heads be bent at an angle to the bars.

**How it works:** Each typebar has a guide lug that enters the center guide near the print point. The guide is mounted on the main frame rather than the shift frame and the shift frame carries members preventing deflection. Bending the type heads above the guide lets the bars pivot on a larger-radius segment without lengthening them and lowers twisting strain on the bar bearings.
