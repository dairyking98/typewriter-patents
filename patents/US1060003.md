## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more-particularly to ball bearings for the typebars of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A single row of balls runs in a race between the type bar and its hanger; one bearing cone is adjusted to remove looseness, and a detent between the cone and its mating part holds it without adding space.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bar bearings needed to be compact, rigid and cheap to make, and to take up wear without an exposed securing device.

**Solution:** A single row of balls runs in a race between the type bar and its hanger; one bearing cone is adjusted to remove looseness, and a detent between the cone and its mating part holds it without adding space.
