## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stereotype matrix made with typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Forme preparation by embossing, e.g. with a typewriter

**How it works (reading):** Uses a shuttle typewriter whose letter-bearing shuttle is swapped easily for another font, with an electrically driven hammer that strikes the paper to a uniform depth set by a stop independent of the operator's strength.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stereotype matrices need even impression depth and several fonts, which a hammer typewriter cannot give because it is tied to one font and its hammer face bottoms out unevenly.

**Solution:** Uses a shuttle typewriter whose letter-bearing shuttle is swapped easily for another font, with an electrically driven hammer that strikes the paper to a uniform depth set by a stop independent of the operator's strength.
