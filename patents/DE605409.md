**How it works (reading):** A foot pedal drives a rod and cam that lift the roller smoothly, and the device fits on existing machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting the paper roller by hand on calculators is tiring and slow when many lines are set.

**Solution:** A foot pedal drives a rod and cam that lift the roller smoothly, and the device fits on existing machines.
