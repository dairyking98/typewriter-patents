## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter table top that folds down needs the machine support plate to rise fast and then settle slowly.

**Solution:** A pin on the lever runs in a curved slot in the support plate; the slot shape gives an accelerated lift, then a slow final lift into use position.
