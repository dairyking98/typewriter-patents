## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide a simple mechanism of the above-indicated class in which a single operating element or shaft is employed for governing the action of two sets of pressure-rollers and in which provision is made for a ready adjustment of the pressure with which said rollers bear against the platen and for a quick and positive release of the paper from said rollers when desired.

**How it works (reading):** A single operating shaft controls two sets of pressure rollers bearing on the platen, with an adjustment for the roller pressure and a fast release that lifts the rollers off the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-guide pressure rollers needed a simpler way to set their pressure on the platen and a quick, positive release of the paper.

**Solution:** A single operating shaft controls two sets of pressure rollers bearing on the platen, with an adjustment for the roller pressure and a fast release that lifts the rollers off the paper.
