## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved arrangement which will enable the position of the carriage to be adjusted in a direction at right angles to the track so as to compensate for the thickness of variable numbers of copies to be typed and insure a uniformly straight blow of the type on the paper.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage and paper roller recede as a unit, with the rear rail carried on pivoted arms and adjusted at right angles to the track, so the paper is moved back an appropriate distance for the thickness.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A thick stack of paper or many copies makes the type strike at an unsquare, non-uniform blow on a front-action machine, because the type lever stroke is fixed.

**Solution:** The carriage and paper roller recede as a unit, with the rear rail carried on pivoted arms and adjusted at right angles to the track, so the paper is moved back an appropriate distance for the thickness.
