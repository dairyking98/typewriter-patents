## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the external periphery of the wound ribbon

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to automatic ribbon switching for electrically operated typewriters and accounting machines, in which the movements the type lever is derived from a continuously rotating toothed drive shaft will.

**How it works (reading):** A shift tooth on the switching lever engages the electrically driven toothed roller when the expired spool's feeler moves, and the drive switches the ribbon automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeler levers reverse the ribbon by frictional tightening, which strains the ribbon and makes switching jam under higher load.

**Solution:** A shift tooth on the switching lever engages the electrically driven toothed roller when the expired spool's feeler moves, and the drive switches the ribbon automatically.
