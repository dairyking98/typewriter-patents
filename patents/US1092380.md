## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a novel form of card holder or clamp applied t0 a front strike visible machine such, for instance, as the Royal Standard.

**How it works (reading):** A detachable card holding plate is mounted between the platen and the ribbon guide, holding the card at the printing point against the platen, with a card guide and a feed-roll lift that moves the scale to the print line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards cannot be printed close to their edges on a front-strike visible machine, because the card is not held steady at the printing point.

**Solution:** A detachable card holding plate is mounted between the platen and the ribbon guide, holding the card at the printing point against the platen, with a card guide and a feed-roll lift that moves the scale to the print line.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
