## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets entering a carriage can be crooked, so the advance margin is not squared to the platen axis.

**Solution:** A narrow left-hand margin guide attached to the paper shield squares the leading edge of each sheet as it is inserted.
