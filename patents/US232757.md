## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a new and improved paper-presser for type-writing machines, which carries and holds small pieces of paper, such as cards, envelopes, smallsized note-paper, 850.

**How it works (reading):** A loosely mounted presser roller on a spring-curved arm presses small paper against the printing cylinder so it is carried round to receive the impressions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Small sheets such as cards and envelopes cannot be held and carried around the platen by the rubber belts and feed rollers.

**Solution:** A loosely mounted presser roller on a spring-curved arm presses small paper against the printing cylinder so it is carried round to receive the impressions.
