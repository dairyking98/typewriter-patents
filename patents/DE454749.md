## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letter spacing for power typewriters needed a simple and reliable mechanism.

**Solution:** Two spring-loaded pawls driven by the type-lever drive rail alternately engage a grooved drive wheel and advance the carriage rack in two half steps.

**How it works:** The drive rail moves two pawl arms through a lever and links. A plate with a cam setting chooses which pawl engages the grooved wheel first, which sets forward or backward travel. The first half step is made at the rail's going stroke and the second at its return, turning a gear that meshes with the carriage rack.
