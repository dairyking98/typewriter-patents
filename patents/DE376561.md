**How it works (reading):** The support arm of the intermediate member pivots on the pawl carrier about an axis across the carrier's rotation, so the link can move freely and can also be switched off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Striking an accent key should not advance the carriage, but the known intermediate link slides on the pawl carrier, rubs and can jam.

**Solution:** The support arm of the intermediate member pivots on the pawl carrier about an axis across the carrier's rotation, so the link can move freely and can also be switched off.
