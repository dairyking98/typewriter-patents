## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Multiple web-feeding apparatus for webs superimposed during printing

**How it works (reading):** A long carbon sheet is wound in a roll and advanced at right angles to the paper, so fresh carbon comes under the printing point for each copy; the used portions are torn away.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Loose carbon sheets are awkward when several copies of long webs or sheets are needed on a flat-bed machine.

**Solution:** A long carbon sheet is wound in a roll and advanced at right angles to the paper, so fresh carbon comes under the printing point for each copy; the used portions are torn away.
