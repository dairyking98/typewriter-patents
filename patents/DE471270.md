## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on typewriters for writing of templates for addresses or the like, on devices of the type which consist of a frame-like holder, the two or more rollers for pressing of the upper part of the template against the platen and a guide for the having the lower end of the template.

**How it works (reading):** A one-piece frame with pins hooks into the paper pressure arms in place of the paper roller, and rollers press the template to the platen while the existing release lever lifts it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil writing needs a separate release device and holder suspension on the typewriter.

**Solution:** A one-piece frame with pins hooks into the paper pressure arms in place of the paper roller, and rollers press the template to the platen while the existing release lever lifts it.
