## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Erasers, pencils and other desk items are not within easy reach of the typist's hands.

**Solution:** A clamp-mounted holder with elastic curved strips grips erasers and pencils and is clamped to any convenient part of the typewriter or table.

**How it works:** A wooden block carries elastic metal holders on each side; a threaded bar draws a clamp plate against a fixed plate to fasten the device to the machine or table.
