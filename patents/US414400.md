## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to matrix making, and is in the nature of an improvement on the invention set forth in an application, Serial No.

**How it works (reading):** An electrically actuated depressing device replaces the cam, keeping the magnet-and-pawl feed and a circuit controller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The eccentric cam that drives the type-die into the matrix is replaced to change the impression.

**Solution:** An electrically actuated depressing device replaces the cam, keeping the magnet-and-pawl feed and a circuit controller.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
