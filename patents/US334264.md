## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punching machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cheque stamping machines

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to that class of devices which are known as check-punching machines, and which are employed for the purpose of punching out figures .

**How it works (reading):** A frame carries a row of vertically moving punches with springs that overhang a perforated plate, with a lever operating them to cut out the amount figures.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks could be altered after writing, so the amount needed to be cut out securely.

**Solution:** A frame carries a row of vertically moving punches with springs that overhang a perforated plate, with a lever operating them to cut out the amount figures.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
