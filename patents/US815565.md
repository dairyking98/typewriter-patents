## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The tension of the springs on the carrier or shaft needs simple adjusting, and the set position must be held.

**Solution:** The carrier shaft is fitted with a pawl-and-ratchet device that holds it at the adjusted position, and a collar fixed on the shaft locates it; the key-levers drive type-bars through sublevers and links.
