## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**How it works (reading):** One spool with closed discs, a set of holes and resilient wreaths fits axles of different diameters and driving pins at different spacings.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Many typewriter models need different ribbon spools, making production and storage heavy.

**Solution:** One spool with closed discs, a set of holes and resilient wreaths fits axles of different diameters and driving pins at different spacings.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** spool discs; holes; resilient wreaths; sleeve; drive pins
