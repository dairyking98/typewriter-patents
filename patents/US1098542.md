## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The point where type strikes the platen cannot be varied, so each type head cannot be given several printing positions.

**Solution:** A raise-and-lower mechanism of a vertically movable strip on which the carriage rides, operated by two finger keys, varies the platen height relative to the type bars.

**How it works:** The carriage rides by a bearing ball on a vertically movable strip beneath a fixed frame strip. A rock shaft with two arms lifts or lowers the strip through hinged rods with stop collars and guide pins. A raising lever with a key rocks the shaft to lift both ends of the strip equally; a lowering lever with a key pulls the rods down. A spring returns the strip to normal position when the key is released.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:impression-adjust](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** frame strip; vertically movable strip; bearing ball; rock shaft; arm; hinged rod; stop collar; raising lever; lowering lever; return spring; carriage; platen roller
