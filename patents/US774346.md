## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator of a typewriter cannot tell what line is being written or when the page bottom is reached.

**Solution:** A clamp-on line counter geared to the platen turns an index finger over a scale, with a cam that trips an alarm at a set line.

**How it works:** A frame clamps to the front bar of the Remington No. 6 platen frame. A spur gear on the platen head drives a spindle and bevel pinion, which turns a bevel gear and an index finger on a second spindle. The gear drives the spindle through a cup-spring friction clutch, so the finger can be reset by hand with a milled head. A cam disk on the spindle trips an alarm at a predetermined line.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** frame; clamp screw; spur gear; bevel pinion; bevel gear; spindle; cup spring; index finger; milled head; cam disk; line-space ratchet wheel
