## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sorting recording sheets into classes by hand is slow and wastes time in record keeping.

**Solution:** A holder keeps many recording sheets in classified order for use with book typewriters, so matter for one subject is kept together without refingering the whole stack.
