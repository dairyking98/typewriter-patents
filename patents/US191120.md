## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Where types strike upward against the underside of the platen, the writing is hidden from the operator.

**Solution:** Place a reflector between ribbon and paper so the printed line is seen mirrored in it.

**How it works:** An upward-facing reflecting plate is fixed under the paper cylinder and above the types, horizontal or inclined, with an opening for the types to pass. The printed letters show in it, inverted, as they are struck. Where a ribbon is used, the reflector sits between ribbon and paper and also shields the paper from smudging.

**Features:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** reflector; paper cylinder; ink ribbon; top plate
