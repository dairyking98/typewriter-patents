## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switchover device for the platen working at different heights, in which a releasable locking device is able to hold the switchover device in the respectively assumed writing position.

**How it works (reading):** The locking member is made adjustable on a rotatable arm, fixed by a small screw in a slot concentric with the arm pivot, so the arm sets the lock position without reworking the notched lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen shift lock must line up with the shift frame, and adjusting the notched lever to do this was slow trial and error.

**Solution:** The locking member is made adjustable on a rotatable arm, fixed by a small screw in a slot concentric with the arm pivot, so the arm sets the lock position without reworking the notched lever.
