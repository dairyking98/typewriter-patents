## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Multiple web-feeding apparatus for webs superimposed during printing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, but more particularly to that type which embodies a flat platen with which is associated the machine proper, disposed to travel over the platen and over the work supported thereon in aflat or spread-out condition.

**How it works (reading):** The platen carries webs or strips of work sheet and carbon sheet that advance intermittently over the writing surface, and written portions are severed from the web after advancing away from the printing area.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Loose work-sheets and carbon sheets cause delay and annoyance in billing and manifold work on a flat platen.

**Solution:** The platen carries webs or strips of work sheet and carbon sheet that advance intermittently over the writing surface, and written portions are severed from the web after advancing away from the printing area.

**Features:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
