## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Portable typewriters of about 6 kg are heavy and hard to assemble because each function (ribbon lift, ribbon feed, escapement trigger) has its own shift rod.

**Solution:** One common shift rod on a pivoting platform on two rigid end levers drives the escapement, ribbon lift and ribbon spool feed.

**How it works:** A shift rod behind the key rows, carried by two rigid end levers on a shaft parallel to the keys, swings on an arc when any type key or the space bar is struck. Pull rods and intermediate shafts from the end levers drive the carriage escapement (fixed and loose tooth), the ribbon lift and the spool advance. A notched setting lever alters the ribbon pull rod relative to a carrier on the right end lever, selecting black, red or stencil with the ribbon lift disabled. The machine splits into two main assemblies, frame and carriage, fixed with two screws.
