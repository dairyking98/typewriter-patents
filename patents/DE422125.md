## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (hammer printing unit for calculators) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for selecting or suppressing individual hammers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention consists in a particularly simple one new training of the hammer drive mechanism, through which any and easily adjustable subdivision of the zero pressure in the resting position is possible.

**How it works (reading):** Hammers held by springs are released by a hook lever when the machine drive swings an oscillating frame; coupled hammers fire together so the zeros to the right of a set type are printed, with the subdivision set by the hook-lever arrangement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic zero printing in calculating-machine printing units needs an easily adjustable way to subdivide the zero pressure into groups.

**Solution:** Hammers held by springs are released by a hook lever when the machine drive swings an oscillating frame; coupled hammers fire together so the zeros to the right of a set type are printed, with the subdivision set by the hook-lever arrangement.

**Features:** [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** angle levers 2, 12; Z-rail 13; print hammers 15; hook levers 20; release levers 23; type bar 26
