## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon-inked typewriters hide the writing from the operator.

**Solution:** Dispense with the ribbon and ink each type with a small roller carried on the type-arm, with the arms arranged radially so the writing stays in plain view.

**How it works:** Type-lever arms are pivoted at equal spacing around a segment of a circle (a little more than a semicircle) and strike radially at a central point on the paper cylinder, which traverses endwise per keystroke. An ink roller on an arm jointed to a slide is drawn over the type face before the stroke and over an inked surface to replenish, then again on return, the slide being moved by the key. Arms may be driven by electromagnets with the keys closing contacts. The cylinder may be above or below the circle so the operator sees the line.

**Features:** [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** type-lever arm; type; ink roller; roller arm; slide; inked surface; paper cylinder; finger key; electromagnet; contact mechanism

**Improves on:**

- ribbon-inked typewriters: inks each type by roller so the writing is not hidden
