## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** Automatic ribbon reversal: bevel-gear drives to the two reels are switched by a shaft that shifts when a reel empties, reversing ribbon travel without extra tension.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink ribbon reels must be reversed by hand and the ribbon wears unevenly without automatic reversal.

**Solution:** Automatic ribbon reversal: bevel-gear drives to the two reels are switched by a shaft that shifts when a reel empties, reversing ribbon travel without extra tension.
