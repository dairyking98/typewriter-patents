**How it works (reading):** The key set's top surface is undulating, with valleys guiding the middle fingers and crests for the little fingers, so each finger reaches its keys with the same movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Flat key tops make it hard for a writer's fingers to find the correct keys.

**Solution:** The key set's top surface is undulating, with valleys guiding the middle fingers and crests for the little fingers, so each finger reaches its keys with the same movement.

**How it works:** The key tops are arranged in wave troughs and crests matching the finger positions.
