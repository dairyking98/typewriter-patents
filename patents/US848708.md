## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the ribbon-controlling devices of type-writing machines, and particularly to the mechanism in front-strike and other visible writing machines by which the ribbon is caused to cover and uncover the printing-point at each type-stroke.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoted two-slot actuator on the type-stroke frame engages one of two studs at different distances from the ribbon lever fulcrum, giving a short or long ribbon lift, or neither (stencil position).

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Two-colour ribbon changing on a front-strike Underwood needed a simple operator control, plus a way to disable the ribbon for cutting stencils.

**Solution:** A pivoted two-slot actuator on the type-stroke frame engages one of two studs at different distances from the ribbon lever fulcrum, giving a short or long ribbon lift, or neither (stencil position).

**How it works:** The universal bar reciprocates a frame at each stroke, carrying a U-shaped actuator with two slotted plates. The ribbon-vibrating lever has two studs at unequal distances from its pivot. Swinging the actuator sideways engages the near stud (long ribbon stroke, lower colour band) or the far stud (short stroke, upper band). In the midway position neither stud is engaged, so the frame vibrates idly and the ribbon stays down for stencils. A spring detent holds the three positions, and two colour-coded keys rock a shaft that moves the actuator through a link.
