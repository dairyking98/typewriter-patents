## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper or record sheets for condensed record work are hard to control on the platen and paper carriage of a Monarch machine.

**Solution:** Devices attached to the paper carriage and platen frame, including adjustable supports and a centre paper feed roller, that control the paper sheets.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
