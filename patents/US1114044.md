## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**How it works (reading):** A power-driven shaft carries an actuator that trips operating levers engaged by the finger keys to drive the Underwood type-bars; a lever whose key is held depressed is locked out of the actuator path.
