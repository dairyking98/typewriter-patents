## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a guide for the carriage for address printing plate holders with embossing machines. So far, the carriages have had three rollers or guide pieces equipped, which ran on rails attached to the machine frame and through Counterholders were secured against lifting. L m an easy gear of the slide to achieve, this leadership required a particularly careful design of the Parts and most accurate setup. The management in the company remained particularly sensitive. The object of the invention is to create a guide that, despite high requirements allows a simpler structure and a simpler set-up in terms of working accuracy. An essential feature of the invention is that the main guide of the Slide is created by a rod-shaped guide rail, which is particularly is easy to manufacture as a round bar. The sliding pieces for this round bar exist according to a further feature of the invention from pierced side walls of the carriage. According to the invention, the carriage has a roller as a further guide, which is supported on a rail fixedly attached to the machine frame.
