## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriages for address printing plate holders on embossing machines ran on three rollers or guide pieces, which needed careful design and exact setup to keep working accuracy.

**Solution:** The carriage slides on a round guide rod passing through pierced side walls, and a roller on a fixed rail supplies the second guide, so the guide is simpler to build and set up. A spring drum with a tension band moves the carriage.

An essential feature of the invention is that the main guide of the Slide is created by a rod-shaped guide rail, which is particularly is easy to manufacture as a round bar. The sliding pieces for this round bar exist according to a further feature of the invention from pierced side walls of the carriage. According to the invention, the carriage has a roller as a further guide, which is supported on a rail fixedly attached to the machine frame.
