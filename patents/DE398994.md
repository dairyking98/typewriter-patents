## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register type wheel auxiliary) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** An extra freely rotating segment with one, two or three zeros sits next to the units wheel on the same axis in both printing and display, and a locking pin in its notches sets the capacity by a simple movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash register printing and display capacity is fixed, so changing the amounts needs rebuilding or a specialist when money loses value.

**Solution:** An extra freely rotating segment with one, two or three zeros sits next to the units wheel on the same axis in both printing and display, and a locking pin in its notches sets the capacity by a simple movement.
