## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-space wheel could not be released from the platen to rotate the platen between lines without disturbing the line feed.

**Solution:** The line-space wheel can be released from and relocked to the platen; released, it acts as a friction brake on the platen, with the mechanism enclosed inside the platen.
