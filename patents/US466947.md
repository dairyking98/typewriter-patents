## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On Remington-type machines the carriage must be lifted to see the writing, and the ratchet-bar release is awkward to reach.

**Solution:** Type levers are arranged to strike the platen from the front so writing is visible, with a front release key, a ribbon guide and a platen shift for two characters per type.

**How it works:** Type levers strike the front of the impression roller so the work can be inspected without moving the carriage. A vertical key at the front left of the carriage lifts the ratchet-bar from the escapement through a lever train. A ribbon guide holds the ribbon near the roller, and the roller on a sliding frame moves to print the upper or lower character of each type.
