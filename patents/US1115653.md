## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewritlng machines, and particularly to mechanism lfor regulating the spacing of the lines of the work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing is hard to set quickly and accurately to any width of space.

**Solution:** A collar on the platen spindle, slid by links and levers, gives an adjustable line-spacing setting so any desired space between written lines can be set accurately.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** collar on spindle; links; levers; fulcrum arms
