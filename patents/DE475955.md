## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for attaching machines to baseboards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a connection x-on Typewriters, especially portable typewriters, with the floorboard, but they can also find use in calculating machines and similar machinery.

**How it works (reading):** Pins on the floor board engage elastic rubber rings pressed onto the machine's feet, so the connection is firm but gives sideways and vertically and absorbs sound.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rigidly screwed typewriter on a floor board transmits board warp into the machine and does not dampen noise, while unscrewing it for portable use is a cumbersome job.

**Solution:** Pins on the floor board engage elastic rubber rings pressed onto the machine's feet, so the connection is firm but gives sideways and vertically and absorbs sound.

**Features:**

- **Frame and body:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** floor board pins; ring-shaped rubber feet; frame holes
