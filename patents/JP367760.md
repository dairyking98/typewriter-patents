## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A hand-turned type magazine tends to run on past the wanted character, so characters print out of line.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded roller engages one of a ring of waves inside the drum, one per type column, holding the drum at each position while the drum can still rise and fall.

**How it works:** The drum carries pivoted type holders, each with three tiers of types, struck at the bent lower end through the knob mechanism. Round the inside lower end of the drum are as many waves as there are type columns. A roller on a parallelogram link on a bearing post is pressed by a spring into one wave, giving a detent with equal pressure at any height. The drum turns on an outer shaft that rises and falls along the knob shaft, and an index dial sits on top.
