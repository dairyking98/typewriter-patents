## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection; Cylindrical Japanese-language typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a type cylinder for a Japanese-language typewriter, made by fitting into ring plates both ends of a number of type cases, each a bar of channel (コ) section with many types arranged in a row passing through its upper and lower flanges, so as to form a cylinder in which the types can move in and out radially or at an angle to the radius, and fastening these ends with locking rings.

**How it works (reading):** Build the cylinder from many channel-section type cases whose ends fit into ring plates at both ends, fastened with locking rings.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A round type cylinder for a Japanese-language typewriter, with types that move in and out, must be sturdy and easy to make and repair.

**Solution:** Build the cylinder from many channel-section type cases whose ends fit into ring plates at both ends, fastened with locking rings.

**How it works:** Each type case is a bar of channel section with many types in a row passing through the upper and lower flanges. The case ends fit into ring plates at both ends to form a cylinder, locking rings over the fitted ends fasten the plates. Types slide radially, or at an angle to the radius if the cases or only the types are inclined. The hammer is inside and the platen outside the cylinder; the cylinder rotates and slides to bring the wanted type to the print position.
