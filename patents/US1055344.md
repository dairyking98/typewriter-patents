## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** A series of individually adjustable gage pins and sheet-guiding fingers on a rock shaft replace the single long gage, and the gage releases automatically with the pressure rolls.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Sheet gages and directrices spanning the full platen make the sheet hard to adjust.

**Solution:** A series of individually adjustable gage pins and sheet-guiding fingers on a rock shaft replace the single long gage, and the gage releases automatically with the pressure rolls.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
