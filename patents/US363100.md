## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes hand-held

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to that class of typewriting machines in which a revolving hub carrying radial flexible arms bearing type is employed for making the impressions by depressing the flexible arms as they reach the impression-point;
