## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**How it works (reading):** A thin, pliable base sheet, softer than the types but harder than the platen, carries sharp-edged corrugations formed from its own material; they cut the paper where the types strike.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing ways to make writing non-erasable (emery sheets, corrugated metal rails) either wreck the types or the machine, or are too thick to give a good impression.

**Solution:** A thin, pliable base sheet, softer than the types but harder than the platen, carries sharp-edged corrugations formed from its own material; they cut the paper where the types strike.

**How it works:** The sheet is laid between platen and paper; each struck character meets a corrugation that shreds the paper fibres at that spot.
