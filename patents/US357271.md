## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot readily find and correct mistakes without raising the paper carrier, and settings for figure columns need numbering bars.

**Solution:** An attachment with a reflector and pointer, a ribbon-mover with an adjusting screw, and a movable paper carrier, allowing corrections and column setting without the carrier raised.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)
