## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The decimal tabulator's column stop rod had to be scaffolded and assembled from several pieces, which made it complex and expensive.

**Solution:** The column stop rod is a U-shaped rail with transverse slots that the tabs slide in, made by pressing and punching sheet metal, and it is assembled from two Z-shaped rails.
