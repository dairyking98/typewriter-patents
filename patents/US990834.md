## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The margin gage on one rack blocks the other gage at the carriage extremes, and the signal and key-lock gear is bulky.

**Solution:** A margin gage slides on a crossbar with a tappet on a rockshaft that operates the bell and key lock, letting both gages reach either end of travel.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
