## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in copy-holders, and more especially to that class thereof designed for use by compositors and therefore to be applied to a type-case, though, as will hereinafter appear, the device is appllcable to typewriters, or any other support wherein it is desired to conveniently hold and indicate the line of copy.

**How it works (reading):** Wire frame with a rubber roll turned by a milled disk holds the copy line; springs guide the line and the holder swings aside.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Compositor's copy line is hard to keep at eye level and the case compartments are hard to reach.

**Solution:** Wire frame with a rubber roll turned by a milled disk holds the copy line; springs guide the line and the holder swings aside.
