## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Unyielding keytops tire and sore the operator's fingers, and a plain keytop cannot show a blank or a second keyboard.

**Solution:** A cushioned touch member fitted above the key that can be reversed so either face (lettered, blank, or differently marked) is uppermost.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** key cap; cushion touch member
