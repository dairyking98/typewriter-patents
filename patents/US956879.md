## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters that step the carriage left to right cannot print right-to-left languages such as Hebrew without a specially built machine.

**Solution:** On a Blickensderfer-type machine, make the step-by-step carriage feed reversible by changing the feeding devices, and fit keys carrying characters of two alphabets with a removable type wheel per language.

**How it works:** The type mechanism is a removable type wheel on a shaft with a gear meshing two racks moved by sectors and rock-shafts driven from the key levers (as in US583840). Changing the language means swapping the wheel. The carriage is a sliding plate with a rack and a toothed-block feeder on an arm, whose arrangement is changed so the carriage steps either left to right or right to left. Keys carry both Roman and Hebrew letters. Opening text only; feeder detail not shown.
