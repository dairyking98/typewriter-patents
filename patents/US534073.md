## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention has reference to typewriting machines of the class wherein the type are provided with pistons or plun gers that ride in cylinders or ducts, meansA being provided for forcing a fluid into said cylinders or ducts and against said pistons or plungers.
