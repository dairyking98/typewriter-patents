## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage feed and release of a typebar machine (L. C. Smith) needed a carriage release that can be worked from either end of the carriage in any position, an adjustable escapement spring, and a quick-detach spring band.

**Solution:** A release bar on the carriage that releases the escapement dogs when slid in either direction from its mid position, plus an adjustable rocker spring and a keyhole-slot band coupling.

**How it works:** A rack bar on the carriage meshes with a pinion on a shaft carrying the escapement wheel. A rocker with a holding dog and a stepping dog (spring-held, free to swing down as the carriage returns) is vibrated by the key levers via the universal bar and lets the wheel advance one tooth per letter under the carriage spring. A release bar on the rack bar has two slots with inclined floors over shouldered screws, centred by a spring. Sliding the bar either way cams it down, rocking a slide and lever that depresses both dogs clear of the wheel, so the carriage runs free. Finger levers at each end of the carriage push the bar through bell cranks. A U-shaped tension device with a screw sets the rocker spring tension, and a cylindrical housing keeps the spring off the rocker shaft. The spring-drum band attaches to a headed pin through a keyhole slot with a locking spring tongue.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** carriage; rack bar; pinion; escapement wheel; rocker; holding dog; stepping dog; release bar; release lever; slide; bell-crank finger lever; tension adjusting device; rocker spring; spring drum; band; headed pin

**Improves on:**

- prior carriage release mechanisms: one bar released by motion in either direction and operable from both ends in any carriage position
