## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Irregular alignment of letters on the line caused by worn parts and variable key strokes.

**Solution:** A funnel-shaped centring cone is fixed to the frame top with its larger end flush with the opening, so the type are guided upward through an apex hole to a fixed print point.

**How it works:** Funnel cone with inclined walls guides each type to the same aperture.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
