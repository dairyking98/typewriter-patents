## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Tabulator column stops and carriage positioning on the Oliver machine are hard to set and control.

**Solution:** Column stops on a carriage-mounted rack are positioned by a cooperating device; a key shifts that device into operative relation with the stops and releases the carriage from letter spacing so the spring moves it to the stop.
