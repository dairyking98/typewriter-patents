## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Gummed paper strips need a convenient supply on the typewriter platen.

**Solution:** A roll of gummed paper is carried on an adjustable shaft and frame hooked to the machine, so the strip is drawn from the roll by turning the platen.
