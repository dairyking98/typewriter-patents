## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** The typebar is clamped in a jig with bending edges and a long extension piece, so the head bends to a fixed correct angle and position with spring-back allowed for.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar heads must be bent to the right angle by hand, which is laborious and needs skilled adjusters.

**Solution:** The typebar is clamped in a jig with bending edges and a long extension piece, so the head bends to a fixed correct angle and position with spring-back allowed for.
