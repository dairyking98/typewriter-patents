## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** In the known sum printing device the decimal relay switching element is moved by hand, and forgetting this adjustment causes wrong printed totals.

**Solution:** The relay switching element for the decimal position is adjusted automatically rather than by hand; the exact arrangement is cut off in the OCR, so details are uncertain.

**How it works:** Counter contact devices close current paths in order, and electromagnets strike the number and space keys to print the total; the cam-roller switching element sets the decimal point.
