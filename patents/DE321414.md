## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (heavy switch operation) · **Subject (patent classes):** Power arrangements internal to the switch for operating the driving mechanism using spring motor

**How it works (reading):** An opening spring is tensioned by hand through a ratchet lever and latched; releasing a stop lets it snap the contacts closed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Large manual switches for high currents needed too much hand force to close directly.

**Solution:** An opening spring is tensioned by hand through a ratchet lever and latched; releasing a stop lets it snap the contacts closed.
