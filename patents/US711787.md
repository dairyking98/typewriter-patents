## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by applications of scales or indicators

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates particularly to improvements in devices designed to be attached to type-writing machines, linotype-machines, and others similarly operated (and where the` word type-writer is hereinafter used other machines similarly operated are to beunderstood) for convenience in tabulating;

**How it works (reading):** A casing on the type-writer scale carries sliding indexes in slots; set indexes stop the carriage at the wanted tabulation points.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating needs a device with no parts liable to get out of order that can be set quickly to stop the carriage at column positions.

**Solution:** A casing on the type-writer scale carries sliding indexes in slots; set indexes stop the carriage at the wanted tabulation points.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
