## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar bearings wear and cause looseness, and the bars must be closely spaced; dust also enters the bearing.

**Solution:** Anti-friction bearings on the type-bar hangers can be adjusted independently to take up play, with a dust guard protecting the bearing.
