## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register double receipt print)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register with a device for printing receipts twice by means of the same Types in which the rotation of the raised and lowered conveyor rollers for the Receipt by a clamping lock moved back and forth by the cash register is effected.

**How it works (reading):** A locking arm moves the raised and lowered conveyor rollers for the receipt through a rod from the checkout gear, and the lower set of type wheels strikes the receipt against a pressure plate acting from below.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cash register must print the same type twice on a receipt and feed the receipt precisely, which earlier devices did only with many gear parts.

**Solution:** A locking arm moves the raised and lowered conveyor rollers for the receipt through a rod from the checkout gear, and the lower set of type wheels strikes the receipt against a pressure plate acting from below.
