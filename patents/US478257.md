## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to matrix-making machines of the kind fullydisclosed in the application filed by myself and Alex.

**How it works (reading):** The cross-head is freed to slide without binding, and the key lever is linked to the alignment plate to give the key a longer arc.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The cross-head of the die carriage binds in its guideway and the key travel is too short.

**Solution:** The cross-head is freed to slide without binding, and the key lever is linked to the alignment plate to give the key a longer arc.
