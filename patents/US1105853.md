## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Prior paper feed devices were not precise enough for regular line feed.

**Solution:** Friction driving rollers and feed rollers, regulated to any degree between limits, feed the paper forward at intervals.

**How it works:** Adjustable friction roller drives the platen and feed rollers for regulated feed.

**Features:** [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md)
