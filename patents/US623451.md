## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column, line and back spacing on Remington and Caligraph machines are slow by hand.

**Solution:** A side attachment with a circle of numbered push-keys and treadle releases the platen carriage to run forward and stop at multiples of five, with line and back spacing.

**How it works:** A cylinder on the frame carries push-keys marked 5, 10, 15 etc. working levers on a shaft; pressing a key slides the shaft and frees ratchet disks from the pawl. Two ratchet disks, one loose on a spiral spring, let the carriage step one tooth per stroke from the rocking bar's pawl. A drum and treadle mechanism connect to the cylindrical platen for line spacing and backspace.
