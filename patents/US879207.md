## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of this invention is to provide mechanism so constructed as to eliminate impact or concussion of the moving parts during the operation of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** Toggle mechanism and cushioned stops remove impact in a silent typewriter.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are noisy from impact of moving parts.

**Solution:** Toggle mechanism and cushioned stops remove impact in a silent typewriter.

**How it works:** Type-bar actuators use a toggle joint mechanism adjustable toward and from the platen, with low-wear bearings, low-friction detachable connection to key levers, and a stop arresting key levers without concussion. Based on Kidder patent US567241.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** toggle; type bar; key lever; stop

**Improves on:**

- [US567241](../patents/US567241.md): improved silent mechanism
