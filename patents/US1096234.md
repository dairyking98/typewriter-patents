## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists needed a scale for setting line position and an adjustable paper scale around the platen.

**Solution:** A scale mounted on the platen end with an adjustable paper scale and stop pin sets the line position.

**How it works:** An annular scale sits at the platen end and a stop pin on an adjustable stop locates the paper.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
