## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A separate spacing stroke between words costs time, which limits typing speed.

**Solution:** Pressing the space key together with the last letter key of a word makes the escapement advance two spaces on one release.

**How it works:** The escapement lever carries a fixed detent and a spring-loaded pivoted shifting detent that engage the carriage rack. A lever pivoted on the escapement lever sits between the end of the escapement lever and the shifting detent and normally acts as a stop that limits the detent to one tooth. When the space key is pressed along with a letter key, a rod from the space-key levers pulls this lever down so it no longer stops the detent. On release the detent and rack move two teeth, and a spring bolt in the escapement lever holds the lever down until the double movement is complete. Letter alone gives one space, space alone or together gives two, and space after the letter gives three.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** escapement lever; fixed detent; shifting detent; carriage rack; space key; universal space bar; stop lever; connecting rod; spring bolt; carriage spring

**Numbers:** one letter space, two spaces with combined key, three with sequential keys

**Improves on:**

- ordinary bar-lock escapement: letter key and space key can be struck together so the word space costs no extra stroke
