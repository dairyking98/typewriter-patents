## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**How it works (reading):** The plate runs on double pull-out guides carried by spring-loaded half-turn angle levers, giving full-length rigid support with free space below.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter support plates on lever parallelograms or struts wobbled under impact and blocked leg space.

**Solution:** The plate runs on double pull-out guides carried by spring-loaded half-turn angle levers, giving full-length rigid support with free space below.
