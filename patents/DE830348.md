## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a device on typewriters, to achieve better writing surface brightness and also to save space in the office to win. The device consists in that within the field of view between Platen and keyboard are arranged showing the writing surface and mirror if necessary, illuminate the keyboard indirectly while the person typing is alongside his machine is looking out of the window. So far it has been customary that the typewriter is lit from the side by direct outside light: mostly from the left. This unfavorable lighting with falling obliquely on the writing surface Light rays require that on cloudy days and regularly in the twilight the electric Light must be used prematurely. In contrast, the means uniform, indirect Lighting according to the invention a noticeable protection of the eyes. Since, according to the invention, the writer is directed to the window with his eyes and seat, a better use of space takes place, as now with the usual window width two people have space next to each other, which is essential for many offices and was previously impossible.
