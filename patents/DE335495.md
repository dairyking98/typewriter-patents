## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (subtraction rod in writing machine)

**How it works (reading):** The subtraction setting is released from the carriage stop through a spring-loaded linkage, with manual release, locking and pawl safeguards that block the switch in specified situations.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accidental switching to subtraction on the calculating typewriter must be prevented at the wrong time.

**Solution:** The subtraction setting is released from the carriage stop through a spring-loaded linkage, with manual release, locking and pawl safeguards that block the switch in specified situations.
