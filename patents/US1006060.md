## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Machines or apparatus for embossing decorations or marks, e.g. embossing coins characterised by the power drive

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to that type of machines in which the carriage whereon the paper is held instead of being animated with a reciprocating rectilinear motion, consists of a cylinder which revolves around its axle in such a manner that the lines, instead of following a rectilinear displacement of the carriage, develop along the cross section of said cylinder.

**How it works (reading):** A horizontal hollow cylinder platen, perpendicular to the operator, rotates under a spring and is shifted along its axis by one line key, with the rotation spring tensioned automatically when paper is inserted.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machines with a revolving cylinder platen need three extra keys (finish rotation, margin, shift cylinder to new line) and a vertical platen.

**Solution:** A horizontal hollow cylinder platen, perpendicular to the operator, rotates under a spring and is shifted along its axis by one line key, with the rotation spring tensioned automatically when paper is inserted.

**How it works:** The paper is a sheet gripped by its edge in a slot in the hollow cylinder platen by a spring-loaded blade, the gripped part forming the margin and the rest hanging loose on the outer surface. A toothed wheel on the axle turns the platen intermittently, so lines develop around the cylinder cross-section rather than along a carriage. A long spiral spring inside a telescoping tube pair, keyed against rotation, shifts the platen longitudinally to the next line. One finger key shifts lines.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** hollow cylinder platen; slot; gripping blade; draw spring; telescoping tubes; spiral spring; toothed wheel; axle

**Improves on:**

- vertical revolving-cylinder typewriters: horizontal platen, single line key, spring tensioned automatically by inserting paper
