## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to transmitting connections designed for interposition between the key-levers of type-writing machines and the type-bars thereof.

**How it works (reading):** An intermediate connector that accommodates the varying angle between key-lever and transmitting lever without side strain, with adjustable length to fit the distance between the parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says key-levers set at varying angles to the transmitting levers cause side strain when linked to the type-bars, and the link length cannot be varied.

**Solution:** An intermediate connector that accommodates the varying angle between key-lever and transmitting lever without side strain, with adjustable length to fit the distance between the parts.
