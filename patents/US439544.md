## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Writing columns of words or figures on a page accurately, so the page looks better and figures add correctly.

**Solution:** A slotted rack on the carriage carries clampable column stops that the carriage dog runs against, giving an exact column start, with graduation marks and pointers.

**How it works:** A rack fixed to the rear carriage bar of a Caligraph-type machine has the usual letter-space teeth plus a second notched edge under it. Adjustable stop pieces pass through the rack's slot and a lug engages the notched edge, clamped from above by a pivoted eccentric (or set screw). A lever on the rocking bar throws the spacing dog clear of the rack so the carriage runs under its main spring until the dog hits a column stop. The clamp prevents the stop being displaced by the concussion. Graduated marks on the rack's rear match the front carriage scale, and index fingers on the stops allow exact setting.
