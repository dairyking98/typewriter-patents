## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil punching device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a device for punching sheets of paper or similar material to be used for stencils;

**How it works (reading):** Two rotating disks carry punches that a plunger drives through paper fed by feed wheels, with a reciprocating block carrying the plates.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Metal stencils for shipping addresses are too costly, so paper stencils must be cut cheaply.

**Solution:** Two rotating disks carry punches that a plunger drives through paper fed by feed wheels, with a reciprocating block carrying the plates.
