## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Form holding devices with elastic fingers against a rail make it hard to insert a bundle of forms when the platen turns.

**Solution:** Additional paper pressure rollers carried by the elastic fingers lift off the platen when the fingers are pressed against the rail, and press onto the platen when the fingers are released, forming a funnel that guides the insertion of the form.

**How it works:** Pivoting levers move the finger strip and the pressure rollers together, so the form is held against the rail from behind or the front and guided in by the rollers.
