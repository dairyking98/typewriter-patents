## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typography type frame) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** Types on a rotating disc are depressed into a soft moulding block, and a pawl on a rack advances the block to space letters and form lines, so no type needs distributing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing from a stock of types needs an expensive set of types and their distribution after each form is used.

**Solution:** Types on a rotating disc are depressed into a soft moulding block, and a pawl on a rack advances the block to space letters and form lines, so no type needs distributing.
