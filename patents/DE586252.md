Is particularly beneficial according to the invention, the arrangement is made such that in a case of four rows of keys lying one below the other comprehensive. honeycomb keyboard the types of each vertically one above the other Keys of the first and third row on one side and the second and fourth row on the other hand, strike at the same point on the writing surface. It is known that the keys in syllable typewriters are like a chessboard to arrange. You can use one, two or four buttons with one finger hold down, never three keys. But three buttons are mainly required for frequently occurring combinations, such as a. B. sch, cht, ung, u. a. That. Depressing four keys would also strain a finger.
