## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet materials for use therein by using carbon paper or the like

**How it works (reading):** Adding ultramarine to the carrier mass lets much more of the printing dye be incorporated without smearing, giving a more durable ink transfer body.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Colour transfer bodies (ribbons, carbon) containing a good printing dye such as aniline run dry quickly because the dye bleeds out of the carrier mass, so few prints are made.

**Solution:** Adding ultramarine to the carrier mass lets much more of the printing dye be incorporated without smearing, giving a more durable ink transfer body.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
