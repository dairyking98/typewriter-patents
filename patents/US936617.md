## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Leading-edge paper stops were tied to the release key, so they moved whenever the paper feed was released.

**Solution:** A separate special key brings the leading-edge stops into operation against the platen independently of the release key, and they release automatically when the feed mechanism is restored.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
