## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On Williams' grasshopper typebar machine, the carriage that shifts to three positions needs a better centering and locking device, ratchet feed, ink pads and typebar guide.

**Solution:** A track carrying the paper carriage rocks on arms and is held centered by a spring, shifted forward or back by a tilting double key lever with locking cams, with refinements to the feed rack, pads and a combined guide and index.

**How it works:** Typebars with three characters each swing in two stationary banks in front of and behind a carriage that can sit in a central, forward or rear position, giving three characters per bar. The carriage runs on ball bearings on a track frame hung on rock arms. A horizontal shifting bar attached to the track is moved by a tilting double lever, and a spring with two legs around a lug returns the track to center. A locking lever with cam ends catches a central stop to hold the shift for capitals or figures, and pins on the shifting bar and lever lock the carriage against vibration when centered. The pivoted feed rack-bar rides over a ratchet, can be lifted by finger levers to release the carriage, and a small spring-driven sliding rack advances one step when the feed bar is pressed. A forked guide with a pointer indexes the center strike point and the carriage scale. Spring-eared ink pads clip onto pins to touch the type blocks.
