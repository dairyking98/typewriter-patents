## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to lowering devices for typewriters in the knee area below the desk drawer can be raised and lowered using parallel lever guides Typewriter support plate.

**How it works (reading):** Parallelogram levers on double pull-out slide rails hold the support plate at the best writing height, held steady by gravity with no springs or lugs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Knee-space lowering devices leave the typewriter at the wrong height and vibrate, and hang too low in rest.

**Solution:** Parallelogram levers on double pull-out slide rails hold the support plate at the best writing height, held steady by gravity with no springs or lugs.
