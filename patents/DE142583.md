**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenography machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a stenography machine which makes it possible to to retype the winged words of the speaker.

**How it works (reading):** A stenotype machine whose type levers carry whole letter combinations or syllables (such as st) on a single lever, combined with Gabelsberger vowel symbols, so a word needs only a couple of keystrokes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Speech should be retyped quickly by stenography, but ordinary keys write one letter at a time, so frequent words and syllables take several strokes each.

**Solution:** A stenotype machine whose type levers carry whole letter combinations or syllables (such as st) on a single lever, combined with Gabelsberger vowel symbols, so a word needs only a couple of keystrokes.
