> Description Translated from German Switching device with power drive for typewriters The invention relates to a switching device with power drive for typewriters in which the machine part to be switched when pressing the switch key in one and when it is released it is moved in the other direction by the power drive will. When in the known switching devices of this type, the upward and downward movement of the machine part to be switched, e.g. B. the car, several times at short intervals takes place one after the other, it can happen that the power drive on one of the does not respond to both key movements (up or down movement). In such cases the toggle movement and the key movement fall out of step by switching over causing machine parts take up such a position in relation to one another, that the machine part to be switched, z. B. the carriage, lowers when the Shift key is depressed and rises when the button is released and vice versa. These shortcomings are avoided according to the invention in that the Reversal of the direction of movement of the machine part to be switched over inevitably brought about by a control linkage directly shifted by the shift key will.
