## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor; Mechanism driven by cams engaging rotating roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switching device with power drive for typewriters in which the machine part to be switched when pressing the switch key in one and when it is released it is moved in the other direction by the power drive will.

**How it works (reading):** A knee-lever of two toggles, one on the drive lever and one on the switched part, keeps one toggle stretched in each end position so the shift runs without impact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Power-driven carriage shift makes noise and falls out of step when limit stops are struck at speed.

**Solution:** A knee-lever of two toggles, one on the drive lever and one on the switched part, keeps one toggle stretched in each end position so the shift runs without impact.
