## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving mechanisms, e.g. springs stressed during carriage return

**How it works (reading):** Two coaxial gears act on the rack: a spring-driven gear pulls forward while the ratchet gear holds back, removing the dead play.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A drive gear on the carriage rack gave dead play that changed letter spacing.

**Solution:** Two coaxial gears act on the rack: a spring-driven gear pulls forward while the ratchet gear holds back, removing the dead play.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
