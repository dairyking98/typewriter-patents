## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, cheap typewriter is wanted as a children's or kindergarten machine.

**Solution:** A pointer turned over an alphabet plate (with locking notches) rotates a type wheel carrying an elastic band of pictures; a finger button drives a hammer inside the rim that stretches the chosen picture to the card.

**How it works:** A shaft on the base carries a detachable pointer above a fixed indicator plate marked with the alphabet and a clock face. The plate has notches in which the spring-loaded pointer locks, giving steady positioning. The shaft also carries a type wheel with a perforated rim and an elastic rubber band stretched around it, held in place by flanges and a lug in a band hole. The band carries pictures whose names begin with the plate letters. Pressing a finger button swings a hammer lever inside the rim, which pushes through a rim perforation and stretches the band against a card in a fixed pocket platen. Removing the pointer and plate gives access to the type carrier for cleaning or changing.
