> Description Translated from German Device for placing and straightening writing and between them sheets of carbon paper to be arranged The invention relates to a desk-shaped device for laying down and straightening sheets of writing paper and sheets of carbon paper to be arranged between them, especially for use in typewriters with stop bars for the leaves and cutouts on the sides and on the lower edge of the inclined desk top For easy removal of the layered sheets: The known devices of this Art simplify the classification compared to the procedure by hand alone, but they do not offer any advantage in inserting the sheets into the typewriter or when separating the writing sheets from the carbon paper sheets, because the sheets, with their edges congruent, are piled up. By a well-known Device provided alignment pins for the coal leaves at the end of the plate the leaves slightly damaged. Eventually the carbon paper sheets get to these Devices always in one and the same position to the writing sheets, whereby they Worn out very quickly in individual areas, but mostly in the intervening areas not be exploited.
