## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Registering devices

**How it works (reading):** A desk-like inclined plate with an adjustable clamp holds carbon sheets offset from the writing sheets, so each carbon sheet is used over a different area, and sheets are laid and separated easily.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon sheets stacked with edges congruent wear out at the same spot, and alignment pins damage the sheets.

**Solution:** A desk-like inclined plate with an adjustable clamp holds carbon sheets offset from the writing sheets, so each carbon sheet is used over a different area, and sheets are laid and separated easily.
