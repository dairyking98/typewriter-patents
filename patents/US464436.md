**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriting machines in which a series of type bars or levers are grouped about and arranged to strike at a common point or center, past which point the paper is carried by a traveling carriage advanced by a spring and controlled in its advance by spacing mechanism.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Remington letter spacing with rigid and yielding dogs needed refinement and a way to disengage the dogs from the rack-bar.

**Solution:** A two-part yielding dog pivoted on the rigid dog, so it tips sidewise independently, and a disengagement of dogs from the rack.

**How it works:** The rack-bar hangs on a pivoted frame on the spring-driven carriage; a rigid dog and a yielding dog on a rocker engage it alternately. The yielding dog is in two pivoted parts cut away so the upper part can tip sideways, with a light spring moving it out of line when not acted on. Finger keys act through the universal space bar to drive the dogs forward and a coil spring returns them.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)
