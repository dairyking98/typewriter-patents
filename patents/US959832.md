## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved device adapted for tabulating purposes on typewriting machines, the object being to provide a tabulating device which is composed of a comparatively few parts, said parts being assembled and designed in a manner to completely fulfil their object by very simple mechanical combinations that` can be cheaply assembled and manufactured.
