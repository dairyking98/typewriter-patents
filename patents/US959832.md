## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved device adapted for tabulating purposes on typewriting machines, the object being to provide a tabulating device which is composed of a comparatively few parts, said parts being assembled and designed in a manner to completely fulfil their object by very simple mechanical combinations that` can be cheaply assembled and manufactured.

**How it works (reading):** A vertically movable escapement rack with pins engaging slots in the carriage ends, with arms and a block, gives tabulating action from a few simply combined parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating devices use too many parts and are complicated to make and assemble.

**Solution:** A vertically movable escapement rack with pins engaging slots in the carriage ends, with arms and a block, gives tabulating action from a few simply combined parts.
