## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Column stops cammed back to inoperative position by the resetting cam put undue strain on the column stop bar.

**Solution:** A resisting bracket on the column stop bar, used with the resetting cam of the tabulator, to resist that load (Remington No. 11 embodiment).

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
