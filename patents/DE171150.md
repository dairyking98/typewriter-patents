## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** An ink tube with small outlet openings feeds a felt moistening roller; the ribbon runs over the roller, and ribbon tension regulates the flow. Metal washers adjust the roller's absorbency.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing ink moisteners (rollers or brushing) cannot give the ribbon a steady, limited ink or moisture content for a clear print.

**Solution:** An ink tube with small outlet openings feeds a felt moistening roller; the ribbon runs over the roller, and ribbon tension regulates the flow. Metal washers adjust the roller's absorbency.
