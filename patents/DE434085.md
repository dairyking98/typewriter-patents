## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Folding knives need combs to lift off the cloth at the fold, timed by a separate linkage.

**Solution:** A lever driven by a moving machine part, or by the knife, lifts the comb shaft at the end of each knife stroke.
