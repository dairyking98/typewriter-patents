## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Its purpose is to provide a self-contained car jumping device create that can be attached to a machine without making changes for this purpose must be carried out on the engine or other structure of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** The setting and extinguishing members for the positioning stops and the counter-stop are all carried on one removable frame attached to the paper carriage, with a brake shoe, so it can be fitted without changing the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage jumping devices must otherwise be built into the machine, so machines without them cannot be retrofitted easily and the separate parts are expensive.

**Solution:** The setting and extinguishing members for the positioning stops and the counter-stop are all carried on one removable frame attached to the paper carriage, with a brake shoe, so it can be fitted without changing the machine.
