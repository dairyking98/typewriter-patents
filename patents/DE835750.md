## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A resilient two-strand wire handlebar is anchored at one end to the type lever and at the other to a pendulum support, which is pulled by a pull wire from the key lever around a fixed axle, reducing the drive to four links and six joints.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The standard typewriter key linkage has six limbs, nine joints and about 15 parts, with friction, play, noise and wear, and is heavy and costly.

**Solution:** A resilient two-strand wire handlebar is anchored at one end to the type lever and at the other to a pendulum support, which is pulled by a pull wire from the key lever around a fixed axle, reducing the drive to four links and six joints.

**Features:** [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** two-strand resilient handlebar; pendulum support; pull wire; key lever; fixed axle

**Numbers:** six limbs and nine joints (15 parts) before; four links and six joints after; about 50 percent improvement
