## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to copy holders for typewriters, and it consists in the combinations, constructions and arrangements of parts herein described and claimed.

**How it works (reading):** A clamp-type copy holder with feed rolls, driven by the typewriter's own motion, that can be fitted to an ordinary or book typewriter with little change to the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A copy holder for typewriters, to hold copy and feed it forward by operating the machine itself.

**Solution:** A clamp-type copy holder with feed rolls, driven by the typewriter's own motion, that can be fitted to an ordinary or book typewriter with little change to the machine.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** feed rolls; paper shelf; guide
