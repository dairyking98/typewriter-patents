## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (punch card controlled tabulator)

**How it works (reading):** Punch cards close electrical circuits through brushes, agate rollers and series semiconductors, which operate the clutch levers that drive the registration units.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Tabulating machines have too many intermediate organs to drive registration.

**Solution:** Punch cards close electrical circuits through brushes, agate rollers and series semiconductors, which operate the clutch levers that drive the registration units.

**Features:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
