## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing machine, typebar class) · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Hammers are arranged in a circle so they all strike one common centre point, keys act on them through wires and bell-cranks, and a spring and catch advance the paper one letter per key stroke, with an interlock that prevents two hammers acting at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboard-operated writing machines were practically useless when two hammers could strike each other and cause serious damage.

**Solution:** Hammers are arranged in a circle so they all strike one common centre point, keys act on them through wires and bell-cranks, and a spring and catch advance the paper one letter per key stroke, with an interlock that prevents two hammers acting at once.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
