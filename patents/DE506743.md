## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** A lifting lever on the key link lets the pull or push rod's camshaft engagement reset while the key is still held, instead of repeatedly coupling and uncoupling.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A held key lever makes the type lever bounce back and hit repeatedly.

**Solution:** A lifting lever on the key link lets the pull or push rod's camshaft engagement reset while the key is still held, instead of repeatedly coupling and uncoupling.
