## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type-bar hanger should be simple and cheap to build but still give ready adjustment of the type-bar bearings.

**Solution:** The hanger is two vertical plates held apart by rivets with shoulders, carried on the arc-plate, with the type-bar pivoted in it; the bearing can be adjusted by the plate spacing.

**Features:**
