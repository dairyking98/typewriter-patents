## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Platen must receive type bar pressure when printing through several impression sheets but must also be accurately adjustable toward or away from the printing plane.

**Solution:** A platen mounting that is held fixed during printing yet can be adjusted to or from the printing plane and locked immovably at any adjusted position.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
