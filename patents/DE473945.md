## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cam disc with adjustable thrust curve) · **Subject (patent classes):** Adjustable cams

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cam discs give fixed thrust curves, and screw-machine feeds need varied motion without new discs.

**Solution:** An adjustable thrust-cam piece on a base disc lets the slope and central angle of the thrust curve be changed.
