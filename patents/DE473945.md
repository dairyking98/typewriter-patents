## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cam disc with adjustable thrust curve) · **Subject (patent classes):** Adjustable cams

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a cam with adjustable Thrust cam and a device separate from the cam disc for fast and precise setting of the thrust curve.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable thrust-cam piece on a base disc lets the slope and central angle of the thrust curve be changed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cam discs give fixed thrust curves, and screw-machine feeds need varied motion without new discs.

**Solution:** An adjustable thrust-cam piece on a base disc lets the slope and central angle of the thrust curve be changed.
