## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier Arabic-script type attempts used compromise or modified characters that do not match standard letter forms.

**Solution:** Arabic letters are drawn in their standard forms, one per type block, so that the characters keep recognized shapes on uniform blocks.
