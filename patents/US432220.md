## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The dog-holder of the Caligraph escapement springs sideways under the shock of carriage feed, misaligning the writing.

**Solution:** A tie or brace from the dog-holder pivot to a fixed bracket, adjustable by turnbuckle or screw, allows back and forth motion but prevents lateral flexure.

**How it works:** The carriage carries a fixed rack and a spring-shifted movable rack; a feed dog on a vertical dog-holder on a rocker frame vibrates between them as keys are pressed, stepping the carriage one tooth. A tie hooked over the dog pivot and pinned to a fixed bracket on the frame runs horizontally while the holder swings vertically. A turnbuckle, slotted bracket or screw-fed bracket adjusts its tension.
