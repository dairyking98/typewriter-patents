## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In keyboard machines with a moving type cylinder and a hammer behind the paper, transmitting key motion to the hammer past the paper space needs long springy linkage that loses force and speed, and the hammer slams the paper against the type with a crack.

**Solution:** A hammer swinging in a horizontal plane is driven by a rigid linkage from a rocking bar at the machine end, with a separate slow lever that brings the paper almost to the type first, plus a two-sector drive for the type cylinder and a key-lever adjustment piece.

**How it works:** Key stirrups on two spindles are tied by a rod so either side pulls a link to a U-shaped rocking member, whose arm drives the hammer through a push rod nearly in line with the hammer pivot at the end, giving strong late acceleration to a light hammer. The same U-member moves a second lever through a short rod, slowly bringing the paper, normally held a few millimetres off the type so the last letter stays visible, to about half a millimetre from the type. The type cylinder is turned by two toothed sectors driving two toothed wheels with plain and recessed portions so only one sector engages at a time and the wheels return to mid position. Stops on a cylinder in the path of the key levers limit key travel at the moment the selected type reaches the print point. Each key lever carries a pivoted U-shaped clamp with a screw and jam nut to adjust the timing of type setting, printing and escapement.

**Features:** [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [IMP:impression-adjust](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** type cylinder; hammer; key stirrup; rocking bar; U-shaped member; push rod; paper-pressing lever; toothed sector; driving wheel; stop cylinder; key-lever adjusting clamp; adjusting screw; hammer return spring

**Numbers:** paper held a few millimetres from the type; paper brought to about 0.5 mm before impact

**Improves on:**

- older hammer drives with long parallel spindle and link arrangement: rigid non-elastic horizontal-plane drive with no springy long rod guide, less lost force
