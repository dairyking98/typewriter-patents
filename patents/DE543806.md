## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (shorthand typewriter producing stenographic characters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**How it works (reading):** Shorthand characters are redrawn to use only two height levels so they print cleanly like ordinary typescript, with up to about 135 characters on a typewriter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier shorthand typewriters used three height levels, clumped, needed exact adjustment and too much carriage width.

**Solution:** Shorthand characters are redrawn to use only two height levels so they print cleanly like ordinary typescript, with up to about 135 characters on a typewriter.

**How it works:** Only the middle full level is split into two half levels; the symbols then fit a two-level writing space.
