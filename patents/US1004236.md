## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars carrying several types each need heavy case-shift motion of the platen or segment.

**Solution:** Each typebar carries one type; case shift chooses which of two bars (short or long) a key drives.

**How it works:** Two sets of typebars, short and long, are mounted on two segments under the top plate with ball bearings, the inner segment nearer the back. Every bar has one type. Each key lever serves a pair of bars and the shift mechanism determines which bar of the pair is worked when the key is depressed.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** short typebar; long typebar; segment; ball bearing; top plate; platen; carriage; shift mechanism

**Improves on:**

- conventional shifting platen: no carriage shift; the shift selects which bar a key drives
