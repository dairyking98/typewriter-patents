## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding machine card guide frame) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**How it works (reading):** A card frame runs between the printing unit and the paper roller on the carriage, shifts sideways to the column positions, advances one line with each indexing of the paper, and a second ribbon and a translucent strip give simultaneous copies.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Entries that must be written at the same time on an account card, the paper roll and a third strip cannot be made by a calculator with a paper roll alone.

**Solution:** A card frame runs between the printing unit and the paper roller on the carriage, shifts sideways to the column positions, advances one line with each indexing of the paper, and a second ribbon and a translucent strip give simultaneous copies.
