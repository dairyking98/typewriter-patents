**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A thin 2 to 3 mm layer of clay on a rigid cardboard base is pressed into the type, and the dirty layer is replaced rather than kneaded.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter types are cleaned with kneaded modelling clay, which is messy and leaves dirt in the clay.

**Solution:** A thin 2 to 3 mm layer of clay on a rigid cardboard base is pressed into the type, and the dirty layer is replaced rather than kneaded.
