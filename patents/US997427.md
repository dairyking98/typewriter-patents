## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (record-receiving element holder) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for mounting or holding the sheet around the drum

**How it works (reading):** A clamp pivots on one edge of its central portion and lifts the other edge to release the sheet end, with an operating piece inside the drum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A record sheet on a drum must be held and released simply.

**Solution:** A clamp pivots on one edge of its central portion and lifts the other edge to release the sheet end, with an operating piece inside the drum.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
