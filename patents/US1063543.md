## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriage, its bed and platen shift needed improvement.

**Solution:** A carriage on ball bearings with a bed hooked on to frame standards and a counterbalanced cradle for platen shifting.

**How it works:** The carriage base rides on ball bearings in the bed; bed brackets drop onto frame standards with pin and hole guides and are locked by hooked levers. The platen shifts with a cradle of three-armed levers linked by a spring that counterbalances the weight of the carriage.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** carriage base; ball bearing; carriage bed; bracket; hooked lever; cradle lever; spiral spring; key lever
