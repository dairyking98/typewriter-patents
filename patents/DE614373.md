## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on writing business machines to secure the writing limit at the bottom of the writing sheet, in which a feeler after passing the around a platen with an uninterrupted cylindrical surface placed writing sheet in a release position is moved and when the write limit is reached, a locking device takes effect got.

**How it works (reading):** The feeler is held lifted by holding members and released into contact with the sheet, so it rests under steady pressure independent of platen rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bottom writing-limit feelers are pressed by platen rotation, causing sudden pressure changes and false triggering.

**Solution:** The feeler is held lifted by holding members and released into contact with the sheet, so it rests under steady pressure independent of platen rotation.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** feeler; holding members
