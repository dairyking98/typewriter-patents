## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (railway buffer) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers; Buffers with metal springs with helical springs arranged to damp each other by mutual friction

**How it works (reading):** Telescoping seamless steel tubes are enclosed in a self-contained housing with a buffer plate and base plate, so the whole unit mounts and replaces as one.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Railway spring buffers need tubes that guide well, resist bending and stop twisting without breaking the car frame.

**Solution:** Telescoping seamless steel tubes are enclosed in a self-contained housing with a buffer plate and base plate, so the whole unit mounts and replaces as one.
