## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tape or page guiding or feeding devices

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the arrangement of types striking a common center, and connected by curved type-rods and levers with suitablydisposed keys, governing, by means of a double pawl that is operated by each depression of a key, the motion of the sliding and weighted carriage that is arranged with the printingribbon, platen, and paper-feeding mechanism.
