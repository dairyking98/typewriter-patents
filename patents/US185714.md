## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tape or page guiding or feeding devices

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the arrangement of types striking a common center, and connected by curved type-rods and levers with suitablydisposed keys, governing, by means of a double pawl that is operated by each depression of a key, the motion of the sliding and weighted carriage that is arranged with the printingribbon, platen, and paper-feeding mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Curved type-rods striking a common center are keyed from stepped keys, with a double pawl on a weighted sliding carriage spacing the paper letter by letter.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple typewriter where the carriage is returned for a new line and the paper fed forward in one move.

**Solution:** Curved type-rods striking a common center are keyed from stepped keys, with a double pawl on a weighted sliding carriage spacing the paper letter by letter.

**How it works:** Stepped keys connect through wire rods to parallel levers whose rear ends carry curved type-rods guided by plates, converging on a common print point on the ribbon and platen. Each key press rocks a vibrating bar carrying a spring-acted double pawl that engages a toothed back of the carriage: one lug releases a cog and a cord, pulley and weight drag the carriage one tooth, and the second lug stops it on the next tooth. A space bar works the same pawl. A button and cord return the carriage while a sliding rod and button release the pawl, and two spring-pressed feed rollers on the carriage, with a spur wheel and stationary set-spring, advance the paper for the next line.
