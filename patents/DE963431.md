## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-basket segment guidance with prisms or leaf springs wears or loses accuracy, and three rigid handlebars are costly to make accurately.

**Solution:** At least some hinge points of the three rigid handlebars that guide the segment carrier are formed by resilient straps (thin steel links stiffened in the middle), giving precise, wear-free, cheap guidance of the type basket for case shifting.
