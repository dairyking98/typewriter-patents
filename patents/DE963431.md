## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements; Bearings or hangers for type levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a segment guide on typewriters with the type basket in its segment the type levers are pivoted to switch between upper and lower case letters or other characters can be adjusted in height.

**How it works (reading):** At least some hinge points of the three rigid handlebars that guide the segment carrier are formed by resilient straps (thin steel links stiffened in the middle), giving precise, wear-free, cheap guidance of the type basket for case shifting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-basket segment guidance with prisms or leaf springs wears or loses accuracy, and three rigid handlebars are costly to make accurately.

**Solution:** At least some hinge points of the three rigid handlebars that guide the segment carrier are formed by resilient straps (thin steel links stiffened in the middle), giving precise, wear-free, cheap guidance of the type basket for case shifting.
