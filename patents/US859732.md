## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** A simplified automatic reversing device that reverses the two ribbon reels when one is emptied, so the feed continues without stopping.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing ribbon reversal mechanisms are complicated, and the feed stops when one reel is emptied.

**Solution:** A simplified automatic reversing device that reverses the two ribbon reels when one is emptied, so the feed continues without stopping.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
