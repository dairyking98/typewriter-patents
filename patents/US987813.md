## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to means for supporting and guiding the carriage and for maintaining the bearing rollers therefor properly spaced with reference to one laanother and to the tracks on which they ear.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage runs on inclined tracks in upper and lower fixed guide rails carried by brackets; separators hold the cylindrical anti-friction bearing rollers at proper spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage bearing rollers must stay correctly spaced relative to one another and to their tracks; without this, the carriage binds or wears.

**Solution:** The carriage runs on inclined tracks in upper and lower fixed guide rails carried by brackets; separators hold the cylindrical anti-friction bearing rollers at proper spacing.
