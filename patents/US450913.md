## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A check punch that is simple and durable and quickly aligns numerals on the check is wanted.

**Solution:** A check punch in which spring-pressed rods carrying male dies slide toward a common centre, and a check holder slides under the rods and is driven by the same action.

**How it works:** A die wheel carries female dies for digits and dollar and cent signs; a clamping plate pressed by a spring holds the check, and the holder frame slides laterally to bring the check under the rods.
