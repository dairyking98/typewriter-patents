## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (duplicate printing, perforated stencil) · **Subject (patent classes):** Embossing, i.e. producing impressions formed by locally deep-drawing, e.g. using rolls provided with complementary profiles

**How it works (reading):** Typebars carry types whose faces are covered in fine points that perforate the paper; the perforated sheet is then laid over a sheet and ink forced through the holes prints duplicates.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing from typed impressions needed a way to duplicate a page without a conventional inked ribbon.

**Solution:** Typebars carry types whose faces are covered in fine points that perforate the paper; the perforated sheet is then laid over a sheet and ink forced through the holes prints duplicates.
