## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to have an attachment that can be placed on any typewriter for the purpose of winding ribbons, and for the further purpose of having more than one ribbon on the machine at the same time, any of which may be used without delay.
