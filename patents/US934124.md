**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to ribbon mechanisms for typewriting machines and its object, generally stated, is to provide improved devices or' the class specied.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon position relative to the platen must suit both lower and upper case on a shifting-platen front-strike machine.

**Solution:** A vibratory ribbon carrier with an operating lever whose relation to the printing portion of the ribbon is adjusted for both platen positions.

**How it works:** Ribbon runs on a vibratory carrier operated by a lever from the key action. Diagrams show where the printing portion of the ribbon lies relative to the platen in lower and upper case and in the several settings of the control. Applied to a Remington front-strike shifting-platen machine.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; vibratory ribbon carrier; operating lever; platen
