## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object is to provide a construction which is simple, durable, accurate, and little likely to get out of order.

**How it works (reading):** A hand-lever drives a rack and pinion; a pawl on the pinion turns a ratchet on the feed roll for stepped paper feed.

**Solution:** A hand-lever drives a rack and pinion; a pawl on the pinion turns a ratchet on the feed roll for stepped paper feed.
