## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism for typewriting machines and its general object is to provide improved mechanism of the character specified and more especially to provide devices which positively control the vibratory movements of the ribbon so as to prevent overthrow of the ribbon at printing operation.

**How it works (reading):** A normally broken toggle in the ribbon-vibrator train positively limits the ribbon lift and can be reset to serve more than one ribbon field.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator can overthrow when raised to the type at printing, and bichrome ribbons need different lift for each field.

**Solution:** A normally broken toggle in the ribbon-vibrator train positively limits the ribbon lift and can be reset to serve more than one ribbon field.

**How it works:** The ribbon passes through a vibrating carrier mounted on a lever arm. That lever is linked to a toggle of two members which is normally bent. On a printing stroke the toggle straightens, raising the ribbon exactly to the type and no further. A fulcrumed lever with a slot at the toggle joint, held against a stop by a spring, can be moved to shift the toggle's normal setting for the lower ribbon field. Ribbon spools at each side feed the ribbon alternately via the carriage.
