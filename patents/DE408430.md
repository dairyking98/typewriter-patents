## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers both mounted on a common carrier

**How it works (reading):** Resilient sheet-metal support legs hinged in pairs flex and twist so each guide roller seats against the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guide rollers on rigid holders do not sit evenly on out-of-round or bent rollers.

**Solution:** Resilient sheet-metal support legs hinged in pairs flex and twist so each guide roller seats against the paper.
