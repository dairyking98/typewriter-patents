**How it works (reading):** Each type lever is driven by a spring that is stretched by the pressure cam between prints, connected to its pull rod by a rolling rack engagement, so a blocked lever simply stops and the impact force is set by the spring preload.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In telegraph printers the type levers are struck directly by an intermittently rotating cam, so a jammed lever gets damaged and the impact force cannot be regulated.

**Solution:** Each type lever is driven by a spring that is stretched by the pressure cam between prints, connected to its pull rod by a rolling rack engagement, so a blocked lever simply stops and the impact force is set by the spring preload.
