## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage line spacing on a right-to-left writing machine needs a simple finger-piece action.

**Solution:** Pressure from right to left on a finger piece line-spaces the platen and also moves the carriage back to the left.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
