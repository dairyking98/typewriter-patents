## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon vibrator simultaneously adjusts the stop devices, using the ribbon actuating means of an earlier patent with new visible manual controls.
