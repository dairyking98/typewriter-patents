## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing circular-arc typebar machines are bulky and costly, with paper basket and receiver at the sides and the carriage frame cast in one piece.

**Solution:** A compact visible-writing typebar machine with separately attached carriage supports, a paper basket and receiver in front of the platen, and overlapping typebar bearings in two staggered rows.

**How it works:** Cast posts on the foundation hold two beams carrying support and guide rollers on which a prismatic frame carries the paper carriage. The platen sits in slots so the carriage can move forward to print under a second row of characters. Paper rolls in a basket in front and leaves into a spiral receiver, which saves space. Typebars are mounted radially on two stepped plates in fork-shaped bearings notched alternately so they overlap, giving long bearing axes. Key levers on knife edges drive the typebars through connecting rods with threaded ends, and a ribbon guide device is shown.
