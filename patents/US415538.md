## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Mounting typebars on a one-piece segment or in single-bar brackets makes it hard to fit them snugly and square on their pivots so they strike the print centre accurately.

**Solution:** Mount typebars two to a small bracket slotted radially, with cross holes bored from each side for the pivot pins.

**How it works:** Each bracket has a shoulder resting on the supporting frame and is held by a set screw. Its head has two radial slots, one per typebar, separated by a central wing of exactly the bar spacing, and two perforations for the pivot pins. The perforations are bored from each side at right angles to the slots so the pins lie square to the bars. The bracket is squeezed in a vise to take up play, and the thin outer wings spring so the bars fit snugly even if their thickness varies.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** bracket; typebar; pivot pin; supporting segment; set screw; central wing; platen

**Numbers:** two typebars per bracket

**Improves on:**

- single-bar brackets and one-piece typebar segments: two-bar brackets allow squeeze fitting, rigidity, square pivot holes and half the bracket count
