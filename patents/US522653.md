## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** With many sheets or manifold, the thicker paper raises the print line and spoils type alignment.

**Solution:** The platen turns on an eccentric arbor, set by a clamped lever and scale, so it rises vertically by the paper thickness.

**How it works:** The platen has a loose axial sleeve with the fixed arbor journaled eccentrically in it. A lever on the sleeve with a pointer and set-screw clamp rotates the sleeve, raising the platen almost vertically so the printing line stays in the same plane. A scale marks positions for 1, 3, 5 and 8 sheets. The feed roller is rigidly mounted, avoiding spring-pressed rollers that crock manifold paper.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; sleeve; eccentric arbor; lever; pointer; scale; set screw; feed roller; platen frame

**Numbers:** scale positions for 1, 3, 5, 8 sheets

**Improves on:**

- spring-pressed feed roller platens: rigid roller, platen raised eccentrically
