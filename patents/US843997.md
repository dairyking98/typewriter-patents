## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriers stationary for impression, e.g. with the types or dies not moving relative to the carriers with the types or dies moving relative to the carriers or mounted on flexible carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a construction of type-bars in that class of type-writers wherein a series of type-bars are mounted to strike at a common printing-point and are guided near their heads by a guide with a flaring slot, into which each type-bar strikes as it approaches the printing-point.

**How it works (reading):** Give each typebar a broad spot (anvil) near the head on its back so a second bar strikes it rather than passing alongside.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** With typebars in a flaring guide slot, a second bar can wedge beside a first bar still in the slot.

**Solution:** Give each typebar a broad spot (anvil) near the head on its back so a second bar strikes it rather than passing alongside.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot in a segment of less than half a circle and are positioned at the printing point by a flaring-slot guide. A broad spot on the back of each bar near its head lets a second bar hit the first bar's broad spot if the first is still in the guide. Neither bar can enter the V-shaped gap and wedge.
