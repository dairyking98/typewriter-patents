## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Escapement feed that can be set to act on either the downstroke or the upstroke of the key lever.

**Solution:** A dog-rocker with two dog members and a switch that can be set so the carriage steps on key descent or key return.

**How it works:** Hook-like members on the key levers act on a universal bar. A rack on the carriage truck meshes with a pinion on a shaft whose rear end carries an escapement wheel. A pivoted dog-rocker carries two dog members on a pivot screw, spring-loaded, with tails. A locking switch member on a squared pivot pin, with a slotted screw, selects whether the feed occurs on the down or up stroke.
