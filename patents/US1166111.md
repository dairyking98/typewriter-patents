## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for perforating or stencil cutting using special types or dies

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to mechanism for controlling the ink ribbon of a typewriting or likemachine and one object of the invention is to provide for automat1- cally discontinuing the ribbon feed during a tabulating advance of the paper carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Beveled gears on a sliding shaft are alternately engaged so the ribbon feed stops during a tabulating advance of the carriage, and a simplified ribbon vibrator is driven from the spring drum through this gearing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon keeps feeding during a tabulating advance, and the ribbon vibrator and spring-drum drive to the ribbon shaft are complicated.

**Solution:** Beveled gears on a sliding shaft are alternately engaged so the ribbon feed stops during a tabulating advance of the carriage, and a simplified ribbon vibrator is driven from the spring drum through this gearing.
