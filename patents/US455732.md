## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The key-lever contact is slow, which limits the speed of working in matrix machines.

**Solution:** An alignment bar of a matrix machine: a flexible bar or lever carries a plate of characters on a sliding pivot, and a contact key at the front is pressed through a beveled hole to bring the die to the print point.

**How it works:** The lever carries the die plate on a sliding pivot; the contact key presses down on a contact beneath a beveled index hole, which brings the die to the fixed point and closes the circuit for the impression device.
