## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with means for effecting line or character spacing in either direction

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists infoertain features of construction and combinations and arrangements of parts, all of which will be same, fully set forth herein and particularly pointed out in the claims.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage feed is reversed so it steps left to right, and the escapement is changed so some characters give a greater carriage movement than others, with minimal changes to the standard machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An ordinary machine must write Arabic, moving step by step right to left with unequal character widths.

**Solution:** The carriage feed is reversed so it steps left to right, and the escapement is changed so some characters give a greater carriage movement than others, with minimal changes to the standard machine.

**Features:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
