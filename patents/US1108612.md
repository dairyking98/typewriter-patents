## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines and contemplates an improved means for uniformly positioning the paper on the r-aten.

**How it works (reading):** A positioning device on the platen sets each card so the first line is at the same distance from its top.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards needed the first writing line placed at a uniform distance from the card top.

**Solution:** A positioning device on the platen sets each card so the first line is at the same distance from its top.

**How it works:** A card-positioning device on the platen locates each card to a uniform first line.
