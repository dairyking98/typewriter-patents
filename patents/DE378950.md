## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (buffer damper) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for providing different damping effects at different parts of the stroke

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a buffer or StoMimpfervorrichtung, consisting of one with the buffer or Piston rod connected outer and a sliding inside, the buffer rod encompassing inner housing, widened at one end, in which a spring action upright piston carried along by the buffer rod is slidably mounted, whose recoil is absorbed.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded piston moves in a bell housing, and tapering channels in the bell bottom let air escape more as compression rises, cushioning the stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Buffers and shock absorbers can burst or give harsh recoil when compressed beyond their design load.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded piston moves in a bell housing, and tapering channels in the bell bottom let air escape more as compression rises, cushioning the stop.

**Features:** [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** bell; stepped shaft; housing; piston; spring plate; tapering air channels
