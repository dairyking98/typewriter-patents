## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to a variablefeed mechanism for type-writers and the obj ect of my invention is to enable the carriagefeed to be adjusted according to requirements, the construction being such that the detent members of the escapement are not in immediate engagement with the rack secured to the carriage, but are connected therewith by spur and escape wheels.

**How it works (reading):** Several escape wheels or racks with different pitches are set radially or side by side on a spindle, so one can be brought into engagement with the detents to give any fraction or multiple of the tooth space.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage feed can only be changed by omitting a rack tooth, which doubles the spacing, and differing font widths need the whole escapement exchanged.

**Solution:** Several escape wheels or racks with different pitches are set radially or side by side on a spindle, so one can be brought into engagement with the detents to give any fraction or multiple of the tooth space.

**How it works:** The detents engage whichever escape wheel is selected, through spur and escape wheels, so the carriage feed is set to the font spacing.
