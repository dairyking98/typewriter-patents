## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing device for cash registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing device for cash registers and accounting machines, in which of several type carrier sets arranged at one printing point @vahlweise an impression is to be taken of one or more of them.

**How it works (reading):** A common pressure pad carrier slides and runs its stroke until just before the types; only then does the selected pressure pad move alone to make the imprint, separating large-mass carrier motion from small-mass pad motion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With several type carrier sets at one print point, each needs its own hammer, or moving the whole pressure pad carrier at full stroke causes large inertia forces that wear the types and adjustment shafts.

**Solution:** A common pressure pad carrier slides and runs its stroke until just before the types; only then does the selected pressure pad move alone to make the imprint, separating large-mass carrier motion from small-mass pad motion.

**How it works:** The carrier stroke uses the document carriage movement, and a small displacement of the single selected pad delivers the printing impact.
