> Description Translated from German Printing device for cash registers and accounting machines. the The invention relates to a printing device for cash registers and accounting machines, in which of several type carrier sets arranged at one printing point @vahlweise an impression is to be taken of one or more of them. There are devices known in which for the purpose of optional Imprint of different type carrier sets of the same type for each type set a special one, print hammer rotatable about a fixed axis is provided and only that print hammer is driven, which is to cause an imprint. Arrangements are also known in which for a majority of Pressure pad a common pressure pad carrier is provided, which the Executes pressure stroke, whereby those pressure cushions which are to take effect, receive a previous feed movement in the direction of the flapping movement. The invention consists essentially in the fact that for the sliding Pressure pad joint pressure pad carrier during the printing process until shortly before the types is brought and only then by moving the selected pressure pad the desired imprint is made.
