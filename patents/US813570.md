## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Keys strike with a jar on the fingers because the type strikes the platen directly, and several bars struck together can pound against each other.

**Solution:** A yieldable rock-bar joins each key-stem to its type-bar, cushioning the key depression and preventing bars from pounding; the key-action is made as a unit that can be mounted and removed from the main frame.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** yieldable rock-bar; key-stems or plungers; type-bars; key-action unit
