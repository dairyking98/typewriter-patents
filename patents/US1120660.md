## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, and with regard to the more specific features thereof', to line lock and margin release mechanism therefor.

**How it works (reading):** A line lock and margin release mechanism that relieves strain on the locking member when it is not in the correct relative position, and returns moving parts silently without impact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line lock and margin release can jam or be damaged when the locking member is actuated out of position, and they make noise.

**Solution:** A line lock and margin release mechanism that relieves strain on the locking member when it is not in the correct relative position, and returns moving parts silently without impact.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
