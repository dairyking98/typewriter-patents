## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Rivets, shouldered rivets and screws joining a type-bar's hanger arms do not hold the bearing adjustment reliably, and the arms can spring outward.

**Solution:** The hanger arms of a type bar are united by a fastening that the OCR does not render legibly, so the exact joining method cannot be read from this text.
