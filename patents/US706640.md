## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed with two or more sets of type or printing elements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-actions'for writing-machines;

**How it works (reading):** A type-bar set with many bars arranged forward of and below the printing point, each connected to its key. The keys get extra initial purchase, the key leverage drops during the printing stroke, and the return springs are adjustable.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Full-keyboard front-strike machines need many type-bars, and the key leverage is too weak to start the type-bars well.

**Solution:** A type-bar set with many bars arranged forward of and below the printing point, each connected to its key. The keys get extra initial purchase, the key leverage drops during the printing stroke, and the return springs are adjustable.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
