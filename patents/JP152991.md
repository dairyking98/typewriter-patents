## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter's platen feed rack can be fed forward by a full or half pitch, but there is no simple way to step it back by the matching full or half pitch.

**Solution:** A back-feed pawl and a forward-feed pawl are each limited by a stepped L-lever pair moved together by one knob, so the back-feed stroke equals the forward feed, full or half.

**How it works:** A spring-loaded tilting feed pawl (4) engages a rack (3) on the platen frame; stepped L-levers (14) and (15), moved by a knob and pull rod via rocking lever (17), choose which of two steps catches the pawl tip, giving a full or half feed per stroke. The printing lever (8) swings a stop-pawl rocker (7) through rocking levers (10) and (11), so that the rack is held while the feed pawl is repositioned, then fed as the stroke ends. A separate back-feed pawl (24) hinged on rocking lever (26) works against the matching steps (20) and (21) of L-lever (15). Moving a handle head (32) along an arc slot turns a shaft (31) and link (30), swinging the lever (26) so the pawl moves the rack back by the same distance as the forward pawl.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Platen back-feed device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a platen back-feed device for a typewriter, made as follows. An L-shaped bent lever, whose tip has horizontal steps for engaging the back-feed pawl, is fixed to the end of a lever that can move forward and back. To the upright end of this L-lever is hinged the upright end of a second L-shaped bent lever, whose tip has horizontal steps for engaging the forward-feed pawl. A spring-loaded back-feed pawl, which is to engage the back-feed steps, is hinged on a rocking lever. A link that swings this rocking lever left and right engages its lower end, and the link is connected to the end of a striking lever that raises and lowers it. The object is to make it possible, by a simple striking action, to feed the platen back by a pitch that matches the forward feed, whether that is a set pitch or half of it.
