## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Printing machines needing quick, simple, accurate impression want a strong striking device that gives a repeatable blow however the hand presses.

**Solution:** Pressing a U-shaped frame drives a weighted cam that cocks a spring-loaded hammer and slips off it, so the spring throws the rubber-tipped hammer against the type face.

**How it works:** Two hammer supports stand on a base with a hammer on a shaft between them, its upper tip faced with rubber. Pressing a handle bar lowers a U-shaped lifting frame, whose ends push down one end of two pivoted levers; their other ends rise and tilt a friction motion piece against a return spring. A weighted cam on that piece pushes the lower part of the hammer open, stretching a coil spring behind it. When the cam slips off, the spring snaps the hammer so the rubber tip hits the work against the type face. The cam falls back by weight and the return spring; an adjustable spring stops the hammer staying on the type or bouncing.
