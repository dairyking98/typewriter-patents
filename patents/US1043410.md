## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A means to operate a typewriter automatically from a perforated sheet to reproduce form letters or address envelopes, with the carriage returned automatically.

**Solution:** Pneumatic bellows controlled by perforated belts on a tracker-board operate striking levers that press the typewriter's keys and space bar.

**How it works:** A typewriter sits in a desk with striking levers beneath, one per key and the space bar and shift keys. Perforated sheets pass over a tracker-board, and perforations register with tubes that admit air to bellows connected to a suction pump. The bellows rock the levers, which strike the keys. A motor-driven gear and clutches feed the belts, and pneumatic means return the carriage at line end.
