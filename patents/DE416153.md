## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter cleaning is inconsistent when done by unskilled workers and oil draws dust into the works.

**Solution:** Machines are hung in a solvent bath and moved up and down by eccentrics, then blown dry with compressed air.
