## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists of novel featuresand parts and combinations of the same, which will be more fully described hereinafter and then pointed out in the claims.

**How it works (reading):** A pointer pivoted on a lever at the escapement support is adjusted to the impression point and indicates both the position on the scale and the line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot see where the next character will print when the carriage is raised, which hampers blanks and ruled paper.

**Solution:** A pointer pivoted on a lever at the escapement support is adjusted to the impression point and indicates both the position on the scale and the line.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
