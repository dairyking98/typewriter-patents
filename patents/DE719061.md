## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts in which one paper or set is fed towards printing position from the front of the apparatus

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a fore-stalk device for typewriters, typewriters and similar machines with a front the platen lying below the feed rollers, because imports of a pre-inserted Writing sheet in the gap between platen and P, paper guide sheet serving, depending on the front, on the other operated control elements during a write process in and out of action baffle, which in its action in the path of the Types lays.

**How it works (reading):** The baffle and a key lock that stops keystrokes are moved automatically in and out of action by the carriage movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A pre-inserted sheet's baffle was left in the type path after insertion, so typing struck it and it was damaged.

**Solution:** The baffle and a key lock that stops keystrokes are moved automatically in and out of action by the carriage movement.
