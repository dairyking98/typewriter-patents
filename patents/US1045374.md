## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Underwood bichrome ribbon vibrator the lever can swing and cover the printing point when writing stencils.

**Solution:** A guide panel positively holds the ribbon vibrator lever down whenever the actuator is in its neutral position, whatever the platen shift.

**How it works:** The universal bar actuator engages one of two pins on the vibrator lever at different distances from its fulcrum, giving a short or long throw to show the top or bottom ribbon color. A middle actuator position engages neither pin. An arm on the lever then meets a guide panel that locks the vibrator down so the types strike the paper directly for stencils. The lever fulcrum moves with the platen shift rail.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator lever; actuator; universal bar; guide panel; platen shift rail; shift key
