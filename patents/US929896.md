## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates more particularly to improvements in means for shifting` the platen or its equivalent to print either upper or lower case letters in a typewriting machine of the class having` two or more types on each type bar.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded plunger acting on a double-armed rock shaft holds the platen in either shifted position yieldingly, and a shift key temporarily overrides it.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines with two types per bar shift the platen to print upper or lower case; the inventors want the platen to be settable to either case normally yet temporarily switchable to the other case by a key that returns automatically when released.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded plunger acting on a double-armed rock shaft holds the platen in either shifted position yieldingly, and a shift key temporarily overrides it.

**How it works:** The platen shaft ends ride in bearings on vertical slides in the carriage end bars. A pair of toggle levers under the shaft, linked by a rod to an arm on a rock shaft, raise the platen for upper case. A spring-pressed plunger with a roller bears on one of two arms of the rock shaft, holding the platen in either position and yielding when the spring is compressed. A key lever linked to a plate with a curved slot on a second rock shaft shifts the platen temporarily and the spring restores it when the key is released. A locking device ties the platen to the carriage.
