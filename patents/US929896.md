## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates more particularly to improvements in means for shifting` the platen or its equivalent to print either upper or lower case letters in a typewriting machine of the class having` two or more types on each type bar.

**How it works (reading):** A toggle-lever platen lift that holds the platen in either position by spring and lets a key temporarily select the opposite case, returning on release.

## Reading (opening only, OCR [])

**Problem:** Platen-shift typewriters with two types per bar need a way to set the platen for upper or lower case and to flip temporarily to the other case.

**Solution:** A toggle-lever platen lift that holds the platen in either position by spring and lets a key temporarily select the opposite case, returning on release.

**How it works:** The platen shaft bearings ride in vertical slides on the carriage end bars. A pair of toggle levers, moved by a rod that slides with the carriage and engages an arm on a rock shaft, raise the platen to the upper-case position. A plunger with roller, pressed by a spring against arms on the rock shaft, holds the platen yieldingly in either position. A key temporarily sets the other case and the platen returns automatically when it is released.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen shaft bearing; toggle lever; rock shaft; spring plunger; keeper
