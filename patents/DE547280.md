**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the known ones Flat-plate typewriters with a movable in the main frame for line separation Writing board frame, in which one is elastic for carrying and fixing the document Supported plate is built in, which is pressed down by hand to release the document can be.

**How it works (reading):** A control lever on the plate frame runs up a cam fixed to the machine frame at the end of travel and pivots a rod that depresses the plate automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In flat-plate machines the spring-supported document plate must be released by hand at the end of frame travel.

**Solution:** A control lever on the plate frame runs up a cam fixed to the machine frame at the end of travel and pivots a rod that depresses the plate automatically.

**Features:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
