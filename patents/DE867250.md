## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hard type-lever rests are noisy, while soft ones let falling type levers bounce back into neighbours.

**Solution:** A rubber tube floats freely on a frame between support points; it absorbs the energy of a falling type lever and does not spring the lever back.
