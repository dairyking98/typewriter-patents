## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing-point indicators catch paper-feed rollers, interfere with carriage travel, blur the print and tear paper.

**Solution:** A printing-point indicator that can be attached to or detached from its moving means, so it stays clear of the paper and feed devices and can be moved away for erasures.

**How it works:** A printing-point indicator that can be attached to or detached from its moving means, so it stays clear of the paper and feed devices and can be moved away for erasures.
