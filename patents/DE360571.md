## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** Each decimal tab key has its own spring-loaded catch lever with a nose that releases the carriage only when the tab lever is in position; a shared bracket with a shock arm is driven in sequence by two stops on the tab push rods, and the catch lever doubles as a brake against the toothed rack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column tabulator release on earlier designs can let the paper carriage move before the tab lever is in its working position, and the tab linkage is hard to arrange on the machine.

**Solution:** Each decimal tab key has its own spring-loaded catch lever with a nose that releases the carriage only when the tab lever is in position; a shared bracket with a shock arm is driven in sequence by two stops on the tab push rods, and the catch lever doubles as a brake against the toothed rack.
