## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix machine feed devices) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines for forming stereotype-matrices by the successive impression of independent type-dies, and has for its object the improvement of the feed devices for the matrix-carriage and of the arrangement of the electrical devices by which variable amounts of feed are obtained for the varying r 5 sizes of letters.

**How it works (reading):** A matrix machine in which space-throwing keys and electrical contact plates and magnets give variable feed amounts for letters of different widths.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed devices of the matrix carriage and the electrical arrangement for variable feed need improvement for varying letter sizes.

**Solution:** A matrix machine in which space-throwing keys and electrical contact plates and magnets give variable feed amounts for letters of different widths.

**How it works:** Electrical contact plates connect the circuits to magnets; the space-throwing keys set variable feed, and an escapement portion moves the carriage a set amount.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
