## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Positive-feed mechanisms automatically operated in response to carriage return

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to that class of typewriters in which an impression or paper-carrying cylinder or roller mounted in a traveling frame or carriage is employed, such frame being automatically moved forward with a step-by-step motion as the keys are operated and moved backward by the operator whenever a new line is to be commenced.

**How it works (reading):** A hinged inclined track on the frame trips a pawl lever on the roller axis so that returning the carriage automatically line-spaces the roller.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On the Crandall typewriter the operator needs two separate motions at line end: push the carriage back and turn the roller for line space; forgetting the second overprints the line.

**Solution:** A hinged inclined track on the frame trips a pawl lever on the roller axis so that returning the carriage automatically line-spaces the roller.

**How it works:** A track is hinged to the fixed frame in front of and parallel to the paper roller, in the path of a pawl-carrying lever pivoted on the roller axis. As the operator pushes the carriage back, the lever rides the track and its pawl turns a ratchet wheel on the roller shaft. A knob on the left end of the roller shaft is pivoted so the roller can turn independently of it. The track hinges so it clears the lever on the forward travel.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md)

**Parts:** hinged track; pawl carrier; pawl; ratchet wheel; paper roller; carriage frame; return knob; thumb lever; pressure roller

**Improves on:**

- Crandall typewriter: carriage return now automatically gives the line space in one motion
