## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen can only turn in fixed steps from its line-spacing ratchet, which does not match ruled lines or blank spaces on printed forms.

**Solution:** Endwise movement of the platen shaft disconnects the platen from its ratchet-wheel so it can be turned freely a variable distance, then springs back to re-engage the ratchet.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen shaft; line-spacing ratchet-wheel; detent; spring ring; line-space pawl and lever; dog carrier

**Numbers:** Patent 648,100; Serial No. 680,801
