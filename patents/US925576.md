## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Front-strike machines let the paper fall forward and block the view of the writing or drop into the type basket.

**Solution:** A clip on the paper table holds a post and arms that keep the paper in its rear position and guide long record sheets without disturbing sheets already in place.
