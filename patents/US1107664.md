## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** U-shaped type bars carrying two or three letters each are hard to assemble in two groups, because some bars would have to be too long and would bend.

**Solution:** Simple curved type bars arranged in four groups around the platen, so that shifting the platen selects the different forms of type; the writing is visible while typing.
