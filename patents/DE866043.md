## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for Management and attachment of the removable type lever basket for writing and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A top and a bottom pair of links on each side, one link of each pair on the basket and the other on the frame, guide the basket into its seat: the two lower pairs set the basic position, and the two upper pairs, under adjustable spring tension, push and lock it in one exact end position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dovetail prism-rod guides for the removable type lever basket are costly, and large screws through the frame wall are bulky and spoil the appearance; inserting the basket also needs some skill.

**Solution:** A top and a bottom pair of links on each side, one link of each pair on the basket and the other on the frame, guide the basket into its seat: the two lower pairs set the basic position, and the two upper pairs, under adjustable spring tension, push and lock it in one exact end position.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** upper link pairs; lower link pairs; adjustable spring tension
