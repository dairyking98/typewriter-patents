> Description Translated from German Device for guiding and fastening the removable type lever basket in writing and the like. The invention relates to a device for Management and attachment of the removable type lever basket for writing and the like. Machinery. In the known devices serving this purpose, the type lever basket is usually guided in dovetail-like prism rods. The production Such tours are quite cumbersome and the prime costs are therefore quite right high. To fasten the type lever basket to the machine frame, large Screws with rimmed heads that pass through the wall of the machine frame from the outside engage through into the side walls of the basket, the same not only bulky act, but also adversely affect the appearance of the machine. Even the handling of inserting the type lever basket requires a certain skill. These disadvantages are caused by a facility that is not visible from the outside fixed that a quick and safe handling when removing and reinserting of the type lever basket in the machine frame and because of its simplicity can be produced cheaply.
