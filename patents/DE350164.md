## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side lays or gauges

**How it works (reading):** The paper support plate is widened upward, with a window cut-out exposing the margin scale and pointers at eye level.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops hidden behind the paper support plate are hard to reach.

**Solution:** The paper support plate is widened upward, with a window cut-out exposing the margin scale and pointers at eye level.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
