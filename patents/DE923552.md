## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inking apparatus with ink applied by pads or rotary discs

**How it works (reading):** Colour the platen itself with a solid ink stick, either full platen length or travelling along the platen, so the writing system transfers colour from platen to paper.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carbon paper used to colour the paper in picture telegraph devices is mechanically delicate and awkward to run with the paper over the platen.

**Solution:** Colour the platen itself with a solid ink stick, either full platen length or travelling along the platen, so the writing system transfers colour from platen to paper.

**How it works:** A coloured stick (oil, grease or wax) is pressed against the platen or moved along it at least as fast as the print head travels. The paper runs over the platen without being coloured by the stick. A magnetic writing system presses a diamond tip through the paper against the platen, and the paper takes up colour at that point. Any print head, even a type lever, could be used. The ink carrier is moved at line feed rate since each impression removes ink.
