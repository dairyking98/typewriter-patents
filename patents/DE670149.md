## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for coloring typewriter color ribbons or the like, where the color on the # I'and iii means of a rotating disc is applied.

**How it works (reading):** The transfer disc is carried on its shaft by a helical spring that lets it move sideways on its shaft under the pressure of the ribbon, so it presses elastically against the ribbon and the ink feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rotating ink-transfer disc in a ribbon re-inking device is pressed against the ribbon and the ink supply by a spreading spring fixed to the frame, which is awkward to set.

**Solution:** The transfer disc is carried on its shaft by a helical spring that lets it move sideways on its shaft under the pressure of the ribbon, so it presses elastically against the ribbon and the ink feed.
