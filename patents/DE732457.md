## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a scball and: vibration-reducing pad for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Rubber balls or rollers held loosely with lateral play in opposed recesses between two stacked plates, so both vertical and horizontal shocks are cushioned.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sound-absorbing pads with springs fail against sideways carriage impacts, and resilient feet only damp vertical shocks, so the machine can tip over.

**Solution:** Rubber balls or rollers held loosely with lateral play in opposed recesses between two stacked plates, so both vertical and horizontal shocks are cushioned.
