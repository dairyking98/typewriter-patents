## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be turned by an arbitrary amount independent of the line-space wheel, which blocks fractional line spacing.

**Solution:** Connect and disconnect the platen from the line-space wheel so the platen can turn any amount on its own; several locking forms are shown.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
