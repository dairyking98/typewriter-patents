## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A second ribbon wastes material, and carbon paper is consumed on every line even when only part of it is worn.

**Solution:** Thin carbon paper is held by guide plates in a bulge over the platen at the writing line so that it stays put, and a supply roll is housed within the bulge.
