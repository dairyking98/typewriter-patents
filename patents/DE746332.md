## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In type-wheel printers on punched-card tabulating machines, a spring pulling the wheel against the abutment makes the type bounce and strike repeatedly, smearing the print.

**Solution:** Swing each type wheel's arm against the abutment under a spring that passes through its state of lowest tension during the swing, so the rising tension at the end prevents re-striking after rebound.

**How it works:** Each type wheel is set by rolling on a drive wheel under card control: a feeler brush senses a hole, a magnet releases a coupling pawl, and a shaft is coupled to the drive in step with the brush so the wheel turns to the sensed value. The wheel then swings on its arm, without disengaging from the drive wheel, against the printing abutment. Because the spring is at minimum tension partway, near the end of travel it pushes the wheel back from the abutment rather than into it. A device regulates the type stroke pressure.
