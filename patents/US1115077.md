## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in front strike or visible writing typewriting machines in which the type-bars are mounted on a supporting segment which is adapted to be shifted for placing one or another of the sets of type on the type-bars in printing relation to the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Fit a shiftable type-bar segment with a single cast top plate, escapement and ribbon vibrator operated directly by the type bars through the universal bar.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A shiftable type-bar segment needs workable carriage escapement, ribbon vibrator and easy assembly in a front-strike Moyer-type machine.

**Solution:** Fit a shiftable type-bar segment with a single cast top plate, escapement and ribbon vibrator operated directly by the type bars through the universal bar.

**How it works:** The top plate is one casting screwed to the main frame with an opening for the type bars and universal bar. A separate track casting carries a ball-bearing carriage guide, so different carriage lengths can be supplied. The ribbon vibrator lever connects directly to the universal bar, can move different fields of the ribbon, and can be disengaged. The type-bar segment shifts to bring another set of types to the platen.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** top plate; track casting; carriage; type bar; segment; universal bar; ribbon vibrator; ribbon spool; escapement

**Improves on:**

- Moyer typewriter: adds a shiftable segment and direct-acting escapement and vibrator
