## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes hand-held

**How it works (reading):** Types on a barrel that slides axially and rotates by a shaft driven by a keying mechanism; each type slides radially in recesses in the barrel, drawn inward by a spring, and selected by a dial on the casing lid.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type carrier must bring each character to the printing point by a simple dial selection.

**Solution:** Types on a barrel that slides axially and rotates by a shaft driven by a keying mechanism; each type slides radially in recesses in the barrel, drawn inward by a spring, and selected by a dial on the casing lid.

**Features:** [SEL:dial](../features/sel-dial.md), [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md)

**Parts:** barrel; radial types; spiral springs; character dial; hollow shaft
