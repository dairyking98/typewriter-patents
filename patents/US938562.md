## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanisms for typewritin machines and has for its object to provide improved devices of A the class specified.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon mechanism with a ribbon vibrator carried in an upright portion of the frame, moving the ribbon to the print point, with spool and ribbon arrangement under the top plate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanisms of the period need improved devices for moving and guiding the ribbon; the stated problem is general.

**Solution:** A ribbon mechanism with a ribbon vibrator carried in an upright portion of the frame, moving the ribbon to the print point, with spool and ribbon arrangement under the top plate.

**Features:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
