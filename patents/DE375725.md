## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an accented typewriter that works by depressing it a key carrying an accent mark the switching device of the paper carriage by only influencing the switching wheel of the accent key for the Advancing is blocked.

**How it works (reading):** Releasing the accent key also releases the carriage pawl block, so the blocking does not have to be lifted again on the next stroke, and the ribbon is raised by the accent lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The accent key blocks the carriage escapement, and re-engaging the blocking after release needs extra force on the next keystroke.

**Solution:** Releasing the accent key also releases the carriage pawl block, so the blocking does not have to be lifted again on the next stroke, and the ribbon is raised by the accent lever.
