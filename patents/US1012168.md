## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type action is wanted with a very light touch at the start of key depression and a typebar accelerating towards the platen.

**Solution:** Each key lever rolls on a curved contact face of an angular sub-lever so the engagement point moves from the key fulcrum toward the sub-lever fulcrum, giving gradually rising typebar speed.

**How it works:** A second-order key lever pivoted at the rear presses the contact face of a rearward arm of an angular sub-lever fulcrumed on a rod under the key system. One of the faces is curved, giving a rolling contact whose point recedes from the key fulcrum as the key goes down. A depending sub-lever arm joins a horizontal pull link to the typebar. A contractile spring between key lever and sub-lever restores them.
