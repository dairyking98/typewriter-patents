## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a platen-shift machine the line-space lever must work in every platen position, stay put on the carriage, and allow different line feeds without overthrow.

**Solution:** A compound line-space lever with a roller near the platen-shift pivot and an adjustable pawl-stop that sets the line feed to one, two or three ratchet teeth.

**How it works:** The platen carrier pivots on the carriage; a lever on it with a pull-pawl engages the platen ratchet. A hand lever on the carriage presses on a roller close to the shift pivot, so shifting barely changes their relation. A spring pin on a pawl-stop locates in one of several holes in a segment, limiting pawl engagement to one, two or three teeth. A platen-stop engages the ratchet backs to prevent overthrow, a detent roller holds the platen, and the hand lever also lifts the rack out of mesh with the pinion during line spacing.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** platen carrier; platen; line-space ratchet wheel; pull-pawl; pawl-stop; hole segment; platen-stop; detent spring and roller; hand lever; rack bar

**Numbers:** line feed of 1, 2 or 3 ratchet teeth
