## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbons give weak outlines, prevent bold type, and the blow damages rubber type.

**Solution:** Replace the ribbon on a Hammond-type machine with automatic inking rollers fed from a reservoir and driven by the type-segment shafts.

**How it works:** Vertical shafts moved at each keystroke gear to a reservoir roller and two working rollers that keep a thin ink film, supplied from a screw-in squeezable ink tube. A space-lever-driven sliding bar swings an arm carrying distributing rollers across the type segments to ink the face. The same shafts formerly fed the ribbon.

**Features:** [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [ELEM:sector-segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md), [ELEM:rubber-type](../features/elem-rubber-type.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md)
