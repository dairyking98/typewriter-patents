## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Folding or stowable tables

**How it works (reading):** A fourth scissor pair on the front end links the upper feet with wing-screw Nuremberg scissors, leaving room under the table and adding document shelves.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Scissor-lever table feet obstruct the writer's legs.

**Solution:** A fourth scissor pair on the front end links the upper feet with wing-screw Nuremberg scissors, leaving room under the table and adding document shelves.
