## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The usual printing ribbon has to be fed, reversed and replaced.

**Solution:** Use paper coated with a material that visibly changes colour under electric current and make the platen surface a conductor, so the type closes a battery circuit through the paper and prints with no ribbon.

**How it works:** The platen has a yielding hard-rubber layer covered with a conductive tin-foil layer. A brush on the carriage contacts the foil and connects through an insulated bar on the carriage to a brush on the frame, which links to one side of a battery; the other side connects to the typewriter frame and the type-levers. When a type touches the specially prepared paper, the circuit closes through paper and foil, and the chemical change forms the letter. A bare metal cylinder platen is an alternative.
