## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In the Shimada-type machine the frame holding the type tray is awkward to move over the character index board.

**Solution:** Open the machine's side plates at the front and carry the holding frame on a rail frame, with a T-shaped arm bringing pointer and handle out over the index board so the frame moves easily in any direction.

**How it works:** U-shaped side plates open at the front carry rollers supporting a rectangular rail frame that slides forward and back. A holding frame rides on that rail frame and slides left and right, and holds the type frame. A T-shaped arm fixed to the holding frame's front bar reaches out over the character index board and carries a pointer and a handle. Working the handle moves the holding frame forward, back, sideways, obliquely or in a circle, passing freely through the front opening, until the pointer rests on the wanted character.
