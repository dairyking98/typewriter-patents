## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (receipt printing in cash registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a method and an apparatus for printing double, additionally folded receipt slip, and the required text, which is more than one line, is applied to both the Original as well as printed on the copy, but without changing the usual length difference between both pieces of paper (original and copy).

**How it works (reading):** Receipt printing method and device that prints text across the original and the shorter-offset copy of a folded slip.
