## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A Blickensderfer-type wheel typewriter is to be driven by a compact, simple power mechanism, still usable by hand in an emergency, with the carriage also returned by power.

**Solution:** A constantly running motor shaft is clutched for exactly one revolution by each key, and that one revolution drives an actuating bail that, through key-selected locking levers and graduated tumblers, positions the type wheel and prints.

**How it works:** A motor turns a power shaft with a ratchet wheel; a loose crank wheel carries a pawl that is held off the ratchet by a cam on a controller. A key moves a slide bar that rocks the controller, the pawl engages, and the crank wheel makes one turn, its stop arms releasing it and re-catching it by notches. The crank rocks a shaft carrying an actuating bail; the pressed key has swung a notched bell-crank so that its segment lever locks to the bail. That lever meshes with a graduated tumbler, which slides until its notch grips one of two sector sleeves and turns that sector, rolling a pinion on the type-wheel shaft while the other sector is held locked. When the type is in place the tumbler lifts a bail whose pawl enters a stop ratchet on the wheel shaft and releases the second sector, so both sectors and the wheel then swing together to strike the paper. A friction wheel on the power shaft returns the carriage by power; a thumb screw disconnects the power so keys operate the same chain by hand.

**Parts:** power shaft; ratchet wheel; crank wheel; clutch pawl; controller with cam; slide bar; connecting rod with slip spring; stop arms; actuating bail; locking bell-crank lever; segment lever; graduated tumbler; sector sleeve; sector; pinion; type wheel; stopping ratchet; stop pawl; locking finger; key lever; spacing bar; carriage-return friction wheel; comb guide

**Numbers:** two sectors, one for each half of the keys; one crank-wheel revolution per printing movement

**Improves on:**

- standard hand-operated Blickensderfer machine: adds a constantly running power drive with a key-engaged clutch, and keeps hand operation as a fallback
