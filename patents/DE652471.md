**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type placing and soldering device Soldering device, in particular for placing and soldering types on the type holding members of typewriters with one with alignment links according to the alignment guidelines of the existing type groups.

**How it works (reading):** A clamping part fixed to a polygonal disc of interchangeable alignment stops, one face per type group, determines the position of each type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Placement devices for types are tied to specific type sets, so the types may not sit correctly relative to the type face.

**Solution:** A clamping part fixed to a polygonal disc of interchangeable alignment stops, one face per type group, determines the position of each type.

**How it works:** A rotating polygonal head presents the alignment stop for each type group, and the stops are replaced for other type sets.
