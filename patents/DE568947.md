## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons, carbon paper and ink pads release ink unevenly, so prints get weaker as the supply runs down, and the usual ink-proof coatings do not give an even large supply.

**Solution:** An inking carrier (ribbon, carbon or pad) meant to release ink evenly over its life with a large ink reserve; the construction is only outlined in the first 3000 characters.
