**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed with two or more sets of type or printing elements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to twin typewriting machines, in which two frames are mounted side 'by side, each frame carrying a set of typebars and keys, each type set having a separate printing point, and a paper carriage mounted to travel in each printing one and to cross over from one zone to the other;

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A twin typewriter (two frames, one carriage) must be compact and strong and the carriage must always be under control of one of the two letter-feed mechanisms.

**Solution:** One long rack on the carriage meshes with the pinions of two escapements spaced a rack-length apart, with carriage rails running unbroken across both frames.

**How it works:** Two Underwood-type frames, each with typebars, keys and its own escapement wheel and pinion, are screwed to a common base and joined by bracket-supported rails. The carriage rack is as long as the distance between the two escapement pinions, so when one train finishes the other takes over. The carriage runs on the continuous rails, with a scale bar numbered continuously across both zones.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** machine frame; base; carriage; guide rail; escapement wheel; escapement pinion; carriage rack; scale bar; bracket
