## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix impression clutch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to die-impressing`\| devices for matrix-machines, and specifically to the clutch-box form of devices described in earlier applications for patent made by me and hereinafter definitely referred to.

**How it works (reading):** A two-part clutch-box on a continuously rotated stem is held by a spring; the reciprocating member carries a pin that depresses each presented die and a beveled stop-pin stops its rotation, and the parts are easily separated.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The clutch-box impression devices are hard to take apart for inspection or repair, and their operation and construction could be improved.

**Solution:** A two-part clutch-box on a continuously rotated stem is held by a spring; the reciprocating member carries a pin that depresses each presented die and a beveled stop-pin stops its rotation, and the parts are easily separated.
