## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typist copying from a sheet needs the copy supported and the line and point reached marked, without the mark moving with the carriage.

**Solution:** A copy holder with a roller and scale mounted on the typewriter carriage, whose pointer is held fixed relative to the machine frame through wires, so it steps along the line as the carriage moves.

**How it works:** A frame carrying a copy roller and a scale plate with spring-pressed rollers clips onto the top of the moving carriage. A pointer slides in the frame; a yoke slides on a bar and engages the fixed shaft of the typewriter's escapement wheel, so as the carriage steps the yoke stays still relative to the machine and moves relative to the holder. Piano wires over pulleys connect yoke and pointer so the pointer moves along the scale in step with the carriage. A belt joins the copy roller to the platen so line spacing turns both equally, with a clutch thumb-turn to move them independently.
