## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stenographic machine printing on a longitudinally fed paper band, needing paper and ribbon feed from key action and compact spool layout.

**Solution:** Keys feed the paper band and ribbon, take up the written band on a receiving drum, and a shiftable carriage of multi-type levers.

**How it works:** Two rows of keys act on a cross-bar and type-levers pivoted on a shaft in a carriage at the rear. Each lever carries two or more types. Two shift keys, through levers and a connecting rod, move the carriage forward or back to bring one type to the platen. Key depression feeds the paper from a magazine drum to a receiving drum, feeds the ribbon, and reverses ribbon direction. A winding device is shown that lets the paper band be read in sequence.
