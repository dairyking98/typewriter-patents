## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Writing on the pages of a bound book with a typewriter is awkward.

**Solution:** An adjusting frame fixed to the table supports the platen and tilting base frame so the typewriter can lie over a book and leave leaves free.

**How it works:** A base plate on the table has a spring pawl and ratchet segment plus a lifting lever, pivoted so a cross-bar stays horizontal at every adjusted position (parallelogram). The platen and the base frame carrying the typewriter attach to this adjusting frame and tilt up or back to free a leaf. The platen rests on the book to hold a leaf in place for printing. The carriage can rock through somewhat more than ninety degrees on its frame, with a curved lock to keep the rack in the dog and a finger resting on a bar.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** adjusting frame; base plate; ratchet segment; pawl; lifting lever; cross-bar; base frame; platen; carriage frame; curved lock

**Numbers:** tilt more than 90 degrees
