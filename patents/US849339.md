## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon does not stay in working relation with the platen and typebar.

**Solution:** Links the key levers through a rod and shafts to a ribbon guide so the ribbon is held in front of the typebar at the print point.

**How it works:** A rod under the key levers, held by a spring, drives arms and a shaft to a connecting link; an oscillating lever on a segment moves the ribbon guide by fork-and-pin.
