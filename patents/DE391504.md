## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing speed is limited by one character at a time; earlier group typewriters left gaps or had flexible typebars.

**Solution:** A high-speed typewriter with several keyboards that print letter groups simultaneously, with the carriage stepping by the group's actual width.

**How it works:** The OCR covers the preamble criticizing syllable typewriters, shorthand machines and earlier group typewriters, where each letter group needed several keyboards pressed at once. The invention is a group typewriter in which advance depends on the number of types in the new group, not the previous one. The mechanism description is outside the opening.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** keyboard; type lever; paper carriage

**Improves on:**

- earlier group typewriters: advance follows the new group, no gaps
