## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ten-key calculator keyboard with pen box) · **Subject (patent classes):** Transfer mechanisms, e.g. transfer of a figure from a ten-key keyboard into the pin carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key arrangement for ten-key calculating machines with a pen box.

**How it works (reading):** Key levers swing about one of three axes depending on row and engage a row of pins directly through bent rear ends, with levers on both sides of the pin row centreline and sliding guide rails.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Transferring three rows of keys to a sliding pin box needs several intermediate links, which is awkward on a ten-key calculator keyboard.

**Solution:** Key levers swing about one of three axes depending on row and engage a row of pins directly through bent rear ends, with levers on both sides of the pin row centreline and sliding guide rails.
