## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locks for subsidiary keys, e.g. for shift keys

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement in typewriters with a switchover lock, which is released once again by the keystroke following the switchover or, for individual cases, by actuating a release lever, according to Patent 851 502.

**How it works (reading):** A support member, held against a spring, permanently disables the shift lock by blocking its entry into the switching frame path.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A shift lock that engages after every shift key press slows two-handed touch typing.

**Solution:** A support member, held against a spring, permanently disables the shift lock by blocking its entry into the switching frame path.

**Features:** [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
