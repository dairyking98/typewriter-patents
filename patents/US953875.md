## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriting in a book is impossible with normal machines, which are costly and need trained operators.

**Solution:** A support holds an ordinary book so that its leaves sit on the platen and follow the carriage, with means for carbon copies in the book.
