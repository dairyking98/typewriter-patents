## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guides in the printing zone, e.g. guides for preventing contact of conveyed sheets with printhead

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of typewriters to which the Oaligraph and Remington belong.

**How it works (reading):** A bow-shaped guide spanning the full platen width so the paper edge slides under it automatically when drawn around the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be inserted under a small central tongue, which wastes time and lets the paper sag or tear.

**Solution:** A bow-shaped guide spanning the full platen width so the paper edge slides under it automatically when drawn around the platen.
