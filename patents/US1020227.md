## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper controlling mechanism for typewriting machines and its general object is to provide improved paper clamping, paper-feeding and paper stopdevices which are especially adapted for use inv connection vwith heavy or thick work sheets, such as stencil sheets.

**How it works (reading):** Paper clamping, feeding and stop devices on the carriage hold heavy sheets against the platen and stop them at set positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Thick work sheets such as stencils cannot be held and stopped reliably by the usual platen.

**Solution:** Paper clamping, feeding and stop devices on the carriage hold heavy sheets against the platen and stop them at set positions.
