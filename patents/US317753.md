## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter that is simple, durable, easy to operate and cheaper to make than the machines commonly used.

**Solution:** A type-disk with one or more alphabets on its underside, turned by a lever whose point locks into an indented letter-plate, is carried on a hinged cover that is pressed down to print through a slot in an inking pan; releasing it springs the paper carriage one letter space.

**How it works:** A circular type-disk is on a spindle through a hinged top plate, with a small perforated disk above it. An operating lever on a loose spring plate has a stud engaging one of the perforations, which chooses which alphabet on the disk is in use, and a pointed stud that is turned into an indentation on a segmental letter-plate engraved with the characters. This brings the chosen type to the print position over a slot in an inking pan holding a pad, which inks the type as the disk turns. Pressing the cover down prints the character on the paper and also clamps it. On release an arm on the cover has turned a rock-shaft which lets a flat spring push the carriage forward one tooth of its ratchet bar. A lever lifts the spring dog from the ratchet bar to return the carriage; a perforated disk and crank on the feed roller give line spacing.
