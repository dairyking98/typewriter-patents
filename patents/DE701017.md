## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to line switches for platen rollers to write Business machines with a slide that carries the ratchet, where the Switching different line spacings the start of the engagement of the pawl is adjusted in the ratchet by an actuator.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable actuator sets the engagement start of the pawl in the ratchet; the stop returns automatically after setting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing ratchet setting needed self-locking gears or a separate lock, which made assembly difficult.

**Solution:** An adjustable actuator sets the engagement start of the pawl in the ratchet; the stop returns automatically after setting.
