## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the construction of devices for writing music with a machine;

**How it works (reading):** A keyboard attachment with musical-character keys, using detachable keys and rack-bars to drive the carriage.
