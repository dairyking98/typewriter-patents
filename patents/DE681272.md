## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letter-width and line-justifying typewriters were unreliable or expensive, and rack or screw feeds could not justify lines since word spaces stayed fixed.

**Solution:** Carriage feed is built up from binary-weighted basic values added in a multiple differential gear, with a counting device adding extra feed per letter or space in a second pass.

**How it works:** Key lever arms actuate none or some of six switch brackets via notched rails; each bracket releases a motor-driven wheel to advance the carriage a fixed amount, from 0.1 to 6.3 mm in 63 steps. The increments add in the differential gear. For line justification a first typing pass counts spaces and the missing length, and a second pass adds the extra feed per space or letter. A simpler variant with three basic values gives seven type widths.

**Parts:** key lever; switch bracket; notched rail; pawl; feed wheel; differential gear; friction clutch; motor axle; counting device
