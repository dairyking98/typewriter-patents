Numerous proposals have already been made to equip typewriters with a line compensation device, but these types of construction have various deficiencies. Some of them were not suitable for the necessary long-term stress, others were too expensive, so that usable machines have not yet appeared on the market. For the same reasons, letter-compensated machines have not yet been able to be introduced into practice on a large scale. In the known machines with letter compensation, the paper trolley in each case advanced by a distance that corresponds to the width of the typeface of the posted Type corresponds, whereby the advance of the paper carriage by means of a rack or by a screw spindle drive.
