## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accent keys must print without advancing the carriage, and the ribbon makes a full up-and-down stroke on every keystroke, which costs force and noise in fast typing.

**Solution:** Cut away the accent key lever so it does not press the universal bar, and join the ribbon lever to the bar through a weakly spring-held jaw so the ribbon stays up during fast typing.

**How it works:** Normal key levers press a pivoted universal bar that trips the carriage feed through a ratchet wheel and also lifts the ribbon fork via a pin and lever arms. An accent key lever has a cutout, so it does not depress the bar and the carriage does not advance. The ribbon is still lifted through a connecting rod to the same lever arms. The pin drives the ribbon lever through a hinged jaw held by a weak spring, so on quick successive strokes the jaw opens slightly and the ribbon stays near the print point, dropping fully only in a pause.
