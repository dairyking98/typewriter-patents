## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Multiple web-feeding apparatus for webs superimposed during printing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to automatic feeding devices for the carbon-sheet, mounted on a holder on the track-frame of a type-Writing machine of that type having a flat platen.

**How it works (reading):** Pawls on the carbon roller track-frame ratchets advance the carbon sheet forward or backward automatically when the track-frame is raised or lowered.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carbon sheet on a flat platen must be moved by hand, which wastes it and soils the fingers.

**Solution:** Pawls on the carbon roller track-frame ratchets advance the carbon sheet forward or backward automatically when the track-frame is raised or lowered.
