## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to mechanism attachable to typewriters for thepurpose of returning the carriage either to the commencement of the line, 'or' toany intermediate point, and at the same time, except when otherwise desired, advancing the platen, so as to write it new line.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment with a front operating lever on a rocking shaft, connected to the carriage or line spacer, returns the carriage and advances the platen; a lever or treadle can be used, and an adjustable stop on the frame cushions the carriage on its return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage to the line start, with a line advance, normally needs the typist to reach for the carriage, and the marginal stop takes shock when the carriage is pulled back.

**Solution:** An attachment with a front operating lever on a rocking shaft, connected to the carriage or line spacer, returns the carriage and advances the platen; a lever or treadle can be used, and an adjustable stop on the frame cushions the carriage on its return.
