## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A calculating machine cannot print non-additive numbers such as dates or years without those keyed values entering the counter.

**Solution:** A non-addition key and the sum lever act on one common linkage that disengages the counter from the drive shaft during the crank reverse stroke, so keyed numbers print without being counted; the same part also handles the sum printing.
