## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check embossing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines for indeliblyimpressin g, marking orcuttin g checks, drafts, or other monetary instruments with figures representing their value, or other desired letters, figures, marks or characters, for the purpose of defeating any attempt at fraudulent alteration of such instruments, and particularly to that class of machines in which the said characters are embossed, and not merely perforated.

**How it works (reading):** A check embosser with male and female dies, feed wheels and a notched guide plate that operate from one lever to emboss characters into the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Perforated figures on checks can be altered; embossing is a stronger safeguard.

**Solution:** A check embosser with male and female dies, feed wheels and a notched guide plate that operate from one lever to emboss characters into the paper.
