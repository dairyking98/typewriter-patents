## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing typebar machines need a compact arrangement where bars strike a common point on the platen and the platen shifts for capitals.

**Solution:** Pivot the typebars obliquely to strike one point on the upper front of the platen, and shift the platen obliquely in a plane parallel to the typebar supports for upper case.

**How it works:** Typebar hangers sit on ring segments inclined to the horizontal and concentric with the printing point, the bars swinging rearward and downward. A carriage with rollers and a rack, driven by a pinion, carries the platen on parallel rods and arms from two parallel shafts, so shifting the platen moves it along a line parallel to the support planes and the two type faces on each bar. Each typebar has two or more types. The cut text does not give the key action.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
