## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text of the opening does not state the problem beyond a new construction of type-writing machine.

**Solution:** A bed machine in which two semicircular rows of typebars are pivoted on an oscillating type-plate under a carriage, with a pointer and letter-plate on the bed and a hammer striking upward against the paper.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on grooved rollers over the bed with its paper roller on a flip-over frame so the paper index can be inspected. Typebars sit in two semicircular rows on pivot wires in grooves of a type-plate on an oscillating shaft with a toothed segment, and a spring roller bears on projections of the plate to hold positions. A pointer at the front and a letter-plate (Fig. 1a) suggest the operator picks the character by moving the pointer over the plate. A hammer then strikes the chosen type upward against the paper through a gap between two curved paper guide plates. Further parts (inking device, bell) are described past the opening and not seen here.
