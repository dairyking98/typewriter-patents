## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Carbon copies look like originals and the imprint cannot be told apart from the type face or pigment colour.

**Solution:** A ribbon with fine wires set between the warp threads gives a distinctive imprint that also transfers to the carbon copy.

**How it works:** Wires in the weave are compressed by the type strike, leaving a mark independent of ribbon colour.
