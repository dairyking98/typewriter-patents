## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The low-priced rubber-type segment machine of US427637 prints only capitals (small caps), but the trade wants lower case plus capitals.

**Solution:** The rubber type segment carries two concentric rows, lower case inside and capitals outside, and can slide on its post so either row's centre of curvature is brought to the print position.

**How it works:** The rubber segment is fixed to a metal plate that is slotted to straddle the post carrying it, with a screw slot and guide plate on the post end, so it can slide longitudinally. A spring holds it so the lower-case row, centred on one pivot, is in position. Pinching a small lever against the post pulls the segment back so the capital row, centred on a second pivot, takes the same place. Releasing returns it by the spring. The segment is vibrated around its post by the operating lever as before.
