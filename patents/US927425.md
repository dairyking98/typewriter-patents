## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** Rails with opposing grooves carry rollers in pairs with axes at right angles; one rail is adjustable by holding and set screws to give a close fit, and a channel rail is removable for substituting carriage lengths.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage truck needs to be rigid against vibration and easily fitted to the rails, and the inventor says the rail setting should be adjustable.

**Solution:** Rails with opposing grooves carry rollers in pairs with axes at right angles; one rail is adjustable by holding and set screws to give a close fit, and a channel rail is removable for substituting carriage lengths.
