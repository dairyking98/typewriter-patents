## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to attachments for type-writers.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoted rack-bar on the carriage with a carriage-feed release lets the carriage space to set positions.
