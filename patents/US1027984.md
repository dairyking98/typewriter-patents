## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electric typewriters have required new construction rather than the parts of ordinary machines.

**Solution:** A thrust bar between key bar and operating bar is struck by a rotating motor-driven impact member, so the key only positions it and electric power delivers the blow.

**How it works:** A motor belt-driven shaft carries disks and a rod as the rotating impact member. Depressing a key swings the key bar, whose arm pin engages the thrust bar and swings its lug into the path of the impact member against a spring. The impact member strikes the lug and drives the operating bar and type bar link, throwing the typebar at the platen. The thrust bar links to the operating bar by a slot and pin, and a spring returns it. Designed to build electric machines from ordinary parts.
