## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**How it works (reading):** A simple check punch that outlines each figure with a row of small holes, using a dial to choose the character and an automatic feed roll to move the check.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing check punches are complicated, liable to get out of order and costly to make.

**Solution:** A simple check punch that outlines each figure with a row of small holes, using a dial to choose the character and an automatic feed roll to move the check.
