## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (generic mechanical movement) · **Subject (patent classes):** Gearings comprising essentially only toothed gears or friction members and not capable of conveying indefinitely-continuing rotary motion for interconverting rotary or oscillating motion and reciprocating motion comprising a friction shaft; Tripping mechanism; Rotary to or from reciprocating or oscillating

**How it works (reading):** A toothed driver is engaged by a pivoted dog-and-pawl carrier spring-pressed by plungers, so a pawl-disengaging lever throws the driven mechanism in and out of engagement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rotating or oscillating drive needed a way to impart oscillating motion to a lever that can be engaged or released by a pawl-disengaging lever.

**Solution:** A toothed driver is engaged by a pivoted dog-and-pawl carrier spring-pressed by plungers, so a pawl-disengaging lever throws the driven mechanism in and out of engagement.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
