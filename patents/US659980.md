## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Offices such as railway offices need many copies of way-bills, and the typewriter should make stencils as well as ordinary print from the same keyboard.

**Solution:** Two sets of type-levers, one with perforating stencil type, are switched by a shiftable bar that re-links the key levers, over a flat impression bed.

**How it works:** Two opposed type-levers, each with a draw-rod, are joined at the bottom by a segment-shaped link with a curved slot. A shifting link from each key lever rides in the slot, and a shifting bar moves all links forward or back so a key works either the stencil or the ordinary type. A lock holds the bar as long as needed. The carriage has a flat rubber impression bed on a metal plate, with feed rollers on both sides at the bed's level so the paper slides smoothly.
