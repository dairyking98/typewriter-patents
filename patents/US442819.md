## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A strong, cheap typewriter is wanted that is easily and quickly worked from ordinary keys, with type on vertical disks above the carriage.

**Solution:** Keys depressed to different depths rotate a shaft by different angles, turning an overhead type wheel to the matching type, and the platen is raised against the type by tilting the whole main frame.

**How it works:** Type sit on the peripheries of one or more vertical disks above the platen. Key levers on a common shaft stop in slots of different depth in a rib, so each key turns the shaft through a different angle and thereby rotates the type wheel to a distinct position. The wheel can also slide along its axis to reach other rows, with a device to hold it in its longitudinal position, so the same keys print capitals, small letters and figures. The main frame is pivoted to the bed and tilts to bring the platen against the type. Springs return levers and shaft; spacing and inking mechanisms are included.

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md)
- **Impression:** [Platen pressed to the element](../features/imp-platen-to-element.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink roller](../features/ink-roller.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** type wheel; key lever; key lever shaft; stop slot rib; main frame; bed; return spring; stop buffer; platen

**Numbers:** three rows of keys; about one hundred words per minute is cited as a typing rate in a related patent
