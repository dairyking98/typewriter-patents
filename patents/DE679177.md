## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**How it works (reading):** The eccentric drive pulleys are moved to the rear of the machine next to the link system, and the release pawl sits at the end of an extended key lever, which also gives noise damping.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Drive pulleys in the middle of the machine are hard to reach for assembly and checking.

**Solution:** The eccentric drive pulleys are moved to the rear of the machine next to the link system, and the release pawl sits at the end of an extended key lever, which also gives noise damping.
