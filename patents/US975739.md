## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A machine should print matter in larger characters mixed with smaller ones, and still be usable for smaller characters.

**Solution:** A full keyboard machine carries large and small letters and uses a feed escapement with a loose dog and regulating stop so single teeth feed for the mixed sizes.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)

**Parts:** escape wheel; loose dog; regulating stop; feed dogs; carriage feed mechanism
