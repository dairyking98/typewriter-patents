Since only one eyelet works on one side, they all have one these devices have a connecting rod lying parallel to the ribbon the parts acting on the switching elements. There is a particularly simple device among other things, directly on two connected by such a connecting rod, the switching on and off of the ratchets driving and locking the ratchets acting leverage. Special coils are used for the ribbon a pivotable stop is used, which triggers when the bobbin is empty.
