## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Erasing or correcting tables

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an adjustable erasure-shield for the use of type-writers and bookkeepers.

**How it works (reading):** Two thin slotted metal leaves, one sliding over the other, that expose only a chosen letter or word for erasing and shield the remaining text, usable on the platen or flat.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a single typed letter must be erased, the rest of the typed page must be protected from the eraser.

**Solution:** Two thin slotted metal leaves, one sliding over the other, that expose only a chosen letter or word for erasing and shield the remaining text, usable on the platen or flat.
