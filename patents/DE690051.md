## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboards for syllable typewriters made the weaker fingers move among three rows and overloaded the thumbs.

**Solution:** A Velotype-style keyboard: five or more vowel keys for the index fingers, two-row consonant groups left and right, and four liquid consonant keys for the thumbs.

**How it works:** Initial consonants sit under the left hand and final consonants under the right, each in two horizontal rows so little, ring and middle fingers only operate two keys vertically. Vowels in the middle are struck by the index fingers. A row of four keys in front of the vowels, for liquid consonants, is hit by the thumbs, arranged so the thumbs need not hit adjacent pairs at once. Syllables are struck as chords.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** vowel key group; initial consonant group; final consonant group; thumb key row

**Numbers:** at least 5 vowel keys; 4 thumb keys; two rows per consonant group
