## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Debit and credit entries on the Elliott Fisher bill machine need to be written in different colours automatically, and the adding mechanism must be switched on or off as needed.

**Solution:** An automatic ribbon colour selector, set by an adjustment device with several positions, picks black or red for each entry and engages or disengages the adding mechanism and decimal mechanism.
