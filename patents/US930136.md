## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column tabulation of figures and names needs the carriage to stop at the same position in successive lines.

**Solution:** A tabulator mechanism uses a column stop mechanism so the carriage is positioned automatically at the start of each column, lining up successive numbers or names in vertical rows.
