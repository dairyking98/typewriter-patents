> Description Translated from German Method for cleaning the types of typewriters o. The like. By Dabbing by means of a plastic mass The invention relates to a method for Cleaning types of typewriters or the like by dabbing with a plastic Mass and a plastic mass for performing the procedure. It is well known that the types of typewriters and similar office machines not only contaminated when hitting the ribbon or ink pad, but exposed to a corrosive effect due to the influence of the color. The invention aims in particular to remedy this latter deficiency. This is done according to of the invention in that the types of typewriters o. The like. By the plastic Mass not only cleaned by dabbing, but also with a film-like one Skins are coated, which in turn provides protection against the corrosive Effects of the drying and decomposing types: color causes. This film-like protective membrane is produced according to the invention in that a plastic Mass is used as a result of the addition of suitable chemicals or film-forming Substances that prevent corrosion by mechanical, chemical or electrolytic means Protective membrane generated.
