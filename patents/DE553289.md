## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Preparing for use and conserving printing surfaces by use of detergents

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method for Cleaning types of typewriters or the like by dabbing with a plastic Mass and a plastic mass for performing the procedure.

**How it works (reading):** The types are dabbed with a plastic mass containing excess film-forming oils, resins or lacquers, which cleans them and leaves a protective film against corrosion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter types are contaminated by ink and corroded by the drying ink color, and existing cleaning masses do not protect the types afterwards.

**Solution:** The types are dabbed with a plastic mass containing excess film-forming oils, resins or lacquers, which cleans them and leaves a protective film against corrosion.
