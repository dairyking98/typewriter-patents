## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to devices for releasably connecting the platen to the linespace wheel of a typewriting machine.

**How it works (reading):** The line-space wheel is loose on the platen axle with an annular gear meshing a pinion on a radial shaft; spring levers cam a friction disk against a support to lock the wheel to the axle, released by a wedge on a thumbpiece-actuated sleeve.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-space wheel needed a reliable friction clutch to lock it to the platen, with positive release.

**Solution:** The line-space wheel is loose on the platen axle with an annular gear meshing a pinion on a radial shaft; spring levers cam a friction disk against a support to lock the wheel to the axle, released by a wedge on a thumbpiece-actuated sleeve.
