## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column-selecting tabulation needs the carriage to skip intermediate stops and stop at the selected column.

**Solution:** A column-selecting mechanism is set so the carriage is arrested at the selected column stop, skipping the stops in between.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
