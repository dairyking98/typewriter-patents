## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the invention are to improve the above-referred-to machines, to minimize the liability'of binding or cramping the keylevers and to improve the mounting thereof, to improve the key-lever springs, to improve the type-bar-shifting mechanism, to improve the mounting of the universal bar, andto effeet various other improvements in the machine, all as will hereinafter appear.

**How it works (reading):** Improved key-lever mounting and springs, an improved type-bar shifting mechanism, and an improved mounting of the universal bar in a front-strike machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-levers bind or cramp, their mounting and springs need improvement, and the type-bar shifting and universal bar mounting need better design in front-strike machines.

**Solution:** Improved key-lever mounting and springs, an improved type-bar shifting mechanism, and an improved mounting of the universal bar in a front-strike machine.

**How it works:** Key-levers with a reduced tendency to bind, key-lever springs, and a type-bar shifting mechanism that moves the bars; details are in the OCR-illegible drawings.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** key-levers; key-lever springs; type-bar shifting mechanism; universal bar

**Numbers:** US 687,500; 1901-11-26; application filed 1901-04-26; prior patents 657,927 and 668,713
