## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-bar pivots in circle machines wore so that the type no longer struck truly.

**Solution:** A one-piece type-hanger with the pivot pin fixed in it and conical bearings, with hangers alternated in two planes by washers.

**How it works:** The hanger is forked, one fork carrying the pivot pin, the other spanning the securing screw so the hanger and bar can be removed without removing the screw. The type-arm bears on the pin at two conical points. A bell-crank piece pivoted in the arm end takes the connecting rod. Washers raise alternate hangers to two planes, allowing wider hangers and longer pivots.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** type hanger; pivot pin; type arm; bell-crank piece; washer; connecting rod
