> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a closure device for protective covers from typewriters or similar devices. Typewriters and similar devices come with a solid lid to protect them against dust and damage during transport. So that the machine can also be carried comfortably, the lid must be on the base attached to the machine. For this purpose one has on the back wall of the protective cover Locking links attached, which are hooked into corresponding links of the pedestal will. The lid must be held in an elevated position and after the intervention Locking members can be folded forward over the machine. This type of attachment is quite cumbersome. In the invention, the protective cover is made by a straight downward movement placed on the typewriter and engages directly when pressed down the locking links on the base. 1?
