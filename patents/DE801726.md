Typewriters and similar devices come with a solid lid to protect them against dust and damage during transport. For this purpose one has on the back wall of the protective cover Locking links attached, which are hooked into corresponding links of the pedestal will. The lid must be held in an elevated position and after the intervention Locking members can be folded forward over the machine. This type of attachment is quite cumbersome.
