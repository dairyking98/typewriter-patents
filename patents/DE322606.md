## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter was needed that is very small, light and cheap, and can be operated by anyone.

**Solution:** Types sit in individual plungers in hand-moved frames; a hand-held pestle knocks down the type cell onto the writing surface, with return springs.
