## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicating with ink and typing with ribbon needed two machines.

**Solution:** A flat printer with fixed plate and travelling roller frame prints with inking rollers and ribbon in one pass.

**How it works:** A roller frame on rails carries a pressure roller and inking rollers; going one way the pressure roller prints, on the return the inking rollers re-ink the plate, with the pressure and ink rollers linked so one is lifted when the other is lowered. Ribbon spools are advanced by pawls struck by buffers at frame end. An ink box with adjustable ruler feeds a fixed roller. Sheets are fed through a mouthpiece against a stop and ejected.
