## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (music printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**How it works (reading):** Two carriages on a screw spindle and graded ruler set the position, and a pawl and toothed wheel space the staff notes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Music notes must be printed on autographic paper at correct spacing.

**Solution:** Two carriages on a screw spindle and graded ruler set the position, and a pawl and toothed wheel space the staff notes.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
