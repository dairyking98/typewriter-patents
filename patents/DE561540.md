## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Magnetic pulse generators for measuring devices, in particular for electricity meters The invention relates to a magnetic pulse generator for measuring devices, in particular for electricity meters, in which a relative to the direction of rotation in strength alternating field relative to a number of magnetic bodies revolving, one of which at least one part is movably mounted and connected to a switch. Its purpose is to prevent impairments in the accuracy of measurement, in particular to prevent the starting of the measuring devices driving the pulse generator from becoming more difficult. According to the invention, the bodies are distributed along the circulation path in such a way that that a full revolution is an integral multiple of one of the magnetic holding torque curve equivalent half-wave or that with half-waves of different length the circulation path is equal to the sum of such multiples of the individual half-waves.
