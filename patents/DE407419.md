**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on a ribbon fork drive for typewriters with a ribbon stroke regulating adjustable backdrop with guide slots running at an angle to each other.

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon fork is moved by an arm on the changeover frame driven by a plunger on the switch bridge or by the accent key, so drive parts lift with the frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon fork drive needed to be simple and the keystroke easy when the ribbon is used for different color zones.

**Solution:** Ribbon fork is moved by an arm on the changeover frame driven by a plunger on the switch bridge or by the accent key, so drive parts lift with the frame.
