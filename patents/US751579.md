## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A platen line-space ratchet should either lock at line intervals or be free to stop at any point.

**Solution:** A controlling lever acts on the two ends of one spring to engage either a locking lever (stopping the ratchet between teeth) or a friction brake lever.

**How it works:** A platen shaft carries a ratchet turned by a line-spacing lever and feed pawl. A brake lever and a locking lever are pivoted beside the ratchet and urged inward by one coiled spring whose ends project. A controlling lever lifts one spring end or the other. In one position the roller-tipped locking lever drops between ratchet teeth to hold exact line spaces; in the other the brake lever presses the ratchet with friction so the platen stays wherever it is turned. The spring ends also hold the controlling lever in either extreme position.
