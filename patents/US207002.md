## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carrying bands that move the paper get inked and soil the margins, and the front pulleys slip along their axle.

**Solution:** Guide shields over the carrying-band pulleys that also hold a reversed scale.

**How it works:** A forked guide shield attached to the axle rod straddles each band pulley and runs back under the band, with a groove or flanges guiding it, keeping pulleys from slipping and keeping the ribbon off the band. A reversed scale is attached to the ends of the shields.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** guide shield; carrying band; band pulley; reversed scale; platen; pressure roller; hinged finger; paper carriage
