## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling

**How it works (reading):** A sliding shoe on the paper-holding rail places the line-drawing wheel just under the baseline of each line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line pullers on a slidable bar are long and flexible so the line wheel twists and misses the baseline.

**Solution:** A sliding shoe on the paper-holding rail places the line-drawing wheel just under the baseline of each line.
