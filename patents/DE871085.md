## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing device, business machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriers swinging for impression about an axis parallel to the axis of rotation of the carrier

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to increase the printing capacity of such printing devices, without increasing the number of symbols.

**How it works (reading):** A group is selected from the type wheel by a stored impulse from the card, then the wheel turns to that group and a character within it is selected and printed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punch-card controlled printers with type wheels have too few holes per column to select enough characters.

**Solution:** A group is selected from the type wheel by a stored impulse from the card, then the wheel turns to that group and a character within it is selected and printed.
