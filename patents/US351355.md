**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to printing-types used in type-writers, type-writing machines, and printing-machines.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons, tapes and rollers are needed to ink type; the inventor wants type that carry their own ink.

**Solution:** Type bodies made of porous or minutely tubular material draw ink from a well or ink-charged substance by capillary action to the printing face.

**How it works:** The type body is porous clay, porous vulcanite, bundled glass tubes, fine wire, baked wood or fibre-bearing material, with the character in relief, recessed or drawn on its printing end. An absorbent extension dips into an ink well or touches an ink-charged substance, and the ink passes through the pores to the face and straight onto the paper. The outside except the face and the absorbing part is glazed or varnished to stop leaks. The body can be a single type, a longitudinal bar, a segment with several faces, or a circular form.
