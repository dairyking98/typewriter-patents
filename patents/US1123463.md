## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my present invention is to overcome this objection and render the operation of the esca pement mechanism substantially noiseless.

**How it works (reading):** A quiet, speedy and accurate escapement with reduced bearing friction between relatively movable parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The clicking of escapement parts makes most of the noise, and friction between moving bearings is high.

**Solution:** A quiet, speedy and accurate escapement with reduced bearing friction between relatively movable parts.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
