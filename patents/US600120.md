## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing speed is limited by large keyboards and one letter at a time.

**Solution:** Permutational selector with few keys plus two sets of typebars (left and right hand) that print two letters simultaneously at adjacent points.

**How it works:** Two sets of keys each control a permutational selector connected to one of two arcs of typebars, each set striking its own printing point, the two adjacent on the same line. Both hands act at once for two consecutive letters. A release lets bars return though keys stay down. Spacing mechanism handles two-letter steps. The selector is also shown driving the escapements of a Mergenthaler linotype.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Keys that only set the element](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** typebar set; key set; permutational selector; release mechanism; spacing mechanism; printing point
