## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator needs a simple way to turn the platen a line while returning the carriage.

**Solution:** A lever on the carriage handle turns the platen ratchet first, then pushes the carriage back.

**How it works:** A lever pivoted on the carriage handle engages a ratchet wheel on the platen. Pressing it moves it against the handle or a stop, turning the platen, then continued pressure moves the carriage back. An eccentric triangular stop sets the platen turn to a half or full line. Pressing the lever's outer end rocks it out of the ratchet to turn the platen backward. A spring returns it, and a paper guide holds a roller on the platen.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** carriage handle; lever; ratchet wheel; platen; eccentric stop; spring; keeper; paper guide roller
