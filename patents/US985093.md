## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Key levers are irregularly arranged, so the keys do not form a regular bank, and the key stem and key head connection is complicated.

**Solution:** The keys are arranged in a regular bank by their key stems and heads, with simple means connecting each key stem to its key lever; a tabulator stop lever arrangement sits on a tabulator frame.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
