## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Platen can only turn step by step through the ratchet; no way to turn it a fraction of a line without disturbing the usual step.

**Solution:** Attachment on the platen shaft end with a separate finger wheel that rotates the platen independently of the ratchet wheel.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
