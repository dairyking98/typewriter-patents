## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a Remington typewriter with a divided platen carrying a main invoice sheet and a separate tally strip, the two sheets need different line spacing: the main sheet every line, the tally strip only when it is in the printing field.

**Solution:** A single line-space hand piece drives two pawl-and-ratchet trains, and a spring-loaded preventive finger holds the tally pawl out of action until a stationary cam on the machine top releases it as the carriage travels.

**How it works:** The platen is split into a main section and a tally section with a take-up roll holder, each with its own ratchet wheel and pawl, both pawls swung by one hand piece (rock shaft, bell crank, link). A paper-finger-like preventive device normally presses on a pin of the second pawl so only the main platen advances. When the carriage moves far enough left that the tally section enters the printing field, a finger on the carriage rides up a stationary adjustable cam and swings the preventive finger away. The weaker spring then lets the second pawl engage, so one actuation line-spaces both the main sheet and the tally strip, with gear trains turning the take-up roll and tally platen together. Moving the carriage back lets the stronger spring restore the preventive finger.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** divided platen; tally platen section; tally strip roll holder; line-space ratchet wheel; pawl; hand piece; preventive finger; stationary cam; carbon strip; gear train

**Improves on:**

- Yaw application Serial No. 762,141 (tally strip mechanism): no separate tally feed rollers; tally strip is advanced by the take-up roll gearing to the tally platen and a single hand piece
