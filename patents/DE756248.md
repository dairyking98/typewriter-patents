## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and manually actuated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a fuse for the central position of the paper slide on typewriters, which in particular Can be used advantageously on small typewriters with an inevitable ball guide should be.

**How it works (reading):** A U-shaped resilient wire locking body fits into opposed openings in the guide rails at both ends of the paper carriage, locking it centrally without tools.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage shipping locks are either screwed on or use wire clips that fail on elongated ball cages.

**Solution:** A U-shaped resilient wire locking body fits into opposed openings in the guide rails at both ends of the paper carriage, locking it centrally without tools.
