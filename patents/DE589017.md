## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen shell noise from type strikes is too loud, and older damping methods weaken the platen.

**Solution:** The platen body is a thick-walled or solid light metal cylinder of low elasticity and low sound conduction, with the usual rubber covering.
