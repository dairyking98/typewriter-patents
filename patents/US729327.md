## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typebar machine with two types per bar on opposite sides needs a platen carriage that can be shifted toward either the front or the rear from a central position, with convenient platen handling.

**Solution:** The whole carriage bed rides fore and aft on two rails under two shift keys linked by a pivoted lever and a spring latch, so one key moves it forward and the other backward, while the platen runs along the bed on its own runways.

**How it works:** The shiftable bed rolls on two rods, one engaged by a grooved roller and one by a plain roller. One shift key on an intermediate-pivot lever pushes the bed one way; the other key sits on a spring latch whose heel rests on that lever, so pressing it first slips the heel off and then swings an angle lever to move the bed the opposite way, with leaf springs returning it to the centre. The platen carriage runs sideways on the bed on a roller and a ball-supported groove, pulled by a spring drum. A rock frame with a holding tooth and a sliding feed tooth steps a ratchet bar one tooth per key or space stroke, and pivoted side arms let the ratchet bar be lifted to free the carriage. The line-space pawl carrier has an adjustable stop, and when pulled it also lifts the ratchet bar clear. A rubber roller, presser bar, adjustable margin stop and bell complete the carriage.
