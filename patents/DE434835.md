## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, reliable and compact pocket label or strip typewriter is wanted whose parts fit in a very small flat housing.

**Solution:** A setting pin is put into the hole for the wanted character in a type wheel and turns the wheel to a stop, then the same pin swings the wheel against the platen to print and the paper strip is stepped.

**How it works:** The type wheel, with types on its circumference and one setting hole per type, sits in a fork lever pivoted in the housing. The operator puts the pin in the hole for the character and turns the wheel against the tension of a coil spring until the pin hits the lower edge of the lever, which puts that type at the print point. Pressing the pin then swings the lever and wheel down against the paper roller, printing onto a paper strip. A fixed ink roller touches every type except at one blank hole at the lowest point. During the stroke a hook on the lever engages the platen ratchet, and on return under spring force it advances the platen one tooth, held by a locking lever. The strip comes from a replaceable reel and may be perforated and gummed on one side so strips can be stuck onto a sheet or postcard.
