## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (card punch with writing device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tape or page guiding or feeding devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an assembly with a writing device Card punching device with power drive, through which the information is both in a Card can be punched as well as written on the card.

**How it works (reading):** Separate power drives for writing and punching; the key-lever coupling engages the punch drive, and the punch's own power-drive members release the card-carriage inhibitor on retraction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Combined writing and card-punching typewriter with power drive lacks a guaranteed operating sequence, and the punch retraction is not tied to the card carriage.

**Solution:** Separate power drives for writing and punching; the key-lever coupling engages the punch drive, and the punch's own power-drive members release the card-carriage inhibitor on retraction.
