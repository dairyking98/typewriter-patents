## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasures need an underlying surface between carbon sheets without blurring them.

**Solution:** A plate pivoted on the carriage, reversible up and down, lies between the sheet and the platen so that it can be turned for either face.
