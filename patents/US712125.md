## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of apparatus in which a series of tools each adapted to cut or punch or cut out a separate letter or character is carried by a head or carrier actuated to bring the tools into operation as desired.

**How it works (reading):** An improved stencil-cutting machine with a series of tools each cutting a letter, carried by a head actuated to bring the tools into operation, plus improved feeding of the bed-strip and stencil-blank.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier stencil apparatus needed more complicated construction than necessary for the accuracy and speed required; actuation of the tools and feeding of the bed-strip need improvement.

**Solution:** An improved stencil-cutting machine with a series of tools each cutting a letter, carried by a head actuated to bring the tools into operation, plus improved feeding of the bed-strip and stencil-blank.
