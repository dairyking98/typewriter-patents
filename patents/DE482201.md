In the drawing, Fig.z shows a fitted with the new device, Typewriter shown partially broken off from the front, Fig. a the new facility for itself in a graphical representation. Fig. I shows the device in effective, Fig. A in inoperative position. Immediately above the key levers a flat bar L, which extends over all the key levers t and is equipped with round ends W, is rotatably mounted in the side walls of the typewriter. With the help of a handle outside of the machine, this rail can be moved from its (flat) rest position to its upright edge by a quarter turn. , Shift-Lock-, Reverse and Edge release keys can be depressed and all types can be lifted llebely.
