## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to bearings for the type-bars of a type-writing machine and the like, and has for its object to provide a simple construction of the above-indicated character.

**How it works (reading):** A two-section bearing mounted on the segment has a central conical projection engaging a depression in the type-bar pivot, with balls in an annular groove carrying the bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The type-bar bearing needs a simple construction that holds the bar against sideways movement.

**Solution:** A two-section bearing mounted on the segment has a central conical projection engaging a depression in the type-bar pivot, with balls in an annular groove carrying the bar.
