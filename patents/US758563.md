## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The machine must bring the required type to the striking position and strike with a force that matches the key pressure and can be adjusted.

**Solution:** Type-carriers each hold several types, positioned by type-disposing devices, and a striking lever that is not positively linked to the key strikes the carrier against the paper roll.

**Features:** [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
