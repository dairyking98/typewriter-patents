**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers

**How it works (reading):** The paper carriage is stowed in the box lid on supports and a spring-loaded latch lever, so it occupies the space above the keyboard only when the lid is closed, giving a lower box height.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A portable typewriter in its box is too tall because the paper carriage sits on top, and carriage hinges are stressed when turned over.

**Solution:** The paper carriage is stowed in the box lid on supports and a spring-loaded latch lever, so it occupies the space above the keyboard only when the lid is closed, giving a lower box height.
