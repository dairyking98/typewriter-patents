## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing single sheets for each typed form wastes time in billing work.

**Solution:** Cheek plates on the carriage carry paper rolls and a carbon roll, feeding superposed strips under the platen and cutting them into sheets after writing.

**How it works:** Rolls are journalled in plates that ride with the carriage; strips run beneath the platen and a severing device cuts them transversely.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
