## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars need to be held securely on their pivot wire yet be quickly released and removed individually.

**Solution:** A spring-returned toothed sector slides along the typebar segment; its teeth block the bar hooks, and shifting it aligns notches with the slots so any bar can be lifted off.

**How it works:** Typebars sit in slots of the segment, each with a hook over a common pivot wire driven into a curved groove. A toothed retainer sector slides in an arc on pins through slots, with notches spaced like the bars. A spring holds it so its teeth sit next to the hooks and keep the bars on the pivot. Moving the sector by hand lines the notches with the slots, so a bar's hook can lift clear of the pivot and the bar be removed alone; releasing the sector lets the spring return it.
