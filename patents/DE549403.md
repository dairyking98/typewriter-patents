## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements of selective printing mechanisms, e.g. ink-jet printers or thermal printers, specially adapted for supporting or handling copy material in continuous form, e.g. webs

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is shown in one embodiment in the drawing for example shown, namely the figure shows a cross section through same.

**How it works (reading):** Each form packet (forms, copies and carbons glued at the top edge) is pinned or glued to a continuous carrier belt, so every packet moves evenly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Endless form sets move unevenly when stacked and glued together, causing wrinkles and misfeeds.

**Solution:** Each form packet (forms, copies and carbons glued at the top edge) is pinned or glued to a continuous carrier belt, so every packet moves evenly.

**How it works:** Packets are laid one behind another on the conveyor belt, which carries them through the machine evenly.
