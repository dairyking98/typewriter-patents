## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier compact machines required one hand to shift or set parts for the other hand.

**Solution:** A sliding frame carries the type-bars and keyboard, with compound key slides, so a cheap two-hand machine has many characters.

**How it works:** A frame on a cylindrical rod slides in a standard, stopping in three positions (end and middle) set by a lever and notched opening. Key bars or slides made of two members carry several printing characters, and pressing a key brings its character to the printing point. Frame and keyboard move relative to the paper roll.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** base; standard; sliding rod; frame; lever; compound key slide; type-bar; platen

**Numbers:** 3 positions

**Improves on:**

- [US585838](../patents/US585838.md): related Burridge machine
