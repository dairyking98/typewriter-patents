## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (character transmission receiver, pixel scanning) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or local circuits for mosaic printer telegraph systems at the receiving end

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a receiving arrangement for the transmission of characters by a transmitter with the help of a keyboard or a punched tape through specified Pulse series broken down into pixels and transmitted on the receiving side through a Magnet system and a rotating scanning element are recorded.

**How it works (reading):** The character picture lines are grouped into line groups, one per text line, and recorded by a magnet system with a cutting edge against a rotating roller, so that a whole sheet of text is recorded as lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheet receivers for type printing telegraphs use a full type for each character, which is not a way to build a sheet-format picture-line receiver.

**Solution:** The character picture lines are grouped into line groups, one per text line, and recorded by a magnet system with a cutting edge against a rotating roller, so that a whole sheet of text is recorded as lines.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Receiving arrangement for the transmission of characters The invention relates to a receiving arrangement for the transmission of characters by a transmitter with the help of a keyboard or a punched tape through specified Pulse series broken down into pixels and transmitted on the receiving side through a Magnet system and a rotating scanning element are recorded. According to the invention, the receiving apparatus is used as a sheet-paper receiver made usable that the picture lines forming the individual characters are grouped into picture line groups for one or more characters are summarized in such a way that a or several image line groups form the text lines of the sheet. The receiving machine is designed so that the magnetic system causing the recording when changing lines or with a fixed magnet system the carriage in that of other sheet printers known way is returned to the line catch. The height of each group of lines is expediently chosen equal to the height of a character. There are already sheet recorders for type printing telegraph apparatus known, in which, however, the printing device is constructed like normal office typewriters is.…
