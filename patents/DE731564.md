## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A two-legged lever with a pusher on each side of the copy apparatus lifts the front ribbon off the one behind, so sheets can be inserted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting the front carbon ribbon off the one behind by hand to insert sheets is awkward and dirties the fingers.

**Solution:** A two-legged lever with a pusher on each side of the copy apparatus lifts the front ribbon off the one behind, so sheets can be inserted.
