## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card holder guides on a platen can only be adjusted by moving the type carriage back, which is slow.

**Solution:** Add a hand-operated shifting mechanism that moves the holding guides to suit each card size without touching the carriage.

**How it works:** Holding-guides on the platen engage the edges of a card or sheet. Each guide has its own shifting mechanism, or one handle moves both guides together, operated from the front of the platen. Several forms are shown, including a simplified one and one adjusting both guides synchronously. Only the object of the invention is in the opening.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** holding guide; shifting mechanism; operating handle; platen

**Improves on:**

- Halle card holder application (1899): guides can be shifted without moving the carriage
