## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** To spread wear over the ribbon, it should move fore-and-aft as well as sideways so the impressions fall on diagonal lines, without complex mechanism.

**Solution:** A rack-carrying lever driven by a pinion on the carriage mainspring pulley moves the ribbon spool frames fore-and-aft as the carriage advances.

**How it works:** The carriage is pulled by a spring inside a hollow pulley via a chain. A pinion on the pulley meshes with a rack on a U-shaped lever, so as the carriage advances the lever oscillates. A slot in the lever engages an arm on the spool-carrying frames, moving them toward the front. A rocking shaft joins the two spool frames so they move together. A pawl and ratchet on each spool advance the ribbon sideways as usual, which combined with the fore-and-aft motion gives a diagonal path. A pitman variant is shown.
