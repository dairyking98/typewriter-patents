## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapements give only a fixed one-letter feed; a variable feed is wanted.

**Solution:** Two semicircular flange teeth on a rocking shaft alternately engage the feed rack, and the loose one springs forward until stopped by a step selected by the depressed spacing key, giving a variable number of spaces.

**How it works:** A shaft parallel to the feed rack carries a fixed sleeve with a beveled semicircular flange (tooth) and a loose, spring-loaded sleeve with a second flange. Several spacing keys press a stepped yoke by different amounts, rocking the shaft through a pitman and crank. This swaps the rack from the loose tooth to the fixed tooth; the freed tooth is thrown forward by its spring until it hits a step of a stepped piece, the step reached depending on the key. On release the shaft rocks back and the loose tooth re-engages the rack further along.
