## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shifting to upper case pays out ribbon and leaves slack that smudges the paper.

**Solution:** A pawl on the lift rail frame that turns the ribbon ratchet on return to lower case, taking up the slack.

**How it works:** On upper case shift the pawl slides over the ratchet teeth; on return to lower case it engages and gives the ratchet a partial turn, removing the slack paid out by the shift.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** lift rail frame; pawl; ribbon ratchet; ribbon spool; ribbon guide; shift key
