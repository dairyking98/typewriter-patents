## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter placed on a surface transmits shock and vibration, and existing resilient supports are not stiff enough or do not fit machines of different frame widths.

**Solution:** A resilient support of arched sheet-metal strips sits under the machine, with slotted plates that fit different frame widths and attach to the surface without metal-to-metal contact.
