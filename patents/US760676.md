## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible typewriters needed better control of the position and feed of the inking ribbon through which the type-bars print.

**Solution:** A ribbon plate and spool drive controlled from the universal bar and space-bar levers, with clutch plates on the spools.

**How it works:** The space-bar levers pivot on a comb bar and have springs holding the bar raised. The universal bar between them is pivoted to the comb bar and rocked each time a key or the space bar is pressed. The ribbon spools are on a shaft with a cam sleeve, and each spool has a clutch plate. A ribbon plate supports the ribbon at the print position. Only the opening is available so the detailed action is not read.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; spool shaft; cam sleeve; clutch plate; ribbon plate; universal bar; space-bar lever; comb bar
