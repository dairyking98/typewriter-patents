**How it works (reading):** An add-on unit reads text and justification values from punched tape in two sensing stations and sets interword spacing in whole units for an electric typewriter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-justified typing needs whole spacing units; fractions from the proportional-carriage calculation can cause inaccuracies.

**Solution:** An add-on unit reads text and justification values from punched tape in two sensing stations and sets interword spacing in whole units for an electric typewriter.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
