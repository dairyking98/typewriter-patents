## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines hide the writing, need a supplemental shifting carriage for capitals, and have bulky type-bar hangers.

**Solution:** A tilting platen that exposes the writing, a single carriage that shifts for capitals, and a cheaper type-bar hanger.

**How it works:** The paper carriage frame is hinged on a rod carried by a rock shaft; rocking the shaft shifts the whole carriage forward for capitals, with no inner carriage. The platen can be tilted to show the writing without lifting the carriage. Type-bars on a ring use a new compact hanger, and an automatic ribbon feed with spool and guide is included.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** main frame; carriage frame; rock shaft; carriage shift rod; platen; type-bar hanger; ribbon spool
