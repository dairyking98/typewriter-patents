## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Punching check amounts with the check held still needs a handy selection of the punch.

**Solution:** A swinging punch frame with an arc of punches and dies is turned by a doubly pivoted lever over a reversed-character index with a notched rack, and a plate presses the aligned punch.

**How it works:** A pivot with head carries a hub with a swinging frame holding an arcuate group of punches in sockets above matching dies. A horizontally swinging arm with a hinged lever carries a knob and a tongue; the fork on the swinging frame embraces the lever so it turns the frame. A character index on the top plate, reversed, sits in an arc with a notched rack that guides and retains the lever at the chosen character. A hinged spring-held punch-depressing plate with a finger pushes the aligned punch when the lever is pressed. Recoil of the mechanism shifts the document one character backward.
