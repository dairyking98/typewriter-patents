## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens; Supply holders for sheets or fan-folded webs, e.g. shelves, tables, scrolls, pile holders; Hammer-impression mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriters and, particularly, to structures employed in conjunction therewith for supporting and positioning manifolding material in relation to the platen forming a part of said typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** A carbon-sustaining support, held by parts other than the carriage rails, positions manifolding material against the platen and lets the operator change its surface or position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon manifolding material cannot be positioned and moved easily relative to the platen.

**Solution:** A carbon-sustaining support, held by parts other than the carriage rails, positions manifolding material against the platen and lets the operator change its surface or position.
