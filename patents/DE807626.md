## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversal devices need control parts on both sides of the machine, one for each spool.

**Solution:** Only the left ribbon guide arm triggers a central reversing device that shifts the ribbon feed shaft to the other spool with one keystroke.

**How it works:** When a spool empties, the ribbon pull moves the two-armed left ribbon guide arm, freeing a crankshaft that a spring pushes under the shift rod. The shift rod, moved by each keystroke, pushes the crankshaft which pushes a double pawl, flipping an armature that slides a clutch disc on the ribbon transport shaft to engage the other spool. A sprung cam holds the armature. When feed ends, the spent spool is rewound and the arm returns by spring.
