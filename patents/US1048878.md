## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding of the material to be moulded, e.g. into a mould cavity of several materials

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriters and has-particular reference to a device adapted to ellminate to a certain extent, the noise attendant upon the impact of the type bars upon the platen.

**How it works (reading):** The type-carrying member is mounted on a flexible vibratable member attached to a base with a yielding pad, so the type cushions impact and deadens the click.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars are noisy and deliver a harsh click on the platen.

**Solution:** The type-carrying member is mounted on a flexible vibratable member attached to a base with a yielding pad, so the type cushions impact and deadens the click.
