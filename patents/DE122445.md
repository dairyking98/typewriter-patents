## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage advance circuit was affecting the keystroke, which the invention aims to reduce.

**Solution:** Keys in superimposed rows act through rods, levers and a tie rod, which drive a ratchet and pawl mechanism that moves the carriage, keeping key travel separate from the advance.
