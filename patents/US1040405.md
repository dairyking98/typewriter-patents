## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism for typewriting machines and its object is to provide variable ribbon throwing or vibrating mechanism in combination with improved stop mechanism for said variable ribbon throwing mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Use two separate stops: a fixed immovable one for the long throw and a movable one that swings into action for the short throw as the throw-varying mechanism is set.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Variable-throw ribbon vibrators with stops that are reset together are hard to assemble accurately for both the short and long throw, especially with a shifting platen.

**Solution:** Use two separate stops: a fixed immovable one for the long throw and a movable one that swings into action for the short throw as the throw-varying mechanism is set.

**How it works:** A throw-varying device sets how far the ribbon vibrator is lifted so either of two ribbon fields reaches the print point. The long-throw stop is fixed to its support so it can be assembled precisely. The short-throw stop moves into the vibrator's path concurrently with, and automatically by, the throw setting so it arrests the vibrator before the fixed stop. A new vibrator has a roof or lip across it to keep the top edge of the ribbon straight between the guides and prevent overthrow. Shown on a Monarch front-strike machine and a modified No. 10 Remington.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; vibrating lever; fixed stop; movable stop; throw-varying device; actuating link; ribbon; hand control lever

**Numbers:** two ribbon fields

**Improves on:**

- prior variable-throw ribbon stops: separate fixed long-throw stop and movable short-throw stop for accuracy
