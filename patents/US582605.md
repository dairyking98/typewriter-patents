## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the printing or marking process for which they are designed characterised by selective application of impact or pressure on a printing material or impression-transfer material

**In the patent’s own words:** The improvements relate more particularly to that class of typewriting machines in which the type-levers are arranged in the arc of a circle facing the operator and in which the printing-point is located between the typelevers and the keyboard, but some parts thereof are also applicable to other forms of type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** Girders support the paper carriage, with broad hangers, liftable ribbon guide and adjustable connecting rods.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage support and typebar hangers on an arc-bar machine were weak.

**Solution:** Girders support the paper carriage, with broad hangers, liftable ribbon guide and adjustable connecting rods.

**How it works:** Girders guide the carriage, the shifting bar couples to it, hangers give broad bearings, the ribbon guide lifts for visibility and rods are length-adjustable.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** girder; paper carriage; typebar hanger; ribbon guide; connecting rod
