## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices actuated when platen reaches the end of a line

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to the line-lock mechanisms thereof.

**How it works (reading):** A line-lock mechanism automatically locks the type-carriers against printing at a predetermined carriage point; it is released by the carriage-release key or a special finger key, and can be permanently disabled.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must be able to stop type-carriers automatically at a chosen point in the carriage travel, and later release them by hand to finish a word.

**Solution:** A line-lock mechanism automatically locks the type-carriers against printing at a predetermined carriage point; it is released by the carriage-release key or a special finger key, and can be permanently disabled.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)

**Parts:** line-lock mechanism; carriage-release key; special finger-key; type-carriers
