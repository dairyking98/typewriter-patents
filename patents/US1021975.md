**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Celluloid key caps with paper and glass character disks come loose from the metal key plate.

**Solution:** The key plate and disks are enclosed in a celluloid ring and two semicircular segments fused with acetic ether, so the key cap cannot come off.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [How the element is made](../features/elem-element-construction.md)
