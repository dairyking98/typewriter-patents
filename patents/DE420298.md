## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon conveyor for cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a conveyor, in known devices of this type device for the ribbon of cash registers : the switching device is imprinted by beads, in which by stops of the j influences that at certain distances from the Ribbon the switching device is influenced in such a way that a greater advance of the Ribbon occurs when the seam approaches one of the two pressure points.

**How it works (reading):** Two independent switching devices are used: one always gives the normal ribbon feed, and a second adds a larger feed when the seam approaches one of the two pressure points.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon advance keyed to beads at fixed distances fails when the bead spacing is not exactly matched to the seam, so the seam can drift out of the print hammer area and the beads can disturb printing.

**Solution:** Two independent switching devices are used: one always gives the normal ribbon feed, and a second adds a larger feed when the seam approaches one of the two pressure points.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** switching device 66, 63, 61; second switching device 76, 72, 71; print hammer

**Numbers:** German priority 1924-06-29; US priority 1923-07-05
