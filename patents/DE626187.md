## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on writing business machines to secure the writing limit at the bottom of the writing sheet according to patent 614,373, in which a feeler after passing placed around a platen with an uninterrupted cylindrical surface Writing sheet is moved into a release position and when the writing limit is reached a locking device comes into effect.

**How it works (reading):** A manually adjustable actuator with two detent grooves lifts the sensor off the sheet so the locking device can be switched off and back on.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The writing-limit sensor always locks the machine at the bottom of the sheet, which is a nuisance when cards or postcards are fed one after another.

**Solution:** A manually adjustable actuator with two detent grooves lifts the sensor off the sheet so the locking device can be switched off and back on.
