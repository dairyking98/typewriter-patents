## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting the platen or type support must hold position without effort, not rebound, and require little force at the start of the movement.

**Solution:** An oscillating eccentric driven through a rock-lever gives greatest leverage at the start and accelerating then retarding motion.

**How it works:** Shift levers drive a rock-lever that oscillates an eccentric (with gear in one form). The eccentric moves the platen or type-support from normal to shifted position and positively holds it in either place. Leverage is highest at the start. Adjustments increase or decrease the travel for different type sizes or spacing.
