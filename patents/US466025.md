## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator could not see at a glance where the platen carriage was.

**Solution:** A dial with an index hand geared to the carriage-moving wheel.

**How it works:** A cord from the platen arm winds on a wheel turned by a spring through a lever and cord on a pulley on the same shaft. An arm fast to the wheel shaft engages an arm on the index-hand shaft, so the hand over a numbered dial on top of the frame turns as the carriage moves.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** platen arm; cord; wheel; spring; arm; index hand; dial plate
