## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cards bent around the platen are hard to feed and can break, so they must be kept flat at the print point.

**Solution:** A card support keeps the card flat at the printing point, adjusts it to position automatically, and gives accurate line spacing using the ordinary platen action.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
