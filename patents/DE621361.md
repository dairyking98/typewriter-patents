## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Template (copy) holders that advance by the carriage return lever cannot follow templates with a different line spacing than the typewriter; separate-drive holders are unsuited to handwritten copy.

**Solution:** A spring (flexible member) between the line-space lever and the holder's clamping slide rod lets an adjustable stop set the line advance of the template independently of the platen's line spacing.

**How it works:** A line pointer (transparent bookmark) is carried on a rod advanced by a clamping lever. When the platen line-space lever is moved, a stop on a drive lever pulls a bolt and connecting member that jams the clamping lever on the rod and pushes it down to an adjustable stop on a scale. A spring absorbs the lever travel beyond the set advance. The frame is fixed to the carriage so the template line sits vertically above the print point.
