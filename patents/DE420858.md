## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters with accent dead keys and a multi-colour ribbon printed accents in a different colour than the letter.

**Solution:** A rotatable shaft carried by the shift frame lets dead accent keys drive the ribbon lifter so the accent takes the colour zone of the adjacent letter without advancing the carriage.

**How it works:** Accent types sit one letter width to the left of the usual print centre, so the accent is typed with a non-advancing dead key before or after the letter. A shaft mounted on the shift frame, which moves with it, has one end under the accent key levers and the other at the manually set ribbon-lift mechanism. Pressing an accent key turns the shaft, which lifts the ribbon to the same zone as for the letters. A second version uses a shaft with fingers, those under ordinary keys broken off.
