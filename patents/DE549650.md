These devices have the disadvantage that when they are installed in existing typewriters must be carried out on this considerable, associated with fairly high costs, so that a savings over the expense of getting a new typewriter does not appear with an electrical type stop. This disadvantage is eliminated according to the invention in that as a common stop rail a universal rail that is mechanically carried along by the buttons is used, s5 which itself is the breaker of the circuit of the electromagnet closes. Such universal rails are common to all Typewriters available, so that when installing the new device in such a Machine, the change costs and labor are reduced.
