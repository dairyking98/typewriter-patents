## Summary

**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes hand-held

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to secure a type-writing machine of low cost which shall be capable of doing work as good, or nearly so, as any of the type-writing machines now on the market, and which shall be capable of manifolding and be especially adaptable for operation in connection with the production of stencils by the well-known mimeograph process of producing type-writer stencils.

**How it works (reading):** A type wheel with loose type in slots, turned by an index lever and gear sector over a letter chart, struck by a hammer.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cheap typewriter is wanted that writes nearly as well as the standard machines, makes manifold copies and cuts mimeograph stencils.

**Solution:** A type wheel with loose type in slots, turned by an index lever and gear sector over a letter chart, struck by a hammer.

**How it works:** The type wheel is two toothed metal plates joined by a band, with loose shouldered type sitting in slots. A slotted sector chart carries the characters in the wheel's order, and an index lever on the same spindle as a toothed sector turns the wheel through a gear. A key lever movement then works the feed pawl, an alignment tooth and a hammer which strikes the type against the platen. The carriage moves on a rack rod with a feed pawl, and a bell is provided.
