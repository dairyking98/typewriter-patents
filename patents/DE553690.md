## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for adjusting the platen in the direction of the hinge of the types on typewriters with reversible paper carriage carriers, which is guided in parallel by two pairs of handlebars.

**How it works (reading):** Carriage guide rails in the lower part are adjusted against each other by opposing screws bearing on support arms, giving a fine, lockable platen-to-type setting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen could only be adjusted for type strike by eccentrics or slotted holes, which lacked fine adjustment and shifted height.

**Solution:** Carriage guide rails in the lower part are adjusted against each other by opposing screws bearing on support arms, giving a fine, lockable platen-to-type setting.
