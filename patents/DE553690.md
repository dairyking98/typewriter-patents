> Description Translated from German Device for adjusting the platen in the stroke direction of Types The invention relates to a device for adjusting the platen in the direction of the hinge of the types on typewriters with reversible paper carriage carriers, which is guided in parallel by two pairs of handlebars. With such machines it is known to adjust the platen in the stroke direction of the types by that the link axles or articulated connections are provided with eccentric bolts. It is also known for both pairs of links to be adjustable on one relative to the machine housing Arrange plate, which is provided with elongated holes for adjustment. While the adjustments by eccentric have the specialist part that at an intended adjustment in the horizontal direction at the same time an unintentional one Adjustment takes place in height, is missing in the case of adjustment by means of elongated holes the fine adjustability. In addition, the stop screws come when the adjustment is greater from your area of the stops that work with them and are attached to the frame.
