## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a sheet music typewriter with an additional Adjustment keyboard for angle adjustment of the paper roller by means of a lever transmission.

**How it works (reading):** A second adjustment keyboard drives a lever linkage with a changeable ratio to the platen rotation, so line spacing can be set to the staff width.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Music typewriters needed a separate, complicated platen rotation for staff spacing.

**Solution:** A second adjustment keyboard drives a lever linkage with a changeable ratio to the platen rotation, so line spacing can be set to the staff width.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
