## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to improve the general construction of stencil machines, and particularly to improve the feeding mechanism by which the card-boarder other material from which the stencil is formed is controlled.

**How it works (reading):** An improved feed mechanism controls the movement of the card-board stencil blank under the cutting tools, with coils and feed parts shown in the drawings.
