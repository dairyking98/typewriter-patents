## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing front-stroke machines had a poor arrangement of type-bars relative to the platen, key-levers and key-to-bar connections, and too few type-bars for the required characters.

**Solution:** Improved arrangement of type-bars around the platen, key-levers and their operative connections; type-bars carry partly rotatable type-bearing sleeves or heads, and the pivot-to-printing-point relation changes to give more characters.

**How it works:** Type-bars with rotatable sleeves give several characters per bar; changing the bar pivot relative to the printing point selects among them.
