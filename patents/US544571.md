## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboard letter groupings do not allow common letter combinations in consecutive order, and typing accuracy and cost suffer.

**Solution:** A regrouped keyboard arrangement with typebars carrying one or two characters each, so common letter groups sit in consecutive rows.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
