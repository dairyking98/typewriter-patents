## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A small typewriter with a standard four-row keyboard needs a type-bar drive of great simplicity and strength.

**Solution:** An extension bent from the key lever, ending in a bent-over guide loop, acts directly on the intermediate lever carrying the type bar.

**How it works:** Pressing a key lowers the key lever and its integral extension, which ends in a guide loop formed by bending. The loop engages the lever arm of an intermediate lever and pulls it down. This throws the type bar in a straight line against the platen.
