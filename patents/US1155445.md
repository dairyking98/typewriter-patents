## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are noisy and need hand force; the inventor wants a nearly noiseless machine driven by stored power with a copying cylinder.

**Solution:** A spring-or-motor driven machine with a rotating paper cylinder and circle of type-bars whose keys only release the driving mechanism.

**How it works:** Paper is wrapped on a cylinder carried in a frame that slides in a casing and is stepped for line spacing. A circle of pivoted standards carries type-bars with cams having radial slots. Vertical bars with pins engage the slots to swing a type-bar down to the paper. A spiral-grooved cylinder moves the bars up or down through pivoted dogs, with wedge cams and catches controlling engagement. Keys only release the mechanism, and an auxiliary cylinder reproduces the writing in compact form.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Power-driven type action](../features/bar-power-action.md)
- **Impression:** [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** casing; paper cylinder; segment clamp; circle of standards; type-bar; operating bar with pin; spiral cylinder; dog; wedge cam; catch; copying cylinder
