## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guide fingers should be simple, cheap and adjustable on their support bar.

**Solution:** The guide finger is a resilient strip curved around the platen, with a clamp loop notched to grip the corners of the square support bar.
