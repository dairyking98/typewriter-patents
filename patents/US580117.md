## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage must advance two spaces at once after a word and retract a space for correction.

**Solution:** Carriage-retracting and double-advance devices on the escapement.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md)
