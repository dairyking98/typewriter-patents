## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A flat platen used for sales slips and duplicates makes it hard to keep duplicates aligned and to remove printed slips after making out a sale.

**Solution:** The platen carries side-by-side paper webs with underlying duplicate webs; after a slip is printed the web is drawn forward, the printed portion is severed, and a fresh area is presented to the printing point.

**Features:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
