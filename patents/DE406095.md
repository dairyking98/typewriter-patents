## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type lever pivot bearings needed to be compact and keep dust out of the bearing.

**Solution:** Ball bearing carried by the two fork ends of the type lever, with the inner race formed by threaded cones so the bearing fits within the lever shaft and stays flush with the fork ends.
