## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (loom for ribbon weaving) · **Subject (patent classes):** Shedding mechanisms not otherwise provided for; Frame-operating devices for ribbon looms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to one Loom with a vertically extending warp, in particular one for weaving of ribbons and similar webs of material, in which the moving limbs are their drive obtained by a single drive element, namely directly from the drive shaft, which the latter carries cams for this purpose.

**How it works (reading):** One cylinder fixed on the drive shaft carries a figure-8 groove, so one cam drives both a back-and-forth shuttle motion and a slower part that returns only every second revolution.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A loom needs several moving parts, each with its own drive, and the usual crank drives make the drive shaft too busy.

**Solution:** One cylinder fixed on the drive shaft carries a figure-8 groove, so one cam drives both a back-and-forth shuttle motion and a slower part that returns only every second revolution.
