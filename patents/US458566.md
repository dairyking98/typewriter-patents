## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators need to see the printed line and advance the paper with little effort.

**Solution:** A platen and paper-guide arrangement that turns the platen forward to expose the printed line, then feeds and returns it, tied to the carriage return lever.

**How it works:** A spring-driven gear segment drives the carriage by a rack. The paper guard, a curved sheet-metal guide with a roller, is carried on supports independent of the platen, and a sprung corrugated block engages the platen's toothed wheel to turn both together when a rod is drawn forward, exposing the line. The front guide ends in a pointer and may work with a scale on the carriage rod. The same return lever then advances the paper to the next line and returns the platen.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** platen; toothed wheel; paper guard; guide support; corrugated block; spring; rod; gear segment; rack; anti-friction wheel; index finger; scale
