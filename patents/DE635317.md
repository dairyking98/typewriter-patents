## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (print hammer switch-off for cash registers)

**How it works (reading):** The print hammer is coupled by a coupling pawl to an impact arm that moves per machine operation, so it is driven only when the operation calls for printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The print hammer of cash register printing units keeps moving after the impact stroke, smearing receipts, and existing fixes make it hard to switch the hammer off.

**Solution:** The print hammer is coupled by a coupling pawl to an impact arm that moves per machine operation, so it is driven only when the operation calls for printing.
