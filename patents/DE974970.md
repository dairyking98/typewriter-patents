## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (program-controlled form feed drive) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering

**How it works (reading):** A perforated endless control strip read by sensing brushes at two feed speeds, through dialers, drives the form feed and ends it under group control.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Form feed for tabulating and alphabetic writing machines needs a control hole or mark on every form, or a rotating disk that must cycle fully.

**Solution:** A perforated endless control strip read by sensing brushes at two feed speeds, through dialers, drives the form feed and ends it under group control.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
