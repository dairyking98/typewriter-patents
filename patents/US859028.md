## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter dust and noise are a nuisance, and the operator should still see the work.

**Solution:** A case of base board, rear wall, side walls, glass top and glass front, with a hinged cover, encloses the machine except for the keyboard.

**How it works:** The enclosure is sized slightly larger than the machine; flanges clear the carriage and the machine is operated inside the closed case.
