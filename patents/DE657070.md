## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A flat-bed typewriter running over the paper should be operated like a conventional keyboard machine.

**Solution:** Each key's pin depresses a spring-mounted grate that first swings a fan of type carriers out until the carrier's stop meets the pressed key's pin, then trips the hammer and advance.

**How it works:** The housing runs on rollers over a transverse rail, which in turn runs on a base rail along the writing mat, with a split keyboard in the housing. Each key bolt pushes down a parallel-guided grate on levers. The grate first swings the fan-shaped type compartment (leaf springs carrying types) out toward the writing line until a stop lever on the compartment hits the pressed key's bolt, setting the type. Further travel moves the hammer, which strikes the type over the ribbon from behind, and actuates the letter and line spacing mechanism on the rails. Ribbon rollers swing the ribbon into the line.
