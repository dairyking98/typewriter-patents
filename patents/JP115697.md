## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Printers attached to punched-card tabulators need fast printing of many characters at once with a type wheel that does not smear on the paper.

**Solution:** A type wheel is rolled over a stopped ratchet gear so its selected type has zero speed relative to the paper, then thrown against the paper by a guide edge.

**How it works:** A continuously turning main shaft drives a type wheel on a movable support through gears; another gear on a fixed shaft meshes with it. Card-hole signals fire an electromagnet that releases levers, and a sector stop meshes with the ratchet wheel and halts that gear. The driving gear then rolls over the stopped gear, lifting the support while the wheel keeps turning, so the type to print has zero speed. A roller on the support strikes a bump on a guide lever and throws the wheel to the paper. Extra holes set stop faces so each hole position selects one of three letters. After printing, the support is held up and later returned with a cam brake.
