## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Printing in different ink colours or qualities on a typewriter is awkward with a single ribbon.

**Solution:** Two pairs of ribbon reels, each with its own ink, are shifted along a splined spindle so only one pair is driven at a time.

**How it works:** Each reel has a tooth in its outer disk that engages a longitudinal groove in the middle of the reel spindle. A handle moves a slotted shifting bar and a lever links the two ends of the machine. Sliding the reels engages one pair with the grooved section so it turns and winds, while the other pair sits on the smooth end and stays still. A spring plate presses the idle reels to stop them drifting and the ribbon sagging. A detachable ribbon support guides the ribbon clear of the type bar screws.
