## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A copy has to be kept in step with the writing paper, and its indicator has to match the typewriter's.

**Solution:** A copy roll with a trough and rubber collars rides on the carriage and advances the copy in step with the paper, with its indicator tied to the machine's.
