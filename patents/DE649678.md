**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter base, which are caused by the power of the carriage spring when writing and when the keys are pressed, the resulting vibrations are absorbed, nrmt and the same destroyed and at the same time attenuates the resonance noises of the mechanisms.

**How it works (reading):** The typewriter stands on a feet made of a resilient hollow pipe section whose cavity changes shape under load, so the bumps are absorbed; a version has a resilient sleeve insert for heavy office use.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage spring shocks and key blows make typewriters vibrate, resonate and wear the mechanism, and the machine can walk across the desk.

**Solution:** The typewriter stands on a feet made of a resilient hollow pipe section whose cavity changes shape under load, so the bumps are absorbed; a version has a resilient sleeve insert for heavy office use.
