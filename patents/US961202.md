## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the Helmond ribbon reverse (US828518) both reversing dogs can engage their crown wheels at once, locking the winding shaft and risking stripped teeth when hand-winding.

**Solution:** Mount one crown wheel so it can yield (wobble on a stud and spring) when both dogs engage.

**How it works:** Ribbon trips near each end pass through slots in the spool cups and strike swinging arms on vertical shafts, whose latches release friction-driven hubs with dogs. A released dog turns with the spool, hits its crown or ratchet wheel and shifts the sliding winding shaft endwise, swapping which bevel gear pair drives the spools. The winding shaft has a ratchet driven by a pawl on a rock shaft vibrating at every key or space stroke. One crown wheel is loose on the shaft, held to a collar by a stud in an aperture and a spring, with a bore large enough to tilt, so it yields if both dogs engage.
