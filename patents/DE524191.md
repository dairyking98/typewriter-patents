## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to devices for automatic switching the longitudinal movement of the ribbon the grouping of all parts around the ribbon reel axis, which results in significant advantages for the structural arrangement.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon-drive member and the reversing switch are both mounted rotatably on each reel's coil axis in parallel planes stacked close together, giving a compact flat layout.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon winding parts hinder each other when spread out, so the typewriter is bulky.

**Solution:** The ribbon-drive member and the reversing switch are both mounted rotatably on each reel's coil axis in parallel planes stacked close together, giving a compact flat layout.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for automatic switching of the longitudinal movement of the ribbon on typewriters The invention relates to devices for automatic switching the longitudinal movement of the ribbon the grouping of all parts around the ribbon reel axis, which results in significant advantages for the structural arrangement. Fundamental for this grouping is according to the invention that on the ribbon reels both the drive member moved back and forth in a known manner by each keystroke for the step-by-step movement of the ribbon as well as the switch on top of each other are arranged rotatably about the coil axis. That gives the possibility of the different To arrange gear parts clearly without them hindering each other, and also allows a compact, flat design of the whole typewriter to be achieved, because all parts of the winding mechanism are in planes parallel to each other at very short intervals lie on top of each other.
