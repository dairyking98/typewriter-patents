## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line counters on line-writing office machines stop the machine at the wrong place because varying group sizes change the gaps between lines.

**Solution:** An endless belt or disc turns in step with paper advance, one revolution equal to a page height; metal strips on it close contacts that switch the machine off when the page is full.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
