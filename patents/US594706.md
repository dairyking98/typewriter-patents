## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon wears in one stripe and colour is uneven.

**Solution:** Ribbon automatically unwinds between spools, reverses at end of travel, and is shifted sideways across its width.

**How it works:** Two reels on splined shafts slide lengthwise and are geared to turn together. Pawls on a lever linked to the spacing lever turn the gears a little at each space. A 16-tooth spaced gear, through a single-tooth gear and six-tooth gear, turns a six-sided race cam that moves a slide bar to shift pawls and the reels sideways.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** ribbon reel; shaft; gear train; pawl; spacing lever; spaced gear wheel; race cam; slide bar; pitman

**Numbers:** 16 teeth; 6 teeth; six-sided cam
