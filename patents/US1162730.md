## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (computing attachment digit wheels) · **Subject (patent classes):** Making cutting tools

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the present invention is to improve the means whereby the number of digit wheels are driven by a source of power controlled by the numeral keys.

**How it works (reading):** A motor drives a master wheel, and a settable index wheel with pins is set by the numeral keys so that the projected pin limits the stroke of the index and master wheels, with an escapement releasing the pinion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter computing needs the digit wheels driven by power under control of the numeral keys, with the stroke set accurately.

**Solution:** A motor drives a master wheel, and a settable index wheel with pins is set by the numeral keys so that the projected pin limits the stroke of the index and master wheels, with an escapement releasing the pinion.

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
