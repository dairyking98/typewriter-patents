## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple and efficient way is needed to shift the printing instrumentalities relative to the platen for upper and lower case.

**Solution:** The typebar segment is raised and lowered on grooved ball guides by a rock shaft, with a locking ratchet for held shift and a spring-restored temporary shift key.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot in a segment that slides vertically in grooved guides on anti-friction balls. Adjustable set screws on lugs of the segment abut a fixed bracket to set the lower and upper case positions. Links connect the segment to crank arms on a rock shaft under the key levers. A ratchet sleeve with a coiled spring on the rock shaft, adjustable through a lock nut, provides a locked shifted position, while a temporary shift key lever acts on a crank arm so the segment returns to normal after one or more capitals.
