## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention is directed to escape- IO ment mechanism for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** An escapement with feed wheels, a feed shaft and pinion, and a cooperating feed dog that can be set for ordinary or reverse feed by a cover-plate and thumb-nut.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says earlier escapements are not simple, easy-acting and speedy, and cannot be readily set for reverse feed as well as ordinary feed.

**Solution:** An escapement with feed wheels, a feed shaft and pinion, and a cooperating feed dog that can be set for ordinary or reverse feed by a cover-plate and thumb-nut.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md)
