## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A carriage scale on typewriters runs right to left, confusing the operator; the position on a narrow slip or card is hard to see.

**Solution:** A slip-holder with notched scale attached to the carriage passes a fixed pointer on the body, showing where the next type strikes.

**How it works:** A metal holder is screwed to the carriage front, with a groove at the bottom holding a strip of paper, card or envelope, kept by end springs. The upper edge of the front wall is toothed or graduated as a scale. A pointer fixed to the machine body sits close to the holder; as keys are struck the scale moves left past the pointer, showing the printing position and margins marked on the slip.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** slip-holder; groove; end spring; graduated scale; pointer; screw

**Numbers:** paper width 6.5 to 9.5 inches (scale range)
