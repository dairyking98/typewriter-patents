**How it works (reading):** Line switching is done by a friction wheel and a spring-loaded friction roller, with the rack punched from the plate itself.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line switching of the font plate by toothed rack and ratchet is imprecise and rack teeth wear quickly.

**Solution:** Line switching is done by a friction wheel and a spring-loaded friction roller, with the rack punched from the plate itself.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

> Description Translated from German Type holders for typewriters. The invention relates to a Font holder for typewriters or similar machines, in which the Document-carrying platen can be switched line by line. The switching mechanisms previously used for such font holders existed from toothed ratchet wheels or gear racks. These have the disadvantages that an exact line setting is not possible with coarse teeth, while the Production of fine teeth with the required precision is quite expensive and these racks are subject to early wear because of the specific The surface pressure on the individual tooth is too great. These disadvantages are according to the invention eliminated in that the circuit of the font plate by a Friction gear is effected. According to the invention, this consists of a friction wheel, which is fixedly arranged on the shaft carrying the drive wheel, and one through a spring influenced friction roller, which in a section of one on the Axis rotatably mounted bracket is slidably mounted, which the role The leading outer edge of the cutout forms an acute angle with that at the point of contact the roller with the friction wheel on this drawn tangent forms.
