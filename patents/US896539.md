## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to ribbon mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon mechanism provides automatic reversal and direct manual operation in either direction, and adjusts the ribbon throw so the type strikes a selected zone of the ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With a polychrome ribbon, letters can encroach on an adjoining colour zone or run past the ribbon edge unless the ribbon is positioned correctly at the moment of printing.

**Solution:** The ribbon mechanism provides automatic reversal and direct manual operation in either direction, and adjusts the ribbon throw so the type strikes a selected zone of the ribbon.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** rocking ribbon reverse lever; ribbon spools; bichrome ribbon
