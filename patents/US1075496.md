## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work sheets slip out of alignment as they are fed around the platen.

**Solution:** Perforated work sheets engage conical studs on the platen ends, and the studs are arranged in a partial row so the sheet can be inserted without interference.
