## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** The object is to provide such a copy-holder adapted to be fixed to the carriage of a typewriting machine, and to be manipula ed by the movements of the hand or carriage lever of the machine, and which will present the copy in a convenient position for reading.

**How it works (reading):** Standards clamped to the carriage carry aid rollers and a feed-roller band; a ratchet on the feed roller is driven by the carriage lever to advance the copy.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The copy is hard to read from the side, and it must be presented in a convenient position for reading.

**Solution:** Standards clamped to the carriage carry aid rollers and a feed-roller band; a ratchet on the feed roller is driven by the carriage lever to advance the copy.
