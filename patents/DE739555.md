## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing device for overlapping receipts)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a further embodiment of the patent 6.

**How it works (reading):** The stop is moved in front of the receipts so they shift to a different line, and the printing takes place on the return stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Overlapping receipts on a register print on the same spot and crush the sheet edges.

**Solution:** The stop is moved in front of the receipts so they shift to a different line, and the printing takes place on the return stroke.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
