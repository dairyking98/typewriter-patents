> Description Translated from German Springy button. It is known that all typewriters regard their construction, in particular the type lever mechanism, the carriage construction and of the roller, always only for a certain maximum load by the recorder are calculated. Often the typewriters in the Ways destroyed, that parts of the type lever mechanism break, the types and rollers themselves premature wear and tear and the ribbon wears out very quickly. This drawback is alleviated according to the invention by a new key arrangement so remedied that the blow exerted on the key on the associated type lever mechanism is transmitted by a spring, the rest tension of which is equal to the highest permissible Stop force is. The inclusion of resilient members in the key itself or in the key bar is known per se, but these springs have so far been exclusive arranged for the purpose of resiliently closing the finger on the key make, namely by the fact that the spring tension is so low that when it hits of the finger on the key initially a gradually increasing compression of the Spring enters and only after a certain compression that Type lever system comes into action.
