## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mounting or connecting key buttons on or to key levers

**How it works (reading):** Each key bar is made as a hollow spring housing with a strongly preloaded spring, bearing on a crossbolt, that passes the blow to the lever; the preload equals normal stop force, so only over-hard strokes compress it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hard key strikes overstress the type lever mechanism, break parts and wear types, rollers and ribbon.

**Solution:** Each key bar is made as a hollow spring housing with a strongly preloaded spring, bearing on a crossbolt, that passes the blow to the lever; the preload equals normal stop force, so only over-hard strokes compress it.
