## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters with a substantially horizontally movable changeover frame and intended for the changeover movements to reduce the force required in such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Support springs counteract the weight of the changeover frame and its attached parts, so the horizontal shift takes less force. The example is a Remington portable with a carriage carrier on hanging rods and arms on an oscillating shaft.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Horizontal shifting of the changeover frame needs too much force because the frame and the carriage carrier are heavy.

**Solution:** Support springs counteract the weight of the changeover frame and its attached parts, so the horizontal shift takes less force. The example is a Remington portable with a carriage carrier on hanging rods and arms on an oscillating shaft.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)
