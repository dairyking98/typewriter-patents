## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for typewriters, with which the stops for setting the edge, which are located behind the roller carriage can be easily adjusted using a side crank permit.

**How it works (reading):** Each margin stop sits on a threaded spindle turned by a front-mounted side crank through a chain drive, with a rotating scale showing the stop position at the front, so the margin can be set from the front.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops behind the platen carriage require the operator to get up and reach behind the machine to adjust them, wasting time, while stops in front block the view of the paper and access to the ribbon.

**Solution:** Each margin stop sits on a threaded spindle turned by a front-mounted side crank through a chain drive, with a rotating scale showing the stop position at the front, so the margin can be set from the front.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
