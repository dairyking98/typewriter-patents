## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Writing a date needs six to eight characters, normally one keystroke each.

**Solution:** A widened typebar head carries several type blocks side by side, each individually interchangeable without tools, so a whole date prints with one stroke.

**How it works:** The wide type lever head has guide grooves on its face, round or prismatic. Type blocks with matching foot strips slide in from above. A transverse cylindrical bolt through the head projects into the grooves and engages a stop on each foot, locking the blocks. Turning the bolt a half turn by a small lever aligns milled gaps with the feet, freeing the blocks to be exchanged.
