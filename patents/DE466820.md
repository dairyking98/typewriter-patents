## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for automatically reversing the longitudinal movement of the ink ribbon for writing, calculating or similar machines, in which a rolling lever is toggle-like against a.

**How it works (reading):** The rolling lever is coupled directly to the ribbon guide levers and is shifted by the ribbon's own tension against a spring, so the ribbon itself triggers the reversal and the drive is by friction without teeth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing automatic ribbon reversal needed a pawl to release the rolling lever, extra tooth engagement, and special safeguards, and friction made the gear work harder.

**Solution:** The rolling lever is coupled directly to the ribbon guide levers and is shifted by the ribbon's own tension against a spring, so the ribbon itself triggers the reversal and the drive is by friction without teeth.
