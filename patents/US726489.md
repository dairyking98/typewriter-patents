## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** The aim of my invention is more particularly to provide an escapement for typewriters of that class using an escapementwheel and in connection with which I use a dog comprising two rigid members extending According to my construction the teeth of the escapement-wheel are always in engagement with one of the rigid members of a double-membered and integral dog, both of said members being rigid and relatively fixed.
