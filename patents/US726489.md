## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** The aim of my invention is more particularly to provide an escapement for typewriters of that class using an escapementwheel and in connection with which I use a dog comprising two rigid members extending According to my construction the teeth of the escapement-wheel are always in engagement with one of the rigid members of a double-membered and integral dog, both of said members being rigid and relatively fixed.

**How it works (reading):** A double-membered rigid dog kept in continuous engagement with the escapement wheel, with its faces positioned so the carriage advances part of the distance on the downward key stroke and the rest on the upward stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A carriage escapement that advances the carriage in jerks, with the dog disengaging from the wheel, is unsteady.

**Solution:** A double-membered rigid dog kept in continuous engagement with the escapement wheel, with its faces positioned so the carriage advances part of the distance on the downward key stroke and the rest on the upward stroke.
