## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writers and like ro machines, and has for its object to provide a type-bar basket which may be bodily removed from the machine and again inserted without any difficulty, so as to facilitate repairs and cleaning.

**How it works (reading):** The basket rests on the carriage frame members by its segmental front member and extensions, is held against lateral motion by side bars, and is connected to the frame by a rear support and an operating-lever, so it can be lifted out bodily.
