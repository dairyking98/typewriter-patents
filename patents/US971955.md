## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabu ating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Simpler tabulator mechanism that quickly determines the carriage arrest position and sets the tabulator accordingly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting column stops for each carriage index is time-consuming and hard to plan.

**Solution:** Simpler tabulator mechanism that quickly determines the carriage arrest position and sets the tabulator accordingly.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
