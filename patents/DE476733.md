## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a sheet music typewriter with in the direction of Staff lines displaceable type carrier, which in each of its positions also a pressure movement can perform perpendicular to the workpiece.

**How it works (reading):** A type carrier that moves in both directions perpendicular to the workpiece is driven by a stationary pedal lever, so the carrier can slide along the staff lines and press at each position, and the machine is narrower.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sliding type carriers with a hand-moved type in a rod were too slow for music writing, and idle hand movements disrupted the work.

**Solution:** A type carrier that moves in both directions perpendicular to the workpiece is driven by a stationary pedal lever, so the carrier can slide along the staff lines and press at each position, and the machine is narrower.
