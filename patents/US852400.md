## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices actuated when platen reaches the end of a line

**How it works (reading):** A line-locking mechanism on the carriage frame engages a stop at the desired point, warning the operator beforehand and locking the keys once the carriage halts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage must be locked automatically at a chosen point, with the operator warned in advance and the keys locked while the carriage is stopped.

**Solution:** A line-locking mechanism on the carriage frame engages a stop at the desired point, warning the operator beforehand and locking the keys once the carriage halts.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
