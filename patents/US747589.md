## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen line spacing should be quick to adjust between full and half spacing.

**Solution:** A hand-operated line-spacing arm with a pawl whose engagement point is set by an adjustable pawl-controlling piece held by a pin in one of several holes.

**How it works:** A line-spacing arm pivots fore and aft on the platen frame, sprung to rest. It carries a spacing pawl at right angles to the arm pivot, spring-pressed toward the platen ratchet wheel and limited by abutments. A controlling piece on the platen axis keeps the pawl off the ratchet until part of its travel is done. A spring plate with perforations lets a pin hold the controlling piece in positions, so the pawl engages earlier or later, giving one tooth or more. A spring detent holds the platen.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet wheel; line-spacing arm; spacing pawl; pawl-controlling piece; spring plate; pin; abutment; detent; hand wheel
