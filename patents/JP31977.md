## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A machine for scripts with thousands of characters (kanji) needs easy, quick, sure selection while staying small and light.

**Solution:** A single handle moved over a flat chart board turns a large type drum (forward-back) and slides the paper-cylinder carriage along the drum (left-right), so the pointer position on the chart maps to the type at the print point.

**How it works:** A type drum with many types around its surface sits at the back of the frame. A paper-cylinder holder slides on rails parallel to the drum, and a guide arm from it runs over the chart board with a handle and pointer sliding on it. Sliding the handle along the arm drives a rack and gear on a splined shaft, which through a gear train turns the drum half a revolution over the arm's length; a reversing lever swaps gear trains to bring the other half of the drum round. Holding the locking key frees both motions; releasing it lets a rack and a detent into a toothed ring lock the carriage and drum at exact position. Pressing the printing key tilts a long plate that pushes a lever, swinging a hammer with a rubber head so it strikes paper against the type, and the same stroke trips the escapement so a spring barrel moves the cylinder one character space. A separate spacing key spaces alone.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention concerns a printing machine provided with a type drum having a great many types arranged on its surface; a chart board on which the characters and signs corresponding to the drum are shown on a flat surface in the same order; and a handle that can be moved lengthwise and crosswise to any position on the chart board. Moving the handle to the wanted position produces, in linked fashion, the turning of the drum and the sliding of the paper holder parallel to the drum, and so brings to the printing point the type on the drum that matches the character or sign shown by the pointer on the handle. Its object is to let the wanted character or sign be chosen easily and printed quickly and surely, to keep the size and weight as small as possible, and to suit the machine above all to those, such as kanji printing machines, that need a great many types.
