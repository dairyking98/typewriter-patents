## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing mechanism needs to be simple and efficient for a front-strike typewriter.

**Solution:** A line-spacing lever carries a pawl or puppet-dog that engages the platen ratchet, with an adjustable stop and a brake-shoe lever on the carriage to control the line feed.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
