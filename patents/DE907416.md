## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In tabulators the advanced tab stop is returned by spring or drive, and friction between an advanced stop and its column stop must be overcome after each tab step.

**Solution:** The tab stop is advanced by a stored force such as a spring, held in its position by a pawl, and then withdrawn by motor power once it strikes a column stop, which overcomes the friction left after tabulation.
