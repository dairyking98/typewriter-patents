## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inking apparatus

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to produce a type-Writing machine which perforates the paper in forming the characters, the perforations which form the characters being formed with or without inked or otherwise colored edge or marginal portions, accordingly as the machine is provided with a color-applying mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Type are slid endwise in a carrier to the printing point and pressed up through a die plate by a toggle lever to perforate the paper.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter that perforates characters through the paper with a die plate, with or without coloured edges.

**Solution:** Type are slid endwise in a carrier to the printing point and pressed up through a die plate by a toggle lever to perforate the paper.

**How it works:** A stationary frame carries a toothed index guide with vertical slots, a rocker shaft with levers 15 and 20 and a toggle of bars 16 and 17 worked from a key rod. A combined die-plate, type-carrier and paper-receiver holds a row of type bars, each with a lug 23. The carrier slides lengthwise so a chosen type bar sits between the lever ends. Pressing the key raises lever 15 so the toggle drives the type up through the die plate, and lever 20 presses it back down with a yielding action. Ink rolls beside the perforation 27 may colour the edges, and a paper carriage with roll and plate runs on rods. Only the opening is available.
