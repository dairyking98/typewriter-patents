## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing device for registering machines)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to printing devices for registering machines, e.

**How it works (reading):** A movable support carries the document and is driven by one conveyor that gives both sliding and rotation, so prints can be made at any position and angle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Documents in registering machines can only be printed in one direction and conveyor masses are large, so skewed or repositioned prints are impractical.

**Solution:** A movable support carries the document and is driven by one conveyor that gives both sliding and rotation, so prints can be made at any position and angle.

**Features:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
