## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a speed controller for the paper roll carriage typewriters, especially those with tabs.

**How it works (reading):** Friction blocks on a rotating body are thrown outward by centrifugal force against the housing wall, so they brake only through centrifugal action and give a stronger braking effect for return movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Centrifugal speed governors for the carriage jam or act only in one direction, which is wrong for table writing.

**Solution:** Friction blocks on a rotating body are thrown outward by centrifugal force against the housing wall, so they brake only through centrifugal action and give a stronger braking effect for return movement.
