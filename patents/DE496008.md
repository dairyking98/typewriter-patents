## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (fibre pulp molding) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manufacture of hollow articles from fibre suspensions or papier-mâché by deposition of fibres in or on a wire-net mould

**How it works (reading):** The mould interior is loaded with air pressure and a vacuum is applied from outside, with the overheated steam handled to avoid condensate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Large pulp hollow bodies dewater too slowly, and steam pressure condenses inside the mould.

**Solution:** The mould interior is loaded with air pressure and a vacuum is applied from outside, with the overheated steam handled to avoid condensate.
