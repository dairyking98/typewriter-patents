## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating work needs a way to stop the carriage at predetermined points.

**Solution:** A key lever pushes a sub-lever so a stop on the frame enters the carriage path and releases the carriage, which then arrests on the stop rack bar.
