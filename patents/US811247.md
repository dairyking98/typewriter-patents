## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A typewriter operable over an ordinary telegraph wire, with the type wheels of two machines at opposite ends kept in unison and the carriage working automatically.

**Solution:** A motor-driven type wheel that is stopped on the chosen character by a magnet-operated pawl when a key at either end closes the line circuit.

**How it works:** A spring or other motor continuously turns a vertical tubular shaft. A cone-shaped slide on the shaft engages serrations in a cone on the type wheel, a cylindrical wheel with a single line of type on its periphery and a toothed flange. A magnet on the line circuit pulls an armature rod; its lever shifts the spindle and the spring-pressed pawl catches a tooth of the flange so the wheel stops at a given character, with a spring cushion in the spindle. The carriage then prints through a ratchet and pawl, platen rotation and return at line end are automatic. Keys of an operating board complete the circuit at the right moment. The cut text ends before the sending circuit is detailed.
