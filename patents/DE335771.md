## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Original and carbon copy sheets need separate guides on the paper carriage.

**Solution:** A paper trolley of two parallel tracks with couplable carriers: one carrier guides the original on rollers and the other guides the carbon copy.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
