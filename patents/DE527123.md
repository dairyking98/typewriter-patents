## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing tabulator with zero types) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for producing a permanent visual presentation of the output data, e.g. computer output printers using printers by rack-type printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing tabulator in which the zero types a blind position can only be brought into the printing position if the type carrier of the higher places in turn come into print position, so that an imprint of zeros does not take place before the highest digit of the value.

**How it works (reading):** Each zero-type holder carries a projection that the next higher number-type carrier pushes directly into print position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Zero-type holders of a printing tabulator need intermediate linkage to be blanked until the higher digits arrive.

**Solution:** Each zero-type holder carries a projection that the next higher number-type carrier pushes directly into print position.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
