## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating mechanism for typewriters needs denominational stops that can be fixed at set points on a scale to define a column.

**Solution:** Key levers with bell cranks and a fulcrum bar drive denominational stops set on a graduated carrier; the stops are carried by the carrier and a rack releasing device guides the rack vertically, letting the carriage move to the column position.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
