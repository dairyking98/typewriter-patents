## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Condensed record billing and feeding of envelopes and tags need better platen control than the standard machine.

**Solution:** Platen-control devices with a locking lever for the stops and paper feeding parts are added to the platen carriage.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
