## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection; Cylindrical Japanese-language typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** The object is to make the space that holds the type smaller, to keep the type [printed 活定, read 活字] from falling out when the machine's position changes, so that it can be carried, and to remove the noise of printing, and so to obtain a small, simple, portable Japanese-script typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** Many footed types are wedged into the rims of spring-mounted type wheels on one shaft, turned together and located by a scrolling index cloth, and one wheel is pushed out to print onto a rubber plate behind the paper.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Loose-type Japanese typewriters are bulky, lose types when moved, and are noisy; a small portable machine is wanted.

**Solution:** Many footed types are wedged into the rims of spring-mounted type wheels on one shaft, turned together and located by a scrolling index cloth, and one wheel is pushed out to print onto a rubber plate behind the paper.

**How it works:** Each type wheel carries cube types with feet in a dovetail groove, sits on the shaft through its own spiral spring, and is held aligned by cords tied to a sliding disc kept taut by a coil spring and a V-brake. A hand crank turns the shaft, so all the wheels rotate together, and the same gears turn an index cloth between rollers; a carriage carrying the shaft slides on ball-bearing rails and the character under the index needle shows which type is at the print position. Pressing the key first swings an arc lever that wipes an ink pad over the type and releases the brake, then lifts a push-out arm that thrusts one wheel out so its type prints through the paper onto a rubber plate. The key also depresses a space key that, through gears, a link and a hook, ratchets the paper roller one tooth (1.5 type lengths) on the return; the space key can also be struck alone. A worm and handle move the paper rail table for line feed.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Portable Japanese-script typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. In this invention, types with feet are fitted into the outer circumference of a ring to make a type wheel. A large number of these are fitted, each through its own spiral spring, onto the circumference of one shaft, so that every wheel turns in unison with the shaft; the wheels are linked by cords so that their positions always stay aligned. When the printing lever is pressed down, the rise of an arm pushes out one type wheel only, in a fixed direction; ink is spread on the type face on its circumference, and that one type face is pressed on the surface of paper backed by a rubber plate and prints. An index sheet made of a flexible material is wound between rollers that turn in unison with the shaft, showing [half] of it on the surface. The object is to make the space that holds the type smaller, to keep the type [printed 活定, read 活字] from falling out when the machine's position changes, so that it can be carried, and to remove the noise of printing, and so to obtain a small, simple, portable Japanese-script typewriter.
