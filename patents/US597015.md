## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing typewriters are complex, heavy and costly.

**Solution:** A light rectangular-case machine with a flat platen, parallel multi-character typebars selected by a stylus-operated condensed keyboard, and an automatic inking pad.

**How it works:** A flat platen sits in the bed under a movable type-carriage on a rod. Shift disks rock a rod to throw the type-carriage back one or two points, bringing the middle (capital) or last (numeral) characters on each typebar over a notch for printing. The carriage is stepped by ratchet bars, a struck dog on a crank-driven rocking shaft impresses the bars, and an inking pad inks the type automatically. Lines of paper are adjusted automatically.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:pointer-chart](../features/sel-pointer-chart.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** bed; flat platen; type-carriage; typebar; shift disk; shift arm; ratchet bar; stylus keyboard; inking pad; rocking shaft; crank

**Numbers:** three characters per typebar; shift of one or two points
