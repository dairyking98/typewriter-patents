The invention relates to a device on the housing for the ribbon reel a typewriter. It comprises a divided housing which is arranged in such a way that a part or all Parts of the same can be opened laterally to insert or remove a coil. The housing is equipped with a device that, when the Housing prevents removal of the coil B59985 changes and supports any slack tape winding within the housing so that it does not fall and become entangled in any part of the tape guide. The invention is particularly applicable to Machines with type stop against the front of the paper roller.
