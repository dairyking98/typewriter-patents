## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools cannot be easily inserted or removed, and slack ribbon falls and tangles in the tape guide.

**Solution:** The ribbon spool housing is split into two hinged parts that open sideways, with an internal support that holds slack ribbon and a lock that keeps the housing closed.
