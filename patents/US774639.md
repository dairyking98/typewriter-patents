## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Raising and lowering the platen between lower case, capitals and figures needs an efficient, well-guided mechanism.

**Solution:** A vertically rocking platen frame of shaft, rail and arms, held at a spring-supported middle position, is raised or lowered by shift keys and guided by an upper rock-shaft.

**How it works:** The platen is journaled in adjustable posts of a platen carrier that rides on a rail with loose-fit screws. A lower rock-shaft with arms and a rod forms the shifting frame, raised and lowered from a middle position by a link connected to the shift levers. A pivoted lever under a lug on the link, held up by a spring with an adjusting screw on a swivelled collar, supports the middle (small-case) position. An upper rock-shaft with hooked arms engages the platen shaft to guide it truly vertical, adjustable lengthwise by slotted slides.
