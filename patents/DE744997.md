## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Noiseless typewriters reduced type speed but ignored vibration and resonance between hard, stiff engine parts and a light frame.

**Solution:** Make the type engine of internally damped unhardened steel with low natural frequencies and the frame of stiff closed side frames, braced by cross struts, with high natural frequencies, so the two never resonate.

**How it works:** The engine parts are unhardened, high-damping steel shaped and dimensioned for low natural frequencies. The frame uses strong closed side parts joined by front and rear cross struts and thick-walled partly arched crosspieces, raising its natural frequency far from the engine's. Curves in the drawings show vibration amplitude peaking when the two frequency ratio is one, and comparison tests against known machines show lower sound levels across vibration ranges.
