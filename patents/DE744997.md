> Description Translated from German Silent Typewriter The invention relates to silent typewriters Typewriters with one stored in a frame, the types with a reduced one Speed and increased pressure to the type engine producing the imprint. The purpose of the invention is to produce noiseless typewriters of this type so train. that the emergence and propagation of vibrations and sound waves, especially those in a physiologically uncomfortable area, if possible is suppressed. In previously known machines of the type mentioned, one has essentially limited by operational measures the speed of the types at To reduce the impact on the impression site. The moving parts and the parts used to support them, in particular the frame, were designed only with regard to strength and inexpensive manufacturing processes, without considering their interaction with regard to the development and propagation of vibrations and noises.
