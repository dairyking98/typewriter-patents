> Description Translated from German Method and device for closing and aligning the type carrier of line typewriters or typesetting machines. The final coloring of the type carrier takes place according to the invention only after the closing device has finally been closed. Of the Drive of the locking device works according to the invention in two stages, from which the first stage brings about a restriction of the mobility of the type carrier and the second causes the final closure.
