## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Syllable, line, or like type selection

**How it works (reading):** A two-stage locking drive first restricts the mobility of the type carrier so it can be pre-merged and aligned while still movable, then brings it to the exact final line length, with the colouring device working only after final closing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Closing and aligning the type carrier of a line writer or typesetter in one step can misalign the yielding spaces, so the line length must be reached gradually.

**Solution:** A two-stage locking drive first restricts the mobility of the type carrier so it can be pre-merged and aligned while still movable, then brings it to the exact final line length, with the colouring device working only after final closing.

The final coloring of the type carrier takes place according to the invention only after the closing device has finally been closed. Of the Drive of the locking device works according to the invention in two stages, from which the first stage brings about a restriction of the mobility of the type carrier and the second causes the final closure.
