## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide a ready means for depressing the keys marked Gap.

**How it works (reading):** A plate fixed to the machine carries a pivoted screw and a linkage of bars and standards, so knee movement presses the Gap and Fig keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Gap and Fig keys must be pressed by hand, which ties up the hands.

**Solution:** A plate fixed to the machine carries a pivoted screw and a linkage of bars and standards, so knee movement presses the Gap and Fig keys.
