## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in a combined type-cleaning and ribbon-winding device, and the objects of my improvement are, first, to provide an interchangeble cleaning device and an interchangeable ribbonspool for type-Writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** Crank-driven spindle with a gear, clamping either a cleaning brush or a ribbon spool, in an offset of the handle rod.
