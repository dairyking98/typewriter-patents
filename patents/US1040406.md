## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a Remington visible machine with a two-colour ribbon, changing the ribbon vibrator throw by one key without a matching stop let the ribbon be thrown past the chosen colour field, and independently set throw and stops could be reset out of step.

**Solution:** Give the ribbon vibrator a second, separate key-set pair of stops, and a check that blocks resetting the throw until the stops have been reset first.

**How it works:** A universal bar and rock shaft vibrate an operating lever that lifts a ribbon vibrator to present the ribbon in front of the type at each stroke; the lever is made of two jointed parts so a platen shift also lifts the ribbon to match. A finger button at the front slides a shift frame to move a stud nearer the fulcrum, so the lever has a longer throw and presents the lower ribbon field. A second button at the left side turns a rock shaft carrying a stop with two faces at different distances, held by a V-notch detent, so the vibrating lever is stopped by the correct face and cannot overthrow the ribbon past the field. An arm on that rock shaft lies in the path of the throw lever so the throw cannot be re-set until the stop shaft is re-set first. A snap-in sheet metal ribbon frame with ears fits spring forks on the vibrator to hold the ribbon at the printing point.

- Remington No. 10 key-set ribbon throw: adds a separate stop key and an order check so the ribbon cannot overthrow
