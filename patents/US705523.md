## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier Fisher book-typewriter ribbon feeds (lateral shift per letter space, then longitudinal feed through the key action) were complex or loaded the key action.

**Solution:** A simple automatic ribbon feed, driven from an operating rack as the carriage moves, with an operator-reversible direction.

**How it works:** On the flat-platen Fisher book machine the ribbon runs between two spools and the feed is effected automatically as the machine spaces. Almost all feed elements are carried on the traveling frame with only an operating rack separate. The operator can shift a part to reverse the feed direction when the ribbon has wound onto one spool. The opening reviews Fisher patents 569,625 and 637,749 as earlier forms.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon spool; operating rack; traveling frame; carriage

**Improves on:**

- [US569625](../patents/US569625.md): ribbon no longer shifted laterally with carriage motion
- [US637749](../patents/US637749.md): feed no longer loads the key action or needs a ribbon mechanism advanced along the frame
