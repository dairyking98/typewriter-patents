## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-powered carriage return gives a force and speed that vary with carriage position, and clutches must be switched.

**Solution:** The carriage is returned by the motor through a permanent force-fit connection, so it moves at the same speed and force in every position without a separate clutch.
