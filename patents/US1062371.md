## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen mechanism of the ribbon-inked machine is to be improved; the inventor's stated object is improved platen mechanism for the special ribbon mechanism.

**Solution:** A hollow stationary casing or drum replaces the cylindrical platen; it holds an anvil element that works with the types through a longitudinal slit in the casing, and the paper is fed around the stationary casing.

**How it works:** The casing stays stationary during paper feed. Inside it, an anvil or platen element cooperates with the types through a slit running along the casing. Paper is fed around the hollow casing by feed rolls at its ends. The invention is said to work with ribbon or pad inking.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** hollow casing; drum; anvil element; slit; feed rolls; ribbon mechanism

**Improves on:**

- regular cylindrical rotating platen: stationary hollow casing with internal anvil and slit
