## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators of the Caligraph find it hard to centre a heading on the sheet, since the starting point on the carriage scale must be worked out.

**Solution:** A numeral chart, printed in duplicate 1 to 70 on the typewriter base, lets the operator count one number per letter from the centre mark and read off the starting scale position.

**How it works:** The chart numbers match the type-space scale on the machine (70 spaces across, centre at 35). To centre a heading, count from 35 to the right one number per letter and one per space between words; the number reached is the carriage pointer setting at which to begin. Because the chart numbers run in duplicate, counting halves the heading's length and leaves half left of centre. Higher numbers are used for right margins, lower for left margins.

**Parts:** numeral guide-chart; carriage scale; pointer

**Numbers:** 70 character spaces across the writing space; centre at space 35
