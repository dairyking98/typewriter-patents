## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and is directed more particularly to the line-locking mechanism thereof, the object being to simplify the construction of the adjustable trip mechanism and to provide an efficient and compact structure of the charactor described.

**How it works (reading):** A simplified adjustable trip and locking pawl for the line-lock in a Densmore-type machine, giving a compact arrangement of key-levers linked to a lever that controls the carriage stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The adjustable trip mechanism for line-locking is complicated and the carriage must be held in position reliably.

**Solution:** A simplified adjustable trip and locking pawl for the line-lock in a Densmore-type machine, giving a compact arrangement of key-levers linked to a lever that controls the carriage stop.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
