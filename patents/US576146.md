## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Half-basket typebar machines need many bars for capitals and figures, and the writing is hard to see.

**Solution:** Each typebar carries three characters and strikes a shiftable anvil whose impact also spaces the letter and moves the ribbon.

**How it works:** Type-bars with three engraved characters strike an anvil face that can be moved to present any one of them. The anvil is also raised with the carriage to inspect writing. The impact drives letter spacing and ribbon movement.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [IMP:anvil-backing](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** typebar; anvil; auxiliary frame; paper carriage; ribbon spool; shift key

**Numbers:** 27 typebars; 3 characters per type; 1/8 in. character spacing

**Improves on:**

- roller-platen half-basket machines: flat movable anvil instead of platen roller
