## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Process for the production of color ribbons. Procedures are already known for the production of ribbons for typewriters, type printing devices u. Like. In which a long, color-soaked tape in pieces of the required Length is divided, which are provided with lacquer coating at the ends. The invention consists in dotting the ends of the pieces prior to application the paint coating can be freed from paint. This achieves the following advantages: The usual solvents for the protective layer often also have a dissolving effect on the colored impregnations, so that when choosing the material for the protective layer and its solvent the property of the impregnation must be taken into account as far as possible. This hindrance now disappears because the protective layer with the impregnation does not come into contact at all. The ribbons are manufactured according to the invention in such a way that one made of a fabric in the manner already known per se Stripes of great length soaked with paint and completely soaked the stripe then broken down into as many parts as there are ribbons to be made.
