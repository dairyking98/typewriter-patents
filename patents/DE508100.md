## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens

**How it works (reading):** A second locking lever, driven by the carriage base plate and a bolt hinged on the removable carriage, locks the spring lever when the carriage is lifted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting a removable carriage can let the carriage spring run off because the locking lever is disengaged.

**Solution:** A second locking lever, driven by the carriage base plate and a bolt hinged on the removable carriage, locks the spring lever when the carriage is lifted.
