## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon base is a swollen tragacanth gel in glycerine instead of fats or oils, mixed with a suitable dye and zinc ammonium chloride, and applied to the raw tape.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Usual typewriter ribbons impregnated with oils or fats cannot carry salts such as zinc ammonium chloride for reproduction, since the fats block their effect.

**Solution:** The ribbon base is a swollen tragacanth gel in glycerine instead of fats or oils, mixed with a suitable dye and zinc ammonium chloride, and applied to the raw tape.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)

**Parts:** raw tape; tragacanth gel; glycerine; dye; zinc ammonium chloride

**Numbers:** 2.5 parts tragacanth; 97.5 parts glycerine

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Process for the production of colored ribbons, in particular for reproduction purposes The usual ones, by impregnating the raw tape with a solution of a suitable one Coloring agent in oils or fats of mineral, vegetable or animal origin with the addition of glycerine or digl. let manufactured typewriter ribbons opt for reproduction purposes in such cases: do not use where it is on it arrives to incorporate salts, such as zinc ammonium chloride or the like, into the ribbon at the same time, which do not dissolve in fats and oils and their effectiveness due to the simultaneous Presence of fats and oils in the ribbon is adversely affected. It has now been found that one is very suitable for such purposes Ribbon can be made by using as a base instead of fats, oils or waxing a swelling of tragacanth in glycerine used. For example, one rubs 2.5 parts tragacanth with 97.5 parts glycerine and lets this mixture swell on a boiling water bath until a gelatinous mass is formed, which is mixed with a suitable dye and zinc ammonium chloride or the like Raw tape is used.
