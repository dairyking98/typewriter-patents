## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a cipher typewriter, a rarely used letter (x) stands for word separation; the plaintext's natural word gaps are lost in the cipher.

**Solution:** A stop pin on the type roller meets a swing-out flap on the shaft guide at the x position so the roller cannot hit the platen for that letter.

**How it works:** The machine has a character field, setting pointer and type roller. The x lies at the roller's lower edge; a pin at the roller's far end points down at that position and abuts a shoulder on a flap pivoted on the shaft guide, preventing impression but only for that character. When writing the cipher itself, the flap is swung aside so x prints too.
