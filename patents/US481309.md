## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to devices for varying IO the feed of type-writers proportionately to the difference in the space required by the various characters imprinted.
