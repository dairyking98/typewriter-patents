## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to devices for varying IO the feed of type-writers proportionately to the difference in the space required by the various characters imprinted.

**How it works (reading):** A variable-feed escapement of several ratchet wheels and a selecting pawl, giving 2 to 7 unit spaces per character in constant ratio to a matrix machine.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters offer only two or three feed degrees, so copy for a matrix machine cannot show true proportional width for justification.

**Solution:** A variable-feed escapement of several ratchet wheels and a selecting pawl, giving 2 to 7 unit spaces per character in constant ratio to a matrix machine.

**How it works:** Characters are classed by width in units: i-class 2, I-class 3, a-class 4, A-class 5, m-class 6, M-class 7, with 2 units as the word space. An escapement with several ratchet wheels and stop-pins on a supporting bracket is fitted to a Caligraph; an operating pawl is moved to a given ratchet wheel according to the key struck, producing the corresponding feed. The type is cut to the same unit scale so line length shows the justification spaces needed.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** ratchet wheel; operating pawl; stop-pin; supporting bracket; escapement

**Numbers:** character classes of 2,3,4,5,6,7 units; word space 2 units
