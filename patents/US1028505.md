## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in the means forrotatinga platen in a typewriting machine.

**How it works (reading):** A pivoted arm with an overthrow stop and pawl engages the platen ratchet, and a hand piece with notches on a bracket sets the stop position, so the amount of platen rotation between lines is adjusted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Double spacing between printed lines needs a simple device added to the ordinary platen operating means.

**Solution:** A pivoted arm with an overthrow stop and pawl engages the platen ratchet, and a hand piece with notches on a bracket sets the stop position, so the amount of platen rotation between lines is adjusted.
