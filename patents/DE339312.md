**How it works (reading):** The locking is moved to a link that moves only in the direction of the universal rail, pushed into its blocking position by a slot guide and held there, so the lock acts reliably at high typing speed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin-stop line-delimiter locking bars do not swing into or out of their locked position fast enough during fast typing, so keys can still strike and the carriage can shift.

**Solution:** The locking is moved to a link that moves only in the direction of the universal rail, pushed into its blocking position by a slot guide and held there, so the lock acts reliably at high typing speed.
