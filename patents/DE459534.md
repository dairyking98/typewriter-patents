**How it works (reading):** The computing drive is mounted on the front wall, with slides driven by the number key pull rods, a control shaft with a screw cam, and a drive gear arch that engages the counter drive wheel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculating drives built into the typewriter's flat slides could not be fitted to an ordinary keyboard machine, and the drive needed to sit close to the front wall to keep the keys visible.

**Solution:** The computing drive is mounted on the front wall, with slides driven by the number key pull rods, a control shaft with a screw cam, and a drive gear arch that engages the counter drive wheel.
