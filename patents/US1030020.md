## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators find it hard to know which shift key to press for a given character.

**Solution:** Colour the characters on the keys and the shift keys to match so the right shift key is evident.

**How it works:** Lower-case characters are black, upper-case red, and certain auxiliary characters blue. One shift key bears a red designation (Fig.) and the other a blue one (Cap). The operator presses the shift key whose colour matches the character wanted, and neither for black characters. A modified form uses only black and red with a red-lettered shift key.

**Features:** [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key; shift key; lower case character; upper case character; auxiliary character
