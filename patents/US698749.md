## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar operating slides are hard to reach for repair, and key depression is not uniform because of different type-arm lengths.

**Solution:** Slides that are easily removable, a simplified slotted guide plate, and key-lever leverage varied to give uniform key depression.

**How it works:** Horizontal arms move endwise to swing transverse type arms into a common plane; the operating slides are removable, and the key-lever leverage on each slide is set so all keys depress equally.
