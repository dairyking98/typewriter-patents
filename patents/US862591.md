## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type actions and morepariticularly.

**How it works (reading):** Type bar hangers secured to a segment, with bars mounted on them, so that many type bars sit close together with widely separated pivots and fewer hangers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A large number of type bars must fit in a small space or on a short segment, with widely separated pivot bearings, and too many separate hangers are needed.

**Solution:** Type bar hangers secured to a segment, with bars mounted on them, so that many type bars sit close together with widely separated pivots and fewer hangers.
