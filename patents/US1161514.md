## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**How it works (reading):** A platen or matrix is moved into engagement with the printing characters by an operating lever, and cutting or tearing devices cut the fibre of the check at the printed amount so that erasure is practically impossible.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Amounts printed on checks can be erased or altered, and check protectors need many working parts.

**Solution:** A platen or matrix is moved into engagement with the printing characters by an operating lever, and cutting or tearing devices cut the fibre of the check at the printed amount so that erasure is practically impossible.
