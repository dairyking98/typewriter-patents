## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper-feeding devices orrevoluble platensof typewriting machines, and particularly to the means whereby the paper is fed or moved in line-spacing direction.

**How it works (reading):** Compact mounting of the platen stepping mechanism so the platen can be rotated in regular or irregular line steps by the ordinary line-spacing mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen line-spacing must be irregular and must repeat the same advance on printed forms, which the existing helical stop arrangement does not allow in compact form.

**Solution:** Compact mounting of the platen stepping mechanism so the platen can be rotated in regular or irregular line steps by the ordinary line-spacing mechanism.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
