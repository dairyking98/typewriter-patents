## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by applications of scales or indicators

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in a holder 3, which is removably secured to the machine in a manner presently describedand c0nstitutes,in effeet, a supplemental scale for facilitating accurate spacing in tabular work.

**How it works (reading):** A removable triangular metal holder carries three printed heading strips, one per face, and is clipped to the carriage scale; turning it brings the chosen heading into use, and holders can be swapped between jobs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typist preparing tabulated bills had to set up and change column positions by hand, with no quick way to switch between different heading layouts.

**Solution:** A removable triangular metal holder carries three printed heading strips, one per face, and is clipped to the carriage scale; turning it brings the chosen heading into use, and holders can be swapped between jobs.
