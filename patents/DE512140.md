## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers or sets of front or rear rollers each mounted on a separate carrier

**How it works (reading):** Make the shift lever an angle lever, one arm engaging a slot on a lever on the control shaft, the other reaching up over the keyboard to a grip plate pressed briefly downward.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On machines with a shiftable platen, the lever turning the shaft that presses and lifts the paper guide rollers needs too large a swing, and its handle is too high and far back, so rapid repeated use is slow.

**Solution:** Make the shift lever an angle lever, one arm engaging a slot on a lever on the control shaft, the other reaching up over the keyboard to a grip plate pressed briefly downward.

**How it works:** An angle lever pivots on the machine frame, so it does not move with the platen shift. Its one arm grips a pivot in a slotted lever on the control shaft, the slot allowing the platen's up and down motion. The other arm is bent forward and ends in a grip plate within easy reach from the keyboard. A short press on the plate turns the shaft to press or lift the paper guide rollers.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** angle lever; grip plate; control shaft; slotted lever; paper guide roller

**Improves on:**

- paper guide release levers with large swing and remote handle: short downward press on a plate by the keyboard
