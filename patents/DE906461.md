## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a friction disk clutch for vehicle winding devices of office machines. In car elevator devices of this type, the car is through the driven clutch brought into its starting position. while its gradual Forward movement takes place under the influence of spring forces, through which the loosened coupling half must be turned back. With the known car couplings on office machines has the disadvantage that the frictional forces between the rotated shaft of the coupling and the pressure plate with pressure spring mounted on it occur, a delay in the last switching steps of the carriage is caused, which adversely affects the working speed of the machine. The invention avoids this disadvantage in that with a coupling between two shafts the Clutch spring and the pressure plate of the clutch on a permanently attached hollow cylinder stump, which encloses the coupling shaft with a gap, and is called a single-shaft coupling with drive or output via the toothed housing, the clutch spring and the pressure plate are rotatably mounted on a pin which is permanently attached in extension of the shaft.
