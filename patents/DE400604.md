## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line counters

**How it works (reading):** A chain or toothed wheel on the paper roller shaft drives an endless flexible belt with numbered holes for the retaining pin, which runs over a spring-loaded sprocket that returns the belt to zero automatically when the roll wheel is loosened.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier side-mounted numbered wheel is hidden from the writer, bulky, and must be disengaged and returned to zero for each new page.

**Solution:** A chain or toothed wheel on the paper roller shaft drives an endless flexible belt with numbered holes for the retaining pin, which runs over a spring-loaded sprocket that returns the belt to zero automatically when the roll wheel is loosened.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** roller shaft wheel; endless numbered belt; spring-loaded sprocket; clamping screw; retaining pin

**Numbers:** gear about 28 teeth; belt wheel about 84 teeth

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Line counters for typewriters. Addition to Patent 386407. Patent 386 ¢ 07 describes a line counter for typewriters of the type in which the paper roller is inevitably connected to a step-wise moved part to be stopped by a stop after a certain path has been passed, which part according to the invention is a toothed part Number wheel was formed, which was provided with numbered holes for the insertable retaining pin and was in engagement with a gear mounted on the shaft of the paper roller. This device has the disadvantage that the numbering is on the side and is not visible to the writer, and that with each new page of writing, even if the number of lines remained the same, the number wheel after it has been disengaged from the gear wheel by removing a spring had to be brought into the zero position, whereupon the intervention was restored. In order to accommodate all the holes, the number wheel must also have a not inconsiderable size, which makes it bulky. The present additional invention has the purpose of applying the numbering in such a way that it always remains visible and the insertion of the retaining pin is possible without bending over,…
