## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage line stop needs a release, an adjustable stop, and a detachable form that can be fitted or removed as desired.

**Solution:** A line stop on the carriage is released by a mechanism, and detachable adjustable stops embrace a graduated margin rack on the machine, so the stop can be applied or removed.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
