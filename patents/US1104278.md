## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting the platen relative to the paper guide on a typewriter needed many parts, key-controlled levers and a preliminary unlocking movement.

**Solution:** Eccentrics on a sleeve around the platen axle shift the platen when a hand wheel turns them relative to the platen, with a pin-and-slot lock so shifting is only possible at the right platen position.

**How it works:** The platen end plates sit on eccentric disks fixed to a sleeve that turns on the platen shaft. A wheel on the sleeve rotates the eccentrics, shifting the platen transversely. A pin projecting from the platen end sits in a circular opening in a frame plate and can move out only opposite a slot extension, so shift is possible only at a set rotary position. A second form uses a hand wheel that slides endwise to clutch with the eccentric sleeve only when a pin aligns with a notch. Slotted disk and pins drive the platen from the shaft.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** platen shaft; hand wheel; line-spacing wheel; eccentric disk; sleeve; slotted drive disk; locking pin; frame plate; clutch

**Improves on:**
