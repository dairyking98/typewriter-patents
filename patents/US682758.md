## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement needs to support shift keys and give adjustable carriage movements.

**Solution:** A pawl-carrier and ratchet escapement driven by a rocking frame over the key-levers, with change gears for the carriage step.

**How it works:** The carriage is pulled by a spring barrel through a rack and gear train. A ratchet on the escape shaft is held by a spring pawl on an oscillating pawl-carrier stopped by an adjustable screw. A detent catches the ratchet when the pawl is lifted. A frame over all key-levers rocks a lever that moves the detent and pawl. Replaceable gears change the carriage travel per step. Shown on a Hammond with two shift keys.

**Features:** [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** spring barrel; rack bar; pinion; ratchet; pawl-carrier; spring pawl; detent; adjustable stop screw; key-lever frame; change gears

**Improves on:**

- Hammond escapement: detent and pawl carrier give adjustable stepping with swappable gears
