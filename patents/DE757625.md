## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to office machine bases where the anti-skid is arranged on the upper part, separated by an intermediate layer.

**How it works (reading):** The anti-skid pad is separated from the upper part by a non-adhesive intermediate layer and fixed only at the middle, so the outer part stays loose and the pad keeps its grip on the floor.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Anti-skid pads bonded to the upper part of a machine base move with the machine's own thrust, so the feet slide when the carriage is moved to the stop.

**Solution:** The anti-skid pad is separated from the upper part by a non-adhesive intermediate layer and fixed only at the middle, so the outer part stays loose and the pad keeps its grip on the floor.
