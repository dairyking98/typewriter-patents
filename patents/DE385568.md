## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Finding and recognizing the keys of a typewriter or calculator is difficult for blind operators.

**Solution:** A box attached over the keyboard holds sleeves on the keys with auxiliary key stems of different lengths, so the keys sit in two parallel levels with a raised middle band as a finding guide.
