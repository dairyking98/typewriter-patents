## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier Smith tabulator gave column selection but denominational selection within the chosen columns was not available at will.

**Solution:** Add denominational selection in selected columns, with means to switch it off so the column selector works indefinitely without interference.

**How it works:** Building on application 730,350, a column-to-column tabulator key is tied to the carriage releasing means. Denominational selecting mechanism can be made effective or ineffective at will, so in a single line one portion may tabulate column to column and another portion may select denominations. Only the opening text was read in detail, the rest is on the same tabulator theme.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** column-to-column tabulator key; carriage release; denominational selector; column stop

**Improves on:**

- [US1147821](../patents/US1147821.md): adds denominational selection within selected columns, switchable off
