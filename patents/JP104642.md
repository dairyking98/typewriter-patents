## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A single feed wheel needs to give accurate character feeds of several different spacings, without the feed pawl catching the wheel on the forward stroke and without feeding on an incomplete or trial print stroke.

**Solution:** A flip-over T-shaped control piece carrying the feed pawl and a trip roller, held by a toggle spring lever, drops the pawl into the wheel only at the end of a full print stroke and is flipped out again by a preset adjustable limiter, which also re-engages a brake pawl.

**How it works:** A feed wheel sits on the platen shaft with a rocking plate hung on the same shaft, driven by the print action through a link and lever. A T-shaped control piece pivoted on the plate carries the feed pawl at one end and a trip roller at the other, and is held in either of two angular positions by a link to a spring lever. On the forward stroke the pawl stays clear of the wheel, so a half or trial stroke causes no feed. At the end of a full stroke the control piece strikes a limit stop and flips, dropping the pawl into the wheel and pressing down the brake lever so its brake pawl is released. On the return stroke the pawl turns the wheel until the trip roller meets the limiter set on a scale, which flips the control piece back, lifts the pawl and lets the brake pawl lock the wheel. The wheel teeth may be ratchet or cycloidal, and the platen can still be turned freely backward for loading paper.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Feed device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a feed device that gives several character spacings with a single toothed wheel without mistaken action. To keep the feed pawl, which is to engage the wheel, always clear of the wheel during its forward movement, a T-shaped control piece that can flip over is fitted on a part of the mechanism linked to the print key, and carries the feed pawl and a trip roller. A forward-limit device is provided so that the pawl drops into the feed wheel, like a pestle (杵搗狀), only at the limit of the printing action, that is, when the forward movement reaches its end; at the same moment the brake pawl is released. The feed begins with the return of the print key, and when the feed wheel has turned the set amount, the trip roller meets a limiter set in advance as the return-limit device, which flips the pawl back, frees it from the wheel and at the same moment engages the brake pawl in the wheel; the set feed is so done.
