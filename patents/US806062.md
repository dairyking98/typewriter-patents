## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Position or arrangement of bristles in relation to surface of the brush body, e.g. inclined, in rows, in groups

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in cleaners for type-writing machines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type clog and must be cleaned frequently, and whole-machine brushing does not clean individual type well.

**Solution:** A hand tool with two yielding arms: one holds the type, the other carries a reciprocating brush whose pressure is adjusted by the grip, so one hand can clean a single type.
