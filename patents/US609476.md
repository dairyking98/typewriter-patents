## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink ribbons need automatic lengthwise reversal without adjusting for ribbon length, uniform widthwise wear, and spools that can be turned over or swapped.

**Solution:** Detachable, transposable, endwise-reversible ribbon spools whose winding automatically switches at the end of the ribbon, plus a widthwise ribbon motion independent of the winding.

**How it works:** The ribbon and its two spools can be removed bodily, swapped or flipped over to expose the other face of the ribbon, and still wind and reverse correctly. When the ribbon unwinds fully from one spool a switching mechanism acts (shown in sequence in Figs. 2 to 5) and winding begins on the empty spool. A separate arrangement moves the ribbon widthwise for even wear. The patent also covers carriage propulsion and an adjustable carriage alarm that warns near line end.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon spool; switching mechanism; widthwise feed; carriage alarm
