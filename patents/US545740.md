## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Trunnion pivots on typebars get bent or gouged when the hanger arms are sprung together, wear unevenly on the lower side, and let the bar play sideways, spoiling alignment.

**Solution:** Pivot each typebar on loose balls seated in opposed sockets in the bar and in a two-part hanger whose arms can be screwed together to take up wear.

**How it works:** The hanger is a cap with one fixed arm and a second arm sliding in a groove, held by a set screw. Each arm has a concave socket near its free end, and the typebar has two bosses with matching sockets. A loose ball sits in each socket pair and acts as the pivot. An adjusting screw bearing on the sliding arm tightens or relieves the balls so wear is taken up without relying on the spring of the arms. The balls roll and change position in use so they wear evenly.
