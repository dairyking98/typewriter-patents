## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-wheel machines operated by key-driven oscillation use many mechanical parts and give impact that varies with the touch.

**Solution:** Each key oscillates a type wheel or sector to its letter through a driving rod and geared sector, with a battery-powered electric circuit giving a uniform impression.

**How it works:** Keys in two banks carry upper-case and numeral strips, and a shifting frame under the keys raises or lowers the type-wheel shaft to one of three positions against a coil spring, so three series of letters on the wheel face are used. Key depression swings a driving rod whose geared sector meshes with a pinion on a collar below the wheel hub. The collar has stop arms with serrations, one notch per key, that lock the wheel at the chosen angle. Light bars over the keys close insulated contact points to send battery current to the impression devices, so the stroke is uniform regardless of touch.

- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md)
