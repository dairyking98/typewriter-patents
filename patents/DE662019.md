## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Power-driven type-lever drive lacks gradual coupling and jam protection in the linkage.

**Solution:** A star wheel is coupled to the toothed drive roller only after a locking member releases it, and a keyboard voter element swings it out of drive if the linkage jams.
