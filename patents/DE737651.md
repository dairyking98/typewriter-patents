## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles characterised by being applied to printers having transversely- moving carriages and detached from the carriage

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded articulated parallelogram guides the web so it stays taut and in line with the platen as the carriage moves.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Roll-paper guides on movable carriages either slow carriage return or need the paper roll to move with the carriage.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded articulated parallelogram guides the web so it stays taut and in line with the platen as the carriage moves.
