## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving parts impacted and concussed during machine operation, making noise.

**Solution:** Mechanism arrests the movement of operative parts silently, with the universal rock shaft and links supporting the universal bar under key levers, reducing impact and concussion.
