## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**How it works (reading):** Each polygonal printing wheel is driven by an endless chain over sprockets of different size inside the lever, which also locks it in position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Polygonal printing wheels driven by racks are heavy and rack drives bend.

**Solution:** Each polygonal printing wheel is driven by an endless chain over sprockets of different size inside the lever, which also locks it in position.
