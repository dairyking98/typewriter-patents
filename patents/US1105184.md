## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting worksheets was obstructed by the ribbon vibrator and centre guide in front of the platen.

**Solution:** A paper-guiding chute at the front of the platen lets the worksheet be introduced and then run around the platen clear of the ribbon guide.

**How it works:** A chute beside the platen guides the sheet between it and the platen, so the sheet goes around the platen and into feed rolls.
