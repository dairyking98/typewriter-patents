## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The shift rack's switching shock and noise on the carriage are propagated through rigid mounting; a leaf spring mount makes the carriage pendulum and blur the print.

**Solution:** Mount the rack ends in bushings with pressed-in rubber or other non-metallic elastic material, elastic in all directions.

**How it works:** The rack ends sit on pins held in riveted bushes in the carriage side walls. Rubber or similar is pressed (and vulcanised) around the pin inside grooved bore of the bush, secured against turning by flats. The number and width of grooves tune the stiffness. The pins allow adjusting tooth mesh with the drive wheel. The elastic mount absorbs the loose escapement tooth impact without pendulum motion.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** rack; bush; pin; rubber; side wall; loose tooth; drive wheel

**Improves on:**

- Leaf-spring-supported shift rack: elastic in all directions, no pendulum
