## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**How it works (reading):** The loosely rotating column stops sit on a shaft to which a bent flanged rail is firmly fixed; turning a reset lever rotates the rail flange and returns all set stops to rest.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Resetting set column stops to rest needs slotted rings, spacers and complex linkage to clear the shaft.

**Solution:** The loosely rotating column stops sit on a shaft to which a bent flanged rail is firmly fixed; turning a reset lever rotates the rail flange and returns all set stops to rest.
