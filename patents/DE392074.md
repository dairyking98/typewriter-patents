## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on typewriters and particularly relates to a new perfected device for the transverse oscillation of the ribbon, which is adjustable for different stroke sizes, to write in different colors.

**How it works (reading):** A two-branch oscillating lever with a stepped wire lets the ribbon-lift link engage either the near or far branch, selecting the ribbon zone to strike.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing ribbon zones for different stroke sizes and colors needs a complicated lever train.

**Solution:** A two-branch oscillating lever with a stepped wire lets the ribbon-lift link engage either the near or far branch, selecting the ribbon zone to strike.
