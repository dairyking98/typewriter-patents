## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists of the features of construction, arrangements of parts and combinations of devices to be described in the following specification and set forth in the accompanying claims.

**How it works (reading):** Denominational stops and their actuating levers, spacing blocks and a justifying device on the tabulating mechanism place the point for the numeral set.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Numerals need different decimal pointing or punctuation depending on the work, which the tabulator does not give.

**Solution:** Denominational stops and their actuating levers, spacing blocks and a justifying device on the tabulating mechanism place the point for the numeral set.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
