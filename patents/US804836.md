## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a spring-driven carriage escapement the shifting member should snap back rapidly and letter spacing should start as soon as the key is released.

**Solution:** A two-wheel escapement with a fixed wheel and a loose wheel sprung one letter-space from it, so the dog releasing the loose wheel lets the spring step it forward at once.

**How it works:** A pinion on a short shaft meshes with the carriage rack; a fixed escapement wheel is pinned to the shaft and a loose wheel sits beside it linked by a coiled spring. A pin through the fixed wheel in a slot of the loose wheel limits relative throw to one letter space and is adjustable by an eccentric end and lock nut. A universal-bar-driven dog rocker moves the dog onto the fixed wheel while a key is down, holding the carriage during the strike. On release the dog leaves the loose wheel, which the spring advances one space, and the dog catches the next tooth, letting the carriage move one letter space.

**Numbers:** relative throw of loose wheel limited to one letter-space
