## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a device on typewriters with which Characters and words as well as entire sentences are underlined at the same time when writing, set up so that two type levers hit the paper roller at the same time and align themselves in a three-part guide fork.

**How it works (reading):** Two type levers carry single and double underscore characters with the same design as letter levers; a coupling button makes two adjacent levers strike together, and pressing the space key prints the line alone, so continuous lines can be printed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Underlining with a separate underline plate allows only one line form, breaks at word gaps, and needs a letter-bearing type lever.

**Solution:** Two type levers carry single and double underscore characters with the same design as letter levers; a coupling button makes two adjacent levers strike together, and pressing the space key prints the line alone, so continuous lines can be printed.
