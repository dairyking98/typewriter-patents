## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stamp device with ink ribbon) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inking devices or other accessories therefor with a flat stamping surface and changeable characters having adjustable type-carrying wheels; Stamping surfaces for multicolour stamping; Stamping surfaces with ink ribbons, ink sheets, or carbon tape or paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a stamping device with an ink ribbon Stamp device with ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** The multicolour ribbon runs on each side of the device through pivotable guide rails inclined to the vertical, so either half can be brought under the stamp by moving the rails.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Swinging the whole multicolour ribbon loop under the stamp to bring the other colour half down is difficult.

**Solution:** The multicolour ribbon runs on each side of the device through pivotable guide rails inclined to the vertical, so either half can be brought under the stamp by moving the rails.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** type roller; ribbon rollers; ratchet wheel; claw; guide rollers; pivotable guide rails
