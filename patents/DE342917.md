## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**How it works (reading):** The typewriter table slides horizontally out of the cabinet first and then rises along an inclined guide to the writing height.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Desks with a sunk typewriter are awkward to reach because the machine must be deeply lowered.

**Solution:** The typewriter table slides horizontally out of the cabinet first and then rises along an inclined guide to the writing height.

**Features:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)
