## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**How it works (reading):** An annular disk with an index pointer is mounted concentric with the platen shaft; an adjustable pointer on the platen turning knob shows the rotation needed for the set blank space between entries.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator has no indication of how far to turn the platen backward by hand when inserting new sheets for carbon-copy writing with set spacing.

**Solution:** An annular disk with an index pointer is mounted concentric with the platen shaft; an adjustable pointer on the platen turning knob shows the rotation needed for the set blank space between entries.
