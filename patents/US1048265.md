## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Toy typewriters

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the use of an intermittently rotating wheel having teeth for operating with the teeth of the carriage rack bar and in the details of construction by which this escapement wheel is operated.

**How it works (reading):** An intermittently rotating escapement wheel with teeth replaces the oscillating dog and engages the carriage rack bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The oscillating two-tooth escapement dog wears and is inaccurate.

**Solution:** An intermittently rotating escapement wheel with teeth replaces the oscillating dog and engages the carriage rack bar.
