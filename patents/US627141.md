## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in a novel construction of the escapement by which the springimpelled carriage is permitted to advance as each letter is printed and in the spaces between the words;
