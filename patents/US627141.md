## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Escapement of the spring-driven Remington-type carriage needs a different feed-dog arrangement.

**Solution:** Two feed dogs moving in the plane of the rack: a retaining dog that engages and disengages and a let-off dog that also slides along the rack by one tooth.

**How it works:** The carriage rack has inclined ratchet teeth spaced one letter apart and is pulled by the mainspring. A rocking lever driven by the universal bar raises the retaining dog into the rack when a key is pressed. The let-off dog, guided by a slot on a fixed pin, rises and pivots so its top moves along the rack and releases one tooth; on key release the dogs swap so the carriage advances one tooth. The space key works the same way.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** feed rack; retaining dog; let-off dog; rocking lever; universal bar; mainspring; guide slot; pin
