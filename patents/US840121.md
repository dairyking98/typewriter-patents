## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A moving carriage must carry the writing mechanism while the typebars are actuated from a common power source.

**Solution:** An electromagnet pulls a power-bar that drives all key-bars, while key-levers operate the selecting means, so the typebars are driven electrically.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md), [Power-driven type action](../features/bar-power-action.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** electromagnet; armature; power-bar; key-levers; links; type-bars; retractile springs
