## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accounting forms must be fed quickly and discarded, which a fixed platen and stiff form holding does not allow.

**Solution:** The platen frame pivots on the machine frame and a stop mechanism steps the frame along the roller; spring fingers with pressure rollers hold the forms and swing out for quick exchange, with a collector for spoiled forms.
