## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A friction platen release slips when the line-space lever is struck suddenly, and the paper lags or overshoots, so line spacing drifts.

**Solution:** Intermediate gears with a resilient lost-motion connection between the line-space wheel and the two clutch members, acting as a shock absorber so the platen and paper start and stop smoothly.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
