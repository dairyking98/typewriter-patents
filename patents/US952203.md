## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Label printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to, the paper-feedewgitin'g machines, and its object is to facilitate the roduction oi carbon or duplicate copies ot an original typewritten sheet, and particularly to provide means for making a carbon record, tally or duplicate of the prices or amounts entered upona succession of bills or invoices.

**How it works (reading):** An endless inking band and web run around the platen with the bill, duplicating the typed amounts onto one tally strip.
