## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention consists in a simple paper cart for typewriters, the one, as is customary, a slide or slide pulled by a driving spring and comprises a roller frame carried on rollers, which are connected in such a way that the the latter can be folded upwards around the rear running rail as a hinge, whereby for Double-type typewriters this rail also serves as a changeover rail.

**How it works (reading):** Roller frame on a running rail folds upward around its rear rail, which also serves as the shift changeover rail; a ratchet and pawl on the platen steps it by one or two teeth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper trolley for typewriters is complicated and does not double as a changeover rail for double-type machines.

**Solution:** Roller frame on a running rail folds upward around its rear rail, which also serves as the shift changeover rail; a ratchet and pawl on the platen steps it by one or two teeth.
