## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hard platens let the innermost sheet slip during line spacing, so line spacing is uneven; more roll pressure smears carbons.

**Solution:** Add means near the platen ends, outside the print field, that grip the paper more strongly so all sheets move with the platen.

**How it works:** Figures show a platen with end portions (including variants) providing better adherence to paper beyond the printing field. Details of the end gripping parts are not in the excerpt.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)

**Parts:** platen; paper carriage; axle; end grip portion; pressure roll

**Improves on:**

- increasing pressure-roll pressure: avoids retarding outer sheets and carbon smudging
