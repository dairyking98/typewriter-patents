## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of our invention is to furnish a copyholder for type 'writing machines by which the copy is brought immediately behind the key-board, so that the operator can readily see both copy and key-board without moving the head.

**How it works (reading):** A metal plate with a rest and downward-bent upper flange is hung by lateral projections on the posts behind the keyboard; a line-marker slides on a rod on the plate.
