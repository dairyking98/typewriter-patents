## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A type cylinder that is rotated, raised and tilted to select a character builds up momentum, and the farthest characters overshoot or print out of line.

**Solution:** The cylinder is rotated by a pinion that runs against a rack of independently movable teeth chosen by the key, so the rotation is positively controlled and locked throughout.

**How it works:** Each key lever works a cam on a rock-shaft that throws one tooth, and through lateral pins every following tooth, of a rack bar into the path of a pinion on the cylinder shaft. The key also oscillates the cylinder toward the platen, and the pinion sweeps along the rack. It turns the cylinder up to a half revolution either way, by one tooth per column, and stops it with the pinion meshed with the rack, so momentum is absorbed. A spring-loaded returning frame hugging a lug on the shaft brings the cylinder back to its normal position after every impression. Upper and lower locking pinions with brackets lock the cylinder at every stage. A bail under the keys lifts the cylinder shaft along a track to choose the line within a field, and two separate keys lift the carriage to choose the field. Spring-loaded pads ink the type as the cylinder passes. A spacing bail works the check and feed pawls on the carriage rack.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [Interchangeable element](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** type cylinder; cylinder shaft; pinion; locking pinion; rack bar; movable rack tooth; cam; rock-shaft; key lever; bail; lifter lever; returning frame; lug; locking bracket; trunnion; return spring; ink pad; paper carriage track; check pawl; feed pawl; carriage lift lever

**Numbers:** 78 characters; 13 characters per row; 3 fields of 26 characters; 6 rack teeth; 13-tooth pinion

**Improves on:**

- earlier cylinder and wheel typewriters: a toothed rack replaces free rotation, so the cylinder is locked throughout and momentum is neutralised
