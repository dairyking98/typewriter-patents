## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (centrifugal friction clutch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Automatic clutches actuated entirely mechanically controlled by angular speed with centrifugal masses actuating axially a movable pressure ring or the like the pressure ring actuating friction plates, cones or similar axially-movable friction surfaces the centrifugal masses acting directly on the pressure ring, no other actuating mechanism for the pressure ring being provided

**How it works (reading):** A loose intermediate disk on the drive shaft is set in rotation by the balls, so the engagement is gradual.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Centrifugal friction clutches engage abruptly and can stall when the driven load is large.

**Solution:** A loose intermediate disk on the drive shaft is set in rotation by the balls, so the engagement is gradual.
