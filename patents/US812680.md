## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a back-spacing attachment to typewriting machines, and particularly to one having special utility in connection with the Remington and similar types of machines.

**How it works (reading):** The smooth disk on the pinion shaft is given ratchet teeth, and a single pawl-carrying lever connected to a key engages them so the carriage moves back space by space.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The Remington carriage can only move left to right by the escapement, so it has no backspacing action.

**Solution:** The smooth disk on the pinion shaft is given ratchet teeth, and a single pawl-carrying lever connected to a key engages them so the carriage moves back space by space.

**Features:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** pinion shaft; disk 3 with ratchet teeth; pawl-carrying lever; escapement wheel; key
