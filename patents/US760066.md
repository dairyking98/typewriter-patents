## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention being to provide simple and efficient means for regulating and controlling the feed of thepaper 1 through the carriage of a type Writing machine such as employed with a machine of the key-wheel class and to so construct and arrange the feed devices that an extensive range of adjustment will be afliorded and so that the paper will be firmly held while being written upon.

**How it works (reading):** A paper-feed roller in elongated bearings is pressed by springs against a rubber-covered feed roller, and a paper holder is carried on a rod at the carriage.
