## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Need for a simple typewriter that can be worked by hand or foot and make heavy impressions, as for engraving.

**Solution:** A type-holder in a swinging type-carriage is slid sideways under a toothed register-plate, which locates each character on a centring pin or wheel, and the carriage is pressed down on the paper.

**How it works:** A rock-shaft with arms and links carries a rod on which the type-carriage is hung, free to slide side to side and swing up or down; set screws in slotted brackets adjust its height. The carriage frame holds a rotatable type-holder and, above it, a slotted or toothed register-plate lettered over each slot to match the type below. A fixed centring pin or cogged wheel on a depending arm enters the slot of the chosen letter, bringing it to exact printing centre. Pressing the carriage down (by hand or foot) prints on paper in a separate paper-holding carriage over an impression bed, with an ink-pad holder inking the type.
