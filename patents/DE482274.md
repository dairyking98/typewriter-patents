**How it works (reading):** The pressure roller bracket and paper table are joined on a common shaft behind the paper roller, so folding one folds the other, and the long lever arm eases lifting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A foldable paper table is hard to lift off, and the pressure roller bracket needs reinforcing.

**Solution:** The pressure roller bracket and paper table are joined on a common shaft behind the paper roller, so folding one folds the other, and the long lever arm eases lifting.
