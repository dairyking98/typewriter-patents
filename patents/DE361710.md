**How it works (reading):** A sickle-shaped rotating knife on a shaft, linked to the feed rollers, moves back at the rate of the strip and cuts across it while feed continues, guided by a slotted plate that holds the strip and the knife.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks cut from a continuous strip need the strip to stop during cutting, which requires special feed switching.

**Solution:** A sickle-shaped rotating knife on a shaft, linked to the feed rollers, moves back at the rate of the strip and cuts across it while feed continues, guided by a slotted plate that holds the strip and the knife.
