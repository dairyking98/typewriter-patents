## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter arithmetic unit)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a typewriter, whose arithmetic unit is set in action by the fact that a of the gradually moving carriage axially displaceable drive wheel comes into engagement with the rake wheels one after the other.

**How it works (reading):** The feed mechanism moves the main drive wheel axially so it leaves the current calculating wheel and engages the next only shortly before the end of the carriage travel, so that the wheel can complete its rotation before disengaging.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a typewriter with an arithmetic unit, the drive wheel moves from one calculating wheel to the next during carriage motion, and errors occur at high speed.

**Solution:** The feed mechanism moves the main drive wheel axially so it leaves the current calculating wheel and engages the next only shortly before the end of the carriage travel, so that the wheel can complete its rotation before disengaging.
