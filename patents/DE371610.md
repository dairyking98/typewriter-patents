## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Folded paper webs make register between layers easy, but the machine cannot use separate webs or sheets of a different paper quality or colour.

**Solution:** The carriage carries a sheet cart, and the paper webs rest on a firm support under their own weight, so separate webs or sheets can be written on while the carriage is relieved of pulling the paper.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen carriage; sheet cart; firm paper support; folded paper web
