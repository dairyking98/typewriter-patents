## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (duplicating copies of typed matter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to means for duplicating copies of typewritten matter and its object generally stated is to provide improved devices of the class specified.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicate copies of typed matter need a carbon supply that works with folded or long work sheets, which the earlier constructions did not handle well.

**Solution:** A carbon-roll carrier and a gravity feeding device or roller on a Monarch typewriter platen carriage feed folded sheets singly and carry carbon in roll form.

**How it works:** Carbon is wound as a roll on a carrier so the sheet passes over it; a gravity-loaded roller presses and feeds the separately fed work sheet to the platen.

**Features:** [INK:carbon](../features/ink-carbon.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
