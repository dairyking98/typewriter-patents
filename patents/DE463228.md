**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanism driven by cams engaging rotating roller

**How it works (reading):** The eccentric and its locking pawl are carried by a replaceable yoke, and the yoke's backward movement is limited by an adjustable stop acting on several push rods.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Eccentrics fixed to the push rods had to be replaced with the whole push rod when damaged, which was impractical since the rods differ.

**Solution:** The eccentric and its locking pawl are carried by a replaceable yoke, and the yoke's backward movement is limited by an adjustable stop acting on several push rods.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
