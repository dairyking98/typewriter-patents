## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator carriage jump at decimal point) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on typewriters, which automatically jump when you press the numeric keys at the decimal point of the trolley with the assistance of a control element arranged on the counter triggers, and consists in the fact that when the numeric keypad is actuated, the control body on the device causing the carriage to jump remains ineffective.

**How it works (reading):** A control member on the vertical counter blocks the number keys at the decimal point, and a coupling makes the carriage skip the point in one double step.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators must lock the number keys at the decimal point, or press the space bar, to advance past it, which costs time and errors.

**Solution:** A control member on the vertical counter blocks the number keys at the decimal point, and a coupling makes the carriage skip the point in one double step.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)
