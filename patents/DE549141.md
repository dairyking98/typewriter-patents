**How it works (reading):** A free disc on the shaft is pressed against a counter-disc coupled to the shaft by a long-lever brake member, with an elastic or multi-plate friction surface so braking is shock-free.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Band brakes on the line-indexing shaft of book typewriters give shock when stopping large masses.

**Solution:** A free disc on the shaft is pressed against a counter-disc coupled to the shaft by a long-lever brake member, with an elastic or multi-plate friction surface so braking is shock-free.
