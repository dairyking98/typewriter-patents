## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards must be held upright and fed vertically over the platen, and the card-holding rollers must be moved out of the way.

**Solution:** Card-holding rollers press the card against the platen and are shifted to an inoperative position by a lever at the carriage end.
