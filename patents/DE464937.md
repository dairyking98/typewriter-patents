## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter for writing on bundles of paper from opposite to the direction of movement of the paper cart fixed supply rolls or boxes with the interposition of a Hanging loop fed to the paper trolley.

**How it works (reading):** A carbon sheet holder slides back so the loop stays taut, with tongues gripping the carbon sheets between work sheets on a swinging table behind the platen, so the platen need not be raised.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multi-sheet carbon sets need the platen raised so the paper loop has slack for carriage travel, which the inventor wants to avoid.

**Solution:** A carbon sheet holder slides back so the loop stays taut, with tongues gripping the carbon sheets between work sheets on a swinging table behind the platen, so the platen need not be raised.
