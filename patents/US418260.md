## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters returned the carriage and fed the paper by hand at line end.

**Solution:** An attachment (for a Remington) that automatically returns the carriage and turns the platen at line end using a spring, gear train and clutch lever.

**How it works:** A standard clamps to the machine ledge carrying a spring-driven shaft with a large cog meshing a smaller one (about ten to one). A disk with stops, a loose collar with arms and levers with a detent roller connect to the carriage so that at line end the detent releases and the spring returns the carriage and turns the platen roller one line.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** standard; coil spring; cog wheel; disk; collar; curved lever; detent; roller

**Numbers:** gear ratio about 10:1
