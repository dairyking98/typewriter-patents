## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to syllable typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Keys for initial consonants, vowels and final consonants are grouped on the keyboard, and pressing several keys together selects a syllable of type from one series for printing on a paper strip that advances one line per syllable.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typing a text with one key per letter needs many keystrokes; syllables should be printed from a few keys pressed together, with key order not affecting the result.

**Solution:** Keys for initial consonants, vowels and final consonants are grouped on the keyboard, and pressing several keys together selects a syllable of type from one series for printing on a paper strip that advances one line per syllable.
