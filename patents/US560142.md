## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Improves the earlier Maskelyne typewriter (US457903) by simplifying it and adding variable letter spacing, free carriage movement without the keys, a column stop, and continuous underlining.

**Solution:** A pad-inked typebar machine with three characters per typebar, in which a lower carriage slides sideways to present the paper to the chosen character and an upper carriage steps the paper roller along under a spring and escapement.

**How it works:** Thirty-two keys (rows of 11, 11, 10) pull multiplying levers that tip each thin-steel type-lever over so the type swings out from under a semicircular ink pad and strikes down onto the paper, which stays in sight. Each type-lever carries three characters; a rocking T-piece with shift keys moves the lower carriage left or right so the paper meets the capital, small or figure character, with a spring returning it to the set position. The upper carriage, holding the impression cylinder, is pulled along by a chain and spring drum and released one step per key by a pallet escapement. Extra spacing levers limit how far the escape sector advances, giving one, two or three teeth against the normal four. A handle disengages the escape wheel pawl for free carriage movement, a spring plunger meeting pins on the carriage stops it at column positions, and a rotating inked disc on a slide along a bar underlines continuously.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** type-lever; multiplying lever; elbow lever; semicircular frame; ink pad; key lever; lower carriage; upper carriage; impression cylinder; rocking T-piece; chain; motor drum; escape wheel; escape lever; sector frame; spacing lever; column stop plunger; underlining disc; inking roller; gong

**Numbers:** 32 keys (11+11+10); three characters per typebar; normal spacing 4 escape teeth, adjustable to 1, 2 or 3; line spacing of 1, 2 or 3 lines

**Improves on:**

- [US457903](../patents/US457903.md): simpler lever construction in thin steel, variable spacing, free carriage movement, column stop and continuous underlining added
