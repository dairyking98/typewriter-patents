## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an aliner for use with a type-writer of the class shown in my Patent No.

**How it works (reading):** Aliner arms on threaded shanks run through slotted standards on the curved type-bar bar; nuts lock the spacing, and turning the arms and moving the whole aliner on its slotted ends sets its distance from the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An aliner needs adjustment of the space between its two members and adjustment toward and from the printing point or platen.

**Solution:** Aliner arms on threaded shanks run through slotted standards on the curved type-bar bar; nuts lock the spacing, and turning the arms and moving the whole aliner on its slotted ends sets its distance from the platen.
