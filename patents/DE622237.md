## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for attaching machines to baseboards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to push-button connections of writing, Calculating machines and the like with the floor board, in which ring-shaped machine feet Engage over pegs attached to the bottom board with protruding heads.

**How it works (reading):** Two-part spread-spring pin heads on the floor-board pegs hold the machine feet without relying on rubber elasticity.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber feet on machine feet loosen and deform the floor board after repeated removal.

**Solution:** Two-part spread-spring pin heads on the floor-board pegs hold the machine feet without relying on rubber elasticity.
