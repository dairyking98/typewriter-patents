## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improvement in type-writing machines whereby the paperfeeding means may be automatically stopped at a certain predetermined time, so as to fix tlie bottom margins of the sheets to suit any need that may arise in operating-the machine.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable platen-stop attachment, fitted to the platen and carriage of a Densmore, Smith Premier or Remington machine, engages and halts platen rotation at a preset point.
