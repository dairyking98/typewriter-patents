**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for controlling character selection

**How it works (reading):** A few-key keyboard, operated by one hand, drives a type-writing machine whose character selection and printing are electric, so one hand can write quickly without looking at the keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing needs to be done rapidly with one hand, without looking at the keys, on a machine with only a few keys.

**Solution:** A few-key keyboard, operated by one hand, drives a type-writing machine whose character selection and printing are electric, so one hand can write quickly without looking at the keys.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
