## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letter spacing in a typebar machine with a space key needs a ratchet that works for both the letter and space actions.

**Solution:** Two joined ratchets on a pivot, one able to swing independently at a right angle, work in one set of teeth with a pivoted cross-bar linking keys to the vibratory frame.

**How it works:** A toothed circular plate on the main axle has a wide untoothed space. A ratchet pivoted to vibrate parallel to the axle engages its notches; a second ratchet attached to it points the same way but is pivoted on a separate axis at right angles, weighted and stopped against the first. A pivoted bar across the key levers lifts the arm of the ratchet so any key advances the carriage one space.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** ratchet; second ratchet; toothed plate; main axle; cross-bar; key lever; vibratory frame; platen; spring; weight; stop
