## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In ordinary (let-off on upstroke) escapements the dogs have lost travel that must be taken up on the key's return before the carriage is released, making the action irregular and slow.

**Solution:** Automatically disconnect part of the linkage between key and escapement member at an adjustable point in the key stroke so the carriage is let off early on the upstroke regardless of key depth.

**How it works:** In a Remington No. 10 style front-strike machine, the key-controlled escapement member is driven through intermediate actuating devices. A device momentarily disconnects those parts at a point in the key movement that can be adjusted. This removes the lost motion of the dogs so let-off occurs early on the upstroke whether the key is fully or partly depressed. Details of the linkage are not in the opening text.
