## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons for manifolding on a folded continuous sheet need to go into opposite folds and multicolour ribbons need shifting.

**Solution:** A ribbon frame carried on the platen spindle holds two ribbons that feed into the folds of a manifold strip, with a shiftable counter shaft for driving the spools and a tilt to bring the colour stripe to the print point.

**How it works:** The frame swings on the platen spindle, is removed by removing the platen, and has two spool frames joined by a bar. Spool gears are meshed in turn by two gears on a longitudinally shifting counter shaft, so only one spool drives at a time. A slight rocking of the frame moves the ribbon sideways to present the right colour stripe. Ribbon guides extend into the opposite folds of the sheet.
