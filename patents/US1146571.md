## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check protector) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a check protector, or protectograph, of simple, durable and inexpensive construction.

**How it works (reading):** A simple, durable check protector positions the check and prints the figures, marking the check with curved lines between the dollar sign and the figures.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks need protection from alteration, so the figures must be printed so that curved lines run between dollars and cents.

**Solution:** A simple, durable check protector positions the check and prints the figures, marking the check with curved lines between the dollar sign and the figures.
