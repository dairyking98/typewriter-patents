## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon mechanism for a visible-writing Monarch typebar machine using two ribbons, each needing its own spools and vibrating.

**Solution:** Two ribbons, one facing the other, with two sets of spools and vibrators that lift them alternately, driven by gearing from a shaft that can be shifted axially to change which spool set is driven.

**How it works:** A shaft parallel to the platen is geared to the carriage spring drum and carries bevel pinions and worms that drive two cross shafts to the ribbon spools. Sliding the shaft axially engages one pinion and disengages the other, so one ribbon set is driven. A spring piston with a roller in notched head holds the shaft in either position. Two vibrators lift the two ribbons alternately to the print point at the front of the platen, pivoted devices at each side of the machine control them.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon spool; ribbon cup; ribbon vibrator; drive shaft; bevel gear; worm; spring drum; spring piston; detent roller; typebar

**Numbers:** two ribbons; two sets of spools
