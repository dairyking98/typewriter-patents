## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**How it works (reading):** Keys advance sleds with stepped inclined surfaces that lift the paper plate to the pitch height before a note pen is pressed.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In music typewriters the note must sit at the correct height against the five staff lines.

**Solution:** Keys advance sleds with stepped inclined surfaces that lift the paper plate to the pitch height before a note pen is pressed.

**How it works:** Music paper lies on a plate with the staff lines parallel to it, carried on a roller on sleds. Each sled has an inclined surface half a staff-line space higher than its neighbour, and a key pulls its sled forward through a pull rod and lever to lift the plate by that amount. The print head is a square rod shifted sideways with a hand-inked note pen pressed on the paper. Slotted rails moved obliquely raise or lower all sleds for the key signature, set by a button locating in holes 1 to 7.
