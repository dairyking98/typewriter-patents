## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A type bar on a type bar segment is driven to the printing position, and the carriage is moved step by step by a feed rack controlled by feed dogs on a dog rocker.
