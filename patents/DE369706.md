## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter is awkward to carry, and moving the frame from collapsed to working position takes time.

**Solution:** The machine has a fixed frame and a movable frame carrying the keyboard and the carriage, joined by coil springs that raise the movable frame automatically into working position; wing screws lock it down for packing.
