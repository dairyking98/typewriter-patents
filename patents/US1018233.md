**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to apparatus for producing impressions of type 1n metal plates or sheets, which can subsequently be used for printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stamped metal plates for printing need characters placed in straight lines, with the stamping driven by a perforated record sheet instead of manual setting.

**Solution:** Stamps on separate levers are moved into position by shafts that a perforated record sheet controls, and each lever is pressed by a stamping block to impress its character.

**How it works:** Pins on the perforated sheet drop through holes and rotate a shaft; the shaft moves one type lever into operating position, the stamping block presses it, and the lever returns to rest.
