## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates in part to an improved anchor-escapement by which the feed of the carriage is controlled.

**How it works (reading):** Clamps hold the feed and escapement housing in position; an automatic dog locks the spring barrel when the unit is removed, and the clamps release the lock when it is installed, setting the feed gear into mesh with the carriage rack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage feed and escapement must be removable from the frame, and the feed spring must not unwind when they are taken out.

**Solution:** Clamps hold the feed and escapement housing in position; an automatic dog locks the spring barrel when the unit is removed, and the clamps release the lock when it is installed, setting the feed gear into mesh with the carriage rack.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
