## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a Hammond-type writer the operator cannot easily inspect the writing, and errors are hard to erase.

**Solution:** An attachment at the back of the machine locks the feed roll when the paper is raised for inspection, and adds an erasing plate and an improved ribbon shield.

**Features:** [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
