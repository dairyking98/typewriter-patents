## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stenographic writing needs fast keying of several keys at once.

**Solution:** A finger-key stenotype: key levers are paired in frames, each lever-frame carrying an independent marker, with a spacing key on one frame.
