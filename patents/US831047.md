## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making a bill and an abstract on one sheet requires removing paper from the machine to swap the abstract blank.

**Solution:** A cylinder platen divided into two sections can be advanced independently or locked together, with a spring-driven escapement for the abstract section.
