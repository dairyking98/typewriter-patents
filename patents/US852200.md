## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In known line-spacing arrangements the lock release for the paper roller is independent of the spacing lever, so the locking pawl can be left disengaged and the paper then feeds inexactly.

**Solution:** The release of the locking pawl is positively connected to the operating pawl lever, so that moving the lever in the opposite direction from line spacing disengages the lock, and the lever position shows whether the lock is released.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
