## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines, and more particularly to the paper feed mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A single sheet metal combined paper table and apron extends beneath the platen, with paper feeding devices that can be placed in normal operative or released positions on the platen carriage.
