## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed rollers need a simple, strong and easily operated arrangement that holds them against the platen.

**Solution:** Feed-roller brackets pivot on a shaft and are pressed to the platen by springs between abutments, with a releasing rod that cams them away.
