## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Disc counters for line or item counts on calculating machine printing devices are hard to read.

**Solution:** A line counter uses number drums, carried by ten-circuit gearing and reset to zero through a periodically controlled lever and zero stop.
