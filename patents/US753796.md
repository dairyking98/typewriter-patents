## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with retarding devices, e.g. brakes

**In the patent’s own words:** Our present invention relates to type-writing and tabulating machines.

**How it works (reading):** A governor controls the speed of the paper carriage so that its motion is slowed before the sudden arrest in the tabulating typewriter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sudden arrest of the paper carriage by the feed mechanism causes injurious shocks and jar.

**Solution:** A governor controls the speed of the paper carriage so that its motion is slowed before the sudden arrest in the tabulating typewriter.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
