## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** One object of my invention is to provide an attachment for laten typewriters, by which it shall be possible to make upon a continuous web of paper a carbon or other copy of the printed matter written by means of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment on the carriage that supports a continuous paper web and carbon or ribbon, with a cutting knife, so copies can be made and cut to lengths.
