## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stop devices must seat the carriage stop and frame stop exactly one letter space apart.

**Solution:** A margin stop device with a gage stop normally one letter space ahead of the carriage stop surface, used to set the carriage stop with the frame stop.
