## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (electromagnetic control of booking machine functions)

**How it works (reading):** Conductors are housed in the contact holders on a stationary bar, with contact rails and a busbar, so no lines run between moving parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Sliding-contact wiring from a moving paper trolley is exposed to damage and wears.

**Solution:** Conductors are housed in the contact holders on a stationary bar, with contact rails and a busbar, so no lines run between moving parts.
