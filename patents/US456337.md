## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Yost typewriter the space bar and letter-space dogs are rigidly tied, so each keystroke carries finger-key tension, driving tension and wear from the carriage feed.

**Solution:** A lost-motion (slotted) coupling between the universal bar and the letter-space dogs lets the typebar travel a set distance before the escapement is actuated.

**How it works:** Key levers push connecting rods that lift a circular universal bar; this raises a slidable spindle which engages a horizontal lever. The lever's pin sits in an elongated slot of a coupling; only after the slot play is taken up does the coupling pull a rod on the rocker arm that carries the independently pivoted feed dogs on a duplex rack. A turnbuckle adjusts the rod length and a coiled spring returns the lever. The space bar is linked through levers and a threaded rod to the same coupling. Shown on the Yost type-ring machine with driving links and guide links.
