## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible writing is obstructed by the margin adjustment device, and the carriage is heavy and hard to balance.

**Solution:** Margin stops are carried on two rails near the writing point, leaving room between the type lever and the platen, with display scales on the rails so the writing stays visible and the carriage is lighter.
