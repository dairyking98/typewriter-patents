## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (multicolor ribbon for cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Shields or masks

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon assembly for cash registers, Booking, calculating, typing or similar machines whose ribbon is not visible is arranged and in which the color zones of the tape in its advancing direction successive, so that registrations in one and the same column of a Documents should reach each other for printing without raising and lowering the ribbon , can be printed in different colors.

**How it works (reading):** A hole in the neutral zone of the ribbon is sensed by contact arms that give an advance visual signal of the colour zone change, which is done by a pawl switch between reels.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Multi-colour ribbon changes need manual lifting of the ribbon and cannot be announced in time.

**Solution:** A hole in the neutral zone of the ribbon is sensed by contact arms that give an advance visual signal of the colour zone change, which is done by a pawl switch between reels.
