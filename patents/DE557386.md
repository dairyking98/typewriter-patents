## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Individually exchangeable type strips for address printing frames either are thick and bulky or are U-profiled sheet metal that embossing machines cannot process, and wide margins make line spacing large.

**Solution:** The embossing strips are cut from a flat sheet of metal that is embossed in the usual machine and then divided along V-grooves into narrow strips, which slide into the retaining lips of the frame.
