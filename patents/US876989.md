## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor's earlier type-cylinder typewriter was complex and costly, and its cylinder position had to be fixed exactly at the moment of printing.

**Solution:** Key levers with two abutments tilt a cam-operating frame by an amount set by the key, rotating a type cylinder to the chosen type, after which the same stroke swings the cylinder frame to print and a notched disk locks it against a knife edge.

**How it works:** The type cylinder and an actuating cam sit on one spindle in a frame that swings on trunnions. Each key lever has two abutments of different lengths. The first tilts a cam-operating frame pivoted on a main frame, and the second stops that tilt when the cylinder has turned to the required extent, so the key picks the rotation. A forked end on the frame turns the cam, and so the cylinder, to select the type. A spring catch locks the main frame until a universal bar under all the keys, with a cam-shaped projection, withdraws it once selection is done. Further key travel pulls the main frame down, turning a toothed segment and pinion on the trunnions to swing the cylinder frame onto the paper and print. A toothed or notched disk at the foot of the spindle meets a fixed knife edge as the frame swings, fixing the cylinder's position with precision during the blow.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Type element:** [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** type cylinder; spindle; actuating cam; swinging frame; trunnion; main frame; cam-operating frame; key lever; abutment; catch; universal bar; toothed segment; pinion; notched disk; knife edge; spring

**Improves on:**

- Moya's earlier US patent (cam, clutch and crown-wheel type-cylinder machine): simpler selection by key-lever abutments and a knife-edge disk lock instead of the clutch and crown wheel
