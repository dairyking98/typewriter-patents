## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon carrier takes a hard strain at the end of its stroke, which wears it out quickly.

**Solution:** A roller sits in a tapered recess in a bracket and is held by a spring, so the carrier is retarded near the end of its initial stroke.

**How it works:** A roller in a recess on the carrier bracket engages a spring-loaded lip near the end of travel, cushioning the carrier.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier bracket; roller in tapered recess; retaining spring
