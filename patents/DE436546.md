## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving mechanisms for functional elements of tabulation devices, e.g. of carriage skip

**How it works (reading):** A ratchet wheel on the worm-wheel shaft is locked by a pawl that moves with the number keys or a motor button, so releasing a key moves the worm axially and triggers the motor clutch, while pressing the key frees the ratchet and the worm returns by spring force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The known motor drives for calculators let the worm move on its shaft under a blocking load, which can disengage the motor clutch and let parts of the mechanism be damaged.

**Solution:** A ratchet wheel on the worm-wheel shaft is locked by a pawl that moves with the number keys or a motor button, so releasing a key moves the worm axially and triggers the motor clutch, while pressing the key frees the ratchet and the worm returns by spring force.
