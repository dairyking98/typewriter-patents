## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

The object of my invention is to make provision in a simple and effective manner for the use of roll paper instead of paper in separate sheets on a typewriter. To this end, I mount a roll of paper immediately on the carriage frame of a typewriter and immediately beneath the roll IL arrange a platen preferably in the form of a stationary bar under and against which the paper is drawn by feed rollers as hereinafter described.
