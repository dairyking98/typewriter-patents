## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On billing machines the crank that rotates the platen back dips below the frame and gets hit by the carriage.

**Solution:** A pinion on the platen axle is driven by a rack and crank at the back of the platen frame, so backward rotation comes from a forward crank stroke that stays above the frame.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
