## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and particularly to mechanism for providing alinement and adjustment of the type and type-bars.

**How it works (reading):** Mechanism on one supporting plate guides the type-bars to the printing position with little friction and adjusts the horizontal position and vertical height of the printed letter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Guiding each type to the printing position is not frictionless, and the printed letter cannot be easily shifted horizontally or vertically.

**Solution:** Mechanism on one supporting plate guides the type-bars to the printing position with little friction and adjusts the horizontal position and vertical height of the printed letter.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
