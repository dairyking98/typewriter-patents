## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On pneumatic machines with continuously rotating typewheels, holding a key too long strikes the same type twice.

**Solution:** Keyboard with valves that open only for an instant when a key is pressed and close again automatically.

**How it works:** An air-tight box fed with compressed air has a valve box under each key. Pressing a key makes an angle lever open the valve rod; as it travels, the other arm strikes an abutment and rotates the lever, releasing the valve rod, which springs closed whether or not the finger lifts. The brief puff checks the typewheel once so the type strikes once. Earlier patents 659,703, 676,590 and 757,066 describe the machine.

**Features:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Printing telegraph](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** air box; valve; valve rod; angle lever; key; abutment; compressed air pipe
