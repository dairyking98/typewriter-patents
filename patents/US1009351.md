## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon adjusted transversely

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mecha- 'nism for typewriting machines and more especially to ribbon controlling and indicating devices for such ribbon mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A shiftable indicator plate with marks shows through a sight hole in a fixed plate, and detents control the ribbon shift between fields.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon mode (cross feeding or one of several ribbon fields, or inoperative for stencil work) is not visible to the operator.

**Solution:** A shiftable indicator plate with marks shows through a sight hole in a fixed plate, and detents control the ribbon shift between fields.
