June 191i claims priority due to the registration in Austria of December 28 , 19z3. The invention relates to an automatic device for reversing the movement of the ribbon of typewriters, in which the ribbon reels sitting next to one another on a shaft are alternately coupled to the drive shaft with the aid of lovelies that act on a coupling located between the reels . According to the invention, the levers displacing the coupling sleeve are designed as angle levers, of which one fork-shaped arm encompasses the coupling sleeve, while the other arm is moved in a manner known per se by a stop on the ribbon. This design is characterized by great simplicity and accordingly cheap manufacturability. In the drawing, Fig. I shows a side view and Fig. Z shows a plan view from the inside onto a ribbon spool. The two ribbon spools are pushed onto a shaft. Adjusting rings prevent the ribbon spools from moving away from each other, while they are fixed invariably at a certain distance from each other by a spacer sleeve g.
