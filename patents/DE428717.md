## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an automatic device for reversing the movement of the ribbon of typewriters, in which the ribbon reels sitting next to one another on a shaft are alternately coupled to the drive shaft with the aid of lovelies that act on a coupling located between the reels .

**How it works (reading):** Each reel is alternately coupled to the drive by a sleeve moved by angle levers, whose fork arm grips the sleeve while the other arm is moved by a stop on the ribbon; the ribbon is passed through slots in opposite twist to reverse it.

According to the invention, the levers displacing the coupling sleeve are designed as angle levers, of which one fork-shaped arm encompasses the coupling sleeve, while the other arm is moved in a manner known per se by a stop on the ribbon. This design is characterized by great simplicity and accordingly cheap manufacturability. In the drawing, Fig. I shows a side view and Fig. Z shows a plan view from the inside onto a ribbon spool.
