> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a line switching device on typewriters, especially those used to write out invoices when using a booklet on which the individual items of the individual invoices are collected. Typewriters have already become known in which, on the one hand, the Paper roller a lever for lifting the paper pressure rollers and on the other side the usual line switch are attached to the paper roller. In this known device there is an additional one on the side on which the line shift lever is attached Hand lever provided, when pivoted out, the line feed is influenced and also the paper pressure rollers of lifted off the roller. It has also already been proposed to have a line switch lever on each side of the paper carriage to be arranged, but only the usual line feed is caused by actuating it. In contrast, according to the invention, the device is made so that one of the to two line switch devices arranged on both sides of the paper carriage a lever is operated, which the release lever for the lower paper pressure rollers influenced in such a way that these roles after the line break from the paper roller be lifted off.
