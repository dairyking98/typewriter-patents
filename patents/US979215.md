## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A column stop bar carries stops that are set in chosen positions by a setting mechanism and can be restored to normal inoperative position quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Selecting different column stops for a different job and clearing the stops in operative position is slow and inconvenient.

**Solution:** A column stop bar carries stops that are set in chosen positions by a setting mechanism and can be restored to normal inoperative position quickly.
