## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** The chief objects of the present invention are to drive the said brush, or cleaner entirely by friction, instead of by positive connections, and to provide for throwing it instantly into or out of the operative position.

**How it works (reading):** Brush driven by friction cones from a crank shaft, spring-pressed and raised into the type opening to clean type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cleaning brush needs quick engagement and disengagement without positive gearing.

**Solution:** Brush driven by friction cones from a crank shaft, spring-pressed and raised into the type opening to clean type.
