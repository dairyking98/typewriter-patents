## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device on typewriters and calculating machines for moving in and out of forms. The invention. relates to a device on typewriters and calculating machines zurr entry and exit of forms, copies of which are on a common Control sheets are recorded in direct succession. Such so-called billing devices are known in which For example, a toothed wheel seated on the axis is rotated by means of a toothed rack and in this way the entry and exit of the sheets of paper is effected. The present invention adopts a different constructive solution to this Task, which is due to the elimination of gears and simple operation excels. The invention consists in the fact that around a drum that is connected to the typewriter roller is coupled by a clutch that can be engaged and disengaged at will, two bands or the like are wound up in the opposite sense, in such a way that the unwinding of one volume the introduction of the papers to be written on and at the same time with it winding up the other tape and unwinding this other tape Return transport of the papers and winding of the first tape in the opposite one Senses is effected.
