## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (character punching machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** The chief object of my invention is to produce an improved machine for use in putting characters on sheet material, as by punching, embossing, or printing the desired charactersr The machine in which my invention is here shown embodied is a machine adapted to punch characters in bank-checks, certificates of stock, and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A positive work-feed and guide-rolls feed the sheet under impression bars carried on a rotary support, which brings each punch or impression into place.
