## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in back-spacers for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A back-spacer on a rock shaft connected to the carriage, with escapement dogs on a dog block engaging the escapement wheel, and a stop that limits the back throw.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Back-spacing needs a simple means that can be fitted to or removed from an existing machine, and a stop that limits the backward throw.

**Solution:** A back-spacer on a rock shaft connected to the carriage, with escapement dogs on a dog block engaging the escapement wheel, and a stop that limits the back throw.
