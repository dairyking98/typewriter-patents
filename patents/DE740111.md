## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (copying receiving strips with typewriters or typesetters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is an arrangement that enables the copying of strip text using typewriters or typesetting machines should facilitate.

**How it works (reading):** A strip puller with a take-up drum is driven only while keys are struck, via a Bowden cable from a ribbon-reel control or a common key-contact device; a magnet presses the strip against a feed roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Strip for copying is fed past the typewriter at a steady speed, which wastes strip and does not follow the writing.

**Solution:** A strip puller with a take-up drum is driven only while keys are struck, via a Bowden cable from a ribbon-reel control or a common key-contact device; a magnet presses the strip against a feed roller.
