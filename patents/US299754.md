## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter should be compact, write fast with very small key movement, and show the whole sheet as written.

**Solution:** Small type wheels with toothed rims, carried between two revolving disks inside a casing with an ink pad, are turned by key levers so the chosen tooth reaches a slot in the pad and prints.

**How it works:** A vertical shaft turned by treadle or motor spins two disks holding horizontal type wheels, each with one tooth per key and a blank sector. Tiny keys set in a small circle are pressed by a stylus, and the key lever inner ends engage the wheels so that they rotate by an amount that brings a chosen letter to a slot in the casing's inking cushion. Teeth rub the pad and are inked, and the letter passes through the slot to leave an impression. When the blank space is at the slot nothing prints. A cam track, pins on springs and a segmental rack with triangular teeth help rotate the wheels.
