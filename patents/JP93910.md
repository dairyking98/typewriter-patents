**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection; Flexible thin piece, plate, or bar slides from a rotary typeface box

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a type-selecting device for typesetting, distributing and printing machines, built as follows: a frame that rises and falls holds a great many types;

**How it works (reading):** A lifting curved frame of tiers and cells with drawable type containers, positioned by an indicator dial, whose selected tier is pushed out by a handle so a push-button gripper can pick one type and carry it to a composing plate (or a paper sheet to print).

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters hold only about 80 characters (Western) or at most a few thousand (kanji); a machine is wanted to hold very many types of many kinds in a small magazine and take them out quickly for setting, distributing or printing.

**Solution:** A lifting curved frame of tiers and cells with drawable type containers, positioned by an indicator dial, whose selected tier is pushed out by a handle so a push-button gripper can pick one type and carry it to a composing plate (or a paper sheet to print).

**How it works:** The curved frame is divided into a dozen or more tiers, each with many cells holding a removable single-type container (e.g. one tier of lower case, one of capitals). Turning the indicator dial drives chains and gears that raise the frame until the chosen tier is level with a pusher plate. Pulling a handle works linkage that moves the pusher forward and pushes all containers of that tier out in a row. Pressing the push-button for a character pushes its type up and a gripper on a swinging arm seizes it and carries it to the composing plate. Run the other way it distributes type back to its container, or with paper in place of the composing plate it prints.

**Parts:** lifting frame; tier; cell; type container; pusher plate; indicator dial; fixed pointer; chain; sprocket; handle; push-button; gripper; composing plate; support frame

**Numbers:** about 80 characters in foreign typewriters; over 3,000 kanji in kanji machines; a dozen or more tiers

**Improves on:**

- foreign-language and kanji typewriters with limited character sets: magazine holds far more types in tiers and cells

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Type-selecting device for typesetting, distributing and printing machines. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a type-selecting device for typesetting, distributing and printing machines, built as follows: a frame that rises and falls holds a great many types; the frame is divided across into many tiers, and each tier into many cells, each cell holding a type container that can be drawn out; the frame is brought, by means of an indicator dial, to put any tier at the required place; at the same time all the type containers of the tier shown on the dial are pushed out in a row by a pusher plate worked through a handle; the wanted type in that tier is then gripped by a gripper linked to a push-button and carried to a separately mounted composing plate; or, for distributing, the type is put back into its container the opposite way; or a sheet of paper is mounted in place of the composing plate, to print. Its object is to hold a great many types of many different kinds in a relatively small magazine, and to obtain machines that take these types out quickly, accurately and easily, to set them, distribute them or print with them.
