## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A ratchet-driven feed needs several step sizes without changing the pawl stroke, in a small, simple, easily assembled change-speed device.

**Solution:** Same sliding-gear change-speed train as JP163316, with the paired eccentric second-group gears clamped together by spacer discs and screw spindles for easy assembly.

**How it works:** A ratchet drive gear turns a shaft with four gears of equal pitch and different diameters. Each meshes with a second-group gear, all aligned on one line on the far side. The second-group gears are mounted in pairs on eccentric bosses of spacer discs, and two discs are joined through a spacer plate by screw spindles whose stub pins pass through the plate into the other disc. Retaining discs in the gear box hold the assembly. A sliding gear on the driven shaft is moved by a knob and rollers to mesh with one gear, changing the ratio and so the platen feed per ratchet tooth.
