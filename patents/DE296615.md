## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is an edge stop device for the paper trolley of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A two-armed lever on the paper carriage underside is set by the right-hand stop to block the universal rail, and also locks one of the left-hand stops, so the locking lever serves as the left margin stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Reliable left and right margins are needed with few parts, since a separate left edge stop would add parts.

**Solution:** A two-armed lever on the paper carriage underside is set by the right-hand stop to block the universal rail, and also locks one of the left-hand stops, so the locking lever serves as the left margin stop.
