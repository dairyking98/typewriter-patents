## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens with balancing means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriters and consists of an improvement therein having for its object to provide means for the correction of mistakes in writing requiring erasure and rewriting in the same space.

**How it works (reading):** Platen-carrying end brackets have a clearance so the platen can shift longitudinally independent of the carriage, adjusting the start of the carriage movement to space the substituted word evenly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A word substituted for an erased one with an extra letter does not space evenly in the line.

**Solution:** Platen-carrying end brackets have a clearance so the platen can shift longitudinally independent of the carriage, adjusting the start of the carriage movement to space the substituted word evenly.
