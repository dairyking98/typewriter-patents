## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The frame is hard to repair and clean, and it is heavy to transport.

**Solution:** The frame is made in two light sections, a skeleton lower frame and an upper frame carrying the carriage rail, so it separates for repair and is lighter.
