## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for controlling character selection

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, especially those of the front strike variety;

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Key touch needs a wide range of variation for different operators, and the connecting rods of the type-action train must be able to elongate or not under sharp blows.

**Solution:** Connecting rods with adjustable elastic idle links let the operator set how far the type-bar actuating links yield, giving a stiff or a yielding touch.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
