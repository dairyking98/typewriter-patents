> Description Translated from German Typewriter. It is in mechanical engineering, especially in automotive engineering, well known to enclose working machine parts in a housing, which allows the use of an oil bath. It has also already become known, these To exploit ideas for typewriters in such a way that, with the exception of the keys, the type carrier and the typewriter roller with the associated parts, all Parts of the machine are enclosed in a housing, then by a special Oil pump and 'oil lines are fed to the individual moving parts. In contrast, the invention consists in the fact that here the overall mechanism the typewriter into individual, collectible groups of gear parts is disassembled, such as B. the carriage switching device, the spring housing, the longitudinal ribbon control, the lifting device for the ribbon guide or the like.
