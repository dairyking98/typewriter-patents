## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon bobbins are hard to insert and remove from a front-strike machine.

**Solution:** Bobbins sit on stud bearings reached through side openings, with ribbon guides above and a vertical ribbon vibrator between them.
