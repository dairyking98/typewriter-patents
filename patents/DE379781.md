> Description Translated from German The invention relates to sensing mechanisms for recording machines working with punched cards. In such machines, as is known, certain work processes of the machine are initiated by those feeler stamps of the feeler mechanism that pass through a hole in the card when the feeler mechanism is working, in that these stamps, for example, close electrical circuits or adjust gear parts of the machine. The aim of the invention is to provide such sensing mechanisms with a device which prevents an error from sneaking in unnoticed if, for example, a sensor does not properly pass through a hole in the card.
