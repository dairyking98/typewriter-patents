## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punched-card feeler stamps could fail to pass through card holes without an indication.

**Solution:** Cutting edges on each feeler stamp cut two notches beside the hole when the stamp passes properly, marking success on the card.

The aim of the invention is to provide such sensing mechanisms with a device which prevents an error from sneaking in unnoticed if, for example, a sensor does not properly pass through a hole in the card.
