## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** If the carriage cord slips off its keeper when the carriage is removed, the spring barrel spins free and has to be reset.

**Solution:** A safety loop that keeps the cord attached to the machine even if it leaves the keeper.

**How it works:** The cord from the spring barrel ends in a U-shaped member with two hooks: the upper catches the carriage, the lower a keeper on the shift frame when the carriage is removed. A safety loop holds the cord to the frame so that an accidental release cannot unwind the spring.

**Features:** [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** spring barrel; cord; hook; keeper; shift frame; safety loop; carriage

**Improves on:**

- Oliver typewriter cord keeper: adds safety loop against accidental release
