## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guards, shields or dust excluders

**How it works (reading):** The front cover flap is hinged at its lower edge and angled to serve as a desk for documents, and can be swung down to open the type basket.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The cover of the typewriter gives no place to rest documents to copy from.

**Solution:** The front cover flap is hinged at its lower edge and angled to serve as a desk for documents, and can be swung down to open the type basket.
