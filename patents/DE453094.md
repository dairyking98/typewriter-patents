## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ten circuit for transverse counters)

**How it works (reading):** A simplified tens transmission with counting wheels driven by a main ratchet wheel on a sliding square shaft, geared to the main ratchet shaft, is provided for the transverse counter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tens transmission of the transverse counter on book typewriters is costly to make and not reliable enough in use.

**Solution:** A simplified tens transmission with counting wheels driven by a main ratchet wheel on a sliding square shaft, geared to the main ratchet shaft, is provided for the transverse counter.

**Features:**

- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)
