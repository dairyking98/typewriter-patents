## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage releasing mechanism of a tabulator needs a better construction so the carriage is released and stopped at the right place when a denomination key is operated.

**Solution:** A denomination key releases its denomination stop, which runs freely under the carriage spring until arrested by a projected stop where the number is begun.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
