## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (inking device for calculating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for multicolour work

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an inking device for printing Machines, especially for calculating machines, cash registers and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A ring-clamped inking cloth (or inked disc) sits between roller and hammer and is indexed by a fixed angle each cycle, driven synchronously from the hammer drive.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Color-change inking units on register printers need the ink carrier to advance a fresh area after each impression.

**Solution:** A ring-clamped inking cloth (or inked disc) sits between roller and hammer and is indexed by a fixed angle each cycle, driven synchronously from the hammer drive.

**Features:** [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Ink disc](../features/ink-disc.md)
