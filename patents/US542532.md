## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons wear along one line and need hand reversal at each end.

**Solution:** Automatic reversal of the ribbon winding direction plus a transverse ribbon shift, driven from the key-lever frame.

**How it works:** A pivoted frame under the key levers is pressed down with each keystroke and moves rods carrying dogs that step ratchet wheels on two spool shafts, one shaft at a time. A rod moves sideways when a spool fills, shifting which dog engages so winding reverses. A spring detent holds each ratchet step. The ribbon is also moved transversely so all of its width is used.
