## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On Oliver typewriters with extra-long carriages for wide paper, the overhanging guide-bars of the shift-frame need support partway along their length.

**Solution:** Add an intermediate support under each guide-bar: a roller on the bar riding a curved horizontal rail fixed to a lug on the base.

**How it works:** The shift-frame hangs from swinging links and carries front and rear guide-bars that project beyond the base. A lug on each side of the base carries a vertical plate and a short rail running transverse to the bar. A roller on a stud, mounted on an angle bar fixed to the front guide-bar, rests on the rail's upper edge. As the frame shifts, the roller rolls along the rail. The rail is curved, high at the ends and low in the middle, matching the arc the links swing the bar through, so the bar's rise and fall is followed. Slotted screw holes let the rail be adjusted.
