## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wanted a simpler typebar machine with fewer keys.

**Solution:** A circle of typebars in a ring whose limited shift moves the bar ring relative to a pawl ring so each key reaches several bars.

**How it works:** A ring on two standards serves as pawl ring and carriage support, with pawls in vertical holes. Within it the type-bar ring, carrying the bars on a common pivot wire in radial grooves, is shifted by levers. A cylinder with tapering slots from its lower edge guides and aligns each bar at the print point and has a cut-away upper part for the ribbon. The heel of each bar is a cam surface so the pawl pulls at constant leverage. The roller platen sits in a recess to shorten the stroke. Spacing pawls and ribbon reversing and carrying mechanism are described.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md)
- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** standard; pawl ring; type-bar ring; typebar; pivot wire; guide and aligner cylinder; pawl; spacing pawl; spacing lever; ribbon carrier; roller platen
