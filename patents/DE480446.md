> Description Translated from German Printing device for cash registers In printing works where the The print strip is routed around the print hammer, wears off with prolonged use the evils noticeable that the. Edges of the pressure bar consisting of a C rubber mass by dragging the print strip past during the sweeping motion and the Förderbe'wegung are worn so heavily that the clarity of the print is strong underneath leiflet. To eliminate this inconvenience, according to the invention, the printing bar, the printing strip guide and the printing block are mutually arranged so that in addition to the joint movement of the printing strip and the printing bar away from the printing block after the printing, an additional movement is given by which it is lifted from the print strip. This arrangement offers the advantage (leave the print strip completely free when the device is in the idle state) - is moved between the print bar and the printing block and can neither damage the print bar nor be soiled by the type wheels or the ribbon.
