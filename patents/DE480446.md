## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register print device)

**How it works (reading):** An additional movement lifts the printing bar off the print strip after printing, and the guide arrangement keeps the strip clear at rest.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The print strip is worn at the edges of the rubber printing bar as it is dragged across during each cycle.

**Solution:** An additional movement lifts the printing bar off the print strip after printing, and the guide arrangement keeps the strip clear at rest.
