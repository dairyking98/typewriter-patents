## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic ribbon reversing devices either use complicated switching gear or switch slowly, so the types strike the same ribbon spot.

**Solution:** A resilient toggle lever, snapping past dead centre, instantly reverses the ribbon drive when the ribbon runs off a spool.

**How it works:** Each ribbon spool support carries a spring-loaded bracket with a guide roller, tied to a switching rod. When the ribbon has fully unwound from one spool, its bracket pulls its rod and starts the toggle lever. The toggle lever's two arms buckle after dead centre, accelerating a control pin that slides a rail carrying the drive bevel gears and turns a three-armed locking lever. That lever alternately locks one shift rod and frees the other to take the ribbon tension, so everything switches suddenly together.
