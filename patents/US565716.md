## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** My improvements relate tomeans for moving the platen and the paper thereon endwise for the purpose of adjusting the, latter correctly with reference to the printing-point for making corrections;

**How it works (reading):** An escapement giving single or double letter spacing, combined with a carriage stop/line lock and a pointer attached to the shift rail.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines with platen shift need an escapement that can feed single or double letter spaces and a pointer for the print point.

**Solution:** An escapement giving single or double letter spacing, combined with a carriage stop/line lock and a pointer attached to the shift rail.

**How it works:** Two-type typebars sit on a ring-shaped top plate and are lifted by levers and connecting rods from key levers. The platen carrier slides on a guide-rod and rides on a shift rail raised by shift levers and links. The escapement can feed one or two spaces, with a carriage-arresting stop plate that also locks the line. A pointer mechanism fixed to the shift rail shows the print position when the platen is turned up.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** top plate; typebar; lifting lever; connecting rod; key lever; guide-rod; platen carrier; shift rail; escapement; carriage stop plate; pointer
