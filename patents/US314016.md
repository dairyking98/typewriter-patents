## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator has no warning when the ribbon reaches the end of its travel and its feed direction must be reversed.

**Solution:** Lugs on the ribbon ends lift a wire arm that drops a visible flap or releases a bell striker when the ribbon nears its limit.

**How it works:** Projections are fixed on the flexible ribbon end pieces. As the ribbon winds onto a spool the lug reaches a wire arm connected to the frame and lifts it. This removes the support of a hinged metal flap, which drops into the operator's sight; alternatively it frees a bell striker. The operator then shifts the feed to wind the other way.
