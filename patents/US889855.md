**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to special paperfeeding devices of typewriting machines, such as shown in United States Patent No.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Condensed billing needs spacing between charges on the carbon record sheet.

**Solution:** A crank mounted on the platen frame drives a dog that skips one or more notches of the toothed wheel during its first part, giving the required line space.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** crank on platen frame; pawl or dog; notched wheel on platen; spring
