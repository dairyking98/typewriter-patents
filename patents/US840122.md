## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improved constructions' for writing mechanisms;

**How it works (reading):** Two parallel links pivoted to the key member and supported on transverse rods hold the key member up with a spring, and the links snap into place for quick assembly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-member returns need a fixed attachment point for the spring, which is a limitation of common constructions.

**Solution:** Two parallel links pivoted to the key member and supported on transverse rods hold the key member up with a spring, and the links snap into place for quick assembly.
