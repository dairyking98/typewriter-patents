## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detachable carriers or holders for impression-transfer material mechanism

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to attachments for type-Writing machines designed for produc- Ic ing duplicates of original manuscript-that is to say, for producing facsimiles of ink-ribbon impressions upon two separate sheets of paper and at the same time, if it is desired, a triplicate impression upon a third sheet.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment produces facsimile ink-ribbon impressions on two or more sheets at once without spooling an auxiliary ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Prior ribbon-duplicating attachments needed a spooled auxiliary ribbon with a protecting strip, adding complex gears, shafts and guides.

**Solution:** An attachment produces facsimile ink-ribbon impressions on two or more sheets at once without spooling an auxiliary ribbon.
