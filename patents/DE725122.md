**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guards, shields or dust excluders

**How it works (reading):** A cam on the inside of the lid pivots the two-armed brake flap away from the reel when the lid opens.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reels in a housing need a brake flap that is hard to clear for replacement.

**Solution:** A cam on the inside of the lid pivots the two-armed brake flap away from the reel when the lid opens.

**How it works:** Lid-opening cam swings the ribbon brake flap out of the reel area.
