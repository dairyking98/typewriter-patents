## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon should lie dead at every key stroke when cutting stencils, but silencing the vibrator leaves the ribbon winding on.

**Solution:** Connect the ribbon winding to the carriage spring barrel by a worm drive that one lever lifts away together with silencing the vibrator.

**How it works:** A worm driven by the spring barrel meshes with a worm wheel on the main ribbon shaft, which slides endwise to connect either spool shaft for reversal. A lengthened worm wheel keeps mesh as the shaft shifts. Keys on a cross arm shift an actuator between two wrists on the vibrator lever at different distances, giving two stroke lengths for two ribbon stripes. A single hand lever sets the cross arm to the midway position, so neither wrist engages, and lifts the worm off the wheel.
