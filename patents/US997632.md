## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Center guide for a shifting-platen front-strike machine must keep a fixed relation to the typebars without blocking the ribbon vibrator or the view.

**Solution:** A very simple center guide fixed to the stationary frame close to the platen, clear of the vibrator and its support.

**How it works:** The shift frame carries the platen carriage and swings to change case; the ribbon vibrator and its slotted support rise from the shift frame in front of the letter scale. The guide lugs on the type heads enter a center guide fixed to the main frame near the platen. The support is slotted for the typebars and the vibrator slides in a guideway on its back.
