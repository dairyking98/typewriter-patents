## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars each carrying several characters need a key organisation that moves the bar to print any of three characters, plus shifting of the type-bearing sleeve.

**Solution:** Three finger-keys per typebar act through a slotted plate to tilt and slide the bar, while each bar's sleeve with three faces is partially rotated by a rocking shifting plate.

**How it works:** Each typebar carries a rotatable sleeve with three longitudinal faces of three type each, giving 81 characters from nine bars. Three keys per bar act through a slotted plate to rock the bar about its pivot and slide it lengthwise so the chosen character reaches the printing point. A rocking shifting plate partially rotates all sleeves to bring another face opposite the platen, locking the bars meanwhile. The text shown is only the opening.
