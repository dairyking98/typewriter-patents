## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key for calculating, typing and similar machines, in which the key cap The receiving key button with the key shaft consists of one piece.

**How it works (reading):** The key shaft is punched from one piece with side tabs bent over into a ring-shaped top, and a keycap is pulled over it, with the resilient lobes holding the cap.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Key buttons made as a separate cap on a key shaft are costly, and the cap can work loose on its ring.

**Solution:** The key shaft is punched from one piece with side tabs bent over into a ring-shaped top, and a keycap is pulled over it, with the resilient lobes holding the cap.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
