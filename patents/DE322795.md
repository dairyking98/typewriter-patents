## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**How it works (reading):** An insulating sliding sleeve on the pin, with a spring, touches the plate and closes the circuit only when pressed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The contact pin for the electric type stop could close the circuit by accident when the pen slid over the plate.

**Solution:** An insulating sliding sleeve on the pin, with a spring, touches the plate and closes the circuit only when pressed.
