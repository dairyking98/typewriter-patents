## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** A cord from the line-adjuster lever runs through the cover to a crank-driven roller outside, letting the operator advance lines and feel or see the carriage position without opening the box.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A sound-absorbing cover keeps the carriage line adjuster out of reach, so operating it and hearing the end-of-line bell is awkward and the bell is muffled.

**Solution:** A cord from the line-adjuster lever runs through the cover to a crank-driven roller outside, letting the operator advance lines and feel or see the carriage position without opening the box.
