## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A card holder on a cylindrical platen must be secured and released easily.

**Solution:** A sheet metal card-holder with teeth runs along the platen, with arms forked over the platen hubs. One arm has claws and the other a pivoted hook that latches under the hub, released by a finger piece.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** card holder with teeth; forked arms; claws; pivoted hook; finger piece
