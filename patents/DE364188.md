> Description Translated from German Soundproof typewriter. By patent 333531 is one Typewriter with sound-absorbing jacket placed under protection, its feature consists in the fact that auxiliary keys are arranged in the jacket, which after your everting of the coat come to rest over the machine on the keys of the same. The invention relates to a further improvement of this device, which consists in that the elastic buffers of the auxiliary keys are made up of two panels elastic material, e.g. B. felt, of which one is sunk by means of a Screw or the like attached to the auxiliary keypad and the other felt plate on this is glued on. This ensures that the buffer not only against the Button, but also spring against the coat, causing the when going up on the Coat impacting button does not generate any sound. The auxiliary keypad can also be hollow and filled with lubricant, the buffer preventing the leaking Lubricant contaminated the main button.
