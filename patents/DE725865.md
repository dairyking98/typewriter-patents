## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (teleprinter receiver with button-driven type levers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or circuits at the receiving end using mechanical translation and type-bar printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a teletype receiver with type levers controlled by buttons.

**How it works (reading):** Horizontal feeler levers and vertical selector flaps pivot draw hooks into the pressure rail; the hooks are on double-armed links under the type levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Teletype receivers with key-driven type levers have no room for electrically driven selection and printing parts.

**Solution:** Horizontal feeler levers and vertical selector flaps pivot draw hooks into the pressure rail; the hooks are on double-armed links under the type levers.

**How it works:** Feeler levers and selector flaps pivot draw hooks that strike the type levers at the pressure rail.
