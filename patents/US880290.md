**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in tabulator mechanisms for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A brake or controller on the driving drum slows the released carriage, and a yielding stop actuator and release linkage are added.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A released tabulator carriage strikes the stop too hard, which wears the machine.

**Solution:** A brake or controller on the driving drum slows the released carriage, and a yielding stop actuator and release linkage are added.
