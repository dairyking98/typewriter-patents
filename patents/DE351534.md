## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the mounting of certain swingable parts on typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Each swing-frame journal is mounted independently and can be slightly adjusted in each direction, so the universal rail is moved freely and evenly by all type levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Universal rail and swing frame bearings on the type-lever segment cause uneven, friction-bound motion of the rail and pawl holder.

**Solution:** Each swing-frame journal is mounted independently and can be slightly adjusted in each direction, so the universal rail is moved freely and evenly by all type levers.
