## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type hammers are noisy on the roller, and paper can be cut into the roller surface.

**Solution:** A light aluminium cylinder filled with plaster core is covered with felt and a rubber sleeve to deaden noise and give partial yield.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
