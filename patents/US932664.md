## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type blocks must be fixed to the bar cheaply and still be adjustable to align the type.

**Solution:** A bifurcated type block pressed onto a tapered dovetail end of the bar clamps by its own springiness and can be slid with pliers to align.

**How it works:** The bar end is reduced and tapered from top edge to bottom, with a shoulder behind it. The block is slit, bent so the arms are closer than the dovetail's lower edge, and driven endwise on. Spring arms clamp the dovetail faces and hold position. Pliers on the bar end or the shoulder slide the block along to align the type.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type bar; type block; dovetail securing portion; shoulder; bifurcated arm

**Numbers:** slot 0.10 in. wide, arms closed to 0.05 in.; dovetail 0.10 in. at the edge, 0.07 in. at the bottom

**Improves on:**

- cut dovetail type blocks: slot formed then closed, avoiding cutting a dovetail in the block
