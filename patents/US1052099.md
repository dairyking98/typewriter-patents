## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to 'typewriting machines and more particularly to" billing mechanism for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Automatically actuated devices at set points in the platen rotation arrest the platen so the operator is relieved of remembering indices (billing mechanism; full detail not confirmed in the excerpt).

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Billing requires the operator to remember indices and watch indicators while the platen turns.

**Solution:** Automatically actuated devices at set points in the platen rotation arrest the platen so the operator is relieved of remembering indices (billing mechanism; full detail not confirmed in the excerpt).
