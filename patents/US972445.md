## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in paper adjusting devices for writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A telescopic bar guides the sheet obliquely above the paper roller; an adjustable stop on the bar shows when the set lowest line is reached.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing runs past the desired bottom margin because the paper roller hides the sheet edge.

**Solution:** A telescopic bar guides the sheet obliquely above the paper roller; an adjustable stop on the bar shows when the set lowest line is reached.
