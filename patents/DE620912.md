## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On flat-plate typewriters the carriage could be uncoupled from the line feed only at one particular margin position, not at other adjustable margins.

**Solution:** A second rod parallel to the carriage push rod with an adjustable stop decouples carriage and line-feed pull rod at any chosen margin position.

**How it works:** The writing carriage runs on a line-switching frame over a flat plate. An adjustable edge stop on the carriage push rod sets the margin. A coupling hook on the carriage engages a stop on a line-switch pull rod near the end of carriage return and triggers the line feed. A separate decoupling rod with an adjustable cam stop then uncouples the two at whatever margin position is set.
