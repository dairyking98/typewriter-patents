## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving individually movable types toward the print point with a simple, controllable path.

**Solution:** A type lever pivoted on a sliding rod rotates when its arms hit fixed stops so the type swings nearly vertically to the print point and then onto an ink pad on return.

**How it works:** A horizontally sliding rod, pushed by a key via rack and gear and returned by a spring, carries the type lever on a pivot held by a spring. When one arm of the lever meets a fixed stop, the lever rotates about it and the type describes an arc to the printing point. On return the spring reverses the arc; the other arm then strikes a stop and presses the type onto an ink pad. Curved stop surfaces can shape the path, and the rod may be driven by lever, wedge, eccentric or air pressure with a cylinder and valves.
