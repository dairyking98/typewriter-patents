## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case-shift by moving the typebar segment normally disturbs the keys and key levers.

**Solution:** A three-position shiftable typebar segment (lower, middle, upper) with fixed key levers, linked so the typebar system shifts without moving the keys.

**How it works:** Three banks of keys sit on levers of the first order, pivoted on fixed rods; upright links lift sub-levers, which pull front-strike typebars to the print point through links. Each typebar carries three types, and the segment supporting the typebars shifts from its normal middle position to an upper or lower position to bring a different type to the printing point. The key levers fan in toward the rear because the typebar system is narrower than the key system. The claims here cover the case shift; claims on the type action stay with the parent application.
