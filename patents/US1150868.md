## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-feed pawl strikes its stop hard, causing wear, battering, overthrow of the platen and breakage.

**Solution:** A spring is placed between the line-space lever handle and the pawl so the spring yields on excess force and limits the power reaching the pawl; the lever has a separate stop.
