## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the main patent (DE39044) the type plate pivots on a hinged pad, so types strike the paper obliquely and print unevenly.

**Solution:** Mount the types on a rubber ring under a bell that is pressed straight down onto the paper, with a pointer-and-scale selection and a ratchet that spaces the paper carriage on each stroke.

**How it works:** Types sit on a rubber ring fixed under a bell that turns and slides on a hollow pin; the bell is turned by a knob by hand until one of two pointers points at the letter on a fixed scale. Large and small letters are on opposite sides (180 degrees apart) of the ring, so the pointer used chooses the case. Pressing the knob drives the bell down, a pin on the bell enters an upward-widening hole in a fixed ring to align the type exactly, and a crank on the pin rocks a lever whose tooth releases and whose arm advances the paper-roller rack by one tooth when the knob rises on a spring. The same lever steps the ink ribbon roll through a switching wheel. A slide under the scale is moved automatically by a stop on the bell to show either figures or punctuation under the scale recesses. The scale can be rotated against the frame to give a cipher offset, and two extra levered types print acute and grave accents.
