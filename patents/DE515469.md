## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage stop levers are heavy, and light travel machines need lighter ones.

**Solution:** Stop levers are made of thin sheet with a slotted tongue on one lever that engages the other, so they support each other against bending.
