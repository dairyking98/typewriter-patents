## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a type-bar machine, the impression depends on the blow of the operator, which is inconsistent and tiring.

**Solution:** A motor in a case on the frame powers the type-bars, so the impression is made by power-actuated mechanism instead of the operator's blow.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Power-driven type action](../features/bar-power-action.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** frame casting base and top; pillars; motor in case; drums with bearings; type-bars; key-levers; spacing-bar

**Numbers:** Patent 656,086; Serial No. not shown
