## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typographic machine with many types or dies, while keeping the moving impression mechanism small and fast.

**Solution:** Rolling impressors on sets of concentric shafts, each shaft shifted axially to the working position and returned after impressing, under selective control.

**How it works:** Rolling impressors carry types or dies and are mounted on sectional carriers, one section per set. The sections are fixed on concentric shafts. To operate a given impressor its shaft is rotated and shifted axially (with the others in its set) to the working axial position, makes the impression, then returns. A selective mechanism lets any one type or die act exclusively. A feed arm advances the work. The remaining description is truncated.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** rolling impressor; type; die; sectional carrier; concentric shaft; feed arm; selective mechanism
