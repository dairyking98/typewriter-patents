## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Shields or masks

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and especially to an indicator device to be used in connectiozi with a polychrome ink ribbon or an ink ribbon having fields of different characteristics.

**How it works (reading):** An indicator device on the machine shows which ribbon field is at the printing point, used with a polychrome ribbon.
