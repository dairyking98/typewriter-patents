## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A pivoting, rotating and shifting type cylinder must be positioned precisely in both rotation and row at the moment of impression.

**Solution:** A toothed disc and a guide disc on the cylinder axis enter a fixed guide piece with one slot per row, locking both the radial and the axial position.

**How it works:** The type cylinder axis, pivoted on a lever, carries a toothed disc and a guide disc. A fixed guide piece on the frame has a toothed front edge matching the disc, and three slots, one per type row. When a key is pressed and the axis swings toward the platen, the guide disc enters the slot for that row and the toothed disc fills its tooth gaps in the guide piece edge, giving a firm fine adjustment in both rotation and axial position. On return to rest, both discs slide out of the guide piece.

**Features:** [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type cylinder axis; toothed disc; guide disc; guide piece; slot; articulated lever; toothed segment

**Numbers:** three slots, one per type row

**Improves on:**

- toothed-disc locking of the radial cylinder position alone: adds a disc and slots that lock the axial row position too
