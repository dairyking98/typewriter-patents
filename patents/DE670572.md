## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (register strip printing in cash registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that by the printing unit either on the one serving as a control strip Register strips either only the individual amounts and the sums to be added or also the dependent line items of multi-item transactions are printed will.

**How it works (reading):** The printing unit prints either only the independent amounts and offset sums or also the dependent line items of multi-item transactions on the control strip, depending on whether a check is issued.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a receipt is not printed, the individual amounts and offset sums all land on one line of the control strip and print illegibly on top of each other.

**Solution:** The printing unit prints either only the independent amounts and offset sums or also the dependent line items of multi-item transactions on the control strip, depending on whether a check is issued.
