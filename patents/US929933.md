## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, reliable way is needed to reverse ribbon travel automatically when the ribbon runs out on either spool, without relying on ribbon tension.

**Solution:** A rocker with an arm inside each hollow spool core senses when the last coil unwinds and throws the driving pinion of the empty spool into mesh while releasing the other.

**How it works:** The carriage spring drum drives a ratchet and pinion train through a worm to shafts at the machine ends, whose flexible couplings carry pinions that mesh with a gear on one spool shaft at a time. Each spool has a hollow core with a side slot; inside it a roller on a rocker arm bears on the ribbon coil. When the last coil leaves the free spool, a light spring pushes the roller out through the slot, tipping the rocker so its lower arm enters between teeth of the constantly turning pinion. The pinion climbs around that point as a fulcrum, moving the flexible shaft and rocking a linked pair of arms through their dead centre, so a switching spring throws one pinion into mesh with the empty spool and the other out of mesh with the full one. Spools lift off after removing a thumb nut, a flared skirt guiding the roller back into the core.
