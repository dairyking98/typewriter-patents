## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar segment and hangers must be rigid, and the segment must remove with the top plate for repair.

**Solution:** A segment constructed to tie into the frame and stiffen thin hangers, so the segment can come off with the top plate.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
