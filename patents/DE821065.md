## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (ink-jet writing meter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the printing or marking process for which they are designed characterised by bringing liquid or particles selectively into contact with a printing material; Nozzles emitting recording material; Electromechanical recording systems using a mechanical direct-writing method

**How it works (reading):** A liquid jet is written from a fine glass-tube mouthpiece that is moved by a torsion-elastic band driven by the measured quantity.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink-writing meters can't follow high frequencies because the pointer has too much inertia.

**Solution:** A liquid jet is written from a fine glass-tube mouthpiece that is moved by a torsion-elastic band driven by the measured quantity.

**Features:** [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
