> Description Translated from German Typewriter with type bars each carried by two handlebars The invention relates to a typewriter finitely supported by two links Type rods according to patent 632 552, addendum to patent 63 t 723, in which the types are withdrawn from the action of the keys at the point of imprint and by inertia to be imprinted on a typewriter with the lowest possible noise and to create great durability. With these machines, the driving force is Transferred to the type bar by spindles attached to the one between the types and attack the part of the type rods lying at the front hinge point. The rods consist of one .U-shaped bent sheet metal strip, both of which have free legs connected by a web extending over the middle part of the drive rod are and at the end engaging the type rod two preferably resilient Forming arms. On the one hand, these drive rods are special to those in such machines exposed to great shock and pressure loads, on the other hand they are subject to the Vibration influence occurring when printing the types.
