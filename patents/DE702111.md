## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The drive rod of a two-link inertia typebar machine suffers heavy buckling and impact loads and vibrates, adding noise.

**Solution:** A U-shaped sheet metal drive rod whose legs are pressed together along the part not at risk of buckling, giving rigidity and damped vibration.

**How it works:** The drive rod 45 transmits all forces from key, swing weight and springs to the type bar at its front hinge. It is bent from one strip into a narrow U, with a large section modulus, and the legs are pressed fully together over the middle section to damp vibration. At the ends the web is cut away and the legs form resilient arms, one carrying a riveted bolt that passes through the type bar bore. Spreading the arms releases the type bar quickly.
