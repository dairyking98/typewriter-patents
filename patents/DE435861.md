## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening devices embodied in machines

**How it works (reading):** Lead intermediate layer separates the impact stop from the bearing so sound and blows do not propagate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Metal impact stops for type levers deform and lose function.

**Solution:** Lead intermediate layer separates the impact stop from the bearing so sound and blows do not propagate.
