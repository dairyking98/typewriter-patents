## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to paper-guides for type-writing machines, and more especially 1.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary paper guide bar on the carriage, behind the platen at the print line, holds the sheet against the platen and can be moved to another position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing is hidden at the point where paper is exposed, and the end platen guides do not hold the paper at the printing line.

**Solution:** An auxiliary paper guide bar on the carriage, behind the platen at the print line, holds the sheet against the platen and can be moved to another position.
