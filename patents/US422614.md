## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Blind people need a machine that writes embossed Braille or New York Point.

**Solution:** Electromagnet-driven punches that emboss dots into paper on a grooved platen, with raised characters on the keys.

**How it works:** Keys on spring levers close circuits through cross bars to groups of electromagnets that pull punches against a platen with three (Braille) or two (New York Point) longitudinal grooves, embossing dot patterns. The platen carriage is spaced by an escapement bar, clutch and ratchet bar with a spring-driven arm; line-spacing pawl and locking dog on the platen ratchet.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [For the blind](../features/use-blind.md)

**Parts:** key; finger lever; cross bar; electromagnet; punch; platen; groove; escapement bar; ratchet bar; clutch; battery

**Numbers:** three grooves for Braille, two for New York Point
