## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guide fingers cannot be swung out individually, so replacing one sheet of a stack releases all of them.

**Solution:** Paper fingers are carried on a jointly rotatable axis, can pivot individually or together, and are removable at points on the axis.
