## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The paper carriage of the Blickensderfer machine should be cheaper to make and repair, quicker in use, and adaptable to wider paper.

**Solution:** An independent carriage slides in an open-ended guideway, is normally disengaged from the feed pawls, and has a hinged upper roller that lifts away for inserting paper.

**How it works:** The carriage is a bottom plate with dovetail edges sliding in a transversely open guideway, longer than the machine is wide, with feeding racks underneath. It engages the feed pawls only when a key is depressed, so it moves freely by hand otherwise, and a longer carriage can replace it. Fixed or hinged brackets with rollers support long carriages and fold in for packing. The upper roller is journaled in a hinged section of one standard and a slot with a spring pawl at the other end, so releasing the pawl lifts it clear of the lower roller for manifolding. A knob, ratchet wheel and pawl on the roller shaft turn it, and an adjustable stop finger limits carriage return.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** bottom plate; dovetail guideway; feeding rack; upper roller; lower roller; standard; hinged standard section; spring pawl; knob; ratchet wheel; check pawl; stop finger; support bracket
