## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on type lever drives for typewriters, where the key levers and intermediate levers are mounted in a segment concentric to the typehell bearing segment and the Swing out the type lever by more than 90 °.

**How it works (reading):** Key and intermediate levers sit in superimposed horizontal slots with pivots on a larger circle, so all slots lie in the same planes and can be cut in one saw pass, with type levers swinging over 90 degrees.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever bearing slots in the segment are hard to cut and the levers need play-free storage.

**Solution:** Key and intermediate levers sit in superimposed horizontal slots with pivots on a larger circle, so all slots lie in the same planes and can be cut in one saw pass, with type levers swinging over 90 degrees.
