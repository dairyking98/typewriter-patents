## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** A fixed spring-steel tooth is inserted at an incline through the oscillating body, a loose switching tooth is mounted on a pivoting angle lever with a stop screw, and a downward lever arm strikes the body wall to allow evasion on carriage retraction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The loose switching tooth of the paper-carriage switch must reliably reach its rack tooth with a firm base support and a fixed stop, and it must evade when the carriage is pulled back.

**Solution:** A fixed spring-steel tooth is inserted at an incline through the oscillating body, a loose switching tooth is mounted on a pivoting angle lever with a stop screw, and a downward lever arm strikes the body wall to allow evasion on carriage retraction.
