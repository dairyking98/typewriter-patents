## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters were costly and often hid the writing; a simple, light, cheap machine with always-visible writing was wanted.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal frame with spring-steel typebars in grooved arc-shaped bed, ball-socket pivots and spring links that absorb the key stroke.

**How it works:** Spring-steel typebars swing in grooves of a curved bed, each pivoting on a ball at its lower end resting in a hard screw-in cup bearing. Each bar has a forward arm joined to its key lever by an extensible spiral-spring link, so the key stroke tensions the spring which swings the bar evenly regardless of finger force. Draw-back springs return the bar. Bars are removed by unhooking springs and sliding the ball out, and turning the cup screw adjusts alignment and wear. The carriage is shown at a later part of the text.
