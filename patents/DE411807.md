## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Character-by-character adjustment

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a method for determining the impact energy of the type lever of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The platen is replaced by a measuring device: each type lever strikes a bolt, the impact deflects a writing lever that records the impact energy as a curve on a paper strip, so type levers can be set mechanically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting each type lever for an even impression depends on the skill and eye of the adjuster, so uniform print is hard to achieve.

**Solution:** The platen is replaced by a measuring device: each type lever strikes a bolt, the impact deflects a writing lever that records the impact energy as a curve on a paper strip, so type levers can be set mechanically.

**How it works:** A firing pin under the type lever transmits the impact to a writing lever that stretches a spring in proportion to the impact, and a stylus records that tension on paper.
