## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (magnetic sound dictation device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms producing characters by printing and also producing a record by other means, e.g. printer combined with RFID writer; Record carriers therefor; Recording on, or reproducing or erasing from, magnetic tapes, sheets, e.g. cards, or wires

**How it works (reading):** The drive is kept running on smooth rollers while idle, so the recording medium does not stop and restart with each pause.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Magnetic dictation recorders stop and restart the drive during every pause, which wastes tape and clips the start of speech.

**Solution:** The drive is kept running on smooth rollers while idle, so the recording medium does not stop and restart with each pause.
