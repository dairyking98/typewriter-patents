## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Literature for the blind in Braille has to be produced by reproducing a master plate, which the existing engraving machines cannot do.

**Solution:** A pointer is moved over a master plate of Braille points; its movement is copied by linked slides and a moving bed carrying punches, which reproduce the pattern as indentations on a copy.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Pointer over a chart](../features/sel-pointer-chart.md)
