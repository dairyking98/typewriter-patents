## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Radial typebar machine where the paper is stationary and the keys and type levers travel over it in a carriage.

**Solution:** A circular carriage holding radially arranged type levers and two concentric rings of buttons travels along a stationary table so each type strikes the centre against a rubber roller.

**How it works:** The table has a rubber roller under a long slot, with paper held by belts and turned by a handwheel. The carriage rides on a front wheel and a rear rack bar and carries a ring of type levers fulcrumed low on a ring, standing erect so each type strikes the carriage centre. Each is driven by a lever under the top plate and a button, buttons in two concentric circles with springs raising them. Feeding mechanism under the carriage moves it on per letter.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** table; rubber roller; paper belts; handwheel; circular carriage; type lever; button; lever; ring; rack bar; spring
