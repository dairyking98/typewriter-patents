## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** AC electromagnets hum at the contact face when the current passes zero; shading coils are normally used to prevent it.

**Solution:** Attach an elastic body with a weighted free end to the movable core so its phase-lagged vibration damps the core's chatter.

**How it works:** A fixed core and coil pull a movable core against a opening spring in an electromagnetic switch. The pull pulsates and drops to zero twice per cycle, letting the core open and hum. An elastic strip with a weight on its free end is fixed to the movable core; its vibration lags in phase and presses the core closed during the zeros. Elasticity and end weight are tuned to the supply frequency.

**Parts:** fixed core; magnet coil; movable core; elastic body; opening spring; movable contact

**Improves on:**

- shading coil: mechanical damping by a tuned elastic body instead of a shading coil
