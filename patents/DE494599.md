**How it works (reading):** The counter-stops are a circular segment on a rotating rod, moved into position by one inclined universal rod raised by the key rods, with the adjustable stops on a swingable frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Counter-stops set from a special keyboard needed many separate handlebars and linkage parts, making the machine intricate.

**Solution:** The counter-stops are a circular segment on a rotating rod, moved into position by one inclined universal rod raised by the key rods, with the adjustable stops on a swingable frame.
