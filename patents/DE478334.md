## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that on the axis or the hub of the drive plate a release disc is rotatably arranged, which by means of a release rod pivotable pawl coupled to the drive plate and opposed on its axis the action of a spring can be rotated.

**How it works (reading):** A release disc on the axis or hub of the drive plate is turned against a spring by a release rod and pivotable pawl, lifting the pawls off the ratchet wheel so the carriage return can run silently and fully free.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper carriage return pawls on the ratchet wheel grind noisily when they run on the ratchet, and known devices that lift them off cannot give a complete release of the freewheel.

**Solution:** A release disc on the axis or hub of the drive plate is turned against a spring by a release rod and pivotable pawl, lifting the pawls off the ratchet wheel so the carriage return can run silently and fully free.
