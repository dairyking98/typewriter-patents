## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A rapid typewriting, type-setting and printing machine needs a simple, reliable character-selecting device with few working parts.

**Solution:** A perforated hollow cylinder with selectable pins telescopes into a cylindrical segment carrying movable type-bars so a chosen set of characters is thrown into alignment on the segment's curved surface for printing.

**How it works:** A hollow cylinder on a shaft has parallel rows of conical holes, each holding a loose conical pin that sits wholly inside the wall until pushed inward by a push-rod, and stop bands keep the pins from falling out. A segment of a cylinder carrying many movable type-bars telescopes over the cylinder, and the bars that meet projected pins are moved into printing position and alignment on the segment. A slicker returns the pins to their normal position. Printing is then from the segment's aligned line.
