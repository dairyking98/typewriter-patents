## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar return springs in earlier designs did not return the bar and finger-piece; cushioning springs were separate from return.

**Solution:** A spring placed under tension by the depression of the finger-piece returns the type-bar to and holds it in normal position, and cushions the finger-piece near the bottom of its stroke.

**How it works:** Depressing the finger-piece stretches the spring; its reaction returns the type-bar and finger-piece to rest, and the same spring absorbs the end of the stroke.
