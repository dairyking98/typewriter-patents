## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriters can be packed into the carrying case in the wrong state (carriage unlocked, shift locked, typebars up) because the user forgets the preparatory steps.

**Solution:** A single crank that raises or lowers the typebar segment is coupled to the shift lock release, carriage shift frame latch and centre carriage lock, so folding the machine forces the correct transport state.

**How it works:** A hand crank on a shaft turns a knee joint that lifts the type-lever segment frame into use or lets it fold down. Turning the shaft to the collapsed position pushes a rod that releases the shift lock, hooks arms that hold the shift frame in rest position, disengages the carriage stepping gear and engages a centre lock for the carriage. The case cannot be closed unless these things have happened.
