**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to billing mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Billing mechanism on a rotative platen arrests its rotation at an adjustable stop, with changeable leverage setting how far it turns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing work needs the platen to turn a controlled amount, which depends on the depth of the billing heading.

**Solution:** Billing mechanism on a rotative platen arrests its rotation at an adjustable stop, with changeable leverage setting how far it turns.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
