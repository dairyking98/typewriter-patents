## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Aprons or guides for the printing section concentric with roller platen

**How it works (reading):** The leaf spring is mounted on the guide plate itself and is fully released by the same lever mechanism that lifts the rollers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A spring on the paper guide plate still presses against the platen when transport rollers are lifted, blocking sheet insertion.

**Solution:** The leaf spring is mounted on the guide plate itself and is fully released by the same lever mechanism that lifts the rollers.
