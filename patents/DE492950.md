## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register with several counters)

**How it works (reading):** Amount keys and a row of special keys select which adder receives an amount; a setting lever chooses subtotal or reset, and special counters on a frame are stepped by pawls from push rods.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cash register needs several separate adding and special counters that print individual amounts, subtotals and totals.

**Solution:** Amount keys and a row of special keys select which adder receives an amount; a setting lever chooses subtotal or reset, and special counters on a frame are stepped by pawls from push rods.
