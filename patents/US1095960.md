## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to obtain a noiseless shift upon pressure of the shift key without regard to the rapidity or nature of the shifting movement.

**How it works (reading):** Chains attached to the platen frame arms stop the upward travel without an abutment, so the shift is quiet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shift stops hit an adjustable screw abutment and make noise.

**Solution:** Chains attached to the platen frame arms stop the upward travel without an abutment, so the shift is quiet.
