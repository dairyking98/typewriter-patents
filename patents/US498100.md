## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter needs two characters per key without shifting the heavy carriage.

**Solution:** One key lever is joined to two typebars and its fulcrum is changed so it prints one or the other.

**How it works:** A key lever has two links to two typebars, attached on opposite sides of a shaft. Two bars above and below the lever, linked to arms on a shaft with stops, can swing to form either the rear fixed fulcrum or a front fulcrum near the key. With the fulcrum at one place pressing the key pulls one type arm through a lost-motion turnbuckle link while the other link slides idle, and vice versa. A shift device turns the shaft between the two positions.
