## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Shorthand on a typewriter needs fast writing for taking down speech, and the shorthand conventions such as vowels and verb tenses need visual marking.

**Solution:** Each of several ribbons carries a different ink colour and is positioned so that the colour used by the key selects a shorthand class such as verb tense; the ribbon that was used is moved on by a device so the next colour is brought to the print point.
