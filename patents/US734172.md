## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (rotary stenciling machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stencilling apparatus for office or other commercial use with curved or rotary stencil carriers with a single cylinder carrying the stencil

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates particularly to rotary stencilingmachines, though certain features of the invention are applicable to printingmachines generally.

**How it works (reading):** A rotary stencilling machine with a sheet receptacle and feed to the stencil cylinder, plus a second receptacle that delivers blotter sheets onto the printed sheets between rolls behind the cylinder.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rotary stencil-printing machines need better paper feed and blotter handling and a better inking arrangement inside the stencil cylinder.

**Solution:** A rotary stencilling machine with a sheet receptacle and feed to the stencil cylinder, plus a second receptacle that delivers blotter sheets onto the printed sheets between rolls behind the cylinder.
