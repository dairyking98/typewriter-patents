## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen drive takes up much room at the outer end of the roller carrier.

**Solution:** The platen drive is placed inside the hollow roller tube, with ratchet and pawl turning the roller and a sliding inner tube for longitudinal movement.
