## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type bars in a narrower key system were subject to uneven strain from the actuating linkage, which the inventor wants to avoid.

**Solution:** Key levers and connecting links actuate the type bars in a system narrower than the platen, with the links keeping the type bar actuation free of irregular strain.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
