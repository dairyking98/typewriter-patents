## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating stops on the carriage need to be selectable and held in position by key operated counterstops.

**Solution:** A rack bar on the carriage carries tabulator stops that are projected by a setting arm moved by a key, held in position by spring detents.
