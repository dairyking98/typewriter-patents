> Description Translated from German Tab. Column stop rods, which are used to hold the stop members or riders have grooves directed transversely to the longitudinal direction of the rod are on Known tabs. Likewise, riders, which are manufactured as stamped parts be inserted into these grooves. These designs have the disadvantage that the rider is often too loose sit in the grooves, easily fall out and cause unpleasant disturbances, so that special devices are necessary to prevent these riders from falling out. The present invention overcomes these disadvantages. The column stop bar also has teeth or grooves directed transversely to the rod for receiving the Rider, but also has a transverse to the invention on each tooth side Long, continuous special groove running in the direction of the tooth cut. In the exemplary embodiment, these stop members or riders exist from leaf springs, which can be made from one or more parts. In the present example, these leaf springs consist of two parts that are firmly attached to each other are connected and have self-suspension.
