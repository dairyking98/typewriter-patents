## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type-bar rebounds from its rest and collides with its neighbour, and the key-stroke length and feel cannot easily be varied.

**Solution:** A cushioned key-touch with an adjustable purchase or leverage of the finger-keys on the type-bars, so the key-stroke may be long and easy or shorter and more resistant.

**How it works:** An adjustable leverage point on the key sets how far the type-bar moves per key stroke; cushioning stops the rebound.
