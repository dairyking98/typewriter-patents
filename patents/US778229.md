## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage should return automatically at line end and advance the paper one line without hand action.

**Solution:** A strong return spring is wound step by step by key and space-bar strokes through a ratchet, and a keyboard lever clutches it to the carriage, which then turns the platen on its return.

**How it works:** Depressing any key or space lever swings a frame and lever linkage so a pawl steps a ratchet wheel, gear and spring drum, winding a carriage-return spring while it is disconnected from the carriage. A lever with a key on the keyboard operates a clutch connecting the return spring to the carriage, which it overpowers the advancing spring and returns. During the return, the carriage actuates platen-turning devices through a pawl and ratchet, advancing the paper one line. The platen turning can also be worked by hand from the keyboard.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen; key lever; space lever; pawl; ratchet wheel; gear; spring drum; return spring; clutch; line-spacing lever; platen ratchet
