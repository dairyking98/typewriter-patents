## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Ribbon inking needs a simple, cheap and compact inker that works reliably.

**Solution:** A ribbon inker with a tank, a bail carrying a rubber-covered roller that dips the ribbon, a squeeze roller to remove excess ink and a spool support to wind the ribbon.

**How it works:** The ribbon is drawn through the ink by a roller on a bail, squeezed of surplus ink, guided and wound on a spool carried by a shaft.
