## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Numbering punches for checks did not feed the paper automatically and were not compact or easy to operate.

**Solution:** A two-handled punch with a spring-pawl and notched roller that feeds the paper forward each time the handles close, and a punch pin with a sector-shaped die block.
