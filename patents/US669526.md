## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checking a face against a gage is hard because the body is not distinctly visible until it is in direct contact with the gage, and the gage image sits in an inconvenient position.

**Solution:** A translucent gage plate with a detachable obscuring medium, under fluid pressure, that shows the contact outline of the body; the gage image is projected to a more convenient viewing position.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
