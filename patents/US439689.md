## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A useful, convenient arrangement of letters, punctuation and other characters on the typewriter keyboard.

**Solution:** A bank of keys in four series arranged on an approximation of the printer's type case.

**How it works:** The keyboard has four series (rows) of keys. The first holds K, the numerals 2 to 9 and some punctuation; the second J, Z, B, O, D, E, I, S, F, G; the third X, L, M, N, H, O, Y, P, W; the fourth Q, V, U, T, A, R and punctuation. The layout is meant to approximate a compositor's case. No mechanism is described.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key bank; key

**Numbers:** 4 series of keys

**Improves on:**

- conventional keyboard arrangements: letters laid out like a printer's case
