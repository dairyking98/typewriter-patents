## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to'iinprovements in typewriting machines and more particularly to visible typewritin'g machines, and seeks to provide a simple and effective key-lever.

**How it works (reading):** Key levers connect directly to the type bars, which hang on hangers with wide bearings, so no intermediate sublevers are needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible machines needed intermediate sublevers or bell-cranks between keys and type bars.

**Solution:** Key levers connect directly to the type bars, which hang on hangers with wide bearings, so no intermediate sublevers are needed.
