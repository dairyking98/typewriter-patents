## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention is directed particularly to improvements in those attachments used in connection with well-known forms of type-writers, which govern or control the operation of the key-levers and their application to the printing of different kinds of characters with as few key-levers as possible.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Hammond the supplemental case keys (capitals, figures) stay locked down until the operator releases them by hand after each character.

**Solution:** An electro-magnet trips the locking dogs of the supplemental shift key levers automatically after each character is printed.

**How it works:** The two supplemental key levers raise the type wheel to different heights and are held down by pivoted dogs catching in openings of a cross-bar. Springs pull the dogs into the locking position. A sliding bar, struck by an extension on an electromagnet armature lever worked by the printing action, tilts the dogs back and frees the shift levers. The machine therefore returns to lower case after every printed character.
