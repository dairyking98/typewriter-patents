## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper and carbon must be fed to the platen without handling the sheets by hand, and kept in alignment with the carriage.

**Solution:** An attachment frame with base blocks rests on the typewriter table and carries one or more paper rolls and an interposed carbon roll, mounted so it travels with the carriage.

**How it works:** The paper carrier moves in step with the carriage so the sheet stays aligned with the platen.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** roll carrier attachment; carbon roll
