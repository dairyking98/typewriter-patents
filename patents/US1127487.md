## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typewriters need a simpler way to select a type among many and strike it.

**Solution:** A type cylinder is turned by key-controlled coupled sleeves and slid axially, then struck against the platen by a half-turn crank.

**How it works:** Keys depress springs that uncouple sleeves, so each sleeve can rotate relative to its neighbour through a slot angle, and the angles add through a bevel gear to turn the type cylinder to the sum. A threaded shaft with a slide shifts the type roller lengthwise to choose a column. A spring-driven crank swings the roller on a hammer head against the paper roller, with a pawl stopping the carriage during the strike and a spring letting the roller bounce off.
