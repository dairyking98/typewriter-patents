## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Side gages clamped tightly to the paper shelf are hard to adjust and mar the shelf.

**Solution:** A side gage with a plate clip is attached and detached from the paper shelf and slides along it, held by springs instead of tight claws.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
