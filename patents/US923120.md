## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typebars in a basket collide and waste space when many are packed around a platen.

**Solution:** Group the bars in two concentric pivot circles on an inclined ring-shaped type-bar plate and bend them so the heads clear each other, printing in sight through the ring opening.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot on a ring plate tilted slightly back, converging on a common printing point on the platen, with the writing visible through the central opening. Bars sit in two circles of different radius, so the pivots can be longer and the heads rest in two circles. Each head carries two or three characters, and clearance bends in the bars, sized to head size and obliquity, prevent collisions. Connecting wires run parallel to the bars to bell cranks and key levers, and are kept out of the line of sight.
