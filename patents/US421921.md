## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attachments operated by the leg, e.g. the foot, the knee

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to certain improvements in type-writers, and more particularly to the mechanisms employed to return the carriage to the starting-point and to feed the paper forward for the next line of printing, the object of the invention being to improve the efficiency and simplify the construction of said mechanisms, adapting the same for ap-' plication to type-writers now in use without alteration.

**How it works (reading):** Two knee levers returning the carriage and feeding paper through a lever and cam.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage and feeding paper needs the hands to leave the keyboard.

**Solution:** Two knee levers returning the carriage and feeding paper through a lever and cam.

**How it works:** Two bell-cranked levers beneath the machine are pressed together by the knees; a cord over a friction pulley moves an operating lever at the rear that pushes the spring arm of a Caligraph to return the carriage. A cam on the operating lever lifts a rod that on release feeds the paper roll, even with the carriage at the start.
