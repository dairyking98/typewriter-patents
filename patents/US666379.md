## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After the carriage stops at the end of a line, the type-bars can still print one character over another.

**Solution:** A locking bar at the back, driven by gears from the key-levers, locks the type-bars or printing mechanism when the carriage reaches the end of a predetermined line.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
