## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an attachment for type-Writers, and it is more particularly a device operated by a key for rotating the platen of a type-writer and for moving the typewriter carriage backward a desired distance after the platen has been rotated.
