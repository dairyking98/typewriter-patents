## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for controlling character selection

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type writing machines, and more particularly to the connecting link between the type bars and the key levers.

**How it works (reading):** A one-piece coupling, formed with the wire link including its jaws, is rigidly fixed to the link shank and can be attached to and removed from the heel of the type bar. The link can be adjusted longitudinally to take up wear.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The coupling between a connecting link and a type bar stretches or is weak, and it is hard to fit compactly in the type basket and to assemble and repair.

**Solution:** A one-piece coupling, formed with the wire link including its jaws, is rigidly fixed to the link shank and can be attached to and removed from the heel of the type bar. The link can be adjusted longitudinally to take up wear.
