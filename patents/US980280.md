## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a pivoted reversing-feeler engaging the interior of the wound ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewriting machines and more particularly to ink-ribbon mechanism therefor,the same being shown in the drawings as applied to a stenographic typewriting machine and controlled by the movement of the type-levers.

**How it works (reading):** A universal bar moved by the type levers drives pawls on two vertical ribbon-reel ratchets, and trip plates reverse the feed automatically.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stenographic typewriter needs ribbon feed over a platen bar in front of vertical type levers, with automatic reversal.

**Solution:** A universal bar moved by the type levers drives pawls on two vertical ribbon-reel ratchets, and trip plates reverse the feed automatically.

**How it works:** Key levers swing type levers; their lower ends move a universal bar in front of the row. The bar carries two pawls that turn ratchets on vertical reel shafts, with fixed dogs preventing backward turn. Bell-cranks under the pawls, linked by a longitudinally sliding bar, lift one pawl while letting the other work. Trip plates on the ribbon (fig. 6) shift the bar at the end of travel to reverse direction.
