## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulators required stopping at each intermediate column before reaching a denominational position in a column.

**Solution:** A front-mounted stop system whose stops serve as column or denominational stops, letting one carriage release jump to any letter-space position.

**How it works:** Stops are shifted, not removable, to any position and locked, and can be used either as column stop or as denominational stop. The operator releases the carriage once and it is arrested at the predetermined letter-space position along the line. Margin stops are combined with the same mechanism. The whole tabular mechanism is at the front of the machine.
