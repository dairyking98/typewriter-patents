## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier printing devices were not true writing machines; Burt wanted a machine to print individual letters in place of handwriting.

**Solution:** A type segment on a swinging lever is brought to one printing point by hand while a single impression lever prints and advances the paper band.

**How it works:** Types sit on the underside of a segment carried by a lever pivoted to swing vertically and horizontally. The operator moves the lever over an index to bring the chosen type to the printing point, then presses the impression lever. The paper rides on an endless band that moves crosswise and is advanced one letter space every time the impression lever is depressed. Line spacing is done by shifting the frame carrying the printing mechanism toward or away from the front. Ink pads sit at each side of the impression point and ink all types except the one printing each time. A dial shows inches of paper passed so the operator knows when to stop at line end.
