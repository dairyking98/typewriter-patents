## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers or sets of front or rear rollers each mounted on a separate carrier

**How it works (reading):** A three-armed lever on the carriage, held by a spring and driven by a common control member, lifts the paper guide and disables the carriage locking device together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After the sheet is clamped and aligned, the operator must also operate the carriage release, which means awkward reaching between separate handles.

**Solution:** A three-armed lever on the carriage, held by a spring and driven by a common control member, lifts the paper guide and disables the carriage locking device together.
