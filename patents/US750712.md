## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices actuated when platen reaches the end of a line

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to line-lock mechanism for type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A divided application describing a simpler line-lock mechanism for type-writers, with an arrangement of parts and combinations of devices that stop the carriage at the line end.
