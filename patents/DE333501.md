## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding and printing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**How it works (reading):** The printing unit is driven from one of the adding machine's auxiliary shafts, extended sideways, giving a compact self-contained arrangement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adding machine and separate printing unit need an external motor or long linkages from the hand crank to drive the printer.

**Solution:** The printing unit is driven from one of the adding machine's auxiliary shafts, extended sideways, giving a compact self-contained arrangement.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)
