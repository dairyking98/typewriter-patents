## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to devices for Setting and fixing the type bodies on the type levers of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Each type body is aligned by pressing one character edge against a fixed stop bar of the setting device, so no per-type marks are needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type alignment needs a special mark on every type or sliding stops, and irregular characters do not sit square to the base line.

**Solution:** Each type body is aligned by pressing one character edge against a fixed stop bar of the setting device, so no per-type marks are needed.

**Features:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** stop bar of the setting device
