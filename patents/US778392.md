## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improved platen equipment, designed more particularly for use 'in connection with type-writing'machines of the class exemplified in Patent No.

**How it works (reading):** An extensible platen with a guide for the transfer web and a lock for a vertically swinging billing strip improves billing on flat-platen machines.

**Solution:** An extensible platen with a guide for the transfer web and a lock for a vertically swinging billing strip improves billing on flat-platen machines.
