## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (sprocket-wheel calculator with printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary functional arrangements, e.g. interlocks

**How it works (reading):** A fixed stop on the frame makes the paper line-advance inoperative when the carriage is pushed fully to the right.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** When a calculation result is split across two lines, the paper carriage moves and the total ends up on two lines.

**Solution:** A fixed stop on the frame makes the paper line-advance inoperative when the carriage is pushed fully to the right.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
