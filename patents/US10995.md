## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A cheap simple typewriter that anyone could build.

**Solution:** A four-sided type bar with a row of guide holes is held by a ring fulcrum, turned to the letter, pinned in position and pressed down onto the paper cylinder.

**How it works:** Types are arranged along the four sides of a square-sectioned bar, each side with a row of holes at the same pitch as the types. The bar passes through a fixed ring that acts as fulcrum and has a handle at the far end. The operator turns the bar to bring the desired side and letter over the paper, drops a fixed vertical guide pin into the matching hole, and presses down. Spring-mounted inking pads sit beside the type, and the pressure overcomes the springs to print. The cylinder holding the paper is turned a little after each letter and the sliding frame is moved for line spacing.
