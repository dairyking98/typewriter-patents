## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fastening means for Christmas tree candle holders; Resilient type clasp; Includes separate device for holding drawn portion of lacing having relatively movable holding components or surfaces with integral resilient linking structure therebetween

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention being to provide an inexpensive construction of clamp, which can be sold to the trade at a' very low figure, and which is adapted for instant attachment to any form of type-v writer frame, and when so attached is adapt-A ed for supporting either a circular or an oblong eraser such as is in common use.
