## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple sideways feed pawl arrangement is wanted that also lets the rack be moved back directly, freely and surely.

**Solution:** The feed pawl rides on a spring-drawn base pawl lever and is locked against reverse by a spring anti-reverse piece, yet yields sideways when the rack is pushed back.

**How it works:** A support lever on a fixed shaft is sprung against a limiter and carries a base pawl lever. A feed pawl is loosely pivoted on it with its own spring. A link rod lifts the base pawl lever so its tip takes the rack while the feed pawl lets go; on return the feed pawl takes the rack and the rack advances one step until the pawl hits a wall plate. A spring anti-reverse piece then fits against the pawl to hold it. Pushing the rack back makes the sloped teeth swing the support lever aside, so the rack moves freely to any position.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Sideways feed device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. The invention is characterised in that a feed pawl for the sideways feed is mounted on a base pawl lever, which is drawn by a spring and makes a horizontal movement as needed, and that each time this pawl, engaged with the saw-toothed rack of the sideways feed, completes one feed movement, a feed-pawl anti-reverse device engages it automatically under a spring. Its object is, with a simple device, to make the sideways feed and to allow the reverse movement to be made freely, surely and directly. Brief description of the drawings.
