## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon inking device, which regulates the humidity of the ribbon during use for cash registers, Booking machines and other business machines.

**How it works (reading):** The ink container and pressure rollers are made to fit inside the printing frame: the ink ribbon passes between the rollers after leaving the dye container, a bracket with a compression spring presses the second roller against the first, and a ribbon conveyor arm with a lock wheel advances the ribbon after each print.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing ribbon re-inking machines are self-contained units that cannot be fitted inside cash registers and booking machines, where space is tight.

**Solution:** The ink container and pressure rollers are made to fit inside the printing frame: the ink ribbon passes between the rollers after leaving the dye container, a bracket with a compression spring presses the second roller against the first, and a ribbon conveyor arm with a lock wheel advances the ribbon after each print.

**Parts:** dye container; pressure rollers; spring-loaded bracket; conveyor lock wheel; ribbon conveyor arm
