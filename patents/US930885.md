## Reading (opening only, OCR [])

**Problem:** The typist must use the hands for shift, tabulator and spacing lever.

**Solution:** A treadle-and-cord attachment on the typewriter stand working the shift key, tabular bar and spacing lever by foot.

**How it works:** A treadle on a rocking shaft in the stand is held inclined by a spring. A cord from its inner end goes to a hinged lever, then over an anti-friction roller to a winding drum on an adjustable telescoping standard. A second drum carries a cord with a hook to the carriage return and spacing lever. Further pedals operate the shift key and tab bar.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** treadle; cord; winding drum; telescoping standard; hinged lever; spring
