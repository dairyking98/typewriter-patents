## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists must use their hands to operate the shift key, tabular bar and carriage return/spacing lever.

**Solution:** A foot-treadle attachment on the typewriter stand operates these parts through cords, leaving the hands free.

**How it works:** A treadle on a rocking axle in the stand is held inclined by a spring. A cord from it passes over a roller to a winding drum on an adjustable telescoping standard; a second cord from a coaxial drum hooks to the spacing and resetting lever so pressing the treadle returns the carriage and turns the platen. Other pedals, with resetting springs, press the shift key and tabular bar.

**Features:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** treadle; cord; winding drum; telescoping standard; hinged lever; return spring; hook
