## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case shifting for two types on one typebar needs both a shift and a shift-lock arrangement that work reliably.

**Solution:** Shift the typebar segment rather than the platen, with a shift key and a shift-lock key acting on a two-slot link and latch.

**How it works:** The typebar hangers sit on a segment moved by arms of a rock shaft. A link with an upper and a lower slot connects to the shift lever pin and to a shift-lock sub-lever pin. Depressing the shift key raises the link and the segment so the upper type prints; the segment drops by its own weight on release. Pressing the shift-lock key raises the segment through the sub-lever and an adjustable cam-shaped latch catches the shoulder on the shift lever to hold it up until the shift key releases it.

**Features:** [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** segment; type bar; rock shaft; link; shift lever; shift-lock lever; sub-lever; latch; bracket; spring

**Improves on:**

- conventional shift and shift-lock keys: a segment shift with latch released by the shift key
