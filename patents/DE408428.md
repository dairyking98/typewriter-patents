## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The Invention relates to a `gate direction, which serves the purpose of pausing a to allow certain lower margin.

**How it works (reading):** Pliable strip is fed with the paper and hooks onto the carriage fender, so further roller turning is mechanically blocked at page end.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing sheet needed a lower-edge stop so the user is warned by a mechanical stop, not a sound.

**Solution:** Pliable strip is fed with the paper and hooks onto the carriage fender, so further roller turning is mechanically blocked at page end.
