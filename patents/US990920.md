## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon wears in one stripe, and two-colour ribbons need hand selection of stripe.

**Solution:** Control the height the ribbon vibrator is thrown, by a hand device to choose a ribbon stripe or an automatic cross feed that steps progressively across the ribbon width.

**How it works:** On a Remington visible machine with shifting platen, the ribbon vibrator is lifted at each key stroke. A hand-operated control sets the throw so either of two coloured stripes reaches the print point. For single-colour ribbon an automatic cross-feed device varies the throw progressively to use the whole width. Both work in either platen position, and the platen can shift without altering the vibrator relationship.
