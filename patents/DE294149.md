## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Swing-out stroke-limiting levers for adjusting the lift height of the paper carriage do not work reliably.

**Solution:** Fit adjustable pins on the lifting frame rod that meet a slidable perforated limit rail, which a switching lever shifts so the stroke is limited at either of two heights.

**How it works:** The carriage is carried by a vertically guided frame lifted by a key lever through a lower cross rod. This rod carries two sets of adjustable pins locked with nuts. A thin perforated limit rail slides crosswise in bearings at the rear corners, held by a spring so its openings line up with the pins. With the normal key, the first pins pass through the holes and the second pins stop on the rail's lower side for the full lift. Operating a second switching lever shifts the rail by a bevelled edge so its solid part covers the holes and the first pins stop on it for a smaller lift. The frame returns by its own weight, balanced by a spring.
