## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts in which one paper or set is fed towards printing position from the front of the apparatus

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to hiding devices for typewriters, typewriters, Adding machines or similar machines for inserting multiple hide sheets independently by means of more than two series of feed rollers arranged above the platen.

**How it works (reading):** One common drive serves several feed-roller rows, so several sheets can be inserted independently in a compact unit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hiding devices for several sheets needed two driven feed-roller series per sheet above the platen, which was bulky and costly.

**Solution:** One common drive serves several feed-roller rows, so several sheets can be inserted independently in a compact unit.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
