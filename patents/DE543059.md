## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (united typewriter and calculator)

**How it works (reading):** Parallel cross-shafts carry arms that move all pin support bars into the setting position, with lifting pieces choosing which units act.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Only one arithmetic unit could be engaged at a time by the usual drive; the pin support bars had to be moved selectively.

**Solution:** Parallel cross-shafts carry arms that move all pin support bars into the setting position, with lifting pieces choosing which units act.

**How it works:** Selector levers on the carriage swing the cross-shafts; each shaft arm moves the pin bars of its unit into the setting position.
