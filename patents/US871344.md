## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon obstructs the view of the writing in front-strike or top-strike machines.

**Solution:** The ribbon is held taut away from the printing point, and the part near the point is slackened as the type bars travel toward the platen.

**Features:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
