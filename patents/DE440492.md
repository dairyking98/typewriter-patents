> Description Translated from German Device for stamping steel types for typewriters and calculating machines. The invention . relates to a device for embossing steel types for writing and calculating machines. According to the invention, the position of the upper and lower punches to each other by -two perpendicular to each other and movable to the longitudinal axis of the stamp Adjustable supports. At least one of the punches is cylindrical and stored in a cylindrical, split clamping chuck. Doing so will be beneficial the non-cylindrical punch, for example the upper punch and the associated stamp shell, with two perpendicular to each other and to the embossing surface standing, ground and hardened mating surfaces and the punch with its Mating surfaces pressed against the mating surfaces of the stamp shell by clamping means. By Such a design of the stamp bearing is both the initial setting the machine as well as the replacement of the stamp is made much easier and the highest precision of the product manufactured with the machine is achieved.
