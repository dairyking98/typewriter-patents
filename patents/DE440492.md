## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Setting up and replacing the stamp punches for typewriter and calculator steel types is slow, and precision suffers.

**Solution:** Upper and lower punches are positioned by two perpendicular adjustable supports; one punch is cylindrical and held in a split clamping chuck, and the non-cylindrical punch is pressed against ground mating faces in its shell by clamps, so setup and replacement are easier.

According to the invention, the position of the upper and lower punches to each other by -two perpendicular to each other and movable to the longitudinal axis of the stamp Adjustable supports. At least one of the punches is cylindrical and stored in a cylindrical, split clamping chuck. By Such a design of the stamp bearing is both the initial setting the machine as well as the replacement of the stamp is made much easier and the highest precision of the product manufactured with the machine is achieved.
