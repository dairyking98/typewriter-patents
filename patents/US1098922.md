## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing superscripts and subscripts needs a partial line move that the line-space wheel does not allow.

**Solution:** A handle moves the spring detent off the line-space wheel so the platen can be moved up or down about half a line, with stops limiting travel.
