## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Punch card printing needs about 36 type levers, a force converter and many selector magnets, which makes the machine large and costly.

**Solution:** Ten type levers each carry a segment-shaped type carrier with four types, and a sliding curve plate acting as a zone selector sets the carrier to the required type as the lever swings out.
