## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ink ribbon reversal for adding machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to mechanism for use in adding machines or other machines which employ an ink ribbon for printing purposes, which mechanism provides for automatically reversing the direction in which such ribbon travels step by step as the machine is operated.

**How it works (reading):** Automatic reversing devices controlled by spool depressions that are covered with rigid coverings so reversal happens only when the ribbon is fully unwound from one spool.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink ribbons must reverse at the end of their travel without straining the ribbon, and the reversal timing should not depend on the ribbon alone.

**Solution:** Automatic reversing devices controlled by spool depressions that are covered with rigid coverings so reversal happens only when the ribbon is fully unwound from one spool.

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
