## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage switching device uses a brake spring that always acts, so the carriage is hard to move by hand and the escapement is not quiet when the carriage is reversed.

**Solution:** The constant brake spring is replaced by a brake that acts only when the decimal tabulator or carriage release lever is idle, so the pawls lift off the ratchet during return and the carriage runs quietly.
