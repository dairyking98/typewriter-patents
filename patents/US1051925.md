## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment

**In the patent’s own words:** The principal object of my invention is to improve the means for releasing the paper from its feed devices.

**How it works (reading):** Means controlled by a finger piece to move the platen away from the feed rolls, and to move both the feed rolls and the platen apart, leaving a wider gap.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper is hard to release from its feed devices and insert when a large bulk of paper is used.

**Solution:** Means controlled by a finger piece to move the platen away from the feed rolls, and to move both the feed rolls and the platen apart, leaving a wider gap.
