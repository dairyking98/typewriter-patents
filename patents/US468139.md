## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typists cannot see their writing as it is formed, and carriage, ribbon and line spacing are clumsy.

**Solution:** A machine with reflecting devices lets the operator see each character and whole lines, with spring-and-cord carriage travel and a shifting carriage to bring other types on the same arm into use.

**How it works:** Type arms hinge on adjustable studs in a circular segment and meet at a common point near the centre. A frame on conical pivots has a rounded bar as track for a carriage with a platen. A spring in a barrel pulls the carriage by cord from a cone pulley, and a long knee lever draws it back. A rear bar lets the carriage slide laterally to bring a different type on the same arm into use.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** type arm; circular segment; pivoted frame; carriage; platen; paper-feeding roller; cone pulley; knee lever; reflecting device
