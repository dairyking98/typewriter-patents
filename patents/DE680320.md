Facilities are also known in which the printing table to select the line a longitudinal shift and to Selection of the column can receive a lateral shift. It is also known two to arrange movable printing tables in mutually perpendicular directions, of which one the setting of the receipt to be printed on the print line, the other takes over the setting of the receipt on the printing column.
