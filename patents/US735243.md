## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the typewriter carriage to the start of the line requires the operator's hands.

**Solution:** A flexible cable attached to the carriage is pulled back by a lever train worked by the operator's knee or foot.

**How it works:** A chain, tape or cable is attached to the carriage (e.g. the paper-roll ratchet lever) and always under tension from the carriage return spring. Its other end goes to a post and rod on a lever pivoted at one end and driven by a second lever in sliding engagement near the pivot, so a small movement gives a large travel. A connecting rod links this to a knee lever with a pad (or a treadle). Pulling the cable returns the carriage and can also line-space.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** flexible cable; spring; lever; sliding joint; ball bearing; connecting rod; knee lever; pad; treadle
