## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide means whereby the movement of a body of air, or the creation of air waves, is utilized for the forcible impaction of printing symbols against a receiving surface.

**How it works (reading):** Rubber key bulbs connected by tubes to force bulbs under a convex type basket, so pressing a key pneumatically propels the type face to the paper.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Seeks a typewriter where finger impact is transmitted by air waves to drive type against paper.

**Solution:** Rubber key bulbs connected by tubes to force bulbs under a convex type basket, so pressing a key pneumatically propels the type face to the paper.

**How it works:** Soft rubber bulb keys with embossed characters connect through hollow stems, elbows and rubber tubes to force bulbs in a convex type basket. Squeezing a key sends an air wave to its force bulb, which expands and drives a type face against the receiving surface. The same manually created air currents also operate inking devices and advance the carriage, and a modified form rotates the platen pneumatically.

**Features:** [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [ELEM:rubber-type](../features/elem-rubber-type.md), [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** rubber key bulb; hollow stem; metal elbow; rubber tube; force bulb; convex type basket; keyboard; carriage; inking device
