## Summary

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper carriage switching device for typewriters, Typewriters and the like, in which a ratchet wheel with two pawls for controlling of the switching step cooperates, both of which are pivotable in the plane of the ratchet wheel and are mounted on a pawl carrier that can be pivoted transversely to the ratchet plane.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known paper carriage switching with spring-loaded pawls creaks and makes the switch lock hard to work.

**Solution:** Two pawls on a pivoting carrier engage a ratchet wheel; the pawl not in use is swung out of the way when the carrier pivots, avoiding springs.

**How it works:** Pivoting the pawl carrier across the ratchet plane disengages one pawl, and the other pawl is displaced into rest.
