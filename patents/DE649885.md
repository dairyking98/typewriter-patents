## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper carriage switching device for typewriters, typewriters and the like. The invention relates to a paper carriage switching device for typewriters, Typewriters and the like, in which a ratchet wheel with two pawls for controlling of the switching step cooperates, both of which are pivotable in the plane of the ratchet wheel and are mounted on a pawl carrier that can be pivoted transversely to the ratchet plane. In these known paper carriage switching devices, the Shift teeth pivoted in the plane of the ratchet action by special springs. This caused creaking noises when working with the machine, which were found to be very annoying. It also made the switch lock work difficult. According to the invention, these disadvantages are now eliminated by the respectively not in engagement with the ratchet when pivoting the pawl carrier The pawl inevitably counteracts the action of the ratchet wheel on the other pawl the direction of the ratchet action is pivoted into its rest position.
