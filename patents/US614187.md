## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the printing or marking process for which they are designed characterised by selective application of heat to a heat sensitive printing or impression-transfer material using thermal heads by selective transfer of ink from ink carrier, e.g. from ink ribbon or sheet

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writer ribbons, and has for its object to produce aribbon which may be used to print in differentv colors and which will not readily wear out nor be cut by the types.

**How it works (reading):** A compound reversible ribbon has two colour layers joined face to face, and turning it selects the colour.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon wears out, is cut by types, and cannot print two colours.

**Solution:** A compound reversible ribbon has two colour layers joined face to face, and turning it selects the colour.
