## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on a line switching device for typewriters, in which the platen by its in the keyway: a pinion engaging, with corresponding The pawl provided with wedge surfaces is indexed.

**How it works (reading):** A coupling pawl with wedge surfaces is moved by a lever on a common pivot whose start position is fixed by a micrometer stop, so the platen advances exactly by the set line distance.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-space switching stops are adjustable and absorb the full thrust of the stroke, so spacing is imprecise and parts get damaged.

**Solution:** A coupling pawl with wedge surfaces is moved by a lever on a common pivot whose start position is fixed by a micrometer stop, so the platen advances exactly by the set line distance.
