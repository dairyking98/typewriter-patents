## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** A numbered sleeve scale on the paper rail, guided by a type-lever segment or pointer, shows the exact stop point of the carriage for each digit column.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Figure columns in billing do not line up exactly when the paper carriage is set by hand.

**Solution:** A numbered sleeve scale on the paper rail, guided by a type-lever segment or pointer, shows the exact stop point of the carriage for each digit column.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
