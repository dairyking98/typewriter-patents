> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a control device for the carriage movement of typewriters and the like with edge stops (edge plate) and tab stops arranged on the carriage, which interact with a pivotable stop lever on the machine frame. Compared to the known control devices of this type, there is something new Invention is that the stop lever extending in the longitudinal direction of the car both in a vertical as well as in a horizontal plane, overcoming an elastic one Resistance is pivotable. This has the advantage that the stop lever can serve three purposes at the same time, namely ■■ "■ - borrowed as a stop lever for the edge stop, as a stop lever for the tab stop and as a shock absorber. That way will not only simplifies the construction by eliminating several parts and thereby the Production cheaper, but it is also achieved that when using the machine transfer unavoidable shocks when operating the stop lever to this and parts sensitive to impact are protected.
