## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple, durable typewriter suited to writing in bound blank books for official records, more legible and compact than handwriting.

**Solution:** A type-wheel carrying short spring-held pivoted type-bars on its rim is mounted on an oscillating frame inside a vertically adjustable carriage that carries the wheel to the book page.

**How it works:** A base carries a fixed frame with uprights and guide standards; a carriage slides vertically in it and holds an oscillating elbow frame in which the type-wheel drive shaft is journaled. A segmental rack on the oscillating frame engages a pinion on the wheel shaft, so rocking the frame turns the wheel while carrying it to and from the work surface. The wheel hub is keyed to a grooved shaft. Short type-bars are pivoted around the wheel circumference on lugs, each held back by a spring to the wheel flange, and carry the type on an offset at their outer ends. A keyboard operates the machine; an alternative wheel construction is shown in the later figures (text truncated).
