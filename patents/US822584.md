## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (bookkeeping machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a machine for bookkeeping or posting and to a system of accounting, and especially to a machine for and a system of posting items to a ledger-account and keeping the ledger accurately balanced at all times.

**How it works (reading):** Loose-leaf ledger sheets are carried on sprocket-driven bars in a frame so that the item is written and posted to the account without hand copying.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Posting many sales and cash items to ledger accounts by hand is laborious, and the ledger must be kept balanced at all times.

**Solution:** Loose-leaf ledger sheets are carried on sprocket-driven bars in a frame so that the item is written and posted to the account without hand copying.
