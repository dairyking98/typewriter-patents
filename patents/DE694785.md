## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known pulse-evaluation gear trains for telegraph selectors have many gears and dead motion, and are bulky.

**Solution:** Helical gears on two parallel axes, where a longitudinal shift of one gear turns the other, so pulse combinations rotate a type wheel by fixed fractions of a turn with fewer gears.
