## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon adjusted transversely

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and its object is to improve upon those parts thereof commonly known as the carriage, the carriage-feed, the feedescapement, the ribbon -feed, and the alarm mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A typebar machine with draw-bar keys, a rack escapement of two toothed wheels with a spring-loaded stud connection, adjustable return spring, tiltable carriage and alarm bell.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improve the carriage, carriage feed, escapement, ribbon feed and alarm of a typewriter.

**Solution:** A typebar machine with draw-bar keys, a rack escapement of two toothed wheels with a spring-loaded stud connection, adjustable return spring, tiltable carriage and alarm bell.

**How it works:** A key drops a draw-bar in parallel motion, a bar across all draw-bars rocks elbow levers and links to swing levers carrying the feed-rack. The rack engages two similarly toothed wheels on the carriage beam, joined by studs in elongated slots with a spring, with a ratchet and retaining pawl, so that striking a letter or space key feeds the carriage one step. A spring hooked in one of several holes sets the rack return tension. The carriage can be tilted upward to inspect the writing, and ribbon spools and an alarm bell are driven from the same parts.
