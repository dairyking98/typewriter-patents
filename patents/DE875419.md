**How it works (reading):** A work-slip holder moves in step with the line-switchable roller, grips slips at its rest position, and does two prints in one stroke sequence.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A printing unit must print each amount once on a control strip and twice on a sheet fed by the computer, without a second printing unit.

**Solution:** A work-slip holder moves in step with the line-switchable roller, grips slips at its rest position, and does two prints in one stroke sequence.
