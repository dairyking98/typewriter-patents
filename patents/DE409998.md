## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Backspace on the carriage needed a simple, cheap downshift device.

**Solution:** Backspace key pushes an angle lever along an arc slot so a pawl engages the carriage rack, and a stop lug prevents more than one tooth per stroke.
