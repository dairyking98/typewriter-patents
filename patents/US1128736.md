**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Writing or like instruments in holders or guides

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to pro ide means for instantly changing the activities of thernachine from writing ordinary matter to automatically running off a line, or part of it line, of dots, dashes, or other characters, and then at any esii i point, instantly resuming the of matter in the ordinary way.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing lines of dots, dashes or other characters needs a repeated key press for each character, wasting time and energy.

**Solution:** Holding one or more key levers depressed while the carriage travels automatically repeats the characters to run off a line, with means to switch the machine between normal writing and line running.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
