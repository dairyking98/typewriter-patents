**How it works (reading):** The keycap is cast in an opaque rubber layer with a clear rubber layer over it, the character plate sits between them, and vulcanising fixes the plate in one solid piece.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Character plates mounted under or inside rubber keycaps tend to loosen or shift under stroke.

**Solution:** The keycap is cast in an opaque rubber layer with a clear rubber layer over it, the character plate sits between them, and vulcanising fixes the plate in one solid piece.
