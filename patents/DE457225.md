## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cleaning types with brushes and gasoline splashes ink onto machine parts and does not remove dirt in the recesses.

**Solution:** A dab pad of rough permeable fabric filled with absorbent material is soaked in solvent and dabbed to wet the type faces.
