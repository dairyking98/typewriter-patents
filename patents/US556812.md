## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, cheap machine is wanted for writing in record books and for general work.

**Solution:** A flat base frame resting on the book page with a line-spacing frame, carriage and curved typebars with toothed segments driven by key levers.

**How it works:** A rectangular base frame rests on the book page with a side rack. A line-spacing frame rides on it with a pinion and spring pawl that steps the line, with a release lever for free movement. A carriage inside has a feed pinion and sliding spring-metal feed pawl worked by a bail-shaped spacing lever. Typebars pivot concentric with a V-shaped type-bar guide and have toothed segments meshing with segments on the key levers; pressing a key swings the typebar and operates the spacing lever. A ribbon on a frame moves with the line-spacing frame.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** base frame; rack bar; line-spacing frame; pinion; line-spacing pawl; release lever; carriage; feed pinion; feed pawl; spacing lever; typebar; toothed segment; key lever; type-bar guide; ribbon-guide; ribbon frame
