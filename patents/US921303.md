## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the carriage is released and drawn by its spring drum, the ribbon is fed while the types are inactive, leaving unused ribbon sections that waste the ribbon as it is used up.

**Solution:** The ribbon feed linked to the carriage drive is changed so the ribbon is not wasted on these released-carriage stretches; the full mechanism is cut off in the OCR.
