## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement in the arrangement of the type lever on typewriters with an arched type lever bearing segment, which reduces the risk of the type lever colliding, which is particularly large in the type lever mounted in the middle of the type lever bearing segment, and thereby increases the writing speed.

**How it works (reading):** The type levers are cranked outward so that the included angle between neighbouring levers in the stop position is larger, and each lever lies in the plane of its fastening tab.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers on an arched segment collide when adjacent levers strike together, limiting writing speed.

**Solution:** The type levers are cranked outward so that the included angle between neighbouring levers in the stop position is larger, and each lever lies in the plane of its fastening tab.
