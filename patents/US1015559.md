## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In an Oliver-type machine with a three-position platen shift, the shift frame needed a simple support whose weight helps it return to centre and whose parts are protected.

**Solution:** The shift frame hangs on pivoted links from the top plate of a hollow base, with arms passing down through holes into the base.

**How it works:** A shift frame carries the paper carriage and platen above a hollow machine base. Two shift levers drive a rock shaft with a crank pin working in cam plates, which swing the frame forward or back to bring one of three types per typebar to the printing point. Rigid arms at each end of the frame go down through holes in the top wall into the base. Hangers pivoted to plates on the top wall and to the lower ends of the arms swing the frame forward and back from a central position. The frame rises as it swings, so its own weight returns it to centre, helped by springs on the shift levers. Stop nuts on a stem limit travel, and a roller supports the front.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** shift frame; paper carriage; platen; hollow base; top plate; depending arm; hanger link; supporting plate; shift lever; rock shaft; crank pin; cam plate; return spring; stop nut; stem; supporting roller

**Numbers:** 3 types per typebar; 2 sheets of drawings

**Improves on:**

- US837611 (Gross, Oliver typewriter): the hangers and arms are placed inside the hollow base, where they are protected
