## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A supplemental ink ribbon must stay in the right relation to the main ribbon and record sheets, and it catches the front edges of sheets on insertion.

**Solution:** A shield or holder partly encircles the platen or feed roll and guides the supplemental ribbon so that the two record sheets pass on opposite sides, with means to prevent impression on the back of the outer sheet.
