## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Remote-controlled typewriters that select a character by pulse duration need precise contacts.

**Solution:** A double-armed contact lever, driven by motor and clutch magnet, connects a distributor contact only in its central position, to select characters by signal duration.

**How it works:** Touching a key plate on the encoder energises coils at transmitter and receiver, starting the motors and closing the clutches. A contact lever sweeps a distributor plate and switches disks with insulating strips of different lengths set the pulse duration per character. The matched receiver selects its type-lever magnet by timing.
