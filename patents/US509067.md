## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Connecting rods between key levers and typebars need a slight length adjustment to give uniform and properly forcible typebar throw.

**Solution:** An extensible connecting rod whose two sections are clamped together by a cross-holed screw and a nut.

**How it works:** One rod section has a transverse hole through which a screw shank passes; the screw head has a cross hole through which the other section slides. Tightening the nut on the far side draws the screw and clamps the second section against the first, locking the length. A groove or offset keeps the sections from turning, and the hole wall is serrated to resist slip.

**Parts:** rod section; screw; nut; key lever; type-bar
