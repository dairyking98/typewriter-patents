## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon switching for cash registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon switching device for cash registers, calculating machines and the like, in which the ink ribbon is effective by an opposite side Pawl gear is gradually unwound from one reel and wound onto a second reel and the switching movement of the ribbon is automatically reversed after it has run out of one reel.

**How it works (reading):** The feeler levers lean on the inner coil flanges instead; they only act when the ribbon has run out, and then move a pawl gear that reverses the ribbon direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Feeler levers that touch the ribbon wear and chafe it through where they contact it.

**Solution:** The feeler levers lean on the inner coil flanges instead; they only act when the ribbon has run out, and then move a pawl gear that reverses the ribbon direction.
