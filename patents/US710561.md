## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing-machine supports, and particularly to the type 1o designed for supporting a book or other work in proper relation to a platen and a traveling printing mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** An adjusting mechanism for the book or work support with fewer parts and less weight that moves the support platform uniformly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The book or work support adjusting mechanism of earlier designs was heavy and had too many parts.

**Solution:** An adjusting mechanism for the book or work support with fewer parts and less weight that moves the support platform uniformly.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** book support platform; adjusting mechanism
