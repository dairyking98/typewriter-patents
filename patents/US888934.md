## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods on side surfaces thereof, e.g. fixed thereto the types or dies being movable relative to the bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines in which electricity is employed for operating certain of the devices, particularly to machines whose type-actions and characters are electrically connected to a distant keyboard from which they are operated.

**How it works (reading):** Operate each typebar by a solenoid, shift the platen electrically and hold it up without draining current, and drive the carriage with a reversible motor of uniform torque, all wired to a remote key card.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A complete typewriter fully equipped with keys needs to be shown working in a shop window with the operator hidden, driven electrically from a distant keyboard.

**Solution:** Operate each typebar by a solenoid, shift the platen electrically and hold it up without draining current, and drive the carriage with a reversible motor of uniform torque, all wired to a remote key card.

**How it works:** An Underwood front-strike machine has upper and lower case types on bars hung on a curved rod and worked by bell cranks and key levers with return springs. Under the frame, a solenoid with a loosely fitting core hooked to each key lever pulls the bar; the core pulls far harder at the end of the stroke for a clear imprint, and adjustable means control the power. Pressing any key at the remote keyboard closes a circuit through the matching solenoid. A shift key electrically raises the platen and a catch holds it so the battery serves the type actions. A reversible motor with a pole-changer and spirally wound armature drives the carriage at constant pull in writing and in return, partly turning the platen for line spacing at each return.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [USE:telegraph-printer](../features/use-telegraph-printer.md)

**Parts:** solenoid; core; tube; type bar; bell crank; key lever; platen; reversible motor; pole-changer; key card

**Improves on:**

- spring-driven carriage and manual key operation: electrical operation from a distant keyboard for show-window display
