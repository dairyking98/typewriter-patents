## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** The primary object of the present invention is to secure a quick release of the carriage after the impact of the type and to provide a machine in which the defects in the type-written matter due to the peculiarities of the touch of different operators will be reduced to a minimum or even wholly eliminated.

**How it works (reading):** Escapement arrangement giving a quick release of the carriage after type impact so touch differences do not cause double or overlapping impressions.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a two-dog escapement the holding dog must clear the rack tooth before the carriage is free, so fast typing makes letters overprint.

**Solution:** Escapement arrangement giving a quick release of the carriage after type impact so touch differences do not cause double or overlapping impressions.

**How it works:** Described only in part: a stepping feed-dog normally engages the rack and a holding-dog engages when a key is struck; the invention changes the dog action so the carriage is released quickly. The machine is a typebar machine with a rack on arms and toothed wheel, the platen on a shifting carrier. The remainder is not in the text.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Key touch and even impression](../features/bar-touch.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** base frame; type-ring; type-bar; key-lever; universal bar; carriage; platen carrier; platen; rack; toothed wheel; stepping feed-dog; holding-dog

**Improves on:**

- common stepping-dog and holding-dog escapement: quicker carriage release after impact
