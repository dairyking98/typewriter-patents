## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a lever drive, preferably with a key drive, for example for office machines, in particular the drive of the type lever.

**How it works (reading):** A single resilient handlebar of spring wire, between a two-armed lever and the key button, stores energy and returns the key and lever to rest; it acts as a self-locking element and its loading changes as the key goes down.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Office machine key drives use many levers, links and joints, with play, friction, noise and heavy weight; rope or band drives are too flexible for uniform, fast typing.

**Solution:** A single resilient handlebar of spring wire, between a two-armed lever and the key button, stores energy and returns the key and lever to rest; it acts as a self-locking element and its loading changes as the key goes down.
