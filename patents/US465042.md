## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Soft platens suit ordinary work, harder platens suit manifolding, and changing between them is slow.

**Solution:** A carriage whose platen can be removed and replaced quickly by backing off a screw journal and nut.

**How it works:** The carriage frame has front and rear rods and end bars. The platen is a hollow wooden core with a rubber sheath and perforated end heads, on a rod with conical bearings that seat on conical journals. To remove it, a nut is run toward the end bar and a screw with conical journal is withdrawn from its seat, releasing the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** carriage frame; platen; hollow wooden core; rubber sheath; end head; rod; conical journal; screw; socket piece; nut
