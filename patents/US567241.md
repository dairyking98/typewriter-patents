## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The quick sharp blow of a typebar causes noise and blurred prints.

**Solution:** Drive an end-thrust type-carrier by a toggle straightened by a key-driven cam, so the type is pressed on to the platen with no concussion.

**How it works:** A key lever carries a cam roll running in an inclined cam path which brings a toggle on its centers with multiplied power. The toggle link pushes the end-thrust type-bar to impression and the velocity falls to zero as it reaches the platen. A guide makes the carrier move straight at the platen. Pads absorb the return stroke, and a spring returns the parts.

**Features:** [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)

**Parts:** key lever; cam roll; cam path; toggle; link; end-thrust type-carrier; guide; spring; pad

**Improves on:**

- Kidder application Serial 508,614 (April 1894): adds the cam so the toggle is brought positively to dead centre
