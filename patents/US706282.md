## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting new sheets each time is slow when taking depositions or dictation direct on the machine.

**Solution:** A feeder frame attached to the carriage that pulls a continuous strip or strips of paper from a roll directly into the typewriter.
