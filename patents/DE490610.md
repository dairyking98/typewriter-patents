## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculator zero-clear sign printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter, calculating machine o.

**How it works (reading):** The clear sign is printed automatically at a chosen point by a machine movement, such as the carriage return or the paper carriage, and the type printing device is positively connected to those parts, with a scanning device reading the digit rollers' zero position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Clear-character printing of counter zero positions depends on manual operation and can be forgotten, and devices that stop the type at zeroing do not print at the desired location.

**Solution:** The clear sign is printed automatically at a chosen point by a machine movement, such as the carriage return or the paper carriage, and the type printing device is positively connected to those parts, with a scanning device reading the digit rollers' zero position.

**How it works:** Digit roller scanning reports zero; the clear-character type is brought to the print point by the carriage or carriage-return movement.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** digit roller scanning device; clear character type; paper carriage; carriage return; switch bracket; feeler levers; key lock rail
