## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Changing ribbon feed direction in the earlier Seib patent was inconvenient.

**Solution:** A notched shaft cam on the feed arm swaps which of two pawls engages the ratchet flanges of two ribbon spools.

**How it works:** The ribbon is wound between two side-by-side spools whose inner flanges are ratchet wheels. A swinging arm on the spool axis, driven by a cord from the carriage, carries two pawls on one shaft. A second shaft with two notches at different angles bears on the pawl arms, letting one pawl drop while holding the other up, with a middle position that frees both. A milled head turns it by hand and a pin limits travel.
