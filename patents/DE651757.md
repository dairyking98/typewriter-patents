**How it works (reading):** The table top is built from several layers of a synthetic wood-like mass with different fillers, some layers with depressions that are bridged by the top layer to form sound-absorbing cavities.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Felt and rubber pads under typewriter table tops harden and lose their sound-absorbing effect, and the shocks of fast typing reach the fingertips.

**Solution:** The table top is built from several layers of a synthetic wood-like mass with different fillers, some layers with depressions that are bridged by the top layer to form sound-absorbing cavities.
