## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an optical and acoustic display device the end of the sheet on a typewriter, which eliminates the aforementioned disadvantages.

**How it works (reading):** A large gear on the platen carrier drives optical and acoustic indicators only when the platen turns in the writing direction, and yields when turned the other way.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing end-of-sheet indicators block platen rotation or need extra actuation for each sheet.

**Solution:** A large gear on the platen carrier drives optical and acoustic indicators only when the platen turns in the writing direction, and yields when turned the other way.
