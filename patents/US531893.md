## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators are not warned when the end of the sheet is near and they may type off the paper bottom.

**Solution:** An electric alarm whose circuit is closed through the conducting-covered paper roller when the paper edge passes the contact arms during a line-feed stroke.

**How it works:** A standard clamped on the frame carries a rocking tubular spindle and an insulated inner spindle, each with a platinum-tipped spring arm. Part of the paper roller is covered with tinfoil. Each line-feed stroke of the pawl hits a lever stud, which rocks the spindles so both arms press on the roller. While paper covers the conducting part, nothing happens; once the paper passes beyond both arms, the battery circuit through roller and frame closes and a bell rings. The lower arm is adjustable to set how near the paper end the warning comes.
