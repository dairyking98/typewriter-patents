Arithmetic units with two drive wheels are already known that specifically are used for invoicing work. For the most part, the billing work was done so far done on arithmetic units with Ouer drive unit. The present invention now has compared to the known arithmetic units Similar types have the advantage that the balancing work by means of presetting pieces the counter-controlled clutch of the second drive wheel for normal vertical computing is kept permanently switched off by a manual adjustment device. It is through it possible to carry out vertical arithmetic work without the presetting pieces of the counters having to adjust. The facility is also characterized by its particular simplicity, in that a control piece for the clutch also locks the drive wheel at the same time and adjusts the display bezel.
