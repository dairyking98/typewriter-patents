## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines, and more particularly to antifriction-bearings for the swing ing type-bars.

**How it works (reading):** Opposable spherical bearing surfaces hold antifriction balls for each typebar, and a member acting along the bearing axis adjusts them toward each other; hangers can be removed individually.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Untrue alignment or wear in the type-bar bearings causes binding and strain; the bearings must be quickly adjustable.

**Solution:** Opposable spherical bearing surfaces hold antifriction balls for each typebar, and a member acting along the bearing axis adjusts them toward each other; hangers can be removed individually.
