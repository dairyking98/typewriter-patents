## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for the variable stroke movement of the ribbon fork for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Two movable links permanently but independently connected to the ribbon fork, so one stays in range of the adjustable drive while the other is set.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the stroke of the ribbon fork forced one link out of the drive range, which caused misadjustment on later changes.

**Solution:** Two movable links permanently but independently connected to the ribbon fork, so one stays in range of the adjustable drive while the other is set.
