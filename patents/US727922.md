## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** The aim of the invention is to provide a simple and inexpensive table which will give proper support to the machine and the traveling carriage when in action and which may be contracted in size and adapted to inclose the machine when it is not in action.

**How it works (reading):** A table with hinged extension leaves that support the book carriage when open and form cabinet ends with a flexible top when closed over the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriter tables are bulky and the carriage needs room to travel when the machine is not in use.

**Solution:** A table with hinged extension leaves that support the book carriage when open and form cabinet ends with a flexible top when closed over the machine.
