## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device for producing form-like records on a registration tape In business machines controlled by counting cards, such as printing tabulating machines, the need often arises to let their printing device work so that forms of a predetermined length can be used or the registration tape after completion of a series of work sheets of the same length can be broken up. The entries on the forms and the registration Band - carried out under the action of counting cards, which are usually sorted before its passage through the tabulating machine in the sorting machine by groups. However, it is in the nature of things that the number of cards in individual groups either exceeds the capacity of the respective form length set or is insufficient for other groups to fully utilize the form length. It is therefore necessary that the machine can be easily and quickly adjusted to a form length corresponding to the average of the number of cards in the groups. It has already been proposed to switch the form accordingly a previously set length both under the supervision of a line counting as also to be submitted to the group control facility.
