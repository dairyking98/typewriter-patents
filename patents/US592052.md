## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter should be very compact and efficient.

**Solution:** Compact machine with converging type-bar carrier plates, elbow-lever keys and a shifting carriage.

**How it works:** Type-bars are pivoted on adjustable plates that converge so all strike a common printing point, each worked by a key stem and elbow lever with a link to the bar below its pivot. The carriage is shifted to bring either of two sets of characters on the bar to the platen. A swinging frame with fixed and wedge-faced pawls steps the platen for line spacing.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; handwheel; ratchet wheel; swinging frame; fixed pawl; swinging pawl; type-bar; carrier plate; bed plate; elbow lever; key stem; link; spring
