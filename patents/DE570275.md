## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** Both rails pivot on axes in front of the platen and fold upward, so arbitrary-width sheets can be exchanged in one operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper holding rails pivot behind the platen, so sheets are hard to slide under them and changing base sheets takes many steps.

**Solution:** Both rails pivot on axes in front of the platen and fold upward, so arbitrary-width sheets can be exchanged in one operation.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
