## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-lever springs are slow and awkward to assemble because each spring is tensioned individually.

**Solution:** Key-lever springs sit between wavy metal bands on a bracket and are all tensioned together by one pressure plate.
