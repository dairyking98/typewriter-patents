## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check cutoff from cash register)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists essentially in the fact that the check slip Check cut off by conveyor disks cooperating with counter disks is moved, which are provided on their circumference with flats, of which at the normal position of the conveyor disks is always opposite the counter disks, so that the check slip is unhindered as it moves forward during printing can be pushed between the panes.

**How it works (reading):** The cut check is moved out by eccentric disks working with conveyor disks that have flats, so the flats face the counter disks in the normal position and the strip can advance unhindered.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cut checks jam the check path when the strip advances during printing.

**Solution:** The cut check is moved out by eccentric disks working with conveyor disks that have flats, so the flats face the counter disks in the normal position and the strip can advance unhindered.

> Description Translated from German Device for conveying out the cut from a check slip Checks from cash registers and the like Removal of the check cut from a strip from cash registers and the like, by which a clogging of the device by the advanced during printing Check slip is prevented. The invention consists essentially in the fact that the check slip Check cut off by conveyor disks cooperating with counter disks is moved, which are provided on their circumference with flats, of which at the normal position of the conveyor disks is always opposite the counter disks, so that the check slip is unhindered as it moves forward during printing can be pushed between the panes. The main parts of the device are shown in Fig. I in a side view, Fig. 2 shows the switching device by itself. The cut check is moved forward by two small eccentrically mounted disks 445 (FIGS. 1 and 2) which work together with conveyor disks 446 which are provided with a toothed wheel 447 on one side.
