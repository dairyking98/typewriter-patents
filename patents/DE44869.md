## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text only partly survives; it claims a new lever mechanism that prints types on an elastic plate while also working the spacing.

**Solution:** A type push lever on a carriage presses the selected type of an elastic type plate onto the paper and at the same time trips the carriage feed.

**How it works:** A rigid frame of side cheeks and rods on a bed plate can be tilted by pegs in notches. A transport roller with a smaller spring-loaded companion roller carries the paper, with a metal strip as printing base and a scale for paper alignment. The carriage slides on a toothed and flat bar, a spring-housed toothed sleeve advancing it along the rack as writing proceeds, with a pointer showing the print position. The carriage has a light metal frame with a hinged cover plate carrying a guide (drawing) plate and a fork-shaped writing lever with a telescopic arm ending in a punch with a spherical pressure face. The punch is placed over the character on the elastic type plate and pressed to print, and the carriage is stepped on. A colouring plate lies under the cover. Remaining details are cut off.
