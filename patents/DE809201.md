## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mounting or connecting key buttons on or to key levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that all Parts of the control button, in particular the button button, the button lever and the intermediate elastic member, preferably a helical compression spring, such can be made pluggable that they are exclusively by the limitation the relative movement serving member are held together.

**How it works (reading):** Button, lever and compression spring are made pluggable and held together only by a locking member that limits their relative travel; the base is cross-shaped on the key-lever leg.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Elastic key-button assemblies are hard to take apart, so replacing the button or its spring takes too much time.

**Solution:** Button, lever and compression spring are made pluggable and held together only by a locking member that limits their relative travel; the base is cross-shaped on the key-lever leg.
