## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of our invention is to provide means separate from the tabulating mechanism for effecting a release of the carriagc and for insuring accuracy in the operation.

**How it works (reading):** Means separate from the tabulating mechanism release the carriage, and tabulating stops are automatically locked in cooperative relation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage release is tied to the tabulating mechanism, and tabulating stops need to lock automatically in cooperative relation.

**Solution:** Means separate from the tabulating mechanism release the carriage, and tabulating stops are automatically locked in cooperative relation.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** tabulator key; release key; tabulating stops; carriage release mechanism
