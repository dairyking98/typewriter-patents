## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to paper guides or holders employed in connection with type writing machines for the purpose of holding down the paper upon the platen and guiding thesame during the feed.

**How it works (reading):** Pairs of spring-loaded rods bear on the paper beside the platen ends, can be swung clear for inserting paper, and adapt to different sheet and card sizes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guides hide writing, crease the paper and strike the type.

**Solution:** Pairs of spring-loaded rods bear on the paper beside the platen ends, can be swung clear for inserting paper, and adapt to different sheet and card sizes.
