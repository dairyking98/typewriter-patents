## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Support devices for long carriage frames take up a lot of space when detached from the machine frame.

**Solution:** Each side support is three hinged U-section sheet-metal rails that fold flat against the carriage frame and latch to the machine frame.
