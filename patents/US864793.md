## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards, envelopes and other forms are not held in close contact with the platen, so they cannot be written on right up to their edges.

**Solution:** Spring-mounted marginal paper fingers slide on a rod above the platen, with a central card holder on a support, holding the paper or card against the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
