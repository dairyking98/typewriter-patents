## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening devices embodied in machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a shock-absorbing stop for the switchable Part of a typewriter that has a supporting shaft.

**How it works (reading):** The stop head is cut into several offset parallel plates with holes, trapping air cushions between them to absorb impact.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Helical shock stops are expensive to make and damp poorly, and laminated springs leave uneven air gaps.

**Solution:** The stop head is cut into several offset parallel plates with holes, trapping air cushions between them to absorb impact.
