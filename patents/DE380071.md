## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Printing device for cash registers and the like with removable color and counter pressure rollers. The invention relates to a printing device for cash registers and the like with removable inking and counter pressure rollers and essentially consists of that the inking and counter-pressure rollers are mounted in a removable frame, that they are removed in the simplest way from the associated cliché rollers and then can be removed from the machine. This arrangement enables thorough coloring of the inking rollers, without any special skill being required, as well as the safe one and easy insertion of a new check slip into the printing device. An embodiment of the invention is shown in the drawing in Fig. i shown in side view and in Fig. 2 in top view from above. Fig. 3 shows the device in section along line xx of Fig. 2. Two inking rollers 4. and their counter-pressure rollers 6 are mounted in a frame which consists of the plates i and 2, which are held together by the cross bars 3.
