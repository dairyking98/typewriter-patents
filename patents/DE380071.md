**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing rollers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing device for cash registers and the like with removable inking and counter pressure rollers and essentially consists of that the inking and counter-pressure rollers are mounted in a removable frame, that they are removed in the simplest way from the associated cliché rollers and then can be removed from the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Inking and counter-pressure rollers in cash-register printers were hard to clean and to clear for new check slips.

**Solution:** The inking and counter rollers mount in a frame that can be removed from the machine, with an eccentric shaft setting pressure.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Printing device for cash registers and the like with removable color and counter pressure rollers. The invention relates to a printing device for cash registers and the like with removable inking and counter pressure rollers and essentially consists of that the inking and counter-pressure rollers are mounted in a removable frame, that they are removed in the simplest way from the associated cliché rollers and then can be removed from the machine. This arrangement enables thorough coloring of the inking rollers, without any special skill being required, as well as the safe one and easy insertion of a new check slip into the printing device.
