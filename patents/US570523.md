## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage feeding, release and line-spacing mechanisms of a typebar machine needed improvement (less rack and pinion wear, exact shift throw).

**Solution:** A spring-loaded hinged feed rack rides on an anti-friction roll to limit its engagement, and a platen carrier with stopped pins gives an exact shift and line-space throw.

**How it works:** A toothed rack hinged on a rod in the main carriage engages a pinion of the escapement, held down by a coiled spring. A bar on the rack rides on a roller of pitch-line radius so the teeth do not bottom. The platen carrier hangs on rock-shaft arms and rests against ears on the carriage, with adjustable screws to make its throw exact. A shift-rail and bell-cranks lift or lower the platen for capitals. A cam-regulated driving pawl normally held clear of the platen ratchet gives line-spacing.
