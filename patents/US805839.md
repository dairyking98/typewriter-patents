## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ball or roller bearings; Means to synchronise movements; Ball cages for multiple rows of balls

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to antifriction-bearings, and has for its object to provide a means for maintaining the relative positions between two or more antifriction-rollers and between said rollers and the carriage adaptedto be guided by and travel on said rollers.

**How it works (reading):** A roller holder with perforated forks keeps the antifriction rollers at fixed distances between the upper and lower track-bars that guide the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Antifriction rollers guiding a carriage tend to creep together, reducing the spacing between them.

**Solution:** A roller holder with perforated forks keeps the antifriction rollers at fixed distances between the upper and lower track-bars that guide the carriage.
