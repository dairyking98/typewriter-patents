## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (photographic lettering machine for film titles) · **Subject (patent classes):** Photographic composing machines having fixed or movable character carriers and without means for composing lines prior to photography with manually-adjustable character carriers to enable characters to be photographed in succession

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is mainly suitable for manufacture -v # oil cinema titles and other filmstrip labels.

**How it works (reading):** Transparent type plates on a common type disk are selected and projected onto film, which is advanced one frame per character; plates are detachable so they can be checked and replaced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting and spacing type for photographic typesetting is cumbersome and hard to check.

**Solution:** Transparent type plates on a common type disk are selected and projected onto film, which is advanced one frame per character; plates are detachable so they can be checked and replaced.
