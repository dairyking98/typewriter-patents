## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Two different pre-printed form webs must be fed to the platen together, and one web shifts or tears relative to the other.

**Solution:** Both web types (stapled block forms and single sheets) come from separate containers and pass over a common clamping point on the carbon-paper trolley, so the webs are stretched before the platen and shift less.

**How it works:** Two form webs enter one common clamping point on the carbon trolley, which pre-stretches them and keeps them from shifting.
