## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hammond's shuttle needed to be light, strong and quick to swap for another language set; changing it previously meant lifting the shuttle out of its working plane to disengage the shuttle-arm.

**Solution:** A ring-form hard-rubber shuttle reinforced by an inner metal flange molded in, driven by a radially releasable shuttle-arm finger so the shuttle can be twirled to a different font set without lifting.

**How it works:** The shuttle is an unbroken hard-rubber ring around a ring-shaped anvil on the centre post, with a metal flange along its inner side; perforated projections on the flange let the rubber flow through and act as rivets. The flange runs between the two halves of a split anvil (the upper half removable to take the shuttle out). Driving arms swing front and rear, turning speed-dogs about the post; a speed-dog engages a pin on the shuttle-arm, whose upturned finger sits in a notch in the flange and swings the shuttle to bring the chosen character to the print point. For changing character set or language, a slide pulls the finger radially out of its notch so the shuttle can be twirled to another set (notches are provided per set) without being lifted.
