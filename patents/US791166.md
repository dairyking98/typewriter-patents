## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the linotype keyboard, the dogs that hold up the descending yokes do not always return quickly, so the yokes are not arrested.

**Solution:** The dog is moved positively, either by an arm on the yoke or by a cam acting on an extension of the dog, at the proper time in the cycle.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
