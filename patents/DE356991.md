## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Writing-tables with substantially horizontally extensible or adjustable parts other than drawers, e.g. leaves with sliding or unfolding parts other than leaves or drawers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a known roll-up file cabinet in connection with a table located inside the cabinet that can be folded up and down, which when not in use, covers the typewriter compartment and is not visible when the shutters are raised.

**How it works (reading):** A plate fixed in the cabinet carries a hinged table top with support feet; it folds down over the typewriter compartment and latches with a snap lock.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Roller-shutter cabinets do not give enough working table space.

**Solution:** A plate fixed in the cabinet carries a hinged table top with support feet; it folds down over the typewriter compartment and latches with a snap lock.
