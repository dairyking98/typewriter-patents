## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making corrections or insertions requires moving the platen back by hand.

**Solution:** A key lever, linked through a bell crank and levers, moves a pivoted back-feed pawl that engages the carriage rack so the carriage can be moved in reverse one printing space at a time.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md)
