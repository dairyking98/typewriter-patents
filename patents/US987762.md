## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike typebars on a small-arc segment allow short light bars with long pivots, but then the actuating links must fan out to the keys, causing torsional strain and uneven touch.

**Solution:** Mount the typebar hangers on a small-arc segment and the sub-levers on a second segment of larger radius, with sub-levers graduated in length, all in one frame unit that drops into the machine.

**How it works:** A segmental frame has a typebar hanger segment shaped like part of a cylinder, with hangers in parallel planes holding uniform short curved typebars on long pivots. Below and behind it a sub-lever segment of greater radius carries graduated sub-levers on a pivot wire in slotted guides. Connecting links join the top of each sub-lever to its typebar and the middle to a key lever. Each sub-lever's lower end picks up a straight universal bar when the type head has travelled a set distance, operating the ribbon and escapement at a fixed moment. Sub-lever dimensions grow in proportion from the centre to the sides to equalise the touch.
