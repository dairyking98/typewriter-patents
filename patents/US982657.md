## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing on small labels and cards: the operator cannot cover the whole label or see the whole line.

**Solution:** Transparent fingers on a carrier that shifts with the platen hold the label against the platen on both sides of the print point.

**How it works:** A carrier slides vertically in the frame cross bar behind the typebar segment, with upright arms bearing celluloid fingers on opposite sides of the printing point. The fingers press lightly so the platen and label slide beneath them during letter-spacing. A tongue on the carrier loosely engages the shift rail, so operating the shift key moves carrier and fingers with the platen. The arms are detachable and the carrier has shoulders to stop it.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** carrier; arm; transparent finger; shift rail; platen; ribbon vibrator
