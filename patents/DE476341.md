## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With an inclined typebar segment the line-height pointer leaves little room for a device making the line-space pawl engage earlier or later, so only two spacings were possible.

**Solution:** An adjustment lever on the carriage side wall, positioned by an eccentric with knob and detent washer, sets the pawl's starting position so one stroke turns the ratchet by different angles.

**How it works:** The line pawl hinges on a lever turning on the platen axis and is pushed by a spring against a lug of the setting lever. The setting lever's forked notch rides on an eccentric turned by a knob, with a flattened detent washer holding each line-space position. Different positions move the lug and so the pawl's start, changing the angle the line ratchet and platen turn. At stroke end the pawl's bevel hits a fixed stop, limiting feed; only the thin lug passes the narrow gap by the pointer.
