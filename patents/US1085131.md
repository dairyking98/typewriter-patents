## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in copy holders for typewriting machines and is directed more particularly to the equipment of what is known as a fiat platen or downwardly printing typewriter with copy holding means adapted to be automatically operated to line space the copy as the typewriter is manipulated to line space the writing on the work sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A copy holding drum on the traveling machine is driven automatically from the normal line spacing, with variable gearing to change the copy feed relative to the typewriter line space and setting means to register the copy.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Flat-platen typewriters need the copy to be line-spaced by hand, and copy whose line spacing differs from the record cannot be matched.

**Solution:** A copy holding drum on the traveling machine is driven automatically from the normal line spacing, with variable gearing to change the copy feed relative to the typewriter line space and setting means to register the copy.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
