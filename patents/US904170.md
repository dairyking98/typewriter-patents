## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A multi-field ribbon (e.g. two colours) must be controllable so the desired field covers the printing point, and uniform ribbons should be usable in more than one longitudinal line to exhaust them.

**Solution:** The ribbon-vibrator operating lever is fulcrumed on an adjustable supporting lever that a hand control shifts, changing the lever's normal position and hence which ribbon field rises to the print point.

**How it works:** On the Monarch typebar machine the usual fixed-fulcrum vibrator operating lever is replaced by one fulcrumed on a supporting lever. A hand-actuated control moves the supporting lever, bodily shifting the operating lever between two normal positions. The lever connects to a link of the vibrator actuating mechanism so the shift does not disturb that mechanism. The shift needs little force and the lever is held positively in either position.
