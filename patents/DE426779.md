## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (picture transmission writing device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Reproducing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** An electromagnetically moved low-mass armature presses a heat-meltable dye film onto writing paper on a roller synchronised with the transmitter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electrochemical image-recording writers need moist paper, and electromagnetic pens or spark writing disturb the receiver.

**Solution:** An electromagnetically moved low-mass armature presses a heat-meltable dye film onto writing paper on a roller synchronised with the transmitter.
