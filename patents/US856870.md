## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars in front-strike machines return slowly and the key touch is heavy when return springs are tensioned throughout the stroke.

**Solution:** Attach a return spring to each typebar heel that only comes under tension in the last part of the stroke near the printing point.

**How it works:** A coiled spring is fixed to the typebar heel and its wire extends rearward through an aperture in a plate, ending in a stop head. Early in the stroke the spring slides slack through the plate. Near the print point the head abuts the plate and the spring stretches, so on release it snaps the bar back quickly. Adjustable stop heads and staggered coils let the springs sit side by side.
