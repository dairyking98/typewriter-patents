PELION, a citizen of the United States, residing at Georgetown, in the county of Scott and State of Kentucky, have invented new and useful Improvements in Type-Writer Ribbons, of which the following is a specification. This invention relates to improvements in typewriter ribbons and more specifically to a ribbon of this character of particular material and weave. It is the object of this invention to produce a typewriter ribbon which is characterized by its thinness, durability, and ink retaining properties, as wellas its freedom from lint.
