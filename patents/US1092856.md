## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbons of ordinary weave are thick, give up too much ink, and shed lint that clogs the type.

**Solution:** A very thin ribbon of pure silk, dyed before weaving and woven with a close ground and filling, is impregnated with ink so it prints clearly, keeps ink well, and stays free of lint.
