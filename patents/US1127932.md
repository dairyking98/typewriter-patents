## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter needs a line lock that locks the keys at the end of a line.

**Solution:** A locking bail locks the shiftable sublevers at any shifted position to lock the keys.

**How it works:** A carriage on rails is pulled by a spring drum and checked by an escapement rack and dogs on a dog rocker operated by a universal bar. Typebars pivot in a shiftable segment driven by key sublevers. A pivoted locking bail on the shift frame engages the sublevers, operated by a bell crank.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** locking bail; sublever; shift frame; bell crank; escapement rack; feed dog; dog rocker; universal bar; spring drum

**Improves on:**

- Smith application of 25 April 1913: adds line lock to that type action
