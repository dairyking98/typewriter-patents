## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Printing letters or phonographic characters in one movement without movable types or a press.

**Solution:** A plate of raised characters is moved by finger plates and ratchets over the paper, and a hinged lever with a pin presses the chosen character onto the paper.

**How it works:** A table lists the characters, and a metal plate carries raised letters matching the table in number and form. A lever hinged beside the table has a pin above the plate and a set-screw to regulate pressure. Hinged blocks, springs, wires and an elbow-lever connect to finger-plates that adjust the position of the type plate. A ratchet and pawl on a sliding beam govern movement of the paper holder, which slides endwise with springs to hold the paper and apertures for line spacing. The text is truncated and the OCR is rough.
