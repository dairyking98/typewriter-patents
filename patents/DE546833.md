## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with an interchangeable paper cart that can be raised and lowered into a switchover position.

**How it works (reading):** Relief springs on bell-crank-style levers under the switching levers take up the extra weight with the heavy carriage and are held inoperative by a releasable lock for the light carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching levers feel very unequal and heavy when a heavier paper carriage is used.

**Solution:** Relief springs on bell-crank-style levers under the switching levers take up the extra weight with the heavy carriage and are held inoperative by a releasable lock for the light carriage.

In typewriters of this type, the switching levers are loaded very differently, depending on whether an ordinary platen trolley or a heavy booking trolley is used. As a result of the different loads, the force to be used to strike the toggle keys is very unequal. The handling of the switch is therefore for the writer, if z. B. the heavy booking car is used, very tiring. This disadvantage is eliminated according to the invention in that relief springs for the toggle levers that are only used when using a heavier one Carriage come into effect while using a normal weight car be held inoperative by a releasable locking device.
