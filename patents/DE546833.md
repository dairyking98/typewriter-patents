> Description Translated from German Typewriter with an exchangeable paper cart that can be raised and lowered into a switchover position. The invention relates to a typewriter with an interchangeable paper cart that can be raised and lowered into a switchover position. In typewriters of this type, the switching levers are loaded very differently, depending on whether an ordinary platen trolley or a heavy booking trolley is used. As a result of the different loads, the force to be used to strike the toggle keys is very unequal. The handling of the switch is therefore for the writer, if z. B. the heavy booking car is used, very tiring. This disadvantage is eliminated according to the invention in that relief springs for the toggle levers that are only used when using a heavier one Carriage come into effect while using a normal weight car be held inoperative by a releasable locking device.
