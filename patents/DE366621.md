## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a typewriter, in the case of a movable support frame for achieving different writing positions .

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon drive is split between the fixed frame and the moving support frame, bridged by a slot-guided parallel link; an adjustable arm point changes ribbon stroke, and a grooved resilient arm with grooves of different depth locks the ribbon-field selection.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a machine whose platen support frame moves between writing positions, the ribbon drive linkage is disturbed by the shift and the ribbon stroke is hard to adjust.

**Solution:** The ribbon drive is split between the fixed frame and the moving support frame, bridged by a slot-guided parallel link; an adjustable arm point changes ribbon stroke, and a grooved resilient arm with grooves of different depth locks the ribbon-field selection.
