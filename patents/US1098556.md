## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**How it works (reading):** A vertically movable platen gets vertical movement for line spacing and rotary movement for letter spacing, held upright at all times.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen must rotate for letter spacing and move vertically for line spacing while staying upright.

**Solution:** A vertically movable platen gets vertical movement for line spacing and rotary movement for letter spacing, held upright at all times.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
