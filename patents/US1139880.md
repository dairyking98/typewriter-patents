## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen movement between lines is large.

**Solution:** A vertical-axis platen both rotates and lifts, returning by gravity.

**How it works:** The platen rides on a rotating carriage on ball races, with guides, rack bars and a pawl spider lifting it for line space, returning by gravity.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen; ball race; rotating carriage; guide; rack bar; spider; pawl
