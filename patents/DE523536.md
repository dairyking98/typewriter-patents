> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a typewriter table with a support plate for the machine which can be lowered under the table top and is suspended by means of parallel handlebars. In the known devices of this type, the locking of the typewriter support plate in the raised position was inadequate. For example, a hook lock has become known, but this has the disadvantage that, owing to the considerable weight of the typewriter, the swinging support plate cannot be prevented from shooting out over the hook lock. Several attempts are required before a final locking of the support plate in the raised position is achieved. Also, vibrations of the device when using the typewriter can trigger the hook lock and cause the whole to collapse, especially if only one hook lock has come into effect with two hook locks due to an imprecise design. The invention consists in that a support plate or a similarly acting device is hinged to the rear edge of the support plate, which rests on a cross bar on the rear wall of the table frame when the support plate is pulled up and z.
