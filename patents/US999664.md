## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding typewriter attachment) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by the provision of additional devices for producing a punched or like record, e.g. simultaneously

**In the patent’s own words:** Its object being to provide a simple, accurate and economically constructed machine of this character, the mechanical elements and assemblage of the adding mechanism being such that it can readily be attached t-o any standard design of typewriter having 'a spring actuatedshiftable paper carriage under escapement control, whereby said escapement is lv:ipable of being released by a tabulator mec anism.

**How it works (reading):** A shallow adding unit sits under the typewriter and is linked to its keylevers, so the typewriter adds while tabulator keys release the escapement.
