## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines of the day lacked rigid alignment, even action, and uniform ribbon advance, and had many small shortcomings in carriage, margin and cylinder mounting.

**Solution:** A long list of improvements to one typewriter: rigid typebar alignment, adjustable connecting rods, uniform ribbon feed, adjustable spring tension, margin stops, bell and key lock, and a swing-up interchangeable platen.

**How it works:** The opening only states the objects: adjustable typebar connecting rods, crosswise and lengthwise ribbon advance per stroke, carriage spring tension adjustable at any moment, ribbon spools on wooden spools, a spacing lever hung to stay level, roller bearings on carriage ways, detent-and-rack escapement with margin stops, a bell and a lock of all keys at line end, a platen hung in fixed bearings so it swings up to inspect work and is removable so hard and soft platens interchange, and an adjustable paper apron and pressure roller. The mechanisms themselves are beyond the text shown.
