**Solution:** Single-arm key levers act through a two-arm intermediate lever pivoting on the common axis, cutting key travel to 15-17 mm.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Type lever drive. The object of the invention is a "rypenhebelantrieb for typewriters, particularly for such Schreibinaschinen that are to be used, etc. because of their small dimensions measurements as a portable typewriters. In the previously known typewriters of this type, the key levers are designed with two arms and their rear end engages directly in a guide slot of the door lever, which is swung out when the associated key is pressed and, finitely, struck against the paper. As a result of the spatial relationships caused by the small dimensions of such a portable typewriter, however, the two arms of the key lever must have such an unfavorable relationship that the key has to be pressed down on average by about 28 to 30 mm in order to bring about the desired deflection of the type lever. The typewriters of this type known up to now therefore require a disproportionately large expenditure of force in order to operate them and also only allow rather slow writing.
