## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**How it works (reading):** A locking lever engages a gear on the spring drum axis while the guide plate is unlocked, and a nose holds the drum against rotation, so the spring cannot unwind during removal.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the paper carriage is removed, the carriage spring can unwind uncontrolled.

**Solution:** A locking lever engages a gear on the spring drum axis while the guide plate is unlocked, and a nose holds the drum against rotation, so the spring cannot unwind during removal.

**Features:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
