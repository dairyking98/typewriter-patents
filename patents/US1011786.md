## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage escapement is slow, and the keys need more finger pressure than they should.

**Solution:** A reworked escapement with a rack and dogs gives quick feed and light key pressure, with positions for single and double spacing.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
