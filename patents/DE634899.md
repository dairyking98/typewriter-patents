There are already typewriters with laterally adjustable guide rails become known, which are arranged one above the other to simultaneously several sheets of paper to lead to the write abutment. In these known embodiments, however, could be one on top of the other arranged pairs of guide rails are not independent of each other in any arbitrary Position to be determined.
