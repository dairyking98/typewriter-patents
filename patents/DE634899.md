## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles characterised by being applied to printers having transversely- moving carriages and coupled to the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper side edge guide on typewriters with a device for duplicating with laterally adjustable guide rails for simultaneous feeding of several Sheets of paper for the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Each pair of guide rails sits on its own mounting rail on the carriage behind the paper table, so each pair can be placed independently, letting sheets of different widths sit side by side on either side of the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** On typewriters with stacked guide rail pairs for multi-part forms, the pairs are not independent and cannot be set to any arbitrary position.

**Solution:** Each pair of guide rails sits on its own mounting rail on the carriage behind the paper table, so each pair can be placed independently, letting sheets of different widths sit side by side on either side of the platen.

**How it works:** Each rail pair is clamped to its own mounting rail, so the sheets are fed to the writing point at independently set positions.
