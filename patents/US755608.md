## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulator dog stays projected if the operator presses a tabulating key after the last tappet has passed, and then jams the carriage return.

**Solution:** The tabulating dogs are given a means of being retracted so the projected dog does not block the carriage on its return.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
