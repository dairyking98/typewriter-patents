## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** In two-ribbon Underwood machines the active ribbon rubs the idle one and smears ink, especially when capitals are printed.

**Solution:** A ribbon-carrier threaded so the front ribbon lies in front of its carrier, plus a spring finger on the shift rail that pushes the idle carrier down in upper case, and latches that lock the idle spools.

**How it works:** The forward ribbon is threaded so the print-point portion lies in front of the carrier, so the two carriers only slide past one another. A spring finger pivoted on the platen shift rail presses down the idle ribbon carrier when the platen is shifted up, so the active ribbon clears it. Latches on the frame, operated by arms from the ribbon-shift mechanism, lock the idle spool set and release the active set. A shiftable connector links the universal bar to either ribbon lever or to neither for stencils.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier; vibrating lever; universal bar; connector; spring finger; platen shift rail; spool latch

**Improves on:**

- [US926014](../patents/US926014.md): uses pivoted latches instead of fingers to lock the idle spools
