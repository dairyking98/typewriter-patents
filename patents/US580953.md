## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists mainly in a novel construction of the bearin g for the ink-ribbon spools and in details of construction of the mechanism for operating the inkribbon spools and of the guards below the papercarriage for supporting'the letter-gage and protecting the platen and paper thereon from the ink-ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** Slotted notch bearings on the frame hold the spool shafts, which lift out without tools, and guards below the carriage protect the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink-ribbon spools need a bearing that can be detached without tools and that keeps the ribbon off the platen.

**Solution:** Slotted notch bearings on the frame hold the spool shafts, which lift out without tools, and guards below the carriage protect the platen.
