## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Clamping several forms with the paper guide depends on the paper width, so the pressure rollers must be placed to suit.

**Solution:** An extra pressure roller and a foldable upper retaining device on the platen hold a second and third form, which are then clamped by a lower roller.
