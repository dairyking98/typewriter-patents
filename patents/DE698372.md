## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**How it works (reading):** A recording mode counts spaces and missing line length on a sample; playback distributes the missing space evenly over the word spaces via counter-driven gearing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Justifying lines by test typing wastes time and paper.

**Solution:** A recording mode counts spaces and missing line length on a sample; playback distributes the missing space evenly over the word spaces via counter-driven gearing.
