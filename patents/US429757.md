## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants the paper held on the cylinder so lines run around the cylinder and the line spacing is the carriage advance.

**Solution:** A cylinder on a sliding carriage is turned one letter by a pawl on a feed lever struck by every type-arm.

**How it works:** The cylinder is journalled parallel to the slideways with clamps holding the paper. Each type-arm blow also presses down a feed lever hinged on the carriage, which has apertures the type strikes through. A hook pawl on the lever engages a ratchet wheel fixed to the cylinder shaft, with as many teeth as letters on a line, and turns the cylinder one tooth when the lever rises under its spring. A spring detent steadies the wheel between blows. The carriage moves along a fixed rack to space between lines.
