## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Stamping device. The invention relates to a stamping device with one that can be displaced in the vertical direction above a counter pressure plate Stamp that is driven by a linkage that is moved by means of a foot lever receives, and the essence of a stamping device according to the invention is in that of the drive linkage for the punch in the horizontal direction taking place backward movement of an inking unit is also derived., which at its movement inks the stamp. So it is possible with a stamping device according to the invention without replacing any parts a very large one Execute number of stampings, so that the stamping device in particular is suitable for business tills for stamping the receipts, especially as the Operation of the stamp device is very simple.
