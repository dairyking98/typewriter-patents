**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a stamping device with one that can be displaced in the vertical direction above a counter pressure plate Stamp that is driven by a linkage that is moved by means of a foot lever receives, and the essence of a stamping device according to the invention is in that of the drive linkage for the punch in the horizontal direction taking place backward movement of an inking unit is also derived.

**How it works (reading):** The backward stroke of the stamp drive linkage, driven by a foot lever, also moves an inking unit that inks the stamp face each cycle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A receipt stamp needed inking without replacing parts for a very large number of stamps.

**Solution:** The backward stroke of the stamp drive linkage, driven by a foot lever, also moves an inking unit that inks the stamp face each cycle.
