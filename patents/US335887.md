## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Brackelsberg wants uniform letter spacing and adjustable space width; the machine also serves for stamping matrices.

**Solution:** A single row of types on a sliding holder is brought opposite a printing bar by placing the beak of a handle rod in a notch beside the character on a register.

**How it works:** Types are fixed in a single row on a holder that slides along a stationary guide-bar. The operator moves the holder with a rod ending in a handle and a knife-edged beak, ball-jointed to the holder. A spring-raised lever carries ledges holding a register of the characters; each has a triangular notch whose inclined faces guide the beak to the exact point so the matching type sits opposite the printing bar. Pressing the lever and bar presses the paper against the type. The ledges are placed at different heights on two or more rows to make notches wider. Notches may be replaced by conical holes with a pin beak.
