## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in the construction and arrangement of a machine for making molds from which to cast stereotyped plates for printing, and for making stereotyped plates, as will be hereinafter more fully set forth.

**How it works (reading):** Dies on levers stand around an annular frame, each lever is thrown horizontal by a key pressed at the outer edge, bringing its die to the centre, and a hammer on a pivoted lever then strikes the die.
