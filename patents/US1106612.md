## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter has no support for a roll of paper that moves with the carriage, and a roll holder must fit a machine of any type.

**Solution:** A roll-carrying attachment with a carriage that rides on a rod fixed to the machine by brackets, with folding roll supports and a severing means.

**Features:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
