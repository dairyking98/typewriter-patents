## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typebar machine needed a shiftable typebar segment for upper and lower case with guided, adjustable parts.

**Solution:** The whole typebar segment is raised by a rock shaft worked from two shift key levers, one for a single capital and one that locks the segment up for a line of capitals.

**How it works:** Typebars on removable hangers on a segment strike rearward and upward at the front of the platen. Key levers drive sub-levers and spring-sheet-metal links to the typebars; spring tension is regulated by adjusting screws. The segment legs connect to arms on a rock shaft with a resilient arm and roller. One shift key lever acts through an elbow lever and cam end under the roller to raise the segment temporarily; a second raises and latches it in a notch.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
