## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars and their mechanism are hard to get at for repair, because they are fixed in the machine.

**Solution:** The basket, key levers and type bars are mounted in a removable auxiliary frame that seats in grooves of the main frame, so the type bar mechanism can be lifted out to give access to the parts.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md), [Typebar and type-head construction](../features/bar-typebar-construction.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** main frame; auxiliary frame; type basket; key levers; universal bar; supporting lugs; annular grooves; side plates
