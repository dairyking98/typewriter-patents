## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**How it works (reading):** The cleaning roller sits on a bell that tumbles on the axle inside the roller, so no part protrudes and the roller can be replaced by pulling it off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The fixed axle nut of a type cleaner roller protrudes, blocks access to type, scratches machine parts and forces uneven pressure.

**Solution:** The cleaning roller sits on a bell that tumbles on the axle inside the roller, so no part protrudes and the roller can be replaced by pulling it off.
