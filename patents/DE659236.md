## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage tension bands must stay attached when the carriage is removed, and reconnect automatically.

**Solution:** A scissors coupling with a spring-spread pair of notched noses on the carriage and the tension band end, engaging on placement.

**How it works:** The spring-spread scissors notches hook the band end when the carriage is set on the frame and release when lifted.
