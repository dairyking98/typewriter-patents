## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Platen shifting is usually done through cumbersome lever gearing, or by a rotating wedge piece acting via intermediate levers.

**Solution:** A longitudinally sliding wedge push rod, guided between a fixed frame part and a moving part of the lifting device, raises the platen directly.

**How it works:** The platen is carried in angle levers pivoting on a shaft; a roller at their link runs on a rail of a rigid frame (rail, levers, shaft). The shift key moves a push rod through an angle lever; its wedge surfaces run up on rollers and lift the rail frame, swinging the platen up so other types strike. A double wedge (top and bottom) gives a large lift for little rod travel. Release returns the rod by spring or the platen's weight; more rods and wedge sizes give more than two positions, and extra keys or encryption-machine capital keys can couple in by linkage.
