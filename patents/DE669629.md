## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register check strip coupling)

**How it works (reading):** A coupling links the control-strip print-disc drive to its driving arm, which a control disc set for the machine type moves; a special adjuster holds the arm in the inoperative position at a certain setting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Independent amounts must be printed on the control strip and dependent items must sometimes be left off, which needs a simple switching device.

**Solution:** A coupling links the control-strip print-disc drive to its driving arm, which a control disc set for the machine type moves; a special adjuster holds the arm in the inoperative position at a certain setting.
