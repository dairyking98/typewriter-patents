## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Addition to DE322034: the stiffening plate construction of the collapsible typewriter is bulky.

**Solution:** Brace the key and typebar mechanism directly on the housing side wall, with key levers hinged on the same shaft as fixed transmission levers and disengaging by eccentric stops when folded.

**How it works:** The housing has a main member and a hinged cover forming a box. A U-shaped key frame swings about a rod; each key lever has two parts hinged on that rod with projections that engage when a key is pressed. Folding the key frame disengages the stops, then the cover closes. Open, the key frame is held at a set distance from the table by the front wall, and the footprint is about half that of DE322034.
