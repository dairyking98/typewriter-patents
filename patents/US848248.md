## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar pivot bearings give lateral play, and the bearings and hangers are costly to build to fit.

**Solution:** The type-bar is a single sheet-metal stamping with flat, parallel pivot ends, pivoted between a bifurcated hanger built from a flat-sided body and two flat side plates that can be stamped and ground true.

**How it works:** The hanger body and side plates are stamped from sheet metal and ground; the type-bar is ground slightly thinner than the body so it swings freely between the flat faces, held by a pivot stud and screw.
