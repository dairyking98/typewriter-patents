## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter A frame that is simple to manufacture and easy to work with.

**How it works (reading):** The frame is built from flat stock plates for the top and bottom, stiffened by rails and joined by square or round columns, all cut and drilled, so the parts are easy to make and assemble.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cast typewriter frames are complicated, shaped to the gear location and hard to access, which makes installing the transmission difficult.

**Solution:** The frame is built from flat stock plates for the top and bottom, stiffened by rails and joined by square or round columns, all cut and drilled, so the parts are easy to make and assemble.

**Features:** [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** base plate; top plate; stiffening strips; square and round columns; frame pieces; electromagnet; type cylinder
