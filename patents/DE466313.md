## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a simple carriage switch for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage with the platen is carried on pivot levers and guide rails fixed to the rear wall, so that only the rear wall and the changeover frame need adjusting to align the platen with the type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting the platen for two-letter types required separate adjustment of the carriage and its switching levers, which was laborious and inaccurate.

**Solution:** The carriage with the platen is carried on pivot levers and guide rails fixed to the rear wall, so that only the rear wall and the changeover frame need adjusting to align the platen with the type.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)
