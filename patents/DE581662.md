**How it works (reading):** A pivoted lever with a friction roller, driven by the carriage tension band, lifts the switching tooth out of the ratchet wheel for the duration of the return, so the parts are disengaged rather than rubbing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ratchet and coupling parts make noise and wear when the paper carriage is returned.

**Solution:** A pivoted lever with a friction roller, driven by the carriage tension band, lifts the switching tooth out of the ratchet wheel for the duration of the return, so the parts are disengaged rather than rubbing.
