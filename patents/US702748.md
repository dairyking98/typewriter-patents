## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the carriage-feedro ing mechanism of type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-propelled carriage feed is released during the down key-stroke when keys are struck fast, which gives a quick release, and during the up stroke at normal speed. The choice between natural and reverse feed is made automatically at each key-stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Quick key strokes make the carriage release late, so type impressions overlap.

**Solution:** A spring-propelled carriage feed is released during the down key-stroke when keys are struck fast, which gives a quick release, and during the up stroke at normal speed. The choice between natural and reverse feed is made automatically at each key-stroke.
