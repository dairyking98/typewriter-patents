## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Key-levers of a typewriter are slow to assemble and can shift out of place during transport or use.

**Solution:** A turnable slotted comb or plate on the fulcrum rod holds all key-lever ends in place once turned upright.

**How it works:** The key-levers' rear ends are inserted into slots in a comb hanging from a transverse rod journaled in the side frames, with the comb turned to an open position. When all levers are in, the comb is turned to vertical, which locks the lever ends against endwise withdrawal. The slots also keep the levers spaced laterally. Turning the comb back frees any lever for removal. Two modifications make the comb separate from the rod, or make both from one flat strip.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** key-lever; slotted comb plate; fulcrum rod; type-bar; type-ring; connecting rod; finger-key; bed-plate
