## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electric typewriter in which a type wheel must be positioned to any letter across a moving line.

**Solution:** A type wheel on a horizontal axle is rotated by racks on a frame that advances until a notched stop bar, raised by the pressed key, halts it, after which wheel and carriage print together.

**How it works:** The type wheel sleeve slides on a shaft held in a carriage that moves along the line. A reciprocating frame with racks meshes with gears on the shaft; solenoids drive it forward until a stop bar with notches at graduated distances meets the stop a pressed key has raised, so the wheel turns by a key-specific angle. Locking claw holds the wheel, then both carriages move to the paper to print and retract. Several keys can be pressed together and are printed in order of shortest travel. Upper and lower case letters alternate on the wheel and a case key changes the stop travel by one letter.
