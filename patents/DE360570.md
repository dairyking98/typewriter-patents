## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**How it works (reading):** Column stops with a full-length stop face on one side and short stepped noses on the other, reversible on a toothed rod, give either skipping or stepwise column advance.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulators could either skip columns or step column to column, not both with the same selection keys.

**Solution:** Column stops with a full-length stop face on one side and short stepped noses on the other, reversible on a toothed rod, give either skipping or stepwise column advance.

**How it works:** Fork-shaped column stops sit in the tooth gaps of a toothed stop rod, each with short stepped noses on one side for skip tabbing and a full-height face on the other. Turning a stop sets which mode a column uses. Slotted filler plates make the stops fit rods with uneven tooth depth, and a snap latch holds them.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** column stop; toothed stop rod; filler piece; side plate; snap latch; column key; carriage
