## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the line spacing mechanism of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Line-spacing ratchet on the platen axle with a releasable connection and a lever and pawl, so the platen can be released and rotated.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen must turn freely, independent of the line-space wheel, for filling blanks and corrections.

**Solution:** Line-spacing ratchet on the platen axle with a releasable connection and a lever and pawl, so the platen can be released and rotated.
