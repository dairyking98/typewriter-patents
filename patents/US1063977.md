## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dead keys for accents need a universal bar that does not space the carriage.

**Solution:** An auxiliary universal bar operated by several dead-key levers.

**How it works:** Dead-key levers move their typebars without operating the escapement. One auxiliary bar is shared by all dead keys, simplifying the construction. The machine is the Oliver with escape wheel on an upright shaft.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)

**Parts:** dead key lever; auxiliary universal bar; typebar; escape wheel shaft; shift frame

**Improves on:**

- US946229 (Knapp): shares one auxiliary bar between dead keys
