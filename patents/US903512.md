## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen needs a sturdy support that shifts it to upper case position and locks it there.

**Solution:** A platen holder on swinging arms is raised by a rod turned from either shift key, with a separate lock lever that latches the shift key down.

**How it works:** Either shift-key lever moves, via bell cranks and links, a rod engaging a grooved roller on a hinged arm fixed to a platen rod. Turning the rod raises two swinging arms carrying the platen holder to the upper case position. Rollers on the holder run in guide blocks on the carriage end pieces to keep movement vertical, and stops limit up and down travel. A lock lever with a cam notch engages a pin on the shift key lever to hold it down for several capitals.
