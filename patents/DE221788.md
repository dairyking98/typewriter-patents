## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bent intermediate levers jam at their bearings, and differing link sizes hinder mass production and repair.

**Solution:** Pull and push rods act along the lever ends, intermediate links have identical dimensions, and angle levers with tie rods transmit key motion to intermediate levers on arc bearings.
