## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Wide ribbon on older typewriters is not fully used, leaving edges unspent when the ribbon's cross adjustment is limited.

**Solution:** A replaceable ribbon-guide insert follows the platen-shift rail so the ribbon is shifted across its width with each case change.

**How it works:** A thin sheet-metal insert is attached to the platen-frame shift rail or its support arms by fork ends, one arm bent into a snap spring for driving. When the platen frame is shifted to capitals, the insert moves the ribbon sideways so the type always strikes fresh ribbon width. On other machines the paper-roller frame slides in slots of the insert.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** ribbon guide insert; changeover rail; support arm; snap spring

**Improves on:**

- older machines with limited ribbon adjustment: uses the full width of ribbon

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Ribbon detection device. Some types of typewriters are older In spite of the design, the wide ribbon used is not fully used Both of the ribbon's cross adjustability exists within certain limits Edge parts of the types remain unfrozen. To remedy this malady, d. H. the use of the unsoiled ribbon edge parts or narrow ribbons at all To enable such typewriters is the purpose of the invention. For this purpose, it consists of a ribbon guide, if necessary easily replaceable insert, which is used when changing over the paper roller or your frame on large letters, numbers, punctuation, etc. or when you pick it up this change is automatically adjusted transversely to the longitudinal direction of the ribbon. This Adjustments can be made in various ways according to the type of typewriter, z. B. with the Remington machines through the direct or indirect connection of the insert with the guide rail used for guiding the paper roller frame or their support arms, or in the case of eagle machines in that the paper roller frame slides in the slots of the insert, which is prevented from moving longitudinally, when moving but takes the insert piece with it in height…
