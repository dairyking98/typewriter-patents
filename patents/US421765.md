## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper perforating machines rely on hand-set gages and the operator's eye, which gives only approximate spacing and needs resetting for every line.

**Solution:** An automatic gage lets the machine feed the sheet and punch lines of holes at equal or unequal intervals on its own, with accurate repeats.

**How it works:** A foot pedal drives a rock-shaft and starts a punch cross-head through a clutch on the drive wheel. A feed wheel with spring clamps on its rim grips the sheet edge and feeds it forward. An automatic gage, set once beforehand, decides when the feed stops so each line of perforations lands in the right place. The finished sheet is discharged automatically.

**Parts:** frame; table; die; stripper; punch bar; cross-head; rock-shaft; pedal; clutch; feed wheel; spring clamp; automatic gage

**Improves on:**

- table gages for perforating machines: set once, no per-line resetting, unequal spacing without resetting
