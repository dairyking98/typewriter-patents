## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter small enough to carry in the pocket and work on any smooth flat surface that takes ink impressions, while remaining efficient and reliable.

**Solution:** A flexible type band with lower- and upper-case rows is slid under a roller by a key placed in a hole of a character chart, and the roller presses the chosen type through a window onto the paper.

**How it works:** A long narrow base plate with a rubber underside carries a rack bar and a scale. A bed plate slides along it, and a spring-held frame on it swings laterally by one finger push to select upper or lower case. Pivoted in that frame is a second frame that carries a character chart with two rows of holes, a slide plate and a flexible type bar with its ends fixed to the slide plate. The operator places a swivelled key on a spring key-lever into the hole for the wanted character, which slides the type band so that character lies over the window in the bed plate. Pressing the key tips the frame, a roller presses the type through the window onto the paper, and a dog riding the rack bar moves the whole machine along one step. Ink pads on the underside of the swinging frame ink the type with each stroke.
