## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Opening text states no explicit problem; the machine aims at a power-driven keyboard printer in which keys act by cam on a revolving cylinder.

**Solution:** A continuously revolving cylinder carries sliding rods, one per key, and a key cam pushes the selected rod out to strike paper against a type ring turning in unison below it.

**How it works:** A cylinder with longitudinal grooves, each holding a spring-returned sliding rod, turns on a shaft driven by a pulley and is geared to a type-ring shaft so both turn in step. Each rod has a cam pin; a key bar under the cylinder lifts its cam surface against the pin as it passes, sliding the rod out over the type ring so it covers the type of that key. The rod is two pieces joined by a hook; its short printing hammer is freed from a retaining band and a plate with a cavity lets a bent spring drive it onto the paper strip, pressing the paper onto the type. A forked spring throws the paper off the type and the paper strip is advanced after each stroke.
