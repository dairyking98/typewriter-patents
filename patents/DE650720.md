## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for clamping auxiliary ribbon pieces for typewriter copies, in which the auxiliary ribbon from holders to be attached to the typewriter is kept tense.

**How it works (reading):** Wire loops hook on the platen journal and carry resilient holders that keep the auxiliary ribbon taut and clinging to the platen curve, and one loop can be unhooked to swing the ribbon aside.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A ribbon piece for typewriter copies must be held tight against the platen, and clipping it on a roller can jam the roller when it is turned.

**Solution:** Wire loops hook on the platen journal and carry resilient holders that keep the auxiliary ribbon taut and clinging to the platen curve, and one loop can be unhooked to swing the ribbon aside.
