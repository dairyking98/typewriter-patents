## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special supports, platforms or trolleys for supporting machines on tables

**How it works (reading):** Felt sheet coated with a hardening paint or rubber layer forms a firm pad under the machine feet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wadding or straw under typewriter feet compresses and cover wears.

**Solution:** Felt sheet coated with a hardening paint or rubber layer forms a firm pad under the machine feet.
