## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to innovations in that class of typewriters in which the characters are attached to type levers arranged in a circle, and particularly relates to the manner in which these type levers move and how they are attached to achieve a very precise stop and enable adjustment.

**How it works (reading):** A circle of type levers on adjustable holders with conical pivot pins, driven by key levers through auxiliary levers of different lengths that equalise the key force.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing typebar machines have uneven key force, wear in type lever bearings and imprecise lever motion.

**Solution:** A circle of type levers on adjustable holders with conical pivot pins, driven by key levers through auxiliary levers of different lengths that equalise the key force.

**How it works:** 64 key levers on a rod below the typebar circle act through spring-loaded auxiliary levers of different lengths, linked to the key levers and by rods to the type levers, so each key needs the same force and travel. Each type lever pivots on a conical pin in a holder with straightening surfaces, keeping its swing plane exact. Holders sit edge-wise on a fixed ring under a flexible annular plate and are radially adjustable individually to take up wear. The carriage on rods with a friction roller is driven by a spring drum and cord, with spacing by a rack and two spring pawls released by keys or space key.
