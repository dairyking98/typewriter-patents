## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** A roller freewheel lets the platen turn freely; pressure pieces hold the line-space ratchet at a fixed engagement when the paper is set.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Platen rotation should be independent of the line-space setting, but the line-space wheel is locked to the platen knob.

**Solution:** A roller freewheel lets the platen turn freely; pressure pieces hold the line-space ratchet at a fixed engagement when the paper is set.
