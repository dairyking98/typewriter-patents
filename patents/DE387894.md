## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On earlier clutches coupling the line-spacing wheel to the platen roller, parts were hard to make and assemble, needed extra devices to hold the actuator in position, and an uncoupled wheel looked like a broken line-spacing action.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded bolt pushes a steel ball that wedges three clutch members into the line-spacing wheel's teeth, with a ribbon-spring lever that uncouples it and blocks the line-shift lever while uncoupled.

**How it works:** The hub on the platen axle carries three radial clutch members in slots, held out of the teeth of the freely turning line-spacing wheel by tension springs. A spring-loaded bolt presses a steel ball against bevels on the members, forcing them outward into internal clutch teeth so the wheel drives the platen. A ribbon-steel fork lever on the carriage side, carrying a double-armed actuator, pulls the bolt out against its spring to release the clutch. In the released position the actuator stands in the path of the line-shift lever, so the lever cannot move unless the clutch is engaged.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** line-spacing wheel; hub; clutch member; steel ball; spring-loaded bolt; ring nut; fork lever of ribbon spring steel; actuator; line-shift lever; platen roller

**Improves on:**
