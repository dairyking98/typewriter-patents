## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters with a front type stop are very noisy.

**Solution:** Mount angled type levers on upright guide rods and drive rods whose pivots are arranged so the type slows to a stop at the platen, and add rolling key-lever linkages that delay the final motion.

**How it works:** Each key rolls its arm against a curved intermediate lever, which pulls a drive rod through a tie rod. The drive rod and a front upright guide rod carry an angled type lever near its apex. In the last part of the motion the guide rod pivot moves down and the drive rod up, rotating the type lever about the drive rod pivot, which delays the type so it meets the platen softly. The rolling surfaces on the key linkage retard it further, and a slotted segment guides the parts.
