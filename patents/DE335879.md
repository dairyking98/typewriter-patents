## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Making diagrams and curves needs separate drawing aids such as diagram paper and special skills.

**Solution:** A typewriter is given a finer horizontal and vertical paper movement, with fine transport buttons, worm-gear shifts or precision notches, so the type can draw ordinates, abscissas and points with the usual character keys.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** fine transport device; coarse ratchet feed; character keys for lines and points

**Numbers:** coarse feed 5 mm or 10 mm; fine feed about 1 mm
