## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper feed for booking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print registering

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary switching element, selected by a punch-card-controlled selector, drives the feed roller so the form tape advances to a set line below the form head.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Booking form tapes need line starts at different heights per section, which the usual feed with one supplementary switching element cannot provide.

**Solution:** An auxiliary switching element, selected by a punch-card-controlled selector, drives the feed roller so the form tape advances to a set line below the form head.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** form tape; main switching element; auxiliary switching element; feed roller; punch-card selector
