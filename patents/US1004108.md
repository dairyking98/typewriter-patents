## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The little-finger shift key is slow on single-keyboard machines.

**Solution:** Shift key placed centrally between two space keys, on the same plane, with greater leverage.

**How it works:** The space lever carries a space bar with upward key portions at both ends so spacing can be done from either side. A shift bar parallel to the space bar has a central shank upturned as a shift key, so either thumb can hold shift while the fingers type.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** space bar; space key; shift bar; shift key; shift lever

**Improves on:**

- little-finger shift key: thumb shift key with greater leverage
