## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing forms with typewriter for offset) · **Subject (patent classes):** Details of, or accessories for, machines for mechanical composition using matrices for individual characters which are selected and assembled for type casting or moulding

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the production of printing forms, especially for offset machines, and consists in that by means of a typewriter Word elements printed on flexible strips of an etchable material excluded and the word elements combined into lines on a plate made of etchable material be attached, which is etched high.

**How it works (reading):** Process making printing forms with letterpress-like writing using a typewriter.
