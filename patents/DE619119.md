## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (sum and decimal printing for calculating typewriters)

**How it works (reading):** Electromagnet-driven sum printing with devices that move the decimal point freely and drop the lowest digits when a product has too many digits.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sum printers of calculating typewriters have a fixed decimal point, which is wrong for products of a multiplying machine.

**Solution:** Electromagnet-driven sum printing with devices that move the decimal point freely and drop the lowest digits when a product has too many digits.

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
