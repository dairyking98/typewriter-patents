**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching pawls that swing parallel to the ratchet plane act arcuately on the teeth, so the carriage stops irregularly and jumps forward, and the carriage transmits its weight undamped to the frame.

**Solution:** Two rigid, coupled pawls are set in planes next to one another, parallel to the ratchet axis and perpendicular to the ratchet shaft, on either side of the ratchet wheel, so they enter the teeth radially and the ratchet stands still.

**How it works:** Pawls set across the ratchet give radial entry and a steady stop; they absorb carriage motion with some elasticity, and the pawl length ratio sets the force available for the return movement of the universal rail.
