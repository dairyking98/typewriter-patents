## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object is the provision of improved and simplified means for releasably locking the platenitself against rotation, to enable the temporary disengagement of the locking mechanism during the insertion and removal of the work sheets, the locking mechanism being left free to operate after the insertion of a sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A trip driven by the work sheet at the platen lets a dog engage the ratchet; a dog releaser temporarily disengages it when the operator's hand presses it, and an adjustable detent can hold it idle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simpler way to release the platen lock temporarily for inserting paper, while leaving the lock free to operate.

**Solution:** A trip driven by the work sheet at the platen lets a dog engage the ratchet; a dog releaser temporarily disengages it when the operator's hand presses it, and an adjustable detent can hold it idle.
