## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object is the provision of improved and simplified means for releasably locking the platenitself against rotation, to enable the temporary disengagement of the locking mechanism during the insertion and removal of the work sheets, the locking mechanism being left free to operate after the insertion of a sheet.
