## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switching device for typewriters, one of which is the ratchet wheel to turn the platen Detecting, spring-loaded switch pawl rotatably connected to the switch slide is.

**How it works (reading):** A spring on the line-switch pawl is tensioned by a fixed stop at the start of the stroke, pressing the pawl home, and a locking roller holds the ratchet wheel and is returned automatically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On light portable machines the line-switch pawl can tremble and engage the wrong ratchet tooth.

**Solution:** A spring on the line-switch pawl is tensioned by a fixed stop at the start of the stroke, pressing the pawl home, and a locking roller holds the ratchet wheel and is returned automatically.
