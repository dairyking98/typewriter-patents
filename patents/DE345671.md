## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A continuous platen axis makes it hard to exchange the platen, and the platen must be able to turn freely of the line-index wheel.

**Solution:** The platen axis is split into two parts joined by a tube; a spring-loaded sliding sleeve with eccentric-driven pawls acts as a freewheel, so the platen can be uncoupled and interchanged.
