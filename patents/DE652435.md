## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holding, feeding, or guiding means therefor

**How it works (reading):** A colored ribbon is looped around two U-shaped sliders at the side edges of the writing plate, so it can be moved line by line and stays in place by friction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Colored strips for carbon-less copying soil the underlying sheets and are awkward to advance from line to line.

**Solution:** A colored ribbon is looped around two U-shaped sliders at the side edges of the writing plate, so it can be moved line by line and stays in place by friction.
