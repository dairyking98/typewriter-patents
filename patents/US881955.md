## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** A wheel or carrier holding dies is held on a shaft by a turret engaged by a locking device, and a hand-operated striker presses the die heads into the material.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Impressions into materials need a precise dies carrier that can be kept steady while the dies are operated.

**Solution:** A wheel or carrier holding dies is held on a shaft by a turret engaged by a locking device, and a hand-operated striker presses the die heads into the material.
