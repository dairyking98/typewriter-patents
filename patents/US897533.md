## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing from different zones of a multi-zone ribbon needs control of the ribbon vibrator lift.

**Solution:** A rotatable stop with three surfaces of different radius limits the vibrator's upward movement, and a spring link lets the universal bar travel fully.

**How it works:** A universal bar rocks a vertical arm whose spring link operates a bell crank on the shift rail, lifting the ribbon vibrator to the print point. A rotatable sleeve with surfaces 1, 2 and 3 arrests the bell crank at different points, giving the lift for a chosen ribbon zone. The spring yields so the escapement still operates.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** universal bar; spring link; bell crank; ribbon vibrator; rotatable stop; shift rail
