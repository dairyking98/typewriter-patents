## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A multi-colored or ordinary ribbon needs convenient regulation to use selected color fields or to move the ribbon out of the way for stencil cutting.

**Solution:** A ribbon-regulating device lets different color fields or portions of the ribbon be brought into the printing field, or the ribbon be removed and its drive made inoperative.

**How it works:** A two-field ribbon (for example copying and record ink, split by a median line) winds between spools across the machine without obstructing writing. A universal bar acts through rock shaft, pull rod and dog carrier to advance the carriage per key stroke; the new device regulates ribbon position and feed on a Victor typewriter. It can move the ribbon fully out of the printing field and disable the ribbon feed for cutting stencils.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon spool; color field; universal bar; rock shaft; pull rod; dog carrier; key lever; type bar

**Numbers:** 5 sheets of drawings; 2 color fields
