## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rolling-impressor typographic machines need to carry many types while keeping the impression mechanism small, light and fast.

**Solution:** Several rolling impressor wheels are mounted on nested coaxial shafts, and each shaft is shifted endwise to bring its wheel into the operative plane, with selectors choosing the individual type.

**How it works:** Rolling impressors carrying types or dies orbit in closed paths while rotating about their own axes. They sit on shafts located one within another, on opposite sides of a fixed operative plane. When a wheel is to print, its shaft is slid lengthwise so the impressor lies in the operative plane, and after the impression it returns to its idle position, while another wheel takes its place. A set of selective devices makes one chosen type on an impressor act to the exclusion of the others. The text says it is shown as a typewriting machine but applies to typographic machines generally.
