## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvement in tabulati ng or column-stop mechanism for "jtypewriting machines by' means of which the carriage is-released from the control of '20 F or points where its movement is arrested the usual letter-spacing mechanism to permit of its movement under the force of its actuatingspring to a predetermined point for the purpose of printing items or numhers in one or more vertical.

**How it works (reading):** A brake checks the rotary motion of the escape wheel while the pinion is released from the carriage rack by the tabulator key, so the spacing parts return to their former relative position on re-engagement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When the tabulator key releases the carriage, the escape wheel spins freely, so the letter-spacing train is out of step when the pinion re-engages the carriage rack.

**Solution:** A brake checks the rotary motion of the escape wheel while the pinion is released from the carriage rack by the tabulator key, so the spacing parts return to their former relative position on re-engagement.
