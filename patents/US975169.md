## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage escapement makes a loud noise at each drop of the carriage in letter space movements.

**Solution:** A modified escapement device for the carriage, related to a companion application, arranged to greatly reduce the escapement noise in a Yost visible machine.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
