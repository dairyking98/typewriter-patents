## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A machine should serve both as a typewriter and a computer, and the typewriting part needs a fast power-assisted type-bar action.

**Solution:** A motor-driven shaft cocks a spring-loaded actuating bar that a key releases to throw the type-bar.

**How it works:** A key lever lifts an operating arm through a slotted segment, raising a type-bar actuating arm. A segmental actuating bar is pulled forward by a stiff spring but held back by a crank that rides a constantly rotating shaft through a pawl and ratchet. A dog normally locks the crank, and key depression releases the dog so the spring carries the bar forward quickly. A cam disengages the pawl on return, and the motor re-cocks the bar.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
