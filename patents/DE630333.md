## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (railway car air heating) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for heating only

**How it works (reading):** Hot and cold air go to each compartment in separate lines, with one adjuster that moves the two flows in opposite directions so the total stays constant.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Railway car heating changes the hot-air quantity per compartment, which also disturbs the ventilation and other compartments.

**Solution:** Hot and cold air go to each compartment in separate lines, with one adjuster that moves the two flows in opposite directions so the total stays constant.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a control system for air heating systems for railroad cars in order to automatically adjust the amount of heat required to heat the heating air by means of a heated air flow that can be regulated by a temperature sensor and a cold air flow added to it to the respective heat requirements of the whole car and in each compartment to adjust the temperature of the compartment flowing into the compartment Allow total air without thereby affecting the heating of the other compartments or changing the amount of air entering each compartment. In known systems, the temperature control is made in the compartments by changing the amount of hot air, which not only affects the heating of the other compartments, but inevitably also changes the ventilation of the compartments. The object is achieved according to the invention in that the warm and cold air is fed to the compartments in a special line and that an adjusting device is arranged in a manner known per se to simultaneously change the quantities of cold and warm air in opposite directions, so that the control temperature is adjustable in each compartment independently of the other…
