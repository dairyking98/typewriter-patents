## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stereotype matrix machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** A horizontal type-wheel carries vertical pins that act as keys near its edge, and punches working in holes in the wheel are driven to impress the letters into a matrix, so the forms are made without movable type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Stereotype plates need movable type to be set and then distributed after use.

**Solution:** A horizontal type-wheel carries vertical pins that act as keys near its edge, and punches working in holes in the wheel are driven to impress the letters into a matrix, so the forms are made without movable type.

**Features:**
