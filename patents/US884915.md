## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets must be placed accurately so their ends are horizontal, and a given line is hard to locate and correct.

**Solution:** A line-indicator and scale system with paper adjustment that sets a sheet position so that writing can be placed at a chosen line or position and corrected in line alignment.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; line scale; indicator; paper adjustment mechanism
