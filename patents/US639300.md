## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar pivots wobble sideways and take space.

**Solution:** Ball-bearing typebar pivot: hanger with ball groove, coned boss on the bar, adjustable second cone, single row of balls.

**How it works:** The typebar has a coned boss on one side entering a hanger eye with a ball groove; an adjustable nut cone on a screw closes the other side of one ball row, with a check nut. Wear is taken up by the nut, and many bars can be packed side by side.

**Features:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** hanger; eye; ball groove; type-bar; cone; screw; nut; check nut; balls; key-lever; rod
