## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A quick or jerky stroke of the line-space lever makes the platen overshoot a line-space, and the spring detent alone cannot stop it, so writing goes crooked.

**Solution:** An arm on the line-space lever arrests the platen-rotating lever and pawl, and the shock of the operator's stroke presses the detent harder into the ratchet, so overthrow is prevented.
