## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and manually actuated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for setting the paper carriage in its Central position and release of a lock on typewriters that locks it in the switch position, In particular, portable typewriters in a tightly enclosing carrying case be packed.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage is locked by a bolt in its central position, and closing the case cover pushes a part of the carriage so the friction on the bolt is released; a spring then returns the bolt and the switch lever, so the machine can be used at once after it is taken out.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A portable typewriter locked in its central position must be unlocked by hand before use, and the old lock is complicated.

**Solution:** The carriage is locked by a bolt in its central position, and closing the case cover pushes a part of the carriage so the friction on the bolt is released; a spring then returns the bolt and the switch lever, so the machine can be used at once after it is taken out.
