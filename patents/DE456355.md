## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the arrangement of type lever bearing segments and intermediate lever bearing segment in typewriters, in which the type lever bearing segment is moved in a straight line and the intermediate lever bearing segment is unyielding connected to the frame.

**How it works (reading):** The guide blocks for the straight-moving type lever bearing segment are mounted on the intermediate lever bearing segment, which is screwed to the base plate, so the whole type lever unit is pre-assembled outside the frame and fixed with a few screws.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** When the type lever bearing segment guide blocks are fixed separately to the frame, assembly is cumbersome and frame distortion reaches the guides, causing jamming.

**Solution:** The guide blocks for the straight-moving type lever bearing segment are mounted on the intermediate lever bearing segment, which is screwed to the base plate, so the whole type lever unit is pre-assembled outside the frame and fixed with a few screws.

> Description Translated from German Arrangement of type lever bearing segment and intermediate lever bearing segment with typewriters. The invention relates to the arrangement of type lever bearing segments and intermediate lever bearing segment in typewriters, in which the type lever bearing segment is moved in a straight line and the intermediate lever bearing segment is unyielding connected to the frame. The invention consists in that the guide blocks for the type lever bearing segment are placed on the intermediate lever bearing segment fastened in the machine frame. Compared to known facilities in which the guide blocks of the Type lever bearing segment and the intermediate lever bearing. Segment fastened separately in the frame are, the invention has the advantage that the assembly is less cumbersome and is time consuming and distortion of the frame is not transferred to the guides and therefore no jamming can occur. In the device according to the invention therefore retain type lever bearing segment and intermediate lever bearing segment permanently in the original position.
