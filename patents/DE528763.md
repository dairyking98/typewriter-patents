## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register control strip switching) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with visual indication, e.g. counter drum

**How it works (reading):** A swivel plate on the pressure frame moves the strip in two phases and holds it fully still during printing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper strip of a cash-register control strip smears or tears when parts stop unevenly during printing.

**Solution:** A swivel plate on the pressure frame moves the strip in two phases and holds it fully still during printing.
