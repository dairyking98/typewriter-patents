## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to tabulators of that class in which stops are mounted in position to be moved into and out of the path of a part of the paper-carriage, so that said stops may be set to arrest the carriage at certain points.

**How it works (reading):** A movable bar on the tabulating frame sets whole groups of tabulator stops at once, so different forms can be selected without resetting each stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators doing several forms must shift many tabulator stops every time the form changes, which is inconvenient.

**Solution:** A movable bar on the tabulating frame sets whole groups of tabulator stops at once, so different forms can be selected without resetting each stop.
