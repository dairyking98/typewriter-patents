**How it works (reading):** A single power-driven locking device replaces the separate manual and power shift locks, so one mechanism holds the platen in upper case and releases it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Continuous capital letters on a power-driven typewriter need a shift stop per capital and tuning between two locking devices.

**Solution:** A single power-driven locking device replaces the separate manual and power shift locks, so one mechanism holds the platen in upper case and releases it.
