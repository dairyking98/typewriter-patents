## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Toggle or cam platen shift mechanisms need very exact parts and wear develops play, so the platen overshoots the middle rest position.

**Solution:** Locking levers forced into position by the shift-key linkage catch an adjustable angle piece on the shift frame, fixing the middle position regardless of play in the linkage.

**How it works:** The shift frame carries the platen and rests on a roller. Two shift-key levers, one to raise and one to lower, push or pull slotted levers that ride on a fixed bolt. The slotted levers turn two locking levers with noses that hold an adjustable angle piece on the shift frame from above and below, so the platen is locked in the middle rest position. Depressing a key frees one lock and lifts or lowers the frame to a stop; releasing returns it. Only springs return the key levers; parts are stamped.
