## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Centring a heading between margin stops requires counting letter spaces by hand.

**Solution:** A scale attached to a margin stop with two graduation series, one counting letter spaces and one counting half that, shows where the carriage must start to centre a line.

**How it works:** The scale-bearing band is fixed to a margin stop and wound on a spring drum on the other end. The carriage pointer indicates on one scale the distance from the stop and on the other, numbered alternately, the half-distance in letter spaces. The operator sets the carriage to the centring point before typing a heading.
