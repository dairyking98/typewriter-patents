## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (sound tape recording material) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon cartridges for endless ribbons; Apparatus or processes specially adapted for their manufacture; Cassettes for webs or filaments for housing endless webs or filaments

**How it works (reading):** A tape with micro-acoustic mechanically scannable writing is sealed in a cassette so only a short piece is exposed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Long-playing records are too long; a short-playing cassette tape needs micro-acoustic writing in a closed cassette.

**Solution:** A tape with micro-acoustic mechanically scannable writing is sealed in a cassette so only a short piece is exposed.
