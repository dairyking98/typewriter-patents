> Description Translated from German Typewriter frame. The typewriter frame in the present case Invention consists of a bracket made of flexible, durable material that Is formed from a piece and is used to attach the individual parts of the frame. The bracket, which is placed on edge, surrounds the front part of the key lever parts, includes these and determines the position of the side parts of the frame, this bracket is connected at its rear end either to a base plate of the frame or with a cross rail that determines the width of the frame. The individual frame parts, like the side walls and the front wall, can be attached to the bracket and in any way fastened.-Here, the side parts can be punched out of sheet metal in a suitable shape or pressed, as well as the front protective cover. The side panels can be with bent flanges on which other associated parts of the typewriter on or used. The key lever guide comb can also be attached to the .Seitenteile or to be connected and attached to the front protective cover, or to the inside of the bracket.
