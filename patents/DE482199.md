## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an inevitable Ball or roller guide for the paper trolley of writing, calculating or similar Machines in which balls or rollers are guided through a holder that is Toothed gear is driven by the moving parts.

**How it works (reading):** The rack teeth are formed by rolling a hard gear into the sole of the guide rail and the carriage base, so the gear runs on the teeth of the guideway inside the rail, with a thin holder carrying it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage with balls or rollers in a driving gear needs space beside the guideways for the gear and its rack, which makes small machines bulky and gives tilting and noise.

**Solution:** The rack teeth are formed by rolling a hard gear into the sole of the guide rail and the carriage base, so the gear runs on the teeth of the guideway inside the rail, with a thin holder carrying it.

**Features:** [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** carriage base plate; guide rails; rolled rack teeth; gear with hard material; thin holder
