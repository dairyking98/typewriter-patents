## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**How it works (reading):** Four ball bearings with cages guide the vertically sliding segment carrier in V-grooves, with springs placed so that each bearing gets about equal preload against the weight of carrier and type basket.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-pressed ball tracks on the sliding type segment carrier tilt and change position under load, so guidance is not exact and free of play.

**Solution:** Four ball bearings with cages guide the vertically sliding segment carrier in V-grooves, with springs placed so that each bearing gets about equal preload against the weight of carrier and type basket.
