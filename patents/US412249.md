## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After each line the operator must take a hand from the keyboard to return the carriage and turn the line space on Remington machines.

**Solution:** A knee-operated lever attachment returns the carriage and turns up the platen.

**How it works:** A base clamps to the machine's support. A rod journaled in two posts carries a short arm at one end, linked to a bell-crank knee lever, and a longer arm at the other end, strapped to a tripping bell-crank on a collar on the paper-table support. Moving the knee lever sideways rotates the rod and pulls the carriage back, also turning the line space.

**Features:** [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** base piece; spring clamp; rod; short arm; long arm; bell-crank knee lever; link; tripping lever; collar; strap

**Improves on:**

- Remington/Standard hand carriage return: operator keeps hands on keys
