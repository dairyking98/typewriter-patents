## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device by means of which the above-mentioned switching and securing by the movement of a single spring-loaded key lever, which always takes place in the same direction happens so that the writer does not operate this switching device Attention, and therefore not distracted from his or her actual work will.

**How it works (reading):** A single spring-loaded key lever, always pressed in the same direction, drives the reversal through angle arms and a tilting weight arm that move the switching lever back and forth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon spool direction must be reversed by hand on machines without automatic reversing.

**Solution:** A single spring-loaded key lever, always pressed in the same direction, drives the reversal through angle arms and a tilting weight arm that move the switching lever back and forth.
