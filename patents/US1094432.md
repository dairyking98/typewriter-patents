## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Positive-feed mechanisms automatically operated in response to carriage return

**How it works (reading):** A racked sector and an adjustable device with a limit stop set the rotary travel of the paper roller, which a crank lever turns through a ratchet ring.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The amount of paper roller rotation for line spacing needs adjustment.

**Solution:** A racked sector and an adjustable device with a limit stop set the rotary travel of the paper roller, which a crank lever turns through a ratchet ring.
