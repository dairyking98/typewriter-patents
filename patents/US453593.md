## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** An instrument of few parts is wanted that can still print a large number of characters.

**Solution:** A thin-shell type cylinder is slid along and rotated about its axis by a single pointer handle, which then drops into a perforated index plate to lock the character and press it down onto the paper.

**How it works:** A type cylinder in a sliding frame is carried by a carriage that runs on rails over a platen. A combined handle and pointer is hinged to a rack; moving it over the printed index plate slides the frame along the cylinder axis and, through rack and gears, rotates the cylinder, so the chosen character comes under an opening in a thin rubber sheet. Pressing the handle down drops a conical lower pointer into the matching hole of a perforated plate, locking the cylinder, and the same pressure swings the whole frame down on a hinge so the type pushes through the sheet onto the paper. A spring lifts the frame on release. An ink roller of felt touches the type on the cylinder. Paper is fed between two geared rollers turned by hand, with a latch to separate them for loading, and a pointer on the carriage shows the print position.
