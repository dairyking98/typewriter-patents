## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars pivoted on a rod through a slotted segment are hard to remove for repair or replacement.

**Solution:** Each typebar carries a spring-held elbow-lever latch that closes the open-ended pivot seat and can be thumbed aside to lift the bar out.

**How it works:** Typebars hang on a pivot rod through slots in a semicircular segment, each with an open-ended seat. Each bar carries a small elbow-lever whose upper arm sits just under the pivot rod, closing the seat so the bar cannot leave. A spring holds it locked. Pressing the handle swings the arm clear so the bar can be lifted until a lug hits the rod and the key link disengages, then drawn forward out of its slot. On reinsertion the bar is pushed in; the lever yields against its spring, guides the bar onto the pivot and snaps back under the rod.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** typebar; segment; pivot rod; elbow-lever latch; latch spring; lug; link; key lever
