## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (accounting machine invoice sheet setter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Flat page-size platens or smaller flat platens having a greater size than line-size platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in the fact that a slide, its upper edge as an attachment serves for the invoice sheet, is displaceable along a guide plate, the Leading edge as well as the adjacent edge of the slider so inclined to the Lines of the ledger sheet runs that when the slide is shifted sideways the contact edge of the slider by the line spacing by the width of a posting plate is moved.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-held slide on a guide plate has an edge inclined to the ledger lines, so sideways shifting moves the contact edge by one line spacing and one posting-plate width.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An accounting machine's flat plate needed invoice sheets and ledger lines to be written in step, with each invoice amount landing on a ledger row.

**Solution:** A spring-held slide on a guide plate has an edge inclined to the ledger lines, so sideways shifting moves the contact edge by one line spacing and one posting-plate width.
