> Description Translated from German Device for accounting machines for setting invoice sheets over a ledger sheet. The priority of filing in the United States of America from October 9th, 1923 is claimed. The device for accounting machines for setting invoice sheets over a general ledger sheet that contains the item of the invention consists in the fact that a slide, its upper edge as an attachment serves for the invoice sheet, is displaceable along a guide plate, the Leading edge as well as the adjacent edge of the slider so inclined to the Lines of the ledger sheet runs that when the slide is shifted sideways the contact edge of the slider by the line spacing by the width of a posting plate is moved. The slide is attached to the guide plate with the help of a spring moved, one end of which engages the slide and the other end behind Holds projections on the guide plate.
