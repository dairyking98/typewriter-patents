## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a typewriter for both vertical and horizontal writing, the character-feed and line-feed handles swap jobs at each changeover, causing operating mistakes.

**Solution:** A sliding-and-rocking shaft that engages one of two handle rods with the paper-feed fork or the lateral-feed lever, so each handle keeps its own job in either writing direction.

**How it works:** A shaft laid crosswise above the base plate carries two downward engaging pieces (character-feed and line-feed) and an upward fork that works the paper-feed mechanism. A character-feed sliding rod and a line-feed sliding rod face the engaging pieces, but never both at once, and connect to the lateral-feed lever by connecting rods. The lateral-feed lever and the shaft are engaged through a bracket. In vertical mode the character-feed rod's stud moves the fork to feed characters, and the line-feed rod drives the lateral-feed lever. Swinging the shaft sideways reverses which rod engages which, so the handles stay matched to their labels in horizontal mode.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Character-feed and line-feed mechanism for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a character-feed and line-feed mechanism made as follows. A sliding-and-rocking shaft is laid crosswise above the top of the base plate. On it are clamped a downward engaging piece for the character-feed handle and an upward fork end for working the paper-feed mechanism. On the top of the base plate, in positions where they can face the two engaging pieces, but not at the same time, a sliding rod for the character-feed handle and a sliding rod for the line-feed handle are provided. These can each be engaged with the lever that works the lateral-feed mechanism, by a character-side connecting rod and a line-side connecting rod. The lateral-feed lever and the sliding-and-rocking shaft are engaged through its bracket. The object is that, on a typewriter for both vertical and horizontal writing, whichever way it is switched, the character-feed handle and the line-feed handle can each still be used for their own job, so that mistakes in operation caused by the handles changing their functions at a changeover are avoided.
