## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the known sound-absorbing box for typewriters, calculating machines and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A lockable box is fastened to the board by adjustable screws or bolts that can only be turned with special keys, with a flange inside the box that holds the bolts in the locked position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter can be removed from its board and stolen or tampered with, and the cover offers no protection.

**Solution:** A lockable box is fastened to the board by adjustable screws or bolts that can only be turned with special keys, with a flange inside the box that holds the bolts in the locked position.
