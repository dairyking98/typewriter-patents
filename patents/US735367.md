## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says tabulating needs a carriage stop that can be set to several positions and raised clear of the path when the carriage moves.

**Solution:** A feed rack swings about an axis parallel to the carriage, held in engagement by a holding means; a scaled axle with arms and an internal rack carries adjustable narrow stops, and a swinging stop on the carriage strikes these stops.
