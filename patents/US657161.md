## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feeds for typewriters and time stamps need to advance progressively and reverse before a spool is fully unwound.

**Solution:** A pawl-and-ratchet shaft shifted endwise between two bevel gears feeds the ribbon, and rollers riding the shrinking ribbon roll trip an automatic reverse.

**How it works:** Two ribbon spools carry bevel gears; a parallel shaft with a bevel gear at each end can slide to mesh with one spool gear or the other. A ratchet on the shaft is turned by a pawl on a loose collar when a rod, worked by a spacing key or other part, is raised and lowered. A spring-pressed roller on a rock-shaft rides each spool's ribbon roll; as the roll empties the shaft rocks and raises a trigger that lifts a locking arm, freeing a spring-loaded rocking lever that shifts the shaft to the other gear and reverses the feed.
