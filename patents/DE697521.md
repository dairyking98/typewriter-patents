## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to devices for refreshing used ink ribbons typewriters by pressing and guiding the tapes past absorbent, Surfaces impregnated with softening substances.

**How it works (reading):** Two spring-clip rollers with absorbent wick coverings and a helical thread press the ribbon between them so it is refreshed evenly, with axial bores to crank the rollers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier ribbon refreshers refreshed the ribbon unevenly, because the bulges worn by the letters were not compensated.

**Solution:** Two spring-clip rollers with absorbent wick coverings and a helical thread press the ribbon between them so it is refreshed evenly, with axial bores to crank the rollers.
