## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Columns of figures need the paper carriage stopped at decimal-aligned points, and existing tabulators need visual checks and cause shocks when the carriage strikes its stop.

**Solution:** A tabulating attachment that tabulates columns without visual reference and relieves the machine of shocks when the released carriage reaches its stop.

**How it works:** The attachment sets stops for the carriage positions of each column of figures and includes a device that cushions the carriage on release against the attachment stop.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** tabulating attachment; stops; detent; paper carriage
