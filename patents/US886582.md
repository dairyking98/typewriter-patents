## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for fixing the film on the axis of a reel or spindle

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide improved means for detachably connecting, flexiblematerial, such as ribbons, strips, and the like, to'rolls or drums without the'use ofpins or tacks', and whereby the ribbon can be readily attached and detached without unduly handling the same, to the end that the fingers of the operator may be ke' t clean.

**How it works (reading):** A gripper runs along a groove in the roll or drum. Pivoted arms carry it, and it is held closed by a catch, so the flexible strip or ribbon is clamped flush with the surface.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon attachment to a roll uses pins or tacks, which soil fingers and are awkward to remove.

**Solution:** A gripper runs along a groove in the roll or drum. Pivoted arms carry it, and it is held closed by a catch, so the flexible strip or ribbon is clamped flush with the surface.
