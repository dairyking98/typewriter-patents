## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On the Caligraph the carriage vibrated at speed and ran with friction on two guide rods, and needed a release key to be shifted.

**Solution:** Run the carriage on a single guide rod through a yoke, hinged so pulling the carriage toward the operator disengages its racks from the spacing pawl, and return it with a spring.

**How it works:** A yoke slides on the rear guide rod and carries the stationary letter-spacing rack; the carriage back rod forms a hinge through lugs on the yoke. Pulling the front handle rocks the carriage so its racks leave the spacing pawl, and the carriage can then be slid either way along the rod. Letting go lets a coiled or flat spring return it into engagement, binding the racks and damping vibration. Return travel is stopped by the paper table striking the yoke lugs, sparing the pawl.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** guide rod; yoke; carriage back rod; stationary rack; spring-moved rack; spacing pawl; spacing lever; front handle; coiled spring; paper table
