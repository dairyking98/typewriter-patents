## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage release is tied to the tabulator, and the tabulator stops need a way to be locked in cooperative relation.

**Solution:** A separate key, independent of the tabulating mechanism, effects carriage release, and simple means lock the tabulator stops in cooperation.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** tabulator key; space key; tabulating stops; escapement and tabulating mechanism
