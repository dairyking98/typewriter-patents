## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Adjusting the pull of the spiral spring that returns the paper cylinder sideways is awkward and takes space.

**Solution:** A handle lever with a click and an operating lever with a pushing pawl share one pivot over a saw-tooth wheel, so holding the operating lever alone winds the spring and holding both lets it down.

**How it works:** A saw-tooth wheel is fixed on the spring arbor. The handle lever's click always engages it. The operating lever, pivoted above on the same pivot, carries a pawl that pushes the wheel forward one or more teeth while the click clicks over; when the lever returns the pawl slips off (a bevel on pawl and teeth in one form, a sprung pawl in the other). Pulling both levers together lifts the click out and the pawl lets the wheel back a tooth at a time, unwinding the spring. A cord from the spring barrel to the paper cylinder gives the stepwise sideways feed.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Device for winding up and letting down a spiral spring. Nature and object of the invention. The invention relates to a device for winding up and letting down a spiral spring, in which a handle lever carrying a click and an operating lever carrying a pushing pawl are pivoted one over the other on one pivot, the click and the pawl both engaging a saw-tooth wheel fixed to the arbor of the spiral spring, so that the spring is wound up by holding the operating lever alone and let down by holding the handle lever and operating lever together. The object is to make winding up and letting down the spring easy, and to make the device compact and as small as possible, especially for printing machines. Brief description of the drawings.
