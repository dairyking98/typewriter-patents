## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to mechanism for operating the platen of a type writing machine, its object being to provide simple and effective means whereby the space between the lines of the type writing is not limited to fixed distances but can be varied to any distance within the operating range of device and whereby when the same is at rest, the platen is free to be rotated in either direction.

**How it works (reading):** A lever with a boss engages gripping dogs in a concentric groove on a plate attached to the platen; moving the lever rotates the platen by a variable, adjustable amount, and the platen is free to turn when at rest.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing on the platen is limited to fixed distances, and the platen cannot be freely rotated in either direction when at rest.

**Solution:** A lever with a boss engages gripping dogs in a concentric groove on a plate attached to the platen; moving the lever rotates the platen by a variable, adjustable amount, and the platen is free to turn when at rest.
