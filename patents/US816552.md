## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type-bars rebound off the cushion on the return stroke and can strike a neighbouring bar, which interferes with fast typing.

**Solution:** A resilient cushion in a recess of the abutment catches the type-bar on its return; the cushion is shaped so the bars do not rebound into adjacent bars (the detailed arrangement is poorly read in the OCR).
