## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention are broadly to indicate to the operator by automatic means the position of his current line upon the sheet.

**How it works (reading):** An indicator driven by the platen shows line by line the distance to the bottom edge, with an early eye signal, an ear signal at the predetermined line, and a lock of the machine until the sheet is released.
