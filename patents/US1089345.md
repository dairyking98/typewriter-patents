**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates more particularly to the line-lock mechanism of a foldable front strike or visible type-writing machine.

**How it works (reading):** A line-lock mechanism stops the carriage at set positions for foldable front-strike machines.
