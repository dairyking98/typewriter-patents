## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**How it works (reading):** Booking-sheet rollers on a shared shaft are spring-loaded so that lifting the writing rollers presses the booking rollers harder against the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Booking sheets on accounting machines must be held while writing sheets are removed.

**Solution:** Booking-sheet rollers on a shared shaft are spring-loaded so that lifting the writing rollers presses the booking rollers harder against the platen.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper guide for accounting typewriters. In accounting work on typewriters where current items are collected on a sheet of paper, is a condition that this sheet of paper when removing the individual writing sheets, which must always be smaller than the book leaf itself, immovably recorded will. It has been suggested to use a paper guide for this purpose, in which the book leaf after releasing the paper pressure rollers independently of these is held by special roles. The original written sheets can can be removed from the machine unhindered and new sheets inserted. Here the roller groups can each be lifted off independently of one another each pressed separately, so that they always apply the same pressure rest against the individual arches. The essence of the present invention is that the on one common shaft-mounted pressure rollers for the accounting sheet and the writing sheet by common springs against the paper roll in the opposite sense be pressed that when you lift the pressure rollers for the writing sheet, the pressure rollers be pressed harder on the booking sheet, creating a secure hold of the booking sheet is guaranteed.
