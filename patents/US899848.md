## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more especially to the ribbon mechanism of typewriting ma chines.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon carrier between the spools is reversed or turned, so the other face or stripe of the ribbon is presented to the types, changing the path the type impressions describe along the ribbon width.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Any of several inking surfaces with different characteristics, such as colours, must be brought into position to cooperate with the types.

**Solution:** A ribbon carrier between the spools is reversed or turned, so the other face or stripe of the ribbon is presented to the types, changing the path the type impressions describe along the ribbon width.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier; ribbon spools; ribbon
