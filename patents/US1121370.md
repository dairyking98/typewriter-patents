## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stenographic machine paper strips come out in a loose roll and must be rewound for transcribing.

**Solution:** An attachment that folds the paper into a tidy stack, driven by the machine's paper feed.

**How it works:** Two arms hold a rocking frame with a spring-loaded platform to receive folded paper. Sliding bars with folding fingers and clamps, geared to segments, alternately fold layers as the frame turns. A rod linked to the paper-feed devices drives ratchet wheels and a clutch to turn the frame.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md)

**Parts:** rocking frame; platform; folding finger; clamp; sliding bar; ratchet wheel; clutch; rod; arm

**Improves on:**
