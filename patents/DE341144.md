**How it works (reading):** Advance is made by switching pawl pairs whose stroke is changed by the ratchet carrier, so different advance ranges come from one pawl pair and one ratchet wheel rather than spring tension.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage advance by spring-loaded jamming ratchets lets the carriage move irregularly, and typefaces of differing width need different advance ranges.

**Solution:** Advance is made by switching pawl pairs whose stroke is changed by the ratchet carrier, so different advance ranges come from one pawl pair and one ratchet wheel rather than spring tension.
