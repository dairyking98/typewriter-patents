## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A compact, light, cheap typewriter controlled from the front.

**Solution:** Front-controlled typebar machine with adjustable bearings for alignment.

**How it works:** Three cross-pieces form the frame, a housing at the front. Woodward-type typebars pivot on endwise-adjustable screws so alignment can be corrected without skill. Details of ribbon, platen pawl and carriage follow.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** frame; typebar; adjusting screw; platen; pawl

**Improves on:**

- [US514141](../patents/US514141.md): adds adjustable bearings
