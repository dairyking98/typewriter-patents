## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (tabulating machine for number listing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing numbers in columns needs a tab stop that stays accurate.

**Solution:** Tabulator stops on a carriage bar cooperate with tabulator rods driven by bell-crank levers and finger rods, with a rack holding the escapement.

**How it works:** Finger rods operate bell-crank levers and tabulator rods; a rack holds the escapement gear.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
