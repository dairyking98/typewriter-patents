## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The earlier Yost circular universal bar only worked when the type carriers were pulled down; it had to be adapted for machines whose type carriers are pushed up by their connecting rods.

**Solution:** A circular universal ring rests on lugs on a circle of vertical connecting rods so that lifting any one rod tilts the ring about the diametrically opposite rod and works the carriage escapement through a central spindle.

**How it works:** Key levers of the first order push vertical connecting rods upward to swing the type carriers to the print point. Each rod has a radial lug that supports a spoked circular ring. When one rod rises it lifts the ring on that side, the ring pivots on the lug of the opposite rod, and its hub raises a spindle sliding in a fixed bracket. The spindle moves a lever, which through a link and rocker arm vibrates the duplex feed dogs on the rack, releasing the spring-drum carriage by one space. The space key acts on the same lever directly, lifting the spindle off the ring hub without moving any rods. Some rods connect via sub-levers for straight stringing.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** bed plate; key lever; connecting rod; radial lug; circular universal ring; spindle; spindle bracket; lever; link; rocker arm; duplex feed dog; compound feed rack; spring drum; space key; sub-lever
