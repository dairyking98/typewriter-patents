## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The existing key lever and type bar action does not give a strong enough stroke for a given key depression.

**Solution:** A power transmitting linkage between key and type bar lets the same key travel produce a stronger type stroke; the striking mechanism is shown in two positions during its operation.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
