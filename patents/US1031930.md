## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper must be guided and clamped on a visible-writing type-bar machine without blocking the type.

**Solution:** Adjustable arms on a transverse bar carry a clamping roll above the platen and a guide finger that overlaps the scale bar; a spring holds each arm down or elevated.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
