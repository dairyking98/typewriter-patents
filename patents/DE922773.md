**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Strips for supporting or holding papers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a backing pad for insertion and retention of carbon copies and normal postcards.

**How it works (reading):** A backing pad has two horizontal pockets, one for carbon copies and one for the address side of a postcard, so the text can be written without re-clamping.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Postcards and carbon sets slip out of the typewriter and must be turned over and re-clamped to write the address and text.

**Solution:** A backing pad has two horizontal pockets, one for carbon copies and one for the address side of a postcard, so the text can be written without re-clamping.
