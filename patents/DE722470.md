## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-drawing devices with pencils give non-uniform lines, and inking-roller or ribbon-based wheels need maintenance.

**Solution:** Wheels run on a rail above the normal ribbon and press on the ribbon to draw lines, swiveled 90 degrees for vertical lines; the rail guides the ribbon.

**How it works:** Line-drawing wheels run on a rail over the ribbon, inked by it, turned 90 degrees for vertical lines.
