## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an edge stop device for typewriters of the type in which a stop arm is provided on the paper carriage of the machine, which for usually under the action of a spring in the same plane as the stationary ones Edge stops held, but when hitting an edge stop over this can be lifted so that the slide ίο can continue its movement beyond the edge stop.

**How it works (reading):** A stop arm on the carriage, mounted on a vibrating shaft that can move independently, lifts over edge stops and also acts as a spring-loaded buffer, with the same spring holding the stops in position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Known edge stop devices are complicated, and the paper carriage needs a buffer against shock when it is pushed back by hand.

**Solution:** A stop arm on the carriage, mounted on a vibrating shaft that can move independently, lifts over edge stops and also acts as a spring-loaded buffer, with the same spring holding the stops in position.
