## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**How it works (reading):** The universal rail, linked to the switch lock linkage and slidably guided, can be pulled up out of the way so a type lever can be replaced at the front of the bearing block.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The type levers at the sides of the bearing block act on the universal rail with a shorter arm, so paper advance is uneven, and the type levers are hard to exchange.

**Solution:** The universal rail, linked to the switch lock linkage and slidably guided, can be pulled up out of the way so a type lever can be replaced at the front of the bearing block.
