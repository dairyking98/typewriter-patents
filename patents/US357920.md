## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Turning the feed rollers a fixed amount for each new line should be easy for the operator.

**Solution:** A rocking wheel with a pawl on a ratchet wheel fixed to the roller shaft, limited by a stop to one tooth per stroke, with a lug that lifts the pawl out when idle.

**How it works:** A ratchet wheel and a hand wheel are fixed on the roller shaft, with a loose wheel carrying a pawl and a weighted thumb piece between. Rocking the thumb piece engages the pawl and turns the ratchet one tooth, equal to the line spacing, until a sleeve segment strikes the pillow block. In rest the weighted thumb piece returns the wheel so the pawl rides up a lug on the pillow block and is held clear, leaving the roller free to be turned by hand in either direction.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** feed roller; ratchet wheel; loose wheel; pawl; thumb piece; hand wheel; stop segment; lug; pillow block

**Numbers:** one tooth per stroke
