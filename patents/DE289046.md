## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a typewriter with a cylindrical type carrier holding two slidable type segments, swapping the segments by turning the carrier 180 degrees needs a lifting device that does not get in the way during writing.

**Solution:** A bracket mounted on the carrier axis against a coil spring lifts first the two type segments and only then the carrier, so the carrier can be raised and rotated to exchange the segments.

**How it works:** A cylindrical type carrier sits on a vertical shaft and has two grooves in one plane, each holding a slidable type segment. A drive arm below the carrier has a pin that engages a bore in the working segment and positions it for the pressed key. The idle segment is held by a pin on a support arm. A bolt engages one of two diametrical holes in the carrier cross arm to locate it in either position. A bracket whose arms bend under the segments has notches that grip bushings on them. A coil spring gives it lost motion on the shaft, so lifting first frees the segments from the pins, then raises the whole carrier high enough to turn it 180 degrees. At rest the spring presses the bracket down clear of the segments.
