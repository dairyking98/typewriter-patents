## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Depressing the key for a blank space lets the printing mechanism drop onto the paper, which is undesirable.

**Solution:** A stop limits the downward motion of the key and printing mechanism when the key is depressed for spacing, and leaves it free when printing.

**How it works:** The key-tilt mechanism of the Lambert patent is limited by a stop on the space stroke; the type clears the paper because the stop holds the parts above the platen.

**Features:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** multiple key; printing mechanism; platen; stop

**Numbers:** US 640,208
