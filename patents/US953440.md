## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** On each key operation the key's relation to the escapement is adjusted automatically, so the carriage is released at the same point of the upstroke for every key.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement parts wear unevenly and must be made and adjusted precisely, so the carriage does not release at the same point in every key stroke.

**Solution:** On each key operation the key's relation to the escapement is adjusted automatically, so the carriage is released at the same point of the upstroke for every key.
