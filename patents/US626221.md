## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The Smith Premier carriage catches on its margin regulator during return when column work leaves the locking lever past the projections, needing two hands.

**Solution:** An inclined surface on the margin regulator lifts the locking lever over the projections as the carriage moves back.

**How it works:** A sliding margin regulator with two projections stops the carriage and locks the typebars when the locking lever's shoulder meets them. Previously a lifted lever dropped behind the projections so the return caught. The attachment adds an inclined face so the lever rides up and over on the return.
