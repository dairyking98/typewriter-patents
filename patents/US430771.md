## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Wear in typebar pivots lets typebars move out of plane and miss the print point.

**Solution:** A conical (tapered) pivot bearing forces the typebar face against an aligning face when printing, and each radial bracket can be adjusted radially to take up wear.

**How it works:** A series of radial brackets circle the print point, each with a tapered bearing fixed to it that carries one type-bar. As the bar is driven toward printing, the taper pulls its face against an opposite parallel face on the bracket so the bar always swings in one plane. Wear on the cone is compensated by sliding the bracket radially so the type still hits the same point.
