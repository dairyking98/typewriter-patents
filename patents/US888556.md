## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens with sound-deadening devices

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewriter platens, and particularly to the construction of a platen whiciL will materially reduce or completely deaden the noise resulting from the striking of the type bars against the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A tubular rubber body with embedded wire-cloth tubes is fitted onto cast end heads joined by a core, which damps the impact and allows assembly and disassembly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The striking of type bars on the platen is noisy, and platens are hard to assemble and repair.

**Solution:** A tubular rubber body with embedded wire-cloth tubes is fitted onto cast end heads joined by a core, which damps the impact and allows assembly and disassembly.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** tubular rubber body; wire cloth layers; cast end heads; hollow core
