## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a front-strike typebar machine with key levers in three different lengths, the key levers move through different arcs, so a ribbon guide driven by them vibrates unevenly, flickers constantly in fast writing and blurs impressions.

**Solution:** A universal bar riding on the key levers drives a bell crank through a cam slot with an initial rising portion and an arc-shaped dwell, so every key gives the ribbon guide the same lift and rapid typing holds the ribbon raised and still.

**How it works:** Key levers in three banks have stops of three heights so every key travels the same distance at the front, while the rear ends move by different amounts. A universal bar rests on the lever rear ends and carries an arm with a roller in a cam slot of a bell crank. The first part of the slot swings the bell crank through its full stroke, the rest is a dwell on a circle about the key-lever pivot, so extra bar travel does nothing. A link lifts the vertical ribbon guide, which reaches height and rests before the type strikes. In fast writing the bar never falls back, so the ribbon stays up and does not flicker. A two-colour ribbon is positioned accurately without precision parts.
