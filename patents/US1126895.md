## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Roller-platen paper feed devices (feed roller, paper apron, margin guides, paper table) should be simple and cheap to make while working well, and the release of the paper should free all of them together.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal paper apron is guided to move bodily toward and away from the platen under springs, carries the margin guide rods, and is pushed away by one release handle that also frees the main feed roller.

**How it works:** The main feed roller is carried on spring-loaded lever arms pivoted on a transverse rod, pressed against the platen by an adjustable contractile spring. A curved paper apron under the platen has ears with inclined slots riding on rods fixed in the end brackets, so its front part moves forward-down or back-up; springs at its rear ends pull it against the platen and are its only support, so it can be depressed to admit a thick sheaf. A release handle on a rock shaft pushes the apron forward away from the platen and frees the feed roller. Marginal paper fingers with small rollers sit on square shafts that are also carried by the apron, so they release the paper in unison with the apron. A sheet-metal paper table behind the platen is hinged on a stamped bracket with a detent so it can tip forward for access and be removed.
