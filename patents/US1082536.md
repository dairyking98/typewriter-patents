## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen did not automatically adjust for impression sheets of varying thickness, and it was not held fixed during printing.

**Solution:** An automatic mechanism moves the platen to the proper printing position under the type-bar pressure for impression sheets of varying thickness, with a means to hold the platen fixed during printing.
