## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**How it works (reading):** One or more mirrors on the machine, moved in slides or on a rack and pinion, reflect the writing so the operator can read it without lifting the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must raise the carriage to read the writing just typed.

**Solution:** One or more mirrors on the machine, moved in slides or on a rack and pinion, reflect the writing so the operator can read it without lifting the carriage.
