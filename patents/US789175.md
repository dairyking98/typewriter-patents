## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A toy typewriter that can be made very cheaply yet is easier to handle than other toy index machines, and keeps ink off the paper.

**Solution:** A flat disc type wheel with a handle sits on a sheet-metal carriage that slides in a slotted base plate lying on the paper; turn the wheel by hand, press it down near the pointer to print, and advance the carriage with a toothed spacing wheel.

**How it works:** A stamped base plate rests on the paper and has a longitudinal slot; its turned-over edges guide a sheet-metal carriage. The carriage carries the flat-disc type wheel on a vertical pin, with the types on its underside and matching characters on top, and a handle for turning it. An aperture in the carriage over the slot lets only one character touch the paper when the operator presses the wheel near the pointer. A three-armed plate with ink pads sits beneath the wheel and inks the types as the wheel is turned. A spacing wheel loosely mounted in an elastic extension of the carriage rolls along a roughened rack on the base plate; a flat spring stops backward motion and clicks at each tooth, and a scale on the flange shows position.
