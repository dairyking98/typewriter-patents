## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon progression for printing telegraph) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends; Tape or page guiding or feeding devices

**How it works (reading):** A slide rail with two alternating extensions is shifted by stops in the ribbon, so an indexing pawl pair switches to the other reel gear and the ribbon reverses direction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing arrangements on printing telegraph devices are failure-prone and need very precise production and adjustment.

**Solution:** A slide rail with two alternating extensions is shifted by stops in the ribbon, so an indexing pawl pair switches to the other reel gear and the ribbon reverses direction.
