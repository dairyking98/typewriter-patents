## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters for ciphering or deciphering cryptographic text wherein the operative connections between the keys and the type-bars are automatically and continuously permuted, during operation, by a coding or key member the connections being mechanical

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to capsules provided with characters for placing on the keys a typewriter that can be used to substitute arbitrary forms in the order of characters for converting plain text into ciphertext.

**How it works (reading):** Capsules on the keys hold stacked, interchangeable transparent platelets with characters, so the key mapping can be changed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Substitution cipher using typewriter keys is easy to break with fixed type mapping.

**Solution:** Capsules on the keys hold stacked, interchangeable transparent platelets with characters, so the key mapping can be changed.
