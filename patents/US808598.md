## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Access to the underside of the machine is needed, but tilting it risks interfering with or damaging its parts.

**Solution:** The machine as a whole can be tilted on rockers that extend up and rearward from the side plates, giving access to the bottom of the machine.
