## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type rails on a Burroughs printing unit are hard to detach for assembly and repair.

**Solution:** A hinge pin on the type rail shaft passes through the drive segment and is locked by a latch part with a slot, held by a head pin and spring, allowing quick release.

**Features:** [Straight type bar or rule](../features/elem-linear-bar.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
