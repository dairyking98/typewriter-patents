## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters of the time inked through a ribbon or apron; the inventor wants a clear impression made directly from inked type onto the paper.

**Solution:** Type levers arranged radially around a circle swing their types to the centre, and on withdrawal strike an annular ink pad so no ribbon is needed.

**How it works:** Each key lever drives a rack bar (spring-loaded in a slotted tube) that meshes with a toothed segment on a crank arm; the crank rocks a radial type lever, which fulcrums on an annular flange and carries its type to the centre of the circle against the paper. The paper is presented by a sliding carrier of rollers over the circle. On return each type strikes an annular ink pad concentric with the circle and re-inks. The carrier is advanced by a spring and an escapement operated by the type levers.
