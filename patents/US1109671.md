## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen must rotate backward to remove a bill and forward to position a new one, and the record sheet must advance a different amount in each direction.

**Solution:** A single driver rod on the carriage, moved in one direction, drives racks that rotate the platen forward through a greater angle and return it without rotating the platen.
