## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices, non-fluid media or methods for cancelling, correcting errors, underscoring or ruling

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, and has particular reference toan improved device or attachment for typewriting enabling the operator to quickly and neatly.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary underscoring character is mounted on the traveling type carriage and brought to the platen by an operating device, so a whole line or part of a line can be underscored in one pass.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Underscoring lines by the usual methods wastes time and leaves untidy results.

**Solution:** An auxiliary underscoring character is mounted on the traveling type carriage and brought to the platen by an operating device, so a whole line or part of a line can be underscored in one pass.
