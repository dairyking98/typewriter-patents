> Description Translated from German Protective device provided for erasing purposes on writing instruments and the like Machines Those intended for erasing purposes on typewriters and similar office machines Protection device according to the invention is formed by a arranged on the paper trolley, in and, outside of the area of the platen, movable swivel flap for collecting and deriving the material waste, which occurs when on one over the platen led writing sheet is erased. This swivel flap can be used in a relatively simple manner Avoid the erasing of: Material waste in the type lever mechanism and other moving and guiding parts of the machine penetrate and as a result leads to faster wear and tear and also causes disturbances in movement processes can. The mentioned swivel flap serving to catch and divert the waste material expediently has a collecting channel intended for the material discharge in the longitudinal direction.
