## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Guards, shields or dust excluders

**How it works (reading):** A pivoting flap on the paper carriage, mounted outside the platen area, has a collecting channel with a longitudinal discharge to catch and divert the erasing waste.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasing on a typewriter leaves rubber waste that falls into the type lever mechanism and guide parts, causing wear and faults.

**Solution:** A pivoting flap on the paper carriage, mounted outside the platen area, has a collecting channel with a longitudinal discharge to catch and divert the erasing waste.
