## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (spacing for combined typewriter and adder) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to spacing mechanism for machines-(if that class which embrace a traveling carriage movable to different positions to space printed characters or to select different denominations, as for instance of an addlng mechamsm.

**How it works (reading):** Automatic spacing mechanism for the traveling carriage that spaces over comma and decimal positions without a separate space key operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing commas or decimal points in adding-typewriter figures wastes time because the operator must press the space key over each comma or decimal gap.

**Solution:** Automatic spacing mechanism for the traveling carriage that spaces over comma and decimal positions without a separate space key operation.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)
