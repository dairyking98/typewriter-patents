## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (character perforating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a machine which will serially perforate a series of letters or characters through a piece of paper, whereby permanent characters are obtained.

**How it works (reading):** A machine that serially perforates numbers and characters through paper, using needles on a plate on a main shaft and a stripper and female die plate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Characters written with ink can be changed or erased, so permanent characters are wanted on checks and bills.

**Solution:** A machine that serially perforates numbers and characters through paper, using needles on a plate on a main shaft and a stripper and female die plate.

**How it works:** A shaft carries a needle plate and a female die plate with slots; the stripper plate has holes so the needles pass through and puncture the paper character by character.

**Features:** [Type disc (types on the face)](../features/elem-disc-face.md), [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
