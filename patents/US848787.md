## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** An operator of a front-strike Underwood needs a simple, easily applied way to bring either colour band of a two-colour ribbon into use.

**Solution:** Two ribbon-vibrating levers of unequal stroke move together at every strike, and the ribbon carrier is rocked sideways to connect with either the short-stroke or the long-stroke lever.

**How it works:** Two levers pivot on one screw on the platen shift rail, and their arms ride in a single slot in an actuator driven by the universal-bar frame at each type stroke. The arm lengths differ, so one lever lifts the ribbon carrier a short distance and the other a long distance. A shifter bar with forks bridging a pin on the carrier rocks the carrier slightly sideways on its guide ears to engage one lever or the other. The bar is moved by a rock shaft with two colour-coded keys over the keyboard, and a spring detent holds either position. The lateral rock is small enough not to hinder the carrier's up and down travel.
