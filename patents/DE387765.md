## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage base plate must be filed to fit the fixed stops of the machine frame, which can only be done in a repair shop when the paper roller is changed.

**Solution:** The front stops are made adjustable as wedge pieces guided in grooves of the frame cross-bars and pressed against the carriage base plate by head screws, so the carriage can be aligned without filing.
