## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier Armstrong type-wheel machine used two curved longitudinal slots for the location pins; the inventor wants a separate location arm with one slot per pin, better key-lever connections, and a better way to raise and lower the wheel between its case rows.

**Solution:** A keyboard of location pins pushes a reciprocating carriage rearward while a pivoted slotted location plate on that carriage is swung by the pin in its slot, rotating a three-row type wheel through a gear, with a separate key-driven slide lifting the wheel for the figure and capital rows.

**How it works:** Each key lever raises a vertical location pin and, through an inclined slot, swings a lever that pushes the type-wheel carriage rearward. The location plate, pivoted on the carriage and toothed at its rear edge, has one angled slot over every pin; as the carriage moves back the raised pin cams the plate sideways, and the plate's teeth turn a gear and arm that rotate the type wheel to the chosen character. The slot angles are opposite either side of centre and shallower toward the ends, so the rotation is proportional to the key position. A notched centering disk on the wheel engages a fixed lug at the print point to lock alignment, and the same lug and notch re-centre the plate on return. A vertical slide with a forked arm in a groove in the wheel hub lifts the wheel to one of three annular rows (lower case, figures, capitals), worked by a figure key and a cap key with a latch that holds the capital position. Spring rods return the keys, and a yoke line gage straddles the print point.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** key lever; location pin; type-wheel carriage; location plate; slotted cam; operating gear; type wheel; centering disk; centering lug; vertical lifting slide; forked arm; figure key; cap key; latch arm; line gage; roller platen

**Numbers:** three annular series of characters on the wheel; six sheets of drawings; one slot per location pin

**Improves on:**

- Armstrong's earlier patent with two longitudinal curved slots: uses transverse angled slots, one per pin, in a separate location plate geared to the wheel
