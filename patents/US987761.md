## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The back-spacing dog does not disengage cleanly from the back-spacing rack when the key is released, and it can be forced into abnormal engagement with the rack teeth.

**Solution:** The rack-engaging dog is formed integral with its carrying link and is resiliently held against the back-spacing rack; it leaves the rack as soon as the back-space key is relieved, and an adjustable stop lets the engaging teeth self-adjust.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
