## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of copyholders especially designed to receive shorthand note-books or similar copy.

**How it works (reading):** A copy-holder board on a standard that holds the note-book and turns its leaves, operated either by the typewriter carriage or by hand.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shorthand copyists must keep their place and turn book leaves without taking the book out of the copy holder.

**Solution:** A copy-holder board on a standard that holds the note-book and turns its leaves, operated either by the typewriter carriage or by hand.
