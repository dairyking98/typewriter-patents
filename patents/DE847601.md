## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by its application to mechanism in which two spools are driven by pawl-and-rachet mechanism comprising a single pawl or integral double-tooth pawl selectively engageable with two ratchets, one for each spool; Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the invention is to provide a ribbon feeding and switching device to create the two coils' automatically alternating by simple means driving and switching allowed.

**How it works (reading):** Both ribbon reels have ratchet wheels that share one feed pawl on a sliding slide and one locking pawl; when the ribbon end reaches a spool, a trigger moves the slide and lever to switch the pawls from the full reel to the empty one.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reels must switch between full and empty positions by a feed and lock arrangement that does not need a separate drive for each spool.

**Solution:** Both ribbon reels have ratchet wheels that share one feed pawl on a sliding slide and one locking pawl; when the ribbon end reaches a spool, a trigger moves the slide and lever to switch the pawls from the full reel to the empty one.
