## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**How it works (reading):** The holder has a suction-plate foot, a rotatable and pivotable joint, and a height-adjustable frame that adapts to paper size.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Document holders on desks are badly placed, slip, and only attach to the back of a machine.

**Solution:** The holder has a suction-plate foot, a rotatable and pivotable joint, and a height-adjustable frame that adapts to paper size.
