## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to What are connnonly termed carbon rollv mechanisms for typewriting machines and the object of said invention, generally stated.

**How it works (reading):** A carbon-roll holder with a guide roller and a material-feeding device carries the carbon, with paper controlling mechanism around the platen; it is shown on a front-strike machine but said to be adaptable to other styles.
