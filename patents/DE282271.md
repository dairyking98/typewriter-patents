## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races; Ball or roller bearings; Means to synchronise movements

**How it works (reading):** The paper carriage runs on two outer rails with ball races; an adjusting screw or wedge moves the two guide rods equally so the platen roller centre stays in the same position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage must stay centred on the platen as it runs on its guide rails.

**Solution:** The paper carriage runs on two outer rails with ball races; an adjusting screw or wedge moves the two guide rods equally so the platen roller centre stays in the same position.
