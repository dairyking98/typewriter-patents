## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator should not have to work separate feeds by hand: pressing the printing key should advance the paper automatically either sideways or vertically, with reversible direction and adjustable pitch.

**Solution:** A slider on the printing-key lever switches the key's motion to a vertical-feed or horizontal-feed lever, and a swingable clam plate carrying two meshing gears chooses the direction of rack feed while a cam sets the pitch.

**How it works:** A slider on the printing-key lever has a pin that rests on a projection of either the vertical-feed lever or the horizontal-feed lever, so pressing the key pushes down one of them. For the vertical feed, a link swings an arm loose on the cylinder shaft; its pawl idles over a ratchet on the press and turns the cylinder when the key is released. For the horizontal feed, a link pulls back a pawl from a gear on the press, and on release the pawl re-engages and turns the gear by a number of teeth set by a cam plate and numbered knob (1, 2, 3). Two meshing gears sit on a clam-shaped plate; swinging it brings one gear or the other into the rack on the cylinder frame, which reverses the direction, a spring roller latching it in notches. A handle swings the rack out of mesh for hand movement.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)
