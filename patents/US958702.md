## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A shift lock for a platen-shift typebar machine needs to hold the carriage raised and release easily.

**Solution:** A shift-lock finger piece whose lever forms a toggle lock with a pin on the shift rock-shaft arm, released by pressing either shift key.

**How it works:** On the Royal Standard, finger pieces on stems rock a shaft at the front, which draws a link and rocks a rock shaft to raise the carriage shift rail until it meets stops; a latch holds the carriage down and is lifted first by lost motion. The shift lock key presses its stem and lever down, carrying the shift key stem and arm down. At the end of the stroke a pin on the arm enters a concave notch on the end of a spring-loaded lever, a toggle lock holding the platen up. Pressing either shift key flexes the parts, frees the pin and the spring returns the lock.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** shift rail; rock shaft; shift link; finger piece; shift lock lever; lever spring; pin; notch; latch; counterbalance spring
