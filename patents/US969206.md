## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Turning the platen and releasing the carriage require raising a hand well away from the keys, interrupting writing.

**Solution:** A detachable frame of rods running with the carriage puts a thumb piece for line spacing and a slide for carriage release next to the space key.

**How it works:** A shifting rod slides in a tubular guide fixed to the front frame bar near the space key, with a thumb piece. A second rod, joined to the first by a cross brace, is carried by a bracket on the carriage and hooked to the line-spacing lever. Pressing the thumb piece moves the frame and turns the platen, and the thumb piece travels with the carriage so it is always in reach. A slide on the cross brace works compound levers that trip the carriage release lever. A variant covers machines with both levers on the same side.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** shifting rod; tubular guide; thumb piece; second rod; cross brace; bracket; line-spacing lever; release slide; compound lever; return spring

**Improves on:**

- standard line-space and release levers: controls moved within reach of the keys
