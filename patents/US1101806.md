## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to provide means by which the envelope to be used with such form letters may be automatically withdrawn from a supply and fed into the embrace of the paper feeding rolls, vhereby they may be addressed by means of his 6r some other suitable typewriter opcrating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A removable attachment withdraws envelopes from a supply and feeds them into the paper feed rolls under the control of the automatic mechanism.
