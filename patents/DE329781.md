## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Preparing or treating stereotype matrices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a switching device for die embossing machines and typewriters, by means of which can be used to create any spacing between the printed characters, so that letters are immediately strung together, or a specific one is more uniform Distance between them as well as a particularly large distance to achieve Barrier font and the like can be achieved.

**How it works (reading):** Worms on the carriage rack engage switching gears on the key transmission shafts, and ratchets on the keys can be engaged at will, so each key sets a chosen spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Fixed spacing between printed characters in embossing machines and typewriters could not be varied to string letters together or set wider spacing.

**Solution:** Worms on the carriage rack engage switching gears on the key transmission shafts, and ratchets on the keys can be engaged at will, so each key sets a chosen spacing.
