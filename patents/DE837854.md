**How it works (reading):** A cotton typewriter ribbon is impregnated with a colourless liquid holding a colour-reactive dye, which marks only sensitive recording material when it is struck.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible records made by typewriter need a tape that transfers a colour reaction onto sensitive paper without staining other objects.

**Solution:** A cotton typewriter ribbon is impregnated with a colourless liquid holding a colour-reactive dye, which marks only sensitive recording material when it is struck.
