## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters, their type carriers are pressed noiselessly to the paper roller by a toggle joint by stretching it by bending an auxiliary knee lever joint, and consists in that the auxiliary toggle joint with its one leg on the machine frame supported, acts with the other leg on the toggle joint and in the starting position is stretched.

**How it works (reading):** Type levers rotate on an arc around a fixed centre and are pressed noiselessly onto the paper roller by a toggle joint; an auxiliary knee-lever joint is stretched in the rest position and bent only when the key is struck, so the stroke is light and the type is held against rebound.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers that strike the paper roller make noise, and after the stroke the type can rebound from the roller.

**Solution:** Type levers rotate on an arc around a fixed centre and are pressed noiselessly onto the paper roller by a toggle joint; an auxiliary knee-lever joint is stretched in the rest position and bent only when the key is struck, so the stroke is light and the type is held against rebound.
