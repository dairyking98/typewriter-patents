## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter with noiseless toggle joint on each Paper roller pressed type carriers The invention relates to typewriters, their type carriers are pressed noiselessly to the paper roller by a toggle joint by stretching it by bending an auxiliary knee lever joint, and consists in that the auxiliary toggle joint with its one leg on the machine frame supported, acts with the other leg on the toggle joint and in the starting position is stretched. This has the advantage that when the type is returned the type lever arrives in its initial position in its rest position, at the same time the auxiliary knee lever joint in the extended position and thereby the type against blocks a rebound. Furthermore, according to the invention, the auxiliary toggle joint, which is stretched in the basic position of the type drive and supported on the machine name, is connected to the key, while the two toggle joints connecting this toggle joint to the type lever are folded together in the basic position and are only stretched when the key is struck.
