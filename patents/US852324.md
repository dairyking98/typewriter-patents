## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a tabulator device for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A rotating graduated stop on a spindle, with a sleeve and lever, is set against the tabular stops with a spring-loaded thumb, and a brake plate on the sleeve acts on a flat brake bar on the carriage when the tabulator releases it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tabulator stop has to be turned and fixed in different positions, and the carriage brake on release must act reliably.

**Solution:** A rotating graduated stop on a spindle, with a sleeve and lever, is set against the tabular stops with a spring-loaded thumb, and a brake plate on the sleeve acts on a flat brake bar on the carriage when the tabulator releases it.
