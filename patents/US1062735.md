## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms for ensuring maximum life of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates broadly to the typewriting art and specifically to a mechanism for preventing excessive vibration of the ribbon vibrator during a continuous operation of printing, and for permitting the said ribbon to assume a position for exposing the printing when the operator ceases to print.

**How it works (reading):** A retarder or controller, a piston in a cylinder with a vent, damps the ribbon vibrator so that less energy is needed to keep it vibrating, which lightens the key touch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon vibrator that continually moves over the printing point throws a changing light into the operator's eyes and wastes energy, making the key touch heavier.

**Solution:** A retarder or controller, a piston in a cylinder with a vent, damps the ribbon vibrator so that less energy is needed to keep it vibrating, which lightens the key touch.

**How it works:** The ribbon vibrator covers and uncovers the printing point like a shutter. A controller with a piston, shown near the top of its stroke, and a vent acts as a retarder on the vibrator, adapted to both vertically and otherwise moving vibrators. It conserves much of the energy needed to keep the vibrator moving, so the key touch is lighter, and lets the ribbon rest in a position that exposes the printing when the operator stops.

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; piston; cylinder; vent; retarder; carriage

**Improves on:**

- ribbon vibrator that flickers and needs continual energy: piston retarder on the vibrator conserves energy and steadies it
