## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by the provision of additional devices for producing a punched or like record, e.g. simultaneously

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the present invention is to overcome the above-mentioned difficulty and render the lateral movement of the computer-operating mechanism independent of the movement of the type-writer carriage.

**How it works (reading):** Give the computer's plunger carriage its own escapement, operated by the numeral keys, so its lateral movement is independent of the typewriter carriage.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In the inventor's earlier combined typewriter and adding machine, the plunger carriage moved with the typewriter carriage so commas and periods that space the carriage threw the plungers onto the wrong computing keys.

**Solution:** Give the computer's plunger carriage its own escapement, operated by the numeral keys, so its lateral movement is independent of the typewriter carriage.

**How it works:** A computing machine with loosely journaled digit wheels sits below a normal typebar typewriter. Numeral keys, through a lever system with a safety device ensuring full travel, strike the computer keys via plungers, while a separate escapement-bar set moves the computer carriage. Tens are carried between digit wheels, and an indicator can stop the typewriter carriage at the proper point in its travel. The rest of the specification is not in the opening text.
