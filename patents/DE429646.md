## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An operator must keep changing gaze between the keys and the written line, which tires the eye and slows work.

**Solution:** A mirror system places the eye-side mirror at roughly equal distance to the keys and the written line so both appear together without looking away.
