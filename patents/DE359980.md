## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**How it works (reading):** Curved surfaces on a roller lever keep the link in contact with the lever during the switch, and a leaf spring adds damping.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper roll switching produces an annoying impact noise at the end of its stroke.

**Solution:** Curved surfaces on a roller lever keep the link in contact with the lever during the switch, and a leaf spring adds damping.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
