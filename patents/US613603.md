## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line spacing is fixed by the ratchet, so lines cannot be spaced at an arbitrary distance.

**Solution:** A clutch section turned by the spacing lever engages the platen only for part of the stroke, set by a cam on an adjustable setting plate with a scale.

**How it works:** A clutch section is loose on the platen shaft, held away by a spring, and oscillated by the spacing lever through a pitman. A clutch-setting plate on the carriage with a cam surface forces the clutch section into engagement with the platen. Turning the plate toward the front causes early engagement and a long platen turn for wide line spacing; toward the rear gives narrow spacing. A thumb nut clamps the plate in a curved slot, with scale and pointer. A graduation under the platen helps match ruled paper.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** spacing lever; clutch section; setting plate; cam surface; quadrant with scale; pointer; thumb nut; pitman
