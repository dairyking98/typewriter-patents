## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen is locked to the ratchet wheel, so it cannot be moved freely or stopped anywhere.

**Solution:** A clutch disconnects the platen from its ratchet wheel when pressure is removed, and a knob-mounted control lets it be turned either way.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen; ratchet wheel; friction disk; bell crank levers

> Description B. DUMMIT. TYPE WRITER ATTAGHMBNT. APPLICATION FILED JUNE 2, 1910. Patented Apr. 18, 1911. Inventor. Witnesses Attorneys BERT DUlVIlVIIT, OF PIERCE CITY, MISSOURI. TYPE-WRITER ATTACHMENT. T all whom "it may concern: Be it known that I, BERT DUMMIT, a citizen of the United States, residing at Pierce City, in the county of Lawrence and State of Missouri, have invented a new and useful Type-Writer Attachment, of which the following is a specification. This invention relates to typewriter attachments. The object of the invention is to provide means for disconnecting the platen of a typewriter from the ratchet wheel which is normally connected therewith so as to move the platen independently of the ratchet wheel. This ratchet wheel normally prevents rearward rotation of the platen and also causes it to move in a step by'step manner, always coming to rest at a certain point. By providing the means of the present invention, whereby the platen can be unclutched from the ratchet wheel, it will be obvious that the platen can be moved either rearward or forward without regard to the ratchet wheel and can be brought to rest at any desired point.
