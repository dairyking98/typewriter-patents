## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage position numbers were too small and occupied too much room, and were hard to read from the operator's position.

**Solution:** Two similar number-wheels side by side (tens and units) show the carriage position through a sight-hole in the top plate, with the units wheel positively connected to the carriage and the tens wheel advanced once per units revolution.

**How it works:** The units wheel is driven by the carriage; a tens-carrying device advances the tens wheel once at each completed units cycle.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** units number-wheel; tens number-wheel; tens-carrying device; sight-hole in top plate

**Numbers:** US 685,121; 1901-10-22
