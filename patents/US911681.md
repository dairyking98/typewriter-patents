## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Actuators in banks cannot easily drive actuated members arranged in a circle, and obstacles between them make linkage impractical.

**Solution:** Fluid conduits transmit a set amount of movement from keys at one end to a piston at the other, so that dissimilarly arranged parts receive the same movement regardless of obstacles.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
