## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** ..Mechanism driven by a spring tensioned by power means

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates mainly to a method by which the paper carriage of a typewriter is worked independently in both its forward and its backward motion.

**How it works (reading):** Two opposed spring barrels pull the carriage by separate tapes; swinging gear frames disconnect each barrel from its spring so the idle barrel turns freely without changing spring tension, and weak auxiliary springs plus extra keys restore tension.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage drive by one mainspring makes the carriage return fight the spring, and the spring tension varies with carriage position; the inventor wants forward and backward motion each powered independently by its own spring.

**Solution:** Two opposed spring barrels pull the carriage by separate tapes; swinging gear frames disconnect each barrel from its spring so the idle barrel turns freely without changing spring tension, and weak auxiliary springs plus extra keys restore tension.

**How it works:** A forward tape and a backward tape are both fixed to the carriage and wound on two spring barrels on shafts, each held by ratchets and pawls. Pressing the forward key releases the forward pawl so the forward spring pulls the carriage left; at the same time a swinging frame shifts a gear pair out of mesh so the backward barrel turns freely on its shaft and its spring is not wound. The backward key does the mirror action. Because the main springs lose tension with position, auxiliary key presses free the shaft so the carriage is pushed by hand against the other spring, winding the weaker auxiliary spring back toward its first tension.
