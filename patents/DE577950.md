## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Travel typewriters need the carriage held in the central position and the shift released, and known devices use too many parts.

**Solution:** A pivoted locking bolt holds the carriage centered and, through a link to the shift lock key, releases the shift lock when it is set.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on ball-bearing rails on a shift frame that moves up and down under a shift key lever, which has a shift lock catch holding it in the upper position. A manually operated angle lever on the left side plate swings a locking arm into the carriage path to stop it at the centre. A link joins the locking bolt to the shift lock so setting the bolt releases the shift lock and drops the shift frame, while moving the shift lock key does not move the bolt. A spring can hold the bolt in its inactive position. A hood slips over the machine on a base plate.
