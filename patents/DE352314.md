## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (character printing unit for calculators) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms; Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a character printing unit that solves this problem.

**How it works (reading):** A crank on the operating axis is set by a firing rod from the keyboard into four rotational positions (normal addition, total or subtotal, subtraction, print without addition), each selecting a ribbon zone and whether the minus sign is carried to the hammer-driven type bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A calculating-machine printing unit must print different ribbon zones and minus or total marks in one billing run, which a single fixed print setting cannot do.

**Solution:** A crank on the operating axis is set by a firing rod from the keyboard into four rotational positions (normal addition, total or subtotal, subtraction, print without addition), each selecting a ribbon zone and whether the minus sign is carried to the hammer-driven type bar.
