## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (rolled strip cards for checkout) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printed matter of special format or style not otherwise provided for

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose is to prevent unauthorized access for the purpose of robbery, but also one to bring about a quick and absolutely correct checkout.

**How it works (reading):** The roll ends are closed with a coloured or plain embossing, which is destroyed on unwinding and shows tampering.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Rolled strip-card rolls can be unwound and tampered with without the seal showing.

**Solution:** The roll ends are closed with a coloured or plain embossing, which is destroyed on unwinding and shows tampering.
