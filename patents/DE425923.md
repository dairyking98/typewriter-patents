## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register subtotals)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register for printing individual amounts, intermediate and Totals with a main totalizer and one or more special totalizers.

**How it works (reading):** A special adder is used for the subtotals and totals; setting a control lever and turning the crank routes the total of the individual amounts to the receipt and control strip, and the special adder is reset to zero on the next operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Subtotals and grand totals for a customer with several receipts must be printed, which the single adder cannot do.

**Solution:** A special adder is used for the subtotals and totals; setting a control lever and turning the crank routes the total of the individual amounts to the receipt and control strip, and the special adder is reset to zero on the next operation.
