## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Inventor wants a compromise between type-wheel machines and machines whose type move individually, with a uniform key stroke, three character sets without a full shift, and a choice of typographic or uniform spacing.

**Solution:** A rotating wheel carries sliding type slugs in three 120-degree segments; the first part of a key stroke turns the wheel to lock on the chosen slug, and the rest of the stroke pushes the shaft down so a hammer throws that slug against the paper.

**How it works:** A vertical shaft carries a dish-shaped wheel with sliding type on dovetail pins around the rim; a stationary cover plate with a groove holds the lugs in except at the print position. The shaft has two sets of spiral vanes wound in opposite directions. Each key lever pulls down two cross-bars by graduated hooks and nuts, so rollers on the bars press the two vane sets and rotate the shaft until both rollers bear, which locks the wheel with the chosen slug at the print position. The key then releases a latch, the locked shaft is carried down, and a link drives a radial hammer bar that pushes the slug outward through a V-socket that centres it onto the paper. A spring returns the slug, and an ink pad swings away and across an ink roller. Two shift keys turn a sleeve 120 degrees to bring another type segment and another vane set into use, and the same shift selects a notched wing that sets the carriage pawl travel for typographic spacing; a straight-edge setting gives uniform spacing.

**Parts:** vertical shaft; dish-shaped wheel; type slug; cover plate; hammer bar; link; spiral vane; roller; key lever; cross-bar; latch; shift key; sleeve; ink pad; ink roller; spacing pawl; ratchet wheel; spacing wing; paper carriage

**Numbers:** three type segments of 120 degrees each; three vanes per set, two sets

**Improves on:**

- Type-wheel machines with whole type fixed on the wheel: slugs slide out to print, so the wheel itself does not strike
- Machines with individually moving type (typebars): the characters sit on a wheel and one uniform key action selects and prints
