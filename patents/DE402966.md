## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In punched-card totalizing and printing machines where a spring drives the types, correct printing and summing depend on the smooth running of many parts, errors are hard to trace, and the printing cannot be checked on the card itself.

**Solution:** A pin dropping through the card hole into a recess of a moving plate drags the type frame forward positively, so the same movement positions the types and later turns the adding wheel.

**How it works:** A card with a hole for a digit lies on a plate with ten recesses, which moves a fixed distance horizontally. A spring pushes a pin down through the hole into the matching recess, and the pin takes the type frame along by the hole's position. At the end of travel, two hammers strike the selected digit onto the printing roller and onto the card itself, and the adding wheel engages the frame's rack. On the return, the frame is pulled back the same distance, turning the adding wheel by that many teeth. A stop shaped like the rack teeth corrects the frame position for straight printing, a lever per row blocks hammers for leading zeros, the plate is lowered at the end to free the pin, and rollers eject the card.
