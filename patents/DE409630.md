**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (duplicating device on typewriters) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** Subject of The invention is a duplicating device for use on typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A common rod carries the ribbon arms in pairs and swings them on short shafts by cranks, so all arms or single pairs move out of working position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Spring-retained ribbon arms weaken and fail to hold the ribbon in working position.

**Solution:** A common rod carries the ribbon arms in pairs and swings them on short shafts by cranks, so all arms or single pairs move out of working position.
