## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (point-by-point printing device, signals) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the printing or marking process for which they are designed characterised by selective application of impact or pressure on a printing material or impression-transfer material using print wires; Actuators for print wires

**How it works (reading):** Characters are composed point by point from grouped printing wires chosen by buttons, and a template pressed against the wire ends prints the selected character.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing punch-card and tape characters in plain text must not slow punching, and a wire-by-wire print must be fast.

**Solution:** Characters are composed point by point from grouped printing wires chosen by buttons, and a template pressed against the wire ends prints the selected character.

**Features:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
