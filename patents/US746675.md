## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Covers for typewriters and adding machines stayed in the way, and their frames did not fold compactly or return to the closed-off position on their own.

**Solution:** A collapsible frame carries a flexible cover; releasing the cover moves it automatically to the inoperative position, and the frame folds compactly, keeping the flexible covering smooth.
