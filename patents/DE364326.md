## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The resilient link between the shift key lever and the lifting parts of a platen-shift carriage needed to be simple, strong and adjustable.

**Solution:** An angular auxiliary lever joined to the shift key lever by an adjustable spring lets the key overtravel without straining the carriage lift.

**How it works:** Pressing the shift key lever swings an auxiliary lever, which through one link releases the locking pawl from the carriage arm and through another lifts the carriage and platen. A loop-and-spring connection gives lost motion so the pawl disengages before lifting. Further key travel is absorbed by a pin sliding in an oversized hole while the spring tensions. The spring attaches to a toothed arc, so tension can be adjusted.
