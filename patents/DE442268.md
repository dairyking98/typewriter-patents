## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting the type segment up or down between rows needed a complex lever linkage with sliders on each shift key.

**Solution:** A lifting shaft with a release cam, directly under the shift lever, both unlocks and moves the raisable segment through simple supports.

**How it works:** The type bearing segment sits in tubular guides and normally is locked in a middle position. Striking a shift key turns the lifting shaft via the shift lever; its cam releases the lock lever and the shaft rises or falls, carrying the segment through supports to the upper or lower case row. The locking device holds it in the middle or end positions.
