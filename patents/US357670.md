## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to secure uniform spacing between the letters of a word in the operation of printing on type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** The paper carriage is fed one, two or three times a minimum step depending on letter width, with types sized so the leftover gap is equal for all letters.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Uniform visual spacing between letters of different widths with a carriage that normally advances a fixed step.

**Solution:** The paper carriage is fed one, two or three times a minimum step depending on letter width, with types sized so the leftover gap is equal for all letters.

**How it works:** Letters are grouped into narrow, medium and wide classes; the carriage feed after each key is the narrowest letter width plus the wanted gap, or double, or triple that. The types are aligned on their left margins and proportioned so the remaining gap after each feed is equal. The feed is made in a single movement of the key or type lever, in a machine with radial type levers driven by toothed segments on rocking arms in a supporting annulus.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; type lever; rocking arm; toothed segment; feed lever; supporting annulus

**Numbers:** feed of 1, 2 or 3 times the minimum step
