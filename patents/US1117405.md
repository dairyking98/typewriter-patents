## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker; Intermittent grip type mechanical movement

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, and is especially directed toward the escapement mechanism which feeds the carriage forward a letter-space ata time as the type keys are operated.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-fed drum engages the carriage ratchet by pawls and carries a rack that controls the carriage; a universal bar driven by each type-stroke operates the escapement dog.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage escapement should feed one letter-space at a time, with the carriage spring-fed and controlled by the universal bar.

**Solution:** A spring-fed drum engages the carriage ratchet by pawls and carries a rack that controls the carriage; a universal bar driven by each type-stroke operates the escapement dog.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** spring drum; ratchet wheel; pawls; rack on drum; universal bar; escapement dog
