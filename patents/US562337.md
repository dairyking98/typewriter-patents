## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** General improvements of a front-strike visible U-type-bar typewriter.

**Solution:** A series of improvements to upstroke visible machines: platen actuating, escapement, margin stop, bell, ribbon shifting and ribbon spools.

**How it works:** The paper carriage has a rack bar and pinion, a platen line-feed ratchet with a feed arm and dog, a margin stop, bell trip and platen shifting. An escape wheel on a shaft with an oscillating detent lever, driven through a rocking frame, spaces the carriage. Ribbon feed and ribbon shifting are by shafts and spools with blank-formed heads.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** type-lever; paper carriage; rack-bar; pinion; escape wheel; detent lever; platen ratchet; margin stop; bell; ribbon spool; shift lever

**Numbers:** 41 figures

**Improves on:**

- US528481 and US524275: further improves the U-form visible typebar machine
