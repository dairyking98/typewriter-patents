## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a universal rod drive for the paper trolley switching device from typewriters, and in particular to one with which the universal rod is moved when switching with the non-switchable carrier.

**How it works (reading):** Two universal rods that both connect to the pawl carrier: the key-moved rod starts the carriage shift and the type-lever-moved rod ends it, combining easy key travel with uniform release.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Universal bars moved by the type lever slow the keystroke, and bars moved by the keys do not release the shift uniformly.

**Solution:** Two universal rods that both connect to the pawl carrier: the key-moved rod starts the carriage shift and the type-lever-moved rod ends it, combining easy key travel with uniform release.
