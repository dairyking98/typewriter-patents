## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks need their face value printed in words or symbols quickly and reliably, with the type set, inked, pressed and reset automatically.

**Solution:** A check-printing machine uses a series of type wheels with characters on their peripheries, rotated to set positions, locked, inked at the print line, then pressed against the article and released and restored.

**How it works:** Setting devices rotate each wheel to its character; brakes lock the wheels; an inking agent wets the presented type; the article is brought against the type line; the wheels are released and returned to rest.

**Features:** [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [IMP:platen-to-element](../features/imp-platen-to-element.md)

**Parts:** type wheels; type-wheel brakes; inking mechanism
