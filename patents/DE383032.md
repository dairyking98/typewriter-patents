## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A shaft carries a sliding coupling member with two arms of different lengths; the accent key rocks the shaft, and the chosen arm length sets the size of the ribbon stroke through inclined slots and pins.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon stroke on visible-type machines is driven by a universal rail with accent keys, and existing devices for varying stroke size are too large and heavy, causing vibration.

**Solution:** A shaft carries a sliding coupling member with two arms of different lengths; the accent key rocks the shaft, and the chosen arm length sets the size of the ribbon stroke through inclined slots and pins.
