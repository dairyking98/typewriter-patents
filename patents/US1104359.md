## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Silent typewriters need a ribbon reversing mechanism that operates with as little noise as possible.

**Solution:** Two worm-driven ribbon spools engage drive pins alternately, and a hand cam shaft lifts one spool off its pin and lowers the other to reverse feed direction.

**How it works:** A shaft turned by a ratchet wheel and pawl on a reciprocating arm worked by the key levers drives oppositely-handed worms at each end. These turn worm wheels on hollow shafts that carry pins engaging holes in the ribbon spool sides. Spool spindles rest on a cam shaft with two cam faces about 120 degrees apart, so only one spool is engaged at a time. Turning the cam shaft by its handle swaps which spool feeds and which receives.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** ratchet wheel; pawl; reciprocating arm; worm; worm wheel; hollow shaft; ribbon spool; drive pin; cam shaft; operating handle

**Numbers:** cam faces about 120 degrees apart

**Improves on:**

- noisy ribbon reversing devices: worm drive and spool-lifting cam operate silently
