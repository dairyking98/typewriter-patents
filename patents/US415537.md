## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machine escapement and key-lever return need a durable, adjustable arrangement.

**Solution:** An escapement plate linked to a bar under the key-levers, pulled back by an adjustable spring which also lifts the levers.

**How it works:** Type-bars are clipped into a frame pivoted on a rear rod. A front bar under the key-levers carries a hook-footed escapement plate carrying dogs; each key stroke moves the plate down vertically against an adjustable spring whose threaded non-round pin is set by a nut. The spring raises key-levers and returns the escapement with a straight pull. The plate's upper end bears against an arc-headed pivoted segment to reduce friction and wear.
