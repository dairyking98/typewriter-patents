**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to new and useful improvements in controllers for type-writers;

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper controllers and fixed fingers block the view of printed matter, and the paper must be held against the platen-roller.

**Solution:** Fine tension wires are stretched between the carriage ends, and adjustable fingers on these wires guide the paper under them against the platen.
