## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (date printing for calculating machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Syllable, line, or like type selection

**How it works (reading):** Two slidable plates with stop lugs, moved by swinging out the main shaft, limit the disc travel so that the ones and tens date keys stop at the prescribed value.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Date setting on ten-key calculators normally needs its own key set, and the Dalton shared-key system cannot give tens digits a full range.

**Solution:** Two slidable plates with stop lugs, moved by swinging out the main shaft, limit the disc travel so that the ones and tens date keys stop at the prescribed value.
