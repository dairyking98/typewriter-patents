## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check printing machine controls) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cheque stamping machines

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to improve the manually-operable mechanism by which hoththe selective and the impressing operations of the printingmeans are controlled, and particularly1 to producev an arrangement in which two easily performed movements of a single manually-operable member serve for Vthe accomplishment of both of these functions.

**How it works (reading):** A single operating member moves sideways to select a character on a rotatable type wheel and then longitudinally to press the check against the type with a movable platen, with a lock preventing sideways movement during the impression.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check-writing machines need a simpler manual control in which one member both selects the character and impresses it, instead of separate operations.

**Solution:** A single operating member moves sideways to select a character on a rotatable type wheel and then longitudinally to press the check against the type with a movable platen, with a lock preventing sideways movement during the impression.
