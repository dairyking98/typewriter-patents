## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The keyless type cylinder, swinging down to print, is not guided accurately enough in its downstroke and wobbles sideways, spoiling the position of the impression.

**Solution:** Two parallel combs, one each side of the cylinder spindle, are engaged by notched arms on the spindle sleeve so the cylinder is held at two lateral points at the instant of impression.

**How it works:** The type cylinder turns on a spindle in a swinging sleeve that can move horizontally and vertically, and is brought down against the paper roller by a key lever. On each down stroke a star wheel on the spindle, entered by a fixed edge, fixes the angular position of the type. A fixed comb parallel to the cylinder axis positions the cylinder lengthwise. The patent adds a second comb so that notched arms on the sleeve engage a comb on each side, holding the cylinder laterally at two points plus the impression point and removing play.

**Features:** [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [IMP:print-lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)

**Parts:** type cylinder; spindle; swinging sleeve; star wheel; comb; notched arm; paper roller; key lever

**Improves on:**
