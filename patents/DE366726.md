**How it works (reading):** The feed table is made of separate side parts with pins that are adjustable relative to a middle part, and the pins are withdrawn by sliding bars with oblique slots so the table can be lifted rearward.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The feed table with alignment pins is hard to fit to a high table and the pins block insertion of new sheets behind the platen.

**Solution:** The feed table is made of separate side parts with pins that are adjustable relative to a middle part, and the pins are withdrawn by sliding bars with oblique slots so the table can be lifted rearward.
