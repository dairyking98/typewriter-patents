## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar hanger bearings wear and lose alignment, needing expert adjustment.

**Solution:** Two pivoting side plates forming the tapered bearing for the typebar journal are spread against it by a tapered screw to take up wear.

**How it works:** Side plates with tapered holes hold the typebar's tapered journal, pivoting on rivets between top and bottom plates. A tapered screw between the plates forces their other ends apart so the holes close equally on the journal. Rivets protrude so the mounting screw does not clamp the plates rigid. Adjustment needs no removal of the hanger and does not disturb alignment.
