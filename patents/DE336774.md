## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter. The invention relates to typewriters, those with a so-called intended for switching the carriage and the ribbon Universal rail are equipped. There are already typewriters of this kind known, where the necessary to move the carriage and the ribbon anyway Universal rail is also used to loosen and return type levers that are caught in their working position. With these known typewriters but the thrust organ intended to return the type lever acts on one of two straight guides, which in turn pass on their movement on the universal rail. The invention aims to simplify the construction of these known machines and to make them easier to use. These advantages (simplification of the design and easier operation) are achieved according to the invention that as a thrust member for releasing .the type lever with the intermediary of the universal rail, a button lever is provided, the stop of which is possible without changing the hand position, and that the one to return: the trapped type lever serving button lever directly on the universal rail able to act.
