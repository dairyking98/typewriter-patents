## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switching device for adding machines, Typing, arithmetic, typing arithmetic and similar machines in which the pawl arranged on a lever pivotable about the platen axis and the duration of engagement the pawl with the ratchet wheel by a pivotable about the platen axis, essentially three-armed line adjustment lever is adjustable.

**How it works (reading):** The line-setting lever is a bracket whose legs carry spring-holding separators that brake the ratchet lever, so the pawl can be moved independently and each position is held.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving the ratchet lever shifts the line-setting lever, giving wrong line spacing.

**Solution:** The line-setting lever is a bracket whose legs carry spring-holding separators that brake the ratchet lever, so the pawl can be moved independently and each position is held.
