## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier tally attachments for flat-platen Elliott-Fisher machines needed cumbersome adjustment and fed the strip in reverse order.

**Solution:** Front and rear tally carriers adjustable and shiftable in unison into and out of position, feeding the strip front to rear so numbers read top to bottom.

**How it works:** On the book typewriter with a stationary flat platen and a movable machine frame, the tally strip is on a supply roll in open bearings on the front carrier. It is fed to feed rolls at the rear, advanced by the line spacing through an actuator, and passes under a cutter to sever the printed tally. A spring plate serves as guard and tension device.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** tally strip; supply roll; front carrier; rear carrier; feed roll; actuator; cutter; spring plate

**Improves on:**

- US816671 King and Milstead: quick shifting of strip in and out
- Ziegler application 388,785: no cumbersome adjustment
