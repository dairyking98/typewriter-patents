## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In closed-case typewriters, manual and automatic ribbon spool switching were coupled through snap springs, requiring excess force and risking wrong winding hidden by the case.

**Solution:** A manual lever acts on a collar on the drive shaft only by fork edges, leaving the shaft free to switch automatically independent of the lever.

**How it works:** A drive shaft slides to engage bevel gears of the left or right ribbon spool; automatic reversing moves it directly. A vertical shaft with a two-armed fork, spring-centred, is spaced wider than the shaft collar so automatic motion does not touch it. A hand wheel segment through a slit in the front cover turns a rod and levers to push a fork arm on the collar. An arrow on the wheel and on spool plates shows which spool is coupled and its winding direction.
