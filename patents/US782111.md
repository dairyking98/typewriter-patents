## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line spacing normally needs a separate action when the carriage is returned; the inventor wants the platen to turn automatically on carriage return and also from a keyboard key.

**Solution:** A spring-drum carriage-return device trips a pawl lever that turns the platen ratchet at the end of the return, with a separate fingerboard key to line-space without returning the carriage.

**How it works:** Each key stroke depresses a bail that rocks a lever whose pawl turns a ratchet and winds a spring drum through a pinion and gear, paying out a cable so the carriage advances under its own spring. A retaining pawl holds the ratchet. A keyboard key lifts the tails of both pawls, freeing the spring drum, which is stronger than the carriage spring and winds the cable to pull the carriage back. At the end of return another pawl lever turns the platen ratchet for line spacing. Lifting the rack bar to shift the carriage by hand also trips the pawls.

**Features:** [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** bail; pawl lever; ratchet wheel; retaining pawl; pinion; spring drum; cable and pulley; carriage spring; rack bar; platen ratchet
