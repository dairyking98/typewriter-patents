## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two independent paper webs fed to tabulating printers can creep, crease or jam, especially on long feeds at high speed.

**Solution:** Each web runs over guide plates and is pressed by brake shoes for tension, controlled by a lever with a set of brake pressure choices. A test device stops the feed before the web end reaches the print position.
