**How it works (reading):** A separate spindle, rotatable by the pin and the platen and arranged in the platen, opens and closes the centring chuck from the outside, so the chuck is worked without turning the parts it grips.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A centring chuck on a removable platen wedges itself by relative rotation and self-locks, so it is hard to tighten and release.

**Solution:** A separate spindle, rotatable by the pin and the platen and arranged in the platen, opens and closes the centring chuck from the outside, so the chuck is worked without turning the parts it grips.
