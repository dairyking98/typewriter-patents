## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**How it works (reading):** The frame is closed on both sides and at the back, high enough that the paper slide can be lowered into it over the type-lever basket; the paper insert plate then covers the type-lever passage cutout, and a roller blind or cover protects the keyboard.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** When the typewriter is not in use, the type-lever basket and mechanism are exposed to damage and dust.

**Solution:** The frame is closed on both sides and at the back, high enough that the paper slide can be lowered into it over the type-lever basket; the paper insert plate then covers the type-lever passage cutout, and a roller blind or cover protects the keyboard.
