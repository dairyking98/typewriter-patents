## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Standard machines give only single, double and triple line spacing, and irregular spacing requires the platen to be turned by hand.

**Solution:** An attachment for old or new machines: an oscillating gripping band grips the platen at an adjustable instant, so any required line space is produced by the spacing lever.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** oscillating gripping band; platen; spacing lever; adjustment
