## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (shorthand typewriting machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to improvements in shorthand type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A Hammond-based shorthand typewriter that uses a character's raised or lowered position to indicate a vowel or heavy type for another, and combines common consonant clusters and prefixes on single keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Taking notes at speech speed on a typewriter is too slow, so shorthand-like writing with fewer strokes is wanted.

**Solution:** A Hammond-based shorthand typewriter that uses a character's raised or lowered position to indicate a vowel or heavy type for another, and combines common consonant clusters and prefixes on single keys.
