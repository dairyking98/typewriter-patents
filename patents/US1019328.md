## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** An escapement with little drop suits ordinary speed but needs more drop for high speed; machines have been fitted with one or the other.

**Solution:** A bifurcated double-dogged loose pawl on the dog rocker gives the right drop automatically at either speed.

**How it works:** A loose pawl with two integral dogs is pivoted on the dog rocker and spring held. One dog normally holds the rack/escapement wheel; the other stops more than one tooth escaping. A fixed dog on the rocker engages only at nearly full backward motion. Extra means acts only when a key is held down or its return is slow, temporarily detaining the rack until the key is released. Teeth have two working faces, one square to the wheel plane. Shown on an Underwood machine, with a second form in Figs. 6-11.
