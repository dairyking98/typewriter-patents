## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Oxide whiskers build up on the writing electrode of a burn-out recorder and spoil the trace.

**Solution:** A hump on the desk roller breaks the whiskers off once per revolution, or the electrode is vibrated to fling them away.
