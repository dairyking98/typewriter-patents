## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an improved mechanism for effecting and controlling the line-spacing movements of the platen roll of a typewriting machine.

**How it works (reading):** An improved mechanism controls platen line-spacing movement with a ratchet and carriage-mounted pawl.
