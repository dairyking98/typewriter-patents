## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ten-key calculator with pen carriage) · **Subject (patent classes):** Keyboards with one set of keys for all denominations, e.g. ten-key board

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ten-key calculator with a pen carriage and a printing device which of the pen carriage sets one of the machine's number of digits the corresponding number of movable racks under spring action is limited, which in turn drive both the counter and the printing mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Each of the ten racks has a fork shape: one fork part is toothed for the type carrier, while the shaft carries the counter toothing and detents that lock the racks and are adjusted by the pen carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The pen-carriage ten-key calculator needs a compact drive with fewer parts.

**Solution:** Each of the ten racks has a fork shape: one fork part is toothed for the type carrier, while the shaft carries the counter toothing and detents that lock the racks and are adjusted by the pen carriage.
