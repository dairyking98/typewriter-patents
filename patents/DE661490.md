**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a card holder for writing, calculating and the like Machines in which two cross the typing line on either side of the imprint extending and after the paper roll too curved, on an on the machine or Switching frame directly or indirectly attached carrier plate to lower.

**How it works (reading):** Fingers mount on journals perpendicular to the platen axis with a curved guide, so each finger pivots within a plane parallel to the card path and holds the card on the roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Card holders with inclined bearing journals take up space and need asymmetric fingers that are hard to make.

**Solution:** Fingers mount on journals perpendicular to the platen axis with a curved guide, so each finger pivots within a plane parallel to the card path and holds the card on the roller.
