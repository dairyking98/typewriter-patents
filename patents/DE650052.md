## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers

**How it works (reading):** Closing the case lid automatically lifts the paper slide off its switch stops and holds it there, and opening the lid releases it, without the machine coming out of the case.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A small typewriter can be damaged in transport if the paper slide is left in its stops, and securing it is often forgotten.

**Solution:** Closing the case lid automatically lifts the paper slide off its switch stops and holds it there, and opening the lid releases it, without the machine coming out of the case.
