## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Tab keys placed at the back or far above the letter keys force the typist to lift a hand from the keyboard, and decimal and tab rods at the back need linkages routed around the mechanism.

**Solution:** The tab rod and decimal pushers sit in front of the paper roller, with their key levers on the common keyboard plate, so tab keys are struck from above like the letter keys.

**How it works:** Short tab key levers lift vertical decimal pushers directly behind the keyboard; a common bracket pivots a linkage that releases the carriage and tightens the table brake.
