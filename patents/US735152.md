## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writers and like machines, and particularly to the means employed for guiding the type-bar adjacent to the printingpoint, and has for its object to provide a simple construction of the aboveindicated character which will be capable of ready adjustment, so that the type-bars may be correctly guided to the printing-point.

**How it works (reading):** A type-bar guide on the top edge of the segment has forwardly flared arms on forked members split by a slit, with nut adjustment so the bars are aimed at the printing point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says type-bars must be guided correctly to the printing point, and the guide must be adjustable both at assembly and later in the machine's life.

**Solution:** A type-bar guide on the top edge of the segment has forwardly flared arms on forked members split by a slit, with nut adjustment so the bars are aimed at the printing point.
