**How it works (reading):** The reference mark is first embossed as a raised mark on the stamp, and the letter matrices are then engraved referenced to an edge of that raised mark, so all dies share one datum.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Recessed reference marks on type are hard to cut accurately because the milling of the embossing die and the letter matrices happen in separate operations.

**Solution:** The reference mark is first embossed as a raised mark on the stamp, and the letter matrices are then engraved referenced to an edge of that raised mark, so all dies share one datum.

**How it works:** A raised mark on the stamp sets the datum; letter matrices are engraved against its edge.

**Features:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md), [How the element is made](../features/elem-element-construction.md)
