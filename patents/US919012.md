## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the paper feeding uv to ineans for controlling the pressure rolls that run upon the usual cylindrical platen.

**How it works (reading):** A key pulls a crank-operated rock shaft whose beveled collars push the lever arms of the spring-loaded pressure rolls up and away from the platen, where they lock until released.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The pressure rolls on the platen block paper insertion and adjustment, so they must be released and held away from the platen.

**Solution:** A key pulls a crank-operated rock shaft whose beveled collars push the lever arms of the spring-loaded pressure rolls up and away from the platen, where they lock until released.
