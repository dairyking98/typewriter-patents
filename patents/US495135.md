## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple, cheap, durable device for marking boxes and packages or printing signs.

**Solution:** A hand-turned type disc with flexible tongues, each bearing a type on its underside, is set by a lettered notched index disc and pressed down onto inking pads and the work.

**How it works:** A cross-shaped sheet-metal base with sharp flanges slides over the surface and carries a vertical spindle. The type form is a flexible canvas or rubber disc cut radially into tongues, clamped between two metal plates, with a type on the underside of each tongue. It turns freely on the spindle and is held up by a flat spring. Above it a notched disc and a lettered scale show the characters, and a pin on the type form drops into a notch to locate each character over an opening in the base. Pressing the type form down against the spring flexes the tongue so its type prints through the opening, and the types pick up ink from removable pads on the base arms.
