## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars rebound from the basket on returning and clash with the next bar in rapid operation.

**Solution:** A flexible ring backing the typebars, with radial plungers held by friction between felt-lined plates, absorbs the return of each bar.

**How it works:** Radial typebars rest at their type ends against the inner edge of a flexible leather ring confined between upper and lower shelves; the upper shelf is felt-lined and may be weighted for friction. The ring is incised on its outer side to form a series of friction pieces or plungers, one per typebar. A returning bar displaces its plunger against friction, and the plungers are connected so displacing one tends to restore a previously displaced one. Friction keeps the bar from rebounding.
