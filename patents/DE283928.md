## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon transverse movement must be switched off for wax paper and the device must work on collapsible typewriters.

**Solution:** A curved guide slot in a swinging bearing plate, concentric with its pivot, moves the ribbon holder in pairs; the whole unit attaches to and detaches from the typewriter.
