## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter and the like with an elevator device controlled by the paper carriage movement, a clear character printing device and driver device for the cross counters.

**How it works (reading):** Operating a step-lock-disabling member also disables the carriage elevator, clear-print and driver devices together, while the decimal tab key disables only the elevator and clear-print devices.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving the carriage while the carriage release or tab keys are pressed can engage the carriage elevator, clear-print or cross-counter drivers and cause damage or errors.

**Solution:** Operating a step-lock-disabling member also disables the carriage elevator, clear-print and driver devices together, while the decimal tab key disables only the elevator and clear-print devices.
