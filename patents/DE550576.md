## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage escapement needs to move quickly enough that no two keystrokes clash, and the original pawl pair is too slow to return.

**Solution:** Retaining and switching pawls get complementary bevels, and a spring-loaded bevel plus a lateral tooth bevel on the escape wheel let both pawls return quickly after each keystroke.
