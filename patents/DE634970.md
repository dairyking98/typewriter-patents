## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing quiet line-shift lever returns needed two rods and two springs, a complex build.

**Solution:** A single spring links the line-shift lever to a pivoted stop lever, which gives a resilient stop for the return and so damps noise.

**How it works:** The line-shift lever pivots on a bracket and drives the platen ratchet via a pawl. A stop lever sits below the bracket, normally held against the carriage side wall. A coil spring joins it to an arm on the lever. Moving the lever tensions the spring; on release the lever returns and strikes the stop lever, pushing it slightly out of position against the spring, then both are drawn back to rest.

**Parts:** line shift lever; platen ratchet; pawl; stop lever; coil spring; mounting arm
