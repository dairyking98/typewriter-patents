## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (motor calculating typewriter with automatic printing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with a motor drive for type levers and arithmetic units and with devices for automatic printing of the arithmetic units displayed values with simultaneous zeroing of the number wheels.

**How it works (reading):** Each adding unit has a control element that, on entering the working column, couples a control disc on a constantly running shaft and disengages it after the controlled members have scanned and printed the value; the tabulator and copier are switched accordingly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Counter totals cannot be printed fully automatically by power drive, and the tabulator cannot move the carriage from one counter to the next while copying.

**Solution:** Each adding unit has a control element that, on entering the working column, couples a control disc on a constantly running shaft and disengages it after the controlled members have scanned and printed the value; the tabulator and copier are switched accordingly.
