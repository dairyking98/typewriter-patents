## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Balls in a ball bearing must stay evenly spaced and be held in place while the bearing members travel.

**Solution:** Each ball sits in a cage of crossed circular strips, and rods link the cages of balls in the same groove so they stay evenly spaced; stop collars on a rod limit the travel of the movable member.

**Features:** [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** ball bearing members; balls; cage strips; sleeve; rod; projection; stop collars
