## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Device on typewriters for displaying the last ones to be written on Sheet line There are already devices for typewriters to display the last to be described arc line become known, in which the lead wires one electrical signal system are connected to the machine in this way and a mating contact is arranged in such a way that it causes the current shorting after the completion of the last line of writing. In these known devices, the mating contact is always open the platen or elsewhere on the machine so attached that he of the contact is separated by the writing sheet passed therebetween, so that the circuit is closed at the moment the lower edge of the Arc has moved through between the contacts. According to the invention, the mating contact which brings about the current connection is attached to the writing sheet. This is in addition to the possibility of a very simple design and Ease of use of the device also allows the width of the lower Edge by offsetting the metal strip designed as a mating contact on the individual sheets can be changed at will.
