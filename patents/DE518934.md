## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** A metal strip is glued to the writing sheet at the last-line height; when it passes between two sleeves on the platen it bridges them and closes the signal circuit.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Known end-of-page signals use a contact fixed on the machine, so the sheet's own edge position cannot be set per sheet.

**Solution:** A metal strip is glued to the writing sheet at the last-line height; when it passes between two sleeves on the platen it bridges them and closes the signal circuit.
