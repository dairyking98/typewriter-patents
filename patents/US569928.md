## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines, and it has for its object to lock the type-Writer at the end of each line of writing, so to prevent letters being printed one over the other, or in case of narrow paper being used to prevent printing on the platen.
