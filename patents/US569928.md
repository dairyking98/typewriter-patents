## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** At the end of a line letters can print over each other, or on narrow paper the type strikes the bare platen.

**Solution:** A carriage-actuated sliding bar locks the space levers, and with them the key levers, at the end of the line.

**How it works:** Every key depression also depresses the space-levers. A notched bar slides under the space-levers, with notches normally beneath them so they can drop. When the carriage reaches the position set by the adjustable bell-trip, the trip swings a pivoted vertical lever that slides the bar so the notches clear the space-levers. The levers are then blocked, and the key-levers cannot be depressed. A spring returns the bar when the carriage moves right to begin a new line.
