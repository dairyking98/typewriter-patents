## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Table-mounted carriage-return and line-space devices broke when the typewriter moved on the table, and built-in versions needed machine modification.

**Solution:** A lever linking a treadle or knee gear to the typewriter's line-space lever sits on a separate support held by the weight of the machine, so it follows machine displacement and fits any typewriter without modification.
