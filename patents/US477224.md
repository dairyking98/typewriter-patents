## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter keys must be released before the next is struck (staccato), limiting speed; type in adjacent rows of a type-wheel can blur; spacing is fixed.

**Solution:** Key levers that can be overlapped in a slur, a type-wheel with types arranged to avoid contiguous-row blurring and universally pivoted to its shaft, and an improved variable-spacing mechanism.

**How it works:** Key levers stop a rotating type-wheel at the right angle through finger stops meeting spirally arranged pins on two transverse check-pin shafts, which brings the chosen character into line with an impression hammer. Each key lever carries a pivoted jack with an adjustable stop so another key can be depressed without releasing earlier ones. A rock-shaft carries a hammer frame driven by an adjustable hammer-impelling spring, so the hammer strikes the type against the paper. The type-wheel is universally pivoted on its shaft and shifted for case. A variable-spacing mechanism moves the paper carriage by different amounts per character.
