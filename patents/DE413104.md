## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register zero print) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices for printing the point

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Printing device for those cash registers where the type carrier is lowest Order when the amount keys are not in use, bring a zero to the printout.

**How it works (reading):** The higher-order racks are moved out of the zero-pressure position by a special device, and then automatically return, so leading zeros or dashes can be printed in front of each number while the lowest-order wheels stay at zero.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash register type wheels rest at a zero position when unused, so the printout cannot show full-line zeros or dashes where they are wanted.

**Solution:** The higher-order racks are moved out of the zero-pressure position by a special device, and then automatically return, so leading zeros or dashes can be printed in front of each number while the lowest-order wheels stay at zero.
