## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing typewriter pads give poor suspension, let the machine tilt or slip, and are tedious to place under each foot.

**Solution:** A thin base plate carrying rubber buffers under the machine feet, made of porous or soft rubber with a thin hard rubber skin on the machine side that protects the feet and eases cleaning, and four cylindrical projections about 20 mm high spaced to suit most machines.

**How it works:** The soft buffers absorb vibration and grip the table; the plate keeps the buffers in position so they cannot be lost or misplaced.

**Features:**

- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** base plate; porous rubber buffers; hard rubber skin; cylindrical projections

**Numbers:** plate about 36 cm by 28 cm; projections about 20 mm high; projection spacing about 80 mm
