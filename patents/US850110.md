## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Front paper-guide bars on front-strike machines obstruct the view of the writing and make paper insertion awkward, and the guide width is tied to the bar.

**Solution:** Paper guides are carried on a rod in front of the platen, each finger adjustable along the rod and swung out of the way, so the sheet can be guided without blocking the line of sight.
