## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A cheap simple typewriter for children to learn spelling, composing and punctuation by printing words and sentences.

**Solution:** A rubber-faced type wheel on a spring-lifted spindle in a slotted post; the operator turns it by a knurl to a letter, presses it down through an opening in the base to print, and V-notches on the wheel's flange engage a knife to align it.

**How it works:** A hollow slotted post stands on a base plate that slides along a ruler guide. The type wheel's axle runs through the post slots in a sliding box and a spiral spring presses the wheel up against an ink pad or roller fixed on top of the post. The operator turns the wheel with a knurl until the wanted letter, shown by an outer flange marked with the characters, lines up under a guide flange on the base. Pressing the wheel down pushes the type through an opening in the base onto the paper. As it goes down, the V-notch for that letter on the inner flange meets a knife on the base, which aligns the letter. The base is then slid along the ruler by hand to the next position.
