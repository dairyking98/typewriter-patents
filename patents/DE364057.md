## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters, where the trolley switching device is both from the letter keys below Mediation of the universal rail movable by the type levers as well as the Distance key is influenced from and has the special drive of the carriage switching device from the distance button to the object.

**How it works (reading):** The space key drives the carriage switching pawl through an adjustable rod loosely hooked into a link of the universal-rail linkage, so the pawl moves independently, can be removed separately, and its stops are adjustable and quiet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The space key drive shares its linkage with the universal rail, so the carriage switching pawl cannot move independently and the space key's rest and end positions cannot be adjusted without affecting the drive.

**Solution:** The space key drives the carriage switching pawl through an adjustable rod loosely hooked into a link of the universal-rail linkage, so the pawl moves independently, can be removed separately, and its stops are adjustable and quiet.
