## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operating devices for a platen clutch that releases the platen from the line-space wheel are not simple or convenient for the operator.

**Solution:** The platen clutch is released by moving a part longitudinally along the platen axle, with a lever or other operating device connected to that part, so the platen can turn free of the line-space wheel.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
