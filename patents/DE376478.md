## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Dot type for typewriters. As is known, the fixed point type damages for typewriters very soon the roller. Likewise, with such a type becomes very easy the writing sheet or the carbon paper and especially in the production of wax matrices pierce the wax arch. These evils are supposed to be caused by the new point types are avoided, and that is why the same is such that they no longer have a single thick point, but a circle with a small one Point created in the middle. The small point at the center of the circle will now be generated in particular by the tip of a special screw bolt, which by is screwed back into the lever head so that it is in the middle of the Circle generating type emerges from the lever head. That way you have the Possibility to use the screw bolt, depending on the type of paper, die or the like, to be able to screw in more or less and thus avoids the penetration of the Paper, the Mtachsmatrize and the like.
