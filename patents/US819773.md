## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Finger-keys should have uniform dip and equal leverage, free of sliding friction, and typebars should not rebound or collide when keys in adjacent banks are struck.

**Solution:** Keys sit on parallel swinging links connected to the typebars so that the start is slow and the motion accelerates; pivots are in line at rest to stop rebound, and a universal bar interlocks the key banks.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** parallel swinging key links; universal bar; bank actuating parts
