## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter in which Auxiliary ribbons used for making copies from on the reverse side the machine frame arranged coils are withdrawn.

**How it works (reading):** Support frames fixed to the back of the frame form a protective cage around the rear ribbon spools and allow the machine to stand upright.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Auxiliary ribbon spools on the back of the typewriter are not protected and make cleaning and upright setting difficult.

**Solution:** Support frames fixed to the back of the frame form a protective cage around the rear ribbon spools and allow the machine to stand upright.
