## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a Japanese typewriter with a sideways-moving platen driven by one of two racks, freeing both racks so the platen slides by hand needed a separate action depending on which rack was in mesh.

**Solution:** A triangular-hole cam on the rack cross bar lets one release lever rock the bar to a neutral position whichever rack is meshed.

**How it works:** Two opposed racks hang from a cross bar between the platen side plates and embrace a one-way drive gear. A change-over handle, through a link, disc and over-centre spring, rocks the bar so either the left or right rack meshes, setting platen feed direction. A cam body fixed to the bar has an isosceles-triangle hole in which the roller of a pivoted release lever rests on one sloping side or the other depending on the rack in mesh. Pressing the lever drives the roller up the side to the apex, turning the bar slightly to a neutral position where neither rack meshes. A spring latch holds the lever there until released, when the over-centre spring restores the previous rack.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Platen sideways-feed release device for a Japanese typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a platen sideways-feed release device for a Japanese typewriter of the following kind: a cross bar is pivoted so as to span between the two side plates that carry the platen at its two ends; two racks, with their rows of teeth facing each other and placed so as to embrace the two sides of a drive gear that turns in one direction only, are hung from and fixed to this cross bar; and the cross bar is turned by a change-over handle so that only one of the two racks meshes with one side of the drive gear. In this device a cam body is provided, joined to the cross bar so as to turn with it, with a cam face in the form of a hole shaped as an isosceles triangle. As the cross bar turns to change which rack meshes with the drive gear, the cam body rocks, and the roller at the tip of a rack-release lever, pivoted at its middle, is brought against one or the other sloping side of the triangle in the cam hole.
