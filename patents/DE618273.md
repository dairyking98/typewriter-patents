## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** A coupling connects the platen to the rotating tabulator body so the same operating lever drives both simple sheet feed and turning the platen to the individual line stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator line stops need a separate drive and a separate operating handle from simple paper feed, which causes faults and confusion when working quickly.

**Solution:** A coupling connects the platen to the rotating tabulator body so the same operating lever drives both simple sheet feed and turning the platen to the individual line stops.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)
