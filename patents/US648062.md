## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter carriages rattle and vibrate, and the platen mount needs to swing to show the writing.

**Solution:** A carriage whose front rail runs on three sides in an adjustable ball bearing, carrying a platen frame that swings forward and up.

**How it works:** The carriage rolls on a rear track by rollers and on a front track through a split ball-retainer bearing on three sides of the rail; adjusting screws close the split to remove looseness. Uprights on the carriage end bars carry the platen frame on fixed pivots so it swings forward and upward, held by a spring on a pin when printing or shifted. The platen rotates by a push-pawl on a swinging line-space lever acting on a ratchet, with a button choosing single or double spacing. A scale on the front rail is reached when the platen swings up.
