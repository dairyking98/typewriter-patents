## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being parallel to the axis of rotation, e.g. with type on the periphery of cylindrical carriers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines, and especially to that class of type-writing machines in which a typewvheel is made to move back and forth in relation to a platen, so as to print on paper passed between itself and the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Each key pushes two sliding bars in turn: one turns the type wheel through pivoted shifting rods, the other pushes a locking rod onto a pin on the wheel to fix its angle, and continued travel carries the whole wheel to the platen while the same slide advances the escapement.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventor wants a cheap, simple, compact type-wheel machine that prints firmly, locks the wheel accurately at each position, and uses a ribbon fully by feeding it first one way and then the other.

**Solution:** Each key pushes two sliding bars in turn: one turns the type wheel through pivoted shifting rods, the other pushes a locking rod onto a pin on the wheel to fix its angle, and continued travel carries the whole wheel to the platen while the same slide advances the escapement.

**How it works:** The wheel has three horizontal rows (lower case, upper case, figures) on a spindle that rides in a slide block. A key rod, driven forward through toggle levers, has an upper pin that engages one slide bar, which pushes two shifting rods linked to the underside of the wheel and so turns it. A lower pin on the same key rod engages a second slide whose locking rod catches one of the pins on the wheel at the moment the desired type faces the platen, stopping its turning. Further key travel advances both slides and so the slide block carrying the wheel against the platen through the ribbon, and a star wheel under the wheel is entered by a fixed tooth to hold it rigid for printing. The slide block abuts the spacer block, so the forward print stroke also trips the pawl-and-rack carriage escapement, and a separate finger piece spaces alone. Two shift keys tilt levers of different throw under a vertical arm to raise the wheel to its upper-case or figure row against a light spring, and springs recentre the wheel when released. The carriage rack drives a ratchet that turns the ribbon spools, with sliding bevel pinions on finger levers to reverse the feed, and spools can be raised in steps of about 1/8 in.
