## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Striking the type-bars against the metal platen is noisy.

**Solution:** An inflated platen where the printing surface is held off the metal ends on an inner air-tight tube, so the cover gives and reduces noise.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
