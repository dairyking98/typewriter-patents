## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typebar machines print one character at a time and need one bar per character.

**Solution:** Multi-character typebars in four groups, each converging on its own printing point, with a shift key that moves the bar-carrying frame sideways to pick the character.

**How it works:** Typebars are in four groups (six, two, two, six), each group converging to its own centre, so four characters can print simultaneously. Each bar forms one side of a jointed parallelogram with upper and lower rods pivoted to a frame; the upper frame section moves laterally when a shift key is pressed, so a different character on the bar reaches the paper. A character key pulls the lower rod through an adjustable link to swing the bar up against the paper.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:rotary](../features/shift-rotary.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** typebar; upper rod; lower rod; movable frame section; fixed frame section; shift key; character key; adjustable link

**Numbers:** four groups of 6, 2, 2 and 6 bars
