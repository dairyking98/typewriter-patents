## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Methods or arrangements for marking the record carrier in digital fashion otherwise than by punching by printing code marks

**How it works (reading):** Two numeral key sets each with their own type bars type tens and units in a column along a paper strip; the space bar advances the strip with a ratchet on the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Weaving draft strips need columns of numbers typed by a typewriter with no letter spacing.

**Solution:** Two numeral key sets each with their own type bars type tens and units in a column along a paper strip; the space bar advances the strip with a ratchet on the platen.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)
