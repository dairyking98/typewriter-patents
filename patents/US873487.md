## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A platen shift needs simple mechanism that can be worked by any one of several shift keys whether the platen is in upper or lower case position.

**Solution:** A platen shifting mechanism on a Densmore machine actuated by any of a plurality of shift keys in either case position.

**How it works:** The excerpt mostly describes the Densmore escapement rather than the shifting itself. Key levers move typebars by links and also press a universal bar, whose adjustable-length link rocks a dog rocker carrying feed dogs, which release a feed wheel on a ball-bearing shaft. A feed pinion meshes with a carriage feed rack, which can be disengaged by a finger piece on a rock shaft with a leaf spring and stop pin. An adjustable plug takes up ball bearing wear. The paper carriage comprises three parts, and the shift mechanism itself is described beyond the portion shown.
