## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper guide device for typewriters, in which the paper fingers can be adjusted along the paper roller in a manner known per se and can release the paper without changing their set position.

**How it works (reading):** Paper fingers on a rod are carried on swing arms and sleeves, and can be lifted and swung sideways around a pin, and locked in working position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper fingers must be swung out of the way for erasing without losing their set position, and existing fingers cannot do so.

**Solution:** Paper fingers on a rod are carried on swing arms and sleeves, and can be lifted and swung sideways around a pin, and locked in working position.
