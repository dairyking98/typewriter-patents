## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons while fitted in the machine using the ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for continuously refreshing the ink ribbon Typewriters in which the ribbon is attached to an absorbent roll soaked with paint Fabric is passed.

**How it works (reading):** A rotating paint container with holes near its upper edge feeds wicks, and a tight-fitting cloth roller takes paint from the wicks to ink the ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon paint feed is uneven and wasteful with a perforated paint container and felt roller.

**Solution:** A rotating paint container with holes near its upper edge feeds wicks, and a tight-fitting cloth roller takes paint from the wicks to ink the ribbon.
