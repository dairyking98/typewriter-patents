**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with visible Font where it is important to reduce the height of the machine if possible and also to decrease the depth from front to back.

**How it works (reading):** Very short type levers are pivoted in an arc on a curved axis, and graded intermediate levers connect each type lever to its key connecting rod. The keys then strike the platen with equal strokes in a narrow basket.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A visible-writing typewriter needs reduced height and depth, but the usual horizontal key levers and type basket take up too much space.

**Solution:** Very short type levers are pivoted in an arc on a curved axis, and graded intermediate levers connect each type lever to its key connecting rod. The keys then strike the platen with equal strokes in a narrow basket.
