## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters must be quickly secured to a base board and held firmly even when the board is tilted.

**Solution:** Recesses with felt cushions receive the machine feet, and spring-projected hook catches on pivoted fastenings engage the frame side bars, so the machine is fixed without screws.

**Parts:** base board; recesses with felt cushions; sockets; spring-projected hook catches; pivoted fastening devices
