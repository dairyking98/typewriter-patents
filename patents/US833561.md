## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In the patent’s own words:** Our improvement consists in the fact that the inking-ribbon s ools are arranged separately from the a justing-bar and that the two pillars carrying the inking-ribbon spools are independent of the adjilsting-bar.

**How it works (reading):** Spool pillars are fixed and the ribbon spindles drive pawls on a disk around the spindle, reversed by rotating disks on a common slide.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Inking-ribbon feed is unreliable when pawl levers rock in a tangent plane and the spool pillars are displaced with the adjusting bar.

**Solution:** Spool pillars are fixed and the ribbon spindles drive pawls on a disk around the spindle, reversed by rotating disks on a common slide.

- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
