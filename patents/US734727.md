## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Underwood shift keys need a way to lock the platen carriage in its upper position or let it return, as the operator wishes.

**Solution:** A shift-lock locking lever catches a pin on the carriage carrier, with a controlling lever that enables or blocks the lock.

**How it works:** Two shift keys at either side swing a carrier that raises the carriage track and platen. The carrier's pin seats in recesses in a locking lever, holding the platen up. A rock-shaft release arm, worked by the other shift key, frees the lock. A pivoted controlling lever either blocks or opens a recess so a pin on the shift key lever can engage and lock the key down, or not.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** shift key; carrier; track; locking lever; rock-shaft; releasing arm; controlling lever; pin
