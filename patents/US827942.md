## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the ribbon-winding mechanisms of type-writing machines, and particularly to means for automatically reversing the direction of winding.

**How it works (reading):** A shaft slides endwise to swap bevel gear pairs driving the two spools, held in each position by a yielding detent; a pawl on a ratchet wheel drives the shaft from each type stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike machines need a simple way to reverse the ribbon winding automatically when a spool empties.

**Solution:** A shaft slides endwise to swap bevel gear pairs driving the two spools, held in each position by a yielding detent; a pawl on a ratchet wheel drives the shaft from each type stroke.

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
