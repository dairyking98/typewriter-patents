## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switching device on line typewriters, line typesetting and printing machines and the like;

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary ratchet driven from the constant stroke of the printing-base carrier engages an indexing disk whose adjustable idle stroke sets the line spacing, so the whole switch sits on the carrier and switches precisely.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line-spacing stop adjustment on the frame gives inaccurate ratchet switching, since the ratchet lever returns by spring tension.

**Solution:** An auxiliary ratchet driven from the constant stroke of the printing-base carrier engages an indexing disk whose adjustable idle stroke sets the line spacing, so the whole switch sits on the carrier and switches precisely.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
