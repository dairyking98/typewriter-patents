## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Monarch ribbon mechanism for multi-field ribbons: bring any field to the print point or disable the ribbon for mimeograph work.

**Solution:** A vibrating lever connected through a forked link that can be moved by a slide to engage one of several pins at different distances from the universal-bar frame pivot, or none.

**How it works:** The operating lever replaces the slotted Monarch lever and is fixed to the upper end of a vertical link. The link's forked lower end has openings that engage pins on the central rear arm of the universal-bar frame at different distances from its pivot. A manual slide or cross-bar guides the link and shifts it transversely, so it engages one pin (varying ribbon throw) or is disconnected for stencils.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon carrier; operating lever; forked link; pin; universal bar frame; slide bar

**Improves on:**

- Monarch slotted vibratory lever: forked link selecting pin distance gives set throws
