## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Electromagnetic typebar drive current depends on how hard or long the key is pressed.

**Solution:** A latch lever on the type lever closes a contact spring and slides along it, giving a fixed current time regardless of key travel.

**How it works:** The key pushes a latch lever that presses an adjustable contact spring, closing the solenoid circuit. The solenoid pulls a universal rail that throws the type lever to the platen. As the lever rises it slides off the spring, ending contact; if the key is held, an insulating layer holds the type lever in an intermediate position.
