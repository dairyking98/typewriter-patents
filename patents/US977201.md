## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar bearings are bulky and need locking devices to adjust for wear, which adds complexity and cost.

**Solution:** A compact ball-bearing pivot in which opposed bearing cones are held concentric and parallel, with an adjustment that can be set and maintained without disturbing that relation.
