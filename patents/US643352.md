## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stenographic machine needs to be simple, portable, durable and fast to operate with ease.

**Solution:** A small shorthand machine with only a few keys driving six nested type-bails, each carrying printing prongs, that strike a platen-belt; springs keep the bails raised.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)
- **Uses:** [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md)

**Parts:** base-plate with upturned sides; six nested type-bails; pins and transverse bars; springs; prongs; platen-belt

**Numbers:** Patent 643,352; six type-bails; two rows of prongs
