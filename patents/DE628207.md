## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for limiting of the lines and to block the type stop on typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A releasable stop engages the margin stops and the end stop, so the carriage stops and blocks the type stop at the line end, and it can be switched off so the carriage lifts out of the frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** End-stop blocking of the type stop is only possible with fixed stop parts that complicate removing the paper carriage, and it can fail at the line end.

**Solution:** A releasable stop engages the margin stops and the end stop, so the carriage stops and blocks the type stop at the line end, and it can be switched off so the carriage lifts out of the frame.
