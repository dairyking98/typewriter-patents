These 'end locking members consist of stop parts rigidly connected to the paper trolley or the machine frame. This makes it time-consuming to remove the paper carriage from its running guide and is not easily done by everyone, there for this purpose the stop parts must be removed or the paper trolley in whole or in part must be taken apart.
