## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Angled typebars on typebar machines smear or double-print because the type slides sideways on impact.

**Solution:** A wide-slotted resilient tongue on each type lever strikes a fixed curved stop just before impact, giving a long springy rebound; side levers are bent opposite ways to cancel twisting.

**How it works:** Each sheet-metal type lever has a wide cut-out leaving a springy tongue that hits a curved front stop on the segment before the type reaches the platen. The slot stays open so the full lever length flexes. Levers on the right of centre have their tongues bent left and vice versa, so the lateral pull is countered.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Impression:** [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type lever; slotted spring tongue; fixed stop; segment; platen

**Improves on:**

- earlier stop-and-rebound typebar designs with a narrow closing gap: wide slot that does not close, so the whole lever length stays resilient
