## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices used in association with a real typewriter, teleprinter, or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a simple method of and apparatus to facilitate type-writer execution with the aim of acquiring speed, in that the digits of both hands are trained to operate a certain system of keys.

**How it works (reading):** Split index rings worn on each finger carry a shield with the characters of that finger's keyboard group, so each digit is trained to its keys.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Learning touch typing is slow and needs a method that trains the fingers to the keyboard.

**Solution:** Split index rings worn on each finger carry a shield with the characters of that finger's keyboard group, so each digit is trained to its keys.
