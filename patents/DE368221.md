## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a document holder for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A resilient sideways-bent arm on the base plate ends in upturned lobes that grip the lower frame edge, so the machine weight locks the holder, with an adjustable rod carrying the document clip.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A document holder on a base plate under the typewriter is not held securely because the base plate does not fit the machine frame.

**Solution:** A resilient sideways-bent arm on the base plate ends in upturned lobes that grip the lower frame edge, so the machine weight locks the holder, with an adjustable rod carrying the document clip.
