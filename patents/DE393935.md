## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Adjustable ribbon devices for multicolour ribbons raise the whole spool drive or the spools in ways that cannot use the full ribbon width or avoid shocks.

**Solution:** Carry both ribbon spools from below on support arms hinged to arms of one shift rod turned by a self-locking worm gear, so the ribbon can be set at any height.

**How it works:** Each spool sits on a drive shaft with driving pins and rests on a support arm with a sliding plate and a ribbon guide roller. The support arms are hinged to arms on one rotating shaft, turned by a self-locking worm gear and hand crank. Turning it raises or lowers both spools together so the ribbon moves in its guide fork to any height relative to the type. In the lowest position the ribbon lies below the type so wax stencils can be typed without removing it, and a release switches off the ribbon feed. The spools turn through bevel gears on a common axis stepped by a ratchet and pawl.
