## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check-dispensing devices of cash registers need their operation tied to whether a receipt is present, and to transaction keys.

**Solution:** Check printing and dispensing apparatus are made dependent on receipt presence and are switched in or out by special setting elements such as transaction keys.
