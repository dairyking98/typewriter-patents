## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons on typebar machines (Densmore) need to be fed lengthwise and shifted crosswise, and reversed by hand when one spool runs out.

**Solution:** The spring-drum carriage drive is tapped through a cord and rock-shaft to wind the ribbon spools, and end-of-ribbon enlargements trip a lever that reverses the feed automatically.

**How it works:** A cord from the carriage spring-drum barrel pulls a rearward arm on a transverse rock-shaft, tilting the spool supports. Driving pawls on each support engage crown ratchet wheels on the ribbon spools and wind the ribbon lengthwise. Each ribbon end carries a pin or enlargement that cannot pass through a hooked lever on the spool support; when the ribbon is fully wound this lever is pulled, moving a curved reversing lever under the type-ring so that its arms alternately engage the pawls and switch the winding to the other spool. A second embodiment uses a reversing switch on the machine's right end.

**Parts:** ribbon spool; crown ratchet wheel; driving pawl; rock-shaft; spring drum; reversing lever; ribbon end pin; spool support frame
