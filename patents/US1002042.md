## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops on the tabulator bar sit in an awkward position and get in the way of the working parts.

**Solution:** Stops hang loosely on a guide rod beside the toothed tabulator bar, so they can be removed, shifted along the bar, and set without being fixed to the bar.

**How it works:** Each stop has an enlarged eye on a parallel guide rod and can be moved laterally and set at any position on the tabulator bar.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** tabulator bar; guide rod; tabulator stops; stop carrying bar
