## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates particularly to 10 type-bar machines and it has 'for its object to provide in machines of this class, improved carriage feed mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-retained dog rocker returns the escapement dog to its working position, and a cover protects the escapement from dust, with space bar mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The loose escapement dog can be displaced to either side, and the escapement is exposed to dust and damage.

**Solution:** A spring-retained dog rocker returns the escapement dog to its working position, and a cover protects the escapement from dust, with space bar mechanism.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)
