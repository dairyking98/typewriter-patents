## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary key buttons give poor finger guidance and injure fingers; a larger striking surface is wanted without remaking the keyboard.

**Solution:** Attachable auxiliary key caps are clamped onto ordinary key buttons, with a cushion spring and guide members, and a removable extension of the striking surface.
