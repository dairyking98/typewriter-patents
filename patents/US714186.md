## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a tabulating mechanism for type-writing machines, and has the Io object to provide an improved carriage stop or tappet for such mechanism which can be quickly and conveniently adjusted as required.

**How it works (reading):** Pivoted dogs on the frame are turned by a shifting rod and elbow lever to engage the carriage; an improved stop is adjustable as required.
