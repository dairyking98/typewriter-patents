## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements to a visible-writing typewriter whose typebars are thrown downward onto the paper, covering key levers, typebar mounting, spacing and ribbons.

**Solution:** A down-striking visible typebar machine with sprung key levers, ball-and-socket typebar bearings, pawl letter spacing and ribbon feed refinements.

**How it works:** Key levers pivot on a rod and are held up by light springs; key stems are riveted into the levers to avoid wobble. Depressing links pass through a perforated curved platform to typebars mounted in two rows of brackets, each typebar seated by a ball-and-socket bearing adjusted by a bolt. The typebars strike downward to the paper so the writing is visible. A carriage with letter-spacing pawls, a bar-lock, a bell and two-ribbon feed ratchets completes the machine.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** key lever; typebar; bracket; depressing link; carriage; letter-spacing pawl; bar-lock; ribbon ratchet; bell
