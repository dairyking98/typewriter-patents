## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (ring stamping attachment) · **Subject (patent classes):** Machines or apparatus for embossing decorations or marks, e.g. embossing coins having a series of embossing tools each of which can be brought into working position

**How it works (reading):** An attachment with a guided type carrier holding all characters in grooves moves past the anvil, or is an intermittently rotated disc, so the ring is indexed in place after each blow.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ring stamping needs a die change after each blow, and a type disk that needs the ring moved between positions is imprecise.

**Solution:** An attachment with a guided type carrier holding all characters in grooves moves past the anvil, or is an intermittently rotated disc, so the ring is indexed in place after each blow.
