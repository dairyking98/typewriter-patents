## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in ribbon mechanism, of a visible writing niachine and comprises an improved means whereby a variable throw may be imparted to the ribbon vibrator to carry different zones of the ribbon to the printing voint.

**How it works (reading):** A link between the universal bar and the ribbon vibrator has surfaces at different elevations; rocking the plate brings a surface into line, so the vibrator moves through a set distance.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A two-colour ribbon needs a variable throw of the ribbon vibrator to bring each zone to the printing point.

**Solution:** A link between the universal bar and the ribbon vibrator has surfaces at different elevations; rocking the plate brings a surface into line, so the vibrator moves through a set distance.
