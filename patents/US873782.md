**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper; Flexible washer

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon copies need uniform spacing, and the line spacing of the platen is not indicated.

**Solution:** A line-space indicator on the carriage, built from a base plate, gear segment and stop arm, shows and sets the spacing between carbon copies.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
