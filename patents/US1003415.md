## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Remington No. 10 ribbon mechanism needs to use chosen fields of the ribbon.

**Solution:** Vibratory ribbon mechanism with adjustable connection, a vibrator restoring spring and adjustable block to select the ribbon field.

**How it works:** The machine has a shifting platen on a truck pulled by a spring drum, with escapement devices. The ribbon vibrator is driven by an operating lever and actuating link, with an adjustable block between them. A restoring spring returns the vibrator. Changing the block selects which ribbon field is at the print point.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; operating lever; actuating link; adjustable block; restoring spring; platen frame; truck; spring drum
