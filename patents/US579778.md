## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cheap typewriter with simple parts, typed by hand-struck keys.

**Solution:** A typewriter whose typebars are flexible sectional stems of type material that pass down through a funnel-shaped type-box to the paper, with a handpiece for striking the keys.

**How it works:** Key-levers and type-levers are pivoted in a bracket; the type-lever ends connect by metal rods to the tops of sectional type-stems, which are linked flexibly by a cord or wire through rounded rectangular sections. The stems drop into a funnel-shaped typebox whose bottom is a rectangular tube guiding the lower section with its character to the paper. A spacing lever, spring returns, a yoke for key return, a handpiece for striking keys, a line-shifting mechanism, a holdback rack and an inking mechanism complete the machine.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** base-plate; type-box; key-lever; type-lever; type-stem; section; cord; spacing lever; yoke frame; handpiece; line-shifting mechanism; holdback rack; inking mechanism
