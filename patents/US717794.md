## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-bar lever mechanism should be simple and efficient, the bars should be individually adjustable at the fulcrum to take up wear, and key levers should be removable without disturbing others.

**Solution:** Fulcrum bars and notched guide bars support the type-bars and key levers; the type-bar fulcrum points are rounded and individually adjustable, and each key lever can be removed alone.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
