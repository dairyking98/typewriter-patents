Application filed January 17, 1881. (No model.) have invented a new and useful Improvement in Type-Writing Machines, of which the following is a specification. My invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines in which a series "of pivoted'type-levers are operated by means of finger-keys in connection with key-leversand connecting-rods. The objects of my improvement are to unite each finger-key to its key-lever by simple means, and at the same time to retain per-. manently their relative position after they are once united. Heretofore finger-keys have been constructed with a strap to pass directly around the key-lever or a portion thereof.
