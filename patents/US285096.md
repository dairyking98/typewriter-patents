## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Finger-keys fastened to key-levers by straps, pins or screw threads either could not be assembled in the cramped space or turned loose so the key legends went crooked.

**Solution:** A finger-key with a cylindrical stem split lengthwise into spring jaws snaps into a flanged sheet-metal link pivoted on the key-lever strap.

**How it works:** A sheet-metal strap is sprung round the wooden key-lever. A thin flanged link is riveted to the strap ends. The finger-key stem is slit at its lower end and the two halves are sprung together slightly. The stem is pushed through the guide-bar holes and over the link until the jaws snap into the link's opening, the flanges stopping it turning or coming off.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** finger-key; split stem; key-lever; metal strap; flanged link; guide bar; rivet

**Numbers:** 6 parallel guide bars

**Improves on:**

- screw-threaded stem keys: snap fit cannot turn and misalign the legend
