## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The paper must be held against the platen so it feeds with it on line spacing yet slides freely during carriage-return and paper insertion.

**Solution:** Curved pressure springs and a pressure roller or bed of polished metal press the paper onto a soft-covered platen, so the paper clings to the platen but slides over the springs.

**How it works:** Long springs on the carriage front rail curve up against the platen, with a roller between their rear ends pressing the paper to the platen; a polished plate may serve as a pressure bed instead. The platen cover (paper, leather, felt, rubber) is soft and grips the paper over a wide area, while the springs and bed are narrow and smooth, so the paper slides on them rather than on the platen. Line spacing is by a ratchet wheel on the platen with a holding ratchet and a propelling pallet.
