## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** A stop lever on the switch lock bracket is spring-loaded against a stop pin so it limits the ratchet carrier while writing, but yields when the switching bridge is used to remove a type lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The swinging stop lever that limits the ratchet carrier must not block the switching bridge when a type lever is pushed back to remove it.

**Solution:** A stop lever on the switch lock bracket is spring-loaded against a stop pin so it limits the ratchet carrier while writing, but yields when the switching bridge is used to remove a type lever.
