## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a switchable type lever bearing segment that differs from the known bearing segments this type differs by the peculiarity of its guide device, insofar the guide neither from an actual flat guide nor from a ball guide, also does not consist of a combination of both, but it is a kind of tail control using the well-known three-point principle.

**How it works (reading):** Two adjustable sliding jaws guide hemispherical-ended bolts on the segment, so it can swing about the line joining the two guide points and stay in a three-point guide without clamping.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Flat and ball guides for a switchable type-lever bearing segment jam under small manufacturing errors, because the guide does not allow the segment to pivot.

**Solution:** Two adjustable sliding jaws guide hemispherical-ended bolts on the segment, so it can swing about the line joining the two guide points and stay in a three-point guide without clamping.
