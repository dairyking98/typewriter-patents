## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The roller lever that quietens the stop of the loose escapement tooth returns the tooth too slowly for top typing speed, and strengthening its spring hinders the stop; swapping springs for tuning is laborious.

**Solution:** A second, independent spring returns the loose tooth, leaving the roller-lever spring weak for silent catching.

**How it works:** A pawl carrier holds a loose and a fixed shifting tooth that swing through the teeth of the ratchet. The loose tooth is stopped in working position by a roller lever pulled by a spring. A second spring acts only on retraction of the loose tooth. Both springs hang from eyes on a thin sheet part of the carrier; twisting the tines adjusts tension.
