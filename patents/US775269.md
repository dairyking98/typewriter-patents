## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in nary speed.

**How it works (reading):** A rack-and-pinion carriage drive with escapement dogs whose adjusting means set the dogs for ordinary or fast speed, and a step-by-step return pawl with its connections.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage needs guiding so that an accurate escapement is possible, and the escapement must be positive in operation.

**Solution:** A rack-and-pinion carriage drive with escapement dogs whose adjusting means set the dogs for ordinary or fast speed, and a step-by-step return pawl with its connections.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
