## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to type actions.

**How it works (reading):** Key levers in banks of different lengths drive angular sub-levers through pin-and-slot joints, and upright arms carry the motion to the segmentally arranged type bars, which swing forward to the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor wants a simple and efficient type action that gives a case-shifting movement of the type bars.

**Solution:** Key levers in banks of different lengths drive angular sub-levers through pin-and-slot joints, and upright arms carry the motion to the segmentally arranged type bars, which swing forward to the platen.
