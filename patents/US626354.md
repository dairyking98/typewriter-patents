## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** My objects are to improve the construction and operation of these portions of a type-writing machine.

**How it works (reading):** A general redesign of a typebar writer: pivoting of typebars, escapement tied to the universal bar, line-locking, platen carrier with height adjustment and release, and ribbon drive.

## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Several parts of the typebar machine (typebar action, escapement, line lock, platen mounting, ribbon) need improving.

**Solution:** A general redesign of a typebar writer: pivoting of typebars, escapement tied to the universal bar, line-locking, platen carrier with height adjustment and release, and ribbon drive.

**How it works:** The opening describes a twelve-sheet typebar machine with improved typebar pivots, an escapement actuated by the universal bar, a line lock that prevents overprinting at the end of line, and margin-stop devices on a carriage rack. The platen carrier can be lowered for manifolding, the platen freed from its ratchet for turning, and a rear pressure roll hung so it can be released. A ribbon mechanism and bell are included. Only the list of figures and objects is in the opening text.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** typebar; universal bar; escapement; platen carrier; platen; pressure roll; paper guide; margin stop; locking lever; carriage rack; ribbon mechanism
