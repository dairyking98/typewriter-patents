## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a device by means of which it is achieved that the actuated by the drive wheel, rack mounted swinging upwards during upshifting (changeover) the platen against jumping out of the drive wheel by itself will.

**How it works (reading):** A locking member on the paper carriage frame is moved by the upshifting platen so it lies over the rack before the platen reaches its top, holding the rack in the drive wheel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressing the shift key violently makes the platen rack jump out of the drive wheel, leaving uneven spacing.

**Solution:** A locking member on the paper carriage frame is moved by the upshifting platen so it lies over the rack before the platen reaches its top, holding the rack in the drive wheel.
