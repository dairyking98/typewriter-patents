## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such shorthand or template holder, which can be attached to a removable typewriter table Base plate.

**How it works (reading):** A concealed pull element in the articulated arm transmits a key lever movement to the ruler rail that advances line by line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The shorthand record holder needs a remote line-spacing action from a key on the table, without a contact linkage in the articulated arm.

**Solution:** A concealed pull element in the articulated arm transmits a key lever movement to the ruler rail that advances line by line.
