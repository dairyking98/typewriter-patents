## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving thick sheets or bills up to the writing line takes more than a full turn of the platen, and the operator must repeatedly work the return lever or finger buttons.

**Solution:** A crank drives a pawl and ratchet on the platen axle, and an adjustable stop on a disk that can rotate about the platen axis limits the angle, so one crank movement turns the platen more than a full revolution.
