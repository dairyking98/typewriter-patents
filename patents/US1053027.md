## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Shift-lock on a platen-shift typewriter needs a simple latch that locks with one key and releases with the other.

**Solution:** A pivoted latch plate with an inclined slot that catches the shift-lock key lever when depressed and is freed by the ordinary shift key.

**How it works:** Two key levers, shift lock and shift, act on a rock shaft and bell crank that raise the platen frame. A latch plate pivoted on the frame has a slot receiving the lock lever. Depressing the lock lever rocks the plate so it overlies the lever and holds it down in upper case. Pressing the shift key slightly moves the plate so the slot returns over the lock lever, which springs up. Depressing the shift key alone does not lock.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** platen; shift key; shift lock key; bell crank; rock shaft; latch plate; spring; platen frame
