**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for changing types in typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A small device clamped to the type lever inside the basket sets the baseline of the old type and then holds the new type in an adjustable alignment and clamping fixture for soldering.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Replacing one type on a type lever needs removal of the whole basket, or a jig that cannot align a single type.

**Solution:** A small device clamped to the type lever inside the basket sets the baseline of the old type and then holds the new type in an adjustable alignment and clamping fixture for soldering.
