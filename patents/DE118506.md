## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** One lever pulls the carriage back and first line-spaces the platen, so every return from any carriage position also feeds the line.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Combined carriage-return and line-space devices only rotate the platen at the end positions of its travel, but a short line ends early, so the line feed must be done separately by hand.

**Solution:** One lever pulls the carriage back and first line-spaces the platen, so every return from any carriage position also feeds the line.

**How it works:** A pull rod is shifted by a shift lever or button. At the start of its stroke it flips a hammer lever that strikes a rod running along the platen, swinging the pawl lever so the pawl turns the platen ratchet one line. On further travel the rod reaches the end of its slot and takes a rack along, which turns the cord drum through a gear and pushes the carriage back to the right. A spiral spring between rack pin and pull rod keeps the order: line feed first, then return. A small lever deflection gives line feed alone.

**Parts:** platen; line-space ratchet wheel; switching pawl; pull rod; shift lever; hammer lever; rack; gear; cord drum; spiral spring
