## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters are complicated and costly. Gardner wants a simpler, cheaper machine.

**Solution:** A type cylinder or segment on a vertical shaft is turned by a stepped lever that the keys press to different depths, and a spring-driven hammer prints through a shield hole from behind the paper.

**How it works:** Each key stem presses a stepped or diagonal U-lever to a depth set by its position along the keyboard. The lever turns a rock-shaft with a toothed segment, which turns the pinion cut in the type cylinder to a different angle for each key. A finger on the cylinder shaft sweeps an arc and is stopped against the depressed key stem, which locates the character exactly. Three fields of two rows each are reached by lifting the cylinder along its shaft: a shift key lifts it one row, and a notched, detented lever lifts it to the second or third field. The key also trips a trigger that releases a spring-driven rubber-faced hammer, which presses the paper through a shield hole onto one character. The same stroke steps an escape wheel one tooth with fast and loose dogs, which moves the paper carriage. The type is inked by passing across a felt pad on each stroke.

**Features:** [Keys that only set the element](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [Type sector or segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md), [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md), [Platen pressed to the element](../features/imp-platen-to-element.md), [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** type cylinder; pinion; toothed segment; rock-shaft; stepped lever; type key; stop finger; shift key; lifting lever; detent; trigger; impression hammer; hammer spring; escape wheel; fast dog; loose dog; release key; ink pad; shield; paper roller; paper carriage

**Numbers:** 3 fields of 2 rows; 6 characters per key; 4 characters engraved per key

**Improves on:**

- conventional typebar typewriters: one cylinder replaces many typebars to cut cost and parts
