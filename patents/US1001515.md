## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Shift key operated by the little finger of the left hand slows writing.

**Solution:** A clip-on attachment lets the left thumb operate the existing shift lever.

**How it works:** A rod journaled in blocks on the machine front, covered underneath with felt, cork or rubber to protect the desk and hold position, carries a vertical plate fixed to it and a loop of tape or cord that engages the shift key on the usual shift lever. The operator presses the plate with the thumb instead of the shift key.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** rod; block; vertical plate; loop; shift lever; shift key

**Improves on:**

- usual shift key: thumb bar added through a loop to the existing lever
