## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the invention is to provide a simple, durable, economic, and readily-applied copy-holder especially adapted to book type-writing machinessuch as, for example, what is known as the Elliott & Hatch machinebut which is also applicable to the frame of any type-writing machine.

**How it works (reading):** A bed-plate on a carriage standard, adjustable laterally and vertically, with a clamping arm and spring that hold the copy and mark its alignment.
