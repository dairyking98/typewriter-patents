## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Returning the paper carriage by hand takes effort and time; a spring return motor loses tension with use.

**Solution:** A spring-drum return motor clutched to a pulley connected to the carriage, with a small electric motor that rewinds the spring automatically and cuts out when tension is restored.

**How it works:** A small electric motor on a standard at the rear right of the typewriter drives a shaft through reducing gears. A loose pinion and drum on the shaft hold a volute spring, and a toothed cam rim and clutch ring couple the drum to a pulley linked to the carriage. A pawl on a lever restrains a ratchet on the drum; a key near the keyboard trips the lever, releasing the spring to pull the carriage back while closing the circuit to the electric motor, which rewinds the spring and is cut out at normal tension. The unit is applicable to any typewriter with a transversely moving carriage.
