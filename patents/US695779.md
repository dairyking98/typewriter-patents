## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-Writers, and has for its object to provide a motor for returning the paper-carriage to its startingpoint and a primary motor operatively connected to the first-mentioned motor to maintain the energy of the first motor.
