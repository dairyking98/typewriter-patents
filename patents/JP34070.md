## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter with a great many characters needs simple construction and handling.

**Solution:** A flat plate engraved with all characters, with a matching index plate fixed on top, lies over the ribbon and paper, and a guide on a key is joined directly to a hammer that presses paper and ribbon against the engraved character beneath it.

**How it works:** The character plate (relief characters on its underside) and the index plate (same characters written above each) slide together on rollers. A carriage on rails moves forward-back and left-right, carrying a key with a spring-held guide at its tip and a hammer directly below the guide. The operator moves the key until the wanted character sits in the notch of the guide, then presses it; a lever turns the hammer, which presses a rubber plate on the platen, the paper and the ribbon up against the engraved plate. A ribbon between two spools passes between platen and character plate. The same stroke, through a rod, bell crank, link and parallel levers, works two pawls on a rack fixed to the platen so that a strong spring pulls the platen back one tooth, shifting the printing point; the platen is returned by hand at line end.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Printing device applied to a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a printing device for a typewriter in which the engravings of many characters are gathered and arranged on a flat plate; an index plate, with the same arrangement, is laid over the engraved plate; a ribbon and the printing paper are placed under the flat plate; and a guide, which points to a character on the index plate, is joined directly to a hammer, which presses the paper and ribbon against the engraved character. Its object is to make the construction and handling simple and easy when it is applied to the kind of typewriter that uses a great many characters. Brief description of the drawings.
