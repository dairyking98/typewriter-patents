## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**How it works (reading):** Each key-lever motion reaches the vertical type lever through an angle lever and intermediate lever whose hinge points are placed so the rest position is over-centre (self-locking) and the first motion accelerates gradually.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-lever drive linkage in flat-plate machines lacks self-locking at rest, rebound protection and gradual acceleration.

**Solution:** Each key-lever motion reaches the vertical type lever through an angle lever and intermediate lever whose hinge points are placed so the rest position is over-centre (self-locking) and the first motion accelerates gradually.
