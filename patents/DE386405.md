## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**How it works (reading):** A slotted drive pin with a neck on the bevel gear passes through elongated holes in the spool flange, and light pressure locks the spool so it turns with the drive.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spool push-on pins are either too loose or stick, so spools fall out or jam.

**Solution:** A slotted drive pin with a neck on the bevel gear passes through elongated holes in the spool flange, and light pressure locks the spool so it turns with the drive.
