## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing in Braille or with keyboards specially adapted for use by blind or disabled persons

**How it works (reading):** Six triangular swinging frames, each carrying a stamp on a bridge near the rear tip, are pushed by cutouts under the key levers.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Braille typewriters with rectangular swinging frames twist, and electric ones are costly.

**Solution:** Six triangular swinging frames, each carrying a stamp on a bridge near the rear tip, are pushed by cutouts under the key levers.

**How it works:** Six frames are pivoted on an axis and each moves one embossing stamp. The frame sides under the key levers have cutouts so a key moves only frames needed for that braille character. Spiral springs return stamps and frames. The triangular shape resists twisting. A switching device advances the paper.

**Features:** [For the blind](../features/use-blind.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** triangular frame; stamp; bridge; key lever; spiral spring; switching device

**Numbers:** 6 stamps; 6 frames
