## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A counter for sums must be adapted to English currency (pence and shillings), but the decimal design of the gear chain does not fit.

**Solution:** Pennies and shillings positions use parallel gear sets with equal numbers of zero teeth and identical pitch, so the decimal counter works for non-decimal currency.
