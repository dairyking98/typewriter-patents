## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typewriters gave impressions that were not permanent, and the inventor wants them to print with ink.

**Solution:** An inking attachment removably fixed to the type bars carries a separate ink supply for each type bar, and the type is made porous so ink passes through it to the paper when the type strikes.
