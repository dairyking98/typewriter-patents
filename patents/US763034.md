## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing must be automatic and must not operate on a plain carriage return without spacing.

**Solution:** A spring-pawl on a lever engages a ratchet on the platen and is driven by the carriage; a spring returns the lever, and the spacing is thrown out when required.
