## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type cylinders get uneven or excessive ink when the inking is not metered.

**Solution:** A paint container valve opens only on each impression and feeds a paint roller that rubs ink onto the type rolls, with an adjustable outflow screw.
