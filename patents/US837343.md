## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book typewriters cannot take paper of any length or width, and the paper is hard to reach.

**Solution:** A table serves as the platen, with a long carriage on guide rails carrying a laterally sliding printing mechanism, so it can type anywhere on any size sheet or roll.

**How it works:** A table acts as a platen and has hinged leaves at both ends. A carriage on rollers runs on two guide rails along the table, offset by a paper-receiving pocket behind. The printing mechanism slides along toothed tracks across the carriage, which has a hinged section so the mechanism can be moved clear for inspection, and steps laterally for letter spacing when a key is struck. The carriage moves along the table for line spacing. Delivery and take-up rolls can feed paper rolls.
