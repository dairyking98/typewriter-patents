## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon paper, copy paper and original must be fed and rewound automatically while typing.

**Solution:** Hinged arms and racks carry rolls of original, carbon and copy paper, linked to the carriage so they move together and are rewound on rolls.
