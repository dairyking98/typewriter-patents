## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal on the Caligraph needed to be simple, durable and automatic.

**Solution:** A spring-actuated lever and sliding plate, tripped when the ribbon draws tight, shift a switch bar that swaps which spool is geared to the drive.

**How it works:** Two pawls on the rocking spacing frame drive ratchets on shafts with bevel pinions meshing with the spool shafts. A shifter bar moves the pinions in and out of mesh. A sliding plate is pushed by the returning carriage and latched; when the ribbon tightens on a cross-bar of the latch the plate is released and the lever spring moves the shifter bar.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; ratchet wheel; bevel gear; shifter bar; spring lever; sliding plate; latch

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph No. 3: adds automatic reversal using ribbon tension as the trigger
