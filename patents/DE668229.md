## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a rest bearing for the printing elements on typewriters or the like.

**How it works (reading):** Rest bearing has collecting surfaces on the sides of the pressure elements, so only the sides touch and the back does not strike.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-lever rest bearings are noisy because the type-lever faces bounce on contact.

**Solution:** Rest bearing has collecting surfaces on the sides of the pressure elements, so only the sides touch and the back does not strike.
