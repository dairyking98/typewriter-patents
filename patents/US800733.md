## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key levers

**How it works (reading):** Key-levers are built of aluminium or soft metal with an externally adjustable contour that contacts the escapement bar, and adjustable connections run from each key-lever to its type-bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-levers are hard to build and adjust, and their connections to the type-bars and to the escapement bar cannot be properly adjusted.

**Solution:** Key-levers are built of aluminium or soft metal with an externally adjustable contour that contacts the escapement bar, and adjustable connections run from each key-lever to its type-bar.
