## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The carbon holder feed is coupled to the line-switch mechanism so it starts before the platen turns and stops before the platen stops, keeping the tapes tension-free during line feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On long paper webs the carbon sheet holder drags the sheets and they slip on the platen at each line break.

**Solution:** The carbon holder feed is coupled to the line-switch mechanism so it starts before the platen turns and stops before the platen stops, keeping the tapes tension-free during line feed.
