## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor wants a simpler and more efficient type action that also gives a case-shifting movement of the type basket.

**Solution:** Key levers of different lengths in banks act through pin-and-slot connections on angular sub-levers and upright sub-levers to swing the type bars to the platen, with the type basket moved for case shift.

**Features:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
