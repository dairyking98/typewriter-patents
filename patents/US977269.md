## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is a special form of construction of the above described mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A feeler bears on the spool periphery and is elastically connected to a pawl that engages a worm on the spool shaft, so only the feeler's intended swing drives the pawl; the pawl then stays engaged until it hits a rigid stop, reversing the ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal must occur automatically when the ribbon is nearly used up, but the feeler and pawl can be pulled out of the worm by reciprocating motion from kinks or loose winding.

**Solution:** A feeler bears on the spool periphery and is elastically connected to a pawl that engages a worm on the spool shaft, so only the feeler's intended swing drives the pawl; the pawl then stays engaged until it hits a rigid stop, reversing the ribbon.
