## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators lose time turning the platen and returning the carriage by hand lever at the end of each line.

**Solution:** A foot treadle linked by cord, bell-crank and levers turns the platen line-space lever and returns the carriage, leaving both hands on the keys.

**How it works:** A clip on the carriage rear bar carries a bell-crank lever whose short arm is corded to the platen-turning lever. A bracket supports an oscillating lever linked to the bell crank and, via a connection, to a treadle. Pressing the treadle pulls the line-space lever and returns the carriage. The design prevents the pull from shifting the Remington platen laterally.
