## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such f facilities and exists in that a ribbon movement from the spring box off;

**How it works (reading):** The space key moves the ribbon drive gear out of mesh with the gear on the spring box, using a one-way coupling so the ribbon stays still on the return; a spring re-engages the gear after the space.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pressing the space key makes the ribbon unwind uselessly, and earlier cures needed a special lever arm to trigger the pawl.

**Solution:** The space key moves the ribbon drive gear out of mesh with the gear on the spring box, using a one-way coupling so the ribbon stays still on the return; a spring re-engages the gear after the space.

**Features:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
