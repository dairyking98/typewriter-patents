## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber faces on a roll stretch and move independently, and the parts are hard to assemble.

**Solution:** Sets of toothed wheels on a shaft carry rubber rings, clamped together by longitudinal bolts and end heads so the rings cannot slip.
