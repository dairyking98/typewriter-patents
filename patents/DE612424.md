## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A counter for positive and negative amounts that shows the result with an aperture must switch the aperture at the right moment even with many digits.

**Solution:** Apertures are switched by the same parts, at the same time and by the same carriage-return spring force as the preparatory tens transfer, after the number is entered.

**How it works:** The highest drive wheel's tooth moves a gear segment one unit on carry or borrow, which prepares a pawl on a shift lever. While the counter moves forward, a roller lifts the lever up an inclined path and a latch holds it. On carriage snap-back, a roller releases the latch (or the lever runs on a slope) and a spring (the carriage return spring) switches the segment one step. The segment drives a gear that moves the toothed aperture plates over the number wheels to show the positive or negative row.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** counter; drive wheel; gear segment; shift lever; pawl; aperture plate; inclined track; latch; return spring; Maltese wheel

**Improves on:**

- counters switching apertures at zero crossing: switches after number entry using the carriage return spring
