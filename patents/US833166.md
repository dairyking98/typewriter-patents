## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The main objects of the invention is to simplify the means fordetermining mechanically how far back to rotate the platen for the insertion of a fresh bill in connection with the same record-sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A platen-rotating hand-wheel head that fixes to the platen axle by a setscrew and uses a clutch to set the margin depth at the top of the record sheet.
