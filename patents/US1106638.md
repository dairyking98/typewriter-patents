## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforated band apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms producing characters by printing and also producing a record by other means, e.g. printer combined with RFID writer; Apparatus or circuits at the transmitting end with keyboard co-operating with code-bars combined with perforating apparatus

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to apparatus for making perforated bands for operating automatic typesetting machines, automatic matrix-setting and line-casting machines and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A punch is selected by the type bar only when it is near its printing position, so the perforation matches the printed character.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Perforated tape must match typed text despite simultaneous or partial key strokes.

**Solution:** A punch is selected by the type bar only when it is near its printing position, so the perforation matches the printed character.

**How it works:** Each type bar selects its punch near the printing position, so print and perforation agree.
