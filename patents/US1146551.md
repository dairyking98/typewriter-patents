## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools are heavy and costly, adding wear to the ribbon mechanism.

**Solution:** A ribbon spool has a light tubular hub, formed from bent sheet metal blank, with heads; this lowers weight and cost.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
