## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** At the start of each sentence the operator must space two or three times, hold the shift key and type the capital, which takes several separate actuations.

**Solution:** An auxiliary key on the space bar, pressed further, advances the carriage by several letter spaces and shifts and locks the case in one action, and the lock releases automatically after the capital is typed.

**How it works:** The escapement shaft carries one fixed escapement wheel and two loose wheels, one sprung to advance one tooth and one sprung to advance two teeth, held together by tie bolts in slots. Ordinary space key travel is limited by a stop so the feed dog moves from the first loose wheel to the fixed wheel and back, giving a single letter space. Pressing the auxiliary key moves the stop clear and lets the space bar go further, so the feed dog engages the two-tooth wheel and the carriage moves a double space on the down stroke plus a further space on the up stroke. The same key motion slides a latch over the left shift key lever so continued depression shifts the platen, and a shift lock holds it until an automatic release unlocks it. The machine is a Yost No. 15 typebar machine.
