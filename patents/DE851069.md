## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**How it works (reading):** Each pressure roller is spring-mounted on its shaft so it can apply the drive force individually across varying document thicknesses.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Document feeding needs pressure rollers that can detect and reverse receipts when paper layers vary in thickness.

**Solution:** Each pressure roller is spring-mounted on its shaft so it can apply the drive force individually across varying document thicknesses.
