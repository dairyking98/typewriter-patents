## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing device for tabulating machines)

**How it works (reading):** Type bodies are stopped by gripping members as they pass the print line so they roll without slipping, letting a whole line print at high speed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers in tabulating printers hit the paper by impact and are slow and loud.

**Solution:** Type bodies are stopped by gripping members as they pass the print line so they roll without slipping, letting a whole line print at high speed.

**Features:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
