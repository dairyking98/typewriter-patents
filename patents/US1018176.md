## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon winding and reversing needed both spindles driven positively with automatic reversal.

**Solution:** Both reel spindles are always driven; each reel is alternately clutched to its spindle by a device tripped when the ribbon runs out, so the unwinding reel turns loosely.

**How it works:** Each spindle is turned by a worm gear and worm wheel with a ratchet wheel stepped by a pawl and rocker, which a typewriter universal bar can operate each keystroke. The spindles turn in opposite directions. A clutch with points slides on a keyed part of the spindle. When the ribbon is fully unwound and becomes taut, a tripping device sets the empty reel's clutch so it winds and releases the other reel, which then turns loosely.
