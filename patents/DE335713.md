**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the type linkage of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** The pin slides in a slot in the type lever foot with smooth walls and a larger hole at the pivot end, and a special finishing tool with scraper edges machines the slot so the linkage can be adjusted after assembly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The pin-in-slot joint between key lever and type lever does not give smooth bearing surfaces, and the slot is hard to finish and adjust during assembly.

**Solution:** The pin slides in a slot in the type lever foot with smooth walls and a larger hole at the pivot end, and a special finishing tool with scraper edges machines the slot so the linkage can be adjusted after assembly.

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to the type linkage of typewriters. In particular, it is a type of linkage with a pin on a linkage and a guide slot for this pin on a cooperating with the former Linkage. The invention aims to perfect this connection and to design so that the slit walls Form smooth surfaces for the pin sliding in this slot and on its walls. The invention further aims for this Provide slot guide processing means in order to adjust the type linkage after to facilitate its composition.
