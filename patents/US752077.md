## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars cannot be removed individually without disturbing the others, lateral motion is not braced, and dirt enters at the type-bar pivots causing friction.

**Solution:** Type-bars are pivoted on a segment with bracing and guiding of the bars and a protective arrangement at the pivots, so one or more bars can be removed without disturbing the rest.
