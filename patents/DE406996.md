## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** Slider on the ratchet shaft carries a ring gear that engages a brake gear, so the brake shaft itself acts as the coupling element.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage brake on/off gear needed a separate coupling and a hollow brake shaft.

**Solution:** Slider on the ratchet shaft carries a ring gear that engages a brake gear, so the brake shaft itself acts as the coupling element.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
