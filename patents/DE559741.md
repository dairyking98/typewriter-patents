**How it works (reading):** A rigid curved register frame is stepped back and forth so its projections strike the keys in turn, leaving the keys free for hand operation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboard embossing machines cannot be hand-operated while a register automatically strikes keys in sequence.

**Solution:** A rigid curved register frame is stepped back and forth so its projections strike the keys in turn, leaving the keys free for hand operation.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
