## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression; Rotary-table machines

**How it works (reading):** The guide piece is made rigid, with the centres of its two arcs on a line that crosses the arcs before their start, so levers are guided past the closest point; the curved walls are cut with a crown milling cutter from both sides and smoothed by a wedge-shaped anvil.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type lever guides with converging curved lead-in surfaces do not stop the type accurately, because levers can reach the stop at an angle after passing the closest point of the arcs.

**Solution:** The guide piece is made rigid, with the centres of its two arcs on a line that crosses the arcs before their start, so levers are guided past the closest point; the curved walls are cut with a crown milling cutter from both sides and smoothed by a wedge-shaped anvil.
