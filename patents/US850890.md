## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A long record sheet with carbon hangs over the back of the machine and blocks the insertion of short bill sheets.

**Solution:** A zigzag wire flap in front of the rear paper shelf throws the long sheets forward over the platen so the rear of the machine stays clear.

**How it works:** A hinged flap sits just in front of the rear paper shelf; it is swung forward to hold the long record sheet over the platen and lets a new short sheet be inserted behind.
