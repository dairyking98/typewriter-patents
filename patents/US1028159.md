## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing line space devices on a rotatable cylinder machine are complicated and give no clear indication of the line space setting.

**Solution:** An adjustable shield on the line space ratchet hub has a flange overlying the ratchet periphery; its edge gives a clearly observable indicator of the line space adjustment.
