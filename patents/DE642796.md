## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**How it works (reading):** The pawl carrier is pivoted on a transverse axis and held by springs, so impacts from the carriage are absorbed as movement against spring force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage shock on the feed pawls is too hard, and a stiff cross spring cannot absorb it.

**Solution:** The pawl carrier is pivoted on a transverse axis and held by springs, so impacts from the carriage are absorbed as movement against spring force.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
