## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage of a Remington-type typewriter automatically; the earlier attachment (US418260, Woodall and Romme, 1889) is to be simplified and improved.

**Solution:** A normally inactive spring stronger than the carriage spring, acting through a compound bent lever and a power-equalizer, drives an auxiliary rack bar to return the carriage.

**How it works:** A standard resting on the machine's ledge houses a shaft with a gear meshing with an auxiliary rack bar attached to the carriage rack. A disc on the shaft carries a pivoted toothed bent lever with a detent and a spring opposing the carriage's advance. When released, the stronger spring pulls the rack back so the carriage returns; at the end the lever strikes an inclined arm to line-space the paper as in the earlier patent. A power-equalizer evens out the force through the stroke.
