## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter should run faster, be compact with few parts, and have visible writing.

**Solution:** Compact visible-writing typebar machine with a type-bar guide segment, rebounder, quick platen set-back and improved inking.

**How it works:** Long and short type-bars (two ranks) are pivoted and guided in a segment. The key levers are pivoted on the frame, a universal bar and rebounder return the bars, and the platen can be quickly set back for corrections.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md), [Type guide](../features/bar-type-guide.md), [Rebound and clash prevention](../features/bar-rebound.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)

**Parts:** type-bar; key lever; guide segment; universal bar; rebounder; platen; paper shelf
