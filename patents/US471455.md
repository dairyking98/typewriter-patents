## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (date and amount perforating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**In the patent’s own words:** The object is to construct a concise machine which will be simple, durable, and economic.

**How it works (reading):** A table slides under a row of punches and the feed clamps the document, returning automatically to its clamping position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dates and amounts must be punched quickly, and the feed must be easily disengaged.

**Solution:** A table slides under a row of punches and the feed clamps the document, returning automatically to its clamping position.
