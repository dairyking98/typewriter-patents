## Summary

**Kind:** index machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines characterised by applications of scales or indicators

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of our invention is to provide a scale for type-writing machines by the use of which the operator may be enabled to determine quickly and accurately the startingpoint for any given heading or headings in order to locate the said heading or headings centrally on the sheet, thus leaving an equal margin on each side of said heading or headings.
