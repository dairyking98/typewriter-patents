## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar pivots have friction and permit lateral wobble.

**Solution:** The typebar has a V-groove ring of small balls bearing against large side balls seated in the hanger arms, with a screw to take up wear.

**How it works:** A hanger of spring metal bent in two parallel arms carries opposite recessed sockets holding large balls. An adjusting screw through the arms sets tension. The typebar has a hole with a V-shaped groove holding about five small balls of diameter less than the bar thickness. The large balls bear on the small balls, forming roller bearings with no lateral displacement; alignment follows the axis in line with the bar.
