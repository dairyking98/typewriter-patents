## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Obtaining sub-total

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a cash register having a Device for printing: individual amounts and totals.

**How it works (reading):** The adder is temporarily coupled to the setting members to zero it, so the total is transferred to the type wheels using existing controls.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash register needed to print subtotals and totals without extra drive parts.

**Solution:** The adder is temporarily coupled to the setting members to zero it, so the total is transferred to the type wheels using existing controls.
