## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in certain features of construction, combinations of devices, and arrangements of parts, all as will be fully hereinafter set forth, and particularly pointed out in the concluding claims.

**How it works (reading):** Paper-controlled mechanism locks the type keys on the next-to-last line, unlocks them to finish the line, rings the bell on return, and relocks for the final line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator cannot tell when the page is nearly full and may print past the sheet edge onto the platen.

**Solution:** Paper-controlled mechanism locks the type keys on the next-to-last line, unlocks them to finish the line, rings the bell on return, and relocks for the final line.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
