## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter connected to card punch)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a connection between a typewriter and typewriter o.

**How it works (reading):** Power-driven intermediate levers, moved by the typewriter's type levers through a transmission, select the punch functions by key, giving a constant key stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Linking a typewriter to a card punch by keys was limited by the punch's number keys and by unreliable contacts or short linkages.

**Solution:** Power-driven intermediate levers, moved by the typewriter's type levers through a transmission, select the punch functions by key, giving a constant key stroke.
