## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on a motor-driven typewriter with one driven by a power shaft Power lever that is usually out of engagement with the power shaft and when depressed a key is brought into engagement with the power shaft.

**How it works (reading):** A resilient plate on a swing-out support damps the initial movement and speeds the final movement of each type lever through a front intermediate lever; the intermediate lever yields to the power lever and is returned by a spring before the type lever reaches the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The power-driven type lever meets the platen with a sudden jolt at the start and end of its stroke.

**Solution:** A resilient plate on a swing-out support damps the initial movement and speeds the final movement of each type lever through a front intermediate lever; the intermediate lever yields to the power lever and is returned by a spring before the type lever reaches the platen.
