## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Manifolding or like arrangements using unwound short lengths of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon is held taut between two arms on a two-part axle pushed apart by a spring; curved guides on a carrier make pins on the axle parts compress the spring when the arms are swung to rest, so the ribbon goes slack for changing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing auxiliary carbon-ribbon devices for typewriter copies use frames that fit poorly, long levers that stretch the ribbon badly, and are messy to change.

**Solution:** The ribbon is held taut between two arms on a two-part axle pushed apart by a spring; curved guides on a carrier make pins on the axle parts compress the spring when the arms are swung to rest, so the ribbon goes slack for changing.
