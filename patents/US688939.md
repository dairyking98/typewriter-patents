## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewritten copies in unlimited number are wanted without carbons.

**Solution:** Keys punch a paper strip while typing; the strip later drives a pneumatic device that depresses key-levers to retype automatically.

**How it works:** Each key stroke also punches a perforated strip and advances it. For copies, the strip passes over pneumatic openings; a perforation registering with an opening depresses its key-lever. Automatic line spacing and carriage return and a bell let one person run several machines. A shift-key also punches for capitals.
