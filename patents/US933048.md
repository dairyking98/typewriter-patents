## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In condensed record (billing) work, a record sheet must stay in place while successive bill sheets and carbon inlays are swapped, without special narrow sheets, extra guides or short end rollers.

**Solution:** A full-length secondary feed roller in the mouth of the pocket between platen and paper table lets one sheet pass between it and the platen and the other pass behind it over the table, while the main feed roller below can be hand-released.

**How it works:** Parallel links on the platen carrier's rear rod carry a spring-pressed secondary roller, lying lengthwise along the platen above the main feed roller. The inclined paper table forms a pocket with the platen, and the secondary roller in its mouth gives two passages: the record sheet goes between roller and platen and is fed by them, while the bill sheet and carbon go over the table, past the roller, to the main roller. A hand lever on the main roller's link rod swings the main roller, paper plate and paper fingers away from the platen, where a latch locks them released, so the bill sheet and carbon can be pulled out and replaced while the record sheet stays held by the secondary roller.
