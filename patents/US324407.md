## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Describes a Remington-style radial typebar machine with new carriage actuating, roller-turning and spacing devices.

**Solution:** Radial type levers strike a roller that is moved a space and turned by a ratchet-wheel and pawl arrangement worked from the carriage.

**How it works:** Key levers connect through links to short type levers around a circular opening; every type strikes on the same vertical line against a paper roller that sits on a sliding carriage. A ribbon passes under the roller on two spools. A ratchet on the roller and a curved lever with spring pawl turn the roller, and a rack, pinion and ratchet wheel with spring bolts move the carriage a space per stroke and disengage the pawls at line end. A secondary frame carries the roller for capitals.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** key lever; type lever; paper roller; carriage; ratchet wheel; pawl; rack; pinion; spring bolt; ribbon spool

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter: new carriage actuating mechanism
