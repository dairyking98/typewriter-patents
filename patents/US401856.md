## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Separate ribbons of different colours need quick swapping and separate shipping without a set of spools for each.

**Solution:** A detachable spool has a hub and side parts, with a bobbin carrying the ribbon end that slides onto the hub, so ribbons can be changed quickly on one set of spools.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
