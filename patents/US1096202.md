## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Registering, tensioning, smoothing or guiding webs longitudinally by retarding devices, e.g. acting on web-roll spindle

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to new and useful improvements in attachments for typewriters and more particularly to a paper roll holder, the object of the invention being to provide a device of the above character which is adapted for attachment to various makes of typewriters to support a roll of paper whereby a continuous feeding of the paper may be effected.

**How it works (reading):** An end-member attachment that mounts on typewriter carriages and holds a paper roll so paper feeds continuously.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Continuous paper feed needed a way to hold a roll of paper on a typewriter.

**Solution:** An end-member attachment that mounts on typewriter carriages and holds a paper roll so paper feeds continuously.

**How it works:** Movable end members clamp to the carriage and carry the paper roll and its tension.
