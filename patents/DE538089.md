## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing music needs a special typewriter or a fixed special keyboard, and existing lever devices are slow and error-prone.

**Solution:** A separate note keyboard is joined by angle levers and a flexible Bowden cable to a clamp on the platen, which twists the platen to each note position, so it works with any standard typewriter.
