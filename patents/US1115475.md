## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Controller-strip machines need quick, reliable control of printing parts by fluid pressure.

**Solution:** Use valves moved positively with the parts that follow the controller to send fluid pressure that positions a rotatable type carrier.

**How it works:** A controller feed roll with an escapement wheel and magnet-operated lever steps the strip, and a cam shaft with a one-tooth escapement drives the cycle through friction gears. Ducts carry fluid pressure to the mechanism that sets the rotatable type carrier's position. Only the opening of the description is available.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** controller strip; feed roll; escapement wheel; escapement lever; electromagnet; cam shaft; valve; duct

**Improves on:**

- application Serial 207,668 (C. T. Moore): adds fluid-pressure valve control for the type carrier
