## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (zero print switching in registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**How it works (reading):** A switching element driven by the setting means moves the type carrier above the highest amount place from space to zero printing, so the printed zeros mark the entry as a cancellation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cancellation entry printed like a normal entry can be faked, because the printed zeros do not mark it apart.

**Solution:** A switching element driven by the setting means moves the type carrier above the highest amount place from space to zero printing, so the printed zeros mark the entry as a cancellation.
