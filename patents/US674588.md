## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Springs pressing the pressure roll against the platen give uneven force along its length, so paper may not feed accurately, especially when creased or uneven.

**Solution:** The pressure roll is clasped at each end of the platen, exactly parallel to it, rather than held by springs; the platen and paper table are integral and removable from the carriage.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
