## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Paper roller trolleys for teaching aids and children's typewriters. The priority due to the exhibition at the Leipzig Spring Fair from i. March 1925 is claimed. The invention relates to a simple paper roller carriage for teaching and children's typewriters and consists in the fact that the paper guide plate also sits resiliently on the same axis of the carriage on which the bearing frame for the paper roller is pivotably mounted in the form of a simple bracket. The axis also serves as the seat of the spring for the paper guide plate and a flaxisch bent from the web of the bracket as an attack bar for the lever of the toggle keys. This simplifies and makes the car construction cheaper. In the embodiment of the invention shown in the drawing Fig. i shows a view of the car seen from the rear and Fig. a shows a Cross section of the carriage along the line ADA in Fig. I. On a transverse wall a of this sheet metal frame of the machine, the guide rails b, b are screwed, which serve to guide the carriage, which consists essentially of a longitudinal plate e with bent guide rails and a flat iron bracket d attached to this plate.
