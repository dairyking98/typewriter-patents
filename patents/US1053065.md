## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting' ma chines, and one/of the objects thereof is to provide improved means adapted silently to arrest the movements of certain operative parts thereof.

**How it works (reading):** A universal bar arresting the key lever strokes silently without concussion, with return means and guides preventing slip between the bar and key levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key levers strike hard against the universal bar and print with impact noise.

**Solution:** A universal bar arresting the key lever strokes silently without concussion, with return means and guides preventing slip between the bar and key levers.

**Features:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [Spring-powered blow](../features/imp-spring-energy.md)
