**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists of one to two parallel strands of bent piece of wire, the bendable points Replace the bearing pin.

**How it works (reading):** The handlebar is one or two parallel strands of bent wire, whose bent points replace the bearing pins; the two strands together are thinner than a single strand, allowing a small bearing eye, and the handlebar can preload a pivoting pressure support to tune the lever's stiffness.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Single bent-wire handlebars with hooked ends can snag and cause uncertainty, and a thick bearing eye adds friction.

**Solution:** The handlebar is one or two parallel strands of bent wire, whose bent points replace the bearing pins; the two strands together are thinner than a single strand, allowing a small bearing eye, and the handlebar can preload a pivoting pressure support to tune the lever's stiffness.
