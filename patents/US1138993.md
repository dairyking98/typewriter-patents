## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-space detent clicks noisily over the teeth of the line-space wheel when the platen is turned.

**Solution:** Latch that holds the detent inoperative while the platen turns, both by the line-space handle and by the billing attachment.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
