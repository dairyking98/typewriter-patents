## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (writing adding machine with exchangeable carriages) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to writing, electrical powered calculating or adding machines with exchangeable paper trolleys.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage elevator, tabulator and line switch drive is put on an interchangeable paper carriage that carries its own drive motor, so a plain machine can be converted by swapping the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Automatic carriage lift and line change need gearing from the main drive, which cannot be fitted to machines already built with a fixed paper roll.

**Solution:** The carriage elevator, tabulator and line switch drive is put on an interchangeable paper carriage that carries its own drive motor, so a plain machine can be converted by swapping the carriage.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
