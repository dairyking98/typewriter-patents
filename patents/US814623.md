## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must return the carriage by hand at line end, slowing typing and occupying a hand.

**Solution:** A pneumatic cylinder and piston on the carriage, with a valve tripped automatically at the end of the forward travel, returns the carriage, lifts the escapement rack clear and turns the platen.

**How it works:** A cylinder is fixed behind the frame and its piston rod is bracketed to the carriage, so the piston moves with it. When the carriage reaches the end of its forward travel a controlling valve is opened automatically, admitting pressure that drives the carriage back. During the return the escapement rack bar is lifted from the escapement wheel to avoid friction and breakage. At the end of return the platen is turned for the next line automatically. A spring and limiting rod on the bracket limit valve actuation. Designed for the Remington.

**Features:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** cylinder; piston; piston rod; bracket arm; sleeve; helical spring; limiting rod; controlling valve; controlling shaft; escapement rack bar; escapement wheel; platen
