## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-stroke visible typewriter needs a key and typebar movement that works very rapidly without keys interfering, yet can still give heavy pressure for manifolding.

**Solution:** Drive each typebar through a slide that first flicks it up by an inclined lug and, near the end of stroke, meshes a rack with a gear segment on the bar for high pressure.

**How it works:** Key levers pivot at the rear and press a spring-returned pressure bar. Each key lever is linked by a pin-and-slot to an intermediate lever, which pushes a slide forward. The slide's front end hits an inclined lug on the typebar and throws it upward to the platen. Late in the stroke the slide's rack teeth mesh with a gear segment on the typebar around its pivot, giving great pressure. A curved spring-loaded universal bar behind the bars checks and returns them.
