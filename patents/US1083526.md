## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Condensed record sheets with wider left margins on invoice sheets are hard to handle and position on the machine.

**Solution:** Attachments that fit existing typewriters control and hold the work sheets on the carriage and platen, including a wider left margin for the record sheet, without modifying the machine.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
