## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**How it works (reading):** Add two interconnected form feed rollers on the carriage, driven independently of the platen, so each form can be fed to the print line separately and cut off.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When typing a control list and separate forms (receipts) together via carbon paper, the forms must be spaced the same as the carbon copy lines, which leaves gaps and needs many line-space teeth.

**Solution:** Add two interconnected form feed rollers on the carriage, driven independently of the platen, so each form can be fed to the print line separately and cut off.

**How it works:** The carriage carries the platen with the carbon paper and the continuous control sheet. Two cheeks hold a roll, guide rollers and a cutter that feed single forms. A hand crank turns a disc and gears drive the guide rollers to bring any form line to the print mark and then cut it off, with a notch locking after one revolution for single-line forms. A second disc toothed like a line index wheel gives line spacing for several lines. Alternatively a rack on the frame, a pinion and bevel gears on a tooth coupling advance the form when the carriage returns, and a locking bar can swap the coupling for a drive of the platen. The text is cut off.
