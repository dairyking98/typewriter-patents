## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to those widely used such as the Underwood typewriter Type lever bearing with a radially slotted elbow, its slots to form the lever joints traversed by a coaxially bent piece of wire are, the levers in the rest position approximately parallel to the axis of the bow piece are directed, so form approximately a cylinder section.

**How it works (reading):** Each lever's pivot axis is not forced through the lever eye at right angles; each lever swings in the plane set by its own slot in the bow piece, so uniform slots and uniform lever bodies can be used without auxiliary bearings.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type levers pivoting on a radially slotted curved bow piece tilt outward at the ends, so the type heads misalign and the row needs more space, while auxiliary bearings to fix this are costly and hard to make.

**Solution:** Each lever's pivot axis is not forced through the lever eye at right angles; each lever swings in the plane set by its own slot in the bow piece, so uniform slots and uniform lever bodies can be used without auxiliary bearings.
