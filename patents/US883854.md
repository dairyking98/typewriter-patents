## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter placed in a public place needs to be unlocked for a set time after a coin is paid.

**Solution:** A coin conduit and a clock-driven lever set trip the locking mechanism. The machine unlocks for a half hour after the coin and locks again when the time runs out.

**Features:**

- **Frame and body:** [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** coin conduit; clock works with minute-hand wheels; trip device; lever; locking mechanism
