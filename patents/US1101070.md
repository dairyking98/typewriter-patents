## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With narrow bichrome ribbon colour bands, the fast ribbon vibrator tends to overthrow and miss the intended band at the printing point.

**Solution:** A stop on the vibrator lever meets a pivoted stop on the universal bar at one of four shoulders, positively limiting the vibrator throw for each case and colour.

**How it works:** Type bars push the universal bar, which carries an actuator that works the ribbon vibrator lever. The lever has two pins at different distances from its fulcrum, and shift keys slide the actuator so one pin or the other enters a vertical slot, giving a long or short throw to bring the lower or upper colour band to the print point. A stop device on the lever has four shoulders matching the four combinations of case and band, and hits a dog on the universal bar to stop upward travel. The dog is pivoted to prevent accidental interlocking.
