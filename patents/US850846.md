## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An accent key on Underwood machines needs to print without feeding the carriage while still vibrating the ribbon, with adjustable colour and stencil options.

**Solution:** The accent typebar has no universal-bar heel, so a separate ribbon lever operated by either the universal bar or the accent key moves the ribbon while the escapement stays still.

**How it works:** The accent typebar carries several accent types and lacks the heel that moves the universal bar, so the escapement dog-rocker is not stepped. A ribbon-vibrating lever with a wrist in a slot is operated either by the universal bar via a stepped member or by the accent key lever. Adjusting the stepped member varies the throw to choose colour bands or none for stencils.
