## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix machine for stereotype) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to machines for producingmatricesfromwhichtomakestereotype or electrotype plates for printing, and to machines for producing matrices for the purpose specified, intended to replace'the forms of individually-set type, as in ordinary practice, in book and newspaper work.

**How it works (reading):** A matrix-making machine that produces stereotype or electrotype matrices in place of individually set type, the character being formed in a flat matrix plate.
