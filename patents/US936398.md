## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Monetary values on checks and bonds need to be marked indelibly in a precisely located position on each paper.

**Solution:** Marking dies with an inking device and work-feeding devices, plus a means for locating the die accurately relative to the work.
