**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and n'iore'particularly to indicating means and to card or work sheet guiding devices therefor.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fractional line spacing gives uneven spacing because the operator judges by eye.

**Solution:** An indicating means on the carriage shows the line spacing so the operator spaces lines evenly.
