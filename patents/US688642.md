## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-lever touch was not uniform across keys, because the connection points between key-levers and type-bars varied in distance from the key pivots.

**Solution:** Type-bars pivoted on a curved segment at one side of the platen are connected to the key-levers at points in a transverse line, and the heels of the bars are given different angles so each bar's action is uniform.

**How it works:** Connection points lie on a straight transverse line in front of the segment ends; bar heels have graded angles so the links act uniformly.
