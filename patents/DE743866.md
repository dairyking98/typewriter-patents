## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (small writing adding machine with type roller) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a writing adding machine, which in its performance and equipment equals a conventional desk adding machine, but in theirs Dimensions are particularly small, especially designed to be particularly flat in height.

**How it works (reading):** The platen is raised into a closed housing and rotated for transport, the foot is set into the housing profile, and the numbers can still be read from above.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A desk adding machine is too bulky and exposed to damage to carry in a briefcase.

**Solution:** The platen is raised into a closed housing and rotated for transport, the foot is set into the housing profile, and the numbers can still be read from above.
