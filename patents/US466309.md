## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-lever alignment is lost through wear of the bearings.

**Solution:** Typebar fulcrum with a hardened steel pin and conical bearings, adjustable, with a looped strap preventing the bearings from spreading.

**How it works:** The finger-key lever is joined to the type-carrying lever by a sectioned connecting rod lengthened or shortened by a nut. The fulcrum is adjustable by a set-screw and has a head with a hardened pin with conical ends. The type-lever is a rod with a bent metal loop riveted to it; the loop surrounds the head, and the connecting portion prevents the bearings from spreading and loosening.
