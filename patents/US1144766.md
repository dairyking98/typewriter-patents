## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type bars must shift up and down for capitals and lowercase while staying connected to the key levers.

**Solution:** Individual slides with pin-and-slot connections to the type bars and bell cranks, allowing play during printing and up-and-down shifting of the type-bar segment.
