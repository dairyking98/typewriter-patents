## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machines with shift need either a shifting platen or doubled-up type; the inventor wants capital and small letters without shifting the carriage.

**Solution:** Two sets of typebars alternate on the frame, and a sliding ring of lifting levers is moved under either set.

**How it works:** Capital and small letter bars alternate on the suspending frame. A ring beneath carries lifter levers spaced equal to the pitch of one set. Sliding the ring by a bell-crank with a press button puts the lifters under the capitals or the small letters, then key action lifts the chosen bar. An arched slot and rod limit ring play. Applied to a Caligraph.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by turning the element](../features/shift-rotary.md)

**Parts:** typebar; lifter lever; sliding ring; sleeve; yoke; bell-crank; press button; suspending frame

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph: alternate bar sets share keys through a shiftable lifter instead of one bar per character
