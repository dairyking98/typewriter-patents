## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be secured on the carrying roll and fed reliably by the machine.

**Solution:** A paper-carrying roll with milled end heads, tensioned bars pressing the paper to the roll, and ratchet-and-pawl lugs on arms that engage stops on the carriage.
