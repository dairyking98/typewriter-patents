## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races; Width

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and more particularly to devices for guiding a side edge of the paper;

**How it works (reading):** A simple side-edge guide device that is attached to existing machines without structural change.
