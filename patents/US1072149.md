## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The ribbon must advance only during the forward carriage stroke and rest on the return stroke.

**Solution:** A spring barrel driven through a pawl and ratchet advances the ribbon on the forward stroke and leaves the ribbon shaft at rest on the return.
