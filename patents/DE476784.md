**How it works (reading):** The front carriage frame is built as a truss with pressure and tension cross-beams, and the middle of the carriage rail gets an adjustable support, so the stop force is taken without tilting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A sudden stop of the carriage at the margin could tilt or jam the carriage frame and deflect the carriage rail backwards.

**Solution:** The front carriage frame is built as a truss with pressure and tension cross-beams, and the middle of the carriage rail gets an adjustable support, so the stop force is taken without tilting.
