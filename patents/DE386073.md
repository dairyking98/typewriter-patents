## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register ribbon switching device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon cartridges for endless ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switching device for the ribbon of cash registers and the like.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon advance is changed automatically at intervals, advancing by a larger or smaller amount so the types strike an unused part of the ribbon and the whole ribbon wears evenly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon advanced by a constant amount strikes the same spots every cycle, so endless ribbons wear out prematurely in those spots.

**Solution:** The ribbon advance is changed automatically at intervals, advancing by a larger or smaller amount so the types strike an unused part of the ribbon and the whole ribbon wears evenly.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Switching device for the ribbon of cash registers and the like The invention relates to a switching device for the ribbon of cash registers and the like. And consists essentially in that the size of the advance of the ink ribbon is temporarily changed automatically, so that when the ribbon is moved forward Do not always hit print types in the same places. In order to avoid this, the known devices the ribbon by a certain amount after each actuation of the printing device, always constant amount moved forward. These known devices have the disadvantage, especially when it is endless ribbons that already with the second round of the ribbon the types of printing hit the same places on the tape as with the first one Circulation, so that premature wear of the ribbon occurs. This evil is eliminated according to the invention in that the ribbon after a certain Time is advanced by an amount greater or lesser than normal, namely the size of the advance is then such that the print types on one part of the ribbon that was not used in the previous cycle was.
