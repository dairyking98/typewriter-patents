## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The Invention consists in that a slide of the locking linkage 'on a carriage guide rail is mounted and displaceable by a connecting member rotatably mounted on it with the and rotatably arranged line delimitation stop is in drive connection, and the return springs, one for the slide and one for the link, are provided in a concealed by the slide recess of the carriage guide rail are protected against damage.

**How it works (reading):** A slide on the carriage guide rail is connected by a link to the rotatable line stop, and both return springs are hidden in a recess of the rail, closed by the slide.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The line delimiter stop mechanism must be interlocked with the key levers, and its return springs need protection from damage.

**Solution:** A slide on the carriage guide rail is connected by a link to the rotatable line stop, and both return springs are hidden in a recess of the rail, closed by the slide.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)

**Parts:** locking bar; angle lever; sheet metal slide; guide rail; tension springs
