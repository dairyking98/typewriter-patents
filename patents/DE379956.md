## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to an improved tabulator device for typewriters of the type in which a swinging frame or rail that the tab stops carries, is driven by a tab key. Such a device generally includes a tab key and drive connections between it and i. the carriage release and 2. a tab stop, such that when the tab key is struck, the carriage release is left free and the tab stop is brought into the path of a nose of the car. This process triggers the car, which then moves under the action of its mainspring until its nose touches the stop, whereby he comes to rest. It has been stated earlier that, even if the tab key is depressed Position is held to keep the carriage release disengaged until the carriage moves away from the stop is held up, damage to the trigger can easily occur, and it is subject of the invention to provide an improved arrangement by which this tendency eliminates damage and eliminates the need to press the tab key continue to hold down after the carriage has begun to move.
