## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix machine for stereotype) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the production of signs on the surface ofsthe matrix-blanks.

**How it works (reading):** The reading matter is printed onto matrix blanks by two machines, a common typewriter altered only as needed, so that several different types are used to produce the imprint.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Newspaper stereotype plates need reading matter in many type fonts, which one operator cannot manage on a common typewriter in the usual way.

**Solution:** The reading matter is printed onto matrix blanks by two machines, a common typewriter altered only as needed, so that several different types are used to produce the imprint.
