## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The front-strike action should be compact, lock against rebound, keep dust out of the link bearings, and still leave room for each part to be adjusted.

**Solution:** A visible front-strike machine with a shiftable type-bar segment, platen-shifting and ribbon-vibrating mechanisms, and a sealed independent bearing for each typebar.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
