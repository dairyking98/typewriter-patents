## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Compact and simple construction of a matrix-making machine is wanted.

**Solution:** A matrix-making machine in which a treadle pushes a plunger that strikes a selected punch, with the punch carrier sliding in guides to bring any punch under the plunger.

**How it works:** The treadle lever drives the plunger onto the chosen punch; springs return the treadle, and the punch carrier moves in two directions so any punch can be placed beneath the plunger.

- **Selection:** [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md)
