## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter with a full-length key mechanism is too long to carry when folded, so it needs a compact collapsed form.

**Solution:** Key levers split into two groups swing sideways into a non-use position parallel to the roller axis, and the front wall hinges into a box base so the machine folds into a rectangular case.
