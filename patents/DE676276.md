## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Device on calculating machines, accounting machines, typewriters and the like Machines for mechanical adjustment of on the front of the machine in front of the Platen inserted writing sheets on a desired line by means of a stop, which the writing sheets touch with their lower edge during the infeed movement.

**How it works (reading):** A separate stop for each writing line, each moved into effect or out of effect by its own actuator operated from the front of the machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing sheets must be set to a desired line by a single adjustable stop, which is slow and awkward to reset from the back of the machine.

**Solution:** A separate stop for each writing line, each moved into effect or out of effect by its own actuator operated from the front of the machine.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
