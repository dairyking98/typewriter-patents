## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a typewriter, in which the rotatable platen is mounted in a subframe is, with the help of which the platen is lifted from the usual feed rollers can be used to prevent the coal leaves from being pulled back between the writing sheets or between the folds of a sheet of paper folded in a fan shape and to describe a new part of these sheets.

**How it works (reading):** The platen is carried in a subframe that lifts it off the feed rollers; a clamp on the platen-holding latch presses a holding device onto the collating sheet while the carbons are withdrawn, with guide fingers that steer the paper web.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Collating sheets fed through the machine get pulled back between carbon sheets or folds, and the carbons must be withdrawn and repositioned each time.

**Solution:** The platen is carried in a subframe that lifts it off the feed rollers; a clamp on the platen-holding latch presses a holding device onto the collating sheet while the carbons are withdrawn, with guide fingers that steer the paper web.
