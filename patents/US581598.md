## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Input/output arrangements for oriental characters

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writing machines, and more particularly to those in which a wheel or a cylinder is employed whose periphery or face is entirely or partly provided with types brought to the operative position by rotation of the said wheel or cylinder about its axis.

**How it works (reading):** A cone-ended rod on a string-and-pulley linkage is placed in a registering hole of a plate to turn a type wheel or cylinder, and lowering the plate completes the registration and gives the impression in one action.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Index-type wheel machines need hole-and-pointer selection that is quick, with an even impression rather than irregular hand pressure.

**Solution:** A cone-ended rod on a string-and-pulley linkage is placed in a registering hole of a plate to turn a type wheel or cylinder, and lowering the plate completes the registration and gives the impression in one action.

**How it works:** A type wheel or cylinder is rotated by a string over two pulleys, wound on a drum on the wheel axis (or ends fixed to a type strip). A traveler slides on a path with the string and carries a long rod, doubly pivoted, ending in a cone with a sleeve. The cone is put into one of several rows of large registering holes in a plate, roughly setting the type; lowering the plate finishes exact registration and strikes the impression. Two symmetrical halves, one per hand, are possible, and an adjustable regular shock replaces hand pressure.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Pointer into a hole or notch](../features/sel-pointer-hole.md), [Press the pointer to print](../features/sel-pointer-press-prints.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Type element:** [Type wheel (types on the rim)](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [Multi-row type cylinder](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [Straight type bar or rule](../features/elem-linear-bar.md)
- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)

**Parts:** type wheel; type cylinder; string; pulley; drum; traveler; long rod; cone; registering plate; carriage
