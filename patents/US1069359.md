## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to clutch mechanism for releasably connecting the platen to the line space wheel of a typewriting machine to permit the platen to be rotated independently of the line-space wheel.

**How it works (reading):** A button on the platen axle end releases a clutch in the drum of the line-space wheel; rollers wedge against the drum and a releasing lever frees them, and an extra push locks the release.
