## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Curing or drying the ink on the copy materials, e.g. by heating or irradiating; Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to attachments for typewriting machines, particularly adapted to facilitate the rapid and accurate placing of work-sheets on the machine in position to be written upon.

**How it works (reading):** A paper table and guide is placed over the intake shelf and extends forward under the platen; arms hinge or rigidly attach to side flanges, guide sheets over the printing line, and feed rolls can be thrown off the platen to position the sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work sheets must be placed and positioned on the platen by hand, which is slow.

**Solution:** A paper table and guide is placed over the intake shelf and extends forward under the platen; arms hinge or rigidly attach to side flanges, guide sheets over the printing line, and feed rolls can be thrown off the platen to position the sheet.
