## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting a two-colour ribbon vibrator needs the operator to return a separate silencing lever to normal before using a colour key.

**Solution:** A self-centering rock shaft that moves endwise to neutral and is set to either ribbon field by the colour-shift keys.

**How it works:** A universal bar frame carries a laterally slidable actuator with two ears with slots. The vibrator lever has two wrist pins at different distances from its fulcrum; the actuator engages one pin or the other, giving a long or short ribbon throw, or neither when centred, so neither ribbon field is brought up. The rock shaft can slide as well as rock; a bevel on it meets a centering surface in a hanger, turning it to the intermediate position. Pressing a colour-shift key shifts the shaft back and sets the colour.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; vibrator lever; wrist pin; actuator; universal bar; rock shaft; hanger; centering bevel; colour-shift key

**Numbers:** two parallel ribbon fields
