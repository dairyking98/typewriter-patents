## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to a receipt printer for cash registers, computing and accounting machines, in particular for producing and issuing a check. The object of the invention is to provide the known printing units of this type to improve and simplify. While so far to drive the cutting knife and for issuing the check not only different, but also spatially separated arranged means were provided, according to the invention, the drive means designed for the perforating or cutting knife so that it also functions as a guide means serves to issue the check. The invention is illustrated and described in an exemplary embodiment, namely shows: Fig. i the side elevation of the receipt printer, Fig. 2 the plan of the Receipt printing unit, Fig.3 a section through the cutting device. The printing unit consists of side walls 1, 2, which are used to accommodate the various axes and shafts. The axis 3 is provided as a bearing point for the drive means 4, which is used to drive the cutting knife 5.
