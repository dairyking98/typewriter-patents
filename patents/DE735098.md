## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Music typewriters set the platen angle for each staff line by hand, leaving only one hand free for writing, with short levers giving imprecise settings.

**Solution:** Rotate the platen to each staff line by a foot-pedal lever system acting on a pendulum frame under the machine, with an adjustable stop bar for staves of different widths.

**How it works:** A rubber platen runs on a tube with friction discs and jaws, fixed or free on its shaft by a small lever so the roller can be hand-set to the staff. A pendulum frame fixed to the shaft is rocked by levers connected through pull rods to pedals set at two heights. Stepping on a pedal tilts the frame and so the platen to that staff line; a spring returns it. Small auxiliary rubber rollers on a ring hold the sheet and are repositioned beside the written line, while a pointer on the carriage gives the alignment and a lug holds the paper still.
