## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing platen/line-wheel couplings are hard to make and assemble, need a release button held by an aid, and need a hub that conflicts with a line-shift lever.

**Solution:** Two double-forked tendons press grooved rollers into engagement with the line wheel, released by turning a handle between the line wheel and lever.

**How it works:** The hub is fixed to the platen axle and has grooves and roof-shaped millings. Springs with adjusting screws press the tendons, whose fork ends push rollers up inclined surfaces against the inner wall of the line wheel, coupling it. Turning the handle by a limited angle pushes the tendons apart, retracting the rollers and freeing the platen. The handle sits close to the line wheel so the line shift lever is unobstructed.
