## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriter tables with horizontally retractable Machine support plate that is lifted when the desktop is pushed back and forth and is lowered as the desk top is pulled back up and down.

**How it works (reading):** Two one-piece link levers on each side of the desk, pivoted to the table top and sliding in guides on the support plate, lift the plate in parallel so the lifting plane stays near the machine's centre of gravity.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known typewriter desks that lift the machine support plate with side levers put the lift force off-centre, so the machine tends to tip and the lift is hard.

**Solution:** Two one-piece link levers on each side of the desk, pivoted to the table top and sliding in guides on the support plate, lift the plate in parallel so the lifting plane stays near the machine's centre of gravity.
