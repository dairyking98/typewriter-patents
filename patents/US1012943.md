## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement wears quickly and the carriage timing needs fine adjustment.

**Solution:** Front and rear dog rockers driven by a universal bar in one direction per key stroke, with adjustment of the carriage let-off timing.
