## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Japanese typewriting needs thousands of characters; the inventor wants types that are easy to choose, printed fast with little finger force, in a compact machine.

**Solution:** Rows of loose-type cells are gathered into a cylindrical magazine that turns and slides sideways on rails; the selected row is pushed out and a key-driven catching lever picks the single loose type from its cell, grips it and prints it on the paper.

**How it works:** Type cases (a plate with a row of open-bottomed cells holding loose types) sit in radial slots of two flanges on a hollow shaft, forming a cylinder whose outer surface is also the character index. The operator pushes in a press disc, turns a handle and slides the cylinder until the wanted character on the index is under a fixed indicator; releasing the disc lets a spring-driven inner shaft swing push-out levers that thrust the chosen case radially outward, a ratchet wheel and a pointed blade first trimming the rotation exactly. A separate locking key drops a ridge between studs on the rail to fix the sideways position. Striking the type key lifts a push piece that drives the single type up into a square tube on the type-catching lever; a half-round pin turns to grip a groove in the type, and the lever swings up to print the type on paper on a roller, then returns the type to its cell. A ribbon and automatic sideways feed are mentioned.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Matsumoto-type Japanese-script printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a Japanese-script printing machine in which a group of type cases, each a row of box-like cells holding types, is formed into a cylindrical body; the body can turn and move sideways, and each type case can move in and out, so that when the wanted type has been chosen and the fixed key struck, the type-catching lever grips that type and prints it on one and the same sheet, on one and the same written face. Its object is to provide a Japanese-script printing machine, very convenient to handle, in which types are easy to choose and printing is done at high speed with very little finger force.
