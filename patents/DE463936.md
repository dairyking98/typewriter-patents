## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for lifting the ribbon fork when changing typewriter ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon fork hangs from an angle lever that a hinged control lever swings out, lifting the fork and ribbon up and out of the way of the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the ribbon meant reaching over the platen to lift the ribbon fork, which was awkward and soiled the fingers.

**Solution:** The ribbon fork hangs from an angle lever that a hinged control lever swings out, lifting the fork and ribbon up and out of the way of the platen.
