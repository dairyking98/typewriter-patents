## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type levers of hard material cannot be straightened in place by ordinary pliers.

**Solution:** Parallel pliers with jaws kinked twice in the same sense grip the type lever at its end and have notches to clear the stiffening rib.

**How it works:** Double-kinked parallel pliers grip type levers at the end and clear the stiffening rib.
