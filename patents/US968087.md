## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Registering devices; Width

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to side edge gages or to means cooperative with the side edge of a sheet of paper to properly gage the paper as it is introduced into the machine.

**How it works (reading):** The side edge gage is automatically moved out of operative position after it has gaged the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A side-edge gage can crumple or tear the sheet when multiple sheets are fed.

**Solution:** The side edge gage is automatically moved out of operative position after it has gaged the paper.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md)
