## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (setting type disks for printing telegraphs)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for setting type disks, for example for type printing, telegraph, letterpress or evaluation machines used for static or other cards with symbolic perforations can be.

**How it works (reading):** Control disks are made as rings with adjusting grooves on their inner surfaces, so the pawl is not on the outer teeth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjusting type disks is slowed by pawls that fly outward under centrifugal force and by strong springs.

**Solution:** Control disks are made as rings with adjusting grooves on their inner surfaces, so the pawl is not on the outer teeth.

**Features:** [ELEM:ring](../features/elem-ring.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
