## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The writing-ball of Hansen's 1874 patent is to be applied in a new, improved arrangement as a complete writing instrument.

**Solution:** A writing-ball with sprung type pistons is mounted above a movable bed-plate carrying an anvil and a sector frame that holds the paper, and the whole bed-plate is pressed and stepped by pawl and ratchet.

**How it works:** The ball, made of light thin upper and lower parts, has type pistons with knobs, each held up by a spiral spring against a ferrule, and a fixed blind central knob for depressing the ball and bed. Pressing a piston strikes the paper against an anvil on the movable foot. The movable bed-plate, suspended on centres and resting on a roller on a flat spring, is stepped by two spring pawls working on opposite sides of a pawl-wheel. A sector-shaped frame with clamps at the ends of its arms holds the paper edges and is overbalanced slightly by a spring. A hand on a bevel-geared spindle moves over a scale to show position, and a knob-and-rod throws the pawls out of gear to return.
