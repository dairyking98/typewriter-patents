## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Syllable, line, or like type selection

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a type-writing machine which while reasonably compact will permit of the use of a larger number of type than usual, so that not only letters may be printed at one stroke of a key, but also syllables and words such as are in common use.
