## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Syllable, line, or like type selection

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a type-writing machine which while reasonably compact will permit of the use of a larger number of type than usual, so that not only letters may be printed at one stroke of a key, but also syllables and words such as are in common use.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ordinary typewriters carry only single characters per key; the inventor wants a compact machine with many more types, including syllables and words.

**Solution:** Several type-bar frames on a movable carriage, each frame holding sliding type-bars with multiple types, positioned so the desired type reaches the printing point.

**How it works:** Four carriers sit in a carriage on a track, each holding three longitudinally sliding type-bars whose axes converge at the printing point. A treadle and lever move the carriage to bring the chosen carrier to the print plane. Key levers engage lifter rods (spring-loaded outward on ball joints) on the lower ends of the type-bars to raise the chosen bar by the required distance, bringing one type on it to the printing point. Paper and spacing are left unspecified.

**Features:** [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:linear-slider](../features/sel-linear-slider.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [USE:special-script](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Numbers:** four carriers; three type-bars per carrier
