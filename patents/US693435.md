## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cast type-blocks vary in distance from tilt center to type face and step.

**Solution:** Mold the type block in two pieces machined to exact distances and screwed together.

**How it works:** One piece carries the type faces, the other the steps. Collars and flanges are dressed to set the distance, and a pin prevents rotation in the split-ring socket. Different materials can be used for the pieces.

**Features:** [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md), [ELEM:interchangeable](../features/elem-interchangeable.md), [ELEM:hub-keying](../features/elem-hub-keying.md), [ELEM:alignment-lock](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)

**Parts:** type block; step; collar; flange; rib; split-ring socket; pin; screw
