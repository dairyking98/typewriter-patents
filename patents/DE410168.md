## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typed rubber-printing plates made by direct metal-paper impressions give recessed gravure rather than planographic forms.

**Solution:** The process forms a planographic printing plate by writing on it directly with the typewriter; the first 3000 characters do not describe the steps fully.
