## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Impact printing of calculator type bars is noisy and wears the machine quickly.

**Solution:** Each toothed type bar has a spring-loaded angle lever whose catch engages a tooth, and a second spring-loaded lever then pushes the bar gently toward the paper roller so the types stop in a straight line without impact.
