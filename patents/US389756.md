## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A writing machine for the blind that embosses dots with simple, cheap parts.

**Solution:** A small carriage with finger-keys carrying plunger dies runs on a feed rack, pulled by a spring roller, advancing one tooth per dot embossed.

**How it works:** The carriage rides on a raised feed-rack rail above the table and carries four finger-keys. Two keys move vertical plunger dies with intaglio points that emboss the paper against a stationary bed-plate with cameo points. A third key and a feed lever lift an oscillating feed pawl with a locking pin out of the rack, and on release it re-engages one tooth farther, as the spring roller on a cord pulls the carriage along. Pressing two keys embosses two dots at once; the carriage can run either way by moving the spring roller.
