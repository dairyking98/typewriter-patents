## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in the various features of construction and combinations and arrangements of parts, all as more fully described and particularly pointed out in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** Rear feed rolls on a shaft in hanger arms are pressed toward the platen by springs, with a deflector cut away to clear the roller sections.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage feed rolls need to be pressed against the platen in a more effective way.

**Solution:** Rear feed rolls on a shaft in hanger arms are pressed toward the platen by springs, with a deflector cut away to clear the roller sections.
