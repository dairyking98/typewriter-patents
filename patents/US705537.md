## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates totype-writing maro chines of the class which print upon the work in a fiat condition and which are associated with a fiat platen upon which the sheet or work is snpportedduring the printing operation.

**How it works (reading):** A work gage associated with the flat platen that uses the track rails or guides of the machine as its clamping support to hold the work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work must be located and held in the proper printing position on the platen during printing.

**Solution:** A work gage associated with the flat platen that uses the track rails or guides of the machine as its clamping support to hold the work.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** work gage; track rails; platen
