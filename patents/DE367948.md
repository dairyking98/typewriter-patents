## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Headlights for magnesium ribbon lamps are awkward to transport and set up, and the lamp and fuel are exposed to weather.

**Solution:** The removable back wall of the headlight has a recess that houses the lamp for transport and holds rolls of magnesium tape, so the lamp can be set up quickly.
