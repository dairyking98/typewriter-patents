## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of attachments to type-writers designed for the purpose of billings.

**How it works (reading):** Stoppers and pegs on a rack cooperate through connecting levers to set the desired space in one movement for billing and tabulating.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating and spacing need to happen in one movement, with stoppers and pegs.

**Solution:** Stoppers and pegs on a rack cooperate through connecting levers to set the desired space in one movement for billing and tabulating.
