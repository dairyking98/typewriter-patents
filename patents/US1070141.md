## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The fragile copy web fed around the platen tears or kinks when it is coiled on a take-up spool.

**Solution:** A guide riding on the growing roll of web keeps the incoming web straight and steady, with its pressure acting as a brake, and the spool has a clamp and a removable head.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
