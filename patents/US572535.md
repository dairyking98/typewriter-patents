## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon must also move sideways step by step as the carriage moves, in Fisher's track-mounted machines.

**Solution:** A feed screw joined to the ribbon-spool spindles is driven axially by a nut turned through a rack as the carriage moves.

**How it works:** Axially movable ribbon spools sit at the carriage sides with ratchets and pawls giving longitudinal ribbon feed. A bar joins the spindle ends and carries a feed screw parallel to them. The screw passes through a feed nut in a yoke on rods behind the typebar ring, with an adjustable pin engaging the thread. A fixed rack on the frame meshes with the nut, so carriage travel turns the nut and moves the screw and spools axially, one way on advance and the other on return. A tubular guide supports the screw.
