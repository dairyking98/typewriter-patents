## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper pressure rollers and guide plates must be lifted off and returned separately when several forms are used.

**Solution:** Each paper guide plate is independently detachable from the common feed rollers so it can be applied by its own rail.
