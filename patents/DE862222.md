This disadvantage is thereby compounded with the object according to the invention avoided that the holding device of the paper roll when the paper roll is not inserted is retractable. According to one embodiment, this is achieved in that on two side parts of the machine for receiving the paper roll axis two slides with pin and slot connections that correspond to the length of their Slots can be pulled out and automatically when the paper roll is used up by means of a spring tensioner to be withdrawn.
