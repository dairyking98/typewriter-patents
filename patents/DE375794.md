> Description Translated from German Typewriter with front entry sheet for writing sheets. The present invention relates to a device on typewriters, with the Help one is able to get small bills that are just on the letter of a few lines arrives, what to keep properly in the typewriter It is particularly important if the sheets receiving the copies have a greater length than the small original leaves. To this end comes a front, approximately tangential to the platen, extending upwards to the rear Entry sheet in use, which has a stop bar on the upper edge, against which the smaller sheet is to be aligned. Entry plates of this Kind are known per se. But it is still with the previously known facilities it has not been possible to get the original sheets, which have only a few lines of writing should be kept in the writing position, since it often happens that the lower part of the original sheet cannot go around the platen.
