## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a device on typewriters, with the Help one is able to get small bills that are just on the letter of a few lines arrives, what to keep properly in the typewriter It is particularly important if the sheets receiving the copies have a greater length than the small original leaves.

**How it works (reading):** A front entry plate with a stop bar on its upper edge has a holding spring clip that keeps the inserted sheet in writing position, touching only the front of the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Short original sheets with only a few lines cannot be held in writing position, because they must be wrapped around the platen to stay in place.

**Solution:** A front entry plate with a stop bar on its upper edge has a holding spring clip that keeps the inserted sheet in writing position, touching only the front of the platen.

> Description Translated from German Typewriter with front entry sheet for writing sheets. The present invention relates to a device on typewriters, with the Help one is able to get small bills that are just on the letter of a few lines arrives, what to keep properly in the typewriter It is particularly important if the sheets receiving the copies have a greater length than the small original leaves. To this end comes a front, approximately tangential to the platen, extending upwards to the rear Entry sheet in use, which has a stop bar on the upper edge, against which the smaller sheet is to be aligned. Entry plates of this Kind are known per se. But it is still with the previously known facilities it has not been possible to get the original sheets, which have only a few lines of writing should be kept in the writing position, since it often happens that the lower part of the original sheet cannot go around the platen.
