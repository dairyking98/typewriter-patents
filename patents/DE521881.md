## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Existing devices for turning the platen backward need a second shift lever behind the platen, requiring a different hand movement from the normal line feed.

**Solution:** A second hand lever sits next to the ordinary line-feed lever on the same pin and drives a pawl that rotates the platen backward by one line.

**How it works:** The second hand lever 6 on the same pivot as the normal lever has a lug that pushes a sliding rod backward against a spring. The rod swings an angle lever, whose raised arm moves a link and a guide arm rotatable about the platen axis. A leaf spring throws a pawl carried by the guide arm into a ring gear on the platen end, and further rotation turns the platen by one line spacing in reverse. On release, a spring returns the parts, and a bevelled sheet-metal piece lifts the pawl out of the ring gear.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; hand lever; sliding rod; angle lever; link; guide arm; pawl; leaf spring; ring gear; return spring

**Improves on:**

- reverse line-feed devices with a second lever behind the platen: lever now sits beside the normal line-feed lever so one hand movement serves both
