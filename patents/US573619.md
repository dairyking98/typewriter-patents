## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the printing or marking process for which they are designed characterised by selective application of impact or pressure on a printing material or impression-transfer material

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a key-operated mechanism--a type-writer or similar machine-and more especially to an interlocking mechanism by means of which all the other operating-keys are locked against movement on the actuation of any one thereof.

**How it works (reading):** An interlock blocks all other keys when one is pressed, and each key selects a short or long carriage feed step.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter keys pressed together jam, and the carriage feed is uniform even though some characters need more room than others.

**Solution:** An interlock blocks all other keys when one is pressed, and each key selects a short or long carriage feed step.

**How it works:** Each key-lever, when operated, acts as a blocker for the others. A blocking member has one surface per non-operated lever, and an actuator moved by the operated lever carries those surfaces into place while moving the operated lever's own surface clear. For spacing, characters are grouped into those needing normal spacing and those needing wider spacing. A rack-feed mechanism on the carriage rail gives a short or long feed depending on which key is struck. Shown on a Caligraph-type machine.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** key-lever; blocking member; blocking surface; blocking actuator; side frame; top plate; platen; carriage; rack; carriage rail
