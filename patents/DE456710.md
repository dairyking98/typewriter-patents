**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (drive segments for portable adding machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a drive segment assembly for portable addition and similar machines with key setting and intended with the smallest dimensions of the machine, the counter and printing mechanism for the operator easily visible as well as a cheap production of the segments from one piece.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable adding machines with key setting needed compact, cheap drive segments with a low crank height.

**Solution:** One-piece drive tooth segments on a common axle, with arms that link to the type rods, keep the counter visible and the keyboard compact.
