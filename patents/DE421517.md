## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters, in which the changeover frame carrying the roller is mounted in a slidable carriage is and relates in particular to a typewriter of this type, in which the sliding Car slides on a runway as well as a shaft.

**How it works (reading):** The lower runway is supported on eccentric-headed screws in slotted brackets, so the carriage can be adjusted transversely to its travel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen roller cannot be adjusted sideways, so it is hard to make it parallel to the carriage direction.

**Solution:** The lower runway is supported on eccentric-headed screws in slotted brackets, so the carriage can be adjusted transversely to its travel.
