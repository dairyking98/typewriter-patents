## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cable or belt constructions for driving print, type or paper-carriages, e.g. attachment, tensioning means

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter of simple construction with a cable-wheel carriage return; the drawings show the carriage thrown back for exposing the writing.
