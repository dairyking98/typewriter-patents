## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Not stated beyond providing a typewriter that can write on loose paper or in thick or thin bound books; the text is a description of the construction only.

**Solution:** A box of 40 vertical type rods, each pressed straight down by its own button onto the paper, with each press stepping a two-axis carriage by ratchet one point (or three for wide letters and spaces).

**How it works:** A pointed, downward-tapering box holds the type rods, guided by a spherically curved top plate and a flat bottom plate with square holes so they cannot turn. Small springs hold each rod up in the air; its lower end carries the character, engraved also on the button above. Pressing a rod down moves a lever and an armature; on release a spring and pull rod drive a pawl that advances a ratchet wheel one tooth, moving the carriage and paper one point left via a gear and rack. One of eight levers has an extra projection that moves the ratchet three teeth, used for the space, m, w or capitals when pressed together with the letter. An ink roller slides on a small track under the types and blackens all of them at once; a second rack and release button set line spacing, and screws adjust the rod height to the paper or book thickness.
