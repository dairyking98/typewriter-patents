## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Keyboard operations of a typewriter or similar machine need to be amplified or reproduced by an apparatus working with the keyboard.

**Solution:** A desk with insulated metal plates engraved with characters, each wired and connected through a switch device for shift current, so that key operation triggers the electrical reproduction of the characters.

**Features:** [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** insulated metal plates; desk; switch device
