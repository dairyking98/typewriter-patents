## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need more bars in a circle with better alignment.

**Solution:** Typebars on long overlapping pivot bars held in adjustable hangers tangential to the striking circle.

**How it works:** Each typebar has a pivot bar spaced apart with the stem oblique to it, mounted in long and short hangers adjustably screwed through slots to the bed ring. Long hangers pass below the neighbour's overlapping pivot bar. A jerk lever on each pivot bar is connected to the key lever by a jerk rod. A double bank is possible.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** typebar; pivot bar; long hanger; short hanger; jerk lever; jerk rod; key lever; bed ring
