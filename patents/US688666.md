## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The main objects of my invention are to assemble a large number of type-bars in a small compass and to prevent lateral vibration of the bars while permitting them to swing I5 freely to and from the printing-point.

**How it works (reading):** Type-bar hubs with a V-groove and a row of anti-friction balls are carried on hanger-plates on an adjustable slotted curved bar, and the hanger is secured after adjustment, so the bars swing freely without lateral vibration.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A large number of type-bars must fit a small space, and the bars vibrate sideways when they swing to the printing point.

**Solution:** Type-bar hubs with a V-groove and a row of anti-friction balls are carried on hanger-plates on an adjustable slotted curved bar, and the hanger is secured after adjustment, so the bars swing freely without lateral vibration.

**How it works:** Balls in the hub's V-groove give bearing surfaces; hangers are adjusted on the slotted curved bar and then clamped.
