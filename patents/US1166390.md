## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter platens were noisy when struck by types, while rubber-covered rolls were heavy and costly.

**Solution:** A light platen whose body is ground pith (corn stalk, rush or sunflower stem) bound with printers' roll gelatine that is poured in hot around a core and covered with rubber, so it is quiet and light.
