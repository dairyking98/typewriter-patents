## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon needs to be fed uniformly past the print point, reversed easily by hand, and have spools that come off readily.

**Solution:** A hand-slid reversing bar with a detent disengages one spool pawl and its anti-backlash spring while engaging the other, with spools that lift off their spring-loaded shafts.

**How it works:** Spool shafts, spring-loaded upward, carry ratchet wheels engaged by pawls on arms of a rock shaft which is rocked by the universal bar at each typebar stroke. A slidable bar with finger pieces and slotted mounting moves a roll on a spring arm onto one of two faces, which holds the bar in position. In either position it frees the pawl of the spool being unwound together with a backlash spring, so that spool pays out freely. Pressing a spool down against its shaft spring lets it slip out from under the spool-supporting arm.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; spool shaft; ratchet wheel; pawl; rock shaft; universal bar; reversing bar; detent roll; spring arm; backlash spring; spool supporting arm
