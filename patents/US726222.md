## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The visible writing machine with oblique platen needs simpler, more perfect construction.

**Solution:** Platen moves endwise and transversely in a plane oblique to horizontal, with inclined type-bars pivoted in front of the platen.

**How it works:** The type-bars rest inclined upward toward the front; the printing point is on the platen at about 45 degrees from vertical. The platen travels along the line and obliquely for case shift, so the operator sees the writing in front.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** platen; type-bar; pivot; carriage

**Numbers:** 45 degrees
