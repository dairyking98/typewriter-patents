## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Regulating touch, key dip or stroke, or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the type actions of writing machines, and especially those in which type bars strike upon the frontside of a'platen.

**How it works (reading):** Touch-adjusting mechanism on the key levers that makes the touch elastic or hard, with type bars on a curved fulcrum wire in a segment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key touch of the front-strike machine is too tiring and cannot be regulated across a wide range.

**Solution:** Touch-adjusting mechanism on the key levers that makes the touch elastic or hard, with type bars on a curved fulcrum wire in a segment.
