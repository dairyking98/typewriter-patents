## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabular and column work needed repeated space-bar presses to reach each column, which was slow and tiring.

**Solution:** Separate devices vary the feed of the paper carriage so impressions can be made at several spaced points in a line instantly, without repeated space-bar use.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
