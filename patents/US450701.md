## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Column and tabulated figure work needed scales and eyeballing to align units under units.

**Solution:** A set of nine independently pushable stops by value (units, tens, hundreds) arrests the carriage so numbers right-align automatically.

**How it works:** A bracket on the frame holds nine stem-keys numbered 1 to 9 that converge onto nine parallel spring-returned pins. The operator pushes the key for the digit count of the number to be written, which projects its pin into the path of an adjustable lug on the carriage's front rod. The carriage is drawn to the right until the lug strikes the pin, then released and the number typed as usual; every line aligns by place value. Several lugs can serve parallel columns.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** bracket; stem-key; stop pin; return spring; carriage lug; paper carriage; platen

**Numbers:** nine stops (units to hundred thousandths)
