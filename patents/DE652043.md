## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing devices shifting the ribbon fork with the case shift were bulky, needing extra parts and keys, unsuited to simple machines.

**Solution:** Two cooperating angle levers, the ribbon-fork rocker pivoted on the short leg of a second angle lever, raise the fork together with platen shift as one compact rear-wall group.

**How it works:** The ribbon fork rocker has a horizontal arm with a bent-up sheet metal lifting eye near the platen and a downward arm pulled by a tension member from the universal rail. Its pivot sits on the short horizontal leg of a second double-armed lever, whose downward arm is pulled by wire from the shift key. The two-part arm leaves free passage for the carriage feed lock.
