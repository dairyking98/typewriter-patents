## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cheap, simple, compact and portable typewriter that is not liable to get out of order.

**Solution:** A hand-held sliding block carrying two rows of raised type and a locating arm is moved over a notched plate and pressed down to print, while a ratchet turns the paper roller.

**How it works:** A block Q slides on the front bar of a spring-held frame G; its arm R enters one of two rows of notches in a slotted plate, which positions the matching row of raised type under the roller. Pushing the block down prints the letter, and the frame's downward and upward motion drives a push-pawl on a ratchet wheel that turns the square roller shaft one tooth, with a holding pawl behind. A second pivoted frame J, pushed farther with the thumb, advances twice as much for a word space. The cloth or rubber covered paper roller, wrapped with colored transfer paper, is also moved endwise by a spiral thread working between teeth of a shaft, so it travels in a spiral; the thread can be disengaged to push it back.
