## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Positive-feed character-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a carriage feed mechanism for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage drive with a ratchet wheel and pawl, with an alternative feed form.
