## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Yost machine the typebar levers can unhook from their fulcrum ring and the ink pad has no secure seat.

**Solution:** A cover ring clamps the typebar levers on a two-part fulcrum wire ring adjusted by conical screws, and also seats an ink pad holder on top.

**How it works:** Key levers push rods that move hook-topped levers hung on a wire fulcrum ring in a groove of the top type-ring, each carrying a typebar linked to a guide link. A cover ring screwed on top stops levers lifting off. Two beads form a seat for a felt ink pad in a three-sided case with inclined walls. The wire ring is in two semicircles with bevelled ends and tapered adjusting screws wedge them apart so the ring is a true circle and locked.

**Features:** [Ink pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key lever; connecting rod; hooked lever; wire fulcrum ring; top type-ring; cover ring; ink pad case; felt ink pad; typebar; guide link; conical adjusting screw
