**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies

**How it works (reading):** A line numbering attachment mounted on the Monarch carriage has number wheels driven by gearing from the line space movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lines of writing should be numbered automatically as they are produced.

**Solution:** A line numbering attachment mounted on the Monarch carriage has number wheels driven by gearing from the line space movement.
