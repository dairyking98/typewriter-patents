## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Side gages on the paper shelf are hard to adjust and scratch the shelf.

**Solution:** The side gage rides on a rail mounted behind the paper shelf, with a grip piece that lets the operator release it and slide it for adjustment.
