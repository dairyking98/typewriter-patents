## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typebar carrying several characters (lower, upper, third) needs a positive, accurate way to select which character reaches the printing line, without shifting the whole platen or basket, and with strong uniform impressions.

**Solution:** Each typebar sits on a vertically shiftable carrier whose upward travel is stopped at one of three heights by pivoted limiting dogs, which are set by two extra key-bars grouped beside the main key-bar.

**How it works:** A group of three key-bars serves one typebar of three characters. The middle bell-crank key-bar is linked to the typebar; the two outer key-bars each have a finger behind it, so pressing either also pulls the middle key-bar. Pressing the middle key alone rocks the typebar to the escapement bail, then lifts the carrier until its stop finger meets the upper shoulder of the first dog, printing the middle character. Pressing one outer key swings a dog so its lower shoulder stops the carrier lower, selecting another character. The other outer key swings the second dog onto the finger to lock the carrier down, so the top character prints. Dogs are held rigid by the key finger head or by resting on the base plate.
