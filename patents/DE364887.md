## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**How it works (reading):** The key-and-type unit is pivoted in the machine frame on coaxial pegs, with a locking lever holding its hooks at a support point, so it can be swung out and back for removal and replacement of keys and types.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Removing and reinserting keys and type on typewriters that take them out by sliding the key-and-type unit is difficult.

**Solution:** The key-and-type unit is pivoted in the machine frame on coaxial pegs, with a locking lever holding its hooks at a support point, so it can be swung out and back for removal and replacement of keys and types.
