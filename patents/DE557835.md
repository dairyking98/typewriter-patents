## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards characterised by means for holding or fastening typewriters or computer equipment

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters tables with a soundproof machine, the upper part of which while writing can be closed by a cylinder hood pivotable about its central axis.

**How it works (reading):** A cylindrical hood in a table is pivoted down below the base plate by a foot lever, which drives a gear segment and gear on the hood axis, with a spring returning and closing the hood.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Soundproof hoods on typewriter boxes must be lifted and placed by hand, which is laborious, and the hood cannot be fully closed.

**Solution:** A cylindrical hood in a table is pivoted down below the base plate by a foot lever, which drives a gear segment and gear on the hood axis, with a spring returning and closing the hood.
