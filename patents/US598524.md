## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters need one key per character, or complicated shift mechanisms for multiple characters per key.

**Solution:** A type with a duplex face of two levels so that light or hard impact prints different characters from one key.

**How it works:** The type has an upper printing surface that prints at any impact and a lower surface that prints only when struck harder. A comma/semicolon is the example: comma alone for light, comma plus dot for heavy. Alternatively a conical projection prints broader when struck harder, thickening a line. Applied to a shorthand system with thirty keys giving two symbols each.

**Features:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** type; upper face; lower face; conical projection; finger-key

**Numbers:** 30 keys
