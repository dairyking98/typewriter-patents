## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing typebar machines are bulky and the type is badly inked; the inventor wants improved bars, supports and inking.

**Solution:** Thin sheet-metal typebars that slide lengthwise in slotted guide plates, driven by key levers through elbow levers, with adjustable stops and a new way of inking the type.

**How it works:** Typebars of thin sheet metal slide lengthwise in grooves of a slotted guide plate, each ending in a thickened type head and with rear arms and a spring end. A segmental slotted border plate registers with the guide slots; fixed and adjustable stops limit bar travel; primary and auxiliary key levers act through an elbow lever. The frame is of side plates joined by curved bars, with a cover. Only the opening was available, so the inking is not described.
