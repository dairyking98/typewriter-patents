## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil sheets needed moistening or softening before they could be cut on a typewriter.

**Solution:** The stencil coating uses a gelatinizing derivative of drying oil or fat that forms a gel, so the sheet is ready to cut without moistening.
