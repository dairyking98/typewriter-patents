## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The text gives no explicit problem statement; it describes a keyboard machine with a swinging segment type carrier that differs from known machines.

**Solution:** Three stacked segment type carriers on one vertical spindle are each rocked to a stop angle by two key groups, raised by two row keys, locked at the selected angle by a pin that is released by the carriage feed ratchet, and struck forward by a hammer.

**How it works:** Keys are in left and right groups; a left key rocks a bell crank which turns a hollow shaft one way, a right key turns it the other way, by an angle set by an adjustable screw under each key. A fork on the hollow shaft rotates whichever of three segment carriers is at its height, while a fixed pin locks the other two. Each segment carries spring-returned type rods on its periphery, struck forward by a hammer so only the one in position prints. Two row keys lift the spindle so the middle or lower segment is at the print level, with a latch to hold them down. A spring locking pin enters a hole in a perforated arc to lock the carrier at the selected angle once the feed pawl releases, and the feed ratchet moves the carriage one tooth on key release. Two toothed wheels of different pitch, chosen by a feather key, give two letter spacings, and a back-feed key moves the carriage back one or more spaces.
