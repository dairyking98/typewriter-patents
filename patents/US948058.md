## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Obernolte's alternating main and auxiliary margin stops did not let the operator lock the auxiliary stop in use with one action.

**Solution:** A slide switch on the carriage cams the stop lever to the auxiliary position and holds it there with a recess.

**How it works:** A margin-stop lever pivoted on the carriage has a main stop on one arm and an auxiliary stop on the other, working alternately against a gage on the frame. A slide with a cam face, thrown one way, cams down the auxiliary arm and lifts the main stop, and a beveled recess catches the lever edge so it stays locked against carriage impacts. Pushing the slide back lets a spring return the lever to the main stop.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** margin-stop lever; main stop; auxiliary stop; margin gage; slide switch; bracket; spring; carriage

**Improves on:**

- Obernolte application 530,326: adds positive lock for the auxiliary stop
