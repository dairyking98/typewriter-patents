**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide ribbon feeding mechanism for that class of typewriting machines known as front strike machines in which the ribbon is fed step by step noon each actuation of the type bars and in whichfthe ribbon is moved upon each operation of a type bar to the printing point to receive the type head impact.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On front-strike typebar machines the ribbon must be fed step by step and brought to the print point, and it lands at the wrong height when the platen is shifted for case.

**Solution:** Ribbon vibrating levers that extend or contract with the platen shift, plus a feed arrangement where only one of two spool feed devices works at a time and reversal swaps them.

**How it works:** Key levers pull typebars through straddle links and bell cranks; a hooked arm on each link rocks a universal bar frame and shaft at each stroke. Overlapping ribbon-vibrating levers lift the ribbon to the print point from the universal bar, and the platen shift mechanism extends the levers so the ribbon starts from a different point in the other case. A spool at each side has its own feed device, linked so that one is engaged while the other is thrown out, which reverses the feed direction. A ribbon frisket on an alining plate is also shown.
