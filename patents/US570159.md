## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A traveling paper carriage needs a mechanism to move it one space per key stroke (and two with a double-space key).

**Solution:** A rack on the carriage engages a spring-driven escape wheel whose dogs are shifted by a rock-shaft operated by every key.

**How it works:** All type-key levers and the single-space key strike a bail or rod that oscillates a rock-shaft. The double-space key swings a finger against a pin on the shaft's finger, adding motion. The carriage rack meshes with a scape-wheel on an oscillating bracket, and a coiled flat spring inside the wheel pulls the carriage along. A hinged dog normally holds the wheel, and the rock-shaft motion trips it through a link and lever so the wheel advances one step.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** base frame; type-key lever; single-space key; double-space key; rock-shaft; bail; arm; spring; carriage; rack; scape-wheel; flat spring; oscillating bracket; dog; link; lever

**Numbers:** four rows of type-keys
