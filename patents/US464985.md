## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object is to provide a duplex revolving type-cleaner which cleans the type in a speedy and thorough manner without soiling the hands of the operator.

**How it works (reading):** A duplex revolving cleaner with two brushes on threaded rods and gears, clamped to the frame and turned by a crank to clean the types.
