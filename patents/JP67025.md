## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection; Cylindrical Japanese-language typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a typewriter in which, by moving a single finger key to any character on a character board, a type wheel is turned and at the same time slid along its axis, after which, by pressing the board with the same key, a printing hammer is worked and the type on the type wheel belonging to that character is printed.

**How it works (reading):** One finger key moved freely over a character board turns the type wheel and slides it along its axis to select the type, and pressing the board with the same key fires a hammer to print.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Kanji printing machines need a great many characters and are expensive and slow, usually needing two hands.

**Solution:** One finger key moved freely over a character board turns the type wheel and slides it along its axis to select the type, and pressing the board with the same key fires a hammer to print.

**How it works:** The finger key rides on a connecting bar with upright levers at both ends. Moving the key front to back makes the lever axles slide in slots of channels, swinging arms and a toothed sector that turn the gears at the ends of the type wheel through one full turn over the board's length, selecting the row of types around its circumference. Moving the key left to right slides the carrier holding the type wheel on balls along the shaft, selecting the ring of types along the axis, independently of the turning. A spring plate on each lever resists until the wheel has turned. Pressing the key down compresses a spring, presses the board, and diagonal arms turn a shaft and pressing arm that drive a hammer through the paper onto the type at the print point.
