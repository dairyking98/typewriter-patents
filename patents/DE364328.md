## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a armored typewriter, the entire mechanism of which is encapsulated dust-proof, has been chosen because the invention is particularly important for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage running rail is mounted on two detachable arms that rotate on the machine frame, so the carriage can be swung up at any time to reach the writing for corrections; a separate device lets the carriage be removed for repair.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On visible-writing machines, correcting a line means shifting the carriage far sideways, rotating the platen several line spaces, or lifting the whole carriage frame out.

**Solution:** The carriage running rail is mounted on two detachable arms that rotate on the machine frame, so the carriage can be swung up at any time to reach the writing for corrections; a separate device lets the carriage be removed for repair.
