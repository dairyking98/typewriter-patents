## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing machine serrated platen, bruising paper) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cheque stamping machines

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to printing machines and an object thereof 1s to improve the mounting of the platen in that type of machine in which the platen has a serrated face for engagement within grooves on the I printing surface for the purpose of bruising or puncturing the paper durlng the printing operation.

**How it works (reading):** The platen is mounted to bear in grooves on a rotary type wheel to eliminate play in the wheel, and the feed is simplified with a work gage mounted to the work support.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen play and type-wheel play during printing cause misalignment, and the feed mechanism for variable-length work is complicated.

**Solution:** The platen is mounted to bear in grooves on a rotary type wheel to eliminate play in the wheel, and the feed is simplified with a work gage mounted to the work support.
