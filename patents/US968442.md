## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting inachines and more particularly to the ribbon mechanism of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon vibrator moves the ribbon to one of two set positions, so the operator prints from either the upper black stripe or the lower red stripe of a visible typewriter ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon vibrator could not move the ribbon to different extents to select either stripe of a two-stripe ribbon.

**Solution:** A ribbon vibrator moves the ribbon to one of two set positions, so the operator prints from either the upper black stripe or the lower red stripe of a visible typewriter ribbon.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
