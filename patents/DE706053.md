## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register with type wheel sets)

**How it works (reading):** The eccentric roller is driven by a single central intermediate wheel with a pin-in-recess coupling, and its type areas are unevenly spaced around the circumference so the right type is always in line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type roller for word types must be eccentric and small, so it cannot be driven from its own setting wheel and the normal drive makes its rotation uneven.

**Solution:** The eccentric roller is driven by a single central intermediate wheel with a pin-in-recess coupling, and its type areas are unevenly spaced around the circumference so the right type is always in line.
