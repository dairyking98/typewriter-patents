## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a lifting device for the ribbon carrier of typewriters according to patent 347699 - with one the adjustment of the lifting height mediating angle lever, which is adjustable by means of a button lever and the a part of a flexible locking mechanism carrying handlebar is mounted.

**How it works (reading):** The bell crank and the locking handlebar are placed so the detent parts sit further apart, allowing larger notches and a more secure hold of the angle lever in each position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Notches of the leaf-spring locking are too close together, making the spring hard to make and the lift position less secure (addition to 347699).

**Solution:** The bell crank and the locking handlebar are placed so the detent parts sit further apart, allowing larger notches and a more secure hold of the angle lever in each position.
