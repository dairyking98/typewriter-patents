## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Carriage feeding needed positive drive and quick manual release and return.

**Solution:** A screw along the carriage engages an adjustable nut that can be moved in and out of engagement so the carriage can be shifted by hand while the screw stays in place.

**How it works:** A feed screw runs longitudinally in the frame and is turned in steps by a ratchet and pawl driven from the key levers through links. A nut on the carriage engages the screw to feed the carriage positively. A slide member throws the nut out of mesh for manual shifting, with the screw staying in operative position.
