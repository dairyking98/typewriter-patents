## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Each key should print more than one character without a full shift mechanism of the old kind.

**Solution:** Type bars carry two type heads with faces at an angle, and the supporting ring is moved toward or away from the printing plane to bring the other face into use.

**How it works:** Each typebar has two heads at an obtuse angle. The crescent bar-supporting ring is mounted on slides in guides with adjustable compensating blocks to take up wear, and is moved toward or from the printing plane to change case. The fulcrum distance change selects which head reaches the platen, and more heads would need more positions.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
