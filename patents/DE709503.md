## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Concept sheets must be held beside a typewriter, and booklets must stay on the board.

**Solution:** A pin-fitted board holds perforated concept booklets, which can be turned over at the top.

**How it works:** A board on a base plate with pins fitting holes in a dictation booklet, and side stops for the sheets; a mirror is set into the board.
