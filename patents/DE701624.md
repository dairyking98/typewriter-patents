## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (accounting machine tab rider) · **Subject (patent classes):** Programming-mechanisms for determining the steps to be performed by the computing machine, e.g. when a key or certain keys are depressed in which the operation of the mechanism is determined by the position of the carriage

**How it works (reading):** The main tab is a two-sided carrier with a U-shaped slot holding a replaceable additional tab, which is locked by one screw and has two lengths of approach for subtraction and counter switch-off.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A carriage-jump tab for accounting machines cannot be removed without taking off other parts, and the optional extra tab needs its own fixing.

**Solution:** The main tab is a two-sided carrier with a U-shaped slot holding a replaceable additional tab, which is locked by one screw and has two lengths of approach for subtraction and counter switch-off.
