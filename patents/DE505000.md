This process, which is superior by Simplicity distinguishes itself from all other exclusionary processes, possesses the Disadvantage that if the printing substrate is stretched more, the writing will be distorted occurs, d. 1i. that the individual letters become bold and with strong exclusion the impression of a different font can arise. The printing pad according to the invention avoids this disadvantage in that that it can only be stretched in the area of the spaces between words or images, so that the following stretching extends only to the gaps while the printed remains undistorted. The printing substrate according to the invention is produced in that the pad during printing between two words or other Print mark receives a cut-out or cut-out that weakens the cross-section.
