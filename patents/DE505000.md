## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stretchable printing pad for letterpress look) · **Subject (patent classes):** Forme preparation by embossing, e.g. with a typewriter; Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** The printing pad is cut or weakened across its section between words, so that stretching acts only in the word gaps and the printed characters stay undistorted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letterpress-like print made by stretching a printing substrate line by line is distorted when the substrate is stretched further, so letters turn bold or take on another font.

**Solution:** The printing pad is cut or weakened across its section between words, so that stretching acts only in the word gaps and the printed characters stay undistorted.
