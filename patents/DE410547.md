## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**How it works (reading):** Line-switch pawl is itself a slide, raised or lowered by a lifting piece to engage either one or more ratchet teeth reliably.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pawl pivoting on a slide gave uncertain line-feed steps because it loosened.

**Solution:** Line-switch pawl is itself a slide, raised or lowered by a lifting piece to engage either one or more ratchet teeth reliably.
