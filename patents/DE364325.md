## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Replace the usual many-lever keyboard with a very simple typewriter having only two strike levers.

**Solution:** A springy type comb carrying several types per tine is positioned by hand in two axes under a pressure lever; one stroke of the single print lever presses the tine, spaces the carriage one tooth and advances the ribbon.

**How it works:** A slide carrying a comb of resilient tines, each tine bearing several types one behind another, rides on a laterally movable carriage. A shaft with a handle moves the slide forward and back, and the carriage moves sideways, so the wanted type is brought over the paper roller. Notches in the slide are sprung into by bolts to hold the position, and a pointer runs over a scale laid out like the comb to show which type is set. Striking the print lever swings a pressure lever onto the tine, pressing the type to the paper, and also draws back and releases a pawl so the roller carriage advances one tooth pitch on its rack. The same stroke lifts a bolt that turns a toothed disc on a ribbon reel through a bracket, linkage and angle lever, winding the ribbon. A second identical lever spaces only (word spaces, block letters).
