## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a number of bills are run with carbon paper, sheets must register and the paper roller must not turn an extra time.

**Solution:** A perforated disc on the paper-roll frame, with fixed and adjustable stops and a detachable crank coupled to the roller axle, limits roller rotation to one sheet height.
