## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Case-shifting by raising the type-bar segment needs to be simple and to lock in the shifted position when desired.

**Solution:** A balanced vertically moving type-bar segment, shifted by links and a rock-shaft, with adjustable stops and a latch to lock it shifted.

**How it works:** The segment carrying the type-bars slides on ball guideways at each side. Links from the segment connect to crank arms on a rock-shaft mounted on adjustable cone bearings. A coiled spring on the shaft counterbalances the weight of the segment. Paired set-screws against arms set the exact upper and lower case positions, and a latch holds the shift. Shown on a front-strike machine.

**Features:** [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:rotary](../features/shift-rotary.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** type-bar segment; guideway; anti-friction ball; link; crank arm; rock-shaft; cone bearing screw; coiled counterbalance spring; set-screw; latch
