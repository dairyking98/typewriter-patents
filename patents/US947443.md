## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stops or stop-racks

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabula-tor devices for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Several tabulator bars each carry their own stops and are swung into operative position one at a time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting many tabulator stops on one bar is slow for fixed column work.

**Solution:** Several tabulator bars each carry their own stops and are swung into operative position one at a time.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
