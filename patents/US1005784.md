## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The adjustable two-pawl escapement needs a control mechanism that works in both regular and speed settings.

**Solution:** A rotatable control shaft with flat and round surfaces bears on stop pins to set rocker travel for regular or speed escapement, with a pointer showing the setting.

**How it works:** A rocker carries two pawls alternately engaging an escapement wheel that drives the carriage pinion through the shaft. A control shaft with a thumb nut and pointer is turned so a round or flat surface faces spring-loaded stop pins on the hanger. This changes which pawl the rocker holds engaged at rest, giving regular or speed escapement while rocker travel stays the same. A tension-regulated restoring spring returns the rocker after the operating arm moves down.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** hanger; escapement shaft; escapement wheel; rocker; pawl; control shaft; thumb nut; pointer; stop pin; restoring spring; operating arm
