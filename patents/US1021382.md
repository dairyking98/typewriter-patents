## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A way to use one of two different widthwise ribbon fields (e.g. black and red) at will, simply.

**Solution:** The ribbon is turned through a guide or vibrator so opposite edge portions come next to the print point, and the guide is shifted along the printing line to pick which field is used.

**How it works:** The ribbon, divided lengthwise into two fields such as black and red or copying and record, is folded and turned through the vibrator as it feeds between spools. This brings the field on either side of the guide into near alignment at the print point. Shifting the guide or vibrator longitudinally of the platen determines which field prints. Several sheet-metal guide variants are shown, on a Monarch machine.

**Features:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon guide; vibrator; ribbon spool; platen

**Numbers:** two ribbon fields
