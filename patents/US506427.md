## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens with balancing means

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of our invention is to produce an attachment by which the printing-cylinder of a typewriter may be securely held in any position of rotation desired, and entirely independent of the ordinary pawl and ratchet mechanism of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** Fit the penetrable disk with metal rings having holes, and give the locking disk spring-actuated pins that enter those holes so the felt carries no strain.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** In the inventors' earlier attachment (US499723) operators tried to turn the cylinder without releasing the locking disk, and the pins tore the felt penetrable disk, which then needed replacing.

**Solution:** Fit the penetrable disk with metal rings having holes, and give the locking disk spring-actuated pins that enter those holes so the felt carries no strain.

**How it works:** A yoke on a sleeve slides along a tubular carriage rod and carries a locking disk with a ring of pins facing the cylinder end. A thin plate on the cylinder end holds a felt penetrable disk clamped by screw-threaded outer and inner rings. The pins pierce the felt to hold the cylinder at any rotation; the new metal bands have recesses that receive spring-loaded pins so an attempt to turn the cylinder loads the metal, not the felt.
