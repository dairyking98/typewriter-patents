## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in that class of type-writin g machines in which the paper-carriage is moved varying distances according with the impressions of types of varying widths for the purpose of permitting the use of types of such proportions as are usually employed in printing or the use of types bearing different numbers of letters.

**How it works (reading):** A high-pitch feed screw driven by key levers through a ratchet and pawl, engaged to the carriage by a ratcheted worm-wheel nut that freewheels on carriage return.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Variable-width carriage feed needs a way to return the carriage without turning the feed screw back, and to stop the feed positively after exactly the type width.

**Solution:** A high-pitch feed screw driven by key levers through a ratchet and pawl, engaged to the carriage by a ratcheted worm-wheel nut that freewheels on carriage return.

**How it works:** The carriage spring pulls the carriage, whose arm carries a toothed wheel fitted to the screw threads and rigidly joined to a ratchet wheel with a pawl. Pulling the carriage forward therefore must turn the screw, while on hand return the ratchet slips so the screw does not rotate backwards. Key levers actuate the screw through a ratchet and pawl, which arrests and locks the screw until a key is struck again. Each key also interposes its own stop through a rock-shaft so it cannot turn the screw further than its type requires.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** feed screw; worm-wheel nut; ratchet wheel; pawl; carriage arm; key lever; rock-shaft; stop mechanism; carriage

**Numbers:** screw with ten threads, one-inch pitch
