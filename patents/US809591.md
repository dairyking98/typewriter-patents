## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (blank stamping machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines for stamping metallic blanks, and more especially to i that class of machines which employ interchangeable matrices in the shape of characterdies and which are impressed into the material by force.

**How it works (reading):** An externally controlled revolving matrix table carries character dies that are located individually against the blank; intermittent advance of the blank brings it to the point of impression, where the die is forced into the material.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Blank stamping machines need the character to be selected from outside the casing and the blank fed and indexed automatically to each impression position.

**Solution:** An externally controlled revolving matrix table carries character dies that are located individually against the blank; intermittent advance of the blank brings it to the point of impression, where the die is forced into the material.
