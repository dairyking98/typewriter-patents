## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines with adjustable linkage lacked a way to adjust rest positions of the intermediate linkage and type carriers independently of the strike setting.

**Solution:** Limit the return of each type carrier and linkage with stops on a common stop rail, while each carrier's bearing parts are adjustable.

**How it works:** Each type carrier runs on link pairs and is driven by a toggle lever plus a swing weight on an intermediate member, set moving by a key-driven curved drive member that is stopped before the print point. Inertia carries the linkage into the printing position. A bearing part per type carrier with adjusters sets stroke and force, and a separate adjustment sets the rest position against the common stop rail so types align side by side and vibration cannot throw parts out of rest.
