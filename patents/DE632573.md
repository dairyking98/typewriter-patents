> Description Translated from German Typewriter with a faint impression. The invention relates on a typewriter with a faint impression of the types, their type carriers and connected to these intermediate rods of the keys by means of drive members are only driven during the first part of the stop movement and in the Imprint position of the action of the keys are withdrawn. In typewriters of this type there is -between those of the key moving drive links and the intermediate linkage connected to the type carrier no permanent connection. When stopping the button and drive links through a stop, the intermediate linkage is released and continues to move until the Types come to the imprint. For exact readjustment of the position of the wood as well as the strength of the type stop of each individual type carrier, actuators are provided here, through which the bearing points of the intermediate rod are adjustable.
