## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (multiplying machine with gelatin ribbon) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for indirectly duplicating from hectographic originals by means of hectographic intermediaries or transfer surfaces, i.e. "dry duplicators" with flat supports over which gelatin-paper is stretched

**How it works (reading):** A motor-driven slide reciprocates under a yoke, and a releasable coupling disengages at the end of the outward stroke, switching the motor on and off through the rest and working positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicating invoices on a gelatin-layer machine needs a slide that moves in a sequence and cuts the motor off when the slide is at rest, and the old slide did not do this automatically.

**Solution:** A motor-driven slide reciprocates under a yoke, and a releasable coupling disengages at the end of the outward stroke, switching the motor on and off through the rest and working positions.
