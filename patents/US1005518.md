**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Separating articles from piles using friction forces between articles and separator; Transporting of cards between stations with continuous movement

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to envelop feeders.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Envelopes must be fed to the platen and removed after printing by hand, which is slow.

**Solution:** An attachment for typewriters feeds envelopes from a stack to the platen, removes the printed envelope and inserts the next one, driven by the platen movement.
