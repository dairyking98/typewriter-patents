## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles characterised by being applied to printers having transversely- moving carriages and coupled to the carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to such devices for writing out invoice forms supplied in tape form and the like by means of typewriters, which allow simultaneous copying of the separate Invoice items on separate accounting sheets that can be moved forward by line spacing enable, while the form tape is pushed forward by a whole length of form must become.

**How it works (reading):** The form feed device rides on its own straight guide, driven by a spring drum and pulling belt linked to the carriage movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Form feed crank on the typewriter carriage adds weight and stress to the carriage guides.

**Solution:** The form feed device rides on its own straight guide, driven by a spring drum and pulling belt linked to the carriage movement.
