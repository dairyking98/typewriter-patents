## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements on the inventor's key-driven adding machine so that it records results by a hammer blow rather than by pressure.

**Solution:** A recording adding machine in which the keys directly power hammer printing of the figures, with ribbon and paper feeds driven from the unit-row keys.

**How it works:** Depressing the number keys moves recording heads which are stopped and held at the proper position; a hammer then strikes to print the figures. Intermediate devices transmit key power to the hammer. A ribbon and paper feed shaft is moved when a unit-row key is operated, with a reversing arm for the ribbon wheels. Additional keys print characters or abbreviations beside the figures, and a device prints a line above or below a column without operating the adding mechanism.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** key; recording head; hammer; ribbon wheel; paper feed roller; numeral wheel; paper guide

**Numbers:** 12 sheets of drawings

**Improves on:**

- US366945: hammer blow replaces pressure printing; keys power the record directly
- US371496: as above
