## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typebar normally carries one fixed set of characters; the inventor wants one typebar to offer several characters and to allow quick replacement and repair of the type.

**Solution:** A typebar made of two plate bars holds a rotatable square type carrier with a different type face on each of four sides, locked in position by pins and a sliding screw clamp.

**How it works:** The typebar consists of two plate bars laid one on the other with registering slots. A square-shaped rotary type carrier sits in bearings between them on a shaft portion, with a type on each of its faces. Eight locking pins (four per side) on the carrier engage openings in extensions of the plate bars. To change the character, the operator unscrews a flanged screw clamp sliding in the slots, which frees the pins, turns the carrier to bring another face to the ribbon and platen, and tightens the clamp again to lock it. The whole carrier can also be lifted out and replaced by another set. The text starts mid-description, so the opening is missing.
