## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to bi-clirome i'ibbon mechanism and makes provision for printing from ditferent zones of a ribbon, as, for instance, black and red, when a printing operation by depression of a so-called silent ykey is effected.

**How it works (reading):** Ordinary printing keys stay connected to the universal bar, silent or accent keys operate the ribbon vibrator transversely without actuating the escapement, and the ribbon mechanism is adjusted to print from the upper or lower zone.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing from different ribbon zones, such as black and red, was needed when a silent or accent key is used.

**Solution:** Ordinary printing keys stay connected to the universal bar, silent or accent keys operate the ribbon vibrator transversely without actuating the escapement, and the ribbon mechanism is adjusted to print from the upper or lower zone.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
