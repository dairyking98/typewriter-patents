## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case shift mechanism for a Yost typebar machine needed a shift lock with adjustable engagement.

**Solution:** Shift key levers on a rock shaft raise the platen frame on parallel links, with a locking shift key pivoted on one lever hooking an adjustable fixed engaging member.

**How it works:** The platen frame hangs on parallel links from a truck running on ball-bearing rails and rests on a shift rail lifted by links from two shift key levers joined by a rock shaft. Counterbalancing springs offset the platen weight and weight returns it to lower case. A lock key pivoted off-centre on the left shift lever has a hook, which, when depressed, swings under a fulcrumed engaging plate on the frame, adjusted with two set screws, and holds the platen up.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** shift key lever; rock shaft; shift rail; parallel link; platen frame; counterbalance spring; lock key; hook; engaging member; set screw; ball rail
