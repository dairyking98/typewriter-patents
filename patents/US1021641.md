## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a framework or holder adapted to receive and support various escapement mechanisms utilized ona typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** The escapement parts are mounted on a framework that comes out of the machine as one unit, and the fixing points double as reference points for alignment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement mechanisms are hard to adjust or repair because each part must be aligned on the machine.

**Solution:** The escapement parts are mounted on a framework that comes out of the machine as one unit, and the fixing points double as reference points for alignment.
