## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Special characters such as accents must be printed without any letter-space feed, using dead keys, while still operating the ribbon throw.

**Solution:** An auxiliary dead key lever moves the accent types and operates the ribbon-throw mechanism through a connecting lever and rod without actuating the letter-spacing mechanism.

**How it works:** On an Oliver machine, key levers connect by links to U-shaped typebars, and the paper carriage rests on a shift frame that moves to bring the printing point beneath one of several types on each bar. An auxiliary key lever moves the special-character type without touching the escapement. A lever and connecting rod link that auxiliary key to the ribbon-throw mechanism so the ribbon still lifts for the stroke.
