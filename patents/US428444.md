## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a device for holding rigidly in position and preventing the movement of the levers and paper-carriage of a type-writin g machine, and which may be readily and quickly placed upon or attached to and detached from the machine;

**How it works (reading):** A bar passes under the bank of key-levers and is carried by rods or a frame to a clamp that locks onto the paper-carriage; the rod length is adjustable for different machine heights.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The key-levers and paper-carriage must be held rigidly so they cannot move, and the holder must be quickly fitted and removed.

**Solution:** A bar passes under the bank of key-levers and is carried by rods or a frame to a clamp that locks onto the paper-carriage; the rod length is adjustable for different machine heights.

**How it works:** Bar under the key-levers, rigidly rod-connected to a carriage clamp that is detachably locked.
