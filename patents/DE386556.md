## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Setting the type magazine levers in their position needs better adjustment and locking so the magazines can be held in height while the type sticks are moved.

**Solution:** A displaceable rod with extensions acts on angle rails and hammers, and a parallel-sliding rail with toothing holds the set levers in their set position while the other parts return.
