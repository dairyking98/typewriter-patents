## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German A printing apparatus for cash registers with a single type gear set for printing of line items, individual, intermediate and final sums The invention relates to - a cash register, the printing apparatus prints by means of a single type of gear set and a plurality of oppositely acting pressure lever optional items as well as single, Zwischenuni totals. According to the invention, this optional pressure is achieved in the simplest way in that the pressure hammers interacting with one side of the printing wheel are combined to form a set, or it is driven by a flexible intermediate member, swivel frame or the like. By engaging the flexible intermediate means between the drive member and print hammer it is possible. That the inevitable movement of the swivel frame can continue while the hammer itself is switched off. Each individual hammer of the set can also be flexibly connected to the swivel frame in the same way. This device is of particular advantage for cash registers and similar machines which are to serve different purposes.
