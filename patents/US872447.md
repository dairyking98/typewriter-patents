## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a sight typewriter the ribbon must be moved over the printing place at each stroke and then returned so the printed character is visible, and the travel needs to vary with colour choice and platen shift.

**Solution:** A lever from the universal bar advances a spring-returned ribbon guide at each stroke, and a flexible shaft with a cam on it, set to red, black or plain, changes how far the guide travels.

**How it works:** A universal bar link rocks a lever on a supporting arm. A ribbon guide slides on a transverse arm and is advanced by a second lever, with a spring pulling it back. A flexible shaft turned through bevel gears by an indicator arm, with three positions marked red, black and plain, carries a cam acting on another lever that sets the rest position of the first. In one position the guide advances only a minimum distance and the ribbon never reaches the print place, for stencil or matrix work. A spring clip on the shaft detents against flats on a collar for the three positions. The same lever can also be tied to the platen shift key so upper and lower case choose different ribbon positions.
