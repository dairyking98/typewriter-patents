## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapements are noisy.

**Solution:** A quieter escapement where the carriage spring tension helps restore the parts and the carriage can be moved back with the rack teeth snapping over one dog.

**How it works:** The carriage on grooved rails with rollers is drawn by a spring drum; a pivoted feed rack meshes with a pinion on a shaft carrying the escapement wheel fixed by a set screw. Feed dogs on a bracket under the top plate are moved by a lever and link pulled down by the universal bar and space key. Detail of noise reduction is beyond the shown text.

**Features:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; feed dog; feed rack; pinion; lever; link; universal bar; spring drum
