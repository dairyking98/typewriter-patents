## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide means whereby the time of cut-ofi on return from each line is regulated according to the extent of letter feed or forward movement of the carriage in the writing of the line from which the return is to be made;

**How it works (reading):** The power cut-off on high-speed return is timed by the carriage's earlier forward movement, and the same key can line space without return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return cut-off must match the momentum of the carriage, so shocks at the stops are avoided.

**Solution:** The power cut-off on high-speed return is timed by the carriage's earlier forward movement, and the same key can line space without return.
