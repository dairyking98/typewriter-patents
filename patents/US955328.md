## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especial-ly to tally strip devices for typewriting machines and its main object is to provide'improved devices of the kind specified.

**How it works (reading):** Tally feed rolls and a roll bracket on the paper carriage feed a tally strip beside the work.
