## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The key lever to type bar connection is fixed and cannot be removed easily.

**Solution:** A detachable interlocking pair of members on the type bar and key lever, with a bell-crank and pitman, makes the connection easy to remove.
