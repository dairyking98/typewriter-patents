## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools need automatic reversal of feed direction without attention.

**Solution:** A shifting plate carrying a driving pinion meshes with either spool, latched until the driven spool fills and a ribbon-following brake lever releases it.

**How it works:** Each spool has gear teeth on its head. A pinion shaft on a shifting plate is turned by a pawl on the oscillating ribbon carrier acting on a ratchet. A lever with a hook latch locks the plate with the pinion meshed to one spool. A spring-loaded brake lever rides the ribbon on the spool and, when the spool is full, trips the latch so the plate shifts the pinion to the other spool. Hand release allows reversal at any time.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** spool; gear teeth; driving pinion; shifting plate; ratchet; pawl; latch lever; brake lever; spring
