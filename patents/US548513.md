## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** characterised by operational sensory functions, e.g. sound feedback

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of our invention is to make economically, for type-writing and similar keyoperated machines, a yielding finger key or cap, which will be durable, but soft and agreeable to the touch, and yet not too elastic to interfere with the proper action of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** A felt key cap with the letter sheet, made of woven or moulded letters, cemented to a felt sheet and cut to shape.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber key caps are harsh, pneumatic caps are not durable and too elastic, and spring caps get out of order.

**Solution:** A felt key cap with the letter sheet, made of woven or moulded letters, cemented to a felt sheet and cut to shape.
