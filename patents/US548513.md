To 0.1% whom it may concern: I Be it known that we, DANIEL BRIGGS and SMITH B. RYND, citizens of the United States, and residents of Brooklyn, Kings county, New York, have invented an Improved Key or Cap for Type-Writers, 6270., of which the following is a specification. The object of our invention is to make economically, for type-writing and similar keyoperated machines, a yielding finger key or cap, which will be durable, but soft and agreeable to the touch, and yet not too elastic to interfere with the proper action of the machine. This object we attain in the manner which we will now describe. In the accompanying drawings, Figure 1 is a perspective View illustrating the manner of making the key or cap.
