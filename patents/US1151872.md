## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work forms (telephone bills) must be placed and positioned by hand on the platen, which wastes time when each form gets little writing.

**Solution:** A chute or paper guide sits behind the platen, curves around it to the printing line, and is spaced from it by rings so a form slides in between guide and platen; pressure rolls held off the platen are released while the form is inserted.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** Underwood platen; rings on platen spacing guide; pressure rolls
