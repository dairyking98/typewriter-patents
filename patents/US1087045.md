## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Applications of alarms, e.g. responsive to approach of end of line responsive to breakage or exhaustion of paper or approach of bottom of paper

**How it works (reading):** An indicator is driven from the platen rotation via a starting-position member, and gives signals or machine locks at predetermined line positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operator cannot easily tell how far the sheet has been written or when it reaches set line positions.

**Solution:** An indicator is driven from the platen rotation via a starting-position member, and gives signals or machine locks at predetermined line positions.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
