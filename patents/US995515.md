## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In prior escapements the carriage steps in two movements per letter space, starting from rest twice, which is slow and depends on how the key is struck.

**Solution:** An escapement that lets the carriage make the full letter-space step in one continuous automatic motion once the key reaches let-off.

**How it works:** After the key reaches the let-off point the rest of the escapement operation is automatic and independent of key motion, so the carriage moves from one position to the next without stopping midway. The same action holds for a hard or slow stroke. It can also be changed between ordinary and reverse escapement. The OCR opening gives the objects, not the parts.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** escapement; carriage; key; driving spring

**Numbers:** drop example 1/100 in. of a 10/100 in. step
