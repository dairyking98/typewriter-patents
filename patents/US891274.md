## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of our invention is to provide a means which, upon the pushing of a shift key, will cause t e carriage of a typewriting machineto be rapidly conveyed to the starting position and to at the same time turn the roller so that the paper will be in proper tlon to receive the type for writing anew 'ne.

**How it works (reading):** An attachment in which a shift key rapidly returns the carriage and turns the platen, driven by belt, drum and wheel from one power source.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manual carriage return and platen turning after each line are slow, and the attachment must fit standard machines.

**Solution:** An attachment in which a shift key rapidly returns the carriage and turns the platen, driven by belt, drum and wheel from one power source.
