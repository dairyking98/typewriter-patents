## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter device for reversing the direction of ribbon travel. The invention relates to a reversing device for the ribbon movement of typewriters of the kind in which switching is achieved by changing the diameter of the ribbon winding is brought about. The ribbon reels are alternately arranged in a known manner rotated by bevel gears from a cross shaft. The to this change required longitudinal displacement of the shaft is carried out by means of a preferably in their center attacking two-armed lever, which through on the ribbon winding overlying feeler lever is controlled. According to the invention are between the sensing levers and the control lever for the drive shaft of the ribbon reel rods with elongated Arranged eyelets, one or the other of which only moves the lever when the predetermined winding diameter is reached. Here a spring is tensioned, which helps when moving in the other direction at the given time. By this training is a simplification of the reverse gear achieved. .
