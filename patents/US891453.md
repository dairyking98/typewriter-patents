## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage impelling force from one spring is too weak for the full travel of the carriage.

**Solution:** Several spring drums are coupled in series so the stretch adds up across the springs, with tension adjusters at the rear.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
