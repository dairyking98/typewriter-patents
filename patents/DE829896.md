## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to writing office machines with through The slots in the paper guide 1) pass through on the underside of the platen Paper guide rollers that hold the sheets of paper to be labeled on the roller and when they are rotated, cause the paper sheets to be fed along by the roller.

**How it works (reading):** Front and rear pressure rollers are carried on swivelling arms on the carriage frame, so each roller's travel can be set on its own.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressure rollers on the platen cannot be set independently, since the front and rear roller paths are interdependent.

**Solution:** Front and rear pressure rollers are carried on swivelling arms on the carriage frame, so each roller's travel can be set on its own.
