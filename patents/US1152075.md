## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be held uncoupled from the line-spacing wheel for a long period without a constant pressure on the uncoupling bolt.

**Solution:** Clutch rollers spring-pressed in cages on a carrier body grip the line-spacing wheel; wedge pins lock them, and a bolt can be held in uncoupled position.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
