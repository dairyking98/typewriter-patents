## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a typewriter and particularly to a construction comprising margin gage devices and other cooperating parts.

**How it works (reading):** The margin stop mechanism can move independently in successive steps, first removing one stop and bringing a secondary stop into engagement with the contact, then shifting the stops longitudinally to operate the line lock, with a bar lock, operating keys and a bell ringer.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A margin stop needs to be cleared and a secondary stop thrown into the path of the contact, and then the stops moved bodily to operate a line lock, which a single stop cannot do.

**Solution:** The margin stop mechanism can move independently in successive steps, first removing one stop and bringing a secondary stop into engagement with the contact, then shifting the stops longitudinally to operate the line lock, with a bar lock, operating keys and a bell ringer.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md)

**Parts:** margin stop; secondary stop; shifting cradle; bar lock; operating keys; contact; bell ringer; line lock; interchangeable carriage
