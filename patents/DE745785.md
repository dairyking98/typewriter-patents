## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (full keypad calculating machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a calculating or accounting machine with a full keypad with subdivisible printing unit and with a Saldierwerlc and several on a common Carrier arranged memory counters, which depend on the movement of the paper cart are successively brought into working position in which different Types of work set automatically by control elements provided on the paper trolley will.

**How it works (reading):** Keypad split into two fields; keys of one field act on rods controlled by the paper carriage, so different work types can be switched in each line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Booking machines with a full keypad and subdivided printing unit can store only as many memory sums as counters; a second line needs its own narrower paper carriage.

**Solution:** Keypad split into two fields; keys of one field act on rods controlled by the paper carriage, so different work types can be switched in each line.
