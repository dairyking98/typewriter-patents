## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements 1n typewriting' machines, and has referencey more especially to theA construction of the frame or base of the machine and means for supporting or sustaining the several main operative parts of the machine on such frame or base.

**How it works (reading):** A base frame with an elevated top wall and side walls carries type bar supporting standards and links, with the parts above the base plate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The frame of an Oliver-type machine must support type bars and operative parts, and the existing frame arrangement is not well suited to this.

**Solution:** A base frame with an elevated top wall and side walls carries type bar supporting standards and links, with the parts above the base plate.
