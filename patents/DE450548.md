## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Sound and shock absorbing base for office machines. The known Foot bases made of felt, rubber, feather etc. only have a certain hole size for the machine feet. Therefore, at least two sizes must always be kept in stock will. This disadvantage is eliminated according to the invention in that the Pedestal that widens conically on both its top and bottom or has narrowing openings for receiving the machine base, both of which Openings of a pedestal have different clearances. In the drawing, the subject matter of the invention is shown, for example. - Fig. I shows the base of the foot in a central section, Fig. 2 in the view of above. The base made of elastic rubber has an opening A for large machine feet and an opening B for small machine feet. Both openings A and B have a conical widening of about 2 to 3 mm towards the middle, which makes it possible to introduce machine feet that are slightly larger than the normal size, since the outer edge of the openings is flexible.
