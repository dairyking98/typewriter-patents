## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fractional line spacing needs the platen and line-space wheel locked together, but existing locks let looseness and lost motion develop with wear.

**Solution:** Several independently operated locking dogs on the line-space wheel, each engaging a toothed ring and locking in one direction only, with oppositely acting dogs holding the parts against movement either way.
