## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a lifting-disc power typewriter, a key held down too long lets the disc go round again and strike the type lever several times.

**Solution:** Give each catch lever a second catch nose above the first, so a held key stops the disc after one stroke.

**How it works:** Pressing a key pivots the catch lever off a nose on the lifting disc, and a pressure lever gives the disc an initial turn into contact with the continuously rotating drive roller. The eccentric disc swings a drive lever and strikes the type lever. The second, upper catch nose lies in the path of the disc nose while the key stays down, so the disc is caught after its half turn instead of repeating. A separate disc unit with a full-turn release drives spacing.
