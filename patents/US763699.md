## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for actuating the universal bar which controls the carriage feeding mechanism of writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable clip on each key-lever sets the stroke it gives the universal bar, so the dog-rocker vibrates uniformly for every key.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keys give non-uniform strokes to the universal bar, so carriage feed dogs cannot be let off precisely at high speed.

**Solution:** An adjustable clip on each key-lever sets the stroke it gives the universal bar, so the dog-rocker vibrates uniformly for every key.
