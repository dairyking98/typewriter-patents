## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants a simple, cheap system of type-bars and levers that can strike hard enough to make deep matrices in plastic material.

**Solution:** Two-piece hinged type-bars worked by toggle levers are driven forcibly by light finger pressure onto a matrix platen, which an escapement advances.

**How it works:** A cylindrical casing holds a ring of two-section type-bars hinged at their top, whose lower parts rest in notches in a ring above the platen. A funnel cup guides them to the impression surface. Each is driven by a pair of toggle levers worked by a key, giving a strong blow. Springs at the joints return the lower parts. Escapement devices with spring pawls advance the matrix platen automatically, and thumb screws set bar depth.
