## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper roll supply on a typewriter needs an automatic rewind after printing and speed control of the rewinding.

**Solution:** A detachable frame on the carriage holds a rotary roll carrier; a governor limits its rewinding speed and stops overthrow at the end of travel.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
