## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing subscripts and superscripts normally needs hand repositioning of the paper.

**Solution:** A notched-slot device on the shift bar that gives the shift a half-way position, so characters print above or below the line.

**How it works:** The device rests on or connects to the shift bar or shift, held by shouldered screws. A notched slot travels on an upper screw or pin, and the upper part is heavier so it falls against the screw as the shift is lowered. The notch holds a special intermediate shift position. In that position the carriage and keyboard sit partway so subscripts and superscripts type directly.
