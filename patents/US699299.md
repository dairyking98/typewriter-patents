## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage and line-spacing by hand is slow.

**Solution:** A spring-drum return device clutched by a button, which winds a cord to pull the carriage back and releases only enough spring tension for one return.

**How it works:** A drum on a shaft carries a cord attached to the carriage that is wound to draw it back. Cooperating clutch-drums on the shaft are engaged by a yoke on a non-rotating disk, moved by a button through levers like those of the type-lever shafts. A spring drum with a coiled spring stronger than the carriage advance spring drives the clutch through gearing and can be locked by a ratchet and pawl. Pressing the button engages the clutch and withdraws lugs from the apertures; the spring unwinds and winds the cord. Releasing it disengages the clutch and relocks the lugs so only the needed tension is used. A tripping device operates the line-space mechanism.
