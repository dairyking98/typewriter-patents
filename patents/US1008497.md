## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper frame must move with the platen, and a heavy carriage with carriage-based linkage is needed for the blow and paper movement.

**Solution:** Racks on the paper-holding frame mesh with gears on the platen shaft, so turning the platen raises or lowers the frame in the auxiliary carriage frame. The platen is spring-mounted in a movable bearing to take the blow of the type.

**How it works:** Platen rotation drives the racks through the gears, moving the paper frame vertically; a spring dog holds the platen at each setting, and a spacing lever with a pivot dog engages a ratchet on the platen shaft.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [IMP:anvil-backing](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)

**Parts:** platen; gear wheels on platen shaft; racks on paper frame; spring-pressed platen bearing; spacing lever; pivot dog; ratchet wheel; spring dog
