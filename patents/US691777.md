## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-lever to type-lever connections give uneven touch and are hard to assemble or take apart, and tension is hard to change.

**Solution:** Connections between the key-levers and the type-levers are improved so the touch stays uniform, with easy assembly and simple means to change tension or release the keys.

**How it works:** Parallel key-levers drive bell-crank levers by links, and the bell-cranks drive type-levers in a curved plane; a tension lever changes the spring tension.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** key-levers; bell-crank levers; links; type-levers; key-lever shaft; tension lever
