## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Platen surface indents or corrugates under type impact, and the platen cannot be adjusted circumferentially independently of the line-space ratchet.

**Solution:** An improved platen construction with a thumb-wheel that adjusts the platen and paper circumferentially independently of the line-space wheel, plus sound-deadening means.

**How it works:** A thumb-wheel rotates the platen shaft through a gear train disconnected from the line-space wheel, so the paper moves in either direction, and sound-deadening parts reduce noise.
