## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tape or page guiding or feeding devices

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and, among its several objects, is particularly designed to effect folding of the machine into compact form when not in use without removing the carriage or dismantling any of the parts of the machine.

**How it works (reading):** The machine folds into compact form without removing the carriage, paper feed is actuated by carriage return through a step-by-step advance, a spring stores energy to return the carriage, and the type is inked without a ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The machine cannot be folded compactly without removing the carriage, paper feed is not automatic, and an inking ribbon is needed.

**Solution:** The machine folds into compact form without removing the carriage, paper feed is actuated by carriage return through a step-by-step advance, a spring stores energy to return the carriage, and the type is inked without a ribbon.
