## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen frame and its supports in a shifting-platen typewriter were costly to make and hard to remove for cleaning.

**Solution:** A platen frame rides on a front shift-rail and a rear slide-rail and can be lifted off the machine as one unit.

**How it works:** The carriage runs endwise on a long slide-rail at the rear. A shift-rail at the front is carried on arms of pivoted levers joined by rods to a shift key-lever, with a spring return, so it moves forward and back. The rectangular platen frame carries the platen journals. Its front trough section rests on the shift-rail through flanged friction rollers, free to slide along it but moved with it when the rail shifts. Bifurcated lugs at the rear embrace the slide-rail and engage lugs on the carriage, so the frame travels with the carriage. To remove it, the operator swings up the front, frees it from the shift-rail and draws it forward off the rear rail and carriage.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen frame; shift-rail; slide-rail; carriage; friction roller; bifurcated lug; pivoted lever; connecting rod; shift key-lever; return spring

**Improves on:**

- earlier shifting platen frames: cheaper construction, and the frame lifts out bodily for cleaning
