## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand-feeding envelopes one at a time into a typewriter and advancing each for the address lines is slow and laborious.

**Solution:** A receptacle holds a stack of envelopes; a rod pressed by hand feeds each one into writing position, and a means advances the envelope one space at a time for successive lines.
