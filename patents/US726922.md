## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book-rests on a book-typewriter table need to move independently and self-adjust to the thickness of a book.

**Solution:** Two book-rests that move independently, self-adjust when loose to the book thickness, and are then fixed for writing.
