## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates generally stated to typewriting machines and more particularly to that, class known as stencil-making -machines, although various features of my invention are applicable to typewriting machines specifically.

**How it works (reading):** The point at which the carriage is arrested on its return is varied automatically under control of the line spacing mechanism.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil-making typewriters need a carriage return arrest that changes automatically with line spacing, and a more efficient front-strike flat platen machine is wanted.

**Solution:** The point at which the carriage is arrested on its return is varied automatically under control of the line spacing mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
