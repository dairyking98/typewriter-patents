## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-colour ribbons need a way to bring either colour band to the printing point, and a way to stop ribbon vibration for stencil cutting.

**Solution:** A yoke with two wrists at different heights lets either engage the universal-bar actuator, with a mid position that engages neither.

**How it works:** The ribbon-vibrating lever is forked and the actuator on the universal-bar frame engages one of two pins (wrists) on a yoke. Sliding the yoke axially chooses the wrist, changing the lever's throw and so which colour band covers the printing point. A front finger-piece turns a rock-shaft and transverse shaft to slide the yoke. In the mid position neither wrist engages, so the ribbon stays still and types strike off the ribbon for stencils.
