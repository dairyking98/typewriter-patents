> Description Translated from German Paper carriage switching device for typewriters o. The like. There are Switching devices for paper trolleys of typewriters o. The like. Known by a spring or similar move and after a key is pressed a Carry out a variable switching step according to the width of the letter. In the main patent two are to achieve a perfectly accurate switching step Clamps are provided which act on a rail connected to the paper trolley. One of these clamps is struck when a key is pressed by one of the width of the struck Corresponding step away from a solid stop and then on clamped to the rail attached to the carriage, while the other is clamped to the machine frame firmly connected clamp is released at this moment, so that the paper trolley move a step equal to the width of the letter you have posted can until the carrier of the 'first clamp hits the fixed stop again. The invention relates to an embodiment of the main patent and is characterized in that both clamps through a linkage when a key is pressed. a common Actuating head to be released or fixed, in which the ends of two pivot levers engage, which act with their other ends on the clamping members of the clamps.…
