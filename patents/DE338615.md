## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon moves between a moveable dye wick fed from a container and an elastic corrugated counter-plate that presses fresh dye onto it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon colour fades unevenly because the felt strip contaminates and only one spot is used.

**Solution:** The ribbon moves between a moveable dye wick fed from a container and an elastic corrugated counter-plate that presses fresh dye onto it.
