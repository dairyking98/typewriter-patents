## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more espec ally to tabulating mechanism for typewritingmachines and its object generally stated is to provide improved devices of the class specitied.

**How it works (reading):** The same key levers used for denominational tabulating also select columns, with column stops on a stop bar on the carriage positioned to match the tabular columns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Denominational tabulating and column selecting needed improved, simplified mechanism that works with a large number of columns.

**Solution:** The same key levers used for denominational tabulating also select columns, with column stops on a stop bar on the carriage positioned to match the tabular columns.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** column stops; stop bar; key levers; denominational stops
