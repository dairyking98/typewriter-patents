## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabulators of type-writing machines, and is in the nature of an im rovement upon the devices disclosed.

**How it works (reading):** Column-stop keys at the front act through levers and thrust rods on tumblers that set each stop on a rack, so the stops are set independently while the tumblers stay stationary.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier column-stop tabulator placed its stop bar at the back, and the inventor wants the adjusting keys at the front with a retrofit to existing machines.

**Solution:** Column-stop keys at the front act through levers and thrust rods on tumblers that set each stop on a rack, so the stops are set independently while the tumblers stay stationary.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
