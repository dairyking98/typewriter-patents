## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is tofovercome these and other disadvantages in tabulating devices heretofore devised and to produce a simple and efficient tabulating device wherein means are provided to assure the proper op-v eration of the tabulating device under all conditions.

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating mechanism that releases the carriage and arrests it at the tab stop, preventing re-engagement with the feed until the carriage has settled at the intended position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In tabulating devices the carriage can rebound off the tab stop and re-engage the feed mechanism, so it stops at the wrong space.

**Solution:** A tabulating mechanism that releases the carriage and arrests it at the tab stop, preventing re-engagement with the feed until the carriage has settled at the intended position.
