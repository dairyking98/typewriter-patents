## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing and cutting paper strips for cash registers)

**How it works (reading):** The conveyor roller feeds the strip continuously; the ejector rollers handle short feeds for multiple printing, and a control member switches the long feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On cash registers, multiple receipt printing and short feeds caused the paper strip to rear up and jam.

**Solution:** The conveyor roller feeds the strip continuously; the ejector rollers handle short feeds for multiple printing, and a control member switches the long feed.

**How it works:** Continuous conveyor roller feeds the strip, with an ejector pair making short feeds for multiple printing; a control member switches between single and multiple print.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
