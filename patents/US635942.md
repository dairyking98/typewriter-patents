## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a carriage ball bearing with a ball holder or separator, the holder can creep out of its fixed relation to the carriage.

**Solution:** Gear the carriage and the ball holder together through a gear train so the holder always keeps a fixed proportion to carriage travel.

**How it works:** Two fixed rails on posts have V-grooves facing V-grooves in a bar-like carriage. Two balls run in the lower track and two crosswise rolls in the upper track, kept in position by a vertical-plane holder with ears around the balls and fingers around the rolls. A bar with a toothed lower edge on the carriage meshes with a pinion on a shaft whose escapement wheel is stepped by an escapement pawl from the key system. The bar's upper rack meshes with a gear on a fixed stud, joined to a smaller gear that engages a rack on the ball holder, so the holder moves at a fixed fraction of the carriage motion. A spring drum pulls the carriage by a cord. A variant uses a ball in place of the two rolls.
