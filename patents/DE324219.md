## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocks between keys, e.g. without detent arrangements with detent arrangements

**How it works (reading):** Sensitive springs on each key act on a rocker bar that moves a locking bar in front of the key levers until a key finishes its stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressing several keys at once jammed the machine parts.

**Solution:** Sensitive springs on each key act on a rocker bar that moves a locking bar in front of the key levers until a key finishes its stroke.
