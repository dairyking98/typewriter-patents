## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Machines or apparatus for embossing decorations or marks, e.g. embossing coins characterised by the power drive

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to syllable writing machines of that class in which the carriage whereon the paper is held, comprises a cylinder which revolves around its axle during the printing of one line and is displaced in axial direction to present the paper for the beginning of a new line of print, the object of the invention being, to shift the cylinder automatically.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a syllable-writing machine whose paper cylinder turns during a line and shifts axially for the next line, the cylinder shift had to be done by hand.

**Solution:** A movable abutment, pulled away by a blade spring near the end of the cylinder's revolution, lets the escapement bar run on so the spring-driven cylinder turns freely to the start of a new line and shifts.

**How it works:** The paper cylinder sits on a shaft driven by a spring barrel through gears. Two juxtaposed six-tooth escapement wheels, stopped by a catch on a sliding bar, release the cylinder one step per syllable typed. A movable abutment limits the bar's leftward travel; when the cylinder nears the end of its turn an arm and roller act on a blade spring that pulls the abutment out of the way. The bar then goes fully left, frees the escapement wheels, and the spring barrel turns the cylinder to present the paper for a fresh line, with the axial shift done in the usual way.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** paper cylinder; spring barrel; escapement wheel; sliding bar; catch; movable abutment; blade spring; stopping lever

**Numbers:** six teeth per escapement wheel
