## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand-drawn special or ornate lettering takes many hours of skilled work.

**Solution:** A pen is moved by three sliding plates with guide-curve slots, one drive rod per character; the character size and slant can be set by the operator.

**How it works:** Each character's guide curve is cut as a slot in three plates; drive rods run in linear guides to move the pen through all three components.
