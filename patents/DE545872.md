> Description Translated from German Machine for making characters with a pen, whose movement is composed of three components by means of guide curves The invention relates to a machine for producing characters with a pen, whose movement is composed of three components by means of guide curves. That The main feature of the invention is that the guide curves of the individual characters are designed as slots in three sliding plates, in which for each character a drive rod engages, which is movable in fixed, linear guides. The machine enables, in a substantially mechanical manner To produce characters or the like by simply moving a button, which up to now could only be made by hand by skilled draftsmen.
