## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens or like sheet supports for manifolding using pressure-sensitive layers or intermediaries, e.g. for book-keeping purposes with stationary clamping means for holding the manifolding assembly in registered position, e.g. resilient clamps for holding non-perforated sheets

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to sheet set holders for copying by hand and with the typewriter.

**How it works (reading):** A sheet holder with two pockets whose walls are carbon sheets lets one entry copy only into the debit or credit column, and a pawl on a toothed edge moves the holder one line at a time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Entering the same item in the journal and in the debit and credit accounts takes two separate entries and a baseline mark on the carbon, which is slow and error-prone.

**Solution:** A sheet holder with two pockets whose walls are carbon sheets lets one entry copy only into the debit or credit column, and a pawl on a toothed edge moves the holder one line at a time.
