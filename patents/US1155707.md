## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A paper finger on the carriage is struck by type when it sits at the printing point.

**Solution:** A paper finger is both slidable and rotatable on the carriage rod; a projection on the frame at the printing point swings it aside while the carriage passes, and it returns to normal afterwards.
