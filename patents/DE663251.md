## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** A finder pin and feeler let inserting the slip sheet, setting the stop, and stopping the sheet happen in one work step.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Slip-sheet insertion devices needed extra knife or stop operations, adding steps beyond the work of typing.

**Solution:** A finder pin and feeler let inserting the slip sheet, setting the stop, and stopping the sheet happen in one work step.
