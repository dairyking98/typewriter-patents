## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German IMPERIAL PATENT OFFICE. PATENT LETTERING CLASS 77: Sports. Patented in the German Empire on August 10, 1883. The ratchet consists of a two-part capsule a, which can be rotated on a pin h provided with a handle. In the capsule are multiple voice (steel) c leaflets attached to one of its ends so, that they otherwise freely in the capsule are, FIGS. 2 and 3. The voice leaflets are c with its exposed end against the center of the capsule forming pin, Fig. 3. The pin is provided with a gripper d on its part lying in the capsule. This gripper sits firmly on the pin and, when the pin is rotated, pushes the exposed ends of the vocal cords to the side until they evade the gripper and snap back into their original position. Here the vocal cords emit a sound, which, since the reeds are tuned differently, causes music. The capsule is provided with sound openings and weighed down on the center with a figure reaching far beyond the edge, which serves as a momentum and, moreover, gives the ratchet a pleasing appearance.
