## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A lever parallel to the lever that drives the ribbon fork pivots when the platen is switched, and the ribbon lift height is changed by shifting that lever's pivot axis.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon guide fork must be lifted higher, and changing the ribbon lift needs an easy adjustment.

**Solution:** A lever parallel to the lever that drives the ribbon fork pivots when the platen is switched, and the ribbon lift height is changed by shifting that lever's pivot axis.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** switching bridge; ribbon guide fork; push rods; parallel lever
