## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Blind users need to write both ordinary letters and Braille points, possibly together, on one machine.

**Solution:** A polygonal type roller carries ordinary and Braille characters on its upper index faces and matching types and pins on its lower printing faces, in a slide frame pressed down to print.

**How it works:** The carriage slides on the frame and feeds letter by letter; it hinges at the rear to turn back. On it a slide frame moves on a bar and a crank bar, and a spring holds its free side raised. In the slide frame is an octagonal roller whose four upper faces show the letters with their Braille pin patterns, eight characters per face, thirty-two in all. The matching printing faces below carry types and pins for the same characters. A notched disk on the roller journal meets a spring detent so any of the four faces locks steady, and a pin limits turning to half a revolution. The operator slides the frame by the thumbpiece to set the character over the paper and presses it down.

**Parts:** main frame; sliding carriage; slide frame; sleeve; crank bar; pressure plate; spring; thumbpiece; octagonal type roller; notched disk; spring detent; limit pin
