## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**How it works (reading):** Each bearing axle is as thick as its spacing, with a central annular groove engaged by a guide web, so no screws or stepped grooves are needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type lever bearings with adjusting screws and stepped grooves are costly to make.

**Solution:** Each bearing axle is as thick as its spacing, with a central annular groove engaged by a guide web, so no screws or stepped grooves are needed.

Through this arrangement a significant simplification of the production is achieved, since numerous screws and thread in omission. Nevertheless, there is a safe and permanent storage Design type.
