> Description Translated from German Bearing for bow-shaped type levers on typewriters. The invention refers to the storage of bracket-shaped type levers on typewriters and consists in the fact that each bracket axis is just as strong as the distance between the axes from each other, and in the middle of the axes an annular groove is provided in which a guide web attached to the bearing block is inserted. Through this arrangement a significant simplification of the production is achieved, since numerous screws and thread in omission. Nevertheless, there is a safe and permanent storage Design type.
