## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Hammond, the ribbon guide and ribbon shield hide the just-printed letter at the printing point.

**Solution:** Drop the ribbon, guide fingers and shield out of the printing plane at rest and raise them by key-driven rods only during the stroke.

**How it works:** Pivoted curved arms with ribbon-guide fingers lie each side of the shuttle, and curved shield arms rest on projections of them. A shield has an opening opposite the type and hammer end. Vertical rods at each side, lower ends resting on rollers of the space-frame, are pushed up when a key is depressed. They swing the arms up, lifting ribbon and shield into the printing plane. On release they drop away so the last letter is visible. The shield swings back for inspection, with an adjustable bearing point setting its height.
