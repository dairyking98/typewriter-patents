## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage had to be moved back for the paper by hand after the last syllable of a line was printed, and the inventor wants this to happen automatically.

**Solution:** A key-wound spring drum drives the carriage through gearing, bars with rack teeth, and elbow levers, returning the carriage automatically once the line is complete.

**Features:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
