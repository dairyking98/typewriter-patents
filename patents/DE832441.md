## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (forwarding sheet setting in booking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for discharge with arrangements for mixing liquids or other fluent materials before discharge; Print registering by marks or formations on the paper being fed; Burners using a direct spraying action of liquid droplets or vaporised liquid into the combustion space the spraying being induced by a gaseous medium, e.g. water vapour

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a device for writing, Calculating or accounting machines for the automatic setting of a forwarding sheet on a predetermined line, in which a sensing element when inserting the Vorsteckblattes works with vain marking on the latter and after each booking made a further marking is incorporated into the pre-cut sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A cutting tool shaped as a sharpened tongue cuts a transverse slit as the mark after each booking; a brake controlled by the sensor stops the sheet when it hits the mark.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A sensor that reads perforations to set a pre-slip sheet onto a line is imprecise, because the perforation weakens the sheet.

**Solution:** A cutting tool shaped as a sharpened tongue cuts a transverse slit as the mark after each booking; a brake controlled by the sensor stops the sheet when it hits the mark.
