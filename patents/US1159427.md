## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A double typewriter with two side-by-side machine frames and one shared paper carriage needs margin stops and line locks that work in each printing zone.

**Solution:** Four alternating margin gages and a rocking rack bar across both frames set line limits and trigger the bell and line locks for each zone while letting the carriage pass between zones.

**How it works:** Two Underwood-style frames sit on a common base, each with typebars and a keyboard, and a carriage runs on guide rails across both. Left and right margin gages slide on a rod. Cams on the left gages are hit by a carriage projection and rock a rack bar, swinging arms that ring the bell and lock the keys of the zone. A spring-latched counter-stop on the carriage rides over a gage to cross zones, and a finger key lets it return. The carriage is driven by a spring drum and escapement.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** machine frame; typebar; carriage; guide rail; margin gage; rack bar; signal bell; line-lock; release key; spring drum; latch

**Numbers:** two printing zones; four margin gages
