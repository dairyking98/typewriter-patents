## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On the Oliver typewriter the right-hand margin stop arrests the carriage and rings the bell at line end, and the inventors want an improved right-hand marginal stop and release.

**Solution:** An adjustable sliding stop member on the carriage rack-bar engages a frame-mounted stop lever to arrest carriage travel and work the alarm bell.

**How it works:** The Oliver carriage rides on a shift frame and is driven by a spring, its letter-spacing controlled by a ratchet rack bar and an escape wheel on a vertical shaft. A longitudinal flange under the rack bar carries a sliding stop block that locks at chosen notches by a tooth. As the carriage nears line end, the stop block meets a lever on the frame, which arrests the carriage and operates the bell mechanism. The opening ends before the release action is described.
