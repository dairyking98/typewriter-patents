## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a ribbon reel for Sehreibmaschiiren, the shaft part of which is punched out of the end disks and the essential features of which are that one disk carries a trough-shaped shaft bearing in its center and the punched parts delimit the trough bearing and with their free ends on the opposite one Disc to be held, and that the latter disc carries the punched eye bearing for the lever .

**How it works (reading):** A spool is punched from two sheet-metal discs, with the hub webs and drive pin cut from the plate, and a reversing lever is formed from one strip.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools were costly to make with separate pegs and parts coming loose.

**Solution:** A spool is punched from two sheet-metal discs, with the hub webs and drive pin cut from the plate, and a reversing lever is formed from one strip.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
