## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (adding machine typebar carrier guidance) · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a advantageous TJ-p carrier guidance on adding and calculating machines with printing unit and under spring action;

**How it works (reading):** Tension springs run through the centre of each carrier's slot and hang from a wire on the upper guide rail, balancing the carrier for smooth sliding.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adding-machine type carriers must slide smoothly in guide rails and stay narrow so digit columns do not offset, which the earlier spring placement hindered.

**Solution:** Tension springs run through the centre of each carrier's slot and hang from a wire on the upper guide rail, balancing the carrier for smooth sliding.
