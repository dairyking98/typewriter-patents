## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing must be simple, easily adjustable for one, two or three spaces, and accurate at any position.

**Solution:** A bell-crank line-spacer lever pivoted to the platen frame, with a spacer-pawl whose throw is adjustable; line-spacing happens in the same movement as the carriage run.
