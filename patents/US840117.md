## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon width should be used fully, or several ribbons shifted into position, on a flat-platen machine.

**Solution:** A ribbon carrier for one or more ribbons shiftable to bring a chosen ribbon to the printing point.

**How it works:** Applied to a flat-platen typewriter, the ribbon carrier holds two ribbons and is shifted so that any ribbon or portion of width is at the print point.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon carrier; guide; flat platen
