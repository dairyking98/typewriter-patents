## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a braking device for the paper trolley from Typing, calculating, booking and similar machines using lamellar, axially displaceable disc groups that can be pressed against each other in a controllable manner, and of which, one group is secured against rotation and the other is rotatable.

**How it works (reading):** A small disc brake fixed to the frame, with disc packs pressed by a conical spring, is coupled through an elastic slidable gear to the ratchet wheel so the brake acts on the carriage at once.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage brake acting through the spring housing is slow and cannot follow the carriage, and friction brakes using washers are too hard to control.

**Solution:** A small disc brake fixed to the frame, with disc packs pressed by a conical spring, is coupled through an elastic slidable gear to the ratchet wheel so the brake acts on the carriage at once.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
