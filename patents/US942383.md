## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to produce a positive automatic reverse of the direction of the ribbon when it has been unwound from one spool and wound on the other spool, without tension on the ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** An endwise-shifting power shaft with bevel gears is flipped by impinger pins on a cylinder, tripped by ribbon followers when the ribbon runs out, switching which spool is driven.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal on a spool-fed typewriter must be positive and happen when the ribbon is fully wound off one spool, without straining the ribbon; the operator also wants to reverse by hand.

**Solution:** An endwise-shifting power shaft with bevel gears is flipped by impinger pins on a cylinder, tripped by ribbon followers when the ribbon runs out, switching which spool is driven.

**How it works:** Key strokes advance a spring motor through an escapement that turns a power shaft with bevel gears at each end meshing with the spool-shaft gears. A cylinder on the shaft carries spring-held impinger pins and a latch; a counter-rotating tubular shaft carries a plate with inclined faces. When the ribbon is fully unwound, ribbon followers on threaded tubular shafts move a ring and rod that push the impingers against the inclined faces, shifting the power shaft endwise so the other bevel gear drives the other spool. A manual means shifts the same part by hand.
