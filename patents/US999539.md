## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typewriter needs a cover that protects the machine, and a copy holder that can be stowed away.

**Solution:** A cover hinged to brackets that folds back out of the way and serves as a copy holder, an arrangement shown on an adding typewriter.
