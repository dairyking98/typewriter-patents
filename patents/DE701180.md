## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Types for calculating machines are costly because the tail profile must be milled after the head is cut from a blank.

**Solution:** The tail profile is rolled or drawn as a continuous bar, blanks are cut off transversely, and the head with its character is embossed in one step.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Method of making types for calculating machines and the like The invention relates to a method for producing types with a type tail for calculating machines and the like In the previous process, the blank was made from a profile body cut off, the profile of which corresponded roughly to the thickness of the type head. On that the font was embossed and then the shape of the type tail was milled, whereupon the Type trimmed and the edges broken. The production of such a Type was laborious as a result, which made them expensive to manufacture. According to the invention, this manufacturing process is now simplified by that the shape of the type tail of the type seen from the side in that for formation of the blanks serving body worked out as a continuous partial profile of this body and the blanks from the body formed in this way (profile rod) transversely are cut off, whereupon the type head with the Type character is embossed. The profile bar is advantageously produced by rolling or drawing. This results in a saving in material.
