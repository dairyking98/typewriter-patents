## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter ribbon spools, pivots and levers are exposed to dust, dirt and bending damage.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal protective jacket covers the sides, back and front, with a releasable upper shell that carries the intermediate lever pivot and stays easy to open.
