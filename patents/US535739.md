## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection the plane of the type or die face being perpendicular to the axis of rotation

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in typewriting machines, and the objects of the same are, first, to furnish a compact keyboard in which the keys will have but slight movement, and each key shall represent two characters, and arrest the movement of a type wheel at certain periods;

**How it works (reading):** A power-spun type wheel on a central shaft is stopped by a finger swinging into the rod of a pressed key, and a printing device then strikes, with feeding that moves either the head over the paper or the paper past the head.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing machines are bulky with long key travel; the inventor wants a compact keyboard of short-stroke keys that each stand for two characters and stop a rotating type wheel.

**Solution:** A power-spun type wheel on a central shaft is stopped by a finger swinging into the rod of a pressed key, and a printing device then strikes, with feeding that moves either the head over the paper or the paper past the head.

**How it works:** Square rods with keys on top stand in a circular dial plate, three circular rows in two sections, each key holding one alternate type space with the opposite section covering the vacant ones. A pressed key pushes its rod down into the sweep of a finger fixed to the central shaft, which also carries the type wheel and an actuating lever. A pulley and clutch device turns the shaft either way, and the finger stops against the rod, bringing the matching letter on the wheel into the printing device. Springs return the finger to a zero position between the sections. Studs on the wheel and plate with arms bring it back, the printing device acts after the wheel is arrested, and a feeding device moves either the type carriage over a book or the paper. A ribbon is held in the carriage.
