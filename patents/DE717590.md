## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to using a device for sinking typewriters into a cabinet of pairs of swing arms attached to two metal rails attached to the side walls of the cabinet.

**How it works (reading):** Metal rails attach to the front and rear corner pillars instead of the side walls, carrying the swing arms, and counterbalance springs sit between rails and walls.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sinking a typewriter into a desk loads the thin wooden side walls, which can distort and jam the lowering mechanism.

**Solution:** Metal rails attach to the front and rear corner pillars instead of the side walls, carrying the swing arms, and counterbalance springs sit between rails and walls.
