## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier column-stop tabulator mechanisms are said to be complicated and poorly constructed.

**Solution:** Key-actuated column-selecting stops on a column stop bar, with a universal bar and arm connection, give a more practical tabulator action.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
