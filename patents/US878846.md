## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to means for effecting a fractional line spacing of the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A platen frame with a detent roller and an added line-spacing pawl and guide plate gives positions between normal lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fractional line spacing is needed to position the platen for exponents or fractions.

**Solution:** A platen frame with a detent roller and an added line-spacing pawl and guide plate gives positions between normal lines.
