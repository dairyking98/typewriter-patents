## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (printing unit for adding machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Output mechanisms with printing mechanisms, e.g. for character-at-a-time or line-at-a-time printing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing unit for adding machines, in which in addition to the type carrier for printing the calculated value a type roll is provided for printing special information carrying pivotable holders is, whereby the details of both parts of the printing unit are printed at the same time.

**How it works (reading):** A printing plate is placed above or below the type rolls, and its fixed characters are printed together with the variable type on the same line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A fixed printed line of information cannot be added beside the variable figures of an adding machine printing unit without widening the machine.

**Solution:** A printing plate is placed above or below the type rolls, and its fixed characters are printed together with the variable type on the same line.
