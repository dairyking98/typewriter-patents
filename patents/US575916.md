## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in certain features of construction and combinations of devices, all as will be hereinafter more fully described, and particularly pointed out in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** A card-holder conforms the card to the platen and adjusts it relative to the line-spacing ratchet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards do not lie on the platen, and printing cannot start at a line on a card.

**Solution:** A card-holder conforms the card to the platen and adjusts it relative to the line-spacing ratchet.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
