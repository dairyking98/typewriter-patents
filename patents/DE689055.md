## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** A single shift rail that moves in a straight line parallel to the line between the spool axes, locked on one side and released by the feeler lever of the emptying spool, so a constantly reciprocating control rod drops into the other of two notches and reverses the feed.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversers with a swinging shift rail give the two feed pawls different paths, so the parts on the two spool sides cannot be mirror images, and feeler-lever controls need a second rail.

**Solution:** A single shift rail that moves in a straight line parallel to the line between the spool axes, locked on one side and released by the feeler lever of the emptying spool, so a constantly reciprocating control rod drops into the other of two notches and reverses the feed.

**How it works:** Each ribbon spool sits on a ratchet bearing bracket with a driver pin. A feeler lever rides on the ribbon turns of each spool and is fixed to an angle lever whose segment-shaped slideway guides the control nose of a three-armed locking lever. As long as the spool has ribbon the nose rides the slideway and the locking bolt is held clear. When the ribbon runs down to a given diameter the nose drops off the slideway end, and a spring on the third arm of the locking lever pushes its bolt into engagement with the shift rail to lock one side only. The control rod, moved constantly back and forth from the machine drive, then engages the other of the two notches on the rail, sliding it to bring the other pawl to its ratchet wheel.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; ratchet wheel; feeler lever; angle lever; segment slideway; three-armed locking lever; shift rail; notch; control rod; control roller; feed pawl; tension spring

**Improves on:**

- ribbon reversers with a swinging shift rail: straight sliding rail gives equal paths so left and right parts are mirror images
- feeler-lever reversers needing a second shift rail: one rail does both pawl shifting and direction reversal
