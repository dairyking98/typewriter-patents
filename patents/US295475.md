## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage escapement needs to advance a letter space at each key stroke using a single spacing ratchet.

**Solution:** Two ratchet wheels turn alternately by one tooth, one released as the key goes down and the other as it comes up, with a single rocking ratchet working in both.

**How it works:** A first toothed wheel is driven by a spring and meshes with the carriage rack. A second toothed wheel loose beside it has a shoulder in a slot of the first and a spring that pushes it forward by one notch. A ratchet on a rocking bar, moved by the key lever, sits in a notch of the second wheel at rest. Pressing the key moves the ratchet into the first wheel, letting the second advance a notch to its stop. Releasing the key moves the ratchet back into the second wheel one notch back, letting the first wheel and carriage advance one letter space.
