## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The noiseless toggle joint that presses the type lever on the paper roller is bulky, and the existing arrangement loads the type lever bearing badly.

**Solution:** Both legs of the auxiliary knee lever are hinged to a lug on the main toggle arm below the main toggle joint, and the main toggle is fixed to the frame at the pressure point so the force runs straight through the type lever to the paper roller.
