## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the receipt feed length in a cash register's conveyor is awkward and requires moving roller bearings or interrupting the checkout.

**Solution:** Each feed roller is built from several coaxial bodies that can be coupled or uncoupled to change the transport route.
