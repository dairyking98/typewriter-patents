## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** A separate clamping member for each locking lever slides on a guide body so the radial release pressure is taken by the guide body; this allows coupling in any position with weaker springs, and the inclined surface is self-locking.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A platen ratchet clutch with locking levers works only in some positions and needs strong springs because of friction in its clamping members.

**Solution:** A separate clamping member for each locking lever slides on a guide body so the radial release pressure is taken by the guide body; this allows coupling in any position with weaker springs, and the inclined surface is self-locking.
