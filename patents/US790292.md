## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cable or belt constructions for driving print, type or paper-carriages, e.g. attachment, tensioning means

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my present invention is to overcome the difficulties heretofore encountered and to provide a simple, rapid, and efiicient letter-'spacing mechanism which will adapt itself to the various conditions and to the differences in speed of operation of the machine.
