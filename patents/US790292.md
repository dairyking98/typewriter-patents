## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letter-spacing by moving the heavy platen (40-50 lb with its paper or book) at every key stroke is too slow and does not adapt to varying typing speeds.

**Solution:** Move the light carriage carrying the type-bars and key-levers over the stationary platen, with an escapement (fixed and loose dogs, escape wheel, pinion on a rack rod) riding on the carriage.

**How it works:** A flat platen (book bed) is pulled by a weighted band on tracks, with a fore-and-aft carriage on it for line spacing. The carriage carries the type-bars, linked to key-levers that bear on a universal bar. The universal bar rocks a dog-rocker with a fixed and a loose dog that engage an escape wheel on a shaft whose feed pinion meshes with a rack on the traverse rod. Parts of the escapement travel with the carriage, so each stroke spaces the carriage relative to the platen. The cut text is truncated before the adaptive spacing action is described.
