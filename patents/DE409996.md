**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** Two switching bridges let the width setting travel less for wide characters, making key effort even; suspended in stable or unstable equilibrium to cut work.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Single and double carriage steps give uneven type, and key effort is heavier for wide characters.

**Solution:** Two switching bridges let the width setting travel less for wide characters, making key effort even; suspended in stable or unstable equilibrium to cut work.

**How it works:** Type lever swings a pendulum switching bridge behind it, moving a roller and tie rod that rock the pawl carrier with fixed and loose teeth on the ratchet. A second bridge in front of the key levers sets the loose tooth deflection for single, double or triple step by key lever shape. Wide heavy types get the full carriage range without extra setting, narrow ones move the setting parts furthest.
