## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters for ciphering or deciphering cryptographic text with auxiliary keys or keyboards acting on the original keys or keyboards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a typewriter for writing simultaneously from dispatches, etc.

**How it works (reading):** A second typewriter is coupled to the ordinary one so its platen and carriage move in step, and an electrical distributor driven by a non-circular disc selects a different key on the second machine for each key pressed on the first.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A telegram must be written in secret at the sending point so that only a matching typewriter at the receiving point can read it.

**Solution:** A second typewriter is coupled to the ordinary one so its platen and carriage move in step, and an electrical distributor driven by a non-circular disc selects a different key on the second machine for each key pressed on the first.
