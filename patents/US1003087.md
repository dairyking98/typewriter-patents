## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets feed unevenly from a pile, and the roller surface wears unevenly.

**Solution:** Sheets are fed through a gateway between two rubber rollers, the lower driven intermittently and the upper one held by a brake so it turns and spreads wear.
