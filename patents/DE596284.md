## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A calculator motor can start while an amount key is only partly depressed, so the entry is wrong.

**Solution:** A breaker in the motor circuit stays closed normally, and a carriage linked to the locking bars opens its contacts whenever any key is only partly pressed.
