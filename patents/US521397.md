## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Blind people need a machine to emboss point writing.

**Solution:** A keyboard of levers drives a row of embossing styluses against a die, several at once if needed, to dent the paper in point-writing patterns.

**How it works:** Eight styluses in two rows, resting on first-class key levers, are guided in a common plate. Pressing a key raises its stylus against a die, denting the paper held between rollers on a carriage. Keys for the front and back rows end at different distances so the blind operator can feel which row. Yokes unite keys so several styluses fire together, and springs restore the keys.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)
- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
- **Uses:** [For the blind](../features/use-blind.md)

**Parts:** stylus; die; key lever; yoke; paper carriage; feed roller; spring; guide plate

**Numbers:** eight styluses
