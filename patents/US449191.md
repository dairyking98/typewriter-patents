## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A curved roller platen gives unclean impressions, and faceted platens are hard to keep parallel to the type face.

**Solution:** A thin, transversely flexible supplemental platen strip beneath the paper that yields to conform to the face of each type.

**How it works:** A metal strip, wider than the height of the character, is fixed at each end of the carriage next to the roller beneath the paper. It is rigid lengthwise but flexible across, so a type striking at an angle bends it until the type face lands flat. Variants use brass or rubber covering, or a heavy strip pivoted at its ends so it self-aligns; the roller then only feeds the paper.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md), [Impression adjustment](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** roller; carriage; metal strip; rubber covering; pivot

**Improves on:**

- faceted platens: conforms automatically to the type face instead of needing precise parallelism
