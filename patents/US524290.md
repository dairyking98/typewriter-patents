## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines print on the underside of the platen so the writing is hidden.

**Solution:** Resilient type-bar holders jointed to the key arms swing up and strike the top of the platen against a stop plate, bringing the writing into view as it is made.

**How it works:** Bars are pivoted on a fixed bed and carry jointed resilient type holders. A fixed stop plate with beveled recess arrests and guides the holders into line as they swing up, centring them for alignment. A ribbon moved intermittently sits between type and the cylinder. Upper and lower case letters are printed with equal facility, and the arrangement keeps each letter visible immediately after its impression.
