## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with a front sole, in which the through the type lever moving plungers are loosely arranged in the type lever bearing segment and affect the universal rail when the button lever stops.

**How it works (reading):** Plungers loosely arranged in the type lever bearing segment, pushed by spring-loaded upright angle levers whose upturned arms contact the universal rail when a button lever stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The universal rail must be reliably engaged by the selector in every position of the sliding type lever bearing segment when the type levers are struck, which the earlier layout did not guarantee.

**Solution:** Plungers loosely arranged in the type lever bearing segment, pushed by spring-loaded upright angle levers whose upturned arms contact the universal rail when a button lever stops.
