## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In ribbon feeds where the spool axes slide on an adjusting rail to switch winding direction, the axes are weak and the rail is heavy to move.

**Solution:** Fix the spool axes and instead switch which spool is driven by lifting one pawl set out of engagement with a sliding rail turning thumb disks.

**How it works:** The key lever's oscillating stirrup swings a drive lever on each side, which swings a pawl-carrying lever about the spool axis. Pawls engage ratchet wheels on the spools. Locking pawls on fixed parts stop the spools turning back. A slide moves two rotatable thumb disks that lift the feed pawl and locking pawl on one side while leaving the other pair working, so ribbon winds onto one spool; moving the slide reverses this.
