## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type bars of a machine that uses them wear out, and renewing them was difficult in the existing construction.

**Solution:** A semicircular segment carries type bars in radial slots with enlarged grooves at the upper ends, so that worn type bars can be removed and replaced.
