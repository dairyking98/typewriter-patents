> Description Translated from German Device for aligning set to form a line Type carriers in line typewriters. The invention relates to a device for aligning type carriers set to form a line in line typewriters and consists in particular in that an alignment means moved in the direction of the lines engages in recesses on the surface of the type carrier carrying the type are arranged. The alignment means is advantageously designed as a rotatable alignment wheel, which is flexible on a strip that can be moved in the direction or parallel to the line is arranged. The alignment means is expediently provided with beveled engagement surfaces equipped, which engage in correspondingly designed upper grooves of the type carrier. It shows: Fig. I an elevation, Fig. 2 a plan and Fig. 3 a Side view of a device according to the invention.
