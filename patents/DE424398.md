## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Syllable, line, or like type selection

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for aligning type carriers set to form a line in line typewriters and consists in particular in that an alignment means moved in the direction of the lines engages in recesses on the surface of the type carrier carrying the type are arranged.

**How it works (reading):** A rotatable alignment wheel on a yielding carrier is drawn along the line and engages bevelled grooves on the type carriers, pressing them into line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars set side by side in a line must be straightened so their printing faces align before printing.

**Solution:** A rotatable alignment wheel on a yielding carrier is drawn along the line and engages bevelled grooves on the type carriers, pressing them into line.
