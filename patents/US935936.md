## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to line-spacers for typewriters, and particularly to those spacers used in connection with a horizontal cylindrical platen mounted y carriage adapted to be shifted and to be intermittently revolved to effect the spacing between the lines.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line-space lever hanging over the keyboard is inconvenient.

**Solution:** Shifting the carriage by the end knob also turns the platen through a pin and curved slot.

**How it works:** The right knob stem is fixed in a sleeve with a ratchet disk. The left knob stem cannot turn and slides endwise through the platen. Pushing the carriage along moves the stem, and a pin in a curved slot of a hollow shaft converts that straight motion into rotation, driving a pawl on the ratchet. The pawl throw is limited by an arm and removable pin to give single or double line spacing.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)

**Parts:** platen; knob; stem; ratchet disk; pawl; curved slot; pin; hollow shaft

**Improves on:**

- ordinary line-space lever: carriage return motion also turns the platen
