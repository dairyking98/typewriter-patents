## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Touch typists need to practise without looking at the keys, and a cover must not obstruct the operator or the shifting lever.

**Solution:** A fabric body on a spring-wire frame that clamps to the front posts of the machine and hangs down over the key bank, hiding the characters, and can be raised or lowered.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)
