## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing between two colour fields of a ribbon, plus a stencil position, needed separate keys for each setting, using keyboard space and extra parts.

**Solution:** One key, pressed repeatedly, alternately sets the ribbon vibrator actuator for ribbon field 1 or 2, and a second key centres it so neither field is active (stencil).

**How it works:** A ribbon vibrator lever has two wrist pins at different distances from its fulcrum, and an actuator driven by the type-bar universal bar carries two slotted ears. Sliding the actuator sideways lets one pin or the other enter its slot, giving a short or long ribbon throw, or neither pin for no motion. A rock shaft sets the actuator, and a single key pulls a plate carrying a two-armed pawl that engages whichever cross-plate pin is in line, rocking the shaft to the other setting each time. A detent holds the shaft in either of two nicks, and a needle on a dial shows the setting. A second key pushes the shaft lengthwise so a wedge plate centres it, making both ribbon fields inactive.

**Parts:** ribbon vibrator; actuator; wrist pin; rock shaft; cross plate; two-armed pawl; key lever; detent; dial and needle; centring wedge; spring
