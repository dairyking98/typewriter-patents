## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with means for effecting line or character spacing in either direction; Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a typewriter, on the frame of which a carriage is carried, the movements of which are controlled by a pinion which is in engagement with a rack and with a device for Umleehr the direction of advance of the carriage is provided.

**How it works (reading):** The rack is mounted symmetrically above or below the carriage pinion so the advance direction reverses quickly without swapping parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adapting a typewriter to write right-to-left changes many parts (gearshift, backspace, transport).

**Solution:** The rack is mounted symmetrically above or below the carriage pinion so the advance direction reverses quickly without swapping parts.
