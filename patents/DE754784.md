## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier magnetic typebar drives needed one magnet and contact per type carrier or left many parts attached, making them complicated and noisy.

**Solution:** Mount each type lever independently of other parts and let a shared segment-shaped electromagnet accelerate the lever through iron lamellae fixed to it, switched by the key lever.

**How it works:** Each type lever pivots on a segment bearing and rests on a felt pad. Iron lamellae riveted to its segment part cooperate with pole pieces of a laminated segment-shaped electromagnet. The key lever, pushed down, brings the lever to a position where the magnet drives it in the stroke, with a contact controlling the current. A universal rail on leaf springs is struck by the levers, and the lever returns while the key is still held.
