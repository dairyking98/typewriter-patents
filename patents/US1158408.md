## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A split platen needs independent line-spacing and independent paper release for each section, so one section can hold a ledger sheet while the other receives individual statements.

**Solution:** A platen made in independently movable sections with a splined shaft, clutches and a connecting device that couples one section or both to the line-space mechanism and to the paper-release key.

**How it works:** The platen shaft carries the usual line-space and hand wheels at the left but is not permanently joined to either platen section. The right hand wheel sits on a short shaft ending in a sleeve splined to the platen shaft; pulling or pushing it shifts a clutch to engage the left or right section, and both engage for ordinary writing. Each section has its own feed rolls with an independent roll-release, and one release key connects to either through the same device that connects the section to the line-space mechanism. A partly written sheet can thus be held motionless on one section while the other is written on.
