## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type lever rest pads wear out from repeated strikes and let levers bounce back.

**Solution:** Rest pad is felt with an elastic rubber-thread fabric layer and a woollen cover where the type lever strikes.
