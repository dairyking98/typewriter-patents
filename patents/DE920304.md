## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A card held by a pre-insertion latch needs the sensor pressed too hard, and the feeder is driven by a spring motor that stops early when the button is tapped.

**Solution:** The insertion lever drives the conveyor with a resilient sensor pressing on the card through a slip clutch on the drive shaft, so the card stops at the desired line.
