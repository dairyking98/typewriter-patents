## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are noisy; type arm positioning needs to be common-centred.

**Solution:** A rigid frame holds a horizontally movable type-arm carrier that is moved and locked to a common centre, with spring tension, by key depression.

**How it works:** Depressing a key moves the carrier horizontally to the platen and its adjustable cam positions side lever arms with bevelled jaws that grip a bevelled recess in the type holder, locating it and holding it at one writing position. A spring gives adjustable, semi-automatic tension to the carrier. The aim is a comparatively noiseless machine.
