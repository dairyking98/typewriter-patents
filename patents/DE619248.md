## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Duplicating attachments with auxiliary ribbon spools on a rear shaft fixed to the frame make the spools shift relative to the traveling carriage, and changing the spools is awkward.

**Solution:** Put the auxiliary ribbon spools in a basket that slides out of a spool frame fixed to the carriage so they travel with it and all come out at once.

**How it works:** A spool frame rides with the carriage on a rail on the main frame and holds a removable cage with a pull-out shaft carrying the auxiliary ribbon spools. The auxiliary ribbons run to the front of the platen between the working sheets, behind the main ribbon, to a ribbon take-up device with switching rollers on the right, driven by the line feed frame movement. Each spool is braked continuously; a manually operated device raises the braking so the spools lock shortly before the feed ends, tightening the ribbons so the sheet cannot draw them upward on the next line feed.
