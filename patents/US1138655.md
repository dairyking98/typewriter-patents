## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon slack tangles in the ribbon devices and the spool can be removed freely.

**Solution:** Hinged two-section housing over the ribbon spool with posts forming a guide slot for the ribbon and a latch to close it.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)
