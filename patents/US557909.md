## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Need enough jointed type-carriers around a common printing point to carry all characters, printing in sight of the operator.

**Solution:** A jointed typebar with a driving link and guide link carrying a socketed type carrier, actuated by a key lever through a rod and a short link that shifts the pulling point.

**How it works:** Typebars radiate in a basket around the printing point and rest on a concentric inking pad. Each has a two-piece driving link pivoted in the basket and a guide link, with the type carrier pivoted on their ends and the type set in a socket at an angle so the stem is vertical when printing. The key lever pulls a rod joined to the driving link through a short curved link, giving a shifting pulling point and large bar motion for small key depression. Types carry upper and lower case, requiring carriage shift.
