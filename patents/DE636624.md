## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Switching regulator for fabric winding on ribbon looms The invention is a switching regulator (regulator) for ribbon looms with special for themselves Ratchet rings that form parts of the same size, but different types of teeth, these are arranged easily interchangeably, without screw connections, for the purpose of band fabric to change the weft density or the repeat. It is already known to make the ring gear replaceable in the case of gears. But here it is about the interchangeability for the purpose of bad or Gear rims of spur gears that have become unusable due to new ones of the same diameter to replace or gear ring halves or entire gear rings of different diameters as intermediate gears, e.g. B. to change the drive speed for the weft impact, the shaft movement etc. from the crankshaft of the loom, or unusable to replace old gear rings with new, precisely working ones; fastening the gear rims or the gear halves is always done by means of several Screws.
