## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (ribbon loom gear regulator) · **Subject (patent classes):** Smallware looms, i.e. looms for weaving ribbons or other narrow fabrics

**How it works (reading):** The ratchet ring is made as a separate interchangeable ring with radial cams; spokes of the carrier rim press against the cams, so it snaps in without screws.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the ratchet wheel of a ribbon-loom switching regulator needs the whole wheel replaced or several screws removed.

**Solution:** The ratchet ring is made as a separate interchangeable ring with radial cams; spokes of the carrier rim press against the cams, so it snaps in without screws.
