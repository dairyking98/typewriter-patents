## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in line-spacing mechanism for type-writers;

**How it works (reading):** A lever-operated shaft turns a beveled sector that meshes with a sector on the platen hub. A pawl with a hooked bill and a tail on the hub arm engages concave-inclined ratchet teeth on the platen end, and a brake holds the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says existing line-spacing pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms can be overthrown.

**Solution:** A lever-operated shaft turns a beveled sector that meshes with a sector on the platen hub. A pawl with a hooked bill and a tail on the hub arm engages concave-inclined ratchet teeth on the platen end, and a brake holds the platen.
