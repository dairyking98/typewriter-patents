## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Manuscript being copied has to be held behind the machine and the operator needs to see the line where reading stopped.

**Solution:** A manuscript frame with a line-indicator fastened to an endless belt driven by a knob, moving up or down the frame to mark the line.
