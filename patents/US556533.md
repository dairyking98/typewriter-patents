## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stenographic shorthand typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for stenographic writing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and particularly to that class of devices designed to use stenographic or shorthand characters;

**How it works (reading):** A stenographic typewriter with grouped character impressions, a carriage-locking pawl, a line-spacing lever clutched to the feed roll spindle, and a ribbon guide.
