## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator has to reverse and shift the ribbon by hand, and uses only part of its width.

**Solution:** Automatic ribbon reversing and widthwise shifting driven from the carriage driving arm.

**How it works:** On a Caligraph, the ribbon runs between two spools on grooved shafts that the spools turn with but can slide along. Bevel gears on each spool shaft are engaged alternately by sliding bevel wheels driven by a ratchet and a pawl moved at every key stroke from the rocker frame, so only one spool winds at a time. The carriage driving arm trips ribbon shifting levers at the ends of travel, which reverse the winding and slide the spools sideways to bring a new width of ribbon to the type.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spool; grooved shaft; bevel gear; sliding bevel wheel; ratchet wheel; driving pawl; rocker frame; carriage driving arm; shifting lever; feed rack
