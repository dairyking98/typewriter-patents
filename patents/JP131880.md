## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies arranged in stationary or sliding cases or frames or upon flexible strips, plates, bars or rods; Japanese-language typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** The object: in a universal typewriter that can print either horizontally or vertically, to release the tray lock as early as possible in vertical writing, where the platen feed throws no inertia onto the frame of the printing mechanism, compared with horizontal writing, which does, and so to speed up character selection.

**How it works (reading):** Link the tray locking piece to the horizontal/vertical changeover so that in horizontal writing it holds until the sideways platen feed finishes, and in vertical writing it releases as soon as the type has dropped back into the tray.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a universal Japanese typewriter printing both horizontally and vertically, the tray lock is held for the longer horizontal-feed period in both modes, which slows vertical writing.

**Solution:** Link the tray locking piece to the horizontal/vertical changeover so that in horizontal writing it holds until the sideways platen feed finishes, and in vertical writing it releases as soon as the type has dropped back into the tray.

**How it works:** A locking piece engages a rack positioning the type tray, and its cam piece normally is held back by a swinging weight piece with a control face so the lock lasts until the printing lever has returned. The weight hangs on a cross shaft that slides axially. A control lever, linked to the horizontal/vertical changeover bar, shifts that shaft by a groove. In vertical mode the weight's control face moves out of the cam's path and the lock lets go at once after the type drops back. In horizontal mode it stays in the path, delaying release until the sideways feed of the platen frame is finishing.
