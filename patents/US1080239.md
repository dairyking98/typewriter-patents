## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Carbon strips interleaved in folded continuous billing sheets are severed with the sheets and used only once, and carbon roll carriers cannot be inserted between the folds.

**Solution:** Devices for typewriting continuous folded billing work sheets that handle the carbon with the sheet so the carbon can be advanced and used repeatedly; details are only partly legible in the OCR.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
