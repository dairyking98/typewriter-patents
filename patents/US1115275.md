## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to produce an electrically controlledmeans for operating the entire structure immediately upon the operating keys being touched by' the operator, thebalance of the operatlon being then carried on by magnetic attraction.

**How it works (reading):** A key releases a separate mechanical (optionally magnet-assisted) striker that drives the typebars.

## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Typebar force depends on operator strength.

**Solution:** A key releases a separate mechanical (optionally magnet-assisted) striker that drives the typebars.

**How it works:** Keys release a mechanical impression mechanism that strikes the type against paper with uniform force.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:spring-energy](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** key; impression mechanism; type rack; magnet
