## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and manually actuated

**How it works (reading):** A device tied to the lid open and close action returns the carriage to centre and locks it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage must be re-centred by hand and locked again after the lid is opened and closed.

**Solution:** A device tied to the lid open and close action returns the carriage to centre and locks it.
