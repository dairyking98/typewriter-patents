## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Many account balances must be stored compactly and any one read back quickly on demand.

**Solution:** Each storage element is a steel surface magnetised by a count of current pulses; a dial selects the element, and the pulses read back to a display.
