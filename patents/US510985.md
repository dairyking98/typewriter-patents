## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding a sheet of telegraph blanks or bill heads needed the feed to be set by platen rotation so the operator can tear off each typed blank.

**Solution:** A paper supply roll is hung on arms, and a notched platen or serrated severing plate lets one complete platen turn feed the paper to the next blank.
