## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable link on the push-off lever carries inclined surfaces that act on spring-loaded switching pawls of each ribbon spool; the spring-loaded angle-lever arms resting on each coil move the link so that the ribbon reverses at the right time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reels that must reverse automatically depend on the winding thickness, which changes and disturbs the reversing action.

**Solution:** An adjustable link on the push-off lever carries inclined surfaces that act on spring-loaded switching pawls of each ribbon spool; the spring-loaded angle-lever arms resting on each coil move the link so that the ribbon reverses at the right time.
