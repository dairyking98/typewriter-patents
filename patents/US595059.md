## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for fixing the film on the periphery of a reel; Resilient gripping member having coiled portion

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements yin ribbon-holders;

**How it works (reading):** A two-part semicylindrical case with spool flanges bearing on the case protects the ribbon edges and surface and has a slot and clasp for the ribbon end.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon needs a protected case and free unwinding from its spool.

**Solution:** A two-part semicylindrical case with spool flanges bearing on the case protects the ribbon edges and surface and has a slot and clasp for the ribbon end.
