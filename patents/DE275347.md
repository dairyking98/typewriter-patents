## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**How it works (reading):** The loose pawl pivots directly below the ratchet axis on a swinging pawl carrier, so it follows the teeth and keeps engagement; an adjustable stop limits the carrier's swing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In earlier paper-carriage switching devices the pawl did not stay in constant engagement with the ratchet teeth.

**Solution:** The loose pawl pivots directly below the ratchet axis on a swinging pawl carrier, so it follows the teeth and keeps engagement; an adjustable stop limits the carrier's swing.
