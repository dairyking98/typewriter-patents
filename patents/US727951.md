## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a flat-platen machine a sheet at one edge tilts the paper-confining frame, so the type-bar pivots no longer strike at one point and alignment is lost.

**Solution:** The paper-confining frame has one side independently vertically adjustable and supported, so the printing point stays parallel to the platen even when paper is under one side only.
