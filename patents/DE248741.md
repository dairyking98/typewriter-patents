## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fount-change arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a switching device for the type lever bearing carrier and the key lever bearing bracket of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A universal rod guided on the machine frame holds the paper-slide inhibiting linkage in the normal position when the button lever group is lifted, letting the type-lever segment move vertically.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching between type-lever bearing carrier and key-lever bracket needs a separate key to the carrier.

**Solution:** A universal rod guided on the machine frame holds the paper-slide inhibiting linkage in the normal position when the button lever group is lifted, letting the type-lever segment move vertically.
