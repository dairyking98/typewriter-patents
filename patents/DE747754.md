## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (counter for typewriters with totalizer)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a counter for typewriters, especially with a totalizer.

**How it works (reading):** A double counter couples selected wheelsets to a storage unit; the upper counter can be coupled to or uncoupled from the lower counter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A booking machine needs a memory counter to store values and total several stored values, which needs wheelsets coupled to a separate unit.

**Solution:** A double counter couples selected wheelsets to a storage unit; the upper counter can be coupled to or uncoupled from the lower counter.

- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** double counter; storage counter; wheelset; mounting rail; gear train
