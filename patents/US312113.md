## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Garrison wants a small typewriter that prints with an inked type roller and no ribbon, with the printed line staying in alignment.

**Solution:** A type roller slides along its axis and tilts, steered by a pointer lever over a fixed letter board, to print on a separate impression cylinder that steps along after each letter.

**How it works:** The type roller and impression cylinder lie at right angles. A lever on the roller frame carries a pointer; moving it over a stationary letter board shifts the roller until the chosen character is above the cylinder, and depressing the lever to the letter brings that type onto the paper. The roller turns on its axis against an inking roller, so no ribbon is needed. When the finger lifts, the roller rises and a space-bar or rack lets the cylinder step forward one space. At line end the cylinder returns and turns to give the line spacing. Alignment devices keep each impression in line with earlier ones.
