## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and it has for its principal object to improve, in several respects, the carriage feed mechanism of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** An improved carriage feed mechanism with escapement wheel and rack-and-pinion drive arranged so the carriage returns to the same position reliably.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The lifting rack and pawl-and-ratchet carriage feed of usual machines give inaccurate and inconsistent carriage positions because of tooth inaccuracies.

**Solution:** An improved carriage feed mechanism with escapement wheel and rack-and-pinion drive arranged so the carriage returns to the same position reliably.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
