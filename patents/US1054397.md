## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type-lock stopping the carriage gets damaged by the tabulator stops when the carriage runs free.

**Solution:** A lock that moves out of the way of the shifting member when the carriage overruns, so the lock is not struck.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
