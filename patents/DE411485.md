## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (snap fastener, not a typewriter) · **Subject (patent classes):** Fastening or securing by means not forming part of the material of the label itself by strings, straps, chains, or wires

**How it works (reading):** Both closure parts are flat discs; one is a plain disc and the other has an inward-curved edge that snaps over it and holds it permanently, so the band can be sold in block or roll form.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Dance-badge ribbon fasteners need a locking part that stands high, which is awkward to handle and to pack.

**Solution:** Both closure parts are flat discs; one is a plain disc and the other has an inward-curved edge that snaps over it and holds it permanently, so the band can be sold in block or roll form.
