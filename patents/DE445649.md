## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters for ciphering or deciphering cryptographic text with auxiliary keys or keyboards acting on the original keys or keyboards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cipher typewriter with a character field and setting pointer at which the character field provided with the characters the typewriter is replaced in a known manner by chopsticks provided with symbols that are interchangeable and relocatable in their order.

**How it works (reading):** Interchangeable small rooms with endless belts carry the characters, and tapes set the order, so the symbol set changes without changing the machine.
