## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**How it works (reading):** Two oppositely directed gear racks with pawls on a switching bridge re-engage each other in turn, so each switching process ends when the type lever snaps back.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage switching devices are fragile, costly, and take up space in the frame.

**Solution:** Two oppositely directed gear racks with pawls on a switching bridge re-engage each other in turn, so each switching process ends when the type lever snaps back.
