## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (flat knitting machine slide drive) · **Subject (patent classes):** Gearings comprising essentially only toothed gears or friction members and not capable of conveying indefinitely-continuing rotary motion for interconverting rotary or oscillating motion and reciprocating motion comprising a friction shaft

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a motorized drive for the slide of Flat knitting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Friction rollers on the carriage are pressed onto a rotating shaft; their inclination sets speed and direction steplessly, with smooth reversal.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Knitting-machine carriage drives need a changed screw or motor speed to vary speed, and reversal is abrupt.

**Solution:** Friction rollers on the carriage are pressed onto a rotating shaft; their inclination sets speed and direction steplessly, with smooth reversal.
