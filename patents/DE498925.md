## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Switching device for type wheels in counters, time stamps or the like. The invention relates to a switching device for the type wheels in counters, time stamps or the like an hour disc. These disks have types on their circumference, namely the minute disk, to denote the minutes, the digits i to 6o (or oo) and the hour disk, to denote the hours, the digits i to 1z or up to ad .. For the operating mode It is necessary that the minute disc, which makes one revolution in steps of 6 ° in one hour, advances the hour wheel in the sixtieth minute .by one step in order to bring the new hourly type into the A b -druckstellung. For this circuit, a drive is generally provided which is carried along in the sixtieth minute by an incision in the minute disc, similar to the gap between two teeth of a gear, with the effect that the hour disc rotates one step further. In counters for any purpose, it is similarly a matter of the fact that after a full revolution of the number wheel of a lower level, for example the one-wheel, the number wheel of the next higher level, such as the tens wheel, is incremented by one digit.
