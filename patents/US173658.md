## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Printing letters with proper proportional spacing according to their widths, adjustable, and using many type varieties without setting forms.

**Solution:** A power-driven machine in which finger-keys engage cam rods on a revolving cylinder to fire a printing hammer and set the spacing, printing from adjustable type carried on a revolving wheel.

**How it works:** A main shaft turned by band power carries a cylinder with longitudinal grooves holding spring-held rods whose beveled ends mate with annular grooves. Fixed rods or bars with triangular cam ends, one per key, are pressed by the finger-keys into contact with projections on the rods as the cylinder revolves, drawing the rods inward. The type-wheel is keyed to the same shaft and carries fixed type or adjustable type carriers. The blow of the printing hammer and the paper movement come from one operation of the power-driven mechanism, with space varied by the cam position. An ink roller slides on a shaft.
