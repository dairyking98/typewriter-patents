**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to devices for actuiating type-bars of the general character disclosed in the United States patents of Edward B.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventors say a flexing type-bar actuating connection does not accelerate the type-bar enough near the end of its travel to the printing point.

**Solution:** The front member of the flexing connection is organized so that the type-bar gains velocity toward the printing point; the connection is flexed by a power applied between its ends.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
