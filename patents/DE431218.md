## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing machine with disks) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes; Machines or apparatus for embossing decorations or marks, e.g. embossing coins having a series of embossing tools each of which can be brought into working position

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an embossing machine of the kind at which the disks carrying the matrices or male molds are coaxial and in parallel Layers are arranged.

**How it works (reading):** Matrix and patrix disks are mounted coaxially on horizontal shafts in vertical planes, so the space between them is free for a sheet of any size, the embossing can be watched, and the disks move together through a gear train.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Horizontal embossing disks stacked close together hide the characters being embossed and limit the sheet size that can be processed.

**Solution:** Matrix and patrix disks are mounted coaxially on horizontal shafts in vertical planes, so the space between them is free for a sheet of any size, the embossing can be watched, and the disks move together through a gear train.
