## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A lighter, more compact and cheaper version of Dement's cylinder and rod type-printing machine.

**Solution:** A revolving disk of radial bars beneath a stationary keyboard plate; a key pin depresses its bar, and cams press the arm onto the matching type in a ring, feed the paper, lift it off and reset.

**How it works:** A keyboard plate has pin holes whose key-pins protrude below. A disk on a vertical shaft carries radial bars with arms overhanging a type ring beneath. Each bar has a cam-pin at a distinct radius, so a key-pin only meets one bar. As the disk turns, a depressed key-pin cams its bar down and a second cam depresses the arm further. A fixed cam then crowds the arm onto the type with the paper between, a following cam pulls the paper one space, and a last cam lifts the arm and bar. A forked spring throws the paper off the type, and inking rollers and milled rings can be added.
