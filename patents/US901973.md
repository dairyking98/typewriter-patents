## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating attachments encumber and spoil the look of the typewriter, the operator needs auxiliary keys to set columns, and column stops are hard to adjust.

**Solution:** A tabulating device fitted beneath the machine conceals it from view; columns are brought into alignment with the numbered typewriter keys, and adjustable, removable column stops and a simple carriage release are accessible to the operator.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)

**Parts:** shift key; shifting and locking device; column stops; tabulating mechanism; carriage release; traveling carriage
