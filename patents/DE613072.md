## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (line counter for register tape punching machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus specially adapted for preparation of record carriers for controlling composing machines

**How it works (reading):** Counting and display members are set by combination rails and work without derailleurs so the pointer shows the exact total width of the line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Register-strip punching machine's line-width counter and pointer display drifts because derailleur-driven counter parts give unwanted movement.

**Solution:** Counting and display members are set by combination rails and work without derailleurs so the pointer shows the exact total width of the line.
