## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods

**How it works (reading):** A low web guides the typebar through a recess on its underside, below the types.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Guides that steady the typebar just before impact are set high and cover the writing.

**Solution:** A low web guides the typebar through a recess on its underside, below the types.

**How it works:** Side guides are joined by a low web. Each typebar has a recess that embraces the web just before it reaches the platen, so the bar cannot spring up and the types stay visible.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** side guide; web; typebar; recess; platen

**Improves on:**

- known typebar guides: guide sits below the types so it does not obscure the writing
