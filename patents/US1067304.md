## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to devices employed in connection with the feed and control of paper in a t-ypewriting machine and one object is to provide a constant line gage to assist the operatorin adjusting the paper.

**How it works (reading):** A constant line gage over the platen, a feed-roll pressure and relief mechanism on the carriage, and a paper clamp.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Solution:** A constant line gage over the platen, a feed-roll pressure and relief mechanism on the carriage, and a paper clamp.
