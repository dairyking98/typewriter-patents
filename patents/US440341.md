**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an improved method of writing tachygraphically and to tachygraphic type-writing machines, and has reference more particularly to that class in which the printing characters print in a straight line across the sheet of paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Too many keys on the keyboard, so that fingers must shift between keys and cannot strike them all with two hands at once.

**Solution:** A tachygraphic typewriter with few keys: characters are grouped into consonant, vowel and terminal sets, and combinations of keys print along a straight line.

**How it works:** Printing characters are secured to a movable bed or plate beneath keybars; the key groups are combined to form further consonants and vowels, and a paper feed and ribbon mechanism complete the machine.
