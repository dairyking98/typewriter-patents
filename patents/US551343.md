## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed has to be reversed by hand.

**Solution:** Attachment that slides a bevel gear shaft to reverse both spools when lugs on a wheel trip a fork.

**How it works:** A sliding shaft between the ribbon shafts carries two bevel wheels, which engage wheels on the spool shafts to turn them one way or the other. A disc with lugs on a post is turned by a worm from the ribbon shaft. The lugs oscillate a fork, whose spring strap slips the sliding shaft endwise to swap the gears and reverse the ribbon.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** ribbon spool; bevel wheel; sliding shaft; lug wheel; worm wheel; fork; spring strap
