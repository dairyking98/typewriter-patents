## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Table writing stop riders needed a simpler and more accurate way to hold their two working and basic positions.

**Solution:** Two rows of riders slide vertically in slots on a rail; round rods pass through the rider slots and are held apart by a tension spring, so the riders snap into the working position and are guided against each other.
