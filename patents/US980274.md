## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters are noisy and need excessive key pressure or deep key travel to get clear impressions.

**Solution:** A toggle-lever arrangement between key-bars and type-bars lets the type-bar ends move only a slight distance against the platen while still delivering adequate impression force.
