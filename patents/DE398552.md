## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (bank posting cash register)

**How it works (reading):** A common amount type-wheel set is positioned by the drive from the depressed key and is inserted into the chosen customer-book column, with two type-carrier groups printing the control strip and the customer book.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Bank and post office cash registers need to register amounts in customer books and cards, and each adding unit needs its own type set.

**Solution:** A common amount type-wheel set is positioned by the drive from the depressed key and is inserted into the chosen customer-book column, with two type-carrier groups printing the control strip and the customer book.
