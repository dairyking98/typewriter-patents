## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Ribbon switching for typewriters and the like on an automatic ribbon switching for typewriters and similar machines a resistance body controlled by the running ink ribbon in the movement path the shift linkage brought and thereby the movement of the ribbon is reversed. Of the known automatic switching devices of this type, in which the Resistance body pivoted on a double lever controlled by the ribbon is, the object of the invention differs in that the resistance body is pivotably mounted on a double lever moved directly from the ribbon and is brought directly into the path of movement of the pawl through this, under the influence of which is inclined and the pawl by toggle action issued a sideways movement. This results in a significant simplification the switching device by only consisting of a double lever and the one located on it Resistance body consists. This simplification ensures greater security in the Establishments.
