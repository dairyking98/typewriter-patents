## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to provide in a type-writer a longitudinally-adjustable work-support, such as a liat platen, or a movable or transversely-slidable book-rest, and also means to eifect such longitudinal Vadjustment, whereby intermediate line-spacing is facilitated.

**How it works (reading):** The platen or book-rest can be moved longitudinally by a separate adjusting device for intermediate spacing, and a retaining means holds the platen in its adjusted position when tilted and releases it when lowered.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A flat-platen typewriter needs intermediate line spacing without disturbing the regular full line-spacing, and the platen can tilt out of its set position.

**Solution:** The platen or book-rest can be moved longitudinally by a separate adjusting device for intermediate spacing, and a retaining means holds the platen in its adjusted position when tilted and releases it when lowered.
