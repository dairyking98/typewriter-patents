## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed and reversing, bell and right margin stop, and swapping platens (hard for manifolding, soft for ordinary writing) needed better mechanisms.

**Solution:** A ribbon feed driven by the carriage spring motor that shifts the spools sideways to reverse, plus a bell and margin-stop attachment and a spring-pin removable roller platen.

**How it works:** Spool shafts at each end of the machine carry spools that slide on the shafts, with a ball in matching half-round grooves to cut friction. The carriage spring motor on one shaft carries a spiral with a groove engaged by a roller on a spool-shifter arm, so carriage motion moves the shifter and reverses the ribbon direction. Separately, a bell mechanism also adjusts the right-hand marginal stop and locks the spacing and keys at line end. The roller platen has end plates with sockets; spring-loaded bearing pins with milled heads in the carriage end caps are withdrawn to swap platens.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [PAP:manifold](../features/pap-manifold.md)
