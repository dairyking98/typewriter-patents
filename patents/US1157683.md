## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** A two-strip rack bar with interlocking tongues forming slideways for stops, ball detents in the rack to hold each stop, and a sliding restoring bar that returns all stops in one movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Tabulator stops on a rack bar must be set precisely, held firmly, and restored together, and the rack must not weaken.

**Solution:** A two-strip rack bar with interlocking tongues forming slideways for stops, ball detents in the rack to hold each stop, and a sliding restoring bar that returns all stops in one movement.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
