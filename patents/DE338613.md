## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** Bearing bodies fully enclose the roll journals, held by a slip-over ring or a snap pawl, so the roll stays put and is exchangeable.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Interchangeable paper-roll bearings open on one side can let the roll shift under vibration.

**Solution:** Bearing bodies fully enclose the roll journals, held by a slip-over ring or a snap pawl, so the roll stays put and is exchangeable.
