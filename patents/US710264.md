## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operating a typewriter electrically by a single button, without a full keyboard.

**Solution:** A rotating insulating cylinder with staggered holes passes under brushes; pressing a single button when the desired hole is under the brushes energises that letter's solenoid.

**How it works:** Each typebar of an ordinary Underwood has a toothed sector connected to a solenoid core; solenoids sit in two staggered arcs. A belt-driven conducting cylinder is sheathed by a perforated insulating cylinder with holes in spiral rows. Brushes, one per solenoid, touch the conducting cylinder only through holes. The operator watches the cylinder and presses one button to complete the battery circuit just as the desired hole passes, firing that solenoid and typebar.
