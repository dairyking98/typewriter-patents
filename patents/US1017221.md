**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (addressing machine ribbon) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means using a reversing-feeler responsive to the tension of the ribbon

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing from plates moved along a conveyor requires a ribbon that is carried correctly under the impression pad and fed from its reel.

**Solution:** A ribbon attached to a reel loosely mounted on a sleeve runs over the printing-plate path beneath the impression pad, with its own feed and movement.

**How it works:** The ribbon passes beneath the impression pad and is moved with the reel and sleeve assembly; the operating arm drives the pad vertically.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Hammer behind the paper](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md)

**Parts:** ribbon reel; sleeve; impression pad; conveyor chain
