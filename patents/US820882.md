## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A book typewriter with adding registers needs the transfer dogs held engaged with the number wheels while a column is computed, and the digits are hard to read.

**Solution:** Numeral keys both print digits and drive the computing registers; the transfer dogs are retained against the number wheels, and a dust guard and magnifying reading-glass sit beside the sight-opening.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
