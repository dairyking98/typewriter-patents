## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**How it works (reading):** A cord of long-fiber asbestos is spun into yarn, twisted into threads and a cord laid inside an abrasion-resistant fabric sheath, forming a rest pad with silent, rebound-free stops.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-lever rest pads made of cotton or coils either damp poorly or let levers rebound.

**Solution:** A cord of long-fiber asbestos is spun into yarn, twisted into threads and a cord laid inside an abrasion-resistant fabric sheath, forming a rest pad with silent, rebound-free stops.
