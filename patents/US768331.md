## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for centralising short lines

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to scale and pointer mechanism for type-writing'machines, the scale and pointer being placed on the upper portion of the keyboard instead of near the platen, as is usually the case.
