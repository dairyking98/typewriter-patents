## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters and calculators vibrate and rattle on the work table, which makes keying difficult.

**Solution:** A bell-shaped rubber foot with a central column and radial webs in one plane with the bell face keeps the air column sealed and stabilises the foot.
