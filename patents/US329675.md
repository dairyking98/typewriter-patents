## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Inking apparatus

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide an improved writing-machine for the blind, whereby writing in embossed characters by puncturing sheets of paper may be performed much more expeditiously than can be done with the usual hand-slate and stiletto and whereby, also, the embossed characters will be formed in the order in which they are read and in accordance with the code of characters usually employed.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing in embossed characters for the blind with hand-slate and stiletto is slow.

**Solution:** Four keys each raise one of four needles grouped in a square, and a chain carries the needle group along so dots are punctured in the standard embossed cell order.

**How it works:** Four flat bars rocked by four keys through rods each lift an arm bearing a writing needle. The arms are carried on a rod by a yoke fixed to an endless chain over two chain wheels, so needles and the sliding needle plate with four square holes move along the line. Springs return the needles and keys. Pressing combinations of keys punctures the paper with the dots of a character.

**Features:** [USE:blind](../features/use-blind.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)

**Parts:** case; flat bar; key; key rod; needle arm; writing needle; yoke; endless chain; chain wheel; needle plate; return spring

**Numbers:** 4 keys; 4 needles in a square

**Improves on:**

- hand-slate and stiletto: keyed needles puncture in reading order and faster
