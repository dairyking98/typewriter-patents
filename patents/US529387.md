## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers on bank books or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and especially to that class of such machines in which the type-carriage has an intermittent feed, while the whole machine when used upon abook is given a separate movement for the line-space.

**How it works (reading):** A book typewriter in which a type-disk is positioned by the differential movement of a single selecting gear driven by key levers, with the whole machine travelling over the book for the line space.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing machines are not suited to writing on bound ledgers and record books, and need accurate letter alignment, a short ribbon and simple carriage return.

**Solution:** A book typewriter in which a type-disk is positioned by the differential movement of a single selecting gear driven by key levers, with the whole machine travelling over the book for the line space.

**How it works:** Key levers produce a differential movement of one selecting gear that turns the type-disk, always in one direction for lower case and in the opposite direction for capitals, with a device that shifts between the two. The same keys give the type-carriage an intermittent letter-space feed. A guide prevents lateral play in the carriage so the alignment is accurate, and the carriage returns automatically at line end. The machine as a whole moves over the page for line spacing, with independently adjustable leaf holders keeping the book flat, and the ribbon is only line length with a limited cross feed at each line end to give fresh ink. A base with a paper cylinder allows use with single sheets.
