## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Derailleur frame for calculating machines The invention relates to a Switchgear frames for calculating machines, in particular for calculating machines with proportional rack and pinion drives. So far, these derailleur frames consisted of a cast frame, which was of considerable weight and required extremely precise machining, to a perfect cooperation in the usual way in the rear derailleur frame mounted derailleur axles and racks and the movement of these links controlling parts. The necessary milling work also meant that several had to be guided and limiting the moving parts serving members independent of the derailleur frame be arranged, whereby the assembly was made considerably more difficult. The purpose of the invention is to obtain a construction through which the production of a rear derailleur frame of extremely low weight despite good and secure guidance and control of the parts used for billing is made possible.
