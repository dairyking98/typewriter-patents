## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Yost typewriter carriage ran with friction and noise, and its feed rack, carriage release and detachable platen carrier needed refinement.

**Solution:** Carry the carriage on three antifriction balls held in a spaced ball-holder that is kept in register by lugs, and feed it with a rack of independently lifting teeth held down by a spring bar.

**How it works:** The carriage rides between front and rear guide bars in V-grooves on two balls at the rear and one at the front, all held in fixed relation by a yoke bar with a transverse arm. Lugs on the carriage and on the rear guide bar nudge the ball-holder back into position on each return so it cannot creep out. The feed rack is a row of loose teeth in slots, kept down by a spring-loaded bar along its length. A holding dog stops the tooth ahead; the space key moves a feeding dog that lifts one tooth past the holding dog so the spring drum advances one space. A release bar lifts all teeth at once for free travel, and teeth with a slot are skipped by it to act as column stops. The platen carrier hinges up on open hooks with keepers, and its release key lever separates at the hinge line.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** guide bar; carriage; antifriction ball; ball-holder yoke; lug; feed rack; feed tooth; holding dog; feeding dog; pressure bar; release bar; platen carrier; keeper; spring drum

**Numbers:** balls spaced several inches apart; platen carrier turns up about 90 degrees; column-stop teeth at positions 15 and 60; optional double spacing
