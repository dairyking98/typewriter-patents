> Description Translated from German Typewriter with removable keys and types. Addendum to patent 36q.887. The invention relates to typewriters with removable keys and types according to patent 364887. The aim of the invention is to create a typewriter of this type in which the keys and types are securely clamped in the machine frame even when they are seated on the machine frame Stop surfaces do not have exactly the same height. This purpose is achieved according to the invention in that of the resilient latches that secure the key and type mechanism in its position of use, one is inevitably connected to a common drive member and the other is connected to a common drive member through the intermediary of a driving clutch that works with play.
