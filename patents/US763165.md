**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to means hy which the extended form of paper-carriage may be adapted to existing forms of the Hammond machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A Hammond paper carriage cannot hold a long roll of wide paper, so the paper feeds unevenly along its length.

**Solution:** An extended carriage with an adjustable intermediate support holds the middle of a long paper roll so it feeds accurately, and can be added to existing Hammond machines.
