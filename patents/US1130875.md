## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing denominational tabulators are bulky, costly, awkward to dismount, cannot tabulate backward, cannot adapt to different letter-space pitches, and their projected stops disengage with difficulty.

**Solution:** A compact sheet-metal tabulator housing holds a row of sliding denominational stops with interchangeable spacing plates, rocking actuators and push rods from the keys, and a clutch that frees the carriage from the escapement.

**How it works:** Pressing a tabulator key pushes a rod and bell crank that rock an actuating member, sliding one denominational stop forward into the path of column stops on the carriage. The same motion draws a universal release bar and link that rock a release frame, withdrawing a spring-loaded clutch section so the escapement wheel shaft is disconnected from the feed pinion. The carriage then runs under its spring drum until a column stop meets the projected denominational stop. On release the springs restore everything and the clutch re-engages. The whole housing can shift slightly sideways so a backward tabulation stops at the same letter space as a forward one, and spacing plates of different thickness set the stop pitch to the carriage's letter feed.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** tabulator housing; denominational stop; column stop; spacing plate; actuating member; push rod; bell crank; tabulator key; release frame; clutch section; escapement wheel; feed pinion; feed rack; spring drum; cover plate

**Numbers:** letter space of one tenth of an inch given as an example; four series of five bearing apertures in the bearing plate; ten denominational keys (two series of five)
