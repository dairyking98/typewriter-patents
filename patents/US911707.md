## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The ink ribbon is inconvenient and gives weaker prints and fewer carbon copies.

**Solution:** Ink the type faces directly with a spring-held swinging inking pad that lies in the path of all type and is knocked aside by each stroke.

**How it works:** A bracket on the frame carries a rod with swinging arms; between the arm ends are rotatable ink wells with sleeve pads fed through holes. The pad sits in the path of the type, which hits it just before reaching the paper and pushes the arms aside. A coiled spring returns them. A second pad of another colour can be swung into use, and a removable stop limits swing to one direction.
