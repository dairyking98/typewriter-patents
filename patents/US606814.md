## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fast key blows give the differential carriage-feeding mechanism momentum so it overshoots its intended travel.

**Solution:** A rocking frame under the key levers with two complementary notched bars so each lever positively limits the frame's movement both ways.

**How it works:** A rectangular frame pivoted in the machine has two transverse notched bars under the key levers. The depth of each notch on the first bar sets the frame's downward movement for that lever; the second bar's notches are complementary (sum constant) and stop the frame and lever at exactly the right point. The frame movement is passed by links and a bell crank to a pawl arm that turns a ratchet, gear and pinions to rack on the carriage. Spring holds the frame up against the levers.
