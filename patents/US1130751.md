## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Heavy paper rolls dragged by the carriage impede its movement.

**Solution:** A paper carrier on the rear frame is connected to the carriage so it moves with it without interfering with carriage movement.

**How it works:** The carrier follows carriage travel through a connection designed to avoid drag from the heavy rolls.

**Features:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
