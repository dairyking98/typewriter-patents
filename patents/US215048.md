## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage spacing with a single sharp step produces shock and limits speed.

**Solution:** A forked detent with two arms offset by half a tooth pitch makes the carriage advance in two half-steps per key stroke.

**How it works:** A rack on the carriage has teeth at letter pitch. A forked detent vibrates transversely with the key, down and up, its two arms alternately engaging the rack so the rack moves half a tooth each phase. The type-bar is pivoted so impact is from momentum.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** rack; forked detent; arm; finger lever; type-bar; paper roller
