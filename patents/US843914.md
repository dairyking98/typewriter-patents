## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type-bars in a full circular arc need wide spacing at the sides for clearance, and extending the arc to get that room makes the machine larger and the types harder to guide at the printing point.

**Solution:** Type-bars pivot on a curved fulcrum rod set skewed in a curved slot, with radial slots in the type-bar hubs, so the bars radiate and overlap at the sides and the types sit at gradually increasing angles and are staggered.

**How it works:** The type-bars are mounted in a segment on a curved fulcrum rod; sub-levers driven by the key-levers pull them up through links to print at the platen.
