## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Condensed billing needs the platen to turn backward more than one full revolution per stroke, but the usual crank limited to under one revolution needs gearing to achieve it.

**Solution:** A crank is guided by a helically slotted drum fixed to the platen frame, so one crank stroke turns the platen more than a full revolution and is connected directly to the platen with no gearing, with adjustable stops limiting the crank throw.
