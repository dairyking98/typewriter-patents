## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink pad removal is blocked because the type bars cannot move far enough away without colliding.

**Solution:** A locking bar or plate holds the type bars away from the ink pad by an individual extent so the pad can be removed.

**Features:** [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)
