## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On typebar machines with four types per bar for two fonts, the order small, large, small, large needs big shift movements between fonts, causing inertia, noise, vibration and bad printing.

**Solution:** Order the four types large and small of font one, then small and large of font two, so each font or case change needs the smallest possible shift, combining platen lift and typebar basket lift.

**How it works:** Type order from the bar end is font-one capitals, font-one lowercase, font-two lowercase, font-two capitals. The platen is lifted by a shift key for capital rows. A second key raises the typebar basket by one type distance to bring font two lowercase into line, and a hook can lock that position; an intermediate lever allows a further one-type step and a direct two-step transition to the top.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift by sliding the element](../features/shift-axial.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md)

**Parts:** typebar; typebar basket; platen; shift key; shift lever; hook; intermediate lever

**Numbers:** 4 types per typebar; basket shift 1 or 2 type distances

**Improves on:**

- machines with two typebar sets for two fonts: halves the typebars by putting both fonts on each bar
