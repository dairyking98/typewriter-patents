## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (watch spring winding protection) · **Subject (patent classes):** Keys or the like with means preventing overwinding

**How it works (reading):** A differential or planetary gear lets a locking hook snap into a winding wheel near full tension and release it on unwinding, limiting overwind without a separate drive.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Winding a watch overstrains the mainspring and gear train, and existing lock and crown-stop devices fail or only suit button-wound clocks.

**Solution:** A differential or planetary gear lets a locking hook snap into a winding wheel near full tension and release it on unwinding, limiting overwind without a separate drive.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)

**Parts:** locking hook; winding wheel; differential gear; mainspring
