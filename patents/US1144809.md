## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stenciling machines need carrier members that can be detached and remain at a uniform heat while in use.

**Solution:** A rotating carrier holds detachable stencil characters and brings each one into position over the work table, with an electric heater kept in a steady relation to the carrier.
