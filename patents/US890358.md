## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper must be inserted and adjusted around the platen while pressure rolls press it against the platen.

**Solution:** A release key rotates a rock shaft that cams down the springs and holds the pressure rolls out of contact with the platen. Releasing them returns the rolls to position.
