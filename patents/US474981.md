## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter needs a simple audible signal when the carriage reaches any chosen point, not only the line end.

**Solution:** A trip pin on an adjustable disk inside the carriage spring drum rotates with the drum and trips a bell hammer at a pre-set carriage position.

**How it works:** The carriage is pulled by a cord from a grooved drum driven by a clock spring, which can be wound by a gear and held by a pawl. A disk with a trip pin sits in a groove of the drum, held by spring fingers, and can be turned from outside by a spindle to set the alarm point. As the drum turns with carriage travel the pin engages a lever that moves a plate carrying a hammer against a gong. Returning the carriage rewinds the spring.
