## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Escapement reeds and levers that release type matrices from magazines can be jammed by a matrix in the wrong position, straining the driving parts.

**Solution:** A yoke or lever drives the escapement reeds through a spring of such strength that it yields when the escapement meets undue resistance, relieving the parts of strain.
