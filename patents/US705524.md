## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Multiple web-feeding apparatus for webs superimposed during printing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to that class of typewriting machines which print upon a sheet in a flat or spread-out condition.

**How it works (reading):** A carbon-roll billing platen carries a roll of carbon or record sheet on a flat platen so the entry is printed on the record while the machine travels over the work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing on a flat platen needs a record sheet with carbon-copy impressions under the work.

**Solution:** A carbon-roll billing platen carries a roll of carbon or record sheet on a flat platen so the entry is printed on the record while the machine travels over the work.
