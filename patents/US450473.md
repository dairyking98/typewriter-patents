## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to certain improvements, as hereinafter claimed, in that class of devices for cleaning the faces of the types of type-writing machines in which the cleaning devices are carried by and partake of the movements of the paper-carriage of the inachines.

**How it works (reading):** A brush facing on a springy steel back has hook-supports at the ends that hang on the carriage paper-guide rods, in position to be struck by the type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage-mounted type cleaner is complicated and hard to attach or remove.

**Solution:** A brush facing on a springy steel back has hook-supports at the ends that hang on the carriage paper-guide rods, in position to be struck by the type.

**How it works:** Springy backed cleaner hangs from the carriage paper-guide rods.
