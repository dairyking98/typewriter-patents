**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a plunger for typewriters and consists in that the plunger two or more pressure surfaces made of different hard or soft or elastic Possesses material and is reversible or adjustable such that each of the pressure surfaces can be positioned as desired.

**How it works (reading):** The platen pressure abutment has two pressure faces of different hardness (hard metal and soft rubber insert) and is reversible or adjustable on its support arms so either face can be brought to the type.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A single platen pressure surface cannot suit every paper type and number of copies.

**Solution:** The platen pressure abutment has two pressure faces of different hardness (hard metal and soft rubber insert) and is reversible or adjustable on its support arms so either face can be brought to the type.
