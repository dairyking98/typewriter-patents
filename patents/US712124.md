## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil cutting apparatus) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that class of apparatus in which a series of tools each adapted to cut or punch or cut out a separate letter or character is carried by the head or carrier actuated to bring the tools into operation as desired;

**How it works (reading):** A series of tools, each cutting one letter or character, is carried by a head or carrier, with a feed for the bed-strip and stencil-blank. The rest of the mechanism is not in the first 3000 chars.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cutting stencils with a fixed series of letter tools needs accuracy in bringing each tool into operation, and the bed-strip and stencil-blank must be fed accurately.

**Solution:** A series of tools, each cutting one letter or character, is carried by a head or carrier, with a feed for the bed-strip and stencil-blank. The rest of the mechanism is not in the first 3000 chars.

**Features:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
