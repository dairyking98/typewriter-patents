## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typebar machine with several types per bar needs a way to bring each type to the print point and keep writing in plain sight.

**Solution:** Typebars with three types each strike down onto a platen that is shifted forward or back by two shift key-levers and sprung back to a centre position, with a ribbon guide that lifts to expose the print line.

**How it works:** Typebars, each with three types, sit in two rows of different length in an arc behind the platen and strike down on a common print point on top of the platen. Two shift key-levers act on a shifting bar that moves the platen frame forward or back from its centre position so a different type on the bar lands at the print point. One flat spring pressed by projections of both shift levers returns the platen to centre and acts as a stop to hold it there. A universal bar worked by every key rocks a ribbon guide toward the platen during the stroke and away after, and also drives the ribbon feed pawl and the letter-space pawl on the carriage rack. A spacing key and friction rollers on the paper frame complete the carriage.
