**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices for justifying

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to matrix-making or composing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A matrix-making machine with a keyboard acting on individually moved dies to form matrices for a linotype composing machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Matrix production needs a keyboard-driven mechanism for the linotype.

**Solution:** A matrix-making machine with a keyboard acting on individually moved dies to form matrices for a linotype composing machine.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
