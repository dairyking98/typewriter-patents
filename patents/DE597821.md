## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**How it works (reading):** Lead-in plates with resilient tongues on the paper table are adjustable by a screw to the platen and feed roller gap for the thickness of the paper layers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Forms need to sit at a fixed start position so each platen rotation starts without wasted travel, but the guide plates are hard to set for varying paper thickness.

**Solution:** Lead-in plates with resilient tongues on the paper table are adjustable by a screw to the platen and feed roller gap for the thickness of the paper layers.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)
