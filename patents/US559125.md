## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing maehines of that class in which the paper-carriage, as it is advanced step by step to bring the paper into position for successive action of the types thereon, is moved through varying distances to permit the use of types having varying widths of faces whether bearing single letters or groups of letters.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebars with types of different widths (single letters or letter groups) need the carriage to advance by a distance matched to each type, on Smith Premier style rock-shaft machines.

**Solution:** A shaft geared to the carriage rack carries a fixed and a loose ratchet, and the key levers arrest the shaft after varying distances to give variable carriage feed.

**How it works:** The carriage spring drives the carriage, whose rack turns a pinion on a horizontal shaft. A ratchet is fixed on the shaft and a second, loose ratchet sits beside it with a coil spring between them tending to turn it forward. Key levers, through rock-shafts and cams, engage the ratchets so the shaft is stopped after a travel proportional to the width of the type actuated. This adapts an earlier variable-feed device (Serial 496,570) to machines whose keys act on type-bars via rock-shafts.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)
