## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cleaning arrangements

**How it works (reading):** A hole in the handle of the spatula lets the finger hold it so each soiled type can be lifted and pressed onto the adhesive layer.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cleaning type with a sticky mass is awkward and touches the adhesive by hand.

**Solution:** A hole in the handle of the spatula lets the finger hold it so each soiled type can be lifted and pressed onto the adhesive layer.
