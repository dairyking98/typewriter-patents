## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing addresses on stencil cards or sheets needs the carriage stopped at different starting points on successive lines.

**Solution:** A pawl on the carriage and a stepped stop on the frame arrest the carriage at progressively different return positions automatically.

**How it works:** The carriage is propelled by a spring drum and band, moving by escapement step by step. A bracket on the carriage carries a pivoted sheet-metal pawl with an engaging nose. A plate on the top plate carries the cooperating stop, so each carriage return is arrested at a position progressively nearer the center. Described on a Remington machine with needle-faced types for stencil cards, and designed as an attachment.

**Features:** [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** carriage; spring drum; band; pawl; bracket; stop plate; guide rail; hopper
