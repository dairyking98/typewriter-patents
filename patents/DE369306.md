## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage play and the impact of type strikes on the platen make the carriage run roughly, wear the platen and types, and make noise.

**Solution:** The carriage hangs on a thin leaf spring running on rollers in a prismatic rail, so it glides without play, and a felt cushion under the carriage frame damps the impact of the types against the platen.

**Numbers:** leaf spring 8 to 10 mm wide
