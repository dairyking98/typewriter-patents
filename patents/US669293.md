## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A character can be written on top of another at the end of a predetermined line.

**Solution:** A line-locking mechanism, improved from an earlier patent, uses a locking bail on the carriage to stop the keys from printing at the set line end, and releases the carriage in a carriage-releasing position.
