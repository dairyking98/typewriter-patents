## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changeable type blocks must be locked tight and reversible to switch alphabets.

**Solution:** Type blocks are reversed on the bars to bring other faces into use, and a spring latch with a tooth holds each block against a shoulder.
