## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The inventor's earlier three-position grasshopper typebar machine needed more durable, adjustable parts for the shifting carriage track, carriage locking, paper feed and typebar bearings.

**Solution:** Refinements to a Williams-type machine whose paper carriage is shifted forward and back from a centre position on a rocking ball-bearing track, so each typebar can print one of three characters.

**How it works:** The carriage rides on a ball-bearing girder track carried on rock-arms with loose hemispherical-head pins and springs, and is shifted by a double-arm lever and a sliding bar adjustable through threaded bushings. A coiled centering spring returns the track to the middle, and two threaded bolts can lock carriage and track for transport. A spring-loaded grooved roller holds the carriage to the track and takes up backlash. The platen is removable and turned by a clutch rod with a spring; a hand lever pawls a small ratchet on a pressure roller for line feed, with an adjustable cam finger to limit the feed stroke and a positive stop finger. Typebars have spring-rod bearings adjusted by a double-thread nut, and a slotted plate replaces the guide comb.
