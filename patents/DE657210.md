## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage-release mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Rotary wedges on a ratchet cylinder engage a clutch wheel, and the wedges sit against a spring-set centre position so no abutment is needed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Resilient pawls for noiseless carriage return fail to engage reliably, so the first letter of a line prints in the wrong position.

**Solution:** Rotary wedges on a ratchet cylinder engage a clutch wheel, and the wedges sit against a spring-set centre position so no abutment is needed.

**How it works:** The wedges swing against a spring-set position and only the one wedge engages the clutch tooth, so the pitch avoids abutment.
