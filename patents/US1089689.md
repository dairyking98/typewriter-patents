## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Print quality control characterised by dot disposition, e.g. for reducing white stripes or banding

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the present invention is the provision of keys so disposed as to be comfortably manipulated by both hands of the operator, there being preferably three series of keys to each hand and six keys to a series, while to provide extra means for operating the different mechanisms of the typewriting machine, wrist actuated keys are also provided one for each hand.

**How it works (reading):** Three series of six keys per hand, plus wrist keys, with a selector so combinations of six keys select the full alphabet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboards need keys placed for both hands and a way to control a full alphabet with fewer keys.

**Solution:** Three series of six keys per hand, plus wrist keys, with a selector so combinations of six keys select the full alphabet.
