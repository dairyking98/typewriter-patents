## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wanted a typebar machine whose impression is on the top of the platen roller in plain sight of the operator.

**Solution:** Typebars arranged so they strike the paper on the upper side of the roller, with a dog escapement stepping the paper roller across.

**How it works:** A frame carries a toothed-pin roller and a rod supporting a bent frame that holds a paper roller, which is turned by a knob and held by a detent spring against dents. A fixed dog and a sliding spring-loaded dog on the frame engage pins on the toothed roller in sequence as the paper roller is pushed sideways, giving a step-by-step escapement. Typebars rise through travel positions and strike the paper on the roller top.
