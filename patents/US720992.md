## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon movement needs a simple step-by-step feed and a reversing device.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools with ratchet wheels driven by gravity dogs from a rock shaft, shifted by a sliding bar to reverse the feed.

**How it works:** Each spool is spring-held against a collar and keyed to its ratchet wheel by a pin, so it can be pulled off. A weighted gravity dog per wheel is linked to arms of a rock shaft rocked by every key or the space key. A sliding bar with finger pieces shifts which dog engages, reversing the ribbon direction. A guide plate lifts the ribbon to the paper; a plate shifts the ribbon for upper and lower case.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; spool; ratchet wheel; gravity dog; rock shaft; shifting bar; spring; guide plate
