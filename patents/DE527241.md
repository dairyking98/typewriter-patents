This training has the disadvantage that as a result of the subdivision of the Guide rollers especially thin paper, e.g. B. wax matrices, not without damage can be passed through the paper guide by wrinkling. It showed rather, that a flawlessly smooth implementation of such paper is only possible with Use of continuously continuous guide rollers is achievable.
