## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers or sets of front or rear rollers each mounted on a separate carrier

**How it works (reading):** Guide rollers are made continuous with hard inserts at the points where the pressure levers act.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Split paper-guide rollers wrinkle or damage thin paper such as wax matrices where pressure levers press on them.

**Solution:** Guide rollers are made continuous with hard inserts at the points where the pressure levers act.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

It showed rather, that a flawlessly smooth implementation of such paper is only possible with Use of continuously continuous guide rollers is achievable.
