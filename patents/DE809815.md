## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to the type lever drive with Segment switching provided writing, booking and the like, flowering machines, in which the Swinging movement of a struck key lever pushes the type lever over an intermediate one The angle lever with the connecting rods hinged to it swings into the stop position.

**How it works (reading):** An angle lever on a horizontal axis near the segment front, with its legs cranked so the eccentric tensile force is stepped, shortens and balances the rear connecting rods so the type head meets the platen smoothly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Outward key levers are hard to operate and the type lever drags on the platen, because the long angle-lever legs and the unequal connecting rods load the linkage obliquely and need about 44 different rods.

**Solution:** An angle lever on a horizontal axis near the segment front, with its legs cranked so the eccentric tensile force is stepped, shortens and balances the rear connecting rods so the type head meets the platen smoothly.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
