## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The carriage can run on too far when released, so the retarding of its travel needs control when blanks or invoices are written.

**Solution:** A retarding dog engages the spacing detent on the carriage and, when the release arm is depressed, the feed pawl is withdrawn from the rack so the carriage travel is restrained; the text is heavily garbled after the opening.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** retarding dog; release arm; release key; feed pawl; rack; spacing detent; scale; indicator; escapement
