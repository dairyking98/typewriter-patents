> Description Translated from German Cleaning preparations for printing types, in particular for typewriters and the like. The invention relates to cleaning agents for printing types, in particular typewriter types, possibly also for speaking machine plates and the like Dust, dirt, printing ink, etc. consist binding mass, which on a suitable Base layer made of woven material, paper, leather, artificial leather or the like. Is applied and by pressing the agent onto the areas to be cleaned, they are cleaned. The invention essentially consists of known means of this type in that the effective mass is only available in a thin coat of paint or the like on the base course applied and the latter backed with a velvet or brush-like pressure layer is that a penetration of the cleaning compound into the pores of the area to be cleaned brings about. Through the training and use of the agent according to the invention the mass production of the agent itself is cheaper and simplified, the application designed more cleanly and enables new pieces to be used for every cleaning job of the detergent.
