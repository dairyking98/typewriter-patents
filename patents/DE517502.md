**How it works (reading):** A thin layer of cleaning compound is bonded to a woven or paper base and backed by a velvet or brush-like pressure layer, so the compound is pressed into the type face and the strip can be replaced for each job.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known cleaning agents for printing types are thick and costly, and the soft cleaning mass wastes material and is hard to apply cleanly.

**Solution:** A thin layer of cleaning compound is bonded to a woven or paper base and backed by a velvet or brush-like pressure layer, so the compound is pressed into the type face and the strip can be replaced for each job.
