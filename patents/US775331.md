## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices actuated when platen reaches the end of a line

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writers and other machines in which key-levers are employed, and has particular reference to mechanism used for the purpose of locking the keylevers at a predetermined 'pointfor instance, when the end of a line is reached.

**How it works (reading):** A locking rod carried on two arms with forks that loosely embrace a support is moved under hooks on the key-levers, blocking their downward movement at a set point such as the end of the line.
