## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Extensible tables with supplementary leaves or drop leaves arranged at the periphery

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting, adding machine and other machine stands.

**How it works (reading):** A self-contained leaf attached to the stand with a tilting copy holder sustained by a support that engages when raised, and a line-indicating finger operated by a handle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter or adding machine stand needs a leaf that is easily raised from idle to working position and carries a tilting copy holder. This is furniture, not a typewriter mechanism.

**Solution:** A self-contained leaf attached to the stand with a tilting copy holder sustained by a support that engages when raised, and a line-indicating finger operated by a handle.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
