## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-spacing on a folding portable (Corona) typewriter needs a simple finger lever that spaces one or more lines evenly, holds the platen positively, lets the whole platen surface be used, and releases for free manual turning.

**Solution:** A finger lever on the carriage that both pivots and slides sideways on a bolt carries a fixed pawl that rotates the platen ratchet, with a notched stop lever to select a one- or two-line throw.

**How it works:** A line-space lever is mounted on a transverse bolt above the platen shaft, opposite a fixed gripping part on the carriage frame. Squeezing the lever toward the fixed part both swings it and slides it sideways and downward so its pawl engages the platen ratchet wheel and turns the platen; the same grip also lets the carriage be pulled back against its spring. A spring on the bolt returns the lever. A pivoted stop lever with two notches limits the lever's travel, giving a short or long throw (one or more lines), and a detent on it clicks into recesses in the carriage frame. A spring-loaded roll detent holds the ratchet, and a separate release lever lifts the roll so the platen can be turned freely either way; the large ratchet teeth let the platen be shifted independently and re-engaged so the whole platen surface is used.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
