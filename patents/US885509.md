## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the keyboards of typewriting and analogous machines.

**How it works (reading):** A removable-key lock on the Underwood frame has a bolt that locks the key levers against operation.

**Solution:** A removable-key lock on the Underwood frame has a bolt that locks the key levers against operation.
