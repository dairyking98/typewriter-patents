## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to provide means, in connection with a typewriter or other writing-machine, for facilitating the positioning and stopping of the traveling-carriage thereof at predetermined points, as for tabulating, or other like work.
