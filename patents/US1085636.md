## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A typewriter cipher needs to change the key-to-character relation unpredictably but reversibly.

**Solution:** Interchangeable paired revolving disks attached to a machine automatically shift the relation of the type wheels' characters to the keys, one disk enciphering and the other deciphering.

**How it works:** Type wheel character forms carry characters arranged arbitrarily relative to a zero point and the fixed key set. Pressing a key prints a different character. Attached disks with spaced portions step the characters relative to the keys, in one direction to encipher and the reverse to decipher.
