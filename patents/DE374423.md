## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pliers used to lift the retaining rings (cups) off typewriter keys crush the thin sheet-metal rings so they cannot be reused.

**Solution:** A hand tool with several spring-loaded gripper claws arranged in a circle around a push-off punch; an adjusting ring sets the grip width to the key ring size, and the punch pushes the key out while the claws hold the ring.
