## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Impressing matrices needs ease and speed; a pin must be freed from the screw thread on return of the cylinder.

**Solution:** A matrix machine in which a rack turns a grooved cylinder and a pin, pivoted to the matrix cylinder, is engaged with its screw thread to advance it, then disengaged for free return.

**How it works:** A bar tilts the pin so it trails across the thread on the return stroke; a rack turns the grooved cylinder, and the matrix cylinder is moved lengthwise by the screw thread.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md)
