## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a paper holding device for typewriters, in particular one that in which the paper roller frame with a swingably arranged, with pressure roller provided paper holder rod is provided, which around its pivot point against the Paper roller in the use position and away from this in the out of use position can be swung, with a pawl the holder rod in the out of use position locks.

**How it works (reading):** A gravity-acting latch that is part of the rod linkage locks the rod out of use; a light key touch releases it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper holder rod must be forced against spring tension, which is tiring to operate.

**Solution:** A gravity-acting latch that is part of the rod linkage locks the rod out of use; a light key touch releases it.
