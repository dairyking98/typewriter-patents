## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators must take a hand off the keyboard to line-space and return the carriage.

**Solution:** A foot treadle, connected by cord over a double pulley that multiplies its motion, tilts the carriage line-space lever.

**How it works:** A treadle spring-held up is corded to the small pulley of a stepped double pulley (the large one about twice the diameter). A second cord from the large pulley pulls a bell-crank on the carriage that tilts the line-space lever, turning the roller one line and releasing the feed so the carriage can be shifted. A weight keeps the cord taut.

**Features:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** treadle; spring; cord; double pulley; bell-crank lever; line-space lever; weight; stand

**Numbers:** large pulley about twice the small one
