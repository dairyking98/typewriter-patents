## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Characters engraved in steel stamps on the embossing discs can only be read from above, so the upper disc cannot easily serve as the setting disc.

**Solution:** Characters are embossed into thin sheet-steel discs so they read correctly from the back, letting the upper disc be used as a setting disc while the strip is embossed between the paired discs.
