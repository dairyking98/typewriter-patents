## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The patent concerns improvements to the paper carriage motion and platen turning of a typebar typewriter.

**Solution:** A carriage with rack and spring pawls plus thumb-lever release, on a typebar machine whose sliding typebars converge on one point.

**How it works:** Key levers on springs connect by rods to angle levers, whose long arms drive sliding type rods guided in square openings in plates so that they converge and strike at one point. The paper carriage frame runs on a cross rail and grooved rollers, held down by hooks and screws, and carries a toothed rack. Two spring pawls fixed to a cross bar stop the carriage moving right while allowing it to move left; a thumb lever with a key lifts one pawl to release it. Further parts cover the ribbon holder with reels, line spacing and a bell with latch. The remainder is cut off.
