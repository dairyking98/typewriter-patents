## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In book typewriters the keyboard travels with the writing mechanism, so the operator must reach over all points of the page.

**Solution:** Keep the keyboard fixed and connect it to the moving typebar carriage by a flexible cable of insulated conductors, with an electromagnet operating each typebar.

**How it works:** On a table, track rails carry a carriage frame that slides along them, and a writing carriage moves across it for letter spacing, over a flat platen or a book page. Typebars on the carriage are each moved by an electromagnet whose armature acts through a link on the bar, with a retractile spring to return it. Each key on the stationary keyboard closes contact between two terminals, completing a battery circuit through one magnet via a flexible multi-conductor cable. A weighted slack take-up and a tie-bar with a pulley carry the cable as the carriage moves.
