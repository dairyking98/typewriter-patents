## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dye applied to ribbon by sponge is uncontrolled, too strong or too weak.

**Solution:** The ribbon runs under pressure between rollers and an elastic hollow dye pad with outlet openings; an adjustable regulator sets the amount of dye emerging.
