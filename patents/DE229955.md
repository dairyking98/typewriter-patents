**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a new, similar to the known Devices simpler device ίο for automatic longitudinal displacement of the paper roller for the purpose of creating the line spacing.

**How it works (reading):** A step-by-step rotation of the platen during writing drives an auxiliary shaft; after one revolution an inhibiting device lets a spring push the spring-loaded hollow roller along its axis by one line gap, without interrupting the work.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier typewriters shifted the paper roller axially by one line spacing at each line end, and the inventor wants a simpler automatic version of this.

**Solution:** A step-by-step rotation of the platen during writing drives an auxiliary shaft; after one revolution an inhibiting device lets a spring push the spring-loaded hollow roller along its axis by one line gap, without interrupting the work.
