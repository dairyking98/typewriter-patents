## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a Hammond typewriter, turning the paper back several lines to fill in an address precisely is hard because the feed ratchet has two clicks per line space.

**Solution:** A graduated knob with indicator lines for each line space and an adjustable friction-tight pointer on the paper-rest arm.

**How it works:** The feed-roll knob has indicator lines around its periphery, one per line space. A pointer fixed on a prolongation of the paper-rest side arm lies near the knob; counting lines passing the pointer shows how many line spaces the paper has moved. Because the ratchet has two teeth per double space, the pointer might sit between lines, so it is adjusted by swinging the paper-rest forward until it lies over an indicator line (or friction-fits on an axis). The operator releases the feed rolls from the pawl with one hand and turns the knob with the other.
