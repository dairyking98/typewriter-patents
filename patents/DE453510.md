## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** A Johnsen-Rahbek relay (a rotating semiconductor roller with a conductive tape and a voltage source) is connected to a frame that mechanically couples the levers pressed by a key, so small control currents move the type levers with large force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operating keys and type levers by hand needs full force, and earlier electromagnetic drives are costly and slow because of self-induction and hysteresis.

**Solution:** A Johnsen-Rahbek relay (a rotating semiconductor roller with a conductive tape and a voltage source) is connected to a frame that mechanically couples the levers pressed by a key, so small control currents move the type levers with large force.
