## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more especially to paper suplply devices for typewriting machines an has forflits main object to provide an improved roll holder and supports therefor.

**How it works (reading):** A roll holder with supporting brackets on the platen carrier carries the tag strip roll, with a spring-loaded detent on the line-space ratchet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A roll of tag strip needs a holder that feeds without fouling the carriage.

**Solution:** A roll holder with supporting brackets on the platen carrier carries the tag strip roll, with a spring-loaded detent on the line-space ratchet.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
