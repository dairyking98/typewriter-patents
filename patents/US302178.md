## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wagner seeks a typewriter in which the type are struck by a hammer rather than by the operator's touch; the opening only introduces the machine.

**Solution:** A rectangular type segment on a shaft is shifted sideways and rocked to bring one of its spring-mounted types under a hammer, with the hammer lever also driving the paper-carriage feed.

**How it works:** A rectangular type segment is fixed to a horizontal shaft that can slide lengthwise and rock. Each type sits face down on a flat spring, in two rows, under holes in the segment. Lateral motion of the shaft picks the row and rocking picks the character, then a hammer passing through the holes drives the chosen type down onto the paper. Cams on bolsters, carried by springs and acted on by levers on a rock-shaft, give the shaft its compound motion; one set of levers works the hammer and another the carriage feed.

**Features:** [ELEM:sector-segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md), [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [IMP:spring-energy](../features/imp-spring-energy.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md)

**Parts:** type segment; type spring; hammer; shaft; sliding cam; bolster; rock-shaft; lever; feed wheel; paper carriage

**Numbers:** two rows of types; 6 sheets of drawings
