## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing the same character repeatedly requires repeated key presses.

**Solution:** A make-and-break contact driven by the hammer lets a held key repeat the print.

**How it works:** In a Hammond, a key raises an adjusting pin and shuttle position; a circuit via abutments and an electromagnet hammer repeatedly strikes while the key is held, lifting the ribbon with a rod.

**Features:** [ELEM:sector-segment](../features/elem-sector-segment.md), [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** key; adjusting pin; shuttle; hammer; electromagnet; abutment; ribbon rod
