## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Caligraph carriage runs with sliding friction and its racks flex.

**Solution:** A roller on a rail carries the rack at the back, with a flexible chain link and adjustable spring take-up to the driving arm.

**How it works:** The lower carriage rod is removed; a stud on the rack bears a roller rolling on a rail on the top plate, and an arm with a lug under the rail prevents lift. A chain link and spring link from the driving arm adjusts the drive force, and the pull lifts the rack to relieve load on the upper rod.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** rail; carriage rod; yoke; double rack; stud; roller; arm; chain link; spring link; driving arm

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph carriage: rolling instead of sliding friction on rear support
