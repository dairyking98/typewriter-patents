## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A complete typebar typewriter combining the inventor's several pending improvements.

**Solution:** A full machine of angle-iron base with key-levers and spacing-levers on a rod, type-carrying bars linked by wires, a carriage on a graduated rail, a ribbon device, a word counter and a tilting device.

**How it works:** Key-levers and spacing-levers pivot on a rod at the rear and are held up by springs on the type-bars and by spring-supported frames. Wires connect the key-levers to type-carrying levers. The carriage is secured to a graduated rail by loops. A ribbon device with spool dogs, cam block and shifting rod moves the ribbon, and a rod holds it back from the platen. A counting attachment indicates words written, and a separate device tilts the whole machine.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** base rail; key-lever; spacing-lever; type-bar; connecting wire; carriage; graduated rail; ribbon spool; cam block; word counter; tilting device

**Numbers:** 7 sheets of drawings
