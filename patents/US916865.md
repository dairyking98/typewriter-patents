## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates is therefore a visible writing machine in which, at the moment of impact of the type against the platen, the ribbon is interposed to effect an impression upon the paper and immediately thereafter it moves away from the rinting point to expose to view what has been printed.

**How it works (reading):** The universal bar is given an adjustable range of travel, which sets how far the ribbon vibrator lifts the ribbon.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a visible front-strike typebar machine with a two-zone ribbon, the operator needs to choose which ribbon zone prints, or to stop the ribbon reaching the print point for stencil cutting.

**Solution:** The universal bar is given an adjustable range of travel, which sets how far the ribbon vibrator lifts the ribbon.

**How it works:** The universal bar, operated by every key lever and also driving the carriage escapement, is connected to a ribbon vibrating lever. Headed bolts on the bar's arms sit in cam-like slots with three resting surfaces. Position 1 gives a small travel so the lower ribbon zone prints, position 2 a larger travel for the top zone, and position 3 leaves the vibrator at rest for stencil work. A rod through the side plate, held by a spring in three notches, shifts the setting.
