## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage feed on typewriters could stick or jump, and the carriage needed an automatic release and return.

**Solution:** A rocking frame depressed by the key levers carries a spring-pressed actuating pawl that pushes the rack forward one tooth on its return, with a locking pawl against retrograde motion and a shift key that trips both pawls to let the spring-barrel return the carriage.

**How it works:** Pressing any key lever lowers a cross-bar and a rocking frame, which momentarily lifts the actuating pawl off the rack. On release, springs return the frame and the pawl engages the next tooth and pushes the rack and carriage forward one tooth space. A locking pawl pivoted on the machine frame holds the rack against backward movement while the actuating pawl re-engages. Depressing the shift key raises a rod that trips the locking pawl, which in turn trips the actuating pawl, freeing the carriage; a tape and spring barrel return it, and a flat spring holds the locking pawl out until the returning rack releases it.
