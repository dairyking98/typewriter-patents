**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing in Braille or with keyboards specially adapted for use by blind or disabled persons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to braille machines for Blind people who have an unwinding strip of paper to hold braille characters is determined.

**How it works (reading):** Two or more cutting pins on a braille machine cut notches in the paper strip's edges on a finger press, giving a tactile overview of changes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Blind writers could not mark notes or changes on the outside edge of the braille paper strip while dictating.

**Solution:** Two or more cutting pins on a braille machine cut notches in the paper strip's edges on a finger press, giving a tactile overview of changes.
