## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Billing machines need aligned items and a running tally strip of totals across separate bills.

**Solution:** An adjustable gauged tablet over the platen plus auxiliary pressure rolls for a tally strip, alternated with the main rolls by one release key.

**How it works:** A tablet with gauges for the leading and side paper edges is mounted over the platen, adjustable along and across it. The release key casts off main pressure rolls and brings in auxiliary rolls that feed a paper ribbon from a coil to a spool, one line per total. Releasing the key restores the main rolls. A shaft with two cam sets moved endwise can instead release both sets together.
