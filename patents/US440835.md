## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stencil cutter needs a punch that cuts cleanly with little power.

**Solution:** A stencil cutter whose punch shape cuts the material easily; flat dovetailed steel dies carrying the character shapes are slipped into sockets on a bed plate, and a punch plate with spring-loaded shanks presses the punches through.

**How it works:** The bed plate turns on a standard and carries the dovetailed dies; the punch-carrying plate sits on it and is held by pins, and the shanks are pressed down against springs.
