## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters use a single typeface and colour whereas printing offers many; documents with many typefaces and colours are wanted.

**Solution:** A circular-segment carrier holds a row of radial pushers, under which interchangeable type boxes of different fonts slide in, striking through a ribbon of different coloured strips.

**How it works:** A circular segment-shaped carrier between two pillars holds one radial pusher per character of a font. Type boxes, each holding a full set of radially arranged type rods in one typeface or character set, are slid in along horizontal slideways under the pushers. A multicoloured ribbon of strips, moved only lengthwise, runs under the carrier, and the types of the chosen box print on a particular colour zone. Springs around the pushers and type rods lift them back after the blow. Carriage, ribbon and pusher movement use usual means.
