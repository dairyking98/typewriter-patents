## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a release device for the rack in typewriters, in which the paper roller is mounted in a switch frame that can be raised and lowered.

**How it works (reading):** The rack rides in slot guides of levers, and a curved thumb lever is actuated by a release lever on the raising frame, so the rack disengages without adding height.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A release lever on a raising paper frame would add to machine height if it had to reach the highest frame position.

**Solution:** The rack rides in slot guides of levers, and a curved thumb lever is actuated by a release lever on the raising frame, so the rack disengages without adding height.
