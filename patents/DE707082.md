## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (booking machine with forward stops) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front lays, stops, or gauges

**How it works (reading):** Control members engage the straps through holes and move the adjustable stops evenly with a common actuator, and pressing means keep the straps tight on the reels.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stops on flexible straps give uneven feed unless the tapes are held evenly, so sheets skew.

**Solution:** Control members engage the straps through holes and move the adjustable stops evenly with a common actuator, and pressing means keep the straps tight on the reels.
