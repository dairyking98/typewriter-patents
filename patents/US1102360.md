**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an indicating device adapted for use with a typewriting machine to indicate to the operator when the writing nears the lower edge of the worksheet and when the age has been completed with any predetermined bottom margin.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators are not warned when typing nears the bottom of the sheet, and indicators need resetting for sheets of different lengths.

**Solution:** A friction-driven pointer on a dial, held by a lever resting on the sheet, starts moving only when the sheet's lower edge passes the lever, so the bottom margin is uniform.

**How it works:** Platen rotation drives a spur gear, a second gear and a crown gear. A friction disk with a pointer rests on the crown gear and is held by a stop lever whose arm bears on the paper through a slot in the paper shelf. When the paper bottom edge passes the slot the lever releases the disk, the pointer moves over the dial and warns the operator. A spring returns the lever.
