## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** A pliable sheet clamped with the paper pack carries an edge reinforcement that stops the roller at the desired length; the roller runs empty, signalling the writer, and the pack can still be pulled through by hand.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A writing limit stop on a paper pack forces unclamping the pack to move it, and it blocks the paper completely even when only a line or two is wanted.

**Solution:** A pliable sheet clamped with the paper pack carries an edge reinforcement that stops the roller at the desired length; the roller runs empty, signalling the writer, and the pack can still be pulled through by hand.
