## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line spacing on a carriage return had to be one fixed amount.

**Solution:** A pivoted line-spacing lever whose stroke is limited by an adjustable regulating lever sets one or more line spaces, with one spring doing several jobs.

**How it works:** A bracket on the carriage end plate carries the line spacing lever with a thumb piece and pawl engaging a platen ratchet. A restraining pawl with a roller is spring-pressed against the ratchet to stop free spin. The regulating lever shifts between positions that limit the pawl travel to one or two lines. One spring keeps the lever in its position and holds the pawl set.
