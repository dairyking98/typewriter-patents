## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriters have tall type-lever baskets and long key travel.

**Solution:** Flat connecting rods and intermediate levers with an upward bend let the keyboard sit lower while the type-lever segment stays vertical.
