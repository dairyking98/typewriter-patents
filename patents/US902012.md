## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Fractional line spacing is not available on the platen roller and needs a means of providing it.

**Solution:** A spindle through the platen roller end carries a finger wheel and a line spacing wheel, with a locking dog and locking parts that cooperate with the platen roller, so the platen can be turned by fractions of a line.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen roller; wooden cylinder; rubber sheath; end piece; sleeve; spindle; finger wheel; line spacing wheel; locking dog; platen frame end bar
