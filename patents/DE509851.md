## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A cash register adding machine cannot guide two paper strips and the ribbon cleanly through the printing unit at the back of its housing.

**Solution:** A U-shaped frame fixed to the back of the housing carries a supply roll for the receipt strip and a bracket that holds the ribbon path.
