## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines need a simple, cheap arrangement for operating carriage switching and ribbon lift from the key levers.

**Solution:** Multi-armed intermediate levers whose arms are the sole adjusting members for the type lever and for two universal rails, one for carriage escapement and one for ribbon lift.

**How it works:** Each key lever pushes a pin on one arm of an angle intermediate lever held by a spring. A second arm of the intermediate lever connects via a tie rod to a type lever; a third arm strikes the universal rail, which through a pull rod steps the paper carriage. The rail also carries a loose additional rail that lifts the ribbon. For dead keys the third arm is shortened so it misses the main rail and hits only the additional rail, lifting the ribbon without spacing. The arm can be bent from below to adjust timing.
