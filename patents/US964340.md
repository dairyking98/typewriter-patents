## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The standard QWERTY keyboard layout does not match the natural resting position of the thumbs and fingers, and shift and space keys are awkward to reach.

**Solution:** A keyboard whose alphabet runs serially outward from the centre in groups of four letters per finger, with thumb-operated Capital, Sign, Space, Back space and Margin keys.

**How it works:** Three banks of keys per hand carry the alphabet in order from the centre line, four letters to each bank row so each finger rests on one row. Extra keys between the banks carry numerals, accented letters and rare characters. Two thumb keys, Capital and Sign, give two different shifts. The space, back space and margin release keys are also placed for the thumbs and act on an ordinary typebar machine.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
