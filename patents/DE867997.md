## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Cleaning typewriter types with solvents or modeling clay is messy, unhealthy and wasteful, and dirt falls back into the machine.

**Solution:** A removable felt ring with a dry adhesive layer on a hard roller at the end of a handle is rolled over the types, lifting dirt from the letter depths; the ring can be re-gummed and replaced.
