## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**How it works (reading):** The pawl carrier is mounted in a yoke that pivots on an axis transverse to the pawl axis and is held by compliant springs, so impacts are absorbed by spring movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage advance pawls get shocks from the carriage, and a stiff cross spring or sound pads do not absorb them well.

**Solution:** The pawl carrier is mounted in a yoke that pivots on an axis transverse to the pawl axis and is held by compliant springs, so impacts are absorbed by spring movement.
