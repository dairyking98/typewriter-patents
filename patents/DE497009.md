## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special supports, platforms or trolleys for supporting machines on tables

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on wooden floorboards for typewriter cases and consists of another Improvement and training of wooden floorboards for typewriter cases according to patent.

**How it works (reading):** Thin steel strip reinforcements are set on edge in the filling and at the corners, with cross-connections, and the base, filling and support plate are riveted together; the board can then be covered with synthetic leather.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Wooden floor boards of typewriter cases warp because the light frame cannot resist the wood's tendency to throw.

**Solution:** Thin steel strip reinforcements are set on edge in the filling and at the corners, with cross-connections, and the base, filling and support plate are riveted together; the board can then be covered with synthetic leather.
