## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulation is possible only forward, and the carriage must stop at the same letter space whichever way it tabulates. Stops can also separate during an arrest.

**Solution:** A two-way tabulator that works by backward carriage movement as well as forward, with a latch on each denominational stop and means holding the carriage against movement that would separate the tabulator stops.

**How it works:** Denominational stops, each with an associated latch, are carried in the tabulator mechanism. A feed pinion and feed rack, shown under different conditions, let the carriage tabulate either forward or backward and stop at the same letter space. Added holding means keep the carriage from moving when the stops arrest it, so the stops do not separate. The mechanism fits existing Monarch-type machines without structural change.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** denominational stop; latch; feed pinion; feed rack; carriage; tabulator mechanism

**Improves on:**

- one-direction tabulation: two-way tabulation with same stop position
