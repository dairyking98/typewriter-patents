## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists in certain features of construction and combintions of devices, all as will be hereinafter more fully described, and Particularly pointed out in the appended claims.

**How it works (reading):** A complete typewriter with a type-bar release that returns the bar early, a carriage feed that runs fast and stops at any point, and a clutch that disengages the feed from the keys for single-column writing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier typewriters were slow to space, hard to stop accurately during tabular work, and needed heavy key touch to run at high speed.

**Solution:** A complete typewriter with a type-bar release that returns the bar early, a carriage feed that runs fast and stops at any point, and a clutch that disengages the feed from the keys for single-column writing.

**Features:** [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
