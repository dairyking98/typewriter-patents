## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check printing devices must feed checks, space the characters, and lock the type wheel in each printing position.

**Solution:** A type or die wheel is locked in the printing position by an arm and gage wheel that also sets the spacing of printed characters, with a feed for the check.
