## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A type action layout for front-strike typebars.

**Solution:** Alternate typebars with sub-levers pivoted on a wire in radial slots of a segment, each link in the plane of the bar's motion.

**How it works:** Type bars alternate on two kinds in the basket. Sub-levers sit nearly horizontal on a pivot wire set in front of the segment, their rear ends linked up to the bars, so all links incline toward the front. Each link and sub-lever lies roughly in its bar's plane of motion.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** type bar; sub-lever; pivot wire; link; segment; top plate; dust guard
