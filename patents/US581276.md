## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention consists of novel means for attaching a printing-ribbon to areel, barrel, &c.

**Solution:** A catch on the ribbon end engages a slot in the reel, joined to the ribbon by a cross-bar, so the ribbon attaches positively.
