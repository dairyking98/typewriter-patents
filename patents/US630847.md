## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cryptography or similar special procedures in a franking system; Typewriters for ciphering or deciphering cryptographic text wherein the operative connections between the keys and the type-bars are automatically and continuously permuted, during operation, by a coding or key member the connections being mechanical; Handle fastening means

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to provide the attachment with interchangeable keys, so that each transmitter may have his or her own individual cipher arrangement at pleasure.
