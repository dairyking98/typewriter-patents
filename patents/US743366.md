## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Machines that print a line across the platen should space regularly to the next line and turn the platen automatically to start the next line.

**Solution:** A carriage prints a line at right angles to the platen axis, and at line end it spaces and turns the platen automatically to the correct position for the next line.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** platen; carriage; type-bars; line-spacing mechanism; frame
