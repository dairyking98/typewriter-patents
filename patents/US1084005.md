## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Characters on a typewriter key are hidden when the finger rests on the key, so the operator cannot see them.

**Solution:** The key head carries a separate character-bearing surface and a finger-engaging surface, with a ring or cushion piece so the character stays exposed while the finger rests on the key.
