## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A raised platen must be held firmly in lower case and its upward travel limited, usually with a separate locking member.

**Solution:** Two toggle levers on the shifting frame, worked by the shift key through bell cranks, hold and limit the platen in both positions, one extended when the other is bent.

**How it works:** The platen rests via a sliding roller on a shifting frame guided at the top and supported at the bottom by a radius rod. Two toggle levers pivoted on a lug of the frame are linked to bell cranks that the shift key moves. In lower case one toggle is straightened to hold the frame, in upper case the other is straightened to stop it, so the mechanism itself defines the end positions and moves almost silently.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** platen; shifting frame; toggle lever; bell crank lever; shift key; radius rod; sliding roller
