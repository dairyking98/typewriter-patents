**How it works (reading):** An annular disk on the bearing sleeve, concentric with the platen shaft, carries an indicator that shows the backward platen turn needed between carbon copies.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator needs to know how far to turn the platen backward by hand when inserting new sheets for carbon copies at uniform spacing.

**Solution:** An annular disk on the bearing sleeve, concentric with the platen shaft, carries an indicator that shows the backward platen turn needed between carbon copies.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
