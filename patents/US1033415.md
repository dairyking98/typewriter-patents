## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper feed rolls must adjust to varying paper thicknesses and carriages of different lengths, and the mechanism should be cheap and light.

**Solution:** Feed or presser rolls are carried on a flexible frame formed partly by the paper guides, and the mechanism is made largely of sheet metal and wire, with a paper feed table attached to the carriage.
