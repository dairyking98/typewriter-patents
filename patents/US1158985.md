## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platens with balancing means

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to cylindrical platen, 'typewriting machines, and has for an object to provide means for automatically causing the lines of typewritten matter to be inscribed upon the paperobliquel-y with reference to the axisof the platen (or in other words, obliquely with reference to the parallel edges of the paper), as may he required, for example, in cases.

**How it works (reading):** A helical cam on the carriage shaft turns the platen by a friction pawl and ratchet during carriage travel, feeding the paper along the platen axis so lines run obliquely.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Words of a song must be written obliquely across a note sheet, which normal platen line spacing cannot do.

**Solution:** A helical cam on the carriage shaft turns the platen by a friction pawl and ratchet during carriage travel, feeding the paper along the platen axis so lines run obliquely.
