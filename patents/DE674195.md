## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**How it works (reading):** The frame rolls on each side between tracks at each end of the frame and a track in the machine frame, using a single ball whose axis moves at half the relative track speed, giving backlash-free guidance.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Lifting the paper slide frame by side arms or parallelogram guides is costly, and roller guides bind.

**Solution:** The frame rolls on each side between tracks at each end of the frame and a track in the machine frame, using a single ball whose axis moves at half the relative track speed, giving backlash-free guidance.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
