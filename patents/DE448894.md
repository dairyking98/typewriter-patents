**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key lever bearing spacers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Keyboards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key lifting device intended in particular for die setting and die casting machines, which makes it possible to use every single lever of the one lever set as common Bearing serving shaft to remove without the storage of the rest of the keypad is adversely affected by this.

**How it works (reading):** Each lever turns on offset intermediate sleeves that space it from its neighbours; after an axial lock is released, the sleeves slide apart and the lever fork can be lifted off the shaft.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Removing one key lever from the common shaft normally disturbs the bearings of all the other levers.

**Solution:** Each lever turns on offset intermediate sleeves that space it from its neighbours; after an axial lock is released, the sleeves slide apart and the lever fork can be lifted off the shaft.
