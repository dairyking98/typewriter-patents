## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Separating articles from piles by pushers engaging the edges of the articles

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to stencil or card writing mechanism for typewriting machines and its general object is to provide improved mechanism of the character specitied.

**How it works (reading):** A stencil or card container with guiding and feeding mechanism, a ribbon arrangement that improves visibility of the writing on the stencil, and full-stroke devices tied to the feed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil and card writing on a typewriter needs a container, guidance and feed, and visible ribbon for the stencil.

**Solution:** A stencil or card container with guiding and feeding mechanism, a ribbon arrangement that improves visibility of the writing on the stencil, and full-stroke devices tied to the feed.
