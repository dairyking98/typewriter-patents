## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters have too many parts and the type must all strike one center.

**Solution:** Type-arms laid like spokes on a disk slide inward then tilt up through a central opening.

**How it works:** Radial arms lie on a horizontal disk, inner ends bent upward carrying the type. A key pulls a cord to slide the arm inward, its outer end falling into an annular recess so the type tilts up into the printing position. Springs return the arms. A carriage over the central opening and a spacing bar are shown.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** disk; type-arm; hub ring; spring; cord; key; carriage; spacing bar
