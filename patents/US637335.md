## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Shifting the multi-face type sleeves needs the operator to hold the shift key.

**Solution:** A shift key that locks the sleeves in their shifted position and releases them on a slight further depression.

**How it works:** Each typebar has a vertical slotted plate with three finger keys whose lugs ride in the slots to rock and slide the bar. Sleeves on the bars, each with three type faces, are partially rotated by a shifting key. The key is held in either of two positions by a locking device, so the finger need not stay on it, and a slight further depression returns it. A key-lock device engaging the universal bar locks the keys.

**Features:**
