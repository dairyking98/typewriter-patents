## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column tabulators always stop the carriage at the same point in a column, forcing price and amount columns to be reset by hand to the right decimal position.

**Solution:** Some tabulator keys skip to a column and stop at a hundreds, tens or units position within it.

**How it works:** All tabulator keys are column-skipping keys; most have one key per column, but groups of three keys serve the price, amount and credit columns. Key-controlled selecting stops arrest the skipping carriage at different decimal positions in those columns, so writing begins immediately. It is applied to an Underwood front-strike machine in the manner of US956144.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** tabulator key; column stop; selecting stop; carriage
