## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writers in which the paper is automatically moved from right to left as each line is filled up;

**How it works (reading):** A grooved wheel with a movable indicating ring is fixed to the machine front and driven by the carriage, showing the line end and the carriage position without looking at the paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must watch the bell to learn when a line is nearly full, which takes the eyes off the keys, and cannot easily shift the carriage to a set column.

**Solution:** A grooved wheel with a movable indicating ring is fixed to the machine front and driven by the carriage, showing the line end and the carriage position without looking at the paper.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
