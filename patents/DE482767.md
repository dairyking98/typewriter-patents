**How it works (reading):** All counters' overflow and cross-counter faults are made visible at one common point, and a single locking member driven by both the vertical counters and the transverse totaliser (scanned by a device) blocks the arithmetic keys when the transverse totaliser overflows or is not at zero at clear.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier overflow indicators on calculating typewriters only signalled miscalculation in specific processes and could not be seen during other operations.

**Solution:** All counters' overflow and cross-counter faults are made visible at one common point, and a single locking member driven by both the vertical counters and the transverse totaliser (scanned by a device) blocks the arithmetic keys when the transverse totaliser overflows or is not at zero at clear.

**How it works:** A scanning device linked to the vertical counters controls a locking member that also responds to the transverse counters, so keys lock whenever a fault exists.
