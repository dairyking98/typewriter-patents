## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter needs a portable cabinet that also holds supplies and keeps dust off the machine.

**Solution:** A folding cabinet with hinged sides and top, chains as stops, and internal pockets, which folds into a closed case or opens as a stand.

**How it works:** Side panels and top hinge from a fixed back and bottom; flexible chains limit opening so the cabinet can be closed and carried.

**Features:** [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md)
