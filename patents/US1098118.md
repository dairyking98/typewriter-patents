## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Feeding fingers can tear the paper after the type presses it against the yielding platen, because there is not enough slack.

**Solution:** Paper feeding guides that give sufficient slack in the paper strip at the instant the type strikes against the yielding platen, so the feed fingers do not tear it.
