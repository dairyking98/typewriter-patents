## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A toy typewriter needs a keyboard chart that children can learn from, and the panel must not add to packaged size.

**Solution:** A panel with a standard keyboard layout slides between a stored position under the frame and a forward active position, and the base doubles as a storage box.

**Features:** [Toy or teaching machine](../features/use-toy.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
