## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Exchangeable type blocks on typebar heads are known but can shift sideways; the inventor wants every typebar of a machine to take a different font reliably.

**Solution:** A type block with a resilient pair of arms at each end, ending in stepped noses, snaps onto lugs on the typebar head while a protruding central part of the head locates in a matching recess of the block.

**How it works:** The typebar head has a lug on each side with sharp edges and a protruding lower part c. The type block carries spring arm pairs with inclined, stepped noses at each end. Pressing the block on spreads the arms over the lugs until the noses latch behind the shoulders; the central part seats in the block so it cannot move to either side. A cross piece at the lower end of the arms gives the head a stop. To remove, bend the noses apart and pull the block off.
