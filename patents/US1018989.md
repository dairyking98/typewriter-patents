## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars cannot be removed individually, and wear in the bar seats cannot be taken up.

**Solution:** The supporting frame has a cone-shaped groove in one rail that receives a cone on each type bar, so any single bar can be lifted out and the seats readjusted as they wear.
