## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Return springs of type actions give a force that cannot be varied without changing the accelerated action of the typebars.

**Solution:** Means to vary the effective force of the return springs and the touch of all keys together, adjustable through the sub-levers.
