## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improves the Hess and Stoughton US610400 machine where each typebar carries several characters on a rotatable sleeve, reached by three keys per bar.

**Solution:** Each pivoted typebar has a three-faced sleeve that is partially rotated by shift keys, and three keys per bar move it to one of three type positions.

**How it works:** For each typebar a vertical slotted plate pivoted in the bed has three finger keys, whose lugs act as stops limiting the plate's motion. The outer keys move the bar around its pivot and along its length so the desired one of three characters on the face opposite the platen hits the paper. Shift keys through slotted plates drive a rack that engages a gear on each sleeve to rotate it a part turn, and a locking comb holds the typebars while the sleeves shift. Latches hold each sleeve in either shifted position.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by turning the element](../features/shift-rotary.md), [Three or more cases](../features/shift-triple.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** typebar; type sleeve; slotted plate; finger key; shift key; shifting rack; locking comb; locking latch; platen

**Numbers:** 3 type-carrying faces per sleeve; 3 types per face; 3 keys per bar
