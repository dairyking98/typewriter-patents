## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A spring-driven platen carriage that can feed either left or right needs spacing control without separate parts for each direction.

**Solution:** One control piece is pulled by a spring from an arm that is flipped past dead centre to reverse the direction of its limited swing.

**How it works:** A toothed piece normally holds the carriage rack against the feed spring. The printing action or a hand key lowers it so a pivoted sector-shaped control piece meshes with the rack, which then advances by the control piece's free travel between a stop and an adjustable spacing piece. On release the toothed piece re-engages and a pulling spring returns the control piece. That spring hangs from a turnable arm; moving the arm by a knob to the other side of its pivot reverses the pull and so the feed direction. A second form uses an idle gear on the rack.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Sideways-spacing control device in an automatic-feed typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. The invention relates to a sideways-spacing control device in an automatic-feed typewriter, characterised in that a control piece, which by the platen's sideways-feed action meshes directly with the platen's rack, or engages a gear meshing with the rack, and is swung by a set angle so as to limit the platen's sideways spacing, is pulled by a spring fastened to one end of an arm that can be turned through a set angle; and that, when the platen's direction of automatic feed is changed, the arm is swung left or right past the dead centre of its spring's pull, so changing the direction in which the control piece is pulled and allowing it a limited swing along the platen's direction of travel. Its object is a device of simple construction and sure action in which, by changing over one control piece, the sideways spacing can be controlled in either direction, left or right.
