## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforating stamp) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to stamps used for stamping checks or other papers with perforated numbers orletters indicating amounts or other data as a security against alteration or forgery.

**How it works (reading):** A common set of independently reciprocating punches in converging guides is selected by an adjustable socket-piece whose holes leave out the punches not needed for a given character.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A separate punch and die for each character is costly in a perforating stamp.

**Solution:** A common set of independently reciprocating punches in converging guides is selected by an adjustable socket-piece whose holes leave out the punches not needed for a given character.
