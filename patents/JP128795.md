## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Moving the heavy paper-cylinder frame over a large type magazine of a Japanese typewriter, and striking, is tiring and slow by hand.

**Solution:** Drive the frame from a motor through a two-link folding arm that works as a differential gear, so the frame follows the hand to any position within range.

**How it works:** A reversible electric drive, controlled by a front key, turns a vertical shaft carrying a pulley. Two links are pinned at a joint; cord-and-pulley (or gear) coupling makes them swing differentially so the arm folds or unfolds, pushing the carriage frame forward and left or back and right. The operator steers the frame by a guide knob. At the wanted type, pressing the knob lifts a pawl from a stop wheel, letting a second shaft turn and slide a rack that works the push-up rod and printing lever. Releasing the key reverses the drive, returning the rack then the arm.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. This invention concerns a power control device for a Japanese-script printing machine in which the power mechanism and the moving part of the printing machine are connected by a folding arm made of two links ⓐ ⓑ; grooved pulleys or gears related to the folding arm are suitably fitted so that the folding arm itself is a differential gear; the rotation of the power mechanism turns the links ⓐ ⓑ differentially relative to each other, and their folding and unfolding moves the moving part of the printing machine to any position within a fixed range. Its object is to move the moving part of the printing machine positively to the required position, and so to make quick printing work possible. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show one embodiment.
