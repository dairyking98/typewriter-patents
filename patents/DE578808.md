## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register print with type wheel groups)

**How it works (reading):** Adjusting the print hammer from its normal position locks the type wheels against rotation, so the selected group is printed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A print hammer selecting among several type-wheel groups must be adjusted to the chosen group, and changing type-wheel position is an awkward way to switch.

**Solution:** Adjusting the print hammer from its normal position locks the type wheels against rotation, so the selected group is printed.

- **Selection:** [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
