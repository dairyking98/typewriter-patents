## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter combined with adding machine)

**How it works (reading):** A self-contained adding unit with its own keyboard clips onto the front frame of an ordinary typewriter and drives the typewriter's keys by force fit, so the typewriter can write without affecting the adder and an addition can be made at any time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing typewriter adders either share the typewriter keys and jam the typing, or sit at the side in a bulky linked design.

**Solution:** A self-contained adding unit with its own keyboard clips onto the front frame of an ordinary typewriter and drives the typewriter's keys by force fit, so the typewriter can write without affecting the adder and an addition can be made at any time.
