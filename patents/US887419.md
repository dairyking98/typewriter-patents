## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rotating the platen back to receive a new bill and forward again to a tinting position takes several operations.

**Solution:** A crank on the platen frame, loose on the platen, carries a pawl that engages a toothed wheel fixed to the platen, so one swing of the crank rotates the platen either way.
