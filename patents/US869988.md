## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to tabulating devices for typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A selected column stop cooperates with a stop member to arrest the carriage at a chosen column, skipping intermediate stops, and at letter spaces within a columnar field.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Free carriage runs skip to the wrong column when tabulating.

**Solution:** A selected column stop cooperates with a stop member to arrest the carriage at a chosen column, skipping intermediate stops, and at letter spaces within a columnar field.
