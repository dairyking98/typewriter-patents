## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**How it works (reading):** Accent keys act through links on a second universal rail carried on a removable bracket, and a slotted swing arm drives the ribbon lifter from either rail.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Accent-writing devices took much space and the auxiliary parts could not be installed as a unit.

**Solution:** Accent keys act through links on a second universal rail carried on a removable bracket, and a slotted swing arm drives the ribbon lifter from either rail.

**How it works:** Letter key levers move the main universal rail by rear extensions. Accent key levers, angled upward, pull links connected to an auxiliary universal rod mounted on a screwed-on support part. Either rail swings an arm that, through a pin, drives the ribbon-lifter lever; the accent rail can be removed as one unit.
