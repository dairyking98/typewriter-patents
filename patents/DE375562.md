## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating typewriter number dialer)

**How it works (reading):** The typewriter's number keys sit on the calculator's key levers; sliding rods with stops hold the counter rods until the number is transferred.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter stacked on a calculator needed a direct key connection and a compact counter with stops.

**Solution:** The typewriter's number keys sit on the calculator's key levers; sliding rods with stops hold the counter rods until the number is transferred.
