## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key lever locks based on comb-tooth detents were not reliable enough.

**Solution:** Locking members in the form of balls rest in hollow bearing seats and return by gravity; a pressed key pushes the balls apart to block other keys.
