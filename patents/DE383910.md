## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (perforating machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**How it works (reading):** The model disc is replaced by a drum built from a stack of discs with fitted edges, so the character forms sit on the drum circumference and the punch pins that should not be pressed enter the gaps between the tooth-like character shapes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Hole-punch stencils for perforating bank checks were hard to make and needed highly skilled workers because the model discs had to be cut with exact punch patterns.

**Solution:** The model disc is replaced by a drum built from a stack of discs with fitted edges, so the character forms sit on the drum circumference and the punch pins that should not be pressed enter the gaps between the tooth-like character shapes.
