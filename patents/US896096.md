## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and has for its main objects to provide an improved construction of type bar hanger, which is adapted to shield the type bar pivots from dust or grit, and to serve also as a guide for a type bar of improved construction, and also to provide means for starting the return of the type bar after printing.

**How it works (reading):** A type bar hanger shields the type bar pivots from dust and guides the type bar; a return-starting means is provided after the print stroke, with key levers linked to the type bar by an actuating link.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bar pivots are exposed to dust and grit, and the type bar needs a better guide and a way to start its return after printing.

**Solution:** A type bar hanger shields the type bar pivots from dust and guides the type bar; a return-starting means is provided after the print stroke, with key levers linked to the type bar by an actuating link.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)

**Parts:** type bar hanger; key lever with sub-lever; actuating link; segmental rest
