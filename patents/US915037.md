## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon mechanism needs spool drive, direction reversal, an end-of-ribbon warning, transverse shifting to use the whole ribbon and different colours, and prevention of blotting by heavily inked ribbons.

**Solution:** A combined ribbon mechanism: geared spool drive reversed by a sliding bar, key lock at ribbon end, and a ribbon guide shifting transversely for banded ribbons.

**How it works:** Gears on the base plate drive the ribbon spools. A sliding bar underneath, held in either of two positions by V-shaped grooves and clamping springs, reverses the direction. When the ribbon is fully unwound from a spool, an arm locks the keys through a connecting rod and blocks on the keys. A ribbon guide on the ribbon shield, which shifts with the platen, moves the ribbon transversely to use different longitudinal colour bands.
