## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed should be driven by the carriage movement and reverse simply.

**Solution:** A rack on the carriage drives pinions and worms that wind either of two inclined ribbon bobbins, selected by sliding a frame.

**How it works:** A rack bar on the carriage gears with two pinions on a slidable skeleton frame, which drive vertical worm shafts engaging worm wheels on the ribbon bobbins. A pawl and toothed ring let the worm turn only when the carriage advances. Sliding the frame engages one worm and disengages the other, reversing the ribbon travel. Stops limit the frame movement.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** rack bar; pinion; worm; worm wheel; ribbon bobbin; skeleton frame; pawl; toothed ring

**Numbers:** bobbin axes about 45 degrees to platen axis
