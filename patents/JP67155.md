## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A portable printing device that can print both European and Japanese text easily from a rotating character disc.

**Solution:** A large rotating multi-row character disc is carried on a centre shaft that also slides along a slot, so disc rotation plus shaft travel bring any character under a printing-hole pointer, where pressing prints it onto paper wrapped round a cylinder.

**How it works:** Several rows of type are set round a rotating circular character disc (with small auxiliary discs). The disc's centre shaft is fixed to a raised bar running in a slot and moves back and forth over a plate, so rotation and traverse together select the character. The wanted character is brought to the printing-hole pointer and pressed: the type descends through the printing hole and prints, and a spring returns it on release. Ink brushes ink the types. Paper is stuck round a cylinder that is stepped by ratchets and racks; two pawls of different length and two racks of different pitch are switched (a handle holds one out of use) for European or Japanese text. A handle sets the cylinder rotation (line position) via locating holes and runs the cylinder back at line end.
