> Description Translated from German Carrying handle stamped and bent from a piece of sheet metal on protective boxes for typewriters and the like. The subject of the invention is a handle for Protective boxes, such as those for typewriters and calculating machines in the way come to use that a machine attached to a base plate The upturned protective arbor is connected to the base plate, - so that the entire machine including the protective box with the help of the handle can be adjusted and worn. When training a handle for the purpose mentioned one has so far the most diverse types have been used. The well-known handles consist of a number of individual parts, which are lathe work, thread cutting, Require nuts, grinding work and the like more. There is no need for emphasis that the resulting costs for such a subordinate part as the handle of a protective box, today's demands on economic Manufacturing does not match. It is not unknown for other purposes, e.g. B. at Pots, pans, etc., to use handles that are punched from a piece of sheet metal and are bent accordingly.
