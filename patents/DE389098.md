## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carrying handles for typewriter protective boxes are made from many machined parts, which costs too much for such a minor part.

**Solution:** The handle is punched and bent from one sheet of metal and fastened to the box lid by bent wire staples through washer bars with hinge rivets, so it is cheap and secure.
