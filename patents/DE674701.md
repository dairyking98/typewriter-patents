## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actuators for print wires

**How it works (reading):** Each character is composed from needle-shaped partial type elements, selected by a key bar touching a block of contact rails and driven by eccentrics on a motor shaft.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines are noisy, need deep key travel, and fit only a limited number of characters on a semicircle; earlier electromagnet-driven composed-character machines are complex and slow to return.

**Solution:** Each character is composed from needle-shaped partial type elements, selected by a key bar touching a block of contact rails and driven by eccentrics on a motor shaft.

**How it works:** Pressing a key lever lays its key rail across a block of contact rails; those not cut away at that point close a circuit. Each closed circuit attracts a contact that puts an angle driver into the path of an eccentric on a continuously rotating motor shaft. The eccentric pushes the corresponding needle-shaped partial character out of its guide to the paper, and a spring plus a counter-eccentric return it. A shift lever slides the contact rail block sideways to select another rail set for capitals. The platen is replaced by a rail with a counter contact.

**Features:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** key lever; key rail; contact rail block; needle type element; guide; angle driver; eccentric shaft; motor; return spring; shift lever

**Numbers:** key lever depth 14 to 16 mm
