## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The writing pad on a telegraph typewriter needs a light, effortless removable mounting while pressing its rollers against the platen.

**Solution:** A sheet metal writing pad pivots on bearing lugs, with a spring pawl torquing it onto the platen through elastic rollers.
