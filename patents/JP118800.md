## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The line spacing of a printing machine with a hand-lever pawl-and-ratchet line feed needs to be held exactly at the set amount, and the chosen setting should be visible at a glance.

**Solution:** A slide that moves with the line-feed lever has its stroke limited by one of several stops, chosen by pressing a push-button that swings the stops into the slide's path.

**How it works:** Turning a hand lever (2) moves an intermediate lever (3) and a pawl (7), which turns a ratchet wheel (9) on the paper cylinder to feed the line. A slide (11) rides in a vertical groove (10) in the frame, driven by a pin in a cross slot of the intermediate lever, so its travel follows the lever. Two push-buttons (17, 18) sit on a rocker (15) held by a spring (19); pressing one pops the other out. The rocker turns a rocking lever (20) through a pin in a slot (24), carrying stops (22, 23) set a fixed distance apart, so one stop or the other lies in the slide's path and limits its stroke, giving a small or a large line feed.
