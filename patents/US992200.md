## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to stop devices for typewriting machines aud has for its main object the provision of automatically controlled means for arresting the traveling element or carriage at differentpoints in its travel so that lines of writing may be begun at different points on the work sheet.

**How it works (reading):** Margin devices on the carriage cooperate with a stop on the frame during return so the carriage stops at chosen points.
