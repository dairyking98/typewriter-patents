## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On the Underwood, rolls sharing one shaft press unevenly, especially on wide carriages or narrow sheets, so paper feeds faster along one edge and the shaft skews.

**Solution:** Each pressure roll is journaled and pressed independently in its own swiveled bail, with all rolls released by one key.

**How it works:** Each soft-rubber roll has a hollow metal bushing that loosely holds an axle with gudgeons in open-ended slots of a bail, so the roll drops out when the bail is lifted. The bail is swiveled midway on an arm about an axis tangential to the platen, letting either end of the roll rock away independently, which compensates for platen or roll irregularity and sheets narrower than the roll. Front and rear sets each have several such rolls, all cast off by a single release key.
