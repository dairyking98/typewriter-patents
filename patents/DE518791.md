## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (electrolytic character production) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus essentially involving a single such process

**How it works (reading):** Spark gaps are switched by light through a photographic negative, so irradiated electrodes make marks on electrolytic paper.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electrode blocks for card printing are complicated to change for different cards, and selenium switch cells are costly.

**Solution:** Spark gaps are switched by light through a photographic negative, so irradiated electrodes make marks on electrolytic paper.
