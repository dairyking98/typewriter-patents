## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (complementary counter for typewriters)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a complementary counter, particularly for typewriters, with one of the two Digit roll sets common tens transfer and one with reciprocal Visualize the two sets of numerical rollers, the volatile one-actuating device, which operates a lock.

**How it works (reading):** The clear-character output is blocked by the reversing element and released only when every digit of both number sets is blank and the reversing device is back in its original position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A complementary counter can output a clear signal after a mistyped digit has reached zero, so a booking error goes unnoticed.

**Solution:** The clear-character output is blocked by the reversing element and released only when every digit of both number sets is blank and the reversing device is back in its original position.
