## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** Each carbon sheet sits on its own holder with a parallel guide shaft running on a fixed rack, and retraction springs pull each holder back separately.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Withdrawing stacked carbon sheets from a long-tape typewriter is hard because friction against the platen is high.

**Solution:** Each carbon sheet sits on its own holder with a parallel guide shaft running on a fixed rack, and retraction springs pull each holder back separately.

**Features:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
