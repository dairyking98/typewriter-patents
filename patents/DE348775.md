## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Uncoupling the line-space wheel from the platen needs one hand to hold a button or lock the release while the other turns the platen, and a forgotten lock leaves the platen decoupled while typing.

**Solution:** Radial pins in the handwheel rim, with cones on their inner ends, push a coupling part axially to release the line-space wheel; pressing the pins alone frees and turns the platen, and releasing re-couples it automatically.

**How it works:** The handwheel is screwed on the platen axis and carries radial spring-loaded pins whose outer ends protrude as buttons. Their inner conical ends bear on inclined surfaces of a coupling part. A coil spring presses that part against the pins and against cones on coupling bolts in the line-space wheel, so the bolts are clamped outward and the wheel is coupled to the platen. Pressing the pins in drives the coupling part along the axis, the bolts are no longer clamped and the line-space wheel is free. The pins protruding from the rim also serve to turn the platen in that state, and on release the wheel re-couples by itself.
