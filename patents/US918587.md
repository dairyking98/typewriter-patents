## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A sliding platen carriage on an adding machine must be stopped at set positions and released when needed.

**Solution:** A stop bar with a notch between raised edges is set by hand, and a spring-loaded finger on the carriage drops into the notch to hold it, and is lifted by a lever for movement.
