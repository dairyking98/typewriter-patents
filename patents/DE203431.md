## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**How it works (reading):** Several independent switching mechanisms, each with one switching range, act on a common middle link whose movement is the arithmetic mean of theirs, so that different ranges result (three ranges from two mechanisms, the third being the sum of the first two).

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Earlier switching devices for different switching ranges of the paper carriage use movable stops and a switch lever, which are fragile, need many parts, and lose accuracy through bending and wear.

**Solution:** Several independent switching mechanisms, each with one switching range, act on a common middle link whose movement is the arithmetic mean of theirs, so that different ranges result (three ranges from two mechanisms, the third being the sum of the first two).
