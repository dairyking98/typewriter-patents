## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Hammond shuttle machines need convenient upper-case and figure shifting.

**Solution:** Several shift keys on one rock-shaft lift the anvil and shuttle to intermediate and upper positions, with locks.

**How it works:** Two capital shift keys (one each side) and a figure shift key connect to one transverse rock-shaft with dogs. The shaft lifts via a lever the anvil shaft carrying the shuttles, bringing the middle or upper type row to the printing point. Adjustable stops limit the lift. Hooked locking arms hold the capital or figure shift down.
