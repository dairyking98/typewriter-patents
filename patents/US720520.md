## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Book type-writers need fine adjustment of the spacing rack so the page can be positioned at a proper margin.

**Solution:** The letter-spacing bar and the tabulator bar are pinned together, and the spacing rack is adjustable along its length.
