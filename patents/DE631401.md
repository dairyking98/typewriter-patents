## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**How it works (reading):** H-shaped stops have two converging legs that straddle one rack and pivot about their contact points, so the stop needs no pins and is limited by the other rack.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pivoting column stops for tab devices need bearing pins, which complicate manufacture, assembly and adjustment and can tilt or jam.

**Solution:** H-shaped stops have two converging legs that straddle one rack and pivot about their contact points, so the stop needs no pins and is limited by the other rack.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** H-shaped column stops; two racks; setting lever; reset device; retaining plates
