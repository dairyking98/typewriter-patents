## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Plural-head type bars need a secure lock holding the head in lower case, and a release that shifts the head to upper case during the printing stroke.

**Solution:** Each type bar carries a shiftable plural type head held by a locking member; a head-releasing trigger contacts a fixed trip during the stroke, which unlocks and shifts the head to upper case.
