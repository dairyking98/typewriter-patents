## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple type-writing machine that prints a fac-simile of raised letters without ink or ribbon, with each letter spaced according to its width.

**Solution:** A lever swung over a notched arc turns a vertical shaft carrying a raised-letter plate to the selected letter, and pressing the lever into the notch rubs a marker over the letter against the paper while the notch depth sets the carriage feed.

**How it works:** A letter plate with raised type is fixed to an upright shaft behind a pair of paper feed rolls carried on a sliding carriage. Lever S, on a sleeve keyed to the shaft, is swung over a notched segment plate B to turn the shaft and bring the chosen letter in front of a marker. Pressing S down into the notch pulls lever N, which turns the marker shaft; first a cam throws the marker against the paper, then it rubs across the letter to leave a fac-simile on the paper. Lifting S raises N, and a pawl on the carriage lever feeds the carriage one step along a ratchet. Notch depth sets how many ratchet teeth the pawl is drawn back, and notch position sets where the letter sits, so narrow and wide letters get different spacing. A knee lever feeds the paper up through the rolls on the carriage return.
