## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (coffee mill) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements for stopping or slowing electric motors, generators, or dynamo-electric converters for stopping or slowing an individual dynamo-electric motor or dynamo-electric converter

**How it works (reading):** A spindle driven by the grinder shaft moves a traveling nut along a calibrated scale; the nut trips a snap switch after the set number of revolutions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Coffee grinders run without supervision and can over-grind; earlier balance-beam cut-offs are unsafe.

**Solution:** A spindle driven by the grinder shaft moves a traveling nut along a calibrated scale; the nut trips a snap switch after the set number of revolutions.
