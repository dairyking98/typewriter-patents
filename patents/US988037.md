## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators cannot easily count words or characters written on a typewriter.

**Solution:** A set of toothed counting wheels with numeral flanges, driven by the spacing bar, shows word and folio counts through a sighting window.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** counting wheels; sighting window; spacing bar
