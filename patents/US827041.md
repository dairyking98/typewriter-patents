## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen shifting needs an adjustable movement and a constant, easy touch on the shift keys.

**Solution:** A platen hung on parallel links from the carriage rides an elevating frame on a flanged track bar whose supports are adjustable by slotted screws.

**How it works:** The platen is suspended on parallel links on the carriage and a swinging frame with a roller travels along a flanged track bar. Lugs keep the roller engaged. Slotted legs under the track bar are loosened and set to regulate the travel and keep the shift touch even. A locking device is part of the shifting mechanism.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; parallel link; swinging frame; antifriction roller; track bar; adjustable leg; locking device
