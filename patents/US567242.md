## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to produce an approximately noiseless type-writing machine and also one in which the impression is given by a dwelling pressure rather than by a quick sharp blow.

**How it works (reading):** Push an end-thrust type-carrier with a toggle of a rocker-arm and link that straightens at impression, with guides at the platen.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars striking the platen make noise and rock on the paper, blurring the print.

**Solution:** Push an end-thrust type-carrier with a toggle of a rocker-arm and link that straightens at impression, with guides at the platen.

**How it works:** A key lever draws down a link which swings a rocker-arm forward, bringing it in line with a link pivoted to the type-carrier. At full movement the toggle is straight and presses the carrier against the platen, with a slight dwell. A table guide supports it and guides at the impression point keep the type head straight. Rubber pads limit noise on both strokes and a spring returns the key.

**Features:** [IMP:press-through](../features/imp-press-through.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** key lever; link; rocker-arm; toggle link; end-thrust type-carrier; table guide; platen; pad; spring
