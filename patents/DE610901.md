## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key lever cash register check printer)

**How it works (reading):** The special check drive is released after a checkout without an inserted slip, but it stays blocked after a checkout with a note until a new checkout without a slip has finished.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On key-lever cash registers the special drive for the check printing unit runs after every checkout, even when no note is inserted.

**Solution:** The special check drive is released after a checkout without an inserted slip, but it stays blocked after a checkout with a note until a new checkout without a slip has finished.

**Features:** [USE:check-writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
