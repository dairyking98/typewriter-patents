## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**How it works (reading):** A part that drops into a strip hole is displaced by an inclined slot so the lever lifts cleanly and returns faster, and a spring returns the lever to its pin.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Control strip machines lose time when the locking pawl must lift out of each hole.

**Solution:** A part that drops into a strip hole is displaced by an inclined slot so the lever lifts cleanly and returns faster, and a spring returns the lever to its pin.
