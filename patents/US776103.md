## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to type-actuating mechanism for type-writing machines, the primary object of the invention being to relieve the finger of shock in the act of manipulating the key, especially the shock incidental to the impact of the type against the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Type-actuating linkage is made to absorb the impact of the typebar striking the platen so that the shock is not felt in the finger on key depression.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Striking the keys sends shock to the finger, mainly from the type striking the platen.

**Solution:** Type-actuating linkage is made to absorb the impact of the typebar striking the platen so that the shock is not felt in the finger on key depression.
