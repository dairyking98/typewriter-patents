## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type bars should be actuated by power from pneumatic means rather than directly by the keys, with the key lever held until the power device operates.

**Solution:** Key levers operate a pneumatic power device that drives the type bars, and a latch holds the key lever until the power device has operated.
