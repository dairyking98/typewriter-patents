## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon feeding should be simple, reliable and work in either direction on either spool, with a way to disconnect it for fitting a new ribbon.

**Solution:** A sliding shaft with a bevel gear at each end, driven from the escapement shaft through a worm and spur wheel, is shifted by a lever to feed the ribbon in either direction or be disengaged.

**How it works:** Each ribbon spool sits on a shaft with a crank at one end and a bevel gear at the other. A cross shaft carries a bevel gear at each end and a spline-mounted spur wheel that meshes with a worm on the vertical escapement shaft, so typing turns it slowly. A lever with a grooved collar slides the cross shaft endwise; in a three-recess rack plate, one end recess meshes one spool gear, the other end the opposite spool, and the middle disconnects the feed so the cranks can run the ribbon by hand.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Numbers:** three lever positions
