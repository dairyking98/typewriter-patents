## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon is to be dispensed with on the Crandall typewriter, whose type sleeve needs inking directly.

**Solution:** Two felt inking rollers close in front of the type sleeve at rest and swing aside as it advances, inking the types as they pass and being re-inked from a rotating fountain.

**How it works:** The Crandall type sleeve carries types around its perimeter and is twisted and slid to bring the wanted type to the print point. At rest the two inking rollers touch each other in front of the sleeve; when the sleeve moves forward it pushes them outward on oscillating arms with return springs, and the types rub the rollers on the way. The rollers take ink from distributing wheels fed by a perforated ink fountain with felt covering, which a ratchet and pawl on the space key turns by steps.
