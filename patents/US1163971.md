## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers co-operating with a roller platen

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the present invention is to provide a simple and practical paper feeding mechanism coacting with the platen rollerwith means whereby the mechanism may be moved out of engagement with the platen roller when it is'desired to straighten or adjust the position of a sheet of paper at any time during the operation of the machine.
