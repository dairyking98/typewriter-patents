## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Type-basket cleaning requires a brush that moves across the type opening.

**Solution:** A brush shaped to the elliptical type opening is fixed on a bar pivoted on a standard; a crank shaft and wrist-pin drive the stroke and tilt the brush end-on to the type.

**How it works:** Crank-driven brush on a pivoted bar, tilted end-on as it sweeps the elliptical type opening.
