## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvement of the Boston Type Writer Company index machine, mainly details: a blurred movable index-plate and the carriage feed.

**Solution:** Stationary index plate on posts, plus pawls on the feed rack and a spring-driven carriage with adjustable tension.

**How it works:** The index plate is mounted on posts on the hinged cover so it stays still while the frame moves. The carriage feed rack is engaged by two semicircular pawls on sleeves around the hinge rod; thumb-pieces and elbow levers move them. A stop and notched sleeve set the space between letters. A spring on a post, adjustable by a disk in radial grooves, drives the carriage through a lever and link. Only the opening is available and the type-selection details are in the earlier patents.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Pointer over a chart](../features/sel-pointer-chart.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** table or cover; hinged frame; index plate; post; feed rack; pawl; sleeve; thumb-piece; elbow lever; spring; disk; radial groove; carriage

**Improves on:**
