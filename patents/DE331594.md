## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for fixing the bearing wire, on which the type levers rotate.

**How it works (reading):** Clamping plates fixed to the head of the segment have openings that take the ends of the bearing wire and press it down into its slot with a moderate pressure, holding the pins aligned without warping.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The bearing wire for the type levers is hard to insert and remove when fitted tightly, but a loose fit lets the pivot pins move out of line.

**Solution:** Clamping plates fixed to the head of the segment have openings that take the ends of the bearing wire and press it down into its slot with a moderate pressure, holding the pins aligned without warping.
