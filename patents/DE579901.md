**How it works (reading):** Unit-price keys each drive a counter, with a control counter for item count and type-wheel counters transferring totals to a common account card.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A department-store cash register needed simple, cheap per-seller units with clear control of items sold, which earlier lever and counter systems did not give.

**Solution:** Unit-price keys each drive a counter, with a control counter for item count and type-wheel counters transferring totals to a common account card.
