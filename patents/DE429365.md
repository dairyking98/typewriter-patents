## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Type rod guide for typewriters, in which the slotted guide through a carrier is held.

**How it works (reading):** The guide carrier is above the typebars, in one piece or bolted to the guide, so drive and bar shape are free.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar guides held by carriers beside the bars constrain the drive and bar design.

**Solution:** The guide carrier is above the typebars, in one piece or bolted to the guide, so drive and bar shape are free.

**How it works:** The typebars run in a circular-arc rail with closed slots; the carrier sits above and either is part of the guide or is screwed to it. Since the bars can still be inserted from the front, they remain interchangeable.
