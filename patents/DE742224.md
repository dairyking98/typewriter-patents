## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hole-duplicating of combination-coded punch cards needs a written record of the hole values as well.

**Solution:** A punch machine is driven from a sensing device on the sample card through a control unit that passes hole combinations as pulses to the key magnets, and writes the values.
