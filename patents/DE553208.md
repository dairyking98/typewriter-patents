## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cash registers and calculating machines need a repeat print of the set amount, and the known repeat devices allow unlimited extra impressions by hand.

**Solution:** A manually operated drive lever can trigger the print hammer again after checkout, but special locking devices allow either a manual repeat or at most one repeat print per checkout.
