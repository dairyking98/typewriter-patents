## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressing the carriage-lift key does not reliably switch the line before the carriage returns when only a few letters have been typed on the new line.

**Solution:** The motor-driven lift rack is disengaged and the carriage is pulled a short distance by its spring, so the line is always switched, and a timed lock holds the rack raised until the drum turns.
