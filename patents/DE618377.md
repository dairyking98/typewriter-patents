**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a motorized paper trolley elevator;

**How it works (reading):** The motor-driven lift rack is disengaged and the carriage is pulled a short distance by its spring, so the line is always switched, and a timed lock holds the rack raised until the drum turns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pressing the carriage-lift key does not reliably switch the line before the carriage returns when only a few letters have been typed on the new line.

**Solution:** The motor-driven lift rack is disengaged and the carriage is pulled a short distance by its spring, so the line is always switched, and a timed lock holds the rack raised until the drum turns.

**Features:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
