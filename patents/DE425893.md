## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper-clamping rollers under a small portable platen take much room.

**Solution:** A flat frame of pinch rollers, each frame free to tilt and spring-pressed to the platen, is lifted off by a pivoting switching rail.

**How it works:** Pinch rollers sit in two sets either side of the platen axis, each frame of front and rear axles joined by cross rails. A common spring presses them upward via frame supports and switching plates. A switching rail with fingers that hook the rear of the cross rails first lifts the rear rollers and then, after a hook engagement, lays the whole frame flat on the base of the paper roller frame, freeing the paper. The edge lever on the platen axle moves the rail.
