## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen must be freely rotatable separately from the line-space wheel, which existing machines do not allow simply.

**Solution:** A line-space wheel drum is bound to a cross-piece on the platen axle by dogs that a finger wheel with helicoidal wedges forces outward when turned, and the finger wheel locks against accidental release.
