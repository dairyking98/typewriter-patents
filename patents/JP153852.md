## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A very simple, strong, portable device that prints signs or large-faced characters easily and quickly.

**Solution:** Pressing a plate lowers a type cylinder and, through a spring-pulled cord, turns it until a stud meets a stop set by a push-button, so the chosen face is at the bottom at the end of the stroke.

**How it works:** The type cylinder with faces round its circumference is fixed on a shaft in a bearing plate that swings on a base shaft and is held up by a plate spring. A drum with stop studs sits on the shaft, and a coiled spring pulls a cord wound on the shaft so the shaft tends to turn. Pressing a push-button pulls a cord that projects a rotation-stop slider inward. Pressing the press-plate swings the bearing plate down while the coiled spring turns the shaft until a stud strikes the slider, and at the bottom of the stroke the face prints. Releasing returns everything by the plate spring and slider spring. Several cylinders can be fitted on the shaft and chosen by sliding the bearing plate along its shaft.
