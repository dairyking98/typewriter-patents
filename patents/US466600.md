## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to that class of type writing machines in which upper and lower y case characters are provided upon separate type-bars, and are adapted to be operated by a single set of key-levers and finger keys or pads.

**How it works (reading):** A shifter plate or ring with guide slots at the type base decides which of two bars is raised by each key.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wanted upper and lower case on separate type levers operated by a single key set.

**Solution:** A shifter plate or ring with guide slots at the type base decides which of two bars is raised by each key.

**How it works:** The keyboard is at one end with lettered pads on shafts going through flat steel key levers beneath the bed. At the other end a circular type base holds the type levers. A shifter ring in a socket around the base is connected by levers to shift keys. Carriage feed is a spring-driven vertical shaft with cord, with an escapement and space key. A ribbon feed on the carriage with two reels.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** bed; keyboard; key lever; type base; type lever; shifter ring; spring shaft; escapement; spacing lever; ribbon reel

**Numbers:** 6 sheets of drawings
