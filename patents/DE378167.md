## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keyboard keys hit the setting mechanism directly, making the keystroke uneven.

**Solution:** Wedge-like inclined slots in rails move the keys down only after the first drive phase, so the keystroke is even.
