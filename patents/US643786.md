## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing one character at a time is slow; the inventor wants several characters struck in one operation.

**Solution:** Type-levers are assembled in horizontal alignment and guided so that several levers operated together are led to a single point and printed as a close justified group in one operation.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type-levers in horizontal alignment; guiding means; paper-feeding mechanism; alphabets in several sets

**Numbers:** Patent 643,786; Fig. 5 modification of alignment
