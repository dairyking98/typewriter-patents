## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Types in circle-arranged machines (Remington, Densmore, Smith Premier) need cleaning.

**Solution:** A cleaner is detachably secured to the ribbon-carrying bar so the types of the circle can be cleaned as they are raised to the ribbon.
