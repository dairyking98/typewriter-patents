## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for assembling matrices and space bands

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention has reference to that class of finger key mechanisms wherein a continuously rotating roll actuates a cam, which in turn effects the movement of a slide for operating an escapement-the parts being controlled in their action by a finger key.
