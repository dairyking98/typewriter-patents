## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (Linotype keyboard mechanism) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements for assembling matrices and space bands

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention has reference to that class of finger key mechanisms wherein a continuously rotating roll actuates a cam, which in turn effects the movement of a slide for operating an escapement-the parts being controlled in their action by a finger key.

**How it works (reading):** A continuously rotating roll drives a cam that lifts a reed to actuate the escapement; the finger key only stops the cam and releases it by friction.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Finger-key mechanisms for line-casting machines need an effective action with fewer parts.

**Solution:** A continuously rotating roll drives a cam that lifts a reed to actuate the escapement; the finger key only stops the cam and releases it by friction.
