## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improve on the Brooks type-bar writer (US323495): use an ink ribbon instead of a pad, shift between two types on a bar without a shifting keyboard, and use a different escapement and platen drive.

**Solution:** A downstrike type-bar machine with two types per bar, a shift mechanism acting on the hinge supports of the bars, ribbon feed and vibrator, and a spring-powered rack-and-pinion carriage escapement.

**How it works:** Horizontal key-levers in the base operate near-upright type-bars at the back, hinged on a grooved fulcrum-plate, which thrust forward and down onto the top of a cylindrical platen so the line is visible. Two types on each bar are chosen by a shifting mechanism connected with the hinge supports and an advancing key. A rack-bar and pinion replaces the worm on the platen carriage, which is driven by a power spring through a feed escapement. The machine also has paper holding, line-spacing, a ribbon-vibrating mechanism and a ribbon feed mechanism, in a cast base frame with a secondary frame.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Shift by turning the element](../features/shift-rotary.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** base casting; key-lever; type-bar; fulcrum-plate; platen; power spring; rack-bar; pinion; feed escapement; shift mechanism; advancing key; ribbon vibrator; ribbon feed

**Numbers:** nine sub-mechanism divisions; nine sheets of drawings

**Improves on:**

- US323495 (Brooks): inking ribbon not pad, hinge-support shift, rack and pinion instead of worm, different escapement
