## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On the Sholes and Glidden typewriter the paper cannot be set on the cylindrical tympan accurately enough to strike a letter at an exact spot or to print a line in a definite direction.

**Solution:** Hinged index marks on a frame lying on the top plate indicate the printing point and the direction of the line.

**How it works:** A frame hinged to the top plate carries indexes that mark the point where a letter will be printed and the line direction on the hinged, laterally movable cylindrical tympan. The frame lies flat on the plate while typing, or can be detached and its hinge ends inserted in holes in the plate.

**Features:** [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** top plate; cylindrical tympan; hinge; frame; index

**Improves on:**

- Sholes and Glidden typewriter: better gauging of letter position and line direction than its existing gauges
