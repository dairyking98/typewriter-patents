The object of the invention is to provide a device which prevents spring-actuated print hammers from rebounding after a print stroke, simply can be established and works effectively. The invention is based on a rebound damping device for a Print hammer from and is characterized in that the device is coaxial with is movably attached to the hammer in such a way that it is both during the pressure stroke and moved with the hammer during the return movement.
