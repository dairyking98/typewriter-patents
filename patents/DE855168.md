## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (rebound damping, print hammer) · **Subject (patent classes):** Hammer-impression mechanisms with anti-rebound arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to Improvements to rebound damping devices for print hammers.

**How it works (reading):** A damper coaxial with the hammer moves with it both on the strike and on return, its effect adjustable by adding weights; a rubber block cushions the lever stop.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Spring-driven print hammers rebound after the strike and make a second, unwanted impression.

**Solution:** A damper coaxial with the hammer moves with it both on the strike and on return, its effect adjustable by adding weights; a rubber block cushions the lever stop.
