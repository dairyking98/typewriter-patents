## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pre-inserted account sheets held between feed rollers on a bookkeeping typewriter get crumpled when the platen is advanced several lines.

**Solution:** The line-feed lever also opens the feed rollers of the pre-insertion device so the sheets slide freely during platen advance.

**How it works:** Two spring-pressed conveyor rollers above the platen pinch the account sheets. A shaft with a hand lever can open them by its counter-arm between the roller shafts. A second arm on the shaft is lifted by a lever that the platen line-shift lever takes along, opening the rollers. A spring-loaded idle arm limits the travel. The opening occurs in the lever's backlash, before the platen starts rotating.
