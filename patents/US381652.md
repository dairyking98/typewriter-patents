## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type-wheel machines needed complicated stop systems to arrest the wheel's momentum when moving between positions, adding parts.

**Solution:** A small three-field, three-row type wheel is selected by keys whose notched profiles drive cams and segments that rotate the wheel, a lifting lever that raises it, and a tilt that throws it against the platen, with a locking wheel holding the chosen position.

**How it works:** Each key lever has a notched underside that successively engages a feed bail (spaces the carriage), a lifting bail (raises the wheel shaft so one of three rows faces the print point) and a compound double bail. The compound bail pushes two cams against pins on two opposed segments that mesh with a pinion on the wheel shaft, rotating the wheel to the chosen character; a detent-held bar slides the cams to select one of three circumferential fields (capitals, lower case, figures). Continued key depression moves both segments together, tilting the pivoted wheel standard so the wheel strikes the platen, with a grooved shaft engaging a fixed stud and a locking wheel engaging a bracket to prevent oscillation during impression. An ink pad on the standard rubs the wheel periphery as it tilts and returns. A mainspring on the rock-shaft powers carriage feed through a pawl and rack.

**Features:** [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md), [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [SHIFT:rotary](../features/shift-rotary.md), [SHIFT:triple](../features/shift-triple.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** type wheel; wheel shaft; pinion; locking wheel; locking bracket; pivoted standard; oscillating segment; cam; double bail; lifting bail; lifting lever; feed bail; feed pawl; check pawl; key lever; spring detent bar; ink pad; platen; carriage rack

**Numbers:** wheel about 1 in. diameter; periphery about 7/8 in. wide; 3 circumferential fields, 3 rows each; 3 detent positions for field bar

**Improves on:**

- earlier wheel and sleeve machines with one key per character: a small-diameter wheel and cam-segment drive reduce momentum and avoid complicated stop systems
