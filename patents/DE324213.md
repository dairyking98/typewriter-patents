## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen roller must be freely and quickly decoupled from the line-space wheel to set the paper by hand; earlier spreading-ring clutches relied on the ring's own spring to disengage and so released unreliably.

**Solution:** A radially sliding wedge, pushed by weak springs into the slot of a split coupling ring, is pulled out by hand with a release pin so the ring only has to leave the ring gear.

**How it works:** The platen body is joined to a disc carrying the platen axle, and the line-space gear wheel turns loosely on it. Pins on the disc guide a wedge radially; light springs push its end into the slot of a split coupling ring that then spreads against the inside of the gear rim and couples platen and wheel. A release pin through the handwheel pin, with a conical tip, is pushed by a button into an eccentric hole in the wedge, pulling the wedge end out of the ring slot and uncoupling the wheel. A locking sleeve with balls can hold the pin pressed in for a lasting release, and a compression spring returns the pin otherwise. The ring is made wide for safe grip, and the wedge of several riveted sheets.
