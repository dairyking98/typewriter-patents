## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The patent seeks visible writing, a smooth even stroke and straight lines.

**Solution:** A linkage of cranks, axes and a long steering rod carries motion from each key to its typebar so all keys, middle or outer, work under the same conditions.

**How it works:** The key rod turns a crank on an axis through about 45 degrees; a pull rod and second crank turn a parallel axis, whose curved crank pushes a long steering rod to a pin on the typebar. The long rod moves nearly parallel to itself, driving the type lever through about 90 degrees against the platen. A spring lifts the type lever back, and one crank may be replaced by a spring for a gentler stroke.
