## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typewriter is deeper front to back than needed because the key guard is separate from the space bar.

**Solution:** The key guard bracket pivots on the machine frame like the key levers and doubles as the space bar, shortening the machine.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter The invention is based on the object of typewriters, especially such a flat design to give the smallest possible scope. to according to the invention, the key guard of the machine frame is for this purpose united with the space bar in such a way that the key protection bracket is attached to the machine frame Rotatable in the sense of the key movement and at the same time designed as a space bar is. This saves space in the direction of the key lever and the typewriter shorter in this direction. As the drawing, which shows a longitudinal section through the machine, can be seen, the key protection bracket 1 surrounding the keys t at the front and on both sides is rotatably connected to the machine frame on its side arms about horizontal pins d and is usually in a similar manner to the Key lever held in the raised position by spring force. This bracket, which can be pivoted in a vertical plane, is then connected to the rocker arm by suitable transmission elements in a manner similar to that of the key levers, and then serves at the same time as a space bar.
