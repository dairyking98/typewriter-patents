## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapements wear quickly, and the key-actuated escapement devices carry the pressure of their returning spring.

**Solution:** An escapement in which the key-actuated dogs are kept free of the return-spring pressure, reducing wear.

**How it works:** In a Remington No. 10 style machine, keys act through sub-levers and links on typebars striking a cylindrical platen. A key-actuated feed dog works with an escapement wheel (a double wheel with oppositely provided teeth in the modified form), with the return spring arranged not to press on the escapement device. Details are not in the opening text.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** key lever; sub-lever; typebar; feed dog; escapement wheel; double escapement wheel; return spring; platen
