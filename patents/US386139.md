## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter cover must be secured to the machine base in a way that can be locked.

**Solution:** A key turns a rock-shaft whose levers drive spring-loaded bolts that hold the cover to the base, with side wings and a hinged flap.

**Features:** [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)
