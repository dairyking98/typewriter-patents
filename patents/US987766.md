## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When a tabulator key moves the carriage to a set column, the printing and escapement parts must not interfere, and the tabulator stop must stay locked until the carriage arrives.

**Solution:** Depressing the tabulator key locks the printing mechanisms and disconnects the carriage from the escapement; the carriage jumps to the stop, a brake controls the drive spring while the stop is positioned, and the carriage is put back under escapement control when it reaches the set point.
