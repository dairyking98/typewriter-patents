## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, cheap typewriter is wanted that fits in the pocket when not in use and can be carried and worked in the hand.

**Solution:** A watch-like pocket case holds a disc of flexible radial type driven by a watch-stem-style shaft, with a pointer on a fixed index disk geared to the type disk, and a finger key that pushes the selected type down through a guide slot onto the paper.

**How it works:** Turning the milled head of the actuating shaft meshes its pinion with a toothed wheel on the type disk, which carries separate flexible types on its periphery, so the types pass over an inking pad on the shaft and then over a type-guide passage in the case floor. A second pair of gear wheels turns an index pointer over a stationary index disk bearing the characters in reverse order, so the pointer shows which type lies beneath the guide passage. The operator presses the spring finger key, fixed at its rear end to the index disk, on the back of the selected type, forcing it through the passage onto the paper, after which both spring back. The machine is then slid forward on the paper by the milled head by one letter space, and the process repeats; ruled paper may help alignment.
