## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reverse mechanisms are complicated and wear quickly.

**Solution:** A positively actuated ribbon reverse that is independent from the feed, working only while reversing.

**How it works:** A plate on the top frame has guide slots. Two U-shaped levers, one a feed lever and the other a check lever limiting rotation of the ribbon spools, carry pawls in boxes at their ends. Key levers operate the feed lever. Ribbon-actuated reversing arms act only when the ribbon is being reversed.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** feed lever; check lever; pawl; reversing arm; mounting plate; ribbon spool; key lever

**Improves on:**

- earlier ribbon reverses: feed and reverse work independently and the reverse parts rest except when reversing
