**How it works (reading):** Two radial type wheels, capitals and small letters, with spring-held blocks pressed by finger knobs through slots, turned by a crank to an arrow.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Teach beginners English letters in an enjoyable way.

**Solution:** Two radial type wheels, capitals and small letters, with spring-held blocks pressed by finger knobs through slots, turned by a crank to an arrow.

**How it works:** Type blocks are set radially between a wooden centre disc and a side plate, held to the disc by springs. Two such wheels carry rubber stamps, one capitals and one small letters, with finger knobs passing through radial slots in the side plate. The wheels are fixed together on a shaft in the base, with an arrow and a crank. Turning the crank brings the letter under the arrow; the operator presses the knob of the capital or small letter to stamp it, inked by an ink roller.

> Latin-letter printing machine. Brief description of the drawings. Fig. 1 is a front view of the machine, and Fig. 2 a side view of it, partly cut away. Nature, action and effect of the device. This machine is a semi-high-grade toy-like thing, devised to give beginners in English a correct and enjoyable knowledge of English. Between a wooden centre disc 4 and a side plate 11, type blocks 2 are set radially, and the centre disc 4 and the type blocks 2 are held together by springs 3. Two such type wheels are made, with finger knobs 8 on the type blocks 2 passing through radial slots 9 in the side plate 11; on one wheel are stuck rubber stamps 1 of capital letters 13, on the other of small letters 12. The wheels are mounted on the shaft 10 of the base 14, with a crank 6 at one end of the shaft, and an arrow 5 is fixed on the shaft 10. 7 is an ink roller and 14 the base. So, in this way, capital and small English letters are arranged radially in alphabetical order between the centre disc 4 and the side plate 11, the type blocks 2 being held to the centre block 4 by the springs 3, and the two wheels so made are fixed together as one.
