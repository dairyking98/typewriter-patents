## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon spools

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to Typewriter ribbon spools with center studs.

**How it works (reading):** A removable plate and a slip-on ribbon sleeve lock to the hub by bayonet projections, so the reel is reused like a normal spool.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The bobbins are thrown away with each ribbon, which wastes material.

**Solution:** A removable plate and a slip-on ribbon sleeve lock to the hub by bayonet projections, so the reel is reused like a normal spool.
