## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a flat-plate typewriter, in which the carriage carrying the writing device rests on one in the longitudinal direction of the document movable line switch frame is mounted and across Moves the document to the end of a line automatically and by everyone any point of the line by pressing a button with the help of an electric drive moved back and set to the beginning of the following line.

**How it works (reading):** A single electric motor drives both the carriage and the line-switch frame through separately engaged clutches, either automatically or by key press, with automatic motor shutoff at end positions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage return and line switching on a flat-plate typewriter need separate manual effort, and the drive must stop safely at end positions.

**Solution:** A single electric motor drives both the carriage and the line-switch frame through separately engaged clutches, either automatically or by key press, with automatic motor shutoff at end positions.
