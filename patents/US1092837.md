## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A line lock should positively stop the carriage at line end yet still allow a few more characters to finish a word, then lock again.

**Solution:** A part riding on the carriage engages a fixed part to limit key action and lock the carriage at line end, with a release allowing further movement and automatic re-locking.

**How it works:** The carriage has a rack bar with ratchet teeth, engaged by a fixed escapement dog and a movable dog carried on an arm pivoted to the carriage bed, so the dogs swing across the rack face and wear less. A transverse bar on the escapement frame, moved by a universal bar through bell cranks, pushes the arm against a spring to let the carriage escape one tooth. A line-lock part moving with the carriage meets a non-moving part at line end to stop the keys.
