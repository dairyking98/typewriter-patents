## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The key action needs a light touch, even key dip and equal leverage, with the typebars accelerating toward the printing point.

**Solution:** Each typebar is pivoted below the platen and swings up and back to strike the front of the platen, with its key linked so the motion is uniform across keys.
