## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The main object of this invention is to overcome these objectionable features and to provide a type-bar construction by means of which a greater number of radially-operating type-bars may be grouped within a given space and by which the danger of clashing or collision in the vicinity of the rest or basket during the printing and return strokes of adjacent types is avoided or reduced to the minimum.

**How it works (reading):** Each typebar carries a pivoted type that normally lies in the plane of the bar's stroke and turns relative to the bar to the common working position at the printing point.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In front-strike machines the outer typebars' types sit at steep angles and take much basket space, causing crowding and clashing.

**Solution:** Each typebar carries a pivoted type that normally lies in the plane of the bar's stroke and turns relative to the bar to the common working position at the printing point.

**How it works:** Typebars are arranged in a curve below the platen. Each has a shiftable pivoted type whose normal position is in the plane of its bar's stroke. As the bar swings to print, the type turns relative to the bar through an angle that grows with distance from the central vertical plane (none for the central bar) until it faces the platen. This lets more bars be grouped in a given space and avoids collisions near the rest. Carriage feed uses a conventional key-operated rocker frame, scape-wheel, pinion and rack.
