## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Drive control means for carriage movement

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for propel-' ling the letter-feeding carriages of type writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** An electric motor geared to the carriage drives it in both directions; a carriage-return key at the keyboard reverses the motor through a pole-changer, and a spring returns the key and the reverser.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The inventor says returning a heavy carriage at the end of each line tires the operator.

**Solution:** An electric motor geared to the carriage drives it in both directions; a carriage-return key at the keyboard reverses the motor through a pole-changer, and a spring returns the key and the reverser.
