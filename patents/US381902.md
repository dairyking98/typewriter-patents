## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (musical character printing device) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in devices for printing musical and other characters;

**How it works (reading):** A rotary shaft carries a type-carrier, with sleeves and arms that hold a pointer over the original characters and a second pointer that marks the matching positions on the copy sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copying music by hand from engraved plates is slow and costly.

**Solution:** A rotary shaft carries a type-carrier, with sleeves and arms that hold a pointer over the original characters and a second pointer that marks the matching positions on the copy sheet.
