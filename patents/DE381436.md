> Description Translated from German Storage for the paper roll of typewriters. The invention relates to a bearing for the paper roller on typewriters, in which the roller is driven by a clutch disc is connected to the axle and can therefore be replaced. Paper rollers or paper roller bearings of this type are known. However, cumbersome locks or screw connections have to be loosened or the effect to overcome strong springs. Another disadvantage is that the Lock, which is intended to prevent the unintentional release of the paper roller, can loosen by itself, which in turn results in an unintentional loosening of the paper roller caused. According to the present invention, the interchangeability of the paper roll made possible without an unintentional release of the roller can occur. this is achieved in that the locking bolt securing the sliding clutch disc is secured by a special member itself, which is designed as a scalar bolt can be.
