## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (presetting calculation type for total counter)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter according to patent 321934, in particular the device which for the presetting of the calculation type in question for the total counter serves.

**How it works (reading):** A presetting slide plate, held in its set position by spring pressure and turned by an adjusting bolt, either lets a sprung roller on the car push a coupling linkage that subtracts from the total counter, or slides past so the counter adds.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The total counter of a typewriter-calculator must be set to add or subtract for each type of calculation without complicated switching.

**Solution:** A presetting slide plate, held in its set position by spring pressure and turned by an adjusting bolt, either lets a sprung roller on the car push a coupling linkage that subtracts from the total counter, or slides past so the counter adds.
