## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Return of the carriage and writing device in flat-plate calculating typewriters used geared motors, which could not be regulated precisely or run together.

**Solution:** Use piston-and-cylinder pneumatic drives to return both carriage and writing device simultaneously from one switching device.

**How it works:** A writing device carrying keys and type levers rides on rails of a carriage, which rolls on slide rails of a frame above a horizontal writing plate for a ledger sheet. Pneumatic cylinders move the carriage and the writing device back to their starting positions, started together by one trip device. A brake band and pawl on the rear roller shaft holds the carriage until released by a key. Only the opening is available.
