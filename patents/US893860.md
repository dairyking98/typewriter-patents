## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Platen adjustments for varying the strength of impression, for a varying number of papers, for wear or for alignment, or for print gap adjustment

**How it works (reading):** The platen is lifted off the paper guides and feed rollers, and a detent holds it raised while it still rotates for line spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Inserting thick or pasted sheets is hard because the platen cannot be lifted clear of the guides.

**Solution:** The platen is lifted off the paper guides and feed rollers, and a detent holds it raised while it still rotates for line spacing.
