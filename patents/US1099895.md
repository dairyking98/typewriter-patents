## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Skewing, correcting skew, i.e. changing slightly orientation of material; Methods or arrangements for marking the record carrier in digital fashion by transferring data from a similar or dissimilar record carrier

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a fair-leading mechanism for justifying the position of a master sheet with respect to its tracker board.

**How it works (reading):** A justifying tracker board ahead of the main board uses space-key apertures to pull the master sheet back into alignment before it reaches the main tracker.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The master sheet of an automatic operator slips out of register with the tracker board.

**Solution:** A justifying tracker board ahead of the main board uses space-key apertures to pull the master sheet back into alignment before it reaches the main tracker.
