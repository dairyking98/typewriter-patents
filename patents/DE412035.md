## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wear in the platen carriage lifting guides makes the carriage wobble, and the writer cannot take up the wear.

**Solution:** The columns are adjustable in their length direction and each is gripped between eccentric pairs, so the writer can take up play in the transverse direction without a fitter.

**How it works:** Slotted screws bear on the columns from outside the side walls, and eccentric pins clamp each column in the upper and lower part.
