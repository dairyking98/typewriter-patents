## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writers, and is more particularly directed to means for readily determining where a character or a line of characters to be written will appear upon the paper before the same are written, so that the' paper may be shifted to the exact position to receive the impact of the type on the line or at the point indicated.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When filling in printed blanks it is hard to tell exactly where typing will land, so the paper cannot be aligned exactly.

**Solution:** A scale-bearing indicating bar that is swung to the printing line and shows the printing point regardless of carriage position.

**How it works:** An indicating bar is mounted on projections of the platen carriage with inclined slots and pins, and is raised and lowered by a pivoted lever whose forked end straddles a pin on the bar. Raised, it lies along the bottom of the printing line and indicates the printing point (v of the printing point). A scale on the bar with indices spaced like the characters registers with the printing point when the bar is at the printing line, whatever the carriage position. The operator shifts the paper until the blank lines up, then lowers the bar and types.
