## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulating mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulator stop rod with notches and mechanism that quickly determines the carriage arrest and sets the stops accordingly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Setting tabulator column stops per carriage index takes time when work changes.

**Solution:** Tabulator stop rod with notches and mechanism that quickly determines the carriage arrest and sets the stops accordingly.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
