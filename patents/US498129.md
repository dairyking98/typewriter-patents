## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The Hammond has no scale above the print point, and its feed rollers sit well below the print point so the paper cannot be written on near its bottom edge.

**Solution:** A clip-on scale across the top of the Hammond carriage, with movable clamps that hold the paper straight after it leaves the rollers and with ruled lines for line spacing and margins.

**How it works:** The scale has a horizontal bar with the usual lines indicating letter positions, and two arms depending from its ends. The arms are placed between the sheet-metal inner portion of the carriage cylinder and its end frame, held by a screw or by the spring clamping action, so the bar sits above the printing point like the Remington's scale. Movable clamps slide in from either end along the scale to grip the paper after it has gone above the rollers, letting the sheet be drawn fully past the print point. Ruled lines on the sides and clamps help align the lines when the sheet is repositioned, and marginal lines are marked at one end.
