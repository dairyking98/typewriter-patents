## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In two-colour ribbons with contiguous colour fields, the inks bleed into each other and spoil the ribbon.

**Solution:** The ribbon has two parallel inking fields of different colours separated by an uninked strip, so the inks cannot meet.

**How it works:** A plain uninked intermediate strip between the fields keeps the inks apart on the weave.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** uninked separator strip; two colour fields
