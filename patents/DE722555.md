## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line index wheel coupling to the platen tube needed a special tube and an awkward fit.

**Solution:** Two semicircular clutch jaws on a hub inside the line index wheel are spread apart by a wedge-shaped bumper against springs, so the wheel couples and releases directly to the platen tube.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Reverse gear clutch for typewriters The invention relates on line switching wheel clutches for typewriters o. the like. With in the middle split approximately ring-shaped clutch disc, its semicircular parts articulated, supported on each other and moved apart like a toggle lever by a bumper are. The one according to the invention differs from the known couplings of this type formed in that the semicircular, pendulum-like moving clamping disc parts loosely stored and in a recess of an extension of the line switching wheel hub are provided with pressure rollers carrying lever arms, which are supported by a in the line wheel hub and whose extension wedge-shaped tapered bumper can be moved apart and are under the influence of springs counteracting the action of the bumper. Here, the entire clutch is only organically connected to the line index wheel, i.e. in none of its parts with the roller tube. This is therefore immediately with coupled to the line index wheel or released from it.
