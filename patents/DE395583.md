## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Posting on loose book sheets by ordinary typewriter requires rolling the sheets and aligning them each time, with transfer errors.

**Solution:** A stationary print unit with a carriage moving in line direction and a writing plate sliding crosswise, holding the base posting sheet and underlying sheets flat in any position.

**How it works:** A typebar machine is mounted on a bridge over two slide rails carrying a carriage. A writing plate with a replaceable rubber-insert receiving plate slides perpendicular to line direction. A clamp holds the base sheet and the booking sheets slide under it by millimetre scales, located by pins on a plate fitted to holes on a cardboard cover.
