## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Removing and applying typewriter ribbons is awkward when done by hand.

**Solution:** A stand-alone ribbon winder with a heavy base and detachable spool, set beside the typewriter to remove and wind the ribbon.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** heavy base; detachable spool; spindle
