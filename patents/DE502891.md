## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (transverse summing units with reversing gear)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with several summing units a special drive wheel is provided for each transverse summing unit and the rotary movement all drive wheels for the transverse summing units through a single reversing gear is controlled.

**How it works (reading):** One reversing gear drives all transverse totalizer drive wheels, selected by engaging each vertical counter into working position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Calculating machines with several transverse totalizers each need their own reversing gear.

**Solution:** One reversing gear drives all transverse totalizer drive wheels, selected by engaging each vertical counter into working position.
