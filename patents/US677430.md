## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The joint between typebar and link needed to be as thin as possible within tight space without weakening it.

**Solution:** A thin steel link carries a pivot and segmental boss concentric with the typebar's bearing, with a guard, so the joint is made with a flush segmental surface.
