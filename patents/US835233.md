## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Mounting typebars and giving them case shift.

**Solution:** Typebars on several separate segments that are shifted toward and from the printing point.

**How it works:** A spring-drum drives the carriage rack through gearing to an escapement wheel with feed dogs on a segmental universal bar. Keys act through rock-shafts and the typebar segments move to change case.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** typebar segment; universal bar; escapement wheel; feed dog; spring drum; rack; key
