## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an exchangeable paper trolley support frame for typewriters, which allows the precise guidance of wagons of great length.

**How it works (reading):** A replaceable base plate carries a running-rail support at each end, each support built from two angled consoles tied by longitudinal and cross webs into a rigid whole, with the carriage rails screwed to the flanged ends.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** On long carriages, running rails joined by connecting pieces can bend, so precise carriage guidance is not assured.

**Solution:** A replaceable base plate carries a running-rail support at each end, each support built from two angled consoles tied by longitudinal and cross webs into a rigid whole, with the carriage rails screwed to the flanged ends.
