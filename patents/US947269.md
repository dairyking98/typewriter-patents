## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and its object, generally stated, is to1 machines with an improved mechanism of the character commonly termed a carbon roll mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** A carbon-roll mechanism applied to a Remington machine, with improved devices for handling the roll of carbon or transfer medium and modified machine parts.
