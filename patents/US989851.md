## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in, f s Qlwer typewriter;

**How it works (reading):** Key levers share a common pivot point or fulcrum, and each has an upwardly open notch engaged by a U-shaped lifting spring hooked to a cross-bar supported on two posts, so all keys act evenly on the universal bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The key levers do not all act with uniform effect on the universal bar, and the lifting springs bearing on them need a better fulcrum.

**Solution:** Key levers share a common pivot point or fulcrum, and each has an upwardly open notch engaged by a U-shaped lifting spring hooked to a cross-bar supported on two posts, so all keys act evenly on the universal bar.
