## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Errors in typing need the carriage backed up, but ordinary escapements only advance.

**Solution:** A key-operated force-feed dog engages the spacing rack and drives the carriage backwards one or more spaces.

**How it works:** A removable bracket near the space dogs carries a pivoted laterally acting dog with a spring that returns it to rest. A key lever in the keyboard links to a rocking arm whose end lifts the dog's angle arm, swinging its flange across the rack path between teeth. A spring finger lets the dog return without lifting the spacing-rack frame, and an extended bell-ringing projection on the rack avoids the dog. Shown fitted to a Remington.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** force-feed dog; removable support; key lever; link; rocking arm; leaf spring; spacing rack; space dogs; bell striker

**Improves on:**

- Remington escapement: adds reverse feed through a separate dog
