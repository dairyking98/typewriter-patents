## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (work machine switch lever) · **Subject (patent classes):** Safety devices

**How it works (reading):** The switch-on lever is bent back and to the side with a foot step at its lower end, so the operator can lean on the hanger and work it by foot.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A press lever can only be worked by hand or by foot, and the operator has to hold the lever up.

**Solution:** The switch-on lever is bent back and to the side with a foot step at its lower end, so the operator can lean on the hanger and work it by foot.

- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
