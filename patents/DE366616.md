## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the present invention is to remedy these inconveniences, what is achieved in that the tie rods on their on the associated type levers attacking ends are bent hook-shaped and in a circular arc-shaped comb be guided, in which the slots are milled perpendicular to the base of the machine are in such a way that the bottom of the slot on which the tie rods rest is concentric runs to the arc-shaped type lever axis.

**How it works (reading):** Hook-shaped tie rods, all of equal length, have pinned ends in slots of the type levers and run in a circular-arc comb with slots concentric to the type-lever axis, giving non-clamping motion with increasing type-lever speed and interchangeable levers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Earlier type-lever drives either needed special type-lever shapes or teeth that jam, and the key levers had to be built to differing lengths.

**Solution:** Hook-shaped tie rods, all of equal length, have pinned ends in slots of the type levers and run in a circular-arc comb with slots concentric to the type-lever axis, giving non-clamping motion with increasing type-lever speed and interchangeable levers.
