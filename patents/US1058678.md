## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A separate universal bar to vibrate the ribbon adds parts, and the carriage universal bar has too short a stroke to do it.

**Solution:** Use the carriage-feeding universal bar through a movement-multiplying lever to vibrate the ribbon via cams that also lock it at the print point.

**How it works:** The typebar heels push the universal bar back. A movement-multiplying lever driven by that bar carries a wrist that rides a long cam on the ribbon-vibrating arm. The ribbon is raised to cover the print point before impact and held there by a dwell on the cam. Two dissimilar cams and opposed wrists give two throws for a two-field ribbon. The unit mounts on a slide that moves with the platen shift rail and is removable as a whole.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [INK:ribbon-color](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** ribbon carrier; vibrating arm; movement-multiplying lever; cam; wrist; universal bar; slide; platen shift rail

**Improves on:**
