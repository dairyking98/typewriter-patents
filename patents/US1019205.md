## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Erasing or correcting tables

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in erasing means for type-writing machines, and the object ofmy improvement is to provide aneraser movable from a fixed base on the'machine to'and' from the movable platen of the machine for convenient and self-adjustable use thereon.

**How it works (reading):** An eraser and brush on a fixed base swing to and from the platen, the brush removing erasure litter as the eraser works.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Erasing on the platen leaves litter, and the eraser has to be moved to the platen by hand.

**Solution:** An eraser and brush on a fixed base swing to and from the platen, the brush removing erasure litter as the eraser works.
