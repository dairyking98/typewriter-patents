## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Top-strike typebar machines have a poor key touch, irregular indentation by the types, and weak key-to-typebar connections.

**Solution:** Radial bell-cranks with a high initial leverage soften the touch, with an optional key stop and folded stirrup links that ease assembly.

**How it works:** Typebars on a fulcrum wire in a curved segment are driven by radial bell-cranks on a larger-radius segment. Folded strip links hang each short bell-crank arm from a parallel key lever. A universal bar lies under the key levers. Pivot points are nearly in line at the start, giving large initial leverage so the typebar starts gently, and leverage falls as the stroke continues. A draw-spring returns the parts. A stop can be thrown in to arrest keys before the types strike the platen.
