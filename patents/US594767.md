## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters rely on the operator's force to drive typebars and carriage.

**Solution:** A power-driven typewriter where light key touch releases spring-wound escapement wheels that drive the typebars and spacing.

**How it works:** Each key turns a rock-shaft carrying an escapement pawl with a fixed and a pivoted tang in the path of teeth on an escapement wheel. The wheel has an internal coil spring wound by a shaft whose pinion is turned by a toothed segment meshed with a constantly driven gear. Releasing the pawl lets the spring turn the wheel, working rock-shafts that swing the typebars and also operate the carriage spacing via a double rack.
