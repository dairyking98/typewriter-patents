## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A check needs to be written and a duplicate record made at once, tamper-evident.

**Solution:** A check writer using type-wheels, a printing lever and a spring-drum carriage, with the duplicate held in a locked box.

**How it works:** A box frame with a locked cover holds the duplicate record. A carriage on a square cross-shaft is moved step by step by a spring drum and cord, controlled by escapement dogs and a rack, with word and letter spacing. Type-wheels turned by bevel gears are positioned by slots in a segmental wall, one slot per character, and a printing lever strikes. An eccentric shifts the wheels between small letters and capitals. The check lies on a flat platen.
