## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (matrix-making machine feed) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** Electrically actuated magnets and pawls drive a ratchet cylinder that feeds the matrix carriage, while a cam depresses the selected type-die into the matrix.
