## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of key buttons; Adaptation of switches to receive distinguishing marks with a tactile symbol or indication, e.g. for blind people; Stabilising means therefor using a scissor mechanism as stabiliser

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing machines, and has special reference to an improved keyboard embodying means for facilitating the manipulation of the keys by'the touch method.
