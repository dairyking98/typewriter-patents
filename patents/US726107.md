## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Learning the touch method is hard because keys feel alike and the fingers lose their place.

**Solution:** Key-tops of distinct shapes, arranged so no two same-shaped keys are adjacent under one hand, with guide keys, let fingers recognise each key by feel.

**How it works:** Key-tops, for example on a Smith Premier universal keyboard, are given varied forms independent of their characters. Shapes are grouped so adjacent keys under the same hand never share a form, and certain keys act as guide keys for their neighbours. The operator identifies keys by touch rather than sight or raised characters, which also serves the blind.
