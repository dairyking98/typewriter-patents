## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvement on the inventor's earlier patent 457,258 and application 350,286, which shifted the type-bars vertically; the ribbon change also needed reeling between two ribbons.

**Solution:** Shift the platen in relation to the type-bars to print one of two or more characters on each bar, with a tilting ribbon frame driven directly by the feed lever and spools carrying two ribbons side by side.

**How it works:** Type-bars pivot on a curved cross-bar at the base, each with a projection that sits between the prongs of a key-lever. Key-levers are in two series on two pivot bars, long and short, so the leverage and touch are almost the same. Division plates between the bars guide them and keep their projections in line with the key-lever projections. The carriage guideways or rails are shifted so the platen takes the impression of a different character from each bar. A ribbon-tilting frame is driven directly by the feed lever and feeds the ribbon as it tilts. Spools hold two ribbons side by side so either can be placed in the frame without reeling.
