## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide a device for feeding cards from a suitable receptacle onto a fiat platen or similar writing or printing surface and to position the cards successively in proper place to be printed upon by the Writing mechanism.
