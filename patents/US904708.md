## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to the type bar bearings of typewriting machines and has for its main object to provide an improved construction of ball bearings for the type bars.
