## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An earlier tabulator needed the carriage-borne stop to be moved into and out of line with the column stop.

**Solution:** Give the carriage stop a hinged flexible end section that yields past column stops and can be locked rigid when a tab stop is wanted.

**How it works:** A tabulating stop bar with column stops is mounted on brackets over a Remington-type frame. An arm on the carriage rear bar has a hinged end section that swings freely and so passes column stops without stopping the carriage. A pivoted locking device, operated by a depressed arm and rod, swings against the hinged section to hold it rigid so it stops the carriage on the next column stop. A spring returns the lock.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** tabulating stop bar; column stop; carriage arm; hinged end section; locking device; spring; rack bar; pinion
