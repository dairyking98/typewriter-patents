## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**How it works (reading):** The platen can be pivoted so that the feed rollers lift off, loosening the stacked sheets and carbons so they can be automatically drawn back to the starting position, using a button-operated lifting piece.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copying several sheets with carbon interleaves requires the carbon sheets to be loosened and returned, which is hard to do by hand.

**Solution:** The platen can be pivoted so that the feed rollers lift off, loosening the stacked sheets and carbons so they can be automatically drawn back to the starting position, using a button-operated lifting piece.
