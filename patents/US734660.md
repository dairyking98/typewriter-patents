## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper rolls and manifold copies are hard to handle in typewriter work.

**Solution:** An attachment that feeds continuous paper from rolls or sheets, with tension devices and means to join the manifold apron.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
