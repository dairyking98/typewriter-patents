## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriters need to be compact when carried.

**Solution:** Typebars hinge on a fixed support and fold down while the intermediate levers' pivots shift together on a toggle-moved support.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot on a wire in a casting segment; each is driven by an intermediate lever and a drive rod from the key lever. A felt-lined support under the typebars is moved up and down by paired toggle levers on a cross shaft turned by an outside handle. Unfolding swings all typebars to use position while the intermediate lever pivots slide with them. Folded, typebar noses rest on a cushion in the cover.
