## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (tabulator with number and letter printing) · **Subject (patent classes):** Methods or arrangements for effecting co-operative working between equipments covered by two or more of main groups G06K1/00 - G06K15/00, e.g. automatic card files incorporating conveying and reading operations

**How it works (reading):** Letter carriers are driven without a dwell at the reversal points, while number carriers keep the dwell for adding wheels.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Letter type carriers are slower than number carriers because they have more types and longer travel.

**Solution:** Letter carriers are driven without a dwell at the reversal points, while number carriers keep the dwell for adding wheels.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
