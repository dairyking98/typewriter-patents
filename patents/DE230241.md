**How it works (reading):** The paper roll axis carries a wheel with teeth matching the line-index wheel, so the crank can be turned more than one full revolution without a gear train; OCR of the hub and wheel details is partly garbled.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper-roll hand crank turns the roll less than a full turn, so bill entries needing more paper travel required a transmission gear that complicates the machine.

**Solution:** The paper roll axis carries a wheel with teeth matching the line-index wheel, so the crank can be turned more than one full revolution without a gear train; OCR of the hub and wheel details is partly garbled.
