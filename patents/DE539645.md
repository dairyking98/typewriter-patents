## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters for the blind are too big and heavy because the paper guide takes too much height, yet blind users need to carry them.

**Solution:** A paper roll is inserted sideways into a slotted sleeve advanced by the keystroke rack, with a spring-lifted upper guide roller so a roll can be changed with one hand in a very low machine.

**How it works:** A cylindrical slotted sleeve holds the rolled sheet and is connected to a rack advanced by each keystroke. The sheet runs under an upper guide roller pressed onto a fixed lower guide roller. The right bearing of the upper roller holds it by a spring. The left bearing is a trough with a pivotable spring that presses the roller down in use. Swinging that spring aside lets the right spring lift the roller and opens the trough for a new roll. The roll is pushed into the sleeve and the sheet start is slid under the raised roller, aided by a tapering cross-section the blind person feels with a finger, then the spring is swung back.
