## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Power-driven typewriters with lifting-disc drive are hard to assemble and service because the drive parts are scattered.

**Solution:** Mount each lifting disc with its holding and pressure levers in a self-contained frame of two angled plates, detachably carried on a bearing part on the machine's cross rails.

**How it works:** A constantly rotating rubber-covered drive roller, turned by a motor through worm gearing, engages toothed lifting discs in two rows. Each disc is released by its key lever pulling a holding lever off a catch, and a spring-loaded pressure arm gives the disc its first turn into contact with the roller. The disc then swings a lever that pushes the type lever through a length-adjustable rod. A spring returns the parts. Each unit is a rigid frame fixed with screws to grooved cross rails.
