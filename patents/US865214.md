## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my present invention is to adapt the carriage of a type-writingmachineto be displaced uniformly throughout its movement through different letter spaces in writing, according to requirements, thereby varying the feed, the construction being such that the operating detent device engages direct with the rack secured to the carriage.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage has several interchangeable escapement racks with teeth of different pitches, arranged radially about a common axis, and adjustable detents that can be brought alternately into engagement, so the feed can be set to fractions or multiples of the pitch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage feed can only be adjusted by omitting one tooth of the rack, so changing to type of a different width requires exchanging the toothed rack and detents.

**Solution:** The carriage has several interchangeable escapement racks with teeth of different pitches, arranged radially about a common axis, and adjustable detents that can be brought alternately into engagement, so the feed can be set to fractions or multiples of the pitch.
