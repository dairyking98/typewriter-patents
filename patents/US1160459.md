## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers; Pile receivers with movable end support arranged to recede as pile accumulates the articles being piled one above another and applied at the top of the pile

**How it works (reading):** The chute is made in two telescoping sections, each with a side gage and an introductory shelf, with front pressure rolls sliding along a shaft and a rod with adjustable stops to set the spacing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work sheets of varying width need a chute that can be adjusted to fit them.

**Solution:** The chute is made in two telescoping sections, each with a side gage and an introductory shelf, with front pressure rolls sliding along a shaft and a rod with adjustable stops to set the spacing.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md)

**Parts:** telescoping chute sections; side gages; adjustable stops
