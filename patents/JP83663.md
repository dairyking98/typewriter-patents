## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Spacing on a Japanese-script typewriter is fixed, so small furigana characters cannot be printed beside main characters at a finer pitch while staying level.

**Solution:** An L-shaped lever with an adjustable lever and roller, engaged with the rocking plate, shifts the cylinder slightly and shortens the feed pawl's stroke for the small characters.

**How it works:** The paper cylinder is turned by a feed pawl on a rotating plate feeding a gear; the pawl normally slides over a roller on an adjusting plate fixed to a rocking plate, feeding e.g. four teeth per main character. For furigana, a step on the rocking plate's tail is engaged with a projection on an L-shaped lever pivoted on the cylinder frame. This moves the rocking plate slightly, giving the cylinder a small shift, and brings a roller on a tiltable adjusting lever against the feed pawl so it feeds only half or a third as many teeth. The adjusting lever tilt is set by a screw and pulled by a spiral spring.
