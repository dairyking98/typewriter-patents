## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stencil sheets can buckle or drift during intermittent feed, and the spacing between cut letters is uneven.

**Solution:** A pair of sheet-feeding wheels driven positively by the same hand lever that depresses the dies, with a stop that prevents overtravel so letter spacing stays even.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
