## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon in a vending machine printer must reverse automatically at the end of its travel.

**Solution:** Two oppositely acting pawls joined by a coupling rod slip on pivot slots so when one ratchet resists, the pawls swap roles.

**How it works:** A reciprocating bar driven from a rotating part via rock-shafts and pitman carries two pawls on slotted pivot screws, held down by springs. A rod links their free ends so one pawl drives its spool ratchet while the other idles over its ratchet. When the ribbon is fully unwound the driven spool resists, the pawl rides up in its slot and rocks over, tripping the idle pawl into drive, so winding reverses.
