**How it works (reading):** A flange of the spool is detachable and a slip-on sleeve carrying the ribbon plugs onto the hub, so only the cheap sleeve is discarded while the spool is reused.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon spools are thrown away with the used ribbon, and hooking a new ribbon end onto the spindle is fiddly and dirty.

**Solution:** A flange of the spool is detachable and a slip-on sleeve carrying the ribbon plugs onto the hub, so only the cheap sleeve is discarded while the spool is reused.
