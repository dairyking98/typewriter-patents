## Summary

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an apparatus for achieving a permanent stop on typewriters, typewriters ti.

**How it works (reading):** The pawl on the key lever has a stepped nose: which step engages the coupling tab depends on key lever position, so the normal and the held position give single or permanent stop without an extra link.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The earlier permanent-stop device needed a separate link to re-engage the coupling member with the drive member each time.

**Solution:** The pawl on the key lever has a stepped nose: which step engages the coupling tab depends on key lever position, so the normal and the held position give single or permanent stop without an extra link.

**Features:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
