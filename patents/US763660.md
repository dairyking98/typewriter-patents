## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Fixed fingers and bars on the paper carriage hide the printed lines as the paper moves up.

**Solution:** A controller on the carriage rear and ends presses the paper onto the platen, held by gravity or springs, and leaves the printed lines open to view.
