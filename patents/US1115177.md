## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed and reversing devices let spools over-feed ribbon and needed separate hand reversal.

**Solution:** Automatic ribbon reversing by a shiftable carrier with spring-linked dogs, plus friction brakes on the spools to stop over-feed.

**How it works:** A carrier bar holds two slides joined by a spring, each with a dog; shifting the carrier changes which spool is driven. Feed pawls on a U-shaped rocker frame, moved by the writing action, turn the spool ratchets. A latch with angled faces holds the carrier in either position. Bowed flat-spring friction plates press the spools to prevent unwanted unwinding.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)

**Parts:** carrier bar; slide; dog; spring; ribbon guide; feed pawl; rocker frame; ratchet wheel; latch; friction spring; ribbon spool

**Improves on:**

- earlier ribbon reversing mechanisms: smoother equilibrium of guides and friction devices avoids over-feed
