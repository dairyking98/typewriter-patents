## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing lists or tables of numbers in columns requires repeated spacing-bar presses to reach each column.

**Solution:** Adjustable slides with numbered indicators grip an E-section rail fixed to the carriage, so each slide is set over the scale position of a column; the indicator is set at a mid-scale point and the slides act as tab stops.

**Features:** [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
