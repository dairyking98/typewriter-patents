## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers

**How it works (reading):** A feed table in front of the platen has stops for the sheet edges, and the platen lifts on command (spring-assisted after a lock release) so sheets can be laid under it and aligned, then the platen is lowered; the table edges curve down to avoid creasing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing sheets cannot easily be classified in sequence on a typewriter, and inserting them is awkward.

**Solution:** A feed table in front of the platen has stops for the sheet edges, and the platen lifts on command (spring-assisted after a lock release) so sheets can be laid under it and aligned, then the platen is lowered; the table edges curve down to avoid creasing.

**Parts:** feed table; stops; platen 1; carriage frame 3; rails 1 and 2; ratchet bearing slide 6; side walls 9
