## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actuators for print wires using piezoelectric elements

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a keywheel type-writer, the construction of which not only permits of a firm arresting of the type-wheel, but also regulates the blow of the hammer and at the same time causes the type to be struck by the whole surface of the hammer.

**How it works (reading):** A segment on the type-wheel socket is stopped by raised stop-pins and an angle-lever entering a slit in a driven disc, with an adjustable spring-loaded hammer.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-wheel typewriters need a firm arrest of the type-wheel, a regulated blow, and the whole hammer face striking the type.

**Solution:** A segment on the type-wheel socket is stopped by raised stop-pins and an angle-lever entering a slit in a driven disc, with an adjustable spring-loaded hammer.

**How it works:** Keys in left and right groups lift single-arm levers that raise one of a row of stopping pins and rock a frame whose driving arms engage notches in a square driving disc on the type-wheel socket, turning the wheel left or right until the segment meets the raised pin and a key-lever-pressed angle lever drops into a slit in the disc. A bail on the key-lever rears swings a three-pronged lever that works an escapement wheel for the carriage and a pawl lever driving the hammer. The hammer slides in an adjustable support and a nut and spring set the blow, striking the fixed wheel with its full face.
