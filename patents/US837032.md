## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates totype-writing machines, and more especially to certain combinations of line-lock mechanism, ribbonvibrator mechanism, and escapement mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** The escapement is connected to the keys so it starts after a variable part of the key stroke, while the ribbon vibrator still completes its movement and a line-lock holds the key.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The inventor says the escapement starts at a fixed point in the key stroke, so lost motion and key lock do not work together with the ribbon vibrator.

**Solution:** The escapement is connected to the keys so it starts after a variable part of the key stroke, while the ribbon vibrator still completes its movement and a line-lock holds the key.

- **Inking:** [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)
