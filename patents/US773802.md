## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The letter-space of a Hammond rack-bar machine needs an adjustment that is fine and that the operator can read.

**Solution:** The carriage is slid along the rack bar by a threaded adjusting rod, and a spring click engaging recesses on a boss on the rod gives audible and felt steps and reads out the adjustment.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Numbers:** 2.5 rod revolutions = one letter space; 4 recesses per revolution; one recess step = one tenth of a letter space
