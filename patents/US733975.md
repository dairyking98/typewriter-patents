## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon guides stationary

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines, and particularly to the ribbon-guide, which is employed adjacent to the printingpoint.

**How it works (reading):** A holder that secures the ribbon guide in its guideway, shiftable to allow removal, mounted to the segment and frame.

**Problem:** The ribbon guide can drop loose or is hard to remove from its guideway.

**Solution:** A holder that secures the ribbon guide in its guideway, shiftable to allow removal, mounted to the segment and frame.
