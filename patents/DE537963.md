## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a typewriter with separate numeric keys, pressing a numeric key while the calculating mechanism is in use must be prevented, and earlier covers changed the keyboard layout that operators rely on.

**Solution:** A device excludes actuation of the writing mechanism by the numeric keys when the arithmetic mechanism is active, by driving the numeric writing keys from arithmetic members so that the keypad stays in place and cannot be pressed.

**How it works:** The numeric writing keys are held in position by a locking linkage driven by the arithmetic mechanism, so they cannot be pressed while calculating; the keyboard image is unchanged.
