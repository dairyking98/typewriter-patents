## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Address-list work needs several inked copies written in one operation.

**Solution:** Several narrow paper strips from rolls behind the machine are interleaved with two auxiliary ribbons on the carriage, fed around a short platen.

**How it works:** A portable support behind the cabinet holds four paper rolls whose strips go up over a guide on the carriage and around a narrow platen. Two auxiliary ribbons on carriage spools run lengthwise along the platen over the printing point, interleaved with the strips: two ordinary and two tissue. Mechanism feeds the ribbons and reverses their direction. Applied to a Smith Premier.

**Parts:** paper roll support; auxiliary ribbon; ribbon spool; guide roller; narrow platen; carriage; latch for rolls

**Numbers:** four paper rolls; platen for strips about 2 in. wide
