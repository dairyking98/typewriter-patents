## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar joints need to be more compact, adjustable for take-up, and securely locked so the adjusted part does not work loose.

**Solution:** A ball-bearing type-bar joint with an adjustable cap cone and a locking nut that binds the adjustable portion of the joint on the hanger.
