## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists of certain novel features of construction, combination and arrangement of parts hereinalter described and claimed.

**How it works (reading):** A stationary cylinder and a sliding telescoping cylinder, moved by fluid pressure or suction through a valve controlled from the keyboard, actuate the type levers, return the carriage and shift the platen after each line.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Operating the type levers, returning the carriage and shifting the platen all need a separate linkage for each action.

**Solution:** A stationary cylinder and a sliding telescoping cylinder, moved by fluid pressure or suction through a valve controlled from the keyboard, actuate the type levers, return the carriage and shift the platen after each line.
