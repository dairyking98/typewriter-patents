## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing is slow or noisy, and one spot of the ribbon is struck repeatedly.

**Solution:** A universal switch bracket driven by the keys couples ribbon spool axis pivoting with the drive, so the ribbon switches in one direction and feeds in the other.

**How it works:** Each key stroke moves the universal bracket, which pivots the drive axis in one direction and drives the feed in the other.
