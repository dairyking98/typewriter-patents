## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The operator needs an always visible scale to set a point on the paper to the printing point; the scale on the platen can be obscured.

**Solution:** Print margin and tabulating scales on the flat horizontal dust guard plates beside the printing point, read against pointers on a rod that moves with the platen.

**How it works:** Two horizontal cover-plate dust guards lie on either side of the typebar guide, below and in front of the platen. A scale is inscribed near each plate's rear edge. The left scale reads from the left side as a margin scale, the right one from the right side as a tabulating scale. A pointer on a transverse rod below the platen, clamped by a set screw, moves with the platen and indicates the scale. The pointers are set once during assembly.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cover or case](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md)

**Parts:** platen; dust guard plate; scale; pointer; transverse rod; set screw; typebar guide

**Improves on:**

- platen-mounted scale: a supplementary scale on the frame is always in view and can use large figures
