## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** Divide the carriage travel into sections marked by route stops that keep the position stops locked until the chosen section is reached.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing combined column and position tabulators need repeated key presses when several column stops are passed before the desired column.

**Solution:** Divide the carriage travel into sections marked by route stops that keep the position stops locked until the chosen section is reached.

**How it works:** Position keys raise position stops into the path of column stops on rods. A further rod carries route stops that divide the carriage travel into sections. A locking device blocks the position stops until the carriage has passed the chosen section, then releases them so the carriage stops at the first column stop in that section. Pressing a key uncouples the carriage from its escapement in the known way.
