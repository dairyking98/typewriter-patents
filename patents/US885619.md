## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to type bar and hanger constructions and the objects of said invention are to provide sim le and efficient means for preventing latera and also endwise movements of the type bars and thus preserving the alinement of the writing.
