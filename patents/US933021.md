## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to line-spacing mechanism and coperating devices for typewriters, and has for its object the provision of improvements as willbe hereinafter set forth.

**How it works (reading):** A sub-carriage carrying a line-space mechanism with a ratchet on the platen, a support arm, and a spacing lever.
