## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wants a simple, durable and effective typewriter, covering the platen carriage, shift, line spacing, escapement, release and ribbon mechanism.

**Solution:** A complete typebar machine with a shifting roller carriage, clutch escapement wheel, and ribbon feed, reverse and cross movement.

**How it works:** Key levers with hooks oscillate a universal bar that drives the escapement. The typebars carry two types each, selected by shifting the platen-carriage. A carriage escapement wheel with driving pinion and clutch, a progress dial, a casting-off mechanism, line spacing, paper guides, release key and a ribbon mechanism with lengthwise, reverse and crosswise motion are claimed. Only the introduction is in the excerpt.
