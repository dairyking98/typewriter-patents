> Description Translated from German Type lever rest bearing The invention relates to a type lever rest bearing for typewriters. Such rest camps are supposed to be caused by the constant opening of the Type levers are neither destroyed nor changed in their shape and hardness. Further the impact of the type lever should cause as little noise as possible. The type levers should come to rest quickly and spring back as little as possible. According to the invention, these conditions are met to a high degree by .ein wickerwork resting cushion made of cotton or the like, in particular one made of cords. First, technology has mastered the creation of such Braid enough to guarantee great evenness and little variability to offer, and secondly, the energy of the type lever falling back is reduced by low temporary change in shape of the braid threads noiselessly consumed without the type lever springs back. The braided pillow has so much elasticity that it is the individual threads keep returning to their original position and none permanent impressions of the type levers arise.
