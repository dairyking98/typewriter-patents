## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type lever rest bearing for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** A woven braided cord cushion, of cotton or metal, is held at its ends with sheet metal clips and catches the lever's fall; its elasticity lets the threads return so no permanent impressions form.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Type lever rest cushions were damaged or dented by repeated impact, and the lever rebounded and made noise.

**Solution:** A woven braided cord cushion, of cotton or metal, is held at its ends with sheet metal clips and catches the lever's fall; its elasticity lets the threads return so no permanent impressions form.
