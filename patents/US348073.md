## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carbon paper and semi-carbon are the usual transfer media, and the inventors want a substitute transfer surface (off-topic: chemistry of carbon sheet).

**Solution:** A sheet of tissue or fabric coated with a precipitate of dye-wood solution mixed with oil or wax, ground warm and applied to paper, to serve as a transfer surface instead of carbon.
