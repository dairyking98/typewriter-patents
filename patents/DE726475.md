## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (plug-in insertion device for booking machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for condensed record, tally strip, or like work using two or more papers, or sets of papers, e.g. devices for switching over from handling of copy material in sheet form to handling of copy material in continuous form and vice versa or point-of-sale printers comprising means for printing on continuous copy material, e.g. journal for tills, and on single sheets, e.g. cheques or receipts in which one paper or set is fed towards printing position from the front of the apparatus

**How it works (reading):** A second soft resilient rail, bowed toward the platen and clamped on the first rail, holds the sheet already inserted while the next is fed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Holding a second or later pre-inserted sheet needs a special operation and takes space.

**Solution:** A second soft resilient rail, bowed toward the platen and clamped on the first rail, holds the sheet already inserted while the next is fed.

**How it works:** A resilient second rail holds the first sheet while the next is inserted without lifting rails.
