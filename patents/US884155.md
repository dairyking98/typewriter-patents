## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dust and rubber grit from erasures fall into the type-bar bearings of front-strike machines, making them sluggish and hard to clean.

**Solution:** Shielding and sealing means keep dust off the type-bar bearings, which are also easier to get at for cleaning.

**Features:** [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)
