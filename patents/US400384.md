## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Special purpose keyboards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-writing machines of the lever class, and particularly to that species of machines wherein the connecting-rods are vertically arranged and disposed in a circle, and are operated by means of keylevers or other devices which extend to the front of the machine, where they are provided with finger-keys for manipulation by the user of the machine.
