## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Left/right platen sideways-feed change-over used many shifting gears, which was complicated, needed precise work and often failed.

**Solution:** Two opposed racks on a rockable cross bar straddle one one-way drive gear, and a handle rocks the bar to mesh one rack or the other.

**How it works:** A cross bar is pivoted between the two platen side plates, and a rack carrier with forked legs hangs from it, each leg carrying a rack with teeth facing the other. A drive gear that turns one way only sits between the racks on the machine frame. A change-over handle, a crank lever on the side plate, moves a link that turns a change-over disc fixed to the cross bar. This rocks the carrier so one rack leaves the gear and the other meshes on the opposite side, reversing the platen's travel. An over-centre spring holds the chosen rack in mesh, and stops limit the disc's turn.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Platen sideways-feed change-over device for a Japanese typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a platen sideways-feed change-over device for a Japanese typewriter, in which a cross bar is pivoted so as to span between the two side plates that carry the platen at its two ends; two racks with their rows of teeth facing each other are hung from and fixed to this cross bar; these racks are placed on the two sides of a drive gear that turns in one direction only; and the cross bar is rocked by working a change-over handle on the side plate so that one or the other of the two racks meshes, which changes the direction in which the platen's sideways feed drives it. Its object is to remove the drawbacks of earlier devices, which made the left/right change by using many gears, to simplify the construction, and to make the action sure and smooth.
