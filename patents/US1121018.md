## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The feed pawl of a platen line-spacing ratchet clicks over the teeth on its return stroke, which makes noise in a noiseless typewriter.

**Solution:** The feed pawl, loosely pivoted on the line-space lever, is held clear of the ratchet and is swung in on the working stroke and out on the return by friction drag of its tail against a plate.

**How it works:** A crown ratchet wheel sits on the platen shaft. A line-spacing lever pivoted on a bracket of the carriage carries a loosely pivoted feed pawl whose tail rubs on a friction plate. When the lever is pulled, the drag swings the pawl into a ratchet tooth, so further motion turns the platen; a fixed cam-faced lug limits the thrust. On release, the spring returns the lever and the drag swings the pawl out at the start of the return stroke so it rides free without clicking. A stepped detent turned by a knurled knob and held by a pin in depressions chooses which shoulder stops the pawl tail, which sets where the pawl engages and hence the line-space amount.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen shaft; crown ratchet wheel; line-spacing lever; feed pawl; pawl tail piece; friction plate; fixed cam lug; stepped detent; return spring; knurled knob; locating pin
