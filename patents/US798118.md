## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to the paper holding and guiding mechanism of a type-writing machine supported on the platen-holder and operated in connection with the platen to retain in position sheets of paper, cards, envelops, &c.

**How it works (reading):** A paper-finger bar is supported on the platen holder and works with the platen to hold sheets of various widths in position.
