## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and the object of said invention may be generally stated tobe to'simplify certain features of the construction such as the escapement and carriage release mechanism and the ribbon vibrator mechanism.

**How it works (reading):** Simplified key lever, sub-lever and pull link arrangement with segmental type bars, and simplified escapement, carriage release and ribbon vibrator.
