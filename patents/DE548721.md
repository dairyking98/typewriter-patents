## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (control strip feed for cash registers)

**How it works (reading):** The printed strip is moved back and forth by a machine cam, with a tensioner keeping the strip taut; line feed is by a keyway disc and pawl.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Control strip transport on cash registers is complicated and the strip is not kept tight.

**Solution:** The printed strip is moved back and forth by a machine cam, with a tensioner keeping the strip taut; line feed is by a keyway disc and pawl.

**How it works:** Lifting discs move a lever and roller that reciprocate the strip; a pawl and keyway disc advance the take-up reel.
