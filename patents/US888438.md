## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dust and dirt from the atmosphere or erasers enter the working parts, and impact noise and vibration are high.

**Solution:** A dust-tight bottom closure plate and ribbon box enclose the working parts, with rubber feet, and a reflecting surface in the ribbon box lights the printing point.

**Features:** [FRM:cover-enclosure](../features/frm-cover-enclosure.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)
