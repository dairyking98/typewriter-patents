## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to typewriters, where the lightweight design is particularly important.

**How it works (reading):** The mechanisms mount on a rib-like steel frame that carries the fasteners, plus a light rigid aluminium subframe attached only to its inner surface, so the outer surface stays smooth and can be polished.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A light, strong, tasteful portable machine is hard to get because a plain structural frame is unattractive or weak, or heavy if made strong.

**Solution:** The mechanisms mount on a rib-like steel frame that carries the fasteners, plus a light rigid aluminium subframe attached only to its inner surface, so the outer surface stays smooth and can be polished.
