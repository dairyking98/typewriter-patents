## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Electric paper-cylinder feed on earlier machines needs repeated handle presses for long moves and is not smooth.

**Solution:** Several clutches on the cylinder drive gear engage a motor-driven disc, each self-releasing on a brake, with only one controlled by the handle, giving a single fixed step or continuous travel.

**How it works:** A handle press withdraws a brake piece and lets a spring-loaded clutch pin drop into a hole of a constantly turning disc, turning a drive gear that moves the carriage rack through worm and gear train. Each clutch rides up a wedge-shaped fixed brake after one interval, releasing; the next clutch arrives at the handle brake, and if the handle is still held it engages, so travel continues. Released quickly, only one interval (one line) is fed. Return is by a spring drum with cord, the pinion being slid out of the rack by a return button.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. This invention concerns a paper-cylinder feed device for an electric typewriter in which the driving gear of the cylinder's drive mechanism, which can be engaged with and disengaged from the paper cylinder, is coupled to a motor-driven disc by clutches; several clutches are set on the driving gear at suitable intervals round its circumference; each clutch is provided with a brake that automatically puts it out of action as it turns round with the driving gear; and only one of these is controlled by a handle. Its object is a feed device for an electric typewriter that moves the paper cylinder smoothly and lightly by electric power, either one fixed interval at a time or several times that length continuously in one operation.
