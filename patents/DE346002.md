## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (ribbon inking for printing forms) · **Subject (patent classes):** Continuous, e.g. endless, band apparatus

**How it works (reading):** The ink ribbon is stretched over a counter-bearing so the form rolls over the ribbon to ink it, and the ribbon is advanced in increments over the counter-bearing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ink pressed out of an inking ribbon during impression and rapid ribbon wear come from the ribbon lying between the type and the sheet.

**Solution:** The ink ribbon is stretched over a counter-bearing so the form rolls over the ribbon to ink it, and the ribbon is advanced in increments over the counter-bearing.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Backing or anvil behind the paper](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
