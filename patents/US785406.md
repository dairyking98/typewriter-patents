## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition; Switches having rectilinearly-movable operating part or parts adapted for pushing or pulling in one direction only, e.g. push-button switch having a plurality of operating members associated with different sets of contacts, e.g. keyboard with contacts carried by or formed from layers in a multilayer structure, e.g. membrane switches

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in connection with keyboards.

**How it works (reading):** Duplicate keys of the most common characters are added between the main keys, so common combinations are struck with little hand movement; shown on a linotype keyboard.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A keyboard has one key per character, so the keys for common letter combinations are far apart.

**Solution:** Duplicate keys of the most common characters are added between the main keys, so common combinations are struck with little hand movement; shown on a linotype keyboard.
