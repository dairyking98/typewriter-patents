## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** The finger meets heavy resistance when the type carrier strikes the platen.

**Solution:** A spring resisting the rocker arm with increasing tension, with the key, link and arm pivots in line (dead centre) at impression, gives a delicate touch.

**How it works:** The impression key is linked to a swinging arm which drives the type-carrier toward the platen. The arm works against a spring-controlled oscillating support, so spring tension rises through the stroke. At impression the three pivots are in line, so the toggle is on dead centre and the carrier arrives gently with maximum spring resistance. Shown in a Wellington typewriter.
