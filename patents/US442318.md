## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Returning the carriage requires the operator to take attention and hands from the keyboard.

**Solution:** A key at the keyboard operates a compound lever that engages a pinion with an auxiliary rack on the carriage, returning it and turning the spacing roller.

**How it works:** On a Remington-type machine, the carriage is pulled by a spring through a band, tensioned when the carriage is returned. An auxiliary rack is attached parallel to the carriage rack. A pivoted lever carries a pinion controlled by a dog; a link and anchor-shaped catch connect this lever to a compound lever whose handle forms a key at the right side of the keyboard. Pressing the key raises the pinion into the rack and positively drives the carriage back, and the spacing roller is turned on return. The device is an attachment fitting different machines.

**Parts:** auxiliary rack; pinion; pivoted lever; link; anchor catch; compound lever; return key; carriage spring; band; spacing roller; bearing plate
