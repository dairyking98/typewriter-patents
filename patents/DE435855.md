## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Carriage stepping tooth and stop lever were in continuous contact and noisy.

**Solution:** The loose tooth and stop lever meet only at the end positions and are joined by a tension spring giving a cushioned stop.

**How it works:** A tooth carrier pivoting on pointed screws has a fixed tooth and a loose tooth. A key stroke swings the carrier so the loose tooth leaves the escape wheel and the fixed tooth enters. The spring pulls the loose tooth out to hit a spring-held stop lever with a recess that yields slightly. On return, the loose tooth enters the next gap and the escape wheel, driven by the carriage spring, takes it until it catches again on the stop lever.
