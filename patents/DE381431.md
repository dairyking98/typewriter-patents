## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Character-by-character adjustment

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type lever drive of typewriters, in which between two articulated 'files' the drive connection a spring is arranged such that it is pressed down the button is cocked when released.

**How it works (reading):** A sliding lock on the pull rod with a toothed clamping lever lets each drive spring tension be adjusted without cutting it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Tension of the type lever drive springs is hard to set, since they are cut or stretched and soldered locks slip.

**Solution:** A sliding lock on the pull rod with a toothed clamping lever lets each drive spring tension be adjusted without cutting it.
