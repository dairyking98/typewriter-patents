## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Denomination stops on a tabulator are usually held ineffective, and the construction here needs them to be normally effective so the carriage can be arrested at the desired column.

**Solution:** Key-operated devices move one or more denomination stops out of effective position, so the first stop not moved co-acts with the column stop to arrest the carriage at the desired point.
