## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Envelopes must be fed in around the platen and then turned by hand, so the address lines do not land at the same place on each envelope.

**Solution:** A positioning device on the carriage lets the envelope be placed quickly, with paper fingers that engage it, so every envelope is addressed at the same distance from its lower edge.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
