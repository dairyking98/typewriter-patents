## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction automatically and characterised by the use of particular reversing control means the reversing-feeler engaging buttons or the like secured to the ribbon near its ends

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a ribbon switching device for a power-driven Typewriter, in which the type lever by a spring-loaded, by the Universal rail that can be triggered by buttons is brought to the stop, the universal rail then retrieved by a motor-driven shaft and by one when pushing any key that is triggered again is automatically locked.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-loaded universal rail is released by the key, runs forward, and is returned by a motor-driven shaft; it is linked by a rod and ratchet lever to the ribbon switch, so the ribbon reverses or advances without extra key pressure.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Switching the ribbon in a power-driven typewriter by the key made the operator push the key with extra force, because the ribbon switch was loaded through the key lever.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded universal rail is released by the key, runs forward, and is returned by a motor-driven shaft; it is linked by a rod and ratchet lever to the ribbon switch, so the ribbon reverses or advances without extra key pressure.

**Features:** [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
