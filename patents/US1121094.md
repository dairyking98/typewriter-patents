## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-strike visible typebar machine is bulky to carry; the inventor wants it to fold into a compact package without disturbing the key and typebar relation.

**Solution:** Mount the carriage, platen, ribbon, escapement, spring drum, bell and line-spacing on one vertically shiftable, foldable support that tips forward over the keyboard when not in use.

**How it works:** Key levers operate front-strike typebars and, through a universal bar and rocking frame, the escapement and ribbon feed. The platen, carriage bed and all the mechanism ride on slides that a cap-shift lever or figure-shift lever lifts to two stop heights set by adjustable screws, with a lock lever to hold them. Foldable bars let the whole carriage assembly swing forward over the keys, and the ribbon and escapement are out of engagement with the keys when folded. Ribbon spools are ratchet-driven by the rocking-frame levers and reverse by tightening a nut on one spool, and a bell rings at line end from the carriage spring drum.
