## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to tabulating attachments for type-writers;

**How it works (reading):** A tabulating attachment fitted to any standard typewriter: a scale with a stepped stop that a pawl engages to step the carriage to set columns, with a brake and key-bar.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing long lists of figures or tables on a standard typewriter is slow and error-prone.

**Solution:** A tabulating attachment fitted to any standard typewriter: a scale with a stepped stop that a pawl engages to step the carriage to set columns, with a brake and key-bar.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
