## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of type-levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type lever for typewriters and the like.

**How it works (reading):** The lever is three separately made pieces: a hardened head, a light sheet-steel hollow centre piece and a bearing piece, so the centre is lighter than the head.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A heavy one-piece typebar mass slows striking, increases bearing wear and limits writing speed.

**Solution:** The lever is three separately made pieces: a hardened head, a light sheet-steel hollow centre piece and a bearing piece, so the centre is lighter than the head.
