## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on other surfaces than ordinary paper on pretreated paper, e.g. parchment, oiled paper, paper for registration purposes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a process for the production of documents on typewriters without ribbon and colorants on accompanying paper.

**How it works (reading):** Metal types (silver, nickel silver or brass) are struck directly onto special paper that has mineral fillers such as chalk or barium carbonate in the surface, so the impression is legible without any ribbon.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typing needs a ribbon and colorants that must be renewed often, which is costly.

**Solution:** Metal types (silver, nickel silver or brass) are struck directly onto special paper that has mineral fillers such as chalk or barium carbonate in the surface, so the impression is legible without any ribbon.
