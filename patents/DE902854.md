## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German One used for office machines, especially for typewriters earlier keys made of non-metallic molding compounds. The characters were ... either painted on or engraved. These keys had the disadvantage that the characters soon tapped, making machine operation difficult and the machine got a bad look. Such buttons are also against hand sweat not permanent and therefore unhygienic. But they could easily be put on the button levers because the material used is elastic. In order to eliminate the disadvantages, printed buttons were developed Paper flakes, glass flakes and rings. These keys have the advantage that the finger hitting the keys receives a guide that the cover slip the Dissipates shock heat and that they are hygienic, especially when celluloid key rings be used. Disadvantages are that the drawing plates rotate and easily yellow and the edge ring interferes with writing. In addition, the Keys are difficult to remove deposited dust. These keys also give typewriters looking bad soon. In addition, the buttons are attached to the button levers difficult. To remedy these shortcomings is the purpose of this invention.
