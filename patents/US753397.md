## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to that member of a type-writer or like machine on which the typebars are pivotally supported, and has for its object to efiiciently protect the pivot portions of the type-bars from dust or other foreign matter.

**How it works (reading):** The type-bar segment that carries the pivoted type-bars is shielded at the front of its slots, protecting the pivot portions of the type-bars from dust.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Dust, paper particles and other foreign matter enter the slots of the segment at the type-bar pivots.

**Solution:** The type-bar segment that carries the pivoted type-bars is shielded at the front of its slots, protecting the pivot portions of the type-bars from dust.
