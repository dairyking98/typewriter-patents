## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus or arrangements for feeding ink ribbons or like character-size impression-transfer material

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to writing machines, particularly those of the front strike class, and its main object is to improve and simplify the ribbon mechanism, and to pro vide improved means for automatically reversing the feed of the ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** A ribbon reverser that automatically reverses the ribbon feed when the ribbon is paid off from either spool on a front-strike machine.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon feed has to be simplified and reversed automatically when the ribbon runs out on either spool.

**Solution:** A ribbon reverser that automatically reverses the ribbon feed when the ribbon is paid off from either spool on a front-strike machine.
