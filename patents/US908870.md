## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type-checker for checks) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-checkers, my more particular object being to produce a device somewhat similar in its action to a typewriter, but used for the special purpose of making out checks and the like, and of preserving a record of the same.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage on rails carries a platen and check-holder, with multicolour ribbon reels, a carbon roll and a writing-paper roll for the record, and a lever for line spaces.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter-like machine is needed for making out checks that also keeps a permanent record of what was written.

**Solution:** A carriage on rails carries a platen and check-holder, with multicolour ribbon reels, a carbon roll and a writing-paper roll for the record, and a lever for line spaces.
