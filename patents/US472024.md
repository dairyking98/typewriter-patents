## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebars could jam if one key was held while others were struck.

**Solution:** A pitman rod rigid on each key lever lifts the type-bar by a curved finger and slips off at the top, so the bar falls back by gravity even while the key is held.

**How it works:** The pitman rises from the key lever to a bearing yoke and its shoulder rests on a curved finger on the type-bar. Depressing the key lifts the bar to strike the ribbon. At the top the finger tilts so the shoulder slides off, and a pin kicks the pitman outward if needed, letting the bar drop. The pitman's own spring snaps the shoulder back over the finger on release. A rock-shaft and dog with a perforated annular plate work the carriage rack.
