## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to inking bands or ribbons which are moved constantly or intermittently, the ends of which are fixed on two winding-spools and are wound alternately from one spool onto the other, and vice versa, and has for its object a means for automatically reversing the direction of movement of the ribbon after the winding up on one of the spools has been completed.

**How it works (reading):** A sudden snap-action reversal: a counter-roller resting on the nearly full spool pushes a notched slide past a spring catch, which snaps over and swaps the two feed pawls.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal between two spools was done by hand or by complicated, unreliable automatic devices.

**Solution:** A sudden snap-action reversal: a counter-roller resting on the nearly full spool pushes a notched slide past a spring catch, which snaps over and swaps the two feed pawls.

**How it works:** Each spool has a ratchet wheel advanced a tooth per stroke by a pawl. Counter-rollers rest on the spools; as one spool nears full the roller is pushed back and moves a two-notched slide. A spring-loaded catch lever then jumps the slide to the next notch in a jerk. A pin on that slide shifts the pawl-carrying slide so one pawl disengages while the other engages at once, avoiding a dead point.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; winding spool; ratchet wheel; pawl; counter-roller; notched slide; catch lever; spring; pawl slide

**Improves on:**

- hand-reversed typewriter ribbons and time-recorder reversing mechanisms: single simple snap action, no dead point
