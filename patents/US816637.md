## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to arresting-pads for type-writers-that is to say, to the pads upon which the type-bars strike as they return to position of rest after executing the printing stroke.

**How it works (reading):** The arresting pad is built of thin, flexible, inelastic metal (tin foil, thin lead or copper, or fine wire-gauze) folded into layers and rolls, alternated with a thin fabric separator, so it absorbs the impact of the return stroke without resilience and the layers do not stick together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars rebound off their arresting pads on the return stroke, causing bars to interfere with adjoining bars and slowing successful operation.

**Solution:** The arresting pad is built of thin, flexible, inelastic metal (tin foil, thin lead or copper, or fine wire-gauze) folded into layers and rolls, alternated with a thin fabric separator, so it absorbs the impact of the return stroke without resilience and the layers do not stick together.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [IMP:anvil-backing](../features/imp-anvil-backing.md)

**Parts:** pad of folded metal foil layers; separating fabric; type-bars; platen
