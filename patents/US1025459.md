## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Addressing machines need a stencil-plate receptacle that stays rigid, can be reversed, keeps plates in order, and leaves the table clear.

**Solution:** A receptacle holds a pile of stencil plates rigidly, is reversible, and is supported near the printing devices; a sliding pusher removes the lowest plate.

**How it works:** A sliding pusher on a reciprocating frame moves the lowest stencil plate out of the pile and holds it in transit for a set time; the pile guides also support it.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** stencil-plate receptacle; sliding pusher; reciprocating frame; controlling device
