## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing downwardly on flat surfaces, e.g. of books, drawings, boxes, envelopes, e.g. flat-bed ink-jet printers on bank books or the like

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates more particularly to that class of typewriters designed for travelin g over and writing upon the pages of open books, or other fiat surfaces, which cannot be conveniently fed through or inserted in the ordinary machine.

**How it works (reading):** A frame travels on rack-bars resting on the page, with the carriage on a rail held by tilting crank-arms so it can be lifted to inspect the writing.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriting in bound books needs a machine that travels over the page rather than holding paper in a carriage.

**Solution:** A frame travels on rack-bars resting on the page, with the carriage on a rail held by tilting crank-arms so it can be lifted to inspect the writing.

**How it works:** Parallel rack-bars lie on the book and the base-frame moves along them by pinions with levers for line spacing. Two standards carry crank-arms joined by a rail; the type carriage rolls on this rail with V-grooved wheels and can swing backward and upward to expose the writing. Flanges and set-screws stop the arms at the right height, and weights on the arm rear ends counterbalance the carriage.

**Features:** [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)
