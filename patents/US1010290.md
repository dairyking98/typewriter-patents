## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machines needed any number of silent keys (printing without carriage feed, e.g. accents) anywhere on the keyboard.

**Solution:** A rock shaft with adjustable arms or plates lets silent-key links operate the ribbon vibrator without moving the universal bar, so the escapement stays idle.

**How it works:** On the Royal Standard, a printing key pulls the typebar and the universal bar, which actuates ribbon vibrator and escapement. For silent keys a rock shaft behind the universal bar carries a wiper arm that strikes the ribbon-vibrator link. A silent key's link pulls a rod or plate on the shaft, rocking it; the ribbon vibrates but the universal bar and escapement do not move. Arms or plates clamp by set screw anywhere along the shaft.
