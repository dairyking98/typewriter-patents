## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for setting or restoring tabulation stops

**How it works (reading):** The longitudinal parts of the support rod have hook-like projections that interlock to form cavities for tab stops, so thin parts are locked together along their length, with ball-catch locks and grooved stops that can be used in any cavity.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tab stop support rods must be strong enough to hold stops firmly, but thick rods are costly and the stops can shift when split rods are loosely joined.

**Solution:** The longitudinal parts of the support rod have hook-like projections that interlock to form cavities for tab stops, so thin parts are locked together along their length, with ball-catch locks and grooved stops that can be used in any cavity.
