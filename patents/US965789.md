## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line-spacing mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide an improved mounting of the platen which enables it to be readily and expeditiously removed and replaced.
