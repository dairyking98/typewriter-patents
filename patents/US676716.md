## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Printing wheels must be cheap to make and let worn type be replaced.

**Solution:** A wheel body with a separate sheet-metal rim strip bearing raised type, crimped onto the body rim.

**How it works:** The wheel body has a hub, web and cylindrical rim projecting on both sides. A sheet-metal strip with raised type characters, equal in length to the rim circumference, wraps the rim; its edges are crimped into L-shaped flanges embracing the rim edges and inner surface and abutting the web. No other fastening is needed, and the body is reused with new strips.
