## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Pawl-and-ratchet mechanisms moving a paper or like carriage in the form of a roller rotated for line spacing the pawl being normally in engagement with the ratchet

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the platen-controlling mechanism of typewriting machines and particularly to devices for releasing the platen from the control of the usual line-space wheel.

**How it works (reading):** Dogs on the platen head bite an interior flange of the line-space drum and a button releases them, giving a simple compact clutch.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Solution:** Dogs on the platen head bite an interior flange of the line-space drum and a button releases them, giving a simple compact clutch.
