## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The swing weight is placed below the impression point and engages the type lever directly through a pin and slot, so it brakes the lever near the end of the keystroke without intermediate linkages.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A swing weight braking the type lever through angle levers causes impact, noise and energy loss, and the linkage blocks the view of the writing.

**Solution:** The swing weight is placed below the impression point and engages the type lever directly through a pin and slot, so it brakes the lever near the end of the keystroke without intermediate linkages.
