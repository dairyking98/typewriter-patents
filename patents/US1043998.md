## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a brake for carriages of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** A brake strip on the carriage is clamped between two opposed jaws, one jaw driven by a key, so the reaction of the fixed jaw doubles the braking force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage braking by key pressure is weak.

**Solution:** A brake strip on the carriage is clamped between two opposed jaws, one jaw driven by a key, so the reaction of the fixed jaw doubles the braking force.
