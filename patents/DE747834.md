## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register or booking machine)

**How it works (reading):** Feed control gears the receipt advance to subtotal and final total, and extra hammers print the cheque and control strip.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Receipts with subtotals and final totals need extra feed and printing control on cash registers and accounting machines.

**Solution:** Feed control gears the receipt advance to subtotal and final total, and extra hammers print the cheque and control strip.

- **Uses:** [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** cheque-printing hammer; control-strip hammer; receipt feed control; feed control member; key bank
