## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulators on a Remington typebar machine serve either column or denominational (decimal-place) selection, but not both with the same keys and stops.

**Solution:** Key-controlled tabulating stops, a traveling stop on a sprocket chain moving at a different speed from the carriage, and an automatically shifted indicator let the same keys act first as denominational selectors then as column selectors.

**How it works:** Tabulator keys act through angular levers and links on a bank of pivoted stop levers. Column stops sit on a toothed bar on the carriage. A traveling stop carried on a sprocket chain moves at a rate set by interchangeable driving sprockets of three sizes, so it is effective only over part of the carriage travel. The tabulator indicator plate is shifted automatically as the mechanism changes between denominational and column duty.
