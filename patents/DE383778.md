## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Current account and statement sheets must be joined with carbon in between so that their lines match when typed.

**Solution:** A main sheet with margin tremors and line-spaced lines takes a folded auxiliary sheet over a cutout, both aligned to the typewriter line spacing, with carbon inserted between them.
