## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine with colored subtraction)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates primarily to Line a calculating machine that is used in the management of current accounts, such as entries, Postings, can be used, with numbers in different sections of the worksheets written and added and possibly also subtracted.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage cam sets the subtraction mechanism to zero and releases a two-colour mechanism so debit balances print in black; a credit-balance key switches the machine to addition and prints the number in red.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Debit balances and credit balances must be printed in fixed colours, and a balance star should follow the balance regardless of the operation.

**Solution:** A carriage cam sets the subtraction mechanism to zero and releases a two-colour mechanism so debit balances print in black; a credit-balance key switches the machine to addition and prints the number in red.
