## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register printing device)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a printing device for cash registers and similar machines .

**How it works (reading):** Several print hammers serve one print point, and hammers of different lengths print only part of the type series on the control strip.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing the same entry several times at one point is slow, and partial prints for the control strip are hard to make.

**Solution:** Several print hammers serve one print point, and hammers of different lengths print only part of the type series on the control strip.

**Features:** [IMP:hammer-behind](../features/imp-hammer-behind.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
