## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for automatic Reversal of the longitudinal movement of the ribbon for typewriters and calculators and in particular such a device in which the drive for the ribbon reels on a swingable Shaft mounted between the drive wheels for the two coils serving worm is that it is connected to one or the other by a lifting disc seated on its shaft Drive wheel is brought into connection.

**How it works (reading):** A pawl that is coupled to the drive wheel of each ribbon spool serves as the driver, and a lifting disc on a swinging worm shaft engages the drive wheel of the spool that is due.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal used release pawls on each spool or needed a special driver on the spool, adding parts and cost.

**Solution:** A pawl that is coupled to the drive wheel of each ribbon spool serves as the driver, and a lifting disc on a swinging worm shaft engages the drive wheel of the spool that is due.
