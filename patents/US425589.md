## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type on a Remington-style typebar machine gets soiled and blurs the impression, and existing hand-cranked cleaning brushes need extra manual work.

**Solution:** An oscillating brush in the type basket is driven by the machine's own carriage-return motion (or by hand) and sweeps all the types in one stroke.

**How it works:** When the carriage rack is lifted out of the escapement for return, an auxiliary rack on the rack-bar meshes with a gear wheel on a bracket-mounted shaft. A crank and pitman on that shaft oscillate a brush lever carrying a brush inside the type basket, which sweeps over all the type faces. The gear has more or less than twice the rack's teeth count so it cannot lock on the pitman dead centre. The bracket mounts under the existing rack-bar stop screws.
