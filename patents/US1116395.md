## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen drum effect amplifies key noise and the solid roll is heavy.

**Solution:** A platen built with internal dead air compartments (disks or walls between core and shell) to deaden sound and lighten the roll.

**How it works:** Disks or division walls are cut around a core so that air spaces sit between them, leaving enough support for the shell.
