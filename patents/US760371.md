## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink-ribbon shields or backings

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a removable shield for the exposed portion of the ink-ribbon in a type-writing machine;

**How it works (reading):** A removable shield fits over the exposed portion of the ribbon on the ribbon plate, covering the ribbon at the point where it is exposed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The exposed ink ribbon on the ribbon plate smudges the operator's fingers.

**Solution:** A removable shield fits over the exposed portion of the ribbon on the ribbon plate, covering the ribbon at the point where it is exposed.
