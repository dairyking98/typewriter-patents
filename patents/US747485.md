## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The typewriter should require little manual labour, so parts are actuated by electrically controlled means instead of by the operator.

**Solution:** A solenoid actuates a type-carrier actuating device that is connected to the escapement, so electrical control brings about the actuation of the type carrier and related parts.

**Features:** [IMP:electric](../features/imp-electric.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)

**Parts:** type carrier; solenoid; actuating device; escapement; finger-rest clamps; printing position
