## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the key-actions of writing-machines, and especially to those in a platen;

**How it works (reading):** A front-strike key-action in which type carriers on levers pivoted on a curved fulcrum rod and idle links swing from the front onto an inking pad; the key-action is arranged to cushion the key depression.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key touch is heavy and the key-action is complicated; the type striking the platen gives a jar to the fingers.

**Solution:** A front-strike key-action in which type carriers on levers pivoted on a curved fulcrum rod and idle links swing from the front onto an inking pad; the key-action is arranged to cushion the key depression.

**Features:**

**Parts:** type carriers; levers 9; idle links 11; curved fulcrum rod 13; segment 14; inking pad 6
