## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** A stiffened trapezoidal tongue fixed to the platen holds the marginal edges of the copy layers so the sheet to be marked passes between extra ribbon and platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When copying with an additional ribbon between sheets, copy layers spread away from the platen and the extra ribbon cannot be placed under the right layer.

**Solution:** A stiffened trapezoidal tongue fixed to the platen holds the marginal edges of the copy layers so the sheet to be marked passes between extra ribbon and platen.

**How it works:** Tongue on the platen separates the copy layers so the extra ribbon sits under the right sheet.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
