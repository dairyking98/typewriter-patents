## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (key lever cash register) · **Subject (patent classes):** Transfer mechanisms, e.g. transfer of a figure from a ten-key keyboard into the pin carriage

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key lever cash register, in which both the engagement and disengagement of the adding units, their progression for recording of the set amount, the imprint by the printing unit as well as the setting the display and other registration devices at the touch of a finger or several key levers takes place.

**How it works (reading):** Adders are set and driven through two toothed fork arms that a special button lever switches in the required sequence.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-lever cash registers need the adding units and printing driven by individual key levers.

**Solution:** Adders are set and driven through two toothed fork arms that a special button lever switches in the required sequence.
