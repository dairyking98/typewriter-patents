## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The right-hand margin stop allows only one stop point, so the operator cannot indent and begin lines at either of two fixed points.

**Solution:** A single stop block on the carriage has two differently located stop faces, so the carriage return can be arrested at either of two positions relative to each other.
