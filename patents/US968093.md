## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On shift-key typewriters the operator must press an extra key by hand for capitals, which costs time and moves the hands away from the home position.

**Solution:** A clamp-on knee lever linked to the shift key lever so capitals are shifted by the operator's knee.

**How it works:** A lever is pivoted on a clamp gripping the machine's frame bar by two plates and thumbscrews, or by a bar and flat spring. One end connects to the shift key lever by adjustable hooks, and the other end carries an adjustable depending bar with a curved cross piece. The knee presses the cross piece, tilting the lever and depressing the shift lever which lifts the type segment. The hook can instead be attached to a tabulator key.

**Features:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** clamp; lever; hook; link; depending bar; knee cross piece; thumbscrew; shift key lever

**Improves on:**

- hand-operated shift key: knee operation keeps the hands on the keys
