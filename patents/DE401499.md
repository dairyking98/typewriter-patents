## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (letter writing device on calculators)

**How it works (reading):** A typewriter and an adding machine share one carriage that is pushed alternately in front of the adder or the typewriter, so a clamped sheet can take both.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adding machines cannot print short text, and combined typewriter-adders are too costly and complicated.

**Solution:** A typewriter and an adding machine share one carriage that is pushed alternately in front of the adder or the typewriter, so a clamped sheet can take both.
