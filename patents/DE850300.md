## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops sit on a rack at the back of the carriage, so setting them is awkward.

**Solution:** A hand lever on the carriage lifts the margin stop pawls off the rack and holds the stops at a fixed plate while the carriage is moved to the new position.

**How it works:** Left and right margin stops of side plates ride on the carriage rack, held by spring-loaded pawls. A hand lever turns a shaft with an angle rail that lifts the pawls and at the same time drops their pins into slots of a fixed plate on the frame. Moving the carriage then slides the rack past the held stops. Returning the lever lets the pawls re-engage in the new position.
