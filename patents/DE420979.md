## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Music-note typing machines with a type disc needed repeated carriage shifts to build one note with its stem, beam and flags.

**Solution:** Place noteheads, beams and flags on the type bodies so that heads sit before or behind the pressure centre and beams land on the stem of the previous note without moving the slide.

**How it works:** A type disc above a cross-slide-carried sheet of music has radial bores into which square-headed, tenoned type bodies are inserted. Turning the disc selects the symbol and moving it down prints it. Heads with stems sit partly above and partly below the pressure centre, beam pieces and sixteenth-note beams are offset to meet the stem of the previously printed note, and arc pieces can be strung along the line by shifting only in line direction. Bodies can be small, so many symbols fit on a small disc.
