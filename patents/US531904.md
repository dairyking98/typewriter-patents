**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to means for writing two letters simultaneously, and consists in a certain improved type-form, and in the arrangement and combination of the type-form, type-form positioning mechanism and carriage spacing mechanism.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing is slow and tiring; the inventor wants to write two letters at once to speed operation.

**Solution:** Two type-forms, each moved in two planes by right-hand and left-hand key groups through movement-aggregating devices, carry single and double (compound) characters so two letters print together.

**How it works:** A carriage holds two sheets side by side or a double sheet. Two type-forms each play to their own printing point and move in two arcs or planes; right-hand keys control one motion and left-hand keys the other, using a movement synthesizer so a few keys give many positions. Each type-form has groups of single characters and groups of double characters that print two letters at once. A single-space key moves the carriage one space and a double-space key moves it two so a word space can be made with a letter. Inking, impression and carriage drive are described as old and unclaimed.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Keys that only set the element](../features/sel-dummy-keyboard.md), [Small keyboard with shift rows](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [Keys drive the element by racks or gears](../features/sel-differential-drive.md)
- **Type element:** [Flat type plate](../features/elem-plate-flat.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; type-form; movement synthesizer; right-hand key; left-hand key; single-space key; double-space key; inking mechanism; impression mechanism

**Numbers:** 17 sheets of drawings; double-space of 2 letter-spaces
