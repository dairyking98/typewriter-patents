## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Timing relay. The subject of the present invention is a timing relay, the switching duration of which can be changed within very wide limits and that automatically again returns to the standby position. The relay consists essentially of one driven with a constant number of revolutions and several planet and sun gears owning planetary gear, whose individual sun gears by a locking device can be held in a certain position of the planetary gears Triggers circuits. The response time of the relay can be set in two ways: both by adjusting the switching path to be covered by the planet gears as well as by changing the reduction ratio of the planetary gear. The first Measure can be achieved easily and quietly by sliding stops and the second by an adjustable locking device, which depends on its position engages in one of the sun gears. The locking device is expediently electromagnetic controlled and is arranged to return to its rest position after the desired circuits have been completed.
