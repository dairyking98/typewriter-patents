## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Coin-freed facilities or services for printing, stamping, franking, typing or teleprinting apparatus

**How it works (reading):** An add-on coin-operated lock whose interchangeable locking member is slid in a tube and driven by a cam or lever against the key lever, trigger bar or switching parts of any typewriter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriter locks are built into one machine type, so they cannot be moved to another machine.

**Solution:** An add-on coin-operated lock whose interchangeable locking member is slid in a tube and driven by a cam or lever against the key lever, trigger bar or switching parts of any typewriter.
