## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Key actions for specified purposes

**How it works (reading):** A positively operated back-stepping pawl with a feed-dog arrangement, actuated by the backspace key, moves the carriage back a set extent so that overthrow is prevented.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage could overthrow when backspacing.

**Solution:** A positively operated back-stepping pawl with a feed-dog arrangement, actuated by the backspace key, moves the carriage back a set extent so that overthrow is prevented.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
