## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When sheets are narrower than the overlying frame, one rail of the machine frame rides up on the sheets while the other sags, losing parallelism with the platen.

**Solution:** An adjusting or leveling device under the machine-supporting frame, improving the Hopkins design, restores the parallelism between the machine frame and the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
