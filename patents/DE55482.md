**How it works (reading):** A typebar machine with semicircular comb-guided typebars carrying three characters each and a sliding paper carriage moved by push buttons.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typebar typewriter with visible writing and a simple paper carriage (problem not stated in the opening).

**Solution:** A typebar machine with semicircular comb-guided typebars carrying three characters each and a sliding paper carriage moved by push buttons.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot on a semicircular wire clamped in a two-part guide comb and are driven by key levers pivoting on a similar wire, the key buttons forming two concentric rows. Each type block carries a small letter, a capital and a figure or other character. A slotted eye guides the type block so it hits the ribbon exactly at the print point, which stays visible. A curved feed lever under the key levers pushes a toothed push rod to advance the paper slide, which slides on a rod carried by arms that can be raised for case change.
