## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tension on the key-levers cannot be adjusted for each lever separately.

**Solution:** A transverse notched plate with one comb-like arm per key-lever, each lever connected to its arm by its return spring, so each lever tension can be set independently.
