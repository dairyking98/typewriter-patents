## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulating mechanism for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Tabulator keys are condensed at the front and joined to rear stops by fore-and-aft connections; the stop levers are mounted in a supplemental frame secured within the base outline.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator keys and stop levers should sit compactly, without projecting beyond the machine's fore-and-aft outline.

**Solution:** Tabulator keys are condensed at the front and joined to rear stops by fore-and-aft connections; the stop levers are mounted in a supplemental frame secured within the base outline.
