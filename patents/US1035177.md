## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to simplify and reduce the number of parts of machines of the character described whereby they are more easily and cheaply constructed, more easily operated and less liable to get out of order.

**How it works (reading):** A motor drives the type operating mechanisms after the operator selects a key, with fewer and simpler parts.
