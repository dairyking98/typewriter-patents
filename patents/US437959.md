## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple, cheap and effective typewriter is wanted.

**Solution:** A small rolling carriage placed on the paper carries a hand-turned, rockable type-disk with rubber types on its beveled edge, located by a conical guide pin entering notches in the disk periphery.

**How it works:** The carriage runs on two broad rubber-tyred rollers directly on the sheet lying flat on a table; one roller has ratchet teeth and a pawl for stepping a measured distance. An axis on the carriage can rock fore and aft against a spring that holds it near vertical. The type-disk, two thin metal sheets, one beveled, with a rubber ring bearing the types projecting through a notched periphery, sits on the axis with a central handle. The operator turns the disk until the wanted type is at the print position, the wheel inking itself on a roller as it turns; a vertical guide pin on the carriage base enters the matching notch for accuracy, then the disk is tilted so the type presses the paper, and the carriage is pushed one step.
