## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriter frames are expensive to build and leave the drive exposed.

**Solution:** Make the frame as a single sheet-metal box open at the bottom that encloses and protects the type drive.

**How it works:** A one-piece sheet-metal box has side walls carrying a pivot pin for the space key lever and switch lever, and a transverse axis in the side walls carrying a swivel plate with the type wheel, moved by a key lever whose rear end grips under the plate. The carriage feed device sits on the rear wall, and the closed top of the box holds the two ribbon reel supports. The lower edges of the walls are flanged for stiffness. The paper roller runs in a carriage.
