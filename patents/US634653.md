## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A simple, compact, accurately aligned letter-writing machine with quick character selection.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal machine in which a printing carriage slides along an index of characters, with pawl-fed carriage slide and paper held by a clamp under a swinging frame.

**How it works:** A base carries a looped swinging frame hinged at the rear beneath a paper clamp; raising the front lifts the clamp to insert paper. A folded sheet-metal carriage guide with a feed rack carries a carriage slide guided by lapped edges with a friction-spring front edge. A feed-pawl slide with a bifurcated pawl engages the rack and is retracted by a spring. The printing carriage is a sheet-metal box capped by an index slide carrying an index of letters and numerals. The text is cut off before the printing action is described; a blotting pad holder and a lock seat are shown.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Slider along a straight index](../features/sel-linear-slider.md), [Pointer over a chart](../features/sel-pointer-chart.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Portable or compact](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [Frame construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md)

**Parts:** base; swinging frame; paper clamp; carriage guide; feed rack; carriage slide; feed pawl; printing carriage; index slide; blotting pad holder
