**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** The object: a printing device that makes the printing action and the paper cylinder's vertical and horizontal feed accurate and light, from a light stroke on the press lever.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a Japanese machine with a movable type magazine and a few fixed press levers, the lifting of the type, the printing and the paper cylinder feed must all be done accurately from a light stroke of one lever.

**Solution:** Each press lever drives, in sequence and through separate links, a cam-slot linked type-lifting rod, a delayed printing arm, and a feed linkage that moves the paper cylinder when the lever is released.

**How it works:** Each press lever is fixed on a pivot shaft and has two links pinned to it. The first, through a cam-slotted lever and several levers, raises a lifting rod that pushes the selected type up into the gripping tube of the printing arm. The second link has a long slot, so it acts a little later: it lifts a lever and rocker that turn the printing arm's shaft, and the arm swings round with the gripped type and strikes the paper cylinder. A lug on the press lever's shaft pulls a chain of rockers and a catch that set a slide ready to work the cylinder's vertical or horizontal feed. When the lever is released, springs return everything and the cylinder is stepped.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Printing mechanism in a Japanese-script printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. A printing device for a Japanese-script printing machine of the kind in which several press levers, and printing arms linked to them, are fixed in set positions at the front of the machine; the type magazine is moved to bring the wanted type directly under a printing arm, and striking a press lever works the printing arm and prints. It is characterised as follows. The root of each press lever is fixed to a pivot shaft. At suitable points on the press lever are pinned one end of each of two links. One of these is linked, through a lever with a cam slot and several lever mechanisms, to a type-lifting rod that rises and falls. The other, which is made to act a little later than the first, is coupled through a lever mechanism so as to turn the shaft fixed to each printing arm. On the press lever's shaft is a lug that moves with the lever; a link joined to it leads through several stages of levers to the paper cylinder's vertical or horizontal feed mechanism.
