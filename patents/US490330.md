## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper carriages of typewriters are complicated and costly to make.

**Solution:** A simplified paper carriage and platen carrier, with a hinged guide rail that doubles as the shift rail and a step-by-step feed slide.

**How it works:** The platen carrier rides on a front guide rail with an anti-friction roller and on a rear grooved-roller rail. The rear rail is on bell-crank arms linked to a shift key, so rocking it moves the platen carrier rearwards for the upper case. A ratchet on the platen is turned by a pawl lever with an adjustable regulator for one- or two-notch line spacing, held by a spring detent. A pinion and ratchet on a stand take the letter-spacing dog of the escapement. The carrier can also be lifted for inspection.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:feed-rollers](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; pressure roller; ratchet wheel; driving pawl; line-space lever; regulator; spring detent; guide rail; shift rail; bell crank; grooved roller; pinion
