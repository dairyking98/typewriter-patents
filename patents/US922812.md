## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage must stop at chosen columns for tabulating, but a plain escapement cannot do this.

**Solution:** A tabulating device releases the carriage from its escapement and reengages it at a point set by the key struck, using a rack disengaged from the escapement pinion.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
