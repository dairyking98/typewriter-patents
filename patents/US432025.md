## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A toy typewriter that is cheap and teaches spelling.

**Solution:** A flat type-head bar with a row of type on each edge and cylindrical handle ends is pressed by hand onto paper over a ridge platen between two ink pads, and rotated to choose the edge.

**How it works:** The base is a plate with a central ridge as platen and parallel slots on each side, with diverging walls (outer ones steeper) so paper is not creased. Ink pads sit on the base on both sides of the platen. The type-head, a light flat bar with strips of type on its two edges, lies across the base; the user holds it by its cylindrical ends, turns it to the wanted edge and presses it down to print. Indicator plates on its sides show which letters are on each edge, and the remaining type touch the pads and are inked each time it is depressed. Paper is moved by hand after each line.
