## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage hits its stop hard on long travels because the brake force does not depend on distance.

**Solution:** A spring-loaded angle lever with a brake pad on a drum runs on a concentric part for short travel and an inclined run-up for long travel, adding brake pressure.

**Features:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** brake pad; angle lever; brake drum
