## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cable or belt constructions for driving print, type or paper-carriages, e.g. attachment, tensioning means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to escapement mechanism for typewriting machines by which a lateral step-by-step motion is produced by means of suitable wheels, ratchets, and pawls or detents, &c.

**How it works (reading):** An electric escapement: holding a key-lever down makes and breaks a circuit automatically, giving rapid step-by-step lateral carriage motion through wheels, ratchets and pawls.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage spacing by hand is slow; the operator must move the hand rapidly to bring the carriage to a point.

**Solution:** An electric escapement: holding a key-lever down makes and breaks a circuit automatically, giving rapid step-by-step lateral carriage motion through wheels, ratchets and pawls.

**How it works:** An electric escapement: holding a key-lever down makes and breaks a circuit automatically, giving rapid step-by-step lateral carriage motion through wheels, ratchets and pawls.
