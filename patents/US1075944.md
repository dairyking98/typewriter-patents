## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting and other key-actuated machines, and particularly to that class of typewriting machines in which a pivoted type bar or other operated partis caused to strike against the front of the platen or other element on which the operated part is intended to act, so that the result of the blow of the type bar or other operated part will be visible to the person operating the keys.

**How it works (reading):** An Underwood-type machine with an auxiliary lever and latch between key lever and typebar, so a motor-driven frame powers the typebars or the keys drive them directly after shifting felted stops.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-strike typebar machine should be operable by key-controlled power or by hand at will.

**Solution:** An Underwood-type machine with an auxiliary lever and latch between key lever and typebar, so a motor-driven frame powers the typebars or the keys drive them directly after shifting felted stops.

**How it works:** Typebars strike upward against the front of the platen. Each typebar arm has a slot holding a pin on a sprung auxiliary lever with a push finger. A latch pivoted on the key lever hooks the finger's toe so that, when the key is pressed, the finger is engaged with a motor frame and driven by power. Shifting felted stops and links draws the key-lever bank down so that the key lever acts manually on the typebar. Contact mechanism on the motor frame controls the power.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** typebar; key lever; auxiliary lever; push finger; latch; motor frame; felted stop; spring; platen

- Underwood front-strike typewriter: adds switchable power operation
