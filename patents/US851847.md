## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage escapements needed to be simple, fast and light without weakening the escapement wheel.

**Solution:** A thin horizontal escapement disk with a ring of round holes inside its rim is stepped by a fixed dog and a loose swinging dog that enter the holes from opposite faces, rocked by a dog rocker.

**How it works:** The carriage spring pulls the carriage and turns a pinion on the feed rack, which is clutched to the escapement disk. Pressing any key (or the space bar) tilts a universal plate, which lifts a rod and rocks the dog rocker. The fixed dog enters the hole the loose dog just left while the loose dog withdraws and a spring swings it to the next hole. On release, the rocker returns under a worm-adjusted spring and the loose dog enters the next hole, where it takes the carriage load against the hole wall and is slid inward until the disk meets a stop, so one letter space is released. The disk is removable and secured to a ball-bearing carrier by studs and a nut, so a disk of different spacing can be fitted. Set screws adjust the fixed dog height and the loose dog stops.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:space-key](../features/esc-space-key.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [INK:pad](../features/ink-pad.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** escapement disk; fixed dog; loose L-shaped dog; dog rocker; restoring spring; worm and worm wheel; feed rack; pinion; universal plate; carrier with ball bearing; clutch; stop; set screw; jointed typebar

**Improves on:**

- Yost escapement with toothed wheel: holes inside the rim give a thin light wheel that is still strong, with quick let-off
- Walker application Ser. 86,821 and Smith application Ser. 85,451: refines the dog rocker and loose dog
