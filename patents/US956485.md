## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printed character keys made of separate parts are costly, and their characters can be obliterated in use.

**Solution:** A molded plastic key body has a recess that takes a paper character disk, which is covered by a transparent layer and held by a flange pressed over its edge; an attaching element is embedded in the body.

**Features:** [ELEM:element-construction](../features/elem-element-construction.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** finger key body; character disk; transparent coating; attaching element
