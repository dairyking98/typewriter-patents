## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (locking device for registration card machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor; Braking or stopping movement

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to an improvement in ratchet jam devices for such registration card machines, like punching, testing machines, which one step by step The adjustable carriage can be seated under the control of a jamming device.

**How it works (reading):** Two juxtaposed pawls engage the same ratchet tooth, and each pawl moves in a plane perpendicular to the ratchet axis, with an oblong pivot hole allowing adjustment. A spring driven card carriage is controlled by the ratchet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stepping carriage escapement that uses two pawls on two teeth is complicated; a simpler jam device is wanted for the registration card carriage.

**Solution:** Two juxtaposed pawls engage the same ratchet tooth, and each pawl moves in a plane perpendicular to the ratchet axis, with an oblong pivot hole allowing adjustment. A spring driven card carriage is controlled by the ratchet.
