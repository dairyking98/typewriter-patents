## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a device for adjusting the stroke of the ribbon guide for typewriters with a multi-colored ribbon.

**How it works (reading):** A connecting rod guides the connecting pin off the oscillating plate's pivot pin, and the pivot runs in the slot itself, so the pin-to-pivot distance stays fixed while the lever arm sets the stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The known ribbon guide stroke adjustment slides a connecting pin in a fork, which wears quickly and adds friction and complexity.

**Solution:** A connecting rod guides the connecting pin off the oscillating plate's pivot pin, and the pivot runs in the slot itself, so the pin-to-pivot distance stays fixed while the lever arm sets the stroke.
