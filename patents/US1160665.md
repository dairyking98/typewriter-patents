## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A stenographic typewriter that prints chords on a strip needs a way to add corrections or notes next to the text without a separate sheet.

**Solution:** A platen with several independent tracks or fields on a strip, shifted so another field lines up with the typebars, with the platen stepped round automatically after each stroke.

**How it works:** Integral key-lever typebars pivot on a common fulcrum and print one or several characters at once onto a paper strip led from a roll over a tension roll and the platen. The platen is pivoted in a hand-slid carriage, and the normal printing track is the one aligned with the typebars. An auxiliary track at its side can be moved into alignment for corrections or memoranda, with an indicator on the binding rod marking the fields. A pawl on the universal bail steps a ratchet on the platen forward one step on each type or space stroke, so lines print in parallel along the strip; a modified form has three fields.

**Features:** [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [Space key or bar](../features/esc-space-key.md), [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)

**Parts:** typebar key lever; platen; carriage; paper strip; tension roll; universal bail; feed pawl; ratchet; binding rod; indicator

**Numbers:** 2 fields (3 in a modification)
