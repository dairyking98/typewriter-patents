## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adjusting the carriage escapement's tooth carrier to the typist's stroke speed required screws and a specialist; earlier two-tooth variants did not catch on.

**Solution:** Make the pivot axis of the tooth carrier a threaded spindle, so a nut turned by a hand control shifts the carrier along the ratchet and self-locks.

**How it works:** A shaft sits non-rotatably but axially displaceable in two bearing brackets on the rear wall. A rocker arm pivots on it carrying a fixed and a loose shift tooth that engage the ratchet wheel. The threaded shaft passes through a nut, which an adjusting member operated by a manual element turns, moving the carrier lengthwise to change timing of the shift relative to the type stroke. The screw drive's self-locking holds the setting.
