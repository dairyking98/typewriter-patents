## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon oscillating cam and eccentric arrangement is complicated and the ribbon vibrator needs simpler drive.

**Solution:** A simplified cam or eccentric on a shaft that oscillates the ribbon vibrator, connected by a flexible, yielding link.
