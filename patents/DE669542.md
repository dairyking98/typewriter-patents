**How it works (reading):** Levers sit between the legs of a fork with opposing adjusting screws, tapered to remove play.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar-lever pivots develop play with wear, causing clatter and inaccurate transmission.

**Solution:** Levers sit between the legs of a fork with opposing adjusting screws, tapered to remove play.
