## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line lock and margin stop let the carriage arrest before the locking device engages, so one letter overprints and the margin drifts.

**Solution:** Improved line-lock and margin-stop mechanism so the locking of printing keys and the stop act reliably at line end.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
