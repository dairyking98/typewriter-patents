## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The two elastic rollers that hold the type wheel of children's typewriters are poorly mounted, so they neither clamp the wheel reliably nor yield enough to let it turn for setting letters.

**Solution:** The roller axes run parallel to the type wheel axis through slots in the frame plate and are pulled toward each other by a connecting spring.

**How it works:** The type wheel turns on the frame plate. Two rollers on axes are placed on opposite sides of the wheel and drop into the gaps between types, holding the wheel immovably in the print position. The roller axes pass through elongated openings in the frame plate and a connecting spring pulls them together. Turning the wheel pushes the rollers aside against the spring. The letters are spaced so each has a recess at 90 degrees to the one in the print position.
