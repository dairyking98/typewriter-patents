## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements of keyboards, e.g. key button disposition; Switches having rectilinearly-movable operating part or parts adapted for pushing or pulling in one direction only, e.g. push-button switch having a plurality of operating members associated with different sets of contacts, e.g. keyboard with contacts carried by or formed from layers in a multilayer structure, e.g. membrane switches

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the invention is to provide a means whereby the touch of existing forms of machines is greatly lightened, and the operator is thereby enabled to acquire-greater speed than is possible with the same machine without the attachment.

**How it works (reading):** Power is stored in a spring (or electric actuator) when the carriage returns; the keys only release latches to this stored power.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keys are heavy because the operator must also drive the carriage and type action.

**Solution:** Power is stored in a spring (or electric actuator) when the carriage returns; the keys only release latches to this stored power.
