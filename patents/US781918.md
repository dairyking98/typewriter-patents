## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Headings and names on forms, waybills and records need to stand out by wide letter spacing, but the Fisher flat-bed typebar machine could only space at one fixed pitch.

**Solution:** Insert switchable differential gearing between the carriage feed pinion and the escapement wheel so that the same uniform escapement step moves the carriage either 10 or 6 characters to the inch.

**How it works:** The escapement wheel is moved off the feed spindle and carries a driven pinion; a driving pinion on the spindle meshes with it through one of two intermediate gear trains. A pivoted gear-carrier lever presents either train, so a single escapement step gives normal or wide advance without touching the dogs. The case key can move the lever so upper case prints wide, with a catch to hold it down and a shifter that disconnects the key; a separate hand lever sets wide spacing for both cases. The escapement itself uses a holding dog and a spacing dog rocked by a vibrator from the key yokes, with a trip lever for carriage release. Case shift is by a trip ring that frees plural type-heads on the typebars.

- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by turning the element](../features/shift-rotary.md), [Shift lock](../features/shift-lock.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** escapement wheel; holding dog; spacing dog; vibrator; driving pinion; driven pinion; intermediate gear; gear-carrier lever; case key; locking catch; shifter; feed spindle; trip ring; carriage-release lever

**Numbers:** 10 characters to the inch normal; 6 characters to the inch wide

**Improves on:**

- slip-dog escapements with graduated stops: spacing is a gear ratio, not a whole number of rack teeth, so any pitch is possible without altering the escapement
- US573868 Fisher machine: adds selectable pitch to the flat-bed Fisher
