## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A light, simple, portable typewriter that can print on flat sheets, books or any surface it is placed on.

**Solution:** An open base frame carries a sliding frame and a ring-shaped carriage with downward-striking typearms in a circle, which feeds letter by letter along a screw rod as keys are pressed.

**How it works:** A flat open base frame carries two guide rods on which a rigid sliding frame moves for line feed by pinions on racks, driven by a ratchet finger-lever. On it a carriage of annular bottom and top plates is supported by a screw rod and a roller on a rail, and can tip up for access. Typearms are pivoted around the bottom ring and strike downward through it to a common point, each linked to a spring-returned vertical spindle with a finger-piece. A ring beneath the spindles is forced by each key to advance the carriage from letter to letter via the feed mechanism and screw.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [FRM:compact-portable](../features/frm-compact-portable.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md), [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** base frame; guide rod; sliding frame; rack and pinion; screw rod; annular carriage; typearm; link; spindle with finger-piece; spiral spring; feed ring; ratchet lever
