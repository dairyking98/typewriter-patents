## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A shift for a typebar keyboard where each key can operate either the upper-case or lower-case key-lever; the earlier endwise-moving key plate is replaced by one swinging sideways.

**Solution:** Keys with non-circular swinging stems pass through a perforated plate that is swung sideways by side levers on a rock-shaft, bringing each stem over the upper or lower lever; double-slotted hinged buttons hold the key sleeves.

**How it works:** Key stems slide in slotted sleeves in a top plate and are sprung upright. The lower stem ends pass through a movable perforated plate hung on links. Depressing side levers at the sides of the keyboard turns a rock-shaft at the back, which via a link and arm moves the plate sideways and shifts the stems to the upper-case levers. A spring returns the plate on release. Two-piece pivoted buttons close the sleeve tops round the stems.
