## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Releasing the paper rolls requires releasing bottom rolls and then lifting the top rolls one at a time.

**Solution:** Connect the top rolls to the main release key through a slide-and-notch finger-piece so both release together or separately.

**How it works:** The platen turns in a platen frame with a paper shelf. The main release key rocks a shaft with cams moving arms that throw off bottom rolls. A rock-shaft on the top roll frames carries an arm with a slide that has a notch. Pulling the slide forward engages the notch with an arm on the release-key lever, so depressing the key lifts top rolls too. Otherwise the top rolls lift by hand.
