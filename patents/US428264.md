## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators cannot tell when the inking ribbon has been wound off one spool and stopped feeding.

**Solution:** A wire signal on each ribbon spool side, pulled by the ribbon end, shows a tablet marked Reverse ribbon.

**How it works:** On the Caligraph ribbon feed, each spool has a tape; when the ribbon is fully unwound, tension on it moves a wire arm. The arm, pivoted under the front track and spring-returned, swings a tablet reading 'Reverse ribbon' into the operator's sight. Two such devices are mounted reversed, one for each direction of ribbon travel. The ribbon switch has slots and dogs to prevent return movement of the spools.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** top plate; ribbon spool; ribbon; tape; signal ring; wire arm; tablet; spring; ribbon switch; dog

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph ribbon feed: adds a visual end-of-ribbon indicator
