## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Raised alignment marks must be ground off after soldering, and recessed marks wear the embossing die.

**Solution:** A type body has two inclined end faces whose planes, with the front face, form a gauge reference at a fixed distance from the baseline, milled with the same cutter as the characters.

Such alignment marks exist in known type bodies from recesses or from wedge-shaped or conical projections. Recessed guidelines are inferior to the raised directing marks because their production in the Embossing die requires sharp-edged protrusions that wear easily. Raised alignment marks on the type bodies must be removed after soldering. -The The invention relates to a type body with a Richtmal which does not suffer from these deficiencies. This is achieved by dividing the straightening mark into two straightening surfaces, so to speak that are located at both ends of the type body. Accordingly, the type body contains at the ends inclined faces, which together with in the plane of the type body or form the guiding mark parallel to this straightening surface.
