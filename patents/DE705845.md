## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (liquid shock absorber) · **Subject (patent classes):** Throttling passages in the form of annular discs or other plate-like elements which may or may not have a spring action, operating in opposite directions or singly, e.g. annular discs positioned on top of the valve or piston body the annular discs being incorporated within the valve or piston body

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to liquid shock absorbers, in particular for motor vehicles, with a venturing which is axially movable in a widened annular groove of the piston 1 and opens and closes the valve openings of the piston on both sides for high and low pressure.

**How it works (reading):** A slotted, resilient valve ring also serves as a piston ring, with graded openings for fine control.
