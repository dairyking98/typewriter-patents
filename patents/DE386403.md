## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mountings for web rolls or spindles characterised by being applied to printers having transversely- moving carriages and coupled to the carriage

**How it works (reading):** A holder hangs from the roll neck with gripper arms and is supported by a wheel on the table; a height-adjustable bracket fits different machines, with springs braking the roll.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper roll holders that rest on the machine strain it and need space that many machines lack.

**Solution:** A holder hangs from the roll neck with gripper arms and is supported by a wheel on the table; a height-adjustable bracket fits different machines, with springs braking the roll.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** roll holder; bracket; wheel; springs; adjusting screw
