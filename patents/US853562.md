## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The adjustment range of the margin regulator and of the line-locking slide was limited by the presence of the line-lock mechanism and by the arrangement of their teeth and pawls.

**Solution:** A rod in front of the carriage rail carries an arm with a cam device that the carriage cam engages, and both margin slides are mounted partly on the rod and partly on the rail, with teeth on the rail and a pawl on the slide, so the usable margin range is greatly increased.
