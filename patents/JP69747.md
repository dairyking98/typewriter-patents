## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A simple, strong, practical typewriter able to carry many characters on one type carrier worked by a single device.

**Solution:** A screw-threaded cylinder with types in a spiral is turned by a handle moving over a character board, so it screws along its axis to bring the chosen type to the print point, and pressing the handle strikes it against the paper cylinder.

**How it works:** Moving the handle (14) over the character board (19) slides a slide (25) carrying a toothed plate (10), which turns a gear (5) and, through a flexible joint (4), a shaft (12) with a square hole fitting the type cylinder (1). The cylinder is screwed by a thread (3) into a crank (2), so turning it moves it along a spiral, presenting the type matching the pointed character. Pressing the handle compresses a spring (15), engages a correcting pawl (17) in correcting teeth (18) to fix the slide position, and through a lever (13) and motion frame (8) swings the crank about the motion shaft (32) to strike the cylinder against the paper cylinder (28). The same press moves a spacer (20) and levers (22, 23) to advance the paper cylinder, and an ink roller (29) inks the types. The flexible joint's crossing point lies on the motion shaft centre line so the cylinder turns freely at any crank position. Releasing the handle returns the cylinder by the spring.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention concerns a typewriter built as follows. A cylindrical type cylinder, with types arranged in a spiral round its circumference, is fitted by a screw thread at one end into a crank that moves at right angles to the paper cylinder. The handle moves a slide, and a toothed plate fixed to it, left and right; this turns a gear, which turns a rotating shaft through a flexible joint, and so turns the type cylinder along its spiral, so that the type matching the character the handle points to on the character board faces the printing point. Pressing the handle moves the crank through a linkage and strikes the type cylinder against the paper cylinder, which prints. The object is a typewriter that is simple, strong and practical.
