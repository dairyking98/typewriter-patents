## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The escapement of the earlier patented designs needs refinement to operate more reliably and to be applicable to other machines.

**Solution:** A dog-rocker frame pivoted on the machine frame is rocked by each key through a universal bar, with pivoted dogs cooperating with a reciprocating rack and escape wheel to step the carriage.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
