## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Positive-feed mechanisms automatically operated in response to carriage return

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriter carfriage-return mechanism of the class in which a carriage-return cord or band is connected'at one end to the typewriter carriage and at the other end to a winding drum or :pulleywhieh ist-urned ,by a suitable motor to wind the band onto the pulley and draw back-the carriage.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage-return band winds on a drum driven by a motor, with a rearranged drive and clutch so the drum runs free on the forward stroke and is clutched for the return, reducing jar and drag.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Motor-driven carriage return stops with a jar and the carriage drags on its forward movement, which wastes motor power and makes the motor larger than needed.

**Solution:** The carriage-return band winds on a drum driven by a motor, with a rearranged drive and clutch so the drum runs free on the forward stroke and is clutched for the return, reducing jar and drag.
