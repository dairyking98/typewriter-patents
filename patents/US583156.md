## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon use only along one direction.

**Solution:** A ribbon carrier mounted in the frame shifts the ribbon crosswise automatically as the carriage travels and also moves it lengthwise.

**How it works:** A sweep bar pivoted on the frame is linked to the carriage; spring and links move the ribbon transversely during carriage travel.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)

**Parts:** sweep bar; link; spring; ribbon carrier; carriage

**Improves on:**

- US558428: mechanism is in the frame, not carried by the carriage
