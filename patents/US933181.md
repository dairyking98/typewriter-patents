## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Visible-writing typewriters need a simple, durable ribbon feed and vibrating mechanism; users also want two ribbons and ribbon reversing without tearing.

**Solution:** One power-driven feed and a simple vibrator system handle two ribbons, selectable or both inactive, with automatic reversing.

**How it works:** Two ribbons lie face to face on a single pair of spools and wind together, each through its own vibrating guide near the printing point. A switch lever renders either vibrator active or both inactive for stencil cutting. The feed is carriage/type-bar driven through ratchet wheels, and successive like movements of one part reverse the feed. A clutch protects ribbon from tearing at the spools; the vibrator adapts to platen-shift machines.
