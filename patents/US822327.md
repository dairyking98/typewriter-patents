## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulating carriage that stops suddenly can jolt the parts out of order.

**Solution:** A fixed air cylinder on the frame holds a piston connected to the carriage, and an adjustable valve regulates air escape so the carriage is checked smoothly.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)
