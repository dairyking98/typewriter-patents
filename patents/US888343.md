## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (addressing machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing mechanisms using line-contact members, e.g. rollers, cylinders

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a machine for addressing articles such as newspapers, magazines &c.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Addressing machines for magazines and newspapers need address stencils that are cheap to make and that separate active subscribers from expired ones automatically.

**Solution:** Disconnected address stencils are carried on a device that feeds articles past a stencil-carrying and brushing mechanism, with means to detach each stencil and sort the active ones; the OCR leaves most mechanism unclear.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
