## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Yielding platen rubber cushions the impression, so only a few legible carbon copies result.

**Solution:** A hard, unyielding backing is placed on the platen under the last sheet, as a simple attachment, to obtain more legible carbon copies.
