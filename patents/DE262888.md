**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The subject of the invention is a locking device for typewriters, which is particularly suitable for collapsible typewriters with visible writing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A collapsible typewriter's paper carriage must be set in the right position before the box lid will close, and transport damages the stepping mechanism.

**Solution:** A latch locks the paper carriage in position and disengages the pawls of its stepping switch, so the machine folds and transports without damage.
