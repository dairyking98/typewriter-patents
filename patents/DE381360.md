## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** Loose and fixed discs on paired rollers let the colour cloth direction be switched by a slide without changing feed roller rotation.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copier colour cloth had to be replaced after each line break and could not change direction without reversing the feed roller.

**Solution:** Loose and fixed discs on paired rollers let the colour cloth direction be switched by a slide without changing feed roller rotation.

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md)
