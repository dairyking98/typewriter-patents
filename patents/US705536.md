## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing maro chines,particularly of that class distinguished by a flat platen upon which the sheet or work is held in a flat or spread-out condition during the printing operation,.

**How it works (reading):** A work gage on the flat platen, outside the track rails and adjustable, with a clamp plate that holds the sheet against the gage and covers its edge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A work gage alone does not protect the edge of the work it holds on the platen.

**Solution:** A work gage on the flat platen, outside the track rails and adjustable, with a clamp plate that holds the sheet against the gage and covers its edge.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)
- **Uses:** [Book typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** work gage; clamp plate; platen
