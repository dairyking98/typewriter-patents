## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Printing on disk-shaped media, e.g. CDs

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to type-writing maro chines,particularly of that class distinguished by a flat platen upon which the sheet or work is held in a flat or spread-out condition during the printing operation,.
