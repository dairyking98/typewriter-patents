## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to tabulator mechanism for typewriters, and is herein shown,` tiled November 24, 1911.

**How it works (reading):** A lock holds the type keys after a tabulator key is depressed and releases automatically when the carriage comes to rest.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After a tabulator key starts the carriage, the operator can strike type keys before the carriage stops.

**Solution:** A lock holds the type keys after a tabulator key is depressed and releases automatically when the carriage comes to rest.
