## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A writing machine was needed that produces raised dot characters readable by touch for the blind.

**Solution:** Eight outer impression rods around a central rod form a dot pattern; the central rod is pressed by hand and the combined rods emboss the dots into the paper on a yielding support.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)
- **Uses:** [For the blind](../features/use-blind.md), [Shorthand machine](../features/use-stenotype.md)
