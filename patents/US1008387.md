## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to means for feeding a plurality of separate cards or tags to be written upon.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage fitted with a holder and feed for a stack of tags lets several lines be typed on each tag in turn.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tags with several lines of entries are hard to hold and feed squarely into a typewriter.

**Solution:** A carriage fitted with a holder and feed for a stack of tags lets several lines be typed on each tag in turn.
