**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to printing point indicators forv typewriting machines and one object of the invention is.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The printing point indicator needs a simple, efficient way to show the carriage position relative to the printing point.

**Solution:** A printing point indicator is automatically moved away from the indicating position at each printing operation, works with a scale to show carriage position, and can be locked out of operation at will.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
