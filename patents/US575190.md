## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriting in bound books needs a light machine with few parts, where neither book nor heavy printing carriage moves along the line.

**Solution:** A stationary base with the printing mechanism on a carriage moving only between lines, and letter spacing done by feeding the page between rollers.

**How it works:** Threaded standards with feed-nuts and a chain raise or lower the carriage guides to suit book thickness. The carriage slides across the line direction for line spacing and carries key-levers, typebars and a space-bar. Any key depresses the space-bar, which turns a rock-shaft and a sliding spacing-lever. The spacing mechanism feeds the sheet or book leaf through upper and lower paper-feed rolls on each side of a fixed platen.
