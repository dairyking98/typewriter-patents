## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** General improvement of a typebar machine's spacing, line spacing, ribbon feed and key levers.

**Solution:** A collection of mechanisms: carriage driven by a spring wheel, rack-bar spacing pawls, spring-finger paper-guide, ring-mounted typebars and a spool-driven ribbon feed.

**How it works:** A ring carries hangers in which the typebars are mounted, with pull-rods from key-levers. The carriage has a spring drum, letter and word spacing rack-bar and pawl mechanism actuated by a rock-shaft with spacing bars and arms. Line spacing uses a push-pawl combined with a brake against the platen ratchet gear. Ribbon spools are driven alternately through bevel gearing from carriage travel.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
- **Inking:** [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** case; top plate; carriage; platen; rack-bar; spacing pawl; spacing bar; rock-shaft; typebar ring; hanger; ribbon spool; bevel gear; spring bail
