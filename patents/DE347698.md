## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The aim of the invention is to provide such a straight line guide for the ribbon carrier to create a typewriter with switching the platen that moves through a particularly simple, only a few individual parts having design before the known Devices and in which the part currently being used for writing of the ribbon in all positions of the ribbon that occur when writing and the platen has a constant distance from the platen.

**How it works (reading):** The ribbon carrier hangs from the height-adjustable lift frame by two links, one an angle lever, so its fork moves on a straight line and the writing part of the ribbon stays at a constant distance from the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon-carrier guides for platen-shift machines are complex and sensitive, and the ribbon position drifts from the platen when the platen shifts.

**Solution:** The ribbon carrier hangs from the height-adjustable lift frame by two links, one an angle lever, so its fork moves on a straight line and the writing part of the ribbon stays at a constant distance from the platen.
