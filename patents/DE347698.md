> Description Translated from German Straight guide for the ribbon carrier. Such devices are already there for moving the ribbon fork for typewriters with switching of the platen known, each with a special device for regulating the position of the color hand fork to the platen when it is switched and to move the ribbon fork when the button is pressed are equipped. The design of these known devices is corresponding to Presence of the two specified facilities very involved and in continuous operation sensitive.
