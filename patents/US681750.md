## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to brushes for cleaning the types in type-writing machines;

**How it works (reading):** Rotary brushes sit outside the type field on a central vertical shaft driven by a crank and bevel gears, and centrifugal force throws the brushes into contact with the types while they turn.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type in a swinging type-bar basket collects dirt and is hard to clean by hand.

**Solution:** Rotary brushes sit outside the type field on a central vertical shaft driven by a crank and bevel gears, and centrifugal force throws the brushes into contact with the types while they turn.
