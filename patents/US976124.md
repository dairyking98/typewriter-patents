## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator has difficulty positioning a sheet squarely and accurately against the platen edge.

**Solution:** Gage pins in the platen are projected by a key-controlled shaft and spring-lock so the sheet's leading edge can be set against them, then withdrawn after the paper is clamped by the pressure rolls.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
