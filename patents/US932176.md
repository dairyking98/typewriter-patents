## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Base plate and type-bar supporting standards of the Oliver machine must attach firmly and be made differently for other machines.

**Solution:** A base plate with a lower forward part and an elevated rear part, carrying upright standards that support the type-bar sets pivoted on them.
