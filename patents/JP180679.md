## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A Japanese-script typewriter needs forward feed and push-back (return) of the platen in either of two spacings, and printing together with feed, from one easily fitted unit.

**Solution:** One push-pull knob rod sets both the feed-pawl gap and the push-back travel between two values, with separate striking levers for feed, push-back and printing.

**How it works:** The knob rod rocks a cam plate whose slanting slot moves a lifting plate, which changes the gap between the swinging and fixed feed pawls and, through a limit plate, the stop for the push-back plate. A feed striking lever rocks the pawl plate to step the platen rack, and the main printing lever, which works the type-cylinder case lift cam, also presses on the feed lever so printing and feeding occur together. A back-feed lever turns a cam drum that, under spring control, moves a push-back plate into the rack teeth until the limit plate stops it, then releases and returns. Blank feed, push-back and print-and-feed can thus be done at either spacing or combinations.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Nature and object of the invention. An actuating device for a Japanese-script typewriter, in which a push-pull knob rod is mounted in the frame and a rocking cam plate, hinged to the frame, is hinged to its end; a pin fixed to a lifting plate, by which both the gap of the platen feed pawls and the travel of the push-back plate can be changed between two values, engages the slanting slot of the cam plate; this construction is linked to a striking lever for the feed pawls and a striking lever for the push-back, both fitted to the same frame, so that each does its work; and the striking lever for the feed pawls is linked so that it can be worked at the same time by the main printing lever, which engages the lift-cam mechanism of the type-cylinder case, also fitted to the frame. Its object is an actuating unit, easily fitted on the typewriter's base, that can feed and push back by either of two spacings or by a combination of them, each on its own, or feed and print at the same time.
