## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to tabulatlng mechanism for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** A combined tabulator sets to act either as a denominational tabulator or as a column selector, so the carriage moves in one operation to a chosen position in a chosen column.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulator can arrest only at denominational positions in the first column reached, and cannot skip columns cleanly.

**Solution:** A combined tabulator sets to act either as a denominational tabulator or as a column selector, so the carriage moves in one operation to a chosen position in a chosen column.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
