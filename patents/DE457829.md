The invention relates to a printing device for key lever cash registers with partial, subtotal and total printing, in which the swiveling frame stored adding units with a common printing unit optionally coupled in this way can be that the respective amount of one or the other adder is printed. The invention consists in the adjustment of the Summen.druckräder takes place essentially by the same drive means that are used for the adjustment the partial amount pressure wheels cause. The invention has the advantage that the previously required special adjustment device for the total pressure wheels are completely eliminated can, -which significantly simplifies the checkout gear and the security of the Cash work is increased.
