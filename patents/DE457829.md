## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A separate adjustment device was needed for the total pressure wheels in a cash register.

**Solution:** The total pressure wheels are adjusted by the same drive means that set the partial-amount pressure wheels, removing the special device.
