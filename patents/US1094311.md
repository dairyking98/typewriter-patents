## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Script supports connected to the typewriter or printer

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to provide means whereby the platen of the copy holder may be set, so that the matter to be copied may be arranged to register with the matter being written.

**How it works (reading):** A copy holder has its own revolving platen on a carriage that is connected to the typewriter carriage and geared to the machine platen shaft, so the copy platen moves and turns with it, and a gear shifter sets the registration.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A copy must be fed along with the typewriter platen and turned in step with it, and the copy must be set so it lines up with the matter being written.

**Solution:** A copy holder has its own revolving platen on a carriage that is connected to the typewriter carriage and geared to the machine platen shaft, so the copy platen moves and turns with it, and a gear shifter sets the registration.
