## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A back-shift (backspace) device for the carriage drive wheel should be easily removable from the machine and light, compact and cheap, as needed for portable typewriters.

**Solution:** The back-shift pawl and ratchet are on the same axis as the drive wheel and are carried on a base plate that can be removed as one unit; a key lever moves a pivoting linkage that engages the back-shift pawl with the drive wheel.

**How it works:** A key pulls a linkage with a tongue that catches a pin on the back-shift pawl, engaging the drive wheel so it turns in reverse.
