## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to paper-feeding devices of type-writing machines.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-arm clip mounted on the platen opens to receive the bottom edge of the sheet inserted at the front, closes when the platen is rotated, and a stop arrests the platen at the first-line position; the platen is rotated forward to release the sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Short sheets, cards and envelopes are hard to insert quickly and accurately to the first line, and to release after writing.

**Solution:** A spring-arm clip mounted on the platen opens to receive the bottom edge of the sheet inserted at the front, closes when the platen is rotated, and a stop arrests the platen at the first-line position; the platen is rotated forward to release the sheet.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen 1; platen-frame with ends 2 and paper-shelf 3; clip with strip 6; spring-arms 7; feed rolls 5; stop
