## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The ribbon mechanism of typewriters is complicated; polychrome ribbons and reversal need improvement.

**Solution:** A ribbon mechanism for multi-colour ribbons with automatic and manual reversal of feed, and a transmission between the two spool shafts.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
