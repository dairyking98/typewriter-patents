## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The locking lever that controls the type-wheel rolling movement on the paper web has a smooth end, so its control is weak.

**Solution:** The lever end carries teeth that engage the type wheel or a related gear at the right moment, so the type wheel rolls on the printing surface under more controlled motion.
