## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in check-punches, and has for its object to provide a machine of this description which shall be easy and efficient in its operation, and in which the parts shall be few in number and simple and inexpensive to manufacture and assemble;

**How it works (reading):** A check-punch built from a small number of simply combined elements that punch the amount into the check.
