## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to powered typewriters and the like, and more particularly to a power-operated paper trolley elevator for such machines.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage-return clutch lever is held in disengagement by a control element and a spring-loaded pawl; the pawl and the control element have control surfaces so that on the key's return stroke the pawl always goes back to its normal position, whether the drive roller is moving or stopped.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On a power typewriter, switching the motor on after it was off can make the carriage jump back to its start position.

**Solution:** The carriage-return clutch lever is held in disengagement by a control element and a spring-loaded pawl; the pawl and the control element have control surfaces so that on the key's return stroke the pawl always goes back to its normal position, whether the drive roller is moving or stopped.
