## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German The invention relates to powered typewriters and the like, and more particularly to a power-operated paper trolley elevator for such machines. Power-driven trolley return devices, those with a usually disengaged clutch that has a sliding pressure plate, and with are provided with a power-driven device that rotates continuously when the machine is working, are already known. According to the invention, the clutch engaging end is used to move the pressure plate Location of two levers that can be slid with respect to one another for use, which are switched on by a Spring are interconnected, with a device controlled by a button has a pawl for pivoting this lever for engaging the clutch, which through Spring action urged into engagement with a driven rotating sprocket, but usually is held out of engagement by a control element, such as a trigger, until this control element; is released by pressing a button. Here have the mentioned The pawl and the control member cooperating control surfaces, which are arranged so that at the Return movement of the key, regardless of whether the toothed roller is rotating or standing still, the pawl always returns to it…
