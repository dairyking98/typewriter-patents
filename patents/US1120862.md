## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to new and useful improvements in carriage return mechanisms for typewriters and the like, the object being to operate the carriage return mechanism by a continuously running motor.

**How it works (reading):** A key operates a clutch that connects the carriage to a continuously running motor through a rack and pinion, and the carriage disengages the motor automatically when it reaches its left-hand position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage return must be done quickly and automatically, using the continuously running motor of an adding machine.

**Solution:** A key operates a clutch that connects the carriage to a continuously running motor through a rack and pinion, and the carriage disengages the motor automatically when it reaches its left-hand position.
