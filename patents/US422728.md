## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with means for deforming or punching the copy matter separate from the stamping means

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to a device for perforating the body of a bank check with nu-' inerals indicative of-the amount for which the check is drawn;

**How it works (reading):** A plunger slides and turns to index an indicator disk with twelve notches held by a spring pawl; the disk's numeral indentations set the numeral the punch cuts into the check.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing bank-check punches are complicated and costly, and the aim is a simpler, cheaper and more effective device.

**Solution:** A plunger slides and turns to index an indicator disk with twelve notches held by a spring pawl; the disk's numeral indentations set the numeral the punch cuts into the check.
