## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bails

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an easily adjustable locking device for the paper holding rail of typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Rail arms rotate on an axle and sit against adjusting collars with notches; once aligned, the collars are screwed tight so the rail sets in a fixed position and forms one rigid piece.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper-rail scale had to be set by bending arms or by threaded pins and jam nuts, and the adjusted rail was less rigid.

**Solution:** Rail arms rotate on an axle and sit against adjusting collars with notches; once aligned, the collars are screwed tight so the rail sets in a fixed position and forms one rigid piece.
