## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Parts, details or accessories of beds; Stands, brackets or the like for supporting infusion accessories; Resilient type clasp

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to certain new and useful improvements in eraser-holders, and has for its object the provision of novel means whereby an eraser may be held in a convenient position upon the frame of a type-writer.

**How it works (reading):** A U-shaped clamp on the frame base holds flexible arms that hold the eraser within reach of the operator.
