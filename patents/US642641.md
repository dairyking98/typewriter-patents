## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention is directed to improvements in machines or appliances for the prod uction of stencils, the object of the improvements being to secure in such a machine or appliance simplicity and durability of construction, ease of operation, and perfection in the finished article.
