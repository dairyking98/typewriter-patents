## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (stencil making machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention is directed to improvements in machines or appliances for the prod uction of stencils, the object of the improvements being to secure in such a machine or appliance simplicity and durability of construction, ease of operation, and perfection in the finished article.

**How it works (reading):** A machine that intermittently feeds the strip of stencil material into position to receive character perforations, with means to govern the feed. Details beyond the first 3000 chars are not available.
