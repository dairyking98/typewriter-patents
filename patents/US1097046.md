## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ribbon-feed devices or mechanisms with arrangements for reversing the feed direction

**How it works (reading):** A reversing lever driven alternately by two crank pins of a key moves a trip arm, and two converging striking surfaces with notches hold the lever at each end.
