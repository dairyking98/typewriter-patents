## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Devices or arrangements of selective printing mechanisms, e.g. ink-jet printers or thermal printers, specially adapted for supporting or handling copy material in continuous form, e.g. webs; Chains of interconnected envelopes

**How it works (reading):** Envelopes are joined in a web with a separable strip glued to the rear edge of the previous envelope, so each envelope's front is fully exposed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Individual envelopes must be clamped and aligned one by one when addressed.

**Solution:** Envelopes are joined in a web with a separable strip glued to the rear edge of the previous envelope, so each envelope's front is fully exposed.
