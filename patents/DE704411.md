## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Controlling or analysing mechanical parameters involving printing of test patterns

**How it works (reading):** A contact rod under the key levers is touched by the levers, closing an electric circuit that drives a counter with no added key force.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing writing-speed counters need extra force or extra mechanical steps, so writing becomes harder.

**Solution:** A contact rod under the key levers is touched by the levers, closing an electric circuit that drives a counter with no added key force.
