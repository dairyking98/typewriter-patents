> Description Translated from German Apparatus for determining the writing speed and writing performance in typewriters Devices for determining the writing speed and writing performance in typewriters are known which operate mechanically or electrically. In the case of mechanical devices, for. B. placed a steel wire in the path of the type lever, which is struck by the type lever and actuates a counter through mechanical transmission. In this case, considerably more force must be expended for the mechanical transmission by the person writing than is necessary for writing. In the case of electrically operating devices, too, additional mechanical operations usually have to be carried out, which make writing with the machine more difficult. So is z. B. a device for counting the written letters is known in which the keys of the typewriter contact springs and each contact spring relays are assigned, which in turn control the counter. The movement of the contact springs to produce the electrical connection requires additional force when writing, while such a device also entails considerable expense and is also not suitable for retrofitting in a typewriter.
