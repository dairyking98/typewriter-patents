## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in tabulating devices for type-writing machines, and has for its object to provide a tabulating stop device which will serve as a column-stop and which is adapted to be connected with and automatically actuate the release device or not, as desired.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable stop on the machine frame is moved into the path of the carriage dogs to act as a column stop, and can be connected to actuate the carriage release or left unconnected.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Column-stop tabulation on the carriage needs an adjustable stop that can be moved into the path of carriage-mounted dogs and optionally tied to the release device.

**Solution:** An adjustable stop on the machine frame is moved into the path of the carriage dogs to act as a column stop, and can be connected to actuate the carriage release or left unconnected.
