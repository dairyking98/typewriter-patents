Furthermore, at the end of the line takes place in a manner known per se a locking of the type levers instead, the one being influenced by the type levers Switching mechanism is disabled and the type lever is not against the Can hit the platen. The device consists of the locking rod a located on the typewriter trolley with stop slides b, which are provided with stop cams c. Below this stop slide rod a sits a stop frame d, which oscillates transversely to the latter and is displaceable in the longitudinal direction and which is guided through the pin e in the bearings f.
