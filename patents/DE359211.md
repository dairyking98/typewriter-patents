## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Top layers; Location or type of the layers in shells for rollers of printing machines characterised by macromolecular organic compounds; Trimming

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** An essential purpose of the invention is to reduce manufacturing costs and to reduce the manufacture of such rollers.

**How it works (reading):** A roller with an elastic core and a tough outer layer of vulcanized vegetable oils, formed by casting in a rotating mould so the material settles on the inner wall.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing and typewriter rollers are costly to make and degrade under ink, moisture and temperature changes.

**Solution:** A roller with an elastic core and a tough outer layer of vulcanized vegetable oils, formed by casting in a rotating mould so the material settles on the inner wall.
