## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electric typewriters of the author's earlier application needed an automatic carriage return at line end.

**Solution:** A spring-barrel drives the carriage letter by letter through a ratchet and gear, and a movable rack bar lets it reverse automatically at the end of the line.

**How it works:** An electromagnet (or mechanical action) moves a pawl lever after each imprint; a gravity pawl advances a ratchet one tooth, while a stop tooth on the lever engages a gear of equal tooth count to limit rotation to one tooth. The ratchet, gear and a spring barrel on a common stud wind and unwind through the letter spacing; at line end a pointer and sliding bar shift a movable rack bar so the carriage runs back to the start. The carriage rides on a hinged guide bar with anti-friction rollers and can be raised to inspect the print.

**Features:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md), [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** electromagnet; pawl lever; gravity pawl; ratchet wheel; gear wheel; spring barrel; movable rack bar; sliding bar; guide bar; pointer

**Improves on:**

- McLaughlin application Serial 276,151: improves its automatic carriage reverse for electric typewriters
