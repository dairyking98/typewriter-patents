## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet output section

**How it works (reading):** The paper guide, support plate and blade holder are hinged below the guide track so they fold together into a layer around the platen for transport; a wire sheet holder engages the support plate curl.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A paper guide of separate plates is too large for portable typewriters that must fit a small case.

**Solution:** The paper guide, support plate and blade holder are hinged below the guide track so they fold together into a layer around the platen for transport; a wire sheet holder engages the support plate curl.
