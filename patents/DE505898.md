## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen clutch hooks must hold firmly without the clamping member exerting torque on the locking hooks.

**Solution:** Locking hooks rest on support bearings in the platen and are guided in circumferential grooves of a non-rotating clamp member, which presses them into the toothed ring.
