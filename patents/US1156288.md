## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of my invention are to provide a typewriter that may be operated at high speed, and having a light touch, and also to provide means whereby any pressure upon a key whether light or heavy will operate the corresponding type bar to print 11111- formly.
