## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electric typewriters need a light touch and uniform printing whatever the key pressure, compact and run from an ordinary lamp circuit.

**Solution:** Each key closes a circuit to a small selective magnet that hooks its type-bar to a universal bar driven by a separate magnet, which is latched on until the stroke finishes.

**How it works:** Key bars with insulating tips press a spring against a cross rod to close a circuit through even a slight movement. Small selective magnets in two staggered rows pull armatures that move links and levers so a hook engages the universal bar. A separate magnet operates the universal bar and its contacts are latched closed until the type-bar stroke is complete, so the key need not be held. The bar is returned and limited in travel by springs and stops.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** key bar; insulating tip; contact spring; selective magnet; armature; link; lever; hook; universal bar; universal bar magnet; latch; platen; carriage
