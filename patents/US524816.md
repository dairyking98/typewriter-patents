## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Earlier machine by the inventor used less positive mechanism to bring the type to the print position with few keys.

**Solution:** A perforated type cylinder is moved endwise and rotated to a predetermined extent by grouped keys through racks, pinions and segmental cams, then printed on a stepping carriage.

**How it works:** Finger keys in groups of two, plus auxiliary keys, sit on levers each with a vertical rack that meshes with a pinion driving a segmental cam. The two cams in a pair have unequal reach, and the sum of single or combined key presses sets the cylinder's rotation and longitudinal travel on its shaft. The type cylinder carries characters on its surface with perforations matching the type arrangement. A traveling paper carriage is stepped along by key depression, and an endless inking ribbon steps across the type face with each stroke. Springs restore keys and a lock holds the mechanism when the carriage is thrown back.

**Features:** [ELEM:cylinder-multirow](../features/elem-cylinder-multirow.md), [SEL:keyboard-few-keys](../features/sel-keyboard-few-keys.md), [SEL:differential-drive](../features/sel-differential-drive.md), [SHIFT:axial](../features/shift-axial.md), [SHIFT:rotary](../features/shift-rotary.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [SEL:selector-locking](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)

**Parts:** type cylinder; cylinder shaft; finger key; auxiliary key; key lever; rack; pinion; segmental cam; rocking lever; helical spring; paper carriage; endless ribbon; turnbuckle rod

**Numbers:** keys in groups of two
