## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side lays or gauges

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to side gages which are usually provided on the paper shelves of typewriting machines for guiding the side edges of the Work sheets as they are inserted at the introductory side of the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A side gage pivots on a carrier that rides a rail behind the paper shelf, pressed by a spring so only its lower edge touches the shelf.

**Solution:** A side gage pivots on a carrier that rides a rail behind the paper shelf, pressed by a spring so only its lower edge touches the shelf.
