## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Tabulating every punch-card column with full tabulators is costly, and calculating-machine attachments are tied to the calculator's digit count.

**Solution:** A separate device tabulates statistical records from hole combinations on punch cards or tape fields, using a combination system instead of a full tabulator (the OCR is garbled, so details are uncertain).
