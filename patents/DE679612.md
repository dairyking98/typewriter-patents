## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guide members of a typewriter must be lifted with two hands.

**Solution:** Paper guide members are pivoted radially away from the platen, with springs and a lever operated by axial movement of the platen knob, so one hand can insert the sheet.
