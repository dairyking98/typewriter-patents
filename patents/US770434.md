## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Elevating the platen for upper-case printing needs an easy, accurate, reliable mechanism.

**Solution:** The platen's inner frame rises vertically in V-grooved column guides on ball-bearing fluted tracks, lifted by a lever under the shift key.

**How it works:** The platen and paper-feed rollers sit in an inner frame whose V-shaped bars slide in rectangular column guides on the laterally moving carriage. A yoke on the frame carries a sleeve of balls running on a fluted rod, and the whole frame hangs from hooked rods. The shift key lever presses a pin on a fulcrumed lever whose fork lifts the hooked rod, raising the platen; a spring returns it and a screw sets the lift. A pivoted locking device holds the shift key down for all capitals.
