## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to card or envelop holders adapted for use in connection with type-writing machines to facilitate the writing on cards or addressing envelops;

**How it works (reading):** A flexible hard-rubber sheet in the carriage holds the card, adjusted vertically at one end by rollers.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A card or envelope cannot be held horizontally and adjusted vertically without lifting the holder.

**Solution:** A flexible hard-rubber sheet in the carriage holds the card, adjusted vertically at one end by rollers.
