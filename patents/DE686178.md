## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink ribbons characterised by the material from which they are woven

**How it works (reading):** Ribbon design in which the separation between colour zones is made to stop dissolved-dye migration.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-colour typewriter ribbons bleed between zones because dissolved dyes migrate across the dividing line.

**Solution:** Ribbon design in which the separation between colour zones is made to stop dissolved-dye migration.
