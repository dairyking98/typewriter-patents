## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversing on time stamps and similar devices was unreliable; the timing of the switch was uncertain, so the ribbon was not used up fully.

**Solution:** A stop on the ribbon trips a two-armed lever linked by an over-centre spring to a second lever, which snaps over and swaps which spool's drive pawl is engaged.

**How it works:** The ribbon runs from roll 2 to roll 3, each roll with a toothed wheel driven by a pawl on a rocker arm worked by a common button. A stop on each end of the ribbon meets a two-armed lever just before the ribbon runs out and swings it slowly. A spring stretched between this lever and a second lever on the same axis suddenly throws the second lever past dead centre when it passes the point. That lever moves rods to the pawl-disabling latches, so the pawl on the full side is lifted out and the pawl on the other roll drops into its gear, reversing the run.
