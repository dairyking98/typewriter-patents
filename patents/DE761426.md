## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter with automatic summation)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter with automatic totalizing device, in which the activated control elements of the totalizing devices for the vertical and cross counters keep the clutch control element common to all arithmetic keys automatically in action, according to patent 721 607.

**How it works (reading):** A locking member holds the summation control elements in action and is released through a connection controlled by carriage movement.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The previous summation locking element was alternately held by two drive clutch links, needing precise coordination and wearing quickly.

**Solution:** A locking member holds the summation control elements in action and is released through a connection controlled by carriage movement.

**Features:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md), [Selection locked for printing](../features/sel-selector-locking.md)
