## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating and margin stop mechanisms need efficient operation, and the margin stops must be released and elevated during carriage travel.

**Solution:** Tabulator stops and margin stops are carried on the machine frame and released through a mechanism that works with the escapement wheel and pinion; the margin stops are lifted during travel of the carriage.
