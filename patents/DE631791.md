## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (calculating machine with two counters)

**How it works (reading):** The levers that hold the interlocking in the zero-set position are aligned by a protrusion on the lever-engaging rail, so no sliding occurs between the contacting surfaces.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Alignment rails that line up the set types by pushing into the toothed type-carrier notches cause friction, wear and inaccurate type setting.

**Solution:** The levers that hold the interlocking in the zero-set position are aligned by a protrusion on the lever-engaging rail, so no sliding occurs between the contacting surfaces.

**How it works:** The protrusion locates the lever directly, avoiding the rubbing motion of the old alignment rail on the box teeth.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Alignment lock at the print point](../features/elem-alignment-lock.md)
- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** alignment rail; interlocking levers; lever-engaging rail; protrusions; counters
