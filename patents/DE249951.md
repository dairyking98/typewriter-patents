## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Pneumatic typewriters are noisy, and the type must strike silently while staying visible and allowing carbon copies.

**Solution:** A type wheel in a frame is moved back and forth by a compressed-air motor and eccentric so the types lightly touch the paper; a stop and fork limit the motor to one revolution per trigger, and a soft pad is removed for copies.
