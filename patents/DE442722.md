## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**How it works (reading):** The rear wall, cover and side walls open on a hinge and the front wall folds forward, so the machine can be lowered into the box and the keyboard fitted back through the front.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** A sound-absorbing box is hard to load because the typewriter must be threaded in through the front opening.

**Solution:** The rear wall, cover and side walls open on a hinge and the front wall folds forward, so the machine can be lowered into the box and the keyboard fitted back through the front.
