> Description Translated from German Typewriter postcard The invention relates to a typewriter carbonless postcard, which forms a booklet with an attached copy sheet. Such known Typewriter cards are also provided with detachable strips that allow that the postcard can be written on up to its »lower edge, including those which, made of paper, by gluing two layers of paper together the required Gaining strength and stiffening of a postkarg. Postcards made of paper are also known in which the address side is formed by two attached pre-folded flaps, which according to the description be glued to the back of the text, creating a continuous butt joint, which has the disadvantage that the postcard can easily kink at the butt joint. The invention consists in the fact that to insert the booklet with one or more sheets of charcoal and carbonless paper in the typewriter on the address flap a foldable flap is arranged, which after removal of the attached copy sheet and folding up the postcard in a lower auxiliary flap, which can also be folded down and glued, so that the ready-to-send postcard on the edges of the address and auxiliary flap is stiffened and completely flush.
