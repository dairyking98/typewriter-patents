## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (paper reel for shorthand machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Attaching webs to cores or spindles

**In the patent’s own words:** Our invention relates to devices for reeling automatically and by the operation of the machine the paper which comes from writingmachines, such as type writing machines,shorthand machines, and machines used for recording market quotations.

**How it works (reading):** A driven printing roller and feed roller on an adjustable arm reel the continuous paper into rolls, driven by an elastic band over two sheaves.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper from a short-hand machine piled up loose and was hard to handle.

**Solution:** A driven printing roller and feed roller on an adjustable arm reel the continuous paper into rolls, driven by an elastic band over two sheaves.
