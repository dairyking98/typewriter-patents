## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage advances only by a fixed amount per character, and the inventor wants a variable advance.

**Solution:** The release lever carries a fixed stop and two laterally movable stops with an arcuate guide piece, so the ratchet tooth stops at either the normal spacing or twice the spacing.
