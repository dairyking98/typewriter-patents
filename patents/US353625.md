## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a cheap and convenient attachment to a typewriter for holding leaves of written or printed matter while copying it with the machine;

**How it works (reading):** A friction feed roll clamped to the frame presses a leaf against the platen, moving it one line at a time by a finger key, and a clutch or ratchet turns the roll.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Copy must be held for copying without the sheet covering the working lines.

**Solution:** A friction feed roll clamped to the frame presses a leaf against the platen, moving it one line at a time by a finger key, and a clutch or ratchet turns the roll.
