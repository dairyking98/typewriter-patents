## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Telegraph Morse signals need to be printed as ordinary letters on a typewriter by remote control.

**Solution:** A character selector and letter selector circuit translate long and short pulses separated by pauses into letters on a typewriter.

**How it works:** A push-button relay reads dots and dashes by pulse length and passes them to a stepping selector. A pause mechanism ends a character when no new pulse arrives within the longest sign time, and the letter selector energises the typewriter's key for that letter.
