## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front and rear rollers or sets of front or rear rollers each mounted on a separate carrier

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in that the on top of the carriage base plate arranged support parts have approaches, which through cutouts in the base plate protrude downwards and here the bearings for the control shaft for adjusting the guide rollers wear.

**How it works (reading):** Guide-roller support parts are attached to the carriage base with upward approaches through cutouts, with the control shaft below the base, so platen and paper table can sit closer to the carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper guide parts add cost and bulk and keep the platen away from the carriage.

**Solution:** Guide-roller support parts are attached to the carriage base with upward approaches through cutouts, with the control shaft below the base, so platen and paper table can sit closer to the carriage.

> Description Translated from German Paper guide for typewriters The invention is a Paper guide for typewriters in which the front and rear guide rollers be supported by two support parts attached to the carriage base plate. The invention consists in that the on top of the carriage base plate arranged support parts have approaches, which through cutouts in the base plate protrude downwards and here the bearings for the control shaft for adjusting the guide rollers wear. At the same time, these support parts can be used to hold the paper guide plate to serve. The arrangement of the control shaft for adjusting the guide rollers below the carriage base allows the platen and paper table to be significant move closer to the sled, thus enabling a very compact, space-saving Design type. The invention makes it possible, before the assembly of the Machine all relevant parts, namely the paper table, the paper guide plate, the front and rear guide rollers and their bearing parts into a single unit To unite component, which can then be installed as such in the machine.
