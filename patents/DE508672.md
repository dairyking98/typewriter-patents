## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** On small travel machines the edge-stop scale cannot sit at the top of a guide plate when the paper table folds forward.

**Solution:** The edge-stop scale is attached to the back of the paper table near its lower edge so it stays visible and legible when the table is folded forward.
