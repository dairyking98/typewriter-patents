## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon feed needed to be driven by the letter-spacing action and reverse automatically when most of the ribbon has passed.

**Solution:** A to-and-fro rod driven by the letter-spacing element ratchets two ribbon drums via worm gearing, reversing automatically.

**How it works:** An armature or other part of the letter-spacing mechanism vibrates a rod through an arm and slot after each imprint. In a rectangular frame at each machine end a ribbon drum on a shaft carries a ratchet head; the shaft is free to slide lengthwise but is turned through a worm and pinion on a counter-shaft. The sliding shaft shifts the engagement so that, when the ribbon is mostly unwound, the feed reverses. Two identical sections serve the two ends.
