## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Writing-tables with vertically-adjustable parts; Desks with double worktop of which one at least is separately height adjustable

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to tables with adjustable in height table top consisting of interdependent movable tabletop parts.

**How it works (reading):** A two-armed lever pivoted on a support connects the table top parts so they move counter-rotating, with rolling pivots and no sliding surfaces.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Interdependent tabletop parts wear at sliding guides and joints, and the swinging motion of the top is awkward.

**Solution:** A two-armed lever pivoted on a support connects the table top parts so they move counter-rotating, with rolling pivots and no sliding surfaces.
