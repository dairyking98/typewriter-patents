## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the rotatable platens of typewriting machines.

**How it works (reading):** Platen ends get a diametrical slot, and heads on the frame spindles carry a matching diametrical feather; the heads couple to the platen and a lock lets the platen be lifted out and replaced without removing the axle.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the platen on the Underwood front-strike machine required removing the whole platen axle with its fittings.

**Solution:** Platen ends get a diametrical slot, and heads on the frame spindles carry a matching diametrical feather; the heads couple to the platen and a lock lets the platen be lifted out and replaced without removing the axle.
