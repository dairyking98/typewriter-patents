## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, and one of the objects thereof is to provide new and improved means for arresting in a silent manner the movements of the various operative part's thereof.

**How it works (reading):** A stop mechanism on the universal bar and key levers arrests the moving parts with less impact, including at a marginal stop block for the carriage return.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Moving parts of a typewriter strike each other too hard, and the carriage return stop is noisy.

**Solution:** A stop mechanism on the universal bar and key levers arrests the moving parts with less impact, including at a marginal stop block for the carriage return.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Carriage return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md)
