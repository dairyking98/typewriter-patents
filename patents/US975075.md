## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Column stops should not be removable and lost from the machine, and should be settable together.

**Solution:** Column stop bar teeth engage the stops so that several column stops can be thrown into and out of engagement at once, with the stops retained in adjusted position on the carriage.
