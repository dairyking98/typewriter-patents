## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention consists in constructions and combinations of parts, all substantially as will hereinafter be made manifest and covered in and by the claims.

**How it works (reading):** A spring-pressed paper bearing roll alongside the platen and adjustable yielding fingers guide the paper, and a spring connection between key-levers and typebars cushions the strike.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Narrow sheets, envelopes and cards needed guiding, and typebars struck the platen too hard.

**Solution:** A spring-pressed paper bearing roll alongside the platen and adjustable yielding fingers guide the paper, and a spring connection between key-levers and typebars cushions the strike.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
