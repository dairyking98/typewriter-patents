## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanical power drives with driven mechanism arranged to be clutched to continuously- operating power source by snatch rolls

**How it works (reading):** A control lever on the drive lever with the pawl snaps the pawl into the toothed roller on key press and disengages it on release, with stops at each end that couple and uncouple it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The drive pawl can stay engaged with a stopped toothed roller when a key is struck, so the type strikes the platen during motor start; complicated locks were needed to prevent this.

**Solution:** A control lever on the drive lever with the pawl snaps the pawl into the toothed roller on key press and disengages it on release, with stops at each end that couple and uncouple it.
