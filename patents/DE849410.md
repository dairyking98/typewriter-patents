## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In low-noise toggle-joint typewriters the type touches the paper while still moving, and the joint has a fixed pivot or complicated guides that waste finger force.

**Solution:** Bring the toggle joint nearly to its extended position by finger force and press the type on by pushing the extended joint forward using the moving mass (flywheel), against an extra spring that also returns the type.

**How it works:** A key moves a knee joint toward the platen. One link is connected to a pivot on a swivel carrier movable in the frame. When the joint extends, the flywheel-driven limb hits the carrier and swings it forward, pressing the type against the writing surface only when extension speed is zero. An extra spring beyond the usual return spring brings the type carrier back by the advance path. Typebars pivoted at the lower end are used with the joint acting on the upper ends.
