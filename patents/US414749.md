## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line feed on the Hammond needs a simpler action.

**Solution:** Thumb bell-crank lever connects through a slotted lever to the pawl, so the thumb pushes the pawl onto the feed-roll ratchet directly.

**How it works:** A bell-crank thumb lever on the left carriage end enters a slot in a lever pivoted on the feed-roll shaft, whose other end pins to the pawl arm. Pressing the thumb-piece draws the pawl down to turn the ratchet-wheel. The return spring only returns the parts, so can be weak. A stop limits the click-lever. Feed rolls press or have friction to resist the hammer stroke, and the milled head turns the roll freely for interlineation.

**Parts:** bell-crank thumb lever; slotted lever; pawl; click-lever; ratchet-wheel; feed roll; roll hanger; stop; spring
