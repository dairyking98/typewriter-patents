## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon wears through at one spot because the type keeps striking it there, and the operator does not notice the end of the ribbon.

**Solution:** A soft rubber pad on the ribbon, sewn or coated on, that sounds when struck by the type to warn that the ribbon is ending.
