## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A wide recording sheet must be shifted to receive records in different columns without moving the platen.

**Solution:** A mechanism moves the paper laterally to column positions and also feeds it for line spacing, with the platen held in place.

**Features:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
