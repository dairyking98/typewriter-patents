> Description Translated from German Lid support, especially for speaking machine housings There are devices for keeping the lid open, especially known for speaking machine housing which a curved support rod between a fixed and a spring-loaded one standing role is performed. In this known device, the spring is horizontal stored and not adjustable, which can be annoying with prolonged use. Self if this spring could be readjusted, this would only be possible after the whole thing had been lifted out Work floor be possible. According to the invention, this disadvantage is eliminated by that the movable roller, which engages in the recesses of the support rod, on the short end of a toggle lever with equal legs is mounted, the long arm of which under the action of an adjustable compression spring attached to a threaded provided collar or pressure plate rests against a nut of the spring bolt. The spring pressure can be regulated by means of an adjusting nut, which z. B. by a screw or rotatable through an edge, but not displaceable in the longitudinal direction is.
