## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register with drawers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Transfer mechanisms, e.g. transfer of a figure from a ten-key keyboard into the pin carriage

**How it works (reading):** A lever in the receipt path is moved out of the way by the printing hammer; while a receipt is still in the check guide the drawer lock stays engaged and only releases when the receipt is removed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators can close the drawer and leave a cash sale without handing over the printed receipt.

**Solution:** A lever in the receipt path is moved out of the way by the printing hammer; while a receipt is still in the check guide the drawer lock stays engaged and only releases when the receipt is removed.
