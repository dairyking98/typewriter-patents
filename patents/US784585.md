## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to machines, such as typewriters, in which a traveling carriage is employed, and has for its object to provide a simple mechanism for returning the carriage to its original position after it has reached the end of its travel.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage rack engages a return wheel on a shaft; a spring in a drum returns the carriage, separate from the step-by-step escapement feed.
