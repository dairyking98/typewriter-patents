## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line (vertical) feed of the platen needs to be limited accurately and easily, with free adjustment of spacing.

**Solution:** A gear meshing with teeth cut inside the platen is driven, under platen spring force, against a spring-loaded toothed sector whose stop position is chosen by a stepped selector plate, and a key-driven lift rod switches the gear between fixed teeth and the sector.

**How it works:** Teeth are cut round the inside of the platen, and a small gear on a shaft meshes with them, with a ratchet and a pawl-carrying large gear on the same shaft. When the printing key is pressed, a lift rod turns a mounting plate so the large gear meshes with fixed teeth, holding the platen, while the sector springs back against the stop of a spacing selector plate. On key release the large gear leaves the fixed teeth and meshes with the sector, and the platen's own spring turns it until the sector hits its limit stop, giving a fixed feed. Steps on the selector plate, held by a spring, set the spacing. A back key turns the ratchet backwards, limited by the same selector, an anti-reverse pawl holds the gear, and a free button disengages everything so the platen turns freely.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Vertical (column) feed device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a vertical feed device for a typewriter in which teeth are cut round the inside of the platen; a small gear meshing with these teeth is placed at a suitable point of the platen frame; a ratchet wheel is fixed on the same shaft as that gear, and a gear carrying a pawl is pivoted on the shaft so that it can turn; a mounting plate, with a spacing selector plate and a sector-shaped turning plate at its upper part and fixed teeth ("fixed pawl") at its lower part, is fitted so that it can turn as required, and these mesh in turn with the gear; and a lift rod is placed near the lower part, from which a rod with a projection sticks out, the projection engaging the mounting plate and turning it. Its object is to limit the turning of the platen accurately and easily, to make the adjustment free, and to make it very convenient to handle. Brief description of the drawings.
