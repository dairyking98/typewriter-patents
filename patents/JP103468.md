## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Wanted characters or signs must be printed automatically on a set length of paper and copied, so the selection can be told from the copy.

**Solution:** A motor-driven printing drum and cutter feed and cut paper, a hand-set selection drum stamps the chosen face, and a copying roller then copies it onto a take-up strip.

**How it works:** A motor turns a rotary printing drum and a roller that feed paper from a roll; a recess in the drum stops feed at a set point and a knife cuts the paper. A hand lever turns a selection drum through bevel gears to choose the face; a cam-track follower lowers a presser that stamps the paper on it at a set time. The cut paper reaches a stop, a toothed disc then engages a gear train starting the copying rollers, which pass the paper with a copy strip pressed to it and wind the copy on a take-up drum kept taut by a weight. Afterwards the gears disengage and the drum feeds the next paper.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Selective automatic printing device. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a selective automatic printing device having: a rotary printing part, made up of a rotary printing drum that prints as it turns and a roller that presses the printing paper against that drum, guides the paper together with it, and carries a knife to cut the paper at a set position; a selective printing part, made up of a selection drum turned by hand to choose a printing face, and a presser, linked to the said roller, that presses the paper against the selection drum; a copying-roller part that starts to turn when the paper has been printed and cut and has come to a set position, and guides the printing paper and a copy paper together while pressing the copy paper onto the printed face; and a take-up part, linked to the copying part, that winds up the copied copy paper. Its object is a simple, suitable selective automatic printing device that prints the wanted characters or signs automatically on a set piece of printing paper and copies the printed characters or signs.
