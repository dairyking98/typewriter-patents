## Summary

**Kind:** type-element machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies mounted on carriers rotatable for selection; Cylindrical Japanese-language typewriter

**In the English translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a Japanese-language typewriter with interchangeable type cylinders, in which rails fixed to a type-cylinder support are laid on rails fixed to the frame so that the support can move lightly;

**How it works (reading):** The type cylinder rests by grooved end rims on rollers in a support that slides on rails, so cylinders can be lifted off and exchanged as spares.

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** A small Japanese-language typewriter cannot print many characters from one cylinder, and a damaged cylinder or faulty type case puts the whole machine out of use.

**Solution:** The type cylinder rests by grooved end rims on rollers in a support that slides on rails, so cylinders can be lifted off and exchanged as spares.

**How it works:** Rails (5) on a type-cylinder support (4) ride on balls over rails (2) fixed to the frame, so the support slides parallel to the platen. The cylinder (6) has circumferential grooves (7) in both end rims that sit on rollers (8) on fixed axles at the front and back of the support, so it turns freely and is simply set on or lifted off. Its types face outward toward the platen (10). A printing device on a stand (11) on the support slide plate (19) reaches inside the cylinder: an operating lever (12) works a crank lever, links and swinging levers to a hammer (18) that strikes the selected type onto the paper on the platen. Turning and sliding the cylinder brings the wanted type to the print position.
