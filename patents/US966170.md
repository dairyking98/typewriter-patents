## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type bars rebound from the basket after they return and the type action must be rapid.

**Solution:** Type actions combine actuating means and locking so the type bars cannot rebound from the basket on return.

**Features:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)
