## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The platen must be advanced by a fixed amount for each character, in connection with the printing stroke, and also be freely turned for line changes or paper removal.

**Solution:** A floating pinion alternately engages an outer and an inner gear on the platen side, so each print stroke winds one gear against a spring and the return stroke advances the platen by a fixed step.

**How it works:** Two same-size gears sit side by side on the platen shaft, the outer one linked to the inner by a tension spring and a coil spring. On the print stroke a wedge or similar mechanism pushes a spring-loaded shaft so its pinion disengages from the outer gear and meshes with the inner gear, stopping the platen, while the outer gear turns under its spring until a pin on a locating bar reaches the end of a hole. On the return the pinion goes back to the outer gear, stopping it, and the inner gear turns by the same amount through the linking spring, advancing the platen one fixed space. Pressing a second shaft makes the pinion straddle both gears and frees a small ratchet, so both turn together for line turning or paper removal, with a claw and ratchet marking the print start.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Platen-turning device for a typewriter. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a platen-turning device for a typewriter, in which, in connection with the pressing down of the printing lever or printing frame, a suitable mechanism frees the mesh between the outer gear of several gears on the platen side and a pinion meshing with it, so that the outer gear turns, and engages the pinion with the inner gear that turns the platen, stopping the inner gear; after printing, in connection with the return of the printing lever or frame, the pinion is again engaged with the outer gear, stopping it, and the inner gear is made to turn by the amount the outer gear turned, fixing the next printing position; and in which, by making the pinion straddle and mesh with both the inner and outer gears, both can be turned together. Its object is to turn one of the gears on the platen side intermittently by a fixed amount at a time, to turn the other gear in connection with it and so turn the platen in one direction, and to do this by the advance and withdrawal of a floating pinion.
