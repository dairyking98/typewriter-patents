## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates more particularly to Io type-writing machines, but is applicable to other key-actuated instruments, and is designed to relieve the lingers and arms of the operator of much of the labor of operating the key-levers.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key levers tire the operator's fingers and arms.

**Solution:** An electromagnet and battery per key lever so the operator closes a contact with the first light part of the stroke and the magnet completes the rest.

**How it works:** A sub-base holds the typewriter and a row of single-core magnets, staggered under the key levers. A pivoted contact lever bears on the heel of each key lever; the first depression closes a circuit through a contact carrier, energizing the magnet, which pulls the key lever or its rod connector as armature (or via an interposed armature lever with a hook). The under side of the contact point is insulated so return does not re-close the circuit.
