## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing machines lack a simple way to move the carriage rapidly to the start of a column of numbers of a chosen denomination.

**Solution:** A tabulating attachment of few parts, placed on an existing machine, lets a denomination key release the carriage to a column-stop set at the chosen position.

**Features:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
