## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing columns and tabular matter on a Blickensderfer needs careful carriage placement, and narrow paper needs a stop.

**Solution:** An adjustable bar-and-sleeve attachment with a spring trip-pin that sounds the carriage bell and a set-screw stop to halt the carriage.

**How it works:** A square bar is clamped in the end of the machine frame. A sleeve slides on it and is fixed by a set-screw. A lug on the sleeve meets an adjustable set-screw in the carriage end to stop travel; a hollow boss holds a spring-loaded trip-pin, set by a nut, which strikes the existing carriage trip so the bell rings at the chosen column. The operator then types the initial of each entry directly below the previous one.
