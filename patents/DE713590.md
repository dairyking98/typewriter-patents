## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (number wheel alignment, printing unit) · **Subject (patent classes):** Aligning means

**How it works (reading):** An elastic coupling lets the higher-order wheel move after the lower one locks, so the higher digit is set correctly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Alignment teeth in two-digit number-wheel displays could mis-align the higher digit when the lower wheel is set near a limit.

**Solution:** An elastic coupling lets the higher-order wheel move after the lower one locks, so the higher digit is set correctly.

**How it works:** Lower-order wheel drives the higher-order wheel through an elastic coupling; the locking pawl then allows the higher wheel a further movement, preventing mis-alignment.
