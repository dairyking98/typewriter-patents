> Description Translated from German Cash register printer The device according to patent 51g 672 for any repetition of an imprint or for successive printing of different documents in the same or different lines by interrupting the checkout process by means of an automatically engaged locking mechanism has undergone a further development according to the invention in such a way that the one-time printing of a single Document is enabled. This is achieved in that the drive of the printing hammer is designed such that it can be disengaged so that it is held during part or the entire checkout process. A parking device required for this consists of a latch with working and resting positions. An embodiment of the invention is shown in the drawing. On the shaft 40, which carries a thumb disk 25 for the print hammer drive, there is also a cam disk 41, which acts on a bolt 42 of a push rod 44 and moves it against the action of a spring 43 acting on it. The end of the push rod 44 is rotatably attached to a rotary arm 45 which, with its hook-shaped end, engages a bolt 46 of the printing hammer 22 and can thereby prevent the printing hammer from swinging out.
