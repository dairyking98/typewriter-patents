## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Front lays, stops, or gauges

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to paper feeding mechanism for typewriting machines, and particularly to leading edge stop devices of such machines.

**How it works (reading):** Pivoted leading-edge stops, spring-pressed toward the platen, are held away by a release-key spring and moved further by a separate hand lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Leading-edge paper stops must be operable more readily than the earlier push-button design.

**Solution:** Pivoted leading-edge stops, spring-pressed toward the platen, are held away by a release-key spring and moved further by a separate hand lever.

**Features:** [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
