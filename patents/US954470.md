## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When copying short statements on a long record sheet, the platen must be turned back to a repeatable position after each bill head is withdrawn so copies do not waste space.

**Solution:** A clamp-on attachment geared to the platen shaft that stops a backward platen turn at a preset amount while forward turning stays unlimited.

**How it works:** A pinion in a two-plate housing is coupled to the platen shaft through a socket under the knob. A single stop, partly movable by the platen and partly on the attachment frame, one adjustable, limits only the backward turn. A release lever allows further backward movement. Arms fit it to L. C. Smith and Smith Premier machines.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)
- **Uses:** [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** pinion; platen shaft; stop; release lever; bracket arm; platen knob
