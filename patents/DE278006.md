## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper carriage cannot skip to a chosen column at a chosen decimal place.

**Solution:** Column stops on the carriage can be individually removed from the working position so the carriage skips them, and a comma-place stop is raised with the column button.
