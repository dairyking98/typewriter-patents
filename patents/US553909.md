## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Justified copy needs spaces of variable width, and ribbon colour changes instantly.

**Solution:** Spacing and ribbon colour are changed on the machine to justify lines and switch colour.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Proportional spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md)
