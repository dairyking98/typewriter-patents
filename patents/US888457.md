## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A small portable typewriter is wanted that is cheap to build yet keeps the advantages of large standard machines.

**Solution:** A three-row type wheel on a sliding carrier is rotated by a keyed oscillator and stop rack, then driven forward as one unit to print, and the same forward stroke steps the carriage.

**How it works:** A slide carrying the type wheel, its pinion, an oscillator and a stop rack runs transversely across the frame. The type wheel has three circumferential rows: lower case, capitals, and figures. Pressing a key through two banks of key levers, links and a toggle-linked bridging beam rocks the oscillator, whose geared segment turns the pinion and type wheel until the key's bell-crank extension meets the stop rack. Further depression then pushes the whole slide forward so the selected type strikes the paper on the roller platen, past two spring-mounted ink rolls, and a centering pin enters a hole beside the type to align it exactly. Each forward stroke pushes the platen carriage one step by a pawl for spacing, with a separate space key limited so the wheel does not strike, and a bell warns near the line end. Case is shifted by a rock shaft that lifts the wheel to the middle or highest row.
