## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar actions are hard to assemble and adjust.

**Solution:** A type action in which hangers, sub-levers and segments can be assembled and each part reached for adjustment or repair.

**How it works:** Typebar hangers are arranged on a segment, with a combined sub-lever segment and type rest with detachable shield; segments are rigidly connected together. Each part is reachable after assembly.

**Features:**

- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Typebar action](../features/bar-type-action.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [Adjustment and wear take-up](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** type bar; hanger; hanger segment; sub-lever; type rest; shield
