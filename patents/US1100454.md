## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin mechanism sits either at the front, in the way and needing a bulky carriage, or at the rear, inaccessible.

**Solution:** Place the margin-gage rack beneath the paper carriage, and tilt the carriage up to expose the rack tappets, ribbon spools and spring tension adjustment.

**How it works:** The margin rack and tappets sit beneath a compact carriage with upper and lower bars. Tilting the carriage up gives access to the adjustable tappets that set the margins and sound the alarm. The same movement raises the ribbon spools and the carriage spring tension adjuster into reach. A three-part adjustable tappet sounds a bell and then successively locks the keys at line end. The platen shifts between lower and upper case.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** margin-gage rack; tappet; carriage; ribbon spool; spring tension adjuster; bell; line lock; type bar; segment; platen; adjustable left margin stop

**Improves on:**

- margin mechanisms at front or rear of machine: rack placed beneath carriage, accessible by tilting
