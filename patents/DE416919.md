## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter driving card punch) · **Subject (patent classes):** Simultaneous marking of record carrier and printing-out of data, e.g. printing-punch

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates on a recorder that is connected to by a typewriter a card punching machine controlled by the keys of the typewriter is so that by the apparatus simultaneously with the writing down of a Business process also its determination according to its numerical.

**How it works (reading):** A contact box set by hand closes circuits for the card-punch magnets, and the typewriter carriage makes a contact across terminals as it moves; the typewriter keys then trigger the punch magnets for the number holes in the chosen columns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An invoice typed on a typewriter must also be punched into a card, which otherwise needs separate handling of the data.

**Solution:** A contact box set by hand closes circuits for the card-punch magnets, and the typewriter carriage makes a contact across terminals as it moves; the typewriter keys then trigger the punch magnets for the number holes in the chosen columns.

**Features:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md), [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
