## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator needs warning when the bottom of the sheet is reached.

**Solution:** A line lock tied to the platen-shift frame that locks the universal bar and shows an End of Page indicator.

**How it works:** A pawl on an angular lever moves with the platen frame and works against a rack with two tooth series spaced by the case-shift movement. The rack, spring-held on slots, is linked to a locking lever whose hook slides beneath a crank arm and locks the universal bar, keys and escapement. The lever also moves a link and rock shaft to raise an End of Page indicator plate in front of the machine. A finger-piece link sets or disables the lock.

**Features:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)

**Parts:** pawl; angular lever; rack; locking lever; universal bar; crank arm; rock shaft; indicator plate; link; spring
