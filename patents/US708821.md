## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Corrections are hard because the line of writing must be aligned exactly with the printing point.

**Solution:** The platen slides endwise by a threaded nut, and an adjustable clamp arm limits platen rotation to the line-space.

**How it works:** The platen spindle slides and rotates in the carriage frame. A milled nut on a screwed boss on the spindle moves it endwise one way while a spring returns it the other way. A swinging arm clamped to the platen boss carries an adjustable limiting screw that bears on the frame, so the platen can be turned forward precisely one line to bring the writing line to the printing point.

**Parts:** platen; spindle; milled nut; spring; swinging arm; limiting screw; lock nut; bracket lug
