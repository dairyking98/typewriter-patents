## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (die setting machine for lines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** The blank is heated inside the same block that contains the die-line slot, so it is stamped at the right temperature.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-casting blanks are heated and stamped in separate places, so blanks cool or overheat.

**Solution:** The blank is heated inside the same block that contains the die-line slot, so it is stamped at the right temperature.
