## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to devise a machine which will be entirely efficient for typewritiug music, the aim being to render possible the presentation of the musical composition accnrately and with a fair degree of rapidity and in a more legible and attractive form than usual in hand written music.

**How it works (reading):** A Remington-type typebar machine with music symbols that feeds the paper crosswise at each stroke and shifts note position and octave by shifting the platen.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Handwritten music is slow and less legible than a typewritten score.

**Solution:** A Remington-type typebar machine with music symbols that feeds the paper crosswise at each stroke and shifts note position and octave by shifting the platen.

**How it works:** The frame and typebars are like the Remington, with symbol typebars for notes and signs. The universal bar, through connecting rods and a swinging frame, moves a pawl lever that turns the platen one ratchet tooth after each stroke to feed the paper along the staff. Note position and octave shift mechanisms move the platen carriage endwise to set the vertical position on the staff. Separate devices space the characters and feed the ribbon, and scales show the position of the paper.

**Features:** [USE:special-script](../features/use-special-script.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [ESC:print-and-space](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)

**Parts:** typebar; key lever; universal bar; swinging frame; pawl lever; ratchet wheel; platen; platen carriage; scale

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter: adds music symbols and note-position shift
