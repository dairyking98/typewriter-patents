## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates especially to the paper-guides which are usually applied at the ends of a cylindrical platen.

**How it works (reading):** A paper guide on a lever at the platen ends holds the sheets evenly at the impression plane for single sheets and manifolding.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Thin multi-sheet paper with carbons wrinkles or creeps, and folded legal paper skews when fed.

**Solution:** A paper guide on a lever at the platen ends holds the sheets evenly at the impression plane for single sheets and manifolding.
