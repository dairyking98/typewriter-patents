## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A manuscript holder must be rigid and absorb the vibrations of writing so the copy does not shake.

**Solution:** The sheet holder is carried on a double Nuremberg scissors with stiffening plates, attached at its top and bottom to rod and ring pieces on a screw clamp fixed to the machine table.

## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Manuscript holder for typewriters. The invention relates to a Manuscript holder for typewriters with Nuremberg scissors as a support for the sheet holder. The invention consists in the fact that the scissors alternate between pairs of rods and full webs which the latter ever turn into, the former, is composed. - This is de # Carrier is rigid and absorbs the fluctuations that occur when writing. An example embodiment of the invention is shown in the drawing. The blade holder is attached by two tabs c to a round rod 12 on which a cross piece 1q. sits. A double Nuremberg pair of scissors made of four struts 15 and two plates 16 are attached to this crosspiece and to other tabs d of the blade holder. The scissors are stiffened by the plates. The free ends of the scissors are attached to a bolt 17 at the top and to a bolt 2o at the bottom. Bolt 17 sits on a rod 19, which is displaceable in a tube 22, bolt 2o on a ring 2 i, which is rotatable on a sleeve 23 provided with a pin 24. The pin 2g is connected to a screw clamp which carries the manuscript holder and which is screwed onto the machine table.
