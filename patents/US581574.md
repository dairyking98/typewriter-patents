## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar machine problems at line end, ribbon reversal and key interference.

**Solution:** Several refinements: end-of-line buffer, spool-powered ribbon reverse, key lock and swinging scale.

**How it works:** A buffer stops printing at line end without locking bars, a spool stores power to throw the reverse bar, only one key can be depressed at a time, and a swinging scale doubles as guide.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** typebar; key lever; ribbon spool; reverse bar; swinging scale; universal bar
