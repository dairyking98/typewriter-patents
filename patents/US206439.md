## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Faster typing with a mechanism where keys do not carry the type's inertia.

**Solution:** A constantly rotating disc carries pivoted types; a depressed key lets a pawl catch a ratchet which inks the chosen type and throws it out against the paper.

**How it works:** A disc with types pivoted around its rim is driven at about 200 rev/min by a spring motor in the base. Each of a set of keys pulls a wire that turns a trigger on a ring frame, putting a projection in the path of a pawl on the disc. The pawl is deflected into a ratchet loose on the shaft, whose arm lifts an inking-roller lever to the type face and then pushes a toothed rack against the type, turning it outward to the paper. A spring then throws the rack back and the ratchet is returned. Several disks can be used for more characters. The paper carriage is moved left by a separate mechanism as impressions are made.

**Features:** [ELEM:wheel-rim](../features/elem-wheel-rim.md), [ELEM:individual-slugs](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [INK:roller](../features/ink-roller.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** type disc; type; key; wire; trigger; pawl; ratchet; rack; inking roller; paper carriage; spring motor

**Numbers:** 200 revolutions per minute
