## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A vertical escapement is best for speed but a low-built typebar machine has no room for a horizontal ratchet shaft.

**Solution:** A plate-shaped ratchet wheel with angular teeth on a slightly inclined shaft lets a vertical escapement fit in the small space.

**How it works:** Carriage rack drives a pinion on the ratchet shaft. A shift-tooth carrier holds a fixed and a loose spring-loaded tooth. The intermediate lever nose pushes a bracket as the key is depressed, swinging the fixed tooth into the ratchet and the loose one out; on release the loose tooth drops into the next gap and carriage spring turns the ratchet one step. On carriage return a movable angle-lever stop lets the loose tooth swing aside.

> Description Translated from German Switching device for the paper trolley of type bar typewriters lower design. The invention relates to a switching device for the paper trolley of type bar typewriters of low design using the so-called Gearshift, which is known to be not only great stability but also, above all, greater Writing speed guaranteed. For their application in the most advantageous so far Type of construction - with a vertical (standing or hanging) arresting device - was valid In the present case it is the space constraint caused by the low construction of the machine to overcome. The gearshift works with a vertically arranged locking device is known to be particularly advantageous because of the weight of the individual parts the swinging is very little hindered. Requirement for the application of a vertically arranged locking device is of course that the teeth of the ratchet wheel also stand vertically, which would be easily achievable if the ratchet shaft could attach lying horizontally.
