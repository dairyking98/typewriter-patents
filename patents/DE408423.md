## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A vertical escapement is best for speed but a low-built typebar machine has no room for a horizontal ratchet shaft.

**Solution:** A plate-shaped ratchet wheel with angular teeth on a slightly inclined shaft lets a vertical escapement fit in the small space.

**How it works:** Carriage rack drives a pinion on the ratchet shaft. A shift-tooth carrier holds a fixed and a loose spring-loaded tooth. The intermediate lever nose pushes a bracket as the key is depressed, swinging the fixed tooth into the ratchet and the loose one out; on release the loose tooth drops into the next gap and carriage spring turns the ratchet one step. On carriage return a movable angle-lever stop lets the loose tooth swing aside.
