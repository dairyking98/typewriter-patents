## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing the type lever basket of a typewriter is difficult because its drive rods are coupled to the key levers.

**Solution:** A guide rail carrying lugs is withdrawn from the drive rods so the basket can be lifted out, and it pushes against the rod lugs to re-engage them when inserted.
