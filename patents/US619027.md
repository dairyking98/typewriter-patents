## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon reversal needs to be positive and automatic when either end of the ribbon is reached.

**Solution:** Ribbon-end fingers on a bar riding in slots of each spool are pulled outward by the last of the ribbon and trip a rock-shaft that snaps an oscillating pinion block over to the opposite spool.

**How it works:** The universal bail's ribbon guide oscillates with each key and, via a pawl and ratchet on a shaft with a pinion at each end, turns the spool gear of the engaged spool. A block carrying the shaft rocks to mesh one pinion or the other, held by two spring-coupled latches. The ribbon is hooked to a bar in a slot across each spool; as the ribbon runs out the pull moves the bar and its fingers outward until they hit a double arm on a rock-shaft. That arm releases one latch, tensions a spring that swings the block, the other latch snaps in and the opposite spool now winds, reversing the feed.
