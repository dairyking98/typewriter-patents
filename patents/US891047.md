## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons with two or more ribbon guides

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to the ribbon winding and vibrating mechanisms of typewriting machines, and especially those of the visible writing class, and has reference principally to machines in which'two independently vibrating ribbons are employed.

**How it works (reading):** Each pair of spools gets its own horizontal driving shaft, and a switching device silences one shaft while the other runs at the type strokes.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-ribbon visible writing machines need simple winding and vibrating gear that makes either ribbon active while the other idles.

**Solution:** Each pair of spools gets its own horizontal driving shaft, and a switching device silences one shaft while the other runs at the type strokes.

**How it works:** Two ribbons sit on two pairs of spools in front of the platen of the Underwood front-strike machine, each spool on a vertical shaft. Long and short horizontal driving shafts, one per pair, are rotated at each stroke. A switching mechanism selects the front ribbon, the rear ribbon, or neither, raising the chosen vibrator to cover the printing point. Automatic reversing devices in the spool cups change the winding direction when a ribbon runs out. Only the opening is available.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [Automatic ribbon reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md)

**Parts:** front ribbon; rear ribbon; vibrator; spool; spool shaft; driving shaft; switching device; reversing device

**Improves on:**

- Underwood two-ribbon mechanism: simplifies the spool winding and ribbon selection
