## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Flat-plate machines need one device for multi-layer carbon duplicating and slitting of folded paper tapes, with awkward part removal.

**Solution:** A rear support part carries carbon-sheet holders, separate paper guides and removable slitting knives, all adjustable to tape width and movable toward and away from the plate.
