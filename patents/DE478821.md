## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (cash register cylindrical keypad with check printing)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a cash register with a cylindrical keypad and a printing device influenced by special keys for the optional Issuing a check or bill with the main drive shaft on each Checkout aisle initially rotated in one direction by means of a slider crank mechanism, then turned back in the opposite direction beyond its rest position and finally is moved back to the rest position.

**How it works (reading):** The main drive shaft turns through three rotations; intermediate members drive the check device in the first and third rotation, and special keys disable it, though the OCR leaves the defect only partly clear.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Cash registers must issue checks or bills by special keys, but the checks and bills use one printing unit that must be switched off reliably.

**Solution:** The main drive shaft turns through three rotations; intermediate members drive the check device in the first and third rotation, and special keys disable it, though the OCR leaves the defect only partly clear.
