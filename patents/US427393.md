## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type faces need cleaning in one pass, and cleaning them one at a time is slow.

**Solution:** A brush on a vertical rod is raised and lowered by a bell-crank lever, so the brush sweeps past the faces of many type in the basket at once.

**Features:** [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)
