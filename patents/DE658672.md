## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cases or covers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a case for portable machines, in particular Office machines, such as typewriters and the like.

**How it works (reading):** A pocket on the lid is hinged to the lid and bag, so stationery stays reachable without removing the bag.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Portable typewriter cases had no convenient access to writing paper without removing the bag or its compartment.

**Solution:** A pocket on the lid is hinged to the lid and bag, so stationery stays reachable without removing the bag.
