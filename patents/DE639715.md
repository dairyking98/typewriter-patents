> Description Translated from German Edge stop for typewriters The invention relates to an edge stop for typewriters and a process for their manufacture. Edge stops have already become known that result from one on the Stop rod displaceable frame and one sitting on this and opposite him There are longitudinally movable adjusting pawls that have a tongue at one end for engaging the bar teeth and at its other end. with an upright standing, the longitudinal movement between the frame and the latch causing the drainage surface of the framework cooperates. Here, however, were during the between frame and Pawl movement, these two parts are not secured against lateral play, so that a smooth engagement of the latch tongue in the teeth of the stop rod does not was guaranteed. This disadvantage is eliminated by the invention, which is characterized is that one of the cooperating parts, preferably the drainage surface of the Frame, is cut in such a way that two fingers standing next to each other are created, while on the other part a tab-shaped extension fitting between the fingers is provided, the lateral play between the parts during their movement each other prevented.
