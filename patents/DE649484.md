## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** Locking pawls on the frame hold it to the machine frame, and a locking device couples to one pawl so the carriage is locked against the frame whenever the frame is lifted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage frame is hard to remove and refit; the carriage can move freely when removed.

**Solution:** Locking pawls on the frame hold it to the machine frame, and a locking device couples to one pawl so the carriage is locked against the frame whenever the frame is lifted.
