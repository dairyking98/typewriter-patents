## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device to facilitate the movement of adjustable parts on typewriters and similar machines, especially with the vertical adjustment of the platen with accessories.

**How it works (reading):** The platen or its switching frame is connected to a body under spring action so that the lever arm of the spring tension shortens as the spring tension grows and lengthens as it drops, so the platen weight is balanced and the key effort is constant in both directions.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Raising and lowering the platen with its accessories takes a force that changes with position, and the spring-assisted return drops the platen too fast.

**Solution:** The platen or its switching frame is connected to a body under spring action so that the lever arm of the spring tension shortens as the spring tension grows and lengthens as it drops, so the platen weight is balanced and the key effort is constant in both directions.
