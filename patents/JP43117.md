## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typebar machines print with a staccato blow of the type against the paper, which is noisy and rebounds.

**Solution:** A weighted energy-storing member and a toggle linkage on each typebar slow the bar in its last swing and press the type on rather than strike it, so printing is nearly silent.

**How it works:** The key lever swings a bell crank, which pulls a link to the foot of the typebar and sends the bar quickly toward the platen. The key lever hits a stop early; lost motion lets the bar carry on. As the bar swings, two toggle links (26, 30) and a weighted member (18, 19) on an auxiliary bell-crank lever (21) swing too, and the weight stores energy from the first part of the stroke. In the last part the toggle links straighten into one line and a second toggle (pivots 20, 25, 22) also lines up, so the bar moves slowly but with high force and prints by pressure. The auxiliary lever's short arm meets a stop, absorbing excess energy, and spring 33 plus the rebound return the bar. Each bracket carrying the toggle is adjustable sideways on a segment plate.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Typewriter (印字器, "printing machine"). Nature and object of the invention. The invention relates to typewriters, and to a construction giving an improved printing action, complete mechanically and functionally, in a comparatively small, light machine. Its objects are: first, to simplify the printing action, which comprises the key lever, the type bar and the chain of action between them, so that the machine can print without the unpleasant rebounding noise that comes from the staccato blow of the type bar against the paper behind; second, to keep the special advantages of a comparatively small construction, easy touch, fast work and practically noiseless printing; and third, to obtain a machine that can do this special printing work with structural and functional advantages.
