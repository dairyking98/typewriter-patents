## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (electric light advertising sign control) · **Subject (patent classes):** Control arrangements or circuits, of interest only in connection with visual indicators other than cathode-ray tubes to give the appearance of moving signs

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on an electrical switching device for electric light letter advertising, which is controlled by the keyboard of a typewriter through which the Lamps of a universal light field belonging to a character are individually converted will.

**How it works (reading):** Electromagnetic light armatures are latched by a rail with slots, and typewriter keys energise the magnets to set each lamp.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Wiring each lamp of a light-letter field to every key is impractical with many characters and fields.

**Solution:** Electromagnetic light armatures are latched by a rail with slots, and typewriter keys energise the magnets to set each lamp.
