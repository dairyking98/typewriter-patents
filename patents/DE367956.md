## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for portability, i.e. hand-held printers or laptop printers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This purpose is made such that the usually lying in a horizontal direction Type lever in its upward movement in a manner known per se essentially a Universal rail arranged in a vertical line and movable parallel to it meet, however, by means of a provided on the type lever bearing with the Space key related lever by depressing this key is moved.

**How it works (reading):** The machine frame is made of two frames that slide diagonally into one another front to back; the keyboard and type basket sit on one, the carriage, roller and ribbon on the other, and the carriage switch drive is taken from the type levers so nothing blocks the collapse.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Folding a typewriter for storage is hindered by the carriage, ribbon and switching parts that stand proud of the keyboard frame.

**Solution:** The machine frame is made of two frames that slide diagonally into one another front to back; the keyboard and type basket sit on one, the carriage, roller and ribbon on the other, and the carriage switch drive is taken from the type levers so nothing blocks the collapse.
