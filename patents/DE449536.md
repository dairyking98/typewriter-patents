## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Paper stacking devices are bulky and costly, and take up scarce desk space.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal sliding surface with bent-up stop rails stacks sheets by gravity against the corner, and lies flat when not used.
