## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Replace the carriage spring drum with an electric feed that keeps constant tension in the letter-feed direction and returns the carriage easily from any point.

**Solution:** An armature on the carriage runs between two rows of electromagnets; a rocker with contact shoes energizes the magnets ahead of or behind the armature to pull the carriage either way.

**How it works:** A rod on the carriage carries an armature running on a table between two rows of electromagnets. A rocker on the armature has contact shoes that slide over the table contacts, normally energizing magnets on the left so a steady tension pulls the carriage in the letter-feed direction against the escapement. Depressing a return key, via a bar and lug, reverses the rocker so the shoe on the carriage side energizes magnets to the right and the armature is drawn right, carrying the carriage back; the active shoe keeps closing circuits ahead of the armature.
