## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Threading and removing the ribbon in the vibrator was slow and the ribbon could jump out of the guide slots.

**Solution:** A sheet-metal ribbon vibrator with diagonal guide slots closed by offset guard fingers, sliding in a removable guide bracket.

**How it works:** The vibrator is a sheet-metal stem with a cross-head whose folded arms form slides running in grooves of a guide bracket screwed under the top plate. Each slide carries a long downward and a short upward guard finger, overlapping and offset so the ribbon can be slipped in sideways but cannot escape. The slots diverge toward the bottom to avoid slack at the lower ribbon edge. A lever engages a pivot pin at the stem's lower end.
