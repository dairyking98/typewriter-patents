## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Conveyor belts or like feeding devices

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to devices for feeding, guiding,controlling, and delivering paper sheets, cards, or envelops ordinarily used in writing-machines to the platen and coacting parts.

**How it works (reading):** A continuous endless band runs in contact with the platen on the non-printing side, as wide as the platen, to hold and feed sheets, cards or envelopes around the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards, envelopes and multiple sheets are not controlled accurately as they pass around the platen.

**Solution:** A continuous endless band runs in contact with the platen on the non-printing side, as wide as the platen, to hold and feed sheets, cards or envelopes around the platen.
