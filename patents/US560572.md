## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Electromagnetic power drives, e.g. applied to key levers applied to elements other than key levers and acting on type members

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to improvements in electrical type-writing machines,and has for its objects, first, to provide a simple, practical, and economical construction and arrangement of electrical magnets which when energized operate the type-bars with greater speed and regularity than can be done by the force of the hand alone;
