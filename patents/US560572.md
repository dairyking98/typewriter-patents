## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Mechanical typewriters need hand force and are slow, and earlier electrical machines were complicated and expensive.

**Solution:** An electromagnet for each typebar, switched by key contacts, drives the typebars, with electrical letter spacing, carriage return and line spacing, applied to a Remington.

**How it works:** A baseboard has a ring of magnets around an opening under the basket, each armature joined by a rod to a typebar near its fulcrum. Depressing a key slightly brings a spring contact against a metal frame and completes that magnet's circuit, which pulls the armature down and swings the type to print through the ribbon. A letter-spacing mechanism advances the carriage one space per typebar stroke or space key. A key touch also returns the carriage, rotates the platen and line-spaces, via an armature lever with a clutch.
