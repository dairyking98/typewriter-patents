## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices actuated when platen reaches the end of a line

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a locking device on a power-driven typewriter with a power-driven carriage retraction.

**How it works (reading):** A line-end locking device is driven from the power transmission members, so if any type-lever power stroke has started while the carriage is at the end position, the locking device moves into its locked position in the following step.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing line-end locks fail on power typewriters, and several keys pressed quickly at the end of the line can defeat locking balls or levers.

**Solution:** A line-end locking device is driven from the power transmission members, so if any type-lever power stroke has started while the carriage is at the end position, the locking device moves into its locked position in the following step.
