## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for printing musical scores

**In the patent’s own words:** The main objects of my invention is to enable these four characters to be placed in exactly the same vertical line which extends at right angles to the lines and spaces of the music-staff.

**How it works (reading):** A carriage sliding on a toothed rack carries a type-carrier with two rows of vertically sliding types, each with a finger key, along with a ribbon-feed crank.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A music typewriter must print musical notation using vertically sliding types, and the carriage must move on a rack.

**Solution:** A carriage sliding on a toothed rack carries a type-carrier with two rows of vertically sliding types, each with a finger key, along with a ribbon-feed crank.

**Features:**

- **Type element:** [Separate type slugs or plungers](../features/elem-individual-slugs.md)
- **Uses:** [Special scripts and characters](../features/use-special-script.md)

**Parts:** toothed rack; movable carriage; type-carrier; vertical types; finger keys; ribbon crank
