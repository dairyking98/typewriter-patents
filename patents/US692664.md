## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A standard type-head with several faces in one plane cannot print on a flat book page, because all characters strike at once.

**Solution:** Type-heads carry characters in different planes on a shiftable head, and a shift key rocks the head to bring one chosen character to the print point.

**How it works:** Each type-bar carries a movable type-head with two or more character series; the shift key rocks the head so one face comes to printing position, and a detent controls the shift.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md)

**Parts:** type-bars; type-heads; shift key
