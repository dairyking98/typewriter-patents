## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (railway car air heating) · **Subject (patent classes):** Means for heating only

**How it works (reading):** A pressure of the evaporating-liquid relay is made changeable so the adjuster changes the hot and cold air mix without the lag of the relay.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The thermal relay in the railway air heater lags behind the room sensor, so room temperature oscillates.

**Solution:** A pressure of the evaporating-liquid relay is made changeable so the adjuster changes the hot and cold air mix without the lag of the relay.
