## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** Inventor wants a simple, inexpensive, durable type-writer where printing is effected by simple means.

**Solution:** A radial-type wheel is turned by a pinion on a sliding rack bar whose handled pointer moves over an indicating bar of characters, and pressing the spring-suspended wheel frame down prints and spaces the carriage one tooth.

**How it works:** The type wheel (letters, numerals, punctuation and whole common words of 3-8 letters) sits in a vertically sliding frame held up by a spring, with ink rolls on the frame. A pinion on the wheel axle meshes with a sliding rack bar carrying a handled pointer; moving the pointer along the indicating bar turns the wheel until the chosen type lies under a slotted guide finger on the carriage, which engages studs at the back of the wheel to locate it. Pressing the handle depresses the frame so the type strikes the paper on the roll. On the downstroke a finger on the frame presses a pawl lever, so that when the spring raises the frame the carriage pawls drop back one rack tooth and the carriage advances one space. Extra spaces for whole words are made by hand on an extended lever arm; the carriage is returned by lifting the pawls, and a ratchet lever turns the paper roll for line spacing.
