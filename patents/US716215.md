## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing exponents and other intermediate-line characters needs hand turning of the paper cylinder, which wastes time and gives uneven results.

**Solution:** A device sets a fixed, adjustable intermediate offset from the normal line on the paper cylinder so that intermediate characters can be printed quickly.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)
