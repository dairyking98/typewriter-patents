## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Pointer typewriters make the hand both guide the pointer and press hard to print, which tires the wrist.

**Solution:** A spherical segmental type carrier hangs from a long single-armed printing lever, with the pointing key at the lever end, so a light press prints and the pointer key and carrier move together by a jointed parallelogram.

**How it works:** An index plate with a spherical recess sits in the base. A printing lever on a horizontal shaft carries the type carrier between its ends and the pointing key at its free end. A forked rod from key to carrier makes a jointed parallelogram that can swing round the lever axis for sideways movement. Moving the key tip over the index plate sets the carrier to the character, then a downward press against a weak spring prints. Carriage feed and inking are not part of this patent.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Press the pointer to print](../features/sel-pointer-press-prints.md)
- **Type element:** [Type sphere](../features/elem-sphere.md)
- **Impression:** [Element struck to the platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md), [Hand presses the type through](../features/imp-press-through.md)

**Parts:** index plate; pointing key; printing lever; spherical type carrier; forked rod; parallelogram linkage; weak spring; paper roller carriage

**Improves on:**

- pointer-key index typewriters: lever leverage reduces the printing effort and drops the gear wheels
