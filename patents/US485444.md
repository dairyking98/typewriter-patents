## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check punching and marking) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**How it works (reading):** A check marking machine in which an elastic printing surface prints each figure while a series of punches acts at the same time to pierce holes in the paper, forming the character both by print and by holes.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Checks could be altered after printing, so the printed value needs a tamper-proof companion.

**Solution:** A check marking machine in which an elastic printing surface prints each figure while a series of punches acts at the same time to pierce holes in the paper, forming the character both by print and by holes.

**Features:** [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [Rubber type](../features/elem-rubber-type.md)
