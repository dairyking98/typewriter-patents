## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter to be shown in operation in a shop window with the operator hidden.

**Solution:** A typewriter whose actions are electrically connected to a remote keyboard, with a reversible motor driving the carriage.

**How it works:** The type actions and characters are connected electrically to a distant keyboard. The carriage motor is reversible by a pole-changer on the armature, driving letter feed during writing and then running the carriage back, and partly turning the platen at each return. The armature is spirally wound so pull is uniform as the carriage starts and stops each impression. The motor feed could replace driving springs on ordinary machines.

- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)
