## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making entries in large record books needs the type mechanism to move over the book page.

**Solution:** A platen is held over the book on adjustable links, and a bed-frame carries a carriage and typebar mechanism that moves longitudinally and transversely over the page.

**How it works:** A table with hinged legs carries a flat rectangular platen on adjustable links so the book fits beneath. Elastic-strip paper clamps with tie-bars clamp the leaf. A bed-frame rests on seats on the platen, with a carriage fitted to its bars and a typebar mechanism of two circular plates, key rods, and spacing levers moving on the carriage. Bell-ringing and spacing-lever retracting devices are added.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md), [USE:book-typewriter](../features/use-book-typewriter.md), [PAP:flat-bed](../features/pap-flat-bed.md), [ESC:bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [ESC:moving-print-head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [PAP:paper-guide](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)

**Parts:** table; platen; link; clamping frame; bed-frame; carriage; type mechanism; spacing lever; bell
