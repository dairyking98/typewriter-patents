## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (accounting typewriter for postings) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bodily-changeable print heads or carriages

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates on a typewriter # ascliixie, especially booking engine that is especially for Postings on booking sheets, invoices and the like, but also for ordinary paperwork suitable is.

**How it works (reading):** The type basket and key levers move laterally across a fixed writing pad, so the sheet stays still during writing.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Writing pad moving with the carriage makes writing swing and reduces speed and print quality on wide booking machines.

**Solution:** The type basket and key levers move laterally across a fixed writing pad, so the sheet stays still during writing.
