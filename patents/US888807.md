## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character or syllable selection controlled by recorded information characterised by form of recorded information by punched records, e.g. cards, sheets by strips or tapes

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a type-writer operator which is adapted to so actuate a type- Writer that the latter performs its usual functions automatically, and in addition thereto receives new sheets of paper upon the completion of the writings.

**How it works (reading):** An operator with a keyboard that actuates the typewriter keys from a perforated record, which is punched as keys are typed by hand, and has keys for carriage return and paper feed.
