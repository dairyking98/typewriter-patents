## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typing and adding figures separately wastes labour.

**Solution:** An adding machine under the typewriter has plungers on a carriage that press its keys when typewriter keys are struck.

**How it works:** Laterally shiftable plungers over the adding machine keyboard are linked to typewriter keys so each key also operates the matching adding key.

**Features:**

- **Uses:** [Typewriter with adding machine](../features/use-calculating.md)

**Parts:** adding machine; plunger; carriage; key

**Numbers:** 8 rows of keys; 9 keys per row
