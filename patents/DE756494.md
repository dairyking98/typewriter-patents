## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mounting or connecting key buttons on or to key levers

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a fastening for display or actuating members From molded materials or the like.

**How it works (reading):** Two offset sets of raised pressed projections on the key lever grip the button firmly without extra parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key buttons press-fit on key levers come off too easily, but metal inserts make the buttons too expensive.

**Solution:** Two offset sets of raised pressed projections on the key lever grip the button firmly without extra parts.
