## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** The sliding drive member is mounted in the fixed frame, and the pin and slot connection is between the sliding member and the parts it drives, so the shifted segment needs only light pivot bearings.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The type lever drive in a shift segment uses a pin and slot on the shifted segment, so the segment needs plain bearings and becomes heavy and bulky.

**Solution:** The sliding drive member is mounted in the fixed frame, and the pin and slot connection is between the sliding member and the parts it drives, so the shifted segment needs only light pivot bearings.

**How it works:** Keys move a sliding member in a straight line at right angles to the type lever segment; the pin and slot link transfers the motion to the type levers on the shifting segment.
