## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings or hangers for type levers

**In the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a cushion of peculiar structure which is sufficiently inelastic to prevent any material rebounding of the type-bar and which is soft enough to prevent any injury to the type-bar or any considerable noise due to its impact.
