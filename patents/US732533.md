## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Roller platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to what may be termed auxiliary platen-feed mechanism for type-writing machines and by means of A parts of a type-writing machine is illustrated which the platen may be rotated in either direction to any extent independently of the ordinary line-spacing mechanism to bring any desired portion of the paper to the printingline.
