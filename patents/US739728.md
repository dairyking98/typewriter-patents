## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having two pawls or like detents and mounted on a single rocker

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in carriage-feed mechanism for type-writers of the Shimer type havinga vertical escapementshaft.

**How it works (reading):** A vertical escapement shaft carries a pinion meshing with the carriage rack and an escapement with teeth at its lower end, driven by a crank bar on a shaft that key levers actuate.
