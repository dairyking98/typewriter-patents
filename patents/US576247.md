## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improvements to the Franklin typewriter as a complete typebar machine.

**Solution:** A typebar machine with ribbon spool drive, bell, shift mechanism, and a platen roll frame, claimed in many detail combinations.

**How it works:** Only the figure list is in the opening: key levers with type-levers in a frame, a paper-roll frame that moves for shifting, ribbon spools with shifting lever and drive, a bell striker near the line end, a carriage frame operating mechanism and a platen with end parts. The mechanism itself is not in the visible text.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Type element:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Ribbon feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** key; type-lever; paper-roll frame; platen roll; ink-ribbon spool; ribbon-shifting lever; bell; carriage frame

**Numbers:** 10 sheets of drawings
