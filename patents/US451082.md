## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-head pivoted to or rotating on lever

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to construct a type-writenin which capitals or lower-case may be written by a single set of keys, so that the use of a capitalizing-key will be avoided.
