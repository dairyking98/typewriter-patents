## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A separate capitalizing key and shift was wanted gone so that one set of keys writes both lower and upper case.

**Solution:** Each typebar carries a pivoted type-lever with lower and upper case types, flipped by a second key part that is pressed together with the first.

**How it works:** Each type-arm is pivoted in a ring and carries at its free end a type-lever with lower-case and upper-case types. A two-part finger-piece interlocks: pressing the front part alone moves one key-lever and swings the arm up with the lower-case type. Pressing both parts pulls a second key-lever whose link and wire turn the type-lever on its pivot, presenting the upper-case type, before the arm strikes. Release returns it to lower case. A compact hollow-arm variation is mentioned.
