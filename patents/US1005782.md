## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator needs an audible warning before the carriage reaches the line end.

**Solution:** An adjustable stop on the carriage engages a pawl on a pivoted support; the pawl's release lets a spring return the support quickly, and a weight on an arm strikes a bell, after which the carriage travels a known distance to the stop.

**How it works:** The carriage stop depresses an oblique pawl until it passes; the spring retracts the support, the arm weight rings the bell, and the carriage then moves a fixed distance to its final stop.

**Features:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage stop bar; adjustable stop; pivoted support; operating pawl; stop pawl; spring arm; weight; bell disk
