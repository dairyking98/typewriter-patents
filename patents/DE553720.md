## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement or simplification of a device described and illustrated in patent 551 171 on typewriters for securing the writing limit at the lower edge of the sheet of paper, its return to the starting position by uncoupling the paper roller takes place automatically.

**How it works (reading):** Locking device with two equal internally toothed drums; a stop on an adjusting ring sets the line count and a spring-loaded stop on the second drum moves line by line, with fewer parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Line-limit locking device at the lower sheet edge was made of too many sensitive parts that jammed.

**Solution:** Locking device with two equal internally toothed drums; a stop on an adjusting ring sets the line count and a spring-loaded stop on the second drum moves line by line, with fewer parts.
