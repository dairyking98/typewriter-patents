## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to certain improvements in type-writers, the object being to provide a construction in a machine of this kind whereby the extent of movement of the carriage carrying the cylinder varies in proportion to the width of the letter that is printed, whereby the spacing of the letters instead of being uniform withoutregard to the width of the letter conforms to the width ofthe letter.

**How it works (reading):** Key levers engage one of three concentric pivoted arms that regulate a detent so the carriage rack moves one, two or three spaces depending on letter width.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Uniform carriage spacing gives wide letters like m and w the same room as narrow ones, so the print looks cramped and uneven.

**Solution:** Key levers engage one of three concentric pivoted arms that regulate a detent so the carriage rack moves one, two or three spaces depending on letter width.

**How it works:** Three concentric pivoted arms with cross-pieces lie under the key levers; each key lever has a projection that engages the cross-piece of the arm for its width class. The arm acts through a detent and regulating dog on the toothed rack of the carriage so the rack moves one, two or three tooth spaces. For example i gets one, a two, and m three. The alphabet is divided into three widths.

**Features:** [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** key lever; pivoted arm; cross-piece; detent; regulating dog; toothed rack

**Numbers:** 3 letter widths; a = twice the space of i, m = three times
