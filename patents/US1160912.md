## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in front strike or visible writing typewriting machines in which the type-bars are mounted on a supporting segment which is adapted to be shifted for placing one or another of the sets of type on the type-bars in printing relation to the platen.

**How it works (reading):** Connect the ribbon vibrator lever directly to the universal bar and operate escapement and vibrator by the typebars, with vibrator motions that bring different ribbon fields to the print point or switch it off.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A Moyer-type front-strike visible typewriter with a shiftable typebar segment needs its ribbon vibrator and carriage escapement made to work with the shifting segment, and parts that assemble easily.

**Solution:** Connect the ribbon vibrator lever directly to the universal bar and operate escapement and vibrator by the typebars, with vibrator motions that bring different ribbon fields to the print point or switch it off.

**How it works:** Typebars pivot in an arc on a segment below the carriage and swing up and back to strike the front of the platen. The segment shifts so another set of types on each bar meets the platen. A single-casting top plate carries the carriage track. The ribbon vibrator lever links straight to the universal bar without loose intermediate connections, and can give different strokes to lift one of several ribbon fields, or be thrown out of action. Escapement and vibrator are both actuated directly by the typebar action.
