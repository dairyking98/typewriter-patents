## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A paper guide must square the sheet against the platen and show the line position.

**Solution:** A platen carries an annulus with radial slits at letter-space intervals, aligned with the line-space ratchet teeth, so a guide and line indicator on the platen can align the paper and writing lines.
