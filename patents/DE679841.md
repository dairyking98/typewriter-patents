## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (web cutting for packaging) · **Subject (patent classes):** Registering sheets, blanks, or webs

**How it works (reading):** Swing arms with feeler buttons enter hole marks in the web and trigger the cutter with purely mechanical elements, without reversing the web.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cutting a web at a printed mark is unreliable when the web must be reversed or when sensors clog with dust.

**Solution:** Swing arms with feeler buttons enter hole marks in the web and trigger the cutter with purely mechanical elements, without reversing the web.
