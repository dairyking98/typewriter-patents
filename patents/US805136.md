## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Framework

**In the patent’s own words:** This purpose being alike at both ends of the carriage and being constructed as follows: In the upper margins of said frame-plates4 A and A are formed upwardly-opening notches a3 a3, adapted to receive the end of the frame-bar, which latter is made long enough to extend beyond the outer faces of said frame-plates.

**How it works (reading):** The platen can be removed from the carriage and disconnected from the line-spacing devices, so a card-holding platen can replace the smooth platen on the same carriage.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The platen cannot be removed from the paper carriage without disconnecting it from the line-spacing drive, so a special card-holding platen cannot be swapped in.

**Solution:** The platen can be removed from the carriage and disconnected from the line-spacing devices, so a card-holding platen can replace the smooth platen on the same carriage.
