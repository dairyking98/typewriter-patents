## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon copies made on a rubber platen are blurred on the lower sheets, because the platen surface is not hard enough.

**Solution:** A sheet of celluloid placed between the paper and the platen gives a hard foundation; its surface is roughened to avoid slipping.

**How it works:** The celluloid sheet, the same size as the paper, sits on the platen beneath the paper and carbon sets.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md)

**Parts:** celluloid sheet; roughened surface
