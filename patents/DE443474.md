## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tables or desks for office equipment, e.g. typewriters, keyboards with vertical adjustable parts

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriter tables with a height-adjustable support plate for the typewriter and with a foldable and retractable front panel.

**How it works (reading):** Guides for the retractable front wall sit below the lowest position of the support plate, so the folded wall serves as a pen rest without interfering.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The raised front wall of a typewriter table collides with the height-adjusted support plate.

**Solution:** Guides for the retractable front wall sit below the lowest position of the support plate, so the folded wall serves as a pen rest without interfering.
