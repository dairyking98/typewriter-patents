## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to improvements in type-writers, and particularly to the carriage construction, In type-writers of this character having a revoluble platen it is often desirable that the operator be able to readily remove the platen and general carriage structure from the other parts of the machine, in order to substitute a carriage having a different capacity for performing special work.

**How it works (reading):** The carriage plate rests on the back plate of the machine and is clamped by adjustable clamps and thumb-screws to a rear guide-rail, so it can be removed and replaced by a simple operation.
