## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Locking devices applied to printing mechanisms and automatically actuated by completion of a page or predetermined number of lines or exhaustion of paper to lock the keyboard

**How it works (reading):** A toothed wheel on the platen arbor is locked against a bushed composite part on the carriage frame by a spring-loaded lever with notches, and a rocking U-piece on a track sets the carriage in relation to the main frame.
