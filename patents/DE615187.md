## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**How it works (reading):** Use rotary magnets, with a cylindrical armature turning in a cylindrical pole cavity with a small air gap, as the power source for each key.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Solenoids and lifting magnets used for electric typewriter drive need large power, heat up, and slam noisily onto the poles.

**Solution:** Use rotary magnets, with a cylindrical armature turning in a cylindrical pole cavity with a small air gap, as the power source for each key.

**How it works:** The armature rotates about its center of gravity within cylindrical pole faces, so the attractions of the two poles cancel and the shaft carries only torque. The air gap stays constant while the overlap grows, giving a very strong initial torque that falls off as the armature turns. The current is switched off at the end position and the armature can continue turning, so it does not stick to the poles. Only the opening is available.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Impression:** [Electric drive](../features/imp-electric.md)

**Parts:** rotary magnet; armature; pole face

**Improves on:**

- solenoid and lifting-magnet drives: constant small air gap, balanced armature, stronger starting torque
