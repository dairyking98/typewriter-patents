## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter and calculator combination)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter, those made up of individual machines with laterally displaceable paper trolleys (typewriter and calculating machine) and relates particularly to locomotion this car.

**How it works (reading):** A control button linked to bell-crank levers presses the tabulator keys of both machines together, and it can also start the adding machine motor alone or with the carriages.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a combined typewriter and calculating machine the carriages must be movable together or separately.

**Solution:** A control button linked to bell-crank levers presses the tabulator keys of both machines together, and it can also start the adding machine motor alone or with the carriages.
