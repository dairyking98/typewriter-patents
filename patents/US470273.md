## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators did not shift the Remington ribbon sideways, so one strip of it wore out.

**Solution:** Link the ribbon reversing gear to the sideways ribbon shift so every second reversal moves the ribbon across its width.

**How it works:** A spring drum pulls the carriage and a ratchet and bevel gears drive two ribbon spool shafts. Wire frames embracing the spools are riveted to sliding bars joined by a rock-shaft and rocker arms. The reversal mechanism now moves the bars so the spools and ribbon shift to a new field at every second reversal.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md), [INK:ribbon-reverse](../features/ink-ribbon-reverse.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md)

**Parts:** spring drum; strap; ratchet; bevel gear; spool shaft; ribbon spool; wire frame; sliding bar; rock-shaft; rocker arm

**Improves on:**

- Remington typewriter: automatic widthwise shift
