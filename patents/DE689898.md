## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**How it works (reading):** Stepped limit stops made of two adjustable screws sit under a thrust piece, giving a fine height adjustment for the ribbon fork lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon fork stroke is hard to set because limit stops are bent or filed; screw-type stops are awkward to adjust.

**Solution:** Stepped limit stops made of two adjustable screws sit under a thrust piece, giving a fine height adjustment for the ribbon fork lever.
