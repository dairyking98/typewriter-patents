## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried on sliding bars or rods

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a type bar typewriter, each with a A plurality of type groups provided type bar with one of the number of types one cooperates with each group corresponding plurality of key levers and in the each type bar is slidably mounted on a pivoting support, as well as with one power drive device controlled by the depression of each key lifter.

**How it works (reading):** Each type bar is driven by a toggle lever with two independent control angle levers connected by tie rods to the power drive, one of which also acts on the pivoting carrier.

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The linkage between a pivoting type bar carrier and its power drive was complicated and costly to make.

**Solution:** Each type bar is driven by a toggle lever with two independent control angle levers connected by tie rods to the power drive, one of which also acts on the pivoting carrier.

**How it works:** Each frame-shaped type bar carries four types and slides on a pivoting support. Two key levers per bar pull rods that engage two angle levers on a common pivot. Their power-drive parts are rotated by the power shaft. One key gives a longitudinal movement only, printing one type. The other also lifts the bar on its carrier, printing a second type. The platen moves to a second position to select the other pair of types.
