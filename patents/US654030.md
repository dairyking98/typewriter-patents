## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Line spacing should happen automatically with the carriage return, with single or double spacing, using a lever that moves along the carriage's line of travel.

**Solution:** A hand rod with a pinion turns a toothed pawl carrier, so one swing both line-spaces the platen and returns the carriage, and a pivoted gage plate changes the pawl fulcrum for single or double space.

**How it works:** A ratchet wheel sits on the platen end. A spring pawl is pivoted on a pawl carrier on a sleeve with bevel teeth on the carriage frame. A rocking rod with a pinion meshes with the sleeve teeth and extends outward and down as a hand lever, with an adjustable stop limiting its rock. Pulling the lever turns the carrier, so the pawl feeds the ratchet and the lever's motion also returns the carriage. A coil spring restores the carrier. A gage plate swung under the pawl heel moves its fulcrum so it advances two teeth instead of one.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [ESC:carriage-return](../features/esc-carriage-return.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** platen roll; ratchet wheel; pawl; pawl carrier; gage plate; rocking rod; pinion; stop pin; coil spring

**Numbers:** one or two ratchet teeth per line
