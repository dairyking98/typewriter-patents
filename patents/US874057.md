## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In visible writing machines the type bars in front of the platen obstruct the operator's view of the writing.

**Solution:** Type bars are arranged in front of the platen so they strike the top of the platen and, at rest, do not block the view; bearing plates with transverse holes carry the type-bar axles.
