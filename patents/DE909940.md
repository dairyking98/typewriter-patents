**How it works (reading):** The electric drive of the carriage elevator is made ineffective automatically when the paper-web adjusting device is swung out and the platen is not in the writing position.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On billing machines with power carriage elevator, the elevator can run while the platen is swung forward, risking accidents.

**Solution:** The electric drive of the carriage elevator is made ineffective automatically when the paper-web adjusting device is swung out and the platen is not in the writing position.
