In such machines, the paper roll is usually such arranged that it can be pivoted from the writing position into a front position, in order to facilitate the pulling forward of the paper web, which is done for the purpose of following parts align the web with the sheets of carbon paper. If with a such a machine the paper carriage is moved forwards or backwards by power drive there is a risk that a power-operated car elevator will be switched on while the platen is in the front position. Definitely the operator must constantly take care to avoid such accidents. This is troublesome and reduces the effectiveness.
