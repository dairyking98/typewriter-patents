## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of the invention are to improve on suchdevices and enable the cylindrical litten of the machine to be utilized for both paper and cards without'l altering or removing any of the parts.

**How it works (reading):** A slot is cut through the platen wall and the rubber covering, and a card-holding clamp inside the platen is projected and retracted through it, so the surface stays smooth when the clamp is retracted.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen cannot take both paper and cards without changing parts, and a plain paper surface is needed when no card is in use.

**Solution:** A slot is cut through the platen wall and the rubber covering, and a card-holding clamp inside the platen is projected and retracted through it, so the surface stays smooth when the clamp is retracted.
