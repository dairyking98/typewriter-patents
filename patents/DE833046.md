## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Shift-type carriage moving the type segment relative to the pallet causes noise and difficulty in paper feed.

**Solution:** A pivoting rocker carries the paper guide and pressure abutment, with a spring and knob lock and a pivot actuated by the shift key.
