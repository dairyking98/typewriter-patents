**How it works (reading):** Keys are blocked from punching a hole unless the guide card has the matching hole in that column, with contacts in the punch magnet circuit enforcing it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hand-keyed punching on punch-card machines needs checking against a guide card to catch errors.

**Solution:** Keys are blocked from punching a hole unless the guide card has the matching hole in that column, with contacts in the punch magnet circuit enforcing it.
