## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Improve the support and shifting of the platen in a typebar machine, controlling the case shift.

**Solution:** A platen frame whose shift is controlled by a case-shift key through shafts and connections.

**How it works:** Typebars with two types each strike upward and rearward against the front of a cylindrical platen. The carriage runs on a grooved bar over rollers and is pulled by a spring drum and strap. A feed rack engages a pinion linked to an escapement wheel engaged by loose and fixed feed dogs on an oscillating dog-rocker driven from the universal bar. The platen frame is carried by arms, and the case-shift key moves it. The opening text does not describe the shift linkage itself.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:spring-drum](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [SEL:keyboard-full](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen frame; carriage; spring drum; strap; feed rack; pinion; escapement wheel; loose dog; fixed dog; dog-rocker; universal bar; case-shift key
