## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Insertions and corrections are hard to place because the paper cannot be shifted precisely to bring a spot to the printing point.

**Solution:** The paper and platen are adjusted longitudinally and circumferentially by minute amounts, with a lever and pawl for endwise motion and a thumb adjustment for rotation.

**How it works:** A lever and pawl move the platen carriage endwise, released automatically from that control; the platen is rotated through minute arcs by a separate adjuster.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; platen carriage; lever; pawl; truck
