> Description Translated from German Device on typewriters for writing the date The invention relates to a device on typewriters for writing the date. There are already known devices in which the day and month number by a Keystroke was imprinted. In these known devices, To enable the types to be attached to your type lever, a special type lever format required, which means that the production and Use of special tools was required. As a result, the manufacture was such devices are very expensive and unsatisfactory to manufacture. According to the invention, these eight parts are now eliminated in that a type that prints the day and the month together on a common and Typically mounted type levers are arranged to be easily exchangeable. ' The day and the types imprinting the month are advantageous on separate, but arranged type body carried by a common type lever. The type bodies are appropriately equipped with guides that grip the type lever head like a fork, and held in place on the type lever by a locking member.
