## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies with types or dies carried upon levers or radial arms, e.g. manually operated

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device on typewriters for writing the date.

**How it works (reading):** A standard type lever carries two interchangeable type bodies (day and month) as a fork-guided pair held on the lever head by a locking member, so ordinary levers can be used and the date types are quick to change.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Date-writing type levers needed a special type lever format, so manufacture and tooling were expensive.

**Solution:** A standard type lever carries two interchangeable type bodies (day and month) as a fork-guided pair held on the lever head by a locking member, so ordinary levers can be used and the date types are quick to change.
