## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Connecting links between key-levers and typebars can come loose in use, and must be adjustable and detachable.

**Solution:** A two-part connecting link joined by a turnbuckle connects a key-lever to a typebar through eyes and detachable pivots, so the link stays secure and can be adjusted.
