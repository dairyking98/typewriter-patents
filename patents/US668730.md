## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** Its objects are to provide improved spring mechanism for the type key-levers and an improved detachable link connection to the type-bars;

**How it works (reading):** Guides keep the type-bars apart on both strokes, a detachable link joins the bars to the key-levers, a front shield deadens the noise, and the type-bar springs can be adjusted both independently and together.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bars strike one another near the basket, the types are noisy when they fall back, the link joints are weak, and the spring adjustment is awkward.

**Solution:** Guides keep the type-bars apart on both strokes, a detachable link joins the bars to the key-levers, a front shield deadens the noise, and the type-bar springs can be adjusted both independently and together.
