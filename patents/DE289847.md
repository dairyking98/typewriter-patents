## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sheets of paper were hard to insert and keep aligned, and the feed belts were inaccessible in the working position.

**Solution:** An insert table with studded endless belts that take the sheets by their side holes and feed them straight onto the paper roller so the front faces the writer.
