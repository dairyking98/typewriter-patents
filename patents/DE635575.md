## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Character- or line-spacing mechanisms with means for effecting line or character spacing in either direction

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a further embodiment the type wheel typewriter according to patent 6o9 811 with a device for switching <ler direction of movement of the type imprint through a ratchet lock or similar.

**How it works (reading):** Two oppositely acting carriage pawls are mounted on the type-wheel slide plate and pass through limit stops on a common adjusting rail, so one pawl works while the other is held off; the off pawl still engages the rack near the end of advance, braking the carriage and making feed inaccurate-free.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** For right-to-left scripts the carriage must move the other way, and with a carriage driven directly by key pressure the feed is uneven because of differing accelerations.

**Solution:** Two oppositely acting carriage pawls are mounted on the type-wheel slide plate and pass through limit stops on a common adjusting rail, so one pawl works while the other is held off; the off pawl still engages the rack near the end of advance, braking the carriage and making feed inaccurate-free.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md)

**Parts:** two switchable carriage pawls; adjusting rail with limit stops; type-wheel slide plate; rack
