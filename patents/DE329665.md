## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ratchet wheel fixed to the carriage rack gear makes teeth slide and wear on carriage return, and earlier pawl couplings still gave noise, wear and uneven line starts.

**Solution:** Not stated in the first 3000 characters, which cut off before the solution; the stated aim is to couple the indexing ratchet to the carriage drive without pawl noise or wear on return.

To eliminate these shortcomings, it has already been proposed to arrange one or more pawls in such a way that they only couple the ratchet wheel with the drive wheel when the paper carriage is moving to the left, while this coupling does not come into effect when returning the paper carriage to the initial writing position.
