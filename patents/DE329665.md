These bodies Have the disadvantage that the teeth of the ratchet wheel when the paper carriage is returned slide along the shift tooth loaded by a spring. need to, on the one hand a strong noise is caused, on the other hand the relevant parts of a exposed to premature wear and, thirdly, increased use of force is required to return the car. To eliminate these shortcomings, it has already been proposed to arrange one or more pawls in such a way that they only couple the ratchet wheel with the drive wheel when the paper carriage is moving to the left, while this coupling does not come into effect when returning the paper carriage to the initial writing position.
