## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Feeding to a particular line, e.g. by rotation of platen or feed roller

**How it works (reading):** Three coupled typewriters print the same entries, while two skip columns with tabulator or large carriage steps to leave gaps for their own columns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Accounting requires journal and account cards to be written at once, but the typewriters print different text.

**Solution:** Three coupled typewriters print the same entries, while two skip columns with tabulator or large carriage steps to leave gaps for their own columns.
