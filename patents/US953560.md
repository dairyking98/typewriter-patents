## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a tabulating mechanism for typewriters, andparticularly to a construction provided with a carriage stop and a cooperating jumper stop adapted to be thrown into the path of the carriage stop and to simultaneously release the car-- riage for movement.

**How it works (reading):** A jumper stop is thrown into the path of an adjustable carriage stop through a key lever connection, with a yielding mount so it can ride the stop, and it releases the controlling dogs at the same time.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The tabulating stop must be moved into the carriage stop's path without hindering the tabulating action, and it must stay correctly aligned with the scales.

**Solution:** A jumper stop is thrown into the path of an adjustable carriage stop through a key lever connection, with a yielding mount so it can ride the stop, and it releases the controlling dogs at the same time.
