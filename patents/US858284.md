## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The frame stop must be governed so that it cooperates with the margin stops and tabulator stops on the carriage, and the earlier arrangement did not control that position.

**Solution:** A toothed margin bar carries removable margin stops, and a toothed tabulator stop bar carries removable tabulator stops; a vertical lever and a rock shaft govern the position of the frame stop that cooperates with them.
