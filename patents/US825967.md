## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** The Hammond shuttle was heavy and slow, and after a key on one side of the keyboard the shuttle had to return to centre before a key on the other side could be struck.

**Solution:** Make the shuttle a very thin hard-rubber ring whose top flange both stiffens it and connects to the shuttle arm, and add independent speed-dogs between the key drivers and the arm so the shuttle can reverse without recentring.

**How it works:** A complete ring of hard rubber surrounds a fixed ring anvil on a post, with an inward flange around its top edge resting on the anvil edge, optionally on ball bearings in a groove or on rollers bearing on the inner side. The shuttle arm passes through an eyelet riveted into the flange and carries a sleeve around the anvil post. Two supplemental swinging speed-dogs, each with notches that engage a pin on the shuttle arm, are driven by the toothed main drivers via pins and can move independently, so one dog follows the shuttle back past centre while the other pushes it toward the opposite key position until the stop arm meets the stop pin. The flange carries a series of eyelets so the shuttle can be lifted, turned to another font, and re-engaged with the arm.
