## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more particularly to the escapement mechanism thereof;

**How it works (reading):** Finger-key motion is transmitted to the escapement, with a back-stop pawl on the escapement wheel and a line lock arrester; touch is varied independently of the feed action.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Escapement, key touch and line lock must be reliable and fast, and key touch should be varied without changing the feed.

**Solution:** Finger-key motion is transmitted to the escapement, with a back-stop pawl on the escapement wheel and a line lock arrester; touch is varied independently of the feed action.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
