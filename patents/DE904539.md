## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Plastic keytops with stamped celluloid characters peel off, moulded-in or two-shot characters are not sharp and need reworking, and engraved characters fill poorly and collect dirt.

**Solution:** The character carrier is encapsulated, except on its underside, in crystal-clear plastic, so the characters show through and the key surface is smooth with no recesses; the characters may be emphasised with paint in the recesses, wiped to leave colour only in the grooves.
