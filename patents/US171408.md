## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Printing text in several type varieties from one machine without setting separate fonts, forms and composing-sticks.

**Solution:** A revolving wheel with radial slots holds pivoted type-carriers (circles, ellipses or polygons) that can all be turned together to present a different variety of type to the hammer.

**How it works:** Type-carriers sit in radial slots of a wheel on a split wire ring in an annular groove. Each carrier has type set into its face or periphery, one of each variety, projecting beyond the wheel's face to receive the blow of the printing hammer or platen. A spur wheel on each carrier engages a rack bar that runs through a lateral slot in the wheel and is keyed into an annular groove of a ring on a sleeve. The sleeve turns with the shaft but slides along it by a lever or screw, and sliding it moves all the rack bars to rotate every carrier at once to the next variety. The wheel is driven by power, mechanical or electric.
