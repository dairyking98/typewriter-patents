## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Scales and indicators, e.g. for determining side margins for determining top and bottom margins or indicating exhaust of paper

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to a novel indicator for use on'paper-carriages of type writing machines, and designed to indicate the extent to which the platen shouldbe turned backwardly by hand for inserting new sheets when making carbon copies .

**How it works (reading):** A rotative part on the platen shaft, normally locked to the shaft by a collar, carries an indicator read against a scale strip above the platen and adjacent to the guide-roller.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** When making carbon copies on a single record strip, the operator needs a visible measure of how far the platen is turned back to insert new sheets at uniform spacing.

**Solution:** A rotative part on the platen shaft, normally locked to the shaft by a collar, carries an indicator read against a scale strip above the platen and adjacent to the guide-roller.

**Features:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen or paper-roller A; paper-carriage end plate B; paper-guides C and D; scale-strip H; guide-roller G; rotative part I; collar M; turning knob
