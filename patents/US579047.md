## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Adding machines need full letter and character printing.

**Solution:** A bank of four key rows and an oscillating type carrier, with a depressible bar acted on by the key-levers, add an alphabet to the adding machine.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
