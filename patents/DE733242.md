## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sound-deadening, or shock-absorbing stands, supports, cases or pads separate from machines

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement of the subject matter of the main sponsor 732457e that on a sound and vibration-reducing pad for typewriters relates.

**How it works (reading):** Give the rubber balls or rollers an adjustable preload to suit machine weight, make them stiffer on the side toward which the carriage pushes, and limit horizontal plate movement by stops faced with felt.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The rubber-ball pad of the main patent gives no adjustable preload and no tuning for the carriage's asymmetric sideways push or the front-mounted keys.

**Solution:** Give the rubber balls or rollers an adjustable preload to suit machine weight, make them stiffer on the side toward which the carriage pushes, and limit horizontal plate movement by stops faced with felt.
