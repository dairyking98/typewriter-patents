## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Holding, feeding, or guiding means therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a fastening device for toes the ends of one guided on the side edges in slidable holders, freely over the writing surface of a copy machine and is taut and consists of that the holder wear jaws, making the ribbon in a manner known per se is grasped and tensed.

**How it works (reading):** Clamping jaws on sliding holders grip the ribbon ends and tension it into the corners of the guide strips, and pressing the hooks releases it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A ribbon strung over a copy writing plate needs its ends prepared, is hung on eyelets, and is not tensed tightly to the edges, which shortens the usable length.

**Solution:** Clamping jaws on sliding holders grip the ribbon ends and tension it into the corners of the guide strips, and pressing the hooks releases it.
