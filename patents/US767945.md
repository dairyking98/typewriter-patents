## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Case change is normally done by shifting the platen; this inventor wants capitals and small letters by shifting the typebars instead.

**Solution:** A vertically movable inner frame carrying the typebar-supporting ring is raised by a shift key, with a locking eccentric for all-capitals.

**How it works:** An inner rectangular frame of light metal runs on grooved rollers on corner tracks inside the outer frame. It carries the ring supporting the typebars and guides to the platen. Depressing the shift key lowers a crank and raises an arm on a cross shaft that lifts a lug under the inner frame, raising the ring and giving upper case. An eccentric on a handle locks the shift lever down so the frame stays raised for continuous capitals.
