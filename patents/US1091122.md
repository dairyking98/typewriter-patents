**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to improvements in typewrlting machines and particularly to improvements 111 line indicating attachments therefor.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operator cannot tell how much writing room is left on the sheet.

**Solution:** A platen attachment indicates the sheet position and remaining lines on the page, for legal and other sizes.
