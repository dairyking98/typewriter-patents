## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage-mounted ribbons for multi-copy forms are hard to thread and manipulate in their low, front-side position.

**Solution:** Ribbon spools at the platen ends with diagonal guides, and a finger piece that couples one spool while uncoupling the other.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [INK:ribbon-feed](../features/ink-ribbon-feed.md)
