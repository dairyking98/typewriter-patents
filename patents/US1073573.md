## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** A flat-platen typewriter needs a way to temporarily shift the spacing structure from its normal travel so exponents, words or lines can be printed off the normal line.

**Solution:** Controllable actuating mechanism with an adjustable stop moves the line-spacing carriage in the opposite direction by a chosen amount, can lock it there and release it.

**How it works:** The writing mechanism rides on a letter-spacing carriage atop a line-spacing carriage that travels on tracks with racks above a flat platen holding the page. Gear wheels on a drive shaft mesh the rack, driven by a power spring and escapement. The operator's actuating mechanism moves the carriage backward against its normal travel by one, two or three steps set by an adjustable stop, a locking device holds it, and unlocking returns it to normal travel while regular operation remains possible.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Moving print head](../features/esc-moving-print-head.md), [Backspace](../features/esc-backspace.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Flat bed or book typewriter](../features/pap-flat-bed.md)

**Parts:** flat platen; line-spacing carriage; letter-spacing carriage; track frame; rack; gear wheel; power spring; adjustable stop; locking device

**Numbers:** single, double or treble space

**Improves on:**

- [US826483](../patents/US826483.md): adds reverse partial movement of the line-spacing carriage
