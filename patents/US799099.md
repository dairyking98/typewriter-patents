## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructional details or processes of manufacture of the input device

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to means for facilitating the mastery of touch-writing or writing by touch upon a type-writing machine that is, the art of operating a type-writer keyboard without looking at the keys.

**How it works (reading):** An opaque sloping screen with a keyboard marking is bracketed above the keys of an Underwood so the operator cannot see the keys, and it can be removed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Touch-typing is hard to learn because the eyes keep looking at the keys.

**Solution:** An opaque sloping screen with a keyboard marking is bracketed above the keys of an Underwood so the operator cannot see the keys, and it can be removed.
