## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangement thereof for line printing

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to type-Writing machines, and the main objects thereof are to provide a simple, reliable and easily operated auxiliary attachment, or apparatus, whereby paper maybe fed to the type in a continuous web, from which the several sheets, as they are printed, may be severed in equal lengths, Without disturbing the engagement of the continuous web with the paper-cylinder and feeding-devices.

**How it works (reading):** An auxiliary attachment feeds a continuous web through the machine's own feed mechanism; a cutter severs sheets of set length, and a letterhead can be printed on each sheet before typing.
