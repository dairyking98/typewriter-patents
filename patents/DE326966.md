**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is an alternating locking and acting Braking device for the paper roll of typewriters, which differs from the known, ' This type differs in that the brake and lock alternate with each other exclude, which is not the case with most known species.

**How it works (reading):** Two brake levers and two locking levers share one rotating piece so that brake and lock alternate, never acting together, with equal spring pressure on both ends of the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Brake and lock on the paper roll were usually fitted together, and two-sided pressure could not be made equal.

**Solution:** Two brake levers and two locking levers share one rotating piece so that brake and lock alternate, never acting together, with equal spring pressure on both ends of the platen.
