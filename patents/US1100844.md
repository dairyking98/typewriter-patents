## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Differential or variable-spacing arrangements

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is primarily, the provision o improvements in controlling means for typewriter escapements, which can be attached to a typewriting machine, and used forprinting a plurality of words of two or three letters with greater speed and less effort on the part of the operator.

**How it works (reading):** Attachment to the keyboard and escapement prints two or three letter word groups with one key stroke.

## Reading (opening only, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Common short words take several strokes.

**Solution:** Attachment to the keyboard and escapement prints two or three letter word groups with one key stroke.

**How it works:** Keys connected to an operating device control a modified escapement sprocket so that the carriage spaces two or three steps while the letters strike in sequence.
