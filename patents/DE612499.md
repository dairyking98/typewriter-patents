## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings therefor

**How it works (reading):** The roller has a rigid acid-proof shell carried on the axle or core by elastic rings, so the shell gives way under paper thickness; retaining rings keep the shell from turning or sliding.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rubber pressure rollers under the platen swell and absorb fat when used with wax matrices, which then contaminates the paper and stops clean feeding.

**Solution:** The roller has a rigid acid-proof shell carried on the axle or core by elastic rings, so the shell gives way under paper thickness; retaining rings keep the shell from turning or sliding.
