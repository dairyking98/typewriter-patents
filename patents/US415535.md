## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The paper guide must hold sheets firmly against the platen and direct paper into the platen, and the existing guide does not do this well.

**Solution:** The lower guide is fixed to the carriage. The upper guide is a separate piece pivoted on arms and carries a roller that bears on the platen, which holds the sheet.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
