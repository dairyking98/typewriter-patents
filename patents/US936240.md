## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** With parallel slot walls a returning typebar can meet the next bar, and the pin can jam.

**Solution:** Use a crescent-shaped guide slot so the key-lever pin drives the bar forward on one wall and returns it faster on the other.

**How it works:** The key lever links by a rod to an intermediate lever with an anti-friction pin that engages a sickle-shaped slot in an enlargement of the typebar. On key press the pin acts only on the longer front surface and swings the bar to the platen. A spring returns the lever; the pin then works on the shorter surface, giving a quicker return that clears the next bar.
