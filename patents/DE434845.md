## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon fork drive from the typebar weakens the stroke.

**Solution:** The universal rail frame acts on a bell crank connected directly to the ribbon fork, and a manual slide sets colour zone and case through pins.

**How it works:** The ribbon fork is moved up and down by an angle lever pivoted on a bearing arm of the switching rail. The lever has two legs of different length; a U-shaped slide, moved by a manual lever, brings one of four pins to the long or short leg to select upper or lower ink zone and small or large letters; further pins limit the stroke for the upper zone and an adjustable stop limits the upward movement for the lower. A coil spring pulls the fork down, a rubber roller damps the rest stop. A position can switch the ribbon off for stencils.
