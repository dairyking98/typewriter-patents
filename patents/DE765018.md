## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a noiseless fan-type typewriter (Underwood, after US1682330) the covered type carriers and their casing rise above the platen and hide the writing line.

**Solution:** Tilt the whole type engine, carriers, casing, carriage support and switching parts as one unit forward and downward by an acute angle about the platen axis, so the line is visible while the types still strike radially.

**How it works:** Type carriers, in holders on a segment, slide in guides on a plane through the platen axis; a pair of keys shares a carrier that is thrown back and shows an upper or lower type. Rotating the whole mechanism about the platen axis lowers guides and casing while the vertical housing walls and keyboard stay put. Further described are a carriage-carrier adjustment forward/backward for the number of sheets and a column tabulator with a one-key variant.
