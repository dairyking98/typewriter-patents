## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side lays or gauges

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting niachines and more particularly to side edge gages and the object of the invention, generally stated, is to provide an improved and efficient adjustable side edge gage.

**How it works (reading):** An adjustable side gage that is pushed clear of the paper table when the table moves, and springs back to its use position when the table returns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The side edge gage blocks the paper table when the table is swung forward, so it must move out of the way.

**Solution:** An adjustable side gage that is pushed clear of the paper table when the table moves, and springs back to its use position when the table returns.
