## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Typewriter. The present invention relates to a typewriter, in the case of the return movement of the carriage to the starting position by a special Button is made under the mediation of a spring. Such typewriters are known per se. The known versions are extremely complicated and not easy enough to use to meet practical requirements. The invention consists in that, in contrast to the known devices two different strong springs are provided, of which the stronger spring directly is connected to the carrier of the pawl for the car, so that when hitting the buttons and the resulting backward movement of the pawl this spring is tensioned and in turn moves the carriage forward when the respective posted Button is released while the weaker spring is connected directly to the carriage and pulls it back into its original position as soon as the Switching device a special return button is struck.
