## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary feed or adjustment devices

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a typewriter, in the case of the return movement of the carriage to the starting position by a special Button is made under the mediation of a spring.

**How it works (reading):** A stronger spring tensioned by the pawl carrier advances the carriage on key release, while a weaker spring pulls the carriage back when a separate return button is struck; the pawl arrangement lets the advance be stopped at any point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known automatic forward and return carriage mechanisms are too complicated and not simple enough for practical use.

**Solution:** A stronger spring tensioned by the pawl carrier advances the carriage on key release, while a weaker spring pulls the carriage back when a separate return button is struck; the pawl arrangement lets the advance be stopped at any point.

The present invention relates to a typewriter, in the case of the return movement of the carriage to the starting position by a special Button is made under the mediation of a spring.
