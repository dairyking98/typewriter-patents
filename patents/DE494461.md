## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Small ticket printers have no room for the reciprocating rollers that normally distribute ink, and feeding ink from a reservoir gives poor results.

**Solution:** The inking roller and the ink-feeding roller are set with their axes inclined so they contact obliquely over their length; a thumb on the forme cylinder shaft swings the inking roller onto the plate and back.
