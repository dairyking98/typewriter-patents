## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Auxiliary type mechanisms for printing distinguishing marks, e.g. for accenting, using dead or half-dead key arrangements, for printing marks in telegraph printers to indicate that machine is receiving

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to accent-key or silent-key mechanism for typewriting machines, in which a ribbon is caused to vibrate at the type strokes to cover and uncover the printing oint.

**How it works (reading):** Depressing the accent key rocks a dog silencer into the path of the feed dog so it cannot skip an escapement tooth, while the universal bar vibrates the ribbon to cover and uncover the printing point.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** An accent or silent key should print an impression without the carriage advancing.

**Solution:** Depressing the accent key rocks a dog silencer into the path of the feed dog so it cannot skip an escapement tooth, while the universal bar vibrates the ribbon to cover and uncover the printing point.
