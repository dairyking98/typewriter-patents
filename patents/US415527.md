## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In a typebar machine where a hammer strikes the bars after they reach printing position, one key press must first position the bar and then drop the hammer.

**Solution:** Each key lever carries a hinged strap held by a spring dog; at the end of the first travel the dog hits a fixed rod and releases the strap, so further key depression pushes a hinged frame that brings the hammer down.

**How it works:** A strap is hinged to the front of each key lever and latched to it by a spring-loaded dog. Early key travel moves the typebar into printing position. Then the dog's upper end strikes a fixed rod and tips out of engagement, unlocking the strap. Further depression moves the strap down onto a cross-barred hinged frame, which through a rod pulls the hammer onto the typebar. A spring around the key stem restores the strap and re-engages the dog when the key rises.
