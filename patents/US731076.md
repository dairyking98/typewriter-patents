## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of this invention is to provide means for attaching apostal card, letter-card, or small slip of paper and the like to the platen of a type-writing machine in such manner as to facilitate the feeding of the object within the sphere of operation of'the type on said machine and at the same time prevent slipping of the object relative to the platen.

**How it works (reading):** A gripping bar pivoted on the platen, forming part of its periphery, which clamps a card and is moved by a crank shaft and locked in contact.

**Problem:** Small cards or slips cannot be fed reliably through a platen and slip during typing.

**Solution:** A gripping bar pivoted on the platen, forming part of its periphery, which clamps a card and is moved by a crank shaft and locked in contact.
