## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (date setting for ticket printers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Apparatus for stamping articles having integral means for supporting the articles to be stamped with stamping surface located above article-supporting surface and movable at right angles to the surface to be stamped having adjustable type-carrying wheels

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a manually operated one Date setting device for ticket printers, cash registers and the like with single and tens printing disks for the date and a month printing disk as well as with display disks for the days attached to the outside of the setting device and months.

**How it works (reading):** A single adjustment shaft sets all printing disks and display disks at once through interlocking gears, so the day advances one step as the shaft turns.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Known date stampers do not set day and month together and need separate drives, which makes setting unreliable.

**Solution:** A single adjustment shaft sets all printing disks and display disks at once through interlocking gears, so the day advances one step as the shaft turns.
