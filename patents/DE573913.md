## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Type lever guide for typewriters, in which the type lever can be pivoted Guide links inevitably set at the point intended for the imprint will.

**How it works (reading):** The eccentric guide members are shaped so they contact each type lever roughly at the middle of its side just before the platen, where the lever is not worn, keeping the impression even even when the levers are worn.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-lever guides with worn sides let the levers swing sideways, so the print is irregular on worn machines.

**Solution:** The eccentric guide members are shaped so they contact each type lever roughly at the middle of its side just before the platen, where the lever is not worn, keeping the impression even even when the levers are worn.
