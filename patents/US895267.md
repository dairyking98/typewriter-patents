## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar actuation needs equal touch and key depression for every key in a visible front-strike machine.

**Solution:** Type bars are pulled through flexing, toggle-like connections to fixed points, with equal-length parts and adjustable reaction springs.

**How it works:** Pivoted typebars in a segment below the platen are each linked at the heel to a forward link, jointed to a fixed anchor so that flexing gives a breaking-toggle accelerating action. The links converge on a focus so all parts are equal length. Key levers have individually and gang adjustable reaction springs on a rocking bar, a comb limits their rise and a stepped plate forms bottom stops.

**Features:** [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [IMP:element-to-platen](../features/imp-element-to-platen.md)

**Parts:** type bar; segment; head rest; flexing link; anchor piece; key lever; reaction spring; comb plate; rocking escapement plate

- [US700687](../patents/US700687.md): new connection scheme for the key levers
