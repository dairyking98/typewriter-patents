## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Not stated in the opening beyond providing a typewriter with a rotatable flexible type-form and devices for flexing it to print, spacing and shifting.

**Solution:** A flexible type-form mounted for rotary movement in a basket carriage, turned by a finger-wheel to locate the type, with devices that flex the type to print and move the carriage to space.

**How it works:** A base has side walls holding a roller platen with finger-wheels and notched-wheel catch, plus a spring-loaded paper roller. A swinging carriage frame, pivoted on the side bars, moves toward and from the platen; its guard smooths the paper as it swings. A circular basket-carriage holding the type-form slides along a track on the frame, held friction-tight to its front plate. The type-form is rotated by a finger-wheel to locate the character, then a swinging frame flexes the type against the paper and spaces the carriage along its track; the later description is cut off.
