## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for rendering the print visible to the operator with reflectors or illuminating devices

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to an attachment for type-writing machines for the purpose of e11- ablin g the operator to inspect the work as fast as the letters are formed and without stopping and turning up the carriage for this purpose.

**How it works (reading):** A refracting prism under the impression roller, on a support attached to the carriage arms, bends light so the letters show right side up and slightly magnified as they are struck.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator must stop and lift the carriage to check each letter as it is formed.

**Solution:** A refracting prism under the impression roller, on a support attached to the carriage arms, bends light so the letters show right side up and slightly magnified as they are struck.
