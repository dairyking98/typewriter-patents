## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**In the patent’s own words:** My present invention relates to an inexensive sim le and easil assembled construction for the above named purpose, having the advantage that it prevents any spreading pressure from being brought to bear on the sides of the frame in which the platen shaft has its bearings.

**How it works (reading):** The spacing ratchet sits on a hollow conical head that revolves freely on the platen shaft within a ring, with a handle-driven pin in a groove, so no spreading pressure reaches the frame sides and the platen turns with equal ease.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The platen needs to turn freely by hand to bring a line to the printing point, but the spacing ratchet mounting presses on the frame and makes the platen bind.

**Solution:** The spacing ratchet sits on a hollow conical head that revolves freely on the platen shaft within a ring, with a handle-driven pin in a groove, so no spreading pressure reaches the frame sides and the platen turns with equal ease.
