## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating is only possible toward the usual direction; reverse tabulating needs a different link.

**Solution:** A two-part link that can be locked or disconnected so that carriage release can be thrown in or out of the tabulator keys, allowing reverse tabulating.
