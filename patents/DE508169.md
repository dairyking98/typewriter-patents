## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detents, brakes, or couplings for feed rollers or platens

**How it works (reading):** Rotatable coupling bolts in bores through the platen hood have end faces that turn out past the roller surface to press against the inner surface of the index wheel.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Coupling the platen to the line index wheel needs many intricate gear parts that are costly to make.

**Solution:** Rotatable coupling bolts in bores through the platen hood have end faces that turn out past the roller surface to press against the inner surface of the index wheel.

**Features:** [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
