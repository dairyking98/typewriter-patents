## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The operator has to calculate the centre of a line by mental arithmetic to centre titles and headings between margin stops.

**Solution:** A second scale rides on the right margin stop, with an auxiliary half-value scale, so the left stop's pointers read the line length and the centre letter-space directly.

**How it works:** The carriage pointer reads the fixed frame scale in letter-spaces. The right margin stop carries a sliding scale graduated like the main one, with zero at that stop, and an auxiliary scale whose numbers are half the sliding scale's. The left margin stop carries pointers that read the main scale (its position), the sliding scale (the number of spaces in the line) and the auxiliary scale (the centre space). Setting the stops therefore sets all scales and no calculation is needed.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** carriage; carriage pointer; frame scale; right margin stop; left margin stop; sliding scale; auxiliary scale; stop pointer; rack

**Numbers:** auxiliary scale divisions are twice the size of the main scale's; example: stop at space 64, line of 52 spaces

**Improves on:**

- Underwood scale and margin stops: adds a shifting scale so the line centre is read directly
