## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In moulded type wheels with characters on the rim, lifting the type-bearing part out of the mould is difficult because of the undercut characters.

**Solution:** Mould the wheel around a toothed metal support disc inside a split, springy steel-band die engraved with the characters, which springs open to release the finished wheel.

**How it works:** A steel disc with a hub, two locating holes and wide undercut teeth has each tooth bent at right angles at mid-length, forming bent type supports and dovetail anchor openings. A cut-open steel ring is bent to spring open, has a wide groove cut inside and the type characters engraved in it. It is forced closed into an annular groove in the mould, aligned by a web in the mould that fills the gap, and the disc is placed on pins so each bent tooth faces an engraved character. Type material (polyamide, cellulose acetate butyrate, rubber, phenolic resin) is injected or pressed around and between the teeth and into the engraved band. Wheel and band are ejected together and the band springs open and separates easily from the type.
