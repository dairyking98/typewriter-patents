## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** On a manuscript holder, it is hard to find the line being typed quickly when reading the copy.

**Solution:** Thin wires of alternating colours are stretched across a spring-loaded frame over the document, so each line sits in a colour-marked field that can be found at a glance.

**Features:** [FRM:writing-visible](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** support plate; spring-loaded frame; coloured transverse wires
