## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type wheel locking in registers) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms characterised by the mounting, arrangement or disposition of the types or dies; Computing machines characterised by the structural interrelation of their functional units, e.g. invoicing machines

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Stamping units print wrong digits when a fast stamp lets a pawl drop into the adjacent tooth gap.

**Solution:** The teeth of the ratchet wheel on the faster type wheel are redesigned so a pawl cannot drop into the wrong gap.

The incorrect stamps occur preferably when the ratchet wheel of the slower rotating type wheel with the tip of a ratchet tooth located approximately under the associated pawl. The invention remedies this deficiency by redesigning the teeth that ratchet wheel that is assigned to the faster rotating type wheel.
