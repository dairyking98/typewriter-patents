## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Two-spool ribbon drives with automatic reversing make it hard to remove a spool and to operate the reversing device.

**Solution:** Each ribbon spool sits on its own drive shaft that can slide lengthways against the spool, one direction releasing the spool for removal and the other direction engaging the reversing device, with a centering spring.

**How it works:** The ribbon spools sit on opposite sides of the machine, each a separate unit on a support screwed to the base plate, with its own ratchet drive and reversing device mounted in mirror image. Tongues on the rear spool disc engage grooves of the drive shaft so the shaft turns the spool. Sliding the shaft one way from a central position disengages it from the spool, which can then be lifted off, and sliding it the other way engages the reversing device; a spring returns it to centre. The two reversing devices are coupled so that automatic reversal or manual shaft displacement on one side acts on the other. The ribbon runs through removable side guides to the ribbon fork, and the carriage is stepped by the usual spring drum and universal rod.
