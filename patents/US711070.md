## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On book typewriters the ribbon contact should not run along one line of the ribbon.

**Solution:** An attachment moves the ribbon transversely as it winds so type contact traces a zigzag path.

**How it works:** On a book typewriter the type-carriage moves across the paper pulled by a cable and is escaped by a pawl on a ratchet. A flat circular frame with fingers around the ribbon slides transversely in slots in brackets on the carriage. A toothed wheel on the carriage, with an eccentric pin in a slot of the frame, meshes with a rack on the stationary frame, so carriage travel rotates the wheel and shifts the ribbon sideways. A modification uses bevel gears from the escapement shaft instead of the rack.
