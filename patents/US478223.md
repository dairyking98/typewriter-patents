## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On wide-carriage machines (about 120 spaces) the end-of-line bell cannot be tripped in the last 25 scale divisions with the ordinary arrangement.

**Solution:** Separate the bell hammer arm and the trip-actuated inclined plate onto two pivoted sleeves linked by a connecting rod so an adjustable carriage trip can ring the bell anywhere, even at the extreme end.

**How it works:** A gong has a hammer arm on a pivoted sleeve; at the left end of the track a second sleeve carries an inclined plate. An adjustable pivoted trip-piece on the carriage front rod strikes the plate when the carriage moves left, rocking the sleeve and, through a connecting rod, raising the hammer. When the trip passes off the plate the hammer drops by gravity and rings the gong. On return the pivoted trip rides over the plate without ringing.

**Features:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** gong; bell hammer arm; pivoted sleeve; inclined plate; connecting rod; trip-piece; front carriage rod; scale bar; pointer

**Numbers:** ordinary carriage about 70 spaces; wide carriage 120 spaces; scale 0 to 120

**Improves on:**

- Caligraph wide-carriage bell: bell can now be set to ring at any point including the last 25 divisions
