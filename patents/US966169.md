## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Stabilising means therefor

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriting machines, and the object thereof is to provide readily detachable key levers therefor;

**How it works (reading):** Key levers are individually detachable, with guides and tension springs formed as one piece per bank.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key levers must be individually removable without disturbing other levers.

**Solution:** Key levers are individually detachable, with guides and tension springs formed as one piece per bank.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md), [Few parts, low cost](../features/frm-few-parts.md)
