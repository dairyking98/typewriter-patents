## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (type wheel printing, card-controlled machines)

**How it works (reading):** The type wheel is driven through a resiliently mounted gear wheel that stops when the character reaches the print position, then after printing returns to its original rest position by an increase in speed.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In card-controlled printing, the type wheels keep moving after printing, so each wheel ends in a different position and type selection by card sensing becomes unreliable.

**Solution:** The type wheel is driven through a resiliently mounted gear wheel that stops when the character reaches the print position, then after printing returns to its original rest position by an increase in speed.
