## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typist cannot change ribbon colour automatically at chosen points in the line.

**Solution:** Shift a multi-field ribbon automatically when adjustable tab stops on the carriage meet a fixed member.

**How it works:** Cooperating shifting members, using tabulator stops, change the throw of the vibratory ribbon carrier at preset carriage positions. A two-colour ribbon then writes one column in one colour and the next in another. Shown on a Monarch No. 3 front-strike machine; a modified rotary field-changing member is also described.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md), [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; vibratory ribbon carrier; tabulating stop; ribbon field-changing member

**Improves on:**

- Monarch No. 3: adds automatic ribbon field change
