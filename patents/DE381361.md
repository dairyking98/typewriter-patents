## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Brushes mounted with horizontal bristles need stiff bristles, wear fast, collect dirt and cast shadows on the keyboard.

**Solution:** A cleaning brush hangs vertically from an adjustable bracket, so fine flexible bristles brush the typebar heads as they swing through and shake dirt off.
