## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops and line locks need to be adjusted easily from the front, and both must be released together by one key without the locking parts jamming.

**Solution:** The margin stop and line-lock actuators sit behind the paper table and are adjustable from the front; a single release key unlocks both, and a lock stop swings into the path of the carriage stop at a predetermined point to lock printing, escapement and ribbon mechanisms.
