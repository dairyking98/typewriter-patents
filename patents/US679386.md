## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Operators lose time and make errors finding their place on copy, and earlier indicators were complicated or could not be adjusted for different line widths.

**Solution:** A line-indicator pointer movable down the copy-holder face, clamped to the typewriter and adjustable to any line width.
