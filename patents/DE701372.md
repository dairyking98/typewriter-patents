## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Types rotatably mounted on type levers lost impression force because they were sprung or decelerated just before the paper.

**Solution:** A crank linkage hinged to a fixed machine axis rotates the type on its lever as the lever swings, with near-zero turning speed at the start and no load on the type.

**How it works:** The type lever hinges on a wire in a fixed bearing body and carries a pivot pin on which the type carrier rotates. A second wire in the bearing body carries levers that act on an angle lever, a fork and a sliding carriage joined to the type carrier by a push rod. Swinging the type lever works this chain as a crank mechanism, turning the type to the writing position as it rises. Initial angular speed is near zero and the writing pressure goes straight through a flat bearing surface.
