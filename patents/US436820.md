## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** With loosely journaled typebars (desirable for easy action and prompt return) the types print out of alignment.

**Solution:** A flexible spring bar carries a conical pin that enters a conical hole in the typebar's type block at the moment of printing, directing the type into alignment.

**How it works:** The typebar ends in a forked block with capital and small-letter types on the two branches and a conical hole in the crotch. A thin springy metal bar parallel to the machine front, under the ribbon, is fixed at one end over a bearing and carries a conical pin at the other. When the bar swings up the pin enters the hole and guides the block into alignment; the spring bar's resilience also helps return the typebar. In a modification the pin is on the type block and the hole in the spring bar.
