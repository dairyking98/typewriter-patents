## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Bell hammer drive for typewriters, which switch over by lifting and lowering of the paper carriage and the bell and bell hammer on the machine frame are arranged.

**How it works (reading):** A double-armed auxiliary lever drives the bell hammer on one side only; its other arm is picked up by the intermediate lever only during part of the stroke, so the bell is released at the same carriage position whatever the carriage height.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The bell hammer is driven by the intermediate lever, so the bell rings at different carriage positions depending on whether the carriage is raised or lowered.

**Solution:** A double-armed auxiliary lever drives the bell hammer on one side only; its other arm is picked up by the intermediate lever only during part of the stroke, so the bell is released at the same carriage position whatever the carriage height.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Line-end bell](../features/esc-bell.md), [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
