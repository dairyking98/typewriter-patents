## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Constructions for the positioning of wipers

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide a simple, economical and efficient brush mechanism, capable of being readily attached to any of the well-known forms of typewriting machines;

**How it works (reading):** Sheet-metal frame clamps to the machine frame; a cleaning brush on a spindle is turned by a crank while a key holds the type against it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type need cleaning and there is no simple cleaner attachable to any typewriter.

**Solution:** Sheet-metal frame clamps to the machine frame; a cleaning brush on a spindle is turned by a crank while a key holds the type against it.
