## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A transcriber needed a tape guide that fed the tape across the typewriter at a fixed length per key press.

**Solution:** A tape-copy holder is driven by a spring motor whose escapement releases one controlled length of tape per key press.

**How it works:** A key controls a spring motor through an escapement whose detent arms bear on a drum, feeding tape by a fixed amount.

**Features:** [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md)
