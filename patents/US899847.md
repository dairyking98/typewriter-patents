**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Other apparatus or arrangements associated with, or incorporated in, ink-ribbon mechanisms

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to ribbon mechanism for typewriting machines and more particu larlyto mechanism in which a ribbon is or may be automatically moved into the path of tion.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** The ribbon needs a way to present a chosen field of a multi-field polychrome ribbon to the printing instrumentalities, and a mode for mimeograph stencil work.

**Solution:** A ribbon vibrating mechanism automatically moves the ribbon into the path of the type, letting any desired field of the ribbon be presented for printing, with a mode for preparing mimeograph sheets.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md), [Ribbon lift](../features/ink-ribbon-lift.md)

**Parts:** ribbon; ribbon vibrating mechanism; vertical central section; front portion of machine
