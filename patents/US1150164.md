## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Work-sheets are hard to square against the feed rolls when inserted.

**Solution:** One set of front or rear pressure rolls can be cast off from the platen while the other set remains in contact, so the sheet can be squared then gripped.
