## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cards for card catalogues must be positioned accurately without relying on operator judgment for each card.

**Solution:** Guiding and squaring devices and an indicator on the carriage show when a card is correctly placed against the printing line, allowing regular or varying line spacing.

**How it works:** A guide at the platen squares the card and an indicating device shows its position relative to the printing line on the carriage.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line and point indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
