## Reading (full text, OCR fair)

**Problem:** On type wheel machines the selected type can shift under the hammer blow, so it must be locked in both rotation directions and against the hammer's impact direction.

**Solution:** Stop pins on the wheel shaft, held between the ends of key-operated levers and a wing-like setting lever, fix the wheel firmly in the chosen position.

**How it works:** One-armed levers pivoted alternately left and right sit above the key levers, and each is pushed up on one side by its key. Between two plates on the type wheel shaft are stop pins, one per lever, which rest between the stepped wing edges of a setting lever fixed to the shaft via a sleeve. A raised lever end acts on a pin and so sets and locks the wheel angle. Springs keep the pins at rest and snap them back on release. The OCR is cut off before the end of the description.
