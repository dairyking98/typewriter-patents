## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Construction of universal bars

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines and more especially to the universal bar and the fdevices associated therewith, and said invention consists in certain features of construction and combinations of parts which will be fully set forth herein and particularly pointed out in the claims.

**How it works (reading):** Keys have stems pivoted to arms on rock shafts, linked to three-armed sub-levers that drive type bars carrying two types each through a universal bar, the sub-levers pivoted in hangers on a fixed segment.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The universal bar and its linkage to the type bars need improved construction in a front-strike machine.

**Solution:** Keys have stems pivoted to arms on rock shafts, linked to three-armed sub-levers that drive type bars carrying two types each through a universal bar, the sub-levers pivoted in hangers on a fixed segment.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md), [Type basket and bearings](../features/bar-basket-bearings.md)
