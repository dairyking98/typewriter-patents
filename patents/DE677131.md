## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Accessories for manifolding purposes

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to one consisting of several layers and in sections - according to the writing sheet subdivided writing paper strips in rolled or folded form - for writing on preferably in a typewriter while simultaneously making an or several copies using carbon paper, each after writing on one Section by means of a device opposite .

**How it works (reading):** Cutouts in each writing-paper section let the operator see the transverse edge of the carbon paper, so it can be set exactly while being advanced.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carbon paper cannot be seen when it is advanced to the next section of a multi-part writing paper strip, so it is misaligned or tears.

**Solution:** Cutouts in each writing-paper section let the operator see the transverse edge of the carbon paper, so it can be set exactly while being advanced.
