**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Carriage and platen-shift construction of a visible-writing typebar machine was complicated and hard to reach.

**Solution:** A simplified, readily dismantled carriage and platen-shifting mechanism for a shift-type visible-writing machine.

**How it works:** Only the opening is available: it states the aim of a simpler, accessible, adjustable carriage and platen shift. Details of the mechanism are not in the text read.

**Features:**

- **Shift and cases:** [Shift by moving the platen](../features/shift-platen.md)
- **Frame and body:** [Visible writing](../features/frm-writing-visible.md), [Cleaning access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** carriage; platen
