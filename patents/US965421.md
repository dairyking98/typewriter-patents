## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** An improvement on the buckling-plate clutch joining line-space wheel to platen, needing a firmer and smoother way to buckle the plate.

**Solution:** A push-pull finger button turns a screw through a helical cam slot, and the screw forces a cross lever to buckle the spring plate so it grips the line-space wheel flange.

**How it works:** As in application 508,902, a dished three-arm spring plate sits inside the line-space wheel drum and turns with the platen axle by pins in slots. A button on a stem moves along the axle, and a key on the stem rides in a helical cam slot in a tubular extension of a screw, so the straight motion rotates the screw. The screw, threaded in a boss on the axle, presses a cross pin that bears on a washer behind the plate, buckling it evenly so the arm tips spread against the flange.

**Features:** [PAP:line-space](../features/pap-line-space.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md)

**Parts:** platen; line-space wheel; flange; buckling plate; pins; screw; boss; cross lever; washer; helical cam slot; stem; button; finger wheel

**Numbers:** three arms

**Improves on:**

- [US965346](../patents/US965346.md): buckling by screw and helical cam instead of a toggle lever
