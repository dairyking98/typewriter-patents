## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** for rendering ink-ribbon inoperative

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a device for adjusting the stroke of the ribbon fork of typewriters with a ribbon containing two colored zones.

**How it works (reading):** Pivot pins are adjustable in the slide to set the ribbon stroke for type spacing, and the stops are formed integral with the slide and set by bending their weakened webs.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon stroke limit stops set by adjusting screws loosen in operation, and one pivot bracket must suit different type sizes.

**Solution:** Pivot pins are adjustable in the slide to set the ribbon stroke for type spacing, and the stops are formed integral with the slide and set by bending their weakened webs.
