## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper is not retained well in position, the feed rolls cannot be released easily, and the platen is not adjustable.

**Solution:** Feed-rolls on supporting arms can be released, paper is held by the rolls, and the platen is adjustable by a structure in the platen shaft, all on a carriage with the platen swung open for inspection.

**Features:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
