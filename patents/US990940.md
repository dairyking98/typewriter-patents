## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typewriters needed an improved denominational (decimal column) tabulator.

**Solution:** A denominational tabulator mechanism on a front-strike typebar machine with spring-drum carriage and rack-and-pinion escapement.

**How it works:** The carriage is drawn by a spring drum, its feed rack held on pinion and escapement wheel with feed dogs on a dog rocker linked to the universal bar under the key levers. Key levers drive typebars through sub-levers and links. Release keys lift the rack. The opening describes only the base machine before the denominational stop mechanism.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Typebars:** [Typebars](../features/elem-typebar.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Mainspring-drawn carriage](../features/esc-spring-drum.md), [Escape-wheel escapement](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [Carriage release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md)

**Parts:** feed rack; feed pinion; escapement wheel; feed dog; dog rocker; universal bar; key lever; sub-lever; typebar; spring drum
