## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a Type lever drive for typewriters, in which the type lever is actuated by two-armed Key levers are driven directly.

**How it works (reading):** Each key lever drive arm is made in two parts, with a leg that clamps onto the stop arm by a slot, pin and spring locking pin, so the leg can be exchanged without lifting the type lever.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Changing a worn type-lever drive pin or a key drive arm is difficult because the type lever must be lifted off the segment.

**Solution:** Each key lever drive arm is made in two parts, with a leg that clamps onto the stop arm by a slot, pin and spring locking pin, so the leg can be exchanged without lifting the type lever.

**How it works:** The slot is pushed over the pin of the stop arm and a locking pin on a spring engages a hole to hold the leg.
