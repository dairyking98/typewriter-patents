## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check perforating) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed

**In the patent’s own words:** My object is to prevent the raising or changing of the amount called for by these instruments.

**How it works (reading):** Perforating devices are heated by an electric current through a resistive material, so that the paper around each perforation is scorched and cannot be refilled.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check amounts were altered by filling in the perforations, which the inventor wanted to prevent.

**Solution:** Perforating devices are heated by an electric current through a resistive material, so that the paper around each perforation is scorched and cannot be refilled.
