## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Arrangements to attach the ribbon to the spool

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to an improvement in type-writer ribbons.

**How it works (reading):** Each ribbon end gets a paper strip with a gummed flap that is folded over the end and moistened to stick to the drum tape.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbon ends slip off the winding-drum tapes and are soiled by handling.

**Solution:** Each ribbon end gets a paper strip with a gummed flap that is folded over the end and moistened to stick to the drum tape.
