## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms permitting the selective use of a plurality of ink ribbons

**How it works (reading):** Several frame plates each hold a ribbon with its own ink reservoir and wick; a toothed bar with a gear drive brings the chosen plate to the type stop, and the ribbon is re-soaked past the wick at each stroke.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Multicolour ribbons with coloured strips have too few colours, and wide colour strips make the setting device complicated; drying ribbons give weak copies.

**Solution:** Several frame plates each hold a ribbon with its own ink reservoir and wick; a toothed bar with a gear drive brings the chosen plate to the type stop, and the ribbon is re-soaked past the wick at each stroke.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Two-colour ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon-color.md)

**Parts:** frame plates; ribbon spools; ink receptacles; wicks; toothed bar; gear drive; setting lever
