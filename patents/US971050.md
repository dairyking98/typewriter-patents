## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Key-lever cups were costly to attach, and the character tablet could turn out of readable position in the cup.

**Solution:** A key-lever cup made separately from the lever and fastened to it, with a locking feature that holds the character tablet against displacement or rotation in the cup.
