## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Paper on tabulators can skew and remain skewed during line-by-line feed.

**Solution:** A separate gripper advances the paper to the form height, detached from the printing pad, and holds it so the bulge takes up the slack.

**How it works:** The gripper device, switched by group change, pulls the paper to form height after the lines are printed.
