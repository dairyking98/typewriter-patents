## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR poor)

**Problem:** Paper must be located accurately on the platen with respect to the type line, and the platen must be secured against a pawl-operated line-spacing ratchet.

**Solution:** A plunger with a conical surface sits against pins that extend radially from the platen head; a thumbscrew through the ratchet's beveled centre hole makes the pins press and centre the ratchet, freeing or jamming the platen.

**Features:**

- **Paper and carriage:** [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** platen; plunger; conical surface; pins; ratchet; pawl; thumbscrew; beveled hole
