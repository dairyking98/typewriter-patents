## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Denomination keys are fixed in what they can print, so the same key cannot serve different denominational positions.

**Solution:** Each key carries a rotatable indicator bar with a sign on several faces, so the key can be turned to show a different denomination or punctuation mark.

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md)
