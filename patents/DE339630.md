> Description Translated from German Typewriter with power and manual operation Type imprint. The typewriter described below belongs to the group in which the drive of the type lever by means of a rotating roller and friction wheels or gears. Are known, among other arrangements with friction segments, which on the keys are rotatably attached and the type lever with the intermediation of intermediate levers move. Such designs are complicated and never trouble-free; they let themselves but also not easily convert and use for manual operation. Likewise, devices are designed with cams or eccentrics; They have not proven to be useful, as this involves hitting the type lever does not coincide with the keystroke. In the simplest form, the type levers can also be attached to their drive ends provided with friction wheels and pressed against a rotating roller. Here However, the pivot point of the type lever is changed, and inequalities can occur arise in the line height. With the arrangement of the present invention, all of these inconveniences become apparent avoided.
