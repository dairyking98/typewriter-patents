## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Copy must be held above the keyboard and advanced one line at a time without the operator handling it.

**Solution:** Copy-holding rollers in a frame are rotated by a rod and ratchet pawl driven down by a key-lever, advancing the copy one line per depression.
