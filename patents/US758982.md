## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A front-strike visible typewriter with a full keyboard (one key per character) needs a light, compact printing mechanism whose key force reaches each typebar in a direct line.

**Solution:** Arrange typebars on curved pivot wires in a segment below the platen, linked by straight connecting rods to keys, with most parts of sheet metal.

**How it works:** Typebars sit side by side on curved pivot wires concentric with the printing point, threaded at their ends through the frame side walls. Each key lever is joined by a straight rod to its typebar so the force runs directly from key position to bar. The typebars are stamped from sheet-metal blanks whose type end is folded over in steps to thicken the type-bearing part. Bearing brackets, spacing blocks and holding arms support the segment.
