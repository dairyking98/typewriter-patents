## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Driving gear therefor

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This Purposes are the sliding pieces, which approximated the shape of guide stones with have a square cross-section, firmly attached to the side panels of the platen frame connected bushings or bearings arranged rotatably.

**How it works (reading):** Square guide stones are pivotably mounted in bushings fixed to the platen frame side panels, so manufacturing misalignment is compensated and the stones wear less.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Sliding platen guides in carriage slots bind when slots, sliders or their fixings are slightly misaligned, and the sliders wear fast.

**Solution:** Square guide stones are pivotably mounted in bushings fixed to the platen frame side panels, so manufacturing misalignment is compensated and the stones wear less.
