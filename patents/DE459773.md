**How it works (reading):** The spring is hooked to a sleeve on the fixed pin that can be turned and clamped at any rotational position, which sets the spring tension with a single undivided hub.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The carriage spring fixing needed a split hub or a difficult fit, making the carriage drive hard to assemble and adjust.

**Solution:** The spring is hooked to a sleeve on the fixed pin that can be turned and clamped at any rotational position, which sets the spring tension with a single undivided hub.
