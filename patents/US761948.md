## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In billing work the operator must alternately use an adding machine and a typewriter on the same paper, which is awkward with separate machines.

**Solution:** A typewriting attachment for an adding machine: a printing roll or platen pivots up into the line of writing so the typewriter can print on the same sheet, and the two can be used alternately.
