## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Label frames on key levers or ticket-printer plates are held by bent tabs that break when labels are changed often; screws would loosen and side-fitted frames shift.

**Solution:** A thin sheet-metal slide pushed between the key plate and the frame's inward tabs locks the frame without bending anything.

**How it works:** The label and a celluloid plate sit on the key plate and the frame is pushed over them; its four inward-curved tabs pass through recesses in the key plate. A thin slide with a cutout for the lever foot is pushed between key plate and tabs, preventing the frame sliding back over the recesses. A pressed dome in the slide drops into a bore in the plate to hold it, and the slide is out of reach of the hand in use.

**Features:** [FRM:few-parts](../features/frm-few-parts.md), [ELEM:interchangeable](../features/elem-interchangeable.md)

**Parts:** key plate; type lever; label; celluloid plate; frame; tab; slide; dome

**Improves on:**

- bent-tab frames: tabs no longer need bending open and shut, so nothing breaks on repeated changes
