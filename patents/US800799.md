## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Escapements having a single pawl or like detent

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide in a type-writing machine an escapement that will enable the carriage to be held completely under the control of the operator at whatever the rate of speed the writing may be done.

**How it works (reading):** A pinion meshing with the carriage rack drives an escape-wheel on a bracket-mounted shaft, and releasable detents let the operator control the carriage at any rate.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The carriage of an escapement-driven machine cannot be held fully under operator control at every writing speed.

**Solution:** A pinion meshing with the carriage rack drives an escape-wheel on a bracket-mounted shaft, and releasable detents let the operator control the carriage at any rate.
