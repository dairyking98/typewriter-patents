## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Removing and replacing the roller frame of a typewriter paper carriage requires moving spring locking levers back by hand each time.

**Solution:** Conical pins on the roller frame push the spring-loaded angle levers aside as the frame is placed and snap them into the pin cut-outs, and springs lift the frame off when the coupling is released.
