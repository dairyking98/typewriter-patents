## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In a book typewriter the linkages are arranged so the typeface is hidden by the mechanism in the rest position, which confuses the typist, and the vertical height is large.

**Solution:** A semicircular universal rail sits directly behind the drive levers of the type levers; key-driven tie rods lift the rail, and the lifting motion is passed both to the letter switching device and to the ribbon fork.

**How it works:** Pressing a key lifts the universal rail through tie rods and approach cams, which moves the letter switching device and the ribbon fork; the typeface stays visible at rest.
