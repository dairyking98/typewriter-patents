## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Renovating or testing ink ribbons

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to typewriter ink ribbons and especially those made from combined or matted vegetable fibers.

**How it works (reading):** A finely pounded sponge is mixed into the fibre mass, roughly 75 percent mulberry fibre and 25 percent sponge, so the ribbon absorbs and re-releases ink and recovers quickly.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Mulberry-fibre typewriter ribbons with hemp or jute mixed in do not redistribute ink fast enough after a character has used up the ink at one spot, so the impression fades.

**Solution:** A finely pounded sponge is mixed into the fibre mass, roughly 75 percent mulberry fibre and 25 percent sponge, so the ribbon absorbs and re-releases ink and recovers quickly.

**Features:** [Ink ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [Attachment or accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** ribbon fibre mass; pounded sponge particles

**Numbers:** 75% mulberry fibre / 25% sponge; 60% fibre / 20% hemp / 20% sponge example
