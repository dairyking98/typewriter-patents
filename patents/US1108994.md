## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Erasing or correcting tables

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to erasing devices forming part of, or being an attachment to a typewriting machine, by means of which the erasure of printed characters may be effected while the paper is still upon the platen, so that changes or corrections can be easily and expeditiously made.

**How it works (reading):** An erasing device attachment erases printed characters while the paper stays on the platen.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Corrections needed erasing while the paper is on the platen, without changing the machine.

**Solution:** An erasing device attachment erases printed characters while the paper stays on the platen.

**How it works:** A movable erasing unit is brought into position for erasure at the printed line.
