## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Bearings with rolling contact, for exclusively rotary movement with bearing balls essentially of the same size in one or more circular rows for both radial and axial load with a single row of balls with angular contact

**In the patent’s own words:** The purpose of the invention is to provide a'construction oftype-levers and a manner of pivoting them whereby each type lever or bar will have an extended bearing, insuring accuracy in printing action and stability in all positions, the bearings being so constructed that while extended the type bars or levers can be arranged the conventional distance apart, all striking at a common center.

**How it works (reading):** Type-bars or levers are pivoted on extended bearings in a support plate with radial rows of tap-screws and pins, so the bars keep a common striking centre and the bearings can be adjusted to take up play.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Type-bar pivots give short bearings, and the bars lose accuracy and need a way to take up lost motion at the bearings.

**Solution:** Type-bars or levers are pivoted on extended bearings in a support plate with radial rows of tap-screws and pins, so the bars keep a common striking centre and the bearings can be adjusted to take up play.
