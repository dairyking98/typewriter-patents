## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of the present invention is to produce a simple and durable type bar joint which is adapted to be easily adjusted and to operate with a minimum of friction.

**How it works (reading):** The hanger has a set of pins in a circle that a circular opening in the type bar closely encircles, or conical pins that retain the bar, with a central screw; the screw can be undercut to leave only a flange bearing on the bar.
