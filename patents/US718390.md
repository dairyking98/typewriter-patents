## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typewriter platen cannot present paper to a nearby adding machine printer.

**Solution:** A platen frame that slides out beyond the right end of the carriage when raised, so paper can be fed to a Burroughs accountant.

**How it works:** The platen frame is fixed to a sleeve sliding and rotating on a rod. A slot in the sleeve registers with a lug when the platen is raised, and a cam lug and rib stop lateral shift so the sleeve lies between the shift screws. The platen then moves outward past the carriage end beside the calculating machine. Normal lateral shift for capitals is kept when the carriage is down.

**Features:** [USE:calculating](../features/use-calculating.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)

**Parts:** paper carriage; platen; frame; sleeve; rod; cam lug; rib; shift screw

**Improves on:**

- Remington No. 3 carriage: platen can leave the carriage end
