## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Dummy practice keyboard apparatus

**In the patent’s own words:** The objects of this invention are to provide a tinger-exercising machine for students of type-writing which will enable the said students to obtain visual indications of the keys struck by their lingers while exercising, thereby getting a better knowledge of the truencss with which the keys are being worked;

**How it works (reading):** A finger-exercising machine with a key set that gives visual indication of each key struck, with working parts tuned for touch and even key depression.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Students of typewriting lack a practice device that shows which keys the fingers strike and that feels like real keys.

**Solution:** A finger-exercising machine with a key set that gives visual indication of each key struck, with working parts tuned for touch and even key depression.
