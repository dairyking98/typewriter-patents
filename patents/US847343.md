## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Front-strike machines of the period needed a platen carriage supported wholly by a shifting frame, with a quicker, surer carriage release and locking mechanism and guides to aid touch.

**Solution:** Support the traveling platen carriage entirely on a pivoted vertical shift-frame that takes no part in the lateral movement.

**How it works:** Only the opening is available. A rectangular shift-frame pivots at the rear on bearing cones and swings vertically against a spring return. The platen carriage travels sideways on it. An auxiliary feed bar with interchangeable stop strips and a carriage-release mechanism are mentioned. Shift levers have thumb levers for case shifting.

**Features:** [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [ESC:carriage-release](../features/esc-carriage-release.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [FRM:frame-construction](../features/frm-frame-construction.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md)

**Parts:** shift-frame; platen carriage; platen; bearing cone; return spring; auxiliary feed bar; stop strip; shift lever
