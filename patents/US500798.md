## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators type one letter on top of another when the carriage reaches the end of the line.

**Solution:** A detent struck by the carriage near line end rocks a shaft that throws a bar under the key levers, locking all keys.

**How it works:** A rock shaft under the free ends of the key levers carries a bar that can swing into their path. A crank, rod and bell crank link the shaft to a spring-loaded detent standing in the path of a carriage sliding block. As the carriage nears its limit the block engages the detent, and the carriage spring pulls the detent down, rocking the bell crank and shaft so the bar blocks every key lever. The operator lifts a hand handle on the crank to release the lock and finish a word or hyphen. In a variant the detent block is slotted and adjustable to lock a chosen distance before the end.
