## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Left margin stops, paper presser rollers and automatic line spacing at carriage end are lacking or inadequate on the Oliver machine.

**Solution:** An improved left-hand margin stop, presser rollers holding the paper against the platen, and automatic line-spacing at the end of carriage travel.

**How it works:** A margin stop rod sits under the carriage and sets the left margin; presser rollers hold the paper to the platen; a device spaces the line when the carriage reaches the end of its travel.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Margin stops](../features/esc-margin-stop.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Feed rollers and paper release](../features/pap-feed-rollers.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)

**Parts:** margin stop rod; presser rollers; platen; line-spacing device
