## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check writer printing couple) · **Subject (patent classes):** Cheque stamping machines

**In the patent’s own words:** The invention is directed in part to a particular relative arrangement of the elements of the printing couple themselves;

**How it works (reading):** A check-writer printing mechanism gives a wide range of fractional and other amounts to the operator, which are printed indelibly together with a maximum limiting value.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Check writers allow raised amounts, so a wide range of fractional and other amounts must be set and printed indelibly, with a maximum limit to prevent alteration.

**Solution:** A check-writer printing mechanism gives a wide range of fractional and other amounts to the operator, which are printed indelibly together with a maximum limiting value.

**Features:**

- **Impression:** [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
