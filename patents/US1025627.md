## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides for the sheet feeding section

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to paper feeding mechanism for typewriting machines and its main object is the provision of improved means for guiding and supporting the paper, more particularly as it is introduced into the machine.

**How it works (reading):** An extension behind the paper table ends in an anti-friction roller edge, so the free paper drops behind the brackets and is guided by that edge.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Hanging tissue sheets drop straight down behind the curved standards, dragging on them and crumpling the paper and resisting line spacing.

**Solution:** An extension behind the paper table ends in an anti-friction roller edge, so the free paper drops behind the brackets and is guided by that edge.

- **Paper and carriage:** [Paper table and guides](../features/pap-paper-guide.md), [Line spacing](../features/pap-line-space.md)
