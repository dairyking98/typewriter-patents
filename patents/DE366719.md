## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-lever actuating mechanisms

**How it works (reading):** Key levers are cranked sideways in their middle group to clear space for the type-lever basket, and bent-arm key levers drive the type levers through intermediate levers of different lengths, which also accelerate the strike, lowering the machine height.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** Typewriters with type levers striking backwards against the platen are too tall to fit a small case or travel bag.

**Solution:** Key levers are cranked sideways in their middle group to clear space for the type-lever basket, and bent-arm key levers drive the type levers through intermediate levers of different lengths, which also accelerate the strike, lowering the machine height.

Like. To be able to accommodate. For the stated purpose, care is taken that between the Type lever bearing segment and the button levers are not in the way of any intermediate linkage. The middle group the key lever can be cranked laterally in such a way that a free space is created into which the type lever basket can be inserted, reducing the machine height is decreased. The intermediate levers with which the bent arm of each key lever is connected, appropriately have such a different length that when hitting the key the type movement is accelerated.
