## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Detecting means for copy material, e.g. for detecting or sensing presence of copy material or its leading or trailing end

**In the patent’s own words:** The object of my invention is to provide simple and efficient printingpoint indicating mechanism which is automatically operated at each printing operation.

**How it works (reading):** A printing-point indicator mechanism, operated automatically at each printing operation, that marks the printing point on a visible machine (applied to a Monarch machine).
