## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Foldable typewriters

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to typewriters and especially those that are downsized for easy transport and for the use can be increased.

**How it works (reading):** The set of type levers is raised on a detent in the working position and folded down in the transport position, with the key-to-type linkage disengaged by a toothed segment and the spacer key moved inward below the keyboard; a movable frame part rises and lowers the type-lever frame.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A typewriter must be made smaller for transport and for use in confined places, without losing a working layout.

**Solution:** The set of type levers is raised on a detent in the working position and folded down in the transport position, with the key-to-type linkage disengaged by a toothed segment and the spacer key moved inward below the keyboard; a movable frame part rises and lowers the type-lever frame.

**Parts:** type levers; detent cushion; intermediate levers; toothed segment; movable frame part; spacer key; paper roller

**Numbers:** fold angle about 120 degrees; fold to about 180 degrees
