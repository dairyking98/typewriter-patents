## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar key linkages should be simple and cheap with easy action.

**Solution:** Bent key levers pivoted high behind the platen drive converging links to rear-striking typebars.

**How it works:** Typebars rest on a curved pad, pivot on a curved rod in radial slots of an inclined segment, and strike rearward at the platen. Bent levers with pendent and forward parts carry the keys, and links converge from the levers to the typebars.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
