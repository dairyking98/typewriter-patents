## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Paper-carriage guides or races

**How it works (reading):** The fixed carriage rail rests only on two bearing blocks on the side plates, which let the rail self-align so it does not bend.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A carriage rail clamped to uneven frame bearing surfaces bends and binds the carriage, and lateral adjusters do not stop bending stress.

**Solution:** The fixed carriage rail rests only on two bearing blocks on the side plates, which let the rail self-align so it does not bend.
