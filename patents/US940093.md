## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Learners need a cheap device for practising the touch method of typewriting on a keyboard that matches a typewriter's key layout.

**Solution:** A casing shaped like a typewriter holds a keyboard of key bars and letter bars, with letter plates and a rubber-buffered spacing bar, arranged in exact imitation of the typewriter keyboard, as a training device.

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
