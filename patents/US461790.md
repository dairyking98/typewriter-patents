## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** A typograph (typebar machine) needs more characters per bar than a single type.

**Solution:** A pivoted type-carrier at the end of each typebar holds two types and is flipped between lower and upper position by a stop-shaft actuator on the working stroke.

**How it works:** Each typebar carries a pivoted carrier with two types and a detent spring and cam that hold it in either position. An upper-case key moves a rocker arm and link that turn a stop shaft so a curved actuator enters the path of a cam arm on the carrier. On the working stroke the arm strikes the actuator and flips the carrier so the other type faces the platen. A torsion spring retracts the actuator.
