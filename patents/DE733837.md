## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Ink ribbons characterised by the material from which they are woven

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a Procedure for marking ribbons.

**How it works (reading):** Print a contrasting pigment, such as carbon black or bronze in a solvent with a binder, onto the finished coloured ribbon, or remove metal bronze from a coated ribbon to leave a lettering pattern.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons are hard to tell apart and have no lasting way to mark them as an advertisement or as the company's own property.

**Solution:** Print a contrasting pigment, such as carbon black or bronze in a solvent with a binder, onto the finished coloured ribbon, or remove metal bronze from a coated ribbon to leave a lettering pattern.
