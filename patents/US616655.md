## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Mechanical typewriter movements (typebar throw, carriage step, release, line feed) cost the operator constant energy and fatigue and limit speed.

**Solution:** Electric magnets perform all the typewriter movements, keys acting as switches, with the keyboard placeable apart from the machine.

**How it works:** Keys on a remote or adjacent keyboard close circuits that electrically move the typebars from rest to impression, move the carriage laterally by one space, release the feeding device and turn the platen for a new line. A shift mechanism tied to the keyboard lets each typebar produce three different impressions. Two keyboards can control one machine. Only the opening is available; 16 sheets of drawings are lost to OCR.
