## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters for ciphering or deciphering cryptographic text with auxiliary keys or keyboards acting on the original keys or keyboards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to traffic by means of cipher and has particular purposes the creation of increased security against translation of the secret documents by unauthorized persons.

**How it works (reading):** Control step switches driven by text keypresses give irregular switching pulses, with tabs used in irregular sequence, to change the cipher circuits unpredictably.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Cipher typewriter step switches advance in a regular pattern, which lets unauthorised persons reconstruct the key sequence.

**Solution:** Control step switches driven by text keypresses give irregular switching pulses, with tabs used in irregular sequence, to change the cipher circuits unpredictably.

**Features:**

- **Selection:** [Full keyboard, a key per character](../features/sel-keyboard-full.md)
- **Spacing and escapement:** [Prints and spaces in one stroke](../features/esc-print-and-space.md)
