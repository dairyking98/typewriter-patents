## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Rack-and-pinion line and letter spacing on book typewriters suffers lost motion and wear, so the carriage can stop at the wrong position.

**Solution:** Lost motion between the gear wheel and rack is taken up by preventing longitudinal carriage movement at a faulty tooth engagement during rest, and the rack is cushioned to reduce wear.
