## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to feeding mechanism, and more particularly to a device whereby definite movements of the feeding mechanism may be made to impart proportionately greater or less movements to the object to be fed, the percentage of increase or decrease being governed at the will of the operator.
