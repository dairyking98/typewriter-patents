## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Operators of typewriters need an easy way to align columns of figures and place decimal points.

**Solution:** A tabulator attachment: an adjustable pointer on the carriage rod moving over a digit scale graduated in digits, tens, hundreds and fractions.

**How it works:** A pointer is clipped to the carriage scale-rod by a collar and set-screw or spring clamp. A digit scale is fixed to the frame (or part of the center pointer) so the pointer shows the digit place, with decimal point line, and the groups marked H, T, M, B or Fr. Shown on Smith Premier, Blickensderfer, Yost and Remington machines. Several graduation layouts are shown.

**Features:** [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md), [ESC:tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [PAP:line-indicator](../features/pap-line-indicator.md)

**Parts:** pointer; digit scale; collar; set-screw; spring clamp; carriage rod; dust guard

**Improves on:**

- Smith Premier, Blickensderfer, Yost and Remington scale-rods: adds a digit scale and pointer to existing carriage
