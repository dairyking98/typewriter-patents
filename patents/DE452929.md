> Description Translated from German Paper guides for typewriters, For paper guides for typewriters with the paper support plate moving with the switchover of the platen it was not previously possible to use the latter for the purpose of using large paper formats accordingly to be kept extensive because of the high weight of such large amounts of paper the switchable paper carriage part was pulled too much backwards, so that jams in the guiding organs for the switching movement and thereby inhibitions.: the latter entered. The present invention aims at paper guides Above type, avoiding the disadvantage mentioned, the use of paper support plates to enable the largest scope, without the switchable paper carriage part significantly to charge. This is achieved according to the invention in that of the support plate during the switching movement only the front part of the up and down movement of the switchable paper carriage part takes part, while the rear part of the support plate is supported by fixed support arms.
