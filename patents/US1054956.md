## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The universal bar blocks typebar removal on Underwood machines and cannot be moved forward without spacing the carriage.

**Solution:** A shiftable feed pawl that can be displaced sideways so the universal bar moves forward without operating the escapement.

**How it works:** An escapement wheel with openings works with a detent dog and a feed pawl on a sliding yoke, driven through a one-way clutch and pinion from the carriage. Typebar heels strike the universal bar to operate the escapement. A shiftable plate pawl normally aligns with the feed operator. A lever pushes it aside so the universal bar can advance without feeding, letting any typebar be unhooked from the fulcrum rod.

**Features:** [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ELEM:typebar](../features/elem-typebar.md), [FRM:cleaning-access](../features/frm-cleaning-access.md)

**Parts:** universal bar; escapement wheel; detent pawl; feed pawl; shiftable pawl; segment; fulcrum rod; typebar; rocker; spring
