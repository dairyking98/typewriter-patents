## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**In the patent’s own words:** This invention relates to tabulators for typewriters and is an improvement in or modification of the tabulator mechanism described in the specification of my prior Patent No.

**How it works (reading):** Adjusting apparatus turned to the right or left shifts the tabulator stop bars between positions by turning the adjusting parts.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulator stop bars must be readjusted for each invoice heading, and this adjustment is cumbersome.

**Solution:** Adjusting apparatus turned to the right or left shifts the tabulator stop bars between positions by turning the adjusting parts.
