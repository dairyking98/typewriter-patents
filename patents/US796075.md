## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Tabulating denomination-spacing is complex and not positive enough in action.

**Solution:** A column-stop bar on a rock-shaft with rock-arms and column stops, combined with a simplified denomination-spacing portion, moves the carriage to the column and then to the denominational point.

**Features:**

- **Spacing and escapement:** [Tabulator](../features/esc-tabulator.md), [Pawl-and-rack escapement](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)
