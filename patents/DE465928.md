## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-head pivoted to or rotating on lever

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a switching device for typewriters where the type lever is equipped with a rotatable type head which have a stop for setting the types intended for printing, which is held in the set position by a tension spring.

**How it works (reading):** A tension spring is attached to the type lever and engages the type head through a semicircular rod, so that the line between the spring's two attachment points straddles the head's fulcrum in both end positions, forcing the head over and holding it in each.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The rotatable type head of an Elliott Fisher type lever needed special tools to be forced into, and held in, its upper and lower case positions.

**Solution:** A tension spring is attached to the type lever and engages the type head through a semicircular rod, so that the line between the spring's two attachment points straddles the head's fulcrum in both end positions, forcing the head over and holding it in each.
