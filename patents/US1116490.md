## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** Lost motion and noise arise between a platen carriage and its support rail when the rail is shifted for case.

**Solution:** Carry the carriage on inclined-periphery rollers on a round rail, so gravity takes up slack automatically.

**How it works:** The platen carriage has diverging legs with pivot pins carrying rollers whose peripheries are inclined, so they bear on the circular rail and self-adjust. Arms under the rail stop the carriage lifting off. A swinging rack stays in mesh with the escapement pinion while the rail lifts. A second form uses spherical rollers on an angular-faced rail.

**Features:** [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [SHIFT:platen](../features/shift-platen.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md), [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md)

**Parts:** platen carriage; rail; roller; spherical roller; carriage rack; feed pinion; pivot arm; shift shaft

**Improves on:**

- earlier shiftable platen rails: gravity-adjusted rollers remove slack and noise
