## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (embossing die for office machines) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing or marking mechanisms characterised by the purpose for which they are constructed for embossing, e.g. for making matrices for stereotypes

**How it works (reading):** The underline die is designed with raised edge strips beside the line so the underline can be embossed without damage to adjacent text.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Underlining in embossed address and text printing plates required separate dies and a second printing step, and the edge ridges disrupted the typeface.

**Solution:** The underline die is designed with raised edge strips beside the line so the underline can be embossed without damage to adjacent text.

**How it works:** Embossing die for office printing plates in which underlines are made with edge strips above and below, so underlining is done in one step.
