**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a typewriter o.

**How it works (reading):** A lock on the numeric keys is held blocked by a cam member of the indexing device for the whole carriage jump and is released only when the carriage stops and the locking mechanism drops into a gap in the cam.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Keying a number key before the paper carriage jump has finished could enter a wrong value, and roller locks did not fully prevent it.

**Solution:** A lock on the numeric keys is held blocked by a cam member of the indexing device for the whole carriage jump and is released only when the carriage stops and the locking mechanism drops into a gap in the cam.
