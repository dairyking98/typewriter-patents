## Summary

**Category:** misc / other, not a typewriter patent (mechanical toy typewriter model) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters

**How it works (reading):** A spring drive moves the carriage in steps as a detent disc releases; a lobed disc on the same shaft raises and lowers the figure's arms via rods through the table slots.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Existing typewriter toys have no motion of their own, so the figure does not move with the carriage.

**Solution:** A spring drive moves the carriage in steps as a detent disc releases; a lobed disc on the same shaft raises and lowers the figure's arms via rods through the table slots.
