**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Actions or mechanisms not otherwise provided for for aligning characters for impression

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to an improvement in the type lever guide for typewriters according to the main patent 368zig with pivotable guide arms about standing axes, which through the nose of the type lever to a guide mechanism that acts like a toggle lever can be added.

**How it works (reading):** The guide arms get nose-like overlapping extensions that touch the opposite arm when swung apart, so each arm's nose acts as its own stroke limiter.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR good)

**Problem:** The separate stroke-limiting stops on the swinging type-lever guide arms of the main patent are an extra part to make and align.

**Solution:** The guide arms get nose-like overlapping extensions that touch the opposite arm when swung apart, so each arm's nose acts as its own stroke limiter.
