## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Line spacing

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a line switching device for power-driven typewriters, in which the coupling between the line switching device and the power drive is established with the aid of a key.

**How it works (reading):** A swingable rail on the carriage is kept in permanent engagement with a lever moved by the power drive; a key engages the clutch that drives the lever, which pivots the rail and pulls back the line-switch pawl so the paper roller advances one or more lines.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** The line switching device on a power-driven typewriter should be simpler and work in every carriage position and every paper roller setting.

**Solution:** A swingable rail on the carriage is kept in permanent engagement with a lever moved by the power drive; a key engages the clutch that drives the lever, which pivots the rail and pulls back the line-switch pawl so the paper roller advances one or more lines.
