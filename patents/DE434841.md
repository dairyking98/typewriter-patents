The purpose of the invention is to simplify the construction of the Zeilenschaltv device of power-driven typewriters as possible and to make it so that a line switch is possible in all carriage positions and in every switching position of the paper roller. For this purpose, a swingable rail connected to the line switching mechanism is arranged on the paper carriage and a lever moved by the power drive is brought into permanent engagement with the same.
