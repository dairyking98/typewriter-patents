## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Carriage return or release devices therefor with special means to maintain character-spacing or back- spacing elements in engagement during case-shift or like movement

**How it works (reading):** The platen is raised or lowered by a screw spindle according to paper thickness.

## Reading (opening only, OCR good)

**Problem:** On Remington/Caligraph typebar machines in a ring, wrapping more sheets around the platen moves the impact point away from the type levers' reach, so letters print above or below the line.

**Solution:** The platen is raised or lowered by a screw spindle according to paper thickness.

**How it works:** The platen sits in a cross-head guided in grooves of the carriage's front bar. A screw spindle with a head knob, screwed through the front bar and rotatably joined to the cross-head, lifts or lowers the platen. A lock nut fixes the spindle. For machines striking from above the platen is lowered instead.

**Features:** [IMP:impression-adjust](../features/imp-impression-adjust.md), [PAP:platen-roller](../features/pap-platen-roller.md), [FRM:adjustment-wear](../features/frm-adjustment-wear.md)

**Parts:** platen; cross-head; carriage front bar; screw spindle; knob; lock nut

**Improves on:**

- Remington and Caligraph typewriters: compensates for paper thickness
