**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Side-stop mechanisms

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on a margin setting device for typewriters.

**How it works (reading):** Two parallel rows of letter-width stops, one for left and one for right margin, sit in opposed sawtooth guides; pressing a setting key releases the previously set tooth.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Margin stops are weak and bend under continuous carriage returns, and old margins are not cleared on a new setting.

**Solution:** Two parallel rows of letter-width stops, one for left and one for right margin, sit in opposed sawtooth guides; pressing a setting key releases the previously set tooth.
