## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Rollers

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to typewriting machines, and particularly to improved means for feeding a worksheet around the platen of a typewriting machine.

**How it works (reading):** A flexible band attached to the platen carries pins that register with holes in the worksheet, and it winds up as the platen turns and rides out on a guide table.
