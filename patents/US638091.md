## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Typewriters or selective printing mechanisms having dual functions or combined with, or coupled to, apparatus performing other functions

**In the patent’s own words:** Object of the invention is to place the type at the ends of-small levers, which rise to make the impression on the paper, and to mount these letters on a wheel or disk, so that this machine is intermediate in construction and operation between the machines with key-levers only and those with key-disks only and has all the'advantages of both systems, with the inconvenience of neither.
