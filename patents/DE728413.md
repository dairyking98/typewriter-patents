## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sheet holders, retainers, movable guides, or stationary guides specially adapted for small cards, envelopes, or the like, e.g. credit cards, cut visiting cards

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to on a device for writing thinner around the platen of typewriters laid paper from the top to the bottom.

**How it works (reading):** Thin leaves are held in an arrangement between the platen and the sheets so the writing lines can be used across the card.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Postcards written one after another need a full writing surface, but slots for them are hard to insert and stacks shift.

**Solution:** Thin leaves are held in an arrangement between the platen and the sheets so the writing lines can be used across the card.
