## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Platen-shift mechanisms need a separate lock for the lower position and a stroke limit for the raised position.

**Solution:** Use two toggle joints in the shift linkage so one is always straight when the other is bent, locking the lower position and limiting the upper by the linkage itself.

**How it works:** The platen rests on a shift frame guided in the machine frame. Two toggle levers act on the frame, driven through angle levers by the shift key's linkage. Down, the first toggle is extended, locking the platen against vibration while the second is bent. Pressing the shift key buckles the first and straightens the second, which stops the upward stroke. Releasing returns by the platen's weight. Eccentric collars on the pivot bolts fine-tune the stroke. A modified form uses a hooked second lever instead.
