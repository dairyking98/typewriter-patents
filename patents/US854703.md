## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Mechanisms for manipulating page-width impression-transfer material, e.g. carbon paper

**In the patent’s own words:** My invention relates to manifolding attachments for typewriters whereby to enable the production at the same time of one or more copies or facsimiles of the original or ribbon-impression sheet.

**How it works (reading):** A manifolding attachment for platen-roll machines with a spring-actuated rewinding roll, controlled by the paper-release lever, that carries a separable manifolding ribbon or sheet.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Making one or more manifold copies needs an attachment that rewinds the manifold ribbon automatically.

**Solution:** A manifolding attachment for platen-roll machines with a spring-actuated rewinding roll, controlled by the paper-release lever, that carries a separable manifolding ribbon or sheet.

**How it works:** The attachment is fitted to the platen roll; a spring rewinding roll takes up the manifolding ribbon and is triggered by the paper-release lever.

**Features:**

- **Inking:** [Carbon paper or inked strip](../features/ink-carbon.md)
- **Paper and carriage:** [Carbon copies](../features/pap-manifold.md), [Roller platen](../features/pap-platen-roller.md)
