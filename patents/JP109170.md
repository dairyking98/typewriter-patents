## Summary

**Kind:** typebar machine · **Subject (patent classes):** Type-selecting or type-actuating mechanisms

**Class note:** J-PlatPat files it as a type carrier stopped by electromagnetic means (B41J7/48); its stops are purely mechanical, so it is shown under type selection in general (B41J7/00).

## Reading (full text, OCR good)

**Problem:** In type-wheel stamping machines the frame can be forced down when the wheel is not exactly positioned or locked, causing wrong prints and soiled paper.

**Solution:** A projection moving with the locking hook rides a fixed guide so the frame can only descend after the hook is fully in its notch, and the guide keeps it locked during the stroke.

**How it works:** A type wheel with gear and cam sits on a horizontally sliding, parallelogram-linked frame. Sliding the selector bar, whose rack turns the wheel gear, aligns a pointer on a character plate and brings the type below the axle. Pressing the knob lever lifts an angle bar, drops a hook into a gear notch and pushes a floating bar whose lug leaves the bend of a fixed guide. Pressing harder lowers the frame against a spring; an ink roller inks the lowest type then moves aside, and the type stamps the paper in the tray. The guide leads the lug throughout so the hook cannot release.

## Description (English translation of the Japanese text, opening)

> Specification. Printing machine. Nature and object of the invention. This invention relates to a printing machine of the kind in which a type wheel with many types fixed round its rim is mounted on a horizontally movable support frame; sliding a type-selector bar turns the wheel and brings the wanted type directly under the wheel's axle, and pressing the bar down lowers the frame to stamp on the paper below. It is characterised as follows: a projection that moves with the hook that stops the wheel's rotation is fitted on the frame and set against a fixed guide on the machine base; only when the hook has been brought to the exact position for stopping the wheel does the projection clear the bend of the guide and let the frame descend; and while the frame rises and falls, the guide leads the projection so that the hook holds the wheel firmly locked. Its object is accurate printing in machines with a type wheel that turns and rises and falls in this way. Brief description of the drawings. The drawings show one embodiment. Fig. 1: side view.
