## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Sizing, starching or impregnating fabrics; Passing of textile materials through liquids, gases or vapours to effect treatment, e.g. washing, dyeing, bleaching, sizing, impregnating of fabrics

**How it works (reading):** A portable inking device with two felt-covered chambers and a cone valve is drawn over the ribbon so it inks both sides in place.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Ribbons on typewriters and adding machines cannot be re-inked without removing them from the machine.

**Solution:** A portable inking device with two felt-covered chambers and a cone valve is drawn over the ribbon so it inks both sides in place.

**Features:** [INK:ribbon](../features/ink-ribbon.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** ink reservoir; felt disc; cone valve; adjustable bracket; slot
