## Summary

**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (check printer, cash registers)

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The present invention relates to a check printer for cash registers, Booking and similar machines with one the printing of subtotals and totals controlling and the check dispenser influencing gait regulators.

**How it works (reading):** The position of the gait setting means picks the printed sign and also controls check output, perforation and cutting.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Single, combined and total check outputs on cash registers need separate control keys for each mode.

**Solution:** The position of the gait setting means picks the printed sign and also controls check output, perforation and cutting.

**Features:** [Check writer](../features/use-check-writer.md), [Separate print key or lever](../features/imp-print-lever.md)
