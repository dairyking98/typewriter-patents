## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** After the final margin stop has arrested the carriage, one more character cannot be printed without the escapement dog jamming.

**Solution:** The text describes a carriage feed/escapement arrangement so that one additional character after margin release is properly fed; the detailed fix is not clear in the first 3000 characters.

**Features:** [ESC:margin-stop](../features/esc-margin-stop.md), [ESC:escape-wheel](../features/esc-escape-wheel.md)
