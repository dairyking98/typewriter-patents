**Category:** related office machine, not a typewriter (typewriter totalizer with multiplying attachment)

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Electrical contact transfer between a typewriter and a multiplier is fault-prone.

**Solution:** A linkage gives a mechanical coupling between the value generator and the multiplier, controlled by the power drive.
