## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Interlocks between keys, e.g. without detent arrangements

**In Google’s translation of the patent’s own words:** The invention relates to a key lock for typewriters with limbs that lock the other keys when one key is pressed.

**How it works (reading):** Wedge-shaped key levers act on a row of loose rollers whose total spacing is less than the wedge thickness, so each new key pushes the previous one out and releases it.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** In fast typing, keys can jam or fail to release because the locking bodies rely on spring force and mass.

**Solution:** Wedge-shaped key levers act on a row of loose rollers whose total spacing is less than the wedge thickness, so each new key pushes the previous one out and releases it.
