## Description (Google Patents OCR, opening)

> Description Translated from German Keylock. The invention relates to a key lock for typewriters with limbs that lock the other keys when one key is pressed. Of the Invention according to the design of the locking members is chosen such that the depressed Button forced back the previously pressed button. In particular is there essential that the self-locking between the locking members and the buttons avoided will. While in the known devices the return of the keys and thus their emergence from the locking members of the mass of the moving parts and the spring force acting on these moving parts is dependent and consequently at You are inhibited from hitting the keys in rapid succession these inhibitions are excluded in the device according to the invention, because each subsequent key pushes the previously struck key out of the locking elements and as a result, the return movement of the previous key is accelerated. The device is particularly essential for the high-speed, mechanically driven typewriters.
