## Summary

**Kind:** a part or mechanism (any kind of machine) · **Subject (patent classes):** Tabulating

**How it works (reading):** A rider rail on the carriage of the sliding type basket is stopped by a tab stop that is released from a locking member when the tab key is pressed; the basket moves step by step with the rider stop set for each column.

## Reading (short summary from the opening, OCR fair)

**Problem:** A tabulator on a sliding type basket of an accounting machine with a fixed keypad needs riders that stop the basket at the set column.

**Solution:** A rider rail on the carriage of the sliding type basket is stopped by a tab stop that is released from a locking member when the tab key is pressed; the basket moves step by step with the rider stop set for each column.
