## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typebar actions needed an easy start, acceleration toward the platen, and no rebound collisions.

**Solution:** Two cooperating sub-levers with contact faces give a variable lever ratio so the typebar accelerates, plus restraint against rebound.

**How it works:** A second-order key lever drives a third-order sub-lever by a pin in a slot; its upper arm pulls the typebar by a link. A second angular sub-lever, pivoted above the key levers, is also driven by a pin in a slot, and the contact faces of the two upright arms change leverage as the stroke proceeds. Restoring spring and locking against rebound keep adjacent bars from colliding.
