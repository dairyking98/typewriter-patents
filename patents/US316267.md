## Reading (opening only, OCR fair)

**Problem:** Typing is slow because every word is spelled letter by letter.

**Solution:** An attachment to the spacing frame lets word-types such as 'the', 'tion' be printed by one key and spaced as prefixes, suffixes or whole words by a laterally movable pawl.

**How it works:** A rocking frame on the ordinary spacing frame carries a fixed pawl D and a second pawl C with extra lateral movement, held in line by a spring-loaded back stop. When a word-type key is struck, the frame advances and pawl C steps over the extra tooth or teeth the wider word-type requires. Suffix and prefix types are spaced without a gap; whole words get a normal space. The attachment can be thrown back out of use.

**Features:** [ESC:pawl-rack](../features/esc-pawl-rack.md), [ESC:variable-spacing](../features/esc-variable-spacing.md), [USE:accessory](../features/use-accessory.md)

**Parts:** rocking frame; fixed pawl; laterally movable pawl; back stop; spring; rack bar; tilting beam; finger key lever
