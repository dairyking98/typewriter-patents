# Guides

Written introductions to index typewriters, drawn from the patents and machines in this library. Every patent,
machine and feature named links to its page. The summaries behind them were made by machine from OCR, so check a
patent's page and drawings before relying on a detail.

- [What is an index typewriter?](index-typewriters.md): what makes a machine an index machine, why they were made,
  the ways of choosing a character, type carriers, printing, inking and spacing, where and when they were popular,
  and a glossary of English and German terms.
- [How index machines work](how-index-machines-work.md): the mechanisms subsystem by subsystem (choosing the
  character, holding it for the print, the type element, cases and shift, impression, inking, spacing), with the
  variants and the best historical examples.
- [A timeline of important features](timeline-of-features.md): the innovations that mattered, 1829 to 1950, era by
  era, with a summary table.
- [German index typewriters, 1880–1950](german-index-typewriters.md): the hand-index machines of the 1880s–90s, the
  Mignon and its pointer-field cylinder, the small and toy machines of the 1920s–30s, and the inventors and firms.
