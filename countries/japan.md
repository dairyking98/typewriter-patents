Listed from J-PlatPat by the Japanese classes for typewriters (FI B41J), and read from the scans: each patent was transcribed and translated into English by machine from the page images, then checked against OCR, so the text reads cleanly. Their classes are the Japanese FI codes, close kin of the CPC classes on US and German pages. Utility models (実用新案, a lesser right for small devices) are marked as such. Many are for Japanese-script typewriters, which pick one type at a time from a tray of over two thousand. **Still coming in:** more are added as they are translated.
